# Volunteer Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Signup Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who want to participate in various projects and activities. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your interest in specific projects, tasks, and dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and surname in the `Name` and `Surname` fields.
2.  Fill in your email address in the `Email` field.
3.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4.  Select your date of birth from the calendar in the `Date of Birth` field.
5.  Fill in your address in the `Address` field.
6.  List your skills in the `Skills` field. You can list multiple skills separated by commas.
7.  Choose the tool you prefer in the `Assigned Tool` field.
8.  Select the project you are interested in by choosing the corresponding option from the `Project` field.
9.  Choose a date for confirmation in the `Confirm Date` field.
10.  Choose a time for confirmation in the `Confirm Time` field.
11.  Select the tasks you are interested in by choosing multiple options from the `Task` field.
12.  Write any additional notes about yourself in the `Notes` field.
13.  Choose your nationality in the `Confirm Nationality` field.
14.  Choose your native language in the `Confirm Language` field.
15.  Choose your role in the `Role` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Signup** (`volunteer_signup_form`, text, required): This is the main title of the form.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, required): List your skills separated by commas.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose the tool you prefer.
* **Project** (`project`, select_one, required): Choose the project you are interested in.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date, required): Choose a date for confirmation.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, time, required): Choose a time for confirmation.
* **Task** (`task`, select_multiple, required): Choose multiple tasks you are interested in.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Write any additional notes about yourself.
* **Confirm Nationality** (`confirm_nationality`, select_one, required): Choose your nationality.
* **Confirm Language** (`confirm_language`, select_one, required): Choose your native language.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Choose your role.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and carefully, as this information will affect your assignment to specific projects or tasks.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your application and get back to you soon.
