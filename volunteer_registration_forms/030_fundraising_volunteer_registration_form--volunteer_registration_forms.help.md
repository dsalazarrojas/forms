# Fundraising Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers to register for fundraising events. It collects information about the volunteer's availability and contact details to ensure effective communication and scheduling. Please fill out this form to register for a fundraising event and contribute to our cause.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields with your information.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure to choose the correct options for your preferences.
3. Once you have completed the form, click submit to register as a volunteer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name <small>First Name (text, required: no)</small>**: Please enter your first name. This will be used to address you in communications related to your volunteer work.
* **Last Name <small>Last Name (text, required: no)</small>**: Please enter your last name. This will be used to confirm your identity and ensure accurate records.
* **Email <small>Email (email, required: no)</small>**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and to keep you updated about your volunteer schedule.
* **Shift <small>Are you available to work a shift? (select_one, required: no)</small>**: Check "Yes" if you are available to work a shift during the event. This will help us schedule you for a shift.
* **Role <small>Do you want to be a volunteer role model? (select_one, required: no)</small>**: Check "Yes" if you are interested in being a volunteer role model during the event. This role typically involves interacting with guests and promoting our cause.
* **Role <small>Role Description (text, required: no)</small>**: Enter a brief description of your role in the event (e.g., "Greeter", "Event Coordinator", etc.). This will help us understand your specific contribution.
* **Contact Number <small>Contact Number (text, required: no)</small>**: Enter your phone number. This will be used by our team to contact you in case of emergencies or last-minute schedule changes.
* **Contact Email <small>Contact Email (email, required: no)</small>**: Enter your email address. This will be used as a backup contact method in case we need to reach you.
* **Availability <small>Availability (time, required: no)</small>**: Choose a time slot you are available to work during the event. This will help us schedule you for a shift.
* **Start Time <small>Start Time (time, required: no)</small>**: Choose a start time for your shift. This will be used to schedule your shift accurately.
* **End Time <small>End Time (time, required: no)</small>**: Choose an end time for your shift. This will be used to schedule your shift accurately.

## Tips
* Make sure to choose your availability carefully to ensure accurate scheduling.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask a member of our team for clarification.
* We appreciate your commitment to our cause and look forward to working with you!
