# Animal Sanctuary Open Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from visitors about their preferences, interests, and needs for an open day event at the animal sanctuary. This information will help us to provide a customized experience for each visitor.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Choose your preferred date for the event.
3. Provide accurate information about your group type and size.
4. Answer truthfully about any accessibility needs.
5. Select your tour preference.
6. Answer the waiver and safety guidelines agreement question.
7. Agree or disagree to the use of photos for promotional purposes.
8. If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (page 1, id: 1)**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address (page 1, id: 2)**: Enter the email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (page 1, id: 3)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Address (page 1, id: 4)**: Enter your street address.
* **City (page 1, id: 5)**: Enter your city.
* **State or Province (page 1, id: 6)**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code (page 1, id: 7)**: Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Select Open Day Date (page 8, id: 8)**: Choose one of the available dates for the event.
* **Number of Guests (page 9, id: 9)**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring, not including yourself.
* **Ages of All Guests (page 10, id: 10)**: List the ages of all your guests.
* **Type of Group (page 11, id: 11)**: Select the type of group you are with (e.g., Family with Children, Adults Only, etc.).
* **Number of Children Under 12 (page 12, id: 12)**: If you are bringing children under 12, enter the number.
* **Do You Have Any Accessibility Requirements (page 13, id: 13)**: Answer truthfully about any accessibility needs you have.
* **Accessibility Details (page 13, id: 14)**: If you answered "yes" to accessibility requirements, provide more details.
* **Are You Interested in Volunteering (page 15, id: 15)**: Check if you are interested in volunteering at the sanctuary.
* **Tour Preference (page 16, id: 16)**: Choose your preferred type of tour (e.g., guided or self-guided).
* **Areas of Special Interest (page 17, id: 17)**: Select the areas that interest you the most.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event (page 18, id: 18)**: Select how you found out about this event.
* **Have You Visited Our Sanctuary Before (page 19, id: 19)**: Check if this is your first visit or if you've visited before.
* **Would You Like to Make a Donation (page 20, id: 20)**: Check if you'd like to make a donation during your visit.
* **Are You Interested in Sanctuary Membership (page 21, id: 21)**: Check if you're interested in becoming a member.
* **Emergency Contact Name (page 22, id: 22)**: Enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (page 23, id: 23)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I Agree to the Sanctuary Waiver and Safety Guidelines (page 24, id: 24)**: Check that you agree to follow the safety rules.
* **May We Use Photos from the Event (page 25, id: 25)**: Check if you consent to the use of photos taken during your visit.
* **Additional Comments or Questions (page 26, id: 26)**: If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to add them.
