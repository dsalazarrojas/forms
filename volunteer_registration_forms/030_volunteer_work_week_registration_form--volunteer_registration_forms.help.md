# Volunteer Work Week Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the Volunteer Work Week Registration Form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to ensure accurate registration information.
2. Enter your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
3. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
4. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
5. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
6. Choose a valid date in the field labeled "date".
7. Choose a valid time in the field labeled "time".
8. Choose the date of the event you are attending in the field labeled "event_date".
9. Choose the time of the event you are attending in the field labeled "event_time".
10. Provide a brief description of your role in the event in the field labeled "event_details".
11. Choose your volunteer type in the field labeled "volunteer_type" (Volunteer, Staff, or Other).
12. Select the organization you are volunteering with in the field labeled "organization" (Non-profit Organization, Community Group, School, Event Organizer, or Educational Institution).
13. Enter a description of your role in the event in the field labeled "description".
14. Choose the tool assigned to you in the field labeled "assigned_tool" (chatjimmy or Other).
15. (Optional) Select the category of the form in the field labeled "category" (Volunteer Registration Forms or Other).
16. (Optional) Select the file output in the field labeled "output_file" (030 volunteer work week registration form--volunteer registration forms or 031 volunteer work week registration form--volunteer registration forms).
17. (Optional) Enter a description of your role in the field labeled "description_2".
18. (Optional) Enter a description of your role in the field labeled "description_3".
19. (Optional) Choose the tool assigned to you in the field labeled "assigned_tool_2" (chatjimmy or Other).
20. (Optional) Enter the form ID in the field labeled "form_ids_2".
21. (Optional) Enter a description of your role in the field labeled "description_4".
22. (Optional) Select the category of your role in the field labeled "category_2" (Volunteer Registration Forms or Other).
23. (Optional) Select the file output in the field labeled "output_file_2" ('Yes' or 'No').
24. (Optional) Enter a description of your role in the field labeled "description_5".
25. (Optional) Enter the form ID in the field labeled "form_ids_3".
26. (Optional) Enter the assigned tool in the field labeled "description_5".
