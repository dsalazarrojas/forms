# Open Resource Sharing Community Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from potential volunteers for the Open Resource Sharing Community. This information will be used to match volunteers with individuals or organizations in need of help in specific areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Create a strong password and confirm it to secure your account.
3. Provide your contact number and a brief description of how you can be contacted.
4. Select the areas you are willing to offer help with (Emergency Food, Emergency Shelter, Emergency Housing, or Other).
5. Specify the types of resources you have to offer (Food, Shelter, Housing, Clothing, or Other).
6. Provide additional notes about yourself and your expertise.
7. Fill in any community information, organization details, and your role within it.
8. Confirm your availability and time preferences using the provided date and time inputs.
9. Choose your timezone and timezone offset (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Please fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Please provide a valid email address.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, **required**): Create a strong password to secure your account.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, **required**): Re-enter your password to confirm it.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, **required**): Provide your contact number.
* **Can you offer help with?** (`offer_help`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the areas you are willing to offer help with (Emergency Food, Emergency Shelter, Emergency Housing, or Other).
* **Are you in need of help with?** (`need_help`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Specify the areas you are in need of help with (Emergency Food, Emergency Shelter, Emergency Housing, or Other).
* **What type of resource do you have to offer?** (`resource_types`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the types of resources you have to offer (Food, Shelter, Housing, Clothing, or Other).
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **optional**): Provide any additional information about yourself and your expertise.
* **Community Information** (`community_info`, `text`, **optional**): Fill in any community information.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, **optional**): Specify the organization you are affiliated with.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, **optional**): Describe your role within the organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, **optional**): Fill in the name of the contact person.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, **optional**): Specify the location of the organization or community.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, **optional**): Confirm your availability using the date input.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, **optional**): Choose your preferred time using the time input.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select your timezone (EST, PST, CST, MST, or Other).
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, `number`, **optional**): Specify your timezone offset (if applicable).
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, `time`, **optional**): Re-enter your preferred time to confirm it.
* **Confirm Timezone** (`confirm_time_zone`, `select_one`, **optional**): Re-select your timezone to confirm it.
* **Confirm Timezone Offset** (`confirm_time_zone_offset`, `number`, **optional**): Re-enter your timezone offset to confirm it.
* **Confirm Availability** (`confirm_availability`, `date`, **optional**): Re-enter your availability to confirm it.
* **Confirm Location** (`confirm_location`, `text`, **optional**): Re-enter your location to confirm it.
