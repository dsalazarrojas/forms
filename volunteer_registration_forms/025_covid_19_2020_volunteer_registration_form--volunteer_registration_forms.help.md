# COVID 19 2020 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The COVID 19 2020 Volunteer Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering for COVID-19 related activities. This form is used to match volunteers with suitable roles based on their skills, availability, and personal circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully review each field and provide accurate information.
2. Ensure you meet the eligibility criteria for volunteering, as specified on the form.
3. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
4. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (2, email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (3, text, required): Enter your best contact number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Street Address** (5, text, required): Enter your residential address.
* **City** (6, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (7, text, required): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (8, text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Current Profession** (9, text, required): Enter your current occupation or profession.
* **Are You a Healthcare Worker?** (10, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" if you are currently employed in the healthcare industry, or "False" otherwise.
* **Medical Credentials** (11, text, optional): If applicable, provide any medical licenses or certifications.
* **Area of Interest** (12, select_multiple, required): Select one or more areas where you would like to contribute, such as patient care support or screening and testing.
* **Availability** (13, select_multiple, required): Indicate when you are available to volunteer, such as weekdays or weekends.
* **Hours Per Week** (14, select_one, required): Choose how many hours you can dedicate per week.
* **Available Start Date** (15, date, required): Enter the date when you can begin volunteering.
* **Remote or In-Person** (16, select_one, required): Indicate your preference for remote or in-person volunteering.
* **Are You High Risk?** (17, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have COVID-19 risk factors, or "False" otherwise.
* **High Risk Household Members?** (18, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you live with individuals with COVID-19 risk factors, or "False" otherwise.
* **COVID-19 Training Completed?** (19, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have completed COVID-19 training, or "No - Willing to complete" or "No - Not interested" otherwise.
* **PPE Experience** (20, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience with personal protective equipment.
* **Languages Spoken** (21, text, optional): If applicable, list any languages you speak besides English.
* **Prior Volunteer Experience** (22, text, optional): If applicable, describe any relevant volunteer experience.
* **Background Check Consent** (23, select_one, required): Answer "Yes I Consent" to consent to a background check.
* **Do You Have Health Insurance?** (24, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you have current health insurance coverage.
* **Liability Waiver** (25, select_one, required): Answer "Yes I Accept" to understand and accept the risks and responsibilities associated with volunteering.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (26, select_one, required): Answer "Yes I Agree" to maintain patient confidentiality.
* **Why Do You Want to Volunteer?** (27, text, required): Provide a brief explanation of your motivations for volunteering.
* **Additional Skills** (28, text, optional): If applicable, list any additional skills you can contribute.
* **Questions** (29, text, optional): If you have any questions, use this field to ask them.
* **Signature** (30, text, required): Sign your full name.
* **Date Signed** (31, date, required): Enter the date you sign this form.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when providing information.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization administering this form.
