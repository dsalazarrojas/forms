# Cub Scout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cub Scout Registration Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians who wish to register their child for the Cub Scout program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant Name**, **Scout Name**, and **Participant Email** fields with the relevant information.
2. Select the **Scout Unit** from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the **Participant Phone**, **Participant Address**, **Participant City**, **Participant State**, and **Participant Zip** fields with the relevant information.
4. Enter the **Participant Date of Birth** and **Participant Age** fields with the relevant information.
5. Select the **Grade** level of the participant from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the **Relationship** between the participant and the parent or guardian in the **Relationship** field.
7. Enter the **Parent or Guardian Name**, **Parent or Guardian Phone**, **Parent or Guardian Email**, **Parent or Guardian Address**, **Parent or Guardian City**, **Parent or Guardian State**, and **Parent or Guardian Zip** fields with the relevant information.
8. Sign the **Signature** field with your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant who is registering for the Cub Scout program.
* **Scout Name**: Enter the name of the scout who is being registered.
* **Scout Unit**: Select the scout unit that the participant is registering with. (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd)
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the participant or parent/guardian.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant or parent/guardian.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the participant or parent/guardian.
* **City**: Enter the city of the participant or parent/guardian.
* **State**: Enter the state of the participant or parent/guardian.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of the participant or parent/guardian.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the participant.
* **Age**: Enter the age of the participant.
* **Grade**: Select the grade level of the participant.
* **Relationship**: Enter the relationship between the participant and the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Address**: Enter the address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian City**: Enter the city of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian State**: Enter the state of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Zip**: Enter the zip code of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature**: Sign with your signature in this field.
