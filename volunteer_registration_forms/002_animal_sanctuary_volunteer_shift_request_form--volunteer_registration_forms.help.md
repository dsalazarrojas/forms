# Animal Sanctuary Volunteer Shift Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Sanctuary Volunteer Shift Request Form is designed to provide information about your availability and preferences for volunteering shifts at the Animal Sanctuary. This form helps the volunteer coordinator schedule volunteers effectively and allocate tasks according to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required fields filled.
2. Fill in your volunteer information, including your full legal name, contact email, and contact phone number (optional).
3. Select your preferred volunteer roles from the available options.
4. Choose the days you are available to volunteer.
5. Select your preferred shift length.
6. If applicable, provide any relevant experience you'd like to highlight.
7. If necessary, provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
8. Choose your preferred start date to begin volunteering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer full name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for scheduling confirmations and volunteer updates.
* **Contact phone number**: Enter your phone number for urgent messages or to be contacted about scheduling changes (optional).
* **Preferred volunteer roles**: Select one or more roles that interest you, such as Animal care, Cleaning and maintenance, Administrative support, Fundraising, or Education and outreach.
* **Availability days**: Choose all the days you are available to volunteer, such as Weekdays or Weekends.
* **Preferred shift length**: Select the typical length of time you'd like to volunteer for a shift, such as 2 hours, 4 hours, or 6 hours.
* **Relevant experience**: Briefly describe any relevant experience you have that would be beneficial for volunteering, such as previous animal care experience or event planning.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: If applicable, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an unexpected situation.
* **Preferred start date**: Choose the date you'd like to begin volunteering, if applicable.

Note: The volunteer coordinator will review your information and contact you to confirm your availability and schedule you for shifts according to your preferences.
