# Santa Claus Parade Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for the Santa Claus Parade. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate data collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the time you wish to work during the parade in the "Shift Time" field.
5. Choose the length of your shift in the "Shift Length" field.
6. Select the task you would like to perform during the parade in the "Task" field.
7. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Registration Form** (`Volunteer Registration Form`, null, required/optional): This is the main registration form.
* **Volunteer Name** (`Name`, text, false): Please fill in your name as a volunteer.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, false): Provide your phone number for communication.
* **Shift Time** (`Shift Time`, select_one, true): Select the time you are available to work during the parade.
*   * 8:00 AM: You will work from 8:00 AM to 9:00 AM.
*   * 10:00 AM: You will work from 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM.
*   * 12:00 PM: You will work from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM.
* **Shift Length** (`Shift Length`, select_one, true): Choose the length of your shift.
*   * 1 hour: You will work for 1 hour.
*   * 2 hours: You will work for 2 hours.
*   * 3 hours: You will work for 3 hours.
* **Task** (`Task`, select_one, true): Select the task you would like to perform during the parade.
*   * Route Captain: You will be in charge of guiding the floats through the parade.
*   * Parade Walker: You will walk along with the floats.
*   * Stage Manager: You will manage the stage and its operations.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, false): Add any additional comments or notes about your volunteer work.
