# Scripture Sharing Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about scripture sharing sessions and activities. It is essential to provide accurate and relevant data to facilitate smooth planning and execution of these sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Session Details** section, providing as much information as possible about the session.
2. In the **How did you hear about us?** field, mention the source from which you heard about the scripture sharing sessions.
3. Provide your **Contact Information** to help us contact you for any updates or follow-ups.
4. Enter your **Name** and **Email** to allow us to personalize your experience.
5. Fill in your **Phone** number to ensure we can reach you in case of any last-minute updates.
6. Indicate your **Session Preferences** by selecting the options that best suit your needs.
7. If applicable, provide the **Session Date**, **Session Time**, and **Group Details** relevant to the session.
8. Select the **Session Topic** relevant to the session.
9. Add any **Notes** about the session if you feel it's necessary.
10. If the session involves using a tool, indicate if it is **Assigned** and provide the **Tool Name**.
11. If you wish to provide feedback or comments about the session, add your **Notes**.
12. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (session_details): Enter the details about the session you are signing up for.
* **How did you hear about us?** (scripture_sharing_signup_form): Explain how you heard about the scripture sharing sessions.
* **Contact Information** (contact_details): Provide your contact information to help us contact you.
* **Name** (name): Enter your name to help us personalize your experience.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone** (phone): Fill in your phone number to allow us to reach you in case of any last-minute updates.
* **Session Preferences** (session_preferences): Select the options that best suit your needs for the session.
* **Session Date** (session_date): If applicable, provide the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (session_time): If applicable, provide the time of the session.
* **Group Details** (group_details): Enter the relevant group details for the session.
* **Session Topic** (session_topic): Select the session topic that best suits your needs.
* **Notes** (notes): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): Indicate if the session involves using a tool and whether it is assigned.
* **Tool Name** (assigned_tool_name): If the session involves using a tool, enter the tool name.
* **Notes for Tool** (notes_for_tool): Add any additional comments or notes about the tool.
* **Submitter** (submitter): Enter your name to act as the submitter of the session.
* **Submitter Email** (submitter_email): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you as the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (submitter_phone): Fill in your phone number to allow us to reach you as the submitter.
* **Session Status** (session_status): Select the current status of the session.
* **Status** (status): Enter the status of the session.
* **Date of Session Status Update** (date_of_session_status_update): If applicable, provide the date the session status was updated.
* **Time of Session Status Update** (time_of_session_status_update): If applicable, provide the time the session status was updated.
* **Notes on Session Status Update** (notes_on_session_status_update): Add any comments or notes about the session status update.
* **Session Notes** (session_notes): Add any additional comments or notes about the session.
