# Content Creation Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Creation Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from potential volunteers to help us understand their availability, experience, and motivation for participating in our content creation projects. This form will be used to determine the best fit for our team and to ensure that we can provide a smooth volunteer experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and mailing address.
2.  Select your relevant skills and expertise.
3.  Choose your preferred volunteer position and availability.
4.  Provide information about your experience level and previous volunteer experience.
5.  Share a link to your portfolio or work samples (if applicable).
6.  Select your preferred start date and volunteer duration.
7.  Indicate your willingness to undergo a background check.
8.  Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number.
9.  Finally, confirm your registration details and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Full Name** (`volunteer_full_name`, `text`, required): Please fill in your legal first and last name.
* **Volunteer Email** (`volunteer_email`, `email`, required): Enter your best email address for us to contact you.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your best contact number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): Fill in your street address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state name.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization you are volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, `select_one`, required): Select one or more roles you are interested in (e.g., Content Writer, Social Media Manager, etc.).
* **Skills and Expertise** (`skills_and_expertise`, `text`, required): List your relevant skills and expertise.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
* **Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, optional): Provide a link to your portfolio or work samples (if applicable).
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_one`, required): Choose when you are available to volunteer (e.g., Weekdays Only, Weekends Only, Evenings Only, Flexible Schedule, Full-Time).
* **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, `select_one`, required): Select how many hours you can volunteer weekly (e.g., 1-5 hours, 5-10 hours, 10-15 hours, 15-20 hours, 20+ hours).
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer Duration** (`volunteer_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long you plan to volunteer (e.g., One-Time Project, 1-3 Months, 3-6 Months, 6-12 Months, Ongoing).
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`previous_volunteer_experience`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you have any previous volunteer experience (True or False).
* **Volunteer Experience Details** (`volunteer_experience_details`, `text`, optional): Describe your previous volunteer work (if applicable).
* **Motivation for Volunteering** (`motivation`, `text`, required): Share your motivation for volunteering with us.
* **Equipment Available** (`equipment_available`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the equipment you have access to (e.g., Computer/Laptop, Camera, Microphone, Lighting Equipment, Editing Software, Smartphone, None).
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you consent to a background check (Yes, I Consent or No, I Do Not Consent).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): The date of this application.
