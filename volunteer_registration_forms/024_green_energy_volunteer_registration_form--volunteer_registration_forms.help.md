# green_energy_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The green energy volunteer registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to participate in green energy-related initiatives. This form helps us understand your availability, contact details, and role in the volunteering process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2.  Provide your Email address in the Email field.
3.  Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4.  Select your Date of Birth from the Date of Birth field.
5.  Enter your Address in the Address field.
6.  Choose your role in the Role field (Volunteer, Participant, or Organizer).
7.  Select your available shifts in the Shift field (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
8.  Choose your availability in the Availability field (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
9.  Select your volunteer type (General, Special, Emergency).
10.  Indicate your membership in any of the volunteer groups (Volunteer Group 2 to 13).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Provide your Email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Select your Date of Birth.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your address.
* **Role** (Select One, Required): Choose your role in the volunteer process (Volunteer, Participant, or Organizer).
* **Shift** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the shifts you are available to work (Monday to Sunday).
* **Availability** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose your availability for volunteer work (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Note your preferred time.
* **Additional Information** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional information you wish to share.
* **Volunteer Type** (Select One, Required): Choose your type of volunteer (General, Special, Emergency).
* **Volunteer Group 2** to **Volunteer Group 13** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate your membership in any of the volunteer groups.

## Tips
*  Be accurate when filling in your personal details.
*  Select all the shifts and groups that apply to you.
*  Ensure your availability is clear and realistic.
*  Provide any additional information that may assist us in understanding your volunteer role.
