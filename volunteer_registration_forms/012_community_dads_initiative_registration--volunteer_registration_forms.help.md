# Community Dads Initiative Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Dads Initiative Registration form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for the Community Dads Initiative program. This form helps the program match volunteers with suitable activities and ensures a smooth process for both volunteers and the program team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents ready to complete the form accurately.
2. Read each section carefully and answer all required fields.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`dad_first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (`dad_last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Street Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
* **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Ages of Your Children** (`children_ages`, text, required): List the ages of all your children.
* **Family Size** (`family_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of family members.
* **Available Days for Activities** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select the days you can participate in activities (e.g., Weekday Mornings, Weekday Afternoons).
* **Areas of Interest** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas where you'd like to volunteer (e.g., Mentoring Youth, Coaching Sports).
* **Skills You Can Share** (`skills_to_share`, text, optional): List any skills or expertise you can offer as a volunteer.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_role`, select_one, required): Select how you'd like to contribute (e.g., Mentor, Coach, Event Organizer).
* **Will You Complete Background Check** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Confirm if you're willing to complete a background check (required for working with youth).
* **Do You Have Reliable Transportation** (`transportation`, select_one, required): Confirm if you can get to event locations.
* **How Did You Hear About This Initiative** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Choose where you learned about the Community Dads Initiative (e.g., Website, Social Media).
* **What Are Your Goals for Participating** (`goals`, text, optional): Describe what you hope to gain from participating in the program.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I Agree to Program Terms** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to follow program guidelines.

Please fill out the form carefully, as it's essential for the program to process your application and match you with suitable volunteer opportunities. If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact the program team for assistance.
