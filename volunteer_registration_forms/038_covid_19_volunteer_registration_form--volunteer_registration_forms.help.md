# Covid 19 Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Volunteer Registration Form is used to gather information from potential volunteers, which will help our organization to manage volunteer teams effectively during the COVID-19 pandemic.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal details, such as first name, last name, email, phone number, and availability.
2. Select your preferred shift (if available).
3. Choose your volunteer type (General, Medical, or Support).
4. If you are a medical professional, provide your medical license details.
5. Indicate if you have any certifications such as CPR, BLS, or ACLS.
6. Mention any languages you speak or other skills you possess.
7. Provide emergency contact information.
8. Enter the date you started volunteering with us.
9. Finally, confirm your availability in terms of volunteer hours per week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Enter the days and hours you are available to volunteer per week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, 8-12, 2-4).
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Mention any skills you possess, such as language proficiency or medical experience.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If you have a preferred location where you would like to volunteer, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (yyyy-mm-dd) if you want to be contacted for certain projects.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth if you want to be contacted for morning or evening shifts.
* **Vaccine Type** (`vaccine_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of vaccine you are eligible for administering.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the shifts you are available to volunteer (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, required): Select your volunteer type (General, Medical, Support).
* **Medical License** (`medical_license`, text, optional): If you are a medical professional, enter your medical license number.
* **Medical School** (`medical_school`, text, optional): If you have attended a medical school, enter the name of the school.
* **Medical Board** (`medical_board`, text, optional): If you are a member of a medical board, enter the name of the board.
* **Medical License Number** (`medical_license_number`, text, optional): Enter your medical license number.
* **Certification** (`certification`, select_multiple, optional): If you have a CPR, BLS, or ACLS certification, select it here.
* **Languages** (`languages`, text, optional): Mention any languages you speak fluently.
* **Other languages** (`languages_other`, text, optional): If you speak languages not listed above, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Volunteer Since** (`volunteer_since`, date, required): Enter the date you started volunteering with us.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, required): Enter the number of volunteer hours you commit to per week.

Note that you can always reach out to us if you have any questions or need further clarification on any field.
