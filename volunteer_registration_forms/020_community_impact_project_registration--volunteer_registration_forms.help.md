# Community Impact Project Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Community Impact Project Registration" form is designed to collect information about community projects that aim to create positive social or environmental impact. This form helps us understand the project's title, lead contact, volunteer needs, and other essential details. By filling out this form, you'll be able to register your project and share it with us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the project title, which should be a short descriptive title for your project.
2. Provide the name of the primary person responsible for the project (Lead Contact).
3. Enter the email address of the project's lead contact for coordination purposes.
4. Describe the volunteer needs of your project, including the roles and estimated numbers of volunteers required.
5. If your project has a proposed start date, enter it in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD). If not, leave this field blank.
6. Estimate the duration of your project in months.
7. List the resources needed for your project, such as materials, funding, or space.
8. Specify the target population served by your project, for example, age groups or demographic focus.
9. Read and agree to our terms and guidelines by selecting the "Agree" option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title**: <project_title> 
    • A short descriptive title for your project.
* **Lead Contact Name**: <lead_contact>
    • The name of the primary person responsible for the project.
* **Lead Contact Email**: <contact_email>
    • A valid email address for project coordination purposes.
* **Volunteer Needs Description**: <volunteer_needs>
    • A description of the roles and estimated numbers of volunteers required.
* **Proposed Start Date**: <start_date>
    • The date when activities will begin (in YYYY-MM-DD format). If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Estimated Duration**: <duration_months>
    • The estimated duration of your project in months.
* **Resources Needed**: <resources_needed>
    • A list of materials, funding, or space needed for your project.
* **Target Population Served**: <target_population>
    • The age groups or demographic focus of the target population served.
* **Agree to Terms and Guidelines**: <agreement>
    • Confirm your agreement to our terms and guidelines by selecting "Agree".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
* Use a valid email address for project coordination.
* If your project's start date is not yet determined, you can leave the "Proposed Start Date" field blank.
* Be specific when describing the volunteer needs and resources required.
* Clearly define the target population served to help us tailor our support.
* Review our terms and guidelines before selecting "Agree" to ensure you understand our expectations.
