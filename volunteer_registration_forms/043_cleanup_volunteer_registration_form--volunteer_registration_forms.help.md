# Cleanup Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a volunteer for cleanup projects. It collects personal, contact, volunteer, and other relevant information from the applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, not required): Enter your date of birth in this field.
* **Age Group** (`age_group`, `select_one`, not required): Select your age range from the given options.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your residential address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city name.
* **State/Province** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select if you have volunteered before.
* **Describe Your Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_history`, `text`, not required): Provide a description of your previous volunteer experience.
* **Type of Cleanup Project** (`cleanup_project_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of cleanup project that interests you.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Hours Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of hours you can commit per week.
* **Duration of Commitment** (`duration_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Select the duration of your commitment.
* **Skills You Can Contribute** (`skills`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select all applicable skills you can contribute.
* **Any Physical Limitations** (`physical_limitations`, `text`, not required): Describe any physical limitations, if applicable.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role** (`preferred_role`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred volunteer role.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_heard_about`, `select_one`, not required): Select how you heard about this opportunity.
* **Organization/Group Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, `text`, not required): Enter your organization or group affiliation, if applicable.
* **Do You Need Transportation?** (`transportation_need`, `select_one`, not required): Select if you need transportation.
* **Agree to Volunteer Waiver** (`agree_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Select if you agree to the volunteer waiver and liability release.
* **Agree to Follow Code of Conduct** (`agree_code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Select if you agree to follow the Code of Conduct.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically filled with today's date.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and completeness of the form.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Contact the volunteer coordinator if you have any questions or concerns.
