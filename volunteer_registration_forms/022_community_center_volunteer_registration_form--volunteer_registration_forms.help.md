# Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from potential volunteers to help them find suitable roles and activities within the organization. This form is a crucial step in the volunteer registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number (if you have one).
2. Specify your availability, including the days and times you are available to volunteer.
3. Choose the areas of interest from the list provided (you can select multiple options).
4. If you have relevant experience or skills, provide a brief summary.
5. Indicate your consent for a background check (this is required for certain roles).
6. Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your preferred contact email address.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to facilitate communication.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required): Specify the days and times you are available to volunteer.
* **Areas of interest** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the volunteer roles you are interested in, such as Event setup, Program assistance, Fundraising, Administrative support, or Outreach.
* **Relevant experience or skills** (`relevant_experience`, `text`, optional): If you have experience or skills that are relevant to volunteering, provide a brief summary.
* **Consent to background check** (`background_check_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your consent for a background check (required for certain roles).
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We will review and verify your application to ensure that we can match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.
