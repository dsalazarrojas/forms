# Senior Outreach Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Outreach Volunteer Registration form is designed to register volunteers for senior outreach programs. It captures essential information to match volunteers with suitable opportunities based on their availability, interests, and volunteer type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Enter your Address.
5. Enter your Zip Code.
6. Select your Availability days.
7. Choose your Availability Time.
8. Select your Interests.
9. Choose your Availability Frequency.
10. Select your Volunteer Type.
11. Choose your Volunteer Hours.
12. Add any Additional Information (optional).
13. Confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use regularly. This will be used to communicate with you about volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during business hours.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your area for geographical location.
* **Availability**: Select the days you are available to volunteer, such as Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
* **Availability Time**: If you have specific time constraints, enter them here, e.g., morning, afternoon, or flexible.
* **Interest**: Select the areas where you are interested in volunteering, such as Outreach Activities, Support Groups, Fundraising, Social Services, Education, Healthcare, or Other.
* **Availability Frequency**: Select the frequency of your availability, such as Weekdays, Weekends, Holidays, Flexible, or As needed.
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose how you would like to volunteer, such as In-Home Volunteer, Group Volunteer, Phone Buddy, or Other.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Select the number of volunteer hours you can commit per session.
* **Additional Information**: Add any additional details or notes about your volunteer preferences.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that the information you have provided is accurate and true.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when providing information to ensure a successful matching process.
- If you have already registered as a volunteer, please access your existing account to update your information.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
