# volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The volunteer opportunity enrollment form is used to collect information about a volunteer's availability and preferences to match them with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (marked as required).
2. Provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
3. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy.
4. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_1`): This is the title of the form, but it's not a field that needs to be filled out.
* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_2`): Enter a brief description of your volunteer opportunity and why you are interested in participating.
* **Contact** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_3`): Enter your name, email, and phone number so we can contact you for further information or to confirm your enrollment.
* **Volunteer Opportunity Details** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_4`): Enter a brief description of the volunteer opportunity you are interested in, including the specific tasks or responsibilities you can perform.
* **Confirm Enrolment** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_5`): Confirm that you are willing to participate in the volunteer opportunity by entering a confirmation message.
* **Volunteer Opportunity ID** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_6`): Enter the ID of the volunteer opportunity you are interested in (if you have one).
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_7`): Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Role** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_8`): Enter your role as a volunteer (e.g., "lead volunteer", "assistant", etc.).
* **Volunteer Email** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_9`): Enter your email address.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_10`): Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Address** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_11`): Enter your address.
* **Availability** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_12`): Select your availability (early bird, morning, afternoon, evening, or nights).
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_13`): Enter the start date of your volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_14`): Enter the end date of your volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer Start Time** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_15`): Enter the start time of your volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer End Time** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_16`): Enter the end time of your volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer Comment** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_17`): Enter any additional comments or details about your volunteer opportunity.
* **Volunteer Opportunity** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_18`): Enter a brief description of the volunteer opportunity you are interested in (this field is not used in the backend, but it's here for your reference).
* **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_19`), **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form 2** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_20`), **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form 3** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_21`), **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form 4** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_22`), **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form 5** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_23`), **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form 6** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_24`), **Volunteer Opportunity Enrolment Form 7** (`volunteer_opportunity_enrollment_form_25`): These fields are not used in the backend and are likely leftovers from a previous version of the form.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
