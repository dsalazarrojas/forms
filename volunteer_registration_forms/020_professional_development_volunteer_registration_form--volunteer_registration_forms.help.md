# Professional Development Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Volunteer Registration Form is a simple form designed to collect information from volunteers about their participation in professional development activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name and any other required information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have a role or assigned tool for the activity.
3. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the 'Comments' field.
4. If applicable, select the assigned tool or output file for the activity.
5. Enter the workshop or meeting location and date for the activity.
6. Select the category for the activity.
7. Enter the title of the activity.
8. Enter the form ID, category, and title for the activity.
9. Upload the output file for the activity.
10. Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Registration Form** (`volunteer_registration_form`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other required information in this field.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification card or official documents.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`, text, required: false): Enter your name in case it appears differently on your identification card or official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop or meeting you are attending.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop or meeting.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the workshop or meeting.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the workshop or meeting.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have a role assigned for the activity, or 'No' if not.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback for the activity.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the assigned tool for the activity.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, required: false): Upload the output file for the activity.
* **Category** (`category`, text, required: false): Enter the category for the activity.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Enter the description of the activity.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, required: false): Enter the form ID for the activity.
* **Form Category** (`form_category`, text, required: false): Enter the form category for the activity.
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter the form title for the activity.
* **Output File 2** (`output_file_2`, text, required: false): Upload the output file 2 for the activity.
* **Assigned Tool 2** (`assigned_tool_2`, select_one, required: false): Select the assigned tool 2 for the activity.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Enter the description of the activity.
* **Form Title 2** (`form_title_2`, text, required: false): Enter the form title 2 for the activity.
* **Form Id 2** (`form_id_2`, number, required: false): Enter the form ID 2 for the activity.
* **Form Category 2** (`form_category_2`, text, required: false): Enter the form category 2 for the activity.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, text, required: false): Enter the category 2 for the activity.
