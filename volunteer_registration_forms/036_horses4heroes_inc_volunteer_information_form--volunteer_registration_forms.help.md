# horses4heroes_inc_volunteer_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to volunteer with Horse4Heroes Inc.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Volunteer Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Email** in the email address field, following the standard email format (username@example.com).
3. If you have a **Phone**, enter it in the text field. This is optional.
4. Provide your **Mailing Address**, including **City** and **State**. Please note that you are required to enter your state.
5. Enter your **Zip** code.
6. In the **Volunteer Information** field, provide any relevant details about your volunteering experience or skills.
7. For the **Mailing Address Line 1** and **Line 2**, enter your address lines 1 and 2, respectively.
8. Enter your **Mailing Address City** and **State**.
9. Enter your **Mailing Address Zip**.
10. If you wish to volunteer, enter your desired **Volunteer Hours** in the number field.
11. Select a **Date** and enter your desired volunteer time in the **Time** field.
12. Choose which days you are available to volunteer by selecting multiple days from the options provided.
13. Enter your desired **Start Time** and **End Time** for volunteering.
14. Select a **Start Date** and **End Date** for your volunteering period.
15. Indicate your **Volunteer Status** by selecting between **Active** and **Inactive**.
16. If applicable, enter your desired **Volunteer Position** in the text field.
17. In the **Volunteer Note** field, provide any additional comments or details.
18. Finally, confirm your understanding and agreement to the **Volunteer Agreement** by selecting the appropriate option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name** (`Volunteer Name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it should be addressed.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your standard email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
* **Mailing Address** (`Mailing Address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`State`, select_one, required): Select your state of residence.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, number, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Volunteer Information** (`Volunteer Information`, note, optional): Enter any relevant details about your volunteering experience or skills.
* **Mailing Address Line 1** (`Mailing Address Line 1`, text, optional): Enter line 1 of your address.
* **Mailing Address Line 2** (`Mailing Address Line 2`, text, optional): Enter line 2 of your address.
* **Mailing Address City** (`Mailing Address City`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **Mailing Address State** (`Mailing Address State`, text, optional): Enter your state of residence.
* **Mailing Address Zip** (`Mailing Address Zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`Volunteer Hours`, number, optional): Enter your desired volunteer hours.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Select a date for volunteering.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Enter your desired volunteer time.
* **Days** (`Days`, select_multiple, required): Select all the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required): Enter your desired start time for volunteering.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required): Enter your desired end time for volunteering.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, required): Select a start date for your volunteering period.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, required): Select an end date for your volunteering period.
* **Volunteer Status** (`Volunteer Status`, select_one, required): Indicate your volunteer status as **Active** or **Inactive**.
* **Volunteer Position** (`Volunteer Position`, text, optional): Enter your desired volunteer position, if applicable.
* **Volunteer Note** (`Volunteer Note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details.
* **Volunteer Agreement** (`Volunteer Agreement`, select_multiple, required): Confirm your understanding and agreement to the volunteer agreement.
