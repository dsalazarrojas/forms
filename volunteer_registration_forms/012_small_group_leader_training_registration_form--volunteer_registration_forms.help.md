# Small Group Leader Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect details from individuals interested in participating in small group leader training sessions, which are likely used for organizational events or programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Training Information** (Page 1). This section asks for general details about the training session you are interested in, including the session type and time. Please select the correct session type from the available options and enter the desired time for the training session.
2. Fill in your **Personal Details** (Pages 2-9). This section requests your name, email, phone number, and other personal details. Please ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review and submit your form. Once you have filled out all the required fields, review your form for accuracy and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Training Information (Page 1)**
* **Training Session**: Select the session type that interests you. Please choose from Session A, Session B, or Session C.
* **Training Time**: Enter the time you would like to attend the training session.
* **Training Date**: Select the date for the training session.

### **Personal Details (Pages 2-9)**
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be known.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for updates or communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further questions or clarifications.
* **Location**: If you have any specific location preferences for the training session, enter them here. 
* **Notes**: If you have any additional comments or preferences, please enter them here.
