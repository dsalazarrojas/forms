# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from volunteers for specific events. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the event details, including event date, time, location, and description.
2. Provide your contact information, including name, email, phone number, and emergency contact details.
3. Specify your volunteer type (Volunteer, Staff, Artist) and status (Active, Inactive, Pending).
4. Choose your volunteer role (Stage, Security, Medical, Artist Liaison).
5. Select your shift details, including start and end times.
6. Indicate whether you have any vehicles.
7. Provide any additional notes or information about your availability or needs.
8. Finally, confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: (`event_details`, text, optional) - Enter the details of the event you will be volunteering for. This includes the date, time, location, and description of the event.
* **Contact Details**: (`contact_details`, text, optional) - This is your contact information, including name, email, phone number, and emergency contact details.
* **Volunteer Info**: (`volunteer_info`, text, optional) - Provide any additional information about yourself as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Type**: (`volunteer_type`, select_one, optional) - Choose your volunteer type, which can be Volunteer, Staff, or Artist.
* **Volunteer Status**: (`volunteer_status`, select_one, optional) - Select your volunteer status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Volunteer Role**: (`volunteer_role`, select_one, optional) - Choose your volunteer role, which can be Stage, Security, Medical, or Artist Liaison.
* **Shift Details**: (`shift_details`, text, optional) - Provide any additional details about your shift, including start and end times.
* **Vehicles**: (`volunteer_vehicles`, select_multiple, optional) - Indicate whether you have any vehicles.
* **Notes**: (`volunteer_notes`, text, optional) - Provide any additional notes or information about your availability or needs.
* **Email**: (`volunteer_email`, email, optional) - Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: (`volunteer_phone`, text, optional) - Enter your phone number.
* **Experience**: (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional) - Provide any relevant experience you have as a volunteer.
* **Emergency Contact**: (`volunteer_emergency_contact`, text, optional) - Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: (`volunteer_emergency_phone`, text, optional) - Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email**: (`volunteer_emergency_email`, email, optional) - Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Info**: (`volunteer_medical_info`, text, optional) - Provide any medical information that may be relevant to your volunteering.
* **Agreement**: (`volunteer_agreement`, select_one, optional) - Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Additional Info**: (`volunteer_additional_info`, text, optional) - Provide any additional information that may be relevant to your volunteering.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your volunteering experience is successful and enjoyable.
