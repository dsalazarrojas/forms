# Volunteer Time Verification Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Time Verification Request Form is used to verify the time volunteered by a volunteer for a specific service. It helps ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the volunteer's details in the "Volunteer Details" field.
2. Enter the service details in the "Service Details" field.
3. Select the dates of service in the "Service Dates" field.
4. Enter the number of hours volunteered in the "Service Hours" field.
5. Choose the supervisor's signature from the "Supervisor Signature" dropdown menu.
6. (Optional) If you are the person submitting the verification request, enter your name in the "Submitted By" field.
7. (Optional) If you are submitting the request on a specific date and time, enter the date and time in the "Submitted On" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Details** (Volunteer Details, text, required): Enter the name and any other relevant details of the volunteer who provided the service.
* **Service Details** (Service Details, text, required): Enter a brief description of the service provided by the volunteer.
* **Service Dates** (Service Dates, date, required): Select the dates on which the volunteer provided the service.
* **Service Hours** (Service Hours, number, required): Enter the total number of hours the volunteer spent providing the service.
* **Supervisor Signature** (Supervisor Signature, select_multiple, required): Select the supervisor who will be signing off on the verification request.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, optional): If you are submitting the verification request, enter your name here.
* **Submitted On** (Submitted On, time, optional): If you are submitting the request on a specific date and time, enter it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with the volunteer or supervisor before submitting the form.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
