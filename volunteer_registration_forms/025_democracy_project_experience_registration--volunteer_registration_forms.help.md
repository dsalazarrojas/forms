# Democracy Project Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your experience with democracy projects. Please fill it out to help us understand your involvement and contributions to such projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your registration details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select your interest in democracy projects.
3. Provide details about your project experiences, including the project type, organization, position, dates, start and end times, and any notes you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Name** (`registration_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it would be displayed in our records.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it would be displayed when contacting you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Interest** (`interest`, `text`, required): Select one option that best describes your interest in democracy projects:
	+ Option 1: Community Development
	+ Option 2: Civic Engagement
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `text`, required): Describe the type of project you are involved with (e.g., "Community Outreach", "Policy Analysis", etc.).
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the organization you are involved with.
* **Position** (`position`, `text`, required): Describe your position or role within the organization (e.g., "Project Manager", "Volunteer", etc.).
* **Project Date** (`project_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you started and ended your project involvement (MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of your project (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of your project (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share about your project experience.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Select one option from the provided list:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more options from the provided list:
	+ Option 1: Option 1
	+ Option 2: Option 2
	+ Option 3: Option 3

**Tips**
* Please ensure that you have already created an account with us before filling this form.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling this form, feel free to contact us for support.
* If you have already registered as an individual and would like to update your information, please fill out this form again.
