# Volunteer Badge Printing Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Volunteer Badge Printing Request" form is used to request a badge for a volunteer. To use this form, you should be a volunteer or an authorized representative of the volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and contact information.
2. Enter the name of the volunteer for whom the badge is requested.
3. Upload a photo of the volunteer.
4. Select the access level of the badge.
5. Choose the type of badge (Standard or Premium).
6. Add any additional comments about the request.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Badge Printing Request** (`volunteer_badge_printing_request`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is automatically generated and does not require input.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as the person submitting the request.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the volunteer for whom the badge is requested.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or the volunteer.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person or the volunteer.
* **Photo URL** (`photo_url`, text, optional): Upload a URL of the volunteer's photo. This field is optional, but it will help with badge production.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_multiple, required): Select the access level of the badge. You can choose from Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Badge Type** (`badge_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of badge you want to print (Standard or Premium).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about the request.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): This field is automatically generated and does not require input.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure successful badge production.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the volunteer program administrators for clarification.
* Please be patient and allow for 2-5 business days for badge production and delivery.
