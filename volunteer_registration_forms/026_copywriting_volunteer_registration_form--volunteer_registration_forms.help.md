# Copywriting Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copywriting Volunteer Registration Form is for individuals interested in volunteering their writing skills to the organization. It is used to gather information about potential volunteers and their writing experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as it would be displayed on official documents.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
3. Enter your phone number for us to reach you about volunteer tasks.
4. Select your availability for volunteer work (this can be a specific date or date range).
5. (Optional) Upload a sample of your writing work (e.g., a blog post, article, or social media post).
6. (Optional) Select up to three writing samples from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (Page 1, Text): Enter a brief description of why you're interested in volunteering your copywriting skills. This is not a required field.
* **Name** (Page 1, Text): Enter your name as it would be displayed on official documents.
* **Email** (Page 1, Email): Enter your email address so we can contact you about volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone** (Page 1, Text): Enter your phone number for us to reach you about volunteer tasks.
* **Availability** (Page 2, Date): Select a date or date range when you are available to volunteer. This can be a specific date, a range of dates (e.g., weekly, monthly), or a time period (e.g., weekdays, weekends).
* **Writing samples** (Page 3, Text): (Optional) Upload a sample of your writing work. This could be a blog post, article, or social media post.
* **writing samples sample 1** (Page 3, Select One): (Optional) If you have multiple writing samples, select this option as a sample of your work. You can choose one of the provided options.
* **writing samples sample 2** (Page 4, Select Multiple): (Optional) If you have multiple writing samples, select up to three options as a sample of your work.
* **writing samples sample 3** (Page 4, Select Multiple): (Optional) If you have multiple writing samples, select up to three options as a sample of your work.

## Tips
- Please ensure your email address is accurate and up to date.
- Make sure to provide a valid phone number.
- Only upload samples that best represent your writing skills.
- Choose up to three writing samples for the organization to review.
- If you have any questions, reach out to us and we will be happy to help.
