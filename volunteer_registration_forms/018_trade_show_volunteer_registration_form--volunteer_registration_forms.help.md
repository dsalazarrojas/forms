# Trade Show Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect information from volunteers who can help with trade shows. By filling out this form, you'll be able to register your availability and preferences to help the trade show organizers with staffing and scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form in a single session for the best experience.
3. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask the trade show organizers for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed on the trade show's volunteer schedule.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address so the trade show organizers can contact you with further instructions and updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so the trade show organizers can contact you if needed.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_one, not required): Choose the shift you're available to work. Options are:
	+ Early Morning
	+ Late Afternoon
	+ All Day
* **Shift Hours** (`shift_hours`, time, not required): If you selected All Day, you can specify your preferred start and end hours. Otherwise, this field will be ignored.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the role(s) you're interested in. Options are:
	+ Event Coordinator
	+ Event Host
	+ Volunteer
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, not required): If you know the dates you're available to work, enter them here. This will help the trade show organizers schedule you more efficiently.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, not required): If you have any experience with tools or equipment, choose them here so the trade show organizers can assign you tasks accordingly.
* **Trade Show Name** (`trade_show_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the trade show you're registering for, if known.

## Tips
* Please make sure to check the trade show's schedule and availability before registering.
* If you're registering for multiple trade shows, you can fill out this form multiple times, but please make sure to keep your information up-to-date.
