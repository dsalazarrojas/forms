# Conservation Heroes Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register volunteers for conservation activities. It helps us understand your contact information, motivation, availability, skills, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your registration date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your official ID.
3. Choose your preferred name (optional).
4. Provide a valid email address that we can use for communication.
5. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
6. Fill in your mailing address.
7. Enter your city and state/province.
8. Provide your postal code (ZIP or area code).
9. Fill in your date of birth for age verification purposes.
10. Check if you are under 18 and provide the name and contact information of your parent or guardian if applicable.
11. Check your availability for volunteering, selecting all that apply.
12. Choose which program(s) interest you the most.
13. Select all the relevant skills you have that can be applied to conservation activities.
14. If you selected "Other" for your skills, provide a description in the field below.
15. Indicate your physical ability level, taking into account the demands of field work.
16. If you have any physical limitations, provide more information in this field.
17. Check if you have reliable transportation.
18. Check if you are registering as a group and provide the group size and name if applicable.
19. Choose your T-Shirt size for volunteer events.
20. Check if you consent to a background check if required.
21. Check if you accept the liability waiver terms.
22. Check if we can use photos of you.
23. Select how you heard about us.
24. Provide any additional comments or information you think we should know.
25. Sign your name to confirm your registration.
26. Check your registration status for our administrative records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Date** (1, `registration_date`, required): Date of registration in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
*   **Full Name** (2, `volunteer_name`, required): Your full name as it appears on your official ID.
*   **Preferred Name** (3, `preferred_name`, required): Your preferred name to be used for communication (optional).
*   **Email Address** (4, `email`, required): A valid email address for communication.
*   **Phone Number** (5, `phone`, required): A valid phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Mailing Address** (6, `address`, required): Your physical address for communication.
*   **City** (7, `city`, required): Your city of residence.
*   **State/Province** (8, `state`, required): Your state or province of residence.
*   **Postal Code** (9, `postal_code`, required): Your ZIP or postal code.
*   **Date of Birth** (10, `date_of_birth`, required): Date of birth for age verification purposes.
*   **Are You Under 18** (11, `is_minor`, required): Check if you are under 18, and if so, provide the name and contact information of your parent or guardian.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (12, `parent_guardian_name`, optional): Name of parent or guardian if you are under 18.
*   **Parent or Guardian Phone** (13, `parent_guardian_phone`, optional): Phone number of parent or guardian for contact purposes if you are under 18.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (14, `emergency_contact_name`, required): Name of emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (15, `emergency_contact_phone`, required): Phone number of emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (16, `emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Relationship to you of the emergency contact person.
*   **Why Do You Want to Volunteer** (17, `volunteer_interest`, required): Your motivation for volunteering with us.
*   **Availability** (18, `availability`, required): Check all the days you are available to volunteer.
*   **Program Preference** (19, `program_preference`, required): Select the program that interests you the most.
*   **Relevant Skills** (20, `skills`, required): Select all the relevant skills you can contribute to conservation activities.
*   **Other Skills** (21, `skills_other`, optional): Provide a description of other skills you can contribute.
*   **Physical Ability Level** (22, `physical_ability`, required): Indicate your physical ability level for field work.
*   **Physical Limitations** (23, `limitations`, optional): Provide any physical limitations you may have for field work.
*   **Do You Have Vehicle Access** (24, `vehicle_access`, required): Check if you have reliable transportation.
*   **Are You Registering a Group** (25, `group_registration`, required): Check if you are registering as a group.
*   **Group Size** (26, `group_size`, optional): Provide the size of your group if you are registering as a group.
*   **Group Name** (27, `group_name`, optional): Provide the name of your group if you are registering as a group.
*   **T-Shirt Size** (28, `t_shirt_size`, optional): Choose your T-Shirt size for volunteer events.
*   **Consent to Background Check** (29, `background_check_consent`, required): Check if you consent to a background check if required.
*   **Liability Waiver Accepted** (30, `liability_waiver`, required): Check if you accept the liability waiver terms.
*   **Photo Release** (31, `photo_release`, required): Check if we can use photos of you.
*   **How Did You Hear About Us** (32, `how_did_you_hear`, optional): Select how you heard about us.
*   **Additional Comments** (33, `additional_comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information you think we should know.
*   **Your Signature** (34, `volunteer_signature`, required): Sign your name to confirm your registration.
*   **Registration Status** (35, `registration_status`, required): Check your registration status for our administrative records.
