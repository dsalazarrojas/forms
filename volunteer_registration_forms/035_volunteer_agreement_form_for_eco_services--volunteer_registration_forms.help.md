# Volunteer Agreement Form For Eco Services - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for Eco Services, allowing them to provide their details and preferences for potential opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Volunteer Details** section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
2. In the **Roles** section, select one or more roles that you are interested in or willing to take on as a volunteer.
3. Please provide additional information about yourself in the **Volunteer Details 2** section, if needed.
4. Read and agree to the terms of the **Agreement** section, which outlines the responsibilities and expectations of volunteering with Eco Services.
5. If you have any additional information you'd like to share, you can do so in the **Additional Information** section.

* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_agreement_form_for_eco_services_page_1`, text, optional): Enter your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Roles** (`volunteer_agreement_form_for_eco_services_page_2`, text, optional): Select one or more roles that you are interested in or willing to take on as a volunteer. This could include areas such as event planning, social media management, or data entry.
* **Volunteer Details 2** (`volunteer_agreement_form_for_eco_services_page_3`, text, optional): This section is for providing additional information about yourself, if needed. You can share your skills, qualifications, or any other relevant details that you think would be helpful for the volunteer management team.
* **Agreement** (`volunteer_agreement_form_for_eco_services_page_4`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms of the agreement, which includes responsibilities and expectations for volunteering with Eco Services.
* **Additional Information** (`volunteer_agreement_form_for_eco_services_page_5`, text, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to share, you can do so in this section.
