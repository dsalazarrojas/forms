# Aquarium Volunteer Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for people who want to volunteer at the aquarium. It allows them to provide personal information, specify their volunteer preferences, and share their availability and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, and email address.
2. Specify your preferred volunteer role(s) from the list provided.
3. Share your availability, choosing between weekdays, weekends, or both.
4. Indicate how many hours you can dedicate per week.
5. Explain why you want to volunteer at the aquarium.
6. If you have any special skills or talents, let us know.
7. Finally, please indicate if you consent to a background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, as it should appear on our records.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth as it is, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Home Address**: This is your address where we can send mail or communicate with you.
* **Volunteer Preferences**: Choose the volunteer role(s) that interest you the most.
* **Relevant Experience**: Share any relevant experience you have that might be useful for your volunteer work.
* **Availability**: Select your available days, either weekdays, weekends, or both.
* **Hours Available Per Week**: Enter the number of hours you can dedicate per week.
* **Why Do You Want to Volunteer**: Explain in a few words why you want to volunteer at the aquarium.
* **Special Skills or Talents**: If you have any special skills or talents, share them with us.
* **Consent to Background Check**: Please select "Yes" to consent to a background check, as required by our policies.
