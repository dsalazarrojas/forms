# Volunteer Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Candidate Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from individuals interested in volunteering for our organization. This form helps us understand your personal details, skills, experience, and volunteer type preferences, which are then used to match you with suitable volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the personal details section with your name and contact information.
2. Select your skills and experience to help us understand your qualifications.
3. Choose your availability for volunteering (weekdays, weekends, or all days).
4. Indicate your volunteer type preference (Volunteer, Staff, or Intern).
5. Confirm your email and phone number for verification purposes.
6. Leave any additional notes or comments in the notes section.
7. Finally, submit the form and confirm your volunteer status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details (1)** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and other personal details.
* **Candidate Name (2)** (`candidate_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be known.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your primary email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your primary phone number for verification purposes.
* **Skills (5)** (`skills`, select multiple, optional): Select the skills you have and would like to use for volunteering (Yes/No).
* **Experience (6)** (`experience`, select one, optional): Indicate your level of experience (Less than 1 year, 1-2 years, More than 2 years).
* **Availability (7)** (`availability`, select multiple, optional): Choose your availability for volunteering (Weekdays, Weekends, All).
* **Volunteer Type (8)** (`volunteer_type`, select one, optional): Select your preference for volunteer type (Volunteer, Staff, Intern).
* **Confirm Email (9)** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Re-enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Phone Number (10)** (`confirm_phone`, text, optional): Re-enter your phone number for verification purposes.
* **Notes (11)** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your volunteer interest.
* **Confirm Volunteer (12)** (`confirm_volunteer`, select one, optional): Confirm your interest in volunteering (Yes/No).
* **Submit (13)** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter a brief message about your interest in volunteering.
* **Submit Comment (14)** (`submit_comment`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about your volunteer interest.
* **Volunteer Type Other (15)** (`volunteer_type_other`, select one, optional): Indicate your interest in other volunteer types (Yes/No).
* **Volunteer Type Other Input (16)** (`volunteer_type_other_input`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your other volunteer type interest.
* **Volunteer Type Other Input 2 (17)** (`volunteer_type_other_input2`, text, optional): Enter another brief description of your other volunteer type interest.
* **Volunteer Type Other Input 3 (18)** (`volunteer_type_other_input3`, text, optional): Enter yet another brief description of your other volunteer type interest.
* **Volunteer Type Other Input 4 (19)** (`volunteer_type_other_input4`, text, optional): Enter additional details about your other volunteer type interest.
* **Volunteer Type Other Input 5 (20)** (`volunteer_type_other_input5`, text, optional): Enter more details about your other volunteer type interest.
* **Volunteer Type Other Input 6 (21)** (`volunteer_type_other_input6`, text, optional): Enter further details about your other volunteer type interest.

By following this guide, you should be able to successfully complete the volunteer candidate registration form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
