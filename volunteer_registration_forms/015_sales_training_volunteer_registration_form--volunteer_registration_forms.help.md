# Sales Training Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for volunteers who want to participate in sales training programs. Fill out this form to register for a training program that suits your background and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select your sales experience level.
4. Choose the training programs you are interested in.
5. Select the training topics you want to learn about.
6. Enter your preferred date and time for the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (2)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for calls and reminders.
* **Sales Experience (4)**: Select the level of sales experience you have:
	+ No Experience: You have no sales experience.
	+ 1-2 years: You have 1-2 years of sales experience.
	+ 2-5 years: You have 2-5 years of sales experience.
	+ More than 5 years: You have more than 5 years of sales experience.
* **Training Programs (5)**: Select the training programs you are interested in:
	+ Basic: A training program for those with little to no sales experience.
	+ Intermediate: A training program for those with some sales experience.
	+ Advanced: A training program for experienced sales representatives.
* **Training Topics (6)**: Select the topics you are interested in learning about:
	+ Product Knowledge: Learning about products and their features.
	+ Sales Skills: Learning about sales techniques and strategies.
	+ Leadership: Learning about leadership and management skills.
* **Date (7)**: Enter your preferred date for the training. The date format is dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time (8)**: Enter your preferred time for the training. The time format is hh:mm.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Check your selections carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the training team.
