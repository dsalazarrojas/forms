# Citizen Science Project Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from volunteers interested in participating in the Citizen Science Project. The form collects personal details, project interests, scientific background, availability, skills, and motivation to help us understand your suitability for the project.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  First, provide your personal details by filling out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Age Group.
2.  Select your interested projects by choosing all that apply from the options provided.
3.  Describe your research interests in the "Describe Your Research Interests" field.
4.  Indicate your highest level of education, science experience, and areas of science background.
5.  Choose your availability in terms of hours per week and commitment duration.
6.  Select your skills and expertise, transportation available, and preferred communication method.
7.  Finally, express your motivation for participating in the project and what you'd like to learn from it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please fill in your first name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Last Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Please fill in your last name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email Address** (`4`, `email`, required): Please enter your active email address where we can contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you.
*   **Age Group** (`6`, `select_one`, optional): Select your age group from the options provided.
*   **Which Project Are You Interested In** (`8`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the projects that interest you from the options provided.
*   **Describe Your Research Interests** (`9`, `text`, optional): Please describe your interests and areas of research in this field.
*   **Highest Level of Education** (`11`, `select_one`, optional): Select your highest level of education from the options provided.
*   **Science or Research Experience** (`12`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your level of science or research experience from the options provided.
*   **Areas of Science Background** (`13`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the areas of science that you have background in from the options provided.
*   **Hours per Week You Can Dedicate** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Select the number of hours per week you can dedicate to the project.
*   **Commitment Duration** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Select the commitment duration from the options provided.
*   **Days of Week You Are Available** (`17`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days of the week you are available to participate in the project.
*   **Relevant Skills** (`19`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the skills that apply to your expertise from the options provided.
*   **Technical Proficiency** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your level of technical proficiency from the options provided.
*   **Equipment You Have Access To** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the equipment you have access to from the options provided.
*   **Transportation Available** (`23`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate whether you have transportation available from the options provided.
*   **What Motivates You to Participate** (`25`, `text`, required): Please describe what motivates you to participate in the project.
*   **What Would You Like to Learn** (`26`, `text`, optional): Describe what you'd like to learn from participating in the project.
*   **Preferred Communication Method** (`27`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred communication method from the options provided.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please refer to the explanations above for guidance.
*   Take your time to fill out the form carefully, and don't hesitate to ask if you need help or clarification.
