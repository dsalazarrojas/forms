# Support Children Initiative Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers information from users who wish to participate in the Support Children Initiative. It is used to register participants and their roles within the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your basic contact information (name and email).
2. Select your organisation from the options in the Organisation section, or leave it blank if you don't have any affiliation.
3. In the Date and Time section, enter your availability to participate in the initiative.
4. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.
5. Select your role within the initiative (Volunteer, Donor, Supporter) from the Supporter Type section.
6. If you are representing an organisation, select the level at which you represent it (Individual or Organisation) in the Supporter Level section.
7. Click the Submit button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and email address to register your basic contact information.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed within the initiative.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications from the initiative.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you are available to participate in the initiative.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter your available time slots to participate in the initiative.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your participation.
* **Organisation** (`select_organisation`, select one, required/optional): Select the organisation you represent from the options provided.
* **Multiple Organisations** (`select_multiple_organisations`, select multiple, required/optional): Select any additional organisations you represent (leave blank if you don't have any affiliations).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to receive calls or messages from the initiative.
* **Supporter Type** (`supporter_type`, select one, required/optional): Select your role within the initiative (Volunteer, Donor, Supporter).
* **Supporter Level** (`supporter_level`, select one, required/optional): Select the level at which you represent your organisation (Individual or Organisation).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to complete and submit your registration.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to register your participation. If you have any questions or issues with the form, reach out to the initiative's team for assistance.
