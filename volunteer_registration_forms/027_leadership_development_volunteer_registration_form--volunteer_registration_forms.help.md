# Leadership Development Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Leadership Development Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers interested in participating in leadership development opportunities within the organization. By filling out this form, you can express your interest in becoming a volunteer and provide essential details about yourself to help us manage our volunteer program effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the designated fields.
2. Provide a valid Email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and notifications.
3. Enter your Phone Number for any follow-up communication.
4. Indicate your volunteer interests by selecting all that apply.
5. Select whether you have Work Experience.
6. Choose a valid Date for your available time slots.
7. Mention any Availability or Special Skills you have.
8. List any Special Skills you may have.
9. Specify any Languages Spoken, Read, or Write.
10. Enter any Other Languages you may know.
11. Provide an estimate of Available Time for your volunteer work.
12. Specify any Available Days for your volunteer work.
13. Mention the Volunteer Location where you are willing to volunteer.
14. Enter any Notes about your volunteer experience or interests.
15. Provide an estimate of Volunteer Hours you can dedicate.
16. Select the Volunteer Frequency of your available time slots.
17. Enter your Volunteer Ending, which is your last day of volunteering.
18. Provide a Volunteer Reference or Referral if applicable.
19. Add any Additional Info about yourself that would be helpful for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for us to contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Volunteer Interests** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the areas you are interested in volunteering for, such as "Yes" for all relevant options.
* **Work Experience** (Select One, Required): Indicate whether you have work experience. If you have, select "Yes," else "No."
* **Available Date** (Date, Required): Choose a valid date for your available time slots.
* **Availability** (Text, Optional): Mention any specific availability or time constraints you have.
* **Skills** (Text, Optional): List any skills you have that may be relevant to volunteer work.
* **Languages Spoken** (Text, Optional): Enter any languages you are fluent in speaking.
* **Languages Read** (Text, Optional): Enter any languages you are fluent in reading.
* **Languages Write** (Text, Optional): Enter any languages you are fluent in writing.
* **Other Languages** (Text, Optional): Enter any other languages you may know.
* **Available Time** (Time, Optional): Provide an estimate of the time you can dedicate to volunteer work.
* **Available Days** (Text, Optional): Specify any days you are available for volunteer work.
* **Volunteer Location** (Text, Optional): Mention the location where you are willing to volunteer.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or information about your volunteer experience or interests.
* **Volunteer Hours** (Text, Optional): Enter an estimate of the volunteer hours you can dedicate.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (Text, Optional): Select the frequency of your available time slots for volunteer work.
* **Volunteer Ending** (Text, Optional): Enter your last day of volunteering.
* **Volunteer Reference** (Text, Optional): Provide a volunteer reference or referral if applicable.
* **Volunteer Referral** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your volunteer referral.
* **Additional Info** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about yourself that would be helpful for us to know.
