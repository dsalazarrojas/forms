# Water Conservation Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Conservation Volunteer Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in water conservation initiatives. This form will be used to gather contact and volunteer details to ensure smooth communication and proper allocation of volunteers for water conservation activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Scroll down to the section that corresponds to the type of information you want to provide (User Details or Volunteer Details or Availability).
2.  Fill out the relevant fields with your information.
3.  Once you have filled out all required fields, review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required/optional): This section is used to gather basic contact information from you, the volunteer. Please fill in your name, email address, and any other information you feel is relevant.
* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect information about your volunteering preferences, such as the types of activities you are interested in or have experience with. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, required/optional): This section will help us understand your availability for water conservation activities. Be as specific as possible, including the dates and times you are available.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide your consent to participate in water conservation activities. This is a requirement for all volunteers.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you need help with filling out this form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
