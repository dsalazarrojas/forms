# Mental Health Support Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in volunteering their time and skills to support mental health causes. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your interest in volunteering and provide us with essential information about your availability and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all required fields (indicated by **required**).
2. Fill out all relevant fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Application** (`volunteer_application`): A brief introduction to your volunteer application.
* **Name** (`name`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Organization** (`organization`): If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter the name of the organization.
* **Role** (`role`): Check "Yes" if you have experience or skills that align with our volunteer opportunities. If you don't have direct experience, check "No" and we will discuss further.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the date you would be available to start volunteering, if applicable.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the date you would be available to end volunteering, if applicable.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`): If you have a specific schedule in mind, please enter it here.
* **Availability** (`availability`): Describe your availability in a few sentences.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`): Share any relevant volunteer experience or skills you think would be beneficial for our organization.
* **Skills** (`skills`): List any skills you think would be valuable for our organization.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): If you would like someone to be contacted in case of an emergency, please enter their name and relation to you.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Notes** (`emergency_notes`): If you have any additional notes about your emergency contact, please enter them here.
* **Volunteer Notes** (`volunteer_notes`): Share any additional information that might be helpful for us to know about you as a volunteer.

## Tips
* Make sure to review each field carefully and answer all questions to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
