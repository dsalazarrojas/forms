# Hospital Volunteer Training Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from users who wish to register for the Hospital Volunteer Training Day. This includes personal details, contact information, and session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you're registering for the correct sessions and providing accurate contact information.
2. Enter your personal details in the "User Info" field.
3. Select the sessions you wish to attend from the "Sessions" field.
4. Enter your contact information, including email, phone number, and address in the "Contact Info" field.
5. Verify your email address and phone number format in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
6. Submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (required)**: Enter your name, title, and hospital ID or department name.
* **Sessions (required)**: Select all sessions you wish to attend. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Contact Info (optional)**: Enter your contact details for future reference.
* **Email (optional):** Enter your email address.
* **Phone (optional):** Enter your phone number.
* **Address (optional):** Enter your address.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your session selections carefully before submitting the form.
* Enter accurate and complete contact information to ensure you receive updates and information about the training day.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please reach out to the volunteer program team for assistance.
