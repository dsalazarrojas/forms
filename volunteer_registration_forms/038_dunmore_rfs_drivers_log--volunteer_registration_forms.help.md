# dunmore_rfs_drivers_log - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dunmore_rfs_drivers_log form is designed to collect essential information from volunteer drivers for the purpose of creating a record of their vehicle and emergency contact details. This record will help the organization keep track of its volunteer drivers and provide necessary support in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have permission to access this form. This form is only intended for actual drivers with a vehicle.
2. Fill out the form with your first name, last name, and any other required information.
3. If you have a driver's license, provide your driver's license number and state.
4. If you have a vehicle, provide your vehicle's make, model, year, license plate, and color.
5. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and email address.
6. Indicate whether your vehicle is used for primary or secondary purposes.
7. If applicable, list your medical conditions and any relevant insurance details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This is your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This is your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the required format.
* **Driver's License Number** (`drivers_license_number`, text, optional): If you have a driver's license, enter its number here.
* **Driver's License State** (`drivers_license_state`, select_one, optional): Choose your driver's license state from the list.
* **Driver's License Expiration Date** (`drivers_license_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter your driver's license expiration date if you have one.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's first name.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any medical conditions you have.
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, select_one, optional): If you have a vehicle, choose its make from the list.
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, optional): Enter your vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, optional): Enter your vehicle's color.
* **Primary Use** (`primary_use`, text, optional): Describe the primary use of your vehicle.
* **Secondary Use** (`secondary_use`, text, optional): Describe any secondary use of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, text, optional): Describe your vehicle's condition.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Details** (`emergency_contact_relationship_details`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact in more detail.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, optional): Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, text, optional): Enter your policy number with your insurance provider.
