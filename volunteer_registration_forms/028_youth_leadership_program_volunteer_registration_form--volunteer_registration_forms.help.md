# Youth Leadership Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Leadership Program Volunteer Registration Form is a tool for volunteers to register for the program. It collects information on contact details and volunteer interests to match volunteers with suitable roles and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your volunteer interests in the Volunteer Interests field.
6. Enter your availability in the Availability field.
7. Confirm your availability in the Confirm Availability field.
8. Select your volunteer committee interests in the Volunteer Committee Interests field.
9. Select your skills in the Skills field.
10. Choose your volunteer leadership role in the Volunteer Leadership Role field.
11. Enter a confirmation in the Confirmation field.
12. Click the Submit button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name to complete your registration.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name to complete your registration.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to ensure you can be contacted about program updates and information.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format of "XXX-XXX-XXXX" for contact purposes.

### Volunteer Interests
* **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests`, select_one, optional): Select one or more of the available volunteer interests:
	+ Community Service
	+ Arts
	+ Athletics
	+ Environmental

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, optional): Select your available time slots for volunteer work in the format of "HH:MM".

### Confirm Availability
* **Confirm Availability** (`confirm_availability`, time, optional): Confirm your available time slots by re-selecting the same time slot.

### Volunteer Committee Interests
* **Volunteer Committee Interests** (`volunteer_committee_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available volunteer committee interests:
	+ Arts
	+ Athletics
	+ Community Service
	+ Environmental

### Skills
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available skills:
	+ Leadership
	+ Teamwork
	+ Communication
	+ Time Management
	+ Organization

### Volunteer Leadership Role
* **Volunteer Leadership Role** (`volunteer_leadership_role`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the available leadership roles:
	+ Team Leader
	+ Team Member
	+ Committee Chair
	+ Committee Member
	+ Officer

### Confirmation
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, optional): Enter a confirmation message for your registration.

### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your registration.
