# ethics_review_board_volunteer_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ethics_review_board_volunteer_registration form is used to register volunteers for the Ethics Review Board. Please fill out this form to provide your information and indicate your interest in serving on the board.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and enter your information accurately.
2. Fill out the Volunteer Information page with your basic contact details.
3. On the Availability page, select your availability for serving on the board.
4. On the Ethics Review Board page, choose the specific board you are interested in serving on.
5. On the Non Profits page, select any non-profit organizations you are affiliated with.
6. On the Additional Details page, provide any additional information that may be helpful.
7. On the Email and Phone page, enter your contact information.
8. On the Consent page, indicate your consent to serve on the board.
9. On the Review Boards page, select the boards you are interested in serving on.
10. On the Non-Profit Board page, select your affiliation with non-profit boards.
11. On the Volunteer Details page, provide any additional information about your volunteer experience.
12. On the University and Hospital pages, select your affiliations with these institutions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information**: Enter your basic contact details, including your name, address, and other contact information.
* **Availability**: Indicate your availability to serve on the board, such as your schedule or frequency of availability.
* **Ethics Review Board**: Choose the specific Ethics Review Board you are interested in serving on. This will help us understand your area of expertise and interest.
* **Non Profits**: Select any non-profit organizations you are affiliated with, such as University or Hospital. This will help us understand your background and potential conflicts of interest.
* **Additional Details**: Provide any additional information that may be helpful in reviewing your application.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Consent**: Indicate your consent to serve on the board. Please note that serving on the board requires a commitment to maintain confidentiality and follow board policies.
* **Review Boards**: Select the boards you are interested in serving on. This will help us understand your area of expertise and interest.
* **Non-Profit Board**: Select your affiliation with non-profit boards. This will help us understand your experience and potential conflicts of interest.
* **Volunteer Details**: Provide any additional information about your volunteer experience that may be helpful in reviewing your application.
* **University**: Select your affiliation with University. This will help us understand your academic background.
* **Hospital**: Select your affiliation with Hospital. This will help us understand your medical background.

Please review each field carefully and submit the form only once. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
