# Disaster Relief Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Disaster Relief Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who want to help with disaster relief efforts. The form helps us match volunteers with the right opportunities and ensures that we have accurate contact information in case of further communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions about your personal and professional details, such as your first and last name, email, phone number, and skills.
2. Select the location and type of disaster you are able to work with (e.g., Hurricane, Tornado, Wildfire).
3. Provide your availability for the disaster relief efforts, including start and end dates.
4. If you have a message or notes to share with the organization, you can add them in the space provided.
5. Confirm that you are able to volunteer by agreeing to be contacted by us for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **I am able to work at any disaster location** (`disaster_relief_volunteer`, text, optional): This field is for you to indicate that you are willing to travel to different locations for disaster relief efforts. Please select "Yes" to indicate your availability.
 
- **Volunteer First Name** (`volunteer_first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name to help us identify you correctly.
 
- **Volunteer Last Name** (`volunteer_last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name to help us identify you correctly.
 
- **Volunteer E-mail** (`volunteer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with further information.
 
- **Volunteer Phone** (`volunteer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you quickly.
 
- **Disaster Location** (`disaster_location`, select_one, optional): Select the type of disaster location you are able to work with (e.g., Hurricane, Tornado, Wildfire).
 
- **Disaster Skill** (`disaster_skill`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the skills you possess that would be useful during disaster relief efforts (e.g., Medical, Search and Rescue, Communication).
 
- **Disaster Start Date** (`disaster_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start your volunteer work.
 
- **Disaster End Date** (`disaster_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to end your volunteer work.
 
- **Additional Notes** (`disaster_note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or information you would like to share, please enter them here.
 
- **Message to Organization** (`volunteer_message`, text, optional): If you have a message or notes for the organization, this is the space to include them.
 
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, please enter their name.
 
- **Organization Email** (`org_email`, email, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter their email address.
 
- **Organization Phone** (`org_phone`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter their phone number.
 
- **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter their address.
 
- **Volunteer Agreement** (`volunteer_agree`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" to confirm that you are able and willing to participate in disaster relief efforts.
 
- **Volunteer Agreement Date** (`volunteer_agree_date`, date, optional): The date on which you confirm your agreement to volunteer.
 
- **Volunteer Agreement Time** (`volunteer_agree_time`, time, optional): The time at which you confirm your agreement to volunteer.
 
- **Disaster Volunteer Type** (`disaster_volunteer_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of volunteer you are (e.g., Individual, Group, Team).
 
Please fill out this form carefully to help us match you with the right opportunities for disaster relief efforts.
