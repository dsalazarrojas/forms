# Sewing Masks Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers who want to participate in a sewing masks event. The information collected will be used to organize and manage the volunteers' involvement in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Volunteer Registration Form" section with any additional information you feel is relevant.
2. Enter your "Volunteer Information" in the provided text field.
3. Provide your "Name" in the next field.
4. Enter your "Email" address in the next field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Registration Form** (`text`, not required): This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about your involvement in the volunteer program.
* **Volunteer Information** (`text`, not required): Here, you can enter any information you'd like about yourself that the volunteer organizers would find helpful.
* **Name** (`text`, not required): Please enter your name as you'd like to be recognized on the volunteer list.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with event updates and instructions.
