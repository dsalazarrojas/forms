# Animal Shelter Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential volunteers about their personal details, availability, and preferences for volunteering at the animal shelter. It is intended to help us get to know you better and understand how you can contribute to our mission of providing care and love to animals in need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide the correct information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **required**): Please enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **required**): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, **required**): Enter your current street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, **required**): Enter the city where you live.
* **State or Province** (`state`, text, **required**): Enter your state or province of residence.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, **required**): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **required**): Enter the full name of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, **required**): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date_preference`, date, **required**): Enter the date you would like to start volunteering.
* **Weekly Availability** (`availability_schedule`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the days and times you are available to volunteer.
* **Programs of Interest** (`volunteer_programs`, select_multiple, **required**): Select all the programs you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Expected Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, select_one, **required**): Choose the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week.
* **Commitment Duration** (`commitment_duration`, select_one, **required**): Choose how long you can volunteer for.
* **Experience with Animals** (`animal_experience`, text, **required**): Describe your experience and background with animals.
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, **optional**): Describe any previous volunteer experience you have.
* **Special Skills or Certifications** (`special_skills`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select all special skills or certifications you have, such as veterinary technician or photographer.
* **Physical Capabilities** (`physical_capabilities`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you can perform required tasks.
* **Please Describe Limitations** (`limitations_description`, text, **optional**): If applicable, describe any physical limitations you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_one, **required**): Choose if you have any allergies.
* **Allergy Details** (`allergy_details`, text, **optional**): If you have allergies, describe them in detail.
* **I Consent to a Background Check** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, **required**): Choose if you consent to a background check.
* **Reference 1 - Name and Contact** (`reference_1`, text, **required**): Enter the full name, phone number, and email of a professional reference.
* **Reference 2 - Name and Contact** (`reference_2`, text, **required**): Enter the full name, phone number, and email of another professional reference.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard`, select_one, **optional**): Choose how you heard about us, such as through a website or social media.
* **Why Do You Want to Volunteer** (`motivation_statement`, text, **required**): Share your motivation for wanting to volunteer.
* **I Agree to Follow All Shelter Policies** (`policy_agreement`, select_one, **required**): Choose if you agree to follow all shelter policies.
* **I Accept the Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, **required**): Choose if you accept the liability waiver.
* **Electronic Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, **required**): Type your full name as an electronic signature.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, **required**): This field is automatically filled in once you submit the form.

## Tips
* Please review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in volunteering and look forward to reviewing your application!
