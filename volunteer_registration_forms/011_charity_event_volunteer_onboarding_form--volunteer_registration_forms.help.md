# Charity Event Volunteer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic information from potential volunteers to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through each section step by step:

1. Provide your basic information in the "Basic Information" section, including your name, email address, phone number, and date of birth.
2. Indicate your interest in specific event types and select multiple areas of interest for volunteer work.
3. Specify your availability for volunteer work, including your preferred work schedule.
4. Provide details about your prior volunteer experience and any relevant skills or certifications.
5. Review and agree to the volunteer code of conduct, liability waiver, and background check consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email Address
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address for communication and updates about the event.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number for communication purposes.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Street Address
* **Street Address**: Enter your home address for contact and logistical purposes.

### City
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.

### Zip Code
* **Zip Code**: Enter your postal code.

### What Type of Event Are You Volunteering For
* **What Type of Event Are You Volunteering For**: Select the type of event you are interested in volunteering for.

### Areas of Interest
* **Areas of Interest**: Select multiple areas that you are interested in volunteering for, such as event planning, fundraising, community outreach, and more.

### Availability
* **Availability**: Choose when you can volunteer, including weekdays, weekends, evening hours, or full days.

### Hours Available Per Week
* **Hours Available Per Week**: Enter the number of hours you can commit to volunteering per week.

### Prior Volunteer Experience
* **Prior Volunteer Experience**: Select your level of volunteer experience, from no experience to 5+ years.

### Special Skills or Certifications
* **Special Skills or Certifications**: Enter any relevant skills or certifications you have.

### Volunteer Code of Conduct
* **Volunteer Code of Conduct**: Agree to abide by the volunteer code of conduct.

### Liability Waiver
* **Liability Waiver**: Acknowledge that you understand the terms of the volunteer liability waiver.

### Background Check Consent
* **Background Check Consent**: Consent to a background check if required by the organization.

### Additional Information
* **Additional Information**: Provide any other relevant information about yourself that you would like to share with the organization.

**Tips:**

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary time and commitment to volunteer before submitting the form.
