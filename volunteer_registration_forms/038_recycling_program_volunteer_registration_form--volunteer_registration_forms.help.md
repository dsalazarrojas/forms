# Recycling Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential volunteers for the recycling program. This form is used to register volunteers for the program and gather necessary details for effective management and communication. By filling out this form, volunteers can provide their personal and professional information, volunteer experience, and skills for the program's record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Provide your real name, date of birth, and contact information (phone number and email address).
* Choose your volunteer experience and skills from the provided options.
* Select the assigned tool and user for your volunteer work (if applicable).
* Provide any additional notes about your availability or any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (Page 1)
* **User Info**: Provide your full name.

### Volunteer Info (Page 2)
* **Volunteer Info**: This field is not currently used. Please skip this page.

### Contact Info (Page 3)
* **Contact Info**: Provide your phone number and email address.

### Date of Birth (Page 4)
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Email (Page 4)
* **Email**: Enter your email address associated with this registration.

### Phone (Page 5)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Address (Page 6)
* **Address**: Provide your address for record-keeping and communication.

### Volunteer Experience (Page 7)
* **Volunteer Experience**: Select any relevant volunteer experience from the options provided.

### Skills (Page 8)
* **Skills**: Select any skills you have that are relevant to the program.

### Notes (Page 9)
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your availability or any other relevant details.

### Assigned Tool (Page 10)
* **Assigned Tool**: If you are assigned a tool, select it from the provided options.

### Assigned User (Page 11)
* **Assigned User**: If you are assigned to a specific user, select 'Yes'.

### Assigned Date (Page 12)
* **Assigned Date**: If you are assigned a specific date, enter it in MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Assigned Time (Page 13)
* **Assigned Time**: If you are assigned a specific time, enter it in HH:MM format.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
