# museum_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The museum_volunteer_registration_form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for the museum's volunteer program. This form helps the museum understand each volunteer's interests, availability, and preferences, enabling us to schedule and coordinate volunteer opportunities effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
### Step 1: Provide Your Information
Fill in the following fields:

*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for communication from the museum.
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
*   **Availability**: Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
*   **Shifts**: Indicate if you are available to work shifts or not.
*   **Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
*   **Volunteer Areas**: Select the areas you are interested in volunteering for (e.g., Exhibition, Education, Conservation, Administration).
*   **Confirm**: Confirm that you are available to volunteer.

### Step 2: Add Additional Information (Optional)
If you have any additional comments or information, please use the "Comments" field. This will help us understand your preferences and interests better.

### Step 3: Review and Submit
Review your registration carefully before submitting it. Make sure to double-check the information you have provided and confirm your availability and interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Volunteer Information**: This field is not applicable in this form.
*   **full_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for communication from the museum.
*   **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number, if applicable.
*   **availability**: Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
*   **shifts**: Indicate if you are available to work shifts or not.
*   **start_date**: Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
*   **Volunteer Areas**: Select the areas you are interested in volunteering for (e.g., Exhibition, Education, Conservation, Administration).
*   **Comments**: Use this field to add any additional comments or information about your preferences and interests.
*   **confirm**: Confirm that you are available to volunteer.
*   **Volunteer Areas 2**: Select the areas you are interested in volunteering for (this is a duplicate of "Volunteer Areas" and is not necessary to complete).
