# Angels Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Angels Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with our organization. This form helps us understand their availability, areas of interest, and other relevant details. Please note that filling out this form is a prerequisite for volunteering with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Fill out the required fields marked as `required` (Volunteer name, Email address, Availability, and Consent to background check).
3. For the optional fields, select all that apply or provide any additional comments you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer name** (`Volunteer name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to while volunteering.
* **Email address** (`Email address`, `email`, `required`): Provide your email address for registration confirmation and communication from our organization.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `text`, `required`): List the days and times you are available to volunteer. Please be as specific as possible, including AM/PM and any time slots you cannot work.
* **Areas of interest** (`Areas of interest`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select all the areas you are interested in, such as Mentoring, Fundraising, or Event support. This will help us understand your passions and skills.
* **Consent to background check** (`Consent to background check`, `select_one`, `required`): Please select `True` if you consent to a background check. This is a mandatory requirement for all volunteers.
* **Phone number** (`Phone number`, `text`, `optional`): If you want to be contacted about volunteer opportunities, please provide your phone number. We will not share your number with anyone.
* **Additional comments** (`Additional comments`, `text`, `optional`): Share any other information you think would be helpful for us to know, such as skills or experience.
