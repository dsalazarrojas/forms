# Zimbabwe Nursing Application Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Zimbabwe Nursing Application Form Template is a comprehensive form used to collect essential information from individuals applying for nursing positions in Zimbabwe. This form ensures that applicants provide accurate and complete details about their medical background, licensure, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section: Enter your name, surname, email, phone number, and other contact information in the designated fields.
2. Provide your medical information: Enter your medical license number, license type, license issued to, and other relevant details.
3. Enter your medical school and license details: Specify the name of your medical school and the institution that issued your medical license.
4. Add emergency contact information: Enter the name, relationship, phone number, and address of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
5. Review and submit your application: Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first and last name as they appear on your identification documents.
* **Surname** (`Surname`, `text`, `required`): Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Name 2** (`Name 2`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your emergency contact (e.g., spouse, parent, or partner).
* **Surname 2** (`Surname 2`, `text`, `required`): Enter the surname of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, `text`, `required`): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, child, etc.).
* **Phone 2** (`Phone 2`, `text`, `required`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Page 2
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, `text`, `required`): Enter any medical conditions you have or are currently experiencing.
* **Medication** (`Medication`, `text`, `required`): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, `required`): List any allergies you have or are currently experiencing.
* **Medical Insurance** (`Medical Insurance`, `text`, `required`): Enter your medical insurance information (e.g., provider, policy number).
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, `required`): Sign the form electronically.
* **E-Signature** (`E-Signature`, `text`, `required`): Enter your electronic signature.

### Page 3
* **Medical License** (`Medical License`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the type of medical license you hold.
* **Medical School** (`Medical School`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select the name of your medical school.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`Emergency Contact Relationship 2`, `text`, `required`): If the relationship with your emergency contact is not listed, enter the relationship here.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`Emergency Contact Phone 2`, `text`, `required`): If you have a second phone number for your emergency contact, enter it here.
* **Medical License Number** (`Medical License Number`, `text`, `required`): Enter your medical license number.
* **Medical License Expiration** (`Medical License Expiration`, `date`, `required`): Enter the expiration date of your medical license.
* **Medical License Issued** (`Medical License Issued`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical School Attended** (`Medical School Attended`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your medical school.
* **Emergency Contact Surname** (`Emergency Contact Surname`, `text`, `required`): Enter the surname of your emergency contact.
* **Medical License Issued By** (`Medical License Issued By`, `text`, `required`): Enter the institution that issued your medical license.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (`Emergency Contact Phone 2`, `text`, `required`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact 2.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, `required`): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical License Status** (`Medical License Status`, `text`, `required`): Enter your current medical license status.
* **Medical License Type** (`Medical License Type`, `text`, `required`): Enter the type of your medical license.
* **Medical License Issued To** (`Medical License Issued To`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of the person your medical license was issued to.

### Additional Pages
* **Relationship Other** (`Relationship Other`, `text`, `optional`): If your emergency contact relationship is not listed, select "Other" and enter the relationship here.
* **Phone Other** (`Phone Other`, `text`, `optional`): Enter a phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Medical License Number Other** (`Medical License Number Other`, `text`, `optional`): Enter an alternate medical license number.
* **Medical License Issued By Other** (`Medical License Issued By Other`, `text`, `optional`): Enter an alternate institution that issued your medical license.
* **Medical License Issued Date Other** (`Medical License Issued Date Other`, `date`, `optional`): Enter an alternate date your medical license was issued.
* **Medical License Expiration Other** (`Medical License Expiration Other`, `date`, `optional`): Enter an alternate expiration date of your medical license.
* **Medical License Type Other** (`Medical License Type Other`, `text`, `optional`): Enter an alternate type of your medical license.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or HR representative.
* Please review your application carefully before submitting it.
* If you need help with completing the form, do not hesitate to ask.
