# Student Ministry Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Ministry Volunteer Application Form is designed to collect information from students who are interested in volunteering with the ministry. The form helps us to understand their contact details, volunteer information, and preferences to match them with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Provide some information about your interest in volunteering with the ministry.
3.  Indicate your volunteer preferences, such as the type of events or activities you would like to participate in.
4.  Select the volunteer committee that aligns with your interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Details
*   **Contact Details** (`name`, `email`, `phone_number`): This field is used to collect your contact information so that we can get in touch with you about your volunteer opportunities. Please fill out your name, email address, and phone number to help us contact you efficiently.

### Volunteer Info
*   **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_info`): This field is used to gather information about your interest in volunteering with the ministry. Please provide a brief description of your volunteer experience, skills, or qualifications that you think would be beneficial for us to know.

### Volunteer Preferences
*   **Volunteer Preferences** (`volunteer_preferences`): This field is used to indicate your interests and preferences for volunteering. Please select the types of events or activities you would like to participate in, such as event planning, community service, or administrative tasks.

### Volunteer Committee
*   **Volunteer Committee** (`volunteer_committee`): This field is used to match you with a volunteer committee that aligns with your interests and skills. Please select the committee that you think you would be a good fit for, and we will take care of the rest.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
*   We appreciate your interest in volunteering with the ministry, and we look forward to reviewing your application!
