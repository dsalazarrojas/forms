# Educational Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for individuals to register as volunteers for educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Institution Name**: Select the institution where you are affiliated with from the available options.
2. **Volunteer Type**: Choose the type of volunteer you wish to be, from the available options.
3. **Start Date** and **End Date**: Enter the start and end dates of your volunteer period.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for quick contact.
6. **Description**: Provide a brief description of your role as a volunteer.
7. **Notes**: (Optional) Provide any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution Name**: Select your institution affiliation from the list.
	+ This will help us understand which institution you are representing.
* **Volunteer Type**: Choose your volunteer type from the available options.
	+ This will help us categorize your role within the program.
* **Start Date**: The date you will start your volunteer period.
	+ Please format it as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: The date you will end your volunteer period.
	+ Please format it as MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Your email address for contact purposes.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for quick contact.
	+ Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Description**: A brief description of your role as a volunteer.
	+ This will help us understand your responsibilities and duties within the program.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information about your registration.
	+ This is optional, but may be helpful for our administrators.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* Once you submit the form, you will be contacted by our team to confirm your registration.
