# product_sampling_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product sampling volunteer registration form is designed to collect information from volunteers interested in participating in product sampling activities. It's a straightforward process that requires basic information about the volunteer, their volunteer status, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your basic information in the first field, including any relevant details.
2. Enter your volunteer information in the second field, describing your experience and qualifications.
3. Share your sampling preferences in the third field, specifying the type of products you're interested in sampling.
4. Complete the contact information field with your address, email, or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Basic Info <small>(id: 1, type: text, required: false)</small>
* This field is for sharing basic information about yourself.

### Volunteer Info <small>(id: 2, type: text, required: false)</small>
* Describe your volunteer experience and qualifications to help us understand your fit for our product sampling activities.

### Sampling Preferences <small>(id: 3, type: text, required: false)</small>
* Indicate the type of products you'd like to sample by listing them or describing your interests.

### Contact Info <small>(id: 4, type: text, required: false)</small>
* Enter your contact details, such as address, email, or phone number, so we can reach out to you.
