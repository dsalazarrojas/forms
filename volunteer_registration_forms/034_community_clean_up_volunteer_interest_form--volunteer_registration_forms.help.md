# Community Clean Up Volunteer Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in participating in community clean-up events. Please complete this form to express your interest in helping with clean-up events and provide necessary information for event coordination and waiver purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following pages:

1. **Volunteer Details**: Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. **Availability and Preferences**: Select the clean-up events you are interested in participating in and choose your preferred volunteer role.
3. **Skills and Logistics**: Describe your background and any logistical needs.
4. **Acknowledgment and Submission**: Review and digitally sign the form to confirm your interest.
5. **Newsletter and Referral**: Choose whether you want to receive the community center's newsletter and how you heard about this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Volunteer**: Enter your full name as it should appear on any waivers or other documents. 
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for event coordination and results delivery. 
* **Primary Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for event coordination and communication. 
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for age verification and waiver purposes. 
* **Residential Address**: Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code. 
* **Which clean-up event(s) are you interested in**: Select all the clean-up events you are interested in participating in. 
* **Preferred Volunteer Role**: Choose your top choice for a volunteer role. 
* **Participation Level**: Select the size of your group. 
* **Group or Organization Name (If applicable)**: Enter your group or organization name if you are representing one. 
* **What key skills or expertise would you bring to the clean-up**: Describe your key skills or expertise that would be beneficial to the clean-up event. 
* **Describe any previous volunteer experience**: Describe any previous volunteer experience you have. 
* **Do you have any accessibility or accommodation needs**: Describe any accessibility or accommodation needs you may have. 
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**: Digitally sign the form to confirm your interest. 
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date you are submitting this form. 
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter**: Choose whether you want to stay updated on our programs and events. 
* **How did you hear about this clean-up interest form**: Choose how you heard about this form. 

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
