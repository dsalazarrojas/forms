# Book Signing Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers interested in participating in book signing events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by selecting the book signing event you are interested in volunteering for. Please choose only one event.
- Fill in your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
- If you have a date of birth, please provide it, but it is not mandatory.
- Enter your address, city, and ZIP code.
- Select your preferred volunteer role.
- Choose your availability for the event.
- If you have previous experience as a volunteer, please select "Yes at book events" or "Yes at other events".
- Select any relevant skills you have that would be beneficial for the event.
- Choose a T-Shirt size if you need one.
- Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
- Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
- If required, please provide consent for background checks.
- Indicate if you waive liability for volunteering.
- Grant permission for photo releases.
- Choose how you found out about this opportunity.
- Share your motivation for volunteering.
- Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
- Agree to follow the volunteer guidelines.
- Finally, select the date of registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we will send updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for coordination purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: If you have a date of birth, enter it. If not, leave blank.
* **Street Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Book Signing Event**: Choose the event you are interested in volunteering for. Please choose only one event.
* **Your Availability**: Select when you can volunteer for the event. Please choose all applicable options.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role**: Select your preferred role for the event.
* **Previous Volunteer Experience**: If you have volunteered before, select "Yes at book events" or "Yes at other events".
* **Relevant Skills**: Select any skills you have that would be beneficial for the event.
* **T-Shirt Size**: If you need a T-shirt, choose your size.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Consent to Background Check**: If required, select "Yes I consent".
* **Liability Waiver**: Indicate if you waive liability for volunteering.
* **Photo Release**: Grant permission for photo releases.
* **How Did You Hear About This Opportunity**: Choose how you found out about this opportunity.
* **Why Do You Want to Volunteer**: Share your motivation for volunteering.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **I Agree to Volunteer Guidelines**: Select "Yes I agree" to follow the volunteer guidelines.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date of registration.

Please note that this form is a one-time registration and will not be duplicated for repeat volunteers. If you have any questions, please contact us.
