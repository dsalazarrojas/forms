# Stem Outreach Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stem Outreach Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for outreach activities related to Stem fields (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Math). This form helps us understand your interests, availability, and other relevant details to best match you with suitable volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the form submission information page with your details.
2.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" (e.g., 01/01/1999).
6.  Select your interests in the "Volunteer Interests" field by choosing one or more of the following options: Programming, Science, Technology, Engineering, Math.
7.  Enter your availability in the "Availability" field using the format "HH:MM-HH:MM" (e.g., 08:00-17:00).
8.  If you wish to join a specific volunteer group, choose one of the options provided in the "Volunteer Group" field.
9.  Finally, add any additional comments or information in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Submission Information
* **Form Submission Information (id: 1)**: This is the first page of the form where you'll enter your basic contact information. 

### Name
* **Name (id: 2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### Email
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth (id: 5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" (e.g., 01/01/1999).

### Volunteer Interests
* **Volunteer Interests (id: 6)**: Choose one or more of the following options: Programming, Science, Technology, Engineering, Math.

### Availability
* **Availability (id: 7)**: Enter your available work hours using the format "HH:MM-HH:MM" (e.g., 08:00-17:00).

### Volunteer Group
* **Volunteer Group (id: 8)**: Choose the volunteer group you're interested in joining from the options provided.

### Note
* **Note (id: 9)**: Add any additional comments or information that might help us understand your volunteer preferences.
