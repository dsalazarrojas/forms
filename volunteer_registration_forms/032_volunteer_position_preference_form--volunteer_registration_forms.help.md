# Volunteer Position Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Position Preference Form is a tool used to match volunteers with volunteer opportunities that fit their skills and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you have all necessary information available.
2. Select the skills and volunteer opportunities that you are interested in.
3. Enter your availability in the required format (date for regular availability, number for frequency, and time for specific time slots).
4. Provide any additional comments or notes that may be relevant for your volunteer work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Preferences** (`volunteer_preferences`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional comments or notes about your volunteer preferences. Please use this space to elaborate on your skills, experience, or any other relevant information that may help us match you with the right volunteer opportunity.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the skills you have that may be relevant for volunteer work. You can choose as many or as few as you like, depending on your abilities.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, optional): This field is for you to enter your regular availability in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`. Please use this format to indicate when you are usually available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, number, optional): This field is for you to indicate how often you are available to volunteer per week. Please enter a number that represents how many days you are available to volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Time Availability** (`volunteer_time_availability`, time, optional): This field is for you to indicate specific time slots when you are available to volunteer. Please enter the time slots in `HH:MM` format.
* **Volunteer Preferences 2-4** (`volunteer_preferences_2`, `volunteer_preferences_3`, `volunteer_preferences_4`, text, optional): These fields are for you to provide any additional comments or notes that may be relevant for your volunteer work. Please use these spaces to elaborate on your skills, experience, or any other relevant information that may help us match you with the right volunteer opportunity.

## Tips
* Please be as specific as possible when entering your availability and skills.
* If you have any conflicts or limitations that may impact your availability, please indicate them in the comments or notes fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
