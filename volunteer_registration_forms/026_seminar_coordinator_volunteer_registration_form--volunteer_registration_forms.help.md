# Seminar Coordinator Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seminar Coordinator Volunteer Registration Form is a registration form for volunteers to provide their information and role for a specific seminar. The form gathers personal contact information, role, and position, helping seminar coordinators to organize and communicate with the volunteers effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Seminar Coordinator Volunteer Registration Form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal details (first name, last name, email, and phone number).
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Enter your address.
4. Describe your volunteer position.
5. Choose your role (if applicable).
Note: Not all fields are required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (id: 1)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (id: 2)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (id: 3)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number (id: 4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth (id: 5)**: Enter your date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address (id: 6)**: Enter your home address for communication and logistics purposes.
* **Volunteer Position (id: 7)**: Describe your role or position within the seminar event.
* **Role (id: 8)**: If you have a specific role or title within the event, describe it here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact the seminar coordinator for assistance.
* Only register once for a single seminar to avoid duplication. If you want to volunteer for multiple seminars, create a new account and register separately for each event.
