# Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows volunteers to register themselves for events and provide essential information about their availability and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the form by entering your details in the provided fields.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3.  Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Event Title** (Text): Enter the title of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
    *   Please enter the name of the event you would like to volunteer for.
*   **Event Date** (Date): Enter the date of the event you are interested in volunteering for.
    *   Use the format `YYYY-MM-DD` to enter the date.
*   **Volunteer Name** (Text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on event materials.
    *   Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on event materials.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter your email address.
    *   Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
    *   Please enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
*   **Volunteer Details** (Text): Enter any additional information about yourself that would be helpful for us to know.
    *   Please use this field to provide any additional information about yourself that may be useful for us to know.
*   **Volunteer Skills** (Text): Enter any relevant skills or experience you have that would be beneficial for the event.
    *   Please list any relevant skills or experience you have that would be beneficial for the event.
*   **Assign Tool** (Select One): Select the tool that you would like to be assigned for the event.
    *   Please select one of the available tools: `tool1`, `tool2`, `tool3`.
*   **Submit** (Submit Button): Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to finalize your registration.

## Tips

*   Please review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
*   Only submit once for each event.
*   If you have any issues or questions, contact us at your email address provided.
