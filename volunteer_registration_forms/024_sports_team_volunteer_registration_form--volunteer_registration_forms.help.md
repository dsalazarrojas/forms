# Sports Team Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sports Team Volunteer Registration Form is used to collect vital information from volunteers for sports team events. This form helps team organizers track volunteers' details, availability, and assignments to ensure a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself as a volunteer.
2.  Enter your email address for communication purposes.
3.  Provide your contact phone number for emergency or communication needs.
4.  Select the Event you wish to volunteer for from the options provided.
5.  Choose your available Shift Time and Shift End for the event.
6.  Select your role in the event (e.g., Volunteer, Leader).
7.  Indicate your Availability for the event.
8.  Add any additional Notes if needed.
9.  Choose the person who will be submitting your volunteer registration.
10. Select the tool assigned to you for the event.
11. Select the person or department that will handle your volunteer registration.
12. Confirm when your volunteer registration was submitted.
13. Confirm when the form was last updated and who updated it.
14. If applicable, provide your team ID and User ID.
15. Enter your User Name, if required.
16. Select whether you are an Individual or part of a Team.
17. Finally, confirm your Volunteer Status.
