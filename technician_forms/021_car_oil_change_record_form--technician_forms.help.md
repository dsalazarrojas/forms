# Car Oil Change Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for technicians to record and document information about oil changes for vehicles. It ensures accurate and thorough documentation of service history, aiding in future maintenance and repairs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the "Vehicle Information" page to enter the following details:
	* Vehicle Year: The year the vehicle was manufactured.
	* Vehicle Make: The manufacturer of the vehicle.
	* Vehicle Model: The model of the vehicle.
	* Vehicle VIN: The Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
	* Current Mileage: The current odometer reading.
	* Owner Name: The name of the vehicle's owner.
	* License Plate: The plate number of the vehicle.
2. Proceed to the "Service Details" page and enter the following:
	* Service Date: The date of the oil change.
	* Service Time: The time of the oil change (optional).
	* Oil Type: The type of oil used (e.g., Conventional, Synthetic Blend, Full Synthetic, High Mileage, or Diesel Oil).
	* Oil Viscosity: The weight/grade of the oil (e.g., 0W-20, 5W-20, 5W-30, 10W-30, 10W-40, 20W-50, or Other).
	* Oil Quantity: The amount of oil used (4 quarts, 5 quarts, 6 quarts, 7 quarts, 8 quarts, or Other).
	* Oil Brand: The brand of oil used (optional).
	* Filter Changed: Whether a new filter was installed (True or False).
	* Filter Type: The type of filter used (optional).
	* Filter Brand: The brand of the filter used (optional).
	* Drain Plug Gasket: Whether the drain plug gasket was replaced (Yes, No, or Not Applicable).
3. If necessary, select additional services performed during the oil change from the provided list:
	* Fluid top-up
	* Tire rotation
	* Tire pressure check
	* Battery check
	* Brake inspection
	* Air filter replacement
	* Cabin filter replacement
	* Multi-point inspection
4. Enter any other services performed or fluids checked:
	* Which fluids were checked (e.g., Coolant, Brake Fluid, Transmission Fluid, etc.).
	* Notes on fluid conditions.
5. Confirm whether any leaks were detected during the service:
	* No leaks
	* Minor seepage
	* Visible leak
	* Major leak
	* If leaks were detected, enter the location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Year (ID 2)**: Enter the year the vehicle was manufactured.
* **Vehicle Make (ID 3)**: Enter the manufacturer of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model (ID 4)**: Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle VIN (ID 5)**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **Current Mileage (ID 7)**: Enter the current odometer reading.
* **Owner Name (ID 8)**: Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
* **License Plate (ID 6)**: Enter the plate number of the vehicle.
* **Service Date (ID 10)**: Enter the date of the oil change.
* **Service Time (ID 11)**: Enter the time of the oil change (optional).
* **Oil Type (ID 12)**: Select the type of oil used.
* **Oil Viscosity (ID 13)**: Select the weight/grade of the oil.
* **Oil Quantity (ID 14)**: Select the amount of oil used.
* **Oil Brand (ID 15)**: Enter the brand of oil used (optional).
* **Filter Changed (ID 16)**: Confirm whether a new filter was installed.
* **Filter Type (ID 17)**: Enter the type of filter used (optional).
* **Filter Brand (ID 18)**: Enter the brand of the filter used (optional).
* **Drain Plug Gasket (ID 19)**: Confirm whether the gasket was replaced.
* **Technician Information (ID 20)**: Enter any technician notes or comments.
* **Technician Name (ID 21)**: Enter the name of the technician performing the service.
* **Technician ID (ID 22)**: Enter the technician's employee ID (optional).
* **Service Location (ID 23)**: Enter the shop or location where the service was performed.
* **Additional Services (ID 24)**: Select any additional services performed during the oil change.
* **Fluids Checked (ID 25)**: Select any fluids checked during the oil change.
* **Fluid Conditions (ID 26)**: Enter notes on fluid conditions (optional).
* **Vehicle Condition Notes (ID 27)**: Enter any observations about the vehicle's condition.
* **Leaks Detected (ID 28)**: Confirm whether any leaks were detected during the service.
* **Leak Location (ID 29)**: Enter the location of any leaks detected (optional).
* **Recommendations (ID 30)**: Enter any suggested future services.
