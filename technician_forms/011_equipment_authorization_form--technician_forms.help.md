# equipment_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request authorization for equipment usage within an organization. It is meant to ensure that all necessary information is collected and approved before equipment is used.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Select the organization from the drop-down menu.
* Choose the type of equipment being used.
* Select the personnel authorized to use the equipment.
* If applicable, enter the start and end dates for the equipment usage period.
* Enter the duration of the equipment usage.
* Select the assigned tool.
* Choose the status of the equipment (available, authorized, or authorized with conditions).
* Enter the submission date and last update date.
* Select the status of the equipment (open or closed).
* Select the comments status (open or closed).
* Enter the equipment number and equipment name.
* Enter any comments about the equipment.
* Select the submission ID and comments ID.
* Enter the person who created the form and when it was created.
* Enter the person who last updated the form and when it was updated.
* Enter the version number of the form.
* Enter the number of comments on the form.
* Select the personnel assigned to the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the organization that will be using the equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of equipment being used (e.g. Crane, Digger, Other).
* **Authorized By** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the personnel authorized to use the equipment.
* **Start Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the equipment usage period.
* **End Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the equipment usage period.
* **Duration** (`6`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the equipment usage period.
* **Assigned Tool** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool assigned to the equipment.
* **Equipment Status** (`9`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the status of the equipment (available, authorized, or authorized with conditions).
* **Submission Date** (`10`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Last Update** (`11`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Status** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the status of the equipment (open or closed).
* **Comments Status** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the comments status (open or closed).
* **Equipment Number** (`14`, `text`, required): Enter the number of the equipment.
* **Equipment Name** (`15`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the equipment.
* **Comments** (`16`, `text`, required): Enter any comments about the equipment.
* **Submission ID** (`17`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the form submission.
* **Comments ID** (`18`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the comments.
* **Created By** (`19`, `text`, required): Enter the person who created the form.
* **Updated By** (`20`, `text`, required): Enter the person who last updated the form.
* **Created Date** (`21`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated Date** (`22`, `date`, required): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Version** (`23`, `number`, required): Enter the version number of the form.
* **Comments Count** (`24`, `number`, required): Enter the number of comments on the form.
* **Assigned To** (`25`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the personnel assigned to the equipment.
