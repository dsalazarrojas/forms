# Computer Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Computer Survey form is designed to assess the computer skills and training needs of respondents. It collects information on their proficiency in various areas, such as Windows and Mac, and their preferred training topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reviewing the form title and purpose.
2. If you want to complete the form anonymously, you can leave your name optional. Otherwise, enter your name in the "Respondent name" field.
3. Answer the following questions:
	* Rate your proficiency in Windows and Mac using the select_one option (Expert, Proficient, Beginner, None).
	* Choose your self-rated network troubleshooting ability (Strong, Moderate, Basic).
	* Select your preferred training topics (Hardware troubleshooting, Software installation, Security, Networking).
4. Optionally, provide any additional feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Respondent name** (Respondent name, text, optional): Enter your name, if you're not completing the form anonymously.
* **Proficiency Windows** (Proficiency Windows, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency in Windows using the options (Expert, Proficient, Beginner, None).
* **Proficiency Mac** (Proficiency Mac, select_one, required): Rate your proficiency in Mac using the options (Expert, Proficient, Beginner, None).
* **Networking skills** (Networking skills, select_one, required): Rate your network troubleshooting ability using the options (Strong, Moderate, Basic).
* **Preferred training topics** (Preferred training topics, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred training topics (Hardware troubleshooting, Software installation, Security, Networking).
* **Comments** (Comments, text, optional): Provide any additional feedback or comments for the technicians.
