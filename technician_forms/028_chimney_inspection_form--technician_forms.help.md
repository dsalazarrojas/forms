# Chimney Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the inspection of a chimney. The goal is to gather data on the condition and safety of the chimney, which will help in determining the necessary actions to be taken for its maintenance and repair.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Inspection Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2. Fill in the **Inspector Name** and **Certification Number** fields with your name and certification number, respectively.
3. Provide the **Company Name** where you are employed.
4. Enter the **Property Address** where the inspection is being conducted.
5. Enter the **City** and **State** where the property is located.
6. Enter the **Zip Code** of the property.
7. Fill in the **Customer Name**, **Customer Phone Number**, and **Customer Email** fields with the relevant contact information.
8. Select the type of **Chimney Structure**, **Chimney Type**, and **Fireplace or Appliance Type**.
9. Enter the **Approximate Chimney Height** in feet.
10. Select the **Creosote Buildup Level** based on your findings.
11. Determine if **Cleaning is Recommended** and select the corresponding option.
12. Check if **Obstructions Were Found** and provide a description of the obstructions in the **Obstruction Description** field.
13. Select the **Overall Safety Status** based on your assessment.
14. If necessary, enter a detailed description of any **Recommended Repairs or Maintenance**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, required): Date of the inspection in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, required): Name of the technician conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Certification** (`inspector_certification`, optional): Technician's certification number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Name of the company the technician is employed by.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, required): Address where the inspection is being conducted.
* **City** (`city`, required): City where the property is located.
* **State** (`state`, required): State where the property is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, required): Zip code of the property.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, required): Name of the customer.
* **Customer Phone Number** (`customer_phone`, required): Phone number of the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, optional): Email address of the customer.
* **Chimney Structure** (`chimney_structure`, optional): Type of chimney structure.
* **Chimney Type** (`chimney_type`, required): Type of chimney.
* **Fireplace or Appliance Type** (`fireplace_type`, required): Type of fireplace or appliance.
* **Approximate Chimney Height** (`chimney_height`, optional): Height of the chimney in feet.
* **Creosote Buildup Level** (`creosote_buildup`, required): Level of creosote buildup.
* **Cleaning Recommended** (`cleaning_recommended`, required): Determine if cleaning is recommended.
* **Obstructions Found** (`obstructions_found`, required): Check if obstructions were found.
* **Obstruction Description** (`obstruction_description`, optional): Description of obstructions.
* **Overall Safety Status** (`safety_status`, required): Overall safety status of the chimney.
* **Recommended Repairs or Maintenance** (`recommended_repairs`, optional): Description of recommended repairs or maintenance.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Double-check your answers for any inconsistencies.
- If you are unsure about any field, use your best judgment and provide a brief explanation.
- If you need to add any additional information, use the **Recommended Repairs or Maintenance** field.
