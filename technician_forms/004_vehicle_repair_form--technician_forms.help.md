# vehicle_repair_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vehicle_repair_form" is used to document vehicle repairs, including vehicle information, repair type, cost, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. Enter the vehicle year in the "Vehicle Year" field.
3. Enter the vehicle make and model in the "Vehicle Make" and "Vehicle Model" fields.
4. Describe the vehicle problem in the "Vehicle Problem" field.
5. Select the repair type (Oil Change, Maintenance, or Repair) from the "Repair Type" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
7. Enter the customer's email (if applicable) in the "Customer Email" field.
8. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
9. For the "Customer Notes" field, enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.
10. Enter the labor cost in the "Labor Cost" field.
11. Enter the parts cost in the "Parts Cost" field.
12. Enter the total vehicle cost in the "Vehicle Cost" field.
13. Select the technician(s) who worked on the repair from the "Technician" dropdown menu.
14. Enter the date the problem started in the "Date of Problem" field.
15. Enter the date the repair was completed in the "Repair Date" field.
16. Enter the time spent on the repair in the "Repair Time" field.
17. Enter the total repair cost in the "Repair Cost" field.
18. Select the repair status (Pending, Complete, or In Progress) from the "Repair Status" dropdown menu.
19. Enter any additional notes from the mechanic in the "Notes" field.
20. Sign the form with your signature in the "Signature" field.
21. Sign the form with the customer's signature in the "Customer Signature" field.
22. Sign the form with the mechanic's signature in the "Mechanic Signature" field.
23. If applicable, enter a vehicle repair form ID in the "Vehicle Repair Form ID" field.
24. Enter the vehicle number in the "Vehicle Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **Vehicle Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle (e.g., Corolla, Mustang, etc.).
* **Vehicle Problem**: Describe the problem or issue with the vehicle.
* **Repair Type**: Select the type of repair (Oil Change, Maintenance, or Repair).
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email (if applicable).
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Labor Cost**: Enter the labor cost of the repair.
* **Parts Cost**: Enter the parts cost of the repair.
* **Vehicle Cost**: Enter the total vehicle cost (labor + parts).
* **Technician**: Select the technician(s) who worked on the repair.
* **Date of Problem**: Enter the date the problem started.
* **Repair Date**: Enter the date the repair was completed.
* **Repair Time**: Enter the time spent on the repair.
* **Repair Cost**: Enter the total repair cost.
* **Repair Status**: Select the status of the repair (Pending, Complete, or In Progress).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments from the mechanic.
* **Signature**: Sign with your signature.
* **Customer Signature**: Sign with the customer's signature.
* **Mechanic Signature**: Sign with the mechanic's signature.
* **Vehicle Repair Form ID**: If applicable, enter a vehicle repair form ID.
* **Vehicle Number**: Enter the vehicle number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a colleague or supervisor for guidance.
* Remember to sign the form with your signature, the customer's signature, and the mechanic's signature.
