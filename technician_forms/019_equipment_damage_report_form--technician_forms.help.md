# Equipment Damage Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report any damages or issues found on company equipment. Fill out this form to provide detailed information about the equipment's condition, damage description, and repair requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the equipment's ID number in the "Equipment ID" field.
2. Select the date and time the equipment was found damaged in the "Date and Time" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the damage in the "Description" field.
4. Specify the damaged part of the equipment in the "Damaged Part" field. Please select from the available options:
	* Front Door
	* Rear Door
	* Handle
5. Indicate the current damage condition in the "Damage Condition" field. You can select multiple conditions if necessary. Please choose from:
	* Broken
	* Cracked
	* Dented
6. In the "Repair Info" field, provide any additional details about the repair, such as repair instructions or required parts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment ID** (equipment_id, number, required): This field is used to identify the equipment that has been damaged. Enter the unique ID number assigned to the equipment.
* **Date and Time** (date_and_time, date, required): This field is used to record the date and time the equipment was found damaged.
* **Description** (description, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the damage, including its location and extent.
* **Damaged Part** (damaged_part, select_one, required): Select the part of the equipment that was damaged from the available options: Front Door, Rear Door, or Handle.
* **Damage Condition** (damage_condition, select_multiple, required): Select the condition of the damage from the available options: Broken, Cracked, or Dented. You can select multiple conditions if necessary.
* **Repair Info** (repair_info, text, required): In this field, provide any additional details about the repair, such as instructions or required parts.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the correct information is recorded.
* If you're unsure about the damaged part or condition, please consult with the equipment's operator or supervisor.
* If you need to report multiple pieces of equipment, please fill out a separate form for each one.
