# Computer Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a tool for employees to check out company equipment, providing essential information for inventory tracking, asset management, and employee accountability.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's details, including their name, employee ID, and department.
2. Select the equipment type and brand from the given options.
3. Enter the serial number and model of the equipment being checked out.
4. Choose the checkout reason and expected return date.
5. Specify the checkout duration and status.
6. Sign and acknowledge the liability statement and terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checkout ID**: Enter the unique checkout identifier, which will be assigned to the equipment for tracking purposes. (Required)
* **Checkout Date**: Enter the date when the equipment is being checked out. (Required)
* **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician processing the checkout. (Required)
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee checking out the equipment. (Required)
* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's identification number. (Required)
* **Department**: Select the employee's department from the given options. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter the employee's work email address. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the employee's contact phone number. (Required)
* **Equipment Type**: Select the type of equipment being checked out from the given options. (Required)
* **Equipment Brand**: Enter the brand or manufacturer of the equipment. (Required)
* **Equipment Model**: Enter the model number of the equipment. (Required)
* **Serial Number**: Enter the equipment's serial number. (Required)
* **Asset Tag Number**: Enter the company's asset tag number. (Required)
* **Equipment Condition**: Select the current condition of the equipment from the given options. (Required)
* **Accessories Included**: Select all accessories included with the equipment from the given options. (Required)
* **Software Installed**: List any key software installed on the equipment. (Optional)
* **Checkout Reason**: Select the reason for checking out the equipment from the given options. (Required)
* **Expected Return Date**: Enter the date when the equipment is expected to be returned. (Required)
* **Checkout Duration**: Select the duration of the checkout from the given options. (Required)
* **Manager Approval**: Select whether the manager has approved this checkout. (Required)
* **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the approving manager. (Optional)
* **Terms and Conditions Acknowledged**: Select whether you agree to take care of the equipment and return on time. (Required)
* **Liability Statement**: Select whether you understand you are responsible for the equipment. (Required)
* **Employee Signature**: Type your full name as signature. (Required)
* **Technician Signature**: Type the technician's full name as signature. (Required)
* **Checkout Status**: Select the current status of the equipment from the given options. (Required)

Please note that all fields marked as "Required" must be filled in for the form to be considered complete and valid.
