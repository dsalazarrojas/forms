# Maintenance Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for maintenance reports, allowing technicians to document the details of a specific maintenance activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the maintenance report form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in the format of the form.
2. Review your answers carefully, as they will be saved and associated with the relevant work order.
3. Sign the form to confirm that the reported maintenance activity has been completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner** (`owner`, select_one, not required): Select whether the maintenance activity is for yourself or someone else (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): Enter the name of the organization or entity responsible for the maintenance activity.
* **Designation** (`designation`, select_multiple, not required): Check all applicable designations that apply to the maintenance activity (e.g., "Yes" or "No" for each).
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Describe the location where the maintenance activity took place.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, not required): Briefly explain the reason or goal of the maintenance activity.
* **Description** (`description`, text, not required): Provide a detailed description of the maintenance activity.
* **Equipment** (`equipment`, text, not required): List any equipment used or involved in the maintenance activity.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, text, not required): Note any notable issues or problems encountered during the maintenance activity.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, not required): Sign the form to confirm that the reported maintenance activity has been completed.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, not required): Date and time the form was signed.

## Tips
- Be as detailed as possible when completing the form, as this will help in understanding the full scope and impact of the maintenance activity.
- If you need to report multiple maintenance activities, please submit multiple reports.
- Review the report carefully before submitting it, as any errors or incomplete information may lead to delays or issues with processing.
- For further information or assistance, please contact our maintenance support team.
