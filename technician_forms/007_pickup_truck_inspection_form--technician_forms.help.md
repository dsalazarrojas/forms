# Pickup Truck Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the pickup truck's specifications and condition. It is intended for mechanics, service personnel, or inspectors to gather data on various aspects of the truck.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct vehicle make from the list.
2. Enter the vehicle year.
3. Enter the vehicle model.
4. Choose the engine type.
5. Select the transmission type (Manual or Automatic).
6. Evaluate the engine condition (Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Choose the drive train type (Front Wheel Drive, Rear Wheel Drive, or 4-wheel Drive).
8. Select the suspension system type (Hydrolic, Semi-Electric, or Hydraulic).
9. Evaluate the brakes system condition (Power Brakes, Drum Brakes, or Disc Brakes).
10. Select the steering system type (Rack & Pinion, Recirculating, or Power).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Make**: Please enter the make of the pickup truck, e.g., Ford, Toyota, or Chevrolet.
* **Vehicle Year**: Please enter the year of the pickup truck.
* **Vehicle Model**: Please enter the model of the pickup truck, e.g., F-150, F-250, or Tacoma.
* **Engine Type**: Please enter the type of engine the pickup truck is equipped with, e.g., V6, V8, or Diesel.
* **Transmission**: Please select the type of transmission, either Manual or Automatic.
* **Engine Condition**: Please evaluate the engine condition based on the provided options, choosing from Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Drive Train**: Please select the drive train type, choosing from Front Wheel Drive, Rear Wheel Drive, or 4-wheel Drive.
* **Suspension System**: Please choose the type of suspension system, selecting from Hydrolic, Semi-Electric, or Hydraulic.
* **Brakes System**: Please evaluate the brakes system condition, selecting from Power Brakes, Drum Brakes, or Disc Brakes.
* **Steering System**: Please select the type of steering system, choosing from Rack & Pinion, Recirculating, or Power.
 
## Tips
* Double-check the entered information to ensure accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult the relevant documentation or seek help from a colleague.
* If you're using the form for the first time, take your time to review each field carefully.
