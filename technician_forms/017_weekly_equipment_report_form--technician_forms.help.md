# weekly_equipment_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The weekly equipment report form is used to track the status of equipment, ensuring accuracy and consistency in reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the **Equipment ID**, a unique identifier assigned to each piece of equipment.
2. Provide the **Equipment Name**, a brief description of the equipment.
3. Report the **Status** of the equipment, which can be **Available**, **Offline**, or **Maintenance**.
4. Enter the date of the last update using the **Last Updated** field.
5. If necessary, add any **Comments** regarding the equipment.
6. Enter the **Technician ID**, a unique identifier for the technician responsible for the equipment.
7. Select the **Equipment Type**, which can be **Hand Tool**, **Power Tool**, or **Handheld Drill**.
8. Enter the **Serial Number** of the equipment.
9. Enter the **Manufacturer** of the equipment.
10. Enter the **Model Number** of the equipment.
11. Add any additional **Notes** about the equipment.
12. Select the date and time of the last update using the **Date Updated** and **Time Updated** fields.
13. Enter the **Location** of the equipment.
14. Add any **Remarks** regarding the equipment.
15. Assign the equipment to a team member using the **Assigned To** field.
16. Identify the person responsible for assigning the equipment using the **Assigned By** field.
17. Add any additional **Comments** about the equipment.
18. Identify the person responsible for creating the form using the **Created By** field.
19. Enter the unique identifier of the person responsible for creating the form using the **Created By ID** field.
20. Enter the date the form was created using the **Created On** field.
21. Identify the last person who updated the form using the **Last Updated By** field.
22. Enter the unique identifier of the last person who updated the form using the **Last Updated By ID** field.
23. Enter the unique identifier of the person who updated the form last using the **Updated By ID** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment ID** (1, Number, Required): Unique identifier for the equipment.
* **Equipment Name** (2, Text, Required): Brief description of the equipment.
* **Status** (3, Select One, Required): Available, Offline, or Maintenance status.
* **Last Updated** (4, Date, Required): Date of the last update.
* **Comments** (5, Text, Optional): Additional comments regarding the equipment.
* **Technician ID** (6, Text, Required): Unique identifier for the technician responsible for the equipment.
* **Equipment Type** (7, Select One, Required): Hand Tool, Power Tool, or Handheld Drill.
* **Serial Number** (8, Text, Required): Unique serial number of the equipment.
* **Manufacturer** (9, Text, Optional): Manufacturer of the equipment.
* **Model Number** (10, Text, Optional): Model number of the equipment.
* **Notes** (11, Text, Optional): Additional notes regarding the equipment.
* **Date Updated** (13, Date, Required): Date of the last update.
* **Time Updated** (14, Time, Required): Time of the last update.
* **Location** (15, Text, Optional): Location of the equipment.
* **Remarks** (16, Text, Optional): Additional remarks regarding the equipment.
* **Assigned To** (17, Text, Required): Equipment is assigned to a team member.
* **Assigned By** (18, Text, Required): Person responsible for assigning the equipment.
* **Comments** (19, Text, Optional): Additional comments regarding the equipment.
* **Created By** (20, Text, Optional): Person responsible for creating the form.
* **Created By ID** (21, Number, Optional): Unique identifier for the person responsible for creating the form.
* **Created On** (22, Date, Optional): Date the form was created.
* **Last Updated By** (23, Text, Optional): Person responsible for the last update.
* **Last Updated By ID** (24, Number, Optional): Unique identifier for the last person who updated the form.
* **Updated By ID** (25, Number, Optional): Unique identifier of the person who updated the form last.

**Note:** All fields with a label starting with "Created" or "Updated" should be left blank if not applicable, as they are only meant for internal tracking and reporting purposes.
