# Fire Pump Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Pump Inspection Form is a tool used by fire department personnel to inspect and document the status of fire pumps. This form helps to ensure that all fire pumps are in proper working condition, meet safety standards, and provide critical information for maintenance and repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Select the department responsible for the pump (Fire Department, Police Department, or Other).
3. Choose the type of pump being inspected (Fire Pump, Test Pump, or Other Pump).
4. Record the date of the inspection.
5. Evaluate the pump's condition (Normal, Broken, or Malfunctioning).
6. Provide any additional comments or notes about the pump's condition.
7. Enter the name of the fire station associated with the pump.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for reference purposes.
* **Department**: Select the department responsible for the pump (Fire Department, Police Department, or Other).
* **Pump Type**: Choose the type of pump being inspected (Fire Pump, Test Pump, or Other Pump).
* **Inspection Date**: Record the date of the inspection in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Pump Condition**: Evaluate the pump's condition (Normal, Broken, or Malfunctioning).
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the pump's condition.
* **Fire Station**: Enter the name of the fire station associated with the pump.
