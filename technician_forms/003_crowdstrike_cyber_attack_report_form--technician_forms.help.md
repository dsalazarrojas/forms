# CrowdStrike Cyber Attack Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CrowdStrike Cyber Attack Report Form is designed to gather information about suspected or detected cyber attacks within an organization. This form helps to document and track incidents, which aids in the response and resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you are filling out this form for a legitimate reason, such as reporting a suspected or detected cyber attack.
2.  Start by providing an overview of the attack details, including the nature of the incident and its impact.
3.  Describe the response and resolution steps taken, if applicable.
4.  If available, provide information on the attacker's details and the vulnerabilities involved.
5.  Review and verify the accuracy of the information you provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **cyber\_attack\_details**: A field for providing an overview of the cyber attack. This can include a brief description of the incident, including the date, time, and location.
*   **impact**: A field for describing the impact of the cyber attack. This can include any financial losses, data breaches, or system downtime.
*   **response**: A field for detailing the response and resolution steps taken. This can include any immediate actions taken to contain the incident, and long-term measures implemented to prevent future attacks.
*   **resolution**: A field for describing the final outcome and lessons learned from the incident. This can include any changes made to policies, procedures, or systems to prevent similar incidents in the future.
*   **attacker\_details**: A field for providing information on the attacker's details, if known. This can include IP addresses, login credentials, or other identifying information.
*   **vulnerability\_details**: A field for describing the vulnerabilities involved in the incident. This can include software, hardware, or configuration vulnerabilities that contributed to the attack.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
*   Ensure you have the necessary authorization and clearance to report the incident.
*   If the incident is ongoing, report it as soon as possible to facilitate timely response and resolution.
*   Review and verify the accuracy of the information you provide before submission.
