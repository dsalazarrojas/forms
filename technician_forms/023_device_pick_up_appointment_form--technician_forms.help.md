## device_pick_up_appointment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The device pick up appointment form is used to schedule a time for a technician to pick up a device from a user. The form is designed to collect necessary details about the user and the appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required user information, including their name and contact details.
2.  Provide the appointment details, such as the date and time of the pick up.
3.  Optionally, enter any relevant information about the technician, such as their name and contact number.
4.  Add any additional notes about the appointment, such as special instructions or requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Details (required)** (`user_details`, `text`, `true`): Enter your name and contact information, such as phone number and email address.
*   **Appointment Details (required)** (`appointment_details`, `text`, `true`): Enter the date and time you'd like the technician to pick up your device. Please format the date and time as MM/DD/YY HH:MM.
*   **Technician Details (optional)** (`technician_details`, `text`, `false`): If you have a preferred technician, please enter their name and contact information.
*   **Notes (optional)** (`notes`, `note`, `false`): Add any additional details or instructions for the technician to be aware of during the pick up.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate contact information for the user to ensure a smooth communication process.
