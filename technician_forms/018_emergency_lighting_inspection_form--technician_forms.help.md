# emergency_lighting_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The emergency_lighting_inspection_form is a custom form used by certified technicians to record the results of inspections of emergency lighting systems. The form is designed to collect information on location, inspection date, inspection time, inspector name, inspector license, and any notes relevant to the inspection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by entering the location where the emergency lighting system was inspected.
2. Enter the date of the inspection in the "inspection_date" field.
3. Enter the time of the inspection in the "inspection_time" field.
4. Enter your name as the technician who performed the inspection in the "inspector_name" field.
5. Enter your certification or license information in the "inspector_license" field.
6. Enter any additional notes or comments related to the inspection in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **emergency_lighting_inspection** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_1`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the emergency lighting system being inspected.
* **inspection_location** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_2`, text, optional): Enter the location where the emergency lighting system is located.
* **inspection_date** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_3`, date, optional): Enter the date of the inspection in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **inspection_time** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_4`, time, optional): Enter the time of the inspection in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **inspector_name** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_5`, text, optional): Enter your name as the technician performing the inspection.
* **inspector_license** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_6`, text, optional): Enter your certification or license information, if applicable.
* **inspector_signature** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_7`, text, optional): Enter your signature as the technician performing the inspection.
* **notes** (`emergency_lighting_inspection_form_8`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the inspection.
