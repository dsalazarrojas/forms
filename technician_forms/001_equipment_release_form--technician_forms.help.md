# Equipment Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Release Form is used to request and document equipment release from the inventory. This form ensures that the correct equipment is released to the right person and that all relevant information is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the type of equipment you would like to borrow.
2. Enter the name of the equipment you would like to borrow.
3. Enter your name as the technician who will be handling the equipment.
4. Select the date the equipment was issued.
5. Select the date you expect the equipment to be returned.
6. Choose the current status of the equipment.
7. Enter any comments or notes about the equipment.
8. Select the person or group that borrowed the equipment.
9. Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
10. Enter your email address.
11. Enter your phone number.
12. Enter your company or institution.
13. Enter your title or position.
14. Enter your department or division.
15. Enter any additional extensions or information (e.g., office number).
16. Enter your job title.
17. Enter the name of your supervisor.
18. Enter your location or work site.
19. Enter any comments or notes about your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request to borrow** (`request_borrow`): Enter a brief description of the equipment you would like to borrow.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Select the type of equipment (e.g., laptop, printer, etc.).
* **Equipment Name** (`equipment_name`): Enter the specific name of the equipment (e.g., MacBook, Epson printer).
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`): Enter your name as the technician who will be handling the equipment.
* **Date Issued** (`date_issued`): Select the date the equipment was issued.
* **Expected Return Date** (`expected_return_date`): Select the date you expect the equipment to be returned.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`): Choose the current status of the equipment (e.g., Available, Loaned out, Lost, Broken).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments or notes about the equipment or your request.
* **Loaner Name** (`loaner_name`): Select the person or group that borrowed the equipment.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`): Enter your name as the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`): Enter your company or institution.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`): Enter your title or position.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitter_department`): Enter your department or division.
* **Submitter Extension** (`submitter_extension`): Enter any additional extensions or information (e.g., office number).
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_jobtitle`): Enter your job title.
* **Submitter Supervisor** (`submitter_supervisor`): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Submitter Location** (`submitter_location`): Enter your location or work site.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`): Enter any comments or notes about your request.
