# Equipment Inventory Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Equipment Inventory Form" is a tool used to record and manage information about equipment in the inventory. This form helps ensure that all equipment is accurately and thoroughly documented, which helps in preventing loss or damage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of equipment you want to add to the inventory.
2. Fill in the details of the equipment in the text area provided.
3. Click the "Submit" button to add the equipment to the inventory.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment List (Select Multiple)**:
	+ This field allows you to select the type of equipment you want to add to the inventory.
	+ Select one or more of the options provided to choose the correct equipment type.
* **Equipment Details (Text)**:
	+ In this field, you will provide a detailed description of the equipment.
	+ Please provide as much information as possible about the equipment, such as its condition, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Submit (Button)**:
	+ Click this button to add the selected equipment to the inventory.
	+ Make sure to review your input before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Tips
Please make sure to accurately and thoroughly document each piece of equipment to prevent loss or damage. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact the relevant department for assistance.
