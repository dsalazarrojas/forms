# Monthly Tech Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Tech Evaluation Form is a tool for managers to provide feedback and comments on a technician's performance on a monthly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the evaluation period from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the technician's name in the "Technician Name" field.
3. Enter the manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
4. Enter the date of the evaluation in the "Evaluation Date" field.
5. Select the rating of the technician's performance in the "Rating" field.
6. Enter any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
7. Select the frequency of the evaluation (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, or Weekly) in the "Evaluation Frequency" field.
8. Enter the Evaluation Form ID in the "Evaluation Form ID" field.
9. Enter the name of the tool assigned to the technician in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Enter the name of the manager's manager in the "Manager Name 2" field (if applicable).
11. Enter any additional comments or feedback from the manager's manager in the "Manager Comments" field (if applicable).
12. Select the category of the evaluation form in the "Evaluation Form Category" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): This field is for entering the name of the technician being evaluated.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): This field is for entering the name of the manager providing the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): This field is for selecting the date of the evaluation.
* **Rating** (`rating`, number, required): This field is for selecting the rating of the technician's performance (likely on a scale of 1-5).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): This field is for entering any comments or feedback on the technician's performance.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, text, required): This field is for entering the status of the form submission.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the period of the evaluation (Monthly, Bi-Weekly, or Weekly).
* **Evaluation Frequency** (`evaluation_frequency`, select_one, required): This field is for selecting the frequency of the evaluation (Monthly, Quarterly, or Semi-Annual).
* **Evaluation Form ID** (`evaluation_form_id`, number, required): This field is for entering the unique identifier of the evaluation form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): This field is for entering the name of the tool assigned to the technician.
* **Manager Name 2** (`manager_name_2`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the manager's manager (if applicable).
* **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, optional): This field is for entering any additional comments or feedback from the manager's manager (if applicable).
* **Evaluation Form Category** (`evaluation_form_category`, text, optional): This field is for selecting the category of the evaluation form (if applicable).

Tips:
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and access to fill out this form.
* Be specific and detailed in your comments and feedback.
* Review and revise this form as needed to meet the evolving needs of your team.
* Store and retain a record of previous evaluations to track progress and identify areas for improvement.
