# Radio Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for technicians to report and document the repair process, symptoms, and resolution of a radio. The form helps to ensure that all necessary information is captured and communicated effectively, reducing the risk of errors and improving the overall repair process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of device you are repairing (Radio or TV).
2. Describe the symptoms of the issue.
3. Enter the serial number of the device.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue.
5. Choose a repair method (optional).
6. Estimate the cost of the repair (optional).
7. Choose your payment method (optional).
8. Enter your contact number for follow-up.
9. Add any relevant comments or notes from the technician.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Radio Type** (`radio_type`, select_one): Select the type of device you are repairing (Radio or TV).
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, text): Describe the symptoms of the issue, such as what is not working or how the device is behaving.
* **Serial Number** (`serial_number`, text): Enter the serial number of the device, which can be found on the device's packaging or manufacturer's website.
* **Issue Description** (`issue_description`, text): Provide a detailed description of the issue, including any error messages or codes you have encountered.
* **Repair Method** (`repair_method`, text): If applicable, choose the method used to repair the device (e.g., replacement, adjustment, or other).
* **Cost** (`cost`, number): Estimate the cost of the repair (optional, not required for payment method).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Choose how you intend to make payment (Cash, Credit, PayPal).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text): Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **Technician Comment** (`technician_comment`, text): Add any relevant comments or notes from the technician performing the repair.
