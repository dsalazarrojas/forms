# Hardware Issue Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hardware Issue Report Form is designed to collect information about hardware issues that need to be reported to the technical team. This form helps to streamline the process of reporting and resolving hardware issues, ensuring that all necessary details are captured and communicated effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have permission from the reported-by section to report the issue on their behalf.
2. Fill in the user-input fields (e.g., user input, technical details, product details, product model, serial number).
3. Select the correct current status and resolution method for the issue.
4. Provide a detailed description of the issue and steps to reproduce it.
5. Attach any relevant files or images related to the issue.
6. Enter the submitted-by section details (e.g., submitted by, submitted title, submitted department, submitted position, submitted phone, submitted email).
7. Select the submitted-to section details (e.g., submitted to, submitted to title, submitted to department, submitted to position).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This is the initial input from the person reporting the issue.
* **Technical Details**: Provide detailed description of the problem.
* **Product Details**: Enter the product details (model number).
* **Product Model**: Enter the product model.
* **Serial Number**: Enter the product serial number.
* **Date Of Purchase**: Enter the date of purchase of the hardware.
* **Description Of Issue**: Describe the problem in detail.
* **Steps To Reproduce**: Describe the steps to reproduce the issue.
* **Expected Revised Behavior**: Describe the expected outcome after resolution.
* **Current Status**: Select the current status of the issue (e.g., New, Open, In Progress, Resolved).
* **Resolution Method**: Select the method of resolution (e.g., Phone, Email, Live Chat, In Person, Other).
* **Reported By**: Enter the name of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Title**: Enter the title of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Department**: Enter the department of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Position**: Enter the position of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Email**: Enter the email of the person reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Date**: Enter the date of reporting the issue.
* **Reported By Time**: Enter the time of reporting the issue.
* **Reported To**: Select the department to which the issue is reported (e.g., Tech Support, Quality Assurance, Management).
* **Reported To Title**: Enter the title of the person receiving the issue.
* **Reported To Department**: Enter the department of the person receiving the issue.
* **Reported To Position**: Enter the position of the person receiving the issue.
* **Reported To Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person receiving the issue.
* **Reported To Email**: Enter the email of the person receiving the issue.
* **Issue Description**: Describe the issue in detail.
* **Attachment**: Attach any relevant files or images related to the issue.
* **Attachment Description**: Describe the attached file or image.
* **Attachment Upload**: Upload the attached file or image.
* **Submitted By**: Select the person submitting the issue (e.g., Technician, Client).
* **Submitted Title**: Enter the title of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted Department**: Enter the department of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted Position**: Enter the position of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted Email**: Enter the email of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time**: Enter the time of submission.
* **Submitted Comment**: Enter any additional comments related to the issue.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the issue.
* **Submitted To**: Select the department to which the issue is submitted (e.g., Tech Support, Quality Assurance, Management).
