# Appliance Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Appliance Service Request Form is designed to gather necessary information from customers for our appliance service requests. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure we can provide the best service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your full name in the **Full Name** field (Field 3).
2. Enter your email address in the **Email Address** field (Field 4).
3. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field (Field 5).
4. Provide the service address where the appliance is located in the **Service Address** field (Field 6).
5. Select the **Type of Appliance** (Field 7) that requires service.
6. Enter the brand and model of the appliance in the **Brand and Model** field (Field 8).
7. If applicable, enter the serial number of the appliance in the **Serial Number** field (Field 9).
8. If the appliance is still under warranty, select **Yes, manufacturer warranty** or **Yes, extended warranty** in the **Is it under warranty?** field (Field 11).
9. In the **Describe the problem or issue** field (Field 13), describe the problem or issue you are experiencing with your appliance.
10. Select how often the issue occurs in the **How often does the issue occur?** field (Field 15).
11. If you have tried any troubleshooting steps, select **True** or **False** in the **Have you tried any troubleshooting steps?** field (Field 16). If you have tried steps, briefly describe them in the **Describe what troubleshooting you have done** field (Field 17).
12. Select your preferred service date and time slot in the **Preferred service date** and **Preferred time slot** fields (Fields 19 and 20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Field 2): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (Field 3): Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you about your service request.
* **Phone Number** (Field 4): Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your service request.
* **Service Address** (Field 5): Enter the address where we will need to visit for service.
* **Type of Appliance** (Field 7): Select the type of appliance that requires service from the list:
	+ Refrigerator
	+ Washing Machine
	+ Dishwasher
	+ Microwave
	+ Oven
	+ Air Conditioner
	+ Water Heater
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Brand and Model** (Field 8): Enter the brand and model of the appliance that requires service.
* **Serial Number** (Field 9): If the appliance has a serial number, enter it here (optional).
* **Is it under warranty?** (Field 11): Select one of the warranty options if applicable.
* **Describe the problem or issue** (Field 13): Clearly describe the problem or issue with your appliance.
* **How often does the issue occur?** (Field 15): Select from the options:
	+ Constantly
	+ Frequently
	+ Occasionally
	+ Rarely
* **Have you tried any troubleshooting steps?** (Field 16): Select if you have tried any troubleshooting steps.
* **Describe what troubleshooting you have done** (Field 17): If you have tried troubleshooting steps, describe them in this field.
* **Preferred service date** (Field 19): Select the date you prefer the service visit to be scheduled.
* **Preferred time slot** (Field 20): Select your preferred time slot for the service visit:
	+ Morning - 8 AM to 12 PM
	+ Afternoon - 12 PM to 5 PM
	+ Evening - 5 PM to 8 PM
	+ Flexible
