# welding_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The welding inspection form is designed for weld inspections on production lines, focusing on ensuring quality and safety standards are met.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Enter production process information**. In the "Production Process" field, enter a brief description of the production process used.
2.  **Select the welding method**. Choose the method used for welding from the options provided.
3.  **Specify inspection date and time** (optional). If you want to track the inspection date and time, use the "Inspection Date" field to enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4.  **Enter inspector name and signature**. In the "Inspector Name" field, enter your name, and in the "Inspector Signature" field, write your signature.
5.  **Evaluate equipment condition**. In the "Equipment Condition" field, select whether the equipment was in good, fair, or poor condition.
6.  **Determine production status**. In the "Production Status" field, select whether the production was active or inactive.
7.  **Add notes and comments**. In the "Notes" field, provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
8.  **Enter weld and material IDs**. In the "Weld ID" and "Material ID" fields, enter the relevant IDs.
9.  **Specify machine ID**. In the "Machine ID" field, enter the ID of the machine used for welding.
10. **Enter production location**. In the "Production Location" field, specify the production location where the weld was performed.
11. **Specify shift**. In the "Shift" field, enter the shift during which the weld was performed.
12. **Inspect and record results**. In the "Inspection Result" field, select whether the weld passed or failed inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Production Process** (`production_process`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the production process used.
- **Welding Method** (`welding_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose the method used for welding from the provided options.
- **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, `date`, optional): If you want to track the inspection date and time, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name.
- **Inspector Signature** (`inspector_signature`, `text`, required): Write your signature.
- **Equipment Condition** (`equipment_condition`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the equipment was in good, fair, or poor condition.
- **Production Status** (`production_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether the production was active or inactive.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or observations about the inspection.
- **Weld ID** (`weld_id`, `text`, required): Enter the relevant ID for the weld.
- **Material ID** (`material_id`, `text`, required): Enter the relevant ID for the material used.
- **Machine ID** (`machine_id`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the machine used for welding.
- **Production Location** (`production_location`, `text`, required): Specify the production location where the weld was performed.
- **Shift** (`shift`, `text`, required): Enter the shift during which the weld was performed.
- **Inspection Result** (`inspection_result`, `select_multiple`, required): Select whether the weld passed or failed inspection.

**Tips**

*   Always ensure that you are filling out the form correctly and accurately, as any errors or inconsistencies may lead to incorrect or missed records.
*   Make sure to use the correct equipment condition and production status to reflect the actual state of the production line.
*   Use clear and legible writing for notes and comments.
*   Always sign and date your form to maintain accountability and compliance.
