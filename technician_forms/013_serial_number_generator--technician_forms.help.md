# Serial Number Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Serial Number Generator form is a tool used to generate serial numbers for various purposes. This form is typically used to create serial numbers for inventory tracking, product identification, and other similar applications. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired length for your serial number.
2. Choose a prefix for your serial number.
3. Choose a suffix for your serial number.
4. Select whether the generated serial number should be Good or Fair.
5. Once all fields have been filled in, press the Generate Serial Number button to generate your serial number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Serial Number Length** (`<number>`, optional): Select the length of the serial number you want to generate. This can be any whole number between 1 and 10.
- **Prefix** (`<text>`, optional): Enter a prefix for your serial number. This can be any combination of letters and numbers.
- **Suffix** (`<text>`, optional): Enter a suffix for your serial number. This can be any combination of letters and numbers.
- **Serial Number** (`<text>`, optional): This field is automatically populated with the generated serial number.
- **Generate Serial Number** (`<select_one>`, required): Select Good or Fair to determine the quality of the generated serial number. This option will affect the generated serial number.

## Tips
* Make sure to select a serial number length that is suitable for your intended use.
* Keep the prefix and suffix short and simple to avoid errors.
* The Serial Number field is populated automatically once you press the Generate Serial Number button.
* The quality of the generated serial number (Good or Fair) will affect its accuracy and consistency.
