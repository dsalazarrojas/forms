# Daily Vehicle Inspection Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Vehicle Inspection Report is a form used to document the condition of a vehicle after inspection. It helps identify any issues that may affect the vehicle's performance or safety and provides a record of the inspection date, vehicle details, and conditions of the engine, brake, and tire systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Vehicle ID to identify the vehicle being inspected.
2. Enter the Vehicle Year and Model details.
3. Enter the 17-digit Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) to identify the vehicle uniquely.
4. Select the Vehicle Type to indicate the type of vehicle being inspected (e.g., passenger, commercial, etc.).
5. Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
6. Assess the condition of the engine and enter your comments.
7. Assess the condition of the brake system and enter your comments.
8. Assess the condition of the tires and enter your comments.
9. Enter the date of the inspection to record the completion time of the form.
10. Add any comments or additional information relevant to the vehicle inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle ID (1)**: Enter the Vehicle ID as printed on the vehicle's registration plate or in your fleet management system.
* **Vehicle Year (2)**: Enter the year of manufacture for the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (3)**: Enter the vehicle's manufacturer (e.g., Toyota, Ford, etc.).
* **VIN (4)**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number to uniquely identify the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Type (5)**: Select the type of vehicle being inspected (e.g., passenger, commercial, etc.).
* **Mileage (6)**: Enter the current mileage of the vehicle.
* **Engine Condition (7)**: Assess the condition of the engine and enter your comments (e.g., normal, worn, needs repair, etc.).
* **Brake Condition (8)**: Assess the condition of the brake system and enter your comments.
* **Tire Condition (9)**: Assess the condition of the tires and enter your comments.
* **Vehicle Inspection Report Date (10)**: Enter the date of the inspection to record the completion time of the form.
* **Comments (11)**: Add any additional comments or information relevant to the vehicle inspection.
