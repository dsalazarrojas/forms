# Fire Damper Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Damper Inspection Form is designed to collect essential information related to the fire damper system's functionality. This form helps ensure that the system is in good working condition and meets relevant standards or regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of fire damper system you are inspecting.
2. Choose the type of system (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
3. Identify the type of damper (Automatic, Semi-Automatic, or Manual).
4. Provide the location of the fire damper system.
5. (Optional) Enter any comments or observations regarding the system.
6. Select the date of the inspection.
7. Choose the time of the inspection.
8. Assign an inspector to record their name and/or initials.
9. (Optional) Enter additional comments or observations about the system.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Damper System**: Select one or more of the options to indicate the type(s) of fire damper system you are inspecting. Possible options: Yes or No.
* **System Type**: Select the type of system (Residential, Commercial, or Industrial).
* **Damper Type**: Choose the type of damper (Automatic, Semi-Automatic, or Manual).
* **Location**: Enter the specific location of the fire damper system, such as a room or building number.
* **Comments**: Enter any comments or observations you have about the system. This field is optional and allows you to provide additional context.
* **Date Of Inspection**: Select the date on which the inspection took place.
* **Time Of Inspection**: Select the time on which the inspection occurred.
* **Assigned Inspector**: Select the inspector who performed the inspection. Choose from chatjimmy or Other (type in the inspector's name).
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or observations about the system. This field is optional and allows you to provide further context.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy when selecting the system type and damper type options.
* Be as specific as possible when entering the location of the fire damper system.
* Use the comments fields to provide any additional context or information about the system.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact your supervisor or team leader for assistance.
