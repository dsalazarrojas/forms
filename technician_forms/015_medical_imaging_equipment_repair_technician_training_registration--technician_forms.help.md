# Medical Imaging Equipment Repair Technician Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register medical imaging equipment repair technicians for training programs. This form captures information about the technician, including their provider name, course name, enrollment date, training provider, confirmation date, course hours, and training status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Select the correct training provider from the options listed.
3. Ensure that the enrollment date and confirmation date are in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter the correct number of course hours completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name (1)**: Enter the name of the medical imaging equipment repair technician provider, e.g., ABC Medical Equipment Services.
* **Course Name (2)**: Enter the name of the training course, e.g., Fundamentals of MRI Repair.
* **Enrollment Date (3)**: Enter the date the technician enrolled in the training course, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Training Provider (4)**: Select the training provider from the options listed, e.g., Provider 1, Provider 2, or Provider 3.
* **Confirmation Date (5)**: Enter the date the technician confirmed their enrollment in the training course, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Course Hours (6)**: Enter the total number of hours completed in the training course.
* **Course Hours Hint (7)**: This field is a text field for additional information about the course hours, e.g., a description or notes.
* **Training Status (8)**: Select the status of the training, e.g., Not Started, Completed, or In Progress.
