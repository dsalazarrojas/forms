# Technical Hire Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The technical hire agreement form is a document used to formalize the terms and conditions of a technical engagement between a manager and a contractor. This form helps in documenting all the necessary details of the engagement to ensure that all parties are on the same page and that there's a clear understanding of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all fields are accurate and complete.
- Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand what information is being requested.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant personnel for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technical Hire Agreement Form Description**: This is a brief description of the technical hire agreement form. It's an optional field where you can provide a short summary of the agreement.
* **Hire Date**: This field is used to specify the date when the technical hire will start. Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Hire Time**: This field is used to specify the time when the technical hire will start. Enter the time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Contractor Name**: This is the name of the contractor being hired.
* **Contract Title**: This is the title of the contract, which can be a descriptive name of the project or engagement.
* **Contract Start Date**: This field is used to specify the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date**: This field is used to specify the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Type**: This is the type of contract being made. You can select one of the available options.
* **Contract Term**: This is a brief description of the contract duration.
* **Manager Name**: This is the name of the manager who is making the hire.
* **Manager Title**: This is the title of the manager who is making the hire.
* **Manager Email**: This is the email address of the manager.
* **Manager Phone**: This is the phone number of the manager.
* **Contractor Email**: This is the email address of the contractor.
* **Contractor Phone**: This is the phone number of the contractor.
* **Contractor Title**: This is the title of the contractor.
* **Contractor Address**: This is the address of the contractor.
* **Signature**: This is a field for the contractor to sign and confirm the agreement.
* **Manager Signature**: This is a field for the manager to sign and confirm the agreement.
* **Contract Notes**: These are any additional notes or comments about the contract.
* **Manager Contract Notes**: These are any additional notes or comments from the manager about the contract.

## Tips
- Please ensure that both the manager and the contractor sign and date the agreement.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
- If there are any discrepancies or issues, do not hesitate to reach out to the relevant personnel for clarification.
