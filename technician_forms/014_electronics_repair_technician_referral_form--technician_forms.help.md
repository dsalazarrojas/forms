# Electronics Repair Technician Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to refer customers to other electronic repair technicians when their issues are beyond the expertise of the current technician.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine if the referral is necessary for the customer.
2. Answer "Yes" or "No" to the referral question.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for service type and service area if applicable.
4. Enter the customer's email address and contact person's name (if known).
5. Briefly describe the issue or reason for the referral.
6. Check if you recommend the technician to the customer.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referral** (`referral_form`, dropdown, required/optional): Answer "Yes" if you believe a referral is necessary, "No" otherwise.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the electronic repair technician referring the customer.
* **Customer Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the customer's email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number (if known).
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, dropdown, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the service type is beyond your expertise, "No" otherwise.
* **Service Area** (`service_area`, dropdown, required/optional): Select "Yes" if the service area is beyond your expertise, "No" otherwise.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the issue or reason for the referral.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name (if known).
* **Recommend Technician** (`technician_recom`, dropdown, required/optional): Check "Yes" if you recommend the technician to the customer.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click to submit the form and initiate the referral.

## Tips
* Ensure the referral is necessary and not a duplication.
* Complete the form thoroughly and accurately to ensure the referral is successful.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor for guidance.
