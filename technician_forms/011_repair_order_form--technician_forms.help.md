# Repair Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Repair Order Form is designed to collect essential details from customers for the purpose of processing their repair order. This includes customer information, product details, contact information, payment information, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields. While there are no mandatory fields marked as required, it's best to fill in all fields that have information to ensure accurate submission.
2. Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant customer details in the "Customer Details" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the product being sent for repair in the "Product Details" field.
4. Add any relevant contact information, including email and phone number, in the "Contact Info" field.
5. For the "Repair Order 2" field, please provide any additional comments or notes about the repair order.
6. If paying by email invoice, please provide the payment details in the "Payment Info" field. If paying by other means, please indicate so in this field.
7. Use the "Notes" field for any additional comments or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Repair Order**: This is the main field for describing the repair order. Please provide a detailed description of the product being sent for repair.
* **Customer Details**: This field is for entering customer information, including your name and contact details.
* **Product Details**: This field is for providing a detailed description of the product being sent for repair.
* **Contact Info**: This field is for adding any relevant contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Repair Order 2**: This field is for providing any additional comments or notes about the repair order.
* **Payment Info**: This field is for providing payment details if paying by email invoice.
* **Notes**: This field is for providing any additional comments or instructions.
* **Customer Email**: This field is for entering the customer's email address for communication purposes. 

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the fields to ensure accurate submission.
* If any fields are unclear or you are unsure of the expected format, please contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
* You can use this form for both new and existing customers.
* Please ensure the submitted form is in English, as other languages are not supported.
