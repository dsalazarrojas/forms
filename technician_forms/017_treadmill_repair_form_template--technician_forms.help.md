# Treadmill Repair Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to guide technicians through the process of documenting and reporting on treadmill repairs. The purpose of this form is to provide a structured format for technicians to collect and record all necessary information related to a treadmill repair, including problem descriptions, repair procedures, and notes.

## How To Complete This Form

### 1. General Information (ID: 1)
**General Information** (text, optional)
This field is for any additional information related to the repair that does not fit into other categories. Please enter any relevant details about the equipment being repaired, such as its age, condition, or any previous maintenance history.

### 2. Problem Description (ID: 2)
**Problem Description** (text, required)
Describe the issue that prompted the repair, including any symptoms or error messages that appeared.

### 3. Problem Image (ID: 3)
**Problem Image** (text, optional)
Upload an image of the issue with the treadmill. This can be a photo of the problem area or a diagram illustrating the problem.

### 4. Problem Video (ID: 4)
**Problem Video** (text, optional)
Upload a video recording of the problem, if relevant. This can be a helpful way to demonstrate complex issues.

### 5. Repair Information (ID: 5)
**Repair Information** (text, required)
Describe the repair process and any steps taken to address the problem. Be thorough and include any relevant details about parts replaced, actions taken, or procedures followed.

### 6. Repair Image (ID: 6)
**Repair Image** (text, optional)
Upload an image of the repaired area, if applicable.

### 7. Repair Video (ID: 7)
**Repair Video** (text, optional)
Upload a video recording of the repair process, if relevant.

### 8. Repair Notes (ID: 8)
**Repair Notes** (note, optional)
Add any additional comments or notes related to the repair, including any challenges encountered or concerns raised.

### 9. Technician Notes (ID: 9)
**Technician Notes** (text, required)
Enter your personal notes and observations about the repair, including any recommendations or follow-up actions.

### 10. Technician Signature (ID: 10)
**Technician Signature** (text, optional)
Add your signature to confirm that you completed the repair and reviewed the form.

### 11. Customer Signature (ID: 11)
**Customer Signature** (text, optional)
Add the customer's signature to confirm that the repair was completed and that they understand any necessary next steps or follow-up actions.

### 12. Customer Name (ID: 12)
**Customer Name** (text, optional)
Enter the customer's name.

### 13. Customer Phone (ID: 13)
**Customer Phone** (text, optional)
Enter the customer's phone number.

### 14. Customer Email (ID: 14)
**Customer Email** (email, optional)
Enter the customer's email address.

## Tips

- Make sure to be thorough and accurate when completing the form to ensure a clear record of the repair process.
- Use the "Repair Information" field to document any relevant procedures or actions taken during the repair.
- If you have any concerns or questions about the repair, use the "Technician Notes" field to document them.
- Always obtain the customer's signature and contact information, if applicable, to ensure follow-up and next steps are well communicated.
