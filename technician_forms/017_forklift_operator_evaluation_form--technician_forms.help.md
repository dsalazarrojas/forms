# Forklift Operator Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Forklift Operator Evaluation Form is designed to assess a forklift operator's performance in the workplace. It helps facilities management, maintenance, or operations teams to evaluate the operator's adherence to safety procedures, load handling skills, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the forklift operator being evaluated.
2. Enter the date of the evaluation.
3. Select the rating for Safety Procedures, Load Handling, and Overall Performance.
4. Enter any comments or additional information.
5. Enter the signature of the evaluator.
6. Enter the completion date and time of the evaluation.
7. Enter the department and facility where the evaluation took place.
8. Enter the equipment type and status.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Operator Name** (`operator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the forklift operator being evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Safety Procedures** (`rating_1`, select_one, required): Select the rating for Safety Procedures:
	+ Forklifts
	+ Safety Precautions
	+ Warehouse Operations
* **Load Handling** (`rating_2`, select_one, required): Select the rating for Load Handling:
	+ Correct
	+ Incorrect
* **Overall Performance** (`rating_3`, select_one, required): Select the rating for Overall Performance:
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or additional information.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): Enter the signature of the evaluator.
* **Evaluation Completed** (`evaluation_completed`, text, required): Enter the completion date and time of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation By** (`evaluation_by`, text, required): Enter the name of the evaluator.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, required): Enter the date and time the evaluation was completed.
* **Tool Used** (`tool_used`, text, optional): Enter any information about the tool or equipment used during the evaluation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the evaluation.
* **Forklift Model** (`forklift_model`, text, optional): Enter the model of the forklift being evaluated.
* **Evaluation By Title** (`evaluation_by_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the evaluator.
* **Operator ID** (`operator_id`, text, required): Enter the ID number of the forklift operator being evaluated.
* **Evaluation By Department** (`evaluation_by_department`, text, required): Enter the department conducting the evaluation.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the name of the department conducting the evaluation.
* **Department ID** (`department_id`, text, required): Enter the ID number of the department conducting the evaluation.
* **Facility** (`facility`, text, required): Enter the name of the facility where the evaluation took place.
* **Facility ID** (`facility_id`, text, required): Enter the ID number of the facility where the evaluation took place.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of equipment being evaluated.
* **Equipment Status** (`equipment_status`, text, optional): Enter the status of the equipment being evaluated.
* **Rating 1 Comment** (`rating_1_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the rating for Safety Procedures.
* **Rating 2 Comment** (`rating_2_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the rating for Load Handling.
* **Rating 3 Comment** (`rating_3_comment`, text, optional): Enter any comments about the rating for Overall Performance.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.
* Ensure that the evaluations are being conducted by authorized personnel.
* Use the correct equipment and facility for the evaluation.
* Keep the evaluation form up-to-date and consistent with company policies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please consult with your supervisor or designated team member.
