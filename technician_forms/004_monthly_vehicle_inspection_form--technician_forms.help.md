# Monthly Vehicle Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Monthly Vehicle Inspection Form is a tool for technicians to record information about a vehicle's condition during a monthly inspection. This form helps to gather data on the vehicle's year, make, model, registration, color, condition, and inspection date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the vehicle's year.
2. Enter the vehicle's make.
3. Enter the vehicle's model.
4. Provide the vehicle's registration number.
5. Select the vehicle's color from the options provided.
6. Assess the vehicle's condition (Good, Fair, or Poor).
7. Enter the date of the inspection.
8. Add any additional notes about the vehicle's condition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Year** (`vehicle_year`, Number, **Required: No**): Fill in the vehicle's year.
* **Make** (`vehicle_make`, Text, **Required: No**): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Model** (`vehicle_model`, Text, **Required: No**): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, Text, **Required: No**): Provide the vehicle's registration number.
* **Color** (`vehicle_color`, Text, **Required: No**): Select the vehicle's color from the options provided.
* **Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, Select One, **Required: No**): Assess the vehicle's condition as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, Date, **Required: No**): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, **Required: No**): Add any additional notes about the vehicle's condition.
