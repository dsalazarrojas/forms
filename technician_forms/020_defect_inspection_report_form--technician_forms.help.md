# Defect Inspection Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record defects found during an inspection of inspected items. The form captures the essential information needed to report and document defects, including the date and time of the inspection, the items inspected, and any defects found.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have all the necessary information and documentation available before starting the form.
2. On the first page, enter the date of the inspection. This should be the date on which the inspection took place.
3. On the second page, select the time of the inspection from the available time options or enter the time manually.
4. On the third page, enter the items that were inspected.
5. On the fourth page, enter your name and title as the inspector.
6. On the fifth page, enter your email address.
7. On the sixth page, provide a detailed description of any defects found during the inspection.
8. On the seventh page, enter any additional information or notes about the defects found.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, date, required/optional): The date on which the inspection took place.
* **Inspection Time** (`inspection_time`, time, required/optional): The time at which the inspection took place.
* **Inspected Item** (`inspected_item`, text, required/optional): A description of the items that were inspected.
* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`, text, required/optional): Your name and title as the inspector.
* **Inspector Email** (`inspector_email`, email, required/optional): Your email address.
* **Defects Found** (`defects_found`, text, required/optional): A detailed description of any defects found during the inspection.
* **Defect Details** (`defect_details`, note, required/optional): Additional information or notes about the defects found.

## Tips
* Ensure that you accurately and thoroughly document any defects found during the inspection.
* Be as detailed as possible when describing the defects.
* If you need to report multiple defects, you can provide multiple entries on the "Defect Details" page.
* If you are unsure about any of the information required, contact your supervisor or refer to your organization's policies and procedures.
