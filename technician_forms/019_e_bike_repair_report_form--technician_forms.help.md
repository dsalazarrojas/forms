* **Repair Status** (`repair_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the current status of the repair (Complete, Incomplete, or Pending).
* **Technician** (`technician`, text, optional): Enter the name or initials of the technician who performed the repair.
* **Customer Service Note** (`customer_service_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from customer service, such as follow-up actions or customer concerns.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool or equipment used for the repair.
