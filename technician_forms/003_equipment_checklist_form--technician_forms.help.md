# equipment_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment checklist form is used to create a list of equipment for inventory or asset management purposes. This form helps users to identify the essential and non-essential equipment, along with the date and time of usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the essential equipment from the available options.
2. Choose the non-essential equipment from the list.
3. Enter the date of usage.
4. Enter the time of usage.
5. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Checklist Fields**: This section is used to select the available options for essential and non-essential equipment.
* **Essential Equipment**: Select the equipment that is necessary for the task or duty. This will help in prioritizing the equipment for inventory or asset management.
* **Non-Essential Equipment**: Select any other equipment that is not necessary but can be used in the task or duty.
* **Date**: Enter the date of usage, including the day, month, and year.
* **Time**: Enter the time of usage, including the hour, minute, and period (AM/PM).
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the usage of the equipment.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct equipment for the task or duty.
* Be accurate when entering the date and time of usage.
* Use the notes section to add any additional information that might be useful for future reference.
