# Auto Service Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Auto Service Appointment Form is designed to collect information from customers for scheduling auto service appointments. This includes customer contact information, vehicle details, appointment preferences, and any specific service concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields (marked with \*).
2. Ensure you enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your driver's license or government-issued ID.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you about your appointment and any updates.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your appointment and any updates.
* **Preferred Contact Method (5)**: Select how you would like to be contacted about your appointment and any updates (Phone, Email, or Text Message).
* **Vehicle Year (7)**: Enter the current year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make (8)**: Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., Toyota, Ford, Honda).
* **Vehicle Model (9)**: Enter the model of your vehicle (e.g., Camry, F-150, Civic).
* **Vehicle Color (10)**: Enter the color of your vehicle (if applicable).
* **License Plate (11)**: Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Current Mileage (12)**: Enter your vehicle's current odometer reading.
* **Preferred Service Date (14)**: Select a date for your appointment (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Preferred Time (15)**: Select a time slot for your appointment (8:00 AM - 4:00 PM).
* **Type of Service Needed (16)**: Select all services you need (e.g., Oil Change, Tire Service).
* **Specific Issues or Concerns (18)**: Describe any problems or issues with your vehicle.
* **Warning Lights (19)**: Indicate if any warning lights are currently on in your vehicle.
* **Loaner Vehicle (21)**: Indicate if you need a loaner vehicle for your appointment.
* **Maximum Approval Amount for Repairs (22)**: Enter a maximum amount you're willing to spend on repairs (if you're concerned about potential costs).
* **Consent to Proceed (23)**: Authorize the shop to perform the service by selecting "Yes, proceed" or "No, contact me first."

Please submit the form once you have completed all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
