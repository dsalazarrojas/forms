# Fleet Service Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fleet Service Report Form is a document used to record and document the service history of vehicles in the fleet. It captures essential information about the vehicle's condition, maintenance needs, and services performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the truck number in the "Truck Number" field. This is the unique identifier for the vehicle.
2. Enter the VIN number in the "VIN Number" field. This is the Vehicle Identification Number, which is unique to each vehicle.
3. Record the odometer reading in the "Odometer Reading" field. This indicates the current mileage of the vehicle.
4. If any maintenance needs are identified, enter them in the "Maintenance Needs" field. This is an opportunity to note any issues or concerns with the vehicle.
5. Select the service date in the "Service Date" field. This is the date the services were performed.
6. Sign and date the "Mechanic Signature" field. This is a verification of services performed by the mechanic.
7. Sign and date the "Customer Signature" field. This is a verification of services received by the customer.
8. If desired, enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field. This is an opportunity to add any other relevant information about the vehicle.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Truck Number** (`truck_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identifier for the vehicle.
- **VIN Number** (`vin_number`, text, required): Enter the Vehicle Identification Number, which is unique to each vehicle.
- **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, number, required): Record the current mileage of the vehicle.
- **Maintenance Needs** (`maintenance_needed`, text, optional): Note any issues or concerns with the vehicle.
- **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required): Select the date the services were performed.
- **Mechanic Signature** (`mechanic_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date to verify services performed by the mechanic.
- **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, note, optional): Sign and date to verify services received by the customer.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant information about the vehicle.
