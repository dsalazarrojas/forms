# Cell Tower Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to guide technicians in inspecting and evaluating the condition of cell towers. It helps them collect data on various aspects of the tower's condition, including its foundation, antennas, cabling, lighting, and overall safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter the date of inspection in the "Date of Inspection" field.
2.  Enter the time started in the "Time Started" field.
3.  Enter the unique tower ID and location name in the "Tower ID - Location Name" field.
4.  Enter your full name as the inspector in the "Technician Name" field.
5.  Evaluate the foundation condition of the tower by selecting one of the provided options in the "Foundation Condition" field.
6.  Check the tension and security of the guy wires and anchors, and select the corresponding option in the "Guy Wires and Anchors" field.
7.  Assess the severity of any metal degradation in the "Rust or Corrosion Status" field.
8.  Select all types of antennas inspected and present in the "Types of Antennas Inspected" field.
9.  Check if the cabling is properly secured in the "Cabling Security" field.
10. Verify the condition of weatherproofing by selecting one of the provided options in the "Weatherproofing Status" field.
11. Check if the FAA lighting is working properly in the "FAA Lighting Functionality" field.
12. Verify if the grounding system is functioning correctly in the "Grounding System Status" field.
13. Evaluate the condition of fencing and signage in the "Fencing and Signage Condition" field.
14. If necessary, describe any safety concerns or needed repairs in the "Safety Concerns or Comments" field.
15. Finally, select the overall inspection result in the "Overall Inspection Result" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Inspection** (`date_of_inspection`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the inspection in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Time Started** (`time_started`, `time`, required): Enter the time when the inspection started.
* **Tower ID - Location Name** (`tower_id`, `text`, required): Enter the unique ID and location name of the tower being inspected.
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the inspector.
* **Foundation Condition** (`foundation_condition`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the foundation condition by selecting one of the provided options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Guy Wires and Anchors** (`guy_wires_anchors`, `select_one`, required): Check the tension and security of the guy wires and anchors, and select the corresponding option: Pass, Fail, or N/A.
* **Rust or Corrosion Status** (`rust_corrosion`, `select_one`, required): Assess the severity of any metal degradation by selecting one of the provided options: None, Mild, Moderate, or Severe.
* **Types of Antennas Inspected** (`antennas_inspected`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all types of antennas inspected and present.
* **Cabling Security** (`cabling_security`, `select_one`, required): Check if the cabling is properly secured, and select the corresponding option: Secure or Needs Adjustment.
* **Weatherproofing Status** (`weatherproofing_status`, `select_one`, required): Verify the condition of seals and connectors, and select one of the provided options: Intact or Damaged.
* **FAA Lighting Functionality** (`faa_lighting`, `select_one`, required): Verify if the FAA lighting is working properly, and select the corresponding option: Working or Not Working.
* **Grounding System Status** (`grounding_system`, `select_one`, required): Verify if the grounding system is functioning correctly, and select the corresponding option: Verified or Not Verified.
* **Fencing and Signage Condition** (`fencing_signage`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate the condition of fencing and signage, and select the corresponding option: Good or Needs Repair.
* **Safety Concerns or Comments** (`safety_concerns`, `text`, optional): Describe any safety concerns or needed repairs.
* **Overall Inspection Result** (`overall_result`, `select_one`, required): Select the overall inspection result: Pass or Fail.
