# HACCP Cleaning Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) Cleaning Submission Form is designed to collect information from technicians about their cleaning activities. This form helps ensure that cleaning schedules, areas, and tasks are accurately recorded and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant options for each field, as needed.
2. Enter your information in the required fields.
3. Review your entries for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Technician Details** (`technician_details`)
Technician name and contact information should be entered here.

### **Equipment Used** (`equipment_used`)
Select the type of cleaning equipment used from the options provided (Cleaning, Sanitizing, Disinfecting).

### **Area** (`area_cleaning`)
Select the specific area that was cleaned from the options provided (Cleaning Area 1, Cleaning Area 2, Cleaning Area 3).

### **Tasks** (`cleaning_tasks`)
Select the tasks performed during the cleaning from the options provided (Task 1, Task 2, Task 3).

### **Schedule** (`schedule`)
Enter the date on which the cleaning was performed.

### **Time** (`schedule_time`)
Enter the time at which the cleaning was performed.

### **Verification** (`verification`)
Select the verification methods used from the options provided (Verification 1, Verification 2, Verification 3).

## Tips
* Ensure that you accurately report your cleaning activities.
* Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant team or department.
