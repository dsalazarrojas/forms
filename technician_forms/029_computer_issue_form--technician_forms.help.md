# Computer Issue Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Computer Issue Form is used to report computer issues to the technicians for support. This form gathers information about the reported issues to help technicians resolve them efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact email in the "Reporter name" and "Contact email" sections, respectively. These are required fields to ensure that the technician can get in touch with you for further assistance.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue in the "Issue summary" field. This should be a short description of the problem you are experiencing.
3. If you are able to reproduce the issue, please provide the steps to reproduce it in the "Steps to reproduce" field. This will help the technician narrow down the cause of the issue.
4. If you have any error messages or other relevant information, please copy and paste them into the "Error messages" field.
5. Indicate the severity of the issue by selecting the appropriate level from the "Severity" dropdown menu: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
6. Choose your preferred method of contact for the technician in the "Preferred contact" dropdown menu: Email, Phone, or Remote session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reporter name** (`Reporter name`, required): Please fill in your full name so the technician can get in touch with you.
* **Contact email** (`Contact email`, required): This will be used by the technician to follow up on your case.
* **Device model** (`Device model`, optional): If possible, provide the model or serial number of the affected device.
* **Issue summary** (`Issue summary`, required): Give a short description of the problem you are experiencing.
* **Steps to reproduce** (`Steps to reproduce`, optional): If you are able to consistently reproduce the issue, please describe the steps to do so.
* **Error messages** (`Error messages`, optional): Copy and paste any error text you see.
* **Severity** (`Severity`, required): Select the appropriate level of impact on your work or operations: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
* **Preferred contact** (`Preferred contact`, required): Choose how you would like the technician to contact you: Email, Phone, or Remote session.
