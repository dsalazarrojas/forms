## Personal Cyber Security Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you assess your cybersecurity posture and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page below and answer the questions honestly.
2. Select your answer for each question from the provided options.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, even if the answers seem obvious or you're unsure.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Strong Password** (`password_strength`, `number`, required: `false`): Please rate the strength of your password, with 1 being weak and 10 being extremely strong. Note that this is a subjective assessment, but a higher rating generally indicates a stronger password.
* **Use Two-Factor Authentication** (`two_factor_auth`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Do you use two-factor authentication for your online accounts? Please select 'Yes' if you do, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Please enter your email address associated with your online accounts.
* **Password Manager** (`password_manager`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Do you use any password manager software? If so, which one do you use? Please select multiple options if you use more than one.
* **Antivirus Software** (`antivirus_software`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Do you use antivirus software on your device? Please select 'Yes' if you do, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Regular Backups** (`backup_data`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Do you regularly back up your important data? Please select 'Yes' if you do, and 'No' if you don't.
* **Software Updates** (`software_updates`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Do you keep your software up to date? Please select 'Yes' if you do, and 'No' if you don't.
