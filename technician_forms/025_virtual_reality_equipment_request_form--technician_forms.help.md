# virtual_reality_equipment_request_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The virtual reality equipment request form is designed to allow students to request virtual reality equipment for educational purposes. This form is intended for students who need assistance with technical issues related to the use of virtual reality equipment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your student ID or identification card.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, `text`, required): Please enter the name of your instructor who is requesting the virtual reality equipment.
* **Class Number** (`class_number`, `text`, optional): If you are a student in a specific class, please enter the class number or course code.
* **Equipment Requested** (`equipment_requested`, `text`, required): Describe the virtual reality equipment you need assistance with.
* **Reason For Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation of why you are requesting the virtual reality equipment.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter a number where you can be reached if additional information is required.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates on your equipment request.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, `select_one`, required): Select the technician who will assist you.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your equipment request.
* **Submitted Date** (`submitted_date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask your instructor or technical support for clarification.
* Please review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
