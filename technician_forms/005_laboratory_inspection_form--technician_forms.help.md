# laboratory_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The laboratory inspection form is designed to gather essential information about a facility's laboratory and the person conducting the inspection. It helps document and track laboratory inspections, ensuring accuracy and completeness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by a *asterisk) with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you've provided the correct information.
3. If you're unsure about any field or question, consult with the relevant personnel or refer to your organization's guidelines and policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (id: 1): Enter the contact person's name and relevant contact details, such as phone number and email.
	+ *Required*
* **Laboratory Type** (id: 2): Select the type of laboratory (e.g., Clinical, Non-Clinical, etc.).
* **Laboratory Type Other** (id: 3): If the laboratory type is not listed, provide a brief description.
* **Facility Name** (id: 4): Enter the facility's name.
* **Facility Street Address** (id: 5): Enter the facility's street address.
* **Facility City** (id: 6): Enter the facility's city or town.
* **Facility State** (id: 7): Enter the facility's state or region.
* **Facility Zip** (id: 8): Enter the facility's zip or postal code.
* **Contact First Name** (id: 9): Enter the contact person's first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (id: 10): Enter the contact person's last name.
* **Contact Phone** (id: 11): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (id: 12): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Laboratory License** (id: 13): Enter the laboratory's license number or certificate.
* **Laboratory Certifications** (id: 14): Enter any relevant laboratory certifications.
* **Facility Type** (id: 15): Enter the type of facility (e.g., public, private, etc.).
* **Facility Status** (id: 16): Select the facility's status (e.g., Active, Closed, etc.).
* **Inspection Date** (id: 17): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **Inspection Time** (id: 18): Enter the time of the inspection.
* **Inspector Name** (id: 19): Enter the name of the inspector.
* **Inspector ID Number** (id: 20): Enter the inspector's ID number or badge number.
* **Inspection Type** (id: 21): Enter the type of inspection (e.g., routine, special, etc.).
* **Facility License** (id: 22): Enter the facility's license number or certificate.
* **Facility License Number** (id: 23): Enter the facility's license number or certificate number.
* **Certification Date** (id: 24): Enter the date of the facility's certification.
* **Notes** (id: 25): Enter any additional notes or comments from the inspection.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field or question, consult with the relevant personnel or refer to your organization's guidelines and policies.
* Use a clear and legible handwriting for all text fields.
* Save the completed form as a document for record-keeping purposes.
