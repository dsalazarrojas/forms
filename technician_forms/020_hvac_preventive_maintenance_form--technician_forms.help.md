# hvac_preventive_maintenance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hvac_preventive_maintenance_form is a form for recording and tracking HVAC equipment preventive maintenance activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the technician's ID in the Technician Id field.
2. Select a date for the Maintenance Date field.
3. Enter the equipment's ID in the Equipment Id field.
4. Describe the equipment being maintained in the Equipment Description field.
5. Enter the name of the person performing the maintenance in the Maintenance Performed By field.
6. Select the type of maintenance (Routine or Emergency) in the Maintenance Type field.
7. Select the status of the maintenance (Completed, Not Completed, or Pending) in the Maintenance Status field.
8. Enter any additional notes regarding the maintenance in the Notes field.
9. Enter the contact phone number in the Contact Phone field.
10. Enter the contact email address in the Contact Email field.
11. Select who was assigned as the primary technician (chatjim or Other) in the Assigned Technician field.
12. Select if there is a second technician assigned (Yes or No) in the Assigned Technician 2 field.
13. Select if there is a third technician assigned (Yes or No) in the Assigned Technician 3 field.
14. Select if there is a fourth technician assigned (Yes or No) in the Assigned Technician 4 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Id (number)**: Enter the ID of the technician performing the maintenance.
* **Maintenance Date (date)**: Enter the date the maintenance was performed.
* **Equipment Id (number)**: Enter the ID of the equipment being maintained.
* **Equipment Description (text)**: Describe the equipment being maintained.
* **Maintenance Performed By (text)**: Enter the name of the person performing the maintenance.
* **Maintenance Type (select_one)**: Select whether the maintenance was Routine or Emergency.
* **Maintenance Status (select_multiple)**: Select the status of the maintenance (Completed, Not Completed, or Pending).
* **Notes (note)**: Enter any additional notes regarding the maintenance.
* **Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (email)**: Enter the contact email address.
* **Assigned Technician (select_one)**: Select the primary technician assigned to the maintenance (chatjim or Other).
* **Assigned Technician 2 (select_one)**: Select if there is a second technician assigned (Yes or No).
* **Assigned Technician 3 (select_one)**: Select if there is a third technician assigned (Yes or No).
* **Assigned Technician 4 (select_one)**: Select if there is a fourth technician assigned (Yes or No).
