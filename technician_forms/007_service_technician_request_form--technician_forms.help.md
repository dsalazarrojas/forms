# Service Technician Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Technician Request Form is a tool for users to submit a request for support with a service technician. It is a simple form that collects information about the user, including their name, contact information, and a description of the problem they are experiencing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the field labeled "Name".
2. Provide your email address in the field labeled "Email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "Phone".
4. Describe the problem you are experiencing in the field labeled "Problem Description".
5. If you have a preferred date for the service technician to visit, enter it in the field labeled "Preferred Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your full name to ensure that the service technician can contact you directly.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so that the service technician can contact you to confirm the appointment or provide updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number to ensure that the service technician can contact you quickly and efficiently.
* **Problem Description** (`problem_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the problem you are experiencing in as much detail as possible. This will help the service technician understand the issue and provide more accurate assistance.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, required/optional): If you have a preferred date for the service technician to visit, enter it in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year).
