# Wheelchair Repair Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about wheelchair repairs, including the type of wheelchair, customer details, and repair status. It helps technicians to record repair information accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of wheelchair that needs repair.
2. Enter the customer's name.
3. Enter the customer's date of birth (if available).
4. Choose the type of repair required (Minor, Major, or Maintenance).
5. Select the status of the repair (In Progress, Complete, Pending, or Cancel).
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the repair.
7. Review and submit the form to record the repair information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **wheelchair_type** (select_one): Select the type of wheelchair that needs repair. This will help identify the specific repair requirements and ensure the correct parts or materials are used.
* **customer_name** (text): Enter the customer's name as it is spelled on their identification documents.
* **date_of_birth** (date): Enter the customer's date of birth if available. This helps in identifying any age-related requirements or restrictions.
* **repair_type** (select_one): Choose the type of repair required. Options include:
	+ **Minor**: For minor repairs such as adjustments or tuning.
	+ **Major**: For more extensive repairs such as replacement of parts.
	+ **Maintenance**: For routine maintenance or upkeep.
* **repair_status** (select_multiple): Select all applicable statuses to track the repair process. Options include:
	+ **In Progress**: The repair is currently being performed.
	+ **Complete**: The repair is finished and ready for collection.
	+ **Pending**: The repair is scheduled but not yet started.
	+ **Cancel**: The repair has been cancelled.
* **notes** (note): Add any additional comments or notes about the repair. This can include details about the repair process, any issues encountered, or follow-up actions required.
