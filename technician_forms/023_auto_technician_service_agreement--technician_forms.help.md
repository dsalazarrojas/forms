# Auto Technician Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Auto Technician Service Agreement form is designed to gather necessary information from clients before performing services on their vehicles. This form covers various aspects of the service, including client details, vehicle information, service requirements, and agreement terms. By completing this form, both the client and the technician have a clear understanding of the service being performed and can avoid potential misunderstandings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read through each page carefully**: Each page is designed to collect specific information from clients. Please review each page thoroughly before proceeding to the next.
2. **Provide accurate information**: Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Sign and date the form**: Sign and date the form where indicated to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information presented.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Client Information
* **First Name** (First Name, text, **Required**): Your legal first name.
*   **Last Name** (Last Name, text, **Required**): Your legal last name.
*   **Email Address** (Email Address, text, **Required**): Your primary email address.
*   **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, **Required**): Your best contact number.
*   **Street Address** (Street Address, text, **Required**): Your street address.
*   **City** (City, text, **Required**): The city of your residence.
*   **State** (State, select_one, **Required**): The state of your residence.
*   **ZIP Code** (ZIP Code, text, **Required**): Your 5-digit ZIP code.

### Page 2: Vehicle Information
*   **Vehicle Year** (Vehicle Year, number, **Required**): The year of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Make** (Vehicle Make, text, **Required**): The manufacturer of your vehicle.
*   **Vehicle Model** (Vehicle Model, text, **Required**): The model name of your vehicle.
*   **VIN (Vehicle Identification Number)** (VIN, text, **Required**): Your vehicle's identification number.
*   **Current Mileage** (Current Mileage, number, **Required**): The current odometer reading of your vehicle.

### Page 3: Service Information
*   **Service Category** (Service Category, select_one, **Required**): The type of service being performed.
*   **Description of Services** (Description of Services, text, **Required**): A detailed description of the services being performed.
*   **Estimated Duration** (Estimated Duration, select_one, **Required**): The estimated time required to complete the service.
*   **Appointment Date** (Appointment Date, date, **Required**): The scheduled date for the service.
*   **Appointment Time** (Appointment Time, time, **Required**): The scheduled time for the service.

### Page 4: Service Details
*   **Service Details** (Service Details, note, **Optional**): Any additional information about the service.
*   **Vehicle Information** (Vehicle Information, note, **Optional**): Any additional details about your vehicle.
*   **Parts Information** (Parts Information, note, **Optional**): Any information about parts included or not included in the service.
*   **Parts Warranty** (Parts Warranty, select_one, **Optional**): The warranty coverage for the parts used in the service.
*   **Consent and Terms** (Consent and Terms, note, **Optional**): Please read and understand the terms of the service agreement.
*   **Work Authorization** (Work Authorization, select_one, **Required**): Do you authorize this work to be performed?

### Page 5: Additional Information
*   **Additional Work Authorization** (Additional Work Authorization, select_one, **Required**): If more work is needed, do you authorize up to 10% or 25% more work.
*   **Liability Waiver** (Liability Waiver, select_one, **Required**): Do you understand and accept the risks involved with the service.
*   **Vehicle Condition Acknowledgment** (Vehicle Condition Acknowledgment, select_one, **Required**): Have you noted any pre-existing conditions with your vehicle?
*   **Personal Items** (Personal Items, select_one, **Required**): Have you removed all valuable items from your vehicle?
*   **Test Drive Authorization** (Test Drive Authorization, select_one, **Required**): Do you authorize the technician to test drive your vehicle?
*   **Cancellation Policy** (Cancellation Policy, select_one, **Required**): Do you understand the cancellation policy?
*   **Warranty Terms** (Warranty Terms, select_one, **Required**): Do you understand the warranty terms?

### Page 6: Payment Information
*   **Deposit Required** (Deposit Required, select_one, **Required**): Is a deposit required for the service?
*   **Deposit Amount** (Deposit Amount, number, **Optional**): If a deposit is required, please enter the amount.
*   **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, **Required**): How will you pay for the service?
*   **Estimated Cost** (Estimated Cost, number, **Required**): The estimated cost for the service.
*   **Parts Included** (Parts Included, select_one, **Required**): Are parts included in the service?
*   **Parts Type** (Parts Type, select_one, **Optional**): What type of parts are included in the service?
*   **Client Signature** (Client Signature, text, **Required**): Please sign to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information provided.
*   **Date of Signature** (Date of Signature, date, **Required**): The date of your signature.
*   **Technician Name** (Technician Name, text, **Optional**): The name of the assigned technician.
*   **Technician Signature** (Technician Signature, text, **Optional**): The digital signature of the technician.
