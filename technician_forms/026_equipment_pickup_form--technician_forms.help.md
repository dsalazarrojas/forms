# Equipment Pickup Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This Equipment Pickup Form is used to collect information from customers about their equipment pickup requests. The form helps technicians gather necessary details to process the pickup efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your customer's name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter your customer's phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
3. Select a date for the pickup in the **Pickup Date** field.
4. Choose a time for the pickup in the **Pickup Time** field.
5. Enter the Equipment ID of the item being picked up in the **Equipment ID** field.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about the pickup in the **Pickup Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's name.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Pickup Date** (`pickup_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date for the pickup.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time for the pickup.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the Equipment ID of the item being picked up.
* **Pickup Notes** (`pickup_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the pickup.
