# Shift Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shift Inspection Form is used to inspect shifts worked by employees, checking if they are working their assigned shifts or not.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct shift inspection status for the shift being inspected.
2. Select the employee(s) who worked the shift.
3. Choose the date and time of the shift.
4. Take any additional notes about the shift.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Shift Inspection (shift_inspection)**: Select "Yes" if the employee was present during their assigned shift, or "No" if they were not.
  * This field helps us determine if the employee is adhering to their schedule.
  * A "Yes" means the employee was present during their shift.
  * A "No" means the employee was not present during their shift.
- **Employee (employee)**: Select all employees who worked the shift.
  * This field allows you to specify which employees worked the shift.
  * If an employee was not present, select "No" for them.
- **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the shift.
  * This field is used to record the date of the shift.
  * Enter the date in the standard format (e.g., 2022-07-25).
- **Time (time)**: Enter the time of the shift.
  * This field is used to record the time of the shift.
  * Enter the time in the standard format (e.g., 14:30).
- **Notes (notes)**: Take any additional notes about the shift.
  * This field is for any comments or issues that need to be addressed.
  * Keep your notes concise and clear for easy reference.
