# Locomotive Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Locomotive Inspection Checklist is used to record inspection results and findings for locomotives. This form is a critical tool for ensuring that locomotives are in good working condition, and helps identify any issues that need to be addressed. It is intended to be used by technicians to record their findings after inspecting a locomotive.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Locomotive Inspection" page from the form.
2. Enter the locomotive number (a unique identifier for each locomotive).
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the official inspection has been completed.
4. Enter the date of the inspection using the date picker.
5. Enter the time of the inspection using the time picker.
6. Select all applicable issues that were found during the inspection from the "Inspection Result" field.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
8. Select the technician who was assigned to perform the inspection.
9. Select whether the inspection was performed at the locomotive's inspection location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Locomotive Number** (2): Enter the unique identifier for the locomotive being inspected.
* **Inspection Official** (3): This field indicates whether an official inspection has been completed. Please answer "Yes" if the inspection was officially sanctioned and conducted.
* **Inspection Date** (4): Enter the date on which the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Time** (5): Enter the time at which the inspection was performed.
* **Inspection Result** (6): Select all applicable issues that were found during the inspection. This field is intended to capture any problems or concerns that were identified.
* **Additional Comments** (7): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection, such as any actions that need to be taken or observations that were noted.
* **Assigned Technician** (8): Select the technician who was assigned to perform the inspection.
* **Inspection Location** (9): Indicate whether the inspection was performed at the locomotive's designated inspection location. Please answer "Yes" if the inspection was completed at the intended location.
