# Lash Technician Team Meeting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for lash technicians to schedule and coordinate team meetings. Fill out this form to create a new meeting appointment and provide essential information to ensure a smooth and productive meeting experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk) with the necessary information.
2. Enter your details in the provided fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<name>**, text, required: false):** Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (<name>**, text, required: false):** Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (<name>**, email, required: false):** Enter your official email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (<name>**, text, required: false):** Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Meeting Subject (<name>**, text, required: false):** Briefly describe the meeting subject to help the team understand the purpose of the meeting.
* **Meeting Description (<name>**, text, required: false):** Provide a more detailed description of the meeting's purpose and expected topics.
* **Meeting Location (<name>**, text, required: false):** Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time (<name>**, time, required: false):** Choose the time of the meeting in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Meeting Date (<name>**, date, required: false):** Select the date of the meeting in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Meeting Duration (<name>**, number, required: false):** Enter the duration of the meeting in minutes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, contact your team lead or administrator for assistance.
* Double-check your meeting details before submitting to ensure accuracy and avoid any last-minute changes.
