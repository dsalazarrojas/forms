# CrowdStrike Global Threat Report Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to report and track threats that have been discovered within your network or systems.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields to ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
2. Select all applicable categories that best describe the threat.
3. Provide a detailed description of the threat.
4. Choose the impact and frequency of the threat.
5. Select the severity and status of the threat.
6. Identify the source and vector of the threat.
7. Choose the threat level.
8. Enter the location and timestamps of the threat.
9. Enter any additional comments or comments about awareness of the threat.
10. Enter status updates and reasons for any updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Threat Summary**: A brief overview of the threat.
* **Threat Categories**: Select all that apply. (Malware, Phishing, Ransomware, SQL Injection, Cross-site scripting, Other)
* **Threat Description**: A detailed description of the threat.
* **Threat Impact**: The impact of the threat. (High, Medium, Low)
* **Threat Frequency**: The frequency of the threat. (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Rarely)
* **Threat Severity**: The severity of the threat. (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
* **Threat Status**: The status of the threat. (Active, Dormant, Inactive)
* **Threat Source**: The source of the threat. (Network, Email, Web, USB, Unknown)
* **Threat Vector**: The vector of the threat. (Malicious email attachments, Phishing emails, Drive-by download, Infected website, Unknown)
* **Threat Level**: The level of the threat. (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, Level 4, Level 5)
* **Threat Location**: The location of the threat.
* **Threat Timestamp**: The timestamp of when the threat was first discovered.
* **Threat Timestamp End**: The timestamp of when the threat ended.
* **Threat Timeframe**: The timeframe of the threat.
* **Threat Timeframe End**: The end timeframe of the threat.
* **Threat Comment**: Any additional comments about the threat.
* **Aware of this threat?**: Are you aware of this threat? (True, False)
* **Reason for awareness**: If yes, provide a reason for awareness.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments about the threat.
* **Comments about awareness**: Any comments about awareness of the threat.
* **Status Update**: Enter status updates here.
* **Status Update Reason**: Enter a reason for status updates.
