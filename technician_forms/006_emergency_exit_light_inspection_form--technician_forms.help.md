# Emergency Exit Light Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Exit Light Inspection Form is a tool used to collect and track information related to emergency exit light inspections. This form is designed to help technicians gather data on the status and maintenance of emergency exit lights, ensuring they are functioning correctly and are not a safety hazard.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before starting the inspection.
2. Ensure you have all necessary information and equipment before beginning.
3. Complete each field as instructed, providing accurate and detailed information.
4. Use the select options for scheduling and status fields to choose the relevant options.
5. If additional notes are required, use the "Notes" field.
6. Ensure all required fields are completed before saving the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (General Info, Text, Required: False):** Enter any relevant comments or descriptions about the emergency exit light in question.
* **Lighting Schedule (Lighting Schedule, Select Multiple, Required: False):** Select the lighting schedule for the emergency exit light, such as 8am-5pm or 11am-8pm.
* **Inspection Status (Inspection Status, Select One, Required: False):** Choose the status of the inspection, such as Not Completed, Completed, or In Progress.
* **Maintenance Record (Maintenance Record, Text, Required: False):** Enter any relevant maintenance or repair history of the emergency exit light.
* **Next Scheduled Inspection (Next Scheduled Inspection, Date, Required: False):** Enter the date of the next scheduled inspection.
* **Notes (Notes, Note, Required: False):** Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection or light.
* **Assigned Technician (Assigned Technician, Text, Required: False):** Enter the name of the technician assigned to the inspection.
* **Assigned Date (Assigned Date, Date, Required: False):** Enter the date the inspection was assigned.
* **Completed Date (Completed Date, Date, Required: False):** Enter the date the inspection was completed.
* **Completed Time (Completed Time, Time, Required: False):** Enter the time the inspection was completed.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete all required fields before saving the form.
* Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.
* Use the "Notes" field for any additional information that may not fit in the other fields.
* If unsure about any field, contact your supervisor or refer to relevant guidelines and procedures.
