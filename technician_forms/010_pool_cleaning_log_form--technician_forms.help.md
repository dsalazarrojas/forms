# Pool Cleaning Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pool Cleaning Log Form is a tool for logging and tracking pool maintenance activities. It helps pool operators or maintenance personnel to keep a record of pool cleanings, including the type of pool, size, cleaning date, time, and maintenance tasks performed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of pool (Pool, Spa, Hot tub, etc.) for the maintenance activity being logged.
2.  Choose the size of the pool (Small, Medium, Large, Extra Large) for accurate record-keeping.
3.  Enter the date the pool cleaning took place.
4.  Enter the time the pool cleaning took place.
5.  Select the maintenance tasks performed during the cleaning process (Skimmer cleaning, Filter cleaning, Vacuuming).
6.  Leave any additional notes or comments regarding the cleaning activity.
7.  If required, enter the name of the technician or person who performed the maintenance task.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pool Type
*   **Pool Type**: Select the type of pool being cleaned, such as Pool, Spa, or Hot tub.

### Pool Size
*   **Pool Size**: Choose the size of the pool, which helps in accurate record-keeping and planning for future maintenance.

### Cleaning Date
*   **Cleaning Date**: Enter the date when the pool cleaning took place.

### Cleaning Time
*   **Cleaning Time**: Enter the time when the pool cleaning took place.

### Maintenance Task
*   **Maintenance Task**: Select the maintenance tasks performed during the cleaning process, such as Skimmer cleaning, Filter cleaning, or Vacuuming.

### Notes
*   **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes regarding the cleaning activity.

### Technician Name
*   **Technician Name**: Enter the name of the technician or person who performed the maintenance task (if required).

Note: Some fields are optional, meaning you do not need to fill them out unless they are required for your specific use case.
