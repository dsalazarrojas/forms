# Job Work Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Job Work Order Form is designed to collect relevant information about a job, including the job title, description, and details about the payment method. This form helps to ensure that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling in the job title**, which should be a brief and descriptive title of the job.
2. **Provide a brief description** of the job, including any relevant details or requirements.
3. **Upload any relevant files**, such as images or documents, to support your job description.
4. **Specify the payment method**, by selecting "Yes" or "No" to indicate if payment is involved.
5. **Enter the payment issuer**, if applicable.
6. **Enter the customer's address**, including the city and state.
7. **Enter the customer's zip code**.
8. **Enter the customer's phone number**.
9. **Enter the customer's email**, ensuring it is a valid email address.
10. **Enter the date**, if relevant to the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief and descriptive title of the job.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the job, including any relevant details or requirements.
* **Upload Files** (`form_files`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files, such as images or documents, to support your job description.
* **Payment Method** (`form_payment`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if payment is involved, "No" otherwise.
* **Payment Issuer** (`form_payment_issuer`, text, optional): Enter the payment issuer, if applicable.
* **Customer Address** (`form_address`, text, optional): Enter the customer's address, including the city and state.
* **City** (`form_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the customer's address.
* **State** (`form_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the customer's address.
* **Zip** (`form_zip`, text, optional): Enter the customer's zip code.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter the customer's email, ensuring it is a valid email address.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date, if relevant to the job.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Enter accurate and descriptive information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant department for assistance.
