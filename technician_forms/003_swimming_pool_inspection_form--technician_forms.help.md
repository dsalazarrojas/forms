# swimming_pool_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The swimming_pool_inspection_form is designed to gather information for pool inspections, including pool location, type, and condition, for maintenance and safety purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the Pool Location: This is the address or location where the pool is situated.
2. Select the Pool Type: Choose the type of pool (Above ground, Inground, Solar, Filled, or Other).
3. Enter or Select Pool Size: Provide the pool size in a numerical format.
4. Assess the Pool Condition: Evaluate the pool's overall condition using the options Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement.
5. Evaluate the Equipment Condition: Select the condition of the pool's equipment (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
6. Identify Safety Hazards: Select any safety hazards present at the pool site (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
7. Check Electrical Outlets and Hardware: Evaluate the condition of electrical outlets and hardware (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
8. Review Fencing: Assess the condition of fencing around the pool (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
9. Inspect Deck and Walking Paths: Evaluate the condition of deck and walking paths (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
10. Examine Pool Equipment: Assess the condition of pool equipment (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
11. Check the Pool Drain System: Evaluate the condition of the pool drain system (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
12. Determine Water Level: Evaluate the water level in the pool (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
13. Examine the Pool Surrounding Area: Assess the condition of the surrounding area (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
14. Verify Water Testing: Select the current status of water testing (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement).
15. Schedule Pool Cleaning: Provide the next scheduled pool cleaning date.
16. Record Next Inspection Date: Provide the next scheduled inspection date.
17. Record Last Inspection Date: Provide the last inspection date.
18. Add Notes: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
19. Assign Technician: Provide the name or ID of the assigned technician.
20. Add Supervisor Comments: Enter any comments or feedback from the supervisor.
21. Add Inspector Comments: Enter any comments or feedback from the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pool Location** (1, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the location where the pool is situated. It helps the pool management track the pool's location for future reference and accessibility.
* **Pool Type** (2, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of pool from the options Above ground, Inground, Solar, Filled, or Other to help categorize the pool for inspection purposes.
* **Pool Size** (3, number, required/optional): Enter the pool size in a numerical format to aid in assessing pool capacity and maintenance needs.
* **Pool Condition** (4, select_multiple, required/optional): Evaluate the pool's overall condition as Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement to identify potential issues and prioritize maintenance.
* **Equipment Condition** (5, select_multiple, required/optional): Assess the condition of pool equipment using the options Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement to determine if the equipment is functioning properly.
* **Safety Hazards** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Identify any safety hazards present around the pool using the options Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement to prioritize corrective actions.
* **Electrical Outlets** (7, select_multiple, required/optional): Evaluate the condition of electrical outlets around the pool (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) for electrical safety.
* **Electrical Hardware** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Assess the condition of pool electrical hardware (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) to prevent electrical hazards.
* **Fencing** (9, select_multiple, required/optional): Check the condition of fencing surrounding the pool (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) for safety purposes.
* **Deck and Walking Paths** (10, select_multiple, required/optional): Inspect the condition of deck and walking paths around the pool (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) for safety and accessibility.
* **Pool Equipment** (11, select_multiple, required/optional): Evaluate the condition of pool equipment (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) for proper functioning and maintenance.
* **Pool Drain System** (12, select_multiple, required/optional): Assess the condition of the pool drain system (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) to prevent water hazards.
* **Water Level** (13, select_multiple, required/optional): Determine the current water level in the pool (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) to prevent drowning hazards.
* **Pool Surrounding Area** (14, select_multiple, required/optional): Inspect the condition of the pool's surrounding area (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) to ensure a safe environment.
* **Water Testing** (15, select_multiple, required/optional): Verify the current status of water testing (Good, Needs Repair, or Needs Replacement) to ensure clean and safe pool water.
* **Pool Cleaning Schedule** (16, date, required/optional): Provide the next scheduled pool cleaning date to maintain pool hygiene and safety.
* **Next Inspection Date** (17, date, required/optional): Record the next scheduled inspection date for future reference and planning.
* **Last Inspection Date** (18, date, required/optional): Record the last inspection date to track maintenance history.
* **Notes** (19, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection for future reference.
* **Assigned Technician** (20, text, required/optional): Provide the name or ID of the assigned technician for record-keeping and communication.
* **Supervisor Comments** (21, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the supervisor for future reference.
* **Inspector Comments** (22, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or feedback from the inspector for future reference.
