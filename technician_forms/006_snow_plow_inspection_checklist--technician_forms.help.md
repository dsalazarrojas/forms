# Snow Plow Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Plow Inspection Checklist is designed to help technicians evaluate the condition and functionality of snowplows, ensuring they are in good working order and identifying any potential issues that need to be addressed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the inspection.
2. For each field, select the condition of the equipment or feature being evaluated (e.g., Good, Fair, or Poor).
3. Provide any additional comments or photos in the text fields as needed.
4. Complete the form as thoroughly and accurately as possible to ensure that the snowplow is properly evaluated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Used (`snow_plow`)**
	+ This field allows you to select the type of equipment used (Snow Plow, Blower, or Both).
* **Snow Type (`snow_type`)**
	+ Select the type of snow the plow is intended to handle (Powder, Packed, or Ice).
* **Plow Condition (`plow_condition`)**
	+ Evaluate the overall condition of the plow, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Engine Condition (`engine_condition`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the engine, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic Circuit Pressure (`hydraulic_circuit_pressure`)**
	+ Select the pressure of the hydraulic circuit, evaluating it as Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic System (`hydraulic_system`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the hydraulic system, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Electrical System (`electrical_system`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the electrical system, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic Oil Level (`hydraulic_oil_level`)**
	+ Check the hydraulic oil level, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic Filtration (`hydraulic_filtration`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the hydraulic filtration system, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic Pump Wear (`hydraulic_pump_wear`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the hydraulic pump, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Hydraulic Leakage (`hydraulic_leakage`)**
	+ Check for leakage in the hydraulic system, selecting None, Minor, or Major.
* **Hydraulic Hoses (`hydraulic_hoses`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the hydraulic hoses, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Electrical Connection (`electrical_connection`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the electrical connection, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Electrical Wiring (`electrical_wiring`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the electrical wiring, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Snow Blade Condition (`snow_blade_condition`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the snow blade, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Snow Blade Alignment (`snow_blade_alignment`)**
	+ Check the alignment of the snow blade, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Snow Blade Wear (`snow_blade_wear`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the snow blade, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Snow Blade Bending (`snow_blade_bending`)**
	+ Check for bending or warping of the snow blade, selecting None or Major.
* **Plow Tires (`plow_tires`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the plow tires, selecting Good, Fair, Poor, or None.
* **Plow Frame (`plow_frame`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the plow frame, selecting Good, Fair, Poor, or None.

* **Snow Blade Holder (`snow_blade_holder`)**
	+ Evaluate the condition of the snow blade holder, selecting Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **General Conditions (`general_conditions`)**
	+ Provide any additional comments about the overall condition of the snowplow.
* **Comments (`comments`)**

* **Photo (`photo`)**
	+ Upload any photos of the snowplow that support your evaluation.
