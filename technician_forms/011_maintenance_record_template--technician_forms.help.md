# Maintenance Record Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Maintenance Record Template form is designed to document and track maintenance activities for assets. It ensures consistency and accuracy in recording maintenance information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps outlined in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Maintenance Record Date** (`maintenance_record_date`, date, required/optional): Record the date when maintenance was performed on the asset.
- **Maintenance Record Start Time** (`maintenance_record_start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time maintenance started.
- **Maintenance Record Duration** (`maintenance_record_duration`, number, required/optional): Track the duration of the maintenance in hours or minutes.
- **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the technician who performed the maintenance.
- **Maintenance Type** (`maintenance_type`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate if the maintenance was a regular or irregular activity (Yes/No).
- **Maintenance Description** (`maintenance_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the maintenance activities performed.
- **Maintenance Record Status** (`maintenance_record_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Track the status of the maintenance record (Active/Inactive).
- **Asset ID** (`asset_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the unique identifier of the asset.
- **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, select_one, required/optional): Check if a technician was assigned to the maintenance (Yes/No).
- **Asset Status** (`asset_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Track the status of the asset (Active/Inactive).
- **Maintenance Record Notes** (`maintenance_record_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the maintenance activity.
- **Maintenance Record Comments** (`maintenance_record_comments`, text, required/optional): Provide comments or explanations for the maintenance record.
