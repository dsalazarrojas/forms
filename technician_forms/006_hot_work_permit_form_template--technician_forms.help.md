# Hot Work Permit Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hot Work Permit Form Template is used to request a hot work permit for a job. Hot work involves electrical or other types of work that can ignite flammable materials or create a fire hazard. This form is used to ensure that necessary safety precautions are taken before commencing work that involves hot work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Hot Work Permit Form Template" link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form by entering the required information on each page.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Job Number**
* **Label**: job_number
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Number
* **Explanation**: Enter the job number assigned to the work being performed.

### 2. **Work Area**
* **Label**: work_area
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Text
* **Explanation**: Enter the area where the hot work will be performed.

### 3. **Job Description**
* **Label**: job_description
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Note
* **Explanation**: Enter a brief description of the job being performed.

### 4. **Permit Date**
* **Label**: permit_date
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Date
* **Explanation**: Enter the date when the hot work permit was issued.

### 5. **Permit Time**
* **Label**: permit_time
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Time
* **Explanation**: Enter the time when the hot work permit was issued.

### 6. **Work Hours**
* **Label**: work_hours
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Text
* **Explanation**: Enter the number of hours the hot work will be performed.

### 7. **Job Type**
* **Label**: job_type
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Text
* **Explanation**: Enter the type of job being performed, such as electrical or welding.

### 8. **Supervisor Name**
* **Label**: supervisor_name
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Text
* **Explanation**: Enter the name of the supervisor who issued the hot work permit.

### 9. **Supervisor Phone**
* **Label**: supervisor_phone
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Text
* **Explanation**: Enter the phone number of the supervisor who issued the hot work permit.

### 10. **Supervisor Email**
* **Label**: supervisor_email
* **Required**: False
* **Type**: Email
* **Explanation**: Enter the email address of the supervisor who issued the hot work permit.

### 11. **Tips**

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a hot work permit can be issued.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required information is included.
