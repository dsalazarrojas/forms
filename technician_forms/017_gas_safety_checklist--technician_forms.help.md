# Gas Safety Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gas Safety Checklist is designed to ensure that gas appliances are properly inspected and verified for safety. It helps to identify potential issues that need to be addressed to prevent gas leaks and other safety hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply answer the questions and provide any additional comments or notes as needed. Make sure to answer each question truthfully and accurately, and provide any relevant information that may be required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Install Gas Appliance** (`install_gas_appliance`, text, not required): This field is used to verify that the gas appliance is properly installed and connected according to the manufacturer's instructions.
* **Check For Leaks** (`check_leaks`, select_multiple, not required): In this field, select "Yes" if you find any leaks or potential leaks on the gas appliance. Select "No" if you do not find any leaks.
* **Verify Valves** (`verify_valves`, select_one, not required): Select the correct valve type from the options provided. This field is used to ensure that the correct type of valve is installed and verified.
* **Ensure Proper Installation** (`ensure_proper_installation`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the proper installation of the gas appliance.
* **Confirm Gas Leak Test** (`confirm_gas_leak_test`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the gas leak test performed.
* **Check Fitting And Connections** (`check_fitting_and_connections`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the fitting and connections of the gas appliance.
* **Verify Gas Valve Location** (`verify_gas_valve_location`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the location of the gas valve.
* **Inspect Valve Body** (`inspect_valve_body`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the valve body.
* **Ensure Proper Gasket Seal** (`ensure_proper_gasket_seal`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the gasket seal of the gas appliance.
* **Check Gas Leak Test Equipment** (`check_gas_leak_test_equipment`, select_one, not required): Select the correct equipment type from the options provided. This field is used to ensure that the correct equipment is used for gas leak testing.
* **Inspect Gasket And Valve** (`inspect_gasket_and_valve`, text, not required): In this field, provide any comments or notes about the gasket and valve inspection.
* **Ensure Correct Gas Valve Type** (`ensure_correct_gas_valve_type`, select_one, not required): Select the correct gas valve type from the options provided. This field is used to ensure that the correct type of valve is installed and verified.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* Provide any additional comments or notes that may be relevant to the inspection.
* Ensure that the form is completed in a way that is consistent with other safety protocols and procedures used in the organization.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, seek guidance from a qualified expert or supervisor.
