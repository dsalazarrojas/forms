# Daily Technician Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Technician Report is a form designed to document the activities and events that occurred during a technician's shift. This form is used to track progress, identify areas for improvement, and provide insights for administrative and operational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the relevant options for each field based on your activities during the shift.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Daily Activities** (`daily_technician_report_1`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe the key events, tasks, and activities that occurred during your shift. Be as specific as possible.
*   **Maintenance Tasks** (`daily_technician_report_2`, `select_one`, required: false): Check the box that best describes your maintenance activities during the shift. 
      *   Yes: You completed one or more maintenance tasks during the shift.
      *   No: You did not complete any maintenance tasks during the shift.
*   **Completed Work Orders** (`daily_technician_report_3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Check the boxes for all work orders that were completed during the shift. 
      *   Yes: Select all work orders that you completed.
*   **Start Date** (`daily_technician_report_4`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of your shift (mm/dd/yyyy format).
*   **End Date** (`daily_technician_report_5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of your shift (mm/dd/yyyy format).
*   **Technician Name** (`daily_technician_report_6`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID badge or personnel file.
*   **Technician Phone** (`daily_technician_report_7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
*   **Technician Email** (`daily_technician_report_8`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email address.
*   **Technician Notes** (`daily_technician_report_9`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments that are relevant to your activities during the shift.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill in all required fields accurately.
- Use specific and detailed descriptions for the Daily Activities field.
- Ensure that you have the approval of your supervisor before submitting this form.
