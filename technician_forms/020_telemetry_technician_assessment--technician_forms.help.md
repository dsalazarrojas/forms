# Telemetry Technician Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telemetry Technician Assessment form is a self-assessment tool designed to collect information from candidates regarding their experience, skills, and availability. This form will help candidates to assess themselves on their fit for various roles and positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by reviewing the form's pages, which are divided into sections. Each page will have a unique ID and name.
2. Read each field's label carefully and understand its meaning and purpose.
3. Complete each field with the most accurate and relevant information possible.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
5. Review and submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Details** (Page 1, id: 1): This field is for your personal details, such as your name, address, and contact information. Please enter your real name, address, and contact information accurately.
* **Shift Readiness** (Page 1, id: 2): This field is for you to express your availability and readiness to work shifts. Enter a number that represents your level of readiness, with 1 being the highest and 5 being the lowest.
* **Telemetry Skills** (Page 2, id: 3): This field is for you to select your relevant skills and experience in telemetry. Choose the options that best represent your skills and experience.
* **Experience** (Page 2, id: 4): This field is for you to enter your years of experience in telemetry. Please enter a number that represents the number of years you have worked in the field.
* **Work Hours** (Page 2, id: 5): This field is for you to enter your availability to work hours per week. Please enter a number that represents the number of hours you are willing to work per week.
* **Shift Preference** (Page 3, id: 6): This field is for you to select your preferred shift options. Choose the options that best fit your availability and preferences.
* **Available Shifts** (Page 3, id: 7): This field is for you to select the shifts you are available to work. Choose the options that best represent your availability.
* **Available Days** (Page 3, id: 8): This field is for you to select the days you are available to work. Choose the options that best represent your availability.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 4, id: 9): This field is for you to enter the contact information of an emergency contact. Please enter their name, phone number, and email address accurately.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Page 4, id: 10): This field is for you to describe your relationship with the emergency contact. Please enter a brief description of your relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (Page 4, id: 11): This field is for you to enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Page 4, id: 12): This field is for you to enter the emergency contact's email address.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when completing the form.
* Review each field carefully and ensure you have entered the correct information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
