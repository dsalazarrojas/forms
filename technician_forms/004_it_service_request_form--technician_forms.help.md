# IT Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Service Request Form is used to submit requests for help with IT-related issues. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form to ensure your request is accurately documented and efficiently processed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the red asterisk (\*) next to the field label.
2. Provide a brief description of the issue in the "Description" field.
3. Select one or more categories for your request from the list provided.
4. Choose the priority level of your request (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Select one or more request types (Hardware, Software, or Other).
6. If applicable, select a tool that has been assigned to you for the issue.
7. Provide any additional information that may be relevant to the issue in the "Additional Information" field.
8. Finally, click the submit button to send your request to the IT team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **IT Service Request (\*)**: Enter a brief title for your service request.
	+ This field is required to help identify your request.
* **Description (\*)**: Briefly describe your issue here.
	+ This field is required to provide context for the IT team.
* **Category (\*)**: Select one or more categories for your request.
	+ This field helps the IT team to prioritize and categorize your request.
* **Priority (\*)**: Choose the priority level of your request (High, Medium, Low).
	+ This field determines the urgency of your request.
* **Request Type (\*)**: Select one or more request types (Hardware, Software, or Other).
	+ This field helps the IT team to understand the nature of your request.
* **Request Date (optional)**: Enter the date you first noticed the issue (YYYY-MM-DD).
	+ This field provides context for the IT team.
* **Request Time (optional)**: Enter the time you first noticed the issue (HH:MM).
	+ This field provides additional context for the IT team.
* **Assigned Tool (**)**: Select a tool that has been assigned to you for the issue (chatjimmy or Other).
	+ This field is required to ensure the correct tool is used.
* **Additional Information (optional)**: Provide any additional relevant information about your issue.
	+ This field can be used to provide more context.
* **Comments (optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your request.
	+ This field can be used to provide more information or clarify your request.

## Tips
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you have already tried basic troubleshooting steps before submitting your request.
* The IT team will review and process your request promptly, and you will receive a response with next steps and estimated resolution time.
* If you need to make changes to your request, please contact the IT team directly.
