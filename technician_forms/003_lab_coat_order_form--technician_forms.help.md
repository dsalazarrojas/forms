# Lab Coat Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Coat Order Form is used to capture order information and specifications for lab coats for technicians.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of order you are placing (lab coat or other).
2. Enter the quantity of lab coats you are requesting.
3. Choose the size of the lab coat (small, large, or medium).
4. Enter the desired color for the lab coat.
5. Enter the fabric type for the lab coat.
6. Enter the thread count for the lab coat.
7. Choose the sleeve style (button, snap, or velcro).
8. Enter the date and time you would like the order to be completed.
9. Enter your name and title as the technician placing the order.
