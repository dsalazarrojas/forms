# vehicle_maintenance_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit a new vehicle maintenance request. Please use this form to provide details about your vehicle's maintenance needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle you have (Vehicle, Tractor, Motorcycle, or Other).
2. Choose the services required for your vehicle (Oil Change, Tire Change, Brake Change, or Other).
3. Enter the date of service for your vehicle.
4. Enter the time of service for your vehicle.
5. Add any additional technician's notes.
6. Enter your email address for communication.
7. Enter your phone number for contact.
8. Select the assigned tool from the list.
9. Select the assigned technician from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Details** (`vehicle_details`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of vehicle you have (Vehicle, Tractor, Motorcycle, or Other).
* **Service Required** (`service_required`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the services required for your vehicle (Oil Change, Tire Change, Brake Change, or Other).
* **Date of Service** (`date_of_service`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of service for your vehicle.
* **Time of Service** (`time_of_service`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of service for your vehicle.
* **Technician's Notes** (`technician_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes for the technician.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the assigned tool from the list.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the assigned technician from the list.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure of any information, please contact your supervisor for assistance.
* Please review the form carefully to ensure that the information provided is accurate.
