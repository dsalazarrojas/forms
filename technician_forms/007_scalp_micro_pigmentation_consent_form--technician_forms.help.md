# Scalp Micro Pigmentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to obtain informed consent and medical history from patients before undergoing scalp micro pigmentation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully read through each field to ensure you understand its purpose.
2. Fill out the form completely, including providing medical history and treatment goals.
3. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the contents.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This field is not editable and is only for identification purposes.
* **Patient Informed Consent**: Please mark "Yes" if you have already provided informed consent and medical history. If not, please provide your informed consent and medical history in the text box.
* **Medical History**: Select all the medical conditions that apply to you. This will help the practitioner understand any potential risks associated with the procedure.
* **Treatment Goals**: Please describe your treatment goals, including any specific results you hope to achieve.
* **Treatment Details**: Provide any additional information about your treatment, such as any medications you are taking or previous treatments you have undergone.
* **Patient Signature**: Sign this field to confirm that you have read and understood the contents of the form.
* **Practitioner Signature**: This field is for the practitioner to sign and confirm that they have informed you of all the necessary risks and benefits of the procedure.
* **Patient Informed Consent 2**, **Medical History 2**, **Treatment Goals 2**, **Treatment Details 2**, **Patient Signature 2**, and **Practitioner Signature 2**: These fields are for patients who have previously provided informed consent and medical history and are only for additional medical history and treatment goals.
* **Medical History 3, 4, 5, 6, 7**: Please select all the medical conditions that apply to you for the corresponding page.
* **Treatment Goals 3, 4, 5, 6, 7**: Please describe your treatment goals for the corresponding page.
* **Treatment Details 3, 4, 5, 6, 7**: Please provide any additional information about your treatment for the corresponding page.
* **Medical History 8, 9, 10**: Please select all the medical conditions that apply to you for the corresponding page.
* **Practitioner Signature 8, 9, 10**: These fields are for the practitioner to sign and confirm that they have informed you of all the necessary risks and benefits of the procedure.
