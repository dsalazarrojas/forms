# Security Equipment Technician Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Equipment Technician Contact Form is a document used to collect information about security equipment issues that need to be addressed. This form will help the technician to identify the problem, schedule a time for service, and follow up on the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the `contact_details` field.
2. Enter the name of the person who reported the issue in the `contact_person` field.
3. Describe the issue in as much detail as possible in the `issue_description` field.
4. Select the services required for the issue in the `service_requests` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `email_address` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.
7. Schedule a time for the technician to visit in the `technician_schedule` field.
8. Choose how you would like to be followed up on in the `follow_up` field.
9. Date and time of the issue occurred in the `issue_date` and `issue_time` fields.
10. Enter the location where the issue occurred in the `issue_location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter your contact information so that the technician can follow up with you.
* **Contact Person** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter the name of the person who reported the issue.
* **Issue Description** (`<name>`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to describe the issue in as much detail as possible.
* **Service Requests** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to select the services required for the issue.
* **Email Address** (`<name>`, `email`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter your email address so that the technician can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter your phone number so that the technician can contact you.
* **Technician Schedule** (`<name>`, `time`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to schedule a time for the technician to visit.
* **Follow Up** (`<name>`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ This field allows you to choose how you would like to be followed up on.
* **Issue Date** (`<name>`, `date`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter the date of the issue.
* **Issue Time** (`<name>`, `time`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter the time of the issue.
* **Issue Location** (`<name>`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for you to enter the location where the issue occurred.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide as much detail as possible in the `issue_description` field.
* Make sure to select all the services required in the `service_requests` field.
* Enter your email address and phone number accurately so that the technician can contact you.
* Schedule a specific time for the technician to visit in the `technician_schedule` field.
* Choose a follow-up method that suits your preference.
