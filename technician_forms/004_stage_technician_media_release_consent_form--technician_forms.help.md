# Stage Technician Media Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Technician Media Release Consent Form is a document that captures essential information about stage technicians and their consent to the use of their likeness, voice, and media content.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

* In the **Media Usage Consent** field, indicate that you have read and understood the purpose of the form and are willing to grant media usage consent.
* In the **Name** field, enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* In the **Contact Email** field, provide a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* In the **Contact Phone** field, provide a valid phone number that can be used to contact you (optional).
* In the **Company** field, enter your company name (if applicable).
* In the **Job Title** field, enter your job title (if applicable).
* In the **Department** field, enter your department (if applicable).
* In the **Contact Address** field, provide your physical address.
* In the **Consent Date** field, select the date of the consent.
* In the **Release Type** field, select the type of media you are granting consent to (e.g., Video, Photo, Audio, Live Stream).
* In the **Release Location** field, select all applicable locations where the media will be used (e.g., Stage, Backstage, Online).
* In the **Release Expiration Date** field, select a date to restrict media usage (optional).
* In the **Release Expiration Time** field, select a time to restrict media usage (optional).
* In the **Notes** field, enter any relevant comments or remarks (optional).
* In the **Release Signature** field, sign the form with your digital signature or a handwritten signature.
* In the **Release Name** field, enter your full name for media credits.
* In the **Release Company** field, enter your company name (if applicable).
* In the **Release Job Title** field, enter your job title (if applicable).
* In the **Release Department** field, enter your department (if applicable).
* In the **Address** field, enter your physical address.
* In the **Release Contact Email** field, provide a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* In the **Release Contact Phone** field, provide a valid phone number that can be used to contact you (optional).
* In the **Release Media URL** field, provide a URL to view the media content (optional).
