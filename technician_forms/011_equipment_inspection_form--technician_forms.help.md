# Equipment Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the condition and maintenance of various types of equipment. It is intended for use by technicians or equipment owners to inspect and track the status of their equipment, ensuring it remains in good working order and meets safety and performance standards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on a computer or mobile device with the required information.
2. For each field, enter the requested details as accurately as possible.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (Equipment Inspection Form 1): Enter a brief description of the equipment being inspected, including its location and any relevant details.

### Equipment Type (Page 2)
* **Equipment Type** (Equipment Inspection Form 2): Select the type of equipment being inspected, such as:

	+ Valve
	+ Conveyor
	+ Pneumatic
	+ Electrical
	+ Hydraulic
	+ Pneumatic Conveyer
	+ Gearbox
	+ Gearbox/Pump
	+ Other (please specify)

### Condition of Equipment (Page 3)
* **Condition of Equipment** (Equipment Inspection Form 3): Assess the current condition of the equipment, using the following options:
	+ Good
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
	+ N/A (if the equipment is not applicable or inoperable)

### Last Inspection Date (Page 4)
* **Last Inspection Date** (Equipment Inspection Form 4): Enter the date of the last inspection.

### Last Inspection Due Date (Page 5)
* **Last Inspection Due Date** (Equipment Inspection Form 5): Enter the date when the next scheduled inspection is due.

### Last Maintenance Date (Page 6)
* **Last Maintenance Date** (Equipment Inspection Form 6): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the equipment.

### Last Maintenance Due Date (Page 7)
* **Last Maintenance Due Date** (Equipment Inspection Form 7): Enter the date when the next scheduled maintenance is due.

### Last Repair Date (Page 8)
* **Last Repair Date** (Equipment Inspection Form 8): Enter the date of the last repair performed on the equipment.

### Next Scheduled Date (Page 9)
* **Next Scheduled Date** (Equipment Inspection Form 9): Enter the date of the next scheduled maintenance or inspection.

### Next Scheduled Frequency (Page 10)
* **Next Scheduled Frequency** (Equipment Inspection Form 10): Choose how often the next scheduled maintenance or inspection is due:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Bi-Annually
	+ Annually
	+ Never
	+ Never Scheduled

### Notes (Page 11)
* **Notes** (Equipment Inspection Form 11): Enter any additional comments or observations about the equipment's condition, maintenance, or repair history.

### Additional Tips
* Ensure accurate and up-to-date information is entered for each field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Use this form to track and document equipment maintenance and inspection history.
