# hvac_maintenance_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hvac_maintenance_checklist_form is designed to help HVAC technicians conduct regular system checks, ensuring that all components are functioning correctly and making any necessary adjustments or repairs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant sections that apply to the specific system being inspected.
2. Answer each question in the relevant section with "Yes" if the task is complete or "No" if it's not.
3. Provide any relevant comments or notes for each section.
4. Repeat this process for each section until you've completed the entire form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Tuning** (`general_tuning`, `text`, optional): Record any general system tuning issues or concerns.
* **Thermostat Check** (`thermostat_check`, `text`, required): Check if the thermostat is functioning correctly, and document any issues.
* **Filter Cleans** (`filter_cleans`, `text`, required): Check if the air filters are clean and free of blockages.
* **Drain Cleans** (`drain_cleans`, `text`, optional): Record any issues with drainage or drain-related concerns.
* **Compressor Check** (`compressor_check`, `text`, required): Check the compressor for any issues, blockages, or malfunctions.
* **Electrical Check** (`electrical_check`, `text`, required): Check if the electrical connections are secure and functioning correctly.
* **Refrigerant Leak Check** (`refrigerant_leak_check`, `text`, required): Check for any refrigerant leaks or concerns.
* **Evaporator Coil Cleaning** (`evaporator_coil_cleaning`, `text`, optional): Check if the evaporator coil is clean and free of blockages.
* **Condenser Coil Cleaning** (`condenser_coil_cleaning`, `text`, optional): Record any issues with condenser coil cleanliness.
* **Drain Line Cleaning** (`drain_line_cleaning`, `text`, optional): Check if the drain lines are clean and free of blockages.
* **Electrical Components Inspection** (`electrical_components_inspection`, `text`, required): Check all electrical components for any issues or malfunctions.
* **Contact Points Inspection** (`contact_points_inspection`, `text`, required): Check the contact points of electrical connections for any signs of wear or issues.
* **Start and Stop Procedure** (`start_and_stop_procedure`, `text`, required): Document the start and stop sequence of the system.
* **Refrigerant Leak Test** (`refrigerant_leak_test`, `text`, required): Perform a leak test on the refrigerant system.
* **Refrigerant Charge Level** (`refrigerant_charge_level`, `text`, required): Record the current refrigerant charge level.
* **Electrical Connections** (`electrical_connections`, `text`, optional): Check if electrical connections are secure and functioning correctly.
* **Refrigerant Recharge** (`refrigerant_recharge`, `text`, required): Recharge the refrigerant system if necessary.
* **Electrical System Check** (`electrical_system_check`, `text`, required): Check the entire electrical system for any malfunctions.
* **Refrigerant Recharge Retest** (`refrigerant_recharge_retest`, `text`, required): Retest the refrigerant charge level after recharge.
* **Refrigerant Charge Pressure Test** (`refrigerant_charge_pressure_test`, `text`, required): Test the pressure level of the refrigerant charge.
* **Electrical System Test** (`electrical_system_test`, `text`, required): Check the entire electrical system for any malfunctions.
* **System Leak Test** (`system_leak_test`, `text`, required): Check for any system leaks.
* **Refrigerant Charge Test** (`refrigerant_charge_test`, `text`, required): Test the refrigerant charge level.
* **Electrical Wiring Test** (`electrical_wiring_test`, `text`, optional): Check the electrical wiring for any issues or malfunctions.
* **Final Inspection** (`final_inspection`, `text`, required): Perform a final system inspection to ensure all checks are complete and accurate.

## Tips
* Always refer to the relevant system manual or manufacturer's guidelines for specific maintenance procedures.
* Consult with a supervisor or experienced technician if unsure about any system component or procedure.
* Document all issues, observations, and actions taken during the maintenance check.
* Use this form as a reference guide for future maintenance checks to ensure consistent and accurate results.
