# damaged_equipment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "damaged_equipment_form" is a tool for reporting damaged equipment. It helps in gathering information about the equipment's condition, the company it belongs to, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully, ensuring you provide accurate information.
2. Start by entering the name of the company that owns the damaged equipment.
3. Provide a detailed description of the damaged equipment.
4. Indicate if the equipment is of a type that can be damaged ('Yes' or 'No').
5. If applicable, select the type of damage that occurred ('Yes' or 'No').
6. Enter the date on which the damage occurred.
7. If possible, indicate the time of the damage.
8. Provide the name of the person who reported the damaged equipment.
9. Upload any relevant images to support the report.
9. Indicate if images are being uploaded ('Yes' or 'No').

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **damaged_equipment_form_1** (text, Required): Enter a brief description of the damaged equipment.
* **company** (text, Required): Enter the name of the company that owns the equipment.
* **description** (text, Required): Describe the damage in detail, including its nature and severity.
* **equipment_type** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if the equipment can be damaged ('Yes' or 'No').
* **equipment_condition** (Select One, Optional): Indicate the overall condition of the equipment after the damage.
* **damage_type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of damage that occurred.
* **damage_date** (Date, Required): Enter the date on which the damage occurred.
* **damage_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the damage, if known.
* **reported_by** (text, Required): Enter your name or the name of the person reporting the damage.
* **image_uploads** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose if you are uploading images to support the report ('Yes' or 'No').
* **images** (Note, Optional): Attach any relevant images to the report.

## Tips
- Ensure the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If unsure about any field, refer to the relevant documentation or ask the relevant personnel.
- Uploading images can help provide visual evidence of the damage.
- If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the relevant department or personnel.
