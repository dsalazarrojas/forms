# Refrigeration Service Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for technicians to record notes and details of refrigeration services provided to customers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the **customer_name** field.
2. Provide the customer's address in the **customer_address** field.
3. Enter the customer's contact number in the **contact_number** field.
4. Select the date of the service visit in the **service_date** field.
5. Enter the time of the service visit in the **service_time** field.
6. Record any notes or details of the service visit in the **service_details** field.
7. Sign your technician's signature in the **technician_signature** field.
8. If applicable, have the customer sign the **customer_signature** field.
9. Record the date you submitted the form in the **date_submitted** field.
10. Enter your email address in the **email** field (optional but recommended for tracking).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **customer_address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **contact_number**: Enter the customer's contact number.
* **service_date**: Select the date of the service visit.
* **service_time**: Select the time of the service visit.
* **service_details**: Record any notes or details of the service visit.
* **technician_signature**: Sign with your technician's signature.
* **customer_signature** (optional): If applicable, have the customer sign the field.
* **date_submitted**: Record the date you submitted the form.
* **email** (optional): Enter your email address for tracking purposes.
