# Laptop Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information for a laptop request from a student in a school setting. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the correct information is captured for the requested laptop.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by entering the student's school name in the "School Name" field.
- Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
- Select the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
- Enter the student's contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
- If applicable, enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name**: Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student requesting the laptop.
* **Date of Birth**: Select the student's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the student's contact number for follow-up purposes.
* **Student ID**: If the student has an ID number, enter it here. This field is not always required.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter the correct school name to ensure that the laptop request is directed to the correct location.
- Double-check the date of birth to ensure that the student is eligible for a laptop request.
- Ensure that you enter the correct contact number so that the technician can contact the student for follow-up purposes.
