# Foreign Object Incident Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report incidents involving foreign objects in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are reporting an incident involving a foreign object.
2. Fill out the form in a clear and concise manner, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to review your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Report** (`incident_report_form_page`): This is the main page for the incident report. Provide a brief summary of the incident, including the nature of the foreign object involved.
* **Incident Details** (`incident_report_form_page_2`): This field is for providing more details about the incident, such as any relevant dates, times, or other circumstances surrounding the incident.
* **Reporting Technician Information** (`incident_report_form_page_3`): This field is for providing information about the technician submitting the report, including their name, role, and contact information.
* **Reporting Information** (`incident_report_form_page_4`): This field is for providing any additional information that may be relevant to the incident report.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_report_form_page_5`): This field is for providing the location where the incident occurred.
* **Reporting Location** (`incident_report_form_page_6`): This field is for providing the location where the report is being submitted from.
* **Reporting Technician** (`incident_report_form_page_7`): This field is for providing the name and contact information of the technician submitting the report.
* **Incident Description** (`incident_report_form_page_9`): This field is for providing a detailed description of the incident.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_report_form_page_10`): This field is for indicating the current status of the incident, such as "resolved" or "ongoing".

## Tips

* Please provide as much detail as possible when filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please leave it blank or note that you are unsure.
* Make sure to review your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the report.
* If you have any additional information that is not captured in this form, please provide it in the "Reporting Information" field.
