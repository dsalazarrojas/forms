1. Enter the Slushie Machine ID, which is a unique identifier for the machine.
2. Record the date of the inspection.
3. Enter the current temperature reading of the machine.
4. Enter the current pressure reading of the machine.
5. Enter the current flow reading of the machine.
6. Select the machine's operational state: Operational or Non-operational.
7. Add any additional notes about the machine's condition.
8. Enter your name and title as the technician completing the form.
