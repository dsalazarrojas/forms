# Plumbing Inspection Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help plumbers, inspectors, and other individuals inspect and report on the condition of a property's plumbing systems, including general areas, kitchen, bathroom, laundry, outdoor areas, electrical systems, plumbing systems, heating and air conditioning systems, and safety features.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the appropriate pages to inspect by choosing from the "General Areas", "Kitchen", "Bathroom", "Laundry", "Outdoor", "Electrical Systems", "Plumbing Systems", "Heating and Air Conditioning Systems", and "Safety Features" options.
2. For each page, select the relevant sections that apply to the inspected area.
3. Indicate whether each selected section is in "Good condition", "Repair needed", or "Not inspected" status.
4. Finally, select the overall status of the inspection, either "Passed" or "Failed", in the "Certification" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: General Areas

* **General Areas** (`General Areas`, text, required: false): This page is used to inspect general areas of the property, such as floors, walls, and ceilings, for any signs of water damage or leaks.

### Page 2: Kitchen

* **Sinks, faucets, and water heaters** (`Sinks, faucets, and water heaters`, select_multiple, required: false): Inspect the kitchen area, including sinks, faucets, and water heaters, and select all applicable options.
* **Drain and disposal systems** (`Drain and disposal systems`, select_multiple, required: false): Check the drain and disposal systems in the kitchen area.
* **Water supply lines and connections** (`Water supply lines and connections`, select_multiple, required: false): Inspect the water supply lines and connections in the kitchen area.
* **Other (specify)** (`Other (specify)`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Other" if the kitchen area requires additional inspection or attention.

### Page 3: Bathroom

* **Sinks and vanities** (`Sinks and vanities`, select_multiple, required: false): Inspect the sinks and vanities in the bathroom area.
* **Other (specify)** (`Other (specify)`, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Other" if the bathroom area requires additional inspection or attention.

### Page 4: Laundry

### Tips

* Make sure to inspect all areas thoroughly and accurately.
* Select all applicable options for each page.
* Use "Other (specify)" to indicate any additional areas or issues that require inspection.
* Select "Passed" or "Failed" to certify the findings.
* Review and verify the inspection findings before submitting the form.
