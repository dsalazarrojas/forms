# Cyber Security Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from end-users on their cyber security posture. The purpose of this form is to gather feedback on the types of threats you face, your current risk level, and other relevant details to help us better understand your situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These are the fields that are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **first_page** (first_page_label, optional): Please enter a brief introduction to your response.
* **survey_questions** (survey_questions_label, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you have regarding cyber security.
* **risk_level** (risk_level_label, required): Select the level of risk you believe you are currently facing:
	+ High: You are highly vulnerable to cyber threats.
	+ Low: You are not highly vulnerable to cyber threats.
	+ Moderate: You are moderately vulnerable to cyber threats.
* **frequency** (frequency_label, optional): Select the frequency of threats you face:
	+ Daily: You face threats on a daily basis.
	+ Weekly: You face threats on a weekly basis.
	+ Monthly: You face threats on a monthly basis.
* **date** (date_label, optional): Enter the date of your last known security incident or concern.
* **time** (time_label, optional): Enter the time of your last known security incident or concern.
* **email** (email_label, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (phone_number_label, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **notes** (notes_label, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

Note: If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us. We are here to help.
