# Street Light Maintenance Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by street light maintenance technicians to collect and document information about the status of street lights. It is intended to be completed during regular street light maintenance checks.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Street Light Maintenance Checklist Form" title to begin the form.
2. In the "Description" field, enter a brief description of the street light, including its location and any notable features or issues.
3. Choose the correct category from the "Category" dropdown menu:
	* General: for general maintenance information
	* Technician: for technical information about the street light's operation and repair
	* Supervisor: for supervisory information, such as work order status
4. Select the correct power source from the "Power Source" dropdown menu:
	* Electric: for electric-powered street lights
	* Solar: for solar-powered street lights
	* Gas: for gas-powered street lights
5. Choose the utilities that are currently serving the street light from the "Utility" dropdown menu:
	* Electric: for electric utilities
	* Gas: for gas utilities
	* Water: for water utilities
	* None: for street lights not connected to any utilities
6. Select the type(s) of foundations used for the street light from the "Foundations" dropdown menu:
	* Concrete: for street lights with concrete foundations
	* Steel: for street lights with steel foundations
	* Wood: for street lights with wood foundations
7. Select the type(s) of poles used for the street light from the "Poles" dropdown menu:
	* Steel: for street lights with steel poles
	* Aluminum: for street lights with aluminum poles
	* Wood: for street lights with wood poles
8. Enter any additional comments about the street light in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Street Light Maintenance Checklist Form**: Select this title to begin the form.
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the street light, including its location and any notable features or issues.
* **Category**: Choose the correct category for the information being collected.
* **Power Source**: Select the correct power source for the street light.
* **Utility**: Choose the utilities that are currently serving the street light.
* **Foundations**: Select the type(s) of foundations used for the street light.
* **Poles**: Select the type(s) of poles used for the street light.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the street light.

## Tips
* Make sure to save your progress as you complete each field.
* Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, contact your supervisor or technical support team for guidance.
