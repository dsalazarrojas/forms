# IT Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The IT Request Form is a tool used to collect and document IT requests from users. This form helps the IT team to efficiently address and resolve issues related to software, hardware, and other IT-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant category for your issue (Software, Hardware, or Other).
2. Choose the urgency level of your request (Low, Medium, or High).
3. Provide a detailed description of the issue.
4. Specify the date and time of the incident.
5. Add any additional report details.
6. Enter any relevant category details.
7. Provide contact information for follow-up.
8. Review and submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`category`, select_one): Select the category that best describes your issue (Software, Hardware, or Other).
* **Urgency** (`urgency`, select_multiple): Choose the urgency level of your issue (Low, Medium, or High).
* **Description** (`description`, text): Provide a detailed description of the issue, explaining what happened and what you experienced.
* **Report Date** (`report_date`, date): Enter the date of the incident.
* **Report Time** (`report_time`, time): Enter the time of the incident.
* **Report Details** (`report_details`, note): Add any additional details that may be helpful for the IT team to resolve your issue.
* **Category Details** (`category_details`, text): Provide any relevant details about the category you selected.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text): Enter your contact information for follow-up purposes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one): Choose to submit your request to the IT team or cancel it.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in the description field.
* Be as specific as possible when providing report details and category details.
* If you are unsure about the category or urgency level, you can ask the IT team for guidance.
* Review your request before submitting it to ensure it accurately reflects your issue.
