# hsvc_survey_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The hsvc_survey_form is used to collect vital information from technicians when servicing equipment on-site. This form ensures that all necessary details are recorded accurately, which helps the service team make informed decisions on equipment repair or replacement.


## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form for each equipment item as it is being serviced**
2. **Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as required (Active, Equipment Model, Serial Number, Installation Date)**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name (Facility Name, text, Optional):**
	+ Enter the name of the facility where the equipment is being serviced
* **Equipment Status (Equipment Status, select_one, Required):**
	+ Choose the current status of the equipment (Active or Inactive)
* **Equipment Model (Equipment Model, text, Required):**
	+ Enter the model number of the equipment being serviced
* **Equipment Manufacturer (Equipment Manufacturer, text, Optional):**
	+ Enter the manufacturer of the equipment (if applicable)
* **Serial Number (Serial Number, text, Required):**
	+ Enter the serial number of the equipment being serviced
* **Installation Date (Installation Date, date, Required):**
	+ Enter the date the equipment was installed
* **Service History (Service History, text, Optional):**
	+ Enter any relevant service history or notes about the equipment
* **Notes (Notes, note, Optional):**
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment

## Tips

* Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to the service team for guidance.
* This form should be completed for each piece of equipment being serviced during the visit.
