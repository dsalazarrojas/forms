# Laboratory Competency Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Laboratory Competency Assessment form is designed to evaluate the competencies and skills of laboratory personnel in various areas of laboratory work, including medical laboratory work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and choose the answer that best reflects your current level of knowledge, skills, and experience.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select all that apply.
3. Enter your answer to numerical questions in the designated fields.
4. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Survey
* **Survey** (`survey_1`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks you to indicate which areas you have experience with. Select all that apply from the list of options.

### 2. Knowledge Level
* **Knowledge Level** (`knowledge_level`, `number`, not required): This question asks you to rate your level of knowledge in the areas you selected in the previous question. Rate your knowledge from 0 to 100, where 0 means no knowledge and 100 means expert level.

### 3. Competency
* **Competency** (`competency`, `number`, not required): This question asks you to rate your level of competency in the areas you selected in the previous question. Rate your competency from 0 to 100, where 0 means no competency and 100 means expert level.

### 4. Scientist
* **Scientist** (`scientist`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks you to indicate which areas you have experience with. Select all that apply from the list of options.

### 5. Laboratory
* **Laboratory** (`laboratory`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks you to indicate which areas you have experience with. Select all that apply from the list of options.

### 6. Medical Laboratory
* **Medical Laboratory** (`medical_laboratory`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks you to indicate which areas you have experience with. Select all that apply from the list of options.

### 7. Coding
* **Coding** (`coding`, `select_multiple`, not required): This question asks you to indicate which areas you have experience with. Select all that apply from the list of options.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any question, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant expert.
* If you have any comments or feedback, please use the designated comment field.
