# Specimen Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Specimen Collection Form is a crucial tool used to collect and record vital information about a specimen. This form is used to standardize the collection process and minimize errors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all fields carefully and accurately.
- If a field does not apply, please leave it blank.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Specimen Collection Form**: This is the form title.
- **Patient ID** (number): Enter the unique patient identification number.
- **Patient Name** (text): Enter the patient's full name.
- **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the patient's date of birth.
- **Specimen Type** (select_one): Select the type of specimen being collected (Specimen, Swab, Urine, or Other).
- **Specimen Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the specimen.
- **Collection Method** (select_multiple): Select all applicable collection methods (Blood, Urine, Swab, or Other).
- **Technician Name** (text): Enter the name of the technician collecting the specimen.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the supervisor for guidance.
- Ensure that all information is accurate and legible.
- This form should be completed once per specimen.
