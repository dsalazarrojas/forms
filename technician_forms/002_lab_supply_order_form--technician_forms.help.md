# Lab Supply Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lab Supply Order Form is used to gather information for ordering lab supplies from the lab's inventory.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and phone number.
2. Enter the details of your order, including the items you wish to order.
3. Select your preferred payment method.
4. Add any additional notes about your order.
5. Submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information** (1: contact_details, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to gather your contact information. Please enter your name and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you about your order.
* **Email** (9: email, email, optional):
	+ This field is used to gather your email address. If you have any questions or need to confirm your order, we will use this email to contact you.
* **Phone** (10: phone, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to gather your phone number. If you have any questions or need to confirm your order, we will use this phone number to contact you.

### Order Details (Page 2)
* **Order Details** (2: order_details, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the details of your order. Please describe the items you wish to order, including any special instructions or requests.
* **Order Items** (5: order_items, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to select the items you wish to order from the lab's inventory. Please choose the items that match your needs.
* **Order Items 2** (6: order_items_2, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to select additional items you wish to order from the lab's inventory. Please choose the items that match your needs.
* **Order Items 3** (7: order_items_3, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to select more items you wish to order from the lab's inventory. Please choose the items that match your needs.

### Payment Information (Page 3)
* **Payment Details** (3: payment_details, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to enter your payment information. Please describe your payment method and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Options** (4: payment_options, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to select your preferred payment method from the available options. Please choose the payment method that applies to you.
* **Payment Options** (8: payment_options_2, select_multiple, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to select additional payment options. Please choose the options that apply to you.

### Notes (Page 4)
* **Notes** (8: notes, text, required/optional):
	+ This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about your order. Please use this field to clarify any questions or requests you have.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your order details before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before submitting your order.
* We will use the email address and phone number you provide to contact you about your order.
* Please choose the payment method that best suits your needs.
* Please do not duplicate or submit the same order multiple times.
