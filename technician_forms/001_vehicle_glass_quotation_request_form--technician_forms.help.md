# Vehicle Glass Quotation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vehicle owners or technicians for a vehicle glass quotation request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the required details about the vehicle that needs quotation.
2. Fill out the vehicle details such as vehicle type, year, model, and color.
3. Provide the quotation request details such as damage details, contact number, email address, and estimated insurance coverage.
4. Attach any required vehicle images or documents.
5. Enter the quotation details such as repair cost, insurance claim, and quotation submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Glass Quotation Request** (1): This is the title of the form, it is not a field that needs to be filled.
* **Vehicle Type** (2): Select the type of vehicle that needs the quotation (e.g., Vehicle, Van, or Truck).
* **Vehicle Year** (3): Enter the year of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (4): Enter the model of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (5): Enter the color of the vehicle.
* **Damage Details** (6): Provide a brief description of the damage to the vehicle glass.
* **Quotation Requester** (7): Enter the name of the person requesting the quotation.
* **Contact Number** (8): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (9): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Vehicle Image** (10): Attach an image of the vehicle for reference.
* **Repair Location** (11): Enter the location where the repair will take place.
* **Estimated Insurance** (12): Enter an estimated value of the insurance coverage.
* **Quotation Details** (13): Provide any additional details about the quotation.
* **Vehicle Owner** (14): Enter the name of the vehicle owner.
* **Contact Person** (15): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (16): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone** (17): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Quotation Request Date** (18): Enter the date of the quotation request.
* **Vehicle Image Upload** (19): Upload an image of the vehicle for reference.
* **Additional Notes** (20): Provide any additional information about the quotation.
* **Repair Cost** (21): Enter the estimated repair cost.
* **Insurance Claim** (22): Check if the quotation is for an insurance claim.
* **Quotation Submission** (23): Check if the quotation has been submitted.
* **Quotation Accepted** (24): Check if the quotation has been accepted.
* **Quotation Status** (25): Select the status of the quotation (In Progress, Completed, Cancelled, or Accepted).

By following these steps, you will be able to complete the vehicle glass quotation request form accurately and efficiently.
