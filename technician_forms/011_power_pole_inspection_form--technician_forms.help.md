# Power Pole Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the inspection of power poles, including their condition, maintenance history, and any necessary repairs or replacements. It is intended for use by field personnel to gather information about power poles and ensure they are properly maintained.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form for each power pole being inspected.
2. Answer the questions based on your observation and best judgment.
3. Make sure to include any necessary notes or comments in the "notes" field.
4. Enter the date of the inspection in the "inspection_date" field.
5. Select the correct option for "pole_condition" based on your observation.
6. Select the correct option for "inspector" based on who performed the inspection.
7. Enter the "inspection_location" where the power pole is located.
8. Enter the "pole_height" if it is available and relevant to the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **power_pole_inspection_form** (`power_pole_inspection_form`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the power pole being inspected.
* **pole_condition** (`pole_condition`, select_one, required: false): Select the condition of the power pole from the options: Inspect, Repair, Replace, or Other. This field helps to categorize the pole's condition and guide further action.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the power pole's condition, maintenance history, or any necessary repairs.
* **pole_height** (`pole_height`, number, required: false): Enter the height of the power pole if it is relevant to the inspection.
* **inspection_date** (`inspection_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the inspection.
* **inspector** (`inspector`, select_one, required: false): Select the inspector who performed the inspection from the options: John, Jane, or Joe.
* **inspection_location** (`inspection_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the power pole is situated.
* **Other** (Other, select_one, required: false): This field is not used in the form.

# Tips
* Be sure to fill out all relevant fields.
* If the power pole's condition is not listed in the options, select "Other" and add a brief explanation in the "notes" field.
* Make sure to include the correct inspection location to ensure accurate tracking and mapping.
* This form is intended to be a standard template for power pole inspections. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your supervisor or maintenance team.
