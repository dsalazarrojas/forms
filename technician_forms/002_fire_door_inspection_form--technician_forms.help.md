# Fire Door Inspection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fire Door Inspection Form is a critical tool for assessing fire safety in buildings. This form is designed to gather information about the condition and number of fire doors, which is essential for ensuring fire safety standards are met.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the inspection date and time in the "Inspection Date" field.
2. Answer the question about the presence of fire exit doors. Select "Yes" if the fire exit doors are present, and "No" otherwise.
3. Answer the question about the presence of regular fire doors. Select "Yes" if the fire doors are present, and "No" otherwise.
4. Evaluate the condition of the fire doors and select "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" from the provided options.
5. Enter the number of fire exit doors and fire doors, respectively.
6. If required, provide a brief description of the fire exit doors and regular fire doors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspection Date**: This field is for entering the date and time of the inspection. Please format it according to your company's standard date and time notation.
* **Fire Exit Doors**: This field asks if there are fire exit doors present. Choose "Yes" if you find fire exit doors in the building, and "No" otherwise.
* **Fire Doors**: This field asks about the presence of regular fire doors. Select "Yes" if you find regular fire doors, and "No" otherwise.
* **Condition**: This field assesses the condition of the fire doors. Choose from "Excellent", "Good", "Fair", or "Poor" based on the observed condition.
* **Number of Fire Exit Doors**: Enter the number of fire exit doors found in the building.
* **Number of Fire Doors**: Enter the number of regular fire doors found in the building.
* **Description of Fire Exit Doors**: Provide a brief description of the fire exit doors, if required.
* **Description of Fire Doors**: Provide a brief description of the regular fire doors, if required.
* **Notes**: If you have additional information about the fire doors or any observations, please enter them in this field. Note that this field is not required.

## Tips

* Always complete this form accurately and honestly.
* Ensure you have access to the building or area being inspected before completing this form.
* If you're unsure about any aspect of the form, please consult with your supervisor or a colleague.
* This form is for fire door inspections only. If you need to use it for other purposes, please use a different form.
