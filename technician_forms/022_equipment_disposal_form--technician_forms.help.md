# Equipment Disposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the disposal of equipment. It captures information about the equipment's status, condition, and location, as well as the reason for its disposal and any relevant authorization or confirmation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate disposal status from the list provided.
2. Choose the type of asset being disposed of (e.g., Equipment, Supplies, Vehicles, Other).
3. Provide a brief description of the asset, including its serial number and any other identifying information.
4. Enter the date of disposal.
5. Enter any notes or comments about the disposal.
6. Select the condition of the asset at the time of disposal.
7. Enter the reason for disposal.
8. Indicate whether disposal authorization was given.
9. Select the status of disposal confirmation.
10. Provide any additional remarks or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Disposal Status** (`disposal_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the current status of the equipment, such as "Equipment Sold", "Refurbished", "Donated", "Scrap", or "Salvage".
* **Asset Type** (`asset_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type of asset being disposed of, such as "Equipment", "Supplies", "Vehicles", or "Other".
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the asset, including its serial number and any other identifying information.
* **Asset Tag Number** (`asset_tag_number`, text, required: false): Enter the asset's tag number, if applicable.
* **Asset Serial Number** (`asset_serial_number`, text, required: false): Enter the asset's serial number, if applicable.
* **Disposal Date** (`disposal_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of disposal.
* **Disposal Notes** (`disposal_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the disposal.
* **Asset Condition** (`asset_condition`, text, required: false): Describe the condition of the asset at the time of disposal.
* **Reason for Disposal** (`reason_for_disposal`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for the equipment's disposal.
* **Disposal Authorization** (`disposal_authorization`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether disposal authorization was given.
* **Authorization Date** (`authorization_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of authorization.
* **Disposal Confirmation** (`disposal_confirmation`, select_one, required: false): Select the status of disposal confirmation.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, text, required: false): Enter any additional remarks or comments.
* **Equipment ID** (`equipment_id`, text, required: false): Enter the equipment's ID number, if applicable.
* **Asset Location** (`asset_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the asset at the time of disposal.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or the relevant personnel.
* This form is designed to be easy to use and complete; however, if you experience any issues, please report them to your supervisor.
