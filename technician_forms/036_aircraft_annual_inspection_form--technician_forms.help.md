# Aircraft Annual Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aircraft Annual Inspection Form is designed to gather information about the condition and history of an aircraft after a routine inspection. This information is crucial for the aircraft owner/manager to identify potential issues and maintain airworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about the aircraft's identity, maintenance history, and current condition.
2. Ensure all fields are filled out completely and correctly. Missing or incorrect information may lead to errors or discrepancies.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name**: Full name of the inspector conducting the inspection.
* **Inspector Certificate Number**: A&P or IA certificate number of the inspector.
* **Inspection Date**: Date of the inspection.
* **Aircraft Registration**: N-number or registration of the aircraft.
* **Aircraft Make**: Manufacturer of the aircraft.
* **Aircraft Model**: Model designation of the aircraft.
* **Aircraft Serial Number**: Manufacturer serial number of the aircraft.
* **Total Airframe Hours**: Total time in service.
* **Engine Make**: Engine manufacturer.
* **Engine Model**: Engine model.
* **Left Engine Serial Number**: (Optional) Serial number of the left engine. This field is only required if the left engine is different from the right engine.
* **Right Engine Serial Number**: (Optional) Serial number of the right engine. This field is only required if the right engine is different from the left engine.
* **Total Engine Hours**: Time since new.
* **Hours Since Overhaul**: TSO.
* **Propeller Type**: Propeller manufacturer and type.
* **Airworthiness Directives Status**: Status of compliance with ADs.
* **Fuselage Inspection**: Condition assessment of the fuselage.
* **Wing Inspection**: Condition assessment of the wing.
* **Empennage Inspection**: Condition assessment of the empennage.
* **Landing Gear Inspection**: Condition assessment of the landing gear.
* **Flight Controls Inspection**: Condition assessment of the flight controls.
* **Engine Inspection**: Condition assessment of the engine.
* **Avionics Inspection**: Condition assessment of the avionics.
* **Electrical System Inspection**: Condition assessment of the electrical system.
* **Fuel System Inspection**: Condition assessment of the fuel system.
* **Hydraulic System Inspection**: Condition assessment of the hydraulic system.
* **Emergency Equipment Check**: Check on all emergency equipment, including life vests, first aid kits, etc.
* **Discrepancies Found**: List all discrepancies observed during the inspection.
* **Corrective Action Taken**: Describe any corrective actions taken.
* **Parts Replaced**: List any parts replaced during the inspection.
* **Airworthiness Status**: Final determination of the aircraft's airworthiness.
* **Next Annual Inspection Due**: Due date for the next annual inspection.
* **Inspector Signature**: Digital signature of the inspector.
* **Owner Signature**: Owner's acknowledgment of the inspection results.

Note: This guide is meant to be a general help guide and is not intended to be exhaustive. Users should refer to relevant regulations and industry standards for detailed instructions and requirements.
