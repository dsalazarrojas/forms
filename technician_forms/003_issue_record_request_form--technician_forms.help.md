# issue_record_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and document issues that require technical support. It helps the support team to quickly gather necessary information and provide timely assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Select the correct options for the requestor and issue status from the respective dropdown menus.
3. Double-check the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, optional): This field is for the title of the issue record.

### Client Name
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client associated with the issue.

### Client Email
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the client.

### Issue Date
* **Issue Date** (`issue_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the issue was first reported.

### Issue Details
* **Issue Details** (`issue_details`, text, optional): Describe the issue in as much detail as possible.

### Requestor
* **Requestor** (`requestor`, select_multiple, optional): Select the individual or team responsible for submitting the request.

### Requestor Department
* **Requestor Department** (`requestor_department`, select_multiple, optional): Select the department of the requestor.

### Requestor Email
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the requestor.

### Issue Status
* **Issue Status** (`issue_status`, select_one, optional): Select the current status of the issue from the options provided.

### Requestor Phone
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the requestor.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the issue.

Note: Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely.
