# Teleopto Services Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers seeking teleopto services. Please ensure you are using this form for this specific purpose to ensure accurate and efficient service delivery.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field.
3. Enter the date you are seeking teleopto services in the "date" field.
4. Enter the time you are available for services in the "time" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or information about your teleopto service request in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name for our records.
* **date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **date** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date you are available for services. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **time** (`time`, required/optional): Enter the time you are available for services. Please use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **phone** (`phone`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm your appointment.
* **email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can send you appointment reminders and notifications.
* **note** (`note`, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your teleopto service request.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure efficient service delivery.
* Use the correct date and time formats when filling out the "date" and "time" fields.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the "note" field.
* Our team will review and contact you to confirm your appointment after submitting this form.
