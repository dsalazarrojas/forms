# Equipment Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Equipment Evaluation Form is used to collect information about the equipment's overall condition, performance, reliability, and quality. This form helps technicians and maintenance personnel assess the equipment's current state and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the equipment's current condition and functionality.
2. Complete the form fields with the relevant information.
3. Provide detailed comments and explanations for each field as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (General Information)** (`general_info`, note, **not required**): This section is for providing a brief introduction to the equipment being evaluated.
* **Overall Rating (Overall Rating)** (`rating`, number, **not required**): Rate the equipment's overall performance from 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Performance (Performance)** (`performance`, text, **not required**): Describe the equipment's performance, including any issues or problems encountered.
* **Reliability (Reliability)** (`reliability`, text, **not required**): Discuss the equipment's reliability, including any issues or concerns.
* **Quality (Quality)** (`quality`, text, **not required**): Evaluate the equipment's quality, including any defects or malfunctions.
* **Features (Features)** (`features`, text, **not required**): List the equipment's features and any notable specifications.
* **Value (Value)** (`value`, text, **not required**): Assess the equipment's value, including its usefulness and impact on the organization.
* **User Comments (User Comments)** (`user_comments`, note, **not required**): Add any additional comments or observations about the equipment.

## Tips

* Be as detailed and specific as possible when completing the form.
* Use clear and concise language when providing explanations and comments.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask for clarification or assistance from a colleague.
* This form is intended for internal use only; do not share it with external parties without permission.
