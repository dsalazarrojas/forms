# Equipment Replacement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a replacement for a piece of equipment. It ensures that all necessary information is gathered for the timely and efficient fulfillment of the replacement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the type of equipment you are requesting a replacement for from the "Equipment Type" dropdown menu.
2.  In the "Description" field, briefly explain why you need the equipment replaced.
3.  In the "Requested Parts List" field, list the specific parts you are requesting to be replaced.
4.  Enter your contact number and email address in the "Contact Number" and "Email" fields respectively.
5.  Choose the technician assigned to handle your request from the "Assigned Technician" dropdown menu.
6.  Select the current status of the equipment from the "Equipment Status" dropdown menu.
7.  If you have any additional comments, type them into the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Equipment Type**:
  This field should be filled out with the type of equipment you're requesting a replacement for. This will help determine the correct replacement process.
*   **Description**:
    *   Briefly explain why you need the equipment replaced. This helps in understanding the context and reason for the replacement.
*   **Requested Parts List**:
    *   List the parts you are requesting to be replaced or upgraded. This will help the technician know exactly what needs to be ordered or repaired.
*   **Contact Number**:
    *   Enter your work phone number where you can be reached for further instructions or updates.
*   **Email**:
    *   Enter your work email address where you can receive updates or information about your request.
*   **Assigned Technician**:
    *   Choose the technician who will handle your request. This ensures they are aware of the specific details and requirements of your request.
*   **Date Requested**:
    *   Enter the date you requested the equipment replacement. This helps track the timeline of the replacement process.
*   **Equipment Status**:
    *   Select the status of your equipment from the provided options. This helps in determining the urgency and priority of the replacement process.
*   **Additional Comments**:
    *   If you have any other notes or comments related to your request, type them into this field. This provides additional context for the technician handling your request.
