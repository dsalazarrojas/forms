# Heavy Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Heavy Equipment Checklist is a form used by technicians to inspect and report on the condition, status, and maintenance history of heavy equipment. This form is meant to be completed for a one-time or periodic inspection and report, and any subsequent inspections should only be completed after a new or modified piece of equipment has been installed or when a significant change has occurred.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Equipment Condition: Provide a brief description of the equipment's condition.
2. Enter the Machine ID: Enter the unique identifier assigned to the equipment.
3. Select the Equipment Type: Choose the type of equipment being inspected.
4. Enter the Serial Number: Provide the serial number of the equipment.
5. Enter the Model Number: Provide the model number of the equipment.
6. Select the Year Manufactured: Choose the year the equipment was manufactured.
7. Record the Last Serviced Date: Enter the date the equipment was last serviced.
8. Enter the Next Servicing Due Date: Enter the date the equipment is due for its next service.
9. Add Comments: Provide any additional comments or notes about the equipment's condition or maintenance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Equipment Condition**: A brief description of the equipment's condition, e.g., "Good", "Needs repair", "Out of service".
* **Machine ID**: The unique identifier assigned to the equipment.
* **Equipment Type**: A select field where you can choose from pre-defined options, such as "Backhoe", "Excavator", or "Crane".
* **Serial Number**: A text field to enter the serial number of the equipment.
* **Model Number**: A text field to enter the model number of the equipment.
* **Year Manufactured**: A select field where you can choose from pre-defined options, such as "2000", "2005", or "2010".
* **Last Serviced**: A field to record the date the equipment was last serviced.
* **Next Servicing Due Date**: A field to record the date the equipment is due for its next service.
* **Comments**: A text field for any additional comments or notes about the equipment's condition or maintenance.
