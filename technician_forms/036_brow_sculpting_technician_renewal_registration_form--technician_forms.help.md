# Brow Sculpting Technician Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by registered Brow Sculpting Technicians (BSTs) to renew their registration. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Submit the form for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Technician Name** (`technician_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your registration records.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your unique registration or license ID.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, required): Choose the date you are submitting this renewal application.
* **Training Completed** (`training_completed`, text, optional): If you have completed any recent training or courses, list them here.
* **Continuing Education Hours** (`continuing_education_hours`, number, optional): If you have completed hours in continuing education, enter the total number.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your current salon or employer.
* **Certification Documents** (`certification_documents`, note, optional): Attach any relevant certification documents with your application.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for renewal confirmation and updates.
