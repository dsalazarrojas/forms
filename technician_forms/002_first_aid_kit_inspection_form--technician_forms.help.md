# First Aid Kit Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to inspect and record the contents and condition of first aid kits in facilities. It's a critical document used to maintain accurate records and ensure compliance with health and safety regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form to understand the required information and sections.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly for each section.
3. Answer questions honestly, and if unsure, note the uncertainty.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Aid Kit Inspection** (`first_aid_kit_inspection_form`, Note, Optional): This is a section to describe the purpose and context of the inspection.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, Select One, Optional): Check the correct facility type from the provided options (e.g., 'Yes', 'No') to indicate the type of facility being inspected.
* **Kit Contents Location** (`kit_contents_location`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all applicable options from the list to indicate where first aid kits are typically stored within the facility.
* **First Aids** (`first_aids`, Number, Optional): If applicable, report the quantity of first aid items found or expected to be in the kit.
* **Bandages** (`bandages`, Number, Optional): Report the quantity of bandages found or expected to be in the kit.
* **Antiseptic** (`antiseptic`, Number, Optional): Report the quantity of antiseptic items found or expected to be in the kit.
* **Gauze** (`gauze`, Number, Optional): Report the quantity of gauze found or expected to be in the kit.
* **Medical Kits** (`medical_kits`, Number, Optional): Report the quantity of medical kits found or expected to be in the kit.
* **Cold Pack** (`cold_pack`, Number, Optional): Report the quantity of cold packs found or expected to be in the kit.
* **Gloves** (`gloves`, Number, Optional): Report the quantity of gloves found or expected to be in the kit.
* **First Aid Manual** (`first_aid_manual`, Date, Optional): Record the date of the last first aid manual update or revision.
* **Inspection Date** (`inspection_date`, Date, Optional): Record the date and time of the inspection.
* **Inspected By** (`inspected_by`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and title of the person performing the inspection.
* **Comments** (`comments`, Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the inspection.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and title of the person completing the form.
* **Assigned Technician** (`assigned_technician`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and title of the technician assigned to complete the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Text, Optional): Enter the name and title of the tool or equipment used for the inspection.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are accurately and thoroughly completed.
* Review the form for completeness before submitting.
* Keep records of past inspections for future reference and compliance checks.
* If unsure or unable to complete a section, note the uncertainty and proceed with the best available information.
* If any discrepancies or issues arise during the inspection, note them in the Comments section and address them accordingly.
