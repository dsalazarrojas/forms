# Equipment Breakdown Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Equipment Breakdown Form is a tool for technicians to report and track equipment failures. It is designed to be used in the field or on-site, and helps with troubleshooting and repair of equipment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Open the form and select the correct page for the type of breakdown you are reporting.
2. Fill out the relevant information in the "Broken Equipment" and "Broken Parts" fields as needed.
3. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
4. Enter the location where the equipment was broken down.
5. Save the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Broken Equipment** (1/2): This field is for describing the specific piece of equipment that has broken down. Describe the equipment in detail, including its model number or serial number if applicable.
* **Broken Parts** (1/2): This field is for describing the specific part or parts that have failed. Be as specific as possible when describing the parts.
* **Notes** (3/2): This field is for adding any additional information that may be relevant to the breakdown, such as circumstances leading up to the failure or any other details that may be helpful for repair.
* **Broken Location** (4/2): This field is for describing where the equipment was broken down, including any relevant details about the location such as room or site numbers.
