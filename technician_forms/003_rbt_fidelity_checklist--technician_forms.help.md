# RBT Fidelity Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The RBT Fidelity Checklist form is designed to collect information about the execution of a job, ensuring that tasks were conducted safely and effectively, and that all necessary safety equipment and tools were available and functioning properly. This form is crucial for maintaining a high level of fidelity in the workplace, which is essential for the team's credibility and reliability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions in Checklists Section 1, providing detailed information about the tasks performed during the job.
2. Select the correct options for Checklists Section 2, which will help you categorize the outcome of your job.
3. Fill in the Date and Time fields, which are required for tracking and reporting purposes.
4. Provide any additional information in the Notes field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Checklist Section 1
* Checklist Section 1 Question 1: What are the tasks performed during the job? (`checklist_section_1_question_1`, Text, Required)
	+ This question asks you to describe the tasks you performed during the job. Please provide a clear and concise description of the activities you undertook.
* Checklist Section 1 Question 2: What were the main hazards and risks identified during the job? (`checklist_section_1_question_2`, Text, Required)
	+ This question asks you to identify any hazards or risks that were present during the job. Please provide details about the nature of these hazards and the measures taken to mitigate them.
* Checklist Section 1 Question 3: Were all necessary safety equipment and tools available and in good working order? (`checklist_section_1_question_3`, Radio, Required)
	+ This question asks you to confirm that all necessary safety equipment and tools were available and functioning properly during the job. Please select the correct option to indicate whether they were available and in working order.
* Checklist Section 1 Question 4: Were all safety procedures followed correctly? (`checklist_section_1_question_4`, Radio, Required)
	+ This question asks you to confirm that all safety procedures were followed correctly during the job. Please select the correct option to indicate whether the procedures were followed correctly.

### Checklist Section 2
* Checklist Section 2: (`checklist_section_2`, Select One, Optional)
	+ This section allows you to select one of the provided options to categorize the outcome of your job. Please choose the option that best describes the outcome.
* Checklist Section 2 Subsection 1: (`checklist_section_2_sub_1`, Select Multiple, Required)
	+ This question asks you to select all the options that apply to the outcome of your job. Please choose all the relevant options.

### Additional Information
* Date (`date`, Date, Required)
	+ This field is required for tracking and reporting purposes. Please enter the date on which you completed the job.
* Time (`time`, Time, Required)
	+ This field is required for tracking and reporting purposes. Please enter the time at which you completed the job.
* Email (`email`, Email, Optional)
	+ This field is optional. If you wish to contact you about your experience, please enter your email address here.
* Phone (`phone`, Text, Optional)
	+ This field is optional. If you have any additional comments or questions, please feel free to provide your phone number here.
* Notes (`notes`, Note, Optional)
	+ This field is optional. If you have any additional information or comments, please enter them here.
