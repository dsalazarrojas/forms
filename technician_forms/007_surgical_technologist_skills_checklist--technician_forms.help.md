# surgical_technologist_skills_checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assess the skills and qualifications of surgical technologists, evaluating their abilities in various areas such as patient care, safety, role, scrubbing and suturing, emergency skills, general knowledge, education, and equipment and supplies.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer questions honestly.
2. For each field, select the appropriate response based on your level of knowledge or experience.
3. For text fields, provide a brief description of your skills or qualifications.
4. For select multiple fields, choose the options that best match your skills or qualifications.
5. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
