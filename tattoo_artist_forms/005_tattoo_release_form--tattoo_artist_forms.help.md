# tattoo_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a tattooing process, including the artist's and client's contact information, the tattoo design, and the relevant dates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name and contact information as a tattoo artist.
2. Enter the client's name and contact information.
3. Provide details about the tattoo design, including any relevant notes.
4. Enter the date of consent for the tattoo.
5. Sign and date the form as the tattoo artist.
6. Sign and date the form as the client.
7. Enter the date this form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tattoo Artist** (`tattoo_artist`, `text`, required): Enter your name and contact information as a tattoo artist.
* **Client** (`client`, `text`, required): Enter the client's name and contact information.
* **Tattoo Design** (`tattoo_design`, `text`, required): Provide details about the tattoo design, including any relevant notes.
* **Tattoo Details** (`tattoo_details`, `text`, required): Add any additional details about the tattoo, such as placement, size, or other relevant information.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the client gave consent for the tattoo.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the tattoo artist.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the client.
* **Tattoo Artist Signature** (`tattoo_artist_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name as the tattoo artist (this should only be done after all other fields are completed).
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this form was completed.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the tattoo will expire, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all fields accurately and thoroughly to ensure accurate documentation.
- If the tattoo has multiple components or designs, provide details for each component or design.
- If the client is unable to sign the form, consider providing an alternate signature method, such as a witness signature.
- This form is designed to be used for one-time use; if the client requires multiple tattoos, use this form for each tattoo session.
