# Piercing Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Piercing Consultation Form is a mandatory form that helps us assess your eligibility for a piercing. Please answer all fields truthfully to ensure a safe and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details.
2. Answer all questions carefully.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official government documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official government documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

## Tips
- Please answer all fields truthfully and accurately.
- If you have any medical conditions or concerns, please let us know.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to ask.
