## Tattoo Aftercare Acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The form is used to ensure the client's understanding of the aftercare instructions provided after a tattoo session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name and contact information (first name, last name, email, phone number)
2. Enter the date of your last tattoo session.
3. Read and acknowledge that you have received and understood the aftercare instructions.
4. Select whether you have read and understood the aftercare instructions.
5. Optionally, you can choose to acknowledge that you have signed and returned the aftercare instructions.
6. Optionally, you can choose to acknowledge that you have received a copy of the aftercare instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Tattoo Date** (`tattoo_date`, number, optional): Enter the date of your last tattoo session.
* **Aftercare Instructions** (`aftercare_instructions`, note, optional): Please read and make sure you understand the aftercare instructions before proceeding.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply to confirm your understanding:
	+ Client has read and understood the aftercare instructions
	+ Client has signed and returned the aftercare instructions
	+ Client has received a copy of the aftercare instructions
* **Client Has Understood** (`client_has_understood`, select_one, optional): Select 'True' to confirm that you have read and understood the aftercare instructions.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand the aftercare instructions before proceeding.
- Take the necessary steps to maintain your skin's integrity post-tattoo.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your artist or the studio for further assistance.
