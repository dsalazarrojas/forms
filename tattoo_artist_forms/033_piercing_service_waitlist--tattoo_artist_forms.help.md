The Piercing Service Waitlist form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their preferred piercings, enabling piercing artists and service providers to prepare and plan accordingly.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Client Details" page.
2.  Fill in your client details, if required.
3.  Click on the "Preferred Services" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Details
* **Client Details** (`Client Details`, text, required/optional): This section is for providing your basic information such as name, contact number, and other relevant details that the piercing service provider may need to know you.
### Preferred Services
* **Preferred Services** (`Preferred Services`, text, required/optional): Here, you can specify the types of piercings you're interested in, which will help the service provider prepare for your appointment.
### Appointment Details
* **Appointment Details** (`Appointment Details`, text, required/optional): In this section, you can provide any additional information about your appointment, such as date and time preferences.
