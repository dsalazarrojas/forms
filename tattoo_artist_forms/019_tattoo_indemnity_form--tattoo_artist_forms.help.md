# tattoo_indemnity_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The tattoo indemnity form is a required document that ensures both you and the artist understand your rights and responsibilities before a tattooing session. This form is designed to collect information about your tattoo experience, including details about the tattoo, payment terms, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Ensure you have read and understand the form's purpose and requirements.
* Fill out all required fields carefully and accurately.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (<code>client_info</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
* **Tattoo Details** (<code>tattoo_details</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Describe the tattoo you wish to get, including size and design.
* **Artist Info** (<code>artist_info</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Provide the artist's name and any relevant contact information.
* **Tattoo Location** (<code>tattoo_location</code>, Select One, <code>Required</code>): Indicate whether the tattoo will be located on your body or in a specific area.
* **Tattoo Design** (<code>tattoo_design</code>, Select Multiple, <code>Required</code>): Choose which designs or elements you wish to include in the tattoo.
* **Tattoo Size** (<code>tattoo_size</code>, Number, <code>Required</code>): Enter the size of the tattoo in inches.
* **Estimated Time** (<code>estimated_time</code>, Number, <code>Optional</code>): Estimate the time required for the tattooing session.
* **Total Cost** (<code>total_cost</code>, Number, <code>Required</code>): Enter the total cost of the tattooing session.
* **Session Info** (<code>session_info</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Provide any additional information about the session.
* **Payment Terms** (<code>payment_terms</code>, Select Multiple, <code>Required</code>): Choose how you wish to pay for the session.
* **Client Signature** (<code>client_signature</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Sign this form to confirm you understand and agree to the terms.
* **Artist Signature** (<code>artist_signature</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): The artist must sign this form to confirm they have read and understood the terms.
* **Client Name** (<code>client_name</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your full name.
* **Client Email** (<code>client_email</code>, Email, <code>Required</code>): Enter your email address.
* **Client Phone** (<code>client_phone</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your phone number.
* **Client Address** (<code>client_address</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your address.
* **Client City** (<code>client_city</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your city.
* **Client State** (<code>client_state</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your state.
* **Client Zip** (<code>client_zip</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter your zip code.
* **Artist Name** (<code>artist_name</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter the artist's name.
* **Artist Email** (<code>artist_email</code>, Email, <code>Required</code>): Enter the artist's email address.
* **Artist Phone** (<code>artist_phone</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter the artist's phone number.
* **Artist Address** (<code>artist_address</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter the artist's address.
* **Artist License** (<code>artist_license</code>, Text, <code>Required</code>): Enter the artist's license number.
