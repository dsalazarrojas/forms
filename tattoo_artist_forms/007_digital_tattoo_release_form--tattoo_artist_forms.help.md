# Digital Tattoo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document that allows a client to grant permission for a tattoo artist to display, use, or reproduce their work. It's a crucial step in the tattoo process, ensuring that the artist has the necessary rights to share their art publicly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the `client_name` field.
2. Choose the type of release in the `release_type` field. You can select from the following options:
	* Model and/or likeness
	* Image/photograph
	* Voice
	* Personal story
	* Other (Please specify)
3. If you are releasing your image or voice, specify the location where it will be displayed or used in the `release_location` field.
4. Enter the purpose of the release in the `release_purpose` field.
5. Check the box indicating that the release is permanent (if applicable) and enter any additional terms in the `additional_terms` field.
6. Date and time of signing in the `signature_date` and `time_signed` fields (if applicable).
7. Sign the form by entering your name in the `signature_name` field.
8. Confirm that you have signed the form by signing the `client_signed` field.
9. Optionally, have a witness or other representative sign the `witness_signed`, `other_signed`, `artist_signed` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_name**: Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, etc.).
* **release_type**: Choose how the client wishes to release their art (see explanation above).
* **release_location**: If releasing visual content, specify where it will be displayed or used (e.g., social media, website, art exhibit, etc.). 
* **release_purpose**: Explain why the client is granting the release, e.g., for commercial use, art exhibition, or personal sharing.
* **release_permanent**: Check if the release is permanent or not.
* **additional_terms**: List any additional conditions or requirements for the release.
* **signature_date**: Date of signing.
* **signature_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **client_signed**: Confirm that you have signed the form.
* **artist_signed**: Enter your name if you are the artist or have been authorized to sign on their behalf.
* **witness_signed**: Enter the name of the witness or representative who has verified your signature.
* **other_signed**: Enter the name of any other person who has signed or authorized the release.
* **date_signed**: Date of signing.
* **time_signed**: Time of signing.
* **location_signed**: Location of signing.
* **location_signed**: Enter the location where the signing took place.
