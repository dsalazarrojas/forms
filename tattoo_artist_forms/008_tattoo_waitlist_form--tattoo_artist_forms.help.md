# Tattoo Waitlist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tattoo Waitlist Form is used to collect information from clients about their tattoo preferences and schedule appointments for tattoo artists.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. For fields that allow multiple selection, select all relevant options.
3. Double-check that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the appointment or tattoo session.
* **Form Date** (`form_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the appointment or tattoo session.
* **Form Time** (`form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time for the appointment or tattoo session.
* **Client Name** (`form_client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Client Phone** (`form_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email** (`form_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email.
* **Tattoo Style** (`form_tattoo_style`, select one, required): Select the style of the tattoo from the available options.
* **Tattoo Size** (`form_tattoo_size`, text, optional): Enter the size of the tattoo.
* **Tattoo Color** (`form_tattoo_color`, text, optional): Enter the color of the tattoo.
* **Client Phone (Again)** (`form_client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number again.
* **Client Email (Again)** (`form_client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email again.
* **Message to Client** (`form_message`, text, optional): Enter a message to the client.
* **Status** (`form_status`, select one, required): Select the status of the appointment or tattoo session from the available options.
* **Waitlist** (`form_waitlist`, text, optional): Enter additional information about the waitlist.
* **Note for Tattoo Artist** (`form_note`, note, optional): Leave a note for the tattoo artist.
* **Note to Client** (`form_signature`, note, optional): Leave a note for the client.
* **Awaiting** (`form_awaiting`, select multiple, required): Select the things the client is awaiting from the available options.
* **Next Available** (`form_next_available`, text, optional): Enter the next available appointment or session date and time.
* **Tattoo Artist** (`form_tattoo_artist`, text, required): Enter the name of the tattoo artist.
* **Date Booked** (`form_date_booked`, date, required): Enter the date of the appointment or tattoo session.
* **Time Booked** (`form_time_booked`, time, required): Enter the time of the appointment or tattoo session.
* **Number in Waitlist** (`form_number_in_waitlist`, text, required): Enter the number in the waitlist.
* **Position in Waitlist** (`form_position_in_waitlist`, text, required): Enter the position in the waitlist.
* **Message to Client** (`form_message_to_client`, text, required): Enter a message to the client.
* **Signature to Client** (`form_signature_to_client`, text, required): Enter a signature or confirmation to the client.
* **Awaiting (Again)** (`form_awaiting`, select multiple, optional): Select the things the client is awaiting from the available options.
