# Contact Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from individuals who are interested in getting a tattoo. The form is used to gather necessary details about the client, such as their name, email, phone number, preferred contact method, and tattoo style preferences, to facilitate communication and provide personalized service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Full Name" and "Email Address" fields respectively.
2. If you have a phone number, provide it in the "Phone Number" field.
3. Choose how you would like us to contact you from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown menu.
4. If you are not a first-time tattoo client, answer "False" to the "Is this your first tattoo" question.
5. Select all that apply from the list of tattoo styles that interest you in the "Preferred Tattoo Styles" field.
6. If you would like to receive updates about our guest artists and events, select "Yes, please sign me up" in the "Subscribe to our newsletter" field.
7. Finally, answer "How did you hear about us" with the method that applies to you.
8. If you have any additional comments or questions, use the "Additional Comments or Questions" field for that purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, optional): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, required): Choose how you would like us to contact you:
	+ Email
	+ Phone Call
	+ SMS/Text
	+ WhatsApp
* **Is this your first tattoo** (`first_tattoo`, required): Answer "True" if this is your first tattoo, "False" otherwise.
* **Preferred Tattoo Styles** (`preferred_styles`, optional): Select all the tattoo styles that interest you:
	+ Traditional (Old School)
	+ Realism
	+ Watercolor
	+ Tribal
	+ New School
	+ Neo Traditional
	+ Japanese (Irezumi)
	+ Blackwork
* **Subscribe to our newsletter** (`newsletter_subscription`, required): Choose to stay updated on our guest artists and events.
* **How did you hear about us** (`how_did_you_hear`, optional): Answer how you found out about us:
	+ Instagram
	+ Facebook
	+ Word of mouth
	+ Google Search
	+ Walking by the studio
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, feel free to enter them here.
