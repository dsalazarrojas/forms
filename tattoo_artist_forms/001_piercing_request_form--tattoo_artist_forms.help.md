# piercing_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a piercing from a tattoo artist. The form collects information about the client, their body areas of interest, the type of piercing they want, and some basic contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your information in the **client_info** field, including your name, if desired.
2. Select the body areas you are interested in getting pierced in the **body_area** field.
3. Choose the type of piercing you want in the **piercing_type** field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field.
5. Choose the date of your request in the **date_of_request** field.
6. Enter your desired tattoo artist's name in the **tattoo_artist** field.
7. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
8. Enter your phone number in the **phone_number** field.
9. Enter your address in the **address** field.
10. Add any additional information or comments in the **message** field, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **client_info**: Enter your name and any other information you want to share with the tattoo artist.
* **body_area**: Select one or more areas of the body where you would like to get pierced. This could be your ear, neck, face, hand, or foot.
* **piercing_type**: Choose the type of piercing you are interested in. Please select one of the options provided.
* **date_of_birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **date_of_request**: Choose the date you would like to request the piercing.
* **tattoo_artist**: Enter the name of the tattoo artist you would like to work with, if desired.
* **email**: Enter your email address so the tattoo artist can contact you.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number so the tattoo artist can contact you.
* **address**: Enter your address so the tattoo artist can contact you in person, if necessary.
* **message**: Add any additional information or comments you would like to share with the tattoo artist.
