# Home Bodies Tattoo Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information necessary for a safe and successful tattooing experience. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure that your needs are met during the tattoo session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your title.
2. Provide your name and date of birth.
3. Enter your address and phone number.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Provide the name, relationship, and contact information of an emergency contact person.
6. Describe any medical history or allergies.
7. Choose a payment method and enter the payment amount and date.
8. Select the tattoo artist and studio where you will be receiving the tattoo.
9. Choose the date and time of the tattoo session.
10. Enter the expected length of the tattoo session.
11. Enter the session price.
12. Confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Home Bodies Tattoo Consent Form** (`home_bodies_tattoo_consent_form`, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Please enter your title to help the tattoo artist and studio staff get to know you better.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please enter your name accurately.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (e.g., 12/01/1990).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your current address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Emergency Contact Person Name** (`emergency_contact_person_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Person Relationship** (`emergency_contact_person_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Phone** (`emergency_contact_person_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Email** (`emergency_contact_person_email`, email, required): Enter the contact email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Person Address** (`emergency_contact_person_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): Describe any medical history or allergies you have.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): Describe any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact Person Address** (`emergency_contact_person_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact person. Please use the same address as entered for the emergency contact person.
* **Tattoo Consent** (`tattoo_consent`, text, required): Please confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the agreement.
* **Tattoo Artist** (`tattoo_artist`, text, required): Please select the tattoo artist who will be performing the tattoo.
* **Tattoo Studio** (`tattoo_studio`, text, required): Please select the tattoo studio where the session will take place.
* **Tattoo Date** (`tattoo_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the tattoo session.
* **Tattoo Time** (`tattoo_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the tattoo session.
* **Tattoo Session Length** (`tattoo_session_length`, number, required): Enter the expected length of the tattoo session.
* **Tattoo Session Price** (`tattoo_session_price`, text, required): Enter the price of the tattoo session.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method you will use.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the payment.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of the payment.
