# Tattoo Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers or clients who want to book a tattoo appointment or inquire about a specific tattoo design. It will be used by a business or service that offers tattoo services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information. You will need to provide your:
* First name
* Last name
* Email address
* Phone number (optional)
* Date and time you would like to book the appointment (optional)
* A message or note about your inquiry (optional)
* Select a date and time for your appointment if you have provided one (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional): If you provide a phone number, the tattoo artist will be able to contact you to confirm your appointment or answer any questions you may have.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your appointment, please select it from the available options. If no date is selected, the tattoo artist will contact you to arrange a time that suits them.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for your appointment, please select it from the available options. If no time is selected, the tattoo artist will contact you to arrange a time that suits them.
* **Message** (`Message`, note, optional): If you have any specific requests or questions for the tattoo artist, please include them in the message field.
* **Date** (`Date`, select_one, optional): This field is used to select a date and time for your appointment. Please choose one of the available options.
  - 08:00: This is the earliest available appointment time.
  - 600: This is a 10 minute time slot.
  - 720: This is a 12:20 minute time slot.
  - 840: This is a 14:40 minute time slot.
  - 960: This is a 16:00 minute time slot.
