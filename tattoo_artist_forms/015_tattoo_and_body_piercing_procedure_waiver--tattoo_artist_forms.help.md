# Tattoo And Body Piercing Procedure Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect client information and consent for a tattoo or body piercing procedure. It is essential that clients understand the risks and aftercare instructions associated with the procedure before signing this waiver.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your contact information (page 2) and any medical conditions that may impact your ability to receive a tattoo or piercing (page 3).
2. Indicate your current medication use and any allergies (page 4).
3. Specify if you have any previous tattoos or piercings (page 5).
4. Choose the type of tattoo or piercing you are interested in (page 6).
5. Provide emergency contact information for a person or persons you would like to be notified in case of an emergency (page 8).
6. Specify if you are aware of and have read the aftercare instructions (pages 10-11).
7. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and consent (pages 21-22).

## Tips

* Please answer all questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the Medical Conditions and Allergies fields.
