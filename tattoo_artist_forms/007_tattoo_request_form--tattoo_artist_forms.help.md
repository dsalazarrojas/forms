# Tattoo Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is to help you, our valued customer, to communicate with our tattoo artists and provide them with the necessary information to create a unique and personalized tattoo design for you.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
- Fill out each section completely, even if you're not sure about something.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to our staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Tattoo Type (`tattoo_type`)

*   **Tattoo Type**: Check if you want to get a real tattoo or not. If you're not sure, you can always consult with our staff for guidance.

### Tattoo Size (`tattoo_size`)

*   **Tattoo Size**: Please specify the size of the tattoo you're looking for in inches. Be as accurate as possible to help our artist create the perfect design.

### Tattoo Color (`tattoo_color`)

*   **Tattoo Color**: Describe the color you're interested in getting for your tattoo. You can choose a single color or multiple colors.

### Tattoo Design (`tattoo_design`)

*   **Tattoo Design**: Select all that apply for a design that suits your preference. Please be realistic in your expectations and let our artist guide you through the process.

### Tattoo Image (`tattoo_image`)

*   **Tattoo Image**: Please provide a photo of your desired tattoo image, or describe the idea you have in mind. If you don't know the name of the image, you can try searching for reference images online.

### Tattoo Message (`tattoo_message`)

*   **Tattoo Message**: If you have any specific message or quote you'd like to be included in your tattoo, please let us know here.

### Request Date (`tattoo_request_date`)

*   **Request Date**: Please select a date when you'd like to get your tattoo, if you're not ready yet, just select the current date.

### Request Time (`tattoo_request_time`)

*   **Request Time**: Choose a time when you'd like to get your tattoo, if you're not ready yet, just select the current time.

### Tattoo Artist (`tattoo_artist`)

*   **Tattoo Artist**: Check if you want to work with a specific artist or not. This will help us to assign the best artist for your needs.

## Tips
- Be honest about your expectations and preferences.
- Don't hesitate to ask for guidance or clarification if you're unsure about anything.
- Remember, this form is to help our artists create a unique and personalized design for you, so please provide as much information as possible.
