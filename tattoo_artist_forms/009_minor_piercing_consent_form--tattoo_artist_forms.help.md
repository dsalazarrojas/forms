# Minor Piercing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Piercing Consent Form is used to obtain consent from minors (individuals under 18 years of age) and their parents or legal guardians for a medical procedure, in this case, a piercing. This form is a legal requirement for tattoo or piercing establishments to document the consent of a minor before performing any body modification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name.
2.  Enter the client's date of birth.
3.  Enter the client's parent or legal guardian's name.
4.  Enter the client's address.
5.  Enter the client's contact number.
6.  Enter a description of the medical procedure, including any relevant details.
7.  If applicable, select 'Yes' under 'Consent Signed' and enter the date and time the consent was signed if required.
8.  Enter the date the consent was signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please enter the minor's name.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the minor's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the minor's parent or legal guardian.
* **Address**: Enter the minor's address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the minor's contact number.
* **Medical Procedure**: Describe the piercing or tattoo in detail, including any necessary medical details.
* **Consent Signed**: Select 'Yes' if the consent has been signed, otherwise, select 'No'. This field is optional.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date the consent was signed in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Signed**: Enter the time the consent was signed in the 12-hour format (e.g., 02:30 PM).

Note: This form is intended to be used by minors and their parents or legal guardians to provide consent for a piercing or tattooing medical procedure. The form is a legal requirement for any piercing or tattooing establishment.
