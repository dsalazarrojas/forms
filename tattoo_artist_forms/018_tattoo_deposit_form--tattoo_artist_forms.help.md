# Tattoo Deposit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tattoo Deposit Form is used to gather details from clients for their upcoming tattoo appointment. This includes their client information, deposit details, design options, appointment details, contact information, and payment information. This form helps the tattoo artist understand the client's preferences and requirements, ensuring a smooth and efficient tattoo process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Details** (Page 1):
	* Enter your name and contact information.
2. Provide **Deposit Details** (Page 1):
	* Enter the amount of the deposit and any relevant details about the deposit.
3. Select your **Design Options** (Page 2):
	* Choose one or more design options that appeal to you.
4. Enter **Appointment Details** (Page 3):
	* Provide any necessary details about your appointment.
5. Enter your **Contact Information** (Page 4):
	* Enter your email address and phone number.
6. Provide **Payment Information** (Page 5):
	* Enter any relevant payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details** (Page 1, text):
	+ This field is used to collect your name and contact information.
	+ Please enter your full name and a valid email address and phone number.
* **Deposit Details** (Page 1, text):
	+ This field is used to collect the amount of the deposit and any additional details about the deposit.
	+ Please enter the amount of the deposit and any other relevant information.
* **Design Options** (Page 2, select multiple):
	+ This field allows you to select one or more design options that appeal to you.
	+ Please choose one or more of the available design options.
* **Appointment Details** (Page 3, text):
	+ This field is used to provide any necessary details about your appointment.
	+ Please enter any relevant information about your appointment.
* **Contact Info** (Page 4, text):
	+ This field is used to collect your email address and phone number.
	+ Please enter your email address and phone number.
* **Payment Info** (Page 5, text):
	+ This field is used to collect any relevant payment details.
	+ Please enter any relevant payment information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your tattoo artist for clarification.
* If you need to make any changes, please contact your tattoo artist directly.
