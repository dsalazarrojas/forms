# responsive_wordpress_sidebar_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact information from visitors who want to reach out to the website owners. It is intended for website administrators or content editors to use and review the submitted information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and a message or comment. You can also choose to provide your name and website URL if you have one.
* If you want to upload multiple files, select the "Yes" option in the "Form Files" field.
* Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click the "Form Submit" button to submit your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Email** (`form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive a response from the website owners.
* **Form Subject** (`form_subject`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief subject line for your message.
* **Form Message** (`form_message`, text, required/optional): Enter your message or comment to the website owners.
* **Form Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number to enable the website owners to contact you.
* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name if you have one.
* **Form Website** (`form_website`, text, required/optional): Enter your website URL if you have one.
* **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you have for the website owners.
* **Form Files** (`form_files`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you want to upload multiple files with your message.
* **Form Submit** (`form_submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your contact information to the website owners.
