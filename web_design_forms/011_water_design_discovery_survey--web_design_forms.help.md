# Water Design Discovery Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your water design project. By providing accurate details, you will help us better understand your needs and ensure that your project is managed effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of project you are submitting (Residential, Commercial, or Public).
2. Choose the type of water feature you are interested in (Pond, Fountain, or Pool).
3. Enter your project's budget, which is a required field.
4. Select your project's priority (High, Medium, or Low).
5. Choose your priority as a designer (High, Medium, or Low).
6. Enter the size of the pool (if applicable).
7. Select the shape of the pool (if applicable).
8. Enter the water depth (if applicable).
9. Enter the name of the client.
10. List the stakeholders involved in the project.
11. Enter your contact email.
12. Enter your contact phone number.
13. Select the type of project (Residential, Commercial, or Public).
14. Select the status of the project (In Progress, Complete, or On Hold).
15. Select the project's priority (High, Medium, or Low).
16. Enter the project start date (if applicable).
17. Enter the project completion date (if applicable).
18. Enter any additional notes about the project.
19. Select the areas of budget allocation (if applicable).
20. Enter any designer notes (if applicable).
21. Enter any client comments (if applicable).
22. Enter any designer comments (if applicable).
23. Select the water quality (if applicable).
24. Sign the form to confirm.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Residential Project**: Select the type of project you are submitting (Residential, Commercial, or Public).
* **Project Budget**: Enter the total budget for your project.
* **Client Priority**: Select your project's priority (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Designer Priority**: Select your priority as a designer (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Water Feature**: Choose the type of water feature you are interested in (Pond, Fountain, or Pool).
* **Pool Size**: Enter the size of the pool (if applicable).
* **Pool Shape**: Select the shape of the pool (if applicable).
* **Water Depth**: Enter the water depth (if applicable).
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client.
* **Stakeholders**: List the stakeholders involved in the project.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Project Type**: Select the type of project (Residential, Commercial, or Public).
* **Project Status**: Select the status of the project (In Progress, Complete, or On Hold).
* **Project Priority**: Select the project's priority (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Project Start Date**: Enter the project start date (if applicable).
* **Project Completion Date**: Enter the project completion date (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the project.
* **Budget Allocation**: Select the areas of budget allocation (if applicable).
* **Designer Notes**: Enter any notes from the designer (if applicable).
* **Client Comments**: Enter any comments from the client (if applicable).
* **Designer Comments**: Enter any comments from the designer (if applicable).
* **Water Quality**: Select the water quality (if applicable).
* **Client Signature**: Sign the form to confirm.

Note: This guide is a general explanation of the form, and some fields may not be applicable to your project. If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for clarification.
