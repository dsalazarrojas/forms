# Scroll Website Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scroll Website Design Request Form is used to gather information for website design requests. It's a simple form that asks for the basic details of the request, such as the website URL, design requirements, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the website URL where you'd like to request the design service.
2. Describe your design requirements, including the style and color scheme you have in mind.
3. Choose the font styles and background image you prefer.
4. If you have any specific background image, you can provide the URL or upload it.
5. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you.
6. Select the tool or user assigned to work on your request.
7. Choose the due date and time for the completion of the design.
8. Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Design Request** (`user_input`, text, **required**): Enter a brief description of the design request.
* **website_url** (`website_url`, text, **required**): Enter the URL of the website you'd like to request the design service for.
* **Design Requirements** (`design_requirements`, text, **required**): Describe your design requirements, including the style and color scheme.
* **Style** (`design_style`, select_one, **required**): Choose the style for your website design.
* **Color Scheme** (`color_scheme`, select_multiple, **required**): Select multiple color schemes for your website design.
* **Font Styles** (`font_styles`, select_multiple, **required**): Choose multiple font styles for your website design.
* **Background Image** (`background_image`, text, **required**): Enter the URL or upload the background image for your website design.
* **E-Mail** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **required**): Select the tool assigned to work on your request.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_one, **required**): Choose the user assigned to work on your request.
* **Assigned Group** (`assigned_group`, select_one, **required**): Select the group assigned to work on your request.
* **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, **required**): Enter the due date for the completion of the design.
* **Due Time** (`due_time`, time, **required**): Enter the due time for the completion of the design.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, **required**): Click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Tips
Before submitting your request, make sure to fill in all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
