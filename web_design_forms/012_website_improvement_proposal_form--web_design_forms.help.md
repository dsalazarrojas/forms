# Website Improvement Proposal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Improvement Proposal Form is a tool to collect and track proposals for improving the website. It's a way for teams and individuals to submit their ideas for enhancing the website's functionality, user experience, and overall quality.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the `website_improvement_proposal` field with a brief description of your proposal. This should include any relevant details about the improvement, such as the problem you're trying to solve and the expected outcome.
2. In the `business_requirements` field, provide any specific business needs or requirements that your proposal addresses. This can include technical details, such as integration with existing systems, or user experience considerations, like accessibility standards.
3. If applicable, indicate whether the proposal is feasible (`Yes` or `No`) in the `proposed_solutions` field. This will help us gauge the feasibility of your idea and prioritize it accordingly.
4. In the `project_budget` field, provide an estimated budget for the proposed improvement, if applicable.
5. If your proposal requires a specific start and end date, enter them in the `project_timeline_start` and `project_timeline_end` fields, respectively.
6. If your proposal is related to a specific tool or system, enter the name of the tool in the `assigned_tool` field.
7. Finally, categorize your proposal in the `category` field, so we can group similar ideas together and track them effectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **website_improvement_proposal**: A brief description of your proposal, including any relevant details about the improvement.
* **business_requirements**: Any specific business needs or requirements that your proposal addresses.
* **project_timeline_start**: The start date for your proposed project timeline.
* **project_timeline_end**: The end date for your proposed project timeline.
* **proposed_solutions**: Indicate whether your proposal is feasible or not.
* **project_budget**: An estimated budget for the proposed improvement, if applicable.
* **project_timeline_start**: The start date for your proposed project timeline.
* **project_timeline_end**: The end date for your proposed project timeline.
* **assigned_tool**: The name of any specific tool or system related to your proposal.
* **category**: A category for your proposal, so we can group similar ideas together and track them effectively.
* **client_feedback**: Any additional feedback or comments from clients or stakeholders related to your proposal.
* **output_file**: A file or asset related to your proposal, if applicable.
* **output_file**: This field is currently not relevant to the form's purpose and can be safely ignored.
