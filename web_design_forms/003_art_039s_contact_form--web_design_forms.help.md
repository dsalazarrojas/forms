# Art's Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to send us a message regarding a specific project or service, and we'll get back to you with a personalized response.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form in the following steps:

1. Fill in your contact information:
	* Full name: Your complete name as you would like to be addressed.
	* Email address: We will use this to respond to your inquiry.
	* Phone number: Optional, but helps us get back to you quickly.
2. Choose your inquiry type:
	* Type of Inquiry: Select the category that best describes your question or project.
3. Provide a brief summary:
	* Subject: A short description of your inquiry.
	* Message or Description: A detailed explanation of your project or question.
4. Additional details (if applicable):
	* Project Name: If your inquiry is about a specific project, provide its name.
	* Project Type: If you're inquiring about a project, select the type it falls under.
	* Budget Range: Approximate budget for your project.
	* Timeline: When you need to complete the project.
5. Preferred contact method:
	* How would you prefer we contact you?: Choose your preferred communication method.
	* Best Time to Contact: When you are available to be contacted.
6. Confirm and agree:
	* I confirm this information is accurate: Please verify your details.
	* I agree to the privacy policy: Confirm you have read and agree to our privacy terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Your complete name as you would like to be addressed.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): We will use this to respond to your inquiry.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Optional, but helps us get back to you quickly.
- **Type of Inquiry** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, required): Select the category that best describes your question or project.
- **Subject** (`subject`, text, required): A short description of your inquiry.
- **Message or Description** (`message`, text, required): A detailed explanation of your project or question.
- **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): If your inquiry is about a specific project, provide its name.
- **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, optional): If you're inquiring about a project, select the type it falls under.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred communication method.
- **Best Time to Contact** (`best_time_to_contact`, select_one, optional): When you are available to be contacted.
- **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Approximate budget for your project.
- **Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, optional): When you need to complete the project.
- **How did you find us?** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Where did you hear about our services?
- **I would like to receive news and updates** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, optional): Optional - we will not share your information.
- **Do you have files to attach?** (`attachment_notes`, text, optional): You can provide file details here if needed.
- **I confirm this information is accurate** (`confirmation_checkbox`, select_one, required): Please verify your details are correct.
- **I agree to the privacy policy** (`privacy_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm you have read and agree to our privacy terms.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. This form is not intended for general inquiries or spam, so please refrain from using it for those purposes. We'll get back to you with a personalized response once we receive your form submission.
