# Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user-generated content, specifically for websites that offer submission features. It allows users to submit information about their websites, including a brief description and the type of advertising used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Submit Your Website" field with a valid website URL or title.
2. Enter a brief description of your website in the "Website" field.
3. Provide more information about your website in the "Description" field.
4. Select the type of advertising used for your website from the "Advertising Type" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Your Website**: Enter a valid website URL or title. This field is used to identify your website.
* **Website**: Enter a brief description of your website. This field should be a concise summary of your website.
* **Description**: Provide more information about your website. This field is optional, but can be useful for providing additional context.
* **Advertising Type**: Select the type of advertising used for your website from the options provided. This field helps us understand the advertising strategies used for your website.
