# website_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website_order_form is designed to collect information about a website's order, specifically for businesses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the domain of the website you are ordering.
2. Provide the name of your business.
3. Select the type of business you have (if applicable).
4. Enter the name of the website (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **domain** (`text`, required: false): Enter the domain of the website you are ordering. This is the address of the website, such as example.com or example.net.
* **business_name** (`text`, required: false): Provide the name of your business. This will help us understand who you are and what services you offer.
* **business_type** (`text`, required: false): Select the type of business you have (if applicable). This can be an office, store, or other types of businesses.
* **website_name** (`text`, required: false): Enter the name of the website (if you are providing this information). This will help us understand the website's identity.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the domain and website name you enter are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please review this guide carefully or contact us for assistance.
* You can use this form to order a website for yourself or on behalf of someone else. If so, please ensure that you have the necessary permissions or authority to complete the form.
