# Domain Leasing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Domain Leasing Application Form is a crucial step in the domain leasing process. It allows you to provide detailed information about your domain leasing needs and preferences. By filling out this form accurately, you'll be helping the leasing team to understand your requirements better and process your application efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully:

1. Enter your domain name in the "Domain Name" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Lessee Name" and "Lessee Contact Info" fields.
3. Specify the lease duration in the "Lease Duration" field.
4. Enter the monthly fee in the "Monthly Fee" field.
5. Describe your payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
6. Select the domains that are available for leasing in the "Domains Available" field.
7. Include a liability clause in the "Liability Clause" field.
8. Choose whether you require website maintenance in the "Website Maintenance" field.
9. Sign and date the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Domain Name** (`domain_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the domain you wish to lease.
* **Lessee Name** (`lessee_name`, `text`, required: false): Provide your name as the lessee.
* **Lessee Contact Info** (`lessee_contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information for easy communication.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, `number`, required: false): Specify the duration of the lease in a numerical value (e.g., 1 month, 3 months).
* **Monthly Fee** (`monthly_fee`, `number`, required: false): Enter the monthly fee for the leased domain.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required: false): Describe how you plan to pay for the lease (e.g., bank transfer, credit card).
* **Domains Available** (`domains_available`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the domains that are available for leasing from the dropdown options.
* **Liability Clause** (`liability_clause`, `text`, required: false): Include a clause that explains your liability in case of any issues with the leased domain.
* **Website Maintenance** (`website_maintenance`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you require website maintenance (True or False).
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: false): Sign and date the form to confirm your application.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues during the leasing process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us process your domain leasing application efficiently.
