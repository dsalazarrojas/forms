# Site Recce - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your visit to a website. It will help us understand your experience and gather essential details about the site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the URL of the website you visited in the `site_url` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `user_email` field. This is a required field for us to contact you if we need more information.
3. Select the date you visited the site in the `site_recce_date` field.
4. Select the time you visited the site in the `site_recce_time` field.
5. Add any additional notes or observations about your visit in the `site_recce_notes` field.
6. If you have any comments or feedback, please share them in the `site_recce_comments` field.
7. Finally, if you'd like to add a note for internal reference, enter it in the `user_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **site_recce_form**: A text field where you can enter any additional information.
* **site_url**: <label> Enter the URL of the website you visited.
* **user_email**: <label> Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. (Required)
* **site_recce_date**: <label> Select the date you visited the site. (Required)
* **site_recce_time**: <label> Select the time you visited the site. (Required)
* **site_recce_notes**: <label> Add any notes or observations about your visit.
* **user_notes**: <label> Enter any notes for internal reference.
* **site_recce_comments**: <label> Share any comments or feedback about your visit.
