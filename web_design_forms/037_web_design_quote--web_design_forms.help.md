# web_design_quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
The web_design_quote form is designed to request a quote for web design services from clients. It ensures that clients provide essential information to help web designers understand their project requirements accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter a brief title for your project in the "Title" field.
2. Add a description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Select the services you require from the "Web Design Services" field (required).
4. Enter your preferred start and end dates for the project in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields (optional).
5. Enter the total hours you expect to spend on the project in the "Total Hours" field (optional).
6. Enter your hourly rate in the "Hourly Rate" field (optional).
7. Select the project's status from the "Project Status" field (required).
8. Enter your client's information, including address, state, and country (optional).
9. Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`title`, text, required): A brief title for your project.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Your name or business name.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): A brief description of your project.
* **Web Design Services** (`web_design_services`, select_multiple, required): Select all services you want included.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): When you want to start your project.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): When you want to end your project.
* **Total Hours** (`total_hours`, number, optional): Total hours you expect to spend on the project.
* **Hourly Rate** (`hourly_rate`, number, optional): Your hourly rate for the project.
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): The total cost of your project.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Payment terms for your project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Select project status (Not Started, In Progress, Done).
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Your client's address.
* **Client State** (`client_state`, text, optional): Your client's state.
* **Client Country** (`client_country`, text, optional): Your client's country.
* **Designer Name** (`designer_name`, text, optional): Your name or business name.
* **Designer Email** (`designer_email`, email, optional): Your email address.
* **Designer Phone** (`designer_phone`, text, optional): Your phone number.

This form ensures that you can provide essential information to web designers and receive accurate quotes for your project. If you have any questions or need help, please refer to this guide.
