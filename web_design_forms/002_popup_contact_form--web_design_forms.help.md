# Popup Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Popup Contact Form is a straightforward form meant to collect users' contact information, allowing them to quickly leave a message or request for support.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To submit a message, simply fill out the form fields below.
2. Provide your name in the "Name" field. This field is not mandatory, but it's helpful to have your name on record.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us get back to you, in case we need more information or have a question regarding your query.
4. In the "Message" field, type your message or description of your request. Don't worry about formatting or length; we'll take care of it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text): Enter your name. This field is not required, but it's a good idea to provide it.
* **Email** (email): Provide your valid email address. We'll use this to contact you back.
* **Message** (text): Type your message or description. Don't worry about length or formatting; just type as much as you need to.
* **Contact Form** (text): This is the main field where you submit your message. It's not a drop-down or selection field; just type your message.

## Tips
* Please keep your messages clear and concise for our team to quickly understand your request.
* If you need to attach files or have a large amount of text, consider using other methods (like email or our support chat) to communicate with us.
* Our team will review and respond to your messages as soon as possible. If we need more information, we'll contact you at your provided email.
