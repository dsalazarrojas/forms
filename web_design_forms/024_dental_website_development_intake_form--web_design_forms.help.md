# Dental Website Development Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information from clients about their dental website development project, which will help the development team understand their requirements and preferences, ensuring a smooth and successful project execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Enter your contact information, including phone number and email address.
3. Provide details about the project, including project requirements and expectations.
4. Specify the project schedule and budget.
5. Add any additional project notes.
6. Choose the project status and type.
7. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Info** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and other relevant client information.
* **Project Requirements** (`project_requirements`, `text`, required: false): Describe your project requirements and expectations.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information.
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide detailed information about the project.
* **Dental Info** (`dental_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter dental-related details (if applicable).
* **Website Preferences** (`website_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Describe your website preferences (if applicable).
* **Client Expectations** (`client_expectations`, `text`, required: false): Describe your client expectations.
* **Project Schedule** (`project_schedule`, `number`, required: false): Enter project schedule information (e.g., start date, end date).
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter project budget information.
* **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional project notes.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your contact email address.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required: false): Specify which tool(s) should be used for the project.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the project status (Active or Inactive).
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose which project type (Yes or No).
