# website_design_quotes - Help Guide
## Purpose
The website_design_quotes form is used to collect and manage quote information for web design projects. It helps in organizing essential details related to client quotes, ensuring a standardized and efficient process for both clients and internal teams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter all the required information, marked as "required" in the form fields.
2. Fill in the quote details, including client information.
3. Select the quote date and time as per the provided format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Choose the services offered by selecting the relevant options.
5. Rate the quality of your quote.
6. Enter the quote amount.
7. Add any additional notes for context or future reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Quote Details (text)** (`quote_details`, text, optional): Enter the specific details related to the quote, such as the project's specifics or unique requirements.
* **Client (text)** (`client`, text, optional): This field is intended for capturing information about the client, including their name, contact information, or any other relevant data.
* **Quote Date (date)** (`quote_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the quote in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Quote Time (time)** (`quote_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the quote.
* **Services (select_multiple)** (`services`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the services that apply to the quote.
* **Rate (select_one)** (`rate`, select_one, optional): Rate the quality of your quote by selecting between 'Good' or 'Fair'.
* **Quote (number)** (`quote`, number, optional): Enter the exact quote amount.
* **Notes (text)** (`notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments relevant to the quote.

Please ensure to fill out this form accurately and completely for efficient and effective management of the quote information.
