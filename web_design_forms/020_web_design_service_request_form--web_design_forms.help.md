# Web Design Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential clients about their web design service requests. It will help us understand your project needs and provide the best possible service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your first name and email address in the first field.
2. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the second field.
3. Select the type of project you are interested in (Web Design, Graphic Design, or UX/UI Design) in the third field.
4. In the fourth field, provide a brief description of your project, including the services you need and any specific requirements.
5. If applicable, select your budget range for the project in the fifth field.
6. If you have any custom requests or additional information, please provide them in the sixth field.
7. If you'd like to receive our newsletter, select 'Signup' in the seventh field.
8. If you'd like to receive emails about your project, enter your email address in the eighth field (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Please enter your full first name as it appears on your official ID or business name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can contact you for further discussion.
* **Project Type** (`Project Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of project you are interested in: Web Design, Graphic Design, or UX/UI Design.
* **Project Description** (`Project Description`, text, required): Briefly describe your project, including the services you need and any specific requirements.
* **Budget Range** (`Budget Range`, select_multiple, optional): Select your budget range for the project (Less than $1000, Less than $5000, Less than $10000, or More than $10000).
* **Custom Requests** (`Custom Requests`, text, optional): If you have any custom requests or additional information, please provide them here.
* **Newsletter** (`Newsletter`, select_one, optional): If you'd like to receive our newsletter, select 'Signup'.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, optional): If you'd like to receive emails about your project, enter your email address here.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as it will help us better understand your needs and provide the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you to discuss and refine project scope and details after receiving this form.
