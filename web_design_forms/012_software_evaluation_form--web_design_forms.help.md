# Software Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Evaluation Form is a tool designed to collect user feedback on the performance and usability of software products. It provides an overall rating and allows users to comment on various aspects of the software.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through all the questions carefully and make sure you have understood what is being asked.
* Select the options that best fit your experience with the software.
* For text-based questions, type in your comments and thoughts clearly.
* Make sure to answer all required fields (those marked as "required").

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (`Overall Rating`, `select_one`, required/optional): This is the first question, where you will rate the software overall. Please choose from the options: Excellent, Good, or Bad.
* **Usability** (`usability`, `number`, optional): This question asks for your rating of the software's usability, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Software Evaluation Form 2** (`Software Evaluation Form 2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all options that apply to your experience with the software, from Very Good, Good, to Average.
* **Software Features** (`Software Features`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments about the software's features.
* **Software Usability** (`Software Usability`, `number`, optional): This question asks for your rating of the software's usability, on a scale of 1-10.
* **Software Support** (`Software Support`, `time`, optional): Estimate the time it took for you to get help when you needed it.
* **Software Evaluation Form 3** (`Software Evaluation Form 3`, `note`, optional): This is a free-form text field for any additional comments about your experience with the software.
* **Software Ease of Use** (`Software Ease of Use`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the options: Easy, Medium, or Difficult, to rate how easy or hard it was to use the software.

## Tips
* Answer each question honestly and to the best of your ability.
* Be as specific as possible in your answers, providing concrete examples or details.
* If you are unsure about any question, ask for clarification or leave a comment in the "Software Evaluation Form 3" field.
