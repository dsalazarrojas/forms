# Blogger Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential bloggers who want to sign up for our blogging platform. The goal is to gather necessary details about the blogger, evaluate their suitability, and communicate with them regarding their application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Choose your preferred content niche, blogging experience, and publishing frequency.
3. Provide your portfolio URL, if applicable, and indicate the number of writing samples you have.
4. Rate your writing skills and indicate whether you have social media presence.
5. Enter your approximate social media followers, if applicable.
6. Tell us why you want to blog with us.
7. Choose a start date for your blogging journey.
8. Agree to our terms and conditions.
9. Finally, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Become a Blogger With Us --** (`signup_intro`): This is an introductory note explaining the purpose of this form.
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name. (Required)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name. (Required)
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your email address. (Required)
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number (optional)
* **Preferred Content Niche** (`content_niche`): Choose a category that interests you the most. Options are: Lifestyle, Fashion, Travel, Food, Tech, Business, Health, Parenting, Beauty, Mixed.
* **Blogging Experience** (`blogging_experience`): Choose your level of blogging experience. Options are: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional.
* **Portfolio or Blog URL** (`portfolio_url`): Enter a link to your portfolio or blog, if you have one. (Optional)
* **Number of Writing Samples Available** (`writing_samples`): Enter the number of writing samples you have. (Optional)
* **How frequently can you publish?** (`publishing_frequency`): Choose how frequently you can publish content. Options are: Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed, Multiple times per week.
* **Specific topics you want to cover** (`content_topics`): Enter topics you are interested in writing about. (Optional)
* **Rate your writing skills** (`writing_skills`): Choose how good you rate your writing skills. Options are: Good, Very good, Excellent.
* **Do you have social media presence?** (`social_media_presence`): Choose whether you have a social media presence. Options are: Yes, No.
* **Approximate social media followers** (`social_followers`): Enter the approximate number of followers on your social media. (Optional)
* **Why do you want to blog with us?** (`motivation`): Tell us why you are interested in blogging with us.
* **When can you start?** (`availability_start`): Enter the date when you can start blogging.
* **I agree to the blogger terms and conditions** (`terms_accepted`): Choose whether you agree to our terms and conditions. Options are: I agree, I do not agree.
* **Signup Date** (`signup_date`): This date will be automatically filled by our system.
