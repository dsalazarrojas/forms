# Web Design Client Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Web Design Client Questionnaire Form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their web design needs, preferences, and requirements. This form helps web design agencies understand the clients' expectations and create a tailored design plan.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure you've filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **client_terms** (1, text, optional):
	+ This field is for you to describe your terms and conditions for the project.
	+ Please provide any specific requirements or expectations you have regarding your website.
* **design_details** (2, text, optional):
	+ Describe the design details you have in mind for your website, including any specific design preferences.
* **design_budget** (3, number, required):
	+ Specify your budget for the website design project. This will help us understand your financial constraints and allocate resources effectively.
* **client_info** (4, text, optional):
	+ Share any additional information about yourself or your organization that may be helpful for us to know.
* **terms** (5, text, required):
	+ Confirm that you've read and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **website_type** (6, select_one, required):
	+ Choose the type of website you need: (Yes/No)
	+ This will help us understand the type of website you require.
* **website_size** (7, select_multiple, required):
	+ Select all that apply:
		+ Yes
		+ No
	+ This will help us gauge the size and complexity of your website.
* **color_scheme** (8, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply:
		+ Yes
		+ No
	+ This will help us understand your color preferences for your website.
* **fonts_used** (9, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select all that apply:
		+ Yes
		+ No
	+ This will help us understand your font preferences for your website.
* **design_style** (10, text, optional):
	+ Describe the design style you envision for your website.
* **content** (11, text, required):
	+ Provide a detailed description of the content you plan to include on your website.
* **submit** (12, text, required):
	+ Confirm your submission of this questionnaire.
