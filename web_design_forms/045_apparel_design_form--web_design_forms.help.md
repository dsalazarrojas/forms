# Apparel Design Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Apparel Design Form is a tool to gather information for custom apparel printing. It helps clients provide essential details for their design orders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Client Information**: Enter your name and contact email in the respective fields. These details will be used for design approval and correspondence.
2. Select the type of apparel you want to design from the options provided.
3. Provide the breakdown of sizes and quantities required for your order.
4. If you have a preferred color palette or Pantone codes, enter them in the text field.
5. Share the links or filenames of your artwork files (logos, etc.) for printing.
6. Choose the preferred print method from the available options (Screen printing, Direct to garment, Embroidery, Heat transfer).
7. If you have any additional instructions or special handling requirements, provide them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client name**: Your name, which will be used for design approval and correspondence.
* **Contact email**: Your email address, used for proofs and communication about your order.
* **Apparel type**: Select the type of apparel you want to design (T shirt, Hoodie, Jacket, Cap).
* **Sizes and quantities**: Provide the size breakdown and quantity counts for your order.
* **Color palette or pantone codes**: Enter your preferred color palette or pantone codes for printing.
* **Artwork files**: Share links or filenames of your artwork files (logos, etc.) for printing.
* **Preferred print method**: Choose the print method you want to use (Screen printing, Direct to garment, Embroidery, Heat transfer).
* **Additional instructions**: If you have any special handling requirements or additional instructions for production, provide them here.
