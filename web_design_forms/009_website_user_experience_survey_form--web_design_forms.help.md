# Website User Experience Survey Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your feedback on the website's user experience. Your input will help us identify areas where the website can be improved to better serve its users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form completely.
*   Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
*   Ensure your email address is correct for any follow-up questions or responses.
*   If any question doesn't apply to you, just move on to the next one.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Website User Experience Survey Form** (`title`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is displayed above the form fields.
*   **First Question** (`first_question`, text, optional): This question is an open-ended prompt asking about your first experience on the website. Feel free to provide as much detail as you like.
*   **Second Question** (`second_question`, select_one, optional): This question is a multiple-choice question asking you to confirm whether your second experience with the website was positive or not.
*   **Third Question** (`third_question`, text, optional): Similar to the first question, this one is an open-ended prompt asking about your third experience with the website.
*   **Last Question** (`last_question`, select_multiple, optional): This is a multiple-choice question asking you to confirm whether you've had positive or negative experiences with the website's last interaction.
*   **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is for any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide that didn't fit in the previous questions.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you for any follow-up questions or to keep you informed of any updates we make based on your feedback.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number, in case we'd like to reach you directly for further feedback or discussion.
