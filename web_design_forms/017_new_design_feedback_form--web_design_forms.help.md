# New Design Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from clients on the design of a project or product.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the first field. This is the name of the client who is providing feedback.
2. In the **Feedback Question** field, describe the specific design aspect that you would like to provide feedback on.
3. In the **Feedback** field, type in your thoughts, concerns, or ideas regarding the design.
4. In the **Suggestions** field, provide any suggestions you may have for improving the design.
5. Choose your answer for **Category** to indicate whether or not the design was well-received by the client.
6. If applicable, select a **Sub Category** to provide more context to your feedback.
7. Finally, enter the name of the **Assigned Tool** if it's relevant to your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client who is providing feedback.
* **Feedback Question**: Describe the specific design aspect that you would like to provide feedback on.
* **Feedback**: Type in your thoughts, concerns, or ideas regarding the design.
* **Suggestions**: Provide any suggestions you may have for improving the design.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool or software used in the design, if relevant.
* **Category**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not the design was well-received by the client.
* **Sub Category**: Provide more context to your feedback by selecting a sub category, if applicable.
