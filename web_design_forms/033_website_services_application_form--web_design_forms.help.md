# Website Services Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Services Application Form is a tool used to gather essential information from applicants for our services. It's used to collect general information, contact details, billing address, company details, payment method, and other relevant data required for the service application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the general information section, providing a brief description of your needs and services.
2.  In the contact details section, enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
3.  Next, enter your billing address, including your street address, city, state, zip code, and any other relevant billing information.
4.  Move on to the company details section, where you can enter any additional information about your company or the service you're applying for.
5.  In the payment method section, select one of the available options: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check.
6.  Read through and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the "True" box in the Terms and Conditions section.
7.  Finally, confirm your information by selecting "True" or "False" from the Confirm section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_information`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your needs and services.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Enter your name, email address, and phone number so that we can get in touch with you.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, not required): Enter your billing address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Company Details** (`company_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional information about your company or the service you're applying for.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, not required): Select one of the available payment options: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Check.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, not required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions by checking the "True" box.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_multiple, not required): Confirm your information by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Date** (`date`, date, not required): Enter the date (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
