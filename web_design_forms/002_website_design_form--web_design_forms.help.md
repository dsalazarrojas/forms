# Website Design Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Website Design Form" is designed to collect information from customers interested in getting a website designed. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to provide the required details about your website.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the form_name field with a unique name for your website.
2. Provide a detailed description of your project in the project description field.
3. Choose your preferred payment method for the project (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Wire Transfer).
4. Select the type of website you need (Static, Dynamic, or E-commerce).
5. Enter the domain name for your website in the website domain field.
6. Enter the URL for your website in the website url field.
7. Provide your name in the customer name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_name**: A unique name for your website. This will help us to differentiate your website from others.
* **project description**: A detailed description of your project will help us to understand your requirements better.
* **payment method**: Choose how you want to pay for the project (Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or Wire Transfer). Please note that this field is not used for actual payment processing.
* **payment date**: The date you plan to make the payment. This field is not currently used.
* **payment amount**: The amount you plan to pay for the project. This field is not currently used.
* **website type**: Select the type of website you need (Static, Dynamic, or E-commerce).
* **website domain**: The domain name for your website. This is the address where your website will be accessible.
* **website url**: The URL for your website. This will be the address that will be displayed in the address bar when users visit your website.
* **customer name**: Your name and contact information. This will help us to keep in touch with you and update you about your website status.
