# Masselgfx Order - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information related to Masselgfx orders, including order type, product details, customer contact, and shipping information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the necessary information for the "Order Information" section.
2. Enter details about the product(s) being ordered in the "Product Information" section.
3. Fill in the customer's contact information in the "Customer Information" section.
4. Add any shipping details in the "Shipping Information" section.
5. If required, include payment information in the "Payment Information" section.
6. Finally, add any additional order notes in the "Order Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Order Information** (order_info, text, optional): This section is for general order details.
	+ You can provide any additional information about the order here, such as the order type or any specific requirements.
* **Product Information** (product_info, text, optional): Enter details about the product(s) being ordered, such as product name, quantity, or description.
* **Customer Information** (customer_info, text, optional): Fill in the customer's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Shipping Information** (shipping_info, text, optional): Provide shipping details, including address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Payment Information** (payment_info, text, optional): If required, enter payment information, including payment method and amount.
* **Order Notes** (order_notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the order.
