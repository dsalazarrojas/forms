# Ecommerce Domain Name Suggestion Request - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to request the suggestion of a domain name for an ecommerce project. It gathers information to identify the type of ecommerce project and help us generate a suitable domain name suggestion based on that information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information about your ecommerce project. You can skip any fields that do not apply to your project.
2. Choose the category that best describes your project (Ecommerce, Online Store, or Marketplace).
3. Select the type of store (B2C, B2B, Both, or None).
4. Choose the business model (Subscription, Advertising, Affiliate, Both, or Other).
5. Provide a short description of your project.
6. Enter your contact email and store information (phone, address, website, etc.).
7. Fill in the submitter's information (name, email, phone, etc.).
8. Finally, enter any additional notes or comments about your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Ecommerce Domain Name Suggestion Request** (<title>, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Choose the category that best describes your project. You can select from Ecommerce, Online Store, Marketplace, or Other.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of store you are planning. You can select from B2C, B2B, Both, or None.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): This field is intended to describe the people you expect to visit your store.
* **Business Model** (`business_model`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more business models that apply to your project. You can choose from Subscription, Advertising, Affiliate, Both, or Other.
* **Store Description** (`store_description`, note, optional): Write a short description of your store.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the store.
* **Store Website** (`store_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the store (if any).
* **Store Phone** (`store_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the store (if any).
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the store (if any).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the request.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization submitting the request.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization submitting the request.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization or company submitting the request.
* **Submitter Job Title** (`submitter_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person submitting the request.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the submission.
* **Submitter Agency** (`submitter_agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of the agency or organization responsible for the submission.
* **Agency Contact Person** (`submitter_agency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the agency or organization.
* **Agency Address** (`submitter_agency_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the agency or organization (if any).
* **Agency Phone** (`submitter_agency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the agency or organization (if any).
* **Agency Email** (`submitter_agency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the agency or organization.
* **Agency Website** (`submitter_agency_website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the agency or organization (if any).
* **Agency Job Title** (`submitter_agency_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the agency or organization contact person.
* **Agency Notes** (`submitter_agency_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the agency or organization submission.
* **Agency Agreement** (`submitter_agency_agreement`, text, optional): Enter the agreement or terms between the submitter and the agency or organization.
