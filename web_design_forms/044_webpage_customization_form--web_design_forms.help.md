# Webpage Customization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webpage Customization Form is used to collect information for customizing a webpage to fit the needs of a business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Select the correct options for the color scheme, font, and other customizations.
3. Ensure that you are authorized to access the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, **Text**): Enter the name of the business or organization.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, **Select One**): Choose the type of business or organization (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Business Url** (`business_url`, **Text**): Enter the URL of the business or organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, **Text**): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, **Email**): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, **Text**): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, **Text**): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Webpage Color Scheme** (`webpage_color_scheme`, **Select One**): Choose the color scheme for the webpage (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Webpage Font** (`webpage_font`, **Select One**): Choose the font for the webpage (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Content** (`content`, **Text**): Enter the content for the webpage.
* **Background Image** (`background_image`, **Text**): Upload or enter the URL of the background image.
* **Background Color** (`background_color`, **Text**): Enter the background color of the webpage.
* **Header Background Image** (`header_background_image`, **Text**): Upload or enter the URL of the header background image.
* **Header Background Color** (`header_report_background_color`, **Text**): Enter the background color of the header.
* **Footer Background Image** (`footer_background_image`, **Text**): Upload or enter the URL of the footer background image.
* **Footer Color** (`footer_color`, **Text**): Enter the background color of the footer.
* **Navigation Bar Color** (`navigation_bar_color`, **Text**): Enter the background color of the navigation bar.
* **Navigation Bar Background Color** (`navigation_bar_background_color`, **Text**): Enter the background color of the navigation bar.
* **Button Background Color** (`button_background_color`, **Text**): Enter the background color of buttons.
* **Button Color** (`button_color`, **Text**): Enter the color of buttons.
* **Text Color** (`text_color`, **Text**): Enter the text color of the webpage.
* **Background Gradient** (`background_gradient`, **Text**): Enter the background gradient of the webpage.
* **Header Background Repeat** (`header_background_repeat`, **Select One**): Choose whether the header background image should be repeated (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Background Repositioned** (`background_repositioned`, **Select One**): Choose whether the background image should be repositioned (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Save Changes** (`save_changes`, **Select One**): Choose whether to save changes (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Save To** (`save_to`, **Select Multiple**): Choose where to save changes (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
* **Save As** (`save_as`, **Select Multiple**): Choose what to save changes as (e.g., 'Yes', 'No').
