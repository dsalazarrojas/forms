# Website Interface Feedback Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback from users about your website's interface. This form helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement. Please fill in the form below to provide your honest feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select a category for your feedback from the options provided.
2. Enter your message describing the issue you experienced or suggestion you have.
3. If you have more to add, enter additional feedback in the text area below.
4. Enter your email address so we can contact you (if needed).
5. Enter your phone number (optional).
6. Select your browser version and operating system to help us understand the context of your feedback.
7. Provide a rating for your feedback (optional).
8. Finally, submit your feedback by clicking the "Submit Feedback" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category (Select One)** (`feedback_category`, select_one, required): Choose a category for your feedback from the options provided. This will help us understand the context of your feedback.
* **Feedback Message** (`feedback_message`, text, required): Enter a detailed description of the issue you experienced or suggestion you have.
* **Additional Feedback** (`feedback_message_additional`, text, optional): If you have more to add, enter additional feedback in the text area below.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you (if needed) to discuss your feedback.
* **User Phone** (`user_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you would like us to call you for further questions).
* **Browser Version** (`browser_version`, text, optional): Select the version of your browser from the options provided.
* **Operating System** (`operating_system`, select_one, required): Choose your operating system from the options provided.
* **Feedback Rating** (`feedback_rating`, number, optional): Provide a rating for your feedback (1-5 stars).
* **Submit Feedback** (`submit_feedback`, text, required): Click this button to submit your feedback to us.
