## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user information section with the relevant details.
2. Provide website information, such as the website URL and any other relevant details.
3. Specify the maintenance plan, including the frequency of maintenance and any other relevant details.
4. Review and confirm the contract terms.
5. Select the renewal plan for the website.
6. Enter payment details, including the amount and any other relevant financial information.
7. Add any additional payment details as necessary.
8. Review and sign the agreement, confirming the terms and conditions.
9. Sign and date the agreement, indicating your acceptance of the terms.
