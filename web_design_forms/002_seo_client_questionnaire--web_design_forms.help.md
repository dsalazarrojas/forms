# seo_client_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "seo_client_questionnaire" form is designed to collect crucial information from clients about their SEO needs, preferences, and requirements. This information helps in creating an effective SEO strategy that aligns with the client's goals and objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Please fill in the **General Information** section, providing basic details about yourself and your organization.
2.  In the **Website Information** section, provide information about your website, including its URL and any other relevant details.
3.  For **Target Audience**, select whether your website is intended for the general public or a specific group of people.
4.  In the **Design Preferences** section, describe your preferred design style and any specific requirements you may have.
5.  Choose the **SEO Services** you are interested in from the list of options provided.
6.  The **Content Strategy** section is where you can provide detailed information about your content goals and requirements.
7.  For **Analytics**, provide the desired timeframe for the project.
8.  Enter your estimated **Budget** for the project.
9.  Choose the **Timeline** for the project completion.
10. In the **Contact Person** section, provide your email address so we can get in touch with you.
11. Enter your **Email** address to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
12. In the **Phone** section, provide your phone number so we can reach you.
13. The **Notes** section is optional, but it's a good place to add any additional comments or information that might be helpful.
14. If you have any **Additional Info**, please provide it here.
15. In the **Website** section, choose whether your website is already live or not.
16. If you have any **Design Files**, please attach them here.
17. For **Other Services**, choose from the list whether you are interested in any additional services.
18. The **Client Notes** section is where you can add any notes that you would like to share with the team.
19. The **Project Notes** section is also optional, but it's a good place to leave any project-specific notes.
20. In the **Next Steps**, describe the actions you would like the project team to take next.
21. The **Internal Notes** section is for our internal team use only.
22. Lastly, choose the **Follow Up** frequency you would like us to follow up with you.
