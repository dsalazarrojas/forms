# web_developer_programmer_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The web_developer_programmer_application_form is a job application form designed for web developers to apply for a job position. It collects information about the applicant's name, email, job title, phone number, experience, skills, and projects.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your experience in years in the "Experience" field. This field accepts numerical values only.
6. Select your relevant skills from the "Skills" field. Choose all that apply.
7. Select your relevant projects from the "Projects" field. Choose all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as you would like it to be presented on the job application.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number. Ensure that you have the permission to share your phone number.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required/optional): Enter your experience in years as a web developer. This field accepts numerical values only.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant skills you have as a web developer, including programming languages, frameworks, databases, and certifications.
* **Projects** (`projects`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all relevant projects you have worked on, including apps, websites, and Github profiles.
