# website_estimate_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for estimating the cost of a website project. Please fill it out with your project details to receive an accurate estimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information, including your **First Name**.
2. Provide your **Email** address so we can get back to you with the estimate.
3. Enter your **Phone** number for any follow-up questions.
4. Enter the **Estimated Cost** of your project.
5. Describe the **Description of Services** you would like to have included in the estimate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Please provide your name as you would like us to address you.
- **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get back to you with the estimate.
- **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Your phone number for any follow-up questions.
- **Estimated Cost** (`number`, required: false): Enter the estimated cost of your project.
- **Description of Services** (`text`, required: false): Describe the services you would like to have included in the estimate.
