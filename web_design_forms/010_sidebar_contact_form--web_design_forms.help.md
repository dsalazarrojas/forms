# Sidebar Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sidebar Contact Form is a contact form designed to collect basic contact information from users, allowing them to reach out with questions, comments, or concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the form to begin filling it out.
2. Enter your First name in the First name field.
3. Enter your Last name in the Last name field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address in this format: example@example.com. This field is for communication purposes, and your email will be used to respond to your query.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure your message is sent successfully.
* Double-check your email address for any typos or errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through other means.
* The form is a simple contact form and does not require registration or any personal data.
