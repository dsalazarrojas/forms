# Website Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a website's design requirements and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all the required fields, marked with <font color="#666"> *</font>.
- Provide detailed and accurate information for each field.
- Make sure to save the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Form Data** (1) (text, optional): A brief description of what the website is about.
- **Business Information** (2) (text, optional): Provide relevant information about your business, such as company name, industry, or target audience.
- **Expectations** (3) (text, optional): Share your expectations for the website design, such as functionality, features, or specific design elements.
- **Perceptions** (4) (text, optional): Describe how you perceive the website's purpose and goals.
- **Additional Services** (5) (select multiple, optional): Check the services you are interested in, such as hosting, maintenance, or development.
  - Select one or more of the options: Yes, No
- **Comments** (6) (text, optional): Share any additional comments or notes about the project.
- **Email** (7) (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
- **Phone** (8) (text, optional): Provide your phone number for further communication or clarification.
- **Comments** (9) (text, optional): Add any other comments or notes about the project.
