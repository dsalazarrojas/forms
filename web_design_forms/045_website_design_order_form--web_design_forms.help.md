# Website Design Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a new website design project, including project specifics, budget, estimated turnaround time, theme, and contact email.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the project details in the "Project Details" field.
2. Provide your budget for the project.
3. Specify the estimated turnaround time in days or hours.
4. Choose a theme for the design (Modern, Minimalist, or Responsive).
5. Enter a valid email address for communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, optional):
This field is for providing any additional information about the project, such as its description, goals, or any specific requirements.
* **Budget** (`budget`, text, optional):
Enter the budget for the project, including any specific requirements or constraints you have for the budget.
* **Estimated Turnaround Time** (`estimated_turnaround_time`, number, optional):
Specify the estimated turnaround time for the project in days or hours, considering the complexity and scope of the project.
* **Theme** (`theme`, select_one, optional):
Choose from three available theme options: Modern, Minimalist, or Responsive.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required):
Enter a valid email address for communication and to receive project updates.
