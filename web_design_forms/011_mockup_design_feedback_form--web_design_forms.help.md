# mockup_design_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mockup design feedback form is a tool to collect feedback and ratings from users about a mockup design. It is used to gather usability ratings and comments about the design, as well as collect contact information from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter a title for the form in the "Form Title" field.
2. Rate the mockup design's usability using the "Mockup Usability" field.
3. Provide any comments or feedback about the design in the "Mockup Comments" field.
4. Choose whether or not you would like to submit the form by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Form Submit" field.
5. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up communication, provide your email address in the "Email" field.
6. If you would like to be contacted for follow-up communication, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Optionally, add any additional notes about the design in the "Note" field.
8. Select the form category from the "Form Category" field, which includes options such as "Web Design Forms" and "Web Forms".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title (1)**: A text field where you can enter a title for the form.
* **Mockup Usability (2)**: A number field where you can rate the design's usability, with options ranging from 1 to 10.
* **Mockup Comments (3)**: A text field where you can provide any comments or feedback about the design.
* **Form Submit (4)**: A select-one field where you can choose whether or not to submit the form by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Email (5)**: An email field where you can provide your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone (6)**: A text field where you can provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Note (7)**: A note field where you can add any additional notes about the design.
* **Form Category (8)**: A select-multiple field where you can select the form category, which includes options such as "Web Design Forms" and "Web Forms".
