# website_design_quote_and_order_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect contact information, project details, and payment information from a customer or client.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (if any).
2. Enter your contact details, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Provide project details, such as the project deadline and website URL.
4. Enter your payment information, including the payment method and terms.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Project Details**: Enter a brief description of the project you are interested in.
* **Customer Quote**: Enter a quote from the customer, if any.
* **Project Deadline**: Enter the deadline for the project.
* **Website URL**: Enter the URL of the website you are interested in.
* **Website Description**: Enter a brief description of the website.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the project.
* **Designer Quote**: Enter the quote from the designer.
* **Payment Details**: Enter your payment information.
* **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms, such as "Pay by bank transfer".
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method, such as "Credit card".

Note: Fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be filled out. Other fields are optional but can provide additional information to support your request.
