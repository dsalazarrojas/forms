# the_hobbit_the_battle_of_the_five_armies_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is intended for users who want to sign up for the Battle of the Five Armies game. It collects basic user information to facilitate their participation in the game.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First name in the "First name" field.
2. Fill in your Last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you about your participation in the game.
4. Create a password for your account in the "Password" field. Make sure it's a combination of characters and numbers that are easy to remember but hard to guess.
5. Confirm your password in the "Confirm Password" field. This ensures that you entered the correct password.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`text`, required: false): Your first name is requested for identification purposes. Please type in your first name as it appears on your records.
* **Last name** (`text`, required: false): Your last name is requested for identification purposes. Please type in your last name as it appears on your records.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Your email address is requested for communication purposes. Please ensure it is valid and accessible.
* **Password** (`text`, required: false): Create a strong and unique password for your account. It should be a combination of characters and numbers that are easy for you to remember but hard for others to guess.
* **Confirm Password** (`text`, required: false): Confirm your password by re-typing it. This ensures that your password is correct and you're not typing it incorrectly.
