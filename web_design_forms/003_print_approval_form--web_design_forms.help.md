# Print Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Print Approval Form is used to gather necessary information from customers before printing documents on their behalf. This form is essential for ensuring that the customer's identity, invitation status, and approval status are accurately recorded, and that they have given their consent for the printing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the **Customer Name** field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the **Customer E-mail** field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the **Customer Phone** field.
4. Select the invitation status from the dropdown menu: **Invitation Status**.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the **Notes** field (optional).
6. Select whether the customer has approved the printing process in the **Customer Approval** dropdown menu.
7. Select whether the customer has given their consent in the **Customer Consent** dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name (Customer Name, text, required)**: Enter the customer's full name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Customer E-mail (Customer E-mail, email, required)**: Enter the customer's email address where they can be reached for further correspondence.
* **Customer Phone (Customer Phone, text, required)**: Enter the customer's phone number for any necessary contact or follow-up.
* **Invitation Status (Invitation Status, select_one, required)**: Select the status of the invitation sent to the customer, either Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
* **Notes (Notes, note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or details about the customer and their printing request.
* **Customer Approval (Customer Approval, select_one, required)**: Select whether the customer has approved the printing process, either Approved or Not Approved.
* **Customer Consent (Customer Consent, select_one, required)**: Select whether the customer has given their consent for the printing process, either Consented or Not Consented.
