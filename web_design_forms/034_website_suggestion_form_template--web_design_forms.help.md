# Website Suggestion Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect suggestions from users on how to improve our website. Your input will help us create targeted updates that address specific areas of our site, making it more useful and efficient for you and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2.  Fill out each field with the required information.
3.  Review each field for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of your website suggestion?** (`website_name`, text, required): This field is where you can enter the name or title of the website you'd like to suggest an improvement for.
* **Can you describe your website?** (`description`, text, required): Please provide a brief description of the website, including its purpose and any relevant details.
* **What is your email address?** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you for further clarification or to keep you updated on any changes made to the suggested website.
* **Which category does your website belong to?** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best suits your website from the provided options.
* **What is the date for your website?** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date for the website you're suggesting.
* **Can you suggest a form type for your website?** (`form_type`, select_one, required): Choose the most suitable form type for your website from the provided options.
* **Can you provide another date for your website?** (`repeat_date`, date, required): Enter another date for the website you're suggesting.
* **Can you suggest another website?** (`suggestion`, text, required): If you have another website that needs improvement, feel free to add it here.
* **Can you suggest a date for your website?** (`select_date`, select_one, required): Choose the most suitable date for your website from the provided options.
* **Can you suggest another form type?** (`select_suggestion`, select_one, required): If you have an alternative form type in mind, select it from the provided options.
* **What is your phone number?** (`phone`, number, required): This is your contact phone number, which we'll use to contact you if needed.
* **Is this a repeat question for dates?** (`confirm_date`, checkbox, required): Check the box if this is a repeated question for dates.

Please submit your completed form, and we'll review your suggestions to create targeted updates for our website.
