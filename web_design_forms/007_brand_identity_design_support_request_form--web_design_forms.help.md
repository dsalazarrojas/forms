# Brand Identity Design Support Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about your brand identity design project. Please fill in all required fields to help us better understand your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out this form completely before submitting.
- Answer each question accurately and concisely.
- If you have any doubts or uncertainties, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address for future communication.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Provide your best contact number for any questions or updates.
* **Company or Business Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of your company or business.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): If you have a website, enter the URL.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry you operate in from the list.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your company from the options.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of design support you need for your project.
* **Current Brand Status** (`current_brand_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your current branding.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, required): Describe your ideal customers or target audience.
* **Core Brand Values** (`brand_values`, text, required): List the core values that your brand represents.
* **Design Style Preferences** (`design_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select the design style preferences that suit your brand.
* **Color Preferences** (`color_preferences`, text, optional): If you have any specific color preferences or dislikes, enter them here.
* **Main Competitors** (`competitors`, text, optional): List your main competitors in the industry.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, date, required): Enter the date by which you need the project completed.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Select the budget range for your project.
* **Inspiration Examples** (`inspiration_examples`, text, optional): Share any design inspiration examples that you like.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any other information that may help us understand your project requirements.

Note: Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible brand identity design support. If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, don't hesitate to ask.
