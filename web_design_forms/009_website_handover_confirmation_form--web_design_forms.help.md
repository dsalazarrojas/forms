# Website Handover Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Handover Confirmation Form is a collection of fields to gather information about website handovers from a designer to a client. This form is used to ensure that all details are captured and that the handover process is properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out each field according to your role: designer, client, or team manager.
*   Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as required.
*   Use the notes fields to add any additional information that is not captured in the other fields.
*   Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Handover Confirmation** (<website_handover_confirmation_form>, text, required): Enter a brief confirmation that the handover has occurred.
* **Client Confirmation** (<client_confirmation>, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the client has confirmed the handover or 'No' to indicate that the client has not confirmed yet.
* **Designer Confirmation** (<designer_confirmation>, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to confirm that the designer has confirmed the handover or 'No' to indicate that the designer has not confirmed yet.
* **Transfer Date** (<transfer_date, date, optional): Enter the date when the website transfer was confirmed.
* **Transfer Time** (<transfer_time, time, optional): Enter the time when the website transfer was confirmed.
* **Transfer Method** (<transfer_method, select_one, optional): Select how the website transfer was done (this field is not used in the provided YAML).
* **Transfer Details** (<transfer_details, note, optional): Enter any additional details about the website transfer.
* **Comments** (<comments, text, optional): Enter any comments about the handover process.
* **Email Address** (<email_address, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (<phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Website URL** (<website_url, text, optional): Enter the URL of the website that was transferred.
* **Client Name** (<client_name, text, optional): Enter your client's name.
* **Client Email** (<client_email, email, optional): Enter your client's email address.
* **Client Phone** (<client_phone, text, optional): Enter your client's phone number.
* **Client Website** (<client_website, text, optional): Enter your client's website URL.
* **Designer Name** (<designer_name, text, optional): Enter the name of the designer who handed over the website.
* **Designer Email** (<designer_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the designer who handed over the website.
* **Designer Phone** (<designer_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the designer who handed over the website.
* **Designer Website** (<designer_website, text, optional): Enter the website URL of the designer who handed over the website.
* **Website Status** (<website_status, select_one, optional): Select the status of the website (Active or Inactive).
* **New Website URL** (<new_website_url, text, optional): Enter the URL of the new website.
* **New Domain** (<new_domain, text, optional): Enter the domain name of the new website.
* **Hosting Details** (<hosting_details, note, optional): Enter any details about the website hosting.
* **Next Steps** (<next_steps, note, optional): Enter any next steps that should be taken.
* **Comments** (<comments, text, optional): Enter any comments about the handover process.
