# Website Visual Inspection Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Website Visual Inspection Checklist is a tool used to evaluate and verify the quality of a website's visual aspects. It assesses the overall website design, color scheme, navigation menu, performance, content quality, accessibility, content updating frequency, content updating impact, navigation menu items, website security, user experience, and visual inspection checklist verified status. This form helps identify areas of improvement and ensures the website meets the required standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and select the most accurate answer for each category.
2. For the "Color Scheme" question, choose one of the three options: Primary color, Secondary color, or Accent color.
3. For the "Navigation Menu" question, select all the options that apply to your website's menu (e.g., Responsive, Consistent, Easy to navigate).
4. For the "Website Performance" question, choose between "Fast" and "Slow" based on your website's loading speed.
5. For the "Content Quality" question, select between "High quality", "Medium quality", and "Low quality" based on the overall quality of your website's content.
6. For the "Accessibility" question, choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether your website is accessible to all users.
7. For the "Content updating frequency" and "Content updating impact" questions, enter a numerical value (e.g., 1-10).
8. For the "Navigation Menu Items" question, choose between "Limited", "Moderate", and "Extensive" based on the number of menu items on your website.
9. For the "Website Security" question, choose between "Secure" and "Insecure" based on your website's security level.
10. For the "User Experience" question, enter a numerical value (e.g., 1-10).
11. For the "Website Visual Inspection Checklist Verified" question, choose between "Yes" and "No" to indicate whether your website meets the required standards.
12. For the "Website Visual Inspection Checklist Verified Impact" question, choose between "High impact", "Medium impact", and "Low impact" based on the significance of the verified status.
