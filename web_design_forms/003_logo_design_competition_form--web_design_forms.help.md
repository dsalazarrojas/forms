# Logo Design Competition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture submissions for a logo design competition. It allows designers to provide their contact information, logo details, and submission status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and contact information in the "logo_designer_name", "email", and "phone" fields.
2. Describe your logo design in the "logo_design" field.
3. Choose the type of logo you designed (vector, raster, or both) in the "logo_type" field.
4. Select the color scheme for your logo in the "color_scheme" field.
5. Upload your logo file in the "submission_file" field.
6. Provide a submission URL if applicable in the "submission_url" field.
7. Agree to the submission terms and conditions in the "agreement" field.
8. Choose a category for your submission in the "category" field.
9. Indicate if the submission has been submitted in the "submithed" field.
10. Indicate the submission type in the "submission_type" field.
11. Enter the date and time of submission in the "submission_date" and "submission_time" fields.
12. Track the submission status in the "submission_status" field.
13. Add any comments about your submission in the "submission_comments" field.
14. Allow reviewers to leave comments about your submission in the "reviewer_comments" field.
15. Update the submission status after review in the "submission_status_modified" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form_title** (note): This is a title for the form, which is not mandatory and is meant to be a brief description of your submission.
* **logo_designer_name** (text, optional): Enter your name as the logo designer.
* **logo_design** (text, optional): Describe your logo design in this field.
* **logo_type** (select one, optional): Choose between Vector, Raster, or Vector or Raster for your logo type.
* **color_scheme** (select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple color schemes for your logo (Monochromatic, Bichromatic, Trichromatic).
* **logo_file** (text, optional): Upload your logo file for review.
* **submitter_info** (text, optional): Provide any additional information about your submission.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **date_submitted** (date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **submission_file** (text, optional): Upload any additional files for your submission.
* **submission_url** (text, optional): Provide a URL for your submission.
* **agreement** (select one, optional): Agree to the submission terms and conditions.
* **category** (select multiple, optional): Choose one or multiple categories for your submission (Web, Graphics).
* **submithed** (select multiple, optional): Indicate if your submission has been submitted.
* **submission_type** (select multiple, optional): Indicate the type of submission (Yes, No).
* **submission_date** (date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **submission_time** (time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **submission_status** (select one, optional): Track the submission status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
* **submission_comments** (note, optional): Add any comments about your submission.
* **reviewer_comments** (note, optional): Allow reviewers to leave comments about your submission.
* **submission_date_modified** (date, optional): Update the submission date after review.
* **submission_time_modified** (time, optional): Update the submission time after review.
* **submission_status_modified** (select one, optional): Update the submission status after review.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting.
* Ensure that your logo file is in the correct format.
* Reviewers will see your submission status and comments.
* This form is for internal use, so be sure to follow the submission guidelines carefully.
