# Bootstrap Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your contact information and preferences for communication and potential business opportunities. It should only be filled out once per user, and the data will be checked for consistency and accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If you have an address, enter it in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your city in the "City" field.
7. Enter your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
8. Enter your zip or postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
9. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
10. Choose your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
11. Enter the subject of your message in the "Subject" field.
12. Enter your message or inquiry in the "Message" field.
13. Select all the areas of interest that apply to you in the "Areas of Interest" field.
14. If you have already contacted us before, choose where you found us in the "How Did You Hear About Us" field.
15. Optionally, choose whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter in the "Subscribe to Newsletter" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address (3)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Address (5)** (`text`, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City (6)** (`text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State or Province (7)** (`text`, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Postal Code (8)** (`text`, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Country (9)** (`text`, optional): Enter your country.
* **Preferred Contact Method (10)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how we should contact you: Email, Phone, SMS, Mail, Other.
* **Subject (11)** (`text`, required): Enter the subject of your message.
* **Message (12)** (`text`, required): Enter your message or inquiry.
* **Areas of Interest (13)** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all the areas of interest that apply to you: Web design, Web development, Mobile apps, E-commerce, Branding, Marketing, Other services.
* **How Did You Hear About Us (14)** (`select_one`, optional): If you have already contacted us before, choose where you found us: Search engine, Social media, Referral, Advertisement, Friend recommendation, Other.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter (15)** (`select_one`, optional): Choose whether you would like to subscribe to our newsletter: Yes, subscribe me, No, thanks, Maybe later.

Note: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) and must be filled out in order to submit the form.
