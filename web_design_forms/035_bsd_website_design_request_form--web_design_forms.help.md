# BSD Website Design Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a website design for your company's or business's online presence. Please provide us with your company information, project details, and design preferences to help us understand your needs and create a website that meets your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your company's information:
	* Enter your full name in the "Client Name" field.
	* Enter your company's name in the "Company or Business Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Company Email" field.
2. Specify your company's industry or type:
	* Describe what your company does in the "Industry or Business Type" field.
3. Project Type:
	* Select the type of website you need:
		+ New Website
		+ Website Redesign
		+ Landing Page
		+ E-commerce Site
		+ Blog or Content Site
		+ Portfolio
		+ Other (please specify)
4. Define your project's primary purpose:
	* Enter a brief description of the main goal of your website.
5. Identify your target audience:
	* Describe who will visit your site.
6. Select required features and functionality:
	* Choose all the features that apply to your project.
7. Specify your design preferences:
	* Enter a brief description of the design style you prefer (e.g. modern, minimalist, creative, corporate, etc.).
8. Provide your preferred colors or brand colors:
	* Enter any specific colors you like or have.
9. Reference websites that inspire you:
	* Enter any websites that you like or admire.
10. Check if you already have a logo:
	* Select "True" if you already have a logo, "False" if you don't, or "Will provide later" if you'll provide it later.
11. Specify when you need the project completed:
	* Enter your desired project timeline.
12. Estimate your project's budget:
	* Enter an approximate budget range.
13. Check if you have content ready:
	* Select "Yes, we have it ready" if you have content ready, "Partialy ready" if you have some content ready, or "No, we need help" if you need assistance.
14. Enter any special requests or notes:
	* Enter any unique requirements or notes for your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Required): Enter your full name.
* **Company or Business Name** (Required): Enter your company's or business's name.
* **Industry or Business Type** (Required): Describe what your company does.
* **Company Email** (Required): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (Required): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Address** (Optional): Enter your company's full street address.
* **Project Type** (Required): Select the type of website you need.
* **Primary Purpose of Website** (Required): Enter a brief description of the main goal of your website.
* **Target Audience** (Required): Describe who will visit your site.
* **Required Features and Functionality** (Optional): Choose all the features that apply to your project.
* **Preferred Design Style** (Required): Enter a brief description of the design style you prefer.
* **Color Preferences** (Optional): Enter any specific colors you like or have.
* **Reference Websites** (Optional): Enter any websites that you like or admire.
* **Do You Have a Logo?** (Optional): Select "True" if you already have a logo, "False" if you don't, or "Will provide later" if you'll provide it later.
* **Desired Project Timeline** (Required): Enter your desired project timeline.
* **Budget Range** (Optional): Enter an approximate budget range.
* **Will You Provide Content?** (Optional): Select "Yes, we have it ready" if you have content ready, "Partialy ready" if you have some content ready, or "No, we need help" if you need assistance.
* **Special Requests or Additional Notes** (Optional): Enter any unique requirements or notes for your project.
