# Tattoo Consultation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used as a consultation form for tattoos, allowing users to provide their personal and tattoo-related information for a consultation process with a tattoo artist or tattoo studio. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Customize the form with your business information in the "Customize the form for your business" field.
2. Link your form to your website and Facebook "Book Now" button in the "Link form to your website and Facebook Book Now button" field.
3. Create a free Zapier forever account in the "Create Zapier free forever account" field.
4. Create a free Trello free forever account in the "Create Trello free forever account" field.
5. Create a Trello board in the "Create a board in Trello called" field.
6. Add a new card to the Trello board in the "Add a card to board" field.
7. Go back to Zapier and create a new Zap in the "Go back to Zapier and create a Zap" field.
8. Move a Trello card to the appointment list on your board in the "Move a Trello card to the appointment list on your board" field.
9. Create a new integration with Google Calendar in the "Create a zap to your Google Calendar when you move a Trello card to the appointment list on your board" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customize the form for your business (1)**: This field allows you to add your business name, address, and other relevant information to the form.
* **Link form to your website and Facebook Book Now button (2)**: This field enables you to connect your form to your website and Facebook Book Now button, making it easier for customers to access your business.
* **Create Zapier free forever account (3)**: This field helps you create a free account on Zapier, a platform that allows you to automate tasks and workflows.
* **Create Trello free forever account (4)**: This field assists you in creating a free account on Trello, a project management and collaboration tool.
* **Create a board in Trello called (5)**: This field allows you to create a new Trello board, where you can organize and track tasks and projects.
* **Add a card to board (6)**: This field enables you to add a new card to the Trello board, where you can store information and tasks.
* **Go back to Zapier and create a Zap (7)**: This field guides you to create a new Zap in Zapier, an automated workflow that can perform tasks and actions.
* **Move a Trello card to the appointment list on your board (8)**: This field helps you move a Trello card to a specific list on your board, making it easier to manage tasks and appointments.
* **Create a zap to your Google Calendar when you move a Trello card to the appointment list on your board (9)**: This field enables you to create a new integration between Trello and Google Calendar, automating calendar updates when a Trello card is moved to the appointment list.
