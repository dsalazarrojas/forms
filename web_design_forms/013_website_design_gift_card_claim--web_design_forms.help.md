# Website Design Gift Card Claim - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you claim a website design gift card. Please ensure you are eligible for the gift card before completing this form. If you have already claimed a gift card, please do not attempt to claim another one.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form (optional, but recommended for clarity).
2. Enter the gift card code (required for verification).
3. Enter your client name (required for identification purposes).
4. Enter your project goals (optional, but helpful for us to understand your needs).
5. Enter your client email address (required for communication).
6. Enter your client phone number (optional, but recommended for further communication).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title (1)**: Enter a brief title for your form submission. This will help us identify your request.
* **Gift Card Code (2)**: Enter the gift card code provided to you. This is required for verification purposes.
* **Client Name (3)**: Enter your full name as the client or representative for this gift card claim.
* **Project Goals (4)**: Describe your project goals and objectives. This is optional but helps us understand your needs.
* **Client Email (5)**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you regarding your gift card claim.
* **Client Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number for further communication. If you prefer not to, you can skip this field.
