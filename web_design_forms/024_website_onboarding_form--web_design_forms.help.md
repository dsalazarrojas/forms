# Website Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Website Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from users for identification and registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your **Company Name** in the "company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name (first_name)** 
*   **Label:** first_name
    **Type:** text
    **Required:** Optional
    **Explanation:** Enter your first name here. This is a text field where you can input your first name.

### **Last Name (last_name)**
*   **Label:** last_name
    **Type:** text
    **Required:** Optional
    **Explanation:** Enter your last name here. This is a text field where you can input your last name.

### **Company (company)**
*   **Label:** company
    **Type:** text
    **Required:** Optional
    **Explanation:** If you are representing a company, enter your company's name here. This is a text field where you can input your company's name.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you input accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth onboarding. 
* If you are experiencing issues with the form, contact our support team at form@gic.mx.
