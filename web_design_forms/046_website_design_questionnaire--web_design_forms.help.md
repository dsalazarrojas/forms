# Website Design Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Website Design Questionnaire" form is designed to gather information from clients about their website design needs and preferences. This form will help our team understand your project requirements, allowing us to create a tailored design that meets your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. For select_one fields, choose one option that best describes your preference.
3. For select_multiple fields, choose all applicable options.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter the full name of the client or the company name you are representing.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you for further information or to discuss your project.
* **Company** (Company, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are representing or the name of your business.
* **Website Type** (Website Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of website you need: Blog, E-commerce, Portfolio, Business, or Non-profit.
* **Website URL** (Website URL, text, optional): If you have a URL for your current or previous website, enter it here for reference.
* **Project Budget** (Project Budget, number, required): Enter a budget for your project, and we will work with you to create a design within this budget.
* **Project Deadline** (Project Deadline, date, required): Enter a target deadline for your project, and we will work to complete it on or before this date.
* **Color Scheme** (Color Scheme, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable color schemes for your design.
* **Font Style** (Font Style, select_one, required): Choose the font style that best suits your design preferences.
* **Content Style** (Content Style, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable content styles for your design.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted for project updates and information: Phone, Email, or Skype.
* **Preferred Communication Frequency** (Preferred Communication Frequency, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive project updates and information: Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.

Please note that some fields have specific requirements, such as the Client Name, Email, Company, Website Type, Project Budget, Project Deadline, Color Scheme, Font Style, Content Style, Preferred Contact Method, and Preferred Communication Frequency fields, which are essential for us to understand your project requirements. Fill these fields accurately and thoroughly to avoid any misunderstandings.
