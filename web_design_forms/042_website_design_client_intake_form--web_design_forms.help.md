# Website Design Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients about their website design project. Completing this form will help us understand your project goals, details, and preferences, enabling us to create a tailored website that meets your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Answer each section thoroughly and concisely.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Goals (Project Goals)**: Describe the main goals and objectives of your project. This will help us understand what you want to achieve with your website.
* **Client Details (Client Details)**: Please provide us with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself or your organization. This will help us establish communication and ensure we have the right contact information.
* **Project Preferences (Project Preferences)**: Select all the options that apply to you or your project. This will help us understand your preferences and tailor our approach accordingly.
* **Communication Channels (Communication Channels)**: Select how you would prefer to communicate with us. This will help us know your preferred method of communication.
* **Project Deadlines (Project Deadlines)**: If you have specific deadlines for the project, please enter them in the date format (e.g., MM/DD/YY).
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)**: Provide any additional contact information or details that you feel are important for us to know.
* **Email Preferences (Email Preferences)**: Enter your preferred email address for us to communicate with you.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)**: If you have specific payment terms or conditions, please enter them here. This will help us understand your financial expectations.

## Tips
- Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- Use clear and concise language when answering each question.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you haven't missed any important sections or fields.
