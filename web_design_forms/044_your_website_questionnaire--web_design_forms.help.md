# your_website_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your website, including its name, description, URL, and contact details. This information will help us understand the characteristics of your website and improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* Enter your website's name in the "Website Name" field.
* Provide a brief description of your website in the "Description" field.
* Enter your website's URL in the "URL" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
* Enter a tagline that describes your website in the "Tagline" field.
* Select the target audience for your website in the "Target Audience" field. Select all applicable options.
* Select the type of website you are managing in the "Website Type" field.
* Enter the content management system you are using in the "Content Management System" field.
* If you have a LinkedIn account, enter its link in the "LinkedIn" field.
* If you have an Instagram account, enter its link in the "Instagram" field.
* If you have a YouTube account, enter its link in the "YouTube" field.
* If you have a Twitter account, enter its link in the "Twitter" field.
* If you have a Facebook account, enter its link in the "Facebook" field.
* If you have a Pinterest account, enter its link in the "Pinterest" field.
* If you have a Google+ account, enter its link in the "Google+" field.
* If you have a Tumblr account, enter its link in the "Tumblr" field.
* Upload a logo for your website in the "Logo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Website Name** (`website_name`, text, required): Enter your website's name.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your website.
* **URL** (`url`, text, required): Enter your website's URL.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Tagline** (`tagline`, text, required): Enter a tagline that describes your website.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable options for your website's target audience.
* **Website Type** (`website_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of website you are managing.
* **Content Management System** (`content_management_system`, text, optional): Enter the content management system you are using.
* **LinkedIn** (`social_media_linkedin`, text, optional): If you have a LinkedIn account, enter its link.
* **Instagram** (`social_media_instagram`, text, optional): If you have an Instagram account, enter its link.
* **YouTube** (`social_media_youtube`, text, optional): If you have a YouTube account, enter its link.
* **Twitter** (`social_media_twitter`, text, optional): If you have a Twitter account, enter its link.
* **Facebook** (`social_media_facebook`, text, optional): If you have a Facebook account, enter its link.
* **Pinterest** (`social_media_pinterest`, text, optional): If you have a Pinterest account, enter its link.
* **Google+** (`social_media_google`, text, optional): If you have a Google+ account, enter its link.
* **Tumblr** (`social_media_tumblr`, text, optional): If you have a Tumblr account, enter its link.
* **Logo** (`logo`, text, optional): Upload a logo for your website.
