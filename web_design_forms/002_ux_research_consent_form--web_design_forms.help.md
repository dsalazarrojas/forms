# UX Research Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for use by a research team to collect information from participants and obtain their consent for participating in a UX research project.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all participants who will be participating in the research project.
2. Provide participant details, including their name, email, and any other relevant information.
3. Enter contact information for the research participant, including their phone number.
4. Obtain participant consent by selecting one of the available options (Yes, I consent, No, I do not consent, or I am unsure).
5. Optionally, provide participant notes and researcher information.
6. Describe the research goals and objectives.
7. Enter the participant's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participants
* **Participants (Select Multiple)** (`research_participants`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all participants who will be participating in the research project. This field allows you to list multiple participants.

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the participant's details, such as their name, job title, or company.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact information for the research participant, such as their work phone number or email.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the research participant.

### Participant Consent
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_one`, required): Obtain participant consent by selecting one of the available options (Yes, I consent, No, I do not consent, or I am unsure). This field is required.

### Participant Notes
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional notes about the research participant, such as their background or qualifications.

### Researcher Info
* **Researcher Info** (`researcher_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the researcher, such as their name or contact information.

### Research Goals
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the research goals and objectives, including the expected outcomes.

### Participant Email
* **Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the research participant.
