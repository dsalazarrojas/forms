# Marketing Request Form 2015 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Request Form 2015 is designed to collect information from requestors about their marketing needs and requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant details.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification or seek support from the marketing team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Information (id: 1)**: Enter your name and contact information so we can reach out to you for further questions or clarification.
* **Name (id: 2)**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Number (id: 3)**: Enter your internal or external contact number.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your company email address to facilitate communication.
* **Budget (id: 5)**: Estimate the budget for the requested marketing activity. This will help us plan and prioritize requests.
* **Cost Center (id: 6)**: Enter the department or team responsible for the marketing activity.
* **Country (id: 7)**: Enter your country of operation or the target market for the marketing activity.
* **Region (id: 8)**: Enter the specific region for the marketing activity (e.g., North America, Europe, etc.).
* **Activity Type (id: 9)**: Select the type of marketing activity you're requesting (e.g., Event, Social Media Campaign, etc.).
* **Activity Title (id: 10)**: Enter a brief title for the marketing activity.
* **Activity Date (id: 11)**: Enter the start and end dates for the marketing activity.
* **Activity Description (id: 12)**: Provide a detailed description of the marketing activity.
* **Activity Location (id: 13)**: Enter the location for the marketing activity (e.g., physical address, website, etc.).
* **Activity Communication Plan (id: 14)**: Enter any additional communication plans or requirements for the marketing activity.
* **Comments (id: 15)**: Enter any additional comments or context about the marketing request.

## Tips

* Be as detailed as possible when filling out the form to ensure we can accurately assess and execute your marketing request.
* If you need help or clarification about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to the marketing team.
