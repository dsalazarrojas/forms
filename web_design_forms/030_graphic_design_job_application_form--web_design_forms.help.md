# Graphic Design Job Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants for graphic design positions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Attach relevant attachments as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide a brief introduction about yourself.
* **University Status** (`university_status`, text, optional): Please provide your current or past university status.
* **Skill Levels** (`skill_levels`, text, optional): Here, you can list your proficiency levels in different graphic design skills.
* **Relevant Attachments** (`relevant_attachments`, text, required): Attach any relevant files that showcase your work, such as images, PDFs, or URLs to your online portfolio.
* **Portfolio Samples** (`portfolio_samples`, text, optional): This is where you can share links to your online portfolio or attach relevant samples.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): Please upload your resume as an attachment or provide a link to a hosted resume file.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all attachments are in a format that can be easily read and reviewed.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please reach out to the contact address provided above.
