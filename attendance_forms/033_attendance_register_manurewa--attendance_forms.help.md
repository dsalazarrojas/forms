# Attendance Register Manurewa - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Attendance Register Manurewa is a form used to record and track student attendance in schools or institutions. It helps monitor attendance patterns, identify trends, and inform decisions to support student learning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Institution Information**: Fill in the name and code of your institution.
2. **Class Information**: Provide the name and level of the class.
3. **Student Information**: Enter the student's name and ID.
4. **Attendance Dates**: Select the date of the record.
5. **Attendance Status**: Mark the student's attendance status (Present, Absent, Late, Excused Absence, or Medical Certificate).
6. **Period Attended**: Select the period(s) the student attended (if applicable).
7. **Reason for Absence**: Provide the reason for the student's absence (if applicable).
8. **Approval Status**: Select the approval status of the absence (if applicable).
9. **Cumulative Information**: Review and update the cumulative data (total days present, total days absent, and attendance percentage).
10. **Notes and Comments**: Add any additional notes or comments about the student's attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Institution Name**: The name of the school or institution.
* **Institution Code**: The official code or identifier of the school or institution.
* **Register Period**: The term or period covered by the attendance record.
* **Class Name**: The name of the class the student is enrolled in.
* **Class Level**: The year or grade level of the class.
* **Class Teacher**: The name of the class teacher.
* **Student Name**: The full name of the student.
* **Student ID**: The unique student identifier.
* **Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the student (optional).
* **Gender**: The gender of the student (optional).
* **Date of Record**: The date when the attendance is recorded.
* **Attendance Status**: The status of the student's attendance (Present, Absent, Late, Excused Absence, or Medical Certificate).
* **Period Attended**: The period(s) the student attended during the record.
* **Reason for Absence**: The reason for the student's absence (if applicable).
* **Absence Approval Status**: The status of the absence approval (Approved, Pending, Not Approved, or Not Applicable).
* **Total Days Present**: The total number of days the student has attended.
* **Total Days Absent**: The total number of days the student has been absent.
* **Attendance Percentage**: The overall attendance percentage of the student.
* **Notes and Comments**: Any additional notes or comments about the student's attendance.
