# Online Worship Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record attendance for online worship services. Fill in your details accurately to ensure correct data is captured.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have one, provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the date of your attendance in the "Date of Attendance" field. Format: YYYY-MM-DD.
6. Enter the time of your attendance in the "Time of Attendance" field. Format: HH:MM.
7. Fill in the location where you attended the online worship service in the "Location of Attendance" field.
8. If you have any comments or feedback, enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Date of Attendance** (`date`, required): Enter the date of your attendance in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 2022-02-01).
* **Time of Attendance** (`time`, required): Enter the time of your attendance in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30).
* **Location of Attendance** (`location`, required): Enter the location where you attended the online worship service.
* **Comments** (`comments`, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have.
