# Employee Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track employee attendance, ensuring accurate reporting for HR or management teams.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name as the employee who is submitting this form.
2. Enter the date of your attendance, following the standard date format (e.g., 02/16/2024).
3. Enter the time of your arrival and departure.
4. Provide a brief description of your attendance, such as the reason for your presence or any notable events that took place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your name as the employee submitting this form.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your attendance in the standard date format (e.g., 02/16/2024).
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Enter the time of your arrival and departure, using the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 08:00).
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, Text, Required): Provide a brief description of your attendance, such as the reason for your presence or any notable events that took place.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure accurate tracking of your attendance.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact your HR or management team for assistance.
* This form is for reporting your attendance, so please be honest and accurate in your responses.
