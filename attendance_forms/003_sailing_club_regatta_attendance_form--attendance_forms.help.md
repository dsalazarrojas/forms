# Sailing Club Regatta Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sailing Club Regatta Attendance Form is a tool for sailors to provide their information for regatta events organized by the club. This form helps the club to keep track of sailors' attendance and other details for regatta events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by entering the **Regatta Name**, which is the name of the regatta event you are attending.
2. Enter your **Sailor Name**, which is your name as a sailor.
3. Add your **Sailor Email**, which is your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Sailor Phone**, which is your phone number for contact purposes.
5. Select the type of **Boat Type** you are using (e.g., racing, cruising, etc.).
6. Enter your **Boat Name**, which is the name of your boat.
7. Choose your **Sailor Country**, **Sailor City**, and **Sailor Zip** for your location.
8. Enter your **Sailor State** and **Sailor Zip 2** for your state and zip code.
9. Select the **Regatta Type**, which is the type of regatta event (e.g., racing, cruising, etc.).
10. Enter the **Start Date** and **End Date** of the regatta event.
11. Select the **Start Time** and **End Time** of the regatta event.
12. Enter the **Regatta Date**, which is the date of the regatta event.
13. Enter the **Regatta Location**, which is the location of the regatta event.
14. Provide a brief **Regatta Description**, which is a description of the regatta event.
15. Enter the **Entry Fee**, which is the fee to participate in the regatta event.
16. Select the **Entry Fee Currency**, which is the currency of the entry fee.
17. Enter the **Entry Fee Amount**, which is the amount of the entry fee.
18. Select the **Payment Status**, which indicates if you have made a payment for the regatta event.
19. Add any additional **Notes** about your regatta event participation.
20. Finally, confirm your attendance by selecting **Yes** or **No**.
