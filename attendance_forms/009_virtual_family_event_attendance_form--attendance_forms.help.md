# Virtual Family Event Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from families about their attendance and needs at a virtual family event. The data collected will help event organizers tailor the event experience to meet the families' requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name.
*   Provide your Date of Birth for identification purposes.
*   Enter your Email address so we can contact you with event updates.
*   Enter your Phone number for easy communication.
*   If you plan to attend the event, enter the Event Date and Event Time.
*   Indicate the size of your family.
*   Select the type of family you belong to (Family with kids, Family with no kids, or Other).
*   Confirm whether you plan to Attended the event (True, False, or Maybe).
*   Add any Comments about your family or event expectations.
*   Choose the tool(s) that have been assigned to you (Chatjimmy or Other).
*   Enter any additional Comments2 (if needed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your Date of Birth for identification purposes.
*   **Email**: Enter your Email address so we can contact you with event updates.
*   **Phone**: Enter your Phone number for easy communication.
*   **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you plan to attend.
*   **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event you plan to attend.
*   **Family Size**: Select the number of family members attending the event.
*   **Family Type**: Choose the type of family you belong to:
    *   **Family with kids**: Families with children.
    *   **Family with no kids**: Families without children.
    *   **Other**: Other family types not listed.
*   **Attended Event**: Confirm whether you plan to attend the event:
    *   **True**: You plan to attend the event.
    *   **False**: You do not plan to attend the event.
    *   **Maybe**: You are unsure about attending the event.
*   **Comments**: Add any comments or expectations about the event.
*   **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned to you for the event.
*   **Comments2**: Additional comments or expectations about the event.

## Tips

*   Be sure to fill out all fields that apply to your family.
*   Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
