# Safety Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Attendance Form is designed to capture attendance information for meetings and events, ensuring accurate headcounts and easy reference in emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information about the attendees, including their job titles, departments, and job locations.
2. Select the correct options for "Attendees" to indicate who was present or not.
3. Fill in the "Attendee Name" field with the name of each attendee you've selected.
4. Provide any additional comments or details in the "Form Comments" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Meeting Title** (`meeting_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the meeting or event.
  - This helps identify the specific gathering for which you're reporting attendance.
- **Date Time** (`date_time`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the meeting or event.
  - This provides a precise timestamp for record-keeping purposes.
- **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate who was present or not.
  - Select "Yes" for attendees who were present, and "No" for those who were not.
  - If an attendee is not present, provide a brief reason for their absence in the "Attendee Name" field.
- **Attendee Name** (`attendees_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attendee you selected as "Yes" or "No" in the "Attendees" field.
  - Provide the name of the attendee who was present or not present, if applicable.
- **Job Title** (`attendees_job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the attendee.
  - If provided, help identify the attendee's role or responsibility.
- **Department** (`attendees_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of the attendee.
  - If provided, help identify the departmental context of the attendee's presence.
- **Job Location** (`attendees_job_location`, text, optional): Enter the job location of the attendee.
  - If provided, help identify the location where the attendee was present.
- **Notes** (`attendees_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the attendee.
  - Use this field to record any relevant information about the attendee's presence or absence.
- **Form Comments** (`form_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or observations about the form completion process.
  - Use this field to record any relevant comments or feedback about the form or its completion.

## Tips
- Always check the "Attendees" field carefully to ensure accurate selections.
- If an attendee's status is "No", provide a brief reason for their absence in the "Attendee Name" field.
- Be thorough in filling out the form to ensure accurate record-keeping.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are accurately filled out.
