# Homeschool Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeschool Attendance Form is a tool used to record student attendance at a homeschooling program. It is designed to be a simple and efficient way for parents to report their child's daily attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the student's grade level from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of attendance.
4. Choose the time of attendance.
5. Indicate the student's attendance status by selecting from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (Text)**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Student Grade (Select One)**: Choose the student's grade level from the options provided. If the student is in multiple grades, please select the highest grade level applicable.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of attendance in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time (Time)**: Enter the time of attendance in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 or 14:30).
* **Attendance (Select One)**: Indicate the student's attendance status by selecting from the options provided.
* **Parent Name (Text)**: If the student is attending homeschooling under a parent's supervision, enter the parent's name.
