# Wedding Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about wedding attendance, including the number of guests, contact information, and details about arrival and departure times.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Number of guests**: Enter the number of guests you plan to bring to the wedding. This field is optional.
2. **Will attend**: Select '1' if you plan to attend the wedding, '2' if you plan to attend with a guest, or '3' if you plan to attend with both guests and other family members. This field is optional.
3. **Contact info**: Enter any relevant contact information for you or your group. This field is optional.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is optional.
5. **Phone number**: Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
6. **Name**: Enter your name. This field is optional.
7. **Address**: Enter your address. This field is optional.
8. **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY or YYYY-MM-DD. This field is optional.
9. **Time of attendance**: Select the time you plan to arrive. This field is optional.
10. **Will arrive late**: Select 'True' if you expect to arrive late, 'False' if you plan to arrive on time. This field is optional.
11. **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details about your attendance. This field is optional.
12. **Will arrive early**: Select 'True' if you plan to arrive early, 'False' if you plan to arrive on time. This field is optional.
13. **Arrival time**: Select the time you plan to arrive. This field is optional.
14. **Arrival time of day**: Select the time of day you plan to arrive. This field is optional.
15. **Arrival date**: Select the date you plan to arrive. This field is optional.
16. **Arrival time range**: Enter the time range you plan to arrive (e.g., 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM). This field is optional.
17. **Will leave late**: Select 'True' if you expect to leave late, 'False' if you plan to leave on time. This field is optional.
18. **Will leave early**: Select 'True' if you plan to leave early, 'False' if you plan to leave on time. This field is optional.
19. **Estimated leave time**: Select the time you plan to leave. This field is optional.
20. **Estimated leave time of day**: Select the time of day you plan to leave. This field is optional.
21. **Estimated leave date**: Select the date you plan to leave. This field is optional.
22. **Estimated leave time range**: Enter the time range you plan to leave (e.g., 12:00 PM - 2:00 PM). This field is optional.
23. **Comments**: Add any additional comments or details about your attendance. This field is optional.
24. **Will send gift**: Select 'True' if you plan to send a gift, 'False' if you do not. This field is optional.
25. **Gift details**: Add any details about the gift you plan to send. This field is optional.
