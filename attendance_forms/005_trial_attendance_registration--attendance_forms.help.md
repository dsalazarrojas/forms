# Trial Attendance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to attend a trial session. By submitting this form, you will be providing essential details to schedule and manage your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Trial Session Details section with any relevant information about the session you wish to attend.
2. Select your preferred dates for the session using the drop-down menu.
3. Choose your preferred times for the session using the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your name and email address in the Attendee Details section.
5. Confirm your email address in the Email (again) field to ensure accuracy.
6. Provide your phone number (if applicable) and any additional information you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trial Session Details** (trial_session_details, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the trial session you wish to attend, including its title or any other relevant details.
* **Preferred Dates** (preferred_dates, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the dates you are available to attend the session. You can choose multiple dates.
* **Preferred Times** (preferred_times, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your preferred times for the session. You can choose multiple times.
* **Attendee Details** (attendee_details, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other details you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Name** (attendee_name, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (attendee_email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Email (again)** (attendee_email_repeat, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Phone** (attendee_phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you via phone.
* **Additional Info** (attendee_additional_info, text, required/optional): Share any additional information you think would be helpful for us to know about your attendance.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is accurate to receive important information about the session.
* If you selected multiple dates or times, please prioritize your preferences in case of conflicts.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the Additional Info field.
