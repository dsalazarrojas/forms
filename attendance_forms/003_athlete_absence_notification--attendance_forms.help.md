# Athlete Absence Notification - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to notify the coach and other relevant parties of an athlete's absence from a specific event. It collects essential information about the athlete's name, team affiliation, absence details, and contact information to facilitate communication and make necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the athlete's full name in the "Athlete Full Name" field.
2. Select the team or squad the athlete belongs to in the "Team or Squad Name" field.
3. Choose the type of event the athlete is missing from using the "Type of Event Missed" dropdown.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the absence in the "Absence Start Date" and "Absence End Date" fields, respectively.
5. Select a reason for the absence from the "Reason for Absence" dropdown.
6. Provide any additional details in the "Additional Details" text field if necessary.
7. If the athlete is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name and contact phone number in the "Parent or Guardian Name" and "Contact Phone Number" fields.
8. If the coach has been notified through other means, select "True" in the "Has the Coach Been Notified via Other Means?" dropdown. Otherwise, select "False".
9. Enter the date of submission in the "Date of Submission" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter the athlete's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Team or Squad Name**: Select the team or squad the athlete belongs to, for example, Varsity Basketball or Under-15 Soccer.
* **Type of Event Missed**: Choose the primary event the athlete is missing from using the dropdown menu. Options include Practice, Game, Team Meeting, Training Session, and Tournament.
* **Absence Start Date**: Enter the date the athlete's absence will start, using the date format MM/DD/YY.
* **Absence End Date**: Enter the date the athlete's absence will end, using the date format MM/DD/YY.
* **Reason for Absence**: Select the most accurate reason for the athlete's absence from the dropdown menu. Options include Illness, Injury, Academic Commitment, Family Emergency, Pre-planned Vacation, and Other.
* **Additional Details**: Provide any additional context or information about the absence if necessary.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: If the athlete is under 18, enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the best contact phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **Has the Coach Been Notified via Other Means?**: Select "True" if the coach has been notified through other means, such as email or text, or "False" otherwise.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date the form was submitted.
 
## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, ask the relevant parties for guidance.
* Double-check the form for errors before submitting to avoid any delays.
