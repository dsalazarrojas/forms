# Morning Arrival Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track employee morning arrival status and report any symptoms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee ID (if you don't have one, you can leave this field blank).
2. Select your current Health Status (Healthy, Unhealthy, or Unknown).
3. If you are experiencing any symptoms, type them in the text box provided.
4. Add any additional notes about your morning arrival status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee ID
* **Employee ID** (number, optional): Enter your employee ID if you have one.

### Health Status
* **Health Status** (select_one, optional): Select one of the following options:
	+ **Healthy**: You are feeling well and do not have any symptoms.
	+ **Unhealthy**: You are not feeling well and are experiencing symptoms.
	+ **Unknown**: Your health status is uncertain or you are not sure.

### Symptoms
* **Symptoms** (text, optional): If you are experiencing any symptoms, please specify what they are.

### Notes
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information about your morning arrival status.
