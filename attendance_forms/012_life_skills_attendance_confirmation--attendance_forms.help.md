# life_skills_attendance_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record participant attendance at life skills sessions or workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the session details, including the date and time of the session.
2. Provide participant information, such as their name, phone number, and email.
3. Select the session topics covered during the session.
4. Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Add any additional comments or feedback about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the session details, such as the date, time, and any other relevant information about the session.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required/optional): Provide your participant information, including your name, phone number, and email.
* **Date of Session** (`date_of_session`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time of Session** (`time_of_session`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Location of Session** (`location_of_session`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the session.
* **Session Topics** (`session_topics`, select_one, required/optional): Select one or more topics covered during the session, such as communication, problem-solving, or teamwork.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your attendance by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or feedback about the session.
