# Church Adult Attendance Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Adult Attendance Form is a tool for recording attendance information and gathering feedback from adults who participate in church events. It collects details about the event, the participant's status (e.g., present, absent, late), personal information (name, email, phone number), and engagement level during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the event and its corresponding date and time from the form.
2. Choose your attendance status (e.g., present, absent, late).
3. Provide your personal details (name, email, phone number).
4. If you were a volunteer, select the role you played.
5. Rate your experience of the event (e.g., excellent, good, average).
6. Add any additional comments about the event.
7. Choose how you would like to be contacted for future events (e.g., yes, no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event you attended.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Choose the time of the event.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`): Select your status for the event (e.g., present, absent, late).
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Member Status** (`member_status`): Select your current member status (e.g., active member, visitor, prospective member).
* **Level of Participation** (`participation_level`): Choose your engagement level during the event (e.g., full, partial, observed only).
* **Volunteered During Event** (`volunteered`): Select if you volunteered during the event.
* **Volunteer Role (if applicable)** (`volunteer_role`): Enter your volunteer role (if applicable).
* **Event Experience** (`event_feedback`): Rate your experience of the event (e.g., excellent, good, average).
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the event.
* **May we contact you about future events** (`contact_permission`): Select how you would like to be contacted for future events.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`): Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., email, phone, text message, mail).
