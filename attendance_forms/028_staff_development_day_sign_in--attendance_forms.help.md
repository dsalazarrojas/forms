# Staff Development Day Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Staff Development Day Sign In form is used by staff members to record their attendance and participation in sessions during a specific event. This information will help the organizers track staff presence, session participation, and gather feedback for future planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name and role in the School.
2. Select the school you are currently affiliated with.
3. Choose whether you attended the session (Yes or No).
4. Enter the start and end times for the session you attended.
5. Indicate your attendance status (Active or Inactive) and add any additional comments in the Notes field.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. If applicable, indicate whether you participated in the session (Yes or No).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Development Day** (`1`): This field is the session identifier. It should not be filled in by the user.
* **Full Name** (`2`): Enter your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Role in School** (`3`): Enter your role or position within the school (e.g., Teacher, Admin, etc.).
* **School Name** (`4`): Enter the name of the school you are currently affiliated with.
* **Grade Level** (`5`): Enter the grade level you are teaching or working with.
* **Session** (`6`): Indicate whether you attended the session (Yes or No).
* **Start Time** (`7`): Enter the start time of the session you attended.
* **End Time** (`8`): Enter the end time of the session you attended.
* **Attendance Status** (`9`): Indicate your attendance status (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`10`): Add any additional comments or feedback about the session.
* **Email** (`11`): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`12`): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Comments** (`13`): If applicable, indicate whether you participated in the session (Yes or No).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers.
* Please note that this form is for actual session attendance. Do not fill it in for hypothetical or non-attended sessions.
