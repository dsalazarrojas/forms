# Liturgy Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liturgy Attendance Form is used to record the attendance and details of a liturgy service. This form is intended for staff members and service officers to provide accurate and up-to-date information about the service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the **Service Date** field to choose the date of the service.
2. Choose the **Meeting Time** field to select the time of the service.
3. Enter the **Location** field to specify the location of the service.
4. Select the **Service Type** field to choose whether it's a yes or no service.
5. Choose the **Preacher** field to select whether the preacher attended or not.
6. Enter the **Attendees Count** field to record the number of attendees.
7. Select the **Total Attendees** field to record the total number of attendees.
8. Select the **Attendees** field to choose which attendees have attended the service.
9. Enter the **Remarks** field to add any additional remarks about the service.
10. Select the **Service Officer** field to choose whether the service officer attended or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required: false): This field is used to enter the date of the service.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false): This field is used to select the time of the service.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter the location of the service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select whether it's a yes or no service. 
* **Preacher** (`preacher`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select whether the preacher attended or not.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`, number, required: false): This field is used to record the number of attendees.
* **Total Attendees** (`total_attendees`, number, required: false): This field is used to record the total number of attendees.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to choose which attendees have attended the service.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, required: false): This field is used to add any additional remarks about the service.
* **Service Officer** (`service_officer`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to choose whether the service officer attended or not.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely.
- If the form has already been filled out, please do not fill it out again.
- If you need any further assistance, please contact the relevant authorities.
