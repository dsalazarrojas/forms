# Daycare Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Attendance Form is used by staff to record attendance information for each child in their care. This information is essential for maintaining accurate records and ensuring the well-being of children in the daycare.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill out the date**: Enter the date of the attendance report using the provided date field.
2. Enter the time**: Enter the time of the report using the provided time field.
3. **Enter the child's name**: Fill out the "Name of attended child" field with the name of the child you are reporting attendance for.
4. **Staff present**: Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu next to "Name of staff present" to indicate whether you are present while the child was in care.
5. **Number of children attended**: Enter the number of children you are reporting attendance for.
6. **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the child's attendance.
7. **Submit**: Enter your name and contact information to submit the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fill out the date**: Enter the date of the attendance report in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Enter the time**: Enter the time of the report in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Name of attended child**: Enter the name of the child you are reporting attendance for.
* **Name of staff present**: Select "Yes" if you are present while the child was in care, or "No" otherwise.
* **Number of children attended**: Enter the number of children you are reporting attendance for.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the child's attendance.
* **Submit**: Enter your name and contact information (email and phone number) to submit the report.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to maintain accurate attendance records.
- If you are reporting for multiple children, please repeat the form for each child.
- Review and submit the report before leaving the childcare facility.
- Keep a copy of the report for your records.
