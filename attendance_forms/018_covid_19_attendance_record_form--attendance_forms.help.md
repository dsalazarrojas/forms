# Covid 19 Attendance Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and record attendance information for patients in medical practices. It helps healthcare providers monitor patient attendance and identify any issues or patterns.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Enter the **Scheduled Appointments** section, which is the first page.
*   In this section, you can enter a brief description of scheduled appointments.
*   Proceed to the second page by clicking the "Next" button.
*   Here, you can enter the patient's details, including **Patient Name**, **Medical Practice**, **Patient Dosage Form**, and **Patient DOB**.
*   Next, enter the appointment details: **Appointment Status**, **Reason Missed** (if applicable), **Medical Practice Staff**, **Patient Phone**, **Patient Email**, **Medical Practice Address**, **Appointment Date**, and **Appointment Time**.
*   Move to the third page by clicking the "Next" button again.
*   In the last section, you'll find fields for **Patient Attendance Status**, **Appointment Number**, **Appointment Number Prefix**, **Patient ID**, and **Medical Practice ID**. Fill in the required details.
*   Once you've completed all sections, click "Submit" to save your record.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Scheduled Appointments**: Enter a brief description of scheduled appointments.
*   **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's name as it is known to the medical practice.
*   **Medical Practice**: Enter the name of the medical practice or clinic where the patient is being treated.
*   **Patient Dosage Form**: Enter the dosage form of the medication, if applicable.
*   **Patient DOB**: Enter the patient's Date Of Birth. This is a required field to help verify the patient's identity.
*   **Appointment Status**: Choose from the list of options whether the appointment was **Scheduled**, **Attended**, **Missed**, **Rescheduled**, or **Cancelled**.
*   **Reason Missed**: If an appointment was **Missed**, provide a brief explanation for why it was missed.
*   **Medical Practice Staff**: Enter the name of the medical practice staff member who attended to the patient.
*   **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's contact phone number.
*   **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
*   **Medical Practice Address**: Enter the address of the medical practice or clinic.
*   **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment.
*   **Appointment Time**: Enter the time of the appointment.
*   **Patient Attendance Status**: Choose whether the patient was **Present**, **Absent**, **Tardy**, or **Early Departure**.
*   **Patient Medical Practice ID**: Enter the unique ID of the medical practice or clinic assigned to the patient.
*   **Appointment Number**: Enter the unique number assigned to the appointment.
*   **Appointment Number Prefix**: Enter any prefix for the appointment number.
*   **Patient ID**: Enter the unique ID of the patient.
*   **Medical Practice ID**: Enter the unique ID of the medical practice or clinic.
*   **Medical Practice Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person at the medical practice.
*   **Medical Practice Contact Person Phone**: Enter the phone number of the medical practice contact person.
*   **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's visit.
*   **Patient Attendance Status**: Choose the attendance status of the patient.
