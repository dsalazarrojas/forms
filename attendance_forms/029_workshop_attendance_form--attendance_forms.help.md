# Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Attendance Form is used to track and record attendance for workshops. To use this form, please ensure that you have completed the necessary steps to attend the workshop and that you are using the correct version of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in your details as instructed by your workshop facilitator or supervisor.
3. Choose your attendance status (Present, Absent, or Unable to attend) and provide any comments if necessary.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workshop Attendance
*   **<label>** (text, required/optional: false): This is the main content area for the workshop attendance form. Please provide a brief description of your attendance in this field.
*   **attendee name** (text, required/optional: false): Please enter your name as it should be displayed in the workshop attendance records.
*   **email** (email, required/optional: false): If you have an email address, please enter it so that we can contact you if needed.
*   **attendee phone** (text, required/optional: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it so that we can contact you if needed.
*   **attendance** (select_one, required/optional: false):
    *   **Present**: You attended the workshop as expected.
    *   **Absent**: You were unable to attend the workshop due to unforeseen circumstances.
    *   **Unable to attend**: You were unable to attend the workshop for reasons such as scheduling conflicts or prior commitments.
*   **comments** (text, required/optional: false): If you have any comments or feedback about the workshop, please provide them in this field.
*   **attendee signature** (note, required/optional: false): Please sign and date this form to confirm your attendance.
*   **workshop attendance form submitted by** (text, required/optional: false): Please enter your name and date to confirm that you have submitted this form.

This form is intended to be completed by attendees of workshops to provide a record of their attendance. Please make sure to complete it as instructed by your workshop facilitator or supervisor. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to them for guidance.
