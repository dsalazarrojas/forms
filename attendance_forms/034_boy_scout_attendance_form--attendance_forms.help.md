# Boy Scout Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boy Scout Attendance Form is designed to track attendance and other relevant information for Boy Scout meetings. It is used by Scout leaders to document the attendance status and other important details of Scouts at meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Troop Number** in the designated field. This is a required field.
2. Enter the **Meeting Date** in the "Meeting Date" field. This is also a required field.
3. Select the **Scout Leader in Charge** from the available options.
4. Enter the **Scout Name** of the Scout in attendance.
5. Choose the **Attendance Status** of the Scout from the options: **Present**, **Absent**, **Excused**, or **Tardy**. This is a required field.
6. Check the **Uniform Status** of the Scout, selecting from **Full Uniform**, **Partial**, or **No Uniform**. This is a required field.
7. If applicable, select all completed tasks or requirements from the list.
8. Enter the **Service Hours Earned** (if applicable).
9. Add any additional **Notes on Scout Performance or Needs** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Troop Number** (`troop_no`, text, required): Enter the troop number associated with the meeting.
* **Meeting Date** (`meet_dt`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting being recorded.
* **Scout Leader in Charge** (`leader_name`, text, required): Choose the leader in charge of the meeting.
* **Scout Name** (`scout_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the Scout in attendance.
* **Attendance Status** (`att_status`, select_one, required): Select the attendance status of the Scout from the options: **Present**, **Absent**, **Excused**, or **Tardy**.
* **Uniform Status** (`uniform_status`, select_one, required): Select the uniform status of the Scout from the options: **Full Uniform**, **Partial**, or **No Uniform**.
* **Tasks / Requirements Completed Today** (`finished_tasks`, select_multiple, optional): Select all tasks or requirements completed by the Scout.
* **Service Hours Earned Today** (`svc_hrs`, number, optional): Enter the service hours earned by the Scout (if applicable).
* **Notes on Scout Performance or Needs** (`perf_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes on the Scout's performance or needs.
* **Date Recorded** (`rec_dt`, date, required): Enter the date the form was completed. 

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Be accurate when selecting the **Attendance Status** and **Uniform Status** to ensure accurate tracking.
* Take notes on any performance or needs issues and include them in the **Notes on Scout Performance or Needs** field.
