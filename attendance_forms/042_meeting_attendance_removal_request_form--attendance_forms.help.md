# Meeting Attendance Removal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meeting Attendance Removal Request Form is a document that allows you to request removal from a scheduled meeting. Fill out this form to communicate your absence and provide a valid reason for it.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your meeting title.
2. Provide a valid reason for your absence.
3. Choose your attendee type (Team Lead, Manager, or Colleague).
4. Choose the meeting type (Scheduled Meeting or Ad-Hoc Meeting).
5. Enter the date of the meeting.
6. Enter the time of the meeting.
7. Enter your email address.
8. (Optional) Enter your phone number.
9. Enter any notes or comments about your absence.
10. Click the "Submit" button to send the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Title** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the meeting you are requesting to be removed from.
* **Reason** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_reason`, text, optional): Provide a valid reason for your absence. This will help your colleagues understand your situation.
* **Attendee** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_attendee`, text, optional): Enter your name, as you are the one requesting to be removed.
* **Attendee Type** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_attendee_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of attendee you are (Team Lead, Manager, or Colleague).
* **Meeting** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_meeting`, select one, optional): Choose the type of meeting you are attending (Scheduled Meeting or Ad-Hoc Meeting).
* **Date** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Time** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Email** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your absence.
* **Submit** (`meeting_attendance_removal_request_form_submit_button`, text, optional): Click this button to send your request.
