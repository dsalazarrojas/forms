# Gala Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Gala Attendance Form" is used to collect attendance information for galas or events. This form helps track guest registration, payment, and attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the required fields: 
  - **Event ID** (`event_id`): Enter the unique ID for the event.
  - **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event.
  - **Event Type** (`event_type`): Choose the type of event (Gala, Conference, Workshop).
  - **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event.
  - **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
  - **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
  - **Event Status** (`event_status`): Choose the event status (Confirmed, Confirmed with Changes, Not Confirmed).
* Fill out the registration fields:
  - **Guest Registration** (`guest_registration`): Enter the guest's name and contact information.
  - **Guest Info** (`guest_info`): Enter any additional guest information.
  - **Registration Status** (`registration_status`): Choose the guest's registration status (Registered, Unregistered, Cancelled).
  * Optional fields:
    - **Guest Count** (`guest_count`): Enter the number of guests attending.
    - **Registration Type** (`registration_type`): Choose the registration type (Online, Offline, In-person).
* Fill out the payment fields:
  - **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Choose the payment status (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
  - **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Choose the payment method (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* Fill out the event fields:
  - **Event Description** (`event_description`): Enter a brief description of the event.
  - **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event ID** (`event_id`): A unique ID for the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): The name of the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): The type of event (Gala, Conference, Workshop).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): The location of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): The date of the event.

* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`): The status of the guest's registration (Registered, Unregistered, Cancelled).
* **Guest Registration** (`guest_registration`): A text field for entering guest's name and contact information.
* **Guest Info** (`guest_info`): A text field for entering additional guest information.
* **Guest Count** (`guest_count`): The number of guests attending.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`): The type of guest registration (Online, Offline, In-person).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): The status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, Partially Paid).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): The method of payment (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): A brief description of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): The location of the event.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`): The date of attendance.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`): The time of attendance.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Any additional comments or notes about the guest's attendance.
