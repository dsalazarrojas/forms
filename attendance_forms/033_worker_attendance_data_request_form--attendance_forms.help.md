# Worker Attendance Data Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect worker attendance data for various organizational purposes such as attendance tracking, performance evaluation, and compliance with labor regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields with the requested information.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Select the appropriate options for the "Shift", "Check-in", "Check-out", "Absence Reasons", and "Drag and Drop" fields.
4. Review and submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Basic Info (Required: false)
* **Basic Info**: This field is a text input field where you can enter any additional information about your attendance.
### 2. Name (Required: false)
* **Name**: This field is used to record your name for attendance purposes.
### 3. ID Number (Required: false)
* **ID Number**: This field is used to record your ID number for reference.
### 4. Department (Required: false)
* **Department**: This field is used to indicate the department you belong to for attendance tracking.
### 5. Date (Required: false)
* **Date**: This field is used to select the date of your attendance.
### 6. Shift (Required: false)
* **Shift**: This field has three options: Morning, Afternoon, and Night. Select the shift you worked on that particular day.
### 7. Check-in (Required: false)
* **Check-in**: This field is used to record the time when you checked in for your shift.
### 8. Check-out (Required: false)
* **Check-out**: This field is used to record the time when you checked out for your shift.
### 9. Absence Reasons (Required: false)
* **Absence Reasons**: This field is used to select the reason for your absence. Choose from the available options: Sick, Family Emergency, Personal Reasons, or Other.
### 10. Drag and Drop (Required: false)
* **Drag and Drop**: This field is used to select whether you dragged and dropped on that particular day. Choose from the options: Yes or No.

Note: If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
