The OB GYN Event Attendance Survey is designed to collect feedback from attendees on the quality and effectiveness of an OB GYN-related event or session. This survey will help the organizers understand how well they performed in delivering the content, the engagement of the attendees, and the perceived value of the session. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the organizers can improve future events.

To start, ensure that the event details page (page 1) is filled with the actual event details that took place. This includes the event title, session date, and any other relevant details. Be honest and concise in providing this information to help us understand the context of the session.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the corresponding options that best describe your experience at the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (text):
  * This field is for describing the event details that took place. Please be honest and provide as much information as possible.
* **Session Attendance** (select multiple):
  * Select all options that best describe your attendance at the session. For example, if you attended the session, select "Yes".
* **Satisfaction Rating** (select one):
  * Choose a rating that best describes your satisfaction with the session. The options are:
    - Label 1: Very Satisfied
    - Label 2: Somewhat Satisfied
* **Attendee Feedback** (text):
  * Share your thoughts or feedback about the session. Be as detailed as possible so that we can improve future events.
* **Session Insights** (select multiple):
  * Select all options that best describe what you gained from the session or what insights you received. For example, if you learned something new, select "Yes".
* **Date of Birth** (date):
  * Please enter your date of birth. This information is optional but will help us better understand your demographics.
* **Medical License** (text):
  * If you have a medical license, enter the number or your license. This information is optional but will help us understand your professional background.
* **Email** (email):
  * Enter your email address so that we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone** (text):
  * Enter your phone number so that we can contact you if needed.

## Tips

* Please answer all the questions accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Your feedback is greatly appreciated, and we will use it to improve future events.

This form is designed to be completed by the attendees of the OB GYN-related event or session.
