# Employee Clock In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Clock In Form" is used to record the start and end times of an employee's workday, including any breaks and overtime. This form is crucial for time management and attendance tracking, and its accurate completion is essential for payroll processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct Clock In Date using the date field.
2. Choose the clock-in time using the time field.
3. Confirm whether you are clocking in as "Yes" or "No" in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Enter the "Work Hours" if applicable.
5. If you clocked out, select the correct "Clock Out Date" and "Clock Out Time" fields.
6. Add any relevant "Note" if needed.
7. Fill in your "Email" and "Phone" fields if applicable.
8. Confirm whether you clocked in or out "Yes" or "No" in the "Submitted By" and "Updated By" fields.
9. Select the correct "Clock In Status", "Clock Out Status", "Project Status", and "Deleted By" fields.
10. Select the correct "Updated At", "Deleted At" and "Updated By Name" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clock In Date (1)**: Enter the date you started work.
* **Clock In Time (2)**: Choose the time you started work.
* **Employee ID (3)**: Confirm whether you are clocking in as "Yes" or "No".
* **Work Hours (5)**: Enter the hours worked, if applicable.
* **Clock Out Date (6)**: Enter the date you finished work. (Leave this field blank if you're still at work)
* **Clock Out Time (7)**: Choose the time you finished work. (Leave this field blank if you're still at work)
* **Note (8)**: Add any relevant comments or notes.
* **Email (9)**: Enter your work email.
* **Phone (10)**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Submitted By (11)**: Confirm whether you clocked in or out "Yes" or "No".
* **Submitted At (12)**: Select the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Updated By (13)**: Confirm whether you updated the form "Yes" or "No".
* **Updated At (14)**: Select the date and time the form was updated.
* **Deleted At (15)**: Select the date and time the form was deleted.
* **Clock In Status (16)**: Select the correct status for clocking in.
* **Clock Out Status (17)**: Select the correct status for clocking out.
* **Project Status (18)**: Select the correct status for the project.
* **Deleted By (19)**: Confirm whether the form was deleted "Yes" or "No".
* **Updated By Name (20)**: Select the person who updated the form.
* **Clock Out Status Name (21)**: Select the correct status for clocking out.
* **Project Status Name (22)**: Select the correct status for the project.
* **Deleted By Name (23)**: Select the person who deleted the form.
* **Updated At Name (24)**: Select the date and time the form was updated.
* **Deleted At Name (25)**: Select the date and time the form was deleted.
