# Girl Scout Troop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record attendance details for Girl Scout troop activities. Fill out this form to report attendance at meetings, events, or other troop gatherings. The information collected will help the troop leaders track attendance and stay organized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information.
2. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Troop Name**: Enter the name of the troop.
* **Date and Time**: Record the date and time of the event or meeting.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the event or meeting.
* **Attendance**: Select 'Yes' if the attendee was present, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Attendees**: List the attendees (if any) at the event or meeting.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event or meeting.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form, either 'Assigned' or 'Volunteer'.
* **Submitted At**: Record the time the form was submitted.
* **Updated At**: Record the time the form was last updated.
* **Created At**: Record the time the form was created.
* **Updated By**: Select who last updated the form, either 'chatjimmy' or 'system'.
* **Created By**: Select who created the form, either 'chatjimmy' or 'system'.
* **Form ID**: This field is reserved for internal use and not intended for user input.
* **Category ID**: This field is reserved for internal use and not intended for user input.
* **Output File**: This field is reserved for internal use and not intended for user input.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure that the correct information is recorded.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the troop leader or system administrator for assistance.
* This form is for recording attendance, not for submitting new events or meetings. If you need to schedule a new event, contact the troop leader directly.
