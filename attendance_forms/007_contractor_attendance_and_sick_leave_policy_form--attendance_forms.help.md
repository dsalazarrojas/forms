# Contractor Attendance and Sick Leave Policy Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to record and report attendance and sick leave policy for contractors. This form is used for data collection and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contractor full name in the `Contractor Full Name` field.
2. Enter your contractor ID number in the `Contractor ID Number` field.
3. Enter your primary email address in the `Email Address` field.
4. Enter your best contact phone number in the `Phone Number` field.
5. Select your department from the list in the `Department` field.
6. Enter the name of the current project assignment in the `Project Name` field.
7. Select the start and end dates of the reporting period in the `Reporting Period Start Date` and `Reporting Period End Date` fields.
8. Enter the total number of scheduled work days in the `Total Scheduled Days` field.
9. Enter the number of days actually worked in the `Days Worked` field.
10. Enter the total number of absences in the `Number of Absences` field.
11. If you have taken sick leave, enter the number of sick leave days used in the `Sick Leave Days Taken` field. If you have not taken sick leave, enter 0.
12. Enter the reason for taking sick leave, if comfortable sharing, in the `Reason for Sick Leave` field.
13. If you have taken personal leave, enter the number of personal leave days used in the `Personal Leave Days Taken` field. If you have not taken personal leave, enter 0.
14. If you have taken unpaid leave, enter the number of unpaid leave days used in the `Unpaid Leave Days Taken` field. If you have not taken unpaid leave, enter 0.
15. Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the attendance policy by selecting `Yes` in the `Policy Reviewed` field.
16. Confirm that you understand the attendance requirements by selecting `Yes` in the `Understand Requirements` field.
17. Confirm that you understand the reporting procedure by selecting `Yes` in the `Notification Procedure` field.
18. Confirm that you understand when documentation is required by selecting `Yes` in the `Documentation Required` field.
19. Enter the name of your reporting supervisor in the `Supervisor Name` field.
20. Enter the email address of your reporting supervisor in the `Supervisor Email` field.
21. Enter the date of submission in the `Submission Date` field.
22. Add any additional notes or explanations in the `Additional Notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contractor Full Name (1)**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contractor ID Number (2)**: Enter your employee or contractor identification number.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Department (5)**: Select your department or project assignment from the list.
* **Project Name (6)**: Enter the name of your current project assignment.
* **Reporting Period Start Date (8)**: Enter the start date of the reporting period.
* **Reporting Period End Date (9)**: Enter the end date of the reporting period.
* **Total Scheduled Days (10)**: Enter the total number of scheduled work days.
* **Days Worked (11)**: Enter the number of days actually worked.
* **Number of Absences (12)**: Enter the total number of absences.
* **Sick Leave Days Taken (14)**: Enter the number of sick leave days taken. If you have not taken sick leave, enter 0.
* **Reason for Sick Leave (15)**: Enter the reason for taking sick leave, if comfortable sharing.
* **Personal Leave Days Taken (16)**: Enter the number of personal leave days taken. If you have not taken personal leave, enter 0.
* **Unpaid Leave Days Taken (17)**: Enter the number of unpaid leave days taken. If you have not taken unpaid leave, enter 0.
* **Policy Reviewed (19)**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the attendance policy.
* **Understand Requirements (20)**: Confirm that you understand the attendance requirements.
* **Notification Procedure (21)**: Confirm that you understand the reporting procedure.
* **Documentation Required (22)**: Confirm that you understand when documentation is required.
* **Supervisor Name (24)**: Enter the name of your reporting supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email (25)**: Enter the email address of your reporting supervisor.
* **Submission Date (26)**: Enter the date of submission.
* **Additional Notes (27)**: Add any additional notes or explanations.

## Tips

* Please fill in this form honestly and accurately.
* Please review the attendance policy before submitting this form.
* Please select the correct options for the fields as they apply to your situation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact your supervisor.
* Please submit this form on time and complete all required fields.
