# STEM Fair Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The STEM Fair Attendance Form is used to collect information about a STEM fair event and its participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Event Name" field to enter the name of the STEM fair event.
2. Choose a "Event Date" in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Enter the "Event Time" in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
4. Enter the "Event Location" where the STEM fair will take place.
5. Enter the number of participants in the "Participant Count" field.
6. For each participant, enter their name in the "Participant Name" field.
7. Enter the "Student ID" for each participant, if applicable.
8. Finally, enter the "Teacher Name" for the participant's teacher or mentor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer or person responsible for the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the STEM fair event. This will help in easily identifying the event and keeping track of it.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the STEM fair event. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY to ensure correct sorting and filtering.
* **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time of the event in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the event will take place. This could be a building, room, or online platform.
* **Participant Count**: Enter the total number of participants for the event.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of each participant in the STEM fair.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student ID, if applicable, for each participant. This could be a student ID number or other unique identifier.
* **Teacher Name**: Enter the name of the participant's teacher or mentor.

## Tips
* Ensure to fill out all required fields to avoid incomplete submissions.
* If you are filling out for multiple participants, use the same event details for all participants.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling this form, please contact the event organizers or administrators for assistance.
