# Employee Time Clock Check In/Check Out Log - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to record employee attendance times and related information for time-tracking and payroll management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before proceeding.
2. Enter the Check In Time and Employee Id when checking in.
3. Provide the Shift Duration if applicable.
4. Enter the Check Out Time and Check Out Date when checking out.
5. Add any relevant Notes to provide additional context.
6. Select the Check In Date if necessary.
7. Choose the Supervisor to report to.
8. Select the Check In User if necessary.
9. Review your entries before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time when you started your shift or began working.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, `number`, not required): Enter your employee ID, which can be used to identify you within the organization.
* **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, `number`, not required): If applicable, enter the duration of your shift or working hours.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`, `time`, not required): Enter the time when you completed your shift or finished working.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, not required): Provide any relevant notes or comments about your shift or work hours.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date when you started working, if necessary.
* **Supervisor** (`supervisor`, `select_one`, not required): Select the supervisor you report to, if necessary.
* **Shift Date** (`shift_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of your shift or working hours, if necessary.
* **Check Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date when you finished working, if necessary.
* **Check In User** (`check_in_user`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the user who checked in, which can be yourself or another employee, if necessary.

## Tips

* Be accurate when entering times to ensure correct payroll or attendance records.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or access rights to complete this form.
* Review your entries for completeness and accuracy before submission.
