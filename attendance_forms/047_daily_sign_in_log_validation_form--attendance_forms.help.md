# Daily Sign In Log Validation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record and validate the attendance of employees on a daily basis. It collects information such as employee name, date, start and end time, attendance status, and other relevant details to help manage attendance effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Select the "Date" for which you are signing in.
3. Enter your "Start Time" and "End Time" of your work hours.
4. Choose your "Attendance Status" from the available options.
5. (Optional) Add any "Notes" regarding your attendance.
6. Enter your "Employee Email" and "Employee Phone" for contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Daily Sign In Log Validation Form** (`form_title`, text, required): This is the title of the form, which is already populated.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your official name as an employee.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date for which you are signing in. Please ensure it is the correct date.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter your start time for the day. This is the time you began work.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter your end time for the day. This is the time you ended work.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Choose your attendance status from the available options: present, absent, tardy, sick, vacation, or unexcused.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you need to add any additional information, use this field to provide notes about your attendance.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter your official phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure you select the correct date for your attendance.
- Be accurate when entering your start and end times.
- Make sure to choose a valid attendance status.
- Use the notes field for any additional information that may not fit in the other fields.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
