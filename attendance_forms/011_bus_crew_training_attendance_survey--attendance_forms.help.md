# Bus Crew Training Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data from crew members about their participation and feedback on the training session they attended.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Choose the most appropriate option for each field to ensure accurate results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Title** (Required): Enter the name of the training session you attended.
* **Session Date** (Required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Session Time** (Required): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **Session Location** (Required): Enter the location where the training session was held.
* **Trainer Name** (Required): Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the training session.
* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID** (Required): Enter your employee identification number.
* **Position** (Required): Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Department** (Required): Select your department from the provided options.
* **Years of Experience** (Required): Select the number of years you have worked in your position.
* **Attendance Status** (Required): Select your attendance status from the provided options.
* **Training Type** (Required): Select the type of training from the provided options.
* **Session Feedback** (Optional): Provide any feedback about the session.
* **Content Relevance** (Required): Select how relevant the content was to your role.
* **Trainer Effectiveness** (Required): Select how effective the trainer was.
* **Material Quality** (Required): Select the quality of the training materials.
* **Knowledge Gained** (Required): Select how much knowledge you gained from the training session.
* **Would You Recommend This Training** (Required): Select whether you would recommend this training to colleagues.
* **Additional Training Needed** (Optional): Select any additional training you may need.
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): Enter any additional feedback or comments about the training session.
