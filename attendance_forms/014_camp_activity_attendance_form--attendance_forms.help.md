# Camp Activity Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Activity Attendance Form is a tool used to track and record attendance of camp activities. This form helps activity leaders or supervisors to document the attendance details of participants and ensure that all necessary information is recorded for activity management and participant accountability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Step 1: Activity Date** (`activity_date`): This field is for the date of the activity.
   - Fill in the date of the activity in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. **Step 2: Activity Name** (`activity_name`): This field is for the name or description of the activity.
   - Enter a short description of the activity or its name.
3. **Step 3: Group or Cabin Name** (`group_name`): This field is for the group or cabin that attended the activity.
   - Enter the group or cabin name to associate the attendance with the correct group.
4. **Step 4: Participant's Full Name** (`participant_full_name`): This field is for the name of the participant or attendee.
   - Enter the full name of the participant, including their first and last name.
5. **Step 5: Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`): This field is for marking the participant's attendance status.
   - Choose from options: **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**.
6. **Step 6: Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): This field is for recording the time the participant arrived if they were late.
   - Enter the arrival time in the format `HH:MM`.
7. **Step 7: Departure Time** (`departure_time`): This field is for recording the time the participant left the activity.
   - Enter the departure time in the format `HH:MM`.
8. **Step 8: Instructor or Staff Name** (`instructor_name`): This field is for the name of the instructor or staff overseeing the activity.
   - Enter the name of the staff member or instructor.
9. **Step 9: Notes** (`notes`): This field is for any incidents or observations recorded during the activity.
   - Enter any relevant notes or incidents that occurred during the activity.
10. **Step 10: Supervisor Signature Required** (`signature_required`): This field is for indicating if a supervisor's signature is needed for the form.
   - Choose from options: **True** or **False**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`): This field is required and should be filled in with the date of the activity in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`): This field is required and should be a short description or name of the activity.
* **Group or Cabin Name** (`group_name`): This field is required and should be the name of the group or cabin that attended the activity.
* **Participant's Full Name** (`participant_full_name`): This field is required and should be the full name of the participant.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`): This field is required and should be marked as **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): This field is not required and should be filled in if the participant arrived late.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`): This field is not required and should be filled in if the participant left the activity at a different time.
* **Instructor or Staff Name** (`instructor_name`): This field is required and should be the name of the staff member or instructor overseeing the activity.
* **Notes** (`notes`): This field is not required and should be used for any incidents or observations recorded during the activity.
* **Supervisor Signature Required** (`signature_required`): This field is not required and should be marked as **True** or **False** if a supervisor's signature is needed or not.
