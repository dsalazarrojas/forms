# Professional Development Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Attendance Form is designed to collect information about staff attendance at workshops, training sessions, and other professional development activities. This form helps track attendance, assess the effectiveness of such programs, and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop or training session you attended.
2. Select the **Workshop Date**: Choose the date on which you attended the workshop.
3. Choose the **Workshop Time**: Select the time when the workshop took place.
4. Enter the **Workshop Location**: Type in the location of the workshop.
5. Provide your **Trainer's Name**: Enter the name of the person who conducted the workshop.
6. Enter your **Name**: Type your full name as it appears on official records.
7. Enter your **Job Title/Position**: Type your job title or position as it appears on official records.
8. For each workshop, select whether you **Attended** or **Did not attend**: Choose the correct option for each workshop.
9. Select your **Attendance Status**: Choose the status from the dropdown menu (e.g., Pending, Attended, Declined, No Show, Confirmed).
10. Provide any additional **Notes**: Type in any comments or observations about the workshop.
11. Enter your **Email Address**: Type your official email address.
12. Enter your **Phone Number**: Type your official phone number.
13. Provide any **Comments**: Type in any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title** (Workshop Title, text, required): Enter the title of the workshop or training session you attended.
* **Workshop Date** (Workshop Date, date, required): Choose the date on which you attended the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (Workshop Time, time, required): Select the time when the workshop took place.
* **Workshop Location** (Workshop Location, text, required): Type in the location of the workshop.
* **Trainer's Name** (Trainer Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person who conducted the workshop.
* **Name** (Attendee Name, text, required): Type your full name as it appears on official records.
* **Job Title/Position** (Attendee Title, text, required): Type your job title or position as it appears on official records.
* **Attended Workshops** (Attended Workshops, select multiple, required): Select whether you attended each workshop.
* **Attendance Status** (Attendance Status, select one, required): Choose the status from the dropdown menu (e.g., Pending, Attended, Declined, No Show, Confirmed).
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Type in any comments or observations about the workshop.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Type your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Type your official phone number.
* **Comments** (Comments, text, required): Type in any additional comments or feedback about the workshop.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Be honest and provide detailed comments or observations about each workshop.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or HR representative.
