# Match Attendance Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Match Attendance Confirmation form is used to confirm the attendance of team members to a game or match. It is used to track team attendance and gather relevant information for event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your general information.
2. Select the team name.
3. Specify the game date and time.
4. Indicate whether team members attended the game.
5. Provide any additional notes about the game.
6. Enter your email address (if applicable).
7. Enter your phone number (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info**: Enter your name and contact information.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team that attended the game.
* **Game Date**: Enter the date of the game in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Team Members**: Select whether team members attended the game.
* **Game Time**: Enter the time of the game in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Game Location**: Enter the location of the game.
* **Attendance**: Select whether you attended the game.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the game.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you wish to receive updates).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you wish to receive updates).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are using this form to confirm attendance for a specific game or match.
* Use a separate instance of this form for each event.
* If you are experiencing issues with this form, please contact support.
