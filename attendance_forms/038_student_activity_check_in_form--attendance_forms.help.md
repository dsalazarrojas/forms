# Student Activity Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Student Activity Check In Form" is a tool used to record student activity check-in information for school events. It captures essential details such as student name, event date, activity name, and check-in status.

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the event date from the calendar or manually enter the date in the "Event Date" field.
3. Specify the name of the student's activity in the "Activity Name" field.
4. Choose the check-in status from the options in the "Check In Status" field.
5. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the "Event Details" field.
6. Select the student's attendance status from the options in the "Student Attendance" field.
7. Sign in with your student's signature in the "Student Signature" field.
8. Add your staff's name and signature in the "Staff Name" and "Staff Signature" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student** (`Student`, text, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required: false): Select the event date from the calendar or manually enter the date.
* **Activity Name** (`Activity Name`, text, required: false): Specify the name of the student's activity.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Check In Status** (`Check In Status`, select_one, required: false): Choose from the options "Check In", "No Show", "Cancelled", or "Late".
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Student Attendance** (`Student Attendance`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the student's attendance status from the options "Present", "Absent", or "Late".
* **Student Signature** (`Student Signature`, text, required: false): Sign in with your student's signature.
* **Staff Name** (`Staff Name`, text, required: false): Enter your staff name.
* **Staff Signature** (`Staff Signature`, text, required: false): Sign in with your staff signature.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields for accurate data entry.
- Use the calendar to select event dates for easy and accurate entry.
- Sign in with your student's signature to confirm their presence.
- Verify and confirm your staff's signature to ensure accuracy.
