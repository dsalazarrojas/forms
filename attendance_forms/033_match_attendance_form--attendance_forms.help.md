# Match Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Match Attendance Form is used to record attendance data for a specific match, including team and participant information, attendance status, and additional comments. This form helps maintain accurate records and ensures that attendance is tracked correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Team Name field with the name of the team you are reporting attendance for.
2. Enter the Team Size field with the number of participants in your team.
3. Fill in the Participant Name field with the name of the participant(s) attending the match.
4. Select the Participant Attendance field to indicate whether the participant(s) is/are attending the match.
5. Fill in the Date field with the date of the match.
6. Enter the Time field with the time of the match.
7. (Optional) Fill in the Event Location field with any additional information about the match location.
8. Fill in the Additional Comments field with any further information or comments about the match.
9. Fill in the Submitted By field with your name or username, so we can track who submitted the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (Team Name, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Size** (Team Size, number, required): Enter the number of participants in your team.
* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant(s) attending the match.
* **Participant Attendance** (Participant Attendance, select_multiple, optional): Select the attendance status of the participant(s).
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the match.
* **Time** (Time, time, required): Enter the time of the match.
* **Event Location** (Event Location, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the match location.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, optional): Enter any further information or comments about the match.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, optional): Enter your name or username, so we can track who submitted the report.
