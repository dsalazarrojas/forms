# Library Daily Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Daily Attendance Tracking Form is a tool used to record and monitor visitor activity within the library. This form helps library staff keep track of who is in the library, for how long, and under what circumstances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Library ID** (`library_id`): This is the identifier assigned to the library for tracking purposes. If you're unsure what to enter, please contact library staff for assistance.
2. Select the **Date** (`date`): This is the date of the visit.
3. Enter the **Time In** (`time_in`): This is the time the visitor arrived at the library.
4. Enter the **Time Out** (`time_out`): This is the time the visitor left the library.
5. Enter the **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`): This is the name of the visitor.
6. Enter the **Phone** (`phone`): This is the contact number of the visitor (if provided).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library ID (library_id)**: Enter the library's identification number for tracking purposes.
* **Date (date)**: The date of the visit.
* **Time In (time_in)**: The time the visitor arrived at the library.
* **Time Out (time_out)**: The time the visitor left the library.
* **Visitor Name (visitor_name)**: The name of the person visiting the library.
* **Phone (phone)**: The contact number of the visitor (if provided).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the form is filled in accurately and completely for tracking and reporting purposes.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact library staff for assistance.
* This form is an essential tool for ensuring the efficient and secure management of library operations. By completing this form, you are contributing to the library's overall management and visitor tracking.
