# Employee QR Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow employees to check in for their shifts, providing essential information about their work status, location, and any other relevant details. This form helps in accurate attendance tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have the required information ready.
2. Select your QR code from the options provided.
3. Input your shift start and end times accurately.
4. Enter your employee ID and name.
5. Select your department ID.
6. Choose your job role.
7. Select your check-in device status.
8. Optionally, provide your break time, break status, work status, location, and shift notes.
9. Review and submit the form for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Time** (id: 9, type: date, label: Check In Time): This field is for entering the date and time you check in for your shift.
* **Check In Device** (id: 10, type: text, label: Check In Device): This field is to record the type of device you used to check in (e.g., mobile app, computer, or other).
* **Shift Duration** (id: 11, type: select_multiple, label: Shift Duration): Choose the length of your shift from the options provided (e.g., 1 hour, 2 hours, etc.).
* **Break Time** (id: 12, type: text, label: Break Time): If you take breaks during your shift, enter the duration of your breaks in this field.
* **Break Status** (id: 13, type: select_one, label: Break Status): Select 'Active' if you are on a break or 'Inactive' if you are not.
* **Work Status** (id: 14, type: select_one, label: Work Status): Indicate whether you are actively working or not.
* **Location** (id: 15, type: text, label: Location): Enter the location where you are working, if applicable.
* **Shift Notes** (id: 16, type: text, label: Shift Notes): Provide any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Check In Device Status** (id: 17, type: text, label: Check In Device Status): This field is likely for recording any issues or problems with the check-in device.

Please note that some fields are optional, so fill them out only if necessary or as required by your management or HR policies. If you're still unsure about any field, consult your HR representative or manager for clarification.
