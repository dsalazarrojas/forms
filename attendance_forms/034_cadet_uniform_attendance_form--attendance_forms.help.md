# Cadet Uniform Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cadet Uniform Attendance Form is used to document the attendance details of cadets who receive uniforms. This form helps administrators keep track of uniform distribution, size confirmation, and attendance status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the cadet's full name in the "Cadet name" field.
2. Enter the date of uniform issue in the "Date" field.
3. Specify the items given to the cadet in the "Uniform issued" field.
4. Confirm if the size of the uniform given is correct in the "Size confirmed" field by selecting "True" or "False".
5. Specify the cadet's attendance status on the "Attendance status" field by selecting "Present", "Absent", or "Excused".
6. Provide the name of the staff member who signed out the uniform in the "Signature reference" field.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes or comments in the "Remarks" field.
8. Specify if follow-up is needed for any reason, such as repair or replacement, in the "Follow up needed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cadet name**: Enter the full name of the cadet who received the uniform.
* **Date**: Enter the date the uniform was issued to the cadet.
* **Uniform issued**: Describe the items given to the cadet, such as the type and quantity of uniform items.
* **Size confirmed**: Confirm whether the size of the uniform given matches the cadet's specifications. Select "True" if correct, "False" otherwise.
* **Attendance status**: Specify the cadet's attendance status on the day of uniform issue. Select "Present" if they were present, "Absent" if they were absent, or "Excused" if they were excused from attendance.
* **Signature reference**: Enter the name of the staff member who signed out the uniform to the cadet.
* **Remarks**: Add any additional notes or comments about the uniform issue or cadet's attendance.
* **Follow up needed**: Specify if any follow-up is required for the uniform, such as repair or replacement.
