# Mass Attendance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mass Attendance Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals or families who want to attend a mass or event. This form will help the organizers in planning and organizing the event to ensure everyone's needs are met.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Please provide your name and your family members' names, if any.
- Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for any further information or clarification.
- Enter your email address so that we can keep you updated about the event.
- Select the date and time of the mass you want to attend.
- Choose the location of the mass (if not specified, it will be the main location).
- If you have any special requests (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, seating preferences), please mention them.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in the "Comments" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fill out your details** (`mass_attendance_request_form_1`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the attendance list.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the attendance list.
* **Family Members** (`name_of_family_members`, text, required): Enter the names of all family members attending the mass.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you for any further information or clarification.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address so that we can keep you updated about the event.
* **Date of Mass** (`date_of_mass`, date, required): Select the date of the mass you want to attend.
* **Time of Mass** (`time_of_mass`, time, required): Select the time of the mass you want to attend.
* **Location** (`mass_location`, text, optional): Choose the location of the mass (if not specified, it will be the main location).
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): If you have any special requests (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, seating preferences), please mention them.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please provide them in this field.
