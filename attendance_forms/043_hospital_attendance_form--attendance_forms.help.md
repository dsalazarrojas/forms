# Hospital Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Attendance Form is designed to collect patient information during hospital visits, providing a structured and efficient way to gather essential details about patients' conditions, treatments, and interactions with healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill in the form with the patient's personal details**:
	* Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
	* Provide the reason for the visit in the "Reason for Visit" field.
	* Include any necessary treatment details in the "Treatment Details" field.
2. **Enter the healthcare provider's information**:
	* Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
3. **Record the visit information**:
	* Select the date of the visit in the "Visit Date" field.
	* Choose the time of the visit from the time picker in the "Visit Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name (text)**: Enter the patient's name, as recorded on file or self-identified.
* **Reason for Visit (text)**: Briefly describe the patient's reason for visiting the hospital, e.g., "Consultation", "Follow-up", or "Emergency".
* **Treatment Details (text)**: Provide any necessary details about the patient's treatment, medication, or medical condition.
* **Doctor Name (text)**: Enter the name of the healthcare provider who treated the patient.
* **Hospital Name (text)**: Enter the name of the hospital or healthcare facility where the visit took place.
* **Visit Date (date)**: Select the date of the visit using the date picker.
* **Visit Time (time)**: Choose the time of the visit from the time picker.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is completed accurately and thoroughly to provide the best possible care for the patient.
* If a field is not relevant or not applicable, leave it blank or mark it as "N/A" (Not Applicable).
* If you are unsure about any field, consult with a healthcare professional or the patient's medical file for guidance.
