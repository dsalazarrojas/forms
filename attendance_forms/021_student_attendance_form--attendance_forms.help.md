# Student Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Attendance Form is used to record and document student attendance, including dates, times, and status. This form helps to track attendance patterns and provide an overview of student activity.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Input the date of the student's attendance in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time of the student's attendance in the "Time" field.
4. Select the attendance status from the available options in the "Attendance Status" field.
5. If the student is absent, provide a brief reason for absence in the "Reason for Absence" field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (Student Name, text, required/optional): Enter the student's name to identify the individual.
* **Date** (Date, date, required/optional): Input the date of the student's attendance.
* **Time** (Time, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the student's attendance.
* **Attendance Status** (Attendance Status, select_one, required/optional): Select the attendance status from the available options (Present, Absent, Late, Unavailable).
* **Reason for Absence** (Reason for Absence, text, required/optional): If the student is absent, provide a brief reason for absence.
## Tips
* Make sure to input accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* Use the "Reason for Absence" field only when the student is absent.
* Review and revise your entries as needed to ensure accurate and consistent data.
