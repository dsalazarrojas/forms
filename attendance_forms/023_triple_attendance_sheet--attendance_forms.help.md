# triple_attendance_sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The triple_attendance_sheet form is used to collect attendance information from users. This data can be used to track attendance, schedule events, or generate reports.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Name" field with your name.
2. Enter the "Date" in the date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the "Time" in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
4. Leave the "Signature" field blank for now, as it's not required. You can add your signature when you're ready.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: Your name or ID, which helps us identify you as an attendee.
* **Date**: The date you attended the event, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: The time you attended the event, in the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Signature**: A space for you to add your signature when you're ready. This field is not required, so feel free to skip it for now.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form only once per event or occurrence.
* Double-check the date and time fields to ensure accuracy.
* When you're ready, add your signature to confirm your attendance.
