# School Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Attendance Form is used to track student attendance. It helps teachers and staff members to quickly and accurately record attendance information for each student. This form is used to document the student's ID, name, attendance date, attendance time, attendance status, reason for absence, and any relevant student notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the student's ID number in the Student ID field.
2. Enter the student's full name in the Student Name field.
3. Choose the attendance date from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the Attendance Date field.
4. Choose the attendance time from the time picker or enter it manually in the Attendance Time field.
5. Select the student's attendance status (Present, Absent, or Tardy) from the dropdown menu.
6. If the student is absent, enter a brief explanation of the reason for absence in the Reason for Absence field.
7. Add any relevant student notes in the Student Notes field.
8. Enter the staff member's ID number in the Staff ID field (if required by your school).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `number`, not required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, `date`, not required): Enter or choose the attendance date using the calendar picker.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`, `time`, not required): Enter or choose the attendance time using the time picker.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, `select_one`, not required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Present
	+ Absent
	+ Tardy
* **Reason for Absence** (`reason_absence`, `text`, not required): Enter a brief explanation if the student is absent.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, not required): Enter any relevant notes about the student's attendance.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`, `text`, not required): Enter the staff member's ID number (if required by your school).

## Tips
- Always enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Double-check your entries for errors before submitting.
- If unsure about a field, refer to your school's attendance policy for guidance.
