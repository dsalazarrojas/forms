# Mentoring Session Attendance Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mentoring Session Attendance Confirmation Form is used to record the attendance and confirmation of mentoring sessions, ensuring that both participants and mentors are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Complete the **Participant Form Section**: Enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
2. Complete the **Mentor Form Section**: Enter your name and any other relevant mentor information.
3. Complete the **Session Details**: Enter the session date, start and end times, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Form Section
* **Participant Name** (`participant_form_section`, text, required): Enter your name to confirm your attendance.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_form_section`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.

### Mentor Form Section
* **Mentor Name** (`mentor_form_section`, text, required): Enter the mentor's name to confirm their attendance.
* **Mentor Information** (`mentor_form_section`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the mentor.

### Session Details
* **Session Date** (`session_details_form_section`, text, required): Enter the date of the mentoring session.
* **Start Time** (`session_details_form_section`, text, required): Enter the start time of the mentoring session.
* **End Time** (`session_details_form_section`, text, required): Enter the end time of the mentoring session.
* **Session Details** (`session_details_form_section`, text, required): Enter any other relevant details about the session.
