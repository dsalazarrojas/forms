# Client Attendance Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client attendance information for various programs offered by the organization. It's a one-time requirement for clients to confirm their attendance and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, phone number, and emergency contact information.
2. Select the program type you're joining from the list provided.
3. Choose your preferred session frequency and time.
4. Read and agree to the cancellation policy, late arrival policy, and other terms of participation.
5. Sign the electronic signature field with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`client_first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your personal first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`client_last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your personal last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the best number to reach you.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, **Required**): Choose the program you're joining from the provided list.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you plan to start the program.
* **Expected Session Frequency** (`session_frequency`, select_one, **Required**): Choose how often you plan to attend sessions.
* **Preferred Session Time** (`preferred_session_time`, select_one, **Optional**): Select the time slot that works best for you.
* **I Understand the Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy_understanding`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you understand the 24-hour notice policy for cancellations.
* **I Understand Late Arrival Policy** (`late_arrival_policy`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you're aware that sessions start on time and late arrivals may result in session forfeiture.
* **I Understand Missed Session Make-Up Policy** (`missed_session_makeup`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you're aware that missed sessions cannot always be made up, subject to availability.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **Required**): Enter the best number to reach your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, **Required**): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **Optional**): If applicable, list any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
* **Liability Waiver and Terms of Participation** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, **Required**): Confirm that you've read and agree to the terms of participation.
* **Photo Release Authorization** (`photo_release`, select_one, **Optional**): If you wish to allow us to use your photo for marketing purposes, select "Yes, I Authorize."
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date and time you sign this agreement.
* **Electronic Signature** (`electronic_signature`, text, **Required**): Type your full name as your electronic signature.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure you're providing accurate information to ensure a smooth experience.
