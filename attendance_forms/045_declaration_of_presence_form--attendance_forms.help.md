> # Declaration Of Presence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Declaration Of Presence Form" is a document that is used to record the attendance and presence of individuals at a given event or meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in the "Form Title" field with a brief title for the event or meeting.
2. Enter the date and time of the event or meeting in the "Form Date" and "Form Time" fields.
3. In the "Attendees" field, select all the individuals who are present from the available options.
4. Enter the email addresses of the attendees in the "Attendee's Email" field, separated by commas.
5. Enter the phone numbers of the attendees in the "Attendee's Phone" field, separated by commas.
6. Enter the venue's address in the "Venue Address" field.
7. Add any additional notes about the event or meeting in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Header (Page 1: 1)
* **Form Header**: This field is used to display the title of the form, but it is not required for form submission.
### Form Title (Page 1: 2)
* **Form Title**: Enter a brief title for the event or meeting.
### Form Date (Page 1: 3)
* **Form Date**: Enter the date of the event or meeting.
### Form Time (Page 1: 4)
* **Form Time**: Enter the time of the event or meeting.
### Attendees (Page 1: 5)
* **Attendees (Select Multiple)**: Select all the individuals who are present from the available options.
### Attendee's Email (Page 1: 6)
* **Attendee's Email**: Enter the email addresses of the attendees, separated by commas.
### Attendee's Phone (Page 1: 7)
* **Attendee's Phone**: Enter the phone numbers of the attendees, separated by commas.
### Venue Address (Page 1: 8)
* **Venue Address**: Enter the address of the venue where the event or meeting took place.
### Notes (Page 1: 9)
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the event or meeting.
### Submit (Page 1: 10)
* **Submit**: Select "Proceed" to submit the form, or "Cancel" to discard any changes made.
