# Playgroup Child Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Playgroup Child Attendance Form is used by parents or caregivers to report their child's attendance and provide necessary information for pick-up and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the child's information, including their name and any relevant details.
2. Choose the child's drop-off time and select the correct option from the list.
3. Enter your contact number and email address to ensure we can reach you.
4. Enter the pick-up time and details, if necessary.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your child's attendance and information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child Information (Text)**: Enter your child's name, ensuring it matches their official name.
* **Drop Off (Select One)**: Choose the time your child will be dropped off, selecting from:
	+ Early Morning
	+ Late Morning
	+ Not Yet Arrived
* **Child Name (Text)**: This field is used to verify the child's name; please enter their correct name.
* **Parents Info (Text)**: Enter any additional information about your child's parents or guardians, if necessary.
* **Parent Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the first parent or guardian responsible for your child.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact number, making sure we can easily reach you.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address, allowing us to communicate with you efficiently.
* **Drop Off Time (Time)**: Enter the exact time your child will be dropped off.
* **Pick Up Time (Time)**: Enter the exact time your child will be picked up.
* **Pick Up Details (Text)**: Provide any additional details about pick-up, such as any allergies or special instructions.
* **Parent Signature (Note)**: Sign and date the form to confirm your child's attendance and information.
