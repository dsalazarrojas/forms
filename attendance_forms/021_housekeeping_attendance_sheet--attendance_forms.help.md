# Housekeeping Attendance Sheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for recording attendance information for housekeeping staff, including shift starts and ends, staff names, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Select the Date Shift Start

* **Date Shift Start (id: 1)**: This field is for selecting the start date of the shift. Please enter a date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".

### Step 2: Choose Your Staff Member

* **Staff Member (id: 2)**: Select your staff member from the available options. You can choose either Option 1 or Option 2.

### Step 3: Enter Your Name

* **Staff Name (id: 3)**: Type your name in this text field.

### Step 4: Select Shifts Assigned

* **Shifts Assigned (id: 4)**: Select the shifts you are assigned to from the available options. You can choose either Option 3 or Option 4.

### Step 5: Add Notes (Optional)

* **Shift Notes (id: 5)**: This is an optional field where you can add any notes related to your shift.

### Step 6: Record Shift End Time

* **Time Shift End (id: 6)**: Enter the end time of your shift in the format "HH:MM".

### Step 7: Select Date Shift End

* **Date Shift End (id: 7)**: Select the end date of your shift.

### Step 8: Update Your Status

* **Staff Status (id: 8)**: Select your current status from the available options. You can choose either Option 5 or Option 6.

## Tips
- Ensure you are using the correct date and time for your shift start and end.
- Double-check that you have selected the correct staff member and shifts.
- Use the shift notes field to add any important notes about your shift.
- Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.
