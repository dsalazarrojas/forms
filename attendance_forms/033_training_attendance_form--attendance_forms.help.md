# Training Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Training Attendance Form is designed to collect information about training 
attendance from employees. This form helps the management to track attendance 
records, which is essential for evaluating the effectiveness of training programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the relevant sections and fill in the required fields. 
    * The "Training Attendance Form Section" allows you to select whether you 
      attended the training or not.
2.  Fill in your employee ID and training ID.
3.  Enter the session ID of the training you attended.
4.  Mark 'Yes' or 'No' for attended.
5.  Fill in the date and time of the training.
6.  Add any comments about your attendance in the comments field.
7.  Ensure that you have filled in all the required fields and reviewed the form 
      carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Training Attendance Form Section** (`training_attendance_form_section`, select_multiple, 
    required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you attended the training and 'No' if you didn't.
*   **employee** (`employee`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter your employee ID.
*   **training_program** (`training_program`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have attended the training program.
*   **session** (`session`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have attended the session.
*   **attended** (`attended`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have attended the training.
*   **date** (`date`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the date of the training.
*   **time** (`time`, time, required: false):
    *   Enter the time of the training.
*   **comments** (`comments`, note, required: false):
    *   Add any comments or observations about your attendance.
*   **employee_id** (`employee_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter your employee ID.
*   **training_id** (`training_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the training ID.
*   **session_id** (`session_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the session ID.
*   **attended_id** (`attended_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the attended ID.
*   **created_by** (`created_by`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the created by ID.
*   **updated_by** (`updated_by`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the updated by ID.
*   **created_at** (`created_at`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the created at date.
*   **updated_at** (`updated_at`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the updated at date.
*   **form_id** (`form_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the form ID.
*   **user_id** (`user_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the user ID.
*   **user_group** (`user_group`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you have access to this form.
*   **user_group_id** (`user_group_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the user group ID.
*   **status** (`status`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Active' if the form is active.
*   **updated_at** (`updated_at`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the updated at date.
*   **deleted_at** (`deleted_at`, date, required: false):
    *   Enter the deleted at date.
*   **assigned_to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: false):
    *   Select 'Yes' if you are assigned to this form.
*   **assigned_to_id** (`assigned_to_id`, number, required: false):
    *   Enter the assigned to ID.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields carefully.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting.
*   If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant authorities 
    for clarification.
*   This form is for legitimate purposes only, and you must not use it for 
      any other reasons.
