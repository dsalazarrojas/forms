# Museum Exhibition Volunteer Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendance information from volunteers for museum exhibitions. Volunteers are asked to fill out this form to provide crucial data on their attendance, allowing the museum to accurately track volunteer hours and attendance statistics. This form is usually filled out by volunteers who have already been scheduled for a specific shift or exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have been scheduled for a specific exhibition and have received a unique Exhibition ID.
2. Fill out the form with the required information:
   - Exhibition ID: Enter the ID of the exhibition you have been scheduled for.
   - Volunteer Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
   - Shift Start Time and Shift End Time: Enter the start and end time of your scheduled shift.
   - Attendance Confirmed: Confirm your attendance by selecting 'True' if you attended or 'False' if you did not.
   - Comments: If you have any comments or feedback, please include them here.
   - Email and Phone: Enter your contact information so the museum can get in touch with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibition ID** (`1`, `number`, required: false): Enter the ID of the exhibition you were scheduled for. This is usually provided to you by the museum staff or through your schedule confirmation email.
* **Volunteer Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents. This will help the museum track your attendance and hours.
* **Shift Start Time** (`3`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start time of your scheduled shift. This is the time you were expected to start your volunteer work.
* **Shift End Time** (`4`, `time`, required: false): Enter the end time of your scheduled shift. This is the time you were expected to finish your volunteer work.
* **Attendance Confirmed** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your attendance by selecting 'True' if you attended or 'False' if you did not. This is crucial for the museum to track attendance statistics.
* **Comments** (`6`, `note`, required: false): If you have any comments or feedback on your experience, please include them here. This helps the museum identify areas for improvement.
* **Email** (`7`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so the museum can get in touch with you if needed.
* **Phone** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so the museum can contact you if there are any issues or opportunities.
