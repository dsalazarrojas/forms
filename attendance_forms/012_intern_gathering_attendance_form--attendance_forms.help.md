# Intern Gathering Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intern Gathering Attendance Form is a tool used to track the attendance of interns in a gathering or meeting. It helps in collecting information about the attendees, their roles, and the events they attended.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form on the **First Page**. This page has no specific instructions, so you can begin filling out the form from here.
2. Move on to the **Second Page**, which is a required page. Ensure that you fill out all the fields on this page as it contains crucial information about your attendance.
3. If necessary, move on to the **Third Page**, which is also required. Fill out any additional information that is not included on the previous pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page** (`first_page`, text, optional): This page is an introductory page where you can enter any additional information not covered by the other pages.
* **Second Page** (`second_page`, text, required): This page contains important information about your attendance, such as your role and event details.
* **Third Page** (`third_page`, text, required): This page has additional fields for providing more information about your attendance. Fill out this page if you have more details about your attendance that are not covered by the previous page.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill out all required fields on the **Second Page** and **Third Page** as they are crucial for accurate attendance tracking.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
