# Farming Cooperative Monthly Meeting Attendance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the attendance of members at a monthly meeting of a farming cooperative. It is essential to complete the form accurately to ensure that attendance records are up to date and reflect the actual attendance of members.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, ensure you are on the correct form, "Farming Cooperative Monthly Meeting Attendance".
2.  Click on each field to select the relevant option or type in your response as prompted.
3.  Select the correct meeting date from the calendar provided in the "Meeting Date" field.
4.  In the "Member Name" field, type the name of the member who attended the meeting.
5.  Select "Yes" or "No" from the options for "Member Position" to indicate whether the member attended their position at the meeting.
6.  For "Attendance Status", select all relevant options for the member, such as "True" for attending, "False" for not attending, and "Maybe" for unsure attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting that you attended. Select the correct date from the provided calendar.
*   **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required: false): Type the name of the member who attended the meeting.
*   **Member Position** (`member_position`, select_one, required: false): Choose "Yes" if the member attended their position at the meeting, or "No" if they did not.
*   **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose "True" if the member attended the meeting, "False" if they did not, and "Maybe" for uncertain attendance.
