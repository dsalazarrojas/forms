# School Meeting Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Meeting Attendance Form is a tool used by school administrators to track student attendance at school meetings. This form is designed to collect information about each student's presence, comments, and any additional details they may want to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your personal information in the **Personal Information** section.
2.  Indicate your **Attendance Status** by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the corresponding field.
3.  If you did attend the meeting, you can provide any **Comments** about your experience.
4.  In the **Meeting Details** section, add any relevant information about the meeting.
5.  In the **Additional Information** section, provide any other details you want to share with the school administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information** (`submitter_information`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can enter your name and any other details about yourself.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you attended the meeting, and "No" if you did not.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, optional): Write any comments about the meeting you attended.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional details about the meeting that may be relevant.
* **Additional Information** (`submitter_information_2`, `text`, optional): This is a free-form field for any extra information you want to share with the school administrators.
