# Church Attendance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Attendance Registration Form is designed to help visitors register their attendance after visiting the church. It collects information about their visit, personal details, and preferences to ensure a smooth and welcoming experience for all visitors.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your personal details and preferences.
2. Select the correct options for each field according to your experiences and preferences.
3. Be sure to provide your full name and a valid phone number.
4. Indicate how you would like to be contacted (email, phone call, or no contact).
5. If you have any prayer requests, please include them in the comments section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of your visit to the church.
* **Attendance Type**: Select how you would like to be contacted after visiting the church.
	+ **Is this your first time visiting us?**: Yes/No - If you're a first-time visitor, we'd love to know.
	+ **I have visited before**: Select this if you've visited before.
* **Personal Information**: Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
* **Family Members attending with you**: List the names and ages of family members attending with you.
* **Interests and Requests**: Select all that apply from the list of interests.
* **Best way to contact you**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted (email, phone call, or no contact).
* **Prayer Request**: If you have a prayer request, write it in the comments section.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Select how you became aware of the church.
* **Any questions or comments?**: Share any questions or comments you may have.
* **Follow-up Permission**: Indicate if you'd like someone to follow up with you this week.
* **Referral Source**: Leave a space for any referral information.
* **Any Questions or Comments?**: Share any questions or comments you may have.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Please provide clear and concise answers.
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the church staff.
