# Kindergarten Safety Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Safety Attendance Form is used to record and track the attendance of children in a kindergarten setting, ensuring their safe and accurate care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the checkin and pickup times of the children, indicating when they arrive and leave the kindergarten.
2. Enter the child's name and ID number for accurate identification.
3. Provide the contact person's name, email, and phone number for easy communication.
4. Add any additional notes about the child's day, if necessary.
5. Select the name and ID of the staff member who is responsible for the child's care.
6. Indicate the pickup method, which could be a car or a walking escort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Checkin Time** (`checkin`): Enter the time when the child arrives at the kindergarten.
* **Pickup Time** (`pickup`): Enter the time when the child leaves the kindergarten.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`): Enter the name of the child being recorded.
* **Child ID** (`child_id`): Enter the ID number of the child for accurate identification.
* **Contact Person** (`contact`): Enter the name of the person who is responsible for the child's contact information.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter the email address of the person who is responsible for the child's contact information.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person who is responsible for the child's contact information.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about the child's day, if necessary.
* **Staff Member Name** (`staff_member_name`): Enter the name of the staff member who is responsible for the child's care.
* **Staff Member ID** (`staff_member_id`): Enter the ID number of the staff member who is responsible for the child's care.
* **Pickup Method** (`pickup_method`): Select the method of pickup, which could be a car or a walking escort.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately record the checkin and pickup times to ensure the child's safe and accurate accounting.
- Always provide the most up-to-date and accurate contact information for the child's contact person.
- Use the "Notes" field to add any important or additional information about the child's day.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the designated authority for clarification.
