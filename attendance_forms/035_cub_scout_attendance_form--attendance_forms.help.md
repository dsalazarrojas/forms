# Cub Scout Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cub Scout Attendance Form is a simple form designed to track the attendance of Cub Scouts in activities. It helps organizers and leaders keep up-to-date records of who was present, who was absent, and any relevant comments or achievements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Scout Name** of the Cub Scout who attended the activity.
2. Select the **Activity Type** (e.g., Den Meeting, Pack Meeting, Committee Meeting, or Council Meeting).
3. Enter the **Date** of attendance in the desired format.
4. Add any relevant **Notes** or comments about the activity.
5. Enter the **Parent's Name** of the Cub Scout.
6. Add the **Parent's Email** and **Phone** for easy contact.
7. Select the **Scout Rank** of the Cub Scout (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
8. Choose the **Attendance Status** (e.g., Present, Absent, Late, or Excused).
9. List any **Achievements** earned by the Cub Scout during the activity (e.g., Star, Badge, Rank advancement, or Other).
10. Add any additional **Comments** about the activity or the Cub Scout's experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Scout Name**: Enter the name of the Cub Scout who attended the activity.
* **Activity Type**: Select the type of activity the Cub Scout participated in (e.g., Den Meeting, Pack Meeting, Committee Meeting, or Council Meeting).
* **Date**: Enter the date of attendance in the desired format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Notes**: Add any comments or observations about the activity.
* **Parent's Name**: Enter the name of the Cub Scout's parent or guardian.
* **Email**: Add the parent's email address for easy contact.
* **Phone**: Add the parent's phone number.
* **Scout Rank**: Select the rank of the Cub Scout (e.g., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.).
* **Attendance Status**: Choose the status of the Cub Scout's attendance (e.g., Present, Absent, Late, or Excused).
* **Achievements**: List any achievements earned by the Cub Scout during the activity (e.g., Star, Badge, Rank advancement, or Other).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the activity or the Cub Scout's experience.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately to maintain an up-to-date and reliable attendance record.
* Verify that you're not duplicating an existing entry.
