# TITLE - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees, including their name, date and time of attendance, location details, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Select the date and time of your attendance using the "Date and Time" field.
3. Provide your location details in the "Location Details" field.
4. Optionally, add any comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)** (`attendee_1`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID, ticket, or any other official document.
* **Date and Time (Date)** (`date_and_time`, required: false): Select the date and time you attended the event.
* **Location Details (Text)** (`location_details`, required: false): Describe the location where you attended the event.
* **Comments (Note)** (`comments`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
