# K12 Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The K12 Attendance Form is a daily attendance tracking tool for students at K12 institutions. It helps administrators and teachers monitor student attendance and maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Select the date of the day for which you are reporting attendance.
3. Enter the time the student arrived at school.
4. Enter the time the student left school.
5. Enter the student's ID number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (student_name)**: Enter the name of the student as it appears on their student ID card or in the school's database. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for quick reference.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date for which you are reporting attendance. This field is not required, but it's recommended for accurate tracking.
* **Time In (time_in)**: Enter the time the student arrived at school. This field is optional, but it helps in monitoring students' arrival times.
* **Time Out (time_out)**: Enter the time the student left school. This field is optional, but it's helpful for tracking students' departure times.
* **Student ID (student_id)**: Enter the student's ID number for identification purposes. This field is not required, but it's recommended for accurate tracking and record-keeping.

## Tips
* Complete this form once per day for each student to maintain accurate attendance records.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid errors in attendance tracking.
* Make sure to update the form for any changes in attendance, such as tardiness or absences.
