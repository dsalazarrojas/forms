# Perfect Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Perfect Attendance Form is designed to track attendance for events or activities where participants are required to sign in and sign out. This form is meant to be filled out by attendees to verify their presence during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the start date of the event or activity.
2. Enter the end date of the event or activity.
3. Enter your name to confirm your attendance.
4. Sign your name to confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`): Enter the date when the event started. This is usually the date when the activity or event began.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`): Enter the date when the event ended. This is usually the date when the activity or event concluded.
* **Attendees Name** (`attendees_name`): Enter your name to confirm your attendance. This field is used to identify who was present during the event.
* **Attendees Signature** (`attendees_signature`): Sign your name to confirm your attendance. This field is used as proof of your signature and attendance.
