# Community Meeting Resident Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track attendance at community meetings, gathering information on residents who attend, their contact details, and any additional notes or signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name as the resident who is attending the meeting.
2. Enter your address for verification purposes.
3. Provide your email address for meeting organizers to reach out with minutes or any important updates.
4. Enter the date of the meeting you are attending.
5. If applicable, indicate your arrival time at the meeting.
6. If not attending, specify your reason for absence (optional).
7. Choose your role in the meeting (Attendee, Speaker, or Organizer).
8. Sign your name to confirm your attendance.
9. Add any additional notes related to your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident name** (`resident_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the resident who is attending the meeting.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for verification purposes, this will help confirm your attendance.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, optional): If you would like to receive meeting minutes or updates, provide your email address here.
* **Meeting date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting you are attending.
* **Arrival time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): If applicable, indicate your arrival time at the meeting.
* **Reason for absence** (`reason_for_absence`, text, optional): If you are not attending the meeting, provide a reason for your absence.
* **Role in meeting** (`role_in_meeting`, select_one, optional): Choose your role in the meeting (Attendee, Speaker, or Organizer).
* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your attendance.
* **Additional notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes related to your attendance.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to ensure accurate attendance records.
- If you're unable to attend, provide a valid reason for your absence.
- Meeting organizers may use your email address to share meeting minutes or updates with you.
