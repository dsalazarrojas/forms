# Congregational Membership And Attendance Statistics Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Congregational Membership and Attendance Statistics Entry Form is designed for congregational administrators or secretaries to report monthly membership and attendance statistics. This form helps the organization track and maintain accurate records of congregational growth, attendance patterns, and other relevant metrics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form for each month**: This form is intended for monthly reporting, so ensure you fill it out at the end of each month.
2. **Provide accurate data**: Double-check your numbers to ensure they are correct and up-to-date.
3. **Submit the form**: Once complete, submit the form to ensure your data is recorded and tracked accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report Period**: Select the month you are reporting for from the following options: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, or December.
* **Congregation Name**: Enter the name of your congregation.
* **Total Members at Start of Month**: Enter the total number of members at the beginning of the reporting month.
* **New Members - Baptism**: Enter the number of new members who were baptized during the reporting month.
* **New Members - Transfer**: Enter the number of new members who joined the congregation through transfer during the reporting month.
* **Total Membership Removals**: Enter the total number of membership removals (e.g., deaths, transfers, withdrawals) for the reporting month.
* **Total Members at End of Month**: Enter the total number of members at the end of the reporting month.
* **Average Sunday Service Attendance**: Enter the average attendance for Sunday services for the reporting month.
* **Average Mid-week Service Attendance**: Enter the average attendance for mid-week services for the reporting month (if applicable).
* **First-time Visitors this Month**: Enter the number of first-time visitors for the reporting month.
* **Attendance for Special Events**: Enter the attendance for special events, such as holidays or concerts, for the reporting month.
* **I certify that these statistics are accurate to the best of my knowledge**: Select "I so certify" to confirm that you've reviewed and verified the accuracy of your reported data.
* **Person Submitting this Data**: Enter your name and title/position.
* **Role/Position**: Enter your role or position within the congregation.

## Tips

* Double-check your numbers and data for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to report data for each month, as this helps track congregational growth and attendance patterns.
* If you're unsure about any field or data, contact your church leadership or administration for guidance.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions or authority to report for your congregation.

This help guide should provide a clear understanding of how to complete the Congregational Membership and Attendance Statistics Entry Form accurately and efficiently.
