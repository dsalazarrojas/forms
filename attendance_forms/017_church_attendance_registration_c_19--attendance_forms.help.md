# Church Attendance Registration C 19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Church Attendance Registration C 19" form is designed to collect information from attendees for contact tracing and health screening purposes during church services. This form helps the church administration and staff to keep track of who attended a service and ensure the safety of all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of attendance from the calendar.
2. Choose the correct service time from the available options.
3. Provide your full name, email address, and phone number.
4. Enter the number of people in your household attending the service.
5. Answer the health screening questions:
	* If you are experiencing any symptoms, select "Yes - I have symptoms".
	* If you have been exposed to someone with a confirmed case, select "Yes - Known Exposure".
	* If you have traveled internationally in the last 14 days, select "True".
6. Confirm your agreement to follow safety protocols and consent to use your information for contact tracing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of Attendance**: Select the date you plan to attend the church service.
* **Service Time**: Choose the service time that works best for you. Options include 8:00 AM, 10:30 AM, and 6:00 PM (Midweek).
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Provide your valid email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can reach you if necessary.
* **Number of people in your household attending**: Include yourself in the count.
* **Health Screening**: Answer the health screening questions truthfully:
	+ **Are you currently experiencing any symptoms?**: If you are, select "Yes - I have symptoms".
	+ **Have you been exposed to someone with a confirmed case recently?**: Select "Yes - Known Exposure" if you have.
	+ **Have you traveled internationally in the last 14 days?**: Choose "True" if you have.
* **I agree to follow all safety protocols including mask-wearing**: Confirm your agreement to follow safety protocols.
* **I consent to my info being used for contact tracing if needed**: Check "True" to allow us to use your information for contact tracing.
* **Do you have any special seating or accessibility needs?**: If you have any special needs, provide a brief explanation.
