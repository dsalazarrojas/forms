# Special Exhibition Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Special Exhibition Attendance Form is designed to collect attendance data for exhibitions. This form helps organizers and coordinators to track visitor information, including exhibition name, contact details, arrival and departure dates and times, and other essential information.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Exhibition Name** field with the name of the exhibition.
2.  Enter the **Visitor Name** field with the name of the person attending the exhibition.
3.  Provide the **Email** and **Phone** fields with the contact information of the visitor, if applicable.
4.  Enter the **Visitor Start Date** and **Visitor Finish Date** fields with the dates of the visitor's visit.
5.  Enter the **Visitor Start Time** and **Visitor Finish Time** fields with the start and finish times of the visitor's visit.
6.  In the **Visitor Notes** field, provide any additional comments or information about the visit.
7.  Select the **RSVP Type** field according to your preferred method of contact.
8.  Enter the **Visitor RSVP Date** field with the date you plan to RSVP.
9.  Enter the **Arrival Date** field with the date of arrival.
10. Enter the **Arrival Time** field with the arrival time.
11. In the **Group Size** field, select the number of people attending in your group.
12. Select the **Arrival Method** field according to your mode of transportation.
13. Finally, enter any additional **Arrival Details** in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibition Name** (Exhibition Name, text, required): Enter the name of the exhibition.
* **Visitor Name** (Visitor Name, text, required): Enter the name of the person attending the exhibition.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the visitor, if applicable.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the visitor, if applicable.
* **Visitor Start Date** (Visitor Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the visitor's visit start time.
* **Visitor Start Time** (Visitor Start Time, time, optional): Enter the start time of the visitor's visit.
* **Visitor Finish Date** (Visitor Finish Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the visitor's visit finish time.
* **Visitor Finish Time** (Visitor Finish Time, time, optional): Enter the finish time of the visitor's visit.
* **Visitor Notes** (Visitor Notes, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about the visit.
* **RSVP Type** (RSVP Type, select_one, optional): Select the preferred method of contact (RSVP In Person, RSVP By Phone, RSVP By Email).
* **Visitor RSVP Date** (Visitor RSVP Date, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to RSVP.
* **Arrival Date** (Arrival Date, date, optional): Enter the date of arrival.
* **Arrival Time** (Arrival Time, time, optional): Enter the arrival time.
* **Group Size** (Group Size, select_multiple, optional): Select the number of people attending in your group.
* **Arrival Method** (Arrival Method, select_one, optional): Select the mode of transportation used to arrive (Public Transportation, Private Car, Private Bus, Private Boat).
* **Arrival Details** (Arrival Details, text, optional): Enter any additional details about arrival.

This guide helps ensure that you complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
