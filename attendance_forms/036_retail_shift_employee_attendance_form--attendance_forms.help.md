# Retail Shift Employee Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record retail shift employee attendance data, ensuring accurate and transparent reporting of employee hours worked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the store ID to select the specific store location where you worked.
2. Select the date of your shift.
3. Enter the start and end times of your shift.
4. Select the location where you worked.
5. Mark your attendance status as Active or Inactive.
6. Provide a reason for absence (if applicable).
7. Add any comments or notes about your shift.
8. Sign and date the form as the employee.
9. Your manager will sign and date the form to confirm their approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store**: This field is used to select the store location where you worked.
* **Date**: Enter the date of your shift in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Location**: Select the location where you worked.
* **Attendance Status**: Mark your attendance status as Active or Inactive.
* **Reason for Absence (optional)**: Provide a brief explanation for why you were absent if applicable.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Employee Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your attendance.
* **Manager Signature**: Your manager will sign and date the form to approve your attendance.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Title**: Enter your manager's title.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of the store where you worked.
* **Location Name**: Enter the name of the location where you worked.
* **Shift Number**: Enter the number of your shift.
* **Shift Start Date**: Enter the date of your shift start date.
* **Shift End Date**: Enter the date of your shift end date.
* **Total Hours Worked**: Enter the total number of hours you worked.
* **Break Start Time**: Enter the start time of your break.
* **Break End Time**: Enter the end time of your break.
* **Total Break Time**: Enter the total time spent on break (minutes).
* **Shift Length**: Enter the duration of your shift in the selected unit (minutes, hours, or days).
* **Shift Length Unit**: Select the unit of time for your shift duration.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your shift.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional or not required, meaning you can leave them blank or skip them if not applicable. However, please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to ensure accurate attendance reporting.
