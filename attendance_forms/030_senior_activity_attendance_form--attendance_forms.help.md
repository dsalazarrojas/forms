# Senior Activity Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Activity Attendance Form is used to record the attendance of senior participants in activities and events. This form is required for all senior participants to ensure accurate attendance tracking and reporting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the event that the participant attended in the "Event" field.
2. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Choose the program type (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) in the "Program Type" field.
4. Select the staff members who attended in the "Staff" field.
5. Choose the attendance type (Attended, Missed, or Unable to Attend) in the "Attendance Type" field.
6. Enter the date and time of the activity in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
7. Enter the location of the activity in the "Location" field.
8. Enter any comments or notes related to the activity in the "Comments" field.
9. If applicable, enter the participant's email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
10. If applicable, select the staff members who will be providing comments or updates about the activity in the "Staff Comments" and "Staff Title" fields.
11. Finally, click "Submit" to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, required): This is the participant's name who attended the activity. Please enter their full name as it appears on their record.
* **Event** (`event`, select_one, required): This is the name of the event that the participant attended. Select the correct event from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): This is the date of the activity. Enter the date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): This is the time of the activity. Enter the time in the format HH:MM.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, required): This is the location where the activity took place. Enter a brief description of the location.
* **Activity** (`Activity`, text, required): This is a brief description of the activity. Enter a short summary of the activity.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): This field is for any comments or notes related to the activity.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): This is the participant's email address. If you have it, please enter it.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): This is the participant's phone number. If you have it, please enter it.
* **Submitted** (`Submitted`, date, required): This is the date and time the form was submitted. This will automatically be populated when you click "Submit".
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): This is the person who submitted the form. If you have it, please enter their name.
* **Program** (`Program`, select_one, required): This is the program that the activity belongs to. Select the correct program from the dropdown list.
* **Staff** (`Staff`, select_multiple, required): This is the list of staff members who attended the activity. Select the correct staff members from the dropdown list.
* **Attendance Type** (`Attendance Type`, select_one, required): This is the attendance type (Attended, Missed, or Unable to Attend). Select the correct attendance type.
* **Attendance Notes** (`Attendance Notes`, note, optional): This field is for any notes or comments related to the participant's attendance.
* **Staff Comments** (`Staff Comments`, note, optional): This field is for any comments or notes from the staff members who attended the activity.
* **Staff Name** (`Staff Name`, text, optional): This is the name of the staff member who attended the activity. If you have it, please enter their name.
* **Staff Title** (`Staff Title`, text, optional): This is the title of the staff member who attended the activity. If you have it, please enter their title.
* **Participant Count** (`Participant Count`, text, optional): This is the number of participants who attended the activity. If you have it, please enter the count.
* **Program Type** (`Program Type`, select_one, required): This is the type of program the activity belongs to (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening). Select the correct program type.
* **Session Duration** (`Session Duration`, text, optional): This is the duration of the session. If you have it, please enter the duration.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, text, optional): This is the time of the session. If you have it, please enter the time.
* **Submitted By Email** (`Submitted By Email`, email, optional): This is the email address of the person who submitted the form. If you have it, please enter it.
* **Submitted By Phone** (`Submitted By Phone`, text, optional): This is the phone number of the person who submitted the form. If you have it, please enter it.
* **Participant Comments** (`Participant Comments`, note, optional): This field is for any comments or notes from the participant.
* **Staff Name** (`Staff Name`, text, optional): This is the name of the staff member who will be providing comments or updates about the activity. If you have it, please enter their name.
* **Staff Title** (`Staff Title`, text, optional): This is the title of the staff member who will be providing comments or updates about the activity. If you have it, please enter their title.
