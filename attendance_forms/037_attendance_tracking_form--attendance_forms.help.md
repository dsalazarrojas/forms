# Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Attendance Tracking Form is designed to record attendance and participation details for a program or course session. It is completed by the instructor or facilitator after the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the program information, including the program name and code.
2. Enter the instructor's name, as this is the person facilitating the session.
3. Record the date and start time of the session.
4. Record the location of the session.
5. Enter the participant's name and ID.
6. Mark the participant's check-in status (Present, Absent, or Late).
7. If late, record the check-in time and minutes late.
8. Provide a reason for absence, if applicable.
9. Record participation notes and level (e.g., Full Participation, Active, Moderate, Minimal, or Observer).
10. Rate the engagement quality (e.g., Excellent, Good, Adequate, Fair, Poor).
11. Mark if the participant completed assigned work or tasks.
12. Rate the participant's conduct and behavior (e.g., Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Needs Improvement, Poor).
13. Add any additional instructor notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program or course.
* **Program Code**: Enter the course or program code.
* **Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the instructor or facilitator.
* **Session Date**: Record the date of the session.
* **Session Time**: Record the start time of the session.
* **Session Location**: Enter the location of the session.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the participant's student or employee ID.
* **Check-In Status**: Mark the participant's check-in status (Present, Absent, or Late).
* **Check-In Time**: Record the time of arrival, if late.
* **Minutes Late**: If late, record the number of minutes late.
* **Reason for Absence**: If applicable, provide a reason for the participant's absence.
* **Participation Notes**: Record any additional notes about the participant's participation.
* **Participation Level**: Rate the participant's participation intensity (e.g., Full Participation, Active, Moderate, Minimal, or Observer).
* **Engagement Quality**: Rate the participant's engagement quality (e.g., Excellent, Good, Adequate, Fair, Poor).
* **Assignment Completion**: Mark if the participant completed assigned work or tasks.
* **Conduct and Behavior**: Rate the participant's conduct and behavior (e.g., Excellent, Good, Acceptable, Needs Improvement, Poor).
* **Instructor Notes**: Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Follow-Up Action Required**: If needed, record any follow-up actions required for the participant.
