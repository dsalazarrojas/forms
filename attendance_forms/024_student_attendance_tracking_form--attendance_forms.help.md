# Student Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to track student attendance, including presence or absence, reason for absence, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the "Student ID" field.
3. Select either "Absent" or "Present" in the "Attendance" field to indicate the student's presence.
4. If the student is absent, enter a reason for their absence in the "Reason Absent" field.
5. Select whether the student was checked in by themselves or another teacher/authority figure in the "Checked In By" field.
6. Choose any applicable status labels for the student in the "Label Student" field.
7. Select whether the student checked out themselves or another teacher/authority figure in the "Checked Out By" field.
8. Enter the time the student was checked in and checked out in the "Checked In Time" and "Checked Out Time" fields, respectively.
9. Add any comments or remarks about the student's attendance in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter the student's email address in the "Email" field.
11. Enter the student's phone number in the "Phone" field.
12. Choose the student's status in the "Student Status" field.
13. Select whether the student is active, inactive, or unknown in the "Student Status" field.
