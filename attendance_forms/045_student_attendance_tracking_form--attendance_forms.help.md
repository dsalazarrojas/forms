# Student Attendance Tracking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to track student attendance in a classroom or educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To use this form, please fill in the required fields.
2. Select "Present" or "Absent" to indicate the student's attendance status for the given date.
3. If the student is marked as "Absent", provide a reason for their absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`, date, required): Enter the date of attendance.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Present/Absent** (`present_absent`, select_one, required): Choose "Present" if the student attended the class, or "Absent" if they did not.
* **Reason for Absent** (`reason_for_absent`, text, optional): If the student is marked as "Absent", provide a reason for their absence.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have completed all required fields before submitting the form.
* If a student's attendance status changes, update the form accordingly.
* Review the form regularly to ensure accurate and up-to-date attendance records.
