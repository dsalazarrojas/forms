# Culinary Class Student Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record students' attendance and notes for a culinary class or workshop. It helps instructors and workshop organizers keep track of who attended, their attendance status, and any notes or additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "student_name" field.
2. Enter the student's email address in the "email" field (if applicable).
3. Enter the student's phone number in the "phone" field (if applicable).
4. Select the date of the class or workshop that the student attended.
5. Select the time of the class or workshop that the student attended.
6. Enter any notes or additional information about the student's attendance in the "note" field.
7. Choose the name of the class or workshop the student is attending from the "student_class_name" field.
8. Enter the ID of the workshop the student is attending from the "student_workshop_id" field.
9. Choose the attendance status of the student from the "student_attendance_status" field (e.g., "Student attended", "Student did not attend", etc.).
10. Enter any additional information about the student's attendance in the "student_additional_info" field.
11. Enter the name of the host of the workshop from the "student_workshop_host" field.
12. Enter the name of the host of the workshop from the "workshop_host_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **student_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the student.
* **email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the student (if applicable).
* **phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of the student (if applicable).
* **date (Date)**: Select the date of the class or workshop that the student attended.
* **time (Time)**: Select the time of the class or workshop that the student attended.
* **note (Note)**: Enter any notes or additional information about the student's attendance.
* **student_class_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the class or workshop the student is attending.
* **student_workshop_id (Number)**: Enter the ID of the workshop the student is attending.
* **student_attendance_status (Select One)**: Choose the attendance status of the student (e.g., "Student attended", "Student did not attend", etc.).
* **student_additional_info (Text)**: Enter any additional information about the student's attendance.
* **student_culinary_class_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the class or workshop the student is attending.
* **student_workshop_host (Text)**: Enter the name of the host of the workshop.
* **workshop_host_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the host of the workshop.
