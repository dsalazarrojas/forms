# Group Participation Log - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Group Participation Log form is a document used to record and track group participation, attendance and engagement levels within a group setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form to understand the fields and their intended use.
2. Select the applicable response options for the participant list field.
3. Enter the date and time of the recorded participation.
4. Ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant List** (`participant_list`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether the participant attended or not by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
* **Participant List Hint** (`participant_list_hint`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional information or context that may be useful for understanding participant engagement levels.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the recorded participation in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the recorded participation in the format 'HH:MM:SS'. 

## Tips
* Ensure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Avoid duplicating entries for the same individual or group.
* Use this form for accurate and reliable data collection.
