# Attendance Scorecard Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Attendance Scorecard Form is a tool used to record and evaluate employee attendance and performance metrics. It helps track employee's attendance, punctuality, and overall performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's details, including their name, participant ID, and department or team.
2. Enter the review period dates, including the start and end dates.
3. Record the scorecard date when you completed this form.
4. Enter the total working days, days attended, days missed, days late, and excused absences.
5. Rate the employee's attendance, punctuality, reliability, and engagement.
6. Add any comments, observations, and recommendations for improvement.
7. Summarize your overall assessment of the employee's attendance and performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the participant's employee or member ID.
* **Department or Team**: Enter the department or team the participant belongs to.
* **Manager or Supervisor**: Enter the name of the participant's manager or supervisor.
* **Review Period Start Date**: Enter the start date of the review period.
* **Review Period End Date**: Enter the end date of the review period.
* **Scorecard Date**: Enter the date when you completed this form.
* **Total Working Days**: Enter the total number of working days.
* **Days Present**: Enter the number of days the participant attended.
* **Days Absent**: Enter the number of days the participant missed.
* **Days Late**: Enter the number of days the participant arrived late.
* **Excused Absences**: Enter the number of excused absences.
* **Attendance Score**: Rate the participant's overall attendance (1-100).
* **Punctuality Score**: Rate the participant's punctuality (1-100).
* **Reliability Score**: Rate the participant's reliability (1-100).
* **Engagement Score**: Rate the participant's engagement (1-100).
* **Attendance Strengths**: Describe what the participant does well.
* **Areas for Improvement**: Describe areas where the participant needs to improve.
* **Overall Assessment**: Summarize your assessment of the participant's attendance and performance.
* **Recommended Actions**: Suggest next steps or interventions for the participant.
