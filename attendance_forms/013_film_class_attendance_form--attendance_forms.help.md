# Film Class Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Film Class Attendance Form is used to record participant attendance and feedback for film classes. This form is used to track attendance and gather feedback for film classes, helping instructors and administrators evaluate class effectiveness and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant course attendance options for each participant.
2. Record participant participation using the corresponding number.
3. Provide session feedback by selecting a time slot that matches the session duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Attendance** (`course_attendance`, Number, Optional): Enter the participant's attendance record for the course. This field is used to track attendance and help instructors monitor participation.
* **Participant Participation** (`participant_participation`, Number, Optional): Record the participant's level of participation in the class, using the corresponding number value.
* **Session Feedback** (`session_feedback`, Time, Optional): Select a time slot to provide feedback about the session, matching the session duration. This will help instructors and administrators schedule future sessions and make adjustments as needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and honest feedback to help improve the quality of film classes.
* Use the correct attendance and participation numbers to ensure accurate records.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your instructor or class administrator for guidance.
