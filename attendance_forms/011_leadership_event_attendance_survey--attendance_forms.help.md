# Leadership Event Attendance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Event Attendance Survey is designed to gather feedback from attendees to evaluate the overall quality and effectiveness of leadership events. This form is crucial in helping us understand what worked well and what could be improved for future events.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete the survey once after each event.
- Take a few minutes to review each question carefully and provide honest feedback.
- If you have attended multiple sessions, answer the questions for each session separately.
- If a question does not apply, mark the corresponding field "N/A" or provide a brief explanation in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Overall Event Rating** (`overall_event_rating`, select_one, required): This question asks for your overall rating of the event, considering both the content and the organization. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Session 1 Title** (`session_1_title`, text, optional): If you attended Session 1, provide the title of the session. If not applicable, enter "N/A".
* **Session 1 Rating** (`session_1_rating`, select_one, optional): If you attended Session 1, rate the session's quality. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Total Sessions Attended** (`total_sessions_attended`, number, optional): If you attended multiple sessions, enter the total number of sessions you attended. If not applicable, enter "N/A".
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name, which will help us identify your responses.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): If you have an email address, enter it to help us contact you with follow-up questions or to send you a summary of the survey results.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share your thoughts, suggestions, or any other feedback about the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or observations that may not fit in the other fields.
* **Overall Event Rating 2** (`overall_event_rating_2`, select_one, optional): This question is similar to the first one, but for the second session (if any). If not applicable, mark it "N/A".
* **Total Sessions Attended 2** (`total_sessions_attended_2`, number, optional): If you attended multiple sessions, enter the total number of sessions you attended for the second session (if any). If not applicable, mark it "N/A".
* **Session 2 Title** (`session_2_title`, text, optional): If you attended Session 2, provide the title of the session. If not applicable, enter "N/A".
* **Session 2 Rating** (`session_2_rating`, select_one, optional): If you attended Session 2, rate the session's quality. Please select one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
