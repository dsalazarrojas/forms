# Career Fair Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is meant to capture your information and preferences to help us better organize and prepare for the career fair. Please fill out this form carefully and honestly to ensure you can take full advantage of the opportunities available.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
2. Enter your information in the text fields.
3. Make sure to submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Career Fair Name** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the career fair you're attending.
* **Career Fair Date** (2, date, required): Enter the date of the career fair you're attending.
* **Full Name** (3, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter your email address, as we'll use it to send you event details.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, required): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Type of Attendee** (6, select_one, required): Select your category from the options provided:
	+ Student
	+ Recent Graduate
	+ Job Seeker
	+ Career Changer
	+ Professional Networking
	+ Other
* **Education Level** (7, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education completed:
	+ High School Diploma
	+ Some College
	+ Associate Degree
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ PhD or Doctorate
	+ Vocational or Trade Certification
* **Current Major or Field of Study** (8, text, optional): If you're a student or recent graduate, enter your current major or field of study.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (9, date, optional): If you're a student, enter your expected graduation date.
* **Current Employment Status** (10, select_one, required): Select your current employment status:
	+ Currently Employed
	+ Unemployed - seeking work
	+ Unemployed - not seeking work
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Student
	+ Other
* **Current Job Title** (11, text, optional): If you're employed, enter your current job title.
* **Industries of Interest** (12, select_multiple, required): Select all the industries that interest you:
	+ Technology and IT
	+ Healthcare and Wellness
	+ Finance and Banking
	+ Engineering
	+ Business and Management
	+ Marketing and Advertising
	+ Sales
	+ Education
	+ Government and Public Service
	+ Manufacturing and Production
	+ Retail and Customer Service
	+ Non-profit and Community
* **Types of Positions Interested In** (13, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of positions that interest you:
	+ Entry-level
	+ Internship
	+ Co-op position
	+ Contract or Temporary
	+ Full-time permanent
	+ Part-time
	+ Remote positions
	+ Management or Leadership
* **Salary Expectations** (14, select_one, optional): Select your annual salary range you're seeking:
	+ Under 30k
	+ 30k-50k
	+ 50k-75k
	+ 75k-100k
	+ 100k-150k
	+ 150k+
	+ Open to offers
* **Specific Companies You Want to Meet** (15, text, optional): List any companies participating in the fair that interest you.
* **Key Skills or Competencies** (16, text, optional): Highlight your key skills or competencies.
* **LinkedIn Profile or Portfolio URL** (17, text, optional): Link to your professional profile or portfolio.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance?** (18, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes - need shuttle service
	+ Yes - need parking information
	+ No - have own transportation
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (19, text, optional): If the event includes meal service, enter any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed?** (20, select_one, optional):
	+ Yes - please specify
	+ No - none needed
* **Specific Accessibility Needs** (21, text, optional): Describe any accessibility accommodations required.
* **Additional Information** (22, text, optional): Enter any additional information that the organizers should know.
* **I Accept the Event Terms and Conditions** (23, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I accept" to confirm you've read and accepted the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
