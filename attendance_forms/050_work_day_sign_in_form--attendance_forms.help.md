# Work Day Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to sign in and out of work, providing details such as their name, sign-in/sign-out time, department, and any relevant notes or dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your department from the options provided.
2. Enter your employee ID (optional).
3. Enter your employee name (there are multiple fields for this, likely for duplicate name checks or verification purposes).
4. Select your sign-in and sign-out times using the time input field.
5. Add any relevant notes or comments about your workday.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Department**: Select the department you belong to, which is necessary for attendance tracking and management.
* **Employee ID**: If available, enter your employee ID for identification purposes. This is optional.
* **Employee Name**: There are multiple fields for your employee name, likely for duplicate name checks or verification purposes. Please ensure they match your actual name.
* **Sign-in/Sign-out Time**: Select your sign-in and sign-out times using the time input field. This will help track your work hours accurately.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your workday, such as meetings or tasks completed.
* **Date**: Although not required, if you need to record a specific date, you can enter it here.
* **Submit**: Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form. 

Please note that this form is designed for internal use within an organization, and employees should complete it accurately for their records.
