# Art Reception Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help event organizers collect information about attendees' attendance to an art reception.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about your guests and dietary needs.
3. If you have any questions or special requests, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required): Please enter the date of your RSVP.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the primary guest.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the primary guest.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the primary guest.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company or organization you belong to.
* **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Select "Yes - Will Attend" if you are attending the reception, "No - Cannot Attend" if you cannot attend, or "Maybe" if you are unsure.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the total number of guests in your party.
* **Guest Names** (`guest_names`, text, optional): List the names of all guests in your party.
* **Reception Type** (`reception_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of reception you will attend (e.g., Opening Reception, Closing Reception, Artist Meet and Greet, etc.).
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the exhibition you will attend.
* **Reception Date** (`reception_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the reception.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, select_one, optional): Select the expected arrival time of your party (5:00 PM - 6:00 PM, 6:00 PM - 7:00 PM, 7:00 PM - 8:00 PM, 8:00 PM - 9:00 PM, or Flexible).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select all dietary restrictions that apply (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, text, optional): Provide additional information about your dietary needs if necessary.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred beverage type (Wine, Beer, Cocktails, Non-Alcoholic, or No Preference).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you have any accessibility needs, "False" otherwise.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your accessibility needs if necessary.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you need parking, "False" otherwise.
* **Coat Check Needed** (`coat_check_needed`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you need coat check, "False" otherwise.
* **Interested in Artwork** (`interested_in_artwork`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are interested in purchasing artwork, "False" or "Maybe" otherwise.
* **Press Attendance** (`press_attendance`, select_one, optional): Select "True" if you are attending as press, "False" otherwise.
* **Media Outlet** (`media_outlet`, text, optional): If attending as press, enter the name of the publication.
* **How Heard** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the reception (Email Invitation, Social Media, Gallery Website, Friend, Advertisement, or Other).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Use this field to provide any additional comments or requests.
