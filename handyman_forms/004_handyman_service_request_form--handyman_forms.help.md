# Handyman Service Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Handyman Service Request Form is designed for customers and users to submit service requests to the handyman services department. This form gathers information about the service request, including contact details, description, and service type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your contact information**: Provide your contact details, including your phone number and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. **Describe the issue**: Write a brief description of the problem you're experiencing in the "description" field.
3. **Specify the service request**: Choose the type of service you need from the "service_request_type" dropdown menu.
4. **Provide additional information**: If necessary, add any additional details about the service request in the "additional_information" field.
5. **Schedule a service request**: Select a date and time for the handyman to visit you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and phone number so we can contact you to schedule the service request.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the issue you're experiencing, so the handyman can understand the problem.
* **Service Requests** (`service_requests`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the service request.
* **Detailed Description** (`detailed_description`, text, required: false): If you have more details about the issue, provide them here.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you to confirm the service request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so we can contact you with any updates about the service request.
* **Service Request Date** (`service_request_date`, date, required: false): Select a date for the handyman to visit you.
* **Service Request Time** (`service_request_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time for the handyman to visit you.
* **Service Request Type** (`service_request_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of service you need from the dropdown menu:
	+ Handyman
	+ Electrician
	+ Plumber
	+ Carpenter
	+ Other
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): Provide any further details about the service request.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure we can process your service request efficiently.
- If you need to reschedule the service request, please contact us directly.
- We'll contact you to confirm the service request after receiving your form submission.
