# Pest Control Service Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request pest control services from our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the type of pest that you need help with (Ants, Roaches, Spiders, Termites, or Other).
2. Select all the locations where you would like the service to be performed (Home, Office, Other).
3. Choose the date for the service (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Enter your contact number if you'd like to be reached for any updates.
5. Enter your name for our records.
6. Select the time you'd like the service to take place (24hr format).
7. Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exterior Treatment Request** (`exterior_treatment_request`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the pest control request.
* **Pests** (`pest_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of pest you're dealing with (Ants, Roaches, Spiders, Termites, or Other).
* **Treatment Location** (`treatment_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations where the service is required (Home, Office, Other).
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, optional): Choose the date for the service (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for any updates.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name for our records.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, optional): Choose the time for the service (24hr format).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with our team.
