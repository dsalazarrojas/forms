# Fencing Permit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for a fencing permit application. You will be required to provide details about your application, property, and permit requirements to help the authorities process and manage your permit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your application name in the "Application Name" field.
2. If you are providing this information, fill in the "Property Address" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Owner Name" field.
4. Fill in your address in the "Owner Address" field.
5. Select the "Permit Type" field and choose whether you require a permit or not.
6. If you selected "Permit Type", you may be required to select additional options.
7. Fill in the "Property Size" field if your property is large enough.
8. Fill in the "Permit Cost" field if your permit has a fee.
9. If necessary, provide any information related to zoning regulations in the "Zoning Regulations" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Name** (text, required): Fill in the name of your application.
* **Property Address** (text, optional): If you are filling this form, fill in the address of the property where the fence is being installed or repaired.
* **Owner Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the owner of the property.
* **Owner Address** (text, required): Fill in your address.
* **Permit Type** (select, optional): Choose whether you require a permit or not. If you select "Yes", you will be required to select additional options.
* **Permit Type Options** (select multiple, optional): If you selected "Permit Type", select the options that apply to your permit.
* **Property Size** (number, optional): If your property is large enough, fill in the size of the property.
* **Permit Cost** (number, optional): If your permit has a fee, fill in the cost of the permit.
* **Zoning Regulations** (text, optional): If necessary, provide any information related to zoning regulations.
