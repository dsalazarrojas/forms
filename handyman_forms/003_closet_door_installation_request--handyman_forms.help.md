# Closet Door Installation Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for a closet door installation request. It is intended for customers to provide their contact information, specify the details of the closet door they need, and schedule the installation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the type of closet door you need, choosing from bi-fold, sliding, swing, pocket, or French doors.
3. Specify the material preference for your closet door, such as wood, composite, mirrored glass, clear glass, frosted glass, or vinyl.
4. Measure and enter the width and height of the closet opening in inches.
5. Indicate how many doors you need.
6. Choose a finish or color for your door, such as a paint color or stain.
7. Select a preferred installation date and time frame for the installation.
8. If necessary, specify any special installation instructions or requirements.
9. If applicable, indicate if you would like to reuse existing hardware if compatible.
10. Optionally, select a budget range for the installation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, required): Please enter the full address where the installation will occur.
* **Closet Door Type** (`door_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of closet door you need, such as bi-fold, sliding, swing, pocket, or French doors.
* **Door Material Preference** (`door_material`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred material for the closet door, such as wood, composite, mirrored glass, clear glass, frosted glass, or vinyl.
* **Finish or Color** (`door_finish`, text, optional): Enter the desired finish or color for your closet door.
* **Opening Width - Inches** (`opening_width`, number, required): Measure and enter the width of the closet opening in inches.
* **Opening Height - Inches** (`opening_height`, number, required): Measure and enter the height of the closet opening from the floor to the top in inches.
* **Number of Doors Needed** (`number_of_doors`, number, required): Indicate the number of doors you need.
* **Handle or Hardware Style** (`door_handle_style`, select_one, optional): Choose a handle or hardware style, such as contemporary, traditional, modern minimalist, or vintage.
* **Preferred Installation Date** (`preferred_installation_date`, date, required): Enter the earliest date you would like for the installation.
* **Installation Window Preference** (`installation_window`, select_one, optional): Select the time of day you prefer for the installation, such as morning, afternoon, evening, or anytime.
* **Special Installation Instructions** (`installation_notes`, text, optional): If necessary, enter any special installation requirements or concerns.
* **Reuse Existing Hardware** (`existing_hardware_reuse`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate if you would like to reuse existing hardware if compatible.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, optional): Optionally, select a budget range for the installation, such as under 300, 300-500, 500-1000, 1000-2000, or over 2000.
