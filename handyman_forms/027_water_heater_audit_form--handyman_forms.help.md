# Water Heater Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about water heaters, including model, serial number, installation date, and maintenance history, as well as issues found during an inspection and recommendations for repairs or replacement.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Read the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and the information required.
*   Complete all required fields, as indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol.
*   Provide accurate and thorough information for each field.
*   Use the correct formatting for date fields (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
*   If you need help or have questions, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Water Heater Model** (`water_heater_model`): Enter the model number or description of the water heater.
    *   Example: "GE GWH06B"
*   **Serial Number** (`serial_number`): Enter the serial number of the water heater.
    *   Example: "123456789012"
*   **Installation Date** (`installation_date`): Enter the date the water heater was installed.
    *   Use the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2022).
*   **Last Maintenance Date** (`last_maintenance_date`): Enter the date of the last maintenance performed on the water heater.
    *   If unsure, leave blank.
*   **Maintenance History** (`maintenance_history`): Describe the maintenance performed on the water heater.
    *   Example: "Replaced elements and serviced every 6 months"
*   **Issues Found** (`issues_found`): List any issues or problems found with the water heater.
    *   Example: "Leaking from connection points"
*   **Recommended Repairs** (`recommended_repairs`): List any recommended repairs or maintenance for the water heater.
    *   Example: "Replace worn parts or consider replacing the entire unit"
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Additional comments or notes about the water heater.
    *   Example: "Customer reports no issues with unit"
*   **Water Heater Location** (`water_heater_location`): Describe the location where the water heater is installed.
    *   Example: "Basement"
*   **Technician Signature** (`technician_signature`): Sign here to confirm completion of the form.
*   **Technician Name** (`technician_name`): Enter your name as the technician completing the form.
*   **Technician Phone** (`technician_phone`): Enter your phone number as the technician.
*   **Technician Email** (`technician_email`): Enter your email address as the technician.
*   **Audit Date** (`audit_date`): Enter the date this form is completed.
*   **Notes** (`notes`): Additional comments or notes about the audit.
*   **Water Heater Condition** (`water_heater_condition`): Describe the condition of the water heater.
    *   Example: "Good"
*   **Test Results** (`test_results`): Describe any test results or findings.
*   **Water Heater Type** (`water_heater_type`): Describe the type of water heater (e.g., tankless, tank-based).
*   **Inspection Results** (`inspection_results`): Describe the results of the inspection.
*   **Recommended Replacements** (`recommended_replacements`): Select any recommended replacements.
    *   Options: None, New Water Heater, Parts Only
*   **Replacement Status** (`replacement_status`): Select the replacement status.
    *   Options: Yes, No, Pending
*   **Customer Signed** (`customer_signed`): Sign here to confirm receipt of the form.
*   **Technician Signed** (`technician_signed`): Sign here to confirm completion of the form.
*   **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`): Enter any comments from the customer.
*   **Comments** (`comments`): Additional comments or notes about the form.

Please note that some fields are required, as indicated by an asterisk (\*) symbol. Ensure you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
