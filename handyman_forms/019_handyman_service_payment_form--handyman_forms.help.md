# Handyman Service Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email (if you want to be able to receive updates).
2. Select the payment method you would like to use (cash, bank transfer, or card).
3. Select a payment date for the service.
4. Enter the service cost.
5. Enter the payment amount.
6. Add any additional notes about the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (optional).
* **Payment method**: Select the method you would like to use for payment (cash, bank transfer, or card).
* **Payment date**: Select a date for the payment (e.g., 2023-02-15).
* **Service cost**: Enter the total cost of the service.
* **Payment amount**: Enter the amount you are paying for the service.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the payment.
