# home_service_theme_selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you select and customize your home service theme. It guides you through a series of pages to collect your preferences and information for the purpose of providing you with tailored home services.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your desired Service Theme.
2. On the second page, select your Preferred Theme from the available options.
3. On the third page, choose your Style Preferences.
4. On the fourth page, add any additional notes you'd like to include.
5. On the fifth page, enter your Email address.
6. On the sixth page, enter your Phone number (optional).
7. On the seventh page, select your Service Preference.
8. On the eighth page, choose your Style Preferences 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Service Theme (Page 1)
* **Service Theme** (text, required/optional): Enter your desired Service Theme.

### Preferred Theme (Page 2)
* **Preferred Theme** (select_one, required): Choose your preferred theme from the options provided: Theme 1, Theme 2, or Theme 3.

### Style Preferences (Page 3)
* **Style Preferences** (select_multiple, required): Choose multiple style preferences from the options provided: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Additional Notes (Page 4)
* **Additional Notes** (note, required/optional): Add any additional notes you'd like to include.

### Email (Page 5)
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address.

### Phone (Page 6)
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### Service Preferences (Page 7)
* **Service Preferences** (select_one, required): Choose your service preference from the options provided: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

### Style Preferences 2 (Page 8)
* **Style Preferences 2** (select_multiple, required): Choose multiple style preferences from the options provided: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
