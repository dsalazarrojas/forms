# Handymans Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Handymans Release Form is a document that captures essential information about a handyman, including their name, address, and other details. This form is used to verify and document a handyman's status, license, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the handyman's name in the "Handymans name" field.
2. Provide a detailed description of the task performed in the "task description" field.
3. Enter the date of service in the "date of service" field.
4. If applicable, fill in the handyman's address, city, state, and zip code.
5. Enter the handyman's phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Enter the handyman's email address (if available).
7. Sign and date the form in the "handymans signature" field.
8. Enter the date the form was signed in the "date signed" field.
9. Have a notary sign and date the form in the "notary signature" field.
10. Enter the notary's name in the "notary name" field.
11. Enter the date the notary signed the form in the "date signed notary" field.
12. Enter the date the notary certified the form in the "date notary certified" field.
13. Select the type of license the handyman holds in the "name of handymans license" field.
14. Enter the handyman's license number in the "handyman license number" field.
15. Enter the status of the handyman's license in the "handyman license status" field.
16. Enter the date the license was issued in the "date license issued" field.
17. Enter the date the license is set to expire in the "expiration date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Handymans name**: Enter the name of the handyman.
* **task description**: Provide a detailed description of the task performed.
* **date of service**: Enter the date of service.
* **address**: Enter the handyman's address (if applicable).
* **city**: Enter the city of the handyman's address (if applicable).
* **state**: Enter the state of the handyman's address (if applicable).
* **zip**: Enter the zip code of the handyman's address (if applicable).
* **phone**: Enter the handyman's phone number.
* **email**: Enter the handyman's email address (if available).
* **handymans signature**: Sign and date the form.
* **date signed**: Enter the date the form was signed.
* **notary signature**: Sign and date the form.
* **notary name**: Enter the name of the notary.
* **date signed notary**: Enter the date the notary signed the form.
* **date notary certified**: Enter the date the notary certified the form.
* **name of handymans license**: Select the type of license the handyman holds.
* **handyman license number**: Enter the handyman's license number.
* **handyman license status**: Enter the status of the handyman's license.
* **date license issued**: Enter the date the license was issued.
* **expiration date**: Enter the date the license is set to expire.

Please note that this form is a verification and documentation tool, and the information provided is crucial for accurate records and compliance.
