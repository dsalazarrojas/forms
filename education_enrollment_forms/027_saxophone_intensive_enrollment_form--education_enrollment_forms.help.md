# Saxophone Intensive Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Saxophone Intensive Enrollment Form is a tool for participants to provide information and preferences for the Saxophone program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form carefully and accurately with your details.
- You can select multiple sessions from the preferred sessions field.
- If you need to, you can provide additional notes about yourself.
- If you have a preferred contact method, please enter it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* Participant Details (text, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant to your participation in the Saxophone Intensive. Please be as concise as possible.

### Preferred Sessions
* Preferred Sessions (select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select the times for the sessions you would like to attend. You can choose more than one session by holding down Ctrl (on Windows) or Command (on Mac) and selecting multiple options.

### Session Dates
* Session Dates (date, required: false):
	+ This field is where you select a date or range of dates that you are available to attend the Saxophone Intensive. Please enter a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY or a range of dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY - MM/DD/YYYY.

### Participant Notes
* Participant Notes (note, required: false):
	+ This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or notes about yourself that are relevant to your participation in the Saxophone Intensive.

### Email
* Email (email, required: false):
	+ If you have a preferred email address where you can be contacted, please enter it here.

### Phone
* Phone (text, required: false):
	+ This is where you can enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.

### Participant Name
* Participant Name (text, required: false):
	+ This is the name of the participant, if different from the name used when enrolling, please provide it here.

## Tips
- Please make sure to submit the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us.
- You can submit the form multiple times if you need to make changes, but please do not submit duplicate or incomplete submissions.
