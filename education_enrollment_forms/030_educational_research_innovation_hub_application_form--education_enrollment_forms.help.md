# Educational Research Innovation Hub Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Research Innovation Hub Application Form is designed for researchers and educators to apply for funding and resources for their projects. It collects essential information about the project, including a clear description, research goals, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your project details in the "Project Description" field.
2. Clearly describe your project and its goals in the "Project Description" field.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter your institution's details, including the institution name, department, and address.
5. Select the project start and end dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your name and other identifying information.
	+ Enter your first and last name to help us get in touch with you.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Write a clear and concise description of your project.
	+ This field should be around 200-300 words.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, optional): Describe the research area you are working in.
	+ For example, biology, chemistry, or physics.
* **Research Goals** (`research_goals`, text, optional): Clearly state your research goals and objectives.
	+ Describe what you hope to achieve with your project.
* **Project Budget** (`project_budget`, number, optional): Enter the budget for your project.
	+ If you're not sure about the budget, leave this field blank.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This is where you can enter a short note about your project.
	+ For example, "I am seeking funding for a research project in the field of chemistry."
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
	+ We'll use this to contact you about your application.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your institution.
* **Department** (`institution_department`, text, optional): Enter the department of your institution.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your institution.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`project_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your project.
* **Institution Address** (`institution_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your institution.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your project description is clear and concise.
* Make sure to provide accurate contact information.
* Double-check the institution details.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, leave them blank and we'll help you through the process.
