# High School AP Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for high school students to enroll in Advanced Placement (AP) courses. You will use this form to submit your AP course selection and provide some personal information. A faculty member will review your submission and approve or reject your course selection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **Student Name** field with your full name.
2. Enter your **Student Email** to ensure we can contact you with updates.
3. Add your **Student Phone** number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Choose your **AP Course Selection** from the list of available options. Select all courses that you are interested in taking.
5. If a faculty member needs to contact you, they will **Faculty Approval** to review your course selection.
6. Enter any additional **Notes** you'd like to provide, such as any accommodations or concerns you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required): Add your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, required): Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9, 10, 11, or 12).
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, number, required): This field is for internal use, please ignore.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the AP course you are enrolling in.
* **AP Course Selection** (`ap_course_selection`, select_multiple, required): Select all AP courses you are interested in taking from the list.
* **Faculty Approval** (`faculty_approval`, select_one, required): A faculty member will review and approve your course selection.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or feedback you'd like to provide for the faculty member reviewing your form.
