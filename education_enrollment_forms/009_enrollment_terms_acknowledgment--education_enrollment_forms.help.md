# Enrollment Terms Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge and document the terms of enrollment for a course or program. It is an essential part of the enrollment process and must be completed accurately to ensure that all parties understand and agree to the terms of enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enrollment Terms Acknowledgment**: This is the main field where you will enter your acknowledgment of the terms of enrollment.
2. **Course Program**: Check the box if you are enrolling in a course or program. This field is required.
3. **Agreement**: Check the box if you agree to the terms of enrollment. This field is required.
4. **Terms and Conditions**: This is an optional field where you can enter any additional terms or conditions that you want to acknowledge.
5. **Enrollment Date**: Enter the date you are enrolling in the course or program.
6. **Enrollment Time**: Enter the time you are enrolling in the course or program. This field is not required.
7. **Acknowledgment**: This is a note field where you can enter any comments or acknowledgments related to the terms of enrollment. This field is required.
8. **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign this form.
9. **Signature Time**: Enter the time you sign this form. This field is not required.
10. **Submitted By**: Enter your name as the person submitting this form.
11. **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time you submitted this form. This field is not required.
12. **Submitted By Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form. This field is not required.
13. **Submitted By User**: This is not a required field.
14. **Submitted At Time**: This is not a required field.
