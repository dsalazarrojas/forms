# Student Re Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to re-enroll in a course that they have previously enrolled in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student ID number in the field labeled "student_id".
2. Enter your email address in the field labeled "student_email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "student_phone".
4. Select the name of the course you are re-enrolling in from the field labeled "course_name".
5. Enter the duration of the course in the field labeled "course_duration".
6. Enter the price of the course (if applicable) in the field labeled "course_price".
7. Enter the start and end dates of the course in the fields labeled "course_start_date" and "course_end_date".
8. Select the person submitting the re-enrollment from the field labeled "form_submitter".
9. Add any additional comments about your re-enrollment in the field labeled "additional_comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID Number** (student_id, text, required): Enter the unique student ID number assigned to you.
* **Student Email** (student_email, email, required): Enter the email address associated with your student account.
* **Student Phone** (student_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for verification purposes.
* **Course Name** (course_name, select_one, required): Select the name of the course you wish to re-enroll in.
* **Course Duration** (course_duration, time, required): Enter the number of years you plan to take the course.
* **Course Price** (course_price, number, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the course, if applicable.
* **Course Start Date** (course_start_date, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start the course.
* **Course End Date** (course_end_date, date, required): Enter the date you plan to end the course.
* **Form Submitter** (form_submitter, select_multiple, required): Select the person submitting the re-enrollment application.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your re-enrollment.
