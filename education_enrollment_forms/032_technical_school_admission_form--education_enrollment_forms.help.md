# Technical School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from prospective students who wish to enroll in the technical school.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
4. Enter your **Contact Phone** in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Write a brief description of your **Education Background** in the "Education Background" field.
6. Describe your **Academic History** in the "Academic History" field.
7. Provide details about your **References** in the "References" field.
8. Upload any relevant **Uploaded Docs** in the "Uploaded Docs" field.
9. Enter your name of the person who **Submitted** the form in the "Submitted By" field.
10. Select the **Submitted Date** and **Submitted Time** using the respective fields.
11. If applicable, choose the **Submitted Time Zone** from the dropdown list.
12. Choose the **Assigned Tool** from the dropdown list.
13. Select the **Submitted User** who filled the form from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prospective Student**: Enter your name and a brief description of your interests.
* **Name**: Enter your name in the format "First Name Last Name".
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Education Background**: Briefly describe your educational background.
* **Academic History**: Describe your academic history.
* **References**: Provide details about your references.
* **Uploaded Docs**: Upload any relevant documents you'd like to include with your application.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Select the date on which the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time**: Select the time at which the form was submitted.
* **Submitted Time Zone**: If applicable, choose the time zone from the dropdown list.
* **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool that is assigned to you.
* **Submitted User**: Choose the user who submitted the form from the dropdown list.

Note: Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely for a successful application.
