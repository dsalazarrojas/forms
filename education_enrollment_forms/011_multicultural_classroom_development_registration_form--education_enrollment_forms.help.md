# Multicultural Classroom Development Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Multicultural Classroom Development training program. It helps to capture information about the participant's general information, educational level, training interests, and preferences. Please ensure that you are registering for a valid reason and that your submission is accurate and complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your education level (e.g., Bachelor, Master, Doctoral) in the "Education Level" field.
5. Choose your training interests (e.g., Diversity, Classroom Management, Leadership) in the "Training Interests" field (select multiple options as needed).
6. Select your preferred training participation method (e.g., Online, In-Person, Both) in the "Training Participation" field.
7. Enter the submission date in the "Submission Date" field (YYYY-MM-DD).
8. Enter the submission time in the "Submission Time" field (HH:MM).
9. Confirm your attendance by selecting "True" in the "Confirm Attendance" field.
10. Enter any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" field (optional).
11. Confirm that your submission is complete and accurate by selecting "I confirm that my submission is complete and accurate." in the "Confirm Submission" field.
12. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field (optional).
13. Confirm that your submission is complete by selecting "True" in the "Submission Complete" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information about your registration.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest level of education (e.g., Bachelor, Master, Doctoral).
* **Training Interests** (`training_interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose your training interests (e.g., Diversity, Classroom Management, Leadership). Select all that apply.
* **Training Participation** (`training_participation`, select_one, required): Select your preferred training participation method (e.g., Online, In-Person, Both).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you submit this form (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you submit this form (HH:MM).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, note, optional): This field is not used but you can enter any notes about your submission.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback you have.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, text, optional): This field is not used but you can attach any supporting documents.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`confirm_attendance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you will attend the training program.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **Confirm Submission** (`confirm_submission`, select_one, required): Confirm that your submission is complete and accurate.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
* **Submission Complete** (`submission_complete`, select_one, required): Confirm that your submission is complete.
