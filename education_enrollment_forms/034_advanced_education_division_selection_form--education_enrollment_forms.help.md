# Advanced Education Division Selection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help students select their desired division or major within the Advanced Education institution. It is used to collect information on student's name, email, division preference, prior qualifications, preferred start term, supporting documents, admission priority category, and submission date. This form is essential for the institution's admission process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **Student full name** in the "Student full name" field. This is your legal name as per official documents.
2. Enter your **Student email** in the "Student email" field. This will be used for enrollment confirmation and further communication.
3. Select your desired **Division or major** from the dropdown menu. Choose the division that best fits your interests and goals.
4. If applicable, provide your **Prior qualifications** (highest degree or certificate held).
5. Choose your **Preferred start term** (semester or quarter) when you plan to start your education.
6. If you have any supporting documents (transcripts, CV, or certificates) that you would like to provide, enter them in the "Supporting documents provided" field.
7. If you have been assigned a **Admission priority category**, select it from the dropdown menu.
8. Finally, select a **Submission date** for your selection request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student full name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student email**: A valid email address that can be used for enrollment confirmation and future communication.
* **Division or major**: Choose your preferred division or major from the provided options.
  + Science and Technology: This is ideal for students interested in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics fields.
  + Arts and Humanities: This option suits students interested in arts, design, humanities, and social sciences.
  + Business and Management: This is perfect for students aiming to develop business and management skills.
  + Health Sciences: This option is for students interested in health sciences, medicine, and wellness.
* **Prior qualifications**: If applicable, provide information about your prior qualifications (highest degree or certificate held).
* **Preferred start term**: Select the term you would like to start your education (Fall, Spring, or Summer).
* **Supporting documents**: If you have any supporting documents that you would like to provide (transcripts, CV, or certificates), enter them here.
* **Admission priority category**: If assigned, select your admission priority category from the dropdown menu.
* **Submission date**: Choose a date for your selection request.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email address for correct formatting (e.g., [username]@institution.edu).
* Select your division or major carefully as it will impact your course selection and academic path.
* If you are unsure about your admission priority category, contact the admissions office for guidance.
