# Summer School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer School Enrollment Form is used to gather information from students and their parents/guardians for the purpose of enrolling them in summer school programs. This form helps us understand the student's interests, availability, and other relevant details to provide the best possible experience for them during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Summer School Enrollment Form Student Name" field. This is the student's legal name as it appears on their identification.
2. Enter the parent/guardian's name in the "Parent/Guardian Name" field. This is the name of the adult responsible for the student's care and well-being.
3. Enter the student's email address in the "Summer School Enrollment Form Student Email" field. This will be used for important communications about the program.
4. Enter the parent/guardian's email address in the "Student Email" field. This will be used for important communications about the program.
5. Enter the parent/guardian's phone number in the "Parent/Guardian Phone" field. This will be used for quick contact and updates.
6. Enter the student's birthdate in the "Student Birth Date" field. This is the student's date of birth as it appears on their identification.
7. Enter the parent/guardian's birthdate in the "Parent Birth Date" field. This is the date of birth of the adult responsible for the student's care.
8. Select the student's grade level in the "Grade Level" field. Choose from the options: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th.
9. Select the student's course choices by checking the boxes next to each course in the "Course Choices" field. Select multiple courses that the student is interested in.
10. Select the student's program choices by checking the boxes next to each program in the "Program Choices" field. Select multiple programs that the student is interested in.
11. Enter the date the student is available to start the program in the "Summer Program Dates" field. This will help us schedule the program accordingly.
12. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Student Comments" field. This will help us get a better understanding of the student's needs and interests.
13. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Parent/Guardian Comments" field. This will help us get a better understanding of the student's needs and interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer School Enrollment Form Student Name** (student_name): Enter the student's legal name as it appears on their identification.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (parent_name): Enter the name of the adult responsible for the student's care and well-being.
* **Summer School Enrollment Form Student Email** (student_email): Enter the student's email address for important program communications.
* **Student Email** (student_email_2): Enter the email address of the parent/guardian for important program communications.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (parent_phone): Enter the phone number of the adult responsible for the student's care.
* **Student Birth Date** (student_birth_date): Enter the student's date of birth as it appears on their identification.
* **Parent Birth Date** (parent_birth_date): Enter the date of birth of the adult responsible for the student's care.
* **Grade Level** (grade_level): Select the student's grade level from the options: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th.
* **Course Choices** (course_choices): Select multiple courses that the student is interested in by checking the boxes next to each course.
* **Program Choices** (program_choices): Select multiple programs that the student is interested in by checking the boxes next to each program.
* **Summer Program Dates** (summer_program_dates): Enter the date the student is available to start the program.
* **Student Availability** (student_availability): Enter any additional information about the student's availability.
* **Parent/Guardian Availability** (parent_availability): Enter any additional information about the parent/guardian's availability.
* **Student Comments** (student_comments): Enter any additional comments or information about the student.
* **Parent/Guardian Comments** (parent_comments): Enter any additional comments or information about the parent/guardian.

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required.
