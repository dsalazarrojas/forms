# Special Education Admission Checklist Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Education Admission Checklist Form Template is designed to collect comprehensive information from parents or guardians of students who are applying for special education services. This form helps schools, institutions, or organizations understand the student's background, needs, and goals to provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for each section.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Intake Form** (`intake_form`, text, required): A brief summary of the student's current situation, including any relevant medical or educational background.
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, text, required): Information about the parent or guardian completing the form, including name, contact details, and any relevant background.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required): Details about the student, including name, date of birth, and student ID (if applicable).
* **Disability Type** (`disability_type`, select multiple, required): Select one or more from the following options: 
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder
  • Deaf or Hard of Hearing
  • Hearing Impairment
  • Language and Speech Disorder
  • Other (please specify)
  • Physical Disability
  • Visual Impairment
* **Disability Info** (`disability_info`, text, required): More detailed information about the student's disability or medical condition.
* **Academic Info** (`academic_info`, text, required): Details about the student's current academic status, including current grade level and academic performance.
* **Education History** (`education_history`, text, required): A summary of the student's educational background, including previous schools attended and any notable achievements.
* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, required): Any relevant medical information about the student.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Contact details for the student's emergency contact, including name, phone number, and address.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, text, required): Authorization for medical treatment or services, including name, relationship, and contact information.
* **Educational Goals** (`educational_goals`, text, required): A statement of the student's educational goals and objectives.
* **Student Achievements** (`student_achievements`, text, required): A list of the student's notable achievements, accomplishments, or interests.
* **Student Behavior** (`student_behavior`, text, required): Information about the student's behavior, including any challenging or concerning issues.
* **Student Needs** (`student_needs`, text, required): A list of the student's needs, including dietary, social, or emotional requirements.
* **Student Accommodations** (`student_accommodations`, text, required): A list of the student's accommodations or assistance needs.
* **Student Support** (`student_support`, text, required): Information about the support the student receives, including services or programs.
* **Teacher Recommendations** (`teacher_recommendations`, text, required): Recommendations from teachers or instructors about the student's strengths, challenges, and progress.
* **Other Support** (`other_support`, text, required): Additional support the student receives, including services or programs not listed above.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`, text, required): Contact information for the student's guardian or custodian.
* **Parent Concerns** (`parent_concerns`, text, required): Any concerns or issues the parent or guardian has about the student's situation.
* **Student Concerns** (`student_concerns`, text, required): Any concerns or issues the student has about their own situation.
* **Student Conduct** (`student_conduct`, text, required): Information about the student's behavior and conduct.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required): Contact information for emergency contacts, including name, phone number, and address.
* **Medical Authorization 2** (`medical_authorization_2`, text, required): Additional medical authorization or consent.
* **Review** (`review`, select one, required): Please confirm that the form has been reviewed and is accurate.

## Tips

* Double-check each field for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Provide clear and legible handwriting or typing for all answers.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the contact person listed below.
* You can modify this form to suit your specific needs, but please be sure to keep the required fields intact.
