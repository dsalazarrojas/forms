# Classical Ballet Education Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Classical Ballet Education Registration Form is designed to collect information from students and their parents or guardians to register for our Classical Ballet Education programs. The form includes fields that require personal and contact information, as well as preferences for ballet training.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing the student's basic information, including their name and date of birth.
2. Select the student's gender.
3. Report the student's previous ballet experience.
4. Provide the full name of the parent or guardian.
5. Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
6. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
7. If the parent or guardian is unavailable, provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
8. If applicable, list any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of.
9. Select the preferred class type for the student.
10. Choose the preferred schedule for the student's classes.
11. Select how the student learned about our ballet school.
12. (Optional) Report the student's goals and expectations for ballet training.
13. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (`student_information`): Provide the student's name and date of birth.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`): Select the student's gender.
* **Previous Ballet Experience** (`previous_ballet_experience`): Report the number of years of ballet training the student has had.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact if the parent is unavailable.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number for the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`): (Optional) List any medical conditions or allergies that we should be aware of.
* **Class Preference** (`class_preference`): Select the preferred class type for the student.
* **Preferred Schedule** (`preferred_schedule`): Choose the preferred schedule for the student's classes.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear`): Select how the student learned about our ballet school.
* **Goals and Expectations** (`goals_and_expectations`): (Optional) Report the student's goals and expectations for ballet training.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions** (`agreement_terms`): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

- Make sure to review the terms and conditions carefully before confirming your agreement.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
