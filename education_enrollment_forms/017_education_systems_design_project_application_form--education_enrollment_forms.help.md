# Education Systems Design Project Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Systems Design Project Application Form is used by educators to submit their project proposals for review and consideration for funding. This form collects essential information about the project, including the project team, description, timeline, budget, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct project category from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the project description in the project description field.
3. Choose the project team members from the select multiple options (e.g., Team Lead, Project Coordinator, Project Manager, etc.).
4. Enter the project timeline in the date field.
5. Enter the budget amount in the budget field.
6. Enter the contact person's name and email address.
7. Enter the contact person's phone number.
8. Enter the submitter's name.
9. Select the status of the project (e.g., In Progress, Complete, etc.).
10. Enter the submitted date and time.
11. Select the project category.
12. Enter the name of the submitter's institution or university.
13. Select the review status of the project (e.g., Approved, In Progress, Complete, Rejected).
14. Select the project status (e.g., Active, In Progress, Complete, Rejected).
15. Enter the name of the submitter.
16. Enter the department of the submitter.
17. Enter the school of the submitter.
18. Enter the university of the submitter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Category** (`project_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best fits your project (e.g., Design, Grant, Innovation).
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your project in a few sentences.
* **Project Team** (`project_team`, select_multiple, required): Select all team members involved in the project (e.g., Team Lead, Project Coordinator, etc.).
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, date, required): Enter the project start and end dates.
* **Budget** (`budget`, number, required): Enter the project budget amount.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name and email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Project Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your project (e.g., In Progress, Complete, etc.).
* **Submitted Date and Time** (`submitted_by_date`, date, required): Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Project Category** (`project_category`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best fits your project.
* **Submitter Information** (`project_submitted_by`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, select_one, required): Choose the review status of your project (e.g., Approved, In Progress, Complete, Rejected).
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your project (e.g., Active, In Progress, Complete, Rejected).
* **Submitter Name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Department** (`submitted_by_department`, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Submitter School** (`submitted_by_school`, text, required): Enter the school you are affiliated with.
* **Submitter University** (`submitted_by_university`, text, required): Enter the university you are affiliated with.
