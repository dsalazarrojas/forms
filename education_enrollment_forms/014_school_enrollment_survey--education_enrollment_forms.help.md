# school-enrollment-survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "School Enrollment Survey" is a form designed to collect demographic information from students and their guardians to help the educational institution understand the student body better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Please provide the following information about yourself if you are a student:
    *   Student Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
    *   Student Email: Enter your valid email address that can be used to contact you.
    *   Date of Birth: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
2.  If you are a guardian or parent, please provide your relevant information:
    *   Guardian Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
    *   Guardian Email: Enter your valid email address that can be used to contact you.
3.  Select your relationship with the student:
    *   How do you relate to this student? (Select one or more options from the list: Parent, Grandparent, Guardian, Other)
4.  If you have other siblings enrolled in the school, please indicate the number:
    *   Number of Siblings: Enter the number of siblings you have enrolled in the school.
5.  Finally, select your current enrollment status:
    *   Enrollment Status: (Select one option from the list: Enrolled, In Progress, Not Enrolled)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Student Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Student Email** (email, required): Please enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
*   **Guardian Name** (text, optional): If you are a guardian or parent, please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Guardian Email** (email, optional): If you are a guardian or parent, please enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
*   **Student Date of Birth** (date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **How do you relate to this student?** (select_multiple, required): Please select one or more options from the list: Parent, Grandparent, Guardian, Other.
*   **Number of Siblings** (number, optional): If you have other siblings enrolled in the school, please indicate the number.
*   **Enrollment Status** (select_one, required): Please select one option from the list: Enrolled, In Progress, Not Enrolled.
