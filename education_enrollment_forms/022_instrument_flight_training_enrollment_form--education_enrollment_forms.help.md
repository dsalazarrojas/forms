# Instrument Flight Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Instrument Flight Training Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential information from users interested in enrolling in instrument flight training programs. Completing this form accurately will help us understand your needs and preferences for an optimal training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
	* Student information
2. Select one or more flight schools that interest you from the list provided.
3. Choose one or more flight instructors that you would like to work with.
4. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Student Information**
	* (`student_info`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your student information, including your name, contact details, and any other relevant details.

### 2. **Flight Schools**
	* (`flight_schools`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more flight schools that interest you. This will help us better understand your training preferences.

### 3. **Flight Instructors**
	* (`flight_instructors`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more flight instructors that you would like to work with. This will help us tailor your training experience to your needs.

## Tips
- Take your time and fill out the form accurately to provide us with the best information possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
