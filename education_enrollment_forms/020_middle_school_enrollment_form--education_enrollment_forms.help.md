# Middle School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Middle School Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from students and their parents/guardians for middle school enrollment purposes. This form will help the school administration to collect student and parent information, track student attendance, and facilitate communication between teachers, students, and parents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Enter the student's and parent's information in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide the student's and parent's contact information.
4. Add any comments or notes about the student.
5. Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Middle School Enrollment** (Page 1):
	+ This is the title of the form, indicating that it is for middle school enrollment purposes.
* **Student Info** (Page 2):
	+ Enter the student's name, age, and grade level.
* **Parent Contact Info** (Page 3):
	+ Enter the parent's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Student Details** (Page 4):
	+ Enter the student's student ID, date of birth, and any relevant medical conditions.
* **Student Signature** (Page 5):
	+ The student's signature, indicating that they have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Parent Signature** (Page 6):
	+ The parent's signature, indicating that they have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (Page 7):
	+ Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.
* **Student Attendance** (Page 8):
	+ Track the student's attendance by entering the date and reason for absence.
* **Student Notes** (Page 9):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about the student.
* **Student Comments** (Page 10):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes from the student or teacher.
* **Parent Comments** (Page 11):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes from the parent.
* **Parent Signature Confirmation** (Page 12):
	+ The parent's signature, indicating that they have reviewed and confirmed the information.
* **School Name** (Page 13):
	+ Enter the name of the school.
* **School Address** (Page 14):
	+ Enter the address of the school.
* **Teacher Name** (Page 15):
	+ Enter the name of the teacher responsible for the student.
* **Teacher Email** (Page 16):
	+ Enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Student Email** (Page 17):
	+ Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (Page 18):
	+ Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Cell** (Page 19):
	+ Enter the student's cell phone number.
* **Guardian Name** (Page 20):
	+ Enter the name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Phone** (Page 21):
	+ Enter the guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (Page 22):
	+ Enter the guardian's email address.
* **Guardian Cell** (Page 23):
	+ Enter the guardian's cell phone number.

Note: The required fields are marked as required in the form. Please ensure that you complete all the required fields before submitting the form.
