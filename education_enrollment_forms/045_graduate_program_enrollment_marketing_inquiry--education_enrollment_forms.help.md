# Graduate Program Enrollment Marketing Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect marketing inquiries for graduate programs. It helps potential students express their interest in specific graduate programs, allowing marketing teams to follow up and tailor their efforts accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, select the options that best describe your inquiry. This includes selecting the graduate programs you're interested in and indicating whether you've found us through a specific channel. You may also provide any additional comments to help our team better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **graduate_program_enrollment_marketing_inquiry** (`1`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you're interested in graduate programs, but note that this field is not required for form submission.
* **Program** (`2`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have a specific graduate program in mind, otherwise select 'No' if you're still researching.
* **Start Term** (`3`, select_one, required: false): Indicate when you plan to start your graduate program by selecting 'Yes' for a specific term.
* **Prospective Student Name** (`4`, text, required: false): Fill in your full name as a potential graduate student.
* **Prospective Student Email** (`5`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can follow up on your inquiry.
* **Prospective Student Phone** (`6`, text, required: false): Add your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **School Name** (`7`, text, required: false): If you're affiliated with a school, provide its name for us to note.
* **Campus Location** (`8`, text, required: false): If you're interested in a specific campus location, enter it here.
* **Prospective Student Interest** (`9`, text, required: false): Share any additional comments or interests you have about our graduate programs.
* **How Did You Find Us** (`10`, text, required: false): Indicate how you discovered our graduate programs, e.g., social media, website, etc.
* **Program Interested In** (`11`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the graduate programs you're interested in, or 'No' if you're still exploring options.
* **Comments** (`12`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or questions you have about our graduate programs.
* **Graduate Program Enrollment Marketing Inquiry Comments** (`13`, note, required: false): Leave any further comments or questions for our marketing team to address.
