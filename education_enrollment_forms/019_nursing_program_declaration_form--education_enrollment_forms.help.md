# Nursing Program Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursing Program Declaration Form is a document that captures the details of an applicant's nursing program preferences. The form helps the applicant to declare their intent to enroll in a nursing program, allowing them to provide information about their program choice, training provider, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and carefully, following the instructions provided for each field.
- If you need help with a field or have questions, feel free to contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Applicant Details (Page 1)**:
  * This section is where you provide general information about yourself, such as your name and contact details.
  * Please fill in your:
    * Phone Number
    * Email

- **Program Type (Page 1)**:
  * This field is used to indicate the type of program you are applying for (e.g., Diploma, Certificate, Associate's Degree).
  * Select all that apply.

- **Campus Location (Page 1)**:
  * This field is used to indicate the location where you would like to take the program (e.g., On Campus, Off Campus, Online).
  * Select one that best suits your needs.

- **Start Date and End Date (Page 1)**:
  * These fields are used to indicate the start and end dates of the program you are applying for.
  * Please enter the dates in the required format (YYYY-MM-DD).

- **Name of Program (Page 1)**:
  * This field is used to provide the name of the program you are applying for.
  * Please fill in the name of the program as it appears on the program's official documentation.

- **Training Provider (Page 1)**:
  * This field is used to indicate the training provider of the program you are applying for.
  * Please fill in the name of the training provider as it appears on their official documentation.

- **Comments (Page 1)**:
  * This field is used to provide additional comments or information about your program choice.
  * Please use this field to ask questions or provide context that may be helpful for us to process your application.

- **Confirm Phone Number and Email (Page 2)**:
  * These fields are used to confirm your phone number and email address.
  * Please fill in the same information you provided in the Applicant Details section.

- **Signature (Page 2)**:
  * This field is used to confirm that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms and conditions of the program.
  * Please sign and date the form to indicate your intent to enroll.

- **Submit (Page 2)**:
  * This is the final step to confirm your declaration of intent to enroll in the nursing program.
  * Select one of the options provided to indicate your status:
    * I confirm I am applying for the nursing program
    * I need more information

Please note that some fields are not required and are provided for informational purposes only. If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact our support team.
