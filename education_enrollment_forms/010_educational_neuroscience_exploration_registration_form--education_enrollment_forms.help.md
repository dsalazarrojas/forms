# Educational Neuroscience Exploration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Neuroscience Exploration Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from participants in an educational neuroscience project. This form will help us understand your background, contact preferences, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information**: 
  • Label: Registration Information
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: Please provide a brief description of your participation in the project.

* **Participant Information**: 
  • Label: Participant Information
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: Please provide some basic information about yourself, including your name, age, and any relevant background details.

* **Emergency Contacts**: 
  • Label: Emergency Contacts
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: Provide the names, phone numbers, and relationships of any emergency contacts you have for yourself.

* **Medical History**: 
  • Label: Medical History
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: If you have any medical conditions or allergies that may impact your participation in the project, please provide details.

* **Participant Notes**: 
  • Label: Participant Notes
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: If you have any specific needs or concerns about your participation in the project, please provide details.

* **Contact Preference**: 
  • Label: Contact Preference
  • Type: select_one
  • Required: No
  • Description: How would you prefer to be contacted? Choose 'By Phone', 'By Email', or 'By Post'.

* **Participant Email**: 
  • Label: Participant Email
  • Type: email
  • Required: No
  • Description: Your personal email address for communication purposes.

* **Participant Phone**: 
  • Label: Participant Phone
  • Type: number
  • Required: No
  • Description: Your personal phone number for contact purposes.

* **Participant Address**: 
  • Label: Participant Address
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: Your address for postal contact purposes.

* **Emergency Contact Name**: 
  • Label: Emergency Contact Name
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: Name of your emergency contact person.

* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: 
  • Label: Emergency Contact Relationship
  • Type: text
  • Required: No
  • Description: Relationship to your emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse).

* **Emergency Contact Phone**: 
  • Label: Emergency Contact Phone
  • Type: number
  • Required: No
  • Description: Phone number of your emergency contact person.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately.
* You can ask for assistance if you have any doubts about the form.
* If you have any other concerns or questions, please contact us.
