# Adaptive Learning Research Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining the Adaptive Learning Research Pilot, a study aimed at exploring the effectiveness of adaptive learning systems in educational settings. By filling out this form, you will be providing essential details about yourself and your experience with education and technology, which will help researchers understand your needs and challenges in participating in this pilot study.

## How To Complete This Form

Before you begin, make sure you have a stable and secure internet connection. If you are using a mobile device, please ensure it is configured to display the correct date and time. 

1. Click on each section header to navigate through the form.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and institution name as required. Your email address will be used for communication purposes.
3. Select your role in the educational setting and the level of education you primarily work with.
4. Rate your experience with educational technology and indicate whether you have previously used adaptive learning systems.
5. Specify your estimated time commitment to the study and your access to a computer and internet.
6. Describe your research interests, anticipated challenges, and goals for participating in the pilot study.
7. Provide your consent to having your data collected and analyzed for research purposes and indicate whether you agree to publication of findings.
8. Confirm your understanding of withdrawal rights and research benefits.
9. Date and sign this form electronically to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`participant_email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for study communication.
*   **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your school, university, or educational organization.
*   **Role in Educational Setting** (`role_in_education`, Select One, Required): Choose your position or role in the educational environment: Student, Teacher, Administrator, Researcher, Parent, or Other.
*   **Education Level** (`education_level`, Select One, Optional): Choose the level of education you primarily work with: Elementary School, Middle School, High School, College/University, Adult Education, or Other.
*   **Years of Experience in Education** (`years_experience`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of years you have worked in the education sector.
*   **Experience with Educational Technology** (`technology_experience`, Select One, Required): Rate your comfort level with educational technology: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
*   **Previous Experience with Adaptive Learning** (`adaptive_learning_experience`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you have previously used adaptive learning systems: Yes, extensive experience, Yes, some experience, No, but interested, or No, unfamiliar.
*   **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, Text, Optional): Describe what aspects of adaptive learning interest you most.
*   **Estimated Time Commitment** (`time_commitment`, Select One, Required): Select the estimated time you can dedicate to this pilot study per week: Less than 2 hours, 2-5 hours, 5-10 hours, or More than 10 hours.
*   **Access to Computer/Internet** (`computer_access`, Select One, Required): Confirm your access to a computer and internet: Yes, reliable access, Yes, limited access, or No, limited access.
*   **Goals for Participating in Pilot** (`pilot_goals`, Text, Optional): Describe what you hope to gain from participating in this pilot study.
*   **Expected Challenges** (`challenges_expected`, Text, Optional): Specify any anticipated challenges during the pilot study.
*   **Data Privacy Consent** (`data_privacy_consent`, Select One, Required): Indicate whether you consent to having your data collected and analyzed for research purposes: Yes, I consent or No, I do not consent.
*   **Publication Consent** (`publication_consent`, Select One, Required): Agree to the publication of findings, anonymized: 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Withdrawal Rights Acknowledged** (`withdrawal_rights`, Select One, Required): Confirm your understanding of withdrawal rights: 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Research Benefits Understood** (`research_benefits_understood`, Select One, Required): Confirm your understanding of the research benefits: 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you sign this form.
*   **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, Text, Required): Sign this form electronically, confirming your participation in the study.

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
*   Be honest and accurate in your responses.
*   If you need help or have questions, contact the research team at the provided contact information.
*   The study's data protection policy is in place to ensure the confidentiality and security of your data. Review it before participating.
