# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for their child's enrollment in a preschool program. This form will help us get to know you and your family better, and ensure a smooth transition for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): This is your name, which will be used to identify you and for communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your address so we can send important information and notifications to you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address so we can keep in touch.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This is your contact phone number for us to reach you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date your child was born.
* **Parent Name (contact)** (`parent_name_contact`, text, optional): This is the name of the person we should contact in case of emergencies.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the child being enrolled.
* **Child's DOB** (`child_dob`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the child.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, optional): Please print your signature as proof of identity.
* **Date of Enrollment** (`date_of_enrollment`, date, optional): Enter the date you are enrolling your child.
* **Parent Contact Email** (`parent_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email for us to contact you.
* **Parent Contact Phone** (`parent_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.

# Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out to avoid delays in the enrollment process.
* If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to contact us.
