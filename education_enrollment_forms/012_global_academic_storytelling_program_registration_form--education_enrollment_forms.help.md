# Global Academic Storytelling Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Academic Storytelling Program Registration Form is used to collect demographic information and program preferences from users interested in participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please note that this field will be used to send you program-related emails.
- Select all your time zones in the "Time Zone" field.
- Enter your language in the "Language" field. For example, if you prefer to communicate in Spanish, select "Spanish".
- Enter your program name in the "Program" field.
- Enter your program location in the "Program Location" field.
- Enter your program start date in the "Program Start Date" field. Please note that this field should be in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field to ensure accuracy.
- Enter your time zone 1 in the "Time Zone 1" field.
- Enter your time zone 2 in the "Time Zone 2" field.
- Optionally, enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" field. If you have any specific requirements or preferences, you can mention them here.
- If you have already entered a program location and start date, please enter your repeated program location and start date in the "Program Location (repeated)" and "Program Start Date (repeated)" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address that will be used for program-related communications.
* **Time Zone** (select multiple, required): Select all your time zones. Please note that the available options are EST (Eastern Standard Time), GMT (Greenwich Mean Time), CST (Central Standard Time), PST (Pacific Standard Time), MST (Mountain Standard Time), AKST (Alaska Standard Time), and YST (Yellowstone Standard Time).
* **Language** (text, required): Enter your preferred language.
* **Program** (text, required): Enter your program name.
* **Program Location** (text, required): Enter your program location.
* **Program Start Date** (date, required): Enter your program start date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Confirm Email** (email, required): Enter your email address again to ensure accuracy.
* **Time Zone 1** (time, required): Enter your time zone 1.
* **Time Zone 2** (time, required): Enter your time zone 2.
* **Additional Information** (note, optional): Enter any additional information, such as specific requirements or preferences.
* **Program Location (repeated)** (text, optional): If you have already entered a program location, enter it again for verification purposes.
* **Program Start Date (repeated)** (date, optional): If you have already entered a program start date, enter it again for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, contact our support team.
