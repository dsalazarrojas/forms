# Veterinary Course Enrollment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in enrolling in a veterinary course. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the following required and optional fields:
	* Course Interest
	* Background Details
	* Contact Information
	* Select a Course
2. Provide additional information if needed in the optional fields:
	* Email
	* Phone Number
	* Date of Birth
	* Time of Birth
	* Select Multiple Courses
	* Note
3. Submit your form once you have completed all the necessary fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Interest**: Describe your interest in enrolling in a veterinary course. This field is not required, but it helps us understand your motivation.
* **Background Details**: Share any relevant background information about yourself, such as previous experience or relevant skills.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and/or phone number so we can contact you with more information about the course.
* **Select a Course**: Choose the type of course you are interested in:
	+ Basic Training
	+ Intermediate Training
	+ Advanced Training
* **Email**: Enter your email address if you have not already provided it in the Contact Information field.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you have not already provided it in the Contact Information field.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth for verification purposes.
* **Select Multiple Courses**: Choose multiple course types you are interested in (if applicable).
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or information about your course interest.
* **Course Enrollment Request**: Enter any additional information or requests you have regarding course enrollment.
