# School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a student's enrollment in the school. This form is designed to be filled out by parents or guardians and may be used by school administrators to track student progress and manage student records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Make sure to fill out all the fields in the correct format as specified in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Enrollment** (`school_enrollment_form`): This field is likely a brief description of the reason for the student's enrollment or a note about any special circumstances. It is optional, so you do not have to fill it in.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): This field should be filled out with the name of the parent or guardian filling out the form.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`): Fill in the name of the student enrolling in the school.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): This field should be filled out with a valid contact number (e.g., phone number) to enable school contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter the student's date of birth in the correct format. This will help the school keep accurate records.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`): This field allows you to select the student's current grade level. You can choose from a list of available grades.
* **Student Enrolled Grade** (`student_enrolled_grade`): This field is also a grade level, but it is more likely related to the grade level the student will be enrolling in. Choose the correct grade level according to the school's curriculum.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): This field is likely for the parent's or guardian's signature, which may be required for verification purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the school administration for clarification.
* Please fill out the form in a way that is respectful and clear.
