# mba_program_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The MBA program enrollment form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in enrolling in the program. This form is used to assess the eligibility and suitability of applicants for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in your personal details such as **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth**.
2. Select your **Current Employment Status**, which can be **Active** or **Inactive**.
3. Provide your **Email** and **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
4. If you have any **Work Experience**, enter the number of years.
5. Select your **Expected Graduation Date** and enter your **Program Name** (Yes/No).
6. Choose your **MBA Program Options** (Yes/No).
7. Enter your **Employment Type** (Yes/No) and **Current Employer**.
8. Enter your **Job Title** and **Employment Start Date**.
9. Select your **Current Location** (text field).
10. Enter your **Employment End Date**.
11. Select your **Employment Type** (Yes/No).
12. Enter your **Highest Degree** (Yes/No).
13. Select your **Options** (Yes/No).
14. Enter your **Current Location (Employer)**.
15. Enter your **Expected Graduation Date**.
16. Attach your **Resume** and **References**.
17. Enter any **Other Information** you may want to provide.
18. Finally, confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete by selecting **Yes** or **No**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Program Name**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your interest in the MBA program.
* **MBA Program Options**: Choose from the list to select your desired program options.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Expected Graduation**: Enter the expected date of graduation.
* **Highest Degree**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have a highest degree.
* **Options**: Choose from the list to select your highest degree options.
* **Current Employment Status**: Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate your current employment status.
* **Employment Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are employed.
* **Current Employer**: Enter your current employer.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date you started working.
* **Employment End Date**: Enter the date you ended your employment.
* **Current Location**: Enter your current location.
* **Current Location (Employer)**: Enter your current employer's location.
* **Work Experience**: Enter the number of years of work experience.
* **Expected Graduation Date**: Enter the expected date of graduation.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume for consideration.
* **References**: Provide references for your work experience.
* **Other Information**: Enter any other information you may want to provide.
* **Confirm**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm the accuracy of the provided information.
