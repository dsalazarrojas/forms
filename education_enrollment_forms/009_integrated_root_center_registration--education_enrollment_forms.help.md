# Integrated Root Center Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Integrated Root Center Registration form is a tool used to register individuals with the organization's internal systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
* Enter your user details, participant details, and program details as prompted.
* Select the correct options and formats for each field as needed.
* Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal details as prompted.
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the participant as prompted.
### Program Details
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the details of the program as prompted.
### Review
* **Review** (`review`, text, required/optional): Enter a review or comment as prompted.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address as prompted. Please format as per standard email format (e.g., [username@example.com](mailto:username@example.com)).
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number as prompted. Please format as per standard phone number format (e.g., +1234567890).
### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
### Time of Registration
* **Time of Registration** (`time_of_registration`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of registration as prompted. Please format as per 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:30).
### Review Date
* **Review Date** (`review_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the review date as prompted. Please format as per DD/MM/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
### Review Time
* **Review Time** (`review_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the review time as prompted. Please format as per 24-hour clock (e.g., 14:30).
