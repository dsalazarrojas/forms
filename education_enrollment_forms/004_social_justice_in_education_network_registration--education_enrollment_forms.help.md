# Social Justice In Education Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Justice In Education Network Registration form is designed to collect registration information for individuals who want to join the network. This form helps us understand who you are, your interests, and your role in the network.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
4. Create a password for your account. This password must be at least 8 characters long.
5. Confirm your password in the "Confirm Password" field.
6. Select your member type from the "Member type" dropdown menu. Choose from Educator, Student, or Advocate.
7. Check the boxes of the policies you agree to in the "Consent" field. This includes "Terms of Service" and "Privacy Policy".
8. If you want to add any additional notes, you can do so in the "Notes" field. This is optional.


## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Text, required): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (Text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Password** (Text, required): Create a password that is at least 8 characters long.
* **Confirm Password** (Text, required): Confirm the password you created above.
* **Member type** (Select One, required): Choose from Educator, Student, or Advocate.
* **Consent** (Select Multiple, required): Check the boxes of the policies you agree to. These include "Terms of Service" and "Privacy Policy".
* **Notes** (Note, optional): Add any additional notes you want to provide. This field is optional.
* **Interests** (Text, optional): This field is not currently in use.
