# Educational Research Innovation Hub Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help applicants apply for funding from the Educational Research Innovation Hub. It collects information about proposed research projects, their funding, and relevant institutional details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and institution's address.
2. Describe your research project, including its title, description, and justification.
3. Provide details about the funding, such as the amount, currency, start and end dates, and status.
4. Select the relevant research innovation hub(s) related to your project.
5. Specify the funding agency, program, and budget.
6. Finally, confirm your institutional representative's approval and submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Name**: Enter your name as the contact person for this application.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your institutional email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your institutional phone number.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person representing your institution.
* **Research Area**: Select the relevant research area for your project (not applicable in this version).
* **Research Title**: Enter a clear and concise title for your research project.
* **Research Description**: Provide a detailed description of your research project.
* **Research Justification**: Explain why your project is important and relevant to the Hub's focus areas.
* **Funding Institution**: Enter the name of the institution funding your project.
* **Institution Address**: Enter the address of your institution.
* **Institution City**: Enter the city of your institution.
* **Institution State**: Enter the state of your institution.
* **Institution Zip**: Enter the zip code of your institution.
* **Research Innovation Hubs**: Select all relevant hubs related to your project.
* **Funding Amount**: Enter the total amount of funding for your project.
* **Funding Currency**: Select the currency of the funding (e.g., Dollar, Euro, Pound).
* **Funding Start Date**: Enter the start date of the funding.
* **Funding End Date**: Enter the end date of the funding.
* **Research Funding Status**: Specify the current funding status (e.g., Funded, Not Funded).
* **Research Funding Type**: Select all relevant funding types for your project (e.g., Grant, Fellowship, Other).
* **Institution Representative**: Confirm that your institutional representative has approved this application.
* **Research Funding Agency**: Select the agency providing the funding (e.g., NIH, NSF, Other).
* **Research Funding Program**: Select the program under which your project is funded.
* **Research Funding Budget**: Select the budget category for your project (e.g., Budget1, Budget2, Budget3).
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission of your application.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have all the required information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact and institutional information for accuracy.
* Select all relevant research innovation hubs and funding agencies for your project.
* Clearly describe your research project and its justification.
* Confirm your institutional representative's approval.
* Submit your application before the submission deadline.
