# Online Degree Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Online Degree Program Application" form is a tool for students to apply to online degree programs offered by the institution. This form helps us gather essential information about the student, their program of interest, and their application details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your User Name, Email, and Phone Number.
2. Select your preferred Course Name from the options provided.
3. Provide your Date of Birth, Program Start Date, and Expected Graduation Date.
4. Choose your University Name and Program Name.
5. Select your Degree Title, Program Type, and Application Type.
6. Submit your application by providing your Submission Date and Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Page 2

* **Student Id** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred course name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start the program.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter your expected graduation date.

### Page 3

* **University Name** (`university_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the university you are applying to.
* **Degree Title** (`degree_title`, select_one, required): Select your degree title.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are applying for.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the program.

### Page 4

* **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your application type.
* **Submitted by** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Select who submitted the application.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Submitted At** (`submitted_at`, time, required): Enter the time you submitted your application.

### Page 5

* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_multiple, required): Select your program type.
* **Submitted To** (`submitted_to`, select_one, required): Select who the application was submitted to.
* **Status** (`status`, select_one, required): Select the status of your application.
* **Reason for Change** (`reason_for_change`, text, optional): Enter a reason for any changes made to your application.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your application.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the application process.
* **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the application process.
* **System Comments** (`system_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the application process.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, time, required): This field is automatically generated and cannot be edited.
