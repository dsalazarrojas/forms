# religious_education_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register for religious education courses. It collects information required for the registration process, including student and contact details, course selection, and demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student information (page 1).
2. Provide your contact information (page 2).
3. Select the courses you want to register for (page 3).
4. Enter your date of birth (page 4).
5. Add your email address (page 5).
6. Enter your phone number (page 6).
7. Provide your religious background (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information** (Page 1):
  - This field is optional and asks for your name and other student details.
  - Please fill in your student name and any relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (Page 2):
  - This field is optional and asks for your contact details such as address and emergency contact person.
  - You may provide any relevant contact information for yourself.
* **Courses** (Page 3):
  - Select all the courses you wish to register for from the list provided.
  - Be sure to check the required fields to ensure you meet the course prerequisites.
* **Date of Birth** (Page 4):
  - Please provide your date of birth in the correct format (month/day/year).
  - This will help us confirm your eligibility for the courses you register for.
* **Email** (Page 5):
  - Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
  - We will use this to contact you about your course registration and other important information.
* **Phone Number** (Page 6):
  - Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you quickly and easily.
  - Please format your number according to your country's standards.
* **Religious Background** (Page 7):
  - This field is optional and allows you to provide your religious background.
  - Please be honest and clear about your beliefs and practices.
* **Submit** (Page 8):
  - Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the submit button to complete your registration.
  - Make sure to review your form before submitting to ensure accuracy.
