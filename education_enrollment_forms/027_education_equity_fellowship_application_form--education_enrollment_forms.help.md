# Education Equity Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education Equity Fellowship Application Form is designed to help organizations collect information from candidates to evaluate their suitability for a fellowship opportunity. This form is intended for candidates who have not been awarded or denied a fellowship by the same organization in the current fiscal year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure that you have not already submitted this form previously and are eligible to apply for this opportunity.
* Review each field carefully and provide clear and concise answers.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organization's contact email: form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please provide a brief summary of your background, including your name, title, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your current contact information, including your email and phone number.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your previous work experience, including your job title, company, and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, `text`, required/optional): List your educational background, including your highest level of education achieved and any relevant certifications.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required/optional): Please attach a copy of your resume, or provide a link to your online profile where your resume is hosted.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, required/optional): Provide names and contact information for two professional references.

## Tips
* Make sure to tailor your answers to the specific requirements of the fellowship opportunity.
* Use clear and concise language in your responses.
* Proofread your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.
