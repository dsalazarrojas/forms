# Cultural Center Language Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help applicants to apply for admission to the Cultural Center's language courses. To ensure a smooth application process, please read this guide carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before completing the form.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please select the language you are applying for from the "Language" dropdown menu.
- Choose your level of language proficiency, which is divided into three categories: Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced. Please select the one that best represents your current skill level.
- Select the type(s) of courses you are interested in from the "Course Type" dropdown menu.
- Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
- Choose a time slot for your courses from the available options.
- Provide a date for your application.
- If applicable, enter the length and frequency of the courses you are interested in.
- If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language** (`Language`, `select_one`): Select the language you are applying for, e.g., English, Spanish, French, Chinese, or Japanese.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Language Level** (`LanguageLevel`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of language proficiency, which is divided into three categories: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Course Type** (`CourseType`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type(s) of courses you are interested in, which are Language Course, Culture Course, or Art Course.
* **Application Date** (`ApplicationDate`, `date`, required): Select a date for your application.
* **Time Slot** (`TimeSlot`, `time`, required): Choose a time slot for your courses.
* **Cultural Center** (`CulturalCenter`, `select_one`, required): Select the Cultural Center you would like to apply to, which are Language Institute, Culture Institute, or Art Institute.
* **Course Length** (`CourseLength`, `number`, required): Enter the length of the courses you are interested in.
* **Course Frequency** (`CourseFrequency`, `number`, required): Enter the frequency of the courses you are interested in.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your application.
* **Language Proficiency** (`LanguageProficiency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your level of language proficiency, which are Proficient, Beginner, or Intermediate.
* **Language Proficiency Level** (`LanguageProficiencyLevel`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your level of language proficiency, which are High, Medium, or Low.
* **Language Proficiency Scale** (`LanguageProficiencyScale`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your scale for language proficiency, which are 1-5, 6-10, or 11-15.
* **Language Proficiency Scale Scale** (`LanguageProficiencyScaleScale`, `number`, required): Enter your level of language proficiency scale.
