# Tutor Appointment Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutor Appointment Request Form is designed for students to request a tutor appointment with a specific tutor, indicating their availability and preferred time slots. This form helps the tutoring service understand their student's needs and schedule appointments accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form completely and accurately** to ensure your tutor appointment request is processed correctly.
2. **Provide your Student ID** in the "Student ID" field. This is a unique identifier assigned to you by the university.
3. **Choose a Session Date** for your appointment. Select a date that works best for you.
4. **Select your preferred Start Time** and **End Time** for your appointment. Be sure to choose times that fit within your available hours.
5. **Choose a Subject** you would like to work on during your appointment. This helps the tutor understand your focus areas.
6. **Provide a brief message** to the tutor, if needed, to discuss any specific requirements or expectations you have.
7. **Select your Preferred Date** for your appointment. If this conflicts with your Session Date, please contact the tutoring service to discuss alternative arrangements.
8. **Choose your available days** for the appointment, selecting one or more from the available options.
9. **Select your available time slots** for the appointment, choosing one or more from the available options.
10. **Submit the form** to complete your tutor appointment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student ID (Student ID)**: Enter your unique university-issued Student ID.
* **Session Date (Session Date)**: Select a date for your appointment.
* **Start Time (Start Time)**: Choose your preferred start time for the appointment.
* **End Time (End Time)**: Choose your preferred end time for the appointment.
* **Subject (Subject)**: Select the subject you would like to focus on during your appointment.
* **Tutor Request (Tutor Request)**: Enter any specific requirements or expectations you have for your appointment.
* **Preferred Date (Preferred Date)**: Select your preferred date for the appointment.
* **Preferred Time (Preferred Time)**: Choose your preferred time for the appointment.
* **Available Days (Available Days)**: Select one or more days you are available to meet.
* **Available Time (Available Time)**: Select one or more time slots you are available to meet.
* **Message (Message)**: Enter any additional information or notes for the tutor, if needed.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your tutor appointment request is processed correctly.
* Contact the tutoring service if you have any conflicts or difficulties with your preferred Session Date or Preferred Date.
* Be specific with your Subject and Tutor Request to help the tutor understand your needs.
* Choose your available days and time slots carefully to accommodate the tutor's schedule.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the tutoring service.
