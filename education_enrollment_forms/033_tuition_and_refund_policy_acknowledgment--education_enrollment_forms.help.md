**Tuition And Refund Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide**
## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge the Tuition and Refund Policy of the educational institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the Tuition and Refund Policy carefully.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the first field to indicate your agreement to the policy.
3. Provide your date of enrollment in the format MM/DD/YY.
4. Review the terms of service to understand your responsibilities as a student.
5. Enter the version number of the current Tuition and Refund Policy.
