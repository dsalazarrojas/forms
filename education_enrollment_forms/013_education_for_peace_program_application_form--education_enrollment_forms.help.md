# Education For Peace Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Education For Peace Program Application Form is designed to help students and educators apply to a program focused on promoting peace education. This program aims to support applicants in their educational journey and foster a culture of peace and understanding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name and contact information, including your Institution Name and Contact Phone and Email.
2. Provide a brief description of the program you are interested in, under Program Details.
3. Share your motivation for applying to this program, under Motivation.
4. Select a valid date for when you will be available, under Availability.
5. Finally, submit your application by selecting the correct submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Title (Program Title)** (`text`, **Optional**):
	+ A brief title of the program you are applying for.
	+ Please enter a concise title of the program.
* **Institution Name (Institution Name)** (`text`, **Optional**):
	+ Your institution's name where you are affiliated with.
	+ Please enter your institution's name as it appears on official documents.
* **Program Details (Program Details)** (`note`, **Optional**):
	+ A description of the program you are applying for.
	+ Please provide a brief description of the program, including its focus and goals.
* **Motivation (Motivation)** (`text`, **Optional**):
	+ Explain why you are applying for this program.
	+ Please provide a brief explanation of your motivation, including your goals and aspirations.
* **Availability (Availability)** (`date`, **Optional**):
	+ Select a date when you will be available for the program.
	+ Please select a valid date when you will be available to start the program.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)** (`text`, **Optional**):
	+ Your phone number where you can be reached.
	+ Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)** (`email`, **Optional**):
	+ Your email address where you can be reached.
	+ Please enter a valid email address.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`select_one`, **Required**):
	+ Select the correct submission status of your application.
	+ Please select "Application Submitted" to submit your application.
* **Review Status (Review Status)** (`select_multiple`, **Optional**):
	+ Select the status of your application review.
	+ Please select one or more status options to track your application's progress.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your institution's name and contact information are accurate and up-to-date.
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of the program you are applying for.
* Select a valid date for your availability to ensure you can start the program on time.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
