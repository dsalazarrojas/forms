# Academic Peer Coaching Circle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is meant for participants who are interested in joining an Academic Peer Coaching Circle. The purpose of this form is to collect information about your preferences, goals, and experience to ensure a successful and productive coaching circle experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read through each field carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
2. Fill in your name, email, and institution accurately.
3. Choose your preferences for coaching focus area, experience level, weekday availability, meeting time, meeting format, group size, and coaching commitment level.
4. If you have any previous coaching experience, feel free to mention it.
5. Add any additional comments or requests you may have.
6. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: True
* **Email Address**: Enter your professional or institutional email address.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: True
* **Institution**: Enter your university, college, or organization name.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: True
* **Role/Position**: Enter your current role or position.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: True
* **Academic Discipline**: Enter your primary field of study or work.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: True
* **Coaching Focus Area**: Choose the area you'd like to focus on in the coaching circle.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "Teaching strategies", "Research methods", "Career development", "Work-life balance", "Grant writing", "Publication", "Leadership skills", "Other"
	+ Required: True
* **Coaching Experience Level**: Choose your level of experience with peer coaching.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "Complete beginner", "Some experience", "Moderate experience", "Experienced coach", "Certified coach"
	+ Required: True
* **Weekday Availability**: Choose the weekday that works best for your coaching circle meetings.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday", "Thursday", "Friday", "Any weekday", "No weekday preference"
	+ Required: True
* **Meeting Time Preference**: Choose the time that works best for your coaching circle meetings.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "Morning (8am-12pm)", "Afternoon (12pm-5pm)", "Evening (5pm-8pm)", "Flexible", "Weekends only"
	+ Required: True
* **Meeting Format Preference**: Choose how you'd like to meet for coaching sessions.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "In-person", "Virtual", "Hybrid", "Flexible", "No preference"
	+ Required: True
* **Group Size Preference**: Choose the group size that works best for you.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "Small (3-4 people)", "Medium (5-6 people)", "Large (7-8 people)", "Any size", "No preference"
	+ Required: True
* **Coaching Commitment Level**: Choose how committed you are to participating regularly.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "High commitment", "Moderate commitment", "Flexible commitment", "Testing the waters"
	+ Required: True
* **Goals for Participation**: Enter your goals for participating in the coaching circle.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Special Accommodations**: If you need any special accommodations, mention them here.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Previous Coaching Experience**: If you've participated in peer coaching circles before, choose your experience level.
	+ Type: Select One
	+ Options: "Yes, multiple times", "Yes, once or twice", "No, but other coaching", "No, this is new to me"
	+ Required: False
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other information about your coaching circle preferences.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
