# Evidence Based Curriculum Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information from educators and subject matter experts to develop high-quality, evidence-based curriculum.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the title of the curriculum in the "Title" field.
2. Select the category of the curriculum from the provided options (Education or Other) in the "Category" field.
3. If the category is Other, provide a brief description of the curriculum in the "Form Category Other" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the evidence-based curricula you are registered for from the provided options (Curriculum A, Curriculum B, or Curriculum C) in the "Evidence Based Curriculum" field.
6. If you are registered for other curricula, provide a brief description in the "Other Curriculum" field.
7. If necessary, enter the name of the tool assigned to you for this task in the "Assigned Tool" field.
8. If you have any additional information about the output file, enter it in the "Output File" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (Text, Required): Provide a concise and descriptive title for the curriculum.
* **Category** (Text, Required): Choose from the options (Education, Other).
* **Category Other** (Text, Optional): Provide a brief description if you have chosen 'Other' as the category.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Evidence Based Curriculum** (Select Multiple, Required): Select from the provided options (Curriculum A, Curriculum B, or Curriculum C).
* **Other Curriculum** (Text, Optional): If you have selected other curricula, provide a brief description.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you.
* **Output File** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the output file.

## Tips

* Please fill in the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can develop high-quality, evidence-based curriculum.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your contribution to the development of high-quality curriculum!
