# Global Online Academy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Global Online Academy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field to ensure accuracy.
5. Select your time zone in the "Time Zone" field.
6. Type in your time zone abbreviation in the "Time Zone Abbreviation" field.
7. Choose a course from the "Course" field.
8. Select a date for the course in the "Date" field.
9. Select a time for the course in the "Time" field.
10. Enter any additional information in the "Additional Information" field.
11. Confirm your first name in the "Confirm First Name" field.
12. Confirm your last name in the "Confirm Last Name" field.
13. Enter your course abbreviation in the "Course Abbreviation" field.
14. Enter your course name in the "Course Name" field.
15. Enter your course code in the "Course Code" field.
16. Select a course date in the "Course Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, select_one, required/optional): Select your time zone.
* **Time Zone Abbreviation** (`timezone_abbreviation`, text, required/optional): Type in your time zone abbreviation.
* **Course** (`course`, select_one, required/optional): Select a course.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for the course.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select a time for the course.
* **Additional Information** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Confirm First Name** (`first_name_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Confirm your first name.
* **Confirm Last Name** (`last_name_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Confirm your last name.
* **Course Abbreviation** (`course_abbreviation`, text, required/optional): Enter your course abbreviation.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your course name.
* **Course Code** (`course_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your course code.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, required/optional): Select a course date.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any issues or questions, please contact the Global Online Academy support team.
