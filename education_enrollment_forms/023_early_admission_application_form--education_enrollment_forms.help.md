# Early Admission Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Early Admission Application Form is a generic application form designed for students seeking early admission to an academic program. This form is intended to gather information necessary for the admission process, including school information, student details, contact information, academic history, supporting documents, and academic programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the school information section (page 1) with your school's name and address.
2. In the student details section, provide your full name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
3. In the contact information section, enter your email address and phone number.
4. In the academic history section, write a brief overview of your academic background.
5. In the supporting documents section, upload any relevant documents, such as transcripts or certificates.
6. If applicable, select your preferred academic program(s) in the academic programs section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, required: false): This section is for entering your school's name and address. Please enter the name of the school you are currently or have previously attended.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required: false): This section is for entering your full name, date of birth, and any other relevant details. Please ensure that your details are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): This section is for entering your email address and phone number. Please ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Academic History** (`academic_history`, text, required: false): This section is for providing a brief overview of your academic background. Please be concise and provide relevant information about your educational qualifications and experience.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required: false): This section is for uploading any relevant documents, such as transcripts or certificates, to support your application. Please ensure that the documents are clear and legible.
* **Academic Programs** (`academic_programs`, select_multiple, required: false): This section is for selecting your preferred academic program(s). Please choose one or more programs that align with your interests and goals.
* **Submission** (`submission`, text, required: false): This section is for entering any additional comments or information about your application. Please keep this section brief and relevant to your application.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Be concise and clear in your academic history and submission sections.
* Upload clear and legible supporting documents.
* Choose your preferred academic programs carefully, as this will help the admission committee assess your eligibility.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the relevant department or authority for guidance.
