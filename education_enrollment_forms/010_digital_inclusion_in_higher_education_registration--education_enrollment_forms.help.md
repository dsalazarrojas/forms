# Digital Inclusion In Higher Education Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your personal and technological needs so that we can provide the necessary resources and support for a smooth and inclusive experience in higher education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "Student Information" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field.
5.  If you have a date of birth, enter it in the "Date of Birth" field.
6.  Enter your student email address in the "Student Email" field.
7.  Select any support needs you have from the "Support Needs" field.
8.  Provide a description of your support needs in the "Description of Support Needs" field.
9.  Select your preferred language from the "Preferred Language" field.
10.  Provide a description of your preferred language in the "Description of Preferred Language" field.
11.  Select any technology access you have from the "Technology Access" field.
12.  Select any device access you have from the "Device Access" field.
13.  Provide a description of your device access in the "Description of Device Access" field.
14.  Select any internet access you have from the "Internet Access" field.
15.  Provide a description of your internet access in the "Description of Internet Access" field.
16.  Select any technology needs you have from the "Technology Needs" field.
17.  Provide a description of your technology needs in the "Description of Technology Needs" field.
18.  Select any accommodation needs you have from the "Accommodation Needs" field.
19.  Provide a description of your accommodation needs in the "Description of Accommodation Needs" field.
20.  Select your preferred device from the "Preferred Device" field.
21.  Provide a description of your preferred device in the "Description of Preferred Device" field.
22.  Select your preferred device type from the "Preferred Device Type" field.
23.  Provide a description of your preferred device type in the "Description of Preferred Device Type" field.
24.  If you have any other needs, please select them from the "Other Needs" field.
25.  If you have any other comments, please enter them in the "Student Comments" field.
