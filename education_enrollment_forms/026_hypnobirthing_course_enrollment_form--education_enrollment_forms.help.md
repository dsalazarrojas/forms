## Purpose
The Hypnobirthing Course Enrollment Form is designed to collect essential details from students enrolling in the Hypnobirthing course. This form helps the administrative team to efficiently manage course enrollments and provide necessary support during the course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each field and understand what information is required.
2. Fill in your personal information, such as your name and email address.
3. Provide your date of birth and emergency contact information.
4. Choose the correct relationship with your emergency contact.
5. Select the type of program you are enrolling in.
6. Enter your course start and end dates.
7. Provide your payment information, including the payment method and status.
8. Complete any additional fields as necessary, such as student and teacher comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): This field is for the student's name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): This field is for your email address. Please ensure it is correct and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number. Please enter it as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, optional): This field is for your date of birth. Please enter it in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): This field is for the course start date. Please enter it in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): This field is for the course end date. Please enter it in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): This field is for your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): This field is for your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): This field is for your relationship with your emergency contact. Please select the correct option from the list.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): This field is for the type of program you are enrolling in. Please select the correct option from the list.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): This field is for the program start date. Please enter it in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): This field is for the program end date. Please enter it in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Program Fees** (`program_fees`, text, optional): This field is for any additional fees associated with your program enrollment.
* **Program Location** (`program_location`, text, optional): This field is for the location of your program.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): This field is for your payment method. Please select the correct option from the list.

* **Program Status** (`program_status`, select_one, optional): This field is for your program status. Please select the correct option from the list.
