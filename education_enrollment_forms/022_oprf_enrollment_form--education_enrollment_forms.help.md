# OPRF Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The OPRF Enrollment Form is a multi-page form designed to collect basic contact information and program-specific details for the purpose of enrolling into one of three available programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your **First Name** (page 1).
2. On the same page, fill out **Last Name** (page 1).
3. Proceed to the next page and enter your **Email** address (page 2). Please ensure it is formatted correctly (e.g., `[user_name]@example.com`).
4. On the following page, enter your **Phone** number (page 3).
5. On the next page, select the **Program** you are interested in enrolling into from the provided options (page 4).
6. On the following page, choose a date for your **Enrollment Date** (page 5) and select a time for your **Enrollment Time** (page 6).
7. On the final page, leave any additional **Notes** you may have about your enrollment (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name (if applicable).

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the correct format (e.g., `[user_name]@example.com`).

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Program
* **Program** (`program`, select_one, required): Select the program you are interested in enrolling into from the provided options.

### Enrollment Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose a date for your enrollment.

### Enrollment Time
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose a time for your enrollment.

### Notes
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Leave any additional notes about your enrollment (if applicable).
