# diesel_engine_wiring_course_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll in a diesel engine welding course.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2.  Enter your Email in the Email field.
3.  Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4.  Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5.  Enter the course name in the Course Name field.
6.  Select the Course Level from the dropdown menu.
7.  Select the Student Status from the dropdown menu.
8.  Enter the Class Date in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
9.  Enter the Instructor Name in the Instructor Name field.
10.  Enter the Instructor Title in the Instructor Title field.
11.  Enter the Course Description in the Course Description field.
12.  Enter the Course Location in the Course Location field.
13.  Enter your Student Address in the Student Address field.
14.  Enter your Student City in the Student City field.
15.  Enter your Student State in the Student State field.
16.  Enter your Student Postal Code in the Student Postal Code field.
17.  Select the Course Status from the dropdown menu.
18.  Enter the Course Start Date in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
19.  Enter the Course End Date in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
20.  Enter the Course Duration in the Course Duration field.
21.  Enter the Course Hours in the Course Hours field.
22.  Enter the Course Price in the Course Price field.
23.  Enter the Instructor Price in the Instructor Price field.
24.  Enter the Student Price in the Student Price field.
25.  Enter any additional notes about the course or student in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your name as it is written on your ID.
- **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Course Name** (Course Name, text, required): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
- **Course Level** (Course Level, select_multiple, required): Select the level of the course you are enrolling in from the dropdown menu.
- **Student Status** (Student Status, select_multiple, required): Select your student status from the dropdown menu.
- **Class Date** (Class Date, date, required): Enter the date of the class in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Instructor Name** (Instructor Name, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor.
- **Instructor Title** (Instructor Title, text, required): Enter the title of the instructor.
- **Course Description** (Course Description, text, required): Enter a description of the course.
- **Course Location** (Course Location, text, required): Enter the location of the course.
- **Student Address** (Student Address, text, required): Enter your address.
- **Student City** (Student City, text, required): Enter your city.
- **Student State** (Student State, text, required): Enter your state.
- **Student Postal Code** (Student Postal Code, text, required): Enter your postal code.
- **Course Status** (Course Status, select_multiple, required): Select the status of the course from the dropdown menu.
- **Course Start Date** (Course Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the course in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Course End Date** (Course End Date, date, required): Enter the end date of the course in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Course Duration** (Course Duration, text, required): Enter the duration of the course.
- **Course Hours** (Course Hours, text, required): Enter the hours of the course.
- **Course Price** (Course Price, number, required): Enter the price of the course.
- **Instructor Price** (Instructor Price, number, required): Enter the price of the instructor.
- **Student Price** (Student Price, number, required): Enter the price of the student.
- **Notes** (Notes, note, required): Enter any additional notes about the course or student.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are correctly filled out before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any fields, contact the system administrator for assistance.
