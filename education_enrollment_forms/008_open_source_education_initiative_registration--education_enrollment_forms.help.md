# Open Source Education Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Source Education Initiative Registration form is designed to collect participant information for the Open Source Education Initiative. This form is a crucial step in registering for the initiative and ensuring that your organization or institution can participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by providing your participant information, including your name, interests, and consent to participate.
2.  Next, provide details about your organization or institution, including its interests and goals.
3.  Select your community group(s) that align with your organization or institution.
4.  Confirm your non-profit status and education participant status.
5.  List your program details, including participants, goals, and partnerships.
6.  Provide any additional notes or information about your organization or institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Information (1)**:
    *   **Participant Information (id: 1, name: participant_info, type: text, required: false)**: This field is used to capture general information about the participant. Please provide your name in this field.
*   **Participant Interests (2)**:
    *   **Participant Interests (id: 2, name: interests, type: select_multiple, required: true)**: Select all the interests that align with your organization or institution.
*   **Consent (3)**:
    *   **Consent (id: 3, name: consent, type: select_one, required: true)**: Select the option that indicates your consent to participate in the Open Source Education Initiative.

*   **Non-Profit Status (8)**:
