# Music School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from students who want to enroll in the music school. This form will help the music school staff to provide an optimal learning experience for the students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First Name, Last Name, Date Of Birth).
2. Enter your contact information (Contact Number, Email).
3. Choose your preferred instrument to play (Instrument).
4. Select your student category (Student Category).
5. Choose your student level (Student Level).
6. Choose your payment method (Payment Method).
7. Add any additional notes (Notes).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number that the music school can use to reach you.
* **Instrument**: Choose the instrument you want to learn to play.
* **Student Category**: Select all the categories that apply to you (Violin, Cello, Piano).
* **Student Level**: Choose your current level of expertise (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Preferred Time**: Enter the time of day you prefer to take lessons (not implemented, this field might not be relevant).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your lessons (Cash, Credit/Debit, Bank Transfer).
* **Notes**: Add any additional information that will help the music school staff to understand your learning needs.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth enrollment process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the music school staff.
* Please note that the music school will contact you to confirm your enrollment and provide further instructions.
