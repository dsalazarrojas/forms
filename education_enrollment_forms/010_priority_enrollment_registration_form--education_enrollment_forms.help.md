# Priority Enrollment Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Priority Enrollment Registration Form is designed to gather information from applicants for priority enrollment in an educational program. This form captures individual details and priority status to facilitate the enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information about yourself as the applicant.
2. Select your priority status (High, Standard, or Low Priority) to indicate the urgency of your enrollment.
3. Choose any supporting documents you have that are relevant to your application.
4. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as the applicant.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Priority Status**: Select your priority status from the options provided (High, Standard, or Low Priority).
* **Supporting Documents**: Choose any relevant documents you have that support your application (ID Card, Birth Certificate, or Transcript).
* **Submit Button**: This button is not a field but an action item. You will not be able to edit or submit your answers without taking this action.
