# Academic Learning Support Hub Access Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Learning Support Hub Access Request Form is a form designed to collect information from students, faculty, and staff who need access to specific resources or services for academic support. This form is used to ensure that requests are properly assessed and approved by faculty or staff members before granting access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in a new tab or window to avoid losing your progress.
*   Enter the following required information:
    *   Your full name as the requester (field 2)
    *   Your institutional email address (field 3)
    *   Your affiliation (Student, Faculty, Staff, or Other) (field 4)
    *   The purpose of your access request (field 6)
    *   A preferred start date for access (field 8)
*   Select the resources or services you require for access (field 7)
*   Optionally, you can provide additional comments or information to support your request (field 10)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Requester name** (field 2): Please enter your full name as the requester.
    *   This information will be used to identify you as the person requesting access.
*   **Requester email** (field 3): Please enter your institutional email address.
    *   This information will be used for internal communication and approval purposes.
*   **Affiliation** (field 4): Select your affiliation as Student, Faculty, Staff, or Other.
    *   This information will help us understand your role and provide you with the correct resources.
*   **Student id or employee number** (field 5): If available, please provide your institutional identifier.
    *   This information will help us verify your identity.
*   **Purpose of access** (field 6): Select the reason for your access request.
    *   This information will help us understand your needs and assign the correct resources.
*   **Requested resources or services** (field 7): Please list the resources or services you need.
    *   This information will help us understand your specific requirements.
*   **Preferred start date** (field 8): Enter the date you would like to start accessing the resources.
    *   This information will help us schedule your access.
*   **Approver confirmation** (field 9): This field is not required, but it is meant for a faculty or staff approver to confirm your access request.
*   **Additional comments** (field 10): Please provide any other information to support your request.
    *   This field is optional but can be useful for us to better understand your needs.

## Tips

*   Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
*   This form was created to provide you with an easy way to request access to academic resources and services.
*   We will review your request promptly and assign you with the correct resources.
