# Academic Peer Network Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you express your interest in joining the Academic Peer Network. It will help us match you with suitable peers for collaboration and knowledge sharing. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure the right connections are made within the network.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the required fields, marked with an *.
2. For the optional fields, you may choose to answer them based on your preferences.
3. Be specific and honest when answering the questions to ensure you're matched with the right peers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (2): Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (3): Your professional or institutional email address.
* **Institution** (4): The name of your university, college, or organization you are affiliated with.
* **Role/Position** (5): Your current role or position within your institution.
* **Academic Discipline** (6): Your primary field of study or work.
* **Years of Experience** (7): The number of years you have experience in your field.
* **Type of Network Interested In** (8): Choose one or more of the options that best describe the type of peer network you are interested in joining (e.g., Research collaboration, Teaching strategies, Career development, etc.).
* **Participation Goals** (9): Describe your primary goals for joining this network.
* **Availability Level** (10): Choose how much time you can dedicate to network activities per week (e.g., High, Moderate, Low, Variable).
* **Preferred Interaction Format** (11): Select all the ways you'd like to interact with network members (e.g., Virtual meetings, In-person events, Online forums, etc.).
* **Geographic Scope** (12): Choose the geographic scope of the network that interests you (e.g., Local, Regional, National, International).
* **Previous Network Participation** (13): Have you participated in similar networks before? If so, describe your level of involvement.
* **Collaboration Interest** (14): Are you interested in collaborating with network members on projects? If so, what kind of projects?
* **Special Expertise Offered** (15): If you have unique skills or expertise you can offer to the network, describe them here.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (16): Do you have any special requirements for participating in network activities? If so, describe them.
* **Additional Comments** (17): Any other information about your interest in the network?
