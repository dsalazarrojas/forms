# Education Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students to create a record for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
* Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field.
* Select your date of birth in the "Birthday" field.
* Select your gender from the options provided in the "Gender" field.
* Select the class you are registering for from the options provided in the "Class" field.
* Enter the name of the course you are registering for in the "Course Name" field.
* Enter the course code in the "Course Code" field.
* You may optionally provide any additional information in the "Any Other Info" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required, Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Required, Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Required, Email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (Required, Text): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Student ID** (Required, Text): Enter your student ID number as per the institution's requirement.
* **Birthday** (Required, Date): Select your date of birth from the provided format.
* **Gender** (Required, Select One): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Class** (Required, Select Multiple): Select all classes you are registering for from the provided options.
* **Course Name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Code** (Required, Text): Enter the course code for the course you are registering for.
* **Any Other Info** (Optional, Note): If you need to provide any additional information, please enter it here.
