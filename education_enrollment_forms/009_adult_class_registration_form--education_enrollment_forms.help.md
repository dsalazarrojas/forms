# Adult Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Class Registration Form is a mandatory form for students who want to enroll in one of the institution's adult classes. By filling out this form, you will be able to select a class, provide your personal details, and choose a payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form online or in person, depending on your preference.
* Enter your student's full name in the "Student full name" field.
* Provide a valid email address in the "Email address" field for registration confirmation.
* Choose how you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency by filling out the "Emergency contact name and phone" field.
* Select the class you would like to enroll in from the "Selected course" dropdown menu.
* If you have prior experience that is relevant to the class, describe it in the "Prior experience or prerequisites" field.
* Choose your preferred start date for the class in the "Approximate start date for enrollment" field.
* Select a payment method from the "Payment method" dropdown menu.
* Finally, agree to the institution's terms and policies by selecting the correct option in the "Agree to terms and policies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student full name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to send you registration confirmation and class updates.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your mobile or landline phone number for emergency contact.
* **Selected course** (`selected_course`, `select_one`, required): Choose the class you would like to enroll in from the dropdown menu. Options include:
  * Evening language course
  * Professional development workshop
  * Computer skills basics
  * Photography fundamentals
* **Approximate start date for enrollment** (`preferred_start_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to start the class. This is an approximate start date and may be subject to change.
* **Prior experience or prerequisites** (`prior_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any relevant experience or prerequisites you have for the class.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you will pay for the class from the dropdown menu. Options include:
  * Credit card
  * Bank transfer
  * PayPal
  * Invoice
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and phone number of a contact person to reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Agree to terms and policies** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select "I agree" to indicate that you have read and agreed to the institution's terms and policies.
