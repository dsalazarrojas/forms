# Enrolment Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrolment Information Form is a crucial document that collects essential details about a student's enrolment in a program. This form helps the institution to verify the student's information and their emergency contact's details for enrolment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name and contact information.
2. Provide the student's date of birth.
3. Identify the student's relationship with their emergency contact.
4. Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
5. Select the program name and start date.
6. Choose the program duration.
7. (Optional) Select a graduation date, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the school/ institution the student is enrolling in.
* **Student Name** (text, required): Enter the student's full name.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the student's emergency contact.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (select_one, required): Choose the relationship between the student and their emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Student Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Program Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the program the student is enrolling in.
* **Program Start Date** (time, required): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program Length** (number, required): Enter the duration of the program in months or years.
* **Graduation Date** (date, optional): If the program has an end date, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

Note: The graduation date is not required if the program is ongoing or has no end date.
