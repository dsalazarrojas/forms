# Conference Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Expense Claim Form is used for submitting expenses related to conference or event attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and receipts before submitting the form.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Itemize your expenses in the "Expense Details" section.
4. Attach all relevant receipts.
5. Obtain approval from your manager before submitting the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Claim Date** (<code>claim_date</code>, required): This field is where you enter the date you submit this claim.
* **Employee Name** (<code>employee_name</code>, required): This is your full name.
* **Department** (<code>department</code>, required): Please enter your department name.
* **Manager Name** (<code>manager_name</code>, required): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Email Address** (<code>email_address</code>, required): Enter your contact email.
* **Conference Name** (<code>conference_name</code>, required): Enter the name of the conference or event.
* **Conference Location** (<code>conference_location</code>, required): Enter the city and venue of the conference.
* **Start Date** (<code>start_date</code>, required): Enter the date you departed for the conference.
* **End Date** (<code>end_date</code>, required): Enter the date you returned from the conference.
* **Registration Fee** (<code>registration_fee</code>, optional): If you incurred a registration fee, enter the amount.
* **Airfare** (<code>airfare</code>, optional): If you incurred airfare expenses, enter the amount.
* **Ground Transportation** (<code>ground_transportation</code>, optional): If you incurred ground transportation expenses, enter the amount.
* **Accommodation** (<code>accommodation</code>, optional): If you incurred accommodation expenses, enter the amount.
* **Meals** (<code>meals</code>, optional): If you incurred meals expenses, enter the amount.
* **Incidentals** (<code>incidentals</code>, optional): If you incurred other expenses, enter the amount.
* **Total Amount** (<code>total_amount</code>, required): This is the sum of all your expenses.
* **Reimbursement Method** (<code>reimbursement_method</code>, required): Choose how you want to receive your reimbursement.
* **Bank Account Number** (<code>bank_account</code>, optional): If you choose direct deposit, enter your bank account number.
* **Receipts Attached** (<code>receipts_attached</code>, required): Indicate if you have all receipts attached.
* **Business Justification** (<code>business_justification</code>, required): Provide a brief explanation why this expense was necessary.
* **Employee Signature** (<code>employee_signature</code>, required): Certify that this claim is accurate.
* **Manager Approval** (<code>manager_approval</code>, optional): Your manager can approve or deny this claim.
