# Travel Agent Income Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agent Income Form is a tool designed to gather information about travel agents' income from various sources. It is crucial to report income accurately for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form once per travel period.
- Enter the travel period in the "Travel Period" field (Field 2).
- Enter the total income earned during this travel period in the "Total Income" field (Field 3).
- Enter any expenses incurred during this travel period in the "Expenses" field (Field 4).
- Select the type of income earned (e.g., Commissions, Fees, or Other) in the "Income Type" field (Field 5).
- Select all applicable expense types (e.g., Transportation, Accommodation, Food, or Miscellaneous) in the "Expense Type" field (Field 6).
- Enter the travel date in the "Travel Date" field (Field 7).
- Enter the travel duration in the "Travel Duration" field (Field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1** (Travel Agent Income Form) - A page where you can start filling out the form.
* **Travel Period** (Field 2): Enter the travel period during which you earned income or incurred expenses.
* **Total Income** (Field 3): Enter the total income earned during the travel period.
* **Expenses** (Field 4): Enter any expenses incurred during the travel period.
* **Income Type** (Field 5): Select the type of income earned (e.g., Commissions, Fees, or Other).
* **Expense Type** (Field 6): Select all applicable expense types (e.g., Transportation, Accommodation, Food, or Miscellaneous).
* **Travel Date** (Field 7): Enter the travel date.
* **Travel Duration** (Field 8): Enter the duration of the travel.
