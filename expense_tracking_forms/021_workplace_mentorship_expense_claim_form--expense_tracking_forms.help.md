# Workplace Mentorship Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for submitting claims for expenses related to the Workplace Mentorship program. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before starting.
2. Fill out the form completely, including all required fields.
3. Review your answers carefully, as they will be used to process your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mentorship Program**: This field is used to describe the specific mentorship program you are claiming expenses for. Please enter the name of the program you are claiming expenses for.
* **Date Of Claim**: This field is used to record the date you are submitting the claim. Please enter the date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`. This field is required.
* **Amount Claimed**: This field is used to record the total amount you are claiming as an expense. Please enter the amount in the format `xx.xxxxx`. This field is required.
* **Purpose Of Claim**: This field is used to describe the reason for your expense claim. Please select the most appropriate option from the list.
	+ Program Development: For expenses related to developing a new program within the mentorship program.
	+ Program Management: For expenses related to managing a program within the mentorship program.
	+ Travel: For expenses related to travel related to the mentorship program.
* **Supporting Documents**: This field is used for any additional documentation you would like to include with your claim. Please use this field if you have any supporting documents that back up your claim.
* **Submitter Email**: This field is used to capture your email address. Please enter the email address associated with your company account. This field is required.
* **Submitter Phone**: This field is used to capture your phone number. Please enter your work phone number.
* **Submitter Note**: This field is an optional note field. You can use this to include any additional information that may be helpful to support your claim.
* **Submitter Name**: This field is used to capture your name. Please enter the name associated with your company account. This field is required.
* **Submitter Title**: This field is used to capture your title within your company. Please enter the title associated with your company account. This field is optional.
