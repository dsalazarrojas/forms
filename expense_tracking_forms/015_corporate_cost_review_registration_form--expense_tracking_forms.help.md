# Corporate Cost Review Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather and record information about corporate expenses submitted by employees. The data collected will be reviewed and analyzed to ensure compliance with company policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to the best of your abilities.
2. Ensure that you have all necessary supporting documents attached to your submission.
3. Review and verify the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Employee Name** (employee_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your company ID card.
- **Department** (department, select_one, optional): Indicate if your department is affected by this expense.
- **Expense Type** (expense_type, select_multiple, required): Check all the applicable expense types (Yes, No) that relate to this submission.
- **Expense Date** (expense_date, date, required): Enter the date of the expense in the format MM/DD/YY.
- **Expense Amount** (expense_amount, number, required): Enter the total cost of the expense.
- **Supporting Documents** (supporting_documents, note, optional): Attach any relevant documents or receipts supporting your expense submission.
- **Approval Status** (approval_status, select_one, optional): Indicate the status of your approval (Active, Inactive).
- **Assigned Approver** (assigned_approver, select_one, optional): Select the person assigned to review and approve your submission (Yes, No).

**Tips**

- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled.
- Be accurate when entering dates and amounts.
- Attach any supporting documents to justify your expenses.
- This form is not intended to replace other expense tracking systems or processes. If you are unsure about any part of this form, consult your supervisor or HR representative.
