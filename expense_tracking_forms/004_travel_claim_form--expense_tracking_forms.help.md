# travel_claim_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The travel claim form is used by employees or representatives to submit claims for travel-related expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief description of the travel claim in the text area.
2. Select the type of expense from the list of options (e.g., Airfare, Hotel, etc.).
3. Enter the date the expense was incurred in the date field.
4. Enter your email address as the claimant in the email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the claimant_phone field.
6. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form in the submitter_email field.
7. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form in the submitter_phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Claim**: (`travel_claim`, text, required) Enter a brief description of the travel claim.
* **Expense Type**: (`expense_type`, select_one, required) Select the type of expense from the list (e.g., Airfare, Hotel, etc.).
* **Expense Date**: (`expense_date`, date, required) Enter the date the expense was incurred.
* **Description**: (`description`, text, optional) Enter a detailed description of the expense.
* **Claimant Email**: (`claimant_email`, email, required) Enter your email address as the claimant.
* **Claimant Phone**: (`claimant_phone`, text, optional) Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email**: (`submitter_email`, email, required) Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone**: (`submitter_phone`, text, optional) Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter all required fields (Travel Claim, Expense Type, Expense Date, Claimant Email, and Submitter Email) to ensure your form is complete and can be processed.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact your supervisor or HR department.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate.
- If the form is incomplete or contains errors, it may not be processed correctly.
