# Expense Reconciliation Tool Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Expense Reconciliation Tool Registration form is used for employees to register for company-approved expense reconciliation tools. This form helps to collect and verify employee information and tool details, ensuring accurate and efficient expense reconciliation processing.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have the necessary approvals from your manager or supervisor.
2. Fill out the form only once for each tool you want to register.
3. Enter the required information in the corresponding fields, which are indicated by a required flag.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Info** (`employee_info`, text, optional): Enter your employee information for registration purposes.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your employee identification.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address for communication purposes.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number for contact purposes.
* **Tool Name** (`tool_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tool you are registering for.
* **Tool Description** (`tool_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the tool you are registering for.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the correct tool you are registering for from the provided options.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Enter the text to be displayed on the submission button.
