# Relocation Expense Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Relocation Expense Claim Form is used to document business-related moving expenses incurred by employees when they relocate to a new work location that is at least 50 miles from their previous residence.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your personal details (Employee Name).
2.  Enter the date you incurred the expense (Expense Date).
3.  Provide the new work location (Expense Location).
4.  Indicate whether the expense was business-related (Business Expense) or not (Non-Business Expense).
5.  Enter the total expense amount (Expense Amount).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your personnel file.
*   **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required): Enter the date you incurred the expense in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Expense Location** (`expense_location`, text, required): Enter the city and state of your new work location.
*   **Business Expense** (`business_expense`, text, required): Check the box if the expense was for business purposes.
*   **Non-Business Expense** (`non_business_expense`, text, required): Check the box if the expense was not for business purposes.
*   **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, number, required): Enter the total expense amount, including receipts and documentation as requested by your finance department.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure proper reimbursement.
*   Attach receipts and documentation as requested by your finance department.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR representative or finance department for assistance.
