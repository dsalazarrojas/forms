# Expense Recovery Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to gather information for the Expense Recovery Program, a program that allows employees to recover expenses related to work-related activities and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Ensure that you only submit a single expense claim per form.
3. If you've already submitted an expense claim for the same date of expense, update the submission remarks to indicate that this is a revision of your previous claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User ID** (`user_id`, number, required)
The User ID is used to identify the user submitting the form. Please fill in your unique ID as assigned to you by your system administrator.

### **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required)
The Employee ID is used to identify the employee who incurred the expense. Please fill in your departmental ID as assigned to you by your HR department.

### **Date of Expense** (`date_of_expense`, date, required)
The Date of Expense is the date when the expense was incurred.

### **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, date, required)
The Expense Date is the date when the expense was incurred.

### **Expense Receipts** (`expense_receipts`, text, required)
The Expense Receipts field is where you'll upload or enter any supporting receipts or documents for the expense. Please attach or enter any relevant receipts or documents that support your expense claim.

### **Reason for Expense** (`reason_for_expense`, text, required)
The Reason for Expense field is where you'll describe the reason for the expense. Please be as detailed as possible to help the program administrators understand the business need behind your expense.

### **Submissions** (`submissions`, text, required)
The Submissions field is where you'll list all previous submissions for the same expense. If you've already submitted an expense claim for the same date of expense, please provide a list of all previous submissions, including submission remarks.

### **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required)
The Submission Date is the date when the expense claim was submitted.

### **Submission Remarks** (`submission_remarks`, text, required)
The Submission Remarks field is where you'll provide any additional remarks or comments about your submission. If this is a revision of a previous submission, please indicate this in the remarks.

Tips: 

- Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and accurately.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your system administrator or program administrator for clarification.
- Please be honest and accurate when filling in the form.
