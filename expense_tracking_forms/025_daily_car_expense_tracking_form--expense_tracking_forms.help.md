# Daily Car Expense Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps users track daily car expenses, including fuel consumption, maintenance, and other costs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are:
  * odometer_reading
  * fuel_consumed
  * fuel_type
  * vehicle_repair_cost
  * tolls_taxes
2. Enter the date of the day you want to report expenses for.
3. Select the type of fuel used (Petrol, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric).
4. Enter the amount of fuel consumed (in a unit of your choice, e.g., liters, gallons).
5. Enter the amount of the vehicle repair cost, if any.
6. Enter the amount of tolls and taxes paid.
7. Enter any other expenses (e.g., parking fees, maintenance costs).
8. Add any notes about your trip.
9. Enter your contact information (email and phone number) for any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### date
* **Date** (`date`, not required): Enter the date of the day you want to report expenses for.

### odometer_reading
* **Odometer Reading** (`odometer_reading`, required): Enter the current odometer reading of your vehicle.

### fuel_type
* **Fuel Type** (`fuel_type`, required): Select the type of fuel used for your vehicle (Petrol, Diesel, Hybrid, Electric).

### fuel_consumed
* **Fuel Consumed** (`fuel_consumed`, required): Enter the amount of fuel consumed on this day.

### vehicle_repair_cost
* **Vehicle Repair Cost** (`vehicle_repair_cost`, optional): Enter any vehicle repair cost for this day.

### distance_driven
* **Distance Driven** (`distance_driven`, not required): Enter the total distance driven on this day.

### vehicle_repair_cost
* **Vehicle Repair Cost** (`vehicle_repair_cost`, optional): Enter any vehicle repair cost for this day.

### parking_fees
* **Parking Fees** (`parking_fees`, not required): Enter any parking fees paid on this day.

### tolls_taxes
* **Tolls and Taxes** (`tolls_taxes`, not required): Enter any tolls and taxes paid on this day.

### maintenance_cost
* **Maintenance Cost** (`maintenance_cost`, not required): Enter any maintenance cost for this day.

### other_expenses
* **Other Expenses** (`other_expenses`, not required): Enter any other expenses (e.g., tolls, parking fees, maintenance costs).

### notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, not required): Add any notes about your trip.

### email
* **Email** (`email`, not required): Enter your email address for any additional comments or questions.

### phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, not required): Enter your phone number for any additional comments or questions.

Note: This form should be repeated for each day you want to report expenses for.
