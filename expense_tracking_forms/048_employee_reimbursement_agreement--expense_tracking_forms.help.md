# Employee Reimbursement Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Reimbursement Agreement form is used to document and track employee expenses for reimbursement. This form helps employees to provide detailed information about their expenses, including employee name, employee ID, travel dates, total expense claimed, claim date, category, and reimbursed amount. The form will help the accounting department to process reimbursement claims efficiently and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the required information before filling out the form.
2. Enter your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
4. Select the date of your travel in the "Date of Travel" field.
5. Enter the total amount of your expense claim in the "Total Expense Claimed" field.
6. Enter the date you are claiming the expense reimbursement in the "Claim Date" field.
7. Select the category of your expense from the options provided in the "Category" field.
8. Enter the reimbursed amount in the "Reimbursed Amount" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID card.
* **Employee ID**: Enter your employee ID number for identification purposes.
* **Date of Travel**: Enter the date you traveled for business or personal purposes.
* **Total Expense Claimed**: Enter the total amount of your expense claim, including receipts and supporting documents.
* **Claim Date**: Enter the date you are submitting your expense claim.
* **Category**: Select the category of your expense from the options provided:
	+ **Travel**: For expenses related to business travel.
	+ **Entertainment**: For expenses related to entertainment.
	+ **Miscellaneous**: For other expenses not covered by the above categories.
* **Reimbursed Amount**: Enter the amount reimbursed after processing your expense claim.
