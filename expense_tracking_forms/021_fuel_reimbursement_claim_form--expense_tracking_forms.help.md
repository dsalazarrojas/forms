## Fuel Reimbursement Claim Form - Help Guide

### Purpose
The Fuel Reimbursement Claim Form is used to track and manage fuel-related expenses for reimbursement purposes.

### How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether the claim should be submitted or not by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the "Submit Claim" dropdown menu.
2. Enter the date the claim was submitted in the "Date Claim Submitted" field.
3. Enter the start and end mileage in the "Mileage Start" and "Mileage End" fields, respectively.
4. Calculate the total mileage by entering the difference between "Mileage End" and "Mileage Start".
5. Choose whether the fuel type is 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. Enter the fuel cost in the "Fuel Cost" field.
7. Select whether the submitter is 'Yes' or 'No'.
8. Enter the submitter's email address in the "Submitter Email" field.
9. Enter the submitter's phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
10. Choose the department for the claim by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
11. Select the claim status as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
12. Enter the date the claim was submitted by the submitter in the "Submitter Date" field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submit Claim**: Select 'Yes' if you want to submit the claim, 'No' otherwise.
* **Date Claim Submitted**: Enter the date the claim was submitted, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Mileage Start**: Enter the start mileage of the trip.
* **Mileage End**: Enter the end mileage of the trip.
* **Total Mileage**: This field will automatically calculate the difference between "Mileage End" and "Mileage Start" (required if 'Submit Claim' is 'Yes').
* **Fuel Type**: Choose 'Yes' if the claim is related to fuel, 'No' otherwise.
* **Fuel Cost**: Enter the total cost of the fuel used (only applicable if 'Submit Claim' is 'Yes' and 'Fuel Type' is 'Yes').
* **Submitter**: Choose 'Yes' if the claim is submitted by the person filling out the form, 'No' otherwise.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter the email address of the person submitting the claim (only applicable if 'Submitter' is 'Yes').
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person submitting the claim (only applicable if 'Submitter' is 'Yes').
* **Department**: Choose the department related to the claim by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Claim Status**: Select the status of the claim as 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date the claim was submitted by the submitter in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
