# Travel Expense Receipt Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document travel expenses for business purposes. It gathers information about employee's travel expenses, including date, employee name, and expense details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields marked as "required" and the optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure that you have not previously submitted a travel expense receipt with the same date, employee, and expense type.
3. If you are unsure about any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Expense Receipt Validation Form** (`title`, , required/optional): This is the form title, which cannot be edited.
* **Date** (`date`, , required/optional): The date of the travel expense receipt.
* **Employee Name** (`employee-name`, , required/optional): The name of the employee who submitted the receipt.
* **Business Unit** (`business-unit`, , required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the expense was on behalf of the business unit.
* **Expense Type** (`expense-type`, , required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the expense is a business expense.
* **Expense Amount** (`expense-amount`, , required/optional): Enter the expense amount.
* **Currency** (`currency`, , required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the currency used.
* **Receipt Image** (`receipt-image`, , required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the receipt has an attached image.
* **Receipt Description** (`receipt-description`, , required/optional): Enter a brief description of the receipt.
* **Receipt Date** (`receipt-date`, , required/optional): The date of the receipt.

## Tips

* Ensure that you provide accurate information to enable the accounting team to process the expense reimbursement efficiently.
* Attach any supporting documents or images to the receipt if required.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the accounting team for assistance.
