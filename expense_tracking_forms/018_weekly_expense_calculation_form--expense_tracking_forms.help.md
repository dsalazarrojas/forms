# Weekly Expense Calculation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report weekly expenses across different categories for internal expense tracking or budgeting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and make sure you've filled in all required fields.
2. Select your total expenses from the "Total Expenses" field.
3. Answer "Yes" or "No" to whether you have expenses in each category (Food, Transportation, Other).
4. If you have expenses in a category, enter the amount and name of the expense in the corresponding fields (e.g., "Category 1" and "Category 1 Label", etc.).
5. Enter the total amount of expenses for each category that you have selected as having expenses in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Total Expenses**: Enter the total amount of expenses you've had for the week.
* **Expense Categories**: Check "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have expenses in each category. If you've selected "Yes", you'll be asked to provide more details in the corresponding category fields.
* **Food**:
	+ **Category 1**: Enter the amount of food expenses you've had this week.
	+ **Category 1 Label**: Enter a brief description of your food expenses (e.g., "Lunch at restaurant").
* **Transportation**:
	+ **Category 2**: Enter the amount of transportation expenses you've had this week.
	+ **Category 2 Label**: Enter a brief description of your transportation expenses (e.g., "Gas for the week").
* **Other**:
	+ **Category 3**: Enter the amount of other expenses you've had this week.
	+ **Category 3 Label**: Enter a brief description of your other expenses (e.g., "Miscellaneous purchases").
* **Category 4**:
	+ **Category 4**: Enter the amount of expenses for this category.
	+ **Category 4 Label**: Enter a brief description of your expenses for this category.
* **Total Food**: Enter the total amount of food expenses you've had this week.
* **Total Transportation**: Enter the total amount of transportation expenses you've had this week.
* **Total Other**: Enter the total amount of other expenses you've had this week.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your expense report.

## Tips
* Make sure to be accurate when entering your expenses to ensure accurate tracking and reporting.
* If you're unsure about a category, you can always ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
