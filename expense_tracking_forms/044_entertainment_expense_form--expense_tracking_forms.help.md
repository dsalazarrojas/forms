# entertainment_expense_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for tracking company-sponsored entertainment expenses, such as dining out or attending events. Please use it only for such events, not for other business expenses.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
2. Describe the purpose of the event, e.g., "Dinner meeting with clients" or "Team-building activity."
3. Enter the total expense amount for the event, e.g., "$200 for dinner."
4. Select the type of expense that best describes the event, e.g., "Business Meal" or "Business Entertainment."
5. Enter the location of the event, e.g., "Restaurant XYZ" or "Convention Center."
6. Provide a brief description of the expense, e.g., "Client dinner meeting at Restaurant XYZ."
7. Enter the date you received the receipt or invoice for the expense, in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
8. If you have a receipt or invoice for the expense, attach it here. You can upload a file or take a photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Date of Event**: `YYYY-MM-DD`
* **Purpose of Event**: Briefly describe the purpose of the event.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the total expense amount for the event.
* **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense that best describes the event.
* **Expense Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Expense Description**: Provide a brief description of the expense.
* **Date of Receipt**: `YYYY-MM-DD`
* **Receipt Attachment**: Attach a receipt or invoice for the expense.

Note: This help guide is not meant to be a comprehensive resource, but rather a quick reference to help users understand the form's purpose and how to complete it accurately.
