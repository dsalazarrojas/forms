# Event Expense Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Expense Request Form is used to track and record expenses related to company events or activities. This form is a centralized platform for employees, volunteers, and managers to report expenses incurred during such events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the expense from the provided options.
2. Provide a brief description of the request.
3. Choose the date of the expense and the expense date.
4. Enter the total amount of the expense.
5. Choose the type of expense from the provided options.
6. Select the employee or volunteer responsible for the expense.
7. Choose the currency of the expense.
8. Choose whether the expense has been approved or rejected.
9. If rejected, provide a brief reason for rejection.
10. Finally, select the person who submitted the expense request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Expense Request Form**: This is the title of the form. It is not a field to be filled out but a heading for the form.
* **Expenses**: This field is for listing the various expenses. Enter any expenses you incurred during the event or activity.
* **Date of Request**: Select the date on which you submitted this expense request.
* **Date of Expense**: Select the date when the expense was actually incurred.
* **Expense Total**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
* **Expense Type**: Choose the type of expense from the options provided. Examples include travel, food, or entertainment.
* **Expense Item**: Describe the item or service you are requesting as an expense.
* **Employee or Volunteer**: Select the employee or volunteer responsible for the expense.
* **Description of Request**: Provide a brief description of the reason for the expense request.
* **Expense Amount**: Enter the amount of the expense in the selected currency.
* **Approved**: Choose whether the expense has been approved or rejected.
* **Rejected Reason**: If the expense was rejected, provide a brief reason for rejection.
* **Expense Currency**: Choose the currency in which the expense was incurred.
* **Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the expense request.
* **Expense Category**: Select the category of the expense from the options provided.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a similar request to avoid duplication.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing a description of the request.
* If the expense has been rejected, provide a brief reason for rejection to help with future requests.
