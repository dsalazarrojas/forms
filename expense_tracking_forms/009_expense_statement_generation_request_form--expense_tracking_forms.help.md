# Expense Statement Generation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable employees to request an expense statement for themselves or on behalf of another employee. The form collects necessary information to process the request and ensure accurate reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Employee ID**: Enter the ID number of the employee who is requesting the expense statement.
2. **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee who is requesting the expense statement.
3. **Employee Email**: Enter the email address of the employee who is requesting the expense statement.
4. **Expense Type**: Select the type of expense the employee is incurring, which can be:
	* Employee Expenses
	* Company Expenses
	* Travel Expenses
	* Meal Expenses
	* Others
5. **Itemized Expenses**: Indicate whether the request is for itemized expenses or not.
6. **Receipts**: Indicate whether receipts are attached to the request or not.
7. **Submit Request**: Enter a brief description of the request.
8. **Note**: Add any additional information about the request.
9. **Category**: Enter a brief description of the category of the expense.
10. **Description**: Enter a brief description of the expense.
11. **Assigned Tool**: Enter the name of the tool or resource used for the expense.
12. **Form ID**: Enter a unique ID for the form (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (number, required): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your employee record.
* **Employee Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Expense Type** (select_one, required): Select the type of expense you are requesting.
* **Itemized Expenses** (select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are requesting itemized expenses.
* **Receipts** (select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you are attaching receipts to the request.
* **Submit Request** (text, required): Enter a brief description of your request.
* **Note** (note, required): Add any additional information about your request.
* **Category** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the category of your expense.
* **Description** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of your expense.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Enter the name of the tool or resource used for the expense.
* **Form ID** (text, optional): Enter a unique ID for the form (if required by your manager).

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed before submitting the form.
