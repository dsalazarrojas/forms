# Budget Management Portal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Budget Management Portal Registration Form is designed to collect essential information for user registration, ensuring proper onboarding and access control within the portal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your applicant name in the **Applicant name** field.
3. Enter your work email in the **Contact email** field.
4. Select your department or cost center from the list.
5. Choose your role in budget management from the options provided.
6. (Optional) Specify any specific permissions you require.
7. Provide a brief justification for your portal access in the **Justification** field.
8. Enter your manager's approval email.
9. Add any onboarding notes for the portal setup team (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the portal.
* **Contact email**: Enter your work email address that you want to use for portal access.
* **Department**: Select the department or cost center you belong to.
* **Role**: Choose your role in budget management from the options:
	+ **Viewer**: Allows you to view budget information.
	+ **Editor**: Grants access to edit budget information.
	+ **Approver**: Enables you to approve budget requests.
	+ **Admin**: Gives full administrative access to the portal.
* **Requested permissions**: (Optional) Specify any specific permissions you require, if applicable.
* **Justification**: Explain why you need access to the portal.
* **Manager approval email**: Enter your manager's email for approval.
* **Onboarding notes**: Add any notes for the portal setup team (if necessary).
