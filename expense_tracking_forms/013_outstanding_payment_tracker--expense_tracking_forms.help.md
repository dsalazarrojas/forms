# outstanding_payment_tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The outstanding_payment_tracker form is used to track outstanding payments. You can use this form to record and manage details about pending and overdue payments.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the outstanding payment details in the text field.
2. Provide payer details in the text field.
3. Enter the payment amount in the numeric field.
4. Select the due date using the date picker.
5. Select the payment status from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **outstanding_payment_details** (outstanding_payment_details, text, required): Enter a brief description of the outstanding payment.
- **payer_details** (payer_details, text, required): Enter information about the payer, such as their name or account number.
- **payment_amount** (payment_amount, number, required): Enter the total amount of the payment.
- **due_date** (due_date, date, required): Select the date by which the payment is due.
- **payment_status** (payment_status, select_multiple, required): Select the current status of the payment from the available options:
  - pending
  - paid
  - pending payment
  - payment processing
  - overdue
- **payment_note** (payment_note, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.
