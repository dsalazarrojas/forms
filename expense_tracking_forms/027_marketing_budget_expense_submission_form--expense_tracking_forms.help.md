# Marketing Budget Expense Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit marketing budget expense information, providing details on the date of submission, marketing budget, budget currency, expense category, expense amount, expense type, reason for the expense, notes, email address, and phone number. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Form Submit Date** (`form_submit_date`): This is a date field where you can enter the date you are submitting this form. 
2. **Marketing Budget** (`marketing_budget`): Enter the budget for your marketing expenses.
3. **Marketing Budget Currency** (`marketing_budget_currency`): Select the currency for your marketing budget from the available options (USD, EUR, or GBP).
4. **Marketing Expense Category** (`marketing_expense_category`): Choose one or more categories that the expense belongs to from the available options (Category 1, Category 2, or Category 3).
5. **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`): Enter the amount of the expense.
6. **Expense Type** (`expense_type`): Select the type of expense from the available options (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
7. **Expense Reason** (`expense_reason`): Describe the reason for the expense in a text field.
8. **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional notes regarding the expense.
9. **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
10. **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
