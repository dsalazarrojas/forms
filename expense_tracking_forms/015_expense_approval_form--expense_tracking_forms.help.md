# Expense Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tracking and approving employee expenses, ensuring accurate and timely processing of expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, you need to fill in the required fields, which will depend on the fields marked as "required" below. Please make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Id**
 (`user_id`, `number`, required: `false`)

*   A unique identifier for the user who is submitting the expense.
*   Please enter your unique user ID as a number.

### 2. **Date**
 (`date`, `date`, required: `false`)

*   The date when the expense was incurred.
*   Enter the date in the format you are most comfortable with, as long as it is a valid date.

### 3. **Expense Item Id**
 (`expense_item_id`, `number`, required: `false`)

*   A unique identifier for the expense item.
*   Please enter the expense item ID if you know it.

### 4. **Amount**
 (`amount`, `number`, required: `false`)

*   The amount of the expense.
*   Enter the amount as a number without any currency symbols or commas.

### 5. **Submitter Id**
 (`submitter_id`, `number`, required: `false`)

*   A unique identifier for the person submitting the expense.
*   Please enter the submitter ID as a number.

### 6. **Approver Id**
 (`approver_id`, `number`, required: `false`)

*   A unique identifier for the person who approved the expense.
*   Please enter the approver ID as a number.

### 7. **Submitted At**
 (`submitted_at`, `time`, required: `false`)

*   The time when the expense was submitted.
*   Enter the submitted time in 24-hour format with four digits for the hour and two for the minutes.

### 8. **Updated At**
 (`updated_at`, `time`, required: `false`)

*   The time when the expense was last updated.
*   Enter the updated time in 24-hour format with four digits for the hour and two for the minutes.

### 9. **Approved At**
 (`approved_at`, `time`, required: `false`)

*   The time when the expense was approved.
*   Enter the approved time in 24-hour format with four digits for the hour and two for the minutes.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
*   Enter accurate and truthful information to avoid any discrepancies.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.

Note: The fields marked as "required: `false`" can be left blank if you don't have the necessary information. However, please be aware that leaving those fields blank may delay or impact the processing of your expense.
