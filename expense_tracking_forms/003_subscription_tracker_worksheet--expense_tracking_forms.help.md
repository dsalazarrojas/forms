# Subscription Tracker Worksheet - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Subscription Tracker Worksheet is a form designed to track and manage subscription-based services or products. It helps you collect and organize essential information about these services, making it easier to handle billing, accounting, or customer relationship management tasks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Service Name** field with the name of the service or product you're tracking.
2. In the **Service Start Date** field, input the date the service began.
3. If this service is still active, you don't need to fill in a **Service End Date**. If it's inactive, input the date it ended.
4. Choose whether the service is **Recurring** or not.
5. Enter the **Service Price** (e.g., $10, $20, etc.).
6. Select the **Status** of the service from the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
7. In the **Note** field, provide any additional information about the service.
8. If available, enter the **Email** or **Phone** contact information for the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Service Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter the name of the service or product you're tracking.
- **Service Start Date** (2, date, required: false): Input the date the service began.
- **Service End Date** (3, date, required: false): If the service is inactive, enter the date it ended. Otherwise, leave blank.
- **Is Recurring?** (4, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether the service is recurring or not. Options: Yes, No.
- **Service Price** (5, number, required: false): Enter the price of the service (e.g., $10, $20, etc.).
- **Status** (6, select_one, required: false): Select the status of the service from the options: Active, Inactive, or Pending.
- **Note** (7, note, required: false): Provide any additional information about the service.
- **Email** (8, email, required: false): If available, enter the email contact information for the service.
- **Phone** (9, text, required: false): If available, enter the phone contact information for the service.
