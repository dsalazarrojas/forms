# monthly_spending_details_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The monthly_spending_details_form is a simple tool for tracking and recording details about your monthly spending, including income and expenses. It helps you to capture essential information about your financial transactions during a specific month and year.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month and year you want to track details for.
2. Enter the date of your expenses.
3. Select the type of expense (e.g., expense or income).
4. Enter the amount of your expense or income.
5. Specify the category and subcategory of your expense.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes about your expense or income.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **monthly_spending** (`monthly_spending_details_form_1`): Enter a brief description of the month and year you are tracking.
* **expense_date** (`expense_date`): Enter the date of your expense.
* **income_date** (`income_date`): Enter the date of your income.
* **expense_type** (`expense_type`): Select "expense" for an expense or "income" for an income.
* **amount** (`amount`): Enter the amount of your expense or income.
* **note** (`note`): Enter any additional comments about your expense or income.
* **email** (`email`): Enter your email address (not used for this form).
* **phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number (not used for this form).
* **address** (`address`): Enter your address (not used for this form).
* **expense_category** (`expense_category`): Select the category of your expense (e.g., food, transportation, etc.).
* **expense_subcategory** (`expense_subcategory`): Select the subcategory of your expense.
* **expense_description** (`expense_description`): Enter a description of your expense.
* **amount_currency** (`amount_currency`): Select the currency of your expense or income (e.g., USD, USD(C), EUR, etc.).
* **income_currency** (`income_currency`): Select the currency of your income (e.g., USD, USD(C), EUR, etc.).
* **total_expenses** (`total_expenses`): This field is not used (not implemented).
* **total_income** (`total_income`): This field is not used (not implemented).
* **total_expenses_currency** (`total_expenses_currency`): This field is not used (not implemented).
* **total_income_currency** (`total_income_currency`): This field is not used (not implemented).
* **month** (`month`): Select the month you are tracking (e.g., January, February, etc.).
* **year** (`year`): Enter the year you are tracking.
* **year_starting** (`year_starting`): Enter the starting year of your tracking (not used).
* **notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your expense or income.
* **comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your expense or income.
* **category_1** (`category_1`): This field is not used (not implemented).
* **category_2** (`category_2`): This field is not used (not implemented).

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct month and year for the form.
* Be accurate when entering the date of your expenses and income.
* Select the correct category and subcategory for your expenses.
* Enter all required information for each expense or income.
* Review and submit the form for accurate tracking.
