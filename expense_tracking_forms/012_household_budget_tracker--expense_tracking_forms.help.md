# Household Budget Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your household expenses, income, and savings over time. It will guide you through various fields to provide a clear picture of your financial situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "household_budget" page to start entering your household's income and expenses.
2. Enter the total income for your household in the "income" field.
3. Enter the total expenses for your household in the "expenses" field.
4. Enter the amount you have saved so far in the "savings" field.
5. Select the "income_source" page to provide details about the source of your income.
6. Select the "budgeted_expenses" page to track your allocated expenses.
7. Select the "budgeted_savings" page to set a target savings goal.
8. Choose your progress towards your savings goal in the "progress" page.
9. Select a savings goal amount in the "goal" page.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "note" fields, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Household Budget** (`household_budget`, Text, required): Enter a brief description of your household's income and expenses.
* **Income** (`income`, Number, required): Enter your household's total monthly income.
* **Expenses** (`expenses`, Number, required): Enter your household's total monthly expenses.
* **Savings** (`savings`, Number, required): Enter the amount you have saved so far.
* **Savings Goal** (`savings_goal`, Number, required): Set a target savings goal.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, Text, required): Specify the source of your income.
* **Allocated Expenses** (`expenses_2`, Text, required): Track your allocated expenses.
* **Progress** (`progress`, Select One, required): Choose how much progress you've made towards your savings goal.
* **Savings Progress** (`savings_progress`, Text, required): Track your savings progress.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, required): Enter your household's date of birth (or founding date).
* **Date** (`date`, Select One, required): Select a time period for tracking (e.g., month, year).
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, Time, required): Enter the current time of day.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, required): Enter your email address for correspondence.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Savings Goal Amount** (`goal`, Select One, required): Set a target savings goal amount.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, Email, required): Enter your second email address for correspondence.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`, Text, required): Enter your secondary phone number for contact.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, Note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, Date, required): Enter your household's date of death (or dissolution date).
* **Date of Birth 2** (`date_of_birth_2`, Date, required): Enter your household's alternative date of birth (or founding date).
