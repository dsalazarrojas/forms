# travel_expense_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and document employee travel expenses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the dates of your travel in the "Report travel dates" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your trip in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the expense amount and select the expense type (Business Class, Economy, or First Class) in the "Expense amount" and "Expense type" fields, respectively.
4. Indicate who reimbursed your expenses (Business Class, Employee, or Company) in the "Reimbursed by" field.
5. Enter the name of the person submitting the claim in the "Claimant name" field.
6. If approved, enter the date of approval in the "Approval date" field.
7. Enter the name of the person approving the claim in the "Approver name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Report travel dates (id: 1)**: Enter the dates of your travel in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Description (id: 2)**: Briefly describe your trip, including the purpose and destination.
* **Expense amount (id: 3)**: Enter the total expense amount for your trip.
* **Expense type (id: 4)**: Select the class of service you used for your trip (Business Class, Economy, or First Class).
* **Reimbursed by (id: 5)**: Indicate who reimbursed your expenses (Business Class, Employee, or Company).
* **Claimant name (id: 6)**: Enter your name as the person submitting the claim.
* **Approval date (id: 7)**: Enter the date the claim was approved.
* **Approver name (id: 8)**: Enter the name of the person approving the claim.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid delays in the approval process.
- Double-check your information to ensure that it is correct before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your supervisor or the finance department.
