# Home Office Deduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Office Deduction Form is used to track and report home office expenses for employees who work from home. The form is completed by the employee and reviewed and signed off by both the employee and their manager.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee ID number.
2. Enter the date you are submitting this form.
3. Enter your gross income for the period (this will help with tax calculations).
4. Describe the location of your home office.
5. List any home office equipment used for work purposes.
6. List any expenses you have incurred for home office (e.g., internet, utilities, etc.).
7. Select the type of expense (e.g., office supplies, software, etc.).
8. Enter the amount of expense incurred.
9. Select the frequency of expense (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
10. Enter the total deduction amount.
11. Select any additional employees who may also work from home and their manager's signatures.
12. Select your manager's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id** (employee ID): Enter your employee ID number. This is used to identify you as an employee who works from home.
* **date**: Enter the date you are submitting this form. This helps with record-keeping and tracking.
* **gross_income**: Enter your gross income for the period. This information will help with tax calculations.
* **home_office_location**: Describe the location of your home office. Be as detailed as possible (e.g., apartment number, city, state, zip).
* **home_office_equipment** (home office equipment): List any equipment used for work purposes, such as a computer or printer.
* **home_office_expenses** (home office expenses): List any expenses you have incurred for home office, such as internet or utilities.
* **home_office_expenses_type** (expense type): Select the type of expense (e.g., office supplies, software, etc.).
* **home_office_expenses_amount** (expense amount): Enter the amount of expense incurred.
* **home_office_expense_frequency** (expense frequency): Select the frequency of expense (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **total_deduction_amount** (total deduction amount): Enter the total deduction amount.
* **employee_signoff** (employee signoff): Select any additional employees who may also work from home and their manager's signatures.
* **manager_signoff** (manager signoff): Select your manager's signature.

## Tips
Make sure to accurately complete each field as it applies to you. If you are unsure about any field, please consult your manager or HR representative.
