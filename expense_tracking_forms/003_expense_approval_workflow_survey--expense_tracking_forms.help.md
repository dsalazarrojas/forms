# Expense Approval Workflow Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Expense Approval Workflow Survey is a form used to track and approve expenses. It captures information about the expense, the approver's decision, and other relevant details. This form is used to ensure that all expenses are reviewed and approved correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the **Approver Name** field with the name of the person who is approving the expense. This is a required field.
2. Enter the **Approver Email** field with the email address of the approver. This is also a required field.
3. Fill out the **Expense Date** field with the date the expense was incurred.
4. Enter a brief **Expense Description** of the expense in the corresponding field.
5. Choose the **Expense Category** from the dropdown menu (Yes/No).
6. Enter any **Approver Comments** in the text area provided.
7. Choose the **Approver Approval Status** from the dropdown menu (Active/Inactive).
8. Select the **Approver Decision Date** and **Approver Decision** from the respective fields.
9. Choose the **Approver Status** from the dropdown menu (Active/Inactive).
10. Select the **Approver Workflow** from the dropdown menu (Yes/No).
11. Fill out the **Approver Process** field with any additional information.
12. Select the **Approver Review** date.
13. If the expense is rejected, fill out the **Approver Rejection** field with the reason for rejection.
14. Enter the **Approver Rejection Date** and **Approver Rejection Reason**.
15. Choose the **Approver Rejection Status** from the dropdown menu (Active/Inactive).
16. Select the **Approver Rejection Workflow** from the dropdown menu (Yes/No).
