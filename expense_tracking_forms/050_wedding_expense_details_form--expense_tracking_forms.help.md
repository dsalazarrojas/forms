# Wedding Expense Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Expense Details Form is used to record and track individual expenses related to a specific wedding event. This form helps in maintaining accuracy and transparency in the financial aspects of the wedding planning process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Categories** field to select the relevant category for the expense.
2. Enter the **Date Spent** field to record the date the expense was incurred.
3. Enter the **Total Spent** field to record the total amount spent on the expense.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Categories**: 
  • (Select One): Choose the category for the expense from the provided options. 
  • Purpose: Selecting the correct category will help in organizing and categorizing expenses for easier tracking and analysis.
* **Date Spent**: 
  • (Date): Enter the date the expense was incurred.
  • Format: Use the YYYY-MM-DD format for accurate date entry.
  • Purpose: Recording the date helps in keeping track of the expense timeline.
* **Total Spent**: 
  • (Number): Enter the total amount spent on the expense.
  • Purpose: Accurately recording the total amount spent ensures accurate financial records.
