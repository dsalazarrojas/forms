# Rental Income And Expenses Worksheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users track and manage their rental income and expenses. It allows users to break down their income into different sources, frequencies, and amounts, and to capture other income and expenses related to rental properties.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the **Income Sources** field. Choose the types of income you have from your rental properties, such as **rental**, **investment**, or **other**.
2. Enter the **Property Address** in the corresponding field.
3. Select the **Rent Start Date** and **Rent End Date** for your rental property.
4. Choose the **Rental Frequency** (e.g., **monthly**, **quarterly**, or **yearly**) for your rent payments.
5. Enter the **Rent Amount** for each rent period.
6. If you have any other income not related to rent, enter it in the **Other Income** field.
7. Choose the **Other Frequency** (e.g., **monthly**, **quarterly**, or **yearly**) for your other income.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Income Sources** (rental, investment, other): List the types of income you receive from your rental property. This will help you categorize your income for easier tracking and management.
- **Property Address**: Enter the physical address of your rental property. This will help identify the property for which you are tracking income and expenses.
- **Rent Start Date** (mm/dd/yyyy): Enter the start date of your rent payments. Format the date as mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Rent End Date** (mm/dd/yyyy): Enter the end date of your rent payments. Format the date as mm/dd/yyyy. If you do not know the end date, enter a future date or leave it blank.
- **Rental Frequency** (monthly, quarterly, yearly): Choose how often you receive rent payments.
- **Rent Frequency** (monthly, quarterly, yearly): Choose how often your rent payments are made.
- **Rent Amount**: Enter the amount of rent you receive for each rent period.
- **Other Income**: Enter any other income not related to rent.
- **Other Frequency** (monthly, quarterly, yearly): Choose how often you receive other income.
