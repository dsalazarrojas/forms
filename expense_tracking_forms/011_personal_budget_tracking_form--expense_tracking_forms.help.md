# Personal Budget Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help individuals track their monthly income, expenses, savings, and financial goals, providing a comprehensive view of their personal finances.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by filling out the "Monthly Income" section, where you can input your total monthly income.
2. Next, move on to the "Expenses" section, where you can list your monthly expenses and categorize them by type.
3. In the "Savings" section, you can set a target range for your monthly savings goal.
4. The "Financial Goals" section allows you to set specific financial objectives, such as saving for a emergency fund or a down payment on a house.
5. Progress charts are provided to help you track your progress towards your financial goals.
6. Finally, review your form to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and consistently.
