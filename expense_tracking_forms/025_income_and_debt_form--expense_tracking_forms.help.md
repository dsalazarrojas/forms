# income_and_debt_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "income_and_debt_form" is designed to collect data on an individual's income and debt situation for expense tracking and financial planning purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Income and Debt Survey section with any relevant details.
2. Provide your average monthly income in the `income` field.
3. Enter your monthly gross income in the `monthly_gross` field.
4. Enter your total monthly net income (after taxes) in the `monthly_net` field.
5. Enter your total annual expenses in the `total_expenses` field.
6. Enter your average monthly debt in the `monthly_debt` field.
7. Enter your total annual debt in the `annual_debt` field.
8. Enter your yearly income in the `annual_income` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Income and Debt Survey**: This section is used to provide a brief overview of your income and debt situation.
* **Income**: Enter your average monthly income.
* **Monthly Gross**: Enter your monthly gross income before taxes.
* **Monthly Net**: Enter your monthly net income after taxes.
* **Total Expenses**: Enter your total annual expenses.
* **Monthly Debt**: Enter your average monthly debt.
* **Annual Debt**: Enter your total annual debt.
* **Annual Income**: Enter your yearly income.

## Tips
- Fill out the form accurately to ensure that you receive any financial benefits or support you may be eligible for.
