## Tips

Please note that the form purpose is to collect and submit expenses that have been incurred using a debit card. 
This form is for employees or users to submit information about their expenses, such as date, type, amount, and currency of the expense, as well as any supporting documentation like receipts. 
The form is used to track and manage expenses within the organization, ensuring that expenses are accurately recorded and reported. 
Before filling out the form, please ensure that you have all necessary documentation, such as receipts, ready for upload. 

### How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all necessary documentation, such as receipts, ready for upload.
2.  Fill out each field with the required information, as indicated in the field-by-field explanation below.
3.  Attach any required supporting documentation, such as receipts.
4.  Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Expense Date** (`expense_date`, `date`, optional): The date on which the expense was incurred.
*   **Expense Type** (`expense_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of expense, either Personal or Business.
*   **Expense Amount** (`expense_amount`, `number`, optional): The total amount of the expense, in the selected currency.
*   **Currency** (`expense_currency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the currency in which the expense was incurred.
*   **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, `text`, optional): A brief summary of the expense submission. Please provide a short description of the expense.
*   **Description** (`description`, `text`, optional): A detailed description of the expense.
*   **Receipt Image** (`receipt_image`, `text`, optional): Attach a receipt image if applicable.
*   **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the person or team submitting the expense.
*   **Approved By** (`approved_by`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the people or teams that have approved this expense.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Any comments or notes for the expense.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the submitter, in case additional information is required.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the submitter, in case additional information is required.)
