# Expense Report Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Report Verification Form is used to verify the legitimacy and accuracy of expense reports submitted by employees. This form helps ensure that expenses are properly accounted for and that the employee's claims are legitimate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **Employee ID**: Enter the employee's ID as per your organization's record.
	* **Submitted By**: Select "Manager" if you are the manager who submitted the report, or "Employee" if you are the one submitting the report.
	* **Approval Status**: Select "Approved" if the report is verified, or "Rejected" if it is not verified.
2. Provide additional information:
	* **Category**: Briefly describe the category of the expense (e.g., "Travel", "Entertainment", etc.).
	* **Amount**: Enter the total amount of the expense.
	* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the expense.
	* **Verified By**: Select who verified the report (Manager, Accountant, or HR).
	* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the report.
3. Date fields are not required but can be filled in if necessary:
	* **Date Submitted**: Enter the date the report was submitted.
	* **Date Verified**: Enter the date the report was verified.
