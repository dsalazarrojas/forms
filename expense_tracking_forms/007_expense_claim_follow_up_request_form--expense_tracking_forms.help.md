# Expense Claim Follow Up Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Claim Follow Up Request Form is a document used to update the status of an expense claim submitted earlier. It is essential to fill this form accurately and completely to ensure the correct follow-up actions are taken.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Details, including your name, ID number, or any other relevant information.
2. Provide the Claim Details, including any necessary information about the claim, such as the claim amount or submission date.
3. Select the Expense Category from the provided options, if applicable.
4. Fill in the Claim Amount, Date of Submission, and Time of Submission if required.
5. Describe the Expense Description and provide any additional Employee Comments.
6. Update the Submission Status if necessary.
7. Identify the Assigned User, if applicable.
8. Enter the Submission Date and Time if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Details**: Enter your Employee ID number or any other required information to identify yourself as the employee submitting this form.
* **Claim Details**: Provide any additional necessary information about the original claim, such as the claim amount or submission date.
* **Expense Category**: Choose the category that best describes the type of expense you are submitting.
* **Claim Amount**: Enter the exact amount of the expense claim.
* **Date of Submission**: Enter the date when the original expense claim was submitted.
* **Time of Submission**: Enter the time when the original expense claim was submitted.
* **Expense Description**: Briefly describe the expense you are claiming.
* **Follow-up Request**: If you have any specific requests or questions, enter them here.
* **Employee Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the submission.
* **Submission Status Update**: If the submission status has changed, update it here.
* **Assigned User**: Identify the person who will be following up on your submission, if assigned.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when the original submission was made.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time when the original submission was made.
* **Assigned User**: Identify the person responsible for handling your submission.
* **Assigned User**: This field should be left blank or removed.
