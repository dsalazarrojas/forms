# Expense Statement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Statement form is designed to collect information about a user's expenses. This form is used to document and track expenses for accounting and financial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the "Expense Statement" page, which serves as a general introduction to the form.
2. On the next page, select a "Category" for your expenses. This will help categorize and organize your expenses.
3. Finally, enter the "Total Expenses" amount, which is the total cost of your expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Expense Statement**: This is the main section of the form where you can provide a brief description of your expenses.
* **Category**: Select a category for your expenses to help categorize and organize them. This will help with future reference and reporting.
* **Total Expenses**: Enter the total cost of your expenses. This is a numerical field where you can input the total amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your expenses in the "Expense Statement" field.
* If you are unsure of the category for your expenses, select "Other" or choose a generic category to allow for future re-categorization.
* Double-check your total expense amount for accuracy to ensure correct financial reporting.
