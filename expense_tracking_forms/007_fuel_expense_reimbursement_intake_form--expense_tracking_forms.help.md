# Fuel Expense Reimbursement Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fuel Expense Reimbursement Intake Form is designed to gather information for fuel expenses related to work trips. This form will be used by employees to report their fuel expenses for reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the type of fuel you used (Gas, Diesel, or Hybrid).
2.  Enter the quantity of fuel used for the trip.
3.  Enter the date of the trip.
4.  Record the odometer reading at the start of the trip.
5.  Record the odometer reading at the end of the trip.
6.  Enter the total amount of reimbursement you are requesting.
7.  Enter your email address for communication and reimbursement purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Type of Fuel**: Choose the type of fuel used during the trip (Gas, Diesel, or Hybrid). This information helps the accounting team process your reimbursement accurately.
* **Fuel Quantity**: Enter the amount of fuel used during the trip. Be as precise as possible to ensure accurate calculation of reimbursement.
* **Date of Trip**: Enter the date of the trip. This helps the accounting team track expenses by date.
* **Odometer Reading Start**: Record the odometer reading at the start of the trip. This helps calculate the total distance traveled and ensure accurate reimbursement.
* **Odometer Reading End**: Record the odometer reading at the end of the trip. This helps calculate the total distance traveled and ensure accurate reimbursement.
* **Reimbursement Amount**: Enter the total amount of reimbursement you are requesting. This will be used by the accounting team to process your expense.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and reimbursement purposes. This ensures that the accounting team can contact you with any questions or issues related to your expense reimbursement.
