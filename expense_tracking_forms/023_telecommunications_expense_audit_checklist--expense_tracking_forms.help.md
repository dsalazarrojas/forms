# Telecommunications Expense Audit Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Telecommunications Expense Audit Checklist is a form designed to help companies review and manage their telecommunication expenses. It is used to document key information and findings, ensuring accurate accounting and cost control.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each section carefully, ensuring you understand the purpose of each field.
2. For each section, select the relevant option from the provided list or enter any additional information as required.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding to the next section.
4. Document any findings or discrepancies you encounter during the review.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Review Telecommunications Contract** (`telecoms_contract_review`, note, required: false): This field is used to review and document the details of telecommunication contracts, including rate schedules and contract end dates.
* **Review Invoices** (`invoice_review`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to select which types of invoices you have reviewed, such as Telecom services - monthly, Telecom services - annual, Equipment - monthly, or Equipment - annual.
* **Document Usage Patterns** (`usage_patterns`, note, required: false): Use this field to document any overuse or waste of telecommunication resources.
* **Review Vendor Management** (`vendor_management`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select the type of telecommunication services or equipment managed by the vendor.
* **Document Findings** (`findings`, note, required: false): Document all findings, including any discrepancies encountered during the review.
* **Review Telecom Services** (`review_telecom_services`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of telecommunication services you have reviewed.
* **Review Equipment** (`review_equipment`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select the type of equipment you have reviewed.
* **Document Cost Control** (`cost_control`, select_one, required: false): Document cost control, including any rate changes.
* **Review Telecom Billing** (`review_telecom_billing`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to review and document telecom billing, including rate changes.
* **Review Internal Audits** (`review_internal_audits`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of internal audits you have reviewed.
* **Review External Audits** (`review_external_audits`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select the type of external audits you have reviewed.

## Tips

* Ensure you have all relevant information before completing the form.
* Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
* Document any discrepancies or findings encountered during the review.
* Adapt the form to suit your company's specific needs and circumstances.
