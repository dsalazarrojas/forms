# Fuel Expense Validation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fuel Expense Validation Form is used to gather information from employees for fuel expenses. This form helps ensure that all fuel expenses are properly validated and accounted for within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee who is submitting the expense report (page 1).
2. Enter the date of the expense (page 2).
3. Enter the amount spent (page 3).
4. Enter the location of the expense (page 4).
5. Enter the purpose of the expense (page 5).
6. Enter the mileage driven (page 6).
7. Enter the vehicle used for the expense (page 7).
8. Enter the mileage rate (page 8).
9. Add any additional notes about the expense (page 9).
10. Enter the email and phone number of the employee submitting the expense report (page 10).
11. Enter the form ID (page 11).
12. If assigned to a specific tool or project, enter the tool name (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee** (`employee`, `select_one`, optional): Select the employee who is submitting the expense report. This helps the organization track whose expenses are being reported.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the expense. This helps to ensure that expenses are recorded in the correct time period.
- **Amount** (`amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the total amount spent on fuel.
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location where the expense was incurred.
- **Purpose** (`purpose`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the purpose of the expense.
- **Mileage** (`mileage`, `number`, optional): Enter the total mileage driven for the expense.
- **Vehicle** (`vehicle`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle used for the expense.
- **Mileage Rate** (`mileage_rate`, `number`, optional): Enter the mileage rate applicable to the expense.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the expense.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the employee submitting the expense report.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the employee submitting the expense report.
- **Form ID** (`form_id`, `text`, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the form, if required.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, optional): If assigned to a specific tool or project, enter the tool name.
