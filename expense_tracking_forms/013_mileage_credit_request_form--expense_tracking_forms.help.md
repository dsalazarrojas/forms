# Mileage Credit Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mileage Credit Request Form is used to request mileage credits for business trips. This form is designed to gather information about your trip, which will be reviewed and approved by an approver. Please fill out this form to request mileage credits for your business trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Trip Details page with the necessary information about your trip.
2. Select the correct options for the Supporting Documents and Approval fields.
3. Enter your email address in the Submitter field.
4. Choose the correct approver from the Approver field.
5. Enter any comments in the Comments field.
6. Select the submission date in the Submitted Date field.
7. If approved, select the correct approved date in the Approved Date field.
8. Enter your ID in the Submitted By ID field, if applicable.
9. Enter the approver's ID in the Approved By ID field, if applicable.
10. Finally, review your form to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting.
