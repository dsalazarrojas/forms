# First Notice Of Loss Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The First Notice Of Loss Form is a document used to report and record losses in the insurance industry. This form is designed to collect essential information about the loss, including the policy number, date, time, and location of the loss, as well as details about the insured and adjuster involved. This information is crucial for the insurance company to process and resolve claims efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the form in the "First Notice Of Loss Form" field.
2. Provide the policy number related to the loss in the "policy_number" field.
3. Enter the name of the insured person or entity affected by the loss in the "name_of_insured" field.
4. Describe the address where the loss occurred in the "address_of_loss" field.
5. Enter the date and time of the loss in the "date_of_loss" and "time_of_loss" fields, respectively.
6. Specify the location of the loss in the "location_of_loss" field.
7. Provide a detailed description of the loss in the "description_of_loss" field.
8. Enter the estimated damage amount in the "damage_amount" field.
9. Select the status of the loss (e.g., "reported", "verified", etc.) in the "loss_status" field.
10. Enter the status of the claim (e.g., "pending", "resolved", etc.) in the "claim_status" field.
11. Add any additional notes about the loss in the "adjuster_notes" field.
12. Enter the name of the insurance agent handling the claim in the "insurance_agent_name" field.
13. Provide the phone number of the insurance agent in the "insurance_agent_phone" field.
14. Sign and date the form as the adjuster in the "adjuster_signature", "adjuster_date", "adjuster_title", and "adjuster_company" fields.
15. Enter the name of the insurance company in the "insurance_company" field.
