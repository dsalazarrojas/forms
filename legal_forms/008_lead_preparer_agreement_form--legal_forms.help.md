# Lead Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the agreement between a lead preparer and a client, outlining the responsibilities and expectations of the lead preparer. It's essential to review and understand this form to ensure a smooth working relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the project information, including the project title and any relevant details.
2. Describe the project responsibilities and timelines.
3. Select the acceptance options (Client, Lead Preparer, or Preparer) to confirm your agreement.
4. Add any additional responsibilities or notes in the responsibilities field.
5. Fill in your name and email address in the lead preparer section.
6. Enter your contact information in the contact_info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_info** (1): Enter the project title, reference number, or any other relevant project details.
* **project_responsibilities** (2): Briefly describe your project responsibilities, including any deliverables or tasks assigned to you.
* **timelines** (3): Enter the project start and end dates, or any specific timelines that need to be met.
* **acceptance** (4): Select "Client" if you're accepting the project on behalf of the client, "Lead Preparer" if you're accepting the project as the lead preparer, or "Preparer" if you're accepting the project as a preparer.
* **responsibilities** (5): Add any additional responsibilities or notes related to the project.
* **lead_preparer_name** (6): Enter your name as the lead preparer.
* **lead_preparer_email** (7): Enter your email address as the lead preparer.
* **contact_info** (8): Provide any contact information, such as phone number or physical address, to help the client reach you.
* **lead_preparer_phone** (9): Enter your phone number as the lead preparer.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your project manager or contact the client for clarification.
* Complete this form once you've received a project assignment from the client and have agreed on the project terms.
* Keep this form on file for future reference and updates.
