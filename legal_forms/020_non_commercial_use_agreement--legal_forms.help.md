# Non Commercial Use Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non-Commercial Use Agreement form is used to document the terms and conditions of non-commercial usage of intellectual property. It helps to clearly define the scope of use, usage duration, and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Non Commercial Use" field with a brief description of the intended use of the intellectual property.
2. In the "Define Usage" field, specify how the intellectual property will be used.
3. Enter the duration of the non-commercial use in the "Duration" field.
4. Choose the specific area of non-commercial use from the "Non Commercial Use Area" dropdown menu.
5. Specify the version of the agreement in the "Agreement Version" field.
6. Choose the current status of the agreement from the "Agreement Status" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the non-commercial use.
8. Enter the version date of the agreement.
9. Add any additional comments or notes about the agreement in the "Comments" field.
10. Enter your email signature.
11. Finally, fill out the "Print Name" field with your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Commercial Use** (`non_commercial_use_agreement`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the non-commercial use.
* **Define Usage** (`non_commercial_usage_definition`, `text`, required: false): Specify how the intellectual property will be used.
* **Duration** (`non_commercial_usage_duration`, `number`, required: false): Enter the duration of the non-commercial use.
* **Non Commercial Use Area** (`non_commercial_usage_area`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the specific area of non-commercial use.
* **Agreement Version** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_version`, `text`, required: false): Specify the version of the agreement.
* **Agreement Status** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the current status of the agreement (Active or Inactive).
* **Date Start** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_date_start`, `date`, required: false): Enter the start date of the non-commercial use.
* **Date End** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_date_end`, `date`, required: false): Enter the end date of the non-commercial use.
* **Version Date** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_version_date`, `time`, required: false): Enter the version date of the agreement.
* **Comments** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_comments`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the agreement.
* **Signature** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_signature`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email signature.
* **Print Name** (`non_commercial_use_agreement_print_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your print name.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that the agreement is complete and accurate.
* Be clear and concise in your descriptions and explanations.
* Review the agreement carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.
