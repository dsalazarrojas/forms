# witness_testimony_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Witness Testimony Consent Form is a legal document that requires witnesses to provide their testimony and consent to the use of their statement as evidence. This form is used to collect essential information from witnesses, including their contact method, statement type, and agreement to the terms of the form.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your witness information.
2.  Write your witness statement.
3.  Choose your preferred contact method for follow-up questions.
4.  Select the type of statement you are providing (Witness, Affidavit, or Statement Under Oath).
5.  Agree to the terms of the form by signing the Witness Agreement section.
6.  Sign your witness statement with the Witness Signature section.
7.  Date the witness statement with the Date of Statement Signature Date section.
8.  Sign the witness declaration with the Witness Declaration Signature section.
9.  Date the witness declaration agreement with the Witness Declaration Agreement Signature section.
10. Date the witness signature with the Witness Signature Date section.
11. Date the witness testimony consent form agreement with the Witness Testimony Consent Form Agreement section.
