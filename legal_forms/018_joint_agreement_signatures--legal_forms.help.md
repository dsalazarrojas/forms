# Joint Agreement Signatures - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Agreement Signatures form is used to collect information from individuals who will be signing a joint agreement. This form captures details such as name, email, signature image, role, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Upload a scanned or photographed signature image in the "Signature" field.
3. Select the relevant role from the dropdown menu in the "Role" field.
4. Enter the date and time of signing in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
5. Provide additional contact information in the "Contact" field.
6. Add any relevant notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Repeat steps 1-6 for any additional signers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`agreement_signatures`, `text`, optional): Enter the first name of the signer.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the last name of the signer.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the signer.
* **Signature** (`signature_image`, `text`, optional): Upload a scanned or photographed signature image.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the signer's company.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Select the relevant role from the dropdown menu.
* **Date** (`sign_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of signing.
* **Time** (`sign_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of signing.
* **Location** (`sign_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of signing.
* **Contact** (`sign_contact`, `text`, optional): Provide additional contact information.
* **Note** (`sign_note`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments.
* **Sign Date 2** (`sign_date_2`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of signing for the second signer.
* **Sign Time 2** (`sign_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of signing for the second signer.
* **Sign Location 2** (`sign_location_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of signing for the second signer.
* **Sign Contact 2** (`sign_contact_2`, `text`, optional): Provide additional contact information for the second signer.
* **Sign Note 2** (`sign_note_2`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments for the second signer.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use a clear and legible font for the signature image.
* Use the dropdown menu to select the relevant role for the signer.
* Enter the date and time in the format MM/DD/YYYY and HH:MM AM/PM respectively.
* Use the space provided to add any additional contact information.
