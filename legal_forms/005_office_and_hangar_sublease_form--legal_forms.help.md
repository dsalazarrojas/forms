# office_and_hangar_sublease_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for an office and hangar sublease agreement, ensuring a formal understanding between the tenant and property manager.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled before proceeding.
3. Review the form carefully for any potential errors or misunderstandings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sublease Request**: A brief description of the sublease request, outlining the purpose and scope of the agreement.
* **Start Date**: The date the sublease is scheduled to begin. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date**: The date the sublease is scheduled to end. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Rental Term**: Select the duration of the sublease, either Weeks, Months, or Years.
* **Sublease Start Date**: The date the sublease is actually starting. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Sublease End Date**: The date the sublease is actually ending. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Sublease Rental Term**: Select the duration of the sublease, either Weeks, Months, or Years.
* **Tenant Name**: The name of the tenant, as they will be referred to in the agreement.
* **Company**: If the sublease is for a business, include the company name.
* **Email**: The contact email address for the tenant or property manager.
* **Phone**: The contact phone number for the tenant or property manager.
* **Property Manager**: The name of the property manager, if different from the tenant.
* **Tenant Signature**: A note field for the tenant to sign and date the agreement.
* **Manager Signature**: A note field for the property manager to sign and date the agreement.
* **Approval**: Select whether the sublease is approved or denied.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information regarding the sublease.
* **Approval Date**: The date the approval was granted.
* **Approval Time**: The time the approval was granted.
* **Approval Reason**: A brief explanation for the approval or denial.
* **Comments**: Additional comments or information regarding the sublease.
* **Approval Signature**: A note field for the property manager to sign and date the agreement.
* **Tenant Acknowledgement**: A field for the tenant to acknowledge and sign the agreement.
* **Manager Acknowledgement**: A field for the property manager to acknowledge and sign the agreement.
* **Sublease Agreement**: A field for the tenant and property manager to outline the terms and conditions of the sublease.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or information regarding the sublease.

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and valid information is entered.
- Review and sign the agreement with care and attention.
- Consult with the property manager if any field is unclear or requires further explanation.
- Save a copy of the completed form for future reference.
