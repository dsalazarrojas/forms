## Purpose
The form is used to collect and document the details of an "Integrity Pact Agreement" for structured cases.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the project name in the `project_name` field. This is the name of the project you are creating an agreement for.
2. Provide a brief description of the project in the `project_description` field. This will help identify the scope and nature of the project.
3. Enter the date you signed the agreement in the `date_signed` field.
4. Enter the date the project was completed in the `date_completed` field.
5. In the `case_manager` field, enter the name of the person managing the case.
6. Enter the name of the person in charge of the case in the `case_officer` field.
7. In the `declaration` field, enter any declaration or statement related to the agreement.
