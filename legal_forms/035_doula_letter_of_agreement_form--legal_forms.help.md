# Doula Letter Of Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doula Letter Of Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a doula's services, including payment details and cancellation policies. It helps establish clear expectations between the doula and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Describe the services provided in the "Service Details" field.
3. Enter your name as the doula in the "Doula Name" field.
4. Select the start and end dates of the service period in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
5. Provide the location of the service in the "Location" field.
6. Specify the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
7. Choose the payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
8. Select the cancellation policy from the "Cancellation Policy" dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional contact information in the "Contact Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the client.
* **Service Details**: Describe the services provided by the doula, including any specific details or expectations.
* **Doula Name**: Enter your name as the doula providing the services.
* **Start Date**: Select the date when the service will begin.
* **End Date**: Select the date when the service will end.
* **Location**: Enter the location where the service will take place.
* **Payment Terms**: Specify the payment terms, including any late fees or penalties.
* **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method from the available options (Bank, Check, Cash, or Other).
* **Cancellation Policy**: Select the cancellation policy that applies to the agreement (Client requests, Doula requests, Both, or Other).
* **Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information, such as phone numbers or email addresses.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and sign the agreement carefully before starting the service.
* Discuss any questions or concerns with your client before filling out the form.
* Keep a copy of the agreement for your records.
