# Name Change Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Name Change Declaration Form is a document used to declare a name change for an individual in legal settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information and documentation required for the name change.
2. Answer all questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Ensure that you have the correct dates (if applicable) and addresses.
4. If you are unsure about any information or questions, consult with the relevant authorities or seek guidance from a legal professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name Change Declaration Form** (`name_change_declaration_form`, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether you are declaring a name change.
* **Person Name** (`name_change_declaration_form_person`, select_one, required: false): Indicate whether the name change applies to yourself or another person.
* **Reason For Name Change** (`name_change_declaration_form_reason`, text, required: false): Enter the reason for the name change.
* **Date of Birth** (`name_change_declaration_form_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Death** (`name_change_declaration_form_date_of_death`, date, required: false): Enter the date of death (if applicable).
* **Current Address** (`name_change_declaration_form_current_address`, text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Previous Address** (`name_change_declaration_form_previous_address`, text, required: false): Enter your previous address (if applicable).
* **Relationship Status** (`name_change_declaration_form_relationship_status`, text, required: false): Indicate your relationship status (e.g., single, married, etc.).
* **Occupation** (`name_change_declaration_form_occupation`, text, required: false): Enter your occupation.
* **Employer** (`name_change_declaration_form_employer`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your employer.
* **Employer Address** (`name_change_declaration_form_employer_address`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's address.
* **Employer Phone** (`name_change_declaration_form_employer_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your employer's phone number.
* **Employer Email** (`name_change_declaration_form_employer_email`, email, required: false): Enter your employer's email address.
* **Employment Status** (`name_change_declaration_form_employment_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your employment status (e.g., active, inactive, etc.).
* **Employed** (`name_change_declaration_form_employment_status_employed`, text, required: false): Enter your employment status (if applicable).
* **Unemployed** (`name_change_declaration_form_employment_status_unemployed`, text, required: false): Enter your employment status (if applicable).
* **Marital Status** (`name_change_declaration_form_marital_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your marital status (e.g., married, unmarried, etc.).
* **Married** (`name_change_declaration_form_marital_status_married`, text, required: false): Enter your marital status (if applicable).
* **Unmarried** (`name_change_declaration_form_marital_status_unmarried`, text, required: false): Enter your marital status (if applicable).
* **Education** (`name_change_declaration_form_education`, text, required: false): Enter your education level.
* **Education Level** (`name_change_declaration_form_education_education_level`, select_one, required: false): Indicate your education level (e.g., primary, secondary, tertiary).
* **Primary** (`name_change_declaration_form_education_education_level_1`, text, required: false): Enter your primary education level (if applicable).
* **Secondary** (`name_change_declaration_form_education_education_level_2`, text, required: false): Enter your secondary education level (if applicable).
* **Tertiary** (`name_change_declaration_form_education_education_level_3`, text, required: false): Enter your tertiary education level (if applicable).
* **Declaration Date** (`name_change_declaration_form_declaration_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the declaration.
