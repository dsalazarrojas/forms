# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement is a legal document used to establish a confidential relationship between two or more parties. It ensures that sensitive information shared by one party will not be disclosed to third parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of agreement that best suits your needs (e.g., Non-Disclosure Agreement, Non-Compete Agreement, or Confidentiality Agreement).
2. Choose the duration of the non-disclosure agreement.
3. Enter the start date of the agreement.
4. Enter the end date of the agreement.
5. Provide a description of the confidential information being shared.
6. Specify the scope of the non-disclosure agreement.
7. Explain the purpose of the non-disclosure agreement.
8. Outline the obligations and responsibilities of both parties.
9. Identify the confidential matters involved.
10. Describe the terms of the agreement.
11. List the signatories involved in the agreement.
12. Add a signature date and time (optional).
13. Add a signature location (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the type of agreement that best suits your needs.
* **Non-Disclosure Duration** (`non_disclosure_duration`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Non-Disclosure Start Date** (`non_disclosure_start_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Non-Disclosure End Date** (`non_disclosure_end_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Confidential Information** (`confidential_information`, note, required: false):
	+ Describe the confidential information being shared.
* **Non-Disclosure Scope** (`non_disclosure_scope`, text, required: false):
	+ Specify the scope of the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Non-Disclosure Purpose** (`non_disclosure_purpose`, text, required: false):
	+ Explain the purpose of the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Non-Disclosure Obligations** (`non_disclosure_obligations`, text, required: false):
	+ Outline the obligations and responsibilities of both parties.
* **Confidential Matters** (`confidential_matters`, text, required: false):
	+ Identify the confidential matters involved.
* **Non-Disclosure Terms** (`non_disclosure_terms`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe the terms of the agreement.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required: false):
	+ Add any additional terms of the agreement.
* **Signatories** (`signatories`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ List the parties involved in the agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of the signature.
* **Signature Location** (`signature_location`, text, required: false):
