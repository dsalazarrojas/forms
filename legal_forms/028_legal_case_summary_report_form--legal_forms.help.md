# Legal Case Summary Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Case Summary Report Form is used to collect key information about a legal case, allowing users to summarize the essential details in a concise and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. For fields that are optional, provide only relevant and helpful details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Case Parties** (case_parties)
(case_parties, select_multiple, required: false)
This field allows you to indicate whether the case involves multiple parties. Choose 'Yes' if there are multiple parties involved, and 'No' if it involves only one party.

### **Case Courts** (case_courts)
(case_courts, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the court where the case is being heard.

### **Case Judgement** (case_judgement)
(case_judgement, select_one, required: false)
Choose the current status of the case, either 'Yes' for active cases or 'No' for inactive cases.

### **Case Status** (case_status)
(case_status, select_one, required: false)
Select the status of the case, which can be either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.

### **Case Date** (case_date)
(case_date, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the case.

### **Case Time** (case_time)
(case_time, time, required: false)
Enter the time of the case.

### **Notes** (notes)
(notes, note, required: false)
Provide any additional comments or notes about the case.

### **Case Number** (case_number)
(case_number, text, required: false)
Enter the case number, if applicable.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy and relevance of the information you enter in this form.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with a colleague or supervisor before proceeding.
* Use standard date and time formats to avoid confusion.
* Avoid entering duplicate or outdated information.
