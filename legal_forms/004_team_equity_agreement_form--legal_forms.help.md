# Team Equity Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Team Equity Agreement Form" is used to document the equity distribution among team members in a business setting. It provides a clear and concise way to outline the equity percentages and effective dates for each team member.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the team members who will be receiving equity distribution.
2. Choose the role of each team member and provide a description if necessary.
3. Enter the equity percentage for each team member.
4. Select the effective date for the equity distribution.
5. Add a comment or agreement text if necessary.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select all team members who will be receiving equity distribution. This field will help you identify all individuals involved in the agreement.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Choose the role or position of each team member to indicate their specific responsibility within the team.
* **Equity Percentages** (`equity_percentage`, number, optional): Enter the percentage of equity for each team member, up to two decimal places. This field is essential for calculating the actual equity distribution.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the equity distribution becomes effective. This field helps establish the timeline for the equity distribution.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign and date the form to confirm that the agreement has been reached and understood by all parties involved.
* **Agreement Text** (`agreement_text`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or explanations related to the agreement, if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand the form carefully before completing it.
* Ensure all team members are aware of the equity distribution and agree to the terms outlined in the form.
* Keep a record of the signed and dated forms for future reference and updates.
