# Legal Associate Employment Contract Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review the employment contract of a Legal Associate, ensuring they have properly reviewed and understood the terms of the contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully, and choose the correct answer based on your understanding and agreement.
2. Select "Yes" for questions you are confident in understanding and agreeing to the terms.
3. Select "No" for questions where you are not sure or disagree with the terms.
4. Make sure to answer all questions in this form to ensure your understanding and agreement.
5. Once you have completed this form, review your answers carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Review Form** (`1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" for each question, indicating your understanding and agreement with the terms of the contract.
* **Concerns** (`2`, `note`, optional): If you have any concerns or questions about the contract, please provide a brief explanation in this field.
* **Employment Contract** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you understand and agree to be bound by the employment contract.
* **Agreement Evaluations** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Choose "Yes" or "No" for each agreement, indicating your understanding and agreement to its terms.

## Tips

* Please review your answers carefully before submission to ensure accurate representation of your understanding and agreement.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please address them in the "Concerns" field.
* If you have any doubts about the contract, please reach out to the relevant parties for clarification.
* Ensure you understand the terms before selecting "Yes" for any questions.
* Take your time to complete this form accurately, as it is a crucial step in the employment contract process.
