# Naturalization Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document an individual's intention to naturalize, which is the process of becoming a citizen or obtaining permanent residency in a country or region. It's a crucial document that outlines the applicant's understanding of the naturalization process, their eligibility, and their commitment to the rules and regulations of the country or region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide accurate and complete information about yourself, including your name, date of birth, and contact details, in the **Applicant Details** section.
2. Make declarations and statements as requested in the **Declarant Declarations** section.
3. Provide information about your signature and its authenticity in the **Signatures** section.
4. Describe the contract details and terms in the **Contract Details** section.
5. Sign the **Naturalization Agreement** section to confirm your understanding and commitment to the naturalization process.
6. Review and sign the **Agreement Confirmation** section to confirm that you've read and understood the agreement.
7. Review the **Contract Review** section to ensure you've reviewed and agreed to all the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, required): Please provide your name, date of birth, and contact details to help us verify your identity.
* **Declarant Declarations** (`declarations`, text, required): Please make declarations and statements as requested, and ensure they are accurate and truthful.
* **Signatures** (`signature`, text, required): Please provide your signature to authenticate your identity and confirm your agreement to the naturalization process.
* **Contract Details** (`contract_details`, text, required): Please describe the contract terms and conditions, including any obligations or responsibilities you will assume as a naturalized citizen.
* **Naturalization Agreement** (`naturalization_agreement`, text, required): Please sign this section to confirm your understanding and commitment to the naturalization process.
* **Agreement Confirmation** (`agreement_confirmation`, text, required): Please review and sign this section to confirm that you've read and understood the agreement.
* **Contract Review** (`contract_review`, text, required): Please review and agree to all the terms and conditions outlined in the contract.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully to avoid delays or complications in the naturalization process.
- If you're unsure about any aspect of the naturalization process or the form, please consult the relevant authorities for guidance.
- Please sign all the required fields to authenticate your identity and confirm your understanding of the naturalization process.
