# Joint Venture Terms Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Venture Terms Acknowledgement Form is used to document the partnership terms of a joint venture. The form ensures that all parties involved are aware of and agree to the terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Partnership Name.
2. Fill in the Company Name.
3. Fill in the Business Name.
4. Fill in the Address.
5. Fill in the City.
6. Fill in the State.
7. Fill in the Zip Code.
8. Fill in the Contact Name.
9. Fill in the Contact Email.
10. Fill in the Contact Phone.
11. Fill in the Agreement Date.
12. Select one of the Joint Venture Agreement options.
13. Fill in the Acknowledgement Date.
14. Fill in the Acknowledgement By.
15. Fill in the Acknowledgement Time.
16. Fill in the Signed By.
17. Fill in the Role.
18. Fill in the Company Representative.
19. Fill in the Joint Venture Name.
20. Fill in the Partner Name.
21. Fill in the Partner Representative.
22. Fill in the Joint Venture Address.
23. Fill in the Partnership Percentage.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Name** (`partnernship_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the partnership.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the company.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the business.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Fill in the address of the joint venture.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Fill in the city of the joint venture.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Fill in the state of the joint venture.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Fill in the zip code of the joint venture.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Fill in the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Fill in the phone number of the contact person.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of the agreement.
* **Joint Venture Terms** (`joint_venture_terms`, text, required): Fill in the terms of the joint venture.
* **Joint Venture Agreement** (`joint_venture_agreement`, select_one, required): Select one of the joint venture agreement options.
* **Acknowledgement Date** (`acknowledgement_date`, date, required): Fill in the date of acknowledgement.
* **Acknowledgement By** (`acknowledgement_by`, text, required): Fill in the name of the person acknowledging.
* **Acknowledgement Time** (`acknowledgement_time`, time, required): Fill in the time of acknowledgement.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, required): Fill in the name of the person signing.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Fill in the role of the person.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, required): Fill in the name of the company representative.
* **Joint Venture Name** (`joint_venture_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the joint venture.
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Fill in the name of the partner.
* **Partner Representative** (`partner_representative`, text, required): Fill in the name of the partner representative.
* **Joint Venture Address** (`joint_venture_address`, text, required): Fill in the address of the joint venture.
* **Partnership Percentage** (`partnership_percentage`, number, required): Fill in the percentage of partnership.
