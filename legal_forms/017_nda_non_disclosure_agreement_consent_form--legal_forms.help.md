# Nda Non Disclosure Agreement Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from individuals or companies to not disclose confidential information. It is a requirement for companies to have their employees and contractors sign this form before commencing work on a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Name** in the "User Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Company** details in the "Company" field. This is a required field.
3. If you have a physical **Address**, fill it in the "Address" field. This is an optional field.
4. Enter your **Email** address for business purposes in the "Email" field. This is an optional field.
5. Fill in your **Phone Number** for business purposes in the "Phone Number" field. This is an optional field.
6. Select your **Role** from the options provided in the "Role" field. This is a required field.
7. Choose the type(s) of **Company Type** from the options provided in the "Company Type" field. This is a required field.
8. If applicable, fill in the **Project Name** and **Project Details** for the project you are working on.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Company** (Text, Required): Enter your company's name.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your physical work address. This field is only required if your physical address is different from your company's address.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your business email address. This is where we may contact you for work-related purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your business phone number. This is where we may call you for work-related purposes.
* **Role** (Select One, Required): Select the role you are working on the project, such as "Project Manager" or "Software Developer".
* **Company Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type(s) of company you represent. For example, if you are a consultant, you may choose "Consulting Firm" and "Software Company".
* **Project Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the project you are working on.
* **Project Details** (Text, Optional): Enter the details of the project, such as the objectives and timeline.
