# Eviction Notice Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help landlords or property managers formally notify tenants of their intention to evict them from the rented property.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the tenant being notified of the eviction.
2. Enter the address of the property that the tenant is being evicted from.
3. Describe the reason for the eviction.
4. Provide any relevant information about the tenant's rights as stated below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Tenant Name
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tenant being notified of the eviction.

### Tenant Occupying Address
* **Tenant Occupying Address** (`tenant_occupying_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property that the tenant is being evicted from.

### Property Address
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property that the tenant is being evicted from.

### Reason for Eviction
* **Reason for Eviction** (`reason_for_eviction`, text, optional): Describe the reason for the eviction, if applicable.

### Tenant Rights
* **Tenant Rights** (`tenant_rights`, text, optional): Provide any relevant information about the tenant's rights, if applicable.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely.
* Please do not use this form for purposes other than for formal eviction notice.
* Please seek professional legal advice before using this form.
* Please keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and not for official legal purposes.
