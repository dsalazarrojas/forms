# Estate Planning Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to aid in the process of estate planning by providing a structured checklist for individuals to document their estate planning needs. The form helps to identify and record important information related to wills, trusts, beneficiaries, powers of attorney, advance directives, guardianship, and other key aspects of estate planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most applicable option that best describes your situation.
3. Provide detailed information for text fields, such as Beneficiaries and Beneficiary Information.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wills** (id: 2, required: true): Select the type of will you have or need:
	+ Trust: A will that holds assets in a trust.
	+ Living Will: A document that outlines medical treatment and care instructions in the event of an incapacitating illness.
	+ Joint Will: A will that is jointly owned and controlled by two or more individuals.
	+ Last Will: A traditional will that outlines the distribution of assets after death.
* **Trusts** (id: 3, required: true): Select all applicable types of trusts you have or need:
	+ Revocable Trust: A trust that can be easily revoked or modified.
	+ Irrevocable Trust: A trust that cannot be revoked or modified.
	+ Trust A: A specific type of trust (type A).
	+ Trust B: A specific type of trust (type B).
* **Beneficiaries** (id: 4, required: false): List your beneficiaries, including their names, addresses, and relationships.
* **Beneficiary Information** (id: 5, required: false): Enter detailed information about each beneficiary, including their date of birth, marital status, and any other relevant details.
* **Power of Attorney** (id: 6, required: true): Select the type of power of attorney you have or need:
	+ General Power of Attorney: A document that grants broad power to act on an individual's behalf.
	+ Durable Power of Attorney: A document that remains in effect even if the individual becomes incapacitated.
	+ Limited Power of Attorney: A document that grants specific powers to act on an individual's behalf.
* **Advance Directives** (id: 7, required: true): Select the type of advance directive you have or need:
	+ Health Care Directive: A document that outlines medical treatment and care instructions.
	+ Living Will Directive: A document that outlines treatment and care instructions in the event of an incapacitating illness.
	+ Do Not Resuscitate (DNR) Directive: A document that prohibits resuscitation or artificial life support.
* **Guardianship and Custody** (id: 8, required: true): Select the type of guardianship and custody arrangement you have or need:
	+ Guardianship: A legal arrangement that grants one person to make decisions on behalf of another.
	+ Custody: A legal arrangement that grants one person temporary or permanent care and control over another person's child.
* **Estate Planning Checklist Notes** (id: 9 and 10, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments related to your estate planning, including any specific arrangements or instructions.
