# Divorce Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals seeking legal separation or divorce from their spouse by providing a structured and comprehensive guide for documenting their situation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the complainant (the person initiating the separation).
2. Enter your spouse's name.
3. Provide a brief reason for the divorce.
4. Specify the grounds for the divorce.
5. Enter the date of your marriage.
6. Enter the date of your separation.
7. If applicable, provide additional details about the divorce you are complaining about.
8. Enter the location where the court hearing will take place.
9. Select the county court where you will be filing the complaint.
10. Enter your state of residence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Name** (`complainant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as the person initiating the separation.
* **Spouse Name** (`spouse_name`, Text, Required): Enter your spouse's name.
* **Reason for Divorce** (`reason_for_divorce`, Text, Optional): Provide a brief reason for the divorce.
* **Grounds for Divorce** (`grounds_for_divorce`, Text, Optional): Specify the grounds for the divorce.
* **Date of Marriage** (`date_of_marriage`, Date, Required): Enter the date of your marriage.
* **Date of Separation** (`date_of_separation`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of your separation.
* **Divorce Complained Of** (`divorce_complained_of`, Text, Optional): If applicable, provide additional details about the divorce you are complaining about.
* **Court Location** (`court_location`, Text, Required): Enter the location where the court hearing will take place.
* **County Court** (`county_court`, Text, Required): Select the county court where you will be filing the complaint.
* **State** (`state`, Text, Required): Enter your state of residence.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used for official court purposes. It is also recommended that you consult with a licensed attorney for personalized guidance and representation.
