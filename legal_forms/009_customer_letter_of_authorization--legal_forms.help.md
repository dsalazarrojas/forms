# Customer Letter Of Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Letter Of Authorization form is a document that serves as a formal agreement between a customer and a third-party service provider. This form is used to authorize the customer to act on behalf of the company in specific business transactions or activities. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the customer has the necessary authority to engage in business dealings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the customer's name in the "customer_name" field.
2.  Provide the customer's email address in the "customer_email" field. This is the email address where any communication will be sent to.
3.  Enter the customer's company information in the "company" field.
4.  Fill in the customer's address in the "address" field.
5.  Enter the customer's phone number in the "phone" field.
6.  Fill in the authorization date in the "date" field in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7.  Enter the authorization time in the "time" field in the correct format (HH:MM AM/PM).
8.  Finally, sign your name in the "signature" field to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the customer who will be acting on behalf of the company. This is the name of the person who will be making business decisions.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the customer. This is where any communication or updates will be sent to.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the company on behalf of which the customer is acting.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the customer or company.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date on which the authorization is being made. Use the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time on which the authorization is being made. Use the HH:MM AM/PM format.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required: false): Sign your name in this field to complete and finalize the form.
