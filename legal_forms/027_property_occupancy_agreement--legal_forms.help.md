# Property Occupancy Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Property Occupancy Agreement is a document used to establish the terms of a rental agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form outlines the details of the property occupation, including the move-in and move-out dates, lease duration, rent amount, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the tenant and landlord names in the respective fields.
2. Enter the move-in and move-out dates in the specified format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Specify the lease duration in months.
4. Enter the rent amount.
5. Choose the rent frequency (e.g., monthly, weekly, quarterly).
6. Select the payment method (e.g., bank transfer, cash).
7. Enter the property address and unit number (if applicable).
8. Sign and date the form as the landlord and tenant.
9. If a witness is present, include their signature and name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the tenant occupying the property.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the landlord owning the property.
* **Move-in Date** (`move_in_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the tenant moves into the property (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Move-out Date** (`move_out_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date the tenant moves out of the property (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, Number, Required): Specify the length of the lease in months.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, Number, Required): Enter the rent amount.
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, Select One, Required): Choose how often rent is paid (e.g., monthly, weekly, quarterly).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Choose the method of payment (e.g., bank transfer, cash).
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, Text, Required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Unit Number** (`unit_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the unit number of the property (if applicable).
* **Move-in Condition** (`move_in_condition`, Text, Optional): Describe the condition of the property when the tenant moved in.
* **Move-out Condition** (`move_out_condition`, Text, Optional): Describe the condition of the property when the tenant moved out.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the property.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, Note, Required): Sign and date the form as the landlord.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, Note, Required): Sign and date the form as the tenant.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, Note, Optional): If a witness is present, sign and date the form as the witness.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the witness.
* **Landlord Company** (`landlord_company`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the landlord's company.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the landlord's phone number.
* **Tenant Phone** (`tenant_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the tenant's phone number.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, Select One, Required): Choose the type of property (e.g., residential, commercial, other).
* **Rent Start** (`rent_start`, Select One, Required): Choose when rent is due (e.g., first of the month, last of the month).
* **Rent End** (`rent_end`, Select One, Required): Choose when rent payment ends (e.g., last of the month, first of the month).
* **Rent Frequency Unit** (`rent_frequency_unit`, Text, Optional): Enter the unit of rent (e.g., days, weeks).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, Text, Optional): Add any other agreements or terms to the contract.
* **Witness Signature** is not required for tenant or landlord to sign, but it's a legal requirement for witness to sign in case of disputes. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the dates and amounts for accuracy.
* Ensure the landlord and tenant sign the form as required.
* If a witness is present, have them sign and date the form as well.
* Keep a copy of the form for future reference.
