# Family Court Modification Response Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather responses from parties involved in a family court case modification. It helps to ensure that all relevant information is collected and recorded.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to provide your response to the court's modification request.
2. Answer the questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. If you have any relevant documentation or evidence to support your response, upload it using the designated upload field.
4. Enter your court case number to help the court track your case.
5. Provide the date and time of your court hearing for the court's reference.
6. If you are represented by a lawyer, provide their name, phone number, email, and address.
7. You may include any additional notes or comments in the text field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Court Modification Response**:
	+ This field is where you can provide your response to the court's modification request.
	+ Please answer this question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
* **Agree/Disagree**:
	+ Select "agree" if you are in agreement with the proposed modification.
	+ Select "disagree" if you are not in agreement with the proposed modification.
	+ Select "no answer" if you are unable to provide a response.
* **Reason for Response**:
	+ Provide any reason or explanation for your response.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps the court understand your position.
* **Upload Documents**:
	+ Upload any relevant documentation or evidence that supports your response.
	+ This field is optional, but it helps to strengthen your argument.
* **Case Management**:
	+ Provide any additional information about your case management, if applicable.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Case Number**:
	+ Enter the court case number for your case.
	+ This helps the court track your case.
* **Court Date and Time**:
	+ Enter the date and time of your court hearing.
	+ This helps the court schedule and prepare for your hearing.
* **Lawyer Information**:
	+ If you are represented by a lawyer, provide their name, phone number, email, and address.
	+ This helps the court contact your lawyer for further communication.
* **Lawyer Notes**:
	+ Provide any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with your lawyer.
	+ This field is optional.
