# Bankruptcy Assistance Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bankruptcy Assistance Survey is designed to gather information about your current financial situation to provide guidance on navigating the bankruptcy process. Please complete this form to ensure you receive accurate and relevant assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Fill in your answers in the corresponding fields.
3. Select all relevant options that apply to your situation.
4. Provide any additional information you think is necessary to help us understand your financial circumstances.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email Address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`respondent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Filing Status** (`filing_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current bankruptcy situation from the options: Considering Filing, Recently Filed, Currently in Bankruptcy, Already Discharged, or Exploring Options.
* **Type of Bankruptcy** (`bankruptcy_type`, `select_one`, optional): If applicable, select the type of bankruptcy you are considering, such as Chapter 7 or Chapter 13.
* **Household Status** (`household_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your household status from the options: Individual, Married Filing Jointly, Married Filing Separately, or Head of Household.
* **Number of Household Members** (`household_members`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of dependents in your household.
* **Current Employment Status** (`current_employment`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current employment status from the options: Employed Full-Time, Employed Part-Time, Self-Employed, Unemployed, Retired, or Disabled.
* **Approximate Monthly Gross Income** (`monthly_income`, `number`, required): Enter your monthly income before taxes and deductions.
* **Sources of Income** (`income_sources`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all sources of income that apply to you, such as Wages or Salary, Self-Employment, Social Security, or Other.
* **Approximate Total Debt** (`total_debt`, `number`, required): Enter your total debt amount.
* **Types of Debt** (`debt_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all types of debt that apply, such as Credit Card Debt, Medical Debt, Student Loans, or Other.
* **Primary Assets** (`primary_assets`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all assets that are primary to your financial situation, such as Home, Vehicle, or Savings Account.
* **Home Ownership Status** (`home_ownership`, `select_one`, required): Choose your home ownership status from the options: Own Outright, Have Mortgage, Renting, or Other.
* **Estimated Home Value** (`home_value`, `number`, optional): Enter the estimated current market value of your home if applicable.
* **Remaining Mortgage Balance** (`mortgage_balance`, `number`, optional): Enter the remaining mortgage balance if applicable.
* **What Is Your Primary Financial Hardship?** (`financial_hardship`, `select_one`, required): Choose the main issue causing your financial hardship from the options: Job Loss or Reduced Income, Medical Emergency, Divorce or Separation, Natural Disaster, Excessive Debt, or Other.
* **When Did the Financial Hardship Begin?** (`hardship_timeline`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the financial hardship began.
* **Previous Bankruptcy Filing** (`previous_bankruptcy`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you have filed bankruptcy before from the options: Yes or No.
* **Previous Bankruptcy Discharge Date** (`bankruptcy_discharge_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your previous bankruptcy discharge if applicable.
* **Have You Completed Credit Counseling?** (`credit_counseling`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you have completed credit counseling from the options: Yes, No, or In Progress.
* **What Assistance Are You Seeking?** (`assistance_sought`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all assistance you are seeking, such as Legal Representation, Financial Counseling, or Debt Negotiation.
* **Any Additional Financial Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any other information you think is relevant to your financial situation.
