# Business License Dispute Resolution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report business license disputes, allowing you to formally document and address issues related to license fees, rejections, renewals, and compliance. This form helps you communicate your concerns and seek resolution from the licensing board.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before submitting.
2.  Fill out all required fields.
3.  Answer each question accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name**: This field is required. Please enter the name of the business with a dispute.
*   **License Number**: This field is required. Enter the relevant license number associated with the dispute.
*   **Dispute Type**: This field is required. Select the primary nature of the issue from the options provided:
    *   Fee Dispute / Incorrect Billing
    *   Rejection Appeal (New or Renewal)
    *   Significant Renewal Delay
    *   Compliance / Violation Notice Appeal
    *   Technical Error on License Document
*   **Summary of Dispute**: This field is required. Clearly state the problem and relevant facts of the dispute.
*   **Date of Incident or Notice**: This field is required. Enter the date when the issue first occurred.
*   **Desired Resolution Outcome**: This field is required. Enter the desired outcome or resolution you seek from the licensing board.
*   **Evidence and Supporting Materials**: This field is required. Select if you have evidence or materials supporting your claim:
    *   Yes, will upload separately
    *   Attached to this submission
    *   Available upon request
*   **Primary Contact for Dispute**: This field is required. Enter the name of the primary contact person handling the dispute.
*   **Contact Phone Number**: This field is required. Enter the contact number of the primary contact person.
*   **Digital Signature**: This field is required. Type your full name to authorize the filing.
*   **Filing Date**: This field is required. Enter the date you are submitting this form.

Note to users: This form is for reporting business license disputes. Please be as accurate and detailed as possible to ensure the best possible resolution.
