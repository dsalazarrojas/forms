# intellectual_property_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Intellectual Property Release Form" is used to gather information about intellectual property release, which is typically required for official or legal purposes. This form helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the "General Information" field (Field 1) and enter any relevant information about the intellectual property release. This field is a text area where you can provide general details.
2. Move to the "Intellectual Property Licence" field (Field 2) and select either "Licence" or "Other" from the dropdown list. This field determines the type of intellectual property license.
3. If you selected "Licence" in Field 2, move to the "Licence Duration" field (Field 3) and enter the duration of the license in numerical format (e.g., days, weeks, months, etc.). If you selected "Other", this field will be skipped.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Field 1)
• **General Information**: A text field for providing general information about the intellectual property release. Enter any relevant details you have about the release.

### Intellectual Property Licence (Field 2)
• **Intellectual Property Licence**: A select-one field with two options: "Licence" and "Other". Select "Licence" if you are releasing intellectual property under a license, or "Other" if the release is not covered by a standard licence.

### Licence Duration (Field 3)
• **Licence Duration**: A number field for specifying the duration of the license. If you selected "Licence" in Field 2, enter the duration of the license in numerical format (e.g., days, weeks, months, etc.). This field will be skipped if you selected "Other" in Field 2.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and clear information in the "General Information" field.
* Ensure you select the correct option in the "Intellectual Property Licence" field to ensure the correct license duration is captured.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities or department for guidance.
