# Supersedeas Bond Motion Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supersedeas Bond Motion Form is used to request a supersedeas bond in a case, which is a motion made by a party to set aside or suspend a court order or judgment. The form is typically used in bankruptcy and civil cases. This form requires information about the case, the motion, and the party making the motion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the relevant information for the case, motion, and party making the motion.
2. Select the correct case type and bond filing fee from the options provided.
3. Fill in the motion type and cause.
4. Fill in the bond filing date and time, as well as the bond hearing date and time.
5. Select the bond hearing status and location.
6. Enter the opposing party's name, phone number, and email (if available).
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Motion Request** (`motion_request`, `text`, optional): This field is where you describe the request being made in the motion.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, `number`, optional): This field is not typically used in this form.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, `number`, optional): This field is used to identify the case number associated with the motion.
* **Case Type** (`case_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of case (e.g., bankruptcy, civil, etc.).
* **Bond Amount** (`bond_amount`, `number`, optional): This field is not typically used in this form.
* **Motion Filing Date** (`motion_filing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date on which the motion was filed.
* **Motion Filing Time** (`motion_filing_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time at which the motion was filed.
* **Motion Hearing Date** (`motion_hearing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the motion hearing.
* **Motion Hearing Time** (`motion_hearing_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the motion hearing.
* **Motion Hearing Location** (`motion_hearing_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the motion hearing.
* **Motion Hearing Status** (`motion_hearing_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the motion hearing (e.g., active, dismissed, etc.).
* **Opposing Party Name** (`opposing_party_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Phone** (`opposing_party_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Email** (`opposing_party_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the opposing party (if available).
* **Motion Filing Fee** (`motion_filing_fee`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of fee associated with the motion filing (e.g., no fee, fixed fee, etc.).
* **Attorney Fee** (`attorney_fee`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of fee associated with the attorney (e.g., hourly, retainer, etc.).
* **Motion Type** (`motion_type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of motion being made (e.g., motion for a new trial, motion to dismiss, etc.).
* **Motion Cause** (`motion_cause`, `text`, optional): Enter the cause or reason for the motion.
* **Bond Filing Date** (`bond_filing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date on which the bond was filed.
* **Bond Filing Time** (`bond_filing_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time at which the bond was filed.
* **Bond Hearing Date** (`bond_hearing_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the bond hearing.
* **Bond Hearing Time** (`bond_hearing_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the bond hearing.
* **Bond Hearing Location** (`bond_hearing_location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the bond hearing.
* **Bond Hearing Status** (`bond_hearing_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the bond hearing (e.g., active, dismissed, etc.).
* **Review Status** (`review_status`, `text`, optional): This field is not typically used in this form.
