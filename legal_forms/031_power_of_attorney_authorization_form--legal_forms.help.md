# Power Of Attorney Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to grant authorization to someone (the attorney) to act on behalf of another person (the principal). This form is typically used in legal contexts where the principal wants to delegate power to someone else to manage certain aspects of their life or business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter the principal's information**: This includes filling out the "Power Of Attorney Form" field with the principal's name and any other relevant details.
2. **Enter the principal's date of birth**: This will help verify the principal's identity.
3. **Enter the name and contact information of the attorney**: This will grant the attorney the necessary authority to act on the principal's behalf.
4. **Enter the principal's address and date of signature**: This will serve as a reference point for any actions taken by the attorney.
5. **Sign and date the form**: Both the principal and the attorney must sign and date the form to confirm their agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Power Of Attorney Form**: This field is where you enter the name and any other relevant details of the principal.
* **Name Of Principal**: Enter the name of the person granting power of attorney.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the principal's date of birth to verify their identity.
* **Name Of Attorney**: Enter the name of the person being granted power of attorney.
* **Address Of Principal**: Enter the address of the principal.
* **Address Of Attorney**: Enter the address of the attorney.
* **Date Of Signature**: Enter the date of signature for both the principal and the attorney.
* **Attorney Signature**: This field is where the attorney signs to confirm their agreement.
* **Principal Signature**: This field is where the principal signs to confirm their agreement.
* **Note**: Make sure both the principal and the attorney sign and date the form to confirm their agreement.
