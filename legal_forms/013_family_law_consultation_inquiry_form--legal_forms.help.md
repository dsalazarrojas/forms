# Family Law Consultation Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Law Consultation Inquiry Form is a tool used by individuals to gather information related to legal consultation on family law matters. This form helps our team understand your specific needs and provides a foundation for our legal experts to offer informed guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by selecting the relevant contact details that best describe you or your family member(s) seeking legal advice.
2.  Provide a clear description of the legal issue you are facing. This will help our legal experts understand the nature of your concern.
3.  Choose the type of consultation you require (in-person, phone, or email).
4.  Fill in the date and time you are available for consultation.
5.  Provide any additional information you think is relevant to your case.
6.  Enter your email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
7.  If applicable, upload any supporting documents.
8.  Finally, review your information carefully to ensure it is accurate and complete before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details** (Contact Details): This field lets you describe yourself or your family member(s) in a few words, helping us understand who is seeking consultation.
*   **Legal Issue** (Legal Issue): Here, you should briefly describe the legal issue you are facing. This will help our legal experts understand the context and provide the most relevant advice.
*   **Consultation Request** (Consultation Request): Choose the type of consultation that suits you best: in-person, phone, or email.
*   **Date and Time** (Date and Time): Enter a date and time that you are available for the consultation.
*   **Additional Information** (Additional Information): If there's anything else you'd like to share about your case, this is the place to do so.
*   **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you for further discussion.
*   **File Upload** (File Upload): If you have any supporting documents, you can upload them here to help us better understand your case.
*   **Phone Number** (Phone Number): This is your contact number, and we'll use it to reach you if needed.
*   **Supporting Document** (File Upload): Attach any relevant documents to support your claim or issue.

Note: The form fields and labels have been kept simple and clear to ensure ease of use. Each field has been explained in a way that makes it easy to understand its purpose and requirement status.
