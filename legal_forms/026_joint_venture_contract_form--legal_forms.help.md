# Joint Venture Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Venture Contract Form is a tool used to collect and document the key terms and details of a joint venture. This form ensures that all relevant information is captured accurately and consistently to facilitate effective joint venture operations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Select the correct options for each field based on your joint venture details.
* Fill in the text fields with the relevant information.
* Choose the correct dates for the contract duration and start and end dates.
* Ensure that the contract status is accurately reflected.
* Fill in the payment terms and frequency details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Joint Venture Details**: A text field to capture the details of the joint venture.
* **Joint Venture Members**: A select_multiple field to indicate whether there are joint venture members.
* **Contract Terms**: A text field to document the contract terms.
* **Contract Duration**: A date field to specify the duration of the contract.
* **Contract Start Date**: A date field to specify the start date of the contract.
* **Contract End Date**: A date field to specify the end date of the contract.
* **Contract Status**: A select_multiple field to indicate the status of the contract (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes**: A note field to capture any additional notes.
* **Joint Venture Parties**: A select_one field to indicate whether there are joint venture parties.
* **Other Joint Venture Party**: A text field to capture any other joint venture party details if necessary.
* **Payment Frequency**: A text field to specify the payment frequency.
* **Payment Terms Frequency**: A select_multiple field to indicate whether payment terms frequency applies.
* **Payment Amount Frequency**: A text field to specify the payment amount frequency.
* **Payment Terms Amount Frequency**: A select_multiple field to indicate whether payment terms amount frequency applies.
* **Contract Type**: A select_one field to specify the type of contract.
* **Other Contract Type**: A text field to capture any other contract type details if necessary.
* **Tax ID**: A text field to capture the tax ID number.
* **Number**: A text field to capture the tax ID number.
* **Tax ID Expiration**: A date field to specify the tax ID expiration.
* **Tax ID Status**: A select_multiple field to indicate the status of the tax ID (Active or Inactive).
* **Joint Venture Agreement Terms**: A text field to document any joint venture agreement terms.
* **Joint Venture Dispute Terms**: A text field to document any joint venture dispute terms.
* **Notes 2**: A note field to capture any additional notes.
