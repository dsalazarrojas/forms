# Affidavit Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create an affidavit, a sworn statement that provides evidence or support for a particular claim or fact. The affidavit submission form is a standardized template designed to collect all necessary information for a formal affidavit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the submitter's details: Full Name, Residential Address, Contact Number, and Email Address.
2. If applicable, enter the Case / Reference Number and specify the Court / Jurisdiction.
3. Select the Type of Affidavit that best describes the purpose of the affidavit.
4. Enter the full text of your Sworn Statement.
5. If necessary, upload supporting documents via the Supporting Documents Link.
6. Specify the Notary Public Name and Commission Expiry Date (if already notarized).
7. Finally, take an Oath or Affirm that the contents of this affidavit are true and correct, and sign it with the Date Signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (Affiant)** (`full_name`): Enter the full name of the person making the statement.
* **Residential Address** (`address`): Enter the address of the person making the statement.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`): Enter a contact number for the person making the statement.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter the email address of the person making the statement.
* **Case / Reference Number** (`case_number`): If applicable, enter a case or reference number related to this affidavit.
* **Court / Jurisdiction** (`court_name`): If applicable, specify the court or jurisdiction where this affidavit is being filed.
* **Type of Affidavit** (`affidavit_type`): Select the type of affidavit that best describes the purpose of the document.
* **Sworn Statement** (`statement_text`): Enter the full text of your sworn statement.
* **Supporting Documents Link** (`file_upload_link`): If necessary, upload scans of supporting documents via a secure link.
* **Notary Public Name** (`notary_name`): If already notarized, enter the name of the notary public who notarized the document.
* **Commission Expiry Date** (`commission_expiry`): If already notarized, enter the commission expiry date of the notary public's commission.
* **Oath** (`oath_affirmation`): Select to take an Oath or Affirm that the contents of this affidavit are true and correct.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`): Sign the affidavit with the date of signing.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
- Ensure that the notary public information is accurate and up-to-date (if applicable).
- Review and edit your sworn statement carefully before finalizing the affidavit.
- Upload supporting documents securely if necessary.
- Sign the affidavit with the correct date.
