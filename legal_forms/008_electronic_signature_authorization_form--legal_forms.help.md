# Electronic Signature Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Electronic Signature Authorization Form is used to authorize electronic signatures for a set of documents. This form collects information about the person authorizing the signature, including their name, the name of the signatory, the method of signature, and the documents to be signed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name of person authorizing" field.
2. Enter the name of the person signing the documents in the "name of signatory" field.
3. Select the signature method from the "signature method" dropdown menu (Electronic Signature, Digital Signature, or Physical Signature).
4. Select the documents to be signed from the "documents to sign" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date and time of signing, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of person authorizing** (`name_of_person_authorizing`, text, required): Enter your full name as the person authorizing this signature.
* **Name of signatory** (`name_of_signatory`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person signing the documents.
* **Signature method** (`signature_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of signature (Electronic Signature, Digital Signature, or Physical Signature).
	+ This field determines the type of signature that will be accepted for the documents.
* **Documents to sign** (`documents_list`, select_multiple, required): Select the documents that will be signed using the authorized signature method.
* **Document IDs** (`document_ids`, number, required): Enter a unique ID for each document selected.
* **Date signed** (`date_signed`, date, optional): Enter the date of signing, if applicable.
* **Time signed** (`time_signed`, time, optional): Enter the time of signing, if applicable.
* **Electronic Signature URL** (`electronic_signature_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL to access the electronic signature.
* **Signature image** (`signature_image`, note, optional): This field is used to store a note about the signature image.
* **Signature image URL** (`signature_image_url`, text, optional): Enter a URL to access the signature image.
* **Electronic signature** (`electronic_signature`, select_one, required): Select whether the signature is electronic (true) or not (false).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about the signature.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of signature.
* **Signature time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of signature.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that the signature is properly authorized and recorded.
* Ensure that the signature method and documents selected match the actual signed documents.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please contact the relevant department or authority.
