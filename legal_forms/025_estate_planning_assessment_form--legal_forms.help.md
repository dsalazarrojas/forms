# Estate Planning Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Estate Planning Assessment Form is designed to collect vital information related to estate planning, including general information about the individual or organization, wills and trusts, asset distribution, and contact details. This information helps in creating a comprehensive understanding of the estate planning needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose before proceeding.
2. Fill in the General Information fields, providing accurate and complete details about yourself or your organization.
3. Select the relevant options for Wills And Trusts, Asset Distribution, and Asset Preferences sections.
4. Enter the required numerical values for Asset Value and other numerical fields.
5. Provide detailed contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: Enter a brief description of yourself or your organization, including any relevant background information.
* **Wills And Trusts (2)**: Select the types of wills and trusts that apply to you or your organization.
* **Asset Distribution (3)**: Select the distribution preferences for your assets.
* **Estate Preferences (4)**: Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your estate preferences.
* **Contact Information (5)**: Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone Numbers (6)**: Enter any phone numbers where you can be reached.
* **Notes (7)**: Enter any general notes or comments.
* **Asset Value (8)**: Enter the numerical value of your assets.
* **Asset Type (9)**: Select the type of asset you possess.
* **Asset Location (10)**: Enter the location of your asset.
* **Asset Security (11)**: Select the level of security for your asset.
* **Asset Frequency (12)**: Select the frequency of your asset use.
* **Asset Duration (13)**: Select the duration of your asset possession.
* **Asset Frequency Type (14)**: Select the type of frequency for your asset use.
* **Asset Location Type (15)**: Select the type of location for your asset.
* **Asset Value Type (16)**: Select the type of value for your asset.
* **Asset Value Frequency (17)**: Select the frequency of your asset value.
* **Asset Value Location (18)**: Enter the location of your asset value.
* **Contact Person (19)**: Enter the name and title of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Relationship (20)**: Enter the relationship of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Address (21)**: Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Phone (22)**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email (23)**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Estate Notes (24)**: Enter any additional notes or comments regarding your estate.
* **Asset Notes (25)**: Enter any general notes or comments regarding your assets.

Note: Please review each field carefully and ensure you provide accurate and complete information to get the most out of this form. If you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
