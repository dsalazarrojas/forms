# Consumer Rights Grant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consumer Rights Grant Application Form is designed to gather essential information about your organization and project in order to assess your eligibility for grant funding. The form will help us understand your organization's needs, goals, and timelines, ensuring that we can provide the right support and resources for your project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your organization's name and type.
2. Fill in your tax ID number.
3. Enter your organization's address, city, state, and ZIP code.
4. Complete the contact information for the primary contact person.
5. Describe your consumer rights project, including its title, description, focus area, target population, expected number of beneficiaries, and project goals.
6. Outline your project's timeline, including the proposed start and end dates.
7. Specify the amount of funding you are requesting.
8. Provide a breakdown of how you plan to allocate the funds.
9. List any other funding sources you have or plan to use.
10. Describe your organization's capacity to execute the project.
11. Share your previous grant experience.
12. Outline how you will measure the success of your project.
13. Explain how the project will continue after the grant period.
14. List any collaborating organizations.
15. Provide any additional relevant information.
