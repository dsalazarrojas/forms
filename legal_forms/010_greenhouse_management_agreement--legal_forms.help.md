# Greenhouse Management Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Greenhouse Management Agreement form is used to establish a management agreement between the greenhouse owner and a manager/lessee. It ensures that both parties have a clear understanding of their roles and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Greenhouse Owner Information section with the owner's details.
2. Specify the location of the greenhouse in the Greenhouse Location field.
3. Enter the agreement dates in the Agreement Dates field.
4. Provide payment information in the Payment Information field.
5. Add any additional notes in the Additional Notes field.
6. Enter the contact information in the Contact Information field.
7. If applicable, enter the agreement end date in the Agreement End Date field.
8. Specify the rent amount in the Rent Amount field.
9. Select the payment method in the Payment Method field.
10. Choose the payment frequency in the Payment Frequency field.
11. Enter the total rent in the Total Rent field.
12. Add any additional notes in the Notes2 field.
13. Enter the agreement date in the Agreement Date field.
14. If applicable, enter the agreement end date in the Agreement End Date2 field.
15. Add more additional notes in the More Additional Notes field.
16. Select the payment method in the Payment2 field.
17. Choose the payment frequency in the Payment Frequency2 field.
18. Enter the total rent in the Total Rent2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Greenhouse Owner Information** (`greenhouse_owner_info`, `text`, required): Enter the details of the greenhouse owner.
* **Greenhouse Location** (`greenhouse_location`, `text`, required): Specify the location of the greenhouse.
* **Agreement Dates** (`agreement_dates`, `text`, required): Enter the dates of the agreement.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, `text`, required): Enter the payment details.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter the contact information.
* **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the agreement end date.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required): Specify the rent amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the payment frequency.
* **Total Rent** (`total_rent`, `number`, required): Enter the total rent.
* **Notes2** (`notes2`, `note`, required): Add any additional notes.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the agreement date.
* **Agreement End Date2** (`agreement_end_date2`, `date`, required): Enter the agreement end date.
* **More Additional Notes** (`notes3`, `note`, required): Add more additional notes.
* **Payment2** (`payment2`, `select_one`, required): Select the payment method.
* **Payment Frequency2** (`payment_frequency2`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the payment frequency.
* **Total Rent2** (`total_rent2`, `number`, required): Enter the total rent.
* **Agreement End Date2** (`agreement_end_date2`, `date`, required): Enter the agreement end date.
