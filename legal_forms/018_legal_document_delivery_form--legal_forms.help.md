# Legal Document Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information about the delivery of legal documents to clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Enter the date and time of delivery in the "Delivery Date" and "Delivery Time" fields.
3. Select the type of document being delivered from the "Document Type" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the document in the "Document Description" field.
5. Check the status of the document as "Pending", "Delivered", or "Draft" in the "Document Status" field.
6. Enter any additional details about the document in the "Document Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the client being served.
- **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of delivery.
- **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of delivery.
- **Document Type** (`document_type`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more types of documents being delivered.
- **Document Description** (`document_description`, `text`, required/optional): Describe the document being delivered.
- **Document Date** (`document_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the document.
- **Document Time** (`document_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time of the document.
- **Document Status** (`document_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the document as "Pending", "Delivered", or "Draft".
- **Document Details** (`document_details`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the document.
