# Company Winding Up Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to gather information for a Company Winding Up Petition, a legal process to liquidate a company's assets and pay off debts. Fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your personal details as the Petitioner.
2. Provide the company's information being petitioned.
3. Describe the nature of the debt and its origin.
4. List any supporting documents or previous legal actions taken.
5. Confirm your representation and provide your legal counsel's details if applicable.
6. Sign and date the form to declare the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioner Full Name**: Enter your full legal name as the person filing the petition.
* **Petitioner Type**: Select the type of entity filing the petition (Individual, Corporation, Partnership, etc.).
* **Petitioner Address**: Enter your full mailing address.
* **Petitioner Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Petitioner Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Name**: Enter the legal name of the company being petitioned.
* **Company Registration Number**: Enter the official company registration number.
* **Company Registered Address**: Enter the official registered address of the company.
* **Debt Amount**: Enter the total amount of debt owed.
* **Currency**: Select the currency of the debt amount.
* **Debt Origin**: Select the origin of the debt (Unpaid invoice for goods, Unpaid invoice for services, Loan agreement, etc.).
* **Debt Details**: Describe the circumstances surrounding the debt.
* **Debt Due Date**: Enter the original due date of the debt.
* **Was demand letter sent**: Check if a formal demand for payment was sent to the company.
* **Demand Letter Date**: Enter the date the demand letter was sent (if applicable).
* **Company Response to Demand**: Describe how the company responded to the demand (if at all).
* **Supporting Documents Available**: Select if you have supporting documents (invoiced, contracts, court judgments, etc.).
* **List of Documents**: Describe the documents you will submit.
* **Previous Legal Action Taken**: Select if you've taken other legal steps (Small claims court, civil court, arbitration, etc.).
* **Legal Action Details**: Describe previous legal actions and their outcomes.
* **Other Creditors Known**: Check if you're aware of other creditors.
* **Company Solvency Status**: Select the company's solvency status (Appears insolvent, Appears solvent, Unknown, etc.).
* **Grounds for Winding Up**: Select the legal basis for winding up the company (Unable to pay debts, Just and equitable, Statutory demand not complied with, etc.).
* **Legal Representation**: Check if you're represented by legal counsel.
* **Attorney Name**: Enter the name of your representing attorney.
* **Attorney Contact**: Enter your attorney's contact information (email or phone).
* **Petitioner Signature**: Sign your full name as the Petitioner.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.
* **Statement of Truth**: Declare that the information provided is accurate.
* **Additional Information**: Add any other relevant information about the case.
