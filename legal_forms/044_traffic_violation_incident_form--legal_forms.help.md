# Traffic Violation Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Traffic Violation Incident Form is a document used to report and document traffic incidents. It collects information about the date and time of the incident, the location, vehicle details, and witness information.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Incident Date**: Enter the date of the traffic incident.
2. **Incident Time**: Enter the time of the traffic incident.

4. **Police Station**: Select the police station that responded to the incident.
5. **Vehicle Violation**: Select all applicable vehicle violations that occurred during the incident.

15. **Driver Age**: Enter the age of the driver involved in the incident.
