# Patent Application Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain authorization from the patent owner for a patent application. It is essential for the completion of the patent application process. Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth processing of the patent application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

* First Name: Enter the patent owner's first name.
* Last Name: Enter the patent owner's last name.
* Email: Enter the patent owner's email address.
* Phone: Enter the patent owner's phone number.
* Street Address: Enter the patent owner's street address.
* City: Enter the patent owner's city.
* State: Enter the patent owner's state.
* Zip Code: Enter the patent owner's zip code.
* Date of Birth: Enter the patent owner's date of birth.
* Authorization Date: Enter the date of authorization.
* Relationship: Describe your relationship with the patent owner.
* Authorization Level: Select the level of authorization from the options provided.
* Authorized Name: Enter the name of the person authorized.
* Representative Name: Enter the name of the representative.
* Representative Address: Enter the representative's address.
* Representative Phone: Enter the representative's phone number.
* Representative Email: Enter the representative's email address.
* Representative Relationship: Describe your relationship with the representative.
* Authorized Signature: Enter the authorized representative's signature.
* Representative Signature: Enter the representative's signature.
* Submit: Select "Submit" to complete the form, or "Cancel" to abort the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (first_name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (last_name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (phone_number, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Street Address** (street_address, text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
- **City** (city, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
- **State** (state, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
- **Zip Code** (zip_code, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
- **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
- **Authorization Date** (authorization_date, date, required/optional): Enter the date of authorization.
- **Relationship** (relationship, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the patent owner.
- **Authorization Level** (authorization_level, select_one, required/optional): Select the level of authorization from the options provided.
- **Authorized Name** (authorized_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the person authorized.
- **Representative Name** (representative_name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the representative.
- **Representative Address** (representative_address, text, required/optional): Enter the representative's address.
- **Representative Phone** (representative_phone, text, required/optional): Enter the representative's phone number.
- **Representative Email** (representative_email, email, required/optional): Enter the representative's email address.
- **Representative Relationship** (representative_relationship, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the representative.
- **Authorized Signature** (authorized_signature, note, required/optional): Enter the authorized representative's signature.
- **Representative Signature** (representative_signature, note, required/optional): Enter the representative's signature.
