# Residential Lease Surety Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Residential Lease Surety Agreement Form is a legal document that outlines the terms of a residential lease. It is used to secure the guarantor's financial responsibility for the rent payments of the landlord's tenant.
## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Landlord Information** section with your personal details, including your address, contact number, and email.
2.  Enter the **Guarantor Information** section with the guarantor's personal details.
3.  Provide the **Guarantor Details** section with any additional information about the guarantor.
4.  Sign the **Guarantor Signature** section to confirm the guarantor's acceptance of the agreement.
5.  Enter the **Date** to record the date of this agreement.
6.  Choose the **Rent Frequency** from the provided options (e.g., every week, every 2 weeks, etc.).
7.  Describe the **Rent Frequency Details** in the given field.
8.  Select the **Lease Terms** from the provided options (e.g., 6 months, 12 months, etc.).
9.  Enter the **Lease Terms Details** in the given field.
10.  Record the **Move In Date** and **Move In Time** for the lease to start.
11.  Record the **Lease End Date** and **Lease End Time** for the lease to end.
12.  Enter the **Rent Amount** for the monthly rent.
13.  Set the **Rent Frequency Start Date** and **Rent Frequency End Date** for the rent frequency.
14.  Record the **Rent Frequency End Time** for the rent frequency.
15.  Enter the **Guarantor Name**, **Guarantor Phone**, and **Guarantor Email** in the given fields.
16.  Enter the **Landlord Phone** and **Landlord Email** in the given fields.
17.  Leave any additional notes in the **Notes** section if necessary.
18.  Enter your **Signature** to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Landlord Information** (Page 1): Enter your name, address, contact number, and email for communication purposes.
*   **Guarantor Information** (Page 1): Enter the guarantor's name, address, contact number, and email.
*   **Guarantor Details** (Page 1): Enter any additional information about the guarantor.
*   **Guarantor Signature** (Page 1): Sign to confirm the guarantor's acceptance of the agreement.
*   **Date** (Page 2): Record the date of this agreement.
*   **Rent Frequency** (Page 2): Choose how often the rent will be paid (e.g., every week, every 2 weeks, etc.).
*   **Rent Frequency Details** (Page 2): Describe the frequency of the rent payments.
*   **Lease Terms** (Page 2): Select the duration of the lease (e.g., 6 months, 12 months, etc.).
*   **Lease Terms Details** (Page 2): Enter any additional details about the lease terms.
*   **Move In Date** (Page 3): Record the date when the lease will start.
*   **Move In Time** (Page 3): Record the time when the lease will start.
*   **Lease End Date** (Page 3): Record the date when the lease will end.
*   **Lease End Time** (Page 3): Record the time when the lease will end.
*   **Rent Amount** (Page 3): Enter the amount of the rent.
*   **Rent Frequency Start Date** (Page 4): Record the date when the rent frequency will start.
*   **Rent Frequency End Date** (Page 4): Record the date when the rent frequency will end.
*   **Rent Frequency End Time** (Page 4): Record the time when the rent frequency will end.
*   **Guarantor Name** (Page 4): Enter the guarantor's name.
*   **Guarantor Phone** (Page 4): Enter the guarantor's phone number.
*   **Guarantor Email** (Page 4): Enter the guarantor's email.
*   **Landlord Phone** (Page 4): Enter the landlord's phone number.
*   **Landlord Email** (Page 4): Enter the landlord's email.
*   **Notes** (Page 5): Leave any additional notes if necessary.
*   **Signature** (Page 5): Sign to confirm the agreement.
