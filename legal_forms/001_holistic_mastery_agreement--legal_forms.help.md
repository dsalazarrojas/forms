# Holistic Mastery Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holistic mastery agreement is a form used to collect contact information and demographic data from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information fields on the first page, which includes name, email, contact number, address, date of birth, time of birth, date of termination, time of termination, date of signature, time of signature, and signature. Make sure to select the correct signature from the dropdown menu.
2. If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them on the second page, which is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Page
* **Name** (`first_page_item`, text): Enter the client's name.
* **Email** (`first_page_item_2`, email): Enter the client's email address.
* **Contact Number** (`first_page_item_3`, text): Enter the client's contact number.
* **Address** (`first_page_item_4`, text): Enter the client's address.
* **Date of Birth** (`first_page_item_5`, date): Enter the client's date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`first_page_item_6`, time): Enter the client's time of birth.
* **Date of Termination** (`first_page_item_7`, date): Enter the date the client's agreement will terminate.
* **Time of Termination** (`first_page_item_8`, time): Enter the time the client's agreement will terminate.
* **Date of Signature** (`first_page_item_9`, date): Enter the date the client signed the agreement.
* **Time of Signature** (`first_page_item_10`, time): Enter the time the client signed the agreement.
* **Signature** (`first_page_item_11`, select_one): Select the signature of the person who signed the agreement from the dropdown menu.
* **First Page Item 12** (`first_page_item_12`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Second Page** (`first_page_item_13`, note): This is the second page of the form, which is optional for additional comments or notes.
* **Second Page (2)** (`second_page`, note): This is the second page of the form, which is also optional for additional comments or notes.
* **Second Page Item** (`second_page_item`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes on this page.
* **Second Page Item 2** (`second_page_item_2`, email): Enter any additional email addresses.
* **Second Page Item 3** (`second_page_item_3`, text): Enter any additional comments or notes on this page.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields on both pages.
* If you need to add additional comments or notes, use the "Second Page" section on both pages.
* Select the correct signature from the dropdown menu on the first page.
