# Receipt of Inheritance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Receipt of Inheritance Form is used to document the receipt of inheritance from a deceased person. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the inheritance process is legally valid and compliant with relevant laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in a quiet and distraction-free environment with all the necessary information at hand.
2. Provide a clear and concise description of the inheritance in the "Inheritance Form Description" field.
3. Enter the name and relationship of the person inheriting in the "Inherited From" and "Inherited Items Received" fields.
4. Provide the value of the items received in the "Inherited Value" field.
5. Record the date and time of the receipt of the inheritance in the "Receipt Date" and "Receipt Time" fields.
6. Enter the name of the person receiving the inheritance in the "Received By" field.
7. Record the value of the items received in the "Received Value" field.
8. Enter the tax reported as part of the inheritance in the "Tax Reported" field.
9. Confirm if the tax reported is identified or not in the "Tax Identified" field.
10. Enter the person who identified the tax in the "Tax Identified By" field.
11. Record the date and time of the tax identification in the "Tax Date" field.
12. Enter the time of the tax identification in the "Tax Time" field.
13. Provide any additional notes about the inheritance in the "Notes" field.
14. (Optional) Enter the email address and phone number of the recipient in the "Email Address" and "Phone Number" fields.
15. (Optional) Add any additional information about the inheritance in the "Additional Information" field.
16. (Optional) Enter the declaration date and time in the "Declaration Date" and "Declaration Time" fields.
17. Enter the name of the person declaring the inheritance in the "Declaration By" field.
18. (Optional) Enter the location of the declaration in the "Declaration Location" field.
19. Check if the declaration was witnessed or not in the "Witnessed By" field.
20. (Optional) Enter the name and relationship of the witness 1 and 2 in the "Witness1 Name" and "Witness1 Relation" fields.
21. (Optional) Enter the name and relationship of the witness 2 in the "Witness2 Name" and "Witness2 Relation" fields.
22. (Optional) Enter the name and relationship of the witness 3 in the "Witness3 Name" and "Witness3 Relation" fields.
23. Enter the name of the person who witnessed the declaration in the "Witnessed By" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inheritance Form Description** (`inheritance_form_description`, `text`, `false`): Describe the inheritance, including the type and nature of the assets or property received.
* **Inheritance From** (`inheritance_from`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of the person or entity that passed away and whose assets or property are being inherited.
* **Inherited From** (`inherited_from`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of the person or entity that passed away and whose assets or property are being inherited. (Duplicate of "Inheritance From")
* **Inherited Items Received** (`inherited_items_received`, `text`, `true`): List the specific items or assets received as part of the inheritance.
* **Inherited Value** (`inherited_value`, `number`, `true`): Enter the value of the items or assets received.
* **Receipt Date** (`receipt_date`, `date`, `true`): Record the date of the receipt of the inheritance.
* **Receipt Time** (`receipt_time`, `time`, `true`): Record the time of the receipt of the inheritance.
* **Received By** (`received_by`, `text`, `true`): Enter the name of the person receiving the inheritance.
* **Received Value** (`received_value`, `number`, `true`): Enter the value of the items or assets received.
* **Tax Reported** (`tax_reported`, `number`, `true`): Enter the tax reported as part of the inheritance.
* **Tax Identified** (`tax_identified`, `select_one`, `true`): Confirm if the tax reported is identified or not.
* **Tax Identified By** (`tax_identified_by`, `text`, `true`): Enter the person who identified the tax.
* **Tax Date** (`tax_date`, `date`, `true`): Record the date of the tax identification.
* **Tax Time** (`tax_time`, `time`, `true`): Record the time of the tax identification.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, `false`): Provide any additional notes about the inheritance.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the email address of the recipient.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the phone number of the recipient.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Add any additional information about the inheritance.
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, `date`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the date of the declaration.
* **Declaration Time** (`declaration_time`, `time`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the time of the declaration.
* **Declaration By** (`declaration_by`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of the person declaring the inheritance.
* **Declaration Location** (`declaration_location`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the location of the declaration.
* **Witnessed By** (`witnessed_by`, `select_one`, `false`): Check if the declaration was witnessed or not.
* **Witness1 Name** (`witness1_name`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the name of the witness 1.
* **Witness1 Relation** (`witness1_relation`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the relation of the witness 1.
* **Witness2 Name** (`witness2_name`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the name of the witness 2.
* **Witness2 Relation** (`witness2_relation`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the relation of the witness 2.
* **Witness3 Name** (`witness3_name`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the name of the witness 3.
* **Witness3 Relation** (`witness3_relation`, `text`, `false`): (Optional) Enter the relation of the witness 3.
* **Witnessed By** (`witnessed_by`, `text`, `false`): Enter the name of the person who witnessed the declaration.
