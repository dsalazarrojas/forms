# Process Server Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Process Server Complaint Form is a tool used to collect and document complaints from servers, allowing for effective processing and resolution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the **Complaint Description** field with a detailed account of the issue or incident.
3. Enter the **Date of Incident** when the complaint was received.
4. Provide your **Complainant's Name** and any relevant contact information.
5. Enter the **Server's Name**, **Server's Address**, **Server's Phone**, and **Server's Email** as relevant.
6. Once you have completed all the required fields, review the form for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complaint Description (complaint_description)**: A detailed account of the issue or incident being reported.
* **Date of Incident (complaint_date)**: The date when the complaint was received.
* **Complainant's Name (complainant_name)**: The name of the person or entity making the complaint.
* **Server's Name (server_name)**: The name of the server or business being complained about.
* **Server's Address (server_address)**: The address of the server or business being complained about.
* **Server's Phone (server_phone)**: The phone number of the server or business being complained about.
* **Server's Email (server_email)**: The email address of the server or business being complained about.
