# Case File Requisition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case File Requisition Form is used to request access to files and records from the legal department. This form helps the legal department understand the requester's identity, reason for the request, and necessary information for efficient processing and delivery.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Enter your name and title in the "Requester name" field.
- In the "Department or firm" field, enter your department or firm's name.
- Enter your contact email address in the "Contact email" field for delivery and follow-up purposes.
- In the "Case reference number" field, provide the internal case or file ID.
- Describe the specific documents, date ranges, or file sections you are requesting in the "Records requested" field.
- Enter the reason for accessing the file in the "Purpose of request" field.
- Select the preferred delivery method: "Secure email", "Physical collection", or "Internal file transfer" in the "Preferred delivery method" field.
- If the records are required by a specific date, enter it in the "Date needed by" field.
- Enter the name of the manager or supervising attorney approving the release in the "Approver name" field.
- Finally, enter your full name to confirm the request in the "Requester electronic signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requester name**: Full name of the person requesting files.
* **Department or firm**: Team or department making the request.
* **Contact email**: Email address for delivery and follow-up purposes.
* **Case reference number**: Internal case or file ID.
* **Records requested**: Specific documents, date ranges, or file sections you are seeking.
* **Purpose of request**: Reason for accessing the file.
* **Preferred delivery method**: Secure email, Physical collection, or Internal file transfer.
* **Date needed by**: Date the records are required by.
* **Approver name**: Manager or supervising attorney approving the release.
* **Requester electronic signature**: Electronic signature confirming the request.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure efficient processing and delivery.
* If you are an external partner, please ensure your company's representative fills out this form.
* If you are an employee, please ensure your manager or supervising attorney reviews and signs this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <insert contact information>.
