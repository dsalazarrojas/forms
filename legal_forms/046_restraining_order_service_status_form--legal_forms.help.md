# Restraining Order Service Status Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Restraining Order Service Status Form is used to track and manage the status of restraining orders. This form helps users to provide essential information about service status, dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the **Service Status** (id: 4) by selecting one of the available options (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
2. Enter the **Date of Service** (id: 3), which is the date the service was provided.
3. If necessary, add **Notes** (id: 5) to provide additional context or comments.
4. Select the **Assigned to** (id: 7) options (Administrative Team, Law Enforcement, or Clerk) to indicate who is responsible for the case.
5. If another option is needed, enter it in the **Assigned to other** (id: 8) field.
6. Choose the **Service Level** (id: 9) by selecting one of the available options (High, Medium, or Low).
7. Enter the **Case Manager Email** (id: 10) and **Case Manager Phone** (id: 11) for the case manager responsible for the case.
8. Add any additional **Case Manager Notes** (id: 12) if necessary.
9. Provide the **Client Name** (id: 13) and **Client Address** (id: 14) for the client.
10. Select whether there are **Service Providers** (id: 15) by choosing one or more options from the list.
11. If another option is needed, enter it in the **Service Providers other** (id: 16) field.
12. Enter the **Service Status Date** (id: 17) and **Service Level Date** (id: 18) if applicable.
13. Enter the **Case Number** (id: 19) for the case.
14. Choose the **Service Level 2** (id: 20) by selecting one of the available options (High, Medium, or Low).
15. Enter the **Date Of Filing 2** (id: 21) and **Date of Disposition Date** (id: 22) if necessary.
16. Add any additional **Notes 2** (id: 23) or **Date Of Disposition 2** (id: 24) if applicable.
17. Enter the **Case Manager Notes 2** (id: 25) if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Restraining Order Service Status** (id: 1, text, required: false): Enter the current service status of the restraining order.
* **Date of Filing** (id: 2, date, required: false): Enter the date the restraining order was filed.
* **Date of Service** (id: 3, date, required: false): Enter the date the service was provided.
* **Service Status** (id: 4, select_one, required: false): Select the current service status (Active, Inactive, or Cancelled).
* **Notes** (id: 5, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the service status.
* **Assigned to** (id: 7, select_multiple, required: true): Select who is responsible for the case (Administrative Team, Law Enforcement, or Clerk).
* **Assigned to other** (id: 8, text, required: false): Enter another option if necessary.
* **Service Level** (id: 9, select_one, required: false): Choose the service level (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Case Manager Email** (id: 10, email, required: false): Enter the email of the case manager responsible for the case.
* **Case Manager Phone** (id: 11, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the case manager.
* **Case Manager Notes** (id: 12, note, required: false): Add any additional notes from the case manager.
* **Client Name** (id: 13, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client.
* **Client Address** (id: 14, text, required: false): Enter the address of the client.
* **Service Providers** (id: 15, select_multiple, required: false): Select whether there are service providers (Yes or No).
* **Service Providers other** (id: 16, text, required: false): Enter another option if necessary.
* **Service Status Date** (id: 17, date, required: false): Enter the date of the service status.
* **Service Level Date** (id: 18, date, required: false): Enter the date of the service level.
* **Case Number** (id: 19, number, required: false): Enter the case number.
* **Service Level 2** (id: 20, select_one, required: false): Choose the service level 2 (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Date Of Filing 2** (id: 21, date, required: false): Enter the date of filing 2.
* **Date of Disposition Date** (id: 22, date, required: false): Enter the date of disposition date.
* **Notes 2** (id: 23, note, required: false): Add any additional notes 2.

* **Case Manager Notes 2** (id: 25, note, required: false): Add any additional case manager notes 2.
