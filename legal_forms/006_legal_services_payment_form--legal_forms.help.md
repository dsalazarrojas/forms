# Legal Services Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Services Payment Form is designed to track payments received from clients for various legal services. This form is intended for internal use within the organization to record client payments, update payment statuses, and maintain accurate financial records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2.  Select the type of legal service provided in the "Service Type" field.
3.  Choose the payment method(s) used for the payment in the "Payment Method" field. You can select multiple methods if applicable.
4.  Enter the amount paid by the client.
5.  If necessary, provide additional notes about the payment or any other relevant information.
6.  Update the payment status as "Paid", "Unpaid", or "Pending" in the "Payment Status" field.
7.  Optionally, enter the client's email address and phone number for easier contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name (client_name)**: Enter the name of the client who made the payment.
    *   This field is optional but recommended for accurate record-keeping.
*   **Service Type (service_type)**: Select the type of legal service provided to the client.
    *   This field helps differentiate between different services for billing and tracking purposes.
*   **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Choose the payment method used by the client, such as Check, Online Payment, or Bank Transfer.
    *   This field indicates the method used to process the payment.
*   **Amount (amount)**: Enter the exact amount paid by the client.
    *   This is a required field to accurately record the payment amount.
*   **Date (date)**: Enter the date the payment was received.
    *   This field helps track the payment date for accounting and record-keeping purposes.
*   **Notes (notes)**: Provide any additional information about the payment or service provided.
    *   This field is optional but can be useful for reference and future communication.
*   **Payment Status (payment_status)**: Update the status of the payment as "Paid", "Unpaid", or "Pending".
    *   This field helps track the payment status for follow-up and billing purposes.
*   **Client Email (client_email)**: Enter the client's email address for easy contact.
    *   This field is optional but can be useful for communication and updates.
*   **Client Phone (client_phone)**: Enter the client's phone number for contact.
    *   This field is optional but helps with communication and follow-up.

## Tips

*   Be sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field to ensure accurate records.
*   If you're unsure about any field, refer to the organization's standard operating procedures for guidance.
