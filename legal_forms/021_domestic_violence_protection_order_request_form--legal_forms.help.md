# Domestic Violence Protection Order Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Domestic Violence Protection Order Request Form is a legal form designed to provide a structured approach for requesting a domestic violence protection order. This order can provide relief from domestic violence or abuse, and it's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your request is considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the relevant fields, including your:
	* **First Name**
	* **Last Name**
	* **Address**
	* **Email**
	* **Phone**
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Describe your situation and the reason for your request in the **Description** field.
4. If applicable, select the relevant options in the **Select 1** field.
5. If necessary, select multiple options in the **Select Multiple 1** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address**: Provide your current address.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Description**: Provide a detailed explanation of your situation, including any relevant dates, times, or details about the alleged abuse.
* **Date of Service**: If known, enter the date when you last experienced the abuse or violence.
* **Time of Service**: If known, enter the time when you last experienced the abuse or violence.
* **Select 1**: Choose the option that best describes your situation.
* **Select Multiple 1**: If your situation involves multiple aspects, select all the relevant options.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, please seek guidance from a relevant authority or legal professional.
* This form is a guide, and it's always best to seek legal advice before proceeding with any legal matters.
