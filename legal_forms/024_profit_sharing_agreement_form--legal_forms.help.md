# Profit Sharing Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record the terms of a profit sharing agreement between partners.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of each partner in the "Partner Name" fields.
2. Enter the percentage each partner will share in the "Share Percentage" fields.
3. Enter the profit value each partner will receive in the "Profit Dollar Value" fields.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the partnership in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
5. Choose the role of each partner from the "Role" fields.
6. Enter any additional partner names, share percentages, profit values, start and end dates, and roles as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required): Enter the name of each partner.
* **Share Percentage** (`share_percentage`, number, required): Enter the percentage each partner will share.
* **Profit Dollar Value** (`profit_dollar_value`, number, required): Enter the profit value each partner will receive.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the partnership.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the partnership.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose the role of each partner from the options: Partner, Co-Owner, or Collaborator.
* **Share Percentage 2**, **Share Percentage 3**, **Share Percentage 4** (`share_percentage_2`, `share_percentage_3`, `share_percentage_4`, number, required): Enter the percentage each additional partner will share.
* **Profit Dollar Value 2**, **Profit Dollar Value 3**, **Profit Dollar Value 4** (`profit_dollar_value_2`, `profit_dollar_value_3`, `profit_dollar_value_4`, number, required): Enter the profit value each additional partner will receive.
* **Start Date 2**, **Start Date 3**, **Start Date 4** (`start_date_2`, `start_date_3`, `start_date_4`, date, required): Enter the start date of each additional partnership.
* **End Date 2**, **End Date 3**, **End Date 4** (`end_date_2`, `end_date_3`, `end_date_4`, date, required): Enter the end date of each additional partnership.
* **Role 2**, **Role 3**, **Role 3** (`role_2`, `role_3`, select_one, required): Choose the role of each additional partner from the options: Partner, Co-Owner, or Collaborator.
* **Partner Name 2**, **Partner Name 3**, **Partner Name 4** (`partner_name_2`, `partner_name_3`, `partner_name_4`, text, required): Enter the name of each additional partner.
* **Sign** (`sign`, note, required): This field is not used in this form.

Note: Some fields may not be displayed if they are not necessary for your partnership.
