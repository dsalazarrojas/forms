# find_a_legal_service_provider - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "find_a_legal_service_provider" form is designed to help users search for legal service providers based on various criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the legal service providers you are interested in by checking the "Legal Service Provider" field.
2. Enter the name of the organization you are searching for in the "Organization Name" field.
3. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Provide a brief description of your search in the "Description" field.
6. Select the service provider you are interested in from the "Service Provider" field.
7. Select the assigned user who will be handling your request from the "Assigned User" field.
8. If necessary, provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Service Provider**: Select the legal service providers you are interested in. This field allows you to filter the search results by multiple service providers.
* **Organization Name**: Enter the name of the organization you are searching for. This field helps narrow down the search results to specific organizations.
* **Contact Email**: Provide your email address for the service provider to contact you.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number for the service provider to contact you.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your search. This field helps service providers understand your needs.
* **Service Provider**: Select the service provider you are interested in.
* **Assigned User**: Select the assigned user who will be handling your request.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information you would like to share with the service provider.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Organization Name, Contact Email, Contact Phone, Description, Service Provider, and Assigned User).
* Use the "Legal Service Provider" field to filter the search results by multiple service providers.
* Use the "Additional Comments" field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the service provider to understand your needs.
