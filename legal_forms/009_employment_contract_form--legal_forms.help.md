# Employment Contract Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from employees, typically used to formalize employment agreements and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your name as your official name, which will be recorded in our HR system.
2. Provide your active email address to facilitate communication and updates from our company.
3. Select the date and time when you signed this employment contract.
4. Sign your contract by writing your name in the "Employee Signature" field. This will serve as an official acknowledgement of your understanding and agreement with the terms of the employment contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your official name as it appears on your identification documents. This is a required field for our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your active email address to ensure we can contact you with important updates and notifications from our company.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when you signed this employment contract. This is a required field for our records.
* **Time Signed** (`time_signed`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when you signed this employment contract. This is a required field for our records.
* **Employee Signature** (`employees_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your name to indicate your understanding and agreement with the terms of the employment contract. This is a required field for official purposes.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your information is recorded correctly.
* Review the employment contract terms carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
