# Landlord Agreement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to discharge a landlord agreement, which is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary information for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the tenant who is discharging the agreement.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the landlord who is discharging the agreement.
* **Agreement Number** (`agreement_number`, text, **Required**): Enter the number of the agreement being discharged.
* **Lease Date** (`lease_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date the lease started.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date the lease is to end (if known).
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, **Required**): Enter the monthly rent amount as agreed upon in the lease.
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, **Required**): Enter the security deposit amount as agreed upon in the lease.
* **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, text, **Required**): Explain the reason for discharging the agreement.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, **Required**): The tenant must sign this form to confirm they are discharging the agreement.

## Tips
Please fill out this form accurately and completely. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities or seek advice from a legal expert.
