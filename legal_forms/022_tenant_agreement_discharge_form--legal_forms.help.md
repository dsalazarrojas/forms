# Tenant Agreement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Agreement Discharge Form is used to formally terminate a rental agreement between a landlord and a tenant. This form is used to provide a clear understanding of the reason for discharge and the terms of the termination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
2. Fill in the required fields as needed.
3. If a field is not applicable, leave it blank.
4. Double-check your work before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant ID**: This field is used to identify the tenant. Please enter the relevant tenant ID.
* **Landlord ID**: This field is used to identify the landlord. Please enter the relevant landlord ID.
* **Reason for Discharge**: Enter a clear and concise reason for discharging the rental agreement. Be specific and honest in your explanation.
* **Discharge Date**: Enter the date when the rental agreement is to be discharged.
* **Notice Period**: Enter the notice period agreed upon for the discharge of the rental agreement. If not specified, leave blank.
* **Agreement Type**: Select the type of rental agreement being discharged (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **Agreement Number**: Enter the relevant agreement number (if applicable).
* **Landlord Name**: Enter the name of the landlord involved in the agreement.
* **Tenant Name**: Enter the name of the tenant involved in the agreement.
* **Address**: Enter the address associated with the rental agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, refer to the original rental agreement or seek clarification from the relevant parties.
* This form is intended for formal termination of a rental agreement, so please ensure that you're using it for the correct purpose.
* Before submitting the form, double-check your work to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.
