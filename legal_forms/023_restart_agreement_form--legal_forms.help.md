# Restart Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and document the details of a restart agreement. It is intended for use by team administrators and leads to gather necessary information for agreement management.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure that the correct team members are selected for the agreement. Select multiple team members from the provided options, as this field is required.
2. Choose the correct team lead(s) for the agreement. Select one team lead from the provided options, as this field is required.
3. Enter the start date of the agreement.
4. Enter the approval date of the agreement.
5. Choose the correct agreement status for the agreement.
6. Enter the reason for the restart agreement.
7. Select the follow-up date for the agreement.
8. Repeat steps 1-7 for any additional team members and leads, as required for the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement ID** (`agreement_id`, `number`, required): Enter a unique identifier for the agreement.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple team members involved in the agreement.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `select_one`, required): Choose one team lead for the agreement.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, `date`, required): Enter the approval date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the agreement.
* **Restart Reason** (`restart_reason`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for the restart agreement.
* **Follow-up Date** (`follow_up`, `date`, required): Enter the follow-up date for the agreement.
* **Team Members 2** (`team_members_2`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple team members involved in the agreement.
* **Team Lead 2** (`team_lead_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose one team lead for the agreement.
* **Approval Date 2** (`approval_date_2`, `date`, required): Enter the approval date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Status 2** (`agreement_status_2`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of the agreement.
* **Reason 2** (`restart_reason_2`, `text`, required): Enter the reason for the restart agreement.
* **File Upload 2** (`file_upload_2`, `text`, optional): Enter additional information related to the agreement (optional).
* **Comments 2** (`comments_2`, `text`, optional): Enter additional comments related to the agreement (optional).
