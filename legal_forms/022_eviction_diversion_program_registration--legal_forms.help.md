# Eviction Diversion Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eviction Diversion Program Registration form is designed to gather information from tenants involved in eviction cases. This form helps housing agencies or legal services providers understand the circumstances surrounding the eviction and determine the best course of action.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic information in the first field, "tenant_info".
2. Select the status of your current eviction situation in the "eviction_status" field.
3. Indicate whether you are seeking legal aid services in the "legal_aids_services" field.
4. Choose if you have partnered with any housing agency (or multiple) in the "housing_agency_partners" field.
5. Provide any relevant court information in the "court_info" field.
6. Share details about the eviction program you are involved in in the "program_info" field.
7. Enter any relevant information about your landlord in the "landlord_info" field.
8. If you have any additional information, you can provide it in the "other_info" field.
9. Finally, confirm whether you have completed this form by selecting the correct option in the "submission_confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information** (tenant_info, text, required): Enter your basic information, such as your name and contact details.
* **Eviction Status** (eviction_status, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your current eviction situation from the options: Pending, Scheduled, or Evicted.
* **Legal Aids Services** (legal_aids_services, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are seeking legal aid services from the options: Court-appointed attorney, Private attorney, Non-profit legal aid provider, or Other (please specify).
* **Housing Agency Partners** (housing_agency_partners, select_multiple, required): Choose if you have partnered with any housing agency (or multiple) from the options: Partnered with a housing agency, Partnered with multiple housing agencies, or Not partnered with a housing agency.
* **Court Information** (court_info, text, required): Provide any relevant court information that may be helpful in your case.
* **Program Information** (program_info, text, required): Share details about the eviction program you are involved in, including any relevant dates or circumstances.
* **Landlord Information** (landlord_info, text, required): Enter any relevant information about your landlord, such as their contact details or a description of their role in the eviction process.
* **Additional Information** (other_info, note, required): If you have any additional information that is not covered in the above fields, you can provide it here.
* **Submission Confirmation** (submission_confirmation, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed and submitted this form by selecting the correct option.
