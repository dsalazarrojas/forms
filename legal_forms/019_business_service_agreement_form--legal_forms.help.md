# Business Service Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Service Agreement Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a business service agreement between a service provider and a client. It ensures both parties have a clear understanding of the services to be provided, payment terms, and other essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the start date for the agreement.
2. Enter the full name of the service provider.
3. Provide the business address of the service provider.
4. Enter the contact phone number of the service provider (optional).
5. Enter the contact email address of the service provider.
6. Enter the full name of the client.
7. Provide the business address of the client.
8. Enter the contact phone number of the client (optional).
9. Enter the contact email address of the client.
10. Describe the services to be provided in detail.
11. Specify the scope of work and deliverables for the project.
12. Outline the project timeline, including start dates, milestones, and end dates.
13. Specify the total project cost.
14. Define the payment terms and schedule.
15. If applicable, enter any upfront payment or deposit required.
16. Specify when the final payment is due.
17. Describe how the completed work will be evaluated (acceptance criteria).
18. Provide any warranties or guarantees offered by the service provider.
19. Specify any liability limitations or exclusions.
20. Define the conditions for terminating the agreement.
21. Choose whether confidentiality is required.
22. (Optional) Enter the date the service provider signs the agreement.
23. (Optional) Enter the date the client signs the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Effective Date**: Enter the date when this agreement becomes effective. This is the start date of the service agreement.
* **Service Provider Name**: Enter the full name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Address**: Provide the business address of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the service provider (optional).
* **Service Provider Email**: Enter the contact email address of the service provider.
* **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the client.
* **Client Address**: Provide the business address of the client.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the client (optional).
* **Client Email**: Enter the contact email address of the client.
* **Service Description**: Describe the services to be provided in detail.
* **Scope of Work**: Specify the scope of work and deliverables for the project.
* **Project Timeline**: Outline the project timeline, including start dates, milestones, and end dates.
* **Total Project Cost**: Specify the total project cost.
* **Payment Terms**: Define the payment terms and schedule.
* **Deposit or Advance Payment**: If applicable, enter any upfront payment or deposit required.
* **Final Payment Due Date**: Specify when the final payment is due.
* **Acceptance Criteria**: Describe how the completed work will be evaluated (acceptance criteria).
* **Warranties and Guarantees**: Provide any warranties or guarantees offered by the service provider.
* **Liability Limitations**: Specify any liability limitations or exclusions.
* **Termination Clause**: Define the conditions for terminating the agreement.
* **Confidentiality and NDA**: Choose whether confidentiality is required for the service provider.
* **Service Provider Signature Date**: (Optional) Enter the date the service provider signs the agreement.
* **Client Signature Date**: (Optional) Enter the date the client signs the agreement.
