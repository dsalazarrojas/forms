# Judges Contract Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Judges Contract Agreement form is designed to gather information from users regarding their contracts with judges. This form will help you to collect the essential details that are relevant to creating and managing these legal agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the User Details page**: This page is where you will enter information about the user who is signing the contract. Please provide the necessary details such as name and any other relevant information.
2. **Complete the Event Details page**: This page is where you will enter information about the event or case related to the contract. Provide the necessary details such as event name, date, and any other relevant information.
3. **Review the Contract Terms page**: This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or terms specific to the contract.
4. **Select the Terms and Conditions**: On this page, you will select one or more of the available terms and conditions that apply to the contract. Please note that these are crucial to the agreement and are required fields.
5. **Enter the Agreement Details**: This is another text area where you can provide any additional information related to the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Details**: This is the first page where you will enter information about the user who is signing the contract. Enter your name and any other details that are relevant to the user.
  - *Type*: text
  - *Required*: False
  - *Hint*: null

- **Event Details**: This is the second page where you will enter information about the event or case related to the contract. Enter event name, date, and any other relevant information.
  - *Type*: text
  - *Required*: False
  - *Hint*: null

- **Contract Terms**: This is a text area where you can provide any additional comments or terms specific to the contract.
  - *Type*: note
  - *Required*: False
  - *Hint*: null

- **Terms and Conditions**: On this page, you will select one or more of the available terms and conditions that apply to the contract. Please note that these are crucial to the agreement and are required fields.
  - *Type*: select_multiple
  - *Required*: True
  - *Hint*: Select one or more of the available terms and conditions that apply to the contract.

- **Agreement Details**: This is another text area where you can provide any additional information related to the agreement.
  - *Type*: text
  - *Required*: False
  - *Hint*: null

## Tips

- Make sure to carefully read through each page and ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Double-check the terms and conditions selected to ensure they accurately reflect the contract.
- If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, please contact form@gic.mx.
