# Joint Venture Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Joint Venture Discharge Form is a document used to officially terminate or end a joint venture agreement between two or more business entities. This form is typically used when one or both parties wish to dissolve their partnership, either by mutual agreement or due to certain circumstances. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **General Information**: This section is for the basic details of the joint venture. Please fill in the following:
	* **Joint Venture Name**: Enter the name of the joint venture that you are discharging.
	* **Joint Venture Address**: Enter the address of the joint venture.
	* **Joint Venture Email**: Enter the email address of the joint venture.
	* **Joint Venture Phone**: Enter the phone number of the joint venture.
2. **Partners Information**: This section is for the details of the partner(s) involved in the joint venture. Please fill in the following:
	* **Partners Information**: Enter the number of partners involved.
	* **Partner Signature**: Enter the date on which the partner signed this form.
3. **Assets Distribution**: This section is for the distribution of assets after the joint venture is discharged. Please select the distribution method from the available options:
	* **Option 1**
	* **Option 2**
4. **Liability Distribution**: This section is for the distribution of liabilities after the joint venture is discharged. Please select the distribution method from the available options:
	* **Option 1**
	* **Option 2**
5. **Terms**: This section is for any additional terms or conditions of the discharge. Please enter any relevant details.
6. **Discharge Terms**: This section is for any special terms or conditions of the discharge. Please enter any relevant details.
7. **Agreement Signature**: Enter the date on which this form was signed.
8. **Partner Signature**: Enter the date on which this form was signed by the partner.
9. **Business Entity Information**: This section is to select the type of business entity involved.
	* **Option 1**
	* **Option 2**
10. **Joint Venture Address**: Enter the address of the joint venture.
11. **Partner Address**: Enter the address of the partner.
12. **Joint Venture Email**: Enter the email address of the joint venture.
13. **Partner Email**: Enter the email address of the partner.
14. **Joint Venture Phone**: Enter the phone number of the joint venture.
15. **Partner Phone**: Enter the phone number of the partner.
