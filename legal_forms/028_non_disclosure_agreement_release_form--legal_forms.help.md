# Non-Disclosure Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non-Disclosure Agreement Release Form is used to document the release of confidential information by an individual or organization. It helps ensure that all parties involved understand their obligations and responsibilities regarding the handling and protection of sensitive information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select one of the options in the "Non Disclosure Agreement Release Form" field. This field indicates whether you are releasing your confidentiality obligations.
2. Enter a reason for releasing the confidential information in the "Reason For Release" field. This is an optional field, but it helps the reviewer understand the context of the release.
3. If required, select a date for releasing the confidential information in the "Release Date" field.
4. Sign and date the form by entering your signature in the "Signee Signature" field and the date in the "Sign Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Non Disclosure Agreement Release Form** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ This field indicates whether you are releasing your confidentiality obligations. Select 'Yes' if you are willing to release the confidentiality obligations, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Release Confidential Information** (`text`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to enter a reason for releasing the confidential information.
* **Reason For Release** (`text`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to enter a reason for releasing the confidential information. This is an optional field, but it helps the reviewer understand the context of the release.
* **Non Disclosure Obligation** (`select_one`, required: false):
	+ This field indicates whether you have a non-disclosure obligation. Select 'Yes' if you have a non-disclosure obligation, or 'No' if you do not.
* **Release Date** (`date`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to select a date for releasing the confidential information.
* **Sign Date** (`date`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to select a date for signing the form.
* **Signee Signature** (`text`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to enter your signature, indicating your agreement to the release of confidential information.
* **Reviewer Signature** (`text`, required: false):
	+ This field is used to enter the signature of the reviewer, indicating their agreement to the release of confidential information.

## Tips

* This form is not required for every project or collaboration. Use it when necessary to document the release of confidential information.
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that all parties understand their obligations and responsibilities regarding the handling and protection of sensitive information.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the relevant project manager or collaborator.
