# Manuscript Review Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Manuscript Review Agreement form is designed to collect information and agreements from reviewers and publishers for manuscript review purposes. This form ensures a clear understanding of the review process and terms between both parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you have all required information before proceeding.
2.  Fill in your Reviewer Information, including your name and contact details.
3.  Provide your Publisher Information, including their name and contact details.
4.  Read and agree to the Review Terms, selecting the correct option from the provided list.
5.  Describe the Review Process, ensuring a clear understanding of the review procedure.
6.  Sign and date the Reviewer Agreement to confirm your commitment.
7.  Sign and date the E-Signature of Reviewer to electronically sign the agreement.
8.  Review and sign the Publisher Agreement to confirm their commitment.
9.  Sign and date the E-Signature of Publisher to electronically sign the agreement.
10.  Review the form to ensure accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Reviewer Information** (`reviewer_info`, text, required: false): This field is for the reviewer to fill in their name and contact details.
*   **Publisher Information** (`publisher_info`, text, required: false): This field is for the publisher to fill in their name and contact details.
*   **Review Terms** (`review_terms`, note, required: false): This field explains the terms and conditions of the review process. Select the correct option to indicate your agreement.
*   **Review Process** (`review_process`, note, required: false): Describe the review process to ensure a clear understanding between the reviewer and publisher.
*   **Reviewer Agreement** (`reviewer_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Sign and date to confirm your commitment to the review process.
*   **Date of Review** (`date_of_review`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of the review to ensure accurate tracking.
*   **Reviewer Signature** (`reviewer_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date to confirm your commitment.
*   **Publisher Signature** (`publisher_signature`, text, required: false): Sign and date to confirm the publisher's commitment.
*   **Date of Publishing** (`date_of_publishing`, date, required: false): Fill in the date of publishing to ensure accurate tracking.
*   **E-Signature of Reviewer** (`e_signature_of_reviewer`, text, required: false): Electronically sign to confirm your commitment.
*   **E-Signature of Publisher** (`e_signature_of_publisher`, text, required: false): Electronically sign to confirm the publisher's commitment.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Submit the form once all fields have been filled out accurately.
*   **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required: false): Cancel the form if there are any issues or concerns during the review process.
