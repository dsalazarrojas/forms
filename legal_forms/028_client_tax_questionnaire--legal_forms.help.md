# Client Tax Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect detailed information about a client's tax situation. It will help us understand their tax-related details, including employment status, income, expenses, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, please review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information. The form is divided into several sections, each focusing on a specific aspect of your tax situation. Follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Start with the "Tax Information Questionnaire" section, which serves as an introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
2. In the "Client Information" section, provide your full name, last 4 digits of your Social Security Number, email address, and phone number.
3. The "Tax Year" section is where you specify the tax year this form is for.
4. Choose your "Filing Status" from the options provided.
5. Enter the number of dependents you have.
6. Indicate your employment status and any self-employment information.
7. If applicable, provide details about your business and employment.
8. In the "Deductions and Expenses" section, select whether you want to itemize or take the standard deduction and answer questions about mortgage interest, property taxes, charitable donations, medical expenses, and student loan interest.
9. Finally, answer questions about education costs and other relevant expenses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tax Information Questionnaire**: This section serves as an introduction to the form and explains its purpose.
* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last 4 Digits of Social Security Number** (text, required): Enter the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address for any future communications.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number for any follow-up questions or communications.
* **Tax Year** (number, required): Specify the tax year this form is for.
* **Filing Status** (select_one, required): Choose your filing status from the options provided.
* **Number of Dependents** (number, required): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Employment Type** (select_one, required): Indicate your employment status and any self-employment information.
* **Spouse Employment Status** (select_one, required): If applicable, indicate your spouse's employment status.
* **Do you have self-employment income?** (select_one, required): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Type of Business** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your business or self-employment.
* **When did you start the business?** (date, optional): If applicable, specify when you started your business.
* **Where is your business located?** (text, optional): Provide the address of your business location.
* **Do you use a home office for business?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Approximate home office square footage** (number, optional): If applicable, provide an approximate value.
* **Do you want to itemize deductions or take standard deduction?** (select_one, required): Choose between itemizing deductions, taking the standard deduction, or being unsure.
* **Did you pay mortgage interest?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Did you pay property taxes?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Did you make charitable contributions?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Approximate amount of charitable donations** (number, optional): If applicable, provide an approximate value.
* **Did you pay significant medical expenses?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Did you pay student loan interest?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.
* **Did you or family members have education costs?** (select_one, optional): Answer this question truthfully.

Note: The fields marked as "optional" are only required if the condition specified in the hint is applicable.
