# Consulting Services Agreement Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to create a comprehensive consulting services agreement between a client and a consulting firm or individual.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and documents ready to complete the form.
2. Start by filling out the "Agreement Overview" field with a brief summary of the agreement.
3. Enter the "Client name" and "Consultant name" fields with the legal names of the client organization or individual and the consulting firm or individual, respectively.
4. Select a date for the "Effective date" field, which represents the start date of the agreement.
5. Describe the services to be provided, including deliverables, in the "Scope of services" field.
6. Define the "Compensation structure" including fee schedule and invoicing frequency.
7. Outline the "Payment terms" including net terms and late fee policy.
8. Specify the "Confidentiality requirement" by selecting one of the provided options (Standard, Enhanced, or No confidentiality required).
9. Enter the number of days required for a termination notice in the "Termination notice period in days" field.
10. Briefly state any liability limitations or indemnification in the "Liability limitations summary" field.
11. Identify the "Governing law jurisdiction" if applicable.
12. Complete the "Authorized signatory name" field with the name of the person authorized to sign on behalf of the client.
13. Enter the job title of the authorized signatory in the "Signatory title" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Overview** (Note): A brief summary of the agreement.
* **Client name** (Text, required): Legal name of the client organization or individual.
* **Consultant name** (Text, required): Legal name of the consulting firm or individual.
* **Effective date** (Date, required): Agreement start date.
* **Scope of services** (Text, required): Describe services to be provided, including deliverables.
* **Compensation structure** (Text, required): Fee schedule and invoicing frequency.
* **Payment terms** (Text, required): Net terms and late fee policy.
* **Confidentiality requirement** (Select One, required): Select one of the provided options (Standard, Enhanced, or No confidentiality required).
* **Termination notice period in days** (Number, required): Number of days required for termination notice.
* **Liability limitations summary** (Text, optional): Briefly state any liability limitations or indemnification.
* **Governing law** (Text, optional): State or country governing this agreement.
* **Authorized signatory name** (Text, required): Name of person authorized to sign on behalf of client.
* **Signatory title** (Text, required): Job title of authorized signatory.
