# landlord_tenant_agreement_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Landlord-Tenant Agreement Declaration Form is a document that collects information and declarations from both the tenant and landlord in a rental property agreement. It helps establish a clear record of the terms and conditions of the rental, benefiting both parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Tenant Info**: Enter your personal information as the tenant, including your name and contact details.
2.  **Landlord Info**: If applicable, enter the landlord's name and contact information.
3.  **Terms**: This is a text field for any conditions or agreements between you and the landlord.
4.  **Tenant Acknowledgments**: Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the terms and conditions outlined in the form.
5.  **Signature 1**: Sign this field, usually by the tenant, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
6.  **Signature 2**: The landlord typically signs this field to confirm their acknowledgment of the agreement.
7.  **Landlord Signature**: Sign this field by the landlord to finalize the agreement and confirm they have reviewed the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Tenant Info (Required)**: Enter your name and contact information as the tenant.
*  **Landlord Info (Optional)**: If applicable, enter the landlord's name and contact information.
*  **Terms (Optional)**: A text field for any conditions or agreements between you and the landlord.
*  **Tenant Acknowledgments (Optional)**: Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the terms and conditions.
*  **Signature 1 (Optional)**: Sign this field, usually by the tenant, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
*  **Signature 2 (Optional)**: The landlord typically signs this field to confirm their acknowledgment of the agreement.
*  **Landlord Signature (Optional)**: Sign this field by the landlord to finalize the agreement and confirm they have reviewed the terms.

## Tips

*   Be sure to review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the agreement.
*   Make sure to have both parties sign and date the agreement where applicable.
*   Keep the original document for reference and record-keeping purposes.
