# Labor Rights Compliance Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's labor rights compliance declaration, and is likely used by HR departments or management for tracking and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. The form is divided into two parts: the first part collects information about the employee, and the second part collects information about their employment details.
2. The first field is for the employee's name, and should be filled out with the employee's full name.
3. The next field is for the date of the declaration, which should be filled out with the current date.
4. The following field is for the time of the declaration, which should be filled out with the current time in the chosen time zone.
5. Next is the time zone of the declaration, which should be selected from the provided options (PST, EST, CST, MST, HST).
6. The form also requires the employee's department, which should be filled out with the employee's work department.
7. The employee's job status (Active or Non-Active) should be selected from the provided options.
8. The form also requires the employee's job title, which should be filled out with the employee's job title.
9. The form requires the employee's job category and subcategory, which should be filled out with the employee's job category and subcategory.
10. The next field is for the employee's job level, which should be filled out with the employee's job level.
11. The form also requires the employee's job group, which should be filled out with the employee's job group.
12. The form requires the employee to provide information about their labor union, contract type, and contract details.
13. The form also requires the employee's union fees, union dues, labor union dues, and contract renewal date.
