# prophets_buyout_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prophets Buyout Agreement Form is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a buyout agreement between co-owners of a property. This form is essential for establishing a clear understanding of the ownership rights and responsibilities among co-owners, particularly when one co-owner intends to transfer or sell their shares to another party. The form is used to gather information about co-owners' names, their shares, property address, and the date of the agreement.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the co-owners' names in the **Co-owners Names** field. This field is required.
2. Indicate the share percentage for each co-owner in the **Co-owners Share** field. This field is optional but recommended for accurate calculation of co-owners' shares.
3. Provide the property address in the **Property Address** field. Although not required, this field helps ensure clarity on the property's location.
4. Enter the date when the agreement was made in the **Agreement Date** field. This field is optional but helps establish a timeline for the agreement.
5. Enter the date when the signature was obtained or when the co-owners' signatures were collected in the **Signature Date** field. This field is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Co-owners Names** (`coowner_names`, `text`, **required**): Enter the names of the co-owners of the property. Please separate each name with commas.
* **Co-owners Share** (`coowner_share`, `number`, optional): If applicable, specify the percentage of share for each co-owner. This will help calculate the total percentage of ownership.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, optional): Provide the exact address of the property to help identify the location.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the agreement was made, if applicable.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the co-owners' signatures were obtained or when the agreement was signed, if applicable.)

## Tips

*   Ensure all fields are filled accurately for a clear and effective buyout agreement.
*   Review the provided examples or templates for a better understanding of the agreement layout.
*   Have all co-owners review and sign the agreement before it's considered valid.
