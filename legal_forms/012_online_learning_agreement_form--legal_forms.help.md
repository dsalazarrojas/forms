# Online Learning Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Learning Agreement Form is designed to collect information and agreements from students participating in online courses. This form helps to establish a mutual understanding between the student and the course provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Fill in the start and end dates of the course, including the start and end times, in the respective fields.
5. Choose the status of your agreement (Completed, Draft, In Progress, or Pending).
6. Select the type of agreement (Master Agreement, Project Agreement, or Student Agreement).
7. Enter the version of the agreement.
8. Provide the date and time of the agreement.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Terms of Use" field (optional).
10. Confirm your understanding of the terms of service and provide any additional comments in the "Terms of Service" field.
11. Enter the start date of the course.
12. Enter the end date of the course.
13. Enter the duration of the course.
14. Enter the location of the course.
15. Enter your student ID.
16. Enter the instructor ID.
17. Confirm your agreement status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (Student Name Label, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address that will be used for course communication.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number that will be used for contact purposes.
* **Date** (Date, Date, Required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Time** (Time, Time, Required): Enter the start time of the course.
* **Note** (Note, Note, Not Required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Course Name** (Course Name Label, Text, Required): Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
* **Instructor ID** (Instructor ID, Text, Required): Enter the ID of the instructor leading the course.
* **Student ID** (Student ID, Text, Required): Enter your student ID.
* **Agreement Status** (Agreement Status, Select One, Required): Choose the status of your agreement (Completed, Draft, In Progress, or Pending).
* **Agreement Type** (Agreement Type, Select Multiple, Required): Choose the type of agreement (Master Agreement, Project Agreement, or Student Agreement).
* **Agreement Version** (Agreement Version, Number, Required): Enter the version of the agreement.
* **Agreement Date** (Agreement Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (Agreement Time, Time, Required): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Terms of Use** (Terms of Use, Text, Not Required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Terms of Service** (Terms of Service, Text, Required): Confirm your understanding of the terms of service and enter any additional comments.
* **Agreement Version Note** (Agreement Version Note, Note, Not Required): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Course Start Date** (Course Start Date, Date, Required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course Start Time** (Course Start Time, Time, Required): Enter the start time of the course.
* **Course End Date** (Course End Date, Date, Required): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course End Time** (Course End Time, Time, Required): Enter the end time of the course.
* **Course Duration** (Course Duration, Text, Not Required): Enter the duration of the course.
* **Course Location** (Course Location, Text, Required): Enter the location of the course.
* **Student Email** (Student Email, Email, Required): Enter your email address that will be used for course communication.
