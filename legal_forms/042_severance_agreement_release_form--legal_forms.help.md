# Severance Agreement Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Severance Agreement Release Form is a document used to formalize the terms of an employee's termination of employment. It ensures that both the employer and employee understand and agree on the terms of the severance package, including the start and end dates of employment, reason for severance, and amount paid. This form is crucial for a smooth transition for both parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you understand what each field represents.
2.  Gather all necessary information and documents before filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company providing the severance package.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee receiving the severance package.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee's employment began.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date the employee's employment ended.
* **Reason for Severance** (`reason_for_severance`, text, required): Choose the reason for the employee's termination. Common reasons include but are not limited to: 
    + Retirement
    + Resignation
    + Layoff
    + Other (Specify)
*   **Severance Amount** (`severance_amount`, number, required): Enter the total severance amount paid to the employee.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company.
*   **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, text, required): Enter the employee's ID Number.
*   **Date of Severance** (`date_of_severance`, date, required): Enter the date the severance package was issued.
*   **Employer Sign Date** (`employer_sign_date`, date, required): The date the employer signs this form.
*   **Employee Sign Date** (`employee_sign_date`, date, required): The date the employee signs this form.
*   **Employer Sign** (`employer_sign`, text, required): Enter the signature of the employer.
*   **Employee Sign** (`employee_sign`, text, required): Enter the signature of the employee.

## Tips
*   Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
*   Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms.
*   Both the employer and employee should sign the form to make it official.
