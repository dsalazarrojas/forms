# Estate Inventory Services Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Inventory Services Agreement form is used to document and formalize an estate inventory service. It captures relevant information about the service, including the approver's details and the service's status and type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Define the service provided by the estate inventory services agreement.
2. Select the approver from the available options.
3. Enter the approver's email address.
4. Choose the date the service was approved.
5. Select the time the service was approved (optional).
6. Enter your signature for verification.
7. Choose the type of service provided.
8. Select the status of the service.
9. Add any additional comments about the service (optional).
10. Enter the approver's phone number (optional).
11. Enter any notes from the approver (optional).
12. Enter the approver's company (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Define Services** (Page 1, Service Definition): Enter a brief description of the service provided.
* **Approver** (Page 2, Select one): Select the approver from the available options.
* **Approver Email** (Page 2, Email): Enter the approver's email address.
* **Date Approved** (Page 3, Date): Choose the date the service was approved.
* **Time Approved** (Page 3, Time): Select the time the service was approved (if applicable).
* **Signature** (Page 4, Text): Enter your signature for verification.
* **Service Type** (Page 4, Select one): Choose the type of service provided.
* **Service Status** (Page 4, Select multiple): Select the status of the service.
* **Comments** (Page 5, Text): Add any additional comments about the service (optional).
* **Approver Phone** (Page 5, Text): Enter the approver's phone number (optional).
* **Approver Notes** (Page 5, Note): Enter any notes from the approver (optional).
* **Approver Company** (Page 6, Text): Enter the approver's company (optional).
