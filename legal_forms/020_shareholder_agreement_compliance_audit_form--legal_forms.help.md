# Shareholder Agreement Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track information related to shareholder agreements, ensuring that all necessary compliance requirements are met. It will help identify potential issues and facilitate regular reviews of shareholder agreements, promoting transparency and adherence to regulatory standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company details: Enter the name of the company in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide contact information: Enter the contact person's name, title, email, and phone number in the "Contact Person", "Contact Title", "Email", and "Phone" fields, respectively.
3. Set up agreement dates: Enter the start, review due, and end dates of the agreement in the "Agreement Start Date", "Review Due Date", and "Agreement End Date" fields, respectively.
4. Select agreement status and terms: Choose the current status and terms of the agreement from the "Agreement Status" and "Agreement Terms" fields.
5. Specify company size and shareholder count: Select the size of the company and enter the number of shareholders in the "Company Size" and "Number of Shareholders" fields.
6. Enter share details: Enter the total number of shares outstanding, issued, owned, reserved, transferable, non-transferable, issued to public, directors, investors, and employees in the "Total Shares Outstanding", "Total Shares Issued", "Shares Owned", "Shares Reserved", "Transferable Shares", "Non-Transferable Shares", "Shares Issued To Public", "Shares Issued To Directors", "Shares Issued To Investors", and "Shares Issued To Employees" fields.
7. Set the review status: Choose the status of the review from the "Review Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this agreement.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the title of the contact person.
* **Email**: Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Agreement Start Date**: Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Review Due Date**: Enter the date by which this agreement should be reviewed.
* **Agreement End Date**: Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Status**: Select the current status of the agreement from the following options: Active, Inactive, or Expired.
* **Agreement Terms**: Describe the terms of the agreement in detail.
* **Company Size**: Select the size of the company from the following options: Small, Medium, or Large.
* **Number of Shareholders**: Enter the total number of shareholders.
* **Total Shares Outstanding**: Enter the total number of shares outstanding.
* **Total Shares Issued**: Enter the total number of shares issued.
* **Shares Owned**: Enter the number of shares owned.
* **Percentage Owned**: Enter the percentage of shares owned.
* **Shares Reserved**: Enter the number of reserved shares.
* **Transferable Shares**: Enter the number of transferable shares.
* **Non-Transferable Shares**: Enter the number of non-transferable shares.
* **Shares Issued To Public**: Enter the number of shares issued to public.
* **Shares Issued To Directors**: Enter the number of shares issued to directors.
* **Shares Issued To Investors**: Enter the number of shares issued to investors.
* **Shares Issued To Employees**: Enter the number of shares issued to employees.
* **Review Status**: Select the status of the review from the following options: Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.

Note: Ensure all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
