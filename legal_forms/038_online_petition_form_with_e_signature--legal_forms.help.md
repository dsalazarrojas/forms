# Online Petition Form With E Signature - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help users understand and fill out the Online Petition Form With E Signature, a form designed to gather information and electronic signatures for online petitions. The form is intended for use in a legal context, such as gathering signatures for a petition, but it can also be adapted for other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Fill out the form sections**: The form is divided into multiple sections, each with its own fields. Fill out each section as prompted.
2. **Choose the correct category**: Select the correct category for your petition from the options provided in the second field.
3. **Enter a description**: Provide a brief description of your petition in the third field.
4. **Set up electronic signature options**: In fields 4-6, choose whether to allow users to sign electronically and whether to embed signatures in the report.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Online Petition Form With E Signature** (`online-petition-form-with-e-signature`, text, required: false)
	* This field is the title of the form, which should be filled out as the title of your online petition.
### 2. **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false)
	* Choose the category of the petition from the options provided, such as "Legal Forms" or "Other".
### 3. **Description** (`description`, text, required: false)
	* Enter a brief description of your petition to provide context for users.
### 4. **Use HTML Reports** (`use-html-reports`, select_one, required: false)
	* Choose whether to allow users to view HTML reports in the petition.
### 5. **Sign Electronically** (`sign-electronically`, select_one, required: false)
	* Choose whether to allow users to sign the petition electronically.
### 6. **Embed Signatures** (`embed-signatures`, select_one, required: false)
	* Choose whether to embed signatures in the petition report.
### 7. **Other Field 1** (`other-field-1`, text, required: false)
	* This field is for any additional information or details you want to provide about your petition.
### 8. **Other Field 2** (`other-field-2`, text, required: false)
	* This field is for any additional information or details you want to provide about your petition.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Take care to choose the correct category and description to accurately represent your petition.
- Consider whether to allow electronic signatures and embedding them in the report to suit your needs.
- Use the "Other Field" sections to provide any additional information that doesn't fit into the other fields.
