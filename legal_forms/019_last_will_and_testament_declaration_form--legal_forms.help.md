# Last Will And Testament Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Last Will And Testament Declaration Form is a legal document that allows individuals to declare their last will and testament, stating their wishes and intentions for the distribution of their assets after death.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by carefully reading through each section of the form, making sure you understand what is being asked of you.
2. Fill out each section as honestly and accurately as possible, using the provided options as guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assets Owned** (`assets_owned`, text, optional): This field is for listing the assets you own, such as properties, investments, or other valuable items.
* **Wishes Declaration** (`wishes_declarations`, text, optional): This field is for stating your wishes for the distribution of your assets after death. Please be as specific as possible, and include any relevant details.
* **Beneficiaries Naming** (`beneficiaries_naming`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows you to name the beneficiaries of your will. Select one or more of the provided options to indicate who will receive your assets.
* **Testamentary Funds** (`testamentary_funds`, number, optional): This field is for declaring the amount of funds you wish to allocate to your beneficiaries. Please enter a numerical value.

## Tips
* Make sure to sign and date the form once you have completed it.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that your wishes are accurately reflected.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, please reach out to a legal professional for guidance.
