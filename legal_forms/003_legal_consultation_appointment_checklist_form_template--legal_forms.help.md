# legal_consultation_appointment_checklist_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal consultation appointment checklist form template is designed to help legal professionals capture the details of client meetings, track client information, and record agreements or decisions made during the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `client_meeting` field with a brief description of the meeting.
2. Enter the client's name in the `client_name` field.
3. Describe the legal issues discussed during the meeting in the `legal_issues_discussed` field.
4. Select the current status of the case from the `case_status` field.
5. Provide the client's contact information in the `client_contact_info` field.
6. Outline the next steps to be taken for the client in the `next_steps` field.
7. Indicate if legal advice was given to the client in the `legal_advice_given` field.
8. Select the client's agreement to attend the meeting and/or provide information from the `client_agreement` field.
9. Record any notes from the meeting in the `meeting_notes` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Meeting** (`client_meeting`): Enter a brief description of the meeting.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`): Enter the client's name.
* **Legal Issues Discussed** (`legal_issues_discussed`): Describe the legal issues discussed during the meeting.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`): Select the current status of the case (e.g., Pending, Active, Closed).
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`): Provide the client's contact information.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`): Outline the next steps to be taken for the client.
* **Legal Advice Given** (`legal_advice_given`): Indicate if legal advice was given to the client.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`): Select which of the client's agreements were made during the meeting (e.g., attend, provide information).
* **Meeting Notes** (`meeting_notes`): Record any notes from the meeting. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use clear and concise language when filling in the fields.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
