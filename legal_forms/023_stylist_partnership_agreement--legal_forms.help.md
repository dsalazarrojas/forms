# Stylist Partnership Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stylist Partnership Agreement form is used to document the partnership agreement between a stylist and a salon. It captures essential information such as the salon name, stylist name, contact details, and terms of agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, follow these steps:
	* Start by filling in your salon's name in the **Salon Name** field.
	* Enter your name in the **Stylist Name** field.
	* Provide your contact email address in the **Contact Email** field.
	* Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
	* Select either **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you have agreed to the terms of the partnership in the **Terms Agreed** field.
	* Enter the date you signed the agreement in the **Signature Date** field.
	* Provide your signature in the **Signature** field.
	* Enter your title (e.g., Manager, Supervisor, etc.) in the **Contact Title** field.
	* If applicable, enter your business name in the **Contact Business** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Salon Name** (Text, Required): Enter your salon's name.
* **Stylist Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as the stylist.
* **Contact Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Terms Agreed** (Select One, Optional): Check if you have agreed to the terms of the partnership.
* **Signature Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you signed this agreement.
* **Signature** (Note, Optional): Provide your handwritten or typed signature.
* **Contact Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your title or position at the contact business.
* **Contact Business** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter the name of the business you represent.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately fill in all required fields.
* Review the terms of the partnership agreement before indicating your agreement.
* Keep a copy of this document for future reference.
