# Wedding Videography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients for a wedding videography service. It includes essential details about the client and their wedding event to ensure a smooth experience for both the client and the videographer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Enter the details about your wedding, such as the date, time, and location.
3. Specify the video package you have selected, including the type of video you want and the duration of the coverage.
4. Confirm that you have understood and agreed to the photo release, as you will be granting permission for the videographer to capture your likeness and events.
5. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent and agreement with the terms of the contract.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_information`, text, not required): This field is for entering your name and other identifying information.
* **Wedding Details** (`wedding_details`, text, not required): Here, you can enter the details of your wedding, such as the date, location, and time.
* **Intended Video Package** (`intended_video_package`, text, not required): Select the video package that you have chosen for your wedding coverage.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, not required): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
* **Customers Consent** (`customers_consent`, text, not required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the contract.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, not required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for urgent matters.
* **Customer Signature** (`customer_signature`, text, not required): Sign and date the form to confirm your consent and agreement with the terms of the contract.
* **Wedding Date** (`wedding_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your wedding.
* **Wedding Time** (`wedding_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of your wedding.
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location of your wedding.
* **Video Capture** (`video_capture`, text, not required): Specify the type of video capture you have chosen for your coverage.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, text, not required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the photo release, granting permission for the videographer to capture your likeness and events.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all the fields are filled accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion or miscommunication.
- Make sure to review the terms of the contract carefully before signing.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the videographer for clarification.
