# Civil Litigation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Civil Litigation Intake Form is designed to collect detailed information about civil cases. It will help the intake process by providing a structured and organized way to collect essential details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2.  Make sure to answer all required fields as they are marked with a star (*).
3.  Use the hints provided for each field to help guide your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client full name** (`client_full_name`, `text`, required): This is the full name of the individual or company involved in the case.
* **Contact information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required): This field is for the client's contact information, including their email and phone number.
* **Date of incident** (`incident_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the incident, if known. If unknown, please enter an approximate date.
* **Description of issue** (`description_of_issue`, `text`, required): Provide a detailed description of the incident, including the facts and outcomes.
* **Desired outcome** (`desired_outcome`, `text`, required): Specify the desired outcome of the case, such as settlement or injunctive relief.
* **Witness information** (`witness_information`, `text`, optional): If there are witnesses involved, please provide their names and contact details.
* **Prior actions taken** (`prior_actions_taken`, `text`, optional): List any prior actions taken, such as complaints filed or negotiations with counsel.
* **Urgency level for intake** (`urgency_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the urgency level of the case, with options: High, Medium, or Low.
* **Confidentiality agreement note** (`confidentiality_agreement`, `note`, required): Please note if the client has a confidentiality agreement, and what it entails.
* **Intake date** (`intake_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this form is completed.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
