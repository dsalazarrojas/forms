The Buyer Broker Compensation Agreement is a legal form that outlines the terms and conditions of the compensation for a real estate broker. It is typically used by brokers to define their rights and responsibilities when working with a buyer to find and purchase a property. This form ensures that both parties are clear on the compensation structure, services provided, and any exclusions or limitations.

Before filling out this form, it is essential to understand that a broker is an individual or company that represents a buyer in a real estate transaction. This form is not applicable to real estate agents or other industry professionals.

When filling out this form, make sure to review and understand each section carefully. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose and requirements.

## Purpose
The Buyer Broker Compensation Agreement is a legally binding document that outlines the terms and conditions of the compensation for a real estate broker. It is essential to review and understand this document carefully before signing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the broker's name and license number in the "Broker Name or Firm" and "Broker License Number" fields.
2. Enter the broker's email address in the "Broker Email Address" field.
3. Enter the broker's phone number in the "Broker Phone Number" field.
4. Enter the buyer's name in the "Buyer Name(s)" field.
5. Enter the buyer's email address in the "Buyer Email Address" field.
6. Enter the buyer's phone number in the "Buyer Phone Number" field.
7. Select the effective date of the agreement in the "Agreement Date" field.
8. Select the duration of the agreement in the "Agreement Duration" field.
9. Choose how the broker will be compensated in the "Compensation Type" field.
10. Enter the commission percentage or flat fee in the "Commission Percentage or Flat Fee" field.
11. Select the source of the compensation in the "Source of Compensation" field.
12. Describe the scope of the property search in the "Scope of Property Search" field.
13. If there are any properties excluded from the agreement, select "Yes, certain properties excluded" and list the excluded properties or sellers in the "List of Excluded Properties or Sellers" field.
14. Select all services provided by the broker in the "Services Provided by Broker" field.
15. Acknowledge the acknowledgment of liability limits in the "Liability Disclaimer" field.
16. Commit to the confidentiality and fair dealing agreement in the "Confidentiality and Fair Dealing Agreement" field.
17. Choose whether the agreement can be terminated early in the "Early Termination Allowed" field.
18. Finally, confirm that you understand and agree to the terms in the "I Understand and Agree to Terms" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Broker Name or Firm** (text, required): Enter the name of the real estate brokerage firm or individual broker.
* **Broker License Number** (text, required): Enter the brokerage's license number.
* **Broker Email Address** (email, required): Enter the email address where the broker can be contacted.
* **Broker Phone Number** (text, required): Enter the phone number where the broker can be contacted.
* **Buyer Name(s)** (text, required): Enter the full name of the buyer or buyers.
* **Buyer Email Address** (email, required): Enter the email address where the buyer can be contacted.
* **Buyer Phone Number** (text, required): Enter the phone number where the buyer can be contacted.
* **Agreement Date** (date, required): Select the effective date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Duration** (select_one, required): Select the duration of the agreement: Until property found, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, Other period, or No specific end date.
* **Compensation Type** (select_one, required): Choose how the broker will be compensated: Buyer agent commission from seller proceeds, Flat fee from buyer, Hourly rate, Percentage of purchase price, Combination method, or Other arrangement.
* **Commission Percentage or Flat Fee** (number, required): Enter the commission percentage or flat fee.
* **Source of Compensation** (select_one, required): Select the source of the compensation: Seller side commission split, Buyer pays broker directly, Split arrangement between buyer and seller, MLS listing information, or Other source.
* **Scope of Property Search** (text, required): Describe the scope of the property search, such as geographic area or property type.
* **Are Any Properties Excluded From Agreement?** (select_one, required): Select "Yes, certain properties excluded" if there are properties excluded from the agreement.
* **List of Excluded Properties or Sellers** (text, optional): If there are properties excluded, list them here.
* **Services Provided by Broker** (select_multiple, required): Select all services provided by the broker: Property search and recommendations, Property showings and viewings, Negotiation services, Inspection coordination, Financing advice, Document preparation, Closing coordination, Post-closing support.
* **Acknowledgment of Liability Limits** (select_one, required): Acknowledge the limits of liability.
* **Confidentiality and Fair Dealing Agreement** (select_one, required): Commit to the confidentiality and fair dealing agreement.
* **Early Termination Allowed** (select_one, required): Choose whether the agreement can be terminated early: Yes, by mutual agreement, Yes, with notice period, Only for cause, or No early termination.
* **I Understand and Agree to Terms** (select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand each section carefully before signing the agreement.
* If there are any questions or concerns, discuss them with the broker before signing.
* Review and understand the liability limits and confidentiality agreement sections carefully.
* Ensure that you have a clear understanding of the compensation structure and services provided.
* Keep a copy of the signed agreement for your records.
