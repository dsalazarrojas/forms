# Lawyer Conduct Complaint Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lawyer Conduct Complaint Form is used to report or investigate allegations of misconduct by lawyers. It's designed to gather essential information about the complaint, the lawyer involved, and the outcome of the process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter the client's name, which is the person filing the complaint.
2. Enter the lawyer's name against whom the complaint is made.
3. Provide a detailed description of the complaint or misconduct.
4. Specify the date of the incident.
5. Select the type of misconduct alleged (Lawyer Conduct Type).
6. Choose the case status (Active or Inactive).
7. Specify the type of complaint (Yes or No).
8. Confirm whether the complaint was submitted by a client (Yes or No).
9. Choose the level of lawyer conduct (High or Low).
10. Confirm whether a resolution was reached (Yes or No).
11. Select the follow-up status (Active or Inactive).
12. Enter any follow-up comments (if the follow-up status is Active).
13. Enter any assigned tool or action items (if relevant).
14. Enter the case manager's name and notes (if assigned).
15. Enter the complaint ID, form ID, and form version (for tracking).
16. Enter the date the complaint was submitted and completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client filing the complaint.
* **Lawyer Name**: Enter the name of the lawyer against whom the complaint is made.
* **Complaint Description**: Provide a detailed description of the complaint or misconduct.
* **Complaint Date**: Enter the date of the incident.
* **Lawyer Conduct Type**: Select the type of misconduct alleged (Yes or No).
* **Case Status**: Choose the current status of the case (Active or Inactive).
* **Complaint Type**: Specify whether this is an actual complaint (Yes or No).
* **Complaint Submitted By**: Confirm whether the complaint was submitted by a client (Yes or No).
* **Lawyer Conduct Level**: Choose the level of misconduct (High or Low).
* **Complaint Resolution**: Confirm whether a resolution was reached (Yes or No).
* **Follow-Up Status**: Select the follow-up status (Active or Inactive).
* **Follow-up Comments**: Enter any comments related to follow-up activities.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any assigned tool or action items (if relevant).
* **Case Manager**: Enter the case manager's name and any relevant notes (if assigned).
* **Complaint ID**: Enter the unique ID of the complaint for tracking.
* **Form ID**: Enter the unique ID of the form for tracking.
* **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form used.
* **Submitted Date**: Enter the date the complaint was submitted.
* **Completed Date**: Enter the date the complaint was completed.
* **Submitted By**: Confirm whether the complaint was submitted by a lawyer (Yes or No).
* **Assigned To**: Select whether the case is assigned to another person (Yes or No).
* **Submitted By Name**: Enter the name of the person who submitted the complaint.

Note: This guide assumes that the form fields will be filled out by someone familiar with the process and purpose of the form. If you're unsure or need further clarification, please contact the relevant authorities or personnel handling complaints.
