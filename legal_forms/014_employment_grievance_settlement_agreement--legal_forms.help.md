# Employment Grievance Settlement Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Employment Grievance Settlement Agreement form is a document used to record the details of an employment grievance, including the complainant's information, the resolution outcome, and the settlement terms.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all the necessary information to fill out the fields accurately.
2.  Begin by filling out the complainant's information, including their response to the question "Complainant" (ID 2).
3.  Select the relevant resolution outcome for the grievance (ID 3).
4.  Choose the manager's approval status for the resolution (ID 4).
5.  Enter the settlement amount, if applicable (ID 5).
6.  Select the payment terms for the settlement (ID 6).
7.  Fill out the complainant's contact information (ID 7).
8.  Enter the manager's contact information (ID 8).
9.  Select the employee's status (ID 9).
10.  Enter the termination date, if applicable (ID 10).
11.  Provide the reason for leave, if applicable (ID 11).
12.  Sign the form to confirm your agreement (ID 12 and ID 13).
13.  Enter the date and reason for any revisions made to the form (ID 15 and ID 16).
14.  Record any form revisions in the "Form Revision History" field (ID 17).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Complainant** (ID 2, select_one, **optional**): The person who filed the grievance. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the complainant has filed the grievance.
*   **Resolution Outcome** (ID 3, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose the outcome of the grievance, selecting one or more options that apply.
*   **Manager Approval** (ID 4, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the manager's approval status for the resolution.
*   **Settlement Amount** (ID 5, number, **optional**): Enter the settlement amount, if applicable.
*   **Payment Terms** (ID 6, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether payment terms are included in the settlement.
*   **Complainant Contact** (ID 7, text, **optional**): Enter the complainant's contact information, including their name, phone number, and address.
*   **Manager Contact** (ID 8, text, **optional**): Enter the manager's contact information, including their name, phone number, and address.
*   **Employee Status** (ID 9, select_one, **optional**): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the employee's status.
*   **Termination Date** (ID 10, date, **optional**): Enter the termination date, if applicable.
*   **Reason for Leave** (ID 11, text, **optional**): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of leave.
*   **Employee Signature** (ID 12, note, **optional**): Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
*   **Manager Signature** (ID 13, note, **optional**): Sign the form to confirm the manager's agreement.
*   **Date of Signature** (ID 14, date, **optional**): Enter the date you signed the form.
*   **Date of Revision** (ID 15, date, **optional**): Enter the date of the last revision made to the form.
*   **Revision Reason** (ID 16, text, **optional**): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of revision.
*   **Form Revision History** (ID 17, note, **optional**): Record any revisions made to the form, including the date, reason, and details of the change.
*   **Complaint Type** (ID 18, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the complaint type.
*   **Complaint Details 2** (ID 19, text, **optional**): Provide additional details about the complaint.
*   **Resolution Outcome 2** (ID 20, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose the outcome of the second complaint, selecting one or more options that apply.
*   **Manager Approval 2** (ID 21, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the manager's approval status for the resolution of the second complaint.
*   **Settlement Amount 2** (ID 22, number, **optional**): Enter the settlement amount, if applicable.
*   **Payment Terms 2** (ID 23, select_one, **optional**): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether payment terms are included in the settlement of the second complaint.
*   **Complainant Contact 2** (ID 24, text, **optional**): Enter the complainant's contact information, including their name, phone number, and address.
*   **Employee Status 2** (ID 25, select_one, **optional**): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the employee's status.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
