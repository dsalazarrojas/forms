# Arbitration Services Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and details about your dispute so that we can provide you with tailored arbitration services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Describe the type of dispute you are facing and provide a brief description of the issue.
3. Indicate your prior experience with arbitration and preferred method of contact.
4. Select your preferred timeline for resolution and services of interest.
5. Provide any additional information or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`inquirer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Company or Organization Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are representing a company or organization, enter their name here.
* **Email Address** (`inquirer_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`inquirer_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Type of Dispute** (`dispute_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of dispute you are facing from the list:
	+ Contract dispute
	+ Commercial dispute
	+ Employment dispute
	+ Construction dispute
	+ Intellectual property
	+ Insurance claim
	+ Other
* **Other Party Name** (`other_party_name`, text, optional): If the other party is a company or organization, enter their name here.
* **Brief Description of Dispute** (`dispute_description`, text, required): Describe the dispute in a few sentences.
* **Estimated Claim Amount** (`dispute_amount`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the approximate value of the dispute.
* **Prior Arbitration Experience** (`arbitration_experience`, select_one, required): Select your experience with arbitration:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Not Sure
* **Preferred Method of Contact** (`callback_preference`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ Text message
	+ Video call
* **Best Time to Contact You** (`best_time_contact`, text, optional): If you have a preferred time to contact you, enter it here.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (`additional_information`, text, optional): If you have any additional information or questions, enter them here.
