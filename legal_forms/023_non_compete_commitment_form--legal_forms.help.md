# Non Compete Commitment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document an employee's commitment to not engage in any competitive activities or share confidential information with third parties when leaving or separating from the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `employee_id` field with your employee ID or the number assigned to you by the company.
2. Date of Non-Compete field should be populated with the current date or the date of your departure.
3. If you are completing the form on behalf of an employee, fill in the `company_representative_name`, `company_representative_title`, `company_representative_email`, and `company_representative_phone` fields with the relevant information.
4. Select the `employee_non_compete_agreement` field to indicate whether you agree or disagree with the non-compete commitment.
5. Sign the `signature` field with your name and date.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required and optional fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **employee_id (Number)**: This field is used to identify you as an employee. Please fill in your employee ID or the number assigned to you by the company.
* **date_of_non_compete (Date)**: This field should be populated with the current date or the date of your departure.
* **signature (Note)**: Please sign this field with your name and date to indicate your commitment.
* **employee_non_compete_agreement (Select Multiple)**: Select 'Yes' to agree to the non-compete commitment, or 'No' to decline.
* **company_representative_name (Text)**: If completing the form on behalf of an employee, fill in the name of the company representative.
* **company_representative_title (Text)**: If completing the form on behalf of an employee, fill in the title of the company representative.
* **company_representative_email (Email)**: If completing the form on behalf of an employee, fill in the email address of the company representative.
* **company_representative_phone (Text)**: If completing the form on behalf of an employee, fill in the phone number of the company representative.
