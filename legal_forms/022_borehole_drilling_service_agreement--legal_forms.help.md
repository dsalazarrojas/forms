# Borehole Drilling Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients for borehole drilling services, including client contact details, project scope, commercial terms, and authorization for work commencement. It's a crucial document that outlines the agreement between the client and the service provider, ensuring both parties are on the same page regarding the scope of work, payment terms, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Provide your client's personal or company name in the **Client Full Name or Company** field (Field 2).
2. Enter the service address where the borehole drilling will take place in the **Service Address / Site Location** field (Field 3).
3. Provide your client's email address in the **Client Email** field (Field 4).
4. Enter your client's phone number in the **Client Phone** field (Field 5).
5. Select the type of service required from the list in the **Type of Service Required** field (Field 7).
6. Enter the estimated drilling depth in meters in the **Estimated Drilling Depth - meters** field (Field 8).
7. Select a proposed start date for the project in the **Proposed Start Date** field (Field 9).
8. Agree to the commercial terms by entering the total quoted amount in the **Total Quoted Amount** field (Field 11).
9. Select the payment terms from the available options (Field 12).
10. Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy by typing "I AGREE" in the **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** field (Field 13).
11. Finally, sign the digital signature with your full name in the **Client Digital Signature - Type Full Name** field (Field 15).
12. Date the agreement by selecting the **Date of Agreement** field (Field 16).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name or Company** (Field 2, Required): Enter your client's personal or company name.
* **Service Address / Site Location** (Field 3, Required): Enter the address where the borehole drilling will take place.
* **Client Email** (Field 4, Required): Enter your client's email address for communication purposes.
* **Client Phone** (Field 5, Required): Enter your client's phone number for contact.
* **Type of Service Required** (Field 7, Required): Select the type of service needed from the available options.
* **Estimated Drilling Depth - meters** (Field 8, Required): Enter the estimated depth of the borehole drilling in meters.
* **Proposed Start Date** (Field 9, Required): Select a proposed start date for the project.
* **Total Quoted Amount** (Field 11, Required): Enter the total amount quoted for the service.
* **Payment Terms** (Field 12, Required): Select the payment terms from the available options.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment** (Field 13, Required): Confirm your understanding of the cancellation policy by typing "I AGREE".
* **Client Digital Signature - Type Full Name** (Field 15, Required): Sign the agreement with your full name.
* **Date of Agreement** (Field 16, Required): Date the agreement by selecting the date.

Note: Make sure to review the form carefully and enter accurate information to ensure a smooth and successful project execution.
