# Legal Offences Assessment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Offences Assessment Survey form is designed to collect data on legal cases, allowing users to track and assess various aspects of these cases. This form will help users evaluate and understand legal matters more effectively.


## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by reviewing the form pages and identifying the specific areas of the form that you need to fill out.
2.  Select the relevant options for each field, following the instructions provided below for each field.
3.  Ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
4.  Review your responses for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Offence Type** (`offense_type`, select_one, required: false): This field asks you to identify the type of legal offense involved. Please select the most accurate option from the provided choices.
*   **Offence Date** (`offence_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date related to the legal offense.
*   **Offence Location** (`offence_location`, text, required: false): Provide the location where the legal offense took place.
*   **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required: false): Enter the case number for the legal case.
*   **Police Officer** (`police_officer`, text, required: false): Identify the police officer associated with the case.
*   **Court Case Number** (`court_case_number`, text, required: false): Enter the court case number.
*   **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the current status of the case by selecting from the provided options.
*   **Case Outcome** (`case_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the outcome of the legal case.
*   **Case Notes** (`case_notes`, text, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about the case.
*   **Case Counsel** (`case_counsel`, text, required: false): Identify the counsel or lawyer associated with the case.
*   **Case Docket** (`case_docket`, text, required: false): Enter the docket number of the case.
*   **Court Name** (`court_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the court.
*   **Court Level** (`court_level`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the level of the court by selecting from the provided options.
*   **Jurisdiction** (`jurisdiction`, text, required: false): Specify the jurisdiction related to the case.
*   **Legal Representative** (`legal_representative`, text, required: false): Identify the legal representative associated with the case.
*   **Case Jurisdiction** (`case_jurisdiction`, text, required: false): Enter the jurisdiction related to the case.
*   **Case Type** (`case_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the types of legal cases related to the case.
*   **Case Outcome** (`case_outcome`, text, required: false): Describe the outcome of the case.
*   **Court Docket Number** (`court_docket_number`, text, required: false): Enter the court docket number.
*   **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, required: false): Indicate the status of the case by selecting from the provided options.
*   **Case Officer** (`case_officer`, text, required: false): Identify the case officer.
*   **Case Date** (`case_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the case.
*   **Case Number 2** (`case_number2`, text, required: false): Enter the case number 2.
*   **Case Number 3** (`case_number3`, text, required: false): Enter the case number 3.
*   **Case Number 4** (`case_number4`, text, required: false): Enter the case number 4.


## Tips
*   Please ensure that you complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review your responses carefully before submitting the form.
*   If you have any doubts or questions about the form, refer to the form's purpose and instructions provided above.
