# Healthcare Operations Privacy Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients for the purpose of establishing a privacy agreement in healthcare operations. Please read the following instructions carefully to ensure that you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please select the "Patient Name" field to enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
- Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field, following the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
- Select one of the provided options in the "Contact Relationship" field to indicate the relationship between the patient and the healthcare provider (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Guardian, Caregiver, or Other).
- Enter any relevant privacy concerns or questions in the "Privacy Concerns" field.
- Select your healthcare provider(s) from the options in the "Provider Info" field, as applicable.
- In the "Agreement Date" field, enter the date on which you sign this agreement.
- Sign the "Signature Date" field on the date of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_patient_information`, text, optional): Please provide a brief description of the patient.
* **Patient Name** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_patient_name`, text, required): Enter the patient's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the patient's date of birth following the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Contact Info** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_contact_info`, text, required): Enter the patient's contact information, including phone number and email.
* **Contact Relationship** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to indicate the relationship between the patient and the healthcare provider (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Guardian, Caregiver, or Other).
* **Privacy Concerns** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_privacy_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any relevant privacy concerns or questions.
* **Provider Info** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_provider_info`, select_multiple, required): Select the applicable healthcare providers for the patient.
* **Agreement Date** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date on which you sign this agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`healthcare_operations_privacy_agreement_form_sign_date`, date, required): Sign on the date of signing.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any information, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before signing to ensure that all information is correct.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
