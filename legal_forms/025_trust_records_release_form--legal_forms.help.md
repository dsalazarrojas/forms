# trust_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the release of trust records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `release_form` field with the relevant details about the trust records to be released.
2. Indicate whether `trust_related_information` is required or not.
3. Select the relevant `trust_info_release` option (Yes or No) to confirm the release.
4. Choose the `legal_professionals` involved in the release.
5. Enter the `consent_date` and `consent_time` for the release.
6. Add any additional notes about the trust records in the `trust_info_note` field.
7. Enter the email address of the recipient for the release in the `trust_release_email` field.
8. Provide the phone number of the recipient for the release in the `trust_release_phone` field.
9. Select the assigned tool for the release from the options (e.g., chatjimmy).
10. Enter the unique form ID in the `form_id` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **release_form** (release_form, text, required/optional): Enter the details about the trust records to be released.
* **trust_related_information** (trust_related_information, text, optional): This field is used to provide additional information related to the trust records.
* **trust_info_release** (trust_info_release, select_multiple, optional): Select Yes or No to confirm the release.
* **legal_professionals** (legal_professionals, select_one, optional): Choose the legal professional involved in the release.
* **consent_date** (consent_date, date, optional): Enter the date of consent for the release.
* **consent_time** (consent_time, time, optional): Enter the time of consent for the release.
* **trust_info_note** (trust_info_note, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the trust records.
* **trust_release_email** (trust_release_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the recipient for the release.
* **trust_release_phone** (trust_release_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the recipient for the release.
* **assigned_tool** (assigned_tool, select_one, optional): Select the assigned tool for the release (e.g., chatjimmy).
* **form_id** (form_id, text, optional): Enter the unique form ID for the release.
