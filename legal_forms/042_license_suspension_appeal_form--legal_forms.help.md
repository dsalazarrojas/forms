# License Suspension Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The License Suspension Appeal Form is a form designed to help individuals who have had their license suspended to submit an appeal for review. This form is intended to gather information about the reason for the suspension, any relevant details, and supporting documentation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the reason for your license suspension and select the correct option from the drop-down menu.
2. Enter the date on which your license was suspended.
3. Provide a clear and detailed explanation of what happened and why you are appealing the suspension.
4. If applicable, attach any supporting documents that may help support your appeal.
5. Sign and date the form with your name, indicating that you are the individual submitting the appeal.
6. If the reviewer has comments, sign and date the form again, indicating that you have received and reviewed their comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **License Suspension Appeal Form**: This is the title of the form and the main topic of the appeal.
* **reason_for_suspension**: Select the reason for your license suspension from the drop-down menu. This helps ensure that your appeal is relevant and focused.
	+ License Suspended: The license was suspended due to a temporary reason, such as a pending investigation.
	+ License Cancelled: The license was cancelled due to a permanent reason, such as non-payment of fees.
	+ License Restricted: The license was restricted due to a specific condition or limitation.
	+ License Terminated: The license was terminated due to a serious infraction or violation.
	+ License Denied: The license was never granted in the first place.
* **date_of_suspension**: Enter the date on which your license was suspended.
* **explain_the_suspension**: Provide a clear and detailed explanation of what happened and why you are appealing the suspension. Be concise and to the point.
* **supporting_documents**: If applicable, attach any documents that may support your appeal. These can be scans or photos of documents, such as receipts or contracts.
* **signature_of_appealer**: Sign and date this field with your name, indicating that you are the individual submitting the appeal.
* **reviewer_signature**: If the reviewer has comments, sign and date this field with your name, indicating that you have received and reviewed their comments.
* **comments_of_reviewer**: If the reviewer has comments, review and acknowledge them in this field.
