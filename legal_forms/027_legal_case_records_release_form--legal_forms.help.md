# legal_case_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The legal_case_records_release_form is used to collect and maintain accurate records of legal cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the case number, if known.
2. Select the current status of the case (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
3. Choose the type of case (Criminal, Civil, or Family).
4. Enter the date the case was opened, if known.
5. Enter the date the case was closed, if known.
6. Enter the date the case was received, if known.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the case attorney, if applicable.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the case court, if applicable.
9. Enter the docket number, if known.
10. Confirm who authorized this release, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Number (1)** (text, optional): Enter the case number, if known. This helps identify the case within the system.
* **Case Status (2)** (select_one, optional): Select the current status of the case (Active, Inactive, or Closed).
* **Case Type (3)** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of case (Criminal, Civil, or Family).
* **Case Level (4)** (number, optional): Enter the level of the case. This field is currently not populated with options.
* **Case Date Opened (5)** (date, optional): Enter the date the case was opened, if known.
* **Case Date Closed (6)** (date, optional): Enter the date the case was closed, if known.
* **Case Date Received (7)** (date, optional): Enter the date the case was received, if known.
* **Case Attorney (8)** (text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the case attorney, if applicable.
* **Case Court (9)** (text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the case court, if applicable.
* **Case Docket Number (10)** (text, optional): Enter the docket number, if known.
* **Authorized By (11)** (text, optional): Confirm who authorized this release, if required.

## Tips

* Ensure that each case is only entered once into the system.
* If a case status or type is unclear, consult with your supervisor or relevant documentation for guidance.
* Enter all required information, even if it's not available, to ensure accuracy and consistency.
* Review and edit your entry for accuracy before submitting.
