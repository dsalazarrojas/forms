# Gift Letter For Mortgage - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of assets or funds between a homebuyer and a donor in a real estate transaction. It helps ensure transparency and accuracy in recording the gift.

## How To Complete This Form
Please complete the form by providing the required information for both the homebuyer and the donor. Fill out the form in sections, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homebuyer Name** (`homebuyer_name`, text, required): The name of the homebuyer, as recorded on the public records.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): The name of the individual or entity providing the gift.
* **Date of Donation** (`date_of_donation`, date, required): The date the gift was made.
* **Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, number, required): The amount of the gift.
* **Purpose of Gift** (`purpose_of_gift`, text, optional): A brief description of the purpose of the gift.
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, text, optional): The address of the donor.
* **Homebuyer Address** (`homebuyer_address`, text, optional): The address of the homebuyer.
* **Date of Gift** (`date_of_gift`, date, required): The date the gift was received.
* **Relationship to Homebuyer** (`relationship_to_homebuyer`, select_one, required): The relationship between the homebuyer and the donor (e.g. Mother, Father, Spouse, etc.).
* **Donor Income** (`donor_income`, select_one, required): The donor's annual income range.
* **Gift Funds** (`gift_funds`, select_multiple, optional): The type of gift made (e.g. Cash, Real estate, etc.).
* **Donor Signature** (`donor_signature`, text, required): The donor's signature, indicating their agreement to the gift.
* **Homebuyer Signature** (`homebuyer_signature`, text, required): The homebuyer's signature, indicating their acceptance of the gift.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): The date both parties signed the form.
* **Donor Acknowledgement** (`donor_acknowledgement`, text, optional): A statement acknowledging the gift.
* **Homebuyer Acknowledgement** (`homebuyer_acknowledgement`, text, optional): A statement acknowledging the gift.
* **Acknowledgement to Courts** (`acknowledgement_to_courts`, text, optional): A statement acknowledging the gift to courts or authorities.
* **Donor Acknowledgement 2** (`donor_acknowledgement_2`, text, optional): A second statement from the donor acknowledging the gift.
* **Homebuyer Acknowledgement 2** (`homebuyer_acknowledgement_2`, text, optional): A second statement from the homebuyer acknowledging the gift.
* **Other Terms** (`other_terms`, text, optional): Any additional terms or conditions related to the gift.
* **Other Terms Acknowledgement** (`other_terms_acknowledgement`, text, optional): A statement acknowledging any additional terms.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Any additional terms or conditions related to the gift.
* **Terms and Conditions Acknowledgement** (`terms_and_conditions_acknowledgement`, text, optional): A statement acknowledging any additional terms.
* **Date of Transfer** (`date_of_transfer`, date, optional): The date of the gift transfer.
* **Other Donors** (`other_donors`, text, optional): A list of any other donors contributing to the gift.

Note: Ensure that the form is filled out accurately and truthfully by both parties.
