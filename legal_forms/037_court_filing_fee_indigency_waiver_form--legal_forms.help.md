# Court Filing Fee Indigency Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to request a waiver of court filing fees for indigent individuals who are unable to pay the required fees due to a financial hardship.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the designated fields.
2.  Enter the case number of the case for which you are requesting the fee waiver.
3.  Select the type of fee you are requesting to waive (indigent, financial hardship, or other).
4.  Enter the date and time of the document you are submitting to support your request.
5.  Select the type of document (affidavit, motion, or other).
6.  Enter the document number (if applicable).
7.  Provide a detailed explanation of your financial hardship and why you are unable to pay the required fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Financial Hardship Requests** (<id: 1>): Enter a brief description of your financial situation and why you are unable to pay the required fees.
*   **Name** (<id: 2>): Enter your full name as it appears on your court documents.
*   **Phone** (<id: 3>): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Email** (<id: 4>): Enter your email address.
*   **Case Number** (<id: 5>): Enter the case number of the case for which you are requesting the fee waiver.
*   **Fee Type** (<id: 6>): Select the type of fee you are requesting to waive (indigent, financial hardship, or other).
*   **Fees Paid** (<id: 7>): Enter any fees you have already paid.
*   **Explanation** (<id: 8>): Provide a detailed explanation of your financial hardship and why you are unable to pay the required fees.
*   **Document Date** (<id: 9>): Enter the date of the document you are submitting to support your request.
*   **Document Time** (<id: 10>): Enter the time of the document you are submitting to support your request.
*   **Document Type** (<id: 11>): Select the type of document (affidavit, motion, or other).
*   **Document** (<id: 12>): Enter a brief description of the document.
*   **Document Number** (<id: 13>): Enter the document number (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information to support your request.
*   Be honest and transparent about your financial situation.
*   Attach supporting documents as necessary to demonstrate your financial hardship.
*   Follow the instructions carefully to ensure your form is complete and accurate.
