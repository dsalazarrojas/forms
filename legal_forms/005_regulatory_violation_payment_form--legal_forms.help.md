# Regulatory Violation Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regulatory Violation Payment Form is used to collect information about regulatory violations and related payments. It is intended for use by authorized personnel to track and manage violations that have already been identified and resolved or are in the process of being resolved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the "user_name" and "email" fields, respectively.
2. Select the payment method used to make the payment (Cash, Check, or Credit Card) in the "payment_method" field.
3. Enter the amount paid in the "amount" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the violation in the "description_of_violation" field.
5. Identify the regulatory body involved in the violation in the "regulatory_body" field.
6. Enter the date the payment was made in the "payment_date" field.
7. Select the current payment status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid) in the "payment_status" field.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about the payment in the "notes" field.
9. Finally, enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "submitted_by" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_name**: Enter your name as it should be listed as the submitter of the form.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted about this payment.
* **payment_method**: Select how the payment was made (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
* **amount**: Enter the exact amount paid to resolve or mitigate the violation.
* **description_of_violation**: Provide a brief description of the regulatory violation that was resolved or is being resolved.
* **regulatory_body**: Identify the regulatory body involved in the violation.
* **payment_date**: Enter the date the payment was made.
* **payment_status**: Select the current status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about the payment.
* **submitted_by**: Enter your name as the person submitting the form.
 
## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and complete to avoid any processing errors.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the relevant authorities or seek further clarification.
* If the form is incomplete or duplicate, it will not be processed.
