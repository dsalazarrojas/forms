# Art Licensing Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a licensing agreement between a licensor (artist or rights holder) and a licensee (individual or organization) for the use of a specific artwork. The form captures essential information about the licensing scope, duration, and payment terms to ensure both parties are clear on their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Licensor name**, which is the name of the artist or rights holder.
2. Enter the **Licensee name**, which is the name of the individual or organization licensing the artwork.
3. Enter the **Artwork title**, which is the title of the licensed artwork.
4. Select the **License scope**, which defines the usage rights granted (Commercial, Editorial, Personal, Nonexclusive, or Exclusive).
5. Enter the **Territory**, which specifies the geographical scope of the license.
6. Enter the **Duration of license**, including start and end dates.
7. Enter the **License fee**, which is the agreed-upon fee for the license.
8. Specify the **Payment terms**, including schedule and method.
9. Optionally, enter any **Usage restrictions**, which outlines any limitations or prohibited uses.
10. Finally, provide both **Signatures** and dates for the licensor and licensee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Licensor name**: Enter the name of the artist or rights holder.
* **Licensee name**: Enter the name of the individual or organization licensing the artwork.
* **Artwork title**: Enter the title of the licensed artwork.
* **License scope**: Select one of the following options:
	+ **Commercial**: Granting usage for commercial purposes.
	+ **Editorial**: Granting usage for editorial or publishing purposes.
	+ **Personal**: Granting usage for personal, non-commercial purposes.
	+ **Nonexclusive**: Granting non-exclusive usage rights.
	+ **Exclusive**: Granting exclusive usage rights.
* **Territory**: Enter the geographical scope of the license.
* **Duration of license**: Enter start and end dates for the license.
* **License fee**: Enter the agreed-upon fee for the license.
* **Payment terms**: Specify the payment schedule and method.
* **Usage restrictions**: Optionally, enter any limitations or prohibited uses.
* **Signatures**: Enter both signatures and dates for the licensor and licensee.
