# Trusts Legal Knowledge Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trusts Legal Knowledge Survey is a tool to gather information from legal professionals about their knowledge and experience with trusts. The survey aims to identify areas where users may need further education or training to improve their understanding of trusts and provide resources to bridge that knowledge gap.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply answer the questions in the following pages. Each question has a specific purpose, and answering them accurately will help us understand the current state of trusts in the legal community.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1

* **What is the primary function of trusts**: Answer this question by providing a brief description of the main purpose of trusts.
* **What are the different types of trusts** (Select One): This question asks you to identify the type of trusts you are most familiar with. Please select the option that best describes your primary knowledge area.
	+ **Trusts**: This option is likely the best fit if you are most familiar with traditional trusts.
	+ **Estates**: This option may be best if you are more familiar with estates and their administration.
	+ **Fiduciaries**: This option may be suitable if you are familiar with fiduciaries and their role in trusts.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options fit your experience, please select "Other" and describe your answer in the question below.

* **How do you currently use trusts** (Required): This question aims to understand how you currently utilize trusts in your practice. Please provide a brief description of your current uses.
* **Which of the following knowledge areas do you wish to focus on first** (Select Multiple): This question helps us understand where you would like to focus your educational efforts. Please select all the knowledge areas that are relevant to your current needs.
	+ **Property Law**: This option is suitable if you want to improve your knowledge on property-related trusts.
	+ **Wills and Estates**: This option may be the best fit if you want to focus on trusts related to wills and estates.
	+ **Trust and Estate Planning**: This option is likely the most relevant if you want to improve your knowledge on trust and estate planning.
	+ **Corporate Law**: This option may be suitable if you want to focus on corporate law-related trusts.
* **Which of the following knowledge areas do you wish to focus on second** (Select Multiple): This question is similar to the one above, but for the second area you want to focus on.
* **What type of training do you think would be most beneficial for you or your firm** (Select One): This question helps us understand what type of education you would like to receive. Please select the option that best fits your preferences.
	+ **Classroom instruction**: This option is likely suitable if you prefer hands-on, in-person training.
	+ **Online training**: This option may be the best fit if you prefer self-paced, online education.
	+ **Workshops**: This option may be suitable if you want a combination of both in-person and online education.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options fit your preferences, select "Other" and describe your answer in the question below.
* **How often do you receive training or support on trusts** (Required): This question aims to understand how frequently you receive education or support on trusts. Please provide a brief description of your answer.
* **Do you provide any training or educational resources for trusts?** (Select One): This question helps us understand if you are willing to share your knowledge with others. Please select the option that best describes your answer.
	+ **True**: If you do provide resources, please describe them in the question below.
	+ **False**: If you do not provide resources, please continue to the next question.
* **Do you plan to use our resources?** (Select One): This question is similar to the one above, but for our resources specifically.
	+ **True**: If you plan to use our resources, please describe them in the question below.
	+ **False**: If you do not plan to use our resources, please continue to the next question.
* **What types of resources are you interested in?** (Select Multiple): This question helps us understand what type of resources you are interested in using. Please select all the options that best fit your preferences.
	+ **Videos**: This option is likely suitable if you want video-based resources.
	+ **Webinars**: This option may be the best fit if you want online presentations or lectures.
	+ **PDF guides**: This option is likely the most relevant if you want downloadable guides.
	+ **Other**: If none of the above options fit your preferences, please select "Other" and describe your answer in the question below.
