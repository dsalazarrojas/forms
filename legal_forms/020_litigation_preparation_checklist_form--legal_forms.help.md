# Litigation Preparation Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Litigation Preparation Checklist Form is a tool designed to aid in the preparation and handling of cases that are or have been in the process of litigation. It helps in identifying and tracking various phases and aspects of the preparation process, ensuring that no critical details are overlooked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the case details and indicate the status of the case by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the options provided.
2. Provide a list of parties involved in the case, including their names and titles.
3. Choose whether or not the discovery phase has been completed.
4. Select the court procedures that have been followed or are planned to be followed.
5. Indicate the status of document review and preparation.
6. Select whether or not witness preparation and statements have been completed.
7. Choose the exhibit management methods used.
8. Indicate the status of trial preparation.
9. Provide any additional information related to post-trial activities.
10. Add any miscellaneous notes or comments related to the case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Case Details** (`case_details`, Select Multiple, required: false): This field asks for a brief overview of the case, including its status (e.g., ongoing, settled, or dismissed).
* **Parties Involved** (`parties_involved`, Text, required: false): List the names and titles of all parties involved in the case.
* **Discovery Phase** (`discovery_phase`, Select One, required: false): Indicate whether or not the discovery phase has been completed.
* **Court Procedures** (`court_procedures`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select the court procedures that have been followed or are planned to be followed.
* **Document Review** (`document_review`, Text, required: false): Provide a list of documents reviewed and prepared for the case.
* **Witness Preparation** (`witness_preparation`, Select Multiple, required: false): Select whether or not witness preparation and statements have been completed.
* **Witness Statements** (`witness_statements`, Text, required: false): List the witness statements prepared.
* **Exhibit Management** (`exhibit_management`, Select Multiple, required: false): Choose the exhibit management methods used.
* **Trial Preparation** (`trial_preparation`, Select One, required: false): Indicate the status of trial preparation.
* **Post Trial** (`post_trial`, Text, required: false): Provide any information related to post-trial activities.
* **Miscellaneous** (`miscellaneous`, Text, required: false): Add any miscellaneous notes or comments related to the case.
