# Fair Payment Terms Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fair Payment Terms Agreement form is a document that outlines the payment terms and conditions between two parties involved in a business transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the requirements and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Start by filling out the client information section, which includes the client's name, email, and phone number.
3. Move on to the project timeline section, where you can provide an overview of the project's start and end dates.
4. In the payment terms section, describe the payment schedule, including the amount, frequency, and any other relevant conditions.
5. Specify the payment methods that will be used for the transaction.
6. Enter the payment details, such as the payment amount and any other requirements.
7. Finally, sign the client signature section to confirm that you have read and agree to the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`client_information`, text, optional): Enter the client's name, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Project Timeline (Project Timeline)** (`project_timeline`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of the project's start and end dates.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Describe the payment schedule, including the amount, frequency, and any other conditions.
* **Payment Schedule (Payment Schedule)** (`payment_schedule`, text, optional): Specify the payment schedule, including the amount, frequency, and any other requirements.
* **Payment Methods (Payment Methods)** (`payment_methods`, text, optional): Enter the payment methods that will be used for the transaction.
* **Payment Details (Payment Details)** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter the payment details, such as the payment amount and any other requirements.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature)** (`client_signature`, text, optional): Sign the client signature section to confirm that you have read and agree to the payment terms.
* **Date (Date)** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Name (Name)** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before signing the client signature section.
* This form is a legally binding contract, so please be sure to understand the implications of signing this agreement.
