# Prison Injury Compensation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for individuals who have been injured while in prison to document their incident and claim compensation. The form will ask for details about the incident, including date, time, location, and description. Additionally, it will ask for the claimant's personal information, such as name, contact number, and address. The form is meant to be completed by the claimant, but it can also be used by legal representatives or prison staff to report incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read through the form carefully to understand what information is required.
2.  Fill in the details of the incident you wish to report, including date, time, location, and description.
3.  Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, contact number, and address.
4.  Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Reporting (id: 1)**: Please describe the incident that resulted in your injury in as much detail as possible.
* **Supporting Documentation (id: 2)**: If you have any documents or photos that can support your claim, such as medical records or witness statements, attach them here.
* **Incident Location (id: 3)**: Where did the incident occur? Please enter the location of the incident, including the unit/building/block number, cell number, etc.
* **Incident Date (id: 4)**: What date did the incident occur? Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Incident Time (id: 5)**: What time did the incident occur? Please use the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM.
* **Incident Description (id: 6)**: Please describe the injury you sustained during the incident in as much detail as possible.
* **Claimant Name (id: 7)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Claimant Date of Birth (id: 8)**: Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Claimant Contact Number (id: 9)**: Please enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Claimant Address (id: 10)**: Please enter your address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.

## Tips

*  Make sure to fill in as much detail as possible to ensure your claim is processed correctly.
*  If you need help with this form, contact your legal representative or prison staff.
