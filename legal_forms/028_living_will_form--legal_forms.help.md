# Living Will Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create a living will, a legal document that outlines your wishes and preferences for end-of-life care. It's a private and personal document that ensures your wishes are respected if you become incapacitated or pass away.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal information, such as your name, address, and contact details.
2. Choose how you want to distribute your assets (if applicable) and who will be your beneficiaries.
3. Sign and date the form to make it legally binding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as you prefer it to be used.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as you prefer it to be used.
- **Address**: Enter your home address where you currently reside.
- **City**: Enter the city where you currently reside.
- **State**: Enter the state where you currently reside.
- **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
- **What type of assets do you have?**: Select the types of assets you have, such as Checking, Savings, Stocks, Bonds, Mutual Funds, Real Estate, or Retirement Accounts.
- **Beneficiary Name**: If you have designated beneficiaries, enter their names.
- **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the beneficiaries.
- **Gift Amount**: If applicable, specify the amount you wish to distribute to each beneficiary.
- **Gift Description**: If applicable, describe the gift you wish to leave for each beneficiary.
- **Asset Location**: If applicable, specify where the assets are located.
- **Distribution Method**: Choose the method(s) by which you want to distribute your assets, such as Direct Deposit, Joint Bank Account, or Trust.
- **Beneficiaries**: Choose who will receive your assets, such as Spouse, Children, Parents, Siblings, or Other.
- **Notes**: Add any additional information or comments you wish to include.
- **Date of Signature**: Date and sign the form to make it legally binding.
- **Signature**: Sign the form with your signature.
- **Date of Death**: If applicable, specify the date of death.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **E-Mail**: Enter your email address.
- **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1.
- **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2.
- **City 2**: Enter the city of your secondary address.
- **State 2**: Enter the state of your secondary address.
- **Zip 2**: Enter the zip code of your secondary address.

Note: Ensure that the form is filled out carefully and accurately, as it will be used for the purposes of the living will. If you need clarification on any of the fields, please consult with an attorney or relevant authorities.
