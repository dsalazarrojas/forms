# Website Agreement Confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm that a user understands and agrees to the website's terms and conditions. It also collects additional details, such as email and phone numbers, for future correspondence.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your response to the website agreement confirmation in the "Confirm Agreement" field. You can choose "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
2. If you confirm agreement, provide the date in the "Date" field. This is optional.
3. If you confirm agreement, provide the time in the "Time" field. This is optional.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is optional.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional.
6. Provide any additional agreement terms or notes in the "Additional Agreement Terms" field. This is optional.
7. Confirm if you have signed the website agreement in the "Website Agreement Confirmation Signed" field. You can select multiple options from the dropdown list.
8. Enter the date and time when you signed the website agreement in the "Website Agreement Confirmation Date" and "Website Agreement Confirmation Time" fields. These are optional.
9. Enter your email address and phone number for the website in the "Website Agreement Confirmation Email" and "Website Agreement Confirmation Phone" fields. These are optional.
10. Provide any notes about the website agreement in the "Website Agreement Confirmation Notes" field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Website Agreement Confirmation** (`website_agreement_confirmation`, text, required): Enter your response to the website agreement confirmation.
* **Confirm Agreement** (`confirm_agreement`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **Confirmed Agreement Signed** (`confirm_agreement_signed`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **Date** (`confirm_agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you signed the website agreement. This is in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`confirm_agreement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you signed the website agreement. This is in the format of HH:MM.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Agreement Terms** (`additional_agreement_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or notes about the agreement.
* **Website Agreement Confirmation Signed** (`website_agreement_confirmation_signed`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **Website Agreement Confirmation Date** (`website_agreement_confirmation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you signed the website agreement. This is in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Website Agreement Confirmation Time** (`website_agreement_confirmation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time when you signed the website agreement. This is in the format of HH:MM.
* **Website Agreement Confirmation Email** (`website_agreement_confirmation_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the website.
* **Website Agreement Confirmation Phone** (`website_agreement_confirmation_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the website.
* **Website Agreement Confirmation Notes** (`website_agreement_confirmation_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes about the website agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields that are marked as required.
- You can leave optional fields blank if not applicable.
- Use the format YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM for dates and times, respectively.
- Use "Yes" or "No" for dropdown lists, and enter the exact text as presented.
- Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
