# statuary_declaration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Statuary Declaration Form is designed to gather information from individuals for the purpose of making a statutory declaration. This form is used to record and document specific events or situations that require legal or official recognition. Completing this form accurately and thoroughly will aid in the processing and verification of the declaration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your personal details on the "Personal Details" page.
2. Next, describe the declaration in the "Declaration" page.
3. Indicate whether you have any witnesses for the declaration and list them in the "Witnesses" page.
4. Finally, sign and date the "Signature" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (1, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information to identify the declarant.
* **Declaration** (2, text, optional): Provide a clear and concise description of the declaration, including the date and circumstances of the event.
* **Witnesses** (3, select multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have witnesses for the declaration, and list their names and contact information in the space provided.
* **Signature** (4, text, optional): Sign and date the declaration to verify the information provided.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any part of the form, do not hesitate to ask for assistance.
* The information entered in this form is for official purposes and will be treated confidentially.
