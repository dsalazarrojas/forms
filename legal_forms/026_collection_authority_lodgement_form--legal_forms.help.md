# Collection Authority Lodgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Collection Authority Lodgement Form is a standardised form used by creditors to lodge authorities related to debt collection activities. It requires accurate and complete information to ensure consistency and accuracy in data collection.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before completing it.
2. Fill in the required information as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
3. Use the provided field explanations to guide you through the required details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Creditor name** (id: 2, required): Enter the name of the party lodging the authority.
* **Debtor name** (id: 3, required): Enter the name of the debtor.
* **Debt amount** (id: 4, required): Enter the currency value of the debt.
* **Account reference** (id: 5, optional): If applicable, enter the invoice or account number related to the authority.
* **Lodgement date** (id: 6, optional): Enter the date of the lodgement in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Supporting documents** (id: 7, optional): List any attachments provided as evidence or supporting documents.
* **Legal representation** (id: 8, optional): Enter the name of the legal representative if applicable.
* **Contact email** (id: 9, required): Enter the email address for legal correspondence.
* **Additional notes** (id: 10, optional): Enter any other relevant information to support the lodgement.

## Tips
- Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- Ensure all required fields are filled in correctly.
- Use the provided hints to guide you through the required details.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
