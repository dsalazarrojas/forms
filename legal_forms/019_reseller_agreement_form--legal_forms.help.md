# Reseller Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to create a legally binding agreement between the reseller and the product owner, outlining the terms and conditions of the reselling business.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Title in the first three fields. These fields are required.
2. Enter your Company name and Address 1 and Address 2 in the next three fields. These fields are optional.
3. Provide your Email and Phone number in the next two fields. These fields are required.
4. Enter the name of the Product Owner.
5. Enter your Reseller name.
6. Select the Agreement Term and Agreement Date for the agreement.
7. Sign the Signature 1 field with your signature.
8. Have the Product Owner sign the Signature 2 field.
9. Choose whether you accept or reject the terms of the agreement.
10. If you accept the terms, enter the Date accepted terms. If you reject the terms, do not fill out this field.
11. Sign the Reseller Signature field with your signature.
12. Have the Product Owner sign the Product Owner Signature field with their signature.
13. Enter the Date created, Date updated, and Date returned fields with the respective dates.
14. Select the Agreement Status as Approved or Rejected.
15. Enter the Agreement ID (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as required by law.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as required by law.
* **Company** (Text, Optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Title** (Text, Required): Enter your job title as required by law.
* **Address 1** (Text, Optional): Enter your work address if applicable.
* **Address 2** (Text, Optional): Enter your physical address if applicable.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your company email address as required.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your company phone number as required.
* **Product Owner** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the product owner as required.
* **Reseller** (Text, Required): Enter your reseller name as required.
* **Agreement Term** (Date, Required): Select the term of the agreement as required.
* **Agreement Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the agreement as required.
* **Signature 1** (Time, Required): Sign this field with your signature as required.
* **Signature 2** (Time, Required): Sign this field with the product owner's signature as required.
* **Terms Accepted** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you accept or reject the terms of the agreement.
* **Other terms** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional terms of the agreement if applicable.
* **Date accepted terms** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you accepted the terms if applicable.
* **Reseller Signature** (Text, Required): Sign this field with your signature as required.
* **Product Owner Signature** (Text, Required): Sign this field with the product owner's signature as required.
* **Date created** (Date, Required): Enter the date the agreement was created as required.
* **Date updated** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the agreement was updated if applicable.
* **Date signed** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the agreement was signed if applicable.
* **Date returned** (Date, Optional): Enter the date the agreement was returned if applicable.
* **Agreement Status** (Select One, Required): Select the status of the agreement as Approved or Rejected.
* **Agreement ID** (Text, Optional): Enter the ID of the agreement if applicable.
