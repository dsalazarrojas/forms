# Wills Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals interested in creating a will. It will be used to gather details such as name, address, and other personal information to ensure accurate and efficient will creation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided the correct information.
3. Double-check the confirmation fields to verify the accuracy of your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Street** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the street where you reside.
* **City** (Text, Optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (Text, Optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip** (Number, Optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Confirm First Name** (Text, Optional): Re-enter your first name for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Last Name** (Text, Optional): Re-enter your last name for verification purposes.
* **Confirm Street** (Text, Optional): Re-enter your street address for verification purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays in the will creation process.
* If you have any doubts or uncertainties about a particular field, contact the relevant authorities or seek advice from a professional.
* Double-check the confirmation fields to ensure the accuracy of your information.
