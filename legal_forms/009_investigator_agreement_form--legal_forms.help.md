# Investigator Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investigator Agreement Form is a document that ensures an investigator has understood and agreed to the terms and conditions of an agreement or contract.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the **investigator_name** field.
2. Write your agreement terms in the **agreement_terms** field.
3. Sign your name in the **investigator_signature** field.
4. Select the date you agree to the terms in the **investigator_agreement_date** field.
5. Select the agreement type in the **investigator_agreement** field.
6. (Optional) Select additional agreements in the **investigator_agreement_2**, **investigator_agreement_3**, and **investigator_agreement_4** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **investigator_name**: Enter your name as the investigator.
- **agreement_terms**: Write a brief summary of the agreement terms you are agreeing to.
- **investigator_signature**: Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the terms.
- **investigator_agreement_date**: Select the date you agree to the terms.
- **investigator_agreement**: Choose the type of agreement or contract you are entering into.
- **investigator_agreement_2**: (Optional) Select additional agreements or contracts you are entering into, if applicable.
- **investigator_agreement_3**: (Optional) Select additional agreements or contracts you are entering into, if applicable.
- **investigator_agreement_4**: (Optional) Select additional agreements or contracts you are entering into, if applicable.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read and understand the agreement terms before signing.
- Select the correct agreement type in the **investigator_agreement** field.
- If you have multiple agreements or contracts, select all that apply in the **investigator_agreement_2**, **investigator_agreement_3**, and **investigator_agreement_4** fields.
- Keep a copy of this form for your records.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with your supervisor or the contract owner.
