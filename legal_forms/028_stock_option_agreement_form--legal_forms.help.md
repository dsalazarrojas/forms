# Stock Option Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to be used for the creation and management of stock options within a company. It captures essential information about the stock options, such as the grant date, stock option type, and participant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by a red star) with the required information.
2. Select the relevant options for each field as necessary.
3. Review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`1`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company that is offering the stock option.
* **Grant Date** (`2`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the stock option was granted.
* **Stock Option** (`3`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the stock option being offered.
* **Participant** (`4`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant who received the stock option.
* **Stock Options Granted** (`5`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that stock options have been granted.
* **Option Price** (`6`, number, optional): Enter the price of the stock option.
* **Vesting Date** (`7`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the stock option vesting period ends.
* **Participant Signature** (`8`, text, optional): Sign and date the participant's signature.
* **Company Representative Signature** (`9`, text, optional): Sign and date the company representative's signature.
* **Date Signed** (`10`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the form was signed.
* **Participant Email** (`11`, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`12`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Phone Ext** (`13`, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone extension.
* **Participant Address** (`14`, text, optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Option Description** (`15`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the stock option.
* **Participant Title** (`16`, text, optional): Enter the participant's title.
* **Option Type** (`17`, select_one, optional): Select the type of stock option being offered (Equity, Options, or Stock).
* **Stock Option Type** (`18`, select_multiple, optional): Select the type of stock option being offered (Equity, Incentive Stock Options, or Non-Qualified Stock Options).
* **Participant Corp Name** (`19`, text, optional): Enter the corporation name of the participant.
* **Participant Incorporation Number** (`20`, text, optional): Enter the participant's incorporation number.
