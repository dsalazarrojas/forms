# Legislative Request Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legislative Request Form is designed to be a standardized template for requesting access to government information, services, or resources. It helps guide the requestor through the process of submitting a formal request.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the **Requestor Name** field with your name and contact information.
2. If applicable, provide your **Contact Info**, which can include your phone number and email address.
3. If your request is on behalf of an organization, fill out the **Organization** field with the name of the organization.
4. Choose the type of request from the **Request Type** options (Law Office, Government Agency, or Advocacy Group).
5. Enter the date of the request in the **Request Date** field.
6. In the **Request Details** field, provide a clear and concise description of the request.
7. If necessary, attach any supporting documents in the **Supporting Documents** field.
8. Provide a valid email address in the **Contact Email** field.
9. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the **Requestor Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, text): This is the name of the person or organization making the request.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text): This is the contact information of the person or organization making the request, including phone number and email address.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text): If the request is on behalf of an organization, fill out this field with the name of the organization.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, select_one): Choose from Law Office, Government Agency, or Advocacy Group.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, date): Enter the date of the request.
* **Request Details** (`request_details`, text): Describe the request clearly and concisely.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text): Attach any supporting documents relevant to the request.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email): Provide a valid email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): This field is optional and not currently used.
* **Requestor Message** (`requestor_message`, note): Any additional comments or questions from the requestor.
* **Request Status** (`request_status`, select_one): Select from Draft, Approved, Rejected, or Completed.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one): Choose from ChatJimmy or Other.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, select_multiple): This field is not currently used and is marked as optional.
* **Created On** (`created_on`, time): This field is not currently used and is marked as optional.
* **Updated On** (`updated_on`, time): This field is not currently used and is marked as optional.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one): This field is not currently used and is marked as optional.
