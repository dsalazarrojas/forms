# Separation Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document the separation agreement between two parties regarding the division of assets and properties, as well as the custody of any children involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand the purpose and scope of the agreement.
2. Fill in the form with the required and optional information.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Partner Names** (`partner_names`): This field is for the names of both parties involved in the separation.
* **Asset Values** (`asset_values`): This field is for the monetary value of any assets to be divided. This can include property, investments, or other financial assets.
* **Asset Description** (`asset_description`): This field is for a description of the assets to be divided, including type, quantity, or other relevant details.
* **Asset Division** (`asset_division`): This field is for selecting how the assets will be divided between the parties. Options include:
	+ Assets acquired before the separation date will go to [Party A]
	+ Assets acquired after the separation date will go to [Party A]
	+ Assets acquired before the separation date will go to [Party B]
	+ Assets acquired after the separation date will go to [Party B]
	+ Both parties agree that the assets will be split 50/50
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Child Custody** (`child_custody`): This field is for selecting how the children involved will be cared for after the separation. Options include:
	+ Party A will have sole custody
	+ Party B will have sole custody
	+ Joint custody, but [Party A will have primary physical custody]
	+ Joint custody, but [Party B will have primary physical custody]
	+ Joint custody
* **Property Values** (`property_values`): This field is for the monetary value of any real or personal property to be divided.
* **Property Description** (`property_description`): This field is for a description of the property to be divided, including location, size, or other relevant details.
* **Property Division** (`property_division`): This field is for selecting how the property will be divided between the parties. Options include:
	+ Both parties agree that the property will be split 50/50
	+ Other (please specify)

## Tips

* Review the form carefully to ensure all necessary information is included.
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out the form.
* Make sure to initial or sign the form before submitting it.
* Consider having a lawyer review the form for accuracy and completeness before signing.
