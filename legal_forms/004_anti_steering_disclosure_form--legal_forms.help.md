# Anti Steering Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the borrower's loan application, ensuring they have been properly informed about the terms and conditions of their loan, and to monitor and report on lender compliance with anti-steering regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information about your loan application.
- Ensure all required fields are completed.
- For fields like "Which loan products were presented and discussed?", select all options that apply.
- If unsure about any field, please ask your loan officer for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Disclosure date**: The date when the loan products were presented and discussed with the borrower.
  (<label> Disclosure date</label>, <type>date</type>, required>
  - This is the date when the loan officer met with the borrower to discuss loan options.
- **Loan officer name**: <name> Loan officer name</name>
  (<label>Loan officer name</label>, <type>text</type>, required>
  - This is the name of the loan officer who met with the borrower.
- **Borrower name**: <name> Borrower name</name>
  (<label>Borrower name</label>, <type>text</type>, required>
  - This is the full name of the loan applicant.
- **Borrower email**: <name> Borrower email</name>
  (<label>Borrower email</label>, <type>email</type>, required>
  - This is the email of the borrower.
- **Property address**: <name> Property address</name>
  (<label>Property address</label>, <type>text</type>, required>
  - This is the address of the property being financed.
- **Type of loan**: <name> Type of loan</name>
  (<label> Type of loan</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select the type of loan applied for, such as FHA, VA, etc.
- **Purpose of loan**: <name> Purpose of loan</name>
  (<label>Purpose of loan</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select the purpose of the loan, such as purchase, refinance, etc.
- **Estimated loan amount**: <name> Estimated loan amount</name>
  (<label>Estimated loan amount</label>, <type>number</type>, required>
  - Enter the estimated loan amount in dollars.
- **Borrower credit score range**: <name> Borrower credit score range</name>
  (<label>Borrower credit score range</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select the approximate credit score range of the borrower.
- **Which loan products were presented and discussed?**: <name> Which loan products were presented and discussed?</name>
  (<label>Which loan products were presented and discussed?</label>, <type>select_multiple</type>, required>
  - Select all loan options presented to the borrower.
- **Were rates and terms fully explained?**: <name> Were rates and terms fully explained?</name>
  (<label>Were rates and terms fully explained?</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select if rates and terms were fully explained.
- **Borrower understands anti-steering policy**: <name> Borrower understands anti-steering policy</name>
  (<label>Borrower understands anti-steering policy</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select if the borrower understands the anti-steering policy.
- **Recommended loan option**: <name> Recommended loan option</name>
  (<label>Recommended loan option</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select the recommended loan option for the borrower.
- **Basis for recommendation**: <name> Basis for recommendation</name>
  (<label>Basis for recommendation</label>, <type>text</type>, required>
  - Explain the reason for the recommended option.
- **Does borrower accept the recommended option?**: <name> Does borrower accept the recommended option?</name>
  (<label>Does borrower accept the recommended option?</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select if the borrower accepts the recommended option.
- **If declined, which alternative preferred?**: <name> If declined, which alternative preferred?</name>
  (<label>If declined, which alternative preferred?</label>, <type>text</type>, required: false>
  - If the borrower declined the recommended option, select the alternative they prefer.
- **Were all borrower questions answered?**: <name> Were all borrower questions answered?</name>
  (<label>Were all borrower questions answered?</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select if all borrower questions were addressed.
- **Borrower acknowledges receipt of disclosure**: <name> Borrower acknowledges receipt of disclosure</name>
  (<label>Borrower acknowledges receipt of disclosure</label>, <type>select_one</type>, required>
  - Select if the borrower acknowledged receipt of the disclosure.
- **Additional notes or disclosures**: <name> Additional notes or disclosures</name>
  (<label>Additional notes or disclosures</label>, <type>text</type>, required: false>
  - Add any additional notes or disclosures relevant to the loan application.
- **Follow-up appointment scheduled**: <name> Follow-up appointment scheduled</name>
  (<label>Follow-up appointment scheduled</label>, <type>date</type>, required: false>
  - If a follow-up appointment is scheduled, enter the date.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues with regulatory compliance.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your loan officer for clarification.
- Ensure all required fields are completed.
- Select all applicable options for "Which loan products were presented and discussed?" and "If declined, which alternative preferred?"
