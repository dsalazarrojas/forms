# Customer Credit Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Credit Agreement Form is a document used to collect and track information about a customer's credit agreement. This information helps the organization to establish a credit relationship with the customer and ensure consistent management of credit agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer's identification number.
2. Provide your customer's name.
3. Enter your customer's address.
4. Enter your customer's city.
5. Enter your customer's state.
6. Enter your customer's zip code.
7. Enter your customer's phone number.
8. Enter your customer's email address.
9. Specify the date of the credit agreement.
10. Enter the total amount of the credit agreement.
11. Choose the payment terms of the credit agreement.
12. Select the payment frequency of the credit agreement.
13. Choose the interest rate of the credit agreement.
14. Add any additional notes about the credit agreement.
15. Specify the name of the person signing the credit agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Id** (`customer_id`, Number, Required): Enter your customer's identification number.
- **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, Text, Optional): Provide your customer's name.
- **Address** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter your customer's address.
- **City** (`city`, Text, Optional): Enter your customer's city.
- **State** (`state`, Text, Optional): Enter your customer's state.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, Number, Optional): Enter your customer's zip code.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your customer's phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Enter your customer's email address.
- **Credit Agreement Date** (`credit_agreement_date`, Date, Optional): Specify the date of the credit agreement.
- **Total Amount** (`total_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the total amount of the credit agreement.
- **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, Select One, Optional): Choose the payment terms of the credit agreement (e.g., First Payment, Second Payment, Third Payment).
- **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the payment frequency of the credit agreement (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
- **Interest Rate** (`interest_rate`, Select One, Optional): Choose the interest rate of the credit agreement (e.g., 10%, 12%, 15%).
- **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about the credit agreement.
- **Signed By** (`signed_by`, Text, Optional): Specify the name of the person signing the credit agreement.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information about your customer.
- Double-check the form for any errors or missing fields before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
