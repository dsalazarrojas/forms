# legal_support_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information for legal support services and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields. Your first name is not required, but your last name is mandatory.
2. Enter your business name (if applicable) in the "Business Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Fill out your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
7. If you have a specific date of birth, enter it in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Select the type of subscription you are interested in from the "Subscription Type" field, either "Legal Subscription" or "Business Subscription".
9. Finally, provide any additional legal issues you would like addressed in the "Legal Issues" field.
10. If you would like to provide any payment details, enter them in the "Payment Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): If you are completing this form on behalf of a business, enter the business name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address here.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code here.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you have a specific date of birth, enter it here.
* **Legal Issues** (`legal_issues`, text, optional): If you have any specific legal issues you would like addressed, enter them here.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select multiple, required): Select the type of subscription you are interested in, either "Legal Subscription" or "Business Subscription".
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): If you would like to provide any payment details, enter them here.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
