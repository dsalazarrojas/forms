# Trademark Ownership Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to verify the ownership of a trademark. It is used by trademark owners to provide accurate information about their trademark and by trademark offices or authorities to ensure that the trademark information is up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the trademark name in the "Trademark Name" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the trademark in the "Trademark Description" field.
3. Enter the trademark registration number in the "Trademark Number" field.
4. Select the type of trademark application made (e.g., TEAS, MadRID, TEAS Plus) in the "Application Type" field.
5. Enter the date of the application in the "Application Date" field.
6. Select the current status of the application (e.g., Pending, Approved, Registered, etc.) in the "Application Status" field.
7. Enter the name of the trademark owner in the "Owner Name" field.
8. Enter the address of the trademark owner in the "Owner Address" field.
9. Enter the email address of the trademark owner in the "Owner Email" field.
10. Enter the phone number of the trademark owner in the "Owner Phone" field.
11. Enter the date of birth of the trademark owner in the "Owner Date of Birth" field.
12. Select the nationality of the trademark owner from the options provided in the "Owner Nationality" field.
13. Enter the residence of the trademark owner in the "Owner Residence" field.
14. Select the type of trademark (e.g., wordmark, service mark, design mark, etc.) in the "Trademark Type" field.
15. Select the category of the trademark (e.g., Class 1, Class 2, Class 3, etc.) in the "Trademark Category" field.
16. Enter the country where the trademark is registered in the "Trademark Country" field.
17. Enter the state where the trademark is registered in the "Trademark State" field.
18. Enter the city where the trademark is registered in the "Trademark City" field.
19. Enter the zip code where the trademark is registered in the "Trademark Zip" field.
20. Enter the name of the representative (if applicable) in the "Owner Representative" field.
21. Select whether the representative is a designated agent in the "Representative Designated Agent" field.
22. Enter the address of the representative in the "Representative Address" field.
23. Enter the phone number of the representative in the "Representative Phone" field.
