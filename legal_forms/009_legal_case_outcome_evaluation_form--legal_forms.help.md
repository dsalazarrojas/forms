# Legal Case Outcome Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Case Outcome Evaluation Form is used to evaluate the outcome of legal cases, allowing users to track and review the results of cases, identify areas for improvement, and assign responsibility for follow-up actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have access to the necessary information and resources for the case.
2. Enter the case number in the "Case Number" field.
3. Select the outcome of the case from the "Case Outcome" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the evaluation score in the "Evaluation Score" field.
5. Enter the date the case was closed in the "Date Closed" field.
6. Identify any areas for improvement in the "Improvement Areas" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback in the "Comments" field.
8. Select the team member responsible for the case from the "Assigned To" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the name of the case manager in the "Case Manager" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, required: false): Enter the unique case number assigned to the case.
* **Case Outcome** (`case_outcome`, select_one, required: false): Select the outcome of the case from the provided options.
* **Evaluation Score** (`evaluation_score`, number, required: false): Enter a numerical score to evaluate the case.
* **Date Closed** (`date_closed`, date, required: false): Enter the date the case was closed.
* **Improvement Areas** (`improvement_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Identify any areas where the case can be improved.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or feedback about the case.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_one, required: false): Select the team member responsible for the case.
* **Case Manager** (`case_manager`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the case manager.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure you have accurate and up-to-date information when completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or option, refer to the original case files or consult with a colleague.
* Remember to provide clear and concise comments and feedback to help improve future cases.
* If you're responsible for assigning tasks, ensure the team member selected is aware of their responsibilities.
