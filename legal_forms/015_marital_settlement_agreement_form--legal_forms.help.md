This form is intended to capture the details of a marital settlement agreement between two spouses. The form is designed to record the division of assets, spousal support, and other terms agreed upon by the couple. The purpose of this form is to provide a clear and concise way for spouses to document their agreement, which can help prevent misunderstandings and disputes in the future.

# Marital Settlement Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marital Settlement Agreement Form is a document used to record the terms of a marital settlement between two spouses. It helps to ensure that both parties have a clear understanding of their rights and responsibilities regarding asset division, spousal support, and other important aspects of their separation or divorce.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including Spouse 1's and Spouse 2's names and addresses.
2. List the assets that will be divided between the spouses.
3. Detail the property division, including any specific terms or agreements.
4. Determine the spousal support and payment terms.
5. Sign and date the agreement, indicating the time and location where the signature was made.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Spouse 1 Name** (`spouse_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of Spouse 1.
* **Spouse 1 Address** (`spouse_1_address`, text, required): Enter the address of Spouse 1.
* **Spouse 2 Name** (`spouse_2_name`, text, required): Enter the name of Spouse 2.
* **Spouse 2 Address** (`spouse_2_address`, text, required): Enter the address of Spouse 2.
* **Assets Divided** (`assets_divided`, text, optional): List the assets that will be divided between the spouses.
* **Property Division** (`property_division`, text, optional): Detail the property division, including any specific terms or agreements.
* **Spousal Support** (`spousal_support`, text, optional): Determine the spousal support terms.
* **Spousal Support Payment** (`spousal_support_payment`, text, optional): List the amount of spousal support to be paid.
* **Property Care Payment** (`property_care_payment`, text, optional): Determine the property care payment terms.
* **Property Care Payment Frequency** (`property_care_payment_frequency`, text, optional): Specify the frequency of property care payments.
* **Property Care Payment Amount** (`property_care_payment_amount`, text, optional): List the amount of property care payments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required): This is a placeholder for a digital signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): The date on which the signature was made.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, required): The time on which the signature was made.
* **Signature Location** (`signature_location`, text, required): The location where the signature was made.
* **Signature Type** (`signature_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of signature (Lawyer, Mediator, Spouse 1, Spouse 2, Notary).
* **Signature Notary Info** (`signature_notary_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional notary information.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date_signed`, date, required): The date on which the signature was made.
* **Time Signed** (`signature_time_signed`, time, required): The time on which the signature was made.
* **Location Signed** (`signature_location_signed`, text, required): The location where the signature was made.
* **Spouse 1 Signature** (`spouse_1_signature`, text, required): This is a placeholder for Spouse 1's digital signature.
* **Spouse 2 Signature** (`spouse_2_signature`, text, required): This is a placeholder for Spouse 2's digital signature.
* **Mediator Comments** (`mediator_comments`, text, optional): Provide any comments from the mediator.
* **Mediator Sign** (`mediator_sign`, text, optional): This is a placeholder for the mediator's digital signature.

## Tips

* Please ensure that both spouses sign and date the agreement in front of the mediator or notary.
* Please keep a copy of the signed agreement for future reference.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact a legal professional for guidance.
