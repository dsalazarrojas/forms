# Settlement Agreement Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather details about a dispute resolution process, including the dispute details, parties involved, mediator or mediator details, resolution details, and next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Dispute Details section with a brief description of the dispute.
2. Provide details about Party 1 and Party 2, including their names, addresses, and any other relevant information.
3. Select the Mediator or Mediator details from the provided options.
4. Choose the type of resolution (Mediated or Litigated) that applies to the dispute.
5. Enter the date of the resolution.
6. If applicable, provide the next steps and settlement fees.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dispute Details** (Dispute Details): Enter a brief description of the dispute.
* **Party 1 Details** (Party 1 Details): Provide details about Party 1, including their name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Party 2 Details** (Party 2 Details): Provide details about Party 2, including their name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Mediator Details** (Mediator Details): Select the name of the mediator or mediator who handled the dispute from the provided options.
* **Resolution Details** (Resolution Details): Select the type of resolution that applies to the dispute (Mediated or Litigated).
* **Dispute Resolution Type** (Dispute Resolution Type): Select the type of dispute resolution (Arbitrated or Mediated).
* **Resolution Date** (Resolution Date): Enter the date of the resolution.
* **Next Steps** (Next Steps): If applicable, provide a brief description of the next steps to be taken.
* **Settlement Fees** (Settlement Fees): Enter any settlement fees associated with the dispute resolution.
* **Dispute Resolution Date** (Dispute Resolution Date): This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* **Resolution Time** (Resolution Time): This field is not currently used and can be ignored.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
