# Case Settlement Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Case Settlement Discharge Form is used to document the details of a settlement in a legal case, including settlement terms, payments, and any conditions or contingencies that apply. This form is essential for ensuring that all parties involved understand the terms of the settlement and for maintaining accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the **Settlement Date** field with the date of the actual settlement (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the **Form Completion Date** with the date you are completing this form (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. In the **Case Identification** section:
    * Enter the **Case Number** and **Case Title**.
    * Enter the **Court Name** where the case was heard.
5. In the **Parties to the Settlement** section:
    * Enter the **Plaintiff Name**, **Plaintiff Attorney Name and Bar Number**.
    * Enter the **Defendant Name** and **Defendant Attorney Name and Bar Number**.
6. In the **Settlement Terms** section:
    * Enter the **Total Settlement Amount (dollars)**.
    * Describe the **Payment Schedule or Lump Sum**.
7. If applicable, fill out the **Special Conditions or Contingencies**.
8. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Non-Disclosure Agreement Included?**.
9. Select **Yes** or **No** for **Non-Admission of Liability?**. If admitting liability, select **Defendant admits liability**.
10. In the **Discharge of Claims** section:
    * Select **Yes, all claims fully released** or **No, partial release**.
    * Describe what is being released in the **Description of Released Claims**.
11. If any claims were retained, describe them in **Any Claims Retained by Plaintiff?**.
12. Enter the **Effective Date of Settlement** (MM/DD/YYYY).
13. Add any **Additional Notes or Special Terms**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Settlement Date (id: 2)**: This is the date when the settlement was reached. Please enter it in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Date

- **Form Completion Date (id: 3)**: This is the date you are completing this form.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Date

- **Case Number (id: 5)**: This is the number of the case in the court.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Case Title (id: 6)**: This is a brief title of the case.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Court Name (id: 7)**: Enter the name of the court where the case was heard.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Plaintiff Name (id: 10)**: Enter the name of the plaintiff.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Plaintiff Attorney Name and Bar Number (id: 11)**: Enter the name and Bar number of the plaintiff's attorney.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Defendant Name (id: 12)**: Enter the name of the defendant.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Defendant Attorney Name and Bar Number (id: 13)**: Enter the name and Bar number of the defendant's attorney.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Total Settlement Amount (dollars) (id: 15)**: Enter the total amount of the settlement in dollars.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Number

- **Payment Schedule or Lump Sum (id: 16)**: Describe how the payments will be made.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Special Conditions or Contingencies (id: 17)**: If there are any special conditions or contingencies, describe them here.
  - **Required**: No
  - **Type**: Text

- **Non-Disclosure Agreement Included? (id: 18)**: Select **Yes** if a non-disclosure agreement was signed.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Select One

- **Non-Admission of Liability? (id: 19)**: Select **Yes** if the defendant admits liability.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Select One

- **All Claims Released? (id: 21)**: Select **Yes, all claims fully released** if all claims are released.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Select One

- **Description of Released Claims (id: 22)**: Describe the claims being released.
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Text

- **Any Claims Retained by Plaintiff? (id: 23)**: If any claims were retained by the plaintiff, describe them here.
  - **Required**: No
  - **Type**: Text

- **Effective Date of Settlement (id: 24)**: Enter the date when the settlement becomes effective (MM/DD/YYYY).
  - **Required**: Yes
  - **Type**: Date

- **Additional Notes or Special Terms (id: 25)**: Enter any additional notes or special terms of the settlement.
  - **Required**: No
  - **Type**: Text

**Tips**: Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting this form. If you're unsure about any field, please consult a legal expert or professional.
