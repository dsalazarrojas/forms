# Last Will Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Last Will Intake Form is a document used to collect information about the testator's wishes regarding their estate distribution after death. It is a crucial tool for the legal process to ensure that the testator's wishes are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Provide personal details such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
2.  List the executors and beneficiaries who will manage and inherit the estate.
3.  Specify the assets to be distributed, including their value in dollars.
4.  Sign the form with a note to verify the information provided.
5.  Date and time of death and time of death are optional but can be helpful for further legal proceedings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Info**: Enter your name and date of birth to identify the testator.
    (Required: No)
*   **Estate Info**: Describe the property, assets, and possessions that will be distributed after death.
    (Required: No)
*   **Executors**: Choose the individuals who will manage the estate, select all applicable options.
    (Required: No)
*   **Beneficiaries**: Select the individuals who will inherit the assets, select one option.
    (Required: No)
*   **Assets**: Enter the value of the assets in dollars.
    (Required: No)
*   **Signature**: Sign with your name to verify the information provided.
    (Required: No)
*   **Date Of Death**: Enter the date of death (optional).
    (Required: No)
*   **Time Of Death**: Enter the time of death (optional).
    (Required: No)
*   **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
    (Required: No)
*   **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information.
    (Required: No)
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the testator's wishes.
    (Required: No)

## Tips

*   Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure that all information is correct.
*   Sign and date the form when completed to confirm the information provided.
*   Store the form in a safe and secure location to prevent loss or tampering.
