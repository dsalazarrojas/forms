# Society Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a society's details for regulatory or administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the society details in the text fields (fields 1-8, 10-13, 15-20).
2. Indicate whether the society has authorized persons (field 3).
3. Confirm whether you are confirming the society declaration (field 15).
4. Add your signature to the note field (field 26).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Society Details (1)**: Enter a brief description of the society.
* **Society Name (2)**: Enter the name of the society.
* **Authorized Persons (3)**: Indicate whether the society has authorized persons or not.
	+ Select "Yes" if the society has authorized persons.
	+ Select "No" if the society does not have authorized persons.
* **Society Details 1-8, 10-20**: Provide additional details about the society. These fields are text fields for entering information.
* **Confirm (15)**: Confirm that the information entered is accurate.
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm.
	+ Select "No" to indicate that the information is not accurate.
* **Signature (26)**: Add your signature to confirm the accuracy of the information entered.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information in the text fields.
* Indicate truthfully whether the society has authorized persons or not.
* Confirm the accuracy of the information entered.
* Ensure your signature is legible and clear.
