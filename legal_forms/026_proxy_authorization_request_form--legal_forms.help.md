# Proxy Authorization Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for requesting proxy authorization, typically for accessing restricted areas or resources. Fill out this form if you need someone else to vouch for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with your information:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Scope** (`scope`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of what you are requesting access for. Be specific about what you need access to.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false): The email address of the contact person, typically the proxy.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): The phone number of the contact person, typically the proxy.
* **Valid Until** (`valid_until`, `date`, required: false): The end date for the proxy authorization. If left blank, the authorization will be valid until further notice.
* **Valid From** (`valid_from`, `date`, required: false): The start date for the proxy authorization. If left blank, the authorization will be considered to have started immediately.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required: false): Your current or future organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Your current or future job title.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: false): The address of the contact person, typically the proxy.
* **Proxy First Name** (`proxy_first_name`, `text`, required: false): The first name of the proxy.
* **Proxy Last Name** (`proxy_last_name`, `text`, required: false): The last name of the proxy.
* **Proxy Job Title** (`proxy_job_title`, `text`, required: false): The job title of the proxy.
* **Proxy Organization** (`proxy_organization`, `text`, required: false): The proxy's organization.
* **Proxy Contact Email** (`proxy_contact_email`, `email`, required: false): The email address of the proxy.
* **Proxy Contact Phone** (`proxy_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): The phone number of the proxy.

## Tips
* Please fill out all fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are requesting proxy authorization for someone else, be sure to enter their information accurately.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification.
