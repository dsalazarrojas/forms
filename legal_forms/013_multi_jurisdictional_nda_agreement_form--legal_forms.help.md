# Multi Jurisdictional NDA Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Multi Jurisdictional NDA Agreement Form is a comprehensive document designed to establish a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) that is applicable across multiple jurisdictions. This form captures essential details from both parties, including their agreement to maintain confidentiality and non-disparagement, signer information, and terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select all jurisdictions applicable to the agreement.
2. Enter a detailed confidentiality and non-disparagement agreement, specifying any terms or conditions.
3. Provide accurate signer information, including their name and contact details.
4. Sign the agreement with a date and time stamp.
5. Select the acceptance of the agreement.
6. Specify the signer's verification type (individual or organization).
7. Choose an agreement termination period (immediate, 30 days, or 60 days).
8. Add any additional notes or comments.
9. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
10. Sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Jurisdiction Selection** (Required): Select all jurisdictions applicable to the agreement. This field allows you to choose from multiple jurisdictions, such as Jurisdiction A, B, or C.
* **Confidentiality and Non-Disparagement Agreement** (Required): Enter a detailed confidentiality and non-disparagement agreement, specifying any terms or conditions. This field ensures that both parties understand and agree to maintain confidentiality and not disparage each other.
* **Signer Information** (Required): Provide accurate signer information, including their name and contact details. This field ensures that both parties can contact each other for any future agreement-related matters.
* **Signer Signature and Date** (Required): Sign the agreement with a date and time stamp. This field serves as proof of your agreement to the terms.
* **Agreement Acceptance** (Required): Select the acceptance of the agreement. This field ensures that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Signer Verification** (Required): Specify the signer's verification type (individual or organization). This field helps determine who has signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Expiration** (Optional): Specify the termination period of the agreement (immediate, 30 days, or 60 days). This field gives both parties a clear understanding of when the agreement will expire.
* **Termination** (Required): Choose a termination period for the agreement (immediate, 30 days, or 60 days). This field determines when the agreement will end.
* **Note** (Optional): Add any additional notes or comments. This field allows both parties to capture any extra information or context.
* **Signer Note** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments from the signer. This field allows signers to provide extra information or context.
* **Agreement Version** (Required): Specify the agreement version. This field helps track any changes or revisions to the agreement.
* **Signer Agreement** (Required): Select the signer's agreement status (agree or decline). This field indicates the signer's stance on the agreement.
* **Termination Date** (Optional): Specify the date of termination. This field helps determine when the agreement will end.
* **Termination Time** (Optional): Specify the termination time. This field helps determine the exact time the agreement will end.
* **Termination Note** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments regarding termination. This field provides extra information or context.
* **Signer Confirmation** (Required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement. This field ensures that both parties are in mutual understanding.
* **Agreement Revision** (Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments on the agreement revision. This field allows both parties to track any changes or updates to the agreement.

**Tips**

* Read and understand all fields before signing the agreement.
* Review and agree to the terms carefully.
* Use accurate and clear information to avoid any confusion.
* Sign the agreement only after you are certain of the terms.
