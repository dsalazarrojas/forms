# International Non Disclosure Agreement NDA Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) Form is a document used to protect confidential information shared between companies. This form captures essential details to ensure a mutually agreeable and secure exchange of information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company details in the `Company` field.
2. Enter the employee information in the `Employee` field.
3. Specify the employee's position in the `Position` field.
4. Enter the company's email in the `Email` field.
5. Provide the phone number in the `Phone` field.
6. Fill in the agreement start date in the `Agreement Start Date` field.
7. Enter the agreement end date in the `Agreement End Date` field.
8. Add any additional non-disclosure agreements in the `Non-Disclosure Agreement` field.
9. Specify the sign date and time in the `Sign Date` and `Sign Time` fields.
10. Choose `Yes` or `No` to indicate if you sign the agreement in the `Sign` field.
11. Indicate if there are signers for the agreement in the `Signers` field.
12. Enter the email and phone numbers of signers in the `Signers Email` and `Signers Phone` fields (if applicable).
13. Choose a time for the agreement end in the `Agreement End Date Time` field (if applicable).
14. Enter a second agreement end date in the `Agreement End Date 2` field (if applicable).
15. Add any additional non-disclosure agreements in the `Non-Disclosure Agreement 2` field (if applicable).
16. Fill in the company name again in the `Company (3)` field (if applicable).
17. Enter the employee's information again in the `Employee (2)` field (if applicable).
18. Specify the position of the second employee in the `Position 2` field (if applicable).
19. Enter any additional agreement details in the `Agreement` field (if applicable).
20. Add a second agreement in the `Agreement 2` field (if applicable).
21. Fill in any notes about the agreement in the `Notes` field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (`Company`, text, required): Enter the name of the company that is entering into this agreement.
* **Employee** (`Employee`, text, required): Enter the name of the employee who is signing this agreement.
* **Position** (`Position`, text, required): Specify the position of the employee who is signing this agreement.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the company.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the company.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`Agreement Start Date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Agreement End Date** (`Agreement End Date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement** (`Non-Disclosure Agreement`, text, optional): Add any additional non-disclosure agreements.
* **Sign Date** (`Sign Date`, date, required): Enter the date of signing.
* **Sign Time** (`Sign Time`, time, required): Enter the time of signing.
* **Sign** (`Sign`, select multiple, required): Choose if you sign the agreement.
* **Signers** (`Signers`, select multiple, required): Choose if there are signers for the agreement.
* **Signers Email** (`Signers Email`, select multiple, optional): Enter the email of signers (if applicable).
* **Signers Phone** (`Signers Phone`, select multiple, optional): Enter the phone number of signers (if applicable).
* **Agreement End Date Time** (`Agreement End Date Time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the agreement end.
* **Agreement End Date 2** (`Agreement End Date 2`, date, optional): Enter a second agreement end date.
* **Non-Disclosure Agreement 2** (`Non-Disclosure Agreement 2`, text, optional): Add any additional non-disclosure agreements.
* **Company (3)** (`Company (3)`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company again (if applicable).
* **Employee (2)** (`Employee (2)`, text, optional): Enter the employee's name again (if applicable).
* **Position 2** (`Position 2`, text, optional): Specify the position of the second employee.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, text, optional): Enter any additional agreement details.
* **Agreement 2** (`Agreement 2`, text, optional): Add a second agreement (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about the agreement.
