**Legal Representation Authorization Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose

This form grants a legal representative authorization to act on a client's behalf in legal matters.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Provide your name as the client.
2.  Select the role of the legal representative (Primary or Secondary).
3.  Enter the date and time of signature for both the client and the legal representative.
4.  Enter the location of signature.
5.  Confirm that the client and legal representative have signed the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client** (`client`, text, required): Enter the client's name as it appears on their identification.
*   **Legal Representative** (`legal_representative`, text, required): Enter the name and title of the legal representative.
*   **Role** (`role`, select_one, true): Choose the role of the legal representative (Primary or Secondary).
*   **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, false): Enter the date the client signed the form.
*   **Time of Signature** (`signature_time`, time, false): Enter the time the client signed the form.
*   **Location of Signature** (`signature_location`, text, false): Enter the location where the client signed the form.
*   **Client Signed** (`client_signed`, text, false): This field is not applicable and should be left blank.
*   **Legal Representative Signed** (`legal_representative_signed`, text, false): This field is not applicable and should be left blank.

## Tips

- Ensure all fields are completed accurately.
- Obtain a wet ink signature from the client before submission.
- Save the form as a PDF after completion.
