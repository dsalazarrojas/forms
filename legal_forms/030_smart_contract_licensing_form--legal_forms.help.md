# Smart Contract Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Contract Licensing Form is used to collect information for licensing smart contracts. This form is intended for businesses to provide details about their smart contracts, such as contract type, product description, and contact information. It is essential to ensure accuracy and completeness when filling out this form to avoid issues during the licensing process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the business name, product name, and other required fields.
2. Select the correct contract type from the provided options.
3. Enter the agreement start and end dates if applicable.
4. Provide a detailed description of the product and its terms.
5. Enter the business contact email and phone number, as well as fax number if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the product or smart contract.
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of agreement being licensed, such as Software Licensing Agreement or Intellectual Property License Agreement.
* **Agreement Start Date** (`agreement_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the agreement if applicable.
* **Agreement End Date** (`agreement_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the agreement if applicable.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your business or organization.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the product or smart contract.
* **Contract Terms** (`contract_terms`, text, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions related to the contract.
* **Business Contact Email** (`business_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the business contact.
* **Business Contact Phone** (`business_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the business contact.
* **Business Contact Fax** (`business_contact_fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number of the business contact.
* **Agreement Version** (`agreement_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number of the agreement if applicable.

## Tips
* Verify the accuracy and completeness of the information before submitting the form.
* If there are any doubts or concerns about the form or its fields, contact the relevant department or support team for assistance.
* Make sure to save the submitted form for future reference, as it may be required for updates or modifications to the license.
