# Legislation Control Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legislation Control Checklist is a tool used to track and document the control and review process of legislation within an organization. It helps monitor the progress of departmental reviews, record findings, and follow-up actions to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Begin by selecting the relevant department for the legislation review from the "Departmental Reviews" section.
2. Enter any record findings or notes for the department review in the "Record Findings" field.
3. Specify the review status as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started" in the "Review Status" field.
4. Enter the review date in the "Review Date" field.
5. Identify the person who reviewed the legislation in the "Reviewed By" field.
6. Select the department that was reviewed in the "Department Reviewed" field.
7. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the department in the "Department Reviewer" field.
8. Specify the follow-up due date for the legislation review in the "Follow-up Due Date" field.
9. Enter the follow-up status as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started" in the "Follow-up Status" field.
10. Record any follow-up notes or actions taken in the "Follow-up Notes" field.
11. Identify the person who reviewed the follow-up in the "Follow-up Reviewed By" field.
12. Select the department that was reviewed in the "Department Reviewer" field.
13. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the follow-up in the "Follow-up Reviewer" field.
14. Specify the follow-up follow-up status as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started" in the "Follow-up Follow-up Status" field.
15. Enter the follow-up follow-up date in the "Follow-up Follow-up Date" field.
16. Enter the follow-up follow-up review status in the "Follow-up Follow-up Review Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Departmental Reviews**: Select the department responsible for the legislation review.
* **Record Findings**: Enter any notes or findings from the department review.
* **Review Status**: Specify the status of the legislation review as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started".
* **Review Date**: Enter the date of the legislation review.
* **Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the legislation.
* **Department Reviewed**: Enter the department that was reviewed.
* **Department Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the department.
* **Follow-up Due Date**: Enter the due date for follow-up actions on the legislation review.
* **Follow-up Status**: Specify the status of the follow-up actions as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started".
* **Follow-up Notes**: Enter any notes or actions taken for the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Reviewed By**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Reviewer**: Enter the name of the person who reviewed the follow-up.
* **Follow-up Follow-up Status**: Specify the status of the follow-up follow-up actions as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started".
* **Follow-up Follow-up Date**: Enter the date of the follow-up follow-up actions.
* **Follow-up Follow-up Review Status**: Specify the status of the follow-up follow-up review as "Completed", "In Progress", or "Not Started".
