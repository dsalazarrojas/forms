# Criminal Defense Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a criminal defense consultation. It is essential to fill it out accurately and honestly to ensure that you receive the best possible assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the main page with any relevant information about your case.
2. Provide details about your criminal history, including any previous convictions or pending cases.
3. Enter your contact information, such as your name and phone number.
4. Indicate your preferred language and any languages you understand.
5. Choose your preferred contact method and follow-up method.
6. Indicate whether you have a legal assistant.
7. If applicable, select your follow-up method and message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page** (Page 1): A brief summary of your case.
* **Main Information** (Page 2): Provide any relevant information about your case.
* **Criminal History** (Page 3): This section is for providing information about any previous criminal history, including convictions or pending cases.
* **Contact Information** (Page 4): Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number.
* **Case Information** (Page 5): Enter any additional information about your case.
* **Attorney Information** (Page 6): If you have an attorney, enter their name and contact information.
* **Legal Representation** (Page 7): Indicate the type of legal representation you are looking for (e.g., Criminal, Civil, Family, Probate, Trust).
* **Preferred Language** (Page 8): Select your preferred language (English, Spanish, Other).
* **Languages You Understand** (Page 9): Select the languages you understand (English, Spanish, French, German).
* **Phone Availability** (Page 10): Enter a message indicating your availability for phone calls.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Page 11): Select your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, Mail, In-person).
* **Legal Assistant** (Page 12): Indicate whether you have a legal assistant.
* **Follow-up Method** (Page 13): Select your preferred follow-up method (e.g., Email, Phone, Mail, In-person).
* **Follow-up Message** (Page 14): Enter a message for follow-up communication.
* **Follow-up Method Options** (Page 15): Select your preferred follow-up method options (e.g., Email, Phone, Mail, In-person).

## Tips
* Fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure that you receive the best possible assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
