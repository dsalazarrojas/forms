# Office Rental Lease Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for an office rental lease agreement between a tenant and a landlord.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand the sections and fields that need to be filled out.
2.  Fill out the required fields with accurate information:
    *   **Last Name**: The last name of the tenant.
    *   **First Name**: The first name of the tenant.
    *   **Email**: The email address of the tenant.
    *   **Phone Number**: The phone number of the tenant.
    *   **Tenant Signature**: A signature of the tenant to confirm their agreement.
3.  Fill out the **Lease Term**, **Lease Type** and **Rent Amount** if they are applicable. These fields are not always necessary and depend on the specific rental agreement.
4.  Review and fill out the **Payment Frequency**, **Rent Due Date** and **Security Deposit** fields according to the agreement terms.
