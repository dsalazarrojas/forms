# UCC Filing Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide details about a UCC filing, which is an official document that gives public notice of a security interest in a transaction.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields, marked as required: true.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
3. Make sure to select the correct UCC type from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Filing Request Details (1)**: This is the main section that describes the details of the UCC filing. Enter a brief description of the filing.
* **Applicant Information (2)**: This is where you can enter any additional information about the applicant or party involved in the filing.
* **UCC Type (3)**: Select the type of UCC filing from the dropdown menu. For example:
	+ UCC-1: Article 1 (General Definitions)
	+ UCC-3: Article 3 (Bodies of Debtor and Unmatured Securities)
* **UCC Date (4)**: Enter the date of the UCC filing, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **UCC Number (5)**: Enter the UCC number assigned to the filing.
* **UCC Amount (6)**: Enter the amount of the security interest, if applicable.
* **Collateral (7)**: Enter any collateral or assets that are being secured by the UCC filing.
* **Collateral Type (8)**: Enter the type of collateral, for example: "Equipment" or "Vehicle".
* **Authorized Person (9)**: Enter the name of the authorized person who signed the UCC filing.
* **Signature Date (10)**: Enter the date the authorized person signed the UCC filing.
* **Signature by (11)**: Enter the name of the person who signed the UCC filing.
* **Note (12)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the UCC filing.

Note: This form is not a substitute for legal advice. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please consult a legal expert.
