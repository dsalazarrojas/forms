# limited_liability_company_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a Limited Liability Company (LLC) to create a comprehensive agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling out the "Owner Details" section with the name and relevant contact information of the LLC's owner.
2. Provide the "Owner Address" in the next section.
3. In the "Manager Details" section, enter the name and contact information of the person managing the LLC.
4. Enter the "Manager Address" for the person managing the LLC.
5. In the "Capital Structure" section, specify the type of capital structure the LLC will have (e.g., number of shares).
6. Indicate the "Number of Members" in the LLC.
7. Specify the "Capital Currency" in which the LLC will operate.
8. Enter the percentage of the manager's share in the "Manager Percentage" field.
9. Choose the "Management Structure" from the provided options (e.g., Member Management, Managerial Management).
10. In the "Operating Agreement" section, describe the operating agreement or rules of the LLC.
11. Enter the name and contact information of the manager in the "Manager Details 2" field.
12. Enter the address of the manager in the "Manager Address 2" field.
13. Specify the "Capital Amount" in which the LLC will operate.
14. Describe the "Distribution of Purpose" of the LLC.
15. In the "Operating Agreement 2" section, describe any additional operating agreements or rules of the LLC.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Details** (Owner Details, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the LLC's owner.
* **Owner Address** (Owner Address, text, required): Enter the address of the LLC's owner.
* **Manager Details** (Manager Details, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person managing the LLC.
* **Manager Address** (Manager Address, text, required): Enter the address of the person managing the LLC.
* **Capital Structure** (Capital Structure, number, required): Specify the type of capital structure the LLC will have (e.g., number of shares).
* **Number of Members** (Number of Members, number, required): Indicate the number of members in the LLC.
* **Capital Currency** (Capital Currency, text, required): Specify the currency in which the LLC will operate.
* **Manager Percentage** (Manager Percentage, number, required): Enter the percentage of the manager's share.
* **Management Structure** (Management Structure, select_one, required): Choose the management structure from the provided options (e.g., Member Management, Managerial Management).
* **Operating Agreement** (Operating Agreement, text, required): Describe the operating agreement or rules of the LLC.
* **Manager Details 2** (Manager Details 2, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the manager.
* **Manager Address 2** (Manager Address 2, text, required): Enter the address of the manager.
* **Capital Amount** (Capital Amount, number, required): Specify the capital amount of the LLC.
* **Distribution of Purpose** (Distribution of Purpose, text, required): Describe the purpose of the LLC's distribution.
* **Operating Agreement 2** (Operating Agreement 2, text, required): Describe any additional operating agreements or rules of the LLC.
