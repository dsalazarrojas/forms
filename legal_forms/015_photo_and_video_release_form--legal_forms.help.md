# Photo And Video Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain permission from clients or subjects to use their photos and videos for specific purposes, such as marketing, social media, or print/online advertising.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the following fields with the required information:

- **Client**: Enter the name of the person or organization you are releasing photos and videos to.
- **Description**: Provide a brief description of the photo or video shoot, including any relevant details.
- **Location**: If applicable, enter the location where the photo or video was captured.
- **Date of Capture**: Enter the date the photo or video was captured.
- **Time of Capture**: Enter the time the photo or video was captured.
- **Duration**: Enter the duration of the photo or video capture.
- **Usage**: Select all applicable purposes for which you are releasing the photos and videos to, such as Client, Social Media, Print/Online Advertising, or Publicity/Promotion.
- **Credits**: Select the type of credit you want to give for the photo or video, such as Photographer, Videographer, Model, Location, or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client**: Enter the name of the person or organization you are releasing photos and videos to.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of the photo or video shoot, including any relevant details.
* **Location**: If applicable, enter the location where the photo or video was captured.
*   **Date of Capture**: Enter the date the photo or video was captured.
*   **Time of Capture**: Enter the time the photo or video was captured.
*   **Duration**: Enter the duration of the photo or video capture.
*   **Usage**: Select all applicable purposes for which you are releasing the photos and videos to, such as:
	+ Client: For use by a specific client or company.
	+ Social Media: For use on social media platforms.
	+ Print/Online Advertising: For use in print or online advertising.
	+ Publicity/Promotion: For use in promotional materials.
*   **Credits**: Select the type of credit you want to give for the photo or video, such as:
	+ Photographer: Give credit to the photographer who captured the photo or video.
	+ Videographer: Give credit to the videographer who captured the video.
	+ Model: Give credit to the model who appeared in the photo or video.
	+ Location: Give credit to the location where the photo or video was captured.
	+ Other: Select "Other" if none of the above options apply.

## Tips
- Make sure to accurately complete all required fields.
- Be as specific as possible when entering description and usage fields.
- Ensure that you have permission to release the photos and videos to the client.
