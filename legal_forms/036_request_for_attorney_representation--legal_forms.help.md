# Request For Attorney Representation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Request For Attorney Representation" form is used to provide the legal department with information about potential clients who are interested in obtaining legal representation. This form helps the legal department to understand the client's needs and preferences, allowing them to connect them with the most suitable attorney.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact information in the "name" and "contact number" fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Briefly describe your legal needs in the "description" field.
4. Choose the attorney experience and area of law that align with your needs.
5. Select the location where you would like to work with an attorney.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request for attorney representation**: A short description of your interest in seeking legal representation. This is the main reason for submitting this form.
* **name (Text, required/optional)**: Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed by the legal department.
* **contact number (Text, required/optional)**: Provide your contact number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **email (Email, required/optional)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **description (Text, required/optional)**: Briefly describe your legal needs and goals. This will help the legal department understand your situation and connect you with the right attorney.
* **attorney experience (Select Multiple, required/optional)**: Choose from 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5+ years of experience that matches your needs.
* **area of law (Select One, required/optional)**: Select from civil law, criminal law, bankruptcy law, estate planning, family law, intellectual property law, personal injury law, real estate law, or tax law that aligns with your needs.
* **location (Select One, required)**: Choose from New York, New Jersey, Connecticut, or other (if your location is not listed).
* **location (Select One, required)**: This field is required as it's crucial for the legal department to know where you would like to work with an attorney.
