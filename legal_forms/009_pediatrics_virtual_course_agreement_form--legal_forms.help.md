# pediatrics virtual course agreement form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pediatrics virtual course agreement form is designed to collect information for the pediatrics virtual course. This form is crucial for the organizers to ensure the smooth and safe running of the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely, entering your details and those of your emergency contacts.
2. Enter your medical information, as required.
3. Review and sign the consent section.
4. Add your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your basic details such as name, age, or any other relevant personal information.
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact details, including address and phone number, for emergency purposes.
- **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, text, optional): Share any important medical information or conditions that might affect your participation in the course.
- **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, optional): List the names and contact details of your emergency contacts.
- **Course Info** (`course_info`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about your course preferences or requirements.
- **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions outlined.
- **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Add your digital signature, indicating your agreement to the form's terms.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the consent section carefully before signing the form.
- In case of an emergency, ensure your emergency contacts are aware of their responsibilities.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
