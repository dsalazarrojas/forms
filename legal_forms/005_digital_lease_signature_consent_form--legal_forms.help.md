# digital_lease_signature_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain a digital signature from a tenant and landlord, confirming their understanding and agreement on the lease terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Tenant Information section with your details.
2. Review and agree to the Lease Terms.
3. Sign the Lease Signature section with your digital signature.
4. Fill out the Landlord Contact Information section with your details.
5. Confirm the Lease Start and End Dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information** (1): Enter your name, email, and phone number to confirm your identity.
* **Lease Terms** (2): Review and agree to the terms of the lease by selecting the corresponding option.
* **Lease Signature** (3): Sign with your digital signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Lease Terms Acknowledgement** (4): Select which terms you have read and understood.
* **Tenant Signature** (5): Sign with your digital signature to confirm your agreement.
* **Landlord Signature** (6): Sign with your digital signature to confirm their agreement.
* **Landlord Name** (7): Enter your name.
* **Landlord Phone** (8): Enter your phone number.
* **Landlord Email** (9): Enter your email address.
* **Tenant Phone** (10): Enter your phone number.
* **Tenant Email** (11): Enter your email address.
* **Date In Effect** (12): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (13): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Rent Amount** (14): Enter the rent amount.
* **Move In Date** (15): Enter the date you are expected to move in.
* **Move In Time** (16): Enter the time you are expected to move in.
* **Move Out Date** (17): Enter the date you are expected to move out.
* **Move Out Time** (18): Enter the time you are expected to move out.
* **Lease Start Date** (19): Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease Duration** (20): Enter the duration of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (21): Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Landlord Company** (22): Enter your company name.
* **Tenant Company** (23): Enter your company name.
* **Property Address** (24): Enter the address of the property.
* **Building Number** (25): Enter the building number or unit number.

Note: Ensure you fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to avoid any issues with lease agreement.
