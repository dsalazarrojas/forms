# Trademark Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to release ownership and control of a trademark to someone else. The owner of the trademark grants permission for someone else to use the trademark under specific conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your trademark, including the trademark owner's name, the trademark name, and the effective and expiry dates.
2. Indicate whether you grant permission for the trademark to be used in a specific way, such as for a logo, name, or other purposes.
3. Choose where the trademark can be used, such as online, offline, or both.
4. Choose how the trademark can be used, such as in print, digital, or both.
5. Acknowledge the rights you're granting to the trademark.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trademark Owner**: Enter the name of the person or organization that owns the trademark.
* **Trademark Name**: Enter the name of the trademark you're releasing.
* **Permission Granted**: Indicate if you grant permission for someone else to use your trademark. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date when the trademark release takes effect.
* **Expiry Date**: Enter the date when the trademark release expires.
* **Permitted Use**: Choose how the trademark can be used, such as for a logo, name, or other purposes.
* **Permitted Location**: Choose where the trademark can be used, such as online, offline, or both.
* **Permitted Medium**: Choose how the trademark can be used, such as in print, digital, or both.
* **Acknowledgement of Rights**: Acknowledge the rights you're granting by choosing 'None', 'Some', or 'Full'.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure the form is complete and accurate.
* Review the form carefully before signing it, as it may affect your legal rights.
