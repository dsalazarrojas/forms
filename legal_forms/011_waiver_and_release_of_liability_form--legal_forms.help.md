# Waiver And Release Of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Waiver And Release Of Liability Form is used to release a participant from liability for any injuries or damages that may occur during an activity or event. This form is designed to protect both the participant and the host of the activity or event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information, including your name, address, and email.
2. Select your participant signature from the dropdown list.
3. Enter the activity details, including the date and time of the activity.
4. Select the waiver version you wish to sign.
5. Enter your host information, including their name, address, and email.
6. Select your host signature from the dropdown list.
7. Select your host state from the list of options.
8. Enter any additional information as required.

* **Host Signature**: This is a dropdown list of host signatures. Please select your signature from the list.
