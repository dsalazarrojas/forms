# Partnership Agreement Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Partnership Agreement Authorization Form is used to collect information about business partnerships, including business and partner details, agreement terms and conditions, and authorization and agreement status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields.
2. Enter your business name and address.
3. Enter your partner's name and address.
4. Select the partner's title.
5. Enter the agreement date and time.
6. Specify the agreement duration.
7. Choose whether the partner is authorized (Yes or No).
8. Choose the agreement type (Yes or No).
9. Select the partner's roles (Yes or No).
10. Enter the percentage of ownership.
11. Enter the agreement number and version.
12. Choose the agreement status (Yes or No).
13. Enter any agreement notes.
14. Enter partner notes.
15. Enter business notes.
16. Enter the terms and conditions of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (id: 1)**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Partner Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of your partner.
* **Partner Title (id: 3)**: Select the title of your partner.
* **Address (id: 4)**: Enter your business address.
* **Business Address (id: 5)**: Enter the address of your business.
* **Partner Address (id: 6)**: Enter the address of your partner.
* **Phone (id: 7)**: Enter your business phone number.
* **Fax (id: 8)**: Enter your business fax number.
* **Email (id: 9)**: Enter your business email address.
* **Agreement Date (id: 10)**: Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Agreement Time (id: 11)**: Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Agreement Duration (id: 12)**: Specify the duration of the agreement.
* **Authorization (id: 13)**: Choose whether the partner is authorized (Yes or No).
* **Authorization Date (id: 14)**: Enter the date of authorization.
* **Agreement Type (id: 15)**: Choose the type of agreement (Yes or No).
* **Role (id: 16)**: Select the roles of the partner (Yes or No).
* **Percentage (id: 17)**: Enter the percentage of ownership.
* **Agreement Number (id: 18)**: Enter the agreement number.
* **Agreement Version (id: 19)**: Enter the agreement version.
* **Agreement Status (id: 20)**: Choose the agreement status (Yes or No).
* **Agreement Notes (id: 21)**: Enter any agreement notes.
* **Partner Notes (id: 22)**: Enter any partner notes.
* **Business Notes (id: 23)**: Enter any business notes.
* **Terms (id: 24)**: Enter the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Conditions (id: 25)**: Enter the conditions of the agreement.

Please note that all fields are optional, and users can fill in as much or as little information as required.
