# Executor Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executor Checklist Form is a tool used by executors to track the progress of estate administration, confirm or deny certain aspects of the process, and collect details about the estate. It helps ensure that all necessary steps are taken to manage the estate efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Use the provided options for select_multiple and select_one fields to select the relevant answers.
4. Enter dates and details in the text fields as needed.
5. Save your progress regularly to avoid losing your work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Estate Administration Progress** (`estate_administration_progress`, text, optional): Describe the current state of estate administration, e.g., "Probate pending," "Probate in progress," or "Probate completed."
* **Confirmations** (`confirmations`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm or deny the following:
	+ Yes: Indicate that certain confirmations have been made.
	+ No: Indicate that certain confirmations have not been made.
* **Data Collection** (`data_collection`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about data collection, e.g., "Tax returns collected," "Financial records collected," or "No data collected."
* **Probate Progress** (`probate_progress`, date, optional): Enter the date of the probate progress, e.g., "January 2022."
* **Estate Details** (`estate_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the estate, e.g., "Size of the estate," "Assets listed," or "Debts owed."
* **Assets** (`assets`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the assets, e.g., "Cash," "Real estate," or "Business interests."
* **Asset Distribution** (`asset_distribution`, select_one, optional): Confirm or deny the distribution of assets:
	+ Yes: Indicate that assets have been distributed.
	+ No: Indicate that assets have not been distributed.
* **Estate Accounting** (`estate_accounting`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about estate accounting, e.g., "Financial records updated," "Tax returns filed," or "No accounting done."
* **Accounting Details** (`accounting_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about accounting, e.g., "Bank account statements," "Investment reports," or "No accounting records."
* **Executor Details** (`executor_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the executor's role or involvement, e.g., "Resided with the deceased," "Not resided with the deceased," or "Unknown."
* **Estate Status** (`estate_status`, time, optional): Enter the current status of the estate, e.g., "Probate pending," "Probate in progress," or "Probate completed."
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Enter any next steps or actions to be taken, e.g., "Meet with the lawyer," "Update financial records," or "No next steps."
* **Probate Courts** (`probate_courts`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about probate courts, e.g., "Court filing date," "Court case number," or "No probate court involvement."
* **Estate Administrators** (`estate_administrators`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about estate administrators, e.g., "Fiduciaries," "Trustees," or "No estate administrators."
* **Probate Attorney** (`probate_attorney`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the probate attorney, e.g., "Representing the estate," "Not representing the estate," or "Unknown."
* **Probate Agent** (`probate_agent`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the probate agent, e.g., "Fiduciary," "Power of attorney," or "No probate agent."
