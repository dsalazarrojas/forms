# Value-Based Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for applicants who wish to apply for value-based licenses. A value-based license is a type of license that is granted based on a specific value or metric. This form will guide you through the process of providing the necessary information to apply for a value-based license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Application Form page with your contact information.
2. Select the type of license you are applying for.
3. Enter the value or metric that the license is based on.
4. Enter the date when the value or metric was last checked.
5. Enter the start date of the value or metric.
6. Select the current status of your license (Active, Pending, or Expired).
7. Enter the expiration date of your license.
8. Add any additional notes about your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter your contact information, including any relevant details about yourself or your organization.
* **Contact Information** (`2`, `text`, required: false): This field is also used to enter your contact information, including any relevant details about yourself or your organization. This is the second field to enter contact information, please make sure the information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **License Type** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of license you are applying for from the options provided.
* **Value-Based Licensing** (`4`, `number`, required: false): Enter the value or metric that the license is based on. This is the core information required for your application.
* **Value-Based Licensing Date** (`5`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the value or metric was last checked.
* **Value-Based Licensing Start Date** (`6`, `time`, required: false): Enter the start date of the value or metric.
* **License Status** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the current status of your license (Active, Pending, or Expired).
* **License Expiration Date** (`8`, `date`, required: false): Enter the expiration date of your license.
* **Notes** (`9`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
* **Contact Details** (`10`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to enter any additional contact details, please make sure the information is accurate and up-to-date.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the value or metric you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our licensing team for clarification.
* Please review your application carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are completed accurately.
