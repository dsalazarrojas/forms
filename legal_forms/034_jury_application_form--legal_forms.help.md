# Jury Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help us determine your eligibility and availability to serve on a jury. To be considered, you must be a US citizen, at least 18 years old, and have a valid government-issued ID.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the required information for each field. If you need help, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your ID or government documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your ID or government documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your current address as it appears on your ID or government documents.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (text, required): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code** (text, required): Enter your zip code as it appears on your ID or government documents.
* **Available Dates** (select multiple, required): Select all dates that you are available to serve on a jury.
* **Available Times** (select multiple, required): Select all times that you are available to serve on a jury.

## Tips
* Please be truthful and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure that you have selected all dates and times that you are actually available.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
