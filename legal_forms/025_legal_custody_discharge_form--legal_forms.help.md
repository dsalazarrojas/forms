# Legal Custody Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the discharge of a legal custody arrangement between parents or guardians of a minor child. This form captures information about the individuals involved, the reason for the discharge, and the formal signature of the discharging parties.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Form ID** field with the unique identifier for this form, as assigned by the relevant authority.
2. Enter the **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Address** of Parent 1.
3. Enter the **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Address** of Parent 2 (if applicable).
4. Choose a **Date of Discharge** that corresponds to the date of the actual discharge of the custody arrangement.
5. Provide a brief explanation for the **Reason for Discharge**.
6. Sign the **Signature** field with your official signature.
7. Enter the **Date Signed** field with the date you signed the form.
8. Enter the **Name of Court** where the custody arrangement was established.
9. Enter the **Judge Signing** name, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID (1)**: A unique identifier for this form, as assigned by the relevant authority.
* **Parent 1 First Name (2)**: Enter the first name of the first parent or guardian.
* **Parent 1 Last Name (3)**: Enter the last name of the first parent or guardian.
* **Parent 1 Address (4)**: Enter the address of the first parent or guardian.
* **Parent 2 First Name (5)**: Enter the first name of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent 2 Last Name (6)**: Enter the last name of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent 2 Address (7)**: Enter the address of the second parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Date of Discharge (8)**: Select the date that corresponds to the actual discharge of the custody arrangement.
* **Reason for Discharge (9)**: Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the discharge.
* **Signature (10)**: Sign with your official signature.
* **Date Signed (11)**: Enter the date you signed the form.
* **Name of Court (12)**: Enter the name of the court where the custody arrangement was established.
* **Judge Signing (13)**: Enter the name of the judge who signed the form (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please ensure that the form is signed by the relevant parties with their official signatures.
* Double-check the dates entered, especially the **Date of Discharge** and **Date Signed** fields.
* Make sure to save a copy of the completed form for your records.
