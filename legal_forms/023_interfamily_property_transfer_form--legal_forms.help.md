# Interfamily Property Transfer Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Interfamily Property Transfer Form is a document that helps establish property transfer agreements between family members. This form ensures the smooth transfer of property rights and provides a record of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for Family Member 1 and Family Member 2 fields to indicate if they are involved in the property transfer.
3. Provide a detailed description of the property in the Property Description field.
4. Enter the value of the property in the Property Value field.
5. Specify the Date of Transfer and Time of Transfer fields to document the exact date and time of the transfer.
6. Sign the form with E Signature 1 and E Signature 2 to confirm the agreement.
7. Enter your contact information in the Email, Phone, and Address fields to ensure we can get in touch with you if needed.
8. Add any additional notes in the Notes field if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Family Member 1 (Yes/No)**: Indicate if the first family member is involved in the property transfer.
* **Family Member 2 (Yes/No)**: Indicate if the second family member is involved in the property transfer.
* **Property Description**: Provide a detailed description of the property being transferred.
* **Property Value**: Enter the value of the property being transferred.
* **Date of Transfer**: Specify the date of the property transfer.
* **Time of Transfer**: Specify the time of the property transfer.
* **E Signature 1**: Sign the form to confirm the agreement.
* **E Signature 2**: Sign the form to confirm the agreement.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address**: Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you are located.
* **Country**: Indicate if you are from the country of the property or not.
* **State**: Indicate if you are from the state of the property or not.
* **Zip**: Enter the zip code of your location.
* **E Signed By**: Sign the form to confirm the agreement.
* **Date Signed**: Specify the date you signed the form.
* **Time Signed**: Specify the time you signed the form.
* **E Signed At**: Specify the location where you signed the form.
* **E Signed By Email**: Enter your email address to confirm your identity.
* **E Signed By Phone**: Enter your phone number to confirm your identity.
* **E Signed At Location**: Specify the location where you signed the form.
* **File Upload**: Upload any supporting documents or files related to the property transfer.
* **E Signed**: Confirm that you have signed the form.

Note: This form is designed to be used for internal purposes only and is not intended for external sharing. Please ensure you complete the form accurately and honestly to avoid any issues.
