# <string> - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for capturing individual's work experience and internship information for legal or compliance purposes, such as creating a confidential information disclosure agreement or internship agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your non-prophet's name.
2. Provide your title as it appears on your resume.
3. Enter your current company name.
4. Enter your department name.
5. Enter your job title.
6. Describe your job description.
7. Select your work period (if applicable).
8. Enter your work location.
9. Enter your supervisor's name.
10. Enter your supervisor's email address (if applicable).
11. Enter your supervisor's phone number.
12. Enter your supervisor's department.
13. Read and agree to the non-disclosure terms.
14. Select your acknowledgement of confidentiality agreement terms.
15. Enter the submission date (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Non Prophets Name** (`non_prophets_name`, text, **required**): Please fill in your non-prophet's name.
* **Title** (`title`, text, **optional**): Please provide your title as it appears on your resume.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, **required**): Enter your current company name.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, **required**): Enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, **required**): Enter your job title.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, **required**): Describe your job description.
* **Work Period** (`work_period`, date, **optional**): If you have a specific work period, select it.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, text, **required**): Enter your work location.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, **required**): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, **optional**): Enter your supervisor's email address (if applicable).
* **Supervisor Phone** (`supervisor_phone`, text, **required**): Enter your supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Department** (`supervisor_department`, text, **required**): Enter your supervisor's department.
* **Non Disclosure Terms** (`non_disclosure_terms`, note, **optional**): Read and agree to the non-disclosure terms.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, select_one, **required**): Select your acknowledgement of confidentiality agreement terms.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, **required**): Enter the submission date (if applicable).
