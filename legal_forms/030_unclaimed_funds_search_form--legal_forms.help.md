# unclaimed funds search form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The unclaimed funds search form is a tool for customers or individuals to provide information about their search for unclaimed funds. This form helps the financial institution or government agency process and respond to search requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the **Search Request** field with the specific details of your search, including any relevant keywords or descriptions.
2. Provide your **Authorization Details** in the next field, including your name and contact information. This will enable the institution to verify your identity and contact you about the status of your search.
3. Select the **Date of Search** field and choose the date you are searching for unclaimed funds.
4. In the **Search Results** field, enter any information you have about the search results, including the date of the search, name, and location.
5. Choose the **Search Status** to indicate the current status of your search. Options are:
	* Active: Your search is ongoing.
	* Inactive: Your search is inactive or has been closed.
6. Select the **Search Status Options** to provide more information about your search status. Options are:
	* Completed: The search has been completed.
	* Open: The search is ongoing and more information is needed.
	* Unknown: The search status is currently unknown.
7. Enter the **Follow-up Schedule** field to select a date and time for a follow-up if the search results are not found.
8. Enter your **Customer Contact** information in the next field, including phone number and/or email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Search Request**: This field is where you can enter the specific details of your search, including any relevant keywords or descriptions.
* **Authorization Details**: This field is where you can enter your name and contact information, which will be used to verify your identity and contact you about the status of your search.
* **Date of Search**: Select the date you are searching for unclaimed funds.
* **Search Results**: Enter any information you have about the search results, including the date of the search, name, and location.
* **Search Status**: Choose the current status of your search.
* **Search Status Options**: Provide more information about your search status, such as if it's completed, open, or unknown.
* **Follow-up Schedule**: Select a date and time for a follow-up if the search results are not found.
* **Customer Contact**: Enter your phone number and/or email for communication with the institution.
* **Customer Email**: Enter your email address for the institution to contact you about the status of your search.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter your phone number for the institution to contact you about the status of your search.

## Tips

* Ensure you have not submitted a similar search request recently to avoid duplicating efforts.
* Be as specific as possible when providing search details to help the institution process your request quickly.
* If your search status changes, update it in the **Search Status** field to keep the institution informed.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the institution's customer support.
