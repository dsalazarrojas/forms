# Handgun Qualification License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a Handgun Qualification License. It collects information from applicants and agencies to process the application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. If you're an agency, fill in your agency's details, including name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Enter the license expiration date.
4. Provide your address and zip code.
5. Add your contact phone number and email.
6. Select the agency's approval status.
7. Enter the agency's approval and applicant acceptance dates.
8. Sign the form with your signature and the agency's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth (2)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **License Expiration Date (3)**: Enter the date your current license will expire, if applicable.
* **Address (4)**: Enter your address in the format: "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **Zip Code (5)**: Enter your five-digit zip code.
* **Contact Phone (6)**: Enter your phone number in the standard format (###-####-####).
* **Contact Email (7)**: Enter your email address in the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Agency Name (8)**: Enter the name of the agency that is sponsoring your application.
* **Agency Address (9)**: Enter the address of the agency that is sponsoring your application.
* **Agency Phone (10)**: Enter the phone number of the agency that is sponsoring your application.
* **Agency Email (11)**: Enter the email address of the agency that is sponsoring your application.
* **Submit Date (12)**: Enter the date you submitted the application.
* **Signature (13)**: Sign the form with your signature.
* **Agency Approval Date (14)**: Enter the date the agency approved your application.
* **Agency Approval Status (15)**: Select the status of the agency's approval (Approved, Rejected, Pending).
* **Applicant Acceptance Date (16)**: Enter the date you accepted the agency's approval.
* **Applicant Signature (17)**: Sign the form with your signature.
