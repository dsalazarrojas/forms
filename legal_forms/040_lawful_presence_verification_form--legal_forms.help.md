# Lawful-Presence-Verification-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lawful-Presence-Verification-Form is designed to collect information for the verification of an individual's lawful presence. This includes details about the individual, document uploads, and specifics about the document itself such as ID, expiration, issuing authority, country, type, description, and series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide individual information in the "Individual Info" section.
2.  Upload documents as required in the "Document Upload" section.
3.  Enter document details in the subsequent sections, including ID, expiration, issuing authority, country, type, description, and series.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual Info (`individual_info`, required):** This is the primary section where you need to provide personal details about the individual whose presence needs to be verified. Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Document Upload (`document_upload`, required):** This is where you need to upload supporting documents for the verification process. Ensure that you attach the correct documents as specified in the form.
* **Signature (`signature`, required):** This field is reserved for the individual's signature, which may be required for verification purposes. Please provide your signature as instructed.
* **Document ID 2 (`document_id_2`, required):** Enter the ID number of the document you uploaded or the relevant ID associated with your presence.
* **Document Date (`document_date`, required):** This is the date the document was issued or became valid. Enter the correct date as specified in the document or as you understand it.
* **Document Expiration (`document_expiration`, required):** This is the date when the document expires or ceases to be valid. Enter the correct expiration date.
* **Document Issuing Authority (`document_issuing_authority`, required):** This is the authority that issued the document. Enter the name or abbreviation of the issuing authority as stated on the document.
* **Document Country (`document_country`, required):** This is the country where the document was issued or has validity. Enter the name of the country of issue as specified on the document.
* **Document Type (`document_type`, required):** This is the type of document provided. Enter the type as stated on the document, for example, passport, ID, or driver's license.
* **Document Description (`document_description`, required):** This is a brief description of the document provided. Enter a clear description of the document to help with the verification process.
* **Document Series (`document_series`, required):** This is any series or category the document belongs to. Enter the series or category as specified on the document.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to support the verification process.
- Attach the correct documents as specified in the form.
- If a field's label or requirement is unclear, refer to the specific document you are providing for clarification.
- This form is designed to be user-friendly and accessible, following best practices for user experience and data security. If you encounter any issues, please refer to the form's help section or contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
