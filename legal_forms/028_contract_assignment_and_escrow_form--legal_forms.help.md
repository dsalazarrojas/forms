# Contract Assignment And Escrow Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the transfer of rights and obligations between parties in a contract.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, start by filling in the basic information about the assignor and assignee. This includes the name, type, and signature of both parties.
2.  Next, provide the details about the original contract, including its title, date, and contract number.
3.  Then, specify the rights and obligations being transferred and the amount of consideration (payment or value exchanged).
4.  Indicate whether an escrow is required and, if so, provide the required information about the escrow agent, amount, and conditions.
5.  Finally, indicate the status of legal review, the governing law, and whether notarization is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Assignor Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the party transferring rights.
*   **Assignor Type** (`2`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of entity transferring rights, e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Individual, or Other.
*   **Assignee Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the party receiving rights.
*   **Assignee Type** (`4`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of entity receiving rights, e.g., Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Individual, or Other.
*   **Assignment Date** (`5`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the assignment.
*   **Original Contract Title** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the original contract.
*   **Original Contract Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the original execution date of the contract.
*   **Contract Number** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter the contract reference number.
*   **Rights Assigned** (`9`, `text`, required): Specify the rights being transferred.
*   **Obligations Assigned** (`10`, `text`, required): Specify the obligations being transferred.
*   **Assignment Consideration** (`11`, `number`, required): Enter the amount of consideration (payment or value exchanged).
*   **Escrow Required** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether an escrow is required, e.g., 'True' or 'False'.
*   **Escrow Agent Name** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the escrow agent, if required.
*   **Escrow Amount** (`14`, `number`, optional): Enter the amount held in escrow.
*   **Escrow Conditions** (`15`, `text`, optional): Specify the conditions for release of escrow.
*   **Escrow Release Date** (`16`, `date`, optional): Enter the expected release date of escrow.
*   **Consent Obtained** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether consent has been obtained from all parties, e.g., Yes, from all parties, Yes, from some parties, or False.
*   **Legal Review Status** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Indicate the status of legal review, e.g., Completed, In progress, or Not started.
*   **Governing Law** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select the state law that applies, e.g., California, New York, Texas, or Other.
*   **Effective Date** (`20`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the assignment takes effect.
*   **Termination Conditions** (`21`, `text`, optional): Specify the conditions under which this contract can be terminated.
*   **Assignor Signature** (`22`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the assignor.
*   **Assignee Signature** (`23`, `text`, required): Enter the signature of the assignee.
*   **Witness Signature** (`24`, `text`, optional): Enter the witness's name, if required.
*   **Notarization Required** (`25`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether notarization is required, e.g., 'True' or 'False'.
