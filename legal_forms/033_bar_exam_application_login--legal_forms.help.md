# Bar Exam Application Login - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to apply for a bar exam in a specific jurisdiction. Users will be asked to provide personal details and other relevant information to ensure a successful application process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the required information ready before starting the application process.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Login Date** (`login_date`, date): Enter the date you are currently logged in.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email): Enter your email address on file.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Bar Admission or License Number** (`bar_admission_number`, text): If applicable, enter your bar admission or license number.
* **Last 4 Digits of Social Security Number** (`social_security_last_four`, text): Enter the last four digits of your social security number for verification.
* **Bar Exam Jurisdiction** (`bar_exam_jurisdiction`, select_one): Choose the state or jurisdiction for which you are applying for the bar exam.
* **Bar Exam Year** (`exam_year`, number): Enter the year you took the bar exam.
* **Exam Session** (`exam_session`, select_one): Choose the exam session you took (if applicable).
* **Applicant Status** (`applicant_status`, select_one): Choose your current applicant status (First Time Applicant, Retaking Exam, etc.).
* **Law School Name** (`law_school_name`, text): Enter the name of your law school.
* **Law School Graduation Year** (`law_school_graduation_year`, number): Enter the year you graduated from law school.
* **Previous Bar Attempts** (`previous_bar_attempts`, number): Enter the number of times you have attempted the bar exam.
* **Previously Admitted in Another Jurisdiction?** (`previous_jurisdiction`, select_one): If yes, select the state or jurisdiction you were previously admitted in.
* **Previous Bar State or Jurisdiction** (`previous_bar_state`, text): If previously admitted, enter the state or jurisdiction you were previously admitted in.
* **Good Moral Character Certified** (`good_moral_character`, select_one): Choose whether you have been certified as having good moral character.
* **Character and Fitness Committee Status** (`character_committee_status`, select_one): Choose your character and fitness committee status.
* **Any Character or Fitness Issues?** (`character_issues`, select_one): Choose whether you have any character or fitness issues.
* **Password Reset Needed?** (`password_reset_needed`, select_one): Choose whether you need to reset your password (if applicable).
* **What Is Your Mother's Maiden Name?** (`security_question`, text): Enter your mother's maiden name for account security verification (if applicable).
