# severance_agreement_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The severance agreement authorization form is used to collect details about an employee's severance agreement. This form ensures that all necessary information is collected correctly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
3. Enter the date of severance in the "Date of Severance" field.
4. Enter the reason for severance in the "Reason for Severance" field.
5. Enter the severance amount in the "Severance Amount" field.
6. Enter the date of severance in the "Severance Date" field.
7. Have the employee sign their name in the "Employee Signature" field.
8. Have the company representative sign their name in the "Company Signature" field.
9. Enter the date the form was signed in the "Date Signed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`Employee Name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the employee leaving the company.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID number of the employee leaving the company.
* **Date of Severance** (`Date of Severance`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the employee is leaving the company.
* **Reason for Severance** (`Reason for Severance`, text, required/optional): Enter the reason for the employee's departure.
* **Severance Amount** (`Severance Amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of severance pay the employee is receiving.
* **Severance Date** (`Severance Date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the severance pay will be paid out.
* **Employee Signature** (`Employee Signature`, text, required/optional): Have the employee sign their name to confirm they have agreed to the severance terms.
* **Company Signature** (`Company Signature`, text, required/optional): Have the company representative sign their name to confirm they have agreed to the severance terms.
* **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, date, required/optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Date Signed** (`Date Signed`, date, required/optional): This field is not to be filled out by the user, it should be automatically populated by the system with the current date and time.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for actual human review and approval of the severance agreement. Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any potential issues.
