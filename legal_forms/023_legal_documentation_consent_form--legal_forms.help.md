# Legal Documentation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Documentation Consent Form is designed to collect users' consents for the use, sharing, and processing of their data. This form is a crucial step in ensuring that users understand and agree to the terms and conditions of data usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1.  Review each field to ensure you understand the purpose and implications of providing your consent.
2.  Select your responses to each question carefully, as they will affect how your data is used.
3.  Ensure that you have filled out all required fields (marked as required).
4.  Once you have completed the form, review and submit it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent to Use Data (<consent_to_use_data>, Select Multiple, Optional)**:
	+ This field requires you to confirm whether you agree to the use of your data.
* **Consent to Share Data (<consent_to_share_data>, Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select one of the options to indicate whether you consent to sharing your data or not.
* **Data Usage (<data_usage>, Text, Optional)**:
	+ This field is provided for you to provide any additional information related to the use of your data.
* **Contact Information (<contact_information>, Text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter your contact details for any further communication or clarification related to data usage.
* **Signature (<signature>, Number, Optional)**:
	+ You are required to sign this form, which serves as a confirmation of your consent.
* **Date Signed (<date_signed>, Date, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the date you signed this form to keep a record of when you provided your consent.
* **Time Signed (<time_signed>, Time, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the time you signed this form to keep a record of when you provided your consent.
* **IP Address (<ip_address>, Text, Optional)**:
	+ Provide the IP address associated with your computer for any further analysis or tracking purposes.
* **User Agent (<user_agent>, Text, Optional)**:
	+ Enter the user agent associated with your browser for further analysis or tracking purposes.
* **Accept Terms (<accept_terms>, Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select to accept or decline the terms and conditions related to data usage.
* **Accept Privacy (<accept_privacy>, Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select to accept or decline your consent for data privacy and security measures.
* **Accept Cookies (<accept_cookies>, Select One, Optional)**:
	+ Select to accept or decline to use cookies for further analysis or tracking purposes.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully read each field description to understand its purpose.
- Select your responses based on your understanding and agreement to data usage.
- Review the form for completeness before submission.
