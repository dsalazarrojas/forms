# Mediation Agreement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Agreement Release Form is used to document the agreement reached between parties involved in a mediation session. It captures the terms and conditions of the mediation process, including the parties involved and any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the fields and their purpose.
2. Answer each field according to the provided guidelines and explanations below.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out, if applicable.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Reached** (`agreement_reached`, text, optional): This field is used to record the primary agreement reached during the mediation session. Provide a brief summary of the main points discussed and agreed upon.
* **Parties Involved** (`parties_involved`, select_multiple, optional): Select all parties involved in the mediation session. This field allows for multiple selections, enabling you to include all relevant parties in the agreement.
* **Terms Agreed** (`terms_agreed`, note, optional): This field is used to elaborate on the specific terms agreed upon during the mediation session. Provide a detailed description of the terms, if necessary.
* **Mediation Date** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_4`, date, optional): Enter the date of the mediation session, if applicable. This will help keep a record of the timing of the mediation.
* **Mediation Session Date** (`mediation_agreement_release_form_5`, date, optional): Enter the date of the mediation session. This is the date when the agreement was reached.
* **Mediator** (`mediator`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the mediator involved in the mediation session.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Signature Name** (`signature_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person signing the agreement.
* **Printed Date** (`printed_date`, date, optional): This field is used to record the date the form was printed, if applicable.
