# Annuity Licensing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request an annuity license from the state insurance authority. It collects information about the applicant, their professional experience, and licensing history to ensure they meet the required standards for licensing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details, including the applicant's name, social security number, date of birth, home address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Provide the email address and phone number of the applicant.
3. Specify the state requesting the license for.
4. Choose the type of license requested.
5. Select the lines of insurance requested.
6. Enter the sponsoring insurance company's name and address.
7. If applicable, provide the company's license number.
8. Enter the total years of insurance experience.
9. If applicable, list previous insurance licenses held.
10. Indicate if the applicant has ever been denied a license, and provide an explanation if necessary.
11. Indicate if the applicant has disciplinary actions against them, and provide details if necessary.
12. Indicate if the applicant has any criminal convictions, and provide details if necessary.
13. Confirm if the applicant has completed the required exams, and provide the exam date if applicable.
14. Specify the exam date or planned date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter the applicant's full name, including their first name, middle name, and last name.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter the applicant's social security number as per the format XXX-XX-XXXXX.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the applicant's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Home Address**: Enter the applicant's current home address.
* **City**: Enter the city of the applicant's current residence.
* **State**: Enter the state of the applicant's current residence.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the applicant's current residence.
* **Email Address**: Enter the applicant's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the applicant's phone number.
* **State Requesting License For**: Enter the state in which the applicant is requesting the license.
* **License Type Requested**: Choose the type of license the applicant is requesting (e.g., Resident agent license, Non-resident agent license, Annuity specialist certification, Broker-dealer license, or Other).
* **Lines of Insurance Requested**: Select the type of annuity lines requested (e.g., Fixed annuity, Variable annuity, Indexed annuity, Immediate annuity, Deferred annuity).
* **Sponsoring Insurance Company**: Enter the name of the company requesting the license.
* **Sponsoring Company Address**: Enter the address of the sponsoring company.
* **Company License Number**: If applicable, enter the sponsoring company's license number.
* **Years of Insurance Experience**: Enter the total number of years the applicant has been in the insurance industry.
* **Previous Insurance Licenses Held**: If applicable, list any previous insurance licenses held by the applicant.
* **Have you ever been denied a license**: Indicate if the applicant has ever been denied a license.
* **Explanation if previously denied**: If applicable, provide details about the license denial.
* **Any disciplinary actions against you**: Indicate if the applicant has any disciplinary actions against them.
* **Disciplinary details**: If applicable, provide details about the disciplinary actions.
* **Any criminal convictions**: Indicate if the applicant has any criminal convictions.
* **Criminal history details**: If applicable, provide details about the criminal convictions.
* **Have you completed required exams**: Indicate if the applicant has completed the required exams.
* **Exam Date or Planned Date**: If applicable, provide the date of the exam or the planned date.
