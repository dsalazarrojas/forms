# Divorce Proceedings Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Proceedings Discharge Form is a legal document used to record the discharging of spouses in a divorce process. This form collects essential information about the spouses, the discharge date, reason for discharge, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the first spouse (Spouse 1) in the **spouse_1_name** field.
2. Provide the address of the first spouse (Spouse 1) in the **spouse_1_address** field.
3. Enter the name of the second spouse (Spouse 2) in the **spouse_2_name** field.
4. Provide the address of the second spouse (Spouse 2) in the **spouse_2_address** field.
5. Enter the discharge date in the **discharge_date** field.
6. Specify the reason for discharge in the **reason_for_discharge** field.
7. Enter the name of the witness who signed the discharge in the **signed_by_1** field (for the first witness).
8. Enter the name of the second witness in the **signed_by_2** field.
9. Enter the date and time when the discharge was signed in the **date_signed** field.
10. Enter the name of the first witness who signed the discharge in the **witness_1_name** field.
11. Enter the signature of the first witness in the **witness_1_signature** field.
12. Enter the name of the second witness who signed the discharge in the **witness_2_name** field.
13. Enter the signature of the second witness in the **witness_2_esignature** field.
14. Enter the name of the attorney representing the first spouse in the **signed_by_attorney_1** field.
15. Enter the name of the attorney representing the second spouse in the **signed_by_attorney_2** field.
16. Enter the signature of the first attorney in the **attorney_1_esignature** field.
17. Enter the signature of the second attorney in the **attorney_2_esignature** field.
18. Enter the date when the first attorney signed the discharge in the **attorney_1_sign_date** field.
19. Enter the date when the second attorney signed the discharge in the **attorney_2_sign_date** field.
20. Enter the date of the divorce in the **divorce_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **spouse_1_name** (`Spouse 1 Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the first spouse involved in the divorce process.
* **spouse_1_address** (`Spouse 1 Address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the first spouse involved in the divorce process.
* **spouse_2_name** (`Spouse 2 Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the second spouse involved in the divorce process.
* **spouse_2_address** (`Spouse 2 Address`, text, optional): Provide the address of the second spouse involved in the divorce process.
* **discharge_date** (`Discharge Date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the discharging of the spouses takes place.
* **discharge_time** (`Discharge Time`, time, optional): Enter the time when the discharging of the spouses takes place.
* **reason_for_discharge** (`Reason for Discharge`, text, optional): Specify the reason for discharging the spouses.
* **signed_by_1** (`Signed by 1`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness who signed the discharge.
* **signed_by_2** (`Signed by 2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness who signed the discharge.
* **date_signed** (`Date Signed`, date, optional): Enter the date and time when the discharge was signed.
* **witness_1_name** (`Witness 1 Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness who signed the discharge.
* **witness_2_name** (`Witness 2 Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness who signed the discharge.
* **witness_2_esignature** (`Witness 2 Signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the second witness who signed the discharge.
* **witness_1_signature** (`Witness 1 Signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the first witness who signed the discharge.
* **signed_by_attorney_1** (`Signed by Attorney 1`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney representing the first spouse.
* **signed_by_attorney_2** (`Signed by Attorney 2`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney representing the second spouse.
* **attorney_1_esignature** (`Attorney 1 Signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the attorney representing the first spouse.
* **attorney_2_esignature** (`Attorney 2 Signature`, text, optional): Enter the signature of the attorney representing the second spouse.
* **attorney_1_name** (`Attorney 1 Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney representing the first spouse.
* **attorney_2_name** (`Attorney 2 Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the attorney representing the second spouse.
* **attorney_1_sign_date** (`Attorney 1 Sign Date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the first attorney signed the discharge.
* **attorney_2_sign_date** (`Attorney 2 Sign Date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the second attorney signed the discharge.
* **divorce_date** (`Divorce Date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the divorce.
* **notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the discharge process.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and truthfully.
- Double-check the information before signing the discharge form.
- Ensure that all witnesses and attorneys sign the discharge form with their correct signatures.
- Keep the discharge form in a secure location to avoid tampering or loss.
