# Child Custody Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a child custody case, providing details from both parents and outlining the type of custody and visitation arrangements requested.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Case Information**: This is a section that provides general information about the case. If a case number is already assigned, please enter it in the `Court Case Number` field.
2. **Parent A Details**: Enter the full name, residence, and other required information for Parent A, the petitioner or respondent.
3. **Parent B Details**: Enter the full name, residence, and other required information for Parent B.
4. **Children Information**: Provide a list of the names and ages of all children involved in the case.
5. **Custody Request**: Select the type of custody arrangement requested from the options provided.
6. **Visitation Schedule**: Describe the proposed visitation schedule, including details about weekends, holidays, and weekdays.
7. **Existing Orders**: Indicate whether there are existing court orders regarding custody or support in place.
8. **Date of Filing**: Enter the date the form is being filed, which should be the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent A Full Name** (Parent A Full Name, Text, Required): Enter the full name of Parent A.
* **Parent A Residence** (Parent A Residence, Text, Required): Enter the city and state where Parent A resides.
* **Parent B Full Name** (Parent B Full Name, Text, Required): Enter the full name of Parent B.
* **Parent B Residence** (Parent B Residence, Text, Required): Enter the city and state where Parent B resides.
* **Names and Ages of Children** (Children Details, Text, Required): Provide a list of all children involved in the case, including their names and ages.
* **Type of Custody Requested** (Custody Type, Select One, Required): Select the desired custody arrangement from the options provided.
* **Proposed Visitation Schedule** (Visitation Schedule, Text, Required): Describe the proposed visitation schedule, including details about weekends, holidays, and weekdays.
* **Existing Court Orders** (Existing Orders, Select One, Required): Indicate whether there are existing court orders regarding custody or support in place.
* **Date of Filing** (Filing Date, Date, Required): Enter the date the form is being filed, which should be the current date.
