# Tax Debt Bankruptcy Discharge Eligibility Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help you determine if you are eligible for bankruptcy discharge. Please fill out the form with your basic information to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- You can skip any question that does not apply to you or your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Name** (`user_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
- **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, text, optional): Enter your tax ID number.
- **Case Type** (`case_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of bankruptcy case you are applying for. Options are:
  - Bankruptcy: Chapter 13
  - Bankruptcy: Chapter 11
  - Other
  - Unknown
- **Bankruptcy Status** (`bankruptcy_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current bankruptcy status. Options are:
  - Active
  - Dismissed
  - Discharge Granted
  - Discharge Denied
- **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, number, optional): Enter the tax year your case pertains to.
- **Tax Debt Amount** (`tax_debt_amount`, number, optional): Enter the total tax debt you are seeking discharge for.
- **Case Opening Date** (`case_opening_date`, date, optional): Select the date your bankruptcy case was opened.
- **Discharge Date** (`discharge_date`, date, optional): Select the date your bankruptcy discharge was granted.
- **Other Case Type** (`case_type_other`, select_one, optional): If your case type is not listed above, select 'other'.
- **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select your case status. Options are:
  - Active
  - Closed
  - Dismissed
  - Discharge Granted
  - Discharge Denied
- **Income Verification Method** (`income_verification_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you verified your income. Options are:
  - Income Verification
  - Other

Please note that this form is for informational purposes only and should not be considered as a substitute for professional advice. If you are unsure about any part of the form, please consult a legal professional.
