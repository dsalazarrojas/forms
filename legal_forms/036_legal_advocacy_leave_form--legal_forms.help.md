# legal-advocacy-leave-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees who need to request time off for personal or family-related legal matters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the required fields.
2. Provide your reason for taking time off in the "Reason" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of your leave.
4. Specify the number of days off you are requesting.
5. If applicable, select the approval status and approved by individuals.
6. Add any comments or additional information as needed in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your official email address.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact number for any follow-up questions or notifications.
- **Reason** (Text, Required): Explain the reason for your leave request, including any relevant dates or details.
- **Start Date** (Date, Required): Enter the start date of your leave.
- **End Date** (Date, Required): Enter the end date of your leave.
- **Number of days off** (Number, Required): Specify the number of days you will be taking off.
- **Return Date** (Date, Optional): If applicable, enter the date you plan to return to work.
- **Approval Status** (Select One, Optional): Select "Active" if your leave request is approved, otherwise select "Inactive".
- **Approved by** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" for each individual who has approved your leave request.
- **Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional information or comments about your leave request.

## Tips
* Ensure you are submitting the correct information and dates for your leave request.
* Double-check that you have selected the correct approval status and individuals who have approved your request.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or have further questions, please contact your HR or legal department for assistance.
