# Passenger Disclosure And Attestation To The United States Of America - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from passengers traveling to or from the United States of America. It guides passengers through the necessary steps and requirements for their travel, including following CDC guidelines and contacting aircraft operators. The form helps passengers understand their responsibilities and obligations related to their travel.

## How To Complete This Form
- To start, please ensure you have all the required information and documentation ready.
- Begin by filling out the form, providing all the necessary details as prompted.
- Review each section carefully to ensure you have completed all the required fields.
- Once you have finished, the form will automatically generate a PDF document that summarizes your submission.
