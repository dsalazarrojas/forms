# Sale Proceeds Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sale Proceeds Release Form is used to report the sale of a property and provide details about the sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information about the sale.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any discrepancies.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sale Proceeds Release** (Page 1, id: 1): Enter a brief description of the sale.
* **Buyer Name** (Page 1, id: 2): Enter the name of the buyer.
* **Buyer Name Address** (Page 1, id: 3): Enter the address of the buyer.
* **Seller Name** (Page 1, id: 4): Enter the name of the seller.
* **Seller Name Address** (Page 1, id: 5): Enter the address of the seller.
* **Property Address** (Page 1, id: 6): Enter the address of the property.
* **Sale Date** (Page 1, id: 7): Enter the date of the sale.
* **Purchase Price** (Page 1, id: 8): Enter the purchase price of the property.
* **Commission Amount** (Page 1, id: 9): Enter the commission amount.
* **Payment Method** (Page 1, id: 10): Select the payment method used (Bank, Cheque, or Cash).
* **Payment Date** (Page 1, id: 11): Enter the date of payment.
* **Bank Name** (Page 1, id: 12): Enter the name of the bank.
* **Account Number** (Page 1, id: 13): Enter the account number.
* **Branch Name** (Page 1, id: 14): Enter the branch name.
* **Contact Name** (Page 1, id: 15): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 1, id: 16): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (Page 1, id: 17): Enter the contact email.
* **Signature Date** (Page 1, id: 18): Enter the date of signature.
* **Signature Method** (Page 1, id: 19): Select the method of signature (Signature, Witness, or Electronic signature).
* **Signed By** (Page 1, id: 20): Enter the name of the person signing.
* **Remarks** (Page 1, id: 21): Enter any additional remarks.
* **Sale Agreement Date** (Page 1, id: 22): Enter the date of the sale agreement.
* **Sale Agreement Status** (Page 1, id: 23): Select the status of the sale agreement (Approved, Pending, or Rejected).
* **Sale Agreement Comments** (Page 1, id: 24): Enter any comments about the sale agreement.
* **Form Submitted By** (Page 1, id: 25): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
* Always ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete.
* Review the form carefully before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the relevant personnel for assistance.
