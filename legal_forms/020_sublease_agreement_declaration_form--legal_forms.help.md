# Sublease Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information to create a sublease agreement between the tenant and the landlord. Please fill out this form to declare your sublease agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the necessary information for both the tenant and the landlord.
2. Review the form carefully for any errors or missing information.
3. Sign the sublease agreement at the end of the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Information** (`tenant_info`, `text`, required): This section collects information about the tenant taking over the lease. Please fill in your name, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Landlord Information** (`landlord_info`, `text`, required): This section collects information about the landlord who is allowing the sublease. Please fill in their name and contact information.
* **Sublease Terms** (`sublease_terms`, `text`, required): This section is for describing the terms of the sublease, including the duration and rent amount.
* **Tenant Representatives** (`tenant_representatives`, `text`, required): If the tenant is taking over the lease on behalf of multiple people, list their names and contact information here.
* **Sublease Duration** (`sublease_duration`, `number`, required): This is the length of time you are planning to sublease the property. Please fill in the number of months or years you plan to sublease.
* **Sublease Start Date** (`sublease_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the sublease will begin.
* **Sublease End Date** (`sublease_end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the sublease will end.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, `number`, required): Fill in the amount of rent you will pay for the property during the sublease.
* **Subleasing Fees** (`subleasing_fees`, `number`, required): Some subleases may incur additional fees. Please fill in any fees you will pay.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `text`, required): Describe how and when you will pay the rent and any other costs associated with the sublease.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, `time`, required): If you need to give notice to end the sublease, enter the time frame you will use.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `time`, required): Enter the date when the sublease will automatically end.
* **Tenant Phone** (`tenant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact information so the landlord can reach you.
* **Landlord Phone** (`landlord_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the landlord's contact information so you can reach them.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so the landlord can reach you via email.
* **Sublease Notes** (`sublease_notes`, `text`, required): Add any additional information you feel is necessary for the sublease.
* **Sublease Signature** (`sublease_signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm your agreement with the landlord.
* **Date Subleased** (`date_subleased`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the sublease began.
* **Tenant Signed At** (`tenant_signed_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date when you signed the sublease agreement.
* **Landlord Signed At** (`landlord_signed_at`, `date`, required): Enter the date when the landlord signed the sublease agreement.
* **Sublease Agreement** (`sublease_agreement`, `text`, required): Describe the terms of the sublease.
* **Landlord Notes** (`landlord_notes`, `text`, required): Add any additional information from the landlord.
* **Tenant Agreed To Sublease** (`tenant_agreed_to_sublease`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the box to confirm you agreed to sublease the property.
* **Landlord Agreed To Sublease** (`landlord_agreed_to_sublease`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the box to confirm the landlord agreed to sublease the property.
* **Sublease Agreement Signed** (`sublease_agreement_signed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Check the box to confirm the sublease agreement was signed.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" must be filled in for the form to be valid.
