# Legitimate Interest Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legitimate Interest Assessment Questionnaire is a tool designed to help organizations assess whether they have a legitimate interest in collecting, processing, or disclosing personal data, ensuring compliance with data protection laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name**: This is the first name of the individual or organization being assessed.
2.  Enter your **Last Name**: This is the last name of the individual or organization being assessed.
3.  Provide your **Email**: A valid email address is required for contact purposes.
4.  Select the **Data Categories**: Choose the categories of data being processed or collected.
5.  Select the **Data Types**: Identify the types of data being processed or collected.
6.  Specify the **Data Processing Purposes**: Enter the purposes for which data is being collected or processed.
7.  Identify the **Data Subjects**: Choose the subjects involved in the data processing or collection.
8.  Select the **Legal Bases**: Choose the legal bases for data collection or processing.
9.  If applicable, specify the **Other Data Subjects**: Enter any additional data subjects.
10. If applicable, specify the **Additional Data Processing Purposes**: Enter any additional data processing purposes.
11. Enter the **Review Date**: The date of the review or assessment.
12. Specify the **Review Time**: Enter the time of the review or assessment.
13. If applicable, provide the **Review Location**: Enter the location of the review or assessment.
14. Enter the **Reviewer Name**: The name of the reviewer or assessor.
15. Provide the **Reviewer Contact Information**: Enter the contact information of the reviewer or assessor.
16. If applicable, specify the **Legal Entity**: Enter the legal entity involved in the data collection or processing.
17. Enter any **Review Note**: Additional comments or notes from the reviewer or assessor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **First Name** (`first_name`, required/true)
*   The first name of the individual or organization being assessed.
### **Last Name** (`last_name`, required/true)
*   The last name of the individual or organization being assessed.
### **Email** (`email`, required/true)
*   A valid email address for contact purposes.
### **Data Categories** (`data_categories`, required/true)
*   The categories of data being processed or collected, such as Financial, Health Information, Personal Information, etc.
### **Data Types** (`data_types`, required/true)
*   The types of data being processed or collected, such as Contact Information, Transaction Details, Location Information, etc.
### **Data Processing Purposes** (`processing_purpose`, required/true)
*   The purposes for which data is being collected or processed.
### **Data Subjects** (`data_subjects`, required/true)
*   The subjects involved in the data processing or collection.
### **Legal Bases** (`legal_basis`, required/true)
*   The legal bases for data collection or processing.
### **Review Date** (`review_date`, required/true)
*   The date of the review or assessment.
### **Review Time** (`review_time`, required/true)
*   The time of the review or assessment.
### **Review Location** (`review_location`, required/false)
*   The location of the review or assessment, if applicable.
### **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, required/true)
*   The name of the reviewer or assessor.
### **Reviewer Contact Information** (`reviewer_contact_info`, required/true)
*   The contact information of the reviewer or assessor.
### **Legal Entity** (`legal_entity`, required/false)
*   The legal entity involved in the data collection or processing, if applicable.
### **Review Note** (`review_note`, required/false)
*   Additional comments or notes from the reviewer or assessor.
