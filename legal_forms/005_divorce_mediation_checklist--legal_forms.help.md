# Divorce Mediation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Divorce Mediation Checklist is designed to guide mediators, clients, and other stakeholders through the divorce mediation process, ensuring that all necessary information is captured and progress is tracked.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections to understand what information is required for each session.
2. Gather all relevant details for the current session, including session details, client and mediator information, and any agreements or next steps.
3. Fill out the form accordingly, using the options provided for each field.
4. Ensure that all required fields are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Meeting**: 
  - **First Meeting** (Note): Record the notes from the first meeting.
* **Session Details**:
  - **Session Details** (Text): Enter any additional details or notes about the session.
* **Parties Agree**:
  - **Parties Agree** (Select Multiple): Indicate if both parties have agreed on certain points, such as division of assets or custody arrangements.
* **Communication Methods**:
  - **Communication Methods** (Select One): Choose the preferred method of communication between the mediator, client, and other parties (e.g., Phone, Email, In Person).
* **Readiness Info**:
  - **Readiness Info** (Select One): Assess the readiness of the client and other parties to proceed with the mediation process (e.g., Ready, Not Ready).
* **Priorities**:
  - **Priorities** (Select Multiple): Identify the priorities for the next steps in the mediation process.
* **Client Info**:
  - **Client Info** (Number): Enter the client's ID or a unique number for tracking purposes.
* **Mediator Info**:
  - **Mediator Info** (Number): Enter the mediator's ID or a unique number for tracking purposes.
* **Agreements**:
  - **Agreements** (Select Multiple): List any agreements reached during the session.
* **Next Steps**:
  - **Next Steps** (Select One): Determine the next course of action, such as continuing the session or ending it.
* **Session Comments**:
  - **Session Comments** (Text): Record any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Client Signature**:
  - **Client Signature** (Select One): Indicate if the client has signed the agreement or document.
* **Mediator Signature**:
  - **Mediator Signature** (Select One): Indicate if the mediator has signed the agreement or document.
* **Client Name**:
  - **Client Name** (Text): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Phone**:
  - **Client Phone** (Text): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Client Email**:
  - **Client Email** (Text): Enter the client's email address.
* **Parties Signed**:
  - **Parties Signed** (Select One): Indicate if both parties have signed the agreement or document.
* **Mediator Signed**:
  - **Mediator Signed** (Select One): Indicate if the mediator has signed the agreement or document.
* **Session Status**:
  - **Session Status** (Select One): Update the session status (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Next Session Date**:
  - **Next Session Date** (Date): Enter the date of the next session.
* **Next Session Time**:
  - **Next Session Time** (Time): Enter the time of the next session.
* **Client Address**:
  - **Client Address** (Text): Enter the client's address.
* **Client City**:
  - **Client City** (Text): Enter the client's city.
* **Client State**:
  - **Client State** (Text): Enter the client's state.
* **Client Zip**:
  - **Client Zip** (Text): Enter the client's zip code.

Note: The fields marked as required or optional will be clearly indicated in the form, so ensure to fill them out accordingly. Consult with the relevant stakeholders to understand the specific requirements of your organization.
