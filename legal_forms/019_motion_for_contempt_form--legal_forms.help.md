# Motion For Contempt Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Motion For Contempt Form" is a document used to provide information about a motion for contempt in a legal proceeding. It is used to gather details about the court order ID, the defendant's name, the attorney's name, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals before filling out the form.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Motion for Contempt
* **Motion for Contempt** (`title`, `text`, optional):
	+ This is the main heading of the form.
	+ Enter a brief title for the motion for contempt.
* **court order id** (`court_order_id`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the court order ID related to the motion for contempt.
* **defendant name** (`defendant_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the defendant involved in the motion for contempt.
* **attorney name** (`attorney_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the attorney representing the party involved in the motion for contempt.
* **legal clinic name** (`legal_clinic_name`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the legal clinic involved in the motion for contempt.

### Page 2
* **violation details** (`violation_details`, `text`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional details about the violation that led to the motion for contempt.
* **contempt date** (`contempt_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the contempt.
* **contempt time** (`contempt_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the contempt.
* **motion hearing** (`motion_hearing`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the type of hearing related to the motion for contempt:
		- Hearing
		- Trial
		- Other
* **hearing date** (`hearing_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the date of the hearing.
* **hearing time** (`hearing_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the time of the hearing.
* **motion status** (`motion_status`, `select_one`, optional):
	+ Select the status of the motion for contempt:
		- Pending
		- Scheduled
		- Completed
* **notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional):
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments about the motion for contempt.

### Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals before filling out the form.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities or experts.
