# Estate Planning Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Authorization Form is a document that grants power to a legal representative to act on behalf of an individual or entity in various legal matters. This form is used to authorize a representative to make decisions, sign documents, and take actions on behalf of the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's identifier (ID) to uniquely identify the client.
2. The client's signature is not required but is recommended for authentication.
3. Enter the legal representative's identifier (ID) to uniquely identify them.
4. The legal representative's signature is not required but is recommended for authentication.
5. Enter the legal representative's name and address as it appears on file.
6. Select the type of authorization being granted (Estate Planning, Living Will, Trust, Power of Attorney, or Advance Directive).
7. Enter the date the authorization takes effect.
8. If applicable, enter the expiration date of the authorization.
9. Enter the client's date of birth, address, city, state, and zip code.
10. Enter the legal representative's address, city, state, and zip code.
11. Enter the legal representative's phone number and email (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Estate Planning Authorization Form Fields** (`id: 1`): This is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Client Identifier** (`id: 2`): Enter a unique identifier for the client to help us track their information.

* **Legal Representative Identifier** (`id: 4`): Enter a unique identifier for the legal representative to help us track their information.
* **Legal Representative Signature** (`id: 5`): A signature from the legal representative is not required but recommended for authentication.
* **Legal Representative Name** (`id: 6`): Enter the legal representative's name and address as it appears on file.
