# Judge Agreement And Confidentiality Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Judge Agreement and Confidentiality Form is a legal document that outlines the terms of confidentiality for a court case. It is essential for the parties involved to understand their responsibilities regarding sensitive information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Judge Agreement And Confidentiality Form**: This form is intended for judges to agree to confidentiality terms for a court case. Please ensure you have the necessary authority to access this form.
2. **Judge**: Enter your name as the judge in charge of the case.
3. **Agreement Date**: Enter the date on which you agreed to the confidentiality terms.
4. **Court Number**: Enter the number of the court where the case is being heard.
5. **Court Level**: Select the type of court where the case is being heard:
	* Supreme Court
	* Federal Court
	* District Court
	* Bankruptcy Court
	* Other (please specify)
6. **Confidentiality Agreement**: Select whether the case is under a confidentiality agreement:
	* True
	* False
7. **Agreement Terms**: Provide a brief description of the confidentiality terms agreed upon.
8. **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information for future reference.
9. **Confidentiality Terms**: Provide a detailed description of the confidentiality terms, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Judge**: Your name as the judge in charge of the case.
* **Agreement Date**: The date on which you agreed to the confidentiality terms.
* **Court Number**: The number of the court where the case is being heard.
* **Court Level**: The type of court where the case is being heard.
* **Confidentiality Agreement**: A yes/no indicator of the presence of a confidentiality agreement.
* **Agreement Terms**: A brief description of the confidentiality terms agreed upon.
* **Contact Details**: Your contact information for future reference.
* **Confidentiality Terms**: A detailed description of the confidentiality terms, if any.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the necessary authority to access this form.
* Be accurate and truthful in your answers.
* This form is for case management purposes only.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the court administration.
