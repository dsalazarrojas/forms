# Transactional Licensing Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a transactional licensing program application form. It is designed for businesses to apply for a license that enables specific usage rights. The form collects necessary information to process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Make sure to select the correct license type and dates.
3. Enter your contact number, organization name, and email.
4. Confirm the license fee.
5. Choose your payment method.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Sign the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and other details as requested.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, required): Enter your business contact number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your business email.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of license you are applying for.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of the license period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of the license period.
* **Usage Right** (`usage_right`, `text`, required): Describe the specific usage rights you need the license for.
* **License Fee** (`license_fee`, `number`, required): Confirm the license fee.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you will pay for the license.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `note`, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form to confirm your application.
* **Review Comment** (`review_comment`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, optional): Choose to submit or save as draft.
* **Confirm License Fee** (`confirm_license_fee`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether the license fee is correct.
* **Confirm License Fee Options** (True/False, required): Confirm that the license fee is correct.
Note: If you select "True", the license fee will be applied as is. If you select "False", you will be asked to correct the license fee.
