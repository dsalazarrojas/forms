# Pro Bono Legal Assistance Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about cases and individuals seeking legal assistance. The form helps intake providers gather relevant data, which is then used to provide effective and targeted support to those in need.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to understand the categories and questions.
2. Answer each section accurately and completely.
3. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the required label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Individual Information** (`individual_info`, text, Optional): Enter general information about the individual seeking legal assistance, such as their name and any other identifying details.
* **Case Information** (`case_info`, text, Optional): Provide a brief summary of the case, including any relevant background or context.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, Optional): List the contact information of the individual seeking legal assistance, such as phone number and email.
* **Contact Person Information** (`contact_person_info`, text, Optional): If applicable, list contact information for a specific contact person, such as their name and phone number.
* **Legal Information** (`legal_info`, text, Optional): Enter any relevant legal details or background information about the case.
* **Case Status** (`case_status`, select_one, Optional): Select the current status of the case (Open, Closed, or In Progress).
* **Priority** (`priority`, number, Optional): Enter a priority level for the case, if applicable.
* **Date of Intake** (`intake_date`, date, Optional): Enter the date the case was initiated.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, Optional): Enter the last date the case was updated.
* **Legal Specialties** (`legal_specialties`, select_multiple, Optional): Choose any applicable legal specialties relevant to the case (Family Law, Civil Law, Labor Law, or Criminal Law).
* **Legal Areas** (`legal_areas`, select_one, Optional): Select the legal areas applicable to the case (Family Law, Civil Law, Labor Law, or Criminal Law).
* **Legal Document** (`legal_document`, select_one, Optional): Indicate whether a legal document is required or not.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the individual seeking legal assistance.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the individual seeking legal assistance.
* **Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter the address of the individual seeking legal assistance.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, text, Optional): Enter the case number, if applicable.
* **Attorney** (`attorney`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the attorney, if applicable.
* **Court** (`court`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the court, if applicable.
* **Intake Provider** (`intake_provider`, text, Optional): Enter the name of the intake provider, if applicable.
* **Intake Date/Time** (`intake_date_time`, time, Optional): Enter the date and time of the intake, if applicable.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, time, Optional): Enter the last update time, if applicable.
