# Participation Expectation Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides step-by-step instructions on how to complete and understand the Participation Expectation Agreement Form.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your relevant information, ensuring you fill out all required fields. The form is split into different sections, each with specific fields that we explain below.
2.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your first name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to capture your last name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your job title or position.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your official email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your company or organization name.
*   **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the team, such as Manager, Team Member, or Customer Service.
*   **Manager** (`manager`, `select_multiple`, optional): If applicable, check whether you have a manager or are a manager.
*   **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you agree to the terms of service.
*   **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, `date`, optional): If applicable, enter the date your agreement might terminate.
*   **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, `text`, optional): If applicable, provide a reason for termination.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your agreement or participation.
*   **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your understanding and acceptance of the terms of service, selecting 'Yes', 'No', or 'Not Applicable' as needed.
*   **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, `text`, required): Summarize the terms of your service or agreement, which you must sign off on.
*   **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required): Enter the date you sign this agreement.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Place your signature here, indicating your consent.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, `text`, required): If you're an employee, place your signature to confirm your agreement.
*   **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, `text`, required): If you're a manager, place your signature to confirm your agreement with your employee.
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name if you're a manager.
*   **Team Lead** (`team_lead`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your team lead's name.
*   **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your team lead's phone number.
*   **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your team lead's email address.
*   **Team Lead Designation** (`team_lead_designation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm whether your team lead is 'Yes' or 'No'.
*   **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the terms of service by selecting 'I agree', 'I do not agree', or 'I do not agree at all'.
