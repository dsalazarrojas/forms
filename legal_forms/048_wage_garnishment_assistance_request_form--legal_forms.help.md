# Wage Garnishment Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request assistance with wage garnishment issues. It is intended to provide relevant information to help us understand the situation and provide guidance on the next steps.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form and ensure that all relevant information is provided.
2. Select the correct options for each field based on your situation.
3. Provide as much detail as possible in the free text fields.
4. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide your name and contact information.
	+ Enter your name and any relevant contact information you would like us to use to reach out to you.
* **Wage Garnishment Issue** (`wage_garnishment_issue`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Identify the nature of the wage garnishment issue.
	+ Select "Yes" if you are currently experiencing a wage garnishment issue.
* **Current Garnishment Status** (`current_garnishment_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Indicate the current status of your wage garnishment.
	+ Select "Active" if the garnishment is currently active.
* **Financial Aid Clinic Name** (`financial_aid_clinic_name`, `text`, required: `false`): If you are working with a financial aid clinic, enter the name of the clinic.
	+ If applicable, enter the name of the financial aid clinic assisting you.
* **Case Number** (`case_number`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the case number associated with your wage garnishment.
	+ Enter the case number if applicable.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of your employer.
	+ Enter the name of your employer.
* **Employer Address** (`employer_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of your employer.
	+ Enter the address of your employer.
* **Employer Address Line 2** (`employer_address_line2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional information about your employer's address.
	+ Enter any additional information about your employer's address.
* **Employee Id** (`employee_id`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your employee ID number.
	+ Enter your employee ID number.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name.
	+ Enter your name.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your address.
	+ Enter your address.
* **Employee City** (`employee_city`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the city where you reside.
	+ Enter the city where you reside.
* **Employee State** (`employee_state`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the state where you reside.
	+ Enter the state where you reside.
* **Employee Zip** (`employee_zip`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your zip code.
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Layoff** (`date_of_layoff`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date you were laid off.
	+ Enter the date you were laid off.
* **Last Paydate** (`last_paydate`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the date of your last payment.
	+ Enter the date of your last payment.
* **Garnishment Amount** (`garnishment_amount`, `number`, required: `false`): Enter the amount being garnished.
	+ Enter the amount being garnished.
* **Garnishment Frequency** (`garnishment_frequency`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Indicate how often garnishment occurs.
	+ Select "Weekly", "Bi-Weekly", or "Monthly" based on how often garnishment occurs.
* **Garnishment Start Date** (`garnishment_startdate`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the start date of garnishment.
	+ Enter the start date of garnishment.
* **Garnishment End Date** (`garnishment_enddate`, `date`, required: `false`): Enter the end date of garnishment.
	+ Enter the end date of garnishment.
* **Garnishment Notes** (`garnishment_notes`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any additional notes regarding garnishment.
	+ Enter any additional notes about garnishment.
* **Financial Institutions Name** (`financial_institutions_name`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the financial institution involved.
	+ Enter the name of the financial institution involved.
* **Financial Institutions Address** (`financial_institutions_address`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the financial institution.
	+ Enter the address of the financial institution.
* **Financial Institutions Phone** (`financial_institutions_phone`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the phone number of the financial institution.
	+ Enter the phone number of the financial institution.

Please note that some of the fields may not be applicable to your situation, so only provide the information that is relevant to you.
