# Community Mediation Recording Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Mediation Recording Consent Form is designed to collect information from participants in a mediation session about their consent to be recorded. This form is used to inform participants about their rights, the purpose of recording, and the allowed uses of the recordings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the consent section carefully to understand your rights and the allowed uses of recordings.
2.  Fill out the required fields: Participant name, Role in mediation, Consent to record, Permitted uses of recordings, Confidentiality agreement acknowledgement, and Signature name.
3.  Provide your signature date and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant name**: This field requests your full legal name.
*   **Role in mediation**: Select your role in the mediation session as Mediator, Participant, or Observer.
*   **Consent to record**: Choose whether you agree or do not agree to be recorded during the session.
*   **Permitted uses of recordings**: Select the allowed uses of recordings. These options are Case review, Training, and Confidential archive. You can also choose "Other" for custom use.
*   **Confidentiality agreement acknowledgement**: Confirm that you understand the confidentiality agreement.
*   **Rights to withdraw consent**: This is an optional field where you can express your intention to withdraw consent. However, it's not a required field.
*   **Signature name**: Provide your full name to sign the consent form.
*   **Signature date**: Enter the date of your signature.
*   **Contact for questions**: Provide an email address to contact about any questions or concerns.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields.
*   Review the permitted uses of recordings carefully to ensure you understand how your recordings will be used.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the provided contact email address.
