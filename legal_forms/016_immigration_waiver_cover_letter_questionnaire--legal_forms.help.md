# Immigration Waiver Cover Letter Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide supporting documentation for immigration waiver requests. This information will be used to facilitate the review and processing of your waiver application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section with your First name, Last name, and Email address.
2. Indicate whether you have uploaded supporting documents, such as a passport, I-20 or I-94.
3. Choose the status of your waiver request from the options provided.
4. If you have any supporting documents, they can be uploaded through the form.
5. Fill in the submission date of your waiver request.
6. Add any additional notes about your waiver request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Supporting documents** (5, select_multiple, optional): Check the box if you have uploaded supporting documents such as a passport, I-20 or I-94.
* **Waiver status** (6, select_one, optional): Choose the status of your waiver request from the options provided.
* **Submission date** (7, date, optional): Enter the date you submitted your waiver request.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details about your waiver request.
