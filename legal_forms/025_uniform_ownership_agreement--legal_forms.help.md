# Uniform Ownership Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Uniform Ownership Agreement form is designed to capture the terms of ownership between two or more parties in a legal agreement. This form outlines the percentage of ownership for each partner, their roles, and the terms agreed upon.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Enter the title of the agreement**: In the "agreement_title" field, provide a brief and descriptive title for the agreement.
2. **Enter partner 1 details**: Fill in the "partner_1_name", "partner_1_percentage", and "partner_1_role" fields with the relevant information for the first partner.
3. **Enter partner 2 details**: Fill in the "partner_2_name", "partner_2_percentage", and "partner_2_role" fields with the relevant information for the second partner.
4. **Agree to terms**: If applicable, add any additional terms or conditions in the "ownership_terms_agreed" field.
5. **Sign and date the agreement**: Enter the date and time you agreed to the terms in the "date_agreed" and "time_agreed" fields.
6. **Assign agreement details**: Enter the person or department assigned to this agreement in the "assigned_to" and "assigned_by" fields.
7. **Agreement version and status**: Enter the version of the agreement in the "agreement_version" field and select the current status of the agreement from the dropdown menu in the "agreement_status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Title**: The title of the agreement (agreement_title)
* **Partner 1**: Enter the name of the first partner (partner_1_name)
* **Partner 1 Percentage**: The percentage of ownership for the first partner (partner_1_percentage)
* **Partner 1 Role**: The role of the first partner (partner_1_role)
* **Partner 2**: Enter the name of the second partner (partner_2_name)
* **Partner 2 Percentage**: The percentage of ownership for the second partner (partner_2_percentage)
* **Partner 2 Role**: The role of the second partner (partner_2_role)
* **Ownership Terms Agreed**: Any additional terms or conditions agreed upon (ownership_terms_agreed)
* **Date Agreed**: The date the agreement was signed (date_agreed)
* **Time Agreed**: The time the agreement was signed (time_agreed)
* **Assigned To**: The person or department assigned to this agreement (assigned_to)
* **Assigned By**: The person or department that assigned this agreement (assigned_by)
* **Agreement Version**: The version of the agreement (agreement_version)
* **Agreement Date**: The date the agreement was created (agreement_date)
* **Agreement Time**: The time the agreement was created (agreement_time)
* **Agreement Status**: The status of the agreement (agreement_status)
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the agreement (notes)
* **Agreed By Party**: The person or department that agreed to the terms (agreed_by_party)
