# Non Compete Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an employee's non-competitive agreement status, ensuring that they do not have any agreements that could conflict with the company's interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information, including:
	* `employer_id`
	* `name`
	* `start_date`
	* `end_date`
	* `employee_id`
	* `email`
	* `employee_signature`
	* `employer_signature`
2. If applicable, fill in the optional information:
	* `job_title` (not required, but recommended for context)
	* `non_compete_agreement` (select one or more options that apply)
	* `phone` (not required, but recommended for contact purposes)
	* `non_compete_clause` (optional notes about the agreement)
	* `non_compete_termination` (optional notes about the termination)
3. Select the correct dates and times for the sign_date, sign_time, and submitted fields.
4. Optionally, fill in the ip_address, user_ip, employee_ip, non_compete_ip, non_compete_employer_ip fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### employer_id
* **employer_id** (text, required): Enter the ID of the employer.
* **name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **job_title** (text, optional): Enter your job title for context.
* **non_compete_agreement** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more options that apply to your non-competitive agreement status.
* **start_date** (date, required): Enter the start date of your non-competitive agreement.
* **end_date** (date, required): Enter the end date of your non-competitive agreement.
* **employee_id** (text, required): Enter your employee ID.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **non_compete_clause** (text, optional): Add any notes about your non-competitive agreement.
* **non_compete_termination** (text, optional): Add any notes about the termination of your non-competitive agreement.
* **sign_date** (date, required): Enter the date you signed the agreement.
* **sign_time** (time, required): Enter the time you signed the agreement.
* **location** (text, optional): Enter the location where you signed the agreement.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes about the agreement.
* **employee_signature** (text, required): Sign your name as an employee.
* **employer_signature** (text, required): Sign your name as an employer.
* **date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the agreement (if different from sign_date).
* **time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the agreement (if different from sign_time).
* **ip_address** (text, optional): Enter the IP address where you signed the agreement.
* **user_ip** (text, optional): Enter the IP address of the user who submitted the agreement.
* **employee_ip** (text, optional): Enter the IP address of the employee who submitted the agreement.
* **non_compete_ip** (text, optional): Enter the IP address of the non-competitive agreement.
* **non_compete_employer_ip** (text, optional): Enter the IP address of the employer who submitted the agreement.
* **submitted** (date, optional): Enter the date and time the agreement was submitted.

Note: Some fields are optional and are only required for specific circumstances.
