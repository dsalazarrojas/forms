# Litigation Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the release of litigation records, ensuring that all parties involved are informed and records are properly secured. It is essential for legal professionals, clients, and other relevant parties to understand and fill out this form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of legal professional involved in the litigation case (Attorney, Paralegal, Litigation Consultant, or Other).
2. Provide the client's name and email address (if applicable).
3. Enter the date the records were released.
4. Choose the recipient of the release (Attorney, Law Firm, Client, or Other).
5. Describe the records being released.
6. Provide any additional notes regarding the release.
7. Enter the client's email address (if different from the one provided earlier).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Professional** (`legal_professional`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of legal professional involved in the litigation case.
* **Client** (`client`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the client (if applicable).
* **Date Released** (`date_released`, date, required: false): Enter the date the records were released.
* **Release To** (`release_to`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the recipient of the release (Attorney, Law Firm, Client, or Other).
* **Records Released** (`records_released`, text, required: false): Describe the records being released.
* **Reason Released** (`reason_released`, text, required: false): Provide the reason for releasing the records.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes regarding the release.
* **Email Client** (`email_client`, email, required: false): Enter the client's email address (if different from the one provided earlier).
