# Legal Documentation Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Legal Documentation Assessment form is used to collect and manage information about legal documents. This form helps ensure accuracy and consistency in documentation, making it easier for legal professionals to create, manage, and store documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To start, ensure you have all the necessary information about the document you are creating or managing. This may include details about the document type, date, and location.

1. Enter the **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** of the person involved in the document.
2. Enter the **Phone Number** and **Address** of the person or organization associated with the document.
3. Select the correct **Document Type** from the provided options. This will help categorize and identify the document accurately.
4. Enter the **Document Date**, **Document Number**, and **Document Location** to provide further context.
5. Enter a brief **Document Description** to describe the document's purpose or content.
6. Select whether the document was **Witnessed By** someone. This helps establish the document's authenticity.
7. Leave a note in the **Signature** field, which is not required but can be useful for additional comments or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, not required): Enter the first name of the person involved in the document.
* **Last Name** (text, not required): Enter the last name of the person involved in the document.
* **Email** (email, not required): Enter the email address of the person or organization associated with the document.
* **Phone Number** (text, not required): Enter the phone number of the person or organization associated with the document.
* **Address** (text, not required): Enter the address of the person or organization associated with the document.
* **Document Type** (select_one, required): Select the correct category for the document from the provided options (Will, Living Will, Other, Power of Attorney, Trust, Other (please specify)).
* **Document Date** (date, not required): Enter the date the document was created or signed.
* **Document Number** (number, not required): Enter a unique identifier for the document.
* **Document Location** (text, not required): Enter the location where the document was created or signed.
* **Document Description** (text, not required): Enter a brief description of the document's purpose or content.
* **Witnessed By** (select_multiple, required): Select whether the document was witnessed by someone.
* **Signature** (note, not required): Leave a note in this field for additional comments or clarification.
