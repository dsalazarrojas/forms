# Bail Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bail Application Form is a tool used to gather information for a bail request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form to understand each field and its purpose.
2. Ensure you are filling in the correct and accurate information for each field.
3. If any field is not applicable to the defendant's situation, leave it blank or note a brief explanation for the judge.
4. Fill out the form completely and accurately to avoid any issues with the bail request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Defendant Full Name** (1, Defendant Full Name, required): Enter the full legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Defendant Date of Birth** (2, Defendant Date of Birth, required): Enter the date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Defendant Current Address** (3, Defendant Address, required): Enter the full street address, city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Defendant Phone Number** (4, Defendant Phone Number, required): Enter the primary contact number.
* **Defendant Email Address** (5, Defendant Email Address, required): Enter an active email address for court notifications.
* **Arresting Agency** (6, Arresting Agency, required): Enter the police department or agency that made the arrest.
* **Date of Arrest** (7, Date of Arrest, required): Enter the date when the defendant was taken into custody.
* **Charges Filed** (8, Charges Filed, required): List all criminal charges.
* **Case Number** (9, Case Number, required): Enter the court docket or case reference number.
* **Bail Amount Requested** (10, Bail Amount Requested, required): Enter the proposed bail amount in dollars.
* **Type of Bail Requested** (11, Type of Bail Requested, required): Choose the preferred bail arrangement.
* **Employment Status** (12, Employment Status, required): Choose the current employment situation.
* **Employer Name** (13, Employer Name, not required): Enter the current employer or most recent if unemployed.
* **Monthly Income** (14, Monthly Income, not required): Enter the gross monthly income before taxes.
* **Prior Criminal History** (15, Prior Criminal History, required): Choose any previous arrests or convictions.
* **Flight Risk Assessment** (16, Flight Risk Assessment, required): Choose factors that may indicate flight risk.
* **Community Ties** (17, Community Ties, required): Choose connections to the local community.
* **Proposed Release Conditions** (18, Proposed Release Conditions, not required): Choose conditions the defendant agrees to follow.
* **Attorney Name** (19, Attorney Name, required): Enter the legal representative handling the case.
* **Attorney Contact Information** (20, Attorney Contact Information, required): Enter the phone number and email.
* **Additional Comments** (21, Additional Comments, not required): Enter any other relevant information for the court.

**Tips**

* Double-check the form for accuracy and completeness before submitting.
* If unsure about a field, seek guidance from the court or a representative.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
