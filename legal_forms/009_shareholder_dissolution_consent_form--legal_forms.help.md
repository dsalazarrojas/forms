# Shareholder Dissolution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather shareholder consent for the dissolution of a company. It is intended for use by the company's administrators or legal representatives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have read and understood the purpose of the form before proceeding.
* Complete all the required fields (marked as required) before submitting the form.
* For non-required fields, you can provide additional information or details if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): This field is for the official name of the company that is being dissolved.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): This field is for the address of the company's registered office or main location.
* **Date of Dissolution** (`date_of_dissolution`, `date`, required): This field is for the date on which the company will be dissolved.
* **Reason for Dissolution** (`reason_for_dissolution`, `text`, required): This field is for the reason(s) why the company is being dissolved.
* **Shareholder Consent** (`shareholder_consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Select 'Yes' if you consent to the dissolution of the company, or 'No' if you do not. You can also provide an 'Other' reason for your consent in the **Shareholder Consent Other Reason** field.
* **Shareholder Consent Other Reason** (`shareholder_consent_other_reason`, `text`, required): If you selected 'No' for Shareholder Consent, please provide a reason for your refusal in this field.
* **Company Status** (`company_status`, `select_one`, required): Select the current status of the company (Active, Dissolved, or Inactive).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required): This field is for any additional comments or explanations you may have.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the company's administrators or legal representatives.
* Once you have completed and submitted the form, please note that the company's dissolution process will proceed accordingly.
