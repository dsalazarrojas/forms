# Request A Legal Consultation Quote - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request a legal consultation quote from our legal experts. To get a quote, please fill out the following information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Legal Consultation Quote" text field with a brief description of your consultation request.
2. Enter your name in the "Attorney Name" field as we will need to know who to contact for further consultation.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field to enable easy communication.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field to receive updates on your consultation.
5. If you require an urgent consultation, select "Yes" for "Urgency Priority".
6. Choose a consultation date from the date picker or input manually in the "Consultation Date" field.
7. Select your preferred consultation time in the "Consultation Time" field.
8. In the "Special Requests" text field, mention any specific questions or requests you may have for the consultation.
9. Enter the Submission Form ID in the "Submission Form ID" field if you have already submitted a similar request.
10. The other fields are for internal use and can be filled out by the system administrators.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Consultation Quote** (`legal_consultation_details`, text, required): A brief description of your consultation request.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, optional): The name of the attorney who will be handling your consultation.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your contact number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address where you can receive updates on your consultation.
* **Urgency Priority** (`priority`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you require an urgent consultation.
* **Consultation Date** (`consultation_date`, date, optional): Choose or input the date for the consultation.
* **Consultation Time** (`consultation_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred time for the consultation.
* **Special Requests** (`message`, note, optional): Mention any specific questions or requests you have for the consultation.
* **Submission Form ID** (`submission_form_id`, text, optional): ID of the submission if you have already submitted a similar request.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, text, optional): System generated timestamp for record keeping.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, text, optional): System generated timestamp for record keeping.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, text, optional): System generated timestamp for record keeping.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, text, optional): System generated timestamp for record keeping.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): System generated identifier for the form.
* **Submission ID** (`submission_id`, text, optional): System generated identifier for the submission.
* **JotForm Page ID** (`jotform_page_id`, text, optional): System generated identifier for the JotForm page.
