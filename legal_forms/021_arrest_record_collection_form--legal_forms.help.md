> # Arrest Record Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about an individual's arrest record, including their name, contact information, and history of arrests, convictions, and record status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the individual's name as it appears on official documents.
2. Enter the date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
3. Enter the individual's current address.
4. Enter the individual's contact phone number.
5. Enter the individual's email address (if applicable).
6. Select the correct answer for the "Were you convicted" question, and provide the date of conviction or final disposition if applicable.
7. Enter the sentence or disposition of the conviction.
8. Indicate whether the record has been sealed or expunged, and provide the date of sealing or expungement if applicable.
9. Indicate whether there are any active warrants.
10. Certify that the information provided is accurate and complete.
11. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: As it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Social Security Number**: Enter the individual's social security number.
* **Current Address**: Enter the individual's current address.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the individual's contact phone number.
* **Email Address**: Enter the individual's email address (if applicable).
* **Were you convicted**: Select "True" if the individual was convicted, "False" if they were not convicted, "Charges dismissed" if charges were dismissed, or "Plea deal" if the individual entered a plea deal.
* **Date of Conviction or Final Disposition**: Enter the date of conviction or final disposition.
* **Sentence or Disposition**: Enter the sentence or disposition of the conviction.
* **Has the record been sealed or expunged**: Select "Yes, sealed", "Yes, expunged", "False", or "Pending".
* **Date of Sealing or Expungement**: Enter the date of sealing or expungement (if applicable).
* **Are there any active warrants**: Select "False" if there are no active warrants, "True" if there are active warrants, or "Unknown" if you are unsure.
* **I certify this information is accurate and complete**: Select "Yes, I certify" if the information is accurate and complete, "False" if it is not.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date you are signing the form.
* **Location of First Arrest**: Enter the location of the first arrest in the format "City, State".
* **Charge or Charges**: Enter the type of offense(s) charged.
* **Arresting Agency**: Enter the arresting agency (e.g. police department, sheriff, etc.).
* **Case Number**: Enter the case number (if applicable).
* **Number of Arrests**: Enter the number of arrests.
* **Date of First Arrest**: Enter the date of the first arrest.
* **Active Warrants**: Select "False" if there are no active warrants, "True" if there are active warrants, or "Unknown" if you are unsure.
