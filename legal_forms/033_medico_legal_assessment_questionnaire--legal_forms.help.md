# Medico Legal Assessment Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Medico Legal Assessment Questionnaire is a form used to collect information from medical professionals for legal purposes. This form is used to assess the medical status of an individual, providing necessary details for legal proceedings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
3. Provide your Contact Details, including your phone number, address, and email.
4. Select your contact relationship (Spouse, Partner, Friend, or Other), and if Other, provide additional details.
5. Enter your Legal Organisation and Legal Case Number.
6. Identify the Medico Legal Assessor who will be assessing your medical status.
7. Enter the Date Assessment was conducted.
8. Enter the Date of Submission of the form.
9. Enter your name and contact information as the Submitter.
10. Indicate whether you consent to the assessment and provide any additional comments if necessary.
11. Confirm your signature by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' and provide comments if necessary.
12. Finally, sign the form with your name and enter the date.
