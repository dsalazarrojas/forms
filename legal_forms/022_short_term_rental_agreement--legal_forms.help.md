# Short Term Rental Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Term Rental Agreement is a document used to outline the terms and conditions of a short-term rental or lease between a landlord and a tenant. It is essential to have this agreement in place to ensure that both parties are on the same page and to prevent any potential disputes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you have provided the correct information.
3. Sign and date the agreement once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the landlord who is renting out the property.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter the name of the tenant who is renting the property.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the start date of the rental or lease period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Please enter the end date of the rental or lease period.
* **Rent Amount** (`rent_amount`, number, required/optional): Please enter the amount of rent that will be paid per month or year.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required/optional): Please enter any additional payment terms or conditions, such as method of payment or due date.
* **Security Deposit** (`security_deposit`, number, required/optional): Please enter the amount of security deposit that must be paid.
* **Utilities** (`utilities`, text, required/optional): Please list any additional utilities or fees that will be included in the rent.
* **Lease Duration** (`lease_duration`, number, required/optional): Please enter the length of the rental or lease period in months or years.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the agreement carefully before signing.
* Both parties should keep a copy of the agreement for their records.
* This form is not a substitute for professional legal advice.
* Consult with a lawyer if you have any questions or concerns about the agreement.
