# Liability and Terms Agreement Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant and instructor acknowledgement of the waiver and liability terms of an activity or program. It is intended to promote transparency and accountability by ensuring that all parties involved are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the participant's name and contact details in pages 1 and 2.
* Select the correct waiver and liability terms in page 3 (waiver_consent) to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms or have not read, understand, and do not agree to the terms.
* Review the terms and conditions in page 4 (terms_and_conditions).
* Sign the participant's signature (page 11) and instructor's acknowledgement (page 12) to acknowledge that you are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.
* Review the waiver terms in pages 6-8 (waiver_term_1, waiver_term_2, and waiver_term_3).
* Select the correct acknowledgement option in page 10 (participant_acknowledgement) to indicate that you are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.
* Select the correct acknowledgement option in page 9 (review_and_accept) to indicate that you have read, understand, and agree to the waiver and liability terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (page 1):
	+ This field is for the participant's name.
* **Participant Contact Details** (page 2):
	+ This field is for the participant's contact information.
* **Waiver Consent** (page 3):
	+ This field is to select whether you have read, understand, and agree to the waiver and liability terms or have not read, understand, and do not agree to the terms.
* **Terms and Conditions** (page 4):
	+ This is a note field to review the waiver and liability terms of the activity.
* **Participant Signature** (page 5):
	+ This field is for the participant's signature, acknowledging that they are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.
* **Waiver Term 1** (page 6):
	+ This field is for the first waiver term.
* **Waiver Term 2** (page 7):
	+ This field is for the second waiver term.
* **Waiver Term 3** (page 8):
	+ This field is for the third waiver term.
* **Review and Accept** (page 9):
	+ This field is to select whether you have read, understand, and agree to the waiver and liability terms or have not read, understand, and do not agree to the terms.
* **Acknowledgement of Liability** (page 10):
	+ This field is to select whether you are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity or are not aware of the risks.
* **Instructor Acknowledgement** (page 11):
	+ This field is to sign as the instructor, acknowledging that you are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.
* **Participation Signature** (page 12):
	+ This field is the participant's signature, acknowledging that they are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the waiver and liability terms carefully before completing this form.
* Ensure that you understand the risks associated with participating in the activity.
* Sign and date the participant's signature and instructor acknowledgement fields to confirm that you are aware of the risks associated with participating in the activity.
* Keep a record of completed forms for reference and liability purposes.
