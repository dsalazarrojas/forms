# Criminal Defense Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Criminal Defense Intake Form is designed to collect essential information about your case, enabling your defense attorney to provide you with the most effective representation possible.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your Case Number (Page 1).
- Enter your Client Name (Page 2).
- Provide your Contact Information (Reporter and Defendant) (Page 3-4).
- Enter your Client Address (Page 5).
- Select the Offense Charges that apply to your case (Page 6).
- If applicable, provide the name of your Defense Attorney (Page 7).
- Record the Court Date and Court Time (Page 8-9).
- If applicable, enter any Pre-arranged Notes (Page 10).
- Provide the Court Location (Page 11).
- Enter your Client Statement and Attorney Statement (Page 12-13).
- Attach any Police Reports and Other Documents (Page 14-15).
- List any Witnesses (Page 16).
- Record your Client Fees (Page 17).
- Enter any additional Notes (Page 18).
- Select the Case Status (Page 19).
- Enter the Court Date (Page 20).
- Enter Defense Notes (Page 21).
- Enter Trial Date (Page 22).
- Select the Jury Decision (Page 23).
- Finally, enter your Jury Deliberations Brief (Page 24).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Case Number** (Page 1): A unique identifier for your case, ensuring all documentation is accurately linked to your case.
- **Client Name** (Page 2): Your name as the individual in question.
- **Contact Reporter** (Page 3): The contact information for the reporter who may have information about your case.
- **Contact Defendant** (Page 4): Your contact information.
- **Client Address** (Page 5): Your address for correspondence and visits from your defense attorney.
- **Offense Charges** (Page 6): The specific charges against you, which you can select from the provided options.
- **Defense Attorney** (Page 7): The name of your defense attorney, if you have one.
- **Court Date** (Page 8): The date of the court hearing, if scheduled.
- **Court Time** (Page 9): The scheduled time of the court hearing.
- **Pre-arranged Notes** (Page 10): Any special arrangements or requests you have regarding your case.
- **Court Location** (Page 11): The location of the court where you will be appearing.
- **Client Statement** (Page 12): A personal statement about your side of the case.
- **Attorney Statement** (Page 13): A statement from your defense attorney about their strategy and plans.
- **Police Report** (Page 14): Any relevant police reports or evidence.
- **Other Documents** (Page 15): Any other supporting documents for your case.
- **Witness List** (Page 16): A list of people who may be able to testify on your behalf.
- **Client Fees** (Page 17): Any fees you have paid for legal services.
- **Notes** (Page 18): Additional comments or reminders for your defense attorney.
- **Case Status** (Page 19): The current status of your case, which is one of Open, Pending, or Closed.
- **Court Date 2** (Page 20): Another court date, if scheduled.
- **Defense Notes** (Page 21): Any important notes from your defense attorney.
- **Trial Date** (Page 22): The date of the trial, if scheduled.
- **Jury Decision** (Page 23): The outcome of your case, if resolved by a jury.
- **Jury Deliberations Brief** (Page 24): A note to the jury about your case.
