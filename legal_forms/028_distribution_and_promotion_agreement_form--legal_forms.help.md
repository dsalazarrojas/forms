# Distribution And Promotion Agreement Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for distribution and promotion purposes. It captures business details and agreement information for both the business and the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the **Distribution And Promotion Agreement Form** page with the required information.
2. On this page, you will find fields for business details, agreement file upload, and signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution And Promotion Agreement Form (`distribution_and_promotion_agreement_form`, text, optional)**:
	+ This field is the title of the form, which should be self-explanatory.
* **Business Details (`business_details`, text, optional)**:
	+ Enter the details about your business, such as its name and address.
* **Agreement File (`agreement_file`, text, optional)**:
	+ Upload the agreement file related to the distribution and promotion.
* **Signature - Business (`signature_business`, text, optional)**:
	+ Sign this field to confirm that the business has read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Signature - Agreement (`signature_agreement`, text, optional)**:
	+ Sign this field to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms of the agreement.
* **Business Signature (`business_signature`, text, optional)**:
	+ Enter the signature of the business representative.
* **Date Signed - Business (`date_signed_business`, date, optional)**:
	+ Enter the date the agreement was signed by the business.
* **Date Signed - Agreement (`date_signed_agreement`, date, optional)**:
	+ Enter the date the agreement was signed.
* **Email - Business (`email_business`, email, optional)**:
	+ Enter the email address of the business representative.
* **Email - Agreement (`email_agreement`, email, optional)**:
	+ Enter the email address where the agreement can be sent.
* **Phone - Business (`phone_business`, text, optional)**:
	+ Enter the phone number of the business representative.
* **Phone - Agreement (`phone_agreement`, text, optional)**:
	+ Enter the phone number where the agreement can be contacted.

## Tips
- Be sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Ensure that you're providing accurate and complete information.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with the relevant department or person before proceeding.
