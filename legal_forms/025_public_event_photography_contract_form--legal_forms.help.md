# Public Event Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the details of a public event where photography services will be provided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Name field with the name of the event.
2. Enter the name of the person responsible for the photography services in the Photographer field.
3. Enter the date and time of the event in the Event Date and Event Time fields.
4. Enter the name of the person or organization that has hired you for the event in the Client Name field.
5. Enter the email address of the client in the Client EMail field.
6. Enter the phone number of the client in the Client Phone field.
7. If you will be rendering any services during the event, select "Yes" in the Services Rendered field.
8. Enter the location where the event will take place in the Location field.
9. Enter any additional details about the event in the Event Details field.
10. Sign the contract by entering your name in the Contract Signed By field.
11. Date and time of signing the contract in the Contract Signed On and Contract Signed At fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): The name of the event where you will be providing photography services.
* **Photographer** (`photographer_name`, text, required): The name of the person responsible for the photography services.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): The date of the event where you will be providing services.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): The time of the event where you will be providing services.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): The name of the person or organization that has hired you for the event.
* **Client EMail** (`client_email`, email, required): The email address of the client.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): The phone number of the client.
* **Services Rendered** (`services_rendered`, select_multiple, required): Select "Yes" if you will be rendering any services during the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): The location where the event will take place.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about the event.
* **Contract Signed By** (`contract_signed_by`, text, required): Sign the contract by entering your name.
* **Contract Signed On** (`contract_signed_on`, date, required): Date of signing the contract.
* **Contract Signed At** (`contract_signed_at`, time, required): Time of signing the contract.

Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise noted.
