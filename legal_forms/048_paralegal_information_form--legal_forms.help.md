# Paralegal Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from a paralegal for various legal purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Date of Birth.
4. Provide your Contact Email.
5. Enter your Phone Number.
6. Fill in your Job Title.
7. Select your Experience Level (Junior, Senior, Paralegal).
8. Enter your Available Start Date.
9. Enter your Available End Date.
10. Select your Available Hours (Part-time, Full-time, Flexible).
11. Enter your Salary Range (if applicable).
12. Select your Work Location (On-site, Remote, Hybrid).
13. Enter your Notice Period (if applicable).
14. Fill in your Job Type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Provide your professional email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Experience Level** (select_one, optional): Select your level of experience (Junior, Senior, Paralegal).
* **Available Start Date** (date, optional): Enter your available start date.
* **Available End Date** (date, optional): Enter your available end date.
* **Available Hours** (select_multiple, optional): Select your available hours per week (Part-time, Full-time, Flexible).
* **Salary Range** (number, optional): Enter your current or desired salary range.
* **Work Location** (select_one, optional): Select your desired work location (On-site, Remote, Hybrid).
* **Notice Period** (number, optional): Enter your notice period (if applicable).
* **Job Type** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the job you are looking for or applying for.

Note that some fields may be marked as "required" or "optional". Please fill in all required fields before submitting the form. If a field is marked as "optional," you can leave it blank if not applicable. 

This guide is designed to be helpful and clear for completing the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out.
