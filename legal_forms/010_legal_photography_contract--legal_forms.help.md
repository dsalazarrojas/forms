# Legal Photography Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Photography Contract form is used to document the details of a photography session, including the client's contact information, session specifics, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Select the session date in the "Session Date" field.
3. Select the session time in the "Session Time" field.
4. Enter the location of the session in the "Location" field.
5. Provide information about the photographer in the "Photographer Info" field.
6. Enter the cancellation policies in the "Cancellation Policies" field.
7. Enter any relevant text for the legal agreement in the "Legal Agreement" field.
8. Enter the session length in the "Session Length" field.
9. Specify the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" field.
10. Select the payment plan from the options provided in the "Payment Plan" field.
11. Sign the agreement by clicking the "Signature" field.
12. Date and time of agreement should be entered in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
13. Finally, sign the contract on behalf of the photographer in the "Photographer Signature" field and the client in the "Client Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the client.
* **Session Date** (Date, Required): Select the date of the photography session.
* **Session Time** (Time, Required): Select the time of the photography session.
* **Location** (Text, Required): Enter the location where the photography session will take place.
* **Photographer Info** (Text, Required): Provide any relevant information about the photographer.
* **Cancellation Policies** (Text, Required): Enter the cancellation policies for the photography session.
* **Legal Agreement** (Text, Required): Enter any relevant text for the legal agreement.
* **Session Length** (Number, Required): Enter the length of the photography session.
* **Payment Terms** (Text, Required): Enter the payment terms for the photography session.
* **Payment Plan** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the payment plan from the provided options.
* **Signature** (Note, Required): Sign the agreement on behalf of the photographer.
* **Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of the agreement.
* **Time** (Time, Required): Enter the time of the agreement.
* **Photographer Signature** (Note, Required): Sign the contract on behalf of the photographer.
* **Client Signature** (Note, Required): Sign the contract on behalf of the client.
