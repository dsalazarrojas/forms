# district_court_juror_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The district court juror questionnaire is used to gather information from potential jurors about their availability and suitability to serve on a district court case. This form helps to ensure that jurors are aware of their responsibilities and obligations as a juror.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure to answer all the questions truthfully.
2. For each question, select the most accurate answer from the provided options.
3. Use the notes field to add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your response.
4. Once you have completed the form, review it for accuracy and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Juror Selection Process**: This section is to be filled with information regarding the juror selection process. Please enter a detailed description of the juror selection process.
* **Juror Demographics**: Please select the options that best describe your demographics. This includes 'Yes' or 'No' for the following: *Are you a US citizen?* *Are you a resident of the state or county where the court case is being held?* 
* **Juror Conflicts**: Select 'Yes' if you have a conflict of interest that would prevent you from serving as a juror. A conflict of interest could be a personal or professional relationship with one of the parties involved in the case.
* **Juror Scheduling**: Select 'Yes' if you are able to serve as a juror at the scheduled trial dates. If you are unable to serve, select 'No'.
* **Juror Availability**: Select 'Yes' if you are available to serve as a juror. If you are not available, select 'No'.
* **Juror Committees**: Select 'Yes' if you are able to serve on a jury committee. If you are not able to serve, select 'No'.
* **Juror Committee**: Select 'Yes' if you are available to serve on a jury committee. If you are not available, select 'No'.
* **Important Dates**: Select the dates that are very important to you. This could include family events, work commitments, or other non-negotiable appointments.
* **Juror Notes**: This field is for you to add any additional information that you feel is relevant to your application. Please be as detailed as possible to help the court understand your availability and suitability.
