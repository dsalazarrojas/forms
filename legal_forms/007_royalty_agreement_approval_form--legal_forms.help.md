# Royalty Agreement Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Royalty Agreement Approval Form is used to obtain approval for a royalty agreement from the approver. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured for approval and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information for the form before starting.
2. Fill in the Contract ID.
3. Enter the contract date.
4. Provide your name as the approver.
5. Enter your email address.
6. Select your role.
7. Add any comments if necessary.
8. Check the box to indicate that the agreement has been signed.
9. Enter the agreement date.
10. Enter your name as the submitter.
11. Enter your email address.
12. Enter your phone number.
13. Enter your department.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract ID**: Enter the unique identifier of the contract.
* **Contract Date**: The date of the contract.
* **Approver Name**: Your name as the approver.
* **Approver Email**: Your email address.
* **Approver Role**: Your role in the organization.
* **Approval Date**: The date of approval.
* **Approval Comments**: Add any comments for the approval.
* **Agreement Signed**: Check if the agreement has been signed.
* **Agreement Date**: The date of the agreement.
* **Submitter Name**: Your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email**: Your email address.
* **Submitter Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Submitter Department**: Your department.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the form before submission to ensure all information is correct.
* If any field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the form administrator.
