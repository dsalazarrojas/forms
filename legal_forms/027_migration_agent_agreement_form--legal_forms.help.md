# Migration Agent Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Migration Agent Agreement Form is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of the migration service between a client and a migration agent. It is essential to complete this form carefully to ensure both parties understand their responsibilities and obligations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the migration service.
2. Complete the Client Details section with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself.
3. Confirm your consent to various communication methods, such as email, SMS, and postal mail.
4. Review and confirm the Service Terms Agreement, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions of the migration service.
5. Check the agreement and confirm that you have reviewed it thoroughly.
6. Provide a signature and signature date, indicating your acceptance of the agreement.
7. Complete the form by confirming your agreement with the migration agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (client_details, text, required): This section requires information about yourself as a client, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **Service Terms Agreement** (service_terms_agreement, text, required): This section requires you to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the migration service.
* **Consent to Email** (consent_to_email, select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm whether you agree to receive email communications from the migration agent.
* **Consent to SMS** (consent_to_sms, select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm whether you agree to receive SMS messages from the migration agent.
* **Consent to Post** (consent_to_post, select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm whether you agree to receive postal mail from the migration agent.
* **Client Confirmation** (client_confirmation, text, required): This section requires you to confirm your understanding of the migration service and its terms.
* **Migration Agent Confirmation** (migration_agent_confirmation, select_one, required): This field asks you to confirm whether you have discussed and agreed on the migration service with the migration agent.
* **Agreement Reviewed** (agreement_reviewed, text, optional): This field is for you to confirm that you have reviewed the terms and conditions of the migration service.
* **Agreement Signed** (agreement_signed, text, optional): This field is for you to confirm that you have signed the agreement, indicating your acceptance of its terms.
* **Signature** (signature, select_one, required): This field requires you to choose between "Client's Signature" and "Migration Agent's Signature" to confirm your identity.
* **Signature Date** (signature_date, date, required): This field requires you to enter the date of your signature.
* **Migration Agent Agreement Form Confirmation** (migration_agent_agreement_form_confirmation, text, required): This field is for you to confirm that you have completed the form accurately and thoroughly.
