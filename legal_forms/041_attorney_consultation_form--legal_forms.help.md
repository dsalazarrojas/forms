# Attorney Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Attorney Consultation Form is designed to gather essential information about your legal consultation needs, ensuring that the attorney understands your situation and preferences before your consultation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your contact information, including your full legal name, primary contact email, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Choose a preferred date and time for your appointment, and select a meeting format that suits you best.
3. Describe your legal matter and the type of case you are dealing with.
4. Provide details about when the legal issue arose and if there are any opposing parties involved.
5. If applicable, indicate if you have had previous legal representation and provide the name of your previous attorney.
6. Specify your consultation objectives and any relevant documents you may have.
7. Indicate the urgency level of your matter and any budget or cost concerns.
8. Finally, provide any additional information that may be helpful for your consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`client_address`, text, required): Your street address for mail correspondence.
* **City, State, ZIP** (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Your city, state, and ZIP code.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`preferred_appointment_date`, date, required): Select a date for your consultation.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_appointment_time`, time, required): Choose a time that suits you for your consultation.
* **Meeting Format** (`meeting_format`, select_one, required): Select how you would prefer to meet (In-Person, Phone, Video Conference, or No Preference).
* **Type of Case** (`case_type`, select_one, required): Indicate the type of case you are dealing with (Family Law, Criminal, Civil, Corporate, Intellectual Property, Real Estate, Immigration, Probate, Personal Injury, Contract, or Other).
* **Case Description** (`case_description`, text, required): Briefly describe your legal situation.
* **Date Issue Occurred** (`date_issue_occurred`, date, optional): If applicable, provide the date when your legal issue arose.
* **Opposing Party** (`opposing_party`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of any opposing parties involved.
* **Prior Attorney Representation** (`prior_attorney`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have had previous legal representation. If yes, provide the name of your previous counsel.
* **Previous Attorney Name** (`previous_attorney_name`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of your previous attorney.
* **Outcome of Previous Case** (`outcome_previous_case`, text, optional): If applicable, describe the outcome of your previous case.
* **Consultation Objectives** (`consultation_objectives`, text, required): Specify what you hope to achieve from the consultation.
* **Documentation Available** (`documentation_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have relevant documents available (Yes, No, or Some).
* **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level`, select_one, required): Indicate the urgency level of your matter (Very Urgent, Urgent, Moderately Urgent, or Not Urgent).
* **Budget or Cost Concerns** (`budget_concerns`, text, optional): If applicable, describe any specific concerns about legal fees.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant details that may be helpful for your consultation.
