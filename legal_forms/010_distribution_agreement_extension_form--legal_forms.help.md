# Distribution Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distribution Agreement Extension Form is used to extend a distribution agreement in the legal context. This form requires users to provide necessary information for the agreement extension process.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have the necessary information and documents before filling out the form.
* Select the relevant options for each field as per your agreement details.
* Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement ID (#1)**: This field is used to identify the agreement for which you are extending. Enter the agreement ID as per your records.
* **Business Name (#2)**: This field is used to specify the business name associated with the agreement. Enter the business name as per your records.
* **Agreement Title (#3)**: This field is used to specify the title of the agreement. Enter the title of the agreement as per your records.
* **Agreement Type (#4)**: Select the type of agreement from the options provided. Ensure you choose the correct type as per your agreement details.
* **Extension Reason (#5)**: This field is used to specify the reason for extending the agreement. Provide a brief explanation of the reason for extension.
* **Agreement Status (#6)**: Select the status of the agreement from the options provided. Ensure you choose the correct status as per your agreement details.
* **Distribution Method (#7)**: This field is used to specify the distribution method for the extended agreement. Select from the options provided.
* **Agreement Start Date (#8)**: This field is used to specify the start date of the agreement. Enter the start date as per your records.
* **Agreement End Date (#9)**: This field is used to specify the end date of the agreement. Enter the end date as per your records.
* **Agreement Start Time (#10)**: This field is used to specify the start time of the agreement. Enter the start time as per your records.
* **Agreement End Time (#11)**: This field is used to specify the end time of the agreement. Enter the end time as per your records.
* **Distribution Address (#12)**: This field is used to specify the distribution address for the extended agreement. Enter the address as per your records.
* **Notes (#13)**: This field is used for any additional comments or notes about the agreement extension.
* **Email (#14)**: This field is used to specify the email address for contact purposes. Enter the email address as per your records.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
