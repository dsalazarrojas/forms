# Content Licensing Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Content Licensing Contract Form is a crucial document used to formalize and record the terms and conditions of content licensing agreements. It ensures that all parties involved understand their rights, responsibilities, and expectations. This form helps prevent misunderstandings and ensures that both the licensor and licensee are on the same page.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Required Information

- Make sure you have all necessary information and documents ready before starting the form.

### Step 2: Fill Out the Form

- Follow the instructions carefully, and ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Review each section to understand the requirements and implications of each field.

### Step 3: Review and Edit

- Once you've filled out the form, review it for accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out and that there are no mistakes or omissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Licensor Name** (`licensor_name`, `text`, required)
Full name or company granting license.

### 2. **Licensor Email** (`licensor_email`, `email`, required)
Email address of the licensor.

### 3. **Licensor Address** (`licensor_address`, `text`, required)
Mailing address of the licensor.

### 4. **Licensee Name** (`licensee_name`, `text`, required)
Full name or company receiving license.

### 5. **Licensee Email** (`licensee_email`, `email`, required)
Email address of the licensee.

### 6. **Licensee Address** (`licensee_address`, `text`, required)
Mailing address of the licensee.

### 7. **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, required)
Date of this agreement.

### 8. **Content Description** (`content_description`, `text`, required)
Describe the content being licensed.

### 9. **Content Title** (`content_title`, `text`, required)
Title of the licensed content.

### 10. **Content Type** (`content_type`, `select_one`, required)
Format of the content (e.g., Written Article, Photograph, etc.).

### 11. **License Type** (`license_type`, `select_one`, required)
What type of license is granted (e.g., Exclusive, Non-Exclusive, etc.).

### 12. **License Scope** (`license_scope`, `select_one`, required)
What rights are granted (e.g., Reproduction Only, Distribution Only, etc.).

### 13. **Territory** (`territory`, `select_one`, required)
Where the license is valid (e.g., Local, Regional, etc.).

### 14. **License Duration** (`license_duration`, `select_one`, required)
How long the license is valid (e.g., 6 Months, 1 Year, etc.).

### 15. **License Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required)
When the license begins.

### 16. **License End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required)
When the license ends.

### 17. **License Fee** (`license_fee`, `number`, required)
Total fee for the license.

### 18. **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required)
How payment will be made (e.g., Upfront Payment, Installment Plan, etc.).

### 19. **Royalty Percentage** (`royalty_percentage`, `number`, optional)
If applicable, what percentage.

### 20. **Attribution Required** (`attribution_required`, `select_one`, required)
Must credit be given to licensor (e.g., Yes, Full Attribution, etc.).

### 21. **Modification Rights** (`modification_rights`, `select_one`, required)
Can the content be modified (e.g., No Modifications, Minor Edits Only, etc.).

### 22. **Sublicensing Allowed** (`sublicensing_allowed`, `select_one`, required)
Can the content be sublicensed (e.g., True, False).

### 23. **Termination Clause** (`termination_clause`, `text`, required)
Conditions for termination of the license.

### 24. **Additional Terms** (`additional_terms`, `text`, optional)
Any other terms or conditions.

### 25. **Licensor Signature Date** (`licensor_signature_date`, `date`, required)
Date of licensor signature.

### 26. **Licensee Signature Date** (`licensee_signature_date`, `date`, required)
Date of licensee signature.

### Tip 1: Ensure accuracy in all fields.
Review each field for completeness and correctness.

### Tip 2: Read and understand each section before proceeding.
Each field is connected, so understanding its context is key to completing the form effectively.

### Tip 3: Don't hesitate to seek help.
If unsure about any field or section, seek guidance from a legal expert or your organization's legal department.
