# W4 Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The W4 Form is a type of tax-related form used for various purposes such as withholding allowances, employee's choice to have tax withholdings, and other employment tax-related matters.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your employment situation.
2. Ensure you are completing the form for the correct reason and have the necessary permissions and approvals.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **W4 Form** (`form_data`, text, required/optional): This is a title field that provides a label for the form.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your home address as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your employer's name as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your Social Security number as it appears on your tax documents.
* **Employee Chose** (`w4_option_1`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have chosen to have tax withholdings.
* **Employee Chose 2** (`w4_option_2`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have other tax choices.
* **Employee Chose 3** (`w4_option_3`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have other tax choices.
* **Employee Chose 4** (`w4_option_4`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have other tax choices.
* **Employee Chose 5** (`w4_option_5`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose whether you have other tax choices.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm you have filled out the form accurately.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign your employer's name to confirm they have verified the information.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you completed the form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you completed the form.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of tax withholdings you have chosen.
* **Percentage** (`percentage`, number, required/optional): Enter the percentage of tax withholdings you have chosen.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your tax situation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact information.
