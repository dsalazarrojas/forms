# sole_member_borrowing_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sole member borrowing confirmation form is designed to collect information about a loan or borrowing event involving a single entity. This form is likely used for internal purposes in a business or organization to track and record borrowing events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Entity Name in the first field. This is the name of the entity involved in the borrowing event.
2. Provide the Borrowing Date in the second field. Select the date of the borrowing event.
3. Enter the Borrowing Time in the third field. Select the time of the borrowing event.
4. Select the type(s) of borrowing from the options provided in the fourth field. This can be 'bank', 'loan', or 'other'.
5. Enter any additional details about the borrowing event in the fifth field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entity Name** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the entity involved in the borrowing event.
* **Borrowing Date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the borrowing event.
* **Borrowing Time** (time, required: false): Select the time of the borrowing event.
* **Borrowing Type** (select multiple, required: false): Select the type(s) of borrowing from the available options: 'bank', 'loan', or 'other'.
* **Details** (text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the borrowing event.
