# Negligence Law Evaluation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Negligence Law Evaluation Survey is a tool used to assess the circumstances surrounding a potential negligence claim. It is designed to evaluate whether a breach of duty occurred, if so, whether it caused harm, and if so, what kind of harm it caused.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read each question carefully and provide a clear and concise answer to the best of your knowledge.
2.  For questions with multiple options, select the answer that best describes your situation.
3.  If a question does not apply to your situation, select "No Answer" or provide a clear explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Introduction** (Page 1)
    *   This page is meant to provide an introduction to the survey and its purpose.
    *   Please read it carefully before proceeding to ensure you understand the survey's goals.
*   **Duty** (Page 2)
    *   A duty is a legal obligation or responsibility that one person or organization has towards another.
    *   Select the answer that best describes the duty in question.
    *   Options:
        - **Breach of Duty**: A duty was clearly breached.
        - **No Duty**: No duty was involved.
        - **Unclear Duty**: The duty is unclear or ambiguous.
        - **Implied Duty**: The duty was implied or not explicitly stated.
        - **Statutory Duty**: The duty was imposed by a statute or law.
        - **No Answer**: This situation does not apply.
*   **Breach of Duty** (Page 3)
    *   A breach of duty is a failure to meet a legal obligation.
    *   Select the answer that best describes the breach of duty.
    *   Options:
        - **Breach of Duty**: A duty was breached.
        - **Not Breached Duty**: A duty was not breached.
        - **No Answer**: This situation does not apply.
*   **Causation** (Page 4)
    *   Causation refers to the link between the breach of duty and the harm suffered.
    *   Select the answer that best describes the causation.
    *   Options:
        - **Breach Caused Harm**: A breach of duty caused harm.
        - **No Breach Caused Harm**: A breach of duty did not cause harm.
        - **Unclear Causation**: The causation is unclear or ambiguous.
        - **Implied Causation**: The causation was implied or not explicitly stated.
        - **Statutory Causation**: The causation was imposed by a statute or law.
        - **No Answer**: This situation does not apply.
*   **Harm** (Page 5)
    *   Harm refers to the negative consequences or losses suffered as a result of the breach of duty.
    *   Select the answer that best describes the harm.
    *   Options:
        - **Physical Harm**: Physical harm was suffered.
        - **Emotional Harm**: Emotional harm was suffered.
        - **Financial Harm**: Financial harm was suffered.
        - **No Harm**: No harm was suffered.
        - **No Answer**: This situation does not apply.
*   **Breach of Duty Definition** (Page 6)
    *   Provide a clear and concise definition of the duty that was breached.
*   **Causation Definition** (Page 7)
    *   Provide a clear and concise definition of the causation that led to the harm.
*   **Harm Definition** (Page 8)
    *   Provide a clear and concise definition of the harm suffered.

## Tips

*   Answer each question carefully and accurately to ensure the survey's effectiveness.
*   If a question does not apply to your situation, select "No Answer" and provide a clear explanation.
*   This form is not a substitute for legal advice or professional consultation. Consult a qualified legal professional for accurate and applicable information.
*   Ensure that your answers are consistent with your specific situation.
