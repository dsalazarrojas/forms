# Dissolution Of Marriage Petition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide a structured and organized template for the Petitioning Party to initiate the dissolution of marriage process. Please note that this form should be filled out accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details as the Petitioning Party.
2. Provide the address of the Petitioning Party.
3. Enter the name of the spouse involved.
4. Specify the employment status of the spouse (if applicable).
5. State the grounds for divorce (if applicable).
6. Confirm your current marital status.
7. Choose a date for the divorce (if applicable).
8. Identify the court where you wish to file this petition.
9. Name the judge who will preside over the case (if known).
10. If applicable, list any other documents you have in support of your petition.
11. Finally, indicate the dissolution document fee (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Petitioning Party**: Enter your name as the individual initiating the dissolution of marriage process.
* **Petitioning Party's Address**: Enter the current address of the Petitioning Party.
* **Spouse**: Enter the name of the spouse involved in the dissolution process.
* **Spouses Employment**: If the spouse has a job, describe their employment status (e.g., employed, unemployed, student).
* **Grounds For Divorce**: If you are seeking divorce due to specific reasons, provide a brief explanation (e.g., irreconcilable differences, infidelity, etc.). 
* **Marital Status**: Confirm your current marital status (e.g., separated, married, divorced).
* **Divorce Date**: Choose a date for the divorce (if you know it already or if it's an approximate date).
* **Court**: Enter the name of the court where you wish to file this petition.
* **Judge**: Name the judge who will preside over your case (if you know their name).
* **Lawyer**: Enter the name of your lawyer (if you have one).
* **Other Documents**: List any other documents you have in support of your petition (e.g., proof of separation, witness statements).
* **Dissolution Document Fee**: If applicable, enter the amount you are willing to pay for the dissolution document fee.

Note: This guide assumes that users have a basic understanding of the dissolution of marriage process. If you are unsure or need further clarification, please seek legal counsel.
