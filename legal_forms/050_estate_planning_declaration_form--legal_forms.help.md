# Estate Planning Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help individuals declare their estate planning intentions and preferences, ensuring that their wishes are respected and carried out in the event of their passing or incapacitation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the questions and options provided.
2. Answer the questions honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
3. Make sure to fill out all the fields that apply to you.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm that you have completed it voluntarily and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Asset Distribution**: This field allows you to declare your intentions regarding the distribution of your assets after your passing. Please select all the options that apply to you.
	+ **Yes**: I intend to distribute my assets according to this option.
	+ **No**: I do not intend to distribute my assets according to this option.
* **Care Preferred Provider**: This field is for you to declare your preferred provider for care and medical assistance. Please select the option that best represents your preferences.
	+ **Yes**: I prefer to be cared for by [insert name].
	+ **No**: I have no preferred provider.
* **Care Alternate Provider**: This field is for you to declare your preferred alternate provider in case your primary care provider is not available. Please select the option that best represents your preferences.
	+ **Yes**: I prefer [insert name] as my alternate provider.
	+ **No**: I have no preferred alternate provider.
