# Immigration Consultancy Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Consultancy Liability Waiver form is designed to be used by immigration consultants to obtain a waiver from their clients, protecting themselves against potential legal claims.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately for each new client before rendering any services.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out according to the guidelines and explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consultancy Name (consultancy_name)** (`consultancy_name`, Text, Optional): This is the name of your immigration consultancy. Enter your consultancy's name here.
* **Address (address)** (`address`, Text, Optional): Enter the physical address of your consultancy.
* **Date of Submission (date_of_submission)** (`date_of_submission`, Date, Optional): This is the date you are submitting the form. Please enter it in the standard date format (e.g., 2024-03-12).
* **Client Signature (client_signature)** (`client_signature`, Text, Optional): This is the section for your client to sign, acknowledging they have read and understood the form. The client should sign this section to acknowledge their agreement to the waiver.
