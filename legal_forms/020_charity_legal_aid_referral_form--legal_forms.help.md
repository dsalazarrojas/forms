# Charity Legal Aid Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Charity Legal Aid Referral Form is used to gather information from clients seeking legal aid and referrals to attorneys. This form is a critical part of the intake process, helping legal aid providers to assess and prioritize cases.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the sections and ensure you are familiar with the information requested in each section.
2.  Enter the required information for each field as prompted.
3.  Be thorough and accurate when providing information, as it will help legal aid providers to make informed decisions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
*   **Client Full Name** (`Client Full Name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the client's full name.
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, **Optional**): Enter the client's email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Address** (`Address`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the client's address.
*   **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, **Optional**): Enter the client's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Referral Information**
    *   **Who Referred This Client** (`Who Referred This Client`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the name of the person who referred the client.
    *   **Role of Person Making Referral** (`Role of Person Making Referral`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter the role of the person making the referral.
    *   **Date of Referral** (`Date of Referral`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date the client was referred.
### Case Information
*   **Type of Legal Issue** (`Type of Legal Issue`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the area of law that applies to the client's situation.
*   **Description of Legal Issue** (`Description of Legal Issue`, `text`, **Required**): Enter a description of the client's legal issue.
*   **Urgency Level** (`Urgency Level`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select the level of urgency for the case.
### Financial Information
*   **Approximate Monthly Income** (`Approximate Monthly Income`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the client's approximate monthly income.
*   **Household Size** (`Household Size`, `number`, **Optional**): Enter the number of people in the client's household.
*   **Qualifies for Legal Aid Based on Income** (`Qualifies for Legal Aid Based on Income`, `select_one`, **Required**): Indicate if the client meets the poverty guidelines.
### Access and Barriers
*   **Language Assistance Needed** (`Language Assistance Needed`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Indicate if the client needs language assistance.
*   **Accessibility Accommodations** (`Accessibility Accommodations`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any accessibility needs the client may have.
*   **Additional Notes or Concerns** (`Additional Notes or Concerns`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any additional information or concerns about the client's situation.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by clients seeking legal aid and referrals to attorneys. It is intended to be used by legal aid providers to assess and prioritize cases.
