# Guardian Beneficiary Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Guardian Beneficiary Form is a legal document used to declare a guardian and beneficiary for a minor child. This form is used to establish the legal relationship between the guardian and the minor child, as well as the beneficiary's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the Guardian Nominator's Name: Enter the name of the person who is nominating themselves as the guardian.
2. Specify the Relationship to the Minor Child: Select the relationship between the guardian nominator and the minor child (Mom, Dad, Grandparent, or Other).
3. Enter the Guardian Nominator's Address: Enter the address of the person who is nominating themselves as the guardian.
4. Enter the Dependent Child's Name and Date of Birth: Provide the name and date of birth of the minor child.
5. Enter Contact Information: Provide the contact phone number and email address of the guardian nominator (optional but recommended).
6. Nominee Beneficiary's Name and Address: Enter the name and address of the person who will be the beneficiary.
7. Signature of Guardian Nominator: Select the method of signature (Signature or Other) and sign the form as the guardian nominator.
8. Witness Signature and Name (optional): If the form is being witnessed, select the method of signature (Signature or Other) and sign the form as the witness. Enter the name of the witness if they are signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guardian Nominator Name** (`guardian_nominator_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who is nominating themselves as the guardian.
* **Relationship To Minor Child** (`relationship_to_minor_child`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the guardian nominator and the minor child (Mom, Dad, Grandparent, or Other).
* **Guardian Nominator Address** (`guardian_nominator_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person who is nominating themselves as the guardian.
* **Dependent Child Name** (`dependent_child_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the minor child.
* **Dependent Child DOB** (`dependent_child_dob`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the minor child.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the guardian nominator.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the guardian nominator (optional).
* **Nominee Beneficiary Name** (`nominee_beneficiary_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person who will be the beneficiary.
* **Nominee Beneficiary Address** (`nominee_beneficiary_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the person who will be the beneficiary.
* **Guardian Nominator Signature** (`guardian_nominator_signature`, select_multiple, required): Select the method of signature (Signature or Other) and sign the form as the guardian nominator.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, select_multiple, optional): Select the method of signature (Signature or Other) and sign the form as the witness if applicable.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the witness if they are signing the form.
* **Witness Relationship** (`witness_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship between the witness and the guardian nominator.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting.
* Double-check the relationship between the guardian nominator and the minor child.
* Use the correct method of signature for the guardian nominator and witness (if applicable).
* Enter accurate and up-to-date contact information for the guardian nominator.
