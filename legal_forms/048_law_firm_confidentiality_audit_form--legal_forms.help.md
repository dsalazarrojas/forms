# Law Firm Confidentiality Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Law Firm Confidentiality Audit Form is used to audit and track the status of documents within a law firm, ensuring that sensitive information remains confidential. This form helps identify potential confidentiality issues and provides a framework for follow-up actions to maintain confidentiality.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter the name of the document** in the "document_name" field, which is a required field.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "document_type" field to indicate whether the document is of a sensitive nature.
3. For "department," select all departments that have access to the document.
4. If the document is completed, enter the date in the "date_completed" field.
5. Enter the name of the person who reviewed the document in the "reviewer_name" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "reviewer_department" field to indicate if the reviewer is from a department.
7. If the document has a status, select "Active" or "Inactive" in the "document_status" field.
8. Provide any follow-up actions taken in the "follow_up_actions" field.
9. Enter the date for the next follow-up action in the "next_follow_up_date" field.
10. Select "Active" or "Inactive" in the "follow_up_status" field to indicate the follow-up status.
11. Provide any comments in the "comments" field.
12. Enter the document number in the "document_number" field.
13. If the document was reviewed, enter the date in the "date_reviewed" field.
14. Enter any reviewer comments in the "reviewer_comments" field.
15. Provide the name of the next reviewer in the "next_reviewer_name" field.
16. Enter the date for the next follow-up action by the next reviewer in the "follow_up_date_next_reviewer" field.
17. Select "Active" or "Inactive" in the "follow_up_status_next_reviewer" field to indicate the next reviewer's follow-up status.
18. Enter any follow-up notes in the "follow_up_notes" field.
19. Finally, provide the signature of the reviewer in the "reviewer_signature" field, which is a required field.
