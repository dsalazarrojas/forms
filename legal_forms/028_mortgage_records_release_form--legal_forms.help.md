# Mortgage Records Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mortgage Records Release Form is a crucial document that facilitates the release of mortgage records. It ensures that all necessary information is accurately provided, and the process is completed correctly, preventing any potential issues or disputes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Mortgage Records Release Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Mortgage Holder Name** field with the name of the person or entity that holds the mortgage.
2. Provide the **Mortgage Holder Address** in the corresponding field.
3. If applicable, fill in the **Email** and **Phone** fields with the contact details of the mortgage holder or their representative.
4. Enter the **Mortgage Institution Name**, which is the name of the financial institution that issued the mortgage.
5. Provide the name of the **Contact Person** who is responsible for the release of the documents.
6. Fill in the **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** fields with the contact details of the contact person.
7. Select the **Document Type** from the provided options (Mortgage, Deed, Affidavit, or Other).
8. Enter the **Document Number** and **Document Date** accurately and correctly.
9. Explain the **Reason for Release** in the corresponding field.
10. Enter the name of the person **Authorized By** to release the documents.
11. Confirm that all attached documents are accurate and complete by checking the **I confirm that all the attached documents are accurate and complete.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mortgage Holder Name**: The name of the person or entity that holds the mortgage.
* **Mortgage Holder Address**: The address of the person or entity that holds the mortgage.
* **Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Mortgage Institution Name**: The name of the financial institution that issued the mortgage.
* **Contact Person**: The name of the person responsible for releasing the documents.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Document Type**: The type of document being released (Mortgage, Deed, Affidavit, or Other).
* **Document Number**: The number of the document being released.
* **Document Date**: The date of the document being released.
* **Reason for Release**: A brief explanation for the reason the documents are being released.
* **Authorized By**: The name of the person authorizing the release of the documents.
* **I confirm that all the attached documents are accurate and complete.**: A confirmation checkbox to ensure that all provided documents are accurate and complete.
