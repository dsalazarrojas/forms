# Witness Statement Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Witness Statement Access Form is a document used to track and verify the access to witness statements in a legal process. It is essential for ensuring that witness statements are properly reviewed, signed, and stored in a secure manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the witness statements and ensure they are complete and accurate.
2. Check if the statements have been signed or not.
3. Verify the reviewer's name, title, and contact information for the reviewed statements.
4. Fill in the necessary fields with the relevant information.
5. Add any comments or notes as required.
6. Submit the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Witness Statements** (`Witness Statements`, Select Multiple): This field allows you to select multiple witness statements to access.
* **Witness Statement Details** (`Witness Statement Details`, Note): A text area for entering specific details about the witness statement.
* **Witness Statements Signed** (`Witness Statements Signed`, Select One): Check if the witness statement has been signed or not.
* **Witness Statements Not Signed** (`Witness Statements Not Signed`, Select Multiple): Select any witness statements not signed.
* **Witness Statements Signed By** (`Witness Statements Signed By`, Select One): Identify who signed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed** (`Witness Statement Reviewed`, Select One): Check if the witness statement has been reviewed or not.
* **Witness Statement Confidential** (`Witness Statement Confidential`, Select One): Select if the witness statement is confidential or not.
* **Witness Statement Confidential Reason** (`Witness Statement Confidential Reason`, Note): Enter the reason why the witness statement is confidential (if applicable).
* **Witness Statement Reviewed By** (`Witness Statement Reviewed By`, Select One): Identify who reviewed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed By Title** (`Witness Statement Reviewed By Title`, Text): Enter the title of the person who reviewed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed Time** (`Witness Statement Reviewed Time`, Date): Enter the time when the witness statement was reviewed.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed Date** (`Witness Statement Reviewed Date`, Date): Enter the date when the witness statement was reviewed.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed Datetime** (`Witness Statement Reviewed Datetime`, Date): Enter the datetime when the witness statement was reviewed.
* **Witness Statement Access Form Comments** (`Witness Statement Access Form Comments`, Note): Enter any comments or notes about the witness statement access form.
* **Witness Statement Form ID** (`Witness Statement Form ID`, Text): Enter a unique ID for the witness statement form.
* **Witness Statement Form Version** (`Witness Statement Form Version`, Text): Enter the version of the witness statement form.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed By Email** (`Witness Statement Reviewed By Email`, Email): Enter the email of the person who reviewed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed By Phone** (`Witness Statement Reviewed By Phone`, Text): Enter the phone number of the person who reviewed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed By Address** (`Witness Statement Reviewed By Address`, Text): Enter the address of the person who reviewed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Reviewed By Department** (`Witness Statement Reviewed By Department`, Text): Enter the department of the person who reviewed the witness statement.
* **Witness Statement Access Form Notes** (`Witness Statement Access Form Notes`, Note): Enter any additional notes or comments about the witness statement access form.
* **Witness Statement Access Form Comments** (`Witness Statement Access Form Comments`, Note): Enter any final comments or notes about the witness statement access form.
