# Copyright Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Copyright Application Form is a tool used to facilitate the copyright registration process for original works. This form helps you gather necessary information to apply for copyright protection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Copyright Application" field with a brief summary of your work.
2. Provide a title for your work in the "Title" field.
3. Enter the name of the owner of the original work in the "Owner" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Copyright Application**: This field is a short description of your original work. Please describe the work you are applying for copyright protection in a few sentences.
* **Title**: Enter the title of your work as it appears on the original version, or as it was published.
* **Owner**: List the name of the owner of the original work. This can be an individual, a company, or any other entity that holds the rights to the work.

## Tips
* Make sure to accurately describe your work and provide all required information.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
