# Inheritance Distribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inheritance Distribution Form is used to document the distribution of an individual's estate after their death. This form helps to ensure that the estate is divided fairly and according to the law.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information about the deceased and their estate.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. Provide the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **family_member1_name** (`family_member1_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the first family member.
* **family_member1_share_percentage** (`family_member1_share_percentage`, number, required/optional): Enter the percentage of the estate that the first family member will inherit.
* **family_member2_name** (`family_member2_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the second family member.
* **family_member2_share_percentage** (`family_member2_share_percentage`, number, required/optional): Enter the percentage of the estate that the second family member will inherit.
* **estate_description** (`estate_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe the estate.
* **executor_name** (`executor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the executor.
* **executor_relationship** (`executor_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe the relationship of the executor to the deceased.
* **date_of_death** (`date_of_death`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of death.
* **total_estate_value** (`total_estate_value`, number, required/optional): Enter the total value of the estate.
* **family_member1_share_value** (`family_member1_share_value`, number, required/optional): Enter the value of the first family member's share.
* **family_member2_share_value** (`family_member2_share_value`, number, required/optional): Enter the value of the second family member's share.
* **family_member1_share** (`family_member1_share`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the percentage of the estate that the first family member will inherit.
* **family_member2_share** (`family_member2_share`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the percentage of the estate that the second family member will inherit.
* **distribution_method** (`distribution_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select how the estate will be distributed (equal, per stirpes, as described in the will).
* **executor_inheritance** (`executor_inheritance`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the executor's inheritance.
* **executor_fees** (`executor_fees`, number, required/optional): Enter the fees of the executor.
* **total_fees** (`total_fees`, number, required/optional): Enter the total fees.
* **family_member1_inheritance** (`family_member1_inheritance`, number, required/optional): Enter the inheritance for the first family member.
* **family_member1_fees** (`family_member1_fees`, number, required/optional): Enter the fees for the first family member.
* **family_member2_inheritance** (`family_member2_inheritance`, number, required/optional): Enter the inheritance for the second family member.
* **family_member2_fees** (`family_member2_fees`, number, required/optional): Enter the fees for the second family member.
* **other_inheritance** (`other_inheritance`, text, required/optional): Enter any other heirs (e.g., children, charities).
* **other_fees** (`other_fees`, number, required/optional): Enter the fees for other heirs.
* **other_inheritance_value** (`other_inheritance_value`, number, required/optional): Enter the value of other heirs' shares.
* **review_date** (`review_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date for the final review.
