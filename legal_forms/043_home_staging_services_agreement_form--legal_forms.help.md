# home-staging-services-agreement-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect client information, service details, and other essential elements of the home staging agreement from clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information about yourself and your request before starting.
2. Fill in your contact information (Client Information) in the first section.
3. Provide details about the service you require (Service Details).
4. Review and agree to the payment terms (Payment Terms).
5. Sign the service agreement (Service Agreement).
6. Sign as the client (Client Signature).
7. Add your signature as the representative (Representative Signature).
8. Date and time of signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`text`, required: false): This is the section where you will provide your name, phone number, and email address so that we can get in touch with you about your request.
* **Service Details (Service Details)** (`text`, required: false): Please describe the specific service you require for your home. Be as detailed as possible so that we can understand your needs correctly.
* **Payment Terms (Payment Terms)** (`text`, required: false): Review and acknowledge our payment terms, which are not explicitly stated in this form.
* **Service Agreement (Service Agreement)** (`text`, required: false): Sign this agreement to confirm you have read and understand our terms and conditions.
* **Client Signature (Client Signature)** (`text`, required: false): Please sign below to confirm that you have read and understood this agreement.
* **Representative Signature (Representative Signature)** (`text`, required: false): Sign above as the representative of the client (e.g. property owner).
* **Date (Date)** (`text`, required: false): Date you completed this form.
* **Time (Time)** (`text`, required: false): Time you completed this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth service process.
* Review our payment terms and service agreement carefully before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.
