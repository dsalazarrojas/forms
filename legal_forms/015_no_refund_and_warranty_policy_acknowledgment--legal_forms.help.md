# No Refund And Warranty Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to acknowledge a customer's understanding of a no-refund and no-warranty policy. It is essential to ensure that customers are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the product or service they are purchasing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose "Yes" or "No" for "Customer Information" to indicate whether you have understood the no-refund and no-warranty policy.
2. Fill out the "Contact Person" field with the name of the person responsible for the transaction.
3. The "Acknowledgment" field is a note field where you can provide any additional comments or questions about the policy.
4. Select "No refund and no-warranty terms" or "I am aware of the terms" for "Acceptance of Terms" to indicate your understanding of the policy.
5. Choose "None" or "Other" for "Signature of Customer" to select your signature type.
6. Fill out the "Name", "Company", "Job Title", and "Email" fields with your relevant information.
7. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Fill out the "Consent" field with your consent to the policy.
9. Choose "None" or "Other" for "Signature of Service Provider" to select your signature type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Choose "Yes" to indicate that you have understood the no-refund and no-warranty policy.
	+ Choose "No" if you have not understood the policy.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Fill out the name of the person responsible for the transaction.
* **Acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment`, `note`, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional comments or questions about the policy.
* **Acceptance of Terms** (`acceptance`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose "No refund and no-warranty terms" to indicate that you understand the policy.
	+ Choose "I am aware of the terms" to indicate that you have read and understand the policy.
* **Signature of Customer** (`signature1`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose "None" to indicate that you do not have a signature.
	+ Choose "Other" to select a different signature type (please provide details in the "Consent" field).
* **Name** (`customer_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Fill out your name.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Fill out your company name (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Fill out your job title (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false):
	+ Fill out your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Provide your phone number.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Fill out your consent to the policy.
* **Signature of Service Provider** (`signature2`, `select_one`, required: false):
	+ Choose "None" to indicate that the service provider has no signature.
	+ Choose "Other" to select a different signature type (please provide details in the "Consent" field).
