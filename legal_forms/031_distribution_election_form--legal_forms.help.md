# Distribution Election Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to determine how a company's assets, resources, or products will be distributed among employees. It's essential to provide accurate information to ensure efficient distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Choose the correct distribution method and type from the provided options.
3. Enter your name, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide your preferred distribution date.
5. Select the correct distribution method and type for the assets, resources, or products being distributed.
6. Add any additional notes about the distribution process if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Distribution Election Form** (Page 1):
	+ This is the title of the form, which should be self-explanatory.
* **Name**: 
	+ Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Phone Number**: 
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Distribution Method**:
	+ Select how you would like to receive the distributed assets, resources, or products (Manual, Automatic, or Other).
* **Distribution Date**:
	+ Enter the date when you would like the distribution to take place.
* **Distribution Type**:
	+ Select the type of distribution method you prefer (Manual, Automatic, or Manual/Automatic).
* **Notes**:
	+ Add any additional information about the distribution process if necessary.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you're unsure about any field, ask your supervisor or manager for guidance.
- Double-check your information before submission to ensure accuracy.
- If you need to make changes, please contact the designated person for assistance.
