# White Label Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you create and manage white-label agreements with partners. It collects essential information about partners, including their name, contact details, and agreement terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Partnership Name field with the name of your partnership.
2. Enter the Partner's Name in the field provided.
3. Provide the Partner's Email address in the email field.
4. Enter the Partner's Company name in the corresponding field.
5. Fill in the Partner's Address in the provided field.
6. Enter the Partner's Phone number in the field provided.
7. Enter the Agreement Terms in the text field.
8. If applicable, specify the Agreement Version number.
9. Select the Partnership Type from the available options (Sole Partnership, General Partnership, Limited Partnership, or Corporation).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partnership Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your partnership.
* **Partner's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your partner.
* **Partner's Email (Email)**: Enter your partner's email address.
* **Partner's Company (Text)**: Enter your partner's company name.
* **Partner's Address (Text)**: Enter your partner's address.
* **Partner's Phone (Text)**: Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Agreement Terms (Text)**: Enter the terms of your agreement.
* **Agreement Version (Number)**: If applicable, enter the version number of your agreement.
* **Partnership Type (Select One)**: Select the type of partnership you are entering into (Sole Partnership, General Partnership, Limited Partnership, or Corporation).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (none are marked as required in this form).
* Double-check the information provided to ensure accuracy.
* The partnership type selection may affect the terms of your agreement, so ensure you select the correct type.
