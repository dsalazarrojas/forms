# Mediation Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mediation Session Booking Form is designed to collect essential information for booking a mediation session. This form helps to ensure that all relevant details are captured accurately, allowing for a smooth and effective mediation process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Read all fields carefully** and fill out each field according to your needs.
2. **Submit the form once you've completed it** to initiate the mediation session booking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

1. **Session Request Details**
	* A text field for requesting a mediation session. Please provide a clear and concise description of the reason for the session. (<em>Example: Requesting mediation for conflict resolution between two parties</em>)
2. **Client Info**
	* A text field for providing information about the client or parties involved in the mediation session. (<em>Example: Name, age, and contact number</em>)
3. **Client Name**
	* A text field for entering the client's name. (<em>Example: John Doe</em>)
4. **Case Type**
	* A text field for selecting the type of mediation case (if applicable). (<em>Example: Family mediation, Commercial mediation, etc.</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
5. **Session Date**
	* A date field for selecting the date of the mediation session. (<em>Example: 2024-03-01</em>)
6. **Session Time**
	* A time field for selecting the time of the mediation session. (<em>Example: 14:00</em>)
	* _Note: This field is not required, but it's recommended for scheduling purposes_
7. **Mediation Location**
	* A text field for selecting the location of the mediation session. (<em>Example: Conference Room A</em>)
8. **Session Length**
	* A number field for selecting the duration of the mediation session. (<em>Example: 60 minutes</em>)
9. **Contact Number**
	* A text field for providing the contact number of the client or representative. (<em>Example: 123-456-7890</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
10. **Confirmation Date**
	* A date field for selecting the date of confirmation for the mediation session. (<em>Example: 2024-03-15</em>)
	* _Note: This field is not required_
11. **Confirmation Time**
	* A time field for selecting the time of confirmation for the mediation session. (<em>Example: 10:00</em>)
	* _Note: This field is not required_
12. **Cancellation Date**
	* A date field for selecting the date of cancellation for the mediation session. (<em>Example: 2024-03-20</em>)
	* _Note: This field is not required_
13. **Cancellation Time**
	* A time field for selecting the time of cancellation for the mediation session. (<em>Example: 12:00</em>)
	* _Note: This field is not required_
14. **Follow Up Session**
	* A select multiple field for choosing whether to schedule a follow-up session. (<em>Example: Follow up session, No follow-up session</em>)
15. **Follow Up Contact Info**
	* A text field for providing the contact information for the follow-up session. (<em>Example: Phone number and email</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
16. **Follow Up Session Date**
	* A date field for scheduling the follow-up session. (<em>Example: 2024-04-01</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
17. **Follow Up Session Time**
	* A time field for scheduling the follow-up session. (<em>Example: 15:00</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
18. **Follow Up Location**
	* A text field for selecting the location of the follow-up session. (<em>Example: Conference Room B</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
19. **Follow Up Length**
	* A number field for selecting the duration of the follow-up session. (<em>Example: 30 minutes</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
20. **Session Notes**
	* A text field for providing additional notes or comments about the mediation session. (<em>Example: Notes about the session</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
21. **Mediator Info**
	* A text field for providing information about the mediator assigned to the session. (<em>Example: Mediator's name and contact number</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
22. **Mediator Name**
	* A text field for entering the name of the mediator assigned to the session. (<em>Example: John Smith</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
23. **Mediator Email**
	* An email field for entering the email address of the mediator assigned to the session. (<em>Example: john.smith@email.com</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
24. **Mediator Phone**
	* A text field for entering the phone number of the mediator assigned to the session. (<em>Example: 123-456-7890</em>)
	* _Note: This field is optional_
25. **Assigned Tool**
	* A select one field for selecting the tool assigned to the session. (<em>Example: chatjimmy</em>)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the session details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the mediator or support team for assistance.
