# Host Event Legal Release Authorization - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect legal release authorizations from participants in an event. It is a necessary legal requirement to obtain a release from each participant, confirming that they are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the event, including the possibility of being photographed or recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Event Name** in the field at the top of the form. This is the name of the event you are participating in.
2. Provide **Event Details** in the text field below. This includes information such as event date, time, location, and any other relevant details about the event.
3. Enter your **Participant Details** in the text field below. This includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
4. In the **Release Authorization** field, select the correct option that reflects your understanding of the event terms and conditions. Options include:
	* Participant understands the risks of photography
	* Participant understands that they may be photographed
	* Event host is responsible for participant safety
	* Event host has right to use participant information in marketing materials
5. If applicable, select **Model Release** options to indicate your understanding of the event terms and conditions. Options include:
	* Yes
	* No
	* Not sure
6. Enter **Event Host Details** in the text field. This includes the name and contact information of the event host.
7. In the **Event Host Agreement** fields, enter any additional agreements or terms that have been agreed upon between you and the event host. This is a critical section, as it ensures that both you and the event host are on the same page regarding the terms and conditions of the event.
8. Review and agree to the **Participant Agreement** and **Participant Agreement 2** fields. These sections are essential to ensure that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Details**: Provide information about the event, including date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Release Authorization**: Select the correct option that reflects your understanding of the event terms and conditions.
* **Model Release**: Indicate your understanding of the event terms and conditions. Options include:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Not sure
* **Event Host Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the event host.
* **Event Host Agreement**: Enter any additional agreements or terms that have been agreed upon between you and the event host.
* **Event Host Agreement 2**: Enter any additional agreements or terms that have been agreed upon between you and the event host.
* **Participant Agreement**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Participant Agreement 2**: Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Event Terms**: A note field for the event host to provide any additional terms or conditions that you need to be aware of.

## Tips

* Make sure to carefully read and understand each field before completing the form.
* Review and agree to all the agreements and terms provided in the form to ensure a smooth and safe event experience for both you and the event host.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask the event host for clarification.
* Ensure that you have all necessary contact information for the event host in case of any issues during the event.
