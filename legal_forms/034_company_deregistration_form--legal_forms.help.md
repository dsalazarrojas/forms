# Company Deregistration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to officially deregister a company from the relevant authorities. Please fill it out accurately to provide the necessary information for deregistration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the company's legal name as registered.
2. Provide the official registration or tax ID number.
3. Specify the desired effective date for deregistration.
4. Offer a brief explanation for deregistration.
5. Input the name of the authorized person approving deregistration.
6. Enter a valid email address for further correspondence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company name (Legal name as registered)** (`company_name`, text, *Required*): Enter the company's name as registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Registration number (Official registration or tax ID)** (`registration_number`, text, *Required*): Enter the official registration or tax ID number for the company.
* **Effective deregistration date (Desired effective date for deregistration)** (`effective_deregistration_date`, date, *Required*): Select the date when the company should be deregistered.
* **Reason for deregistration (Provide a brief explanation)** (`reason_for_deregistration`, text, *Required*): Briefly describe the reasons for deregistration.
* **Authorized person name** (`authorized_person`, text, *Required*): Enter the name of the person approving the deregistration.
* **Contact email for further correspondence** (`contact_email`, email, *Required*): Enter a valid email address for further communication and follow up.
