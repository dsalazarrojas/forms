# Court Document Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request access to court documents. Users can fill in their details, document information, and specify their preferred access level to request access to court documents.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and fill in all required and optional fields as needed.
- Enter your User Input in the field provided.
- Select the Document Type of the document you are requesting access to from the provided options.
- Choose your preferred Access Level from the provided options.
- If applicable, provide your Date of Birth.
- Enter your Document ID if required.
- Specify your preferred Contact Method.
- Leave any Additional Comments if you wish to provide further context to your request.
- Enter your Email address if you wish to be contacted.
- Enter your Phone number if you wish to be contacted.
- Finally, provide a brief summary of your Document Access Request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or any other relevant information to help us identify you.
- **Document Type** (`document_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of document you are requesting access to. Options include Court Record, Legal Document, and Court Transcript.
- **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred access level. Options include Read Only, Read and Copy, and Read and Print.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If applicable, provide your date of birth.
- **Document ID** (`document_id`, number, optional): If required, enter your document ID.
- **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, text, optional): Specify your preferred contact method.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Leave any additional comments or context for your request.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
- **Document Access Request** (`document_access_request`, text, optional): Provide a brief summary of your document access request.
