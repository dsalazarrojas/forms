# Trade For Print Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade For Print Agreement form is used to document the terms and conditions of a trade agreement between a company and a service provider. It is essential to establish a clear understanding of the agreement's scope, duration, and terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the photographer or model you are working with.
2. Indicate whether the photographer or model is a Model or Other.
3. Enter the date of the agreement.
4. (Optional) Enter the start and end dates of the contract.
5. Select the Terms of Use that apply to this agreement.
6. (Optional) Enter any additional notes regarding the agreement.
7. Sign and date the agreement, indicating that you have read and understood the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer** (id: 1, select_one, required): Select the name of the photographer or model you are working with. This field is required to establish the relationship between your company and the service provider.
* **Model** (id: 2, select_one, required): Indicate whether the photographer or model you selected is a Model or Other. This field helps to clarify the type of service being provided.
* **Usage** (id: 3, text, optional): Enter the usage details of the agreed-upon services. This field is optional but provides additional context to the agreement.
* **Location** (id: 4, text, optional): Enter the location where the services will be rendered. This field is optional but helps to clarify where the services will take place.
* **Agreement Date** (id: 5, date, required): Enter the date on which this agreement was reached. This field is required to establish the date of the agreement.
* **Contract Start Date** (id: 6, date, optional): Enter the start date of the contract, if applicable. This field is optional and provides additional context to the agreement.
* **Contract End Date** (id: 7, date, optional): Enter the end date of the contract, if applicable. This field is optional and provides additional context to the agreement.
* **Terms of Use** (id: 8, select_multiple, required): Select the Terms of Use that apply to this agreement. This field is required to establish the terms and conditions of the agreement.
* **Agreement Notes** (id: 9, note, optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the agreement. This field is optional but provides an opportunity to add context to the agreement.
* **Signature** (id: 10, select_one, required): Sign and date the agreement, indicating that you have read and understood the terms. This field is required to confirm that you have reviewed the agreement.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have read and understood all the terms and conditions before signing the agreement.
* Complete the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any misunderstandings or disputes.
* Review the agreement with your service provider before signing it.
