# Legal Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Consultation Intake Form is designed to collect basic contact and case-related information from clients or individuals seeking legal services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form with your personal information**, including your first name, last name, and contact details (phone and/or email).
2. **Choose the type of case** that you are seeking legal assistance for (e.g., civil, criminal, family, etc.).
3. **Select a date of birth** if requested, as this might be necessary for age verification purposes.
4. **Enter your case number** (if known) to help our team identify your case.
5. **Provide your address**, so we can contact you effectively.
6. **Review and submit your form** before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Case number**: A unique identifier for your case, if known. This helps us track your case more efficiently.
* **Case type**: Select the type of case you are seeking legal assistance for. This helps us allocate resources and services accordingly.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (month/day/year). This might be used for age verification or other relevant purposes.
* **Contact info**: Enter your contact phone number and/or email address so we can reach out to you.
* **Contact email**: Enter your email address if you prefer to be contacted via email.
* **Address**: Enter your physical or postal address for communication and service purposes.
* **Case type**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Civil
	+ Criminal
	+ Family
	+ Immigration
	+ Probate
	+ Probate - Guardianship
	+ Probate - Trusts

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your input for any errors before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
