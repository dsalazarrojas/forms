# Nomination For Judicial Positions - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for nominating individuals for judicial positions. It collects information about the nominee and their qualifications to ensure consistency and accuracy in the nomination process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Nomination Form with a brief description of the nomination.
2. Enter the Nominee's Details: Provide the nominee's name, position, qualifications, and contact information.
3. Select the Nominee's Qualifications: Choose from the options provided.
4. Upload Supporting Documents: If applicable, upload documents to support the nomination.
5. Enter Additional Comments or Notes: Provide any additional information about the nominee or the nomination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, text, required: false): A brief description of the nomination.
* **Nominee Details** (`nominee_details`, text, required: false): Enter the nominee's name, position, and contact information.
* **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the nominee's qualifications from the options provided.
* **Reasons** (`reasons`, text, required: false): Provide a brief explanation for the nomination.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, required: false): Upload any supporting documents for the nomination.
* **Submit Note** (`submit_note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of submission.
* **Submit Note 2** (`submit_note2`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about the submission.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submit Position** (`submit_position`, text, required: false): Enter the position or title of the person submitting the nomination.
* **Submit Location** (`submit_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of submission.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of submission.
* **Supporting Document 1** (`supporting_doc1`, text, required: false): Upload supporting document 1.
* **Supporting Document 2** (`supporting_doc2`, text, required: false): Upload supporting document 2.
* **Supporting Document 3** (`supporting_doc3`, text, required: false): Upload supporting document 3.
