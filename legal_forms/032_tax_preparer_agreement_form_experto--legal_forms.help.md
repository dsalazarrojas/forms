# Tax Preparer Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from tax preparers and tax preparers' representatives, ensuring their agreement with taxpayers for tax preparation services. This includes collecting tax preparer contact information and the terms of their agreement with the taxpayer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your tax preparer's name as the preparer.
2. Enter your phone number and email address as the tax preparer.
3. Select the date of the agreement.
4. Enter your address as the tax preparer.
5. Enter your address as the taxpayer, if applicable.
6. Add any additional notes, if necessary.
7. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Taxpayer Name** (`taxpayer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the taxpayer. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **Tax Preparer Name** (`preparer_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the tax preparer.
* **Preparer Phone** (`preparer_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the tax preparer.
* **Preparer Email** (`preparer_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the tax preparer.
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Select the date the agreement was made.
* **Preparer Address** (`preparer_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the tax preparer.
* **Taxpayer Address** (`taxpayer_address`, text, optional): Enter the taxpayer's address, if applicable.
* **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, text, optional): Check if the agreement has been signed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes regarding the agreement, if necessary.

## Tips
- Ensure the taxpayer and tax preparer agree on all terms of the agreement before completing this form.
- If you're unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or add a note to clarify.
- This form is for information purposes only and should not be considered as a legally binding contract.
