# Green Lease Addendum Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Lease Addendum Consent Form is used to obtain the consent of both tenants and landlords for lease terms that include sustainability commitments. This form is used to ensure that all parties are aware of and agree to these commitments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Review the provided options for tenant and landlord consent to ensure that you agree with the sustainability commitments.
3. Select the correct options for tenant and landlord consent.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tenant Signature**: Enter your name as the tenant, as a signature of consent.
* **Landlord Signature**: Enter the name of the landlord or property manager as a signature of consent.
* **Tenant Consent**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Tenant accepts the green lease terms with the sustainability commitments listed.
	+ Tenant declines the green lease terms with the sustainability commitments listed.
	+ Tenant accepts and signs the green lease terms with the sustainability commitments listed below.
* **Sustainability Commitments**: Select all that apply for the commitments you agree to:
	+ Tenant is committed to recycling all materials used during the term.
	+ Tenant is committed to reducing energy consumption during the term.
	+ Tenant is committed to using water-efficient appliances during the term.
* **Landlord Consent**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Landlord accepts the green lease terms with the sustainability commitments listed.
	+ Landlord declines the green lease terms with the sustainability commitments listed.
	+ Landlord accepts and signs the green lease terms with the sustainability commitments listed below.
* **Lease Start Date**: Enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date**: Enter the end date of the lease.
* **Tenant Name**: Enter your name as the tenant.
* **Landlord Name**: Enter the name of the landlord or property manager.
* **Lease Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the lease.
* **Building Address**: Enter the address of the building or property.
* **Unit Number**: Enter the unit number of the property.
* **Email Address**: Enter your or the landlord's email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your or the landlord's phone number.
* **Tenant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Landlord Email**: Enter the landlord's email address.
* **Building Name**: Enter the name of the building or property.
* **Building Manager**: Enter the name of the building manager.
* **Lease Start Time**: Enter the start time of the lease.
* **Lease End Time**: Enter the end time of the lease.
* **Lease Length**: Enter the length of the lease in months or years.
* **Rent**: Enter the rent amount.
