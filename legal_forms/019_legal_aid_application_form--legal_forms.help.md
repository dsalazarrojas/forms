# Legal Aid Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from applicants seeking legal aid services. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that you receive the help you need.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your contact information, including your name, contact number, and address.
2. Provide your email address so that we can contact you about your application.
3. Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and identify you as an applicant.
4. Describe the legal problem you are experiencing, providing as much detail as possible.
5. Indicate whether you have any legal problems or not.
6. Enter the name and address of your law firm (if applicable).
7. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Aid Application Form** (`legal_aid_application_form`, text, optional): This is the main title of the form, which is not a required field but provides a heading for the form.
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for further information or assistance.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your current address for us to contact you or send important documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your application.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and identify you as an applicant.
* **Legal Problem Description** (`legal_problem_description`, text, optional): Describe the legal problem you are experiencing in as much detail as possible, including the nature of the issue and any relevant documentation or evidence.
* **Do you have any Legal Problems?** (`legal_problems`, select_multiple, optional): Check 'Yes' if you have any outstanding legal problems or issues that require attention.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address where you can be contacted for further information or assistance.
* **Law Firm Name** (`law_firm_name`, text, optional): If you are currently represented by a law firm, enter their name and contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
* We strive to provide timely and efficient service, but please note that our response time may vary.
