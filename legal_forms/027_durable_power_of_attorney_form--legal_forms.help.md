# Durable Power Of Attorney Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to establish a legal relationship between the Grantor and the Attorney, giving the Attorney the authority to act on behalf of the Grantor in a specific manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all the required fields as indicated in the form.
2. Ensure that all the fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Have both the Grantor and the Attorney sign the form, and have two witnesses sign it as well.
4. Date and time stamp the form when it's signed.
5. Keep the original copy of the form and send a copy to the relevant parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the Grantor.
* **Attorney Name** (`attorney_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the Attorney.
* **Attorney Address** (`attorney_address`, text, not required): Enter the address of the Attorney.
* **Attorney City** (`attorney_city`, text, not required): Enter the city of the Attorney's address.
* **Attorney State** (`attorney_state`, select_one, true): Select the state where the Attorney resides.
* **Attorney Zip** (`attorney_zip`, text, not required): Enter the zip code of the Attorney's address.
* **Attorney Phone** (`attorney_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the Attorney.
* **Grantor Name** (`grantor_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the Grantor.
* **Grantor Age** (`grantor_age`, number, not required): Enter the age of the Grantor.
* **Grantor Residence** (`grantor_residence`, text, not required): Enter the address of the Grantor's residence.
* **Grantor Zip** (`grantor_zip`, text, not required): Enter the zip code of the Grantor's residence.
* **Grantor Phone** (`grantor_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of the Grantor.
* **Relationship** (`attorney_relationship`, select_one, true): Select the relationship between the Grantor and the Attorney (e.g., Spouse, Partner, Parent, Child, Friend, Other).
* **Grantor Signature** (`grantor_signature`, date, not required): Sign and date this field by the Grantor.
* **Attorney Signature** (`attorney_signature`, date, not required): Sign and date this field by the Attorney.
* **Grantor Note** (`grantor_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the Grantor.
* **Attorney Note** (`attorney_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the Attorney.
* **Witness 1 Name** (`witness1_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the first witness.
* **Witness 1 Signature** (`witness1_signature`, date, not required): Sign and date this field by the first witness.
* **Witness 1 Relationship** (`witness1_relationship`, select_one, true): Select the relationship of the first witness with the Grantor (e.g., Spouse, Partner, Parent, Child, Friend, Other).
* **Witness 1 Note** (`witness1_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the first witness.
* **Witness 2 Name** (`witness2_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the second witness.
* **Witness 2 Signature** (`witness2_signature`, date, not required): Sign and date this field by the second witness.
* **Witness 2 Relationship** (`witness2_relationship`, select_one, true): Select the relationship of the second witness with the Grantor (e.g., Spouse, Partner, Parent, Child, Friend, Other).
* **Witness 2 Note** (`witness2_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes or comments from the second witness.
