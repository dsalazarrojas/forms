# life_insurance_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Life Insurance Records Release Form is used for collecting and managing life insurance information from policy holders or their beneficiaries.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select the type of life insurance by choosing from the options "Term Life", "Whole Life", or "Other" in the "What type of life insurance" field.
2.  Enter the Policy Holder's Name in the "Policy Holder Name" field.
3.  Enter the Policy Holder's address in the "Policy Holder Address" field.
4.  Enter the Policy Holder's Date of Birth in the "Policy Holder Date of Birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
5.  Enter the Policy Holder's phone number in the "Policy Holder Phone" field in the format "(XXX) XXX-XXXX".
6.  Choose the Type of Policy by selecting from "Term Life", "Whole Life", or "Other" in the "Type of Policy" field.
7.  Enter the Policy Amount in the "Policy Amount" field.
8.  Enter the Policy Effective Date in the "Policy Effective Date" field.
9.  Enter the Policy End Date in the "Policy End Date" field.
10. Select the Policy Status from "Active", "Inactive", or "Canceled" in the "Policy Status" field.
11. Enter the Policy Owner's Name in the "Policy Owner Name" field.
12. Enter the Policy Owner's address in the "Policy Owner Address" field.
13. Enter the Policy Owner's Date of Birth in the "Policy Owner Date of Birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
14. Enter the Policy Owner's phone number in the "Policy Owner Phone" field in the format "(XXX) XXX-XXXX".
15. The "Policy Holder Signature" and "Policy Owner Signature" fields are for collecting signatures, but they do not require any input.
16. Enter the Policy Owner's Date of Signature in the "Policy Owner Signature Date" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
17. Enter the Authorized Person's Name in the "Authorized Person Name" field.
18. Enter the Authorized Person's Title in the "Authorized Person Title" field.
19. Enter the Authorized Person's phone number in the "Authorized Person Phone" field in the format "(XXX) XXX-XXXX".
20. Enter the Authorization Date in the "Authorization Date" field.
21. Enter the Authorization Expiration Date in the "Authorization Expiration Date" field.
22. Enter the Policy Cancellation Date in the "Policy Cancellation Date" field.
23. Enter the Effective Date of Policy in the "Policy Effective Date" field.
24. Note: Make sure to enter the dates in the correct format and fill out the required information as per your use case.
