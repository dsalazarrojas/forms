# Tax Preparation Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from clients about their tax preparation needs.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, using the options provided.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please reach out to our team for guidance.
3. Please complete each section according to your situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `number`, required: false): Please provide your contact information, including your phone number.
* **Tax Year** (`tax_year`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the tax year for which you are preparing your tax return.
* **Tax Return Status** (`tax_return_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select the status of your tax return, including filed, amended, or audit.
* **Filing Type** (`filing_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select the type of tax return you are filing, including single, joint, or married.
* **Dependents** (`dependents`, `number`, required: false): Please enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Income Source** (`income_source`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all the sources of your income, such as wage, business, capital gain, social security, or interest and dividends.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select your employment status, including employed, unemployed, student, or retired.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your current address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.

## Tips
Please provide accurate and clear information in each field. If you need help or clarification, you can reach out to our team.
