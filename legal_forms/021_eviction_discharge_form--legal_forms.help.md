# eviction_discharge_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to document the details of an eviction case, including the tenant's name, landlord's name, reason for discharge, date of discharge, witness information, and other relevant particulars.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling in the **Tenant Name**, which is a required field.
2.  Enter the **Landlord Name** in the corresponding field, which is also required.
3.  Provide a clear and concise **Reason for Discharge**, detailing the circumstances surrounding the eviction.
4.  Enter the **Date of Discharge**, which marks the specific date of the eviction.
5.  The **Witness Name** field should be filled in accurately, as the witness provides valuable testimony in the process.
6.  Obtain a **Witness Signature**, which confirms the witness's involvement in the eviction process.
7.  Ensure that the **Landlord Signature** is obtained, confirming the landlord's acknowledgment of the eviction.
8.  Enter the **Date of Landlord Signature**, which verifies the time of the landlord's acknowledgment.
9.  If applicable, fill in the **Court Fees** and **Other Fees** fields, which may be relevant to the case.
10.  Complete the **Witness Address**, **Witness Phone**, and **Witness Email** fields, if the witness is to be contacted for further information.
11.  Fill in the **Witness Date of Birth**, **Witness License Number**, **Witness License State**, and **Witness License Expiration** fields, if relevant to the witness's identity and involvement.
12.  Complete the **Witness Occupation** field, which helps in understanding the witness's relationship with the tenant.
13.  Fill in the **Witness License Number** and **Witness License State** fields, which are essential for verifying the witness's identity.
14.  The **Court Documents** and **Other Documents** fields are optional, but may be necessary for further documentation.
15.  Finally, review and submit the form once you have filled in all required and applicable fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the tenant involved in the eviction case.
*   **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the landlord who is discharging the tenant.
*   **Reason for Discharge** (`reason_for_discharge`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise explanation of the reason for the eviction discharge.
*   **Date of Discharge** (`date_of_discharge`, `date`, required): Enter the date on which the eviction was discharged.
*   **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the witness who can provide testimony in the eviction case.
*   **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, required): Obtain a signature from the witness confirming their involvement in the eviction process.
*   **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, `text`, required): Obtain a signature from the landlord confirming their acknowledgment of the eviction discharge.
*   **Date of Landlord Signature** (`date_of_landlord_signature`, `date`, required): Verify the time of the landlord's signature.
*   **Court Fees** (`court_fees`, `text`, required): Fill in if court fees are applicable to the case.
*   **Other Fees** (`other_fees`, `text`, optional): Fill in if there are other fees involved in the case.
*   **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the witness, if required.
*   **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the witness, if required.
*   **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the witness, if required.
*   **Witness Date of Birth** (`witness_date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of birth of the witness, if required.
*   **Witness Relationship** (`witness_relationship`, `text`, optional): Fill in the witness's relationship to the tenant, if necessary.
*   **Witness Occupation** (`witness_occupation`, `text`, optional): Enter the witness's occupation, if relevant.
*   **Witness License Number** (`witness_license_number`, `text`, optional): Enter the witness's license number, if required.
*   **Witness License State** (`witness_license_state`, `text`, optional): Enter the witness's license state, if required.
*   **Witness License Expiration** (`witness_license_number_expiration`, `date`, optional): Enter the witness's license expiration date, if required.
*   **Court Documents** (`court_documents`, `text`, optional): Fill in if any court documents are involved in the case.
*   **Other Documents** (`other_documents`, `text`, optional): Fill in if other documents are involved in the case.
*   **Reason for Discharge Hint** (`reason_for_discharge_hint`, `text`, required): Provide a clear and concise hint for the reason of discharge.
*   **Date of Discharge Hint** (`date_of_discharge_hint`, `date`, required): Enter a hint for the date of discharge.

## Tips

*   Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required fields are completed.
*   Verify the accuracy of witness and witness's contact information.
*   Ensure that all dates are formatted correctly and according to the required format.
