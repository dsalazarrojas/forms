# trademark_infringement_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report a trademark infringement incident. It provides a structured way to collect relevant information about the incident, including details about the trademark owner, the type of trademark, the date and location of the infringement, and any supporting evidence or witness statements.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Fill out the required fields with accurate information about the trademark infringement incident.
- Start by filling out all required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*) or labeled as "Required" in this guide.
- Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and truthful.

### Step 2: Provide additional information if necessary.
- If you have any additional comments or statements that are relevant to the incident, you can add them to the "Additional Comments" field.
- You can also attach any supporting images or evidence to the "Infringement Image" field if it's relevant to the incident.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Report A Trademark Infringement** (`report_infringement`, text, Required):
  Fill in a brief description of the incident, stating how the trademark was infringed upon.
* **Trademark Owner Name** (`trademark_owner_name`, text, Required):
  Enter the name of the trademark owner or entity owning the trademark in question.
* **Trademark Owner Address** (`trademark_owner_address`, text, Required):
  Provide the address of the trademark owner or entity owning the trademark in question.
* **Trademark Type** (`trademark_type`, select_one, Required):
  Choose from the following options: Trademark, Service Mark, Certification Mark, or Collective Mark, to indicate the type of trademark that was infringed upon.
* **Date of Infringement** (`date_of_infringement`, date, Required):
  Enter the date when the trademark infringement occurred.
* **Description of Infringement** (`description_of_infringement`, text, Required):
  Provide a detailed description of the incident, including what happened and how it affected the trademark owner or entity.
* **Infringement Location** (`infringement_location`, text, Required):
  Enter the location where the trademark infringement occurred.
* **Trademark Number** (`trademark_number`, text, Required):
  Enter the trademark registration number or any other identifying number associated with the trademark.
* **Infringement Image** (`infringement_image`, text, Optional):
  If relevant, attach an image or document that supports your claim of trademark infringement.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`, text, Optional):
  If you have any witness statements or corroborating evidence, provide them here.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional):
  If you have any additional information or comments that are not mentioned above, add them here.
