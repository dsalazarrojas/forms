# Lease Termination Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used by landlords to document the termination of a lease agreement. It helps to provide a clear and concise record of the reasons for termination, notice periods, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the lease termination, including the reason for termination and the date of termination.
2. Provide details about the notice period given to the tenant.
3. If the reason for termination is "Other", please provide a brief description of the reason.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lease Termination Declaration**: This is a brief statement of the lease termination, which should be a clear and concise description of the reason for termination.
* **Landlord Name**: This field is for the name of the landlord who is terminating the lease.
* **Tenant Name**: This field is for the name of the tenant who is being terminated.
* **Reason for Termination**: Select the reason for termination from the list provided (e.g. Tenant moved out, Landlord's economic hardship, etc.) or type a custom reason in the "Reason for Termination other" field.
* **Date Terminated**: This is the date on which the lease was terminated.
* **Termination Amount**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the amount of termination is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Lease End Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the lease end date is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Notice Period**: This is the time frame given to the tenant before the lease termination takes effect.
* **Lease Term Start Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the start date of the lease term is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Lease Term End Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the end date of the lease term is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Lease Duration**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the duration of the lease is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Lease Agreement Signed Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the signed date of the lease agreement is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Reason for Termination other**: This field is for providing additional information about the reason for termination if it is not listed in the provided options.
* **Landlord's Name other**: This field is for the name of the other landlord if the original landlord is not available.
* **Tenant's Lease Id**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Landlord's Lease Id**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Other Lease Term Start Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the start date of the other lease term is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Other Lease Term End Date**: This field is not applicable for this form, as the end date of the other lease term is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Tenant's Other Lease Id**: This field is not applicable for this form, as it is not relevant to the lease termination declaration.
* **Other Reason for Termination**: This field is for providing additional information about the reason for termination if it is not listed in the provided options.
