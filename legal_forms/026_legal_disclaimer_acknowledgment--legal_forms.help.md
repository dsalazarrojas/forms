# Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment form is a document that requires users to confirm their understanding and agreement to the organization's legal terms and conditions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment" field and enter your response in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment** (`Legal Disclaimer Acknowledgment`, text, optional): This field is a text box where you can enter your response to the legal disclaimer. Please provide a clear and concise statement confirming that you have understood and agreed to the terms and conditions. 
* **confirmation** (`confirmation`, select one, optional): This field asks you to confirm your understanding and agreement to the legal disclaimer. Please select either 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your confirmation. 
* **organization id** (`organization id`, text, optional): This field is not currently used in this form. Please ignore it.
