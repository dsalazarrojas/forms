# vehicle_repossession_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and document information about a vehicle repossession. It captures details about the vehicle, the owner, the lender, and the authorization process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Answer the questions accurately and completely, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
* Choose the correct options for vehicle type and authorization status.
* Provide the correct date and time of repossession.
* Sign the form with your name and title (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle that is being repossessed (e.g., Car, Truck, Motorcycle, etc.).
* **Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle being repossessed.
* **Make**: Enter the make of the vehicle being repossessed.
* **Model**: Enter the model of the vehicle being repossessed.
* **Vehicle VIN**: Enter the Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) of the vehicle being repossessed.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the vehicle's owner.
* **Owner Address**: Enter the address of the vehicle's owner.
* **Lender Name**: Enter the name of the lender (financial institution).
* **Lender Address**: Enter the address of the lender (financial institution).
* **Reason for Repossession**: Enter the reason for the repossession (e.g., default, foreclosure, etc.).
* **Date of Repossession**: Enter the date of the repossession.
* **Time of Repossession**: Enter the time of the repossession.
* **Authorization**: Select the authorization status (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Submitted By**: Enter your name and title (if applicable).
* **Submitter Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself and your role.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter your address.
* **Submitter Company**: Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Submitter Title**: Enter your title (if applicable).
* **Submitter Date**: Enter the date you submitted this form.
* **Submitter Time**: Enter the time you submitted this form.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the repossession.
* **Submitter Signature**: Sign the form with your signature.
* **Lender Signature**: Sign the form with the lender's signature.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* Double-check all entered information for accuracy.
* Complete all fields relevant to your role and involvement in the repossession process.
