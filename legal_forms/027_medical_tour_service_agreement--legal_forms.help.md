# Medical Tour Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from both the user and service provider for the medical tourism process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as User First Name, User Last Name, Date of Birth, and User Contact Number in sections 1-4.
2. For medical facility information, select the service providing country (e.g. Japan, USA, Canada, Australia) in section 7 and enter Medical Facility License Number in section 9.
3. Ensure that the medical facility's status is set to Valid in section 10.
4. Enter the service providing facility's address, phone number, and name in sections 11-13.
5. Enter the service provider's email and license number in sections 14 and 15.
6. Review the medical tourism agreement terms in sections 16-20.
7. Finally, enter any additional medical history and medical service terms if necessary in sections 21-25.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (1, text, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **User Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required: true): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **User Address** (4, text, required: false): Please enter your address.
* **User Contact Number** (5, text, required: false): Please enter your contact number.
* **User Email** (6, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Service Providing Country** (7, select_one, required: true): Select the country where the medical service will be provided (e.g. Japan, USA, Canada, Australia).
* **Service Providing City** (8, text, required: false): Please enter the city where the medical service will be provided.
* **Medical Facility License Number** (9, text, required: true): Please enter the medical facility's license number.
* **Medical Facility Status** (10, select_one, required: true): Ensure that the medical facility's status is set to Valid.
* **Service Providing Facility Address** (11, text, required: false): Please enter the address of the service providing facility.
* **Service Providing Facility Phone** (12, text, required: false): Please enter the phone number of the service providing facility.
* **Service Provider Name** (13, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Provider Email** (14, email, required: false): Please enter the email address of the service provider.
* **Service Provider License Number** (15, text, required: true): Please enter the license number of the service provider.
* **Medical Tourism Agreement Terms** (16, text, required: false): Please review the medical tourism agreement terms.
* **Medical Tourism Agreement Terms 2** (17, text, required: false): Please review the medical tourism agreement terms 2.
* **User Medical History** (18, text, required: false): Please enter any relevant medical history.
* **Medical Service Terms** (19, text, required: false): Please review the medical service terms.
* **Medical Tourism Agreement Terms 3** (20, text, required: false): Please review the medical tourism agreement terms 3.
* **User Medical History 2** (21, text, required: false): Please enter any additional medical history.
* **Medical Service Terms 2** (22, text, required: false): Please review the medical service terms 2.
* **Medical Tourist Agreement** (23, text, required: false): Please enter any medical tourist agreement terms.
* **Medical Tourism Agreement Terms 4** (24, text, required: false): Please review any additional medical tourism agreement terms.
* **Service Terms** (25, text, required: false): Please review any service terms.
