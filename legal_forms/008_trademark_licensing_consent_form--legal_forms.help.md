# Trademark Licensing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trademark Licensing Consent Form is a legal document used to obtain consent from a trademark owner to license their trademark.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business License Number** (`business_license_number`, 2, optional): This field is used to enter the business license number if required.
* **Trademark Owner Agreement** (`trademark_owner_agreement`, 3, optional select one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the trademark owner agrees to the terms of the license agreement.
* **Trademark Agreement Terms** (`trademark_agreement_terms`, 4, optional): Enter any additional terms or conditions for the trademark license agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, 5, optional): This field is used to enter the date of signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, 6, required): This is a signature field that requires a digital or physical signature to confirm consent.
* **Business License Number Hint** (`business_license_number_hint`, 7, optional): This field provides a hint for the business license number.
* **Trademark Ownership Status** (`trademark_ownership_status`, 8, optional): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the status of the trademark.
* **Trademark Agreement Duration** (`trademark_agreement_duration`, 9, optional select one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to determine if the agreement is in duration.
* **Trademark Agreement Termination** (`trademark_agreement_termination`, 10, optional select multiple): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each termination option.
* **Business License Number Hint** (`business_license_number_hint`, 11, optional): This field provides a hint for the business license number.

## Tips
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that all required fields are completed before signing the document.
* Keep a record of the submitted form for future reference.
