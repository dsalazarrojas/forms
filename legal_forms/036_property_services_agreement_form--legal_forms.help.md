# property_services_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Services Agreement Form is a document used to establish the terms and conditions of a service relationship between a client and a service provider. It helps to clarify the responsibilities, rights, and obligations of both parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Legal Description field with a brief explanation of the services being provided.
2. Select Yes or No for the Legal Service field, indicating whether the service is legal in nature.
3. Choose the appropriate Service Type from the list of options.
4. Enter the Service Provider's name in the Service Provider field.
5. Select the start and end dates of the service in the Service Start Date and Service End Date fields, respectively.
6. Choose the frequency of the service from the list of options in the Service Frequency field.
7. Enter the agreement terms in the Agreement field.
8. Select the Agreement Type from the list of options.
9. Enter any additional comments in the Terms field.
10. Add any additional comments in the Additional Comments field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Services Agreement Form** (`property_services_agreement_form_form`): This is the main field where you can enter a brief description of the service.
* **Legal Description** (`property_services_agreement_form_legal_description`): Enter a detailed description of the legal services being provided.
* **Legal Service** (`property_services_agreement_form_legal_service`): Select Yes or No to indicate whether the service is legal in nature.
* **Service Type** (`property_services_agreement_form_service_type`): Choose from the list of options to describe the type of service being provided.
* **Service Provider** (`property_services_agreement_form_service_provider`): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Service Start Date** (`property_services_agreement_form_service_start_date`): Enter the start date of the service.
* **Service End Date** (`property_services_agreement_form_service_end_date`): Enter the end date of the service.
* **Service Frequency** (`property_services_agreement_form_service_frequency`): Choose from the list of options to describe the frequency of the service.
* **Agreement** (`property_services_agreement_form_agreement`): Enter the terms and conditions of the service agreement.
* **Agreement Type** (`property_services_agreement_form_agreement_type`): Select from the list of options to describe the type of agreement.
* **Terms** (`property_services_agreement_form_terms`): Enter any additional terms and conditions of the service agreement.
* **Additional Comments** (`property_services_agreement_form_additional_comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the service.
* **Service Provider 1-12** (`property_services_agreement_form_service_provider_1`-`property_services_agreement_form_service_provider_12`): Enter the names of multiple service providers (if necessary). 
* **Additional Comments 2** (`property_services_agreement_form_additional_comments_2`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the service (optional).
