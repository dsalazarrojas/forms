# Estate Planning Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients to create an estate plan. The details provided will be used to provide personalized advice and planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your name in the **Client Name** field.
2. Provide your address in the **Address** field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
4. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
5. Select the type of estate you are interested in planning for from the **Estate Type** dropdown menu.
6. List all of your assets in the **Assets** field.
7. List all beneficiaries you would like to include in your estate plan in the **Beneficiaries** field.
8. If applicable, enter the name of your legal representative in the **Legal Representative** field.
9. In the **Estate Plan** field, provide any additional information or details about your estate planning needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Please enter your current address.
- **Contact Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Please enter your contact phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter your email address.
- **Estate Type** (`estate_type`, select_one, required/optional): Please select the type of estate you are interested in planning for, such as Living Trust, Will, Power of Attorney, or Living Will.
- **Assets** (`assets`, text, required/optional): List all assets you own, such as property, investments, or retirement accounts.
- **Beneficiaries** (`beneficiaries`, text, required/optional): List all individuals or organizations you would like to include as beneficiaries in your estate plan.
- **Legal Representative** (`legal_representative`, text, required/optional): If you have a legal representative, such as a lawyer or attorney, please enter their name.
- **Estate Plan** (`estate_plan`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional information or details about your estate planning needs, please include it here.

## Tips
- Please be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns about this form, please reach out to our estate planning team.
- We will use the information provided to create a personalized estate plan tailored to your needs.
