# land_ownership_records_release_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The land_ownership_records_release_form is a document used to release ownership records for a piece of land. This form is used to provide information about the land owner, real estate agency, and real estate agent, among other details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information for each field, ensuring that all required fields are completed.
2. If you are an authorized individual, select "True" for "release_authorization" to confirm that you are authorized to release the records.
3. Provide detailed information about the real estate agency and real estate agent, including their contact information and license details.
4. Enter the date and time of the release, if applicable.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about the release, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **owner_info** (id: 1, text, required: true): Enter the name and contact information of the land owner.
* **release_authorization** (id: 2, select_one, required: true): Select "True" if you are authorized to release the records.
* **realtor_info** (id: 3, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the realtor.
* **record_release** (id: 4, text, required: false): Enter details about the record release, if necessary.
* **real_estate_agency_info** (id: 5, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the real estate agency.
* **record_verification** (id: 6, text, required: false): Enter details about the record verification, if necessary.
* **real_estate_pro_info** (id: 7, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the real estate professional.
* **signature** (id: 8, select_multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" if the signature is required.
* **date** (id: 9, date, required: false): Enter the date of the release, if applicable.
* **time** (id: 10, time, required: false): Enter the time of the release, if applicable.
* **additional_comments** (id: 11, text, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the release.
* **real_estate_agency** (id: 12, text, required: false): Enter the name of the real estate agency.
* **real_estate_agent** (id: 13, text, required: false): Enter the name of the real estate agent.
* **real_estate_license** (id: 14, text, required: false): Enter the license number of the real estate agent or agency.
* **real_estate_address** (id: 15, text, required: false): Enter the address of the real estate agency or agent.
* **contact_info** (id: 16, text, required: false): Enter the contact information of the real estate agency or agent.
* **real_estate_phone** (id: 17, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the real estate agency or agent.
* **real_estate_email** (id: 18, text, required: false): Enter the email of the real estate agency or agent.
* **real_estate_license_num** (id: 19, text, required: false): Enter the license number of the real estate agent or agency.
* **real_estate_license_date** (id: 20, text, required: false): Enter the license date of the real estate agent or agency.
* **real_estate_notes** (id: 21, text, required: false): Add any additional notes about the real estate agency or agent.
* **real_estate_agency_phone** (id: 22, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the real estate agency.
* **real_estate_agency_email** (id: 23, text, required: false): Enter the email of the real estate agency.
* **real_estate_agency_license_num** (id: 24, text, required: false): Enter the license number of the real estate agency.
* **real_estate_agency_license_date** (id: 25, text, required: false): Enter the license date of the real estate agency.

Note: This form is designed to be completed by authorized individuals only, and is intended to provide detailed information about the land ownership records release.
