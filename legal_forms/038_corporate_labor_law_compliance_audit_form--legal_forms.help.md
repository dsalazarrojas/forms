# Corporate Labor Law Compliance Audit Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Corporate Labor Law Compliance Audit Form is designed to help auditors assess a company's compliance with relevant labor laws. This form collects information about the company, its contact person, location, and other relevant details to facilitate the audit process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the company's name in the "Company Name" field.
2. Provide the name and title of the person to contact in the "Contact Person" field.
3. Enter the location of the company in the "Location" field.
4. Select the date of the visit from the calendar icon in the "Date" field.
5. Select the time of the visit from the clock icon in the "Time" field.
6. Enter the name of the manager in the "Manager Name" field.
7. Enter the phone number of the manager in the "Manager Phone" field.
8. (Optional) Provide additional notes in the "Notes" field.
9. Enter the email address of the company in the "Email" field.
10. Enter comments or observations from the audit in the "Comments" field.
11. Select the status of the audit from the dropdown menu in the "Audit Status" field.
12. Select the status of labor law compliance from the dropdown menu in the "Labor Law Compliance Status" field.
13. Select the status of follow-up from the dropdown menu in the "Follow-up Status" field.
14. (Optional) Select additional details about labor law compliance in the "Labor Law Compliance Notes" field.
15. Select the review frequency from the dropdown menu in the "Review Frequency" field.
16. (Optional) Select the next follow-up date and time from the calendar and clock icons in the "Next Follow-up Date" and "Next Follow-up Time" fields.
17. (Optional) Select additional details about labor law compliance in the "Review Notes" field.
18. (Optional) Select the last review date and time from the calendar and clock icons in the "Last Review Date" and "Last Review Time" fields.
19. (Optional) Select the status of the last review from the dropdown menu in the "Last Review Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the company's name to be audited.
* **Contact Person** (`2`, `text`, required): Provide the name and title of the person to contact for further information.
* **Location** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter the company's location or address.
* **Date** (`4`, `date`, required): Select the date of the visit from the calendar icon.
* **Time** (`5`, `time`, required): Select the time of the visit from the clock icon.
* **Manager Name** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the manager or person responsible.
* **Manager Phone** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the manager.
* **Notes** (`8`, `note`, optional): Provide additional notes or comments about the audit.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required): Enter the company's email address.
* **Comments** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter comments or observations from the audit.
* **Audit Status** (`11`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of the audit from the dropdown menu.
* **Labor Law Compliance Status** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of labor law compliance from the dropdown menu.
* **Follow-up Status** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Select the status of follow-up from the dropdown menu.
* **Labor Law Compliance Notes** (`14`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional details about labor law compliance.
* **Review Frequency** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Select the review frequency from the dropdown menu.
* **Review Notes** (`22`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select additional details about labor law compliance.
* **Last Review Date** (`24`, `date`, optional): Select the last review date from the calendar icon.
* **Last Review Time** (`25`, `time`, optional): Select the last review time from the clock icon.
* **Last Review Status** (`25`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of the last review from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Use the calendar and clock icons to select dates and times for fields like "Date", "Time", "Next Follow-up Date", and "Last Review Date".
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the auditor or compliance officer for guidance.
* Save your progress and review the form regularly to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* If you need to edit any field, make sure to update the entire form, as some fields are linked or depend on others.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness. 

Note: This guide is for general guidance only, and specific instructions or guidelines may vary based on company or organizational policies. Consult your local compliance officer or auditor for any clarification or updates.
