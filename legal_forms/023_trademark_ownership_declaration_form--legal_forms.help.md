# Trademark Ownership Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to declare details about a trademark and its owner for the purpose of managing trademarks. Please fill out this form to provide the required information about your business and trademark ownership.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Read the instructions and explanations for each field carefully.
2. Complete each field with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
4. Submit the form to complete the declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_business_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your business as it is registered or known to the public.
  * Please enter the exact name of your business.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Business Address
* **Business Address** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_business_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address where your business is registered or located.
  * Provide the physical or postal address where your business operates.
  * This field may also be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Legal Entity Name
* **Legal Entity Name** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_legal_entity_name`, text, required/optional): If your business is a legal entity, such as a corporation or partnership, enter its legal name.
  * Check with your business registration documents if you are unsure about this field.
  * This field is required for legal entities.

### Trademark ID Number
* **Trademark ID Number** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_trademark_id_number`, number, required/optional): Enter the official trademark ID number assigned by the relevant authority.
  * Check with the trademark office or your registration documents if you are unsure about this field.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Date of Registration
* **Date of Registration** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_date_of_registration`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your trademark was registered.
  * Check with your registration documents or the trademark office for the exact date.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Date of Last Update
* **Date of Last Update** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_date_of_last_update`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your trademark registration was last updated.
  * Check with your registration documents or the trademark office for the exact date.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Owner Name
* **Owner Name** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_owner_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the owner or the person responsible for the trademark.
  * Please enter the exact name of the owner as it appears on your registration documents.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Owner Address
* **Owner Address** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_owner_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the owner or the person responsible for the trademark.
  * Provide the physical or postal address of the owner.
  * This field may also be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Owner Email
* **Owner Email** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_owner_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the owner or the person responsible for the trademark.
  * Please ensure the email address is accurate and up-to-date.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Owner Phone
* **Owner Phone** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_owner_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the owner or the person responsible for the trademark.
  * Please ensure the phone number is accurate and up-to-date.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Is Licensed
* **Is Licensed** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_is_licensed`, select_one, required/optional): Check if your trademark is licensed to the owner.
  * Answer 'Yes' if the trademark is licensed to the owner.
  * Answer 'False' if the trademark is not licensed to the owner.

### Is Registered with TPO
* **Is Registered with TPO** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_is_registered_with_tpo`, select_multiple, required/optional): Check if your trademark is registered with the Trademark Office or a similar authority.
  * Answer 'Yes' if the trademark is registered with the Trademark Office.
  * Answer 'False' if the trademark is not registered with the Trademark Office.

### Date of Last Renewal
* **Date of Last Renewal** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_date_of_last_renewal`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your trademark was last renewed.
  * Check with your registration documents or the trademark office for the exact date.
  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Date of Last Publication

  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Date of First Approval

  * This field may be marked as required depending on your jurisdiction's regulations.

### Date of Last Rejection
* **Date of Last Rejection** (`trademark_ownership_declaration_form_date_of_last_rejection`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when your trademark was last rejected.

## Tips
