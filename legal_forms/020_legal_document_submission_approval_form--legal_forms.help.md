# Legal Document Submission Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and approval for legal documents. It's intended for users to provide details about submitted documents, including user and submitter information, document details, and approval status. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your User Information, including any relevant details you'd like to share with the reviewer.
2.  Enter Document Information, such as the document type and submission date and time.
3.  Enter Submitter Information, including details about who submitted the document.
4.  Provide Approval Information, indicating the approval status of the document.
5.  Enter any comments or feedback about the document.
6.  Select the Document Type.
7.  Enter the Submission Date and Time.
8.  Enter the Reviewer's Name and Email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (id: 1)**: Please enter your name, company, and any other relevant details you'd like to share with the reviewer.
* **Document Information (id: 2)**: Enter the details about the document you're submitting, such as its type and date.
* **Submitter Information (id: 3)**: Enter the name and contact information of the person submitting the document.
* **Approval Information (id: 4)**: Indicate the approval status of the document, such as approved or rejected.
* **Submit (id: 5)**: Click this button to submit the form.
* **Review Information (id: 6)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the document.
* **Approve (id: 7)**: Click this button to approve the document.
* **Reject (id: 8)**: Click this button to reject the document.
* **Comments (id: 9)**: Enter any comments or feedback about the document.
* **Document Type (id: 10)**: Select the type of document you're submitting.
* **Submission Date (id: 11)**: Enter the date you submitted the document.
* **Submission Time (id: 12)**: Enter the time you submitted the document.
* **Reviewer (id: 13)**: Enter the name of the person reviewing the document.
* **Reviewer Email (id: 14)**: Enter the email address of the reviewer.
