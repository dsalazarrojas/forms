# legal-case-inquiry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from legal experts about a legal case, including its details, current status, and perceived value. The goal is to gather input from experts to help make informed decisions about the case.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), as they are essential for the form to be complete and processed correctly.
2.  Review each field carefully and provide accurate information to ensure that your input is helpful and relevant.
3.  If you are unsure about any field, you can leave it blank or contact the form administrator for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Case Details**: A brief description of the legal case, including its relevant facts and circumstances. 
*   **Legal Expertise**: Indicate whether you have expertise in this area of law. 
*   **Legal Experts**: List any other legal experts who have worked on or are familiar with the case. 
*   **Case Description**: A more detailed description of the case, providing context and background information. 
*   **Contact Details**: Your name, email, and any other relevant contact information. 
*   **Case Status**: Select the current status of the case, whether it's Active or Inactive. 
*   **Case Value**: Provide an estimate of the case's value, if applicable. 

## Tips

*   Be as accurate and detailed as possible when filling out the form.
*   If you are not sure about any field, you can contact the form administrator for guidance.
*   Make sure to save your input before navigating away from the form.
*   Review the fields carefully to ensure you have provided all necessary information before submitting the form.
