# Acquisition Letter Of Intent Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Acquisition Letter Of Intent Submission form is used to express your interest in acquiring a specific company or asset. It provides a high-level overview of the proposed terms and conditions for the acquisition.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all necessary permissions and approvals to submit this form.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, **required**: true)
	+ Full name of submitting party
* **Company name** (`company_name`, `text`, **required**: false)
	+ Company representing the applicant
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**: true)
	+ Email for correspondence
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **required**: false)
	+ Phone number for quick contact
* **Target entity** (`target_entity`, `text`, **required**: true)
	+ Company or asset proposed for acquisition
* **Proposed terms summary** (`proposed_terms_summary`, `text`, **required**: true)
	+ Brief summary of proposed consideration and terms
* **Indicative timeline** (`indicative_timeline`, `text`, **required**: false)
	+ Expected timeline for due diligence and closing
* **Confidentiality requirements** (`confidentiality_requirements`, `select_one`, **required**: false)
	+ Options: NDA in place, Confidentiality requested, No confidentiality required
* **Financing arrangements** (`financing_arrangements`, `text`, **required**: false)
	+ High-level description of financing source
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, **required**: true)
	+ Type full name to sign letter of intent
* **Date submitted** (`date_submitted`, `date`, **required**: true)
	+ Date of LOI submission
* **Additional documents** (`additional_documents`, `text`, **required**: false)
	+ Attachments such as financials or NDAs

**Tips**

* Please ensure all fields are complete and accurate before submission.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* This form is a starting point for further discussion and negotiation.
* Review and agree upon the terms and conditions before proceeding with the acquisition process.
