# Wholly Owned Subsidiary Incorporation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track the progress of a wholly owned subsidiary incorporation process. It will help you keep track of the entity's details, the incorporation progress, and any additional information needed for the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the entity's details, including its name, address, and contact information.
2.  Track the incorporation progress by selecting the appropriate status (e.g., "Yes" or "No") for each relevant task.
3.  Enter the number of completed tasks and any additional notes about the progress.
4.  Provide the incorporation date and status.
5.  Enter the total legal fees paid.
