# Lottery Winnings Agreement Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from lottery winners to ensure they understand and agree to the terms of their winnings. It is a one-time agreement that requires completion by the winner to formalize their understanding and acceptance of the terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Form Details" field and enter a brief description of the lottery win, including the date and time of the draw, the prize amount, and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your Name and Email address in the respective fields.
3. Enter your Address and Phone number in the respective fields.
4. Read the terms and conditions carefully, and select "I agree" from the "Agree" dropdown menu if you understand and accept the terms.
5. If you have any additional comments or questions, please enter them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Details** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_form_1`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the lottery win, including the date and time of the draw, the prize amount, and any other relevant details.
* **Name** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Address** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_address`, text, required): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Agree** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_agree`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to indicate that you understand and accept the terms of the winnings.
* **Comments** (`lottery_winnings_agreement_waiver_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the winnings.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact us at the email address listed above.
* This form is a one-time agreement that must be completed before you can receive your winnings.
