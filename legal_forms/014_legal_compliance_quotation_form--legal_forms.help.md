# Legal Compliance Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather necessary information from clients for a legal compliance quotation. It ensures that all required fields are filled out before proceeding to the next page, ensuring accurate and complete information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing client information, including their name and contact details.
2.  Select the type of service you wish to provide to the client.
3.  Provide quote details, including the quote price and date.
4.  Check the quote terms and conditions you wish to apply.
5.  Enter payment details, if applicable.
6.  Add any additional information to the quote footer.
7.  Agree to the terms and conditions of the quote.
8.  Provide your signature and the date you sign the quote.
9.  Enter billing details, including address and terms.
10. Enter client email and phone number.
11. Add any additional notes about the client.
12.  Confirm whether the quote terms have been accepted by the client.
13.  Confirm whether the quote has been signed by the client.
