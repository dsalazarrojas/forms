# Legal Compliance Quotation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather necessary information from clients for a legal compliance quotation. It ensures that all required fields are filled out before proceeding to the next page, ensuring accurate and complete information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by providing client information, including their name and contact details.
2.  Select the type of service you wish to provide to the client.
3.  Provide quote details, including the quote price and date.
4.  Check the quote terms and conditions you wish to apply.
5.  Enter payment details, if applicable.
6.  Add any additional information to the quote footer.
7.  Agree to the terms and conditions of the quote.
8.  Provide your signature and the date you sign the quote.
9.  Enter billing details, including address and terms.
10. Enter client email and phone number.
11. Add any additional notes about the client.
12.  Confirm whether the quote terms have been accepted by the client.
13.  Confirm whether the quote has been signed by the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Information (1)** (`Client Information`, `text`, required):
    Provides a space for the user to enter the client's name and contact details.
*   **Service Information (2)** (`Service Information`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Allows the user to select the type of service they wish to provide to the client.
*   **Quote Details (3)** (`Quote Details`, `text`, required):
    Collects the details of the quote, including the quote price and date.
*   **Quote Terms (4)** (`Quote Terms`, `text`, required):
    Collects any additional terms and conditions that apply to the quote.
*   **Payment Information (5)** (`Payment Information`, `text`, optional):
    Collects payment details, if applicable.
*   **Quote Footer (6)** (`Quote Footer`, `text`, optional):
    Allows the user to add any additional information to the quote.
*   **Terms and Conditions (7)** (`Terms and Conditions`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the terms and conditions that apply to the quote.
*   **Signature Block (8)** (`Signature Block`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the client's signature.
*   **Payment Terms (9)** (`Payment Terms`, `text`, optional):
    Collects payment terms.
*   **Billing Information (10)** (`Billing Information`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the billing address.
*   **Email (14)** (`Email`, `email`, required):
    Collects the client's email address.
*   **Phone (15)** (`Phone`, `text`, required):
    Collects the client's phone number.
*   **Notes (16)** (`Notes`, `text`, optional):
    Collects any additional notes about the client.
*   **Signature Date (17)** (`Signature Date`, `date`, optional):
    Collects the date of the client's signature.
*   **Billing Terms (18)** (`Billing Terms`, `text`, optional):
    Collects billing terms.
*   **Quote Number (19)** (`Quote Number`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the quote number.
*   **Quote Date (20)** (`Quote Date`, `date`, optional):
    Collects the date of the quote.
*   **Client Name (21)** (`Client Name`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the name of the client.
*   **Quote Price (22)** (`Quote Price`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the quote price.
*   **Client Company (23)** (`Client Company`, `text`, optional):
    Collects the name of the client's company.
*   **Quote Terms Accepted (24)** (`Quote Terms Accepted`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Collects whether the client has accepted the quote terms.
*   **Quote Signed (25)** (`Quote Signed`, `select_one`, required):
    Collects whether the quote has been signed by the client.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out all required fields before proceeding to the next page.
*   Check the quote terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
*   Ensure that the quote has been signed by the client before proceeding with further actions.
