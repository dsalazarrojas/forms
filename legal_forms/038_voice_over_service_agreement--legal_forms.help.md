# Voice Over Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Voice Over Service Agreement form is designed to provide a structured framework for both the client and the voice actor to agree on the terms and conditions of a voice-over project. This form captures essential information to ensure a clear understanding of the project's scope, payment terms, and sign-offs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2.  Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3.  Sign and date the form as indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Client Name**: Provide the name of the client.
*  **Project Name**: Enter the title of the project.
*  **Project Description**: Briefly describe the project, if applicable.
*  **Voice Actor Name**: Enter the name of the voice actor.
*  **Project Terms**: Describe the project's terms and conditions.
*  **Signature 1**: Select "Client Signature" or "Voice Actor Signature" to indicate who is signing the agreement.
*  **Date Signed**: Enter the date when the agreement was signed.
*  **File Upload**: If applicable, upload any relevant files or documents.
*  **Agreement Terms**: Describe the agreement terms, including any specific conditions or requirements.
*  **Agreement Date**: Enter the date when the agreement was made.
*  **Voice Actor Notes**: If applicable, provide notes or comments from the voice actor.
*  **Client Notes**: If applicable, provide notes or comments from the client.
*  **Payment Terms**: Describe the payment terms, including any specific conditions or requirements.
*  **Payment Terms 1-10**: Provide details on each payment term, including any specific conditions or requirements.
*  **Submission Terms**: Describe the submission terms, if applicable.
*  **Voice Actor Signature**: Select "Client Signature" or "Voice Actor Signature" to indicate who is signing the agreement.
*  **Client Signature**: Select "Client Signature" or "Voice Actor Signature" to indicate who is signing the agreement.
