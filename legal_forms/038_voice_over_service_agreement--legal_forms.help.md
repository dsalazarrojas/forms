This form captures essential information to ensure a clear understanding of the project's scope, payment terms, and sign-offs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2.  Review the form carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3.  Sign and date the form as indicated.
