# Sports Organization Legal Complaint Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to report and gather information about sports-related complaints, ensuring that your organization can effectively address and resolve these issues.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Organization Name**, which is the name of your sports organization.
2. Enter the **Contact Person Name** and **Contact Person Phone** and **Contact Person Email** information to ensure we can reach out to you for further clarification or investigation.
3. Choose the **Sport Name** (if applicable) if the complaint is related to a specific sport.
4. Provide the **Date of Occurrence** and **Time of Occurrence** when the incident happened.
5. Specify the **Location of Occurrence** where the incident happened.
6. Indicate whether there were any **Witnesses** to the incident, and if so, provide their names and email addresses.
7. Select the type of **Complaint** you are reporting (e.g., Yes or No).
8. Click on the **Submit** button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, required): Enter the name of your sports organization.
* **Contact Person Name** (`Contact Person Name`, required): Enter the name of the person to contact for further information.
* **Contact Person Phone** (`Contact Person Phone`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Person Email** (`Contact Person Email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Sport Name** (`Sport Name`, optional): If the complaint is related to a specific sport, enter the name of the sport (e.g., football, basketball, etc.).
* **Date of Occurrence** (`Date of Occurrence`, required): Enter the date when the incident happened in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Occurrence** (`Time of Occurrence`, required): Enter the time when the incident happened in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Location of Occurrence** (`Location of Occurrence`, required): Enter the exact location where the incident happened.
* **Witness List (Names and Emails)** (`Witness List (Names and Emails)`, optional): If there were witnesses, provide their names and email addresses.
* **Type of Complaint** (`Type of Complaint`, required): Choose Yes or No to indicate the type of complaint you are reporting.
* **Witness 1 Name** (`Witness 1 Name`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their names.
* **Witness 1 Email** (`Witness 1 Email`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their email addresses.
* **Witness 2 Name** (`Witness 2 Name`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their names.
* **Witness 2 Email** (`Witness 2 Email`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their email addresses.
* **Witness 3 Name** (`Witness 3 Name`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their names.
* **Witness 3 Email** (`Witness 3 Email`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their email addresses.
* **Witness 4 Name** (`Witness 4 Name`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their names.
* **Witness 4 Email** (`Witness 4 Email`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their email addresses.
* **Witness 5 Name** (`Witness 5 Name`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their names.
* **Witness 5 Email** (`Witness 5 Email`, optional): If you reported witnesses, enter their email addresses.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, required): Click to submit your complaint.
