# Ria Registration Support Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ria Registration Support Request Form is designed to collect necessary information from users to assist with their registration process. This form helps to gather details such as user's name, email address, status, jurisdiction, and other relevant information that will be used to support their registration request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the `FirstName` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Select your status from the `Status` dropdown menu.
4. Select your jurisdiction from the `Jurisdiction` dropdown menu.
5. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
6. Enter your date in the `Date` field.
7. Enter your time in the `Time` field.
8. Enter your Legal Name in the `Legal Name` field.
9. Enter your Business Name in the `Business Name` field.
10. Enter your Address in the `Address` field.
11. Enter your City in the `City` field.
12. Select your State from the `State` dropdown menu.
13. Enter your Zip code in the `Zip` field.
14. Enter your Country in the `Country` field.
15. Enter any relevant legal documents in the `Legal Doc 1` to `Legal Doc 8` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **FirstName**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to communicate with you.
* **Status**: Select the current status of your registration request.
* **Jurisdiction**: Select the state or region where you would like to register.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Date**: Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time**: Enter the time in the format 12:00 AM.
* **Legal Name**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter your city or town.
* **State**: Select the state where you would like to register.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip or post code.
* **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
* **Legal Doc 1** to **Legal Doc 8**: Enter any relevant legal documents.
* **Note**: Enter any additional comments or information that may be helpful to support your registration request.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to send your request for registration support.
