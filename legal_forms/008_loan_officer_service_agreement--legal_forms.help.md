# Loan Officer Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for capturing information related to a loan officer's service agreement with a client. It includes client information, loan officer details, services offered, compensation terms, dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide client information, including contact details.
2. Select the loan officer's details, including name and signature.
3. Specify the services offered, such as Mortgage Origination, Mortgage Refinance, and others.
4. Choose the compensation terms, including Flat Fee, Tiered Pricing, or Flat Fee (Tiered Pricing).
5. Enter the agreement start and end dates.
6. Enter the compensation amount and currency.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (id: 1)**: Enter the client's name, contact information (email and phone number), and other relevant details.
* **Loan Officer (id: 2)**: Enter the loan officer's name, signature, and other relevant details.
* **Services Offered (id: 3)**: Select one or more services offered by the loan officer, such as Mortgage Origination, Mortgage Refinance, Home Equity, and others.
* **Compensation Terms (id: 4)**: Choose the compensation terms, including Flat Fee, Tiered Pricing, or Flat Fee (Tiered Pricing).
* **Agreement Start Date (id: 5)**: Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Agreement End Date (id: 6)**: Enter the end date of the agreement.
* **Compensation (id: 7)**: Enter the compensation amount.
* **Notes (id: 8)**: Add any additional notes or comments related to the agreement.
* **Client Consent (id: 9)**: Enter the client's email address for confirmation.
* **Loan Officer Signature (id: 10)**: Enter the loan officer's signature.
* **Client Signature (id: 11)**: Enter the client's signature.
* **Client Date (id: 12)**: Enter the date the client signed the agreement.
* **Loan Officer Date (id: 13)**: Enter the date the loan officer signed the agreement.
* **Assigned User (id: 14)**: Enter the loan officer's name or username.
* **Assigned Date (id: 15)**: Enter the date the loan officer was assigned.
* **Loan Officer Commission (id: 16)**: Enter the loan officer's commission amount.
* **Client Commission (id: 17)**: Enter the client's commission amount.
* **Loan Officer Fees (id: 18)**: Enter the loan officer's fees amount.
* **Loan Officer Fees Currency (id: 19)**: Choose the currency for the loan officer's fees.
* **Client Fees (id: 20)**: Enter the client's fees amount.
* **Client Fees Currency (id: 21)**: Choose the currency for the client's fees.
* **Loan Officer Commission Currency (id: 22)**: Choose the currency for the loan officer's commission.
* **Client Commission Currency (id: 23)**: Choose the currency for the client's commission.
* **Loan Officer Notes (id: 24)**: Add any additional notes or comments from the loan officer.
* **Client Notes (id: 25)**: Add any additional notes or comments from the client.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete.
- Review and understand each field carefully before filling out the form.
- Double-check the dates and compensation amounts for accuracy.
