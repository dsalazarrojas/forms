# Opposition To Venue Changes Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from community members on changes made to the venue. It is intended for those who would like to contest or oppose changes made to the venue.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. **Community Feedback**: In this field, describe your concerns or objections to the changes made to the venue. Please be specific about your reasons for opposing these changes.
2. **Venue Update Details**: In this field, provide additional details about the changes made to the venue.
3. **Opposition Statement**: Here, please submit your formal opposition statement. This is your chance to clearly state your reasons for opposing the changes.
4. **Supporting Documents**: If you have any evidence or supporting documents that back up your claim, upload them here.
5. **Submit**: Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to send your feedback to the relevant authorities.
6. **Email Address**: Please enter your email address, so we can contact you with any updates or further questions.
7. **Phone Number**: If you prefer to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here. (Note: this field is optional)
8. **Venue Update**: Choose one of the options below to describe the nature of the changes you're opposing (e.g., "Changes to rules" or "Changes to rules and regulations").
9. **Opposition Reason**: In this field, provide a clear and concise statement of your opposition reason.
10. **Supporting Parties**: If there are other community members who share your concerns, you may list their names here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Community Feedback** (Required): This field allows you to express your concerns or objections to the changes made to the venue. Please be specific about your reasons.
* **Venue Update Details** (Required): Provide additional details about the changes made to the venue. 
* **Opposition Statement** (Required): Clearly state your formal opposition to the changes.
* **Supporting Documents** (Required): Attach any evidence or supporting documents that support your claim. 
* **Submit** (Not Required): This is not a required field, but it allows you to submit your feedback once you've completed the form.
* **Email Address** (Required): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with updates or questions.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number here.
* **Venue Update** (Optional): Choose one of the options to describe the nature of the changes you're opposing.
* **Opposition Reason** (Required): Provide a clear and concise statement of your opposition reason.
* **Supporting Parties** (Required): If there are other community members who share your concerns, list their names here.

## Tips

* Please be specific and clear in your responses.
* Be respectful of other community members' opinions.
* Provide supporting evidence when possible.
* Make sure to include your email address and phone number so we can contact you with updates or questions.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact us directly.
