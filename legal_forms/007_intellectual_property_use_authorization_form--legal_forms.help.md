# intellectual_property_use_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to request authorization to use intellectual property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the intellectual property you want to use.
2. Provide your user name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your organization name.
5. Select the start and end dates of the use.
6. Enter a brief description of your purpose for using the intellectual property.
7. Provide any additional notes if necessary.
8. Select the type of authorization you need.
9. Sign the form to confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, text, required: false): Enter a title for the form, this will be the title of your request for intellectual property use.
* **Form Description** (`form_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the intellectual property you want to use.
* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required: false): Enter your username.
* **User E-Mail** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Select the start date of your use of the intellectual property.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Select the end date of your use of the intellectual property.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, text, required: false): Briefly describe your purpose for using the intellectual property.
* **Intellectual Property** (`intellectual_property`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the intellectual property you want to use.
* **Authorization Type** (`authorization_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of authorization you need.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Provide any additional notes or comments about your request.
* **User Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form to confirm your submission.
