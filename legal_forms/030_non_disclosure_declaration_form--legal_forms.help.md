# Non Disclosure Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about non-disclosure agreements from employees or individuals in various settings where confidentiality is important.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): This is the name of the individual filling out the form. Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
2.  **Employee Title** (`employee_title`): This is your job title or position within the company or organization.
3.  **Employee Department** (`employee_department`): If applicable, please enter the department or division you belong to within the organization.
4.  **Business Name** (`business_name`): If you are representing a business, please enter the name of your company or organization.
5.  **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): If there is a specific contact person associated with your business or organization, please enter their name.
6.  **Contact E-Mail** (`contact_email`): Please enter the contact email of the person who can be reached for further information.
7.  **Non-Disclosure Agreement** (`non_disclosure_agreement`): Select the type of agreement you are signing, such as "Non-Disclosure Agreement" or "Confidentiality Agreement." If none of the options apply, choose "Other" and describe the type of agreement in the next field.
8.  **Non Disclosure Agreement - Other** (`non_disclosure_agreement_other`): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please provide a brief description of the type of agreement you are referring to.
9.  **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): This is the date you sign the agreement, which should be in standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
10. **Time Signed** (`time_signed`): This is the time you sign the agreement, which should be in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
11. **Non Disclosure Form Signature** (`non_disclosure_form_signature`): Select one of the provided options to confirm that you have read and will adhere to the non-disclosure agreement. You can choose "Signed below, I confirm that I have read and will adhere to the above non-disclosure agreement." or "Not Signed" if you do not intend to sign. If you choose "Other," you can describe your reason in the next field.
12. **Non Disclosure Form Signature - Other** (`non_disclosure_form_signature_other`): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please provide a brief description of why you are not signing the agreement.
13. **Non Disclosure Form Date Signed** (`non_disclosure_form_date_signed`): This is the date you sign the form, which should be in standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
