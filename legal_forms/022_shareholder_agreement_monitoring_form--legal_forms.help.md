# Shareholder Agreement Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shareholder Agreement Monitoring Form is designed to collect and track information about shareholder agreements, ensuring that all relevant details are accurately recorded and easily accessible for regulatory compliance, risk assessment, or other business purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the company name and agreement date in the respective fields.
2. Enter the shareholder name and address in the designated fields.
3. Select the correct provisions and agreement type from the provided options.
4. Enter the number of shareholders and the share percentage.
5. Choose the agreement term and the review frequency from the provided options.
6. Select the status of the agreement and review result.
7. (Optional) Upload files related to the agreement and review result.
8. Add any additional notes or reminders as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company involved in the agreement.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date when the agreement was made.
* **Shareholder Name**: Enter the name of the shareholder(s) involved in the agreement.
* **Shareholder Address**: Enter the address of the shareholder(s) (optional).
* **Provisions**: Select the relevant provisions from the options provided.
* **Shareholders Number**: Enter the number of shareholders involved in the agreement.
* **Agreement Type**: Select the type of agreement from the options provided.
* **Share Percentage**: Enter the percentage of shares owned by the shareholder.
* **Agreement Term**: Enter the term of the agreement.
* **Next Review Date**: (Optional) Enter the date when the next review will take place.
* **Review Frequency**: Select how frequently the agreement should be reviewed (e.g., annually, bi-annually).
* **Status**: Select the current status of the agreement (e.g., active, inactive).
* **Result**: Enter the result of the review (e.g., success, failure).
* **Agreement File**: (Optional) Upload a file related to the agreement.
* **Review Result File**: (Optional) Upload a file related to the review result.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the agreement.
* **Review Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the review.
* **Reminders**: Select any reminders or notifications needed for the agreement review.
* **Reminder Date**: (Optional) Enter the date when the reminder is due.
* **Review Date**: (Optional) Enter the date when the review was conducted.
* **Last Review Date**: (Optional) Enter the date when the last review was conducted.
* **Last Review Frequency**: (Optional) Select the last review frequency.
* **Review Frequency Hint**: A note field for providing additional information about the review frequency.
* **Status Hint**: A note field for providing additional information about the status.
* **Review Result Hint**: A note field for providing additional information about the review result.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the correct format for dates and numbers.
* Upload any relevant files related to the agreement or review result.
* Use the provided options for selections where applicable.
* Add any additional notes or comments as needed.
