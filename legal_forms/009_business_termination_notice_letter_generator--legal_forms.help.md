# Business Termination Notice Letter Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to generate a formal business termination notice letter, which is essential for ending agreements with partners, vendors, or service providers. It ensures that all necessary details are included and that the notice is comprehensive.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company name and address in the "Company Name" and "Company Address" fields.
2. Provide your name and title in the "Sender Name" and "Sender Title" fields.
3. Enter your contact email and phone number in the "Sender Email" and "Sender Phone" fields (optional).
4. Enter the recipient's name and company name in the "Recipient Name" and "Recipient Company Name" fields.
5. Enter the recipient's address in the "Recipient Address" field.
6. Select the type of agreement being terminated from the "Type of Agreement Being Terminated" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the start date of the agreement in the "Agreement Start Date" field.
8. Enter the requested termination date in the "Requested Termination Date" field.
9. Select the reason for termination from the "Reason for Termination" dropdown menu.
10. Provide any additional termination details in the "Additional Termination Details" field (optional).
11. Enter the number of days of notice provided in the "Notice Period Provided" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (1, text, required): Enter your business name, e.g., "ABC Corporation".
* **Company Address** (2, text, required): Enter your business address, e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **Sender Name** (3, text, required): Enter your full name, e.g., "John Doe".
* **Sender Title** (4, text, required): Enter your title or position in the company, e.g., "CEO".
* **Sender Email** (5, email, required): Enter your contact email, e.g., "john.doe@abccorporation.com".
* **Sender Phone** (6, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Recipient Name** (7, text, required): Enter the name of the recipient, e.g., "Jane Smith".
* **Recipient Company Name** (8, text, required): Enter the recipient's company name, e.g., "XYZ Corporation".
* **Recipient Address** (9, text, required): Enter the recipient's address, e.g., "456 Elm St, Anytown, USA 12345".
* **Recipient Email** (10, email, optional): Enter the recipient's email (optional).
* **Type of Agreement Being Terminated** (11, select_one, required): Select the type of agreement being terminated, e.g., "Service Agreement".
* **Agreement Start Date** (12, date, required): Enter the start date of the agreement.
* **Requested Termination Date** (13, date, required): Enter the requested termination date.
* **Reason for Termination** (14, select_one, required): Select the reason for termination, e.g., "Performance Issues".
* **Additional Termination Details** (15, text, optional): Provide any additional termination details or conditions.
* **Notice Period Provided** (16, number, required): Enter the number of days of notice provided.
