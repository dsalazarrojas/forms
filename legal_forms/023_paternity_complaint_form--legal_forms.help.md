# Paternity Complaint Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Paternity Complaint Form is used to document and report incidents or complaints related to paternity, such as allegations of child abuse, neglect, or custody disputes. This form helps to gather information from the complainant, including their contact details and relationship to the child, and provides an opportunity to attach supporting documents if necessary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the complainant's details, including their name, email, and phone number. Ensure you are providing accurate and up-to-date contact information.
2. Select the complainant's relationship to the child from the drop-down menu.
3. Provide a brief description of the incident or complaint in the "Complaint Details" field.
4. If relevant, upload any supporting documents that may help to support the complaint.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Complainant Name** (`complainant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person filing the complaint.
* **Complainant Email** (`complainant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person filing the complaint.
* **Complainant Phone** (`complainant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person filing the complaint. This is not required but can be helpful for follow-up communication.
* **Complainant Date of Birth** (`complainant_dob`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the person filing the complaint.
* **Complainant Address** (`complainant_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the person filing the complaint. This is not required but can be helpful for contact purposes.
* **Complainant Relationship** (`complainant_relation`, select one, required): Select the relationship of the complainant to the child (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Grandmother, Grandfather, Son, Daughter, Other).
* **Complaint Details** (`complaint_details`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of the incident or complaint.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, text, optional): Attach any relevant documents that support the complaint.
* **Upload Supporting Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, optional): Upload the attached supporting documents.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, text, required): Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.
