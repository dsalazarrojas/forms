# custody_agreement_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Custody Agreement Release Form is used to release custody of a child to another individual or organization. This form is crucial for updating the child's custody status and ensuring their well-being and safety. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the parent's name in the `parent_name` field.
2.  Enter the current custodian's name in the `current_custodian` field.
3.  Enter the new custodian's name in the `new_custodian` field.
4.  Provide the child's date of birth in the `date_of_birth` field.
5.  Enter the address where the child resides in the `address` field.
6.  Enter the city where the child resides in the `city` field.
7.  Select the state where the child resides in the `state` field.
8.  Enter the child's zip code in the `zip` field.
9.  Enter the child's phone number in the `phone` field.
10.  Enter the child's email in the `email` field.
11.  Enter the date the agreement is made in the `date` field.
12.  Enter the name of the new custodian in the `custodian` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **parent_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the parent releasing custody.
*   **current_custodian** (text, optional): Enter the name of the current custodian.
*   **new_custodian** (text, optional): Enter the name of the new custodian.
*   **date_of_birth** (date, optional): Enter the child's date of birth.
*   **address** (text, optional): Enter the address where the child resides.
*   **city** (text, optional): Enter the city where the child resides.
*   **state** (select_one, optional): Select the state where the child resides.
*   **zip** (text, optional): Enter the child's zip code.
*   **phone** (text, optional): Enter the child's phone number.
*   **email** (email, optional): Enter the child's email address.
*   **date** (date, optional): Enter the date the agreement is made.
*   **custodian** (text, optional): Enter the name of the new custodian.
*   **Tips**: Ensure all fields are accurate and complete to avoid any issues with the child's custody status.
