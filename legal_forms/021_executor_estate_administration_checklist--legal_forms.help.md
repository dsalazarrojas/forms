# Executor Estate Administration Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to help you track and evaluate the progress of estate administration tasks. It provides a structured approach to assessing your current situation and identifying areas where further action is needed.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and answer the questions honestly.
2. Choose the correct options for each field based on your current situation.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### estate_administration_progress (Page 1)
* **estate_administration_progress** (`Select Multiple`, optional): This field asks if the estate administration is currently progressing (`Yes`) or not (`No`). Select all that apply.

### current_date (Page 2)
* **current_date** (`Date`, optional): Enter the current date to help keep track of your progress over time.

### estate_value (Page 3)
* **estate_value** (`Number`, optional): Enter the current value of the estate to help you understand its financial context.

### executor_info (Page 4)
* **executor_info** (`Note`, optional): This is a field for additional information about the executor's role and responsibilities.

### estate_administration_steps (Page 5)
* **estate_administration_steps** (`Select One`, optional): This field asks if you are actively taking (`Yes`) or not (`No`) the necessary steps to administer the estate.

### key_documents (Page 6)
* **key_documents** (`Select Multiple`, optional): Identify the essential documents required for estate administration. Select all that apply.

### beneficiaries (Page 7)
* **beneficiaries** (`Select Multiple`, optional): Identify the individuals or organizations benefiting from the estate. Select all that apply.

### estate_administration_status (Page 8)
* **estate_administration_status** (`Select One`, optional): Choose the status of the estate administration, either `Active` or `Inactive`.

### next_steps (Page 9)
* **next_steps** (`Select Multiple`, optional): Determine the next steps required for estate administration. Select all that apply.

## Tips

* This form is designed to be a flexible tool to help you track and evaluate your estate administration progress.
* Be sure to complete all required fields and review each section carefully.
* Use this form as a starting point for your estate administration tasks, and refer back to it regularly for updates and progress tracking.
