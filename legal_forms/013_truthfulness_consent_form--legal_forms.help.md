# truthfulness_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This truthfulness consent form is designed to obtain a statement from an individual regarding the accuracy and completeness of the information they provide. It is a critical component in various legal and administrative processes where trustworthiness of information is essential.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information before starting.
* Fill in the truthfulness statement in the "truthfulness_statement" field.
* Provide details about the report in the "report_details" field (optional).
* Enter the date of the truthfulness statement in the "truthfulness_date" field.
* Specify the time of the truthfulness statement in the "truthfulness_time" field.
* Enter your email address in the "truthfulness_email" field.
* Provide your phone number in the "truthfulness_phone" field.
* Enter a statement confirming you have read and agreed to the terms in the "truthfulness_consent_statement" field.
* If you have any additional comments, provide them in the "truthfulness_additional_comments" field (optional).
* Select "Yes" if you have agreed to the terms, otherwise select "No" in the "truthfulness_agreed_to_terms" field.
* If you have any other conditions or terms that apply, provide them in the "truthfulness_agreed_to_terms_other" field (optional).
* Sign your name in the "truthfulness_signature" field.
* Enter your date of birth in the "truthfulness_dob" field.
* Enter your address in the "truthfulness_address" field (optional).
* Specify your city in the "truthfulness_city" field.
* Enter your state or province in the "truthfulness_state" field.
* Enter your zip code in the "truthfulness_zip" field.
* Enter your country of residence in the "truthfulness_country" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Truthfulness Statement (text)**: A statement declaring the accuracy and completeness of the information provided.
* **Report Details (text)**: Optional field for providing more context about the report.
* **Truthfulness Date (date)**: The date of the truthfulness statement, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Truthfulness Time (time)**: The time of the truthfulness statement, in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Truthfulness Email (text)**: The email address of the individual providing the truthfulness statement.
* **Truthfulness Phone (text)**: The phone number of the individual providing the truthfulness statement.
* **Truthfulness Consent Statement (text)**: A statement declaring that you have read and agreed to the terms.
* **Truthfulness Additional Comments (text)**: Optional field for providing additional comments or context.
* **Truthfulness Agreed to Terms (select_one)**: Select "Yes" if you have agreed to the terms, otherwise select "No".
* **Truthfulness Agreed to Terms Other (text)**: Optional field for providing other conditions or terms that apply.
* **Truthfulness Signature (text)**: The individual's signature.
* **Truthfulness Date of Birth (text)**: The date of birth of the individual.
* **Truthfulness Address (text)**: The address of the individual (optional).
* **Truthfulness City (text)**: The city of the individual's residence.
* **Truthfulness State (text)**: The state or province of the individual's residence.
* **Truthfulness Zip (text)**: The zip code of the individual's residence.
* **Truthfulness Country (text)**: The country of the individual's residence.
