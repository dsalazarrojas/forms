# Client Legal Data Summary - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Legal Data Summary form collects basic information about a client's 
legal data, specifically for a particular matter. This form is used to 
create a comprehensive and standardized summary of the client's legal data, 
allowing for easier tracking and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the Client Legal Data Summary form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the label.
2. Enter the client's name in the `Client Name` field.
3. Select the appropriate `Legal Entity Type` from the provided options.
4. Enter the client's `Tax ID or Social Security Number`.
5. Enter the details about the `Matter Name` and `Matter Description`.
6. Enter the `Primary Legal Counsel` and `Case or Matter Number` (if applicable).
7. Enter the contact information, including `Primary Contact Person`, `Email Address`, `Phone Number`, and `Physical Address`.
8. Confirm the `Conflict Check Completed?` and `Engagement Letter Signed?` status.
9. Confirm the `Retainer Received?` status (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: This is the name of the client. Please enter their full name, 
  including their first and last name.
* **Legal Entity Type**: This field allows you to select the type of entity 
  the client represents, such as Individual, Corporation, LLC, Partnership, 
  or Non-Profit.
* **Tax ID or Social Security Number**: This is the client's Tax ID or 
  Social Security Number.
* **Matter Name**: This is the name of the matter the client is involved 
  in.
* **Matter Description**: This is a brief description of the matter.
* **Primary Legal Counsel**: This is the name of the primary legal counsel 
  handling the matter.
* **Case or Matter Number**: This is the case or matter number, if 
  applicable.
* **Primary Contact Person**: This is the name of the primary contact 
  person for the client.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number**: This is the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Physical Address**: This is the physical address of the primary contact 
  person.
* **Conflict Check Completed?**: This field is used to confirm if the 
  conflict check has been completed.
* **Engagement Letter Signed?**: This field is used to confirm if the 
  engagement letter has been signed.
* **Retainer Received?** (if applicable): This field is used to 
  confirm if the retainer has been received.

## Tips

* Please fill out each section carefully and accurately to ensure the 
  information is up-to-date and reliable.
* If you are unsure about any information, please reach out to the legal 
  team for clarification.
* This form is designed to be self-explanatory, but if you have any 
  questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
