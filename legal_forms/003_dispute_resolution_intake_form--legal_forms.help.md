# Dispute Resolution Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a dispute and facilitate a resolution process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Party 1 Name** (`party_1_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the first party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 1 Address** (`party_1_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the first party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 2 Name** (`party_2_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the second party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 2 Address** (`party_2_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the second party involved in the dispute.
* **Nature of Dispute** (`nature_of_dispute`, text, required/optional): Describe the nature of the dispute.
* **Date of Dispute** (`date_of_dispute`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the dispute.
* **Time of Dispute** (`time_of_dispute`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the dispute.
* **Description of Dispute** (`description_of_dispute`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed description of the dispute.
* **Requested Resolution** (`requested_resolution`, text, required/optional): Describe the desired resolution.
* **Resolution Methods** (`resolution_methods`, select multiple, required/optional): Select one or multiple resolution methods (Mediation, Arbitration, Legal Action).
* **Mediators** (`mediators`, select multiple, required/optional): Select one or multiple mediators (John, Jane, Other).
* **Resolution Location** (`resolution_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the resolution should take place.
* **Dispute Type** (`dispute_type`, select one, required/optional): Select the type of dispute (Type 1, Type 2, Type 3).
* **Party 1 Phone** (`party_1_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the first party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 1 Email** (`party_1_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the first party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 2 Phone** (`party_2_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the second party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 2 Email** (`party_2_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email of the second party involved in the dispute.
* **Party 1 Agreed to Mediation** (`party_1_agreed_to_mediation`, select one, required/optional): Check whether the first party agreed to mediation.
* **Party 2 Agreed to Mediation** (`party_2_agreed_to_mediation`, select one, required/optional): Check whether the second party agreed to mediation.
* **Agree to Mediation** (`agreed_to_mediation`, select one, required/optional): Check whether both parties agreed to mediation.
* **Dispute Resolution Date** (`dispute_resolution_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the dispute resolution.
* **Dispute Resolution Time** (`dispute_resolution_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the dispute resolution.
* **Dispute Resolution Status** (`dispute_resolution_status`, select one, required/optional): Select the status of the dispute resolution (Ongoing, Closed, Resolved).
