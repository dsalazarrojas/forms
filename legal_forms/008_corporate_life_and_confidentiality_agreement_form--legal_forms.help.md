# corporate_life_and_confidentiality_agreement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document the corporate life and confidentiality agreement between a company and its lawyer or client. It captures essential details such as the agreement's date, duration, and status, as well as confidential information and any notes related to the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly, as the information will be used to establish a clear understanding between both parties involved.
- Ensure that you have all necessary information required for the fields before proceeding.
- If you need to make corrections or updates, refer to the instructions below for guidance on how to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Corporate Lawyer Name** (`corporate_lawyer_name`, text, optional): This field is intended for the name of the corporate lawyer or legal representative associated with the agreement.
- **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the client or company involved in the agreement.
- **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): This field is intended for the name of the business or company being represented.
- **Confidentiality Terms** (`confidentiality_terms`, note, optional): This field is for any additional terms or conditions related to confidentiality.
- **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): This field is for the date on which the agreement was made or is to be made.
- **Agreement Time** (`agreement_time`, time, optional): This field is for the time at which the agreement was made or is to be made.
- **Agreement Duration** (`agreement_duration`, number, optional): This field is for the expected duration of the agreement.
- **Confidential Information** (`confidential_information`, note, optional): This field is for any confidential information that needs to be shared or kept private according to the agreement.
- **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`, select multiple, optional): This field can be used to indicate the status of the agreement - Accepted, Declined, or Pending.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is for any additional notes or comments related to the agreement. 

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- If you need to make changes or updates after submission, refer back to this guide for guidance. 
- This form is meant to be a general guide; if you are unclear about any information or need further guidance, please contact your legal representative.
