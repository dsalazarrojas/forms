# Emergency Medical Services Negligence Claim Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Medical Services Negligence Claim Intake Form is a standardized template used to collect information from individuals who wish to file a claim against Emergency Medical Services (EMS) for negligence or malpractice. This form aims to gather relevant details about the claimant, witnesses, and other key information to facilitate efficient review and processing of the claim.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
2. Be as detailed as possible when providing information about yourself and any witnesses involved.
3. Double-check your contact information to ensure it is up-to-date and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Claimant Information (Claimant)**: This section is where you will provide your personal details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Claim Details**: This field is for you to describe the incident or event that you are claiming negligence or malpractice against Emergency Medical Services (EMS). Be as detailed as possible in describing what happened.
* **Medical Information**: This field is for you to provide any relevant medical information related to the incident or event. This may include medical history, treatment, or any other relevant medical details.
* **Contact Information**: This section is where you will provide your contact details, including your address, phone number, and email. Please ensure that this information is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Witness Information**: This section is for you to provide information about any witnesses involved in the incident or event. This may include their name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Witness One**: This field is for you to provide information about the first witness, including their name and contact information.
* **Witness Two**: This field is for you to provide information about the second witness, including their name and contact information.
* **Witness One Phone**: This field is for you to provide the phone number of the first witness.
* **Witness Two Phone**: This field is for you to provide the phone number of the second witness.
* **Witness One Email**: This field is for you to provide the email address of the first witness.
* **Witness Two Email**: This field is for you to provide the email address of the second witness.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when providing information about the incident or event.
* If you have any supporting documentation or evidence, please attach it to the form or provide a link to it.
* If you have any further questions or need assistance, please contact Emergency Medical Services (EMS) for support.
