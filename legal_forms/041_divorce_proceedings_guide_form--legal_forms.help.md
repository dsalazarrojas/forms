# Divorce Proceedings Guide Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Proceedings Guide Form is a tool to gather information and guide users through the divorce process. It covers various topics related to divorce, including custody, support, financials, and property division.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Begin with the "What is Custody?" field, which is intended to capture information about custody arrangements.
2. Move on to "Supportive," which is related to spousal support.
3. Answer the "Grounds for Divorce" field, which will help you understand the reasons for divorce.
4. Identify who is filing for divorce in the "Who is Filing" field.
5. Provide reasons for filing in the "Reasons for Filing" field.
6. Review the divorce process steps in the "Divorce Process Steps" field.
7. Enter relevant information about legal documentation in the "Legal Documentation" field.
8. Estimate costs associated with the court process in the "Court Costs" field.
9. Enter financial details in the "Divorce Financials" field.
10. Answer questions about property division in the "Divorce Property" field.
11. Address the topic of divorce and children in the "Divorce Children" field.
12. Enter any additional information in the "Divorce Other" field.
13. Finalize the divorce process in the "Divorce Finalization" field.
14. Leave any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
15. Report on divorce legal expenses in the "Divorce Legal Expenses" field.
16. Enter other expenses related to the divorce process in the "Other Expenses" fields (labelled 17-20).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Divorce Proceedings Guide Form**: This is the form's title and a placeholder to help users understand its purpose.
* **Custody**: This field captures information about custody arrangements, such as visitation, custody agreements, and any other relevant details. Please provide a detailed description of the custody arrangements to help facilitate the process.
* **Supportive**: Enter details about spousal support, including any alimony or spousal maintenance agreements.
* **Grounds for Divorce**: This field is intended to capture the reasons why the divorce is taking place. Please be honest and thorough in your response, as this information will be helpful in understanding the context of the divorce.
* **Who is Filing**: Identify who is initiating the divorce process.
* **Reasons for Filing**: Provide a detailed explanation for the reasons why the divorce is being sought.
* **Divorce Process Steps**: This field guides users through the divorce process, including any necessary steps or procedures.
* **Legal Documentation**: Enter any relevant legal documentation related to the divorce process.
* **Court Costs**: Estimate the costs associated with the court process, including fees, filing fees, and any other expenses.
* **Divorce Financials**: Enter financial details, including income, assets, and debts.
* **Divorce Property**: Provide information about property division, including any assets, properties, or possessions to be divided.
* **Divorce Children**: Address any children involved in the divorce process, including their age, needs, and any other relevant details.
* **Divorce Other**: Enter any additional information not covered in other fields.
* **Divorce Finalization**: Finalize the divorce process, including any necessary steps or procedures.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional comments or notes about the divorce process.
* **Divorce Legal Expenses**: Report on any legal expenses associated with the divorce process.
* **Other Expenses 1-8**: Enter any additional expenses related to the divorce process. 

## Tips
Please note that this form is a guide, and users are encouraged to provide detailed and accurate information to help facilitate the divorce process.
