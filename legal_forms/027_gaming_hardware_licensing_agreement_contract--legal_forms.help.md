# Gaming Hardware Licensing Agreement Contract - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Gaming Hardware Licensing Agreement Contract form is used to document the details of a gaming hardware licensing agreement. This form is used to gather essential information about the agreement, including the equipment type, owner details, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Agreement Details**: In this section, you will provide the details of the licensing agreement, including the type of equipment being licensed, the term of the agreement, and any other relevant information.
2. **Owner Details**: In this section, you will provide information about the owner of the gaming hardware, including their name and contact information.
3. **Event Details**: In this section, you will provide any event-related information, including the date, location, and time of the event. Note that these fields are not required, so if not applicable, please indicate in the "notes" field.
4. **Agreement Status**: In this section, you will indicate the status of the agreement, either Accepted, Denied, or Expired.
5. **Notes**: In this section, you can provide any additional information or comments about the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **License Agreement Details** (page 1):
	+ **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`): Describe the type of gaming hardware being licensed.
	+ **Term** (`agreement_term`): Specify the term of the licensing agreement.
	+ **Equipment Count** (`equipment_count`): Indicate the number of gaming hardware units being licensed.
	+ **Equipment Value** (`equipment_value`): Enter the value of the licensed gaming hardware.
* **Owner Details** (page 1):
	+ **Name** (`owner_name`): Enter the name of the owner of the gaming hardware.
	+ **Contact** (`owner_contact`): Enter the contact information of the owner.
* **Event Details** (page 2):
	+ **Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event (if applicable).
	+ **Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event (if applicable).
	+ **Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event (if applicable).
* **Agreement Status** (page 3):
	+ **Agreement Status** (`agreement_status`): Select the status of the agreement from the provided options (Accepted, Denied, Expired).
* **Notes** (page 4):
	+ **Notes** (`notes`): Provide any additional comments or information about the agreement.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* If a field is not applicable, please provide a clear explanation in the "notes" field.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
