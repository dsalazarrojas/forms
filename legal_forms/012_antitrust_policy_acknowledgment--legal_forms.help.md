# Antitrust Policy Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Antitrust Policy Acknowledgment" form is used to document the acknowledgment of the company's antitrust policy by employees or stakeholders.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*). Start by selecting the date of acknowledgment, then enter your full name, employee ID number (if applicable), department or division, position or job title, and direct manager or supervisor's name. Next, select the antitrust policy version reviewed and enter the date the policy was provided. Indicate your understanding of the antitrust policy, including your completion of antitrust compliance training, your understanding of key antitrust principles, and your agreement to comply with the policy. Finally, confirm that you understand how to report potential violations and that this acknowledgment is required for compliance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of acknowledgment** (`acknowledgment_date`): Enter the date you are acknowledging the policy.
* **Employee or stakeholder full name** (`employee_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Employee ID number** (`employee_id`): Enter your employee ID number if applicable.
* **Department or division** (`department`): Enter your department or division.
* **Position or job title** (`position_title`): Enter your position or job title.
* **Direct manager or supervisor** (`manager_name`): Enter your direct manager or supervisor's name.
* **Antitrust policy version reviewed** (`policy_version`): Select the antitrust policy version you reviewed.
* **Date policy was provided** (`policy_receipt_date`): Enter the date the policy was provided.
* **I have read and understood the antitrust policy** (`policy_read_confirmation`): Select "Yes, fully read and understood" if you have completely read and understood the policy.
* **Have you completed antitrust compliance training?** (`training_completed`): Select "Yes, completed recently" if you completed the training recently, "Yes, completed previously" if you completed the training in the past, "No, but scheduled" if you have a scheduled training, or "False" if you haven't completed the training.
* **Date of most recent training** (`training_date`): Enter the date of your most recent training if you completed it recently.
* **I understand the key antitrust principles** (`key_principles_understanding`): Select "Yes, completely understand" if you understand the key antitrust principles, "Yes, mostly understand" if you partially understand, "Somewhat understand" if you have some understanding, or "Do not understand" if you don't understand.
* **I agree to comply with the antitrust policy** (`policy_agreement`): Select "Yes, I agree" if you agree to comply with the policy.
* **I understand how to report potential violations** (`violation_reporting`): Select "Yes, clear on reporting procedures" if you understand how to report potential violations.
* **I understand this acknowledgment is required for compliance** (`acknowledgment_understanding`): Select "Yes, understood" if you understand that this acknowledgment is required for compliance.
* **Do you have any questions about the policy?** (`questions_about_policy`): Select "True" if you have questions about the policy.
* **If yes, please describe your questions** (`questions_details`): Enter your questions if you selected "True" in the previous question.
* **I understand this acknowledgment will be retained in my file** (`document_retention`): Select "Yes, understood" if you understand that this acknowledgment will be retained in your file.
* **This acknowledgment serves as my signature confirming receipt and understanding** (`legal_acknowledgment`): Select "Yes, I acknowledge" if you understand and confirm this acknowledgment.
* **Supervisor has reviewed and documented** (`supervisor_signature_date`): Enter the date your supervisor reviewed and documented your acknowledgment.
