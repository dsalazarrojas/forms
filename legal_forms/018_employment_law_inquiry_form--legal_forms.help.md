# employment_law_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is for individuals seeking employment law guidance or information who need help filling out the employment_law_inquiry_form.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the employment_law_inquiry_form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the form fields as needed.
2. Choose the relevant employment status from the "employment_status" field.
3. Select the types of employment you are seeking guidance on from the "employment_type" field.
4. Provide your contact details in the "contact_info", "employee_id", "employee_name", "contact_number", and "email_address" fields.
5. Review your selections and submit your form by selecting "submit" from the "submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information for follow-up or communication with you about your employment law inquiry.
* **employee_id** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID or other relevant identifier.
* **employee_name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on any employment-related documents.
* **employment_issue** (`employment_issue`, text, optional): Describe the employment law issue or topic you are inquiring about.
* **employment_status** (`employment_status`, select one, optional): Select your current employment status.
    + Employed: You are currently employed.
    + Self-Employed: You are self-employed.
    + Other: (Other employment status not listed, enter in notes)
* **employment_type** (`employment_type`, select multiple, optional): Select the type of employment you are inquiring about.
    + W-2: Wage and Taxation-related issues.
    + Non-W-2: Non-wage and Taxation-related issues.
* **submit** (`submit`, select one, optional): Confirm that you are submitting your form.
    + submit: Confirm that you are submitting your form.
    + Other: (Indicate other submission method or reason)
* **confirm_submit** (`confirm_submit`, select one, optional): Confirm that you are submitting your form.
    + submit: Confirm that you are submitting your form.
    + Other: (Indicate other submission method or reason)
