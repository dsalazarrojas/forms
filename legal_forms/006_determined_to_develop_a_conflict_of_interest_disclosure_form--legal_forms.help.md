# Determined To Develop A Conflict Of Interest Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential conflicts of interest from individuals in a professional setting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer each question accurately and honestly.
2. If you have a relationship with a business entity, select "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
3. If you have a stock holding, enter the exact percentage of ownership.
4. If you are an officer or board member, provide your title and committee or committee title, if applicable.
5. If you have any other relationships or roles, provide detailed explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conflict of interest disclosure form** (`conflict_of_interest_disclosure_form`, select_multiple): This field asks if you have any relationships with business entities, such as vendors, suppliers, or clients. Please select "Yes" if you have any such relationships.
* **Name** (`name`, text): This field collects your name as it appears on your identification documents or as you would like it to be displayed on your name tag.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text): This field is used to collect your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text): This field is used to collect the department or team you belong to.
* **Business entity** (`business_entity`, text): If you have a relationship with a business entity, enter the name of the entity.
* **Business entity type** (`business_entity_type`, select_one): If you have a relationship with a business entity, select the type of relationship (Business, Non-profit, Non-governmental, Government, Government-University, Government-Agency, Other (specify)).
* **Other business entity** (`other_business_entity`, text): If you have a relationship with a business entity that is not listed in the above options, please enter the name of the entity.
* **Percentage ownership** (`percentage_ownership`, number): If you have a stock holding, enter the exact percentage of ownership.
* **Dollar value** (`dollar_value`, number): If you have a stock holding, enter the dollar value of the holding.
* **Stock holding** (`stock_holding`, text): This field is used to collect any additional details about your stock holding.
* **Stock holding percentage** (`stock_holding_percent`, number): If you have a stock holding, enter the exact percentage of ownership.
* **Officer** (`officer`, text): If you are an officer, please enter your title and any other relevant details.
* **Officer role** (`officer_role`, text): If you are an officer, please enter your role or position.
* **Other officer** (`other_officer`, text): If you have any other officer roles, please enter the details.
* **Other officer role** (`other_officer_role`, text): If you have any other officer roles, please enter the details.
* **Board member** (`board_member`, text): If you are a board member, please enter your title and any other relevant details.
* **Board member title** (`board_member_title`, text): If you are a board member, please enter your title.
* **Board member committee** (`board_member_committee`, text): If you are a board member, please enter the committee you belong to.
* **Employee** (`employee`, text): If you are an employee, please enter your job title and any other relevant details.
* **Employee title** (`employee_title`, text): If you are an employee, please enter your job title.
* **Committee member** (`committee_member`, text): If you are a committee member, please enter your title and any other relevant details.
* **Committee member title** (`committee_member_title`, text): If you are a committee member, please enter your title.
* **Committee** (`committee`, text): If you are a committee member, please enter the committee you belong to.
* **Committee title** (`committee_title`, text): If you are a committee member, please enter the committee title.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text): Please sign and date this form to confirm your declaration.
* **Date** (`signature_date`, date): Please enter the date you sign this form.
* **Name** (`signature_name`, text): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Certification** (`certification`, note): This field is used to acknowledge that you have read and understand the conflict of interest policy.
