# Right Of First Refusal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Right Of First Refusal Request Form is used to document a request for information or action regarding a specific property. This form is intended for use by property owners or their representatives to initiate a request for information or action regarding a property.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure that you have filled out the form accurately and completely.
2.  Review and sign the form in front of the property owner or their representative if necessary.
3.  Submit the form to the property manager or their representative for review and action.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Property Owner** (`property_owner`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, Optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `text`, Optional): Enter a brief description of the property type.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, `text`, Optional): Enter the current status of the property.
* **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, `text`, Optional): Enter the reason for the request.
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date of the request.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, Optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Requestor Name** (`requestor_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the person making the request.
* **Requestor Address** (`requestor_address`, `text`, Optional): Enter the address of the person making the request.
* **Requestor Relationship** (`requestor_relationship`, `select_one`, Optional): Select how the requestor is related to the property owner. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Request Type** (`request_type`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the type of request being made. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional notes regarding the request.
* **Signed Date** (`signed_date`, `date`, Optional): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the person signing the form.
* **Requestor Phone** (`requestor_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the person making the request.
* **Requestor Email** (`requestor_email`, `text`, Optional): Enter the email of the person making the request.
* **Property Manager** (`property_manager`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Phone** (`property_manager_phone`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Email** (`property_manager_email`, `email`, Optional): Enter the email of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Address** (`property_manager_address`, `text`, Optional): Enter the address of the property manager.
* **Property Manager Relationship** (`property_manager_relationship`, `select_one`, Optional): Select how the property manager is related to the property. Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3.
* **Requestor Signature** (`requestor_signature`, `text`, Optional): Enter the signature of the person making the request.
* **Property Manager Signature** (`property_manager_signature`, `text`, Optional): Enter the signature of the property manager.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required in the form definition. Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
