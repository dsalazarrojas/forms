# lease-agreement-records-release-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to record the release of a lease agreement. It helps to document the details of the agreement, including the records of the agreement, date signed, and the person who signed the agreement. This form is essential in maintaining accurate records and preventing confusion or disputes that may arise from lease agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting or using a digital device.
*   Make sure to enter the correct information in each field, as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **tenant-info** (text, required/optional): Enter the information of the tenant or lessee.
*   **agreement-records** (select one, required/optional): Choose the type of agreement records you are releasing, e.g., "1", "2", or "3".
*   **permissions** (number, required/optional): Enter the permissions granted or received in the agreement.
*   **date-signed** (date, required/optional): Enter the date the agreement was signed.
*   **signed-by** (note, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
