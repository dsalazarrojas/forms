# Parking Ticket Hearing Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parking Ticket Hearing Request Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to contest or request a hearing for a parking ticket they received. This form helps ensure that the required information is gathered in a clear and concise manner, facilitating the review and processing of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your preferred hearing option from the "Hearing Preferences" section.
2. If applicable, provide any supporting documentation in the "Supporting Documentation" field.
3. Enter the date of the event that led to the parking ticket in the "Date of Event" field.
4. Enter the date you would like to have the hearing in the "Date of Hearing" field.
5. Enter the time you would like to have the hearing in the "Time of Hearing" field.
6. Specify the location where the hearing should take place in the "Location" field.
7. If you are requesting an officer to be present at the hearing, select all relevant officers from the "Requesting Officer" field.
8. If you have any additional information or details regarding your request, you can provide them in the "Additional Information" field.
