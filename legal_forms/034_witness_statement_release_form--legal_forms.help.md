# Witness Statement Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture a witness statement, which is a written account of what a witness saw, heard, or experienced during an event. The form helps ensure that the witness statement is accurately recorded and released.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your witness statement in the "Witness Statement" field. This is where you will describe what happened during the event.
2. If necessary, provide a brief description of your statement in the "Statement Description" field.
3. Enter the date of the event in the "Date of Event" field. This should be the date when the event occurred.
4. Enter the time of the event in the "Time of Event" field. This should be the time when the event occurred.
5. Enter the location of the event in the "Location of Event" field.
6. Sign your witness statement by entering your name in the "Signed By" field.
7. Sign the document by selecting a date in the "Signed Date" field, indicating the date you have witnessed the statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement_release_form_witness_statement`, text, required/optional): Enter a written account of what you saw, heard, or experienced during the event. This field is required.
* **Statement Description** (`witness_statement_release_form_statement_description`, text, required/optional): A brief description of your witness statement. This field is optional.
* **Date of Event** (`witness_statement_release_form_date_of_event`, date, required/optional): The date when the event occurred. This field is optional.
* **Time of Event** (`witness_statement_release_form_time_of_event`, time, required/optional): The time when the event occurred. This field is optional.
* **Location of Event** (`witness_statement_release_form_location_of_event`, text, required/optional): The location where the event occurred. This field is optional.
* **Signed By** (`witness_statement_release_form_witness_statement_release_form_signed_by`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the witness. This field is required.
* **Signed Date** (`witness_statement_release_form_witness_statement_release_form_signed_date`, date, required/optional): Select a date to sign the witness statement. This field is required.
