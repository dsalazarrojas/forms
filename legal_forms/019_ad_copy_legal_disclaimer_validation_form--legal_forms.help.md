# Ad Copy Legal Disclaimer Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ad Copy Legal Disclaimer Validation Form is designed to collect essential information about a marketing campaign's ad copy, focusing on ensuring the accuracy and compliance of the legal disclaimers displayed with the ads. This form guides through the process of validating the disclaimers for regulatory standards, visibility, and reviewer's approval, aiming to maintain a high level of accuracy and legal compliance in marketing campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Start by providing the marketing campaign's basic information: **Campaign Name** and **Ad Copy / Headline**.
*   Identify where the ad copy will be displayed, such as Social Media (Meta / TikTok / etc), Search Engine (Google Ads), Television / Video, Print Media, or Radio / Podcast.
*   Enter the exact wording of the **Full Disclaimer Text**, and describe its **Disclaimer Placement and Visibility**.
*   Ensure that the **Font size meets minimum legibility requirements** and that the **Exposure duration is sufficient for reading (if video/radio)**.
*   Identify which **Regulatory Standard(s) Applicable** (e.g., FTC Guidelines, FDA Regulations, FINRA / Financial Services, CCPA / Privacy, Local State Laws).
*   Estimate the **Legal Risk Level** of the campaign (Low - Standard Disclaimer, Medium - Specific Claims Made, High - Highly Regulated Industry).
*   Add any **Reviewer Internal Notes** for further reference.
*   Indicate the **Final Validation Status** of the campaign (Approved as is, Approved with Changes, Rejected - Needs Major Revision).
*   Enter the **Reviewer Name / Legal Counsel** and the **Date of Review**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Campaign Name**: This field is required to accurately identify the marketing campaign being assessed. Enter the actual name of the campaign.
*   **Ad Copy / Headline**: Enter the exact headline or title of the ad as it will appear in the marketing campaign.
*   **Media Channel**: Select the media channel where the ad copy will be displayed. This includes options such as Social Media (Meta / TikTok / etc), Search Engine (Google Ads), Television / Video, Print Media, or Radio / Podcast.
*   **Full Disclaimer Text**: This is the exact wording of the legal disclaimer that will appear with the ad copy. Enter it exactly as it will be displayed.
*   **Disclaimer Placement and Visibility**: Describe where and how the disclaimer will be displayed with the ad copy.
*   **Font size meets minimum legibility requirements**: Ensure that the font size of the disclaimer meets the minimum legibility standards. This field is crucial for readability.
*   **Exposure duration is sufficient for reading (if video/radio)**: If the ad copy is a video or radio, ensure that the exposure duration is sufficient for viewers or listeners to read the disclaimer before it ends.
*   **Regulatory Standard(s) Applicable**: Identify which regulatory standards are applicable to the campaign, such as FTC Guidelines, FDA Regulations, FINRA / Financial Services, CCPA / Privacy, Local State Laws.
*   **Legal Risk Level**: Estimate the legal risk level of the campaign, with options being Low - Standard Disclaimer, Medium - Specific Claims Made, High - Highly Regulated Industry.
*   **Legal Reviewer Internal Notes**: Enter any internal notes from the legal reviewer for further reference.
*   **Final Validation Status**: Indicate the final status of the campaign's validation, with options such as Approved as is, Approved with Changes, Rejected - Needs Major Revision.
*   **Reviewer Name / Legal Counsel**: Enter the name of the reviewer or legal counsel who performed the validation.
*   **Date of Review**: Enter the date when the review was performed.
