# Service Agreement For Development - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the completion of the Service Agreement For Development form, which is a critical document outlining the terms and conditions of a service agreement between a client and a developer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Service Agreement** field, providing a clear and concise description of the service being agreed upon.
2. Enter the **Client Details** in the designated field, including any relevant information about the client.
3. Provide a detailed description of the **Service Details** in the next field.
4. In the **Agreement Terms** field, outline the terms and conditions of the service, including any specific requirements or limitations.
5. Describe the **Scope of Work** in the following field, including any tasks, timelines, and responsibilities.
6. Enter the **Timeline** for the project, including any key milestones or deadlines.
7. In the **Payment Details** field, outline the payment terms, including any payment methods or schedules.
8. Choose the **Approval** option that best fits the agreement, either Client Approval, Developer Approval, or Client and Developer Approval.
9. In the **Client Signature** and **Developer Signature** fields, add your physical or digital signature to confirm agreement.
10. Select the **Date** for when the agreement was signed.
11. In the **Service Agreement Terms**, **Payment Terms**, **Notice to Client**, and **Notice to Developer** fields, provide any additional details or notes.
12. In the **Service Providers** field, list any third-party services involved in the project.
13. If applicable, review the **Service Agreement Review** field to check for any errors or discrepancies.
14. In the **Approval Terms**, add any additional terms or conditions for approval.
15. In the **Signature** field, add your signature again for final confirmation.
16. In the **Terms of Service**, add any additional terms or conditions for the service.
17. In the **Service Agreement Acknowledgement** field, acknowledge your understanding of the service agreement.
18. In the **Acknowledgement of Service Agreement**, acknowledge your understanding of the service agreement.
19. In the **Approval by**, select who should approve the agreement.
20. In the **Approved By** field, confirm who has approved the agreement.
21. In the **Approval Date**, select the date the agreement was approved.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Agreement** (`service_agreement`, text, required): A brief description of the service being agreed upon.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, text, required): Information about the client, including contact details or any other relevant details.
* **Service Details** (`service_details`, text, required): A detailed description of the service, including any specific requirements or limitations.
* **Agreement Terms** (`agreement_terms`, text, required): The terms and conditions of the service, including any specific requirements or limitations.
* **Scope of Work** (`scope_of_work`, text, required): A description of the tasks, timelines, and responsibilities involved in the project.
* **Timeline** (`timeline`, text, required): The project schedule, including any key milestones or deadlines.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Payment terms, including any payment methods or schedules.
* **Approval** (`approval`, select_one, required): The option for approval, either Client Approval, Developer Approval, or Client and Developer Approval.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, text, optional): A physical or digital signature from the client to confirm agreement.
* **Developer Signature** (`developer_signature`, text, optional): A physical or digital signature from the developer to confirm agreement.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): The date the agreement was signed.
* **Service Agreement Terms** (`service_agreement_terms`, text, optional): Additional terms or conditions for the service agreement.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, optional): Additional payment terms or conditions.
* **Notice to Client** (`notice_to_client`, text, optional): A notice to the client, including any important information or reminders.
* **Notice to Developer** (`notice_to_developer`, text, optional): A notice to the developer, including any important information or reminders.
* **Service Providers** (`service_providers`, text, optional): A list of any third-party services involved in the project.
* **Service Agreement Review** (`service_agreement_review`, text, optional): A field for reviewing and revising the service agreement.
* **Approval Terms** (`approval_terms`, text, optional): Additional terms or conditions for approval.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): A physical or digital signature again for final confirmation.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, text, optional): Additional terms or conditions for the service.
* **Service Agreement Acknowledgement** (`service_agreement_acknowledgement`, text, optional): Acknowledge understanding of the service agreement.
* **Acknowledgement of Service Agreement** (`acknowledgement_of_service_agreement`, text, optional): Acknowledge understanding of the service agreement.
* **Approval by** (`approval_by`, select_one, optional): Select who should approve the agreement.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, text, optional): Confirm who has approved the agreement.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, date, optional): The date the agreement was approved.
