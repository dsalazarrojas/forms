# Inheritance Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inheritance Reporting Form is designed to collect information about inheritance-related matters. This form is meant to help individuals report and document details about their inheritance, including estate specifics, key dates, and information about the heir or beneficiary.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide a brief description of the Inheritance Details** (Field 1 - `inheritance_details`):
	* Enter a concise statement about the inheritance, including the type of property or asset involved.
2. **Enter Estate Details** (Field 2 - `estate_details`):
	* Describe the estate's properties, assets, or interests.
3. **List Key Dates** (Field 3 - `key_dates`):
	* Provide dates relevant to the inheritance, such as dates of death, births, or transactions.
4. **Enter Heir Details** (Field 4 - `heir_details`):
	* Describe the heir or beneficiary, including their name, relationship, or other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Inheritance Details** (Field 1 - `inheritance_details`):
	+ A brief description of the inheritance, including the type of property or asset involved.
* **Estate Details** (Field 2 - `estate_details`):
	+ A detailed description of the estate's properties, assets, or interests.
* **Key Dates** (Field 3 - `key_dates`):
	+ A list of dates relevant to the inheritance, such as dates of death, births, or transactions.
* **Heir Details** (Field 4 - `heir_details`):
	+ A description of the heir or beneficiary, including their name, relationship, or other relevant information.

## Tips
* Be as concise as possible when filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any information, it's best to ask for clarification or advice from a qualified professional.
