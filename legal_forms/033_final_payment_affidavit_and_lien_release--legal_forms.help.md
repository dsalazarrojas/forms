# Final Payment Affidavit And Lien Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create an affidavit and lien release for final payment of projects in various categories.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the category of the project (e.g. Construction, Property, etc.).
2. Enter the address of the project.
3. Enter the date of the contract and the date of the final payment.
4. Enter the amount paid for the final payment.
5. Select the payment method used (e.g. Cash, Check, Credit Card, etc.).
6. Enter the name of the contractor and the owner.
7. Check the box to confirm that the final payment has been made (Final Payment Confirmed) and that the lien has been released (Lien Released).
8. Sign and date the affidavit and lien release with the witness signature and the notary seal.
9. Include the witness signature and date on the witness signature line.
10. Enter the date of the notary seal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, `select_one`): Select the category of the project. This will help determine the specific details and requirements for the form.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`, `text`): Enter the address of the project location.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the contract signing.
* **Final Payment Date** (`final_payment_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the final payment.
* **Amount Paid** (`amount_paid`, `number`): Enter the total amount paid for the final payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`): Select the method used for the payment (e.g. Cash, Check, Credit Card, etc.).
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the contractor or company performing the work.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`): Enter the name of the owner or property owner.
* **Notary Seal** (`notary_seal`, `note`): This field is for notary stamp or seal information.
* **Witness Signatures** (`witness_signatures`, `text`): Enter the signature and date of the witness.
* **Final Payment Confirmed** (`final_payment_confirmed`, `select_one`): Check the box to confirm that the final payment has been made.
* **Lien Released** (`lien_released`, `select_one`): Check the box to confirm that the lien has been released.
* **Lien Released Date** (`lien_released_date`, `date`): Enter the date when the lien was released.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`): Enter the signature of the witness.
* **Witness Signature Date** (`witness_signature_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the witness signature.
* **Notary Seal Date** (`notary_seal_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the notary seal.
* **Final Payment Amount** (`final_payment_amount`, `number`): Enter the total amount paid for the final payment.
* **Contractor Signature** (`contractor_signature`, `text`): Enter the signature of the contractor or company.
* **Contractor Signature Date** (`contractor_signature_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the contractor signature.
* **Owner Signature** (`owner_signature`, `text`): Enter the signature of the owner or property owner.
* **Owner Signature Date** (`owner_signature_date`, `date`): Enter the date of the owner signature.
