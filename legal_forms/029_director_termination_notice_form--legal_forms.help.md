# Director Termination Notice Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to formally notify a director of their termination, outlining the reason for the termination and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the `Director Name` field with the name of the director being terminated.
2. Enter the `Director Email` address in the corresponding field.
3. In the `Notice Details` field, provide a clear and concise reason for the termination.
4. Select the `Termination Reason` from the list provided, which outlines the reason for the termination.
5. Fill in the `Effective Date` and `Termination Date` fields with the dates of termination.
6. In the `Termination Notice` field, enter any additional details regarding the termination.
7. If applicable, fill in the `Reviewed By` field with the name of the person who reviewed the termination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Director Name**: Enter the name of the director being terminated.
* **Director Email**: Enter the email address of the director being terminated.
* **Notice Details**: Provide a clear and concise reason for the termination.
* **Termination Reason**: Select the reason for termination from the list provided:
	+ **Poor performance**: The director's performance has not met expectations.
	+ **Conflict of interest**: The director's actions or relationships have created a conflict of interest.
	+ **Retirement**: The director has reached retirement age or has chosen to retire.
	+ **Redundancy**: The director's role has become redundant due to changes in the organization or business.
* **Effective Date**: Enter the date when the termination is to take effect.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date of the termination.
* **Termination Notice**: Enter any additional details regarding the termination, such as any next steps or actions required.
* **Reviewed By**: If applicable, enter the name of the person who reviewed the termination.
