# Copyright Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request permission to use copyrighted materials. It is meant to be filled out by content creators or users who want to use copyrighted materials for a specific purpose.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Copyright Requester Name** in the corresponding field.
2. Enter the **Copyright Holder Name** of the material you want to use.
3. Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** so that the copyright holder can contact you.
4. Describe the **Purpose Of Request**, explaining why you need to use the copyrighted material.
5. Choose your **Contact Preference**: By phone, By email, or By mail.
6. If necessary, specify the **Date**, **Time**, and **Location** where you plan to use the material.
7. If you want to attach any additional comments or explanations, use the **Comments** field.
8. Finally, decide on whether you agree to the **Copyright Agreement**. This is a crucial step; if you don't agree, your request will not be processed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Copyright Requester Name** (Required): Enter your name as the person or entity requesting permission to use the copyrighted material.
* **Copyright Holder Name** (Required): Enter the name of the copyright holder or owner of the material you wish to use.
* **Email Address** (Optional): Provide your email address so that the copyright holder can contact you regarding your request.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number for added contact information.
* **Purpose Of Request** (Optional): Describe the reason you need to use the copyrighted material.
* **Contact Preference** (Required): Select how you prefer to be contacted (by phone, email, or mail).
* **Date**, **Time**, and **Location** (Optional): If necessary, specify when and where you plan to use the material.
* **Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional information or explanations that may be helpful.
* **Copyright Agreement** (Required): Decide whether you agree to the copyright agreement, indicating whether you will respect the copyright holder's rights.
* **Copyright License** (Optional): Not necessary for the form's purpose.
* **Copyright Information** (Optional): Not necessary for the form's purpose.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional): Not necessary for the form's purpose.
* **Form ID** (Optional): Not necessary for the form's purpose.
* **Copyright Permission Form** (Optional): Not necessary for the form's purpose.
