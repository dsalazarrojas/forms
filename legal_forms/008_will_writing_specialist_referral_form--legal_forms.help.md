# Will Writing Specialist Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
<thinking>The purpose of this form is to refer cases to a Will Writing Specialist for review and assistance with estate planning. When filling out the form, please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date, as it will be shared with the Will Writing Specialist to facilitate their work.</thinking>

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to select the correct state from the options list for the "State" field.
3. For the "Date of Death" field, enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the first name of the individual who requires Will writing assistance.
* **Reported Name** (`reported_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the person or organization referring the case to the Will Writing Specialist.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter the email address of the individual or organization referring the case to the Will Writing Specialist.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the primary contact number of the individual or organization referring the case to the Will Writing Specialist.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of the individual or organization referring the case to the Will Writing Specialist.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the city of the address.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the state in which the estate is located from the list of options.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the ZIP code of the address.
* **Date of Death** (`date_of_death`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date of death in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Estate Size** (`estate_size`, select_one, required):
	+ Select the size of the estate from the list of options.
	+ Small: Up to $10,000
	+ Medium: $10,001 to $50,000
	+ Large: $50,001 to $200,000
	+ Extra-Large: Over $200,000

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact the Will Writing Specialist for assistance.
