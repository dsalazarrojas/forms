# court_fee_reimbursement_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to file a Court Fee Reimbursement Claim, providing detailed information about the claimant, the claim being made, and associated supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and address in the "Claimant Name" and "Claimant Address" fields.
2. Provide your contact information: phone number, email, and signature in the relevant fields.
3. Enter the amount of court fees claimed and the date of the court fee in the "Court Fee Claimed" and "Court Fee Date" fields.
4. Enter the case number and provide details about the case in the "Court Fee Case Number" and "Case Details" fields.
5. If applicable, enter the contact information and declaration of your representative in the "Claimant Representative" and "Claimant Representative Declaration" fields.
6. If you have other claims, provide details in the "Other Claims" and "Other Claims Details" fields.
