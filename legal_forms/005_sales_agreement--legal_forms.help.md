# Sales Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sales Agreement form is used to create a comprehensive sales agreement between a seller and a buyer, capturing essential details such as agreement terms, conditions, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Seller**: Enter the seller's name, contact information, and any relevant notes or comments.
2. **Buyer**: Enter the buyer's name, contact information, and any relevant notes or comments.
3. **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the sales agreement.
4. **Purchase Price**: Enter the total purchase price of the sale.
5. **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the purchase (e.g., Cash, Check, Credit Card, or Other).
6. **Seller Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the seller.
7. **Buyer Notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the buyer.
8. **Agreement Type**: Select the type of sales agreement (e.g., Sales Agreement, Purchase Agreement, Lease Agreement, or Other).
9. **Agreement Term**: Enter the term of the sales agreement in months or years.
10. **Agreement Duration**: Enter the duration of the sales agreement.
11. **Contract Date**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
12. **Contract End Date**: Enter the date the contract will expire.
13. **Contract Location**: Enter the location where the contract was signed.
14. **Payment Frequency**: Enter the payment frequency (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly, etc.).
15. **Payment Status**: Select the payment status (e.g., Paid or Unpaid).
16. **Payment Status Date**: Enter the date the payment status was last updated.
17. **Seller Contact Info**: Enter the seller's contact information.
18. **Buyer Contact Info**: Enter the buyer's contact information.
19. **Agreement Terms**: Enter any additional terms or conditions of the sales agreement.
20. **Contract Version**: Enter the version of the contract.
21. **Contract Number**: Enter a unique contract number.
22. **Contract Date Signed**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
23. **Contract Expiration Date**: Enter the date the contract will expire.
24. **Contract Status**: Select the status of the contract (e.g., Active, Inactive, or Expired).
