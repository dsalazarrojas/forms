# Legal Collection Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for legal events and conferences. It gathers information about the event, such as the type of event, dates, location, and number of attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, including the "Legal Profession" field and the "Email" field.
2. Select the type of event you are attending by choosing one or more options from the "Type of Event" field.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the event.
4. Enter the location of the event.
5. Provide any additional information about the event, such as accommodations or parking availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Profession** (`Legal Profession`, select_one, required):
	+ Please select your legal profession from the available options (Lawyer, Judge, Attorney, Other).
* **Firm Name** (`Firm Name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of your law firm, if applicable.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required):
	+ Enter your email address to receive updates and information about the event.
* **Firm Address** (`Firm Address`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the address of your law firm, if applicable.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number, if you want to be contacted.
* **Type of Event** (`Type of Event`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more options to specify the type of event you are attending (Conference, Seminar, Webinar, Meeting, Other).
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the end date of the event.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional):
	+ Enter the location of the event.
* **Number of Attendees** (`Number of Attendees`, number, optional):
	+ Enter the number of people attending from your firm.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select one or more options to provide additional information about the event (None of the above, Parking is available, Food is available, Accommodations are available).
