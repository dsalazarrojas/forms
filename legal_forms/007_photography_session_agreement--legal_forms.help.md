# Photography Session Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information and agreements from clients before a photography session. It covers crucial details such as coverage location, session date, costs, terms and conditions, customer consent, and electronic signature.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) after the label. Begin with the **coverage_location** field.
2. Enter the session date in the **session_date** field using the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. If applicable, provide a brief description of the costs associated with the session in the **costs** field.
4. Read and acknowledge the **terms_and_conditions** field by selecting the checkbox that indicates you have read and agree to the terms.
5. For the **customer_consent** field, select all options that apply to your consent, such as agreeing to the session's coverage.
6. Finally, provide your electronic signature in the **e_signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **coverage_location**
* **Coverage Location** (text, optional): Enter the location where the photography session will take place. This could be a physical address, a specific room within a building, or even a remote location.

### **session_date**
* **Session Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the photography session.

### **costs**
* **Costs** (text, optional): Provide a description of any associated costs or expenses related to the session.

### **terms_and_conditions**
* **Terms and Conditions** (text, optional): This field is meant to serve as an agreement to the session's terms and conditions. Typically, this would include details about session expectations, responsibilities, and any other relevant information.

### **customer_consent**
* **Customer Consent** (select multiple, optional): Select all options that apply to your consent regarding the session's coverage. This field might include options like agreeing to the session's coverage or declining it.

### **e_signature**
* **Electronic Signature** (text, optional): Sign your name electronically to confirm your acceptance of this agreement.
