# unclaimed_assets_recovery_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information for the recovery of unclaimed assets, including money, securities, real estate, vehicles, or goods.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the contact information section with your relevant details to ensure the inquiry can be properly processed.
2. Select the type of asset you are inquiring about from the "Unclaimed Asset Type" dropdown menu.
3. If necessary, choose the relevant documents required for asset recovery from the "Requested Documents" dropdown menu.
4. Add any additional comments or details about the asset in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Provide the date you are requesting the asset be recovered and the date it was received.
6. Select the status of the asset recovery process from the "Status" dropdown menu.
7. Choose the tool assigned to the asset recovery process from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
8. Finally, click submit to send the inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unclaimed Assets Recovery Inquiry** (Unclaimed Asset Type):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the type of unclaimed asset you are inquiring about.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information):
	+ A field to enter your contact name.
* **Asset Details** (Asset Details):
	+ A field to enter any additional details about the asset.
* **Unclaimed Asset Type** (Unclaimed Asset Type):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the type of asset you are inquiring about.
* **Requested Documents** (Requested Documents):
	+ A dropdown menu to select any documents required for asset recovery.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments):
	+ A field to enter any additional comments or details about the asset.
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name):
	+ A field to enter the name of the person inquiring about the asset.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email):
	+ A field to enter the contact email of the person inquiring about the asset.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone):
	+ A field to enter the contact phone number of the person inquiring about the asset.
* **Requested Date** (Requested Date):
	+ A field to enter the date you are requesting the asset be recovered.
* **Received Date** (Received Date):
	+ A field to enter the date the asset was received.
* **Status** (Status):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the status of the asset recovery process.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the tool assigned to the asset recovery process.
* **User ID** (User ID):
	+ A numeric field to enter the user ID associated with the asset recovery process.
