# Story Creation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Story Creation Agreement form is a template for documenting the terms and conditions of a story creation project. It is designed to gather essential information from both the client and the writer to ensure a clear understanding of the project's scope, timeline, and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the project details, including the project title, client details, and project deadline.
2.  Specify the compensation and payment terms for the project.
3.  Define the delivery date and any specific terms or conditions related to the project.
4.  Enter the details of the client and agency (if applicable) and the writer.
5.  Review and agree to the payment terms and project deadline.
6.  Sign and date the agreement with both the client and the writer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details** (Project Details, text, required/optional): This field is for entering the project title, brief description, or any other relevant details about the project.
* **Compensation** (Compensation, number, required/optional): Fill in the amount of compensation for the project, which can include a flat fee or an hourly rate.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, date, required/optional): Enter the expected date for project delivery, which is crucial for meeting the project deadline.
* **Terms** (Terms, note, required/optional): This field is for including any specific terms or conditions related to the project, such as deadlines, submission formats, or content requirements.
* **Client Details** (Client Details, text, required/optional): Enter the client's contact information or any other relevant details about the client.
* **Writer Details** (Writer Details, text, required/optional): Provide your contact information, skills, or experience relevant to the project.
* **Agency Details** (Agency Details, text, required/optional): Enter the agency's contact information if you are working through an agency.
* **Project Deadline** (Project Deadline, number, required/optional): Specify the expected completion date of the project.
* **Payment Terms** (Payment Terms, text, required/optional): Describe the payment terms, including the payment method, frequency, and any other relevant details.
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, text, required/optional): Sign and date the agreement with the client, indicating agreement to the project terms.
* **Writer Signature** (Writer Signature, text, required/optional): Sign and date the agreement with you as the writer, indicating agreement to the project terms and conditions.
* **Agency Signature** (Agency Signature, text, required/optional): Sign and date the agreement with the agency if you are working through one.

Note: The fields with "required/optional" are currently set to false, which means all fields are technically optional. This is to ensure that the form can be used in a more flexible way to accommodate different types of projects. However, in a real-world scenario, certain fields might be required for the project's success, and these should be reviewed and adjusted accordingly.

**Tips**:
- Be clear and specific when filling out the form to avoid misunderstandings.
- Ensure that all parties involved agree to the terms and conditions before signing.
- Keep a copy of the agreement for reference and record-keeping purposes.
