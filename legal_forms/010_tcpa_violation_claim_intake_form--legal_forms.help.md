# tcpa_violation_claim_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the claimant regarding a potential TCPA violation claim. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to help our team process and evaluate the claim.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct claimant's information.
* Enter the claimant's contact information in the "claimant_contact_info" field.
* Provide the claimant's identification information in the "claimant_identification" field.
* Enter the details of the claim in the "claim" field.
* Enter the claimant's address information, including street address, state, and zip code, in the "claimant_address" field.
* Enter the claimant's phone number in the "claimant_phone" field.
* Enter the claimant's email address in the "claimant_email" field.
* Enter the claimant's date of birth in the "claimant_date_of_birth" field.
* Enter the claimant's residence country in the "claimant_residence_country" field.
* Enter the claimant's residence zip code in the "claimant_residence_zip" field.
* Enter the claimant's street address in the "claimant_residence_street" field.
* Enter the claimant's state of residence in the "claimant_residence_state" field.
* Enter the claimant's city of residence in the "claimant_residence_city" field.
* If the claimant's state of residence is not the US, enter the full state name in the "claimant_residence_state_full" field.
* Enter the claimant's zip + 4 in the "claimant_residence_zip_4" field.
* Enter the claimant's state abbreviation in the "claimant_residence_state_abbrev" field.
* Enter the claimant's country of residence abbreviation in the "claimant_residence_country_abbrev" field.
* Enter the type of phone (e.g., cell, landline, etc.) in the "claimant_phone_type" field.
* Enter the claimant's phone number in the "claimant_phone_number" field.
* Enter the claimant's alternate phone number in the "claimant_alternate_phone" field.
* Enter the date of the claim in the "claimant_date_of_claim" field.
* Enter a brief description of the claim in the "claimant_call_description" field.
* Enter the type of call (e.g., robocall, live person, etc.) in the "claimant_call_type" field.
* Enter the caller's ID number in the "claimant_caller_id" field.
* Enter the type of caller (e.g., robocaller, live person, etc.) in the "claimant_caller_type" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the general information about the claim.
* **claimant_contact_info** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's contact information for communication purposes.
* **claimant_identification** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's identification information, such as ID number or name.
* **claim** (Text, Optional): Enter the details of the claim, including dates and amounts involved.
* **claimant_address** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's address, including street address, state, and zip code.
* **claimant_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **claimant_email** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's email address.
* **claimant_date_of_birth** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's date of birth.
* **claimant_residence_country** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's country of residence.
* **claimant_residence_zip** (Number, Optional): Enter the claimant's zip code + 4.
* **claimant_residence_street** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's street address.
* **claimant_residence_state** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's state of residence.
* **claimant_residence_city** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's city of residence.
* **claimant_residence_state_full** (Text, Optional): If the claimant's state of residence is not the US, enter the full state name.
* **claimant_residence_zip_4** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's zip + 4.
* **claimant_residence_state_abbrev** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's state abbreviation.
* **claimant_residence_country_abbrev** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's country abbreviation.
* **claimant_phone_type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of phone (e.g., cell, landline, etc.).
* **claimant_phone_number** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's phone number.
* **claimant_alternate_phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the claimant's alternate phone number.
* **claimant_date_of_claim** (Text, Optional): Enter the date of the claim.
* **claimant_call_description** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the claim.
* **claimant_call_type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of call (e.g., robocall, live person, etc.).
* **claimant_caller_id** (Text, Optional): Enter the caller's ID number.
* **claimant_caller_type** (Text, Optional): Enter the type of caller (e.g., robocaller, live person, etc.).
