# Foreign Beneficial Owner Certification - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foreign Beneficial Owner Certification form is used to collect information about a foreign beneficial owner. This form is used to gather data for regulatory reporting purposes, such as FATCA and AML/ATF requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information about the foreign beneficial owner.
2. Review each field to ensure that the information provided is correct and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Beneficial Owner
* **Beneficial_owner_certification_form** (`beneficial_owner_certification_form`, text, required/optional): This field is the main section header for the form.
* **foreign_entity** (`foreign_entity`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the foreign entity owning the interest.
* **beneficial_owner_address** (`beneficial_owner_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_ownership_interest** (`beneficial_owner_ownership_interest`, text, required/optional): Enter the percentage of ownership or other type of interest the foreign beneficial owner has.
* **beneficial_owner_occupation** (`beneficial_owner_occupation`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the foreign beneficial owner is an individual; select 'No' otherwise.
* **beneficial_owner_date_of_birth** (`beneficial_owner_date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of birth of the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_place_of_residence** (`beneficial_owner_place_of_residence`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the foreign beneficial owner resides in the same country as their place of business; select 'No' otherwise.

### 2. Additional Information
* **beneficial_owner_ultimate_economic_interest** (`beneficial_owner_ultimate_economic_interest`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the ultimate economic interest of the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_country_of_residence** (`beneficial_owner_country_of_residence`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the foreign beneficial owner resides in the same country as their place of business; select 'No' otherwise.
* **beneficial_owner_type_of_interest** (`beneficial_owner_type_of_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all types of interest the foreign beneficial owner has.
* **beneficial_owner_percentage_of_interest** (`beneficial_owner_percentage_of_interest`, number, required/optional): Enter the percentage of ownership or other type of interest the foreign beneficial owner has.
* **beneficial_owner_relationship** (`beneficial_owner_relationship`, text, required/optional): Enter any relationship information about the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_date_of_formation** (`beneficial_owner_date_of_formation`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of formation of the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_registration_number** (`beneficial_owner_registration_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the registration number of the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_address_2** (`beneficial_owner_address_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the address of the foreign beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_occupation** (`beneficial_owner_occupation`, select_one, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if the foreign beneficial owner has an occupation; select 'No' otherwise.
