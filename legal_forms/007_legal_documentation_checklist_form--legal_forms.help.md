# legal_documentation_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal documentation checklist used to collect and verify information for legal documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Client Name field with the name of the individual or organization associated with the legal document.
2. Select the Document Type from the options provided to specify the type of legal document being dealt with.
3. Enter the Document Number if applicable.
4. Enter the Date Signed and Date Issued dates if relevant.
5. Select the Status Document to indicate whether the document is Active or Inactive.
6. Add any additional Notes about the document.
7. Enter the Phone number of the client or contact person.
8. Enter the client's Email address.
9. Upload any relevant Files if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the client or individual associated with the legal document.
* **Document Type** (`document_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of legal document from the provided options (Will, Trust, Estate).
* **Document Number** (`document_number`, `number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the document, if applicable.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the document was signed.
* **Date Issued** (`date_issued`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the document was issued.
* **Status Document** (`status_document`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether the document is Active or Inactive.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about the document.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the client or contact person.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the client or contact person.
* **File Upload** (`file_upload`, `text`, required: false): Upload any relevant files or attachments to support the document.

Tips:

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
* If a field is not relevant to the specific document, you can leave it blank.
* This form is designed to be used for general legal documentation purposes, but if you need to use it for a specific type of document, please adjust the form fields accordingly.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, please contact your legal administration or support team.
