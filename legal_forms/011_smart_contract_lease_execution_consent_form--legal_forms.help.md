# Smart Contract Lease Execution Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document used to formalize and record a tenant's consent to execute a smart contract for a rental property.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information.
2. Ensure that all required fields (marked as "Required: true" in the form) are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, `email`, required: true): Enter the email address of the tenant.
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the landlord.
* **Landlord Email** (`landlord_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the landlord.
* **Lease Start Date** (`lease_start_date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the start date of the lease.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, `date`, required: false): If applicable, enter the end date of the lease.
* **Smart Contract Address** (`smart_contract_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the smart contract.
* **Contract Execution Date** (`contract_execution_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of contract execution.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required: false): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the smart contract.
* **Contract Execution Method** (`contract_execution_method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how the smart contract will be executed (via smart contract, lawyer, notary, or other).
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of consent.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of consent.

Tips:

* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Double-check the dates entered are correct.
* If unsure about any field, refer to your organization's documentation or seek input from your supervisor or legal department.
