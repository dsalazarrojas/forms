# Divorce Vocational Evaluation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Divorce Vocational Evaluation Request Form is designed to collect information about the vocational status of each spouse in a divorce case. This form will help in determining the alimony or other post-divorce arrangements based on the spouses' employment status, occupation, education, income, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for each question based on the facts of your case.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields and that the form is complete before submitting it.
3. Review the form carefully before submission to prevent errors or omissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Request for Evaluation** (divorce_vocational_evaluation_request)
- This field asks if you are requesting a vocational evaluation for your divorce case. Select 'Yes' if you are requesting it, and 'No' otherwise.
### 2. **Case Number** (case_number)
- Enter the case number assigned to your divorce case.
### 3. **Case Type** (case_type)
- Choose the correct case type from the options provided.
### 4. **Date of Divorce** (date_of_divorce)
- Enter the date of your divorce, if applicable.
### 5. **Spouse 1 Employment Status** (spouse1_employment_status)
- Select the employment status of Spouse 1 from the provided options.
### 6. **Spouse 1 Occupation** (spouse1_occupation)
- Enter Spouse 1's occupation.
### 7. **Spouse 1 Education** (spouse1_education)
- Select Spouse 1's highest level of education from the options provided.
### 8. **Spouse 1 Income** (spouse1_income)
- Enter Spouse 1's annual income.
### 9. **Spouse 2 Employment Status** (spouse2_employment_status)
- Select the employment status of Spouse 2 from the provided options.
### 10. **Spouse 2 Occupation** (spouse2_occupation)
- Enter Spouse 2's occupation.
### 11. **Spouse 2 Education** (spouse2_education)
- Select Spouse 2's highest level of education from the options provided.
### 12. **Number of Children** (children)
- Enter the number of children involved in the divorce case.
### 13. **Reason for Divorce** (reason_for_divorce)
- Briefly describe the reason for your divorce.
### 14. **Divorce Counseling Attended** (divorce_counseling_attended)
- Select whether you have attended or are currently attending divorce counseling.
### 15. **Alimony** (alimony)
- Enter the annual alimony amount.
### 16. **Date of Marriage** (date_of_marriage)
- Enter the date of your marriage, if applicable.
### 17. **Years Married** (years_married)
- Enter the number of years you were married.
### 18. **Assets** (assets)
- Describe any assets you wish to mention in the divorce case.
