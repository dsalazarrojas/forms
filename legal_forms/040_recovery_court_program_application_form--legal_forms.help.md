# Recovery Court Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recovery Court Program Application Form is a required document for individuals seeking access to the Recovery Court Program. This form is used to gather essential information about the applicant and their eligibility for the program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Ensure that you provide all necessary supporting documentation and proof of identity.
3. Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have included all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **Email**: Enter your active and valid email address.
* **Date Available for Recovery Program**: Select the date you are available to start the program.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of program updates or emergencies.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone 2**: Enter an additional contact phone number for the contact person.
* **Tips**

* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact the Recovery Court Program administrator.
* Please note that some fields may be subject to verification processes, and you will be asked to provide proof of identity and other required documents. 

Note: The Recovery Court Program Application Form is not a substitute for the actual application process. You must submit the form through the proper channels and follow the instructions of the Recovery Court Program administrator.
