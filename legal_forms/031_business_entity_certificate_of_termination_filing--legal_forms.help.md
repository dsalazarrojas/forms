# Business Entity Certificate Of Termination Filing - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to file a Business Entity Certificate of Termination, which is a formal notification of the closure or dissolution of a business entity. It is usually filed with the state government agency responsible for business entities in that jurisdiction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Gather all the necessary information about the business entity, including its name, type, tax ID or EIN, state of formation, original file date, registration number, and last known business address.
*   Choose the correct reason for termination and type of dissolution from the provided options.
*   Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
*   Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the legal name of the business entity.
*   **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_one, required): Choose the structure of the entity from the options provided: Corporation, LLC, Partnership, Non-Profit, or Sole Proprietor.
*   **Tax ID or EIN** (`tax_id`, text, required): Enter the Federal tax ID or Employer Identification Number (EIN) of the business entity.
*   **State of Formation** (`state_filed`, text, required): Enter the state where the business was originally filed.
*   **Original File Date** (`file_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the business entity was originally registered.
*   **Registration or Charter Number** (`registration_number`, text, optional): Enter the state registration or charter number of the business entity, if applicable.
*   **Termination or Dissolution Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the business entity is terminating or dissolving.
*   **Reason for Termination** (`termination_reason`, select_one, required): Choose the reason for the termination or dissolution from the options provided: Business Closed Voluntarily, Business Sold or Merged, Business Bankruptcy, Administrative Reasons, Failure to Renew, or Other.
*   **Type of Dissolution** (`dissolution_type`, select_one, required): Choose the method of dissolution from the options provided: Voluntary Dissolution, Involuntary Dissolution, Administrative Dissolution, or Merger Dissolution.
*   **Principal Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to file this form.
*   **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the title or position of the principal contact.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the business email of the principal contact.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the principal contact.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the last known business address of the entity.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city name of the business address.
*   **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state code of the business address.
*   **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of the business address.
*   **I Confirm This Information is Accurate** (`confirm_accuracy`, select_one, required): Confirm that the provided information is accurate.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of filing.
