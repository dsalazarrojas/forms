# Tax Dependency Claim Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Dependency Claim Release Form is used to document the release of a tax dependency claim.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for the claim release.
2. Fill in the correct tax year.
3. Enter your name and sign the form.
4. Enter your email address, phone number, and address.
5. Enter your signed by title.
6. Enter any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (Legal Forms, Other): Select the correct category for the claim release.
* **Tax Year** (2017, 2018, 2019): Select the correct tax year for the claim release.
* **Form ID** (Text): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text): Enter the tool or system used to prepare this form.
* **Output File** (Text): Enter the name of the output file.
* **Output File Name** (Text): Enter the name of the output file.
* **Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about the claim release.
* **Signed By** (Co-Parent 1, Parent 1, Parent 2, Child, Other): Select the person who signed the form.
* **Signed By Title** (Text): Enter the title of the person who signed the form.
* **Date Signed** (Date): Enter the date the form was signed.
* **Time Signed** (Time): Enter the time the form was signed.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person who signed the form.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person who signed the form.
* **Address** (Text): Enter the address of the person who signed the form.
* **City** (Text): Enter the city of the person who signed the form.
* **State** (Text): Enter the state of the person who signed the form.
* **Zip** (Text): Enter the zip code of the person who signed the form.
* **Other Signed By** (Text): Enter the name of the person who signed the form if different from the person who prepared the form.
* **Other Signed By Title** (Text): Enter the title of the person who signed the form if different from the person who prepared the form.
* **Other Signed By** (Text): Enter the other signed by person's name.
* **Other Signed By Title** (Text): Enter the other signed by person's title.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the category and tax year are correct to avoid any confusion.
* Please fill in all the required fields, and only the required fields.
* If you are unsure of any of the information, please contact your representative for guidance.
