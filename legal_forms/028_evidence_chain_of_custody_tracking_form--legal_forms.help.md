# evidence_chain_of_custody_tracking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to document the chain of custody for evidence collected during a case.

## How To Complete This Form
This form has multiple pages, each with specific fields to be filled out. Please complete each page thoroughly and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Witness Identification** (`witness_identification`, `text`, required: false): 
  Identify the witness who collected or handled the evidence. This will help establish the chain of custody and ensure that the evidence is handled properly.

* **Evidence Description** (`evidence_description`, `text`, required: false): 
  Provide a detailed description of the evidence, including its type, location, and any other relevant details.

* **Chain of Custody** (`chain_of_custody`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Indicate whether the evidence was handled by more than one person. This helps track the evidence's movement and prevent tampering.

* **Collector** (`collector`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name and title of the person collecting the evidence.

* **Collector Organization** (`collector_organization`, `text`, required: false): 
  Identify the organization or entity responsible for collecting the evidence.

* **Collection Date/Time** (`collection_date_time`, `date`, required: false): 
  Record the date and time the evidence was collected.

* **Storage Location** (`storage_location`, `text`, required: false): 
  Specify where the evidence is stored, including the exact location and any other relevant details.

* **Transporter** (`transporter`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name and title of the person transporting the evidence.

* **Transporter Organization** (`transporter_organization`, `text`, required: false): 
  Identify the organization or entity responsible for transporting the evidence.

* **Transport Date/Time** (`transport_date_time`, `date`, required: false): 
  Record the date and time the evidence was transported.

* **Storage Date/Time** (`storage_date_time`, `date`, required: false): 
  Record the date and time the evidence was stored.

* **Custody Transfer** (`custody_transfer`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Indicate whether the evidence was transferred between parties. This helps track the evidence's movement and prevent tampering.

* **Transferor** (`transferor`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name and title of the person transferring the evidence.

* **Transferee** (`transferee`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the name and title of the person receiving the evidence.

* **Transfer Location** (`transfer_location`, `text`, required: false): 
  Specify where the evidence was transferred, including the exact location.

* **Transfer Date/Time** (`transfer_date_time`, `date`, required: false): 
  Record the date and time the evidence was transferred.

* **Remarks** (`remarks`, `text`, required: false): 
  Provide any additional comments or notes about the evidence's handling and storage.

* **Case Number** (`case_number`, `text`, required: false): 
  Record the case number associated with the evidence.

* **Case Officer** (`case_officer`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter the officer in charge of the case.

* **Evidence Type** (`evidence_type`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Specify the type of evidence collected (e.g. Photographs, Fingerprints, Video, Audio, Documents, or Other).

* **Evidence Description Details** (`evidence_description_details`, `text`, required: false): 
  Provide a detailed description of the evidence, including its characteristics and any other relevant details.

* **Collector Signature** (`collector_signature`, `text`, required: false): 
  Sign and date the collector's signature to confirm that they collected the evidence properly.

* **Transporter Signature** (`transporter_signature`, `text`, required: false): 
  Sign and date the transporter's signature to confirm that they transported the evidence properly.

* **Case Officer Signature** (`case_officer_signature`, `text`, required: false): 
  Sign and date the case officer's signature to confirm that they received the evidence properly.

* **Remarks 2** (`remarks_2`, `text`, required: false): 
  Provide any additional comments or notes about the evidence's handling and storage.

## Tips
- Complete each page thoroughly and accurately.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
- Use the chain of custody to track the evidence's movement and prevent tampering.
- Keep a record of all signatures and dates for future reference.
- Review the form regularly to ensure that the evidence is handled and stored properly.
