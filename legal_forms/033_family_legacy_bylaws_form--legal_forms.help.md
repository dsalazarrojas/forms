# Family Legacy Bylaws Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Legacy Bylaws Form is a document that outlines the key principles and guidelines for your family's business and decision-making processes. It is used to define your family's vision, values, and rules for making decisions and interacting with each other.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and concisely, providing specific examples where possible.
2. Review and discuss each section with your family members to ensure everyone is on the same page.
3. Use the space provided for each question to elaborate on your answers.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and commitment to these bylaws.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Values**: Describe the core values that guide your family's decisions and actions. What are the most important principles that your family holds dear?
* **Decision-Making Structures**: Explain the process for making decisions within your family's business. Who is involved in the decision-making process, and how are decisions reached?
* **Review Processes**: Describe how you review and update your family's decisions and actions. Who is involved in these reviews, and what is the frequency of these reviews?
* **Family Leading Principles**: Define the principles that guide your family's leadership and decision-making roles within the business.
* **Family Members**: List all family members, including children, parents, or other significant individuals involved in the family's business. (Separate names with commas)
* **Next Gen Family Leaders**: Identify next-generation family leaders and their role in the family business.
* **Family Vision**: Describe the long-term vision for your family's business and goals.
* **Family Values Statement**: Summarize your family's values and principles in a concise statement.
* **Next Gen Family Leaders Statement**: Define the principles guiding next-generation family leaders' roles and responsibilities.
* **Review Process**: Explain the process for reviewing and updating your family's decisions and actions.
