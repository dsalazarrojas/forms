**Lawyer Application Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Lawyer Application Form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals applying for legal services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, ensure you have all required documents and details ready to be uploaded.
2. Fill in your applicant information, including your name, contact number, and email address on the "Applicant Info Form" section.
3. Provide your work experience and relevant background information in the "Work Experience" section.
4. List your educational qualifications and institutions attended in the "Educational Background" section.
5. Upload any necessary documents in the "Upload Documents" section.
6. Complete the "Contact Info" form with your preferred communication details.
7. Double-check your details in the "Confirm Details" section before proceeding.
8. Review the "Terms And Conditions" and confirm your understanding of the application process.
9. Click the "Submit" button to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info Form** (`applicant_info_form`, text, required): Provide your full name, contact number, and email address to help us contact and communicate with you efficiently.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience_form`, text, optional): Describe your previous work experience, including relevant roles and industries.
* **Educational Background** (`education_form`, text, optional): List your educational qualifications, including institutions attended and relevant courses completed.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents_form`, text, optional): Upload any supporting documents related to your application.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info_form`, text, optional): Provide your preferred contact details, such as phone number and email address.
* **Confirm Details** (`confirm_details_form`, text, optional): Double-check your provided details before finalizing your application.
* **Submit** (`submit_form`, text, optional): Confirm your application and proceed with the next steps.
* **Legal** (`legal_form`, text, optional): This field is intended for additional legal-related information.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review and confirm your understanding of the application process and terms of service.
* **Confirm** (`confirm_form`, text, optional): Double-check your information and ensure it's accurate.
* **Error Handling** (`error_handling_form`, text, optional): This field is intended for potential error handling and review purposes.

Please note that some fields are not displayed to maintain simplicity, and some fields are marked "optional" to reduce user burden.
