# Adhesion Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adhesion Agreement Form is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions of a contract between a provider and a customer. This form is used to formalize the agreement and ensure that both parties understand their obligations and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure all sections are relevant to your situation.
2. Enter the provider's name in the "Provider name" field.
3. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer name" field.
4. Enter the date on which the agreement takes effect in the "Effective date" field.
5. Provide a brief description of the material terms of the agreement in the "Terms summary" field.
6. Confirm that you have read and understood the terms by selecting "I accept" in the "Acknowledgment of terms" field.
7. If applicable, enter the process for resolving disputes in the "Dispute resolution clause" field.
8. Enter the conditions under which the contract may be terminated in the "Termination conditions" field.
9. Sign your typed name in the "Provider signature name" field if you are the provider.
10. Sign your typed name in the "Customer signature name" field if you are the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parties information**: Enter the details of the parties involved in the contract, including the provider and customer names. This field is required.
* **Provider name**: Enter the name of the company providing goods or services. This field is required.
* **Customer name**: Enter the name of the party accepting the terms of the contract. This field is required.
* **Effective date**: Enter the date on which the agreement takes effect. This field is required.
* **Terms summary**: Provide a brief overview of the material terms of the agreement. This field is required.
* **Acknowledgment of terms**: Confirm that you have read and understood the terms of the agreement. This field is required.
* **Dispute resolution clause**: Briefly describe the process for resolving any disputes that may arise. This field is optional.
* **Termination conditions**: Enter the conditions under which the contract may be terminated. This field is optional.
* **Provider signature name**: Enter your typed name as the provider signatory. This field is required.
* **Customer signature name**: Enter your typed name as the customer signatory. This field is required.
