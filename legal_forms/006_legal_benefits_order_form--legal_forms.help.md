# Legal Benefits Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to place an order for legal benefits. It requires information such as business details, request details, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Start by providing your company's information in the "Company" field.
2. Provide your business information in the "Business Information" field.
3. In the "Request Information" field, enter any additional details about your request.
4. In the "Choose Benefit" field, select the benefits you are interested in, multiple selections are allowed.
5. Enter the "Request Date" for when you would like to start the benefits.
6. Enter your signature in the "Signature" field, as this is a legal requirement.
7. Finally, enter your name, email, and phone number in the "Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Business Information**: Enter any additional details about your business.
* **Request Information**: Enter any additional details about your request.
* **Choose Benefit**: Select the benefits you are interested in.
	+ Options: 
		- Option 1
		- Employee Benefits
		- Family and Medical Leave
* **Request Date**: Enter the date you would like to start the benefits.
* **Signature**: Please sign below.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Employee Benefits**: Enter the benefits you are interested in for your employees.
* **Family and Medical**: Enter the benefits you are interested in for family and medical.
* **Employee Benefit Choices**: Select one of the options for employee benefits.
	+ Options:
		- None
		- Other
