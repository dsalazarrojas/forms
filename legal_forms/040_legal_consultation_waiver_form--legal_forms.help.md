# Legal Consultation Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Consultation Waiver Form is designed to inform clients of the terms and conditions of a legal service or treatment. This form ensures that clients understand and agree to these terms, which is crucial for a successful and legally compliant process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the Waiver Text carefully, ensuring you understand the terms and conditions of the legal service or treatment.
2. Select "I have read and understand the waiver text above" for both the Client Confirmation and Legal Professional Confirmation fields to confirm your understanding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client Identification** (`client_identification`, `number`, required: false)
* The client identification field is a unique identifier for the client.

### 2. **Waiver Text** (`waiver_text`, `text`, required: true)
* The waiver text is the terms and conditions of the legal service or treatment. Please read it carefully before proceeding.

### 3. **Client Confirmation** (`client_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: true)
* Select "I have read and understand the waiver text above" to confirm that you have read and understand the waiver text.

### 4. **Legal Professional Identification** (`legal_professional_identification`, `number`, required: false)
* This field is typically used to identify the legal professional providing the service.

### 5. **Legal Professional Confirmation** (`legal_professional_confirmation`, `select_one`, required: true)
* Select "I have read and understand the waiver text above" to confirm that the legal professional has read and understands the waiver text.

### 6. **Legal Professional Confirmation Hints** (`legal_professional_confirmation_hints`, `text`, required: false)
