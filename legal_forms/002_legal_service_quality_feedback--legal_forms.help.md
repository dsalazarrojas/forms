# Legal Service Quality Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Service Quality Feedback form is designed to collect feedback on the quality of services provided by legal service providers. It helps us understand your experience and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Rate the service on a scale of 1-5.
2. Provide a brief description of your overall quality feedback.
3. Enter the date and time of the service.
4. Enter the provider's name and their email address (if available).
5. Rate the provider (optional).
6. Enter your overall satisfaction level (optional).
7. Provide reasons for your satisfaction or dissatisfaction (optional).
8. Add any additional comments or concerns.
9. If you have any further suggestions or requests for improvement, please let us know.
10. Enter your IP address and user agent (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Rate the service**: This field is where you can rate the quality of the service you received, from 1 to 5.
* **What was your overall quality feedback**: Please provide a brief description of your experience with the service.
* **Date of service**: Enter the date you received the service.
* **Time of service**: Enter the time you received the service.
* **Provider's name**: Enter the name of the provider who assisted you with the service.
* **Provider's rating**: Rate the quality of service provided by the provider (optional).
* **Overall satisfaction**: Rate your overall satisfaction with the service (optional).
* **Reasons for satisfaction or dissatisfaction**: If you're satisfied, please describe what made you happy. If you're dissatisfied, please explain why.
* **Additional comments**: Add any additional comments or concerns you may have.
* **Is there anything else we can improve or change?**: Share any ideas you may have for improving the service.
* **Provider's email**: Enter the provider's email address (if available).
* **Provider's phone**: Enter the provider's phone number (if available).
* **Submitted by**: This field is for your identification and verification purposes.
* **Submitted at**: Enter the date and time you submitted this form.
* **IP address**: This field is for tracking and verification purposes.
* **User agent**: This field is for tracking and verification purposes.
