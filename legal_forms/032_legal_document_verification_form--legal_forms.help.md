# Legal Document Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Document Verification Form is designed to collect verification information related to legal documents. This form is used to verify the authenticity and validity of legal documents, ensuring that they are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the options for each field carefully and select the correct options for your document.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information to complete the form, including the document ID, type, and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Document Verification** (`legal_document_verification_form`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are verifying the document's authenticity or accuracy.
* **Document Id** (`legal_document_verification_form_document_id`, select_multiple, optional): Select all IDs related to the document you are verifying.
* **Document Type** (`legal_document_verification_form_document_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of document being verified (e.g., contract, agreement, etc.).
* **Document Date** (`legal_document_verification_form_document_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the document.
* **Verification Date** (`legal_document_verification_form_verification_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the verification.
* **Verification Method** (`legal_document_verification_form_verification_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method used for verification (e.g., in-person, online, etc.).
* **Document Status** (`legal_document_verification_form_document_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of the document (e.g., Active, Inactive, etc.).
* **Additional Notes** (`legal_document_verification_form_additional_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the document.
* **Verification Comments** (`legal_document_verification_form_verification_comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about the verification process.
* **Verified By** (`legal_document_verification_form_verified_by`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are verifying the document.
* **Verified Date** (`legal_document_verification_form_verified_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the verification.

## Tips

* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form to ensure the document's authenticity and validity are verified correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please review the form carefully or seek assistance from a relevant authority.
* Ensure all fields are filled out completely and accurately to avoid errors and confusion.
