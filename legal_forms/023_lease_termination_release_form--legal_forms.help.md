# Lease Termination Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lease Termination Release Form" is a document used to formalize the termination of a lease agreement between a tenant and a landlord. This form captures essential details regarding the termination date, termination reasons, and financial obligations between the parties involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide the termination date of the lease agreement in the "Termination Date" field.
2.  If required, sign and date the "Tenant Signature" field with your name as the tenant.
3.  If required, sign and date the "Landlord Signature" field with your name as the landlord.
4.  Provide a clear reason for the lease termination in the "Reason for Termination" field.
5.  Indicate whether the property will be returned to the landlord or not in the "Return Property" field.
6.  If necessary, specify the outstanding balance and interest in the "Outstanding Balance" and "Outstanding Interest" fields, respectively.
7.  Enter the amount of rent paid through the lease in the "Rent Paid Through" field.
8.  Enter the lease end date in the "Lease End Date" field.
9.  If applicable, specify any outstanding fees and interest in the "Outstanding Fee" and "Outstanding Interest" fields.
10.  Review and confirm the outstanding amounts and fees in the "Total Outstanding" and "Total Outstanding Fee" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Termination Date** (`lease_termination_date`, date, required/optional): The date when the lease agreement is terminated.
* **Tenant Signature** (`tenant_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign here to acknowledge your agreement to terminate the lease.
* **Landlord Signature** (`landlord_signature`, text, required/optional): Sign here to acknowledge your agreement to terminate the lease.
* **Reason for Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, text, required/optional): Clearly state the reason for terminating the lease.
* **Notice Period** (`notice_period`, number, required/optional): Specify the notice period for lease termination.
* **Outstanding Balance** (`outstanding_balance`, number, required/optional): Enter the outstanding balance of the lease agreement.
* **Return Property** (`return_property`, select_one, required/optional): Check 'Yes' if the property will be returned, 'No' if it won't, or 'Maybe' for uncertain situations.
* **Rent Paid Through** (`rent_paid_through`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when rent was last paid.
* **Lease End Date** (`lease_end_date`, date, required/optional): Specify the end date of the lease.
* **Outstanding Amount** (`outstanding_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter any outstanding amounts from the lease.
* **Outstanding Interest** (`outstanding_interest`, number, required/optional): Enter any outstanding interest on the lease.
* **Total Outstanding** (`total_outstanding`, number, required/optional): Review and confirm the total outstanding amount.
* **Outstanding Fee** (`outstanding_fee`, number, required/optional): Enter any outstanding fees from the lease.
* **Total Outstanding Fee** (`total_outstanding_fee`, number, required/optional): Review and confirm the total outstanding fee.
