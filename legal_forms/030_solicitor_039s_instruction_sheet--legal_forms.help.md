# Solicitor's Instruction Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solicitor's Instruction Sheet is a template used by solicitors to collect and record information and instructions related to their work with clients. This form helps solicitors provide clear and concise instructions to clients and captures relevant details about the client and solicitor's work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a brief overview of the client's information, including their name, in the "Client Information" field.
2. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
3. Enter your solicitor name in the "Solicitor Name" field.
4. Enter your solicitor's phone number in the "Solicitor Phone" field.
5. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
6. Select the type of document(s) related to the client's matter using the "Document Type" field. You can choose one or multiple options from the list.
7. Provide clear and concise instructions in the "Instructions" field, as this is where you will detail any specific actions or tasks the client needs to perform.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information** (solicitor_instruction_sheet, text, optional): This field is a brief overview of the client's information. You can enter any relevant details here.
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, optional): Enter the client's full name here.
* **Solicitor Name** (solicitor_name, text, optional): Enter your full name as the solicitor.
* **Solicitor Phone** (solicitor_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the solicitor.
* **Client Email** (client_email, email, optional): Enter the client's email address here.
* **Document Type** (document_type, select_multiple, optional): Choose the type of document related to the client's matter. Select one or multiple options from the list.
* **Instructions** (instruction, note, optional): Provide clear and concise instructions to the client, detailing any specific actions or tasks they need to perform.
