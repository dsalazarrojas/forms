# legal_client_intake_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The legal_client_intake_form is designed to collect information from clients regarding their legal concerns, client details, case specifics, contact information, agreement terms, and any additional notes. This form is intended for internal use in the legal industry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.
2. Enter your legal concerns, client details, case specifics, and contact information in the respective text fields.
3. Select one or more agreements from the options provided.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
5. Ensure you have filled in all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Concerns** (`legal_concerns`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the client's legal issues or concerns.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Case Details** (`case_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief overview of the case, including any relevant dates, locations, or circumstances.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the client's contact information, including phone number, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following agreements:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the case.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately to facilitate proper case management.
* Review each field carefully to avoid duplication of information.
* Use this form as a template for internal use only.
* For more complex or sensitive cases, seek additional guidance from a licensed attorney.
