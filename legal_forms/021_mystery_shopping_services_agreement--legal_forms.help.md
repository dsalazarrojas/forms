# Mystery Shopping Services Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture the details of a mystery shopping services agreement between a shopper and an approver, ensuring both parties have a clear understanding of their roles, responsibilities, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the shopper information section (page 1) with your details as a shopper.
2. Review and agree to the assignment terms on page 2.
3. Confirm your confidentiality expectations and conduct as a shopper on page 3.
4. Acknowledge your understanding of the agreement on page 4.
5. Provide approver information on page 5.
6. Approve the agreement on page 6.
7. Sign and date the agreement on page 7.
8. Sign and date the approval on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Shopper Info (Page 1)
* Shopper Info: Enter your name, email address, and other relevant contact information as the shopper.
### Assignment Terms (Page 2)
* Assignment Terms: Describe the specific services you will provide as a shopper, including any specific tasks or responsibilities assigned to you.
### Confidentiality Expectations (Page 3)
* Confidentiality Expectations: Confirm that you understand the importance of maintaining confidentiality and agree to keep all information related to the mystery shopping services confidential.
### Shopper Conduct (Page 4)
* Shopper Conduct: Explain how you will conduct yourself during the mystery shopping services, including any specific expectations or rules you must follow.
### Shopper Acknowledgement (Page 5)
* Shopper Acknowledgement: Confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions of the agreement.
### Approver Info (Page 6)
* Approver Info: Enter the name, email address, and other relevant contact information of the person approving the agreement.
### Approval (Page 7)
* Approval: Confirm that you, as the approver, have reviewed and approved the agreement and the shopper's information.
### Shopper Sign Date (Page 8)
* Shopper Sign Date: Date and sign the agreement to confirm that you, as the shopper, have read and agreed to the terms.
### Approver Sign Date (Page 9)
* Approver Sign Date: Date and sign the agreement to confirm that you, as the approver, have reviewed and approved the terms.
### Shopper Sign (Page 10)
* Shopper Sign: Date and sign the agreement to confirm that you, as the shopper, have read and agreed to the terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the entire form carefully before signing.
* Ensure that you understand all the terms and conditions before agreeing to the mystery shopping services.
* Communicate with the approver if you have any questions or concerns about the form or the services.
