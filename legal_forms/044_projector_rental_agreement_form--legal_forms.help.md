# Projector Rental Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to enter their details for a projector rental agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the form fields with your information.
* Review each field carefully for any required information.
* Make sure to enter your information correctly, as any mistakes may delay the rental process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rental Agreement Form** (`<no label>`): This is the title of the form. Please enter your name in the space provided, if you'd like to give a title to the form.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed on any communication related to this rental.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, optional): Enter your email address for any communication related to this rental.
* **Rental Start Date** (`rental_start_date`, optional): Enter the date you'd like to start renting the projector.
* **Rental End Date** (`rental_end_date`, optional): Enter the date you'd like to end renting the projector.
* **Rental Start Time** (`rental_start_time`, optional): Enter the start time you'd like to use the projector.
* **Rental End Time** (`rental_end_time`, optional): Enter the end time you'd like to return the projector.
* **Rental Price** (`rental_price`, optional): Enter the rental price of the projector.
* **Rental Terms** (`rental_terms`, optional): Enter any special rental terms or conditions here.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, optional): Enter any special payment terms or conditions here.
