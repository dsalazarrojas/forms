# Gift Card Seller Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by sellers to agree to the terms and conditions of selling gift cards. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth selling experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Seller Details** page with your information.
2. Review and agree to the **Terms And Conditions**.
3. Select your agreement type from the **Agreement Type** dropdown menu.
4. Set your **Seller Status** to Active, Inactive, or Awaiting Verification.
5. Fill out the **Agreement Date** if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Details** (text): Enter your company's name, address, and other relevant contact information. This is the first page of the form.
* **Seller Name** (text): Enter your name as the seller representative. This field is part of the seller_details page.
* **Seller Address** (text): Enter your company's address. This field is also part of the seller_details page.
* **Seller Email** (email): Enter your email address. This field is used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Terms And Conditions** (note): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of selling gift cards. This is a critical section that outlines the responsibilities and expectations of selling gift cards.
* **Agreement** (select_one): Select "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions" to confirm your agreement.
* **Review Period** (time): Enter the review period for your agreement (optional).
* **Agreement Date** (date): Enter the date of your agreement (optional).
* **Seller Status** (select_multiple): Choose your seller status as Active, Inactive, or Awaiting Verification.
* **Agreement Type** (select_one): Select the type of agreement as Merchant Agreement, Reseller Agreement, or Both.
* **Agreement** (select_one): Although this field has the same label as the above field, it appears to be a duplicate field with the label "Agreement" which is actually "I have read and agree to the terms and conditions".
