# Board Consent For Stock Options - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Board Consent for Stock Options form is used to collect information for the approval of stock options grants to employees or directors. This form is used to ensure that the board has approved stock options grants in accordance with the company's policies and procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the Company Name field with the full legal name of the company.
- Enter the State of Incorporation where the company is incorporated.
- Select the Board Meeting Date when the board approved the stock options grant.
- Choose the type of meeting where the board approved the grant (Regular Meeting, Special Meeting, or Written Consent).
- List the Board Members Present at the meeting.
- Select whether the Quorum was met.
- Enter the name of the Option Grant Recipient.
- Enter the Recipient Title or position.
- Enter the total Number of Options granted.
- Choose the type of stock option (Incentive Stock Options, Non-Qualified Stock Options, or Restricted Stock Units).
- Enter the Exercise Price Per Share.
- Enter the Grant Date when the options were awarded.
- Choose the Vesting Schedule (4 Year with 1 Year Cliff, 4 Year Equal Monthly, etc).
- Enter the Vesting Commencement Date.
- Enter the Option Expiration Date.
- Enter the Total Shares Authorized under the plan.
- Enter the Shares Remaining in the pool.
- Check if the grant includes Acceleration Clause (Single Trigger, Double Trigger, or None).
- If applicable, enter the Board Approval Resolution Number.
- Enter the name of the Secretary for certification.
- Enter the Certification Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of the company.
* **State of Incorporation** (`company_state`, text, required): Enter the state where the company is incorporated.
* **Board Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Select the date of the board meeting where the stock options grant was approved.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of board meeting (Regular Meeting, Special Meeting, or Written Consent).
* **Board Members Present** (`board_member_present`, select_multiple, required): List the Board members who attended the meeting.
* **Quorum Met** (`quorum_met`, select_one, required): Select whether the quorum was met (True or False).
* **Option Grant Recipient** (`option_grant_recipient`, text, required): Enter the name of the individual who received the stock options grant.
* **Recipient Title** (`recipient_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title or position of the recipient.
* **Number of Options** (`number_of_options`, number, required): Enter the total number of stock options granted.
* **Option Type** (`option_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of stock option (Incentive Stock Options, Non-Qualified Stock Options, or Restricted Stock Units).
* **Exercise Price Per Share** (`exercise_price`, number, required): Enter the price per share in dollars.
* **Grant Date** (`grant_date`, date, required): Enter the date the stock options were awarded.
* **Vesting Schedule** (`vesting_schedule`, select_one, required): Choose the vesting terms (4 Year with 1 Year Cliff, 4 Year Equal Monthly, etc.).
* **Vesting Commencement Date** (`vesting_commencement`, date, required): Enter the date when vesting begins.
* **Option Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the date the stock options expire.
* **Total Shares Authorized** (`total_shares_authorized`, number, required): Enter the total shares under the plan.
* **Shares Remaining in Pool** (`shares_remaining`, number, required): Enter the number of shares remaining in the pool.
* **Acceleration Clause** (`acceleration_clause`, select_one, optional): Check if the grant includes Acceleration Clause (Single Trigger, Double Trigger, or None).
* **Board Approval Resolution Number** (`board_approval_resolution`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the official resolution number.
* **Secretary Certification** (`secretary_certification`, text, required): Enter the name of the secretary for certification.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, required): Enter the date of certification.

Note: This help guide is a general guide to assist users in completing the form. Please refer to the company's policies and procedures for specific requirements and guidelines.
