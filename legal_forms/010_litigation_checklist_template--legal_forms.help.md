# Litigation Checklist Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Litigation Checklist Template is a tool to help track and document key information and status updates for litigation cases.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the sections as needed, providing relevant information for each field.
2. Make sure to review and proofread your input to ensure accuracy.
3. Save your progress and update the form as the case changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Plaintiffs Info**: Enter brief information about the plaintiff, including any relevant details or background.
* **Case Details**: Provide a short description of the case, including the type of case and any relevant circumstances.
* **Parties Involved**: Select the type of parties involved in the case (Plaintiff, Defendant, or Both).
* **Case Status**: Choose the current status of the case (Pending, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Next Steps**: Record any next steps or actions required for the case.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the case.
* **Save Data**: This field is for internal use only, do not fill it out.
* **Plaintiffs Attorney**: Enter the name of the plaintiff's attorney.
* **Defendants Attorney**: Enter the name of the defendant's attorney.
* **Plaintiffs Address**: Enter the plaintiff's address.
* **Defendants Address**: Enter the defendant's address.
* **Plaintiffs Phone**: Enter the plaintiff's phone number.
* **Defendants Phone**: Enter the defendant's phone number.
* **Case Number**: Enter the case number (if applicable).
* **Case Date**: Enter the date the case was opened.
* **Case Time**: Enter the time the case was opened.
* **Plaintiff Info**: Enter any additional information about the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Info**: Enter any additional information about the defendant.
* **Litigation Status**: Choose the current litigation status (Active, Completed, or Suspended).
* **Plaintiff Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the plaintiff.
* **Defendant Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the defendant.
* **Other Parties**: Enter the name of any other parties involved in the case.
* **Other Parties Info**: Enter any additional information about other parties involved.
* **Other Parties Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about other parties involved.
* **Case Type**: Choose the type of case (Civil, Criminal, or Personal).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out each field accurately and thoroughly.
- Review and proofread your input regularly.
- Update the form as the case progresses.
- This form is for internal use only, do not share it with clients or third parties without permission.
