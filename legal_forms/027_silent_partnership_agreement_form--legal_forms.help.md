# Silent Partnership Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Silent Partnership Agreement Form is a document used to formalize and outline the terms of a silent partnership, a type of business partnership where one partner is actively involved in the business while the other partner is not.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for both the active and silent partners.
2. Select the roles and responsibilities of each partner.
3. List the contributions of each partner, including any monetary or asset-based contributions.
4. Determine the profit share for the silent partner.
5. Choose the type of share distribution for the silent partner (monetary, percentage, or other).
6. List any confirmations or agreements made between the partners.
7. Sign the form to confirm the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Active Partner**
	+ The active partner is the individual who will be actively involved in the day-to-day operations of the business.
* **Silent Partner**
	+ The silent partner is the individual who will not be actively involved in the day-to-day operations of the business but will still have a stake in the profits.
* **Roles**
	+ Choose the roles and responsibilities of each partner by selecting "Active Partner", "Silent Partner", or "Other".
* **Contributions**
	+ List all the contributions made by each partner, including any monetary or asset-based contributions.
* **Profit Share**
	+ Determine the percentage of profit share for the silent partner, which can be 50%, 60%, or 70%.
* **Share Type**
	+ Choose the type of share distribution for the silent partner, which can be "Monetary", "Percentage", or "Other".
* **Confirmations**
	+ List any confirmations or agreements made between the partners.
* **Active Partner's Signature**
	+ The active partner's signature confirms their agreement to the terms of the partnership.
* **Silent Partner's Signature**
	+ The silent partner's signature confirms their agreement to the terms of the partnership.

## Tips
- Ensure that both partners sign and date the form to confirm their agreement.
- Review the form carefully to ensure all terms are clear and understood.
- Keep a copy of the signed form for future reference and record-keeping.
