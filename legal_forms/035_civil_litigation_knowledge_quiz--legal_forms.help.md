# Civil Litigation Knowledge Quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a self-assessment tool designed to test your knowledge on civil litigation, a type of legal action that involves disputes between private parties or organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your name and email address, which will be used for results and feedback.
2. Answer the questions on each page, using the corresponding text boxes or select options.
3. For questions with text boxes, provide a short description in the space provided.
4. For questions with options, select the correct answer from the provided choices.
5. Finally, submit the form to review your results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Participant name
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on your results.

### Page 2: Email address
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for results and feedback. If you do not wish to provide an email address, you can leave this field blank.

### Page 3: Statute of limitations concept
* **What is the statute of limitations concept** (`q1`, `text`, required): Describe the concept of statute of limitations in a short sentence.

### Page 4: Remedy orders specific performance or damages
* **Which remedy orders specific performance or damages** (`q2`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct answer from the options below:
	+ Specific performance
	+ Damages
	+ Both

### Page 5: Plausible note
* **What is a plaint note** (`q3`, `text`, optional): Provide a short description of a plaint note.

### Page 6: Burden of proof in civil cases
* **Identify burden of proof in civil cases** (`q4`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct answer from the options below:
	+ Preponderance of evidence
	+ Beyond reasonable doubt
	+ Clear and convincing

### Page 7: Discovery phase
* **What is the discovery phase** (`q5`, `text`, optional): Provide a short description of the discovery phase.

### Page 8: Time spent studying civil litigation
* **Time spent studying civil litigation hours** (`time_spent_studying`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate hours spent studying civil litigation.

### Page 9: Confidence level in knowledge
* **Confidence level in knowledge** (`confidence_level`, `number`, optional): Enter your confidence level in the knowledge, from 1 low to 5 high.

### Page 10: Submission date
* **Submission date** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): Use the current date, which will be used to timestamp your results.

Note: This help guide is intended to be a basic reference for users. It assumes that users have a basic understanding of the form's purpose and functionality.
