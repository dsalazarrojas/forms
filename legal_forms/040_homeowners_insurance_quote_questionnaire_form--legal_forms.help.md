# Homeowners Insurance Quote Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeowners Insurance Quote Questionnaire Form is a simple form designed to collect necessary information from homeowners seeking insurance quotes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with your information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter your home's value, street, city, and zip code.
4. Indicate how many years you have resided in your current home.
5. Select the type(s) of insurance you are interested in (e.g., Automobile, Renters, etc.).
6. Submit the form to receive a quote for your insurance needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for quick contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Home Value** (`home_value`, `text`, required: false): Enter the value of your home.
* **Street** (`street`, `text`, required: false): Enter the street address of your home.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where your home is located.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where your home is located.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Years Resided** (`years_resided`, `number`, required: false): Enter how many years you have resided in your current home.
* **Type of Insurance** (`insurance_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the type(s) of insurance you are interested in (e.g., Automobile, Renters, etc.).
* **Insurance Type Options** (`insurance_type_options`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one insurance type from the list (e.g., Automobile, Renters, etc.).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to receive an accurate quote.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
