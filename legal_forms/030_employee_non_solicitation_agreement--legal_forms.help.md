# Employee Non Solicitation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Non-Solicitation Agreement is a form that helps protect an employee's employer from potential solicitation activities that may lead to conflicts of interest or intellectual property theft. This agreement is typically used to outline the rules and expectations of an employee's work, ensuring they do not engage in activities that may harm the company or its interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields as you go along, but note that all fields are optional.
2. The form has 8 sections:
	* Employee Acknowledgement
	* Employee Name
	* Company Name
	* Job Title
	* Date Of Joining
	* Non Solicitation Terms
	* Company Address
3. Answer the questions as accurately and honestly as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Acknowledgement** (`employee_acknowledgement`, text, optional): Please provide a clear and honest statement indicating that you have read and understand the terms and conditions of this agreement.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current or future employer.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as per your official employment records.
* **Date Of Joining** (`date_of_joining`, date, optional): Enter the date you started your employment with the company.
* **Non Solicitation Terms** (`non_solicitation_terms`, text, optional): Describe the terms you are agreeing not to engage in, such as contacting previous colleagues or sharing confidential information.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your current or future employer. 

## Tips
* Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting the form.
* Use your official employment records and company policies as reference for the required information.
* If you are unsure about any information, do not hesitate to contact HR or your supervisor for clarification.
