# Termination For Cause Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to document the termination of a contract between an employee and an employer. It's intended to provide a standard template for recording the essential details of the termination process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee's details in the "Employee Name", "Employee Email", "Employee Title", and "Company" fields.
2. Enter the contract termination date in the "Termination Date" field.
3. Describe the reason for termination in the "Termination Reason" field.
4. Attach any supporting documents to the "Supporting Documents" field.
5. Sign and date the "Employee Signature" and "Employer Signature" fields to confirm the termination.
6. Ensure that the "Date Submitted" field is accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Termination For Cause** (`termination_for_cause_contract_form`, Text, Optional): This field is for the main topic of the form. Enter a brief description of the termination process.
* **Cause Of Action** (`cause_of_action`, Text, Optional): Describe the reason for the termination. This can include details about performance issues, misconduct, or other reasons for ending the contract.
* **Date Of Termination** (`date_of_termination`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the contract was terminated.
* **Reason For Termination** (`reason_for_termination`, Text, Optional): Elaborate on the reason for termination, including any specific incidents or events that led to the decision.
* **Support Documentation** (`support_documentation`, Text, Optional): Attach any supporting documents, such as emails, contracts, or witness statements, to corroborate the termination.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, Text, Optional): Sign and date this field to confirm your agreement to the termination.
* **Employer Signature** (`employer_signature`, Text, Optional): Sign and date this field to confirm the employer's agreement to the termination.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, Text, Optional): Attach any supporting documents to this submission, such as contracts, emails, or witness statements.
* **Date Submitted** (`date_submitted`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the employee being terminated.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, Text, Optional): Enter the email address of the employee being terminated.
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, Text, Optional): Enter the title of the employee being terminated.
* **Company** (`company`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the company involved in the termination.
* **Contract Number** (`contract_number`, Text, Optional): Enter the contract number related to the termination.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the contract was terminated.
* **Termination Reason** (`termination_reason`, Text, Optional): Elaborate on the reason for termination.
* **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, Text, Optional): Attach any supporting documents to this submission.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* Attach supporting documents to corroborate the termination.
* Sign and date the "Employee Signature" and "Employer Signature" fields to confirm the termination.
