# Contract Amendment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Contract Amendment Form is used to document changes made to an existing contract. This form ensures that all parties are aware of the changes and their impact, and that all parties consent to the amendments. It is an essential process for maintaining transparency and clarity in contractual agreements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your requestor information, including your name, job title, and organization.
2. Enter the requestor's date and the original contract details, such as the title, date, and number.
3. Specify the date of the amendment and the parties involved.
4. Indicate the type of amendment being made (e.g., price adjustment, timeline extension, etc.).
5. Identify which section of the contract is being amended.
6. Describe the current and proposed language of the amendment.
7. Provide a reason for the amendment and an impact assessment.
8. Enter any financial impact amount if applicable.
9. Specify the effective and expiration dates of the amendment.
10. Confirm that the legal review is completed or indicate otherwise.
11. Confirm that all parties have given their consent.
12. Add any attachments or supporting documents.
13. Enter the authorized signature and date signed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Requestor Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as the individual requesting the amendment.
* **Requestor Title** (2, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Organization** (3, text, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Amendment Date** (4, date, required): Enter the date of the amendment.
* **Original Contract Title** (5, text, required): Enter the name of the original contract.
* **Original Contract Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date of the original contract.
* **Original Contract Number** (7, text, not required): Enter the original contract reference number (if applicable).
* **Parties Involved** (8, text, required): List all parties involved in the amendment.
* **Amendment Type** (9, select_one, required): Select the type of amendment being made (e.g., price adjustment, timeline extension, etc.).
* **Section Affected** (10, text, required): Identify which section of the contract is being amended.
* **Current Language** (11, text, required): Enter the existing contract language.
* **Proposed Language** (12, text, required): Enter the new amended language.
* **Reason for Amendment** (13, text, required): Describe why this change is needed.
* **Impact Assessment** (14, select_one, required): Assess the impact of the amendment (e.g., no financial impact, cost increase, etc.).
* **Financial Impact Amount** (15, number, not required): Enter the dollar amount of the financial impact (if applicable).
* **Effective Date** (16, date, required): Enter the date when the amendment takes effect.
* **Expiration Date** (17, date, not required): Enter the new expiration date if changed (if applicable).
* **Legal Review Completed** (18, select_one, required): Confirm if the legal review is completed.
* **All Parties Consent** (19, select_one, required): Confirm if all parties have given their consent.
* **Attachments** (20, text, not required): List any attached documents (if applicable).
* **Authorized Signature** (21, text, required): Type your name as the authorized signature.
* **Signature Date** (22, date, required): Enter the date signed.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only and may not be applicable to every situation. Consult a legal expert for specific advice on contract amendments.
