# Trademark Search Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trademark Search Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from users about their trademark search requests. This form helps the system understand the user's needs and provide relevant search results.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. Select the search type that best describes your needs.
3. Provide a brief description of your search request.
4. Add any relevant search terms or keywords.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Select your preferred contact method.
7. Choose the location where you would like to search.
8. Select the date and time you would like to search.
9. Choose the status of your search.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, **optional**): This field is used to specify the name of the business related to your search request.
* **Search Type** (`search_type`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the type of search you would like to perform, such as "Trademark Status" or "Trademark Availability".
* **Description** (`description`, text, **optional**): Add a brief description of your search request, which will help the system understand your needs.
* **Search Terms** (`search_terms`, text, **optional**): Enter any search terms or keywords related to your search request.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address where you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number where you would like to be contacted.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose how you would like to be contacted, such as "Email" or "Phone".
* **Search Location** (`search_location`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the location where you would like to search, such as "United States" or "Global".
* **Search Date** (`search_date`, date, **optional**): Select the date when you would like to search.
* **Search Time** (`search_time`, time, **optional**): Select the time when you would like to search.
* **Search Status** (`search_status`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the status of your search, such as "Active" or "In Progress".
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, **optional**): Choose the tool that you would like to use for your search.
