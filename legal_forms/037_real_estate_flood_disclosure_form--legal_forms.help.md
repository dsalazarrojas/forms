# Real Estate Flood Disclosure Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Real Estate Flood Disclosure Form is a document used to collect information about a property and its flood risk. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the buyer or renter is aware of the flood risks associated with the property.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Select the appropriate flood zone for your property from the available options (Zone 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
3. Choose the level of flood risk associated with your property (Low, Moderate, or High).
4. If your property has a flood elevation, please enter the exact elevation.
5. If you have a contact method (Phone, Email, or In Person), select it from the options.
6. Enter your contact number (if Phone is selected) or contact email (if Email is selected).
7. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information:**
	+ **Contact Information** (Text, Required: false): This is the first field on the form where you provide your contact information.
* **Property Details:**
	+ **Property Details** (Text, Required: false): This field is for you to provide any additional information about the property.
* **Flood Zones:**
	+ **Flood Zones** (Select Multiple, Required: false): Choose one or more flood zones that apply to your property.
* **Flood Risk:**
	+ **Flood Risk** (Select One, Required: false): Choose the level of flood risk associated with your property.
* **Flood Elevation:**
	+ **Flood Elevation** (Number, Required: false): If your property has a flood elevation, please enter the exact elevation.
* **Flood Date:**
	+ **Flood Date** (Date, Required: false): If you have a flood date, please enter the date.
* **Contact Method:**
	+ **Contact Method** (Select One, Required: false): Choose how you would like to be contacted (Phone, Email, or In Person).
* **Contact Number:**
	+ **Contact Number** (Text, Required: false): If you selected Phone as the contact method, enter your contact number.
* **Contact Email:**
	+ **Contact Email** (Email, Required: false): If you selected Email as the contact method, enter your email address.
* **In Person:**
	+ **In Person** (No label, Required: false): If you selected In Person as the contact method, this field is not applicable. 

Please note that all fields are optional unless marked as required. If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to the instructions above or consult a professional for guidance.
