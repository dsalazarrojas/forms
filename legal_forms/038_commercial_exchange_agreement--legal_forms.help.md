# Commercial Exchange Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document commercial exchanges between two parties, including details such as agreement date, exchange type, and terms and conditions. This form helps to ensure that both parties are on the same page and provides a clear understanding of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Agreement Date field with the date the agreement was signed.
2. Enter the Agreement Number for reference purposes.
3. Provide information about Party A, including their name, address, contact person, email, and phone number.
4. Provide information about Party B, including their name, address, contact person, email, and phone number.
5. Describe the exchange type (e.g., Goods for Goods, Goods for Services, etc.).
6. Specify what Party A is providing (description of goods or services) and the estimated value.
7. Specify what Party B is providing (description of goods or services) and the estimated value.
8. Enter the exchange timeline (when the exchange will occur).
9. Choose the delivery method (e.g., Pickup, Standard Shipping, etc.).
10. Check if cash payment is required.
11. If payment is required, enter the payment amount.
12. Check if a warranty is included.
13. If a warranty is included, describe the warranty terms.
14. Check if returns are accepted.
15. Specify how disputes will be handled.
16. Enter the governing law that applies to the agreement.
17. Sign and date the agreement for both parties.
18. (Optional) Add any additional terms or conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Date**: The date the agreement was signed.
* **Agreement Number**: A reference number for the agreement.
* **-- Party A Information --**: This section is for Party A's information.
	+ **Party A Name**: The legal business name of Party A.
	+ **Party A Address**: The complete address of Party A.
	+ **Party A Contact**: The contact person's name for Party A.
	+ **Party A Email**: The email address of Party A.
	+ **Party A Phone**: The phone number of Party A.
* **-- Party B Information --**: This section is for Party B's information.
	+ **Party B Name**: The legal business name of Party B.
	+ **Party B Address**: The complete address of Party B.
	+ **Party B Contact**: The contact person's name for Party B.
	+ **Party B Email**: The email address of Party B.
	+ **Party B Phone**: The phone number of Party B.
* **Exchange Type**: The type of exchange (e.g., Goods for Goods, Goods for Services, etc.).
* **Party A Provides**: A description of what Party A is providing.
* **Party A Value**: The estimated value of what Party A is providing.
* **Party B Provides**: A description of what Party B is providing.
* **Party B Value**: The estimated value of what Party B is providing.
* **Exchange Timeline**: The date and time when the exchange will occur.
* **Delivery Method**: The method of delivery (e.g., Pickup, Standard Shipping, etc.).
* **Additional Payment Required**: Check if a payment is required.
* **Payment Amount**: If a payment is required, enter the payment amount.
* **Warranty Included**: Check if a warranty is included.
* **Warranty Terms**: If a warranty is included, describe the terms.
* **Return Policy**: Check if returns are accepted.
* **Dispute Resolution**: Specify how disputes will be handled.
* **Governing Law**: Enter the state laws that apply to the agreement.
* **-- Signatures --**: This section is for signatures from both parties.
	+ **Party A Signature**: The authorized signature of Party A.
	+ **Party A Signature Date**: The date signed by Party A.
	+ **Party B Signature**: The authorized signature of Party B.
	+ **Party B Signature Date**: The date signed by Party B.
* **Witness Name**: (Optional) The name of the witness.
* **Witness Signature**: (Optional) The signature of the witness.
