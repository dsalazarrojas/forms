# Opposition Motion Response Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Opposition Motion Response Form is designed to gather necessary information to respond to an opposition motion in a legal context. This form ensures that all required details are captured and presented in a clear and concise manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter the **Motion Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
2.  Enter the **Motion Time** in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
3.  Enter the **Motion Hour** as a numerical value to indicate the hour of the motion.
4.  Enter the **Motion Number** as a text response, which may be a reference number or a brief description of the motion.
5.  Provide **Written Responses** to the motion in the space below.
6.  Select the **Supporting Documents** that are relevant to the motion. You may choose one or more documents from the options provided.
7.  Sign and date your **Signature** in the space provided. This section is for your signature and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Motion Date** (`motion_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date on which the motion was received or submitted.
*   **Motion Time** (`motion_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time at which the motion was received or submitted.
*   **Motion Hour** (`motion_hour`, number, required/optional): Enter the hour of the motion, if applicable.
*   **Motion Number** (`motion_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the reference number or brief description of the motion.
*   **Written Responses** (`written_responses`, text, required/optional): Provide a detailed response to the motion, addressing all points.
*   **Supporting Documents** (`supporting_documents`, select_one, required/optional): Select the supporting documents relevant to the motion from the options provided.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Sign and date your response in the designated space.

## Tips

*   Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
*   Review and proofread your responses carefully for accuracy and completeness.
*   Attach all required supporting documents to support your response.
*   Sign and date your response to confirm your opposition's position.
