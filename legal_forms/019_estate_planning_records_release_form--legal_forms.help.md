# Estate Planning Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Estate Planning Records Release Form is a legal document that allows clients to release their estate planning documents, ensuring they understand what they are consenting to and agreeing to have their information shared.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately with your name and contact information in the top section (`Client Name`, `Email` and `Phone` fields).
- Select the relevant estate planning documents for release (in the `Estate Planning Documents` field) and ensure you've checked the correct boxes in `Legal Authority`.
- Specify the date and time you wish to have control over the release, if applicable (in `Release Date` and `Release Time` fields).
- Provide the name of the authorized person or organization (in `Authorized Person` field) and confirm with their signature (in `Authorized Signature` field) that you've authorized them to handle your records.
- Explain the reason for release (in `Reason for Release` field) and confirm your consent with a signature (in `Client Signature` field).
- Specify the method of authorization (in `Authorization Method` field).
- Ensure the authorized person or organization has signed the `Authorized Agreement` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter your full name, as it should be on your documents.
* **Release Date**: The date you wish to have control over the release. If you're unsure, you can leave it blank.
* **Release Time**: If you've specified a release date, choose a time you'd like to have control over.
* **Client Address**: Enter your current address to ensure we can reach you with any updates or notifications.
* **Estate Planning Documents**: Check the boxes for which documents you wish to release.
* **Legal Authority**: Confirm which legal authorities you're releasing your documents to.
* **Email/Phone**: Enter your email and phone number so we can contact you about your records.
* **Authorized Person**: Enter the name of the person or organization authorized to handle your records.
* **Authorized Signature**: This person must sign to confirm their authorization.
* **Client Signature**: You must sign to confirm you authorize the release of your documents.
* **Reason for Release**: Briefly explain why you're releasing your documents.
* **Client Consent**: Ensure you've checked the box to confirm your consent.
* **Client Agreement**: Enter any conditions or terms you want to agree on with the authorized person or organization.
* **Authorized Agreement**: The authorized person or organization must agree to these conditions.
* **Authorization Code**: If required, enter any authorization codes or passwords for your records.
* **Release Type**: Specify the type of release you're making (e.g., full, partial).
* **Release Date 2**: If you've made a release before, you can specify a second date for another type of release.
* **Release Time 2**: Choose a time for this release.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information you want to share with the authorized person or organization.
* **Submit**: Confirm you've reviewed and agree to all details by selecting 'True'.
