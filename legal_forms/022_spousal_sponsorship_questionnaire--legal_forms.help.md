# spousal_sponsorship_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spousal Sponsorship Questionnaire is a form used to gather information on the relationship status between a spouse/sponsor and their dependents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. Double-check your answers for completeness and accuracy.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Relationship Details**: Enter a brief description of your relationship with your spouse/dependent.
* **Married Citizen**: Select whether you are married or not.
* **Other Information**: Enter any other relevant information about your relationship (e.g., date of marriage, location of marriage).
* **Sponsorship Status**: Select the status of your sponsorship (Active or Inactive).
* **Sponsorship Information**: Enter any information about your sponsorships (e.g., sponsor names, dates of sponsorship).
* **Supporting Documents**: Enter any documents that support your relationship (e.g., marriage certificate, sponsorship letter).
* **Spousal Status**: Select the status of your spousal (Active or Inactive).
* **Spousal Sponsorship Information**: Enter any information about your spousal sponsorship (e.g., dates of sponsorship, names of dependents).
* **Spousal Marriage Certificate**: Enter information about your marriage certificate (e.g., date of marriage, location of marriage).
* **Spousal Sponsorship Letter**: Enter information about your spousal sponsorship letter (e.g., date of sponsorship, purpose of sponsorship).
* **Spousal Sponsorship Declaration**: Enter a brief declaration about your spousal sponsorship (e.g., statement of intent to sponsor).
* **Supporting Documents 2**: Enter any additional supporting documents (e.g., birth certificates, identification cards).
