# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template used to establish a formal agreement between the employee and the organization regarding laptop usage. It covers essential points such as employee information, agreement terms, and signatures. By filling out this form, employees will understand their responsibilities and obligations related to the use of company-provided laptops.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* `employee_id`: Enter your employee ID number.
	* `employee_name`: Enter your full name.
	* `employee_phone`: Enter your work phone number.
	* `employee_email`: Enter your work email address.
	* `employee_work_location`: Enter your work location.
	* `employee_work_phone`: Enter your work phone number.
	* `employee_work_email`: Enter your work email address.
	* `employee_supervisor`: Enter your supervisor's name.
	* `employee_supervisor_phone`: Enter your supervisor's phone number.
	* `employee_supervisor_email`: Enter your supervisor's email address.
2. Select the relevant options:
	* `is_responsible`: Check if you are responsible for the laptop.
	* `it_department`: Enter the name of the IT department contact.
	* `department_manager`: Enter the name of your department manager.
3. Fill in the date fields:
	* `start_date`: Enter the date you started using the laptop.
	* `end_date`: Enter the expected date of returning the laptop.
4. Fill in the other fields:
	* `total_laptop_days`: Enter the total number of days you plan to use the laptop.
	* `total_laptop_hours`: Enter the total number of hours you plan to use the laptop.
	* `agreement_terms`: Enter any additional agreement terms or conditions.
	* `date_signed`: Enter the date you signed the agreement.
	* `agreement_signed_by`: Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
