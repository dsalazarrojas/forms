# Confidentiality Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare your understanding and agreement to maintain confidentiality of sensitive information. It's an essential document for employees who handle confidential data or work with sensitive projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, answering all the questions truthfully.
2. Review each field to ensure you understand its purpose and the implications of your answers.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Legal Name** (`declarant_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide your contact email address for any follow-up information or communications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for any queries or updates.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full address for contact or delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter your city for address or location purposes.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state or province for address purposes.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your postal code for address purposes.
* **Company Name** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name as per your official employment documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your official job title as per your employment contract.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to, if any (not required).
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID or staff number, if any (not required).
* **Declaration Type** (`declaration_type`, select_one, required): Select the purpose of the declaration:
	+ New Employee Onboarding: For new employees to understand their responsibilities.
	+ Project-Specific: For project-related declarations.
	+ Annual Renewal: For annual renewal of confidentiality agreements.
	+ Promotion or Transfer: For promotion or transfer-related declarations.
	+ Contractor Onboarding: For contractors to understand their responsibilities.
	+ Other: For other purposes.
* **Categories of Confidential Information** (`information_categories`, select_multiple, required): Select the categories of confidential information you'll access:
	+ Trade Secrets
	+ Customer Data
	+ Financial Information
	+ Product Designs
	+ Marketing Strategies
	+ Source Code
	+ Business Plans
	+ HR Records
	+ Legal Documents
	+ Other: Specify other categories if not listed.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`, select_one, required): Select the clearance level you require:
	+ Public
	+ Internal
	+ Confidential
	+ Highly Confidential
	+ Restricted
* **Declaration Date** (`declaration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this declaration.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`policy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm if you've read the policies:
	+ Yes Read and Understood
	+ Yes But Need Clarification
	+ No Not Yet Read
* **Confidentiality Training** (`training_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm if you've completed the training:
	+ Yes Completed
	+ In Progress
	+ Not Started
* **Training Completion Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of training completion, if completed.
* **Obligations Understood** (`obligations_understood`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of your duties:
	+ Yes Fully Understood
	+ Partially Understood
	+ Need More Information
* **Key Obligations** (`key_obligations`, text, required): Summarize your duties and responsibilities.
* **Breach Consequences Understood** (`breach_consequences`, select_one, required): Confirm your understanding of breach consequences:
	+ Yes Fully Understood
	+ Partially Understood
	+ Need Clarification
* **Prior Confidentiality Agreements** (`prior_agreements`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have other NDAs:
	+ Yes None Conflict
	+ Yes May Conflict
	+ No Prior Agreements
* **Prior Agreement Details** (`prior_agreement_details`, text, optional): Describe any prior agreements, if applicable.
* **Outside Activities Disclosure** (`outside_activities`, text, optional): Describe any outside activities that may conflict with this NDA.
* **Conflict of Interest** (`conflict_of_interest`, select_one, required): Confirm if you have any conflicts of interest:
	+ No Conflicts
	+ Potential Conflict
	+ Yes Have Conflict
* **Conflict Details** (`conflict_details`, text, optional): Describe any conflicts of interest, if applicable.
* **Any Questions** (`questions`, text, optional): Mention any questions or concerns you have.
* **Declaration Statement** (`declaration_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm your declaration:
	+ I Declare and Affirm
	+ I Cannot Declare
* **Commitment Statement** (`commitment_statement`, select_one, required): Confirm your commitment:
	+ I Commit to Confidentiality
	+ I Cannot Commit
* **Date of Declaration** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of this declaration.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter your supervisor or manager's name.
* **Supervisor Approval** (`supervisor_approval`, select_one, required): Confirm your supervisor's approval:
	+ Approved
	+ Not Approved
	+ Pending
