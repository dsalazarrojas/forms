# Severance Settlement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help employees and managers record the details of a severance settlement, including general information, severance package, employee and manager signatures, date, and other relevant company details. This form is intended to streamline the process of documenting the settlement and ensure that all necessary information is collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with your employee and company details.
2. Describe the severance package offered to the employee.
3. Sign the form with your employee and manager signatures.
4. Provide the date of the settlement.
5. Enter your employee name, manager name, and manager title.
6. Enter the department, company, employee address, manager address, manager contact, manager phone, and manager email.
7. Enter the company address, company employees, company phone, company email, and company title.
8. Finally, enter the company manager and company designation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
- **General Information** (`general_information`, text, required: false): Enter your company's general information, such as your employee name, manager name, and other relevant details.

### Severance Package
- **Severance Package** (`severance_package`, text, required: false): Describe the severance package offered to the employee, including details such as compensation, benefits, and any other relevant information.

### Employee Signature
- **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form with your employee signature to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the severance package.

### Manager Signature
- **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required: false): Sign the form with your manager signature to confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the severance package.
