# Moot Court Brief Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moot Court Brief Upload Form is a template to submit or upload a Moot Court Brief to a Moot Court. It is used to collect information about the brief, including its type, title, author, and date of the Moot Court.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the Moot Court Brief's Title.
2. Choose the Brief Type from the options provided.
3. Enter the Brief Author's name.
4. Enter the Moot Court Name.
5. Select the Moot Court Date.
6. (Optional) Upload a file related to the brief.
7. Provide your Email address.
8. (Optional) Provide your Phone number.
9. Click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Moot Court Brief**: Enter the title of the Moot Court Brief you are submitting.
* **Brief Type**: Choose the type of the Moot Court Brief from the provided options.
* **Brief Title**: Enter the title of the Moot Court Brief you are submitting.
* **Brief Author**: Enter the author of the Moot Court Brief.
* **Moot Court Name**: Enter the name of the Moot Court.
* **Moot Court Date**: Enter the date of the Moot Court.
* **File Upload**: If required, upload a file related to the brief. (Note: This field is not marked as required, so you can choose not to upload a file.)
* **Email**: Provide your email address. (Note: This field is not marked as required, so you can choose not to provide it.)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Note: This field is not marked as required, so you can choose not to provide it.)
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your Moot Court Brief.
