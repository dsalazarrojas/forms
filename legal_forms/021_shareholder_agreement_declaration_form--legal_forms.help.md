# Shareholder Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shareholder Agreement Declaration Form is used to collect information about shareholder agreements between a shareholder and a company. This form helps to document and clarify the terms of the agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the name of the company involved in the agreement.
2. Enter the name of the shareholder involved in the agreement.
3. Enter the percentage of ownership held by the shareholder.
4. Indicate whether the shareholder has voting rights.
5. Indicate the distribution of profits among shareholders.
6. Indicate whether the shareholder is entitled to exit proceeds.
7. Enter any other clauses that are part of the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Name (id: 2)
* Company Name (`company_name`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for entering the name of the company involved in the agreement.
### Shareholder Name (id: 3)
* Shareholder Name (`shareholder_name`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ This field is for entering the name of the shareholder involved in the agreement.
### Percentage of Ownership (id: 4)
* Percentage of Ownership (`percentage_of_ownership`, `number`, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter the percentage of ownership held by the shareholder.
### Voting Rights (id: 5)
* Voting Rights (`voting_rights`, `select_one`, required/optional: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if the shareholder has voting rights, "No" otherwise.
### Profit Distribution (id: 6)
* Profit Distribution (`profit_distribution`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: false)
	+ Select all options that apply regarding profit distribution among shareholders.
### Exit Proceeds (id: 7)
* Exit Proceeds (`exit_proceeds`, `select_one`, required/optional: false)
	+ Select "Yes" if the shareholder is entitled to exit proceeds, "No" otherwise.
### Other Clauses (id: 8)
* Other Clauses (`other_clauses`, `text`, required/optional: false)
	+ Enter any other clauses that are part of the agreement.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If a field is not applicable, mark it as "No" or leave it blank.
* Review the form carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
