# Pet Adoption Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Adoption Agreement Form is used to formalize the adoption process between a pet shelter or rescue organization and a prospective pet owner. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the pet is adopted responsibly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and answer all the required questions.
2. Provide accurate and detailed information about the pet and yourself.
3. Sign the agreement to confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Information (Page 1)
* **Pet Details (pet_adoption_agreement_form_1)** (`Pet Details`, `note`, **required**: **false**): This page is a note section for the pet's details. Please provide any relevant information about the pet, such as its name, breed, age, and any medical conditions.

### Pet Information (Page 2)
* **Pet Name (pet_information)** (`Pet Name`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter the name of the pet you are interested in adopting.
* **Pet Type (pet_information)** (`Pet Type`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter the type of pet (e.g., cat, dog, etc.).
* **Age (pet_information)** (`Age`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter the age of the pet.
* **Health Issues (pet_information)** (`Health Issues`, `text`, **required**: **false**): If the pet has any health issues, please describe them here.

### Owner Information (Page 3)
* **Owner Name (owner_information)** (`Owner Name`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter your name and surname.
* **Address (owner_information)** (`Address`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter your address.
* **Contact Number (owner_information)** (`Contact Number`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter your contact number.
* **Email (owner_information)** (`Email`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Enter your email address.

### Agreement Details (Page 4)
* **Agreement Details (agreement_details)** (`Agreement Details`, `text`, **required**: **false**): Describe the terms of the adoption agreement, including your understanding of the pet's care and any specific needs or requirements.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask the pet shelter or rescue organization for assistance.
* This form is a legally binding agreement. Please review it carefully before signing.
