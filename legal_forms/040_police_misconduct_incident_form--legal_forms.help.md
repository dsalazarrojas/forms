# Police Misconduct Incident Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Police Misconduct Incident Form is a tool used to document and report incidents of police misconduct. It is intended for use by police departments and law enforcement agencies to gather information about incidents that have occurred involving their officers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, including all required fields.
2. Provide as much detail as possible about the incident, including dates, locations, and any witnesses or evidence collected.
3. If you are reporting an incident involving a police officer, please provide the officer's name, badge number, and department.
4. If you are a police officer, please provide your own information and status as well.
5. Once you have completed the form, review it carefully to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
6. Submit the form to the designated authority for review and investigation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Incident Report** (`incident_report`):
	+ A brief summary of the incident, including dates, locations, and details.
* **Date Of Incident** (`date_of_incident`):
	+ The date on which the incident occurred.
* **Location Of Incident** (`location_of_incident`):
	+ The location where the incident took place.
* **Police Officer Name** (`police_officer_name`):
	+ The name of the police officer involved in the incident.
* **Description Of Incident** (`description_of_incident`):
	+ A detailed description of what happened during the incident.
* **Witness Statement** (`witness_statement`):
	+ Any statements or information provided by witnesses to the incident.
* **Officer ID Number** (`officer_id_number`):
	+ The badge or identification number of the police officer involved.
* **Police Department** (`police_department`):
	+ The department or agency of the police officer involved.
* **Date Officer Hired** (`date_officer_hired`):
	+ The date the police officer was hired.
* **Date Officer Terminated** (`date_officer_terminated`):
	+ The date the police officer was terminated (if applicable).
* **Officer Status** (`officer_status`):
	+ A field to select the status of the police officer (Active or Inactive).
* **Reporting Officer Name** (`reporting_officer_name`):
	+ The name of the officer or person submitting the report.
* **Reporting Officer Title** (`reporting_officer_title`):
	+ The title of the officer or person submitting the report.
* **Reporting Officer Agency** (`reporting_officer_agency`):
	+ The agency or department of the officer or person submitting the report.
* **Reporting Officer Contact** (`reporting_officer_contact`):
	+ Contact information for the officer or person submitting the report.
* **Reporting Officer Email** (`reporting_officer_email`):
	+ Email address of the officer or person submitting the report.
* **Date Report Submitted** (`date_report_submitted`):
	+ The date the report was submitted.
* **Incident Status** (`incident_status`):
	+ A field to select the status of the incident (Active or Inactive).
* **Case Number** (`case_number`):
	+ A unique identifier for the case or incident.
* **Incident Location** (`incident_location`):
	+ The location where the incident took place.
* **Officer Name** (`officer_name`):
	+ The name of the police officer involved in the incident.
* **Officer Title** (`officer_title`):
	+ The title of the police officer involved.
* **Officer Department** (`officer_department`):
	+ The department or agency of the police officer involved.
* **Officer Status** (`officer_status`):
	+ A field to select the status of the police officer (Active or Inactive).
* **Reporting Unit** (`reporting_unit`):
	+ The unit or section within the police department responsible for submitting the report.

# Tips
* Always ensure that you are reporting a legitimate incident and not duplicating an existing report.
* Provide as much detail as possible about the incident, including dates, locations, and any evidence collected.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any aspect of the form, please contact the designated authority for assistance.
