# Third-Party Legal Collaboration Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a third-party collaboration, which is a partnership or agreement between two or more parties where they share resources, expertise, or other assets to achieve a common goal. This form will help the legal team to understand the terms of the collaboration and what information should be shared.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the third party's name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter the legal entity's name and address if applicable.
3. Enter the client's name and contact information if applicable.
4. Describe the type of information that will be shared between the parties.
5. Enter any other details that are relevant to the collaboration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Collaboration Form** (`legal_collaboration_form`, text, required: false): This field is a text area where you can describe the purpose and terms of the collaboration.
* **Third Party Name** (`third_party_name`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the name of the third party involved in the collaboration.
* **Third Party Address** (`third_party_address`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the address of the third party involved in the collaboration.
* **Legal Entity Name** (`legal_entity_name`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the name of the legal entity involved in the collaboration if applicable.
* **Legal Entity Address** (`legal_entity_address`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the address of the legal entity involved in the collaboration if applicable.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the name of the client involved in the collaboration if applicable.
* **Client Contact Info** (`client_contact_info`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter the contact information of the client involved in the collaboration if applicable.
* **Shared Information** (`shared_information`, text, required: false): This field is where you describe the type of information that will be shared between the parties.
* **Legal Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required: false): This field is where you select the type of information that will be shared, which can be Client Information and documents, Confidential documents, Financial information, or Other.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, required: false): This field is where you enter any other details that are relevant to the collaboration.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure that the collaboration terms are clearly understood.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the type of information that will be shared.
* If you have any other details that are relevant to the collaboration, enter them in the Other Details field.
* Review the Legal Consent field carefully to ensure that you are selecting the correct type of information to be shared.
