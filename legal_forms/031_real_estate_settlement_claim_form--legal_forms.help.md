# real_estate_settlement_claim_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "real_estate_settlement_claim_form" is used to gather details for a real estate settlement claim. It helps to record and track information related to a claim, including property address, claimant and witness details, payment terms, and signature verification.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by providing your property address in the "property_address" field.
- Indicate your claim status as "Yes" or "No" in the "claimant_info" field.
- If applicable, provide your relationship with the settlement company in the "settlement_company" field.
- Specify whether the property is of a specific type (e.g., "Yes" for a specific type) in the "property_type" field.
- Provide detailed information about your claim in the "claim_details" field.
- Indicate the availability of supporting documents in the "supporting_documents" field.
- Sign and date your claim in the "signature" field.
- Enter your email address in the "email_address" field.
- Provide your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
- Specify the date of the settlement in the "date_of_settlement" field.
- Mention the time of the settlement in the "time_of_settlement" field.
- Enter the amount paid in the "amount_paid" field.
- Indicate whether the payment method was "Yes" or "No" in the "payment_method" field.
- Explain any payment terms in the "payment_terms" field.
- Choose the current status of your claim as "Active" or "Inactive" in the "claim_status" field.
- If necessary, add any additional notes in the "notes" field.
- Sign and date your claim with your name in the "signature_name" field.
- If applicable, provide the name of a witness in the "witness_name" field.
- Specify the title of the witness in the "witness_title" field.
- Enter the witness address in the "witness_address" field.
- Provide the witness phone number in the "witness_phone_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### property_address
* **property_address** (text, required): Enter the address of the property related to the claim.

### claimant_info
* **claimant_info** (select_one, required): Indicate your relationship with the claim.

### settlement_company
* **settlement_company** (text, optional): If applicable, provide your relationship with the settlement company.

### property_type
* **property_type** (select_multiple, required): Specify whether the property is of a specific type (e.g., "Yes" for a specific type).

### claim_details
* **claim_details** (text, required): Provide detailed information about your claim.

### supporting_documents
* **supporting_documents** (select_multiple, required): Indicate the availability of supporting documents.

### signature
* **signature** (note, required): Sign and date your claim.

### email_address
* **email_address** (email, required): Enter your email address.

### phone_number
* **phone_number** (text, required): Provide your phone number.

### date_of_settlement
* **date_of_settlement** (date, required): Specify the date of the settlement.

### time_of_settlement
* **time_of_settlement** (time, required): Mention the time of the settlement.

### amount_paid
* **amount_paid** (number, required): Enter the amount paid.

### payment_method
* **payment_method** (select_one, required): Indicate whether the payment method was "Yes" or "No".

### payment_terms
* **payment_terms** (text, required): Explain any payment terms.

### claim_status
* **claim_status** (select_one, required): Choose the current status of your claim as "Active" or "Inactive".

### notes
* **notes** (text, optional): Provide any additional notes if necessary.

### signature_name
* **signature_name** (text, required): Sign and date your claim with your name.

### witness_name
* **witness_name** (text, optional): If applicable, provide the name of a witness.

### witness_title
* **witness_title** (text, optional): Specify the title of the witness.

### witness_address
* **witness_address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the witness.

### witness_phone_number
* **witness_phone_number** (text, optional): Provide the phone number of the witness.

### signature_date
* **signature_date** (date, optional): Sign and date your claim if necessary.

### signature_time
* **signature_time** (time, optional): Enter the time of your signature if necessary.

### signature_name
* **signature_name** (text, required): Your name for signature verification.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure the form is correctly submitted.
* Double-check your information for any errors before signing and submitting.
* Ensure you have supporting documents ready for the claimant.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the form instructions or contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
