# Cybersecurity Non Disclosure Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legally binding agreement that confirms an employee's understanding of the company's cybersecurity policies and procedures for handling sensitive information. It is essential for employees who work with confidential data to sign this form to demonstrate their commitment to maintaining confidentiality and data protection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to sign the form in the presence of a witness or supervisor.
3. Review the form carefully before signing to ensure you understand the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`form_header`, text, required: false): This field is for internal purposes and not required to be filled out.
* **Full Name** (`name`, text, required: true): Your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: true): Your job title within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: true): The department you belong to or the team you're part of within the company.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required: true): The date you sign this form, indicating your understanding and agreement to the non-disclosure agreement.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, required: true): Your employee ID number or other unique identifier assigned to you by the company.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required: true): The name of the company you are employed by.
* **Non Disclosure Agreement** (`non_disclosure_agreement`, note, required: true): This is a statement that you are aware of and agree to the company's non-disclosure agreement. Please read and sign this carefully before continuing.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, checkbox, required: true): You need to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of the non-disclosure agreement. Please select "Yes" if you have read and understand the terms.

## Tips
* Make sure to carefully read and understand the non-disclosure agreement before signing the form.
* If you are unsure about any part of the agreement, ask your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records to demonstrate your understanding and commitment to the non-disclosure agreement.
