# Legislative Proposal Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legislative Proposal Submission Form is a tool used to gather information for proposals submitted to the legislative department. It captures key details such as user identification, proposal title, and status of the proposal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Name and User Email address for identification purposes.
2. Enter a clear and concise title for your proposal.
3. Describe the details of your proposal in the text box provided.
4. Specify the status of your proposal (Draft, Submitted, or Approved).
5. Add any additional comments about your proposal if necessary.
6. Select the department related to your proposal.
7. Enter the location where the proposal was made or should be implemented.
8. Enter the budget allocated or required for the proposal.
9. Attach any supporting files or documents to your proposal.
10. Enter the time and date you submitted your proposal.
11. Provide your contact phone number.
12. Enter your contact email address.
13. Enter your name and title for official contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name for identification purposes.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for identification and contact purposes.
* **Proposal Title** (`proposal_title`, Text, Optional): Enter a clear and concise title for your proposal.
* **Proposal Details** (`proposal_details`, Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your proposal.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you submitted your proposal.
* **Proposal Status** (`proposal_status`, Select One, Optional): Choose the status of your proposal from the available options (Draft, Submitted, Approved).
* **Comments** (`comments`, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your proposal.
* **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Select the department related to your proposal.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Optional): Enter the location where the proposal was made or should be implemented.
* **Budget** (`budget`, Text, Optional): Enter the budget allocated or required for the proposal.
* **Attachments** (`attachments`, Text, Optional): Attach any supporting files or documents to your proposal.
* **Submission Date/Time** (`submission_date_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you submitted your proposal.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address for official contact purposes.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and title for official contact purposes.

## Tips
- Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays or miscommunication.
- Use clear and concise language to describe your proposal.
- Attach relevant files or documents to support your proposal.
- Check the status of your proposal regularly for updates.
