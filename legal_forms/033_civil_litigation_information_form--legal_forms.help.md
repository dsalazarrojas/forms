# Civil Litigation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals involved in civil litigation cases. It helps guide the legal process by providing a comprehensive overview of the case details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's name and contact details.
2. If applicable, provide the opposing party's name.
3. Give a brief description of the case, including the timeline.
4. Specify the relief or remedy sought.
5. List any relevant documents available.
6. Mention any important deadlines or limitation periods.
7. Indicate the current legal representation status.
7. Add any confidentiality and privacy notes.
8. Finally, select the submission date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client name**: Full legal name of the person involved in the civil litigation case.
- **Contact details**: Email and phone number of the person or their representative.
- **Opposing party name** (If known): The name of the party against whom the litigation is being pursued.
- **Case summary**: A brief description of the issue and timeline of the case.
- **Relief or remedy sought**: Specifics of what the person is seeking in the lawsuit.
- **Documents available**: List any relevant contracts, correspondence, or other evidence.
- **Important deadlines or limitation periods**: Dates for statute of limitations or filings.
- **Current legal representation**: Represented by counsel or self-represented.
    - Represented: The person is represented by an attorney.
    - Self-represented: The person is representing themselves.
    - Seeking representation: The person is looking for representation.
- **Confidentiality and privacy note**: A note about any sensitive information that should be handled with care.
- **Submission date**: The date the form is being submitted.
  (Note: This is the date of intake, not the date of service.) 

This form is designed to be user-friendly and adaptable to your specific situation. Please fill it out thoroughly and accurately to ensure the best possible outcome.
