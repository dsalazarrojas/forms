# Parent Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parent Agreement Form is a document that captures essential information from parents or guardians regarding their child's care and services provided by a care provider. This form helps to establish a clear understanding between the care provider and the parent regarding the care services, payment terms, and emergency procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Choose the relevant options for each section based on your circumstances.
3. Provide the required signatures at the end of the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Parent Information** (Parent's Name, type: select_multiple): Select one or more of the options to identify yourself as the parent or guardian.
- **Caregiver Information** (Caregiver's Name, type: select_multiple): Select one or more of the options to identify the person or people providing care to your child.
- **Emergency Procedures** (Emergency Procedure, type: text): In the event of an emergency, provide a brief description of the emergency procedure that will be followed.
- **Daily Schedule** (Daily Schedule, type: time): Provide your child's daily schedule, including any regular activities or routines.
- **Payment Terms** (Payment Method, type: select_one): Choose the method of payment you will use to pay for the care services.
  - **Payment Method 1**
  - **Payment Method 2**
- **Billing Schedules** (Billing Schedule, type: select_one): Choose the schedule for billing and payment of fees.
  - **Schedule 1**
  - **Schedule 2**
- **Additional Fees** (Fee, type: number): Enter any additional fees or charges.
- **Parent Signature** (Parent Signature, type: text): Sign this section to confirm your agreement.
- **Caregiver Signature** (Caregiver Signature, type: text): Sign this section to confirm your agreement.
- **Date** (Date, type: date): Enter the date of this agreement.
- **Time** (Time, type: time): Enter the time of this agreement.
- **Email** (Email, type: email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (Phone, type: text): Enter your contact phone number for emergency situations.
- **Emergency Name** (Emergency Name, type: text): Identify the person to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Phone** (Emergency Phone, type: text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Address** (Emergency Address, type: text): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Relationship** (Emergency Relationship, type: text): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Procedure** (Emergency Procedure, type: text): In the event of an emergency, describe the procedure that will be followed.
- **Payment Method** (Payment Method, type: text): Enter your preferred payment method.
- **Billing Method** (Billing Method, type: text): Enter your billing method.
- **Payment Term** (Payment Term, type: text): Enter the payment term agreed upon.
- **Billing Schedule** (Billing Schedule, type: text): Enter the billing schedule agreed upon.
- **Fee** (Fee, type: text): Enter any additional fees or charges.
- **Fee Schedule** (Fee Schedule, type: text): Enter the fee schedule agreed upon.
- **Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, type: text): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of this agreement.
