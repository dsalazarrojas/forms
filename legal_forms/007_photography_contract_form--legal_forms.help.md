# Photography Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to create a photography contract between the photographer and the client, outlining the essential details of the photo shoot, payment, and other relevant information.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your client's name in the Client Name field.
2. Provide your email address in the Email field.
3. Specify the location of the photo shoot.
4. Choose a date and time for the shoot.
5. Select the payment method (Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card, or PayPal).
6. Enter the amount due for the photo shoot.
7. Provide any special payment terms (if applicable).
8. Specify the coverage terms (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name (text)**: Enter your name as the client.
* **Email (email)**: Provide your email address for communication.
* **Location (text)**: Specify the location of the photo shoot.
* **Date (date)**: Choose a date for the photo shoot.
* **Time (time)**: Choose a time for the photo shoot.
* **Payment Method (select one)**: Select the method of payment you prefer (Bank Transfer, Check, Credit Card, or PayPal).
* **Amount (number)**: Enter the amount due for the photo shoot.
* **Payment Terms (text)**: Provide any specific payment terms, if applicable.
* **Coverage Terms (text)**: Specify any coverage terms, if applicable.
