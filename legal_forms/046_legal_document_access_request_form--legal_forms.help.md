## # Legal Document Access Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Document Access Request Form is used to request access to legal documents. To complete this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number (if applicable).
2. Select the type of document you are requesting access to (Document Type).
3. Enter your Date of Birth and Date of Request.
4. Choose a category from the list (Category).
5. If required, enter your Organization and Document Number.
6. Enter the name of the party requesting access (Requesting Party).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`legal_document_access_request_form_first_name`, text, required)
Your first name is the first part of your name that identifies you. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name (`legal_document_access_request_form_last_name`, text, required)
Your last name is the last part of your name that identifies you. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (`legal_document_access_request_form_email`, email, required)
Your email address is where you can be contacted for further information about your request. Please enter your email address as it appears on your identification documents or the email address you prefer to be contacted at.

### Phone Number (`legal_document_access_request_form_phone`, text, optional)
If you have a phone number, please enter it to ensure that we can contact you for further information about your request.

### Document Type (`legal_document_access_request_form_document_type`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the types of documents you are requesting access to. You can select more than one type of document if necessary.

### Date of Birth (`legal_document_access_request_form_date_of_birth`, date, required)
Enter your Date of Birth as it appears on your identification documents.

### Date of Request (`legal_document_access_request_form_date_of_request`, date, required)
Enter the date on which you are requesting access to the document.

### Category (`legal_document_access_request_form_category`, select_one, required)
Choose a category from the list to ensure that your request is handled by the correct department.

### Organization (`legal_document_access_request_form_organization`, text, optional)
If you are requesting access on behalf of an organization, please enter the name of the organization.

### Document Number (`legal_document_access_request_form_document_number`, text, required)
Enter the document number as it appears on your identification documents.

### Requesting Party (`legal_document_access_request_form_requesting_party`, text, required)
Enter the name of the party or person requesting access to the document.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid delays in processing your request.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact us at the email address provided.
