# Playwright Script Reproduction Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to document the reproduction of a Playwright script for legal and administrative purposes. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that all necessary details are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, making sure to enter your first name, last name, and email address accurately.
2. Provide your phone number, production type, performance location, and performance date.
3. Select the correct reproduction type and medium from the available options.
4. Confirm whether reproduction permission is granted or not.
5. Specify the purpose of the reproduction and production organisations involved.
6. Add any additional notes or comments if necessary.
7. Enter the details of the production organisation, reproduction location, and any other relevant details.
8. Confirm that the agreement has been signed and signed by whom.
9. Enter the date of signing and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 1`, `name: first_name`, `required: true`): Enter your full first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`, `name: last_name`, `required: true`): Enter your full last name in this field.
* **Email** (`id: 3`, `name: email`, `required: true`): Enter a valid email address associated with your identity.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 4`, `name: phone`, `required: true`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Production Type** (`id: 5`, `name: production_type`, `required: true`): Select the type of production (e.g., School, Theatre, Production Company, Other).
* **Performance Location** (`id: 6`, `name: performance_location`, `required: true`): Enter the location where the performance took place.
* **Performance Date** (`id: 7`, `name: performance_date`, `required: true`): Enter the date of the performance.
* **Performance Time** (`id: 8`, `name: performance_time`, `required: true`): Enter the time of the performance.
* **Performance Duration** (`id: 9`, `name: performance_duration`, `required: true`): Enter the duration of the performance in hours.
* **Script Version** (`id: 10`, `name: script_version`, `required: true`): Enter the version number of the script.
* **Reproduction Type** (`id: 11`, `name: reproduction_type`, `required: true`): Select the type of reproduction (e.g., Stage, Film, Television, Radio, Digital, Other).
* **Reproduction Medium** (`id: 12`, `name: reproduction_medium`, `required: true`): Select the medium of reproduction (e.g., Live, Video, Audio, Digital, Other).
* **Reproduction Permission** (`id: 13`, `name: reproduction_permission`, `required: true`): Confirm whether reproduction permission is granted.
* **Reproduction Purpose** (`id: 14`, `name: reproduction_purpose`, `required: true`): Specify the purpose of the reproduction.
* **Production Organisations** (`id: 15`, `name: production_organisations`, `required: true`): Select the production organisations involved (e.g., School, Theatre, Production Company, Other).
* **Script Reproduction Request** (`id: 16`, `name: script_reproduction_request`, `required: true`): Add any additional comments or requests regarding script reproduction.
* **Notes** (`id: 17`, `name: notes`, `required: true`): Add any other notes or comments.
* **Submitted By** (`id: 18`, `name: submitted_by`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At** (`id: 19`, `name: submitted_at`, `required: true`): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Production Organisations Details** (`id: 20`, `name: production_organisations_details`, `required: true`): Enter any additional details about the production organisation.
* **Reproduction Location** (`id: 21`, `name: reproduction_location`, `required: true`): Enter the location of the reproduction.
* **Reproduction Details** (`id: 22`, `name: reproduction_details`, `required: true`): Enter any other details about the reproduction.
* **Agreement Signed** (`id: 23`, `name: agreement_signed`, `required: true`): Confirm that an agreement has been signed.
* **Agreement Signed By** (`id: 24`, `name: agreement_signed_by`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed Date** (`id: 25`, `name: agreement_signed_date`, `required: true`): Enter the date of signing.
