# Service Agreement Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for documenting a service agreement between a service provider and a client. It captures the essential details of the agreement, including the client's and service provider's information, services offered, and terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the service provider's name and address in the corresponding fields.
2. Indicate the services to be provided (Yes or No).
3. List the specific services agreed upon (select Multiple).
4. Agree or disagree with the listed terms (Yes or No).
5. Provide your signature and the date and time of signing for both yourself and the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Service Agreement Template** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the service agreement.
* **Client Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the client.
* **Service Provider's Name** (`text`): Enter the name of the service provider.
* **Services** (`select_one`): Indicate if the service provider offers services (Yes or No).
* **Services** (`select_multiple`): List the specific services agreed upon by the service provider.
* **Service Provider's Address** (`text`): Enter the address of the service provider.
* **Agreed Services** (`select_multiple`): List the specific services agreed upon by the client.
* **Terms** (`text`): Enter a brief description of the terms and conditions of the service.
* **Agreed Terms** (`select_multiple`): Agree or disagree with the listed terms (Yes or No).
* **Signature Date** (`date`): Enter the date of signing.
* **Signature Time** (`time`): Enter the time of signing.
* **Signature** (`note`): Sign the agreement by printing or typing your name.
* **Client Signature Date** (`date`): Enter the date of the client's signature.
* **Client Signature Time** (`time`): Enter the time of the client's signature.
* **Client Signature** (`note`): Sign the agreement by printing or typing your name as the client.
