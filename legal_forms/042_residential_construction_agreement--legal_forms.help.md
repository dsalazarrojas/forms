# Residential Construction Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a construction project, including project details and participant details, ensuring a smooth and secure construction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter project details in the **Project Details** section.
2. Provide **Payment Terms** as per the project requirements.
3. Fill out **Owner Details** as requested.
4. Complete **Contractor Details** as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Details**: This section is used to gather essential project information, including the project's name, description, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Terms**: Enter the payment terms and conditions, including payment methods and schedules, to ensure both parties are on the same page.
* **Owner Details**: This is where you'll enter your information as the owner of the property, including your name, contact details, and any relevant documentation.
* **Contractor Details**: Complete this section with your contractor's information, including name, contact details, and any relevant licenses or qualifications.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed as accurately as possible.
* Double-check the form before submission to avoid any errors.
* Review the form carefully and ensure it suits the specific needs of your project.
