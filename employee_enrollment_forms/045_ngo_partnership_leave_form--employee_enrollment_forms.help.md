# NGO Partnership Leave Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by employees to request or report time off related to an NGO partnership. This could include activities such as attending partnership events, meetings, or other activities that require their presence.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and other requested information.
2. Select the correct start and end dates for your leave.
3. Provide a reason for your leave (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_enrollment_form**: This is the title of the form, and it is not a field that you need to fill out.
* **start_date**: Enter the date you will be leaving on.
* **end_date**: Enter the date you will be returning to work.
* **reason**: This field is optional, but it helps us understand your reason for taking time off. If you choose not to fill it out, it's okay!

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR department or the relevant team members.
* You can always ask a colleague or your manager for help if you need to fill out the form.
* Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to make sure everything is correct.
* If you are requesting time off for a reason not listed in the "reason" field, you can simply type it in and we will do our best to accommodate your request.
* Please be mindful of your work schedule and try to provide as much notice as possible when requesting time off.
