# Talent Development Program Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Development Program Request Form is a document that captures the necessary details for requesting enrollment in the Talent Development Program. This form is used to ensure that employees have a clear and structured way to request training and development opportunities that align with their interests and skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the relevant department from the list of options provided in the "Employee Enrollment Form" page.
2. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
3. Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
4. Provide a brief description of the training proposals in the "Training Proposals" field.
5. Select the type of training desired from the options provided in the "Training Type" field.
6. Choose the frequency of the training from the options provided in the "Training Frequency" field.
7. Enter the expected duration of the training in the "Training Duration" field.
8. Select the start and end dates of the training in the "Training Start Date" and "Training End Date" fields, respectively.
9. Enter the department where the training will take place.
10. (Optional) Enter the location where the training will be held.
11. Select the current status of the training from the options provided in the "Training Status" field.
12. Add any additional remarks in the "Remarks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Enrollment Form** (`employee_enrollment_form`, select_multiple, required): Select the department you belong to for which you're requesting training.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your official company email address.
* **Training Proposals** (`training_proposals`, note, required): Provide a brief description of the training you're requesting.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of training you're looking for (e.g., Classroom, On the Job Training, E-learning, Online Training, Instructor Led Training).
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you'd like to have the training (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Annually, Annualy).
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required): Enter the number of days the training will last.
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, required): Select the date when the training will start.
* **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, required): Select the date when the training will end.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department that will be responsible for the training.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the training will take place (if applicable).
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of the training (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Cancelled).
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes regarding the training request.
