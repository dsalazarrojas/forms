# Voluntary Life Insurance Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voluntary Life Insurance plan provides employees with additional coverage in the event of death. By enrolling in this plan, employees can choose the coverage they need and select the beneficiaries they want to protect.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's number.
2. Fill in the first and last name fields as per the employee's record.
3. Select the plan selection(s) the employee wants to enroll in.
4. Choose the relationship type for the beneficiary (e.g., Parent, Child, Spouse).
5. Enter the name of the beneficiary.
6. Enter the date of birth of the beneficiary.
7. Enter the Social Security Number of the beneficiary.
8. Enter the date of birth of the beneficiary.
9. Enter the Social Security Number of the beneficiary.
10. Enter the percentage of coverage the employee wants.
11. Enter the premium amount the employee wants to pay.
12. Enter the plan effective date.
13. Enter the plan end date.
14. Enter any additional notes about the enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Number (1)** (`employee_number`, text, optional): Enter the employee's ID number, if you do not know it, you can leave this field blank but it will not be able to be saved.
* **First Name (2)** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the employee as per their record.
* **Last Name (3)** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the employee as per their record.
* **Plan Selections (4)** (`plan_selections`, select multiple, required): Select the coverage options you want to enroll in.
* **Relationship (5)** (`relationship`, select one, optional): Choose the relationship type for the beneficiary (e.g., Parent, Child, Spouse).
* **Beneficiary Name (6)** (`beneficiary_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the beneficiary.
* **Date of Birth (7)** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the beneficiary.
* **Social Security Number (8)** (`social_security_number`, text, required): Enter the Social Security Number of the beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary Date of Birth (9)** (`beneficiary_dob`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the beneficiary.
* **Beneficiary Social Security Number (10)** (`beneficiary_ssn`, text, required): Enter the Social Security Number of the beneficiary.
* **Percentage (11)** (`percentage`, number, required): Enter the percentage of coverage the employee wants.
* **Premium (12)** (`premium`, number, required): Enter the premium amount the employee wants to pay.
* **Plan Effective Date (13)** (`plan_effective_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the plan takes effect.
* **Plan End Date (14)** (`plan_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the plan ends.
* **Notes (15)** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the enrollment.
