# Temporary Employee Payroll Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and process payroll information for temporary employees to ensure accurate tax calculation and compensation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each section with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field for any errors or inconsistencies before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire** (`new_hire`, `text`, required: false): This field is used for entering the name of the new temporary employee.
* **Tax Information** (`tax_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant tax details for this employee, if applicable.
* **Wages** (`wages`, `text`, required: false): Enter the wage details for this temporary employee. This field is crucial for accurate compensation calculation.
* **Tax Rate** (`tax_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter the tax rate applicable for this employee's tax calculation.
* **Pay Rate** (`pay_rate`, `number`, required: false): Enter the pay rate for this temporary employee, which affects their compensation.
