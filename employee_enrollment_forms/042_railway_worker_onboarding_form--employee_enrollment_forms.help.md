# Railway Worker Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Railway Worker Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information about new railway workers during the onboarding process. This form helps the organization to understand the worker's personal details, work experience, and other relevant background information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth, which is required for administrative purposes.
3. If you have a pre-existing emergency contact, please provide their name and phone number.
4. Indicate whether you have any railway experience and if you have a railway license.
5. If you have any medical conditions, please note them in the space provided.
6. Sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
7. Take a photo or upload a digital image of your signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, not required): This is the general section for you to provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, not required): This is the name of the individual filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, not required): This is the email address of the individual filling out the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, not required): This is the phone number of the individual filling out the form.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This is the date of birth of the individual.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, not required): This is the name of the individual's emergency contact person.
* **Name of Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, not required): This is the name of the individual's emergency contact person.
* **Phone of Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, not required): This is the phone number of the individual's emergency contact person.
* **Railway Experience** (`railway_experience`, `select_one`, not required): This is a selection between 'Yes' or 'No' indicating if you have any railway experience.
* **Railway License** (`railway_license`, `select_multiple`, not required): This is a selection between 'Yes' or 'No' indicating if you have a railway license.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `note`, not required): This is a free text field for you to note any medical conditions you may have.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, not required): This is the section for you to sign the form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
* **Signature Image** (`signature_image`, `text`, not required): This is the section for you to take a photo or upload a digital image of your signature.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
* The information collected will only be used for internal purposes within the organization.
