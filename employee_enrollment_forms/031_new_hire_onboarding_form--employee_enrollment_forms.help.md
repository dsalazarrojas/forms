# New Hire Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Hire Onboarding Form is designed to collect essential information from new employees during the onboarding process. This form helps the HR team to quickly gather and process relevant data for smooth and efficient onboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Select your Department.
6. Select your Job ID(s).
7. Enter your Hire Date.
8. Enter your Start Date.
9. Enter your End Date.
10. Add any additional Notes about your new role or position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your work email address.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title as per your employee contract.
- **Department** (`department`, select_one, required/optional): Select the department you are part of, e.g. Human Resources, Operations, Finance, etc.
- **Job ID** (`job_title_id`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your job ID(s) as per your employee contract. 
- **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you were hired on.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started working.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your employment is expected to end.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your new role or position.
