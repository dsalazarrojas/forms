# Employee Digital Transformation Fellowship Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect your application and motivation for the Employee Digital Transformation Fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all necessary details and motivation ready before starting.
2. Carefully review each field to provide accurate and complete information.
3. Submit your application once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Form** (`application_form`, text, optional): A text box where you can provide your detailed application, explaining your interest and suitability for the Fellowship.
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): A text box where you can provide your motivation for applying for the Fellowship. Explain why you are the best fit and what you hope to achieve through the program.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): A text box where you can describe your relevant experience that supports your application.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the provided options that best describe your relevant skills or experience. This will help us understand your strengths.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): A text box where you can provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): A text box where you can provide your email address for communication and updates.

## Tips
* Be as clear and concise as possible when filling out each field.
* Proofread your application carefully before submission to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact your HR representative.
