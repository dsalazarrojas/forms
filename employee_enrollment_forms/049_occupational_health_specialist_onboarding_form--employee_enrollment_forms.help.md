# Occupational Health Specialist Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Occupational Health Specialist Onboarding Form is used to collect information for new employees who are joining the company as Occupational Health Specialists. This form is used to gather personal details, job-related information, and administrative information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the required information for each field. If you need help with any field, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your given name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname (e.g., Doe).
* **Prophet's Name** (`prophets_name`, text, optional): This field is optional and typically used for religious or cultural practices.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company email address (e.g., johndoe@company.com).
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Select the department you will be working in (e.g., Occupational Health Specialists).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as an Occupational Health Specialist.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Hire Date** (`hire_date`, date, required): Enter your hire date in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_multiple, required): Select your area of medical expertise (e.g., General Practice, Internal Medicine).
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, required): Enter your unique employee ID provided by the company.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your home or work address for contact purposes.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Enter your personal or professional goals related to your role.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current job status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select your job type (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract).
* **Job Classification** (`job_classification`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job classification (e.g., Management, Sales, Marketing).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, optional): Enter your termination date if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, contact your HR representative or supervisor.
* This form is intended for informational purposes only and will not be shared outside the company.
