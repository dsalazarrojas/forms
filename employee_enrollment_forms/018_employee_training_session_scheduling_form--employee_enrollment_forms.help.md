# Employee Training Session Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule employee training sessions. It captures essential details such as employee contact information, session details, and preferences. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that we can efficiently process your training session request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the fields with the required information. The form is divided into sections, and each section is described in detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **not required**): Enter the name of the employee filling out the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **not required**): Enter the last name of the employee filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **not required**): Enter the email address of the employee. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, **not required**): Enter the phone number of the employee. This is optional but helps in case we need to contact them.
* **Date and Time** (`date_time`, date, **not required**): Select the date and time when the employee would like to have the training session. Please format the date in the standard `YYYY-MM-DD` format and the time in the `HH:MM` format.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, **not required**): Briefly describe the type of training session the employee is interested in (e.g., "Software Development", "Data Analysis", etc.).
* **Location** (`location`, text, **not required**): Enter the location where the employee would like to have the training session.
* **Training Topic** (`training_topic`, text, **not required**): If you have a specific topic in mind, enter it here. This helps us to tailor the training content to your needs.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, **not required**): Select the duration of the training session in hours and minutes (e.g., "1:30" for 1 hour 30 minutes).
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, **not required**): Select the date of the training session.
* **Additional Information** (no label, text, **not required**): If you have any additional information or comments, please include them here. This helps us to better understand your needs and preferences.

**Tips**
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you're unsure about any field, please feel free to ask your supervisor or the training team for assistance.
