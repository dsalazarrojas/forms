# Network Administrator Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new network administrators to onboard them into the system. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your **Password** in the "Password" field. Note that this field is not secure, and it's recommended to use a secure method to create and store passwords.
6. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
7. Select your **Time** zone in the "Time" field.
8. Add any additional **Note** you'd like to share with the administrator in the "Note" field.
9. Enter your **Network Domain** in the "Network Domain" field.
10. Select your **Admin Type** from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address associated with your account.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Enter your password. Note that this field is not secure, and it's recommended to use a secure method to create and store passwords.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select your time zone.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes you'd like to share with the administrator.
- **Network Domain** (`network_domain`, text, required): Enter the domain name associated with your network.
- **Admin Type** (`admin_type`, select_one, required): Select your admin type from the drop-down menu.
