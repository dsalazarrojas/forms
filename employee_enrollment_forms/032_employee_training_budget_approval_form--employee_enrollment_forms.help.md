# Employee Training Budget Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Training Budget Approval Form is used to collect information for employee training budgets and requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Training Request Form section with the required details about your training request.
2. Provide your approver's details in the Approver Details section.
3. Specify the type of training you are requesting in the Training Type section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Request Form** (`training_request_form`, text, required):
	+ This section is the main form where you will input your training request details.
* **Approver Details** (`approver_details`, text, required):
	+ In this section, please enter the name and title of the person who will review and approve your training request.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, text, required):
	+ Here, specify the type of training you are requesting, e.g. online course, in-person workshop, certification, etc.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your request is reviewed and approved.
* Double-check the approver's details to ensure they can review and approve your request in a timely manner.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can contact your HR or department representative for assistance.
