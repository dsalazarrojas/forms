# Short Term Medical Coverage Election - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Short Term Medical Coverage Election form is used by employees to choose their short-term medical coverage options. This form is designed to collect information on an employee's coverage choices, effective date, time of year, medical group, medical provider, and medical plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions and fields carefully.
2. Select the options for short-term medical coverage that apply to you.
3. Choose the medical group that best suits your needs.
4. Select your medical provider.
5. Choose your medical plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **short_term_medical_coverage_election**: This field is a text field where you can enter your short-term medical coverage choice.
* **short_term_medical_coverage_choices**: This field is a multiple-choice field where you can select one or more of the following options:
	+ Choice 1
	+ Choice 2
	+ Choice 3
* **date_effective**: This field is a date field where you can enter the effective date of your medical coverage.
* **time_of_year**: This field is a time field where you can select the time of year for your medical coverage.
* **medical_group**: This field is a single-choice field where you can select one of the following medical groups:
	+ Group 1
	+ Group 2
	+ Group 3
* **medical_provider**: This field is a text field where you can enter your medical provider's information.
* **medical_plan**: This field is a single-choice field where you can select one of the following medical plans:
	+ Plan 1
	+ Plan 2
	+ Plan 3

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before completing this form.
* Double-check your choices to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR department for assistance.
