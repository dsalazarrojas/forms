Employee Development Metrics Program Registration Form - Help Guide
===========================================================

## Purpose

The employee development metrics program is a tool used to track and analyze employee performance and progress. This form is used to collect data from employees regarding their participation in the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`): Enter the employee's ID number to track their participation in the development metrics program.
* **Program ID** (`program_id`): Enter the ID number of the development program the employee is participating in.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the development program the employee is participating in.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of the employee's participation in the program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of the employee's participation in the program.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`): Enter the employee's email address.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
* **Reporting Manager** (`reporting_manager`): Enter the name of the employee's reporting manager.
* **Reporting Manager Email** (`reporting_manager_email`): Enter the email address of the employee's reporting manager.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`): Select the status of the job.
* **Job Status ID** (`job_status_id`): Enter a unique ID for the job status.
* **Job Status Description** (`job_status_description`): Enter a description of the job status.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`): Enter the email address of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`): Enter the job title of the employee's manager.
* **Manager Location** (`manager_location`): Enter the location of the manager's work.
* **Manager Job Status** (`manager_job_status`): Select the status of the manager's job.
* **Manager Job Status ID** (`manager_job_status_id`): Enter a unique ID for the manager's job status.
* **Manager Job Status Description** (`manager_job_status_description`): Enter a description of the manager's job status.
