# Employee Benefits Reform Committee Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to collect information from employees who wish to participate in the Benefits Reform Committee. It helps the committee understand the applicant's background, experience, and availability to serve.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact details in the "Employee Contact Details" section. 
2. List your relevant experience in the "Relevant Experience" section. 
3. Acknowledge your participation in the "Participation Acknowledgment" section. 
4. Select your availability to serve in the "Available to Serve" section. 
5. Provide additional contact details, such as your name, email, phone number, job title, department, and location, in the "Contact" section. 
6. Specify your experience details, count, and preferences in the "Experience Details" section. 
7. Select your committee preferences, including the committee you wish to participate in, duration, frequency, role, and participation agreements, in the "Participation" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Contact Details**: This section collects your personal contact details, including your name, email, phone number, job title, department, and location.
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name.
	+ **Email**: Enter your work email.
	+ **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
	+ **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
	+ **Department**: Enter the department you belong to.
	+ **Location**: Enter the location where you work.
* **Contact Details**: This section is a duplicate of "Employee Contact Details" and should be filled out with the same information.
* **Relevant Experience**: List your relevant experience in benefits reform committees or similar leadership positions.
* **Acknowledgment of Participation**: Check the box to acknowledge that you understand the participation process and the commitment involved.
* **Available to Serve**: Select all the options that apply to your availability to serve on the committee.
	+ **Available**: Select if you are available to serve.
	+ **Unavailable**: Select if you are not available to serve.
* **Experience Details**: Provide more details about your experience.
* **Count**: Specify the count of your experience.
* **Committee**: Select the committee you wish to participate in.
	+ **Benefits Reform Committee**: Select this option if you wish to participate in the Benefits Reform Committee.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if you wish to participate in a different committee.
* **Duration**: Select the duration of your participation in the committee.
	+ **1 year**: Select this option if you plan to serve for one year.
	+ **2 years**: Select this option if you plan to serve for two years.
	+ **3 years**: Select this option if you plan to serve for three years.
* **Frequency**: Select all the frequencies that apply to your participation in the committee.
	+ **Quarterly**: Select this option if you plan to participate in the committee quarterly.
	+ **Monthly**: Select this option if you plan to participate in the committee monthly.
	+ **Bi-Monthly**: Select this option if you plan to participate in the committee bi-monthly.
* **Role**: Select all the roles that apply to your participation in the committee.
	+ **Chair**: Select this option if you wish to be the chair of the committee.
	+ **Vice Chair**: Select this option if you wish to be the vice chair of the committee.
	+ **Member**: Select this option if you wish to be a member of the committee.
* **Participation Agreement**: Check the boxes to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the participation process and the commitment involved.
	+ **Yes, I understand and agree**: Check this option to acknowledge your understanding and agreement.
	+ **I have reviewed but do not agree**: Check this option if you have reviewed but do not agree to the participation process and commitment.
