# Agricultural Worker Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Worker Onboarding Form is used to collect information for employment verification, tax purposes, and other administrative tasks. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal details, such as your full name, date of birth, and social security number.
2. Provide your home address, emergency contact information, and select your employment status (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Seasonal/Temporary).
3. Enter your position title and start date of work.
4. Select your employment status (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Seasonal/Temporary) and choose your direct deposit information (bank name, routing number, and account number).
5. If required, please scan and email your W-4 and I-9 tax forms to HR@farm.com.
6. Acknowledge your understanding of safety training requirements and select your uniform shirt size.
7. Finally, sign the form with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Social Security or ID Number**: Enter your social security number for tax and employment verification purposes.
* **Home Address**: Enter your street, city, state, and zip code for home address.
* **Emergency Contact Info**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Position Title**: Enter your job title (e.g., Field Hand, Tractor Operator).
* **Start Date**: Enter your first day of work.
* **Employment Status**: Select one of the three options: Full-Time, Part-Time, or Seasonal/Temporary.
* **Bank Name**: Enter the name of your bank.
* **Routing Number**: Enter the 9-digit routing number of your bank account.
* **Account Number**: Enter your account number for direct deposit.
* **Tax Forms Upload**: If required, please scan and email your W-4 and I-9 tax forms to HR@farm.com.
* **Safety Training Acknowledgement**: Select "I Agree" to acknowledge your understanding of safety training requirements.
* **Uniform Shirt Size**: Select your uniform shirt size (S, M, L, XL, or XXL).
* **Employee Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.

Note: All fields marked as `required` are mandatory and must be completed to progress in the onboarding process.
