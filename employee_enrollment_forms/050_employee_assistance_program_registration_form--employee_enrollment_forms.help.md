# prophets_employee.registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employee registration purposes, allowing users to provide information about an employee. The form is designed to collect relevant details such as name, email, phone number, assistance programs, date of birth, start and end dates, and more. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your employee registration is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the employee registration form with the required information.
2. Select the correct assistance programs from the options provided.
3. Enter the date of birth and start and end dates as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Registration Form** (`employee_registration_form`): This field is a text field where you can enter any additional comments or details about the employee registration.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Enter the employee's name in this field.
* **Email** (`employee_email`): Enter the employee's email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Phone** (`employee_phone`): Enter the employee's phone number in this format: `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Select One Assistance Programs** (`employee_assistance_programs`): Choose one of the assistance programs from the options provided.
* **Select Two Assistance Programs** (`employee_assistance_programs_2`): Choose multiple assistance programs from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth** (`employee_date_of_birth`): Enter the employee's date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Start Date** (`employee_start_date`): Enter the employee's start date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **End Date** (`employee_end_date`): Enter the employee's end date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Select Three Assistance Programs** (`employee_assistance_programs_3`): Choose one of the assistance programs from the options provided.
* **Select Four Assistance Programs** (`employee_assistance_programs_4`): Choose multiple assistance programs from the options provided.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing.
* Double-check the assistance programs selections to ensure you've chosen the correct options.
* Enter dates in the correct format to avoid errors.
