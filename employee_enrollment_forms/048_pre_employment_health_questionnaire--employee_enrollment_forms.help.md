# pre_employment_health_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "pre_employment_health_questionnaire" is a form used to collect general and medical information from new employees as part of the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the General Information section with your personal details.
2. In the Medical Information section, provide any pre-existing medical conditions or health issues that you have been diagnosed with.
3. If you have any allergies, list them in the Allergies section.
4. In the Medications section, list any prescription or over-the-counter medications you are currently taking.
5. The Medical Conditions section is for any pre-existing medical conditions that are not listed in the Medical Information section.
6. If you have had any medical procedures or surgeries, answer "Yes" in the Medical Procedure section.
7. If you have had a medical procedure or surgery that is not listed, use the "Other" section to provide more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information
* **General Information (Text)**: This is the first section of the form where you will provide your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
### Medical Information
* **Medical Information (Text)**: In this section, please list any pre-existing medical conditions or health issues that you have been diagnosed with.
### Allergies
* **Allergies (Text)**: If you have any allergies, list them here, including the type of allergy and any symptoms or reactions.
### Medications
* **Medications (Text)**: List any prescription or over-the-counter medications you are currently taking, including the name, dosage, and frequency.
### Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions (Text)**: This section is for any pre-existing medical conditions that are not listed in the Medical Information section.
### Medical Procedure
* **Medical Procedure (Select One)**: Answer "Yes" if you have had any medical procedures or surgeries.
### Other Medical Procedure
* **Other (Text)**: If you have had a medical procedure or surgery that is not listed, provide more details here.
### Medical Procedure Explain
* **Explain (Text)**: If you answered "Yes" to Medical Procedure, please provide an explanation of your medical procedure or surgery.
