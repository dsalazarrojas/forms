# hybrid_team_membership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hybrid Team Membership Application Form is used to collect information from applicants to determine their eligibility and suitability for a hybrid team role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This will be the primary contact point for communication.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field for easy contact.
4. Fill in your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Select your Job Title from the options provided.
6. Indicate your team preference by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Team field.
7. Enter your Physical Work Location in the text field.
8. Choose your Work Hours from the time options provided.
9. Select your Work Schedule by choosing multiple options from the provided list.
10. Finally, select your Work Location from the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your primary email address for communication.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Job Title** (text, optional): Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Team** (select_one, optional): Indicate your team preference by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Physical Work Location** (text, optional): Enter your physical work location.
* **Work Hours** (time, optional): Choose your work hours from the provided options.
* **Work Schedule** (select_multiple, optional): Select multiple work schedule options that apply to you.
* **Work Location** (text, optional): Enter your work location.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays or rejections.
- Double-check your contact information for correct formatting.
- If you have any questions or need help with the application process, feel free to contact the provided email address.
