# Voluntary Health Insurance Overview - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voluntary Health Insurance Overview form is a crucial document that allows employees to enroll in or update their health insurance coverage. It collects essential details about employees and their companies, which will be used for health insurance enrollment and claims processing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form with your personal and work-related details as prompted.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your work email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `note`, required: false): Please enter your address as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Employee Type** (`employee_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are an employee, 'No' if you are not.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Broker' if your company is a broker, 'Insurance Company' if your company is an insurance company, or 'HR Team' if your company is the HR department.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's address as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's website address.
* **Employee Age** (`employee_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your age in years.
* **Employee Salary** (`employee_salary`, `number`, required: false): Enter your annual salary.
* **Employee Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Job Type** (`employee_job_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have a job with your company, 'No' if you do not.
* **Company Job Type** (`company_job_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Part-Time', 'Full-Time', or 'Temporary' to describe your company's employment type.
* **Employee Job Classification** (`employee_job_classification`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if your job has a job classification, 'No' if it does not.
* **Company Job Classification** (`company_job_classification`, `select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if your company has a job classification, 'No' if it does not.
* **Education** (`employee_education`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you have any education, 'No' if you do not.
* **Company Education** (`company_education`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if your company has any education, 'No' if it does not.
* **Date of Birth** (`employee_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth.
* **Company Date of Birth** (`company_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter your company's date of birth (this field is generally not used in health insurance enrollment).
* **Date of Hire** (`employee_dod`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of hire with your company.
* **Company Date of Hire** (`company_dod`, `date`, required: false): Enter your company's date of hire (this field is generally not used in health insurance enrollment).

## Tips
* Please double-check your accuracy before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* Ensure that all fields that are not filled out are marked as 'No' or left blank.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your HR department or the relevant authorities for clarification.
