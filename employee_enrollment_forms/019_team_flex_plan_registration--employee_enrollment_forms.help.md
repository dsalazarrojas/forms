# Team Flex Plan Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Flex Plan Registration form is designed to collect information about team scheduling and planning within an organization. It's used to gather details about team names, key personnel, and schedule dates and frequencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the team's name in the **team_name** field.
2. Input the email addresses of the team manager and team lead in the **manager_email** and **team_lead_email** fields respectively.
3. If applicable, select the start and end dates of the team's schedule in the **schedule_start_date** and **schedule_end_date** fields.
4. Choose the frequency of the team's schedule in the **schedule_frequency** field by selecting one of the provided options.
5. List the team members who will be part of the team by selecting them from the options in the **team_members** field.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the **comments** field.
7. Finally, input the date the form was submitted in the **submission_date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **team_name** (text): The name of the team.
*   **manager_email** (email): Email address of the team manager or supervisor.
*   **team_lead_email** (email): Email address of the team lead or coordinator.
*   **schedule_start_date** (date): Start date of the team's schedule.
*   **schedule_end_date** (date): End date of the team's schedule.
*   **schedule_frequency** (select one): Frequency of the team's schedule (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, etc.).
*   **team_members** (select multiple): List of team members who will be part of the team.
*   **comments** (note): Additional comments or notes about the team.
*   **submission_date** (date): Date the form was submitted.
