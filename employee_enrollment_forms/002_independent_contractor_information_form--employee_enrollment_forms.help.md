# independent_contractor_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about independent contractors and their employment contracts. The form captures various details about the contractor, including contract terms, tax identification, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are authorized to fill out this form. Only relevant users should have access to this form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contract Details** (required): Enter the details of the contract, including any relevant terms or conditions.
* **Payment Terms** (required): Enter the payment terms agreed upon in the contract, such as the rate per hour.
* **Contact Information** (required): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Tax Identification** (required): Enter your tax identification number or other relevant tax information.
* **Tax ID Number** (required): Enter your tax ID number, as required for tax purposes.
* **Social Security Number** (required): Enter your social security number, as required for tax and employment purposes.
* **Contract Start Date** (required): Enter the start date of the contract, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contract End Date** (required): Enter the end date of the contract, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Hours Worked** (required): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Rate Per Hour** (required): Enter your rate per hour of work.
* **Tax Rate** (required): Enter the tax rate applicable to you.
* **Contract Type** (required): Select the type of contract you have, either Full-Time or Part-Time.
* **Contract Status** (required): Select the current status of your contract, either Active or Inactive.
