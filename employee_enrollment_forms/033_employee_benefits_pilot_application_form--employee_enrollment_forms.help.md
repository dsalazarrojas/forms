# employee_benefits_pilot_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from employees for the employee benefits pilot program, which aims to provide various benefits to employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed. While not all fields are required, providing complete and accurate information will help the benefits program work effectively.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please refer to the field-by-field explanation below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Interest** (`employee_interest`, text, optional): This field allows you to express your interest in participating in the employee benefits pilot program. Please provide a brief description of your interest and why you would like to be part of the program.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): If you have any comments or suggestions about the program, please use this field to provide your feedback. This will help us improve the program and tailor it to your needs.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Check 'Yes' if you are willing to participate in the employee benefits pilot program. This is a mandatory field for the program to function correctly, as we need to know your consent to include you.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, optional): Select your department from the provided options. This helps us track which departments are participating in the program.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Please provide your full name as it appears on your official documents. This is essential for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official work email address. This is used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your work phone number. This will help us contact you for any program updates or benefits.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format. This helps us ensure accurate age calculations for benefits eligibility.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, time, optional): If you have a specific time you would like to begin participating in the program, please enter the start date and time. Otherwise, leave this field blank.

## Tips
- Ensure you are filling out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure you are eligible for the benefits.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your HR or management team for clarification.
- Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
