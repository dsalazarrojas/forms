# IT System Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the IT System Onboarding process for new employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. 1. Personal Details
2. 2. Job Details
3. 3. Contact Info
4. 4. System Access
5. 5. IT Systems
6. 6. Emergency Contact

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Details** (Personal Details, text, optional): Please fill in your personal details, including your name, employee ID, and any other relevant information.
- **Job Details** (Job Details, text, optional): Please provide your job title, department, and any other relevant job details.
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, number, optional): Please fill in your contact information, such as phone number and email.
- **System Access** (System Access, select_one, optional): Please select whether you need system access or not.
- **IT Systems** (IT Systems, select_multiple, optional): Please select which IT systems you need access to.
- **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional): Please provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you need help or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to the IT department.
- Make sure to review the form before submitting it to ensure that all required information is provided.
