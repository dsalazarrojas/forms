# hr_career_growth_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hr_career_growth_program_registration_form is a tool used to collect information from employees for the career growth program. This program aims to support employees' career development and growth opportunities within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the "Employee Details" section.
2. Describe your career goals and aspirations in the "Goals" section (optional).
3. Provide information on any necessary approvals, such as supervisor or HR department approvals, in the "Approvals" section.
4. Add any additional information you feel is relevant to your career growth in the "Additional Info" section (optional).
5. Confirm your submission in the "Submission" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Details** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required): Fill in your employee details, including your name and job title, to help the HR department identify you in the system.
* **Goals** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, optional): Describe your career goals and aspirations, such as positions you would like to move into or skills you would like to develop. This will help the program understand your career growth objectives.
* **Approvals** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required): List any necessary approvals, such as supervisor or HR department approvals, that need to be obtained before participating in the program.
* **Additional Info** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, optional): Add any additional information you feel is relevant to your career growth, such as relevant experience or education.
* **Submission** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required): Confirm your submission by providing a final confirmation of your registration.

## Tips
- Ensure you complete all required fields (Employee Details and Submission).
- Be specific and clear in your responses to provide the HR department with a clear understanding of your career goals and aspirations.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the HR department.
- The information provided will be used to support your career growth within the organization.
- The program is designed to support career development, so be open-minded and receptive to opportunities.
