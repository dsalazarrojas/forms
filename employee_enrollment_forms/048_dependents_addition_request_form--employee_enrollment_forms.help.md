# Dependents Addition Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dependents Addition Request Form is used to request the addition of dependents to an employee's benefits. This form allows employees to provide information about their dependents, including their name, date of birth, address, and relationship to the employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Confirm that you are not requesting dependents that are already covered under your previous submissions.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all required information.
3. Select the correct relationship of each dependent from the provided options.
4. Indicate the correct dependents count.
5. Add a brief description of each dependent's information in the corresponding text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dependents Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will provide a brief description of your dependents, including their name, date of birth, and address.
* **Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the dependent.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the dependent.
* **Address** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the dependent.
* **Relation** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship of the dependent from the provided options (e.g. Father, Mother, Son, Daughter, etc.).
* **Dependents Count** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the number of dependents you are adding to your benefits.
* **Dependent 1 Information** (`7`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will provide a brief description of the first dependent.
* **Dependent 2 Information** (`8`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will provide a brief description of the second dependent.
* **Dependent 3 Information** (`9`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you will provide a brief description of the third dependent.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your HR representative for assistance.
