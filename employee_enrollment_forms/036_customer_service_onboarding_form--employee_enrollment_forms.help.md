# Customer Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to onboard new employees into the customer service department. It captures crucial information about the new hire, which is essential for their smooth transition into the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your new hire information in the "New Hire Info" field.
2. Select your job title from the "Job Title" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your work start date in the "Work Start Date" field.
4. Select your job status from the "Job Status" dropdown menu.
5. Choose your reporting manager from the "Reporting Manager" dropdown menu.
6. Select your department from the "Department" dropdown menu.
7. Choose your job function from the "Job Function" dropdown menu.
8. Select your work location from the "Work Location" dropdown menu.
9. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
10. Enter your manager's email address in the "Manager Email" field.
11. Select team members who report to you in the "Team Members" dropdown menu.
12. Choose your team leader from the "Team Leader" dropdown menu.
13. Enter any relevant job notes in the "Job Notes" field.
14. Enter your employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field.
15. Enter your hire date in the "Hire Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **New Hire Info (Text)**: A text field for entering new hire information.
- **Job Title (Select One)**: A dropdown menu to select your job title from the options provided.
- **Work Start Date (Date)**: A field to enter your work start date.
- **Job Status (Select Multiple)**: A dropdown menu to select your job status from the options provided.
- **Reporting Manager (Select One)**: A dropdown menu to choose your reporting manager.
- **Department (Select One)**: A dropdown menu to select your department.
- **Job Function (Select Multiple)**: A dropdown menu to choose your job function from the options provided.
- **Work Location (Select One)**: A dropdown menu to select your work location.
- **Manager Name (Text)**: A text field to enter your manager's name.
- **Manager Email (Email)**: A field to enter your manager's email address.
- **Team Members (Select Multiple)**: A dropdown menu to select team members who report to you.
- **Team Leader (Select One)**: A dropdown menu to choose your team leader.
- **Job Notes (Text)**: A text field for entering any relevant job notes.
- **Employee ID (Number)**: A field to enter your employee ID number.
- **Hire Date (Date)**: A field to enter your hire date.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If a field is not required, it is not essential to fill it out.
- This form is intended to be used by HR administrators, not employees.
- If you are an employee completing this form, please direct any questions or concerns to your HR representative.
- If you are an HR administrator, please review the form carefully before submitting it.
