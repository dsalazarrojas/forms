# Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from employees regarding their part-time health insurance enrollment. This form is intended for employees who work part-time and are eligible for the company's health insurance benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out all the necessary fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all required information is provided.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact your HR representative for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Part Time Health Insurance Enrollment Form** (`part_time_health_insurance_enrollment_form`): This is the title of the form, please do not fill out this field.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Please enter your job title as listed on your company's records.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Social Security Number** (`social_security_number`): Please enter your social security number as listed on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`): Please enter your email address as it appears on your company's records.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Please enter your phone number as it appears on your company's records.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`): Please select your medical group from the options provided. If your medical group is not listed, please enter it in the "Medical Group Other" field.
* **Medical Group Other** (`medical_group_other`): Please enter your medical group if it is not listed in the previous field.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Please enter the name of the person you would like to list as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`): Please enter the relation to the person you listed as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Please enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Dependents** (`dependents`): Please select the number of dependents you would like to enroll.
* **Dependent 1 Name** (`dependent_1_name`): Please enter the name of your first dependent.
* **Dependent 1 Date of Birth** (`dependent_1_dob`): Please enter the date of birth of your first dependent in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Dependent 1 Social Security Number** (`dependent_1_social_security_number`): Please enter the social security number of your first dependent as listed on their identification documents.
* **Dependent 1 Phone Number** (`dependent_1_phone_number`): Please enter the phone number of your first dependent.
* **Dependent 2 Name** (`dependent_2_name`): Please enter the name of your second dependent.
* **Dependent 2 Date of Birth** (`dependent_2_dob`): Please enter the date of birth of your second dependent in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Dependent 2 Social Security Number** (`dependent_2_social_security_number`): Please enter the social security number of your second dependent as listed on their identification documents.
* **Dependent 2 Phone Number** (`dependent_2_phone_number`): Please enter the phone number of your second dependent.
* **Dependent 3 Name** (`dependent_3_name`): Please enter the name of your third dependent.
* **Dependent 3 Date of Birth** (`dependent_3_dob`): Please enter the date of birth of your third dependent in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Dependent 3 Social Security Number** (`dependent_3_social_security_number`): Please enter the social security number of your third dependent as listed on their identification documents.
* **Dependent 3 Phone Number** (`dependent_3_phone_number`): Please enter the phone number of your third dependent.
