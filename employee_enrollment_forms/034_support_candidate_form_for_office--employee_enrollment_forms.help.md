# Support Candidate Form For Office - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from job applicants for support candidates. It helps us to better understand the candidates' details, skills, and preferences. Please fill out the form with accurate and detailed information to ensure a smooth hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the candidate's name and contact information, including their first name, last name, and email.
2. Provide your contact number for easy communication.
3. Enter the date of the job application.
4. Attach your resume for us to review.
5. Select the support skills you have, and choose your preferred work location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your official email address.
* **Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Enter a number where we can contact you.
* **Job Application Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of when you applied for the job.
* **Resume** (Text, Optional): Attach your resume for our review.
* **Support Skills** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the support skills you have, such as "Option 1", "Option 2", or "Option 3".
* **Preferred Work Location** (Select One, Optional): Choose where you would prefer to work, such as "Option 1" or "Option 2" or "Option 3".

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and detailed information.
* Attach your resume and select your support skills and preferred work location carefully.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
