# Physician Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physician Onboarding Form is a crucial tool for new physicians to onboard into our organization. This form collects vital information about the physician's background, practice details, and onboarding dates.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by providing your personal details:
  - Fill out your first and last name on page 1.
  - Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - Provide your phone number for contact.
  - Enter your date of birth.
- Proceed to page 2:
  - Select your current residency state (New York, California, or Illinois).
  - Choose your medical license status (Active, Lapsed, or Pending).
  - Choose your work status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Leave, or Other).
- Move on to page 3:
  - List your physician specialties (Family Medicine, General Practice, Surgery, Pediatrics, or Other).
  - Choose your emergency contact options (Primary Care Physician, Family Member, or Other).
- Continue to page 4:
  - Enter your onboarding date.
  - Provide your termination date (if applicable).
- Fill out your practice details on page 5:
  - Enter your practice address.
  - Provide details about your practice.
  - Enter your medical group.
- Enter your practice's name and address on page 6:
  - Enter your physician name.
  - Enter your medical license number.
  - Enter your medical license state.
- Finally, fill out your practice location:
  - Enter your practice's city.
  - Enter your practice's state.
  - Enter your practice's zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name.
  - Please ensure this is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name.
  - Ensure this is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
  - This will be used for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work or mobile phone number.
  - This will be used for internal communication.
- **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth.
  - This is required for demographic and insurance purposes.
- **Medical School** (`medical_school`, text, required): Enter the name of your medical school.
  - Please ensure this is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter the date you graduated from medical school.
  - Please ensure this is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Current Residency** (`current_residency`, select_one, required): Select your current residency state (New York, California, or Illinois).
  - Choose the state where you currently reside.
- **Medical License** (`medical_license`, select_one, required): Choose your medical license status (Active, Lapsed, or Pending).
  - Ensure this is up-to-date and accurate.
- **Work Status** (`work_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose your work status (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Leave, or Other).
  - Ensure this is up-to-date and accurate.
- **Physician Specialties** (`physician_specialties`, select_multiple, required): Choose your physician specialties (Family Medicine, General Practice, Surgery, Pediatrics, or Other).
  - Choose all applicable specialties.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, required): Choose your emergency contact options (Primary Care Physician, Family Member, or Other).
  - Please ensure this is up-to-date and accurate.
- **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Enter the date you onborded into our system.
  - Ensure this is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Termination Date** (`termination_date`, date, required): Enter the date you terminated from your previous position (if applicable).
  - Please ensure this is accurate and up-to-date.
- **Physician Address** (`physician_address`, text, required): Enter your home address.

- **Practice Details** (`practice_details`, text, required): Enter details about your practice (number of physicians, patient volume, etc.).
