# Employee Casual Cook Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from employees about their interest in being a casual cook for the company's cafeteria. This is an optional form, and employees are not required to fill it out.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is being collected.
2. Answer each question truthfully and accurately.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes in the availability note field if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, optional): This field asks for the number of years of experience you have in cooking. You can enter a numerical value to provide more detail about your experience.
* **Job Title and Experience** (`job_title_experience`, text, optional): This field allows you to describe your relevant work experience or training related to cooking. You can enter a brief description of your experience in this field.
* **Cuisines** (`cuisines`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the cuisines you have experience in. You can choose from a list of options, including Asian, Mediterranean, Continental, Seafood, Italian, Mexican, and Other.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all the days you are available to work in the cafeteria. You can select from a list of options, including Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, and Sunday.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to start working as a casual cook. This should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available to start working as a casual cook. This should be in the format of HH:MM.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available to end working as a casual cook. This should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are available to end working as a casual cook. This should be in the format of HH:MM.
* **Availability Note** (`availability_note`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your availability. You can enter any relevant information that may affect your availability.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so that the cafeteria team can contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so that the cafeteria team can contact you.

Note: All fields are optional, and you can choose to fill out only the fields that are relevant to your situation.
