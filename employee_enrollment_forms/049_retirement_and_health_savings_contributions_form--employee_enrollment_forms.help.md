# Retirement And Health Savings Contributions Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track information related to an employee's retirement and health savings contributions. It provides a way to update or add new contributions to their benefits, including both current and future plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your employee ID.
2. Select your department from the provided options.
3. Choose the health and retirement plans you're contributing to.
4. Enter your contribution amounts.
5. Select the effective dates for your plans.
6. Update your benefits status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, Number, required): Enter your employee ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, Text, required): Select your department from the provided options to indicate your affiliation.
* **Health Savings Plan** (`health_savings_plan`, Select One, optional): Choose whether you have a health savings plan. This will help in tracking your contributions to the plan.
* **Retirement Plan** (`retirement_plan`, Select Multiple, optional): Select the retirement plans you're contributing to.
* **Contributions** (`contributions`, Number, optional): Enter your contribution amounts for each plan.
* **Contribution Amount** (`contribution_amount`, Number, optional): Enter the amount you contribute each month.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, Date, optional): Select the date when your plans become effective.
* **Benefits Effective Date** (`benefits_effective_date`, Date, optional): Select the date when your benefits start.
* **Employee Status** (`employee_status`, Text, optional): Update your status in relation to your benefits.
* **Health Savings Rate** (`health_savings_rate`, Number, optional): Enter the rate for your health savings plan.
* **Health Savings Rate Percentage** (`health_savings_rate_percentage`, Number, optional): Enter the percentage rate for your health savings plan.
* **Retirement Rate** (`retirement_rate`, Number, optional): Enter the rate for your retirement plan.
* **Retirement Rate Percentage** (`retirement_rate_percentage`, Number, optional): Enter the percentage rate for your retirement plan.
* **Health Savings Contribution** (`health_savings_contribution`, Number, optional): Display the total health savings contributions.
* **Retirement Contribution** (`retirement_contribution`, Number, optional): Display the total retirement contributions.
* **Benefits Status** (`benefits_status`, Text, optional): Update your benefits status.
* **Benefits Start Date** (`benefits_start_date`, Date, optional): Select the date when your benefits start.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Employee ID and Department) for accurate tracking.
- If you have multiple plans, select all the relevant plans for correct contributions and rates.
- Review your contribution amounts and rates carefully to ensure accuracy.
- Enter the correct effective and start dates for your plans.
- Update your employee status and benefits status to ensure accurate tracking.
- If you're unsure about any field, consult with your HR representative for clarification.
