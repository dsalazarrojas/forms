# Incentive Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employees to enroll in the incentive program. Please complete this form to provide necessary information for your enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Review and update any optional fields as necessary.
3. Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incentive Program Enrollment Form** (`form_title`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of the incentive program you are enrolling in.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, not required): Enter your job title as it appears on your employment contract or job description.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, text, not required): Enter your job position or role within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, not required): Enter your work address.
* **Date Start** (`date_start`, date, not required): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **Date End** (`date_end`, date, not required): Enter the date you expect to end working with the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Enter your department or team name.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, not required): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, not required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, not required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Team** (`team`, text, not required): Enter your team name or affiliation.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional information about your enrollment or role within the company.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click submit to finalize your enrollment.
