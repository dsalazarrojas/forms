# Black Friday Wishlist Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a personal shopping assistant for Black Friday sales. It helps users plan their shopping and stay within their budget by creating a wishlist of items they want to buy.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and create a title for your wishlist.
2. Set your maximum spending limit.
3. Add each item you want to buy on a separate page.
4. Fill out each item's details, including name, store, original price, and sale price.
5. Set the item's priority.
6. Add any notes about planning and who the gift is for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **My Black Friday Wishlist** (`wishlist_header`): Enter a title for your wishlist.
* **Wishlist Name / User** (`user_name`): Enter your name to associate with your wishlist.
* **Overall Black Friday Budget** (`total_budget`): Enter your maximum spending limit for Black Friday sales.
* **-- Item 1 --** (`item_1_header`): Start a new page for each item you want to add.
* **Item 1 Name** (`item_1_name`): Enter the name of the item you want to buy.
* **Store / Website** (`item_1_store`): Enter the store or website where the item is available.
* **Original Price** (`item_1_original_price`): Enter the original price of the item.
* **Black Friday Sale Price** (`item_1_sale_price`): Enter the sale price of the item.
* **Priority** (`item_1_priority`): Choose how important you think this item is to you (Must Have, Nice to Have, If Budget Allows).
* **-- Item 2 --** (`item_2_header`): Add another item to your wishlist.
* **Item 2 Name** (`item_2_name`): Enter the name of the item you want to buy.
* **Store / Website** (`item_2_store`): Enter the store or website where the item is available.
* **Black Friday Sale Price** (`item_2_sale_price`): Enter the sale price of the item.
* **Priority** (`item_2_priority`): Choose how important you think this item is to you (Must Have, Nice to Have, If Budget Allows).

## Tips

* Make sure to stay within your budget by keeping an eye on the total cost.
* Use the "Priority" field to help prioritize your shopping list.
* You can add multiple items to your wishlist, each on a separate page.
* You can add notes about your planning and who the gift is for in the "Planning Notes" field.
