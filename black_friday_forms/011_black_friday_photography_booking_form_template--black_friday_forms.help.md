# Black Friday Photography Booking Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Black Friday Photography Booking Form Template is designed to collect information necessary for a successful and enjoyable photography session. Please follow the guide to complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
2.  Select your preferred session type (Family Portrait, Individual Portrait, Couple Session, Pet Photography, or Product Photography).
3.  Choose your preferred date and time for the session.
4.  Select your preferred location for the session (Studio Session, Outdoor Location, Client Home, Business Location, or Other Venue).
5.  Indicate the number of subjects or individuals to be photographed.
6.  If applicable, provide the ages of any children participating in the session.
7.  Select a photography package that suits your needs (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Deluxe).
8.  If you have specific photography requests or requirements, please detail them in the provided field.
9.  Choose how many outfit changes you will need during the session.
10. If you want printed photos, select the desired print package (Digital Only, Small Print Pack, Medium Print Pack, Large Print Pack, or Premium Album).
11.  Finally, let's know how you heard about us (Social Media, Website, Friend Referral, Previous Client, Advertisement, or Other).
12.  Choose to pay the deposit now or indicate that you have further questions.
13.  Any additional notes or comments can be provided in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name (`client_name`, Text, Required):** This field is for the full name of the client. Please fill in your name as it should be written on your identification.
* **Email Address (`email`, Email, Required):** This is your primary contact email address. Ensure it's correct and up-to-date.
* **Phone Number (`phone`, Text, Required):** This is your contact number for any communication or scheduling purposes. Please ensure it's accurate.
* **Session Type (`session_type`, Select One, Required):** This field allows you to choose the type of photography session you're booking (Family Portrait, Individual Portrait, Couple Session, Pet Photography, or Product Photography).
* **Preferred Date (`preferred_date`, Date, Required):** Select your preferred date for the photography session. Remember, this is your first choice.
* **Preferred Time (`preferred_time`, Time, Required):** Indicate the time of day you prefer for the session.
* **Alternate Date (`alternate_date`, Date, Optional):** If your preferred date is not available, please select an alternate date for the session.
* **Location Preference (`location_preference`, Select One, Required):** Choose where you'd like the session to take place (Studio Session, Outdoor Location, Client Home, Business Location, or Other Venue).
* **Number of Subjects (`number_of_subjects`, Number, Required):** Indicate the number of people to be photographed during the session.
* **Children Ages (`children_ages`, Text, Optional):** If children are among the subjects, please specify their ages for the photographer's awareness.
* **Package Selection (`package_selection`, Select One, Required):** Select a photography package that suits your needs (Basic, Standard, Premium, or Deluxe).
* **Special Requests (`special_requests`, Text, Optional):** If you have any specific photography requests or requirements, please detail them here.
* **Number of Outfit Changes (`outfit_changes`, Select One, Optional):** Indicate how many outfit changes you expect to need during the session.
* **Print Package (`prints_needed`, Select One, Required):** Select your desired print package (Digital Only, Small Print Pack, Medium Print Pack, Large Print Pack, or Premium Album).
* **How Did You Hear About Us (`how_did_you_hear`, Select One, Optional):** Please let us know where you heard about us (Social Media, Website, Friend Referral, Previous Client, Advertisement, or Other).
* **Deposit Payment (`deposit_payment`, Select One, Required):** Choose to either pay the deposit immediately or indicate you have further questions.
* **Additional Notes (`additional_notes`, Text, Optional):** Finally, if you have any additional comments or notes, please share them here to help the photographer understand your needs better.
