# Product List Order Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture information related to product ordering, including personal details and product-specific data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: 
   * Enter your **First Name**.
   * Enter your **Last Name**.
   * Enter your **Email** address.
2. Select the products you'd like to order:
   * Choose **Yes** or **No** for each product to add it to your order list.
3. Enter the price and quantity for each product:
   * For each product, enter the **Price** and **Quantity**.
4. Review and submit your order.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: This is not required, but if you'd like to enter your phone number for our reference.
* **Product List**: Select **Yes** for each product you'd like to order. Note that this field is not related to the product price or quantity.
* **Price 1**: Enter the price for the first product.
* **Price 2**: Enter the price for the second product.
* **Price 3**: Enter the price for the third product.
* **Quantity 1**: Enter the quantity for the first product.
* **Quantity 2**: Enter the quantity for the second product.
* **Quantity 3**: Enter the quantity for the third product.
* **Total**: This field is auto-calculated and cannot be edited by the user.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and complete information to avoid errors or re-submission.
* Review your order carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or need help, please contact our support team.
