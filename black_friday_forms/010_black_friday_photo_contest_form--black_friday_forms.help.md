# Black Friday Photo Contest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Black Friday Photo Contest Form is a platform to submit your best Black Friday shopping moments for a chance to win prizes. To be eligible, you must be 18 or older and submit a photo that meets the contest rules.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the contest rules and terms of agreement
2. Fill in your basic information:
	* Your Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
3. Verify your age (must be 18 or older)
4. Provide your photo details:
	* Photo Title
	* Photo Description
	* Select the best category for your photo
	* Link to your uploaded photo
	* Location where the photo was taken
	* Date the photo was taken
5. Confirm your original work and sharing permission
6. Join our newsletter for contest updates and future promotions (optional)
7. Review and agree to the contest rules
8. Confirm that the submitted photo is your original work
9. Provide additional comments or information (optional)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Black Friday Photo Contest Entry**: This is a note field to introduce and describe your photo submission.
* **Your Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: The email address we will use for winner notification.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Age Verification**: Confirm that you are 18 or older to enter the contest.
* **Photo Title**: Give a creative title to your photo.
* **Photo Description**: Describe the story behind your photo.
* **Photo Category**: Select the best category for your photo among the options provided.
* **Photo Upload URL**: Link to the location where your photo is uploaded.
* **Photo Location**: Where was the photo taken.
* **Shopping Date**: When was the photo taken.
* **Store Name**: The store where you shopped (optional).
* **Social Media Handle**: Your Instagram or Twitter handle (optional).
* **Sharing Permission**: Confirm if we can share your photo on social media.
* **Join Our Newsletter**: Opt-in to receive contest updates and future promotions (optional).
* **Contest Rules Agreement**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the contest rules.
* **Original Work Declaration**: Confirm that the submitted photo is your original work.
* **How Did You Hear About Contest**: Source of contest information (optional).
* **Additional Comments**: Anything else you want to share about your submission (optional).
