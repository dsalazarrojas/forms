# Tenant Black Friday Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Black Friday Feedback form is designed to collect feedback from tenants about their experience on Black Friday. This feedback will help identify areas of improvement for future Black Friday events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions as truthfully as possible.
2. Choose an answer for the "Overall Satisfaction" question from the 1-5 scale.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Black Friday Performance** (`black_friday_performance`, text, optional): Share your experience with us about how your Black Friday shopping experience at our property was. 
* **Black Friday Purchases** (`black_friday_purchases`, number, optional): If you made any purchases on Black Friday, please tell us how many you made. 
* **Black Friday Discounts** (`black_friday_discounts`, number, optional): If you took advantage of any discounts offered on Black Friday, please tell us how many you received. 
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, optional): Rate your overall satisfaction with your Black Friday experience on a scale of 1-5.

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to help us understand your experience better.
* If you have any further comments or suggestions, please include them in the text area below each question.
* Only fill out this form if you have visited the property on Black Friday, as duplicate submissions will be automatically discarded.
