# Black Friday Order Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for special Black Friday orders. It will help us process your order efficiently and ensure that you receive the correct products at the correct prices.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name, email address, and phone number under the "Customer Information" section.
2. Enter your shipping address, including city and zip code.
3. Select the products you would like to purchase from the "Select Products to Order" section.
4. Choose the quantity of each item you would like to order.
5. If you have a discount coupon code, enter it in the "Discount Coupon Code" field.
6. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" options.
7. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Black Friday Special Order Form --**: This is a header for the form, providing a clear title and introducing the purpose of the form.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your order confirmation and delivery.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can receive order confirmation and other important updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about your order.
* **-- Shipping Details --**: This header provides a clear separation between the customer information and the shipping details.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address where the order will be delivered.
* **City**: Enter the city where the shipping address is located.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the shipping address.
* **-- Your Order --**: This header separates the shipping details from the order selection.
* **Select Products to Order**: Choose one or more products from the list to include in your order. Sale prices already apply to each product.
* **Quantity of Items**: Enter the total number of items you would like to order for each selected product.
* **Discount Coupon Code**: If you have a valid coupon code, enter it here to receive the discount.
* **Thank you for your Black Friday purchase!**: This is a note to thank you for your purchase.

Tips:
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues with your order, contact us at your convenience.
* Our sale prices are already applied to the products listed, so you don't need to add any additional discounts.
