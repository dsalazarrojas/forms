# Gift Card Giveaway Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users participating in a gift card giveaway contest. It will collect details such as user information, gift card details, and the source where the user found out about the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out the form completely and accurately. Each field is described below.
2. Click on each field to learn more about what information it is intended to collect.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide your name and contact information to be eligible for the gift card. Please fill in your full name, email, and phone number so we can contact you if you win.
- **Gift Card Information** (`Gift Card Information`, text, required/optional): This field is for you to provide information about the gift card you are eligible for, including its value and any other details you think would be useful.
- **Contest Information** (`Contest Information`, text, required/optional): Here, you can provide any additional information about the contest you are participating in, like how you heard about it or any specific details you have.
- **How did you find out about this contest?** (`How did you find out about this contest?`, select multiple, required/optional): Choose how you found out about this contest from the list provided:
  • Social Media
  • Email
  • Print Ad
  • TV Ad
  • Radio Ad
  • Friend
  • Family Member
  • Online Search
  • Other
- **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, required/optional): This is an optional field to provide any additional information you think would be helpful.
- **I agree to the terms and conditions of this contest** (`I agree to the terms and conditions of this contest`, select one, required/optional): Check this box to agree to the terms and conditions of the contest. If you do not agree, please don't proceed.
- **Gift Card Details** (`Gift Card Details`, text, required/optional): Here, you can provide any extra information about the gift card you are eligible for.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and honestly.
- Each field is optional, but filling them out will give you a chance to win or get more information about the contest.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact us at: form@gic.mx
