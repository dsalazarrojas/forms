# Fundraising Terms Guide - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a fundraising campaign, including contact details, the purpose of the campaign, target goal amount, planned methods of fundraising, and agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
2. Briefly describe the purpose of your fundraising campaign in the "Fundraising Purpose" field.
3. Enter your goal amount for the campaign in the "Goal Amount" field. This field is required.
4. Select the planned methods for your fundraising campaign from the list in the "Planned Methods" field. Multiple methods can be selected.
5. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the campaign by selecting "I Agree" in the "Agreement to Terms" field.
6. Confirm that you agree to the organisation's terms by selecting all applicable items from the list in the "Agreement to Organisation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (text, required/optional): Enter your name, email address, and other relevant contact information.
- **Fundraising Purpose** (text, required/optional): Describe the purpose of your fundraising campaign, e.g. "Raising funds for a charity event" or "Supporting a social cause".
- **Goal Amount** (number, required): Enter the target amount you aim to raise for your campaign.
- **Planned Methods** (select_one, required): Select one or multiple methods from the list:
	+ Donation
	+ Fundraising
	+ Grant
	+ Other
- **Agreement to Terms** (select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
- **Agreement to Organisation** (select_multiple, required): Select all applicable items from the list:
	+ Terms and Conditions
	+ Privacy Policy
	+ Refund Policy

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your campaign is complete.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
