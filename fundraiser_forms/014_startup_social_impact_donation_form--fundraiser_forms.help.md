# Startup Social Impact Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Social Impact Donation Form is designed to collect donations for social impact from startups. This form is intended for users who want to contribute financially to support social causes related to startups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your **name** in the first field.
* Provide a valid **email address** for communication purposes.
* Enter your **phone number** for any follow-up or verification.
* Specify the **amount donated** in the designated field.
* Describe the **purpose of the donation**, so that we can understand the impact of your gift.
* Choose your **donation frequency** from the options provided:
	+ Option 1: One-time donation
	+ Option 2: Regular donation
	+ Option 3: Monthly subscription
* Select the **method of donation**:
	+ Option 1: Online transfer
	+ Option 2: Bank transfer
	+ Option 3: Other ( specify in additional comments)
* You can add any **additional comments** if you have any specific reasons for your donation or preferences.
* Review and confirm your **form comments** for any further information or details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title**: A field to enter the title of your donation.
* **name**: A field to enter your name for identification and recognition purposes.
* **email**: A field to enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **phone**: A field to enter your phone number for follow-up or verification.
* **amount_donated**: A field to specify the amount donated for the social impact.
* **purpose_donated**: A field to describe the purpose of the donation.
* **donation_frequency**: A field to choose the frequency of your donation.
* **donation_method**: A field to select the method of donation.
* **additional_comments**: A field to add any additional comments or reasons for your donation.
* **form_comments**: A field to review and confirm your form comments.
* **form_comments_2** to **form_comments_15**: Additional fields to provide further information or comments about your donation.

Note: All fields are required unless specified otherwise in the form.
