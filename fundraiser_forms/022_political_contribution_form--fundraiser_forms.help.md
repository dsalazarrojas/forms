# Political Contribution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Political Contribution Form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations that make political contributions. This form is for reporting and auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User Name** in the field below.
2. Select your **Contributor Type**: Choose whether you are an **Individual**, **PAC**, or **Organization**.
3. Enter your **Address** or **Address 2** if necessary.
4. Enter the **Contribution Amount** and select a valid payment method from the options provided.
5. Provide the **Date of Contribution** and **Time of Contribution** if applicable.
6. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name**: A unique identifier for the contributor. This is a required field if you have previously submitted a contribution.
* **Contributor Type**: A selection to indicate if you are an individual, PAC, or organization making a contribution.
* **Address**: Enter a real address for the contributor. This field is not required but recommended for identification purposes.
* **Address 2**: Enter an additional address if necessary for the contributor. This field is not required.
* **Contribution Amount**: Enter the amount of the contribution. This field is not required but recommended for accurate tracking.
* **Payment Method**: Select from the options provided (Cash, Check, Credit Card).
* **Date of Contribution**: Enter the date of the contribution in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time of Contribution**: Enter the time of the contribution in the format HH:MM.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
