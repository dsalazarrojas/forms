# Leadership Workshops Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from individuals who want to attend leadership workshops. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to help us better understand your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you about the workshop and your donation.
3. If you want to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number in the Phone field.
4. In the Donation Amount field, enter the amount you wish to donate.
5. Choose your Preferred Donation Frequency to let us know how often you'd like to be contacted.
6. Indicate if you'd like to be contacted with Yes or No in the Donation Frequency field. If you have any other preferences, please specify in the Other field.
7. In the Purpose of Donation field, briefly describe the reason for your donation (optional).
8. Choose your preferred communication method with Yes or No in the Preferred Communication field.
9. If you have any comments or additional information, please use the Comments field.
10. You will receive a Form Submission ID, which you can use to track your submission.
