# Learning Tools Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Learning Tools Donation Form is used to collect information from donors about their donations, including personal details and donation specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Select the **Donation Type** you wish to make (Recurring or One-Time).
4. Enter the **Amount** of your donation.
5. Choose the **Donation Date**.
6. Select the **Time Donated** (if applicable).
7. Add any **Notes** about your donation.
8. Check the box if you wish to make an **In-Kind Donation** and enter a brief description.
9. Enter your **Donor Name** and **Donor Email** if you are donating on behalf of someone else.
10. Enter your **Donor Phone Number** and **Donor Address** if you are donating on behalf of someone else.
11. If you are making an **In-Kind Donation**, select the item(s) you are donating.
12. Add any additional comments about the donation.
13. If you are making an **In-Kind Donation**, choose whether you want a receipt.
14. Finally, add your **Signature** (handwritten or digitally signed).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (<type:text>): Enter your name as you wish to be recognized.
* **Email** (<type:email>): Enter your email address.
* **Donation Type** (<type:select_one>): Select whether this is a Recurring or One-Time donation.
* **Amount** (<type:number>): Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Date** (<type:date>): Choose the date of your donation.
* **Time Donated** (<type:time>): Select the time of your donation (if applicable).
* **Notes** (<type:note>): Add any notes about your donation.
* **In-Kind Donation** (<type:select_multiple>): Check to indicate if you are making an In-Kind donation.
* **In-Kind Description** (<type:text>): Enter a brief description of your In-Kind donation.
* **Donor Name** (<type:text>): Enter the name of the donor (if you are donating on behalf of someone else).
* **Donor Email** (<type:email>): Enter the email of the donor (if you are donating on behalf of someone else).
* **Donor Phone Number** (<type:text>): Enter the phone number of the donor (if you are donating on behalf of someone else).
* **Donor Address** (<type:text>): Enter the address of the donor (if you are donating on behalf of someone else).
* **In-Kind Item** (<type:select_multiple>): Select the item(s) you are donating (if you are making an In-Kind donation).
* **In-Kind Value** (<type:text>): Enter the value of the item(s) you are donating (if you are making an In-Kind donation).
* **Donor Comments** (<type:text>): Add any comments about the donation.
* **In-Kind Receipt** (<type:select_one>): Choose whether you want a receipt for your donation.
* **Donor Signature** (<type:note>): Sign your name (handwritten or digitally signed).
* **Signature Type** (<type:select_one>): Choose whether your signature is Handwritten or E-Signed.
