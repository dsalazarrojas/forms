# University Hospital Research Wing Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The University Hospital Research Wing Donation Form is a form designed to collect donations for research purposes from various sources, including individuals and organizations. This form helps the hospital's research wing to track and manage donations, ensuring accurate records and efficient processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Select "Yes" or "No" for the donation type, donor type, and other relevant fields as needed.
3. Provide your name, email, and phone number.
4. Enter the donation amount.
5. Choose a payment method and, if necessary, select the payment processor for youth donations.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments.
7. Assign a file and tool for further processing, if required.
8. Ensure the donation date and donor acknowledgement details are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Type**: Select "Yes" if you are making a donation, "No" otherwise. 
* **Donor Type**: Choose whether you are an individual or an organization.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method, such as bank transfer, credit card, etc.
* **Payment Processor** and **Payment Processor - Youth**: Select "Yes" if you require a payment processor, "No" otherwise.
* **Donor Acknowledgement**: Select "Yes" if you wish to receive an acknowledgement email or phone call.
* **Donation Date**: Enter the date of your donation.
* **Donor Acknowledgement Email** and **Donor Acknowledgement Phone**: Enter your email and phone number for acknowledgement purposes.
* **Donor Acknowledgement Note**: Provide any additional comments or notes for the acknowledgement.
* **Donor Acknowledgement File**: Upload any relevant file for further processing.
* **Donor Acknowledgement File Name**: Enter a name for the uploaded file. 

## Tips
- Ensure accurate and complete information for efficient processing and tracking.
- Review the form for any errors before submission.
- Contact the Research Wing for any assistance or clarification.
