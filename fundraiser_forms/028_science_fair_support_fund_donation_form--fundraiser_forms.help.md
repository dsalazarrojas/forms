# Science Fair Support Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Fair Support Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations for local science fair participants. Fill out this form to make a one-time donation to support our students' science endeavors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely to ensure that your donation is processed correctly.
2. Provide your name and email address to contact you for confirmation and updates.
3. Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Science Fair Support Fund.
4. If you have a phone number, please include it to help us contact you if needed.
5. Choose the method by which you would like to make your donation (Online or Offline).
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, feel free to include them in the "Additional Comments" section.
7. You can also provide more details about the science fair you are supporting (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Please enter your full name to identify yourself as a donor.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Science Fair Support Fund. This can be any whole number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number**: If you have a phone number, please enter it to help us reach you if needed.
* **Donation Method**: Choose how you would like to make your donation (Online or Offline).
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please include them here.
* **Donation Amount Details**: You can provide more details about the science fair you are supporting (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Other).
* **Science Fair Details**: You can select multiple science fair categories you would like to support (e.g., Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Other).
* **Donation Method**: You can choose between Online or Offline donations. Please select one of these options to proceed with your donation.
