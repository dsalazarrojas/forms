## Fundraising Event Filming Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Event Filming Consent Form is designed to obtain consent from participants for filming and photography at a fundraising event. This form ensures that attendees are aware of and agree to the capturing of their image or likeness for promotional purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Choose whether you are willing to be filmed "With permission" or "Without permission".
3. If you choose "With permission", select which types of filming you are comfortable with (Photos, Video, or both).
4. You may add any additional comments or notes in the "Additional comments" section.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Please enter your email address as it appears in official records.
* **Filming** (`filming_informal`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are willing to be filmed. Options are:
	+ "With permission" - You are willing to be filmed with your consent.
	+ "Without permission" - You are not willing to be filmed.
* **Filming** (`filming_formal`, select_multiple, optional): If you chose "With permission", select which types of filming you are comfortable with. Options are:
	+ "Photos" - You are comfortable with still images (photos) being taken.
	+ "Video" - You are comfortable with video recording being made.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): You may add any additional comments or notes about your consent.
