# law_school_scholarship_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The law school scholarship fund donation form is used to collect information from donors for the law school scholarship fund. It includes fields for donor name, email, address, donation amount, payment method, date, time, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name as the donor.
2. Provide your email address.
3. If you have an address, enter it. This is optional.
4. Enter the amount of your donation.
5. Select the payment method you used to make the donation.
6. Enter the date of the donation.
7. If you want to specify a time for the donation, enter it. This is optional.
8. Select the status of your payment (Pending, Complete, or Declined).
9. If you have a payment ID, enter it. This is optional.
10. If you have a donor ID, enter it. This is optional.
11. Add any additional notes you'd like to include with your donation.
12. If you used an other payment method, describe it.
13. Add any notes you'd like to include with your donation.
14. If you have any additional donation, specify the amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`1`, `text`, required): This is the name of the person making the donation.
* **email** (`2`, `email`, required): This is your email address.
* **Address** (`3`, `note`, optional): This is the address of the person making the donation. This field is optional, but it's useful for the law school to contact you.
* **Amount** (`4`, `number`, required): This is the amount of the donation you're making.
* **Payment Method** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the method you used to make the donation (Cash, Check, Credit Card).
* **Date** (`6`, `date`, required): This is the date of the donation.
* **Time** (`7`, `time`, optional): If you want to specify a time for the donation, enter it here.
* **Payment Status** (`8`, `select_one`, optional): This is the status of your payment (Pending, Complete, Declined).
* **Payment ID** (`9`, `number`, optional): If you have a payment ID, enter it here.
* **Donor ID** (`10`, `text`, optional): If you have a donor ID, enter it here.
* **Note** (`11`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes you'd like to include with your donation.
* **Other Payment Method** (`12`, `text`, optional): If you used an other payment method, describe it here.
* **Donor Note** (`13`, `note`, optional): Add any notes you'd like to include with your donation.
* **Additional Donation** (`14`, `number`, optional): If you have any additional donations, specify the amount here.
