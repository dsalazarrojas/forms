# Fundraising Campaign Success Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and data from participants and fundraisers to help improve future fundraising campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

*   Read the introduction to understand the form's purpose and context.
*   Answer each section honestly and thoroughly, providing valuable insights and feedback.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Introduction**: This section is a brief introduction to the form and its purpose.
    *   This section is not required, but it's recommended to read it to understand the form's context.
*   **Participant Feedback**: This section collects feedback from participants, providing valuable insights into their experiences and thoughts about the campaign.
    *   Please answer this section honestly and thoroughly, providing feedback about your experiences with the campaign.
*   **Donation Amount**: This section asks for the amount of donation made for the campaign.
    *   Please enter the exact amount of donation made.
*   **Participant Donors**: This section asks if participants have any donors or supporters who have contributed to the campaign.
    *   Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' if you have donors or supporters.
*   **Participant Satisfied/Participant Satisfaction**: This section assesses participant satisfaction with the campaign, including their level of satisfaction and whether they would recommend the campaign.
    *   Please choose 'Satisfied' or 'Dissatisfied' based on your experiences with the campaign.
*   **Participant Comments**: This section allows participants to provide additional comments about their experiences.
    *   Please provide any additional comments or insights you think will be helpful.
*   **Fundraiser Feedback**: This section collects feedback from the fundraiser, providing insights into their experiences and thoughts about the campaign.
    *   Please answer this section honestly and thoroughly, providing feedback about your experiences with the campaign.
*   **Fundraiser Comments**: This section allows fundraisers to provide additional comments about the campaign.
    *   Please provide any additional comments or insights you think will be helpful.
*   **Participant Referral Count**: This section asks participants to report the number of referrals they have made.
    *   Please enter the exact number of referrals made.
*   **Fundraiser Referral Count**: This section asks fundraisers to report the number of referrals they have made.
    *   Please enter the exact number of referrals made.
*   **Participant Satisfaction Score**: This section assesses participant satisfaction with the campaign, on a numerical scale.
    *   Please enter a numerical value between 1 and 5 that reflects your satisfaction with the campaign.
*   **Participant Next Steps Frequency**: This section asks participants to report how frequently they will take next steps with the campaign.
