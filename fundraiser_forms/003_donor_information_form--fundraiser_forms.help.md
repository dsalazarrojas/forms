## Donor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Information Form is a crucial document that captures essential details about donors, helping us tailor our fundraising efforts to meet their specific needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To ensure accurate and complete data is collected about the donor, please follow these steps:
*   Fill out the form with your first name. Please note that this field is not required, so you can leave it blank if you prefer not to share your first name.
*   Provide your last name as required.
*   Enter a valid email address for communication and follow-up purposes.
*   If you have a phone number, please provide it for further updates and information.
*   Select your donor type: Individual, Organization, or Foundation.
*   Specify the amount you wish to donate.
*   Choose your donation frequency: One Time, Regular, or Ongoing.
*   Enter your full address, city, and state for accurate location-based communication.
*   Enter your zip code for postal and shipping purposes.
*   You can add any notes about yourself or your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (optional): Enter your first name for our records.
*   **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name for accurate identity verification.
*   **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address for further updates and communication.
*   **Phone Number** (optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up calls or further information.
*   **Donor Type** (required): Select your donor type: Individual, Organization, or Foundation.
*   **Donation Amount** (required): Enter the amount you are willing to donate.
*   **Donation Frequency** (required): Choose the frequency of your donation: One Time, Regular, or Ongoing.
*   **Address** (optional): Enter your address for postal updates, shipping, or location-based services.
*   **City** (optional): Enter your city for precise location-based services.
*   **State** (optional): Enter your state of residence.
*   **Zip Code** (optional): Enter your zip code for accurate postal and shipping services.
*   **Notes** (optional): Add any notes or comments about your donation or your preferences.
