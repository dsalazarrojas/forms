# PayPal Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a tool for nonprofits to collect donations from their supporters. It allows users to provide their information, specify the type and frequency of their donations, and schedule when they want to start and end their donations. 

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin the donation process.
2.  Fill out the required information fields with your details.
3.  Select your preferred donation type and frequency from the respective dropdown options.
4.  Specify the amount you wish to donate.
5.  Choose your preferred start and end dates for your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the donation receipt.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address. This will be used to communicate with you about your donation.
* **Donation Type** (`donation_type`, Select One, Optional): Choose between "One-Time" and "Recurring" donations.
* **Amount** (`amount`, Number, Optional): Specify the amount you wish to donate. 
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, Select One, Optional): Choose how frequently you want to donate. Options are "Weekly", "Bi-Weekly", and "Monthly".
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you wish to start making your donation. This will help us schedule your donations.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): If you've chosen a "Recurring" donation, enter the date you want to end your donations.
* **Frequency End Date** (`frequency_end_date`, Date, Optional): If you've chosen to donate "Bi-Weekly" or "Monthly" and you want your donation to end at a specific date, enter it here. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you encounter any errors or issues while filling out the form, please contact our support team.
* We appreciate your support and contribution to our nonprofit organization.
