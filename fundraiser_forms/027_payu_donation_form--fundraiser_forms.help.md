# payu_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donators for donations, which is used to process and manage donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Donator Name** in the field labeled "Donator Name". This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Donator Email** in the field labeled "Donator Email". Please ensure it is a valid email address. This is a required field.
3. If you have a **Donator Phone**, fill it in the field labeled "Donator Phone". This is an optional field.
4. Enter the **Amount Donation** in the field labeled "Amount Donation". This is the amount you are willing to donate. This is a required field.
5. If you have a **Purpose of Donation**, fill it in the field labeled "Purpose of Donation". This is an optional field.
6. Choose your **Payment Method** from the available options (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer). This is a required field.
7. Select the **Donation Status** from the available options (Pending, Accepted, Declined, or Canceled). This is a required field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donator Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Donator Email** (2, email, required): Enter a valid email address in this field.
* **Donator Phone** (3, text, optional): If you have a phone number, fill it in this field.
* **Amount Donation** (4, number, required): Enter the amount you are willing to donate in this field.
* **Purpose of Donation** (5, note, optional): If you want to specify the purpose of your donation, fill it in this field.
* **Payment Method** (6, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer).
* **Donation Status** (7, select_multiple, required): Select the status of your donation from the available options (Pending, Accepted, Declined, or Canceled).

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about the payment method, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
* If you have any other questions or concerns, you can contact us at our support email or phone number.
