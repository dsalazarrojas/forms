# COVID 19 Response Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for COVID-19 response efforts. It will be used to gather information about the donor, including their contact information, donation amount, and designation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Enter your donation date (mm/dd/yyyy).
3. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address.
4. You can choose not to fill in your phone number, address, and other details if you prefer.
5. Select the donation amount you want to donate.
6. Choose how you would like to pay (e.g., credit card, PayPal).
7. If you have a credit card, select the type (e.g., Visa, MasterCard).
8. If you have a credit card, enter the 16-digit card number.
9. Enter your expiration date (mm/yy).
10. If you have a credit card, enter the 3 or 4 digit CVV code.
11. Choose whether your billing address is the same as your home address.
12. If it's not the same, enter your billing address details.
13. Check if your employer offers donation matching.
14. Select whether you'd like to be recognized in our donor honor roll.
15. Choose whether you'd like to receive our newsletter and updates.
16. If you'd like, choose a dedication for your donation (e.g., in honor or memory of someone).
17. Enter the name of the person being honored (if you chose a dedication).
18. Enter any additional comments or questions.
19. Agree to the terms and conditions, and confirm that your donation is tax-deductible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Date**: Enter the date of your donation in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for receipt and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Optional. If you choose not to enter, you can still fill out the form.
* **Street Address**: Optional. If you choose not to enter, you can still fill out the form.
* **City**: Optional. If you choose not to enter, you can still fill out the form.
* **State**: Optional. If you choose not to enter, you can still fill out the form.
* **ZIP Code**: Optional. If you choose not to enter, you can still fill out the form.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to donate (e.g., one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **How would you like your donation to be used (Fund designation)**: Choose how you'd like your donation to be used (e.g., where most needed, COVID-19 relief efforts, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay (e.g., credit card, PayPal, bank transfer).
* **Card Network**: If you have a credit card, select the type (e.g., Visa, MasterCard).
* **Card Number**: If you have a credit card, enter the 16-digit card number.
* **Expiration Date**: If you have a credit card, enter the expiration date in mm/yy format.
* **CVV**: If you have a credit card, enter the 3 or 4-digit CVV code.
* **Is billing address the same as above**: If your billing address is not the same as your home address, choose "no".
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address if it's different from your home address.
* **Billing City**: Enter the city of your billing address.
* **Billing State**: Enter the state of your billing address.
* **Billing ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your billing address.
* **Does your employer offer donation matching**: Check if your employer offers donation matching.
* **Would you like to be recognized in our donor honor roll**: Choose whether you'd like to be recognized in our donor honor roll.
* **Would you like to receive our newsletter and updates**: Choose whether you'd like to receive our newsletter and updates.
* **Would you like to dedicate this donation**: Choose whether you'd like to dedicate this donation (e.g., in honor or memory of someone).
* **Dedication Name**: If you'd like to dedicate this donation, enter the name of the person being honored.
* **Dedication Message**: Enter any optional message for the dedication.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions and understand my donation is tax-deductible**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions and understand that your donation is tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.
