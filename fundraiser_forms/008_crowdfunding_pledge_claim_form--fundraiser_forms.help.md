# Crowdfunding Pledge Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from supporters and donors who want to claim a pledge made in a crowdfunding campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and email address as a supporter or donor.
* Select the correct campaign and pledge amount you are claiming.
* Provide your phone number and address (if applicable).
* Add any additional notes or messages about your pledge.
* Select your supporter or donor category and status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supporter Name** (`supporter_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a supporter.
* **Supporter Email** (`supporter_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as a supporter.
* **Pledge Date** (`pledge_date`, `date`, optional): Optional date you made your pledge. If not provided, we will use the campaign end date.
* **Pledge Amount** (`pledge_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the amount you have pledged.
* **Supporter Phone** (`supporter_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number (if applicable).
* **Supporter Address** (`supporter_address`, `text`, optional): Provide your address (if applicable).
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes or messages about your pledge.
* **Supporter Notes** (`supporter_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your supporter profile.
* **Supporter Type** (`supporter_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your supporter type (Individual, Organization, Business, or Other).
* **Supporter Status** (`supporter_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your supporter status (Active, Pending, or Archived).
* **Supporter Category** (`supporter_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select your supporter category (New, Existing, or Both).
* **Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, `text`, required): Select the campaign you have pledged to.
* **Campaign Type** (`campaign_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the campaign type (Donation, Pledge, or Auction).
* **Campaign Status** (`campaign_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the campaign status (Active, Completed, or Archived).
* **Campaign Category** (`campaign_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select the campaign category (Fundraiser, Donation, or Pledge).
* **Campaign Goal** (`campaign_goal`, `number`, required): Enter the goal amount of the campaign.
* **Campaign Currency** (`campaign_currency`, `select_one`, required): Select the currency of the campaign (USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **Campaign Total** (`campaign_total`, `number`, required): Enter the total amount raised in the campaign.
* **Campaign Target** (`campaign_target`, `number`, required): Enter the target amount of the campaign.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a donor.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address as a donor.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your phone number (if applicable).
* **Donor Address** (`donor_address`, `text`, optional): Provide your address (if applicable).
* **Donor Notes** (`donor_notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about your donor profile.
* **Donor Category** (`donor_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select your donor category (Individual, Organization, Business, or Other).

Remember to fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure we can follow up with you and potentially reward your contribution.
