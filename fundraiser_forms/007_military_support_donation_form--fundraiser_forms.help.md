# Military Support Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for providing support to a military-related organization through a one-time or recurring donation. It will help the organization keep track of donations and donors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the donation fields with your relevant information.
2. Select your desired donation frequency from the options provided.
3. If you have any other donation preferences or details, you may provide them in the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you'd like to appear in our records.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, provide your phone number.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose from "Monthly", "One-time", "Quarterly", or "Yearly" to specify how often you'd like to support.
* **Other** (`donation_frequency_other`, text, optional): If your donation frequency doesn't match the provided options, describe your preferred frequency here.
* **Other Details** (`donation_frequency_other_description`, text, optional): If "Other" is selected, provide additional details about your chosen frequency.
* **Address** (`address`, note, optional): You can provide any relevant contact or address information to aid in communication or shipping if need be.
