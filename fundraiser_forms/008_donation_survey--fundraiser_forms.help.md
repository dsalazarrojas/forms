# Donation Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Survey form is used to collect information from donors about their donations. This form helps us understand our donors' demographics, donation preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Donation Survey form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out all required fields**: Some fields are marked as "required," which means you must fill out these fields to submit the form. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting.
2. **Enter information for each field**: Fill out each field with accurate and relevant information. If a field is labeled as "select_one" or "select_multiple", choose the applicable option(s) from the list.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click the submit button to send your information to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information**: This field collects your name and other contact information.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Donation Type**: Choose whether your donation is a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select whether your donation is a one-time or recurring event.
* **Donation Date**: Enter the date you made your donation.
* **Donation Time**: Enter the time you made your donation.
* **Additional Information**: Use this field to provide any additional details about your donation.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* If you experience any technical issues while filling out the form, try refreshing the page or contacting our support team.
