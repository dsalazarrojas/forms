# Responsive Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Charity Donation Form is designed to allow donors to make easy and straightforward charitable donations. It helps donors provide their details and preferences in a clear and structured way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Donor Name in the "Donor Name" field.
2. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. This is required for us to contact you.
3. If you have a preferred phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your preferred donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
6. Select your preferred currency in the "Currency" field.
7. Choose how often you would like to make donations in the "How often would you like to give?" field.
8. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can enter them in the "Notes (optional)" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name (Donor Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about your donation.
* **Phone (Phone)**: If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it so we can contact you.
* **Address (Address)**: Enter your home or work address where we can send any further information.
* **Donation Amount (Donation Amount)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate. You can enter numbers without decimal points (e.g., 10 for $10).
* **Currency (Currency)**: Select your preferred currency for the donation. Currently, you can choose between USD, EUR, and GBP.
* **How often would you like to give? (How often would you like to give?)**: Choose how often you would like to make donations. Select all the frequencies that apply to you.
* **Notes (optional) (Notes (optional))**: Enter any additional comments or information about your donation.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate as possible when filling in your details.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us and we will be happy to help.
* You can always contact us if you need to make changes to your donation preferences.
