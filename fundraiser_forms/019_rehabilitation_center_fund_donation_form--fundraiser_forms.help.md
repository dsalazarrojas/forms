# Rehabilitation Center Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rehabilitation Center Fund Donation Form is used for users to contribute money or other resources to support the center's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Enter the desired donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Provide your name in the "Donor Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Donor Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Donor Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred donation method from the "Donation Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: This field requires you to enter the amount you wish to donate to the Rehabilitation Center. It is a numeric field and will not accept text. Please enter the exact amount you are willing to donate.
* **Donor Name (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: This field is used to collect your name as the donor. Please provide your full name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Donor Email (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: This field is your email address where we may contact you regarding your donation or to send updates about our activities.
* **Donor Phone (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: This field is your phone number, which we may use to contact you if we need to confirm any details.
* **Donation Method (<name>, <type>, required/optional)**: Select one or more methods through which you would like to make your donation. You can choose from "Online", "In Person", or "Mail" options. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the contact information provided on our website or through the form.
* If you are unable to complete the form, you may contact us and we will assist you.
