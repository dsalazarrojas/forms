# square_sponsor_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record information about a square sponsorship donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select whether or not you have made a square sponsorship donation on the first page.
2.  Enter your name in the second page if you have made a square sponsorship donation.
3.  Enter the amount of the donation on the third page.
4.  Select whether or not you have made regular donations on the fourth page.
5.  Select the type of donation on the fifth page.
6.  Enter the date of the donation on the sixth page.
7.  Enter the time of the donation on the seventh page.
8.  Add any additional comments or notes on the eighth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **square_sponsor_donation_form_page** (`page 1`, select_multiple, not required): Select the status of your square sponsorship donation.
* **sponsor_name** (`sponsor_name`, text, not required): Enter your name to be associated with this donation.
* **donation_amount** (`donation_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **donation_frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, not required): Select whether you make regular donations.
* **donation_type** (`donation_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of donation.
* **date_of_donation** (`date_of_donation`, date, not required): Enter the date of the donation.
* **time_of_donation** (`time_of_donation`, time, not required): Enter the time of the donation.
* **note** (`note`, note, not required): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.

## Tips
This form is for your convenience. Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
