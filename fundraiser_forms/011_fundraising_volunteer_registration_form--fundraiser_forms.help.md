# fundraising_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fundraising volunteer registration form is used for volunteers to provide their information and details about their volunteer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Volunteer Info** section with your details.
2. Provide information about the **Event Info** you are interested in volunteering for.
3. Enter your **Contact Info**, including your **Email** and **Phone**.
4. Enter the **Volunteer Hours** you have committed to volunteering for.
5. Select your preferred **Volunteer Shirts** and **Volunteer Hoodies** sizes.
6. Enter the **Event Dates** of the event you are volunteering for.
7. Enter the **Volunteer Hours Earned** and **Volunteer Hours Available**.
8. If you have any **Volunteer Hours Conflict**, please indicate the amount.
9. Add any additional **Notes** about your volunteer experience.
10. Finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Info** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and other basic information about yourself.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the event you are interested in volunteering for, such as the name of the event and the date range.
* **Contact Info** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email and phone number so we can contact you.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you have committed to volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Shirts** (`volunteer_shirts`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the size of the shirt you want for your volunteer work. You can choose from Small (S), Medium (M), or Large (L).
* **Volunteer Hoodies** (`volunteer_hoodies`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the size of the hoodie you want for your volunteer work. You can choose from Small (S), Medium (M), or Large (L).
* **Event Dates** (`event_dates`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the dates of the event you are volunteering for.
* **Volunteer Hours Earned** (`volunteer_hours_earned`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you have earned from volunteering.
* **Volunteer Hours Available** (`volunteer_hours_available`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of hours you have available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Hours Conflict** (`volunteer_hours_conflict`, `number`, required/optional): If you have any conflict, enter the number of hours you are unable to work.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or information about your volunteer experience.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
