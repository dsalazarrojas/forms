# Campaign Fundraising Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a crucial document that outlines the essential aspects of your campaign, including its purpose, methods, and financial information. Completing this form ensures transparency and compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully, using the information specific to your campaign.
2. Ensure you haven't already submitted a similar declaration for this campaign.
3. Review each field carefully and answer all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: The full legal name of your organization, as registered with the relevant authorities.
* **Organization Address**: The street address of your organization.
* **City**: The city or town where your organization is located.
* **State**: The state or province where your organization is located.
* **ZIP Code**: The postal code of your organization.
* **EIN or Tax ID Number**: The Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Tax ID number assigned to your organization.
* **Contact Person Name**: The full name of the person handling the campaign.
* **Contact Person Title**: The job title of the contact person.
* **Contact Email**: The email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: The phone number of the contact person.
* **Campaign Name**: The official name of your campaign.
* **Campaign Purpose**: Select one of the provided options to describe the purpose of your campaign.
* **Fundraising Goal Amount**: The target amount you aim to raise.
* **Fundraising Start Date**: The date when your campaign is expected to start.
* **Fundraising End Date**: The date when your campaign is expected to end (if applicable).
* **Fundraising Methods**: Select all applicable methods you will use to solicit funds.
* **Type of Solicitation**: Choose how you will solicit funds (public, private, or a mix).
* **Are You Using a Professional Fundraiser?**: Answer whether you are using a professional fundraiser or not.
* **If applicable, Professional Fundraiser Name**: The name of the professional fundraiser (if applicable).
* **Fundraiser Fee Structure**: Describe the fee arrangement with the professional fundraiser (if applicable).
* **How Will Funds Be Used**: Describe how the funds will be used.
* **Percentage of Funds Going to Program**: Estimate the percentage of funds going to the program.
* **Will You Provide Financial Reports to Donors?**: Answer whether you will provide financial reports to donors.
* **How Will You Acknowledge Donors?**: Select all applicable methods to acknowledge donors.
* **Are You Compliant with State Solicitation Laws?**: Answer whether you are compliant with state solicitation laws.
* **Charity Registration Status**: Select the current status of your charity registration.
* **Declaration Statement**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Authorized Signature**: Type your full name as the authorized person signing the declaration.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date of signing.
