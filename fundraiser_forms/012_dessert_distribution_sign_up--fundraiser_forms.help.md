# Dessert Distribution Sign Up - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you sign up for the dessert distribution event. It will guide you through the necessary information needed to help us prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Select all the options that apply to you from the "Volunteer Info" section.
2. Enter your email address in the "Volunteer Email" field.
3. Provide details about the dessert you plan to distribute in the "Dessert Info" field.
4. Specify the name of the dessert in the "Dessert Name" field.
5. Choose the quantity of the dessert to distribute from the "Quantity" field.
6. Enter a brief description of the dessert in the "Item Description" field.
7. Select the volunteer(s) you'd like to join you from the "Volunteer" field.
8. Choose if you will be distributing the dessert "Yes" or "No" from the "Volunteer List" field.
9. Select the type of dessert you plan to distribute from the "Dessert" field.
10. Choose the number of desserts to distribute from the "Quantity List" field.
11. Provide a brief description of your dessert in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Info**: This section allows you to specify your interest in volunteering for the event.
	+ If you're interested, select "Yes" from the options.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further details.
* **Dessert Info**: Provide any additional details about the dessert you plan to distribute.
* **Dessert Name**: Specify the name of the dessert you're distributing.
* **Quantity**: Choose the number of desserts you plan to distribute.
* **Item Description**: Briefly describe the dessert you're distributing.
* **Volunteer**: Select the volunteer(s) you'd like to join you.
* **Volunteer List**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you'll be distributing with a volunteer.
* **Dessert**: Select the type of dessert you plan to distribute.
* **Quantity List**: Choose the number of desserts to distribute.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your dessert.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you've selected all relevant options for your role and dessert details.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Clearly describe your dessert to help us prepare for the event.
