# small_business_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help small businesses gather funding for their projects or initiatives. It requires detailed information from the business, including contact and business information, donation amount, and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before starting to fill it out.
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in all the fields marked as required.
- If you are unsure about any field, try to enter a value or select an option that best describes your situation.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (id: 1, number, required): Enter the amount of money you are requesting as a donation. This field is required and must be filled out carefully.
* **Donation Frequency** (id: 2, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to receive a donation. Options are One-time and Recurring. If you would like to receive a donation more frequently, select Recurring.
* **Other** (id: 3, text, optional): If you would like to specify a donation frequency other than One-time or Recurring, enter it here. This field is optional and only needed if you selected Other in the previous field.
* **Frequency Other** (id: 4, text, optional): Enter a value for the frequency you selected in the previous field.
* **Email** (id: 5, email, required): Enter your email address. This field is required and must be a valid email address.
* **Business Address** (id: 6, text, optional): Enter your business address.
* **City** (id: 7, text, optional): Enter the city of your business.
* **ZIP** (id: 8, number, optional): Enter the ZIP code of your business.
* **Phone** (id: 9, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your business.
* **Fax** (id: 10, text, optional): Enter the fax number of your business.
* **Contact Name** (id: 11, text, required): Enter the name of the person contacting you.
* **Title** (id: 12, text, optional): Enter the title of the person contacting you.
* **Phone** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person contacting you.
* **Email** (id: 14, email, required): Enter the email address of the person contacting you.
* **Business Type** (id: 15, select_one, required): Choose the type of your business. Options are Small Business, Medium Business, and Large Business.
* **Description** (id: 16, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Payment Method** (id: 17, select_multiple, required): Choose how you would like to receive the donation. Options are Check, Online, and Other. Select all the methods you are comfortable with.
* **Payment Terms** (id: 18, note, optional): Enter any specific payment terms you have.
* **Terms and Conditions** (id: 19, select_one, required): Check if you agree with the terms and conditions.
* **Date** (id: 20, date, optional): Enter the date you would like to receive the donation.
* **Time** (id: 21, time, optional): Enter the time you would like to receive the donation.
* **Note** (id: 22, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
* **Additional Notes** (id: 23, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the donation.
* **Confirm Email** (id: 24, text, optional): Enter a value to confirm your email address.
* **Submit** (id: 25, select_one, required): Choose if you would like to continue to the application form or finish the current form.

Please note that some fields are marked as required and must be filled out carefully. If you are unsure about any field, try to enter a value or select an option that best describes your situation. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
