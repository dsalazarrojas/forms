# Paymentwall Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paymentwall Online Donation Form is designed to allow users to make donations to the Paymentwall platform. This form is not meant for general inquiries or contact, so please do not use it for those purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `complete_name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Select the products you would like to donate to in the `products` field.
4. Enter your contact number in the `contact_number` field.
5. Choose the type of donation you would like to make in the `type_of_donation` field.
6. Add any comments or remarks in the `comment_box` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (`complete_name`, text, required: false)
* Your name will be used to identify you as a donor.

### Email (`email`, email, required: false)
* Enter your email address so we can contact you.

### Products (`products`, select multiple, required: false)
* Select the products you would like to donate to.

### Contact Number (`contact_number`, text, required: false)
* Enter your contact number so we can get in touch with you.

### Type of Donation (`type_of_donation`, select one, required: false)
* Choose the type of donation you would like to make.

### Comment Box (`comment_box`, note, required: false)
* Add any comments or remarks about your donation.

### Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact email].
