# Fund Giving Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for non-profit organizations to request funding from our organization. To ensure a successful application, please fill out the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your non-profit organization from the list of available options.
2.  Enter your contact details, including any relevant information about your organization.
3.  Enter the amount of funding you are applying for, in numerical format.
4.  Describe why you are requesting funding in the text field.
5.  Attach any supporting materials, such as images or pdfs, as needed.

*   Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.

*   Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've filled out all required and optional fields.
