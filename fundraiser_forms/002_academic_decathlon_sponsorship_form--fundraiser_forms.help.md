# Academic Decathlon Sponsorship Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect sponsorship information from organizations or individuals who wish to support Academic Decathlon events. This form helps us understand your level of commitment, preferred method of contribution, and recognition preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `Organization Name`, `Contact Person Name`, `Contact Email Address`, `Contact Phone Number`, `Business Address`, `Desired Sponsorship Level`, `Specific Sponsorship Amount`, `Preferred Payment Method`, `Authorized Signature`, and `Date of Signature`.
2. Select your preferred `Preferred Payment Method` from the available options.
3. Choose how you would like to be recognized by selecting all the relevant options from `Recognition Preferences`.
4. Select the level of sponsorship you are committed to by choosing a value from `Desired Sponsorship Level`.
5. Enter the exact amount you wish to contribute in the `Specific Sponsorship Amount` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Organization Name** (`sponsor_organization_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of your company or organization.

### 2. **Contact Person Name** (`sponsor_contact_person`, text, required)
Enter the full name of the person who will serve as the primary contact.

### 3. **Contact Email Address** (`sponsor_email`, email, required)
Enter the business email for correspondence.

### 4. **Contact Phone Number** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required)
Enter the contact phone number, including area code and extension if applicable.

### 5. **Business Address** (`sponsor_address`, text, required)
Enter the street address, city, state, and zip code.

### 6. **Desired Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, required)
Select the level that corresponds to your commitment:
	* Platinum - $5000+
	* Gold - $2500-$4999
	* Silver - $1000-$2499
	* Bronze - $500-$999
	* Friend - $100-$499
	* Community Supporter - Under $100

### 7. **Specific Sponsorship Amount** (`sponsorship_amount`, number, required)
Enter the exact dollar amount you wish to contribute.

### 8. **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required)
Select how you plan to make the contribution:
	* Check
	* Credit Card
	* Bank Transfer
	* Cash
	* Other

### 9. **Recognition Preferences** (`recognition_preferences`, select_multiple, required)
Select all the ways you would like to be recognized:
	* Logo on event banners
	* Logo in event program
	* Social media mention
	* Website listing
	* Press release mention
	* Stage acknowledgment
	* Booth space at event
	* None

### 10. **Marketing Materials Deadline** (`marketing_materials_deadline`, date, optional)
When do you need your logo or materials submitted by?

### 11. **Terms and Conditions Acknowledgment** (`sponsorship_terms_acknowledged`, select_one, required)
Do you acknowledge that sponsorship funds will be used exclusively for Academic Decathlon activities?:
	* Yes, I acknowledge
	* No, I do not acknowledge

### 12. **Authorized Signature** (`sponsor_signature`, text, required)
Enter your full name as signature.

### 13. **Date of Signature** (`signature_date`, date, required)
Enter the date when this agreement is signed.

### 14. **Tips**
Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us for assistance.
