# Charity Collection Permit Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from charities and collectors about their fundraising activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of form you wish to submit. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific aspect of the collection.
2. Provide your name, job title, and organization name as required.
3. Enter your charity's registration number.
4. Specify the start and end dates of the collection.
5. List all the locations where the collection will take place.
6. Choose the method of collection (e.g. buckets, QR code, merchandise, or ticket sales).
7. Estimate the number of collectors or staff participating.
8. (Optional) Specify the target fundraising amount.
9. Describe the safety and security measures in place to protect collectors and funds.
10. Confirm whether you have active public liability insurance.
11. Confirm that you acknowledge and accept the accuracy of the data provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as the representative of the organization applying for the permit.
* **Job Title or Position** (`applicant_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization you are representing.
* **Charity Registration Number** (`charity_reg_number`, text, required): Enter the charity's registration number.
* **Collection Start Date** (`collection_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the collection.
* **Collection End Date** (`collection_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the collection.
* **Specific Collection Locations** (`collection_locations`, text, required): List all the public areas where the collection will take place.
* **Method of Collection** (`collection_method`, select_one, required): Choose the method of collection (e.g. buckets, QR code, merchandise, or ticket sales).
* **Number of Collectors** (`expected_participants`, number, required): Estimate the number of volunteers or staff participating in the collection.
* **Fundraising Target Amount** (`target_amount`, number, optional): (Optional) Enter the target fundraising amount.
* **Safety and Security Measures** (`safety_plan`, text, required): Describe the safety and security measures in place to protect collectors and funds.
* **Public Liability Insurance** (`public_liability_insurance`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have active public liability insurance.
* **Acknowledge Accuracy of Data** (`acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you acknowledge and accept the accuracy of the data provided.

## Tips
* Ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information in the form.
* Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields or questions, please consult the relevant authorities or seek clarification.
