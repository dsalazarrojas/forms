# Epidemic Response Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Epidemic Response Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations for the Epidemic Response Fund, which supports individuals and communities affected by epidemics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your general information to us so we can better understand who you are.
2. Share your contact details with us so we can reach out to you.
3. Describe the details of your donation so we can properly process it.
4. Enter the payment information to facilitate processing your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (`general_information`, text, required: false):**
	+ Please provide some brief general information about yourself, such as your name, organization, or affiliation.
* **Contact Information (`contact_information`, text, required: false):**
	+ Enter your contact details, including your name, email, phone number, or address, so we can reach out to you about your donation.
* **Donation Information (`donation_information`, text, required: false):**
	+ Describe the details of your donation, including the amount and purpose.
* **Payment Information (`payment_information`, text, required: false):**
	+ Enter the payment information to facilitate processing your donation.
