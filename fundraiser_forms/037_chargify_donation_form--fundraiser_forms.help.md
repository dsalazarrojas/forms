# Chargify Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donor information and donation details for the Chargify platform, making it a vital tool for fundraising purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your donor name in the "Donor Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Donor Email" field.
3. Specify the donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
4. Choose the donation frequency from the "Donation Frequency" dropdown menu (e.g., one time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
5. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal).
6. (Optional) If you wish to make an anonymous donation, select "Yes" in the "Anonymous Donation" field.
7. (Optional) If you would like to receive a tax receipt for your donation, select "Yes" in the "Tax Receipt Needed" field.
8. (Optional) You can also provide a message for the organization in the "Donor Message" field.
9. Finally, if you have a dedicated fund in mind, you can specify it in the "Dedicated Fund" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`Donor Name`, `Text`, `True`): Enter your full name for receipt and record-keeping purposes.
* **Donor Email** (`Donor Email`, `Email`, `True`): Enter your email address to receive a confirmation of your donation.
* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, `Number`, `True`): Specify the amount you wish to donate in your local currency.
* **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, `Select One`, `True`): Choose how frequently you would like to make this donation (e.g., one time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `Select One`, `True`): Select your preferred method of payment (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal).
* **Anonymous Donation** (`Anonymous Donation`, `Select One`, `False`): If you'd like to remain anonymous, select "Yes." If not, select "No."
* **Dedicated Fund** (`Dedicated Fund`, `Text`, `False`): If you'd like to dedicate your donation to a specific program or fund, enter its name here.
* **Tax Receipt Needed** (`Tax Receipt Needed`, `Select One`, `False`): If you would like a tax receipt for your donation, select "Yes."
* **Donor Message** (`Donor Message`, `Text`, `False`): Provide a message for the organization, if you have one.
* **Tax Receipt Needed** (`Tax Receipt Needed`, `Select One`, `False`): If you would like a tax receipt for your donation, select "Yes."
