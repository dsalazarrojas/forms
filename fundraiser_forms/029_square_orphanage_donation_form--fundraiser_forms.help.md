# Square Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Orphanage Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations for a specified orphanage. Fill out this form to make a one-time or recurring contribution to the chosen orphanage.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your donation amount on the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Indicate your preferred donation frequency by selecting one of the options in the "Donations Frequency" field.
3. Choose your preferred payment method(s) for the donation from the "Payment Method" field options.
4. Enter the name of the orphanage you'd like to support.
5. Provide your contact information by filling in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields.
6. Specify the date and time of your donation in the "Donation Date" and "Donation Time" fields.
7. If desired, include a note about your donation in the "Note" field.
8. If applicable, indicate which tool is assigned to the orphanage you're supporting in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate to the orphanage. This can be a one-time or recurring amount.
* **Donations Frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to make your donations. Options include "Monthly", "Weekly", or "One time".
* **Payment Method**: Select the method you'd like to use for the donation, such as "Bank Transfer", "PayPal", or "Check".
* **Orphanage Name**: Please enter the name of the orphanage you're making the donation to.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address to be contacted about your donation or to receive updates about the orphanage.
* **Contact Phone**: Provide a contact phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Donation Date**: Specify the date on which you'd like to make the donation.
* **Donation Time**: Choose the time of day you'd like to make the donation.
* **Note**: Include any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, indicate which tool is assigned to the orphanage you're supporting.
