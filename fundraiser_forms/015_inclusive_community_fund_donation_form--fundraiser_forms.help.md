# Inclusive Community Fund Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Inclusive Community Fund Donation Form is a tool to collect donations for the Inclusive Community Fund, allowing individuals to support community activities and events. This form ensures that donations are properly recorded and acknowledged by the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as required) to ensure a successful submission.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (donor name, text, optional): Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you about your donation and for updates on our community activities.
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Inclusive Community Fund. Please note that we appreciate all donations, big or small.
* **Donation Frequency** (donation frequency, select_one, optional): Choose how often you'd like to make your donation:
	+ **Monthly**
	+ **Quarterly**
	+ **Annually**
* **Recurring Donations** (Recurring Donations, select_multiple, optional): Select if you'd like to make recurring donations:
	+ **True**
	+ **False**
* **Donor Phone** (Donor Phone, text, optional): If you have any phone number that you'd like to be reached back at, please enter it here.
* **Frequency Other** (frequency other, text, optional): If you'd like to specify a different donation frequency that isn't listed above, please enter it here. This could be a one-time donation or any other frequency you prefer.

## Tips
- Be accurate when filling out the form to avoid any errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to us directly.
- Thank you for supporting the Inclusive Community Fund!
