# Alumni Donation Form Worldpay Us Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for alumni to make donations to their alma mater. The form collects information for processing the donation and for sending a receipt to the donor.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your donor type (individual, organization, corporation, foundation, or other)
2. Enter your name and last name
3. Enter your email address for a receipt to be sent
4. Optionally, enter your graduation year
5. Enter your donation amount
6. Select how often you would like to donate (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually)
7. Choose where you would like your donation to be directed
8. Select if you would like to be included in the honor roll
9. Enter your employer name (if you have a matching gift opportunity)
10. Select if your employer offers matching gifts
11. Choose your preferred payment method
12. Enter your card number and expiry date
13. Enter your CVV/security code
14. Enter your billing address and ZIP code

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Type**: Select your classification as an individual, organization, corporation, foundation, or other.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address where you want to receive a receipt.
* **Graduation Year**: Enter your graduation year, or leave blank if not an alumnus.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation in USD.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how often you would like to donate (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Designation**: Choose where you would like your donation to be directed.
* **Honor Roll Recognition**: Select if you want to be included in the honor roll.
* **Employer Name**: Enter your employer name (if you have a matching gift opportunity).
* **Matching Gift Available**: Select if your employer offers matching gifts.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Card Number**: Enter your card number securely.
* **Card Expiry Date**: Enter your card expiry date in MM/YY format.
* **CVV/Security Code**: Enter your 3 or 4-digit security code from the back of your card.
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address for street address.
* **Billing City**: Enter your billing city.
* **Billing State**: Enter your billing state.
* **Billing ZIP/Postal Code**: Enter your billing ZIP code.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any message you would like to share.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select if you agree to the terms and conditions.
