# WorldPay US Donation Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The WorldPay US Donation Template is a form designed to collect donations from donors in the United States. This form helps the organization track and process donations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the donation amount you wish to donate.
2.  Choose the frequency of your donation (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or One-time).
3.  Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
4.  Provide your phone number for any further contact.
5.  Add any additional notes or comments about your donation.
6.  Optionally, enter any donation fees applicable to your donation.
7.  Enter your tax ID (if applicable).
8.  Enter your billing address for any further details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate. This can be any value and is not required for a one-time donation.
* **Frequency of Donations** (`recurring_donation_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you want to make this donation. You can select Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or One-time.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Donor Phone** (`donor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any further contact.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your donation.
* **Donation Fees** (`donation_fees`, number, optional): Enter any fees applicable to your donation. This is not required, but will be taken into consideration during the donation process.
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, text, optional): Enter your tax ID if required for your donation.
* **Billing Address 1** (`billing_address_1`, text, optional): Enter your billing address for further details.
