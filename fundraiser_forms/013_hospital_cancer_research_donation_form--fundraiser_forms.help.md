# Hospital Cancer Research Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hospital Cancer Research Donation Form is used to collect information from donors who wish to support cancer research at the hospital. Fill out this form to provide your details, payment method, donation preferences, and submission details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your name and other details in the **Donor Details** field.
2. Select your preferred payment method: **Credit Card** or **Bank Transfer**.
3. Choose your donation preferences: **Public**, **Private**, or **Both**.
4. Select the research team you would like to support: **Team1**, **Team2**, or **Team3**.
5. Finally, provide any additional comments or details about your submission in the **Submission Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Details** (`<name>`, `text`, required): Provide your name and any other details you would like to share.
* **Payment Details** (`<name>`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred payment method. You can choose between **Credit Card** or **Bank Transfer**.
* **Donation Details** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your donation preferences. You can select **Public**, **Private**, or **Both**.
* **Team** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the research team you would like to support. You can choose **Team1**, **Team2**, or **Team3**.
* **Submission Details** (`<name>`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or details about your submission.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are filling out this form for a genuine purpose and not duplicating previous submissions.
* Double-check your payment method and donation preferences before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our team for assistance.
