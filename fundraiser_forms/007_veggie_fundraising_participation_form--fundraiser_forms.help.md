# veggie_fundraising_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants in a veggie fundraising campaign. It collects information about the participant, their delivery or pickup preferences, and their interest in volunteering. The form helps the organizers of the campaign to manage the participant's data and facilitate the delivery or pickup of the veggies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Veggie Fundraiser" field.
2. Choose your delivery or pickup preference in the "Delivery or Pickup" field.
3. If you selected delivery, enter your address in the "Delivery Details" field.
4. If you selected pickup, choose a date and time in the "Pickup" field.
5. Answer "True" or "False" to indicate if you are interested in volunteering in the "Volunteer Interest" field.
6. If you answered "True" to the previous question, enter your shifts and availability in the "Volunteer Details" field.
7. Enter your email address and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" fields.
8. Optionally, add more details in the "Output" field.
9. Choose the location where you would like to pick up or have your veggies delivered in the "Output Location" field.
10. Select the method of delivery or pickup in the "Output Method" field.
11. Enter your address, city, state, and zip code in the "Output Address" field.
12. Optionally, add more details in the "Output Details" field.
