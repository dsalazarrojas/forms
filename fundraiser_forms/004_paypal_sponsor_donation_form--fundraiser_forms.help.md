# payPal Sponsor Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PayPal Sponsor Donation Form is a tool for individuals to make a donation to our organization using their PayPal account. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter the amount of your donation in the Amount field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name here. 

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name here. 

### Amount
* **Amount** (`Amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the amount of your donation here. This is a numerical field, so please use numbers only. 

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address here. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `user@example.com`). 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields accurately.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting. 
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us.
