# Gift Basket Raffle Ticket Purchase - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed for individuals to purchase raffle tickets for a gift basket, with the goal of raising funds for a charitable or non-profit organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Indicate if you would like to participate in the gift basket raffle.
3. Enter the Quantity of tickets you'd like to purchase.
4. Enter your Email address.
5. Provide your Contact Number.
6. Enter your Address.
7. Enter the City.
8. Enter the State.
9. Enter your Zip code.
10. Select your Country.
11. Choose your Payment Method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
12. Enter the Total Amount to be paid (if payment is made through bank transfer or check).
13. Leave any additional Notes about your purchase.
14. Confirm your Email address.
15. Confirm your Contact Number.
16. Enter the Gift Card Number.
17. Enter the Gift Card PIN.
18. Enter the Gift Card Type.
19. Enter the Gift Card Value.
20. Enter the Gift Card Expiration date (start and end).
21. Enter the Payment Date.
22. Enter the Payment Time.

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

* **Gift Basket**: Indicate if you would like to participate in the gift basket raffle.

* **Email**: Enter your email address as it appears in your identification.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **City**: Enter your city as it appears on your identification.
* **State**: Enter your state as it appears on your identification.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter your country as it appears on your identification.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you'd like to pay for your raffle tickets (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
* **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount to be paid if you're paying through bank transfer or check.
* **Notes**: Leave any additional notes about your purchase.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address again to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your contact number again to ensure accuracy.
* **Gift Card Number**: Enter the gift card number.
* **Gift Card PIN**: Enter the gift card PIN.
* **Gift Card Type**: Enter the type of gift card.
* **Gift Card Value**: Enter the value of the gift card.
* **Gift Card Expiration**: Enter the expiration date of the gift card (start and end dates).
* **Payment Date**: Enter the date you're paying for the gift card.
* **Payment Time**: Enter the time of payment.
