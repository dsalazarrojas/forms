# professional_certification_assistance_donation_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from individuals seeking assistance for professional certifications. The form is designed to gather contact information and donation amount for the purpose of the certification assistance program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the fields provided.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Indicate the amount you would like to donate for the certification assistance program.
4. Select the purpose of your donation from the drop-down menu.
5. If you have any additional inquiries or comments, type them in the additional_inquiries field.
6. Click the submit button to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us understand who you are.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to help us understand who you are.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number, required/optional): Indicate the amount you would like to donate for the certification assistance program.
* **Purpose of Donation** (`purpose_donation`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the purpose of your donation from the list to help us understand how we can use your support.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required/optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, please enter it here.
* **Additional Inquiries** (`additional_inquiries`, note, required/optional): If you have any questions or comments, type them here.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to send your information and complete the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We appreciate your support for the certification assistance program!
