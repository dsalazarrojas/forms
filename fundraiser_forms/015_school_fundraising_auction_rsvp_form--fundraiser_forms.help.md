# School Fundraising Auction RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about the school fundraising auction event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the event details, including date, time, and location.
2.  Provide your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
3.  Indicate if you can attend the event ("Yes" or "No").
4.  Specify the number of people you plan to bring with you.
5.  Provide their names and relation to you (if applicable).
6.  Add any additional comments or notes about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **event_details** (`event_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe the event details, like date, time, and location.
*   **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide your contact information, like name, email, and phone number.
*   **rsvp_info** (`rsvp_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your RSVP.
*   **rsvp_choice** (`rsvp_choice`, select multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you plan to attend the event, "No" otherwise.
*   **contact_first_name** (`contact_first_name`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your first name.
*   **contact_last_name** (`contact_last_name`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your last name.
*   **event_date** (`event_date`, date, optional): This field is where you can enter the date of the event.
*   **event_time** (`event_time`, time, optional): This field is where you can enter the time of the event.
*   **event_location** (`event_location`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the location of the event.
*   **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is where you can enter your email address.
*   **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number.
*   **rsvp_choice_other** (`rsvp_choice_other`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or notes about your RSVP.
*   **rsvp_comment** (`rsvp_comment`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the event.
*   **rsvp_attendee_count** (`rsvp_attendee_count`, number, optional): This field is where you can specify the number of people you plan to bring with you.
*   **rsvp_attendee_names** (`rsvp_attendee_names`, select one, optional): This field is where you can enter the names of the people you plan to bring with you.
*   **rsvp_attendee_relation** (`rsvp_attendee_relation`, select one, optional): This field is where you can specify the relation of the people you plan to bring with you.
*   **event_description** (`event_description`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe the event in detail.
*   **school_name** (`school_name`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the name of the school.
*   **event_attendee_count** (`event_attendee_count`, number, optional): This field is where you can specify the number of attendees the event is expected.
*   **rsvp_note** (`rsvp_note`, note, optional): This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes about your RSVP.
*   **event_attendee_details** (`event_attendee_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes about the event attendees.
*   **event_type** (`event_type`, text, optional): This field is where you can specify the type of the event.
*   **event_status** (`event_status`, select one, optional): This field is where you can select the status of the event as "Active" or "Inactive".
*   **school_id** (`school_id`, number, optional): This field is not applicable for this form.
*   **event_status_other** (`event_status_other`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or notes about the event status.
