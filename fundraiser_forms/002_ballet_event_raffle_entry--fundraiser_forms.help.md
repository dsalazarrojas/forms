# Ballet Event Raffle Entry - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Ballet Event Raffle Entry form collects information from participants to register for the ballet event raffle. To complete the raffle entry form accurately, you need to fill in the following fields:

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you have all the required fields filled before submitting the form.
2. If you need help or encounter any issues, refer to the Field-by-Field Explanation section for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address for prize notification and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide your address for event catering or delivery purposes, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **Number of Raffle Tickets** (`ticket_quantity`, number, required): Indicate how many raffle tickets you would like to purchase.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`, select_one, required): Choose the price per ticket you wish to pay. Options include $5, $10, $20, $50, or $100 per ticket.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select how you intend to pay for your tickets. Options include Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Venmo, Cash at Event, or Check.
* **Preferred Prize Category** (`preferred_prize`, select_one, optional): If you win, select the prize category that interests you most. Options include Ballet Performance Tickets, Dance Class Package, Meet and Greet with Dancers, Ballet Merchandise Bundle, VIP Experience Package, or No Preference.
* **Will You Attend the Ballet Event** (`attending_event`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you plan to attend the ballet event. Select Yes, definitely, Maybe, or No, but I want to support.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any food allergies or restrictions, provide details for event catering purposes.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): If you are interested in volunteering at future events, select Yes, contact me, Maybe, or No thank you.
* **Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Choose whether you'd like to receive updates about future ballet events. Select Yes, subscribe me, or No thank you.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Indicate where you learned about this raffle. Options include Social Media, Friend or Family, Email Newsletter, Website, Previous Event, or Other.
* **Additional Donation Amount** (`additional_donation`, number, optional): If you'd like to make an extra donation, enter the amount here.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you believe is essential for us to know.
