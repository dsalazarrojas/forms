# Building Fund Pledge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors who want to pledge their support for a building project. It helps the organization understand the donor's details and preferences for the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Enter the address, city, and state (if available) for your contact information.
3. Choose your preferred currency for the donation.
4. Select your preferred payment method and schedule.
5. Confirm if you want to be recognized for your contribution.
6. Enter the project name you are pledging for.
7. If you are making an anonymous donation, select the corresponding option.
8. Provide any additional information or requests you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Name** (1): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your email address for pledge confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (4): Enter your street address (optional).
* **City** (5): Enter your city name (optional).
* **State or Province** (6): Enter your state or province name (optional).
* **Zip or Postal Code** (7): Enter your postal code (optional).
* **Pledge Amount** (8): Enter the amount you want to pledge.
* **Currency** (9): Select the currency you want to use.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (10): Select how you want to pay (e.g., cash, check, credit card).
* **Payment Schedule** (11): Choose when you want to pay (e.g., single payment, monthly installments).
* **Tax Deductible Status** (12): Confirm if you want a tax-deductible receipt.
* **Organization or Company Name** (13): Enter the name of the organization you are representing (if applicable).
* **Building Project Name** (14): Enter the name of the project you are pledging for.
* **Make Donation Anonymous** (15): Select if you want to remain anonymous.
* **Recognition Preference** (16): Choose how you want to be recognized for your contribution (in public, private, or in the newsletter).
* **Additional Information** (17): Enter any special requests or notes you have.
