# Charitable Giving Strategy Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help non-profit organizations evaluate their current charitable giving strategy and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **organization name**, **assessment date**, **recommended actions**, and **assessor name**.
2. Answer the questions about your current **donor segment focus** and **channels used**.
3. Describe your **key metrics**, **challenges**, and **opportunities**.
4. Review and prioritize your **recommended actions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your non-profit organization or program.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are conducting this assessment.
* **Donor Segment Focus** (`donor_segment_focus`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant donor segments targeted by your organization. Choose from:
	+ Major donors
	+ Monthly donors
	+ One time donors
	+ Corporate sponsors
	+ Foundations
* **Channels Used** (`channels_used`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant channels used for donor outreach. Choose from:
	+ Email
	+ Social media
	+ Events
	+ Direct mail
* **Key Metrics** (`key_metrics`, text, optional): Describe the revenue, retention, and acquisition metrics you use to measure the success of your charitable giving strategy.
* **Challenges** (`challenges`, text, optional): Describe the biggest obstacles you face in meeting your fundraising goals.
* **Opportunities** (`opportunities`, text, optional): Describe opportunities to expand giving or engagement.
* **Recommended Actions** (`recommended_actions`, text, required): Prioritize next steps to improve your charitable giving strategy.
* **Assessor Name** (`assessor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person completing this assessment.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and prioritize your recommended actions carefully.
* Use clear and specific language when describing your key metrics, challenges, and opportunities.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask a colleague or supervisor for guidance.
