# Cultural Fundraising Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Fundraising Night RSVP Form is designed to collect information from attendees and help organizers track RSVPs, gather contact information, and manage payment details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the pages to fill in the required and optional fields.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Select your RSVP status from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**:
	* `title`: This field is not currently used, but it could be used to describe the event.
* **Attendee Info**:
	* `attendee_info`: This field is intended for attendees to provide their basic details, such as name and possibly age or contact number.
* **Contact Information**:
	* `contact_info`: This field is designed to gather additional contact details, including email addresses, phone numbers, or other relevant information.
* **Payment Information**:
	* `payment_details`: This field is expected to collect payment-related information, such as donation amounts or payment methods.
* **RSVP Status**:
	* `rsvp_status`: Please select your RSVP status from the options: Interested, Maybe, or No.

## Tips
* Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask the event organizers for clarification.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact form@gic.mx.
