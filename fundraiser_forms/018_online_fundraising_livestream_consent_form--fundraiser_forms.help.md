# Online Fundraising Livestream Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Fundraising Livestream Consent Form is a crucial document that captures critical information from participants in our online fundraising events. It helps us to collect consent and relevant details from participants, ensuring a seamless and secure experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and consent to proceed.
2. Fill in the participant details section, providing accurate and up-to-date information about the participant.
3. Sign the media release to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms.
4. Upload a photo of the participant, if requested (optional).
5. Provide event details and event date.
6. Enter the participant's contact information, including phone number and email address.
7. Add any additional notes about the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (Page 1)** (`participant_details`, `text`, required): Please fill in the participant's name, age, and any other identifying information that will help us understand who they are and why they're participating.
* **Media Release (Page 2)** (`media_release`, `text`, required): This section requires a signature to acknowledge that the participant understands and agrees to our terms and conditions.
* **Signature (Page 3)** (`signature`, `text`, required): Please sign your name to confirm your commitment to our terms and conditions.
* **Participant Photo (Page 4)** (`participant_photo`, `text`, optional): If requested, please upload a photo of the participant.
* **Event Details (Page 5)** (`event_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any relevant information about the event, such as its name and description.
* **Event Date (Page 6)** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event, including the start and end times.
* **Participant Phone (Page 7)** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email (Page 8)** (`participant_email`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Note (Page 9)** (`participant_note`, `text`, optional): Add any additional notes about the participant.

## Tips
* Always ensure that you have obtained the participant's consent before filling out this form.
* Double-check that you've entered accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you're unsure about any section, don't hesitate to reach out for assistance.
* Please note that this form requires completion within a specific timeframe, so try to fill it out as soon as possible.
