# Community Theater Play Development Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate donations for the community theater's play development fund, allowing donors to contribute and support the theater's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the donor's name, email address, and donation amount.
2. Choose a designation for your donation (Play development, Production costs, Scholarships, or General fund).
3. Select your preferred payment method (Credit card, Check, or Bank transfer).
4. Indicate how you would like to be acknowledged (Public recognition, Anonymous, or Private letter).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor full name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Please enter the full name of the person making the donation.
* **Donor email address** (`donor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person making the donation. This will be used for receipt and communication purposes.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the numeric amount of the donation. Please ensure it is accurate and complete.
* **Designation** (`donation_designation`, select_one, optional): Choose a specific designation for your donation, such as Play development, Production costs, Scholarships, or General fund.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method of payment you would like to use (Credit card, Check, or Bank transfer).
* **Acknowledgement preference** (`acknowledgment_preference`, select_one, optional): Indicate how you would like to be acknowledged, such as Public recognition, Anonymous, or Private letter.
* **Comments or dedication** (`comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or a message for the program, please enter them here.
