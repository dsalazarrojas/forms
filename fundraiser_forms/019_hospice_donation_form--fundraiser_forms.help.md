# Hospice Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hospice Donation Form is a tool for individuals or organizations to make donations to support local hospices. This form is used to collect information about the donor, the amount donated, and the purpose of the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Email address in the "User Email" field. This will help us contact you with any updates or confirmations regarding your donation.
2. Select the amount you wish to donate by entering a numeric value in the "Amount Donated" field.
3. In the "Purpose of Donation" field, briefly describe why you are making this donation.
4. If you know it, please enter the name of the hospice you are supporting in the "Name of Hospice" field.
5. Lastly, if you are aware, please enter the address of the hospice you are supporting in the "Address of Hospice" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, Email, required: false): This field is optional, but entering your email will allow us to contact you with updates or confirmations regarding your donation.
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, Number, required: false): Please enter the numeric value of the amount you wish to donate. You can use any positive number (e.g., 10, 20, 50.99).
* **Purpose of Donation** (`purpose_donation`, Text, required: false): Provide a brief description of why you are making this donation (e.g., to support a specific program or service).
* **Name of Hospice** (`name_of_hospice`, Text, required: false): If known, please enter the name of the hospice you are supporting. This will help us direct your donation to the correct location.
* **Address of Hospice** (`address_of_hospice`, Text, required: false): If known, please enter the address of the hospice you are supporting. This will help us confirm receipt of your donation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered accurate information, especially for the Name of Hospice and Address of Hospice fields if known.
* You can enter any positive number in the Amount Donated field.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your donation, please contact us at [insert email or contact information].
