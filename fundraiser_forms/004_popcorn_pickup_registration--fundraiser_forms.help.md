# Popcorn Pickup Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Popcorn Pickup Registration form is designed to collect information from schools and students for a fundraiser event. The form helps event organizers gather necessary details from schools, including school names, contact emails, pickup times, and student names.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **School Name** field with the name of the school.
2. In the **School Email** field, enter the email address of the school.
3. Choose a **Pickup Time** that suits the school's needs.
4. Select a **Pickup Date** for the event.
5. Enter the **Preorder Name** if the school has preordered popcorn.
6. List the **Student Names** separated by commas, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### School Name (`school_name`):
Enter the name of the school participating in the fundraiser event.

### School Email (`school_email`):
Enter the email address of the school for communication purposes.

### Pickup Time (`pickup_time`):
Select the time when the school will pick up the popcorn.

### Pickup Date (`pickup_date`):
Select a date for the pickup event.

### Preorder Name (`preorder_name`):
If the school has preordered popcorn, enter the name associated with the preorder.

### Student Names (`student_names`):
List the names of the students participating in the fundraiser, separated by commas. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Ensure the school name and email are accurate for proper communication.
* Choose a pickup time and date that works best for the school.
* If preordering, provide the preorder name for tracking purposes.
* List student names separated by commas for easy tracking.
* If no students are participating, leave the `student_names` field empty.
