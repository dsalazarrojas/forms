# Quick Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quick Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations from users for various funds and causes. This form allows users to contribute a specific amount to a chosen fund and optionally provide additional information about their affiliation and any other comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the amount you wish to donate in the **Amount** field.
* Select the **Fund** you wish to support from the dropdown list.
* If you have a special affiliation with the cause, enter it in the **Affiliation** field.
* If you have any additional comments or information, type them in the **Message** field.
* If you have a specific amount in mind that is not listed in the dropdown, you can enter it in the **Other** field.
* Click the **Submit** button to complete your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Amount (`amount`, number, required: false)**: The amount you wish to donate. This field is not required, but if you do enter an amount, it will be used to process your donation.
* **Fund (`fund`, select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the fund you wish to support from the list. You can choose one or multiple funds.
* **Affiliation (`affiliation`, text, required: false)**: If you have a special affiliation with the cause, such as being a member or volunteer, enter it here.
* **Other (`other`, number, required: false)**: If you have a specific amount in mind that is not listed in the dropdown, you can enter it here.
* **Message (`message`, text, required: false)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your donation.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter a valid amount in the **Amount** field to complete your donation.
* If you select multiple funds in the **Fund** field, your donation will be split across all selected funds.
* You can leave the **Affiliation** field blank if you do not have a special affiliation with the cause.
* The **Other** field is only necessary if you have a specific amount in mind that is not listed in the dropdown.
* You can leave the **Message** field blank if you do not have any additional comments or information about your donation.
