# Sports Fundraiser Guessing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to participate in a sports fundraiser event by guessing game scores and providing tips and comments. Users are required to provide personal contact information for donation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required personal contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
2. Guess the outcome of the games by selecting the number corresponding to the correct team.
3. If you are not sure of the outcome of any game, you can provide an estimated dollar amount for the entry.
4. Add any additional tips or comments you think might be helpful.
5. Once you have completed the form, submit it.
