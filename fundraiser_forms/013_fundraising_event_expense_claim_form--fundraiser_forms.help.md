# fundraising_event_expense_claim_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to claim expenses for a fundraising event. Fill out the form with the required information to submit a claim for reimbursement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event details, including the event date, location, and type.
2. Check if any expenses were incurred during the event, and fill in the expense amount, description, and date.
3. If applicable, attach a receipt for the expense.
4. Provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5. Click the "Submit" button to submit the claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_details**: The event details, including the event date, location, and type.
* **event_date**: The date of the event.
* **event_location**: The location of the event.
* **event_type**: The type of event.
* **expense_type**: Check this field to indicate if expenses were incurred during the event.
* **expense_amount**: The total expense amount for the event.
* **expense_description**: A description of the expense.
* **expense_date**: The date the expense was incurred.
* **expense_receipt**: If you have a receipt for the expense, attach it here.
* **claimant_name**: Your name.
* **claimant_email**: Your email address.
* **claimant_phone**: Your phone number.
* **submit**: Submit the claim.
* **back**: Go back to the previous page.
* **help**: View help documentation for this form.
* **reset**: Reset the form.
* **cancel**: Cancel the claim.
* **save_draft**: Save a draft of the claim.
* **confirm**: Confirm the claim.
* **delete**: Delete the claim.
* **view**: View previous claims.
* **edit**: Edit the claim.
* **save**: Save the claim.
* **cancel_submit**: Cancel submitting the claim.
* **submit_claim**: Submit the claim.
