# Fundraising Gala Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register and purchase tickets for a fundraising event. It allows event organizers to collect registration information, payment details, and preferences for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields to register your information.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** if you wish to be contacted by the event organizers.
3. Select the **Event Name** of the fundraising gala you are registering for.
4. Choose a **Ticket Price** and the number of **Tickets** you wish to purchase.
5. Enter your **Payment Amount** and select the **Payment Method** (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
6. For those who wish to purchase an additional ticket type, enter the **Ticket Price 2**, **Num Tickets 2**, and **Payment Amount 2** fields, and select the **Payment Method 2**.
7. Add any **Notes** if you have any special requests or messages for the event organizers.
8. Select the date and time of the **Event Date** and **Event Time** for the fundraising gala.
9. Enter the **Event Location** where the fundraising gala will take place.
10. Choose your preferred **Ticket Type** (Vip Ticket, Standard Ticket, or Premium Ticket).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by the event organizers.
* **Event Name**: Select the name of the fundraising gala you are registering for.
* **Ticket Price**: Enter the price of the ticket you are purchasing.
* **Number of Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total amount you are paying for the tickets.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
* **Notes**: Enter any special requests or messages for the event organizers.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the fundraising gala.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the fundraising gala.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the fundraising gala.
* **Ticket Type**: Choose the type of ticket you wish to purchase (Vip Ticket, Standard Ticket, or Premium Ticket).
* **Ticket Price 2**: For those purchasing an additional ticket type, enter the price of the second ticket.
* **Num Tickets 2**: For those purchasing an additional ticket type, enter the number of tickets for the second type.
* **Payment Amount 2**: For those purchasing an additional ticket type, enter the total amount for the second ticket type.
* **Payment Method 2**: Choose how to pay for the second ticket type (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
