# Opt Out Fundraiser Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Opt Out Fundraiser Form is a simple way to notify us of your intention to opt out from a fundraiser. Please fill out the form with the required information to help us keep track of your donation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your Fundraiser Name, the name of the specific fundraiser you wish to opt out from.
3. Provide your name and email address, ensuring we can contact you if needed.
4. If you wish to donate a specific amount, select one of the options listed.
5. If you are an individual, a parent, a school, or an organization, please select the appropriate type.
6. If applicable, choose the type of fundraiser you are supporting.
7. If you have any additional comments or questions, please feel free to include them.
8. Ensure your email address and phone number are accurate for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fundraiser Name**: Please enter the name of the specific fundraiser you wish to opt out from.
* **Donor**: Please enter your name as the donor or representative of the organization.
* **Donation Amount**: Select one of the available donation amounts to indicate your intention.
* **Comments**: If you have any additional comments or questions, please include them here.
* **Type of Donor**: Choose one of the listed options to describe your status as a donor.
* **Type of Fundraiser**: Select one or more of the listed options for the type of fundraiser you are supporting.
* **Event Date**: If applicable, provide the date of the event you are opting out from.
* **Event Time**: If applicable, provide the time of the event you are opting out from.
* **Donor Email**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Donor Phone**: Enter a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Donor Address**: If you would like to provide an address for the donor.
* **Donor City**: Enter the city where the donor resides.
* **Donor State**: Enter the state where the donor resides.
* **Donor Zip**: Enter the donor's postal zip code.
