# BlueSnap Orphanage Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from individuals who want to support the BlueSnap Orphanage's various programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your donor name and email address to be recorded for tax purposes and for us to contact you.
2. Specify the amount you wish to donate to the BlueSnap Orphanage.
3. Choose the program you prefer to support (Education, Healthcare, Food security, or General fund).
4. Decide if you want to make this a recurring donation (monthly).
5. If you are donating in memory or honor of someone, provide their name.
6. Select how you would like to be acknowledged for your donation (Email, Postal mail, or No acknowledgement).
7. If you have any message you would like to include for the recipients or staff, use this field for that purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name for our records.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for tax receipt and future correspondence.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, **required**): Enter the numeric amount you wish to donate.
* **Preferred Program to Support** (`preferred_program`, `select_one`, **optional**): Choose which program you prefer to support (Education, Healthcare, Food security, or General fund).
* **Make this donation recurring** (`recurring`, `select_one`, **required**): Decide if you want to make this donation recurring (monthly).
* **Tribute Name** (`tribute_name`, `text`, **optional**): If donating in memory or honor of someone, enter their name.
* **Acknowledge this gift** (`acknowledgement_method`, `select_one`, **required**): Decide how you want to be acknowledged (Email, Postal mail, or No acknowledgement).
* **Message for Recipients or Staff** (`message`, `text`, **optional**): If you have any message you would like to include for the recipients or staff, use this field.
