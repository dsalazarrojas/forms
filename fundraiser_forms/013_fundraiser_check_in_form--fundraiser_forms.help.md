# Fundraiser Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from fundraisers after they have checked in for an event. The form is used to track attendance, guest information, and other relevant details. Please ensure that this form is completed accurately and consistently to maintain reliable records.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the check-in status from the "Check In Status" dropdown menu.
4. Enter any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. If applicable, enter the number of guests and volunteers who are accompanying you in the "Number of Guests" and "Number of Volunteers" fields.
6. Enter the event code in the "Event Code" field.
7. Provide the check-in location in the "Check In Location" field.
8. Enter the check-in time in the "Check In Time" field.
9. Select the check-in date from the calendar picker or enter the date in the "Check In Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required: No): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required: No): Enter your email address to be contacted.
* **Check In Status** (`check_in_status`, Select, Required: No): Select your check-in status:
	+ Not Checked In
	+ Checked In
	+ Left Early
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Note, Required: No): Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, Number, Required: No): Enter the number of guests accompanying you, if any.
* **Number of Volunteers** (`number_of_volunteers`, Number, Required: No): Enter the number of volunteers accompanying you, if any.
* **Event Code** (`event_code`, Text, Required: No): Enter the event code for the fundraiser.
* **Check In Location** (`check_in_location`, Text, Required: No): Provide the location where you checked in.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, Time, Required: No): Enter the time you checked in.
* **Check In Date** (`check_in_date`, Date, Required: No): Select the date you checked in from the calendar picker or enter the date manually.
