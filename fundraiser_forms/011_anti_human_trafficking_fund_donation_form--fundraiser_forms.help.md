# Anti Human Trafficking Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect donations for the Anti Human Trafficking Fund. To make a donation, simply follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select your donor type: Choose whether you are donating as an individual or as an organization (e.g., business, non-profit, or foundation).
2. Enter your name: Type in your full name as it should appear on the receipt.
3. Enter your email address: Provide your email address for donation receipts and updates.
4. Choose your donation amount: Select the amount you would like to donate.
5. Select your donation frequency: Choose how often you would like to make donations (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
6. Choose which program to support: Select which Anti Human Trafficking program you would like your donation to support.
7. Determine if the donation is a tribute: If you are making a donation in honor or memory of someone, select 'True'.
8. Provide optional details for the tribute donation: If you selected a tribute donation, enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
9. Choose how you would like to be contacted for tribute notifications: If you would like to send a notification to the honoree's family, select 'True'.
10. Enter the email address for tribute notifications: If you selected to send tribute notifications, enter the email address to send the notification to.
11. Select your payment method: Choose how you would like to make your donation (credit card, debit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or check by mail).
12. Enter your card number and expiry date: If you selected credit or debit card as your payment method, enter your 16-digit card number and expiry date in MM/YY format.
13. Enter your CVV or security code: If you selected credit or debit card as your payment method, enter your 3 or 4 digit CVV or security code.
14. Enter your billing address: If you selected bank transfer or check by mail as your payment method, enter your billing address.
15. Select if your employer matches donations: If your employer has a matching gift program, select 'True'.
16. Choose how you would like to be listed: Select how you would like to be listed as a donor (publicly, anonymously, or internal records only).
17. Subscribe to our newsletter: Select if you would like to receive updates on our work.
18. Express your interest in volunteering: Select if you would like to volunteer for the Anti Human Trafficking Fund.
19. Add any additional message or dedication: If you have a special message or dedication, you can enter it here.
20. Accept our terms and conditions: Select 'Yes, I agree' to accept our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Type** (`donor_type`, `select_one`): Choose whether you are donating as an individual or as an organization.
* **Full Name or Organization Name** (`donor_name`, `text`): Enter your full name as it should appear on the receipt.
* **Email Address** (`donor_email`, `email`): Enter your email address for donation receipts and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`donor_phone`, `text`): Enter your contact number (optional).
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, `select_one`): Choose how often you would like to make donations (one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Designate Your Donation** (`designated_fund`, `select_one`): Select which Anti Human Trafficking program you would like your donation to support.
* **Tribute Donation** (`tribute_donation`, `select_one`): If you are making a donation in honor or memory of someone, select 'True'.
* **Honoree or Memorial Name** (`tribute_name`, `text`): If you selected a tribute donation, enter the name of the person being honored or remembered.
* **Send Notification to Honoree Family** (`tribute_notification`, `select_one`): If you would like to send a notification to the honoree's family, select 'True'.
* **Tribute Contact Email** (`tribute_contact_email`, `email`): If you selected to send tribute notifications, enter the email address to send the notification to.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`): Choose how you would like to make your donation (credit card, debit card, PayPal, bank transfer, or check by mail).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, `text`): If you selected credit or debit card as your payment method, enter your 16-digit card number.
* **Expiry Date** (`card_expiry`, `text`): If you selected credit or debit card as your payment method, enter your expiry date in MM/YY format.
* **CVV or Security Code** (`cvv`, `text`): If you selected credit or debit card as your payment method, enter your 3 or 4 digit CVV or security code.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`): If you selected bank transfer or check by mail as your payment method, enter your billing address.
* **City** (`billing_city`, `text`): If you selected bank transfer or check by mail as your payment method, enter your billing city.
* **State or Province** (`billing_state`, `text`): If you selected bank transfer or check by mail as your payment method, enter your billing state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`billing_zip`, `text`): If you selected bank transfer or check by mail as your payment method, enter your billing ZIP or postal code.
* **Will your Employer Match Donations** (`employer_match`, `select_one`): If your employer has a matching gift program, select 'True'.
* **Anonymity Preference** (`anonymity_preference`, `select_one`): Select how you would like to be listed as a donor (publicly, anonymously, or internal records only).
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, `select_one`): Select if you would like to receive updates on our work.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`): Select if you would like to volunteer for the Anti Human Trafficking Fund.
* **Additional Message or Dedications** (`additional_message`, `text`): If you have a special message or dedication, you can enter it here.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`): Select 'Yes, I agree' to accept our terms and conditions.
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, `date`): Enter the date of donation.
