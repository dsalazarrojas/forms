# Square Food Bank Donation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Square Food Bank Donation form is designed to collect information from donors who wish to make a donation to the Square Food Bank. The form will help us track and manage donations, and may be used to follow up with donors for further information or to request additional donations in the future.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Select the type of donation** you wish to make (Option 1 or Option 2).
2. **Enter your organization's name, if applicable** (if you are donating on behalf of an organization).
3. **List any sponsors** associated with your donation (if applicable).
4. **Enter the amount donated** to Square Food Bank.
5. **Enter a reason for your donation** (optional).
6. **Enter a description of your donation** (optional).
7. **Enter your contact information**, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
8. **Enter your email address and phone number**.
9. **Enter your contact person's name**.
10. **Enter your contact person's phone number**.
11. **Enter your organization's website** (if applicable).
12. **Enter your Square Food Bank email**.
13. **Enter your Square Food Bank phone number**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donating** (`square_food_bank_donation`, select_one): Choose between Option 1 and Option 2 for the type of donation you are making.
* **Square Food Bank Name** (`square_food_bank_name`, text): Enter the name of the Square Food Bank you are donating to.
* **Sponsors** (`sponsors`, select_multiple): List any sponsors associated with your donation (if applicable).
* **Amount Donated** (`amount_donated`, number): Enter the amount donated to Square Food Bank.
* **Reason for Donation** (`reason_donated`, note): Enter a reason for your donation (optional).
* **Square Food Bank Description** (`square_food_bank_description`, text): Enter a description of your donation (optional).
* **Organization** (`organization`, text): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Contact** (`contact`, text): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text): Enter your contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text): Enter your contact person's phone number.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`, email): Enter your email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, text): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text): Enter your state.
* **State 2** (`state_2`, text): This field is not used in this form and can be ignored.
* **State Code** (`state_code`, text): This field is not used in this form and can be ignored.
* **Zip 2** (`zip_2`, text): Enter your zip code.
* **Website** (`website`, text): Enter your organization's website (if applicable).
* **Square Food Bank Email** (`square_food_bank_email`, email): Enter your Square Food Bank email address.
* **Square Food Bank Phone** (`square_food_bank_phone`, text): Enter your Square Food Bank phone number.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure your donation is recorded correctly.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
- The form will be reviewed for completeness and accuracy before submission.
- The form will only ask for relevant and necessary information to be completed by the donor.
