# Fundraising Event Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to obtain permission from participants and guardians for a fundraising event. It collects essential information from both participants and their guardians, including participant and guardian contact details, relationship, and emergency contact information. The form also includes sections for participants and guardians to agree to the event terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide participant information, including name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2.  Provide guardian information, including name, phone number, and email address.
3.  Specify the relationship between the participant and guardian.
4.  Provide emergency contact information, including phone number and email address.
5.  Indicate whether the participant has agreed to participate in the event.
6.  Sign the participant's signature section to confirm agreement.
7.  Indicate whether the guardian has agreed to participate in the event.
8.  Sign the guardian's signature section to confirm agreement.
9.  Enter any additional participant notes.
10.  Enter any additional guardian notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Name, Participant Date of Birth, Participant Phone, Participant Email): This section collects essential information about the participant, including their name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
* **Participant Permission** (Participant Agreement, Participant Signature): This section asks the participant to agree to participate in the event and to sign the agreement to confirm their agreement.
* **Guardian Information** (Guardian Name, Guardian Phone, Guardian Email): This section collects essential information about the guardian, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Guardian Permission** (Guardian Agreement, Guardian Signature): This section asks the guardian to agree to participate in the event and to sign the agreement to confirm their agreement.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Relationship, Emergency Phone, Emergency Email): This section collects emergency contact information, including relationship, phone number, and email address.
* **Participant Address**, **Guardian Address**: These sections collect address information for the participant and guardian.
* **Participant Agreement** , **Guardian Agreement**: These sections ask the participant and guardian to agree to participate in the event.
* **Participant Signature** , **Guardian Signature**: These sections ask the participant and guardian to sign to confirm their agreement.
* **Participant Notes**, **Guardian Notes**: These sections are for entering any additional participant or guardian notes.
