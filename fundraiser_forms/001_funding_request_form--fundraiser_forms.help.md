# Funding Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Funding Request Form is used to collect information about funding requests from users. This form is designed to gather necessary details for internal purposes, such as budgeting or resource allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for contact and communication.
4. Enter your Address for delivery or other relevant purposes.
5. Specify the Funding Amount requested.
6. Provide the Budget Duration for the project or activity.
7. Enter the name of the Project or activity.
8. Describe the Project or activity in the Project Description field.
9. Explain the Funding Justification for the request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it is typically spelled.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it is typically spelled.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address as it will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This field is for your address. This will be used for delivery or other relevant purposes.
* **Funding Amount** (`funding_amount`, number, optional): This field is for specifying the funding amount requested. Please enter a valid numerical value.
* **Budget Duration** (`budget_duration`, text, optional): This field is for providing the budget duration for the project or activity. Please enter the duration in a suitable format (e.g., weeks, months, years).
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, optional): This field is for entering the name of the project or activity.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): This field is for describing the project or activity in more detail.
* **Funding Justification** (`funding_justification`, text, optional): This field is for explaining the funding justification for the request.
