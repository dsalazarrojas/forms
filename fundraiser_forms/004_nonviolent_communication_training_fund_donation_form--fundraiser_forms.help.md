# Nonviolent Communication Training Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for the Nonviolent Communication Training Fund. The purpose of this form is to allow individuals to contribute financially to support the development and dissemination of Nonviolent Communication (NVC) principles and training. By filling out this form, users can choose the amount and currency they wish to donate, providing their email address, name, and optional phone number for communication and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field. This field is optional, but it allows you to specify the amount you wish to contribute.
2. Select your preferred currency from the dropdown menu next to the "Currency" field. This field is optional, but it helps us to ensure that your donation is processed correctly.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is an optional field, but it enables us to contact you with updates about your donation and any follow-up communications.
4. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is also an optional field, but it helps us to address you correctly in our communication.
5. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This field is not required for the donation process, but it can be useful for us to contact you in case we need to discuss your donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Donation Amount
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you wish to donate to the Nonviolent Communication Training Fund. This field is optional, and you can enter a numerical value.
### Currency
* **Currency** (`currency`, select_one, optional): Select the currency in which you wish to make your donation. Please choose from USD, EUR, GBP, or JPY.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation and any updates.
### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name so we can address you correctly in our communication.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you to discuss your donation.
