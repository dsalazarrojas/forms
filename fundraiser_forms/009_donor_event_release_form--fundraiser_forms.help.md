# Donor Event Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Donor Event Release Form is a document that outlines the terms and conditions of a donation. It's a crucial agreement between the donor and the recipient that helps prevent disputes and ensures a smooth transaction.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Details** (`event_details`): Provide a brief description of the event, including its title, date, and any other relevant details.
2. **Donor Information** (`donor_info`): Enter your name and contact information so that we can reach out to you with any updates or questions.
3. **Signature** (`signature`): Sign this form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the donation.
4. **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
