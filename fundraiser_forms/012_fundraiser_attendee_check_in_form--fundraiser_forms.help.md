# Fundraiser Attendee Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fundraiser Attendee Check In Form is used to record the attendance status of fundraiser attendees, helping to track who has arrived and when they arrived, and to gather additional relevant information for the fundraiser organizers. This form is an important part of event planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information accurately and completely.
2. Enter your first name and last name in their respective fields.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Select the time you arrive for "check_in_time".
6. Choose whether you have checked in individually or as part of a group with "check_in_type".
7. If you are checking in as part of a group, select all that apply for "check_in_group".
8. Add any additional comments or messages in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome** (`check_in_page`, text, optional): This is the welcome page of the form where you can take a moment to review the form before proceeding.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Check In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, optional): Select the time you arrive at the event.
* **Check In Type** (`check_in_type`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you checked in individually or as part of a group.
* **Check In Group** (`check_in_group`, select_multiple, optional): Select all that apply if you checked in as part of a group.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or messages here.
* **Fundraiser Attendee Check In Form** (`fundraiser_attendee_check_in_form`, text, optional): This field is not used by users but is part of the form metadata.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* The form is an essential part of event tracking and planning; please use it to help us run the event smoothly.
