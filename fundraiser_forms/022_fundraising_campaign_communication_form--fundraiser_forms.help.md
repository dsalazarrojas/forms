# Fundraising Campaign Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather contact information and message from users for fundraising campaigns.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide a message in the space provided.
3. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form 1** (`form_1_name`, text, optional): This field is not a mandatory field and is used to capture any additional information or comments about the form.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This field is used to capture any additional contact information such as phone numbers, addresses, or any other relevant details.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please fill out your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please fill out your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Fill out your address to ensure we can contact you.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required): Select your state from the provided options.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Message** (`message`, text, required): Please enter a brief message.
Note: All fields with `required: true` are mandatory fields and must be filled out.
