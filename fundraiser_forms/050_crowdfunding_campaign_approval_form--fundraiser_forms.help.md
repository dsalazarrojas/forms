# Crowdfunding Campaign Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowdfunding Campaign Approval Form is used to collect and assess the details of a crowdfunding campaign before it is approved for launch.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information for all required fields.
2. For fields not requiring a specific format (e.g., dates), type in the relevant information directly into the field.
3. If a field requires selection from a list, choose the most accurate option from the provided choices.
