# startup_fund_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in supporting startups. The form includes fields for personal details, donation preferences, and startup information.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill in all the required fields accurately and truthfully. 
- Select the correct frequency and duration for your donation.
- Enter your message and any additional notes you would like to share.
- Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Email** (`user_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required: true): Select how often you would like to make your donation (Monthly or One-Time).
* **Frequency Duration** (`frequency_duration`, select_multiple, required: true): Select the duration of your monthly donation (1 month, 3 months, 6 months).
* **Supporter Name** (`supporter_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person or organization making the donation.
* **Supporter Email** (`supporter_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person or organization making the donation.
* **Supporter Phone** (`supporter_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person or organization making the donation.
* **Message** (`message`, note, required: false): Share any additional comments or notes.
* **Startup Name** (`startup_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the startup you are interested in supporting.
* **Startup Description** (`startup_description`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the startup.
* **Donation Status** (`donation_status`, select_one, required: true): Select the status of your donation (Pending, Approved, or Declined).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Startup URL** (`startup_url`, text, required: false): Enter the URL of the startup's website or social media page.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: true): Select the tool assigned to your donation (chatjimmy or Other).
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, required: false): Select whether you created a new donation (Yes or No).
* **Created Date** (`created_at`, date, required: false): Select the date the donation was created.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, select_one, required: false): Select whether your donation was updated (Yes or No).
* **Updated Date 2** (`updated_at_2`, date, required: false): Select the date your donation was updated.
* **Updated Date 3** (`updated_at`, date, required: false): Select the date your donation was updated.
* **Updated By** (`updated_at`, date, required: false): Select whether your donation was updated by someone (Yes or No).
* **User Id** (`user_id`, text, required: false): Enter the ID of the user making the donation.
* **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, text, required: false): Enter the user to whom the donation is assigned.
