# Job Training Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Job Training Donation Form is designed to collect information from donors who wish to support job training programs. By completing this form, you will provide essential details that help us track and manage donations effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Donation Amount** field and enter a valid amount in the corresponding field.
2. Enter your **Donor Name**, as we will use this information to address communications to you.
3. Provide a valid **Donor Email** address where we can reach you.
4. Enter your **Donor Address 1** and **Donor Address 2** to ensure we can contact you if needed.
5. Write a brief **Donation Description** to help us understand the purpose of your donation.
6. Choose the **Training Program** you wish to support (optional).
7. Select the **Frequency of Donations** (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly, optional).
8. Enter the **Date of Donation** and **Time of Donation** for accurate tracking.
9. Provide your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
10. Leave **Additional Comments** if you have any specific requests or details.
11. If you wish, select the **Donation Status** (Active or Inactive).
12. Indicate whether you are a **Donor ID** (Yes or No, optional).
13. Choose your **Donor Type** (Individual or Organization, optional).
14. Select your **Payment Method** (Online or Offline, optional).
15. Select your preferred **Currency** (USD or EUR, optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our communication.
* **Donor Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Donor Address 1** and **Donor Address 2**: Enter your full address for our records.
* **Donation Description**: Briefly describe the purpose of your donation.
* **Training Program**: Choose a job training program you wish to support.
* **Frequency of Donations**: Select how frequently you wish to donate (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Date of Donation**: Enter the date of your donation for accurate tracking.
* **Time of Donation**: Enter the time of your donation for accurate tracking.
* **Phone Number**: Provide a valid phone number for contact purposes.
* **Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments or details you wish to share.
* **Donation Status**: Select whether your donation is Active or Inactive.
* **Donor ID**: Indicate whether you have a donor ID (Yes or No).
* **Donor Type**: Choose whether you are an Individual or Organization.
* **Payment Method**: Select whether you will pay Online or Offline.
* **Currency**: Select your preferred currency (USD or EUR).
