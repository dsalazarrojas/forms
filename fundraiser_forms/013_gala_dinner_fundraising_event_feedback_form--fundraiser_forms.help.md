# Gala Dinner Fundraising Event Feedback Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect feedback on the gala dinner event, and we would be grateful if you could take a few minutes to share your thoughts and opinions. Your input will help us understand what worked well and where we can improve for future events.

## How To Complete This Form

* Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as "required" to ensure your feedback is submitted accurately.
* For fields like "select_one" and "select_multiple", choose the option that best reflects your experience or preference.
* You can submit your email address and phone number if you wish to be contacted for further feedback or to be informed about future events.
* Take your time and answer each question honestly, and don't hesitate to express your opinions and suggestions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What was your impression of the event? (id: 1)** (`gala_dinner_event`, text, optional)
	+ Please provide a brief description of your overall impression of the event.
* **How would you rate the food? (id: 2)** (`food_rating`, number, optional)
	+ Choose a number to rate the quality of food at the event.
* **How would you rate the entertainment? (id: 3)** (`entertainment_rating`, number, optional)
	+ Choose a number to rate the quality of entertainment at the event.
* **Any additional feedback? (id: 4)** (`feedback`, note, optional)
	+ Feel free to share any thoughts or suggestions you have about the event.
* **Who organized this event? (id: 5)** (`event_organizer`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the name of the person or organization that organized the event.
* **Contact information (id: 6)** (`contact_info`, text, optional)
	+ Provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact information if you wish to be contacted.
* **Email (id: 7)** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted or informed about future events.
* **Phone (id: 8)** (`phone`, text, optional)
	+ Provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted or informed about future events.
* **Date (id: 9)** (`date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **Time (id: 10)** (`time`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **What is your relationship with the event? (id: 11)** (`select_one`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Donor, Attendee, Volunteer, or Other.
* **What did you enjoy most? (id: 12)** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose all that apply: Food, Entertainment, Music, or Other.
* **Event date (id: 13)** (`date_range`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time (id: 14)** (`time_range`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **Event location (id: 15)** (`event_location`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the location of the event.
* **Select all that apply (id: 16)** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose all that apply: Music, Food, Dancing, or Other.
* **How would you rate the overall event? (id: 17)** (`select_one`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **How likely are you to attend another event like this? (id: 18)** (`select_one`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Yes, No.
* **How satisfied are you with the value of your donation? (id: 19)** (`select_one`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one of the options: Very satisfied, Somewhat satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat dissatisfied, Very dissatisfied.
