# stripe_sponsor_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donor information and donation details for fundraising purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  To complete this form, please follow the steps below.
2.  Start by filling in your name in the "name" field.
3.  Next, enter your email in the "email" field.
4.  Enter your address in the "address" field.
5.  Finally, provide your zip code in the "zip_code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **name** (`name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name.
*   **email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address.
*   **address** (`address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your street address.
*   **zip_code** (`zip_code`, `text`, **required**): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill in your email address correctly, as it will be used for communication.
*   For address, you can enter a street address or a PO box.
*   Enter your zip code in the format "12345".
