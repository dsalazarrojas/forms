# Fundraiser Referal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to be used when you have a potential fundraiser to refer to our team. This form will help us collect information about the potential fundraiser and their contact details so we can follow up with them. Please ensure that you only submit a referral for a fundraiser who is new to our organization and not already working with us.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring that the required fields are filled out.
2. Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Submit the form once you have collected all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1) (optional): Please enter the potential fundraiser's first name.
- **Last Name** (2) (optional): Please enter the potential fundraiser's last name.
- **Email** (3) (optional): Enter the potential fundraiser's email address. We will use this to contact them with more information about the opportunity.
- **Phone** (4) (optional): Enter the potential fundraiser's phone number. We will use this to contact them with more information about the opportunity.
- **Message** (5) (optional): Enter a brief message about the potential fundraiser and why you think they would be a good fit for our organization. Please keep this concise and to the point.
## Tips
* Be sure to only refer to funders who are new to our organization.
* Only submit the form once you have all necessary information.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide before submitting the form.
