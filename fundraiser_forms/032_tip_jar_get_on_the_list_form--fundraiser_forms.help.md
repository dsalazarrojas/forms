# Tip Jar Get On The List Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who want to get on a list for a specific charity or organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields marked with **(*)**.
2. Enter your first and last name in the **First Name** and **Last Name** fields.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter the name of the charity or organization in the **Charity Name** field.
6. Enter the name of the organization supporting the charity in the **Organization** field.
7. Enter your preferred method of donation in the **Donation Method** field.
8. Enter your preferred frequency of donation in the **Donation Frequency** field.
9. Enter any additional message to the recipient in the **Message** field.
10. Enter your physical address in the **Address** field.
11. If you want to provide a tip amount, enter the amount in the **Tip Amount** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number here.
* **Charity Name** (`charity_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the charity you're interested in supporting.
* **Organization** (`organization_name`, text, **Required**): Enter the name of the organization that supports this charity.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`, select_one, **Optional**): Choose your preferred method of donation (Card number, Bank transfer, PayPal, Check, Online banking).
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_multiple, **Optional**): Choose your preferred frequency of donation (Monthly, One time, Quarterly, Annually, Never).
* **Message** (`message`, note, **Optional**): Enter any additional message to the recipient here.
* **Address** (`organization_address`, text, **Optional**): Enter your physical address here.
* **Tip Amount** (`amount`, number, **Optional**): Enter a tip amount if you'd like.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the employee you're supporting.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, **Optional**): Enter the ID of the employee you're supporting.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, **Optional**): Enter the email of the employee you're supporting.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter the phone number of the employee you're supporting.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, text, **Optional**): Enter the physical address of the employee you're supporting.
* **Job Title** (`employee_job_title`, text, **Optional**): Enter the job title of the employee you're supporting.
* **Department** (`employee_department`, text, **Optional**): Enter the department of the employee you're supporting.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, **Optional**): Enter the name of the manager of the employee you're supporting.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, **Optional**): Enter the email of the manager of the employee you're supporting.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, **Optional**): Enter the phone number of the manager of the employee you're supporting.
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`, text, **Optional**): Enter the physical address of the manager of the employee you're supporting.
