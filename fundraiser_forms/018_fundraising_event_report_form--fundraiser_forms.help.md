# Fundraising Event Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about fundraising events, allowing for accurate and organized data tracking and analysis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the fundraising event in the `event name` field.
2. Enter the date of the event in the `event date` field in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
3. Enter the time of the event in the `event time` field, using 24-hour format (e.g., `HH:MM`).
4. Enter the location where the event took place in the `event location` field.
5. Enter the number of participants who attended the event in the `number of participants` field.
6. Enter the total amount of money collected from donations in the `total donations collected` field.
7. Describe the goals and objectives of the event in the `event goals` field.
8. Provide a brief description of the outcomes and results of the event in the `event outcomes` field.
9. If applicable, provide feedback from participants in the `participant feedback` field.
10. Enter any additional information relevant to the event in the `additional info` field.
11. Enter the contact email of the event organizer in the `event organizer` field.
12. Enter the name and contact details of the main contact person for the event in the `event contact` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event name** (`event_name`, text, required: false): This field is for entering the name of the fundraising event. Please provide the name of the event, including any relevant details that will help identify it.
* **event date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the format `MM/DD/YY`. This will help track the timing of the event.
* **event time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event using 24-hour format (e.g., `HH:MM`). This will help track the duration of the event.
* **event location** (`event_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where the event took place. This can be a physical address or a description of the venue.
* **number of participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, required: false): Enter the number of people who attended the event. This helps to track attendance and participation metrics.
* **total donations collected** (`total_donations_collected`, number, required: false): Enter the total amount of money collected from donations. This helps to track fundraising goals and outcomes.
* **event goals** (`event_goals`, text, required: false): Describe the objectives and targets set for the event. This could include fundraising goals, number of participants expected, etc.
* **event outcomes** (`event_outcomes`, text, required: false): Describe the outcomes and results of the event. This could include financial metrics, feedback from participants, etc.
* **participant feedback** (`participant_feedback`, note, required: false): If applicable, enter feedback from participants in this field. This helps to improve future events.
* **additional info** (`additional_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information relevant to the event. This could include context, background information, etc.
* **event organizer** (`event_organizer`, email, required: false): Enter the contact email of the event organizer. This helps to track communication and follow-up.
* **event contact** (`event_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and contact details of the main contact person for the event. This helps to track communication and follow-up.
