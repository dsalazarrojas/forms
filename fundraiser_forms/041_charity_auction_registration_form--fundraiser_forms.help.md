# Charity Auction Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees and bidders for the charity auction event. It helps the organizers collect necessary information to manage the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form header section.
2. Provide your full name in the "attendee name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "phone" field (if applicable).
5. Select your role at the event from the "attendee type" dropdown menu (Guest, Donor, Sponsor, or Volunteer).
6. Specify the number of tickets you would like to reserve in the "ticket quantity" field.
7. Indicate whether you plan to bid during the event from the "bidder interest" dropdown menu (True, False, Maybe).
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them in the "dietary restrictions" field.
9. Choose your payment method for tickets or items from the "payment method" dropdown menu (Credit card, Invoice, At door).
10. If you have any special requests (e.g., seating or accessibility needs), please specify them in the "special requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Auction Registration --**: This is the header section of the form, providing a brief title and description of the form's purpose.
* **attendee name**: Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the auction's registration and bidder number materials.
* **email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event confirmations and updates.
* **phone**: Please enter your contact phone number for event day coordination, if applicable.
* **attendee type**: Select your role at the event:
	+ Guest: Attending the event but not participating in bidding.
	+ Donor: Contributing financially to the charity but not participating in bidding.
	+ Sponsor: Supporting the event through sponsorship or donations.
	+ Volunteer: Working at the event or participating in the planning process.
* **ticket quantity**: Specify the number of tickets you would like to reserve for the event.
* **bidder interest**: Indicate whether you plan to participate in bidding during the event:
	+ True: You plan to bid on items.
	+ False: You do not plan to bid on items.
	+ Maybe: You're unsure or haven't decided yet.
* **dietary restrictions**: If you have any meal restrictions or dietary requirements, please specify them here.
* **payment method**: Choose how you will pay for tickets or items:
	+ Credit card: Paying with credit card at the event.
	+ Invoice: Payment will be invoiced to you in advance or upon request.
	+ At door: Payment made upon arrival at the event.
* **special requests**: If you have any special requests (e.g., seating or accessibility needs), please specify them here.
