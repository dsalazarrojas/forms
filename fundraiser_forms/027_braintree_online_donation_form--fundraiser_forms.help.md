# Braintree Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Braintree Online Donation Form is designed for donors to contribute to our cause using a simple and intuitive interface. This form guides you through various sections, ensuring you provide the necessary information for a smooth donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the Donor Information Section**: Enter your full name and email address to receive a donation receipt and updates about our work.
2. **Choose Your Donation Type and Amount**: Select from preset amounts or enter a custom amount. You can also opt for a matching gift or a recurring donation.
3. **Specify the Program to Support**: Select the program you'd like your donation to fund, or choose to support a specific program of your choice.
4. **Customize Your Donation Options**: Decide if you want to receive a tax receipt, subscribe to our newsletter, or make an anonymous donation.
5. **Finalize Your Donation**: Review and authorize your donation details before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your official ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address**: Provide the email address associated with your donation to receive updates and a donation receipt.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact information.
* **Company or Organization**: If you're representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Donation Type**: Select between a preset amount, a custom amount, a matching gift, or a recurring donation.
* **Select Preset Amount**: Choose a suggested donation amount or select "Other amount" to enter your own amount.
* **Custom Donation Amount**: Enter a specific amount you'd like to donate.
* **Make This a Recurring Donation**: Decide if you'd like to make a one-time or recurring donation (e.g., monthly, quarterly, or annually).
* **Program to Support**: Choose the program you'd like to support or leave it blank to support the default program.
* **Impact Awareness**: Check to understand the impact of your donation.
* **Donate in Honor or Memory of**: Choose to make a tribute donation, either in honor or in memory of someone.
* **Honoree Name**: Enter the name of the person being honored.
* **Donate in Honor or Memory of**: If you've chosen to make a tribute donation, select the honoree's name.
* **Tribute Type**: Select the type of tribute donation you'd like to make.
* **Receive Tax Receipt**: Check to receive a tax receipt for your donation.
* **Subscribe to Newsletter**: Check to stay updated about our work.
* **Anonymous Donation**: Choose to hide your name from public view.
* **I Authorize This Donation**: Review and confirm your donation details before submitting.
