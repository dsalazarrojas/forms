# Sponsorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the sponsorship donation form for your business.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form by providing the required information for each field. The form is divided into several sections for your convenience. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Form Header**
* Form Header: This section is a placeholder for the form title and header. You can add any information you like, but it is not required.

### 2. **Business Name**
* Business Name: Please enter the name of your business. This will help us identify your company and ensure that we have accurate records.

### 3. **Sponsorship Amount**
* Sponsorship Amount: This is the amount of money you are willing to sponsor. Please enter a numerical value.

### 4. **Business Website**
* Business Website: Please provide a link to your website so we can easily verify your business information.

### 5. **Contact E**
* Contact E: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding sponsorship progress and other inquiries.

### 6. **Contact Phone**
* Contact Phone: Add your phone number so we can get in touch with you quickly.

### 7. **Logo URL**
* Logo URL: If you have a logo that represents your business, please provide the URL where it can be accessed.

### 8. **Submission Date**
* Submission Date: This will automatically be populated with the date and time you submit the form.

### 9. **Archived Submissions**
* Archived Submissions: If you are a repeat sponsor, please select 'Yes' to indicate that this submission should be archived.

### 10. **Submit**
* Submit: Select one of the provided options to complete the form submission process.

### 11. **Form Footer**
* Form Footer: This section is a placeholder for any additional information or instructions for form completion.
