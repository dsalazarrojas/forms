# lemonade_stand_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants and guardians to a lemonade stand fundraising event. It collects contact and demographic information to ensure accurate data collection for event planning and coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the participant's information, including their title, address, and phone number.
2.  Add the guardian's information, including their name and email address.
3.  Enter the lemonade stand details, including any relevant information about the stand.
4.  Enter the form submitter's information, including their name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **participant_info** (Participant Information, required: false): Enter the participant's title.
- **guardian_info** (Guardian Information, required: false): Enter the guardian's name.
- **lemonade_info** (Lemonade Information, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the lemonade stand.
- **participant_address** (Participant Address, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
- **participant_phone_number** (Participant Phone Number, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
- **guardian_name** (Guardian Name, required: false): Enter the guardian's name.
- **guardian_phone_number** (Guardian Phone Number, required: false): Enter the guardian's phone number.
- **lemonade_details** (Lemonade Details, required: false): Enter any additional details about the lemonade stand.
- **form_submitter** (Form Submitter, required: false): Enter the person submitting the form.
- **form_submitter_email** (Form Submitter Email, required: false): Enter the form submitter's email address.
- **guardian_email** (Guardian Email, required: false): Enter the guardian's email address.
- **lemonade_stand_details** (Lemonade Stand Details, required: false): Enter any other details about the lemonade stand.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.
- Double-check the form for any required fields before submission.
- If unsure about any field, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
