# Public Broadcasting Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from supporters who are making donations to our public broadcasting initiatives. It helps us to understand their donation dates, contact details, and preferences, which enables us to provide better services to them.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Fill out the form by providing the required information in the corresponding fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Date**: Enter the date of your donation.
* **Supporter Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Supporter Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Supporter Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of your donation.
* **Currency**: Select the currency of your donation from the options provided (AUD, USD, EUR, or GBP).
* **Other**: If your currency is not among the options, enter the currency of your donation in this field.
* **Donation Frequency**: Select how frequently you would like to make donations (One time, Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
* **Supporter Address**: Enter your physical address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you are located.
* **State**: Enter the state where you are located.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Country**: Enter the country where you are located.
* **Supporter Notes**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, enter it here.
* **Supporter Comments**: If you have any comments or suggestions, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your information is accurately recorded.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
* If you've filled out this form previously, you may be able to access your existing information in our system.
