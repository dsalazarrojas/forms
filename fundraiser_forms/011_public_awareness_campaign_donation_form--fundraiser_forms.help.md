# public_awareness_campaign_donation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a public awareness campaign donation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Donor Information section with your details.
2. Select the amount and frequency of your donation.
3. Choose the currency and gift card options if applicable.
4. Set up your recurring donation (if desired).
5. Enter your payment method and status.
6. Add any additional notes or gift message (if desired).
7. Confirm your email and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **donor_info** (Text): Enter your donor name, phone number, and email address.
* **donation_amount** (Number): Enter the amount you want to donate.
* **donation_frequency** (Select One): Choose how often you want to donate (one-time, monthly, quarterly, annually, bi-annually, monthly 3, monthly 6, monthly 12).
* **donation_currency** (Select Multiple): Choose the currency for your donation (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, CHF, JPY).
* **gift_card** (Select One): Choose if you want to receive a gift card (Yes, No).
* **recurring_donation** (Select One): Choose if you want to set up a recurring donation (Yes, No).
* **donation_frequency_duration** (Select Multiple): Choose the duration of your recurring donation (one time, monthly, quarterly, annually, bi-annually, monthly 3, monthly 6, monthly 12).
* **donation_start_date** (Date): Enter the start date for your recurring donation.
* **donation_end_date** (Date): Enter the end date for your recurring donation.
* **donation_frequency_interval** (Number): Enter the interval between donations.
* **donation_amount_interval** (Number): Enter the interval between donation amounts.
* **donation_currency_interval** (Select Multiple): Choose the currency for your interval (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, CHF, JPY).
* **payment_method** (Select Multiple): Choose your payment method (Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer, Cash App, Check, Other).
* **payment_status** (Select One): Choose the status of your payment (Paid, Pending, Cancelled).
* **notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **gift_message** (Text): Enter a message for the gift (if applicable).
* **acknowledgement** (Text): Confirm that you have received an acknowledgement for your donation.
* **acknowledgement_sent** (Select Multiple): Confirm if an acknowledgement has been sent.
* **email** (Email): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **address** (Text): Enter your address.
* **city** (Text): Enter your city.
* **state** (Text): Enter your state.
* **zip** (Text): Enter your zip code.
* **country** (Text): Enter your country.
