# PTA Fundraising Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The PTA Fundraising Sponsorship Form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in sponsoring the PTA. This form helps us understand the sponsor's preferences and requirements, ensuring we can effectively communicate and manage our fundraising efforts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your Name, Email, and Phone Number.
2. Specify the Amount you wish to donate.
3. Choose your preferred Donation Method (Cash, Check, or Online Donation).
4. Select your Payment Terms (One-Time or Monthly).
5. Review your submission for accuracy before clicking the Submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be recognized as a sponsor.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further contact or updates.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. This can be a one-time or recurring donation.
* **Donation Method**: Choose how you would like to make your donation:
	+ **Cash**: Hand-deliver or mail a check to the PTA.
	+ **Check**: Mail a check to the PTA with the specified amount.
	+ **Online Donation**: Donate online through our secure portal.
* **Payment Terms**: Select how frequently you would like to make your donation:
	+ **One-Time**: Donate a single amount at a time.
	+ **Monthly**: Set up a recurring monthly donation.

## Tips
* Please note that submitting this form does not automatically register you as a sponsor. Our team will review and follow up with you to confirm your sponsorship details.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
