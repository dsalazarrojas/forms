# Fundraising Event Success Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect data on the success of a fundraising event. This form will help event organizers evaluate the effectiveness of their fundraising efforts and identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of event that was held.
2. Enter the date the event took place.
3. Enter the location where the event was held.
4. Enter the number of attendees that participated.
5. Enter the fundraising goal for the event.
6. Enter the amount of money raised.
7. Rate your satisfaction level with the event.
8. Provide any areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What type of event?** (`event_type`, select_one): Select the type of event that was held, such as Event, Charity Run, Charity Walk, etc.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date): Enter the date the event took place. This can be in any date format that is easily understood by your organization.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text): Enter the location where the event was held.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text): Enter the name of the person or organization that organized the event.
* **Number of Attendees** (`attendees_number`, number): Enter the number of people who participated in the event.
* **Fundraising Goal** (`fundraising_goal`, number): Enter the amount of money that was raised as a goal for the event.
* **Fundraising Achieved** (`fundraising_achieved`, number): Enter the actual amount of money raised at the event.
* **Satisfaction Level** (`satisfaction_level`, select_multiple): Rate your satisfaction level with the event. Select all options that apply.
	+ Very Satisfied
	+ Somewhat Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Somewhat Dissatisfied
	+ Very Dissatisfied
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvements`, text): Provide any areas for improvement that were noted during the event.

## Tips
- Be as specific as possible when answering each question.
- Use the select options provided for the Satisfaction Level field.
- If you have any additional comments or suggestions for improvement, provide them in the Areas for Improvement field.
