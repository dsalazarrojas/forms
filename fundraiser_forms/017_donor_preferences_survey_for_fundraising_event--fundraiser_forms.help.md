# Donor Preferences Survey For Fundraising Event - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences and habits related to donating and volunteering with our organization. The information you provide will help us tailor our approach to engage with you more effectively and improve our fundraising and volunteer events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. For each field, select the answer that best matches your preference.
2. If you have an opinion or additional information for any field, feel free to provide it in the space provided.
3. Double-check your selections before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donations Frequency (<page: 1>):** 
	* Select how often you like to donate to our organization.
* **Favorite Organisations (<page: 2>):** 
	* Indicate which organizations you are most passionate about supporting.
* **Preferred Communication (<page: 3>):** 
	* Choose your preferred method of communication from us (e.g., email, phone, mail).
* **Preferred Language (<page: 4>):** 
	* Select your preferred language for communication from us (English, French, Spanish).
* **Donations Amounts (<page: 5>):** 
	* Indicate which donation amounts you are comfortable with.
* **Time Of Year (<page: 6>):** 
	* Select the time of year when you prefer to donate.
* **Recognition Preferences (<page: 7>):** 
	* Indicate which types of recognition you prefer (e.g., acknowledgement, social media shoutout).
* **Volunteer Opportunities (<page: 8>):** 
	* Choose the types of volunteer opportunities you are most interested in.
* **Social Media (<page: 9>):** 
	* Indicate which social media platforms you prefer to use.
* **Volunteer Frequency (<page: 10>):** 
	* Select how frequently you like to volunteer.
* **Contact Method (<page: 11>):** 
	* Choose your preferred method of contact (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **Contact Frequency (<page: 12>):** 
	* Indicate how often you like to be contacted.
* **Volunteer Types (<page: 13>):** 
	* Select which types of volunteer work you enjoy doing.
* **Event Type (<page: 14>):** 
	* Choose the type of event you are most interested in attending.
* **Event Location (<page: 15>):** 
	* Select the location where you would like events to take place.
* **Donations Frequency (<page: 16>):** 
	* Indicate how often you like to donate.
* **Donations Amount (<page: 17>):** 
	* Select the amount you are comfortable donating.
* **Volunteer Time (<page: 18>):** 
	* Indicate how much time you can realistically commit to volunteering.
* **Volunteer Time Period (<page: 19>):** 
	* Choose when you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Frequency Period (<page: 20>):** 
	* Select how frequently you like to volunteer over a period.
* **Volunteer Status (<page: 21>):** 
	* Choose your current volunteer status (Active, Inactive).
* **Volunteer Status Other (<page: 22>):** 
	* If you have a status that doesn't match the above options, describe it here.
* **Volunteer Status Other Details (<page: 23>):** 
	* Provide additional details about your volunteer status.
* **Recognition Preferences (<page: 24>):** 
	* Indicate which types of recognition you prefer (e.g., acknowledgement, social media shoutout).
* **Contact Info (<page: 25>):** 
	* Provide any additional contact information that may be necessary for future communication.
