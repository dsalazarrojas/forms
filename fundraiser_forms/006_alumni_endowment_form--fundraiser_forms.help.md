# Alumni Endowment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Endowment Form is designed for alumni to create an endowment to support their alma mater. This form collects necessary information to process and acknowledge donations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your name, donation amount, and other requested information.
2. Choose your preferred payment method and designation of funds (if applicable).
3. Select your recognition preference, if desired.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments about your gift.
5. Submit the form to complete your endowment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donation amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate in USD.
* **Donation type** (`donation_type`, select_one, required): Choose from One-time, Monthly, or Annual payment options.
* **Designation of funds** (`designation`, text, optional): Specify a particular fund or program you wish to support, if applicable.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose from Credit card, Check, or Bank transfer options.
* **Receipt email** (`receipt_email`, email, required): Enter the email address where you would like to receive a donation receipt.
* **Recognition preference** (`recognition_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose from Anonymous, Name displayed, or Name and gift amount recognition options.
* **Additional notes** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any optional information about your gift, such as why you made the endowment.
