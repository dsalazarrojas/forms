# Theater Accessibility Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for a theater's accessibility fund. It is designed to provide a straightforward way for donors to contribute to the theater's efforts in making their facilities more accessible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the amount you wish to donate to the theater's accessibility fund.
2. Provide your email address so we can contact you for any further information or updates.
3. Enter your phone number (if you would like to be contacted via phone).
4. Enter your name as you would like it to appear on our records.
5. If you have any additional comments or information related to your donation, please enter them in the Donation Note field.
6. Choose the payment gateway through which you would like to make your donation.
7. Select the method of payment you would like to use (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, check).
8. If your donation is intended to be recurring, select "Yes" in the Is Recurring field.
9. Enter the start date for the recurring donation.
10. Enter the end date for the recurring donation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount**: This field allows you to enter the amount you wish to donate. You can enter a whole number value.
* **Donor Email**: This field captures your email address so we can contact you for further information or updates related to your donation.
* **Donor Phone**: This field is where you can enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted via phone.
* **Donor Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Donation Note**: This field is for any additional comments or information related to your donation.
* **Payment Gateway**: Choose the payment gateway through which you would like to make your donation. We currently support Stripe, PayPal, and Authorize.net.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment you would like to use (e.g. credit card, bank transfer, check).
* **Is Recurring**: If you would like to set up a recurring donation, select "Yes". This will allow you to schedule regular payments.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you would like your recurring donation to start.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you would like your recurring donation to end.

Tips:

* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are up to date and accurate so we can contact you efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns about your donation, please contact us directly.
* We appreciate your contribution to the theater's accessibility efforts!
