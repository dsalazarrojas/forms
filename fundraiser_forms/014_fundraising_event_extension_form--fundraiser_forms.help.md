# Fundraising Event Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a fundraising event and its participants. It's used to extend or update existing events, ensuring that all necessary details are up-to-date and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: 
  * **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
  * **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
  * **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
  * **Event Description**: Describe the event in detail.
  * **Event Status**: Choose the status of the event (active or inactive).
  * **Donation Currency**: Choose the currency of the donation (USD, EUR, CAD, or AUD).
  * **Donation Frequency**: Choose how often the donation will be made (one-time or recurring).
  * **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
  * **Participant Email**: Enter the email of the participant.
  * **Participant Details**: Enter any additional details about the participant.

2. Optional fields can also be filled out:
  * **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation.
  * **Feedback**: Add any comments or feedback about the event or participant.
