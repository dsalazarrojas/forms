# Pledge Drive Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pledge Drive Form is designed to collect donations or contributions from individuals for fundraising purposes. It gathers essential information about the donor, such as their name, email, and payment method, and allows them to commit to a specific pledge amount.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name to identify yourself as the donor.
2. Provide your email address to allow us to contact you regarding your pledge.
3. Choose how you would like to make your payment: Card, Check, Cash, or PayPal.
4. Enter the amount you are willing to pledge.
5. Optionally, provide your address and phone number for further contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donations Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are willing to donate. This is the amount you are committing to contribute.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like to be identified as the donor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your pledge.
* **Pledge Amount** (`pledge_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount you are pledging to contribute.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to make your payment: Card, Check, Cash, or PayPal.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address if you want us to contact you at your home or office.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted via phone.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your pledge.
* Choose your preferred payment method to make it easier for you to fulfill your pledge.
* If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us using the email address you entered.
