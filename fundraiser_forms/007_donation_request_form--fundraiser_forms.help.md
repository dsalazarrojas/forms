# Donation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from donors to support a fundraiser. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your donation request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review each field carefully and provide accurate information.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Double-check that you have filled out all fields before submitting the form.

* **Donor Details**:
	+ **Donor Details** (Donor Details): A field for you to provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a donor.
* **Donation Details**:
	+ **Donation Type** (Donation Type, select_one): Choose between one-time and recurring donation options. If you have any other type of donation, please provide details in the "Notes" field.
	+ **Amount** (Amount, number): Enter the amount you would like to donate. This field is required.
* **Frequency**:
	+ **Frequency** (Frequency, select_one): Choose between one-time and recurring donation options.
* **Donation Date**:
	+ **Donation Date** (Donation Date, date): Enter the date you would like to make the donation.

	+ **Donation Frequency** (Donation Frequency, text): Enter the frequency of your donation (e.g., weekly, monthly, etc.).
* **Donation Value**:
	+ **Donation Value** (Donation Value, number): Enter the value of your donation.

	+ **Notes** (Notes, note): Enter any additional comments or details about your donation.
