# Fundraising Skills Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a fundraising skills training program. It will help assess their skills and abilities, which will inform the training process and outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Participant Info** field, providing any relevant details about the participant.
2. In the **Skills Info** field, provide a brief description of the skills and information relevant to the participant.
3. You can add any additional notes in the **Notes** field.
4. Select the **Participant Skills** you think the participant possesses from the list. You can choose one or more skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about the participant.
* **Skills Info**: Please describe the skills and abilities the participant has, such as their experience, accomplishments, or areas of expertise.
* **Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to add any additional comments or information about the participant.
* **Participant Skills**: Choose the skills you think the participant possesses from the list. This can include skills like leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, communication, time management, adaptability, or self-motivation.
