# Donation Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donation Tracking Form is designed to collect information about donations made to our organization. This form is meant to gather details about each donation, including the donation date, time, amount, donor details, email address, and contact number. The information collected will help us track and manage our donations effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Click on the form to start filling it out.
2. Enter the donation date in the format `mm/dd/yyyy` in the `Donation Date` field.
3. If applicable, enter the time of the donation in the `Time` field.
4. Enter the amount of the donation in the `Donation Amount` field.
5. Provide a brief description of the donor in the `Donor Details` field.
6. If you want to contact the donor, enter their email address in the `Email Address` field.
7. If you need to contact the donor via phone, enter their contact number in the `Contact Number` field.
8. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Date** (`donation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the donation in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
* **Time** (`donation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the donation if it's known.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Donor Details** (`donor_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the donor, such as their name, location, or other relevant details.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the donor if you need to contact them.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact number of the donor if you need to contact them via phone.

## Tips
* Always ensure you are filling out the form for the correct purpose.
* Be as detailed as possible when providing donor information.
* Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
