# Nonprofit Fundraising Specialist Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from candidates applying for non-profit fundraising specialist positions. The form is designed to be a basic application process for non-profit organizations to collect essential information from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your candidate information, such as your name, email, and phone number in the **Candidate Information** section.
2. Provide your contact details in the **Contact Details** section.
3. Describe your experience and skills in the **Experience and Skills** section.
4. Share your non-profit background and any relevant experience in the **Non-Profit Background** section.
5. Add any references that can vouch for your experience and skills in the **References** section.
6. Upload your resume and cover letter in the **Resume** and **Cover Letter** sections.
7. If you have any additional comments or information to share, add them in the **Additional Comments** section.
8. Finally, select the **Non-Profit Organization** you are applying to and provide details about your non-profit experience in the **Non-Profit Experience** section.
9. Select your relevant skills in the **Skills** section.
10. Specify your education in the **Education** section.
11. Add any additional references in the **References 2** and **References 3** sections.
12. Provide details about the non-profit sector you are applying to in the **Non-Profit Sector** section.
13. Finally, upload your updated resume, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Information**: Please fill in your name and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your email address, phone number, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Experience and Skills**: Describe your experience and skills related to non-profit fundraising, including any relevant certifications or training.
* **Non-Profit Background**: Share your non-profit experience, including any relevant positions you've held or programs you've managed.
* **References**: Add up to 3 references that can vouch for your experience and skills.
* **Resume**: Upload your updated resume that highlights your non-profit experience and qualifications.
* **Cover Letter**: Write a brief letter explaining why you're the best fit for the non-profit position.
* **References 2**: Add any additional references you'd like to share.
* **References 3**: Add any further references you'd like to share.
* **Non-Profit Organization**: Select the non-profit organization you are applying to.
* **Non-Profit Experience**: Describe your non-profit experience, including any relevant positions or programs you've managed.
* **Skills**: Select your relevant skills related to non-profit fundraising.
* **Education**: Specify your education and any relevant certifications or training.
* **Non-Profit Sector**: Select the non-profit sector you are applying to.
* **Years of Experience**: Provide the number of years you have experience in non-profit fundraising.
* **Submit**: Click the **Submit** button to complete your application.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be honest and accurate when providing information.
- Use clear and concise language when describing your experience and skills.
- Proofread your application before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the contact provided.
