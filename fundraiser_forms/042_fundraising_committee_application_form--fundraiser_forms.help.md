# Fundraising Committee Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Committee Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining the fundraising committee. This form is used to collect details about your role, committee preferences, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully and accurately. Make sure to provide your contact information, volunteer role, and committee preferences. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
### Volunteer Role
* **Volunteer Role** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your role in the fundraising committee, i.e., Volunteer, Staff, or Student.
### Volunteer Committee
* **Volunteer Committee** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the types of committees you are interested in, i.e., Parent, Teacher, or School Admin.
* **Other (please specify)** (`6`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Other", enter your committee preference here.
### School Name
* **School Name** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your school name if applicable.
### School Grade Level
* **School Grade Level** (`8`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your grade level, i.e., 1st, 2nd, 3rd, etc.
### Start Date
* **Start Date** (`9`, `date`, required: false): Enter your start date if you are planning to start participating in the committee.
### End Date
* **End Date** (`10`, `date`, required: false): Enter your end date if you are planning to end your participation.
### Time Committee Meets
* **Time Committee Meets** (`11`, `time`, required: false): Enter your preferred meeting time.
* **Other (please specify)** (`12`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Other", enter your meeting time here.
### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`13`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
### Additional Comments
* **Additional Comments** (`14`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or details about your committee preference.
### Volunteer Hours
* **Volunteer Hours** (`15`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of volunteer hours you plan to contribute.
* **Volunteer Hours Other** (`16`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "Volunteer Hours Other", enter your volunteer hours here.
### Committee Chair Name
* **Committee Chair Name** (`17`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the committee chair if applicable.
### Committee Chair Email
* **Committee Chair Email** (`18`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the committee chair.
### Committee Chair Title
* **Committee Chair Title** (`19`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the committee chair if applicable.
### Committee Chair Phone
* **Committee Chair Phone** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the committee chair.
### Committee Chair Volunteer Hours
* **Committee Chair Volunteer Hours** (`21`, `number`, required: false): Enter the volunteer hours of the committee chair.
### School District
* **School District** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school district if applicable.
### School District Other
* **School District Other** (`23`, `text`, required: false): If you selected "School District Other", enter your school district here.
### School Address
* **School Address** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school address if applicable.
### Committee Chair Additional Comments
* **Committee Chair Additional Comments** (`25`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the committee chair.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, ask for clarification.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure you have filled it out correctly.
