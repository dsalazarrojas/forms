# Matching Gift Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Matching Gift Form is a form used to collect information about a matching gift for a fundraising campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Matching Gift Form** title and description.
2. Fill out the **Gift Amount** field with the amount of the matching gift.
3. Enter the **Donor Name** as it appears on the check or bank statement.
4. Enter the **Donor Email** of the person or organization making the matching gift.
5. Enter the **Donor Phone** of the person or organization making the matching gift.
6. For **Gift Type**, select the option that best describes the type of matching gift.
7. If necessary, add additional information in the **Gift Status Options** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Matching Gift Form** (text, required): Enter the title and description of the matching gift.
* **Gift Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount of the matching gift.
* **Donor Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the person or organization making the matching gift.
* **Donor Email** (email, required): Enter the email of the person or organization making the matching gift.
* **Donor Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization making the matching gift.
* **Gift Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of matching gift.
* **Gift Status Options** (text, optional): Add any additional information about the status of the matching gift.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter all the required fields.
* Double-check the information you enter to ensure it is accurate.
* If you need help or have questions, contact your fundraising team.
