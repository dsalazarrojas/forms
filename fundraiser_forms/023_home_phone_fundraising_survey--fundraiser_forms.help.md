# Home Phone Fundraising Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from users about their experiences and perceptions related to fundraising, including their level of involvement, sources of funding, and concerns about outreach and phone calls.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Indicate your level of concern about fundraising in the "concerned_about_fundraising" field (if applicable).
5. Select whether you have ever participated in fundraising activities in the "have_participated_in_fundraising" field (if applicable).
6. Choose from the options whether you have donors for fundraising in the "have_donors_for_fundraising" field (if applicable).
7. Enter any concerns about donors in the "concerns_about_donors" field (if applicable).
8. Enter any concerns about outreach in the "concerns_about_outreach" field (if applicable).
9. Enter any concerns about phone calls in the "concerns_about_phone_calls" field (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your personal or work email address.
* **phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number in the format of "XXX-XXX-XXXX".
* **concerned_about_fundraising** (Text, Optional): If you are concerned about fundraising, describe your concerns in a few words.
* **have_participated_in_fundraising** (Select One, Optional): Check if you have ever participated in fundraising activities.
* **have_donors_for_fundraising** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose from the options whether you have donors for fundraising:
	+ Fundraising
	+ No donors for fundraising
	+ None
* **concerns_about_donors** (Text, Optional): If you have concerns about donors, describe them here.
* **concerns_about_outreach** (Text, Optional): If you have concerns about outreach, describe them here.
* **concerns_about_phone_calls** (Text, Optional): If you have concerns about phone calls, describe them here.

## Tips

- Please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to fill out this form.
- This form is for information purposes only and will not be used for any malicious activities.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the form administrators.
