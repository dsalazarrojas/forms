# Donor Program Renewal Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Program Renewal Request Form is a tool for program participants to express their interest in renewing their involvement in the program. By completing this form, you'll be able to provide essential information about your participation status and contact preferences, which will help us to process your renewal smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your User Information: Fill in your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide Renewal Information: Explain why you're seeking to renew your participation in the program.
3. Share Contact Preferences: Indicate your preferred communication channels and frequency.
4. Review and Submit: Double-check your details and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (id: 1)** (`name` `text`, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant personal details that you'd like to share with us. This will help us to better understand your identity.
* **Renewal Information (id: 2)** (`name` `text`, optional): In this field, please describe your reasons for renewing your participation in the program. Be as specific as possible about your motivations and goals.
* **Contact Preferences (id: 3)** (`name` `text`, optional): Indicate your preferred contact methods (e.g., email, phone, or mail) and how frequently you'd like to receive updates about the program.
* **Submission (id: 4)** (`name` `text`, optional): This field is for any additional comments or remarks you might have regarding your program participation. You can share your thoughts, suggestions, or any other relevant information that you feel is important.
