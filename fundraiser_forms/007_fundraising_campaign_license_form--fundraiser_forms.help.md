# fundraising_campaign_license_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Fundraising Campaign License Form" is designed to collect information required for a fundraising campaign registration or license application. This form is used to gather details about the campaign organizers or owners, including their name, contact information, and a brief description, to ensure that the campaign meets the necessary requirements for official licensing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the category of your campaign from the "Category" field. Choose from "Non Profit", "Charity", "Association", "Foundation", or "Organization" to accurately classify your campaign.
2. Provide a brief description of your campaign in the "Description" field.
3. Enter the name of the person responsible for the campaign in the "Name" field.
4. Enter the contact email address and phone number of the person in the "Email" and "Phone" fields, respectively.
5. Enter the location of the campaign in the "Location" field.
6. Choose the start and end dates of your campaign license in the "License Start" and "License End" fields, respectively.
7. Optionally, you can provide more details about your campaign in the "Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (`Category`, Select One, required): Choose the category of your campaign from the available options.
- **Description** (`Description`, Text, required): Enter a brief description of your campaign.
- **Name** (`Name`, Text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for the campaign.
- **Email** (`Email`, Email, required): Enter the contact email address of the person in charge.
- **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person in charge.
- **Location** (`Location`, Text, required): Enter the location of the campaign.
- **License Start** (`License Start`, Date, required): Choose the start date of your campaign license.
- **License End** (`License End`, Date, required): Choose the end date of your campaign license.
