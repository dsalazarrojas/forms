# Charity Gala Table Host Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information and preferences from table hosts at a charity gala event. The details collected will be used to assign tables, contact guests, and coordinate event logistics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Table Host Information" section with your name, contact email, and phone number.
2. Choose the gala event you will be hosting a table for.
3. Select the number of guests that will be attending with you.
4. List the names of all your guests attending.
5. Provide any combined dietary restrictions for your group.
6. Select your preferred seating location (if applicable).
7. If applicable, specify any special occasions or celebrations for your table.
8. Provide the cost per table and any additional contribution or sponsorship commitment.
9. Choose your sponsorship level (if making a commitment).
10. Select "Today's date" for the date of this registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Table Host Full Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for the event.
* **Email Address** (`host_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`host_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company or Organization** (`host_company`, `text`, optional): Enter your business or organization name.
* **Gala Event Name** (`gala_name`, `text`, required): Choose the gala event you will be hosting a table for.
* **Gala Date** (`gala_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the gala event.
* **Preferred Table Number** (`table_number`, `number`, optional): If available, select your preferred table number.
* **Number of Guests at Your Table** (`guests_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests attending with you.
* **Names of Guests at Your Table** (`guest_list`, `text`, required): List all confirmed attendees.
* **Combined Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_needs`, `text`, optional): Enter any combined dietary restrictions for your group.
* **Seating Location Preference** (`seating_preference`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred seating location (if applicable).
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, `text`, optional): Specify any accessibility requirements for your group.
* **Special Occasions or Celebrations** (`special_occasions`, `text`, optional): Enter any special occasions or celebrations for your table.
* **Table Cost** (`table_cost`, `number`, required): Enter the cost per table.
* **Additional Contribution or Sponsorship** (`additional_contribution`, `number`, optional): Enter any additional contribution or sponsorship commitment.
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your sponsorship level (if making a commitment).
* **Date of This Registration** (`confirmation_date`, `date`, required): Select the current date.
