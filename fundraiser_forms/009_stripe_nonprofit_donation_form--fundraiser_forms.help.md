# Stripe Nonprofit Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stripe Nonprofit Donation Form is designed to collect donor information and donation details for a nonprofit campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Campaign Name** in the first field.
2. Enter the **Donation Amount** in the second field.
3. Choose your **Donation Frequency** from the options provided.
4. If desired, select the **frequency** of your donation date.
5. Select the **Donor Details** you'd like to share.
6. Enter your **Email** address.
7. If you'd like to be contacted, enter your **Phone number**.
8. Fill out any **Custom Fields** as required.
9. Enter a brief description of the campaign in the **Campaign Goal** field.
10. Write a short description of the campaign in the **Campaign Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Campaign Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the campaign you are donating to.
  - **Donation Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount you would like to donate.
  - **Donation Frequency** (select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like to donate:
    - Option 1
    - Option 2
    - Option 3
  - **frequency** (date, optional): Enter the frequency of your donation date (if chosen above).
  - **Donor Details** (select_multiple, optional): Select any applicable details about the donor:
    - Option 1
    - Option 2
  - **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address (if you'd like to be contacted).
  - **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).
  - **Custom Fields** (note, optional): Fill out any additional custom fields as required.
  - **Campaign Goal** (text, optional): Enter a brief description of the campaign goal.
  - **Campaign Description** (text, optional): Enter a short description of the campaign.
