# online_donation_form_theme - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "online_donation_form_theme" is a customizable form designed to collect donations for a specific online campaign or theme. This form is intended to streamline the donation process and allow users to easily contribute to their chosen cause.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's title and purpose to understand the specific campaign or theme you're supporting.
2. Enter your information in the first field, "online_donation_form_theme_form_fields_1", to provide your name and any other relevant details as prompted.
3. Select your desired response to the second field, "online_donation_form_theme_form_fields_2", to indicate your interest in receiving updates or communications related to the campaign.
4. Enter any additional comments or information in the third field, "online_donation_form_theme_form_fields_3", if you have any specific questions or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **online_donation_form_theme_form_fields_1** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details as prompted.
* **online_donation_form_theme_form_fields_2** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to receive updates or communications related to the campaign, or 'No' to decline.
* **online_donation_form_theme_form_fields_3** (`text`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the campaign.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in the first field to ensure that your donation is processed correctly.
* If you select 'Yes' in the second field, be aware that you will receive regular updates and communications about the campaign.
* You can enter any additional comments or questions in the third field, and our team will respond promptly to address your concerns.
