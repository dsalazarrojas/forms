# Animal Shelter Fundraising Campaign Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in an animal shelter fundraising campaign. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure you are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field (Page 1).
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field (Page 1).
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field (Page 1).
4. If applicable, enter your **Organization Affiliation** in the "Organization Affiliation" field (Page 2).
5. Enter the **Name of the Campaign** in the "Fundraising Campaign Name" field (Page 2).
6. Choose the **Type of Campaign** from the dropdown list (Page 2).
7. Write a **Brief Description** of the campaign in the "Brief Description" field (Page 2).
8. If applicable, set the **Campaign Fundraising Goal** (Page 3).
9. Check your consent to participate in the campaign by agreeing to "Consent to Participate in This Campaign" (Page 4).
10. Select your **Role in the Campaign** from the dropdown list (Page 4).
11. Check your consent to share your contact information with partner organizations (Page 5).
12. Check your consent to receive future communications about the campaign (Page 5).
13. If applicable, choose to share your contact information on social media (Page 6).
14. If applicable, choose to use your likeness in campaign media (Page 6).
15. Check your agreement to the terms and conditions of the campaign by agreeing to "I Agree to Campaign Terms and Conditions" (Page 7).
16. Set the **Date of Consent** (Page 8).
17. If applicable, enter any **Additional Notes or Comments** in the text field (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required on Page 1): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required on Page 1): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required on Page 1): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, text, optional on Page 2): If you are representing an organization, enter your affiliation.
* **Fundraising Campaign Name** (`campaign_name`, text, required on Page 2): Enter the name of the campaign.
* **Campaign Date** (`campaign_date`, date, required on Page 2): Enter the date of the campaign.
* **Type of Campaign** (`campaign_type`, select_one, required on Page 2): Choose the type of campaign.
* **Campaign Description** (`campaign_description`, text, required on Page 2): Write a brief description of the campaign.
* **Campaign Fundraising Goal** (`campaign_goal`, number, optional on Page 3): If applicable, enter the target fundraising goal.
* **Consent to Participate in This Campaign** (`consent_participate`, select_one, required on Page 4): Check to indicate your consent to participate in the campaign.
* **Your Role in the Campaign** (`participation_role`, select_one, required on Page 4): Select your role in the campaign.
* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_contact`, select_one, required on Page 5): Check to indicate your consent to be contacted about the campaign.
* **Consent to Share Contact Information with Partners** (`consent_sharing`, select_one, required on Page 5): Check to indicate your consent to share your contact information with partner organizations.
* **Consent to Future Communications** (`consent_newsletter`, select_one, optional on Page 5): Check to indicate your consent to receive future communications about the campaign.
* **Consent to Photography** (`consent_photography`, select_one, required on Page 6): Check to indicate your consent to be photographed for campaign purposes.
* **Consent to Video Recording** (`consent_video`, select_one, optional on Page 6): Check to indicate your consent to be video recorded for campaign purposes.
* **Consent to Social Media Use** (`consent_social_media`, select_one, optional on Page 6): Check to indicate your consent to share campaign photos on social media.
* **Consent to Use as Testimonial** (`consent_testimonial`, select_one, optional on Page 6): Check to indicate your consent to be used as a testimonial for the campaign.
* **Consent to Release and Liability** (`consent_waiver`, select_one, required on Page 7): Check to indicate your understanding of and agreement with the waiver of liability.
* **Consent to Campaign Terms and Conditions** (`consent_terms`, select_one, required on Page 7): Check to indicate your understanding and agreement with the terms and conditions of the campaign.
* **Date of Consent** (`consent_date`, date, required on Page 8): Enter the current date.
* **Additional Notes or Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional on Page 8): Enter any additional notes or comments about your participation.
