# Global Peace Summit Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Peace Summit Fund Donation Form is designed to collect donations for the Global Peace Summit, a charity event promoting global peace and unity. This form is used to gather information from donors, ensuring accurate and efficient processing of donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your donation amount**: Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Global Peace Summit.
2. **Choose your donation type**: Select whether you would like to make a one-time or monthly donation.
3. **Enter your name**: Provide your name as the donor.
4. **Enter your email address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your donation.
5. **Enter your phone number (optional)**: If you'd like to be contacted for any additional details, enter your phone number.
6. **Additional information (optional)**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, you can type it here.
7. **Review and submit**: Once you've completed all the required fields, review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donation Amount (Page 1)**: Enter the amount you would like to donate to the Global Peace Summit.
* **Donation Type (Page 2)**: Choose between a one-time or monthly donation. If you select one-time, you'll be able to make a single donation. If you select monthly, you'll be able to schedule recurring donations.
* **Donor Name (Page 3)**: Provide your name as the donor. This will help us to recognize and acknowledge your contribution.
* **Donor Email (Page 4)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your donation. We'll use this to send you updates and information about the Global Peace Summit.
* **Donor Phone (Page 5)**: If you'd like to be contacted for any additional details, enter your phone number. This is optional but helps us to reach out to you more easily.
* **Additional Information (Page 6)**: If you have any additional information you'd like to share with us, you can type it here. This is an open-ended field for any other comments or details you'd like to provide.
* **Submit Button (Page 7)**: Once you've completed all the required fields, click this button to submit your form and complete the donation process.
