# fundraising_event_planning_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for submitting appointments related to fundraising events. It is a template for collecting information about events and appointments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page of the form, starting from "form_submission_page" and moving on to "event_details_page" and "appointment_details_page".
2. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information for each field, as this will help the event organizers and stakeholders in planning and coordinating the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### form_submission_page
* **No Label (Text Field)** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: false`): This is a free text field where you can input any information related to the submission.
* **No Label (Text Field)** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Another free text field for additional information.
* **No Label (Text Field)** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: false`): A third free text field for extra details.

### event_details_page
* **Event Details (Text Field)** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required: false`): A text field for providing details about the event.

### appointment_details_page
* **Appointment Details (Text Field)** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required: false`): A text field for providing details about the appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to proofread your submission for accuracy and clarity before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the event organizers or stakeholders.
* This form is designed to be a template, so feel free to modify it to suit your specific needs.
