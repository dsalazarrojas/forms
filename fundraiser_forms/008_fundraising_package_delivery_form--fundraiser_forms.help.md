# Fundraising Package Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising Package Delivery Form is used to collect information about the delivery of packages for fundraising purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the package details in the `package_details` field.
2. Enter the date and time of delivery in the `delivery_date` and `delivery_time` fields, respectively.
3. Provide the address of the delivery in the `delivery_address` field.
4. Select whether the package is prophetic or not in the `package_prophets` field.
5. Enter the quantity of packages being delivered in the `package_quantity` field.
6. Enter the total number of packages being delivered in the `total_packages` field.
7. Select the method of delivery in the `delivery_method` field.
8. Enter the value of the package in the `package_value` field.
9. Provide your contact email address in the `contact_email` field.
10. Enter your phone number in the `phone_number` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **package_details**: This field is used to describe the details of the package being delivered. Enter a brief description of the package.
* **delivery_date**: This field is used to enter the date of delivery.
* **delivery_address**: This field is used to enter the address where the package will be delivered.
* **delivery_time**: This field is used to enter the time of delivery.
* **package_prophets**: This field is used to indicate whether the package is prophetic or not.
* **package_quantity**: This field is used to enter the number of packages being delivered.
* **total_packages**: This field is used to enter the total number of packages being delivered.
* **delivery_method**: This field is used to select the method of delivery (e.g. yes or no).
* **package_value**: This field is used to enter the value of the package.
* **contact_email**: This field is used to enter your contact email address.
* **phone_number**: This field is used to enter your phone number.
