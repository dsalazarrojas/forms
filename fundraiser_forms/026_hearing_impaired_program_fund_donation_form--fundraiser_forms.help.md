# Hearing Impaired Program Fund Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations for the Hearing Impaired Program Fund, which supports individuals who are deaf or hard of hearing. Your contribution will help make a positive impact on their lives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Donation Amount.
2. Provide your Email and Phone number to help us contact you for any further assistance or to confirm your donation.
3. Enter the amount you'd like to donate.
4. Optionally, describe the purpose of your donation (e.g., to support a specific cause or program).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number. We’ll use this to contact you if needed.
* **Donation Amount** (number, required): Enter the amount you'd like to donate. Please use numerical values only.
* **Purpose of Donation** (text, optional): Describe the specific cause or program you’d like your donation to support (if applicable).

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* You can enter any additional details about your donation in the “Purpose of Donation” field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [Your Contact Information].
