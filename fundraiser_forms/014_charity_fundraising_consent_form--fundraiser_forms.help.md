# Charity Fundraising Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and consent from participants who are planning to engage in fundraising activities for charity. The form helps organizers understand the participants' details, goals, and expectations, as well as their consent to share personal data and participate in fundraising activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please complete the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled out.
2. Review each section carefully, and make sure to understand the implications of your answers.
3. If you're unsure about any field, ask your event organizer or contact person for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Participant Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Email, Required): Provide your email address to receive updates and communications about the fundraiser.
* **Phone Number** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter your phone number for potential contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, Date, Required): Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Name of Fundraising Activity** (Activity Name, Text, Required): Describe the specific activity you're participating in, e.g., "Cancer Walk" or "Charity Run".
* **Date of Activity** (Activity Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of the fundraising activity.
* **Description of Activity** (Activity Description, Text, Required): Provide a brief description of the activity, including location and details.
* **Fundraising Target Amount** (Target Amount, Number, Optional): Specify the amount you aim to raise for the charity.
* **Data and Communication Consent** (Data Collection, Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to allow us to collect and share your information for promotional purposes.
* **I Want to Receive Email Updates** (Email Updates, Select One, Optional): Choose "Yes" to receive regular email updates about the fundraiser.
* **I Consent to Phone Contact** (Phone Contact, Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" to receive phone calls or messages from the organizer or charity representatives.
* **I Consent to Being Asked for Donations** (Donation Consent, Select One, Required): Choose "Yes" to allow potential donors to contact you directly.
* **I Acknowledge and Accept Liability Terms** (Liability Acknowledgment, Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to acknowledge and accept the liability terms and conditions related to the activity.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (Terms and Conditions, Select One, Required): Confirm "Yes" to agree to the terms and conditions of the fundraiser.
* **I Consent to Photos and Videos** (Photo and Video Consent, Select One, Optional): Choose "Yes" to allow the use of your photo or video for promotional purposes.
* **I Consent to Background Check** (Background Check, Select One, Optional): Select "Yes" if a background check is required for the activity.
* **Date of Consent** (Consent Date, Date, Required): This field is automatically populated with the current date.
* **I Confirm I Have Read and Agree to All Terms Above** (Signature Acknowledgment, Select One, Required): Choose "Yes, I Confirm" to finalize your agreement to all the terms and conditions above.

Note: This form is designed to be completed carefully and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and successful fundraiser experience for both participants and organizers.
