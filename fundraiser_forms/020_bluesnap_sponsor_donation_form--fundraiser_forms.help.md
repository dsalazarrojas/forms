# BlueSnap Sponsor Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The BlueSnap Sponsor Donation Form is used to collect sponsor information and details for fundraising events. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to help us understand your sponsorship needs and facilitate a smooth partnership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your sponsor name and contact person's name.
2. Enter your contact email address for sponsorship coordination.
3. Enter the sponsorship amount.
4. Choose the sponsorship level (Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum).
5. Select the benefits you're requesting (e.g., Logo on website, Event signage, Speaking opportunity, etc.).
6. Indicate if an invoice is required for payment.
7. Add any additional notes or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor name** (required): Enter the name of the organization or individual sponsoring the event.
* **Contact person** (required): Provide the name of the person responsible for the sponsor's contact.
* **Contact email** (required): Enter the email address for sponsorship coordination.
* **Sponsorship amount** (required): Enter the numeric amount of the sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship level** (required): Select the appropriate tier (Bronze, Silver, Gold, or Platinum).
* **Benefits requested**: Choose the benefits you're requesting, such as Logo on website, Event signage, Speaking opportunity, etc.
* **Invoicing required** (required): Indicate if an invoice is needed for payment.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional notes or instructions for the sponsor.

## Tips

* Double-check the information provided before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Make sure to review the selected benefits and sponsorship level to ensure they align with the event's requirements.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us for support.
