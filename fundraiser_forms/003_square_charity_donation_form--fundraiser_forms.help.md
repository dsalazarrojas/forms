# Square Charity Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Charity Donation Form is a tool used to collect donations from individuals who want to contribute to a charity. The form is designed to be user-friendly and efficient, gathering all necessary information to process and follow up on the donation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your desired donation amount using the provided options.
2. Choose how frequently you would like to make this donation (One Time, Ongoing, or Recurring).
3. Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Account).
4. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
5. If you have any comments or additional information to provide, you can do so in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`Donation Amount`, number, required: false):
    This field allows you to specify the amount you would like to donate. You can select from the provided options or enter a custom amount.
* **Donation Frequency** (`Donation Frequency`, select_one, required: false):
    Choose how frequently you would like to make this donation. Options include One Time, Ongoing, or Recurring.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, select_multiple, required: false):
    Select your preferred payment method from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Account.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required: false):
    Enter your name so we can follow up with you about your donation.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false):
    Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required: false):
    Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Comment** (`Comment`, note, required: false):
    If you have any additional comments or information about your donation, you can provide them here.
* **Donation Comment** (`Donation Comment`, note, required: false):
    This field is similar to the Comment field, providing additional space for you to express your thoughts about your donation. 

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your information accurately to ensure we can follow up with you correctly.
* If you have any issues with the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* The form is designed to be user-friendly, but if you need help, please do not hesitate to reach out.
