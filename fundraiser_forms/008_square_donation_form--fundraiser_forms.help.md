# Square Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations from users to support a good cause or charitable organization. It allows users to input their donor information, specify a donation amount, leave a tip, select a tip amount, provide their email and phone number (if applicable), and confirm the donation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form's purpose and fields to understand what is being asked of you.
2.  Fill out the **Donor Information** field with your details (name, address, etc.).
3.  Enter a valid **Donation Amount** in the specified format.
4.  Optionally, provide a **Tip** to show your appreciation.
5.  If you'd like to, select a **Tip Amount** from the provided options.
6.  If you want to share your contact information, enter your **Email** and/or **Phone** numbers.
7.  Optionally, add any additional **Note** about your donation.
8.  Confirm the **Donation Date** and **Donation Time** (if applicable).
9.  Select the **Submit** option to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Information (donor_info)**: This field is where you can enter your name, address, and other details to help us identify you as a donor.
* **Donation Amount (donation_amount)**: Enter a valid donation amount in the specified format.
* **Tip (tip)**: A message of appreciation for your donation.
* **Tip Amount (tip_option)**: Select a tip amount to show your appreciation, if you'd like.
* **Email (email)**: If you'd like to share your email address with us, enter it here.
* **Phone (phone)**: If you'd like to share your phone number with us, enter it here.
* **Note (note)**: If you have any additional comments or notes about your donation, enter them here.
* **Donation Date (donation_date)**: Enter the date of your donation.
* **Donation Time (donation_time)**: Enter the time of your donation.
* **Submit (submit_button)**: Select this option to complete and submit your donation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Check your form for any errors before submitting it.
* If you're having trouble understanding a field, try to fill it out and then review the form again to see if everything looks correct.
