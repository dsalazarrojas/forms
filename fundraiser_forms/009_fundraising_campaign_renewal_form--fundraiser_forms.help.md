# Fundraising Campaign Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for existing donors who want to renew their support for an ongoing fundraising campaign. If you're a new donor or have not donated before, you cannot use this form.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your donor information, including your contact details.
- Select the campaign you want to support by choosing from the list of available campaigns.
- Choose your preferred donation amount.
- Indicate your preferred payment method.
- If you would like to make regular donations, specify the frequency.
- Add any additional notes you'd like to include.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Donor Information** (donor_info, text, required: false): Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant details so that we can contact you about your donations.
- **Campaign Information** (campaign_info, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the campaign you want to renew your support for. You can select multiple campaigns if you're supporting multiple initiatives.
- **Donation Amount** (donation_amount, number, required: false): Indicate how much you'd like to donate each time you renew your support. You can choose to donate a specific amount each time or leave it blank for a variable amount.
- **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, required: false): Select how you'd like to make your donation: Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
- **Gift Frequency** (gift_frequency, number, required: false): If you'd like to make regular donations, specify how often you'd like to donate. This can be a one-time gift or a regular monthly, quarterly, etc. gift.
- **Notes** (notes, note, required: false): You can add any additional comments or messages you'd like to include with your donation.
- **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donations.
- **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): If you have a preferred phone number, you can enter it here so we can contact you about your donations.

Note: The required status of each field is set to false, but it's expected that users will complete the form in a way that makes sense for their situation.
