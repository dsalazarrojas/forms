# Donate For Education Using E.Check.Net - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for donations made using the E.Check.Net payment method for educational purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and the purpose of each field.
2. Select the "E.Check.Net" payment method for your donation.
3. Enter the donation amount in the "Donation Amount" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Sponsor Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Sponsor Phone" field (this is optional).
6. Enter any additional information about the donor in the "Donor Information" field (this is optional).
7. Enter the donor's name in the "Sponsor Name" field (this is optional).
8. Select "chatjimmy" as the "Form Submitted By" field (this is required, as this form is submitted by chatjimmy).
9. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Details** (`main_page`, text, no): This field is the introduction page of the form. Please review the form carefully before proceeding.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, no): Enter the amount of the donation you would like to make. Please ensure this is the correct amount to avoid any discrepancies.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, no): Select "E.Check.Net" as the payment method for your donation.
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, no): Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding your donation.
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, no): Enter your phone number for us to contact you (optional).
* **Donor Information** (`donor_info`, note, no): If you have any additional information about the donor, please enter it here (optional).
* **Sponsor Name** (`donor_name`, text, no): Enter the name of the sponsor or donor (optional).
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, yes): Select "chatjimmy" as the person who submitted this form.
* **Other** (`other`, text, no): This field is not used in this form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have reviewed the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at your convenience.
* Thank you for your donation!
