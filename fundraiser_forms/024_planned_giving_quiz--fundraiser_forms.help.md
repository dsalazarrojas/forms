# planned_giving_quiz - Help Guide
## Purpose
The planned giving quiz form collects information from donors to track their commitment to make planned gifts, including their contact information, gift preferences, and follow-up details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "True" or "False" from the Planned Gift field to indicate whether the donor is making a planned gift.
2. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number, in the User Info field.
3. If the donor has a preferred contact method (e.g., phone, email, mail), select it from the Contact Method field.
4. Choose your preferred follow-up method (e.g., phone, email, mail) from the Follow-up Method field.
5. Enter any additional follow-up information in the Additional Follow-up Info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name and any other information about yourself.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your contact information, such as your phone number and email address.
* **Planned Gift Preferences** (`gift_preferences`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the types of gifts you are interested in making (e.g., Cash, Securities, Other).
* **Planned Gift Amount** (`gift_amount`, Number, Optional): Enter the amount you are committed to giving.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred contact method (e.g., Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Best Contact Method** (`best_contact_method`, Select One, Required): Select the best way we can contact you.
* **Planned Gift** (`planned_gift`, Select One, Required): Select "True" to indicate that you are making a planned gift.
* **Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information to help us follow up with you.
* **Follow-up Frequency** (`follow_up_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate how often you would like to be followed up on (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_method`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the methods you would like to be followed up on (e.g., "Yes", "No").
* **Follow-up Phone** (`follow_up_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter any phone numbers to be used for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Email** (`follow_up_email`, Email, Optional): Enter your email address to be used for follow-up.
* **Follow-up Mail** (`follow_up_mail`, Text, Optional): Enter your mailing address to be used for follow-up.
* **Planned Giving Info** (`planned_giving_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about your planned giving.
* **Follow-up Note** (`follow_up_note`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional follow-up note.
* **Additional Follow-up Info** (`additional_follow_up_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional follow-up information.
* **Other Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_other`, Text, Optional): Enter any other follow-up information.
* **Other Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_other_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any other follow-up information.
* **Other Follow-up Method** (`follow_up_other_method`, Select One, Required): Select the other follow-up method (e.g., Phone, Email, Mail).
* **Other Follow-up Method Info** (`follow_up_other_method_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any other follow-up method information.

**Tips**

* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth communication.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact email or phone number].
