# Performing Arts Auditorium Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performing Arts Auditorium Donation Form is a tool for patrons and supporters to donate to the venue. This form collects information about the donor, their contact details, and the type of donation they wish to make.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether this is a one-time or recurring donation.
2. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
3. Choose the donation amount and frequency (if applicable).
4. Select the acknowledgement type for your donation (e.g., cash, goods, or services).
5. Optionally, provide a message for the Performing Arts Auditorium staff.
6. Identify your supporter type (individual, organization, or business).
7. Provide a message for the Performing Arts Auditorium about your support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **One Time Donation (id: 1)**: A one-time donation is a single payment made towards the Performing Arts Auditorium. Enter the amount of your donation here.
* **Recurring Donations (id: 2)**: If you wish to make recurring donations, select the frequency of your contributions (Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly).
* **Full Name (id: 3)**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates about the Performing Arts Auditorium.
* **Phone (id: 5)**: Provide your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Donation Amount (id: 6)**: Enter the amount of your donation. This will be the base amount for recurring donations.
* **Acknowledgement Type (id: 7)**: Select the type of acknowledgement you would like to receive for your donation (e.g., Cash, Goods, or Services).
* **Message (id: 8)**: Optionally, provide a message for the Performing Arts Auditorium staff.
* **Supporter Type (id: 9)**: Identify yourself as an Individual, Organization, or Business.
* **Supporter Message (id: 10)**: Provide any additional information about your support for the Performing Arts Auditorium.
