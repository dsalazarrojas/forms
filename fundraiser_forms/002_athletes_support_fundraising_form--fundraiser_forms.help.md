# Athletes Support Fundraising Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from donors who want to support athletes and their fundraising campaigns. It ensures that your contribution is acknowledged and recognized properly, and that you have a clear understanding of how your donation will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**, your **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** (if available). These details will help us contact you for confirmation and any follow-up questions.
2. Provide the **Organization/Team Name** of the athlete or team you're supporting. This helps us understand the context of your donation.
3. Specify the **Fundraising Campaign Goal**, such as Equipment Purchase, Travel Expenses, Training Programs, or Scholarships. This will help us understand how your donation will be used.
4. Enter the **Donation Amount** you wish to contribute in USD.
5. Choose whether your **Donation Frequency** is one-time, monthly, quarterly, or annually.
6. Select your preferred **Payment Method**, such as Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check.
7. If you have a **Relationship to Athlete/Organization**, select how you know the athlete or team (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Coach, etc.).
8. Choose how you'd like to be **Recognized**, such as publicly, anonymously, or through email.
9. If your organization is tax-exempt, indicate this and provide your **Tax ID**.
10. Enter your **Mailing Address** (if different from your contact information).
11. Select whether your organization is **Tax-Exempt** or not.
12. If you're interested in volunteering or sponsoring, select the relevant options.
13. Finally, leave a **Message to Athletes** if you'd like to share a word of encouragement.
14. **Sign up for updates** to stay informed about campaign progress and news.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name (1)**: Please enter your name to be contacted for confirmation and any follow-up questions.
* **Email Address (2)**: We will use this to send you donation confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number (3)**: This is an optional contact number for further communication.
* **Organization/Team Name (4)**: Enter the name of the athlete or team you're supporting.
* **Athlete Name (5)**: Specify the name of the athlete(s) being supported.
* **Fundraising Campaign Goal (6)**: Select the reason for your donation (e.g., Equipment Purchase, Travel Expenses).
* **Donation Amount (7)**: Enter the amount you wish to contribute in USD.
* **Donation Frequency (8)**: Choose how often you'd like to make a donation (one-time, monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Payment Method (9)**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal).
* **Relationship to Athlete/Organization (10)**: Indicate how you know the athlete or team (e.g., Family Member, Friend, Coach).
* **Relationship to Athlete/Organization (10)**: Select how you'd like to be recognized (e.g., publicly, anonymously, through email).
* **Recognition Preference (11)**: If you're a tax-exempt organization, enter your Federal Tax ID.
* **Tax ID (12)**: If you have a tax-exempt organization, enter your Federal Tax ID.
* **Mailing Address (13)**: Enter your street address for receipt or acknowledgment purposes.
* **City (14)**: Enter your city for mailing purposes.
* **State/Province (15)**: Enter your state or province for mailing purposes.
* **ZIP/Postal Code (16)**: Enter your zip code for mailing purposes.
* **Is your organization tax-exempt? (17)**: Indicate whether your organization is tax-exempt or not.
* **Tax ID (if applicable) (18)**: If tax-exempt, enter your Federal Tax ID.
* **Message to Athletes (19)**: Share a message of encouragement or support with the athlete or team.
* **Sign me up for updates (20)**: Choose to receive campaign updates and news through email.
* **Other Ways to Support (21)**: Select if you're interested in volunteering or sponsoring.
