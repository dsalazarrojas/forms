# Donor Drive Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from donors who wish to participate in a Donor Drive for a specific cause or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. If you are affiliated with an **Organization**, enter the name of the organization in the "Organization" field.
4. Select your **Role** in the "Role" field. This will help us understand your connection to the cause.
5. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter your **Address** in the "Address" field. This is optional but helps us to get in touch with you.
8. Indicate how much you are willing to **Donate** in the "Donation Amount" field.
9. Select how often you would like to **Donate** by choosing from "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "Annually".
10. Choose your preferred **Donation Method** from "Bank Transfer", "Credit Card", or "Check".
11. Select how you would like to be contacted in the "Preferred Communication Method" field. You can choose from "Email", "Phone", or "Text Message".
12. Answer **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you would like to be a **Volunteer**.
13. Add any additional **Other Notes** you would like to share with us in the "Other Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): This field is used to collect your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): This field is used to collect your last name.
* **Organization** (Text, Optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter the name of the organization here.
* **Role** (Select One, Optional): Select your role in relation to the cause, such as "Donor", "Volunteer", or "Supporter".
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Address** (Note, Optional): Enter your address for us to get in touch with you.
* **Donation Amount** (Number, Optional): Indicate how much you are willing to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select how often you would like to donate, such as "Monthly", "Quarterly", or "Annually".
* **Donation Method** (Select One, Optional): Choose how you would like to make your donation, such as "Bank Transfer", "Credit Card", or "Check".
* **Preferred Communication Method** (Select One, Optional): Select how you would like to be contacted, such as "Email", "Phone", or "Text Message".
* **Volunteer** (Select One, Optional): Answer **Yes** or **No** to indicate if you would like to be a volunteer.
* **Other Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or information you would like to share with us.
* **Submit** (Text, Optional): This is the final step to submit your registration.
