# Alumni Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Alumni Donation Form is a tool for collecting donations from alumni of an educational institution. It helps the alumni association or development office to track and acknowledge donations, maintaining the relationship between the institution and its alumni.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your **Full Name** in the first field. This is the required field to identify you as an alumni.
2.  If you know your **Alumni ID Number**, enter it in the second field. If you do not know your ID, you can leave it blank.
3.  If this donation is for a specific **Event**, enter the name of the event in the third field.
4.  Enter the amount of your **Donation** in the fourth field. This should be in USD.
5.  Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
6.  If you wish to make your donation **In Honor or Memory Of**, enter the name of the person being honored in this field.
7.  Enter your **Mailing Address** to receive a physical tax receipt. This is optional.
8.  Provide your **Phone Number**. This field is not required.
9.  Enter your **Email Address**. This field is required.
10. In the **Notes for the Alumni Office** field, you can provide any notes or comments for the institution.
11. Finally, a **Thank You for Your Support** message acknowledges your contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name to identify yourself as an alumni.
*   **Alumni ID Number (2)**: If you know your ID number, enter it here. If not, leave it blank.
*   **Event Name (3)**: If this donation is for a specific event, enter the name here.
*   **Donation Amount (4)**: Enter the amount of your donation in USD.
*   **Payment Method (5)**: Select how you wish to pay (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Check).
*   **In Honor or Memory Of (6)**: If you wish to honor someone, enter their name here.
*   **Mailing Address (7)**: Enter your address to receive a physical tax receipt.
*   **Phone Number (8)**: Your phone number is optional.
*   **Email Address (9)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Notes for the Alumni Office (10)**: Provide any additional comments or notes for the institution.
*   **-- Thank You for Your Support -- (11)**: Acknowledges your contribution to the institution.
