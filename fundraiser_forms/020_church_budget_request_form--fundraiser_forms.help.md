# Church Budget Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Budget Request Form is used by church departments and ministries to submit their budget requests to the finance committee. This form is essential for ensuring that all budget requests are properly documented and reviewed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name and department/ministry affiliation.
2. Select the department or ministry that you belong to.
3. Enter the date of your budget request.
4. Provide a detailed description of the budget item or activity.
5. Choose the budget category for your request.
6. Enter the total amount requested.
7. If applicable, provide information about the recommended vendor.
8. Select the priority level of your request (High, Medium, or Low).
9. Specify the date by which you need funds.
10. Indicate if this is a one-time or recurring expense.
11. Confirm if you have attached the necessary documentation (quote or invoice).
12. Add any additional comments for the finance committee.
13. If you have already received approval from your ministry lead, confirm this.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name of Requester** (ID 2): Enter your name, as you would like it to be recognized on the form.
* **Department or Ministry** (ID 3): Choose the department or ministry that you represent.
* **Date of Request** (ID 4): Enter the date you submitted this budget request.
* **Name of Item or Activity** (ID 6): Enter a clear and descriptive name for the budget item or activity.
* **Detailed Description and Purpose** (ID 7): Provide a detailed explanation of the budget item or activity, including how it supports the church mission.
* **Budget Category** (ID 8): Choose the category that best fits your budget request.
* **Total Amount Requested** (ID 9): Enter the total amount you are requesting.
* **Recommended Vendor (if applicable)** (ID 10): If you have a preferred vendor, enter their information here.
* **Priority Level** (ID 11): Choose the level of priority for your request (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Date Funds are Needed By** (ID 12): Enter the date by which you need access to funds.
* **Is this a recurring expense?** (ID 13): Indicate if this is a one-time or recurring expense.
* **Quote or Invoice Attached?** (ID 14): Confirm if you have attached the required documentation.
* **Additional Comments** (ID 15): Add any additional comments or information for the finance committee.
* **Has your ministry lead approved this request?** (ID 16): Confirm if your ministry lead has approved this request.
