# Gift Aid Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gift Aid Declaration Form is used to declare and manage Gift Aid tax relief on donations to charities. This form is required to be completed by individuals who wish to claim Gift Aid on their donations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the donor information section.
2. Ensure you have the necessary information about the charity you are making a donation to, including the charity name, reference, and Gift Aid number.
3. Enter the date of your declaration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on official documents.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1, which is usually the street address or the first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2, which is usually the second line of your address.
* **Post Code**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Charity Name**: Enter the name of the charity you are making a donation to.
* **Charity Reference**: Enter the charity reference number provided by the charity.
* **Gift Aid Number**: Enter your Gift Aid number, which is usually provided by the charity.
* **Gift Aid Authorisation**: Enter the Gift Aid authorisation number provided by the charity.
* **Declaration Date**: Enter the date you are making this declaration.
* **Gift Aid Ref**: Enter any additional gift aid reference number provided by the charity.
*   **Note:** If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the charity or the relevant authorities for clarification.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and consistently with the information on your official documents.
* Check with the charity for any specific requirements or guidelines they may have for completing this form.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
