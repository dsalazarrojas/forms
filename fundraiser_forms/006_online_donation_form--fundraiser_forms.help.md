# Online Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Donation Form is designed to collect donations for a cause with various fields to gather personal and payment information from the donor.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling out the **Donor Information** section with your name, phone number, and email.
2.  Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
3.  Enter the **Donation Amount** you wish to donate.
4.  Provide your **Donor Address**, including street address, city, state, zip, and country.
5.  Fill in your **Card Number**, **Exp. Date**, **Exp. Month**, and **Exp. Year** for credit card payments.
6.  Enter your **Card Holder Name** and **CVV** for credit card payments.
7.  If you are using a credit card, provide your **CVC**.
8.  Enter any additional **Message** you may have.
9.  Finally, review your details and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Donor Information**: Please provide your name, phone number, and email.
*   **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount you wish to donate.
*   **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Cash.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address.
*   **Phone**: Provide your phone number.
*   **Donor Address**: Enter your address for donation tracking and communication.
*   **Message**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
*   **Payment Date**: Enter the date you plan to make the payment.
*   **Payment Time**: Enter the time you plan to make the payment.
*   **Note**: Enter any additional information about your donation.
*   **Card Number**: Enter your credit card number.
*   **Card Holder Name**: Enter the name on your credit card.
*   **Card Security Code**: Enter the security code on your credit card.
*   **CVV**: Enter the CVV code on your credit card.
*   **CVC**: Enter the CVC code on your credit card.
*   **Card Holder Name**: Enter the name of the card holder.
*   **Card Security Code**: Enter the security code on your credit card.
*   **CVV**: Enter the CVV code on your credit card.
*   **CVC**: Enter the CVC code on your credit card.
