# Tech Incubator Mentorship Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect donations from individuals interested in supporting the tech incubator program. Please fill out this form to make a donation and help the program grow.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Enter your name" field.
2. Enter your work email in the "Enter your email" field.
3. Select the donation amount you'd like to make.
4. Choose your donation frequency from the "Donation frequency" dropdown menu.
5. If you have any additional comments or information about your donation, please enter it in the "Additional comments" field.
6. If you are representing a company, enter the company name in the "Company" field.
7. Enter the contact name and email (if different from the donor) in the "Contact name" and "Contact email" fields, respectively.
8. If you'd like to provide a contact phone number, enter it in the "Contact phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your name** (user_name, text, required: false): This field is where you enter your name, which will be associated with the donation.
* **Enter your email** (email, email, required: false): This field is where you enter your work email address, which will be used to communicate with you about your donation.
* **Donation amount** (amount, number, required: false): This field allows you to select the amount you'd like to donate to the tech incubator program.
* **Donation frequency** (frequency, select_one, required: false): Choose how frequently you'd like to make this donation. Options are One-time, Recurring (weekly), Recurring (monthly), Recurring (quarterly), and Recurring (yearly).
* **Additional comments** (comments, note, required: false): If you have any additional information or comments about your donation, please enter it here.
* **Company** (company, text, required: false): If you are representing a company, enter the company name here.
* **Contact name** (contact_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who will be contacted about the donation.
* **Contact email** (contact_email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person who will be contacted about the donation.
* **Contact phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the person who will be contacted about the donation.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is valid and can be used for communication about your donation.
* If you are making a recurring donation, please be aware that you will be contacted regularly to confirm your donation.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us through the provided contact information.
