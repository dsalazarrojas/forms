# Emergency Fundraising Benchmarking Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data and feedback on emergency fundraising surveys. It is meant to be completed by individuals who have participated in or responded to emergency fundraising surveys.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant survey questions that apply to you.
2. Provide the results of your emergency fundraising benchmarking survey.
3. Enter the date and time of the survey.
4. Enter the number of respondents.
5. Enter your emergency fundraising benchmarking survey score.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Questions** (`survey_questions`, text, optional): Enter any relevant survey questions that you have been asked.
* **Additional Survey Questions** (`survey_questions_2`, text, required): Enter any additional survey questions that were asked.
* **Emergency Fundraising Benchmarking Surveys** (`emergency_fundraising_benchmarking_surveys`, select one, required): Select the emergency fundraising benchmarking survey you participated in or responded to.

* **Date of Survey** (`date_of_survey`, date, required): Enter the date of the survey.
* **Time of Survey** (`time_of_survey`, time, required): Enter the time of the survey.
* **Number of Respondents** (`number_of_respondents`, number, required): Enter the number of respondents.
