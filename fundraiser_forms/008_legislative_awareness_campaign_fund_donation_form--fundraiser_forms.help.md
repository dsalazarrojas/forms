# Legislative Awareness Campaign Fund Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for the Legislative Awareness Campaign, a fund established to support efforts to raise awareness about legislative issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields, marked with a *.
2. Choose a valid payment method from the options provided.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Campaign ID (1)**: This is an optional field to associate your donation with a specific campaign.
* **Donor Name (2)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Donation Amount (3)**: Enter the amount you wish to donate. Make sure to enter a numerical value.
* **Email (4)**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Payment Method (6)**: Choose how you would like to pay for your donation:
	+ Cash: Cash payment at our office.
	+ Credit Card: We accept Visa, Mastercard, and Amex.
	+ PayPal: Use your PayPal account to make a secure online payment.
	+ Check: Mail a check to the address provided below.
* **Notes (7)**: Enter any additional information or comments you would like to provide.
* **Donor Organization (8)**: If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Address (9)**: Enter your physical address.
* **City (10)**: Enter your city.
* **State (11)**: Enter your state.
* **Zip (12)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Credit Card Number (13)**: If you are paying by credit card, enter the 16-digit number.
* **CVV (14)**: If you are paying by credit card, enter the 3-digit security code found on the back of your card.
* **Expiration Date (15)**: If you are paying by credit card, enter the expiration date.
* **Expiration Month (16)**: If you are paying by credit card, select the expiration month of your card. 

Note: Please ensure that you have all necessary fields filled before submitting the form.
