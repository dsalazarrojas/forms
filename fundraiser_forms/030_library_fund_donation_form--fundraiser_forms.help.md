This help guide is for the "library_fund_donation_form" form. The purpose of this form is to collect information about donations made to a library fund. The form is designed to gather details about the donor, their contact information, and the specifics of the donation, such as the amount, method, and recipient of the donation. The form is intended for internal use within the library or organization, ensuring that accurate and consistent data is collected for record-keeping and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Confirm Recipient" option to specify who the donation is for (Library, Organization, or Other).
2. Enter the donor's email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
3. Enter the donor's phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
4. Confirm the donation amount in the "Confirm Donation Amount" field.
5. Select the payment method from the options provided (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
6. Provide additional payment details in the "Confirm Payment Details" field.
7. Enter any other relevant information in the "Confirm Donation Date" and "Confirm Donation Time" fields.
8. Confirm the payment method again in the "Confirm Payment Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirm Recipient**: Select the entity receiving the donation, such as Library, Organization, or Other.
	+ **Donor Info**: Enter the name and contact information of the donor.
	+ **Payment Info**: Enter details about the payment method and amount.
* **Confirm Email**: Enter the donor's email address.
* **Confirm Phone**: Enter the donor's phone number.
* **Confirm Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation.
* **Confirm Payment Method**: Select the method of payment (Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Confirm Payment Details**: Enter additional details about the payment.
* **Confirm Donation Date**: Enter the date of the donation.
* **Confirm Donation Time**: Enter the time of the donation.
* **Confirm Donation Amount 2**: This field is intended to confirm the donation amount.
* **Confirm Payment Method 2**: Select the payment method again, to ensure accuracy.
* **Confirm Payment Details 2**: Enter any additional payment details.
* **Confirm Total Donated**: Enter the total amount donated.
* **Confirm Fundraising Goal**: Enter any additional details about the fundraising goal.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the library or organization's administrative contact.
