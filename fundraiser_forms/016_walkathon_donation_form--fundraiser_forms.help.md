# Walkathon Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for making donations to the Walkathon campaign. It is intended for both participants and donors. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the amount you wish to donate by typing a numerical value in the "Donation Amount" field.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the donor's name and email address in the "Donor Name" and "Donor Email" fields, respectively.
4. Choose a payment gateway (e.g. Stripe, PayPal, or Square) and payment plan (e.g. Monthly, One-time, or Recurring) that suits your needs.
5. Select the frequency of your donation (e.g. One-time, Monthly, or Quarterly).
6. Choose your preferred payment type (e.g. Card or Bank-transfer).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, not required): Enter the amount you wish to donate, in a numerical value.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name as the donor.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Donor Name** (`donor_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person you are donating on behalf of.
* **Donor Email** (`donor_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the person you are donating on behalf of.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Amount Given** (`amount_given`, number, not required): This field will display the total amount donated by the participant to date.
* **Payment Gateway** (`payment_gateway`, select_one, not required): Select the payment method you wish to use (e.g. Stripe, PayPal, or Square).
* **Payment Gateway Selected** (`payment_gateway_selected`, text, not required): This field is used internally to track the selected payment gateway. Do not fill this field.
* **Payment Plan** (`payment_gateway_plan`, select_one, not required): Choose the payment plan that suits your needs (e.g. Monthly, One-time, or Recurring).
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, not required): Select the frequency of your donation (e.g. One-time, Monthly, or Quarterly).
* **Recurring Payment Frequency** (`recurring_payment_frequency`, select_one, not required): This field is used internally to track the frequency of recurring payments. Do not fill this field.
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, not required): Choose your preferred payment type (e.g. Card or Bank-transfer).
* **Donation Frequency 2** (`donation_frequency_2`, select_one, not required): Select the frequency of your donation (e.g. Monthly or Quarterly).
* **Payment Gateway Plan 2** (`payment_gateway_plan_2`, text, not required): This field is used internally to track the selected payment gateway plan. Do not fill this field.
* **Custom Message** (`custom_message`, text, not required): Enter any additional message you wish to include with your donation.

Please note that some fields may not be required for you to fill, but providing accurate and complete information helps the Walkathon campaign organizers to better process your donation.
