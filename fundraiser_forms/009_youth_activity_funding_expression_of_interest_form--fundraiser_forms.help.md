# Youth Activity Funding Expression Of Interest Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for expressing interest in youth activity funding to support projects and activities that align with our funding priorities. It is an internal expression of interest form to help us understand your project's requirements and suitability for funding.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Required Information
Before starting, please ensure you have all the necessary information ready. You will need to provide personal and project details, as well as contact information for yourself and/or your organization.

### Step 2: Fill In The Form
Complete each field accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 1`): Enter your name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`id: 3`): Enter your valid email address.
* **Organization** (`id: 4`): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Phone** (`id: 5`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`id: 6`): Enter your physical address.
* **Project Name** (`id: 7`): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description** (`id: 8`): Briefly describe your project's objective, goals, and expected outcomes.
* **Requested Funds** (`id: 9`): Enter the amount of funds you are requesting.
* **Project Start Date** (`id: 10`): Enter the proposed start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (`id: 11`): Enter the proposed end date of your project.
* **Category** (`id: 12`): Choose one or multiple categories that your project belongs to. Please note that this is a select-multiple field.
* **Sub Category** (`id: 13`): Choose the sub category for your project.
* **Location** (`id: 14`): Enter the location of your project.
* **Submitted By** (`id: 15`): Enter the name and position of the person submitting this form.
* **Project Budget** (`id: 16`): Enter the budget for your project.
* **Youth Group** (`id: 17`): Enter the name of the youth group you are representing.
* **Contact Person** (`id: 18`): Enter the name and contact information of the person to contact for your project.
* **Email** (`id: 19`): Enter a contact email address for your project.
* **Phone** (`id: 20`): Enter a contact phone number for your project.
* **Time** (`id: 21`): Enter the duration of your project in the format "HH:MM".
* **Date** (`id: 22`): Enter the date of submission.
* **Notes** (`id: 23`): If you have any additional information or clarification, enter them here.
* **Assigned Tool** (`id: 24`): If your project requires specific tools, choose 'Yes' if they are assigned.
* **Submitting For** (`id: 25`): Choose who is submitting this form on behalf of the project.

### Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We appreciate your interest in youth activity funding and look forward to reviewing your application.
