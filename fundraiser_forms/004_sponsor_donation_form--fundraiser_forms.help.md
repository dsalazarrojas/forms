The purpose of the Sponsor Donation Form is to collect information from sponsors and supporters who want to donate to a cause or organization. This form helps to ensure that the donation process is smooth and secure while also allowing the organization to track and manage sponsor contributions effectively. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately to avoid any issues with the donation process.

## Purpose
The Sponsor Donation Form is designed to collect vital information from sponsors who wish to contribute to a specific cause or organization. This form provides a structured and organized way to collect necessary details that will help process the donation efficiently and securely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sponsor Donation Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **sponsor_information** section with your contact details and any relevant information about yourself or your organization.
2. Provide your **contact_information** in the next section, including any necessary contact details such as email or phone number.
3. Choose your preferred **payment_method** from the provided options.
4. Enter the **amount_donated** you wish to contribute.
5. Select the **date_donated** when you plan to make the donation.
6. Optionally, provide the **time_donated** if you prefer to donate at a specific time.
7. Write a brief **sponsor_message** to express your motivation for the donation or any specific requirements you may have.
8. Enter your **email** address for contact purposes.
9. Provide your **phone** number if you wish to be contacted.
10. Finally, confirm that your **confirm_payment** details are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **sponsor_information**: This section is where you can enter any information about yourself or your organization that will be helpful for us to know.
* **contact_information**: Please provide any necessary contact details such as email or phone number so we can reach out if needed.
* **payment_method**: Choose how you wish to make the donation (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **amount_donated**: Enter the amount you wish to contribute.
* **date_donated**: Specify the date when you plan to make the donation.
* **time_donated**: If you prefer to donate at a specific time, select it here.
* **sponsor_message**: A brief message expressing your motivation for the donation is welcome.
* **email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone**: Provide your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **confirm_payment**: Confirm that your payment details are accurate.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are not mandatory, but providing accurate information will help us process your donation efficiently.
