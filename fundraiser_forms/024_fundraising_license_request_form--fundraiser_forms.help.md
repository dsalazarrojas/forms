# Fundraising License Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundraising License Request Form is used to collect information about your project and license request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through the form carefully and ensure you understand each field.
2. Fill out your full name in the field labeled "Full Name".
3. Enter your contact email address in the field labeled "Email".
4. Enter your contact phone number in the field labeled "Contact Phone".
5. For your project, select the appropriate type from the options listed under "Project Type" (e.g., Art, Literature, Music, etc.).
6. Provide a detailed description of your project in the field labeled "Project Details".
7. If you project requires funding, enter the budget amount in the field labeled "Project Budget".
8. Choose the type of license you are requesting (e.g., Non Profit, For-Profit) from the options under "License Type".
9. Indicate the status of your license request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Revoked) from the options under "License Status".
10. Select the expiration date of your license from the calendar picker or enter it manually in the field labeled "License Expires On".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Project Type** (Select One, Optional): Select the type of project you are requesting a license for (e.g., Art, Literature, Music, etc.).
* **Project Details** (Text, Optional): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Project Budget** (Number, Optional): Enter the budget amount for your project.
* **License Type** (Select One, Optional): Choose the type of license you are requesting (e.g., Non Profit, For-Profit).
* **License Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Indicate the status of your license request (e.g., Pending, Approved, Denied, Revoked).
* **License Expires On** (Date, Optional): Select the date your license expires or enter it manually in YYYY-MM-DD format.
