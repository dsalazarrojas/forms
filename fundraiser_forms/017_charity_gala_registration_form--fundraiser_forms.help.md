# Charity Gala Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Charity Gala Registration Form" is a tool for registering attendees for a charity gala event. The form is designed to collect information about the attendee, their guests, and their payment details. This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the form and provide explanations for each field to ensure a smooth and accurate registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the "Attendee Information" section.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** for event communications.
3. If applicable, provide your **Organization or Company Name**.
4. In the "Guest Information" section, list the **Number of Guests** attending and their **Names**.
5. Select the **Gala Date** for the event.
6. Choose the **Ticket Type** (Individual, Couple, Group of 4, Table of 8, or VIP).
7. Enter the **Number of Tickets** needed.
8. If necessary, provide **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** for special meal needs.
9. In the "Payment Information" section, enter the **Price Per Ticket** and **Total Amount Due** (calculated).
10. Optionally, add an **Additional Donation Amount** if you want to give more.
11. Finally, enter your **Registration Date** (today's date).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is spelled on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address for event communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for event organizers.
* **Organization or Company Name**: If you are attending on behalf of an organization, provide its name.
* **Number of Guests**: Enter the number of guests attending the event.
* **Names of Guests**: List the names of all guests attending.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If you have any special meal needs, describe them here.
* **Gala Date**: Select the date of the gala event.
* **Ticket Type**: Choose the type of ticket you are purchasing (Individual, Couple, Group of 4, Table of 8, or VIP).
* **Number of Tickets**: Enter the number of tickets needed for your group.
* **Seating Preference**: If you have any seating preferences, describe them here.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed**: If you require any accessibility accommodations, describe them here.
* **Price Per Ticket**: Enter the total price per ticket.
* **Total Amount Due**: The calculated total amount due is automatically calculated based on the ticket type and quantity.
* **Additional Donation Amount**: Optionally, enter an additional donation amount if you wish to give more.
* **Registration Date**: Enter today's date to confirm your registration.

By following this guide, end-users should be able to complete the "Charity Gala Registration Form" accurately and efficiently.
