# Donor Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Survey form is a tool to gather information from donors about themselves, their motivations, and their interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Donor Information in the first field, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Answer the questions in the second field, providing as much information as possible.
3. Enter your Contact Information in the third field, including your name, phone number, and email address.
4. Provide any comments or feedback in the fourth field.
5. If you wish to specify the amount donated, please enter it in the fifth field.
6. Choose the options that best describe why you donate in the sixth field.
7. Select the interests that apply to you from the list in the seventh field.
8. Use the eighth field to add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Donor Information (1)** (`donar_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about yourself, such as your name, address, or a brief description of your involvement with the organization.
* **Questions (2)** (questions, text, optional): Answer any questions provided, providing as much information as possible about your experience with the organization.
* **Contact Information (3)** (contact_info, text, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and email address for us to contact you.
* **Comments (4)** (comments, text, optional): Provide any comments or feedback about your experience with the organization.
* **Amount Donated (5)** (amount_donated, number, optional): If you wish to specify the amount donated, please enter it here.
* **Why You Donate (6)** (select_one, optional): Choose the option that best describes why you donate. This will help us understand your motivations.
* **Interests (7)** (select_multiple, optional): Select the interests that apply to you from the list provided. This will help us tailor our engagement strategies to you better.
* **Any Other Comments (8)** (note, optional): Use this field to add any additional comments or information about yourself.
