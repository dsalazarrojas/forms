# Fullscreen Donation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect donations for a fundraising campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Organization** (if applicable), and **Email**.
2. Choose an **Donation Amount** that fits your budget and desired contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Required: False):
	+ This field is used to collect your first name, which will be used to address you in any future correspondence.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Required: False):
	+ This field is used to collect your last name, which will be used to address you in any future correspondence.
* **Organization** (Organization, Text, Required: False):
	+ If you are representing an organization, please enter its name here.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required: False):
	+ This field is used to collect your email address, which will be used to contact you about your donation or for other campaign-related purposes.
* **Donation Amount** (Donation Amount, Number, Required: False):
	+ Choose the amount you would like to donate. Please note that this field only accepts numerical values (e.g., 10, 25, or 50).

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter a valid email address in the **Email** field to ensure that we can contact you regarding your donation.
* If you experience any issues with the form, please don't hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.
