# Candle Fundraiser Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Candle Fundraiser Form is designed to collect necessary information from customers who are purchasing candles from a fundraiser event. Please ensure all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid duplication and ensure successful processing of your order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the Organization Details section with your organization's name and contact information.
2.  Select the Scented Candle Options that you would like to purchase.
3.  Enter the quantity of candles in total you are ordering.
4.  Provide your Contact Information, including your name, address, and phone number.
5.  Select your preferred payment method and total amount paid.
6.  Confirm your agreement with the final sale and indicate if you'd like more information on future fundraisers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Organization Name** (`fundraiser_organization_name`, required): Enter your organization's name, such as a charity or school club.
*   **Fundraiser Coordinator Name** (`fundraiser_coordinator_name`, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for coordinating the fundraiser.
*   **Contact Email** (`fundraiser_contact_email`, required): Enter your email address, which will be the primary point of contact for the fundraiser.
*   **Contact Phone** (`fundraiser_contact_phone`, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication about the order.
*   **Customer Full Name** (`fundraiser_customer_name`, required): Enter the name of the person purchasing the candles.
*   **Customer Address** (`fundraiser_customer_address`, optional): Enter the address where you'd like to receive your order. If you choose home delivery, this is where our driver will deliver the order.
*   **Scented Candle Options** (`scented_candle_choices`, required): Select the scents you would like to purchase from our available options:
    *   Lavender Bliss ($10)
    *   Vanilla Bean ($10)
    *   Ocean Breeze ($12)
    *   Apple Cinnamon ($12)
    *   Midnight Jasmine ($15)
*   **Quantity Total** (`fundraiser_candle_quantity`, required): Enter the total number of candles you are purchasing.
*   **Order Date** (`fundraiser_order_date`, required): Enter the date for the order.
*   **Pickup or Delivery Preference** (`pickup_delivery_preference`, required): Select how you'd like to receive your order:
    *   Pick up at Office
    *   Home Delivery ($5 extra)
*   **Payment Method** (`fundraiser_payment_method`, required): Select your preferred payment method:
    *   Credit/Debit Card
    *   Cash
    *   Check
    *   Venmo or PayPal
*   **Total Amount Paid** (`total_amount_paid_val`, required): Enter the total amount paid, including any additional delivery fees.
*   **Reference or Check Number** (`payment_reference_number`, optional): If you've paid with a check or other payment method with a reference number, enter it here.
*   **I understand all sales are final** (`sales_final_agreement`, required): Confirm your understanding of our refund policy.
*   **I would like more information on future fundraisers** (`future_fundraiser_info_pref`, optional): Indicate if you'd like more information on future fundraisers.
*   **Submission Date** (`fundraiser_submission_date_val`, required): Enter the date for the submission.

This help guide should provide a clear understanding of how to complete the form and what each field's purpose is.
