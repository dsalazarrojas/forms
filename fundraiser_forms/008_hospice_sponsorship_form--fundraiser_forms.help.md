# hospice_sponsorship_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track information about hospice sponsorships.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the charity name and address on page 1.
2. Enter the sponsor's contact information, including name, title, address, phone number, email, and organization (if applicable).
3. Specify the grant amount and date/time of completion.
4. Optionally, add additional notes or details about the grant in the "Notes" field.
5. Select the grant status from the dropdown menu or provide an alternative status in the "Grant Status Other" field.
6. If necessary, provide additional information about the sponsor's relationship and grant status.
7. Finally, add a signature and any additional organization information (name, logo, website, contact name, and contact email).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Charity Name** (Page 1, required): Enter the name of the charity being sponsored.
* **Address** (Page 1, required): Enter the address of the charity being sponsored.
* **Phone Number** (Page 1, required): Enter the phone number of the charity.
* **Email** (Page 1, required): Enter the email of the charity.
* **Grant Amount** (Page 1, required): Enter the amount of the grant.
* **Grant Purpose** (Page 1, optional): Provide a brief description of the grant purpose.
* **Notes** (Page 1, optional): Add any additional notes or details about the grant.
* **Date Completed** (Page 1, optional): Enter the date of completion.
* **Time Completed** (Page 1, optional): Enter the time of completion.
* **Sponsor Name** (Page 2, required): Enter the name of the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Title** (Page 2, required): Enter the title of the sponsor.
* **Organization** (Page 2, optional): Enter the sponsor's organization (if applicable).
* **Address Sponsor** (Page 2, optional): Enter the sponsor's address.
* **Phone Sponsor** (Page 2, optional): Enter the sponsor's phone number.
* **Email Sponsor** (Page 2, optional): Enter the sponsor's email.
* **Sponsor Relationship** (Page 2, optional): Describe the sponsor's relationship.
* **Grant Status** (Page 2, dropdown menu/optional): Select the grant status from the dropdown menu or provide an alternative status in the "Grant Status Other" field.
* **Grant Status Other** (Page 2, optional): Enter an alternative grant status if not listed in the dropdown menu.
* **Notes Staff** (Page 2, optional): Add any additional notes or details about the grant.
* **Signature** (Page 3, required): Add a signature to confirm completion.
* **Organization Logo** (Page 3, optional): Enter the sponsor's organization logo.
* **Organization Name** (Page 3, optional): Enter the sponsor's organization name.
* **Website** (Page 3, optional): Enter the sponsor's website.
* **Contact Name** (Page 3, optional): Enter the contact name.
* **Contact Email** (Page 3, optional): Enter the contact email.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use accurate and correct information for each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the relevant sections in this guide or consult with the relevant staff member.
