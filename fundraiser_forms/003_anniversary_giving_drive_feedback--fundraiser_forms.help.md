# Anniversary Giving Drive Feedback - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from individuals who participated in the anniversary giving drive. The goal is to understand their experiences, satisfaction, and willingness to participate in future drives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Full Name** in the first field.
2.  Enter your **Email Address** for follow-up purposes.
3.  Select all that apply from the **How Did You Participate?** field, which covers various ways you contributed, such as making a donation, volunteering time, sharing on social media, attending events, and more.
4.  Choose the **Donation Amount Range** that best reflects your contribution.
5.  Rate your **Overall Satisfaction** with the giving drive, using the provided scale.
6.  Evaluate the **Communication Rating**, considering the clarity and frequency of communications.
7.  Assess the **Ease of Donation Process**, indicating how easy it was to make your donation.
8.  Rate the **Event Organization**, considering the organization of events and activities.
9.  Select your **Primary Motivation** for participating, which could be a personal connection, anniversary significance, community impact, tax benefits, social pressure, matching gifts, or something else.
10. Choose all that apply from the **How Did You Hear About Us?** field, which covers various sources of information, such as email newsletters, social media, word of mouth, website, print materials, news articles, and more.
11. Indicate how well you understand the **Impact of Your Contribution** using the provided scale.
12. Rate the **Transparency Rating**, considering the organization's transparency about fund usage.
13. Choose your **Likelihood to Donate Again**, expressing your willingness to participate in future drives.
14. Select your **Likelihood to Recommend**, indicating if you would recommend the giving drive to others.
15. Provide any **Suggestions for Improvement** you may have for future drives.
16. Share any **Additional Comments** you would like to mention.
17. Indicate your interest in **Volunteering** for future events, if applicable.
18. Finally, choose your preferred contact method for future drives, such as email, phone, text message, mail, or no contact at all.

*  **Impact Understanding**: Indicate how well you understand the impact of your contribution.
*  **Transparency Rating**: Rate the organization's transparency about fund usage.
*  **Likelihood to Donate Again**: Express your willingness to participate in future drives.
*  **Likelihood to Recommend**: Indicate if you would recommend the giving drive to others.
