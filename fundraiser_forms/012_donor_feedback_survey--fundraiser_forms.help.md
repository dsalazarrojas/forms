# Donor Feedback Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Feedback Survey is designed to gather feedback from donors about their experience with our organization. Your input will help us understand your satisfaction, preferred contact method, and any suggestions for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the survey, simply follow these steps:

1. For each question, choose the most accurate answer from the available options.
2. If you have any suggestions or comments, type them in the designated text box.
3. Enter your email address and phone number in the respective text boxes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Overall experience with our organization**
**Overall experience with our organization (Yes/No)**

This question asks about your overall experience with our organization. Select "Yes" if you have had a positive experience, and "No" if you have not.

### 2. **How much have you given to our organization in the past 5 years?**
**How much have you given to our organization in the past 5 years?**

Enter the total amount you have donated to our organization in the past 5 years.

### 3. **What is your preferred contact method?**
**What is your preferred contact method? (Phone/Email/Mail/In-person)**

Choose the method that you prefer to communicate with us. If you selected "Phone" or "Mail", you will be contacted through that method. If you selected "Email", we will only contact you through email. If you selected "In-person", you will be contacted through in-person meetings or events.

### 4. **How satisfied are you with our fundraising efforts?**
**How satisfied are you with our fundraising efforts? (Very satisfied/Somewhat satisfied/Neutral/Somewhat dissatisfied/Very dissatisfied)**

Choose the answer that best reflects your level of satisfaction with our fundraising efforts.

### 5. **Do you have any suggestions for our organization?**
**Do you have any suggestions for our organization?**

If you have any suggestions, write them here. This is a text box for any ideas or comments you might have.

### 6. **What is your email address?**
**What is your email address?**

Enter your current email address.

### 7. **What is your phone number?**
**What is your phone number?**

Enter your current phone number.

### 8. **Comments**
**Comments**

If you have any additional comments or thoughts, write them here. This is a text box for any further ideas or suggestions.
