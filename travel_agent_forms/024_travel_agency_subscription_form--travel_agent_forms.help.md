# Travel Agency Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agency Subscription Form is designed to help you subscribe to our travel services. Please fill out this form to create a new subscription or update your existing one. We'll use the information you provide to tailor our services to your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" fields, respectively.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Customer Phone" field.
3. Select your preferred subscription type from the "Subscription Type" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the date you'd like to start your subscription in the "Subscription Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Name**: <label>customer_name</label> (optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Customer Email**: <label>customer_email</label> (optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Customer Phone**: <label>customer_phone</label> (optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Subscription Type**: <label>subscription_type</label> (optional): Choose from:
	+ Monthly
	+ Annual
* **Payment Method**: <label>payment_method</label> (optional): Choose from:
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Payroll Deduction
	+ Online Payment
	+ Check Payment
* **Subscription Date**: <label>subscription_date</label> (optional): Enter the date you'd like to start your subscription. Please use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
