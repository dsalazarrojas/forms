# Travel Experience Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help travel agents understand the quality of service they provide and gather feedback from customers about their recent travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the "trip_details" field with a brief description of your recent trip.
2. Select all travel agents involved in your trip from the list in the "travel_agent" field. You can select multiple agents.
3. Choose your level of satisfaction with the trip from the "trip_satisfaction" field.
4. Provide any additional comments or feedback about your trip in the "trip_comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **trip_details** (`trip_details`, text, optional): Please describe your recent trip, including destination, mode of transportation, and any notable events or experiences.
* **travel_agent** (`travel_agent`, select multiple, optional): Select all travel agents who assisted you with your trip. You can choose multiple agents if there were multiple agents involved.
* **trip_satisfaction** (`trip_satisfaction`, select one, optional): Choose your level of satisfaction with the trip, from "Very Satisfied" to "Very Dissatisfied".
* **trip_comments** (`trip_comments`, text, optional): Share any additional comments or feedback about your trip experience.

## Tips

* Be as descriptive as possible when filling out the "trip_details" field to help travel agents understand your experience.
* Select all travel agents who assisted you with your trip to ensure accurate feedback is received.
* Choose your level of satisfaction honestly, as this will help travel agents improve their service.
* Use the "trip_comments" field to provide any additional context or feedback that may not fit in the other fields.
