# Travel Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from the user for travel-related purposes. This form helps gather details about the user's travel destination, dates, travel type, number of travelers, and other relevant information. This form is used by our travel agency to assist in planning and organizing travel arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Travel Inquiry Form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the destination of your travel.
2. Choose your travel dates (yes or no).
3. Specify the type of travel you are interested in.
4. Select the type of travel (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
5. Choose the number of travelers (if applicable).
6. Enter the travel price (if applicable).
7. Provide your email address.
8. Enter your phone number.
9. Add any additional notes about your travel.
10. Select the tool(s) assigned to you (if applicable).
11. Choose the category of your travel (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
12. Select the form ID (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination**: Please enter the location you plan to travel to.
* **Travel Dates**: Choose whether you are traveling on yes or no.
* **Travel Type**: Enter a description of the type of travel you are planning.
* **travel_type_type**: Select the type of travel (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **travel_type_type2**: Select multiple types of travel (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Number of Travelers**: Enter the number of people traveling with you.
* **Travel Price**: Enter the price of your travel (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your travel.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool(s) assigned to you.
* **Assigned Tool 2**: Select multiple tools assigned to you.
* **Category**: Choose the category of your travel (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Form ID**: Enter the form ID (if applicable).
* **Form ID 2**: Select the form ID (if applicable).
* **Output File**: Select the output file to generate.

Note: If a field is marked as "required" in the form, please ensure that it is completed accurately and thoroughly.
