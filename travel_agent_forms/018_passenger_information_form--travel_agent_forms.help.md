# Passenger Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Information Form is designed to collect essential details from passengers for travel purposes. It consists of three sections: Passenger Information, Contact Information, and Travel Details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your passenger information in the "Passenger Information" section.
* Provide your contact details in the "Contact Information" section.
* Enter your travel information in the "Travel Details" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Information (Page 1)**
    * **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
    * **Type**: (Optional) Select your passenger type (e.g., adult, child, infant).
    * **Required/Optional**: Required

* **Contact Information (Page 2)**
    * **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your contact information.
    * **Email**: Enter your email address.
    * **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
    * **Required/Optional**: Required

* **Travel Details (Page 3)**
    * **Departure City**: Enter the city you are departing from.
    * **Destination City**: Enter the city you are traveling to.
    * **Travel Date**: Enter your travel date.
    * **Required/Optional**: Required

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
