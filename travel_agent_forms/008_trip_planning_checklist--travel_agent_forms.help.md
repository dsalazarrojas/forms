# Trip Planning Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for trip planning purposes, including travel dates, agency information, travel type, destination, duration, group size, and other relevant details. The form is intended for travel agencies or tour operators to collect data from customers or clients for planning and organizing trips.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the required information available before filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, focusing on one section at a time.
3.  Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
4.  If a field is optional, only fill it out if it is relevant to your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **travel_dates** (1, date, required): Enter the travel dates for the trip (start and end dates).
*   **travel_agency** (2, select_one, optional): Select the travel agency handling your trip.
*   **travel_type** (3, select_one, optional): Choose the type of travel (Domestic, International, Group).
*   **trip_type** (4, select_multiple, optional): Select the purpose of the trip (Vacation, Business, Event).
*   **destination** (5, text, optional): Enter the destination of your trip.
*   **duration** (6, number, optional): Specify the duration of your trip.
*   **group_size** (7, select_multiple, optional): Enter the size of your travel group (1-5, 6-10, 11-15).
*   **travel_agency_email** (8, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the travel agency.
*   **travel_agency_phone** (9, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
*   **notes** (10, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your trip.
*   **flight_details** (11, text, optional): Enter the flight details for your trip.
*   **arrival_info** (12, text, optional): Enter the arrival information for your trip.
*   **flight_booking** (13, select_multiple, optional): Indicate whether or not you have booked flights.
*   **travel_agency_phone2** (14, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency (second instance).
*   **trip_details** (15, text, optional): Add any additional trip details.
*   **departure_info** (16, text, optional): Enter the departure information for your trip.
*   **travel_agency_email2** (17, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for the travel agency (second instance).
*   **flight_numbers** (18, text, optional): Enter the flight numbers.
*   **flight_status** (19, select_multiple, optional): Select the flight status (Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled).
*   **arrival_status** (20, select_multiple, optional): Select the arrival status (On Time, Delayed, Arrived).
*   **notes2** (21, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your trip.
*   **travel_agency** (22, select_one, optional): Select the travel agency handling your trip.
*   **travel_agency_phone3** (23, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency (third instance).
*   **travel_agency_phone4** (24, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency (fourth instance).
*   **travel_agency_phone5** (25, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency (fifth instance).

## Tips

*   Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
*   Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
*   If you have any additional questions or concerns, contact the travel agency or tour operator handling your trip.
