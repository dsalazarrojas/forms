# travel_agency_incident_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by travel agencies to report and track incidents that occur within their operations. The form helps agencies to gather and document incident information in a standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `incident_date` field with the date the incident occurred.
2. Enter the `incident_time` field with the time the incident happened.
3. Provide the `incident_location` field with the location where the incident took place.
4. Describe the `incident_description` field with a brief summary of what happened.
5. Choose the correct `incident_status` from the options provided.
6. Select the type of incident that occurred from the `incident_type` field.
7. Enter the cause of the incident in the `incident_cause` field.
8. Choose who reported the incident from the `incident_reporter` field.
9. Select whether an investigation was conducted or not from the `incident_investigation` field.
10. Choose whether the incident was resolved or not from the `incident_resolution` field.
11. Select whether follow-up actions were taken from the `incident_follow_up` field.
12. Add any additional notes about the incident in the `incident_notes` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **incident_form** (1): This is the main page of the form.
* **date_of_incident** (2): Enter the date the incident occurred.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **time_of_incident** (3): Enter the time the incident happened.
	+ Format: 24-hour clock (HH:MM)
* **incident_location** (4): Provide the location where the incident took place.
* **description_of_incident** (5): Describe the incident in detail.
* **incident_status** (6): Choose the current status of the incident.
	+ Options: Active/Inactive
* **incident_type_of_incident** (7): Select the type of incident.
	+ Options: Accident/Natural Event/Incident
* **incident_cause** (8): Enter the cause of the incident.
* **incident_reporter** (9): Choose who reported the incident.
	+ Options: Travel Agent/Tour Guide/Other
* **incident_investigation** (10): Select whether an investigation was conducted.
	+ Options: Conducted/Not Conducted
* **incident_resolution** (11): Choose whether the incident was resolved.
	+ Options: Completed/Not Completed
* **incident_follow_up** (12): Select whether follow-up actions were taken.
	+ Options: Done/Not Done
* **incident_notes** (13): Add any additional notes about the incident (optional).
* **incident_attachment** (14): Attach any relevant documents or evidence (optional).

Note: The `incident_attachment` field allows you to attach files or images related to the incident.
