# Post Travel Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from travelers after they have completed a trip. Its purpose is to help our travel agents understand their strengths and weaknesses, and to provide feedback that can help us improve the services we offer.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Please fill out this form completely, even if it takes a few minutes.
*   Answer the questions honestly and to the best of your ability.
*   You may use as much space as needed to provide your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Travel Survey** (`travel_survey`, text, required: false): 
    This field is meant to provide a space for you to describe your trip experience. Please provide any details you feel are relevant to your experience with our travel agency.
*   **Rate** (`rating`, number, required: false): 
    Please rate your overall experience with our travel agency, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): 
    Please provide any additional comments or feedback you may have about your experience with our travel agency.
*   **Email** (`email_address`, email, required: false): 
    Please provide your email address so we can get in touch with you if needed.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): 
    Please provide your phone number so we can get in touch with you if needed.

## Tips

*   If you have any issues with your trip, please do not hesitate to contact us. We value your feedback and want to hear about any problems you may have encountered.
*   If you would like to provide feedback on how we can improve our services, please use the "Comments" field to do so.
*   Please note that the information you provide will be kept confidential and will be used solely for improving our services.
