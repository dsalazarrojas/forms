# Luxury Cruise Packages Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in luxury cruise packages. It will help travel agents gather essential details about the user's preferences, including their name, contact information, and travel preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your Phone Number for the travel agent to contact you.
4. Indicate if you have a Travel Agent assigned to assist you.
5. Select the option that best describes your preference for Travel Agencies.
6. Choose a Preferred Travel Date for your cruise.
7. Specify the Travel Length of your desired cruise.
8. Select your Budget for the cruise.
9. Indicate if you have any Cruise Preferences.
10. Enter any Special Requests you may have.
11. Add any comments or notes for the Travel Agent in the Travel Agent Note field.
12. Add any comments or notes for the Travel Agency in the Travel Agency Note field.
13. Confirm if you are the one who submitted the form (Submitted By).
14. Choose the date you submitted the form (Submitted On).
15. Confirm if someone updated the form (Updated By).
16. Choose the date the form was updated (Updated On).
17. Confirm if the form was deleted (Deleted By).
18. Choose the date the form was deleted (Deleted On).
19. Confirm if the form was created (Created By).
20. Choose the date the form was created (Created On).
21. Select the Form Status (Active or Inactive).
22. Add any notes for the Form Status (Form Status Notes).
23. Enter the number of times the form was submitted (Submission Count).
24. Enter the Form ID for internal purposes (only for travel agents).

* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required): Enter any special requests you may have.
* **Travel Agent Note** (`travel_agent_note`, `note`, required): Add any comments or notes for the travel agent.
* **Travel Agency Note** (`travel_agency_note`, `note`, required): Add any comments or notes for the travel agency.
