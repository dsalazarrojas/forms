# Travel Agents Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agents Association Membership Form is designed to collect information from travel agents for registration, membership update, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the First Name field with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This is your contact address for communication with the association.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field. This will help us reach you in case of any urgent matters.
4. Select your Membership Type by choosing from the options provided in the Membership Type dropdown menu. This determines your membership status and benefits.
5. Enter your work experience in the Work Experience field. This helps us assess your qualifications and suitability for membership.
6. For the Years of Experience field, enter the number of years you have been working as a travel agent.
7. Enter your Job Title in the Job Title field. This is your official title within your agency.
8. Enter the number of years you have been serving as a travel agent in the Years of Service field.
9. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field. This is required for age verification purposes.
10. Select your Membership Status by choosing from the options provided in the Membership Status dropdown menu. This indicates your current status within the association.
11. Choose your Payment Method by selecting from the options provided in the Payment Method dropdown menu. This determines how you will make your membership payments.
12. Enter your preferred Payment Terms in the Payment Terms field. This helps us understand your payment preferences.
13. Enter your Travel Agency details in the Travel Agency field. This is your affiliated agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email**: Your contact email address for communication with the association.
*   **Phone**: Your contact phone number for urgent matters.
*   **Membership Type** (`Membership Type`): Choose from **Free**, **Pro**, or **Premium** options to determine your membership status and benefits.
*   **Work Experience**: Enter your work experience in the field.
*   **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working as a travel agent.
*   **Job Title**: Enter your official title within your agency.
*   **Years of Service**: Enter the number of years you have been serving as a travel agent.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your Date of Birth for age verification purposes.
*   **Membership Status** (`Membership Status`): Choose from **Active**, **Non-Available** options to indicate your current status within the association.
*   **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`): Choose from **Card** or **Bank Transfer** options to determine your payment method.
*   **Payment Terms**: Enter your preferred payment terms.
*   **Travel Agency**: Enter your travel agency details.
*   **Membership Fee** (`Membership Fee`): Enter the membership fee for your chosen membership type.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please contact the association.
* This form can be used for registration, renewal, or update of membership details.
