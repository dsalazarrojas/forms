# Cruise Cancellation Claim Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Cancellation Claim Form is designed to collect information for travel agencies to submit claims for cancelled cruises. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the name of your travel agency in the "Travel Agency Name" field.
2. Provide your contact email and phone number in the "Contact Email" and "Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
3. Enter the "Travel Date" and "Travel Time" of the cancelled cruise in the respective fields.
4. If applicable, enter the "Reason for Cancellation" and the "Claim Amount" of the cancellation.
5. Enter the "Travel Agency ID" (if available) and the "Customer Name" and "Customer Email" of the customer who was affected by the cancellation.
6. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agency Name (1)**: Enter the name of your travel agency as it appears on your business card or website.
* **Contact Email (2)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further information or clarification.
* **Contact Phone (3)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information or clarification.
* **Reason for Cancellation (4)**: Enter the reason for the cancellation, such as "Weather conditions", "Ship maintenance", "Illness of crew", etc.
* **Travel Date (5)**: Enter the date of the cancelled cruise in MM/DD/YY format.
* **Travel Time (6)**: Enter the departure and arrival time of the cancelled cruise in 12-hour AM/PM format.
* **Claim Amount (7)**: Enter the amount being claimed for the cancelled cruise.
* **Travel Agency ID (8)**: If available, enter the unique identifier for your travel agency.
* **Customer Name (9)**: Enter the name of the customer who was affected by the cancellation.
* **Customer Email (10)**: Enter the email address of the customer who was affected by the cancellation.
