# Group Travel Arrangement Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Travel Arrangement Request Form is used to collect information from travelers to facilitate efficient planning and arrangement of group travel. This includes gathering details about the travelers themselves, their travel preferences, and other relevant information to ensure a smooth travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Traveler Information (Group Travel Arrangement Request Form)**: This is the initial page where you will find questions about the travelers themselves. You can fill out this page first.
2.  **Travel Arrangement Details**: Once you have filled out the first page, click on the "Next" button to move on to the travel arrangement details page. This page includes questions about the trip itself, such as dates, departure and return airports, and accommodation preferences.
3.  **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required information, click the "Submit" button to submit your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Group Travel Arrangement Request Form

*   **Traveler Information (Group Travel Arrangement Request Form)**:
    *   **Traveler Information**: 
        Please provide information about the travelers in your group. This could include names, dates of birth, and any other relevant details.
    *   **Travel Arrangement Details**: 
        Provide details about the trip, such as the dates you plan to travel, departure and return airports, and your preferences for accommodation.
    *   **Trip Dates**: 
        Enter the dates of the trip you are planning. The format for the dates should be in the standard mm/dd/yyyy format.
    *   **Departure Airport**: 
        Select the departure airport for your trip.
    *   **Return Airport**: 
        Select the return airport for your trip.
    *   **Travel Agent**: 
        Enter the name of the travel agent assigned to your group.
    *   **Group Size**: 
        Enter the number of people in your group.
    *   **Trip Type**: 
        Select the type of trip you are taking (Business, Leisure, Other, or Personal).
    *   **Accommodation Preference**: 
        Select your preference for accommodation during the trip.
    *   **Special Requests**: 
        If you have any special requests, please list them here.
    *   **Comments**: 
        If you have any additional comments about your trip, please enter them here.
    *   **Contact E-mail**: 
        Enter your contact e-mail address.
    *   **Contact Number**: 
        Enter your contact number.
    *   **Assigned User**: 
        Enter the name of the user assigned to your trip.
    *   **Assigned Tool**: 
        Enter the name of the tool assigned to your trip.
    *   **Submitted By**: 
        The system will automatically fill out this field with the name of the person who submitted the request.
    *   **Created At**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
    *   **Updated At**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
    *   **Deleted At**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
    *   **Submitted At**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
    *   **Deleted By**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
    *   **Comments**: 
        If you have any additional comments about your trip, please enter them here.
    *   **Status**: 
        Select the status of your trip (In Progress, Rejected, Approved, or Done).
    *   **Submitted**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.
    *   **Assigned**: 
        This field is automatically populated by the system and should not be edited.

Note that some fields are automatically populated by the system and cannot be edited by the user.
