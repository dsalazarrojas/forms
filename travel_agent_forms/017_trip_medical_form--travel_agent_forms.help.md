# trip_medical_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trip_medical_form is designed to collect essential medical and contact information from travelers, ensuring their safety and well-being during their trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
2. Fill in the fields as instructed, providing accurate and complete information for each section.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler info (Traveler info, text, optional):** Enter your basic traveler information, such as your name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical conditions (Medical conditions, text, optional):** List any pre-existing medical conditions you may have, including allergies, chronic illnesses, or other relevant health concerns.
* **Medications (Medications, text, optional):** If you take any medications, list them here, including their dosages and frequency.
* **Allergies (Allergies, text, optional):** Specify any known allergies or sensitivities, including foods, substances, or environments.
* **Vaccinations (Vaccinations, text, optional):** Enter any vaccinations or immunizations you have, including dates and types.
* **Medical procedure (Medical procedure, text, optional):** If you have any pre-trip medical procedures or surgeries, provide details.
* **Emergency contact (Emergency contact, text, optional):** Name and contact information for an emergency contact person or organization.
* **Relationship (Relationship, text, optional):** Specify your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Contact address (Contact address, text, optional):** Enter the address and contact information for the emergency contact person.
* **Contact phone (Contact phone, text, optional):** Enter the phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Contact email (Contact email, text, optional):** Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical info (Medical info, text, optional):** Enter any other relevant medical information not covered in the above fields.
