# Tourist Group Transport Inquiry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a tourist group's transportation needs from a travel agent. It helps the travel agent to understand the group's preferences and requirements, ensuring that they can provide suitable services to meet their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your travel agent ID in the `travel_agent_id` field. This is the unique identifier assigned to you as a travel agent.
2. Enter the name of the group in the `group_name` field. This will help the travel agent to identify the group and their preferences.
3. Select the travel dates in the `travel_dates` field. This is when the group will be traveling.
4. Choose whether the group requires any transport services by selecting `Yes` or `No` in the `transport_type` field.
5. Enter the size of the group in the `group_size` field. This will help the travel agent to assess the group's transportation needs.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person in the `contact_email` field. This will allow the travel agent to communicate with the group.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the `contact_phone` field. This is the best contact method to reach the group.
8. Provide a detailed note about the group in the `group_details` field. This will help the travel agent to understand the group's specific needs.
9. Add any comments about the group in the `travel_agent_comments` field. This will help the travel agent to record their own thoughts and observations.
10. Add any comments from the group in the `group_comments` field. This will allow the group to provide their feedback and suggestions to the travel agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_agent_id**: This field is used to enter your unique travel agent ID.
* **group_name**: Enter the name of the group you are dealing with.
* **travel_dates**: Select the travel dates for the group's journey.
* **transport_type**: Choose whether the group requires transport services by selecting `Yes` or `No`.
* **group_size**: Enter the number of people in the group.
* **contact_email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **group_details**: Provide a detailed note about the group, including their specific needs and requirements.
* **travel_agent_comments**: Add any comments or observations from the travel agent.
* **group_comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the group.
* **travel_agent_comments**: Add any additional comments or notes from the travel agent.
* **group_comments**: Add any comments or feedback from the group.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely to ensure that the travel agent can provide the best services to the group.
* Use the correct format for dates (e.g., dd/mm/yyyy) when filling out the `travel_dates` field.
* Be clear and concise in the `group_details` field to help the travel agent understand the group's needs.
* Double-check the `contact_email` and `contact_phone` fields for accuracy to ensure that the travel agent can contact the group correctly.
* Use the `travel_agent_comments` field to record any observations or suggestions that may be helpful for future reference.
