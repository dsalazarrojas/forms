# Trip Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trip Evaluation Form is used by clients to provide feedback and evaluation of their trip experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your trip details, including any relevant information about your trip such as dates and destinations.
2. Specify the dates of your trip, including start and end dates.
3. Indicate whether or not you visited any of the trip locations.
4. Select the services you received or experienced during the trip.
5. Provide your feedback and comments about the trip.
6. Enter your name and email address so that we can contact you for further follow-up.
7. Enter your phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments about the trip.
9. Enter the name of the event planner or tour operator who arranged the trip.
10. Evaluate the trip on a scale of 1-10, with 10 being the highest rating.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details**: Please provide a brief description of your trip, including any relevant information such as destinations, activities, or notable events.
* **Trip Dates**: Please enter the start and end dates of your trip, in the format of MM/DD/YY.
* **Trip Locations**: Check the boxes for any locations you visited during the trip.
* **Trip Services**: Select the services you received or experienced during the trip.
* **Client Feedback**: Please provide your feedback and comments about the trip.
* **Client Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Client Email**: Please enter your email address so that we can contact you for further follow-up.
* **Client Phone**: Please enter your phone number so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Trip Notes**: Please provide any additional notes or comments about the trip.
* **Event Planner**: Please enter the name of the event planner or tour operator who arranged the trip.
* **Trip Evaluation**: Evaluate the trip on a scale of 1-10, with 10 being the highest rating.
