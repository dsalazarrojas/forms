# Travel Preferences Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather travel preferences information from end users. It helps us understand your preferences to tailor our services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully and ensure you understand the questions asked.
2. Complete all required fields (identified by a *).
3. For fields with options, select the most appropriate answer for your travel preferences.
4. For free text fields, provide detailed information as requested.
5. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel-preferences-questionnaire-intro**: This is a general introduction to the form. Please read it carefully to understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
* **travel-preferences-questionnaire-category**: This field asks about your travel preferences, specifically whether you prefer to travel with us. Please select 'Yes' if you are interested in travel with us.
* **travel-preferences-questionnaire-duration**: This field asks about the duration of your travel. Please select all durations that apply to your travel preferences.
* **travel-preferences-questionnaire-priority**: This is the main priority field for your travel preferences. Please select the most relevant option from the list.
* **travel-preferences-questionnaire-priority-1**: This field is for additional specific priority details. Please provide a brief description of your priority 1.
