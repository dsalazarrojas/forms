# Travel Informationistency Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Informationistency Form is designed to collect information about an upcoming trip, including travel dates, destinations, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Travel Details" page, which is the first page of the form.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with your travel information:
	* Travel dates (start and end dates)
	* Travel destinations (select multiple)
	* Travel type (e.g., Corporate Business, Event, Meeting)
	* Travel purpose
	* Travel agent (yes/no)
	* Travel agency (free text field)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Details** (`travel_information_form`): The travel details page collects general information about your trip.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`): Enter the start and end dates of your trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is a required field, but you can enter a date range or specific dates.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): Enter the start date of your trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): Enter the end date of your trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Travel Locations** (`travel_locations`): Select multiple destinations for your trip. You can choose from a list of cities, including New York, Los Angeles, and Chicago.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`): Select the type of travel you will be doing, such as Corporate Business, Event, or Meeting.
* **Travel Purpose** (`travel_pistency`): Enter a brief description of your travel purpose.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`): Indicate whether you will be using a travel agent for your trip.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`): Enter the name of the travel agency you will be using, if any.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
* Double-check your travel dates and destinations to ensure they are correct.
* If you are using a travel agent, please provide their name and any relevant contact information.
* If you are using a travel agency, please provide their name and any relevant contact information.
