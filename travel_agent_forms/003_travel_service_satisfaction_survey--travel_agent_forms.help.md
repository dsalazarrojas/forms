# Travel Service Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your recent experience with our travel agency's service. Please take a few minutes to answer the questions below and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the page with the question that corresponds to your most recent experience with our travel agency.
2. Read each question carefully and provide detailed answers.
3. If you have any additional comments or concerns, feel free to provide them in the "Trip Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, number): Rate your overall experience with our travel agency on a scale of 1-5.
	+ 1: Very dissatisfied
	+ 2: Somewhat dissatisfied
	+ 3: Neutral
	+ 4: Somewhat satisfied
	+ 5: Very satisfied
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text): Please enter the name of the travel agent who assisted you.
* **Travel Agent Contact** (`travel_agent_contact`, text): Please enter the contact information of the travel agent who assisted you (e.g. email, phone number).
* **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, text): Please provide any additional details about your trip (e.g. destination, dates, travel type).
* **Travel Arrangement** (`travel_arrangement`, select_one): Please select one of the following options to describe your travel arrangement.
	+ Option 1: Customized travel arrangement
	+ Option 2: Group travel arrangement
	+ Option 3: Other (please specify in the next field)
* **Travel Arrangement Other** (`travel_arrangement_other`, text): If you selected "Other" in the previous field, please describe your travel arrangement.
* **Travel Arrangement Other Description** (`travel_arrangement_other_description`, text): Please provide a detailed description of your travel arrangement.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, number): Rate your overall satisfaction with our travel agency on a scale of 1-5.
* **Recommendations** (`recommendations`, text): Please provide any recommendations or feedback you have for our travel agency.
