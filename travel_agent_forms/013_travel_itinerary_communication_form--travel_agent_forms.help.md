# Travel Itinerary Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Itinerary Communication Form is used by travel agents to gather and communicate travel-related information between the travel agency and the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the itinerary details in the "Itinerary Details" field.
2.  Select the travel dates in the "Dates" field.
3.  Choose the destinations for the trip from the "Destinations" dropdown menu.
4.  Select the activities for the trip from the "Activities" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter any comments or notes about the trip in the "Comments" field.
6.  Enter the name of the travel agent who will be coordinating the trip in the "Travel Agent" field.
7.  Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
8.  Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
9.  Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone" field.
10.  Enter the name of the travel agency in the "Travel Agency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Itinerary Details** (`itinerary_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the trip, including any relevant details such as hotel information, restaurant reservations, or special requests.
*   **Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Select the start and end dates of the trip.
*   **Destinations** (`destinations`, select_one, optional): Choose from the dropdown menu the destinations for the trip, for example, "Option 1" or "Option 2".
*   **Activities** (`activities`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple activities for the trip, for example, "Option 1", "Option 2", and "Option 3".
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the trip.
*   **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel agent who will be coordinating the trip.
*   **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the client.
*   **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter the client's email address.
*   **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of the travel agency.

**Tips**
*   Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure smooth communication and organization of the trip.
*   If you are unsure about any field or option, contact your travel agent for clarification.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
