# Travel Package Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Package Extension Form is used by customers or agents to request an extension to their travel package. This form helps to gather necessary information to process the extension request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Travel Package** field with the name of the travel package you are requesting to extend.
2. In the **Customer Request** field, briefly describe the reason for the extension request.
3. In the **Package Details** field, provide any additional information about the package being extended, such as changes to travel dates or itinerary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Package**: Enter the name of your travel package, as assigned by your travel agent. This is required information to identify the package being extended.
* **Customer Request**: Explain the reason for requesting an extension to your travel package. This helps us to understand your needs and process your request effectively.
* **Package Details**: Provide any additional details that may affect the package extension, such as changes to travel dates or itinerary. This will help us to assess the impact of the extension on your package.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth processing of your request.
* If you have any questions or concerns while filling out the form, please contact your travel agent for assistance.
* This form is intended for customers or agents who have already been assigned a travel package. If you have not been assigned a package, you will need to contact your travel agent to initiate the booking process.
