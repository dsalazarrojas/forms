# flight_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers to facilitate the process of making a flight reservation. It gathers details such as passenger information, flight details, payment information, and contact details to ensure a smooth and efficient reservation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct travel agency from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the type of flight that best suits your needs.
4. Enter any special requests or additional flight details.
5. Provide contact information for the travel agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, select_one, required): Select the travel agency that will be handling your reservation.
* **Flight Type** (`flight_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of flight that best suits your needs (e.g., Flight 1, Flight 2, Flight 3).
* **Seat Number** (`seat_number`, number, optional): Enter the seat number if required or requested.
* **Travel Agency Contact** (`travel_agency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the contact information for the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Phone** (`travel_agency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Email** (`travel_agency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Address** (`travel_agency_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Notes** (`travel_agency_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments for the travel agency.
* **Confirmation Code** (`confirmation_code`, text, optional): Enter any confirmation code if required.

Note: All other fields are optional and can be filled in as needed.
