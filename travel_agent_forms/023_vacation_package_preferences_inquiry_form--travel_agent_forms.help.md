# Vacation Package Preferences Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Package Preferences Inquiry Form is a tool used to gather information from clients about their travel preferences and requirements for their vacation package inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please select your travel date in the "Travel Date" field.
2. Choose your destination from the options provided in the "Destination" field.
3. Select the type(s) of travel you are interested in from the "Travel Type" field.
4. Provide any additional package preferences in the "Package Preferences" field.
5. Specify any travel documentation requirements in the "Travel Documentation" field.
6. Select any travel insurance options that apply to you in the "Travel Insurance" field.
7. Enter any special requests or preferences in the "Special Requests" field.
8. Provide any trip details in the "Trip Details" fields.
9. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
10. Add any trip preferences in the "Trip Preferences" field.
11. Enter any traveler information in the "Traveler Info" field.
12. Enter information about the travel agency or agency representative you are inquiring with in the "Travel Agency" field.
13. Enter any additional special requests in the "Special Requests 2" field.
14. Enter any contact person or representative information in the "Contact Person" field.
15. Enter any package preferences in the "Package Preferences 2" field.
16. Enter any trip details in the "Trip Details 2" field.
17. Enter any additional special requests in the "Special Requests 3" field.
18. Enter any travel documentation information in the "Travel Documentation 2" field.
19. Enter any contact information in the "Contact Info 2" field.
20. Enter any travel insurance information in the "Travel Insurance 2" field.
21. Enter any trip preferences in the "Trip Preferences 2" field.
22. Enter any travel agency information in the "Travel Agency 2" field.
23. Enter any contact person information in the "Contact Person 2" field.
24. Enter any special requests in "Special Requests 4" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This field is for any additional information or details that you would like to provide about your travel plans.
* **Travel Date**: Please select the date you would like to travel.
* **Destination**: Select the location you would like to visit.
* **Travel Type**: Choose the type(s) of travel you are interested in (e.g., leisure, business, adventure).
* **Package Preferences**: If you have any specific preferences for your vacation package, please enter them here.
* **Travel Documentation**: If you require any specific travel documentation, please select it from the options provided.
* **Travel Insurance**: If you need travel insurance, please select the options that apply to you.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements for your travel.
* **Trip Details**: Please enter any additional trip details or information that you think would be helpful for the travel agency.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so that the travel agency can reach out to you.
* **Trip Preferences**: If you have any specific preferences for your trip, please enter them here.
* **Traveler Info**: Enter any information about the traveler(s) in your group.
* **Travel Agency**: Please enter information about the travel agency you are inquiring with.
* **Special Requests 2**: Enter any additional special requests or requirements.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact information of the travel agent or representative you are working with.
