# Traveling Preferences Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your preferences for your upcoming trip. It will help us better understand your needs and provide you with a tailored travel experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. Select the options that best describe your travel preferences.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) next to the field label.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination**: Please enter the name of the destination you would like to visit.
* **Travel Date**: Please select a date for your trip. Note that this field is not required, but it will help us better understand your travel plans.
* **Travel Duration**: Please select the length of your trip, if applicable.
* **Travel Type**: Select one of the following options to describe the purpose of your trip:
	+ Business: For work or business-related travel.
	+ Leisure: For pleasure or relaxation.
	+ Adventure: For activities like hiking, exploring, or other adventurous activities.
* **Budget**: Please select your budget range for the trip.
* **Travel Frequency**: Please select how often you travel:
	+ Rarely: You travel very infrequently, perhaps only occasionally.
	+ Sometimes: You travel from time to time, but not very frequently.
	+ Often: You travel regularly.
* **Travel Style**: Select how you prefer to travel:
	+ Active: You enjoy active, action-packed trips.
	+ Relaxed: You prefer to take it easy and relax during your trip.
	+ Balanced: You enjoy a mix of both active and relaxed activities.
* **Travel Partner**: Please select who you will be traveling with:
	+ Solo: You will be traveling alone.
	+ With friend: You will be traveling with a friend.
	+ With Family: You will be traveling with family members.
* **Communication Style**: Select how you prefer to communicate:
	+ Directly: You prefer direct, face-to-face communication.
	+ Email: You prefer to communicate through email.
	+ Chat: You prefer to communicate through chat or messaging apps.
* **Accommodation**: Please select your preferred type of accommodation:
	+ Hotel: You prefer staying in a hotel.
	+ Resort: You prefer staying in a resort.
	+ Hostel: You prefer staying in a hostel.
* **Transport Method**: Please select your preferred mode of transportation:
	+ Flight: You prefer flying.
	+ Train: You prefer taking the train.
	+ Bus: You prefer taking the bus.
* **Travel Agency**: Please select whether you have used a travel agency before:
	+ Yes: You have used a travel agency before.
	+ No: You have not used a travel agency before.
## Tips

* Please answer each question honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* If you have any other travel preferences or requirements that are not listed, please let us know in the "Notes" section at the end of the form.
