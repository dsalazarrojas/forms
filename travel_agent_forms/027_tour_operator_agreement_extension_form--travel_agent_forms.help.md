# Tour Operator Agreement Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by travel agencies and their customers to extend agreements for tour operations. It collects information about the customer, travel agency, and agreement details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "customer-name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "customer-email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
3. Select the travel agency you are working with from the "travel-agency" dropdown list.
4. Choose a date for the agreement extension in the "date" field.
5. Select the time for the agreement extension in the "time" field.
6. Choose the type of agreement you are extending from the "agreement-type" dropdown list.
7. Provide your signature by signing in the "customer-signature" field.

### 1. **Customer Name (`customer-name`)**
 Customer name is required.

### 2. **Customer Email (`customer-email`)**
 Customer email is required and should be a valid email address.

### 3. **Travel Agency (`travel-agency`)**
 Select your travel agency from the list.

• **Travel Agency (`travel-agency`, optional)**: Choose the travel agency you are working with.

### 4. **Date (`date`)**
 Date is optional.

• **Date (`date`, optional)**: Select a date for the agreement extension.

### 5. **Time (`time`)**
 Time is optional.

• **Time (`time`, optional)**: Choose a time for the agreement extension.

### 6. **Agreement Type (`agreement-type`)**
 Select the type of agreement you are extending.
