# Client Dream Trip Inquiry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Dream Trip Inquiry form is designed to collect information about your upcoming travel plans. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your needs and preferences for your dream trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by asterisk *).
2. Enter your first and last name, email address, phone number, and preferred contact information.
3. Choose your destination type and specific destination or region (if known).
4. Select your travel dates flexibility (how flexible you are with your travel dates).
5. Choose your preferred departure and return dates (if applicable).
6. Select the number of travelers and their age ranges.
7. Choose your budget range per person.
8. Select your accommodation preference and room type.
9. Choose your transportation preference.
10. Select your activities of interest.
11. Enter any dietary restrictions or special occasions.
12. Enter any accessibility needs or travel insurance preferences.
13. Check if you've used a travel agent before.
14. Describe your dream trip in detail.
15. Add any additional notes or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your primary name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City**: Enter the city you'd like to visit.
* **State**: Enter the state you'd like to visit.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your desired destination.
* **Destination Type**: Select the type of destination you're interested in (Beach Resort, Mountain Retreat, etc.).
* **Specific Destination or Region**: If you have a specific location in mind, enter it here.
* **Are Your Travel Dates Flexible?**: Choose how flexible you are with your travel dates (Very Flexible, Somewhat Flexible, Fixed Dates).
* **Preferred Departure Date**: Enter your preferred departure date (if fixed dates are not flexible).
* **Preferred Return Date**: Enter your preferred return date (if fixed dates are not flexible).
* **Trip Duration**: Choose the number of days you'd like your trip to be.
* **Number of Travelers**: Enter the total number of people traveling with you.
* **Number of Adults**: Enter the number of adults (18+ years old).
* **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children (2-17 years old).
* **Children Ages**: If you have children, list their ages here.
* **Budget Range Per Person**: Select the budget range per person (excluding shopping money).
* **Accommodation Preference**: Choose the type of accommodation you'd like to stay in (Luxury Resort, etc.).
* **Room Preference**: Choose the type of room you'd like to have.
* **Transportation Preference**: Select the type of flight class you prefer (Economy, etc.).
* **Activities of Interest**: Select all activities that interest you (Sightseeing Tours, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any food requirements, enter them here.
* **Special Occasions**: If you're celebrating a special occasion, select it here (Honeymoon, etc.).
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special requirements, enter them here.
* **Are You Interested in Travel Insurance?**: Check if you'd like to consider travel insurance.
* **Have You Used a Travel Agent Before?**: Check if you've used a travel agent before.
* **Describe Your Dream Trip**: Paint a picture of your dream trip in this text field.
* **Additional Notes or Requests**: Add any additional information or requests you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure we can provide you the best possible service.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We'll do our best to tailor your dream trip to your preferences.
