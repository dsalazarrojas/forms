# Wholesale Booking Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for wholesale travel agencies to register with the platform, providing essential information about their agency, contact details, and travel practices. By completing this form, agencies can ensure a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Registration Form** (Page 1).
2. Provide your **Contact Details** (Page 2).
3. Enter your **Wholesale Agency Information** (Page 3).
4. Choose your **Order Frequency** and select the **Order Date Range** and **Order Time Range** (Page 4).
5. Select your **Order Type** and **Product Type** (Page 5).
6. Enter the **Travel Date** and **Travel Time** (Page 6).
7. Add any **Comments** you'd like to share with our team (Page 7).
8. Review and agree to our **Terms and Conditions** and sign the **Agreement** (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): This section is where you'll enter your agency's name.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Please provide your agency's contact person's name, email, and phone number.
### Page 2: Contact Details
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your agency's official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your agency's contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your agency's physical address.
### Page 3: Wholesale Agency Information
* **Wholesale Agency Information** (`wholesale_agency_info`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your agency's services and offerings.
* **Wholesale Agency Information** (`wholesale_agency_info`, text, required): Enter any other relevant information about your agency.
### Page 4: Sales and Travel Information
* **Order Frequency** (`order_frequency`, number, required): Select how often you plan to place orders with us.
* **Order Date Range** (`order_date_range`, date, required): Enter the range of dates you'd like to make reservations for.
* **Order Time Range** (`order_time_range`, time, required): Enter the time range you'd like to make reservations for.
### Page 5: Order and Product Information
* **Order Type** (`order_type`, select_one, required): Select whether you're looking to book for **Wholesale** or **Retail** purposes.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of product you're interested in booking (**Hotel**, **Flight**, or **Car Rental**).
### Page 6: Travel Information
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the time of your travel.
### Page 7: Additional Information
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): If you have any additional information or requests, please share them here.
### Page 8: Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_multiple, required): Review and agree to our **Terms and Conditions**.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required): Sign this agreement to confirm your understanding of our policies.
### Page 9: Payment Information
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (**Credit**, **Cash**, or **PayPal**).
* **Bank Account** (`bank_account`, text, required): Enter your agency's bank account number (for payment purposes).
### Page 10: Additional Notes
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, required): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with our team.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, required): You may be asked to upload documents as part of the registration process.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Please sign this document to confirm your understanding of our terms and conditions.

### Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* Make sure to answer all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
