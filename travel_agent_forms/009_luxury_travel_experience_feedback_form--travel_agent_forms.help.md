# luxury_travel_experience_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The luxury travel experience feedback form is designed to collect detailed feedback from users about their luxury travel experiences. This form helps travel agents to understand user preferences, identify areas of improvement, and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the destination of your travel experience from the dropdown menu.
2. Choose the type of trip you took (e.g., leisure, business, adventure).
3. Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided by the travel agent.
4. Provide any additional comments about your experience.
5. Enter your email address (optional).
6. Enter your phone number (optional).
7. Select the level of service you received (e.g., excellent, average, poor).
8. Choose the mode of transportation you used for your trip (e.g., plane, car, train).
9. Select how frequently you travel for leisure (e.g., frequently, occasionally, never).
10. Choose your overall travel satisfaction (e.g., very satisfied, satisfied, dissatisfied).
11. Choose the travel agency you used for your trip (e.g., Agent 1, Agent 2, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination** (1, Select One): This field asks for the destination of your travel experience.
* **Trip Type** (2, Select Multiple): Choose the type of trip you took (e.g., leisure, business, adventure).
* **Rating** (3, Number): Rate your overall satisfaction with the service provided by the travel agent (on a scale of 1-5).
* **Comments** (4, Note): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Email** (5, Email): Enter your email address (optional).
* **Phone** (6, Text): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Service Level** (7, Select One): Select the level of service you received (e.g., excellent, average, poor).
* **Overall Satisfaction** (8, Select One): Choose your overall travel satisfaction (e.g., very satisfied, satisfied, dissatisfied).
* **Travel Agent** (9, Select Multiple): Choose the travel agent you used for your trip (e.g., Agent 1, Agent 2, etc.).
* **Additional Comments** (10, Note): Provide any additional comments about your experience.
* **Travel Dates** (11, Date): Enter the dates of your travel experience.
* **Travel Times** (12, Time): Enter the times of your travel experience.
* **Travel Duration** (13, Number): Enter the duration of your travel experience in days.
* **Travel Budget** (14, Number): Enter your travel budget.
* **Travel Type** (15, Select One): Choose the type of trip you took (e.g., leisure, business, adventure).
* **Travel Destination** (16, Select Multiple): Choose the destination of your travel experience.
* **Travel Mode** (17, Select Multiple): Choose the mode of transportation you used for your trip (e.g., plane, car, train).
* **Travel Frequency** (18, Select One): Choose how frequently you travel for leisure (e.g., frequently, occasionally, never).
* **Travel Satisfaction** (19, Select One): Choose your overall travel satisfaction (e.g., very satisfied, satisfied, dissatisfied).

Note: Some fields are not required, so you can skip them if you don't have the information or prefer not to provide it.
