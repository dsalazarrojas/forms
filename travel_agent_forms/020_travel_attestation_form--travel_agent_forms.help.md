# Travel Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Attestation Form is used to record and verify travel information for customers of a travel agency. This form collects details such as the traveler's name, travel dates, destination, reason for travel, and contact information for the travel agent, among other things.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have provided correct information.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to the travel agent or the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler's Name** (`traveler_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter the full name of the traveler.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of travel.
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city or state the traveler is visiting.
* **Travel Documents** (`travel_documents`, `text`, required: false): List any travel documents attached, such as visa, passport, etc.
* **Return Date** (`travel_return_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of return.
* **Reason for Travel** (`travel_reason`, `text`, required: false): Please describe the reason for the traveler's trip.
* **Travel Agency Details** (`travel_agent_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant details about the travel agency or trip organizer.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the travel agent who is handling the trip.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact number of the travel agent for any further inquiries.

Tips:
* Ensure that you have all necessary information and attachments before filling out the form.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to reach out to the travel agent or relevant authorities for guidance.
