# Japan Visa Waiver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a Japan Visa Waiver on behalf of your clients. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth processing of the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Traveler Details** page with your client's information.
2. On the **Travel Team Details** page, enter a brief description of your travel group.
3. In the **Declaration** field, please provide a clear and concise statement of your travel plans.
4. In the **Traveler Passport** field, select the country of your client's passport.
5. On the **Travel Dates** page, enter the dates of your trip.
6. On the **Travel Duration** page, enter the number of days you plan to stay in Japan.
7. On the **Travel Purpose** page, select all the reasons for your trip.
8. On the **Travel Group Size** page, enter the number of people in your group.
9. Fill out the **Contact Information** page with your contact details.
10. On the **Email** page, enter your email address.
11. On the **Travel Agency** page, enter your travel agency's name.
12. On the **Agency Phone** page, enter your agency's phone number.
13. On the **Agency Address** page, enter your agency's address.
14. On the **Agency Email** page, enter your agency's email address.
15. Finally, in the **Notes** field, provide any additional information you'd like to include with your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveler Details** (Text, Required): This page is where you'll enter your client's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Travel Team Details** (Text, Optional): This page is where you can provide a brief description of your travel group.
* **Declaration** (Note, Optional): Please provide a clear and concise statement of your travel plans.
* **Traveler Passport** (Select One, Required): Select the country of your client's passport. Options include Japan, USA, Canada, UK, Australia, China, and Other.
* **Travel Dates** (Date, Optional): Enter the dates of your trip.
* **Travel Duration** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of days you plan to stay in Japan.
* **Travel Purpose** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all the reasons for your trip. Options include Business, Leisure, and Tour.
* **Travel Group Size** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Contact Information** (Text, Optional): This page is where you'll enter your contact details.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Travel Agency** (Text, Optional): Enter your travel agency's name.
* **Agency Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your agency's phone number.
* **Agency Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your agency's address.
* **Agency Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your agency's email address.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to include with your application.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" must be filled out, while fields marked as "Optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
