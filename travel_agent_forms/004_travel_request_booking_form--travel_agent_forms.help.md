# Travel Request Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for travel agents to collect essential information from clients for travel booking requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the travel request information, including travel dates, destination, and preferred mode of transportation.
2.  Provide information about the client's preferences, such as their preferred mode of transportation and travel dates.
3.  Enter the client's contact information, including email and phone number.
4.  Fill in the travel agency's contact information, including email and phone number.
5.  Add any additional travel notes, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Title** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the title of the travel request.
*   **Client Preferences** (`client_preferences`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the client's preferred mode of transportation, such as Air, Train, or Bus.
*   **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `date`, required: false): Enter the travel dates in the format `MM/DD/YY`.
*   **Travel Date Range** (`travel_date_range`, `number`, required: false): Enter the travel date range in numerical format (e.g., 1-5 days).
*   **Trip Length** (`trip_length`, `number`, required: false): Enter the length of the trip in numerical format (e.g., 1-10 days).
*   **Travel Budget** (`travel_budget`, `number`, required: false): Enter the client's travel budget.
*   **Client Preferences 2** (`client_preferences_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select additional modes of transportation preferred by the client.
*   **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the travel agency.
*   **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the destination city, such as Paris, London, or New York.
*   **Travel Notes** (`travel_notes`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional travel notes, if necessary.
*   **Client Email** (`travel_requester_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the client's email address.
*   **Client Phone** (`travel_requester_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's phone number.
*   **Travel Agency Email** (`travel_agency_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the travel agency's email address.
*   **Travel Agency Phone** (`travel_agency_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the travel agency's phone number.
*   **Travel Agency Name** (`travel_agency_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the travel agency.

**Tips**

*   Always review the form information carefully before submitting the travel request.
*   Ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
*   Use the form to collect relevant and necessary information for travel booking requests.
