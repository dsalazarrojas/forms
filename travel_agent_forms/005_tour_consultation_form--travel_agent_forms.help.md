# tour Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information about the customer's preferences and needs for a tour. It helps the travel agency to provide accurate and personalized tour recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the required fields:
	* **travel_agency_name**: Enter the name of the agency handling your tour.
	* **agency_email**: Enter the email address of your agency's contact person.
	* **first_name**: Enter your first name as the customer.
	* **last_name**: Enter your last name as the customer.
	* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
	* **start_date**: Select the starting date of your tour.
	* **end_date**: Select the ending date of your tour.
	* **duration**: Enter the duration of your tour in days.
	* **destination**: Enter the destination of your tour.
	* **tour_type**: Enter the type of tour you are interested in (e.g., adventure, cultural, etc.).
2. If applicable, provide any additional information in the optional fields:
	* **request1**: Enter any specific requests or preferences you have for your tour.
	* **request2**: Enter any additional requests or preferences you have for your tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **travel_agency_name**: Enter the name of the agency handling your tour, which will be in charge of providing the tour experience.
* **agency_email**: Enter the email address of your agency's contact person for any future communication.
* **first_name**: Enter your first name as the customer for the agency's records.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name as the customer for the agency's records.
* **phone_number**: Enter your phone number for the agency to contact you for any updates or follow-up questions.
* **start_date**: Select the starting date of your tour to help the agency plan the itinerary.
* **end_date**: Select the ending date of your tour to determine the duration of the trip.
* **duration**: Enter the duration of your tour in days to ensure the agency knows how many days to plan for.
* **destination**: Enter the destination of your tour to help the agency plan the trip details.
* **tour_type**: Enter the type of tour you are interested in (e.g., adventure, cultural, etc.) to help the agency tailor the experience.
* **request1**: Enter any specific requests or preferences you have for your tour.
* **request2**: Enter any additional requests or preferences you have for your tour.

## Tips
* Double-check the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in to avoid any issues with tour planning.
* You can always contact the agency's contact person at the email address provided if you have any questions or requests.
