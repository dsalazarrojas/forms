# Helicopter Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking a helicopter tour with our company. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that we have all the necessary information to process your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Type your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select a date and time for your reservation in the "Reservation Date" and "Reservation Time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX (e.g., 123) 456-7890).
* **Reservation Date** (date, optional): Select a date for your helicopter tour. If left blank, we will contact you to schedule the tour.
* **Reservation Time** (time, optional): Select a time for your helicopter tour. If left blank, we will contact you to schedule the tour.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or needs for your tour, please contact us separately.
* Our team will be in touch with you to confirm your booking and provide further information.
