# Wildlife Photographer Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help wildlife photographers plan and book their accommodations for photo shoots. It ensures that their specific needs are met during their stay, allowing for a successful and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the start and end dates of your visit.
2. Enter the number of guests accompanying you.
3. If you have any special requests (e.g., dietary restrictions, early check-in/check-out), please provide them in the text field.
4. Choose the type of accommodation you prefer (House, Apartment, or Hotel).
5. Enter the location of your accommodation.
6. Select the equipment you will be bringing (Camera, Lens, Tripod, Flash, Microphone, Drone, or Computer).
7. If you have any other special equipment not listed, please provide details in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **start_date** (Date, Required): Select the start date of your visit.
* **end_date** (Date, Required): Select the end date of your visit.
* **num_guests** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of guests accompanying you.
* **special_requests** (Text, Optional): Provide any special requests you may have (e.g., dietary restrictions, early check-in/check-out).
* **accommodation_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of accommodation you prefer (House, Apartment, or Hotel).
* **accommodation_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of your accommodation.
* **equipment_type** (Select One, Optional): Select the equipment you will be bringing (Camera, Lens, Tripod, Flash, Microphone, Drone, or Computer).
* **special_equipment** (Text, Optional): If you have any other special equipment not listed, provide details.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the operations team for clarification.
* Please make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests, please be specific to ensure they can be fulfilled.
