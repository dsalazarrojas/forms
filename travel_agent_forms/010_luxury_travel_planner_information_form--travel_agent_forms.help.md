# Luxury Travel Planner Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Luxury Travel Planner Information Form is a comprehensive tool designed to gather essential information from travel agents and their clients for planning and executing luxury travel experiences. This form helps travel agents provide accurate and personalized services to their clients, ensuring a seamless and enjoyable journey.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  **Please note that this form is for travel agents and their clients.** If you are not a travel agent or a client, please do not fill out this form.
2.  As a travel agent, select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are the travel agent handling the client's travel arrangements.
3.  Enter the agency name if you are a travel agent.
4.  Enter the client's name.
5.  Enter the travel dates in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
6.  Enter the departure date in the format `yyyy-mm-dd`.
7.  Enter the destination of the travel.
8.  Enter your budget for the travel.
9.  Enter your email address.
10. Enter your phone number.
11. Enter any special requests or instructions for the travel agent.
12. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are the travel agent's contact person.
13. Enter any comments or notes for the travel agent.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are a travel agent.
15. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have a travel agent ID.
16. Enter any special instructions for the travel agent.
17. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are the travel agent's contact person.
18. Enter any comments for the travel agent.
19. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are the client's contact person.
