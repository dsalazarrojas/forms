# Business Travel Itinerary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Travel Itinerary Form is a mandatory tool for employees to submit their travel plans for approval and management. It helps to ensure that all necessary details are captured and reviewed before the trip, making it easier for employees to plan and execute their business travels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your employee name, ID, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2.  Select the primary purpose of your trip from the options provided.
3.  Enter the destination city and country you will be visiting.
4.  Provide your departure and return dates.
5.  Specify the city you are leaving from.
6.  Select the primary mode of transportation you will be using for your trip.
7.  Indicate if you need hotel accommodations, and provide details if applicable.
8.  Enter your estimated travel budget.
9.  Specify the number of travelers accompanying you.
10. Enter any special requirements or notes you may have for the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employee Name** (Employee Name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your employee ID or business card.
*   **Employee ID** (Employee ID, text, optional): Your company-issued employee number, if applicable.
*   **Employee Email** (Employee Email, email, required): Enter your business email address for communication.
*   **Employee Phone** (Employee Phone, text, required): Your phone number for contact during travel.
*   **Department** (Department, text, required): Specify your department or division within the company.
*   **Manager Name** (Manager Name, text, required): Enter your direct supervisor's name for approval.
*   **Manager Email** (Manager Email, email, required): Enter your supervisor's email address for approval and communication.
*   **Trip Purpose** (Trip Purpose, select_one, required): Choose the primary purpose of your trip from the options provided.
*   **Destination City and Country** (Destination City and Country, text, required): Enter the city and country you will be visiting.
*   **Departure Date** (Departure Date, date, required): Enter the date you will be departing.
*   **Return Date** (Return Date, date, required): Enter the date you will be returning.
*   **Departure City** (Departure City, text, required): Specify the city you are leaving from.
*   **Primary Transportation Type** (Primary Transportation Type, select_one, required): Choose the primary mode of transportation you will be using.
*   **Accommodation Needed** (Accommodation Needed, select_one, required): Indicate if you need hotel accommodations; if applicable, provide details.
*   **Hotel Details if Applicable** (Hotel Details if Applicable, text, optional): If you need accommodations, provide the name and confirmation number of your hotel.
*   **Estimated Travel Budget** (Estimated Travel Budget, number, required): Enter the total expected costs for your trip.
*   **Number of Travelers** (Number of Travelers, number, required): Specify the number of people accompanying you on your trip.
*   **Special Requirements or Notes** (Special Requirements or Notes, text, optional): Enter any special needs, considerations, or notes related to your trip.
