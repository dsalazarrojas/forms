# Travel Agency Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from customers who have traveled with the agency. Please fill out this form to share your experience and help us improve our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that best describe your experience and provide any additional comments you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **How was your stay?** (`travel_agency_feedback`, select_one, optional):
	+ Please rate your stay as Good or Bad to give us an idea of your overall experience.
* **What type of travel do you usually take?** (`travel_agency_feedback_question_1`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select the type of travel that best describes your typical experiences, such as Beach, City, or Outdoors.
* **How would you rate your experience?** (`travel_agency_feedback_question_2`, number, optional):
	+ Please rate your experience as a number between 1 and 10, where 1 is poor and 10 is excellent.
* **Please describe your experience.** (`feedback`, note, optional):
	+ Share your thoughts and feelings about your experience with us. This will help us understand what we did well and what we can improve on.
* **Would you recommend our agency to a friend?** (`travel_agency_feedback_question_3`, select_one, optional):
	+ If you would recommend our agency to a friend, please select 'True'. If not, please select 'False'.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ If you'd like to be contacted for further discussion or updates, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ If you'd like to discuss your experience over the phone, please enter your phone number.

Note: The form is designed to be simple and easy to use. If you have any trouble completing the form, please contact us directly for assistance.
