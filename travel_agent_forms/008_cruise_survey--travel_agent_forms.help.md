# Cruise Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cruise Survey form is designed to gather feedback from travelers about their experiences on a cruise. This form will help us understand what you enjoyed most about your cruise, your preferences for shore excursions, and your overall satisfaction with your cabin, food, entertainment, and staff service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

*   Answer each question to the best of your ability.
*   If a question has a selection field (e.g., select\_one, select\_multiple), choose the option that best describes your experience.
*   If a question is not relevant, you can leave it blank or type N/A (Not Applicable).
*   Please keep your answers concise and clear, and provide as much detail as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **What did you enjoy most about your cruise?** (`cruise_survey_page`, text, required: false)
    Please share your favorite experience or memory from your cruise.
*   **Favorite port of call?** (`favorite_port`, select\_multiple, required: false)
    Select all the ports that you visited during your cruise.
*   **Favorite port of call** (`favorite_port_of_call`, select\_one, required: false)
    Choose the port that you enjoyed the most.
*   **Favorite shore excursion** (`favorite_shore_excursion`, text, required: false)
    Describe your favorite shore excursion.
*   **Overall satisfaction with your cruise** (`overall_satisfaction`, note, required: false)
    Please rate your overall satisfaction with your cruise (e.g., very satisfied, satisfied, neutral, dissatisfied, very dissatisfied).
*   **Date of cruise** (`date_of_cruise`, date, required: false)
    Enter the date of your cruise.
*   **Time of day** (`time_of_day`, time, required: false)
    Choose the time of day when you were on your cruise.
*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, email, required: false)
    Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false)
    Share any additional comments or suggestions you have about your cruise.
*   **Cruise Ship** (`cruise_ship`, select\_one, required: false)
    Choose the name of your cruise ship.
*   **Cabin Details** (`cabin_details`, text, required: false)
    Describe your cabin, including its amenities and location.
*   **Cabin size** (`cabin_size`, select\_one, required: false)
    Choose the size of your cabin (e.g., small, medium, large).
*   **Cabin location** (`cabin_location`, select\_one, required: false)
    Choose the location of your cabin (e.g., on the deck, midship, aft).
*   **Cabin type** (`cabin_type`, select\_one, required: false)
    Choose the type of your cabin (e.g., oceanview, interior, balcony).
*   **Cabin Rating** (`cabin_rating`, text, required: false)
    Rate the quality of your cabin.
*   **Cabin cleanliness** (`cabin_cleanliness`, select\_one, required: false)
    Rate the cleanliness of your cabin.
*   **Food and Bverage** (`food_and_beverage`, text, required: false)
    Describe the quality of the food and beverages on the cruise.
*   **Entertainment on board** (`entertainment_on_board`, text, required: false)
    Describe the entertainment options on the cruise.
*   **Staff Service** (`staff_service`, text, required: false)
    Rate the quality of the staff service on the cruise.
*   **Port tour** (`port_tour`, text, required: false)
    Describe your experience with the port tour.
*   **Shore excur** (`shore_excur`, text, required: false)
    Describe your shore excursion experience.
*   **Shore excur price** (`shore_excur_price`, text, required: false)
    Rate the price of your shore excursion.
*   **Shore excur rating** (`shore_excur_rating`, text, required: false)
    Rate the quality of your shore excursion.
*   **Shore excur comment** (`shore_excur_comment`, text, required: false)
    Share any comments about your shore excursion experience.
*   **Overall satisfaction rating** (`overall_satisfaction_rating`, number, required: false)
    Rate your overall satisfaction with your cruise (e.g., 1-5 stars).
