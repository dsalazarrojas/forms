# travel_plan_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_plan_form is a tool for travel agents to provide their information so that customers can contact them with travel-related inquiries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill out the form with the requested information about yourself, the travel agent.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Agent Name** (`travel_agent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or letterhead.
* **Travel Agent Email** (`travel_agent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address that customers can use to contact you.
* **Travel Agent Phone** (`travel_agent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your business phone number that customers can use to contact you.
* **Travel Agent Location** (`travel_agent_location`, text, required: false): Enter your business location or city.
* **Travel Arrangement Type** (`travel_arrangement_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of travel arrangement you are planning (e.g. Travel Plan, Hotel Booking, etc.). 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure customers can contact you successfully.
* Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
