# tour-code-of-conduct-agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain consent and gather contact information from both the tour participant and the tour operator. This form ensures that both parties are clear on their roles, responsibilities, and emergency contacts for any potential issues that may arise during the tour. This form will help in creating a safe and well-documented record of tour arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Consent section, confirming your agreement to the tour terms and conditions.
2. Provide your emergency contact details in the Emergency Contact section.
3. Sign the Participant Signature section with your name to indicate your agreement to the terms.
4. Fill out the Participant Name field with your full name.
5. Enter your email address in the Participant Email field.
6. If you'd like to provide your phone number, fill out the Participant Phone field. (Note: This is optional.)
7. Fill out the Tour Operator Name field with the name of your tour operator.
8. Enter the tour operator's email address in the Tour Operator Email field.
9. If you'd like to provide the tour operator's phone number, fill out the Participant Phone field. (Note: This is optional.)
10. Sign the Tour Operator Signature section with their name.
