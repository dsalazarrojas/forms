# Travel Protection Plans Lead Generation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Protection Plans Lead Generation Form is a tool used to collect information from potential customers interested in learning more about travel protection plans. This form helps us understand your preferences, needs, and sources of information for travel protection plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Lead Generation Form, providing a brief description of your travel protection plan inquiries.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. Select how you found out about our travel protection plans (Lead Source).
4. Indicate if this lead is for lead generation purposes or not.
5. Choose the travel agencies you are interested in or currently working with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Lead Generation Form** (`lead_generation_form`, text, optional): A brief description of why you're inquiring about our travel protection plans. Please provide a clear and concise message.
* **Traveler Details** (`traveler_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the traveler(s) you're working with (e.g., age, destination, travel dates).
* **Travel Protection Plans** (`travel_protection_plans`, text, optional): Describe the travel protection plans you're interested in learning more about (e.g., trip cancellation, medical, etc.).
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Provide a brief message describing the travel protection plans you're inquiring about.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Lead Source** (`lead_source`, select_one, optional): Select how you found out about our travel protection plans:
	+ Online Search
	+ Social Media
	+ Referral
	+ Brochure
	+ Trade Show
	+ Other
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the travel agencies you are interested in or currently working with:
	+ Agency A
	+ Agency B
	+ Agency C
* **Lead Generation** (`lead_generation`, select_one, optional): Indicate if this lead is for lead generation purposes:
	+ Yes
	+ No

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Only enter the information requested for the travel protection plans you are genuinely interested in.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
