# Cruise Parental Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The cruise parental consent form is a critical document that travel agents must complete to gather necessary information and obtain consent from parents or guardians for minor cruises.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the travel agent who is submitting the form.
2. Enter the name of the parent or guardian who is providing consent.
3. Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
4. Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
5. Enter the minor's name.
6. Enter the minor's date of birth.
7. Select the minor's gender.
8. Enter the name of the cruise.
9. Enter the departure date of the cruise.
10. Enter the return date of the cruise.
11. Enter the duration of the cruise.
12. Enter the location of the cruise.
13. (Optional) Add any comments or notes from the travel agent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agent** (`Travel Agent`, text, required): Enter your name as the travel agent submitting the form.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian providing consent.
* **Parent E-mail** (`Parent E-mail`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (`Parent Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Minor Name** (`Minor Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the minor traveling.
* **Minor Date of Birth** (`Minor Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the minor.
* **Minor Gender** (`Minor Gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of the minor (Male, Female, Other).
* **Cruise Name** (`Cruise Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the cruise.
* **Departure Date** (`Departure Date`, date, required): Enter the departure date of the cruise.
* **Return Date** (`Return Date`, date, required): Enter the return date of the cruise.
* **Cruise Duration** (`Cruise Duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the cruise.
* **Cruise Location** (`Cruise Location`, text, required): Enter the location of the cruise.
* **Travel Agent Comment** (`Travel Agent Comment`, note, optional): Enter any comments or notes from the travel agent.
