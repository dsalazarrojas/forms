# Honeymoon Planning Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from couples planning their honeymoon to provide a customized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Couple Details** field with your names and contact information.
2.  If applicable, mention specific destinations or types of destinations you are interested in in the **Destination Preferences** field.
3.  Describe your travel plans, including dates, duration, and activities in the **Travel Details** field.
4.  Specify your honeymoon budget in the **Honeymoon Budget** field.
5.  Mention any specific travel preferences you have in the **Travel Preferences** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Couple Details** (Required): This field is used to gather your names and contact information. Please enter your names and email addresses separated by commas.
*   **Destination Preferences** (Optional): This field is where you can mention specific destinations or types of destinations you are interested in. For example, beach, city, or countryside.
*   **Travel Details** (Required): In this field, describe your travel plans, including dates, duration, and activities you'd like to do during your honeymoon.
*   **Honeymoon Budget** (Required): Specify your honeymoon budget to help us provide you with more accurate recommendations.
*   **Travel Preferences** (Required): Mention any specific travel preferences you have, such as preferred modes of transportation or accommodation.
