# Travel Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Questionnaire Form is a tool used by travel agents to gather information from travelers to create customized travel itineraries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your travel destination in the "destination" field.
2. Select your departure date using the date picker in the "departure_date" field.
3. Select your arrival date using the date picker in the "arrival_date" field.
4. If you are a travel agent, enter your name in the "travel_agent" field.
5. If you are a travel agent, enter your travel agency's name in the "travel_agency" field.
6. If necessary, enter a note for the travel agent in the "travel_agent_note" field.
7. Enter your name as the traveler in the "traveler_name" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
9. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
10. If necessary, enter any travel requirements you have, such as "Sustainable tourism" or "Adventure tourism", in the "travel_requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Questionnaire Form**: The title of the form.
* **Destination**: Enter the city or location you are traveling to.
* **Departure Date**: Select the date you will be departing from your current location.
* **Arrival Date**: Select the date you will be arriving at your destination.
* **Travel Agent**: If you are a travel agent, enter your name.
* **Travel Agency**: If you are a travel agent, enter your travel agency's name.
* **Travel Agent Note**: If you are a travel agent, enter a note about the travel.
* **Traveler Name**: Enter your name as the traveler.
* **E-Mail**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Travel Agency Note**: Enter any additional notes about the travel agency.
* **Travel Requirements**: Select any travel requirements you have, such as "Sustainable tourism" or "Adventure tourism".
