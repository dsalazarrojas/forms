# travel_and_hotel_partnership_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel and hotel partnership inquiry form is used to gather information from travel and hotel partners who are interested in partnering with our company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form with your travel and hotel partnership details.
2. Provide your contact person's email address in the format `example@example.com`.
3. Enter your contact person's phone number in the format `123-456-7890`.
4. Provide your travel and hotel's address.
5. Enter your travel and hotel's city.
6. Click "Submit" to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_and_hotel_partnership_inquiry_form** (page 1): Enter a brief description of your travel and hotel partnership opportunity.
* **partnership_request_details** (page 2): Provide a detailed description of your partnership request.
* **travel_and_hotel_name** (page 3): Enter your travel and hotel's name.
* **travel_and_hotel_website** (page 4): Enter your travel and hotel's website URL.
* **contact_person_email** (page 5): Enter the email address of the contact person.
  Example: `example@example.com`.
* **contact_person_phone** (page 6): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
  Example: `123-456-7890`.
* **contact_person_mobile** (page 7): Enter the mobile phone number of the contact person.
* **travel_and_hotel_address** (page 8): Enter the address of your travel and hotel.
* **travel_and_hotel_city** (page 9): Enter the city of your travel and hotel.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that we can process your inquiry correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [insert contact email or phone number].
- If you are already in our partnership list, please do not fill out this form again.
