# Traveler Preferences Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Traveler Preferences Form is designed to collect information from travelers to help plan their trips. This form is meant to be filled out by travelers to provide details about their preferences and requirements for their upcoming journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First Page** section with any relevant information about your trip.
2. Select the **Destination** you will be traveling to.
3. Enter the **Date of Departure** for your trip.
4. Choose your preferred **Travel Class** (Economy, Business, or First Class).
5. Select the **Flight Duration** that suits your needs (Less than 3 hours, 3-6 hours, or More than 6 hours).
6. Choose your preferred **Accommodation Type** (Hotel, Resort, or Hostel).
7. Enter your **Travel Agent** contact information.
8. Provide any additional **Notes** about your trip.
9. Enter your **Email** address.
10. Enter your **Phone** number.
11. Select the **Date of Arrival** for your trip.
12. Choose the **Travel Documents** you need to provide (Passport, Visa, or Travel Insurance).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page**: Enter any relevant information about your trip.
* **Destination**: Select the city, country, or region you will be traveling to.
* **Date of Departure**: Enter the date you will be leaving for your trip.
* **Travel Class**: Choose the class of service you prefer (Economy, Business, or First Class).
* **Flight Duration**: Select the length of your flight (Less than 3 hours, 3-6 hours, or More than 6 hours).
* **Accommodation Type**: Choose the type of accommodation you prefer (Hotel, Resort, or Hostel).
* **Travel Agent**: Enter the contact information of your travel agent.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your trip.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Arrival**: Enter the date you will arrive at your destination.
* **Travel Documents**: Select the documents you need to provide (Passport, Visa, or Travel Insurance).
