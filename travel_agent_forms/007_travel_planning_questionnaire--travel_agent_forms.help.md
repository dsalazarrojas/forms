# Travel Planning Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from travelers to plan their trips efficiently. It will help us in providing personalized travel recommendations and services tailored to their specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct options for each field as per your requirements.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date (id: 1)**: The travel date is the date you plan to travel. Please enter the date in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Travel Length (id: 2)**: Choose the duration of your trip from the options provided:
	+ One day
	+ Two days
	+ Three days
* **Prefered Travel Agent (id: 3)**: Select the travel agent you prefer to handle your travel arrangements:
	+ One day
	+ Two days
	+ Three days
* **Select Your Travel Agents (id: 4)**: You can select multiple travel agents to contact for your travel plans:
	+ Agent A
	+ Agent B
	+ Agent C
* **Travel Budget (id: 5)**: Enter your travel budget to help us provide accurate estimates and recommendations.
* **Travel Notes/Comments (id: 6)**: Share any relevant travel notes or comments to help us better understand your needs.
* **Email Address (id: 7)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you for further communication.
* **Phone Number (id: 8)**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you for additional information.
* **Staying Duration (id: 9)**: Select the number of days you plan to stay:
	+ One day
	+ Two days
	+ Three days
* **Additional Requests/Questions (id: 10)**: If you have any other requests or questions, please enter them here.

Note: Fields marked as required are essential for the smooth processing of your travel arrangements.
