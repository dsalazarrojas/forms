# Travel Agency License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to apply for a travel agency license. It will help us evaluate your agency's qualifications and determine the type of license required.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide the following information about your agency:
	* **Agency Name**: The name of your travel agency
	* **Agent Name**: The name of the person filling out the form
	* **Contact Number**: A contact number for your agency
	* **Agency Address**: The address of your agency
	* **Business Hours**: The hours of operation for your agency
2. Choose the type of license your agency requires:
	* **License Type**: Select the type of license your agency needs (License A, License B, or License C)
3. Specify the ownership structure of your agency:
	* **Agency Ownership**: Choose the type of ownership (Individual, Partnership, or Corporation)
4. Provide additional details about your agency:
	* **Agency Experience**: The number of years your agency has been in operation
	* **License Number**: Any existing license number for your agency
	* **Agent Email**: The email address of the agent filling out the form
	* **Agency Employees**: The number of employees working for your agency
	* **Agency Services**: Select the services offered by your agency (Travel Agents, Travel Agencies, or Tour Operators)
5. List any additional agency services:
	* **Agency Services List**: A list of any additional services your agency offers
6. Specify any agency certificates:
	* **Agency Certificate**: Select the type of certificate your agency holds (Certificate A, Certificate B, or Certificate C)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your travel agency.
* **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter a contact number for your agency.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your agency.
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, time, optional): Enter the hours of operation for your agency.
* **License Type** (`license_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of license your agency needs (License A, License B, or License C).
* **Agency Ownership** (`agency_ownership`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of ownership (Individual, Partnership, or Corporation).
* **Agency Experience** (`agency_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years your agency has been in operation.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, text, optional): Enter any existing license number for your agency.
* **Agent Email** (`agent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the agent filling out the form.
* **Agency Employees** (`agency_employees`, number, optional): Enter the number of employees working for your agency.
* **Agency Services** (`agency_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the services offered by your agency (Travel Agents, Travel Agencies, or Tour Operators).
* **Agency Services List** (`agency_services_list`, text, optional): Enter any additional services your agency offers.
* **Agency Certificate** (`agency_certificate`, select_one, optional): Select the type of certificate your agency holds (Certificate A, Certificate B, or Certificate C).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, refer to your agency's documentation or contact us for clarification.
* This form is intended to guide you through the necessary steps to apply for a travel agency license. If you have any questions or need assistance, don't hesitate to reach out.
