# post_trip_report_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information about a trip, including trip details, customer feedback, and agent notes. It's a crucial tool for travel agents to record and review trip experiences, allowing them to improve their services and customer satisfaction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter the trip details, including the start and end dates.
* Provide feedback and comments as a customer, including their name, contact information, and rating of the trip.
* Rate your level of satisfaction with the trip and provide comments.
* Enter the trip status, which can be Completed, In Progress, or Not Started.
* Add any additional notes or comments as the travel agent, customer, or both.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **trip_details**: Enter the basic information about the trip, including the start and end dates.
* **tour_supervisor_comments**: Enter any comments or feedback about the tour supervisor.
* **customer_name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **customer_contact**: Enter the customer's contact information.
* **travel_agent**: Enter the name of the travel agent handling the trip.
* **trip_start_date**: Select the start date of the trip.
* **trip_end_date**: Select the end date of the trip.
* **trip_type**: Select the type of trip (e.g. Day Tour, Overnight Tour, Multi Day Tour, Cruise, or Other).
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about the trip.
* **travel_agent_notes**: Enter any notes or comments from the travel agent.
* **customer_comments**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
* **customer_satisfaction**: Rate your level of satisfaction with the trip (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
* **customer_comments_2**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
* **travel_agent_comments_2**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the travel agent.
* **travel_agent_satisfaction**: Rate the travel agent's level of satisfaction with the trip (Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, or Very Dissatisfied).
* **customer_rating**: Enter a numerical rating of the trip (e.g. 1-5).
* **travel_agent_rating**: Enter a numerical rating of the travel agent (e.g. 1-5).
* **trip_status**: Select the current status of the trip (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **trip_status_2**: Select the current status of the trip (Completed, In Progress, or Not Started).
* **customer_comments_2**: Enter any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
* **travel_agent_comments_2**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the travel agent.
* **notes_3**: Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **travel_agent_comments_2**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the travel agent.
* **notes_4**: Enter any additional notes or comments.

Note to self: This form is designed to be a template for travel agents to report and review trip experiences. It helps them gather valuable insights and feedback from customers, which can be used to improve their services and customer satisfaction.
