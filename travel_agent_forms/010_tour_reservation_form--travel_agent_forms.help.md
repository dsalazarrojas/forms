# Tour Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers who want to book a tour reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out your personal information, including any relevant details about yourself that you feel comfortable sharing.
2. Enter your contact information so we can get back to you about the tour reservation.
3. Select the tour packages you are interested in.
4. If you have any feedback or comments about the tour, feel free to share them in the "Feedback" field.
5. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send us your tour reservation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information**: <small>(personal_info)</small> (**text**, personal information, optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other personal details you feel comfortable sharing.
* **Contact Information**: <small>(contact_info)</small> (**text**, contact information, optional): Enter your phone number and any other relevant contact details.
* **Select Tour Packages**: <small>(tour_packages)</small> (**select_multiple**, tour packages, optional): Choose the tour packages you are interested in.
* **Feedback**: <small>(feedback)</small> (**text**, feedback, optional): Share any feedback or comments about the tour.
* **Comments**: <small>(comments)</small> (**text**, comments, optional): Share any additional comments about the tour.
* **Submit**: <small>(submit)</small> (**text**, submit, optional): Click this button to send us your tour reservation request.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out completely and accurately.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll get back to you as soon as possible after receiving your form submission.
