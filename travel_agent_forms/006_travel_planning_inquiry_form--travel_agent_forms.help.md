# Travel Planning Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Planning Inquiry Form is designed to gather essential information from clients to assist travel agents in providing personalized travel planning services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Choose the destination you wish to travel to from the options provided.
- Select the date you want to travel on.
- Enter the duration of your trip in days.
- Select the travel agent(s) you would like to assist you with your travel planning.
- Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
- Provide any additional message or information about your travel preferences.
- Take note of any relevant information for the travel agent(s) assigned to your case.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Destination (<code>Where do you want to travel to</code>)
* **Where do you want to travel to**: Select the destination for your trip.
*   Options: Beach, Mountain, City.

### Travel Date (<code>When do you want to travel</code>)
* **When do you want to travel**: Enter the date of your trip.

### Duration (<code>How long do you want to travel</code>)
* **How long do you want to travel**: Enter a duration in days for your trip.

### Travel Agent (<code>Choose a travel agent</code>)
* **Travel Agent**: Select the travel agent(s) you would like to work with for your trip.

### Contact Information
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact details to ensure we can get in touch with you efficiently.
*   This field is for internal note-keeping only.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
*   This field is required for us to get in touch with you.

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
*   This field is required for us to get in touch with you.

### Additional Message
* **Additional Message**: Provide any additional information about your travel preferences or needs.
*   This field is optional, but it helps us better understand your requirements.

### Travel Agent Note
*   **Travel Agent Note**: Take note of any information relevant to your travel agent.
*   This field is optional and for internal reference only.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure we can best assist you with your travel planning needs.
