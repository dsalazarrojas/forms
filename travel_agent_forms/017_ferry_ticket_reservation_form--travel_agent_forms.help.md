# ferry_ticket_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ferry ticket reservation form is designed for travel agents to collect essential information from passengers to facilitate the reservation process. This form helps gather details about the passenger and travel agent, ensuring accurate and efficient ferry ticket reservations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Form ID**: Enter the unique form ID to identify the reservation.
2. **Departure City**: Enter the city of departure for the ferry.
3. **Departure Time**: Select the time of departure for the ferry.
4. **Destination City**: Enter the city of destination for the ferry.
5. **Passenger Name**: Enter the name of the passenger(s) for whom the ticket is being reserved.
6. **Passenger Phone**: Enter the phone number of the passenger(s).
7. **Passenger Email**: Enter the email address of the passenger(s).
8. **Ferry Type**: Select the type of ferry (Ferry, Bus, or Train) for the reservation.
9. **Passenger Count**: Enter the number of passengers for the reservation.
10. **Departure Date**: Enter the date of departure for the ferry.
11. **Arrival Time**: Select the arrival time for the ferry.
12. **Ferry Schedule**: Enter any additional notes or instructions about the ferry schedule.
13. **Passenger Type**: Select the type(s) of passenger (Adult, Child, Senior).
14. **Travel Agent Name**: Enter your name as the travel agent.
15. **Travel Agent Email**: Enter your email address as the travel agent.
16. **Travel Agent Phone**: Enter your phone number as the travel agent.
17. **Travel Agent ID**: Enter your travel agent ID (optional).
18. **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the customer (if different from the travel agent).
19. **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the customer (if different from the travel agent).
20. **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the customer (if different from the travel agent).
21. **Customer ID**: Enter the customer ID (if different from the travel agent).
