The form purpose is to collect and track information about trips taken by employees of the organization. This information is used to monitor and analyze travel patterns, costs, and status of trips. # Trip Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trip Tracking Form is used to collect and track information about trips taken by employees of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide details about the trip, including the travel date and travel time.
2. Enter the travel destination and any costs associated with the trip.
3. Add any additional trip notes that you would like to include.
4. Specify the current status of the trip (Active or Inactive).
5. If applicable, select the purpose of the trip (Yes or No).
6. If applicable, enter the contact information of the travel agent.
7. Provide any other relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Trip Details (Trip Details)** (`trip_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of the trip.
* **Travel Date (Travel Date)** (`travel_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the trip.
* **Travel Time (Travel Time)** (`travel_time`, time, required: false): Select the time of the trip.
* **Travel Destination (Travel Destination)** (`travel_destination`, text, required: false): Enter the location(s) visited during the trip.
* **Travel Cost (Travel Cost)** (`travel_cost`, number, required: false): Enter the cost of the trip.
* **Trip Notes (Trip Notes)** (`trip_notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the trip.
* **Trip Status (Trip Status)** (`trip_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the current status of the trip (Active or Inactive).
* **Trip Purpose (Trip Purpose)** (`trip_purpose`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the purpose of the trip (Yes or No).
* **Travel Agent (Travel Agent)** (`travel_agent`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the travel agent (if applicable).
* **Contact Info (Contact Info)** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter any contact information related to the trip.
