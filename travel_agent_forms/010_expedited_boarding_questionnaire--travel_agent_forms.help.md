# Expedited Boarding Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expedited Boarding Questionnaire is a form used to gather travel-related information from passengers to facilitate expeditious boarding procedures. This form helps travel agents collect accurate and necessary details to ensure a smooth boarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for Passenger Type, Document Type, Travel Class, Document Status, and Country.
3. Enter your contact information, including contact number, email address, and address.
4. If applicable, upload any relevant documents or provide a Document URL.
5. Select your travel agency and travel agent.
6. Add any Comments if necessary.
7. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Passenger Type**:
	+ Select Infant for children under 18 years old.
	+ Select Child for children 18 and above, or adults traveling with children.
	+ Select Adult for adult passengers traveling alone.
* **Document Type**:
	+ Select Passport for international travel documents.
	+ Select Visa for visa-related documents.
	+ Select ID for identification documents.
* **Document Number**: Enter your passport number or ID number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yy format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Travel Date**: Enter your travel date in the mm/dd/yy format (e.g., 02/10/2024).
* **Travel Class**:
	+ Select Economy for standard travel classes.
	+ Select Business for business-class travel.
	+ Select First for first-class travel.
* **Flight Number**: Enter your flight number.
* **Flight Date**: Enter your flight date in the mm/dd/yy format (e.g., 03/15/2024).
* **Boarding Number**: Enter your boarding number.
* **Travel Agency**:
	+ Select the travel agency you are affiliated with.
* **Travel Agent**:
	+ Select your travel agent's name.
* **Document Status**:
	+ Select Pending for documents that are still pending approval.
	+ Select Approved for documents that have been approved.
	+ Select Denied for denied documents.
* **Comments**: Add any relevant comments or notes about your document status.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Country**: Select your country of origin or residence.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **Document URL**: Provide a URL for your document, if applicable.
* **Uploaded By**: Enter the name of the person who uploaded the document.
* **Document Type**: Enter the type of document uploaded.
