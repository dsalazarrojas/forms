# Travel Agent Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for travel agents to register their information and preferences for onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company information in the **Travel Agent** field.
2. Enter your company's name, address, phone number, and email address in the respective fields.
3. Enter your name, phone number, and email address in the **Travel Agent** field.
4. Select your preferred onboarding type (Full Onboarding or Light Onboarding).
5. Choose your availability (morning, afternoon, or evening).
6. Add any additional notes about yourself or your company in the **Travel Agent Notes** and **Company Notes** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Travel Agent
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent_info`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name.

### 2. Company Name
* **CompanyName** (`company_name`, text, required: false): Enter your company's name.

### 3. Company Address
* **companyAddress** (`company_address`, text, required: false): Enter your company's address.

### 4. Company Phone
* **companyPhone** (`company_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your company's phone number.

### 5. Company Email
* **companyEmail** (`company_email`, email, required: false): Enter your company's email address.

### 6. Agent Name
* **agentName** (`agent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name.

### 7. Agent Phone
* **agentPhone** (`agent_phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

### 8. Agent Email
* **agentEmail** (`agent_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.

### 9. Onboarding Type
* **onboardingType** (`onboarding_type`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred onboarding type (Full Onboarding or Light Onboarding).

### 10. Onboarding Date
* **onboardingDate** (`onboarding_date`, date, required: false): Enter your onboarding date (if applicable).

### 11. Availability
* **agentAvailability** (`agent_availability`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose your availability (morning, afternoon, or evening).

### 12. Travel Agent Notes
* **Travel Agent Notes** (`travel_agent_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about yourself.

### 13. Company Notes
* **Company Notes** (`company_notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about your company.
 
### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your information is complete and accurate.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact our support team.
