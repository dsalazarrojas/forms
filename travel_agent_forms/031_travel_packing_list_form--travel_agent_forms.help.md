# travel_packing_list_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel_packing_list_form is a tool designed to help you create a personalized packing list for your trip. It collects information about your destination, travel dates, transportation, and other relevant details to ensure you pack everything you need.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the required fields with your travel information:
	* Destination: Enter the name of your destination city or town.
	* Travel Dates: Enter your travel dates in the date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
	* Mode of Transportation: Select the mode of transportation you will be using (Airplane, Train, Car, or Bus).
2. Add additional details:
	* Accommodations: Enter the name of your hotel or accommodation.
	* Transportation: Enter any additional transportation information, such as rental car details.
	* Miscellaneous: Enter any other items you need to pack.
3. Emergency Contact Information:
	* Emergency Contact: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
	* Emergency Phone: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
	* Emergency Email: Enter the email of your emergency contact.
4. Flight Information:
	* Flight Number: Enter the flight number (if applicable).
	* Flight Details: Enter any additional flight information (e.g., flight status).
	* Return Flight Number: Enter the return flight number (if applicable).
	* Return Flight Details: Enter any additional return flight information.
5. Host Information:
	* Host Name: Enter the name of your host or contact person.
	* Host Phone: Enter the phone number of your host or contact person.
	* Host Email: Enter the email of your host or contact person.
6. Travel Agency Information:
	* Travel Agency: Select the travel agency you are working with (Travel Agency or Local Agency).
	* Travel Agency Address: Enter the address of the travel agency.
	* Travel Agency Phone: Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
	* Travel Agency Email: Enter the email of the travel agency.
