# Visa Quotation Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from travelers to create a visa quotation request. You will be asked to provide details about your travel plans, including destination, travel type, and other relevant information. This will help us provide you with an accurate and efficient quotation for your visa needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the destination country for your trip.
2. Choose the type of travel (Tourism, Business, or Study).
3. Enter your departure and return dates (if applicable).
4. Select the duration of your trip.
5. Enter your name and contact information.
6. Provide any additional message or comment about your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Destination** (`Destination`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the country you plan to visit.
* **Travel Type** (`Travel Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the following options: Tourism, Business, or Study.
* **Departure Date** (`Departure Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to depart.
* **Return Date** (`Return Date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you plan to return (if different from departure date).
* **Travel Duration** (`Travel Duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of your trip in days.
* **Travel Agent** (`Travel Agent`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your travel agent (if you have one).
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name, phone number, and any other contact information that you'd like us to have.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Message** (`Message`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions about your travel plans.
* **Travel Agency** (`Travel Agency`, `select_one`, optional): Select the travel agency that you are affiliated with (if any).
* **Visa Type** (`Visa Type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more of the following options: Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3.

## Tips
Please note that some fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help us provide a more accurate quotation for your visa needs.
