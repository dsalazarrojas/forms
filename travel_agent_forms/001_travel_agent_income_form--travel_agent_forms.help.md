# Travel Agent Income Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agent Income Form is used to report income and fees received from travel agencies by travel agents. This form helps to track the status of payments and fees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the Client field.
2. Enter the source of income in the Source Income field.
3. Enter the travel agent's name in the Travel Agent field.
4. Enter the income date in the Income Date field.
5. Enter the amount received in the Amount Received field.
6. Enter the travel agent commission in the Travel Agent Commission field.
7. Enter the travel agent fee in the Travel Agent Fee field.
8. Enter the travel agency in the Travel Agency field.
9. Enter the travel type in the Travel Type field.
10. Enter the travel agent manager in the Travel Agent Manager field.
11. Enter the payment status in the Payment Status field.
12. Enter the travel type ID in the Travel Type ID field.
13. Enter the travel agency ID in the Travel Agency ID field.
14. Enter any additional notes in the Notes field.
15. Enter the travel agent commission status in the Travel Agent Commission Status field.
16. Enter the travel agent fee status in the Travel Agent Fee Status field.
17. Enter the travel agency status in the Travel Agency Status field.
18. Enter the email of the travel agent or agency in the Email field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client** (`client`, text, required): Enter the name of the client from whom you received income.
* **Source Income** (`source_income`, text, required): Enter the source of the income, e.g., travel agency.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required): Enter your name or alias as a travel agent.
* **Income Date** (`income_date`, date, required): Enter the date you received the income.
* **Amount Received** (`amount_received`, number, required): Enter the amount of income you received.
* **Travel Agent Commission** (`travel_agent_commission`, number, required): Enter the commission you received as a travel agent.
* **Travel Agent Fee** (`travel_agent_fee`, number, required): Enter the fee you received as a travel agent.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, required): Enter the name of the travel agency.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, text, required): Enter the type of travel (e.g., air, land, sea).
* **Travel Agent Manager** (`travel_agent_manager`, text, required): Enter your manager's name or alias.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of payment (Pending, Received, Reimbursed, or Refunded).
* **Travel Type ID** (`travel_type_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the travel type.
* **Travel Agency ID** (`travel_agency_id`, text, required): Enter the ID of the travel agency.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the transaction.
* **Other Notes** (`notes_other`, note, optional): Enter any other notes or additional information about the transaction.
* **Travel Agent Commission Rate** (`travel_agent_commission_percentage`, number, required): Enter the commission rate as a decimal value (e.g., 0.1 for 10%).
* **Travel Agency Manager** (`travel_agent_manager`, text, required): Enter your manager's name or alias at the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Contact** (`travel_agency_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name or alias at the travel agency.
* **Notes Status** (`notes_status`, note, optional): Enter the status of the notes (optional).
* **Other Notes Status** (`notes_other_status`, note, optional): Enter the status of the other notes (optional).
* **Travel Agent Commission Status** (`travel_agent_commission_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of travel agent commission (Pending, Received, Reimbursed, or Refunded).
* **Travel Agent Fee Status** (`travel_agent_fee_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of travel agent fee (Pending, Received, Reimbursed, or Refunded).
* **Travel Agency Status** (`travel_agency_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of travel agency (Active or Inactive).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the travel agent or agency (optional).

This guide will help you understand how to complete the Travel Agent Income Form correctly. If you have any further questions or need clarification on any field, please feel free to ask.
