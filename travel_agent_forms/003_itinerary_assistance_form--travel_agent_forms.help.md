# Itinerary Assistance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from travel agents who need assistance with planning or modifying existing itineraries.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your **Participant Name**, the name of the person(s) traveling.
*   If applicable, enter the **Participant Number**, the unique identifier associated with the participant.
*   Provide your **Travel Dates**, the dates of the trip in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person(s) traveling.
*   **Participant number** (`participant_number`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the unique identifier associated with the participant.
*   **Travel dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of the trip in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

## Tips
*   Double-check your participant's name and participant number (if provided) to ensure they match existing records.
*   Be as specific as possible when entering travel dates to ensure accurate itinerary planning.
