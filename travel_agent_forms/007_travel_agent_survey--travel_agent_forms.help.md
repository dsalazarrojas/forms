# Travel Agent Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback and information from travel agents to help us understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select all that apply for the travel agent category you belong to.
2. Please provide a brief description of your experiences and opinions about our travel-related services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Travel Agent Category**
 * **travel_agent_survey_category** (`select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to select all categories that apply to your role or affiliation with the travel industry.
	+ Please choose 'Yes' for any category that accurately describes your position.
### **Travel Agent Description**
 * **travel_agent_survey_description** (`text`, required: false):
	+ This field is where you can provide a brief description of your experiences and opinions about our travel-related services.
	+ Please type in your thoughts and suggestions to help us improve our services.
### **Tips**
* Be as specific as possible when describing your experiences and opinions.
* If you have any additional comments or suggestions, you can include them in the description field.
* This form is for your convenience, and your input will help us create better services for you and our shared customers.
