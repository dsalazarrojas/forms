# travel_agency_personal_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal information and travel-related details from travel agencies for administrative and record-keeping purposes. The form is meant to gather essential data about the agency's clients, including contact person details, email, phone number, travel dates, destination, reason for travel, type of travel agency, license information, and address.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the client's information, including their name, contact person, and email address.
2. Provide the contact person's phone number and the client's travel agency information.
3. Enter the travel dates and destination.
4. Specify the reason for the client's travel.
5. Select the type of travel agency (e.g., Domestic, International, Local, Out of Country).
6. Enter the travel agency's license information, if applicable.
7. Provide the agency's address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the client's name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Person (Contact Person)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the name of the contact person at the travel agency.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, **required**: false): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Travel Agency (Travel Agency)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the name of the travel agency.
* **Travel Dates (Travel Dates)** (`date`, **required**: false): Enter the travel dates in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time (Time)** (`time`, **required**: false): Enter the travel time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Destination (Destination)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the destination of the travel.
* **Reason (Reason)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the reason for the client's travel.
* **Type of Travel Agency (Type of Travel Agency)** (`select_one`, **required**: false): Select the type of travel agency (Domestic, International, Local, Out of Country).
* **Travel Agency License (Travel Agency License)** (`number`, **required**: false): Enter the travel agency's license number, if applicable.
* **License Number (License Number)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the license number.
* **Address (Address)** (`text`, **required**: false): Enter the address of the travel agency.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check the information for accuracy.
- Enter the information clearly and legibly.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask your travel agency representative for assistance.
