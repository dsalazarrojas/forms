# Vacation Itinerary Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Itinerary Form is a tool for travel agents to provide essential details about their travel preferences and needs. This information will be used to create a customized travel plan.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, select your travel agent information by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
2. If you are representing a travel agency, select "Yes" to indicate that this is a agency-related form submission.
3. Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates or follow-ups about your travel plan.
4. Select the start and end dates of your trip.
5. Choose the duration of your trip in hours.
6. Optionally, add your address as a travel agent or a travel agency address.
7. Review and submit the form to create your vacation itinerary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agent Information** (`Yes / No`, select_multiple, required: false)
    + This field allows you to indicate whether this is a travel agent-related form submission.
* **Travel Agency** (`Yes / No`, select_one, required: false)
    + This field helps to identify whether this form submission is related to a travel agency.
* **Travel Agent Details** (text, required: false)
    + Use this field to enter any additional details about your role as a travel agent.
* **Email** (email, required: false)
    + Enter your email address if you'd like to stay updated about your travel plan.
* **Start Date** (date, required: false)
    + Select the start date of your trip.
* **End Date** (date, required: false)
    + Choose the end date of your trip.
* **Start Time** (time, required: false)
    + Select the start time of your trip.
* **End Time** (time, required: false)
    + Choose the end time of your trip.
* **Duration** (number, required: false)
    + Enter the duration of your trip in hours.
* **Agent Address** (note, required: false)
    + Optionally, enter your address as a travel agent.
* **Agency Address** (note, required: false)
    + Optionally, enter the address of the travel agency you represent.

## Tips

* Make sure to accurately select your travel agent information and agency affiliation.
* Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
* Be specific about your travel dates and duration to ensure accurate itinerary creation.
* If you are a travel agent representing an agency, make sure to provide the agency address for better contactability.
