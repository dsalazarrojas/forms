# Trip Planning Quote Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from the travel agent and client about trip planning and quoting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. The travel agent is required to fill out the form, including:
	* Travel Agent Details: This section includes fields such as travel agent name, contact information, and services provided.
	* Travel Agent Agreement: This section includes fields to confirm the travel agent's agreement to provide services and abide by terms and conditions.
2. The client is required to fill out the form, including:
	* Contact Details: This section includes fields such as client name, contact information, and preferences.
	* Client Agreement: This section includes fields to confirm the client's agreement to the travel agent's terms and conditions.
3. Both the travel agent and client must sign the form to confirm their agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agent** (`1`): Enter your travel agent name and contact information.
* **Departure Date** (`2`): Enter the departure date of the trip.
* **Travel Type** (`3`): Select 'Yes' if you are offering travel services, 'No' otherwise.
* **Travel Agent Details** (`4`): Enter any additional details about your travel services.
* **Quote Request** (`5`): Enter any specific requests or requirements from the client.
* **Contact Details** (`6`): Enter your contact information and any additional details.
* **Client Preferences** (`7`): Enter any client preferences or special requests.
* **Quote Details** (`8`): Enter any specific quote details or requirements.
* **Travel Agent Services** (`9`): Select any additional services you provide, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Travel Agent Comments** (`10`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client's trip.
* **Travel Agent Agreement** (`11`): Confirm your agreement to provide services and abide by terms and conditions.
* **Client Agreement** (`12`): Confirm the client's agreement to your terms and conditions.
* **Travel Agent Terms and Conditions** (`13`): Confirm your agreement to abide by travel agent terms and conditions.
* **Client Terms and Conditions** (`14`): Confirm the client's agreement to your terms and conditions.
* **Travel Agreements** (`15`): Select any additional travel agreements, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Trip Planning Quote Form** (`16`): Select 'Yes' if you are offering trip planning services, 'No' otherwise.
* **Other Travel Agent Fields** (`17`): Select any additional travel agent fields, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Travel Agent Services** (`18`): Select any additional travel agent services, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Travel Agent Comments** (`19`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client's trip.
* **Other Travel Agent Agreements** (`20`): Select any additional travel agent agreements, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Client Fields** (`21`): Select any additional client fields, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Other Client Terms and Conditions** (`22`): Select any additional client terms and conditions, 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Travel Agent Signature** (`23`): Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Client Signature** (`24`): Sign the client's name to confirm their agreement.
* **Form Submit** (`25`): Submit the form to complete the trip planning quote.
