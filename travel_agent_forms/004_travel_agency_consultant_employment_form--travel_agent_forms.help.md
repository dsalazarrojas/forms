# Travel Agency Consultant Employment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential travel agency consultants who wish to collaborate with our agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the text fields. These include:
	* Agency Experience: Describe your experience in the travel industry.
	* Location: Provide the city or region where you are based.
	* Consultant Profile: Briefly introduce yourself and your qualifications.
	* Consultant Contact: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
2. Select your specialization from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your area of expertise.
3. Choose a date for your availability.
4. Indicate how long you are available to work with us.
5. Select your availability frequency (e.g., full-time, part-time, project basis).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Agency Experience
* **Agency Experience** (`travel_agency_experience`, text, required): Describe your experience in the travel industry. Please provide details about your past work, relevant skills, and any achievements.

### Location
* **Location** (`travel_agency_location`, text, required): Provide the city or region where you are based. This will help us understand your geographic coverage.

### Consultant Availability
* **Consultant Availability** (`travel_agency_consultant_availability`, date, required): Choose a date when you are available to work with us. Please select a specific date to avoid ambiguity.

### Availability Duration
* **Availability Duration** (`travel_agency_availability_duration`, number, required): Indicate how long you are available to work with us. Enter a number of days or weeks, depending on your availability.

### Availability Frequency
* **Availability Frequency** (`travel_agency_availability_frequency`, text, required): Select how frequently you are available to work with us. Choose from the options provided (e.g., full-time, part-time, project basis).

### Specialization
* **Specialization** (`travel_agency_specialization`, select_one, required): Select your area of expertise from the dropdown menu. This will help us understand your strengths and areas of specialization.

### Consultant Profile
* **Consultant Profile** (`travel_agency_consultant_profile`, text, required): Briefly introduce yourself and your qualifications. This will help us get to know you better and understand your fit with our agency.

### Consultant Contact
* **Consultant Contact** (`travel_agency_consultant_contact`, text, required): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you. Please provide a phone number and email address.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form. We'll be in touch with you based on your submission.
* We'll review your application and get back to you within a reasonable time frame.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We look forward to hearing from you and potentially collaborating with you in the future.
