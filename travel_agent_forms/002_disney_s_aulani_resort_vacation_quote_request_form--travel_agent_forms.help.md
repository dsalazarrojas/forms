# Disney's Aulani Resort Vacation Quote Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information from guests to provide them with a personalized vacation quote for their stay at the Disney's Aulani Resort.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Select your preferred contact method from the Contact Method dropdown menu. You can choose from Phone, Email, Fax, Text Message, Chat, Social Media, or Video Call. If you have a different preference, select "Other (Specify Below)" and provide your preferred contact method in the Other (Specify Below) field.
4. Select your Arrival Date and Arrival Time in the corresponding fields.
5. Enter the number of guests traveling with you.
6. Enter your Departure Date.
7. (Optional) If you have a preferred Travel Agent, you can provide their name in the Travel Agent field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (`required`): 
* Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
### Last Name (`required`): 
* Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
### Email (`required`): 
* Enter your valid email address to receive important updates and notifications from us.
### Contact Method (`optional`): 
* Select your preferred method of contact. You can choose from Phone, Email, Fax, Text Message, Chat, Social Media, or Video Call. If you select "Other (Specify Below)", please provide your preferred contact method in the Other (Specify Below) field.
### Other (Specify Below) (`optional`): 
* If you selected "Other (Specify Below)" in the Contact Method field, enter your preferred contact method here.
### Arrival Date (`required`): 
* Enter the date you plan to arrive at the resort.
### Arrival Time (`required`): 
* Enter the time you plan to arrive at the resort.
### Number of Guests (`required`): 
* Enter the number of people traveling with you.
### Departure Date (`required`): 
* Enter the date you plan to depart from the resort.
### Travel Agent (`optional`): 
* If you have a preferred travel agent, enter their name here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date to receive important updates and notifications from us.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please let us know in the comments field or through your preferred contact method.
* Our travel agents will review and verify the information provided and contact you to discuss your vacation quote.
