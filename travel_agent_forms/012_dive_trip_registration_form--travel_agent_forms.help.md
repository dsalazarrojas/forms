# Dive Trip Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dive Trip Registration Form is a crucial tool used to prepare for and manage your dive trip. By filling out this form, you'll help us better understand your needs and preferences, ensuring a more enjoyable and safe diving experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete the form, please review the instructions carefully and ensure you provide accurate information.
- Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, completing one page at a time.
- For each field, read the label and hint carefully to understand what information is required.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Dive Prophets** (`prophets`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more dive instructors that will be accompanying you on the trip. This helps us assign the right dive guides and plan activities accordingly.
- **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, `date`, required: false): Choose the travel dates for your dive trip. Please ensure you select the exact dates you plan to travel.
- **Trip Details** (`trip_details`, `note`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your trip, including any specific activities or requirements you may have. This helps us tailor the experience to your needs.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you regarding any updates or changes to the trip.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about any last-minute changes or updates.
