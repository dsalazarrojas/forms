# Tour Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tour Booking Form is used to book a tour with a travel agent. It gathers information from customers to create a tour package.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the "Tour Type" field by selecting the type of tour you are interested in (e.g., kayaking, hiking, cycling).
2. Enter your "Customer Name".
3. Enter your "Customer Email" and verify that it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your "Customer Phone" number.
5. Enter a brief "Activity Name" and description in the "Activity Name" and "Activity Description" fields.
6. Select the "Activity Type" of the tour (e.g., kayaking, hiking, cycling).
7. Enter the "Start Date" and "End Date" of the tour.
8. Enter the "Number of Guests" attending the tour.
9. Enter the "Tour Cost" of the tour.
10. Select the "Payment Status" of the tour (e.g., paid, unpaid).
11. Choose the "Payment Method" (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, PayPal).
12. Enter the "Payment Date" of the tour.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Tour Type** (Select One): Select the type of tour you are interested in (e.g., kayaking, hiking, cycling).
- **Customer Name** (Text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
- **Customer Email** (Email): Enter your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
- **Customer Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
- **Activity Name** (Text): Enter a brief name for the activity you are interested in.
- **Activity Description** (Text): Enter a brief description of the activity you are interested in.
- **Activity Type** (Select One): Select the type of the activity (e.g., kayaking, hiking, cycling).
- **Number of Guests** (Text): Enter the number of people attending the tour.
- **Tour Cost** (Text): Enter the cost of the tour.
- **Payment Status** (Select One): Select the status of the payment (e.g., paid, unpaid).
- **Payment Method** (Select Multiple): Choose the method of payment (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, PayPal).
- **Payment Date** (Date): Enter the date of payment.
