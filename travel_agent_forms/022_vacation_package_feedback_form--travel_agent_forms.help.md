# Vacation Package Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide feedback on your recent vacation package experience with [Travel Agency Name]. Your feedback will help them understand what they did well and what areas need improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your **Travel Agency Name** in the field below. This will help the travel agency identify your feedback and connect it with your specific package.
2. Rate your **Vacation Package Rating** on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
3. Describe your **Vacation Experience** in the text box below.
4. Indicate your **Satisfaction Level** with the package by selecting one of the options (1, 2, or 3).
5. Provide any additional **Feedback** you have about your package.
6. If you have any **Additional Comments**, please feel free to add them below.
7. Enter your **Contact Email** so that the travel agency can respond to your feedback.
8. Provide your **Contact Phone** number so that the travel agency can contact you if needed.
9. Please enter the **Date of Travel** for your vacation package.
10. If applicable, select your **Travel Period**.
11. Select your **Travel Duration** in days or hours.
12. Choose the number of **Travelers** in your party.
13. Select the travel agency's **Service** level (Good, Fair, or Poor).
14. Evaluate the **Travel Agent's Attitude** (Good, Fair, or Poor).
15. Choose the **Travel Agent's Skills** level (Good, Fair, or Poor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Agency Name**: Please enter the name of the travel agency that provided your vacation package.
* **Vacation Package Rating**: Rate your experience with the package on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Vacation Experience**: Describe your experience with the package in the space below.
* **Satisfaction Level**: Indicate your satisfaction level with the package by selecting one of the options (1, 2, or 3).
* **Feedback**: Provide any feedback or suggestions you have about the package.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so that the travel agency can respond to your feedback.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the travel agency can contact you if needed.
* **Date of Travel**: Enter the date of your travel.
* **Travel Period**: If applicable, select your travel period.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of your travel in days or hours.
* **Number of Travelers**: Enter the number of travelers in your party.
* **Travel Agency Service**: Select the level of service provided by the travel agency (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Travel Agent Attitude**: Evaluate the travel agent's attitude (Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Travel Agent Skills**: Evaluate the travel agent's skills (Good, Fair, or Poor).
