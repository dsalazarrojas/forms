# Travel Agency Template Cruise Packing List Generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Agency Template Cruise Packing List Generator is a tool for travel agents to create a suggested packing list for their customers based on their trip details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the trip details, including travel dates, cruise type, departure airport, flight number, ship name, cabin type, and travel documents.
2. Review and edit the generated packing list as necessary to ensure it meets the customer's needs and preferences.
3. Use the completed packing list as a starting point for further customization and review with the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trip Details** (Trip Details, text, optional):
	+ This field allows you to enter a brief description of the trip, including any relevant details or notes.
* **Travel Dates** (Travel Dates, number, optional):
	+ Enter the travel dates for the trip, including departure and return dates.
* **Cruise Type** (Cruise Type, select_one, optional):
	+ Choose the type of cruise, such as Sea Cruise, River Cruise, or Lake Cruise.
* **Departure Airport** (Departure Airport, text, optional):
	+ Enter the code for the departure airport.
* **Flight Number** (Flight Number, text, optional):
	+ Enter the flight number for your reference.
* **Ship Name** (Ship Name, text, optional):
	+ Enter the name of the ship on which you will be traveling.
* **Cabin Type** (Cabin Type, text, optional):
	+ Choose the type of cabin or accommodation for your trip.
* **Travel Documents** (Travel Documents, text, optional):
	+ Enter any necessary travel documents, such as visas or travel insurance information.
* **Packing List** (Packing List, note, optional):
	+ Review and edit the generated packing list to ensure it meets your needs and preferences.

## Tips

* Double-check your trip details and travel dates for accuracy before generating the packing list.
* Make sure to include any necessary or recommended items based on your destination and activities.
* Review and edit the generated packing list to ensure it is accurate and sufficient for your trip.
* Customize the packing list as needed for individual customers based on their preferences and needs.
