# Travel Authorization Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Travel Authorization Access Form is used to request travel authorization for business purposes. Fill out this form to inform your manager and travel department about your travel plans and ensure that everything is in order before embarking on your trip.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information and approvals from your manager or travel department before filling out this form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form for approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Authorization Access** (`travel_authorization_access`, text, optional): This field is where you provide a brief description of your travel purpose and the type of authorization you need.
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, optional): In this field, describe your travel itinerary, including the type of activity you will be doing and any relevant contacts or meetings.
* **Destination** (`destination`, select_one, optional): Select the city or location you will be traveling to. Available options are: London, New York, Paris.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your travel.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your travel.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the reason for your travel. Options are: Business, Personal, Other.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, optional): Enter the number of days you will be traveling.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): If you are traveling with an agent or have specific travel arrangements, provide their contact information here.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, note, optional): If you need to attach any documents or supporting information for your travel, upload or enter it here.
* **Travel Budget** (`travel_budget`, number, optional): If you need to estimate or provide a budget for your travel, enter it here.
* **Travel Itinerary** (`travel_itinerary`, note, optional): Enter your travel itinerary in this field if you need to provide more details about your trip.
* **Travel Approver** (`travel_approver`, select_one, optional): Choose the person who will be responsible for approving your travel. Options are: John Doe, Jane Smith, Bob Johnson.

Note:
* Ensure you provide accurate and complete information in the form to avoid delays or rejections.
* You may need to attach supporting documents or files to certain fields.
* If you have any questions or need help with filling out this form, contact your supervisor or travel department.
