# Tourism Regeneration Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help travel agents register their initiatives for the Tourism Regeneration Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Complete each field with the requested information.**
2. **Review and submit the form once you have filled all fields.**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and contact information.
  • Please provide your name and any contact details you want to be associated with your registration.
- **Project Details** (`project_details`, text, required: false): Enter project details.
  • Provide a brief description of your project.
- **Partner Details** (`partner_details`, text, required: false): Enter partner details.
  • If you have any partners associated with your project, provide their information.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Enter contact details.
  • Provide contact information for the person filling out this form.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes.
  • Use this field to include any other relevant information you would like to share with the organizers.

## Tips
* **Please double-check all fields before submitting the form.** 
* **If you have any issues or concerns, contact: form@gic.mx**
