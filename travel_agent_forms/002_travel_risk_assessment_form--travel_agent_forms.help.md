# Travel Risk Assessment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to assess the risk level of a traveller, taking into account their past experiences, medical history, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page. Each page has its own unique requirements and options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Traveller History** (Select Multiple): Indicate if you have had any previous travel-related incidents or medical issues.
* **Traveller Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Traveller Contact Number** (Text, Optional): Provide your contact information for emergency purposes.
* **Traveller Address** (Note, Optional): Enter your current address or contact address.
* **Traveller Medical History** (Note, Required): Report any existing medical conditions that may affect your travel.
* **Traveller Medical Notes** (Note, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information related to your medical history.
* **Medical Guidelines** (Date, Optional): Enter any medical precautions or requirements for your travel.
* **Risk Factor** (Select One, Required): Select your risk level based on your medical history.
* **Next Steps** (Note, Optional): Enter guidance on what to do next.
* **Risk Assessment Result** (Select One, Required): Confirm your risk level.
* **Form Submit** (Select One, Optional): Choose to submit or cancel your response.
* **Medical Accommodation** (Select One, Required): Indicate if medical accommodations are required.
* **Accommodation Details** (Select One, Optional): Enter any additional information on accommodations.
* **Travel Documentation** (Select One, Required): Indicate if travel documentation is required.
* **Medical Certificate** (Date, Optional): Enter any additional medical information if required by travel documentation.
