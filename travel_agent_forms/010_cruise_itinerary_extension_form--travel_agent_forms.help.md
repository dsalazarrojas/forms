# Cruise Itinerary Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for travel agents to extend a client's cruise itinerary. It's intended to be filled out by the client or their authorized representative to provide essential details about their travel plans.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully before starting.
2. Fill in the required information as accurately as possible.
3. Double-check your answers for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`Client Name`, text, optional): Enter the client's name as per your cruise records.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the client's cruise as per their travel plans.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the client's cruise as per their travel plans.
* **Travel Agent Name** (`Travel Agent Name`, text, optional): Enter your name if you're a travel agent filling out this form for yourself or the client.
* **Reason For Extension** (`Reason For Extension`, text, optional): Enter a clear reason for extending the client's itinerary. Be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Cruise Details** (`Cruise Details`, text, optional): Enter the cruise line, cruise name, and cabin number as per the client's travel records.

## Tips
- Be thorough and accurate when filling out this form.
- Make sure you're entering the correct dates for the client's travel.
- Double-check your answers for any mistakes before submitting the form.
