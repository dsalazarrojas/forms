# Honeymoon Travel Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential details about a couple's honeymoon travel plans. The information gathered will help travel agents understand their preferences and provide better services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with your travel information. You can leave blank fields if they are not applicable to your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Destination** (`travel_destination`, text, optional): Enter the country or region you plan to travel to for your honeymoon.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, optional): Select the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of travel that best fits your preferences:
	+ Beach
	+ City
	+ Adventure
	+ Cruise
	+ Road Trip
* **Travel Budget** (`travel_budget`, number, optional): Enter your estimated budget for the trip.
* **Travel Group Size** (`travel_group_size`, text, optional): If you're traveling with a group, enter the size of your group.
* **Travel Language** (`travel_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select the languages you speak or would like to learn during your trip:
	+ English
	+ Spanish
	+ French
	+ German
	+ Italian
	+ Portuguese
* **Travel Currency** (`travel_currency`, text, optional): Enter the currency you will be using for your trip.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, time, optional): Enter the length of time you plan to travel.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, optional): If you are working with a travel agent, enter their name.
