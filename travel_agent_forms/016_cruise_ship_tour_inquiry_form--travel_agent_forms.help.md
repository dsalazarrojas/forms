# Cruise Ship Tour Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to inquire about available tours and cruises.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Info" section.
2. Select your tour preferences in the "Tour Preferences" section.
3. Choose your travel agent and their contact information in the "Travel Agent" section.
4. Select a date and time that suits you in the "Date Preferences" section.
5. Choose any special tour preferences in the "Tour Preferences 2" section.
6. Add any special requests in the "Special Requests" section.
7. Enter your email and phone number in the "Email" and "Phone" sections.
8. Select your preferred travel agency from the options provided in the "Travel Agency" section.
9. Specify the duration of your tour in the "Tour Duration" section.
10. Choose the type of tour you are interested in from the "Tour Type" section.
11. Submit your inquiry by clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your name, address, and other contact details to help us contact you regarding your tour inquiry.
* **Tour Preferences (tour_preferences)**: Describe the type of tour you are interested in, e.g., "relaxation" or "adventure".
* **Travel Agent (travel_agent)**: Select the travel agent who will assist you with your tour inquiry.
* **Travel Agent Contact (travel_agent_contact)**: Enter the contact information of your selected travel agent.
* **Date Preferences (date_preferences)**: Select a date for your tour, e.g., "Next Saturday" or "Anytime this month".
* **Time Preferences (time_preferences)**: Select a time for your tour, e.g., "Morning" or "Afternoon".
* **Tour Preferences 2 (tour_preferences_2)**: Choose any additional tour preferences, e.g., "Couples only" or "Group tour".
* **Notes (notes)**: Add any additional information or requests you have about your tour.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your tour.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your tour.
* **Travel Agency (travel_agency)**: Select the travel agency that will handle your tour.
* **Tour Duration (tour_duration)**: Specify the number of days you would like your tour to last.
* **Tour Type (tour_type)**: Choose the type of tour you are interested in, e.g., "Cruise" or "Island hopping".
* **Special Requests (special_requests)**: Enter any special requests you have for your tour.
* **Submit (submit)**: Click this button to submit your tour inquiry.
* **Form ID (form_id)**: This field is not visible to the customer and is used for internal purposes only.
