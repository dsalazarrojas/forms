# Flight Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flight Booking Form is used to gather information for flight-related bookings. It is a crucial document that helps us process bookings efficiently and ensure that all necessary details are captured.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Flight Details** section with the necessary information about the flight you wish to book.
2. In the **Customer** section, provide your personal details:
	* **Customer Name**
	* **Customer Surname**
	* **Phone Number**
	* **Email**
3. In the **Travel Dates and Times** section:
	* Select the **Departure Date** and **Arrival Date** for your flight.
	* Specify the **Departure Time** and **Arrival Time** for your flight.
4. Enter the **Flight Duration** to specify the length of your flight.
5. In the **Customer Contact Information** section, fill in:
	* **Customer Name**
	* **Customer Surname**
	* **Phone Number**
	* **Email**

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Flight Details (Page 1)
* **Flight Details** (`flight_details`, text, required): Enter the specific details about the flight you wish to book.

### Customer (Page 2)
* **Customer** (`customer`, text, required): Enter your personal details.

### Departure Airport (Page 3)
* **Departure Airport** (`departure_airport`, text, required): Specify the departure airport for your flight.

### Arrival Airport (Page 4)
* **Arrival Airport** (`arrival_airport`, text, required): Specify the arrival airport for your flight.

### Departure Date (Page 5)
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, date, required): Select the departure date for your flight.

### Departure Time (Page 6)
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, required): Specify the departure time for your flight.

### Arrival Date (Page 7)
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, required): Select the arrival date for your flight.

### Flight Duration (Page 8)
* **Flight Duration** (`flight_duration`, time, required): Specify the duration of your flight.

### Customer Contact Information (Page 9)
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Customer Surname** (`customer_surname`, text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Phone Number** (`customer_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`customer_email`, email, required): Enter your email.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your booking is processed efficiently.
* Double-check the accuracy of your input information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the form, please reach out to our customer support team.
