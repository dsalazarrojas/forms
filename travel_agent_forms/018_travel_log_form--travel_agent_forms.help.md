# Travel Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Log Form is designed to gather information about a traveler's trip for the travel agents to document and track.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the trip in the "form-title" field. This is a brief description of the trip.
2. Select the date of travel in the "travel-date" field.
3. Choose the origin of the trip from the "origin" field. This is the location from which the traveler started their journey.
4. Choose the destination(s) of the trip from the "destination" field. You can select multiple destinations.
5. Select the mode of transportation used for the trip from the "transport" field.
6. Enter the travel agency that handled the trip in the "travel-agency" field (optional).
7. Enter any additional comments about the trip in the "activity" field (optional).
8. Enter the estimated budget for the trip in the "trip-budget" field. This is a required field.
9. Enter any notes about the trip in the "note" field (optional).
10. Enter the traveler's email address in the "email" field. This is a required field.
11. Enter the traveler's phone number in the "phone" field (optional).
12. Enter any comments about the trip in the "form-comments" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **form-title** (text): A brief description of the trip.
* **travel-date** (date): The date of travel for the trip.
* **origin** (select): The location from which the traveler started their journey. Choose from New York City, San Francisco, or Los Angeles.
* **destination** (select multiple): The location(s) to which the traveler journeyed. Choose from New York City, San Francisco, or Los Angeles.
* **departure-date** (date): The date of departure for the trip (optional).
* **stay-duration** (number): The duration of the stay for the trip (optional).
* **transport** (select): The mode of transportation used for the trip. Choose from Car, Flight, or Train.
* **travel-agency** (text): The travel agency that handled the trip (optional).
* **activity** (text): Additional comments about the trip (optional).
* **trip-budget** (number): The estimated budget for the trip (required).
* **note** (text): Any notes about the trip (optional).
* **email** (email): The traveler's email address (required).
* **phone** (text): The traveler's phone number (optional).
* **form-comments** (text): Any comments about the form (optional).
* **form-submitted-at** (date): The date the form was submitted (not editable).
* **form-submitted-by** (text): The agent who submitted the form (not editable).
* **form-created-at** (date): The date the form was created (not editable).
* **form-last-updated-at** (date): The last time the form was updated (not editable).
* **form-status** (select): The status of the form. Choose from Draft or Published (optional).
* **form-comments** (text): Any comments about the form (optional).
* **activity-type** (select): The type of activity for the trip. Choose from Hiking, Swimming, or Shopping (optional).
* **activity-details** (text): Additional details about the activity (optional).
* **activity-date** (date): The date of the activity (optional).
* **trip-details** (text): Additional details about the trip (optional).
* **form-assigned-to** (text): The agent who is assigned to the form (not editable).
