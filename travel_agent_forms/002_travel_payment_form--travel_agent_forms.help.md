# travel_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect payment information for travel-related transactions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as required.
2. Review the form carefully to ensure all fields are accurate.
3. Submit the form to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (text, optional): This field is for any additional information the user wants to provide.

### Travel Group
* **Travel Group** (number, optional): If the user has a travel group, enter their ID number here. If not, leave this field blank.

### Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (number, optional): Enter the amount of the payment.

### Payment Date
* **Payment Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the payment.

### Payment Time
* **Payment Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the payment.

### Payment Status
* **Payment Status** (select_one, optional): Select the current status of the payment: Complete, Incomplete, or Pending.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (select_multiple, optional): Select the method used for payment: Bank Transfer, Cash, or Credit Card.

### Notes
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the payment.

### Email
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the customer or travel agency.

### Phone
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the customer or travel agency.

### User Comments
* **User Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the user.

### Travel Agency
* **Travel Agency** (text, optional): Enter the name or ID of the travel agency.

### Travel Agency Phone
* **Travel Agency Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.

### Travel Agency Email
* **Travel Agency Email** (text, optional): Enter the email address of the travel agency.

### Manager Comments
* **Manager Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the manager.

### Travel Agency Comments
* **Travel Agency Comments** (text, optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the travel agency.
