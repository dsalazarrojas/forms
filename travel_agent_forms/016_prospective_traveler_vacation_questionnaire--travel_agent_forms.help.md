# Prospective Traveler Vacation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a questionnaire for prospective travelers to provide information about their vacation plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the "Vacation Planning" page and read the information provided.
2. Scroll down to the "Travel Agency Information" section and fill in the "Travel Agency Name" field.
3. In the "Contact Info" section, enter your email address and phone number.
4. In the "Vacation Preferences" section, select your preferred type of vacation (e.g., Adventure, Relaxation, etc.).
5. In the "Trip Details" section, provide any additional information about your trip.
6. Finally, confirm that you have read and agreed to the "Travel Agency Terms and Conditions".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vacation Planning**: This page provides a brief introduction to the questionnaire and explains the purpose of the form.
* **Travel Agency Information** (`travel_agent`): Enter the name of the travel agency you are planning to use.
* **Travel Agency Email** (`travel_agent_email`): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
* **Contact Info** (`email`, `phone`): Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Vacation Preferences** (`vacation_preferences`): Select your preferred type of vacation (e.g., Adventure, Relaxation, etc.).
* **Trip Length** (`trip_length`): Enter the length of your trip (in days).
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`): Select the dates of your trip.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_type`): Select the type of travel (e.g., Family, Romantic, etc.).
* **Budget** (`budget`): Enter your budget for the trip.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`): Provide any additional information about your trip.
* **Travel Agency Terms and Conditions** (`travel_agency_terms`): Read and agree to the travel agency's terms and conditions.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the travel agency's terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your information is processed correctly.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them in the "Additional Info" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the travel agency directly.
