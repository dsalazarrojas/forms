# Prospective Travel Client Vacation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prospective Travel Client Vacation Form is designed to gather information from prospective travel clients to provide tailored services and accurate quotes. By completing this form, you'll help travel agents understand your travel preferences, plans, and requirements, ensuring that they can offer you the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please start by filling out the form completely, including all requested fields.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
3. If you have any additional comments or questions, please use the "Notes" field to provide more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Client Information**: This section is for your personal details, such as your name and contact information. Please fill out this section completely.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of your desired travel period, including the start and end dates. This will help us understand your travel schedule.
* **Travel Locations**: Indicate your preferred travel destinations. Select all that apply, as you may have multiple destinations in mind.
* **Travel Details**: Use this field to provide any additional information about your travel plans, such as activities or events you'd like to attend.
* **Travel Budget**: Enter your estimated travel budget to help us provide more accurate quotes and recommendations.
* **Travel Agent Information**: This section is not required, but if you have a preferred travel agent or contact, please provide their information here.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address or phone number so that we can get in touch with you for any further questions or quotes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used as a backup contact method in case we need to reach out to you.
* **Notes**: Use this field to add any additional comments or questions you may have. This is a great place to ask questions or provide further clarification on your travel plans.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not currently required, but you may be assigned a specific tool or resource to help with your travel planning. If you're unsure, please ask your travel agent for guidance.
