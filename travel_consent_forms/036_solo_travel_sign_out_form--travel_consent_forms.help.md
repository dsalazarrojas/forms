# Solo Travel Sign Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Solo Travel Sign Out Form is designed to collect essential information from solo travelers to ensure their safety and the safety of others. This form helps gather relevant details about the traveler's trip, including contact information, travel dates, itinerary, emergency contact information, medical information, and relationships with travel agencies and their representatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your name, contact number, and email address. This will help us keep in touch with you throughout your trip.
2. **Specify travel dates**: Enter the dates of your trip, including the start and end dates.
3. **Describe your itinerary**: Provide a brief description of your travel plans, including any destinations and modes of transportation you plan to use.
4. **Emergency contact information**: Provide the name, relationship, and contact information of a person who can contact if needed.
5. **Medical information**: Describe any medical conditions that may affect your travel, such as allergies or medications.
6. **Contact method**: Specify how you can be contacted in case of an emergency.
7. **Travel agency information**: If you have booked your trip with a travel agency, provide their name and contact information.
8. **Supervisor information**: If you have a supervisor or representative from the travel agency, provide their name and contact information.
9. **Relationship to supervisor**: Describe your relationship to the supervisor.
10. **Supervisor's contact information**: Provide the supervisor's contact information.
11. **Emergency contact for supervisor**: Specify the supervisor's emergency contact information.
12. **Relationship to travel agency**: Describe your relationship to the travel agency.
13. **Travel agency notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your travel agency.
