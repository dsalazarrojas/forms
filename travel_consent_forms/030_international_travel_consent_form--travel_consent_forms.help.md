# International Travel Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The International Travel Consent Form is a mandatory form that ensures the parent or guardian of a traveler has given their consent for the trip. It is used to collect critical information about the traveler and their travel itinerary.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the traveler in the **traveler's name** field.
2. Choose the **relationship** between the traveler and their parent or guardian.
3. Enter the destination of the traveler's trip in the **travel destination** field.
4. Select the dates of travel for the trip in the **travel dates** field.
5. (Optional) If you have a travel document (e.g., visa, passport), you can attach a copy of it in the **travel document** field.
6. If you need to book a return flight, provide the flight details in the **return flight details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler's Name**: Enter the name of the traveler (the student) who is going on the trip.
* **Relationship**: Choose how the traveler is related to their parent or guardian (e.g., mother, father, both, etc.).
* **Travel Destination**: Enter the city, country, or region of travel (e.g., New York, Tokyo, Paris).
* **Travel Dates**: Select the start and end dates of the trip.
* **Travel Document**: This field is optional and can be used to attach a copy of the travel document (e.g., visa, passport).
* **Return Flight Details**: This field is optional and can be used if you need to book a return flight.
