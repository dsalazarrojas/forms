# Field Trip Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a waiver form designed for participants and activity leaders to acknowledge and document their involvement in a field trip. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Please fill out your personal information on Page 1: Participant Information.
2. On Page 2, provide details about the activity you will be participating in.
3. Sign the form with your date in the Participant Signature field.
4. If applicable, indicate your medical conditions in the Medical Conditions field.
5. Read and agree to the Participant Agreement and Activity Leader Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name, date of birth, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Activity Details** (`activity_details`, text, required/optional): Provide a brief description of the activity you will be participating in, including any specific details about what you will be doing.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, date, required/optional): Sign the form with your date to confirm you have read and understood the waiver.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required/true): If you have any medical conditions that might affect your participation, please select one of the available options:
	+ Healthy
	+ Has an EpiPen
	+ Has a Wheelchair
* **Medical Emergency Procedure** (`medical_emergency_procedure`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions, please describe the emergency procedure to be followed in case of an accident.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms of the participant agreement.
* **Activity Leader Agreement** (`activity_leader_agreement`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms of the activity leader agreement.
* **Activity Leader Agreement Acknowledgement** (`activity_leader_agreement_acknowledgement`, text, required/optional): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the activity leader agreement.
* **Participant Information Acknowledgement** (`participant_information_acknowledgement`, text, required/optional): Acknowledge that you have read and understood the participant information.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any medical conditions, please indicate them in the Medical Conditions field.
- Read and understand the terms of the participant and activity leader agreements before signing the form.
