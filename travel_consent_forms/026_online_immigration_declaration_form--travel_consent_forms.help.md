# Online Immigration Declaration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Online Immigration Declaration Form is designed to collect personal and travel-related information from applicants for immigration purposes. It's a critical part of the process, and accuracy is essential to ensure that applications are processed efficiently and correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have all the necessary documents and information before starting the form.
2. Fill in your first name, last name, and birth date accurately.
3. Select your nationality correctly.
4. Enter your passport number and expiration date.
5. Indicate your country of residence.
6. Select the reason for your travel.
7. Enter your travel dates and duration.
8. Choose your mode of transportation.
9. Confirm your travel document status.
10. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your passport or identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or identification.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): If you are unsure of your birth date, please enter the best available estimate. This field is optional but recommended for accuracy.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_one, required): Select your nationality as it appears on your passport or identification.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, text, required): Enter your passport number accurately, as it appears on your passport.
* **Passport Expiration Date** (`passport_expiration_date`, date, required): Enter the expiration date of your passport accurately.
* **Country of Residence** (`country_of_residence`, select_one, required): Select the country where you currently reside.
* **Reason for Travel** (`reason_for_travel`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable reasons for your travel.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_dates`, date, required): Enter your intended travel dates accurately.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, text, required): Enter the length of your trip in days or hours.
* **Mode of Transportation** (`mode_of_transportation`, select_multiple, required): Choose all applicable modes of transportation for your travel.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, select_one, required): Confirm the status of your travel document.
