# Parental Travel Consent For Away Games Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain travel-related information and consent from parents/guardians of minors who are traveling away from home.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the necessary information in each field below, as applicable.
2.  Make sure to check and confirm the accuracy of your answers.
3.  If any of the required fields are left empty or not filled in correctly, you may not be allowed to travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parental Travel Consent**: Please provide your consent or refusal to let your child participate in the travel.
*   **Emergency Contact**: Please select 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact person that we should contact in case of an emergency. If 'Yes', please provide the contact person's name and phone number.
*   **Travel Details**: Please enter the date of the travel.
*   **Travel Date**: Please enter the date of the travel.
*   **Return Time**: Please enter the expected return time of your child.
*   **Travel Mode**: Please select the mode of travel your child will use (e.g., Car, Bus, Train).
*   **Destination**: Please enter the destination address of the travel.
*   **Accommodation**: Please select whether your child will stay in accommodation or not (e.g., 'Yes' if they will be staying in a hotel, 'No' if they will not).
*   **Contact Person**: Please enter your contact information so we can reach out to you if necessary.
*   **Contact Person Phone**: Please enter your phone number.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check the form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness of information.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.
*   This form will be kept confidential and used solely for travel-related purposes.
