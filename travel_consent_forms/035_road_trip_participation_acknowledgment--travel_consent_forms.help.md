## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (text, required: false): This field is for you to provide any necessary details about yourself as a participant.
* **emergency_contacts** (text, required: false): List any emergency contacts who can be reached in case of an emergency, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* **health_notes** (text, required: false): Provide any relevant health information that the trip organizers should know about you before the trip.
* **consent** (text, required: false): Sign this form to acknowledge your understanding and participation in the trip. This includes agreeing to any rules, risks, and responsibilities associated with the trip.
* **participant_goals** (text, required: false): Specify your goals or objectives for participating in this trip.
* **trip_details** (text, required: false): Enter the details about the trip, including departure and return dates, and distance.
* **departure_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to depart for the trip.
* **return_date** (date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to return from the trip.
* **trip_duration** (number, required: false): Enter the expected duration of the trip.
* **trip_distance** (number, required: false): Enter the approximate distance of the trip.
* **participant_phone** (text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes, if necessary.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the trip organizers for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you've provided all necessary information.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask a trip organizer for clarification.
