# International Travel Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The International Travel Waiver Form is used to document travel details for official trips and obtain an acknowledgement of risks associated with international travel.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Carefully review the form and ensure you understand the purpose and the required fields.
2. Complete the form in its entirety, as it is considered a mandatory requirement for official travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 2. travel_acknowledge
* travel_acknowledge (Select One)
	+ **travel_acknowledge**: Choose "Acknowledge" to confirm you understand the associated risks of international travel.
* travel_acknowledge options: "Acknowledge", "Acknowledge with conditions", and "Acknowledge with medical conditions"

### 4. risk_acknowledge
* risk_acknowledge (Select One)
	+ **risk_acknowledge**: Choose "Acknowledge" to confirm you are aware of the potential risks associated with travel to the destination.

### 6. emergency_contact
* emergency_contact (Text)
	+ **emergency_contact**: Enter the contact information of a person or organization that will be notified in case of an emergency during your travel.

### 10. travel_agency
* travel_agency (Select One)
	+ **travel_agency**: Choose "True" if the travel agency will provide travel insurance.

### 17. travel_currency
* travel_currency (Select One)
	+ **travel_currency**: Select your travel currency (USD, EUR, or JPY).

### 19. travel_insurance_provider
* travel_insurance_provider (Select One)
	+ **travel_insurance_provider**: Choose "True" if you have purchased travel insurance for your trip.
