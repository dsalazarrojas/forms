# Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Authorization Form is a document used to gather information about an employee's or user's travel plans, which will be reviewed and approved by a manager or administrator.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the requested information.
2. Select the correct travel type (business or personal).
3. Enter the travel dates (start and end dates) in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., 2022-07-25).
4. Enter the departure and return times in the format `HH:MM AM/PM` (e.g., 08:00 AM).
5. Make sure to fill out all fields that apply to your travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **travel_authorization_form** (Text, Optional): A general travel authorization form that requires a brief description or explanation of the travel purpose.
* **travel_type** (Select-one, Optional): Choose whether the travel is for business or personal purposes.
* **travel_dates** (Date, Optional): Enter the dates of your travel (start and end dates) in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **departure_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the departure date of your travel in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **departure_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the departure time of your travel in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **return_date** (Date, Optional): Enter the return date of your travel in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **return_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the return time of your travel in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Double-check the format of the date and time fields.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your manager or the person responsible for travel approval for guidance.
- This form is intended for internal use within an organization, so do not share it with external parties.
