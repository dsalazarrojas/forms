# innovation_center_field_trip_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information for students to participate in a field trip. It ensures that both the student and their guardian provide consent and necessary details for a smooth and secure experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your student ID if it's been assigned to you.
- Provide your name as it appears on official documents.
- Enter a valid contact number where you can be reached during the trip.
- If asked for your date of birth, please fill it in according to your official documents.
- Select your current grade level from the options provided.
- Indicate your relationship with your guardian (Mother, Father, Other, or Mother and Father).
- If your guardian is not listed, choose 'Other' and explain briefly.
- Fill in the name of your school.
- Please sign both the student consent and guardian consent sections to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required/optional): A unique ID assigned to you. If not given, leave it blank.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): The name of your parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): A phone number where you can be reached during the trip.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required): Your date of birth as per your official documents.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required): Your current grade level in school.
* **Guardian Relationship** (`guardian_relationship`, select_one, required): Your relationship with your guardian.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Your school name (if you're a student from this school).
* **Student Consent** (`student_consent`, note, required): Please sign to confirm your agreement for the trip.
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, note, required): Please sign to give your consent for your child's participation.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with your school or guardian.
- Ensure both consents are signed by their respective parties.
- Review the details and submit the form once complete.
