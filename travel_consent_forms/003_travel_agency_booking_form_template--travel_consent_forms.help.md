# travel_agency_booking_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel agency booking form template is designed to collect information from clients about their travel preferences, agency details, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and your name as a client.
2. Enter your phone number and email address so that the travel agency can contact you.
3. Select the travel dates for your trip.
4. Choose the type of travel you're interested in (e.g., yes/no options for specific travel types).
5. Indicate how many travelers are in your group.
6. Choose the currency you'd like to use for your trip.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments about your trip.
8. Fill in the agency information, including their name, phone number, email address, address, website, agency license, and license ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Title** (`travel_agency_form_title`): Enter a brief description of your trip, such as a vacation package or tour name.
* **Client Name** (`travel_agency_form_name`): Enter your full name as a client.
* **Client Phone** (`travel_agency_form_telephone`): Enter your phone number so that the travel agency can contact you.
* **Client Email** (`travel_agency_form_email`): Enter your email address so that the travel agency can reach you.
* **Travel Dates** (`travel_agency_form_date`): Choose the dates for your trip using the date picker.
* **Travel Type** (`travel_agency_form_travel_type`): Select the type of travel you're interested in (e.g., yes/no options for specific travel types).
* **Travel Agency Form Travel Type Options** (`travel_agency_form_travel_type_options`): This field is for internal use by the travel agency; you can ignore it.
* **Travelers** (`travel_agency_form_travelers`): Indicate how many travelers are in your group (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Travel Agency Form Travelers Options** (`travel_agency_form_travelers_options`): This field is for internal use by the travel agency; you can ignore it.
* **Currency** (`travel_agency_form_currency`): Choose the currency you'd like to use for your trip (e.g., US dollars, euros, etc.).
* **Travel Agency Form Currency Options** (`travel_agency_form_currency_options`): This field is for internal use by the travel agency; you can ignore it.
* **Notes** (`travel_agency_form_notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about your trip.
* **Agency Name** (`travel_agency_form_agency_name`): Enter the name of the travel agency you're booking with.
* **Agency Phone** (`travel_agency_form_agency_phone`): Enter the phone number of the travel agency.
* **Agency Email** (`travel_agency_form_agency_email`): Enter the email address of the travel agency.
* **Agency Address** (`travel_agency_form_agency_address`): Enter the address of the travel agency.
* **Agency Website** (`travel_agency_form_agency_website`): Enter the website of the travel agency.
* **Agency License** (`travel_agency_form_agency_license`): Enter the agency's license number (e.g., IATA, ARC, etc.).
* **Agency License ID** (`travel_agency_form_agency_license_id`): Enter the agency's license ID (if applicable).
* **Agency License Expiration Date** (`travel_agency_form_agency_license_expire_date`): Enter the expiration date of the agency's license (if applicable).
* **Terms and Conditions** (`travel_agency_form_terms_conditions`): Indicate whether you've read and accepted the terms and conditions of the travel agency.
* **Travel Agency Form Terms Conditions Options** (`travel_agency_form_terms_conditions_options`): This field is for internal use by the travel agency; you can ignore it.
* **Payment Plan** (`travel_agency_form_payment_plan`): Choose the payment plan for your trip (e.g., yes/no options for specific payment plans).
* **Travel Agency Form Payment Plan Options** (`travel_agency_form_payment_plan_options`): This field is for internal use by the travel agency; you can ignore it.

## Tips

* Be sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the travel agency has the necessary information to process your booking.
* Double-check the agency information for accuracy to ensure that the travel agency can contact you properly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the travel agency.
