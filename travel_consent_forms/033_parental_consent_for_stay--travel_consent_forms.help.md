# Parental Consent For Stay - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Parental Consent For Stay form. The form is a simple, three-page form designed to gather essential information from parents or guardians when their child stays with a caregiver or family member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please fill in the form on page 1, "Parent Information".**
2. **On page 2, "Authorization", select who will be authorizing the child's stay** (Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other)
3. **On page 3, "Consent", choose whether the parent/guardian consents to the child's stay or not**

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Information (Page 1)
* **Parent Information (text)**: This field is used to capture your name and contact information as a parent or guardian. Please fill in your full name and any relevant contact details, such as a phone number or email address.

### Authorization (Page 2)
* **Authorization (select one)**: This field is used to specify who will be authorizing the child's stay. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Guardian
	+ Other

### Consent (Page 3)
* **Consent (select one)**: This field is used to indicate whether you, as the parent or guardian, consent to the child's stay or not. Please select "True" if you consent or "False" if you do not.
