# Online Immigration Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Immigration Declaration Form is used to collect information about an individual's travel details for internal processing.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before starting the form.
- Fill out each field accurately and completely.
- Save your progress and come back to the form if you need to make changes.
- Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to save your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Team** (`travel_team`, text, optional): Enter the team you are traveling with, if any.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your travel.
* **Travel Reason** (`travel_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your travel.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of your arrival.
* **Travel Duration** (`travel_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your travel.
* **Travel Document** (`travel_document`, text, optional): Enter the travel document details (e.g., passport number).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person you are traveling with or the person responsible for your travel.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Travel Location** (`travel_location`, text, optional): Enter the destination of your travel.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you need to make changes, save your progress and come back to the form.
* Contact your internal team or supervisor if you need assistance or have questions.
