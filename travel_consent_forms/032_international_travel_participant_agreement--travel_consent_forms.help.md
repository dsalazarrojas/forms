# International Travel Participant Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Travel Participant Agreement form is a document that requires participants to provide their personal details, medical information, and travel plans. This form is used to ensure that participants are aware of and agree to certain conditions and rules before embarking on their travels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Participant Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Provide your current Address.
5. Enter the Emergency Contact Name.
6. Select the Relationship with the emergency contact.
7. Enter the Emergency Contact Phone number.
8. List any Medical Conditions that may affect your travel.
9. Select the Travel Dates.
10. Choose your Travel Destination.
11. Enter the Travel Reason.
12. Select your agreement to participate in the trip.
13. Select your consent to participate.
14. Select whether you have school permission or not.
15. Select the travel supervisors for the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents. This information will be used to identify you as a participant.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about any travel-related updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where we can send important documents or contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of a person who we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship you have with the emergency contact (e.g., Parent, Child, Sponsor).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions that may affect your travel, such as chronic illnesses or allergies.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of your travel.
* **Travel Destination**: Select the destination city of your travel (e.g., Paris, London, Rome, Barcelona, New York).
* **Travel Reason**: Enter a brief description of why you are traveling (e.g., business, pleasure, education).
* **Participant Agreement**: Select "I agree" if you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the trip.
* **Participant Consent**: Select "I consent" if you agree to participate in the trip.
* **School Permission**: Select "Yes" if you have permission from your school to participate in the trip.
* **Travel Supervisors**: Select the person(s) who will be supervising you during the trip (e.g., Teacher, School Administrator, Group Leader, Other).
* **Participant Consent 2** and **School Permission 2** and **Participant Consent 3**: These fields are optional and used to gather additional information for specific travel-related purposes.

Note: This form is designed to ensure your safety and well-being during travel. Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to ensure a successful and safe trip.
