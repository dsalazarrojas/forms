# travel_advisory_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The travel advisory communication form is designed to gather information from travelers to provide them with important safety updates and advisories for their destination. This form helps to ensure that travelers are aware of any risks or updates that may affect their journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the "Advisory" page and enter your travel dates and destination in the text field.
*   If you have any safety concerns or protocols, please click on the "Safety Protocols" page and describe them.
*   On the "Travel Updates" page, mention any important updates or changes that have occurred at your destination.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Advisory** (optional)
    This is the first page of the form where you will input your travel dates and destination.
*   **Safety Protocols** (optional)
    This field is where you will mention any safety concerns or protocols you have for your trip.
*   **Travel Updates** (optional)
    Here, you will provide any important updates or changes that have occurred at your destination.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all relevant fields to ensure that you receive accurate and timely information about your destination.
*   If you have any further questions or need clarification on any of the fields, do not hesitate to reach out.
*   Please be as descriptive as possible when filling out the safety protocols and travel updates fields to provide the most accurate information. 
*   This form will only be used for communication of information and travel advisories. Do not provide any financial information.
