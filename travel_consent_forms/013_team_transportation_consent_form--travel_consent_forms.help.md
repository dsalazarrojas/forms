# Team Transportation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Transportation Consent Form is a document that team members must complete to provide consent and details for team transportation. It is essential for team members to understand their travel plans, ensure a smooth travel experience, and comply with organizational requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Parent Information**: Fill in the "Parent Information" section with your relevant details.
2. **Choose a Trip Type**: Select the type of trip you will be taking from the options provided.
3. **Provide Travel Dates**: Enter the dates of your trip in the required format (e.g., DD/MM/YYYY).
4. **Add Travel Details**: Enter any additional details about your travel in the "Travel Details" section.
5. **Provide Return Information**: Fill in the "Return Information" section with your return details.
6. **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the emergency contact person's name and number.
7. **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about your trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Information (id: 1)
* **Label:** Parent Information
* **Type:** Text input field
* **Purpose:** Enter your parent/guardian's name and contact information if you are under 18.

### Type of Trip (id: 2)
* **Label:** Type of Trip
* **Type:** Select one option (drop-down menu)
* **Purpose:** Indicate the type of trip you will be taking (e.g., business, leisure, etc.).

### Travel Dates (id: 3)
* **Label:** Travel Dates
* **Type:** Date input field
* **Purpose:** Enter the dates of your trip in the DD/MM/YYYY format.

### Travel Details (id: 4)
* **Label:** Travel Details
* **Type:** Text input field
* **Purpose:** Enter any additional details about your travel, such as flight numbers or hotel information.

### Return Information (id: 5)
* **Label:** Return Information
* **Type:** Text input field
* **Purpose:** Enter your return details, including the date and time of your return.

### Emergency Contact (id: 6)
* **Label:** Emergency Contact
* **Type:** Text input field
* **Purpose:** Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact person.

### Additional Information (id: 7)
* **Label:** Additional Information
* **Type:** Text input field
* **Purpose:** Enter any additional information about your trip that may be relevant to your safety and travel experience.
