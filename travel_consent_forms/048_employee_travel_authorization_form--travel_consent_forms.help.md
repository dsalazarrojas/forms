# Employee Travel Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Employee Travel Authorization Form is a document used to gather necessary information for employee travel. It is a crucial tool for ensuring that all relevant details are captured, and approval is obtained from the employee's manager or supervisor before the travel takes place.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your travel details, including travel dates, destination, purpose, and travel duration.
2. Select the type of travel (Conference, Seminar, Auditing, Inspection, or Other) that applies to your travel.
3. Choose the approval level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3) required for your travel type.
4. Enter the estimated travel cost and select the travel currency.
5. Enter any relevant travel agency or company information.
6. Get approval from your manager or supervisor by selecting "Approved" or "Pending" and adding a note (if required).
7. Save the form to ensure that your travel authorization is recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel to Conference/Seminar** (1): Enter the name or code of the conference or seminar you will be attending.
* **Travel Dates** (2): Enter the start and end dates of your travel.
* **Travel Duration** (3): Enter the number of days you will be away from the office.
* **Destination** (4): Enter the city, state, or country you will be visiting.
* **Purpose** (5): Enter a brief description of the reason for your travel.
* **Return Date** (6): Enter the date you will return to the office.
* **Return Time** (7): Enter the estimated time of return (if applicable).
* **Employee Name** (8): Enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
* **Manager Name** (9): Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Approval Status** (10): Select "Approved" to indicate that your travel has been approved, "Denied" to reject your travel request, or "Pending" to indicate that your travel is waiting for approval.
* **Travel Type** (11): Select the type of travel (Conference, Seminar, Auditing, Inspection, or Other) that applies to your trip.
* **Travel Frequency** (12): Enter the number of times you have traveled to this destination in the past year.
* **Approval Date** (13): Enter the date your travel was approved (if applicable).
* **Employee Department** (14): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Company** (15): Enter the name of your company.
* **Travel Location** (16): Enter the city or location of your travel.
* **Travel Reason** (17): Enter a brief description of the reason for your travel.
* **Employee ID** (18): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Manager ID** (19): Enter the ID number of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Supervisor ID** (20): Enter the ID number of your supervisor (if applicable).
* **Approval Level** (21): Select the approval level required for your travel type.
* **Travel Cost** (22): Enter the estimated cost of your travel.
* **Travel Currency** (23): Select the currency of your travel expenses (USD, EUR, CNY, or Other).
* **Approval Note** (24): Enter any additional comments or notes from your manager or supervisor.
* **Travel Agency** (25): Enter the name of the travel agency or company you will be using.
