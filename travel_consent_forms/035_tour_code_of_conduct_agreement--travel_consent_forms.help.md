# Tour Code Of Conduct Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is required to be completed by participants to provide their consent and essential information for a smooth and safe tour experience. It is essential to read and understand the information provided in this form before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the Participant Consent field with your agreement to participate in the tour.
2. Provide emergency contact information, including the name and contact number of your emergency contact person.
3. Choose one of the available options for emergency contact.
4. Enter your email address in the Participant Email field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Participant Phone field.
6. Fill in your Participant Name and Participant Address fields.
7. Enter the Tour Code Conduct details in the Tour Code Conduct field.
8. Enter the Tour Operator in the Tour Operator field.
9. Specify the date of travel in the Travel Date field.
10. Choose the travel time in the Travel Time field (if applicable).
11. Enter the duration of travel in the Travel Duration field.
12. Enter the location of travel in the Travel Place field (if applicable).
13. Provide any additional travel details in the Travel Details field.
14. Select one of the available emergency contacts for Participant Emergency Contact.
15. Choose whether you agree or disagree with the Participant Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Consent (Participant Consent)** (`text`, `required`): 
Please enter your consent to participate in the tour by filling in this field.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`text`, `required`): 
Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Signature (Signature)** (`select_one`, `required`): 
Select one of the available options for your signature.
* **Participant Email (Participant Email)** (`email`, `required`): 
Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)** (`text`, `required`): 
Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`text`, `required`): 
Fill in your name.
* **Participant Address (Participant Address)** (`text`, `required`: 
Fill in your address.
* **Tour Code Conduct (Tour Code Conduct)** (`text`, `required`): 
Enter the code of conduct details for the tour.
* **Tour Operator (Tour Operator)** (`text`, `required`): 
Enter the name of the tour operator.
* **Travel Date (Travel Date)** (`date`, `required`): 
Enter the date of travel.
* **Travel Time (Travel Time)** (`time`, `required`: 
Choose the travel time.
* **Travel Duration (Travel Duration)** (`number`, `required`): 
Enter the duration of travel.
* **Travel Place (Travel Place)** (`text`, `required`: 
Enter the location of travel.
* **Travel Details (Travel Details)** (`text`, `required`): 
Provide any additional travel details.
* **Participant Emergency Contact (Participant Emergency Contact)** (`select_multiple`, `required`): 
Select one of the emergency contacts.
* **Participant Agreement (Participant Agreement)** (`select_one`, `required`): 
Choose whether you agree or disagree with the participant agreement.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
- Review the participant agreement carefully before making a choice.
