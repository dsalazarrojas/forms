# Field Trip Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Trip Consent Form is a document that aims to gather important information about the student and their parent/guardian in preparation for a field trip. This information includes contact details, medical information, and emergency response plans.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the student information form (fields 1-4) with the student's name, contact details, and medical information.
2.  Select the relationship between the student and emergency contact person (field 3).
3.  Provide the student's medical information in fields 6-10.
4.  Choose the emergency response plan for the student (field 17).
5.  Fill out the student's and parent's absence dates if applicable (fields 18-20).
6.  Provide emergency contact person and phone number for the student (fields 21-22).
7.  Enter the contact email for the student (field 23).
8.  Sign the student and parent agreement forms (fields 24-25).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Information**: Enter the student's name, date of birth, and grade level. This information will help us identify the student and their current academic status. (Field 1)
*   **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, phone number, and email address of the student's emergency contact person. This information is crucial in case of an emergency during the trip. (Field 2)
*   **Relationship to the Student**: Select the relationship between the student and emergency contact person, e.g. Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other. (Field 3)
*   **Contact Details**: Enter the contact person's address and any other relevant contact information. (Field 4)
*   **Student Information**: Enter any additional medical or special needs information about the student. (Field 5)
*   **Medical Information**: Enter any medical conditions or allergies the student may have, and any medication the student is currently taking. (Fields 6-10)
*   **Special Needs**: Select the student's special needs status, if applicable. (Field 7)
*   **Allergies**: Enter any allergies the student may have, and any medical conditions that may affect them. (Field 8)
*   **Medication**: Enter any medication the student is currently taking, and any medical conditions that may affect them. (Field 9)
*   **Medical Conditions**: Enter any medical conditions the student may have, and any medication they may be taking. (Field 10)
*   **Medical Procedure**: Enter any medical procedures or treatments the student may be undergoing. (Field 11)
*   **Medical Supervision**: Enter the name of the medical professional who will supervise the student's medical needs. (Field 12)
*   **Emergency Medical Response**: Enter the student's emergency medical response plan, including any specific instructions or procedures. (Field 13)
*   **Medical Release**: This field is for any medical releases or consent forms related to the student's medical care. (Field 14)
*   **Student Agreement**: Choose whether the student understands and agrees with the field trip terms and conditions. (Field 15)
*   **Parent Agreement**: Choose whether the parent understands and agrees with the field trip terms and conditions. (Field 16)
*   **Emergency Response Plan**: Select the student's emergency response plan status, e.g., True or False. (Field 17)
*   **Student Absence**: Enter the student's date of absence, if applicable. (Field 18)
*   **Emergency Absence**: Enter the date of the student's emergency absence, if applicable. (Field 19)
*   **Parent Absence**: Enter the parent's date of absence, if applicable. (Field 20)
*   **Emergency Contact Person**: Enter the name and contact details of the emergency contact person. (Field 21)
*   **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the emergency contact person's phone number. (Field 22)
*   **Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact person's email address. (Field 23)
*   **Student Signature**: Sign the student agreement form. (Field 24)
*   **Parent Signature**: Sign the parent agreement form. (Field 25)
