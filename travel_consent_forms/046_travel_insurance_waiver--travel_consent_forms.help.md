# Travel Insurance Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to collect travel-related information and obtain a waiver for travel insurance. It includes fields for travel insurance type, travel date, travel location, travel reason, travel insurance provider, travel insurance ID, signature date, and signature.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Step 1: Select Travel Insurance Type**: Choose the type of travel insurance you have: "Travel Insurance - Domestic", "Travel Insurance - International", or "Travel Insurance - None".
2.  **Step 2: Enter Travel Date**: Enter the date of your travel.
3.  **Step 3: Enter Travel Location**: Enter the destination of your travel.
4.  **Step 4: Select Reason for Travel**: Choose the reason for your travel, which can be "Medical", "Business", or "Other".
5.  **Step 5: Select Travel Insurance Provider**: Choose your travel insurance provider, either "Provider A" or "Provider B".
6.  **Step 6: Enter Travel Insurance ID**: Enter your travel insurance ID number.
7.  **Step 7: Sign and Date**: Sign and date the form to complete it.
