# Fire Safety Daily Work Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for recording and reporting on daily fire safety work conducted by fire safety personnel.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by selecting the start time of the fire safety inspection or work.
2. If any fire hazards were encountered during the inspection, list them in the "Fire Hazards Encountered" field.
3. For each fire hazard, select the relevant prevention method used to mitigate or address it. This could be a firewatcher on site, regular fire watch, or both.
4. List any supervisory personnel who were present during the inspection in the "Fire Supervision" field.
5. Provide a brief description of any fire prevention procedures used in the facility.
6. Describe any fire supervision procedures that were followed during the inspection.
7. Count and record the number of fire extinguishers and fire drills completed during the inspection.
8. List any emergency contacts that were used or made during the inspection.
9. Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection in the "Notes" field.
10. If applicable, select the status of the fire inspection completion.
11. Identify any fire hazards that were found during the inspection.
12. Finally, add any comments or observations about the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Start Time** (id: 1, time): Record the start time of the fire safety inspection or work.
* **Fire Hazards Encountered** (id: 2, text): If any fire hazards were encountered, list them here.
* **Fire Hazard Prevention** (id: 3, select_one): Select the relevant prevention method used to mitigate or address each fire hazard.
* **Fire Supervision** (id: 4, select_multiple): List the supervisory personnel who were present during the inspection.
* **Fire Prevention Procedures** (id: 5, text): Provide a brief description of any fire prevention procedures used in the facility.
* **Fire Supervision Procedures** (id: 6, text): Describe any fire supervision procedures that were followed during the inspection.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (id: 7, number): Count and record the number of fire extinguishers found during the inspection.
* **Fire Drills** (id: 8, number): Count and record the number of fire drills completed during the inspection.
* **Emergency Contacts** (id: 9, text): List any emergency contacts that were used or made during the inspection.
* **Notes** (id: 10, note): Add any additional notes or comments about the inspection.
* **Fire Safety Meeting** (id: 11, date): Record the date of the fire safety meeting (if applicable).
* **Fire Inspection Completed** (id: 12, select_one): Select the status of the fire inspection completion.
* **Fire Hazards Identified** (id: 13, select_multiple): Identify any fire hazards that were found during the inspection.
* **Comments** (id: 14, text): Add any comments or observations about the inspection.
