# Fire Alarm Testing Entry Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire alarm testing entry consent form is used to collect information from users who want to participate in fire alarm testing. This form ensures that we gather necessary details from participants before scheduling the testing and conducting the test itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply click on the "Submit" button after filling in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (user_info, text, not required): Enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Tenant Information** (tenant_info, text, not required): Provide information about the tenant or location you are representing. 
* **Testing Information** (testing_info, text, required): Enter details about the testing you wish to conduct.
* **Contact Information** (contact_info, text, not required): Enter contact details such as phone number, email or any other contact method.
* **Testing Time** (testing_time, number, required): Select the time slot you are available for the testing.
* **Testing Date** (testing_date, date, required): Select the date on which you wish to conduct the testing.
* **Testing Time Zone** (testing_time_zone, select_one, required): Choose the time of day you prefer to conduct the testing (e.g., Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Comments** (comments, text, not required): Leave any additional comments or remarks about your participation.
