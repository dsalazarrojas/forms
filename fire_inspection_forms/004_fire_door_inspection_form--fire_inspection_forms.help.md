# fire_door_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire door inspection form is designed to collect information on fire door inspections, which is crucial for fire safety and compliance. This document helps you document and track fire alarm systems, inspection frequencies, and related activities.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the fire door inspection form, follow these steps:

1. **Fire Door Inspection**: This text field is for noting any notable issues or observations during the fire door inspection.
2. **Fire Exit Doors**: Choose the fire exit doors inspected (e.g., fire door 1 or fire door 2).
3. **Inspection Date**: Select the date of the inspection.
4. **Fire Alarm System**: Choose any fire alarm systems inspected (e.g., fire alarm system 1 or fire alarm system 2).
5. **Fire Extinguishers**: Choose any fire extinguishers inspected (e.g., fire extinguisher 1 or fire extinguisher 2).
6. **Fire Exit Doors**: This text field is for noting any observations or issues with fire exit doors.
7. **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
8. **Fire Alarm Testing**: Choose any fire alarm tests performed (e.g., fire alarm testing 1 or fire alarm testing 2).
9. **Inspection Frequency**: Choose the recommended inspection frequency (e.g., quarterly or annually).
10. **Inspection Time**: If applicable, enter the time spent on the inspection.
11. **Fire Alarm Status**: Select the status of the fire alarm system (e.g., working or not working).
12. **Fire Alarm Test Date**: Select the date of the last fire alarm test.
13. **Fire Alarm Inspector**: Enter the name of the person conducting the fire alarm inspection.
14. **Fire Alarm Test Status**: Choose the status of the fire alarm test (e.g., passed, failed, or pending).
15. **Fire Alarm Inspection Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
16. **Fire Alarm Inspection Frequency**: Choose how often the fire alarm system should be inspected (e.g., daily, weekly, or monthly).
17. **Fire Alarm System Inspector**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the fire alarm system.
18. **Fire Alarm System Tested**: Choose any fire alarm systems that have been tested (e.g., fire alarm system tested 1 or fire alarm system tested 2).
19. **Fire Alarm System Inspection Frequency**: Choose how often the fire alarm system should be inspected (e.g., quarterly or semi-annually).
20. **Fire Alarm System Inspection Status**: Choose the status of the fire alarm system inspection (e.g., active or inactive).
21. **Fire Alarm Certificate Status**: Choose whether a fire alarm certificate is active or inactive.
22. **Fire Alarm Certificate Date**: Select the date of the fire alarm certificate.
23. **Fire Alarm Certificate Expiration Date**: Select the expiration date of the fire alarm certificate.
24. **Fire Alarm Certificate Status**: Choose the status of the fire alarm certificate (e.g., active or inactive).
