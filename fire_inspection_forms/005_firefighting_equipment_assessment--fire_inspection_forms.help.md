# Firefighting Equipment Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Firefighting Equipment Assessment form is designed to evaluate the status of firefighting equipment in a given location. This form helps inspectors assess the presence and condition of essential firefighting equipment such as fire extinguishers, fire hoses, pumps, sprinkler systems, and other critical systems. The primary goal is to identify potential risks and ensure that these essential systems are in working order.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form instructions carefully.
2. Evaluate each field based on the provided information.
3. Fill in the relevant fields as you inspect the equipment.
4. Submit the form after completing all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Firefighting Equipment Assessment Title** (`Firefighting Equipment Assessment`, text): This field is the title of the form, which is self-explanatory.
* **Inspector Notes** (`Inspector Notes`, text): Use this field to record any observations, comments, or concerns about the equipment or its location.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`Fire Extinguishers`, select_multiple): Check "Yes" if fire extinguishers are present in the location, and "No" if they are not. If you're unsure, check "No".
* **Fire Hose** (`Fire Hose`, select_one): Check "Yes" if a fire hose is present in the location, and "No" if it is not. If you're unsure, check "No". Note that this field is for a single hose, if there are multiple, check multiple options under "Fire Extinguishers".
* **Fire Sprinkler Systems** (`Fire Sprinkler Systems`, select_one): Check "Yes" if fire sprinkler systems are present in the location, and "No" if they are not. If you're unsure, check "No".
* **Fire Sprinkler System 2** (`Fire Sprinkler System 2`, select_one): This field is similar to "Fire Sprinkler Systems" and has the same options.
* **Fire Sprinklers** (`Fire Sprinklers`, select_multiple): Check "Yes" for each sprinkler system present in the location. If unsure, check "No".
* **Fire Sprinkler 2** (`Fire Sprinkler 2`, select_multiple): Similar to "Fire Sprinklers", check "Yes" for each sprinkler system present in the location. If unsure, check "No".
* **Firehose Length** (`Firehose Length`, text): If a fire hose is present, record its length in feet.
* **Hose Size** (`Hose Size`, text): If a fire hose is present, record its size.
* **Pump Size** (`Pump Size`, text): If a fire pump is present, record its size.
* **Fire Extinguisher Location** (`Fire Extinguisher Location`, select_one): Check "Yes" if the fire extinguisher is located in the correct place, and "No" if it's not.
* **Fire Extinguisher Type** (`Fire Extinguisher Type`, select_one): Check "Yes" if the fire extinguisher is of the correct type, and "No" if it's not.
* **Fire Extinguisher Capacity** (`Fire Extinguisher Capacity`, text): If a fire extinguisher is present, record its capacity.
* **Fire Extinguisher Charge Time** (`Fire Extinguisher Charge Time`, time): If a fire extinguisher is present, record its charge time.
* **Fire Extinguisher Charge Pressure** (`Fire Extinguisher Charge Pressure`, text): If a fire extinguisher is present, record its charge pressure.
* **Fire Extinguisher Flow Rate** (`Fire Extinguisher Flow Rate`, text): If a fire extinguisher is present, record its flow rate.
* **Firehose Thread** (`Firehose Thread`, text): If a fire hose is present, record its thread.
* **Firehose Material** (`Firehose Material`, text): If a fire hose is present, record its material.
* **Fire Pump Hose Size** (`Fire Pump Hose Size`, text): If a fire pump is present, record its hose size.
* **Fire Pump Thread** (`Fire Pump Thread`, text): If a fire pump is present, record its thread.
* **Fire Pump Size** (`Fire Pump Size`, text): If a fire pump is present, record its size.
* **Fire Pump Hose Thread** (`Fire Pump Hose Thread`, text): This field is similar to "Fire Pump Thread" and has the same options.
* **Pump Hose Size** (`Pump Hose Size`, text): This field is similar to "Fire Pump Hose Size" and has the same options.

## Tips

* Take your time to carefully evaluate each field before submitting the form.
* Be thorough in your assessment, but do not overfill the form with unnecessary information.
* If you're unsure about a specific field, check "No" and make a note to follow up with the relevant department or expert.
* Remember to submit the form after completing all required fields.
