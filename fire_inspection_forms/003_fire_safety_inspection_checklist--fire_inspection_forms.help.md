# Fire Safety Inspection Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Inspection Checklist is designed for businesses or organizations to conduct thorough fire safety inspections. This form helps identify potential risks and ensure compliance with fire regulations and codes.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Contact Information
Enter your name and contact information in the "Contact Person" field, as you will be the one responsible for ensuring fire safety and responding to emergencies.

### Step 2: Select Fire Stations
Choose the fire stations that are present on the premises. This will help ensure that fire response and suppression capabilities are adequate.

### Step 3: Select Fire Extinguishers and Hydrants
Select the types of fire extinguishers and hydrants present on the premises. This will help identify the types of fires you can extinguish and ensure water supply is adequate.

### Step 4: Verify Fire Alarm Systems
Check the fire alarm systems in place, including the types and locations of systems 1, 2, and 3. Ensure they are functioning correctly.

### Step 5: Check Emergency Exit Paths
Select the emergency exit paths available, including paths 1, 2, and 3. Ensure they are clear, accessible, and well-maintained.

### Step 6: Verify Emergency Exit Signs
Check the emergency exit signs, including signs 1, 2, and 3. Ensure they are easily visible and correctly located.

### Step 7: Check Emergency Contact Information
Select the emergency contact information, including contacts 1, 2, and 3. Ensure they are easily accessible and up-to-date.

### Step 8: Record Inspection Date and Time
Enter the date and time of the inspection in the respective fields.

### Step 9: Add Comments (Optional)
Use the "Comments" field to note any issues or concerns found during the inspection. This will help track progress and identify areas for improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person (Required)** (`contact_person`, `text`, `Required`): Enter your name and contact information, as you will be responsible for ensuring fire safety and responding to emergencies.
* **Date of Inspection (Required)** (`date_of_inspection`, `date`, `Required`): Enter the date of the inspection in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Fire Stations (Required)** (`fire_stations`, `select_multiple`, `Required`): Choose the fire stations present on the premises.
* **Fire Extinguishers (Required)** (`fire_extinguishers`, `select_one`, `Required`): Select the type of fire extinguishers present on the premises.
* **Fire Hydrants (Required)** (`fire_hydrants`, `select_one`, `Required`): Select the type of fire hydrants present on the premises.
* **Fire Alarm System (Required)** (`fire_alarm_system`, `select_multiple`, `Required`): Choose the fire alarm system types and locations on the premises.
* **Emergency Exit Paths (Required)** (`emergency_exit_paths`, `select_one`, `Required`): Select the emergency exit paths available on the premises.
* **Emergency Exit Signs (Required)** (`emergency_exit_signs`, `select_multiple`, `Required`): Choose the emergency exit signs present on the premises.
* **Emergency Contact Info (Required)** (`emergency_contact_info`, `select_multiple`, `Required`): Select the emergency contact information present on the premises.
* **Inspection Date (Required)** (`inspection_date`, `date`, `Required`): Enter the inspection date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Inspection Time (Required)** (`inspection_time`, `time`, `Required`): Enter the inspection time in 12-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Comments (Optional)** (`comments`, `text`, `Optional`): Use this field to note any issues or concerns found during the inspection.
* **Emergency Exit Paths 2 (Optional)** (`emergency_exit_paths_2`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): Choose the additional emergency exit paths available on the premises (if any).
* **Emergency Exit Signs 2 (Optional)** (`emergency_exit_signs_2`, `select_multiple`, `Optional`): Choose the additional emergency exit signs present on the premises (if any).
* **Comments 2 (Optional)** (`comments_2`, `text`, `Optional`): Use this field to note any additional issues or concerns found during the inspection.

## Tips

* Always complete the form in the presence of the fire safety officer or responsible contact person.
* Ensure you have the necessary knowledge and training to conduct fire safety inspections.
* Keep the form up-to-date and accessible for future reference.
* Use the form to identify potential risks and take corrective actions to improve fire safety.
* Conduct regular inspections to maintain fire safety compliance and prevent incidents.
