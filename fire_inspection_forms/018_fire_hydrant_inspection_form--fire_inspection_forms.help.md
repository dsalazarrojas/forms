# Fire Hydrant Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Hydrant Inspection Form is used to collect information about fire hydrants, their status, condition, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the fire hydrant's address on page 1.
2.  Record the status of the fire hydrant on page 3.
3.  Provide any notes about the inspection on page 4.
4.  Enter the contact email address on page 5.
5.  Enter the fire hydrant's phone number on page 6.
6.  Assess the condition of the fire hydrant on page 7.
7.  Determine the type of fire hydrant on page 8.
8.  Record the last inspection date on page 9.
9.  Set the next scheduled inspection date on page 10.
10. Enter the fire hydrant's height (optional) on page 11.
11. Enter the fire hydrant's diameter (optional) on page 12.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Address** (Firehydrant_address, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the fire hydrant being inspected.
- **Street Number** (Street_number, number, required/optional): Enter the street number of the fire hydrant.
- **Status** (Firehydrant_status, select_one, required): Choose the current status of the fire hydrant:
    - Active
    - Inactive
- **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about the fire hydrant inspection.
- **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter the contact email address for the fire hydrant owner or manager.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter the contact phone number for the fire hydrant owner or manager.
- **Condition** (Firehydrant_condition, select_multiple, required): Assess the condition of the fire hydrant:
    - Good
    - Fair
    - Poor
- **Type** (Firehydrant_type, select_one, required): Determine the type of fire hydrant:
    - Dry
    - Wet
    - Test
- **Last Inspection Date** (Last_inspection_date, date, required): Record the date of the last inspection.
- **Next Inspection Due Date** (Next_inspection_due_date, date, required): Set the next scheduled inspection date.
- **Height** (Firehydrant_height, number, required/optional): Enter the height of the fire hydrant (if applicable).
- **Diameter** (Firehydrant_diameter, number, required/optional): Enter the diameter of the fire hydrant (if applicable).
