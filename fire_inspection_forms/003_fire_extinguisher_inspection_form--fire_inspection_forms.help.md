# fire_extinguisher_inspection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fire_extinguisher_inspection_form is a form used to record the status of fire extinguishers in a facility or building.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and ID number.
2. Record the date the inspection was completed.
3. Indicate whether each fire extinguisher is operational or not.
4. Check whether each fire extinguisher is tagged or not.
5. Record the distance from each fire extinguisher to a fire hydrant.
6. Note the type of fire suppressant used (if any).
7. Note the size of the fire suppressant (if any).
8. Record the location of each fire extinguisher.
9. Record the last inspection date and the next inspection due date for each fire extinguisher.
10. Record the tag number of each fire extinguisher.
11. Record the model number of the fire suppressant (if any).
12. Enter any additional notes from the inspector.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Inspector Name** (`inspector_name`): Enter your name as the inspector.
* **Inspector ID** (`inspector_id`): Enter your ID number as the inspector.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`): Enter the date the inspection was completed.
* **Fire Extinguisher Model** (`fire_extinguisher_model`): Check if the fire extinguisher is operational or not.
* **Fire Extinguisher Tagged** (`fire_extinguisher_tagged`): Check whether the fire extinguisher is tagged or not.
* **Is Operational** (`is_operational`): Check whether the fire extinguisher is operational or not.
* **Fire Hydrant Distance** (`fire_hydrant_distance`): Enter the distance from the fire extinguisher to a fire hydrant in feet or meters.
* **Fire Suppressant Type** (`fire_suppressant_type`): Enter the type of fire suppressant used (if any).
* **Fire Suppressant Size** (`fire_suppressant_size`): Enter the size of the fire suppressant (if any).
* **Fire Extinguisher Location** (`fire_extinguisher_location`): Enter the location of the fire extinguisher.
* **Last Inspection Date** (`last_inspection_date`): Enter the last inspection date.
* **Next Inspection Due Date** (`next_inspection_due_date`): Enter the next inspection due date.
* **Fire Extinguisher Tag Number** (`fire_extinguisher_tag_number`): Enter the tag number of the fire extinguisher.
* **Fire Suppressant Model** (`fire_suppressant_model`): Enter the model number of the fire suppressant (if any).
* **Inspector Notes** (`inspector_notes`): Enter any additional notes from the inspector.
* **Fire Suppressant Location** (`fire_suppressant_location`): Enter the location of the fire suppressant.
* **Fire Suppressant Inspected** (`fire_suppressant_inspected`): Check whether the fire suppressant was inspected or not.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions and authorizations to access the fire extinguishers and areas being inspected.
* Be accurate and thorough in your reporting.
* If a fire extinguisher is not operational, do not use it.
* If a fire extinguisher is tagged, ensure that it is properly tagged and easily identifiable.
* Ensure that the fire hydrant is easily accessible.
* Review and update the form regularly to ensure that all fire extinguishers are inspected and maintained according to the schedule.
