# Fire Suppression Audit Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Suppression Audit Consent Form is used to gather information from the owner or manager of a property, or from a safety professional, to schedule and conduct a fire suppression audit. The form helps ensure that the fire suppression system is functioning correctly and meets regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Please complete this form with accurate and up-to-date information about the property and its fire suppression system.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner or Manager** (`owner_or_manager`, `text`, required/true): Please enter your name or the name of the property's owner or manager. This field is required.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/true): Please enter your phone number or the phone number of the property's owner or manager. This field is required.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/false): Please enter a valid email address. This field is not required, but it is recommended for communication purposes.
* **Schedule Audit** (`schedule_audit`, `date`, required/true): Please select a date and time for the fire suppression audit. This field is required.
* **Audit Date** (`audit_date`, `date`, required/true): Please select the date and year of the most recent fire suppression system audit. This field is required.
* **Safety Professional** (`safety_professional`, `text`, required/true): Please enter the name of the safety professional who will be conducting the audit. This field is required.
* **Authorization** (`authorization`, `text`, required/true): Please enter your authorization to conduct the audit. This field is required.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/false): Please enter any additional notes or comments about the fire suppression system. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly to avoid delays or re-inspections.
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate a smooth and efficient audit process.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the audit team for clarification.
