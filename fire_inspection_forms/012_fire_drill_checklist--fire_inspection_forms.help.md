# Fire Drill Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about fire safety procedures and testing of fire drills to ensure everyone's safety.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate options for each field based on the information you have.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure that fire drills are conducted according to fire safety regulations.
3. If there is any doubt or uncertainty, please contact the fire chief or the person in charge of fire safety.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Drill Procedures** (1, text, optional): Describe the fire drill procedures used during the fire drill. This field is for providing additional information about the procedures.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (2, select_one, optional): Select the emergency contact information, such as "Manager", "HR", "Fire Marshal", or other contacts.
* **Fire Drill Scheduling** (3, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the fire drill, if applicable.
* **Fire Drill Conducted by** (4, select_one, optional): Select who conducted the fire drill, e.g., "Fire Marshal", "Fire Chief", "Fire Captain".
* **Fire Drill Responders** (5, select_multiple, optional): Select who responded during the fire drill, e.g., "Fire Marshal", "Fire Chief", "Fire Captain".
* **Fire Drill Responder Training Status** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select the training status of the responders, e.g., "Trained", "Needs Refresher Training", "Untrained".
* **Fire Drill Procedures Reviewed** (7, select_one, optional): Select whether the fire drill procedures were reviewed.
* **Fire Drill Fire Extinguishers** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select the fire extinguishers used during the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Evacuation Plan** (9, note, optional): Describe the evacuation plan used during the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Annual Testing** (10, number, optional): Enter the number of times the fire drill has been conducted annually.
* **Fire Drill Reporting** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the fire drill was reported.
* **Fire Drill Reporting Frequency** (12, select_multiple, optional): Select the reporting frequency of the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Testing Method** (13, select_multiple, optional): Select the testing method used during the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Testing Frequency** (14, select_multiple, optional): Select the testing frequency of the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Annual Testing Frequency** (15, select_one, optional): Select the frequency of annual testing.
* **Fire Drill Test Result** (16, select_multiple, optional): Select the test result of the fire drill, e.g., "Passed", "Failed", "In Progress".
* **Fire Drill Testing Result** (17, select_multiple, optional): Select the testing result of the fire drill, e.g., "Pass", "Fail", "In Progress".
* **Fire Drill Testing Completed** (18, select_one, optional): Select whether the fire drill testing has been completed.
* **Fire Drill Testing Completed Status** (19, select_multiple, optional): Select the completed status of the fire drill testing.
* **Fire Drill Testers** (20, select_multiple, optional): Select who tested during the fire drill, e.g., "Fire Marshal", "Fire Chief", "Fire Captain".
* **Fire Drill Testing Status** (21, select_multiple, optional): Select the testing status of the fire drill.
* **Fire Drill Training Frequency** (22, select_multiple, optional): Select the training frequency of the fire drill, e.g., "Monthly", "Quarterly", "Annually".
