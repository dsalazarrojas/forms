# Fire Safety Equipment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fire Safety Equipment Checklist is a form used to assess the status of fire safety equipment in a facility or building. It ensures that all necessary equipment is properly installed, inspected, and maintained to prevent and respond to fires.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the list of fire safety equipment on each page and answer the questions accordingly.
2. For each question, select the correct response from the options provided.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
4. Submit the form to generate a report of the assessment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facilities** (`Facilities`, `Select Multiple`, **required**):
  The facilities listed on this page are crucial for fire safety. Indicate if the facilities on this list are present in the building.
* **Safety Equipment** (`Safety Equipment`, `Text`, **not required**):
  This field is a text input for additional safety equipment not listed in the other pages.
* **Fire Extinguishers** (`Fire Extinguishers`, `Select One`, **required**):
  Fire extinguishers are essential for fire suppression. Select one of the following types of fire extinguishers:
  * Fire Extinguisher
  * Standpipe
  * Wet Chemical
* **Sprinkler Systems** (`Sprinkler Systems`, `Select Multiple`, **required**):
  Sprinkler systems help prevent and suppress fires. Indicate if the systems are present and functioning correctly.
* **Exit Signs** (`Exit Signs`, `Select One`, **required**):
  Exit signs are critical for emergency evacuation. Select the correct color:
  * Red
  * Green
  * Yellow
* **Fire Alarm Systems** (`Fire Alarm Systems`, `Text`, **not required**):
  This field is a text input for additional fire alarm systems not listed in the other pages.
* **Fire Extinguishers Hydrants** (`Fire Extinguishers Hydrants`, `Select Multiple`, **required**):
  Fire extinguishers hydrants are essential for fire suppression. Indicate if they are present and functioning correctly.
* **Emergency Power Generators** (`Emergency Power Generators`, `Select One`, **required**):
  Emergency power generators are essential for emergency lighting and power. Select if they are present and functioning correctly:
  * True
  * False
* **Fire Safety Equipment** (`Fire Safety Equipment`, `Select Multiple`, **not required**):
  This field is a text input for additional fire safety equipment not listed in the other pages.
* **Fire Safety Equipment 2** (`Fire Safety Equipment 2`, `Select Multiple`, **required**):
  This field is a repetition of the previous one to double-check the presence of fire safety equipment.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
- If unsure about any equipment, consult with the relevant authorities or experts.
- This form is designed for fire safety assessment; do not use it for general information gathering.
