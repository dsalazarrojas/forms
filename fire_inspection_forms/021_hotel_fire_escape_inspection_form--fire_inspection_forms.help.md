# Hotel Fire Escape Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and track the results of fire escape routes and safety features inspections in hotels.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Review and understand the form's sections and fields.
2.  Answer all required and optional questions based on your observation and expertise.
3.  Provide accurate and descriptive information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hotel Fire Escape Inspection Form Details** (`hotel_fire_escape_inspection_form_details`)
	+ Enter a brief description of the hotel, including its address, location, and any other relevant information.
* **Condition Rating** (`condition_rating`)
	+ Rate the overall condition of the fire escape routes and safety features in the hotel, based on the options:
		- 5: Excellent
		- 4: Good
		- 3: Fair
		- 2: Poor
		- 1: Critical
* **Urgent Issues** (`urgent_issues`)
	+ List any urgent issues found during the inspection, such as hazards or safety concerns.
* **Signatures** (`signatures`)
	+ Collect signatures of the inspector and other relevant personnel, if applicable.
* **Emergency Exit Signatures** (`emergency_exit_signatures`)
	+ Collect signatures of individuals who inspected the emergency exit routes, if applicable.
* **Fire Alarm System** (`fire_alarm_system`)
	+ Indicate the status of the fire alarm system in the hotel:
		- Active: The system is functioning properly.
		- Inactive: The system is not functioning properly.
		- Not Installed: The system is not installed.
* **Sprinkler System** (`sprinkler_system`)
	+ Indicate the status of the sprinkler system in the hotel:
		- Active: The system is functioning properly.
		- Inactive: The system is not functioning properly.
		- Not Installed: The system is not installed.
* **Fire Resistive Materials** (`fire_resistive_materials`)

		- Active: The materials are in good condition and functioning properly.
		- Inactive: The materials are not in good condition or not functioning properly.
		- Not Installed: The materials are not installed.
