# Fire Safety Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fire Safety Monitoring Form is used to monitor fire safety in various buildings. It is a general-purpose form to track and report on fire safety status, risk level, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Select the Fire Safety Monitoring Form from the relevant section or menu.
*   Ensure you have access to the form and have the necessary permissions to fill it out.
*   Fill in the required and optional fields as per the form's instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Fire Safety Monitoring Form** (`fire_safety_monitoring_form`): This field is the title of the form and is automatically filled in for you.
*   **Fire Safety Inspection Date** (`fire_safety_inspection_date`): Enter the date of the fire safety inspection in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Fire Safety Inspection Time** (`fire_safety_inspection_time`): Enter the time of the fire safety inspection in 12-hour format (e.g., 01:30 PM).
*   **Building Name** (`building_name`): Enter the name of the building that was inspected.
*   **Building Address** (`building_address`): Enter the address of the building that was inspected.
*   **Fire Safety Monitoring Status** (`fire_safety_monitoring_status`): Choose the status of the fire safety monitoring:
    *   Fire Safety Not Conducted: The fire safety inspection has not been conducted yet.
    *   Fire Safety Conducted: The fire safety inspection was conducted.
    *   Ongoing Monitoring: Regular monitoring is being done for this building.
*   **Fire Safety Risk Level** (`fire_safety_risk_level`): Choose the level of fire safety risk:
    *   Low: The building has a low fire safety risk.
    *   Moderate: The building has a moderate fire safety risk.
    *   High: The building has a high fire safety risk.
*   **Fire Safety Risk Factors** (`fire_safety_risk_factors`): Enter any risk factors that were identified during the inspection.
*   **Fire Safety Equipment** (`fire_safety_equipment`): Enter any fire safety equipment that were inspected or noted during the inspection.
*   **Fire Safety Training** (`fire_safety_training`): Enter any fire safety training that were conducted for the building's personnel.
*   **Fire Safety Plan** (`fire_safety_plan`): Enter any fire safety plans that were put in place after the inspection.
*   **Fire Safety Inspector** (`fire_safety_inspector`): Enter the name of the fire safety inspector who conducted the inspection.
*   **Fire Safety Inspector Phone** (`fire_safety_inspector_phone`): Enter the phone number of the fire safety inspector.
*   **Fire Safety Inspector Email** (`fire_safety_inspector_email`): Enter the email address of the fire safety inspector.
*   **Fire Safety Notes** (`fire_safety_notes`): Enter any additional notes about the inspection.

## Tips

*   Always ensure you have the necessary permissions to access and complete the form.
*   Enter accurate and clear information for each field to ensure that the data is useful and reliable.
*   Review the form for any required fields before submitting it.
*   Keep the form up-to-date and accurate to ensure that it remains a useful tool for fire safety monitoring.
*   If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact your supervisor or the fire safety team.
