# Fire Alarm System Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to report on the current state of their fire alarm systems. It's meant to be filled out by people responsible for monitoring and maintaining fire alarm systems.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the form sections one by one.
2. Begin with the first section, which asks for information about the fire alarm system.
3. For each field, provide the requested information as accurately as possible.
4. Continue filling out the form until you reach the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fire Alarm System** (fire_alarm_system, text, optional): This field is meant for entering the name or description of the fire alarm system being monitored.
* **Fire Alarm Panel** (fire_alarm_panel, text, optional): This field is for entering information about the fire alarm panel, which may include its location, type, or other relevant details.
* **Fire Alarm Zone 1** (fire_alarm_zone_1, text, optional): This field is for reporting on the status of the first fire alarm zone.
* **Fire Alarm Zone 2** (fire_alarm_zone_2, text, optional): This field is for reporting on the status of the second fire alarm zone.
* **Fire Alarm Zone 3** (fire_alarm_zone_3, text, optional): This field is for reporting on the status of the third fire alarm zone.
* **Fire Alarm Zone 4** (fire_alarm_zone_4, text, optional): This field is for reporting on the status of the fourth fire alarm zone.
* **Fire Alarm Zone 5** (fire_alarm_zone_5, text, optional): This field is for reporting on the status of the fifth fire alarm zone.
* **Fire Alarm System Monitoring Form 8** (fire_alarm_system_monitoring_form_8, text, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of another field and is meant to be empty.
