# Au Pair Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Au Pair Application Form is designed to collect essential information about you, the applicant, to help us assess your suitability for au pair placement. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and professional information.
2. Select the correct options for each field, such as your age, nationality, and preferred countries for placement.
3. Provide accurate and detailed information in the text fields, such as your childcare experience and special skills.
4. Make sure to review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport or identification document.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter your current age.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, required): Enter your country of citizenship.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, text, required): Enter your passport number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number in international format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Current Address** (`current_address`, text, required): Enter your current street address.
* **City and Country** (`city_country`, text, required): Enter the city and country where you currently reside.
* **Childcare Experience** (`childcare_experience`, note, optional): This section is not required, but it's essential to provide information about your childcare experience, such as any relevant certifications or training.
* **Years of Childcare Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked with children.
* **Age Groups You Have Cared For** (`age_groups_experience`, select_multiple, required): Select all the age groups you have experience with, such as Infants 0-1 year, Toddlers 1-3 years, etc.
* **Type of Childcare Experience** (`childcare_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all the types of childcare experience you have, such as babysitting, daycare, nanny work, etc.
* **Special Skills with Children** (`special_skills`, text, optional): List any special skills you have that may be useful in childcare, such as language proficiency or education qualifications.
* **Native Language** (`native_language`, text, required): Enter the language you speak as your first language.
* **English Language Level** (`english_proficiency`, select_one, required): Rate your English language proficiency, such as Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, etc.
* **Other Languages Spoken** (`other_languages`, text, optional): List any additional languages you speak.
* **Preferred Countries for Placement** (`preferred_countries`, text, required): Enter the countries where you would like to work as an au pair.
* **When Can You Start?** (`start_date_availability`, date, required): Enter your desired start date for placement.
* **Length of Commitment** (`duration_commitment`, select_one, required): Select the length of commitment you can offer, such as 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 12+ months, or Flexible.
* **References** (`references`, note, optional): This section is not required, but it's essential to provide information about your professional references, such as at least one professional reference.
* **Reference Name and Contact** (`reference_information`, text, required): Provide at least one professional reference's name and contact information.
