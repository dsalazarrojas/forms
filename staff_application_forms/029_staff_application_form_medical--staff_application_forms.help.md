# Staff Application Form Medical - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect medical history information from staff applicants. It helps to ensure the applicant's confidentiality and accuracy of the information they provide.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your valid email address in the email field.
3. Type your phone number in the phone number field.
4. In the Medical History sections, enter any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries you have had. This includes any previous medical conditions, allergies, or other health issues.
5. Be sure to provide accurate and complete information, as this will help us ensure your confidentiality and ensure we can best support you in your new role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, note, required): Enter any relevant medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries you have had. Be as detailed as possible to help us understand your medical needs.
* **Medical History 2**, **Medical History 3**, **Medical History 4**, **Medical History 5**, **Medical History 6**, **Medical History 7**, **Medical History 8**, **Medical History 9**, **Medical History 10** (`Medical History 2` to `Medical History 10`, note, required): Enter any additional medical history information that may be relevant to your application. Please provide as much detail as you can about any medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries you have had. 

Note: Each of these fields is a continuation of the medical history section, allowing you to provide more detailed information about your medical conditions, treatments, or surgeries. 

## Tips
* Please be as honest as possible when answering these questions.
* If you're unsure about what information to provide, please contact our HR team for assistance.
* This form is used to ensure your confidentiality, so please ensure you answer accurately and honestly.
