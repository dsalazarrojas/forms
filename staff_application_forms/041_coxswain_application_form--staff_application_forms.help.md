# coxswain_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect relevant details from applicants applying for a coxswain position. It is a collection of questions that will help the company evaluate each applicant's background, experience, and skills required for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your details.
2. Ensure that you have answered all the required fields.
3. If you have any additional information that is not covered by the form, please include it in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details**: This field is for providing a brief introduction about yourself, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Experience**: This field is for describing your relevant work experience, including any positions you have held or projects you have completed.
* **Availability**: This field is for specifying your availability to work, including any specific dates or periods you are available.
* **Skills**: This field allows you to select one or more skills you possess that are relevant to the coxswain role. Please choose the options that apply to you from the list.
* **Additional Info**: This field is for providing any additional information that you feel is important for the company to know about your application. Please be as detailed as possible.
