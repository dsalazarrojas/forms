# Staff Leave Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request staff leave for a specific period of time. It's essential to fill it out accurately to ensure your leave is recorded correctly and processed smoothly. Before proceeding, please note that this form should only be filled out once for each leave period. If you need to request multiple leaves, please submit a new form for each separate leave period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. **Enter your Employee ID**: Please fill in your Employee ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you for record-keeping purposes.
2. **Select your Leave Start Date**: Choose the date when you intend to start your leave. Use the date format: YYYY-MM-DD.
3. **Enter your Leave End Date**: Choose the date when your leave ends. Use the date format: YYYY-MM-DD.
4. **Leave Type**: Check the box next to 'Yes' if you are requesting leave and 'No' if you are not requesting leave. This helps us understand your intention.
5. **Leave Duration**: Select all dates you will be absent from work. You can select multiple dates if your leave spans over multiple days.
6. **Enter your Leave Start Time**: Choose the time you intend to start your leave. Use the 24-hour time format: HH:MM.
7. **Leave End Time**: Choose the time you intend to finish your leave. Use the 24-hour time format: HH:MM.
8. **Leave Reason**: Provide a brief explanation for your leave, which will help us understand your circumstances.
9. **Leave Location**: If you are not working from the office, please specify the location where you will be.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID**: This field is for your unique identifier, please enter your correct ID.
* **Leave Start Date**: This is the date when you plan to start your leave. Make sure to use the correct date format.
* **Leave End Date**: This is the date when your leave will end. Again, use the correct date format.
* **Leave Type**: Check the 'Yes' box if you wish to request leave and 'No' if you are not requesting leave.
* **Leave Duration**: Select all dates you will be absent from work.
* **Leave Start Time**: This is the time when you plan to start your leave. Use the 24-hour time format.
* **Leave End Time**: This is the time when you plan to finish your leave. Use the 24-hour time format.
* **Leave Reason**: Please give a brief explanation for your leave.
* **Leave Location**: If you are not working from the office, specify the location where you will be.

## Tips
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you enter to ensure your leave is processed correctly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR representative.
