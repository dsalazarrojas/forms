# Event Announcer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event announcers to apply for a specific event. Please fill out this form with your relevant information to help us evaluate your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Event Announcer Form Data** section with a brief description of your experience and qualifications as an event announcer.
2. Indicate whether you have demo files available for review in the **Demo Files** section. Choose from the options "Yes", "No", or "Other" if you have a different type of demo file to share.
3. List your preferences in the **Preferences** section. This can include any specific equipment or software requirements you need to perform your duties.
4. Enter your availability in the **Availability** section, including the dates and times you are available to work.
5. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Info** section, including your name, email address, and phone number.
6. Enter your email address in the **Email** field, which will be used as your primary contact method.
7. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
8. Enter any additional notes or information in the **Notes** field.
9. If you are not the event planner, enter the name of the event planner you will be working with in the **Event Planner** field.
10. If you are part of a production team, enter the team name and contact information (name, email, and phone number) in the **Production Team** fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Announcer Form Data**: A text field for you to describe your experience and qualifications as an event announcer.
* **Demo Files**: A select multiple field to indicate if you have demo files available for review. Choose from "Yes", "No", or "Other" if you have a different type of demo file to share.
* **Preferences**: A text field to list any specific equipment or software requirements you need to perform your duties.
* **Availability**: A date field to enter the dates and times you are available to work.
* **Contact Info**: A text field for you to enter your name, address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Email**: An email field for you to enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number.
* **Notes**: A note field to enter any additional notes or information.
* **Event Planner Info**: A text field to enter the name of the event planner you will be working with.
* **Event Planner Email**: An email field to enter the event planner's email address.
* **Event Planner Phone**: A text field to enter the event planner's phone number.
* **Event Planner**: A text field to enter the name of the event planner.
* **Production Team Info**: A text field to enter the name of the production team you will be working with.
* **Production Team Email**: An email field to enter the production team's email address.
* **Production Team Phone**: A text field to enter the production team's phone number.
* **Production Team**: A text field to enter the name of the production team.
