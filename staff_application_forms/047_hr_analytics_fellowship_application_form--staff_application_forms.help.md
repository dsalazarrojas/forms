# HR Analytics Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Analytics Fellowship Application Form is a tool for staff members to apply to the HR Analytics Fellowship program. The program is designed for staff members who are interested in developing their skills in HR analytics and want to be part of a community that shares knowledge, best practices, and innovation in HR analytics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Read the information on this page carefully before proceeding.
2.  Fill in the required fields, including your name, email, and contact information.
3.  Ensure that your contact information matches your existing records.
4.  Submit your application for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required: false): This is the first page of the application form. Please read and familiarize yourself with the program details.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your personnel record.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your company email address as it appears on your personnel record.
*   **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Your work contact number, this will allow us to get in touch with you for further questions and updates.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Your contact email address, this will allow us to communicate with you about your application and any further steps required.
