# Medical Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from medical staff applicants. It is intended for internal use and should only be filled out by individuals applying to join the medical staff.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to increase your chances of being considered for staff membership.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out (marked as "required" on the form).
- You can select multiple options for certain fields (e.g., education level).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Contact Information
**Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, such as your address or other relevant details.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Education Level
**Education** (`education`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable levels of education you have completed, such as High School, College, or University.

### Years of Experience
**Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): Enter your years of experience in the medical field.

### License
**License** (`license`, select_one, optional): Select the type of medical license you hold, such as MD, PhD, or RN.
