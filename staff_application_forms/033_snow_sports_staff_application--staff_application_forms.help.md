# Snow Sports Staff Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Snow Sports Staff Application form is designed to collect information from staff members interested in working in the snow sports industry. Please use this form to apply for roles within this field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and enter your information accurately.
2. For each field, select the correct option or enter your details as required.
3. Ensure you meet the required fields, denoted by the "required" label.
4. Preview your form before submission to review your entries.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Job Position**: Enter the job title or position you are applying for.
- **Contact Method**: Select all contact methods you prefer to be contacted through. You can choose from phone or email.
- **Employment Status**: Select one of the two available options: permanent or seasonal.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact method selections to ensure accuracy.
- If you need help with any field, please reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
- Ensure you have read and agree to our terms and conditions before submission.
- If you have already applied using this form, do not resubmit.
