# FullStack Team Lead Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide FullStack with detailed information about your background, skills, and preferences, so we can better understand your qualifications for a Team Lead position. Please fill out this form carefully to help us get to know you and evaluate your potential for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the step-by-step guide below:

1. Enter your personal details, such as your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number.
2. Provide information about your Job Title, Job Description, Experience Years, and Experience Job Title.
3. If you have any relevant education, please select the correct level (e.g., High School, Bachelor, Master, or Doctorate).
4. If applicable, specify your Available Start Date and Available End Date for your work schedule.
5. Choose your preferred work location (Remote, On-Site, or Hybrid).
6. Select the languages you are proficient in.
7. Choose the available tools you have experience with (e.g., Sketch, Figma, Adobe XD, Adobe Illustrator, or Inkscape).
8. Select the type of work you would be interested in (Design, Development, Data Science, or Project Management).
9. Choose your available work schedule (Flexible, Monday to Friday, Tuesday to Thursday, or Monday to Wednesday).
10. Finally, please review all fields carefully before submitting your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address for communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, text, required): Briefly describe your current or desired job title.
* **Job Description** (`Job Description`, text, required): Write a concise summary of your current or desired job description.
* **Experience Years** (`Experience Years`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have relevant experience in the field.
* **Experience Job Title** (`Experience Job Title`, text, required): Briefly describe your previous job titles and their relevance to the Team Lead position.
* **Experience Company** (`Experience Company`, text, required): Enter the names of your previous employers.
* **Education** (`Education`, select_one, optional): Select the level of education you have achieved (High School, Bachelor, Master, or Doctorate).
* **Available Start Date** (`Available Start Date`, date, optional): Enter your earliest available start date for the position.
* **Available End Date** (`Available End Date`, date, optional): Enter your latest available end date for the position.
* **Work Location** (`Work Location`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred work location (Remote, On-Site, or Hybrid).
* **Available Work Hours** (`Available Work Hours`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available to work (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday).
* **Available Language** (`Available Language`, select_multiple, optional): Select the languages you are proficient in (e.g., English, Mandarin, Japanese, French, Spanish, or Portuguese).
* **Available Tools** (`Available Tools`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the tools you have experience with (e.g., Sketch, Figma, Adobe XD, Adobe Illustrator, or Inkscape).
* **Work Type** (`Work Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of work you would be interested in (Design, Development, Data Science, or Project Management).
* **Work Schedule** (`Work Schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred work schedule (Flexible, Monday to Friday, Tuesday to Thursday, or Monday to Wednesday).

By following these instructions and explanations, you will be able to complete the FullStack Team Lead Application form successfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us for support.
