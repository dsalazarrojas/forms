# Camp Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Staff Application Form is used to collect information from potential staff applicants, including personal details, work experience, and other relevant information. This form helps us assess your suitability for staff positions at our camp and ensures we have all the necessary information to process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring you answer every question accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have not missed any required fields.
3. If you need help with any question, do not hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant First Name** (`applicant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ID.
* **Applicant Last Name** (`applicant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your street address as it appears on your ID.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city or town where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code as it appears on your ID.
* **Position Applying For** (`position_applying_for`, select_one, required): Select the position you are applying for from the list of options.
* **Desired Session** (`desired_session`, select_one, required): Select the session you prefer to work in from the list of options.
* **Availability Start Date** (`availability_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you can start working.
* **Availability End Date** (`availability_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you must leave.
* **Do You Have Previous Camp Experience** (`previous_camp_experience`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes - as staff' if you have previous camp staff experience, 'Yes - as camper' if you have previous camper experience, or 'No - first time' if this is your first time applying.
* **Describe Your Camp Experience** (`camp_experience_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your previous camp experience if applicable.
* **Relevant Skills or Certifications** (`relevant_skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant skills or certifications you have.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest education level from the list of options.
* **Current Occupation or Student Status** (`current_occupation`, text, required): Enter your current occupation or student status.
* **Experience Working with Youth** (`work_with_youth_experience`, text, required): Describe your experience working with youth.
* **Preferred Age Group to Work With** (`age_group_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select all the age groups you are comfortable working with.
* **Special Talents or Abilities** (`special_talents`, text, optional): Describe any special talents or abilities you may have.
* **Why Do You Want to Work at This Camp** (`why_do_you_want_to_work`, text, required): Explain why you want to work at this camp.
* **What Is Your Greatest Strength as a Staff Member** (`strength_as_staff`, text, required): Describe your greatest strength as a staff member.
* **How Would You Handle a Challenging Camper** (`challenge_handling`, text, required): Describe how you would handle a challenging camper.
* **Can You Provide Professional References** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Select the number of references you can provide.
* **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, text, required if 'Yes - 3 references' or more selected): Enter the name of your first professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, text, required if 'Yes - 3 references' or more selected): Enter the phone number of your first professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Email** (`reference_1_email`, email, required if 'Yes - 3 references' or more selected): Enter the email of your first professional reference.
* **Reference 1 Relationship** (`reference_1_relationship`, text, required if 'Yes - 3 references' or more selected): Explain how your first professional reference knows you.
* **I Consent to a Background Check** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes - I consent' if you agree to a background check.
* **I Consent to a Drug Test** (`drug_test_consent`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes - I consent' if you agree to a drug test.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance** (`transportation_needed`, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes - I need information' or 'Yes - need ride share' if you need transportation assistance.
* **Do You Need Staff Housing** (`housing_needed`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes - need housing' if you require staff housing.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select all dietary restrictions you have.
* **How Did You Hear About This Position** (`how_heard_about_position`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the ways you heard about this position.
* **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, text, required): Sign your name as the applicant.
* **Date Signed** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer every question accurately and completely.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you have not missed any required fields.
* If you need help with any question, do not hesitate to contact us.
