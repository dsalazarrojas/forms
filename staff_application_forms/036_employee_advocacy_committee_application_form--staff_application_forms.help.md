# Employee Advocacy Committee Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Advocacy Committee Application Form is designed to help you apply for membership to the Employee Advocacy Committee. This committee promotes staff interests, provides support, and advocates for employee rights. Before filling out this form, please ensure you haven't already applied or been selected for the committee. If you're unsure, contact your HR or relevant department for clarification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.
2. Click on each field below to enter your information.
3. Complete each field as instructed.
4. Review your application for accuracy and completeness.
5. Click "Submit Form" to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Information** (<font-size="1">Contact Information</font>): Enter your first and last name, job title, department/affiliation, and contact information (e.g., email and/or phone number).
* **Work Experience** (<font-size="1">Work Experience</font>): Describe your relevant work experience, highlighting accomplishments and skills gained.
* **Education Background** (<font-size="1">Education Background</font>): List your relevant education, degrees earned, and institutions attended.
* **Skills and Qualifications** (<font-size="1">Skills and Qualifications</font>): Enumerate your relevant skills, qualifications, and certifications gained through work or education.
* **Availability** (<font-size="1">Availability</font>): Specify your availability to attend meetings and participate in committee activities.
* **Availability Details** (<font-size="1">Availability Details</font>): Provide specific details about your availability, such as work schedules or constraints.
* **Why Join Advocacy Committee** (<font-size="1">Why Join Advocacy Committee</font>): Clearly state your reasons for joining the Employee Advocacy Committee, demonstrating your interest and commitment to the committee's purpose.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Proofread your application before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact your HR or relevant department.
* You can only submit one application per form; if you need to update your application, contact your HR or relevant department for assistance.
