# Editor Selection Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Editor Selection Application form is designed to gather necessary information from candidates applying for an editor position. It consists of 6 fields that cover various aspects of the candidate's qualifications and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through the form carefully and answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
- Make sure to review each field's description and examples to help guide your response.
- Complete all required fields (if any) to ensure your application is not incomplete.
- If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, contact the form creator at `form@gic.mx`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Candidate Details** (`candidate_details`, text, not required): This field is a brief description of the candidate's background and qualifications. Please provide a short paragraph (max 200 characters) explaining your relevant experience, skills, and qualifications.
- **Editors Experience** (`editor_experience`, select_multiple, not required): Select all relevant experience from the list provided. This could include previous editor roles, writing or editing experience, or any other relevant positions.
- **Writing Samples** (`writing_samples`, select_multiple, not required): Choose 1-3 writing samples that best represent your editorial work. This could be a published article, a book, or any other writing-related work.
- **Name** (`candidate_name`, text, not required): This field is to be filled with your full name as it appears on your official documents or ID. Please use the format as per your ID or official documents.
- **Name** (`candidate_name_2`, text, not required): This field is a duplicate of the previous field, in case the first one does not meet the format requirements.
- **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, note, not required): This field is for any additional information you would like to share about yourself as a candidate. You can use this field to provide more details about your experience, qualifications, or any other relevant information.
- **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Choose the category that best fits your experience and qualifications as an editor. Please select one from the provided options.

## Tips
- Make sure to proofread your application before submitting it to ensure accuracy and consistency.
- If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the form creator at `form@gic.mx`.
- Please note that the form is for internal use only and is not intended for external sharing or publishing.
