# Summer Activities Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Activities Staff Application Form is designed to collect information from staff members who are interested in working at summer activities, such as camps, programs, or events. This form helps us understand your experience, qualifications, and availability, which will enable us to place you in the best possible role for you and our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select your title from the "Summer Camp Counselor" dropdown menu.
2. Fill out your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Share your experience in the "Experience" field.
6. Specify your available dates in the "Available Dates" field.
7. Select your available days in the "Available Days" field.
8. Choose how many hours you are available to work in the "Available Hours" field.
9. Provide a brief explanation of why you are applying in the "Why Application" field.
10. Select which weekends you are available to work in the "Available Weekends" field.
11. Choose which shifts you are available to work in the "Available Shifts" fields.
12. Select any relevant certifications from the "Certification" fields.
13. Choose your highest level of education from the "Education" field.
14. Select your native language in the "Available Language" field.
15. Select your language level in the "Available Language Level" field.
16. Choose your work experience from the "Work Experience" field.
17. Select how available you are to work (Full-time or Part-time) in the "Availability" field.
18. Choose whether you are looking for a Full-time or Part-time job in the "Job Type" field.
19. Select your employment status (Employee or Volunteer) in the "Employment Status" field.
20. Select who you would like to contact in case of an emergency in the "Emergency Contact" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Counselor**: Select your role at the summer activities, such as Summer Camp Counselor, Activity Leader, or Program Director.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be known at work.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for work-related matters.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for work-related matters.
* **Experience**: Describe your relevant experience, including previous work, education, or volunteer work.
* **Available Dates**: Specify the dates you are available to work, including the start and end dates of your availability.
* **Available Days**: Select the days of the week you are available to work, such as Monday to Friday.
* **Available Hours**: Choose how many hours you are available to work per day.
* **Why Application**: Briefly explain why you are applying for the summer activities staff position.
* **Available Weekends**: Select which weekends you are available to work.
* **Available Shifts**: Choose which shifts you are available to work, such as Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Certification 1**: Select any relevant certifications, such as First Aid or Wilderness First Aid.
* **Certification 2**: Select any relevant certifications, such as Emergency Response or Water Safety.
* **Certification 3**: Select any relevant certifications, such as WFA or Lifeguard.
* **Certification 4**: Select any relevant certifications, such as First Aid, Emergency Responder, or Water Safety.
* **Education**: Choose your highest level of education, such as Bachelor, Master, or Doctorate.
* **Available Language**: Select your native language.
* **Available Language Level**: Select your language proficiency level, such as Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Work Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience, including previous work, education, or volunteer work.
* **Availability**: Choose whether you are available to work Full-time or Part-time.
* **Job Type**: Choose whether you are looking for a Full-time or Part-time job.
* **Employment Status**: Select your employment status, such as Employee or Volunteer.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select who you would like to contact in case of an emergency, such as Parent, Spouse, or Other.
