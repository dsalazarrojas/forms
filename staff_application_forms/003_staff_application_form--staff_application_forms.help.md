# Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from applicants for staff positions. Please fill out the form accurately to help us understand your background and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and clear information.
2. Be thorough in answering each section, as incomplete or inaccurate information may lead to further inquiries.
3. Use the provided hints for guidance if you need help with a particular field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **On Call Positions**: Choose the on-call positions that you are interested in or have experience in.
* **Resume**: Attach your resume, if you have one, to provide more information about your qualifications.
* **Upload Resume**: If you don't have a resume, you can upload a document to provide additional details.
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address for communication purposes.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title you are applying for or the position you are interested in.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in your field.
* **Job Interests**: Choose the job interests that align with the position you are applying for.
* **Job Availability**: Enter your availability to start working or schedule conflicts.
* **Job Schedule**: Enter a specific date when you are available to start working or a preferred schedule.

Note: Please ensure you have completed all required fields, but you are not required to answer all fields.
