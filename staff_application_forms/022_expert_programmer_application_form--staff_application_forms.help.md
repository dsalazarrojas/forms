# Expert Programmer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expert Programmer Application Form is a tool used by applicants to apply for the position of expert programmer. This form gathers information about the applicant's general information, contact details, education, work experience, skills, and other relevant sections to evaluate their qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the General Information section with your basic details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section.
3. List your employment history in the Employment History section.
4. Enter your education information in the Education Info section.
5. Select your relevant skills from the Select Skills section.
6. Upload your resume in the Resume section.
7. Share your portfolio in the Portfolio section.
8. Provide references in the References section.
8. If you have any other relevant work history, please list it in the Work History sections (Work History, Work History 2, Work History 3, Work History 4, Work History 5, Work History 6, Work History 7, Work History 8).
9. Make sure to select your languages proficiency in the Languages section.
10. List your certifications in the Certifications section.
11. Finally, click the Submit button to submit your application.
