# Skincare Product Developer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be used by applicants to apply for the Skincare Product Developer role. Please ensure that you are filling out this form for this specific role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  Fill out your contact information (page 1).
2.  Select your current job title from the options provided (page 2).
3.  Provide a brief description of your work experience (page 6).
4.  Share your education background (page 7).
5.  Upload your portfolio (page 9).
6.  Write a brief statement on why you are interested in the role (page 10).
7.  Agree to the terms and conditions (page 11).
8.  Submit the form (page 12).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **contact_information** (Text, Optional): Please enter your name and contact information for us to get in touch with you.
*   **job_title** (Select One, Required): Please select your current job title.
*   **name** (Text, Optional): This is your name. You can enter your name here.
*   **email** (Email, Optional): Please enter your email address. This will be used for internal communication.
*   **phone** (Text, Optional): Please enter your phone number for us to contact you.
*   **work_experience** (Select Multiple, Optional): Please select your work experience in years.
*   **education** (Text, Optional): Share your educational background.
*   **linkedin** (Text, Optional): If you have a linkedin profile, please share it here.
*   **portfolio** (Text, Optional): Upload your portfolio to showcase your work experience.
*   **why_jotform** (Text, Optional): Please write a brief statement on why you are interested in this role.
*   **terms and conditions** (Note, Optional): Please agree to the terms and conditions of the application process.
*   **submission_status** (Select One, Required): Please select the current status of your submission.
*   **submission_status_details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional information regarding your submission status.
*   **submission** (Date, Optional): Please select the date you submitted your application.
*   **submission_time** (Time, Optional): The time you submitted your application.
*   **submission_date** (Date, Optional): The date you submitted your application.
*   **submission_location** (Text, Optional): Where you submitted your application.
*   **submission_status_details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional details regarding your submission status.

Note: Some fields are not required but are optional for you to provide additional information. Please fill out the required fields for your application to be considered complete.
