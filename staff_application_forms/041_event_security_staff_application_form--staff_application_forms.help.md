# Event Security Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from staff applying for event security roles at your organization. It gathers personal and professional details, as well as availability and references. Please ensure you only submit one form per individual applying for the role, and that your application meets the required fields (marked as "required") to avoid duplication of effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your title and event details on the main page (page 1).
2. Describe your relevant security experience and skills on the "Experience" page (page 2).
3. Enter your availability date on the "Availability" page (page 3).
4. Provide your contact information (phone, email, address) on the "Contact Information" page (page 4). Please note that this field is required.
5. List any relevant references on the "References" page (page 5).
6. Provide any additional information you think would be helpful for the selection process on the "Additional Information" page (page 6).
7. Upload any relevant documents (e.g., certificates, licenses) on the "Upload Documents" page (page 7).
8. Click "Submit" (page 8) to send your application.
9. Review your application carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Main Page** (Page 1)
This is the first page of the form, where you can fill in your basic event details and title.

* **Title**: Enter your event title or name.
* **Event Details**: Briefly describe the event you are applying for.

### 2. **Event Details** (Page 2)
* **Event Details**: Further describe the event you are applying for.

### 3. **Experience** (Page 3)
* **Experience**: Please describe your relevant security experience and skills.

### 4. **Availability** (Page 4)
* **Availability**: Enter the date(s) you are available to work.

### 5. **Contact Information** (Page 5)
* **Contact Information**: Please select which contact methods you are willing to use (Phone, Email, Address).

### 6. **References** (Page 6)
* **References**: List any references who can vouch for your work experience.

### 7. **Additional Information** (Page 7)
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional details that are relevant to your application.

### 8. **Upload Documents** (Page 8)
* **Upload Documents**: Upload any relevant documents (e.g., certificates, licenses).

### 9. **Submit** (Page 9)
* **Submit**: Review and submit your application.

### 10. **Back** (Page 10)
* **Back**: Go back to previous pages to review or change any information.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
