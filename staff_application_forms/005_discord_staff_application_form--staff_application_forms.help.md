# Discord Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Discord Staff Application Form is a tool used to collect information from potential candidates interested in joining the Discord staff team. It's a crucial step in the hiring process to assess a candidate's qualifications, experience, and fit for the available roles. Before starting, make sure you're a suitable candidate for the roles offered and that you haven't already submitted an application for this position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the guidelines and instructions carefully before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information in the specified format.
3. Double-check your details for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Data** (`user_data`, text, optional): This field is for additional comments or information about yourself.
* **Discord Username** (`discord_username`, text, optional): Enter your Discord username that you use for your account.
* **Discord Tag** (`discord_tag`, text, optional): This field is not directly used, but it's for your Discord tag. You can leave it blank if you don't have a tag.
* **Role Interests** (`role_interests`, select_one, required): Choose one or more roles you're interested in, such as Support, Server Admin, or Developer.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that you use for contact or communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is for your phone number, which is not used by our team. You can leave it blank.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience with Discord and related communities. Be specific about your skills and qualifications.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, optional): Select a time zone that best represents your availability. Note that this field is not used by our team.
* **Time Zone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, optional): Enter your offset from UTC (zero if you're in the US Eastern Time (ET) or US Central Time (CT) zone). This field is also not used by our team.
* **Time Zone** (`timezone`, text, optional): Choose your time zone. This field was replaced by "Availability".
