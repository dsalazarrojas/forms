# Temporary Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals applying for temporary staff positions within the organization. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details such as name, contact email, position title, and start date to facilitate the hiring process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field, so please make sure to fill it out.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is an optional field, but we recommend filling it out for identification purposes.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This is an optional field, but it's recommended for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Position Title** in the "Position Title" field. This is an optional field, but it's helpful for us to know the specific position you're applying for.
5. Select your **Start Date** in the "Start Date" field. This is an optional field, but it's recommended for scheduling purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your personal name as it is known in common usage (e.g., John). This is the first name of the individual filling the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your personal surname or last name (e.g., Smith). This is the last name of the individual filling the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address you check and send emails from (e.g., [johndoe@example.com](mailto:johndoe@example.com)). This is the email address of the individual filling the form.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, optional): The specific position or job title you are applying for (e.g., Sales Associate). This is the title of the position the individual is applying for.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): The date you are available to start the position (e.g., 2023-02-20). This is the date the individual is available to start the position.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields (First Name and Email) to ensure a successful submission. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
