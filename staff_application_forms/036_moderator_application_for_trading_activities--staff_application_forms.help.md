# Moderator Application For Trading Activities - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Moderator Application For Trading Activities form is designed to help moderators apply for trading activities such as mediating disputes or managing trading relationships. The form is intended to gather information about your experience, availability, and skills in handling disputes, as well as your time commitment to the trading activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Trading experiences" field with a brief description of your relevant trading experience, including any notable successes or challenges you've faced.
2. Enter your availability in the "Availability" field, including the dates and times you are available to participate in trading activities.
3. In the "Dispute handling" field, describe your approach to handling disputes, including any relevant skills or training you've received.
4. Finally, estimate your time commitment to the trading activities in the "Time commitment" field, using a 24-hour clock format (e.g., 2 hours, 4 hours, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Trading experiences
* **Trading experiences** (`trading_experiences`, `text`, required: false): Describe your relevant trading experience, including any notable successes or challenges you've faced.

### Availability
* **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required: false): Enter your availability, including the dates and times you are available to participate in trading activities.

### Dispute handling
* **Dispute handling** (`dispute_handling`, `text`, required: false): Describe your approach to handling disputes, including any relevant skills or training you've received.

### Time commitment
* **Time commitment** (`time_commitment`, `time`, required: false): Estimate your time commitment to the trading activities in 24-hour clock format (e.g., 2 hours, 4 hours, etc.).
