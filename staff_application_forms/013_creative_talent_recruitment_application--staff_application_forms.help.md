# Creative Talent Recruitment Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Talent Recruitment Application is a form designed to collect information from potential candidates interested in being part of a creative talent recruitment process. This form will help us understand your skills, experience, and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. If you have a resume, please attach it in the "resume" field.
3. Select your role of interest (Graphic Designer, Writer, Artist, or Other).
4. Briefly describe your experience in the "experience" field.
5. List your relevant skills in the "skills" field.
6. Provide a link to your portfolio, if applicable.
7. Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Text).
8. Fill in your contact information in the "contact_info" field.
9. Finally, click the "submit" button to send your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required): If you have a resume, attach it here.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Choose your role of interest (Graphic Designer, Writer, Artist, or Other).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): Briefly describe your experience.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant skills you possess (Illustration, Photography, Graphic Design, Writing).
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required): Provide a link to your portfolio, if applicable.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preferred`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred contact method (Phone, Email, or Text).
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Fill in your contact information.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to send your application.
* **Confirmation Name** (`confirmation_name`, text, required): Re-enter your name for verification.
* **Confirmation Email** (`confirmation_email`, email, required): Re-enter your email address for verification.
* **Confirmation Role** (`confirmation_role`, text, required): Re-enter your role of interest.
* **Confirmation Experience** (`confirmation_experience`, text, required): Briefly re-describe your experience for verification.
* **Confirmation Skill** (`confirmation_skill`, text, required): Re-select all relevant skills you possess.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them here.
