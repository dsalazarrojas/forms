# Principal Resume Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Principal Resume Submission Form is designed to gather essential details from principals seeking to apply for a job. It includes questions about their background, contact information, and work experience. Ensure you fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

3. Provide your **Contact Email** in the Contact Email field (Field 3).
4. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** in the Contact Phone field (Field 4).
5. Upload your **Resume** in the Resume Upload field (Field 5).
6. Enter a **Resume Text** in the Resume Text field (Field 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Principal Name** (Field 1): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **School Name** (Field 2): Enter the name of the school where you last worked or are currently working.
* **Contact Email** (Field 3): Enter your work or personal email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone** (Field 4): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Resume Upload** (Field 5): Upload a PDF or docx file of your resume.
* **Resume Text** (Field 6): You can enter any relevant information that isn't included on your resume.
* **Date Of Birth** (Field 7): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Experience** (Field 8): Provide details about your work experience in education, including roles and achievements.
* **Job Title** (Field 9): Enter the title of the job you're applying for.
* **Current School Name** (Field 10): Enter the name of the school where you're currently working or most recently worked.
* **Job Description** (Field 11): Enter a brief description of your job role.
* **Principal References** (Field 12): Enter names and contact information of your professional references.
* **Job Start Date** (Field 13): Enter the date you started your job.
* **Job End Date** (Field 14): Enter the date you ended your job.
* **Job Length** (Field 15): Enter the number of years you worked in the job.
* **Highest Degree** (Field 16): Enter the highest degree you've earned.
* **Field 1** (Field 17): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for this field.
* **Field 2** (Field 18): Enter any information relevant to this field.
* **Field 3** (Field 19): Enter your details for this field.
* **Field 4** (Field 20): Enter any relevant information in this field.
* **Field 5** (Field 21): Enter any details for this field.
* **Field 6** (Field 22): Select 'Yes' or 'No' for this field.
* **Field 7** (Field 23): Enter any details in this field.
* **Field 8** (Field 24): Enter a number for this field.
* **Field 9** (Field 25): Enter a number for this field.
