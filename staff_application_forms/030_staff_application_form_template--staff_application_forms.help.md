# Staff Application Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This template is designed to collect information about an individual's work experience. It's intended to help applicants or employees provide details about their background and history in the workforce. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. Enter your address in the address field.
6. Enter the start date of your work experience in the work experience start date field.
7. Enter the end date of your work experience in the work experience end date field.
8. Enter your job title during your work experience in the work experience job title field.
9. Enter the company you worked for during your work experience in the work experience company field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (Text)
* `first name`: Enter your first name here.

### Last Name (Text)
* `last name`: Enter your last name here.

### Email (Email)
* `email`: Enter your email address here. This field is where your email address will be used for communication.

### Phone (Text)
* `phone`: Enter your phone number here. This field is for your contact information.

### Address (Text)
* `address`: Enter your address here. This field is for contact and location purposes.

### Work Experience Start Date (Date)
* `work experience start date`: Enter the date you started your current or previous job here.

### Work Experience End Date (Date)
* `work experience end date`: Enter the date you ended your current or previous job here, if applicable.

### Work Experience Job Title (Text)
* `work experience job title`: Enter your job title during your work experience here. This field is for describing your role in the company.

### Work Experience Company (Text)
* `work experience company`: Enter the company you worked for during your work experience here. This field is for identifying the company where you gained your experience.
