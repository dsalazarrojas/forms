# Event Staffing Application & Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for event staff to apply for events and indicate their availability. It will help event organizers to better understand who is available and willing to work on their event. 

## How To Complete This Form
To use this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your event information: 
   - event_organizer_information
   - event_organizer_name
   - event_organizer_email
   - event_organizer_phone
   - event_description
   - event_date
   - event_time

2. Select the roles you are interested in working on the event: 
   - role_preferences

3. Indicate your availability for the event: 
   - availability
   - days_available
   - time_available

4. Confirm your staff availability for the event: 
   - staff_availability

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer_information`): Enter the name of the event you are applying for.
* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`): Enter your name.
* **Event Organizer Email** (`event_organizer_email`): Enter your email address.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (`event_organizer_phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Briefly describe the event you are applying for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Role Preferences** (`role_preferences`): Choose the roles you are interested in working on the event.
* **Role ID 1** (`staff_role_id_1`): Choose the role for which you are interested in working on the event (if applicable).
* **Role ID 2** (`staff_role_id_2`): Choose the role for which you are interested in working on the event (if applicable).
* **Role ID 3** (`staff_role_id_3`): Choose the role for which you are interested in working on the event (if applicable).
* **Availability** (`availability`): Indicate whether you are available to work on the event.
* **Day Available** (`days_available`): Choose the days you are available to work on the event.
* **Time Available** (`time_available`): Choose the time of day you are available to work on the event.
* **Staff Availability** (`staff_availability`): Confirm your availability for the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional information or notes about your availability.
