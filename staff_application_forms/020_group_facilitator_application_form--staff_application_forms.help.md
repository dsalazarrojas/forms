# Group Facilitator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Facilitator Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in leading a Group Facilitation workshop. This form is used to gather essential details about the workshop, its participants, and the facilitator's qualifications. Please note that only completed forms with all required fields will be reviewed for approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting your application.
2.  Enter your details in the required fields (indicated by *).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name**: A field to input your name (*).
*   **Email**: Enter your email address to be contacted for further discussion and updates.
*   **Phone**: A contact number where you can be reached.
*   **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are interested in leading (*).
*   **Start Date**: The date you plan to start the workshop (*).
*   **End Date**: The date you plan to end the workshop (*).
*   **Group Name**: If you are already part of a group, input its name.
*   **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your workshop or yourself (optional).
*   **Submitter**: Choose how you are submitting the application (submitting for, submitting to, or both) (*).
*   **Comments**: Enter any comments or additional details about your application (optional).

## Tips
1.  Please ensure that all fields marked with \* are completed before submitting the form.
2.  Be as detailed as possible in the additional information field to ensure your application is thoroughly reviewed.
3.  If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
