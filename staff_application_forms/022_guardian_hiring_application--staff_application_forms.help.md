# Guardian Hiring Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a guardian candidate, including their details, references, and qualifications. This form is typically used by schools, educational institutions, or other organizations interacting with children.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Application Form**: Fill in the necessary information under this section, including any relevant details about the candidate.
2. **Candidate Details**: Provide accurate and up-to-date information about the candidate, including their name, any relevant qualifications or experience.
3. **References**: Select the names of relevant references from the provided options or add a custom reference if not listed.
4. **Experience**: Describe the candidate's relevant work experience, if applicable.
5. **Availability**: Specify the candidate's availability for the position.
6. **References (Text)**: Provide any additional information or explanation about the references selected.
7. **Submit Form**: Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the "Submit Form" button to finalize your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: A free-text field for providing any additional information about the candidate.
* **Candidate Details**: A field for providing the candidate's name and any relevant qualifications or experience.
* **References**: A multiple-choice field with options for selecting relevant references. You can also add a custom reference if not listed.
* **Experience**: A field for describing the candidate's relevant work experience.
* **Availability**: A field for specifying the candidate's availability for the position.
* **References (Text)**: A free-text field for providing additional information or explanation about the selected references.
* **Submit Form**: The final button to submit the completed application.

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Only select references that are relevant to the candidate's qualifications and experience.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for further clarification.
* Once submitted, the form will be reviewed and verified before approval.
