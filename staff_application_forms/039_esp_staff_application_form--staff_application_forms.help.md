# Esp Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for staff applications, and you are required to provide the following information to be considered for a position. Your input will be reviewed and processed efficiently, and you may be contacted for further information or to discuss your application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in their respective fields. These fields are mandatory.
2. Provide a valid **Email** address that is used as your contact point.
3. Enter your **Phone** number for easy contact.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date format (DD/MM/YYYY).
5. Select your **Position** from the available options:
	* Manager
	* Team Lead
	* Team Member
6. Choose your **Department** by selecting one or more of the available options. You can select multiple departments if applicable.
7. Enter your **Work Schedule**, which is the time you are available to work.
8. (Optional) Provide any additional **Notes** about yourself that you'd like to be considered.
9. (Optional) **Upload Documents** that support your application, such as a CV or references.
10. Read and **Agree with the terms and conditions** of the application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Position**: Select the position you are applying for:
	+ Manager: Team leader or supervisor role.
	+ Team Lead: A team leader or coordinator role.
	+ Team Member: Regular team member role.
* **Department**: Select one or more departments you are applying to:
	+ Yes: You are applying to this department.
	+ No: You are not applying to this department.
* **Work Schedule**: Enter your available work schedule in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* (Optional) **Notes**: Provide any additional information that you'd like to be considered.
* (Optional) **Upload Documents**: Upload documents that support your application, such as a CV or references.
* **Agree with the terms and conditions**: Read and agree to the application process terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* Upload any relevant documents to support your application.
* Review your application carefully before submitting it.
* Contact the HR or recruitment team if you have any questions or need further assistance.
