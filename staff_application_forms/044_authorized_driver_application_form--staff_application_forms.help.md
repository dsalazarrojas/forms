# Authorized Driver Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from applicants who wish to be considered for driver positions within our organization. The information gathered will be used to evaluate the applicant's suitability for a position and to create a record of their details for administrative and compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required personal details in pages 2-5.
2. Fill in your application date in page 1.
3. Select your driver license details in pages 6-9.
4. Fill in your years of driving experience in pages 10-11.
5. Check the types of vehicles you have driven experience with in page 12.
6. Answer questions about your driving history and record in pages 13-15.
7. Consent to background checks in page 16.
8. Provide professional references (if applicable) in page 18.
9. Review your answers for completeness and accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Date** (page 1, date): When you are applying for the driver position.
* **Position Title** (page 2, text): The driver position you are applying for.
* **First Name** (page 3, text): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (page 4, text): Your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (page 5, date): Your birth date.
* **Email Address** (page 6, email): Your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (page 7, text): Your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (page 8, text): Your residential address.
* **City** (page 9, text): Your city.
* **State or Province** (page 10, text): Your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (page 11, text): Your zip or postal code.
* **Driver License Number** (page 12, text): Your driver license number.
* **License State** (page 13, text): State where your license was issued.
* **License Expiration Date** (page 14, date): When your driver license expires.
* **License Class** (page 15, select_one): The class of your driver license (e.g., Class A, Class B, etc.).
* **Years of Driving Experience** (page 16, number): Total years as a licensed driver.
* **Commercial Driving Experience** (page 17, number): Commercial or professional driving years.
* **Types of Vehicles Driven** (page 18, select_multiple): Check all the types of vehicles you have driven (e.g., Sedan, SUV, etc.).
* **Current MVR Status** (page 19, select_one): Check the status of your current MVR (e.g., Clean, Minor Violations, etc.).
* **Traffic Violations in Past 3 Years** (page 20, number): Number of traffic violations within the last 3 years.
* **At-Fault Accidents in Past 3 Years** (page 21, number): Number of at-fault accidents within the last 3 years.
* **DUI or DWI Conviction** (page 22, select_one): Check if you have been convicted of DUI or DWI.
* **Consent for Background Check** (page 23, select_one): Check if you consent to background and driving record checks.
* **Previous Employer** (page 24, text): Last employer as a driver (if applicable).
* **Professional References Available** (page 25, select_one): Check if you have professional references available (if applicable).
