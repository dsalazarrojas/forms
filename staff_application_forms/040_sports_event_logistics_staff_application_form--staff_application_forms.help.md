# Sports Event Logistics Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Event Logistics Staff Application Form is designed to collect information from potential staff members for sports events. This form will be used to gather details about your availability, skills, and experience to help event organizers assign roles and schedule staff for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Describe your previous experience related to the sport or event management.
3. Select one or more skills you have that would be beneficial to the event (e.g., first aid, catering, event management).
4. Choose the role(s) you are interested in or willing to take on during the event (e.g., Event Coordinator, Staff Manager, Catering Manager).
5. Enter your available work hours, including the start and end times you are available.
6. Provide the dates you are available to work.
7. Enter any notes or comments you'd like to share with the event organizers.
8. Finally, select "Submit and Continue" to save your progress and allow the event organizers to review your application, or "Submit and Finish" to submit your application for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that event organizers can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in case event organizers need to contact you directly.
* **Experience**: Describe your previous experience related to the sport or event management. This can include volunteering, working, or participating in events.
* **Skills**: Select one or more skills that you have that would be beneficial to the event. For example, first aid or catering experience.
* **Roles**: Choose the role(s) you are interested in or willing to take on during the event. For example, Event Coordinator or Staff Manager.
* **Work Hours**: Enter your available work hours, including start and end times.
* **Available Hours**: Enter your available work hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Days Off**: Enter the dates you are not available to work.
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you are available to start working.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you are available to end working.
* **Notes**: Enter any notes or comments you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Submit**: Select "Submit and Continue" to save your progress and allow event organizers to review your application, or "Submit and Finish" to submit your application for review.
