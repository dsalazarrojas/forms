# Standby Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are available to work on-call shifts or short-notice requests. By filling out this form, you will help us identify potential candidates to fill staffing gaps and allocate them to suitable on-call shifts.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each page carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2.  Fill out your contact details, availability, qualifications, and on-call details.
3.  Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Contact Details

• **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your name, email, phone number, or any other contact information we may need to reach you for further discussions or notifications. Please enter your name, email, and phone number in the format: "Name (email) (phone number)".

### 2. Availability

• **Availability** (`availability`, `date`, required/optional): Here, you can select the date(s) you are available to work on-call shifts. If you have multiple available dates, please separate them with commas. Note: This field is a date field, so select dates in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.

### 3. Qualifications

• **Qualifications** (`qualifications`, `note`, required/optional): This field is used to highlight any special qualifications, skills, or experience relevant to on-call shifts. Please type in a concise note about your qualifications, and we will review them for relevance.

### 4. On Call Details

• **On Call Details** (`on_call_details`, `note`, required/optional): Here, you can provide any additional information about your on-call experiences, skills, or certifications. Type in a concise note, and we will review it for suitability for on-call shifts.

## Tips

- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
- Use clear and concise language in your responses.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
