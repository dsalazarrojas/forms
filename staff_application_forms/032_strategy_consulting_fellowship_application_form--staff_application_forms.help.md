# Strategy Consulting Fellowship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Strategy Consulting Fellowship Application Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information about your background, skills, and experiences as part of the application process for the Strategy Consulting Fellowship program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete each page of the form carefully and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide your current contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Answer all questions truthfully and to the best of your ability.
4. If you have any relevant documents or attachments, please provide them along with your application.
