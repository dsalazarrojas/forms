# Voice Acting Talent Agency Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for voice acting talent agencies to collect information from potential talent. It's designed to gather necessary background information and experience details to evaluate applicants for casting opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and understand its purpose.
2. Fill out the form in the following pages:
	* Page 1: "Welcome to Voice Acting Talent Agency Application" (Informational page).
	* Page 2: "Voice Acting Background Information" (Section to provide general background details).
	* Page 3: "Voice Acting Experience" (Section to provide details about your experience as a voice actor).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of your talent group/agency.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Page 2:**
	+ **Contact Information** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address.
	+ **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Voice Acting Background Information:**
	+ **About Yourself** (`background`, text, required): Provide a brief description about yourself, including any relevant experience or education in the voice acting field.
* **Voice Acting Experience:**
	+ **Experience Overview** (`experience`, text, required): Describe your relevant voice acting experience, including any notable projects or credits.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, contact the form owner: form@gic.mx.
