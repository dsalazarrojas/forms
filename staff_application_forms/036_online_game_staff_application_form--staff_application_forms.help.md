# Online Game Staff Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for players applying to be staff in an online game. We are looking for players who have the right background, skills, and experience to excel in the game.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the available positions and choose the one that best fits your experience and skills.
2. Fill out the "Background About Game" section with a brief description of your experience with the game.
3. In the "Player Experience" section, describe your experience with the game, including any relevant achievements or accomplishments.
4. In the "Player Gameplay" section, describe your play style and any strategies you use in the game.
5. In the "Player Knowledge" section, list any knowledge you have about the game mechanics, lore, or other relevant information.
6. In the "Availability" section, select your availability for staff work, including your work hours and time zones.
7. In the "Preferred Communication" section, select your preferred communication methods, such as Discord, Teamspeak, or Skype.
8. In the "Contact Details" section, enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
9. In the "Player References" section, list any players who can vouch for your skills and experience.
10. In the "Player Skills" section, select any skills you have that are relevant to the position you are applying for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player In Game Staff Application Form** (`player_in_game_staff_application_form`, text, optional): A brief description of your experience with the game.
* **Position Applying For** (`position_applying_for`, select_one, optional): Choose the position you are applying for (Staff Manager, Officer, Helper, or other).
* **Background About Game** (`background_about_game`, note, optional): A brief description of your experience with the game, including any relevant stories or achievements.
* **Player Experience** (`player_experience`, note, optional): Describe your experience with the game, including any relevant achievements or accomplishments.
* **Player Gameplay** (`player_gameplay`, note, optional): Describe your play style and any strategies you use in the game.
* **Player Knowledge** (`player_knowledge`, note, optional): List any knowledge you have about the game mechanics, lore, or other relevant information.
* **Availability** (`availability`, time, optional): Select your availability for staff work, including your work hours and time zones.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, time, optional): Select the time zone you are in.
* **Preferred Communication** (`preferred_communication`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred communication methods, such as Discord, Teamspeak, or Skype.
* **Other** (`other`, text, optional): Any additional information you would like to share.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Player References** (`player_references`, select_multiple, optional): List any players who can vouch for your skills and experience.
* **Player Skills** (`player_skills`, select_multiple, optional): Select any skills you have that are relevant to the position you are applying for, such as leadership, teamwork, communication, or problem-solving.
