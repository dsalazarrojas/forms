# Digital Nomad Service Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Nomad Service Application Form is designed for digital nomads to apply for services offered by Digital Nomad Service, a platform providing support, tools, or resources for remote workers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your personal information, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Select the services you offer, which can be any combination of digital marketing, social media, graphic design, web development, photography, or video production services.
3. Write a brief bio about yourself.
4. Confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Digital Nomad Service.
5. Select the date of your agreement.
