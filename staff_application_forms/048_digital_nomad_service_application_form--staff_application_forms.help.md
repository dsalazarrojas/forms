# Digital Nomad Service Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Nomad Service Application Form is designed for digital nomads to apply for services offered by Digital Nomad Service, a platform providing support, tools, or resources for remote workers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your personal information, including First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone.
2. Select the services you offer, which can be any combination of digital marketing, social media, graphic design, web development, photography, or video production services.
3. Write a brief bio about yourself.
4. Confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Digital Nomad Service.
5. Select the date of your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* Label: First Name
* Type: Text
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Enter your first name.

### Last Name
* Label: Last Name
* Type: Text
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Enter your last name.

### Email
* Label: Email
* Type: Email
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Enter your valid email address.

### Phone
* Label: Phone
* Type: Text
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Enter your phone number.

### Date of Birth
* Label: Date of Birth
* Type: Date
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.

### Services Offered
* Label: Services Offered
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Select all the services you offer, such as digital marketing, social media, graphic design, web development, photography, or video production.

### Bio
* Label: Bio
* Type: Note
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Write a brief bio about yourself, including your experience and skills.

### Agreement
* Label: Agreement
* Type: Select One
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Confirm your agreement with the terms and conditions of the Digital Nomad Service. Select "I agree with the terms and conditions of the Digital Nomad Service" if you accept the terms.

### Agreement Date
* Label: Agreement Date
* Type: Date
* Required: Required
* Explanation: Select the date you agree with the terms and conditions of the Digital Nomad Service.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your application is complete.
* Be honest when selecting the services you offer.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* Select the correct date for your agreement.
