# NGO Policy Advisor Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for staff to apply for the NGO Policy Advisor position. The goal is to collect relevant information about the applicant's qualifications, work experience, and availability for this role.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your First Name and Last Name accurately.
2. Enter your valid Email and Phone number, which will be used for communication purposes.
3. If applicable, fill in your Date of availability or preferred date.
4. If you have an Address, please enter it to facilitate contact.
5. Fill in your Job Title, which is your current or previous role within your organization.
6. Select your current Job Type (Yes/No) to indicate whether you are currently employed.
7. Choose your Education Level (High/Low) to indicate your level of education.
8. Select your Work Experience (Yes/No) to indicate whether you have worked on similar projects before.
9. Select your available Skills (Yes/No) to indicate which skills you have.
10. Select the languages you are proficient in (Yes/No).
11. Upload your Resume to demonstrate your qualifications.
12. In the Job Description field, describe your past or current role, highlighting your key responsibilities and how they relate to the NGO Policy Advisor position.
13. If you are affiliated with an Organization, select Yes/No to indicate your relationship.
14. If you are looking to work in a specific Job Location, please enter it.
15. Select your current Work Status (Active/Inactive) to indicate your current employment status.
16. Lastly, select your Job Status (Active/Inactive) to indicate your availability for work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: This is your given name at birth. Please enter your first name accurately.
* **Last Name**: This is your surname or family name. Please enter your last name accurately.
* **Email**: This will be used for communication and will be used by the hiring team to contact you. Please ensure it is valid and easily accessible.
* **Phone**: This will be used for communication and interviews. Please ensure it is your active contact number.
* **Date**: If you have a preferred date of availability, please enter it. If not, leave it blank.
* **Address**: If you have a physical address, please enter it for contact purposes.
* **Job Title**: This is your current or previous role within your organization. Please be as accurate as possible.
* **Job Type**: Select Yes if you are currently employed, No if you are not.
* **Education Level**: Select High if you have high-level education, Low if you have low-level education.
* **Work Experience**: Select Yes if you have worked on similar projects, No if you have not.
* **Skills**: Select the skills you have, from the given options, to showcase your expertise.
* **Language**: Select the languages you are proficient in, from the given options, to indicate your ability to communicate effectively.
* **Resume**: Please upload your resume to demonstrate your qualifications and work experience.
* **Job Description**: In this field, describe your past or current role, highlighting key responsibilities and how they relate to the NGO Policy Advisor position.
* **Organization**: Select Yes if you are affiliated with an organization, No if you are not.
* **Job Location**: If you are looking to work in a specific location, please enter it.
* **Work Status**: Select Active if you are currently employed, Inactive if you are not.
* **Job Status**: Select Active if you are available for work, Inactive if you are not.
