# Pilates Informed Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pilates Informed Consent Form is a document that requires participants to provide their personal and medical information before starting a Pilates program. This form helps the instructor or healthcare professional understand any potential risks or concerns related to the participant's health and ability to perform the exercises safely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, such as your name, address, and emergency contact details.
2. Provide any relevant medical history, allergies, or previous injuries that may affect your ability to perform the exercises.
3. Choose your preferred medical supervision level (in-person, virtual, or none).
4. Review and sign the participant agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (1)
* **Participant Information (1)**: A text field to enter your personal details.
*   Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### Participant Name (2)
* **Participant Name (2)**: A text field to enter your name as it appears on your identification.
*   Enter your name as it appears on your identification.

### Date of Birth (3)
* **Date of Birth (3)**: A date field to enter your date of birth.
*   Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.

### Email (4)
* **Email (4)**: A text field to enter your email address.
*   Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.

### Phone (5)
* **Phone (5)**: A text field to enter your phone number.
*   Enter your valid phone number where you can be reached.

### Participant Address (6)
* **Participant Address (6)**: A text field to enter your address.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (8)
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (8)**: A text field to enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
*   Enter your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, friend).

### Emergency Contact Phone (9)

### Previous Injuries (12)
