# Holistic Health Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect comprehensive information about your health and well-being, allowing our health coaches to provide you with personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
- Make sure to select only the relevant options that apply to you.
- Double-check your responses to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information**: Please fill in your basic contact information, including your name and email address.
- **Medical History**: Share any previous medical experiences or conditions you've had.
- **Health Abilities**: Describe your physical or mental health abilities, such as your fitness level or any disabilities.
- **Allergies**: List any allergies you have, including food or environmental allergies.
- **Medications**: Report any medications you are currently taking.
- **Medical Conditions**: Share any current medical conditions you are managing.
- **Medical Procedures**: Describe any medical procedures or surgeries you've had.
- **Medical Tests**: List any recent medical tests or exams you've undergone.
- **Health Coach Services**: Select the health coach services you are interested in receiving (e.g., nutrition guidance, stress management, etc.).
- **Appointment Preferences**: Choose your preferred appointment time, considering your schedule and availability.
- **Note**: Add any additional information or comments you'd like to share with your health coach.
- **Email**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with updates and guidance.

## Tips
- Be thorough and honest in your responses, as this information will help us provide you with personalized support.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us at `form@gic.mx`.

This form will help our health coaches understand your unique needs and provide you with tailored guidance to support your overall health and well-being. Thank you for taking the time to complete this form, and we look forward to supporting you on your health journey.
