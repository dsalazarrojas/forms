# Ob Gyn Patient History Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects patient medical history and information for obstetric and gynecologic care. It helps our healthcare team understand your medical condition, allergies, and current medications, ensuring we provide the best care for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your basic information**:
	* Select "Patient Info" page to enter your basic details.
	* Fill in your name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
2. **List your allergies**:
	* On the "Allergies" page, select any allergies you have from the list.
	* You can choose multiple allergies if needed.
3. **Share your medical history**:
	* On the "Medical History" page, describe any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
	* This will help us understand your medical history.
4. **List your current medications**:
	* On the "Medications" page, select any medications you are currently taking.
	* This includes any supplements, vitamins, or other substances.
5. **Provide additional medical history**:
	* On the "Medical History 2" page, describe any additional medical conditions or concerns.
	* This will help us provide comprehensive care.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Info** (`patient_info`, `text`, required: false): This page is for entering your basic information, including your name, date of birth, and contact details.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This list of pre-defined options allows you to select any allergies you have. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false): Describe any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with. This will help us understand your medical history.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_one`, required: false): This list of pre-defined options allows you to select any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History 2** (`medical_history2`, `text`, required: false): This page is for additional medical history or concerns.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when providing medical history and allergies.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask your healthcare provider.
* Remember to update this form if your medical status changes.
* If you need help with completing this form, ask your healthcare provider for assistance.
