# Longevity Protocol Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Longevity Protocol Intake Form is designed to collect information about your lifestyle habits, health conditions, and demographics to help our team provide personalized guidance and support for your longevity journey. This form is required to be completed by all participants in our Longevity Protocol program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Select the correct options for each field.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Current Weight** (1, number, **Required**):
	+ Please enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Exercise Frequency** (2, select multiple, **Required**):
	+ Choose the frequency of your exercise routine:
		- Daily
		- Weekly
		- Monthly
* **Sleep Hours** (3, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of hours you sleep each night.
* **Diet** (4, select one, **Required**):
	+ Select the best description of your average monthly diet:
		- Yes (mostly healthy diet)
		- No (unhealthy diet)
		- Other (Please specify)
* **Health Insurance** (5, select multiple, **Required**):
	+ Check the box if you have health insurance:
		- Yes
		- No
		- Other (Please specify)
* **Social Media Time** (6, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the average number of hours you spend on social media each day.
* **Fruits and Vegetables** (7, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of servings of fruits and vegetables you consume daily.
* **Chronic Conditions** (8, checkbox, **Required**):
	+ Check the boxes if you have any of the following chronic health conditions:
		- Option A
		- Option B
* **TV Hours** (9, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of hours you spend watching TV each day.
* **Water Intake** (10, select one, **Required**):
	+ Select the accuracy of your average daily water intake:
		- Yes
		- No
		- Other (Please specify)
* **Eating Out** (11, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of times you eat out per week.
* **Allergies** (12, checkbox, **Required**):
	+ Check the boxes if you have any allergies:
		- Option A
		- Option B
* **Work Hours** (13, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of hours you spend on work-related tasks each day.
* **Stress Level** (14, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter your average stress level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Relaxation Hours** (15, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of hours you spend on relaxation activities each day.
* **Sleep Conditions** (16, checkbox, **Required**):
	+ Check the boxes if you have any medical conditions that affect your sleep:
		- Option A
		- Option B
* **Lean Protein Intake** (17, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of servings of lean protein you consume daily.
* **Hobbies** (18, number, **Required**):
	+ Enter the number of hours you spend on hobbies each day.
* **Note** (19, note, **Required**):
	+ Write any additional information or comments you'd like to add.
* **Email** (20, email, **Required**):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (21, text, **Required**):
	+ Enter your phone number.
