# Health And Wellness Center Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health and Wellness Center Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals for their registration. This form is used to gather demographic data, medical history, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To get started, please fill out the form completely, leaving no fields blank.
2. Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. If you are unable to fill out a particular field, please contact the Health and Wellness Center staff for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your full last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Family Members** (`Family Members`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all family members who will be registering.
* **Family Member 1** (`Family Member 1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship of the first family member (e.g., spouse, child, etc.).
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your birth date (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the relationship of your emergency contact person (e.g., spouse, child, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`Emergency Contact Address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your emergency contact person's address.
* **Medical Conditions** (`Medical Conditions`, `text`, required: false): List any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
* **Medications** (`Medications`, `text`, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, required: false): List any allergies you have.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: false): Describe your medical history, including any surgeries, illnesses, or injuries.
* **Medical Notes** (`Medical Notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional medical notes or information.
* **Medical Facilities** (`Medical Facilities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any medical facilities you have visited or are currently receiving treatment from.
* **Medical Specialties** (`Medical Specialties`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select any medical specialties you are receiving treatment from.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the Health and Wellness Center staff for assistance.
* Please submit the form completely before continuing.
