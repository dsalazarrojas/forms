# COVID 19 Vaccine Contact List - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals who have received or are eligible to receive the COVID-19 vaccine. The form collects personal and contact details, health and eligibility information, and vaccination preferences. The information collected will be used to facilitate vaccine distribution and contact individuals about vaccine availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** and **Phone Number** in the respective fields. Make sure your email address is accurate so that you can receive important notifications about vaccine availability.
3. Provide your **Street Address**, **City**, and **State or Province**, and **Zip Code**. This information is necessary for vaccine distribution purposes.
4. Select your **Date of Birth** from the calendar.
5. Choose your **Age Group** from the dropdown menu.
6. If you are a **Healthcare Worker** or an **Essential Worker**, select the corresponding option. If you are an essential worker, please also indicate your **Type of Essential Worker**.
7. If you have **High-Risk Health Conditions**, describe them in the provided field.
8. Let us know if you live with **Vulnerable or High-Risk Individuals**.
9. Select your **Vaccine Type Preference** and **Preferred Appointment Schedule**.
10. Choose your **Preferred Location Type** and indicate if you need **Transportation Assistance**.
11. If you require **Accessibility Accommodations**, provide a description in the provided field.
12. Select your **Preferred Language**.
13. Finally, confirm that you **Consent to be Contacted about vaccine availability**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: The email address where you can be reached for updates on vaccine availability.
* **Phone Number**: Your primary phone number for contacting you.
* **Alternate Phone Number**: Optional secondary phone number for alternative contact methods.
* **Street Address**: The address where you can be reached for vaccine distribution.
* **City**: The city where you can be reached for vaccine distribution.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where you can be reached for vaccine distribution.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code where you can be reached for vaccine distribution.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth as recorded in your identification documents.
* **Age Group**: Your age group as per the dropdown menu.
* **Occupation or Profession**: Optional field for your occupation or profession.
* **Vaccine Type Preference**: Select the type of vaccine you prefer to receive.
* **Preferred Appointment Schedule**: Select your preferred schedule for the vaccine appointment.
* **Preferred Location Type**: Select the type of location where you would like to receive the vaccine.
* **Transportation Need**: Indicate if you require transportation assistance.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Describe any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Preferred Language**: Select your preferred language for communication.
* **Consent to be Contacted**: Confirm that you agree to be contacted about vaccine availability.
