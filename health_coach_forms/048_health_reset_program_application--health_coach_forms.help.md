# Health Reset Program Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Reset Program is designed to support individuals who want to reset their health and well-being. This program aims to provide a comprehensive approach to health and fitness, and our goal is to ensure that each participant is properly qualified and prepared for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide a brief overview of your Health Goals, describing what you hope to achieve through the program.
4. Enter your desired Start Date for the program, if applicable.
5. Add any additional details that you think are relevant to your participation in the program.
6. Identify your preferred Clinic Name for program sessions.
7. If applicable, specify the name of your assigned Trainer.
8. Confirm if you prequalify for the program by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Health Goals**: A text field for you to describe your health goals, such as losing weight, improving fitness, or achieving overall well-being.
* **Start Date**: A date field to select your desired start date for the program, if applicable.
* **Participant Details**: A note field for you to add any additional details that you think are relevant to your participation in the program.
* **Clinic Name**: A text field to select your preferred clinic location for program sessions.
* **Trainer Name**: A text field to enter the name of your assigned trainer, if applicable.
* **Prequalify**: A select-one field to confirm if you prequalify for the program by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth application process.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
