# Health Screening Absence Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Screening Absence Form is designed to capture information about an individual's health screening absence, including their organization name, reason for absence, date of absence, and any additional absence details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your organization's name in the `Organization Name` field. This will help the health screening team identify your institution and ensure they have the correct information.
2. In the `reason_for_absence` field, provide a clear and concise description of why you are absent from health screening. This will help the team understand your current state and plan accordingly.
3. Enter the date you are absent from health screening in the `Date_of_absence` field. Please use the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
4. If you have any additional information or comments about your absence, you can provide them in the `Absence_details` field. This is an optional field for providing extra context.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: This field is where you enter your organization's name. It helps the health screening team identify your institution and ensure they have the correct information.
* **reason_for_absence**: This field is for providing a clear and concise description of why you are absent from health screening. Please be as detailed as possible so the team can understand your current state.
* **Date_of_absence**: This field is for entering the date you are absent from health screening. Please use the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Absence_details**: This optional field is for providing any additional information or comments about your absence. You can use it to give the health screening team extra context if needed.
