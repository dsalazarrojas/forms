# General Health Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect general health appraisal information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide honest answers to all questions.
3. Use the provided options for select type fields, if available.
4. If you are unsure about any questions or answers, please consult a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **User Info** (Text, Optional)
* This field is for providing basic information about yourself.
* Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant details.
### 2. **Medical History** (Text, Optional)
* This field is for providing information about your medical history.
* Please describe any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies you may have.
### 3. **Health Status** (Text, Optional)
* This field is for providing information about your current health status.
* Please describe any current health concerns or issues you may be experiencing.
### 4. **Lifestyle** (Text, Optional)
* This field is for providing information about your lifestyle.
* Please describe your daily habits, exercise routine, and eating habits.
### 5. **Health Coach Info** (Select One, Required)
* This field is for selecting the health coach you are working with.
* Please choose the health coach that is assigned to you.
### 6. **Medical History (Multiple)** (Select Multiple, Required)
* This field is for selecting multiple medical histories from the provided options.
* Please choose all relevant medical histories that apply to you.
### 7. **Medical Info** (Text, Optional)
* This field is for providing additional medical information.
* Please enter any other relevant medical information not listed in the medical history section.
### 8. **Medical History (Select One)** (Select One, Required)
* This field is for selecting a single medical history from the provided options.
* Please choose the medical history that best describes your situation.
