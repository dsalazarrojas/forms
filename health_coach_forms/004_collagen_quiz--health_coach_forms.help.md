# Collagen Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Collagen Quiz is designed to test your knowledge on collagen and its various aspects. This quiz will help you understand your current understanding and assess your interest in collagen supplementation.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions to the best of your knowledge.
2. Select your choice from the provided options for select_one and select_multiple questions.
3. Provide detailed answers for text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`participant_name`, select_one): Enter your full name.
* **Your Email Address** (`participant_email`, select_one): Enter your email address.
* **-- Collagen Supplement Knowledge Assessment --** (`quiz_intro`, text): This is a brief introduction to the questions about collagen.
* **What is collagen primarily composed of** (`q1_collagen_definition`, select_one): Select the correct composition of collagen.
* **Which of these is a known benefit of collagen supplementation** (`q2_collagen_benefits`, select_multiple): Select all the benefits you know of collagen.
* **What are the primary sources of dietary collagen** (`q3_collagen_sources`, select_one): Select the primary sources of collagen.
* **At what age does collagen production naturally decline** (`q4_age_production`, select_one): Select the age at which collagen production declines.
* **How many main types of collagen exist in the human body** (`q5_collagen_types`, select_one): Select the correct number of collagen types.
* **Hydrolyzed collagen has better bioavailability than regular collagen** (`q6_bioavailability`, select_one): Select whether hydrolyzed collagen has better bioavailability.
* **What is a typical recommended daily dose of collagen** (`q7_dosage`, select_one): Select the correct dose of collagen.
* **Collagen supplements interact negatively with most medications** (`q8_interaction`, select_one): Select whether collagen supplements interact negatively with most medications.
* **How long does it typically take to see results from collagen supplementation** (`q9_timeframe`, select_one): Select the time frame for results from collagen supplementation.
* **Collagen can support gut barrier function** (`q10_gut_health`, select_one): Select whether collagen supports gut barrier function.
* **What aspects of collagen supplementation interest you most** (`q11_personal_interest`, select_multiple): Select all the aspects of collagen that interest you.
* **Do you currently use collagen supplements** (`q12_current_use`, select_one): Select whether you currently use collagen supplements.
* **If yes, please share your experience** (`q13_experience_feedback`, text): Optional - describe your results or challenges with collagen supplements.
* **Any specific health goals related to collagen** (`q14_health_concerns`, text): Optional - describe your specific health goals related to collagen.
* **Any additional comments or feedback** (`q15_additional_comments`, text): Optional - provide any additional comments or feedback.

Please provide answers to all questions and submit the form to get your results.
