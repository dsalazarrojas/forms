# Wellness Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from users about their general health and wellness status. This form helps healthcare professionals understand the users' medical history, schedule, wellness goals, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **General Information** section, which includes details about the user's basic demographics.
2. Next, provide **Medical Information**, including any relevant medical conditions or allergies.
3. Describe your **Schedule**, including any regular appointments or routines.
4. Share your **Wellness Information**, discussing your current health and wellness status.
5. Specify your **Goals**, whether personal, fitness, or health-related.
6. Provide **Emergency Contact** information, including their name and contact details.
7. If necessary, provide **Emergency Medical** information, such as medical conditions requiring urgent attention.
8. Enter **Emergency Contact Info**, including the contact person's name and relationship to you.
9. Finally, include any **Additional Info** that you deem relevant to your health and wellness.
10. Sign the form digitally with your **Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, optional): Provide your basic demographics, including your name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): List any relevant medical conditions, allergies, or previous medical procedures.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, text, optional): Describe your regular appointments, routines, or any other relevant scheduling information.
* **Wellness Information** (`wellness_info`, text, optional): Discuss your current health and wellness status, including any concerns or challenges you're facing.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Specify your personal, fitness, or health-related goals, such as weight loss or stress management.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and contact information of a trusted contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Medical** (`emergency_medical`, text, optional): If necessary, provide any medical conditions requiring urgent attention or special care.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and relationship to you.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Include any other relevant information about your health and wellness status.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Sign the form digitally to confirm your completion and agreement to the terms.

## Tips

* Please answer each question truthfully and accurately.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
* Remember to review and revise your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact person listed in the **Emergency Contact Info** field.
