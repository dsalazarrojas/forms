# Yoga Registration And Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Yoga Registration And Waiver Form" is designed to collect essential information from users for the purpose of registering them for yoga programs and classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each section carefully before submitting.
3. Make sure to answer all questions truthfully and provide as much information as possible.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info**: This section is intended to capture your basic contact information and general details about your participation in the yoga program. Please provide your name, which will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Medical Conditions**: Please select any medical conditions that apply to you from the provided options. This information will help us tailor the yoga program to your needs.
* **Waiver**: Please fill out this section with any relevant medical or physical information that may impact your participation in the yoga program.
* **Emergency Contact**: In case of an emergency, please list the contact information for your emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance**: If you have medical insurance, please provide the necessary information to ensure we can support your healthcare needs.
* **Contact Info**: This section is for your convenience and will be used to contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Emergency Phone**: Please provide your emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Address**: Provide the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Profession**: Please indicate your medical profession, if applicable.
* **Medical Record**: Please indicate if you have a medical record or if you have a condition that may impact your participation in the program.
* **Yoga Experience**: If you have prior yoga experience, please indicate the number of years you have practiced.
* **Emergency Note**: Please include any additional emergency contact information or special instructions for your emergency contact.
* **Medical Note**: Please provide any medical information that may be important for our instructors or staff to know.
* **Signature**: Please sign and date this section to acknowledge that you have read and understand the information contained in this form.
* **Signature Date**: Please enter the date you sign this form.

Remember, accuracy and completeness are key to ensuring a safe and successful yoga experience. If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to ask for support.
