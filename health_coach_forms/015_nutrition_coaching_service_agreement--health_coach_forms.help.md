# Nutrition Coaching Service Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Nutrition Coaching Service Agreement" form is a document that clients use to express their agreement to the terms and conditions of the nutrition coaching service provided by the health coach. It ensures that the client understands and acknowledges the service's terms, including their responsibilities and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the "Nutrition Coaching Service Agreement" form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the information required.
2.  Fill in the "client_details" field with your personal details, as specified in the form.
3.  Indicate your goals regarding the nutrition coaching service in the "goals" field.
4.  Select whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the service in the "terms" field.
5.  You may be asked to provide a digital signature in the "e_signature" field.
6.  Confirm your understanding and consent to the terms in the "terms_2" field.
7.  Finally, review your information in the "client_details_2" and "client_details_3" fields, which will provide a summary of your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### client_details
*   **client_details** (`text`, required: `false`): This field is used to capture the client's personal details. It is not a required field.

### goals
*   **goals** (`select_multiple`, required: `false`): In this field, please select the goals you aim to achieve through the nutrition coaching service. This can include improving overall health, losing weight, or maintaining a balanced diet.

### terms
*   **terms** (`select_one`, required: `false`): Here, you need to select whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the service. Select "Agreed" to indicate your consent.

### e_signature
*   **e_signature** (`text`, required: `false`): This field is intended for a digital signature, which may be required as part of the agreement process.

### terms_2
*   **terms_2** (`note`, required: `false`): In this field, please provide a note confirming your understanding and agreement to the terms and conditions of the service.

### client_details_2
*   **client_details_2** (`select_multiple`, required: `false`): This field provides an opportunity to confirm your understanding of your agreement. Select "Yes" to confirm.

### client_details_3
*   **client_details_3** (`select_multiple`, required: `false`): Another field to confirm your understanding and consent to the terms and conditions of the service.

### Tips
*   Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
*   Double-check your answers to ensure you're accurately expressing your agreement.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
