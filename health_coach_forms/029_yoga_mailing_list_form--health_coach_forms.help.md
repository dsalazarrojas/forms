# yoga_mailing_list_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from individuals interested in joining a yoga mailing list. Please fill out the form below to join our list and receive updates on upcoming yoga events and promotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your information. All fields are optional, but please fill out at least the Email field to ensure you can receive updates from us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if you wish to be recognized by your first name in our list.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, if you wish to be recognized by your last name in our list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications from us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter your email address correctly, as we will use it to send you updates and notifications.
* If you experience any issues with the form, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
