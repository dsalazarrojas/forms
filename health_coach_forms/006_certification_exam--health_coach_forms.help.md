# Certification Exam - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from exam-takers for the certification program. This information includes personal details, exam-taking environment, and answers to questions related to the certification program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you have answered all required fields.
3. Take your time to answer the questions carefully and to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exam Taker Name** (`exam_taker_name`, text, required): This field is used to capture your full legal name.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required): This field is used to record the date you took the exam.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, required): This field is used to note the start time of the exam.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): This field is used to collect your contact email for exam results.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is used to collect your contact phone number (if you have one).
* **Candidate ID Number** (`candidate_id`, text, required): This field is used to capture the exam identifier provided to you.
* **Certification Program** (`certification_program`, select_one, required): This field is used to select which certification program you are taking the exam for.
* **Number of Previous Attempts** (`previous_attempts`, number, optional): This field is used to track how many times you have taken this exam before.
* **Question 1 - Client Assessment Principles** (`question_1`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of client assessment principles.
* **Question 2 - Nutrition Fundamentals** (`question_2`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of nutrition fundamentals.
* **Question 3 - Behavior Change Strategies** (`question_3`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of behavior change strategies.
* **Question 4 - Exercise Recommendations** (`question_4`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of exercise recommendations.
* **Question 5 - Professional Ethics** (`question_5`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of professional ethics.
* **Question 6 - Client Communication** (`question_6`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of client communication.
* **Question 7 - Goal Setting Frameworks** (`question_7`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of goal setting frameworks.
* **Question 8 - Health Assessment Tools** (`question_8`, select_one, required): This question assesses your understanding of health assessment tools.
* **Testing Environment** (`exam_environment`, select_one, optional): This field is used to note where you are taking this exam (e.g., Home, Testing Center, Workplace, Educational Institution).
* **Testing Accommodations Needed** (`accommodations_needed`, select_one, optional): This field is used to identify any special accommodations you may need to complete the exam.
* **I Attess I am Taking This Exam Independently** (`exam_attestation`, select_one, required): This question confirms that you are taking the exam independently.
