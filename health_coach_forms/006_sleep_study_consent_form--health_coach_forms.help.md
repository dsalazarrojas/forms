# Sleep Study Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Sleep Study Consent Form" is a form used to collect information about a patient's sleep study participation. The form helps patients understand their rights and responsibilities as part of the sleep study and gather their consent for the study's procedures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, address, phone number, and email address (if applicable) in the "Patient Information" page.
2. Provide details about the sleep study you are participating in, including the start and end dates, on the "Study Details" page.
3. Review the "Risks And Benefits" section to understand the potential risks and benefits associated with the sleep study.
4. Sign the "Patient Informed Consent" page to confirm you have read and understood the information provided.
5. Sign the "Patient Signature" page to confirm your consent to participate in the sleep study.
6. Sign the "Health Coach Signature" page to confirm the health coach's acknowledgment of your consent.
7. Date and time of the sleep study should be filled out on the "Date" and "Time" pages.
8. Fill out the "Study Start Date" and "Study End Date" pages with the relevant dates.
