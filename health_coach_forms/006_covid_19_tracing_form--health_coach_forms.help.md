# Covid 19 Tracing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Covid 19 Tracing Form is a guide to help individuals complete a form that was created by an AI using public survey metadata on Covid-19 topics. This form is intended to gather information related to contact tracing and medical history, which can be useful for public health officials to track and manage the spread of the virus. It is recommended to only fill out this form if you are a medical professional or have a legitimate reason for doing so.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form by providing information in the text boxes as prompted.
2.  Be as accurate as possible when answering the questions.
3.  You can skip sections that don't apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contact Information** (`contact_info_form`): Enter your name and contact information.
    Please provide your name and a way for the authorities to contact you.
*   **Symptoms** (`symptoms_form`): Describe your symptoms related to Covid-19.
    Please list any symptoms you are currently experiencing or experienced in the past.
*   **Test Results** (`test_result_form`): Report any test results you have for Covid-19.
    Enter lab results or other documentation of your test results.
*   **Contacts** (`contact_form`): List any people you have had close contact with in the past 14 days.
    Include names, phone numbers, and any other relevant details.
*   **Travel History** (`travel_form`): Describe any recent travel history or plans.
    Include dates, destinations, and any other relevant details.
*   **Medical History** (`medical_form`): Describe your medical history, especially if you have any underlying conditions.
    Include any relevant medical information that may be useful for your care.
*   **Exposure History** (`exposure_form`): Describe any recent exposure to potential sources of the virus.
    Include dates, locations, and any other relevant details.
*   **Quarantine History** (`quarantine_form`): Describe any time you spent in quarantine.
    Include dates, reasons, and any other relevant details.
*   **Contact Tracing** (`contact_trace_form`): Provide information for contact tracing.
    This is used to track individuals who have been in close contact with you.
*   **Follow-up Info** (`follow_up_form`): Leave any additional information for follow-up.
    You can include any relevant details that will help with your care.
*   **Prophets (Medical Provider)** (`medical_provider_form`): Report your healthcare provider or contact.
    Include their name, phone number, and any other relevant details.

## Tips

*   Be as detailed as possible when answering the questions.
*   If you are unsure of any information, please ask for help.
