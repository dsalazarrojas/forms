# Covid 19 Survey Questionnaire Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from individuals about their COVID-19 experiences and concerns. It helps healthcare professionals understand the virus's impact and inform public health strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to provide your contact information (email or phone number) so that we can follow up with you if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Concerns** (`covid_concerns`, `text`, required: false): Describe your concerns or experiences related to COVID-19.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Date of Vaccination** (`date_of_vaccination`, `date`, required: false): If you have been vaccinated against COVID-19, enter the date of your vaccination in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022).

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible when answering the concerns field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* If you have already been contacted by us regarding this form, please do not fill it out again.
