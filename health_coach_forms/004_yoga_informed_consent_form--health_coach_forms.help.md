# Yoga Informed Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to ensure that participants in a yoga session or practice provide their informed consent and provide necessary contact and emergency information. It also gathers data on potential medical conditions, activity risks, and participant understanding of the session or practice.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Participant Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Contact Information** in the next field, if applicable.
3. If applicable, provide your **Contact Relationship** details.
4. List any **Medical Condition** that may affect your practice.
5. Provide **Emergency Contact** information, if necessary.
6. Check the **Informed Consent** box to indicate you have read and understand the participant agreement.
7. Write down your **Participant Understanding** of the session or practice.
8. Acknowledge any **Activity Risks** associated with the practice.
9. Enter the **Consent Date** and time in the respective fields using the "YYYY-MM-DD" and "HH:MM AM/PM" formats.
10. Sign with your **Participant Signature** and date in the last two fields.
11. If a witness is present, enter their **Witness Name**, **Signature**, **Title**, and **Company** details, if applicable.
12. Sign with the **Witness Signature** and date in the last two fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to during the session or practice.
* **Contact Information**: If you have any contact information you'd like to share, such as a phone number or email, please enter it here.
* **Contact Relationship**: If the contact person is a parent, guardian, or other representative, please indicate their relationship to you.
* **Medical Condition**: List any medical conditions that may affect your practice, such as injuries or illnesses.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Informed Consent**: Check this box to indicate you have read and understand the participant agreement.
* **Participant Understanding**: Write down a brief description of what you understand about the session or practice.
* **Activity Risks**: Acknowledge any risks associated with the practice, such as physical strains or allergic reactions.
* **Consent Date** (YYYY-MM-DD): Enter the date when you gave informed consent.
* **Consent Time** (HH:MM AM/PM): Enter the time when you gave informed consent.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign with your name and date in the signature fields.
* **Participant Signature Date** (YYYY-MM-DD): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Participant Signature Time** (HH:MM AM/PM): Enter the time of your signature.
* **Witness Name**: If a witness is present, enter their name.
* **Witness Signature**: Sign with their name and date in the signature fields, if applicable.
* **Witness Signature Date** (YYYY-MM-DD): Enter the date of their signature, if applicable.
* **Witness Signature Time** (HH:MM AM/PM): Enter the time of their signature, if applicable.
* **Witness Title**: If a witness is present, enter their title or position.
* **Witness Company**: If a witness is present, enter their company or organization.
* **Witness Address**: If a witness is present, enter their company or organization address.
* **Participant Signature Date**: Ensure your signature date is accurate and consistent with the time of your signature.
* **Participant Signature Time**: Ensure your signature time is accurate and consistent with the date of your signature.
* **Participant Signature**: Your signature is a critical part of the agreement; ensure it is legible and accurate.
* **Consent Date**: Ensure the consent date is accurate and in the correct format.
* **Consent Time**: Ensure the consent time is accurate and in the correct format.

Note: The witness fields are optional and only required in cases where a witness is necessary to validate the participant's signature.
