# diet_consultation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to schedule a consultation with a health coach.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select your availability for the consultation.
3. Choose your preferred start time for the consultation.
4. Choose the duration of the consultation.
5. Enter any additional information you would like to provide to the health coach.
6. Provide your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Leave any notes for the health coach.
9. If applicable, assign a specific user to be present during the consultation.
10. Specify if you want to assign a specific time for the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details to help the health coach prepare for the consultation.
* **Consultation Details** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose and goals of the consultation.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your contact information so that the health coach can follow up after the consultation.
* **Availability** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select a day of the week when you are available for the consultation.
	+ Options: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday
* **Start Time** (`id: 5`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Choose the start time for the consultation.
	+ Options: 9:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM
* **Consultation Duration** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the duration of the consultation.
	+ Options: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 60 minutes
* **Additional Information** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide any additional information that you think is relevant to the consultation.
* **Email** (`id: 8`, `type: email`, required: false): Enter your email address so that the health coach can contact you after the consultation.
* **Phone** (`id: 9`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your phone number so that the health coach can contact you after the consultation.
* **Notes** (`id: 10`, `type: note`, required: false): Leave any notes for the health coach to refer to during the consultation.
* **Assigned User** (`id: 11`, `type: select_one`, required: false): If applicable, assign a specific user to be present during the consultation.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
* **Assigned Time** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: false): If applicable, assign a specific time for the consultation.
	+ Options: 'Yes', 'No'
