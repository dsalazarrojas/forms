# COVID 19 Precaution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to outline and record precaution measures for individuals or organizations to prevent the spread of COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Ensure you are filling out the form for the correct entity (individual or organization) implementing the precautions.
2. Fill out the required fields:
   - **Entity name**: Provide the name of the individual or organization.
   - **Precaution measures**: Describe the physical distancing, cleaning, and PPE protocols being implemented.
   - **Training completed**: Indicate if staff training has been completed.
   - **Monitoring plan**: Describe the method for monitoring compliance.
   - **Contact for concerns**: Provide a contact phone or email for reporting any concerns.
   - **Review date**: Enter the next scheduled review date of the precaution plan.
3. Fill out the optional fields if applicable:
   - **Responsible person**: The name of the individual overseeing the implementation of precautions.
   - **Next review date**: Enter the next scheduled review date of the precaution plan.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entity name**: Enter the name of the individual or organization implementing precautions.
* **Precaution measures**: Describe the physical distancing, cleaning, and PPE protocols being implemented.
* **Training completed**: Check if staff training has been completed.
* **Monitoring plan**: Describe the method for monitoring compliance.
* **Contact for concerns**: Provide a contact phone or email for reporting any concerns.
* **Review date**: Enter the next scheduled review date of the precaution plan.
* **Responsible person**: The name of the individual overseeing the implementation of precautions.
* **Next review date**: Enter the next scheduled review date of the precaution plan.
* **Acknowledgment**: Select 'Acknowledged' to confirm agreement to follow the outlined precautions.
* **Signature name**: Type your name to serve as electronic signature.

## Tips

- Please ensure to fill out all required fields for a complete and accurate record.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact the designated contact for concerns.
- This form is a guide and may be subject to change as policies and regulations evolve.
- Review the form regularly to ensure it remains in line with your organization's COVID-19 policies and guidelines.
