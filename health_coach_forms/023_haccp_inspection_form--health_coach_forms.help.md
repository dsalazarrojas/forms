# HACCP Inspection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HACCP Inspection Form is designed to help food establishments identify and control potential hazards in their operations. This form guides the completion of a thorough self-inspection to ensure food safety and prevent contamination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully, making sure to answer every question.
2. Answer questions based on your current operations and practices.
3. Check "Yes" or provide a detailed explanation for each question.
4. Record any observations, issues, or concerns that arise during the inspection.
5. Review and revise your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Food Safety Protocols** (id: 1): Describe your food safety protocols in place, including procedures for handling, storage, and disposal of food.
* **Food Storage** (id: 2): Report on the current state of your food storage area, including temperature control, cleanliness, and pest control.
* **Hazard Analysis** (id: 3): Identify potential hazards in your food operations, such as allergens, chemicals, or pests.
* **Temperature Control** (id: 4): Record the current temperature range of your food storage and preparation areas.
* **Water Quality** (id: 5): Report on the quality of the water used for food preparation and cleaning, choosing between "Acceptable" and "Unacceptable".
* **Equipment Cleaning** (id: 6): Indicate whether your equipment is cleaned regularly, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Chemical Use** (id: 7): Record the date of the last chemical use, if any.
* **Employee Training** (id: 8): Report on whether employees have received training on food safety and handling, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Emergency Procedure** (id: 9): Record the current emergency procedure in place, including procedures for spills and equipment failures.
* **First Aid Kit** (id: 10): Report on the availability and accessibility of first aid kits.
* **Waste Disposal** (id: 11): Describe your waste disposal procedures, including storage and removal.
* **Food Labeling** (id: 12): Indicate whether food labels are correctly applied, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Pest Control** (id: 13): Report on the effectiveness of pest control measures, choosing between "Acceptable" and "Unacceptable".
* **Supplier Control** (id: 14): Describe your procedures for controlling suppliers, including quality and food safety checks.
* **Employee Hydration** (id: 15): Report on the availability of water for employees, including water quality and accessibility.
* **Employee Break Area** (id: 16): Indicate whether there is a designated break area for employees, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Employee PPE** (id: 17): Report on the availability and use of personal protective equipment (PPE), choosing between "Acceptable" and "Unacceptable".
* **Employee Hand Washing** (id: 18): Indicate whether employees wash their hands regularly, choosing between "Yes" and "No".
* **Food Storage Area** (id: 19): Describe the current state of your food storage area, including cleanliness and organization.
* **Food Receiving** (id: 20): Report on the procedures for receiving and inspecting incoming food deliveries.
* **Food Handling** (id: 21): Describe your food handling procedures, including handling and storage.
* **Food Preparation** (id: 22): Report on the procedures for preparing food, including utensils and equipment sanitation.
* **Food Service** (id: 23): Describe the procedures for serving food, including food display and presentation.
* **Food Removal** (id: 24): Report on the procedures for removing and disposing of food waste.
* **Employee HVAC** (id: 25): Indicate whether there is a functioning HVAC system in place, including temperature and humidity control.

## Tips
- Complete this form regularly to maintain a safe and sanitary environment.
- Review and revise your answers based on changes in your operations and procedures.
- Record any observations, issues, or concerns that arise during the inspection.
- Use this form as a reference for future self-inspections and training.
