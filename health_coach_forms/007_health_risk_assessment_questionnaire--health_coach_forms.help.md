# health_risk_assessment_questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a patient's health status and risk factors for better guidance and support from healthcare providers or health coaches.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's details in the "Patient Details" page.
2. Select all applicable risk factors from the "Risk Factors" page. You can select multiple risk factors.
3. Write down any next steps or recommendations in the "Next Steps" page. This is an optional field.
4. Provide your contact information in the "Contact" page.
5. If necessary, provide additional family information in the "Family" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Details** (`patient_details`, text, optional): Please enter the patient's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
- **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable risk factors that apply to the patient. These include:
  - Smoking
  - Excessive drinking
  - Poor diet
  - Physical inactivity
  - Lack of sleep
  - Family history of cardiovascular disease
  - Family history of cancer
  - Family history of diabetes
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, note, optional): Write down any next steps or recommendations for the patient.
- **Contact** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your contact information so that the patient can get back to you for follow-up.
- **Family** (`family_info`, text, optional): If applicable, provide additional family information that may be relevant to the patient's health status.
