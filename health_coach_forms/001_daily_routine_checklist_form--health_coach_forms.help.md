# Daily Routine Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Routine Checklist Form is a tool to help you monitor and evaluate your daily habits and activities. By filling out this form, you'll be able to track your progress and identify areas where you can make positive changes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on each page by typing in the corresponding field.
2. Select all that apply for the "Goals" field if you have multiple objectives you'd like to achieve.
3. Enter a numerical value (e.g., 0-100) for fields related to sleep quality, water intake, and stress level.
4. Add any additional notes or thoughts in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Morning activities
* The "Morning activities" field is a text field where you can list the activities you do in the morning, such as exercise, meditation, or reading.

### Exercise
* This field is for you to report the amount and type of exercise you did today.

### Sleep Quality
* Rate your sleep quality on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 is very poor and 100 is excellent.

### Water Intake
* Record the amount of water you drank today, with 0 being very low and 100 being excellent.

### Daily Routine
* Describe your daily routine, including any notable events or activities that occurred.

### Goals
* Select all the goals you're currently working towards. This can include exercise goals, health objectives, or personal milestones.

### Stress Level
* Report your stress level on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 is very low and 100 is very high.

### Energy Level
* Rate your energy level on a scale from 0 to 100, where 0 is very low and 100 is very high.

### Notes
* Add any additional comments or thoughts about your day, such as successes, challenges, or areas for improvement.
