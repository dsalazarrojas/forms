# Health Client Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your health clients.
## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by filling in the required and optional fields as accurately as possible.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): Describe your client's medical history, including any significant health events or conditions that may impact their health.
*   **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of the following health conditions that your client has:
    +   Idiopathic: A condition that cannot be explained by known causes.
    +   Genetic: A condition that is inherited from your genes.
    +   Infection: A condition caused by an invading organism or agent.
    +   Allergies: A condition caused by an abnormal reaction to a substance.
    +   Other: Select 'Other' if your client has any other health conditions not listed above.
*   **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the allergens your client is allergic to:
    +   Penicillin
    +   Ibuprofen
    +   Aspirin
    +   Other: Select 'Other' if your client has any other allergens not listed above.
*   **Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List all the medications your client is currently taking.
*   **Medical Providers** (`medical_providers`, `select_one`, optional): Select the healthcare provider your client sees for medical care:
    +   Dr Smith
    +   Dr Johnson
    +   Other: Select 'Other' if your client sees any other healthcare provider not listed above.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Fill in your client's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
*   **Birthplace** (`birthplace`, `text`, optional): Fill in your client's birthplace.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Fill in the name and contact information for your client's emergency contact person or contact number.
