# Coaching Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Evaluation Form is used to provide feedback on a coaching session, allowing clients to share their experience and coaches to assess their performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review your coaching session and reflect on your experience.
2. Answer each question honestly, providing detailed and specific feedback.
3. Ensure you complete all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the coach who provided services during your session.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the coaching session occurred.
* **Session Duration - minutes** (`session_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of the coaching session.
* **Primary Focus Area of Coaching** (`coaching_focus`, select_one, required): Select the main topic or goal of the coaching session (e.g., Fitness, Nutrition, Mental Health, etc.).
* **Did the coach clearly explain the coaching goals** (`goal_clarity`, select_one, required): Rate how clear the coach's explanation of goals was, using the scale: 1 - Not at all, 2 - Somewhat unclear, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Clear, 5 - Very clear.
* **Coach Knowledge and Expertise** (`coach_knowledge`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's subject knowledge, using the scale: 1 - Poor, 2 - Fair, 3 - Good, 4 - Very Good, 5 - Excellent.
* **Quality of Communication** (`communication_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the coach's listening and communication skills, using the scale: 1 - Poor, 2 - Fair, 3 - Good, 4 - Very Good, 5 - Excellent.
* **Did you receive actionable guidance and next steps?** (`actionable_guidance`, select_one, required): Rate the practicality of recommendations, using the scale: 1 - Not applicable, 2 - Vague guidance, 3 - Some guidance, 4 - Clear guidance, 5 - Very clear action plan.
* **Value for Money** (`value_for_money`, select_one, required): Rate how you feel about the value of the session, using the scale: 1 - Poor value, 2 - Below average, 3 - Average, 4 - Good value, 5 - Excellent value.
* **Overall Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one, required): Rate your overall satisfaction with the coaching session, using the scale: 1 - Very unsatisfied, 2 - Unsatisfied, 3 - Neutral, 4 - Satisfied, 5 - Very satisfied.
* **Would you recommend this coach to others?** (`would_recommend`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would recommend this coach to others.
* **Brief reason for your recommendation** (`recommend_reason`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for your recommendation.
* **Would you book another session with this coach?** (`future_coaching`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you would consider booking another session with this coach.
* **What were the coach's greatest strengths?** (`strengths`, text, optional): Describe what worked well during the session.
* **Areas for improvement** (`improvement_areas`, text, optional): Suggest areas where the coach could improve.
* **Brief reason for areas for improvement** (no label, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for areas of improvement. 

This guide will help you complete the Coaching Evaluation Form effectively and accurately, providing valuable feedback to coaches and enhancing your experience.
