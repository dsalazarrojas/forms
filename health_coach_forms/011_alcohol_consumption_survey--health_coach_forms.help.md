# Alcohol Consumption Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Alcohol Consumption Survey" is a questionnaire designed to collect data on an individual's alcohol consumption habits, demographics, and related behaviors. The form is intended to help health professionals, researchers, or organizations understand the impact of alcohol consumption on individuals and society, and to inform health programs or interventions aimed at promoting healthier drinking habits.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Participant ID**: Enter your unique identifier for this survey. This will help us to track your progress and results.
2. **Survey Date**: Enter the date you completed this survey.
3. **Age**: Please enter your current age.
4. **Gender**: Select your gender from the options provided.
5. **Occupation** (Optional): If you're comfortable sharing, please enter your current occupation.
6. **How Often Do You Drink Alcohol?**: Select the frequency of your alcohol consumption.
7. **Typical Number of Drinks Per Occasion**: Choose the average number of drinks you consume on a typical drinking occasion.
8. **Preferred Type of Alcohol** (Optional): Select all the types of alcohol you prefer.
9. **Where Do You Usually Drink?** (Optional): Select all the locations where you usually drink.
10. **Who Do You Usually Drink With?** (Optional): Select all the individuals you usually drink with.
11. **Binge Drinking Frequency** (Required): Select how often you engage in binge drinking.
12. **Monthly Alcohol Spending** (Optional): Estimate your monthly alcohol expenditure.
13. **Awareness of Health Impact** (Required): Choose your level of awareness of the health effects of alcohol consumption.
14. **Have You Tried to Reduce Consumption?** (Optional): Select your experience with attempting to reduce your alcohol consumption.
15. **Alcohol-Related Problems** (Optional): Select all the problems you've experienced related to alcohol consumption in the past year.
16. **Family History of Alcoholism** (Optional): Indicate if you have a family history of alcoholism.
17. **Do You Drink When Stressed?** (Required): Choose how often you drink when you're stressed.
18. **Does Alcohol Affect Your Sleep?** (Optional): Select the impact of alcohol on your sleep quality.
19. **Exercise Frequency** (Optional): Choose how often you engage in physical exercise.
20. **Overall Health Rating** (Required): Rate your current health status.
21. **Willingness to Change Drinking Habits** (Optional): Select your willingness to change your drinking habits.
22. **Additional Comments** (Optional): Provide any additional information you'd like to share.
