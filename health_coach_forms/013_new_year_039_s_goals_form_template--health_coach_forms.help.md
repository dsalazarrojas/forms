# New Year's Goals Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year's Goals Form Template is designed to help you set and track your goals for the upcoming year. This form will guide you through the process of defining your goals, categorizing them, and setting target dates and frequencies. By using this template, you can create a clear plan for achieving your objectives and make progress throughout the year.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **New Year's Goals Category**: Select a category for your goal from the provided options (e.g., Fitness, Health, Financial).
2.  **New Year's Goal Description**: Enter a brief description of your goal in the text field.
3.  **Goal Category**: Choose a category for your goal from the provided options (e.g., Career, Education, Personal).
4.  **Goal Description**: Enter a brief description of your goal in the text field.
5.  **Target Month**: Select the month when you want to achieve your goal.
6.  **Target Year**: Enter the year you want to achieve your goal.
7.  **How Often**: Choose how often you plan to work on your goal (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
8.  **Goal Frequency**: This field is currently redundant with "How Often" and can be ignored.
9.  **Goal Duration**: Enter the time frame you plan to achieve your goal (e.g., 1 week, 1 month, 1 year).
10. **Goal Status**: Mark the status of your goal as In Progress, Completed, or Abandoned.
11.  **Goal Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your goal.
12.  **Review Date**: Set a date for reviewing your progress on this goal.
13.  **Review Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your progress on this goal.
14.  **Review Status**: Mark the status of your goal review as On Track, Off Track, or Review.
15.  **Goals**: Select which of the provided options apply to your goal (Yes or No).

Please fill out the form by selecting a category, describing your goal, and setting a target date and frequency. You can add any additional notes or comments as needed. Remember to review your progress and update your goal status accordingly.

## Tips

*   Make sure to choose specific and achievable goals.
*   Set realistic target dates and frequencies.
*   Regularly review your progress and update your goal status.
*   Be honest with yourself about your goal status and progress.
