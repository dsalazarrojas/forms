# Hydromassage Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from clients before receiving a hydromassage session. Please take the time to understand the purpose of each field and what we need from you to ensure your comfort and well-being during the session.

## How To Complete This Form
Before filling out the form, please note that all fields marked as "required" are essential to the completion of your session. The following steps will guide you through the form:

- Fill out all the required fields (marked as "true").
- Enter the best possible information in optional fields if you feel it's relevant to your situation.
- Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Information (ID 1)
* **Form Information**: This field is not required, but please enter a brief message if you have any specific concerns or medical conditions we should be aware of.

### Client Name (ID 2)
* **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or government-issued document. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.

### Phone Number (ID 3)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number where you can be reached for any updates or emergency situations.

### Email (ID 4)
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive updates or reminders about your session.

### Address (ID 5)
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.

### Emergency Contact (ID 6)
* **Emergency Contact**: Select whether you have an emergency contact person. If yes, please provide their name and phone number.

### Emergency Contact Name (ID 7)
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person you selected in the previous field.

### Relationship (ID 8)
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, sibling).

### Doctor Contact (ID 9)
* **Doctor Contact**: Select whether you have a doctor or medical professional you'd like to notify in case of an emergency. If yes, please provide their name and phone number.

### Doctor Contact Name (ID 10)
* **Doctor Contact Name**: Enter the name of the doctor or medical professional you selected in the previous field.

### Relationship to Client (ID 11)
* **Relationship to Client**: Describe your relationship with the doctor or medical professional.

### Medical Conditions (ID 12)
* **Medical Conditions**: Please list any medical conditions or allergies you have that we should be aware of.

### Massage Details (ID 13)
* **Massage Details**: Describe the type of massage you'd like to receive, or any specific areas of focus you'd like the provider to target.

### Date of Massage (ID 14)
* **Date of Massage**: Select the date and time you'd like to receive the massage.

### Time of Massage (ID 15)
* **Time of Massage**: Select the time you'd like to receive the massage.

### Length of Massage (ID 16)
* **Length of Massage**: Describe the length of time you'd like the massage to last.

### Frequency of Massage (ID 17)
* **Frequency of Massage**: Select whether you'd like to receive massages regularly or occasionally.

### Client Signature (ID 18)
* **Client Signature**: Please sign your name to acknowledge you've read and understand the liability waiver.

### Provider Signature (ID 19)
* **Provider Signature**: The provider will sign to confirm they've informed you about the risks and benefits of the massage therapy.

### Date (ID 20)
* **Date**: The date you signed this form, which is the date of the massage session.

### Time (ID 21)
* **Time**: The time you signed this form, which is the scheduled time of the massage session.

### Provider Name (ID 22)
* **Provider Name**: Enter the name of the person who will be performing the massage.

### Provider License Number (ID 23)
* **Provider License Number**: Enter the license number of the provider.

### Provider Address (ID 24)
* **Provider Address**: Enter the address of the provider.

### Provider License Type (ID 25)
* **Provider License Type**: Describe the type of license held by the provider (e.g., LMT, LMT-CP).
