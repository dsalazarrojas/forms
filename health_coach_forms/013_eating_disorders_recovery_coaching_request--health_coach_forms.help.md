# Eating Disorders Recovery Coaching Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from clients seeking support for eating disorders recovery coaching. The information collected will be used by recovery coaches to understand the client's needs and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide a brief description of your background and how you are seeking support for your eating disorders.
2. Clearly state your goals for seeking recovery coaching. What do you hope to achieve through this process?
3. If you have specific inquiries or concerns that you would like to address with a recovery coach, please provide them here.
4. Enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number. This will help us to get in touch with you.
5. If you have a preferred recovery coach type, select it from the options provided. If you are unsure, don't worry, we can guide you through the selection process.
6. If you have selected "Other (please specify)" as your preferred recovery coach type, please provide more information about your needs and preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Background**: A brief description of your background, including any relevant medical information, dietary preferences, or previous experiences with eating disorders.
* **Goals**: A clear and concise statement of your goals for seeking recovery coaching. What do you hope to achieve through this process?
* **Coaching Inquiries**: Specific questions or concerns you would like to address with a recovery coach.
* **Contact Details**: Your email address and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Email**: Your email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number.
* **Recovery Coach**: The type of recovery coach you prefer to work with. Please note that if you are unsure, you can select "Eating Disorder Recovery Coach" and we will guide you through the selection process.
* **Other (please specify)**: If you have selected "Other (please specify)" as your preferred recovery coach type, please provide more information about your needs and preferences.
* **Recovery Coach**: If you have selected "Other (please specify)" as your preferred recovery coach type, please specify the type of coach you are looking for (e.g. "Nutritionist", "Mental Health Specialist", etc.).
* **Other (please specify)**: If you have selected "Other (please specify)" as your preferred recovery coach type, please provide more information about your needs and preferences.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when providing information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to reach out to us for guidance.
* The information collected will be kept confidential and only used to provide you with the most suitable recovery coach.
