# Health Coaching Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Health Coaching Questionnaire is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about an individual's overall health status, habits, and awareness. This form helps health coaches to better understand their clients' or patients' health concerns, goals, and behaviors, enabling them to provide more effective guidance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form's questions and understand what each section is asking you to do.
2. Answer each question according to your personal experience and knowledge.
3. Be as accurate and honest as possible when providing your answers.
4. If you're unsure about any question, ask your health coach for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Health Concerns** (`health_concerns`, text, optional): This question invites you to describe any concerns or issues you're currently facing regarding your overall health. Be as detailed as possible, and try to be objective.
* **Health Coaching Goals** (`health_coaching_goals`, text, optional): This question is about your goals for health coaching. What do you hope to achieve through this coaching process? Describe your expectations and objectives.
* **Medical Conditions (Check all that apply)** (`medical_conditions`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Hypertension**: Do you have high blood pressure or concerns about high blood pressure?
	+ **Diabetes**: Do you have diabetes or concerns about your blood sugar levels?
	+ **Heart Disease**: Do you have heart disease or concerns about your heart health?
	+ **Other**: Describe any other medical conditions you're concerned about.
* **Sleep Patterns** (`sleep_patters`, number, optional): How often do you sleep in a 24-hour period? Please enter the number of hours you sleep each day.
* **Exercise Frequently** (`exercise_frequently`, select one, optional): Do you exercise regularly? Select 'yes' if you exercise frequently, 'no' otherwise.
* **Healthy Diet** (`healthy_diet`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Do you maintain a healthy diet?
	+ **No**: Do you have concerns about your diet?
* **Smoking Status** (`smoking_status`, select one, optional):
	+ **Current Smoker**: Are you a smoker?
	+ **Former Smoker**: Have you quit smoking?
	+ **Non-Smoker**: Have you never smoked.
* **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Medication**: Do you take medication for stress management?
	+ **Mindfulness**: Do you practice mindfulness for stress management?
	+ **Other**: Describe any other methods you use for stress management.
* **Mental Health Awareness** (`mental_awareness`, text, optional): This question is about your current mental health awareness. Describe any issues or concerns you're experiencing.
* **Physical Health Awareness** (`physical_awareness`, text, optional): This question is about your physical health awareness. Describe any concerns or issues you're experiencing.
* **Goal Setting** (`goal_setting`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Do you set goals for your health and wellness?
	+ **No**: Do you need help setting health goals?
* **Health Monitoring** (`health_monitoring`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Do you regularly monitor your health?
	+ **No**: Do you need help monitoring your health?
* **Health Finances** (`health_finance`, number, optional): How much money do you spend on health-related expenses each month? Please enter the number.
* **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, select multiple, optional):
	+ **Yes**: Do you have health insurance?
	+ **No**: Do you need help finding health insurance?
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): This question is about your current medication list. Describe any medications you're taking.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when answering the questions.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask your health coach for guidance.
* Review the form's questions and understand what each section is asking you to do.
* Take your time to answer each question carefully and thoughtfully.
