# Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from you to tailor our coaching services to your needs. Please provide accurate and complete responses to ensure a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section with your details.
2. Select the program you are interested in from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred start date and commitment length for the program.
4. Rate your experience level and select your primary fitness or performance goal.
5. If applicable, describe any specific goals or challenges you wish to address.
6. Provide health information to help us tailor the program to your needs.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions, including the liability waiver.
8. Enter emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your contact number for class updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth for liability purposes.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender.
* **Program** (`program_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the coaching or class type you are interested in.
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose your preferred start date for the program.
* **Program Commitment Length** (`commitment_length`, `select_one`, required): Choose the program duration you plan to participate in.
* **Your experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Rate your current fitness or skill level.
* **Fitness or performance goals** (`fitness_goals`, `select_one`, required): Select your primary fitness or performance goal.
* **Specific goals or challenges** (`specific_goals`, `text`, optional): Describe your specific goals or challenges you wish to address.
* **Medical conditions or injuries** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Inform us of any relevant medical conditions or injuries.
* **Medications** (`medications`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are currently taking any relevant medications.
* **Medical clearance for exercise** (`physician_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have medical clearance for exercise.
* **Agree to terms and conditions** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Select "I agree" to register and confirm understanding of the terms.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
