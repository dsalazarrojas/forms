# Coronavirus Awareness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather insights from end-users about their thoughts and experiences with the coronavirus, its risks, and prevention methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Answer each question according to your thoughts and experiences with the coronavirus.
- For the fields with multiple-choice options, select one or more options that best describe your thoughts.
- For the date field, enter the date you believe the risk of transmission is higher.
- For the time field, enter the time of day you believe the risk of transmission is higher.
- For the free-text field, type any additional information or notes you have about the transmission of coronaviruses.
- Make sure to scroll through each page to review and edit your answers if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What are your thoughts about the risk of coronavirus?** (Page 1)
	+ This field is for you to express your general thoughts about the risk of coronavirus. Please provide your honest opinion.
* **What do you think is the biggest risk factor?** (Page 2)
	+ This question asks you to identify the biggest risk factor you believe contributes to the transmission of coronavirus. You can choose one or more options that best describe your thoughts.
* **Have you ever been in contact with a person with coronavirus?** (Page 3)
	+ This question asks about your experience with someone who has been infected with coronavirus. Please select all options that apply to your situation.
* **How long do you think the risk of transmission is?** (Page 4)
	+ This field is for you to enter the date you believe the risk of transmission is higher.
* **What time of day do you think the risk of transmission is higher?** (Page 5)
	+ This field is for you to enter the time of day you believe the risk of transmission is higher.
* **Do you know how to prevent the transmission of coronaviruses?** (Page 6)
	+ This question asks about your knowledge on preventing the transmission of coronaviruses. Please provide any additional information you have.
* **What is your preferred prevention method?** (Page 7)
	+ This question asks you to select your preferred method for preventing the transmission of coronaviruses. Choose one or more options that you think are effective.

## Tips
- Please be honest and provide your genuine thoughts and experiences with the coronavirus.
- If you are unsure or do not know the answer to a question, it's okay to leave it blank or select "Other" as an option.
- If you have any additional information or thoughts that are not covered in the form, please include them in the free-text field.
