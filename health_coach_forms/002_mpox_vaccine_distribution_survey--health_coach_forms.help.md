# Mpox Vaccine Distribution Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the distribution of Mpox vaccine. You can use this form to provide details about the survey type, date, time, location, recipient, and any additional notes or information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of survey you are conducting (e.g., Vaccine Distribution).
2. Enter the date of the survey.
3. Choose the time of the day for the survey.
4. Provide the location where the survey took place.
5. Enter a brief description of the recipient or person you are surveying.
6. Write any additional notes or comments about the survey.
7. If you want to add any other information, click on the "Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Type (select_one):** Choose the type of survey you are conducting (e.g., Vaccine Distribution).
* **Survey Date (date):** Enter the date of the survey in the format mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Survey Time (time):** Select the time of the survey, such as AM or PM.
* **Survey Location (text):** Enter a brief description of the location where the survey took place.
* **Survey Recipient (text):** Enter a brief description of the recipient or person you are surveying.
* **Survey Notes (note):** Write any additional notes or comments about the survey.
* **Other (text):** If you want to add any other information, click on this field and type away.
