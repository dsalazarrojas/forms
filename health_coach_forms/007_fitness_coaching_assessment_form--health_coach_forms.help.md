# Fitness Coaching Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a comprehensive assessment tool for fitness coaches to gather information from their clients before providing coaching services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer all the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Provide accurate and honest information about your general info, medical history, health status, goals, and lifestyle data.
3. Indicate whether or not you have an emergency contact person.
4. Choose the health coaching services you are interested in.
5. Give your consent to participate in the program.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments.
7. Enter the name of the person who submitted the form.
8. Leave the submitted date as it is automatically filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (`general_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Client History** (`client_history`, `text`, required): Please provide a detailed history of your medical conditions, surgeries, and medications.
* **Health Status** (`health_status`, `text`, optional): Please describe your current health status, including any chronic conditions or concerns.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `text`, required): Please list your fitness goals and objectives.
* **Lifestyle Data** (`lifestyle_data`, `text`, required): Please provide information about your daily routine, including your diet, exercise, and sleep habits.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.
* **Health Form** (`health_form`, `text`, optional): This field is not clearly labeled, so please provide any additional information about your health status or medical history.
* **Health Coaching Services** (`health_coaching_services`, `text`, required): Please choose the health coaching services you are interested in.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required): Please read and agree to the terms of service.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Leave any additional comments or notes about your situation.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **Created** (`created_at`, `text`, read-only): This field is automatically filled out by the system.
* **Updated** (`updated_at`, `text`, read-only): This field is automatically filled out by the system.
* **Deleted** (`deleted_at`, `text`, read-only): This field is not applicable as it is not required.
* **Submitted** (`submitted_at`, `text`, read-only): This field is automatically filled out by the system.
