# Morning Routine Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Morning Routine Evaluation Form is designed to help you assess your morning routine, including your wake-up time, sleep quality, and other factors. This form is used to gather information on your morning habits and help identify areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as required, and provide any additional information requested in the text fields.
2. For select_one and select_multiple fields, choose the relevant options that best describe your habits.
3. Ensure you answer all fields accurately and honestly to provide a comprehensive assessment of your morning routine.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Morning Wake Up Time (morning_routine_evaluation_form_1)
* This field is used to record your usual wake-up time.
* Enter your wake-up time as a 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* This field is not required but contributes to your overall routine assessment.

### Morning Wake Up Time Zone (morning_routine_evaluation_form_2)
* This field asks you to confirm if you consistently wake up at your recorded morning wake-up time.
