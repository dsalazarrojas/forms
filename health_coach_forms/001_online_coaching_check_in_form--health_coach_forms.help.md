# Online Coaching Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Coaching Check In Form is a tool for coaches to collect information from clients regarding their progress, assigned tools, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information.
2. Ensure you enter the correct client name and email address.
3. If you have an assigned tool, select the correct one from the options.
4. Choose the correct client and coach types.
5. Enter any comments or feedback.
6. Ensure you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the client you are interacting with.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the check in.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your coach or instructor.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any relevant comments or feedback.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the client or coach.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the client or coach.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required: false): Select the tool assigned to the client from the options provided.
* **Client Type** (`client_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of client (Client, Student, or Other).
* **Coach Type** (`coach_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of coach (Coach, Teacher, or Other).
* **Assigned Coach Type** (`assigned_coach_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of coach assigned to the client from the options provided.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the assigned tool and coach type to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your coach or instructor for guidance.
