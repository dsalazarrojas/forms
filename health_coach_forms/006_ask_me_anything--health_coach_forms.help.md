# Ask Me Anything - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Ask Me Anything" form is designed to gather information from participants about their health goals, preferences, and circumstances to better tailor coaching sessions to their needs. This form helps the health coach understand your background, goals, and expectations, allowing for personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Your Name" field.
2. Provide your email address for session reminders in the "Email Address" field.
3. Select your primary health goal from the list provided in the "Primary Health Goal" field.
4. Describe your current fitness level in the "Current Fitness Level" field.
5. Ask your question for the health coach in the "Your Question for the Health Coach" field.
6. Choose the category related to your question in the "Question Category" field.
7. (Optional) Select a preferred session time, if applicable.
8. If applicable, list any dietary restrictions or medical conditions.
9. (Optional) Provide information about your average hours of sleep per night and daily water intake.
10. (Optional) Mention any medical conditions or supplements you are currently taking.
11. Rate your current stress level from 1 to 10.
12. Indicate whether you are interested in one-on-one coaching sessions.
13. Select how you heard about us (if applicable).
14. Provide your consent for session recording (if applicable).
15. Finally, provide the date for form submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Your Name** (participant_name): This is the field to enter your name for the health coach to address you.
* **Email Address** (email): Enter your email address for session reminders.
* **Primary Health Goal** (health_goal): Select one of the provided options that best suits your health goal.
* **Current Fitness Level** (current_fitness_level): Describe your current level of fitness.
* **Your Question for the Health Coach** (question_for_coach): Pose your question or concern to the health coach.
* **Question Category** (topic_category): Choose the category related to your question.
* **Preferred Session Time** (preferred_session_time): Select a preferred time for sessions, if applicable.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (dietary_restrictions): List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (medical_conditions): List any relevant medical conditions.
* **Current Medications or Supplements** (current_medications): Provide information on any medications or supplements you are taking.
* **Average Hours of Sleep Per Night** (sleep_hours): Enter your average hours of sleep per night.
* **Daily Water Intake** (water_intake): Enter your daily water intake.
* **Exercise Days Per Week** (exercise_days_per_week): Indicate how often you exercise.
* **Current Stress Level** (stress_level): Rate your current stress level from 1 to 10.
* **Interested in One-on-One Coaching** (follow_up_interest): Indicate interest in one-on-one coaching sessions.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (referral_source): Select how you heard about us, if applicable.
* **Consent to Session Recording** (consent_recording): Provide consent for session recording, if applicable.
* **Submission Date** (submission_date): Enter the current date for form submission.
