# Metabolic Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Metabolic Assessment Form is designed to help health coaches understand your metabolic health and behavior. This information will be used to provide personalized guidance and support for your health goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each page carefully and answer the questions to the best of your ability.
2. Be honest and accurate when providing information.
3. If you have any questions or need help, contact your health coach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rate at which you burn calories (`metabolic_rate_question`)**: This field asks about your average rate at which you burn calories. Please enter a number that represents how many calories you burn per hour, based on your current activity level.
* **Metabolic burning rate (`metabolic_burning_rate`)**: This field asks about your estimated metabolic burning rate. This is a measure of how efficiently your body burns calories. Please enter a number that represents your current metabolic burning rate.
* **Exercise frequency (`exercise_frequency`)**: This field asks about how often you exercise. Please select the option that best describes your exercise frequency, such as "Option 1: Rarely", "Option 2: Occasionally", "Option 3: Frequently", or "Option 4: Almost daily".
* **Sleep hours (`sleep_hours`)**: This field asks about how many hours you sleep each night. Please enter a number that represents your average sleep hours per night.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when providing information.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact your health coach.
* This form is for your personal health guidance and support. The data collected will not be shared or used for any other purpose.
