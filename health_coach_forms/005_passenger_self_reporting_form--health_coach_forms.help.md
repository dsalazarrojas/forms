# Passenger Self Reporting Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Passenger Self Reporting Form is designed to collect essential information from passengers about their recent travel history and potential exposures to COVID-19.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the space provided.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used to contact you if necessary.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can reach you if needed.
4. If you have travelled in the last 14 days, select "Yes" from the options provided.
5. If you have any other comments or concerns, please use the space provided for them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): This is where you'll enter your first name as it appears on your ID or travel documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed. Please ensure this is the email address we can use to reach you.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Provide your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Country
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country where you last travelled.

### Select One
* **Have you travelled in the last 14 days?** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you have travelled in the last 14 days, and "No" otherwise.
