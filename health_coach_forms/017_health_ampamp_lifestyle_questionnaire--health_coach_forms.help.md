# Health & Lifestyle Questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Health & Lifestyle Questionnaire is designed to gather comprehensive information about an individual's health and lifestyle habits. This form helps health coaches and professionals make informed decisions about an individual's well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the relevant sections that apply to your situation.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. If you are unsure or unclear about any question, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
4. Please review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)** (`general_information`, text, optional): This section is a brief overview of your current situation. Please provide a short description of your current health and lifestyle status.
* **Height and Weight (2)** (`height_and_weight`, text, optional): Please provide your current height and weight, in inches and pounds respectively.
* **Height (3)** (`height`, number, optional): Your height in inches.
* **Weight (4)** (`weight`, number, optional): Your weight in pounds.
* **Age (5)** (`age`, number, optional): Your age in years.
* **Exercise Frequencies (6)** (`exercise_frequencies`, text, optional): Describe your exercise frequencies and habits.
* **Frequency (7)** (`frequency`, text, optional): Select the frequency at which you exercise, e.g., daily, weekly, monthly, etc.
* **Diet and Physical Activity (8)** (`diet_and_physical_activity`, text, optional): Describe your diet and physical activity habits.
* **Physical Activity Levels (9)** (`physical_activity_levels`, select_one, optional): Select your physical activity level from:
	+ Sedentary
	+ Light
	+ Moderate
	+ Moderate-Heavy
	+ Very Heavy
* **Dietary Restrictions (10)** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, optional): Select your dietary restriction from:
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-Free
	+ Lacto-Ovo
	+ Dairy
	+ Meat
	+ None
* **Health Goals (11)** (`health_goals`, select_one, optional): Do you have any health goals? (Yes/No)
* **Health Concerns (12)** (`health_concerns`, select_multiple, optional): Are you concerned about any health issues? (Yes/No)
* **Medications (13)** (`medications`, select_multiple, optional): Do you take any medications? (Yes/No)
* **Medical Condition (14)** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): Do you have any medical conditions? Please describe.
* **Family History (15)** (`family_history`, text, optional): Do you have a family history of any medical conditions? Please describe.

## Tips

* Please answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure or unclear about any question, please do not hesitate to ask for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
