# covid_19_related_complaint_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about incidents related to COVID-19, ensuring that all necessary details are captured in a structured manner. This includes reporting symptoms, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (*).
2.  Enter the incident date in the "Incident Date" field. This is the date when the incident occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
3.  Fill out the "Location" field with the specific location where the incident occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
4.  If applicable, enter the symptoms you experienced in the "Symptoms" field.
5.  In the "Contact Person" field, provide the name of the person you are reporting for.
6.  Enter the "Contact Number" of the person you are reporting for, if available.
7.  In the "Incident Description" field, provide a detailed description of the incident.
8.  If you have any additional symptoms or concerns, you can enter them in the "Related Symptoms 1" to "Related Symptoms 8" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Incident Date** (1, date, *): Enter the date when the incident occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
* **Location** (2, text, *): Enter the specific location where the incident occurred or is suspected to have occurred.
