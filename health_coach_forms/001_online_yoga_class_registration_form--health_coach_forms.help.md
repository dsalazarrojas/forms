# Online Yoga Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register for an online yoga class. It helps us to schedule and communicate with attendees accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your accurate information.
2. Select your preferred options for the class schedule.
3. Confirm your email and phone number to ensure accurate contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, Date, Required): Choose the date of the class you want to attend.
* **Time** (`time`, Time, Required): Choose the time of the class you want to attend.
* **What type of yoga do you want to attend?** (`select_one`, Select One, Optional): Choose the type of yoga class you're interested in. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. You can choose one of the options.
* **What time of the day do you prefer?** (`select_multiple`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the time of the day you prefer. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'. You can choose one or multiple options.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any additional information or comments about your preferences.
* **Confirm email** (`email_confirmation`, Email, Required): Confirm the email address you entered earlier.
* **Confirm phone** (`phone_confirmation`, Text, Required): Confirm your phone number.
