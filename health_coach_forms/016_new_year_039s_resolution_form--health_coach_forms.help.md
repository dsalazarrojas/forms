# New Year's Resolution Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Year's Resolution Form is designed to help individuals set and track their health and wellness goals for the upcoming year. This form allows users to specify their resolutions, goals, and intentions for the year ahead.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting your **Health Goal** from the provided options (Health, Fitness, Nutrition, Sleep).
2. Choose how often you plan to exercise, with options for 1-2 times a week, 3-4 times a week, or 5 or more times a week.
3. Provide a brief description of your goal(s) in the **Goal Description** field.
4. Select the date you plan to start working towards your goal(s).
5. Choose the timeframe for achieving your goal(s), with options for Never, By the end of the year, In 6 months, or In 12 months.
6. Select the frequency of your workouts, with options for Once a week, 2-3 times a week, or 4 or more times a week.
7. Indicate if you have a support system for your goal(s), with options for Supportive or Non-supportive.
8. Select how often you would like to follow up on your progress, with options for Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
9. Choose the frequency of reminders you would like to receive, with options for None, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
10. Enter any additional comments or information related to your goal(s) in the **Additional Comments** field.
11. Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment to your resolution(s).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Resolution Objectives**: Briefly describe your New Year's resolution(s).
- **Health Goal**: Select the category of health goal you are setting for the year (Health, Fitness, Nutrition, Sleep).
- **Habit**: Enter any specific habits or activities you plan to adopt or change to achieve your goal(s).
- **Exercise Frequency**: Choose how often you plan to exercise, taking into account your schedule and capabilities.
- **Goal Description**: Provide a clear and concise description of your goal(s).
- **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start working towards your goal(s).
- **Goal Timeframe**: Select the timeframe for achieving your goal(s).
- **End Date**: Choose when you plan to have achieved your goal(s).
- **Frequency**: Select how often you plan to work towards your goal(s).
- **Motivation**: Enter any motivation or reasons for setting your goal(s).
- **Support Systems**: Indicate if you have any support systems in place to help you achieve your goal(s).
- **Follow-up**: Choose how often you would like to follow up on your progress.
- **Reminders**: Select the frequency of reminders you would like to receive.
- **Contact Information**: Enter any contact information, such as email or phone number, in case of follow-up or support.
- **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
- **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information related to your goal(s).
- **Additional Comments 2**: Enter any further additional comments or information related to your goal(s).
- **Signature**: Enter your signature to confirm your commitment to your resolution(s).
- **Signature Date**: Enter the date you sign the form.
- **Signature Time**: Enter the time you sign the form.
- **Signature Initials**: Enter your initials to confirm your commitment to your resolution(s).
- **Signature Title**: Enter your title or position for identification purposes.
- **Signature**: Enter your signature again to confirm your commitment to your resolution(s).
- **Signature Date 2**: Enter the date you sign the form again.

## Tips
- Be specific and clear when describing your resolution(s) and goals.
- Ensure your goals are achievable and realistic.
- Set realistic timeframes and frequencies for achieving your goal(s).
- Identify any potential obstacles or challenges and plan accordingly.
- Regularly follow up on your progress to stay on track.
- Communicate with your support system or contacts as needed.
- Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment to your resolution(s).
