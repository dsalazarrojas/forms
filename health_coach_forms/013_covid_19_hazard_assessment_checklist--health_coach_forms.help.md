# Covid 19 Hazard Assessment Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid 19 Hazard Assessment Checklist is a form designed to help assess and identify potential hazards related to Covid 19 in the workplace. This form is intended to be completed by employees, health coaches, or supervisors to document and evaluate the effectiveness of prevention and mitigation measures against Covid 19 hazards.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and questions carefully.
2. Answer each section based on your observations, experiences, and knowledge related to Covid 19 hazards in your workplace.
3. Complete all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction** (`1`, `Introduction`, `text`, `no`): This section is a brief overview of the form and its purpose. Please read and understand the form's instructions before proceeding.
* **Hazard Identification** (`2`, `Hazard Identification`, `select_one`, `no`): This section asks you to identify potential hazards related to Covid 19 in your workplace. Please select one of the options that best describes the hazards you have observed or are concerned about.
* **Risk Level** (`3`, `Risk Level`, `number`, `no`): This field is meant to assess the level of risk associated with the identified hazards. Please enter a number between 1 and 5 to indicate the level of risk.
* **Risk Assessment** (`4`, `Risk Assessment`, `text`, `yes`): This section is where you can provide a detailed assessment of the identified hazards and their risks. Please provide your observations, experiences, and recommendations for mitigation and prevention.
* **Corrective Action** (`5`, `Corrective Action`, `select_multiple`, `no`): This section asks you to select whether corrective actions have been taken to address the hazards identified. Please select all that apply.
* **Prevention** (`6`, `Prevention`, `text`, `yes`): This field is where you can describe the prevention measures taken to address the hazards identified.
* **Training** (`7`, `Training`, `select_multiple`, `no`): This section asks you to select which training measures have been taken to address the hazards identified. Please select all that apply.
* **Review** (`8`, `Review`, `date`, `no`): This field is meant to record the date and time when the hazards were reviewed.
* **Signature** (`9`, `Signature`, `text`, `yes`): This field is where you can sign and date your name to confirm that you have completed and reviewed the form.
