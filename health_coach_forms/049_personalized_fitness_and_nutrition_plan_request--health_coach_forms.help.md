# Personalized Fitness And Nutrition Plan Request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you get a tailored fitness and nutrition plan based on your specific goals, needs, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To get started, please fill out the form by providing the required information. You can choose to answer any or all of the questions, depending on your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Goals** (`client_goals`, text, required: false): Please describe your fitness and nutrition goals, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or endurance improvement.
* **Activity Level** (`activity_level`, select_one, required: false): Select your typical physical activity level:
	+ Sedentary: Little or no exercise
	+ Lightly active: Light exercise/sports 1-2 days/week
	+ Moderately active: Light exercise/sports 3-5 days/week
	+ Very active: Vigorous exercise/sports 6-7 days/week
* **Goals Frequency** (`goals_frequency`, date, required: false): Please provide the date you would like to achieve your goals.
* **Client Details** (`client_details`, note, required: false): If there's anything specific about your health or fitness situation, please describe it here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`diet_restrictions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any dietary restrictions you have:
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Vegan
	+ Gluten-free
	+ Other
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any medical conditions you have:
	+ Diabetes
	+ High blood pressure
	+ Other
* **Health Details** (`health_details`, text, required: false): If you have any specific health concerns or requirements, please describe them here.
* **Goals** (`goals`, select_one, required: false): What is your main goal:
	+ Weight loss
	+ Muscle gain
	+ Endurance
	+ Other
* **Nutrition Plan** (`nutrition_plan`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred nutrition plan:
	+ Macro caloric deficit
	+ Maintenance
	+ Surplus
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, date, required: false): Please provide the date you would like to achieve your goals.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any training programs you're interested in:
	+ Cardio
	+ Strength
	+ Endurance
	+ Other
* **Supplements** (`supplements`, select_multiple, required: false): Select any supplements you're interested in:
	+ Protein shakes
	+ Protein pills
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please share them here.
