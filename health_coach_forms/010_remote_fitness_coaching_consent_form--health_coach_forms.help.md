# remote_fitness_coaching_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to capture the client's information, medical risks, coaching goals, and consent for remote fitness coaching sessions. It's essential to complete this form accurately to ensure the smooth execution of the coaching program.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields. You may choose to complete all fields or leave them blank, depending on your preference. If you're unsure about any of the fields, please refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Details** (<client_details>, text, required: false): Enter your name, address, contact information, and other relevant details as required.
* **Risks** (<risks>, text, required: false): Describe any medical risks or health concerns that may impact your fitness goals.
* **Training Program** (<training_program, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you want to participate in the training program.
* **Consent** (<consent, select_one, required: false): Confirm your consent to participate in the remote coaching sessions.
* **Signature** (<signature, note, required: false): A space for your digital signature to confirm your consent and agreement.
* **Emergency Contact** (<emergency_contact, text, required: false): Provide contact information for an emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions accurately and honestly.
- You may leave any field blank if you're unsure or do not wish to answer.
- If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, please contact your fitness coach or support team.
