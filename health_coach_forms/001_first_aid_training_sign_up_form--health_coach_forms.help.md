# First Aid Training Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to sign up for first aid training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide the title of the first aid training session you wish to sign up for.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
3. Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
4. Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
5. If you have any special requirements or needs, please provide them in the "Notes" field.
6. Select the class date and time you wish to attend.
7. Choose the first aid training class you wish to attend.
8. Select the first aid training coordinator assigned to you.
9. Choose the location where the class will take place.
10. Review your information and submit the form to complete your sign up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Aid Training**:
    - This field should be filled with the type of first aid training session you wish to sign up for (e.g., "Class 1").
* **Name**:
    - Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**:
    - Provide a unique and valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Phone**:
    - Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Notes**:
    - If you have any special requirements or needs, such as accessibility needs, please provide them here.
* **Class Date**:
    - Choose the date of the class you wish to attend.
* **Class Time**:
    - Select the time of the class you wish to attend.
* **First Aid Training Classes**:
    - Choose the type of first aid training class you wish to take.
* **First Aid Training Coordinator**:
    - Select the coordinator assigned to you.
* **Class Location**:
    - Choose the location where the class will be held.
    Note: The locations may be subject to change. Please check your confirmation email for the most up-to-date information.
