# Public Health Travel Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Health Travel Declaration Form is a tool designed to gather essential information from travelers to ensure their health and safety during trips. This form is intended for travelers who need to declare their health status, travel details, and contact information for emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, carefully read the instructions and ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and honestly, providing accurate information as requested.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Details (Page 1)**: This field is a note section where you can provide any necessary travel information not captured in other fields. Please write your travel details clearly and concisely.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your contact information for emergency situations. This includes your phone number and email address.
* **Address**: Enter your permanent address where you can be contacted in case of emergencies.
* **Travel Dates**: Enter the start and end dates of your trip.
* **Travel Type**: Select the type of travel you will be undertaking (Domestic, International, or Both). This helps us understand the nature of your travel.
* **Travel Destinations**: List all the countries or regions you plan to visit during your trip. You can select multiple destinations if necessary.
* **Health Status**: Describe your current health status, any health conditions, or medical concerns you may have. This helps us assess your health risks during travel.
* **Symptoms**: If you are experiencing any symptoms, such as fever, cough, or other health issues, list them here.
* **Medical Information**: Provide any relevant medical information, such as medications or treatments you are currently undergoing.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Travel Purpose**: Describe the purpose of your trip (e.g., business, leisure, or education).
* **Travel Documents**: Attach any relevant travel documents, such as visas or vaccination records, that may be required for your trip.
* **Travel Restrictions**: Indicate whether you are currently under any travel restrictions due to health reasons.
* **Travel Quarantine**: If you have been under travel quarantine, enter the dates you were under quarantine.
* **Travel Return**: Enter the expected date of your return.
* **Travel Vaccinations**: List any travel vaccinations you have received before or during your trip.
* **Travel Quarantine**: If you have been under travel quarantine, enter the dates you were under quarantine.
* **Travel Vaccinations**: List any travel vaccinations you have received before or during your trip.

Note: This form is for public health declaration purposes only. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
