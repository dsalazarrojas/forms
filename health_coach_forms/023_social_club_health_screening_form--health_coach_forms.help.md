# Social Club Health Screening Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Club Health Screening Form is a tool used to gather health-related information from social club members. This form helps track member health data, which can be used for club management and member engagement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your member information, including your name, phone number, and email.
2. Answer the health screening questions truthfully, indicating if you have any medical conditions or allergies.
3. Provide your medication information, if applicable.
4. List your emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
5. Add any additional comments or notes about your health screening.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Info**: Enter your name, which will be used to identify you as a club member.
* **Health Screening**: Indicate if you have any health-related issues by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for emergency contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for club communication.
* **Date Visited**: Enter the date you visited the health screening.
* **Time Visited**: Enter the time you visited the health screening.
* **Symptoms**: Provide any health-related symptoms you are experiencing.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have been diagnosed with.
* **Medication**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about your health screening.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* If you have any concerns or questions, contact the club administrator or health professional.
* This form is designed to be completed in a timely manner after your health screening.
