# Physical Fitness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Physical Fitness Survey is designed to collect information about your physical fitness habits and preferences. This information will help us provide you with personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply go through each page and select the answers that best reflect your current situation. Be honest and accurate in your responses, as this will help us get a more accurate picture of your fitness level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your fitness level?** (`physical_fitness_survey`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
This field asks you to select a level of fitness that best describes your current physical fitness level.
* **What is your weekly exercise frequency?** (`exercise_habits`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
Choose how many times you exercise per week, and be sure to select all that apply.
* **What are your health concerns?** (`health_concerns`, `text`, required: `false`)
Describe any health concerns you may have or any conditions that affect your fitness routine.
* **What are your fitness goals?** (`fitness_goals`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Choose one goal that best describes what you're trying to achieve with your fitness routine.
* **What is your level of physical activity?** (`physical_activity_level`, `number`, required: `false`)
Rate your level of physical activity, with higher numbers indicating higher activity levels.
* **What are your sleep habits?** (`sleep_habits`, `text`, required: `false`)
Describe your sleep habits, including duration and quality of sleep.
* **How would you describe your stress level?** (`stress_level`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Choose a stress level that best describes how you're feeling.
* **Do you have any medical conditions?** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
Check any medical conditions that apply to you.
* **Are you currently taking any medications?** (`medication_usage`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
Check any medications you're currently taking.
* **How would you rate your nutrition?** (`nutrition_habits`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Choose a nutrition level that best describes your current habits.
* **How would you rate your hydration?** (`hydration_habits`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
Choose a hydration level that best describes your current habits.
* **Additional comments** (`physical_fitness_survey_note`, `note`, required: `false`)
Add any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: `false`)
Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: `false`)
Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: `false`)
Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of last workout** (`time_of_last_workout`, `time`, required: `false`)
Enter the time of your last workout.
* **Hours slept last night** (`hours_slept_last_night`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter the number of hours you slept last night.
* **Physical activity days last 7 days?** (`physical_activity_days_last_7`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`)
Choose the number of days you've been active in the past 7 days.
* **How many times do you exercise per week?** (`exercise_frequency_per_week`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter the number of times you exercise per week.
* **What is your age?** (`age`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter your age.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter your height.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter your weight.
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter your blood pressure.
* **Resting Heart Rate** (`resting_heart_rate`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter your resting heart rate.
* **Stress Level Score** (`stress_level_score`, `number`, required: `false`)
Enter your stress level score.
