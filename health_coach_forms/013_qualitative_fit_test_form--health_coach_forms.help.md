# Qualitative Fit Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect qualitative data about patient and respirator fit. The goal is to gather details such as patient and respirator types, testing dates and times, and any issues found during the fit test.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the **patient_name** field.
2. Select the type(s) of respirator used for the fit test in the **respirator_type** field.
3. Enter the date and time of the fit test in the **test_date** and **test_time** fields, respectively.
4. Enter the patient's size in the **patient_size** field.
5. Enter the respirator size in the **respirator_size** field.
6. Select any issues found during the fit test in the **fit_issues** field.
7. Enter any additional comments about the fit test in the **comments** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
10. Enter the name of the assigned tool in the **assigned_tool** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the patient being tested.
* **Respirator Type** (`respirator_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the type(s) of respirator used for the fit test.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the fit test.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the fit test.
* **Patient Size** (`patient_size`, number, required: false): Enter the patient's size.
* **Respirator Size** (`respirator_size`, number, required: false): Enter the respirator size.
* **Fit Issues** (`fit_issues`, select_one, required: false): Select any issues found during the fit test.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the fit test.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the assigned tool.

Note: The required fields are marked with a asterisk (\*). However, please fill in all fields as relevant to your situation.
