# Coaching Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in a coaching program. It ensures that coaches understand the applicant's background, goals, and expectations, making it easier to determine a suitable fit between the coach and the applicant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Applicant Information section:
  * Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  * If you have a Date of Birth, please provide it.
2. Select the coaching program you are applying for:
  * Choose between Health and Wellness Coaching, Weight Management Coaching, Fitness Coaching, Nutrition Coaching, Mental Health Coaching, or Life Coaching.
3. Choose your desired program start date:
  * Select the date you would like to begin the coaching program.
4. Specify the program duration you prefer:
  * Choose between 4 weeks, 8 weeks, 12 weeks, 6 months, 1 year, or select "Custom".
5. Provide an overview of your current health status:
  * Briefly describe your current health status, including any medical conditions or concerns.
6. List any medical conditions or current medications:
  * Provide a list of any medications you are currently taking.
7. Specify your primary and specific goals for the program:
  * Select all that apply for Primary Goals, and be as detailed as possible for Specific Goals.
8. Confirm your commitment level:
  * Choose your level of commitment to the program.
9. Indicate the number of hours you can dedicate per week:
  * Enter the number of hours you can commit to the program each week.
10. Confirm if you have worked with a coach before:
  * Select "True" or "False".
11. Finally, submit your application:

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): If you have a Date of Birth, provide it.
* **Program You are Applying For** (select_one, required): Choose the coaching program you are interested in (Health and Wellness, Weight Management, Fitness, Nutrition, Mental Health, or Life Coaching).
* **Desired Program Start Date** (date, required): Select the date you would like to begin the coaching program.
* **Program Duration Preference** (select_one, required): Choose the duration of the program (4 weeks, 8 weeks, 12 weeks, 6 months, 1 year, or Custom).
* **Current Health Status** (text, required): Briefly describe your current health status, including any medical conditions or concerns.
* **Any Medical Conditions** (text, optional): Provide a list of any medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications** (text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Primary Goals** (select_multiple, required): Select all that apply for your primary goals (Weight loss, Fitness improvement, Nutrition improvement, Stress management, Better sleep, Increased energy, Overall wellness, or Other).
* **Specific Goals You Want to Achieve** (text, required): Be as detailed as possible about your specific goals.
* **Commitment Level to the Program** (select_one, required): Choose your level of commitment to the program (Highly committed, Very committed, Moderately committed, Somewhat committed).
* **Hours Per Week Available for Coaching** (number, required): Enter the number of hours you can dedicate to the program each week.
* **Have you worked with a coach before?** (select_one, required): Select "True" if you have worked with a coach before, "False" otherwise.
* **Application Date** (date, required): Select the date you are submitting your application.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill in all required fields. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us. We are here to help you through the application process.
