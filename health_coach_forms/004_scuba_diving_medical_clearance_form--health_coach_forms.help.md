# Scuba Diving Medical Clearance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scuba Diving Medical Clearance Form is designed to help individuals evaluate their medical suitability for scuba diving activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please answer each question carefully and accurately. If you are unsure about a particular question, please seek clarification from a doctor or a licensed healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Health** (`general_health`, text, required: false): Please describe your general health status, including any chronic conditions or concerns you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Heart condition: Do you have any conditions affecting your heart?
	+ Lung condition: Do you have any conditions affecting your lungs?
	+ Brain condition: Do you have any conditions affecting your brain?
	+ Other (please specify): Select this option if you have a medical condition not listed above.
* **Medications** (`medications`, text, required: false): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Describe any significant medical history, including surgeries, illnesses, or other conditions that may impact your diving safety.
* **Travel History** (`travel_history`, text, required: false): Describe any recent or upcoming travel plans, including destinations and travel dates.
* **Medical Certifications** (`medical_certifications`, text, required: false): List any relevant medical certifications or training you have received.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required: false): List any allergies you have, including medications, foods, or other substances.
* **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, required: false):
	+ 'True': You have received medical clearance for scuba diving.
	+ 'False': You have not received medical clearance for scuba diving.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, required: false): Please sign this form to indicate that you have read and understood the questions.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you completed this form.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional information about your medical status or concerns.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Note: Please fill in the form accurately and completely. If you have any doubts or concerns, please consult a doctor or a licensed healthcare professional.
