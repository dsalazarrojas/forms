# Mpox Quiz - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mpox Quiz is a form to help healthcare professionals gather information about your symptoms and potential exposure history to assess your risk of monkeypox virus transmission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully, providing as much detail as possible.
2. Select the correct answer for each question.
3. If you are unsure or do not know the answer to a question, please select "Unknown" or "Not sure".
4. Please note that some questions are optional, but the more information you provide, the better healthcare professionals can understand your situation.
