# Mental Health Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Survey is a form designed to collect information about the mental health status and needs of patients or clients. This form will help healthcare professionals and therapists understand the mental health challenges faced by the respondents and provide them with necessary support and guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions as honestly as possible.
2. Choose your responses from the options provided for questions that are of type select_one or select_multiple.
3. For questions that are of type text, type your answer in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Health** (Select Multiple): Please select how you would rate your overall general health. You can choose one or multiple options:
	+ Excellent
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Anxiety and Depression** (Select One): Please select which of the following best describes your current mental health status:
	+ Anxiety
	+ Depression
	+ Neither
* **Self-Care** (Text): Describe your current self-care practices, such as exercise, meditation, or other activities that help you manage your mental health.
* **Social Support** (Text): Describe the support you receive from friends, family, or other sources, such as counseling or support groups.
* **Mental Health Professional Help** (Text): Describe any help or support you have received from mental health professionals, such as therapists or counselors.
* **Mental Health Professional Name** (Text): If you have received help from a mental health professional, please provide their name.
* **Date** (Date): Enter the date you are completing this form, in the format of dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time** (Time): Enter the time you are completing this form, in the format of hh:mm.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number, which will be used for emergency contact purposes.

## Tips
Please be honest and thorough when answering the questions. If you need help or support, please don't hesitate to reach out to a mental health professional.
