# Yoga Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Yoga Club Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from potential members to help them find a suitable yoga class that fits their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and choose your options for each section. Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number (required for scheduling purposes).
* **Payment** (`Payment`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or multiple payment options to indicate your payment preferences.
* **Type of Yoga** (`Type of Yoga`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of yoga that suits your preferences (e.g., Hatha, Ashtanga, Vinyasa, Restorative, Yin).
* **Class Duration** (`Class Duration`, `number`, optional): If you have a preferred class duration, please enter the number of minutes or hours.
* **Class Frequency** (`Class Frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you would like to attend classes (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Class Schedule** (`Class Schedule`, `date`, optional): If you have a preferred class schedule, enter the date (optional).
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred class start time, enter the 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00, 12:00).
* **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, optional): If you have a preferred class end time, enter the 24-hour format (e.g., 10:00, 13:00).
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share with the health coach.
