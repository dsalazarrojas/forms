# telehealth_counseling_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect client information, contact details, and consent for telehealth services in a secure and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your information is up-to-date and correct.
2. Review each section carefully to understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to your health coach or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information**: Please provide your basic contact information to ensure that we can reach you efficiently.
	+ Enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Information**: This is where you can choose how you'd like to be contacted (phone, email, text, or chat).
	+ Select the methods you are comfortable with for communication.
* **Emergency Contact**: Please provide details about an emergency contact person in case we need to reach out to someone in case of an urgent matter.
	+ Enter their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
	+ Enter their name as it appears on their ID.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
	+ Explain how you are related to them (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Client Signature**: This is where you'll sign to confirm that you've read and understood the consent form.
	+ Sign your name to confirm your agreement.
* **Date**: The date you sign the form to confirm your agreement.
	+ Enter the current date.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or concerns, please include them here.
	+ Share any relevant information that may be helpful for your health coach.
* **Health Coach Information**: If you have a preferred health coach, please provide their name and contact information.
	+ Enter their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Health Coach Name**: This is the name of your preferred health coach.
	+ Enter their name as they may appear on their ID.
* **HIPAA Consent**: This section is about understanding your rights and the nature of telehealth services.
	+ Read and understand the consent statement carefully.
*   I understand that I have the right to inspect and review any information or records pertaining to me.
	+ This is an acknowledgment of your right to access your medical records.
*   I understand that I am not required to sign this form in order to receive treatment or services.
	+ This is to inform you that your signature is optional, but still required for consent.
*   I understand that my signature below is required to confirm that I have read and understand this consent form.
	+ Sign to confirm your understanding and agreement.
*   I understand that I am providing consent for telehealth services.
	+ This confirms your understanding of the services you're receiving.
*   I have read, understand, and agree to the terms of this consent form.
	+ Sign to confirm your agreement.
*   I have read and understood the terms of this consent form.
	+ Sign to confirm your understanding.
*   I have read and understand that my signature is required for telehealth services.
	+ Sign to confirm your agreement.
*   I have read and understand the terms of this consent form.
	+ Sign to confirm your agreement.
