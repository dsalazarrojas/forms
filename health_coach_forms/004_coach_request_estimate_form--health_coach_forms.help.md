# Coach Request Estimate Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coach Request Estimate Form is used to gather information from clients to determine their coaching needs and preferences. This form helps us assess their requirements and provide accurate estimates for coaching services. Please use this form to submit your coaching requests and we will get back to you with a detailed estimate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the appropriate options for each field as instructed.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Enter the full name of the person requesting the coaching service.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person requesting the coaching service. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person requesting the coaching service.
* **Type of Coach Needed**: Select the type of coach you are looking for (Health and Wellness Coach, Life Coach, Executive Coach, Career Coach, Sports Coach, Business Coach).
* **Number of Coaching Hours**: Enter the estimated total hours needed for coaching.
* **Coaching Duration**: Select the total timeframe for coaching engagement (1-3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, Ongoing).
* **Desired Start Date**: Enter the desired start date for the coaching engagement.
* **Focus Areas for Coaching**: Select all the areas you need coaching in (Performance improvement, Leadership development, Communication skills, Goal setting, Conflict resolution, Professional growth, Work-life balance).
* **Preferred Delivery Method**: Select how you would like to receive coaching (In-person, Virtual, Hybrid).
* **Preferred Session Frequency**: Select how frequently you would like to have coaching sessions (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, As needed).
* **Preferred Session Duration**: Select the preferred session duration (30 minutes, 1 hour, 90 minutes, 2 hours).
* **Budget Range**: If applicable, select the budget range for the coaching service (Under 1000, 1000-5000, 5000-10000, 10000-25000, 25000+).
* **Additional Notes or Requirements**: If any special requirements or preferences, enter them here.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact us for assistance.
