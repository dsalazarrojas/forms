# Traveler Health Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This Traveler Health Form is designed to collect information about your health and medical history for travel-related purposes. This form is essential for your safety and the safety of those around you when traveling.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please go through the following steps:

1. Fill in all the required fields (marked as required) with the requested information.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and honest information about your health and medical history.
3. Sign and date the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **traveler_health_info** (Text, Required): Fill in a brief description of your current health and medical status. This includes any pre-existing medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are currently taking.
* **contact_info** (Text, Required): Provide your contact information, including your name, address, and emergency contact details.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you in case of any medical emergency.
* **phone** (Text, Required): Fill in your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **medical_history** (Text, Required): Describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, injuries, or surgeries.
* **e_signature** (Text, Required): Sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information.
* **medical_info** (Text, Required): Provide any additional medical information that is relevant to your travel plans.
* **travel_info** (Text, Optional): If you have any special travel plans or requirements, such as vaccinations or specific medications, please describe them here.
* **medical_condition** (Text, Required): List any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
* **medical_test** (Text, Required): Describe any medical tests or examinations you have undergone in the past.
* **medical_vaccine** (Text, Optional): If you have had any vaccinations or immunizations, please describe them here.
* **travel_document** (Text, Required): Attach any relevant travel documents, such as medical certificates or prescriptions.
* **e_signature_1** (Text, Required): Sign and date this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the information.
* **medical_history_2**, **medical_history_3**, **medical_history_4**, **medical_history_5**, **medical_history_6**, **medical_history_7**, **medical_history_8**, **medical_history_9**, **medical_history_10** (Text, Required): Describe any additional medical histories that are not covered in the previous fields.
* **e_signature_1** (Text, Required): This is a duplicate of the previous e_signature field, used for verification purposes.
* **medical_history_11**, **medical_history_12**, **medical_history_13** (Text, Required): Describe any additional medical histories that are not covered in the previous fields.

Note: Please ensure you fill in all the required fields accurately and honestly. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for assistance.
