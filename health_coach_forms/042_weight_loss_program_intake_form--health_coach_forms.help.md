# Weight Loss Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weight Loss Program Intake Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals who wish to participate in a weight loss program. This form helps in understanding the individual's general information, medical history, and contact details to ensure a safe and effective program implementation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information.
2. Provide your height in the correct unit of measurement (ft, in, or cm).
3. Enter your weight and age accurately.
4. Share your medical history and any relevant conditions.
5. Indicate your preferred method of weight loss.
6. Enter your email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required): This section is for general information about yourself.
* **Height** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your height in inches or cm.
* **Height Unit** (`3`, `select_one`, required): Please select the unit of measurement for your height (ft, in, or cm).
* **Weight** (`4`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Age** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter your age.
* **Medical History** (`6`, `text`, required): Share any medical conditions or concerns you have.
* **Medical History Condition** (`7`, `text`, optional): If you have any specific medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Medical History Note** (`8`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please share them here.
* **Goal** (`9`, `text`, required): Describe your weight loss goal.
* **Contact Information** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter your contact details so we can reach out to you.
* **Preferred Method** (`11`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the weight loss methods that interest you.
* **Email** (`12`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`13`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

Note: All fields marked as `required` are necessary to complete the form accurately.
