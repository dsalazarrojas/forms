# Yoga Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about their yoga practice habits, medical conditions, and emergency contact details for assessments by healthcare professionals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Choose your preferred yoga style(s) from the options provided.
2. Answer questions about your medical conditions, previous injuries, and allergies.
3. List any current medications you are taking.
4. Indicate your yoga goals and frequency of practice.
5. Answer questions about your pregnancy and breastfeeding status.
6. Provide any physical limitations you may have.
7. Enter any special instructions for your yoga practice.
8. Provide emergency contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Yoga Style** (`yoga_style`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose one or more options that best describe your preferred yoga style.
* **Yoga Level** (`yoga_level`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose the level of your yoga practice (High, Low).
* **Injury Concerns** (`injury_concerns`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any injuries or conditions that may affect your ability to practice yoga.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required: false):
	+ List any medical conditions you have that may require special consideration.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Choose any allergies you have.
* **Previous Injuries** (`previous_injuries`, text, required: false):
	+ List any previous injuries or conditions that may affect your ability to practice yoga.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, required: false):
	+ List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Yoga Goals** (`yoga_goals`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your goals for your yoga practice (e.g., relaxation, flexibility, strength).
* **Yoga Frequency** (`yoga_frequency`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Indicate how often you practice yoga (Yes, No).
* **Pregnancy** (`pregnancy`, text, required: false):
	+ Answer yes or no to indicate if you are pregnant.
* **Breastfeeding** (`breastfeeding`, text, required: false):
	+ Answer yes or no to indicate if you are breastfeeding.
* **Physical Limitations** (`physical_limitations`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any physical limitations you may have.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any special instructions for your yoga practice.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone 2** (`emergency_phone2`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a secondary phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address 2** (`emergency_address2`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a secondary address for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship Other** (`emergency_relationship_other`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any additional relationship details about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone 3** (`emergency_phone3`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a tertiary phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address 3** (`emergency_address3`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a tertiary address for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Relationship 3** (`emergency_relationship3`, text, required: false):
	+ Describe any additional relationship details about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone 4** (`emergency_phone4`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a fourth phone number for your emergency contact.
