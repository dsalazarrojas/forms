# Covid 19 Yoga Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for participants of a Covid-19 yoga class to acknowledge their medical status, emergency contact information, and other relevant details before attending the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Choose the date you acknowledged the waiver in the "waiver_acknowledgement_date" field.
5. If you have a medical condition, please describe it in the "medical_condition" field.
6. Select your Covid-19 status from the options in the "covid_19_status" field.
7. Enter the name of your emergency contact person in the "emergency_contact_name" field.
8. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the "emergency_contact_phone" field.
9. Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person in the "emergency_contact_relationship" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Waiver Acknowledgement Date** (`waiver_acknowledgement_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date when you acknowledged the waiver.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required/optional): If you have any medical condition, please describe it briefly.
* **Covid-19 Status** (`covid_19_status`, select_one, required/optional): Select your Covid-19 status from the options:
	+ Covid-19 Positive
	+ Covid-19 Negative
	+ No Answer
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
