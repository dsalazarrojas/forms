> # New Member Fitness Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Member Fitness Assessment Form is designed to gather information from new members, including their personal details, medical history, and fitness goals. The form aims to assess the member's fitness level, medical conditions, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Answer all questions honestly and thoroughly.
* Be as detailed as possible in your responses.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please reach out to our team for support.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Member Information** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_1`, Text, Optional):
This field is used to collect your basic contact information.
* **Medical History** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_2`, Text, Optional):
Please provide any medical conditions, allergies, or concerns you have.
* **Health and Fitness Details** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_3`, Text, Optional):
Share your current fitness level, exercise habits, and goals.
* **Emergency Contact** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_4`, Text, Optional):
Enter the name and contact information of a person who will contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_5`, Text, Optional):
Enter the name and contact information of a second person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Membership Information** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_6`, Text, Optional):
Please provide any additional information about your membership, such as your membership type or payment method.
* **Payment and Billing** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_7`, Text, Optional):
Enter your payment information, including your billing address and payment method.
* **Additional Information** (`new_member_fitness_assessment_form_8`, Text, Optional):
Provide any additional details that may be relevant to your membership, such as your goals or preferences.
