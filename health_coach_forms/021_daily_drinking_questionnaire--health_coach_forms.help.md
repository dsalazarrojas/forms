# Daily Drinking Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about your daily drinking habits. It helps us understand your current drinking patterns and identify potential areas where you may be able to make positive changes. By providing accurate and honest answers, you'll be able to track your drinking behavior and set realistic goals for reducing your consumption.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Answer each question carefully and honestly**: Please take a few minutes to think about your daily drinking habits and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. **Use numbers**: If you have a specific number of drinks or units, use that number. For example, "2 drinks" or "10 units".
3. **Choose an option**: For questions with select-one options, choose the answer that best describes your behavior. For example, "With friends" or "Alone".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Daily Drinking**: Please describe your daily drinking habits, including the type of drinks you had and any other relevant details.
  * This field is optional, but providing a brief description of your daily drinking habits will help us better understand your behavior.
  
- **How many drinks have you had today?**
  * Please enter the number of drinks you've had today.
  * Required field
  
- **How many units of alcohol have you had?**
  * Please enter the total number of units of alcohol you've had today.
  * Required field
  
- **How many days per week do you drink alcohol?**
  * Please enter the number of days in a week you drink alcohol.
  * This field is used to track your drinking frequency.
  
- **Do you drink more than 7 drinks in one sitting?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you have a pattern of binge drinking.
  
- **Do you drink with someone else?**
  * Please select one of the options: "Alone", "With friends", "With family", "With a partner", or "With others".
  * This field helps us understand your social drinking habits.
  
- **Do you drink with food?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you tend to drink with meals.
  
- **Do you drink to get drunk?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you're drinking for intoxication.
  
- **Do you have any problems with your drinking?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you're struggling with your drinking habits.
  
- **Do you drink for self-care?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you use drinking as a way to cope with stress or anxiety.
  
- **Do you drink to socialize?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you drink to socialize or fit in with others.
  
- **Do you drink alone?**
  * Answer this question with "True" or "False".
  * This field helps us understand if you're drinking alone or with others.
  
- **How many drinks do you have?**
  * Please enter the number of drinks you've had today.
  * Required field
  
- **How many units of alcohol do you have?**
  * Please enter the total number of units of alcohol you've had today.
  * Required field

Note: This form is intended to be completed on a daily basis to track your drinking habits over time.
