# Covid Questionnaire For Customers - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from customers about their COVID-19 related experiences and history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Answer the questions about your travel history, COVID-19 exposure, vaccination status, and high-risk areas according to your experiences.
