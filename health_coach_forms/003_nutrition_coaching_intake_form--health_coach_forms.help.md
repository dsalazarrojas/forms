# Nutrition Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from you about your health, medical history, and nutrition goals. Please answer the questions accurately to help your healthcare coach provide you with effective guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. If you're unsure about any question, please ask your healthcare coach for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Information**: This is the first step in filling out the form. Please enter your basic contact information.
* **Medical History**: Share any medical conditions or concerns that may affect your treatment plan.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions that apply to you, such as Diabetes, Hypertension, or Heart Disease.
* **Medical Procedures**: List any past surgeries, hospitalizations, or medical procedures you've had.
* **Medication List**: If you're taking any medications, please list them here.
* **Medical Notes**: Add any additional medical notes or information that may be helpful for your healthcare coach.
* **Nutrition Goals**: Select your primary nutrition goal: weight loss, weight gain, improve overall health, or improve digestive system.
* **Food Preferences**: Select your food preferences, such as vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, or omnivore.
* **Physical Activity**: Select your physical activity level: none, light, moderate, or intense.
* **Diet Type**: Select your diet type: vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, or gluten free.
* **Meal Schedule**: Enter your preferred meal schedule, such as 3 meals a day or 5 meals a day.
* **Breakfast**: Describe your typical breakfast routine.
* **Lunch**: Describe your typical lunch routine.
* **Dinner**: Describe your typical dinner routine.
* **Snacks**: Describe your typical snack routine.
* **Meal Schedule 2**: Enter an additional meal schedule, if needed.
* **Sleep Schedule**: Enter your typical sleep schedule, including start and end times.
* **Water Intake**: Enter your daily water intake, measured in ounces or liters.
* **Exercise Schedule**: Describe your typical exercise routine.
* **Exercise Frequency**: Enter the frequency of your exercise routine.
* **Exercise Duration**: Enter the duration of your exercise routine.
* **Exercise Intensity**: Enter the intensity of your exercise routine.
* **Supplements**: List any supplements you're currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies or sensitivities you have.
* **Medical Insurance**: Enter your medical insurance information, if applicable.
