# Wellness Coaching Interest Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Coaching Interest Survey is a tool used by wellness coaches to gather information about their clients' goals, preferences, and background data. This form helps coaches design personalized wellness plans and provide the best possible support.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your basic information, including:
	* **Client Name** (1)
	* **Email** (2)
2. Proceed to fill in your goals and preferences:
	* **Coaching Goals** (3)
	* **Coaching Preferences** (4)
	* **Health Goals** (5)
3. Provide your background information:
	* **Health History** (6)
	* **Current Coach** (7)
4. Share how you found us:
	* **How did you find us?** (8)
5. Finally, confirm your next steps and contact information:
	* **Next Steps** (9)
	* **Contact Info** (10)
6. Add any additional notes:
	* **Notes** (11)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Client Name
* **Client Name** (1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Type**: text, **Required**: no
* **Hint**: This will be used to address you in our communication.

### 2. Email
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address as it's registered with us.
* **Type**: email, **Required**: no
* **Hint**: We use this to send you updates and messages.

### 3. Coaching Goals
* **Coaching Goals** (3): Describe your main goals you'd like to achieve through our coaching program.
* **Type**: text, **Required**: no
* **Hint**: Be specific about what you want to accomplish.

### 4. Coaching Preferences
* **Coaching Preferences** (4): Select all that apply (up to three options) to indicate your coaching preferences:
	+ **Goal Setting**
	+ **Mindfulness**
	+ **Stress Management**
* **Type**: select_multiple, **Required**: no
* **Hint**: Choose what you're interested in learning more about.

### 5. Health Goals
* **Health Goals** (5): Describe your health goals, if any.
* **Type**: text, **Required**: no
* **Hint**: This helps us understand your focus areas.

### 6. Health History
* **Health History** (6): Share any relevant health information or medical conditions you've faced.
* **Type**: text, **Required**: no
* **Hint**: This helps us tailor your coaching plan.

### 7. Current Coach

### 8. How did you find us?
