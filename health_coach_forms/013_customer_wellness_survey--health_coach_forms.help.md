# customer_wellness_survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer_wellness_survey form is designed to gather information about customers' physical and emotional well-being, enabling healthcare professionals to provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer the questions as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name and preferred contact method.
3. Share any relevant medical history, doctor's name, and phone number.
4. Provide details about your symptoms, health conditions, and medical facility (if applicable).
5. Indicate your emergency contact information, including name, relationship, phone number, and email (if applicable).
6. Choose how you would like to save and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date First Symptom** (`date_first_symptom`, date, optional): If you are experiencing any symptoms, provide the date they first appeared.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your current temperature.
* **Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, select_one, optional): Select whether you have any health conditions from the list.
* **Customer Wellness Rating** (`customer_wellness_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your current well-being from the provided options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Share any additional comments or concerns you have about your health.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred contact method for further communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Patient Identifier** (`patient_identifier`, text, required): Enter a unique identifier assigned by your healthcare provider or clinic.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): Share any relevant medical history or previous treatments.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current healthcare provider.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your doctor's contact number.
* **Doctor Email** (`doctor_email`, email, optional): Enter your doctor's email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Identify your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, note, optional): Share any additional information about your emergency contact.
* **Medical Facility** (`medical_facility`, text, optional): Enter the name of your current medical facility or clinic.
* **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or instructions for your healthcare provider.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional): Choose how you would like to save and submit your form from the provided options.

Please review each field carefully and complete the form accurately to ensure the best possible support and care. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
