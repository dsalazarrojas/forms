# Fitness Weekly Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fitness Weekly Check In Form is designed to help health coaches and clients track progress, set goals, and identify areas for improvement. It is a weekly form that should be completed by clients to provide updates on their fitness journey.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Weekly Workout Plan** (Weekly Workout Plan, text, optional): Describe your workout plan for the week. This can include the specific exercises, routines, or activities you plan to do.
2. **Weekly Progress** (Weekly Progress, text, optional): Report on your progress during the week. This can include any successes, challenges, or changes you noticed.
3. **Goals** (Goals, select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the following goals for this week:
	* Weight Loss
	* Strength Gain
	* Increased Endurance
	* Improved Flexibility
	* Other (please specify)
4. **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share with your health coach.
5. **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want your health coach to contact you.
6. **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want your health coach to contact you.
7. **Workout Type** (Workout Type, select one, optional): Choose the type of workout you did this week:
	* Running
	* Weightlifting
	* Cardio
	* Yoga
	* Other (please specify)
8. **Frequency** (Frequency, select one, optional): Choose how often you worked out this week:
	* Daily
	* 3-4 times a week
	* 1-2 times a week
	* Rarely
	* Don't know
9. **Duration** (Duration, number, optional): Estimate the total amount of time spent on workouts this week.
10. **Intensity** (Intensity, select one, optional): Choose how intense your workouts were this week:
	* High
	* Moderate
	* Low
11. **Pain Level** (Pain Level, select one, optional): Rate your pain level during workouts this week:
	* High
	* Moderate
	* Low
12. **Motivation** (Motivation, select one, optional): Rate your motivation level for working out this week:
	* High
	* Moderate
	* Low

## Tips
- Be honest about your progress and goals to ensure you get the most out of your fitness journey.
- Make sure to provide as much detail as possible in the weekly workout plan and progress sections.
- If you select "Other" for goals, please specify what that goal is.
- Remember to ask your health coach about any questions or concerns you may have about your progress.
- This form should be completed every week, but feel free to reach out to your health coach if you need help or have questions during the week.
