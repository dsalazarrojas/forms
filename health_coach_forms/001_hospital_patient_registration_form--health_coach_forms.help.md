# Hospital Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from patients for registration purposes at a hospital. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete each field as instructed below.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title** (`General Information`, `text`, required): Fill in the patient's name, date of birth, and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, required): Provide the patient's emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Insurance Information** (`Insurance Information`, `text`, required): Fill in the patient's insurance provider's name and policy number.
* **Hospital Information** (`Hospital Information`, `text`, required): Provide the hospital's address and contact information.
* **Doctor Information** (`Doctor Information`, `text`, required): Enter the doctor's name and specialty.
* **Visit Information** (`Visit Information`, `text`, required): Fill in the patient's visit details such as date and time.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required): This field is for taking notes about the patient's visit.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required): Provide any additional information about the patient.
* **Insurance Plan** (`Insurance Plan`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the patient's insurance provider from the list (Aetna, BlueCross, Cigna, HealthAmerica, Medicare, UnitedHealth, Other).
* **Medical Record Number** (`Medical Record Number`, `text`, required): Enter the patient's medical record number.
* **Medical Record Date** (`Medical Record Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the patient's medical record.
* **Doctor Signature** (`Doctor Signature`, `time`, required): The doctor must sign here to indicate they have reviewed the patient's file.
* **Nurse Signature** (`Nurse Signature`, `time`, required): The nurse must sign here to indicate they have reviewed the patient's file.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required): Enter any medical history or allergies the patient has.
* **Medical Record Reviewed** (`Medical Record Reviewed`, `select_one`, required): Check if the medical record has been reviewed.
* **Medication History** (`Medication History`, `text`, required): Enter any medication the patient is currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`Allergies`, `text`, required): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Patient Signature** (`Patient Signature`, `time`, required): The patient must sign here to indicate they have reviewed their file.
* **Doctor Notes** (`Doctor Notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes the doctor may have about the patient's visit.
* **Nurse Notes** (`Nurse Notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes the nurse may have about the patient's visit.
* **Medical Team Notes** (`Medical Team Notes`, `text`, required): Enter any notes from the medical team about the patient's visit.
* **Lab Results** (`Lab Results`, `text`, required): Enter any lab results from the patient's visit.
* **Medical History Reviewed** (`Medical History Reviewed`, `text`, required): Check if the medical history has been reviewed.
* **Medical Record Updated** (`Medical Record Updated`, `text`, required): Check if the medical record has been updated.
* **Hospital Staff** (`Hospital Staff`, `text`, required): Enter the hospital staff member's name and contact information.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* This form is for administrative purposes only and should not be used for medical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact hospital administration for clarification.
