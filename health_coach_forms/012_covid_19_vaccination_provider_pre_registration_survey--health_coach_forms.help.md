# COVID 19 Vaccination Provider Pre Registration Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey is intended to collect information from healthcare providers about their capacity to provide COVID-19 vaccinations. The goal is to gather data about each provider's ability to distribute vaccines, including their experience, capacity, equipment, and reporting capabilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each question thoroughly and accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (Page 1, **provider_name**):
	+ Provide the name of your healthcare facility or organization.
	+ For example, "ABC General Hospital".
* **Type of Healthcare Provider** (Page 1, **provider_type**):
	+ Select the type of healthcare provider (e.g., Hospital, Clinic, Pharmacy, etc.).
* **Contact Person Name** (Page 1, **contact_person**):
	+ Provide the name of the contact person for your healthcare facility.
* **Contact Email** (Page 1, **contact_email**):
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (Page 1, **contact_phone**):
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Facility Information** (Page 1, **facility_section**):
	+ This is a note section to provide any additional information about your healthcare facility.
* **Facility Street Address** (Page 1, **address_street**):
	+ Enter your facility's street address.
	+ For example, "123 Main Street".
* **City** (Page 1, **address_city**):
	+ Enter your city or town.
* **State** (Page 1, **address_state**):
	+ Enter your state or province.
* **Zip Code** (Page 1, **address_zip**):
	+ Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **Operating Hours** (Page 1, **facility_hours**):
	+ Enter your facility's operating hours.
* **Vaccination Capacity** (Page 1, **capacity_section**):
	+ This is a note section to provide any additional information about your vaccination capacity.
* **Prior COVID-19 Vaccination Experience** (Page 1, **vaccination_experience**):
	+ Select your prior experience with COVID-19 vaccinations (e.g., Extensive, Moderate, Limited, None).
* **Daily Vaccination Capacity** (Page 1, **daily_capacity**):
	+ Enter the number of patients you can vaccinate per day.
* **Number of Healthcare Staff Available** (Page 1, **staff_available**):
	+ Enter the number of healthcare staff available to administer vaccinations.
* **Vaccine Storage Capacity** (Page 1, **vaccine_storage**):
	+ Select your vaccine storage capacity (e.g., Ultra-low freezer, Standard freezer, Refrigerator only, None available).
* **Staff Trained in Cold Chain Management** (Page 1, **cold_chain_training**):
	+ Select whether your staff is trained in cold chain management.
* **Distribution and Readiness** (Page 1, **distribution_section**):
	+ This is a note section to provide any additional information about your distribution and readiness.
* **Preferred Vaccine Types for Distribution** (Page 1, **distribution_preference**):
	+ Select all vaccine types you prefer to distribute (e.g., Pfizer, Moderna, Johnson and Johnson, All types, No preference).
* **Estimated Vaccine Supply Needs** (Page 1, **supply_needs**):
	+ Enter the number of doses you need per week.
* **Equipment or Supplies Needed** (Page 1, **equipment_needs**):
	+ Enter any equipment or supplies you need for vaccination distribution.
* **Challenges and Barriers** (Page 1, **barriers_section**):
	+ This is a note section to provide any additional information about challenges and barriers you are facing.
* **Anticipated Barriers** (Page 1, **anticipated_barriers**):
	+ Enter any anticipated barriers to vaccination distribution.
* **Community Outreach Capacity** (Page 1, **outreach_capacity**):
	+ Select your community outreach capacity (e.g., High, Moderate, Low, None).
* **Multilingual Services** (Page 1, **multilingual_services**):
	+ Select all languages you can provide services in (e.g., Spanish, Mandarin, Vietnamese, Other languages).
* **Accessibility Measures** (Page 1, **accessibility_measures**):
	+ Enter any accessibility measures you have in place (e.g., wheelchair access, accommodations, etc.).
* **Data and Reporting** (Page 1, **data_section**):
	+ This is a note section to provide any additional information about your data and reporting capabilities.
* **Ability to Report Vaccination Data** (Page 1, **reporting_capability**):
	+ Select your ability to report vaccination data (e.g., Real-time electronic reporting, Daily reporting, Weekly reporting, Uncertain).
