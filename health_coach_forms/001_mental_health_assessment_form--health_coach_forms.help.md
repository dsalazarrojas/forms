# Mental Health Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Mental Health Assessment Form is designed to evaluate the mental health status of an individual. It gathers information on their mental state, anxiety level, mood, sleep quality, stress level, mental illness diagnosis, symptoms, treatment, and overall health status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  For each question, select the best answer that describes your current mental state or situation.
2.  Be honest and provide as much detail as possible.
3.  Fill out the form as accurately and completely as you can.
4.  Review the questions carefully before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Mental Health Assessment Form**: This is a note field where you can provide additional information or context about your mental health status.
*   **Mental State**: Select the mental state that best describes your current mental state. Options are:
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Anxiety Level**: Choose the anxiety level that best describes your current anxiety level. Options are:
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
*   **Mood**: Select the mood that best describes your current mood. Options are:
    *   Happy
    *   Sad
    *   Angry
*   **Sleep Quality**: Choose the sleep quality that best describes your current sleep quality. Options are:
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Stress Level**: Select the stress level that best describes your current stress level. Options are:
    *   Low
    *   Medium
    *   High
*   **Mental Illness Diagnosis**: Check if you have been diagnosed with a mental illness. Options are:
    *   Yes
    *   No
    *   Unknown
*   **Mental Illness Symptoms**: Describe any symptoms you are experiencing related to mental illness.
*   **Mental Illness Treatment**: Select the treatment you are undergoing for your mental health. Options are:
    *   Medication
    *   Therapy
    *   Self-care
*   **Mental Health Status**: Select your current mental health status. Options are:
    *   Good
    *   Fair
    *   Poor
*   **Mental Health Recommendations**: Provide any recommendations or comments from your health coach or provider.
*   **Mental Health Comments**: Add any additional comments about your mental health status.
*   **Form Submitter**: Select who submitted the form. Options are:
    *   Patient
    *   Health Coach
    *   Provider
*   **Form Completed**: Check if you have completed the form.
*   **Form Revised**: Check if you have revised the form.
*   **Form Version**: Enter the version of the form you are using.
*   **Form Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form.
*   **Form Modified By**: Enter the name of the person who modified the form.
*   **Form Completed At**: Enter the date you completed the form.
*   **Form Submitter**: Select who submitted the form.

Please note that the form is a comprehensive assessment tool for mental health, and it is essential to provide accurate and honest responses to ensure that you receive the best guidance and support from your health coach or provider.
