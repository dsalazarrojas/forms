# COVID 19 Daily Wellness Assessment - Help Guide

## Purpose

The COVID 19 Daily Wellness Assessment is a form designed to help you evaluate your overall health and wellness during the COVID-19 pandemic. It is intended for individuals to assess their physical and mental health status on a daily basis to identify any concerns or issues that may be related to the virus or its effects.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the "Assessment Date" field to record the date of the assessment.
2. Enter your "Participant Name" in the text field provided.
3. Select your "Participant ID" (if applicable).
4. Choose your current "Location" from the list of options.
5. Select the number of people you are in close proximity to.
6. Assess your "Overall Health" status from the list of options.
7. Rate your "Energy Level" on a scale of 1-10.
8. Select your "Sleep Quality" from the list of options.
9. Rate your "Appetite Level" from the list of options.
10. Answer whether you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
11. Select any "Current Symptoms" that you are experiencing.
12. Assess your "Mood" from the list of options.
13. Rate your "Stress Level" on a scale of 1-10.
14. Answer whether you are feeling "Isolated".
15. Record any "Additional Wellness Notes" you would like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assessment Date (2)**: Record the date of this daily wellness assessment.
* **Participant Name (3)**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Participant ID (4)**: If you have a participant ID, please enter it here (optional).
* **Current Location (6)**: Select your current location, such as Home, Work, School, etc.
* **Number of people in close proximity (7)**: Report the number of people you are in close proximity to.
* **Overall Health (9)**: Rate your overall health status, Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Energy Level (10)**: Rate your energy level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Sleep Quality (11)**: Select your sleep quality from the list, such as Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor, or No Sleep.
* **Appetite Level (12)**: Select your appetite level from the list, such as Normal, Decreased, Increased, or No Appetite.
* **Any COVID-19 symptoms (14)**: Answer whether you are experiencing any COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Current Symptoms (15)**: Select any current symptoms you are experiencing, such as Fever, Cough, Fatigue, etc.
* **Mood (17)**: Assess your current mood from the list, such as Happy, Neutral, Anxious, Sad, or Stressed.
* **Stress Level (18)**: Rate your stress level on a scale of 1-10.
* **Feeling Isolated (19)**: Answer whether you are feeling isolated.
* **Additional Wellness Notes (20)**: Record any additional notes about your wellness.
