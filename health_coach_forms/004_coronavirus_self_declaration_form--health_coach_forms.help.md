# Coronavirus Self Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Coronavirus Self Declaration Form" is a tool for employees to report their potential exposure to or symptoms of the coronavirus. This form will help organizations understand their employee's health status in relation to the coronavirus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Employee" from the first field.
2. Choose your symptoms from the list in the "Symptoms" field.
3. Select how often you have been experiencing the symptoms from the "Symptom Frequency" field.
4. Describe the location where you are experiencing the symptoms in the "Symptom Location" field.
5. Confirm your HIPAA compliance.
6. Add any comments in the "Comments" field.
7. Click "Submit and Sign" to finalize your declaration.
