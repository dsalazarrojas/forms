# Health And Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health And Wellness Survey is a comprehensive form designed to gather information about your health and wellness status. This form will help us understand your overall health situation, enabling us to provide personalized coaching and recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer each question truthfully to the best of your ability.
- Select the relevant options for each field.
- If a field is marked as required, please ensure you provide a complete answer.
- If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Health Information
* **Health Information**: A text field where you can provide any relevant health-related information about yourself. Please describe your overall health status, including any medical conditions, health risks, or concerns you may have.

### 2. Physical Activity
* **Physical Activity**: A select_one field where you can choose how active you are. 
  * Yes: I am physically active
  * No: I am not physically active

### 3. Diet
* **Diet**: A text field where you can describe your eating habits and preferences. Please provide details about your diet, including any dietary restrictions, allergies, or preferences.

### 4. Sleep Patterns
* **Sleep Patterns**: A text field where you can describe your sleep patterns, including your typical bedtime and wake-up time, sleep quality, and any sleep-related issues.

### 5. Medical Conditions
* **Medical Conditions**: A select_multiple field where you can choose any medical conditions you have. 
  * Yes: I have one of these medical conditions
  * No: I don't have any of these medical conditions

### 6. Medication
* **Medication**: A text field where you can list any medications you are currently taking. Please include the name, dosage, and frequency of each medication.

### 7. Allergies
* **Allergies**: A select_one field where you can choose if you have any allergies. 
  * Yes: I have an allergy
  * No: I don't have any allergies

### 8. Medical History
* **Medical History**: A text field where you can describe your medical history, including any previous illnesses, surgeries, or health issues.

### 9. Family History
* **Family History**: A text field where you can describe your family medical history, including any genetic conditions or health risks that may be relevant to your health.

### 10. Medical Imaging
* **Medical Imaging**: A select_multiple field where you can choose any medical imaging you've had. 
  * Yes: I've had one of these types of medical imaging
  * No: I've had none of these types of medical imaging

Remember, this form is designed to help us understand your health status better. Please answer honestly and thoroughly, and feel free to ask if you have any questions or need help during the process.
