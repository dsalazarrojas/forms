# Health Consultation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Consultation Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from users to help health coaches provide personalized and effective consultations. This form is used to gather details about the user's health status, medical history, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and fill in the relevant information for the corresponding section.
2. Be accurate and clear in your responses, especially when filling out the medical history sections.
3. If you have any medical notes or comments, use the "Health Coach Consultation Details" section to provide additional information.
