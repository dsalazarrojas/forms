# COVID 19 Phase 1B Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for Law Enforcement and Fire personnel to register for the COVID-19 Phase 1B vaccination program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are eligible for the Phase 1B vaccination program.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Provide valid information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Fill out your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Agency or Organization Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your agency or organization (e.g., City Fire Dept, County Sheriff).
* **Department** (`personnel_department`, select_one, required): Select your department (Law Enforcement, Fire Department, EMS, Corrections, Dispatch).
* **Badge or Employee ID Number** (`badge_id`, text, required): Enter your badge or employee ID number.
* **Work Phone Number** (`work_phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Personal Email Address** (`personal_email`, email, required): Enter your personal email address.
* **Duty Station Address** (`duty_station`, text, required): Enter the address of your duty station.
* **Have you tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 90 days?** (`previous_infection`, select_one, required): Select 'True' if you have tested positive for COVID-19 in the last 90 days, 'False' otherwise.
* **I consent to receive the vaccine at no charge as part of Phase 1B** (`vaccination_consent`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to confirm your consent or 'I consent' if you are unable to select 'True'.

## Tips
* Ensure each field is filled out correctly and accurately.
* Review your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you experience issues with the form, contact your supervisor or a program administrator for assistance.
