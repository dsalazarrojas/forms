# Coach And Client Expectations Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to outline the expectations and agreements between a coach and client. It ensures that both parties understand their roles, responsibilities, and boundaries, creating a clear foundation for a productive and effective coaching relationship.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your details: Fill in your full name, email, and phone number for both you and your client.
2. Choose the type of coaching: Select the type of coaching services you wish to receive (e.g., Health Coaching, Wellness Coaching, etc.).
3. Determine the schedule: Choose how often you'd like to meet (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.) and the duration of each session.
4. Define the format: Select how you'd like sessions to be conducted (e.g., in-person, video call, etc.).
5. Set goals and expectations: Describe your primary coaching goals and what the client agrees to do between sessions.
6. Agree on responsibilities: Confirm what you, as the coach, agree to provide and what the client agrees to do.
7. Establish communication: Decide how you'll communicate between sessions.
8. Set payment terms: Choose when payment is due (e.g., per session in advance, etc.).
9. Protect confidentiality: Confirm that both parties agree to keep information private.
10. Establish boundaries: Ensure you understand professional boundaries.
11. Track progress: Choose how you'll measure success (e.g., regular assessments, goal milestones, etc.).
12. Homework expectations: Decide if you expect the client to do tasks between sessions.
13. Commitment level: Assess your level of commitment to this process.
14. Potential obstacles: Identify any potential challenges that might hinder success.
15. Support system: Confirm if you have a support system outside of coaching.
16. Start date: Set the beginning date of coaching.
17. Agreement duration: Choose how long the coaching relationship will last.
18. Both parties agree: Confirm that both you and your client agree to these expectations.
19. Date of agreement: Record the date you sign this agreement.
20. Additional notes: Leave any further comments or agreements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as the coach.
* **Coach Email** (`2`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Coach Phone** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Client Name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter the client's full name.
* **Client Email** (`5`, `email`, required): Enter the client's email address for communication.
* **Client Phone** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter the client's phone number for contact.
* **Type of Coaching** (`7`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of coaching services (e.g., Health Coaching, etc.).
* **Session Frequency** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you'd like to meet (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Session Duration** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Choose the length of each session (e.g., 30 minutes, etc.).
* **Session Format** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select how sessions will be conducted (e.g., in-person, video call, etc.).
* **Primary Coaching Goals** (`11`, `text`, required): Describe what you want to achieve through coaching.
* **Client Responsibilities** (`12`, `text`, required): Confirm what the client agrees to do between sessions.
* **Coach Responsibilities** (`13`, `text`, required): Confirm what you, as the coach, agree to provide.
* **Communication Expectations** (`14`, `text`, required): Decide how you'll communicate between sessions.
* **Expected Response Time** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Set the time frame for responding to each other.
* **Cancellation Policy Agreement** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the cancellation terms.
* **Cancellation Notice** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Choose the notice period for cancellations.
* **Payment Terms** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Choose when payment is due (e.g., per session in advance, etc.).
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that both parties agree to keep information private.
* **Professional Boundaries Understood** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Ensure you understand the coach-client boundaries.
* **How Will Progress Be Tracked** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you'll measure success (e.g., regular assessments, goal milestones, etc.).
* **Homework Expectations** (`22`, `select_one`, required): Decide if the client is expected to do tasks between sessions.
* **Client Commitment Level** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Assess your level of commitment to this process.
* **Potential Obstacles** (`24`, `text`, optional): Identify any potential challenges that might hinder success.
* **Support System** (`25`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm if you have a support system outside of coaching.
* **Coaching Start Date** (`26`, `date`, required): Set the beginning date of coaching.
* **Agreement Duration** (`27`, `select_one`, required): Choose how long the coaching relationship will last.
* **Both Parties Agree** (`28`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that both you and your client agree to these expectations.
* **Date of Agreement** (`29`, `date`, required): Record the date you sign this agreement.
* **Additional Notes** (`30`, `text`, optional): Leave any further comments or agreements.
