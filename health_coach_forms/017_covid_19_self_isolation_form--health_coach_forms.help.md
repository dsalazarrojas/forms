# COVID-19 Self Isolation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The COVID-19 Self Isolation Form is used to gather information about your self-isolation status to help health authorities determine when you are eligible to return to work.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately.
2. Fill out all required fields (those marked as "required").
3. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank if it does not apply to you.
4. Use the available options for select fields (e.g., dropdown menus).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Isolation Start Date** (`isolation_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when your isolation began.
* **Expected Isolation End Date** (`expected_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you expect to end your isolation.
* **Employee Full Name** (`employee_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter your employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter the department you work in.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Supervisor Name** (`supervisor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email** (`supervisor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your direct manager or supervisor.
* **Employee Phone** (`employee_phone`, text, required): Enter a contact phone number for you.
* **Employee Email** (`employee_email`, email, required): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the address where you are isolating.
* **Reason for Self-Isolation** (`isolation_reason`, select_one, required): Select the primary reason for your self-isolation.
*   + Tested positive for COVID-19
*   + Experiencing COVID-19 symptoms
*   + Close contact with a positive case
*   + Traveling from a high-risk area
*   + Waiting for test results
*   + Healthcare provider recommendation
*   + Other
* **Symptom Status** (`symptom_status`, select_one, required): Select your current symptom status.
*   + Yes symptomatic
*   + No asymptomatic
*   + Symptoms resolved
* **Which Symptoms Are You Experiencing?** (`symptoms_list`, select_multiple, optional): Select all symptoms you are experiencing from the list.
*   + Fever or chills
*   + Cough
*   + Shortness of breath
*   + Fatigue
*   + Muscle aches
*   + Headache
*   + Loss of taste or smell
*   + Sore throat
*   + Congestion
*   + Nausea or vomiting
*   + Diarrhea
* **COVID-19 Test Date** (`test_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when you were tested for COVID-19.
* **Test Result** (`test_result`, select_one, optional): Select the result of your COVID-19 test.
*   + Positive
*   + Negative
*   + Pending
*   + Not tested
* **Healthcare Provider Name** (`healthcare_provider_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your doctor or healthcare provider.
* **Healthcare Provider Phone** (`healthcare_provider_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your doctor or healthcare provider's office.
* **Household Members** (`household_members`, number, required): Enter the number of people living in your household, including yourself.
* **High-Risk Household Members** (`high_risk_household`, select_one, required): Select if anyone in your household has high-risk conditions.
*   + Yes
*   + No
*   + Not sure
* **Able to Isolate Properly** (`able_to_isolate_properly`, select_one, required): Select if you are able to isolate properly at home.
*   + Yes I have a separate room and bathroom
*   + Yes I have a separate room only
*   + No I cannot properly isolate
* **Need Assistance** (`need_assistance`, select_one, required): Select if you need any assistance during isolation.
*   + No, I am fine
*   + Yes, food delivery
*   + Yes, medical check-ins
*   + Yes, mental health support
*   + Yes, multiple types of assistance
* **Work from Home Capability** (`work_from_home_capability`, select_one, required): Select your remote work capability during isolation.
*   + Yes fully capable
*   + Yes partially capable
*   + No unable to work
* **Daily Check-ups Preference** (`daily_checkup_preference`, select_one, required): Select your preferred method for receiving daily check-ups.
*   + Phone call
*   + Text message
*   + Email
*   + Video call
*   + No check-ups needed
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Additional Comments or Needs** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or needs you have.
* **Acknowledgment** (`employee_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Select if you understand you must not return to work until cleared by health authorities.
*   + Yes I understand and agree
*   + No I do not agree
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the form.

Please fill out this form accurately and completely, and contact your supervisor or HR representative if you have any questions or concerns.
