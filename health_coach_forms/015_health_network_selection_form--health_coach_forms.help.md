# health_network_selection_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about your health network, which will help us improve our services and ensure accurate record-keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure all required fields are filled out. If a field is marked as "required," please make sure to complete it accurately.
2. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information about your health network.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Insurance Plan** (`insurance_plan`, select_multiple, required): 
Please select all insurance plans that apply to you. This may include private insurance, employer-sponsored insurance, or Medicaid.

### 2. **Health Provider** (`health_provider`, select_one, required): 
Please select "Yes" if you have a health provider, such as a primary care physician or specialist, that you see on a regular basis. If not, select "No".

### 3. **Address** (`address`, text, required): 
Enter your address accurately, including the street number, street name, apartment number (if applicable), city, state, and zip code.

### 4. **City** (`city`, text, required): 
Enter the city where you live or have your address.

### 5. **State** (`state`, text, required): 
Enter the state where you live or have your address.

### 6. **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): 
Enter your zip code accurately, as this is essential for postal and medical record-keeping.

### 7. **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): 
Enter your date of birth, using the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### 8. **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): 
Enter your contact phone number, if you prefer to be contacted for further assistance or updates. This is optional, but it will help us communicate with you more efficiently.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you need help or clarification about any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- This form is for administrative or medical purposes, so please be honest and accurate in your responses.
