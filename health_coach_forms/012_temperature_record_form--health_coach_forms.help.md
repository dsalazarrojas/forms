# Temperature Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Temperature Record Form is used to record a patient's temperature readings for monitoring and tracking progress in a healthcare setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you have all necessary information and permissions to fill out this form.
2. Select the correct answer for "User Input" as "Yes" if the patient has a specific medical condition or condition that requires temperature recording, otherwise select "No".
3. Enter the patient's name in the "User Name" field.
4. Select the date of the temperature reading in the "Date" field.
5. Choose the time of day for the temperature reading in the "Time of Day" field.
6. Enter the patient's body temperature in the "Temperature" field.
7. Provide any additional comments or notes about the temperature reading in the "Notes" field.
8. (Optional) If you have the patient's email address, enter it in the "User Email" field.
9. (Optional) If you have the patient's phone number, enter it in the "User Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`User Input`, select_one, **required**): Select "Yes" if the patient has a specific medical condition or condition that requires temperature recording, "No" otherwise.
* **User Name** (`User Name`, text, **required**): Enter the patient's name.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, **required**): Choose the date of the temperature reading.
* **Time of Day** (`Time of Day`, time, **required**): Choose the time of day for the temperature reading.
* **Temperature** (`Temperature`, number, **required**): Enter the patient's body temperature.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, **optional**): Provide any additional comments or notes about the temperature reading.
* **User Email** (`User Email`, email, **optional**): (If available) Enter the patient's email address.
* **User Phone** (`User Phone`, text, **optional**): (If available) Enter the patient's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to record temperature readings at regular intervals as prescribed by the healthcare professional.
* Be accurate when recording the temperature reading.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please consult with the healthcare professional before proceeding.
