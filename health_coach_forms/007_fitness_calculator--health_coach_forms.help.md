# fitness_calculator - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users calculate their fitness goals and track their progress by providing a set of fields to input various aspects of their physical activity, exercise routines, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your height, weight, age, exercise duration, and rest days.
2. Select your exercise frequency, exercise level, and type.
3. Choose your workout days and time of day for exercise.
4. Select your goal (weight loss, gain, maintain, or improve overall fitness).
5. Enter your goal frequency.
6. Provide your current weight.
7. Input your target weight.
8. Choose your exercise type (cardio, strength, flexibility, balance, stretching, or other).
9. Select your exercise intensity (high, low, or medium).
10. Choose your workout type (weightlifting, cardio, stretching, or resting).
11. Input your exercise time (optional).
12. Select your rest type (morning, evening, afternoon, or any time).
13. Choose your exercise time of day (morning, evening, afternoon, or any time).
14. Enter your exercise frequency.
15. Enter your rest frequency.
16. Enter your form submission comments (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **height**: Enter your height in inches.
* **weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **age**: Enter your age in years.
* **exercise_duration**: Enter the duration of your exercises in minutes.
* **rest_days**: Enter the number of rest days you have per week.
* **exercise_frequency**: Select how frequently you exercise per week (number).
* **exercise_level**: Choose your exercise level (Low, Medium, High).
* **exercise_type**: Select the types of exercises you perform (cardio, strength, flexibility, balance, stretching, or other).
* **exercise_intensity**: Choose your exercise intensity (High, Low, Medium).
* **workout_days**: Select the days you perform your workouts (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **workout_time**: Enter the time you exercise (optional).
* **sleep_quality**: Currently not included in the YAML, but it would be a good idea to include this field.
* **goal**: Choose your fitness goal (Lose weight, Gain weight, Maintain weight, Improve overall fitness).
* **goal_frequency**: Enter how often you want to achieve your goal (number).
* **current_weight**: Enter your current weight in pounds.
* **target_weight**: Enter your target weight in pounds.
* **exercise_type**: Select the types of exercises you perform (cardio, strength, flexibility, balance, stretching, or other).
* **workout_type**: Choose your workout type (weightlifting, cardio, stretching, or resting).
* **workout_time**: Enter the time you spend working out (optional).
* **rest_type**: Select the time of day you rest (morning, evening, afternoon, or any time).
* **exercise_time_of_day**: Choose the time of day you exercise (morning, evening, afternoon, or any time).
* **exercise_frequency**: Select how frequently you exercise (number).
* **rest_frequency**: Enter the number of rest days you have per week.
* **form_submission**: Enter any additional comments about your form submission (optional).

Note: This help guide is based on the provided YAML, but some fields may not be included due to missing information (e.g., **sleep_quality**).
