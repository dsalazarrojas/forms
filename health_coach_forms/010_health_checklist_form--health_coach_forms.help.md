# health_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect health and wellness information from a user, including medical history, physical activity, sleep quality, stress levels, nutrition, mental health, social support, substance use, health care, health risk factors, medication, medical conditions, medical procedures, allergies, vital signs, height, weight, blood pressure, blood oxygen saturation, temperature, pulse, and respiratory rate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer each question carefully and accurately, providing as much detail as possible.
2. If you are unsure about any question, please provide your best estimate or indicate that you are unsure.
3. If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or other health concerns, please list them in the relevant sections.
4. If you are unable to answer a question, please indicate that it is not applicable or provide an explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History**: Please provide any medical conditions, surgeries, or injuries you have had in the past.
* **Family Health History**: Please list any family members with medical conditions or health concerns.
* **Physical Activity**: Please select your level of physical activity (Sedentary, Low, Moderate, or High).
* **Sleep Quality**: Please select your sleep quality (Fair, Good, or Poor).
* **Stress Level**: Please select your level of stress (Low, Moderate, or High).
* **Nutrition**: Please provide your dietary information, including any food allergies or restrictions.
* **Mental Health**: Please select your mental health status (Fair, Good, or Poor).
* **Social Support**: Please select your level of social support (High, Moderate, or Low).
* **Substance Use**: Please select your frequency of substance use (None, Occasional, Frequent, or Daily).
* **Health Care**: Please provide any relevant health care information, including current medications or medical procedures.
* **Health Risk Factors**: Please list any health risk factors you have, such as smoking or excessive drinking.
* **Medication**: Please list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Conditions**: Please list any medical conditions you have or are concerned about.
* **Medical Procedures**: Please list any medical procedures you have had or are scheduled for.
* **Allergies**: Please list any allergies you have.
* **Vital Signs**: Please provide your height, weight, blood pressure, blood oxygen saturation, temperature, pulse, and respiratory rate.
* **Height**: Please enter your height in inches or centimeters.
* **Weight**: Please enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
* **Blood Pressure**: Please enter your blood pressure in mmHg.
* **Blood Oxygen Saturation**: Please enter your blood oxygen saturation as a percentage.
* **Temperature**: Please enter your temperature in degrees Celsius or Fahrenheit.
* **Pulse**: Please enter your pulse rate in beats per minute.
* **Respiratory Rate**: Please enter your respiratory rate in breaths per minute.
* **Neurological System**: Please provide any information about your neurological system, including any concerns or conditions.
* **Other Notes**: Please provide any additional information about your health and wellness.
