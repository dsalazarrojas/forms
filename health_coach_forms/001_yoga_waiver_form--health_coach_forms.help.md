# yoga_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The yoga waiver form is used to collect information from clients or patients before they participate in yoga activities or classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and birthdate.
* If you have an emergency contact, fill in their name, relationship to you, and their contact information (phone number, email, and address).
* If you have any medical conditions or procedures, let us know in the "Medical Conditions" field.
* If you've had any previous injuries, please fill in the "Injury" field.
* If you're an instructor, please fill in your name, title, and contact information.
* Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please fill in your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, please fill in their name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Medical Procedure**: If you have undergone any medical procedures, please list them here.
* **Injury**: If you have had any previous injuries, please describe them here.
* **Signed**: Select "Yes" if you have signed this waiver. Select "No" otherwise.
* **Signature Date**: If you have signed this waiver, please enter the date.
* **Signature Time**: If you have signed this waiver, please enter the time.
* **Instructor 1 Name**: If you are an instructor, please fill in your name.
* **Instructor 1 Title**: If you are an instructor, please fill in your title.
* **Instructor 1 Phone**: If you are an instructor, please enter your phone number.
* **Instructor 1 Email**: If you are an instructor, please enter your email.
* **Instructor 1 Address**: If you are an instructor, please enter your address.
* **Instructor 1 Licensing Info**: If you are an instructor, please enter your licensing information.
* **Instructor 2 Name**: If you have another instructor involved, please fill in their name.
* **Instructor 2 Title**: If you have another instructor involved, please fill in their title.
* **Instructor 2 Phone**: If you have another instructor involved, please enter their phone number.
* **Instructor 2 Email**: If you have another instructor involved, please enter their email.
* **Instructor 2 Address**: If you have another instructor involved, please enter their address.
* **Instructor 2 Licensing Info**: If you have another instructor involved, please enter their licensing information.
