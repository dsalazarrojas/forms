# group_therapy_client_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by clients to provide feedback and suggestions to the therapy team. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the link provided by your therapist to access the form.
2. Complete all required fields. Although none are currently required, it's essential to fill out all sections as completely and accurately as possible.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: The form header section is the beginning of the form where you can enter any relevant context. Please keep your response brief and to the point. 
* **Client Information (Client Name, Date of Birth, Contact Method, Therapist Name):** 
   - **Client Name:** The field to enter your name.
   - **Date of Birth:** The field to enter your date of birth.
   - **Contact Method:** The field to select how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, etc.).
   - **Therapist Name:** The field to enter your therapist's name.
* **Therapist Information (Therapist Information):** This field is optional and should only be filled out if you are unsure about your therapist's name or would like to provide additional information about them.
* **Experience:** The field to rate your overall experience with the group therapy session. 
* **Satisfaction (number):** This field should be used to rate your satisfaction with the therapy session, with 1 being not satisfied and 5 being very satisfied.
* **Feedback (text):** This field is for you to provide any comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Suggestions (text):** This field is for you to provide any suggestions or ideas for future sessions.
* **Form Footer (Form Footer):** The form footer section is the end of the form where you can enter any final comments. 

## Tips
Please keep your responses brief and to the point. If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your therapist.
