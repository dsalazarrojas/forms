# Weight Loss Program Participant Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from program participants to help them get started on their weight loss journey. It will be used by the health coach to understand the participant's goals, medical history, and progress throughout the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

- Fill in all the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the label next to each field.
- Enter your name, email, phone number, and program start date accurately.
- Set your weight loss goals, health goals, and program duration.
- Provide medical conditions, medications, supplements, and allergies if you have any.
- Select your height and weight in the respective units you are comfortable with.
- Choose your program status (active or inactive).
- Provide any program notes or emergency contact information if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: <input type="text"> (Required) - Enter the name of the weight loss program you are participating in.
* **Name**: <input type="text"> (Required) - Enter your full name.
* **Email**: <input type="email"> (Required) - Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: <input type="text"> (Required) - Enter your phone number.
* **Program Start Date**: <input type="date"> (Required) - Enter the date you want to start the program.
* **Weight Loss Goals**: <input type="text"> (Required) - Describe your weight loss goals.
* **Health Goals**: <input type="text"> (Required) - Describe any other health-related goals you have.
* **Medical Conditions**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - List any medical conditions you have.
* **Medications**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Supplements**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - List any supplements you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - List any allergies you have.
* **Program Duration**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter the number of weeks or months you plan to participate in the program.
* **Program Length (Days)**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter the number of days in the program duration.
* **Weight Loss Goal**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter your target weight loss goal.
* **Target Weight**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter your target weight.
* **Maximum Weight Loss**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter the maximum weight loss you are aiming for.
* **Height**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter your height in the selected unit (ft, in, cm, m).
* **Weight**: <input type="number"> (Required) - Enter your weight in the selected unit (lbs, kg).
* **Height Unit**: <select> (Required) - Select the unit of measurement for your height.
* **Weight Unit**: <select> (Required) - Select the unit of measurement for your weight.
* **Program Status**: <select> (Optional) - Select your current program status (active or inactive).
* **Program Notes**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - Add any notes about your program.
* **Emergency Contact**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: <input type="text"> (Optional) - Enter the emergency contact's phone number.

Note: If you need to get in touch with the program coach, you can enter your emergency contact information for added support.
