Medical Intake Form - Help Guide
==========================

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from patients about their medical history, past surgeries, medical genetics, and symptoms. The information collected will help healthcare professionals provide better care and make informed decisions about the patient's treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please answer all questions honestly and accurately to the best of your knowledge.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you are unsure about any question, you can ask for clarification or guidance from a healthcare professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required): A brief description of your medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or conditions that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Past Surgeries** (`past_surgeries`, select_multiple, optional): Check all the past surgeries you have undergone, such as 'Yes' if you have had a hip replacement, 'No' if you have not.
* **Medical Genetics** (`medical_genetics`, text, optional): Information about your genetic makeup, including any known genetic disorders or conditions that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Symptoms** (`symptoms`, select_one, optional): Indicate your current symptoms, such as 'Yes' if you are experiencing pain, 'No' if you are not experiencing any symptoms.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* If you are unsure about any question, please ask for guidance from a healthcare professional.
* This form is for your medical information only, and your responses will be kept confidential.
