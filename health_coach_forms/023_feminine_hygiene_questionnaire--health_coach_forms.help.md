# Feminine Hygiene Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Feminine Hygiene Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your experiences and habits related to your menstrual cycle, specifically regarding feminine hygiene products and their management of period-related discomfort and emotions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Carefully read through each section and question, as they are designed to gather specific information about your experiences and habits.
2. Choose the answers that best fit your situation for each question.
3. Make sure to provide honest and accurate information, as it will help us better understand your needs and improve our products and services.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **How often do you use the toilet?** (`user_experience`, select_multiple): This question aims to understand your habits and routines related to using the toilet during your menstrual cycle.
    * Please select all the days you use the toilet during your period: 
    * Yes: All days
    * No: None of the days
    * Other: Specify (please use the free text field for this)
- **How long is your menstrual period?** (`period_length`, select_one): This question is about the length of your menstrual cycle.
    * Choose one of the options that best describes your typical menstrual period length:
    * 3 days or less
    * 4-6 days
    * 7-10 days
    * 11-14 days
    * 15 days or more
- **Do you use any of the following feminine hygiene products?** (`hygiene_product_use`, select_multiple): This question aims to understand your usage habits of various feminine hygiene products.
    * Please select all the products you use during your period:
    * Sanitary pads
    * Menstrual cups
    * Menstrual sponges
    * Other (please specify)
- **Which type of feminine hygiene product do you prefer?** (`product_preference`, select_one): This question is about your preferences for feminine hygiene products.
    * Choose the product you prefer:
    * Sanitary pads
    * Menstrual cups
    * Menstrual sponges
    * Other
- **How often do you use each product?** (`product_use_frequency`, select_multiple): This question aims to understand your usage frequency of each product.
    * Please select all the products you use during your period:
    * Yes: All days
    * No: None of the days
    * Other: Specify (please use the free text field for this)
- **How important is the convenience factor when choosing a product?** (`product_use_convenience`, number): This question is about how much you value convenience when selecting a product.
    * On a scale of 1-5, with 5 being very important
- **Do you experience any discomfort during your period?** (`period_discomfort`, select_one): This question is about your experiences with discomfort during your period.
    * Do you experience discomfort during your period:
    * False: No
    * True: Yes
    * Rarely
    * Sometimes
    * Almost always
- **Have you tried any pain management techniques during your period?** (`pain_management`, select_multiple): This question aims to understand your experiences with pain management during your period.
    * Please select all the pain management techniques you've tried:
    * Yes: All the options
    * No: None of the options
- **Do you find that managing stress during your period helps?** (`stress_management`, select_one): This question is about how managing stress affects your period experiences.
    * Do you find managing stress helpful during your period:
    * True: Yes
    * False: No
- **Do you experience emotional changes during your period?** (`emotional_changes`, select_one): This question is about your experiences with emotional changes during your period.
    * Do you experience emotional changes during your period:
    * False: No
    * True: Yes
