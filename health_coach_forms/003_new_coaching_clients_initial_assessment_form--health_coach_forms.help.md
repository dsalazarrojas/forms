# New Coaching Clients Initial Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a comprehensive initial assessment tool for health coaches and personal trainers to gather essential information about their potential clients' needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To get the most out of this form, please follow these steps:
1. Carefully review each field to understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out your information accurately and completely.
3. If a field is not applicable to you, please indicate this by writing "N/A" (not applicable) in the field.
4. If you need clarification or assistance, please contact the form owner at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (page 1)** (`client_information`, text, optional)
	+ This is the section where you can provide your personal contact information and any other details you'd like to share with your health coach or personal trainer.
* **Health Coach Form (page 1)** (`health_coach_form`, text, optional)
	+ Use this section to share your thoughts, feelings, and experiences related to your health and wellness journey. Please provide as much detail as possible, including any successes, challenges, or obstacles you've faced.
* **Personal Trainer Form (page 1)** (`personal_trainer_form`, text, optional)
	+ If you're working with a personal trainer, use this section to provide information about your fitness goals, experience, and any relevant health or fitness history.
* **Client Goals (page 2)** (`client_goals`, text, optional)
	+ In this section, describe your short-term and long-term goals related to your health, wellness, and fitness. Be as specific as possible, and include any metrics or milestones you'd like to achieve.

## Tips
- Take your time and answer each question carefully to ensure you're providing accurate and relevant information.
- If you've filled out a similar form before, please let us know, so we can assist you better.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to the form owner at form@gic.mx.
