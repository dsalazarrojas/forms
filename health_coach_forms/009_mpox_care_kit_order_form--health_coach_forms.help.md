# Mpox Care Kit Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mpox Care Kit Order Form is designed to collect information from customers to fulfill their specific needs for a Mpox care kit. This form helps you to understand their preferences for the kit type, size, and additional items, ensuring that the order is accurate and efficient. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields. 
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the order date for the care kit.
4. Choose the desired quantity of the care kit.
5. Select the type of care kit you want to order (Standard, Deluxe, or Premium).
6. Choose the size of the care kit you want (Small, Medium, or Large).
7. Select any additional items you would like to include with your order.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your order if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name
* **First name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last name
* **Last name**: This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about your order.

### Phone
* **Phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### Order date
* **Order date**: This field is for the date you would like to fulfill your order. Please select the date from the available options.

### Quantity
* **Quantity**: This field is for the number of care kits you would like to order. Please select the quantity you require.

### Kit type
* **Kit type**: This field is for selecting the type of care kit you want to order. Choose from Standard, Deluxe, or Premium.

### Kit size
* **Kit size**: This field is for selecting the size of the care kit you want to order. Choose from Small, Medium, or Large.

### Additional items
* **Additional items**: This field is for selecting any additional items you would like to include with your order. Choose from Item 1, Item 2, or Item 3.

### Notes
* **Notes**: This field is for leaving any additional comments or notes about your order. If needed, please add any special requests or information in this field.
