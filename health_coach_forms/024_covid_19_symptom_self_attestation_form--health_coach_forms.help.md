# COVID 19 Symptom Self Attestation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect patient information for COVID-19 symptom self-attestation, allowing healthcare professionals to quickly assess and provide guidance or medical attention as needed.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your date of attestation, which is the date you are completing this form.
3. Provide your full name, date of birth, and contact information (phone number and email address).
4. Select your gender and specify your home address, city, and state.
5. Provide your zip code and information about your date of exposure to COVID-19 (if applicable).
6. Describe your exposure circumstances.
7. List any symptoms you are experiencing, selecting all that apply.
8. Indicate the severity of your symptoms.
9. Report any underlying medical conditions you have.
10. List your primary care physician and their contact information (if applicable).
11. Indicate whether you have any health insurance.
12. Provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
13. Confirm whether you have sought medical advice for your symptoms.
14. Summarize any medical advice received (if applicable).
15. Declare your attestation of the information provided.
16. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attestation Date** (id: 1): Enter the date you are completing this form, which is the date of attestation.
* **Patient Full Name** (id: 2): Enter your first and last name as they appear on your identification.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 3): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (id: 4): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Phone Number** (id: 5): Enter your contact phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Email Address** (id: 6): Enter your contact email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Home Address** (id: 7): Enter your street address where you reside.
* **City** (id: 8): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (id: 9): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (id: 10): Enter the zip code of your residence.
* **Date of COVID-19 Exposure** (id: 11): If you have been exposed to COVID-19, enter the date of exposure. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Describe the Exposure** (id: 12): If you have been exposed, describe the circumstances.
* **Date Symptoms Began** (id: 13): Enter the date your symptoms started.
* **Which Symptoms are You Experiencing** (id: 14): Select all symptoms that apply to your condition.
* **How Severe are Your Symptoms** (id: 15): Indicate the level of severity of your symptoms.
* **Current Temperature** (id: 16): Enter your current body temperature in Fahrenheit.
* **Breathing Difficulty** (id: 17): Indicate whether you are experiencing breathing difficulty.
* **Chest Pain** (id: 18): Indicate whether you are experiencing chest pain or pressure.
* **Confusion Symptoms** (id: 19): Indicate whether you are experiencing new confusion or inability to wake.
* **Bluish Lips or Face** (id: 20): Indicate whether you are experiencing bluish lips or face.
* **Underlying Medical Conditions** (id: 21): Select any underlying medical conditions you have.
* **Primary Care Physician Name** (id: 22): If applicable, enter the name of your primary care physician.
* **Physician Phone** (id: 23): If applicable, enter the contact phone number of your primary care physician.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (id: 24): If applicable, enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (id: 25): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 26): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Have you Sought Medical Advice for Your Symptoms** (id: 27): Indicate whether you have sought medical advice for your symptoms.
* **Summary of Medical Advice Received** (id: 28): If applicable, provide a summary of any medical advice received.
* **I Attest that the Information Provided is True and Accurate** (id: 29): Declare your attestation of the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (id: 30): Sign and date the form, indicating your attestation.

## Tips

* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer back to the provided explanations above.
* Be thorough and accurate when completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your healthcare provider or emergency services.

Please note that this form is for COVID-19 symptom self-attestation purposes only and is not intended for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you are experiencing severe symptoms or have concerns about your condition, please contact your healthcare provider or emergency services immediately.
