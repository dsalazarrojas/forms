# flu_mask_compliance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The flu mask compliance form is used to track and document patient information related to flu exposure, symptoms, and prevention measures in a medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the medical facility where the patient was seen or treated.
2. Select the current flu status of the patient (e.g., Flu, No Flu).
3. Choose the method(s) of contact used to reach the patient (e.g., In Person, Phone, No Contact).
4. Select the method(s) used to contact the patient (e.g., In Person, Phone, Email, Text).
5. (Optional) Enter any additional symptoms experienced by the patient that are not listed.
6. (Optional) Enter the date the patient was exposed to the flu.
7. (Optional) Enter the end date of the patient's flu exposure.
8. (Optional) Enter the duration of the patient's flu exposure.
9. Select the type of flu the patient has (e.g., Influenza A, Influenza B, Other).
10. (Optional) Select if any medicine was used to treat the flu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Facility**: The medical facility where the patient was seen or treated. This is a required field and should be completed for every entry.
* **Flu Status**: A select field to indicate the patient's current flu status. Options are: Flu, No Flu. Please select the appropriate status.
* **Patient Contact**: The method used to contact the patient. Choose from the options: In Person, Phone, No Contact. This field is required.
* **Contact Method**: The method of contact used by the healthcare worker to reach the patient. Choose from the options: In Person, Phone, Email, Text. This field is required.
* **Flu Symptoms**: If the patient reported any additional symptoms not listed, enter them here (if applicable).
* **Flu Exposure**: Enter the date the patient was exposed to the flu (if applicable).
* **Flu Exposure End**: Enter the date the patient's flu exposure ended (if applicable).
* **Flu Exposure Duration**: Enter the duration of the patient's flu exposure (if applicable).
* **Flu Type**: Select the type of flu the patient has. Options are: Influenza A, Influenza B, Other.
* **Flu Symptoms Other**: Enter any additional symptoms experienced by the patient that are not listed (if applicable).
* **Flu Medicine Used**: Select if any medicine was used to treat the patient's flu. Choose from the options: True, False.
