# zumba_medical_history_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your Zumba participation, including your frequency, duration, intensity, and medical history, to help health coaches or trainers assess your Zumba experience and potential risks or benefits associated with your practice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly. You can select one or multiple options for certain fields as instructed. You can also provide additional information in the text fields to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_information`, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your name, contact information, or any other relevant details about yourself. If you're not sure what to write, simply leave this field blank.
* **Zumba Participation** (`zumba_participation`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you participate in Zumba:
	+ Zumba 1-2 times per week
	+ Zumba 3-4 times per week
	+ Zumba 5 or more times per week
	+ Never
* **Duration of Zumba** (`duration_of_zumba`, select_multiple, optional): Select how long you have been practicing Zumba:
	+ Less than 6 months
	+ 6 months to 1 year
	+ 1-2 years
	+ 2-5 years
	+ 5+ years
* **Intensity of Zumba** (`intensity_of_zumba`, select_one, optional): Please select the intensity level of your Zumba practice:
	+ Low intensity
	+ Medium intensity
	+ High intensity
* **Frequency of Zumba** (`zumba_frequency`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of your Zumba practice:
	+ 30 minutes
	+ 45 minutes
	+ 1 hour
	+ More than 1 hour
* **Frequency per Week** (`zumba_frequency_per_week`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you practice Zumba per week:
	+ 1-2 times
	+ 3-4 times
	+ 5 or more times
	+ Never
* **Frequency per Month** (`zumba_frequency_per_month`, select_one, optional): Select how often you practice Zumba per month:
	+ 1-2 times
	+ 3-4 times
	+ 5 or more times
	+ Never
* **Frequency per Year** (`zumba_frequency_per_year`, select_one, optional): Please select how often you practice Zumba per year:
	+ 1-2 times
	+ 3-4 times
	+ 5 or more times
	+ Never
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, note, optional): This field is for you to describe any medical conditions that may be relevant to your Zumba practice. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.
* **Doctor Questions** (`doctor_questions`, text, optional): Please answer any questions or concerns your doctor may have about your medical history or Zumba practice.
* **Zumba Injuries** (`zumba_injuries`, text, optional): Describe any injuries you've experienced while practicing Zumba. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Zumba Therapy** (`zumba_therapy`, text, optional): This field is for you to describe any therapies or treatments you've received related to your Zumba practice. Please be specific and provide as much detail as possible.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please leave it blank.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the health coach or trainer for assistance.
