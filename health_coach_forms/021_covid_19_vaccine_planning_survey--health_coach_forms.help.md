# COVID 19 Vaccine Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your organization's COVID-19 vaccine planning and capabilities. This includes details about your organization, vaccine storage, administration, and safety protocols.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer each field according to your organization's current status. 

*   Please fill out the form in its entirety to provide a comprehensive view of your organization's capabilities.
*   If an option does not apply to your organization, please select "No plan" or "No" accordingly.
*   Please provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization Name** (`org_name`, required, text): Enter the name of your healthcare organization.
*   **Organization Type** (`org_type`, required, select_one): Select the type of healthcare organization you are representing.
*   **Organization Size** (`org_size`, required, select_one): Indicate the number of employees in your organization.
*   **Organization Address** (`org_address`, required, text): Enter the street address of your organization.
*   **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person_survey`, required, text): Enter the name of the person responsible for planning.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email_survey`, required, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_survey`, required, phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Has Vaccination Plan** (`has_vaccine_plan`, required, select_one): Indicate whether your organization has a COVID-19 vaccination plan documented or not.
*   **Vaccination Plan Details** (`plan_details`, optional, text): Provide an overview of your vaccination strategy.
*   **Staff Education Plan** (`staff_education_plan`, required, select_one): Indicate whether your organization has a plan to educate staff on COVID-19 vaccines.
*   **Ultra-Cold Freezers** (`ultra_cold_freezers`, required, integer): Enter the number of ultra-cold freezers available.
*   **Standard Freezers** (`standard_freezers`, required, integer): Enter the number of standard freezers available.
*   **Refrigerators** (`refrigerators`, required, integer): Enter the number of refrigerators available.
*   **Total Storage Capacity - Doses** (`total_storage_capacity`, optional, integer): Enter the total storage capacity for vaccine doses.
*   **Temperature Monitoring Systems** (`monitoring_systems`, optional, select_multiple): Indicate how you monitor storage temperatures.
*   **Trained Vaccine Administrators** (`trained_vaccinators`, required, integer): Enter the number of trained vaccine administrators.
*   **Vaccination Sites** (`vaccination_locations`, required, integer): Enter the number of vaccination sites.
*   **Daily Vaccination Capacity - Doses** (`daily_capacity`, required, integer): Enter the number of vaccine doses that can be administered daily.
*   **Adverse Event Plan** (`adverse_event_plan`, required, select_one): Indicate whether you have a formal plan for reporting adverse events.
*   **Estimated Readiness Date for Vaccinations** (`completion_date`, optional, date): Enter the estimated date when you will be ready for vaccinations.

## Tips
*   Please be honest and provide accurate information about your organization's capabilities.
*   If you need help or have questions, contact the form authors.
*   The data collected will be used to assess the readiness of healthcare organizations for COVID-19 vaccination.
