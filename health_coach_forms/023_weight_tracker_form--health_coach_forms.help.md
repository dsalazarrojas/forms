# Weight Tracker Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weight Tracker Form is a tool to track user's weight over time. It is designed to help users monitor their progress in a health coaching context.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your weight in the "user_weight" field. Please enter a numerical value (e.g., 70 kg or 150 lbs).
2. Select a weight unit from the "tracker_unit" field (kg, lbs, or oz).
3. Choose a tracking frequency from the "tracker_frequency" field (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
4. If desired, set a goal weight in the "tracker_goal" field.
5. If desired, set a target weight in the "tracker_target" field.
6. Enter a date in the "weight_tracker" field to log your weight on a specific day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_weight**: Enter your current weight, e.g., 70 kg or 150 lbs.
* **weight_tracker**: Enter a date to log your weight, e.g., 2023-02-01.
* **tracker_frequency**: Choose how often you'd like to track your weight (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **tracker_unit**: Select the unit of measurement for your weight (kg, lbs, or oz).
* **tracker_goal**: (Optional) Set a goal weight to aim for.
* **tracker_target**: (Optional) Set a target weight to aim for on a specific date.

## Tips
* Please ensure to enter accurate data for tracking progress.
* You can adjust tracking frequency and units as per your convenience.
* Goal and target weights can be used to set milestones and deadlines.
