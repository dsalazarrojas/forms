# Face Mask Requirement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Face Mask Requirement Survey is designed to collect data about an individual's face mask requirements and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1.  Please answer the questions based on your personal experiences and habits regarding face masks.
2.  The form is divided into five sections, each focusing on different aspects of face mask usage.
3.  Please answer all questions to the best of your ability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Face Mask Requirement Survey** (`face_mask_requirement_survey`, `text`, Not Required)
This is the introduction section of the form. Please type a brief description of your face mask requirements or habits in this field.

### **Face Mask Type** (`face_mask_type`, `select_one`, Not Required)
Please select the type of face mask you usually wear:

*   Surgical
*   Cloth
*   N95
*   Other

### **Face Mask Frequency** (`face_mask_frequency`, `select_multiple`, Not Required)
Please select how often you wear a face mask:

*   Always
*   When in close proximity
*   When in close proximity and outside
*   Never

### **Face Mask Requiring** (`face_mask_requiring`, `text`, Not Required)
In this field, please describe situations where you require face masks (if any).

### **Face Mask Preventing** (`face_mask_preventing`, `select_one`, Not Required)
Please answer whether face masks prevent the spread of diseases:

*   True
*   False

### **Additional Comments** (`text`, `text`, Not Required)
If you have any additional comments or information about your face mask habits, please provide them here.
