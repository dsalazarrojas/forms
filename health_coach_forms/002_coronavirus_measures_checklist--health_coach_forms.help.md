# coronavirus_measures_checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
The coronavirus_measures_checklist form is designed to help users determine their risk level of coronavirus exposure. It is essential to complete this form carefully to ensure accurate results.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select all the symptoms you are experiencing from the dropdown list (page 1).
2. Enter your travel history in the text field on page 2.
3. Enter your current body temperature in the number field on page 3.
4. Choose "Yes" or "No" for recent travel on page 4.
5. Select all the exposure risks you have had from the dropdown list on page 5.
6. Select a date when you first experienced symptoms on page 6.
7. (Optional) Enter the time when your temperature was taken on page 7.
8. (Optional) Describe your symptoms in the text field on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Symptoms** (required): Select all the symptoms you are experiencing, such as fever, cough, or shortness of breath.
* **Travel History** (optional): Describe your recent travel history, including any countries or regions you have visited within the last 14 days.
* **Temperature** (required): Enter your current body temperature in the format "X.XX", e.g., "37.5".
* **Recent Travel** (required): Choose "Yes" if you have traveled recently, and "No" otherwise.
* **Exposure** (required): Select all the exposure risks you have had, such as contact with someone with COVID-19 or exposure to a COVID-19 positive environment.
* **Symptoms Date** (required): Select a date when you first experienced symptoms.
* **Temperature Taken At** (optional): Enter the time when your temperature was taken, in the format "HH:MM".
* **Symptoms Description** (optional): Describe your symptoms in detail, including any other relevant information.
