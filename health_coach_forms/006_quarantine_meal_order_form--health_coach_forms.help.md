# Quarantine Meal Order Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the user's meal order preferences during a quarantine or isolation period.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address if you want to receive updates on your meal order.
3. Enter your Phone number if you want to receive a call or text from the health coach.
4. Select a Date and Time for your meal order.
5. Add any additional Note or request for your meal order.
6. Select a Category for your meal (e.g., Food, Snack, Drink, etc.).
7. Choose a Food type (e.g., Chicken, Pizza, Burger, etc.).
8. Enter the Quantity of the meal you want to order.
9. If you have an Allergy, add it in the "Allergy" field.
10. If you have a Medication, enter it in the "Medication" field.
11. If you take Medication, enter the Frequency of taking it.
12. If you take Medication, enter the Dosage of it.
13. Enter any Special Request for your meal order.
14. If you have a Therapist, enter their Name and contact information.
15. If you have a Therapist, enter the nature of your relationship.
16. Enter the Treatment you are undergoing.
17. If you take Medication, enter the Dose and Dose Instructions.
18. If you take Medication, enter the Dose Duration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for contact purposes.
* **Last Name** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates on your meal order.
* **Phone** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to receive a call or text from the health coach.
* **Date** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_date`, date, optional): Select a date for your meal order.
* **Time** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_time`, time, optional): Select a time for your meal order.
* **Note** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_note`, note, optional): Add any additional note or request for your meal order.
* **Category** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_category`, select_multiple, optional): Select a category for your meal (e.g., Food, Snack, Drink, etc.).
* **Food** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_food`, select_one, optional): Choose a food type (e.g., Chicken, Pizza, Burger, etc.).
* **Quantity** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_quantity`, number, optional): Enter the quantity of the meal you want to order.
* **Allergy** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_allergy`, note, optional): If you have an allergy, add it in this field.
* **Medication** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_medication`, note, optional): If you have a medication, enter it in this field.
* **Medication Frequency** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_medication_frequency`, text, optional): If you take medication, enter the frequency of taking it.
* **Medication Dosage** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_medication_dosage`, text, optional): If you take medication, enter the dosage of it.
* **Special Request** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_special_request`, text, optional): Enter any special request for your meal order.
* **Therapist Name** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_therapist_name`, text, optional): If you have a therapist, enter their name.
* **Therapist Phone** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_therapist_phone`, text, optional): If you have a therapist, enter their phone number.
* **Therapist Email** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_therapist_email`, email, optional): If you have a therapist, enter their email address.
* **Therapist Relationship** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_therapist_relationship`, text, optional): If you have a therapist, enter the nature of your relationship.
* **Treatment** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_treatment`, text, optional): Enter the treatment you are undergoing.
* **Dose** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_dose`, text, optional): If you take medication, enter the dose.
* **Dose Instructions** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_dose_instructions`, text, optional): If you take medication, enter the dose instructions.
* **Dose Duration** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_dose_duration`, text, optional): If you take medication, enter the dose duration.
* **Other** (`quarantine_meal_order_form_other`, note, optional): Enter any other information not covered in the previous fields.

## Tips

* Please fill in all the required fields to ensure your meal order is accurately processed.
* If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact the health coach.
* This form is designed to be user-friendly and easy to fill out. If you experience any difficulties, please let the health coach know so they can assist you further.
