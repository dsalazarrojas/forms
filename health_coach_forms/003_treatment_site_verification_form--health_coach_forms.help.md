# Treatment Site Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treatment Site Verification Form is designed to collect information about treatment sites, which are locations where medical treatment is provided. This form is meant to verify the accuracy of this information for administrative or record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your user information (page 1).
2. Provide your condition information (page 2).
3. Enter your medical organization (page 3).
4. Share your patient record details (page 4).
5. Confirm the treatment site (page 5).
6. Add any additional information about the treatment site (page 6).
7. Provide information about your illness (page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (1): This field is where you will enter your name and any other relevant user details.
* **Condition** (2): Enter a brief description of your condition, which is likely the health issue you are experiencing.
* **Medical Organization** (3): Type in the name of the medical organization or facility where you received or will receive treatment.
* **Patient Record** (4): Share your patient record information, which can include medical history, identification numbers, or other relevant details.
* **Treatment Site Confirmation** (5): From the provided options, select the medical organization that confirms the treatment site where you received or will receive treatment.
* **Treatment Site Additional Information** (6): This field is a text box where you can add any additional information about the treatment site that is not covered by other fields. For example, room numbers, doctor's names, or treatment dates.
* **Illness Information** (7): Enter any additional information about your illness, which can include symptoms, diagnosis, or treatment specifics. 
# Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to ensure the form's purpose of verifying treatment sites is fulfilled. 
# If the form is not relevant to your situation, please let us know so we can improve the form.
