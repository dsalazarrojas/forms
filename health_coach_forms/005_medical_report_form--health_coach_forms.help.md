# Medical Report Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Report Form is a tool for healthcare providers to gather information about a patient's medical history, treatment plans, and allergies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Select the medical specialty from the "Medical Specialty" dropdown menu.
3. Describe the patient's condition in the "Patient Condition" field.
4. Outline the patient's treatment plan in the "Treatment Plan" field.
5. List any medications the patient is currently taking in the "Medication" field.
6. Provide a brief medical history for the patient in the "Medical History" field.
7. List any allergies the patient has in the "Patient Allergies" field.
8. Note any recent medical tests or procedures the patient has undergone in the "Patient Medical Tests" field.
9. Describe the expected treatment outcome in the "Treatment Outcome" field.
10. Sign your name and title in the "Doctor Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, text, optional): Enter the patient's name.
* **Medical Specialty** (`medical_specialty`, select_one, optional): Select the medical specialty of the patient's condition (e.g., General Medicine, General Surgery, Orthopedics).
* **Patient Condition** (`patient_condition`, text, optional): Describe the patient's condition, including any symptoms, diagnoses, or concerns.
* **Treatment Plan** (`treatment_plan`, text, optional): Outline the patient's treatment plan, including any medications, therapies, or interventions.
* **Medication** (`medication`, text, optional): List any medications the patient is currently taking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Provide a brief medical history for the patient, including any significant events or conditions.
* **Patient Allergies** (`patient_allergies`, text, optional): List any allergies the patient has.
* **Patient Medical Tests** (`patient_medical_tests`, text, optional): Note any recent medical tests or procedures the patient has undergone.
* **Treatment Outcome** (`treatment_outcome`, text, optional): Describe the expected treatment outcome for the patient.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name and title as the doctor completing the form.
