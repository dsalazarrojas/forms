# Fit Life Coaching Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fit Life Coaching Intake Form is a questionnaire designed to gather information from users for fit life coaching services. This form helps the coach understand the user's preferences, goals, and needs to create a personalized coaching plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
* Make sure to check the "Emergency Contact" field to indicate whether or not you have an emergency contact person.
* If you have an emergency contact, provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
* Take a moment to review your answers and make sure everything is correct before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY).
* **Emergency Contact**: Check this box if you have an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Relationship**: If you have an emergency contact, describe their relationship to you (e.g., spouse, parent, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Note**: If you have an emergency contact, add any additional details about them or their role.
