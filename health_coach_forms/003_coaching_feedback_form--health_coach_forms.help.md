# # Coaching Feedback Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from clients about their coaching experience with our organization. Your input will help us understand what we're doing well and where we can improve.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to select or type in the relevant information.
2. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
4. Click "Submit" to send your feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`client_name`, required): This is your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Your email address will be used for follow-up communication.
* **Coaching Program** (`coaching_program`, required): Select the program or service you received coaching for (e.g., Personal Training, Nutrition Coaching, etc.).
* **How long have you been coached by us** (`coaching_duration`, required): Approximate duration of coaching you received.
* **Progress toward your goals** (`goal_progress`, required): Rate your progress from 1 - no progress to 5 - excellent progress.
* **Measurable results achieved** (`results_achieved`, optional): Describe specific improvements you've noticed.
* **Was your coaching personalized to your needs** (`personalization`, required): Rate the level of personalization from 1 - generic to 5 - highly personalized.
* **Level of Support from Coach** (`coach_support`, required): Rate the coach's support and motivation from 1 - unsupportive to 5 - exceptionally supportive.
* **Session frequency met your needs** (`session_frequency`, optional): Describe how well the session frequency met your needs.
* **Coach's expertise in their field** (`expert_knowledge`, required): Rate the coach's professional knowledge from 1 - lacking to 5 - expert level.
* **Quality of technique and instruction** (`technique_instruction`, required): Rate the coach's teaching of proper form and technique from 1 - poor to 5 - excellent.
* **Would you recommend our coaching to others** (`recommend_to_others`, required): Choose whether you would recommend our coaching to others.
* **Will you continue coaching with us** (`continue_coaching`, required): Choose whether you'll continue coaching with us.
* **Additional feedback or suggestions** (`feedback_comments`, optional): Provide any other comments about your coaching experience.

**Tips**

* Please answer each question truthfully and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about a question, select "Unsure" or "Neutral".
* If you have any additional feedback or suggestions, feel free to write them in the "Additional Feedback or Suggestions" field.
