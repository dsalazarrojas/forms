# life_coach_assessment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "life_coach_assessment_form" is a form designed to collect information from Life Coaches for assessment purposes. It is intended to gather data about the coach's background, experience, and certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used as your contact information.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field.
6. Select your coaching certification status from the "Coaching certification" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of years of experience you have as a coach in the "Years of experience" field.
8. Enter your Prophets & Pioneers ID in the "Prophets & Pioneers ID" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Coaching certification** (`coaching_certification`, `select_one`, optional): Select your coaching certification status. Options are:
	+ Certified
	+ Not certified
	+ Certified, but in training
* **Years of experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years you have been coaching.
* **Prophets & Pioneers ID** (`prophets_and_pioneers_id`, `number`, optional): Enter your Prophets & Pioneers ID, if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Life Coach team for assistance.
* Please note that this form is for coaching assessment purposes only. If you are not a Life Coach, please do not complete this form.
