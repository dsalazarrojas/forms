# Personal Hygiene Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This survey aims to gather information on your personal hygiene practices and habits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions as honestly as possible. You may select one or multiple options for each question, depending on the type of question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Hygiene Practices** (`personal_hygiene_practices`, text, required): Describe your personal hygiene practices, such as hand washing, showering, and brushing teeth.
* **Hygiene Frequency** (`hygiene_frequency`, select_one, required): Please select the frequency of your personal hygiene practices:
	+ Daily
	+ Several times a week
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Hand Washing** (`hand_washing`, select_multiple, required): Select all the occasions when you wash your hands:
	+ After using the bathroom
	+ After handling animals
	+ Before eating
	+ After blowing nose/throat
	+ Before bed
* **Showering** (`showering`, select_one, required): How often do you shower?
	+ Every day
	+ Several times a week
	+ Less than once a week
* **Brushing Teeth** (`brushing_teeth`, select_one, required): How often do you brush your teeth?
	+ Twice a day
	+ Once a day
	+ Several times a week
	+ Less than once a week
* **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, select_one, required): Do you exercise regularly?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Smoking Frequency** (`smoking_frequency`, select_one, required): How often do you smoke?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Alcohol Use** (`alcohol_use`, select_one, required): Do you use alcohol?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Stress Management** (`stress_management`, select_one, required): Do you practice stress management?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, select_one, required): How would you rate your sleep quality?
	+ Good
	+ Bad
* **Nutrition** (`nutrition`, select_one, required): How would you rate your nutrition?
	+ Good
	+ Bad
* **Self-Care** (`self_care`, select_one, required): Do you practice self-care?
	+ True
	+ False
* **Health Issues** (`health_issues`, select_multiple, required): Which of the following health issues do you experience?
	+ Headaches
	+ Fatigue
	+ Skin issues
	+ Other
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or insights, please add them here.

**Tips**

* Answer honestly and accurately to the best of your ability.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you may ask them in the additional comments section.
* Please complete the form at your own pace and do not rush.
