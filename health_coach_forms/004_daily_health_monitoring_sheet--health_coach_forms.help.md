# Daily Health Monitoring Sheet - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Health Monitoring Sheet is a form designed for daily monitoring of a patient's health metrics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Your last name.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required/optional): Your current age.
* **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, required/optional): Your current weight.
* **Height** (`height`, `number`, required/optional): Your current height.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Blood Pressure** (`blood_pressure`, `number`, required/optional): Your current blood pressure.
* **Pulse** (`pulse`, `number`, required/optional): Your current pulse.
* **Temperature** (`temperature`, `number`, required/optional): Your current temperature.
* **Blood Sugar** (`blood_sugar`, `number`, required/optional): Your current blood sugar level.
* **Respiratory Rate** (`respiratory_rate`, `number`, required/optional): Your current respiratory rate.
* **Oxygen Saturation** (`oxygen_saturation`, `number`, required/optional): Your current oxygen saturation level.

## Tips
* Please fill out as many fields as possible each day.
* If unsure, please ask for help.
* Review and update your entries regularly.
* Keep this form for your records.
* Share this form with your healthcare professional if required.
