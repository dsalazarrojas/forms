# Executive Coaching Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Executive Coaching Interest Form is a tool for expressing your interest in executive coaching. It helps our organization tailor coaching to your specific needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please answer the questions on each page honestly and thoroughly. You can skip questions that don't apply to you. If you're unsure about a question, feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (`1`, `note`, required: false): A brief introduction to your coaching goals and expectations.
* **Executive Coaching Goals** (`2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all goals that apply to your executive coaching interests. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' options.
* **Coaching Focus Areas** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select one area where you would like to focus on coaching. You can choose from Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Coaching Methods** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Indicate which coaching methods you are interested in. You can choose from 'Yes' or 'No' options.
* **Coaching Style** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the coaching style that resonates with you the most. You can choose from Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Coaching Frequency** (`6`, `number`, required: false): Specify the frequency of coaching sessions you are interested in.
* **Coaching Location** (`7`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the location where you'd like to receive coaching. You can choose from Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Goals for Achieving Coaching Goals** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Briefly describe your goals for achieving coaching goals.
* **Coaching Preferences** (`9`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred coaching approach. You can select from Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Feedback** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Share any feedback or suggestions you have about coaching.
* **Comments** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Add any additional comments about your coaching interests.
* **Executive Coaching Goals 2** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant executive coaching goals (if any).
* **Executive Coaching Goals 3** (`13`, `select_one`, required: false): If applicable, select an additional executive coaching goal.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions thoroughly and honestly.
* If you're unsure about any question, feel free to ask for clarification.
* You can skip sections that don't apply to you.
* If you have any feedback or suggestions, please share them with us.
* We'll review your responses to create a personalized coaching plan tailored to your needs and goals.
