# health_belief_model_questionnaire - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to assess your health beliefs and behaviors, including your motivation to adopt healthy habits, how often you exercise, and how you manage chronic health conditions. It will help us understand your health choices and identify areas where you might need support or guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each page of the form, one by one.
- Answer each question to the best of your ability.
- If you're unsure about a question, try to answer based on your general feelings or experiences.
- You can select multiple options for each question.
- Enter numbers when asked to do so.
