# Covid Questionnaire For Clients - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Covid Questionnaire for Clients is used to collect information about your health status regarding COVID-19. Please answer the questions accurately and honestly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Answer each question on the following page with as much detail as possible.
2. For each question, select one or more of the available options.
3. Proceed to the next page for additional questions.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ask about COVID** (`ask_about_covid`, text, optional): This field is a brief description of your experiences with COVID-19. Please provide any relevant details.
* **Have you been exposed to COVID?** (`have_you_been_exposed_to_covid`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Covid 1: You have been exposed to someone who has COVID-19.
	+ Covid 2: You have been exposed to someone who has symptoms of COVID-19.
	+ Covid 3: You have been exposed to someone who has been tested positive for COVID-19.
* **When did you experience COVID?** (`when_did_you_experience_covid`, date, optional): Please select the date when you first experienced COVID-19 symptoms.
* **Have you been hospitalized due to COVID?** (`have_you_been_hospitalized`, select_one, optional):
	+ False: You have not been hospitalized due to COVID-19.
	+ True: You have been hospitalized due to COVID-19.
* **Have you been diagnosed with COVID?** (`have_you_been_diagnosed_with_covid`, select_one, optional):
	+ False: You have not been diagnosed with COVID-19.
	+ True: You have been diagnosed with COVID-19.
* **Have you been exposed to someone with COVID?** (`have_you_been_exposed_to_someone_with_covid`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ False: You have not been exposed to someone with COVID-19.
	+ True: You have been exposed to someone with COVID-19.
* **Symptoms starting after** (`symptoms_starting_after`, date, optional): If you have been experiencing symptoms, enter the date when they started.
* **Have you fully recovered from COVID?** (`have_you_fully_recovered_from_covid`, select_one, optional):
	+ False: You have not fully recovered from COVID-19.
	+ True: You have fully recovered from COVID-19.
