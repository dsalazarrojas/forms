# Healthy Human Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthy Human Evaluation Form is a tool used to collect basic health-related information from users for evaluation or monitoring purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your height.
2. Enter your weight.
3. Enter your age.
4. Enter your blood pressure (in the relevant unit system, e.g., mmHg).
5. List any medications you are currently taking.
6. List any allergies you have.
7. List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
8. Describe your sleep habits and quality.
9. Review your input for accuracy and completeness.
10. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header**: Enter a brief title or description for your evaluation.
* **Demographics**: Enter your age.
* **Height**: Enter your height in centimeters (cm) or meters (m).
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **Age**: Enter your age.
* **Blood Pressure**: Enter your blood pressure in mmHg or other relevant unit system.
* **Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you are currently experiencing or have experienced in the past.
* **Sleep**: Describe your sleep habits and quality.
* **Tips**:
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, don't hesitate to reach out to the health professionals administering this form.
* Review your input carefully before submitting the form.
