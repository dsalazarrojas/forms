## Self Certification Of Vaccination Status - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Self Certification Of Vaccination Status form is used for employees to self-certify their vaccination status, providing information about their COVID-19 vaccination history. This form is typically used for health and safety purposes in the workplace.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the correct answer from the dropdown menu for "Vaccination Status". You can choose between:
	*   COVID-19 Vaccinated
	*   Not Yet Vaccinated
	*   Partially Vaccinated
2.  If you are fully vaccinated, you can leave the "Date of Vaccination" field blank.
3.  If you have received multiple doses of the vaccine, you can select all applicable doses from the "Vaccine Dose Number" dropdown menu.
4.  Select the correct vaccine manufacturer from the dropdown menu for "Vaccine Manufacturer". You can choose between:
	*   Pfizer
	*   Moderna
	*   Johnson & Johnson
5.  If you have a vaccine lot number, you can enter it in the text field for "Vaccine Lot Number".
6.  Enter your Employee ID Number, which is typically a numerical identifier assigned to you by your employer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Vaccination Status** (Select One, required): Select the option that best describes your vaccination status.
*   **Date of Vaccination** (Date, optional): Enter the date you received your vaccine if you are fully vaccinated.
*   **Vaccine Dose Number** (Select Multiple, optional): Select all applicable doses you have received.
*   **Vaccine Manufacturer** (Select One, optional): Select the manufacturer of your vaccine.
*   **Vaccine Lot Number** (Text, optional): Enter your vaccine lot number if you have one.
*   **Employee ID Number** (Number, required): Enter your employee ID number, which is typically assigned to you by your employer.

## Tips

*   Please ensure you provide accurate information to ensure the safety of yourself and others in the workplace.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask your supervisor or HR representative for guidance.
*   Remember to keep a record of your vaccination status and any relevant documents for future reference.
*   This form is typically reviewed by HR and management. Please make sure to complete it accurately and thoroughly.

Please note that the fields marked as optional can be left blank if you are unsure or do not have the required information. However, if these fields are required for your workplace, you will need to complete them accurately.
