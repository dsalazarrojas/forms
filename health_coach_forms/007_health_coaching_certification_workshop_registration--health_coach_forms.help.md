# Health Coaching Certification Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to register for a health coaching certification workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Participant Details** section with your name and any other required details.
2. Choose your preferred dates for the workshop from the **Preferred Dates** dropdown menu.
3. Select your chosen **Training Provider** from the provided options.
4. Choose your preferred **Training Academy** from the options provided.
5. Provide any additional **Workshop Information** you think is necessary.
6. Enter your **Contact Information**, including your name and email address.
7. Check and agree to the **Agreements** provided.
8. Finally, sign the form with your **Participant Signature**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for entering your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Preferred Dates** (`preferred_dates`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ This field allows you to choose your preferred dates for the workshop. Please select all applicable dates.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This field is for selecting your chosen training provider. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **Training Academy** (`training_academy`, select_one, required: false):
	+ This field is for choosing your preferred training academy. Please select one of the provided options.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for providing any additional information about the workshop.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for entering your name and email address.
* **Agreements** (`agreements`, text, required: false):
	+ This field is for reading and agreeing to the provided agreements.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required: false):
	+ This field is for signing the form to confirm your registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your preferred dates and training provider to ensure you don't miss the workshop.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the workshop organizers before submitting the form.
* Remember to review the agreements carefully before signing the form.
