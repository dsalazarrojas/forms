# COVID 19 Awareness Course Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for individuals who are interested in applying for the COVID-19 awareness course. The course aims to educate individuals on COVID-19 transmission, prevention, and related topics. By filling out this form, you will provide us with information about your background, learning preferences, and expectations from the course.

Before proceeding, please ensure that you have not previously submitted an application for this course. If you are not sure, you can contact us to confirm.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal details, such as your name and email address.
2.  Provide information about your organization or affiliation, if applicable.
3.  Indicate your job title or position.
4.  Specify your professional background, such as Healthcare Professional, Educator, or Business Professional.
5.  Select your preferred course start date and learning format (Online Synchronous, Online Asynchronous, In-person, Hybrid, or No preference).
6.  Mention the estimated hours per week you can commit to the course.
7.  Describe what you hope to learn from the course.
8.  Indicate whether you have previously taken any related courses or training on COVID-19.
9.  If yes, specify the topics you have studied before.
10.  Select specific areas of interest related to COVID-19 (e.g., Transmission and Prevention, Vaccination Information and Safety).
11.  Describe how you plan to apply the knowledge gained from the course professionally.
12.  Indicate whether you plan to share the knowledge with others in your organization or community.
13.  Confirm your access to required technology (reliable internet and equipment).
14.  Specify your preferred language of instruction.
15.  Mention any accessibility needs you may have.
16.  Describe any questions or concerns you have about the course.
17.  Confirm your commitment to completing the course and actively participating.
18.  Finally, submit the form with your preferred application date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name (Required)

• **Full Name**: Your legal name, as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).

### 2. Email Address (Required)

• **Email Address**: Your email address where you can be reached for course updates and information.

### 3. Phone Number (Required)

• **Phone Number**: Your contact number for any course-related communication.

### 4. Organization or Affiliation (Optional)

• **Organization or Affiliation**: The name of your company, school, or organization where you work or study.

### 5. Job Title (Optional)

• **Job Title**: Your position or role in your current organization or institution.

### 6. Professional Background (Required)

• **Professional Background**: Select one or more of the options (Healthcare Professional, Educator, Business Professional, Government Official, Non-profit Staff, Student, Other) that best describes your occupation or profession.

### 7. Preferred Course Start Date (Required)

• **Preferred Course Start Date**: The date when you would like to begin the course.

### 8. Preferred Learning Format (Required)

• **Preferred Learning Format**: Select one of the options (Online Synchronous, Online Asynchronous, In-person, Hybrid, No preference) that best suits your learning style.

### 9. Hours Per Week You Can Commit (Optional)

• **Hours Per Week You Can Commit**: Estimated availability for coursework, in hours per week.

### 10. What Do You Hope to Learn from This Course? (Required)

• **What Do You Hope to Learn from This Course?**: Describe your learning goals and expectations from the course.

### 11. Previous COVID-19 Training or Education (Required)

• **Previous COVID-19 Training or Education**: Indicate whether you have taken any related courses or training on COVID-19 (Yes, No, Not sure).

### 12. Topics You Have Studied Before (Optional)

• **Topics You Have Studied Before**: If yes, specify the topics you have studied before (e.g., COVID-19 Transmission and Prevention, Vaccination Information and Safety).

### 13. Specific Areas of Interest (Optional)

• **Specific Areas of Interest**: Select all topics that interest you (e.g., COVID-19 Transmission and Prevention, Vaccination Information and Safety, etc.).

### 14. How Will You Apply This Knowledge Professionally? (Optional)

• **How Will You Apply This Knowledge Professionally?**: Describe how you plan to use the knowledge gained from the course in your work or professional life.

### 15. Will You Share This Knowledge with Others? (Optional)

• **Will You Share This Knowledge with Others?**: Indicate whether you plan to share the knowledge with others in your organization or community (Yes, Probably yes, Maybe, No).

### 16. Access to Required Technology (Required)

• **Access to Required Technology**: Confirm your access to reliable internet and equipment.

### 17. Preferred Language of Instruction (Optional)

• **Preferred Language of Instruction**: Select your preferred language for the course instruction.

### 18. Accessibility Needs (Optional)

• **Accessibility Needs**: Mention any accommodations you may need to complete the course.

### 19. Questions or Concerns About the Course (Optional)

• **Questions or Concerns About the Course**: Describe any questions or concerns you have about the course.

### 20. I Commit to Completing This Course and Actively Participating (Required)

• **I Commit to Completing This Course and Actively Participating**: Confirm your commitment to complete the course and actively participate in all course activities.

### 21. Application Date (Required)

• **Application Date**: The date when you submit this application form.

## Tips

*   Be honest about your professional background and experience to ensure that you are eligible for the course.
*   Specify your preferred learning format and language of instruction to ensure that you will be able to complete the course successfully.
*   Commit to actively participating in all course activities and complete all coursework.
*   Be honest about your access to required technology to ensure that you can complete course tasks and exercises.
*   If you have any concerns or questions, please let us know so that we can assist you. 

Please submit your application carefully, as any incomplete applications will not be accepted. If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact us.
