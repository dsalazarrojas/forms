# Wellness Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Consultation Questionnaire is designed to collect information about your health and wellness status to help you make informed decisions about your health and well-being. It is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a guide to help you identify areas that may be impacting your health.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Answer each question on the remaining pages to the best of your ability.
3. Be as accurate and detailed as possible when answering each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number (optional).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Please list any medical conditions you are currently managing or have had in the past.
* **Family History** (`family_history`, text, optional): Please list any family members who have experienced health conditions or passed away due to illness or injury.
* **Mental Health** (`mental_health`, text, optional): Please list any mental health conditions you are currently managing or have had in the past.
* **Physical Activity** (`physical_activity`, text, optional): Please describe your level of physical activity (e.g., sedentary, lightly active, very active).
* **Risk Factors** (`risk_factors`, text, optional): Please list any risk factors you are concerned about (e.g., smoking, high blood pressure).
* **Health Goals** (`health_goals`, text, optional): Please list your health goals (e.g., losing weight, improving sleep).
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, text, optional): Please list any current medications you are taking.
* **Past Injuries** (`past_injuries`, text, optional): Please list any past injuries or surgeries.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate when answering each question.
* Please list all relevant information, even if it may seem minor.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact [insert contact information].
