# Telehealth Physical Therapy Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Telehealth Physical Therapy Consent Form is a document used to obtain consent from patients for physical therapy services provided through telehealth. This form is used to ensure that patients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of their care, including payment terms and methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Patient Consent**: This is a note field where you can enter any additional comments or notes about the patient's consent.
2. **Payment Information**: Select one or more payment methods from the options provided. You can choose between Telehealth, Insurance, and Self Pay.
3. **Insurance Coverage**: Select the type of insurance coverage the patient has. Options include Private Insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, and Tricare.
4. **Medical Necessity**: Indicate whether the patient's treatment is medically necessary. Select 'True' or 'False'.
5. **Payment Method**: Select the method by which payment will be made. Options include Credit Card and ACH.
6. **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of payment due. This field is only required if payment method is not Telehealth or Insurance.
7. **Signature**: Select the method of payment signature, which can be done either via Telehealth or In Office.
8. **Patient Signature**: This is a note field where the patient can enter their signature.
9. **Provider Signature**: This is a note field where the provider can enter their signature.
10. **Practice Name**: Enter the name of your practice.
11. **Practice Address**: Enter the address of your practice.
12. **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of your practice.
13. **Payment Terms**: Select the payment terms, which can be Paid at Time of Service, Paid at End of Session, or Paid at End of Month.
14. **Payment Status**: Select the payment status, which can be Paid, Pending, or Due.
