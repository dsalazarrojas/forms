# Fitness Accountability Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Accountability Check In Form is designed to help clients track their progress, set fitness goals, and schedule next workouts. This form is used to provide coaches with a comprehensive overview of clients' fitness journey, allowing for better guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your client's name and contact information (email and phone number) in the designated fields.
2. Answer the questions about your client's training method, weight, height, body fat percentage, and fitness goals.
3. Record the number of workouts completed and missed, as well as the exercises completed and missed.
4. Write any progress notes and next workout schedule in the designated fields.
5. Review and update the form regularly to track your client's progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Client Name**: The client's name as recorded in our system.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter your client's full name as we have recorded it in our system.
- **Training Method**: The method your client is using to train (e.g., strength, cardio, endurance).
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter your client's current training method (e.g., strength training, cardio).
- **Weight**: Your client's current weight in kilograms.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record your client's current weight in kilograms.
- **Height**: Your client's height in centimeters.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record your client's height in centimeters.
- **Body Fat Percentage**: Your client's current body fat percentage.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record your client's current body fat percentage (e.g., 20%).
- **Workouts Completed**: The number of workouts completed by your client.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record the total number of workouts completed by your client.
- **Exercises Completed**: The number of exercises completed by your client.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record the total number of exercises completed by your client.
- **Workouts Missed**: The number of workouts missed by your client.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record the total number of workouts missed by your client.
- **Exercises Missed**: The number of exercises missed by your client.
  * Type: number
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record the total number of exercises missed by your client.
- **Progress Notes**: Any notes about your client's progress.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record any notable progress or concerns about your client's progress.
- **Fitness Goals**: Your client's current fitness goals.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record your client's current fitness goals (e.g., lose weight, gain muscle).
- **Next Workout**: The schedule of your client's next workout.
  * Type: date
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record the date of your client's next scheduled workout.
- **Notes**: Any other notes or concerns about your client.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Record any additional notes or concerns about your client.
- **Email**: Your client's email address.
  * Type: email
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter your client's email address.
- **Phone**: Your client's phone number.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
  * Hint: Enter your client's phone number.
