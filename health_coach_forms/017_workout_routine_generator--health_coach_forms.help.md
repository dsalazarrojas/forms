# workout_routine_generator - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `workout_routine_generator` form is designed to collect relevant information from clients to create personalized workout routines tailored to their fitness goals and preferences. This form helps trainers create a comprehensive plan to help clients achieve their desired outcomes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Goals**: Enter a brief description of your current fitness goals, such as weight loss, muscle gain, etc.
2. **Fitness Level**: Select your current fitness level: beginner, intermediate, advanced, or expert.
3. **Goals Description**: Provide a detailed description of your goals, including any specific areas of focus.
4. **Client Preferences**: Select up to three preferences for your workout routine, such as weight loss, muscle gain, or endurance training.
5. **Exercise Preferences**: Select all exercises you enjoy or are interested in, such as weight lifting, cardio, or stretching.
6. **Schedule**: Enter your preferred start date for the workout routine.
7. **Start Date**: Enter the start date for the workout routine.
8. **End Date**: Enter the end date for the workout routine.
9. **Workout Frequency**: Enter how often you'd like to work out per week.
10. **Training Days**: Enter which days of the week you are available to work out.
11. **Training Duration**: Enter how long each workout should last.
12. **Rest Days**: Enter how many rest days you need per week.
13. **Workout Time**: Enter the preferred time of day for your workouts.
14. **Trainer Name**: Enter your trainer's name for record-keeping purposes.
15. **Trainer Phone**: Enter your trainer's phone number for contact.
16. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
17. **Client Comments**: Share any additional comments or feedback.
18. **Trainer Comments**: Share any comments or feedback from the trainer.
19. **Client Signature**: Sign and date the form to acknowledge completion.
20. **Trainer Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm completion and acceptance of the plan.
21. **Form Submission**: This field is for internal use, do not fill in.
22. **Additional Notes**: Share any additional information the trainer may need to know.
23. **Trainer Notes**: The trainer's notes for future reference.
24. **Training Plan**: A detailed plan of workouts and exercises for the client.
25. **Form Submitted By**: This field is for internal use, do not fill in.
