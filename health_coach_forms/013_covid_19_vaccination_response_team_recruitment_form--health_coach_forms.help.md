# COVID 19 Vaccination Response Team Recruitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential candidates for the COVID-19 Vaccination Response Team. It collects demographic and professional data from individuals with medical experience, as well as their availability and commitment to work.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter your city and state.
5. Describe your professional qualifications.
6. Select your current professional role.
7. Enter your professional license number and the state or country where you hold it.
8. Indicate your years of healthcare experience.
9. Indicate whether you have prior vaccination training or certification.
10. Describe your vaccination training if applicable.
11. Indicate your direct COVID-19 patient care experience.
12. Select your level of COVID-19 patient care experience.
13. Indicate whether you have experience with COVID-19 vaccination administration.
14. If you have administered COVID-19 vaccinations, estimate the number you have administered.
15. Indicate your availability for full-time work.
16. Select your hours of availability per week.
17. Enter your earliest start date for work.
18. Commit to work for a minimum duration (3 months, 6 months, or 1 year).
19. Select your languages spoken fluently.
20. Indicate your comfort level with electronic health records.
21. Describe your leadership or supervisory experience (if any).
22. Provide the names and numbers of your professional references (if any).
23. Indicate your willingness to undergo a background check.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **City** (`applicant_city`, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (`applicant_state`, text, required): Enter your state.
* **Professional Qualifications** (`qualification_section`, note, optional): Describe your professional qualifications.
* **Current Professional Role** (`professional_role`, select_one, required): Select your current professional role (e.g., Physician, Nurse, etc.).
* **Professional License Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your professional license number.
* **License State or Country** (`license_state`, text, required): Enter the state or country where you hold your license.
* **Years of Healthcare Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter your years of healthcare experience.
* **Prior Vaccination Training or Certification** (`vaccination_training`, select_one, required): Select whether you have prior vaccination training or certification.
* **Describe Vaccination Training** (`training_description`, text, optional): Describe your prior vaccination training if applicable.
* **Direct COVID-19 Patient Care Experience** (`covid_exposure`, select_one, required): Select your level of direct COVID-19 patient care experience.
* **COVID-19 Vaccination Administration Experience** (`vaccination_experience`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have experience with COVID-19 vaccination administration.
* **Approximate Number of Vaccinations Administered** (`vaccination_count`, number, optional): If you have administered COVID-19 vaccinations, estimate the number you have administered.
* **Full-time Availability** (`full_time_availability`, select_one, required): Select whether you are available for full-time work.
* **Hours per Week Available** (`hours_per_week`, number, required): Enter the hours per week you are available to work.
* **Earliest Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter your earliest start date for work.
* **Commitment Duration** (`duration_commitment`, select_one, required): Commit to work for a minimum duration (3 months, 6 months, or 1 year).
* **Languages Spoken Fluently** (`language_proficiency`, select_multiple, required): Select the languages you speak fluently.
* **Comfort with Electronic Health Records** (`technology_skills`, select_one, required): Select your comfort level with electronic health records.
* **Leadership or Supervisory Experience** (`leadership_skills`, text, optional): Describe any leadership or supervisory experience.
* **Professional References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide the names and numbers of your professional references.
* **Willingness to Undergo Background Check** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Select whether you are willing to undergo a background check.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, you can contact us for assistance.
