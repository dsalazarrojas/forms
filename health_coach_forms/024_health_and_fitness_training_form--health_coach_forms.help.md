# Health And Fitness Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health And Fitness Training Form is designed to collect information about your health, fitness, and goals to help create a personalized training plan. This form is used by trainers and coaches to understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Answer all fields that apply to you.
2. Leave blank or mark as "N/A" any fields that do not apply.
3. Please be honest and accurate in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your contact information.
  * Please enter your name, address, and other contact details.
  * If you're filling out the form for someone else, please provide their contact information.

- **Client Contact** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect the contact information of the person who will be training with us.
  * Please enter the name and phone number of the person who will be training with us.

- **Medical Info** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any medical information that may impact your training.
  * Please provide any medical conditions, surgeries, or allergies you may have.
  * If you have any medical devices, please describe them.

- **Emergency Contact** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect emergency contact information.
  * Please enter the name and phone number of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.

- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`5`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
  * Please select "Primary Caregiver" if the emergency contact is a family member or spouse.

- **Medical Conditions** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any medical conditions that may impact your training.
  * Please provide any medical conditions, such as diabetes, high blood pressure, etc.

- **Medical History** (`7`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any medical history that may impact your training.
  * Please provide any medical history, such as surgeries, illnesses, etc.

- **Medications** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any medications you are currently taking.
  * Please list all medications, including vitamins and supplements.

- **Allergies** (`9`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any allergies you may have.
  * Please list all allergies, including food, environmental, etc.

- **Emergency Info** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any emergency information.
  * Please provide any emergency information, such as a will or living will.

- **Training Goals** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your training goals.
  * Please describe your goals, such as weight loss, strength gain, etc.

- **Exercise Frequency** (`12`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to determine how often you plan to train.
  * Please select "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly" to indicate how often you plan to train.

- **Exercise Level** (`13`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to determine your current fitness level.
  * Please select "Beginner", "Intermediate", "Advanced" to indicate your current fitness level.

- **Workout Frequency** (`14`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to determine when you plan to train.
  * Please select "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening" to indicate when you plan to train.

- **Workout Days** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to determine the days you plan to train.
  * Please select all the days you plan to train, such as Monday, Tuesday, etc.

- **Training Program** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to determine your preferred training program.
  * Please select all the programs that interest you, such as Weight Loss, Strength Training, etc.

- **Workout Time** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your available training time.
  * Please provide your available training time, such as 30 minutes, 1 hour, etc.

- **Training Schedule** (`18`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect your training schedule.
  * Please provide your training schedule, including days and times.

- **Training Schedule Start Date** (`19`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to collect the start date of your training schedule.
  * Please select a date to start your training schedule.

- **Training Schedule End Date** (`20`, `date`, required/optional): This field is used to collect the end date of your training schedule.
  * Please select an end date for your training schedule.

- **Training Goals Frequency** (`21`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to determine how often you want to achieve your training goals.
  * Please select "Daily", "Weekly", "Monthly" to indicate how often you want to achieve your training goals.

- **Payment Terms** (`22`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect payment terms.
  * Please describe any payment terms, such as payment frequency and methods.

- **Billing Frequency** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field is used to collect billing frequency.
  * Please select all billing frequencies, such as Weekly, Bi-Weekly, etc.

- **Billing Status** (`24`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field is used to collect billing status.
  * Please select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your billing status.

- **Billing Notes** (`25`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect any billing notes.
  * Please provide any billing notes, such as payment method, etc.
