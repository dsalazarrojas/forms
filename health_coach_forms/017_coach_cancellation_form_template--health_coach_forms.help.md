# Coach Cancellation Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose

This Coach Cancellation Form is a tool used to inform your coach of your intention to cancel a coaching session. The form helps the coach understand why you need to cancel, and it allows you to communicate any relevant details about your cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal name as the client.
2. Provide your contact email address.
3. Enter your phone number.
4. Enter the name of your coach.
5. Select the date and time of the cancelled session.
6. Choose the type of session being cancelled (e.g., Initial Consultation, Regular Coaching Session, etc.).
7. Select the reason for cancellation (e.g., Illness or Injury, Family Emergency, etc.).
8. Provide more information about your cancellation reason (if applicable).
9. Choose how much notice you gave the coach (e.g., more than 72 hours, 24-48 hours, etc.).
10. Confirm that you understand any applicable fees for cancellation.
11. Indicate if you want to reschedule another session.
12. Choose a preferred reschedule date and time (if rescheduling).
13. If you are requesting a refund, select the reason for the refund request.
14. Rate your coach's performance (if you've had sessions).
15. Add any additional comments or feedback about your coaching experience.
16. Finally, select a date for your cancellation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email Address** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your coach.
* **Appointment Date Being Cancelled** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select the date of the session to be cancelled.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select the time of the session.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of session being cancelled (e.g., Initial Consultation, Regular Coaching Session, etc.).
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`cancellation_reason`, select_one, required): Select the reason for cancellation (e.g., Illness or Injury, Family Emergency, etc.).
* **Cancellation Details** (`cancellation_details`, text, optional): Provide more information about your reason for cancellation.
* **How Much Notice Are You Giving** (`notice_given`, select_one, required): Select the amount of notice given to the coach (e.g., more than 72 hours, 24-48 hours, etc.).
* **Cancellation Fee Understanding** (`cancellation_fee_understanding`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand any applicable fees for cancellation.
* **Would You Like to Reschedule** (`reschedule_interest`, select_one, required): Indicate if you want to reschedule another session.
* **Preferred Reschedule Date** (`preferred_reschedule_date`, date, optional): Select a date for rescheduling (if rescheduling).
* **Preferred Reschedule Time** (`preferred_reschedule_time`, select_one, optional): Choose a time for rescheduling (if rescheduling).
* **Are You Requesting a Refund** (`refund_request`, select_one, optional): Select if you want a refund for a prepaid session.
* **Refund Reason** (`refund_reason`, text, optional): Enter the reason for refund request.
* **Interest in Future Coaching** (`future_coaching_interest`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you plan to continue coaching later.
* **Feedback on Coaching So Far** (`feedback_on_coaching`, text, optional): Provide feedback about your coaching experience.
* **Coach Performance Rating** (`coach_performance_rating`, select_one, optional): Rate your coach's performance.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Date of Cancellation Request** (`signature_date`, date, required): Select the current date for your cancellation request.
