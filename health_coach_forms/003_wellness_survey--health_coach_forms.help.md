# Wellness Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wellness Survey is a form designed to collect health information from patients to help healthcare providers assess their overall well-being and provide personalized advice.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form carefully and honestly.
2. For each question, select the best answer that represents your current situation or preference.
3. If a question is optional, you can skip it if you're not comfortable providing the information or if it doesn't apply to you.
4. When filling out the "Emergency Contact" section, please provide the name and phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, `text`, optional): This section is for providing any relevant information about yourself that you think is important for the healthcare provider to know.
- **Physical Activity Level** (`physical_activity`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your current physical activity level:
	+ Sedentary: You don't get much exercise.
	+ Light: You get some exercise, but not too much.
	+ Moderate: You get regular exercise.
	+ High: You get a lot of exercise.
	+ Very High: You exercise very frequently.
- **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions you have or have had in the past.
- **Mental Status** (`mental_status`, `select_one`, optional): Rate your current mental status:
	+ Good: You're feeling great.
	+ Fair: You're feeling okay, but not great.
	+ Bad: You're feeling stressed or anxious.
	+ Very Bad: You're feeling very stressed or anxious.
- **Sleep Quality** (`sleep_quality`, `number`, optional): Rate your sleep quality on a scale of 0-10, where 0 is very poor and 10 is excellent.
- **Exercise Frequency** (`exercise_frequency`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate how often you exercise:
	+ Never: You don't exercise at all.
	+ Rarely: You exercise very infrequently.
	+ Occasionally: You exercise occasionally.
	+ Often: You exercise regularly.
	+ Very Often: You exercise very frequently.
- **Nutrition Information** (`nutrition_info`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate your current nutrition level:
	+ Poor: You eat unhealthily.
	+ Fair: You eat healthily, but not perfectly.
	+ Good: You eat a balanced diet.
	+ Very Good: You eat extremely healthily.
- **Health Insurance** (`health_insurance`, `select_one`, optional): Do you have any health insurance?
	+ True: Yes, you have health insurance.
	+ False: No, you don't have health insurance.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Name and phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `select_multiple`, optional): Indicate the type of phone number:
	+ Landline: A fixed phone number.
	+ Cellphone: A mobile phone number.
	+ Other: A different type of phone number.

## Tips
- Please be honest and thorough when answering each question.
- If you're unsure about a question, try your best to provide the most accurate answer.
- If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
