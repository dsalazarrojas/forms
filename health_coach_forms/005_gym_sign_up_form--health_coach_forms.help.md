# Gym Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who wish to sign up for a gym membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field in the format "example@example.com".
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
6. Select the length of your membership in the "Membership Length" field (1-12 months).
7. Choose your preferred gym location from the "Gym Location" dropdown menu.
8. Select your preferred personal trainer from the "Personal Trainer" dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional notes about your membership in the "Notes" field (optional).
10. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
11. Enter the same phone number you entered earlier in the "Confirm Phone" field.
12. Confirm your date of birth in the "Confirm Date of Birth" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Membership Length**: Select the length of your membership (1-12 months).
* **Gym Location**: Choose your preferred gym location.
* **Personal Trainer**: Select your preferred personal trainer.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about your membership.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm the email address you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Phone**: Enter the same phone number you entered earlier.
* **Confirm Date of Birth**: Confirm your date of birth.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly, as these will be used to confirm your identity.
* If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact our staff for assistance.
