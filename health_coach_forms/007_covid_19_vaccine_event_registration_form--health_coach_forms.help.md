# COVID 19 Vaccine Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals seeking COVID-19 vaccine registration. The data collected will be used for event organization, scheduling, and administration of the vaccine.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field. This is your legal name.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field. This will be used for registration confirmation and future updates.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is optional, but will be used for event updates.
5. Select your preferred event date from the available dates in the "Preferred Event Date" field.
6. Choose your preferred event time from the available time slots in the "Preferred Event Time" field.
7. Indicate your current vaccination status in the "Current Vaccination Status" field:
   - Unvaccinated
   - One dose received
   - Two doses received
   - Fully vaccinated
   - Boosted
8. If available, select your preferred vaccine type in the "Vaccine Type Preference" field:
   - Pfizer-BioNTech
   - Moderna
   - Johnson & Johnson
   - No preference
9. Choose whether you are seeking a first, second, or additional dose in the "Dose Seeking" field:
   - First dose
   - Second dose
   - Third dose (booster)
   - Additional booster
10. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
11. Enter your city, state, and ZIP Code in the "City, State, ZIP Code" field.
12. If applicable, provide your insurance provider and ID number in the "Insurance Provider" and "Insurance ID Number" fields.
13. Check any pre-existing medical conditions that might affect vaccination in the "Pre-existing Medical Conditions" field:
   - Diabetes
   - Heart disease
   - High blood pressure
   - Respiratory disease
   - Immunocompromised
   - None
14. Report any known allergies in the "Known Allergies" field.
15. Indicate whether you are currently experiencing fever or illness in the "Currently experiencing fever or illness" field:
   - True
   - False
16. If you have had a recent COVID-19 infection, indicate this in the "Recent COVID-19 infection" field:
   - True
   - False
   - Unknown
17. If necessary, select "True" for transportation assistance in the "Transportation assistance needed" field.
18. Choose your preferred language for event materials and support in the "Language Preference" field:
   - English
   - Spanish
   - Chinese
   - Vietnamese
   - Other
19. Finally, confirm your consent to receive a COVID-19 vaccine at this event in the "I consent to receive COVID-19 vaccination at this event" field:
   - Yes, I consent
   - No, I do not consent

* Ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely to ensure correct registration and event management.
