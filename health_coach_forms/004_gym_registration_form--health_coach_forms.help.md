# gym_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gym registration form is designed to collect information from users, possibly for member registration, coach registration, or staff information collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Input field with your relevant information.
2. Provide your gym's details in the Gym Information field.
3. Enter your payment information in the Payment Information field.
4. Enter your address in the Address field.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
7. Enter a date in the Date field.
8. Enter a time in the Time field.
9. Add any additional notes in the Note field.
10. If you are a manager, fill out the Manager Information field with your details.
11. If you are a manager, enter your name in the Manager Name field.
12. If you are a manager, enter your email address in the Manager Email field.
13. If you are a manager, enter your phone number in the Manager Phone field.
14. If you are a coach, fill out the Coach Information field with your details.
15. If you are a coach, enter your name in the Coach Name field.
16. If you are a coach, enter your email address in the Coach Email field.
17. If you are a coach, enter your phone number in the Coach Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (Required): Enter your relevant information.
* **Gym Information** (Required): Enter your gym's details.
* **Payment Information** (Required): Enter your payment details.
* **Address** (Required): Enter your address.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (Required): Enter a date.
* **Time** (Required): Enter a time.
* **Note** (Required): Add any additional notes.
* **Manager Information** (Optional): If you are a manager, enter your details.
* **Manager Name** (Optional): If you are a manager, enter your name.
* **Manager Email** (Optional): If you are a manager, enter your email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Optional): If you are a manager, enter your phone number.
* **Coach Information** (Optional): If you are a coach, enter your details.
* **Coach Name** (Optional): If you are a coach, enter your name.
* **Coach Email** (Optional): If you are a coach, enter your email address.
* **Coach Phone** (Optional): If you are a coach, enter your phone number.
