# Health Coaching Client Plan Adherence Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a regular check-in point for health coaches and their clients to review and update progress on the client's health and wellness plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the client's current progress and update the relevant fields as necessary.
2. Add any progress photos or updates relevant to the client's progress.
3. Select an option for "Client Agreed" to indicate that the client has agreed to the updates and next steps.
4. Choose a follow-up reminder option to schedule future check-ins.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Plan Details** (`health_coaching_client_plan_adherence_check_in`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter a brief summary of the client's current health and wellness plan.
* **Progress Update** (`client_plan_adherence_details`, text, optional): Use this field to provide an update on the client's progress and any changes or challenges they are facing.
* **Weight** (`current_weight`, number, optional): Enter the client's current weight.
* **Height** (`current_height`, number, optional): Enter the client's current height.
* **Progress Photos** (`progress_photos`, select multiple, optional): Upload photos of the client's progress, such as before and after photos.
* **Progress Notes** (`progress_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments on the client's progress.
* **Next Session Date** (`next_session_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the next coaching session.
* **Next Session Time** (`next_session_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the next coaching session.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, optional): Allow the client to add any comments or feedback on their progress.
* **Coach Comments** (`coach_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or feedback from the coach.
* **Client Agreed** (`client_agree`, select one, optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that the client has agreed to the updates and next steps.
* **Follow Up Reminders** (`follow_up_reminders`, select one, optional): Schedule follow-up reminders for the client, such as daily, weekly, or bi-weekly.
