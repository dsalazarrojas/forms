# Health Product Satisfaction Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather feedback and satisfaction information from users about a specific health product. The form is intended for users who have used the product and want to share their experience.

## How To Complete This Form

- Start by answering each question carefully and honestly about your experience with the health product.
- Use the provided text fields to provide detailed answers to the questions.
- Select the relevant options for questions with dropdown or checkbox options.
- Make sure to complete all required questions, indicated by an asterisk (*).
- When finished, click the "Submit" button to complete the survey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is the health product you are using?** (`product_name`, text, required):
	* This is where you can enter the name of the health product you are using.
	* Be sure to enter the exact name of the product, not just a description.
- **Rate this product** (`product_rating`, number, required):
	* Choose a number from 1 to 5, with 1 being the lowest and 5 being the highest rating.
	* This rating represents your overall satisfaction with the product.
- **Product Rating Scale** (`product_rating_scale`, select_one, options: Excellent, Good, Fair, Bad, Terrible):
	* Select one of the provided ratings to describe your experience with the product.
	* This rating will help us understand the strengths and weaknesses of the product.
- **How satisfied are you with this health product?** (`how_satisfied`, select_multiple, options: Very Satisfied, Somewhat Satisfied, Neutral, Somewhat Dissatisfied, Very Dissatisfied):
	* Choose all that apply from the provided options that describe your level of satisfaction with the product.
	* This will help us understand what we can improve.
- **Do you have any comments or suggestions?** (`comments`, text, required):
	* Enter any additional thoughts or ideas you have about the product.
	* This is a great opportunity to share your feedback and ideas on how we can improve the product.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required):
	* Enter your email address.
	* We will use this to contact you if we need more information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
	* Enter your phone number.
	* We will use this to contact you if we need more information.
- **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, required):
	* Enter the name of the product.
	* This will help us keep track of the product you are referring to.
- **Product Category** (`product_category`, text, required):
	* Enter the category of the product (e.g. supplements, devices, etc.).
	* This will help us understand the type of product you are using.
- **Purchase Date** (`purchase_date`, date, required):
	* Enter the date you purchased the product.
	* This will help us track your purchase history.
- **Last Used** (`last_used`, date, required):
	* Enter the date you last used the product.
	* This will help us understand your usage pattern.
- **Next Purchase** (`next_purchased`, date, required):
	* Enter the date you plan to purchase the product again.
	* This will help us understand your future plans.
- **Product Image** (`product_image`, text, required):
	* Enter a URL or description of the product image.
	* This will help us understand how you interact with the product's visual components.
- **Product Video** (`product_video`, text, required):
	* Enter a URL or description of the product video.
	* This will help us understand how you interact with the product's visual components.
- **Product Audio** (`product_audio`, text, required):
	* Enter a URL or description of the product audio.
	* This will help us understand how you interact with the product's audio components.
- **Product File** (`product_file`, text, required):
	* Enter a URL or description of the product file.
	* This will help us understand how you interact with the product's downloadable components.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required):
	* Enter the name of the contact person.
	* This will help us keep track of who to contact for more information.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required):
	* Enter the contact email.
	* We will use this to contact you for more information.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required):
	* Enter the contact phone number.
	* We will use this to contact you for more information.
- **Product Url** (`product_url`, text, required):
	* Enter the URL of the product.
	* This will help us keep track of the product's online presence.
- **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, required):
	* Enter any additional feedback or suggestions you have.
	* This will help us improve the product and provide better services.

## Tips

- Please answer all questions carefully and honestly.
- If you're unsure about any of the questions, feel free to ask for clarification.
- If you have any issues or concerns while completing this form, contact us at <insert contact email>.
