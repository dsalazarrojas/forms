# Health Consultant Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Health Consultant Information Form is a crucial document that helps health consultants gather essential information about clients' health status, medical history, and other relevant details. This form is used to create a comprehensive understanding of the client's needs, allowing for tailored consulting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name and email address in the respective fields.
2.  Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3.  Enter your birthdate (mm/dd/yyyy).
4.  Specify the time you are available to consult with the health professional.
5.  Select your client type (Client or Non-Client).
6.  Choose any health issues you are experiencing (Hypertension, Diabetes, Allergies).
7.  Describe your medical history, including any health issues, tests, and conditions.
8.  List any medications you are currently taking.
9.  Mention any allergies you have.
10.  Specify any medical conditions you are dealing with.
11.  Report any recent medical tests or results.
12.  Mention any health professionals you have consulted with.
13.  Provide your insurance information.
14.  Include any additional medical history notes.
15.  Specify any medical references you have.
16.  Describe the results of any recent medical tests.
17.  List any medications you are taking, including type and dosage.
18.  Specify any side effects of your medications.
19.  Provide any other medical history information.
20.  Choose how to sign the form (Signature or Initials).
21.  Choose how to sign the form for your health professional (Signature or Initials).
22.  Enter the date you signed the form.
23.  Enter the time you signed the form.
24.  Choose to submit or save the form as a draft.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Client Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Date**: Enter your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **Time**: Specify the time you are available to consult with the health professional.
*   **Client Type**: Select whether you are a client or not.
*   **Health Issues**: Choose any health issues you are currently dealing with.
*   **Medical History**: Describe your medical history, including any health issues, tests, and conditions.
*   **Medication**: List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Allergies**: Mention any allergies you have.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Specify any medical conditions you are dealing with.
*   **Medical Tests**: Report any recent medical tests or results.
*   **Health Professionals**: Mention any health professionals you have consulted with.
*   **Insurance**: Provide your insurance information.
*   **Medical History Notes**: Include any additional medical history notes.
*   **Medical References**: Specify any medical references you have.
*   **Medical Tests Results**: Describe the results of any recent medical tests.
*   **Medications**: List any medications you are taking, including type and dosage.
*   **Medication Side Effects**: Specify any side effects of your medications.
*   **Other Medical History**: Provide any other medical history information.
*   **Client Signature**: Choose how to sign the form (Signature or Initials).
*   **Health Professional Signature**: Choose how to sign the form for your health professional (Signature or Initials).
*   **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.
*   **Time Signed**: Enter the time you signed the form.
*   **Submit**: Choose to submit or save the form as a draft.
