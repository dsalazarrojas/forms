# Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Egg Donor Screening Questionnaire is a comprehensive form used to gather information from potential egg donors. This form helps us understand your medical history, personal characteristics, and relationships, which are essential for the egg donation process. Completing this form ensures that you provide accurate and honest information, which will be evaluated during the screening process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each question carefully and answer truthfully.
2. For text fields, type in a brief response, and for select multiple fields, choose one or more options that apply to you.
3. Make sure to answer all questions, even if you're unsure about some details.
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family History**: This section asks about your family medical history, including any family members who have had certain medical conditions or diseases.
	+ Please list all family members who have had any medical conditions or diseases.
* **Medical Conditions**: Describe any medical conditions or diseases you or your family members have.
* **Medications and Allergies**: List all medications or allergies you have or have had in the past.
* **Physical Characteristics**: Describe your height, weight, and any other relevant physical characteristics.
* **Social History**: This section is for you to describe your social situation, including education background, employment status, and personal relationships.
* **Education Background**: Provide information about your educational background, including degrees earned and institutions attended.
* **Employment Status**: Describe your current and past employment situations.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or diseases you or your family members have.
* **Family Medicine**: List any family members who are medical professionals or have medical backgrounds.
* **Personal Relationships**: Describe your significant relationships, including partners, children, and other relatives.
* **Mental Health**: Describe any mental health conditions or issues you have experienced.
* **Substance Use**: Describe any substance use or abuse issues you have had in the past.
* **Travel History**: Describe any recent or planned travel plans.
* **Donor Donor Contribution**: If you have donated to others or have received an egg donation, provide details.
* **Criminal History**: Describe any convictions or past crimes you have been involved in.
* **Financial History**: Provide information about your financial situation, including income and debts.
* **Social Media**: List your social media profiles or any online presence.
* **Medical History Other**: Describe any additional medical conditions or diseases you or your family members have.
* **Medical History Other Other**: For any conditions or diseases not listed above, please describe them here.
* **Medical Test Results**: Select all medical test results that apply to you, such as test results from previous medical procedures.
* **Medical Test Results Other**: Select all other medical test results that do not apply to the provided options.
* **Medical Family History**: Select all family members who have had certain medical conditions or diseases.
* **Medical Family History Other**: Select all other family members who do not have the conditions listed above.
* **Medical Family History Other Other**: For any family members not listed above, describe their medical conditions here.
* **Medical Test Results Other Other**: Select all other medical test results that do not apply to the provided options.
* **Medical Family History Other Other Other**: For any conditions not listed above, list family members who have had them.

Note: Please answer all questions to the best of your ability, and if you're unsure about anything, please contact the egg donor screening team for further guidance.
