# Online Coaching Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in coaching services. It's a starting point for the coaching process, gathering essential details to help our coaching team provide personalized guidance and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Share your medical history in the "Medical History" section (if applicable).
4. Describe your exercise history in the "Exercise History" section.
5. If relevant, upload any supporting documents in the "Relevant Documents" section.
6. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Notes" section (if needed).
7. Review your submitted information carefully and type 'confirm' in the "Confirm" field.
8. Finally, submit your form by typing 'submit' in the "Submit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details (Personal Details)** (`text`, required): 
  This section is for you to provide basic personal details such as name, age, etc.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`text`, required): 
  Please enter your contact information, including phone number, email, and address.
* **Medical History (Medical History)** (`text`, optional): 
  If you have any pre-existing medical conditions or concerns, please describe them here.
* **Exercise History (Exercise History)** (`text`, required): 
  Share your exercise experience, including any relevant certifications, injuries, or physical limitations.
* **Relevant Documents (Relevant Documents)** (`text`, optional): 
  If you have any relevant documents (e.g., medical reports, certificates) that support your application, please upload them here.
* **Additional Notes (Additional Notes)** (`text`, optional): 
  Use this section to add any additional comments or explanations that might be helpful for our coaching team to know.
* **Confirm (Confirm)** (`text`, required): 
  Type 'confirm' to verify that you have reviewed and agreed to the submitted information.
* **Submit (Submit)** (`text`, required): 
  Type 'submit' to finalize your application and send it to our coaching team.
