# Goal Setting Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Goal Setting Reflection Form is a tool used to help you reflect on your progress, set goals, and plan for the future. This form will guide you through a series of questions to help you identify your current goals, progress, and challenges, and to determine what next steps you can take to achieve your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Take a moment to review the questions on each page of the form. Answer each question honestly and thoroughly.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the response that best answers the question.
3. Use the "Additional Reflections" field to add any additional thoughts or comments you'd like to share.
4. When you've completed the form, review your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your progress and goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What brings you here** (1): This is a space for you to briefly describe the reason you're completing this form. This can help us understand your context and goals.
* **What are your current goals** (2): Please select all the goals that apply to you. This will help us understand what you're working towards and identify potential challenges.
* **What progress have you made** (3): This question is to help you reflect on how far you've come. Select the progress you've made, such as achieving small wins or overcoming obstacles.
* **What challenges did you face** (4): This is an opportunity to identify any challenges that have hindered your progress. Select all the challenges that apply to you.
* **What are your next steps** (5): Based on your progress and challenges, decide on the next steps you'll take to achieve your goals.
* **Additional Reflections** (6): Use this field to add any additional thoughts, comments, or insights that didn't fit into the other questions.
* **What do you want to do next** (7): This question helps you plan for the future. Select the next steps you'll take to achieve your goals.
* **Follow-up Questions** (8): If you have any questions or concerns that came up during the process, use this field to note them for your coach to follow up on.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses.
* Use specific examples or anecdotes to illustrate your progress and challenges.
* Don't worry about grammar or spelling; this is a personal reflection, not a formal writing assignment.
* Take your time, and don't feel rushed. This form is meant to be a collaborative and supportive process.
