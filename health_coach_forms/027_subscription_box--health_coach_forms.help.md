# Subscription Box - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to create a subscription box, a service that sends a periodic shipment of products or services to customers. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your details accurately and thoroughly.
2. Choose your payment method and frequency.
3. Select the subscription frequency and cycle for your shipment.
4. Set up your billing details, including price and currency.
5. Confirm your subscription start and end dates.
6. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
7. Click "Submit" to finalize your subscription box setup.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your shipping address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where your address is located.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province where your address is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter the zip or postal code of your address.
* **Payment Method** (`payment method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you want to pay for your subscription, e.g., Cash, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Subscription Frequency** (`subscription frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how often you want to receive your subscription shipments, e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Delivery Frequency** (`delivery frequency`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose how often you want to receive your shipments, e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Start Date** (`start date`, `date`, required): Set the date when you want your subscription to start.
* **End Date** (`end date`, `date`, required): Set the date when you want your subscription to end.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your subscription box service.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes for your subscription box service.
* **Billing Frequency** (`billing frequency`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you want to be billed, e.g., One-Time, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Billing Cycle** (`billing cycle`, `select_one`, required): Select the billing cycle for your subscription box service, e.g., Yearly, Monthly, or Quarterly.
* **Price** (`price`, `number`, required): Enter the price for your subscription box service.
* **Currency** (`currency`, `select_one`, required): Choose the currency for your pricing, e.g., USD, EUR, or JPY.
* **Currency Symbol** (`currency symbol`, `text`, required): Enter the currency symbol, e.g., $ or €.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription start date`, `date`, required): Set the date when your subscription is set to start.
* **Subscription End Date** (`subscription end date`, `date`, required): Set the date when your subscription is set to end.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms and conditions`, `text`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions for your subscription box service.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreed** (`terms and conditions agreed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click to finalize your subscription box setup.

Note: This guide assumes that all fields are required, except for those marked as optional. Please review the form carefully and ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.
