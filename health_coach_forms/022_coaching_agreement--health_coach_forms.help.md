# Coaching Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a coaching agreement between a coach and client, outlining the terms of their relationship, including coaching goals, payment terms, and liability waivers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and implications.
3. Ensure you have the necessary information and permissions before completing fields that require it (e.g., medical clearance).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Full Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the coach's legal name.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, `email`, required): Enter the coach's contact email address.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the coach's contact phone number.
* **Client Full Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter the client's legal name.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter the client's contact email address.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the client's mailing address (optional).
* **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date this agreement begins.
* **Coaching Package Selected** (`coaching_package`, `select_one`, required): Choose the coaching package you are purchasing (Single Session, 3 Session Package, 6 Session Package, 12 Session Package, Monthly Unlimited, or Custom Package).
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose the length of each session (30 Minutes, 45 Minutes, 60 Minutes, or 90 Minutes).
* **Total Package Fee** (`total_fee`, `select_one`, required): Choose the total cost of the package ($100-$250, $251-$500, $501-$1000, $1001-$2000, or Over $2000).
* **Payment Schedule** (`payment_schedule`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you will pay (Full Payment Upfront, 50 Percent Deposit, Monthly Payments, or Pay Per Session).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you will pay (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal, Check, or Cash).
* **Primary Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, `text`, required): Enter the primary coaching goals you want to achieve.
* **Relevant Health Conditions** (`health_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any relevant health conditions the coach should know.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, optional): List any relevant medications.
* **Cancellation Policy Accepted** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, required): Accept or decline the cancellation policy.
* **Cancellation Notice Required** (`cancellation_notice_required`, `select_one`, required): Choose the amount of notice required (12 Hours, 24 Hours, 48 Hours, or 72 Hours).
* **Refund Policy Understood** (`refund_policy`, `select_one`, required): Accept or decline the refund policy.
* **Confidentiality Clause Accepted** (`confidentiality_clause`, `select_one`, required): Accept or decline the confidentiality clause.
* **Liability Waiver Signed** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Accept or decline the liability waiver.
* **Medical Clearance Obtained** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, optional): Choose if you have obtained medical clearance (Yes I Have Clearance, Not Required, or No I Need Clearance).
* **Commitment Acknowledgment** (`commitment_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Understand that coaching requires effort and commitment.
* **Results Disclaimer Acknowledged** (`results_disclaimer`, `select_one`, required): Understand that results vary by individual.
* **Preferred Communication Method** (`communication_preferences`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how the coach should contact you (Email, Phone Call, Text Message, App Messaging, or No Preference).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Both Parties Agree to Terms** (`both_parties_agree`, `select_one`, required): Accept or decline both parties agreeing to the terms.
* **Client Signature Date** (`client_signature_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the client's signature.
* **Additional Terms or Notes** (`additional_terms`, `text`, optional): Enter any special agreements or terms.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and implications.
* Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions before completing fields that require it.
* Ask your coach if you have any questions or concerns about any field.
