# wellness_coach_interview_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for wellness coaches to collect information from clients about their health status, medical history, and other essential details. Completing this form helps the coach understand the client's needs and provide personalized guidance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all questions relevant to your situation.
2. For multiple-choice questions, select the options that apply to you.
3. Provide as much detail as possible for text fields.
4. If you're unsure about any question, ask your coach for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Introduction** (ID 1, Note, Not Required): This is a general introduction to the form. Please provide any relevant information about your health and wellness goals.
* **Contact Information** (ID 2, Text, Not Required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to help your coach contact you.
* **Goals and Objectives** (ID 3, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select one or more goals you're currently working towards, such as weight loss, stress management, or improving sleep.
* **Medical History** (ID 4, Select One, Not Required): Indicate if you have any medical conditions or concerns.
* **Medical Conditions** (ID 5, Select Multiple, Not Required): List any medical conditions you're currently managing or have in the past.
* **Medication** (ID 6, Text, Not Required): If you take any prescription medication, please list the name and dosage.
* **Allergies** (ID 7, Select Multiple, Not Required): Indicate any allergies you have, such as food, environmental, or medication.
* **Emergency Contact** (ID 8, Select One, Not Required): Confirm if you have an emergency contact person.
* **Medical Facilities** (ID 9, Select Multiple, Not Required): List any medical facilities you've used in the past, such as hospitals or clinics.
* **Insurance** (ID 10, Select One, Not Required): Indicate if you have health insurance.
* **Payment** (ID 11, Number, Not Required): If applicable, enter your payment information, such as a credit card number.
* **Payment Methods** (ID 12, Select Multiple, Not Required): Indicate any payment methods you'd prefer, such as cash, credit card, or online payment.
* **Onboarding Process** (ID 13, Select One, Not Required): Confirm if you've completed the onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Steps** (ID 14, Select Multiple, Not Required): List any onboarding steps you've completed, such as setting up payment or completing paperwork.
* **Coaching Goals** (ID 15, Select Multiple, Not Required): Select one or more coaching goals, such as stress management or nutrition guidance.
* **Coaching Objectives** (ID 16, Select Multiple, Not Required): List one or more coaching objectives, such as improving sleep or increasing energy levels.
* **Communication Preferences** (ID 17, Select Multiple, Not Required): Indicate your preferred communication methods, such as email, phone, or in-person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (ID 18, Text, Not Required): Provide contact information for your emergency contact person.
* **Notes** (ID 19, Text, Not Required): Add any additional comments or notes about your health and wellness journey.
* **Follow-up** (ID 20, Time, Not Required): If applicable, schedule a follow-up appointment with your coach.
* **Follow-up Method** (ID 21, Select One, Not Required): Confirm if you'd like to schedule a follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Status** (ID 22, Select One, Not Required): Indicate if the follow-up appointment is active or inactive.
* **Follow-up Notes** (ID 23, Text, Not Required): Add any notes about your follow-up appointment.
* **Follow-up Schedule** (ID 24, Date, Not Required): If applicable, schedule a follow-up appointment date.
* **Review** (ID 25, Date, Not Required): Confirm the date for a review session with your coach.

## Tips
* Be honest and thorough in your responses to ensure your coach understands your needs.
* If you're unsure about any question, ask your coach for clarification.
* Remember to review and update your information regularly to reflect changes in your health and wellness status.
