# N95 Fit Test Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The N95 Fit Test Form is a document used to record the results of fit testing for N95 respirators. The form is used to capture important information about the wearer, the test sequence, and the test results. This form is crucial for maintaining accurate records and ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the relevant information for the wearer, including their name, department, position, location, and respirator type.
2. Enter the test sequence and test duration.
3. Select the test result from the provided options.
4. Choose the individuals conducting the test from the provided options.
5. Add any comments from the wearer or tester.
6. Enter the date of the test.
7. Review and confirm the information entered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (Page 1, Organization Name, text, not required): This field is used to enter the name of the organization conducting the fit test.
* **Tester Name** (Page 1, Tester Name, text, not required): This field is for entering the name of the individual conducting the fit test.
* **Wearer Name** (Page 1, Wearer Name, text, not required): Enter the name of the individual being fit-tested.
* **Wearer Position** (Page 1, Wearer Position, text, not required): Enter the position held by the wearer.
* **Wearer Department** (Page 1, Wearer Department, text, not required): Enter the department of the wearer.
* **Wearer Location** (Page 1, Wearer Location, text, not required): Enter the location where the fit test was conducted.
* **Respirometer Type** (Page 1, Respirometer Type, text, not required): Enter the type of respirometer used during the fit test.
* **Test Sequence** (Page 1, Test Sequence, text, not required): Enter the test sequence used.
* **Test Duration** (Page 1, Test Duration, number, not required): Enter the duration of the test.
* **Tester Comments** (Page 1, Tester Comments, text, not required): Enter any comments from the tester.
* **Wearer Comments** (Page 1, Wearer Comments, text, not required): Enter any comments from the wearer.
* **Test Date** (Page 1, Test Date, date, not required): Enter the date the fit test was conducted.
* **Fit Test Result** (Page 2, Fit Test Result, select_one, required): Select the result of the fit test from the options: "Not Fit" or "Fit".
* **Test Conducted By** (Page 2, Test Conducted By, select_multiple, required): Select the individual(s) conducting the test from the options: Test Conductor 1, Test Conductor 2, Test Conductor 3.
* **Form Complete By** (Page 2, Form Complete By, time, not required): Enter the time the form is being completed.
* **Form Complete Date** (Page 2, Form Complete Date, date, not required): Enter the date the form is being completed.
