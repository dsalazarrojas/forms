# Fitness Logger Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Logger Form is designed to collect and record a client's fitness-related data. This form is intended to be completed by clients who are participating in a fitness program or working with a personal trainer. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (Client Name, text, required): Enter the client's name to associate their data with their identity.
* **Exercise Name** (Exercise Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the exercise being performed, such as "Squats" or "Lunges."
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the workout, in the format "MM/DD/YY" (e.g., 02/12/2022).
* **Weight** (Weight, number, optional): Enter the weight lifted during the exercise.
* **Reps** (Reps, number, optional): Enter the number of repetitions completed during the exercise.
* **Sets** (Sets, number, optional): Enter the number of sets completed during the exercise.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the workout, such as "Felt fatigue in my legs."
* **Assigned Trainer/Coach** (Assigned Trainer/Coach, select_one, optional): Select the name of the trainer or coach who assigned the workout.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter the client's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the client's phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about a field, refer to your assigned trainer or coach for guidance.
* Review and update your form regularly to track your progress over time.
