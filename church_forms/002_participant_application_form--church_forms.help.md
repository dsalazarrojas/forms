# Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from participants applying for various programs or events in the church. It is a mandatory form and must be completed in its entirety to be considered for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone number and Date.
2. Enter your Participant Name and Participant Number for identification purposes.
3. Provide the Seminar Date, Seminar Time, and Conference Name, which are relevant to the program/event you are applying for.
4. Fill in the Conference Number for further identification.
5. If applicable, provide Event Details and the tool you have been assigned (if any).
6. Add any additional details that you think are relevant to your application.
7. Finally, confirm whether you have been assigned a tool by selecting "Yes" or "No" under the Submit section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to participate in the program/event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you plan to participate in the program/event.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter a unique participant name for identification purposes.
* **Participant Number** (`participant_number`, text, required): Enter a unique participant number for identification purposes.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the seminar you are applying for.
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the seminar you are applying for.
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference you are applying for.
* **Conference Number** (`conference_number`, text, required): Enter the number of the conference you are applying for.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): If applicable, provide any additional details about the event or program you are applying for.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool you have been assigned for the program/event.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether you have been assigned a tool by selecting "Yes" or "No".
