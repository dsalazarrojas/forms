# Worship Team Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals interested in joining the worship team. Please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately to help us evaluate your suitability and availability for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Personal Info** section with your basic contact information.
2. Answer the **Spiritual Report** question by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' depending on your spiritual status.
3. If you have music training, select all the relevant options in the **Music Training** section.
4. In the **References** section, list at least three references with their name, phone number, and email.
5. Indicate your availability in the **Date Available** section by selecting a specific date.
6. In the **Personal Statement** section, provide a brief statement about your personal background and experience.
7. Enter your email address in the **Email** section.
8. In the **Phone Number** section, enter your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info (1)**: This is the basic contact information, please fill in your full name, address, city, state and zip.
* **Spiritual Report (2)**: Please select 'Yes' if you have a personal relationship with God and are actively involved in your spiritual growth, 'No' otherwise.
* **Music Training (3)**: Indicate if you have music training by selecting 'Yes' for each relevant option.
* **References (4)**: List at least three people who can vouch for your character and spiritual growth, with their name, phone number, and email.
* **Date Available (6)**: Select a specific date when you are available to start serving on the worship team.
* **Personal Statement (7)**: Write a brief statement about your personal background, spiritual experience, and why you'd like to join the worship team.
* **Email (8)**: Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone Number (9)**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any issues or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We appreciate your patience and understanding as we review your application.
