# private_prayer_request_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be a private prayer request form for churches to collect information from individuals who have a prayer request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1.  Fill in your name in the **Name** field.
2.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
3.  Describe your prayer request in the **Prayer Request** field.
4.  Enter the date you would like the prayer to be prayed for in the **Prayer Request Date** field.
5.  If you have a response to your prayer request, you can enter it in the **Prayer Response** field.
6.  Enter the date you received the response in the **Prayer Response Date** field.
7.  If you are a church member, enter the name of your church in the **Church** field.
8.  Enter your name as the individual making the prayer request in the **Prayer Requestor** field.
9.  If you would like to specify a church member who will be praying for you, enter their name in the **Assigned to** field.
10.  Enter the date when the prayer request was assigned to the church member in the **Assigned Date** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name**: This field asks for your name, which is required for internal use within the church.
*   **Email**: This field asks for your email address, which will be used to contact you if needed.
*   **Prayer Request**: This field asks you to describe your prayer request in as much detail as possible.
*   **Prayer Request Date**: This field asks for the date when you would like the prayer to be prayed for.
*   **Prayer Response**: This field is optional and allows you to input any response you have received from your prayer request.
*   **Prayer Response Date**: This field is optional and asks for the date when the response was received.
*   **Church**: This field asks for the name of your church.
*   **Prayer Requestor**: This field asks for your name as the individual making the prayer request.
*   **Assigned to**: This field is optional and allows you to input the name of the church member who will be praying for you.
*   **Assigned Date**: This field is optional and asks for the date when the prayer request was assigned to a church member.

## Tips
*   Please be as detailed as possible when describing your prayer request in the **Prayer Request** field.
*   If you have received a response, make sure to update the **Prayer Response Date** field accordingly.
*   If you are assigning the prayer request to a church member, make sure to update the **Assigned Date** field.
*   This form is for internal use within the church community. Do not share your prayer request with anyone not affiliated with the church.
