# Club Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in joining the club, enabling the club to understand their preferences and tailor its activities and services accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, providing your personal and membership information.
2. Select your preferred contact method to receive club updates and notifications.
3. Choose your areas of interest within the club.
4. Agree to the club's rules and code of conduct.
5. Confirm that you understand there is an annual membership fee.
6. Ensure that you are willing to receive communications from the club.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears in official documents.
* **Last Name (3)** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears in official documents.
* **Email Address (4)** (email, required): Enter the email address you would like the club to use to send you updates and newsletters.
* **Phone Number (5)** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, including the area code.
* **Date of Birth (6)** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Membership Type (8)** (select_one, required): Choose the type of membership you wish to join (Basic Member, Premium Member, Patron, Student).
* **How did you hear about our club (9)** (select_one, required): Choose how you heard about the club (Word of mouth, Social media, Website, Newspaper advertisement, Friend or family member, Other).
* **Preferred Contact Method (10)** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like the club to contact you (Email, Phone, Text message, Mail).
* **Areas of Interest (12)** (select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas of interest that apply to you (Social events, Educational workshops, Community service, Recreational activities, Networking, Leadership opportunities).
* **I have read and agree to the club rules and code of conduct (14)** (select_one, required): Choose 'I Agree' to confirm that you have read and agree to the club's rules and code of conduct.
* **I understand there is an annual membership fee (16)** (select_one, required): Choose 'I Agree' to confirm that you understand there is an annual membership fee.
* **Today's Date (17)** (date, required): Enter the current date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
