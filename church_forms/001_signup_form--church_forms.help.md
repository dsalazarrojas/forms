# Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Signup Form is designed to collect information from visitors for internal use within the church community. It is not intended for public submission or publication. Please do not submit this form if you are not associated with the church or if you have already submitted information in the past.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. In the "Message" field, provide a brief **Message** to the church community.
6. If you are representing a **Company**, enter it in the "Company" field.
7. If you have a specific **Department** within the company, enter it in the "Department" field.
8. Select your **Role** within the church community in the "Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Message**: Provide a brief message to the church community. This can include any questions, comments, or concerns you have.
* **Company**: If you are representing a company, enter it here.
* **Department**: If you are in a specific department within your company, enter it here.
* **Role**: Select your role within the church community (e.g. member, volunteer, pastor).

## Tips

* Please make sure to submit a completed and accurate form to ensure that your information is correctly recorded.
* If you have any issues or concerns, feel free to contact the church administration directly.
