# Sunday School Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sunday School Registration Form is used to register new members for Sunday school. It gathers information about the new member, including their contact details, school year, and class attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Sunday School Registration Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the new member's contact information in the "contact" field.
2. Enter the new member's email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter the new member's phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter the new member's address in the "address" field.
5. Choose whether the new member is attending a specific school year by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "school_year" field.
6. Choose which class the new member is attending by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "class" field.
7. Enter the new member's name in the "name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact** (text): Enter the new member's contact information, such as their address.
* **email** (email): Enter the new member's email address.
* **phone** (text): Enter the new member's phone number.
* **address** (text): Enter the new member's address.
* **school_year** (select_one): Choose whether the new member is attending a specific school year. Select "Yes" if they are attending, "No" otherwise.
* **class** (select_one): Choose which class the new member is attending. Select "Yes" if they are attending, "No" otherwise.
* **name** (text): Enter the new member's name.

## Tips
Make sure to complete all the required fields to ensure the new member's information is accurate and up-to-date.
