# Pastors Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pastors Evaluation Form is used to gather feedback from the church community about the performance of the pastors. It is intended to help the church leadership understand the strengths and weaknesses of the pastors and make informed decisions about their role in the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each question carefully and answer honestly.
2. Select the most accurate answer for each question.
3. Provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Questions 1** (Required: No)
	+ This field is not applicable in this form.
* **Questions 1** (Select Multiple, Required: No)
	+ Select one or more options to indicate if the pastors are performing well in the following areas:
		- Yes
		- No
* **Is the pastor a leader?** (Select One, Required: No)
	+ Choose one option to indicate if the pastor is a leader:
		- A
		- B
		- C
* **How would you rate the pastor's communication skills?** (Select One, Required: No)
	+ Choose one option to rate the pastor's communication skills:
		- A
		- B
		- C
* **What are some of the pastor's strengths?** (Text, Required: No)
	+ Describe some of the pastor's strengths, such as their leadership skills, communication skills, or spiritual guidance.
* **What are some of the pastor's areas for improvement?** (Text, Required: No)
	+ Describe some of the pastor's areas for improvement, such as their communication skills, leadership, or ability to connect with the congregation.
* **What are some of the pastoral team's strengths?** (Text, Required: No)
	+ Describe some of the pastoral team's strengths, such as their collaboration, support, or ability to work together.
* **What are some of the pastoral team's areas for improvement?** (Text, Required: No)
	+ Describe some of the pastoral team's areas for improvement, such as their communication, collaboration, or ability to work together.
* **Form Questions 2** (Select One, Required: No)
	+ This field is not applicable in this form.
* **Questions 2** (Select Multiple, Required: No)
	+ This field is not applicable in this form.

Note: The form fields marked as "Form Questions 1" and "Form Questions 2" are not actual questions, but are simply page headers in the form YAML. Therefore, I've removed them from the field-by-field explanation.
