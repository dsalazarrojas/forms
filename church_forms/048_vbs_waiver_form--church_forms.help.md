# VBS Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for VBS programs and provide emergency contact information and medical conditions for your safety and the child's safety.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. For each field, make sure to enter the correct information and select the correct options from the drop-down menu (if applicable).
3. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, make sure to check the correct box in the "Medical Conditions" field.
4. Identify the relationship between you and your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
5. For the child attending the program, enter their full name, date of birth, and any medical conditions or allergies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required:false): Enter your first name as it is written in official documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required:false): Enter your last name as it is written in official documents (e.g., driver's license).
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required:false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for any updates or communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required:false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached in case of emergencies.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required:false): Check any medical conditions you or your child has that the church should be aware of.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, required:false): Check any allergies you or your child has that the church should be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first`, `text`, required:false): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last`, `text`, required:false): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required:false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `select_one`, required:false): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g., Father, Mother, Parent, Guardian, Emergency Contact).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, required:false): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, `text`, required:false): Enter the full name of the child attending the VBS program.
* **Child DOB** (`child_dob`, `date`, required:false): Enter the date of birth of the child attending the VBS program.

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the church administration for clarification.
