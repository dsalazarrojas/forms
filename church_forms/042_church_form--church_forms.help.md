# Church Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Form is designed to gather feedback from visitors about their experience at the church. It is intended to help us understand what we do well and where we can improve, so we can better serve you and our community. Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your time in filling out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "Submit" to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Date of your visit" field with the date you attended the service.
3. Choose which service you attended by selecting one of the options from the "Which service did you attend?" dropdown menu.
4. Answer the question "Is this your first visit?" by selecting one of the options from the dropdown menu.
5. Rate the friendliness and welcoming atmosphere of the congregation by selecting one of the options from the "How friendly and welcoming was the congregation?" dropdown menu.
6. Rate the quality of the service by selecting one of the options from the "Rate the quality of the service" dropdown menu.
7. Rate the relevance of the message by selecting one of the options from the "How relevant was the message to your life?" dropdown menu.
8. Rate the cleanliness and comfort of the facilities by selecting one of the options from the "How clean and comfortable were the facilities?" dropdown menu.
9. If you have any additional thoughts or comments, you can type them in the "Additional Thoughts" section.
10. If you'd like to share what you enjoyed most about your visit, type it in the "What did you enjoy most about your visit?" field.
11. If you'd like to share what was the least enjoyable part of your visit, type it in the "What was the least enjoyable part of your visit?" field.
12. If you'd like to share how you heard about the church, select one of the options from the "How did you hear about us?" dropdown menu.
13. Answer the question "Do you plan to visit us again?" by selecting one of the options from the "Do you plan to visit us again?" dropdown menu.
14. Finally, if you'd like to share a prayer request, type it in the "Do you have a prayer request you'd like to share?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Date of your visit** (`date_of_visit_feedback`, date, required): Please fill out the date you attended the service.
* **Which service did you attend?** (`service_attended`, select_one, required): Choose which service you attended, such as Sunday Morning (Traditional) or Youth Service.
* **Is this your first visit?** (`visitor_type_feedback`, select_one, required): Select whether this is your first time visiting the church.
* **How friendly and welcoming was the congregation?** (`friendliness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the friendliness and welcoming atmosphere of the congregation.
* **Rate the quality of the service** (`service_quality_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the quality of the service, including message, music, and flow.
* **How relevant was the message to your life?** (`message_relevance_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the relevance of the message to your personal life.
* **How clean and comfortable were the facilities?** (`facilities_cleanliness_rating`, select_one, required): Rate the cleanliness and comfort of the facilities.
* **What did you enjoy most about your visit?** (`best_part_of_visit`, text, optional): Share what you enjoyed most about your visit.
* **What was the least enjoyable part of your visit?** (`least_part_of_visit`, text, optional): Share what was the least enjoyable part of your visit.
* **How did you hear about us?** (`hear_about_church`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about the church, such as social media or word of mouth.
* **Do you plan to visit us again?** (`future_visit_intent`, select_one, required): Answer whether you plan to visit us again.
* **Name** (`contact_name_visit`, text, optional): If you'd like to share your name, you can type it here.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email_visit`, email, optional): If you'd like to share your email address, you can type it here.
* **Do you have a prayer request you'd like to share?** (`prayer_request_visit`, text, optional): If you have a prayer request, you can type it here.

Note: All fields with the exception of "Additional Thoughts" and "Best Part of Visit" and "Least Part of Visit" are required.
