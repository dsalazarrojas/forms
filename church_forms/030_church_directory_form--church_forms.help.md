# Church Directory Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Directory Form is designed to collect essential information from church families, ensuring that their contact details are accurate and up-to-date. This form helps us build a comprehensive directory that serves the church community better.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the family basic information section, which includes fields for Family Last Name, Adult 1 Name, and Adult 2 Name (if applicable).
2. In the Mailing Address field, please provide your family's address, including City, State, and Zip.
3. Enter your Primary Household Phone number and Primary Email address.
4. If your family has members not listed above, list their names and birth months/days in the All Family Members field.
5. Please upload a family photo (optional) and select your preferred directory format (Digital, Printed, or Both).
6. Confirm that you consent to your family information being published in the directory and select your preferred publishing options.
7. Finally, sign your submission with your full name and select the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Family Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your family's last name.
* **Adult 1 Name** (`adult_1_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the first adult family member. If the adult's name is different from the last name above, please include both names.
* **Adult 2 Name (Optional)** (`adult_2_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the second adult family member (if applicable).
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, Text, Required): Enter your family's address, including City, State, and Zip.
* **Primary Household Phone** (`primary_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your family's primary phone number.
* **Primary Email** (`primary_email`, Email, Required): Enter your family's primary email address.
* **All Family Members** (`family_member_names`, Text, Required): List the names and birth months/days of all family members, including children.
* **Please upload a family photo** (`photo_submission_note`, Text, Optional): Upload a photo of your family (optional).
* **Preferred directory format** (`directory_format_pref`, Select One, Required): Select your preferred directory format (Digital, Printed, or Both).
* **Consent to publish** (`publish_consent`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you consent to your family information being published in the directory and select your preferred publishing options.
* **Submitted by (Full Name)** (`signature_confirmation`, Text, Required): Enter your full name as the submitter.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date`, Date, Required): Select the date you submit this form.

Please ensure that you review each field carefully and provide accurate information to ensure that your family's directory entry is complete and accurate.
