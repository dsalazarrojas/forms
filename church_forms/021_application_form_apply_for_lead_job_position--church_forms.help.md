# Application Form Apply For Lead Job Position - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential candidates applying for a lead job position in a church.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Applicant Information section with your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. In the Faith Background section, select the correct faith affiliation for you.
3. In the Professional Background section, briefly describe your professional background and experience.
4. In the Leadership Qualities section, describe your leadership qualities and experience.
5. In the References section, provide the names and contact information of two references who can vouch for your character and leadership abilities.
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Current Address** (text, required): Enter your current address for our records.
* **Position Applied For** (text, required): Enter the position you are applying for.
* **Faith Background** (note, optional): Briefly describe your faith background and how it has influenced your life.
* **Religious Affiliation** (select_one, required): Select your religious affiliation from the options provided.
* **Years of Active Service or Involvement** (number, required): Enter the number of years you have been actively serving or involved with the church.
* **Describe Your Involvement with the Church Community** (text, required): Describe your involvement with the church community, including your leadership experience and any notable achievements.
* **What is Your Spiritual Vision for Leadership?** (text, required): Describe your spiritual vision for leadership and how you would guide the church community.
* **Current Occupation** (text, optional): If applicable, enter your current occupation.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (number, required): Enter the number of years of professional experience you have.
* **Relevant Skills for this Position** (text, required): List any relevant skills you have for the lead job position.
* **Educational Background** (text, optional): Enter your educational background if applicable.
* **Leadership Qualities** (note, optional): Briefly describe any leadership qualities you possess.
* **Describe Your Leadership Experience** (text, required): Describe your leadership experience, including any notable achievements.
* **Experience Managing Teams or Groups** (text, optional): Describe your experience managing teams or groups if applicable.
* **How Do You Handle Conflicts or Disagreements?** (text, optional): Describe how you handle conflicts or disagreements.
* **Reference Name and Title** (text, required): Enter the name and title of one of your references.
* **Reference Contact Information** (text, required): Enter the contact information of one of your references.
* **Availability to Start** (date, required): Enter the date you are available to start in the position.
* **How Do You Handle Conflicts or Disagreements?** (text, optional): This field is not actually required and was likely a mistake in the YAML.
