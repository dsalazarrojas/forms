# Prayer Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Prayer Sign Up Form is a tool for collecting and managing prayer requests from members of a community or organization. It is designed to gather detailed information about each prayer request, allowing the requesters to provide specific information about their requests and the status of the prayers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. **Prayers Request**: Please enter a clear and concise description of your prayer request in the "Prayers Request" field.
2. **Requester Name**: Enter your name or the name of the individual making the prayer request in the "Requester Name" field.
3. **Email**: Provide your email address in the "Email" field. This will help us contact you if we need to follow up on your prayer request.
4. **Prayer Details**: Please provide a detailed description of your prayer request in the "Prayer Details" field.
5. **Prayer Status**: Select the current status of your prayer from the options provided in the "Prayer Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prayers Request**: A clear and concise description of your prayer request.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Please enter a description of your prayer request.
* **Requester Name**: The name of the individual making the prayer request.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Please enter your name or the name of the individual making the prayer request.
* **Email**: A valid email address for follow-up communication.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Please enter your email address.
* **Prayer Details**: A detailed description of your prayer request.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Please provide a detailed description of your prayer request.
* **Prayer Status**: Select the current status of your prayer from the options provided.
	+ Type: Select Multiple
	+ Required: No
	+ Hint: Select the current status of your prayer from the options provided.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and clear information about your prayer request.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is intended for internal use within the church or organization and should not be used for external purposes.
* You can use the "Prayer Status" field to track the progress of your prayer request.
