# Children Ministry Volunteer Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather essential information from volunteers who are interested in joining our children's ministry team. The data collected will be used to determine their suitability and availability for various roles within the ministry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your contact information, including your full legal name, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide your availability to volunteer, specifying the days and times you are available.
3. If you have any relevant experience with children, please share a brief summary.
4. Check if you consent to a background check (required for all volunteers).
5. List two references with their names and contact details.
6. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
7. Choose your preferred volunteer roles from the options provided.
8. Add any additional information you think would be helpful for the review team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): This is your full legal name that we will use to identify you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): This is the best phone number for us to contact you with any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is your email address where we can send you important information and updates.
* **Availability** (`availability`, text, required): Please specify the days and times you are available to volunteer.
* **Experience With Children** (`experience_with_children`, text, optional): If you have any experience working with children, please share a brief summary.
* **Consent for Background Check** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Please check "I consent" if you agree to a background check.
* **References** (`references`, text, required): Provide two references with their names and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Please provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Roles Interested In** (`roles_interested`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the following roles: Classroom helper, Activity leader, Storytelling, Event support.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): If you have any other notes or information that would be helpful for the review team, please include it here.
