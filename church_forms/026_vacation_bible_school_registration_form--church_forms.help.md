# vacation_bible_school_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vacation Bible school registration form is used to gather important information from both the child and their parent for the purpose of registration and emergency contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with the requested information.
2. Select the correct options for your child's grade and the permissions you give to the church staff.
3. Check or uncheck the boxes for any medical conditions or allergies that apply to your child.
4. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): The child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): The child's last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): A valid email address for the child's parent or guardian.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): A phone number for the child's parent or guardian.
* **Birth Date** (`Birth Date`, date, required): The child's birth date.
* **Parent First Name** (`Parent First Name`, text, optional): The parent/guardian's first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (`Parent Last Name`, text, optional): The parent/guardian's last name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`Emergency Contact Relationship`, text, optional): The relationship between the emergency contact and the child.
* **Child's Grade** (`Child's Grade`, select_one, required): The grade the child is currently in (1-8).
* **Parent Permission** (`Parent Permission`, select_multiple, required): You must grant permission for the child's participation in vacation Bible school.
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (`Emergency Contact Permission`, select_multiple, required): The emergency contact must grant permission for their contact information to be kept on file.
* **Medical Condition** (`Medical Condition`, note, optional): List any medical conditions the child has.
* **Medical Allergies** (`Medical Allergies`, note, optional): List any allergies the child has.
* **Food Allergies** (`Food Allergies`, note, optional): List any food allergies the child has.
* **Additional Medical Notes** (`Additional Medical Notes`, note, optional): Any additional medical information that is not listed above.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional): The name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`Emergency Contact Email`, email, optional): The email address of the emergency contact.
* **Parent Signature** (`Parent Signature`, note, required): A signature from the parent or guardian.
* **Child's Photo** (`Child's Photo`, note, optional): A photo of the child.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`Emergency Contact Signature`, note, optional): A signature from the emergency contact.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required): The date the form is submitted.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): The time the form is submitted.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Any additional comments or information.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form. Also, be sure to sign and date the form as a parent or guardian to confirm your agreement.
