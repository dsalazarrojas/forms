# Church Donation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to provide their donations to the church.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your donor information, including your name and contact details.
2. Choose how often you would like to donate (one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually).
3. Designate the fund you would like to donate to (General Fund, Building Fund, Mission Fund, etc.).
4. Choose your preferred method of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.).
5. Enter your card number and expiration date (for credit/debit card payments).
6. Decide whether you would like to cover the processing fees (100% of your donation goes to the church).
7. Select whether you would like to remain anonymous.
8. Choose your preferred method of receiving your donation receipt (email, mail, or no receipt).
9. If you would like to take advantage of your employer's matching gifts program, indicate this.
10. If you have a special message or dedication for your donation, enter it here.
11. Sign up for our newsletter to stay updated on how your donations are making an impact.
12. If you would like to add another donation to a different fund, select the option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it recorded.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for donation receipt and other communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your mailing address for tax receipt purposes.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you wish to donate.
* **Donation Frequency** (`donation_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose how often you would like to donate (one-time, weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually).
* **Designate Your Donation** (`donation_fund`, select_one, required): Choose the fund you would like to donate to (General Fund, Building Fund, Mission Fund, etc.).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of payment (Credit Card, Debit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.).
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter your card number (for credit/debit card payments).
* **Card Expiration Date** (`expiry_date`, text, optional): Enter your card expiration date (MM/YY format).
* **CVV Security Code** (`cvv`, text, optional): Enter your 3-digit code on the back of your card (for credit/debit card payments).
* **Cover Processing Fees** (`cover_fees`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to cover the processing fees (100% of your donation goes to the church).
* **Remain Anonymous** (`anonymous`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to remain anonymous (donation will not be publicly acknowledged).
* **Tax Receipt Preference** (`tax_receipt`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred method of receiving your donation receipt (email, mail, no receipt).
* **Is Your Employer Matching Gifts** (`employer_match`, select_one, optional): If you have a matching program with your employer, choose the option.
* **Special Message or Dedication** (`special_message`, text, optional): Enter a special message or dedication for your donation (in honor or memory of someone).
* **Would You Like to Receive Our Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, required): Choose whether you would like to sign up for our newsletter to stay updated on how your donations are making an impact.
* **Would You Like to Add Another Donation** (`additional_donation`, select_one, optional): If you would like to add another donation to a different fund, select this option.
