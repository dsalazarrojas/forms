# Quick And Easy Church Pledge - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect pledges and donations from church members and visitors in a simple and easy-to-use manner.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Quick And Easy Church Pledge" form.
2. Select the type of pledge you would like to make (e.g. campaign pledge, tithe pledge, etc.).
3. Fill out the corresponding fields with your pledge amount and details.
4. Provide your contact information, including email, phone, address, city, state, and zip.
5. If you have any notes or comments, you can add them in the "Notes" field.
6. Select the tool and user you would like to be assigned to your pledge.
7. Review and submit your pledge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Campaign Pledge**: A pledge to support a specific campaign or initiative. Enter the amount you would like to pledge. (Required: False)
* **Tithe Pledge**: A tithe is a tenth of one's income or assets. Enter the amount you would like to tithe. (Required: False)
* **Offering Pledge**: A pledge to support the church's general offerings. Enter the amount you would like to offer. (Required: False)
* **Building Fund Pledge**: A pledge to support the building fund, which is used for church construction and maintenance. Enter the amount you would like to pledge. (Required: False)
* **Recurring Tithing Pledge**: A recurring tithe is a regular tithe made at a specific frequency (e.g. weekly, bi-weekly, etc.). Enter the amount and frequency. (Required: False)
* **One Time Offering Pledge**: A one-time offering is a one-time donation. Enter the amount. (Required: False)
* **Campaign Goal**: The target amount for the campaign. Enter the goal amount. (Required: False)
* **Campaign Status**: The current status of the campaign. Select from the following options: "Active", "Completed", "Cancelled". (Required: False)
* **Donor Details**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your donation. (Required: False)
* **Email**: Enter your email address. (Required: False)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Required: False)
* **Address**: Enter your address. (Required: False)
* **City**: Enter your city. (Required: False)
* **State**: Enter your state. (Required: False)
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code. (Required: False)
* **Country**: Enter your country. (Required: False)
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments. (Required: False)
* **Assigned Tool**: Select a tool to be assigned to your pledge. Select from the following options: "chatjimmy", "Other". (Required: False)
* **Assigned User**: Select a user to be assigned to your pledge. Select from the following options: "User 1", "User 2". (Required: False)
* **Created At**: The date and time this form was created. (Required: False)
* **Updated At**: The date and time this form was last updated. (Required: False)
* **Created By**: Select who created this form. Select from the following options: "Yes", "No". (Required: False)
* **Updated By**: Select who last updated this form. Select from the following options: "Yes", "No". (Required: False)
* **Archived**: Select whether this form is archived. Select from the following options: "true", "false". (Required: False)
