# Church Donation Giving Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for church members and visitors to provide donations to support the church's mission and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name and email address to confirm your donation.
2. Select a preset amount or enter a specific amount you wish to donate.
3. Choose the type of donation (e.g., tithe, offering, etc.).
4. Select how often you would like to give (e.g., one-time, weekly, etc.).
5. Select your preferred payment type (e.g., credit card, bank account, etc.).
6. Enter your card information or billing address if you selected a payment type that requires it.
7. Decide if you would like to make your gift anonymous.
8. Choose whether you would like to cover the processing fee.
9. Optionally, enter a gift dedication or special comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`donor_name`, text, **required**): Enter your first and last name as you would like to be recognized.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address to receive donation confirmations and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **optional**): If you have a phone number, you can enter it for follow-up contact.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, number, **required**): Enter the amount you wish to donate. Please use dollars as the currency.
* **Or Select a Preset Amount** (`preset_amount`, select_one, **optional**): Choose a quick donation amount from the options provided.
* **Type of Donation** (`donation_type`, select_one, **required**): Select the type of donation you would like to make (e.g., tithe, offering, etc.).
* **How Often Would You Like to Give** (`frequency`, select_one, **required**): Choose how often you would like to give (e.g., one-time, weekly, etc.).
* **Payment Type** (`payment_type`, select_one, **required**): Select your preferred payment type (e.g., credit card, bank account, etc.).
* **Card Information** (`card_info`, text, **optional**): If you selected a payment type that requires it, enter your card details.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, text, **required**): Enter the address associated with your payment method.
* **Make This an Anonymous Gift** (`anonymous_gift`, select_one, **required**): Decide if you would like to make your gift anonymous.
* **Cover the Processing Fee** (`processing_fee`, select_one, **required**): Choose whether you would like to cover the processing fee.
* **Gift Dedication** (`dedication`, text, **optional**): Enter a dedication or special message for your gift.
* **Additional Comments** (`comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any special instructions or comments for the church.
