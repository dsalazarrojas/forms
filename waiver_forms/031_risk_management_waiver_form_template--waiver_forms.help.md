# Risk Management Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a template for documenting risk management information for participants and witnesses. It captures their details, acknowledgments, and agreements related to risk-taking activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have the necessary approvals and information before proceeding.
3. Review the form carefully before signing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required): Please enter the participant's name, occupation, email, phone number, and address.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): The participant must sign and date their acknowledgment of the risks involved.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, `text`, required): The witness must sign and date their acknowledgment of the participant's understanding of the risks involved.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, `date`, required): The date the participant signed the document.
* **Date Participants Start Work** (`date_participants_start_work`, `date`, required): The date the participant started their work or activity.
* **Date Participants End Work** (`date_participants_end_work`, `date`, required): The date the participant ended their work or activity.
* **Participant Name** (`participants_name`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Occupation** (`participant_occupation`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's occupation.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participants_address`, `text`, required): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participants_agreement`, `text`, required): The participant must agree to the terms and conditions of the activity.
* **Participant Agreement Date** (`participants_agreement_date`, `date`, required): The date the participant agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Witness Name** (`witness_name`, `text`, required): Enter the witness's name.
* **Witness Occupation** (`witness_occupation`, `text`, required): Enter the witness's occupation.
* **Witness Email** (`witness_email`, `text`, required): Enter the witness's email address.
* **Witness Phone** (`witness_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the witness's phone number.
* **Witness Address** (`witness_address`, `text`, required): Enter the witness's address.
* **Witness Notes** (`witness_notes`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the witness.
* **Witness Agreement** (`witness_agreement`, `text`, required): The witness must agree to the terms and conditions of the activity.
* **Witness Agreement Date** (`witness_agreement_date`, `date`, required): The date the witness agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Supervising** (`participant_supervising`, `text`, required): Check if the participant is being supervised or monitored during the activity.
* **Participant Supervising Date** (`participant_supervising_date`, `date`, required): The date the participant started being supervised or monitored.
* **Participant Supervising Signature** (`participant_supervising_signature`, `text`, required): The participant must sign and date their acknowledgment of being supervised or monitored.

Please note that some fields may be required or optional based on the specific circumstances of the activity. Ensure you have reviewed the relevant policies and procedures before filling out this form.

Tips:
* Ensure you have the necessary approvals and information before proceeding.
* Review the form carefully before signing.
* Be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
* Keep a copy of the completed form for your records.
