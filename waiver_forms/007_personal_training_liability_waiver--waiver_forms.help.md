# Personal Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a Personal Training Liability Waiver, used to collect information necessary for personal training or wellness programs. It includes sections for general information, emergency contact details, medical information, and a waiver and signature section.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your General Information (Section 1).
2. Enter the Emergency Contact (Section 2), including Name, Phone Number (Section 6), and other relevant details.
3. Provide Medical Information (Section 3), including any medical conditions you may have.
4. Enter Medical Procedure Details (Section 10), including Date (Section 21), Time (Section 22), and Location (Section 23).
5. Sign the Waiver (Section 13) and provide your signature and date (Section 15).

* General Information (`General Information`, `text`, required): This section is for you to provide your personal information, such as your name and any relevant contact details.

* Emergency Contact Email (Section 7, `text`, optional): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* Emergency Contact Address (Section 8, `text`, optional): Provide the address of your emergency contact.

### Medical Information (Section 3)
* Medical Information (Medical Information, `text`, required): Please list any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in the training program.
* Medical Conditions (Section 4, `text`, required): Describe any medical conditions you have.

### Medical Procedure (Section 10)

### Tips
- Please fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
