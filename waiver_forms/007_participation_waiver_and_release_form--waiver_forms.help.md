# Participation Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a participant's consent and agreement to participate in an event or activity. It is a legal document that releases the participant from liability for any injuries or damages that may occur during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in its entirety, making sure to answer all required fields.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account your own understanding and agreement.
3. Review the event terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
4. Sign the form to indicate your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participation** (`waiver_and_release_form_participation`, text, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to describe your participation in the event or activity. Please provide a brief description of your participation, including your role or involvement.
* **Event Information** (`waiver_and_release_form_event_info`, text, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to provide more information about the event or activity. Please provide a brief description of the event, including the date, time, location, and any other relevant details.
* **Waiver and Release Liability** (`waiver_and_release_form_liability`, select_one, required): This field is a required field. Please select one of the options to indicate your level of liability release. The options are:
	+ Option 1: Full release of liability
	+ Option 2: Limited release of liability
	+ Option 3: No release of liability
* **Participant Information** (`waiver_and_release_form_participant_info`, text, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to provide more information about yourself as a participant. Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Event Details** (`waiver_and_release_form_event_details`, text, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to provide more information about the event or activity. Please provide a brief description of the event, including any other relevant details.
* **Agreement** (`waiver_and_release_form_agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to agree to certain conditions of the event. Please select all that apply:
	+ Option 1: I have read and understand the event terms and conditions.
	+ Option 2: I have read and understand the event rules and regulations.
	+ Option 3: I have read and understand the event safety guidelines.
* **Participant Acknowledgement** (`waiver_and_release_form_participant_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): This field is a required field. Please select one of the options to acknowledge your understanding of the event terms and conditions. The options are:
	+ Option 1: I have read and understand the event terms and conditions.
	+ Option 2: I do not have read and understand the event terms and conditions.
	+ Option 3: I do not understand the event terms and conditions.
* **Event Terms and Conditions** (`waiver_and_release_form_event_terms`, note, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to read and review the event terms and conditions. Please read and review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
* **Event Terms and Conditions 1** (`waiver_and_release_form_event_terms_1`, note, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to read and review the event terms and conditions. Please read and review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
* **Event Terms and Conditions 2** (`waiver_and_release_form_event_terms_2`, note, required/optional): This field is not a required field. However, it is still an important part of the form as it allows you to read and review the event terms and conditions. Please read and review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the form.
