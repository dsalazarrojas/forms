# Swimming Pool Waiver Release Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a waiver release form designed to obtain informed consent from guests to participate in swimming pool activities. It gathers essential information about their emergency contacts, medical history, and swimming pool usage preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Guest Name** in the first field. This is a required field and should be filled out with your full name.
2. If you have an emergency contact, please enter their **Emergency Contact** in the second field. This field is optional.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field. This should be a valid email address that we can use to contact you in case of an emergency.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field. This should be a valid phone number that we can use to contact you in case of an emergency.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the fifth field. This should be in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
6. If you need to report an emergency contact's **Emergency Address**, please enter it in the sixth field. This field is optional.
7. Enter the **Emergency Phone** number of your emergency contact in the seventh field. This should be a valid phone number that we can use to contact your emergency contact in case of an emergency.
8. If you have any **Medical Conditions**, please describe them in the eighth field. This field is optional.
9. Please sign the **Signature** in the ninth field. This is a required field and indicates that you have read and understood the waiver agreement.
10. If you have any **Medical History**, please describe it in the tenth field. This field is optional.
11. Please select your **Medical Certification** from the list in the eleventh field. This is a required field and indicates that you have been certified for swimming.
12. Select your **Waiver Agreement** preference from the list in the twelfth field. This is a required field and indicates that you agree to waive all rights and claims to any injuries that may occur while in the pool.
13. Choose the **Swimming Pool Area** you plan to use. Please select from either "In-pool areas" or "Outside pool areas".
14. Select the **Swimming Pool Equipment** you plan to use. Please select from "Pool noodles", "Pool volleyball", or "Pool chairs".
15. Choose your desired level of **Swimming Pool Supervision**. Please select from "Adult supervision", "Adult supervision with lifeguard", or "No supervision".
16. Select your desired **Swimming Pool Capacity**. Please select from "Up to 5", "Up to 10", or "Up to 20".
17. Choose your **Relationship to Guest**. Please select from "Parent/Child", "Spouse", or "Other".
18. Finally, select your **Swimming Pool Responsibility**. Please select from "Guest is solely responsible" or "Guest and owner are responsible".
