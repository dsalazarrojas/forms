# Dance Performance Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from participants prior to a dance performance or class. It aims to ensure that each participant is aware of the potential risks involved in a physically active dance class and is taking responsibility for their health and well-being. By signing the waiver, you are acknowledging that you understand the risks associated with the activity and are willing to participate despite these risks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please follow these steps:

a. Start by providing your **Participant Information** in the "Participant Information" field.
b. Next, provide more details about yourself in the "Participant Details" field.
c. If you have an emergency contact, please enter their information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
d. You can also specify your relationship with the emergency contact and their address if you'd like.
e. You can add any emergency contact's phone number and their emergency contact's emergency information.
f. Select your insurance information (e.g., private health insurance, Medicare, Medicaid, military insurance, or indicate you have no insurance) in the "Insurance Info" field.
g. Finally, provide your insurance provider's information and your insurance group number if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (Participant Information)**: Please provide your name and any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Participant Details (Participant Details)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional details about yourself that might be helpful for our dance team to know.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Enter the name and contact information of a person we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship)**: Specify your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, guardian).
* **Emergency Contact Address (Emergency Contact Address)**: Enter the address of the emergency contact's location.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Emergency (Emergency Contact Emergency)**: If you or your emergency contact has any emergency medical information, enter it here.
* **Waiver (Waiver)**: Carefully read and check the boxes for the following:
	+ I acknowledge that I am participating in a physically active dance class, which may include physical contact, lifting, bending, jumping, or other physical movements that involve risks.
	+ I understand that there may be risks involved with participating in a physically active dance class, including physical injury or illness.
	+ I agree to participate in a physically active dance class with these risks in mind.
* **Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)**: If you have any medical conditions, please enter them here.
* **Allergies (Allergies)**: List any allergies you have.
* **Medications (Medications)**: If you are taking any medications, list them here.
* **Doctor Information (Doctor Information)**: If you have any medical information from your doctor, please enter it here.
* **Insurance Info (Insurance Info)**: Select one or more of the following insurance options:
	+ Private health insurance
	+ Medicare
	+ Medicaid
	+ Military insurance
	+ None
* **Insurance Provider (Insurance Provider)**: Enter your insurance provider's name.
* **Insurance Group (Insurance Group)**: Enter your insurance group number.
* **Insurance Group Number (Insurance Group Number)**: Enter your insurance group number.
* **Date of Birth (Date of Birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Medical Clearance (Medical Clearance)**: If you have any medical clearance information from a doctor, enter it here.

## Tips

- Make sure to carefully read and understand the waiver before signing it.
- If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them accurately.
- Ensure you have the correct insurance information and provider details.
- Enter your date of birth correctly.
- If you have any medical clearance information, enter it in the "Medical Clearance" field.
