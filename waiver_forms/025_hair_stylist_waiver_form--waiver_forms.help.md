# Hair Stylist Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a waiver form for hair stylists to ensure informed consent from clients before providing services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Name**, which should be your real name or the name you want to be addressed as.
2. Enter the **Service Date** when the service is scheduled to take place.
3. Enter the **Service Start Time** when the service is expected to begin.
4. Enter your **Stylist Name** for identification purposes.
5. Check the box for **Release of Liability** to confirm that you, as the client, release the service provider from any liabilities.
6. Sign and date the **Signature** field as the service provider.
7. Sign and date the **Client Signature** field as the client.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: The client's name, which should be entered for identification purposes.
* **Service Date**: The date when the service is scheduled to take place.
* **Service Start Time**: The time when the service is expected to begin.
* **Stylist Name**: The name of the hair stylist providing the service.
* **Release of Liability**: Check this box to confirm that you, as the client, release the service provider from any liabilities. If you choose "Other", please describe your choice in the available field.
* **Signature**: The service provider's signature, which should be signed and dated as proof of acceptance.
* **Client Signature**: The client's signature, which should be signed and dated as proof of acceptance.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields to avoid any potential issues.
- Review the **Release of Liability** carefully before signing.
