# Medical Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Medical Waiver Form is a document used to collect necessary information for medical waiver purposes. It is designed to gather essential details from clients or patients to ensure their safety and well-being during medical procedures or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your participation information on the first page.
2. Read and sign the informed consent to acknowledge your understanding of the risks involved.
3. Sign the risk acknowledgement to confirm your understanding of the potential risks.
4. Waive your liability on the liability waiver page.
5. If applicable, provide your medical insurance information.
6. If necessary, list your emergency contact and their contact information.
7. If applicable, provide your medical insurance provider's name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Medical Waiver Form Participation Information**: This is where you provide your participation information, such as your name, date of birth, and medical history.
* **Informed Consent**: Read this page carefully and sign it to acknowledge that you understand the risks involved in the medical procedure or activity.
* **Risk Acknowledgement**: This is a statement to confirm that you understand the potential risks of the medical procedure or activity.
* **Liability Waiver**: Sign this page to waive your liability in case of any accidents or injuries during the medical procedure or activity.
* **Medical Insurance**: If you have medical insurance, please provide your insurance provider's name and contact information. This is optional but recommended.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, please list their name, relationship, and contact information. This is optional but recommended.
* **Medical Insurance Provider**: If you have medical insurance, please provide your insurance provider's name and contact information. This is optional but recommended.
* **Medical Insurance Provider Phone**: If you have medical insurance, please provide your insurance provider's phone number. This is optional but recommended.
* **Tips**: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
