# Dance Ministry Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for dance ministry programs or events. It gathers information from participants and their parents or guardians to ensure that everyone is aware of the risks involved and can provide emergency contact information in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's name, parent/guardian's name, and emergency contact information.
2. Check the waiver status (Approved, Denied, or Partially Approved).
3. Add any additional notes if necessary.
4. Have the parent/guardian and participant sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the participant's name as it appears on their official documentation (e.g., ID, passport, etc.).
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's name as it appears on their official documentation (e.g., ID, passport, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, and address.
* **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the participant (e.g., Mother, Father, Guardian, etc.).
* **Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Address**: Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Sign and date this field as the parent or guardian.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which this waiver is signed.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date this field as the participant.
* **Date**: Enter the date on which this waiver is signed as the participant.
* **Dance Ministry**: Enter the name of the dance ministry or organization hosting the event.
* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program or event for which this waiver applies.
* **Waiver Status**: Select the waiver status (Approved, Denied, or Partially Approved).
* **Additional Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the participant's participation.
* **Emergency Contact Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the emergency contact information.
