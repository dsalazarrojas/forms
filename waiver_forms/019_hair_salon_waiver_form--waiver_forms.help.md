# Hair Salon Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Hair Salon Waiver Form is a document designed to be filled out by clients before receiving services from a hair salon. It contains sections for client details, contact information, service information, and a liability waiver that clients must sign.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the questions.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely with the requested information.
3. Sign the liability waiver section to confirm you have read and understand its terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (`client_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your basic contact information. Please fill out your name, address, phone number, and email address as applicable.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for your contact information, if different from your basic client information. Please fill out your name, address, phone number, and email address as applicable.
* **Service Information** (`service_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is for the details of the services you are requesting. Please be specific about the type of service and any special requirements you have.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, `text`, required: true): This is a critical section where you must sign to confirm you have read and understand the terms of the waiver. Please sign your name and date to indicate your agreement.
* **Client Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required: true): This is the section where you must sign your name to confirm your identity and acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver terms. Please sign your name and date.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you understand the terms of the waiver before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the salon staff for clarification.
* The form must be completed accurately and completely to ensure that we can provide you with the best possible service.
