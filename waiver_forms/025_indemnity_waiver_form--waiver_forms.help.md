# Indemnity Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to document and verify the participant's and witness's information, as well as the notarized information, for an indemnity waiver. The form is divided into several sections, each with its own purpose:

* Participant Info: Gather participant details such as name, signature, and contact information.
* Witness Info: Collect witness information, including their signature and contact details.
* Waiver Info: Record information about the waiver, including the notarized date and time.
* Participant Signatures: Verify participant signatures for the waiver.
* Witness Signatures: Verify witness signatures for the waiver.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's information, including their name, signature, and contact details.
2. Record witness information, including their name, signature, and contact details.
3. Document the waiver information, including the notarized date and time.
4. Verify participant and witness signatures.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`Participant Info 1`, `Participant Info 2`, `Participant Info 3`, etc.): Enter the participant's name, signature, and contact information.
* **Participant Signature** (`Participant Signature 1`, `Participant Signature 2`, etc.): Sign the waiver as the participant.
* **Witness Info** (`Witness Info 1`, `Witness Info 2`, etc.): Enter the witness's name, signature, and contact information.
* **Witness Signature** (`Witness Signature 1`, `Witness Signature 2`, etc.): Sign the waiver as the witness.
* **Notarized By** (`Notarized By 1`, `Notarized By 2`, etc.): Document the notarized information, including date and time.
* **Date** (`Date 1`, `Date 2`, etc.): Enter the date the waiver was notarized.
* **Time** (`Time 1`, `Time 2`, etc.): Record the time the waiver was notarized.
* **Waiver Info** (`Waiver Info 1`, `Waiver Info 2`, etc.): Record information about the waiver, including any relevant details.
* **Waiver Text** (`Waiver Text 1`, etc.): Enter any additional information about the waiver.
* **Witness Information** (`Witness Info 1`, `Witness Info 2`, etc.): Enter witness contact information.
