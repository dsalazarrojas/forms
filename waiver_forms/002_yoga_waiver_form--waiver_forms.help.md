# yoga_waiver_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The yoga_waiver_form is a document used to gather information from individuals who are participating in yoga activities. This form is used to collect contact details, medical information, and emergency response information. It is intended to be filled out by the individual or their representative, such as a parent or guardian.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your contact number.
4. (Optional) Enter emergency contact information, including the name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
5. (Optional) Provide medical information, including medical conditions, doctor's name, and doctor's note.
6. Select your emergency response option.
7. (Optional) Select your medical insurance option.
8. (Optional) Select your emergency contact method.
9. (Optional) Select your emergency response method.
10. Sign and date the form with the current date and time at the location where you signed it.
11. Enter your IP address and browser information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): This is the individual's first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): This is the individual's last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): This is the individual's email address.
* **Contact Number** (4, text, required): This is the individual's contact number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (5, text, required): This is the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (6, text, required): This is the relationship of the emergency contact to the individual.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (7, text, required): This is the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (8, email, required): This is the email address of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Info** (9, text, required): This is any medical information the individual wants to provide.
* **Medical Condition** (10, text, required): This is any medical conditions the individual has.
* **Doctor Name** (11, text, required): This is the name of the individual's doctor.
* **Doctor Phone** (12, text, required): This is the doctor's phone number.
* **Doctor Note** (13, text, required): This is any note from the doctor.
* **Parent Name** (14, text, required): This is the name of the parent or guardian of the individual.
* **Parent Phone** (15, text, required): This is the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Note** (16, text, required): This is any note from the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Response** (17, select_one, required): This is how the individual wants to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Insurance** (18, select_one, required): This is the individual's medical insurance information.
* **Emergency Contact Method** (19, select_one, required): This is the method of emergency contact.
* **Emergency Response Method** (20, select_one, required): This is the method of emergency response.
* **Sign Date** (21, date, required): This is the date and time the form was signed.
* **Sign Time** (22, time, required): This is the date and time the form was signed.
* **Sign Location** (23, text, required): This is the location where the form was signed.
* **Sign IP** (24, text, required): This is the IP address of the device used to sign the form.
* **Sign Browser** (25, text, required): This is the browser used to sign the form.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
* This form is only for individuals who are participating in yoga activities.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the relevant authority.
