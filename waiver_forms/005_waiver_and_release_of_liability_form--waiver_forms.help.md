# Waiver And Release Of Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants and obtain their waiver and release of liability for a specific event or activity. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and honestly to ensure your safety and the organizer's liability protection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Information" section with your details.
2. Provide the waiver information in the "Waiver Information" section.
3. Enter the release information in the "Release Information" section.
4. In the "Emergency Contact" section, fill out the details of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
5. Enter your participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
6. Provide the waiver details in the "Waiver Details" section.
7. Sign the "Signature" section to confirm your agreement.
8. Date and time of signing in the "Date Signed" section.
9. If necessary, add the waiver text in the "Waiver Text" section.
10. Finally, sign the "Participant Signature" section to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant participant information. (Text, optional)
* **Waiver Information**: Describe the waiver you want to sign, including any specific conditions or exceptions. (Text, optional)
* **Release Information**: Describe the release of liability you're signing, including any specific terms or conditions. (Text, optional)
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the details of the person to contact in case of an emergency. (Text, optional)
* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details, including any relevant medical information or contact methods. (Text, optional)
* **Waiver Details**: Describe the waiver you're signing, including any specific conditions or exceptions. (Text, optional)
* **Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your agreement. (Text, required)
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date and time you signed this form. (Date, optional)
* **Waiver Text**: If necessary, add any additional waiver text or conditions. (Text, optional)
* **Participant Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your agreement. (Text, required)

## Tips
* Please fill out this form accurately and honestly.
* Make sure to read and understand the waiver and release of liability before signing.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, ask the organizer for clarification.
* Keep a copy of this form for your records.
