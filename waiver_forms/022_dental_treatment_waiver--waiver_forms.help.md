Dental Treatment Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information and obtain consent from patients before undergoing dental treatment. The form is designed to collect patient information, contact details, medical conditions, waiver information, and consent, which are essential for providing safe and effective dental care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the patient's information in the "Patient Info" field, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant details.
2.  In the "Contact Info" field, enter the patient's contact information, such as their address, phone number, and email.
3.  In the "Waiver Info" field, provide any additional information related to the waiver, such as the treatment being offered, risks associated with the treatment, and any special instructions.
4.  Select any medical conditions that apply to the patient in the "Medical Conditions" field. This will help the dentist understand any potential risks or complications related to the treatment.
5.  Choose one of the options in the "Consent" field to acknowledge that the patient has been informed and agrees to the treatment.
6.  Finally, sign and date the "Patient Signature" field to confirm that the patient has read, understood, and agreed to the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Patient Info**: Enter the patient's name, contact details, and any other relevant information.
*   **Contact Info**: Enter the patient's contact information, such as their address, phone number, and email.
*   **Waiver Info**: Provide any additional information related to the waiver, such as the treatment being offered, risks associated with the treatment, and any special instructions.
*   **Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions that apply to the patient, such as allergies, medical history, or other relevant health conditions.
*   **Consent**: Choose one of the options to acknowledge that the patient has been informed and agrees to the treatment.
*   **Patient Signature**: Sign and date the field to confirm that the patient has read, understood, and agreed to the waiver.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
*   Take your time to read and understand the waiver information carefully before signing.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns, ask your dentist or dental assistant for clarification.
