# Fitness Liability Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver for individuals participating in physical activities or events. It is used to gather information about the participant's general information, emergency contact details, medical history, and allergies or medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **General Information**: Fill in your name and contact information in the General Information section.
2. **Participant Information**: Provide your participant information, which includes your name, email address, and phone number.
3. **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact person, fill in their name, relationship, and contact information.
4. **Medical Information**: List any medical conditions or allergies you may have, such as high blood pressure or a peanut allergy.
5. **Allergies**: Select any allergies or medical conditions from the list that apply to you.
6. **Medical Conditions**: Choose any medical conditions that apply to you.
7. **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify the relationship between you and your emergency contact person.
8. **Medical Authorization**: Confirm whether you give medical authorization for the event organizers to access your medical information.
9. **Medical Provider**: If applicable, provide the name and contact information of your medical provider.
10. **Medical Note**: Enter any additional medical notes or information.
11. **Physical Note**: Enter any physical notes or information, such as any recent injuries or conditions.
12. **Signature**: Sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the waiver.
13. **Date**: Fill in the date you sign this form.
14. **Time**: Fill in the time you sign this form.
15. **Waiver Text**: This is the waiver text that you need to accept. Please read and sign it carefully.
