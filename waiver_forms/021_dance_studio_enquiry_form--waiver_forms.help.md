# Dance Studio Enquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from prospective dance studio clients. Please fill it out accurately to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To get started, click on the "Submit" button and begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. You can select multiple programs of interest from the "Program Interested in" dropdown menu.
4. Make sure to enter a valid date in the "Date" field.
5. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Provide any additional message or comments in the "Message" field.
7. If you have any assigned tools or references, enter them in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Program Interested in** (`program_interested_in`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the dance programs you are interested in (e.g., Ballet, Hip Hop, Jazz, Tap, Ballroom).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter a valid date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for follow-up.
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have any assigned tools or references, enter them here.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field is for internal use and you can ignore it.
* **Waiver Form Version** (`waiver_form_version`, number, optional): This field is for internal use and you can ignore it.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Select all relevant programs of interest.
- Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
