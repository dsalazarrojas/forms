# Hair Stylist Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hair Stylist Waiver Form is designed to collect essential information from clients before and after service. This form helps in maintaining a record of client and stylist details, service information, and waiver terms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Form Submission**: Enter a brief description of the service provided to the client.
2. **Client Information**: Enter the client's name, address, phone number, email, and IP address (yes/no).
3. **Stylist Information**: Enter the stylist's name, address, phone number, and email.
4. **Service Details**: Enter the service provider and any additional service details.
5. **Service Cost**: Enter the cost of the service.
6. **Waiver Information**: Choose whether the client or stylist signed the waiver (yes/no).
7. **Release Information**: Enter the release date and time, if applicable.
8. **Release Location**: Enter where the release took place, if applicable.
9. **Additional Notes**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Submission**: A brief description of the service provided to the client.
* **Stylist Name**: The name of the hair stylist who performed the service.
* **Date of Service**: The date the service was provided.
* **Time of Service**: The time the service was provided.
* **Client Signature**: Whether the client has signed the waiver (yes/no).
* **Stylist Signature**: Whether the stylist has signed the waiver (yes/no).
* **Release of Liabilities**: Any liabilities release information.
* **Form Completion Date**: The date the form was completed.
* **Service Details**: A description of the service provided.
* **Service Description**: A description of the service.
* **Client Name**: The name of the client.
* **Client Address**: The address of the client.
* **Stylist Address**: The address of the stylist.
* **Client Phone Number**: The phone number of the client.
* **Stylist Phone Number**: The phone number of the stylist.
* **Client Email**: The email of the client.
* **Stylist Email**: The email of the stylist.
* **Service Cost**: The cost of the service.
* **Service Provider**: The name of the service provider.
* **Client IP**: Whether the client's IP address was recorded (yes/no).
* **Stylist IP**: Whether the stylist's IP address was recorded (yes/no).
* **Release Date**: The date the release was made.
* **Release Time**: The time the release was made.
* **Release Location**: The location where the release was made.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the service.
