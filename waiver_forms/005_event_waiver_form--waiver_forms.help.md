# Event Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Waiver Form is designed to collect information and acknowledgments related to an event and its participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before filling it out.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, especially if any fields are marked as "required".
3. If a field is optional, only fill it out if it's relevant to your situation or event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Event Waiver Form 1** (`event_waiver_form_1`, `text`, optional)
This field is an open-ended text field where you can enter any relevant information about the event.

### 2. **Participant Info** (`event_waiver_form_2`, `select_one`, optional)
Select the relevant option for the participant's status or involvement in the event.

### 3. **Event Waiver Form 3** (`event_waiver_form_3`, `number`, optional)
This field is intended for entering numbers or quantities related to the event.

### 4. **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, optional)
Enter the age of the participant if it's relevant to your situation or event.

### 5. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter the name of the participant.

### 6. **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, `text`, optional)
Enter any contact information for the participant.

### 7. **Waiver Terms** (`waiver_terms`, `text`, optional)
Enter any specific waiver terms or conditions related to the event.

### 8. **Event Waiver Form 8** (`event_waiver_form_8`, `date`, optional)
This field is intended for entering dates related to the event.
