# Sports Training Liability Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a liability waiver designed for sports training activities. It collects information about the member participating in the activity, their emergency contact, and the coach/training details. This form aims to ensure that both the member and the coach/trainer are aware of and agree to the terms of the activity, including any potential risks involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the member information section with your personal details.
2. Provide emergency contact information in case of any accidents or emergencies.
3. Fill out the coaching/training details, including date, time, coach's name, phone number, and email.
4. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
5. Sign the waiver form to confirm that you have read and understood the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification document.
* **Member Address** (`member_address`, text, required: false): Enter your current address, including street number and postal code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required: false): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship Other** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, required: false): If your emergency contact person does not fit the above relationships, describe their relationship with you (e.g., friend, neighbor, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone Other** (`emergency_contact_phone_other`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person if they do not fit the above relationships.
* **Member Signature** (`member_signature`, note, required: false): This is a signature field for the member to sign and date the waiver form.
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the training facility or location.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the training facility or location.
* **Facility City** (`facility_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the training facility or location.
* **Facility State** (`facility_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state or province of the training facility or location.
* **Facility Zip** (`facility_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the postal code of the training facility or location.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the sports training activity.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the sports training activity.
* **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the coach or trainer.
* **Coach Phone** (`coach_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the coach or trainer.
* **Coach Email** (`coach_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the coach or trainer.
* **Coach Address** (`coach_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the coach or trainer.
* **Coach City** (`coach_city`, text, required: false): Enter the city of the coach or trainer's location.
* **Coach State** (`coach_state`, text, required: false): Enter the state or province of the coach or trainer's location.
* **Coach Zip** (`coach_zip`, text, required: false): Enter the postal code of the coach or trainer's location.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required: false): This is a signature field for the parent or guardian to sign and date the waiver form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any information, consult with the coach or trainer.
* This form is not a substitute for proper medical clearance or evaluation.
* It's recommended to keep a copy of the signed waiver form for your records.
