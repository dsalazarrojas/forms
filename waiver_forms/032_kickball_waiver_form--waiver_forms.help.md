# Kickball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kickball Waiver Form is a mandatory document that players must complete before participating in the Kickball league. It serves as a contract between the player and the league, outlining the terms and conditions of participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Player Name** in the first field. This is the primary identifier for your participation in the league.
2. Provide your **Email** address in the second field. This will be used for communication and updates from the league.
3. Enter the **Date of Prophets Meeting**, but be aware that the "prophets" field seems to be an error in the form schema and is likely intended to be "prophet" (singular). Please ignore this field for now and focus on the rest of the form.
4. Choose the type of waiver that applies to you: **Waiver type**. Select the sport you are participating in.
5. If applicable, select the version of the waiver you are agreeing to. However, this field appears to be optional and may not be applicable to everyone.
6. Check the boxes to indicate your **Player Consent**. You must agree to participate in all games, including playoffs, or indicate that you will not participate in any games.
7. If you have any additional information or comments, you can provide them in the **Note** field.
8. Finally, enter your **Phone Number** for contact purposes.
