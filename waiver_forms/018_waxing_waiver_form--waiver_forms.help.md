# Waxing Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Waxing Waiver Form is a document that collects client information and emergency contact details in case of a medical emergency. It helps us stay informed and ensure that we can contact the client or their emergency contact if needed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal contact information, such as your name and contact details on page 1.
2. Provide your medical history, if applicable.
3. Specify your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you on pages 2-4.
4. List any allergies or medications you take, if applicable, on page 5.
5. Date and sign the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information** (page 1):
	+ A place to provide your personal details.
* **Contact Details** (page 1):
	+ A place to provide your contact information.
* **Consent Details** (page 2):
	+ A place to provide any specific consent details, if applicable.
* **Medical History** (page 3):
	+ A place to provide any medical conditions or history you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (page 4):
	+ A place to provide your emergency contact's name, phone number, and relationship with you.
* **Allergy/Medication** (page 5):
	+ A list of allergies or medications you take.
* **Signature Date** (page 6):
	+ Date and sign the form, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (page 6):
	+ Your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 6):
	+ Your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (page 6):
	+ Your emergency contact's relationship with you.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (page 6):
	+ Your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (page 7):
	+ Additional phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (page 7):
	+ Additional relationship details with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (page 7):
	+ Additional address for your emergency contact.
* **Signature** (page 8):
	+ A note to provide your signature, indicating that you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure we have accurate and up-to-date information about you.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please be as detailed as possible.
* Make sure to get your emergency contact's consent before filling in their details.
* Date and sign the form when you have reviewed and agreed to the terms.
