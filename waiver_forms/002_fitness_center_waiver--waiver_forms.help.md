# fitness_center_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fitness_center_waiver form is used to obtain waivers for participants in activities or programs at the fitness center. It gathers necessary information from individuals, including general information, emergency contact details, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by selecting the Waiver Type that applies to you. If you are unsure, select "Personal Waiver".
2.  If you selected "Personal Waiver", fill in your emergency contact information.
3.  If you selected "Group Waiver", fill in the group information and select the contact relationship with the group members.
4.  Enter your Emergency Phone number.
5.  Enter your Email address.
6.  Fill in your emergency contact information.
7.  Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (1)**: This section is used to gather your general information. Please ensure that all details are accurate and up-to-date.
* **Waiver Type (2)**: Select the type of waiver that applies to you. You can choose between "Personal Waiver" and "Group Waiver".
* **Emergency Contact (3)**: This is the primary contact person in case of an emergency. Please select the relationship between you and this contact person.
* **Contact Relationship (4)**: If you selected "Group Waiver", this is used to describe your relationship with the group members.
* **Emergency Phone (5)**: Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Email (6)**: Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Address (7)**: Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Terms and Conditions (8)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the fitness center.
* **Sign (9)**: Date and sign this form to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Date (10)**: Enter the date you sign this form.
* **Time (11)**: Enter the time you sign this form.
* **Witness Signature (12)**: If a witness is present, they must sign this form.
* **Witness Name (13)**: If a witness is present, enter their name.
* **Witness (14)**: Select whether a witness is present or not.

Note: Some fields are not required, but it's essential to fill in all the necessary fields to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
