# Escape Room And Party Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room And Party Liability Waiver Form is designed to ensure that participants understand and accept the risks associated with participating in an escape room event. It also collects important contact and medical information to guarantee the safety and security of all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Fill in your information accurately and honestly.
3. Make sure to sign the electronic signature and participant signature fields, as they are required to confirm your acceptance of the waiver terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Party Host Information (Party Host Info)
* **party_host_info** (text, required): This field is used to collect information about the party host. Please fill in the name and contact details of the person hosting the event.

### Participant Information (Participant Info)
* **participant_info** (text, required): This field is where you should provide your personal information, including your full name and any other details as required.

### Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)
* **emergency_contact** (text, required): In case of an emergency, please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact who can be reached on your behalf.

### Risk Acknowledgement (Risk Acknowledgement)
* **risk_acknowledgement** (text, required): Please acknowledge that you understand the risks associated with participating in the escape room event. This includes but is not limited to, physical injuries, mental fatigue and other hazards.

### Waiver (Waiver)
* **waiver** (text, optional): This field is used to acknowledge that you are giving up certain rights and privileges. Please review it carefully and understand its implications.

### Medical Conditions (Medical Conditions)
* **medical_conditions** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that might affect your participation in the event, please let us know.

### Allergy Information (Allergy Information)
* **allergy_information** (text, optional): If you have any allergies or medical conditions that we should be aware of, please provide details.

### Emergency Medical Authorization (Emergency Medical Authorization)
* **emergency_medical_authorization** (text, optional): If an emergency medical procedure is required, this field is for providing authorization.

### Electronic Signature (Electronic Signature)
* **electronic_signature** (text, optional): This is an electronic signature confirming that you agree to the terms of the waiver. Please sign carefully as it is required.

### Participant Signature (Participant Signature)
* **participant_signature** (text, required): This is your signature confirming that you have read and agreed upon the waiver terms. Please sign carefully as it is required.

### Medical Provider Information (Medical Provider)
* **medical_provider** (text, optional): If you have a medical provider who needs to be informed in case of an emergency, please provide their information.

### Medical Provider Signature (Medical Provider Signature)
* **medical_provider_signature** (text, optional): If you have a medical provider who needs to sign the waiver, please have them sign here.

### Medical History (Medical History)
* **medical_history** (text, optional): If you have any medical history that might affect your participation in the event, please let us know.

### Participant Age (Participant Age)
* **participant_age** (number, optional): Please provide your age to us.

### Participant Gender (Participant Gender)
* **participant_gender** (text, optional): Please select your gender.

### Participant Date of Birth (Participant Date of Birth)
* **participant_dob** (date, optional): Please provide your date of birth to us.

### Participant Phone (Participant Phone)
* **participant_phone** (text, optional): Please provide your phone number to us.

### Participant Email (Participant Email)
* **participant_email** (email, optional): Please provide your email to us.

### Participant Address (Participant Address)
* **participant_address** (text, optional): Please provide your address to us.

### Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)
* **emergency_contact_name** (text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name.

### Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)
* **emergency_contact_phone** (text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their phone number.

### Emergency Contact Relationship (Emergency Contact Relationship)
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, describe your relationship with them.

### Waiver Terms (Waiver Terms)
* **waiver_terms** (text, optional): Please review the waiver terms carefully and acknowledge that you understand them.

### Additional Waiver Terms (Waiver Terms 2)
* **waiver_terms_2** (text, optional): Please review the additional waiver terms carefully and acknowledge that you understand them.

### Last Waiver Terms (Waiver Terms 3)
* **waiver_terms_3** (text, optional): Please review the last waiver terms carefully and acknowledge that you understand them.

Please review the form carefully and make sure you understand each field before completing it. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us before the event. Thank you for participating, and we look forward to hosting you in the escape room event!
