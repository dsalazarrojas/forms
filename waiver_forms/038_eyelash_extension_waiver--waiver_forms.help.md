# Eyelash Extension Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to capture information from customers and businesses for eyelash extension services. It includes fields for customer and business contact details, service information, and agreement to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Form Id** (if required).
2. Provide your **Customer Name** and **Address**.
3. Enter your **City** and **Zip**.
4. Enter your **Date of Service** and **Time of Service**.
5. Select whether you received **Yes** or **No** to **Type of Service**.
6. Choose which **Services Provided** you received (select multiple options if applicable).
7. Sign with a **Customer Signature** indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
8. Sign with a **Business Signature** indicating their agreement to the terms and conditions.
9. Enter your **Date** of completion.
10. Provide your **Email** and **Phone**.
11. Enter your **Fax**.
12. Enter your **Business Name**, **Business Address**, **Business City**, **Business State**, **Business Zip**, **Business Email**, **Business Phone**, and **Business Fax**.
13. Enter your **Business License**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id** (1): 
    * Type: Number
    * Description: Enter your unique form ID (if required).
* **Customer Name** (2):
    * Type: Text
    * Description: Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Address** (3):
    * Type: Text
    * Description: Enter your home or mailing address.
* **City** (4):

    * Description: Enter the city where you reside.
* **Zip** (6):
    * Type: Text
    * Description: Enter your zip code.
* **Date of Service** (7):
    * Type: Date
    * Description: Enter the date you received the eyelash extension services.
* **Time of Service** (8):
    * Type: Time
    * Description: Enter the time you received the eyelash extension services.
* **Type of Service** (9):
    * Type: Select One
    * Description: Select whether you received the services you were promised.
* **Services Provided** (10):
    * Type: Select Multiple
    * Description: Select which services you received from our team.
* **Customer Signature** (11):
    * Type: Note
    * Description: Sign with your signature indicating your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Business Signature** (12):
    * Type: Note

* **Email** (14):
