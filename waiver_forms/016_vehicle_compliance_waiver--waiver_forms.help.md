# vehicle-compliance-waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle-compliance-waiver form is designed to obtain a legally binding waiver from customers acknowledging and agreeing to specific terms and conditions related to vehicle usage or possession.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your business name.
2.  Sign your name.
3.  Enter the date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Business Name** (Business Name, text, required/optional: Optional)
	* Enter your business name, which is your company's name that will be associated with this vehicle compliance waiver.

### 2. **Customer Signature** (Customer Signature, text, required/optional: Optional)
	* Sign your name, which serves as a legally binding agreement to the terms and conditions.

### 3. **Date** (Date, date, required/optional: Optional)
	* Enter the date in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY, depending on your regional date format. This date will confirm the agreement date.
