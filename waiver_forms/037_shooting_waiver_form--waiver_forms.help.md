# Shooting Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Shooting Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to provide their personal details, medical information, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Participant Information** section, which includes details such as name, date of birth, height, weight, phone number, email, and medical information.
2. Provide **Contact Information** for an emergency contact person, including their name, phone number, and relationship to the participant.
3. Complete the **Participant Details** section, which requires you to provide any medical conditions, allergies, medications, or other health information relevant to your participation.
4. Sign the **Participant Agreement** section, selecting the relevant options for your participation.
5. Choose the option for the **Waiver** section, indicating your understanding and acceptance of the waiver terms.
6. Select one of the options for the **Participant Signature** field, indicating your agreement to the waiver terms.
7. Sign the **Parent/Guardian Agreement** section, selecting one of the options to indicate your understanding and acceptance of the waiver terms.
8. Sign the **Participating Signature** section, selecting one of the options to indicate your agreement to the waiver terms.
