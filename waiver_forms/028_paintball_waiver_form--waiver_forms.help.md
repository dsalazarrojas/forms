# Paintball Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Paintball Waiver Form is used to collect information from users participating in paintball-related activities. It helps the organizer to have a better understanding of the users and to ensure their safety during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the User Information section with your name, address, and other required information.
2. Select your contact number or email address in the Contact Number field.
3. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name in the Emergency Contact Name field.
4. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the Emergency Contact Phone Number field.
5. If you have any medical conditions, select them from the Medical Conditions list.
6. Choose the emergency services that you require, such as an ambulance or the police department.
7. Sign the form by selecting "Yes" in the Signature field.
8. Read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I agree" in the Agreement field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This section is for your personal information, including your name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you enter your phone number or email address where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): This is where you enter your emergency contact's name if you have one.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_phone`, number, required/optional): This is where you enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is where you select the address of your emergency contact if you have one.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This is where you enter your date of birth.
* **Emergency Information** (`emergency_info`, note, required/optional): This is a text field where you can enter any additional emergency information.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is where you select any medical conditions you may have.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is where you choose the emergency services you require, such as an ambulance or the police department.
* **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, required/optional): This is where you sign the form by selecting "Yes".
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): This is where you read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I agree".
