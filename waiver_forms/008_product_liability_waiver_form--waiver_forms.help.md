# Product Liability Waaver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Liability Waiver Form is a document that collects information from users to acknowledge and document their use of products.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "User First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "User Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "User Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "User Phone" field.
5. Enter the name of the product you used in the "Product Name" field.
6. Enter the model of the product you used in the "Product Model" field.
7. Enter the date you used the product in the "Date Used" field.
8. Enter the time you used the product in the "Time Used" field.
9. Enter the location where you used the product in the "Location Used" field.
10. Enter a description of what you used the product for in the "Description Used" field.
11. Enter the date you signed the waiver in the "Date Signature" field.
12. Enter the time you signed the waiver in the "Time Signature" field.
13. Check the box next to "User IP Address" to indicate that you acknowledge the waiver.
14. Enter your city in the "User City" field.
15. Enter your state in the "User State" field.
16. Enter your country in the "User Country" field.
17. Enter your zip code in the "User Zip" field.
18. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the waiver was signed in the "Waiver Signed At" field.
19. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you signed the waiver in the "Waiver Signed By" field.
20. Enter the reason you signed the waiver in the "Waiver Signed Reason" field.
21. Enter a description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
22. Enter the condition of the product in the "Product Condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name (User First Name, text, optional):** Enter your first name here.
* **User Last Name (User Last Name, text, optional):** Enter your last name here.
* **User Email (User Email, email, optional):** Enter your email address here. (Example: john.doe@example.com)
* **User Phone (User Phone, text, optional):** Enter your phone number here.
* **Product Name (Product Name, text, optional):** Enter the name of the product you used.
* **Product Model (Product Model, text, optional):** Enter the model of the product you used.
* **Date Used (Date Used, date, optional):** Enter the date you used the product.
* **Time Used (Time Used, time, optional):** Enter the time you used the product.
* **Location Used (Location Used, text, optional):** Enter the location where you used the product.
* **Description Used (Description Used, text, optional):** Enter a description of what you used the product for.
* **Date Signature (Date Signature, date, optional):** Enter the date you signed the waiver.
* **Time Signature (Time Signature, time, optional):** Enter the time you signed the waiver.
* **User IP Address (User IP Address, text, optional):** Check this box to indicate that you acknowledge the waiver.
* **User City (User City, text, optional):** Enter your city here.
* **User State (User State, text, optional):** Enter your state here.
* **User Country (User Country, text, optional):** Enter your country here.
* **User Zip (User Zip, text, optional):** Enter your zip code here.
* **Waiver Signed At (Waiver Signed At, select one, optional):** Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the waiver was signed.
* **Waiver Signed By (Waiver Signed By, select multiple, optional):** Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you signed the waiver.
* **Waiver Signed Reason (Waiver Signed Reason, text, optional):** Enter the reason you signed the waiver.
* **Product Description (Product Description, text, optional):** Enter a description of the product here.
* **Product Condition (Product Condition, text, optional):** Enter the condition of the product here.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you complete all required fields and provide accurate information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
* This form is for internal use only, please do not share it externally.
