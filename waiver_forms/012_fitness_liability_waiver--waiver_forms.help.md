# fitness_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fitness liability waiver form is a critical document that helps protect individuals who participate in fitness-related activities. It's essential to have a clear understanding of the risks involved and the participant's responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the introduction to understand the purpose and scope of the form.
2. Fill out the **Participant Information** section with your details.
3. Answer the **Medical Declaration** questions carefully, ensuring you're aware of your medical history and any potential risks.
4. Provide emergency contact information in case of an incident.
5. If you're under 18, get your parent's **Parent Consent**.
6. Sign the **Signature** to confirm your understanding and agreement.
7. Finally, review the **Waiver Text** and acknowledge your understanding of the waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction**: A brief overview of the form and its purpose.
* **Participant Information**: Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Medical Declaration**: Answer the questions carefully, considering any medical conditions, allergies, or injuries that may impact your participation.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name, phone number, and address of a contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Parent Consent**: If you're under 18, please get your parent or guardian's signature and contact information.
* **Signature**: Sign this form to confirm your understanding and agreement to participate in the activity.
* **Waiver Text**: Read and acknowledge the waiver, understanding the risks involved and the participant's responsibilities.

## Tips
* Please answer the questions honestly and carefully.
* Make sure to review the waiver text carefully before signing.
* If you're unsure about any question or have concerns, ask for clarification before submitting the form.
