# Safety Management Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form template is designed to collect information about an individual or company seeking a waiver for safety management purposes. It gathers personal details, contact information, and waiver-related data for internal use within an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your email address and contact number for communication purposes.
3. If applicable, select the type of waiver you are seeking.
4. Enter the start and end dates of the waiver period.
5. Optionally, provide a reason for the waiver request.
6. Enter your signature for verification.
7. Date the document when completed.
8. Enter the name and title of the person completing the form, if different from yourself.
9. Provide any reviewer comments, if applicable.
10. Enter the reviewer's signature and date for verification.
11. Enter the assessor's name, comments, and signature, if applicable.
12. Date the assessor's completion of the form.
13. Enter the name of the organization or company associated with the waiver request.
14. Provide the address of the organization or company.
15. Enter the city and state where the organization or company is located.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here. This is the first name that you go by.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. This is the surname you were given at birth or the name you prefer to be known by.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company or organization you are associated with.
* **Company Address**: Enter the address of the company or organization.
* **City**: Enter the city where the company or organization is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where the company or organization is located.
* **Waiver Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the type of waiver you are seeking.
* **Waiver Start Date**: Enter the start date of the waiver period.
* **Waiver End Date**: Enter the end date of the waiver period.
* **Reason for Waiver**: Provide any additional information about why you are requesting a waiver, if applicable.
* **Signature**: Sign your name for verification.
* **Date Completed**: Date the document when you have completed it.
* **Completed By**: Enter the name and title of the person completing the form, if different from yourself.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer, if applicable.
* **Reviewer Signature**: Enter the reviewer's signature for verification.
* **Reviewer Date Completed**: Date the reviewer's completion of the form.
* **Assessor Name**: Enter the name of the assessor.
* **Assessor Comments**: Enter any comments from the assessor, if applicable.
* **Assessor Signature**: Enter the assessor's signature for verification.
* **Assessor Date Completed**: Date the assessor's completion of the form.
* **Assessor**: Enter the name of the assessor, if different from the person completing the form.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company or organization associated with the waiver request.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the company or organization.
* **City**: Enter the city where the company or organization is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where the company or organization is located.
