# Escape Room Waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Escape Room Waiver is a form designed to collect necessary information from customers before participating in an escape room activity. It is used to ensure that customers are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the activity, including any potential risks or hazards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with the requested information. You will need to provide your customer information, emergency contact information, and other relevant details as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (Customer Information, text, required/optional): Please enter your name and contact information as it appears on your identification.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person you would like to have contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, select_one, required/optional): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact, such as spouse, parent, or friend.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, text, required/optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation in the activity, please list them here.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, text, required/optional): If you have any medical history that may affect your participation in the activity, please describe it here.
* **Medical Release** (Medical Release, note, required/optional): Please read and agree to the medical release statement, which is provided on the next page.
* **Waiver Signer** (Waiver Signer, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the individuals signing this waiver, such as yourself, a guardian, or an emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Emergency Contact Information** (Emergency Contact Information, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and address.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your emergency contact or activity staff for assistance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please contact our staff for more information.
