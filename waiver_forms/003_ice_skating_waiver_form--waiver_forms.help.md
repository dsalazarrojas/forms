# Ice Skating Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ice Skating Waiver Form is used to collect necessary information for ice skating events, allowing participants to consent to certain risks and agree to terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select a category that best describes the purpose of the form.
2. Enter a brief description of the event or activity.
3. Indicate whether you are an adult or minor participant.
4. Choose whether or not to save the waiver as a PDF.
5. Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
6. Select whether or not to receive response files related to the waiver.
7. Enter your waiver text.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Id**: Enter a unique identifier for the form, if applicable.
* **Category**: Select one or more categories that best describe the purpose of the form. Options are:
	+ Waiver Forms
	+ Entertainment Forms
	+ Event Planner Forms
* **Description**: Enter a brief description of the event or activity.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Enter your agreement to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **E-Signatures**: Indicate whether or not you are an adult or minor participant. Options are:
	+ True (Adult)
	+ False (Minor)
* **Save as PDFs**: Choose whether or not to save the waiver as a PDF. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **No Coding**: Choose whether or not this waiver is for no-coding participants. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Response Files**: Choose whether or not to receive response files related to the waiver. Options are:
	+ True
	+ False
* **Terms and Conditions (Text)**: Enter your agreement to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Waiver (Text)**: Enter your waiver text.

## Tips

* Please make sure to select the correct category for your form.
* Enter a clear and concise description of the event or activity.
* Make sure to carefully review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* Confirm whether or not you want to receive response files related to the waiver.
* Enter your waiver text in a clear and concise manner.
