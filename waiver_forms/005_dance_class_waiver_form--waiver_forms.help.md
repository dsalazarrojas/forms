## Dance Class Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Class Waiver Form is a document that grants permission for a minor child to participate in dance classes at a studio. It is a mandatory form that must be completed by a parent or guardian before the participant can attend classes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Start by filling out the participant information fields, including participant name, guardian name, and guardian contact information.
2.  Provide emergency contact information, including phone number and email address.
3.  If you have any medical information related to the participant, such as allergies or medical conditions, please note it in the "Medical Information" field.
4.  Read and sign the waiver section, which grants permission for the participant to attend classes and authorizes the studio to provide medical attention if necessary.
5.  Select "Studio" as the signer if you are the studio representative, "Parent" if you are the parent, or "Guardian" if you are the guardian.
6.  Finally, enter the date you are signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the participant who will be attending the dance classes.
    Please enter the name of the participant as it appears on their official documents, such as their birth certificate or ID card.
*   **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the parent or guardian who is signing this form.
    Please enter the name of the guardian as it appears on their official documents, such as their ID card or driver's license.
*   **Guardian Contact Information** (`guardian_contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for the contact information of the guardian, including their address, phone number, and email address.
    Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the parent who is signing this form.
    Please enter the name of the parent as it appears on their official documents, such as their ID card or driver's license.
*   **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, optional): This field is for the contact information of the emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email address.
    Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the phone number of the emergency contact.
    Please enter the phone number in the correct international format (e.g., +1 123 456 7890).
*   **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, text, optional): This field is for the email address of the emergency contact.
    Please ensure that the email address is valid and can receive messages from the studio.
*   **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, optional): This field is for any medical information related to the participant, such as allergies or medical conditions.
    Please provide any necessary information that the studio should be aware of when providing services to the participant.
*   **Waiver** (`waiver`, note, optional): This field contains the waiver text that you must read and sign before submitting the form.
    Please read and understand the waiver carefully before signing it.
*   **Studio Information** (`studio_info`, text, optional): This field is for the studio information, which will be used for contact purposes.
    Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
*   **Signature** (`signature`, select_one, optional): This field is for the signature of the person completing the form.
    Please select "Studio" if you are the studio representative, "Parent" if you are the parent, or "Guardian" if you are the guardian.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, optional): This field is for the date the form is being signed.
    Please enter the date in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is not applicable for this form.

## Tips
*   Please complete the form carefully and accurately.
*   Ensure that all fields are filled out completely before submitting the form.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please contact the studio for clarification.
*   The completed form will be kept on file by the studio for future reference.
