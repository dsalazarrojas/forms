# Sports Management Waiver Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form template is designed to collect essential information from participants in a sports management program. The primary goal is to ensure that participants are aware of and comply with the program's rules and regulations, while also providing crucial information for their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Introduction**: Read and understand the information provided on this page.
2. **Participant Information**: Fill in your basic contact details and any other relevant information.
3. **Guardian Information**: If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's contact information.
4. **Medical History and Conditions**: Disclose any medical history, conditions, or allergies that may affect your participation.
5. **Emergency Contact**: Provide the contact information for your emergency contact person.
6. **Medical Release**: Acknowledge that you are aware of and understand the risks associated with the program.
7. **Participation Conduct**: Confirm your understanding of the rules and regulations of the program.
8. **Safety Precautions**: Acknowledge that you will follow safety guidelines and instructions provided by the program staff.
9. **Signatures**: Complete the required signatures sections to confirm your understanding and agreement.
