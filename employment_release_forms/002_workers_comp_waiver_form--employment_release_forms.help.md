# Workers Comp Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to document a worker's waiver of workers' compensation rights. It is intended to be completed by a worker who is waiving their rights to benefits and medical treatment in exchange for compensation from their employer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand its purpose and implications.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
3. Sign and date the form in the designated area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Worker's Name (worker_comp, text, required)**: Enter the name of the worker waiving their rights.
* **Employer's Compensation (employer_compensation, number, required)**: Enter the compensation amount being offered to the worker.
* **Current Employment Status (employment_status, select_one, optional)**: Select 'Active' if you are currently employed, or 'Inactive' if you are not.
* **Employment Type (employment_type, select_multiple, optional)**: Select 'Yes' if you are a union employee, or 'No' if you are not.
* **Injury Date (injury_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the injury, if applicable.
* **Work Location (work_location, text, optional)**: Enter the location where the injury occurred.
* **Injury Description (injury_description, text, optional)**: Describe the injury.
* **Witness Names (witness_names, text, optional)**: Enter the names of any witnesses.
* **Witness Relation (witness_relation, select_one, optional)**: Select 'Yes' if the witness is a relative, or 'No' if not.
* **Witness Address (witness_address, text, optional)**: Enter the address of the witness.
* **Witness Phone (witness_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the witness.
* **Witness Email (witness_email, email, optional)**: Enter the email of the witness.
* **Employer's Name (employer_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the employer.
* **Employer's Address (employer_address, text, optional)**: Enter the address of the employer.
* **Employer's Phone (employer_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the employer.
* **Employer's Email (employer_email, email, optional)**: Enter the email of the employer.
* **Employee's Name (employee_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the employee.
* **Employee's Address (employee_address, text, optional)**: Enter the address of the employee.
* **Employee's Phone (employee_phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the employee.
* **Employee's Email (employee_email, email, optional)**: Enter the email of the employee.
* **Waiver Date (waiver_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the waiver.
* **Waiver Signed By (waiver_signed_by, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the person signing the waiver.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have reviewed and understand the implications of waiving your workers' compensation rights before completing this form.
* If you are unsure about any information, please consult with your employer or HR representative.
* Complete the form accurately and truthfully, as it will be used to document your waiver of rights.
