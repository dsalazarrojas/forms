# unconditional_waiver_release_on.report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Unconditional Waiver Release on Report is a form used to document an employee's understanding and agreement to release their employment claims. It's an essential document in employment settings, ensuring both the employer and employee have a clear understanding of their rights and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Report**: Enter a brief description of the report, including any relevant details about your employment.
2. **Contractor**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3. **Company**: Enter the name of your current or previous employer.
4. **Address**: Enter your company's address (if different from your home address).
5. **Email**: Enter your work email address (if you have one).
6. **Phone**: Enter your work phone number (if you have one).
7. **Job Start Date**: Enter the date you started your job with your current or previous employer.
8. **Job End Date**: Enter the date you ended your employment with your current or previous employer (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Report**: A brief description of the report, including any relevant details about your employment.
  * A simple text field where you can type a short note about the report.
  * This field is **optional**.
- **Contractor**: Your name as it appears on your identification.
  * Ensure you enter your name accurately and consistently with your official identification.
  * This field is **optional**.
- **Company**: The name of your current or previous employer.
  * Enter the name exactly as it appears on your identification or company documents.
  * This field is **optional**.
- **Address**: Your company's address (if different from your home address).
  * Enter the address exactly as it appears on your company documents.
  * This field is **optional**.
- **Email**: Your work email address (if you have one).
  * Enter a valid email address that your employer uses for official communications.
  * This field is **optional**.
- **Phone**: Your work phone number (if you have one).
  * Enter a valid phone number that your employer uses for official communications.
  * This field is **optional**.
- **Job Start Date**: The date you started your job with your current or previous employer.
  * Use the date format: MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
  * This field is **required** if you are filling out the form to end employment; otherwise, it's **optional**.
- **Job End Date**: The date you ended your employment with your current or previous employer (if applicable).
  * Use the date format: MM/DD/YY (e.g., 01/01/2020).
  * This field is **optional** if you are filling out the form to end employment; otherwise, it's **required** if you are filling out the form to end employment.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the necessary details and permissions before filling out the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of your personal details before submitting the form.
- Save a copy of the completed form for your records.
