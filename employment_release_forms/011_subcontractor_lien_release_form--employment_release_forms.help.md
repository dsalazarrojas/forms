# Subcontractor Lien Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release a lien on a property or project, typically in construction or other fields where liens are common. The form is completed by the subcontractor to confirm that they have been paid or settled their outstanding claims and are releasing their lien.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and the terms of the release.
2. Complete the form with your details, including your name, address, and contact information.
3. Select the correct reason for the release from the options provided.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments as necessary.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your release.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Subcontractor Details** (`contractor_details`, text, optional): Please enter your details as the subcontractor, including your name and address.
* **Subcontractor Type** (`subcontractor_details`, select_one, required): Select the type of subcontractor you are (Homeowner or Business).
* **Subcontractor Name** (`subcontractor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the subcontractor.
* **Subcontractor Address** (`subcontractor_address`, text, optional): Enter your address as the subcontractor.
* **Contractor Details** (`contractor_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your contract or agreement.
* **Contractor Name** (`contractor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contractor or party you are releasing the lien for.
* **Contractor Address** (`contractor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the contractor or party you are releasing the lien for.
* **Lien Amount** (`lien_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the lien you are releasing.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the release.
* **Release Time** (`release_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the release.
* **Reason for Release** (`reason_for_release`, select_multiple, required): Select the reason for the release from the options provided (Good payment history, No outstanding claims, Reasonable suspicion, or Other).
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Provide your signature as the subcontractor.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, optional): Date of signature.
* **Signature Time** (`signature_time`, time, optional): Time of signature.
* **Signed By** (`signed_by`, text, optional): Enter your name as the one signing the form.
* **Title Issued To** (`title_issued_to`, text, optional): Enter the title of the party or contractor you are releasing the lien for.
* **Issuer Name** (`issuer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Issuer Title** (`issuer_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Issuer Address** (`issuer_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Issuer Phone** (`issuer_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Issuer Email** (`issuer_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Issuer Fax** (`issuer_fax`, text, optional): Enter the fax number of the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Issuer Note** (`issuer_note`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the issuer or party issuing the lien.
* **Subcontractor Notes** (`subcontractor_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes or comments from the subcontractor.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
