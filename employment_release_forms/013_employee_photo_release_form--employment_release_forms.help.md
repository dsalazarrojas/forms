# Employee Photo Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Photo Release Form is a document used to collect the necessary consent from employees for their photos to be used in company contexts, such as events, marketing materials, and social media.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please ensure that you have the necessary authority and are using the form for legitimate business purposes.
2.  Complete the form accurately and honestly.
3.  If you are an employee, please fill out your section of the form first, and then pass it to your manager for their review and signature.
4.  If you are a manager, please review the employee's section of the form, add your comments and signature, and then indicate your approval or disapproval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Please fill in your name as it appears on your ID or employee card.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Please fill in your job title.
*   **Department** (`department`, select_one, required): Please select your department from the provided options: Administrative, Marketing, Sales, Operations, Support, or Other.
*   **Was the photo taken** (`was_photo_taken`, date, required): Please fill in the date the photo was taken.
*   **Date** (`date`, date, required): Please fill in the date of the current year when you are filling out this form.
*   **Time** (`time`, time, required): Please fill in the time of the current day when you are filling out this form.
*   **Release Approval** (`release_approval`, select_one, required): Please select whether you are granting release approval for your photo to be used in company contexts.
*   **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Please provide any comments or remarks you may have.
*   **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, required): Please sign your name as the employee.
*   **Date of Signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required): Please fill in the date of your signature.
*   **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Please fill in your manager's name.
*   **Manager Title** (`manager_title`, text, required): Please fill in your manager's job title.
*   **Manager Department** (`manager_department`, text, required): Please fill in your manager's department.
*   **Manager Job Title** (`manager_job_title`, text, required): Please fill in your manager's job title.
*   **Employee Photo Consent** (`employee_photo_consent`, select_multiple, required): Please select whether you are granting photo consent.
*   **Employee Comments** (`employee_comments`, note, required): Please provide any remarks or comments you may have.
*   **Manager Comments** (`manager_comments`, note, required): Please provide any comments or remarks you may have as a manager.
*   **Manager Release Approval** (`manager_release_approval`, select_one, required): Please select whether you are granting release approval as a manager.
*   **Manager Signature** (`manager_signature`, text, required): Please sign your name as the manager.
*   **Date of Manager Signature** (`date_of_manager_signature`, date, required): Please fill in the date of your signature as the manager.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the Employee Photo Release Form accurately and effectively.
