# Caregiver Termination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Caregiver Termination Form is used to document the details of a caregiver's termination from employment. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and that the termination process is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure all required fields are completed.
2. Enter the Caregiver's name in the "Caregiver Name" field.
3. Enter the Employee ID number in the "Employee ID" field, if applicable.
4. Select the last day the caregiver worked in the "Last Working Day" field.
5. Select the official date the employment relationship ended in the "Official Termination Date" field.
6. Choose the category that best describes the termination type in the "Type of Termination" field.
7. Identify the primary reason for separation in the "Primary Reason for Separation" field.
8. Provide detailed explanation of the separation reason in the "Provide details about the separation reason" field.
9. Indicate whether a resignation letter was received in the "Resignation letter received?" field.
10. Determine if the employee is eligible for unemployment in the "Is the employee eligible for unemployment?" field.
11. Decide if the employee is eligible for rehire in the "Is the employee eligible for rehire?" field.
12. Confirm that all items have been returned in the "Facility or Client keys returned?" field.
13. Check if the ID badge was returned in the "ID badge returned?" field.
14. Check if electronic devices were returned in the "Laptops, Tablets, or other devices returned?" field.
15. Confirm that a final paycheck was processed and issued in the "Final paycheck processed and issued?" field.
16. Decide if unused PTO was paid out in the "Unused PTO paid out?" field.
17. Enter your name as the terminating manager in the "Terminating Manager Name" field.
18. Confirm that you have completed all termination steps by signing in the "Confirmation of Separation Process" field.
19. Enter the date you completed the form in the "Today's Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Caregiver Name** (`Caregiver Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the caregiver being terminated.
* **Employee ID** (`Employee ID`, text, optional): Enter the employee ID number, if applicable.
* **Last Working Day** (`Last Working Day`, date, required): Enter the last day the caregiver actually worked.
* **Official Termination Date** (`Official Termination Date`, date, required): Enter the official date the employment relationship ended.
* **Type of Termination** (`Type of Termination`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes the termination. Options: Voluntary Resignation, Involuntary Termination, End of Contract, Retirement, Other.
* **Primary Reason for Separation** (`Primary Reason for Separation`, select_one, required): Identify the main cause of termination. Options: Performance Issues, Policy Violation, Better Opportunity, Relocation, Client Death, Personal Reasons, Mutual Agreement.
* **Provide details about the separation reason** (`Provide details about the separation reason`, text, required): Explain the circumstances of the termination in detail.
* **Resignation letter received?** (`Resignation letter received?`, select_one, required): Indicate whether a resignation letter was received. Options: True, False, N/A.
* **Is the employee eligible for unemployment?** (`Is the employee eligible for unemployment?`, select_one, required): Determine if the employee is eligible for unemployment based on company policy and termination reason. Options: True, False, Unsure.
* **Is the employee eligible for rehire?** (`Is the employee eligible for rehire?`, select_one, required): Decide if the employee is eligible for rehire. Options: True, False, Depends.
* **Facility or Client keys returned?** (`Facility or Client keys returned?`, select_one, required): Confirm that all items have been returned.
* **ID badge returned?** (`ID badge returned?`, select_one, required): Check if the ID badge was returned.
* **Laptops, Tablets, or other devices returned?** (`Laptops, Tablets, or other devices returned?`, select_one, required): Check if electronic devices were returned.
* **Final paycheck processed and issued?** (`Final paycheck processed and issued?`, select_one, required): Confirm that a final paycheck was processed and issued.
* **Unused PTO paid out?** (`Unused PTO paid out?`, select_one, required): Decide if unused PTO was paid out.
* **Terminating Manager Name** (`Terminating Manager Name`, text, required): Enter your name as the terminating manager.
* **Confirmation of Separation Process** (`Confirmation of Separation Process`, text, required): Confirm that you have completed all termination steps by signing.
* **Today's Date** (`Today's Date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed the form.

Note: This help guide is designed to assist users in understanding the purpose and usage of the Caregiver Termination Form. It is not intended to be a substitute for company policies or procedures.
