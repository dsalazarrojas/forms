# Employee Laptop Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about your company-provided laptop from you, the employee. This will help us manage the laptop inventory and ensure that the necessary steps are taken for its maintenance and repair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information about your laptop, including the make and model, serial number, purchase date, and return date.
2. Describe the condition of your laptop and list any additional accessories that come with it.
3. Select your department and indicate whether your laptop status is active or inactive.
4. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Laptop Agreement** (id: 1, employee_laptop_agreement_form, text, required): This is the title of the form. Please enter a brief description of your company-provided laptop.
* **Laptop Make and Model** (id: 2, laptop_make_and_model, text, required): Please enter the make and model of your laptop.
* **Laptop Serial Number** (id: 3, laptop_serial_number, text, required): Enter the serial number of your laptop.
* **Laptop Purchase Date** (id: 4, laptop_purchase_date, date, required): Enter the date when you received the laptop.
* **Laptop Return Date** (id: 5, laptop_return_date, date, required): Enter the date when you plan to return the laptop.
* **Laptop Condition** (id: 6, laptop_condition, text, required): Describe the condition of your laptop, including any scratches, dents, or other issues.
* **Employee Name** (id: 7, employee_name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Department** (id: 8, department, select_multiple, required): Select the department you belong to from the list. If you're not sure, you can ask your manager.
* **Laptop Status** (id: 9, laptop_status, select_one, required): Indicate whether your laptop is active or inactive.
* **Laptop Accessories** (id: 10, laptop_accessories, text, optional): List any additional accessories that come with your laptop.
* **Laptop Warranty** (id: 11, laptop_warranty, text, optional): If your laptop has a valid warranty, provide details about it.
* **Laptop Return Policy** (id: 12, laptop_return_policy, text, optional): Enter the policy regarding the return of your laptop.
* **Employee Signature** (id: 13, employee_signature, note, required): Please sign this form to confirm your agreement.
