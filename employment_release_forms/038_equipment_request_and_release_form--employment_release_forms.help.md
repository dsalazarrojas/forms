# Equipment Request And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to request the release and return of company equipment. It helps to document the request and ensure that the equipment is properly released and returned to the correct department.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your request is processed correctly.
- Enter the date you are requesting the equipment (request_date) to ensure that the request is dated correctly.
- Enter the name of the person requesting the equipment (requestor) to ensure that the request can be traced back to the correct person.
- Specify the equipment being requested (equipment_requested) to ensure that the correct equipment is released and returned.
- Enter the reason for the request (reason_for_request) to ensure that the equipment is released for the correct purpose.
- Enter the equipment's current state or condition (equipment_condition) to ensure that any existing issues are noted.
- If the equipment has an asset tag number (asset_tag_number), please enter it to ensure that the correct equipment is released and returned.
- If the equipment is being returned, enter the reason for the return (return_reason) to ensure that the reason is documented.
- Enter any additional notes or comments about the equipment (notes, notes2) to provide context.
- Sign and date your request to ensure that it is properly authorized.
- Enter the department that the equipment is being released back to (department) to ensure that it is returned to the correct department.
- Enter the type of equipment (equipment_type) to provide additional context.
- If the equipment has a serial number (serial_number), please enter it to ensure that the correct equipment is released and returned.
- Enter the reason for releasing the equipment (release_reason) to ensure that the correct reason is documented.
- Enter the person releasing the equipment (release_by) to ensure that the correct person is releasing the equipment.
- Sign and date the release to ensure that it is properly authorized.
