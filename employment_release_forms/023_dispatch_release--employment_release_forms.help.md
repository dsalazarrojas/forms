# Dispatch Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dispatch Release form is used to document employee departures or terminations from the company. It helps to formally notify the employee of the changes in their employment status and to ensure a smooth transition.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2.  Review the form before submitting it to ensure all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dispatch Release Form** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the main title of the form, which should not be filled out by the user.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Employee ID Number** (`employee_id_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's ID number.
* **Employee Address** (`employee_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the employee's work address.
* **Employee Date of Birth** (`employee_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter the employee's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's name.
* **Manager ID Number** (`manager_id_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's ID number.
* **Manager Address** (`manager_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the manager's address.
* **Effective Date** (`effective_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date when the employee's employment status changes.
