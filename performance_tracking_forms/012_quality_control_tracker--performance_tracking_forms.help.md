# Quality Control Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Quality Control Tracker form is designed to collect information on quality control processes, including inspections, outcomes, quality issues, and corrective actions. This form helps to track and monitor the quality control process, ensuring that any issues are addressed promptly and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the Inspection Date field with the date of the inspection.
* Select the Inspection Outcome for the inspection (Pass or Fail).
* List any Quality Issues encountered during the inspection (select multiple options if needed).
* Provide corrective actions taken to address the Quality Issues.
* Attach any relevant Evidence or comments related to the inspection.
* Select the Assigned To field to assign the inspection report to a specific person.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Quality Control Form**: This is the main section of the form where you can input any other relevant information not covered in the other fields.
* **Inspection Date**: The date when the inspection was conducted.
* **Inspection Outcome**: Select the outcome of the inspection (Pass or Fail).
* **Quality Issues**: List any quality issues encountered during the inspection. Select multiple options if needed.
* **Corrective Actions**: Describe any corrective actions taken to address the quality issues.
* **Evidence**: Attach any relevant evidence or supporting documents related to the inspection.
* **Comments**: Provide any additional comments or notes about the inspection.
* **Assigned To**: Select the person responsible for following up on the inspection report.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all relevant fields to ensure accurate tracking and monitoring of quality control processes.
* If multiple quality issues are encountered, select all applicable options for Quality Issues.
* Attach any relevant evidence to support your inspection findings.
* Assign the inspection report to the relevant person to ensure prompt follow-up on any issues.
