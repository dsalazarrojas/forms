# Delivery Driver Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance of delivery drivers. It captures key metrics and details about their work, including start and end times, duration, distance driven, and more. The data will help management assess their performance and make informed decisions.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the driver's name in the "Driver Name" field.
2. Specify the delivery route in the "Delivery Route" field.
3. Enter the shift details in the "Shift" field.
4. Provide the location where the delivery took place.
5. If applicable, enter the start and end times in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
6. Enter the duration of the delivery in the "Duration" field.
7. Record the distance driven during the delivery in the "Distance Driven" field.
8. If needed, provide comments about the delivery in the "Comments" field.
9. Indicate whether or not you approve of the driver's performance in the "Supervisor Signature" field.
10. Enter your name as the supervisor in the "Supervisor Name" field.
11. Enter the date the evaluation took place in the "Date" field.
12. Specify whether or not the driver was subject to any time zone changes during the delivery in the "Time Zone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Driver Name (text)**: Enter the name of the driver being evaluated.
* **Delivery Route (text)**: Specify the route taken during the delivery.
* **Shift (text)**: Enter the shift details, including start and end times.
* **Location (text)**: Provide the location where the delivery took place.
* **Start Time (time)**: Enter the start time of the delivery (optional).
* **End Time (time)**: Enter the end time of the delivery.
* **Duration (number)**: Record the duration of the delivery.
* **Distance Driven (number)**: Enter the distance driven during the delivery.
* **Comments (note)**: Provide any comments or observations about the delivery (optional).
* **Supervisor Signature (select one)**: Indicate whether or not you approve of the driver's performance.
* **Supervisor Name (text)**: Enter your name as the supervisor.
* **Date (date)**: Enter the date the evaluation took place.
* **Time Zone (select multiple)**: Specify whether or not the driver was subject to any time zone changes during the delivery.
