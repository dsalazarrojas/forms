# Personal Accomplishments Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you track your personal achievements and accomplishments. It's meant to be used in conjunction with a manager or coach to review your progress and provide feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. If applicable, provide your Date Completed and Time Completed for the accomplishment.
3. Select all the Accomplishments Achieved that apply to you from the list.
4. Write a brief description of your Accomplishments Details.
5. If relevant, provide the Manager Name and Coach Name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Date Completed** (date): If you've completed the accomplishment on a specific date, enter it here. Leave blank if not applicable.
* **Time Completed** (time): If you've completed the accomplishment at a specific time, enter it here. Leave blank if not applicable.
* **Accomplishments Achieved** (select_multiple):
	+ Option 1: Select this option if this accomplishment applies to you.
	+ Option 2: Select this option if this accomplishment applies to you.
	+ Option 3: Select this option if this accomplishment applies to you.
* **Accomplishments Details** (note): Briefly describe your accomplishment in this field.
* **Manager Name** (text): If you're tracking this form for your manager, enter their name.
* **Coach Name** (text): If you're tracking this form for your coach, enter their name.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Review and update your form regularly to reflect your progress.
* Use the Manager Name and Coach Name fields only if you're tracking this form for someone specific.
