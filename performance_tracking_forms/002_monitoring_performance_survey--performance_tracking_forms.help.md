# Monitoring Performance Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Monitoring Performance Survey is designed to gather information about the performance of our employees. This form is used to collect data on the number of tasks completed, ratings, and feedback provided to employees on their projects. The data collected will be used to monitor and improve performance and provide insights for future development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to access the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
3. Make sure to provide accurate and truthful information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Monitoring Performance Survey Label**: This is the title of the form, which you will see at the top of the page.
* **Employee**: Enter the name of the employee you are reporting on.
* **Employee Project**: Enter the name of the project you are reporting on.
* **Monitoring Period**: Enter the period during which the monitoring was done (e.g., quarter, year).
* **Rating**: Enter a numerical rating (1-10) for the employee's performance.
* **Feedback**: Provide a brief description of the employee's performance.
* **Date**: Enter the date when the monitoring was done.
* **Time**: Enter the time when the monitoring was done.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the employee you are reporting on.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the employee you are reporting on.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If a field is not applicable, please leave it blank.
- If you have any doubts or questions about this form, please contact our support team.
