# Performance Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employee performance tracking, evaluation, and management purposes. It is designed to collect relevant information from employees on a regular basis to ensure accurate data for performance assessments and evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the employee's information (employee ID, name, job title, department, and location) on the first page.
2. Select the evaluation type (Manager, Peer, or Self) for accurate tracking.
3. Choose a date for the evaluation (evaluation date).
4. Provide a numerical value for the evaluation score.
5. Offer feedback about the performance.
6. Select a rating (numerical value) to reflect performance.
7. Enter a comment about the performance evaluation.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Information
* **Employee ID** (text, required): Enter the employee's unique ID number.
* **Name** (text, required): Enter the employee's full name.
* **Job Title** (text, required): Enter the employee's job title.
* **Department** (text, required): Enter the employee's department name.
* **Location** (text, required): Enter the employee's location name.

### Performance Evaluation
* **Evaluation Date** (date, required): Select the date for the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Score** (number, required): Choose a numerical value to reflect performance.
* **Feedback** (text, optional): Provide comments or suggestions for improvement.
* **Rating** (number, required): Select a numerical value for performance evaluation.
* **Evaluation Comment** (text, optional): Enter comments about the performance evaluation.

### Evaluation Type
* **Evaluation Type** (select_one, required): Select Manager, Peer, or Self for accurate tracking.

### Submission
* **Submit** (text, required): Click to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Ensure that the form is filled out once a period (quarter or month) for accurate performance tracking.
* Avoid duplication of entries to prevent confusion and ensure data accuracy.
* Select relevant feedback and evaluation comments for accurate performance assessments.
