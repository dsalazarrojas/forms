# Board Meeting Performance Review - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather feedback from reviewers on the performance of a board meeting. It helps identify strengths and weaknesses of the meeting and guides future meetings to improve their effectiveness.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the "Your Name" field and enter your name, which will be used to identify your review.
2. Select your position on the board in the "Your Position on the Board" field.
3. Enter the date of the meeting being reviewed in the "Meeting Date" field.
4. Rate the meeting attendance, agenda clarity, engagement, time management, discussion quality, decision-making, action items, leadership effectiveness, content relevance, and overall satisfaction using the rating scales provided.
5. In the "Strengths" field, list positive aspects of the meeting that stood out to you.
6. In the "Areas for Improvement" field, suggest changes or improvements for future meetings.
7. Finally, submit your review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (`reviewer_name`, text): Please enter your name as a reviewer.
* **Your Position on the Board** (`position_title`, text): Select your position on the board.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date): Enter the date of the board meeting being reviewed.
* **Meeting Attendance Rating** (`attendance_score`, select_one): Rate the meeting attendance on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Agenda Clarity** (`agenda_clarity`, select_one): Rate the meeting's agenda on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Overall Board Engagement** (`engagement_level`, select_one): Rate the overall engagement of board members on a scale of Highly Engaged, Mostly Engaged, Somewhat Engaged, or Disengaged.
* **Time Management** (`time_management`, select_one): Rate the meeting's time management on a scale of Stayed on time, Ran slightly over, Ran significantly over, or Finished early.
* **Discussion Quality** (`discussion_quality`, select_one): Rate the quality of discussions on a scale of Excellent - Very productive, Good - Mostly productive, Fair - Some useful discussion, or Poor - Limited discussion.
* **Decision Making** (`decision_making`, select_one): Rate the effectiveness of decision-making on a scale of Yes - Clear decisions made, Mostly - Most decisions made, Partially - Some decisions deferred, or No - No clear decisions.
* **Action Items Clarity** (`action_items_clarity`, select_one): Rate the clarity of action items on a scale of Yes - Very clear, Mostly - Generally clear, Somewhat - Could be clearer, or No - Unclear.
* **Leadership Effectiveness** (`leadership_effectiveness`, select_one): Rate the meeting leadership on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Needs Improvement.
* **Content Relevance** (`content_relevance`, select_one): Rate the relevance of the meeting content on a scale of Highly relevant, Mostly relevant, Somewhat relevant, or Not relevant.
* **Strengths** (`strengths`, text): List positive aspects of the meeting that stood out to you.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`improvements`, text): Suggest changes or improvements for future meetings.
* **Communication and Transparency** (`communication_rating`, select_one): Rate the meeting communication and transparency on a scale of Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Resources Adequacy** (`resources_adequate`, select_one): Rate the adequacy of meeting resources on a scale of 'True', Mostly, Partially, or 'False'.
* **Suggestions for Next Meeting** (`next_meeting_suggestions`, text): Suggest topics or formats you would like to see in the next meeting.
* **Overall Meeting Satisfaction** (`overall_satisfaction`, select_one): Rate your overall satisfaction with the meeting on a scale of Very Satisfied, Satisfied, Neutral, or Dissatisfied.
