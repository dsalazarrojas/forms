# performance_review_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for communication between employees and their reviewers during performance reviews. It helps in capturing and recording the review details, allowing for a more structured and organized process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category for the performance review (e.g., Performance Tracking Forms).
2. Provide a brief description of the review.
3. Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
4. Enter the name of the reviewer.
5. Choose the date the review was conducted.
6. Select the specific performance areas that the employee needs to improve or excel in (e.g., Communication, Teamwork, Leadership).
7. Choose the review outcome based on the employee's performance (e.g., Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Satisfactory, Excellent).
8. Add any comments or feedback for the employee.
9. Specify next steps for the employee to improve or develop their skills.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Performance Tracking Forms, Other): This field allows you to categorize the performance review, helping to group similar reviews together.
* **Description**: Briefly describe the purpose or context of the review.
* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name**: Enter the name of the person conducting the review.
* **Date Reviewed**: Select the date the performance review was conducted.
* **Performance Areas**: Select multiple performance areas where the employee needs to focus on improvement or excel (e.g., Communication, Teamwork, Leadership).
* **Review Outcome**: Choose the outcome of the review based on the employee's performance (e.g., Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Satisfactory, Excellent).
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback for the employee to consider.
* **Next Steps**: Specify the next steps the employee should take to improve or develop their skills.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill in all relevant fields for a complete review process.
- Review the completed form before submitting to ensure accuracy and clarity.
- Customize the form according to your company's specific performance review process.
