# Development Plan Acknowledgment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and acknowledge details about employee development plans. It is intended for internal use within an organization to track progress and status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your employee name in the "Employee Name" field.
* Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
* Provide your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
* Enter any relevant development plan details in the "Development Plan Details" field.
* Select the agreement date from the calendar when "Agreement Date" field is clicked.
* Choose the expected completion date from the calendar when "Expected Completion Date" field is clicked.
* Select the actual completion date from the calendar when "Actual Completion Date" field is clicked.
* Choose the review cycle (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Half-Yearly, Annually) in the "Review Cycle" field.
* Select the current status (e.g., On Track, Off Track, On Hold, Completed) in the "Status" field.
* Add any comments or notes in the "Comments" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (Employee Name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter your job or position within the organization.
* **Manager Name (Manager Name)**: Enter the name of your direct supervisor or manager.
* **Development Plan Details (Development Plan Details)**: Enter any relevant details about your development plan, such as goals, objectives, or tasks.
* **Agreement Date (Agreement Date)**: Select the date when your development plan was agreed upon.
* **Expected Completion Date (Expected Completion Date)**: Choose the date by which you expect to complete the development plan tasks or objectives.
* **Actual Completion Date (Actual Completion Date)**: Select the date when you have actually completed the development plan tasks or objectives.
* **Review Cycle (Review Cycle)**: Choose how often you want to be reviewed or checked on for progress (e.g., Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
* **Status (Status)**: Select the current status of your development plan (e.g., On Track, Off Track, On Hold, Completed).
* **Comments (Comments)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your development plan.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate tracking and monitoring of your development progress.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact your supervisor or HR department.
