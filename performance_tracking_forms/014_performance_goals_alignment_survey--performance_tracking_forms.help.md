# Performance Goals Alignment Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Performance Goals Alignment Survey form is used to collect feedback and performance data from employees regarding their alignment with organizational objectives. This form is intended to be used in a performance review or evaluation process to identify areas where the employee's performance needs to be adjusted to better align with the organization's goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please answer all questions honestly and thoroughly to provide a comprehensive overview of your performance and goals.
2. Select the relevant options for each question to indicate your agreement or disagreement with the statement.
3. Provide clear and concise comments for each question to explain your answers.
4. Make sure to include your name and email address so that we can get back to you with any questions or concerns.
