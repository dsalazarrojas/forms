# Milestone Sharing Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Milestone Sharing Survey form is designed to track and share milestones achieved by teams in the organization. It collects information about milestones completed by teams across various programs, projects, and departments to analyze and report on the progress and performance of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Team Wins" field with the details of the milestone achieved.
2. Select the relevant programs, projects, and departments that the milestone impacts.
3. Enter the date and time of the milestone completion.
4. Provide any additional details about the milestone.
5. Select the impact of the milestone on the program, project, and department.
6. List the recognized employees who contributed to the milestone.
7. Enter the count of recognized employees.
8. Add any notes about the recognized employees.
9. Enter the email and phone number of the recognized employees (if applicable).
10. Click "Submit" to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Wins**: This field is where you enter the details of the milestone achieved. Please be as specific as possible.
- **Program**: Select the program that the milestone impacts. Choose all applicable programs.
- **Project**: Select the project that the milestone impacts. Choose all applicable projects.
- **Department**: Select the department that the milestone impacts. Choose all applicable departments.
- **Date**: Enter the date of the milestone completion.
- **Time**: Enter the time of the milestone completion.
- **Details**: Add any additional details about the milestone.
- **Program Impact**: Select all applicable programs that are affected by the milestone.
- **Project Impact**: Select all applicable projects that are affected by the milestone.
- **Department Impact**: Select all applicable departments affected by the milestone.
- **Recognized Employees**: List all employees who contributed to the milestone.
- **Recognized Employees Count**: Enter the count of recognized employees.
- **Recognized Employees Note**: Add any notes about the recognized employees.
- **Recognized Employees Email**: Enter the email of the recognized employees (if applicable).
- **Recognized Employees Phone**: Enter the phone number of the recognized employees (if applicable).
- **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form.
