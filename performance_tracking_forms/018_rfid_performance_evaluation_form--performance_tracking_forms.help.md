# rfid-performance-evaluation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect data on the performance of RFID readers in the test environment. It is intended to document the results of specific RFID reader tests and highlight any issues or recommendations for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form after completing an RFID reader test.
2. Enter the name of the RFID reader being tested in the "RFID Test" field.
3. Select the issues experienced with the RFID reader in the "Issues" field.
4. Enter any recommendations for improving the performance of the RFID reader in the "Recommendations" field.
5. Select the frequency of data collection for the RFID reader in the "Data Collection" field.
6. Enter a performance metric (e.g. accuracy, range, etc.) and its corresponding value in the "Performance Metric" field.
7. Enter the date and time of the test in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
8. Enter the RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator) value for the RFID reader in the "RSSI" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RFID Test**: Enter the name of the RFID reader being tested.
* **Issues**: Select all issues encountered during the test from the dropdown list. Examples of issues include:
	+ RFID Reader
	+ Antenna
	+ Cable
* **Recommendations**: Enter any suggestions for improving the performance of the RFID reader.
* **Data Collection**: Select the frequency of data collection for the RFID reader. Options include:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
* **Performance Metric**: Enter a performance metric (e.g. accuracy, range, etc.) and its corresponding value.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the test.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the test.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the test.
* **RSSI**: Enter the RSSI value for the RFID reader.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form as soon as possible after completing the test.
* Be as specific as possible when entering issues and recommendations.
* Use the "Data Collection" field to select the frequency of data collection for the RFID reader.
* Use the "Performance Metric" field to enter a performance metric and its corresponding value.
* Enter accurate dates and times for the test.
