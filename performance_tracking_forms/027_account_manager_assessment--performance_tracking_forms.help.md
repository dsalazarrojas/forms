# Account Manager Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Account Manager Assessment form is used to evaluate the performance of Account Managers in a specific period, covering various aspects of their job.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the Assessment Period for which you are evaluating the Account Manager's performance.
2. Complete each field in the form according to the provided explanations below.
3. Provide specific ratings and comments for each assessed parameter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Account Manager Name** (`employee_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the Account Manager being evaluated.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, **required**): Enter the employee ID of the Account Manager being evaluated.
* **Department or Team** (`department`, text, **required**): Enter the department or team of the Account Manager being evaluated.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name as the reviewer or assessor.
* **Assessment Period** (`review_period`, select_one, **required**): Select the assessment period for which you are evaluating the Account Manager's performance. Options: Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4, Annual, Probationary.
* **Assessment Date** (`review_date`, date, **required**): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Client Relationship Management** (`client_relationship`, select_one, **required**): Select the level of client relationship management for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Revenue Generation and Growth** (`revenue_generation`, select_one, **required**): Select the level of revenue generation and growth for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Communication Skills** (`communication_skills`, select_one, **required**): Select the level of communication skills for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Problem Solving and Issue Resolution** (`problem_solving`, select_one, **required**): Select the level of problem solving and issue resolution for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Strategic Thinking and Planning** (`strategic_thinking`, select_one, **required**): Select the level of strategic thinking and planning for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Teamwork and Collaboration** (`teamwork`, select_one, **required**): Select the level of teamwork and collaboration for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Number of Active Accounts Managed** (`accounts_managed`, number, **required**): Enter the number of active accounts managed by the Account Manager.
* **Client Retention Rate (%)** (`retention_rate`, number, **required**): Enter the client retention rate for the Account Manager as a percentage.
* **Was Revenue Target Met** (`revenue_target_met`, select_one, **required**): Select whether the revenue target was met by the Account Manager. Options: Exceeded target, Met target, Below target, Significantly below target.
* **Key Achievements This Period** (`key_achievements`, text, **required**): Enter the key achievements of the Account Manager for this period.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, **required**): Enter the areas for improvement for the Account Manager.
* **Goals for Next Period** (`development_goals`, text, **required**): Enter the goals for the Account Manager for the next period.
* **Overall Performance Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, **required**): Select the overall performance rating for the Account Manager. Options: Exceptional, Exceeds expectations, Meets expectations, Needs improvement, Unsatisfactory.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional comments or feedback for the Account Manager.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you select the correct assessment period and date.
* Be specific and concrete when providing ratings and comments for each parameter.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure that all required fields are completed.
* Remember that this form is designed to help you, as the reviewer or assessor, to provide a structured evaluation of the Account Manager's performance.
* Take your time and provide thoughtful and detailed ratings and comments for each parameter.
