# Sports Scoring Record - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Scoring Record form is used to collect and record information about sports games, including scores, game details, and referee information. This form helps to provide a comprehensive record of past games, which can be valuable for historical analysis, statistics, and other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the sport name from the provided options.
2. Enter the game date and time.
3. Fill out the referee details, including name and location.
4. Enter the scores for both teams.
5. List the players for each team.
6. Add any additional game details.
7. Select the season in which the game took place.
8. Add any notes or comments about the game.
9. Enter the season start and end dates (if applicable).
10. Record the game number.
11. (Optional) Provide the referee's location and weather conditions.
12. (Optional) Enter additional referee details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sport Name**: The name of the sport being played.
* **Game Date**: The date of the game.
* **Game Time**: The time of the game.
* **Referee Name**: The name of the referee.
* **Referee Location**: The location of the referee (if applicable).
* **Team 1 Score**: The score of Team 1.
* **Team 2 Score**: The score of Team 2.
* **Team 1 Players**: A list of players for Team 1.
* **Team 2 Players**: A list of players for Team 2.
* **Game Details**: Additional details about the game.
* **Season**: The season in which the game took place.
* **Notes**: Any additional comments or notes about the game.
* **Season Start Date**: The start date of the season (if applicable).
* **Season End Date**: The end date of the season (if applicable).
* **Game Number**: The number of the game.
* **Referee 1**: A field for additional referee details (if applicable).
* **Referee 1 Name**: A field for the name of Referee 1 (if applicable).
* **Referee 2**: A field for additional referee details (if applicable).
* **Referee 2 Name**: A field for the name of Referee 2 (if applicable).
* **League ID**: A field for the league ID (if applicable).
* **Team 1 Score Difference**: The difference between Team 1 and Team 2 scores (if applicable).
* **Team 2 Score Difference**: The difference between Team 2 and Team 1 scores (if applicable).
* **Weather Conditions**: The conditions during the game (if applicable).

## Tips
