# golf_performance_training_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track the details of golf performance training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the details of the program, including the program name or title.
2. Select the coach who will be leading the training program.
3. Enter the player's name who will be participating in the training.
4. Choose the date and time of the training session.
5. Enter the total number of players and coaches participating in the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Details** (program_details, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the details of the program, including its name or title.
* **Coach Name** (coach_name, select_one, optional)
	+ This field allows you to select the coach who will be leading the training program from a list of options.
* **Player Name** (player_name, text, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the player's name who will be participating in the training.
* **Training Date** (training_date, date, optional)
	+ This field is used to choose the date of the training session.
* **Training Time** (training_time, time, optional)
	+ This field is used to select the time of the training session.
* **Program Duration** (program_duration, number, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the duration of the training program in units suitable for your context (e.g., weeks, days, etc.).
* **Total Players** (total_players, number, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the total number of players participating in the training.
* **Total Coaches** (total_coaches, number, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the total number of coaches participating in the training.
* **Training Hours** (training_hours, number, optional)
	+ This field is used to enter the total number of hours spent in training.
