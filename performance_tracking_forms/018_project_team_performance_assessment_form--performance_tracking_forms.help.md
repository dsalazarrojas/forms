# Project Team Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Project Team Performance Assessment Form is designed to help teams evaluate and improve their performance. It covers various aspects of team functioning and identifies areas for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form's sections and questions carefully.
2. Complete all sections with as much detail as possible.
3. Please provide specific examples or anecdotes to support your answers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What are the team's strengths? (`team_strengths_form`, text, required)**: Describe the team's strengths, such as effective communication, collaboration, or problem-solving skills.
* **What are the team's weaknesses? (`team_weaknesses_form`, text, required)**: Identify areas where the team may struggle, such as conflict resolution, time management, or decision-making processes.
* **How does the team communicate and collaborate? (`team_communication_form`, text, required)**: Discuss how team members interact with each other, share ideas, and work together to achieve goals.
* **What are the team's goals and objectives? (`team_goals_form`, text, required)**: Specify the team's short-term and long-term goals, including any relevant metrics or deadlines.
* **What are the team's KPIs? (`team_kpis_form`, text, required)**: Describe how the team tracks and measures progress towards its goals, including any key performance indicators (KPIs).
* **How does the team measure progress and track performance? (`team_progress_form`, text, required)**: Explain how the team monitors and evaluates its performance, including any relevant tools or systems.
* **What are some areas for improvement for the team? (`team_improvement_areas_form`, text, required)**: Identify areas where the team can improve, such as processes, communication, or collaboration.
* **What kind of support does the team need to achieve its objectives? (`team_support_form`, text, required)**: Specify any resources, training, or guidance required to support the team's growth and development.
* **What is the team's current status in relation to its goals and objectives? (`team_status_form`, text, required)**: Provide an update on the team's current status, including any successes or challenges faced.
* **How does the team manage conflicts and disagreements? (`team_conflict_form`, text, required)**: Describe how the team handles conflicts and disagreements, including any relevant processes or procedures.
* **What are some suggestions for future development and growth? (`team_growth_form`, text, required)**: Suggest ideas or recommendations for the team's future development and growth.
* **Additional comments (`additional_comments_form`, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or insights not captured in the previous sections.

Please note that all fields are required unless specified as optional. If you are unsure about a particular field, please consult your supervisor or team lead for guidance.
