# Fantasy League Player Stats Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fantasy League Player Stats Form is used to track and report performance metrics for a fantasy league. This form is designed to collect relevant information about a specific game or match, including the game details, player statistics, and fantasy league points earned.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the game details from the dropdown menu under "Game Info".
2. Enter the player's statistics under "Player Stats".
3. Select the fantasy league information from the dropdown menu under "Fantasy League Info" and "Fantasy League Info 2".
4. Enter the game results and player performance under "Game Results" and "Player Performance" respectively.
5. Add any additional comments under "Additional Comments" and "Additional Comments 2" if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Game Info (Select Multiple)**: Select the relevant game information, such as Team, Opponent, Location, Date, and Time.
* **Player Stats (Number)**: Enter the player's statistics for the game, as a numerical value.
* **Fantasy League Info (Select One)**: Select the fantasy league type, such as Name, Commissioner, Manager, or Stat Keeper.
* **Game Results (Date)**: Enter the game results, such as the date the game was played.
* **Player Performance (Number)**: Enter the player's performance for the game, as a numerical value.
* **Additional Comments (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the game or player.
* **Team Score (Number)**: Enter the team's score for the game, as a numerical value.
* **Fantasy League Points (Select Multiple)**: Select the fantasy league points earned by the player, such as Points, Points Differential, Winning Streak, or Losses.
* **Fantasy League Info 2 (Select One)**: Select the fantasy league type, such as Name, Commissioner, Manager, or Stat Keeper.
* **Additional Comments 2 (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the game or player.
* **Player Performance 2 (Number)**: Enter the player's performance for the game, as a numerical value.
* **Team Score 2 (Number)**: Enter the team's score for the game, as a numerical value.
