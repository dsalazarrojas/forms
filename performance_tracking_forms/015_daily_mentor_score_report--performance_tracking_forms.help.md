# Daily Mentor Score Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Mentor Score Report form is a tool used to track and record key performance data for mentorship sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Daily Mentor Score Report form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the unique **Session ID** (a unique identifier for the session).
2. Select the **Date** of the session.
3. Record your **Mentor Notes** regarding the session, if any.
4. Write down your **Mentee Notes** to share any important information about your mentee.
5. Enter the **Score**, a numeric value indicating your assessment of the session's outcome (e.g., 1-10).
6. Indicate your plan for the **Next Action** (e.g., "Yes" for a specific action, "No" for no action).
7. Select any **Next Steps** you want to take or suggest to the mentee (e.g., "Yes" for taking a specific action, "No" for no action).
8. Choose any relevant **Category(ies)** for the session (e.g., "Yes" for a specific category, "No" for no category).
9. Select any relevant **Tags** for the session (e.g., "Yes" for a specific tag, "No" for no tag).
10. Attach any relevant **Files** (e.g., notes, reports, or resources).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session ID (1)**: A unique identifier for the session to track progress and maintain accuracy.
* **Date (2)**: The date of the session, ensuring correct date tracking.
* **Mentor Notes (3)**: A field for recording any important notes or comments from the mentor.
* **Mentee Notes (4)**: A field for sharing any important information about the mentee, such as progress, concerns, or achievements.
* **Score (5)**: A numeric value to assess the session's outcome, where 1-10 indicates the level of success or progress made.
* **Next Action (6)**: An indicator for the next step or action to take, select "Yes" to initiate action, "No" otherwise.
* **Next Steps (7)**: A field to select additional actions or steps to be taken in the future, select "Yes" for actions that will be taken, "No" for no actions.
* **Category (8)**: A field to select relevant categories for the session, e.g., "Yes" for specific categories, "No" for no category.
* **Tags (9)**: A field to select relevant tags for the session, e.g., "Yes" for specific tags, "No" for no tags.
* **Files (10)**: A field to attach relevant documents, reports, or resources related to the session.
