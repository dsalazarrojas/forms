# Performance Bonus Reconsideration Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for employees to submit a request for reconsideration of their performance bonuses. It's essential to ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully to facilitate a thorough review process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct response to each question, taking into account the specific details of your situation:
	* If you're requesting a reconsideration, select 'Yes' for "Performance Bonus Request".
	* Select the current status of your case, which can be either 'Active' or 'Inactive', for "Reconsideration Case Status".
	* List all performance bonuses that are included in your reconsideration, if any, for "Performance Bonuses Included".
	* Provide a clear explanation for why you're requesting a reconsideration, for "Reason For Reconsideration".
	* Leave "Manager Signature" and "Date of Reconsideration" fields blank as they are not required.
	* Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport for "Employee Name".
	* Enter your manager's name as it appears on their ID or passport for "Manager Name".
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully, as this information will be used for the review process.
3. Submit the form to initiate the reconsideration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Bonus Request** (`performance_bonus_request`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you're requesting a reconsideration of your performance bonuses.
* **Reconsideration Case Status** (`reconsideration_case_status`, select_one, required): Select the current status of your case, which can be either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Performance Bonuses Included** (`performance_bonuses_included`, select_multiple, required): List all performance bonuses that are included in your reconsideration, if any.
* **Reason For Reconsideration** (`reason_for_reconsideration`, text, required): Provide a clear explanation for why you're requesting a reconsideration.
* **Date of Reconsideration** (`date_of_reconsideration`, date, required): Enter the date on which you're submitting this request for reconsideration.
* **Employee Signature** (`employee_signature`, text, optional): This field is optional and should be left blank unless you're required to fill it out as part of your company's policy.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
