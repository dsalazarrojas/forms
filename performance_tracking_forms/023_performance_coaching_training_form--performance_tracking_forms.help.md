# Performance Coaching Training Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Performance Coaching Training Form is a tool to track the progress and goals of participants in a performance coaching training program. This form helps to gather information about participants' current goals and whether they have achieved specific objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Answer the questions about your current goals and whether you have achieved them. Choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each goal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info** (Text Field): This is where you can provide any additional information about yourself, such as your name or any other relevant details. This field is not required.
* **goals** (Select One Field): This question asks about your current goals. Please choose 'Yes' if you have achieved your current goals, and 'No' if you haven't.
* **participant_info_1** (Text Field): This field is similar to **participant_info**, where you can provide any additional information about yourself. This field is not required.
* **goals_1** (Select One Field): This question is similar to **goals**, asking about whether you have achieved a specific goal. Please choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each question.

## Tips
Remember to fill in each field accurately and honestly, and don't hesitate to ask if you need help with anything.
