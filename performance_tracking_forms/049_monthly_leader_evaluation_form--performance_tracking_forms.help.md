# Monthly Leader Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate a leader's performance on a monthly basis. It is meant to identify strengths and areas for improvement, providing a structured way to track and analyze performance over time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the leader's name in the **Leader Name** field.
2. Select the **Performance Metrics** field and choose one of the three options: **Excellent**, **Good**, or **Needs Improvement**.
3. In the **Areas for Improvement** field, provide specific feedback on what the leader can improve on.
4. Enter a **Leader Comment** if you want to provide additional feedback or context.
5. Choose a **Date of Evaluation** and enter the relevant date.
6. Select the **Assigned Tool**, **Assigned To**, and **Assigned By** fields as they pertain to the evaluation.
7. Enter the leader's **Email** and **Phone** (if applicable).
8. Finally, ensure that you are saving your changes and not duplicating efforts.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Leader Name** (Leader Name, text, required): Enter the name of the leader being evaluated.
* **Performance Metrics** (Performance Metrics, select_one, required): Evaluate the leader's performance as **Excellent**, **Good**, or **Needs Improvement**.
* **Areas for Improvement** (Areas for Improvement, text, required): Provide specific feedback on the leader's areas for improvement.
* **Leader Comment** (Leader Comment, text, optional): Enter any additional feedback or context you'd like to provide.
* **Date of Evaluation** (Date of Evaluation, date, required): Choose the date of the evaluation.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, required): Enter the tool assigned to the leader.
* **Assigned To** (Assigned To, text, required): Enter the person or team assigned to the leader.
* **Date** (Date, date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the evaluation (if applicable).
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter the leader's email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter the leader's phone number (if applicable).
* **Assigned By** (Assigned By, text, required): Enter the name of the person assigning the evaluation.
