# รับประทับ Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The รับประทับ Evaluation Form is designed to collect information about your performance during a race or event. Please use this form to provide accurate and detailed feedback about your performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

1. Select the race or event from the dropdown list (not shown in this example).
2. Enter the date and time of the event.
3. Enter the distance you covered during the event.
4. Describe the weather conditions during the event.
5. Enter your performance metrics, such as your speed, pace, or other relevant metrics.
6. Outline your training plan for the event, including any specific strategies or preparation you did before the event.
7. Rate your performance on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "poor" and 10 being "excellent".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **race_name** (`race_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the race or event you participated in.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date and time of the event in the format DD/MM/YYYY HH:MM.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the start and finish time of the event in the format HH:MM.
* **distance** (`distance`, number, optional): Enter the distance you covered during the event in meters, kilometers, or miles.
* **weather_conditions** (`weather_conditions`, text, optional): Describe the weather conditions during the event, such as sunshine, rain, or other weather-related factors.
* **performance_metrics** (`performance_metrics`, text, optional): Enter your performance metrics, such as your speed, pace, or other relevant metrics.
* **training_plan** (`training_plan`, note, optional): Outline your training plan for the event, including any specific strategies or preparation you did before the event.
* **athlete_rating** (`athlete_rating`, number, optional): Rate your performance on a scale of 1-10, with 1 being "poor" and 10 being "excellent".

## Tips
* Be as detailed and accurate as possible when filling out this form.
* Use this form to provide feedback and insights that can help you improve your performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your coach or designated representative.
