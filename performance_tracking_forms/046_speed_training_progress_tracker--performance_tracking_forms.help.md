# Speed Training Progress Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to track the progress of athletes during speed training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the "Step-by-Step Instructions" section above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speed Session Id** (`speed_session_id`, number, required): Enter a unique identifier for each speed training session.
* **Athlete Id** (`athlete_id`, number, required): Enter the athlete's unique identifier.
* **Coach Id** (`coach_id`, number, required): Enter the coach's unique identifier.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the speed training session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the speed training session.
* **Distance Run** (`distance_run`, number, required): Enter the distance covered during the session.
* **Pace** (`pace`, number, required): Enter the athlete's pace during the session, measured in units of speed.
* **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the session.
* **Speed Training Level** (`speed_training_level`, select one, required): Select the level of difficulty or intensity of the training session, on a scale from 1 to 5.
* **Session Type** (`session_type`, select multiple, required): Select the type of training session, which can be Strength, Endurance, Interval, or Tempo.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field to ensure that you are able to track the athlete's progress correctly.
* If you are unsure about the session type, refer to the coach's guidelines or ask for clarification.
* Use the free-text fields (Session Notes and Notes) to record any additional information that may be helpful for the coach to know.
