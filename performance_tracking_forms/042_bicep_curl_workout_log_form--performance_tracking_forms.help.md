# Bicep Curl Workout Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bicep Curl Workout Log Form is designed to collect data on bicep curl exercises, providing insight into athlete performance, progress, and areas for improvement. This form helps fitness professionals provide personalized guidance and support to their clients, while athletes can monitor their own development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Athlete or Client Name: Provide the full name of the athlete or client, as this will be used for record-keeping purposes.
2. Enter the Athlete ID or Member Number (if applicable): If the athlete has a member number or ID, enter it here for easy identification.
3. Enter the Email Address (optional): Provide the athlete's email address for communication and record-keeping purposes.
4. Select the Workout Date: Choose the date when the workout session took place.
5. Select the Workout Time (optional): If applicable, choose the time of the workout session.
6. Select the Trainer or Coach Name (if supervised): If the athlete had a trainer or coach during the session, enter their name here.
7. Choose the Type of Bicep Curl Exercise: Select the specific type of bicep curl exercise performed.
8. Enter the Weight Used and Unit: Enter the weight used for the exercise and select the unit (Pounds or Kilograms).
9. Complete Set Data (1-3):
	* Enter the Number of Repetitions for each set.
	* Select the Difficulty Level for each set, using the provided scale.
10. Enter Additional Set Data (if more than 3 sets were completed):
	* Provide the number of repetitions completed.
	* Select the difficulty level for the extra sets.
11. Assess Overall Form Quality:
	* Choose the overall form quality using the provided scale.
12. Enter Overall Form Notes or Observations (if necessary):
	* Describe any specific form issues or concerns.
13. Enter Rest Time Between Sets (optional):
	* Provide the time taken between sets.
14. Assess Energy Level During Workout:
	* Choose the energy level using the provided scale.
15. Report Pre-Workout Muscle Soreness (optional):
	* Select the level of soreness from previous workouts.
16. Report Any Pain or Discomfort During Set (optional):
	* Describe any joint or muscle pain experienced during the set.
17. Enter Additional Progress Notes or Comments:
	* Share any other observations or comments.
