# SIPOC Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SIPOC Form is designed to capture relevant data for tracking supplier performance, customer interactions, and process information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant suppliers, inputs, processes, outputs, customers, and other details from the respective fields.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields with accurate and relevant information.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Suppliers Inputs 
* **Suppliers** (`Suppliers`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one or multiple suppliers from the options provided. This field is intended to capture the names of the suppliers involved in the process.
### Suppliers 
* **Supplier** (`Supplier`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one supplier from the options provided. This field is intended to identify a single supplier from the available options.
### Inputs 
* **Inputs** (`Inputs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple inputs from the options provided. This field is intended to capture the inputs used in the process.
### Process 
* **Process** (`Process`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in the process details. This field is intended to capture the process or steps taken.
### Outputs 
* **Outputs** (`Outputs`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or multiple outputs from the options provided. This field is intended to capture the outputs produced in the process.
### Customers 
* **Customers** (`Customers`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one customer from the options provided. This field is intended to identify a single customer involved in the process.
### Customers 
* **Customers** (`Customers`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select one customer from the options provided. (Duplicate field from above, please ignore)
### Notes 
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, required/optional): Fill in any additional notes related to the process. This field is intended to capture any extra information.
### Email 
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address. This field is intended to capture a contact email address for communication.
### Phone 
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Fill in a phone number. This field is intended to capture a contact phone number for communication.
