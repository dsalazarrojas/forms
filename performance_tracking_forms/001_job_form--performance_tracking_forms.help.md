# Job Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data related to jobs and tasks performed by staff members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the job title and staff name fields with the relevant information for the task.
2. Choose whether files are used in the task by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the 'files' field.
3. If you need to input specific dates and times for the task, fill in the relevant date and time fields.
4. Provide a brief description of the work done in the 'task_description' field.
5. Add any relevant staff notes in the 'staff_notes' field.
6. Finally, input any additional staff email addresses if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the job or task at hand.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the staff member performing the job or task.
* **Task Date** (`task_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the task.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the task.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the end time of the task.
* **Work Description** (`work_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the work done for the task.
* **Materials Used** (`materials_used`, `text`, optional): List any materials used during the task.
* **Files** (`files`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether files are used in the task.
* **Files Upload** (`files_files`, `text`, optional): If files are used, upload any relevant files.
* **Files Upload 2** (`files_files_2`, `text`, optional): Another field for uploading files.
* **Task Description** (`task_description`, `text`, optional): Provide a brief description of the task.
* **Staff Notes** (`staff_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional staff notes.
* **Job Title 2** (`job_title_2`, `text`, optional): Enter another job title.
* **Staff Name 2** (`staff_name_2`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of another staff member.
* **Job Date 2** (`job_date_2`, `text`, optional): Enter another date.
* **Start Time 2** (`start_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter another start time.
* **End Time 2** (`end_time_2`, `time`, optional): Enter another end time.
* **Staff Notes 2** (`staff_notes_2`, `text`, optional): Add more staff notes.
* **Job Title 3** (`job_title_3`, `text`, optional): Enter another job title.
* **Staff Name 3** (`staff_name_3`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of another staff member.
* **Job Date 3** (`job_date_3`, `text`, optional): Enter another date.
* **Start Time 3** (`start_time_3`, `time`, optional): Enter another start time.
* **End Time 3** (`end_time_3`, `time`, optional): Enter another end time.
* **Staff Notes 3** (`staff_notes_3`, `text`, optional): Add more staff notes.
* **Staff Email** (`staff_email`, `email`, optional): Input any additional staff email addresses.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
- Double-check the form for any missing or incorrect information.
- Use the form for its intended purpose, i.e., collecting data on jobs and tasks performed by staff members.
