# Performance Review Audit Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance Review Audit Form is designed to provide a structured and objective assessment of an employee's performance. This form aims to identify areas of strength and weakness, set performance goals, and track progress over time.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access this form.
2. Review the employee's recent performance and identify areas that require improvement or recognition.
3. Answer the fields as thoroughly as possible, taking into account the employee's performance during the review period.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Review Form** (`performance_review_form`, text, optional): This field is optional and allows you to provide a brief summary of the employee's performance during the review period.
* **Employee Evaluation** (`employee_evaluation`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to evaluate the employee's overall performance:
	+ Highly Satisfied
	+ Moderately Satisfied
	+ Not Satisfied
* **Performance Rating** (`performance_rating`, number, optional): Enter a numerical rating of the employee's performance, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.
* **Areas for Improvement** (`areas_for_improvement`, text, optional): Describe specific areas where the employee can improve or develop new skills.
* **Date of Last Review** (`date_of_last_review`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last performance review for the employee.
* **Expected Date of Next Review** (`expected_date_of_next_review`, date, optional): Enter the expected date of the next performance review for the employee.
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the employee being reviewed.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person conducting the performance review.
* **Review Comments** (`review_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback for the employee.
* **Rating Comments** (`rating_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback related to the performance rating.
* **Development Goals** (`development_goals`, text, optional): Outline the employee's development goals and objectives.
* **Next Steps for Development** (`next_steps_for_development`, text, optional): Describe the next steps for the employee's development.
* **Date of Submission** (`date_of_submission`, date, optional): Enter the date of submission for this performance review form.
* **Form Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following options to indicate the current status of the form:
	+ In Progress
	+ Approved
	+ Rejected

## Tips
* Ensure that you answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review and proofread your answers before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or the overall process, consult with the relevant personnel.
* Submit the form only once for each performance review period.
