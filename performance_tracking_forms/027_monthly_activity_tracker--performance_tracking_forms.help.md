# Monthly Activity Tracker - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Monthly Activity Tracker" is a form used to track team performance and progress. It provides a structured way to record and analyze activities, goals, and achievements of team members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the month in which the activity took place.
2. Choose the team lead who was responsible for the activity.
3. Indicate the activity level (High, Medium, or Low).
4. Enter a brief reflection on the activity.
5. Add any additional comments (optional).
6. Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
7. Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form (if applicable).
8. If applicable, select the user to whom the activity is assigned.
9. If applicable, enter the manager's comments on the activity.
10. If applicable, mark whether the activity was submitted by the original submitter.
11. Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
12. Enter a unique activity ID.
13. Enter the team ID.
14. Indicate whether the activity was created by the original submitter.
15. If applicable, indicate whether the activity was updated by someone else.
16. Enter the date and time when the form was created and updated.
17. If applicable, indicate whether the activity was deleted.
18. If applicable, enter the date and time when the form was deleted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select a month** (Select a month): Choose a month from the provided options.
* **Team Lead** (Select multiple): Select the team lead who was responsible for the activity.
* **Activity Level** (Select one): Indicate the level of activity (High, Medium, or Low).
* **Goal Achieved** (Text): Enter a brief reflection on the activity.
* **Comments** (Optional, Text): Add any additional comments.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the person who submitted the form.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of the person who submitted the form (if applicable).
* **Assigned User** (Select one): If applicable, select the user to whom the activity is assigned.
* **Manager Approval** (Optional, Note): If applicable, enter the manager's comments on the activity.
* **Manager Comments** (Optional, Note): If applicable, add the manager's comments.
* **Submitted by** (Select one): If applicable, mark whether the activity was submitted by the original submitter.
* **Submitted on** (Date): Enter the date and time when the form was submitted.
* **Submitted by Time** (Time): Enter the time when the form was submitted (if applicable).
* **Activity ID** (Number): Enter a unique activity ID.
* **Team ID** (Number): Enter the team ID.
* **Created by** (Select one): Indicate whether the activity was created by the original submitter.
* **Updated by** (Select one): If applicable, indicate whether the activity was updated by someone else.
* **Created on** (Date): Enter the date when the form was created.
* **Updated on** (Date): Enter the date when the form was updated.
* **Created at** (Time): Enter the time when the form was created and updated.
* **Updated at** (Time): Enter the time when the form was updated (if applicable).
* **Deleted by** (Select one): If applicable, indicate whether the activity was deleted by someone else.
* **Deleted on** (Date): Enter the date when the form was deleted (if applicable).
* **Deleted at** (Time): Enter the time when the form was deleted (if applicable).
