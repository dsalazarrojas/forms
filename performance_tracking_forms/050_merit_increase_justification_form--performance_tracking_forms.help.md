# Merit Increase Justification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to request and justify a merit increase for an employee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the relevant fields with accurate and complete information:

### 1. **User Input**
 * This field is where you can enter any additional information about the employee you are justifying the merit increase for.

### 2. **Job Title**
 * This field is where you can enter the job title of the employee you are justifying the merit increase for.

### 3. **Department**
 * This field is where you can enter the department of the employee you are justifying the merit increase for. (Optional)

### 4. **Justification**
 * This field is where you can enter the clear and concise justification for the requested merit increase.

### 5. **Date**
 * This field is where you can enter the date you are submitting the request.

### 6. **Time**
 * This field is not applicable for this form.

### 7. **Salary Increase**
 * This field is where you can enter the amount of salary increase you are requesting.

### 8. **Amount**
 * This field is where you can enter the amount of the merit increase.

### 9. **Reason**
 * This field is where you can enter any additional comments or justifications.

### 10. **Manager Review**
 * This field is where the manager can enter their review or approval of the merit increase request.

### 11. **Manager Signature**
 * This field is where the manager can enter their signature.

### 12. **Manager Approval**
 * This field is where the manager can select the approval status.

### 13. **Reviewer Name**
 * This field is where the reviewer can enter their name.

### 14. **Reviewer Position**
 * This field is where the reviewer can enter their position.

### 15. **Reviewer Signature**
 * This field is where the reviewer can enter their signature.

### 16. **Reviewer Approval**
 * This field is where the reviewer can select the approval status.

### 17. **Manager Comments**
 * This field is where the manager can enter any comments or feedback.

### 18. **Reviewer Comments**
 * This field is where the reviewer can enter any comments or feedback.

### 19. **Approved**
 * This field is where the reviewer can select the approved status.

### 20. **Comments**
 * This field is where you can enter any additional comments.

### 21. **Reviewer Comments**
 * This field is where the reviewer can enter any additional comments.

### 22. **Date Submitted**
 * This field is where you can enter the date you submitted the form.

### 23. **Submitted By**
 * This field is where you can enter the name of the person who submitted the form.

### 24. **Submitted On**
 * This field is where you can enter the date and time the form was submitted. (Optional)

### 25. **Created On**
 * This field is where you can enter the date and time the form was created. (Not applicable for submission)
