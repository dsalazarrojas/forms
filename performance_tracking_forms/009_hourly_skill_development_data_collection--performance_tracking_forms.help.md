# hourly_skill_development_data_collection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an individual's progress in developing a specific skill, with a focus on tracking hours spent practicing and outcomes achieved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the skill being developed and the activities performed. Follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate submitter type from the options (Learner, Trainer, or Team).
2. Fill in the necessary information for each field, using the hints provided as guides.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Learner ID** (`id: 2`, `type: number`, required: false): This field is used to identify the individual learner, providing a unique number that tracks their progress.
* **Learner Skill Area** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the skill area being developed, such as programming, writing, or design.
* **Skill Level** (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Choose from the options to indicate the current skill level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Mastery).
* **What were you doing?** (`id: 5`, `type: text`, required: false): Describe the activities performed to develop the skill, such as coding exercises or reading materials.
* **How long did you practice?** (`id: 6`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours spent practicing the skill.
* **What did you accomplish?** (`id: 7`, `type: text`, required: false): Report the specific outcomes achieved through practice, such as completing a project or solving a problem.
* **Details about learning outcome** (`id: 8`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide additional context or comments about the learning outcome.
* **Date** (`id: 9`, `type: date`, required: false): Enter the date when the practice took place.
* **Start Time** (`id: 10`, `type: time`, required: false): Record the start time of the practice session.
* **End Time** (`id: 11`, `type: time`, required: false): Note the end time of the practice session.
* **Logged by** (`id: 12`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Select from the options to indicate who logged this form submission (Learner, Trainer, or Team).
* **Team ID** (`id: 13`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the Team that submitted the form.
* **Trainer ID** (`id: 14`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the Trainer who submitted the form.
* **Form Footer** (`id: 15`, `type: note`, required: false): This field is not currently used.
* **Notes** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, required: false): Add any additional comments or feedback.
* **Additional Comments** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter additional comments or feedback.
* **Tags** (`id: 18`, `type: select_multiple`, required: true): Choose from the options to label this form submission with relevant keywords.
* **Form Submitter** (`id: 19`, `type: select_one`, required: true): Select from the options to indicate who submitted the form (Learner, Trainer, or Team).
* **Form Submitted Date** (`id: 20`, `type: date`, required: false): Note the date when the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted Time** (`id: 21`, `type: time`, required: false): Record the time when the form was submitted.
* **Form Submitted By** (`id: 22`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select from the options to indicate who submitted the form (Learner, Trainer, or Team).
* **Form Submitted ID** (`id: 23`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter the unique identifier for the form submitter.
* **Form Submitter IP** (`id: 24`, `type: text`, required: false): Record the IP address of the form submitter.
* **Form Submitter User Agent** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, required: false): Note the user agent of the form submitter.
