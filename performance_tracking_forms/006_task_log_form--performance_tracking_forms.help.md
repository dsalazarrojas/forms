# Task Log Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Task Log Form is a straightforward tool to record and track progress on specific tasks, allowing you to monitor and report on your work status.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Open the Task Log Form and start by entering the **Task Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the task you are tracking, such as "Project Report" or "Meeting Preparation".
2.  Provide a brief **Task Description** that explains the task's objectives, goals, or key points, such as "Create a report on quarterly sales numbers" or "Prepare for a team meeting on marketing strategy".
3.  Set the **Due Date** for the task, which is the date when it should be completed or submitted. This helps keep track of deadlines and timelines.
4.  Choose the **Status** of the task from the options "Active", "Pending", or "Completed", depending on its current state.
5.  Determine the **Priority** level of the task from the options "High", "Medium", or "Low", indicating its relative importance and urgency.
6.  Once you've filled out the required fields, review your entries and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   *Task Name* (`task_log_form_task_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the task you are tracking, such as "Project Report" or "Meeting Preparation".
*   *Task Description* (`task_log_form_task_description`, text, required: false): Briefly describe the task, including key points and objectives.
*   *Due Date* (`task_log_form_due_date`, text, required: false): Set the deadline for the task, keeping in mind its importance and urgency.
*   *Status* (`task_log_form_status`, select_one, required: false): Choose the status of the task from "Active", "Pending", or "Completed" to indicate its current state.
*   *Priority* (`task_log_form_priority`, select_one, required: false): Determine the priority level of the task from "High", "Medium", or "Low" to reflect its relative importance and urgency.

## Tips
*   Always fill out the form accurately and honestly, reflecting the current status of your tasks.
*   Use the Task Log Form to keep track of multiple tasks and their progress over time.
*   Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in task status.
