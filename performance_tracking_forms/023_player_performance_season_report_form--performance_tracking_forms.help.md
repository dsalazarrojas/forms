# player_performance_season_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The player performance season report form is used to collect and document a player's performance throughout a season. It helps team managers, coaches, or other personnel to gather and review performance data for players.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the season report form with the required information, including season start and end dates, player info, team name, and performance metrics such as goals scored, assists made, and penalties given.
2. Select the correct ratings for team, coach, and player performance from the available options.
3. Add any comments if necessary.
4. Select the form completion status to indicate that the form has been completed or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Season Report Form** (`season_report_form`, text, not required): This is the main section of the form where you can add any additional comments or information about the season.
* **Player Info** (`player_info`, text, not required): Enter the player's name and other relevant details.
* **Season Start Date** (`season_start_date`, date, not required): Enter the start date of the season.
* **Season End Date** (`season_end_date`, date, not required): Enter the end date of the season.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the team.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_one, not required): Select the player's position from the available options.
* **Goals Scored** (`goals_scored`, number, not required): Enter the number of goals scored by the player.
* **Assists Made** (`assists_made`, number, not required): Enter the number of assists made by the player.
* **Penalties Given** (`penalties_given`, number, not required): Enter the number of penalties given by the player.
* **Wins** (`wins`, select_multiple, not required): Select the number of wins achieved by the player from the available options.
* **Losses** (`losses`, select_multiple, not required): Select the number of losses experienced by the player from the available options.
* **Draws** (`draws`, select_multiple, not required): Select the number of draws experienced by the player from the available options.
* **Points Scored** (`points_scored`, number, not required): Enter the total points scored by the player.
* **Yellow Cards** (`yellow_cards`, number, not required): Enter the number of yellow cards received by the player.
* **Red Cards** (`red_cards`, number, not required): Enter the number of red cards received by the player.
* **Team Rating** (`team_rating`, select_one, not required): Select the rating for the team from the available options.
* **Coach Rating** (`coach_rating`, select_one, not required): Select the rating for the coach from the available options.
* **Player Rating** (`player_rating`, select_one, not required): Select the rating for the player from the available options.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, not required): Select the overall rating for the season from the available options.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, not required): Add any comments or additional information about the season.
* **Player Coach Rating** (`player_coach_rating`, number, not required): Enter the rating for player coach interaction.
* **Team Coach Rating** (`team_coach_rating`, number, not required): Enter the rating for team coach interaction.
* **Coach Player Interaction** (`coach_player_interaction`, select_one, not required): Select the rating for coach player interaction.
* **Communication with Coach** (`communication_with_coach`, number, not required): Enter the rating for communication with coach.
* **Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status`, select_one, not required): Select the status of the form completion from the available options.

Note: All fields are not required unless marked as required in the YAML.
