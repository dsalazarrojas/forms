# Military Promotion Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Military Promotion Assessment Form is designed to evaluate and assess the performance of military officers and soldiers. This form collects information on their performance, evaluation, and service details. It helps in making informed decisions about promotions and personnel management within the military organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information.
2. Fill out the "Performance Assessment" page with a detailed written assessment of the officer's or soldier's performance.
3. On the "Officer Evaluation" page, enter the officer's performance evaluation details.
4. On the "Soldier Evaluation" page, enter the soldier's performance evaluation details.
5. For the "Promotion Recommendation" section, select all relevant options that apply.
6. If "Other Promotion Recommendation" is selected, provide a detailed description of the promotion recommendation and the date it was made.
7. Enter the officer's or soldier's service details, including name, rank, years of service, date of service start and end, and service hours.
8. Evaluate the officer's or soldier's performance using the "Officer Performance Evaluation" dropdown options.
9. Enter any additional comments about the officer or soldier.
10. Review and submit the form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Assessment (1)**: A text field to enter a detailed written assessment of the officer's or soldier's performance.
* **Officer Evaluation (2)**: A text field to enter the officer's performance evaluation details.
* **Soldier Evaluation (3)**: A text field to enter the soldier's performance evaluation details.
* **Promotion Recommendation (4)**: A dropdown field with two options: "Yes" and "No". If "Yes" is selected, additional fields will appear to provide a detailed description of the promotion recommendation, including the date it was made.
* **Promotion Recommendation Other (5)**: A text field to enter a detailed description of the promotion recommendation, if other than "Yes" or "No".
* **Promotion Recommendation Other Date (6)**: A date field to enter the date of the promotion recommendation.
* **Promotion Recommendation Other Rating (7)**: A numerical field to enter a rating for the promotion recommendation.
* **Overall Evaluation (9)**: A text field to enter an overall evaluation of the officer's or soldier's performance.
* **Officer Name (10)**: A text field to enter the officer's name.
* **Officer Rank (11)**: A text field to enter the officer's rank.
* **Officer Service Number (12)**: A text field to enter the officer's service number.
* **Officer Years of Service (13)**: A numerical field to enter the officer's years of service.
* **Officer Date of Service Start (14)**: A date field to enter the officer's start date of service.
* **Officer Date of Service End (15)**: A date field to enter the officer's end date of service.
* **Officer Service Hours (16)**: A numerical field to enter the officer's service hours.
* **Officer Performance Evaluation (17)**: A dropdown field with four options: "Very Satisfactory", "Needs Improvement", "Below Expectations", and "Unsatisfactory".
* **Officer Performance Evaluation Details (18)**: A text field to enter any additional details about the officer's performance evaluation.
* **Officer Comments (19)**: A text field to enter any comments about the officer.
* **Soldier Name (20)**: A text field to enter the soldier's name.
* **Soldier Rank (21)**: A text field to enter the soldier's rank.
* **Soldier Years of Service (22)**: A numerical field to enter the soldier's years of service.
* **Soldier Date of Service Start (23)**: A date field to enter the soldier's start date of service.
* **Soldier Date of Service End (24)**: A date field to enter the soldier's end date of service.
* **Soldier Service Hours (25)**: A numerical field to enter the soldier's service hours.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
- Review and double-check the information entered before submitting the form.
