# Weekly Sales Activity Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Weekly Sales Activity Report is a form used by sales reps to track and report their individual sales activity on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your sales rep status from the list of options. This will help us understand your role and responsibility within sales.
2. Choose your territory from the list of options. This will help us understand which region you're working in.
3. If required, input your week start date in the date field.
4. Input your sales amount. This is the total sales amount you've made for the week.
5. Input your target amount. This is the target sales amount you had set for the week.
6. Add any key challenges you faced during the week.
7. Add any comments or notes about your sales activity.
8. Input your email address and phone number. These will be used for contact and follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sales Rep** (`Sales Rep`, select_one, required): Choose your sales rep status from the list of options.
* **Territory** (`Territory`, select_one, required): Choose your sales territory from the list of options.
* **Week Start Date** (`Week Start Date`, date, optional): If you need to report on a specific week start date, input it here.
* **Sales Amount** (`Sales Amount`, number, optional): Input your total sales amount for the week.
* **Target Amount** (`Target Amount`, number, optional): Input your target sales amount for the week.
* **Challenge** (`Challenge`, text, optional): Add any key challenges you faced during the week.
* **Progress** (`Progress`, select_multiple, optional): Choose any progress made towards your targets.
* **Key Challenges** (`Key Challenges`, note, optional): Add any key challenges you faced during the week.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your sales activity.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Input your email address. This will be used for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Input your phone number.
* **Weekly Sales Activity Report** (`Weekly Sales Activity Report`, select_one, optional): Choose whether this report is for weekly sales activity.
