# Goal Setting Reflection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users reflect on their current status, progress, and challenges in relation to their goals. It is meant to be completed regularly, allowing users to monitor and adjust their goal setting as needed.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your current status, progress, and thoughts on each section.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Use the form as a tool to track your progress and identify areas of improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Current Status** (`1`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of your current status, such as "On track" or "Behind schedule".
* **Reflection Date** (`2`, `date`, required: false): The date you are reflecting on your progress.
* **Current Progress** (`3`, `number`, required: false): A numerical value representing your current progress, e.g. 50% complete.
* **Goal Achievements** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
	+ Select all applicable options for your achievements, such as "Option 1" and "Option 2".
* **Challenges Faced** (`5`, `text`, required: false): A detailed description of any challenges you have faced in achieving your goal.
* **Next Actions** (`6`, `text`, required: false): A list of actions you plan to take next to achieve your goal.
* **Reflection Summary** (`7`, `text`, required: false): A brief summary of your reflections and thoughts on your progress.
* **Suggestions for Improvement** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Any suggestions you have for improving your goal setting and tracking process.

## Tips
* Complete the form regularly to track your progress and identify areas of improvement.
* Be honest and accurate in your responses to get the most out of this form.
* Use the form as a tool to adjust your goal setting and monitoring process as needed.
