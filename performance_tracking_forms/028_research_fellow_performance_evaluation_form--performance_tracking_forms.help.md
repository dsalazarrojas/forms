# Research Fellow Performance Evaluation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help Research Fellows evaluate their own performance on a regular basis. It is designed to be completed by the Research Fellow themselves and should take approximately 15-20 minutes to complete.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select all relevant departments that you have worked with during the quarter. This will help us understand your breadth of experience and exposure.
2. Enter the date of the evaluation, which should be the last day of the quarter.
3. Enter the time spent on the evaluation, which should reflect the actual time spent on the evaluation.
4. Provide a brief note explaining your evaluation and any notable achievements or challenges.
5. For each performance area, provide a brief description of your progress, goals, and areas of improvement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, optional):
	+ Select all departments you have worked with during the quarter.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, optional):
	+ Enter the last day of the quarter for which you are evaluating your performance.
* **Evaluation Time** (`evaluation_time`, `time`, optional):
	+ Enter the actual time spent on the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Note** (`evaluation_note`, `note`, optional):
	+ Provide a brief note on your evaluation and any notable achievements or challenges.
* **Review Frequency** (`review_frequency`, `number`, optional):
	+ Note the frequency of reviews you received during the quarter (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).
* **Performance Area 1-6** (`performance_area_1`, `performance_area_2`, `performance_area_3`, etc., `text`, optional):
	+ Provide a brief description of your progress, goals, and areas of improvement for each performance area.

## Tips
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- Take the time to provide thoughtful and detailed descriptions for each performance area.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or fields, please reach out to your supervisor or evaluation team for guidance.
- Remember to review and update your previous submissions before completing this form to avoid duplication of effort.
