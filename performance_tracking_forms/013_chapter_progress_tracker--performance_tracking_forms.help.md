# Chapter Progress Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chapter Progress Tracker form is used to monitor and record the progress of members on chapters or modules. It helps to track completion rates, note any obstacles or challenges faced, and outline next steps for continued progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the member's full name in the "member name" field.
2. Type the name of the chapter or group the member is working on.
3. Enter the title of the chapter or module being tracked.
4. Input the completion percentage of the member.
5. Record the date of this progress entry.
6. (Optional) Enter the approximate hours spent on the chapter or module.
7. (Optional) Note any obstacles or challenges encountered.
8. (Optional) Outline next steps for continued progress.
9. (Optional) Provide feedback or comments from the reviewer or mentor.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Chapter/Group Name** (`chapter_name`, `text`, required): Type the name of the chapter or group the member is working on.
* **Module Title** (`module_title`, `text`, required): Enter the title of the chapter or module being tracked.
* **Progress Percent** (`progress_percent`, `number`, required): Input the completion percentage as a whole number.
* **Date Updated** (`date_updated`, `date`, required): Record the date of this progress entry.
* **Hours Spent** (`hours_spent`, `number`, optional): Enter the approximate hours spent on the chapter or module (not required, but helpful for tracking).
* **Obstacles** (`obstacles`, `text`, optional): Note any blockers or challenges encountered.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, `text`, optional): Outline next steps for continued progress.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide feedback or comments from the reviewer or mentor.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any information, please contact the relevant team member or manager.
* The form data will be used to inform and improve our progress tracking and resource allocation.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only, and sensitive information should not be shared externally.
