# performance_management_reflection_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track and evaluate an individual's performance in a structured and efficient manner. It consists of multiple pages with different types of questions and evaluations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by answering the questions on the "Goals" page, selecting whether you have met or not met each goal.
2. On the "Review" page, provide a brief description of your goals and what you have accomplished towards them.
3. On the "Reflection" page, write a detailed reflection on your performance, discussing what you learned, what worked well, and what areas you need improvement.
4. On the "Performance" page, evaluate your performance in various areas by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each area.
5. On the "Goals 2" page, evaluate whether you have met or not met new goals.
6. On the "Reflection 2" page, write a detailed reflection on your performance, discussing what you learned, what worked well, and what areas you need improvement.
7. On the "Review 2" page, provide a brief description of your goals and what you have accomplished towards them.
8. Finally, on the "Evaluation" page, provide an evaluation of your overall performance, and on the "Final Evaluation" page, evaluate whether this evaluation was accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Goals** (`Goals`, `select_one`, required): This page is used to evaluate whether you have met or not met each of your goals. Please select "Yes" if you have met a goal, and "No" if you have not.
* **Review** (`Review`, `note`, required): On this page, provide a brief description of your goals and what you have accomplished towards them. This section is meant to be a quick summary of your goals and progress.
* **Reflection** (`Reflection`, `text`, required): In this section, write a detailed reflection on your performance, discussing what you learned, what worked well, and what areas you need improvement.
* **Performance** (`Performance`, `select_multiple`, required): Evaluate your performance in various areas by selecting "Yes" or "No" for each area. This section is meant to assess your overall performance level.
* **Goals 2** (`Goals 2`, `select_one`, required): This page is used to evaluate whether you have met or not met new goals.
* **Reflection 2** (`Reflection 2`, `text`, required): Write a detailed reflection on your performance, discussing what you learned, what worked well, and what areas you need improvement.
* **Review 2** (`Review 2`, `note`, required): Provide a brief description of your goals and what you have accomplished towards them.
* **Evaluation** (`Evaluation`, `text`, required): Provide an evaluation of your overall performance, discussing what you learned, what worked well, and what areas you need improvement.
* **Final Evaluation** (`Final Evaluation`, `select_one`, required): Evaluate whether this evaluation was accurate, selecting "Yes" or "No" for each area.

## Tips
* Make sure to answer each question thoroughly and honestly.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure of any question, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
* This form is meant to be a tool for personal and professional growth, so be open-minded and honest in your responses.
