# Human Capital Performance Audit Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Capital Performance Audit Application Form is designed to collect data on employee performance. It is used to track employee details and progress across different departments and job grades.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the relevant category (e.g., Performance Tracking Forms) from the options provided in the "Category" field.
2. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date Of Visit" field.
3. Enter the time of your visit in the "Time Of Visit" field.
4. Enter your employee ID in the "Employee ID" field.
5. Select your department from the options provided.
6. Enter your name in the "Employee Name" field.
7. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Enter your job grade (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.) in the corresponding "Job Grade" field (e.g., "Job Grade 1", "Job Grade 2", etc.).
9. Enter your manager's name in the "Manager Name" field.
10. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
11. Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Category** (Category, Select One, Optional): Select the relevant category (e.g., Performance Tracking Forms) from the options provided.
- **Date Of Visit** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of your visit.
- **Time Of Visit** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of your visit.
- **Employee ID** (Number, Optional): Enter your employee ID.
- **Department** (Text, Optional): Select your department from the options provided.
- **Employee Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name.
- **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
- **Job Grade** (Text, Optional): Enter your job grade (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
- **Manager Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your manager's name.
- **Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
- **Submit** (Note, Optional): Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.
- **Submitted By** (Text, Optional): This field is automatically populated and displays the name of the user who submitted the form.
- **Submitted Date** (Date, Optional): This field is automatically populated and displays the date the form was submitted.
