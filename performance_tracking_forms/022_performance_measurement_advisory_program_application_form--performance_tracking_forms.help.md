# performance-measurement-advisory-program-application-form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The performance-measurement-advisory-program-application-form is designed to collect information from organizations that have applied to the Performance Measurement Advisory Program. This form will help the program administrators understand the applicant's goals, objectives, and performance measurement criteria.

## How to Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that the correct type of input is provided (e.g., text, email, date, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required):
  This field is for the name of your organization. Please enter the full name of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required):
  Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this application.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required):
  Enter your organization's email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required):
  Enter your organization's phone number. This will be used for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Performance Measurement Period** (`performance_measurement_period`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the period of time for which you are applying for performance measurement (quarterly, monthly, or yearly).
* **Performance Measurement Duration** (`performance_measurement_duration`, `number`, required):
  Enter the duration of the performance measurement period in months.
* **Performance Measurement Start Date** (`performance_measurement_start_date`, `date`, required):
  Enter the start date of the performance measurement period.
* **Performance Measurement End Date** (`performance_measurement_end_date`, `date`, required):
  Enter the end date of the performance measurement period.
* **Performance Measurement Fund Sources** (`performance_measurement_fund_sources`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the source of the fund for your performance measurement (government funds, private grants, or internal funds).
* **Additional Performance Measurement Fund Sources** (`additional_performance_measurement_fund_sources`, `text`, optional):
  Enter any additional sources of funds not listed above.
* **Performance Measurement Objectives** (`performance_measurement_objectives`, `text`, required):
  Enter a clear and concise statement of your performance measurement objectives.
* **Performance Measurement Outcomes** (`performance_measurement_outcomes`, `text`, optional):
  Enter any expected outcomes of the performance measurement.
* **Performance Measurement Criteria** (`performance_measurement_criteria`, `select_one`, required):
  Select the criteria for your performance measurement (cost savings, customer satisfaction, efficiency gains, or productivity increases).

Note: This form requires accurate and relevant information to ensure a successful application. Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrators for assistance.
