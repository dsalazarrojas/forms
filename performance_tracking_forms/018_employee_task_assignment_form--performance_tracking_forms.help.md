## employee_task_assignment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The employee_task_assignment_form is used to track and assign tasks to employees. This form helps managers and administrators to efficiently assign tasks to employees, ensuring that tasks are completed on time and to the highest standard.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the `employee_name` field.
2. Enter the employee's email address in the `employee_email` field.
3. Enter the employee's phone number in the `employee_phone` field.
4. In the `task_assign_task` field, enter a brief description of the task to be assigned.
5. Select 'Yes' or 'No' in the `Assign Worker` field to indicate whether the task should be assigned to the selected employee.
6. If necessary, provide a review or notes in the `Review` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **employee_name**: Enter the name of the employee to be assigned the task.
* **employee_email**: Enter the employee's email address for communication purposes.
* **employee_phone**: Enter the employee's phone number for communication purposes.
* **task_assign_task**: Enter a brief description of the task to be assigned.
* **Assign Worker**: Select 'Yes' to assign the task to the selected employee. Selecting 'No' means the task will not be assigned.
* **Review**: Optional field for additional comments or review notes.
