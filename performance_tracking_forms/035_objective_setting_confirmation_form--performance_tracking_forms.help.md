# Objective Setting Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Objective Setting Confirmation Form" is a tool used to set and confirm objectives for performance tracking and management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Make sure to provide a clear and concise description of your objective.
3. Select the correct "Set by" field to indicate who set this objective.
4. Choose the due date for the objective from the calendar.
5. Select the status of your objective from the provided options.
6. Choose the priority of your objective from the provided options.
7. Fill in the progress of your objective.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your objective.
9. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you about this objective.
10. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Objective** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_1`, text, optional): Enter a clear and concise description of your objective.
* **Set by** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_2`, select_one, optional): Select who set this objective, such as "Manager", "Team Member", or "HR Team".
* **Due Date** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_3`, date, optional): Choose the due date for this objective from the calendar.
* **Status** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select the status of your objective from the provided options, such as "Active", "Completed", or "Archived".
* **Priority** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_5`, select_one, optional): Choose the priority of your objective from the provided options, such as "High", "Low", or "Medium".
* **Progress** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_6`, number, optional): Enter the progress of your objective, as a percentage or decimal.
* **Notes** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_7`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your objective.
* **Email** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_8`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about this objective.
* **Phone** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_9`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about this objective.
* **Other Contact** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_10`, text, optional): Enter any other contact information you would like to be reached at.
* **Submitter's Email** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_11`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about this objective.
* **Submitter's Phone** (`objective_setting_confirmation_form_12`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about this objective.
