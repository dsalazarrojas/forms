# Punch List Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Punch List Form is a tool used to track and manage tasks within your team or organization. It helps to keep track of tasks, their status, and the responsible employee. This form is designed to be a simple and efficient way to report and manage tasks, allowing you to focus on completing them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Task Name** field to provide a clear and concise title for the task.
2. Enter a brief description of the task in the **Task Description** field.
3. Select a due date for the task in the **Due Date** field. This will help you keep track of deadlines.
4. Enter the name of the employee assigned to complete the task in the **Employee Name** field.
5. Select the employees responsible for completing the task from the **Assigned To** field. You can choose one or multiple employees.
6. Update the **Status** field to reflect the current status of the task (Not Started, In Progress, or Done).
7. Optionally, add any additional comments related to the task in the **Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Task Name** (`task_name`, text, optional): A brief and clear title for the task.
- **Task Description** (`task_description`, text, optional): A short description of the task.
- **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): A date by which the task is due.
- **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): The name of the employee assigned to complete the task.
- **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple employees who will complete the task.
- **Status** (`status`, select_one, optional): The current status of the task (Not Started, In Progress, or Done).
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Any additional comments related to the task.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide a clear and concise title for the task to help you and others quickly understand its purpose.
* Be specific when assigning tasks to employees, so they know what is expected of them.
* Update the status regularly to keep track of progress.
* Use the comments field to share any additional information or context that may be helpful for the task's completion.
