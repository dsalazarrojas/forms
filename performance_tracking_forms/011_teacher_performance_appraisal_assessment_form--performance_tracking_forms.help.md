# Teacher Performance Appraisal Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information and feedback from school administrators, students, and parents about a teacher's performance. This form helps assess a teacher's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement, providing valuable insights for professional development and growth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting, make sure you have all the necessary information and feedback ready.
2. **Fill in required fields**: Complete the fields marked as "required".
3. **Provide thorough feedback**: Answer each section with thoughtful and constructive feedback.
4. **Submit the form**: Once complete, click "Submit" to send the form to the relevant authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Header** (`1`, `note`, required: `false`): This section is an introductory note to the form. Please leave it blank.
* **Teacher Information** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Please provide a brief summary of the teacher's job title, position, or relevant information.
* **School Administrator** (`3`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the name or title of the school administrator who will be receiving this feedback.
* **Student Feedback** (`4`, `text`, required: `false`): Share any comments, suggestions, or feedback from students about the teacher's performance.
* **Parent Feedback** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide any comments, suggestions, or feedback from parents about the teacher's performance.
