# pharmacy_employee_performance_review_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pharmacy_employee_performance_review_form is a tool for tracking employee performance in a pharmacy setting. It is used to document observations, provide feedback, and set goals for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all necessary information and permissions to complete the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and factual information.
3. Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data (1)**: A brief overview of the form's purpose and context.
* **Employee Name (2)**: The name of the employee being reviewed. This should match the employee's official name as it appears on their personnel records.
* **Job Title (3)**: The employee's current job title, as it appears on their personnel records.
* **Start Date (4)**: The date the employee started their current position in the pharmacy.
* **End Date (5)**: The date the employee's current position is expected to end.
* **Work Hours (6)**: The total number of hours the employee has worked in their current position.
* **Performance Comments (7)**: A free-form text field for providing feedback and comments on the employee's performance. This is an open-ended field, so provide specific examples and suggestions for improvement.
* **Meeting Notes (8)**: Any notes or discussions from performance meetings with the employee. This field is also open-ended, so provide any relevant details from these meetings.

## Tips

* Be as accurate and factual as possible when completing this form.
* Avoid including confidential or sensitive information.
* Review the form for completeness and accuracy before submitting.
* Only submit the form if you have all necessary permissions to do so.
