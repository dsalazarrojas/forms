# daily_activity_log_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "daily_activity_log_form" is used to record and track employee activities on a daily basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the date of your activity from the calendar.
2. Enter the time spent on your activity.
3. Describe your activity in the "activity_description" field.
4. Choose the category of your activity from the dropdown menu.
5. Select the outcomes of your activity from the dropdown menu.
6. If you have chosen "other" as your category, provide a description of your activity in the "activity_outcome_other" field.
7. If you need to include any additional information, type it in the "activity_notes" field.
8. Enter your work email and phone number for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **activity_date** (Required): Select the date of your activity from the calendar.
* **activity_time** (Required): Enter the time spent on your activity.
* **activity_description** (Required): Accurately describe your activity in this field.
* **activity_category** (Optional): Choose the category of your activity from the dropdown menu.
* **activity_outcome** (Optional): Select the outcomes of your activity from the dropdown menu.
* **activity_notes** (Optional): Provide additional information or comments about your activity.
* **activity_email** (Required): Enter your work email.
* **activity_phone** (Optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **activity_location** (Optional): Select "Yes" if your activity took place on the company premises, or "No" if it was off-site.
* **activity_outcome_other** (Optional): If you chose "other" as your category, provide a brief description of your activity.
* **activity_outcome_other_other** (Optional): If you chose "other" as your category and need to provide additional information, type it in this field.
* **activity_outcome_other_other_other** (Optional): If you need to provide further details, type them in this field.
* **activity_phone** (Optional): Enter your work phone number.
