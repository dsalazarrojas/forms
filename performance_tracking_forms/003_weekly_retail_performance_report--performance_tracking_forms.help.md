# Weekly Retail Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Weekly Retail Performance Report is a form used to track and report on various key performance indicators (KPIs) for retail stores. This form helps retailers monitor their sales, transactions, foot traffic, inventory status, and other essential metrics to evaluate their performance on a weekly basis.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure you have completed all the necessary information for each field.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Week Sales** (`id: 1`, `type: number`, `required: true`): Enter the total sales amount for the week.
* **Number Of Transactions** (`id: 2`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the number of transactions for the week.
* **Foot Traffic** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Describe the level of foot traffic for the week.
* **Inventory Status** (`id: 4`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the status of inventory for each store (Store 1, Store 2, Store 3).
* **Weekly Average Cost** (`id: 5`, `type: number`, `required: true`): Enter the weekly average cost.
* **Weekly Average Price** (`id: 6`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the weekly average price.
* **Weekly Average Revenue** (`id: 7`, `type: number`, `required: true`): Enter the weekly average revenue.
* **Weekly Retail Performance Index** (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the level of retail performance for the week (High, Low, Medium).
* **Comments** (`id: 9`, `type: note`, `required: false`): Add any additional comments or remarks about the week's performance.
* **Weekly Foot Traffic** (`id: 10`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the level of foot traffic for the week.
* **Weekly Sales** (`id: 11`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the total sales amount for the week.
* **Weekly Transactions** (`id: 12`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the number of transactions for the week.
* **Stores** (`id: 13`, `type: select_multiple`, `required: false`): Select the stores that were visited or managed during the week.
* **Date** (`id: 14`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the report.
* **Time** (`id: 15`, `type: time`, `required: false`): Enter the time of the report.
* **Store Name** (`id: 16`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the store.
* **Store Address** (`id: 17`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the address of the store.
* **Store Phone** (`id: 18`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the store.
* **Store Email** (`id: 19`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the email of the store.
* **Store Manager** (`id: 20`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Email** (`id: 21`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the email of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Phone** (`id: 22`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Ext** (`id: 23`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the extended information of the store manager.
* **Store Manager Ext Email** (`id: 24`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the email of the store manager ext.
* **Store Manager Ext Phone** (`id: 25`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number of the store manager ext.
* **Store Manager Ext Address** (`id: 26`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the address of the store manager ext.
* **Store Manager Ext Phone 2** (`id: 27`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the phone number 2 of the store manager ext.
* **Store Manager Ext Email 2** (`id: 28`, `type: email`, `required: false`): Enter the email 2 of the store manager ext.
* **Store Manager Ext 2** (`id: 29`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the extended information 2 of the store manager ext.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed for the form to be considered complete.
