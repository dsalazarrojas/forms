# Daily Performance Goals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daily Performance Goals Form is a tool used by performance tracking team members to document and report on their daily performance goals and objectives. It is a crucial tool for measuring progress and identifying areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Performance Goals** page and enter a brief description of your performance goals in the text field.
2. Provide a detailed description of your objective in the **Objective Description** field.
3. Enter the target date for your performance goal in the **Target Date** field.
4. Enter your actual performance metric in the **Actual Performance** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Goals**: A brief description of your performance goals.
* **Objective Description**: A detailed description of your objective, including specific details and metrics.
* **Target Date**: The date by which you aim to complete your performance goal.
* **Actual Performance**: A numerical value representing your actual performance metric, if available.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise descriptions in the **Performance Goals** and **Objective Description** fields.
* Use the **Target Date** field to enter a specific date when you expect to complete your performance goal.
* Enter a numerical value in the **Actual Performance** field only if you have actual data to report.
* Review and update your performance goals regularly to reflect changes in your objectives and actual performance.
