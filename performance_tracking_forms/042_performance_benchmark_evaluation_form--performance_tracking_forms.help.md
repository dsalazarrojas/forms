# Performance Benchmark Evaluation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to evaluate the performance and progress of a project or team over a specific period of time. It helps in identifying areas of improvement and development opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before you start:
- Make sure you have a clear understanding of the project or team's goals and targets you want to evaluate.
- Choose the evaluation period based on the project or team's specific requirements (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, monthly).

### To complete this form:
1. Select a rating for the overall performance of the project or team.
2. Choose the evaluation scale that best reflects the project or team's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
3. Select a metric that best describes the performance you want to evaluate (Productivity, Quality, Efficiency, Effectiveness).
4. Provide a detailed description of the development opportunity you've identified for improvement.
5. Outline your action plan to address the development opportunity.
6. Enter the follow-up date when you plan to review progress.
7. Enter the date of the evaluation.
8. Choose the evaluation period (e.g., 1 week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks, 1 month, 2 months, 3 months).
9. Enter your comments, if any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, number): Select a rating between 1 and 5 that reflects the project or team's overall performance. This rating will help in identifying areas of improvement and development opportunities.
* **Evaluation Scale** (`evaluation_scale`, select_multiple): Choose the evaluation scale that best reflects the project or team's performance (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor).
* **Performance Metric** (`performance_metric`, select_one): Select a metric that best describes the performance you want to evaluate (Productivity, Quality, Efficiency, Effectiveness).
* **Development Opportunity** (`development_opportunity`, text): Provide a detailed description of the development opportunity you've identified for improvement.
* **Action Plan** (`action_plan`, text): Outline your action plan to address the development opportunity.
* **Follow-up** (`follow_up`, date): Enter the follow-up date when you plan to review progress.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one): Choose the evaluation period (e.g., 1 week, 2 weeks, 3 weeks, 1 month, 2 months, 3 months).
* **Comments** (`comments`, text): Enter any additional comments related to the evaluation.

### Tips
- Make sure to provide specific and concrete feedback and suggestions for improvement.
- Keep the comments section for any additional information or thoughts that do not fit in the other fields.
- Review and update the form regularly to track progress and changes over time.
