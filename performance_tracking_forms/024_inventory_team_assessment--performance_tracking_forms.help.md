# inventory_team_assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you understand an employee's skills and competencies within their team, specifically in relation to inventory management. It will help you identify their strengths and areas for improvement to support their development and growth within the team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Read each question or prompt carefully and answer it as accurately as possible.
* Select or enter your responses in the provided fields.
* Review and revise your responses as needed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Welcome to the Inventory Team Assessment** (Page 1):
	+ This is an introductory page with a brief welcome message.
	+ You can read and acknowledge the message, but no action is required.
* **Warehouse Experience** (Page 2):
	+ Select all relevant options from the list that best describe your experience with warehouse operations.
	+ Examples: "Warehouse management", "Inventory control", "Supply chain management".
* **Retail Experience** (Page 3):
	+ Select all relevant options from the list that best describe your experience with retail operations.
	+ Examples: "Sales floor management", "Customer service", "Visual merchandising".
* **Logistics Experience** (Page 4):
	+ Select all relevant options from the list that best describe your experience with logistics operations.
	+ Examples: "Delivery management", "Inventory management", "Transportation management".
* **Strengths** (Page 5):
	+ Enter all your strengths, such as teamwork, communication, time management, etc.
* **List of Strengths** (Page 6):
	+ Enter a list of specific skills or qualities you possess that are valuable to the team.
	+ Examples: "Excellent communication skills", "Leadership abilities", "Attention to detail".
* **Improvement Areas** (Page 7):
	+ Enter all areas where you could use improvement or development.
	+ Examples: "Time management", "Conflict resolution", "Adaptability".
* **List of Improvement Areas** (Page 8):
	+ Enter a list of specific areas where you could improve or develop.
	+ Examples: "Improving communication with team members", "Developing leadership skills", "Enhancing problem-solving abilities".
* **Competency 1** (Page 9):
	+ Rate your skill level from 1 to 5 for the first competency.
	+ Examples: "Time management", "Data analysis", "Leadership".
* **Competency 2** (Page 10):
	+ Rate your skill level from 1 to 5 for the second competency.
* **Competency 3** (Page 11):
	+ Rate your skill level from 1 to 5 for the third competency.
* **Competency 4** (Page 12):
	+ Rate your skill level from 1 to 5 for the fourth competency.
* **Competency 5** (Page 13):
	+ Rate your skill level from 1 to 5 for the fifth competency.
