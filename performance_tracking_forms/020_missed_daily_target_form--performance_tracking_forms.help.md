# Missed Daily Target Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Missed Daily Target Form is designed to track missed daily targets in a specific industry or category. It helps in collecting data on daily performance and can be used for analysis and reporting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date when the target was missed.
2. Select the time when the target was expected to be achieved.
3. Briefly describe why the target was missed.
4. Enter the name of the team responsible for meeting the target.
5. Add any additional notes or comments related to the missed target.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form 1** (`form_1`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the form.
* **Target Date** (`target_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date when the target was missed.
* **Target Time** (`target_time`, Time, Optional): Select the time when the target was expected to be achieved.
* **Missed Target** (`missed_target`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe why the target was missed.
* **Reason** (`reason`, Text, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments related to the missed target.
* **Team** (`team`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the team responsible for meeting the target.

# Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the form administrators.
* This form is not intended for every single missed target, but rather for tracking recurring or important missed targets.
