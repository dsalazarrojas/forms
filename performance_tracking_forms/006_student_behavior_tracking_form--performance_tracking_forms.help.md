This form is designed to track student behavior and attendance in an educational setting. The purpose of this form is to collect data on a student's general behavior, attendance, and performance, as well as provide a space for teachers to leave notes and feedback. This form should only be used for tracking student behavior and attendance in a specific class or school, and should not be used for other purposes.

# Student Behavior Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Behavior Tracking Form is a tool used by teachers to document and track student behavior and attendance in a specific class or school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form questions and answer them accordingly.
2. Fill in the relevant fields with the student's information.
3. Make sure to provide as much detail as possible, especially for fields like notes and feedback.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Behavior** (`general_behavior`, text, optional): Provide a general description of the student's behavior, such as their attitude, participation, or any notable incidents.
- **During Hours** (`during_hours`, date, optional): Enter the date on which the student exhibited the described behavior.
- **Student Behavior Note** (`student_behavior_note`, note, optional): Leave a note about the student's behavior, such as any specific incidents or concerns.
- **Note During Hours** (`during_hours_note`, note, optional): Leave a note about the student's behavior during specific hours.
- **General Behavior Phone** (`general_behavior_phone`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the student's behavior, such as any phone calls or messages.
- **Phone** (`student_behavior_note_phone`, text, optional): Leave a note about any phone calls or messages related to the student's behavior.
- **Phone During Hours** (`during_hours_phone`, text, optional): Leave a note about any phone calls or messages related to the student's behavior during specific hours.
- **Teacher Feedback** (`teacher_feedback`, text, optional): Leave feedback or comments from the teacher regarding the student's behavior.
- **General Attendance** (`general_attendance`, number, optional): Track the student's overall attendance.
- **Tardiness** (`tardiness`, number, optional): Record any instances of tardiness.
- **Teacher** (`teacher_attendance`, number, optional): Track the teacher's attendance.
- **Student Performance** (`student_performance`, select_one, optional): Rate the student's performance as Excellent, Poor, or another option.
- **Student Behavior Note Date** (`student_behavior_note_date`, date, optional): Record the date of any notes or feedback left about the student's behavior.
- **Teacher Attendance 2** (`teacher_attendance_2`, number, optional): Track the teacher's attendance.
- **Time In** (`time_in`, time, optional): Record the student's time of arrival.
- **Time Out** (`time_out`, time, optional): Record the student's time of departure.
- **Email** (`student_behavior_note_email`, email, optional): Leave an email address for the student or teacher to contact.
- **General Behavior Email** (`general_behavior_email`, email, optional): Leave an email address for the student or teacher to contact.
- **Email During Hours** (`during_hours_email`, email, optional): Leave an email address for the student or teacher to contact during specific hours.
- **Teacher Attendance Email** (`teacher_attendance_email`, email, optional): Leave an email address for the teacher to contact.
- **General Behavior Phone 2** (`general_behavior_phone_2`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the student's behavior, such as phone calls or messages.
- **During Hours Phone 2** (`during_hours_phone_2`, text, optional): Provide additional information about the student's behavior, such as phone calls or messages, during specific hours.
- **Time Out 2** (`time_out_2`, time, optional): Record the student's time of departure.
- **Teacher Behavior Note** (`teacher_behavior_note`, note, optional): Leave a note about the teacher's behavior.
- **Teacher Attendance 3** (`teacher_attendance_3`, number, optional): Track the teacher's attendance.
