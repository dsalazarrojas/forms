# VSM Item Record Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for tracking and managing Visual System Modeling (VSM) items. It allows users to input and store relevant information about VSM items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct "Item Type" from the options provided.
2. Choose the relevant "Report Type 1" from the available options.
3. Choose the relevant "VSM Items" from the available options.
4. Repeat steps 2-3 for each of the "VSM Items 2" to "VSM Items 5" fields.
5. Enter any necessary notes in the "Note" fields.
6. If required, enter your email address in the "Email" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Item Type** (`item_type_1`, Select Multiple, Required): This field determines the type of VSM item being recorded. Select one or more options that apply to the item.
* **Report Type 1** (`report_type_1`, Select One, Required): This field requires you to choose the type of report related to the VSM item.
* **VSM Items** (`vsm_items_1`, Select One, Required): Choose the relevant VSM item from the available options. Repeat this process for each of the "VSM Items 2" to "VSM Items 5" fields.
* **Note 1** (`note_1`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the VSM item.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the VSM item.
* **Note 3** (`note_3`, Note, Optional): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the VSM item.
* **Email** (`email_1`, Email, Optional): If required, enter your email address for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field or its requirements, please reach out to your supervisor or the designated authority for clarification.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
