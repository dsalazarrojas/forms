# Performance Improvement Inquiry Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Improvement Inquiry Contact Form is designed to collect information from users who have a performance improvement inquiry or concern. This form helps to identify areas of improvement and ensures that the right person is contacted to address the issue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Determine whether you have a performance improvement inquiry or concern by selecting "Yes" or "No" for "Performance Improvement Inquiry".
2. Provide your contact information, including your name (Contact Person), department, phone number, and email address.
3. Specify your performance concerns and how you would like to be followed up on.
4. Indicate the priority of your issue by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. If you have any additional comments or information about your issue, please include them in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Performance Improvement Inquiry** (Page 1):
	+ This is a multiple-choice question asking if you have a performance improvement inquiry or concern.
* **Contact Information** (Page 2):
	+ Enter your name (Contact Person) in this field to ensure that the right person is contacted about your issue.
* **Performance Concerns** (Page 2):
	+ Describe your performance concerns in this field, including any specific issues you are experiencing.
* **Priority** (Page 2):
	+ Indicate the priority of your issue by selecting "Yes" if it is urgent or high-priority, and "No" if it is not urgent.
* **Follow-Up Plan** (Page 3):
	+ If you have a follow-up plan, describe it in this field to ensure that the issue is addressed effectively.
* **Contact Person** (Page 3):
	+ Enter your name, department, and other relevant contact information so that we can reach out to you about your issue.
* **Department** (Page 3):
	+ Provide the department or team responsible for resolving your issue.
* **Email** (Page 3):
	+ Enter your email address so that we can contact you about your issue.
* **Phone** (Page 3):
	+ Enter your phone number so that we can contact you quickly and efficiently.
* **Comments** (Page 4):
	+ This field is optional, but if you have any additional comments or information about your issue, please include them here.
