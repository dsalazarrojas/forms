# Performance Recording Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Recording Upload Form is a tool used to record and track performances, allowing users to upload and categorize videos or audio recordings of their performances.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Determine if you are uploading a video or audio recording of your performance.
2.  Choose whether it's a "Yes" or "No" in the "upload_video_or_audio" field.
3.  If you are uploading a video or audio recording, enter the file name or location in the "video_or_audio_file" field.
4.  Enter a brief description of your performance in the "description" field.
5.  Enter the date of your performance in the "performance_date" field.
6.  Enter the time of your performance in the "performance_time" field.
7.  Enter the name of the performer in the "performer" field.
8.  Enter the venue where your performance took place in the "venue" field.
9.  Add any additional performance notes in the "performance_notes" field.
10.  Choose the category of your performance from the options: Festival, Auditions, or Showcases.
11.  Enter the name of the event where your performance took place in the "event_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **upload_video_or_audio** (Yes/No): Choose "Yes" if you are uploading a video or audio recording of your performance, or "No" if you are not.
*   **video_or_audio_file** (text): Enter the file name or location of the video or audio recording you are uploading.
*   **description** (text): Enter a brief description of your performance.
*   **performance_date** (date): Enter the date of your performance.
*   **performance_time** (time): Enter the time of your performance.
*   **performer** (text): Enter the name of the performer.
*   **venue** (text): Enter the venue where your performance took place.
*   **performance_notes** (note): Enter any additional performance notes.
*   **category** (Festival/Auditions/Showcases): Choose the category of your performance from the options provided.
*   **event_name** (text): Enter the name of the event where your performance took place.

## Tips
*   Make sure to only upload your own performances.
*   Ensure that each performance is recorded and uploaded once.
*   If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
*   This form is designed to be used by performance artists to document and showcase their work. If you are not a performance artist, please do not use this form.
