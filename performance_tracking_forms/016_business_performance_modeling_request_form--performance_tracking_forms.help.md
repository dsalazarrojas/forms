# Business Performance Modeling Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Performance Modeling Request Form is used to collect information about a company's performance and the objectives of a business performance modeling project. This information will guide the modeling process and help to ensure that the results meet the client's expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct options for each field, and provide the required information.
2. Make sure to answer all the required fields (marked as `required: true`).
3. If a field is not required, feel free to add any additional information that will help to provide better results.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter the full legal name of the company.
* **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, required): Select the primary industry sector of the company.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, optional): Enter the number of employees in the company.
* **Years Established** (`years_established`, required): Enter the number of years the company has been operating.
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_name`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person for the project.
* **Contact Title or Position** (`contact_title`, required): Enter the title or position of the contact person.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, required): Enter the business email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the contact person.
* **Modeling Objectives** (`modeling_objectives`, required): Select all applicable objectives for the project, such as Revenue Forecasting, Cost Optimization, Market Analysis, etc.
* **Business Goals for Next 12 Months** (`business_goals`, optional): Enter specific targets or outcomes for the next 12 months.
* **Types of Data Available** (`available_data`, required): Select all the types of data you have available, such as Financial Statements, Sales Data, Customer Demographics, etc.
* **Data Time Period Available** (`data_time_period`, optional): Select the time period for which you have data available.
* **Documents Available for Review** (`documents_available`, optional): Select all the documents that are available for review.
* **Project Start Date** (`project_start_date`, optional): Select the desired start date for the project.
* **Desired Completion Timeline** (`completion_timeline`, optional): Select the desired completion date for the project.
* **Additional Notes or Requirements** (`additional_notes`, optional): Enter any special considerations or requirements for the project.

## Tips

* Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure the best results.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* If this form is not meeting your needs, please let us know and we will work with you to create a custom solution.
