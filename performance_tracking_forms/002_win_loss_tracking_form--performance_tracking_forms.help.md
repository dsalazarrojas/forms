# Win Loss Tracking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Win Loss Tracking Form is a tool used to record and analyze the outcome of business processes. It helps teams to identify successes and failures, understand their performance, and make data-driven decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the details of the win or loss.
2. Choose the outcome (win or loss).
3. Select the category of the process (Sales, Product, Service).
4. Enter the salesperson responsible for the process.
5. Enter any additional comments or details about the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Win Loss Date
* **win_loss_date** (`date`, Required/Optional): Enter the date of the process.
### Win Loss Time
* **win_loss_time** (`time`, Required/Optional): Enter the time of the process.
### Win Loss Outcome
* **win_loss_outcome** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Choose whether the outcome was a win or a loss.
### Win Loss Category
* **win_loss_category** (`select_one`, Required/Optional): Select the category of the process (Sales, Product, Service).
### Win Loss Salesperson
* **win_loss_salesperson** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter the salesperson responsible for the process.
### Win Loss Pipeline
* **win_loss_pipeline** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or details about the process.
### Win Loss Description
* **win_loss_description** (`text`, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the process.
 
## Tips
* Fill out the form for every win and loss to ensure accurate records and data analysis.
* Review and update the form regularly to reflect changes in the team's performance.
* Use the data collected from this form to make informed decisions and identify areas for improvement.
