# Remote Team Performance Assessment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Team Performance Assessment Form is designed to collect data on remote team member performance, including feedback and assessment information. This form helps teams track and evaluate team member performance, identify areas of improvement, and provide constructive feedback for growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the team member's name in the "Team Member Name" field.
2. Select the performance period from the calendar in the "Performance Period" field.
3. Enter the date of the assessment in the "Assessment Date" field.
4. Enter your feedback score for the team member in the "Feedback Score" field.
5. Provide written feedback in the "Feedback Text" field.
6. Enter your name in the "Manager Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Manager Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Manager Phone" field.
9. Enter the name of the team manager in the "Team Manager Name" field.
10. Enter the team member's title in the "Team Member Title" field.
11. Enter the team member's department in the "Team Department" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Member Name** (`team_member_name`, text, required): This field is used to identify the team member being assessed.
* **Performance Period** (`performance_period`, number, optional): Select the performance period for which the assessment is being made.
* **Assessment Date** (`assessment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the assessment.
* **Feedback Score** (`feedback_score`, number, optional): Enter your feedback score for the team member (e.g., 1-5).
* **Feedback Text** (`feedback_text`, text, optional): Provide written feedback for the team member.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the manager.
* **Manager Email** (`manager_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as the manager.
* **Manager Phone** (`manager_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as the manager.
* **Team Manager Name** (`team_manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team manager.
* **Team Member Title** (`team_member_title`, text, optional): Enter the team member's title.
* **Team Department** (`team_department`, text, optional): Enter the team member's department.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the team member's performance is evaluated fairly and effectively. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your team manager or HR representative for assistance.
