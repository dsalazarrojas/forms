# Student Daily Work Hours Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record and track a student's work hours for the day. It helps to provide accurate and transparent information about their work hours, which can be reviewed by instructors or administrators for monitoring and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form once a day, at the end of your work hours.
* Select the tool you were assigned to work with from the list provided.
* Enter the start and end times of your work hours.
* If you worked more than 8 hours, enter the total number of hours worked.
* Provide any additional notes or comments about your work hours, if needed.
* Indicate whether you submitted this form or need to be reviewed by an administrator.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool you were assigned to work with from the list provided. If you were not assigned a tool, select "Other".
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of your work hours. Use 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00 for 8:00 AM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of your work hours. Use 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM).
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, number, required): If you worked more than 8 hours, enter the total number of hours worked. For example, if you worked from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, enter "8".
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes or comments about your work hours, if needed.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you submitted this form on your own or need it to be reviewed by an administrator.
