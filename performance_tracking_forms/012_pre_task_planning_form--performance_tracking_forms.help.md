# Pre Task Planning Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Pre Task Planning Form is used to collect and organize information about tasks before they are executed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the task ID, which is a unique identifier for the task.
2. Provide a brief description of the task.
3. Choose the priority level of the task (High, Low, or Medium).
4. Select the team member responsible for completing the task.
5. Enter the start and end times for the task.
6. Add any additional notes about the task.
7. Enter the client's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
8. Enter the project name, description, and ID (if applicable).
9. Enter the team name, description, and phone number (if applicable).
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### first_name
* **First Name** (text): Enter the name of the person who will be performing the task.

### last_name
* **Last Name** (text): Enter the last name of the person who will be performing the task.

### date_of_birth
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the date of birth of the person who will be performing the task. (Note: This field is not commonly used in this context, but it's included for completeness.)

### email
* **Email** (email): Enter the email address of the person who will be performing the task or the client.

### phone
* **Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the person who will be performing the task or the client.

### task_id
* **Task ID** (number): Enter the unique identifier for the task.

### task_description
* **Task Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the task.

### task_priority
* **Task Priority** (select_one): Choose the priority level of the task (High, Low, or Medium).

### assigned_to
* **Assigned To** (select_one): Select the team member responsible for completing the task.

### start_time
* **Start Time** (time): Enter the start time of the task.

### end_time
* **End Time** (time): Enter the end time of the task.

### notes
* **Notes** (note): Add any additional notes or comments about the task.

### client_name
* **Client Name** (text): Enter the name of the client.

### client_email
* **Client Email** (email): Enter the email address of the client.

### client_phone
* **Client Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the client.

### project_name
* **Project Name** (text): Enter the name of the project.

### project_description
* **Project Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the project.

### team_name
* **Team Name** (text): Enter the name of the team.

### team_phone
* **Team Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the team.

### team_description
* **Team Description** (text): Enter a brief description of the team.

### project_id
* **Project ID** (number): Enter the unique identifier for the project.

### created_at
* **Created At** (date): This field is automatically populated and displays the date the task was created.

### updated_at
* **Updated At** (date): This field is automatically populated and displays the date the task was last updated.

### deleted_at
* **Deleted At** (date): This field is automatically populated and displays the date the task was deleted.

### submitted_at
* **Submitted At** (date): This field is automatically populated and displays the date the task was submitted.
