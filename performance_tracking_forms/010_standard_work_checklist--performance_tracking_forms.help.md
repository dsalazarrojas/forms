# Standard Work Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standard Work Checklist is a form used to track the completion status of tasks and activities at the start and end of each shift. It helps ensure that essential tasks are performed and documented consistently across shifts and teams. This form is used to provide a snapshot of the current state of work at the beginning and end of a shift, allowing for efficient tracking and quality control.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the Standard Work Checklist, follow these steps:

1. **Start of Shift Checklist**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether each task is completed at the start of the shift.
2. **End of Shift Checklist**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether each task is completed at the end of the shift.
3. **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or observations relevant to the shift.
4. **Shift Duration**: Enter the duration of the shift in hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Start of Shift Checklist**: (`start_of_shift_checklist`, select_multiple, optional)
    + Indicates whether tasks were completed at the start of the shift.
* **End of Shift Checklist** (`end_of_shift_checklist`, select_multiple, optional)
    + Indicates whether tasks were completed at the end of the shift.
* **Notes**: (`notes`, note, optional)
    + Enter any additional comments or observations relevant to the shift.
* **Shift Duration** (`shift_duration`, number, optional)
    + Enter the duration of the shift in hours.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and update the checklist regularly to ensure accurate tracking and quality control.
* Use the Notes field for any additional information that may not fit in the other fields.
* If a task is completed partially, select 'No' and provide additional details in the Notes field.
