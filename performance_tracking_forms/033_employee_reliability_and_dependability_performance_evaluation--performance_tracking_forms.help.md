# Employee Reliability And Dependability Performance Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Employee Reliability And Dependability Performance Evaluation" form is designed to help evaluate employees' reliability, dependability, and overall performance. It is primarily used to gather data on employee behaviors, habits, and performance, which can be utilized for internal HR purposes or as part of performance evaluation procedures in various organizations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Answer all required fields truthfully and thoroughly.
3. Ensure that you fill in the correct information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Reliability** (`employee_reliability`, `number`, required: false): This field is an optional numerical field where you can provide feedback on the employee's reliability. Please use a scale where 1 represents a low rating and 5 a high rating.
* **Manager Feedback** (`manager_feedback`, `text`, required: true): In this field, please provide a brief written description of your feedback regarding the employee's reliability and dependability.
* **Ratings** (`ratings`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose any of the provided options (Good, Fair) to express your assessment of the employee's performance in the given areas.
* **Trends** (`trends`, `note`, required: false): If you have any notable trends or observations regarding the employee's performance, please describe them in this field. This field is optional.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, `date`, required: true): Please enter the date of the evaluation.

## Tips
* Always fill out the form accurately and honestly to ensure its purpose is met.
* If you need help, do not hesitate to reach out to the HR department for assistance.
* Remember that this form is for internal use only.
