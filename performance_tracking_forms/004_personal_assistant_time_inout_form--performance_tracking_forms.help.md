# Personal Assistant Time InOut Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect employee information for time tracking and performance assessment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Employee ID number.
2. Select the Start Date and Start Time of your work period.
3. Enter the End Date and End Time of your work period.
4. Enter the number of Hours Worked.
5. Enter any additional Notes or comments about your work.
6. Enter any More Notes or comments.
7. Enter any More Additional Notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, number, optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you started working.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you started working.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you ended working.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you ended working.
* **Hours Worked** (`hours_worked`, number, optional): Enter the total hours you worked.
* **Rendered Hours** (`rendered_hours`, number, optional): Enter the total hours rendered.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any comments or notes about your work.
* **More Notes** (`notes_1`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **More Additional Notes** (`notes_2`, text, optional): Enter any more additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Double-check the employee ID number to ensure accuracy.
* Be as specific as possible when entering dates and times.
* Enter accurate hours worked to reflect your actual work hours.
* Use the "Notes" field for any comments or comments that are not captured by other fields.
