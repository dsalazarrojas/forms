# Performance Appraisal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Appraisal Form is used to assess and document employee performance. It helps managers evaluate an employee's strengths, weaknesses, and areas for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the employee's name from the list of options.
2. Enter the job title and performance rating, using numbers 1-5.
3. Provide feedback on the employee's performance.
4. Offer recommendations for future improvement.
5. Describe the next steps the employee and/or manager should take.
6. Calculate the overall score based on the performance rating.
7. Enter the date and time the form was completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee** (Select One):
	+ This field allows you to select the employee's name.
	+ Choose the correct name from the provided options (Jimmy, John, or Jane).
* **Job Title**:
	+ Enter the employee's job title.
	+ Provide a brief description of their job responsibilities.
* **Performance Rating** (1-5):
	+ Use numbers 1-5 to rate the employee's performance.
	+ A higher rating indicates better performance.
* **Feedback**:
	+ Provide specific, constructive feedback on the employee's performance.
	+ Focus on strengths and weaknesses, as well as areas for improvement.
* **Recommendations**:
	+ Suggest specific actions the employee and/or manager can take to improve performance.
	+ Keep recommendations concrete and actionable.
* **Next Steps**:
	+ Describe the next steps for the employee and/or manager to take.
	+ Outline goals and objectives for future development.
* **Overall Score** (1-5):
	+ Calculate the overall score based on the performance rating.
	+ A higher score indicates better overall performance.
* **Date Completed**:
	+ Enter the date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed**:
	+ Enter the time the form was completed.
	+ Use 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
