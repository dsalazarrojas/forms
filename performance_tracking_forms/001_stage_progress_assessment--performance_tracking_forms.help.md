# Stage Progress Assessment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Progress Assessment form is designed to track and evaluate participant's progress and performance at different stages of a program or training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These include:
	* `program_name`: The name of the program or training being assessed.
	* `stage_name`: The name of the stage being assessed.
	* `participant_name`: The name of the participant being assessed.
	* `trainer_rating`, `manager_rating`, `educator_rating`, and `other_rating`: Ratings of the participant's performance, with options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
	* `comments`: Any additional comments or feedback on the participant's performance.
	* `rating_scale`: Select all relevant ratings from the list provided.
2. Optionally, provide additional information in the `participant_email`, `participant_phone`, `date`, and `time` fields, if applicable.
3. Enter a rating for the participant in the `trainer_rating` field.
4. Enter a rating for the participant in the `manager_rating` field.
5. Enter a rating for the participant in the `educator_rating` field.
6. Enter a rating for the participant in the `other_rating` field.
7. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the `participant_comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the program or training being assessed.
* **Stage Name** (`stage_name`): Enter the name of the stage being assessed.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter the name of the participant being assessed.
* **Trainer's Rating** (`trainer_rating`): Rate the participant's performance with the options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
* **Manager's Rating** (`manager_rating`): Rate the participant's performance with the options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
* **Educator's Rating** (`educator_rating`): Rate the participant's performance with the options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
* **Other's Rating** (`other_rating`): Rate the participant's performance with the options: Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback on the participant's performance.
* **Rating Scale** (`rating_scale`): Select all relevant ratings from the list provided.
* **Participant's Email** (`participant_email`): Enter the participant's email address, if applicable.
* **Participant's Phone** (`participant_phone`): Enter the participant's phone number, if applicable.
* **Date** (`date`): Enter the date of assessment, if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`): Enter the time of assessment, if applicable.
* **Participant's Comments** (`participant_comments`): Enter any additional comments or feedback from the participant, if applicable.
