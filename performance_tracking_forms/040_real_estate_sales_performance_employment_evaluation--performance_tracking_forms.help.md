# Real Estate Sales Performance Employment Evaluation - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Sales Performance Employment Evaluation form is used to evaluate the sales performance of real estate employees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct category for the employee's work (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
2. Select the time period for which the evaluation is being made (6, 12, 18, or 24 months).
3. Enter the employee's name.
4. Enter the date of the evaluation.
5. Enter the number of sales made during the evaluation period.
6. Enter the sales target for the employee.
7. Enter a score for the employee's performance.
8. Provide comments about the employee's performance.
9. If applicable, enter the next evaluation date.
10. Enter the name of the employee's broker.
11. Enter the name of the employee's manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (id: 5, type: text, required): Enter the name of the employee being evaluated.
* **Category** (id: 2, type: select_multiple, required): Select the category of the employee's work (Residential, Commercial, or Other).
* **Date** (id: 3, type: date, required): Enter the date of the evaluation.
* **Time** (id: 4, type: time, required): Enter the time period for which the evaluation is being made (6, 12, 18, or 24 months).
* **Sales Performance** (id: 7, type: number, required): Enter the number of sales made during the evaluation period.
* **Sales Target** (id: 8, type: number, required): Enter the sales target for the employee.
* **Evaluation Score** (id: 9, type: number, required): Enter a score for the employee's performance (likely a numerical value out of 100).
* **Evaluation Comments** (id: 10, type: note, required): Provide comments about the employee's performance.
* **Next Evaluation Date** (id: 11, type: date, optional): Enter the date of the next evaluation (if applicable).
* **Broker Name** (id: 12, type: text, required): Enter the name of the employee's broker.
* **Manager Name** (id: 13, type: text, required): Enter the name of the employee's manager.
