# gap_analysis_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gap analysis form is designed to help identify and measure gaps between current performance and desired performance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the first page with any initial information or observations that can aid in identifying gaps in performance.
2. On the subsequent pages, select the relevant options that best describe your current status and performance goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (optional)**: The title for this gap analysis form is not required, but if provided, it will aid in organizing and tracking submissions.
* **First_page_text** (`first_page_text`, `text`, optional): Enter any initial information or observations that may be helpful in identifying gaps in performance.
* **Performance goal** (`performance_goal`, `number`, optional): Enter the desired performance goal or target value.
* **Gap Description** (`gap_description`, `text`, optional): Describe the gap between current and desired performance in detail.
* **Analysis** (`analysis`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant options that describe the gap in performance.
* **Current Status** (`current_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the current status of the gap, which will guide the next steps to be taken.
* **Future Status** (`future_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the desired future status of the performance gap.
* **Priority** (`priority`, `note`, optional): Enter any priority information or details that need to be considered.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date when the gap analysis was performed or when the desired performance is expected.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when the gap analysis was performed or when the desired performance is expected.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your contact email for any follow-up or updates on the gap analysis.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up or updates on the gap analysis.
* **Submitted** (`submitted`, `time`, optional): Enter the time when the gap analysis was submitted.

Note: Some fields are not required, but providing accurate information will aid in better understanding of performance gaps. If you have any doubts or uncertainties, please contact the relevant authorities for clarification. 

Tips: 
* Please be concise and clear in your submissions.
* If you have any supporting documents or attachments, please upload them with your submission. 

Please fill out this form carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or need further clarification.
