# Athlete Annual Performance Report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Athlete Annual Performance Report" form is designed to capture comprehensive evaluation of an athlete's performance over the course of a season or year. This form is completed by coaches or evaluating staff members to provide accurate and actionable feedback for the athlete's development.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Provide Basic Information**
	* Select the season or year for this report.
	* Enter the athlete's full name.
	* Enter your name as the evaluating coach or staff member.
2. **Evaluating Performance Metrics**
	* Rate the athlete's attendance score (percentage or score representing attendance for the season).
	* Evaluate the athlete's technical skills, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
	* Evaluate the athlete's tactical awareness, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
	* Evaluate the athlete's physical fitness, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
3. **Additional Comments**
	* Provide any additional comments or recommendations for the athlete's development.
4. **Next Season's Goals**
	* Enter any short-term objectives or goals for the athlete's development for the next season.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **athlete name**: Enter the full name of the athlete being evaluated.
* **season year**: Enter the year or season for this report.
* **coach name**: Enter your name as the evaluating coach or staff member.
* **attendance score**: Rate the athlete's attendance score (percentage or score representing attendance for the season).
* **technical skills**: Evaluate the athlete's technical skills, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
* **tactical awareness**: Evaluate the athlete's tactical awareness, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
* **physical fitness**: Evaluate the athlete's physical fitness, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
* **mental attributes**: Evaluate the athlete's mental attributes, choosing from "Excellent," "Good," "Satisfactory," or "Needs improvement."
* **goals for next season**: Enter any short-term objectives or goals for the athlete's development for the next season.
* **overall comments**: Provide any additional comments or recommendations for the athlete's development.
* **evaluation date**: Enter the date the evaluation was completed.
* **evaluator name**: Enter your name as the person completing this report.

## Tips

* Ensure the season year is correctly selected for accurate reporting.
* Be specific when evaluating performance metrics for accurate feedback.
* Use the additional comments section for any relevant feedback or recommendations.
* Enter your name as the person completing the report for accountability.
