# Employee Self Evaluation And Performance Goals Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for employees to evaluate their own performance and set their performance goals for improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the evaluation period for which you are submitting this form.
2. Select your performance metrics (e.g., Revenue, Customer Satisfaction, Team Productivity).
3. Choose your employee name, manager name, and reviewer name (these are optional but recommended for accuracy).
4. In the performance goals section, select the goals you are targeting (e.g., Goals, Objectives, Initiatives).
5. In the job description field, provide a brief description of your current job duties and responsibilities.
6. In the performance metrics field, enter any metrics used to measure your performance (e.g., Revenue, Customer Satisfaction, Team Productivity).
7. Finally, set realistic and achievable performance goals for the selected metrics.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): This field allows you to enter your employee name for reference purposes.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of your manager who will be reviewing your performance.
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): This field is for the name of the person reviewing your performance (usually your manager).
* **Job Description** (`employee_job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your current job duties and responsibilities.
* **Performance Goals** (`performance_goals`, text, optional): Select the goals you are targeting (e.g., Goals, Objectives, Initiatives).
* **Performance Goals Form** (`employee_performance_goals_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple performance goals (e.g., Goals, Objectives, Initiatives).
* **Performance Metrics** (`performance_metrics`, select_one, required): Choose one metric to measure your performance (e.g., Revenue, Customer Satisfaction, Team Productivity).
* **Evaluation Period** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation period (e.g., Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when your performance will be evaluated.
* **Evaluation Date** (`evaluation_period`, select_one, required): Select the evaluation period (e.g., Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).

## Tips
* Be as specific as possible when describing your job duties and performance goals.
* Choose performance metrics that align with your job role and responsibilities.
* Set achievable and realistic goals that you can accomplish within the evaluation period.
* Review and edit your form before submission to ensure accuracy.
