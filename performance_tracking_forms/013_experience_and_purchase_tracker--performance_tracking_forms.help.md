# Experience And Purchase Tracker - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experience And Purchase Tracker form is used to collect information about a customer's purchase and experience with our company. The form includes fields for customer contact information, purchase details, and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "First Time User" if this is the customer's first time using our product or service.
2. Provide a brief description of the customer's experience with our company.
3. Rate the customer's experience using the experience_rating field.
4. Enter the customer's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and address.
5. Enter the purchase information, including the product purchased, purchase amount, and purchase date.
6. Add any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
7. Enter the sales representative and sales manager who handled the sale.
8. Enter any other relevant information that may be helpful for future sales interactions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Time User**: Select "Yes" if this is the customer's first time using our product or service, "No" otherwise.
* **Customer Experience**: Enter a brief description of the customer's experience with our company.
* **Experience Rating**: Rate the customer's experience using a numerical scale (e.g., 1-5).
* **Contact Information**: Enter the customer's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Purchase Information**: Enter the product purchased, purchase amount, and purchase date.
* **Product Information**: Enter any additional details about the product or service purchased.
* **Purchase Amount**: Enter the total purchase amount.
* **Purchase Date**: Enter the date of the purchase (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Purchase Time**: Enter the time of the purchase (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Customer Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback from the customer.
* **Team Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback from the sales team.
* **Other Information**: Enter any other relevant information that may be helpful for future sales interactions.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's name.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of the sales team member who handled the sale.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the customer's email address.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number.
* **Customer Address**: Enter the customer's address.
* **Sales Rep**: Enter the name of the sales representative who handled the sale.
* **Sales Manager**: Enter the name of the sales manager who oversaw the sale.
* **Customer ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the customer.
* **Purchase Order ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the purchase order.
* **Order Date**: Enter the date of the order (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Sales Date**: Enter the date of the sale (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Sales Time**: Enter the time of the sale (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Other Comments**: Add any additional comments or feedback from the sales team.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your input for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your supervisor or the sales team.
