# Creative Networking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Creative Networking Registration form is designed to collect basic information from individuals interested in networking with our creative community. This form helps us gather essential details to facilitate meaningful connections and interactions within our network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your registration details (if you have any).
2. Provide your Attendee Name.
3. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your Phone number for any follow-up conversations.
5. Briefly share your event details, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Details** (Registration Details, text, optional):
  Enter any previous registration details you'd like to share.
- **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, optional):
  Please fill in your full name as you'd like it to be displayed within the network.
- **Email** (Email, email, required):
  Enter a valid email address for communication and updates.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, optional):
  Enter your primary phone number for follow-up calls or discussions.
- **Event Details** (Event Details, text, optional):
  If you're registering for an event, please provide a brief description of the event you're interested in or have participated in.

## Tips
- Make sure your email address is correct, as we'll use it for communication purposes.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
