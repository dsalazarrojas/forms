# PPMT Print Competition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "PPMT Print Competition Form" is designed for artists and creators to submit their work for a competition or exhibition. This form gathers essential information about the participant, their artwork, and payment details to facilitate a smooth and organized process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** and **Email** fields with your contact details.
2. Provide your **Phone** number for further communication.
3. Fill in the **Title of Piece** and **Description** of your artwork.
4. Enter the **Price** of your artwork, if applicable.
5. Upload a photo or image of your artwork to showcase it.
6. Select your preferred **Payment Gateway** (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Authorize, Square).
7. Check the **Confirm** boxes for the competition rules you agree to.
8. Finally, submit your application by clicking the **Submit Button**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form
* **Registration Form**: This is the first page of the form where you can fill in your registration details.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Entry Form**: This section is for additional details about your entry, if applicable.

### Contact Information
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you.

### Artwork Details
* **Title of Piece**: Enter a brief title of your artwork.
* **Description**: Provide a short description of your artwork.
* **Price**: Enter the price of your artwork, if applicable.
* **Upload**: Upload a photo or image of your artwork to showcase it.

### Payment Information
* **Price**: Enter the price of your artwork, if applicable.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select your preferred payment method from the options provided (e.g., Stripe, PayPal, Authorize, Square).
* **Submit Button**: Click this button to submit your application.

### Confirm
* **Confirm**: Check the boxes for the competition rules you agree to.

### Date
* **Date**: This field is not currently used, but is left as a field for future reference or other uses.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correct formatting.
* Choose your preferred payment method and make sure to follow the competition rules.
* Be sure to submit your application before the competition deadline.
