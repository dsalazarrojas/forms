# product_anniversary_celebration_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather RSVP information for a product anniversary celebration. Please use this form to provide your event details, attendee information, number of guests, meal preferences, and any dietary restrictions you may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the Event Details section.
2. If applicable, provide Attendee Information for each member of your group.
3. Enter the number of guests attending.
4. Select your meal preferences.
5. If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: This field is where you should provide a brief description of your plans for the event. Please try to be as specific as possible.
* **Attendee Information**: Please list the name(s) of any other attendees who will be joining you, including their names, phone numbers, and any other relevant details.
* **How many guests are attending?**: Please enter the total number of guests who will be joining you.
* **Meal Preferences**: Select which meal options you would like to have at the event. You may select one or multiple options.
	+ **Grilled Chicken**: Grilled chicken is an option for those who enjoy a good grilled meal.
	+ **Salad**: For those who prefer a lighter option, a salad is available.
	+ **Vegetarian Option**: This option is suitable for those with vegetarian dietary preferences.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them here. This will help us plan for your needs.
