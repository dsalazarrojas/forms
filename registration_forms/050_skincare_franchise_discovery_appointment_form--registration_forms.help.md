# Skincare Franchise Discovery Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for individuals interested in learning more about the skincare franchise opportunity. By filling out this form, you will be scheduling a discovery appointment with a representative to discuss your interests and answer any questions you may have about the franchise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

*   Enter your First Name in the field marked "First Name".
*   Enter your Last Name in the field marked "Last Name".
*   Enter your Email address in the field marked "Email".
*   Enter your Phone number in the field marked "Phone".
*   Enter your physical Address (optional, but recommended for follow-up visits).
*   Select a Date and Time for your appointment.
*   Enter any additional Notes you'd like to share with the representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you about your appointment.
*   **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter a phone number that we can call you at to confirm your appointment.
*   **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your street address or physical location for follow-up visits.
*   **Date** (Date, date, required): Select a date for your appointment. Please choose a date and time that works best for you.
*   **Time** (Time, time, required): Select a time for your appointment. Please note that we will call you at the time you've selected.
*   **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with the representative about your interests and questions about the franchise. 

## Tips
*   Please enter accurate information in each field to ensure smooth communication and appointment scheduling.
*   Be sure to select a date and time that works for you, as we will be calling you at that time.
*   If you are unable to complete the form, please reach out to our support team for assistance.
