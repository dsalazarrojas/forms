# Create Account - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about the user for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your password in the "Password" field. This should be a strong, unique password.
5. Enter your confirmed password in the "Confirm Password" field. This should match the password entered in the previous field.
6. Select your country from the dropdown list.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Enter your date and time of birth in the "Date of Birth" and "Time of Birth" fields, respectively. Use the YYYY-MM-DD and HH:MM format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Enter a strong, unique password.
- **Confirm Password** (`password_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your password by re-entering it here.
- **Country** (`country`, select_one, optional): Select your country from the list.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth.
