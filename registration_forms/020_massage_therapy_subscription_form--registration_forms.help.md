# Massage Therapy Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Massage Therapy Subscription Form to schedule and pay for regular massage therapy sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the client information section with your details.
2. Choose your subscription plan (e.g., Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
3. Select the frequency of your massage sessions (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly).
4. Choose the duration of each session (e.g., 15, 30, 45, 60 minutes).
5. Select your preferred payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Check Payment).
6. Enter the payment amount (if applicable).
7. Provide your email address and phone number for communication.
8. Schedule your appointment by selecting a date and time for your massage sessions.
9. Add any additional notes or service requests.
