# CAD Design Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to subscribe to a CAD design subscription service. It collects information about the user's contact details, industry, experience level, CAD software expertise, primary use for CAD designs, subscription plan, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your name and email address in the first two fields.
2. Select the correct industry and experience level from the dropdown menus.
3. Choose the CAD software you are proficient in from the list.
4. Select the primary use for your CAD designs.
5. Choose your subscription plan.
6. Enter your billing address, city, and state (or province).
7. Select the payment method (e.g., credit card, PayPal, etc.).
8. Agree to the terms and conditions at the end.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your full name.
* **Company Name** (Optional):
	+ A text field for you to enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your contact number.
* **Industry or Field** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the industry you work in (e.g., Architecture, Engineering, etc.).
* **CAD Experience Level** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select your skill level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, etc.).
* **CAD Software Experience** (Required):
	+ A list of select boxes for you to choose the CAD software you use (e.g., AutoCAD, SolidWorks, etc.).
* **Primary Use for CAD Designs** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the primary use for your CAD designs (e.g., Professional Projects, Personal Projects, etc.).
* **Subscription Plan** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select your subscription plan (e.g., Basic - Monthly, Professional - Monthly, etc.).
* **Billing Address** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your billing address.
* **City** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your city.
* **State or Province** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (Required):
	+ A text field for you to enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Payment Method** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select the payment method (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Auto-Renewal Preference** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select whether you want to auto-renew your subscription (Yes, No).
* **Special Features or Requirements** (Optional):
	+ A text field for you to add any special requirements or needs.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (Required):
	+ A dropdown menu to select whether you agree to the terms and conditions (Yes, No).

Note: Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
