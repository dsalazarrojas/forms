# Placement Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Placement Registration Form is used to collect and store information about users who are seeking or have found employment opportunities. This form is designed for internal registration processes within an organization or company, specifically for HR or talent management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal and job-related information as required.
2. Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter your contact name, number, and email address.
* **Placement Details** (`placement_details`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide a brief description of your placement or job opportunity.
* **Placement Status** (`placement_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Select the current status of your placement (pending, approved, or denied).
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, `date`, required: false):
    Enter your graduation date if applicable.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Select your current employment status (employed, seeking employment, unemployed, self-employed).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter your job title or position.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the name of the company related to your job opportunity.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, required: false):
    Provide a detailed description of your job opportunity.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the location where the job opportunity is available.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Select the type of job (full-time, part-time, freelance).
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_multiple`, required: false):
    Select the category of your job opportunity (software development, marketing, customer service, sales, finance, design, human resources).
* **Job URL** (`job_url`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the URL of the job posting or listing.
* **Job ID** (`job_id`, `number`, required: false):
    Enter a unique ID for your job opportunity.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, `select_one`, required: false):
    Select the status of your job opportunity (active, inactive, deleted).
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, required: false):
    Attach a copy of your resume or CV.
* **Job Address** (`job_address`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the address of the job location.
* **Job City** (`job_city`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the city where the job opportunity is available.
* **Job State** (`job_state`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the state where the job opportunity is available.
* **Job Zip** (`job_zip`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the zip code of the job location.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the contact name of the person related to the job opportunity.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required: false):
    Enter the contact number of the person related to the job opportunity.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false):
    Enter the email address of the person related to the job opportunity.

## Tips

* Ensure that you enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* Review the form for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
* Contact your internal stakeholders or team leaders if you have any questions or concerns about the form.
