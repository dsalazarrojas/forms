# New Vendor Registration Request Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a new vendor with our company. It collects essential contact information and vendor details to help us understand your business and consider your partnership request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your company name in the "Vendor Name" field.
2. Provide your contact name and details in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Specify your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your vendor category from the provided options in the "Vendor Category" field.
6. If you have a website, enter the URL in the "Website" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your company or the name by which you would like to be known.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name or the name of the person responsible for this registration request.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Vendor Category** (`vendor_category`, `select_one`, optional): Select one of our pre-defined categories to describe your services: General Services, IT Solutions, Marketing, or Software Development.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, optional): Enter your company's website URL, if applicable.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate information to help us evaluate your partnership request efficiently.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us using the provided email address.
* We will review and respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.
