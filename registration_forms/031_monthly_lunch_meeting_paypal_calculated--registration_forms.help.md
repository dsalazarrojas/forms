# Monthly Lunch Meeting Paypal Calculated - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help users determine the type of meeting (on-site or online) and calculate the corresponding price for the monthly lunch meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of meeting you want to hold (on-site or online) using the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the price for the on-site meeting.
3. Enter the price for the second on-site meeting.
4. Enter the calculated price.
5. Enter the price for online meetings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration form** (`Registration form`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This field allows you to select whether the meeting will be held on-site or online.
* **on_site_price** (`on_site_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price for the on-site meeting.
* **on_site_price_2** (`on_site_price_2`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the second price for the on-site meeting.
* **calculated_price** (`calculated_price`, `number`, required/optional): This field will display the calculated price after you select the meeting type and enter the on-site prices.
* **online_price** (`online_price`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the price for online meetings.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct meeting type (on-site or online) to get accurate pricing.
* If you select online meeting, ignore the on-site price fields.
