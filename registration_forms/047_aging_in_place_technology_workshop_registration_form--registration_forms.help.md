# Aging In Place Technology Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Aging In Place Technology Workshop Registration Form" is designed to gather information from individuals interested in attending an aging in place technology workshop. The form is used to collect demographic information, technology experience, and workshop preferences to tailor the workshop content and accommodations to each attendee's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the "Registrant Full Name," "Registrant Email," and "Registrant Phone" fields, respectively.
2. Select your relationship to the senior attending the workshop from the "Relationship to Senior" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your preferred workshop date from the "Preferred Workshop Date" dropdown menu.
4. Select your preferred attendance format from the "Attendance Format" dropdown menu (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
5. If attending in-person, choose your preferred location from the "Location Preference" dropdown menu (Community Center, Library, Senior Center, or Virtual).
6. Indicate your current level of technology comfort from the "Current Technology Comfort Level" dropdown menu (Beginner, Intermediate, Comfortable, or Advanced).
7. Select which devices you currently use regularly from the "Devices Currently Used" dropdown menu (Smartphone, Tablet, Computer, Smart TV, Voice Assistant, Medical Alert Device, or None).
8. Choose your primary goals for using technology to age in place from the "Primary Technology Goals" dropdown menu (Emergency Response, Medication Reminders, Social Connection, Health Monitoring, Home Automation, Safety and Security, or Other).
9. Specify any specific technologies you are interested in learning about in the "Specific Technology Interests" text field.
10. If you have any accessibility requirements, please indicate them in the "Accessibility Requirements" text field.
11. If you require any special accommodations, please specify them in the "Special Accommodations" text field.
12. If you have dietary restrictions, please indicate them in the "Dietary Restrictions" text field.
13. If someone will be attending with you for support, select "Yes" in the "Additional Support Person" dropdown menu and provide their name and relationship in the "Support Person Details" text field.
14. Provide any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Full Name (1)** (`text`, required): Please enter your full name, including first and last names.
* **Registrant Email (2)** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and confirmations about the workshop.
* **Registrant Phone (3)** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you for any follow-up questions or updates.
* **Relationship to Senior (5)** (`select_one`, required): Choose how you are related to the senior attending the workshop, such as "I am the senior" or "Spouse."
* **Workshop Title (7)** (`text`, required): Enter the title of the aging in place technology workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Preferred Workshop Date (8)** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred workshop date from the available options.
* **Attendance Format (9)** (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred attendance format, such as "In-Person," "Virtual," or "Hybrid."
* **Location Preference (10)** (`select_one`, required/optional): If attending in-person, select your preferred location, such as "Community Center" or "Library."
* **Current Technology Comfort Level (12)** (`select_one`, required): Indicate your current level of comfort with using technology, such as "Beginner," "Intermediate," "Comfortable," or "Advanced."
* **Devices Currently Used (13)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select which devices you use regularly, such as "Smartphone," "Tablet," "Computer," or "None."
* **Primary Technology Goals (15)** (`select_multiple`, required): Choose your primary goals for using technology to age in place, such as "Emergency Response," "Medication Reminders," or "Social Connection."
* **Specific Technology Interests (16)** (`text`, optional): Specify any specific technologies you are interested in learning about.
* **Accessibility Requirements (17)** (`text`, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, please indicate them in this field.
* **Special Accommodations (18)** (`text`, optional): If you require any special accommodations, please specify them in this field.
* **Dietary Restrictions (19)** (`text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, please indicate them in this field.
* **Additional Support Person (20)** (`select_one`, required): If someone will be attending with you for support, select "Yes."
* **Support Person Details (21)** (`text`, optional): If you have selected "Yes," provide the name and relationship of the support person.
* **Additional Comments (22)** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your registration.
