# Artist Management Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Management Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect and store artist management information. This form is used to gather essential details about the artist's contact information, professional background, and management agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Artist Management Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the artist's name in the **Artist Name** field.
2. Provide the artist's **Email Address** and **Phone Number** accurately.
3. If applicable, enter the artist's **Legal Name**.
4. Enter the artist's **Date of Birth** and **Management Agreement Start Date** in the respective fields.
5. Specify the **Management Duration** in months.
6. Enter the percentage of **Management Commission**.
7. Select the artist's **Business Structure** (Sole Proprietor, LLC, Corporation, Partnership, or Other).
8. Enter the artist's **Tax Identification Number** (SSN, EIN, or equivalent).
9. Select the **Required Managerial Duties** (Booking Agents, Contract Negotiation, Financial Management, Social Media Management, Publicity and Marketing, Scheduling, Licensing and Rights).
10. Enter the artist's **Primary Genre or Discipline**.
11. List the artist's **Current or Upcoming Projects**.
12. Select the artist's **Revenue Streams** (Performances, Recording Sales, Streaming, Endorsements, Teaching, Merchandise).
13. Enter the artist's **Business Objectives and Goals**.
14. Specify the **Expectations from Manager**.
15. Enter the **Manager's Name** and **Email Address**.
16. Select **Yes** or **No** for the **I Agree to the Management Terms**.
17. Enter the **Signature Date**.

* **Artist Name** (required): Enter the artist's full name.

* **Tax Identification Number** (optional): Enter the artist's tax identification number (SSN, EIN, or equivalent).
* **Required Managerial Duties** (required): Select the managerial duties required for the artist.
* **Primary Genre or Discipline** (required): Enter the artist's primary genre or discipline.
* **Current or Upcoming Projects** (optional): List the artist's current or upcoming projects.
* **Revenue Streams** (optional): Select the revenue streams for the artist.
* **Business Objectives and Goals** (required): Enter the artist's business objectives and goals.
