# Group Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Check In Form is designed for groups or teams to check in during meetings or events. It collects contact and other relevant information from users within the group.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Input** field with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your **Group Information** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide your **Check In Information** in the field provided.
4. Enter your **Contact Information**, including any necessary details.

- **User Input** (text, required/optional: false):
  This field is where you can enter your name or any other relevant information you'd like to provide.
- **Group Information** (text, required/optional: false):
  Enter your group's name or any other relevant details about your group.
- **Check In Information** (text, required/optional: false):
  Provide any necessary check-in information, such as your role within the group or your attendance status.
- **Contact Information** (text, required/optional: false):
