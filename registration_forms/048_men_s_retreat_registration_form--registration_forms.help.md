# Men's Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect necessary information from men attending the retreat, including contact details, medical conditions, and payment methods. It will help us provide a safe and inclusive space for everyone during the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with accurate and complete information about yourself.
2. Make sure to provide your email address and phone number for emergency contact purposes.
3. Specify your dietary restrictions and preferences to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
4. Choose your payment method and provide the necessary information (e.g., bank account number for online payment).
5. Provide any special requests or comments you may have.
