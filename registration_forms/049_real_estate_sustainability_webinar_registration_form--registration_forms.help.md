# Real Estate Sustainability Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Sustainability Webinar Registration Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending a webinar about real estate sustainability. This form helps us ensure we have accurate contact information, attendee roles, and event details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Registration" page.
2. Fill in any information you have available for the "Registration" page fields.
3. Click on the "Contact Information" page.
4. Fill in your contact information, including your email address and any other details you feel comfortable sharing.
5. Select your role for attending the webinar from the provided options.
6. Click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration** (1): This is the first page of the form where you can enter any relevant details about your interest in the webinar.
* **Contact Information** (2): This page is where you enter your contact details, such as your email address and any other information you'd like to share with us.
* **email** (3): Please enter your email address so we can contact you with further instructions and details about the event.
* **phone** (4): You may enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted via phone.
* **name** (5): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for the event.
* **role** (6): Choose your role for attending the webinar from the options provided.
* **company** (7): If you'd like to associate your registration with a company, please enter it here.
* **job_title** (8): If you'd like to specify a job title related to your attendance, please enter it here.
* **location** (9): If you'd like to specify a location related to your attendance, please enter it here.
* **event_date** (10): Please enter the date you'd like to attend the webinar.
* **event_time** (11): Please enter the time you'd like to attend the webinar.
* **event_duration** (12): This field is currently not being used, but if you'd like to specify a duration for your attendance, please enter a numerical value.
