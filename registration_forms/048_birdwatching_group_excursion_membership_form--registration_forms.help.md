# Birdwatching Group Excursion Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for those interested in joining the Birdwatching Group Excursion Membership program. It collects essential details, birdwatching experience, and preferences for group excursions to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Street Address, City, State, ZIP Code, and Date of Birth.
2. Provide details about your birdwatching experience, including how many years you have been birdwatching, your equipment owned, and your target species or groups.
3. Select your preferred excursion options, including the type of membership you wish to join, your maximum travel distance, availability, and preferred communication method.
4. Confirm your payment information, including payment method and membership fee.
5. Acknowledge and agree to the group policies and rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Your email address for contact and newsletters.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact and event planning.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, required): Your complete street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): The city you reside in.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): The state you reside in.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Your ZIP code for mail delivery.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Your date of birth for membership eligibility.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): The full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): The phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, note, optional): Select any additional membership options you wish to join.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Choose from Individual Annual, Family Annual, Senior Annual, Student Annual, Single Excursion, Trial Membership, or other options.
* **Membership Year** (`membership_year`, number, required): The year of your membership.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_heard_about_group`, select_one, optional): Select from where you heard about our group.
* **Birdwatching Experience** (`birdwatching_experience`, select_one, required): Choose your level of birdwatching experience from beginner to expert.
* **Years of Birdwatching** (`years_of_birdwatching`, select_one, required): Choose how many years you have been birdwatching from less than 1 year to 10+ years.
* **Equipment Owned** (`equipment_owned`, select_multiple, required): Select all birdwatching equipment you own.
* **Binocular Specifications** (`binocular_specifications`, text, optional): If you have binoculars, provide specifications.
* **Excursion Preferences** (`excursion_preferences`, note, optional): Describe your preferences for group excursions.
* **Preferred Excursion Times** (`preferred_excursion_times`, select_multiple, required): Select all times you prefer for group excursions.
* **Preferred Excursion Types** (`preferred_excursion_types`, select_multiple, required): Select all excursion types you prefer.
* **Maximum Travel Distance** (`travel_distance`, select_one, required): Choose your maximum travel distance for group excursions.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Select all days you are available for group excursions.
* **Physical Ability** (`physical_ability`, select_one, required): Choose your physical ability for group excursions.
* **Birding Interests** (`birding_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all birding interests that interest you.
* **Target Species or Groups** (`target_species`, text, optional): List any target species or groups you are interested in.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): Choose your interest in volunteering.
* **Volunteer Areas** (`volunteer_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all volunteer areas that interest you.
* **Skills to Share** (`skills_to_share`, text, optional): List any skills you can contribute to the group.
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, note, optional): Describe your preferred contact methods.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred contact method.
* **Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, required): Choose whether to receive our newsletter.
* **Email Updates** (`email_updates`, select_one, required): Choose whether to receive email updates.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, optional): Select whether to join our social media groups.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, note, optional): Describe your payment method and details.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Membership Fee** (`membership_fee`, number, required): Enter the amount of your membership fee.
* **Financial Assistance** (`financial_assistance`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you need financial assistance.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Describe any dietary restrictions you have for group meals.
* **Transportation Needs** (`transportation_needs`, select_one, required): Choose whether you need or can offer transportation.
* **Personal Goals** (`group_goals`, text, optional): Describe any personal goals you have for joining the group.
* **Agreements** (`agreements`, note, optional): Read and agree to group policies and rules.
* **Code of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Agree to follow the group's code of conduct.
* **Liability Waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Agree to acknowledge the risks associated with group excursions.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, select_one, required): Choose whether to allow the use of your photos.
* **Membership Signature** (`membership_signature`, select_one, required): Sign to confirm your membership agreement.
* **Membership Date** (`membership_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your application.

Please take your time to fill in your details accurately, and don't hesitate to ask if you have any questions or need further clarification on any field.
