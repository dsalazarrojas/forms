# Organizational Optimization Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for the Organizational Optimization Conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your session choice(s) for the conference on the first page.
2. Enter your attendee details, including first name, last name, and email address on the second page.
3. Enter your phone number and any special requirements on the third page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session 1** (`session_1`, select_one, required): Choose the session you would like to attend from the dropdown list.
	+ This field is used to select one of the three available sessions for the conference.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Enter your attendee details in this field.
	+ This field is used to capture any additional information about the attendee.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
	+ This field is used to capture the first name of the attendee.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
	+ This field is used to capture the last name of the attendee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is used to capture the email address of the attendee.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (optional).
	+ This field is used to capture the phone number of the attendee.
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more sessions you would like to attend from the list.
	+ This field is used to select multiple sessions for the attendee.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special requirements for the attendee.
	+ This field is used to capture any special requirements for the attendee.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your registration is complete.
* Please note that the conference organizers may contact you to clarify any missing information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the conference organizers.
