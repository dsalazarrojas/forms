# Intermediate Honors String Ensemble Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering students and their parents for an Intermediate Honors String Ensemble performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid email address for the student and teacher in their respective fields.
3. Select the instrument you will be playing in the ensemble.
4. Choose your grade level (if applicable).
5. Enter the name of your school.
6. Choose your section and ensemble type (e.g., Jazz, Classical, Pop).
7. Select the performance date and time.
8. Enter your student ID number.
9. If your parent is not listed as a contact, please provide their name.
10. Choose your ensemble placement (e.g., A, B).
