# Editable Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Editable Information Form is a registration form designed to collect personal and team-related information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form by entering the required information for each field.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields, as indicated by the asterisk (*) symbol next to the field label.
3. Enter your email address to receive a confirmation email.
4. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (First name, text, **required**): Enter your given name.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, **required**): Enter your surname.
* **Email address** (Email address, email, **required**): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency contact first name** (Emergency contact first name, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their first name.
* **Emergency contact last name** (Emergency contact last name, text, **optional**): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their surname.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, select_one, **required**): Select your relationship with the emergency contact person (Parent, Spouse, Child, Other).
* **Primary phone** (Primary phone, text, **optional**): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Secondary phone** (Secondary phone, text, **optional**): Enter your secondary phone number.
* **Primary phone extension** (Primary phone extension, text, **optional**): Enter your primary phone extension.
* **Communication preference** (Communication preference, select_one, **required**): Select how you would like to be communicated with (Email, Text message, Phone call, Postcard).
* **Language** (Language, select_one, **required**): Select your preferred language.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, **optional**): Enter any additional information you would like to include.
* **School or organization** (School or organization, select_multiple, **required**): Select the school or organization you are affiliated with (School A, School B, School C).
* **Team name** (Team name, text, **required**): Enter your team name.
* **Team type** (Team type, select_one, **required**): Select the type of team you are part of (Sports, Academic, Other).
* **Team level** (Team level, select_one, **required**): Select the level of team you are part of (Elementary, Middle School, High School, College).
* **Relationship** (Relationship, select_one, **required**): Select your relationship with your team lead.
* **Emergency contact phone** (Emergency contact phone, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency contact address** (Emergency contact address, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency contact city** (Emergency contact city, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact person's city.
* **Emergency contact state** (Emergency contact state, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact person's state.
* **Emergency contact zip** (Emergency contact zip, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact person's zip code.
* **Emergency contact country** (Emergency contact country, text, **required**): Enter your emergency contact person's country.
* **Team lead** (Team lead, text, **required**): Enter your team lead's name.
* **Team lead email** (Team lead email, text, **required**): Enter your team lead's email address.

## Tips

* Ensure you have filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Verify your emergency contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your team information carefully before submitting.
