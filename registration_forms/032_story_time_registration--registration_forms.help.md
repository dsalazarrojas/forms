# story_time_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "story_time_registration" form is used to register for the Story Time activity, ensuring accurate headcount and correct group allocation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "group_name" field.
2. Indicate whether you plan to attend the Story Time session by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "story_time_registrations" field.
3. Select the time you plan to arrive at the session in the "story_time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **group_name** (group_name, text, required/optional): Enter your name to identify yourself for the session.
* **story_time_registrations** (story_time_registrations, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" to indicate your attendance and "No" if you are not planning to attend.
* **story_time** (story_time, time, required/optional): Choose the time you plan to arrive at the session. This will help the organizers with headcount and planning.

## Tips
* Ensure you're registering correctly to avoid duplication of effort.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form.
