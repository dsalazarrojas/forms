# health-care-workforce-inclusion-forum-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals who want to join the healthcare workforce inclusion forum. The purpose of this form is to ensure that all registered members have a consistent and accurate representation of their information for the forum's internal management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field (format: <user@example.com>).
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them in the **Accessibility Needs** field.
6. Choose whether you consent to receive communications from the healthcare workforce inclusion forum by selecting **True** or **False** in the **Consent for Communications** field.
7. Describe the event details in the **Event Details** field.
8. Enter your role in the healthcare workforce in the **Role** field.
9. Enter your organisation and Job Title in the **Organisation** and **Job Title** fields respectively.
10. Enter the location of your job in the **Job Location** field.
11. Enter the department you belong to in the **Department** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself in the forum.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself in the forum.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format <user@example.com>.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Consent for Communications** (`consent_for_communications`, select_one, required): Choose whether you consent to receive communications from the healthcare workforce inclusion forum.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Enter the event details if relevant.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role in the healthcare workforce.
* **Organisation** (`organisation`, text, optional): Enter your organisation name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your job.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
