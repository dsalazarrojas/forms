# Potty Training Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for the potty training course.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required information and any optional information you feel is relevant.
2. Make sure to complete all fields accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`Enter Parent Name`, text, **required**): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Child Name** (`Enter Child Name`, text, **required**): Please enter your child's name.
* **Parent Email** (`Parent Email`, email, **required**): Please enter your email address.
* **Child Age** (`Enter Child Age`, number, **required**): Please enter your child's age.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, **required**): Please enter your child's date of birth.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, **required**): Please enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, **required**): Please enter the end date of the course.
* **Parent Phone** (`Parent Phone`, text, **required**): Please enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, **optional**): Please enter the name of an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, **optional**): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`Relationship`, select_one, **optional**): Please select your relationship to the child (e.g. Mom, Dad, Grandparent, Guardian, Other).
* **Medical Issues** (`Medical Issues`, select_multiple, **optional**): Please indicate if your child has any medical issues.
* **Medical Notes** (`Medical Notes`, note, **optional**): Please provide any additional medical notes or concerns.
* **Allergy List** (`Allergy List`, text, **optional**): Please list any allergies your child may have.
* **Medical Alert** (`Medical Alert`, text, **optional**): Please provide any other relevant medical alerts.
* **Special Instructions** (`Special Instructions`, text, **optional**): Please provide any special instructions or requests.
