# social_entrepreneurship_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a social entrepreneurship workshop. Please fill out the form completely to ensure proper registration and workshop preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred workshop date in the "Workshop Date" field.
6. Select your Participant Motivation from the "Participant Motivation" dropdown menu.
7. Select any accessibility needs you may have from the "Accessibility Needs" dropdown menu.
8. Choose your Participant Type from the "Participant Type" dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Select the tool(s) you have been assigned to use during the workshop from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
11. Select the output file(s) you will use during the workshop from the "Output File" dropdown menu.
12. Enter your Category in the "Category" field.
13. Enter a brief description of your project in the "Description" field.
14. For each participant, select the tools you have been assigned to use during the workshop from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
15. For each participant, select the output file(s) you will use during the workshop from the "Output File" dropdown menu.
16. Select any accessibility needs for each participant from the "Accessibility Needs" dropdown menu.
17. Select the participant type for each participant from the "Participant Type" dropdown menu.
18. Select the Form ID(s) for each participant from the "Form ID" dropdown menu.
19. Finally, review and submit your registration form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Choose your preferred workshop date.
* **Participant Motivation** (`participant_motivation`, select_one, required): Select why you are participating in the workshop (University/Research Institution, Community Group/Network, NGO/Non-Profit).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, required): Select any accessibility needs you may have (Wheelchair, Hearing Impaired, Visual Impaired, Other).
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required): Select your type (University/Research Institution, Community Group/Network, NGO/Non-Profit).
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select the tool(s) you have been assigned to use during the workshop (chatjimmy, Other).
* **Output File** (`output_file`, select_one, required): Select the output file(s) you will use during the workshop (023 social entrepreneurship workshop registration--registration forms, Other).
* **Category** (`category`, text, optional): Enter your category.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your project.
