# Repair And Reuse Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals sending in items for repair and reuse. This form will help you provide the best possible service by understanding the details of the item being sent in and your preferences for repair or reuse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Registration Form** (page 1) with your details.
2.  Upload any required **File Upload Form** (page 2) attachments if applicable.
3.  Describe your **Repair Request** (page 3) in the given field.
4.  Select your preferred **Service Type** (page 4) from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form** (page 1, **title**: Registration Form, **type**: text, **required**: no): Please fill in your name, contact information, and any other relevant details for our team to reach out to you.
- **File Upload Form** (page 2, **title**: File Upload Form, **type**: text, **required**: no): If you have any supporting documents or images, please upload them here. This will help us better understand your repair request.
- **Repair Request** (page 3, **title**: Repair Request, **type**: text, **required**: no): Describe your repair request in detail. Please provide as much information as possible about the item and its condition.
- **Service Type** (page 4, **title**: Service Type, **type**: select_one, **required**: no): Please select your preferred service type from the given options: Service, Repair, or Reuse. This will help us determine the best course of action for your item.
