# Workforce Housing Manager Contact Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Housing Manager Contact Form is a tool for external parties to get in touch with the housing manager for workforce housing options and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field, if applicable.
3. Enter a message in the "Message" field, describing your interest in workforce housing options.
4. Select how you heard about us from the options provided in the "How did you hear about us?" field.
5. Choose the options you are interested in learning more about from the "What are you interested in?" field.
6. Select a date for your inquiry, if applicable.
7. Review and submit the form to send your message to the housing manager.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Form**: A text field where you can enter any additional information or comments.
* **Name**: A text field for entering your name.
* **Email**: An email field for entering your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field for entering your phone number.
* **Message**: A text field for describing your interest in workforce housing options.
* **How did you hear about us?**: A dropdown menu with options to select how you heard about us. This will help us understand how you came across our workforce housing information.
* **What are you interested in?**: A multi-select dropdown menu where you can choose one or more options that interest you. Select one or more options that apply to you.
* **Date**: A date field for selecting a specific date for your inquiry, if applicable.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you need help or have questions about housing options, this form will get you in touch with our housing manager.
- If you have any further inquiries or follow-up questions, do not fill out another form. Our housing manager will contact you directly.
