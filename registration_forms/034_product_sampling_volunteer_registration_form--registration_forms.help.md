# Product Sampling Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers interested in sampling products and potentially assisting at events. By filling out this form, you will be helping us to understand your availability and preferences, which will enable us to better organize our volunteer program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in the following steps:
	* Provide your volunteer details in the "Volunteer Details" field (required).
	* If you'd like to be contacted, fill out the "Contact Info" field (optional).
	* Select your availability on a weekly basis (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
	* Choose how frequently you can participate in events (e.g., Every 1 week, Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks, Less frequently (specify)).
	* Describe your volunteer preferences (e.g., Sampling products, Assisting at events, Other (specify)).
2. Be sure to click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Details** (<name>): A required field to collect your details as a volunteer.
* **Contact Info** (<name>): An optional field to provide your contact information if you'd like to be contacted for further questions or updates.
* **Availability** (<name>): Select one or more days of the week you are available to volunteer (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday).
* **Event Frequency** (<name>): Choose how frequently you can participate in events (e.g., Every 1 week, Every 2 weeks, Every 4 weeks, Less frequently (specify)).
* **Volunteer Preference** (<name>): Describe your volunteer preferences (e.g., Sampling products, Assisting at events, Other (specify)).

## Tips
* Be honest about your availability and preferences to ensure we can best match you with volunteer opportunities.
* If you have any special requests or concerns, don't hesitate to mention them in the "Volunteer Preference" field.
* We appreciate your interest in volunteering and look forward to reviewing your application!
