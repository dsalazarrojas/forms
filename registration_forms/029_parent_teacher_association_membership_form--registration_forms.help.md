# Parent Teacher Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents and guardians to register their children for the Parent-Teacher Association membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form instructions carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Enter your name and contact information in the parent information section.
3. Enter your child's information in the student information section.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **parent_name** (Text, Required: false): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **email** (Email, Required: false): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **phone_number** (Text, Required: false): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **membership_type** (Select One, Required: false): Choose from the options to indicate how you would like to be involved with the PTA.
* **other_membership_type** (Text, Required: false): If you have a specific membership type not listed in the options, please enter it here.
* **address** (Text, Required: false): Enter your address for contact and communication purposes.
* **parent_school_year** (Date, Required: false): Enter the current school year your child is in.
* **emergency_contact_name** (Text, Required: false): Enter the name of an emergency contact person for your child.
* **relationship_to_parent** (Text, Required: false): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (Text, Required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_email** (Email, Required: false): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_address** (Text, Required: false): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **child_name** (Text, Required: false): Enter your child's name.
* **child_grade** (Text, Required: false): Enter your child's grade level (e.g., Kindergarten, 1st, 2nd, etc.).
* **child_school** (Text, Required: false): Enter your child's school name.
* **student_id** (Number, Required: false): If your child has a student ID, enter it here.
* **student_birthdate** (Date, Required: false): Enter your child's birthdate.
* **student_grade** (Select One, Required: false): Choose your child's grade level from the options.
* **student_gender** (Select Multiple, Required: false): Choose your child's gender from the options.
* **student_year** (Select One, Required: false): Choose your child's year from the options.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have the necessary information about the student before completing the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the PTA for assistance.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accurate and complete information.
