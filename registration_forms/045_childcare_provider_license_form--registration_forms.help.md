# Childcare Provider License Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childcare Provider License Form is a document designed to collect information from childcare providers seeking to obtain or renew a license to operate a childcare facility. This form helps the childcare licensing agency assess the applicant's qualifications and suitability for a license.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure all fields with a red star (*) are marked as required.
3. Review your answers carefully to ensure they accurately reflect your facility's information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Date**: Date of the license application (format: MM/DD/YYYY)
* **Applicant First Name**: Legal first name of the applicant
* **Applicant Last Name**: Legal last name of the applicant
* **Applicant Title**: Select your title or role (e.g., Owner, Director, Administrator)
* **Applicant Email**: Primary email address of the applicant
* **Applicant Phone**: Primary phone number of the applicant
* **Organization Name**: Name of the childcare organization
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you represent (e.g., Family Childcare Home, Group Childcare Home, Preschool)
* **Facility Address**: Full street address of the facility
* **City**: City where the facility is located
* **State**: State or province where the facility is located
* **Zip Code**: Postal or zip code of the facility
* **Facility Phone**: Phone number for the facility
* **Facility Email**: Email address for the facility
* **License Type Requested**: Select the type of license you are requesting (e.g., Initial, Renewal, Provisional)
* **Age Groups to Serve**: Select all age groups you plan to serve (e.g., Infants (0-12 months), Toddlers (1-2 years))
* **Proposed Capacity**: Maximum number of children you plan to serve
* **Hours of Operation**: Planned hours of operation for your facility
* **Days of Operation**: Select the days you will operate (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday)
* **Facility Ownership**: Select how you own or lease the facility (e.g., Own, Lease, Rent)
* **Facility Square Footage**: Total square footage of the facility
* **Outdoor Play Space**: Select if you have outdoor play space (e.g., Yes, on-site, Yes, off-site nearby)
* **Outdoor Space Square Footage**: Square footage of outdoor play space (if applicable)
* **Staff Count Planned**: Number of staff members you plan to employ
* **Director Qualifications**: Describe the director's qualifications
* **Compliance with Regulations**: Select if you will comply with all state and local regulations
* **Previous License History**: Select if you have ever held a childcare license before
* **License History Details**: Provide details about previous license history (if applicable)
* **Background Checks Completed**: Select if all required background checks have been completed
* **Health and Fire Safety Inspections**: Select if health and fire safety inspections have been completed
* **Liability Insurance**: Select if you have liability insurance
* **Insurance Provider**: Name of the insurance provider (if applicable)
* **Additional Programs Offered**: List any additional programs or services offered
* **Application Fee Payment**: Select how you will pay the application fee (e.g., Credit card, Debit card, Check enclosed)
* **Applicant Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature
* **Signature Date**: Date of electronic signature

Please note that fields with an asterisk (*) are required. Ensure you answer all required fields accurately and completely to avoid delays in the licensure process.
