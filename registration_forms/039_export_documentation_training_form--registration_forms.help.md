# Export Documentation Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Export Documentation Training Form is designed to collect information from participants after a training session, which will be used for evaluation, feedback, or follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the participant_name field.
2. Enter your participant email in the participant_email field.
3. Enter your participant phone number in the participant_phone field.
4. Enter the session ID, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (Text, Required: false): This field is for entering your name as a participant.
* **participant_email** (Email, Required: false): This field is for entering your email address as a participant.
* **participant_phone** (Text, Required: false): This field is for entering your phone number as a participant.
* **session_id** (Text, Required: false): This field is for entering the session ID, if you are able to provide it.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always ask for assistance.
* The form is meant to be filled out by participants who have attended the training session.
* If you're not a participant, please do not fill out this form.
