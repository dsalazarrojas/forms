# Winter Fitness Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Winter Fitness Challenge Registration form is used to collect information from participants to track and monitor progress throughout the challenge. Please fill out this form to register for the Winter Fitness Challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email (optional).
3. Enter your Phone number (optional).
4. Choose the name of the Challenge you are registering for (required).
5. Select your Fitness Level (required).
6. Check the status of your registration (required).
7. If you have already registered, enter the date and time you registered.
8. Enter any additional Notes about your registration (required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you want to be contacted by us, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number to be contacted.
* **Challenge Name** (`challenge_name`, `text`, required): Choose the correct challenge you are registering for.
* **Fitness Level** (`fitness_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current fitness level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Registered** (`registered`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you have already registered for the challenge.
* **Registered Date** (`registered_date`, `date`, optional): If you have already registered, enter the date you signed up.
* **Registered Time** (`registered_time`, `time`, optional): If you have already registered, enter the time you signed up.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional information about your registration. This can be used to help us understand your progress.
