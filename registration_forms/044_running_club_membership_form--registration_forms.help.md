# Running Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Running Club Membership Form is designed to collect information from users who want to join a running club. This form is used to process membership applications and gather necessary details from prospective club members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Running Club Member Information** section with your personal details.
2. Provide your **Membership Details**, including your membership start and end dates.
3. Enter your **Additional Information** if necessary.
4. Confirm your **Email** and **Phone Number**.
5. Select your **Membership Status** (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
6. Enter your **Monthly Membership Fee**.
7. Confirm your **Membership Start Date**, **Membership End Date**, and **Membership Status**.
8. Enter your **Membership ID** (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Running Club Member Information (required)
* **First Name** (text): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Your last name.

### Membership Details (required)
* **Membership Start Date** (date): The date you would like your membership to start.
* **Membership End Date** (date): The date you would like your membership to end.

### Additional Information (optional)
* **Additional Information** (text): Any additional information about your membership or yourself.

### Confirm Information (required)
* **Confirm Email** (email): Confirm your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (text): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Membership Status** (select_one): Confirm your membership status (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **Confirm Membership Start Date** (date): Confirm your membership start date.
* **Confirm Membership End Date** (date): Confirm your membership end date.
* **Confirm Membership Fee** (number): Confirm your monthly membership fee.
* **Confirm Membership Status** (select_one): Confirm your membership status (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **Confirm Additional Information** (text): Confirm any additional information about your membership or yourself.

### Membership (required)
* **Membership ID** (text): Your membership ID (if you have one).
