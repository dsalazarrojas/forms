# Piano Festival Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the piano festival. It gathers essential information about participants, such as contact details, description, and status, to help manage their registration and engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Participant Name** field with your name as it should be displayed in the festival.
2. Select your **Participant Contact Details** from the dropdown menu: Email or Phone.
3. Provide a brief description of yourself in the **Participant Description** field.
4. Enter your **Participant Phone** number.
5. Enter your **Participant Email** address.
6. Provide any additional **Participant Notes** about your participation.
7. Select your current **Participant Status** from the dropdown menu: Confirmed, Pending, or Not confirmed.
8. Enter the **Participant Registration Date** of your registration.
9. Select the time when you last **Participant Last Seen**.
10. Enter your **Piano Festival Participant ID**.
11. (Optional) Select any additional **Participant Status 2** from the dropdown menu, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed in the festival.
* **Participant Contact Details**: Select your preferred contact method: Email or Phone.
* **Participant Description**: Provide a brief description about yourself.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Participant Notes**: Provide any additional information about your participation.
* **Participant Status**: Select your current status: Confirmed, Pending, or Not confirmed.
* **Participant Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the festival.
* **Participant Last Seen**: Select the time when you last attended or engaged with the festival.
* **Piano Festival Participant ID**: Enter your unique participant ID.
* **Participant Status 2** (Optional): Select any additional status updates, if applicable.
