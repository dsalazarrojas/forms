# School Registration Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the school registration process. It is meant to capture important information from students and parents to update student records and ensure accurate enrollment. Please complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure proper registration.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Provide your current address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the grade level you are currently in from the "Grade Level" options.
7. Select the school year you are currently in from the "School Year" options.
8. Confirm that your parents have provided permission for you to participate in the school program in the "Parent Permission" field.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
10. If applicable, select the tool assigned to you from the "Assigned Tool" field.
11. Enter the category and description of your assigned tool in the "Category" and "Description" fields respectively.
12. Enter the form title and form category in the "Form Title" and "Form Category" fields respectively.
13. Optionally, enter the tool assigned to you in the "Tool" field.
14. Enter the form version in the "Form Version" field.
15. Enter the form version history in the "Form Version History" field.
16. Enter the last updated date and time in the "Last Updated" and "Last Updated Time" fields respectively.
17. Enter the user who last updated the form in the "Last Updated By" field.
18. Optionally, provide feedback or comments on the form in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your current address.
* **Grade Level** (Select One, Required): Select your current grade level.
* **School Year** (Select Multiple, Required): Select the school year you are currently in.
* **Parent Permission** (Select One, Required): Confirm that your parents have provided permission for you to participate.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (Text, Optional): Select the tool assigned to you.
* **Category** (Text, Optional): Enter the category of your tool.
* **Description** (Text, Optional): Enter a description of your tool.
* **Form Title** (Text, Optional): Enter a title for the form.
* **Form Category** (Text, Optional): Enter a category for the form.
* **Tool** (Text, Optional): Enter the tool name.
* **Form Version** (Text, Optional): Enter the form version.
* **Form Version History** (Text, Optional): Enter the form version history.
* **Last Updated** (Text, Optional): Enter the last updated date.
* **Last Updated By** (Text, Optional): Enter the user who last updated the form.
* **Last Updated Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the last updated time.
* **Assigned User** (Text, Optional): Enter the assigned user.
