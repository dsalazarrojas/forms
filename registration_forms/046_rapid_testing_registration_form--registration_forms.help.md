# rapid_testing_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for patients to register for rapid testing. It will help you provide your information and schedule an appointment in a timely and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your patient information on page 1.
2. Provide your contact details, including email and phone number.
3. Schedule an appointment by choosing the test type and date and time that suits you best.
4. Confirm your appointment by providing a confirm date and time.
5. Verify your contact details to ensure we can reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Information** (`patient_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name and any other relevant patient information.
* **Contact** (`contact`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details, including your name and any other relevant information.
* **Schedule Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, text, required: false): Choose a date and time that works best for your appointment.
* **Test Type** (`test_type`, select_multiple, required: false): Select one or multiple test types that you would like to be tested for (e.g., option 1, option 2).
* **Testing Event** (`testing_event`, text, required: false): Please enter the testing event you would like to schedule.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): If you have any additional notes or comments, please provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Choose a date for your appointment.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose a time for your appointment.
* **Confirm Date** (`confirm_date`, date, required: false): Confirm the date of your appointment.
* **Confirm Time** (`confirm_time`, time, required: false): Confirm the time of your appointment.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false): Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Confirm your email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact method.
* We'll get back to you with further instructions or questions once we receive your form.
