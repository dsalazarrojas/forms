# Holiday Crafts Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details for the Holiday Crafts Workshop. It's meant to be a registration form to help us get to know you better and ensure a smooth experience during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration form with your name and any relevant details.
2. Provide participant details, such as your name and contact information.
3. Indicate your permissions for us to use your submitted data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration** (Registration): This is the initial section where you can add any relevant details. Please fill in your name and any other information you'd like to share with us.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details): This section is where you can provide your full name and contact information, such as your email address and phone number. This will help us get in touch with you about the workshop.
* **Permissions** (Permissions): Please let us know if it's okay to use your submitted data. This will help us understand your preferences and ensure your data is handled properly.
