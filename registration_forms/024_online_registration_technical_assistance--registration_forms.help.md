# online_registration_technical_assistance - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather information from users who need technical assistance. It will help you provide the support you need by gathering relevant information about your issue, allowing you to schedule a callback or meeting with a technical support specialist, and provide a convenient way to submit your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the delivery type that best describes your technical issue.
2. Enter a brief description of your issue in the Technical Assistance field.
3. Provide your email address and phone number, so we can follow up with you.
4. If you need to schedule a call-back or meeting, select the date and time that works best for you.
5. Add any additional notes or details about your issue in the Additional Notes field.
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your request for technical assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Delivery Type** (`delivery_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the three options to describe the type of technical assistance you need.
  - Option 1: General Technical Support
  - Option 2: Advanced Technical Support
  - Option 3: Specialized Technical Support
- **Technical Assistance** (`technical_assistance`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your technical issue.
- **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date for a callback or meeting.
- **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time for a callback or meeting.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can follow up with you.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can follow up with you.
- **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional details or notes about your issue.

## Tips
- Make sure to select the correct delivery type that best describes your technical issue.
- Enter a clear and concise description of your issue in the Technical Assistance field.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- If you need to schedule a call-back or meeting, select a date and time that work best for you.
- Use the Additional Notes field to add any additional details or context about your issue.
