# Hair Artist Community Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Hair Artist Community Forum Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address, in the **Contact Information** field.
2. Choose your **Specialties**, by selecting one or more options from the dropdown menu.
3. Indicate whether you **Consent to Guidelines**, by selecting an option from the dropdown menu.
4. Provide your **Contact Details** and any additional information you feel is relevant in the **Additional Information** field.
5. Choose your **Contact Preferences** by selecting one of the options from the dropdown menu.
6. Upload an image to represent you in the **Upload Image** field.
7. Confirm your registration by selecting **Yes** from the dropdown menu in the **Confirm Registration** field.
8. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
9. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
10. Select the **Specialty Areas** you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
11. Enter a password in the **Confirm Password** field.
12. Select **Terms and Conditions** to review and agree to the terms of service.
13. Finally, agree to the **Terms of Service** by selecting **Yes** from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required): This is where you provide your name and email address.
- **Specialties** (`specialties`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more options that represent your area of specialization as a hair artist.
- **Consent to Guidelines** (`consent_to_guidelines`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you agree to abide by the guidelines of the forum.
- **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Provide any relevant contact details.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required): Enter any additional information you feel is relevant about yourself.
- **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_one, required): Choose your contact preferences.
- **Upload Image** (`upload_image`, text, required): Upload a representative image of yourself.
- **Confirm Registration** (`confirm_registration`, select_one, required): Confirm your registration by selecting **Yes** from the dropdown menu.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Specialty Areas** (`specialty_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more specialty areas you are interested in.
- **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, required): Enter your password for confirmation.
- **Password** (`password`, text, required): Enter a password.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Review the terms of service.
- **Agree to Terms** (`agree_to_terms`, select_one, required): Select **Yes** from the dropdown menu to agree to the terms of service.
- **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, note, required): Review the terms of service.
- **Upload Image** (`upload_image`, text, required): Upload an image representing yourself. 

Please note that all fields are required, except where noted as optional. Ensure that you fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
