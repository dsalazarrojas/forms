# veterinary_disaster_preparedness_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps to gather information about your animal's health, contact details, and emergency medical conditions to prepare for potential natural disasters or other emergency situations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form accurately and completely.
2. Provide the most up-to-date information about your pet.
3. Be prepared to answer questions about any health conditions or emergencies your pet may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your pet's owner.
* **Owner Contact** (`owner_contact`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number or any other contact information.
* **Animal Information** (`animal_info`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of your pet.
* **Animal Health** (`animal_health`, number, required: false): Indicate your pet's health status, with 1 being excellent and 5 being poor.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter a contact person's name and phone number.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required: false): Enter a contact person's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required: false): Enter a contact person's email address.
* **Veterinary Clinic** (`veterinary_clinic`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your pet's veterinary clinic.
* **Owner Notes** (`owner_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information about your pet or owner.
* **Animal Type** (`animal_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of animal (cat, dog, horse, or other).
* **Animal Breed** (`animal_breed`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the breeds of your pet (cat, dog, horse, or other).
* **Animal Color** (`animal_color`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the color of your pet (brown, black, white, or other).
* **Animal Size** (`animal_size`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the size of your pet (small, medium, large, or other).
* **Emergency Medical Condition** (`emergency_medical_condition`, select_one, required: false): Select any emergency medical conditions (heart condition, or other).
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, required: false): Enter your address.
* **Owner City** (`owner_city`, text, required: false): Enter your city.
* **Owner State** (`owner_state`, text, required: false): Enter your state.
* **Owner Zip** (`owner_zip`, number, required: false): Enter your zip code.
* **Owner Country** (`owner_country`, text, required: false): Enter your country.
* **Date of Visit** (`owner_date_of_visit`, date, required: false): Enter the date of your last visit to the vet.
* **Time of Visit** (`owner_time_of_visit`, time, required: false): Enter the time of your last visit to the vet.
* **Animal Type** (`animal_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of animal (cat, dog, horse, or other).
* **Vaccinations** (`owner_vaccinations`, number, required: false): Indicate the number of vaccinations your pet has received.
