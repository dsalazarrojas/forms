# Art Gallery Assistant Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather feedback from trainee staff members on their training experience at the art gallery. It is used to evaluate the effectiveness of the training session and provide insights for future improvements.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form with your trainee information and training session details.
- Select the status of each training module completed during the session.
- Evaluate your overall performance and provide feedback on areas of strength and improvement.
- Provide your digital signature as a trainee.
- Provide your trainer's digital signature to confirm the training session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Assistant Name**: Enter your name as a trainee staff member.
* **Date of Training Session**: Enter the date of the training session in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of the trainer who conducted the session.
* **Module 1 - Safe Artwork Handling and Transportation**: Select how you completed this module:
	+ Completed - Proficient: You felt well-prepared and completed the module successfully.
	+ Completed - Needs Review: You struggled with some parts of the module and would like to review it again.
	+ Not Covered: This module was not covered during the training session.
* **Module 2 - Point of Sale (POS) and Sales Processing**: Select how you completed this module:
	+ Completed - Proficient: You felt well-prepared and completed the module successfully.
	+ Completed - Needs Review: You struggled with some parts of the module and would like to review it again.
	+ Not Covered: This module was not covered during the training session.
* **Module 3 - Inventory Management (CRM) and Cataloging**: Select how you completed this module:
	+ Completed - Proficient: You felt well-prepared and completed the module successfully.
	+ Completed - Needs Review: You struggled with some parts of the module and would like to review it again.
	+ Not Covered: This module was not covered during the training session.
* **Module 4 - Visitor Engagement and Gallery Etiquette**: Select how you completed this module:
	+ Completed - Proficient: You felt well-prepared and completed the module successfully.
	+ Completed - Needs Review: You struggled with some parts of the module and would like to review it again.
	+ Not Covered: This module was not covered during the training session.
* **Module 5 - Opening - Closing and Security Procedures**: Select how you completed this module:
	+ Completed - Proficient: You felt well-prepared and completed the module successfully.
	+ Completed - Needs Review: You struggled with some parts of the module and would like to review it again.
	+ Not Covered: This module was not covered during the training session.
* **Overall Training Performance Rating**: Evaluate your overall performance during the training session:
	+ Excellent - Ready for independent shifts: You felt well-prepared and confident in your abilities.
	+ Good - Minor supervision needed: You struggled with some parts of the module, but with minor supervision, you could complete tasks.
	+ Fair - Further training required: You need additional training or supervision to complete tasks.
	+ Unsatisfactory: You were not well-prepared and struggled significantly during the training session.
* **Identified Areas of Strength**: Provide a brief description of your areas of strength during the training session.
* **Areas Requiring Further Development**: Provide a brief description of areas where you would like to improve.
* **Recommended Follow-up Actions**: Suggest any additional training or support you would like to receive.
* **How Confident Do You Feel in Performing Your Gallery Duties?**: Select how confident you feel in performing your gallery duties:
	+ Very Confident: You feel well-prepared and confident in your abilities.
	+ Somewhat Confident: You feel somewhat prepared, but with some uncertainty.
	+ Neutral: You are neutral about your abilities.
	+ Not Confident: You do not feel well-prepared and confident in your abilities.
* **How Clear and Helpful Were the Training Materials?**: Evaluate the clarity and helpfulness of the training materials:
	+ 5 - Extremely Clear: The materials were very clear and well-structured.
	+ 4 - Clear: The materials were clear, but with some minor issues.
	+ 3 - Average: The materials were average and could be improved.
	+ 2 - Below Average: The materials were unclear and confusing.
	+ 1 - Poor: The materials were extremely unclear and not helpful.
* **Trainee Digital Signature (Type Name)**: Enter your digital signature as a trainee staff member.
* **Trainer Digital Signature (Type Name)**: Enter your trainer's digital signature to confirm the training session.

## Tips
- Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
- Make sure to provide clear and concise feedback.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your trainer.
