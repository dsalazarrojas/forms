# University For You Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University For You Client Registration Form is a form designed to collect personal details from clients for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your Email in the "Email" field (ensure it is a valid email).
4.  Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field (in the format of YYYY-MM-DD).
6.  Enter your User ID in the "User ID" field (if applicable).
7.  Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
8.  Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email (Email, required: false)**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* **Phone (Phone, required: false)**: Enter your phone number, including the country code.
* **Date of Birth (Date, required: false)**: Enter your Date of Birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD, where:
  *   YYYY: 4-digit year of birth.
  *   MM: 2-digit month of birth.
  *   DD: 2-digit day of birth.
*   **User ID (User ID, text, required: false)**: If you have a User ID, enter it in this field.
*   **Address (Address, text, required: false)**: Enter your address in the format of "House Number, Street Name, Apartment Number, City, State, ZIP Code".
*   **Zip Code (Zip Code, number, required: false)**: Enter your zip code. Ensure it is in 5-digit format for U.S. postal codes.
