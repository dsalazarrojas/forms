# Halloween Parade Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for registration in the Halloween Parade. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the requested information.
2. Review your entries carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the parade.
* **age** (number, optional): If you're 16 years or older, you can provide your age for age-related purposes.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Date** (date, optional): If you're planning to join a specific date or have a preferred date, select it.
* **Contact Number** (text, optional): Provide your contact number for easy communication.
* **Preferred Parade Time** (select_one, optional): Choose your preferred parade time from the options provided.
* **Special Requests** (select_multiple, optional): Select any special activities you'd like to participate in during the parade.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have for the parade organizers.
* **Note 2** (note, optional): If you have any more notes, you can add them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries before submission.
* If you have any special requests, please include them in the Special Requests field.
* Please review the parade schedule and plan accordingly based on your preferred parade time.
