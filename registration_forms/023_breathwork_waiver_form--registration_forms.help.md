# Breathwork Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Breathwork Waiver Form is a crucial document that helps ensure your safety and well-being during a breathwork session. Please take a few minutes to carefully read and fill out this form before attending the session.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Answer each question honestly and thoroughly, providing accurate information about your health and background.
2.  Use the provided examples as a guide, but feel free to add any additional information that may be relevant.
3.  Double-check your answers to ensure they accurately reflect your current situation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant Full Name (Required)
Participant Full Name: Your full legal name as per your identification documents.

### 2. Email Address (Required)
Email Address: Your email address for contact and communication purposes.

### 3. Phone Number (Required)
Phone Number: Your contact phone number for emergency purposes.

### 4. Date of Birth (Required)
Date of Birth: Your date of birth as per your identification documents.

### 5. Emergency Contact Name (Required)
Emergency Contact Name: The name of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.

### 6. Emergency Contact Phone (Required)
Emergency Contact Phone: The phone number of the person you've chosen as your emergency contact.

### 7. Session Date (Required)
Session Date: The date you're scheduled for the breathwork session.

### 8. Practitioner Name (Optional)
Practitioner Name: The name of the breathwork facilitator you're working with (if known).

### 9. Medical Conditions (Required)
Medical Conditions: Select all the medical conditions that apply to you from the list provided.

### 10. Current Medications (Required)
Current Medications: Choose the category that best describes your current medications.

### 11. List Current Medications (Optional)
List Current Medications: If you've selected any of the above, list your medications in this field.

### 12. History of Breathing or Respiratory Issues (Optional)
History of Breathing or Respiratory Issues: Choose your level of breathing or respiratory issues from the options provided.

### 13. History of Mental Health Concerns (Optional)
History of Mental Health Concerns: Choose your level of mental health concerns from the options provided.

### 14. Previous Breathwork Experience (Optional)
Previous Breathwork Experience: Select your level of breathwork experience from the options provided.

### 15. Known Allergies (Optional)
Known Allergies: List any known allergies or sensitivities you may have.

### 16. Physical Limitations or Injuries (Optional)
Physical Limitations or Injuries: List any current injuries or limitations.

### 17. Intention for This Session (Optional)
Intention for This Session: Describe your goals or expectations for this breathwork session.

### 18. Health and Safety Acknowledgment (Optional)
Health and Safety Acknowledgment: Read and understand the risks associated with breathwork.

### 19. Confirmation of Disclosure (Required)
Confirmation of Disclosure: Confirm that you've accurately disclosed all relevant health information.

### 20. Understanding of Risks (Required)
Understanding of Risks: Acknowledge your understanding of the potential risks of breathwork, including dizziness, lightheadedness, and potential psychological responses.

### 21. Waiver Agreement (Required)
Waiver Agreement: Confirm that you release the practitioner and facility from liability related to the session.

### 22. Participant Signature (Required)
Participant Signature: Sign to confirm your understanding and agreement.

### 23. Date of Signature (Required)
Date of Signature: The date you're signing this waiver.

Please ensure you complete the form accurately and with care to avoid any potential risks or issues during your breathwork session. If you're unsure or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
