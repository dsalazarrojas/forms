# Volleyball Open Gym Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volleyball Open Gym Registration form is used to collect information from participants who want to join the volleyball program. This information helps the program staff to get in touch with the participants and their emergency contacts in case of any issues or emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name, birthdate, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide emergency contact information, including the first name, last name, relationship, and phone number of your emergency contact.
3. Add any additional participant information in the "Participant Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant who is signing up for the program.
* **Birth Date** (`birthdate`, date, required: false): Enter the participant's birthdate in the correct date format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **Session Participation** (`session_participation`, text, required: false): Describe the participant's session participation preferences.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* Make sure to double-check the emergency contact information for correct and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the program staff.
