# Virtual_Hiring_Event_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a virtual hiring event. Fill out the form to provide your contact information and register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If you are representing a company, enter your Company name in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name here. This is the name given to you at birth or the name you prefer to be known as.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. This is the surname given to you at birth or the name you prefer to be known as.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address here that can receive event-related communications.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number here. This will help us get in touch with you if needed.
- **Company**: If you are representing a company, enter its name here. This will help us know that you are registering on behalf of your company.
