# Language Camp Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participant's language camp accommodation preferences. It is meant to be used by participants to book their accommodation for a language camp.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in all the required information, marked with a star (*).
* Select multiple options for "Accommodation Type" as needed.
* Enter dates in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Use the provided options for "Booking Method" and select the correct method.
* Select the correct "Participant Booking Status" and "Participant Language Proficiency" options.
* Enter the participant's address in the "Accommodation Address" field.
* Provide any additional details about the accommodation in the "Accommodation Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Language Camp Accommodation**: This is the title of the form.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be spelled and formatted.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Accommodation Type**: Select the type of accommodation you are booking. You can choose one or more options.
	+ Language Camp Accommodation 1
	+ Language Camp Accommodation 2
	+ Language Camp Accommodation 3
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you want to arrive at the camp.
* **End Date**: Enter the date you want to leave the camp.
* **Accommodation Address**: Enter your address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Accommodation Details**: Provide any additional information about your accommodation.
* **Booking Method**: Select how you are booking your accommodation.
	+ Self Booking
	+ Agency Booking
	+ School Booking
* **Participant Booking Status**: Select your booking status.
	+ Booked
	+ Not Booked
* **Participant Language Proficiency**: Select your language proficiency level.
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Participant Arrival Date**: Enter the date you will arrive at the camp.

Tips:

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
