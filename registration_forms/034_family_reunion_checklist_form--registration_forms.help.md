# Family Reunion Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users provide event details, family information, and other relevant activities for a family reunion. It also allows users to track RSVPs and their status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "Event Details" section with the necessary information about the event.
2. Provide family information in the "Family Information" section.
3. Select the activities that will be included in the event from the "Activity List" dropdown menu.
4. Choose who is planning to attend from the "RSVPs" select menu.
5. Update the RSVP status for each attendee from the "RSVP Status" dropdown menu.
6. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Provide contact information for the event organizer in the "Contact Info" section.
8. Enter the email address of the event organizer in the "Email" field.
9. Enter the phone number of the event organizer in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter the details of the event, such as the name, date, time, and location.
* **Family Information**: Enter any relevant family information, such as names of family members, relationships, and ages.
* **Activity List**: Select all the activities that you think will be included in the event. This can include things like family photo booths, lawn games, and potluck meals.
* **RSVPs**: Choose all the people who are planning to attend the event. This can include family members, friends, and other guests.
* **RSVP Status**: Update the RSVP status for each attendee from "Pending" to "Confirmed" once they have responded to the invitation.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the event or attendees.
* **Contact Info**: Enter any contact information for the event organizer, such as a name, address, and phone number.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
