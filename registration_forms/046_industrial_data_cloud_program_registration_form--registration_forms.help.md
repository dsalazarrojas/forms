# Industrial Data Cloud Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants with the Industrial Data Cloud Program. Please fill out this form to provide us with your organization's information and experience.

## How To Complete This Form
Before completing this form, please make sure you have a valid email address and phone number. If you don't have these, please contact your IT department to obtain them. To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your participant's name and organization.
2.  Provide your email address and phone number.
3.  Select your participant type (yes/no).
4.  Choose the data types you are interested in (yes/no).
5.  Select your participant experience (yes/no).
6.  Choose your industry sector (yes/no).
7.  Choose your company size (yes/no).
8.  Select your company type (yes/no).
9.  Choose your data storage (yes/no).
10.  Select your data provenance (yes/no).
11.  Choose your data quality (good/poor).
12.  Select your industry experience (yes/no).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **required**)
Fill out your participant's name as you want it to appear in our database.

### **Organization** (`organization`, text, **required**)
Enter your organization's name as it should appear in our database.

### **Email** (`email`, email, **required**)
Enter your valid email address.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, **required**)
Enter your phone number.

### **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, **required**)
Select yes or no to indicate your type of participant.

### **Participant Type** (`participant_type_options`, select_multiple, **optional**)
Select multiple options to indicate your type of participant.

### **Data Types** (`data_types`, select_multiple, **optional**)

### **Participant Experience** (`participant_experience`, select_multiple, **optional**)

### **Industry Sector** (`industry_sector`, select_multiple, **optional**)

### **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, **optional**)

### **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_multiple, **optional**)

### **Data Storage** (`data_storage`, select_multiple, **optional**)
