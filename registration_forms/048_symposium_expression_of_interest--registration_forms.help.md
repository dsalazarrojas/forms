# symposium_expression_of_interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in attending or contributing to a symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Select the session topics that interest you.
3. Enter your email address and phone number (if available).
4. Confirm your participant type (e.g., speaker, attendee, staff).
5. Enter your name and affiliation (if applicable).
6. Describe your interest in the symposium.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **main_page**: A text box for you to briefly describe why you are interested in the symposium.
* **participant_details**: A text box for you to provide additional details about yourself.
* **session_topics**:
	+ Select all the session topics that interest you. These will be used to match you with the most relevant talks and sessions.
* **email**: Please enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number (if available).
* **participant_type**: Confirm your participant type (e.g., speaker, attendee, staff).
* **participant_name**: Enter your full name.
* **participant_position**: Enter your position or job title.
* **participant_affiliation**: Enter your affiliation (if applicable).
* **participant_interest**: Describe your interest in the symposium.
* **participant_note**: Use this text box to provide any additional information about your interest in the symposium.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, feel free to reach out to us.
* Please do not repeat the form for the same participant.
