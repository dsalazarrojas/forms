# Process Hazard Simulation Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a Process Hazard Simulation Course, providing essential details for course organization, scheduling, and communication with instructors and participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:
1. Enter the title of the course.
2. Provide your name and contact information (first name, last name, and email address).
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter the date and time of the course.
5. Specify the duration of the course.
6. Enter the venue of the course.
7. Provide the name and contact information of the course instructor.
8. Add any additional course notes or comments.
9. Choose the course status from the available options (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
10. If necessary, provide any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Information** (1):
	* Enter the title of the course, briefly describing its topic and focus.
* **First Name** (2): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (3): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address for contact and correspondence purposes.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Course Date** (6): Enter the date of the course, following the standard date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Course Time** (7): Enter the time of the course, using 24-hour clock format (hh:mm).
* **Course Duration** (8): Enter the duration of the course in hours or minutes.
* **Course Venue** (9): Enter the location or venue where the course will take place.
* **Course Instructor** (10): Enter the full name and contact information of the course instructor.
* **Course Notes** (11): Provide any additional comments or notes about the course.
* **Course Status** (12): Choose one of the three available options for the course status (Approved, Rejected, or Pending).
* **Other** (13): If necessary, provide any other relevant information not covered by the above fields.

## Tips
- Be accurate and thorough in filling out the form to avoid errors and confusion.
- Use standard formats for dates (dd/mm/yyyy) and times (hh:mm) as required.
- Ensure that the instructor's information is complete and up-to-date.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid mistakes.
- If you need more information or clarification, contact the course administrators for assistance.
