# Virtual Presentation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for virtual presentations. It aims to gather details about the presentation, attendee demographics, and registration information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before submitting your information.
2. Enter your responses in the designated fields.
3. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required): Enter the title of the presentation.
* **Presentation Details** (`presentation_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the presentation, including the topic, format, and any other relevant details.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant attendee information.
* **Registration Info** (`registration_info`, text, required): Enter your registration information, including your preferred email address for updates and any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and thorough when filling out the form.
* Double-check your entries before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact us at form@gic.mx.
