# Virtual MUN Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Virtual MUN Registration Form is designed to register delegates for the Virtual MUN conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Delegate Details**: 
  + (text)
  + This section collects your personal details, including your name.
* **School Affiliations**: 
  + (text)
  + Please list any relevant school affiliations.
* **Committee Preferences**: 
  + (select_multiple)
  + Choose the committees you are interested in attending. Select one or more from the options provided.
* **Delegate Name**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter your full name as you want it to appear on the delegate list.
* **Email**: 
  + (email)
  + Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter your phone number.
* **School Name**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter the name of your school.
* **School Address**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter your school's address.
* **School Country**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter your school's country.
* **Position Title**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter your position title as a delegate.
* **Role**: 
  + (select_one)
  + Select your role as a delegate: Deleguee, Debater, Head Delegacy, or Observer.
* **Committee Assignments**: 
  + (note)
  + We will assign you to a committee based on your preference and available slots. Please be prepared for any assignment.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter the number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: 
  + (email)
  + Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: 
  + (text)
  + Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Other Info**: 
  + (text)
  + Enter any other information that we need to know.
* **Terms and Conditions**: 
  + (note)
  + By submitting this form, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to our terms and conditions.
