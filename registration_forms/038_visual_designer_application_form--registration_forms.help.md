# Visual Designer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Visual Designer Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants who are interested in applying for a visual design position. This form is used to gather essential details such as username, email, portfolio, skills, experience, and other relevant information. The form is a crucial step in the application process, ensuring that the applicant has a clear understanding of their qualifications and the position they are applying for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your **Username** in the designated text field. This will be your unique identifier on the platform.
2. Next, enter your **Email** address in the corresponding field. This will be used for communication and will help us to verify your application.
3. Provide a valid **Password** to secure your account.
4. Select your **Portfolio** type by choosing from the dropdown options (False or 1). Please note that this field is required.
5. Choose your relevant **Skills** by selecting multiple options from the list (frontend, backend, ui/ux). This field is optional.
6. If applicable, enter your **Experience** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. If you have a **Resume**, please upload it in the designated text field. This is an optional field.
8. Finally, provide a link to your **Portfolio** if available.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Username**: A unique identifier for your account. Please ensure it's a combination of letters, numbers, and symbols. It should not contain any special characters other than hyphens (-), underscores (_), or periods (.) (8 characters minimum, 16 characters maximum).
* **Email**: A valid email address that will be used for communication and verification purposes.
* **Password**: A secure password to protect your account. It should be a combination of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. Please ensure it's 8 characters minimum, 16 characters maximum.
* **Portfolio**: Select one of the two available options (False or 1) to indicate your portfolio type.
* **Skills**: Choose multiple options from the list to select your relevant skills. This includes frontend, backend, and ui/ux.
* **Experience**: If applicable, enter your experience in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD). Please note that this field is optional.
* **Resume**: If you have a resume, please upload it in the designated text field. This is an optional field.
* **Portfolio Link**: If you have a portfolio link, please provide it in this field. This is an optional field.
