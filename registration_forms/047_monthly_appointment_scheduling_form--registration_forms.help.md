# Monthly Appointment Scheduling Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for monthly appointment scheduling purposes. It's meant to be used to create or edit a schedule for a client or user on a monthly basis.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Date**: Choose the date for the appointment.
2. **Select Time**: Choose the time for the appointment.
3. **Select Start Time**: Choose the start time for the appointment.
4. **Select End Time**: Choose the end time for the appointment.
5. **Select Duration**: Choose the duration of the appointment in units of time.
6. **Select Days**: Choose the days for which the appointment is valid (Yes/No).
7. **Select Frequency**: Choose the frequency of the appointment (Yes/No).
8. **Select Status**: Choose the status of the appointment (Active/Inactive).
9. **Note**: Add any additional notes about the appointment.
10. **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
11. **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
12. **Confirm Phone**: Confirm the client's phone number.
