# Organization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about an organization's registration. It is intended for the organization's representative or owner to complete.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: organization name, address, contact person's name, email address, phone number, number of employees, date of creation, and date of last update.
2. If you want to provide additional information, you can do so in the background information field.
3. Select the correct options for created_by and updated_by fields from the provided list.
4. Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization name (required)**: This is the name of the organization to be registered.
* **Address (required)**: This is the physical address of the organization's location.
* **Contact person (required)**: This is the person who will be the primary contact for the organization.
* **Email (required)**: This is the email address of the contact person or the organization's email address.
* **Phone (required)**: This is the phone number of the contact person or the organization.
* **Background information (optional)**: This is a field for providing additional information about the organization.
* **Number of employees (required)**: This is the number of employees working for the organization.
* **Date of creation (required)**: This is the date when the organization was created.
* **Date of last update (required)**: This is the date when the organization was last updated.
* **Created by (required)**: This is the person or entity that created the organization.
* **Updated by (required)**: This is the person or entity that last updated the organization.
* **Updated date (required)**: This is the date and time when the organization was last updated.
* **Select options for created by and updated by fields**: This is a dropdown menu with options for selecting the person or entity that created and updated the organization.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the organization's representative or owner for clarification.
* The form will not be processed if any of the required fields are left blank.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure it is complete and accurate.
