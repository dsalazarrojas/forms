# Architect Visitor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information for the security, safety, and compliance of the firm. By filling out this form, visitors will be able to register their visit and provide necessary details to the hosts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your full name and email address.
3. Enter your phone number and job title or position.
4. Select the purpose of your visit from the options provided.
5. Specify the host or contact name and department (if applicable).
6. Check the box if you require parking and if necessary.
7. Provide any accessibility or special needs.
8. Select the check-in time of your arrival.
9. Provide emergency contact information (name and phone number).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Visitor Name** (`visitor_name`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company or Organization** (`visitor_company`, optional): If you work for a company or organization, please enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title or Position** (`visitor_title`, optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Visit Date** (`visit_date`, required): Select the date of your visit.
* **Visit Time** (`visit_time`, optional): Select the time of your arrival.
* **Purpose of Visit** (`purpose_of_visit`, required): Select the reason for your visit from the options provided.
* **Host or Contact Name (in firm)** (`host_name`, required): Please specify the name of the host or contact person you are meeting with.
* **Department** (`host_department`, optional): If applicable, enter the department of the host or contact person.
* **Consent to background check** (`background_check_consent`, optional): Select 'True' if you agree to background check, 'False' if not applicable, or 'Not Required' if not required by firm policy.
* **Agree to NDA if required** (`nda_agreement`, optional): Select 'True' if you agree to non-disclosure agreement, 'False' if not applicable, or 'Not Required' if not required by firm policy.
* **Parking Required** (`parking_required`, optional): Select 'True' if you will need parking, 'False' if not necessary.
* **Accessibility or Special Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): Provide any accessibility or special needs.
* **Check-In Time** (`check_in_time`, optional): Select the check-in time of your arrival.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
