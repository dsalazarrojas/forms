# Family Retreat Family Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Retreat Family Application form is used to register for a family retreat program. It's meant to gather information about each family member, including contact details and demographic information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2.  Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3.  Enter your email address in the **Email** field. (Note: This should be a valid email address that you have access to.)
4.  Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5.  Enter your date of birth in the **Date Of Birth** field. (Format: MM/DD/YYYY)
6.  Enter your address in the **Address** field.
7.  If you have children, enter the number of children in the **Number Of Children** field.
8.  If you are an adult, enter the number of adults in the **Number Of Adults** field.
9.  Select your relationship with the primary family member in the **Family Member Relationship** dropdown menu.
10.  Choose the type of retreat your family is applying for in the **Family Retreat Type** field.
11.  Add any additional information or comments in the **Other Notes** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date Of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Address** (text, required): Enter your address.
- **Number Of Children** (number, optional): If you have children, enter the number of children.
- **Number Of Adults** (number, optional): If you are an adult, enter the number of adults.
- **Family Member Relationship** (select one, optional): Select your relationship with the primary family member.
	+ options:
		- Primary Family Member
		- Spouse or Partner
		- Child
		- Extended Family Member
		- Other
- **Family Retreat Type** (select multiple, optional): Choose the type of retreat your family is applying for.
	+ Options:
		- Option 1
		- Option 2
		- Option 3
- **Other Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your family.
