Bridge World Series Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
=============================================

## Purpose
This form is used for teams to register for the Bridge World Series tournament. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your team's name and information in the **Team Information** section.
2. Provide the captain's name, email, and phone number in the **Team Captain** section.
3. Enter the players' names and master points in the **Player Information** section.
4. Select the tournament category and skill level in the **Tournament Details** section.
5. Choose your preferred bidding system and select your payment method in the **Registration Agreement** section.
6. Enter any special requirements or requests and emergency contact information.
7. Confirm your agreement to the tournament rules.
