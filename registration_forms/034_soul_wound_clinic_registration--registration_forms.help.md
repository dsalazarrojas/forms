# Soul Wound Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soul Wound Clinic Registration form is used to collect basic information from patients for registration purposes. This form is necessary to ensure that we have the correct contact details and can schedule appointments correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter a valid Email address for correspondence.
4. Provide your Phone number for scheduling and updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name (Text, Optional)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter a valid email address for clinic communication and updates.
* **Phone (Text, Optional)**: Enter your phone number for scheduling and appointment updates.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly for easy clinic correspondence.
* Enter a phone number that is easily accessible for scheduling and updates.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the clinic staff for clarification.
