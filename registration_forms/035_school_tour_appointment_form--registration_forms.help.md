# School Tour Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Tour Appointment Form is used to schedule a tour of a school for a group of students. This form is designed to collect essential information for planning and executing a successful school tour event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and relevant information to ensure that the school tour event is properly planned.
3. Review and proofread your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **School Tour Appointment Form** 
* This is the title of the form, which is provided for your reference.

### **Event Date** (<event_date>)
* This field is for selecting the date of the school tour event.
* Please select the date on which the tour is scheduled to take place.

### **Event Time** (<event_time>)
* This field is for selecting the time of the school tour event.
* Please select the time when the tour is scheduled to begin.

### **School Name** (<school_name>)
* This field is for entering the name of the school that is being toured.
* Please enter the name of the school that will be visited.

### **Event Duration** (<event_duration>)
* This field is for entering the length of the tour in minutes.
* Please enter the number of minutes the tour is scheduled to last.

### **Event Location** (<event_location>)
* This field is for entering the location of the tour within the school.
* Please enter the specific location of the tour, e.g., "Main Hall".

### **Student Count** (<student_count>)
* This field is for counting the number of students attending the tour.
* Please enter the number of students that will attend the tour.

### **Teacher Name** (<teacher_name>)
* This field is for entering the name of the teacher leading the tour.
* Please enter the name of the teacher who will lead the tour.

### **Teacher Email** (<teacher_email>)
* This field is for entering the email of the teacher leading the tour.
* Please enter the email address of the teacher who will lead the tour.

### **Teacher Phone** (<teacher_phone>)
* This field is for entering the phone number of the teacher leading the tour.
* Please enter the phone number of the teacher who will lead the tour.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your form for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the school administration.
* This form is for scheduling a school tour event. If you have any other questions or concerns, please reach out to the appropriate department.
