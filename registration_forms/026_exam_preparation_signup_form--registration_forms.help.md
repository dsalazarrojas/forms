# exam-preparation-signup-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the exam-preparation-signup-form. This form is used to collect information from students registering for an exam preparation program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in the introduction section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Student Information
* **Enter your name** (text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
*   *Email* (email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
*   *Student phone* (text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.

### Page 2: Exam Details
* **Exam date** (date, required/optional): Select or enter the date of the exam.
*   *Start date* (date, required/optional): Select or enter the start date of the exam preparation program.
*   *End date* (date, required/optional): Select or enter the end date of the exam preparation program.
*   *Exam time* (time, required/optional): Select or enter the time of the exam.
*   *Location* (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the exam.

### Page 3: Scheduling Information
* **Exam duration** (number, required/optional): Enter the duration of the exam in hours.
*   *Exam schedule* (select one, required/optional): Select one of the provided schedule options.
*   *Tutor prophets information* (text, required/optional): Enter any additional information about the tutors.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and relevant information in all fields.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* Please review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Note that some fields are optional, but you may need to provide this information for the exam preparation program to function correctly.
