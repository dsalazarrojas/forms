# Guided Nature Walk RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for users to RSVP to a guided nature walk event. It collects user details, event information, and RSVP specifics to help the event organizers prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user details in the "User Details" field.
2. Provide event details in the "Event Details" field.
3. Enter the date of the guided nature walk in the "Date" field.
4. Select the time of the guided nature walk in the "Time" field.
5. Indicate the number of attendees in the "Attendee Count" field.
6. Write an RSVP message in the "RSVP Message" field.
7. Choose the date you plan to RSVP in the "RSVP Date" field.
8. Enter the location of the guided nature walk in the "Event Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Event Details** (text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the event, including any specific requirements or requests.
* **Date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the guided nature walk.
* **Time** (time, required: false): Choose the time of the guided nature walk.
* **Attendee Count** (number, required: false): Enter the number of attendees, including yourself.
* **RSVP Message** (text, required: false): Enter a message explaining your RSVP, such as any special requests or notes.
* **RSVP Date** (date, required: false): Choose the date you plan to RSVP.
* **Event Location** (text, required: false): Enter the location of the guided nature walk.
