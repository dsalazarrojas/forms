# HR Professional Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help HR professionals register for HR certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
* Please complete this form in its entirety, as required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
* For select-one and select_multiple fields, choose the options that best apply to your situation.
* For text fields, enter your information in the format requested.
* For note fields, provide as much detail as possible.
* When uploading a CV, ensure it is in a compatible format and not larger than 5MB.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Page (\*)**: Select 'Yes' if this is your first time registering for an HR certification, 'No' if you have previously registered.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you with any further questions or updates.
* **Certification Information (\*)**: Select 'Yes' if you have a certification, 'No' if you do not.
* **Professional Certification (\*)**: Select all certifications you hold, separated by commas.
* **Work History**: Provide a brief description of your work history, including any relevant experience or education.
* **Upload CV (\*)**: Upload a copy of your CV in a compatible format, no larger than 5MB.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration information for review.
