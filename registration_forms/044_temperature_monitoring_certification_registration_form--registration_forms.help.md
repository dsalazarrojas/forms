# Temperature Monitoring Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in temperature monitoring certification programs. It is a general-purpose registration form that collects basic participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "Participant First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Participant Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field. Please use a unique email address for your registration.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
5. Enter your company name in the "Participant Company" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "Participant Job Title" field.
7. Select the type of certification you are registering for from the "Participant Course" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date you are registering for in the "Participant Date" field.
9. Enter the time you are registering for in the "Participant Time" field.
10. If necessary, enter any additional notes in the "Participant Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): A text field for any additional comments or information.
* **Participant First Name** (`participant_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Participant Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address. Use a unique email address for your registration.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Participant Course** (`participant_course`, select_one, optional): Select the type of certification you are registering for.
	+ Certified Temperature Monitoring
	+ Certified Refrigeration Monitoring
	+ Certified Energy Monitoring
* **Participant Date** (`participant_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are registering for.
* **Participant Time** (`participant_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are registering for.
* **Participant Note** (`participant_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to use a unique email address for your registration.
* If you have previously registered, use the same email address to update your existing registration.
