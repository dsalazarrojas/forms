# Risk Management Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Risk Management Certification Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who are seeking certification in risk management. This form is used to gather relevant details about the certification process, including the individual's certification status, exam date and time, and payment method. The information collected through this form helps us understand the certification process and ensure that all necessary steps are taken to complete the certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the date and time of the exam in the `Exam Date` and `Exam Time` fields.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the `Full Name`, `Email`, and `Phone` fields, respectively.
3. Check the `Certification` field to indicate whether you have completed the certification.
4. Select the level of your certification in the `Certification Level` field.
5. Check the `Course Completion` field to indicate whether you have completed the course.
6. Enter the date and time of course completion in the `Course Completion Date` and `Course Completion Time` fields, respectively.
7. Select the payment method used for the certification in the `Payment Method` field.
8. Enter any additional payment details in the `Payment Details` field.
9. Enter the payment method ID in the `Payment Method ID` field.
10. Enter the course ID and name in the `Course ID` and `Course Name` fields, respectively.
11. Select the status of the course in the `Course Status` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Start (Date)**: Enter the date you started the certification process.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear on your certification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Certification**: Check this field to indicate whether you have completed the certification.
* **Certification Level**: Select the level of your certification (High or Low).
* **Exam Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the exam.
* **Exam Time (Time)**: Enter the time of the exam.
* **Course Completion**: Check this field to indicate whether you have completed the course.
* **Course Completion Date (Date)**: Enter the date of course completion.
* **Course Completion Time (Time)**: Enter the time of course completion.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment used for the certification.
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional details about the payment.
* **Payment Method ID**: Enter the payment method ID.
* **Course ID**: Enter the course ID.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course.
* **Course Status**: Select the status of the course (Active or Inactive).
