# Liturgy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Liturgy Registration Form is used to register for a liturgy event. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form to provide us with the necessary information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
2. **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it to enable us to contact you more easily.
5. **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date of the event you wish to register for.
6. **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select the time of the event you wish to register for.
7. **Event Name** (`event`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
8. **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
9. **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional information or requests, please include them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A required field for your first name.
* **Last Name**: A required field for your last name.
* **Email**: A required field for your email address.
* **Phone Number**: An optional field for your phone number.
* **Date**: A required field for the date of the event you wish to register for.
* **Time**: A required field for the time of the event you wish to register for.
* **Event Name**: A required field for the name of the event.
* **Location**: A required field for the location of the event.
* **Additional Notes**: An optional field for any additional information or requests you may have.
