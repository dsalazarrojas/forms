# Online Dance Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect online dance class registrations from students. Fill out this form to register for a dance class and provide your details for the instructor to contact you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information, and submit it at the end. All fields are optional, but it's best to fill out as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Submission (Form 1)
* **Form Submission** (`text`, required: false): Please fill in your message if you have any comments or requests to the instructor. This is not a field for general comments, but rather a space to add any specific details about your registration.

### Name (Form 2)
* **Name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your name as you would like it to be referred to by the instructor.

### Email Address (Form 3)
* **Email Address** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address so the instructor can contact you for updates and confirm your registration.

### Contact Number (Form 4)
* **Contact Number** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your contact number where the instructor can reach you for any updates or questions.

### Course (Form 5)
* **Course** (`select_one`, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in taking this course, or 'No' if not.

### Dance Style (Form 6)
* **Dance Style** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Select all dance styles you are interested in learning, or 'No' if none are applicable.

### Date (Form 7)
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to start the course.

### Time (Form 8)
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): Enter the time you would like to attend the course.

### Instructor Notes (Form 9)
* **Instructor Notes** (`note`, required: false): Leave any specific notes for the instructor here, such as prior injuries or experience.

## Tips
- Try to fill out as much information as you can for the instructor to contact you efficiently.
- If you need to add a comment to the instructor, please use Form Submission (Form 1). 
- Make sure to select a course and dance style you are interested in, and that aligns with your preferences. 
- Be aware that submitting this form does not automatically guarantee a spot in the course. 
- If you are unsure about any part of the form, please ask for clarification from the instructor. 

This help guide aims to assist users in understanding each field within the online dance class registration form. Please note that the fields are optional, but providing as much information as possible will help the instructor contact and prepare for your class.
