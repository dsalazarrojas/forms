# Marathon Event Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marathon Event Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from volunteers participating in the marathon event. This form helps the event organizers manage volunteer data, track volunteer roles and shifts, and communicate with volunteers throughout the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the "Full Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
2. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field by selecting "True" or "False" accordingly.
3. Select your time zone from the "Time Zone" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your volunteer role(s) from the "Volunteer Role" field, e.g., Water Station, Aid Station, or Finish Line.
5. Provide a brief description of your volunteer details in the "Volunteer Details" field, if necessary.
6. Specify your availability to volunteer in the "Availability" field.
7. Confirm your availability by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirm Availability" field.
8. Choose your volunteer start date and time in the "Volunteer Start Date" and "Volunteer Start Time" fields, respectively.
9. Specify the duration of your volunteer shift in the "Volunteer Hours" field, if necessary.
10. Select your volunteer shift from the "Volunteer Shift" dropdown menu, e.g., AM, PM, or All Day.
11. If applicable, provide any additional notes in the "Volunteer Notes" field.
12. If assigned by the event organizers, confirm whether you've been assigned a volunteer role by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Confirm Assigned Volunteer" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm that the email address you entered is correct.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone to ensure accurate communication and scheduling.
* **Volunteer Role**: Choose one or more roles you're willing to take on, such as Water Station, Aid Station, or Finish Line.
* **Volunteer Details**: Provide any additional information about your volunteer experience or skills.
* **Availability**: Specify your availability to volunteer, e.g., morning, afternoon, or all-day.
* **Confirm Availability**: Confirm your availability to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date you're willing to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer Start Time**: Enter the start time of your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the end date of your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer End Time**: Enter the end time of your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Specify the duration of your volunteer shift, if necessary.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Select your volunteer shift, e.g., AM, PM, or All Day.
* **Volunteer Notes**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your volunteer experience.
* **Assigned Volunteer**: Confirm whether you've been assigned a volunteer role by the event organizers.
* **Confirm Assigned Volunteer**: Confirm whether you've been assigned a volunteer role by the event organizers.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your registration form.
