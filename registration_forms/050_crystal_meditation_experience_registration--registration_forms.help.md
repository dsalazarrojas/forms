# Crystal Meditation Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crystal Meditation Experience Registration form is used to collect demographic and medical information from individuals who are interested in or have participated in the Crystal Meditation Experience. This information is used to ensure the individual's safety and well-being during their participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Provide your Contact Number for emergency contact purposes.
4. Indicate any Medical Conditions you have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
5. List the names and contact numbers of any Emergency Contacts.
6. Specify your Medical Provider (if applicable).
7. List any Medication you are currently taking.
8. Indicate any Dietary Restrictions you have, such as vegan or gluten-free.
9. If necessary, provide Special Needs information.
10. Provide payment information, including payment method (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer) and payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number we can use to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions**: Select any medical conditions you have, such as diabetes or high blood pressure.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person we should contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number**: Enter a phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Medical Provider**: If you have a medical provider, enter their name and contact information.
* **Medication**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegan or gluten-free.
* **Special Needs**: If you have any special needs, such as a wheelchair or other accommodations, list them here.
* **Special Needs Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your special needs.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you would like to use (e.g., Cash, Credit Card, Bank Transfer).
* **Other Payment Method**: If you selected "Other," enter the payment method and amount here.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount of payment you are making.
