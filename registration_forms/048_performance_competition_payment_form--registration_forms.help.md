# performance_competition_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register payment details for a performance competition. It is meant to be filled out by the artists or their representatives to provide their payment information for an event where they have showcased their work or talent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) in the form header.
2. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the payment method used for payment (e.g., Cash, Check, or Bank Transfer) in the "Payment Method" field.
6. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
7. Choose the payment date in the "Payment Date" field.
8. If you have any additional information regarding the payment, you can enter it in the "Additional Info" field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the method used for payment (e.g., Cash, Check, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Choose the payment date.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide about the payment.
