# Client Registration For Dispute Processing - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to initiate dispute resolution services. This form is used to gather information from clients seeking assistance with resolving a dispute with another party.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of client you are representing (individual, business, or organization).
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Enter your phone number and mailing address.
4. If you are a business or organization, enter your business or organization name.
5. Select the type of dispute you are experiencing (e.g. contract, employment, consumer, etc.).
6. Enter a brief description of the dispute.
7. Enter the amount in dispute.
8. Enter the name and contact information of the opposing party.
9. Select whether the opposing party is legally represented.
10. Enter any relevant dates, such as when the dispute began.
11. Indicate whether you have previously attempted to resolve the dispute.
12. Enter the outcome of any previous attempts to resolve the dispute.
13. Indicate your desired outcome.
14. Select your preferred resolution method.
15. Indicate the urgency level of the dispute.
16. Enter any critical deadlines.
17. Indicate whether you have legal representation.
18. If applicable, enter the name and contact information of your attorney.
19. Upload any relevant documents.
20. Select how you heard about our services.
21. Agree to the terms of service.
22. Sign the form with your electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Type**: Select the type of client you are representing (individual, business, or organization).
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Business or Organization Name**: If you are a business or organization, enter your name. (Required only if you selected "Business or Organization" as your client type.)
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your mailing address.
* **Date of Birth**: For individual clients only, enter your date of birth.
* **Business Registration Number**: If you are a business or organization, enter your registration number. (Required only if you selected "Business or Organization" as your client type.)
* **Tax ID or SSN**: Enter your tax ID or SSN. (Required only if you selected "Individual" as your client type.)
* **Type of Dispute**: Select the type of dispute you are experiencing (e.g. contract, employment, consumer, etc.).
* **Brief Description of Dispute**: Enter a brief description of the dispute.
* **Amount in Dispute**: Select the amount in dispute.
* **Name of Opposing Party**: Enter the name of the opposing party.
* **Opposing Party Contact Information**: Enter any contact information for the opposing party.
* **Is Opposing Party Legally Represented**: Select whether the opposing party is legally represented.
* **When Did the Dispute Begin**: Enter the approximate date when the dispute began.
* **Have You Attempted Resolution Before**: Indicate whether you have previously attempted to resolve the dispute.
* **Outcome of Previous Attempts**: Enter the outcome of any previous attempts to resolve the dispute.
* **Desired Outcome**: Indicate your desired outcome.
* **Preferred Resolution Method**: Select your preferred resolution method.
* **Urgency Level**: Select the urgency level of the dispute.
* **Any Critical Deadlines**: Enter any critical deadlines.
* **Do You Have Legal Representation**: Indicate whether you have legal representation.
* **Attorney Name**: If applicable, enter the name of your attorney. (Required only if you selected "Yes" as your answer to "Do You Have Legal Representation.")
* **Attorney Contact Information**: If applicable, enter the contact information of your attorney. (Required only if you selected "Yes" as your answer to "Do You Have Legal Representation.")
* **Upload Relevant Documents**: Upload any relevant documents to support your dispute.
* **How Did You Hear About Our Services**: Select how you heard about our services.
* **I Agree to Terms of Service**: Select "Yes I Agree" to indicate that you agree to the terms of service.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign the form with your electronic signature.
* **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your electronic signature.
