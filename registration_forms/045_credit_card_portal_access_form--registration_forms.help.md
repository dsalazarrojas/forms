# credit_card_portal_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The credit_card_portal_access_form is used to collect user information for credit card portal access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your credit card number.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your credit card expiration date.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, required/optional): Enter your credit card verification value.
* **Card Verification Value** (`cvv_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of your card verification value (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Other).
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the type of your credit card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).
* **Month** (`month`, select_one, required/optional): Select the month of your birth.
* **Year** (`year`, select_one, required/optional): Select the year of your birth.
* **Security Code** (`security_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your credit card security code.
* **Card Holder** (`card_holder`, text, required/optional): Enter your credit card holder details.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required/optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required/optional): Review and agree to the card holder agreement.
* **CVC** (`cvc`, text, required/optional): Enter your credit card verification code.
* **Card Verification Code** (`cvc_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of your credit card verification code (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover).
* **Card Holder Agreement** (`card_holder_agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether you agree to the card holder agreement (I agree, I do not agree).
