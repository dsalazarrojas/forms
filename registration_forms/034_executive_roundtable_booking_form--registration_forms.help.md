# Executive Roundtable Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book an executive roundtable event, allowing participants to register for and provide details about their attendance. The form should only be filled out if the participant has received an email invitation to the event. If you have not received an email invitation, please do not fill out this form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will be attending the event.
2. Enter your event ID if you are attending the event.
3. Provide your name.
4. Choose your role in the event.
5. Enter your contact email.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Select a date for your registration.
8. Provide any additional information about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event** (`event`, `select_one`, optional): Select "Yes" if you are attending the event.
* **Event ID** (`event id`, `number`, optional): Enter the ID of the event you are registering for, if applicable.
* **Name** (`participant name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it should be displayed in the event program.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your role in the event (e.g. speaker, moderator, attendee).
* **Contact Email** (`contact email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address you would like to be contacted at about event details.
* **Phone** (`participant phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Registration Date** (`registration date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you wish to be registered for the event.
* **Additional Info** (`participant additional info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation in the event, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`executive roundtable booking form notes`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
