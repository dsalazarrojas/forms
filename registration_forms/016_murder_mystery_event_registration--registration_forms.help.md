# Murder Mystery Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Murder Mystery Event. Please fill out the form carefully to provide your details and confirm your participation.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by selecting the "Submit" button to begin.
2.  Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3.  Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Choose your role in the event ("Actor", "Director", or "Producer") by selecting an option in the "Role" field.
6.  Enter the time you plan to attend in the "Time" field.
7.  Enter the date you plan to attend in the "Date" field.
8.  Describe the event details in the "Event Details" field.
9.  Select the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
10.  Select the duration of the event in the "Duration" field.
11.  Enter the address of the event in the "Address" field.
12.  Add any additional details in the "Additional Details" field.
13.  Confirm your participation by selecting "True" in the "Confirm participation" field.
14.  Choose the status of the event ("Open", "Closed", or "In Progress") by selecting an option in the "Event status" field.
15.  Select your attendance status ("Attending", "Not Attending", or "Pending") by selecting an option in the "Participant status" field.
16.  Optionally, add any participant notes in the "Participant notes" field.
17.  Select "True" or "False" in the "Confirm participation" field to confirm your participation status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (text, required): Enter your name as you want it to be displayed in the event.
*   **Email** (text, required): Enter a valid email address.
*   **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Role** (select_one, optional): Choose your role in the event ("Actor", "Director", or "Producer").
*   **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time you plan to attend.
*   **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend.
*   **Event Details** (text, optional): Describe the event in brief.
*   **Event Date** (date, required): Select the date of the event.
*   **Event Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the event.
*   **Event Duration** (number, optional): Choose the duration of the event.
*   **Address** (text, optional): Enter the address of the event location.
*   **Additional Details** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about the event.
*   **Form submitted by** (text, required): This field will be filled in automatically after submission.
*   **Participant notes** (note, optional): Add any participant-specific notes.
*   **Confirm participation** (select_one, optional): Confirm your participation status ("True" or "False").
*   **Event status** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the status of the event ("Open", "Closed", or "In Progress").
*   **Participant status** (select_multiple, optional): Select your attendance status ("Attending", "Not Attending", or "Pending").
*   **Participant Status Label** (text, optional): This field is currently optional.
*   **Event Status Label** (text, optional): This field is currently optional.
*   **Submit** (text, required): Click to submit your registration form.



The fields marked as "required" are necessary for the form to be completed correctly, while the fields marked as "optional" are used for additional information or clarification. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure your participation is processed correctly.
