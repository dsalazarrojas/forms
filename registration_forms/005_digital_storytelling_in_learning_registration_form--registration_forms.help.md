# Digital Storytelling In Learning Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a workshop on digital storytelling in learning. Please fill out the form below to let us know if you are interested in attending the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the options that apply to you and fill out the required fields. If a field is marked as required, please make sure to fill it out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Format** (workshop_registration): Choose how you would like to attend the workshop. Please select one of the following options: Online, Offline, or Phone.
* **Name** (name): Please enter your name as it would be on your badge or ID.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (phone): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about the workshop.
* **Date** (date): If applicable, please enter the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (time): If applicable, please enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Participant Background** (participant_background): Please provide some background information about yourself as a participant.
* **Preferred Format** (preferred_format): Check all that apply for the formats you prefer to receive the workshop content in (Video, Podcast, Live).
* **Participant Preferences** (participant_preferences): If there is anything specific you would like to get out of this workshop, please let us know here.
* **Additional Comments** (additional_comments): Any additional information you want to share with us?
