# Child Cancer Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a child diagnosed with cancer and their support needs to provide personalized support services and resources. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read each section carefully and answer all required questions.
2. Enter the required information in the provided fields.
3. Select the relevant options for each field as needed.
4. Review and confirm your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration date** (`registration_date`, required/true): Enter the date you are registering for support services.
* **Parent or caregiver name** (`parent_name`, required/true): Enter the name of the parent or caregiver responsible for the child.
* **Relationship to child** (`parent_relationship`, required/true): Select the relationship to the child (Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other).
* **Email address** (`parent_email`, required/true): Enter the email address of the parent or caregiver.
* **Phone number** (`parent_phone`, required/true): Enter the phone number of the parent or caregiver.
* **Child's name** (`child_name`, required/true): Enter the name of the child diagnosed with cancer.
* **Child's age** (`child_age`, required/true): Enter the child's age.
* **Child's date of birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, required/true): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Type of cancer diagnosed** (`cancer_type`, required/true): Enter the type of cancer diagnosed.
* **Date of diagnosis** (`diagnosis_date`, required/true): Enter the date of diagnosis.
* **Current treatment status** (`treatment_status`, required/true): Select the current treatment status (Active treatment, Recently completed treatment, Remission, Palliative care, or Post-treatment follow-up).
* **Types of treatment received** (`treatment_type`, required/true): Select all types of treatment received (Chemotherapy, Radiation, Surgery, Immunotherapy, Targeted therapy, or Other).
* **Hospital or treatment facility** (`hospital_facility`, optional/required:false): Enter the name of the hospital or treatment facility.
* **Support needs and services desired** (`support_needs`, required/true): Select all support needs and services desired (Emotional support, Financial assistance, Transportation, Meal support, Childcare for siblings, Counseling, Support groups, Educational resources, Hospital equipment).
* **Number of siblings** (`siblings_count`, optional/required:false): Enter the number of siblings.
* **Support needed for siblings** (`sibling_support_needed`, optional/required:false): Select if support is needed for siblings.
* **Experiencing financial hardship due to illness** (`financial_hardship`, required/true): Select the level of financial hardship (Severe hardship, Moderate hardship, Minimal hardship, or No hardship).
* **Insurance coverage status** (`insurance_status`, optional/required:false): Select the insurance coverage status (Fully covered, Partially covered, Limited coverage, or No insurance).
* **Child's school status** (`school_status`, required/true): Select the child's school status (Attending school, Homebound education, Not attending, Graduated).
* **Current coping resources and support system** (`coping_resources`, optional/required:false): Enter a description of the current coping resources and support system.
* **Mental health support needed** (`mental_health_support`, required/true): Select if mental health support is needed.
* **Permission to contact for support services** (`contact_consent`, required/true): Select permission to contact for support services.
* **Permission to share information with approved organizations** (`privacy_consent`, required/true): Select permission to share information with approved organizations.
