# Webinar Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Registration Payment Form is a crucial tool for gathering essential details from webinar attendees. It is designed for event organizers to collect attendee information, payment details, and track registration and payment statuses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as prompted:
	* **Email**: Enter your email address as it appears on your registration confirmation.
	* **Webinar Date**: Select the date of the webinar you are registering for.
	* **Registration Cost**: Enter the payment amount for the webinar.
	* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
	* **Attendee Name**: Enter the name of the person attending the webinar.
	* **Attendee Email**: Enter the email address of the person attending the webinar.
	* **Payment Status**: Select the current payment status of your registration (Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid).
2. For non-required fields:
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the event organizers.
	* **Note**: Provide any additional notes or comments for the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email**: Your email address for registration confirmation and communication.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for potential contact from the event organizers.
* **Webinar Date**: The date of the webinar you are registering for.
* **Registration Cost**: The payment amount for the webinar registration.
* **Payment Method**: Your preferred method for paying for the webinar (Credit Card, PayPal, or Stripe).
* **Attendee Name**: The name of the person attending the webinar.
* **Attendee Email**: The email address of the person attending the webinar.
* **Payment Status**: The current status of your payment for the webinar (Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid).
* **Registration Status**: The status of your registration (Confirmed, Pending, or Declined).
* **Note**: Any additional comments or notes for the event organizers.

## Tips
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you experience any issues or have questions, contact the event organizers directly.
* Fill out the form for the correct webinar date to ensure you are registered for the correct event.
