# Donor Breakfast Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Breakfast Meeting Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from donors about their attendance to a breakfast meeting. This form helps the event organizers to collect the necessary details for a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the second field, which will be verified for correctness. Make sure it matches the email you used to create the account or is a valid email address.
3. If you have a **Phone** number, enter it in the third field. This is optional and not required.
4. In the fourth field, enter the **Event Name** you are attending. This is a required field.
5. In the fifth field, select the **Event Date** for the meeting. This is a required field.
6. In the sixth field, select the **Event Time** for the meeting. This is a required field.
7. In the seventh field, select any applicable **Dietary Restrictions** you may have, such as Gluten Free, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Dairy Free. This is a required field.
8. If you have any **Special Requests**, please enter them in the eighth field. This is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted for any updates or changes.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as Gluten Free, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Dairy Free.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have, such as requests for special seating or accommodations.
