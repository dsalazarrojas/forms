# Digital Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a digital campaign. It's a tool to collect information from users who are interested in participating in the campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Email** (`email_address`, required: false): Enter your valid email address where you'd like to receive updates and communications about the campaign.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
