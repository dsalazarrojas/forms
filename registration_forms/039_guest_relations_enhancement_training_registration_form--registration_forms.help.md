# Guest Relations Enhancement Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Relations Enhancement Training Registration Form is designed to collect contact information and details from external guests and their colleagues who are interested in participating in the Guest Relations Enhancement Training program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that you have a clear understanding of the form's purpose and are registering the correct type of attendee.
2. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Type in your work email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
5. Provide the name of the organization you work for in the "Organization" field.
6. Select your job title within your organization.
7. Enter the department you belong to within your organization.
8. Enter the location of your organization.
9. Choose your role in the training program ("Yes" or "No").
10. Select which teams you would like to join or are part of in the training program.
11. Add any additional comments about your training needs or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your business card or identification.
* **Last Name (2)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your business card or identification.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your work email address for easy communication with us.
* **Phone (4)**: Enter your work phone number for easy contact.
* **Organization (5)**: Enter the name of the organization you work for.
* **Job Title (6)**: Select your official job title within your organization.
* **Department (7)**: Enter the department you belong to within your organization.
* **Location (8)**: Enter the location of your organization's office or location you are based in.
* **Role (9)**: Indicate whether you have a role in the training program ("Yes" or "No").
* **Team (10)**: Select which teams you would like to join or are part of in the training program.
* **Comments (11)**: Add any additional comments about your training needs or preferences.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or difficulties while filling out this form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the training program, you can reach out to us for more information.
