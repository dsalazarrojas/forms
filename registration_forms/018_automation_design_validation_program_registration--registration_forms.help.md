# Automation Design Validation Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automation Design Validation Program Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the Automation Design Validation Program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and professional details.
2. Select your preferred program track (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert Level).
3. Choose your area of specialization and focus.
4. Provide a brief overview of your current project.
5. Outline your key automation goals.
6. Specify your programming languages and automation tools experience.
7. Indicate your learning objectives.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Company Industry** (`company_industry`, select_one, required): Choose the industry related to your company (Manufacturing, Automotive, Electronics, etc.).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in the field.
* **Area of Specialization** (`specialization`, text, optional): Describe your area of specialization.
* **Preferred Program Track** (`program_track`, select_one, required): Choose the level of the program you wish to join (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert Level).
* **Automation Focus Area** (`automation_focus`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas you are focusing on (Process Automation, Robotic Automation, Software Testing, etc.).
* **Project Name** (`project_name`, text, required): Provide a brief overview of your current project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, required): Offer a concise description of your project.
* **Project Timeline** (`project_timeline`, select_one, required): Choose the current phase of your project (Conception, Design, Development, Testing, Deployment, Post-Implementation).
* **Key Automation Goals** (`automation_goals`, text, required): Describe the goals you aim to achieve through automation.
* **Programming Languages** (`programming_languages`, select_multiple, optional): Select the programming languages you are familiar with.
* **Automation Tools** (`automation_tools`, text, optional): Specify the automation tools you have experience with.
* **What Do You Hope to Learn** (`learning_expectations`, text, required): Outline your learning objectives for the program.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Select the date you are registering for the program.
