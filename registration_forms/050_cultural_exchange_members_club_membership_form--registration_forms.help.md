# Cultural Exchange Members Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and program interest information from potential members of the Cultural Exchange Members Club.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information in the "Member Info" section:
	* First, enter your **first name**, then your **Last Name**.
	* Provide your **email** address so we can keep you updated on new programs and events.
	* Add your **contact number** for any follow-up communication.
2. Enter your **address** information:
	* **Address Line 1**: Enter the first line of your address.
	* **Address Line 2**: Enter the second line of your address (if applicable).
	* **Post Code**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
	* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you reside.
	* **State**: Enter the name of the state where you reside.
	* **Country**: Enter the name of your country of residence.
3. Select your **membership details**:
	* **Member Since**: Enter the date you joined the club (or the date you wish to join).
	* **Member Until**: Enter the date you wish to leave the club (or leave blank if you don't have a specific end date).
4. Choose your **program interests**:
	* Select all the programs that interest you from the dropdown list.
5. If you have other **program interests**, provide a brief description in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Info**
	+ **Member Info**: A text box to enter your name, email, and contact number.
	+ **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
	+ **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
	+ **Email**: A text field to enter your email address.
	+ **Contact Number**: A text field to enter your contact number.
* **Address**
	+ **Address Line 1**: A text field to enter the first line of your address.
	+ **Address Line 2**: A text field to enter the second line of your address (if applicable).
	+ **Post Code**: A text field to enter your postal code or zip code.
	+ **City**: A text field to enter the name of the city where you reside.
	+ **State**: A text field to enter the name of the state where you reside.
	+ **Country**: A text field to enter the name of your country of residence.
* **Membership Details**
	+ **Member Since**: A date field to enter the date you joined the club (or the date you wish to join).
	+ **Member Until**: A date field to enter the date you wish to leave the club (or leave blank if you don't have a specific end date).
* **Program Interests**
	+ **Program Interests**: A select multiple dropdown list to choose your program interests.
	+ **Other Program Interest**: A text field to enter any other program interests not listed in the dropdown.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our club administrators.
* This form is for informational and registration purposes only.
