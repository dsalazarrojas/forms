# professional development volunteer registration form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for professional development workshops. It collects information about your personal details and availability to match you with suitable workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the participant info section with your basic details.
2.  Provide your workshop preferences to let us know which workshops you are interested in.
3.  Enter your availability information, including your first available date and the number of days and hours you have available per week.
4.  Enter your email address and contact phone number so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This section is for entering your basic details such as name, email, and other contact information.
* **Workshop Info** (`workshop_info`, text, optional): This section is for specifying which workshops you are interested in. Choose one or more from the options provided.
* **Availability Info** (`availability_info`, time, optional): When is your first available date? Please use the time format (e.g., 2023-02-01 09:00).
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select multiple, optional): This section is for choosing multiple workshops you are interested in. Select as many as you like.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, number, optional): How many days are you available per week? Enter a whole number between 1 and 7.
* **Availability Hours** (`availability_hours`, number, optional): How many hours are you available? Enter a whole number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): This is your contact phone number. Enter your phone number without any special characters or symbols.
