# Game Day Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Game Day Check In Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees at a game day event. This form helps event organizers and staff efficiently manage attendee registrations and ensure a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee information on **Attendee Information**.
2. Provide details about your emergency contact on **Emergency Contact**.
3. Enter your registration information on **Registration Information**.
4. If you have any special requests, such as accommodations or requirements, fill it in on **Special Requests**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Attendee Information
**Attendee Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
**Type** (text, optional): If you have any preferred name to use for identification purposes (e.g., nickname or team name).

### Emergency Contact
**Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person (e.g., parent, partner, or emergency contact).
**Phone Number** (text, required): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
**Relationship** (text, optional): If applicable, describe your relationship to the emergency contact.

### Registration Information
**Name** (text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
**Team Name** (text, optional): If you are part of a team, provide the team name.
**Game Day Number** (text, optional): If you have a specific game day number or ID, enter it here.

### Special Requests
**Special Requests** (text, optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, such as accommodations or equipment needs, enter them here.

## Tips
* Please double-check the accuracy of your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure your emergency contact person is reachable during the event.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
