# worker_productivity_analysis_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Productivity Analysis Program aims to assess the productivity of workers in various areas.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Review the form carefully to ensure you have the necessary information and permissions.
* Fill out the form in a single session.
* Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Name**: The name of the participant.
* **Participant Position**: The position of the participant.
* **Participant Department**: The department of the participant.
* **Participant Supervisor**: The supervisor of the participant.
* **Participant Status**: The status of the participant.

### Goals
* **Goals**: Select one or more goals that the participant wants to achieve through the program.
* **Participant Goals**: Additional goals that the participant wants to achieve through the program.
* **Manager Goals**: Additional goals that the manager wants to achieve through the program.

### Manager Details
* **Manager Name**: The name of the manager.
* **Manager Position**: The position of the manager.
* **Manager Department**: The department of the manager.
* **Manager Level**: The level of the manager.
* **Manager Status**: The status of the manager.
* **Manager Supervisor**: The supervisor of the manager.

### Consent and Additional Information
* **Consent**: Indicate whether you are a participant or a manager.
* **Consent Date**: The date of consent.
* **Assessment Date**: The date of the assessment.
* **Participant Notes**: Any additional comments from the participant.
* **Manager Notes**: Any additional comments from the manager.

### Contact Information
* **Email**: The email of the participant.
* **Phone**: The phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Level**: The level of the participant.
* **Participant Goals**: Additional goals that the participant wants to achieve through the program.
* **Manager Level**: The level of the manager.
* **Manager Goals**: Additional goals that the manager wants to achieve through the program.
