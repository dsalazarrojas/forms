# Property Showing Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to check in for a property showing. It's used to track who is attending a showing and provide important contact information in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Show Time** (`show_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you plan to arrive for the showing. This is optional, but it helps the agent or broker know when to expect you.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so the agent or broker can contact you after the showing.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case the agent or broker needs to reach you urgently.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth showing experience. 
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask the showing agent or broker for clarification.
