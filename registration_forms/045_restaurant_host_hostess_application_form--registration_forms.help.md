# Restaurant Host/Hostess Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals to apply to be a host or hostess for events and gatherings at a restaurant. It is meant to collect information about the applicant's qualifications, experience, and availability to host events.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply navigate through the pages of questions and fill out the fields provided. Make sure to answer all the required fields and be as thorough as possible in your responses.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: A field to collect your basic contact information (name, email, etc.).
*  *Contact Details* (id: 2): A field to provide your contact details such as phone number and address.
* *Experience Details* (id: 3): A field to describe your experience in hosting events or relevant work history.
* *Education Details* (id: 4): A field to share your educational background or relevant training.
* *Work History* (id: 5): A field to provide your work history, including previous employers and job titles.
* *References* (id: 6): A field to list at least one professional reference.
* *Host Availability* (id: 7): A field to indicate your general availability to host events.
* *Availability* (id: 8): A field to specify your availability in terms of days and time slots.
* *Emergency Contact* (id: 9): A field to provide emergency contact information, including name and relationship.
* *Emergency Contact Relationship* (id: 10): A field to specify the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* *Emergency Contact Information* (id: 11): A field to provide the contact information of the emergency contact person.
* *Host Experience* (id: 12): A field to select your level of experience as a host (never, less than 1 month, 1-2 years, 2-5 years, or more than 5 years).
* *Host Skills* (id: 13): A field to select multiple skills you possess as a host (Hospitality Management, Food Service, Event Planning, Communication, or Time Management).
* *Host Language* (id: 14): A field to select your proficiency in languages (English, Spanish, French, or Mandarin).
* *Work Status* (id: 15): A field to specify your current employment status (available, employed, or retired).
* *Employment Type* (id: 16): A field to select your employment type (full-time, part-time, or freelance).
* *Availability Type* (id: 17): A field to select your availability type (full-time, part-time, or flexible).
* *Days of the Week* (id: 18): A field to select multiple days you are available to work.
* *Start Time* (id: 19): A field to select the time slot you prefer to start hosting events (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* *End Time* (id: 20): A field to select the time slot you prefer to finish hosting events (morning, afternoon, or evening).
* *Work Hours* (id: 21): A field to select the number of hours you are available to work (2 hours, 4 hours, 6 hours, or 8 hours).
* *Break Time* (id: 22): A field to select your break time preferences (15 minutes, 30 minutes, or 1 hour).
* *Days Off* (id: 23): A field to select multiple days you would like to have off.
* *Emergency Contact Relation* (id: 24): A field to specify the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* *Emergency Contact Person* (id: 25): A field to provide the contact information of the emergency contact person.

Note: This form is for internal use only, and the information collected will be reviewed by the restaurant management team. Please be honest and thorough in your responses.
