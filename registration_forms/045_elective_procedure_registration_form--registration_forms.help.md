# Elective Procedure Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Elective Procedure Registration Form is a document used to collect information from patients before undergoing an elective procedure. This form is essential for ensuring that the patient's details, medical history, and procedure specifics are accurately recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately with your information.
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in, if any are marked as required.
- Review the form for completeness and correctness before submitting it to the relevant authorities or medical personnel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Elective Procedure Registration Form** (page label, text): This is the header for the form, providing a title that clearly indicates its purpose.
* **patient_name** (name, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, etc.).
* **patient_dob** (date, optional): Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **medical_record_number** (text, optional): Enter your medical record number, if applicable.
* **procedure_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the elective procedure you are undergoing, if known.

## Tips
- If you're unsure about any field, please consult with medical personnel or relevant authorities.
- Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- This form is for your convenience and should be completed with the understanding that the information gathered will be used for your procedure.
