# Customer Loyalty Tier Upgrade Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Loyalty Tier Upgrade Registration Form is used to help customers upgrade their loyalty tier status. This form is for customers who are already registered and want to take advantage of upgraded benefits and rewards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in your current customer information:
	* Email: Your current email address associated with your customer account.
	* Phone: Your current phone number associated with your customer account.
3. Fill in your new customer information:
	* Customer name: The name of the person upgrading their loyalty tier status.
	* Email: The email address associated with the new customer account (this will be created or updated as part of the upgrade process).
	* Phone: The phone number associated with the new customer account (this will be created or updated as part of the upgrade process).
4. Submit the form to complete the upgrade process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`customer_loyalty_tier_upgrade_form_2_email`, email, required): Your current email address associated with your customer account.
* **Phone** (`customer_loyalty_tier_upgrade_form_3_phone`, text, required): Your current phone number associated with your customer account.
* **Customer name** (`customer_loyalty_tier_upgrade_form_4_customer_name`, text, required): The name of the person upgrading their loyalty tier status.
* **Customer email** (`customer_loyalty_tier_upgrade_form_5_customer_email`, email, required): The email address associated with the new customer account.
* **Customer phone** (`customer_loyalty_tier_upgrade_form_6_customer_phone`, text, required): The phone number associated with the new customer account.

## Tips

* Make sure you have all the necessary information before starting.
* Review your current customer account information to ensure it is accurate.
* If you are unsure about any of the information, please contact our customer support team for assistance.
