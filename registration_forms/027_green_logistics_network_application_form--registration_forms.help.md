# Green Logistics Network Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from companies that want to join the Green Logistics Network. The collected data will be reviewed by the Green Logistics Network team to determine if the company meets the required criteria for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your company's information. Please provide accurate and complete answers to all the questions.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have provided all required information.
3. Once you have filled out the form, submit it to proceed with the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Company Information** (`company_info`, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of your company, including its name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person from your company.
* **Sustainability Statement** (`sustainability_statement`, text, optional): Describe your company's sustainability practices and initiatives.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company's website.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of your company (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, optional): Select all industries that apply to your company (Manufacturing, Retail, Services, Transportation, or Wholesale).
* **Sustainability Practices** (`sustainability_practices`, select_multiple, optional): Select all sustainability practices that apply to your company (Carbon offsetting, Renewable energy, Recycling, Supply chain management, Sustainable packaging).
* **Certification** (`certification`, select_multiple, optional): Select all relevant certifications (ISO 14001, LEED, B Corp, FSC, GRS).
* **Green Fleet** (`green_fleet`, text, optional): Describe your company's green fleet details.
* **Emissions Target** (`emissions_target`, number, optional): Enter your company's emissions target.
* **Sustainability Commitment** (`sustainability_commitment`, text, optional): Describe your company's sustainability commitment.
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, optional): Describe your company's business focus and values.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of job you are applying for (Full-time, Part-time, Freelance, or Intern).
* **Employee Count** (`employee_count`, number, optional): Enter your company's employee count.
* **Business Hours** (`business_hours`, time, optional): Enter your company's business hours.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your company's location.
* **Date Started** (`date_started`, date, optional): Enter the date your company started.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, optional): Select the size of your company (Small, Medium, Large, or Extra Large).
* **Business Focus** (`business_focus`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas of business focus that apply to your company (Environment, People, Community, Economy, Society).
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, optional): Enter your company's certification date.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, optional): Select when you are ready to submit your application (I am ready to submit my application, I need more information before submitting my application).
