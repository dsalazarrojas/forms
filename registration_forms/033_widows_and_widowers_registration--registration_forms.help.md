# widows_and_widowers_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are widows or widowers and want to participate in a support program. It is meant to gather contact details, preferences, and other relevant information to provide support services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **Participant Details** on page 1.
2. Select your **Contact Preferences** on page 1.
3. Provide your **Contact Information** on page 2.
4. Choose the **Widowers Support Services** you require on page 3.
5. Add any **Participant Notes** on page 4.
6. Enter your **Email** address on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Page 1, Required): Enter the basic details of the participant such as name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Contact Preferences** (Page 1, Required): Select how you prefer to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, mail).
* **Contact Information** (Page 2, Required): Provide your full contact information (address, phone number, etc.).
* **Widowers Support Services** (Page 3, Required): Choose the support services you require (e.g., counseling, support groups, etc.).
* **Participant Notes** (Page 4, Required): Add any additional notes or comments about your participant experience.
* **Email** (Page 5, Required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for further support or updates.
