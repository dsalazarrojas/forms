# Responsible Beverage Service Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants seeking to register for responsible beverage service training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please fill in all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
2. Enter your details in the "Registration Details" section.
3. Provide information about the participant, including address and phone number in the "Participant Details" section.
4. Confirm your consent for the training program by filling in the "Participant Consent" section.
5. Enter the training provider's information, including address, in the "Training Provider" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (`Registration Details`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details about your registration.
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, required): Enter your name, address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about the training program.
* **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, text, required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the training program.
* **Confirm Consent** (`Confirm Consent`, text, required): Repeat the confirmation of your consent for the training program.
* **Training Provider** (`Training Provider`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Address** (`Training Provider Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the training provider.
* **Participant Address** (`Participant Address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
