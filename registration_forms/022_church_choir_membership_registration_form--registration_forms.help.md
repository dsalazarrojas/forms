> # Church Choir Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Church Choir Membership Registration Form is used to collect essential information from potential members, including personal details, musical experience, and availability. This information will help the choir director understand your interests, skills, and commitment level, ensuring a smooth integration into the choir and providing a fulfilling musical experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Membership Status, Voice Part, Can Read Sheet Music, Weekly Rehearsal Commitment, and Registration Date.
2. Choose your Voice Part from the available options.
3. Select your level of sheet music reading ability.
4. Choose your availability for special services and whether you're interested in singing solos or small ensembles.
5. Provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for the choir director to know.
6. If you have prior musical experience, provide details about it.
7. If you play any instruments, mention it in the "Do you play any instruments?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address (3)** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your active email address for sheet music distribution and updates.
* **Phone Number (4)** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Church Membership Status (5)** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you're a Church Member or Non-Member Guest.
* **Voice Part (7)** (`vocal_part`, `select_one`, required): Choose the part you usually sing, such as Soprano, Alto, Tenor, or Bass.
* **Can you read music? (8)** (`can_read_music`, `select_one`, required): Choose how proficient you are in reading sheet music: Proficiently, Basic, or Learn by Ear.
* **Previous Choir Experience (9)** (`choir_experience`, `text`, optional): If you have prior musical experience, describe it in this field.
* **Do you play any instruments? (10)** (`plays_instrument`, `text`, optional): If you play any instruments, mention it here.
* **Weekly Rehearsal Commitment (12)** (`weekly_rehearsal_commitment`, `select_one`, required): Choose how often you can attend Thursday 7pm rehearsals: Regularly, Most of the time, or Occasionally.
* **Special Services (13)** (`special_service_availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select which special services you're available for, such as Christmas Eve, Easter Sunday, or Good Friday.
* **Singing Solos or Small Ensembles (14)** (`solos_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you're interested in singing solos or small ensembles: True, False, or Maybe Later.
* **Registration Date (16)** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Additional Comments (15)** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information that might be helpful for the choir director to know.
