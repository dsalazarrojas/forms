# Mobile Loyalty App Testing Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mobile loyalty app testing registration form helps individuals provide their details, device information, and availability to be considered for participation in testing mobile loyalty apps. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a title for the testing session in the "Mobile Loyalty App Testing Registration Form".
2. Select the category of device you use to access mobile loyalty apps (e.g. Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel).
3. Provide a brief description of your experience with mobile loyalty apps and what you can offer as a tester (this will help us understand your unique strengths and interests).
4. Enter your name and email address so we can get in touch with you for further instructions.
5. Enter your phone number so we can reach you via SMS or phone call.
6. Describe your device in detail, including its specifications, operating system version, and any notable features.
7. Indicate when you are available to participate in testing.
8. (Optional) If assigned a testing tool, please enter the tool name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mobile Loyalty App Testing Registration Form** (`form_title`, text, required): Enter a title for this testing session.
* **Registration Forms** (`form_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of your mobile device (e.g. Apple iPhone, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel).
* **This form helps product and research teams recruit qualified testers, capture device details and availability, and manage participant sign-ups in one organized place.** (`form_description`, text, required): Briefly describe why you are interested in participating and what makes you a strong candidate for this testing opportunity.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Device Details** (`participant_device_details`, text, required): Describe your device in detail, including its specifications, operating system version, and any notable features.
* **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, date, required): Select the dates and times you are available to participate.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you are assigned a testing tool, enter its name.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information to ensure we can best understand your unique strengths and interests.
* If you already have experience with similar testing forms, please let us know and we will guide you through the process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
