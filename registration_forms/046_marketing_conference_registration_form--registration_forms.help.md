# marketing_conference_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marketing_conference_registration_form is used to collect information from attendees for a marketing conference. This form is used to gather event details, attendee's personal information, and additional details for a successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Event Details**: This section is used to collect event details. Please fill in the required fields to ensure that the event is correctly documented.
2. **Personal Information**: This section is used to collect your personal details. Please fill in the required fields to register for the event.
	* **Name**: This is your full name as it should appear in our records.
	* **Email**: This is your email address for official communication and event updates.
	* **Phone**: This is your contact phone number for any event-related matters.
3. **Additional Details**: This section is used to provide additional information for your event registration.
	* **Date**: This is your preferred date to attend the event.
	* **Time**: This is your preferred time to attend the event.
	* **Registration**: This is your preferred way to register for the event (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
	* **Note**: This is any additional note you would like to share with the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (text): This is a free-form text field for you to provide any event details that might be helpful.
* **Name** (text): Enter your name as it should appear in our records.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for official communication and event updates.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for event-related matters.
* **Date** (date): Select your preferred date to attend the event.
* **Time** (time): Select your preferred time to attend the event.
* **Registration** (select_multiple): Choose your preferred way to register for the event (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Note** (note): Add any additional notes you would like to share with the event organizers.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your form carefully before submitting to ensure all fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
