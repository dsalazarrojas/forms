# Marriage Certificate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering marriage certificates for couples who have recently gotten married. Fill out this form to make your union official and receive a verified certificate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required and optional information.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Couple Info (required: false)**: This is where you will enter the names of both the bride and groom. Please provide the full name of each individual as it appears on their official identification documents.
* **Marriage Info (required: false)**: In this section, you will provide details about your marriage, including the date, place, and circumstances of your union.
* **Certificate Info (required: false)**: If you are submitting a marriage certificate, this is where you will provide information about the certificate itself, such as its serial number and issuing authority.
* **Submit (required: false)**: Once you have filled out the form completely, click the "Submit" button to send your registration. A review process will then be conducted to verify your submission.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Double-check your information for errors before submitting.
* If you have any issues with the submission process, contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
