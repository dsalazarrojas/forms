# property_automation_council_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for registering a new property automation council event. It captures essential information such as event details, date, time, location, and council members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" in the "Event Type" field to indicate whether the event is a property automation council event.
5. Enter event details in the "Event Details" field.
6. Choose the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
7. Select the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
8. Enter the event location in the "Event Location" field.
9. Indicate the number of council members attending in the "Council Members" field.
10. If required, provide additional information in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on the registration confirmation.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that will receive the registration confirmation and any further updates.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication and updates.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate this is a property automation council event.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Choose the date of the event.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of the event.
- **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
- **Council Members** (`council_members`, select_multiple, required): Indicate the number of council members attending the event.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional details or comments about the event.
- **Form Footer** (`form_footer`, note, required): Note: This is a system-generated note to confirm completion of the form.
