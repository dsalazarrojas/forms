# Brow Artist Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for a brow artist workshop, which is a professional development event focused on enhancing skills and knowledge in brow services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields. These will be used to identify you as a registered attendee.
2. Provide your Email Address, which will be used to communicate with you regarding the workshop.
3. Enter your Phone Number, which will be used for contact and communication purposes.
4. If you are self-employed, enter the name of your salon or studio. This is not required, but it will help identify you as an individual or business.
5. Choose your Current Experience Level, which will help us cater to your learning needs during the workshop.
6. Select all the services you currently offer as a brow artist from the list provided. This will help you focus on areas you need improvement or expansion.
7. Choose your Preferred Workshop Date from the available dates. Please note that selecting a date does not secure your registration; you will still need to follow up with a payment confirmation.
8. Select your Preferred Time for the workshop, whether it's in the Morning, Afternoon, or Both days Full.
9. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know so we can accommodate your needs for workshop meals and refreshments.
10. Confirm your Registration Fee payment status, whether it's the Full Fee or a Deposit.
11. Enter your Payment Confirmation Number, which will be provided after making a payment. This will secure your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address we can use to contact you regarding the workshop.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Salon or Studio Name** (`salon_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your business name if you are self-employed.
* **Current Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your level of experience with brow services to help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
* **Services You Currently Offer** (`services_offered`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the services you currently offer as a brow artist.
* **Preferred Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred date for the workshop.
* **Preferred Time** (`workshop_time`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred time for the workshop.
* **What is your certification goal** (`certification_goal`, `text`, optional): Enter your goal for certification, if any.
* **Primary learning objectives** (`learning_goals`, `text`, optional): Enter your primary learning objectives for the workshop.
* **Location Preference** (`workshop_location`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred location for the workshop.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Registration Fee Paid** (`registration_fee`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your payment status for the workshop fee.
* **Payment Confirmation Number** (`payment_confirmation`, `text`, required): Enter your payment confirmation number to secure your registration.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid duplication of efforts.
- Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
- Follow up with a payment confirmation to secure your registration.
