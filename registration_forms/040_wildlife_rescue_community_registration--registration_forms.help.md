# Wildlife Rescue Community Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wildlife Rescue Community Registration form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers to help the organization manage its volunteer schedule and ensure that all volunteers are properly registered and accounted for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Choose your availability for volunteering, including whether you are available to volunteer and what times you are available.
3. Indicate your emergency contact information, including their name and phone number.
4. Confirm your relationship with the emergency contact.
5. Choose your volunteer schedule, including your activity name, date, time, and whether you are available for a volunteer shift.
6. Select your volunteer skills and experience.
7. Confirm your volunteer availability and reference.
8. Choose whether you agree to the volunteer agreement.
9. Indicate your volunteer shift start and end times, and duration.
10. Review and submit your registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1): This is the first name of the person registering.
* **Last name** (2): This is the last name of the person registering.
* **Email** (3): This is the email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (4): This is your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Address** (5): This is your current address where you can be reached.
* **Schedule** (6): This is the date and time you are available to volunteer.
* **Activity name** (7): This is the name of the specific volunteer activity you are interested in.
* **Activity date** (8): This is the date you are available to participate in the activity.
* **Activity time** (9): This is the time you are available to participate in the activity.
* **Volunteer shift** (10): This indicates whether you are available for a volunteer shift.
* **Availability** (11): This indicates whether you are available for a volunteer shift.
* **Special skills** (12): This is a list of special skills you possess that can be useful for volunteering.
* **Emergency contact name** (13): This is the name of the person you have designated as an emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact phone** (14): This is the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (15): This is a description of your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Volunteer notes** (16): This is a field for any additional comments or notes about your volunteering experience.
* **Volunteer shift notes** (17): This is a field for any notes about your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer skills** (18): This is a list of skills you possess that are useful for volunteering.
* **Volunteer experience** (19): This is a field to describe any relevant volunteer experience you have.
* **Volunteer availability** (20): This indicates whether you are available for a volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer reference** (21): This is a confirmation that you have a reference for your volunteering experience.
* **Volunteer agreement** (22): This is a confirmation that you agree to the volunteer agreement.
* **Volunteer shift start time** (23): This is the start time of your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer shift end time** (24): This is the end time of your volunteer shift.
* **Volunteer shift duration** (25): This is the duration of your volunteer shift.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the necessary skills and experience to participate in the volunteer activity you select.
* Make sure to check your emergency contact information carefully to ensure it is up-to-date.
* Review your volunteer shift schedule carefully to avoid conflicts.
* If you have any special skills, please be sure to list them in the "Special skills" field.
* If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to volunteer, please indicate this in the "Volunteer notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Wildlife Rescue organization for assistance.
