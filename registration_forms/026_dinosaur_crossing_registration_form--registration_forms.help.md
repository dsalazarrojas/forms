# Dinosaur Crossing Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dinosaur Crossing Registration Form collects information from participants who wish to join the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form data page with general information about yourself.
2. Provide specific information about the dinosaur participating in the event on the dinosaur data page.
3. Select the event data and event location information on the respective pages.
4. Choose the contact data and submission status on the respective pages.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the "Form Notes" section.
6. Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.
