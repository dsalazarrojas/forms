# School Transfer Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Transfer Request Form is used by parents or guardians to request a change of school for their child. This form is typically used when a student is requesting to attend a different school due to a specific reason such as bullying, relocation, or special needs. Please ensure you are requesting a school transfer for a valid reason and not duplicating a request for the same student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name.
2. Enter the student's grade level (if applicable).
3. Provide the student's date of birth.
4. Enter your contact information as the parent or guardian.
5. If your child is currently enrolled in another school, please provide the name of their current school.
6. Specify the reason for requesting a school transfer.
7. If necessary, include any additional comments regarding the reason for the transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Transfer Request Form Input** (`<text>`, Optional): This field is a placeholder for any specific information regarding the school transfer request.
* **Student Name** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license, etc.).
* **Student Grade** (`<number>`, Optional): Enter the student's current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, etc.). If your child is not enrolled in a traditional grade level (e.g., preschool), please specify their equivalent age or educational level.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`<date>`, Optional): Enter the student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy (e.g., 12/31/1999).
* **Parent/Guardian Contact Information** (`<text>`, Optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number, so we can reach you regarding the school transfer request.
* **Current School Name** (`<text>`, Optional): If your child is currently enrolled in another school, please provide the name of that school.
* **Reason for Transfer** (`<text>`, Optional): Describe the reason why you are requesting a school transfer (e.g., bullying, moving, special needs).
* **Additional Comments** (`<text>`, Optional): If you would like to provide any additional information regarding the reason for the transfer, please include it here.
