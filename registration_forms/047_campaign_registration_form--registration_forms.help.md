# Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the "Campaign Registration Form" to register for a campaign. The form is designed to collect essential information about you as a registrant, including your contact details, preferences, and interests. This information will help us to communicate with you effectively and organize campaign activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name in the "Registrant Full Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your region or constituency from the provided options in the "Region or Constituency" field.
5. If you are interested in volunteering, select "True" in the "Interested in Volunteering" field.
6. Choose the types of campaign events you would be interested in attending from the "Events Interested In" field.
7. Select your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
8. Agree to our privacy policy by selecting "True" in the "Agree to Privacy Policy" field.
9. If you have a referral source, provide it in the "Referral Source" field.
10. Optionally, provide any additional notes or information about yourself in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): This is your full name as a registrant, which will be used for registration and outreach purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address to receive updates about the campaign.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
* **Region or Constituency** (`region`, text, optional): Select your geographic area to help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, required): If you are willing to help with campaign activities, select "True".
* **Events Interested In** (`events_interested`, text, optional): Choose the types of campaign events you would be interested in attending.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or SMS).
* **Agree to Privacy Policy** (`agree_privacy`, select_one, required): Please agree to our privacy policy by selecting "True".
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, text, optional): If you have a referral source, provide it here.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional information about yourself to help us better understand your interests and preferences.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are not interested in volunteering, please select "False" in the "Interested in Volunteering" field.
* If you prefer not to be contacted by any means, please select "None" in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
* By agreeing to our privacy policy, you consent to the use of your data for campaign communications.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
