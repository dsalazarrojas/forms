# LinkedIn Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from users to create a workshop registration record in the system. It's a basic form designed to gather personal details of the users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the Email Address field (this should be a valid email address).
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field (this can be a mobile or landline number).
5. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field (this should be in the YYYY-MM-DD format).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your full last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): A valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): A phone number to reach you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): A valid date in the YYYY-MM-DD format.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to ensure a successful registration.
* Use a valid email address to ensure you receive important updates and information about the workshop.
* If you experience any issues while completing the form, feel free to contact us for assistance.
