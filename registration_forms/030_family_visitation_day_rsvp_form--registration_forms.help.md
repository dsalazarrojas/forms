# Family Visitation Day RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for parents to RSVP for family visitation days, providing essential information for visitation planning. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure we can accommodate your visit and make necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your name as a parent/guardian.
2.  Provide your email address for communication.
3.  Enter your contact number (optional, but recommended for easy communication).
4.  Select your preferred date and time for visitation.
5.  Choose the start and end times for visitation.
6.  List the children attending with you (up to 4 can be selected).
7.  List the adults attending with you (choose up to 3).
8.  Add any other individuals who will be attending (choose up to 2).
9.  If you have any special needs, provide details in the "Special Needs" field.
10.  Finally, add any comments or information for the visitation day.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Please enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you regarding visitation details.
* **Parent Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please provide your contact number.
* **Date of Visitation** (`date_of_visitation`, date, required): Select the date you'd like to visit.
* **Time of Visitation** (`time_of_visitation`, time, required): Choose the start and end times for your visit.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, number, required): Enter the start time in 24-hour format (e.g., 1400 for 2:00 PM).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, number, required): Enter the end time in 24-hour format (e.g., 1700 for 5:00 PM).
* **Attending Children** (`attending_children`, select_multiple, required): Select up to 4 children that will be attending with you.
* **Attending Adults** (`attending_adults`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to 3 adults (Parent, Grandparent, Guardian) who will attend with you.
* **Other** (`attending_other`, select_multiple, optional): Add up to 2 other individuals who will attend with you.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, note, optional): Describe any special needs you may have.
* **Special Needs Details** (`special_needs_details`, text, optional): If you have special needs, provide more information about it.
* **Special Needs Instructions** (`special_needs_instructions`, text, optional): Add any additional instructions for your special needs.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information for the visitation day.
