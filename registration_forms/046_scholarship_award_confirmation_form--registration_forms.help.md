# Scholarship Award Confirmation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to confirm receipt of a scholarship award. The form is filled out by the recipient of the award and is used to record their confirmation of the award details and any additional information that may be required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Recipient Information (Page 1) and fill out the required fields.
2. Enter the Award Information (Page 2) and fill out the required fields.
3. Enter the Recipient Response (Page 3) and fill out the required fields.
4. Enter the Award Amount (Page 4) and fill out the required fields.
5. Enter the Award Details (Page 5) and fill out the required fields.
6. Enter the Confirmation Date (Page 6) and fill out the required fields.
7. Sign the Signature (Page 7) field, indicating that you have received the award.
8. Fill out the Recipient Name (Page 8) field with your name.
9. Enter the Awarding Institution (Page 9) field with the name of the institution that awarded the scholarship.
10. Enter the Contact Email (Page 10) field with your contact email address.
11. Enter the Contact Phone (Page 11) field with your contact phone number.
12. Enter any additional Notes (Page 12) that you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient Information** (Page 1): This page is used to enter the recipient's information, including their name and contact details.
* **Award Information** (Page 2): This page is used to enter the details of the award, including the amount and any other relevant information.
* **Recipient Response** (Page 3): This page is used to enter the recipient's response to the award, including any comments or questions.
* **Award Amount** (Page 4): This page is used to enter the amount of the award.
* **Award Details** (Page 5): This page is used to enter any additional details about the award.
* **Confirmation Date** (Page 6): This page is used to enter the date on which you confirmed receipt of the award.
* **Signature** (Page 7): This page is used to sign the form, indicating that you have received the award.
* **Recipient Name** (Page 8): This page is used to enter your name.
* **Awarding Institution** (Page 9): This page is used to enter the name of the institution that awarded the scholarship.
* **Contact Email** (Page 10): This page is used to enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 11): This page is used to enter your contact phone number.
* **Notes** (Page 12): This page is used to enter any additional notes or comments about the award.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your scholarship awarding institution.
* Please keep this form for your records, as it confirms your receipt of the award.
