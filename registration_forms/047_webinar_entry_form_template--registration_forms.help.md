# webinar_entry_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar_entry_form_template is a registration form for a webinar or online meeting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email (ensure it's a valid email address)
   - Registration Date (in the correct format, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY)
   - Time (in the correct 24-hour time format, e.g., 12:00)
2. Optionally, fill in the following fields:
   - Phone (if you want to be contacted via phone)
   - Notes (if you have any additional information you'd like to share)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification or documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification or documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached after the webinar.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you prefer to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you're registering for the webinar in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the time you prefer to attend the webinar in the 24-hour format, e.g., 12:00.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to provide for your registration.
 
## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- Ensure your registration date and time are correct to avoid conflicts or missed appointments.
- Use the "Notes" field to add any specific requests or requirements you have for the webinar.
