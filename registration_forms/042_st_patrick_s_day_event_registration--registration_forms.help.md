# st_patricks_day_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for St. Patrick's Day events. This form is designed to gather details about the event and the attendees participating in it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the Event Details section**: Provide a brief description of the event, including its title, date, and time.
2. **Provide Attendee Details**: Enter the relevant information about the attendees, such as their names and contact details.
3. **Enter Payment Information**: Add any necessary payment details, such as payment methods or amounts.

* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): Enter the relevant information about the attendees, such as their names and contact details.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): Add any necessary payment details, such as payment methods or amounts.
