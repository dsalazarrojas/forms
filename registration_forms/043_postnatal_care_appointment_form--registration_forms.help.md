# Postnatal Care Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from new mothers and their contacts for postnatal care appointments. The goal is to ensure accurate and efficient communication between healthcare providers and new mothers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Please enter the new mother's information in the "New Mother Info" section.
2.  Enter the contact information of the new mother in the "Contact Info" section.
3.  Provide the appointment details in the "Appointment Details" section.
4.  If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Notes" section.
5.  Finally, please sign the "Provider Signature" field to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **New Mother Info** (`new_mother_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter the new mother's name, address, and any other relevant information.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This field is required. Please enter the new mother's contact information, including phone number and email address.
*   **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required/optional): Please provide the appointment specifics, including date, time, and location.
*   **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or notes about the appointment, please enter them here.
*   **Provider Signature** (`signature`, text, required/optional): This field is required. Please sign your name to confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the information provided.
*   **Submit Button** (`submit_button`, text, required/optional): Please click the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.
