# Smart Appliance Integration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your smart appliances for integration with our platform. Please fill out this form to create your account and enable our services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields as prompted. If you have any issues or need assistance, contact us at your provided email or phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required: false): This is the first section of the form where you provide your general information. You can enter any relevant details about yourself.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Username** (`Username`, `text`, required: false): Create a username to identify yourself on the platform.
* **Password** (`Password`, `text`, required: false): Enter a password to secure your account.
* **Confirm Password** (`Confirm Password`, `text`, required: false): Re-enter your password to confirm it.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all fields to ensure a smooth registration process.
- If you encounter any issues, contact us at your provided email or phone number.
- You can only register your smart appliances once per user, so be sure to complete this form only once.
