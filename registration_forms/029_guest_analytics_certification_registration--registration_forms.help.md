# Guest Analytics Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Guest Analytics Certification Program. Please complete this form to provide your details and show interest in joining the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant details, including any additional information you'd like to share about yourself.
2. Answer the question "What motivates you to join this certification program?" in the "motivation" field.
3. Confirm your consent to participate by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Consent to Participate" field.
4. Choose which certification program you'd like to join from the options provided.
5. Enter your contact information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
6. Finally, confirm your contact information by entering it in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details**: This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you'd like to be known for the purposes of the certification program.
* **Motivation**: Please answer this question briefly and accurately, explaining why you are motivated to join the certification program.
* **Consent to Participate**: Select "Yes" to confirm your willingness to participate in the certification program. Select "No" otherwise.
* **Certification Program Details**: Enter any additional details you'd like to provide about the certification program you're interested in joining.
* **Certification Program Preference**: Select which certification program you'd like to join from the options provided.
* **Contact Information**: This field is optional, but it's where you can provide your email address and phone number for future communication and updates about the certification program.
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address in this field.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any additional comments or information you'd like to provide about yourself or your application.
