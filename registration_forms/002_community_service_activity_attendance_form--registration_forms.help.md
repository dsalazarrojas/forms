# Community Service Activity Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your participation in community service activities. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to receive proper credit for your volunteer work and to help the organization maintain accurate records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form as soon as possible after participating in a community service activity.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed accurately.
3. If you're a student, provide the name of your school if applicable.
4. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required, Text): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required, Email): Enter your email address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required, Text): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, required, Select One):
	+ Select the option that best describes you, either:
		- Returning Volunteer
		- New Volunteer
		- Community Member
		- Student
* **School Name (if applicable)** (`student_school_name`, optional, Text): If you're a student, enter your school's name.
* **Title of the Service Activity** (`activity_title`, required, Text): Provide the name of the project or event you participated in.
* **Date of the Activity** (`date_of_activity`, required, Date): Enter the date of the activity.
* **Time of Arrival** (`arrival_time`, required, Time): Enter the time you arrived at the activity location.
* **Time of Departure** (`departure_time`, required, Time): Enter the time you left the activity location.
* **Total Hours Completed** (`total_hours`, required, Number): Enter the total hours you participated, including minutes (e.g., 4.5).
* **Brief description of tasks performed** (`tasks_performed`, required, Text): Describe your role and responsibilities during the activity.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required, Text): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required, Text): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Was your activity supervised?** (`supervisor_verification`, required, Select One):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you were supervised during the activity.
	+ Select 'No' if you were not supervised.
* **Name of Supervisor on-site** (`supervisor_name`, required, Text): Enter the name of your supervisor or the person who verified your attendance.
