# Digital Title Transfer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Title Transfer Registration Form is used to document the transfer of digital assets such as cars or other vehicles. This form collects information about the dealer, vehicle, seller, buyer, transfer details, and signatures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Dealer Information section with the dealer's details.
2. Fill in the Vehicle Information section with the vehicle's details.
3. Fill in the Seller Information section with the seller's details.
4. Fill in the Buyer Information section with the buyer's details.
5. Select the transfer type and enter the date and time of transfer.
6. Enter the transfer amount.
7. Select the payment method and enter the payment date.
8. Obtain signatures from the seller, buyer, and witness (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dealer Information** (`dealer_info`, text, optional): Enter the dealer's name and contact information.
* **Vehicle Information** (`vehicle_info`, text, optional): Enter the vehicle's make, model, year, and vehicle identification number (VIN).
* **Seller Information** (`seller_info`, text, optional): Enter the seller's name and contact information.
* **Buyer Information** (`buyer_info`, text, optional): Enter the buyer's name and contact information.
* **Transfer Type** (`transfer_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether the transfer is a sale, trade, or other type of transfer.
* **Date of Transfer** (`date_of_transfer`, date, optional): Enter the date of the transfer.
* **Time of Transfer** (`time_of_transfer`, time, optional): Enter the time of the transfer.
* **Transfer Amount** (`transfer_amount`, number, optional): Enter the amount of the transfer.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select the method of payment used for the transfer (e.g. cash, check, wire transfer).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of payment.
* **Buyer Signature** (`buyer_signature`, note, optional): Obtain the buyer's signature as proof of agreement.
* **Seller Signature** (`seller_signature`, note, optional): Obtain the seller's signature as proof of agreement.
* **Witness Signature** (`witness_signature`, note, optional): Obtain a witness signature, if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled in before proceeding with the transfer.
- Verify the accuracy of all information entered.
- Keep a record of the completed form for future reference.
- This form may require review and approval from a supervisor or regulatory authority.
- Consult with a legal or regulatory expert for specific requirements.
