# hawaii_graduation_trip_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "hawaii_graduation_trip_registration" form is designed to collect participant information and details for a program, likely a summer camp. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Read each field carefully to understand what information it requires.
- Provide accurate and relevant details for each field.
- Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (1): Enter the participant's name. 
* **Room Preferences** (2): Select one or more room preferences from the available options (Queen Bed, King Bed, Double Bed).
* **Emergency Contact 1** (3): Enter an email address of an emergency contact, if necessary. This field is optional.
* **Emergency Contact 2** (4): Enter an email address of another emergency contact, if necessary. This field is optional.
* **Emergency Contact 3** (5): Enter an email address of a third emergency contact, if necessary. This field is optional.
* **Participant Consent** (6): This field is a note where the participant can provide any necessary consent or comments. This field is optional.
* **Room Details** (7): Enter any additional details about the room, if necessary. This field is optional.
* **Participant Name** (8): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant School** (9): Enter the participant's school or institution.
* **Participant Grade Level** (10): Enter the participant's grade level.
* **Participant Grade Year** (11): Enter the participant's grade year.
* **Participant Dietary Restrictions** (12): Enter any dietary restrictions the participant has. 

## Tips

- Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Ensure you fill in all required fields, if any.
- If you're unsure about any field, reach out to the organizers for clarification.
- Submit your completed form for processing.
