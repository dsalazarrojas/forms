# Pajama Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Pajama Party event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participants Info field with any additional information you'd like to share.
2. Select your Attendance Type from the dropdown menu.
3. Fill in the Event Info field with any relevant information about the event.
4. Enter your Participant Name in the text field.
5. Enter your Participant Email address in the email field.
6. Enter your Participant Phone number in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participants Info (1)** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Attendance Type (2)** (`attendance_type`, select_one, optional): Choose how you will be attending the event. The options are Label 1, Label 2, and Label 3. 
* **Event Info (3)** (`event_info`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the event, such as the date, time, and location.
* **Participant Name (4)** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name to be associated with your registration.
* **Participant Email (5)** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Participant Phone (6)** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you.
