# 2021 PBE Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teams registering for the 2021 PBE registration process. It helps us get your team's details right, ensure proper communication, and organize the event successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Review each field carefully and ask for help if you're unsure about any field.
3.  Make sure to provide all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Information
* **Official team name**: Enter the name you want displayed on brackets and scoreboards.
* **Competition division**: Select the category that best fits your team's experience level.
* **Total team members**: Include the captain, and specify the number of players in your team, up to 6 members.
* **Team experience level**: Average experience of your team members. Select from Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
* **Equipment rental needed**: Select items your team needs to borrow for the event.

### Captain/Primary Contact
* **Full legal name**: As it appears on your government ID.
* **Email address**: Primary contact for schedules and updates.
* **Mobile phone number**: Used for emergency contact during the event.
* **Date of birth**: Must be 18+ or provide guardian consent form.
* **Age as of event date**: Verify division eligibility.

### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency contact name**: Full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency phone number**: Must be reachable during event hours (8AM-6PM).
* **Relationship to participant**: Select how your emergency contact is related to the captain.

### Team Profile & Equipment
* **Equipment rental needed (select all)**: Select items your team needs to borrow.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Helps us improve our outreach.
* **Dietary restrictions or allergies**: For catering purposes.
* **Medical conditions or medications**: For medical staff to be aware of.
* **Liability waiver acceptance**: Required to participate.
* **Photo release consent**: Can we use photos of your team for marketing?
* **Special requirements or accommodations**: Accessibility needs, hearing impairments, etc.
* **Additional comments or notes**: Anything else the organizers should know?

### Waiver and Consent
* **Accept liability waiver**: Required to participate.
* **Photo release consent**: Can we use photos of your team for marketing?
