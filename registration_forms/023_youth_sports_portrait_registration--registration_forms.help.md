## Purpose
The Youth Sports Portrait Registration form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians about a youth athlete participating in a sports portrait session. This information will help us to prepare for the photo shoot, and ensure that we have all necessary details to deliver high-quality photos.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the athlete's details, such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Enter the parent or guardian's contact information, including their name, phone number, and email.
3. Select the photo package that suits your needs.
4. Confirm your consent for the photo shoot.
5. Provide additional information about the athlete, such as school or league affiliation.
6. Complete the payment information, including the payment method and date.
7. Add any additional notes or comments.
