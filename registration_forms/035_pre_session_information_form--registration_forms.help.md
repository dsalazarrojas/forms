**pre-session-information-form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The pre-session-information-form is a data collection form used to gather essential information from participants before a session. This form helps to gather contact details and basic demographic information about the participant. It is recommended to fill out this form before attending a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Participant Name in the Participant Name field.
2. Provide your Participant Email address in the Participant Email field.
3. Enter your Participant Phone number in the Participant Phone field.
4. Enter your Participant Date of Birth in the Participant Date of Birth field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Enter your Session ID if you have one assigned.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): A field to enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, Email, Required/Optional: Optional): A field to enter your email address as a participant.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, Text, Required/Optional: Optional): A field to enter your phone number as a participant.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, Date, Required/Optional: Optional): A field to enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Session ID** (`session_id`, Number, Required/Optional: Optional): A field to enter your session ID if you have one assigned.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields.
* Double-check your input for accuracy before submitting the form.
