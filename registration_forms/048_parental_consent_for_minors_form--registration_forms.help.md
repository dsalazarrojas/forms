# Parental Consent For Minors Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent For Minors Form is used to obtain the consent of a parent or guardian for a minor's participation in an activity, program, or event. This form helps to ensure that the minor has the necessary permission and supervision to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct answer for "Parental Consent For Minors" (Yes or No).
2. If you answer "Yes", enter the minor's information on page 2.
3. If you answer "No", you can stop here.
4. On page 2, enter the minor's date of birth.
5. On page 3, enter the parent or guardian's contact information (email address and phone number).
6. On page 4, provide the parent or guardian's signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parental Consent For Minors** (`user_input`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" if you are the parent or guardian of the minor, or "No" if you are not.
* **Minor Information** (`minor_info`, number, required/optional): Enter the minor's age (in years) if you answered "Yes" to the previous question.
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, note, required/optional): Enter the parent or guardian's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Email Address** (`email_parent`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter the minor's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Provide the parent or guardian's signature, which confirms that you are the parent or guardian and give consent for the minor's participation.
