# Expressive_Lens_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details and preferences for a workshop, course, or program related to expressive lenses. It helps us understand who is participating and what they would like to achieve or contribute to.
## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill in your participant details.
2. Share your participant preferences.
3. Indicate your interest in the lens preferences.
4. Provide your consent for participation.
5. Let us know about your program participation.
6. If relevant, mention your workshop participation.
7. Finally, specify your course participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`id: 1`, `name: participant_details`, `type: text`, required: false): Please provide your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
- **Participant Preferences** (`id: 2`, `name: participant_preferences`, `type: text`, required: false): Share your interests, goals, or expectations from participating in this workshop, course, or program.
- **Lens Preferences** (`id: 3`, `name: lens_preferences`, `type: text`, required: false): Indicate which lenses you are interested in or would like to learn more about.
- **Participant Consent** (`id: 4`, `name: participant_consent`, `type: text`, required: false): We need your consent to process your participation. Please read and agree to our terms and conditions.
- **Program Participation** (`id: 5`, `name: program_participation`, `type: text`, required: false): Let us know about any program or project you are participating in related to this workshop, course, or program.
- **Workshop Participation** (`id: 6`, `name: workshop_participation`, `type: text`, required: false): If you have participated in workshops related to expressive lenses, mention any previous experience and what you hope to achieve this time.
- **Course Participation** (`id: 7`, `name: course_participation`, `type: text`, required: false): Share any previous or current course participation related to expressive lenses.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in as much information as possible to help us better understand your needs and interests.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask our team for guidance.
- We appreciate your honest participation and look forward to working with you!
