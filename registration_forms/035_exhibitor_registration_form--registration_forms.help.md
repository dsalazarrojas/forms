# Exhibitor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibitor Registration Form is used by exhibitors to provide information for their booth registration at an event. Please fill in your details to register your exhibit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3. Make sure to submit the form only once per company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company name as it appears on your marketing materials.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your exhibit.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your company's email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number where you can be reached.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address for billing and shipping purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your company is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code for your company's location.
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of your assigned booth.
* **Exhibit Type** (`exhibit_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of exhibit you will be bringing (Booth, Non Profit, Other).
* **Company Name Display** (`company_name_display`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether to display your company name on promotional materials.
* **Booth Details** (`booth_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific details about your booth.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Enter any special requests you have.
* **Terms Accepted** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any special requests, be sure to enter them clearly and thoroughly.
