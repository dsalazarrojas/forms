# Corporate Restructuring Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in or working in the field of corporate restructuring.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the "first-name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "last-name" field.
3. Provide a valid **Email Address** in the "email" field.
4. Select your current **Job Title** from the "job-title" field.
5. Enter your **Company** name if applicable.
6. Choose your current **Job Level** from the options provided.
7. Select your **Role** in the company for each field.
8. Enter your **Phone** number if applicable.
9. Select the symposia you will be attending from the "symposium-attendance" field.
10. Add any **Comments** you'd like to include.
11. Enter your company website, address, job address, job location, and other job location details if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on the symposium materials.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Provide your full last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address is required. This is how you'll receive important symposium updates.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Choose your current job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter your department name, if applicable.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, select_one, required): Select your current job level.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, required): Choose your role(s) in the company.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth if you'd like to disclose this information.
* **Symposium Attendance** (`symposium_attendance`, select_multiple, required): Select which symposia you will be attending.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or information you'd like to share with the symposium organizers.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your company website.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company address.
* **Job Address** (`job_address`, text, optional): Enter your job address.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter your job location.
* **Job Location Country** (`job_location_country`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job location country.
* **Job Location City** (`job_location_city`, text, optional): Enter your job location city.
* **Job Location Postal Code** (`job_location_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your job location post office code.
* **Job Location State/Province** (`job_location_state_province`, select_multiple, optional): Select your job location state or province.
* **Job Location Country Region** (`job_location_country_region`, text, optional): Enter your job location country region.
