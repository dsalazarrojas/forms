# New Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Customer Registration Form is used by customers to provide their personal and contact information when creating a new account with our service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure that all required information is collected accurately.
2. Fill out each field according to your personal details and preferences.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): This field is intended for any additional information the customer wants to provide about themselves.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in your account.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to receive notifications and correspondence from our service.
* **Password** (`password`, text, optional): Create a secure password for your account. Passwords must be at least 8 characters long.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable contact via phone calls and SMS notifications.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Provide your physical address for delivery and shipping purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city name for location identification.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province for location identification.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your postal zip code for delivery and shipping purposes.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Select your country of origin or residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, text, optional): Re-enter your password to confirm it is accurate.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately to avoid errors in your account setup.
* Passwords should not be shared with anyone for security reasons.
* You can update your contact information at any time by accessing your account settings.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
