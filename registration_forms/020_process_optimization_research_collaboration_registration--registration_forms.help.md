## Process Optimization Research Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Process Optimization Research Collaboration Registration form is used for employees to register their interests and preferences in participating in process optimization research collaborations. This form is intended for internal use and is used to gather information about the research topics, collaboration preferences, and other relevant details that will be used to assign research collaborations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Registration** field, which is a numerical field where you can enter your registration number.
2. Enter your **FirstName** in the **FirstName** field, which is a text field for your first name.
3. Enter your **LastName** in the **LastName** field, which is a text field for your last name.
4. Enter your **Email** in the **Email** field, which is an email field to validate your email address.
5. Enter your **Phone** in the **Phone** field, which is a text field for your phone number.
6. Select your **Research Interests** by choosing one or more options from the list provided:
	* **Yes**: You are interested in participating in process optimization research collaborations.
	* **No**: You are not interested in participating.
7. Select your **Collaboration Preferences** by choosing one or more options from the list provided:
	* **Yes**: You are interested in collaborating with others on research projects.
	* **No**: You are not interested in collaborating.
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration (required)**: This numerical field is used to uniquely identify your registration.
* **FirstName (optional)**: This text field is for entering your first name.
* **LastName (optional)**: This text field is for entering your last name.
* **Email (optional)**: This email field is to validate your email address and will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone (optional)**: This text field is for entering your phone number.
* **Research Interests (required)**: Select one or more options to indicate your research interests.
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in participating in process optimization research collaborations.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in participating.
* **Collaboration Preferences (required)**: Select one or more options to indicate your collaboration preferences.
	+ **Yes**: You are interested in collaborating with others on research projects.
	+ **No**: You are not interested in collaborating.
* **Submit (optional)**: This button is used to complete your registration and submit your information.
