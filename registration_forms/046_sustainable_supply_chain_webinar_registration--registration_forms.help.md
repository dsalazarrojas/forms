# Sustainable Supply Chain Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for a webinar on sustainable supply chain management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
2. Enter your company name (if applicable).
3. Enter your email address.
4. Choose your role (e.g., decision-maker, expert, or student).
5. Select your topic interests from the provided options.
6. Choose all the topics that interest you from the second selection field.
7. Check the box to consent to receive updates about the webinar and related events.
8. Agree to the terms of service by selecting "Accept".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates about the webinar and related events.
- **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if you are representing a company.
- **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Select your role in the company or organization you represent (e.g., decision-maker, expert, student).
- **Supply Chain Management** (`topic_interest_1`, select_one, required): Select this option if you are interested in supply chain management topics.
- **Sustainable Business Models** (`topic_interest_2`, select_multiple, required): Select all the topics that interest you from the sustainable business models category.
- **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): Check this box to consent to receive updates about the webinar and related events.
- **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, select_one, required): Agree to the terms of service by selecting "Accept". 

## Tips
- Please fill in your details accurately and honestly to ensure we can best support your needs.
- If you have any issues or need help, contact us at [support email] for assistance.
