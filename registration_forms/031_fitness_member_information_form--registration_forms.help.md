# Fitness Member Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Member Information Form is a registration form designed to collect information from fitness program participants. This form is intended for publicly accessible use.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reviewing the form title and purpose** to ensure you understand what the form is used for.
2. **Fill in the member's name** in the text field provided for the "fitness_member_name" field.
3. **Enter the member's email address** in the "email" field. Make sure to include the correct email format (e.g., membername@example.com).
4. **Provide the member's phone number** in the "fitness_member_phone" field. This can be a primary or secondary phone number.
5. **Enter the member's address** in the "fitness_member_address" field, including house number, street, and city.
6. **Enter the member's Date of Birth** in the "fitness_member_dob" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. **Add any additional member notes** in the "fitness_member_notes" field for special cases or instructions.
8. **Select the enrolled programs** from the available options in the "fitness_programs" field.
9. **Submit the form** after reviewing all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **fitness_member_name**: A text field where you can input the member's name.
* **email**: A text field where you can input the member's email address.
* **fitness_member_phone**: A text field where you can input the member's phone number.
* **fitness_member_address**: A text field where you can input the member's address.
* **fitness_member_dob**: A date field where you can input the member's Date of Birth.
* **fitness_member_notes**: A note field where you can add additional member information.
* **fitness_programs**: A multiple-select field where you can choose the enrolled programs the member has joined.

## Tips
- Ensure accuracy and legibility when inputting member information.
- Review all fields before submission.
- Use the provided help guide as a reference.
- If unsure, contact the fitness program administrator for assistance.
