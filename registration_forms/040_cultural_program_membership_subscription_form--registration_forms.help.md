# cultural_program_membership_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a cultural program membership. It gathers essential information to process your membership application.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form with the required information, making sure to enter valid data for all marked fields.
2. Review your answers before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your surname as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of membership you are applying for.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the cultural program you are registering for.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Membership Term** (`membership_term`, select_multiple, optional): Select the membership term (if applicable).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select the payment method.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Membership Fee** (`membership_fee`, number, required): Enter the membership fee.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Tax ID** (`tax_id`, text, optional): Enter your tax ID number (if applicable).
* **Tax ID Type** (`tax_id_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of tax ID (if applicable).
* **Tax ID Number** (`tax_id_number`, text, optional): Enter the tax ID number (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Message** (`message`, note, optional): Enter any additional message or comments.
