# Foreign Object Debris FOD Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a Foreign Object Debris (FOD) training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are registering for the correct training session.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* Session Date
	* Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Training Date
	* Training Time
	* Training Location
	* Number of Attendees
3. Optionally, provide:
	* Session Time
	* Session Location
	* Training Date
	* Training Time
	* Training Location
	* Number of Attendees
	* Attendee Names
	* Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Session Location** (`session_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training.
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, number, required): Enter the number of attendees for the training session.
* **Attendee Names** (`attendee_names`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you and other attendees will be attending the training session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or details about the training session.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the training organizers.
* This form is intended for a single training session. If you need to register for multiple sessions, fill out this form multiple times.
