# military_warrior_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are interested in registering as military warriors.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Warrior Details" page with the required and optional fields.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select your date of enlistment in the "Date of Enlistment" field.
7. Choose your military rank from the "Military Rank" dropdown menu.
8. Select your military branch from the "Military Branch" dropdown menu.
9. Enter the number of years you have served in the "Years of Service" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Warrior Details** (1, text, required/optional): This is the main form section where you can enter your details.
* **Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your full name as it should be used for registration purposes.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the registration process.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required/optional): Select your date of birth from the calendar dropdown menu.
* **Date of Enlistment** (6, date, required/optional): Select your date of enlistment from the calendar dropdown menu.
* **Military Rank** (7, select_one, required/optional): Choose your military rank from the dropdown menu:
	+ Private
	+ Corporal
	+ Sergeant
	+ Officer
* **Military Branch** (8, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your military branch from the dropdown menu:
	+ Army
	+ Navy
	+ Air Force
	+ Marine Corps
* **Years of Service** (9, number, required/optional): Enter the number of years you have served in the military.
