# media_release_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "media_release_permission_form" is a form used for obtaining permission to release media, such as images, videos, or audio recordings, for various purposes. This form is designed to ensure that the individual being photographed or recorded has given their consent for the release of their media.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the purpose and requirements.
2. Answer all required fields (if any).
3. Select your consent type (consent, non-consent, or no answer).
4. Fill out the date of birth fields if applicable.
5. Enter any additional details required in the media release permission form fields (Media Release Permission Form 1, 2, 3, and 4).
6. Enter the date of capture and date of permission fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Consent Type (consent_type)** (`consent_type`, `select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select whether you give your consent to release the media or not. You have three options: Consent, Non-Consent, or No Answer.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): If you are required to fill out this field, please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Media Release Permission Form 1 (media_release_permission_form_1)** (`Media Release Permission Form 1`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional details you would like to provide related to the media release permission form.
* **Media Release Permission Form 2 (media_release_permission_form_2)** (`Media Release Permission Form 2`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information needed related to the media release permission form.
* **Media Release Permission Form 3 (media_release_permission_form_3)** (`Media Release Permission Form 3`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing any further details related to the media release permission form.
* **Media Release Permission Form 4 (media_release_permission_form_4)** (`Media Release Permission Form 4`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information required for the media release permission form.
* **Date of Capture (date_of_capture)** (`date_of_capture`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the media was captured.
* **Date of Permission (date_of_permission)** (`date_of_permission`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the permission was given.
