# Open Access Education Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about potential partners for the Open Access Education Partnership. It will help us to understand your school's or organization's interests and focus areas, as well as your contact information and any other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page tab below to navigate through the form.
2. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
3. Use the provided options for select fields to choose the most relevant answer.
4. Make sure to provide a submission at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Partnership Inquiries
* **Partnership Inquiries** (`partner_inquiries`, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to share about your partnership interests or inquiries.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): This field is for your school's or organization's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This field is for your contact email address.
* **Non-profit** (`nonprofit`, text, optional): This field is for any non-profit status information you would like to share.
* **School Initiative** (`school_initiative`, text, required): This field is for any school-based initiatives you would like to share.
* **Community Initiative** (`community_initiative`, text, optional): This field is for any community-based initiatives you would like to share.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): This field is for any contact person's name and title.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): This field is for the contact person's email address.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, optional): This field is for your school's or organization's address.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, optional): This field is for your school's or organization's phone number.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, text, optional): This field is for your school's or organization's website.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, text, optional): This field is for your school's or organization's email address.
* **School Fax** (`school_fax`, text, optional): This field is for your school's or organization's fax number.
* **School Other** (`school_other`, text, optional): This field is for any other information about your school or organization.
* **Community Address** (`community_address`, text, optional): This field is for the community's address.
* **Community Phone** (`community_phone`, text, optional): This field is for the community's phone number.
* **Community Website** (`community_website`, text, optional): This field is for the community's website.
* **Community Other** (`community_other`, text, optional): This field is for any other information about the community.
* **School Type** (`school_type`, select_one, optional): This field is for the type of school or organization you represent.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, optional): This field is for the type of organization you represent.
* **Open Access Education Focus** (`open_access_education_focus`, select_multiple, optional): This field is for the focus areas of your open access education initiatives.
* **Partnership Status** (`partnership_status`, select_one, optional): This field is for the status of your partnership with us.
* **Partnership Start Date** (`partnership_start_date`, date, optional): This field is for the start date of your partnership with us.
* **Partnership End Date** (`partnership_end_date`, date, optional): This field is for the end date of your partnership with us.
* **Submission** (`submission`, note, required): This field is for any additional information or comments you would like to submit.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* Use the provided options for select fields to choose the most relevant answer.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will review your submission carefully and may contact you for additional information if needed.
