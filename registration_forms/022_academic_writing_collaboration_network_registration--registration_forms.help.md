# Academic Writing Collaboration Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to create a profile for registering in the Academic Writing Collaboration Network. By filling out this form, you will be able to showcase your academic background, interests, and collaboration preferences to potential collaborators.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your **Full Name** as you wish to be displayed in the network.
2. Select your **Institutional Affiliation** (e.g., University, Research Center, or Organization) to connect with others from similar backgrounds.
3. Provide a valid **Contact Email** for network communications.
4. Choose your **Primary Academic Field or Discipline** (e.g., Sociology, Physics, Literature).
5. Briefly list 2-3 **Recent Publications or Projects** that showcase your work.
6. Select the types of **Collaboration** you are open to (e.g., Peer Reviewing, Co-authoring, Brainstorming Sessions).
7. Indicate your **Career Stage** (e.g., Ph.D. Candidate, Early Career Researcher, Senior Faculty, Independent Scholar).
8. Choose your **Preferred Communication Channel** (e.g., Email, Slack, WhatsApp, Video Call).
9. Select your **Desired Frequency of Interaction** (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Project-based).
10. List the **Time Zones** you are available to collaborate in (e.g., GMT, EST, PST, CET, IST, AEST).
11. Set your **Profile Visibility** to **True** or **False**, depending on how visible you want your profile to be within the network.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your name as you wish to be displayed in the network.
* **Institutional Affiliation** (`institutional_affiliation`, `text`, required): Select your academic institution or organization.
* **Contact Email** (`professional_email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid contact email for network communications.
* **Primary Academic Field or Discipline** (`academic_field`, `text`, required): Choose your primary area of academic focus (e.g., Sociology, Physics, Literature).
* **Specific Research Interests or Keywords** (`research_interests`, `text`, required): Briefly list 2-3 of your specific research interests or keywords (e.g., separated by commas).
* **Types of Collaboration** (`collaboration_types`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all types of collaboration you are open to (e.g., Peer Reviewing, Co-authoring, Brainstorming Sessions).
* **Career Stage** (`career_stage`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your current career stage (e.g., Ph.D. Candidate, Early Career Researcher, Senior Faculty, Independent Scholar).
* **Preferred Communication Channel** (`communication_channel`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary communication channel (e.g., Email, Slack, WhatsApp, Video Call).
* **Desired Frequency of Interaction** (`collaboration_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select your desired frequency of interaction (e.g., Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, Project-based).
* **Time Zones you can collaborate in** (`available_time_zones`, `select_multiple`, required): List the time zones you are available to collaborate in (e.g., GMT, EST, PST, CET, IST, AEST).
* **Recent Publications or Projects** (`previous_publications`, `text`, optional): Briefly list 2-3 recent publications or projects that showcase your work.
* **Profile Visibility** (`profile_visibility`, `select_one`, required): Set your profile visibility to **True** or **False**, depending on how visible you want your profile to be within the network.
