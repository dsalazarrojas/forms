# Vendor Vetting Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for vendors to register their interest in participating in the vetting initiative. Please fill out all required fields to ensure your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through each page and fill out the required fields. Start by entering your vendor information, then provide your first contact details and any relevant submissions. You can add notes and other details as needed. Assign the form to a specific person and provide their contact information if applicable. Finally, confirm that you have completed the form and submitted it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Vendor Information Form** (`vendor_vetting_info`, text, not required): Enter your company name and other relevant vendor information.
- **First Contact Details** (`first_contact_details`, text, not required): Provide details about your first point of contact, such as name and email.
- **Submissions** (`submissions`, text, not required): Describe your submission or proposal in this field.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Add any additional notes or comments about your application.
- **Assigned to** (`assigned_to`, select_one, not required): Select the person assigned to handle your application.
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person who will be handling your application.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of the person who will be handling your application.
- **Vetting Status Updated** (`vetting_status_updated`, date, not required): Enter the date your vetting status was last updated.
- **Vetting Status Updated 2** (`vetting_status_updated_2`, time, not required): Enter the time your vetting status was last updated.
- **Submission Details** (`submission_details`, text, not required): Add any additional details about your submission.
- **Form Completion Status** (`form_completion_status`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed this form.
- **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, select_one, required): Confirm that this form has been submitted.
