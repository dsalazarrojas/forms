# Qualifier Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for participant registration, providing necessary information for organizers and researchers.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant's Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Enter your **Age**.
4. Provide your **Contact Information**.
5. Select your **Preferred Communication Channel** from the options provided.
6. Add any **Additional Comments** you'd like to include.
7. Enter your **Participant's Address**.
8. Enter your **Participant's Phone Number**.
9. Enter your **Participant's Email**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant's Name** (text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age** (number, required): Enter your age.
* **Contact Information** (text, required): Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email.
* **Preferred Communication Channel** (select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted:
	+ Email
	+ Phone
	+ In-person
* **Additional Comments** (note, required): Add any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself.
* **Participant's Address** (text, required): Enter your address.
* **Participant's Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant's Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
