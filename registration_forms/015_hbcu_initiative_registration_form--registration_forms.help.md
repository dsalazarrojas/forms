# HBCU Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HBCU Initiative Registration Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from participants. This form is used to register new participants for the HBCU Initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field below and provide the required information.
2.  Fill out all the fields that apply to your participation.
3.  Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, not required)
Provide a brief description of your participation in the HBCU Initiative.

### 2. **Participant Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, not required)
Select one or more roles that describe your participation in the HBCU Initiative. Options include:
*   Participant
*   Co-Participant

### 3. **Interest Statements** (`interest_statements`, `text`, not required)
Share any interest statements or comments about your participation in the HBCU Initiative.

### 4. **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, not required)
Enter your name as a participant in the HBCU Initiative.

### 5. **Participant Email** (`email`, `email`, not required)
Provide your email address for contact purposes.

### 6. **Verify Email** (`email_verification`, `text`, not required)
Re-enter your email address to verify it for contact purposes.

### 7. **Time of Participant Registration** (`time_of_participant_registration`, `time`, not required)
Record the time of your participant registration.

### 8. **Date of Participant Registration** (`date_of_participant_registration`, `date`, not required)
Record the date of your participant registration.

### 9. **Participant Statement** (`participant_statement`, `note`, not required)
Add any additional participant statements or comments.

### 10. **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `note`, not required)
Attach or upload your participant signature.

### 11. **Participant Photo** (`participant_photo`, `text`, not required)
Upload or attach a participant photo.

## Tips
*   Please fill out all applicable fields for accurate participant data.
*   All fields are optional, but providing accurate information will help us better understand your participation in the HBCU Initiative.
*   If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, please contact us for assistance.
