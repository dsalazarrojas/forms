# Game Dev Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for student registration with the Game Dev Club. It collects basic information about the student, their game development experience, and their school affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and game name in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred game platform and genre.
3. Enter your experience level and years of experience in game development.
4. Provide links to your GitHub repository and resume if applicable.
5. Indicate if you're a member of another Game Dev Club.
6. Fill in your school and personal information (name, email, phone, and grade level).
7. Complete your school's address and contact information.
8. Finally, provide the name of the person submitting this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or student card.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): A valid email address for club communication.
* **Game Name** (`game_name`, `text`, required: false): The title of your game project.
* **Game Platform** (`game_platform`, `select_one`, required: false): The platform your game is developed for (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, or Linux).
* **Game Genre** (`game_genre`, `select_multiple`, required: false): The type of game you're developing (Action, Adventure, Role-Playing, Sports, or Strategy).
* **Skill Level** (`skill_level`, `number`, required: false): Your proficiency level in game development (1-10).
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, required: false): The number of years you've been developing games.
* **GitHub Link** (`github_link`, `note`, required: false): A link to your GitHub repository for your game project.
* **Resume Link** (`resume_link`, `select_one`, required: false): A link to your resume or a document that showcases your skills and experience.
* **Are you already a member of a Game Dev Club?** (`game_dev_club_member`, `select_one`, required: false): If you're already a member of another Game Dev Club, select 'True' and fill in the club's name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, required: false): A valid email address for club communication (if not the same as your own).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): A phone number for club communication (if not your own).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of your school.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `select_one`, required: false): Your current school year (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, Freshman, etc.).
* **School District** (`school_district`, `text`, required: false): The district where your school is located.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Your grade level (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): The name of the parent or guardian submitting this form.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`parent_email`, `text`, required: false): A valid email address for the parent or guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required: false): A phone number for the parent or guardian.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required: false): The physical address of your school.
* **School City** (`school_city`, `text`, required: false): The city where your school is located.
* **School State** (`school_state`, `text`, required: false): The state where your school is located.
* **School Zip** (`school_zip`, `text`, required: false): The zip code of your school.
* **School Country** (`school_country`, `text`, required: false): The country where your school is located.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): The name of the person submitting this form.
