# iwoca_2013_camp_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for registration purposes for a specific event or program. It collects personal and general information from users, including name, contact details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct options for the "Select One" and "Select Multiple" fields.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Agree to the "Terms and Conditions" by selecting "Agree".
