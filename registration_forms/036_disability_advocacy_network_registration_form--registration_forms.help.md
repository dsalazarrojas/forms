# Disability Advocacy Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals who wish to join the Disability Advocacy Network. The information collected will be used to create a database of members and provide them with relevant resources and support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your relevant information, making sure to complete all required fields.
2. For fields with a select_one or select_multiple type, select one or multiple options from the list.
3. For fields with a date type, enter your date of birth and date of registration in the correct format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details** (`Member Details`, text, required): This field is used to collect your name, email, phone number, and other contact information. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`Accessibility Needs`, text, required): Please describe your accessibility needs and any challenges you may face in accessing resources or services.
* **Interests** (`Interests`, text, required): Please list your interests and any relevant skills or abilities you have.
* **Accessibility Needs Details** (`Accessibility Needs Details`, text, required): Please elaborate on your accessibility needs and any specific challenges you may face.
* **Level of Accessibility Needs** (`Level of Accessibility Needs`, select_one, required): Select the level of accessibility needs you have. Please choose from Low, Medium, or High.
* **Interests Details** (`Interests Details`, text, required): Please provide more information about your interests and skills.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`, text, required): Please enter your street address.
* **Postal Code** (`Postal Code`, text, required): Please enter your postal code.
* **Province** (`Province`, text, required): Please select your province from the list.
* **City** (`City`, text, required): Please enter the city where you reside.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, required): Please select your country from the list.
* **Interests** (`Interests`, select_multiple, required): Please select all interests that apply.
* **Organisations** (`Organisations`, select_multiple, required): Please select all organisations you are a part of.
* **Languages** (`Languages`, select_multiple, required): Please select all languages you speak or read.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, select_multiple, required): Please select all skills you have.
* **Abilities** (`Abilities`, select_multiple, required): Please select all abilities you have.
* **Disability Group** (`Disability Group`, select_one, required): Select the disability group you belong to. Please choose from Yes or No.
* **Disability Status** (`Disability Status`, select_one, required): Select your current disability status. Please choose from Active or Inactive.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender. Please choose from Yes or No.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Date of Registration** (`Date of Registration`, date, required): Please enter the date you are registering with the Disability Advocacy Network.
