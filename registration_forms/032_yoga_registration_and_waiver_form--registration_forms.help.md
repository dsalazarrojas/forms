# Yoga Registration And Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information and emergency contact details for participants in a yoga class or program. It's essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can best respond to any medical emergencies or other situations that may arise during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. You can ask your emergency contact if you're unsure about any of the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form** (`registration_form`): Enter your name, address, and other basic registration information.
- **Medical Clearance** (`medical_clearance`): List any medical conditions that may affect your practice of yoga, or any medications you're currently taking.
- **Medical Concerns** (`medical_concerns`): If you have any medical concerns or worries about your health, please list them here.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person who can reach you in case of an emergency.
- **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): How are you related to the emergency contact person? (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse).
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Authorization** (`emergency_authorization`): Sign your name to confirm that the emergency contact information is correct.
- **Emergency Medical Insurance** (`emergency_medical_insurance`): If you have medical insurance, enter the policy number or name of your insurance provider.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Date of Birth** (`emergency_contact_dob`): Enter the date of birth of the emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Month** (`emergency_contact_dob_month`): Select the month of the emergency contact person's birthdate.
- **Emergency Contact Day** (`emergency_contact_dob_day`): Select the day of the emergency contact person's birthdate.
- **Emergency Contact Year** (`emergency_contact_dob_year`): Select the year of the emergency contact person's birthdate.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (`emergency_contact_relationship_2`): How are you related to the emergency contact person (if different from the first relationship)?
- **Emergency Contact Phone Relationship** (`emergency_contact_phone_relationship`): How does the emergency contact person know you (e.g., Father, Mother, Spouse)?
- **Emergency Contact Name Relationship** (`emergency_contact_name_relationship`): How are you related to the emergency contact person (e.g., Father, Mother, Spouse)?
- **Emergency Authorization 2** (`emergency_authorization_2`): Sign your name to confirm the emergency contact information.
- **Emergency Medical Insurance 2** (`emergency_medical_insurance_2`): If you have medical insurance, enter the policy number or name of your insurance provider.
- **Medical Insurance** (`emergency_contact_medical_insurance`): If you have medical insurance, enter the policy number or name of your insurance provider.
- **Emergency Authorization Signature** (`emergency_authorization_signature`): Sign your name to confirm that the emergency contact information is correct.
- **Date** (`medical_clearance_date`): Not applicable, please ignore.
- **Time** (`medical_clearance_time`): Not applicable, please ignore.
