# Virtual Summer School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Summer School Registration Form is designed to collect basic information from students interested in attending a virtual summer school program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. If the student is a minor, enter the name and contact information of the parent or legal guardian.
3. Select the student's grade level and grade group.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student who is registering for the program.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the student or their parent/legal guardian.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the student or their parent/legal guardian.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the parent or legal guardian if the student is a minor.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Select the grade level of the student.
* **Student Grade Group** (`student_grade_group`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the student's grade group (A, B, or C).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher who is recommending the student for the program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and complete.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* Submission of this form does not guarantee admission to the summer school program.
