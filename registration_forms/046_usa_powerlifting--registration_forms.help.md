# Usa Powerlifting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Usa Powerlifting registration form is designed to collect essential information from athletes for the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and valid information.
2. Review each field to ensure accuracy before submission.
3. Submit the completed form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Gender** (ID: 3): Select your correct gender to identify yourself as a male or female athlete.
* **Birthdate** (ID: 4): Enter your date of birth in the required format.
* **Birthdate Year** (ID: 5): Select the year of your birth from the provided options.
* **Weight Class** (ID: 6): Choose your weight class (Lightweight, Middleweight, or Heavyweight) to be classified accordingly.
* **Email** (ID: 7): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone** (ID: 8): Provide your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact** (ID: 9): Enter your emergency contact name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (ID: 10): Enter your emergency contact phone number.
* **Club Name** (ID: 11): Enter the name of your powerlifting club.
* **Club Address** (ID: 12): Enter the address of your powerlifting club.
* **Club Phone** (ID: 13): Enter the phone number of your powerlifting club.
* **Region** (ID: 14): Select the region you belong to (USA, Canada, or Mexico).
* **City** (ID: 15): Enter the city where your club is located.
* **Club State** (ID: 16): Select the state of your club (California, New York, or Texas).
* **Club Zip** (ID: 17): Enter the zip code of your club.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (ID: 18): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (ID: 19): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (ID: 20): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Parent, Parent/Spouse, or Parent/Other).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (ID: 21): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (ID: 22): Enter the zip code of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact City** (ID: 23): Enter the city of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact State** (ID: 24): Select the state of your emergency contact (California, New York, or Texas).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (ID: 25): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (Parent, Parent/Spouse, or Parent/Other).

## Tips
1. Ensure all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
2. Double-check your emergency contact information for correct relationship and address.
3. Review the form carefully to avoid any potential errors.
