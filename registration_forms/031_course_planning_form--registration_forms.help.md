# Course Planning Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Course Planning Form is a tool used to collect and organize information about courses, allowing administrators or instructors to effectively plan and manage their educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the course title**
	* Enter a brief description of the course being planned.
2. **Add Course Objectives**
	* In this text field, describe the main goals and objectives of the course. This will help the user understand what the course will cover and what to expect.
3. **Plan Your Lessons**
	* In this text field, outline the structure and organization of the course, including lesson plans and activities.
4. **List Resources**
	* Provide a list of materials, textbooks, or other resources that will be used during the course.
5. **Add Notes**
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about the course.
6. **Enter Contact Information**
	* Enter your email address and phone number so that others can contact you with questions or concerns.
7. **Schedule the Course**
	* Enter the date and time of the course, so that others can schedule their participation accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Objectives** (text, required): Enter a brief description of the course's main goals and objectives.
* **Lesson Plans** (text, optional): Outline the structure and organization of the course, including lesson plans and activities.
* **Resources** (text, optional): List the materials, textbooks, or other resources that will be used during the course.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the course.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address so that others can contact you with questions or concerns.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number so that others can contact you with questions or concerns.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the course, so that others can schedule their participation accordingly.
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the course, so that others can schedule their participation accordingly.

## Tips
* Be as detailed as possible when filling out this form, as it will help the course administrators or instructors to better understand and plan the course.
* Use clear and concise language when describing the course and its objectives.
* Make sure to include all relevant information, such as resources and contact details.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it, to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
