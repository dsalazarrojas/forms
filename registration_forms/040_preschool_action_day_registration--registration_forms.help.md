# Preschool Action Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Action Day Registration form is designed to collect demographic and contact information from parents or guardians for the preschool action day event. This information will be used to organize and plan the event, ensuring that each child's needs are met and that the event runs smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the user information section (Fields 1-5) with your child's details.
2. Select the event type that best describes the reason for your child's attendance (Field 2).
3. Enter the date and time of the event (Fields 3-4).
4. Enter your child's name and date of birth (Fields 5-6).
5. Fill in the guardian information section (Fields 7-9) with your contact details.
6. Select the child's school (Field 10).
7. Enter the child's grade level (Field 11).
8. Choose the payment method and amount (Fields 12-13).
9. Add any additional comments or notes about your child's registration (Field 14).
10. Provide any additional comments or feedback (Field 15).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info (Field 1)**: Enter your child's name, which will be used to identify them in the event.
* **Event Type (Field 2)**: Select the reason for your child's attendance, such as "Goals", "Fun", or "Friends".
* **Event Date (Field 3)**: Enter the date of the event, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time (Field 4)**: Enter the time of the event, in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Child Name (Field 5)**: Enter your child's name, which will be used to identify them in the event.
* **Child DOB (Field 6)**: Enter your child's date of birth, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Guardian Name (Field 7)**: Enter your name, as the guardian of the child.
* **Guardian Phone (Field 8)**: Enter your phone number, which will be used as a contact number for the event.
* **Guardian Email (Field 9)**: Enter your email address, which will be used as a contact email for the event.
* **Child School (Field 10)**: Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Child Grade (Field 11)**: Enter your child's grade level, as a number (e.g., 1, 2, 3).
* **Payment Method (Field 12)**: Select the method of payment for the event, such as PayPal or check.
* **Payment Amount (Field 13)**: Enter the amount to be paid for the event.
* **Notes (Field 14)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your child's registration.
* **User Comments (Field 15)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the event or your child's registration.
