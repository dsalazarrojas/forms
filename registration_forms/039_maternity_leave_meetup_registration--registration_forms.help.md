# Maternity Leave Meetup Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Maternity Leave Meetup event. Please complete the form accurately to ensure that your attendance and preferences are recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page of the form carefully to ensure you have provided all required and optional information.
2. Select your session preferences from the options provided in the "Session Preferences" section.
3. Enter your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" field.
4. Confirm your session preferences again in the "Session Preferences 2" section, as this may differ from your initial selection.
5. If you have any special requirements, describe them in the "Special Requirements" field.
6. Enter your name in the required "Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. If you have any additional special requirements, describe them in the "Special Requirements 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it will be displayed in the event materials.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication about the event.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any additional contact purposes.
* **Session Preferences** (`Session Preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select from the options to express your preferences for the event sessions.
* **Session Preferences 2** (`Session Preferences 2`, select_one, optional): Confirm your session preferences for accuracy.
* **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about yourself that may be helpful for the event organizers.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, text, optional): Describe any special requirements or needs you have for the event, such as dietary restrictions.
* **Special Requirements 2** (`Special Requirements 2`, text, optional): Enter any additional special requirements here.
