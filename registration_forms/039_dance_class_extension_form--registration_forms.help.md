# dance_class_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The dance_class_extension_form is a form designed to collect information from students for extending a dance class. This form helps instructors and administrators to gather details about student's extension requests, such as student information, class details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and make sure you understand the questions asked.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Ensure that you've filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Extension Request** (`extension_request`, text, Optional): This field is where you can provide a brief description of your extension request.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, select_one, Optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have information to provide about your student, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Instructor Info** (`instructor_info`, text, Optional): If you have information to share about your instructor, please provide it here.
* **Class Details** (`class_details`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the relevant details about your class extension request.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the student requesting the extension.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the student requesting the extension.
* **Date/Time** (`date_time`, date, Optional): Enter the date and time you would like to schedule the extension for.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, Optional): If you've been assigned a tool for the extension, please provide its details here.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, Optional): This field is not necessary for the form's functionality and can be ignored.
* **Category** (`category`, text, Optional): This field seems redundant and can be ignored.
* **Description** (`description`, text, Optional): Please provide a brief description of your extension request.
* **Category ID** (`category_id`, text, Optional): This field seems redundant and can be ignored.

## Tips
* Ensure that you've filled out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask your instructor or contact your administrator for clarification.
