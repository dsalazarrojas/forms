# Organizational Capability Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants in various organizational capability courses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the participant information form with the required details.
2. Select the courses you wish to register for.
3. Confirm your participant status.
4. Choose your payment method.
5. Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (1)** (`participant_form`, text, required): Enter the participant's name, title, and organization.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_form`, text, required): Enter the participant's contact details, including email and phone number.
* **Select Courses** (`course_selection`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple courses to register for. This field ensures that the participant and course selections are unique.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, note, required): Read and agree to the participant agreement terms.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_form`, text, required): Enter your payment details, including payment method and amount.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (2)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Select Course** (`course_selection_2`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple courses to register for. This field ensures that the participant and course selections are unique.
* **Submit Button 2** (`submit_button_2`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm your registration.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_multiple, required): Choose the participant's status.
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, text, optional): Enter the participant's role.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose the payment method.
* **Payment Details (2)** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter payment details.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
