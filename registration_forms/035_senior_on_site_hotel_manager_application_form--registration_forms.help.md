# Senior On Site Hotel Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for senior on-site hotel managers to apply for a position. The application form collects personal details, work experience, and professional information required for evaluation.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If applicable, describe your work experience in the "Work Experience" field.
5. If you have any professional references, enter their names and job titles in the "References" field.
6. Upload your resume in the "Resume" field, if you have one.
7. Enter your current job title and the name of your company in the "Job Title" and "Company" fields, respectively.
8. Describe the job you are applying for in the "Job Description" field.
9. Enter the number of years of experience you have in the "Years Of Experience" field.
10. Select your work type (full-time, part-time, or temporary) in the "Work Type" field.
11. Select your current job status (available, pending, or unavailable) in the "Job Status" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Work Experience** (`5`, `text`, optional): Describe your previous work experience.
* **References** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter the names and job titles of your professional references.
* **Resume** (`7`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume.
* **Job Title** (`8`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Company** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Job Description** (`10`, `text`, optional): Describe the job you are applying for.
* **Years Of Experience** (`11`, `number`, required): Enter the number of years of experience you have.
* **Work Type** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select your work type (full-time, part-time, or temporary).
* **Job Status** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your current job status (available, pending, or unavailable).
* **Manager** (`14`, `time`, optional): Not applicable for this form.
