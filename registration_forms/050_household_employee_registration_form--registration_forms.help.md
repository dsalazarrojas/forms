# Household Employee Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register household employees for various purposes, such as tracking employee work history, payment schedules, and contact information. It provides a comprehensive way to capture relevant details about an individual's employment status, work shifts, job type, salary, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields with accurate information. You can start by filling in the main information section, which includes details about the individual's contact information and employee status. Then, proceed to the employee information section, where you will provide information about their job type, work shifts, and employment status. 

If you have any work hours, salary, or payment schedule to report, please fill in those fields. Finally, provide any necessary supporting documents or proofs of employment. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Main Info** (`main-info`, `text`, required: false): This section is used to capture the primary details about the household employee. 
* **Contact Info** (`contact-info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Employee Info** (`employee-info`, `text`, required: false): Provide your employee ID or any other identifying information.
* **Employment Status** (`employment-status`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you are currently employed or not.
* **Job Type** (`job-type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose the type of job you are doing.
* **Work Shifts** (`work-shifts`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the days you work or any shifts you commonly work.
* **Work Hours** (`work-hours`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Salary** (`salary`, `number`, required: false): Enter your salary or payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment-date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of your last payment or payment schedule.
* **Contact Person** (`contact-person`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your employment.
* **Phone Number** (`phone-number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email-address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Work Address** (`work-address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your workplace.
* **Household Info** (`household-info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any household information relevant to your employment.
* **Employee Document** (`employee-document`, `text`, required: false): Attach or upload any supporting employment documents.
* **Upload ID Proof** (`upload-id-proof`, `text`, required: false): Upload your ID proof or any other supporting document.
* **Employment Terms** (`employment-terms`, `note`, required: false): Read and agree to the terms of employment.
* **Household Consent** (`household-consent`, `select_one`, required: false): Select whether you are a household member or not.
* **Payment Method** (`payment-method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your payment method (e.g., cash, card, etc.).
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment-frequency`, `select_one`, required: false): Select how often you are paid (e.g., weekly, bi-weekly, etc.).

## Tips

- Ensure that you are providing accurate and complete information about your employment status.
- Check the options for employment status, job type, and payment method carefully to ensure you are selecting the correct one.
- If you are uploading any documents, make sure they are clear and readable.
- Review the employment terms before agreeing to them.
- If you have any questions, do not hesitate to reach out to your contact person.
