# Visa Application Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information and supporting documents for visa application processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info page with your personal details.
2. Select the relevant visa category and subcategory that applies to your application.
3. Enter your passport details, including your passport number and expiry date.
4. Enter your nationality and contact information (email and phone number).
5. Enter your address and upload any supporting documents.
6. Review and submit the form, or cancel/finish the process if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (page 1): 
  Enter your personal details, including your name and any other required information.
* **Visa Category** (page 1):
  Select the type of visa you are applying for, either Business or Private.
* **Sub Category** (page 1):
  Indicate whether you are applying for a subcategory.
* **Applicant Name** (page 1): 
  Enter your full name as it appears on your passport.
* **Passport Number** (page 1): 
  Enter your passport number.
* **Passport Expiry Date** (page 1): 
  Enter the expiry date of your passport.
* **Nationality** (page 1): 
  Enter your nationality.
* **Email** (page 2): 
  Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (page 2): 
  Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (page 2): 
  Enter your full address.
* **Document Upload** (page 3): 
  Upload any supporting documents required for your application.
* **Signature** (page 3): 
  Sign your application (optional).
* **Notes** (page 3): 
  Add any additional notes for the processing officer.
* **Submit** (page 3): 
  Review and submit your application.
* **Cancel** (page 3): 
  Cancel your application and start over.
* **Back** (page 2 and 3): 
  Go back to the previous page.
* **Next** (page 1): 
  Go to the next page.
* **Previous** (page 2 and 3): 
  Go to the previous page.
* **Finish** (page 3): 
  Complete your application.
* **Submit Again** (page 3): 
  Re-submit your completed application.
* **Error Message** (page 3): 
  Display any error messages.
* **Success Message** (page 3): 
  Display success messages.
* **Cancel All** (page 3): 
  Cancel all pages and start over.
* **Finish Screen** (page 3): 
  Finish the application process.
* **Submit All** (page 3): 
  Submit all pages at once.
