# Air Jump Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Air Jump Registration Form is designed to collect essential details for jumpers participating in an air jumping activity. It is crucial to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and safe experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your jumper name, age, and weight in the respective fields.
2. Select your preferred session time from the available options.
3. Provide your emergency contact information.
4. Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may impact your jumping participation.
5. Accept the liability waiver by selecting "I Agree" from the options.
6. Choose your preferred payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Jumper Name** (Required, text)
	+ This is your full name as it will be displayed on your jump certificate.
* **Age** (Required, number)
	+ Please enter your age, which must be 5+ to participate.
* **Weight (lbs)** (Required, number)
	+ This is for safety harness sizing purposes only.
* **Date of Jump** (Required, date)
	+ Select the date you will be attending the jump.
* **Session Time** (Required, select_one)
	+ Choose your preferred jump session time.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (Optional, text)
	+ If you are under 18, provide your parent or guardian's name.
* **Emergency Contact** (Required, text)
	+ Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (Required, select_one)
	+ If you have any medical conditions, select "Yes - I have a condition" from the options.
* **Liability Waiver** (Required, select_one)
	+ Accept the liability waiver by selecting "I Agree".
* **Add Photo Package?** (Required, select_one)
	+ Choose whether to add a photo package to your jump experience.
* **Payment Method** (Required, select_one)
	+ Select your preferred payment method (online or at arrival).
