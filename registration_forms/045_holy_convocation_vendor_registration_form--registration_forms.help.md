# Holy Convocation Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Holy Convocation Vendor Registration Form is designed to gather information about vendors participating in a holy convocation event. This form helps organizers understand the vendor's offerings, payment terms, and other essential details to ensure a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Vendor Information**: Enter your company's or business's name.
2. **Email**: Enter your official email address.
3. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
4. **Contact Number**: Enter the number you'd like to be contacted at.
5. **Address**: Enter your business's address.
6. **City**: Enter the city where your business is located.
7. **State**: Enter the state where your business is located.
8. **Zip**: Enter your business's zip code.
9. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you're participating in.
10. **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
11. **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
12. **Vendors Products**: Choose whether you offer products or not.
13. **Vendors Services**: Choose whether you offer services or not.
14. **Vendors Description**: Provide a brief description of your business.
15. **Payment Terms**: Enter any payment terms or conditions (e.g., "Pay upon arrival").
16. **Other Payment Terms**: Enter any additional or unique payment terms not listed above.
17. **Description of Other Payment Terms**: Briefly explain the other payment terms you've entered.
18. **Vendor Signature**: Sign this section to confirm your information.
19. **Vendor License**: Choose your business's relevant license type (e.g., License 1 or License 2).
20. **Vendor Certifications**: Choose whether you have any vendor certifications.
21. **Vendor Insurance**: Enter your business's insurance information.
22. **Vendor Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about your business.
23. **Vendor Notes**: Add any notes or comments about your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`Vendor Information`, text, required): Enter your company's or business's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the number you'd like to be contacted at.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your business's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required): Enter your business's zip code.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you're participating in.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Vendors Products** (`vendor_products`, select multiple, required): Choose whether you offer products or not.
* **Vendors Services** (`vendor_services`, select multiple, required): Choose whether you offer services or not.
* **Vendors Description** (`vendor_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your business.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Other Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_other`, text, required): Enter any additional or unique payment terms not listed above.
* **Description of Other Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_other_description`, text, required): Briefly explain the other payment terms you've entered.
* **Vendor Signature** (`vendor_signature`, note, required): Sign this section to confirm your information.
* **Vendor License** (`vendor_license`, select one, required): Choose your business's relevant license type.
* **Vendor Certifications** (`vendor_certifications`, select multiple, required): Choose whether you have any vendor certifications.
* **Vendor Insurance** (`vendor_insurance`, text, required): Enter your business's insurance information.
* **Vendor Additional Info** (`vendor_additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your business.
* **Vendor Notes** (`vendor_notes`, note, required): Add any notes or comments about your business.
* **Vendors Agreement** (`vendor_agreement`, note, required): Add any agreement or terms related to your business's participation in the event.
