# Online Fitness Transformation Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in participating in the Online Fitness Transformation Challenge. This form helps us understand your goals, current fitness level, and medical conditions to provide a safe and effective transformation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field.
3. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the Phone Number field.
4. If you have a date of birth, please enter it in the Date of Birth field.
5. Describe your challenge goals in the Challenge Goals field.
6. Enter your current weight in the Current Weight field.
7. Set your target weight in the Target Weight field.
8. Select your fitness level from the Fitness Level dropdown menu.
9. If you have any medical conditions, please describe them in the Medical Conditions field.
10. Tell us about your current fitness frequency in the Current Fitness Frequency field.
11. Select a preferred workout time that suits you best in the Preferred Workout Time field.
12. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and relationship to you in the Emergency Contact field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Please enter your first name as per your official identity documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Please enter your last name as per your official identity documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number (text)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach you.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: If you have a date of birth, please enter it for age verification.
* **Challenge Goals (text)**: Describe your goals for the transformation challenge.
* **Current Weight (number)**: Enter your current weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Target Weight (number)**: Enter your target weight in kilograms or pounds.
* **Fitness Level (select_multiple)**: Select one or more of the following:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Medical Conditions (text)**: If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
* **Current Fitness Frequency (text)**: Describe your current fitness frequency (e.g., how often you exercise).
* **Preferred Workout Time (time)**: Select a time that suits your schedule best for workouts.
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and relationship to you.
