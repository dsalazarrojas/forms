# DIY Ornament Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to register for a DIY Ornament Workshop. It will be used by the workshop organizers to gather necessary details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your workshop details, such as the workshop title, date, and time.
2. Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
3. Enter your payment information, such as the payment method and amount.
4. Enter your shipping information, including the shipping address and any special instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, text, required: false)
* This field is where you can provide the necessary details about the workshop you wish to register for.

### **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the organizers can contact you.

### **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your payment information, such as the payment method and amount. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to complete your registration.

### **Shipping Information** (`shipping_info`, text, required: false)
* This field is where you can enter your shipping information, including the shipping address and any special instructions. Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the workshop materials are delivered to you.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the workshop organizers.
* If you are unable to complete the form online, please contact the workshop organizers to discuss alternative arrangements.
