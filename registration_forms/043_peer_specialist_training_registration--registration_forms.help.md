# Peer Specialist Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for Peer Specialist Training. It collects essential information from trainees and trainers to facilitate the training process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide trainee information on pages 1 and 2.
2. Enter training details on pages 3 and 4.
3. Provide additional information on page 5.
4. Confirm your training status on page 7.
5. Review and submit your application on page 8.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trainee Information** (page 1):
  * **Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on your training certificate.
  * **Email**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Training Details** (page 2):
  * **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of your assigned trainer.
  * **Trainer ID**: Enter the ID of your assigned trainer.
* **Trainer Information** (page 3):
  * **Trainer Name**: Enter the name of your primary trainer.
  * **Trainer ID**: Enter the ID of your primary trainer.
* **Training Details** (page 4):
  * **Training Topic**: Choose the topic of the training you are registering for.
  * **Training Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the training.
* **Additional Information** (page 5):
  * **Emergency Contact**: Provide information for emergency contact purposes.
* **Confirmation** (page 6):
  * **Training Status**: Review your training status and confirm accuracy.
* **Trainer Agreement** (page 7):
  * **Agree to Terms**: Select 'Yes' to confirm you agree to the trainer's terms and conditions.
* **Submission** (page 8):
  * **Submit**: Review and submit your application.
