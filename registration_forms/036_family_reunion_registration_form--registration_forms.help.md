# Family Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register guests for a family reunion. It will help us get in touch with you and ensure a smooth experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details (First name, Last name, Email, Phone).
2. Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
3. Indicate if you have any special dietary requirements.
4. Select the dates and times of your arrival and departure.
5. Provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Please enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Please enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests or requests for our reunion organizers, please enter them here. This is a free-text field where you can add any comments or requests.
* **Number of Guests**: Please enter the total number of guests you are bringing, including yourself.
* **Attending Children**: Enter the number of children you are bringing. If you are not bringing any children, enter 0.
* **Arrival Date**: Please select the date you plan to arrive for the reunion.
* **Departure Date**: Select the date you plan to leave the reunion.
* **Arrival Time**: Select the time you plan to arrive for the reunion.
* **Special Dietary Requirements**: Select any dietary requirements you have (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or requests, please enter them here.
* **Attending Children**: Please note that if you are bringing children, we may have specific childcare arrangements available. If this is not applicable, please select 0.
* **Special Dietary Requirements**: Please select any dietary requirements you have (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.). We will do our best to accommodate your needs.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any other comments or requests, please enter them here.
