# Marriage Preparation Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for couples to register for a marriage preparation course. It helps the course administrators to gather necessary information about the couple's details, relationship dynamics, and other relevant aspects of their relationship.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Couple Details**: Enter your names, contact information, and other relevant couple details in the "Couple Details" field.
2. **Enter Wedding Planner Details**: Provide information about the wedding planner or coordinator in charge of your wedding in the "Wedding Planner Details" field.
3. **Discuss Communication Plans**: Write about your communication plans and strategies in the "Communication Plan" field.
4. **Resolve Conflicts**: Share your conflict resolution plans and strategies in the "Conflict Resolution Plan" field.
5. **Describe Communication Styles**: Explain your communication styles and how you plan to communicate with each other in the "Communication Styles" field.
6. **Address Intimacy Issues**: Discuss any intimacy issues or concerns you have in the "Intimacy Issues" field.
7. **Plan for the Future**: Share your plans for the future, including family and financial planning, in the "Family Planning" and "Financial Planning" fields.
8. **Address Conflict Resolution Questions**: Answer any conflict resolution questions or concerns you have in the "Conflict Resolution Questions" field.
9. **Commit to Change**: Outline your commitment to changing any negative patterns or behaviors that may be affecting your relationship in the "Commitment Planning" field.
10. **Review Communication Plans**: Review and update your communication plans with each other in the "Communication Plans" field.
11. **Develop a Conflict Resolution Plan**: Create a plan to resolve conflicts in the "Conflict Resolution Plan 2" field.
12. **Evaluate Pre-Marriage**: Discuss any pre-marriage evaluations or assessments you've completed in the "Pre Marriage Evaluation" field.
13. **Set Relationship Goals**: Define your relationship goals and objectives in the "Relationship Goals" field.
14. **Attend Marriage Counseling Sessions**: Schedule and plan for marriage counseling sessions, including Session 1, Session 2, and Session 3, as per the provided fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Couple Details** (`id: 1`): Enter your names and contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Wedding Planner Details** (`id: 2`): Provide information about the wedding planner or coordinator in charge of your wedding.
* **Communication Plan** (`id: 3`): Write about your communication plans and strategies for maintaining a healthy relationship.
* **Conflict Resolution Plan** (`id: 4`): Share your conflict resolution plans and strategies.
* **Communication Styles** (`id: 5`): Explain your communication styles and how you plan to communicate with each other.
* **Intimacy Issues** (`id: 6`): Discuss any intimacy issues or concerns you have.
* **Family Planning** (`id: 7`): Share your plans for the future, including family planning.
* **Financial Planning** (`id: 8`): Discuss your financial planning and how you will manage finances together.
* **Conflict Resolution Questions** (`id: 9`): Answer any conflict resolution questions or concerns you have.
* **Commitment Planning** (`id: 10`): Outline your commitment to changing any negative patterns or behaviors that may be affecting your relationship.
* **Communication Plans** (`id: 11`): Review and update your communication plans with each other.
* **Conflict Resolution Plan 2** (`id: 12`): Create a plan to resolve conflicts.
* **Pre Marriage Evaluation** (`id: 13`): Discuss any pre-marriage evaluations or assessments you've completed.
* **Relationship Goals** (`id: 14`): Define your relationship goals and objectives.
* **Marriage Counseling Session 1** (`id: 15`): Schedule and plan for the first marriage counseling session.
* **Marriage Counseling Session 2** (`id: 16`): Schedule and plan for the second marriage counseling session.
* **Marriage Counseling Session 3** (`id: 17`): Schedule and plan for the third marriage counseling session.

## Tips
* Be honest and open when completing this form.
* Answer all fields truthfully and thoroughly.
* Review and update your answers as needed.
* Ask for help if you're unsure or need clarification on any field.
