# Photo Model Release - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photo Model Release form is designed to collect necessary information from models or individuals appearing in photos used in productions. This form helps verify model consent and ensures compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Gather Information
Fill out the form with the model's details, including their name, date of birth, email, contact person, and phone number. You can also indicate which photos were taken and which models were used. Be sure to sign and date the form to confirm the model's release.

### Step 2: Specify Release Details
Enter the release date and type for the photos. This will help to clarify any conditions or restrictions on the use of their likeness.

### Step 3: Review and Finalize
Review the completed form for accuracy and completeness. Make sure all required fields are filled out before submitting. If any sections are unclear or missing, please ask the model for clarification or additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter the model's name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Record the model's date of birth, if necessary for production purposes.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the model's email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Person** (text, optional): Provide the name and contact information for the person handling the model's affairs.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the model's phone number for easy contact.
* **Signature** (note, optional): A space for the model's signature and date.
* **Photo Release Date** (date, optional): Record the date the photo was taken or released.
* **Photo Taken** (select_one, optional): Select which photo(s) the model appeared in.
* **Model Agreement** (select_multiple, optional): Indicate which models were featured in the photo.
* **Release** (date, optional): Enter the date the model's release was granted.
* **Release Type** (text, optional): Specify the type of release granted (e.g., exclusive, non-exclusive).
* **Model Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the model, if different from the name provided earlier.
