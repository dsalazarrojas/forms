# Trade Show Display Booth Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users for a trade show display booth order. It includes fields for display booth details, product information, and contact details. The form is intended to be completed by individuals ordering a trade show display booth.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the form with the required information in each field.
2. Review the form for any errors or missing fields before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Display Booth Information** (`1`): Enter the details of the display booth you wish to order.
* **Company Name** (`2`): Enter the name of the company associated with the trade show display booth.
* **Contact First Name** (`3`): Enter the first name of the contact person associated with the trade show display booth.
* **Contact Last Name** (`4`): Enter the last name of the contact person associated with the trade show display booth.
* **Contact E-mail** (`5`): Enter the email address of the contact person associated with the trade show display booth. This field should be in the format of `user@example.com`.
* **Contact Phone** (`6`): Enter the phone number of the contact person associated with the trade show display booth.
* **Product Information** (`7`): Enter a brief description of the product you wish to display in the trade show booth.
* **Product Description** (`8`): Enter a detailed description of the product you wish to display in the trade show booth.
* **Product Image** (`9`): Enter the URL or file location of the image you wish to display in the trade show booth.
* **Product Price** (`10`): Enter the price of the product you wish to display in the trade show booth.
* **Booth Type** (`11`): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you want a standard booth, or 'No' if you have a special request.
* **Booth Size** (`12`): Select all the booth sizes that apply to your order:
	+ 'Yes' for a standard booth
	+ 'No' for no booth
* **Order Date** (`13`): Enter the date you would like to order the trade show display booth.
* **Order Time** (`14`): Enter the time you would like to order the trade show display booth.
* **Notes** (`15`): Enter any additional information or comments about your trade show display booth order.
