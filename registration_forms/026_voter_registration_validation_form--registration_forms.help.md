# Voter Registration Validation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to validate and collect information from individuals who are interested in registering as voters. Please use this form for its intended purpose only.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Voter's Name** (voter_name, text, \*) - Enter the name of the individual registering as a voter.
* **Address** (address, text, \*) - Enter the street address of the individual.
* **Zip Code** (zip, number, \*) - Enter the zip code of the individual.
* **Date of Birth** (birth_date, date, \*) - Enter the date of birth of the individual.
* **Phone Number** (phone, text, \*) - Enter the phone number of the individual.
* **Email Address** (email, email, *) - Enter the email address of the individual. (Optional)
* **Select One** (select_one, select_one, \*) - Select one of the options provided: Yes, No, or Other.
* **Select Multiple** (select_multiple, select_multiple, *) - Select multiple values from the options provided: Yes, No, or Other. (Optional)
* **Note** (note, note, *) - Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Time** (time, time, *) - Enter the time of the individual. (Optional)
* **Select Multiple 2** (select_multiple_2, select_multiple, \*) - Select multiple values from the options provided: Yes, No, or Other.
* **Time 2** (time_2, time, *) - Enter the time of the individual. (Optional)
* **Note 2** (note_2, note, *) - Enter any additional notes or comments. (Optional)

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble filling out a field, please review the field label for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team.
