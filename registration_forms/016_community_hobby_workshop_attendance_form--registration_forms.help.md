# Community Hobby Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record the attendance and participation details of workshop participants. Fill it out thoroughly to help us better understand your engagement and ensure a smooth experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing the **Hobby Workshop Title** in the first field, e.g., "Pottery", "Woodworking", "Digital Photography", etc.
2. Select the **Session Date** when the workshop took place or when it will take place, using the date picker tool.
3. Enter the **Participant Full Name** as it appears on their official documents (ID, passport, etc.).
4. For **Attendance Status**, choose one of the options provided to indicate their attendance status:
	* **Present**: If the participant attended the workshop as expected.
	* **Absent**: If the participant didn't attend for any reason.
	* **Excused**: If the participant had a valid reason for not attending, e.g., illness, family emergency, etc.
	* **Late**: If the participant arrived late but attended the workshop.
5. If materials were provided to the participant, select **True**; otherwise, select **False**.
6. For **Materials Fee Payment Status**, choose one of the options:
	* **Paid in Full**: If the participant has paid the materials fee in full.
	* **Pending Payment**: If the participant is still to be paid.
	* **Fee Waived**: If the materials fee was waived for this participant.
	* **Not Applicable**: If the workshop doesn't have materials fee.
7. If applicable and relevant, select the **Level of Engagement/Participation** based on your observation:
	* **High - Very active**: If the participant actively engaged with the material.
	* **Medium - Steady participation**: If the participant participated in some parts but not all of the workshop.
	* **Low - Limited interaction**: If the participant showed limited interaction with the material.
8. If you have any additional comments or feedback for the participant, you can enter it in the **Instructor Feedback for Participant** field.
9. If you need to, add any **Participant Questions or Special Notes** for the participant.
10. Finally, select **Yes** or **No** for **Is follow-up required?** to indicate if any follow-up is required for the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hobby Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop, e.g., "Pottery", "Woodworking", "Digital Photography".
* **Session Date**: Select the date when the workshop took place or when it will take place.
* **Participant Full Name**: Enter the participant's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Attendance Status**: Indicate the participant's attendance status.
* **Time of Arrival**: If not required, this field is not applicable.
* **Were materials provided to the participant?**: Select true if materials were provided; false otherwise.
* **Materials Fee Payment Status**: Select the payment status of the materials fee.
* **Level of Engagement/Participation**: Select the observed level of engagement.
* **Instructor Feedback for Participant**: Enter your feedback for the participant.
* **Participant Questions or Special Notes**: Enter any additional questions or notes for the participant.
* **Is follow-up required?**: Select yes if any follow-up is required.
