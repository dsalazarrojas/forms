# Circular Economy Innovation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Circular Economy Innovation Lab Registration Form is used to gather information from interested individuals and organizations that wish to participate in the Lab's activities, such as workshops, networking events, and project collaborations. By providing the required information, you will be able to:

* Get access to the Lab's resources and events
* Connect with other participants in your industry or with similar goals
* Showcase your project or initiative to potential partners and mentors

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Organization or Company information in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the relevant information for the fields "Industry Sector", "Organization Size", "Circular Economy Focus Area", and "Lab Track" that apply to you or your organization.
3. Briefly describe your project or initiative, including its name and description.
4. Specify the stage of development of your innovation and what circular economy focus areas it addresses.
5. Choose which Lab Track best suits your goals and interests.
6. Indicate your level of experience with the circular economy.
7. List your goals for participating in the Lab.
8. Describe any challenges you are facing.
9. Express your interest in collaboration opportunities and mentorship.
10. Select your preferred meeting times.
11. If applicable, provide information on dietary requirements and accessibility needs.
12. Indicate how you heard about us.
13. Provide any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work or personal phone number for communication.
* **Organization or Company**: Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title within your organization.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the industry sector that best describes your organization or project.
* **Organization Size**: Select the number of employees in your organization.
* **Project or Initiative Name**: Enter the name of your project or initiative.
* **Project Description**: Briefly describe your project or initiative.
* **What Stage Is Your Innovation**: Select the stage of development of your innovation.
* **Circular Economy Focus Area**: Select all the focus areas that apply to your project.
* **Which Lab Track Interest You**: Select the Lab Track that best suits your goals and interests.
* **Circular Economy Experience Level**: Indicate your level of experience with the circular economy.
* **What Are Your Goals for This Lab**: List your goals for participating in the Lab.
* **What Challenges Are You Facing**: Describe any challenges you are facing.
* **Interested in Collaboration Opportunities**: Express your interest in collaboration opportunities.
* **Would You Like Mentorship**: Indicate whether you are interested in mentorship.
* **Available Meeting Times**: Select all the meeting times that apply to your schedule.
* **Dietary Requirements**: If applicable, provide information on any dietary requirements.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If applicable, provide information on any accessibility needs.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Indicate how you heard about the Lab.
* **Any Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about yourself or your project.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
