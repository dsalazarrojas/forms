# speaker_meet_and_greet_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to a meet and greet event, provide guest details, specify accessibility needs, and make any special requests.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Select your RSVP response on the "RSVP Response" field.
2. Choose the relevant details for your guests on the "Guest Details" field.
3. Specify any accessibility needs on the "Accessibility Needs" field.
4. Make any special requests on the "Special Requests" field.
5. Provide VIP details, if applicable, on the "VIP Details" field.
6. Specify the event details on the "Event Details" field.
7. Add any relevant speaker information on the "Speaker Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Response** (Select One, required): This field asks you to indicate your response for the meet and greet event.
* **Guest Details** (Select Multiple, required): Please select which guests will be attending the event, choosing from the provided options.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Select Multiple, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please select them from the provided options. This will help organizers make necessary arrangements for a comfortable and enjoyable experience.
* **Special Requests** (Text, optional): If you have any special requests or requirements, please provide them here. This could include dietary restrictions, specific seating needs, or any other requests.
* **VIP Details** (Text, optional): If you are a VIP guest, please provide more information about your attendance here.
* **Event Details** (Select Multiple, optional): Please select the event details that apply to you, such as the event date, time, or location.
* **Speaker Info** (Text, optional): If there is any relevant information about the speaker, please provide it here.
