# Social Workers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Workers Membership Form is designed to collect information from social workers who are interested in becoming members of the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with your contact information and work details.
2. Provide your social information and member details.
3. Upload a member photo (if required).
4. Select your membership type and status.
5. Enter your registration date and member since information.
6. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**: Enter your name and other registration details. (Required: No)
	+ This section is where you can enter your contact information and work details.
* **Contact**: Enter your contact information, such as email and phone number. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your contact information.
* **Work Info**: Enter your work information, such as workplace and work hours. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your work-related information.
* **Social Info**: Enter your social information, such as social media profiles. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your social media profiles.
* **Member Info**: Enter your member information, such as member type and status. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your member information.
* **Emergency Info**: Enter your emergency contact information. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your emergency contact information.
* **Member Photo**: Upload a photo of yourself. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for uploading a photo of yourself.
* **Registration Date**: Enter your registration date. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your registration date.
* **Registered Since**: Enter the date since when you became a member. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your member since information.
* **Membership Type**: Select your membership type (Member or Associate). (Required: No)
	+ This field is for selecting your membership type.
* **Member Since**: Enter the date since when you became a member. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your member since information.
* **Last Updated**: Enter the last updated date. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your last updated information.
* **Member Status**: Select your member status (Active, Inactive, New). (Required: No)
	+ This field is for selecting your member status.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for adding any additional notes or comments.
* **Category**: Select your category (Member or Non-Member). (Required: No)
	+ This field is for selecting your category.
* **Subcategory**: Select your subcategory (Category or Another Category). (Required: No)
	+ This field is for selecting your subcategory.
* **Location**: Select your location (New York, California, Chicago). (Required: No)
	+ This field is for selecting your location.
* **Work Hours**: Enter your work hours. (Required: No)
	+ This field is for collecting your work hours information.
* **Member Since Month**: Select your member since month (January, February, March, etc.). (Required: No)
