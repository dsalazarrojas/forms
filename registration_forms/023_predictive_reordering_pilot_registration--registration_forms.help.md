# Predictive Reordering Pilot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Predictive Reordering Pilot Registration" form is designed to collect information from companies participating in a pilot program. This form helps us understand your company's goals, contact details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section completely and accurately.
2. Make sure all required fields are filled out.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Details
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Company Type**: Select your company type (SaaS, Operations, or Other).
* **Company Location**: Enter your company's location.
* **Company Description**: Provide a brief description of your company.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person filling out the form.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
### Goals
* **Goals**: Enter your business goals. (e.g., increase sales, improve customer service)
* **Other Goals**: If you have any other goals not listed above, enter them here.
### Address Information
* **Company Address**: Enter your company's address.
* **Company Address 2**: Enter any additional address information.
* **City**: Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State**: Select the state where your company is located. (Note: Some states have multiple options, select all that apply.)
* **Zip Code**: Enter your company's zip code.
### Additional Information
* **Company Size**: Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Website**: Enter your company's website.
* **Notes**: Provide any additional information about your company.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to your company. (Note: This field is not required.)
### Consent and Company ID
* **Consent**: Indicate if your company will adhere to our data guidelines.
* **Company ID**: Enter your company's ID (if applicable).
* **Customer Service**: Enter any customer service information (if applicable).
