# E Commerce UX Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide helps you complete the E Commerce UX Certification Registration form. The form is designed to collect information from applicants to assess their eligibility for the E Commerce UX Certification Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your applicant details such as name, email, and phone number.
2.  Select your experience level in E Commerce UX.
3.  State your motivation for obtaining the certification.
4.  Choose the certification programs you are interested in.
5.  If you represent an institution, provide the name of the institution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.
- **Location** (`location`, `text`, required): Enter your location.
- **Experience level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your level of experience in E Commerce UX.
  - Option 1: Entry-level
  - Option 2: Intermediate
  - Option 3: Advanced
- **Motivation** (`motivation`, `note`, optional): State your motivation for obtaining the certification.
- **Certification programs interested** (`certification_programs_interested`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the certification programs you are interested in.
  - eCommece UX Certification
  - Certified ECommerce UX
  - Advanced E-Commerce UX certification
- **Name of institution** (`name_of_institution`, `text`, optional): If you represent an institution, enter the name of the institution.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you are representing an institution, please ensure that the name of the institution is correct.
- Be honest when stating your motivation and experience level. 
- Select all relevant certification programs you are interested in. 
- Use the correct format for your date of birth.
