# Sports Club Member Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential members of a sports club, including their personal details, membership status, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Choose whether the individual is a member of the sports club by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the "Member Type" field.
2. If the individual is a member, proceed to fill out the relevant fields.
3. Fill out the "Member Email", "Phone Number", "Date of Birth", "Home Address", "City", "State/Province", "Postal Code", "Member Since", "Membership End Date", "Membership Status", "Member ID", "Payment Method", "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Phone", "Emergency Contact Email", and "Relationship" fields accordingly.
4. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Type (Member Type)**: Choose whether the individual is a member or not.
* **Membership Type (Membership Type)**: This field is not currently relevant, but it may be used in the future for categorizing members.
* **First name (first name)**: Enter the first name of the individual.
* **Last name (last name)**: Enter the last name of the individual.
* **Member Email (Member Email)**: Enter the email of the individual that they use to access the club's services.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter the phone number of the individual.
* **Date of Birth (date of birth)**: Enter the date of birth of the individual in the format '1999-12-31'.
* **Home Address (Home Address)**: Enter the home address of the individual.
* **City (city)**: Enter the city where the individual resides.
* **State/Province (State/Province)**: Enter the state or province where the individual resides.
* **Postal Code (postal code)**: Enter the postal code of the individual's location.
* **Member Since (Member Since)**: Enter the date the individual has been a member of the club in the format '2019-01-01'.
* **Membership End Date (Membership End Date)**: Enter the date the individual's membership will end in the format '2022-12-31'.
* **Membership Status (Membership Status)**: Choose the current status of the individual's membership, either 'Active' or 'Inactive'.
* **Member ID (Member ID)**: Enter a unique ID number for the individual.
* **Payment Method (payment method)**: Choose whether the individual has paid for their membership.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Emergency Contact Name)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Emergency Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email (Emergency Contact Email)**: Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship (Relationship)**: Choose the relationship of the emergency contact person, either 'Yes' or 'No'.
