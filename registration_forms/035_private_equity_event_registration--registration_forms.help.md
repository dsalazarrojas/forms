# Private Equity Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Private Equity Event Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals and companies interested in attending a private equity event. The form helps the event organizers to keep track of attendees' details, such as their name, company, job title, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for the event ("Registration Forms" or "Event Registration Forms").
2. Enter your event details, including the title of the event.
3. Fill in your personal details, such as your email, phone number, name, company, job title, and role (Investor or Private Equity).
4. Decide whether you want to be contacted for session planning and attendee communications.
5. Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **category**: Select the category for this form ("Registration Forms" or "Event Registration Forms").
* **description**: Enter a brief description of the event or a brief statement of your interest in attending the event.
* **email**: Enter your company email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your business phone number.
* **name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed on event materials.
* **company**: Enter your company name.
* **job_title**: Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **role**: Select whether you are an Investor or Private Equity representative.
* **session_planning**: Decide whether you want to be contacted for session planning purposes.
* **attendee_communications**: Decide whether you want to receive communications about the event and its attendees.
* **notes**: Add any additional notes or comments you would like to share with the event organizers.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Ensure you have selected the correct category for the event.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please include them in the "notes" field.
