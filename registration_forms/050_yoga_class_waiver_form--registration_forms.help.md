# Yoga Class Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from participants before they attend a yoga class. It is meant to be completed by participants to provide their contact information, medical conditions, and waiver of liability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Participant Information, including your name and any relevant details.
2. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information.
3. If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in the class, please provide them in the Medical Conditions field.
4. If you have insurance, please provide your insurance information in the Insurance Information field.
5. Read and sign the Waiver carefully to acknowledge your understanding of the potential risks and liabilities associated with participating in the yoga class.
6. If you wish to share your contact information with the studio instructor or other participants, please select the appropriate option in the Consent to Share and Share with fields.
7. Provide your email address and phone number in the Email and Phone fields, respectively.
8. Finally, provide any additional information you wish to share with the studio instructor or other participants in the Other field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Please provide your name and any relevant details about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact**: If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may impact your ability to participate in the class, please provide them in this field.
* **Insurance Information**: If you have insurance, please provide your policy number, insurance company, and any other relevant details.
* **Waiver**: Please read and sign this section carefully to acknowledge your understanding of the potential risks and liabilities associated with participating in the yoga class.
* **Signature**: Please sign your name to confirm that you have read and understand the waiver.
* **Date**: Please provide the date you are completing this form.
* **Email**: Please provide your email address so that we can contact you with any updates or notifications.
* **Phone**: Please provide your phone number so that we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Consent to Share**: If you wish to share your contact information with the studio instructor or other participants, please select the appropriate option.
* **Share with**: If you selected "Yes" in the Consent to Share field, please select which individuals you wish to share your contact information with.
* **Other**: Please provide any additional information you wish to share with the studio instructor or other participants.
* **Other (if selected)**: If you selected "Other" in the Share with field, please provide the name and contact information of the individual you wish to share your contact information with.
