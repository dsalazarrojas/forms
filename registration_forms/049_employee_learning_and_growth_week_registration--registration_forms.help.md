# employee_learning_and_growth_week_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register employees for employee learning and growth weeks, which are likely initiatives or programs designed to improve employee skills, knowledge, and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the employee's name in the "Employee Name" field.
2. Enter the employee's email address in the "Employee Email" field.
3. Select the employee's department from the options provided.
4. Enter the employee's job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Choose the registration date from the calendar or enter a specific date.
6. If applicable, select the submitter's purpose for submitting the form (e.g., training, certification, evaluation).
7. Choose whether the form is being submitted on behalf of someone else (if applicable).
8. Enter any additional comments or information about the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the employee being registered for the employee learning and growth week.
* **Employee Email**: Enter the email address of the employee being registered.
* **Department**: Select the department of the employee being registered.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the employee being registered.
* **Registration Date**: Choose the date the employee is registered for the employee learning and growth week.
* **Submitter**: Enter your name as the person submitting this form.
* **Learning Objectives**: Enter any specific learning objectives or goals for the employee.
* **Form Fields**: Enter any additional information about the form fields.
* **Submitter Email**: Enter your email address as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Phone**: Enter your phone number as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Address**: Enter your address as the person submitting this form.
* **Submitter Purpose**: Select the reason why you are submitting this form (e.g., training, certification, evaluation).
* **Submitted on Behalf of**: If you are submitting this form on behalf of someone else, select the relevant option.
* **Submitter Project**: Enter any project related to the submission.
* **Submitter Team**: Enter the team or department associated with the submission.
* **Submitter Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about the submission.
* **Submitter Other Purpose**: Enter any other purpose for the submission.
* **Submitter Declaration**: Select the declaration or affirmation of the submitter (e.g., Option A, Option B).
