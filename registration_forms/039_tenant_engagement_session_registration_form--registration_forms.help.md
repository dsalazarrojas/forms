# Tenant Engagement Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a tenant engagement session. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure you can attend the session and receive updates about any changes or cancellations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form fields as prompted, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to register for the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, Optional): Enter your name and other contact information as we have it on file. This is useful for us to get in touch with you regarding any session updates or changes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`, Optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach you about the session.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, `date`, Optional): Choose the time when you expect to start the session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, `date`, Optional): Choose the time when you expect to end the session.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, Optional): Enter the duration of your session in minutes.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of the client or organization you are representing.
 

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
* We recommend you register only once per session to avoid confusion.
* You can always update your registration information later if your circumstances change.
