# Accounting Reconciliation Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Accounting Reconciliation Training Registration Form is used to register for the upcoming reconciliation workshop. Fill out this form to ensure your spot in the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
*   Make sure to choose the correct options for your attendance mode and consent to recording.
*   Select one preferred training date and current skill level from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, text, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **required**): Provide your email address to receive course materials and updates.
*   **Department or Firm** (`department_or_firm`, text, **required**): Enter the name of your department or firm as it appears on official documents.
*   **Current Skill Level** (`skill_level`, select_one, **required**): Choose your current skill level from the options:
    *   Beginner
    *   Intermediate
    *   Advanced
*   **Preferred Training Date** (`preferred_training_date`, select_one, **required**): Select one of the available training dates:
    *   March 15
    *   March 22
    *   April 5
*   **Topics of Interest** (`topics_of_interest`, select_multiple, **optional**): Choose all the topics that interest you:
    *   Bank Reconciliation
    *   Credit Card Reconciliation
    *   Intercompany Reconciliations
    *   Troubleshooting Discrepancies
*   **Current Software Used** (`current_software_used`, text, **optional**): Enter the name of the software you are currently using.
*   **Attendance Mode** (`attendance_mode`, select_one, **required**): Choose how you would like to attend the training:
    *   In-person
    *   Online Webinar
*   **Consent to Recording** (`consent_to_recording`, select_one, **required**): Choose whether to consent to recording the session:
    *   True
    *   False
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, **required**): The date this form is submitted.

Note: All required fields marked with \*\* are mandatory and need to be filled out. Optional fields can be completed if desired.
