# discover_fine_art_fragrances_registration_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register fine art fragrances for various purposes such as Registration Forms, Other, and is used for various tools like chatjimmy and other. The form collects metadata information about the form, including title, description, category, assigned tool, output file, id, and version.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Title** field with a brief description of the form.
2. Enter a detailed description of the form in the **Description** field.
3. Select the **Category** of the form from the provided options (Registration Forms or Other).
4. Choose the **Assigned Tool** from the provided options (chatjimmy or other).
5. Enter the **Output File** if applicable.
6. Enter a unique **ID** for the form.
7. Optionally, enter the **Version** of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **form_title** (text, required: false): Enter a brief title for the form.
- **form_description** (text, required: false): Enter a detailed description of the form.
- **form_category** (select_one, required: false): Select the category of the form from the options (Registration Forms or Other).
- **form_assigned_tool** (select_one, required: false): Choose the tool assigned to the form from the options (chatjimmy or other).
- **form_output_file** (text, required: false): Enter the output file if applicable.
- **form_id** (number, required: false): Enter a unique ID for the form.
- **form_version** (text, required: false): Optionally, enter the version of the form.

## Tips
- Ensure that the form title is descriptive and accurate.
- Use the category and assigned tool options carefully to ensure the form is properly categorized and assigned.
- The output file and ID fields are optional, but they may be required for specific use cases.
