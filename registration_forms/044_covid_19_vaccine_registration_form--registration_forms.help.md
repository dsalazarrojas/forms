# COVID 19 Vaccine Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a COVID-19 vaccine and gather necessary information from registrants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the registrant information section, including your full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and street address.
2. Select your preferred vaccine type and vaccination status.
3. If applicable, fill out the vaccination details section, including dates for the first dose, second dose, and booster dose.
4. Fill out the registration information section, including your reason for registration and preferred vaccination location and appointment date and time.
5. If applicable, fill out the insurance information section, including your insurance status and provider.
6. Fill out the health information section, including any known allergies, chronic conditions, and current medications.
7. Fill out the emergency contact information section, including the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
8. Confirm that the information provided is accurate and indicate that you are ready to complete your vaccination registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: This is your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: This is your email address that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Phone Number**: This is your phone number that we will use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth**: This is your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Street Address**: This is your residential address.
* **City**: This is the city where you live.
* **State**: This is the state where you live.
* **Zip Code**: This is your zip code.
* **Vaccination Status**: This is your current vaccination status.
* **Preferred Vaccine Type**: This is the type of vaccine you prefer.
* **First Dose Date**: This is the date of your first dose, if applicable.
* **Second Dose Date**: This is the date of your second dose, if applicable.
* **Booster Dose Date**: This is the date of your booster dose, if applicable.
* **Reason for Registration**: This is the reason why you are registering for the vaccine.
* **Preferred Vaccination Location**: This is the location where you would like to receive your vaccination.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: This is the date and time you would like to receive your vaccination.
* **Insurance Status**: This is your current insurance status.
* **Insurance Provider**: This is your insurance provider.
* **Insurance Member ID**: This is your insurance member ID.
* **Known Allergies**: This is any known allergies you have.
* **Chronic Conditions**: This is any chronic conditions you have.
* **Current Medications**: This is any current medications you are taking.
* **Immunocompromised**: This indicates whether you have a compromised immune system.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: This is the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: This is the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Registrant**: This is your relationship to the registrant.
* **Agreement**: This confirms that the information provided is accurate.
* **Registration Complete**: This indicates whether you are ready to complete your vaccination registration.

# Tips

* Please double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to a healthcare provider for assistance.
* Please ensure that you have all necessary documents and information before filling out this form.
* This form is for COVID-19 vaccine registration only. If you are registering for other vaccinations, please use the corresponding form.
