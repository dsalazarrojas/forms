# Toastmasters Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toastmasters Membership Form is a comprehensive registration tool designed to gather essential information from members and potential members. It is used by Toastmasters club administrators to track and manage club membership effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions provided below for each field, and ensure you fill out all required fields accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Registration** (`registration_form`, text, false)
This field is intended for any additional information you may wish to provide regarding your membership application or registration.

### 2. **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, false)
Enter your full name as it appears on your official government-issued ID or passport.

### 3. **Email** (`email`, email, false)
Enter your valid email address that you use regularly to receive important communications. This will be used as the primary contact method.

### 4. **Phone** (`mobile_number`, text, false)
Input your mobile phone number to receive important updates and communications from the club.

### 5. **Phone** (`phone_number`, text, false)
Please input your home or office phone number, if applicable.

### 6. **Club Name** (`club_name`, text, false)
Enter the name of your Toastmasters club or the club you wish to join.

### 7. **Club Preferences** (`club_preferences`, select_one, false)
Select the club type that best suits your preferences:
- Toastmasters
- Rotary Club
- Other

### 8. **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, false)
Choose the type of membership you are applying for:
- Full
- Provisional
- Honorary
- Other

### 9. **Membership Date** (`membership_date`, date, false)
Enter the date you wish to become a member or the date you joined the club.

### 10. **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, select_one, false)
Select the status of your membership:
- Active
- Inactive
- Pending

### 11. **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, select_multiple, false)
Choose the level of experience you have in Toastmasters:
- New
- Experienced
- Charter

### 12. **Committee Membership** (`membership_committee`, select_one, false)
Select the committee you would like to join:
- Membership Committee
- Membership Development
- Membership Development Committee

### 13. **Member Since** (`member_since`, time, false)
Input the duration of your membership or the number of years you have been a member.

### 14. **Member Until** (`member_until`, time, false)
Enter the date you plan to terminate your membership, if applicable.

### 15. **Note** (`note`, note, false)
Add any additional comments or notes about your membership or application.

### 16. **Submit** (submit, text, false)
Click the "submit" button once you have filled out all required fields accurately. Review your information before submission to ensure everything is correct.
