# prop_tech_developer_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from property technology (PropTech) developers to provide support and resources for their projects.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the general information section with your name, contact details, and a brief description of your project.
2. Provide details about your team, including the number of team members and your company information.
3. Describe your technical background, including any relevant skills and experiences.
4. Answer the company details questions, including your company name, type, and website.
5. Fill in the project information section, including the project name, description, and website.
6. Answer the financial questions, including the financial status of your project.
7. Describe your goals and objectives for your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (`1`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing your general contact information and a brief description of your project.
	+ Please fill in your name, email, and phone number.
	+ Provide a brief description of your project (1-2 sentences).
* **Team Details** (`2`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing details about your team.
	+ Describe your team members and their roles.
* **Project Info** (`3`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing project details.
	+ Fill in the project name and description.
* **Technical Background** (`4`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing technical background information.
	+ Describe your technical skills and experiences.
* **Company Details** (`5`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing company information.
	+ Fill in your company name and website.
* **Company Name** (`6`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing company name.
	+ Fill in your company name.
* **Company Type** (`7`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section is for selecting your company type.
	+ Select either 'PropTech Team' or 'PropTech Start-up'.
* **Website** (`8`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing your company website.
	+ Fill in your company website.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required/optional): This section is for providing your email address.
	+ Fill in your email address.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing your phone number.
	+ Fill in your phone number.
* **Project Name** (`11`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing project name.
	+ Fill in your project name.
* **Project Description** (`12`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing project description.
	+ Fill in your project description.
* **Project Website** (`13`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing project website.
	+ Fill in your project website.
* **Technical Background Description** (`14`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing technical background description.
	+ Describe your technical background.
* **Technical Background Info** (`15`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing technical background information.
	+ Describe your technical background info.
* **Company Type 2** (`16`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section is for selecting your company type.
	+ Select either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Company Description** (`17`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing company description.
	+ Fill in your company description.
* **Team Size** (`18`, `number`, required/optional): This section is for providing team size.
	+ Fill in your team size.
* **Team Members** (`19`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing team members.
	+ Describe your team members.
* **Project Stage** (`20`, `select_one`, required/optional): This section is for selecting project stage.
	+ Select either 'Development', 'Planning', or 'Launch'.
* **Business Model** (`21`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing business model.

* **Financials** (`22`, `number`, required/optional): This section is for providing financials.

* **Goals** (`23`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing goals.
	+ Fill in your goals.
* **Info** (`24`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for providing additional information.
	+ Describe your project info.

## Tips
