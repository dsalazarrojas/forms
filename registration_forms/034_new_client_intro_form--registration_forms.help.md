# New Client Intro Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about new clients, including their contact information, industry, company, job title, location, and other relevant details. This form will help you understand the client's needs and preferences for onboarding and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), with the requested information.
- Select the relevant options for industry, timezone, preferred communication channel, onboarding frequency, and goals frequency from the provided lists.
- Enter the onboarding date and time in the format `HH:MM:SS` (e.g., 14:30:00).
- Click the "Submit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the client's name, phone number, and email address.
* **Client Information** (`client_info`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the client's business or company.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the client's industry from the options: Technology, Finance, Healthcare, or Other.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter the client's company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the client's job title.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the client's location or city.
* **Introduction** (`introduction`, note, required): Enter a brief introduction or description of the client.
* **Communication Channels** (`communication_channel`, select_multiple, required): Select the communication channels the client prefers, from Email, Phone, and In-app.
* **Notification** (`notification`, date, required): Enter the onboarding date and time in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Select the client's timezone from EST, CST, PST, or Other.
* **Preferred Communication** (`preferred_communication`, select_one, required): Select the client's preferred communication channel from Email, Phone, and In-app.
* **Onboarding Frequency** (`onboarding_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of onboarding, from Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Onboarding Time** (`onboarding_time`, time, required): Enter the onboarding time in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Enter the onboarding date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Goal** (`goal`, text, required): Enter the client's goal or objective.
* **Goals Frequency** (`goals_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the frequency of goals from Daily, Weekly, Bi-Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the submit button to submit the form.
