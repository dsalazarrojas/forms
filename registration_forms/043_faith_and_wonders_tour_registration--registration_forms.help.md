# Registration Forms - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants to help tour operators or event organizers manage and coordinate tour logistics. Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all the required fields (marked as required).
2. Choose your preferred tour dates and select multiple options if applicable.
3. Select your emergency contact person or organization.
4. Provide your email and phone number for communication purposes.
5. Enter your date of birth, age, and gender.
6. Select your preferred language.
7. Enter your address, province, city, and postal code.
8. Choose your preferred meal option and any special requirements.
9. Indicate how you will travel to the tour (car, train, bus, etc.).
10. Select your preferred tour operator and tour dates and time.
11. Finally, take a moment to add any additional notes about your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (1, text): This section is not visible in the provided YAML. However, it may be used for additional participant notes or information that does not fit into other categories.
* **Participant Details** (2, text): Please enter your name and any other relevant details you'd like to share.
* **Preferred Tour Dates** (3, select_multiple): Select multiple tour dates you're available to participate in. 
* **Emergency Contact** (4, select_multiple): Choose a family member or contact person in case of an emergency.
* **Consent** (5, note): A note for any consent or agreement to be shared with the tour operator.
* **Participant Email** (6, email): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (7, text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Participant DOB** (8, date): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Participant Gender** (9, select_one): Select your gender.
* **Participant Age** (10, number): Enter your age.
* **Participant Religion** (11, select_multiple): Select multiple religions you practice. 
* **Participant Address** (12, text): Enter your current address.
* **Participant Province** (13, select_multiple): Select multiple provinces where you live.
* **Participant City** (14, text): Enter the city where you live.
* **Participant Postal Code** (15, text): Enter your postal code.
* **Participant Country** (16, select_multiple): Select multiple countries where you're registered.
* **Participant Language** (17, text): Enter your native language.
* **Participant Notes** (18, note): Add any additional participant notes or information.
* **Participant Tour Dates** (19, time): Select the tour dates you prefer.
* **Participant Tour Time** (20, time): Select the tour time you prefer.
* **Participant Language Preference** (21, select_multiple): Select multiple languages you'd like to communicate in.
* **Participant Meal** (22, select_multiple): Choose your preferred meal option.
* **Participant Special Requirements** (23, select_multiple): Select any special requirements you need during the tour.
* **Participant Vehicles** (24, select_multiple): Indicate how you'll travel to the tour.
* **Participant Tour Operator** (25, select_multiple): Select your preferred tour operator.
