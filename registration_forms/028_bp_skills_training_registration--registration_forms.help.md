# BP Skills Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register employees for training sessions and collect information about their personal and training preferences. It is required that the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly, especially the manager approval status field.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Personal Information**: Fill out the Employee Name and Email Address fields as per your official personnel documents.
2. **Employee Details**: Enter your Employee ID and Department in the corresponding fields.
3. **Training Selection**: Choose the Training Course Name you want to attend, select the relevant Course Category, and choose your preferred Session Date and Session Time.
4. **Manager Approval**: Select 'Yes, approved' in the Manager Approval Status field if your manager has already approved this training.
5. **Additional Information**: If you have any dietary restrictions or accommodation needs, please provide this information in the Additional Information section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Name** (Required): Enter your full name as per your official personnel documents.
* **Employee ID** (Optional): Enter your employee ID number.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your official work email address.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Not required for registration purposes.
* **Department** (Required): Enter your department name.
* **Manager Name** (Optional): Not required but can be used for verification purposes.
* **Training Course Name** (Required): Choose the training course you want to attend.
* **Course Category** (Required): Select the category of the training course.
* **Session Date** (Required): Choose your preferred session date.
* **Session Time Preference** (Optional): Choose your preferred session time (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Full Day, or Online).
* **Preferred Delivery Format** (Optional): Choose your preferred delivery format (In-person classroom, Online/Virtual, Hybrid, or Self-paced).
* **Expected Training Hours** (Optional): Not required but can be used for tracking purposes.
* **Will This Lead to Certification?** (Optional): Select 'True' if the training will lead to certification.
* **Learning Objectives** (Optional): Describe what you hope to gain from this training.
* **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (Optional): If you have any special requirements, enter them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional): If meals are provided, enter any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Manager Approval Status** (Required): Select 'Yes, approved' if your manager has already approved this training.
