# Expo Health & Safety Staff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expo Health & Safety Staff Registration Form is a mandatory registration form for all Expo Health & Safety staff. It captures essential information about each staff member, including their contact details, job roles, and relationships. This form helps ensure that all staff members are registered and accounted for in case of emergencies or other incidents.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your basic contact information:
  - **Staff Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the Expo's staff directory.
  - **Job Title**: Enter your job title or role in the Expo's Health & Safety team.
2. Add your contact details:
  - **Email**: Enter your email address that you would like to be contacted on in case of emergencies or other incidents.
  - **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
3. Complete the emergency contact section:
  - **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  - **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
  - **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
  - **Relationship**: Briefly describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
4. Upload your image (optional):
  - **Staff Image**: Upload a clear, recent photo of yourself. This will help other staff members identify you easily.
5. Select your job role:
  - **Job Role**: Choose your role in the Health & Safety team from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Staff Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the Expo's staff directory.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or role in the Expo's Health & Safety team.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional but recommended for record-keeping purposes).
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship**: Briefly describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Staff Image**: Upload a clear, recent photo of yourself for identification purposes.
* **Job Role**: Choose your role in the Health & Safety team from the options provided.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form successfully.
- Ensure that your emergency contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
- If you have any difficulties or questions, please contact your supervisor or the Health & Safety team.
