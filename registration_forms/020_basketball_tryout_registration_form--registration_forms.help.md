# Basketball Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential details from participants for the basketball tryout process. It is crucial that participants provide accurate and complete information to avoid any delays or rejections during the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely before submitting.
2.  Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
3.  Provide a valid email address that can be used for communication.
4.  Enter your phone number for efficient communication with coaches and staff.
5.  Specify your current grade level.
6.  Choose your preferred playing position: Guard, Forward, or Center.
7.  Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5 foot 10 inches).
8.  If applicable, enter your weight in pounds.
9.  Choose your experience level in basketball: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Competitive Club.
10.  If necessary, provide additional comments or information about your skills, concerns, or achievements.
11.  Read and agree to the program rules and coaching directions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication with coaches and staff.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for efficient communication with coaches and staff.
* **Current Grade** (`school_grade`, number, required): Specify your current grade level.
* **Preferred Position** (`favorite_position`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred playing position: Guard, Forward, or Center.
* **Height** (`height_measurement`, text, required): Enter your height in feet and inches (e.g., 5 foot 10 inches).
* **Weight (lbs)** (`weight_measurement`, number, optional): If applicable, enter your weight in pounds.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_rating`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level in basketball: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Competitive Club.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If necessary, provide additional comments or information about your skills, concerns, or achievements.
* **Registration Terms** (`terms_header`, note, required): Read and agree to the program rules and coaching directions.
* **I Agree to Follow the Program Rules and Coaching Directions** (`accept_rules`, select_one, required): Choose "I Agree" to confirm your agreement to the program rules and coaching directions.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_timestamp`, date, required): The date and time of your submission.

Note: The form is designed to be filled out accurately and completely, ensuring a smooth and efficient registration process.
