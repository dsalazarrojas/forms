# Preventive Care Research Collaboration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from researchers and institutions interested in collaborating on preventive care research projects.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select your response to the question "Research Collaboration" by choosing between "Yes" and "No".
2. Enter your name in the field "Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field "Phone".
5. Enter your institution in the field "Institution".
6. Select your response to the question "Research Interest" by choosing between "Yes" and "No".
7. Enter your research area in the field "Research Area".
8. Enter your department in the field "Department".
9. Select your research focus areas by choosing between "Yes" and "No" in the field "Research Focus".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Research Collaboration** (`research_collaboration`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate if you are interested in collaborating on a research project.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you are interested in collaborating, and "No" if not.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your name for contact purposes.
	+ Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your email address for communication purposes.
	+ Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your phone number for communication purposes.
	+ Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Institution** (`institution`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your institution or affiliation.
	+ Please enter your institution as it is known.
* **Research Interest** (`research_interest`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to indicate if you have a research interest.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you have a research interest, and "No" if not.
* **Research Area** (`research_area`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your research area or interest.
	+ Please enter a brief description of your research area.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required: false): This field is used to enter your department or affiliation.
	+ Please enter your department as it is known.
* **Research Focus** (`research_focus`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select your research focus areas.
	+ Please select "Yes" if you are interested in a particular research focus area, and "No" if not.

## Tips

* Please take a few minutes to fill out this form carefully to ensure we have an accurate picture of your interests and needs.
* You can always contact us if you need any clarification or have questions about this form.
