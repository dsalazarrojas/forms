# Guest Communication System Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential details from guests about their properties and communication preferences, enabling efficient and personalized communication within the Guest Communication System Pilot.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your property details in the "Guest Property Details" section.
2. Enter your property's address in the "Guest Property Address" field.
3. If you have a property phone number, please enter it in the "Guest Property Phone" field.
4. If applicable, provide the name of the lead handling your registration in the "Guest Communication Pilot Lead" field.
5. If applicable, provide the name of your manager in the "Guest Communication Pilot Manager" field.
6. Select your property's status (Active, Inactive, or Pending) in the "Guest Communication Pilot Status" field.
7. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
8. If desired, enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. The "Date Created" field is automatically populated and should not be edited.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest Property Details** (text): Enter a brief description of your property.
  - This field is a text field where you can provide any information you'd like to share about your property.
- **Guest Property Address** (text): Enter your property's address.
  - This field is where you can enter your property's physical address.
- **Guest Property Phone** (text): Enter a valid 10-digit number for your property's phone.
  - This field is for entering a phone number that can be used for communication with your property.
- **Guest Communication Pilot Lead** (text): Enter the name of the lead handling the registration.
  - If someone is handling the registration for your property, enter their name here.
- **Guest Communication Pilot Manager** (text): Enter the name of your manager.
  - If you have a manager handling your communication, enter their name here.
- **Guest Communication Pilot Status** (select_one): Select the status of your property (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
  - This field lets you choose your property's status within the guest communication pilot.
- **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address for your property.
  - This is where you can enter a contact email for your property.
- **Phone** (text): Enter a 10-digit number for your property's phone.
  - If you have a phone number, you can enter it here.
- **Date Created** (date): This field is automatically populated and should not be edited.
  - This field shows the date when the form was created, but it should not be updated manually.
