# Mergers And Acquisitions Readiness Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mergers and Acquisitions Readiness Program is a program designed to help teams or organizations prepare for mergers and acquisitions. This form is used to collect information about participants in the program, including their team or organization details, contact information, and any additional notes or details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Select Your Team or Organization
* **Team Leadership** (`Team Leadership`, Select One, required): Choose your team's leadership type from the options provided. Please select one of the following options:
	+ Advisors
	+ Consultants
	+ Corporate Team Members
	+ Other

### Step 2: Add Your Team's Information
* **Team Organization** (`Team Organization`, Text, optional): Enter a short description of your team or organization.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, Note, optional): Add any additional information about your team.

### Step 3: Provide Your Contact Information
* **Email** (`Email`, Email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, Text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`Date`, Date, optional): Enter your date of birth or establishment date.
* **Time** (`Time (24hr)`, Time, optional): Enter your availability in 24-hour format.

### Step 4: Add Participant's Information
* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, Text, optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, Email, optional): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, Text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Date** (`Participant Date`, Date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth or establishment date.
* **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, Note, optional): Add any additional information about the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Leadership**: Choose your team's leadership type from the options provided. This will help us understand your team's structure and composition.
* **Team Organization**: Enter a short description of your team or organization, providing context on your team's background and goals.
* **Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for further communication and clarification.
* **Date**: Enter your date of birth or establishment date, which will help us understand your team's age or duration.
* **Time**: Enter your availability in 24-hour format, which will help us schedule meetings and events.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information about your team, which will help us better understand your needs and requirements.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant, who will be the main contact person for your team.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the participant's email address, which will be used for further communication and updates.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number, which will be used for further communication and clarification.
* **Participant Date**: Enter the participant's date of birth or establishment date, which will help us understand their background and requirements.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional information about the participant, which will help us better understand their needs and requirements.

## Tips

* Please ensure you have completed all fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any doubts or queries, please contact us for further assistance.
* Please note that this form is required for all participants in the Mergers and Acquisitions Readiness Program.
