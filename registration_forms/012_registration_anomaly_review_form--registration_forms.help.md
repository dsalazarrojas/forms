# Registration Anomaly Review Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to review and investigate anomalies in registrations. It's a crucial process to ensure the accuracy and consistency of the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by selecting the correct category from the "Category" field. This will help identify the type of registration being reviewed.
- Fill in the "Title" field with a brief description of the anomaly or issue being investigated.
- Enter the "Form ID" field with the specific registration ID being reviewed.
- Select whether "Form IDs" are present or not.
- Choose the review status from the "Review Status" field, indicating whether the review is pending, approved, or rejected.
- Enter the date and time of the review in the "Review Date" and "Review Time" fields.
- Fill in the "Reviewer" field with your name as the reviewer.
- Enter your comment or feedback in the "Reviewer Comment" field.
- Describe the "Action Taken" to resolve the anomaly or issue.
- Enter any follow-up information or next steps in the "Follow-up" and "Next Steps" fields.
- Enter your Reviewer ID and reviewer's email address for identification and contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Category** (Registration Forms/Report Forms)
Select the category for which this anomaly or issue is being reviewed.

### 2. **Title** (Registration Anomaly Review Form Title)
Enter a brief description of the anomaly or issue being reviewed.

### 3. **Form ID** (Form ID)
Enter the specific registration ID being reviewed.

### 4. **Form IDs** (Form IDs)
Indicate whether form IDs are present or not.

### 5. **Output File** (Output File)
This field is currently not required and has no explanation.

### 6. **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool)
This field is currently not required and has no explanation.

### 7. **Review Status** (Review Status)
Select the status of the review: "Pending", "Approved", or "Rejected".

### 8. **Review Date** (Review Date)
Enter the date of the review.

### 9. **Review Time** (Review Time)
Enter the time of the review.

### 10. **Reviewer** (Reviewer)
Enter your name as the reviewer.

### 11. **Reviewer Comment** (Reviewer Comment)
Enter your comment or feedback on the anomaly or issue.

### 12. **Action Taken** (Action Taken)
Describe the action taken to resolve the anomaly or issue.

### 13. **Follow-up** (Follow-up)
Enter any follow-up information or next steps.

### 14. **Next Steps** (Next Steps)
Enter any next steps or actions to be taken.

### 15. **Reviewer ID** (Reviewer ID)
Enter your Reviewer ID for identification purposes.

### 16. **Reviewer's Email** (Reviewer's Email)
Enter your reviewer's email address for contact purposes.
