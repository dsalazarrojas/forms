# cultural_festival_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration to a cultural festival. It helps festival organizers to collect information from participants, such as their name, contact number, email, and dates of attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Festival Name field with the name of the festival you are registering for.
2. Enter your Participant Name in the Participant Name field.
3. Provide your contact number in the Contact Number field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Select the dates you plan to attend the festival in the Dates field.
6. (Optional) If you have a preferred start date for the festival, you can enter it in the Start Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the festival you are registering for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be listed on your badge and in festival communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number, which will be used for communication with you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication with you.
* **Dates** (`dates`, date, required): Select the dates you plan to attend the festival.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, time, optional): If you have a preferred start date for the festival, enter it here.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, please contact the festival organizers.
* This form is part of a larger system and might be used in conjunction with other forms and systems, please ensure that you are filling it out correctly for the intended purpose.
