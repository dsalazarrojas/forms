# Portfolio Construction Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Portfolio Construction Event. Please fill it out to provide your information and preferences to event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information.
2. Review each field for its specific guidelines and requirements.
3. Submit the form to register for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: 
    * (No specific information provided) - This field is likely meant to collect the details of the event, such as the event title.
* **Session Interest**:
    * (Select One): This field allows you to select one option from the available choices. Please choose the session that interests you the most.
* **Attendee Needs**:
    * (Select Multiple): This field allows you to select multiple options from the available choices. Please choose the needs you require to make the most of your experience at the event.
* **Email**: 
    * (Email): This field is for your contact email address. Please enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: 
    * (Phone Number): This field is for your contact phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Event Name**: 
    * (Text): This field is for you to enter the name of the event. Please provide the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Datetime**:
    * (Date): This field is for you to enter the date and time of the event. Please use the format mm/dd/yyyy and 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 for 2 PM).
* **Event Duration**:
    * (Number): This field is for you to enter the duration of the event. Please enter the number of minutes or hours the event will last.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the required fields.
- Double-check your selections before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, please contact the event organizers or administration.
