# Event Coordination Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Coordination Training Registration Form is a form used to collect information from users who want to register for the Event Coordination Training program. It helps us understand user preferences and requirements to tailor the training experience accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and preferences.
2. Select the sessions you are interested in by checking the corresponding boxes.
3. Choose the dates and start times that fit your schedule.
4. Select the duration of each session that suits you best.
5. Review and submit your form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your name and any other relevant information.
* **Session Selections** (`session_selections`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the sessions you are interested in by checking the corresponding boxes. You can choose from multiple sessions.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required: false): Choose the dates you are available to attend sessions on. Select one date from the options provided.
* **Training Start Time** (`training_start_time`, `time`, required: false): Select the start time of the session that suits you best. Please note that this field is not always displayed as it's related to the session selections you make.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, `number`, required: false): Choose the duration of each session that fits your schedule. Please note that this field is not always displayed as it's related to the session selections you make.
* **Session Selections 2** (`session_selections_2`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Similar to the first session selection field, choose the sessions you are interested in for the second set of dates provided.
* **Session Date 2** (`session_date_2`, `date`, required: false): Choose the dates you are available to attend sessions on for the second set of dates provided.
* **Training Start Time 2** (`training_start_time_2`, `time`, required: false): This field is related to the sessions you selected for the second set of dates provided.
* **Training Duration 2** (`training_duration_2`, `number`, required: false): This field is related to the duration of the second set of sessions you selected.
* ... (fields 3-6 have similar explanations)
* **Session Selections 6** (`session_selections_6`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the sessions you are interested in for the sixth set of dates provided.
* **Session Date 6** (`session_date_6`, `date`, required: false): Choose the dates you are available to attend sessions on for the sixth set of dates provided.
* **Training Duration 6** (`training_duration_6`, `number`, required: false): This field is related to the duration of the sixth set of sessions you selected.
