# Football League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a player and their team in the football league. It collects information about the player, team, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Team Name, Email, Phone, Player Name, Player Age, Player Position, Team Experience, Team Age, and Team Gender.
2. Choose the correct options for Player Experience, Player Position, Team Experience, Team Gender, and Player Height Unit.
3. Upload a photo of the player and team (if required).
4. Enter any additional notes about the player and team.
5. Click the "Save and Send to Jotform" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name (18)**: Enter the name of the team that the player is registering with. 
* **Team Position (19)**: Describe the team's position in the league (e.g., top, middle, bottom).
* **Team Experience (20)**: Enter the team's experience level in the league.
* **Team Age**: Enter the team's age in years.
* **Team Gender (22)**: Select the team's gender.
* **Team Height (23)**: Enter the team's height.
* **Player Name (5)**: Enter the name of the player registering.
* **Player Age (6)**: Enter the age of the player in years.
* **Player Position (7)**: Select the player's position in the league (e.g., Goalkeeper, Defense, Midfielder, Forward).
* **Player Experience (8)**: Select the player's experience level in the league.
* **Player Gender (9)**: Select the player's gender.
* **Player Height (11)**: Enter the player's height.
* **Player Weight (12)**: Enter the player's weight.
* **Player Height Unit (13)**: Select the unit of measurement for the player's height (e.g., cm, m, inches, feet).
* **Player Photo (13)**: Upload a photo of the player.
* **Player Notes (14)**: Enter any additional notes about the player.
* **Team Photo (15)**: Upload a photo of the team.
* **Team Notes (16)**: Enter any additional notes about the team.
* **Submit (17)**: Choose the action to take after completing the form.
