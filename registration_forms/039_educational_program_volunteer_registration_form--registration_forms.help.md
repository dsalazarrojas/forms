# Educational Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering volunteers for educational programs. It collects information about the volunteer's name, contact details, volunteer position, program interest, volunteer start and end dates, and other relevant details. The form is used to track and manage volunteer registrations for educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your volunteer position (e.g., Volunteer, Program Coordinator, Assistant Director).
6. Choose your program interest (e.g., Educational, Community, Research).
7. Enter the date you started volunteering.
8. Enter the date you plan to end volunteering.
9. Add any additional notes.
10. Confirm the start and end dates of your volunteering.
11. Select your volunteer status (e.g., In Progress, Complete, On Hold).
12. Choose the tool assigned to you (e.g., chatjimmy, Other).
13. Select the type of program (e.g., Online, In-person, Hybrid).
14. Choose the type of volunteer (e.g., Individual, Group, Family).
15. Select how often you volunteer (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
16. Enter the number of volunteer hours you have completed.
17. Enter the date you were assigned to the program.
18. Enter the time you were assigned to the program.
19. Enter the start date of the program.
20. Enter the end date of the program.
21. Select your volunteer agreement status (e.g., Accepted, Declined, Pending).
22. Select the status of the program (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
23. Choose the tool ID assigned to you (e.g., 123, 456, 789).
24. Choose the ID of the form you are filling out (e.g., 18508, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Position** (`volunteer_position`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select your volunteer position (e.g., Volunteer, Program Coordinator, Assistant Director).
* **Program Interest** (`program_interest`, Select One, Optional): Choose your program interest (e.g., Educational, Community, Research).
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you started volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you plan to end volunteering.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes about your volunteering experience.
* **Confirm Volunteer Start Date** (`confirm_volunteer_start_date`, Time, Optional): Confirm the start date of your volunteering.
* **Confirm Volunteer End Date** (`confirm_volunteer_end_date`, Time, Optional): Confirm the end date of your volunteering.
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, Select One, Optional): Select your volunteer status (e.g., In Progress, Complete, On Hold).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Select One, Optional): Choose the tool assigned to you (e.g., chatjimmy, Other).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the type of program (e.g., Online, In-person, Hybrid).
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the type of volunteer (e.g., Individual, Group, Family).
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select how often you volunteer (e.g., Daily, Weekly, Monthly).
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, Number, Optional): Enter the number of volunteer hours you have completed.
* **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date you were assigned to the program.
* **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time you were assigned to the program.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Volunteer Agreement** (`volunteer_agreement`, Select One, Optional): Select your volunteer agreement status (e.g., Accepted, Declined, Pending).
* **Program Status** (`program_status`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select the status of the program (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Assigned Tool ID** (`assigned_tool_id`, Select One, Optional): Choose the tool ID assigned to you (e.g., 123, 456, 789).
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, Select One, Optional): Choose the ID of the form you are filling out (e.g., 18508, Other).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* Review your form before submission to ensure all fields are filled correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your supervisor or program administrator.
