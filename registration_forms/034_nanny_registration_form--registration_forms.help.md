# nanny_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nanny registration form is designed to collect information from potential nannies about their qualifications, experience, and availability. This information will be used to determine suitability for nanny positions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary documents to upload.
* Fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address to ensure it is correct.
* Be honest about your availability and experience.
* If you have any issues or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Experience** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Please enter your years of experience as a nanny.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, select_multiple, required): Please select the days you are available to work.
* **Available Time** (`available_time`, time, required): Please select the time you are available to work.
* **Availability Dates** (`availability_dates`, date, optional): Please select your availability dates.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please enter your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., mother, father, sibling).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email of your emergency contact person.
* **First Language** (`first_language`, select_one, optional): Please select the language you are proficient in.
* **Second Language** (`second_language`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the second language you are proficient in.
* **Third Language** (`third_language`, select_multiple, optional): Please select the third language you are proficient in.
* **References** (`references`, note, optional): Please provide a brief description of your work experience and qualifications.
* **References Name** (`references_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your reference.
* **References Job Title** (`references_job_title`, text, optional): Please enter the job title of your reference.
* **References Relationship** (`references_relationship`, text, optional): Please enter your relationship with the reference.
* **References Phone** (`references_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of your reference.
* **References Email** (`references_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email of your reference.
* **References Start Date** (`references_starting_date`, date, optional): Please select the start date of your work with the reference.
* **References End Date** (`references_ending_date`, date, optional): Please select the end date of your work with the reference.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, text, optional): Please upload any necessary documents, such as identification or certifications.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required): Please click the submit button to complete the form.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please describe your relationship with the person you are providing as reference.
