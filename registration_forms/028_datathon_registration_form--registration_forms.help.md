# Datathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants of the Datathon event. It includes multiple pages, each with its own fields and purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
Before proceeding, make sure you have all the necessary information handy. This will make the process smoother and help avoid any errors.

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form pages and fields.
2. Fill in each field according to your needs, following the labels and instructions provided.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Data**: This page is the starting point for your registration. It is an optional field and can contain any relevant text.
* **Participant Name**: This field is for your name, which will be used for identification and communication purposes.
* **Participant E-mail**: This is your email address, which will be used for communication and updates regarding the event. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Participant Phone**: This field is for your phone number, which will be used for any necessary contact or communication.
* **Participant Gender**: Please select your gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other). This will help us get to know our participants better.
* **Participant Gender (Other)**: If you select 'Other' in the previous field, you can fill in this optional field with your preferred gender description.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: You can choose the date of your birth using the calendar provided in this field.
* **Participant Date of Birth Year**: This field is for the year of your birth. Please fill in the number corresponding to the year of your birth.
* **Participant Date of Birth Month**: This field is for the month of your birth. Please fill in the month corresponding to your birth.
* **Participant Date of Birth Day**: This field is for the day of your birth. Please fill in the number corresponding to the day of your birth.
* **Participant Date of Birth Time**: This field is for the time of your birth, if applicable. Please fill in the time using 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Participant Company**: This field is for your company affiliation, if any. You can fill it in with your company name, department, or any other relevant information.
