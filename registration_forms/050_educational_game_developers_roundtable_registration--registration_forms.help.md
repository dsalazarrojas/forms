# Educational Game Developers Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Game Developers Roundtable Registration form is designed for game developers who want to participate in the roundtable discussion. By filling out this form, you'll be able to join the event and share your expertise and experiences with other game developers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: `Event Name`, `Email`, `Phone`, and `Session Time` are currently not required, but `Email` is recommended. Please provide the necessary information for these fields.
2. Select your `Roles` from the list: choose one or more of the options `Developer`, `Organizer`, `Sponsor`, or `Attendee`.
3. Select your session `Interests` from the list: choose one or more of the options `AI and Machine Learning`, `Game Design`, `Game Engines`, `Game Engines and Tools`, `Game Theory`, `Game Writing`, `Mathematics and Algorithms`, `Physics Engines`, `Programming`, `Storytelling`, or `Virtual Reality`.
4. Select your `Discussion Topics` from the list: choose one or more of the options `AI and Machine Learning`, `Business and Marketing`, `Game Engines and Tools`, `Game Theories and Mechanic`, `Mathematics and Algorithm`, `Narrative and Storytelling`, `Physics and Math`, `Programming`, or `Virtual Reality`.
5. Fill in the `Email` field with a valid email address.
6. Fill in the `Phone` field with your phone number.
7. Select the `Session Time` for the roundtable discussion.
8. Fill in the `Event Date` field with the date of the event.
9. Fill in the `Note` field with any additional information you'd like to provide.
10. If applicable, select the `Assigned Tool` field with the tool assigned to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you want to attend.
* **Roles** (`roles`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the options: `Developer`, `Organizer`, `Sponsor`, or `Attendee`.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options `AI and Machine Learning`, `Game Design`, `Game Engines`, `Game Engines and Tools`, `Game Theory`, `Game Writing`, `Mathematics and Algorithms`, `Physics Engines`, `Programming`, `Storytelling`, or `Virtual Reality`.
* **Discussion Topics** (`discussion_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the options `AI and Machine Learning`, `Business and Marketing`, `Game Engines and Tools`, `Game Theories and Mechanic`, `Mathematics and Algorithm`, `Narrative and Storytelling`, `Physics and Math`, `Programming`, or `Virtual Reality`.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Fill in your phone number.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Select the session time for the roundtable discussion.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Fill in the event date.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Fill in the `Note` field with any additional information you'd like to provide.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If applicable, select the tool assigned to you.
