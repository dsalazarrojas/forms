# Ethical Data Usage In Academia Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who will be handling sensitive data in academic settings, ensuring they understand and agree to follow ethical guidelines for data usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the relevant fields that apply to your role in academia (Student, Researcher, Faculty).
2. Enter your University Name.
3. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
4. Provide a valid Email address.
5. Enter your Phone number.
6. Select the roles that apply to you (Student, Researcher, Faculty).
7. Choose your commitment to the ethical guidelines for data usage in academia (you can select one or more).
8. If necessary, fill in your Institution and Department.
9. Confirm your understanding and commitment to the ethical guidelines for data usage in academia by selecting the correct option.
10. Add any additional Comments if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **University Name** (`university_id`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the university you are affiliated with.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Select the role you hold in the university (Student, Researcher, Faculty).
- **Commit To Ethical Guidelines** (`commit_to_ethics`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your understanding and commitment to the ethical guidelines for data usage in academia.
- **Institution** (`institution`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your institution (if different from the University).
- **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your department (if applicable).
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your understanding and commitment to the ethical guidelines for data usage in academia.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information about your data usage habits.
