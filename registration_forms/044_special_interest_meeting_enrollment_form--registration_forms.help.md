# Special Interest Meeting Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow individuals to register their interest in attending Special Interest Meetings. Each registration is specific to a particular meeting, so please only submit one registration per meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the meeting date you are interested in attending.
2. Choose your preferred meeting time.
3. Enter the meeting topic you are interested in.
4. Enter the location of the meeting.
5. Enter your name.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number (if applicable).
8. List your interests.
9. Choose your preferred meeting day (Monday to Sunday).
10. Select your preferred meeting time.
11. Select your preferred meeting day.
12. Confirm that you agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **meeting_date**: Select the date of the meeting you are interested in attending. This should match the date of the meeting you're registering for.
* **meeting_time**: Choose your preferred time for the meeting. This will help us coordinate the schedule.
* **meeting_topic**: Enter the topic or theme of the meeting you're interested in.
* **meeting_location**: Enter the location of the meeting. This could be a physical address or a virtual meeting link.
* **participant_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport).
* **participant_email**: Enter your official email address.
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **participant_interests**: List your areas of interest or expertise.
* **preferred_meeting_time**: Select your preferred time slot for the meeting.
* **preferred_meeting_day**: Choose your preferred day of the week for the meeting.
* **participant_preferred_meeting_time**: Enter your preferred time for the meeting, if different from the main meeting time.
* **participant_preferred_meeting_day**: Choose a preferred day of the week for your preferred meeting time.
* **participant_agree1**/.../**participant_agree3**: Please confirm that you agree to the terms of the meeting by checking the boxes provided. These fields are not meant to be filled in, but rather checked.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in your information accurately and completely.
* Double-check the meeting date and time to avoid confusion.
* Be clear and concise in your interest statements.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, reach out to us before submitting the form.
* Each meeting has a separate registration form. Please register for each meeting separately if you wish to attend multiple meetings.
