# STEM Fair Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The STEM Fair Attendance Form is a registration form designed for event organizers to track the attendance of attendees at the STEM fair. This form collects information such as contact details, role, department, event date and time, attendance status, and additional remarks or comments. The form is crucial for event organizers to keep track of attendees' information and feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Fill in your First Name**: Enter your first name in the text field marked "First Name".
2. **Fill in your Last Name**: Enter your last name in the text field marked "last_name".
3. **Enter your Email**: Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please ensure it is formatted as a standard email address (e.g., `[username}@example.com`).
4. **Enter your Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. **Select your Role**: Choose your role at the event from the dropdown menu labeled "role". Choose "Participant" if you're attending as a participant, "Guest" if you're attending as a guest, or "Team Member" if you're part of a team.
6. **Select your Department (Optional)**: If applicable, select one or multiple departments you're associated with from the dropdown menu labeled "department".
7. **Choose the Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the "event_date" field. Use the standard date format (e.g., 2024-03-01).
8. **Choose the Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in the "event_time" field. Use the standard time format (e.g., 14:30).
9. **Mark your Attendance**: Select your attendance status from the dropdown menu labeled "attendance". Choose "Present" if you're attending, "Absent" if you're not, or "Unknown" if you're unsure.
10. **Add any Remarks**: If you have any additional comments or remarks, enter them in the "remarks" field.
11. **Select who Submited this Form**: Choose who submitted this form from the dropdown menu labeled "submitted_by". Choose "Event Organiser" if you're the event organiser, "Participant" if you're a participant, or "Team Member" if you're part of a team.
12. **Enter Date submitted**: This field is required for the form submission time stamp and should be in the standard date format (e.g., 2024-03-01).
13. **Enter any Comments (Optional)**: If you have any additional comments, please enter them in the "comments" field.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required: false): Choose your role at the event from the dropdown menu.
* **Department** (`department`, select_multiple, required: false): If applicable, select one or multiple departments you're associated with from the dropdown menu.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event in the standard date format.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event in the standard time format.
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, select_one, required: false): Choose your attendance status from the dropdown menu.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required: false): Choose who submitted this form from the dropdown menu.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, required: true): Enter the date this form was submitted.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments.
