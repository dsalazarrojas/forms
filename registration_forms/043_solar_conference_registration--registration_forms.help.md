# Solar Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Solar Conference Registration form is designed to collect important information from attendees to provide them with a personalized experience during the conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the registration form by providing the requested information in each field.
2. Select the sessions you are interested in attending from the list provided.
3. Indicate if you have any special requirements that need to be met.
4. Review and submit your registration to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (1): This field is the main registration form where you will enter your name, email, phone number, date of birth, and time of day.
* **Attendee Details** (2): Please provide your details, including your name, email, and any other relevant information.
* **Session Interests** (3): Select all the sessions you are interested in attending from the list provided. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Special Requirements** (4): If you have any special requirements, such as accessibility needs or dietary restrictions, please indicate them here.
* **Email** (5): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications about the conference.
* **Phone Number** (6): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about anything related to the conference.
* **Date of Birth** (7): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Day** (8): Indicate your time of day.
* **Session Interests (Additional)** (9-16): Select whether you are interested in each of the additional sessions listed. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Session Interests (17-25)**: These fields are similar to the ones above, with each one representing a different session.

Note: Please fill out the form carefully, as incomplete or incorrect information may affect your conference experience. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
