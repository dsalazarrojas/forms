This form is a registration form for an insurance webinar. It is primarily used to collect attendee information, including name, email, phone number, insurance company, job title, location, date of birth, and time of event. The form is designed to be completed by individuals attending the webinar to provide their personal details. Before starting, please ensure you have the correct permissions to register and that you are not already registered for this event.

# Insurance Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect attendee information for an insurance webinar, allowing us to track attendees and provide them with relevant information and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
- Click on the form URL to access the form
- Enter your name in the **Name** field
- Enter your email address in the **Email** field
- Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field
- Enter your insurance company in the **Insurance Company** field
- Enter your job title in the **Job Title** field
- Enter your location in the **Location** field
- Enter your date of birth in the **Date of Birth** field in the YYYY-MM-DD format
- Enter the time of the event in the **Time of Event** field in the HH:MM AM/PM format

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (Text)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Insurance Company (Text)**: If you are affiliated with an insurance company, enter it here.
* **Job Title (Text)**: Enter your job title or profession.
* **Location (Text)**: Enter your location where you will be attending the webinar.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Time of Event (Time)**: Enter the time of the event in the HH:MM AM/PM format.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We will be sending updates and information about the webinar to the email address you provide.
