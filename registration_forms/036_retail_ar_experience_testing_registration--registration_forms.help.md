# Retail AR Experience Testing Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail AR Experience Testing Registration form is designed for participants to register for testing sessions related to augmented reality experiences in retail settings. This form gathers information about the participant's experience and willingness to participate, as well as their personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the required information and consent to participate in the testing session.
2.  Read each field carefully and fill in the relevant details.
3.  Review and agree to the participant agreement form.
4.  Submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Retail AR Experience Testing Registration** (`retail_ar_experience_testing_registration`, text, required: false)
This field is the form title and is not required to be filled in. Please leave it as is.

### 2. **Category** (`category`, select_one, required: false)
Select 'Yes' if you have experience related to the testing session, 'No' otherwise.

### 3. **Description** (`description`, text, required: false)
Provide a brief description of your experience and how it relates to the testing session.

### 4. **Scheduled AR Test Sessions** (`scheduled_ar_test_sessions`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all the sessions you're interested in participating in.

### 5. **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, select_multiple, required: false)
Select all the participant details that apply to you.

### 6. **Participant Information Form** (`participant_information_form`, text, required: false)
Fill in your participant information as required.

### 7. **Capture Consent** (`capture_consent`, select_one, required: false)
Select 'Yes' to agree to capture your information for further communication and 'No' otherwise.

### 8. **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false)
Provide your contact details as required.

### 9. **Capture Consent Form** (`capture_consent_form`, text, required: false)
Fill in the capture consent form as required.

### 10. **Participant Agreement Form** (`participant_agreement_form`, text, required: false)
Review and agree to the participant agreement form.

Tips:

*   Fill in all required information carefully and accurately.
*   Review your submission before submitting it.
*   If you're unsure about any field, consult the relevant instructions or contact the form owner.
