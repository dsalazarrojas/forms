# Sports Team Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Team Release Form is used to document a participant's release from a sports team due to injuries or medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
Complete the form by following these steps:
1. Provide your name and contact information in the **participant_info** field.
2. Enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number in the **emergency_contact** and **emergency_contact_phone** fields, respectively.
3. Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person in the **emergency_contact_relationship** field.
4. Disclose any medical information or conditions that may affect your participation in the **medical_info** field.
5. List any pre-existing medical conditions that may impact your participation in the **medical_conditions** field.
6. Check the box for **consent** if you have given your consent to release from the team.
7. Sign and date your **signature** field, which is required for release to be processed.
8. Add a note or comment if necessary in the **waiver** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **participant_info**: Enter your name and contact information, such as email and phone number.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
- **emergency_contact**: Enter the name of the person you'd like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
- **emergency_contact_relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, or spouse).
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
- **medical_info**: Provide any medical information or conditions that may impact your participation in the activity.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
- **medical_conditions**: List any pre-existing medical conditions that may affect your participation.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
- **consent**: Check this box to indicate you have given your consent for release from the team.
  * Type: Multiple selection (Yes/No)
  * Required: Optional
- **waiver**: Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
  * Type: Text
  * Required: Optional
- **signature**: Your signature is required for release to be processed. Please sign and date this field.
  * Type: Time stamp (date and time)
  * Required: Optional

## Tips
- Complete the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth release process.
- Ensure you're only using this form for the intended purpose of obtaining a participant's release.
- Review and verify all information before submitting the form.
