# Green Manufacturing Standard Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Manufacturing Standard Registration Form is used to register facilities that adhere to the Green Manufacturing Standard (GMS), a set of guidelines and standards for sustainable and environmentally-friendly practices. This form ensures that the organization's practices meet the necessary requirements for registration and certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your **name** on page 1. This field is a text input field where you can enter your facility's name.
2. On the same page, enter your **address** in the address field. This field is also a text input field where you can provide your facility's address.
3. On page 2, select your **certifications** status from the dropdown menu. If your facility has certifications related to the GMS, such as ISO 14001 or ISO 50001, choose 'Yes'. Otherwise, choose 'No'.
4. If you have certifications, enter the **date_certified** field with the date of issuance.
5. On the same page, enter numerical values for your **energy_consumption**, **water_consumption**, and **waste_disposal** fields, which represent your facility's energy and water usage, and waste disposal practices, respectively.
6. Finally, on the last page, select the **confirmation** field, confirming that you have accurate information and are willing to undergo an audit to verify your GMS compliance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Name (Page 1)
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your facility's name to identify yourself for the registration process.

### Address (Page 1)
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Provide your facility's address for identification and contact purposes.

### City (Page 1)
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter your facility's city for identification and contact purposes.

### Certifications (Page 2)
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, select multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if your facility has certifications related to the GMS, such as ISO 14001 or ISO 50001. Select 'No' otherwise.

### Date Certified (Page 2)
* **Date Certified** (`date_certified`, date, required: false): If you have certifications, enter the date of issuance.

### Energy Consumption (Page 2)
* **Energy Consumption** (`energy_consumption`, number, required: false): Enter your facility's energy consumption practices.

### Water Consumption (Page 2)
* **Water Consumption** (`water_consumption`, number, required: false): Enter your facility's water consumption practices.

### Waste Disposal (Page 2)
* **Waste Disposal** (`waste_disposal`, number, required: false): Enter your facility's waste disposal practices.

### Confirmation (Page 3)
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, select one, required: true): Confirm that you have accurate information and are willing to undergo an audit to verify your GMS compliance.

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities for support.
