# Food System Digitalization Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the registration and data collection of participants, NGOs, and hubs for the Food System Digitalization initiative. It helps us gather necessary information to ensure a successful and efficient digitalization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your program name in the `Program Name` field.
2.  Enter your NGO name in the `Ngo Name` field.
3.  Enter your hub name in the `Hub Name` field.
4.  Enter your participant name in the `Participant Name` field.
5.  Select your interests in the `Interests` field (select one or multiple options).
6.  Select your consent in the `Consent` field (select `Yes` or `No`).
7.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
8.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
9.  Enter your date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field.
10. Enter your address in the `Address` field (optional).
11. Enter any additional notes in the `Note` field (optional).
12. Enter a brief description of the program in the `Program Details` field (optional).
13. Enter a description of the NGO in the `Ngo Description` field (optional).
14. Enter a description of the hub in the `Hub Description` field (optional).
15. Enter a brief description of the participant in the `Participant Details` field (optional).
16. Select your participant interests in the `Participant Interests` field (select multiple options).
17. Select the program interests in the `Program Interests` field (select multiple options).
18. Select the NGO interests in the `Ngo Interests` field (select multiple options).
19. Select the hub interests in the `Hub Interests` field (select multiple options).
20. Select your participant consent in the `Participant Consent` field (select `Yes` or `No`).
21. Select the NGO consent in the `Ngo Consent` field (select `Yes` or `No`).
22. Select the hub consent in the `Hub Consent` field (select `Yes` or `No`).
23. Enter your participant phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
24. Enter your participant address in the `Participant Address` field.
25. Enter your participant email address in the `Participant Email` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Program Name** (`Program Name`, `text`, required): This field is for entering the name of your program.
*  **Ngo Name** (`Ngo Name`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the name of your NGO.
*  **Hub Name** (`Hub Name`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering the name of your hub.
*  **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your name as a participant.
*  **Interests** (`Interests`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select one or more interests from the list provided.
*  **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_one`, required): This field is for selecting your consent status (Yes or No).
*  **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering your email address.
*  **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your phone number.
*  **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required): This field is for entering your date of birth.
*  **Address** (`Address`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering your address (optional).
*  **Note** (`Note`, `note`, optional): This field is for entering any additional notes.
*  **Program Details** (`Program Details`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering a brief description of the program.
*  **Ngo Description** (`Ngo Description`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering a description of the NGO.
*  **Hub Description** (`Hub Description`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering a description of the hub.
*  **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, optional): This field is for entering a brief description of the participant.
*  **Participant Interests** (`Participant Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select multiple interests from the list provided.
*  **Program Interests** (`Program Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select multiple interests from the list provided.
*  **Ngo Interests** (`Ngo Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select multiple interests from the list provided.
*  **Hub Interests** (`Hub Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): This field allows you to select multiple interests from the list provided.
*  **Participant Consent** (`Participant Consent`, `select_one`, required): This field is for selecting your consent status (Yes or No).
*  **Ngo Consent** (`Ngo Consent`, `select_one`, required): This field is for selecting the NGO's consent status (Yes or No).

*  **Participant Phone** (`Participant Phone`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your phone number.
*  **Participant Address** (`Participant Address`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your address.
*  **Participant Email** (`Participant Email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering your email address.
