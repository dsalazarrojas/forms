# Merchant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help merchants register for an event or conference. It gathers essential information about the merchant, including contact details and other relevant specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*). 
2. Fill out the optional fields with any additional information you want to provide.
3. Review your form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`Registration Form`, text, optional): This is the title of the form, which is not required but can be used to describe the purpose of the form.
* **Sponsor Name** (`Sponsor Name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the sponsor's name, which can be used to associate the merchant with their sponsor.
* **Vendor Email** (`Vendor Email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the vendor's email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`Vendor Phone`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the vendor's phone number.
* **Early Bird** (`Early Bird`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any early bird information, such as a promo code or special offer.
* **Coupon Code** (`Coupon Code`, text, optional): This field is used to collect a coupon code, which can be used for discounts or promotions.
* **Coupon Discount** (`Coupon Discount`, number, optional): This field is used to specify a discount amount for the coupon.
* **Number of Sponsorship Levels** (`Number of Sponsorship Levels`, number, optional): This field is used to select the number of sponsorship levels the merchant is interested in.
* **Space Choice** (`Space Choice`, select_one, optional): This field allows the merchant to choose a space or category for their registration.
* **Meals Preparation** (`Meals Preparation`, select_one, optional): This field allows the merchant to select a meals preparation option.
* **Early Bird Ending Date** (`Early Bird Ending Date`, date, optional): This field is used to specify the early bird ending date.
* **Early Bird Ending Time** (`Early Bird Ending Time`, time, optional): This field is used to specify the early bird ending time.
* **Registration Confirmation Email** (`Registration Confirmation Email`, text, optional): This field is used to collect a confirmation email for registration.
* **Vendor Registration Email** (`Vendor Registration Email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the vendor's registration email address.
* **Vendor Registration Email Subject** (`Vendor Registration Email Subject`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the subject of the vendor's registration email.
* **Merchant Registration Email** (`Merchant Registration Email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect the merchant's registration email address.
* **Merchant Registration Email Subject** (`Merchant Registration Email Subject`, text, optional): This field is used to specify the subject of the merchant's registration email.
* **Number of Days to Arrange Vendor Space** (`Number of Days to Arrange Vendor Space`, number, optional): This field is used to specify the number of days needed to arrange vendor space.
* **Number of Days to Arrange Meals** (`Number of Days to Arrange Meals`, number, optional): This field is used to specify the number of days needed to arrange meals.
* **Sponsorship Levels** (`Sponsorship Levels`, select_multiple, optional): This field allows the merchant to select multiple sponsorship levels.
* **Vendor Name** (`Vendor Name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the vendor's name.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use a valid email address for the vendor registration email field.
* Use a valid email address for the merchant registration email field.
* Use a valid date and time for the early bird ending date and time fields.
* Review your form before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate and complete.
