# educational-program-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational program registration form is used to collect information from students who are interested in enrolling in an educational program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your name in the "Student Name" field. This is the name that will be displayed on your profile and in any correspondence with the educational program.
*   Enter your school name in the "School Name" field. This is the name of your current educational institution.
*   Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you with important updates and notifications.
*   Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This is an important identifier for age and program eligibility.
*   Select the type of program you are interested in enrolling in from the "Program Type" field. This will help us understand which program is right for you.
*   Select your current grade level from the "Student Grade" field. This will help us tailor the program to your needs.
*   Choose the start and end dates of your program from the "Program Start Date" and "Program End Date" fields. These dates will help us schedule your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **School Name**: Enter the name of your current educational institution.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. We will use this to contact you with important updates and notifications.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 01/01/1990).
*   **Program Type**: Choose one or more of the program types offered by this educational institution.
*   **Student Grade**: Choose your current grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th).
*   **Program Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start the program in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 08/01/2022).
*   **Program End Date**: Enter the date you plan to complete the program in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 08/31/2024).
*   **Program End Date**: This field should be after the Program Start Date.
*   **Program Type**: You can select one or more program types that you are interested in.
*   **Student Grade**: You should select a grade level that matches your current level of education.
