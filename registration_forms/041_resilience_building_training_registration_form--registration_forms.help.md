# Resilience Building Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resilience Building Training Registration Form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for a workshop focused on resilience building and training. This form helps us to track participant registration, manage expectations, and tailor the workshop experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the requested information under each field. Ensure you enter your details accurately.
2. Make sure to check your email address is formatted correctly to avoid validation errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Registration Form
* **Registration Form**: This field is not a form field but rather the title of the page. You can skip this field as it's just a heading.

### 2. Participant's First Name
* **participant_first_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name. This is the starting point for our workshop facilitators to address you during the training.

### 3. Participant's Last Name
* **participant_last_name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name. This will help us to personalize the workshop experience.

### 4. Participant's Email
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address. This is crucial for us to communicate with you before, during, and after the workshop. Please ensure it's in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).

### 5. Phone
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your contact phone number. This is useful for reaching out in case of any last-minute updates or cancellations.

### 6. Date of the Workshop
* **workshop_date** (Date, Optional): Choose the date of the workshop you plan to attend. This helps us plan the event schedule accurately.

### 7. Time of the Workshop
* **workshop_time** (Time, Optional): Choose the time slot for the workshop. We will use this to coordinate the schedule efficiently.

### 8. Location of the Workshop
* **workshop_location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location where the workshop will take place. This will help us plan logistics and ensure smooth arrangements for you.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of your details before submitting.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions or approvals (if required) to participate in this workshop.
- If you have any special requests or needs, feel free to contact us separately.
