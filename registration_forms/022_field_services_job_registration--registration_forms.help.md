# Field Services Job Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering field services jobs.

## How To Complete This Form

This form consists of multiple fields to collect relevant information from the field services team and clients. Please follow the steps below to complete this form:

1. **Title**: Enter a brief title for the job, if desired.
2. **Client Info**: Provide any additional information related to the client.
3. **Client Name**: Enter the name of the client or person related to the job.
4. **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the client or person related to the job.
5. **Service Info**: Provide any additional information related to the service.
6. **Service Type**: Select all applicable services for this job (e.g., Yes, No).
7. **Service Duration**: Enter the duration of the service, if applicable.
8. **Service Date**: Enter the date of the service, if applicable.
9. **Service Time**: Enter the time of the service, if applicable.
10. **Notes**: Add any additional notes related to the job.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter a brief title for the job.
* **Client Info** (`client_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional information related to the client.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the name of the client or person related to the job.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
	+ Enter the phone number of the client or person related to the job.
* **Service Info** (`service_info`, text, required: false):
	+ Provide any additional information related to the service.
* **Service Type** (`service_type`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all applicable services for this job.
* **Service Duration** (`service_duration`, number, required: false):
	+ Enter the duration of the service, if applicable.
* **Service Date** (`service_date`, date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date of the service, if applicable.
* **Service Time** (`service_time`, time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time of the service, if applicable.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false):
	+ Add any additional notes related to the job.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out, if applicable.
* If a service type is selected, ensure that the service duration is filled out if applicable.
* The service date and time fields should be filled out if applicable.
* Use the notes field to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the field services team to perform the job.
