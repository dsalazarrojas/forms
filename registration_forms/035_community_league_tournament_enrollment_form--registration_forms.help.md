# Community League Tournament Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community League Tournament Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from teams interested in enrolling in the Community League Tournament. This form is a mandatory step in the enrollment process, and it's essential to complete it accurately to avoid any issues or delays.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all the necessary information required for each field before starting the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, paying attention to the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) or "required: true".
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **Tournament Sport Category** (`sport_category`, select_one, required): Select the category of sport your team plays (e.g., Soccer, Basketball, Softball / Baseball, Volleyball, Pickleball).
* **Competitive Division** (`team_division`, select_one, required): Choose the competitive division your team belongs to (e.g., Youth, Young Adult, Adult Open, Senior, Professional / Elite).
* **-- Coaching and Management --** (`section_coach`, note, not required): This field is for additional information about your team's coaching and management structure. You can add any relevant details here.
* **Coach or Team Manager Full Name** (`coach_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your team's coach or manager.
* **Coach Contact Email** (`coach_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your team's coach or manager, which will be used as the primary point of contact.
* **Coach Contact Phone Number** (`coach_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your team's coach or manager.
* **Total Number of Registered Players** (`player_count`, number, required): Enter the total number of registered players on your team.
* **Full Team Roster Summary** (`team_roster_summary`, text, required): Provide a list of your team members' names and jersey numbers.
* **Primary and Secondary Jersey Colors** (`jersey_colors`, text, required): Enter the primary and secondary colors used by your team's jerseys.
* **Preferred Home Field or Court Location** (`home_field_preference`, text, not required): If applicable, enter the preferred location of your team's home field or court.
* **General Weekend Availability for Games** (`weekend_availability`, select_multiple, required): Select all the weekend days and time slots when your team is available for games.
* **Has this team participated in our tournaments before?** (`prior_participation`, select_one, required): Answer whether your team has participated in our tournaments before or not.
* **I acknowledge the registration fee is due before the first game** (`fee_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the registration fee is due before the first game.
* **Date of Enrollment** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are enrolling your team in the tournament.
