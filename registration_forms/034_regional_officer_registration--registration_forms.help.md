# Regional Officer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Officer Registration form is a crucial step in the registration process for individuals interested in becoming Regional Officers. It helps us verify your identity and assess your suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User Info details.
2. Provide your Contact Details.
3. Complete the Onboarding section.
4. Optionally, add Additional Info (if required).
5. Read and agree to the Terms & Conditions.
6. Submit your form for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`title`, `required: false`):
  Enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Details** (`title`, `required: true`):
  Enter your contact information (address, email, phone number) to enable us to reach you.
* **Onboarding** (`title`, `required: true`):
  Describe your onboarding process, including any relevant documents or certifications you have obtained.
* **Additional Info** (`title`, `required: false`):
  If required, add any additional information that supports your application.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`title`, `required: false`):
  Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the Regional Officer position.
* **Submit** (`title`, `required: false`):
  Review and submit your form for review.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`title`, `required: false`):
  This is an optional field to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
  Please note that this field is not required but is a crucial part of the registration process.
