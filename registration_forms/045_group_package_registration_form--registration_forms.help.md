# Group Package Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from users regarding group packages.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (<name>, <type>, **required: false**: <plain explanation>)
This field is meant to capture general information about the user, such as name, contact details, or any other identifying characteristics. You can fill out as much or as little information as you have available.
* **Package Info (<name>, <type>, **required: false**: <plain explanation>)
This field is for providing details about the package being registered, such as its name, description, or any other relevant details. You can provide as much or as little information as you have available.
* **Participant Info (<name>, <type>, **required: false**: <plain explanation>)
This field is for capturing information about the participants or individuals associated with the package, such as their names, contact details, or any other relevant details. You can fill out as much or as little information as you have available.
* **Group Info (<name>, <type>, **required: false**: <plain explanation>)
This field is for providing additional information about the group or organization related to the package, such as its name, description, or any other relevant details. You can fill out as much or as little information as you have available.
