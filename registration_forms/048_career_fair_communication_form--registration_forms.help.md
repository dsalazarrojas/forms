# Career Fair Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Career Fair Communication Form is used for exhibitors to send messages to event organizers, such as requesting booth assignments, providing additional information, or asking questions about the career fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the `Sender name` field.
2. Enter your company or institution's name in the `Organization` field (if applicable).
3. Provide your email address in the `Contact email` field, so the event organizers can reach out to you.
4. Enter a brief summary of the purpose of your message in the `Message subject` field.
5. Enter the full details and requests in the `Message body` field.
6. Select the `Preferred response time` from the available options, indicating when you'd like a reply from the event organizers.
7. If necessary, attach a link to your company's profile in the `Attach company profile link` field.
8. Decide whether you consent to share your information with other exhibitors in the `Consent to share information with exhibitors` field.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the `Additional notes` field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sender name**: Your name as the representative of the organization.
* **Organization**: Your company or institution's name (if applicable).
* **Contact email**: Your email address for event organizers to reply to you.
* **Message subject**: A brief summary of the purpose of your message.
* **Message body**: Full details and requests.
* **Preferred response time**: When you'd like the event organizers to reply to you.
* **Attach company profile link**: An optional link to your company's profile for event organizers to review.
* **Consent to share information with exhibitors**: Allow event organizers to share your message with other exhibitors.
* **Additional notes**: Any extra information you'd like to include.
