# Teacher's Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and verify information from teachers for a specific event or purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To get started, fill in your **Name**, which will be used to identify you in the registration process.
2.  Enter your **Email**, which will be used for communication and verification purposes.
3.  If you have a **Phone** number, please fill it in for emergency contact or follow-up purposes.
4.  Select **Location** where you will be attending the event. This might be the venue or location of the event.
5.  Enter the **Event Title** to help us understand the purpose or name of the event.
6.  Choose a **Date** for the event from the calendar or input the date manually.
7.  Select the **Time** for the event from the available options or input it manually.
8.  If you have an **Event Venue**, please describe it in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name (Name, text, required)**: A field to fill in your name for identification purposes.
*   **Email (Email, email, required)**: A field to input your contact email for communication and verification.
*   **Phone (Phone, text, optional)**: A field for emergency contact or follow-up purposes.
*   **Location (Select One, optional)**: A field to choose the location of the event.
*   **Event Title (Text, optional)**: A field to enter the name or description of the event.
*   **Event Date (Date, optional)**: A field to select or input the date of the event.
*   **Event Time (Time, optional)**: A field to select or input the time of the event.
*   **Event Venue (Text, optional)**: A field to describe the location of the event.
