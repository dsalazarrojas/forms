# Client Attendance Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Attendance Agreement form is a comprehensive agreement that ensures clients understand and agree to the terms and conditions of their attendance in a program or course. The form covers essential aspects of attendance, such as policies and procedures for cancellations, no-shows, and makeup sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your client's **Client Full Name** and **Program or Course Name** in the corresponding text fields.
2. Select the **Scheduled Session Frequency** that applies to your program or course.
3. Read and agree to the **Cancellation Policy** and **No-show Policy** by selecting the respective options.
4. Understand and agree to the **Makeup Session Policy** by selecting the option.
5. Choose the **Primary Method to Report an Absence** and enter your **Email Address** or **Phone Number** for reporting absences in the optional fields.
6. Sign the form electronically by typing your full name in the **Client Digital Signature** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Full Name** (`client_full_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Program or Course Name** (`program_name`): Enter the name of the program or course you are participating in.
* **Program Start Date** (`course_start_date`): Enter the start date of the program or course.
* **Scheduled Session Frequency** (`scheduled_sessions`): Select the frequency of your scheduled sessions.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy_ack`): Agree to provide 24 hours' notice for any cancellation.
* **No-show Policy** (`no_show_policy_ack`): Understand that 3 consecutive no-shows may lead to program discharge.
* **Makeup Session Policy** (`makeup_session_policy`): Understand the makeup session policy as outlined in the handbook.
* **Primary Method to Report an Absence** (`contact_for_absences`): Choose your preferred method to report an absence.
* **Email Address for Reporting** (`absence_reporting_email`): Enter your email address for reporting absences (optional).
* **Phone Number for Reporting** (`absence_reporting_phone`): Enter your phone number for reporting absences (optional).
* **Client Digital Signature** (`electronic_signature`): Type your full name for electronic signature.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`): If the client is under 18, type the parent or guardian's full name for signature (optional).
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`): Record the date you signed the agreement.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and understand all the terms and conditions before signing the agreement.
* Complete all required fields for accurate and complete submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your program or course administrators for assistance.
