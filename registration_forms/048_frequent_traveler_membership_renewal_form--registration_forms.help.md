# Frequent Traveler Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Frequent Traveler Membership Renewal Form is designed to help existing members renew their membership status. This form is used to update personal and membership details, and provide any additional comments or confirmations required for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, fill in your First Name in the first field.
2.  Move to the next field to enter your Last Name.
3.  Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4.  Provide your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field.
5.  Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
6.  Enter your Address in the Address field.
7.  If you have a membership number, enter it in the Membership Number field.
8.  Enter the Expiration Date of your membership in the Expiration Date field.
9.  If you have any comments or notes, type them in the Member Notes field.
10. If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in the Optional Member Notes field.
11. Confirm your signature by typing it in the Confirm Signature field.
12. Select the Option for the type of submission you prefer from the Submit Options field.
13. Select the Option for the type of cancellation or cancellation reason you prefer from the Cancel Options field.
14. You will receive an email with information about your membership renewal.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Email**: Enter your Email address. This is used for communication and notifications. (Type: email, Required: false)
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your Date of Birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy. (Type: date, Required: false)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your Phone Number. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Address**: Enter your address where you can be reached. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Membership Number**: If you have a membership number, enter it here. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the Expiration Date of your membership. (Type: date, Required: false)
* **Member Notes**: Enter any comments or notes about your membership. (Type: note, Required: false)
* **Optional Member Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership. (Type: note, Required: false)
* **Confirm Signature**: Confirm your signature by typing it in this field. (Type: text, Required: false)
* **Submit Options**: Select the Option for the type of submission you prefer. (Type: select_one, Required: false)
* **Cancel Options**: Select the Option for the type of cancellation or cancellation reason you prefer. (Type: select_one, Required: false)
* **Confirmation Email**: You will receive an email with information about your membership renewal. (Type: text, Required: false)

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
