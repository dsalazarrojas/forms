# Legal Guardian Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Guardian Release Form is a document that allows a legal guardian or parent to provide consent for a minor's participation in a specific activity or event. This form ensures that the minor's rights and interests are protected and that the legal guardian has given their consent for the minor's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **legal guardian's name** in the first field, as it is the parent or guardian providing consent.
2. Enter the **date** in the second field, which is the date on which the guardian is signing the form.
3. Add a personal **guardian signature** in the third field, which is a handwritten signature from the legal guardian.
4. Enter the **minor's name** in the fourth field, which is the name of the individual for whom the guardian is providing consent.
5. Enter the **event/activity** in the fifth field, which is the specific activity or event the minor will be participating in.
6. Enter the **guardian's address** in the sixth field, which is the address of the legal guardian.
7. Enter the **guardian's phone number** in the seventh field, which is the phone number of the legal guardian.
8. Enter the **guardian's email** in the eighth field, which is the email address of the legal guardian.
9. Enter the **emergency contact's name** in the ninth field, which is the name of someone the guardian wants to be contacted in case of an emergency.
10. Enter the **emergency contact's phone number** in the tenth field, which is the phone number of the emergency contact.
11. Enter the **emergency contact's relationship** with the minor in the eleventh field, which describes their relationship to the minor.
12. Enter the **emergency contact's email** in the twelfth field, which is the email address of the emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **legal_guardian_name** (Text, Required): The name of the legal guardian or parent providing consent.
* **date** (Date, Optional): The date on which the guardian is signing the form.
* **guardian_signature** (Note, Optional): A handwritten signature from the legal guardian.
* **minor_name** (Text, Optional): The name of the individual for whom the guardian is providing consent.
* **event_activity** (Text, Optional): The specific activity or event the minor will be participating in.
* **guardian_address** (Text, Optional): The address of the legal guardian.
* **guardian_phone** (Text, Optional): The phone number of the legal guardian.
* **guardian_email** (Email, Optional): The email address of the legal guardian.
* **emergency_contact_name** (Text, Optional): The name of someone the guardian wants to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **emergency_contact_phone** (Text, Optional): The phone number of the emergency contact.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (Text, Optional): A description of the emergency contact's relationship to the minor.
* **emergency_contact_email** (Email, Optional): The email address of the emergency contact.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and honestly to ensure that the guardian's consent is valid and legally binding.
- Make sure to sign the form with a handwritten signature from the legal guardian.
- If you are unsure about any of the information required, please contact the organization or institution administering the form.
