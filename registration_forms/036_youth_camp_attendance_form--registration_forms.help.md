# Youth Camp Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Camp Attendance Form is a document used to collect information about each participant attending the camp. This form is essential for keeping track of participant details, including name, date of arrival and departure, participant type, staff status, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with the relevant information as prompted.
2. Ensure that all required fields (if applicable) are completed before proceeding.
3. Review the form carefully for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for the participant's first name.
  - This field should include the participant's first name, such as John.
  - Ensure that the first name is spelled correctly and accurately represents the participant's full name.
- **Last Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for the participant's last name.
  - This field should include the participant's last name, such as Doe.
  - Ensure that the last name is spelled correctly and accurately represents the participant's full name.
- **Date of Arrival** (Date, Optional): This field is for the participant's date of arrival at the camp.
  - This field should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  - Ensure that the date is accurate and in the correct format.
- **Time of Arrival** (Time, Optional): This field is for the participant's time of arrival at the camp.
  - This field should be in the format HH:MM.
  - Ensure that the time is accurate and in the correct format.
- **Date of Departure** (Date, Optional): This field is for the participant's date of departure from the camp.
  - This field should be in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
  - Ensure that the date is accurate and in the correct format.
- **Participant Type** (Select One, Optional): This field asks whether the participant is a regular participant or a staff member.
  - Select 'Yes' if the participant is attending as a regular participant.
  - Select 'No' if the participant is not attending as a regular participant.
- **Staff Status** (Select One, Optional): This field asks about the staff status of the participant.
  - Select 'Active' if the participant is an active staff member.
  - Select 'Inactive' if the participant is not an active staff member.
- **Email** (Email, Optional): This field is for the participant's email address.
  - Ensure that the email address is in the correct format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
  - This field is useful for camp administrators to contact participants with important information.
- **Phone** (Text, Optional): This field is for the participant's phone number.
  - Ensure that the phone number is in the correct format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
  - This field is useful for camp administrators to contact participants with important information.
- **Parent/Guardian Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
  - Ensure that the name is spelled correctly and accurately represents the parent/guardian's full name.
  - This field is useful for camp administrators to communicate with parents or guardians.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Optional): This field is for the name of the participant's emergency contact.
  - Ensure that the name is spelled correctly and accurately represents the emergency contact's full name.
  - This field is useful for camp administrators to contact the emergency contact in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text, Optional): This field asks about the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact.
  - This field should describe the relationship between the participant and the emergency contact (e.g., mother, father, etc.).
  - This field helps camp administrators understand the participant's emergency contact situation.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): This field is for the emergency contact's phone number.
  - Ensure that the phone number is in the correct format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
  - This field is useful for camp administrators to contact the emergency contact in case of an emergency.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): This field is for the emergency contact's email address.
  - Ensure that the email address is in the correct format (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
  - This field is useful for camp administrators to contact the emergency contact with important information.
