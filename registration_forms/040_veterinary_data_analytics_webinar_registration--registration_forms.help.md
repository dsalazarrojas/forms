# veterinary_data_analytics_webinar_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from potential attendees of a webinar on veterinary data analytics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your contact information in the Contact Information field.
2.  Select your role from the Roles field (e.g., Veterinarian, Researcher, Student, or Other).
3.  If you have any additional goals or objectives for attending the webinar, fill in the Goals field.
4.  If your role is not listed in the Roles field, please provide more information in the Other Role field.
5.  Enter your email address in the Email field.
6.  Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7.  Finally, provide your consent to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (text, required/optional): This is the main entry page for the webinar registration.
* **Contact Information** (text, required/optional): This field is for collecting your contact details.
* **Consent** (text, required/optional): This field is for collecting your consent to complete the registration process.
* **Roles** (select_multiple, required): Select one or more of the following roles: Veterinarian, Researcher, Student, or Other.
* **Goals** (text, required/optional): This field is for collecting any additional goals or objectives you have for attending the webinar.
* **Other Role** (text, required/optional): This field is for providing more specific information about your role if it's not one of the four listed options.
* **Email** (email, required/optional): This is a field for collecting your email address.
* **Phone** (text, required/optional): This field is for collecting your phone number.
