# Human Library Participation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Human Library Participation Agreement form is used to collect information from individuals participating in Human Library events. This information helps in organizing the events efficiently and ensuring that participants are aware of their roles, shifts, and event details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Please provide your details, including your name, email, and contact number.
2. Select your role at the event (Volunteer, Participant, or Organizer).
3. Choose your shift (morning, afternoon, or full-day).
4. Confirm your participation in the event.
5. Provide any additional notes or details about your participation.
6. If applicable, provide volunteer details and event-specific information such as event name, date, start and end times, and venue.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: This field is used to collect any additional information about the event. Please provide any relevant details about the event, such as its name, date, and start and end times.
* **Name**: This field is where you should enter your name.
* **Email**: This field is where you should enter your email address.
* **Role**: Select your role at the event from the options provided. This will help us understand your responsibilities and expectations.
* **Shift**: Choose the shift you will be working or participating in. If you are unsure, please select "morning", "afternoon", or "full-day" based on the event schedule.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you will participate in the event. If you are unable to participate, please select "I do not confirm that I will participate in the event."
* **Notes**: This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional information about your participation or role.
* **Volunteer Details**: If you are a volunteer, this field is used to provide any additional details about your volunteer role.
* **Contact Number**: Please enter your contact number so we can reach out to you with any important updates or information.
* **Event Name**: If you are a volunteer or organizer, please enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Session Time**: Enter the time of the session you will be participating in.
* **Event Place**: Enter the venue or location of the event.

Tips:

* Please ensure that you provide accurate information to help us organize the event efficiently.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to ask.
* We appreciate your participation and contribution to the Human Library initiative.
