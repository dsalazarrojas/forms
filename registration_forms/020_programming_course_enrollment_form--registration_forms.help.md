# programming_course_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **programming_course_enrollment_form** is used for students to register and enroll in a programming course. This form helps course administrators gather necessary information to process the registration and ensure a smooth learning experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Program Name**, a required field that identifies the program you are enrolling in.
2. Enter your **Student Name**, a required field that identifies you as the student enrolling.
3. Enter your **Email**, a required field that will be used for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Student Email**, an optional field that can be used for further communication with the course administrators.
5. Enter your **Student Phone**, an optional field that can be used to contact you in case of emergencies or important course updates.
6. Enter any **Student Note**, an optional field where you can add any relevant information about your enrollment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the programming course you are enrolling in. This is a required field and will help course administrators identify which program you are registering for.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the student enrolling. This is a required field and will help course administrators associate your registration with your identity.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication purposes. This is a required field and will be used by course administrators to contact you about the course.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter an additional email address that can be used for further communication with course administrators. This is an optional field and can be used for additional updates or reminders about the course.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for course administrators to contact you in case of emergencies or important course updates. This is an optional field and can be used for additional contact purposes.
* **Student Note** (`student_note`, note, optional): Enter any relevant information about your enrollment, such as any special requests or requirements. This is an optional field and can be used to provide additional context to course administrators.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have all required information before proceeding with the form.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness to avoid any issues during the enrollment process.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, please contact course administrators for help.
* This form is for programming course enrollment purposes only.
