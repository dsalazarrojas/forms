# Version 2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic demographic information from users, including their First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field (MM/DD/YYYY format, e.g., 01/01/1999).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name, as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name, as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format, e.g., 01/01/1999.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY) to ensure accurate processing.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask the form's administrator or contact form@gic.mx for assistance.
