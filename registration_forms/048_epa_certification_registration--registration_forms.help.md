# EPA Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EPA Certification Registration form is designed to collect information from users who have completed a training program and are seeking EPA certification. This form helps to verify their credentials and track their certification status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter the provider's name and school name in the corresponding text fields.
3. Select the correct dates for the training and certification in the date fields.
4. Enter the provider's contact information, including email, phone number, and website.
5. Select whether the provider is a "Yes" or "No" for the training location and provider status.
6. Enter any additional notes or information about the certification.
7. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Provider** (`provider_name`): Enter the name of the training provider.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school associated with the training.
* **Certification Program** (`training_program`): Enter the name of the certification program completed.
* **Training Dates** (`training_dates`): Enter the start and end dates of the training in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Proof of Completion** (`proof_completion`): This field is not required, but you can enter a proof of completion document or certification number if available.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter the provider's name, email, and phone number.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the provider's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone_number`): Enter the provider's phone number.
* **Training Provider Company** (`training_provider`): Select "Yes" if the provider is a company.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`): Select "Yes" if the training was held at a specific location.
* **Training Provider Email** (`training_provider_email`): Enter the email address of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Phone** (`training_provider_phone`): Enter the phone number of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Website** (`training_provider_website`): Enter the website of the training provider.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`): Enter the date the certification was issued.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`): Enter the date the certification expires.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`): Enter the number of training hours completed.
* **Training Cost** (`training_cost`): Enter the cost of the training.
* **Training Cost Currency** (`training_cost_currency`): Select the currency of the training cost.
* **Certification Status** (`certification_status`): Select the current status of the certification, which can be "Certified", "Pending", or "Cancelled".
* **Status Change Reason** (`status_change_reason`): Enter a reason for any changes to the certification status.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional information about the certification.
* **Address of Training Provider** (`training_provider_address`): Enter the address of the training provider.
* **City of Training Provider** (`training_provider_city`): Enter the city of the training provider.
* **State of Training Provider** (`training_provider_state`): Select "Yes" if the provider has a specific state.
* **Country of Training Provider** (`training_provider_country`): Select "Yes" if the provider is located in a specific country.

# Tips

* Please ensure that you have filled in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the administrator for clarification.
