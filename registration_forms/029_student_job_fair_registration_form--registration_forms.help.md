# Student Job Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form designed for students to provide their information, contact details, and job fair preferences. It includes fields for student information, contact details, job fair details, and submission of preferred job titles. The form is meant to be completed by students who wish to attend the job fair and interact with potential employers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the **Student Information** page with your name, university or school, and grade level.
*   Move on to the **Contact Details** page, where you will be asked to provide your email address and any other relevant contact information.
*   The **Job Fair Details** page allows you to specify the job fair you are interested in attending and any other relevant details.
*   On the **Student Name** page, enter your name as you wish it to be displayed during the job fair.
*   In the **Student E-mail** field, enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
*   Select your preferred **Job Titles** from the list provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Information**: This section is meant to gather general information about the student. Enter your name, university or school, and grade level.
*   **Contact Details**: Here, you will be asked for your email address and any other relevant contact information.
*   **Job Fair Details**: This section is for specifying the job fair you wish to attend and any other relevant details. If you are unsure of the job fair date and time, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Student Name**: Enter your name as you wish it to be displayed during the job fair. This name will be shown on your badge or materials.
*   **Student E-mail**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for follow-up communication.
*   **Job Title**: Select one or more of the job titles you are interested in from the list provided.
*   **Date**: This field is for the job fair date. If you are unsure of the date, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Time**: The job fair time is also an optional field. If you are unsure of the time, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Submit**: Click the **Submit** button to complete and submit your registration form.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid any errors in processing your registration.
*   If you are unsure of any field, feel free to reach out to the event organizers for assistance.
*   You can select one or more job titles from the list provided.
*   This form is designed to be easy to use and understand. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
