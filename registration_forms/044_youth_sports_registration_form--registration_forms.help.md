# Youth Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register children for youth sports programs, capturing their personal and medical information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter the child's first and last names.
3. Provide a valid email address for communication.
4. Enter the child's phone number (optional).
5. Enter the child's address, city, and zip code.
6. Select the state where the child resides.
7. Enter the child's date of birth.
8. Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact (optional).
9. If necessary, list any medical conditions, allergies, or injury history.
10. Select any relevant medical conditions from the list (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* First name of the child: Enter the first name of the child, e.g., John or Jane.

### Last Name
* Last name of the child: Enter the last name of the child, e.g., Doe.

### Email
* Email address: Enter a valid email address for communication.

### Phone Number
* Child's phone number: Enter the child's phone number (optional), e.g., 123-456-7890.

### Parent Name
* Parent's name: Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian, e.g., John Smith.

### Address
* Child's address: Enter the child's home address, including street number and name.

### City
* City: Enter the city where the child resides, e.g., Los Angeles.

### State
* State: Select the state where the child lives, e.g., California.

### Zip
* Zip code: Enter the child's zip code for address verification.

### Date of Birth
* Date of birth: Enter the child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Emergency Contact Name
* Emergency contact name: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of emergency, e.g., John Smith.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* Emergency contact phone: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact, e.g., 123-456-7890.

### Medical Conditions
* Medical conditions: List any medical conditions the child has, e.g., allergy to peanuts.

### Sports Interests
* Sports interests: List any sports or activities the child is interested in, e.g., soccer or swimming.

### Medical Form
* Medical conditions: Select any medical conditions that apply to the child, e.g., Allergies, Disability, or Injury History.

### Allergies
* Allergies: List any allergies the child has, e.g., peanut allergy.

### Disabilities
* Disabilities: List any disabilities the child has, e.g., hearing impairment.

### Injury History
* Injury history: List any injury history the child has, e.g., broken arm.

### Other Medical Conditions
* Other medical conditions: List any other medical conditions not listed above, e.g., ADHD.

Note: Please ensure all required fields are completed before submission. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
