# Zumba In The Park Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zumba In The Park Registration Form is used to gather participant information for Zumba dance classes held in outdoor parks. This guide will walk you through the form to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First Name, Email, Phone, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Select your Class Level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
3. Choose your preferred payment method (cash or check).
4. Select the frequency of your preferred class schedule (once a week, twice a week, or three times a week).
5. Provide any comments or feedback you'd like to share.
6. If applicable, enter your organization name and role.
7. Enter the name and email of the person responsible for your registration (if different from yourself).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is on your official identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address you regularly use.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you plan to attend the class.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you plan to attend the class.
* **Class Level** (`class_level`, select_one, required: true): Select your dancing level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose how you would like to pay for the classes: Cash or Check.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, required: true): Select the type of class you are interested in: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Class Frequency** (`class_frequency`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose how often you'd like to attend the classes: Once a week, Twice a week, or Three times a week.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): Add any comments or feedback you'd like to share with us.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required: false): If you are registering for your organization, enter the name here.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required: false): Describe your role in the organization (if registering for your organization).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required: false): If someone else is paying for your registration, enter their name here.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the person responsible for your registration (if different from yourself).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure we can contact you and register you for the classes correctly.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us through the provided contact information.
* We appreciate your participation in our Zumba classes, and we look forward to seeing you on the dance floor!
