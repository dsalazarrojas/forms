# Unlimited Internet Plan Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers who want to register for the unlimited internet plan. It collects relevant details such as contact information, plan choice, and other necessary fields to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Complete the fields with accurate and complete details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Billing Plan**: Choose the billing plan that suits your needs. Select from monthly, yearly, or one-time payment options.
* **Plan Choice**: Select the internet speed that you require (speed 20mb, speed 50mb, or speed 100mb).
* **Customer Details**: Enter your contact information (name, address, etc.) if you wish to be contacted.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account or contact information.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Installation Address**: Enter the address where the internet will be installed.
* **Installation Date**: Enter the date when you wish to have the internet installed.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date when you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time when you submitted the form.
* **Customer Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes from the customer.
* **Plan Status**: Select the status of your plan (active, inactive, or pending).
Note: Please ensure that all fields are accurate and complete to avoid delays in the registration process.
