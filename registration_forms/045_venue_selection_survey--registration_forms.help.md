# Venue Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about user's preference regarding venue selection for an event. Please fill out this form to help us get a better understanding of your interest in each venue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Answer each question honestly to the best of your ability.
2. For each question, select the answer that best suits your preferences.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields (indicated by the * symbol).
4. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Venue Selection (venue_selection, select_multiple, optional)
* Select all the venues you are interested in. You can choose as many or as few venues as you like.

### Date (date, date, optional)
* Enter the date of the event you are interested in, in the format MM/DD/YY or MM/DD/YY.

### Time (time, time, optional)
* Enter the time of the event you are interested in, in the format 12:00 AM - 11:59 PM.

### Venue Name (venue_name, text, optional)
* Enter the name of the venue you are interested in.

### Address (address, text, optional)
* Enter the address of the venue you are interested in.

### Rating (rating, select_one, optional)
* Rate your experience with the venue on a scale of 1-5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest.

### Review (review, text, optional)
* Enter any comments or feedback you have about the venue.

# Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields correctly.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to contact us for clarification.
* This form is part of a larger set of registration forms, so please be aware that your answers will be used to gather data for event planning purposes.
