# Innovation Membership Club - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovation Membership Club is a platform for individuals to join, learn, and grow with like-minded innovators. This form is used to register and become a member of the club. Members will be able to access exclusive resources, network with others, and participate in discussions and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk*) with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your name and any other relevant information.
4. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration-form`, text, optional): This field is a text input for any additional information or comments you'd like to share about your membership application.
* **User Info** (`user-info`, text, optional): Please provide any relevant details about yourself that you feel will help us get to know you better.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required*): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about your membership and exclusive resources.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy to avoid any issues with notifications and updates.
* Be as detailed as possible in the "User Info" field to help us understand your goals and interests.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, feel free to contact us at contact@gic.mx.
