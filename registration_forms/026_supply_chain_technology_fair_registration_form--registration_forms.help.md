# Supply Chain Technology Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from participants in the Supply Chain Technology Fair. This includes details about your company, role, job description, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the main page, enter your company name and provide a brief description of your company.
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select your job title and job function.
4. Enter the name of your department and the location where you work.
5. Choose your industry from the provided options.
6. Describe your job description.
7. Write a brief description about yourself.
8. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, review your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company** (Company): Enter the name of your company.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your company email address.
* **Phone** (Phone): Enter your company phone number.
* **Name** (Name): Enter your name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title): Select your job title from the provided options.
* **Role** (Role): Choose your role in the event (Attendee or Exhibitor).
* **Job Function** (Job Function): Select whether you have a job function related to supply chain technology.
* **Department** (Department): Enter the name of your department.
* **Location** (Location): Enter the location where you work.
* **Industry** (Industry): Choose your industry from the provided options.
* **Job Description** (Job Description): Describe your job description.
* **About You** (About You): Write a brief description about yourself.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* Once you have submitted the form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions.
* If you need to modify your submission, please contact the event organizers.
* The form will be kept confidential and used solely for event organization purposes.
