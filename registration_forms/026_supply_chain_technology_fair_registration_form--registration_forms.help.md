## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from participants in the Supply Chain Technology Fair. This includes details about your company, role, job description, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On the main page, enter your company name and provide a brief description of your company.
2. Enter your name, email address, and phone number.
3. Select your job title and job function.
4. Enter the name of your department and the location where you work.
5. Choose your industry from the provided options.
6. Describe your job description.
7. Write a brief description about yourself.
8. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, review your submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
