# School Supply Giveaway Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register students for a school supply giveaway program. The program aims to provide essential school supplies to eligible students, and this form collects necessary information to verify eligibility and ensure accurate distribution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your student's information, including their name and birth date.
2. Answer questions about their grade level and school attendance.
3. Verify your own information as a parent, including your contact details and your own school attendance area.
4. Review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name (`student_name`)
*   **Student Name**: Enter your student's full name, as it appears on their records or identification.

### Parent Name (`parent_name`)
*   **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the student's parent or guardian.

### Student Grade Level (`student_grade`)
*   **Student Grade**: Answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the student's grade level (1-5 for elementary, 6-8 for middle school, 9-12 for high school).

### Grade Level (`student_grade_1`)
*   **Grade Level**: Select the grade level the student is in (1-5 for elementary, 6-8 for middle school, 9-12 for high school).

### School Attendance (`school_attendance`)
*   **School Attendance**: Enter the student's current school attendance status (number of days attended in a school year).

### Attendance Status (`school_attendance_1`)
*   **Attendance Status**: Select 'True' or 'False' to indicate the student's attendance status.

### Student Birthdate (`student_birthdate`)
*   **Student Birthdate**: Enter the student's birthdate in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Parent Email (`parent_email`)
*   **Parent Email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates about the program.

### Student Phone (`student_phone`)
*   **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number (if applicable).

### Parent Phone (`parent_phone`)
*   **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication and updates about the program.

### Student Note (`student_note`)
*   **Student Note**: Add any additional information about the student that you think would be helpful for the program.

### Parent Note (`parent_note`)
*   **Parent Note**: Add any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for the program.

### School ID (`school_id`)
*   **School ID**: Enter the school ID number for the student (if applicable).

### School Attendance Area (`school_attendance_area`)
*   **School Attendance Area**: Enter the area the student attends school in (e.g., city, school district).

### Attendance Area (`school_attendance_area_1`)
*   **Attendance Area**: Select the area the student attends school in (City or School District).

### Teacher Attendance Area (`teacher_attendance_area`)
*   **Teacher Attendance Area**: Enter the area the teacher attends school in (e.g., city, school district).

### Area (`teacher_attendance_area_1`)
*   **Area**: Select the area the teacher attends school in (City or School District).

### School Start Date (`school_start_date`)
*   **School Start Date**: Enter the start date of the school year in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Teacher Start Date (`teacher_start_date`)
*   **Teacher Start Date**: Enter the start date of the school year in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Teacher Start Date (`teacher_start_date_1`)
*   **Teacher Start Date**: Enter the start date of the school year in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### School Attendance Area (`school_attendance_area_2`)
*   **School Attendance Area**: Enter the area the student attends school in (e.g., city, school district).

### Attendance Area (`school_attendance_area_2_1`)
*   **Attendance Area**: Select the area the student attends school in (City or School District).
