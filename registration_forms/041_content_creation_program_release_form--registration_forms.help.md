## Content Creation Program Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by program participants to provide their personal and program details for the purpose of program release.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide participant details in the "Participant Details" section.
   - **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
   - **Participant Identification Number**: Enter your participant identification number, if applicable.
2. Provide program details in the "Program Details and Consent" section.
   - **Content Creation Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
   - **Program Start Date**: Enter the start date of the program.
   - **Program End Date**: Enter the end date of the program.
   - **Consent for Content Usage**: Confirm that you give consent for the program content to be used.
3. Confirm the accuracy of your information and agree to the program's privacy policy in the "Confirmation and Release" section.
   - **I confirm that the information provided is accurate**: Choose "I Agree" to confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
   - **I have read and agree to the privacy policy**: Choose "True" to indicate that you have read and agree to the program's privacy policy.
   - **Date of Program Release Agreement**: Enter the date of the release agreement.
   - **Release Form Completed?**: Choose "True" to indicate that you have completed the release form.
