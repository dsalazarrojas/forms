# event-innovation-showcase-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather user information and session interests for an innovation event. It is crucial to fill out this form to be considered for the event showcase.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your user information in the User Information page.
2. Select your session interests on the Session Interests page.
3. Provide your event details on the Event Details page.
4. Fill out any optional fields, such as the Note page if you have additional comments.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Information)** (`User Information`, `text`, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information you'd like to share with the event organizers.
* **Select Session Interests (Session Interests)** (`Session Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the sessions that interest you the most from the provided options. You can select multiple sessions.
* **Presenter Info (Presenter Info)** (`Presenter Info`, `text`, required): Enter details about the presenter, such as their name and title.
* **Note (Note)** (`Note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or thoughts about the event.
* **Email (Email)** (`Email`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Event Details (Event Details)** (`Event Details`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your event or innovation.
* **Select Session Interests Alt (Session Interests Alt)** (`Session Interests Alt`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose alternative session interests from the provided options.
