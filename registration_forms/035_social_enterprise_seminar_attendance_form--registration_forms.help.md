# Social Enterprise Seminar Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Enterprise Seminar Attendance Form is used to collect attendee information for the Social Enterprise Seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the required fields.
2. Fill in the Date of Attendance in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
3. If the event has a specific Time of Attendance, fill it in the 24hr time format.
4. Provide any additional Notes about your attendance.
5. For "Select one", choose one option from the dropdown menu.
6. If applicable, select multiple options from "Select multiple" dropdown menu.
7. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name, as this will help us identify you as an attendee.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name, as this will help us identify you as an attendee.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address, as this will help us contact you with event updates.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for further contact, you can do so here.
- **Date of Attendance** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the seminar you attended in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Time of Attendance** (`time`, `time`, optional): If the seminar had a specific time, enter it in the 24hr time format.
- **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional comments or thoughts about the seminar, provide them here.
- **Select one** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Choose one option from the dropdown menu that best represents your experience.
- **Select multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have experience with multiple options, select all that apply in the dropdown menu.

Tips:
- Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer back to this help guide for clarification.
