# Leadership Retreat Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Leadership Retreat Enrollment Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in the leadership retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact information and preferences.
2. If you have any comments or special requests, please use the "Note" field.
3. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name. This field is not required, but it helps us get to know you better.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name. This field is not required, but it's helpful for us to know your family name.
* **Role**: Select one of the following options: leader, speaker, or staff. This helps us understand your position in the organization.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This field is not required, but it's useful for us to contact you.
* **Date**: Choose the date you would like to attend the retreat. Please select a date from the available options.
* **Time**: Choose the time you would like to attend the retreat. Please select a time slot.
* **Note**: If you have any comments, requests, or special instructions, please use this field.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure we have it correctly. This field is not required, but it's a good double-check.
