# Adventure Tour Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential subscribers to the Adventure Tour service. Fill out this form to subscribe to our tour services and receive updates on upcoming tours and events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your subscriber full name and email address.
2. Select your preferred tour types or destinations.
3. Choose your subscription frequency.
4. Provide payment information.
5. Agree to the subscription terms.
6. Set a start date for your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Subscriber full name** (`subscriber_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address for subscription confirmations and updates.
* **Tour preferences** (`tour_preferences`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the types of tours you're interested in, such as Hiking, Rafting, Cultural tours, or Wildlife watching.
* **Subscription type** (`subscription_type`, `select_one`, **required**): Choose how often you'd like to receive updates, either Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually.
* **Payment information** (`payment_info`, `text`, **required**): Enter your payment card or billing details for subscription-related transactions.
* **Agree to subscription terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, **required**): Confirm that you've read and agree to the subscription terms.
* **Subscription start date** (`start_date`, `date`, **required**): Set the date you'd like your subscription to begin.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate payment information to avoid any issues with subscription payments.
* Review and agree to the subscription terms carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
