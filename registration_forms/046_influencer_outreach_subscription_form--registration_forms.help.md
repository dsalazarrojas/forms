# Influencer Outreach Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are interested in being contacted for outreach purposes by our team. Please fill out this form once to ensure that we do not contact you multiple times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional)
Enter your first name.

### **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional)
Enter your email address to allow us to contact you directly.

### **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by phone as well.

### **Notes** (`note`, `note`, optional)
Add any additional comments or notes that you would like to share with us.

### **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required)
Select whether you are interested in being contacted for the following:
* Yes
* No

### **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional)
Enter your location to help us tailor our outreach efforts to your area.

### **Industry** (`industry`, `select_multiple`, optional)
Select the industries that you are interested in being contacted for:
* Yes
* No

### **Brand** (`brand`, `text`, optional)
Enter the brand(s) that you are interested in being contacted for.

### **Agency** (`agency`, `select_multiple`, required)
Select the agencies that you are interested in being contacted for:
* Yes
* No

### **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional)
Click this button to submit your information for review.

### **Back** (`back`, `text`, optional)
Click this button to go back to the previous page.

### **Confirm** (`confirm`, `text`, optional)
Click this button to confirm that your submission is correct.

### Tips
* Fill out the form only once to avoid duplication of efforts.
* Make sure to select the correct options in the "Category" and "Agency" fields.
* Enter accurate information in the "Email" and "Phone" fields to ensure that we can contact you efficiently.
