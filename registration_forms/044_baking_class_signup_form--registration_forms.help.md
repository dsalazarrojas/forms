# Baking Class Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to sign up for a baking class.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are providing the correct information.
3. If you have any difficulties, do not hesitate to ask for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name
• **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
### 2. Email Address
• **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for class updates.
### 3. Phone Number
• **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication and payment purposes.
### 4. Date of Birth
• **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (MM/DD/YYYY).
### 5. Class Name
• **Class Name**: Select your preferred class from the provided options.
### 6. Class Date
• **Class Date**: Select the date of the class you are registering for.
### 7. Class Time
• **Class Time**: Select the time of the class you are registering for.
### 8. Session Number (if series)
• **Session Number**: If your class is part of a series, enter the session number.
### 9. Baking Experience Level
• **Baking Experience Level**: Select the level of your baking experience: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
### 10. Have You Taken Previous Classes?
• **Have You Taken Previous Classes?**: Check Yes if you have taken a baking class with us before.
### 11. Class Fee
• **Class Fee**: Enter the total fee for the class in dollars.
### 12. Payment Method
• **Payment Method**: Select how you will be paying for the class: Credit Card, Debit Card, Cash, Check, or PayPal.
### 13. Payment Status
• **Payment Status**: Select the payment status: Paid in Full, Partial Payment, or Payment Due.
### 14. Dietary Restrictions or Allergies
• **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
### 15. Health Conditions to Note
• **Health Conditions to Note**: List any health conditions that may affect your participation in the class.
### 16. What Do You Hope to Learn?
• **What Do You Hope to Learn?**: Enter what you hope to learn or achieve from the class.
### 17. Special Requests or Accommodations
• **Special Requests or Accommodations**: Enter any special requests or accommodations you may need for the class.
### 18. I Agree to the Class Waiver and Rules
• **I Agree to the Class Waiver and Rules**: Check to agree to the class waiver and rules.
### 19. Confirm Registration
• **Confirm Registration**: Check to confirm your registration for the class.

Please note that some fields are marked as "required" to ensure accurate and complete data collection.
