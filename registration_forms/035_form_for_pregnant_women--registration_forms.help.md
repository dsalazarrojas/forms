# Form For Pregnant Women - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a pregnant woman and her baby.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration form (page 1)
2. Enter personal details (page 2)
3. Select the baby's sex (page 3)
4. Enter the baby's due date and birth weight (pages 4 and 5)
5. Enter the baby's height and weight (pages 6 and 7)
6. Enter the mother's height and weight (pages 8 and 9)
7. Enter contact information for the mother and baby (pages 10-12)
8. Select the blood type (page 13)
9. Enter any medical conditions (page 14)
10. Enter health insurance information (page 15)
