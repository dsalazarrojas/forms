# symposium_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering individuals to attend a symposium, an academic gathering of experts to discuss and share knowledge on various topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the symposium you wish to attend in the `event_name` field.
2. Provide the start and end dates of the symposium in the `start_date` and `end_date` fields, respectively.
3. Enter the venue of the symposium in the `venue` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **event_name** (`event_name`, text, **optional**)
Enter the name of the symposium you wish to attend. For example, "The Annual Symposium on Advanced Research and Technology."

### 2. **start_date** (`start_date`, date, **optional**)
Enter the start date of the symposium in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY). For example, 06/15/2023.

### 3. **end_date** (`end_date`, date, **optional**)
Enter the end date of the symposium in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY). For example, 06/16/2023.

### 4. **venue** (`venue`, text, **optional**)
Enter the location where the symposium will take place. For example, "Grand Conference Hall, University of Science and Technology."

## Tips
- Double-check the event details to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact the event organizers or check the symposium website.
- Complete the form carefully to avoid delays or issues with event registration.
