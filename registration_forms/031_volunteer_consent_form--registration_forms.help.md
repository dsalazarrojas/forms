# Volunteer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Consent Form is designed to collect information from volunteers who wish to participate in various activities or programs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please enter your general information (page 1, "Volunteer Info").
* Provide your contact information (page 1, "Contact Info").
* Select your role(s) of interest (page 2, "Select Role").
* Choose your availability dates for the selected role(s) (pages 3-7).
* Enter your time preferences (pages 8-11).
* Select your availability for the next role(s) (pages 12-15).
