# 029_ai_powered_marketing_training_registration_form--registration_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Powered Marketing Training registration form is a tool used to collect participant details and preferences for the training sessions. This form is designed to gather essential information for the organizers to provide a tailored experience for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Work Email Address** in the "Work Email Address" field. This is where you will receive training materials and links.
3. Select your **Company / Agency Name** from the drop-down list.
4. Choose your **Primary Marketing Function** from the drop-down list.
5. Enter your **Years of Experience in Marketing**.
6. For the "Specific AI Topics of Interest" field, select up to 3 topics you want to prioritize.
7. Select the **Preferred Attendance Format** (in-person, virtual, or self-paced) and the **Preferred Training Date** (May 12-13, 2026, June 8-9, 2026, or July 15-16, 2026).
8. If you will be attending an in-person workshop, select "Yes, I will bring my own device" for the "Will you bring your own laptop for the workshop?" field.
9. Choose your **Preferred Payment Method** (Credit / Debit Card, Corporate Invoice, or Bank Transfer).
10. If attending in-person, enter any **Dietary Requirements**.
11. Select whether you want to **Subscribe to our "AI in Marketing" monthly newsletter** (Yes or No).
12. Enter the **Registration Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full name for identification and communication purposes.
* **Work Email Address** (Work Email Address, email, required): Enter your work email address to receive training materials and links.
* **Company / Agency Name** (Company / Agency Name, text, required): Select your company or agency name from the drop-down list.
* **Primary Marketing Function** (Primary Marketing Function, select_one, required): Choose your primary marketing function from the drop-down list.
* **Years of Experience in Marketing** (Years of Experience in Marketing, integer, required): Enter your years of experience in marketing.
* **Specific AI Topics of Interest** (Specific AI Topics of Interest, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 topics you are interested in for the training sessions.
* **Will you bring your own laptop for the workshop?** (Will you bring your own laptop for the workshop?, select_one, required for in-person sessions): If you will be attending an in-person workshop, select "Yes, I will bring my own device" to ensure you have a laptop for the session.
* **Preferred Attendance Format** (Preferred Attendance Format, select_one, required): Choose your preferred attendance format (in-person, virtual, or self-paced).
* **Preferred Training Date** (Preferred Training Date, select_one, required): Select your preferred training date.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (Preferred Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit / Debit Card, Corporate Invoice, or Bank Transfer).
* **Dietary Requirements** (Dietary Requirements, text, required for in-person sessions): If attending in-person, enter any dietary requirements.
* **Subscribe to our "AI in Marketing" monthly newsletter?** (Subscribe to our "AI in Marketing" monthly newsletter?, select_one, required): Select whether you want to subscribe to the newsletter.
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required): Enter the date of your registration.
