# Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Event Registration Form is designed to collect participant and event information to manage registration efficiently. It is essential to complete this form to ensure accurate and up-to-date records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Participant Details (Page 1)**: Enter your participant details, which are not required. This section is for your information only.
2. **Event Details (Page 2)**: Enter the event details. This section is required.
3. **Contact Details (Page 3)**: Enter your contact details. This section is required.
4. **Registration (Page 4)**: Enter your registration information. This section is required.
5. **Payment (Page 5)**: Enter your payment details. This section is required.
6. **Participant Status (Page 6)**: This section is to track participant status. Please refer to the event organizers for further instructions.
7. **Additional Comments (Page 7)**: Enter any additional comments, if any. This section is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, email, and any other identifying information you want to share.
* **Event Details**: Enter the event name, date, time, location, and any other relevant event information.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Registration**: Enter your participant registration status (e.g., registered, pending, declined).
* **Payment**: Enter your payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Participant Status**: This field will be filled automatically by the event organizers. Please refer to them for updates.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or instructions for the event organizers.

Note: Please make sure to complete all required fields to ensure accurate records. If you have any issues or questions, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
