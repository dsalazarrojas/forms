# Nursery School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your child with our nursery school. Please fill out this form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your child's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Provide your own contact information, including your phone number and email.
3. Enter your child's guardian's contact information, if applicable.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your child's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your child's address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your contact phone number.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the guardian's phone number, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, optional): Please enter the guardian's email address, if applicable.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask our staff for assistance.
* We will contact you using the contact information provided, so make sure to enter correct phone numbers and email addresses.
