# yoga_class_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and understand your preferences for a yoga class. Please fill out the form to share your first name, last name, email, phone number, preferred class day, available days, available time blocks, class interest, and any comments you might have.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal information.
2. Choose your preferred class day from the options provided.
3. Enter the number of days you can attend classes per week.
4. Enter the number of time blocks you can attend each class per week.
5. Select your class interest(s) from the available options.
6. If you have any additional comments, feel free to share them in the "Comments" field.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your inquiry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Preferred Class Day** (`preferred_class_day`, select_one, required): Choose the day you prefer to attend class.
- **Available Days** (`available_days`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of days you can attend classes per week.
- **Available Time** (`available_time`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of time blocks you can attend each class per week.
- **Available Time Block 1** (`available_time_block_1`, select_one, required): Choose the time block for your first available time slot.
- **Available Time Block 2** (`available_time_block_2`, select_one, required): Choose the time block for your second available time slot.
- **Available Time Block 3** (`available_time_block_3`, select_one, required): Choose the time block for your third available time slot.
- **Class Interest** (`class_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of yoga class you are interested in.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional comments you might have.
- **First Name Label** (`first_name_label`, text, required/optional): Not applicable. This field is not visible to the user.
- **Last Name Label** (`last_name_label`, text, required/optional): Not applicable. This field is not visible to the user.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to send your inquiry.
