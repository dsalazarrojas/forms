# Home Health Aide Self Paced Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is designed to help you complete the Home Health Aide Self-Paced Training Registration form. This form is for users interested in online training for Home Health Aides.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the modules you are interested in from the "Select Module Interest" field.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the date you plan to start the training from the "Module Start Date" field.
7. Choose whether or not you have a training provider from the "Training Provider" field.
8. Enter the length of the training program you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Module Interest (module_interest)**: Select all modules that interest you. This could include a variety of topics relevant to Home Health Aides.
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates or follow-up communications.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further information or updates.
* **Module Start Date (module_start_date)**: Select the date you plan to start the training.
* **Training Provider (training_provider)**: If you have a training provider, select "Yes". If not, select "No".
* **Program Length (program_length)**: Enter the length of the training program you are interested in.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the form owner or a representative.
* This form is intended to be submitted only once, to avoid duplication of records.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out.
