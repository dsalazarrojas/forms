# Adventure Obstacle Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Obstacle Race Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to join an adventure obstacle race. This form helps us get to know you better and provide an enjoyable and safe experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name, which should be your full legal name.
2. If you are registering as part of a team, please enter your team's name. Leave this field blank if you are registering as an individual.
3. Select the correct race category that suits your experience level.
4. Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an emergency.
5. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here for the event staff's reference.
6. Read and agree to the waiver agreement to participate in the event.
7. Choose your preferred t-shirt size for the event tee.
8. Select your preferred payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or cash).
9. Finally, fill in the date and time of your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Team name if applicable**: This field is for team members who are registering together. If you are an individual, leave this field blank.
* **Race category**: Choose the distance or difficulty level that suits your experience and skill level.
	+ 5K beginner: Suitable for new participants or those looking for a casual experience.
	+ 10K intermediate: Suitable for those with some experience in obstacle racing.
	+ Obstacle challenge advanced: Suitable for experienced participants seeking a challenging course.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: Please provide the name and phone number of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical conditions or allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies that the event staff should know about for your safety.
* **Waiver agreement**: Please read and agree to the waiver agreement to participate in the event.
* **T-shirt size**: Choose your preferred t-shirt size for the event tee.
	+ S, M, L, XL, XXL: Choose your preferred size from the options above.
* **Payment method**: Choose your preferred payment method for the event registration.
	+ Credit card: Pay using your credit card.
	+ Bank transfer: Pay using a bank transfer.
	+ Cash: Pay using cash at the event site.
* **Registration date**: Please fill in the date and time of your submission.
