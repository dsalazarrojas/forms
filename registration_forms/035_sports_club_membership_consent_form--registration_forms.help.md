# Sports Club Membership Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Membership Consent Form is a document used to collect essential information from prospective members. It helps the club to understand the individual's background, emergency contacts, and their willingness to participate in activities. This form is a mandatory requirement for all new members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Registration Details on page 1.
2. Provide your Member Information on page 2, including emergency contact details if applicable.
3. Indicate your consent to participate in activities on page 3.
4. Select your Membership Type on page 4.
5. Fill in your membership history on page 5.
6. Enter your Contact Number and Email Address on page 6.
7. Add any Additional Info if required.
8. Sign and date the form as a final step.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (`Registration Details`, text, required/optional): On page 1, fill in your basic contact information such as name, address, and any other relevant details that may aid the club in contacting you.
* **Member Information** (`Member Information`, text, required/optional): On page 2, provide information about yourself as a member of the club, including any emergency contact person's name and contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, text, required/optional): On page 3, provide the contact details of a person that the club can contact in the event of an emergency.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, select_one, required/optional): On page 4, select whether you wish to participate in activities by indicating your consent. This is a crucial step in the registration process.
* **Membership Type** (`Membership Type`, select_multiple, required/optional): On page 5, choose the type of membership you wish to have in the club (Individual, Team, or Both).
* **Member Since** (`Member Since`, date, required/optional): On page 5, fill in the date when you joined the sports club.
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required/optional): On page 6, fill in your phone number for the club to contact you.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required/optional): On page 6, provide your email address for the club to send you important communication.
* **Additional Info** (`Additional Info`, text, required/optional): On page 7, if you need to provide any further information, use this field to do so.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, note, required/optional): On the last page, sign and date the form to confirm your submission.
