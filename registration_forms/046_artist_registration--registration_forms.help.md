# Artist Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from artists or bands for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your artist or band name in the "Artist or Band Name" field.
2. Select your type of artist/band (solo, duo, band, etc.) from the "Type of Artist" dropdown menu.
3. Enter your primary contact's name in the "Primary Contact Name" field.
4. Provide your contact email address in the "Contact Email Address" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
6. Select your primary genre of music in the "Primary Genre" field.
7. Enter any secondary genres you perform in the "Secondary Genres" field.
8. Specify the number of band members in the "Number of Band Members" field.
9. List each band member's name and instrument in the "List of Band Members and Instruments" field.
10. Choose your typical set duration from the "Typical Set Duration" dropdown menu.
11. If applicable, specify any equipment or technical requirements in the "Equipment or Technical Requirements" field.
12. Select the days you are available for performances from the "Days Available for Performances" dropdown menu.
13. Choose your preferred performance times from the "Times Available for Performances" dropdown menu.
14. Specify how much advance notice you require for booking in the "Advance Notice Needed for Booking" field.
15. Enter your band's website or official page URL in the "Website or Official Page" field.
16. List your streaming platform links in the "Streaming Platform Links" field.
17. Provide your social media handles in the "Social Media Handles" field.
18. Confirm the accuracy of the registration information in the "I Confirm This Registration Information is Accurate" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Artist or Band Name**: Enter the name under which you will be performing (e.g., John Smith, The Rock Band, etc.).
- **Type of Artist**: Select the type of artist you are (e.g., solo, duo, band, etc.).
  (Note: Selecting "Band" implies that you have 3+ members. If you're unsure, choose "Band (3-5 members)" for up to 5, and "Large Ensemble (5+ members)" for more than 5.)
- **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for communication and coordination.
- **Contact Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact.
- **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact.
- **Primary Genre**: Select the primary genre of music you perform (e.g., rock, pop, jazz, etc.).
- **Secondary Genres**: List any other genres you perform (e.g., blues, country, etc.).
- **Number of Band Members**: Enter the number of musicians in your band.
- **List of Band Members and Instruments**: List each band member's name and instrument (e.g., John (Guitar), Jane (Vocals), Bob (Drums), etc.).
- **Typical Set Duration**: Select the standard length of your performances.
- **Equipment or Technical Requirements**: If necessary, specify any equipment or technical requirements for your performance.
- **Days Available for Performances**: Select the days you are available for performances.
- **Times Available for Performances**: Choose your preferred performance times.
- **Advance Notice Needed for Booking**: Specify how much advance notice you require for booking.
- **Website or Official Page**: Enter your band's website or official page URL.
- **Streaming Platform Links**: List your streaming platform links (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music, etc.).
- **Social Media Handles**: List your social media handles (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, etc.).
- **I Confirm This Registration Information is Accurate**: Confirm that the registration information you provided is accurate.

Please note that each field is required unless marked as optional. Make sure to fill out each field accurately to avoid any confusion or delays in the booking process.
