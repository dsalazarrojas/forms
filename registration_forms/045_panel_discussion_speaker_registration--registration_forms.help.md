# panel_discussion_speaker_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The panel discussion speaker registration form is designed for speakers to provide their information and confirm their attendance. This form helps us to prepare for the event, ensuring a smooth and successful discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the speaker details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select your topics of discussion, either 'Yes' or 'No', depending on your involvement.
3. In the bio and photo section, provide a brief introduction and a link to your profile photo.
4. Enter your contact information, including any relevant contact details.
5. If you have any additional event details, you can provide them in this section.
6. You can optionally submit any relevant documents or files for review.
7. Finally, confirm your attendance by clicking on the "Speaker Confirmation" section and let us know you are coming.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **speaker_details** (text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant speaker information.
* **speaker_topics** (select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' if you are presenting a topic, and 'No' otherwise.
* **bio and photo** (note, required): Provide a brief introduction and a link to your profile photo.
* **speaker contact info** (text, required): Enter your contact information, including any relevant contact details.
* **event details** (text, optional): If you have any additional event details, you can provide them here.
* **submit and upload** (text, optional): You can optionally submit any relevant documents or files for review.
* **speaker confirmation** (note, required): Confirm your attendance by clicking on this section and let us know you are coming.
* **speaker_experience** (text, optional): This field is not currently utilized in the form, but may be used in the future.
