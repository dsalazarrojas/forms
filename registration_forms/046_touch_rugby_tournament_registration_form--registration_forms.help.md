# Touch Rugby Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Touch Rugby Tournament Registration Form is for teams to provide their details to participate in the "Touch Rugby Tournament". This form is used to collect essential information about the team to ensure a smooth and successful tournament experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your team's registration form with all required fields.
2. Select your team's payment type (Team Fee, Registration Fee, or Other).
3. Provide contact details for your team, including name, email, phone number, and address.
4. Add any additional notes or details about your team.
5. Enter the tournament name, date, and time (if applicable).
6. Select your team's type, level, and players.
7. Choose your team's coach and provide any additional details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (Registration Form): This field is for you to provide your team's registration details. Please fill in all required fields.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details): Enter your team's payment details, including the payment type (Team Fee, Registration Fee, or Other).
* **Team Details** (Team Details): Provide information about your team, including team name, type, level, and description.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes): Add any additional details about your team that you think will be helpful for the tournament organizers.
* **Registration Fee** (Registration Fee): Enter the registration fee for your team.
* **Team Members** (Team Members): Select the team members who will be participating in the tournament.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details): Provide contact information for your team, including name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Payment Type** (Payment Type): Select the payment type for your team (Team Fee, Registration Fee, or Other).
* **Contact Name** (Contact Name): Enter the name of the contact person for your team.
* **Contact E-mail** (Contact E-mail): Enter the email address of the contact person for your team.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone): Enter the phone number of the contact person for your team.
* **Contact Address** (Contact Address): Enter the address of the contact person for your team.
* **Additional Details** (Additional Details): Add any additional details about your team that you think will be helpful for the tournament organizers.
* **Tournament Name** (Tournament Name): Enter the name of the tournament.
* **Tournament Date** (Tournament Date): Enter the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Time** (Tournament Time): Enter the time of the tournament.
* **Team Name** (Team Name): Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Type** (Team Type): Select the type of your team (Team A, Team B, or Team C).
* **Team Description** (Team Description): Enter a brief description of your team.
* **Team League** (Team League): Enter the league your team belongs to.
* **Team Level** (Team Level): Select the level of your team (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Team Players** (Team Players): Select the players participating in the tournament.
* **Team Coach** (Team Coach): Enter the name of the coach for your team.
* **Team Address** (Team Address): Enter the address of your team.

Please ensure to fill in all required fields to avoid any delays or issues with your team's registration. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the tournament organizers.
