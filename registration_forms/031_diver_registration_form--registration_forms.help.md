# Diver Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Diver Registration Form is a form designed to collect information about divers, including personal details, certification, and emergency contact information. This information will help organizations, clubs, or instructors to better manage and serve the needs of their diver community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: "Diver Name", "Email", "Phone" (Emergency Contact), and at least one of "Emergency Contact Phone" or "Emergency Contact Email".
2. Provide accurate and complete information for all the other fields, including "Certification Date", "Expiration", "Last Dive Date", "Dive Site", "Medical Info", and "Diver Info". These fields are not strictly required, but they help us better understand your diving experience and needs.
3. Upload your diver photo (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Diver Name (text)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached in emergency situations.
* **Emergency Contact (text)**: Enter the name of the person or organization that should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (text)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person or organization.
* **Emergency Contact Email (email)**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person or organization.
* **Medical Info (text)**: Describe any medical conditions or allergies that the diver has.
* **Diver Info (text)**: Provide any additional information about your diving experience, qualifications, or certifications.
* **Dive Info (text)**: Describe your diving experience, including locations and types of dives.
* **Dive Site (text)**: Specify the name or location of the last or next dive site you will be visiting.
* **Certification Date (date)**: Enter the date of your certification as a diver (e.g., 2022-01-01).
* **Expiration (date)**: Enter the date of your diver certification expiration (e.g., 2025-01-01).
* **Last Dive Date (date)**: Enter the date of your last dive (e.g., 2022-02-01).
* **Certification Number (text)**: Enter your diver certification number, if applicable.
* **Diver Photo (text)**: Upload a recent photo of yourself.

## Tips
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid errors or confusion.
* If you have any doubts about a particular field, please contact the organization or contact person responsible for maintaining this form.
* This form is designed to facilitate communication and record-keeping, not to be an exhaustive or definitive registry.
