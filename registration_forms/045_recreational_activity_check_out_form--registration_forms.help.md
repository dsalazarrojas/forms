# recreational_activity_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect data about participants who are checking out from recreational activities. It aims to gather details about the participant, the activity, and other relevant information for the staff to keep records updated and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the participant details, including their name and ID.
2. Provide the activity details, such as the activity number and name.
3. Enter the equipment details, if applicable.
4. Fill in the check-out information, including the check-out time and status.
5. Add any necessary participant and staff signatures.
6. Complete the form by filling in the timestamp and any other required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): Enter the participant's name and ID for reference and tracking purposes.
* **Activity Details** (`activity_details`): Provide the activity number and name for accurate record-keeping.
* **Equipment Details** (`equipment_details`): Enter the equipment used by the participant, if any.
* **Check Out** (`check_out`): Enter the time and status of the participant's check-out.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Have the participant sign to confirm their check-out.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`): Have the staff member sign to confirm the participant's check-out.
* **Check Out Time** (`check_out_time`): Enter the time of the participant's check-out.
* **Equipment Count** (`equipment_count`): If applicable, enter the number of equipment items checked out.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`): Enter the date of the participant's signature.
* **Staff Signature Date** (`staff_signature_date`): Enter the date of the staff member's signature.
* **Staff Name** (`staff_name`): Enter the name of the staff member who checked out the participant.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter the name of the participant, if not already provided.
* **Activity Number** (`activity_number`): Select the activity number from the options provided.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Select any additional notes that need to be recorded about the participant's check-out, such as any issues or concerns.
* **Staff ID** (`staff_id`): Enter the ID of the staff member who checked out the participant.
* **Timestamp** (`timestamp`): Enter the date and time of the participant's check-out.
* **Check Out Status** (`check_out_status`): Select the status of the participant's check-out, such as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
