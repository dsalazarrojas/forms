# Cfa Community Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Cfa Community Registration Form is a user-facing form designed to register individuals for classes or programs offered by the community arts school. This form collects essential demographic information and program preferences from participants to ensure accurate and efficient processing of registrations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by reading the form introduction and providing the required information.
2. Fill in your full name in the "full name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field, which will be used for class updates.
4. If you have a contact number, provide it in the "phone" field.
5. Select your age group from the provided options (Child, Teen, Adult, Senior).
6. Specify the class or program you are interested in participating in the "class interest" field.
7. If applicable, describe any previous relevant experience in the "previous experience" field.
8. If necessary, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
9. Agree to the program policies by selecting "I agree" from the "agree to terms" field.
10. If you have any accessibility or support requirements, provide them in the "special needs" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **full_name** (`full_name`, text, required): Your full name, as it appears on official documents.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address used for class updates and communication.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your contact number for further communication.
* **age_group** (`age_group`, select_one, required): Select your age group from the provided options (Child, Teen, Adult, Senior).
* **class_interest** (`class_interest`, text, required): Specify the class or program you are interested in participating in.
* **previous_experience** (`previous_experience`, text, optional): Describe any prior relevant experience that might be beneficial for your selected class or program.
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact, if necessary.
* **agree_terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to consent to program policies.
* **special_needs** (`special_needs`, text, optional): Specify any accessibility or support requirements you may have.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correct formatting and spelling.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Completing this form accurately and thoroughly helps us provide better services and ensure your successful participation in our programs.
