# Business Continuity Planning Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Business Continuity Planning Session Registration form efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required information.
2. Provide accurate and clear answers to all fields.
3. Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department or area of operation.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter your company or agency name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date that works for you to attend the session.
* **Preferred Session Time** (`session_time`, select_one, required): Choose a time slot for the session:
	+ Morning (8am-12pm)
	+ Afternoon (12pm-4pm)
	+ Evening (4pm-8pm)
	+ Flexible
* **Expected Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_one, required): Choose the duration of the session:
	+ 2 hours
	+ Half day (4 hours)
	+ Full day (8 hours)
	+ Multiple days
	+ Flexible
* **Primary Focus Area** (`primary_focus`, select_one, required): Identify your main area of concern for the session:
	+ Developing BC Plan
	+ Updating Existing Plan
	+ Risk Assessment
	+ Recovery Strategies
	+ Testing and Exercises
	+ Training and Awareness
	+ Compliance Review
* **Specific Topics to Cover** (`focus_topics`, select_multiple, required): Select areas of interest for the session:
	+ Business Impact Analysis
	+ Risk Assessment
	+ Recovery Strategies
	+ Data Protection
	+ Backup and Restoration
	+ Crisis Communication
	+ Team Coordination
	+ Testing Methods
* **Session Objectives** (`objectives`, text, required): State what you want to accomplish during the session.
* **Current BC Maturity Level** (`bc_maturity`, select_one, required): Rate your current state of BC planning:
	+ Beginner
	+ Emerging
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Mature
* **Existing Business Continuity Plan** (`existing_plan`, select_one, required): Indicate the status of your current plan:
	+ Yes, in place
	+ Yes, but outdated
	+ In development
	+ No plan exists
* **Expected Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required): Estimate the number of people attending the session.
* **Types of Roles Attending** (`attendee_roles`, select_multiple, required): Identify the roles that will attend the session:
	+ Executive/Senior Management
	+ Risk Management
	+ IT/Technology
	+ Operations
	+ Finance
	+ HR
	+ Communications
	+ Department Managers
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility or other needs, please specify.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information.

Note: This guide is based on the provided YAML fields. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please feel free to ask.
