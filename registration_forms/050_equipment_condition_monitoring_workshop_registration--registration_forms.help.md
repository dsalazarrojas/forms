# Equipment Condition Monitoring Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from participants who want to attend the Equipment Condition Monitoring Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If you have any special requirements, please specify them in the "Special Requirements" field.
5. Select your preferred workshop dates in the "Preferred Workshop Dates" field if you cannot attend on the default date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Name, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the workshop materials and communication.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for workshop updates and information.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for workshop updates and information.
* **Preferred Workshop Dates** (Preferred Workshop Dates, select_multiple, optional): Select the dates you would prefer to attend the workshop. Please note that if you don't select any dates, you will be assumed to be available on the default date. 
* **Special Requirements** (Special Requirements, text, optional): If you have any specific needs or requirements, please specify them here. This could include any special equipment, accommodations, or dietary needs.
