# Organizational Agility Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Agility Workshop Registration form is a tool used to gather information from participants about their interests, preferences, and contact details in order to better understand who is attending the workshop and what they are expecting from the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Describe your interests in the "Interests" field. This will help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
6. Describe your preferences in the "Preferences" field. This will help us understand what you are expecting from the event.
7. Select a date for the workshop that you are interested in attending in the "Workshop Date" field. Please note that this field is not currently required but will be in the future.
8. Select a time for the workshop that you are interested in attending in the "Workshop Time" field. This field is also not currently required but will be in the future.
9. Select the tool that you will be using during the workshop in the "Tool" field. This field is currently not required but will be in the future.
10. Upload any relevant files in the "Output File" field. This is optional.
11. Select a category that best describes your interests in the "Category" field. This will help us group you with others who have similar interests.
12. Select a form ID that is relevant to your workshop in the "Form ID" field. This field is currently not required but will be in the future.
13. Finally, select a tool that you will be using during the workshop in the "Tool" field. This field is currently not required but will be in the future.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name here. Please use the full name as per your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you in case of emergency.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text, optional): Describe your interests and what you are expecting from the workshop. This will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, text, optional): Describe your preferences and what you are expecting from the event.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, not required): Select a date for the workshop that you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, not required): Select a time for the workshop that you are interested in attending.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): If you have been assigned a tool by us, enter it here.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Upload any relevant files that you would like to share.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select a category that best describes your interests.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, select_one, not required): Select a form ID that is relevant to your workshop.
* **Tool** (`tool`, select_one, optional): Select a tool that you will be using during the workshop.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or description about the workshop.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, optional): Enter any relevant form IDs.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, text, optional): Add any additional category that best describes your interests.
* **Description 2** (`description_2`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or description about the workshop.
