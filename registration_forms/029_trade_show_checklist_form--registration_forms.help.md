# trade_show_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The trade show checklist form is designed to gather comprehensive information about trade shows, enabling organizers to evaluate their events and make improvements.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully and provide accurate information regarding your experience with the trade show.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields (marked as true in the form's schema).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booth Design** (`booth_design`, text, required): Briefly describe the booth's design and overall appearance.
* **Booth Construction** (`booth_construction`, select_one, required): Choose the type of booth construction used for the trade show (1, 2, or 3).
* **Booth Installation** (`booth_installation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of booth installation methods used (if any).
* **Marketing Materials** (`marketing_materials`, number, optional): Provide the number of marketing materials distributed or available for the trade show.
* **Staff Management** (`staff_management`, select_one, required): Choose the level of staff management used for the trade show (1 or 2).
* **Communication** (`communication`, text, optional): Share any communication strategies or methods used during the trade show.
* **Customer Experience** (`customer_experience`, date, required): Enter the date of the customer experience (if applicable).
* **Review** (`review`, time, optional): Record the time of your review (if any).
* **Show Schedule** (`show_schedule`, select_multiple, optional): Select the show schedule types used (if any).
* **Evaluation** (`evaluation`, number, optional): Provide the evaluation score (if any).
* **Show Requirements** (`show_requirements`, select_one, required): Choose the level of show requirements fulfilled (1, 2, or 3).
* **Booth Details** (`booth_details`, text, required): Describe the booth's details, including its location, size, and features.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_multiple, optional): Select the customer service types provided (if any).
* **Sales Support** (`sales_support`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of sales support offered (if any).
* **Staff Scheduling** (`staff_scheduling`, select_one, optional): Select the staff scheduling method used (if any).
* **Review and Evaluation** (`review_and_evaluation`, text, required): Share your review and evaluation of the trade show.
* **Overall Experience** (`overall_experience`, select_one, required): Choose the overall experience rating (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, select_multiple, optional): Add any additional comments or suggestions.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Outline the next steps or actions to be taken.
* **Review and Recommendation** (`review_and_recommendation`, text, optional): Share your review and recommendations for future trade shows.
* **Overall Rating** (`overall_rating`, select_one, required): Choose the overall rating (1, 2, 3, 4, or 5).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information regarding the trade show.
* **Show Location** (`show_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select the show location types (if any).
* **Final Comments** (`final_comments`, text, optional): Add any final comments or feedback.

Please ensure you complete all required fields and provide accurate information to help trade show organizers evaluate and improve their events.
