# Auction Participation Rules Acknowledgment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to ensure that participants of an auction understand and agree to the rules of participation. By filling out this form, you will be confirming that you have read and understood all the rules and terms related to the auction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name** in the first field.
2. If you have been assigned a **Bidder Number**, please enter it in the second field.
3. Select a date for your **Date of Participation** in the third field.
4. In the fourth field, select **I Acknowledge** to confirm that you understand that all bids are final and legally binding.
5. In the fifth field, select **I Acknowledge** to agree to pay for all items won by the end of the event.
6. In the sixth field, select **I Acknowledge** to confirm that you are responsible for the removal and transport of items won.
7. In the seventh field, select **I Acknowledge** to confirm that you understand all items are sold as-is - where-is.
8. In the eighth field, you will find a link to read the **Full Terms and Conditions**. Please read this before signing the agreement.
9. In the ninth field, select **True** to confirm that you have read and agree to all the auction rules.
10. Finally, type your **Signature (Full Name)** to digitally sign the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Bidder Number**: If you have been assigned a bidder number during registration, enter it here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Date of Participation**: Select the date you will participate in the auction.
* **I understand that all bids are final and legally binding**: Please confirm that you understand and accept that all bids you make will be final and legally binding.
* **I agree to pay for all items won by the end of the event**: Confirm that you agree to pay for all items you win by the end of the event.
* **I am responsible for removal and transport of items won**: Confirm that you will be responsible for removing and transporting any items you win during the auction.
* **I understand all items are sold as-is - where-is**: Confirm that you understand all items are sold in their current condition, without any warranties or guarantees.
* **Full Terms and Conditions link**: Please read the terms and conditions before signing this agreement.
* **I have read and agree to all the auction rules**: Select true to confirm that you have read and agree to all the auction rules.
* **Type your full name to sign this agreement**: Type your full name to serve as a digital signature for this agreement.
