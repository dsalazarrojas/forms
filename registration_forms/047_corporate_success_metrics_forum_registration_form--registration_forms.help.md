# Corporate Success Metrics Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Corporate Success Metrics Forum. Please fill it out to ensure that your registration is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out all required fields, indicated by a asterisk (\*).
- Ensure that you have not already registered for the event by checking your email for a confirmation message.
- If you have any doubts or queries, please get in touch with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Corporate Success Metrics Forum Registration Form** (*title*): This is the title of the form. Please do not fill this out; it's just a label.
* **Attendee Name** (*attendee_name*, text, *): This is the name of the person registering for the event.
* **Company** (*company*, text, *): This is the company that you represent or are associated with.
* **Job Title** (*job_title*, text, *): This is your job title within the company.
* **Email** (*email*, email, *): Please enter your valid email address so that we can keep you updated about the event.
* **Event Name** (*event_name*, select_one, *): Choose the name of the event you wish to register for from the provided options (Event A, Event B, or Event C).
* **Event Date** (*event_date*, date, *): Please select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (*event_time*, time, *): Please select the time of the event.
* **Event Preferences** (*event_preferences*, select_multiple, *): Choose any preferences you may have for the event.
* **Attendee Details** (*attendee_details*, note, *): Use this field to provide any additional details about yourself and your attendance.
* **Submission Date** (*submission_date*, date, *): Please note: This field is currently not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Submission Time** (*submission_time*, time, *): Please note: This field is currently not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Submission Status** (*submission_status*, select_one, *): Please note: This field is currently not used in the form and can be ignored.
* **Attendee Phone** (*attendee_phone*, text, *): This is your phone number for any further communication regarding the event.

**Tips**
- Please ensure that you have provided all the necessary information to submit the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
- Remember to check your email for a confirmation message after submitting the form.
