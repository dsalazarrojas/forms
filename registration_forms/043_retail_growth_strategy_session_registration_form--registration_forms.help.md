# Retail Growth Strategy Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for customers to register for a retail growth strategy session with one of our experts. The session is optional, and customers can choose the time slot that suits them best. By filling out this form, customers will be able to confirm their session details and provide any additional information or questions they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Read through the form carefully and ensure you are registering for the correct session time.
*   Select the time slot that you would like to register for by clicking on the corresponding option.
*   Enter any additional questions or comments you may have in the "Additional Questions" field.
*   Check the box to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Session Information (session_info):** A text field where you can enter any session details or preferences.
*   **Contact Information (contact_info):** A text field where you can enter your contact information.
*   **Schedule A Session (schedule_a_session):** A dropdown menu where you can select the time slot for the session. Please choose one of the available time slots from the menu.
*   **Confirm Session Time (confirm_session):** A number field where you can enter the time slot you selected in the "Schedule A Session" field. Please enter the same number as the time slot you selected.
*   **Additional Questions (additional_questions):** A text field where you can enter any additional questions or comments you may have.
*   **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions):** A dropdown menu where you can confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions. Please select "Yes" or "No" accordingly.
