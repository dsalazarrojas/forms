# Art Exhibition License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for artists, galleries, or organizations to apply for a license to host an art exhibition. It helps the applicant to provide necessary information for the exhibition and ensures compliance with local regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the applicant type (Individual artist, Gallery, Museum, Non-profit organization, Commercial entity, or Other).
2. Enter your full name or organization name.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Specify the number of artists participating in the exhibition.
6. Fill in the exhibition name, description, and type (Fine art, Commercial art, Photography, Sculpture, Digital art, Installation, Mixed media, or Other).
7. Provide the venue name or location and address.
8. Enter the venue owner or manager contact information.
9. Specify the proposed start and end dates of the exhibition.
10. Indicate the daily operating hours of the exhibition.
11. Confirm that you will carry liability insurance (True, False, or In process).
12. Fill in the insurance coverage amount (only if you have liability insurance).
13. Provide a detailed safety and emergency plan for the exhibition.
14. Agree to comply with all local regulations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Type** (`applicant_type`, select_one, required): Select the correct type of applicant (Individual artist, Gallery, Museum, Non-profit organization, Commercial entity, or Other).
* **Full Name or Organization Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name or organization name.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this application.
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, text, required): Enter a brief title for the exhibition.
* **Exhibition Description** (`exhibition_description`, text, required): Provide a detailed description of the exhibition (concept, theme, and content).
* **Type of Exhibition** (`exhibition_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of exhibition (Fine art, Commercial art, Photography, Sculpture, Digital art, Installation, Mixed media, or Other).
* **Number of Artists or Exhibitors** (`artist_count`, number, required): Enter the number of artists or exhibitors participating in the exhibition.
* **Venue Name or Location** (`venue_name`, text, required): Enter the name or location of the venue.
* **Complete Address** (`venue_address`, text, required): Enter the complete address of the venue.
* **Venue Owner or Manager Contact** (`venue_owner_contact`, text, required): Enter the contact information of the venue owner or manager.
* **Proposed Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed start date of the exhibition.
* **Proposed End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the proposed end date of the exhibition.
* **Daily Operating Hours** (`operating_hours`, text, required): Enter the daily operating hours of the exhibition (Example - 10 AM to 6 PM).
* **Will You Carry Liability Insurance?** (`liability_insurance`, select_one, required): Confirm if you will carry liability insurance (True, False, or In process).
* **Insurance Coverage Amount** (`insurance_amount`, number, required): Enter the insurance coverage amount (only if you have liability insurance).
* **Safety and Emergency Plan** (`safety_plan`, text, required): Provide a detailed safety and emergency plan for the exhibition.
* **Agree to Comply with Local Regulations** (`compliance_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to comply with all local regulations (Yes, I agree or False).
