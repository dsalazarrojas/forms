# Hotel Reservation Confirmation Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Reservation Confirmation Verification Form is designed to verify the details of a hotel reservation in real-time. This form helps to confirm the guest's identity and reservation details, which is crucial for hotel operations and billing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the guest's first name in the "Guest First Name" field.
2. Enter the guest's last name in the "Guest Last Name" field.
3. Provide the guest's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the date of the guest's check-in in the "Checkin Date" field (if applicable).
6. Enter the date of the guest's checkout in the "Checkout Date" field (if applicable).
7. Enter the room number assigned to the guest.
8. Enter the guest's ID number.
9. Enter the confirmation code provided by the hotel (if available).
10. Enter the reservation ID assigned to the guest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest First Name**: Enter the first name of the guest.
* **Guest Last Name**: Enter the last name of the guest.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the guest.
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the guest.
* **Checkin Date**: If the guest has not checked in yet, enter the date they are expected to check in.
* **Checkout Date**: If the guest has not checked out yet, enter the date they are expected to check out.
* **Room Number**: Enter the room number assigned to the guest.
* **Guest ID**: Enter the guest's ID number.
* **Confirmation Code**: If provided by the hotel, enter the confirmation code.
* **Reservation ID**: Enter the reservation ID assigned to the guest.
* **Hotel Name**: This field is optional and is not currently used for any specific functionality. You can enter the hotel name if you have one.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information entered for accuracy before proceeding.
* If you have any issues or doubts while filling the form, contact the hotel's reservation team for assistance.
