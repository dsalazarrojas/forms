# hvac_technician_training_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The hvac_technician_training_form is used for the registration and enrollment of HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning) technician training programs. This form helps to collect relevant information from users who are interested in enrolling in a training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information on each page. If a field is marked as "required", please ensure that you provide the correct information in that field. The form is divided into pages, each page is labeled with a number. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. contact_info (contact_info)
* **Contact Information (contact_info)** (`text`, **required:** false): This field is used to enter the user's contact information. Please provide your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.

### 2. enrollment_date (enrollment_date)
* **Enrollment Date (enrollment_date)** (`date`, **required:** false): This field is used to select the date when you plan to enroll in the training program.

### 3. training_program (training_program)
* **Training Program (training_program)** (`text`, **required:** false): This field is used to select the type of training program you are interested in.

### 4. program_duration (program_duration)
* **Program Duration (program_duration)** (`number`, **required:** false): Enter the duration of the training program you are interested in.

### 5. program_cost (program_cost)
* **Program Cost (program_cost)** (`number`, **required:** false): Enter the cost of the training program.

### 6. contact_phone (contact_phone)
* **Contact Phone (contact_phone)** (`text`, **required:** false): This field is used to enter your contact phone number.

### 7. contact_email (contact_email)
* **Contact Email (contact_email)** (`email`, **required:** false): This field is used to enter your contact email address.

### 8. training_institution (training_institution)
* **Training Institution (training_institution)** (`select_multiple`, **required:** false): Select the institution where you will be enrolling in the training program.

### 9. training_institution_type (training_institution_type)
* **Training Institution Type (training_institution_type)** (`select_one`, **required:** false): Select the type of institution where you will be training.

### 10. course_name (course_name)
* **Course Name (course_name)** (`text`, **required:** false): Enter the name of the training program you are interested in.

### 11. program_hours (program_hours)
* **Program Hours (program_hours)** (`number`, **required:** false): Enter the number of hours in the training program.

### 12. training_schedule (training_schedule)
* **Training Schedule (training_schedule)** (`time`, **required:** false): Enter the schedule for the training program.
 
## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields. If you are unsure about a particular field, please contact the training administrator for guidance.
