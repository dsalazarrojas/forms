## Hockey Ticket Draw Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a person for a hockey ticket draw. The form collects personal information from the registrant, which is used to verify their identity and participation in the draw.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1.  Fill out the form with the required and optional fields.
2.  Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3.  Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to contact you for updates and notifications.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Team**: Enter the name of the hockey team you belong to.
* **Player**: Enter your hockey player status (e.g. player, coach, etc.).
* **Player Position**: Enter your position on the team (e.g. goalie, forward, etc.).
* **Player's Name**: Enter the name of the player you are registering for.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure successful verification and registration.
* If you are registering on behalf of someone else, please provide their name and contact information as well.
