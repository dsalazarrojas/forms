# spiritual-retreat-accommodation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a spiritual retreat. Please use it only for this purpose and ensure you have not already registered for the same retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your details.
2. Enter your name in the participant-name field.
3. Enter your valid email address in the participant-email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the participant-phone field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the participant-date-of-birth field. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter any accommodation details you may need in the accommodation-details field.
7. If you have any special requests, please enter them in the special-request field.
8. Enter your arrival date in the participant-arrival-date field. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
9. Enter your arrival time in the participant-arrival-time field. Please use the time format HH:MM.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false): This is the main section where you can input your general information.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you about the retreat.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number for any last-minute communication.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, note, required: false): If you have any specific accommodation requests, please provide them here.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, note, required: false): If you have any special requests or needs, please include them here.
* **Participant Arrival Date** (`participant_arrival_date`, date, required: false): Enter your arrival date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant Arrival Time** (`participant_arrival_time`, time, required: false): Enter your arrival time in the format HH:MM.

## Tips
Please ensure that you double-check your information before submitting the form, as you will not be able to edit it after submission. If you have any issues or questions, please contact us at your provided email or phone number.
