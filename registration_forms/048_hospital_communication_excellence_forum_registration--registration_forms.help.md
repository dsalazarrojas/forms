# Hospital Communication Excellence Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register their interest in attending the Hospital Communication Excellence Forum, a professional event focused on best practices for effective communication in healthcare settings.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Event Details section with the event you are registering for.
2. Provide your personal Attendee Details.
3. Select your Communication Preferences to ensure you receive relevant information from us.
4. If applicable, specify your Dietary and Accessibility Needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Event Details (page 1)
* **Event Details (event_details)**: Enter the name of the event you are registering for.

### Attendee Details (page 2)
* **Attendee Details (attendee_details)**: Provide your personal information such as name, email, and phone number so we can contact you about the event.

### Communication Preferences (page 3)
* **Communication Preferences (communication_preferences)**: Select your preferred method(s) of communication from the list (e.g. email, phone, or text message).

### Dietary and Accessibility Needs (page 4)
* **Dietary Needs (dietary_needs)**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please select the option that best describes your needs. (Note: This field is optional but helps us plan meals and accommodations.)
* **Accessibility Needs (accessibility_needs)**: If you require any special accommodations, please select all that apply. (Note: This field is optional but helps us ensure your participation in the event is accessible and comfortable for you.)
