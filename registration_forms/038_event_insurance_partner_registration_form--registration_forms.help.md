# Event Insurance Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Insurance Partner Registration Form is designed to collect information from event organizers who need to register with insurance providers as partners. This form helps event organizers share their event details and partner information with the insurance providers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (if any) and any other fields you feel are necessary to provide accurate information about your event.
2. Ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review the entire form before submission to avoid any errors or misunderstandings.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Insurance Partner Registration** (`event_insurance_partner_registration_form_1`, text, false): This is the form title, which is not a field to fill out.
* **Name** (`name`, text, false): Enter the name of your organization or the name you want to be known by the insurance provider.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Provide your contact email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, false): Enter your phone number for the insurance provider to contact you.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, false): Enter the name of the event you are registering as a partner with.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, false): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, false): Enter the end date of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, false): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, false): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Insurance Partner Name** (`insurance_partner_name`, text, false): This field is meant for the insurance provider's internal use, enter your partner's name as it is known to you.
