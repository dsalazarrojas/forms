# manufacturing_risk_assessment_workshop_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Manufacturing Risk Assessment Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from participants who wish to attend the workshop. It gathers necessary details about each attendee, their session preferences, risk assessment, any specific requirements, and date of attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your details in the "Attendee Details" field. This is your name and any other identifying information.
2. Next, select your session preferences in the "Session Choices" field. Please choose from the options provided.
3. In the "Risk Assessment" field, indicate whether you have any potential risks that need to be addressed.
4. In the "Requirements" field, provide any special requirements you have for the workshop, such as accommodations or equipment needs.
5. Finally, select the date you plan to attend the workshop from the calendar in the "Workshop Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details**: A text field to enter your name and any other details you'd like to provide about yourself.
* **Session Choices**: A field to select one of the options provided for the workshop session you'd like to attend.
* **Risk Assessment**: A field where you can indicate if you have any potential risks to be addressed, by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Requirements**: A text field to provide any specific requirements you have for the workshop, such as accommodations or equipment needs.
* **Workshop Date**: A calendar field to choose the date you plan to attend the workshop. 

## Tips
Please ensure you are selecting the correct date you plan to attend, and make sure to indicate any risks or requirements that need to be taken care of to ensure a smooth and productive workshop experience.
