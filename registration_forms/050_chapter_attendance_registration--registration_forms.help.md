# Chapter Attendance Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for chapter members and attendees to register for chapter events. It collects information about the event, attendee, and attendee's preferences and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill in the required fields**: Ensure that all the fields marked as `required` are filled in correctly.
2. **Enter the event details**: Select the event date, name, location, and start time.
3. **Enter your information**: Provide your chapter name, name, email, phone number (if applicable), graduation year, and membership status.
4. **Choose your registration type**: Select whether you are registering as a member, guest, or family.
5. **Provide any necessary details**: If you have dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, provide the required details.
6. **Show interest in volunteering**: Let us know if you are willing to volunteer for future events.
7. **Confirm your attendance**: Confirm that you have registered and checked in for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the meeting or event.
* **Event or Meeting Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or meeting.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location where the event is held.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Chapter Name** (`chapter_name`): Enter the name of the chapter.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`): If you are a member, enter the year you graduated.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`): Select whether you are an active, inactive, or non-member.
* **First Time Attending Event** (`first_time_attending`): Select whether this is your first time attending the event.
* **Accompanied By** (`accompanied_by`): If applicable, enter the name of the person accompanying you.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`): Select whether you are registering as a member, guest, or family.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`): If you have any food restrictions, enter the details here.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_needs`): Select whether you need any special accommodations.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`): If you need special accommodations, provide a brief description.
* **Willing to Volunteer** (`willing_to_volunteer`): Select whether you are willing to volunteer for future events.
* **Volunteer Interests** (`volunteer_interests`): If you are willing to volunteer, list your interests.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_heard_about_event`): Select how you heard about this event.
* **Attendance Confirmed** (`check_in_confirmation`): Confirm that you have registered and checked in for the event.
