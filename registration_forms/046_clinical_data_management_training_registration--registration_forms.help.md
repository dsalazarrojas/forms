# Clinical Data Management Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the Clinical Data Management Training program. It will help us tailor the program to your needs and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely, paying attention to required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
- Be honest and specific when providing information about your background, experience, and goals.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, use the "Special requirements or requests" field to provide more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: \* (Text field) - Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: \* (Text field) - Enter your last name here.
* **Email Address**: \* (Email field) - Enter your email address here (e.g., [name]@example.com).
* **Phone Number**: (Text field) - Enter your phone number here (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Organization Name**: (Text field) - If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name here.
* **Job Title**: \* (Text field) - Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **Data management experience level**: \* (Select one field) - Choose your level of experience in data management, from Beginner to Expert.
* **Years of experience in healthcare IT**: (Number field) - Enter the number of years you have worked in healthcare IT.
* **Training Level**: \* (Select one field) - Choose the level of training you are looking for, from Fundamentals to Specialized track.
* **Preferred course topic**: \* (Select one field) - Choose your preferred course topic from the list provided.
* **Preferred training date**: \* (Date field) - Enter your preferred training date.
* **Preferred training format**: \* (Select one field) - Choose your preferred training format, from In-person classroom to Self-paced online.
* **Time preference**: \* (Select one field) - Choose your preferred time of day for training.
* **Preferred program duration**: \* (Select one field) - Choose the duration of the program that suits you best.
* **What do you want to achieve?**: (Text field) - Describe your learning objectives for the training program.
* **Current challenges with data management**: (Text field) - Describe any challenges you are currently facing with data management.
* **Special requirements or requests**: (Text field) - If you have any special requirements or requests, enter them here.
