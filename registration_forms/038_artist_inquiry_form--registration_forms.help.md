# Artist Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help artists express their interest in showcasing their work at the gallery. By providing accurate and complete information, artists can increase their chances of getting noticed by the gallery team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields marked as `true` in the form schema.
2.  Provide high-quality images of your artwork in the `Images Attached` field.
3.  Proofread and review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Artist Information
*   **Artist Name**: Enter your full name or pseudonym. This will be used as a reference to your artistic identity.
*   **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email. This will be used as the primary means of communication.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number. This will be used for phone calls and follow-ups.
*   **Website or Portfolio**: Enter a link to your online portfolio or website showcasing your artwork. This will help the gallery assess your art style and quality.
*   **Years of Artistic Practice**: Enter the number of years you've been creating art. While not mandatory, this can provide context for your experience.

### Artistic Background
*   **Primary Art Medium**: Choose the type of art you create (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, Photography, etc.). This helps the gallery understand your medium and style.
*   **Artist Statement**: Enter a brief description of your artistic philosophy and goals. This provides insight into your creative vision.
*   **Previous Exhibitions**: List previous exhibitions and shows you've participated in. This demonstrates your experience in showcasing your work.

### Gallery Exhibition Interest
*   **Type of Exhibition You Are Interested In**: Choose the type of exhibition you're interested in (e.g., Solo, Group, Pop-up, etc.). This helps the gallery tailor its approach to you.
*   **Number of Artworks Available for Exhibition**: Enter the number of artworks you're willing to showcase. While not mandatory, this helps the gallery plan exhibitions efficiently.
*   **Description of Your Artwork**: Enter a short description of your current artwork. This helps the gallery understand your current style and themes.

### Portfolio and Documentation
*   **Portfolio or Sample Work Links**: Enter links to your online portfolio or samples of your work. This allows the gallery to review your artwork.
*   **High-Quality Images of Your Work**: Attach images of your artwork. This provides a visual representation of your art style.
*   **Resume or CV**: Attach your professional resume or CV. This provides a detailed overview of your art experience and background.

### Previous Exhibitions
*   **Previous Exhibitions or Shows**: List previous exhibitions, shows, or events you've participated in. This demonstrates your exhibition experience.

### Inquiry Details
*   **Specific Inquiry or Questions**: Enter any specific questions or concerns you'd like to discuss. This helps the gallery tailor its approach to your needs.
*   **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose your preferred contact method (e.g., Email, Phone, Text Message, etc.). This ensures you receive the best communication from the gallery.
*   **I Agree to Submit This Inquiry**: Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes, I Agree" and "Yes, contact me" for permission to follow up about opportunities.

## Tips
-   Please ensure all fields with a `* true` mark are filled in to increase your chances of being considered for exhibition.
-   Attach high-quality images of your artwork.
-   Proofread carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
-   Be clear and concise in your submissions, as this will make it easier for the gallery team to review your application.
