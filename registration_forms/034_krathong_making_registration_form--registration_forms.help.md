# Krathong Making Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants for the Krathong making event. Please use this form to provide accurate and complete information about the event and yourself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Registration Data" page to fill out the registration information.
2.  Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3.  Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Data** (`registration_form_data`, text, required/optional): This is the main registration page where you can fill out your registration information.
    *   Please enter your registration information in this field.
*   **name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
*   **event** (`event`, select_one, required): Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will be attending the event.
*   **event_date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **time_start** (`time_start`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
*   **time_end** (`time_end`, time, required): Enter the end time of the event in the format 12:00 AM/PM.
*   **location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the event.
*   **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
*   **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **note** (`note`, note, optional): This is an optional field for any additional notes or comments about the event.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that you have filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
*   Double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
