## Purpose
This form is for vendors who wish to participate in our craft fair. It is used to collect necessary information about the vendor, their contact details, and their items. The purpose of this form is to help the craft fair committee decide on the acceptance of the vendor to the fair.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Vendor Details" page with your name, address, and other relevant details.
2. In "Contact Person Name", enter the name of the contact person for your vendor booth.
3. In the "E-Mail" field, enter your email address so that we can communicate with you about your application.
4. In the "Phone" field, enter your phone number so that we can contact you about your application.
5. On the "Description" page, provide a brief description of your products or services.
6. On the "Categories" page, select the primary categories of items you will be selling.
7. On the "Images" page, select how many images of your items you will be attaching.
8. Finally, on the "Images" page again, select whether or not you will be attaching any images.
