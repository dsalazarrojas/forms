# Typhoid Vaccination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Typhoid Vaccination Registration Form is a tool designed to collect essential information from individuals who have traveled to or reside in areas with a high risk of typhoid fever. This form helps healthcare professionals track and manage vaccination records for effective disease prevention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Provide basic user information, including your name, contact number, and email address.
2. Share your medical history, including any previous vaccinations or medical conditions that may impact your vaccination eligibility.
3. Answer travel-related questions, such as the country or region you have visited recently.
4. Provide details about the vaccination you are registering for, including the type and dosage.
5. Schedule an appointment for vaccination or provide information about previous vaccinations.
6. Read and agree to the consent terms, indicating you understand the terms and conditions of the vaccination process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (Text, Optional):** This is the basic information about the user, including name, contact number, and email address.
* **Medical History (Text, Optional):** Share any previous vaccinations, medical conditions, or health concerns that may impact your vaccination eligibility or safety.
* **Travel Info (Text, Optional):** Provide details about the country or region you have visited recently, including dates and duration.
* **Vaccination Info (Text, Optional):** Enter the type and dosage of the vaccination you are registering for.
* **Appointment Info (Text, Optional):** Schedule an appointment for the vaccination or provide details about previous vaccinations.
* **Consent (Text, Optional):** Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the vaccination process, indicating you understand the guidelines.
