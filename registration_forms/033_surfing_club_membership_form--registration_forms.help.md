# Surfing Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Surfing Club Membership Form is designed to gather information from applicants for membership at the club. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Select your Membership Type (Member, Member - Family, Member - Student, Member - Senior).
7. Choose your Membership Duration (Month, Six Months, Year, Other).
8. Select the start and end dates for your membership.
9. Choose a Payment Method (Bank Transfer, PayPal, Cash).
10. Enter your Address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Required)

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Collects the first name of the applicant.

### Last Name (Required)

* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Collects the last name of the applicant.

### Email (Required)

* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Collects the email address of the applicant.

### Phone (Optional)

* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Collects the phone number of the applicant.

### Date of Birth (Optional)

* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Collects the date of birth of the applicant.

### Membership Type (Optional)

* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Selects the type of membership the applicant is interested in (Member, Member - Family, Member - Student, Member - Senior).

### Membership Duration (Optional)

* **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Selects the duration of the membership (Month, Six Months, Year, Other).

### Membership Start Date (Optional)

* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, date, optional): Collects the start date of the membership.

### Membership End Date (Optional)

* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, date, optional): Collects the end date of the membership.

### Payment Method (Optional)

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Selects the payment method for the membership (Bank Transfer, PayPal, Cash).

### Address (Optional)

* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Collects the address of the applicant.
