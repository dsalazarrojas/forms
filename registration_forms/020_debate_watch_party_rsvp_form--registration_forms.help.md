# Debate Watch Party RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and RSVP for a watch party related to the debate event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Submit the form to complete your RSVP.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Host** (`host_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the host or person organizing the watch party.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event, e.g. "Debate Watch Party - [Event Name]".
* **Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the watch party in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the watch party in HH:MM format.
* **Venue** (`venue`, text, required): Enter the location where the watch party will take place.
* **RSVP By** (`rsvp_by`, date, required): Enter the date by which you need to RSVP to the watch party.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you want to be listed on the RSVP list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for additional contact information.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please include them in the notes section (not shown here).
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event host or organizer.
