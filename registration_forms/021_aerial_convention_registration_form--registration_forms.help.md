# Aerial Convention Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Aerial Convention Registration Form is used for attendees to provide their personal and workshop preference information for the convention. This information is necessary for the convention organizers to manage attendees' experiences and ensure a smooth event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required information before starting.
2.  Fill in your Full Name.
3.  Enter your Email Address.
4.  Enter your Phone Number.
5.  Provide your Mailing Address.
6.  Select your Aerial Arts Experience Level.
7.  Choose your Ticket Type and Workshop Preferences.
8.  If necessary, specify your Dietary Requirements.
9.  Agree to the Liability Waiver.
10.  Select your Payment Method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself as an attendee.
*   **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you with important information about the convention.
*   **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for us to reach you in case of an emergency or to send you important updates.
*   **Mailing Address** (Mailing Address, text, required): Enter your mailing address so we can send you convention materials and information.
*   **Aerial Arts Experience Level** (Aerial Arts Experience Level, select_one, required): Choose your level of experience in aerial arts to help us tailor the convention to your needs:
    -   Beginner
    -   Intermediate
    -   Advanced
    -   Professional
    -   Instructor
*   **Ticket Type** (Ticket Type, select_one, required): Choose your ticket type for the convention:
    -   Full Weekend Pass ($250)
    -   Saturday Only ($150)
    -   Sunday Only ($150)
    -   Workshops Only (No Shows)
*   **Workshop Preferences** (Workshop Preferences, select_multiple, required): Select up to 3 workshops that interest you:
    -   Silks Fundamentals
    -   Advanced Trapeze
    -   Lyra Flows
    -   Rope & Corde Lisse
    -   Flexibility & Contortion
    -   Rigging Safety 101
*   **Dietary Requirements** (Dietary Requirements, select_one, required): If you have any dietary requirements, choose them here:
    -   None
    -   Vegetarian
    -   Vegan
    -   Gluten-Free
    -   Nut Allergy
*   **Liability Waiver** (Liability Waiver, select_one, required): Agree to the terms and conditions and release liability by selecting "I Agree."
*   **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your payment method:
    -   Credit Card
    -   PayPal

## Tips
-   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
-   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the convention organizers at [insert contact info].
-   We recommend registering early to secure your spot and take advantage of early bird pricing.
