# Call For Abstract Submissions - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for abstract submissions for a research conference or competition. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to increase your chances of being considered for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the submitter information section with your personal details.
2. Write a title and content for your abstract in the Abstract Details section.
3. Select the research area, keywords, and submission type relevant to your work.
4. Confirm that you grant rights to publish your abstract and that it is original, unpublished work.
5. Review and submit your form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your valid phone number for contact purposes (optional).
* **Institution or Organization**: Enter your institution or organization name.
* **Your Role or Position**: Enter your role or position in your institution or organization (optional).
* **Abstract Title**: Enter a concise and descriptive title for your abstract (250-500 words).
* **Abstract Content**: Write a detailed and clear content for your abstract (250-500 words).
* **Research Area or Discipline**: Select the research area or discipline relevant to your work.
* **Keywords**: Enter 3-5 keywords separated by commas that describe your abstract.
* **Type of Submission**: Select the type of submission (e.g., Original Research, Literature Review, Case Study).
* **Preferred Presentation Type**: Select the preferred presentation type (e.g., Oral Presentation, Poster Presentation).
* **Was This Research Funded?**: Check if your research was funded (optional).
* **Funding Source Details**: Enter the funding source details if applicable (optional).
* **List of All Authors**: Enter a list of all authors and their affiliations.
* **Corresponding Author Name**: Enter the name of the corresponding author.
* **This is original, unpublished work**: Confirm that your abstract is original and unpublished work.
* **I grant rights to publish this abstract**: Confirm that you grant rights to publish your abstract.
* **Any other submission type**: Select any other submission type if applicable (optional).
