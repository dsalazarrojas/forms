# sports_organization_online_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register with a sports organization and 
  provide personal and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the name of the sports organization you are registering with.
2.  Select your preferred sports interests.
3.  Enter your email address.
4.  Provide your phone number (optional).
5.  Choose whether you are a member or non-member of the sports organization.
6.  Select the sports you are interested in.
7.  Enter any additional information you think is relevant.
8.  Enter the date you became a member of the sports organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **sports_organization**: This is the name of the sports organization you are registering with.
  Please enter the name exactly as it appears on your official documents or 
  membership cards.
* **sports**: This field is for your to select the sports that interest you.
  Please choose all the sports you are interested in or have experience with. 
  You can select more than one sport.
* **email**: Enter your valid email address. This will be used as your contact 
  information with the sports organization.
* **phone**: This is your phone number. While it's optional, we recommend 
  providing it for easy communication with the sports organization.
* **member_type**: Please choose whether you are a Member or Non-Member of the 
  sports organization. This will help the organization understand your 
  registration status.
* **sports_interests**: Select all the sports that you are interested in or have 
  experience with. You can choose more than one sport.
* **additional_info**: This is a free text field for you to provide any 
  additional information about yourself. This could be anything you think the 
  sports organization should know about you.
* **member_since**: Enter the date you became a member of the sports organization. 
  This will help the organization understand your registration history.

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting 
  the form. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the 
  sports organization directly.
