# health_work_experience_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Health Work Experience Registration" form is designed to collect and store information about healthcare providers and their work experience in the healthcare industry. This form helps us to create a comprehensive directory of healthcare professionals with relevant work experience, making it easier for others to find and connect with them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your relevant details.
2. Ensure that you are providing information about a healthcare provider.
3. Double-check your contact details and ensure that your emergency contact information is up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Healthcare Provider ID** (Number, Optional): If you have a unique ID assigned by a healthcare organization, enter it here.
* **Contact Details** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other relevant contact information.
* **Work Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your work experience in the healthcare industry, including your job title, work setting, and the duration of your experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text, Optional): Enter the name, email address, and phone number of an emergency contact person who can be reached in case of an urgent situation.

## Tips
Please ensure that you are honest and accurate when filling out this form. If you are unsure about any information, please ask us for clarification.
