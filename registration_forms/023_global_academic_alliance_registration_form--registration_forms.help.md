# Global Academic Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and track global academic alliances for your organization. It collects information about partner institutions, partnership status, agreement, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill in the required fields marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Select the correct options for partnership type, status, and relationship level.
3.  Enter the partnership description in the text field.
4.  Upload the agreement document if required.
5.  Submit the form once you have completed all the necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Institution ID** (`institution_id`, number, **required**: no): Enter the institution ID.
*   **Institution Type** (`institution_type`, select_one, **required**: no): Select the type of institution (School of the Arts, School of Engineering, School of Science, University).
*   **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, **required**: no): Enter the name of the institution.
*   **Contact ID** (`contact_id`, text, **required**: no): Enter the contact ID.
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, **required**: no): Enter the contact email.
*   **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, **required**: no): Enter the contact phone number.
*   **Partner ID** (`partner_id`, text, **required**: no): Enter the partner ID.
*   **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, **required**: no): Enter the partner name.
*   **Partnership Start Date** (`partnership_start_date`, date, **required**: no): Enter the partnership start date.
*   **Partnership End Date** (`partnership_end_date`, date, **required**: no): Enter the partnership end date.
*   **Agreement Signed** (`agreement_signed`, select_one, **required**: yes): Check if an agreement has been signed.
*   **Agreement Notes** (`agreement_notes`, note, **required**: no): Enter any notes about the agreement.
*   **Partnership Status** (`partnership_status`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Select the status of the partnership (Active, Inactive, Suspended).
*   **Partnership Type** (`partnership_type`, select_multiple, **required**: yes): Select the type of partnership (Formal, Informal, Unknown).
*   **Partner Institutional Relationships** (`partner_institutional_relationships`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Select the type of relationship between partner institutions (Partnership, Collaboration, Other).
*   **Partner Institutional Partners** (`partner_institutional_partners`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Check if the institutions are in a partnership.
*   **Partner Relationship Level** (`partner_relationship_level`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Select the level of relationship between partners (Strategic, Informal, None).
*   **Partner Ownership** (`partner_ownership`, text, **required**: no): Enter the partner ownership percentage.
*   **Partner Academic Prophets** (`partner_academic_prophets`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Select the type of academic relationship between partners (Partnership, Collaboration, Other).
*   **Partner Academic Partners** (`partner_academic_partners`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Check if the institutions are academic partners.
*   **Agreement Date** (`agreement_date`, date, **required**: no): Enter the date of the agreement.
*   **Partner Institutional Partnership** (`partner_institutional_partnership`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Check if the institutions are in a partnership.
*   **Partner Institutional Partnership Type** (`partner_institutional_partnership_type`, select_multiple, **required**: no): Select the type of partnership between institutions.
*   **Partner Ownership Percentage** (`partner_ownership_percentage`, text, **required**: no): Enter the percentage of ownership.
*   **Partnership Description** (`partnership_description`, text, **required**: no): Enter a description of the partnership.

Please fill out this form completely to register and track global academic alliances accurately. Ensure to upload any required documents and fill out all necessary fields.
