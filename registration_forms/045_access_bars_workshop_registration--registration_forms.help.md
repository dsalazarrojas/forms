# Access Bars Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Access Bars Workshop Registration form, helping you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the participant's personal details:
	* First name: Provide your first name, as it should be spelled as you prefer it to be.
	* Last name: Enter your last name, as it should be spelled as you prefer it to be.
	* Email address: Enter your email address as it appears on your contact information.
	* Phone number: Enter your primary phone number where you can be reached.
2. Select your preferred workshop date: Choose a date for the workshop that suits you best.
3. Choose your experience level: Select the option that best describes your experience with Access Bars:
	* No experience - first time
	* Had a Bars session as a client
	* Previously certified - looking to recertify
	* Certified practitioner seeking a refresher
4. Choose your motivation for attending the workshop: Select all the options that apply to you:
	* Personal growth and wellness
	* Learning a new modality to offer clients
	* Stress and anxiety relief
	* Curiosity about Access Consciousness
	* Recommendation from a friend or practitioner
	* Professional development
5. Provide any health considerations: If you have any health concerns, please enter them here.
6. List any dietary restrictions or allergies: If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them here.
7. Choose how you heard about the workshop: Select the option that best describes how you learned about this workshop:
	* Social media
	* Website
	* Friend or referral
	* Access Consciousness community
	* Local flyer or poster
	* Other
8. Choose your payment method: Select the payment method that suits you best:
	* Credit or debit card
	* PayPal
	* Bank transfer
	* Cash at the door
9. Finally, confirm your understanding of the workshop policies: Select the option that best fits your understanding:
	* Yes - I agree
