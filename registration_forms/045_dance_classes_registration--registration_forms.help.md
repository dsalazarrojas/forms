# Dance Classes Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for dance classes. Fill out this form to sign up for a dance class that suits your interests and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the dance class you are interested in from the "Classes List" drop-down menu.
2. Enter the date you plan to attend the dance class in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the time you plan to attend the dance class in the "Time" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dance Classes Form 1** (`dance_classes_registration_form_1`, text, optional): This field is not currently used in the form. It's likely a placeholder for a future field that will allow users to enter any additional comments or notes about their dance class registration.
* **Classes List** (`dance_classes_registration_form_2`, select_one, optional): Select the dance class you are interested in from the drop-down menu. This will help us know which class you are trying to register for.
* **Date** (`dance_classes_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the dance class. This field is used to schedule your attendance.
* **Time** (`dance_classes_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to attend the dance class. This field is used to schedule your attendance at a specific time.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name to identify you as a student registering for the dance class.
* **Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted by us about your dance class registration.
* **Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number where we can contact you with any last-minute updates about the dance class schedule or changes.
