# Martial Arts Registration And Liability Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect student and emergency contact information, as well as a waiver agreement from students' guardians, for the martial arts school. It is a legal requirement for the school to have informed consent and up-to-date contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1.  **Student Info**: Enter the student's name and contact details.
2.  **Contact Details**: Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the student's care.
3.  **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, relationship, and contact details of the person responsible for the student's emergency contact.
4.  **Student Guardian Info**: Enter the name and contact information of the guardian.
5.  **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Briefly describe the relationship between the emergency contact and the student.
6.  **Waiver**: Choose "True" if you give permission for the martial arts school to use your child's name and contact information for administrative and marketing purposes.
7.  **Waiver Agreement**: Briefly describe your understanding of the waiver agreement.
8.  **E Signature**: Enter your electronic signature, indicating that you have agreed to the terms of the waiver.
9.  **E Signature Date**: Enter the date of your electronic signature.
10. **E Signature Title**: Enter your title or position.
