# BUSTNBURN BBQ Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the BUSTNBURN BBQ event. It will help us tailor the experience to each individual's preferences and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select relevant options from the provided lists for optional fields.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the event.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the BBQ event you are registering for.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are registering as an individual, couple, family, or group.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): If registering for others, specify the number of people in your party.
* **BBQ Preference** (`bbq_preference`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred type of BBQ meat, or choose 'No preference' if you're open to anything.
* **Favorite Side Dishes** (`side_dishes`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the side dishes you'd like to enjoy during the BBQ.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred beverage option.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please list them here.
* **Will Children Be Attending?** (`kids_attending`, select_one, optional): Indicate if children will be attending with you.
* **Number of Children** (`number_of_kids`, number, optional): If children are attending, specify the number of kids.
* **Interested in Volunteering?** (`help_volunteer`, select_one, optional): Let us know if you're interested in helping with the event.
* **Special Skills or Experience** (`special_skills`, text, optional): If you have any special skills or experience that could aid the event, please list them.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay for the event (credit card, PayPal, cash, or other).
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, optional): If applicable, specify the registration fee per person.
* **Special Requests or Notes** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you need any special accommodations or have any notes, please include them here.
