# Group History Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and document group sessions, meetings, or events. It helps gather important information about each session, including session details, organizers, and other key points.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Session Details section with the relevant information about your session.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the session organizer.
3. If there are any special requirements for the session, describe them in the Special Requirements field.
4. Indicate which type of group this session belongs to (e.g., School, Club, or Community Group).
5. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
6. Select the date and time of the session.
7. Add any notes or comments about the session.
8. If applicable, list the required documents that attendees will need to bring.
9. Finally, add any additional comments or information about the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): A brief description of the session, including the topic, format, or activity.
* **Organizer** (`organizer`, text, optional): The person responsible for organizing or leading the session.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Any specific conditions or needs that attendees should be aware of (e.g., allergies, equipment requirements).
* **School, Club, or Group** (`school_club_group`, select_multiple, optional): The type of group this session belongs to (e.g., School, Club, Community Group).
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): The email address of the session organizer or contact person.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the session organizer or contact person.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): The date of the session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Additional comments or information about the session.
* **Required Documents** (`required_documents`, select_multiple, optional): A list of documents that attendees will need to bring to the session (e.g., ID, Passport, Insurance Card).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Any extra information or remarks about the session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields (if any).
* Double-check the accuracy of the information you provide to ensure it reflects the actual session details.
* Use this form to document all your group's sessions, meetings, or events for easy tracking and reference.
