# Golf Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Golf Registration Form is designed to collect information from golf players and enthusiasts. This form helps us understand your preferences, skills, and affiliations related to golf.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely, but feel free to skip any questions that don't apply to you.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5. If you have a phone number, please enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
6. For "Are you good at playing golf?", select one of the options that best describes your skill level.
7. If you have a team, please enter the number of team members in the "Team Member Count" field.
8. In the "Contact Information" field, provide any additional notes or details about yourself.
9. If you want to join a team, select "Team A", "Team B", or "Team C" from the "Team Selection" field.
10. If you are a member of a golf club, select "Member" or "Non-Member" from the "Golf Club Member" field.
11. Finally, enter your registration date in the "Registration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Are you good at playing golf?** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Select "I'm a pro" if you are an expert golfer.
	+ Select "Average" if you are an average golfer.
	+ Select "Novice" if you are a beginner.
	+ Select "Beginner" if you are a complete beginner.
* **Team Member Count** (Select Multiple, Optional): Enter the number of team members you have.
* **Contact Information** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional notes or details about yourself.
* **Team Selection** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Select "Team A" if you want to join Team A.
	+ Select "Team B" if you want to join Team B.
	+ Select "Team C" if you want to join Team C.
* **Golf Club Member** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select "Member" if you are a member of a golf club.
	+ Select "Non-Member" if you are not a member of a golf club.
* **Registration Date** (Date, Optional): Enter your registration date.
