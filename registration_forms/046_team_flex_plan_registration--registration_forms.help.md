# Team Flex Plan Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Flex Plan Registration form is designed to collect information about team members and their participation in the Flexible Work Schedule. Please fill out this form to provide your team's details and ensure a smooth transition to the new schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to include all required information.
- Select all team members who will be participating in the Flexible Work Schedule.
- Enter the manager's and team lead's names and email addresses correctly.
- If a date is required, use the mm/dd/yyyy format.
- Review and agree on the participation with both manager and team lead.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Team Members**: Select the team members who will be participating in the Flexible Work Schedule. This could be multiple team members.
- **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the manager who will be supervising this team. 
- **Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the manager.
- **Team Lead**: Enter the name of the team lead who will be responsible for this team.
- **Team Lead Email**: Enter the email address of the team lead.
- **Team Flex Start Date**: Select the start date for the team's participation in the Flexible Work Schedule.
- **Team Flex End Date**: Select the end date for the team's participation in the Flexible Work Schedule.
- **Manager Agreed**: A text area for the manager to provide their agreement or notes.
- **Team Lead Agreed**: A text area for the team lead to provide their agreement or notes.
- **Team Notes**: A text area for team members to provide any additional notes about the team.
- **Manager Notes**: A text area for managers to provide any additional notes about the team.
- **Form Submitted By**: This field is used to track who submitted the form.
- **Form Submitted Date**: This field is used to track the date the form was submitted.
  This field can be left blank as it is filled by the system automatically.
- **Team Flex Plan Registration**: Enter a title for the team's participation in the Flexible Work Schedule.
  This is a plain text field.
  
#Tips
- Make sure to have the correct email addresses of the manager and team lead.
- Review the participation dates carefully to ensure they are correct.
- Enter any additional notes about the team or the participation in the text fields.
- Both the manager and team lead should agree to the team's participation in the Flexible Work Schedule.

Note that this guide only covers the provided YAML fields.
