# Dog Walking Club Pet Owner Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dog Walking Club Pet Owner Membership Form is designed to collect information from pet owners who are interested in joining the Dog Walking Club. This form is used to gather essential details about the pet owners, their pets, and their membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Pet Owners Details**: Provide your name and contact information.
2. **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and phone number.
3. **Membership Application**: Briefly explain why you are interested in joining the Dog Walking Club.
4. **Pet Details**: Provide information about your pet, including their name, breed, age, and any medical conditions.
5. **Group Walk Schedule**: Indicate how often you would like to walk your pet with the Dog Walking Club.
6. **Payment Details**: Select your preferred payment method and provide any additional payment information.
7. **Owner Signature**: Sign your name to confirm that the information provided is accurate.
8. **Walker Signature**: The Dog Walking Club representative will sign to confirm receipt of your application.
9. **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information or messages for the Dog Walking Club.
