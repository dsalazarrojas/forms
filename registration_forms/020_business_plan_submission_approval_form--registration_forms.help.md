# Business Plan Submission Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from submitters to ensure that their business plans meet certain requirements before approval.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the submission information section with your name and email address.
2. Provide your phone number (optional).
3. Enter the submission date.
4. In the business plan details section, enter the name of your business and its type (industry or sector).
5. Specify the version number or last updated date of your plan.
6. Answer questions about the completeness and quality of your submitted plan.
7. Indicate the approval status and provide any reviewer comments or feedback.
8. Enter the name of the person reviewing your plan.
9. Specify the next steps after this submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, text, required): Enter your full name, as it will be used for submission confirmation and further communication.
* **Email Address** (`submitter_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, as it will be used for submission confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if you'd like to be contacted for further information.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are submitting your plan.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business, as it appears in the submitted plan.
* **Type of Business** (`business_type`, text, required): Enter the industry or sector of your business.
* **Plan Version or Date** (`plan_version`, text, optional): Enter the version number or last updated date of your plan.
* **Executive Summary Included** (`executive_summary_included`, select_one, required): Specify if your plan includes an executive summary.
* **Financial Projections Included** (`financial_projections_included`, select_one, required): Specify if your plan includes financial projections.
* **Market Analysis Included** (`market_analysis_included`, select_one, required): Specify if your plan includes market research and competitor analysis.
* **Operations Plan Included** (`operations_plan_included`, select_one, required): Specify if your plan includes a detailed operations strategy.
* **Management Team Described** (`management_team_described`, select_one, required): Specify if your business plan includes key personnel and roles documented.
* **Document Quality and Formatting** (`document_quality`, select_one, optional): Rate the overall presentation of your plan (Poor, Fair, Good, Very Good, or Excellent).
* **Plan Completeness** (`completeness_assessment`, select_one, optional): Assess the completeness of your plan (Incomplete - Major gaps, Incomplete - Some gaps, Adequate, Complete, or Comprehensive).
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, select_one, required): Indicate the approval decision (Approved, Approved with conditions, Pending further review, Rejected, or Needs revision).
* **Reviewer Name** (`reviewer_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person reviewing your plan.
* **Reviewer Comments** (`reviewer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments from the reviewer.
* **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, text, optional): Enter what happens after this submission.

Note: The reviewer will use this information to make an informed decision about the approval status of your submitted plan.
