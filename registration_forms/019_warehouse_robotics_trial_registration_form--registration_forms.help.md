# Warehouse Robotics Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users who want to participate in the warehouse robotics trial program. It collects essential details for the trial process, ensuring a smooth and productive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please answer the questions truthfully and thoroughly.
- Ensure that you have all the necessary information before completing the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, not required): Enter your name and contact details so we can get in touch with you about the trial process.
- **Trial Details** (`trial_details`, `text`, not required): Describe the specific details of the trial you are interested in participating in.
- **Site Information** (`site_info`, `text`, not required): Provide information about your site or location where the trial will take place.
- **Collection Plan** (`collection_plan`, `text`, not required): Share your plan for collecting data during the trial.
- **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `text`, not required): Enter details about the person submitting the form (e.g., name, title).
- **Site Qualification** (`site_qualification`, `text`, not required): Describe the qualification of your site for the trial (e.g., size, type).
- **Collection Data** (`collection_data`, `text`, not required): List the data you are willing to collect during the trial.
- **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, `text`, not required): Share any follow-up information or questions you have about the trial.

# Tips
- Please double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out.
- Thank you for your participation in the trial registration process!
