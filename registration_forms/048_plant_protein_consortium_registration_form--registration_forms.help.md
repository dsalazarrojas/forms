# Plant Protein Consortium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the Plant Protein Consortium register new members or partners to be part of their initiatives.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
2. Enter your organization's name in the "Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address for your organization in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your organization's phone number in the "Phone" field (optional).
5. Enter your organization's address in the "Address" field.
6. Select the role of your organization within the consortium (Member, Partner, Collaborator) using the "Role in Plant Protein Initiatives" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the number of members within your organization if applicable.
8. Confirm if your organization has data on plant proteins.
9. If yes, provide a brief explanation on what type of data you have.
10. Add any additional comments or information in the "Notes" field (optional).
11. Finally, enter the name and contact information of the contact person for your organization in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, required): This is the name of your organization.
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required): This is the name of your organization.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for your organization to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): This is the phone number of your organization.
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's address.
* **Role in Plant Protein Initiatives** (`Role in Plant Protein Initiatives`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the role of your organization within the consortium.
* **How Many Members in Organization** (`How Many Members in Organization`, `number`, optional): If your organization has members, enter the number here.
* **Has Data on Plant Proteins** (`Has Data on Plant Proteins`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if your organization has data on plant proteins.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your organization.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required): Enter the name and contact information of the contact person for your organization.

## Tips

* Please fill out all the required fields accurately to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can reach out to the Plant Protein Consortium team for assistance.
* Make sure to enter the correct information to avoid duplicate or incorrect registrations.
