# meal_prep_challenge_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about you as a participant in the meal prep challenge. This information is used to match you with a suitable coach who will guide you through the challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter your First Name
2. Enter your Email
3. Enter your Phone Number
4. Enter your Participant Pre-Game Goal (e.g., lose weight, gain muscle, etc.)
5. Enter the duration of your goal (e.g., 1 month, 3 months, etc.)
6. Enter your Weight Goal (e.g., lose 10 pounds, etc.)
7. Choose how often you plan to eat meals:
	* Daily
	* Most Days
	* Some Days
	* Rarely
	* Never
8. Enter the name of your coach
9. Enter your coach's Email
10. (Optional) Enter your coach's Phone Number
11. Choose your Participant Diet (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.):
	* Vegetarian
	* Vegan
	* Low-Carb
	* Gluten-Free
	* Omnivore
	* Keto
12. (Optional) List any Participant Allergies or Medical Conditions
13. (Optional) List any Participant Ingredients Restrictions
14. (Optional) List any Participant Medical Conditions
15. (Optional) List any Participant Special Requests
16. Choose your Coach's Approach (e.g., Macro, Meal Planning, etc.):
	* Macro
	* Meal Planning
	* Mindful Eating
	* Portion Control
	* Mindful Eating/Macro
	* Macro/Portion Control
17. (Optional) Enter any other Coach Approach
18. (Optional) Choose your Coach's Diet (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.):
	* Vegetarian
	* Vegan
	* Low-Carb
	* Gluten-Free
	* Omnivore
	* Omnivore/Low-Carb
19. (Optional) List any Coach Ingredients
20. (Optional) List any Coach Medical Conditions
21. (Optional) Enter your Coach's Experience
22. (Optional) Enter your Coach's Education
23. (Optional) Enter any Coach Licenses
24. (Optional) List any Participant Dietary Restrictions
25. (Optional) List any Participant Ingredients Restrictions
26. (Optional) List any Participant Special Requests

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Participant Pre-Game Goal**: Enter your goal for participating in the meal prep challenge (e.g., lose weight, gain muscle, etc.).
* **Goal Duration**: Enter the duration of your goal (e.g., 1 month, 3 months, etc.).
* **Weight Goal**: Enter your weight goal (e.g., lose 10 pounds, etc.).
* **Meal Frequency**: Choose how often you plan to eat meals:
	+ **Daily**: Eat meals every day.
	+ **Most Days**: Eat meals most days.
	+ **Some Days**: Eat meals some days.
	+ **Rarely**: Eat meals rarely.
	+ **Never**: Do not eat meals.
* **Coach Name**: Enter your coach's name.
* **Coach Email**: Enter your coach's email address.
* **Coach Phone**: (Optional) Enter your coach's phone number.
* **Participant Diet**: Choose your participant diet:
	+ **Vegetarian**: Eat a vegetarian diet.
	+ **Vegan**: Eat a vegan diet.
	+ **Low-Carb**: Eat a low-carb diet.
	+ **Gluten-Free**: Eat a gluten-free diet.
	+ **Omnivore**: Eat an omnivore diet.
	+ **Keto**: Eat a keto diet.
* **Participant Allergies**: (Optional) List any allergies or medical conditions you have.
* **Participant Ingredients Restrictions**: (Optional) List any ingredients you cannot eat.
* **Participant Medical Conditions**: (Optional) List any medical conditions you have.
* **Participant Special Requests**: (Optional) List any special requests you have.
* **Coach Approach**: Choose your coach's approach:
	+ **Macro**: Focus on macronutrients.
	+ **Meal Planning**: Focus on meal planning.
	+ **Mindful Eating**: Focus on mindful eating.
	+ **Portion Control**: Focus on portion control.
	+ **Mindful Eating/Macro**: Combine macro and mindful eating.
	+ **Macro/Portion Control**: Combine macro and portion control.
* **Coach Approach Other**: (Optional) Enter any other coach approach.
* **Coach Diet**: (Optional) Choose your coach's diet:
	+ **Vegetarian**: Focus on vegetarian diet.
	+ **Vegan**: Focus on vegan diet.
	+ **Low-Carb**: Focus on low-carb diet.
	+ **Gluten-Free**: Focus on gluten-free diet.
	+ **Omnivore**: Focus on omnivore diet.
	+ **Omnivore/Low-Carb**: Combine omnivore and low-carb diet.
* **Coach Ingredients**: (Optional) List any ingredients your coach cannot use.
* **Coach Medical Conditions**: (Optional) List any medical conditions your coach has.
* **Coach Experience**: (Optional) Enter your coach's experience.
* **Coach Education**: (Optional) Enter your coach's education.
* **Coach Licenses**: (Optional) Enter any coach licenses.
* **Participant Dietary Restrictions**: (Optional) List any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Participant Ingredients Restrictions**: (Optional) List any ingredients you cannot eat.
* **Participant Special Requests**: (Optional) List any special requests you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact your coach.
