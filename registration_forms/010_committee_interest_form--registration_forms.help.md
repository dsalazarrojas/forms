# Committee Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help the student union committees get to know you and understand your interests and skills. Your input will be used to select the most suitable committees for your skills and strengths.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Personal Information (Pages 1-3):** Start by providing your personal details, including your full name, student ID number, email address, phone number, current academic year, and a brief description of your interest in serving on a committee.
2. **Committee Preferences (Pages 4-5):** Choose two committees that you are interested in serving on from the given options. Be sure to provide a brief explanation for your choices.
3. **Experience and Skills (Page 6):** Describe your relevant experience and skills that would be beneficial for your committee role.
4. **Availability (Page 7):** Indicate your availability for weekly meetings and your preferred evening meetings.
5. **Getting to Know You (Page 8):** Provide a brief description of your personality and working style.
6. **Submission (Page 9):** Confirm that you are interested in serving on the committee and agree to fulfill your duties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_full_name`, `text`, required): Your full name as a student.
* **Student Identification Number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): Your student ID number.
* **Official School Email Address** (`student_email`, `email`, required): Your official school email address.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`student_phone`, `text`, required): Your primary contact phone number.
* **Current Academic Year** (`student_academic_year`, `select_one`, required): Select your current academic year (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate Student).
* **What is your first choice committee** (`first_choice_committee`, `select_one`, required): Choose your first preference from the given committee options (Events and Programming, Finance and Budgeting, etc.).
* **What is your second choice committee** (`second_choice_committee`, `select_one`, required): Choose your second preference from the given committee options.
* **Why are you interested in these specific committees** (`reason_for_choice`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation of your interest in these committees.
* **Describe any relevant experience** (`relevant_experience`, `text`, required): Describe your relevant experience that would be beneficial for the committee role.
* **What key skills would you bring to the committee** (`key_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the skills that you would bring to the committee (Leadership, Organization, Communication, etc.).
* **Are you available for weekly meetings** (`meeting_availability`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you are available for weekly meetings (True or False).
* **Which evenings are you generally available for meetings** (`available_evenings`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the evenings that you are available for meetings.
* **How would you describe your personality or working style** (`personality_traits`, `text`, required): Describe your personality or working style.
* **Share a fun fact about yourself** (`fun_fact`, `text`, optional): Share a fun fact about yourself.
* **Date of Submission** (`submission_date_form`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submission.
* **I confirm that I am interested in serving on the committee and will fulfill my duties** (`acknowledgment_interest`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your interest in serving on the committee and agree to fulfill your duties.
