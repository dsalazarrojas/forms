# Patient Centered Care Model Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Patient Centered Care Model Training program. It is used to collect information from trainees who are interested in participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the training programs you are interested in from the list.
2. Enter your name and contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. Choose a date and time for the training you would like to attend.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Programs**: Select one or more of the training programs offered from the list.
	+ This field allows you to choose which training programs you are interested in participating in.
* **Trainee's Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
	+ This is the name of the person registering for the training.
* **Training Date**: Choose a date for the training you would like to attend.
	+ This is the date you would like to participate in the training.
* **Training Time**: Choose a time for the training you would like to attend.
	+ This is the time you would like to participate in the training.
* **Trainee's Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
	+ This is the email address we will use to communicate with you about the training.
* **Trainee's Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is the phone number we will use to contact you about the training.
* **Trainee's Address**: Enter your address.
	+ This is the address we will use to mail you any materials or information about the training.
* **Trainee's Name (optional)**: If you are registering on behalf of someone else, enter their name.
	+ This is the name of the person you are registering for the training.
