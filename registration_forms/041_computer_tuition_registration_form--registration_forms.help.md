# Computer Tuition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register for computer tuition. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to enable us to process your application correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in all the required fields marked as "required" with a red asterisk (*).
2. Enter your full name and email address in the respective fields.
3. Indicate your interest in a specific course by typing the topic or course code in the "Course interest" field.
4. Select the funding source for your tuition from the options provided.
5. If applicable, provide your preferred availability in the "Availability" field.
6. In the "Emergency contact" field, enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, `text`, *required*): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, *required*): Enter your valid email address to receive tuition confirmation and updates.
* **Course Interest** (`course_interest`, `text`, *required*): Type the topic or course code you are interested in.
* **Funding Source** (`funding_source`, `select_one`, *required*): Select how you are funding your tuition from the options provided.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, optional): Provide your preferred days and times.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): Enter the name and phone number of a contact person in case of an emergency.
