# Sorority Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sorority Registration Form is designed to collect information from prospective members for sorority chapter registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as `true` in the form).
2. Enter your name and contact information (email address, phone number).
3. Select your grade level and academic status.
4. Provide your date of birth.
5. Choose your enrollment fee payment method (if applicable).
6. Confirm your recruitment agreement.
7. Confirm you are submitting on your own behalf.
8. Enter chapter details (chapter name, email, website).
9. If applicable, upload a document.
10. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Sorority Name** (`sorority_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sorority you are registering for.
* **Chapter Name** (`chaptername`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the sorority chapter you are registering for.
* **Email Address** (`emailaddress`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phonenumber`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`dateofbirth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Grade Level** (`gradelevel`, `select_one`, required): Select your current academic grade level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior).
* **Major** (`major`, `text`, required): Enter your major.
* **Academic Status** (`academicstatus`, `select_one`, required): Select your academic status (Active, Active (Transfer), Active (Non-Traditional), Active (Post-Bacc)).
* **Enrollment Fee** (`enrollmentfee`, `number`, required): Enter the enrollment fee for the selected sorority chapter.
* **Recruitment Agreement** (`recruitmentagree`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand the recruitment agreement.
* **Enrollment Disclaimer** (`enrollmentdisclaimer`, `note`, optional): Enter any notes or comments about your enrollment.
* **Upload Document** (`upload_document`, `text`, optional): If applicable, upload a document.
* **Sorority Letters** (`sororityletters`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter sorority letters.
* **Sorority Colors** (`sororitycolors`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the sorority colors.
* **Payment Method** (`paymentmethod`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the payment method used.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have reviewed and agree with the information provided.
* **Submitted By** (`submittedby`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you are submitting on your own behalf.
* **Chapter ID** (`chapterid`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the chapter ID.
* **Chapter Name** (`chaptername`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the chapter name.
* **Chapter Email** (`chapteremail`, `email`, optional): If applicable, enter the chapter email.
* **Chapter Website** (`chapterwebsite`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the chapter website.
* **Payment Gateway** (`paymentgateway`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter the payment gateway used.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, optional): Enter your electronic signature.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the sorority chapter administrators.
