# Student Enrollment Approval Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather student information for enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth in the required fields.
2. Provide your Social Security Number and address information, if applicable.
3. Select the correct Contact Phone and Emergency Contact options.
4. Choose the correct Contact Relationship and Student Category options.
5. Select your School Level and Student Status options.
6. Select your degree program.
7. Choose your Student Type and Graduation Date (if applicable).
8. Enter your contact email addresses.
9. Sign with your Student Signature and Guardian Signature (if applicable).
10. Review and select the Approval Status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-02-28).
* **Social Security Number** (text, required): Enter your Social Security Number, if applicable.
* **Address Line 1** (text, required): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (text, required): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (text, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (text, required): Enter your state name.
* **Postal Code** (text, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (text, required): Enter your country name.
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Enter the emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Contact Relationship** (select_one, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and you (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Other).
* **Student Category** (select_multiple, required): Select all that apply from the options for your student category.
* **School Level** (select_one, required): Select your school level (e.g., High School Diploma, GED, Some College, Bachelor's, Associate, Master's, Ph.D.).
* **Degree Program** (text, optional): Enter your degree program information.
* **Student Status** (select_one, required): Select your student status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Graduated, Other).
* **Student Type** (select_one, required): Select your student type (e.g., Undergraduate, Graduate, Transfer, International, Other).
* **Student Status Date** (Date, optional): Enter your student status date, if applicable.
* **Graduation Date** (Date, optional): Enter your graduation date, if applicable).
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Contact Email 2** (email, optional): Enter your secondary contact email address.
* **Student Signature** (text, required): Sign with your signature as the student.
* **Guardian Signature** (text, optional): Sign with your guardian's signature, if applicable.
* **Approval Status** (select_one, required): Select the approval status for your enrollment request (e.g., Approved, Denied, Pending, In Progress).

## Tips
- Please make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and completely.
- If a field is not applicable, select the "Not Applicable" option or enter "N/A."
