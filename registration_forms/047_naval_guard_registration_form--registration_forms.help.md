### Purpose
The Naval Guard Registration Form is designed to collect personal and professional details from naval guards for administrative and operational purposes.

### How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, including all required fields.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (1)** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (4)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (5)** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Date of Entry (6)** (`date_of_entry`, date, optional): Enter the date you entered the naval guard team in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Role (7)** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select your role from the available options (e.g., Officer, Enlisted, etc.).
* **Unit (8)** (`unit`, text, optional): Enter the name of the unit you are assigned to.
* **Address (9)** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **Location (10)** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you are currently stationed or working.
* **Assigned Tool (11)** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional): Select the tools you are assigned to use (e.g., Chat, Chat2, Chat3).
* **Category (12)** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best describes your role or position.
* **Notes (13)** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Submit (14)** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are ready to submit your registration (Yes/No).

### Tips
* Double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have all the necessary information before starting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or the registration team for assistance.
