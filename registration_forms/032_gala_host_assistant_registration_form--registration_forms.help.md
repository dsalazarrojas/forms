# Gala Host Assistant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register as a gala host assistant for an event. It is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in hosting events, conferences, or other types of gatherings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your name, email address, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your contact information, such as your phone number and address.
3. Specify your contact preferences, including how you would like to be contacted.
4. Indicate your availability to work on events.
5. Describe the event you are interested in hosting or assisting with.
6. Specify your event preferences, including the type of events you are interested in assisting with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your phone number, email address, and any other contact information.
* **Address (3)**: Enter your physical address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Preferences (4)**: Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., phone, email, or text).
* **Availability (5)**: Indicate when you are available to work on events (e.g., specific dates or time slots).
* **Event Info (6)**: Describe the event you are interested in hosting or assisting with (e.g., type of event, expected duration).
* **Event Preferences (7)**: Specify the type of events you are interested in assisting with (e.g., size, location).
* **Submit (8)**: Once you have filled in all the necessary information, click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.
