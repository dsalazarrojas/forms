# Health Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Conference Registration Form is used to collect information from healthcare professionals who want to attend the conference. This form is designed to be completed by the attendees to provide their details and preferences for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your area of specialty from the options provided.
3. Write a brief bio about yourself.
4. If you have a photo or a video that you want to share, you can attach it to the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Personal Information
* **Name** (text): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Personal Information** (text): This field is currently empty, and its purpose is unclear. Please enter a brief description of yourself.

### Area of Specialty
* **Area of Specialty** (select one): Choose your area of specialty from the options provided:
	+ Allergy
	+ Cardiovascular
	+ Dermatology
	+ Endocrinology
	+ Gastroenterology
	+ Nephrology
	+ Ophthalmology
	+ Pediatrics
	+ Pulmonology
	+ Rheumatology

### About Me
* **About Me** (note): Write a brief bio about yourself, including your experience and qualifications.

### Photo/Video
* **Photo/Video** (text): Attach a photo or video that represents you and your work.

Note: The photo/video field is not currently functional in this guide. If you want to attach a photo or video, please ask the conference organizers for further instructions.
