# HR Policy Optimization Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the HR Policy Optimization Conference. It is designed for external attendees only. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Input your email address in the "email" field. This should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Type in your company name in the "company" field.
5. Once you've filled out all the required fields, click submit to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (name)**: Please enter your name as you would like it to appear on conference materials.
* **Email (email)**: This is where we will contact you for further information about the conference. Please ensure it's accurate and up-to-date.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number so we can reach out to you if needed.
* **Company (company)**: Type in your company name as you would like it to appear on conference materials.
* **Attendee Details (attendee_details)**: This field is not required, but if you want to provide additional information about yourself as an attendee, you can use this field.
