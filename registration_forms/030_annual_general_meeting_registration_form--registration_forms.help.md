# Annual General Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Annual General Meeting. It collects information about your attendance, preferences, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
3. Select the meeting year you are attending.
4. Choose how you will attend (In Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
5. Select your membership status.
6. Check if you are eligible to vote and select your proxy name if applicable.
7. Choose your preferred meal option.
8. Select breakout sessions you would like to attend.
9. If needed, specify any accessibility requirements or special requests.
10. Choose how you will receive meeting materials (email, mail, pick-up at meeting, or virtual download).
11. Select your payment method.
12. Enter your registration fee if applicable.
13. Specify how you heard about the meeting.
14. Enter any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, text, optional): Enter your membership identification number.
* **Organization or Company** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization or company.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Meeting Year** (`meeting_year`, select_one, required): Select the meeting year you are attending.
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you will attend (In Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Member Status** (`member_status`, select_one, required): Select your membership status.
* **Voting Eligibility** (`voting_eligibility`, select_one, required): Check if you are eligible to vote.
* **Proxy Name** (`proxy_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your proxy if applicable.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your guest if applicable.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your guest if applicable.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select all dietary restrictions that apply (None, Vegetarian, Vegan, etc.).
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, text, optional): Enter any specific dietary allergies or requirements.
* **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred meal option.
* **Breakout Sessions** (`breakout_sessions`, select_multiple, optional): Select breakout sessions you would like to attend.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any accessibility requirements or special requests.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one, optional): Check if you need parking.
* **Materials Delivery** (`materials_delivery`, select_one, required): Choose how you will receive meeting materials.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your payment method.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, optional): Enter the registration fee if applicable.
* **How Did You Hear About This Meeting?** (`how_heard`, select_multiple, optional): Select all sources where you heard about the meeting.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any other special requests or needs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information or comments.
