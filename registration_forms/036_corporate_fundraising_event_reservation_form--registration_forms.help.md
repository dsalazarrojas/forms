# corporate_fundraising_event_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a corporate fundraising event. It captures the necessary information from the attendees and the event organizer to ensure a smooth planning and execution of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your attendee information.
2. Choose your meal preferences.
3. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions, select them from the "Diet Restriction" field.
5. If you have any special requests for the event organizer, enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
6. Enter the event organizer's contact information, including their name, email, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
7. Enter any additional notes from the event organizer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Information**: Enter your name and any other identifying information that you feel is relevant to the event.
* **Meal Preferences**: Select the type of meal you would like to have at the event.
	+ "Yes" indicates that you are interested in having a meal at the event.
	+ "No" indicates that you do not want a meal.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests you may have for the event.
* **Diet Restriction**: Select any dietary restrictions you may have.
	+ "No Restriction" indicates that you do not have any dietary restrictions.
	+ "Veggie" indicates that you are vegetarian.
	+ "Gluten-Free" indicates that you are gluten-free.
	+ "Lactose Free" indicates that you are lactose-free.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Details**: Enter any additional information about the event.
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Address**: Enter the address of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer City**: Enter the city where the event will take place.
* **Event Organizer State**: Enter the state where the event will take place.
* **Event Organizer Zip**: Enter the zip code of the event location.
* **Event Organizer Notes**: Enter any additional notes from the event organizer.
