# Athletic Trainer Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Trainer Seminar Registration form is designed to collect relevant information from attendees to facilitate efficient processing and ensure a smooth experience during the seminar. This guide will walk you through each field's purpose and required information to ensure accurate completion.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Attendee Information**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your registration certificate and any other documents related to the seminar.
2.  **Professional Title**: Enter your professional title, such as "ATC", "Physical Therapist", or "Coach".
3.  **Organization or Institution**: Enter the name of your organization or institution where you work.
4.  **Email Address**: Enter your contact email address to receive updates and notifications about the seminar.
5.  **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up communication.
6.  **Street Address**: Enter your mailing address to receive any physical materials or packages related to the seminar.
7.  **City**: Enter the city where you reside or work.
8.  **State**: Enter your state or province of residence or work.
9.  **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code for accurate mailing.
10. **Certification Level**: Select your athletic training certification level, which will help us tailor the seminar content to your needs.
11.  **Certification Number**: If applicable, enter your certification number for record-keeping purposes.
12.  **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked in athletic training.
13.  **Seminar Name**: Select the seminar you are attending from the list provided.
14.  **Seminar Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the seminar.
15.  **Seminar Location**: Enter the location of the seminar venue.
16.  **Session Selection**: Select one or more sessions that you are interested in attending from the provided options.
17.  **Continuing Education Credits Needed**: Check if you need continuing education credits for your certification or professional development.
18.  **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, indicate them here for meal planning purposes.
19.  **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any special accessibility needs, please let us know.
20.  **Meal Preference**: Select your meal preference for the seminar's catering arrangements.
21.  **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration you are making (e.g., Early Bird, Regular, Late, Student, Group, etc.).
22.  **Registration Fee**: Enter the fee amount in USD.
23.  **CEU Fee**: If applicable, enter the fee amount for continuing education credits.
24.  **Total Amount**: Enter the total amount in USD, including the registration fee and any other applicable fees.
25.  **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will use to complete your registration.
26.  **Purchase Order Number**: If making payment by purchase order, enter the relevant number here.
27.  **Hotel Accommodation Needed**: Indicate if you need hotel recommendations or accommodation assistance.
28.  **Transportation Information Needed**: Check if you require transportation information.
29.  **How Did You Hear About This Seminar**: Select where you heard about the seminar for our referral tracking.
30.  **What Do You Hope to Learn**: Briefly state what you hope to learn from the seminar for your professional development.
31.  **Additional Comments**: Add any additional information or comments you think would be helpful for our staff to know.
32.  **Terms and Conditions Agreement**: Acknowledge your acceptance of the terms and conditions.
33.  **Type Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as a signature.
34.  **Date of Signature**: Date and sign the form.

Please take the time to review each field carefully and accurately complete it according to the guidelines provided. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact our support team for assistance.
