# Tenant Security Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Security Innovation Registration Form is a document that allows tenants to provide information about themselves and their company. This form is used to collect data that will help us better understand security innovations implemented within our tenant base.

## How to Complete this Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the necessary fields with your information. Make sure to provide accurate and clear details about yourself and your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name as it is registered.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within your company.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job role within the company.
* **Security Programs** (`security_programs`, select_multiple, optional): Select the security programs your company has implemented (Options 1, 2, or 3).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This submit button will finalize your registration.
