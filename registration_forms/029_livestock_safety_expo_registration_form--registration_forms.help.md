# Livestock Safety Expo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Livestock Safety Expo, allowing attendees to provide their personal information and select their preferred activities or sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Contact Note" field.
5. Select your preferred date for the expo.
6. Choose a time slot for the expo.
7. Select one option from the "Select One" dropdown menu.
8. Choose multiple options from the "Select Multiple" dropdown menu.
9. Enter a numerical value in the "Integer" field.
10. Enter a decimal value in the "Decimal" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any expo materials.
* **Email** (Email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, optional): Enter your contact phone number, if you'd like to be reachable.
* **Contact Note** (Note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the expo organizers.
* **Date** (Date, optional): Select the date you'd like to attend the expo.
* **Time** (Time, optional): Choose a time slot for the expo.
* **Select One** (Select One, optional): Choose one option from the available list.
* **Select Multiple** (Select Multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the available list.
* **Integer** (Number, optional): Enter a numerical value.
* **Decimal** (Number, optional): Enter a decimal numerical value.
