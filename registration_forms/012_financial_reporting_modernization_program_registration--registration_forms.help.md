# Financial Reporting Modernization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Reporting Modernization Program Registration form is designed to collect feedback from users about their registration and submission experiences with the Financial Reporting Modernization Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, "User Info Form".
2. Fill out the User Info Form with your information.
3. Move on to the next page, "Organization Info Form", and fill out the relevant information.
4. Proceed to the "Participant Details Form" and fill out the required information.
5. On the "Expectations Form", provide any additional comments or questions.
6. Finally, go to the "Submission Confirmation" pages and indicate your satisfaction with the registration and submission processes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info Form** (`user_info_form`, text, required:false)
This form collects some initial information about you.
* **Organization Info Form** (`organization_info_form`, text, required:false)
Provide information about the organization you are registering from.
* **Participant Details Form** (`participant_details_form`, text, required:false)
Fill out any additional details about the participant.
* **Expectations Form** (`expectations_form`, text, required:false)
Share any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Are you satisfied with the registration process?** (`submission_confirmation_1`, checkbox, required:true)
Indicate your satisfaction with the registration process.
* **Are you satisfied with the submission process?** (`submission_confirmation_2`, checkbox, required:true)
Confirm your satisfaction with the submission process.
* **Are you satisfied with the form design?** (`submission_confirmation_3`, checkbox, required:false)
Provide feedback on the overall design of the form.
* **Are you satisfied with the user experience?** (`submission_confirmation_4`, checkbox, required:false)
Share your thoughts on the overall user experience.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate with your answers.
- Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
- Ensure you have filled out all required fields to submit the form.
- If you have any concerns or issues, contact us at form@gic.mx.
