# facility_maintenance_certification_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the certification and registration of facility maintenance services. This form is designed to gather data about the employer's details and certification schedule to ensure regulatory compliance and quality assurance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Registration Form" page.
2. Fill out the "Registration Form" page with the required information.
3. Move on to the "Employer Details" page.
4. Fill out the "Employer Details" page with the required information.
5. Move on to the "Certification Schedule" page.
6. Fill out the "Certification Schedule" page with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (<name>**: This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to fill out the general information.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Employer Details (Employer Details):**
	+ This page asks for information about the employer's details.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Certification Schedule (Certification Schedule):**
	+ This page is where you will be asked to schedule your certification.
	+ This field is not required.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to review your information before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant authorities or your supervisor.
