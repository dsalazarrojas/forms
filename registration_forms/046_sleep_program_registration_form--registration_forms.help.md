# Sleep Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration details from individuals interested in participating in a sleep program. The form helps us understand your sleep history, preferences, and any additional notes that may be relevant to your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your Participant Details**: Fill in the "Participant Details" section with your information, making sure to fill out the required field.
2. **Share your Sleep History**: In the "Sleep History" section, enter your sleep history as a text description.
3. **Choose your Preference**: In the "Preferences" section, select your preferred time for the sleep program (Early Morning, Late Afternoon, or Evening).
4. **Add any Additional Notes**: If you have any additional notes or comments, you can add them in the "Notes" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details (text)** (Required): Enter your name, age, and other relevant details in this field.

### Sleep History
* **Sleep History (text)** (Optional): Share your sleep history, including any relevant dates or periods of time.

### Preferences
* **Preferences (Select One)** (Optional): Choose your preferred time for the sleep program:
	+ Early Morning: Preferably 6:00 AM - 9:00 AM
	+ Late Afternoon: Preferably 2:00 PM - 5:00 PM
	+ Evening: Preferably 6:00 PM - 9:00 PM

### Notes
* **Notes (text)** (Optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with the program team.
