# Registration Identification Number Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Registration Identification Number Request Form is a vital document for individuals seeking to obtain a registration identification number. This form is designed to collect essential information for identification and tracking purposes. Please note that each entry should be accurate and unique to prevent errors and issues.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you enter your details as requested.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address for follow-up and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up and contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth as it appears on your identification documents.
* **School or Agency ID** (6, number, required): If you have an existing school or agency ID, please enter it.
* **Membership ID** (7, number, required): If you have an existing membership ID, please enter it.
* **Request Type** (8, select_one, required): Choose the reason for your registration identification number request, e.g. School, Agency, Membership.
* **Follow-up Needed** (9, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether follow-up is needed, e.g. True or False.
* **Note** (10, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes for the follow-up process.
* **Follow-up Method** (11, select_one, required): Choose the follow-up method, e.g. Chat, Email, Phone, In Person, Video Call.
* **Follow-up Date** (12, date, required): Enter the date for the follow-up action.

## Tips
* Ensure that each field is filled out accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
