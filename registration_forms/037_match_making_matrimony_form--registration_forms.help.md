# Match Making Matrimony Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather personal details for match making purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Navigate through the pages to select the relevant information.
2. Fill in your details in the provided fields.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Details - Page 1
* **User Details** (User Details, optional): Enter your personal details to help us get to know you better.
### Preferences - Page 2
* **Preferences** (Preferences, optional): Describe your preferences to help us find a suitable match for you.
### Personal Details - Page 3
* **Personal Details** (Personal Details, optional): Provide any additional personal details you think are relevant to your match making.

## Tips
* Please be honest and accurate in your answers to get the best possible match.
* You can review and edit your answers before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
