# Festival Press Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Press Mixer RSVP Form is used to register for the Festival Press Mixer event. This form is used to gather important details about the attendees and their RSVPs for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field. This field is required to identify who is registering.
2. Enter your Email in the "Email" field. This field is used for email confirmation and is not required.
3. Enter your Phone in the "Phone" field. This field is not required.
4. Select the event you will be attending from the "Event" field. This field is required to ensure you are registering for the correct event.
5. If you will be bringing media, select "Yes" or "No" in the "Media" field. This field is not required.
6. Enter the date you will be attending the event in the "RSVP Date" field. This field is not required.
7. Enter the time you will be attending the event in the "RSVP Time" field. This field is not required.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field. This field is not required.
9. Enter the date you will be submitting your entry in the "Submission Date" field. This field is not required.
10. Enter the time you will be submitting your entry in the "Submission Time" field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: This field is required for registration purposes.
* **Email (email)**: Enter the email address you want to use for email confirmation. This field is not required.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for phone confirmation. This field is not required.
* **Event (select_one)**: Select the event you will be attending (Festival or Mixer).
* **Media (select_multiple)**: Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will be bringing media.
* **RSVP Date (date)**: Enter the date you will be attending the event.
* **RSVP Time (time)**: Enter the time you will be attending the event.
* **Note (note)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Email Confirmation (email)**: Enter a different email address for email confirmation. This field is not required.
* **Phone Confirmation (text)**: Enter a different phone number for phone confirmation. This field is not required.
* **Submission Details (text)**: Enter any additional details about your submission. This field is not required.
* **Submission Confirmation (text)**: Enter any confirmation about your submission. This field is not required.
* **Submission Status (select_one)**: Select the status of your submission (Confirmed, Pending, or Declined).
* **Event Details (text)**: Enter any additional details about the event. This field is not required.
* **Media Details (text)**: Enter any additional details about the media you will be bringing. This field is not required.
* **Submission Date (date)**: Enter the date you will be submitting your entry.
* **Submission Time (time)**: Enter the time you will be submitting your entry.

## Tips
* Make sure to select the correct event you will be attending.
* Double-check your email and phone numbers for accuracy.
* Enter any necessary details for submission confirmation and submission status.
