# Inclusive Leadership Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Leadership Summit Registration Form is designed to collect personal details and preferences from attendees for a successful and inclusive summit experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select your preferred date for the summit from the "preferred_date" field.
5. Choose your preferred time for the summit from the "preferred_time" field.
6. Enter any allergies or medical conditions in the "allergies" field.
7. Select your dietary preferences for the summit.
8. Choose your preferred venue for the summit.
9. Add any additional notes or comments in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your official email address.
* **phone** (Number, Required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **preferred_date** (Select One, Required): Choose a date from the options provided (2024-01-01, 2024-02-01, 2024-03-01).
* **preferred_time** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or multiple time slots from the options provided (09:00 AM, 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM).
* **allergies** (Text, Optional): If you have any allergies or medical conditions, enter them here for our team's attention.
* **Preferred Food** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose your preferred type of food (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Vegan).
* **Preferred Venue** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose your preferred venue for the summit (On-site, Off-site, Both).
* **notes** (Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes for our team's reference.
