# Day Worker Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals who are interested in registering as a day worker. It captures essential information about the applicant, including contact details, availability, and work experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your Date of Birth to ensure we have your accurate age information.
3. Enter your Contact Phone and Contact Email to enable us to contact you for further information or interviews.
4. Indicate your available dates by selecting the days you are available to work.
5. Choose your available shifts by selecting the shifts you are available to work.
6. Select your available hours by choosing the hours you are available to work.
7. Indicate the end time of your available hours.
8. List any job titles you have available experience with, by selecting the relevant options.
9. Choose where you would like to work by selecting the locations.
10. Enter any relevant skills you have.
11. Provide your Emergency Contact details to ensure we can reach them in case of an emergency.
12. Enter your Social Security Number for background checks.
13. List your previous employers and the duration of your employment.
14. Provide information about your work experience.
15. List your references, including their name and the duration of your employment with them.
16. Fill in any other required fields to provide a complete application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Available Dates**: Select all the dates you are available to work.
* **Available Days**: Choose all the days you are available to work.
* **Available Shifts**: Select all the shifts you are available to work.
* **Available Hours**: Choose all the hours you are available to work.
* **End Time of Available Hours**: Select the end time of your available hours.
* **Job Titles**: Select all the job titles you have available experience with.
* **Job Locations**: Choose all the locations where you would like to work.
* **Skills**: Enter any relevant skills you have.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact details of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Social Security Number**: Enter your Social Security Number for background checks.
* **Previous Employers**: List your previous employers and the duration of your employment.
* **Work Experience**: Provide information about your work experience.
* **References Listed As**: List your references, including their name and the duration of your employment.
* **References Worked For**: Enter information about the company you worked for.
* **Duration of Employment**: Enter the duration of your employment with each reference.
* **Field 22**: This field is reserved for future use and is currently not relevant.
* **References Worked For**: Enter the company you worked for.
* **Duration of Employment**: Enter the duration of your employment with each reference.
