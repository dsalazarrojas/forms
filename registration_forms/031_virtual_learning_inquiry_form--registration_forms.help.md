# virtual_learning_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The virtual learning inquiry form is designed to help users register their interest in virtual learning programs. This form collects information about the user's preferences and interests to facilitate effective communication and provide tailored support.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To start, fill out your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Select your Program of Interest from the drop-down list. You can choose between Virtual Learning, Regular Learning, or Other.
4. Specify your Contact Method by selecting one or more options (Phone, Email, In-person).
5. Choose your Preferred Contact Time if you have a specific time slot in mind.
6. Add any Additional Comments you may have in the space provided.
7. If you have an Assigned Tool, enter its details.
8. If you have an Output File, provide its link or details.
9. Finally, you can optionally provide your Form ID and Assigned Tool ID for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Enter your surname.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Program of Interest (program_of_interest)**: Select your preferred learning program type. This will help us understand your interests.
* **Contact Method (contact_method)**: Select one or more ways you prefer to be contacted (Phone, Email, In-person).
* **Preferred Contact Time (preferred_contact_time)**: Choose a specific time slot you prefer to be contacted.
* **Additional Comments (comment)**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your inquiry.
* **Assigned Tool (assigned_tool)**: If you have an assigned tool, enter its details.
* **Output File (output_file)**: If you have an output file, provide its link or details.
* **Form ID (form_id)**: This is an optional field for reference purposes.
* **Assigned Tool ID (assigned_tool_id)**: This is also an optional field for reference purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* You can modify this form to suit your needs, and we'll be happy to help you with any changes.
