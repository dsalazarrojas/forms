# Health Behavior Science Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for the Health Behavior Science Symposium. It collects necessary information to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your basic contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Indicate your interest in attending specific sessions by selecting the corresponding options.
3. If you have any special needs or requirements, please provide detailed information.
4. Indicate your accommodation and transportation needs, if applicable.
5. Specify your preferred payment method and provide any additional details.
6. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`text`, `form_1`, required: false)
    * This field is not required, but it helps us to understand your background and interests.
* **Session Interests** (`select_multiple`, `session_1`, required: false)
    * Please select the sessions you are interested in attending. You can choose one or multiple sessions.
* **Session 2** (`select_multiple`, `session_2`, required: false)
    * This is the second session selection field, similar to Session Interests.
* **Attendee Information** (`text`, `attendee_1`, required: false)
    * Please provide your basic attendee information.
* **Name** (`text`, `name`, required: false)
    * This is your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false)
    * Please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, `phone`, required: false)
    * This is your phone number.
* **Special Needs** (`text`, `special_needs`, required: false)
    * If you have any special needs or requirements, please provide detailed information.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`text`, `dietary_restrictions`, required: false)
    * If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them here.
* **Other** (`text`, `dietary_restrictions_other`, required: false)
    * This is an additional field for specifying any other dietary restrictions not listed.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`select_one`, `accessibility_requirements`, required: false)
    * Please indicate if you have any accessibility requirements.
* **Accessibility Requirements Other** (`text`, `accessibility_requirements_other`, required: false)
    * If you have any other accessibility requirements not listed, please specify them here.
* **Accommodation Needs** (`select_one`, `accommodation_needs`, required: false)
    * Please indicate if you have any accommodation needs.
* **Accommodation Needs Other** (`text`, `accommodation_needs_other`, required: false)
    * If you have any other accommodation needs not listed, please specify them here.
* **Accommodation Request** (`text`, `accommodation_request`, required: false)
    * If you have any specific accommodation requests, please provide detailed information.
* **Transportation Needs** (`select_one`, `transportation_needs`, required: false)
    * Please indicate if you have any transportation needs.
* **Transportation Needs Other** (`text`, `transportation_needs_other`, required: false)
    * If you have any other transportation needs not listed, please specify them here.
* **Transportation Request** (`text`, `transportation_request`, required: false)
    * If you have any specific transportation requests, please provide detailed information.
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, `payment_method`, required: false)
    * Please indicate your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Method Other** (`text`, `payment_method_other`, required: false)
    * If you have any other payment method not listed, please specify them here.
