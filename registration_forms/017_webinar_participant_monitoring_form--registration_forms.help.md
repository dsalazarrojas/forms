# webinar_participant_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Webinar Participant Monitoring Form is a tool used to monitor and track participant engagement during webinars. This form helps to collect information about participants' feedback, comments, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the welcome message on the first page to understand the purpose of the form.
2. On the second page, select "Option 1" or "Option 2" to provide basic participant information.
3. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
4. Provide any additional feedback in the "participant_feedback" field.
5. Enter any contact phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
6. Select any relevant comments from the "Option 3" and "Option 4" list in the "participant_comments" field.
7. Finally, click "Submit" to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Welcome to Webinar Participant Monitoring Form** (`title`, `note`, not required): This is an introductory message to welcome you to the form.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Option 1" or "Option 2" to provide basic participant information. This information will be used to categorize and analyze participant responses.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, `text`, not required): Provide any additional feedback about the webinar or topic.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select any relevant comments from the list to provide additional information.
* **Submit Form** (`submit_form`, `select_one`, not required): Finally, select "Submit" to complete and submit your responses.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest answers to help us improve our webinars.
* If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
* If you'd like to participate in future webinars, check your email for follow-up opportunities.
