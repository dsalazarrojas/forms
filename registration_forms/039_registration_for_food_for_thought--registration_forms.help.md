# Registration For Food For Thought - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register for a program or service offered by "Food For Thought". It's a way to collect demographic information and skills for the purpose of the program. Please ensure you have a clear understanding of the program's requirements before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the necessary fields with your information and submit. The form consists of several sections:

### Step 1: Demographic Information
- **First Name** (first_name): Enter your first name to help us know you better.
- **Last Name** (last_name): Enter your last name to help us know you better.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
- **Contact Number** (contact_number): Enter your contact number for us to reach you.
- **Address** (address): Enter your address for our records.

### Step 2: Qualifications
- **What You Do at UFinancial** (select_one_1): Choose one of the provided options to describe what you do at UFinancial or select "Other" if it's not listed.
- **What Skills Do You Have** (select_multiple_1): Select all the skills you have from the provided list.

### Step 3: Additional Information
- **Note** (note): Provide any additional information you feel is necessary to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (first_name): A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name): A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): An email field to enter your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): A date field to enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (time_of_birth): A time field to enter your time of birth.
* **Contact Number** (contact_number): A text field to enter your contact number.
* **Address** (address): A text field to enter your address.
* **Note** (note): A text field to enter any additional note.
* **What You Do at UFinancial** (select_one_1): A dropdown menu to select one of the provided options or choose "Other" if not listed.
* **What Skills Do You Have** (select_multiple_1): A multi-select menu to select all applicable skills.
* **Submit** (submit): A text field to submit the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure you fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
- If you have any issues while filling out the form, please contact our support.
- You can only submit the form once with your information; do not attempt to resubmit.
