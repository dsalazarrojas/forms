# Photography Competition Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in a photography competition.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete the form by providing information in all the fields on each page.
2.  Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
3.  Review your information for completeness and accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information (Page 1)
*   **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.

### Photo Details (Page 2)
*   **Photo Details** (Photo Details, text, optional): Provide a description of your photo or any other relevant details about it.

### Submission (Page 3)
*   **Submission** (Submission, text, optional): Upload or enter your photo.

### Additional Information (Page 4)
*   **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional): Enter any other information you think is relevant to your entry.
