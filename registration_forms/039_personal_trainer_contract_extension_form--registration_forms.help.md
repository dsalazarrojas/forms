# Personal Trainer Contract Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Contract Extension Form is used to update or modify the terms of an existing contract between a client and a trainer. This form allows clients and trainers to agree or disagree to the extension of their current contract.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the type of contract extension you wish to agree to from the options provided.
2. Enter your name as the client.
3. Enter the contract date.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter the trainer's phone number.
6. Agree or disagree to the terms and conditions of the contract extension.
7. Enter the payment terms and payment amount.
8. Enter the contract duration.
9. Enter the date of the contract extension.
10. Agree or disagree to the trainer's agreement.
11. Agree or disagree to the client's agreement.
12. Confirm that the updated contract has been signed by selecting 'True' from the options provided.
13. Enter the date the contract was signed.
14. Enter the name of the trainer who signed the contract.
15. Enter the time of signing of the contract.
16. Enter any relevant remarks for the contract extension.
17. Enter any additional remarks for the contract extension.
18. Enter any further remarks for the contract extension.
19. Enter any final remarks for the contract extension.
20. Add any additional comments or notes for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contract Type** (`contract_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of contract extension you wish to agree to from the options provided.
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the client.
* **Contract Date** (`contract_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the contract.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Trainer Phone** (`trainer_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the trainer's phone number.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required): Agree or disagree to the terms and conditions of the contract extension.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `number`, required): Enter the payment terms and amount.
* **Contract Duration** (`contract_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the contract duration.
* **Extension Date** (`extension_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the contract extension.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Trainer Agreement** (`trainer_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Agree or disagree to the trainer's agreement.
* **Client Agreement** (`client_agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Agree or disagree to the client's agreement.
* **Contract Signed** (`contract_signed`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that the updated contract has been signed by selecting 'True' from the options provided.
* **Contract Signed Date** (`contract_signed_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date the contract was signed.
* **Contract Signed By** (`contract_signed_by`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the trainer who signed the contract.
* **Contract Signed At** (`contract_signed_at`, `time`, required): Enter the time of signing of the contract.
* **Contract Remarks** (`contract_remarks`, `note`, required): Enter any relevant remarks for the contract extension.
* **Contract Remarks 2** (`contract_remarks_2`, `note`, required): Enter any additional remarks for the contract extension.
* **Contract Remarks 3** (`contract_remarks_3`, `note`, required): Enter any further remarks for the contract extension.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Add any additional comments or notes for the form.
* **Contract Remarks 4** (`contract_remarks_4`, `note`, optional): Enter any further remarks for the contract extension.
* **Contract Remarks 5** (`contract_remarks_5`, `note`, optional): Enter any final remarks for the contract extension.
* **Contract Remarks 6** (`contract_remarks_6`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional remarks for the contract extension.
* **Contract Remarks 7** (`contract_remarks_7`, `note`, optional): Enter any last remarks for the contract extension.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review and agree or disagree to all terms and conditions of the contract extension.
- Ensure that you have entered all required fields and information.
- Review your completed form carefully before submitting it.
