# Worship Event Guide - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worship Event Guide form is used to register information about upcoming worship events. It helps event organizers or leaders to collect essential details about the event, including the date, time, location, and participants' contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address for further communication.
3. Provide your phone number for easier contact.
4. Enter the date of the worship event.
5. Choose the time of the worship event.
6. Enter the location of the event.
7. Optionally, provide feedback or additional comments about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name, if you wish to be identified by it.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name, if you wish to be identified by it.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easier contact.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the worship event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the worship event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Optionally, provide feedback or additional comments about the event.
