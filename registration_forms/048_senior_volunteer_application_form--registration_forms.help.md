# Senior Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Senior Volunteer Application Form is a registration form designed to gather information from individuals interested in volunteering for a particular organization. This form helps the organization to understand the volunteer's background, skills, and availability to determine their suitability for volunteer roles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name and contact details, including your email address and phone number.
2. Provide your volunteer experience and background information.
3. Select your volunteer status and available shifts (morning, afternoon, or evening).
4. Enter your availability date and end date for volunteering.
5. (Optional) If you have any additional comments or information to share, please use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`Volunteer Name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to while volunteering.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`Volunteer Experience`, `text`, **Required**): Describe your previous volunteer experience, including any relevant skills or qualifications.
* **Volunteer Status** (`Volunteer Status`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your current volunteer status:
	+ Active: I am currently active and available to volunteer.
	+ Retiree: I am a retiree looking to volunteer.
	+ Disabled: I am disabled and looking to volunteer.
* **Shifts** (`Shifts`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select the shifts you are available to volunteer for:
	+ Morning: Available to volunteer during morning shifts.
	+ Afternoon: Available to volunteer during afternoon shifts.
	+ Evening: Available to volunteer during evening shifts.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date you are available to end volunteering.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `note`, **Optional**): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for us to know.
