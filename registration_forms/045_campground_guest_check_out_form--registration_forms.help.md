# Campground Guest Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Campground Guest Check Out Form is designed to gather information from campers who are checking out of the campground. This form helps the management track the camper's experience, cleanliness of the site, and overall satisfaction with the facilities and staff.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the Guest First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Guest Last Name field.
3. Enter your contact email in the Email Address field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the Phone Number field.
5. Enter your campsite number in the Campsite Number field.
6. Enter the date you arrived in the Check-In Date field.
7. Enter the date you are leaving in the Check-Out Date field.
8. Enter the time you are leaving in the Check-Out Time field.
9. Enter the total number of nights you stayed in the Number of Nights Stayed field.
10. Enter the total number of people in your party in the Number in Your Party field.
11. Enter the number of adults in your party in the Number of Adults field.
12. Select the type of accommodation you used from the Type of Accommodation dropdown menu.
13. If applicable, enter the size of your RV in the RV Size field.
14. Rate the cleanliness of the site by selecting from the Site Cleanliness Rating dropdown menu.
15. Rate the facilities by selecting from the Facilities Rating dropdown menu.
16. Rate the staff friendliness by selecting from the Staff Friendliness Rating dropdown menu.
17. Enter your favorite part of your stay in the Favorite Part field.
18. Enter any suggestions for improvement in the Least Favorite Part field.
19. Rate your overall experience by selecting from the Overall Experience Rating dropdown menu.
20. Check if you left any items behind, and if so, describe them in the Items Left Behind field.
21. Check if you experienced any damage during your stay, and if so, describe it in the Damage Details field.
22. Check if you would recommend the campground to others, and if so, answer the Would You Recommend question accordingly.
23. Enter any additional comments in the Additional Comments field.
24. Sign your name in the Guest Signature field.
25. Enter the date of your check-out in the Date field.
