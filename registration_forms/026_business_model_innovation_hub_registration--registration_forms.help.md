# Business Model Innovation Hub Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Model Innovation Hub Registration form is designed to gather essential information from participants interested in the Business Model Innovation Hub. This form will help us understand your background, interests, and goals, allowing us to tailor the hub's resources and opportunities to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:

1. Read each field description carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out each field according to the provided descriptions, using the options and guidelines as a guide.
3. If you are unsure about a field, refer back to this guide or contact the Innovation Hub team for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
- **Participant Name**: <participant_name>, 
  Your full name as a participant.

### Email Address
- **Email Address**: <participant_email>, 
  Your primary email address for contact and updates.

### Phone Number
- **Phone Number**: <participant_phone>, 
  Your contact phone number.

### Job Title
- **Job Title**: <job_title>, 
  Your current job title or position.

### Company Name
- **Company Name**: <company_name>, 
  The name of your organization.

### Company Size
- **Company Size**: <company_size>, 
  The number of employees in your organization.

### Industry
- **Industry**: <industry>, 
  Your primary industry sector.

### Innovation Role
- **Innovation Role**: <innovation_role>, 
  How you are involved in innovation within your organization.

### Registration Type
- **Registration Type**: <registration_type>, 
  The type of participation you wish to have (Full program, Workshop, etc.).

### Areas of Innovation Interest
- **Areas of Innovation Interest**: Select all areas of interest, 
  This includes Business model innovation, Digital transformation, etc.

### Current Challenges
- **Current Challenges**: Select all challenges you face, 
  This includes Lack of resources, Resistance to change, etc.

### Innovation Goals
- **Innovation Goals**: <innovation_goals>, 
  What you hope to achieve through the Innovation Hub.

### Innovation Experience Level
- **Innovation Experience Level**: <experience_level>, 
  Your level of experience with innovation.

### Preferred Program Track
- **Preferred Program Track**: <program_track>, 
  The track you prefer for the program (Startup, Corporate, etc.).

### Availability
- **Availability**: Select all available times, 
  When you can participate in the Innovation Hub.

### Networking Goals
- **Networking Goals**: <networking_goals>, 
  What networking outcomes you are seeking.

### Mentorship Interest
- **Mentorship Interest**: <mentorship_interest>, 
  If you are interested in mentorship.

### Project Submission
- **Project Submission**: <project_submission>, 
  If you have a project to submit, and its status (Ready, in development, etc.).

### Referral Source
- **Referral Source**: <referral_source>, 
  How you became aware of the Innovation Hub.

### Accessibility Needs
- **Accessibility Needs**: <accessibility_needs>, 
  If you require any special accommodations due to accessibility needs.

### Additional Comments
- **Additional Comments**: <additional_comments>, 
  Any other information about yourself or your project that might be useful for us to know.

## Tips
- Be honest about your experience and interests to ensure you get the most out of the Innovation Hub.
- If you need help with any field, don't hesitate to contact the Innovation Hub team.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure you haven't missed any essential information.
