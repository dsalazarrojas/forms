# Event Sponsorship Extension Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Event Sponsorship Extension Form is used to collect additional information from event sponsors to support event extensions. This form should only be completed for event sponsors who have already agreed to provide some level of support for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the **Event Sponsor** field and enter the name of the sponsor who is providing additional support.
2. In the **Additional Info** field, provide any relevant details about the additional support provided by the sponsor.
3. In the **Event Info** field, provide a brief description of the event or project being supported.
4. Select the **Sponsorship Type** field to indicate the type of sponsorship (Gold, Silver, Bronze).
5. Enter the **Sponsorship Amount** field to specify the amount of additional support provided.
6. Select the **Sponsorship Currency** field to indicate the currency of the sponsorship amount (USD, EUR, CAD).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsor**: Enter the name of the event sponsor providing additional support.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any relevant details about the additional support provided by the sponsor.
* **Event Info**: Briefly describe the event or project being supported.
* **Sponsorship Type**: Select the type of sponsorship (Gold, Silver, Bronze) to indicate the level of support.
* **Sponsorship Amount**: Enter the amount of additional support provided by the sponsor.
* **Sponsorship Currency**: Select the currency of the sponsorship amount (USD, EUR, CAD).

## Tips

* Make sure to discuss and agree on the extension with the event sponsor before completing this form.
* Ensure the additional support does not exceed the original sponsorship commitment.
* Enter accurate and relevant information in each field to ensure correct processing of the form.
