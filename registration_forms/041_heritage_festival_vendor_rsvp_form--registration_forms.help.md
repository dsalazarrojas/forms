# heritage_festival_vendor_rsvp_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect necessary information from vendors participating in the heritage festival. Fill out the form to provide your details and confirm your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Vendor Details" section with your information.
2. Describe your booth requirements in the "Booth Requirements" section.
3. Confirm your participation by selecting the correct option in the "Confirm Participation" section.
4. If you have any additional details or notes, you can add them in the "Additional Details" section.
5. Finally, add any notes in the "Notes" section (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details (Text)**: Enter your company's name, contact person's name, and other essential details.
* **Booth Requirements (Text)**: Describe the space, equipment, or other requirements for your booth.
* **Confirm Participation (Select Multiple)**: Choose 'True' to confirm your participation, 'False' to cancel it, or 'Maybe' to indicate uncertainty.
* **Additional Details (Text)**: Provide any extra information that the festival organizers might need to know.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any comments or notes you have about your registration or participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* Double-check your participation confirmation to avoid any last-minute changes.
