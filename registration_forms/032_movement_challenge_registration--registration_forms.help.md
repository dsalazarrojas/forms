# Movement Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants in a movement challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your personal details and emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
    - Emergency Contact: Emergency contact for the challenge.
    - Parent: Parent or guardian.
    - Spouse: Spouse or partner.
    - Other: Other relationship (e.g. roommate).
- **Relationship Other** (`emergency_relationship_other`, `text`, optional): If you selected "Other" as the relationship, specify the type of relationship.
- **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_city`, `text`, optional): Enter the city of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency Contact Province/State** (`emergency_province`, `text`, optional): Enter the province or state of your emergency contact's address.
- **Emergency Contact Country** (`emergency_country`, `text`, optional): Enter the country of your emergency contact's address.
- **T-shirt Size** (`participant_t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of the T-shirt you would like to have in case of an emergency.
    - S, M, L, XL, XXL: T-shirt sizes.
- **Consent** (`participant_consent`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate whether you have given your consent for the challenge.
    - True: Yes.
    - False: No.
- **Other (specify)** (`participant_consent_other`, `text`, optional): If you answered "False" to the consent question, specify the reason why you cannot give your consent.

Please ensure you fill out the form accurately and truthfully. In case of any confusion, please reach out to the challenge administrators for further guidance.
