# Open Data Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Data Workshop Registration Form is a tool used to collect participant information for the Open Data Workshop. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure accurate registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you about workshop details and updates.
3. Enter your phone number if you have one, to allow us to contact you for any urgent matters.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): This field is for your name as you would like it to be listed in our workshop records.
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Please enter a valid email address where we can send you workshop updates and information.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it to allow us to contact you in case of any last-minute changes or updates.
