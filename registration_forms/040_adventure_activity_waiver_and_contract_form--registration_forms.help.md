# Adventure Activity Waiver And Contract Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information and agreements from participants before they engage in adventure activities. The form helps to clarify the terms and risks associated with these activities, ensure the participant's consent, and provide important details for emergency contacts and potential medical considerations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Read and understand the terms and risks associated with the adventure activity.
2. Check the box for Contract terms acknowledgement (question 3) to confirm your understanding of these terms.
3. Confirm you acknowledge the risks (question 4) related to the activity.
4. If applicable, provide medical clearance (question 5) for the activity.
5. Provide your emergency contact information (question 6) in case of an emergency.
6. If you have an insurance provider (question 7), enter their information.
7. Finally, sign the form with your electronic signature (question 8) and date (question 9) as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Waiver and Contract --** (`waiver_header`, note, not required): This section introduces the purpose and importance of the waiver and contract form.
* **Participant name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contract terms acknowledged** (`contract_terms_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Choose the correct response to acknowledge your understanding of the contract terms.
* **Risk acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, select_one, required): Choose the correct response to confirm you understand the risks associated with the activity.
* **Medical clearance provided** (`medical_clearance`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether you have medical clearance for the activity.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Insurance provider and policy** (`insurance_information`, text, not required): If you have an insurance provider, please enter their information.
* **Signature name** (`signature_name`, text, required): Type your full name as it appears on your ID for electronic signature purposes.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date you signed this form.
