# General Knowledge Examination Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for a general knowledge examination. Fill it out carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully review all sections and fields before submitting your data.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Choose a suitable date and time for your examination from the provided date and time fields.
4. Select the desired examination level from the available options.
5. Submit your completed form to register for the examination.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication and notification purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact and communication purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your examination.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your examination.
* **Score** (`test_score`, number, optional): Enter your expected examination score.
* **Level** (`test_level`, select_one, optional): Select your desired examination level.
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the examination.
* **Test Time** (`test_time`, time, optional): Choose a time for the examination.
