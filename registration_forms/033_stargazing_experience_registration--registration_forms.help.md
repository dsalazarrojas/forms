# Stargazing Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants and observers for stargazing experiences. It is used to collect information about the participants and observers, including their contact details, session preferences, and any necessary equipment or policies for the stargazing session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant details, including your name and email address (if required).
2. Select the session time and duration that suits you best.
3. List any equipment you will be using for the stargazing session.
4. Enter the start and end time of the stargazing session.
5. List any policies you will be following during the session.
6. Provide the emergency contact information for the session.
7. Enter the participant's name (if applicable).
8. Enter the observer's name (if applicable).
9. Select the observer's relationship with the participant (if applicable).
10. Enter the observer's phone number (if applicable).
11. Enter the participant's email address (if applicable).
12. Enter the observer's email address (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details): This field is used to collect information about the participant, including their name and email address.
* **Sessions** (Sessions): Select the session time that suits you best. Please choose from Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Other.
* **Equipment** (Equipment): List any equipment you will be using for the stargazing session.
* **Session Schedule** (Session Schedule): Enter the start and end time of the stargazing session.
* **Session Time** (Session Time): Select the time of the stargazing session. This is a dropdown menu for your convenience.
* **Session Duration** (Session Duration): Enter the duration of the stargazing session. This field is optional.
* **Policies** (Policies): List any policies you will be following during the stargazing session.
* **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact): Enter the emergency contact information for the session.
* **Participant Name** (Participant Name): Enter the name of the participant (if applicable).
* **Observer Name** (Observer Name): Enter the name of the observer (if applicable).
* **Observer Relationship** (Observer Relationship): Select the observer's relationship with the participant. Please choose from Friend, Family, Staff, or Other.
* **Observer Phone** (Observer Phone): Enter the phone number of the observer (if applicable).
* **Participant Email** (Participant Email): Enter the email address of the participant (if applicable).
* **Observer Email** (Observer Email): Enter the email address of the observer (if applicable).
