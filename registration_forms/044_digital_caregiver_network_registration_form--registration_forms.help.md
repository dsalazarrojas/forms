# Digital Caregiver Network Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Digital Caregiver Network Registration Form" is designed for caregivers to register themselves in the Digital Caregiver Network, a platform that provides a community for caregivers to share resources, connect with others, and access services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure you have a valid email address and phone number for emergency contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly. This will be used for communication and password recovery purposes.
* **Phone** (4, `text`, required): Enter your phone number that you use for contact purposes.
* **What type of caregiver are you?** (5, `select_one`, required): Select the type of caregiver you are:
	+ Senior Caregiver
	+ Homebound Caregiver
	+ Respite Caregiver
* **Address** (6, `text`, optional): Enter your address if you wish to receive any mail or messages from the Digital Caregiver Network.
* **Zip** (7, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code for geographical location purposes.
* **What services do you need?** (8, `select_multiple`, required): Select the services you require:
	+ Home Care
	+ Respite Care
	+ Transportation
* **Emergency Contact Name** (9, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (10, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their phone number.
* **Caregiver Profile** (11, `note`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information about yourself or your caregiving experience.
* **Submit** (12, `text`, required): Click the submit button to register your information in the Digital Caregiver Network.

## Tips
- Ensure you have a valid email address and phone number for emergency contact purposes.
- Double-check your information before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact the Digital Caregiver Network support team for assistance.
