# Pet Sitting Pet Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact information from pet owners for pet sitting purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field with the requested information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete contact information.
3. Review your answers for correctness before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Sitting Information** (`pet_sitting_pet_form_1`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about the pet sitting request.
* **Contact Details 1** (`contact_details_1`, text, optional): Enter the contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 2** (`contact_details_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 3** (`contact_details_3`, text, optional): Enter more contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 4** (`contact_details_4`, text, optional): Enter further contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 5** (`contact_details_5`, text, optional): Enter more contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 6** (`contact_details_6`, text, optional): Enter additional contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 7** (`contact_details_7`, text, optional): Enter more contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 8** (`contact_details_8`, text, optional): Enter further contact information for the pet owner or contact person.
* **Contact Details 9** (`contact_details_9`, text, optional): Enter more contact information for the pet owner or contact person.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide clear and accurate contact information.
* You may enter any relevant notes or information about the pet sitting request in the "Pet Sitting Information" field.
* If you have any additional contacts, please add them in the "Contact Details 2", "Contact Details 4", and "Contact Details 6" fields.
* If you have any more contacts, please add them in the "Contact Details 3", "Contact Details 5", "Contact Details 7" fields.
* If you have any further contacts, please add them in the "Contact Details 8" and "Contact Details 9" fields.
* You can always add or remove fields as needed, but please make sure to follow the required instructions.
