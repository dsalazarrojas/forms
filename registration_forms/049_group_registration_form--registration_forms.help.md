# Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Group Registration Form" is a tool for users to provide information about a group and its members. This form helps in collecting and organizing data for internal use.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **User Details**: Provide your personal contact information in this section.
2. **Group Details**: Enter a description or details about the group you are registering.
3. **Group Members**: Select the members who are part of the group from the list provided.
4. **Email**: Enter your email address.
5. **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address by re-entering it in this field.
6. **Date**: Choose a date for the group's registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Provide your name and any other personal details that are relevant to your role within the group.
* **Group Details**: Describe the group's purpose, goals, or any other relevant information.
* **Group Members**: Select all members who are part of the group. This can include multiple choices.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your identity or role in the group.
* **Confirm Email**: Re-enter the email address to ensure accuracy and prevent mistakes.
* **Date**: Choose a date for the group's registration. This date is important for tracking and reference purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are registering a valid group and not duplicating an existing one.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before confirming it.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact your team or project administrator for assistance.
