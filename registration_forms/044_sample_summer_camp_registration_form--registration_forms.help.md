# Sample Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect camper information and registration details for a summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to review each question before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please ask for clarification from the camp staff.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Info** (`camper_info`): This section is for providing camper information. Please enter the camper's name, age, and any other relevant details.
* **Health Details** (`health_details`): If the camper has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Please provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email.
* **Guardian Info** (`guardian_info`): If you're not the camper's parent/guardian, please provide their name, phone number, and email.
* **Cabin Buddies** (`cabin_buddies`): If you'd like to be assigned a cabin with specific friends, select "Yes" here.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Please acknowledge that you've read and understand the camp policies and procedures.
* **Camper Photo** (`camper_photo`): If you'd like to submit a photo of the camper, select "Yes" here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`): If you'd like to provide additional emergency contact information, enter it here.
* **Camper Info 2-16** (`camper_info_2`-`camper_info_16`): These sections are for any additional camper information you'd like to provide.

**Tips**

* Please make sure to review each section carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to ask the camp staff for help.
* We look forward to having you and your camper join us at the summer camp!
