# Technology Training Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Technology Training Consent Form is a document that requires participants to provide their information and consent for attending a training session. This form helps the training facilitators to collect necessary contact and emergency contact information from participants and ensure that they are aware of the training details.

## How To Complete This Form

This form is divided into several sections, each containing fields to collect specific information. To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Participant Information section with your name, contact number, and email address.
2. In the Training Information section, enter the training details, including the course title, duration, and location.
3. In the Emergency Contact section, provide the contact information of a person to be reached in case of an emergency.
4. Sign and date the Participant Signature section to indicate your agreement to the training terms.
5. Finally, sign the Participant Signature section again to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information (Text)**: Enter your name, contact number, and email address.
* **Training Information (Text)**: Enter the training details, including the course title, duration, and location.
* **Participant Signature (Text)**: Sign your name to indicate your agreement to the training terms.
* **Emergency Contact (Text)**: Provide the contact information of a person to be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Signature Date (Date)**: This field is not required but is used to record the date of your signature.
* **Training Details (Text)**: Enter the details of the training, including the course title, duration, and location.
