# Real Estate AI Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering individuals for the Real Estate AI Workshop. It collects information about the attendee, including name, contact details, payment method, and any special requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as follows:

1. **Workshop Registration Form**: This field is not required, but if you are registering for the first time, it's recommended to provide some context about your registration.
2. **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
3. **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for communication and workshop updates.
4. **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.
5. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. **Registration Date**: If you are registering for the workshop, select the date you are registering. If you are canceling or rescheduling, enter the date of your original registration.
7. **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the workshop. You can choose from Stripe, Bank Transfer, or Check. Please note that we will contact you separately for payment details.
8. **Payment Method Note**: If you have any special payment instructions or details, please add them here.
9. **Attendee Type**: Select whether you are registering as a Speaker or Attendee. If you are a Speaker, we will contact you separately for more information.
10. **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or accommodations, please add them here.

* **Workshop Registration Form**: This field is not required but provides context for your registration.
