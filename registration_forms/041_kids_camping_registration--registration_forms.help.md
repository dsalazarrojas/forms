# kids_camping_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kids_camping_registration form collects registration information from parents or guardians for kids' activities or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form instructions and ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the fields as instructed, providing accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Consent** (`parent_consent`, text, required): Provide consent for your child to participate in the activity. Check the box to confirm that you grant permission for your child's participation.
* **Kids Name** (`kids name`, text, required): Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification documents or birth certificate.
* **Kids Email** (`kids email`, email, optional): Enter your child's email address if they have one. This will help organizers contact them directly if needed.
* **Parent Email** (`parent email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the parent or guardian responsible for your child's care.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency contact`, text, required): Provide a contact person and their relationship to your child in case of an emergency.
* **Activity Date** (`activity date`, date, required): Enter the date for the activity in the format "yyyy-mm-dd". Example: "2022-07-15"
* **Activity Time** (`activity time`, time, required): Enter the time for the activity in the format "HH:MM:SS". Example: "10:30:00"
* **Activity Location** (`activity location`, text, required): Enter the location of the activity.
* **Activity Address** (`activity address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the activity location.
* **Activity Notes** (`activity notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your child's participation or any special needs.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* Review and submit the form when you have finished.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the relevant authorities or organization administering the form.
