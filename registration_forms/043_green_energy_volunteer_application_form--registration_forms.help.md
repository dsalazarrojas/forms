# Green Energy Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Green Energy Volunteer Application Form is a collection of fields designed to gather information from potential volunteers interested in working with our organization on sustainable energy-related projects. The form is used to ensure that we have the necessary contact information and commitment from applicants before moving forward with the volunteer process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you with updates and notifications.
3. Provide your **Phone** number for further communication.
4. If applicable, enter your **Organization** and **Position** to help us understand your professional background.
5. Select your areas of interest for volunteering in the **Volunteer Areas of Interest** field.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your experience in volunteering in the **Volunteer Experience** field.
7. Write a brief explanation of why you want to volunteer in the **Why do you want to volunteer?** field.
8. Enter any additional comments in the **Additional Comments** field.
