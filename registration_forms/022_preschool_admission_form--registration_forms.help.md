# Preschool Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents/guardians to ensure the child's smooth transition into the preschool environment.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter the child's name in the "Child Name" field.
2.  Enter your name and contact information in the "Parent Name", "Address", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
3.  Select the child's gender from the "Child Gender" dropdown menu.
4.  Choose the appropriate permission from the "Parent Permission" dropdown menu.
5.  Enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number in the "Emergency Contact" and "Contact Number" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Child Name (text, required: false)
*   **Child Name**: Enter the name of the child to be admitted to the preschool. Please use the name as it appears on their birth certificate or official documents.

### 2. Parent Name (text, required: false)
*   **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child. This will help us to contact you regarding any updates or issues related to your child's care.

### 3. Date of Birth (date, required: false)
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth for us to keep track of their age and provide a proper care environment.

### 4. Address (text, required: false)
*   **Address**: Enter your home address or the address where you can be reached for any communication or pickup purpose.

### 5. Email (email, required: false)
*   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can keep you updated about any important information or events related to your child's education.

### 6. Phone (text, required: false)
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you regarding any urgent matters or updates about your child's care.

### 7. Child Gender (select_one, required: false)
*   **Child Gender**: Select the child's gender, which will help us to provide a proper environment for them.

### 8. Parent Permission (select_multiple, required: false)
*   **Parent Permission**: Choose the appropriate permission for your child's care, such as Male, Female, or Both. This will help us to provide the right level of care for your child.

### 9. Emergency Contact (text, required: false)
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a trusted contact person for your child in case of emergencies or any other situations.

### 10. Contact Number (text, required: false)
*   **Contact Number**: Enter the contact number of the person you have designated as the emergency contact.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth admission process.
*   Review your form carefully before submission to avoid any mistakes or errors.
