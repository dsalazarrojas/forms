# e_commerce_partner_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_commerce_partner_training_registration_form is designed to collect information from e-commerce partners for training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.
3. Review the form before submitting to ensure all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Date** (session_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session you are attending.
* **Session Time** (session_time, time, required: false): Enter the time of the training session you are attending.
* **Training Session** (training_session, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select one of the training session options.
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant attending the session.
* **Business Name** (business_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the business associated with the participant.
* **Email** (email, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the participant or business.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the participant or business.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required: false): Additional comments or information for the session.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required: false): Click to submit the form once all fields have been filled out.
* **Cancel** (cancel, text, required: false): Click to cancel and reset form.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
