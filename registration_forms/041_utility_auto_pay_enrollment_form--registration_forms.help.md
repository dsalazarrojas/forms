# Utility Auto Pay Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll customers to a specific auto pay service with their chosen service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in all the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Enter your name, address, and zip code to identify your location.
3. Input your phone number and email address to enable communication with you.
4. Choose the start and end dates of the service period.
5. Select the service provider from your list of available options.
6. Provide the service provider's contact information (name, address, phone, and email).
7. Set up your payment settings (frequency, bank transfer fee, and start and end dates).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Customer Name** (Text): Enter your full name.
- **Address** (Text): Enter your residential or commercial address.
- **Zip** (Text): Enter your zip code.
- **City** (Text): Enter the city where you reside or operate your business.
- **State** (Text): Enter the state where you reside or operate your business.
- **Customer Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
- **Customer Email** (Email): Enter your valid email address.
- **Service Provider** (Text): Choose the service provider you wish to enroll with.
- **Service Provider Name** (Text): Enter the name of your service provider.
- **Service Provider Address** (Text): Enter the address of your service provider.
- **Service Provider Phone** (Text): Enter the phone number of your service provider.
- **Service Provider Email** (Email): Enter the email of your service provider.
- **Service Provider Tax ID** (Text): Enter the tax ID of your service provider.
- **Bank Transfer Fee** (Select One): Choose the bank transfer fee for your service (No Fee, $2.00, $5.00, or $10.00).
- **Payment Start Date** (Date): Enter the date when you want to start making payments.
- **Service Start Date** (Date): Enter the date when the service starts.
- **Payment End Date** (Date): Enter the date when you want to stop making payments.
- **Service End Date** (Date): Enter the date when the service ends.
- **Terms and Conditions** (Note): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the service.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful enrollment.
- Double-check your contact information to ensure accuracy.
- Review the service provider's details before enrolling.
- Understand the payment settings before submitting the form.
- Read and agree to the terms and conditions before enrolling.
