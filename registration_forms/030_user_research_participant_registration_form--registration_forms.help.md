# User Research Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register users as participants in a research study. By filling out this form, you will be able to sign up for the study and provide information about your availability and research interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This field is not required, but it's helpful for us to know your name for communication purposes.
2. Enter your **email** in the "email" field. This field is also not required, but we will use it to contact you about the study.
3. Select your **Research Interests** by choosing one or multiple options from the dropdown menu. You can select "Computer science", "Social science", or "Other" if your research interests match none of these options.
4. If you have a specific date when you are available, enter it in the "Date available" field. This field is not required.
5. If you have a specific time of day when you are available, enter it in the "Time available" field. This field is not required.
6. If you have any additional information about yourself that you would like to share, enter it in the "Additional information" field. This field is not required.
7. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple options for your research interests.
* **Date available** (`date_available`, date, not required): Enter a specific date when you are available.
* **Time available** (`time_available`, time, not required): Enter a specific time of day when you are available.
* **Additional information** (`note`, note, not required): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
