# Insurance Plan Participant Information - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for insurance plan registration. You will need to provide demographic details to be enrolled in a plan.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each required field (if any)
2. Ensure you've entered accurate and complete information for each field

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter your full name, as it will be displayed on your plan documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, not required): Enter your current address for plan delivery and communication.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, not required): Enter your date of birth to confirm your identity.
* **Participant ID Number** (`participant_id_number`, number, not required): Enter your government-issued ID number (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Participant Group** (`participant_group`, select_one, not required): Choose your group affiliation from the available options (Group A, Group B, Group C).
* **Participant Plan** (`participant_plan`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or more plans you wish to be enrolled in from the available options (Plan A, Plan B, Plan C).

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've selected the correct group and plan options relevant to your needs.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult the insurance plan administrator or support team for guidance.
