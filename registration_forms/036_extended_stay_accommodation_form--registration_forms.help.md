# Extended Stay Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Extended Stay Accommodation Form is a registration form used for guests staying in an extended period to provide essential information about their stay. This form helps the accommodation staff to prepare for the guest's arrival, ensure a smooth check-in process, and provide the best possible experience during their stay.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the **Hotel Name** field with the name of the accommodation where you will be staying.
2.  Enter the **Address** of the accommodation in the corresponding field.
3.  Select the type of **Room Type** you would like to book (Hotel Suite, Private Room, or Shared Room).
4.  Provide the **Check-in Date** and **Check-out Date** to ensure accurate scheduling.
5.  Fill in your **Guest Name** to facilitate communication and identification.
6.  Enter your **Email** address to enable easy contact.
7.  Enter your **Phone** number in case of emergencies or important communication.
8.  Indicate the number of **Room Count** you will be booking.
9.  If needed, select **Room Service** (True or False) to indicate if you require any extra services.
10. In the **Special Requests** field, provide any additional information or requests you may have.
11. Finally, enter your **Arrival Time** and **Departure Time** to facilitate scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Hotel Name** (`hotel_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in the name of the accommodation where you will be staying.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the accommodation.
*   **Room Type** (`room_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of room you would like to book (Hotel Suite, Private Room, or Shared Room).
*   **Check-in Date** (`checkin_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to check-in to ensure accurate scheduling.
*   **Check-out Date** (`checkout_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you plan to check-out.
*   **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required: false): Fill in your name for easier identification and communication.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for easy communication.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number in case of emergencies or important communication.
*   **Room Count** (`room_count`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the number of rooms you will be booking.
*   **Room Service** (`room_service`, `select_one`, required: false): Select True or False if you need any extra room service.
*   **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `note`, required: false): In this field, write any additional requests or information you may need.
*   **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter your expected arrival time.
*   **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter your expected departure time.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that you fill in accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth check-in process.
*   Double-check your room count to ensure it matches the number of guests.
*   If you have any special requests, clearly state them to ensure they are met.
*   Review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.
