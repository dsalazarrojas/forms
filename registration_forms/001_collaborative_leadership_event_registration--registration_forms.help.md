# Collaborative Leadership Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a collaborative leadership event. It is used to collect contact information, event registration details, and other relevant information to ensure a successful and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone Number** fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select your **Registration Group** from the list provided.
3. Choose the days you plan to attend by checking the boxes for **Attendance Days**.
4. If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, list them in the **Dietary Requirements** field.
5. Provide your **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone Number**.
6. If you require overnight **Accommodation**, select the corresponding option.
7. Describe any **Accessibility Needs** you may have.
8. Let us know how you **Heard about this Event**.
9. Finally, add any **Additional Comments or Questions** you have in the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Please enter your first and last name.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): We will send confirmation and other important information to this email.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Primary contact number.
- **Organization or Group Name** (`organization_name`, required): Enter the name of your organization or group.
- **Registration Group** (`registration_group`, required): Select the group you are attending with.
- **Attendance Days** (`attendance_days`, required): Choose the days you plan to attend by checking the relevant boxes.
- **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, optional): If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, list them here.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
- **Do you require overnight accommodation?** (`accommodation_needed`, required): Select 'True' if you need accommodation, and 'False' otherwise.
- **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): Describe any special requirements you have for accessibility.
- **How did you hear about this event?** (`how_did_you_hear`, optional): Choose how you heard about this event.
- **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting your registration.
- If you have any issues or need help, contact the event organizers.
