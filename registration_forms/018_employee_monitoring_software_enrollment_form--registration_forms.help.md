# Employee Monitoring Software Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for employee monitoring software enrollment, ensuring that your organization has a clear understanding of your needs and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
	* Fill out the `enrollment_requests` field with your specific enrollment requests.
	* Select your consent to monitoring and data collection by choosing the relevant options.
	* Enter your preferred software version (if applicable).
	* Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to provide.
	* Enter your employee email address for communication purposes.
	* Specify the assigned contact person and tool (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **enrollment_requests** (`enrollment_requests`, text, required): This field is where you enter your specific enrollment requests for the employee monitoring software. Please describe your needs and requirements in a clear and concise manner.
* **give_us_your_consent** (`consent_to_monitoring`, select_multiple, optional): This field is where you choose your consent to monitoring and data collection. Select "Software Monitoring" if you agree to being monitored for software use, "Data Collection" if you allow data collection, or "Time Coordination" if you agree to coordinate your time for monitoring.
* **installation_timeline** (`installation_timeline`, date, optional): This field is where you indicate when you would like the software to be installed. Enter the date when you'd like the installation to take place.
* **software_version** (`software_version`, select_one, optional): This field is where you select the preferred software version. Please choose from the available options.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, optional): This field is where you add any additional comments or notes about your enrollment request.
* **employee_email** (`employee_email`, email, optional): This field is where you enter your employee email address for communication purposes.
* **assigned_contact** (`assigned_contact`, text, optional): This field is where you specify the contact person assigned to this enrollment request.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): This field is where you specify the tool assigned for this enrollment request.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and completeness in your submission to ensure smooth processing and installation of the software.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to your designated contact person for assistance.
