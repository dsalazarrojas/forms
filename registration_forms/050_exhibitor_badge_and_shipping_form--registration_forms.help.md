# Exhibitor Badge And Shipping Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibitor Badge And Shipping Form is designed to collect essential information from exhibitors for their badges and shipping details. This form helps us to create their badges and arrange for accurate shipping.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. If applicable, enter your company name in the "company_name" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
5. Select your badge type from the "badge_type" field.
6. Enter the badge number in the "badge_number" field.
7. Provide your shipping address in the "shipping_address" field.
8. Enter your shipping city in the "shipping_city" field.
9. Enter your shipping state in the "shipping_state" field.
10. Enter your shipping postal code in the "shipping_postal" field.
11. Enter your shipping country in the "shipping_country" field.
12. Enter your shipping email address in the "shipping_email" field.
13. If applicable, enter your shipping phone number in the "shipping_phone" field.
14. Enter any additional notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your badge.
* **last_name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your badge.
* **company_name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you represent a company, please enter the name here.
* **job_title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **badge_design_id** (`badge_design_id`, number, optional): Not applicable for this form, please ignore.
* **badge_type** (`badge_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of badge you would like to receive (e.g. "Badge Type 1", "Badge Type 2", "Badge Type 3").
* **badge_number** (`badge_number`, number, required): Enter the number of your badge.
* **shipping_address** (`shipping_address`, text, required): Enter your shipping address in the format of "Street Address", e.g., "123 Main St".
* **shipping_city** (`shipping_city`, text, required): Enter your city name.
* **shipping_state** (`shipping_state`, text, required): Enter your state abbreviation (e.g., "CA" for California).
* **shipping_postal** (`shipping_postal`, text, required): Enter your postal/zip code.
* **shipping_country** (`shipping_country`, text, required): Enter your country name.
* **shipping_email** (`shipping_email`, email, required): Enter your shipping email address.
* **shipping_phone** (`shipping_phone`, text, optional): If you have a shipping phone number, enter it here.
* **notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about your shipping.
