# Festival Participation Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Participation Extension Form is designed to collect information from vendors, performers, or volunteers about their participation in a festival event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your registration information in the "Registration Form" field.
2. Provide your vendor information in the "Vendor Info" field.
3. Enter your performance information in the "Performer Info" field.
4. Enter your volunteer information in the "Volunteer Info" field.
5. Select your participation type in the "Participation Type" field.
6. Enter any dates related to your participation in the "Dates" field.
7. Enter the start and end dates of your participation in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields.
8. Specify the duration of your participation in the "Duration" field.
9. Enter the time of your participation in the "Time" field.
10. Enter the venue information in the "Venue" field.
11. Provide your contact information in the "Contact" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
13. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
14. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
