# Performing Arts School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll students in the Performing Arts School. Please provide the required information to complete the student's registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields as accurately as possible.
2. Review and submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the student as it appears on their ID or official documents.
* **Student Email** (`email`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the student's address.
* **Program** (`program`, text, required/optional): Select the program the student wants to enroll in.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the end date of the program.
* **Date Of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter the student's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): Select the relationship between the student and the emergency contact.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required/optional): Enter the parent's signature, if required.
* **Student Name 2** (`student_name_2`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the second student, if applying for multiple students.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact email.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid delays or errors in the enrollment process.
