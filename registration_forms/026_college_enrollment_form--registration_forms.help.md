# College Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Enrollment Form is used to register students to a college or university. It collects information for enrollment, billing, and other administrative purposes. This form is required for all students who wish to enroll in the college.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
3. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`student_first_name`): This is the student's first name.
	+ Please enter your first name in the text box provided.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`): This is the student's last name.
	+ Please enter your last name in the text box provided.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): This is the student's date of birth.
	+ Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`): This is the student's email address.
	+ Please enter your email address in the format [email@example.com](mailto:email@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`): This is the student's phone number.
	+ Please enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship_status`): This is the student's citizenship status.
	+ Please select one of the available options: U.S. Citizen, International Student, Permanent Resident, or Temporary Visa.
* **Social Security Number or Tax ID** (`ssn_or_ein`): This is the student's Social Security Number or tax ID number.
	+ If you have a Social Security Number, please enter it here. If you do not have a Social Security Number, please enter your tax ID number. (Optional)
* **Current Address** (`current_address`): This is the student's current address.
	+ Please enter your current address in the format: 123 Main St, Anytown, USA 12345.
* **City** (`address_city`): This is the city of your current address.
	+ Please enter the city of your current address.
* **State or Province** (`address_state`): This is the state or province of your current address.
	+ Please enter the state or province of your current address.
* **Postal Code** (`address_zip`): This is the postal code of your current address.
	+ Please enter the postal code of your current address.
* **Program or Degree Selected** (`program_selected`): This is the program or degree the student is interested in.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Bachelor's Degree, Associate Degree, Graduate Program, Certificate Program, or Other.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major_selected`): This is the major or field of study the student is interested in.
	+ Please enter the major or field of study the student is interested in.
* **Start Term or Semester** (`start_term`): This is the term or semester the student would like to start.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Spring Semester, Summer Semester, Fall Semester, or Winter Semester.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`): This is the student's enrollment status.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Full-Time, Part-Time.
* **Admitted As** (`admitted_as`): This is the student's admission status.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Freshman, Transfer Student, Graduate Student, Non-Degree Student.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`): This is the name of the student's parent or guardian (if applicable).
	+ If you are a dependent, please enter the name of your parent or guardian here. (Optional)
* **Parent Contact Phone** (`parent_contact_phone`): This is the phone number of the parent or guardian (if applicable).
	+ If you are a dependent, please enter the phone number of your parent or guardian here. (Optional)
* **Parent Email Address** (`parent_email`): This is the email address of the parent or guardian (if applicable).
	+ If you are a dependent, please enter the email address of your parent or guardian here. (Optional)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): This is the name of the student's emergency contact.
	+ Please enter the name of the student's emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): This is the phone number of the student's emergency contact.
	+ Please enter the phone number of the student's emergency contact.
* **On-Campus Housing Requested** (`housing_requested`): This is whether the student has requested on-campus housing.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Yes, No.
* **Meal Plan Selection** (`meal_plan`): This is the meal plan the student has selected.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Unlimited Meal Plan, Standard Meal Plan, Limited Meal Plan, No Meal Plan.
* **Accept Offered Financial Aid Package** (`financial_aid_acceptance`): This is whether the student has accepted the offered financial aid package.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Yes, No, Pending Decision.
* **I agree to the enrollment terms and conditions** (`enrollment_agreement`): This is whether the student agrees to the enrollment terms and conditions.
	+ Please select one of the available options: Yes, No.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the college's admissions office.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate.
