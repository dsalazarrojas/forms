# Family Service Center Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register themselves and their family members with the Family Service Center. It collects personal data, family details, and emergency contact information. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure proper registration.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for all fields.
2. Select the relevant services and family members that apply to you.
3. Ensure you provide a valid emergency contact with the required information.
4. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Services Selected** (`services_selected`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the services you are interested in or are currently using.
* **Family Members** (`family_members`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select family members that apply to you.
* **Service Center** (`service_center`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the service center where you would like to receive services.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone Number** (`emergency_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Service Center** (`emergency_service_center`, text, required/optional): Enter the emergency contact's service center.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required/optional): Sign and date the form to confirm your information.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, required/optional): Upload any relevant documents that support your registration.
* **Upload Attachments** (`upload_attachments`, note, required/optional): Upload any attachments that support your registration.
* **Upload Signature** (`upload_signature`, text, required/optional): Upload a scanned or digital signature.
* **Upload Certificates** (`upload_certificates`, text, required/optional): Upload any relevant certificates that support your registration.
* **Upload IDs** (`upload_ids`, text, required/optional): Upload any identification documents.
* **Upload Other Documents** (`upload_other_documents`, text, required/optional): Upload any other relevant documents.
* **Upload Other Attachments** (`upload_other_attachments`, text, required/optional): Upload any other attachments.
* **Upload Other Certificates** (`upload_other_certificates`, text, required/optional): Upload any other relevant certificates.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Review and submit the completed form.
