# visitor_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Visitor Check Out Form" is designed to gather essential information from visitors during their stay. It helps us understand their feedback, contact details, and other relevant data for future communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your general information in the "General Info" section.
2. Provide your contact details in the "Contact Info" section.
3. Share your feedback in the "Feedback" section.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6. Select the date of your visit in the "Date Of Visit" field.
7. Select the time of your visit in the "Time Of Visit" field.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Info (ID: 1)
* **General Info**: This field is for any additional information you would like to share about your visit.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

### Contact Info (ID: 2)
* **Contact Info**: This field is for your name and contact details.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

### Feedback (ID: 3)
* **Feedback**: Share your thoughts and opinions about your visit.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

### Email (ID: 4)
* **Email**: Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Type**: email
* **Required**: No

### Phone Number (ID: 5)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

### Date Of Visit (ID: 6)
* **Date Of Visit**: Select the date when you visited.
* **Type**: date
* **Required**: No

### Time Of Visit (ID: 7)
* **Time Of Visit**: Select the time when you visited.
* **Type**: time
* **Required**: No

### Comments (ID: 8)
* **Comments**: Share any additional comments or thoughts about your visit.
* **Type**: text
* **Required**: No

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately to ensure correct processing of your submission.
* If you are unable to complete any of the fields, please contact us and we will assist you.
