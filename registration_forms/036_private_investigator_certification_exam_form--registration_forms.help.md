# private_investigator_certification_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and certify private investigators for their certification exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "candidate_info" field with your name, address, and any other relevant information.
2. Select your certification level from the "certification_level" dropdown menu.
3. Choose your exam date, time, and location in the "exam_date", "exam_time", and "exam_location" fields.
4. Enter your contact person's name, phone number, and email in the "contact_person", "phone", and "email" fields.
5. Upload any supporting documents in the "upload_documents" field.
6. Click the "submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate Info (candidate_info)**: This field is used to enter your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Certification Level (certification_level)**: This field is used to select your certification level. Please choose from the available options: Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Exam Date (exam_date)**: This field is used to choose your exam date. Please enter the date of your exam in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Exam Time (exam_time)**: This field is used to choose your exam time. Please choose from the available options or enter a custom time.
* **Exam Location (exam_location)**: This field is used to enter your exam location. Please enter the address or name of the location where you will take the exam.
* **Contact Person (contact_person)**: This field is used to enter your contact person's name. This is the person who will be in charge of coordinating your exam.
* **Phone (phone)**: This field is used to enter your contact person's phone number. Please enter a valid phone number.
* **Email (email)**: This field is used to enter your contact person's email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Upload Documents (upload_documents)**: This field is used to upload any supporting documents required for your exam.
* **Submit (submit)**: This field is used to submit your application. Please review all the fields carefully before submitting.
