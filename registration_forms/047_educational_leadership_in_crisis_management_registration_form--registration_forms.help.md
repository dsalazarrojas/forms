# Educational Leadership In Crisis Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from users to register for a training or course on educational leadership in crisis management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select the type of training or course you are interested in from the Training type dropdown menu.
6. Select your role within the organization from the Role dropdown menu.
7. Select your department from the Department dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date you are available to attend the training or course.
9. Enter the time you are available to attend the training or course.
10. Add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field.
11. Click the Submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Training type**: Select one of the following options:
	+ Crisis Management: Select this if you are interested in managing crises.
	+ Crisis Intervention: Select this if you are interested in intervening in crises.
	+ Leadership Development: Select this if you are interested in developing leadership skills.
* **Role**: Select all that apply:
	+ CEO/President: If you are the head of the organization.
	+ Director: If you are a director within the organization.
	+ Vice-President: If you are the vice-president of the organization.
	+ Manager: If you are a manager within the organization.
* **Department**: Select all that apply:
	+ Academic: If you are part of the academic department.
	+ Business: If you are part of the business department.
	+ Operations: If you are part of the operations department.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are available to attend the training or course.
* **Time**: Enter the time you are available to attend the training or course.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Submission**: This field is for internal purposes only, please do not enter anything in this field.
