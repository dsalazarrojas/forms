# Citrus Industry Meeting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for attendees of the Citrus Industry Meeting. This information will be used to facilitate event planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you about the event.
3. Provide your company or organization name to enable us to send you updates and information about the meeting.
4. Select your job title to help us understand your role in the industry.
5. Enter your business address to help us plan logistics for the meeting.
6. Choose your industry segment and company size to help us tailor the event to your needs.
7. Select the date you plan to attend the meeting.
8. Choose how you plan to attend the meeting.
9. Select the session tracks you are interested in, such as citrus production, pest management, or market trends.
10. Select the networking events you are interested in, such as the welcome reception or poster session.
11. Choose your meal preference for the event.
12. If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them.
13. Indicate your interest in the exhibition booth.
14. Indicate your interest in sponsorship opportunities.
15. If you are interested in presenting, choose how you would like to present.
16. If you are interested in presenting, provide a proposed topic.
17. Indicate if you need accommodation recommendations.
18. Indicate if you need transportation information.
19. Choose how you plan to pay for the event.
20. If you have any special requests or accommodations, please specify them.
21. Leave any additional comments about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address to receive updates and information about the meeting.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number to enable us to contact you.
* **Company or Organization**: Enter your company or organization name to help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title to help us understand your role in the industry.
* **Business Address**: Enter your business address to enable us to plan logistics for the meeting.
* **Industry Segment**: Choose the industry segment you belong to (Grower, Packer, Processor, etc.).
* **Company Size**: Choose the size of your company (1-10, 11-50, etc.).
* **Meeting Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the meeting.
* **Registration Type**: Choose how you plan to attend the meeting (Full conference, single day, virtual, etc.).
* **Session Tracks of Interest**: Select all the session tracks you are interested in, such as citrus production, pest management, or market trends.
* **Networking Events**: Select all the networking events you are interested in, such as the welcome reception or poster session.
* **Meal Preferences**: Choose your meal preference (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them.
* **Interested in Exhibition Booth**: Indicate if you are interested in the exhibition booth.
* **Interested in Sponsorship Opportunities**: Indicate if you are interested in sponsorship opportunities.
* **Interested in Presenting**: If you are interested in presenting, choose how you would like to present.
* **Proposed Presentation Topic**: If you are interested in presenting, provide a proposed topic.
* **Need Accommodation Recommendations**: Indicate if you need accommodation recommendations.
* **Need Transportation Information**: Indicate if you need transportation information.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you plan to pay for the event.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations**: If you have any special requests or accommodations, please specify them.
* **Any Additional Comments**: Leave any additional comments about your registration.
