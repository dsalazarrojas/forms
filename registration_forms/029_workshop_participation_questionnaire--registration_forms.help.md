# Workshop Participation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from workshop participants about their expectations and experiences during the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **General Information** (page 1) with a brief introduction or any relevant details about yourself.
2. Enter your **Full Name** (page 2) as it appears on your official identification or business card.
3. Enter your **Contact Number** (page 3) if you have any questions or need further assistance.
4. Select your **Workshop Participation** (page 4) as 'Yes' or 'No'.
5. Choose your **Expectation Level** (page 5) from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
6. Choose your **Expectation Level: Yes/No** (page 6) as 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. Select your **Participation Level** (page 7) as High, Medium, or Low.
8. Select your **Expectation Level: Yes/No** (page 8) as 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. Select your **Satisfaction Level** (page 9) from the options: Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
10. Finally, choose your **Future Participation** (page 10) as 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (page 1)**: A brief introduction or any relevant details about yourself.
* **Full Name (page 2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification or business card.
* **Contact Number (page 3)**: Enter your phone number or email address if you need any further assistance.
* **Workshop Participation (page 4)**: Select 'Yes' if you attended the workshop or 'No' if you did not.
* **Workshop Expectation (page 5)**: Select the level of expectation you had before the workshop from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Workshop Expectation: Yes/No (page 6)**: Select 'Yes' if you expected to participate in the workshop or 'No' if you did not.
* **Workshop Participation Level (page 7)**: Select the level of participation you experienced during the workshop as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Workshop Expectation: Yes/No (page 8)**: Select 'Yes' if you expect to participate in future workshops or 'No' if you do not.
* **Workshop Satisfaction (page 9)**: Select the level of satisfaction you experienced during the workshop from Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor.
* **Workshop Future Participation (page 10)**: Select 'Yes' if you plan to participate in future workshops or 'No' if you do not.

Note: The required fields are General Information, Full Name, and Contact Number.
