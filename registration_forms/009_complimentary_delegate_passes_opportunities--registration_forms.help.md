# Complimentary Delegate Passes Opportunities - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations requesting complimentary delegate passes for an event. Please fill it out accurately to increase your chances of a successful request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as "true") as thoroughly as possible.
2. Select "true" for the "Marketing Communications Consent" and "Terms and Conditions" fields to allow event organizers to contact you and send you relevant information.
3. Choose your answers carefully, especially for "Industry", "Event Name", "Number of Passes Requested", and "Pass Type", as they may impact the type of passes you are eligible for.
4. If you have any additional information that supports your request, use the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Enter your complete name as it should appear on your delegate pass.
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Your current position within your organization or company.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Your work email address for pass delivery.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Your best contact number.
- **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select your primary industry for a more accurate pass allocation.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, select_one, required): Select the event you are requesting passes for.
- **Number of Passes Requested** (`number_of_passes_requested`, select_one, required): Choose how many passes you need.
- **Pass Type** (`pass_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of delegate pass that suits your needs.
- **Eligibility Criteria Met** (`eligibility_criteria`, select_multiple, required): Select all the criteria that apply to your request.
- **Reason for Request** (`reason_for_request`, text, required): Explain why you are requesting complimentary passes.
- **Expected Benefits** (`expected_benefits`, text, optional): Describe the benefits you expect to gain from attending the event.
- **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select the source that led you to this event.
- **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_one, optional): If you have specific dietary needs, select the relevant option.
- **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, describe them here.
- **Marketing Communications Consent** (`marketing_consent`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, keep me informed" to receive marketing communications from event organizers.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Select "I agree" to accept the terms and conditions for complimentary passes.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that supports your request.
