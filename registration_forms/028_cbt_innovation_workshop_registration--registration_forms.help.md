# CBT Innovation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The CBT Innovation Workshop Registration form is used to collect information from attendees for the CBT Innovation Workshop. This form is intended to gather essential details to ensure a smooth and successful workshop experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Complete the form" button to begin.
2. Fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3. Select the correct options for each field as per the provided options.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`attendee_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Professional Title** (`professional_title_cbt`, select_one, required): Select your professional title from the provided options (e.g., Licensed Therapist, Psychologist, etc.).
* **Professional License Number** (`license_number_cbt`, text, required): Enter your professional license number for CEU credit verification.
* **Registration Email** (`attendee_email_cbt`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Select Your Session Date** (`workshop_session_selection`, select_one, required): Choose the session date you would like to attend from the provided options.
* **How will you be attending?** (`attending_method_cbt`, select_one, required): Select how you plan to attend the workshop (In-Person or Virtual).
* **Level of previous CBT training** (`previous_cbt_training`, select_one, required): Choose your level of previous CBT training (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced/Certified).
* **Dietary Requirements (In-person only)** (`dietary_requirements_cbt`, select_one, optional): If attending in-person, select any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **What are your primary learning goals for this workshop?** (`workshop_learning_goals`, text, required): Describe your primary learning goals for this workshop.
* **How did you hear about this workshop?** (`referral_source_cbt`, select_one, optional): Select how you heard about this workshop.
* **Any specific accessibility needs?** (`accessibility_needs_cbt`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them here.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_option_cbt`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Invoice, or Scholarship Request).
* **I consent to being photographed for educational purposes** (`photo_release_consent`, select_one, required): Select whether you consent to being photographed for educational purposes.
* **I agree to the 48-hour cancellation policy** (`cancellation_policy_ack`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the 48-hour cancellation policy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any concerns or questions, contact the workshop organizers for support.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors or omissions.
