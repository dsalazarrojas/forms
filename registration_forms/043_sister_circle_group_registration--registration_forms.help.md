# Sister Circle Group Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sister Circle Group Registration form is designed to collect information about group members and event details for the Sister Circle program. This form is used to register members for group events and to gather essential information about the group and its activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Member Name, Member Email, and Member Phone in the respective fields.
2. If applicable, select your Member Interests and Group Type (e.g., Yes or No) to indicate your involvement and type of group.
3. Enter your Member Address, Member Notes (if any), and Event Details (Event Start Date, Event End Date, Event Location, Event Time, and Event Registration Limit).
4. If your group has an organizer, provide their name and type in the respective fields.
5. Specify the payment method and any additional fees associated with the event.
6. Review and submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Name
• **Member Name (text)**: Enter your name to identify yourself as a member of the Sister Circle group.

### Member Email
• **Member Email (email)**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about group events and updates.

### Member Phone
• **Member Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number to enable us to reach you quickly and efficiently.

### Member Address
• **Member Address (text)**: Provide your address to help us understand your location and facilitate group activities.

### Member Notes
• **Member Notes (note)**: Add any additional comments or notes about yourself, if applicable.

### Member Interests
• **Member Interests (select_one)**: Indicate your interests by selecting Yes or No to help us tailor group events to your needs.

### Select Member Interests
• **Select Member Interests (select_multiple)**: Choose multiple interests to express your diverse involvement in the group.

### Event Organizer
• **Event Organizer (text)**: If you're part of a group with an organizer, provide their name and type.

### Event Start Date
• **Event Start Date (date)**: Enter the start date of the event.

### Event End Date
• **Event End Date (date)**: Enter the end date of the event.

### Event Location
• **Event Location (text)**: Provide the location of the event.

### Event Time
• **Event Time (time)**: Enter the event time.

### Member Type
• **Member Type (select_one)**: Select Yes or No to indicate your member type.

### Group Type
• **Group Type (select_one)**: Select Yes or No to indicate your group type.

### Group Size
• **Group Size (select_one)**: Select Yes or No to indicate your group size.

### Payment Method
• **Payment Method (select_one)**: Select Yes or No to indicate your payment method.

### Other Payment Method
• **Other (please specify) (text)**: If payment method is "Yes", specify the method used.

### Member Fees
• **Member Fees (number)**: Enter any fees associated with your membership in the group.

### Event Fees
• **Event Fees (number)**: Enter any fees associated with the event.

### Event Registration Limit
• **Event Registration Limit (number)**: Enter the limit for event registration.

### Event Capacity
• **Event Capacity (number)**: Enter the maximum capacity for the event.

### Event Early Bird Date
• **Event Early Bird Date (date)**: Enter the early bird date for the event.

### Event Early Bird Date Note
• **Event Early Bird Date Note (note)**: Add any notes about the early bird date.

### Group Organizer
• **Group Organizer (text)**: If you're part of a group with an organizer, provide their name and type.

### Group Organizer Name
• **Group Organizer Name (text)**: Enter the name of the group organizer.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid any discrepancies.
- Ensure you complete all required fields (if any) to register successfully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
