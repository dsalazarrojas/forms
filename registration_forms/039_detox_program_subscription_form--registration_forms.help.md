# Detox Program Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Detox Program Subscription Form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to subscribe to a detox program. This form will help us gather essential details from you, which will aid us in providing a smooth and personalized experience for our detox program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Detox Program Subscription Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your given name, which is the name you are commonly known by.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your surname, which is the name of your family or family background.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the standard format (MM/DD/YYYY) to help us verify your identity.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your active email address where we can contact you regarding your subscription and other program details. 

## Tips
- Make sure to use a valid email address to receive important updates and notifications from us.
- Use the correct date format (MM/DD/YYYY) for your date of birth field.
- If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
