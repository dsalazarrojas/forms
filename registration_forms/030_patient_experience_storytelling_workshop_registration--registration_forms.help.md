# Patient Experience Storytelling Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register patients for the Patient Experience Storytelling Workshop. Please complete the form to provide your information and preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the designated field.
3. Enter your **Phone** number (optional).
4. Select the **Workshop Date** you plan to attend.
5. Choose the **Workshop Time** you prefer.
6. Provide a brief description of the **Workshop Purpose** (optional).
7. Select your preferred **Format** for the workshop (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
8. Choose any additional consents (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) for the workshop.
9. Enter any **Additional Comments** you may have (optional).
10. If you have a specific purpose for attending the workshop, please describe it in the **Purpose of Workshop** field (optional).
11. Select the **Type of Workshop** you are interested in (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for the workshop.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for workshop updates and information.
* **Phone**: This field is optional, but if you provide your phone number, we can contact you with workshop updates and reminders.
* **Workshop Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop. This will help us schedule the workshop accordingly.
* **Workshop Time**: Choose the time slot you prefer for the workshop. Please note that some workshops may have limited time slots available.
* **Workshop Purpose**: This field is optional, but if you have a specific reason for attending the workshop, please describe it briefly.
* **Preferred Format**: Select how you would like to experience the workshop, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Consent**: Select any consents you agree to for the workshop, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional information or requests, please enter them in this field.
* **Purpose of Workshop**: If you have a specific purpose or goal for attending the workshop, please describe it briefly.
* **Type of Workshop**: Select the type of workshop you are interested in, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
