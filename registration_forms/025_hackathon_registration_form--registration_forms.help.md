# Hackathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register themselves and their teams for a hackathon. It collects information about their skills, experience, and logistical needs to ensure a smooth and productive event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant Details** page with your name, email, and other relevant contact information.
2. Move on to the **Team Details** page and list all team members, including their names, emails, and roles within the team.
3. In the **Skills and Experience** page, list all relevant skills and experience you want to share with the organizers. This will help them understand your strengths and allocate you to the best projects.
4. Finally, on the **Logistical Needs** page, mention any specific requirements you may have, such as accommodations or equipment needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**:
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be referred to during the hackathon.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address for communication and follow-up.
	+ **[Other fields]**: Please fill in any other relevant contact information you would like to share.
* **Team Details**:
	+ **Team Member 1 Name**: Enter the name of the first team member.
	+ **Team Member 1 Email**: Enter the email address of the first team member.
	+ **Team Member 1 Role**: Enter the role of the first team member within the team.
	+ **[Repeat for other team members]**
* **Skills and Experience**:
	+ List all relevant skills and experience you have in areas like programming, design, or project management.
* **Logistical Needs**:
	+ Mention any special requirements you may have, such as accommodations or equipment needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, do not hesitate to mention them in the **Logistical Needs** section.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
