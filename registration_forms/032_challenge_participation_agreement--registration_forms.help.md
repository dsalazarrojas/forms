# Challenge Participation Agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Challenge Participation Agreement form is a mandatory document that requires participants to provide their personal details, consent for media release, and agreement to follow rules and codes of conduct.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
2. Read the hints provided for each field for guidance.
3. Ask for clarification if you are unsure about any field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Agreement Date**: The date on which you are signing this agreement. (Required)
	+ This is the date on which you are agreeing to participate in the challenge.
* **Participant Name**: Your full legal name. (Required)
	+ This is your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for communications. (Required)
	+ This is the email address that the organizers will use to contact you.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number. (Required)
	+ This is your phone number that the organizers will use to contact you in case of emergencies.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth. (Required)
	+ This is your date of birth as per your identification documents.
* **Mailing Address**: Your full mailing address. (Required)
	+ This is your address where you can be contacted or met in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: The full name of your emergency contact. (Required)
	+ This is the name of the person who will be contacted in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of your emergency contact. (Required)
	+ This is the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Challenge Name**: The name of the challenge you are participating in. (Required)
	+ This is the name of the challenge as per the challenge details.
* **Challenge Dates**: The start and end dates of the challenge. (Required)
	+ These are the dates on which the challenge will take place.
* **Team Name**: The name of your team if you are participating in a team. (Optional)
	+ This is the name of your team if you are part of a team.
* **Team Members**: List of team members if you are participating in a team. (Optional)
	+ This is a list of all team members who are participating in the challenge.
* **Medical Conditions**: Do you have any medical conditions? (Required)
	+ If you have any medical conditions, please answer "Yes - Will disclose" to provide more information.
* **Medical Details**: Describe any relevant medical conditions. (Optional)
	+ If you have any medical conditions, please provide a brief description here.
* **Physical Fitness Level**: How would you rate your fitness level? (Required)
	+ Please select your fitness level from the options provided.
* **Risk Acknowledgment**: Do you understand the risks associated with this challenge? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I understand" to confirm that you have read and understood the challenge terms.
* **Liability Waiver**: Do you waive liability for injuries or damages? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I waive" to confirm that you understand the challenge terms.
* **Medical Clearance**: Have you received medical clearance to participate? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - Cleared by physician" to confirm that you have received medical clearance.
* **Rules Agreement**: Do you agree to follow all challenge rules? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I agree" to confirm that you will follow the challenge rules.
* **Code of Conduct**: Do you agree to abide by the code of conduct? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I agree" to confirm that you will abide by the code of conduct.
* **Photo Consent**: Do you consent to photos being taken during the challenge? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I consent" to confirm that you agree to photos being taken.
* **Media Release**: Do you allow your image to be used for promotional purposes? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I allow" to confirm that you agree to your image being used.
* **Data Privacy**: Do you understand how your data will be used? (Required)
	+ Please select "Yes - I understand" to confirm that you understand how your data will be used.
* **Participant Signature**: Your signature as participant. (Required)
	+ Type your full legal name as signature.
* **Signature Date**: The date of signing. (Required)
	+ Enter the date on which you are signing this agreement.
* **Witness Signature**: Witness signature if required. (Optional)
	+ This field is for the witness to sign if required.
 
## Tips

1. Read the challenge terms and conditions before filling out this form.
2. Fill out each section carefully and accurately.
3. Ask for clarification if you are unsure about any field.
4. Ensure that you have received medical clearance before participating in the challenge.
