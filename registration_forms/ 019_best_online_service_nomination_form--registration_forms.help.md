# Best Online Service Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who wish to nominate online services for recognition and consideration. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details about the service, including personal information, service details, and contact preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the personal information section with your details.
2. Choose the relevant date for your nomination.
3. Select the type of service you are nominating (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
4. Enter any additional details or information about the service.
5. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
6. If required, enter the estimated value of the service and your preferred time for scheduling.
7. Add any final comments or notes about the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name**: Enter your legal full name. This is a required field and should match your official identification.
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address for contact purposes. This will be used to reach out to you for further information or to follow up on your nomination.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including any country code if applicable.
* **Date**: Select the relevant date for your nomination. This will help us assess the service in the correct timeframe.
* **Primary selection**: Choose the type of service you are nominating (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
* **Quantity or rating**: Enter a whole number to indicate the quantity or rating of the service. If not applicable, leave this field blank.
* **Additional details**: Provide any additional information about the service that you think is relevant.
* **Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions. Select all applicable options to indicate your level of agreement.
* **Estimated value**: Enter the estimated value of the service, using decimal format if applicable. This is an optional field.
* **Preferred time**: Enter a time if scheduling is required for the service. This is an optional field.
* **Final comments or notes**: Add any final thoughts or notes about the service. This is an optional field.
