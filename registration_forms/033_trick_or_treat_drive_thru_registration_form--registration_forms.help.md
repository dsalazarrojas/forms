# Trick Or Treat Drive Thru Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Trick Or Treat Drive Thru event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the personal information section.
2. Fill out the vehicle information section.
3. Review the form for any errors before submitting.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on your Trick Or Treat Drive Thru registration.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.

### Date of Birth
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).

### Parent's Name
* **Parent's Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of a parent or guardian if under 18 years old.

### Parent's Phone
* **Parent's Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of a parent or guardian if under 18 years old.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of an emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of an emergency contact.

### Vehicle Make
* **Vehicle Make** (`vehicle_make`, `text`, required): Enter the make of your vehicle.

### Vehicle Model
* **Vehicle Model** (`vehicle_model`, `text`, required): Enter the model of your vehicle.

### Vehicle Year
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year of your vehicle.

### Vehicle License
* **Vehicle License** (`vehicle_license`, `text`, required): Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.

### Vehicle Color
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, `text`, required): Enter the color of your vehicle.

### Vehicle Registration
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct year range of your vehicle's registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact the event organizers.
