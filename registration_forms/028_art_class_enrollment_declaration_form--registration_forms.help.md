# Art Class Enrollment Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from students enrolling in art classes at our institution. It helps us understand their background, goals, and requirements, ensuring a smooth and productive learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your student information in the designated fields, making sure to fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Select the relevant options for the class you are enrolling in, including class level, type, and term/session.
3. Describe your prior art experience and learning goals in the provided text fields.
4. Choose your enrollment status and payment status from the drop-down menus.
5. Acknowledge that you understand the class expectations and requirements, as well as any potential costs for art supplies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. (text, required)
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address where you can be contacted for updates and information. (email, required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for additional communication. (text, optional)
* **Date of Birth**: If you are enrolling with us for the first time, please provide your date of birth. (date, optional)
* **Student ID Number**: If you are an existing student, please provide your student ID number. (text, optional)
* **Art Class Name**: Choose the name of the class you are enrolling in. (text, required)
* **Class Level**: Select your current skill level in art class:
	+ Beginner: You have little to no prior experience.
	+ Intermediate: You have some prior experience.
	+ Advanced: You have significant prior experience.
	+ Mixed levels: Your level of experience varies. (select_one, required)
* **Type of Art Class**: Choose the specific art class type you are enrolling in:
	+ Drawing
	+ Painting
	+ Sculpture
	+ Digital art
	+ Printmaking
	+ Mixed media
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Class Term or Session**: Select the term or session you are enrolling in. (text, required)
* **Prior Art Experience**: Describe your background in art, including any relevant education or training. (text, optional)
* **Prior Art Experience**: Select your level of prior art experience:
	+ No prior experience
	+ Some informal experience
	+ Completed other art classes
	+ Extensive art training (select_one, required)
* **Describe Your Art Background**: Briefly describe your background in art, including any notable experiences or accomplishments. (text, optional)
* **Learning Goals**: Describe your goals for this class, including what you hope to achieve or improve in art. (text, required)
* **Specific Techniques or Subjects of Interest**: List any specific techniques or subjects you are interested in exploring in this class. (text, optional)
* **Enrollment Status**: Choose your status as a student:
	+ New student
	+ Returning student (select_one, required)
* **Payment Status**: Choose your payment status:
	+ Payment completed
	+ Payment pending
	+ Financial aid requested (select_one, required)
* **Enrollment Status**: Acknowledge that you understand the expectations and requirements of the class. (select_one, required)
* **Payment Status**: Acknowledge that you understand the potential cost for art supplies. (select_one, required)

Please fill out the form carefully, and if you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
