# Student Enrollment And Payment Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a student for enrollment and payment processes. The form is used to collect student information, payment details, and other relevant data required for student registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Start by selecting the Student Info page**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter general student information.
2. **Fill out the Student Info page**: Enter the student's name and other relevant details on this page.
3. **Move on to the Student ID page**: If applicable, enter the student's ID number or any other identification number.
4. **Proceed to the Date of Birth page**: Enter the student's date of birth on this page.
5. **Enter contact information**: Enter the student's contact email and phone number on this page.
6. **Enter payment information**: Enter the payment details, payment confirmation, and student status on this page.
7. **Enter enrollment details**: Enter the student enrollment date and payment terms on this page.
8. **Enter signature and submission information**: Enter the student's signature, submission time, submission date, and submission status on this page.
9. **Submit the form**: Once all pages are filled out, click the submit button to complete the registration process.
