# Beauty Business Mentorship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a mentorship program for businesses in the beauty industry. It collects information about your business and your goals, so we can match you with the right mentor.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields. Optional fields are for additional information.
- Use the options in the select fields to choose the most accurate answer.
- For text fields, please enter a clear and concise description.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Business Name** (5, text, required): Enter your business name.
* **Business Type** (6, select_one, required): Choose the type of business you have:
	+ Salon
	+ Spa
	+ Beauty Bar
	+ Mobile Beauty Service
	+ Product Line
	+ Beauty School
	+ Other
* **Business Location** (7, text, required): Enter the city and state of your business.
* **Years in Business** (8, select_one, required): Choose how long your business has been operating:
	+ Less than 1 year
	+ 1-3 years
	+ 4-7 years
	+ 8-15 years
	+ Over 15 years
* **Annual Revenue Range** (9, select_one, required): Choose your annual revenue:
	+ Under $50,000
	+ $50,000-$100,000
	+ $100,001-$250,000
	+ $250,001-$500,000
	+ $500,001-$1,000,000
	+ Over $1,000,000
* **Number of Employees** (10, select_one, required): Choose the size of your team:
	+ Just Me
	+ 2-5 Employees
	+ 6-10 Employees
	+ 11-20 Employees
	+ Over 20 Employees
* **Mentorship Goals** (11, select_multiple, required): Choose the areas you want to focus on with mentorship:
	+ Business Growth
	+ Marketing Strategy
	+ Financial Management
	+ Team Building
	+ Product Development
	+ Brand Building
	+ Operations Efficiency
	+ Work-Life Balance
* **Biggest Business Challenges** (12, text, required): Describe the biggest challenges your business is facing.
* **Mentor Preference** (13, select_one, required): Choose the type of mentor you prefer:
	+ Industry Veteran
	+ Marketing Expert
	+ Financial Advisor
	+ Operations Specialist
	+ Brand Builder
	+ No Preference
* **Preferred Meeting Frequency** (14, select_one, required): Choose how often you'd like to meet with your mentor:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (15, select_one, required): Choose the format for meetings:
	+ In-Person
	+ Video Call
	+ Phone Call
	+ Flexible
* **Desired Program Duration** (16, select_one, required): Choose the length of the mentorship program:
	+ 3 Months
	+ 6 Months
	+ 12 Months
	+ Ongoing
* **Are You Ready to Invest in Mentorship** (17, select_one, required): Choose if you are budget ready for mentorship:
	+ True
	+ False
	+ Need More Information
* **How Did You Hear About This Program** (18, select_one, optional): Choose how you heard about the mentorship program:
	+ Website
	+ Social Media
	+ Referral
	+ Industry Event
	+ Advertisement
	+ Other
* **Referral Name** (19, text, optional): If you were referred, enter the name of the person who referred you.
* **Additional Comments** (20, text, optional): Add any other information that would be helpful for your mentorship.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Be clear and concise with your answers.
- If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask!
