# Secret Admirer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your interest in receiving a secret gift or message from someone else. Please fill out the form with your details to be part of this special program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you want to be part of the secret admirer program.
2. Enter your name as you would like to be known by the person sending the gift or message.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used to send you the gift or message.
4. Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
5. Choose your preferred delivery time.
6. Type a personal message for the person sending the gift or message.
7. If desired, provide a name for the person sending the gift or message.
8. Select your preferred delivery method (Handover, Printout, or Email).
9. Choose the color of the printout (Black and white or Color).
10. Enter the date you'd like to receive the delivery.
11. Choose the delivery time.
12. Type a personal message for the person receiving the gift or message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form data** (Select Multiple): 
  - Choose whether you're interested in being part of the secret admirer program.
* **Name** (Text):
  - Enter your name as you'd like to be known.
* **Email** (Email):
  - Enter your email address to receive the gift or message.
* **Phone Number** (Text):
  - Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Delivery Time** (Time):
  - Choose a time when you'd like to receive the delivery.
* **Message** (Text):
  - Type a personal message for the person sending the gift or message.
* **Name for delivery** (Text):
  - Provide the name of the person sending the gift or message (if desired).
* **Delivery method** (Select One):
  - Choose how you'd like to receive the delivery (Handover, Printout, or Email).
* **Printout Color** (Select One):
  - Choose the color of the printout (Black and white or Color).
* **Delivery date** (Date):
  - Enter the date you'd like to receive the delivery.
* **Delivery time** (Time):
  - Choose the time you'd like to receive the delivery.
* **Message for delivery** (Text):
  - Type a personal message for the person receiving the gift or message.
