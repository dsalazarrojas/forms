# Spa Manager Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register spa managers for a leadership training program. The goal of this form is to collect essential information from spa managers who are interested in improving their leadership skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field. (Use the format "mm/dd/yyyy".)
5. Enter the date you plan to attend the training in the "training date" field.
6. Enter the start time for the training in the "training time" field.
7. Select the location where the training will be held.
8. Enter the name of your manager in the "manager name" field.
9. Enter the title of your manager in the "manager title" field.
10. Enter the phone number of your manager in the "spa manager phone" field.
11. (Optional) Select whether or not you are a manager of a spa location in the "location" field.
12. (Optional) Enter the location of your spa in the "spa location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required): Your name as a spa manager.
- **Email** (email, required): Your email address as a spa manager.
- **Phone** (text, required): Your phone number as a spa manager.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required): Your date of birth (in the format "mm/dd/yyyy").
- **Start Date** (date, required): The date you plan to start attending the training. 
- **Start Time** (time, required): The time you plan to start attending the training.
- **Location** (select_multiple, required): The location where the training will be held.
  * Please select either "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether or not you are a manager of a spa location.
- **Manager Name** (text, required): The name of your manager.
- **Manager Title** (text, required): The title of your manager.
- **Spa Manager Phone** (text, required): The phone number of your manager.
- **Training Date** (date, required): The date you plan to attend the training.
- **Training Time** (time, required): The time you plan to start attending the training.
- **Training Location** (text, required): The location of the training.
