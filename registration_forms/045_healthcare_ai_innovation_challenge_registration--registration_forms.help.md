# Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Healthcare AI Innovation Challenge Registration form collects information from teams participating in the challenge. It helps us evaluate each entry and ensure submissions are unique.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the team name, which should be a short description of your team.
2. Add team members by selecting one or more team members from the provided options.
3. Write a brief description of your project in the project summary field.
4. Provide your team's contact email address.
5. Enter your team's phone number.
6. Choose a name for your project.
7. Select the category your project belongs to.
8. Select the status of your project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **team_name** (text, required): Enter a short description of your team.
* **team_members** (select_multiple, optional): Select one or more team members from the options provided.
* **project_summary** (text, optional): Briefly describe your project in 100 words or less.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your team's contact email address.
* **phone** (text, optional): Enter your team's phone number.
* **project_name** (text, optional): Choose a name for your project.
* **project_category** (select_one, optional): Select a category for your project.
* **project_status** (select_one, optional): Select the status of your project.
