# transparency_audit_conference_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for conference attendees to register for the transparency audit conference. It collects details about the conference and attendee's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Provide general details about the conference in the "conference_details" field.
2. Enter attendee details in the "attendee_details" field.
3. Choose your preferred attendance mode for the conference from the options "On-site" and "Remote".
4. Select the sessions you would like to attend from the "session_preferences" field.
5. Enter your name, email, phone number, and any notes in the corresponding fields.
6. Finally, provide your address in the "attendee_address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **conference_details**: Enter a brief description of the conference you're attending.
* **attendee_details**: Enter details about yourself, such as your role or affiliation.
* **attendance_mode**: Choose how you would like to attend the conference: On-site or Remote.
* **session_preferences**: Select the sessions you would like to attend.
* **attendee_name**: Enter your name.
* **attendee_email**: Enter your email address.
* **attendee_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **attendee_notes**: Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **attendee_address**: Enter your address.
