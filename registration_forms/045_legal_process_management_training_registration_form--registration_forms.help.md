# Legal Process Management Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Legal Process Management Training course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
2. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Enter the course details in the "Course Details" section.
4. Confirm your registration in the "Confirm Registration" section.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email" section.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" section.
7. Enter the course name in the "Course Name" section.
8. Enter the course date in the "Course Date" section.
9. Enter the course time in the "Course Time" section.
10. Enter the course duration in the "Course Duration" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your personal details, such as your name and address.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required: false): Please enter your contact details, such as your email address and phone number.
* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required: false): Please enter the details of the course you are registering for.
* **Registration** (`registration`, text, required: false): Please confirm your registration for the course.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Time** (`course_time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time of the course you are registering for.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required: false): Please enter the duration of the course you are registering for.
* **Confirm Registration** (`confirm`, text, required: false): Please confirm your registration for the course.
* **Name** (`confirm_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Course Name** (`confirm_course_name`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the course you are confirming.
* **Confirm Course Date** (`confirm_course_date`, date, required: false): Please enter the date of the course you are confirming.
* **Confirm Course Time** (`confirm_course_time`, time, required: false): Please enter the time of the course you are confirming.
* **Confirm Course Duration** (`confirm_course_duration`, number, required: false): Please enter the duration of the course you are confirming.
* **Course Attendee** (`confirm_course_attendee`, text, required: false): Please enter the number of course attendees.
* **Organizer** (`confirm_organizer`, text, required: false): Please enter the name of the course organizer.
