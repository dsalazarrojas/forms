# Beta Tester Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for registering as a beta tester for an event or product. Fill out this form to provide your personal and contact information, select relevant preferences, and confirm your agreement to participate.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full name" field.
2. Enter your valid email address in the "Email address" field.
3. Enter your phone number, including the country code if outside your local region, in the "Phone number" field.
4. Select the relevant date for your registration.
5. Choose the option that best applies to you from the "Primary selection" field.
6. Provide a whole number for "Quantity or rating" if required.
7. Add any additional relevant information in the "Additional details" field if needed.
8. Select all that apply from the "Please confirm agreement" field.
9. Enter the estimated value for "Estimated value" if required.
10. Enter a preferred time for "Preferred time" if scheduling is required.
11. Add any final comments or notes in the "Final comments or notes" field if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (text, required): Enter your legal full name.
* **Email address** (email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact.
* **Phone number** (text, optional): Include country code if outside local region.
* **Date** (date, required): Select the relevant date for your registration.
* **Primary selection** (select one, required): Choose the option that best applies.
* **Quantity or rating** (number, optional): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
* **Additional details** (text, optional): Provide any additional relevant information.
* **Please confirm agreement** (select multiple, required): Select all that apply.
* **Estimated value** (number, optional): Enter the estimated value in decimal format if required.
* **Preferred time** (time, optional): Enter a preferred time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (text, optional): Provide any final comments or notes.

## Tips

* Be accurate when entering your personal and contact information.
* Ensure you select the correct date for your registration.
* Be truthful when choosing your primary selection.
* Enter accurate and whole numbers for quantity or rating.
* Read and confirm your agreement to participate carefully.
* Enter any estimated value in decimal format if required.
* Provide any final comments or notes if needed.
