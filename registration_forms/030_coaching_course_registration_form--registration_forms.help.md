# Coaching Course Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from potential course participants, enabling the course administrators to verify their eligibility and provide personalized course recommendations.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2. Choose the course you are interested in and select your level of experience with the topic.
3. Select your preferred start date for the course.
4. Choose your course format preference (online self-paced, online live classes, in-person, or hybrid).
5. Enter your time zone (optional).
6. Provide your current occupation, employer (if applicable), and highest education level achieved.
7. Describe any previous coaching experience you may have.
8. Explain why you want to take this course and what you hope to learn.
9. Indicate how you plan to apply what you learn from the course.
10. If applicable, specify any special accommodations you require.
11. Select a payment method for the course fee (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, PayPal, payment plan, or employer-sponsored).
12. Confirm your eligibility for any early bird discounts.
13. Indicate if you are registering as a group (multiple people from the same organization).
14. Choose how you heard about this course (online search, social media, referral, advertisement, email, or other).
15. If applicable, mention the name of the person who referred you to the course.
16. Agree to the terms and conditions of the course.
17. Understand the cancellation policy for the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (1): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (2): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (4): Enter your full mailing address.
* **City** (5): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (6): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (7): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (8): Enter your country.
* **Date of Birth** (9): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Course Selection** (10): Choose the course you are interested in taking.
* **Course Level** (11): Select your level of experience with the chosen course topic.
* **Preferred Start Date** (12): Select your preferred start date for the course.
* **Course Format Preference** (13): Choose your preferred course format (online self-paced, online live classes, in-person, or hybrid).
* **Time Zone** (14): Enter your time zone (optional).
* **Current Occupation** (15): Enter your current job or profession.
* **Employer Name** (16): Enter your organization's name if you are registering as part of an organization.
* **Highest Education Level** (17): Select your highest level of education achieved.
* **Previous Coaching Experience** (18): Describe any previous coaching experience you may have.
* **Why Do You Want to Take This Course?** (19): Explain your motivation for registering for this course.
* **What Do You Hope to Learn?** (20): Describe your learning objectives.
* **How Will You Apply What You Learn?** (21): Explain how you plan to apply what you learn from the course.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (22): If applicable, specify any special accommodations you require.
* **Course Fee** (23): Select your expected course fee (under $500, $500-$1000, $1001-$2500, $2501-$5000, over $5000).
* **Payment Method** (24): Select your payment method (credit card, debit card, bank transfer, PayPal, payment plan, employer-sponsored).
* **Early Bird Discount Eligibility** (25): Confirm if you are eligible for any early bird discounts.
* **Registering as a Group** (26): Indicate if you are registering as a group (multiple people from the same organization).
* **How Did You Hear About This Course?** (27): Choose how you heard about this course (online search, social media, referral, advertisement, email, or other).
* **Referral Name** (28): If applicable, mention the name of the person who referred you to this course.
* **Agree to Terms and Conditions** (29): Confirm that you agree to the course's terms and conditions.
* **Understand Cancellation Policy** (30): Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy for this course.
* **Registration Date** (31): The current date is automatically populated.

Please ensure you have read and understood the course's terms and conditions before submitting this form.
