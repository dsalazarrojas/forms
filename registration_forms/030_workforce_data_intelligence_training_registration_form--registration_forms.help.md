# Workforce Data Intelligence Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register employees for the Workforce Data Intelligence training program. It gathers relevant information to keep track of participants and ensure efficient organization of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the First Name and Last Name fields. These are required fields and will serve as your personal identifiers in the training program.
2. Enter your Email address. This will be used for communication with the training team and as a point of contact for any updates or changes.
3. If you are affiliated with a Company, please provide it. This is an optional field but helps in organizing the training sessions according to company needs.
4. Choose whether you have a Department or not. Like the Company field, this is optional but aids in structuring the training sessions.
5. Select your Job Title. This field is optional but helps in categorizing participants according to their job roles.
6. Enter your Job Title Other if you have a job title that is not listed in the options provided.
7. Provide a description of your Job Title if necessary.
8. Select the Training Date for which you will be attending the training session. This is a required field to ensure timely organization of the sessions.
9. Indicate the Training Time, which is not required but helps in scheduling the sessions accordingly.
10. Enter the Training Location where the session will be held. This field is crucial for logistical arrangements.
11. Select the Training Duration, which is also a required field for scheduling purposes.
12. Select whether you have a preferred Training Language. This is an optional field for accommodating different language needs.
13. If your preferred language is not listed, enter it in the "Training Language Other" field.
14. Enter the Registration Date, which is not required but helpful for tracking registrations.
15. Select your Registration Status as either Active or Inactive. This field is crucial for tracking participant status.
16. Provide any additional Registration Notes if necessary.
17. If you are affiliated with a Company, please provide it. This is an optional field but aids in organizing the training sessions according to company needs.
18. If you are part of a Department, please select it. This is optional but helps in structuring the training sessions.
19. Select the Training Status as Active or Inactive. This field is required for accurate tracking of participant status.
20. Enter any description if your training status is not listed in the options provided.
21. Confirm that you will be attending the training session by selecting "Yes" or "No". This field is required to confirm participation.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is your personal name, and it's essential to fill it in for correct identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is your surname, and it's required for accurate identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your official email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): This field is for listing your affiliated company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): This field is for selecting your department, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): This field is for listing your job title for categorization purposes.
* **Job Title Other** (`job_title_other`, text, optional): If your job title is not listed, fill this field for accurate representation.
* **Job Title Description** (`job_title_description`, text, optional): This field is for providing a description of your job title, if necessary.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): This field is for scheduling the training sessions according to the selected date.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): This field is for further structuring the sessions according to the selected time.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): This is the venue where the training session will be held.
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, number, required): This is the length of the training session, in hours.
* **Training Language** (`training_language`, select_multiple, required): This field allows you to select a preferred training language.
* **Training Language Other** (`training_language_other`, text, optional): If your preferred language is not listed, fill this field for accurate representation.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): This field is for tracking registration dates but is not required.
* **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, select_one, required): This field is for marking your registration status as either Active or Inactive.
* **Registration Notes** (`registration_notes`, note, optional): This field is for adding any additional comments or notes regarding your registration.

## Tips
* Ensure that you fill in all required fields accurately.
* Double-check the training session schedule to avoid conflicts.
* If you need any special accommodations for the training session, please mention them in the "Registration Notes" field.
