# Accounting Ledger Review Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Accounting Ledger Review Registration Form is used to register a request for a ledger review. The form is designed to collect necessary information about the request, including the requester's details, ledger details, and review preferences. The purpose of the form is to ensure that all required information is provided accurately and consistently, making it easier for the reviewer to process the request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1.  Enter the **Requester Name** in the designated field. This is the person or organization requesting the ledger review.
2.  Enter the **Requester Email** in the designated field. This is the contact email address for follow-up purposes.
3.  Enter the **Company Name** in the designated field. This is the name of the company whose ledger will be reviewed.
4.  Select the **Ledger Period** from the given options. This is the specific period of the ledger to be reviewed (e.g., Jan 2025 to Mar 2025).
5.  Select the **Ledger Type** from the given options. This indicates the type of ledger to be reviewed (e.g., General ledger, Sales ledger, etc.).
6.  Select the **Priority** for the review from the given options. This reflects the urgency of the review (e.g., Routine, Urgent, Statutory deadline).
7.  Indicate if **Documents are Attached** with the corresponding option (True or False).
8.  Enter the **Reviewer Preferences** in the designated field, if any. This is for preferred reviewers or teams.
9.  If desired, enter the **Expected Completion Date** in the designated format. This is the target date for completing the review.
10. Indicate if the review is **Confidential** with the corresponding option (True or False).
11. Enter any **Special Instructions** for handling or focus areas in the designated field.
12. Finally, enter your **Submitter Signature** with your full name as an electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Requester Name** (id: 2, text, required): Enter the name of the person requesting the ledger review.
*   **Requester email** (id: 3, email, required): Enter the contact email address for follow-up purposes.
*   **Company name** (id: 4, text, required): Enter the name of the company whose ledger will be reviewed.
*   **Ledger period** (id: 5, text, required): Select the specific period of the ledger to be reviewed.
*   **Ledger type** (id: 6, select_one, required): Choose the type of ledger to be reviewed (e.g., General ledger, Sales ledger, etc.).
*   **Priority** (id: 7, select_one, required): Select the urgency of the review (e.g., Routine, Urgent, Statutory deadline).
*   **Documents attached** (id: 8, select_one, optional): Indicate if supporting documents are attached.
*   **Reviewer preferences** (id: 9, text, optional): Enter any preferred reviewer or team for the review.
*   **Expected completion date** (id: 10, date, optional): Enter the target date for completing the review.
*   **Confidentiality flag** (id: 11, select_one, optional): Indicate if the review is confidential.
*   **Special instructions** (id: 12, text, optional): Enter any special handling or review focus areas.
*   **Submitter signature** (id: 13, text, required): Enter your full name as an electronic signature.
