# Childcare Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for early childhood educators to register for a childcare workshop on professional development. The form captures user information, preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your full name in the "Registrant Full Name" field.
2.  Enter your organization or school name in the "Organization or School Name" field.
3.  Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field, which will receive confirmation.
4.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone Number" field.
5.  Select your preferred workshop session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening) from the "Select Your Workshop Session" dropdown menu.
6.  Choose your primary purpose for attending the workshop (Professional Development Credits, Personal Interest, New Skill Acquisition, or Networking) from the "What is your primary purpose for attending this workshop?" dropdown menu.
7.  If you have specific expectations or goals for the workshop, describe them in the "What are your specific expectations or goals for this workshop?" text field.
8.  Indicate your current professional role (Lead Teacher, Assistant Teacher, Administrator, Parent, or Student) from the "Current Professional Role" dropdown menu.
9.  Indicate your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Invoice Organization, or Check by Mail) from the "Preferred Payment Method" dropdown menu.
10.  If you have dietary requirements for refreshments, select the relevant options from the "Dietary Requirements for Refreshments" dropdown menu.
11.  If you have any additional comments or questions, enter them in the "Additional Comments or Questions" text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Registrant Full Name** (required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
*   **Organization or School Name** (optional): Enter your organization or school name, if applicable.
*   **Contact Email** (required): Enter your contact email address to receive confirmation of your registration.
*   **Contact Phone Number** (required): Enter your contact phone number for follow-up communication.
*   **Select Your Workshop Session** (required): Choose your preferred workshop session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
*   **What is your primary purpose for attending this workshop?** (required): Indicate why you are attending the workshop (Professional Development Credits, Personal Interest, New Skill Acquisition, or Networking).
*   **What are your specific expectations or goals for this workshop?** (optional): Describe your specific expectations or goals for the workshop.
*   **Current Professional Role** (required): Indicate your current professional role (Lead Teacher, Assistant Teacher, Administrator, Parent, or Student).
*   **Preferred Payment Method** (required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Invoice Organization, or Check by Mail).
*   **Dietary Requirements for Refreshments** (optional): Select any dietary requirements you have for refreshments (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergy, or None).
*   **Additional Comments or Questions** (optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the workshop.
