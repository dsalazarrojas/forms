# Prayer And Fasting Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for individuals and groups to participate in prayer and fasting activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information on the "Contact Info" page.
2. On the "Prayer Initiative" page, describe your intentions for prayer and fasting.
3. On the "Fasting Info" page, enter any additional information about your fasting plans.
4. On the "Fasting Type" page, select the type of fasting you're interested in participating in (Prayer, Fasting, or Other).
5. On the "Frequency" page, choose how often you'd like to participate in fasting (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
6. On the "Fasting Duration" page, enter the length of time for which you'd like to fast.
7. On the "Fasting Start Time" page, enter the start time of your fasting period.
8. On the "Fasting End Time" page, enter the end time of your fasting period.
9. On the "Fasting Participants" page, select the type of participants you'd like to join you (Self, Group, or Church).
10. On the "Fasting Location" page, choose where you'd like to fast (Home, Church, or Public Place).
11. On the "Fasting Leaders" page, select the type of leader you'd like to join (Self, Other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, Required): Enter your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Prayer Initiative** (`prayer_initiative`, `text`, Required): Describe your intentions for prayer and fasting.
* **Fasting Info** (`fasting_info`, `text`, Required): Enter any additional information about your fasting plans.
* **Fasting Type** (`fasting_type`, `select_one`, Required): Choose the type of fasting you're interested in (Prayer, Fasting, or Other).
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select how often you'd like to participate in fasting (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Fasting Duration** (`fasting_duration`, `number`, Required): Enter the length of time for which you'd like to fast.
* **Fasting Start Time** (`fasting_start_time`, `time`, Required): Enter the start time of your fasting period.
* **Fasting End Time** (`fasting_end_time`, `time`, Required): Enter the end time of your fasting period.
* **Fasting Participants** (`fasting_participants`, `select_multiple`, Required): Choose the type of participants you'd like to join you (Self, Group, or Church).
* **Fasting Location** (`fasting_location`, `select_multiple`, Required): Choose where you'd like to fast (Home, Church, or Public Place).
* **Fasting Leaders** (`fasting_leaders`, `select_multiple`, Required): Select the type of leader you'd like to join (Self, Other).
