# pantomime_attendance_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendance for a pantomime performance. It captures essential information about the attendees, such as their name, contact details, seat selection, and time slot preferences. The form also includes a field for taking notes and submitting the registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields: **Name**, **Email**, and **Pantomime ID**.
2. Optionally, provide your **Phone** number and **Seat Selection** preferences.
3. Choose a specific **Seat Reservation Date** for your selected seat.
4. Select a preferred **Time Slot** for your attendance.
5. Add any additional notes or comments in the **Note** field.
6. Once you have filled out all the required and optional fields, click the **Submit** button to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like to be listed for the event.
* **Email**: Provide your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Optional field for you to enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Seat Selection**: Choose your preferred seat for the event. Select all that apply.
* **Seat Reservation Date**: Select a date for your seat reservation, if you have already chosen a seat.
* **Time Slot**: Select a time slot for your attendance.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Submit**: Click this button to finalize your registration and submit your data.
* **Pantomime ID**: Enter a unique Pantomime ID for your registration, if required.
* **Event ID**: Enter a unique Event ID, if required.
* **Submit**: This button will save your registration data.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful submission.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers.
* Review your submission before clicking the **Submit** button.
