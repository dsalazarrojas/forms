# thanksgiving_food_basket_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals willing to participate in the Thanksgiving event, allowing the organizers to manage the event effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with your name and contact information (fields 2-5).
2. Choose how you would like to participate in the event by selecting the options in the "registration_type" field (field 9).
3. Select one option from the "select_one" field (field 10).
4. Select multiple options from the "select_multiple_2" field (field 11).
5. Fill out any numerical fields that apply to you, such as "integer_field" and "decimal_field" (fields 12-13).
6. If you have any notes or comments, you can fill out the "note" field (field 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (field 1): This is the main form where you can input your information.
* **Name** (field 2): Enter your full name.
* **Address** (field 3): Enter your address for contact and delivery purposes.
* **Email** (field 4): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone** (field 5): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Date** (field 6): Enter the date you would like to participate in the event.
* **Time** (field 7): Enter the time you would like to participate in the event.
* **Note** (field 8): If you have any comments or notes, you can enter them here.
* **Registration Type** (field 9): Choose how you would like to participate in the event, such as "option 1" or "option 2".
* **Select One** (field 10): Choose one option from the list.
* **Select Multiple 2** (field 11): Choose multiple options from the list.
* **Integer Field** (field 12): Enter any numerical value if required.
* **Decimal Field** (field 13): Enter any decimal value if required.
* **Date Field** (field 14): Enter a date if required.
* **Time Field** (field 15): Enter a time if required.
* **Note Field** (field 16): Enter any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the event organizers.
- Make sure to review your selections before submitting the form.
