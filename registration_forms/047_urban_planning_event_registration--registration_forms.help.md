# Urban Planning Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering events related to urban planning, allowing users to input relevant information such as name, contact details, and registration status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address to enable communication and updates.
3. Provide your Phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select a preferred Date and Time for the event.
5. Choose your preferred Date and Time from the provided options.
6. Select your Role in the event (Organizer, Participant, or Sponsor).
7. If you are a participant, select your Participant Type (General Participant, Group Leader, Speaker, or Sponsor).
8. Enter the number of Participants you are registering.
9. Select your Registration Status (Waiting List, Registered, or Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date (Date)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Time (Time)**: Select the time of the event.
* **Note (Note)**: Leave a note to provide additional information.
* **Preferred Time (Select One)**: Choose your preferred time from 09:00, 10:00, or 11:00.
* **Preferred Date (Select Multiple)**: Select your preferred date from the provided options.
* **Role (Select One)**: Choose your role in the event (Organizer, Participant, or Sponsor).
* **Participant Type (Select Multiple)**: Select your participant type (General Participant, Group Leader, Speaker, or Sponsor).
* **Participant Number (Number)**: Enter the number of participants you are registering.
* **Registration Status (Select One)**: Select your registration status (Waiting List, Registered, or Cancelled).
