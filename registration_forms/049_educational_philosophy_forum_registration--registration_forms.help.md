# Educational Philosophy Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Philosophy Forum is designed for individuals to share, discuss, and learn about various educational concepts and philosophies. By filling out this form, you will be able to register for the forum and participate in the discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant details section (Field 1).
2. Select your interests (Field 2).
3. Confirm your consent to the codes of conduct (Field 3).
4. Enter your email (Field 4).
5. Add your phone number (Field 5), if you wish to be contacted.
6. Provide your name (Field 6).
7. Add any additional interests (Field 7).
8. Enter your organisation (Field 8).
9. Enter your job title (Field 9), if applicable.
10. Enter your institution (Field 10).
11. Specify your country (Field 11).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, title, or any other details you would like to provide.
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, select_one, required/optional): Select one or more of your interests from the options provided.
* **Consent to Codes of Conduct** (`consent_codes_of_conduct`, select_multiple, required/optional): Confirm your consent to the codes of conduct by selecting all that apply.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
* **Additional Interests** (`participant_interests_2`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional interests not listed in Field 2.
* **Participant Organisation** (`participant_organisation`, text, required/optional): Enter your organisation name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Participant Institution** (`participant_institution`, text, required/optional): Enter your institution name.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, required/optional): Enter your country.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your information is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* We will use the information provided to facilitate your participation in the Educational Philosophy Forum.
