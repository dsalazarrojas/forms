# project_collaboration_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help project managers gather information from team members about their ability to collaborate on a project. It's a simple and straightforward form that helps project managers get an overview of the team members' skills, availability, and willingness to collaborate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
2. On the first page, select all the collaborators you are willing to work with on the project.
3. On the second page, select your role, skills, and availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **project_collaboration_signup_form_page_1_label**: This is the label for the first page of the form.
* **select_collaborators**: Select all the collaborators you are willing to work with on the project. 
    (required)
* **select_role**: Select your role in the project (required)
* **select_skills**: Select your skills that you can use on the project.
* **select_availability**: Select your availability for the project.
    (not required)
* **project_collaboration_signup_form_page_2_label**: This is the label for the second page of the form.
