# Gym Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from gym users for registration purposes. It includes contact details, personal data, health metrics, and agreements. Please provide accurate and honest answers to ensure smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information, including your name and email address.
2. Provide your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" or "MM/DD/YYYY".
3. Enter your height and weight in numerical values.
4. Select one or more goals you want to achieve at the gym.
5. Read and accept the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required):
    Enter your name and email address to help us get in touch with you.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required):
    Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required):
    Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required):
    Enter your valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required):
    Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Height** (`Height`, `number`, required):
    Enter your height in centimeters (cm) or meters (m).
* **Weight** (`Weight`, `number`, required):
    Enter your weight in kilograms (kg) or pounds (lbs).
* **Goals** (`Goals`, `select_one`, required):
    Select one or more goals you want to achieve at the gym, such as "Weight Loss" or "Improved Cardiovascular Health".
* **Terms and Conditions** (`Terms and Conditions`, `select_multiple`, required):
    Read and accept the terms and conditions by selecting "I have read and accepted the terms and conditions." or "I have not read the terms and conditions.".

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [support email/phone number].
- We will review your registration and contact you soon after submission.
- Please note that this form is for registration purposes only and is subject to the gym's policies.
