# Student Parking Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Parking Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who need to register for parking services. This form will help us to identify the student's vehicle type, license number, and other essential details for parking management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your student's First Name.
2. Next, provide your student's Last Name.
3. Enter your student's email address.
4. Confirm your student's email address for verification purposes.
5. Provide your student's phone number.
6. Enter your student's Date of Birth.
7. Select the type of vehicle you will be driving from the dropdown menu.
8. Enter your vehicle's license number.
9. Enter your vehicle's make.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student First Name** (`student_first_name`, `text`, optional): The first name of the student registering for parking services.
* **Student Last Name** (`student_last_name`, `text`, optional): The last name of the student registering for parking services.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, optional): The email address of the student. Please use the same email address you used to register for the university.
* **Confirm Student Email** (`student_email_confirmation`, `email`, optional): Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): The phone number of the student.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, `date`, optional): The date of birth of the student.
* **What type of vehicle will you be driving?** (`student_vehicle_type`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of vehicle you will be driving from the list:
	+ Vehicle 1
	+ Vehicle 2
	+ Vehicle 3
* **License Number** (`student_vehicle_license`, `text`, optional): The license number of the vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make** (`student_vehicle_make`, `text`, optional): The make of the vehicle.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email address for accuracy when confirming it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please note that all information is collected for parking management purposes only.
