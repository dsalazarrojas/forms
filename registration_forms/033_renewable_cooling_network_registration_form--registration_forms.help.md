# Renewable Cooling Network Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users interested in joining the Renewable Cooling Network. The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from users, including registration details, contact information, and interest in the network's benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Registration Information section with your name and any other identifying details you'd like to share.
2. Select your Organization in the Organization section.
3. Identify the Contact Person for your organization in the Contact Person section.
4. Enter your Email and Phone numbers in the Email and Phone sections respectively.
5. Answer "Yes" or "No" for Organization Type (e.g., "Yes" for non-profit) and Interest in the Organization Type and Interest sections respectively.
6. If you're registering on a specific date, enter it in the Date of Birth section.
7. Choose your registration time in the Time of Registration section.
8. Add any Registration Notes if necessary.
9. Optionally, select the tool assigned to you in the Assigned Tool section (this field is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`user`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other identifying details you'd like to share.
* **Organization** (`user`, `text`, required/optional): Select your organization name or name.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Identify the person responsible for your organization's contact information.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Answer "Yes" if your organization is a non-profit or similar.
* **Interest** (`interest`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate which benefits you're interested in (select one or multiple options).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Registration** (`time_of_registration`, `time`, required/optional): Choose the time of your registration.
* **Registration Notes** (`registration_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required/optional): Select the tool assigned to you if applicable.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing your application.
* If you need help or have questions about any of the form fields, please contact us.
