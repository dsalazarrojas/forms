# Seasonal Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Seasonal Program Registration form is designed for users to register their seasonal programs, providing essential details about the program's name, date, start time, end date, and time. This form aims to gather accurate and complete information from users to facilitate smooth program execution.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Seasonal Program Name field with the name of your program.
2. Enter the Program Date, which is the date of your program.
3. Set the Program Start and End Dates according to your program's schedule.
4. Enter the Program Time, which is the duration of your program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seasonal Program Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your seasonal program. This field is essential for identifying your program.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your seasonal program.
* **Program Start** (`program_start`, `time`, required/optional): Set the start time of your program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the end date of your seasonal program.
* **Program Time** (`program_time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the duration of your program in hours and minutes (e.g., "2h 30m" for a 2-hour 30-minute program).
