# Youth Soccer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your child in the youth soccer camp. It's essential to fill it out accurately and completely to ensure we have all the necessary information to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your child's athlete information in the "Athlete Information" section, including their name, email, and other relevant details.
2.  Provide your contact information as a parent or guardian in the "Parent Information" section.
3.  Select your choice of consent option for your child's participation in the camp.
4.  Confirm that you have read and understand the camp terms and conditions.
5.  Enter your child's date of birth, emergency contact, and other medical information in the respective sections.
6.  Select your child's team and player position.
7.  Choose your child's player specialty and player skill level.
8.  Indicate your child's experience level in the sport.
9.  Sign the "Parent Signature" section to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, required): Enter your child's name as it appears on their identification documents.
*   **Athlete Email** (`athlete_email`, `email`, required): Enter your child's email address.
*   **Athlete Phone** (`athlete_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your child's phone number.
*   **Athlete Address** (`athlete_address`, `text`, required): Enter your child's home address.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
*   **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
*   **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your child's date of birth.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions your child may have, such as allergies or medical conditions that may affect their participation in the camp.
*   **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions_2`, `text`, optional): List any additional medical conditions your child may have.
*   **Athlete Gender** (`athlete_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your child's gender.
*   **Athlete Age** (`athlete_age`, `number`, required): Enter your child's age.
*   **Team** (`team`, `select_one`, required): Select the team your child will be part of.
*   **Jersey Number** (`jersey_number`, `number`, required): Enter your child's jersey number.
*   **Player Position** (`player_position`, `select_one`, required): Select your child's player position.
*   **Player Specialty** (`player_specialty`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your child's player specialty, such as soccer, basketball, or tennis.
*   **Player Skill Level** (`player_skill_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your child's player skill level, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
*   **Player Experience** (`player_experience`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your child's experience level in the sport, such as 0-1 year, 2-3 years, or more than 3 years.
*   **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, required): Sign your name as the parent or guardian to confirm your consent.

Tips:

*   Be accurate and honest when filling out the form.
*   Ensure that you have all the necessary information and permissions before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
