# Scaffold User Safety Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Scaffold User Safety Registration form is a tool used to collect information about user safety concerns, incidents, and experiences. This form is designed to help users provide detailed accounts of their safety-related events, which can aid in identifying potential risks and taking measures to prevent future occurrences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have understood the purpose and the questions asked.
2. Complete all the required fields (indicated as `required` in the form).
3. Be as detailed as possible when providing information in each field.
4. If you're unsure about any section, refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Safety Registration (Page 1)** (`user_safety_registration`, text, required/optional): This field is a general introduction to the form. Please provide a brief statement about your safety concerns or incidents.
* **User Safety Registration Form (Page 2)** (`user_safety_registration_form`, text, required/optional): This field is a space to provide more detailed information about your safety concerns or incidents.
* **Safety Concerns** (`safety_concerns`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all that apply from the list of safety concerns to indicate any worries or risks you've encountered.
* **Date of Incident** (`date_of_incident`, date, required/optional): Provide the date when the incident occurred. Use the standard date format (YYYY-MM-DD) to ensure accurate recording.
* **Time of Incident** (`time_of_incident`, time, required/optional): Indicate the time when the incident happened in 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Location of Incident** (`location_of_incident`, text, required/optional): Describe the location where the incident occurred, including any relevant details.
* **Witness** (`witness`, text, required/optional): If there were any witnesses, provide their names and contact information.
* **Incident Narrative** (`incident_narrative`, text, required/optional): Share a detailed account of what happened during the incident, including any circumstances or factors that contributed to the event.

## Tips
- Be as thorough as possible when providing information in each field.
- If you're unsure about any section, refer back to the field explanations above.
- If you need help or have questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
