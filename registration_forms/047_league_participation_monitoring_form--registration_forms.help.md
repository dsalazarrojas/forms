# League Participation Monitoring Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from athletes about their participation in a league or tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the required details of the athlete, including their name and other relevant information.
2. Select the league they are participating in.
3. Choose the date of the tournament.
4. Submit the form once all information has been provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter Athlete Details (Name)** (`athlete_details`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the athlete.
* **League Name (league_name, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the league the athlete is participating in.
* **Tournament Date (tournament_date, date, optional)**: Enter the date of the tournament the athlete is participating in.
