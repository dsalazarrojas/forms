# Spa Service Trial Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spa Service Trial Registration Form is a document used to record and document the details of a trial service provided at a spa. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is collected and stored correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the relevant fields with the required information.
2.  Select the correct service type from the drop-down menu.
3.  Ensure that all the required fields (marked with an *) are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **spa_service_trial_registration_form**: This field is the title of the form, it's not editable and should be left blank.
* **Customer Name**: Enter the name of the person receiving the service trial.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the visit in the format `HH:MM`.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service provided from the drop-down menu:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
	+ Option 4
	+ Option 5
* **Service Type Description**: Enter a brief description of the service provided.
* **Customer Email**: Enter the email address of the person receiving the service trial.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person receiving the service trial.
* **Spa Location**: Enter the location of the spa where the service trial was provided.
* **Spa Staff Name**: Enter the name of the spa staff member who provided the service trial.
* **Spa Staff Phone**: Enter the phone number of the spa staff member who provided the service trial.
* **Spa Staff Email**: Enter the email address of the spa staff member who provided the service trial.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the service trial.
* **Service Details**: Enter a brief description of the service provided.
* **Spa Location Details**: Enter any additional details about the location where the service trial was provided.
* **Trial Start Date**: Enter the start date of the trial in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Trial End Date**: Enter the end date of the trial in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
