# Outstanding Online Forum Nomination Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect nominations for outstanding online forum contributions. Please provide accurate and thorough information to ensure the nominee's recognition is acknowledged properly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Data: Enter your name, email, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
2. Nominee Details: Enter the details of the person or entity being nominated, including their name, title/position, and affiliation.
3. Nomination Form: Enter a brief description of the nomination, specifying the reasons why the person or entity should be recognized.
4. Submit: Click the Submit button to send the nomination for review.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Data** (`user_data`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, email, and any other contact information you'd like to share.
* **Nominee Details** (`nominee_details`, Text, Optional): Enter the details of the person or entity being nominated, including their name, title/position, and affiliation.
* **Nomination Form** (`nomination_form`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of the nomination, specifying the reasons why the person or entity should be recognized.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, Text, Optional): Click this button to send the nomination for review.

## Tips
* Please provide accurate and honest information to ensure the nominee's recognition is acknowledged properly.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
* This form is for non-profit purposes only, and the collected information will be used to recognize and promote outstanding contributions within the online community.
