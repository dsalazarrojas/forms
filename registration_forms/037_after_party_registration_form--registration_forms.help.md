# After Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for guests to register for an after-party event. It collects information about their guest details, preferences, and requirements to provide a tailored experience. Please note that this form is intended for guests who are already registered for the original event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
2. Indicate your attending status for the original event and the after-party event.
3. Select your music and activity preferences for the after-party.
4. Choose your seating preference and indicate if you have any accessibility needs.
5. Confirm your transportation arrangements and parking needs.
6. If you are bringing a plus one, enter their name and details.
7. Provide any special requests or dietary restrictions you may have.
8. Confirm your social media consent and contact preferences.
9. Sign the electronic signature section to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Information**: Enter your name, email, and phone number to help us contact you about the event and confirm your registration.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for event updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for last-minute event updates.
* **Will You Attend?**: Choose whether you will be attending the original event before the after-party or not.
* **Plus One Option**: Select "Yes" if you are bringing a plus one. If "Yes", enter their name and details.
* **Plus One Details**: Enter the name and details of your plus one (if applicable).
* **Original Event Attendance**: Select whether you will be attending the original event before the after-party.
* **Expected Arrival Time**: Enter the time you expect to arrive at the after-party.
* **Expected Departure Time**: Enter the time you expect to leave the after-party.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions or food allergies, enter them here.
* **Beverage Preferences**: Enter any specific beverage preferences (e.g., no alcohol, no caffeine).
* **Music Preferences**: Select your preferred music genres for the after-party.
* **Activity Preferences**: Select activities you would like to see at the after-party (e.g., dancing, games).
* **Seating Preference**: Choose your preferred seating arrangement (e.g., standing, casual, formal).
* **Accessibility Needs**: Select "Yes" if you have any accessibility requirements.
* **Accessibility Details**: If you have accessibility needs, provide details about what you require.
* **Transportation Arrangements**: Choose how you plan to get to and from the after-party (e.g., driving, rideshare, public transportation).
* **Parking Needs**: Select "Yes" if you need parking arrangements.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any special requests or requirements you may have.
* **Social Media Consent**: Select "Yes" if you consent to being photographed or filmed at the event.
* **Contact Preferences**: Choose how you would like to be contacted about event updates (e.g., email, text message, phone call).
* **Registration Confirmation**: Review and confirm your registration details.
* **Terms Agreement**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you agree to the event terms and conditions.
* **Information Accuracy**: Confirm that all information provided is accurate.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign to confirm your registration.
