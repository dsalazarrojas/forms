# Partnership Negotiation Meeting Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Partnership Negotiation Meeting Reservation Form is used to collect information about meetings scheduled between your team and our partners for negotiation purposes. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that we have all necessary details for our meeting preparations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Submit Your Reservation Request**: Click on "Submit" at the end of the form after you have filled out all required information.
2. **Fill Out Each Field**: Provide accurate and detailed information for each field as much as you can.
3. **Double-Check Required Fields**: Some fields are marked as required; make sure to fill those out carefully as they are essential for processing your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date (1)**: The date of the meeting. Enter in the format `YYYY-MM-DD` to ensure it is correctly read.
* **Meeting Time (2)**: The time of the meeting. Select the appropriate time of day in 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Partner Name (3)**: The name of your partner or contact person. This helps us identify who we are meeting with and ensure that we address them correctly.
* **Negotiation Objective (4)**: This is a brief description of the purpose of the meeting. This will help us understand the meeting's focus and ensure we are adequately prepared.
* **Meeting Location (5)**: The location of the meeting. This can be a physical address or any other relevant detail.
* **Partner Email (6)**: The email address of your partner. If you have it, please provide this so we can contact them efficiently.
* **Partner Phone (7)**: The phone number of your partner. If you have it, please provide this in case an urgent matter arises before the meeting.

Note that fields 1, 3, 5 are marked as required; please fill those out with accurate information for our records.
