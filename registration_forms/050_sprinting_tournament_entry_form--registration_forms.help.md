**sprinting_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide**
## Purpose
The "sprinting_tournament_entry_form" is a form designed to collect user information for registration in a sprinting tournament. It is intended to gather basic contact and verification information for each participant. This form is not intended for duplicate submissions, so ensure you provide accurate and unique details for each user registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First Name** field with your personal name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide a valid **Email** address that can be used for communication.
4. Confirm your **Email** address by re-entering it in the **Confirm Email** field.
5. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your personal name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your surname or family name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address to receive notifications and updates.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Re-enter your email address to verify its accuracy.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.

### Tips
* Ensure all fields are accurate and valid to avoid form submission issues.
* Use a valid email address that you can check regularly for updates and notifications.
* If you encounter any issues while submitting the form, contact support for assistance.
