# Product Designer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in applying for a product design position. Please fill it out carefully and accurately to provide the best possible chance of getting noticed by our team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first name and last name fields with your name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter a valid email address to which we can respond to regarding your application.
3. Provide your phone number to enable us to contact you for further discussions.
4. If you have a portfolio, describe it briefly in the text field.
5. If you have a link to your online portfolio, enter it in the "Portfolio Link" field.
6. Share your relevant experience in the "Experience" field.
7. List your educational background in the "Education" field.
8. Indicate whether you have the skills required for the position by answering "Yes" or "No" in the "Skills" field.
9. Upload your portfolio files to demonstrate your work to the best of your abilities.
10. Select "Yes" to submit your application, or "No" if you are not ready or willing to proceed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number for further discussions.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required): Briefly describe your portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): A link to your online portfolio (if you have one).
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, required): List your relevant experience in product design.
* **Education** (`education`, text, required): List your educational background relevant to product design.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select_multiple, required): Check "Yes" if you have the skills required for the position.
* **Portfolio Files** (`portfolio_files`, text, required): Upload your portfolio files to demonstrate your work.
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): Select "Yes" to submit your application, or "No" if you are not ready or willing to proceed.
