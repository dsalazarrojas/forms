# Local Chess Blitz Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for players to register for a local chess blitz tournament. To register, players must fill out the form accurately and completely. The form requires the following information: player details and tournament settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **Player Name** field.
2. Enter your email address in the **Player Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Player Phone** field.
4. Enter your current chess rating in the **Player Rating** field.
5. Select your playing section from the **Player Section** field.
6. If necessary, provide more details about your playing section in the **Player Section Hint** field.
7. Select the sections you will be participating in from the **Tournament Section** field.
8. If necessary, provide more details about your tournament section in the **Tournament Section Hint** field.
9. Enter the date of the tournament in the **Tournament Date** field.
10. Enter the time of the tournament in the **Tournament Time** field.
11. If you have any additional rating information, enter it in the **Player Rating Hint** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Name**: Enter the name of the tournament you are registering for.
* **Player Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the tournament scoreboard.
* **Player Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Player Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Player Rating**: Enter your current chess rating. (Example: 2000)
* **Player Section**: Select your playing section: 
  + Chess Section: For players who are strong chess players and have played in local tournaments before.
  + Casual Section: For casual players who are new to local tournaments.
  + Tournament Section: For players who are participating in a specific tournament.
* **Player Section Hint**: Provide more details about your playing section.
* **Tournament Section**: Select the sections you will be participating in. 
  + Section 1-10: Select the sections you will be playing in.
* **Tournament Section Hint**: Provide more details about your tournament section.
* **Tournament Date**: Enter the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Time**: Enter the time of the tournament.
* **Player Rating Hint**: Enter any additional rating information (e.g., ELO or rating number).

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, contact the tournament organizers.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
