# Telecom Customer Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather essential information from new Telecom customers to provide a more personalized experience with our services.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, select the correct option for the 'First Step' field on the first page. If you are unsure about this question, please contact your account manager or our customer service team for guidance.
2.  On the second page, fill in your name and any other relevant details in the 'customer_details' field.
3.  On the third page, provide any special requirements or preferences in the 'service_preferences' field.
4.  On the fourth page, specify your installation requests in the 'installation_requests' field.
5.  Finally, choose whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up on the 'follow_up' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Step** (`1`, `select_one`, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm whether you have been previously onboarded or not.
*   **Customer Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Please fill in your full name to help us identify you in our records.
*   **Service Preferences** (`3`, `text`, required): Specify any special preferences or requirements you have for our services, such as language or accessibility needs.
*   **Installation Requests** (`4`, `text`, required): Describe your installation requests, including the desired date and time.
*   **Follow-up** (`5`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you would like to be contacted for follow-up or not.
