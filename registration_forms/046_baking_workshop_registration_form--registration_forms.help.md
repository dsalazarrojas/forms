# Baking WorkShop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baking WorkShop Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential participants for a baking workshop. This form is used to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the workshop you are interested in attending.
3. Indicate your level of baking experience.
4. Specify any dietary restrictions or special requirements you may have.
5. Choose your preferred payment method.
6. Confirm your registration by selecting "Confirm Registration".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, phone, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization or Bakery (if applicable)** (`organization`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a bakery or organization, enter it here.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, select_one, required): Select the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Select the time of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Duration** (`duration`, select_one, optional): Select the duration of the workshop you are interested in attending (2 Hours, 4 Hours, Full Day, or Multi-Day).
* **Baking Experience Level** (`experience`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of baking experience (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional).
* **Previous Baking Workshops Attended** (`previous_workshops`, integer, optional): If you have attended previous baking workshops, enter the number here.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, text, required): Specify what your learning goals are for attending the workshop.
* **Areas of Interest** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of interest that apply to you (Bread Making, Pastries, Cakes, Cookies, Decorating, Advanced Techniques, or Business Aspects).
* **Workshop Location Preference** (`location`, text, optional): If you have a preference for the workshop location, enter it here.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, specify them here.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If you need any special accommodations (mobility, learning, language, etc.), enter it here.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, decimal, required): Enter the registration fee in dollars.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Cash).
* **Confirm Registration** (`registration_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your registration by selecting "Confirm Registration".
