# Participant Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Intake Form is used to collect contact information and other relevant details about new participants in a program.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your Participant Name in the text field provided.
2. Enter your Participant Email in the email field (ensure it is a valid email address).
3. Enter your Participant Phone Number in the text field provided.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the date field (in YYYY-MM-DD format).
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about yourself in the text field provided.
6. Select the title of the Program you are participating in.
7. Select the activity title related to your participation.
8. Select the title of the work you are engaged in.
9. Choose your current status as a participant (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
10. If your participant status is "Other", please provide a brief explanation in the text field.
11. Select any categories you belong to (Yes or No).
12. Enter any additional comments about yourself in the text field.
13. Check the box to confirm you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
14. Enter the date and time you agreed to the terms and conditions.
14. Enter the time you agreed to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about yourself.
* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, optional): Select the title of the program you are participating in.
* **Activity Title** (`activity_title`, text, optional): Select the activity title related to your participation.
* **Work Title** (`works_title`, text, optional): Select the title of the work you are engaged in.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, optional): Choose your current status as a participant (Active, Pending, or Inactive). If you select "Other", please provide a brief explanation.
* **Other Status Explanation** (`participant_status_other`, text, optional): Please provide a brief explanation of your participant status.
* **Categories** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select any categories you belong to (Yes or No).
* **Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about yourself.
* **Agreed** (`participant_agreed`, text, optional): Check the box to confirm you have agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreed Date** (`participant_agreed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Agreed Time** (`participant_agreed_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you agreed to the terms and conditions.
