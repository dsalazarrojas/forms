# Tournament Field Access Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request field access for upcoming games and practices. It allows teams to provide necessary information to schedule field access.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form) with the requested information.
2. Choose the correct options for field access requested (field_access_requested).
3. Add any additional notes if necessary.
4. Review your form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (1)**: This is the name of the form, which is automatically filled in and does not require input.
* **Team Name (2)**: Enter your team's name as it appears on your uniform or registration paperwork.
* **Coach Name (3)**: Enter the name of your team's coach or the person responsible for field access requests.
* **Practice Date (4)**: Select the date of your scheduled practice. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Practice Time (5)**: Select the time of your scheduled practice. Use the 24-hour clock format HH:MM.
* **Game Date (6)**: Select the date of your scheduled game. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Game Time (7)**: Select the time of your scheduled game. Use the 24-hour clock format HH:MM.
* **Field Access Requested (8)**: Choose whether you are requesting access for a practice or a game.
* **Additional Notes (9)**: Enter any additional information you feel is necessary for your field access request. You can use this field to explain your needs.
* **Email (10)**: Enter your team or coach's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (11)**: Enter your team or coach's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Game Field Count (12)**: Enter the number of fields you are requesting access to for your game.
* **Practice Field Count (13)**: Enter the number of fields you are requesting access to for your practice.
* **Field Access Requesting Party (14)**: Enter the name of the person or team requesting field access.
* **Field Access Requested For (15)**: Enter the name of the team or field access requesting party.
* **Additional Notes (16)**: Enter any additional information you feel is necessary for your field access request.
