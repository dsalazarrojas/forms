# Self Love Experience RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self Love Experience RSVP form is a simple registration form for attendees to provide their personal information, preferences, and attendance status for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Select your event date in the `event_date` field.
4. Choose your event time in the `event_time` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
6. If you have a phone number, enter it in the `phone` field.
7. If you have any special requests, enter them in the `special_requests` field.
8. Choose whether you will attend the event by selecting `Will you be attending?` from the `Will you be attending?` dropdown menu.
9. If you are bringing a guest, enter their name in the `guest_name` field.
10. If you have any additional information about your guest, enter it in the `guest_details` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: Enter your first name.
* **last_name**: Enter your last name.
* **event_date**: Select the date of the event.
* **event_time**: Choose the time of the event.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **special_requests**: Enter any special requests or accommodations you may need.
* **Will you be attending?**: Choose "Will you be attending?" to indicate your attendance status.
* **guest_name**: Enter your guest's name (if you are bringing a guest).
* **guest_details**: Enter any additional information about your guest (if you are bringing a guest).
