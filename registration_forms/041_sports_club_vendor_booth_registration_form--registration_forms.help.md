# Sports Club Vendor Booth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for sports clubs to register their vendor booths for events. It's a two-page form that requires vendor contact information, payment details, and other necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor name, email, phone number, and address on the first page.
2. Choose your payment method from the options provided or type "other" and provide more information.
3. Select your payment terms and vendor needs on the second page.
4. Confirm your registration by clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the vendor.
* **Vendor Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Vendor Address** (text, required): Enter your address.
* **Payment Method** (select_one, required): Choose from a list of payment options or type "other" for custom payment method.
* **Payment Details 2** (text, required): If you chose "other" as your payment method, provide more details about your payment method.
* **Payment Terms** (date, optional): Enter the payment terms if required.
* **Payment Terms Time** (time, optional): Enter the payment terms time if required.
* **Vendor Needs** (select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options for your vendor needs.
* **Vendor Needs Other** (text, optional): If you need to add any other vendor needs that are not listed, provide them here.
* **Booth Number** (number, optional): Enter your booth number if you have one.
* **Vendor Type** (select_one, optional): Select your vendor type.
* **Vendor Type Other** (text, optional): If you need to add any other vendor type that is not listed, provide them here.
* **Vendor Notes** (text, optional): Add any additional notes about your vendor.
* **Payment Terms Terms** (text, optional): Enter any payment terms if required.
* **Payment Terms Notes** (text, optional): Enter any payment terms notes if required.
* **Submit (2)** (text, required): Confirm your registration by clicking this button.
