# Backflow Tester Registration Survey - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registration and verification of backflow testers. It helps us to ensure that you have the necessary experience and qualifications to perform backflow tests, and provides us with the necessary information to schedule inspections and verify your registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full legal name in the "Tester name" field.
2. Enter your license or certification number, along with the issuing authority.
3. If you are employed, enter your company name; otherwise, leave this field blank.
4. If applicable, enter the number of years you have been testing backflow.
5. List the service areas you cover (e.g. cities or regions).
6. Indicate whether you are available for inspections and provide a contact phone number.
7. Enter your email address for registration confirmation.
8. If you have insurance coverage for liability, enter the provider and policy number.
9. Finally, enter the date you submitted this registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tester Name** (`tester_name`, text, required): This is your full legal name.
* **License or Certification Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your license or certification number issued by the relevant authority.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): If you are employed, enter your company name; otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the number of years you have been testing backflow.
* **Service Areas Covered** (`service_areas`, text, optional): List the cities or regions you cover for backflow testing.
* **Available for Inspections** (`available_for_inspections`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you are available for inspections.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for registration confirmation.
* **Insurance Coverage for Liability** (`insurance_info`, text, optional): If you have insurance coverage, enter the provider and policy number.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this registration form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form will be used for registration and verification purposes only.
