# Open House Preparation Checklist - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open House Preparation Checklist is a form designed to collect essential information and preparation tasks for an open house event. This form helps individuals or teams responsible for organizing and hosting the event to gather relevant details, making it easier to prepare and execute the event successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with the necessary information.
2. For each field, provide clear and concise answers.
3. Review and adjust your answers as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (1, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Date** (2, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (3, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Prep Task 1** (4, text, required): Describe the first preparation task.
* **Prep Task 2** (5, text, required): Describe the second preparation task.
* **Prep Task 3** (6, text, required): Describe the third preparation task.
* **Event Details** (7, note, required): Add any relevant details about the event.
* **Notes** (8, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Use clear and concise language in each field.
* Review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Use this form as a starting point and adjust it as needed to suit your specific event requirements.
