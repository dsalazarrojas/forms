# Mailbox Service Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mailbox Service Agreement form is designed to collect essential information from customers regarding their mailbox service preferences and agreement. This form helps us understand your service requirements and ensures a smooth experience for both you and our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Customer Name**, **Customer Email**, and **Customer Phone** details accurately and thoroughly.
2. Select the **Service Preferences** that best suit your needs from the available options.
3. If you have any electronic (e-Signatures) agreements or notes, please attach them to the **E-Signatures** field.
4. Write your **Agreement** clearly and concisely, ensuring it reflects your understanding of our service terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Name (text, required: false)
* This field is for you to enter your name as a customer.

### Customer Email (email, required: false)
* This is your email address where we can contact you about your mailbox service.

### Customer Phone (text, required: false)
* Your phone number for us to reach you about your service.

### Service Preferences (select_multiple, required: false)
* Please select the services you are interested in or require for your mailbox. This will help us tailor our service accordingly.

### E-Signatures (note, required: false)
* If you have any electronic agreements or notes relevant to your mailbox service, please attach them here.

### Agreement (text, required: false)
* Clearly write out your agreement with our service terms, so we are all on the same page.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly to avoid any confusion.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* If you need to make any changes to your service preferences or agreement, please inform us in a timely manner.
