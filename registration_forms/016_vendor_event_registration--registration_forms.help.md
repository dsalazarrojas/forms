# Vendor Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for vendors to register their events. It is a straightforward form with sections to collect necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the "Vendor Event" question by selecting "Yes" or "No".
2. Fill out the "Show Info", "Vendor Info", "Contact Info", "Event Start", "Event End", and "Event Duration" fields with relevant information.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Provide any additional contact notes in the "Contact Note" field (optional).
6. Enter event location information in the "Event Location" and "Event Address" fields.
7. Select the "Assigned Tool" field to assign a tool to your event.
8. Click on the "Submit" button to save and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Event** (`vendor_event`, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this is a vendor event.
* **Show Info** (`show_info`, text, required): Provide a short description of your event.
* **Vendor Info** (`vendor_info`, text, required): Enter relevant vendor information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Event Start** (`event_start`, date, required): Enter the start date of your event.
* **Event End** (`event_end`, date, required): Enter the end date of your event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of your event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Note** (`contact_note`, note, optional): Add any additional contact notes.
* **Vendor Note** (`vendor_note`, note, optional): Add any additional vendor notes.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required): Select the tool assigned to your event.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click to submit your form.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, required): Click to cancel your form.
* **Save and Exit** (`save_and_exit`, text, optional): Save your form and exit.
* **Save and Send** (`save_and_send`, text, optional): Save your form and send it directly.
* **Save and Preview** (`save_and_preview`, text, optional): Save and preview your form.
* **Reset** (`reset`, text, optional): Reset your form.
* **Back** (`back`, text, optional): Go back to the previous page.
* **Next** (`next`, text, optional): Go to the next page.
* **Vendor Event Info** (`vendor_event_info`, text, required): Enter event information.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter event location information.
* **Event Address** (`event_address`, text, required): Enter event address information.
* **Save** (`save`, text, optional): Save your form without exiting.
* **Save and Exit** (`save_and_exit`, text, optional): Save and exit your form.
