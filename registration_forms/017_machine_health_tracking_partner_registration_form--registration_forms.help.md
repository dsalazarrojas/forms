# Machine Health Tracking Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Health Tracking Partner Registration Form is used to register new partners for machine health tracking purposes. It ensures that your machine's health and status are accurately reported and tracked.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the required information about the machine partner, including their name and details.
2. Fill out the machine details section with the relevant information.
3. Provide the machine's ID number.
4. Report on the machine's health status.
5. Enter the partner's details, including any relevant contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **partner_info** (partner_info, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the partner, such as their name and organization.
* **machine_details** (machine_details, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the machine, including its type, model, or other relevant details.
* **machine_id** (machine_id, number, optional): Enter the unique ID number of the machine.
* **machine_health** (machine_health, text, optional): Report on the machine's current health status, such as operational or under maintenance.
* **partner_details** (partner_details, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the partner, including their contact method or other relevant details.
* **contact_person** (contact_person, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the partner contact.
* **contact_email** (contact_email, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (contact_phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_notes** (contact_notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the contact person or partner.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure proper registration.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
- This form is for new partner registrations only, so please don't fill it out if you've already registered the partner.
