# Patient Centered Communication Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for patients to register for a workshop on patient-centered communication. It's a straightforward way to gather necessary information for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your user information in the "User Information" field.
2. Select your preferred sessions from the list provided in the "Sessions" field.
3. If you have any special needs or requirements, please list them in the "Special Needs" field.
4. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details about yourself as a patient.
* **Sessions** (`sessions`, Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Choose one or more sessions that you'd like to attend from the list provided. This will help us tailor the workshop to your interests.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, Text, Required/Optional): If you have any special needs or requirements that we should be aware of, please list them here.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, Text, Required/Optional): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and any other relevant details. This will help us get in touch with you before or after the workshop.
