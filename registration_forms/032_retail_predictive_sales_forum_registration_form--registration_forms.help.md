# Retail Predictive Sales Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for participants to register for the Retail Predictive Sales Forum, providing their personal and professional details to facilitate networking and discussion within the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that you have permission to submit your information on behalf of others.
3. Review and agree to the terms of service before submitting your registration.
4. Click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, Text, Required): Enter your full name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Required): Enter your current job title.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, Text, Required): Enter your company name.
* **Job Company** (`job_company`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the company you work with or are associated with.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of company you are associated with: Retail, Wholesale, Online, or Physical.
* **Job Position** (`job_position`, Text, Optional): Enter a brief description of your job position.
* **Role** (`role`, Text, Required): Enter your role in the company or industry.
* **Job Category** (`job_category`, Text, Required): Enter the category of your job.
* **Location** (`location`, Text, Required): Enter the location of your workplace or company.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, Time, Required): Enter your timezone.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, Note, Required): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, Text, Required): Enter the industry you work in.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, Text, Required): Enter the function of your job.
* **Job Department** (`job_department`, Text, Required): Enter the department you work in.
* **Job Level** (`job_level`, Select One, Required): Select the level of your job: Entry Level, Mid Level, Senior, or Manager.
* **Job Tenure** (`job_tenure`, Number, Required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your job.
* **Job Seniority** (`job_seniority`, Text, Required): Enter your level of seniority or experience.
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, Select One, Required): Select the status of your job: Active or Inactive.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you started your job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date your job ended (if applicable).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, Select Multiple, Required): Select the type of job: Part-time, Full-time, or Contract.
* **Job Hours** (`job_hours`, Text, Optional): Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **Job Comments** (`job_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments about your job or experience.

Note: This help guide assumes that you have already accepted the terms of service before using the form.
