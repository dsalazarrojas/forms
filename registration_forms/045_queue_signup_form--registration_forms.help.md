# Queue Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Queue Signup Form is used to sign up users for a waiting list managed by the service provider. This form is designed to collect user information and ensure that the user can be contacted and managed in the queue.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `queue-signup-form` field with your desired queue name.
2. Enter your name in the `name` field.
3. Provide your phone number in the `phone` field.
4. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
5. Select a date for your queue visit in the `date` field.
6. Choose a time for your queue visit in the `time` field.
7. Select one option from the `select-one` field.
8. Select multiple options from the `select-multiple` field.
9. Optionally, add a note in the `note` field.
10. Click the `submit` button to complete your sign up.
11. Click the `cancel` button to cancel your sign up.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **queue-signup-form**: Enter the name of the queue you are signing up for. (Text input, required)
* **name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the queue. (Text input, required)
* **phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your queue visit. (Text input, required)
* **email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about your queue visit. (Email input, required)
* **date**: Select the date you would like to visit the queue. (Date input, required)
* **time**: Select the time you would like to visit the queue. (Time input, required)
* **select-one**: Select one option from the list. (Select input, required)
* **select-multiple**: Select multiple options from the list. (Select input, required)
* **note**: Optionally, add a note to provide additional information about your queue visit. (Note input, optional)
* **submit**: Click to complete your sign up for the queue. (Text input, required)
* **cancel**: Click to cancel your sign up for the queue. (Text input, optional)
