# Motorcycle License Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a motorcycle license application. It gathers details about the applicant, license status, motorcycle type, and other relevant information required for the license application process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Provide your name and other contact information in the "Applicant Information" section.
3. Enter your motorcycle license details, including the license number, status, and expiration date and time.
4. Add any additional information or comments in the "Additional Info" section.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your acceptance of the application terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information** (`applicant_information_form`, text, optional): This section is for providing your personal contact information.
* **License Details** (`license_details`, text, optional): Enter your motorcycle license number, status, and expiration date and time.
* **Motorcycle Type** (`motorcycle_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of motorcycle you are applying for (e.g., Motorcycle 1, Motorcycle 2, or Motorcycle 3).
* **License Expiration Date** (`license_expiration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date your license expires.
* **License Expiration Time** (`license_expiration_time`, time, optional): Enter the time your license expires.
* **Rider Information** (`rider_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself as a rider.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Add any other relevant information about your license application.
* **Submit** (`motorcycle_license_application_form_submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your application.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments about your application.
* **Rider Signature** (`rider_signature`, text, optional): Sign your name to confirm your application.
* **License Application Date** (`license_application_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are applying for the license.
* **License Application Time** (`license_application_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you are applying for the license.
* **License Application Form Notes** (`license_application_form_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about your application.
* **License Application Form Attachments** (`license_application_form_attachments`, text, optional): Attach any supporting documents for your application.
* **License Application Form Agreements** (`license_application_form_agreements`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the application.
* **Agree** (`license_application_form_agree`, text, optional): Check to confirm you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Disagree** (`license_application_form_disagree`, text, optional): Check if you disagree with the terms and conditions.
* **Comments** (`license_application_form_comments`, text, optional): Add any comments about the application terms.
* **Agree Date** (`license_application_form_agree_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree Time** (`license_application_form_agree_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Agree Reason** (`license_application_form_agree_reason`, text, optional): Explain why you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Remark** (`license_application_form_remark`, text, optional): Add any final remarks about your application.
