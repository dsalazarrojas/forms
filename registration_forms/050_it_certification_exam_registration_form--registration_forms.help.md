# IT Certification Exam Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering for an IT Certification Exam. Please fill out the form with the required information to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your phone number for contact.
4. Select the date and time of the exam you want to register for.
5. Enter the name of the course you want to register for.
6. Select your organization or affiliation.
7. Choose your payment method (Yes/No).
8. Enter the amount you are paying.
9. Select your payment status (Active/Inactive).
10. Choose your status (Active/Inactive).
11. Provide any additional comments, if needed.
11. Finally, confirm your submission by selecting "Yes" or "No" at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the exam you want to register for.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of the exam you want to register for.
* **Course Name** (`course`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the course you want to register for.
* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Select your organization or affiliation.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment method (Yes/No).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment status (Active/Inactive).
* **Status** (`status`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your status (Active/Inactive).
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments you may have.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your submission by selecting "Yes" or "No".
