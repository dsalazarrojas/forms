# Organic Farming Awareness Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for users who want to register for an Organic Farming Awareness Session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct session date and time using the session_date field.
2. Choose the session location from the options provided in the session_location field.
3. Fill out your name, email, and contact number in the name, email, and contact_number fields.
4. Indicate your category as Fresher, Farmer, or Farmer with Family in the attendee_info field.
5. Confirm your email and phone number using the email_confirmation and confirm_phone fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (Text): Enter your name to identify yourself as a registered attendee.
* **Email** (Text): Enter your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Attendee Info** (Select Multiple): Select your category as Fresher, Farmer, or Farmer with Family.
* **Session Date** (Date): Select the date of the session you want to attend.
* **Session Time** (Time): Select the time of the session you want to attend.
* **Session Location** (Select One): Choose the location of the session you are interested in.
* **Comments** (Text): Enter any additional comments or notes you have.
* **Email Confirmation** (Email): Confirm your email address for follow-up communication.
* **Phone Confirmation** (Text): Confirm your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Confirm Email** (Select One): Confirm that the email address you provided is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (Select One): Confirm that the phone number you provided is accurate.

## Tips
* Ensure you select the correct session date and time and location.
* Fill out all required fields (email, comments, confirm_email, confirm_phone).
* Double-check your email and phone number before confirming them.
* If you have any additional comments or concerns, use the comments field to communicate them to the organizers.
* Be accurate when confirming your email and phone number to ensure smooth communication.
