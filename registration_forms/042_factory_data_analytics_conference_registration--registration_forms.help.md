# Factory Data Analytics Conference Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Factory Data Analytics Conference Registration form is used to collect attendee information for the Factory Data Analytics Conference. This information will be used to manage attendee registrations, preferences, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`): This is the main section where you'll provide your basic attendee information.
	+ Enter your name, company, and other details as required.
* **Attendee Preferences** (`attendee_preferences`): Here, you can specify your preferences for the conference, such as topics or speakers you'd like to see.
	+ Please provide a brief description of your preferences.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`): This section allows you to select sessions that interest you.
	+ You can choose from a list of sessions available at the conference.
* **Consent** (`consent`): You must agree to the conference terms and conditions.
	+ Check the box to confirm your consent.
* **Email** (`email`): Provide your email address for conference communication.
	+ Use a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
	+ You can also use this to receive important conference updates.
* **Name** (`name`): Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
	+ This will help us to address you correctly.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name (if applicable).
	+ This will help us to understand your affiliation.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you need to update your information, contact the conference organizers promptly.
