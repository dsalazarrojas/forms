# Program Agreement And Financial Disclosure - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Program Agreement And Financial Disclosure" form is a mandatory document that requires participants to agree to the terms and conditions of a program and disclose their financial information. This form is used to ensure that participants understand and accept the program's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the Terms and Conditions section carefully and agree to its contents.
2. Provide your financial information as prompted.
3. Enter your participant details, including your name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Acknowledge that you have read and understood the program terms.
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Agreement And Financial Disclosure** (`program_agreement_and_financial_disclosure`, text, required): This is the main section where you will agree to the terms and conditions of the program. Please type your agreement in this text field.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required): This section outlines the rules and regulations of the program. Please read it carefully and agree to its contents.
* **Financial Disclosure** (`financial_disclosure`, text, required): This is where you will provide your financial information as prompted.
* **Acknowledgement** (`acknowledgement`, note, required): Please acknowledge that you have read and understood the program's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, select_one, optional): Please select "Yes" if you wish to participate in the program.
* **Program Terms** (`program_terms`, select_multiple, optional): Please select all the terms that apply to your participation in the program.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the program you are participating in.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): If the program has a start date, enter it here.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): If the program has an end date, enter it here.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your participant account.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Program Terms Acknowledgement** (`program_terms_acknowledgement`, note, optional): Please acknowledge that you have read and understood the program terms.
* **Financial Disclosure Acknowledgement** (`financial_disclosure_acknowledgement`, note, optional): Please acknowledge that you have read and understood the financial disclosure information.

## Tips
* Make sure to read the Terms and Conditions section carefully before agreeing to its contents.
* Use a valid email address and phone number (if applicable) to ensure that you can be contacted by the program administrators.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have provided all required information.
