# Speed Networking With Experts Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Speed Networking with Experts event. Please provide the required details to help us organize the event according to your preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Specify your goals for attending the event.
3. Choose your preferred time for the event (in 24-hour format).
4. Select your preferred date for the event.
5. Select your preferred day of the week for the event.
6. Choose your preferred hour for the speed networking session.
7. Select your preferred minute for the minute-long speed networking session.
8. Select your preferred day for the speed networking session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details**: This is the section where you will fill in your name, email, and phone number.
* **Goals**: Please enter your goals for attending the event. This will help us understand your motivations and preferences.
* **Preferred Time**: Enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM) when you would like to attend the event.
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date you prefer to attend the event. Please note that the date will be the main day of the event.
* **Preferred Day**: Choose a day of the week that you prefer to attend the event. You can select multiple days if you wish.
* **Preferred Hour**: Select the hour you prefer to attend the speed networking session.
* **Preferred Minute**: Select the minute you prefer to attend the speed networking session.
* **Speed Networking with Experts Registration Form**: This is a select-one field where you can choose the type of registration form you need. Please note that you can choose "Other" if your preferred form is not listed.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and complete.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the "Goals" field.
* If you need help or have any questions, please don't hesitate to contact us.
