# paddleboard_lesson_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for registering for a paddleboard lesson.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a date for your lesson in the "Date" field.
6. Select a time for your lesson in the "Time" field.
7. Enter the name of your instructor in the "Instructor Name" field.
8. Select the type of lesson you'd like to take in the "Lesson Type" field.
9. Enter the number of students you'd like to attend in the "Number of Students" field.
10. Enter the number of lessons you'd like to attend in the "Number of Lessons" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Select a date for your lesson. Please use the format `DD/MM/YYYY` or `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select a time for your lesson. Please use the 24-hour format `HH:MM`.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required/optional): If you have a preferred instructor, enter their name here.
* **Lesson Type** (`lesson_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of lesson you'd like to take:
	+ Basic
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Number of Students** (`number_of_students`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of students you'd like to attend with you.
* **Number of Lessons** (`number_of_lessons`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of lessons you'd like to attend.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate, as we'll use it to contact you about your lesson.
* Make sure to choose the correct lesson type for your skill level.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please contact us before submitting this form.
* We'll be in touch with you shortly after submission to confirm your lesson details.
