# yoga_studio_membership_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users register or update their membership profile in the yoga studio. It collects essential information to create a customized membership profile.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Select the membership type you are applying for (Monthly or Annual).
2.  Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
3.  Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4.  Enter your phone number for contact and follow-up communication.
5.  Choose the start date of your membership.
6.  Choose the end date of your membership (if applicable).
7.  Select the payment method you'd like to use (Credit Card or PayPal).
8.  Enter the payment amount for your membership.
9.  Add any additional comments or notes about your membership.
10.  Choose the tool that you'd like to be assigned for your membership (Chatjimmy or Other).
11.  Select the category for your membership (Registration Forms or Membership Forms).
12.  Optionally, provide more details in the "Description" field if necessary.
13.  Upload any supporting files or information for your membership.
14.  Confirm your Form ID for verification purposes.
15.  Finally, confirm the category for this membership (Registration Forms or Membership Forms).
