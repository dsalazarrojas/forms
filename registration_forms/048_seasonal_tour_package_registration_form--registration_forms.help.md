# seasonal_tour_package_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a registration form for seasonal tour packages. It allows you to provide information about the traveler and their tour package details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with your information.
2. Double-check that you have filled in all required fields.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Traveler Name** (`traveler_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the traveler registering for the package.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the trip.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the trip.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, Text, Optional): Enter the travel agency associated with the package.
* **Booking Agent** (`booking_agent`, Text, Optional): Enter the booking agent associated with the package.
* **Package ID** (`package_id`, Number, Optional): Enter the ID of the package being registered.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the tour operator or contact person for clarification.
