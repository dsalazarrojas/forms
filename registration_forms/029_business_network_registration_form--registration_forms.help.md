# Business Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from business owners or representatives who wish to join the business network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. Ensure that all required fields are filled out to ensure successful registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Your Name
* **Your Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
### 2. Email Address
* **Email Address** (`registrant_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address.
### 3. Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`registrant_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
### 4. Company Name
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your business name.
### 5. Company Address
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, required): Enter your business address.
### 6. Company Website
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): Enter your business website URL (if applicable).
### 7. Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
### 8. Industry
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the category of your business from the list.
### 9. Business Description
* **Business Description** (`business_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your business.
### 10. Years in Business
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, number, required): Enter the number of years your business has been operating.
### 11. Network Type Interest
* **Network Type Interest** (`network_type_interest`, select_one, required): Select the type of networking you prefer (e.g., online platform, in-person events, etc.).
### 12. Networking Goals
* **Networking Goals** (`networking_goals`, select_multiple, required): Select the goals you hope to achieve through the network (e.g., find new clients, find partners, etc.).
### 13. Referral Capacity
* **Referral Capacity** (`referral_capacity`, select_one, required): Indicate your capacity for providing referrals to other network members.
### 14. Membership Tier
* **Membership Tier** (`membership_tier`, select_one, required): Select the membership level you wish to join (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium, etc.).
### 15. Profile Visibility Preference
* **Profile Visibility Preference** (`profile_visibility`, select_one, required): Choose who can see your profile.
### 16. Communication Preferences
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select how you prefer to receive updates (e.g., email newsletters, event invitations, etc.).
### 17. Social Media Profiles
* **Social Media Profiles** (`social_media_links`, text, optional): Enter your social media profiles (if applicable).
### 18. Your Specialties
* **Your Specialties** (`specialties`, text, optional): List your specialties (if applicable).
### 19. What Are You Looking For
* **What Are You Looking For** (`looking_for`, text, optional): Describe the connections you are looking to make (if applicable).
### 20. How Did You Hear About This Network
* **How Did You Hear About This Network** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_one, optional): Select where you heard about the network (e.g., referral, online search, etc.).
### 21. Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the network.
