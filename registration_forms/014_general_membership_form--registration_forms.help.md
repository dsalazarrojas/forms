# General Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Membership Form is designed to collect general information about new members, including their contact details, membership type, and payment information. This form is intended for both individual and family registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Registration** field with your membership title.
2. In the **Member Details** field, enter your name and other relevant details.
3. Select your **Membership Type** from the dropdown menu.
4. In the **Contact Info** field, enter your contact information.
5. In the **Member Address** field, enter your address.
6. In the **Member Phone** field, enter your phone number.
7. In the **Member Email** field, enter your email address.
8. Select your **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu.
9. Enter the **Payment Amount**.
10. In the **Member Address** field, enter your address.
11. In the **Member Level** field, enter your member level.
12. In the **Membership Status** field, enter your membership status.
13. In the **Notes** field, enter any additional notes.
14. If you have selected **Payment Method** as **Card**, enter the **Payment Method Details**.
15. Select the **Expiration Date** of your membership.
16. Select the **Renewal Date** of your membership.
17. Enter the **Fees** associated with your membership.
18. In the **Member Level Notes** field, enter any notes about your member level.
19. In the **Membership Status Notes** field, enter any notes about your membership status.
20. In the **Member Notes** field, enter any additional information about your membership.
