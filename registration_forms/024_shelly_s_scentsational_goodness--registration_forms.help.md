# Shelly's Scentsational Goodness - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for party hosts to submit their party details to the party planning system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Party Name** in the field labeled "Party Name".
2. Select a **Form Type** from the dropdown menu to specify the type of party you are hosting.
3. Enter the **Guest Count**, **Party Date**, and **Party Time** to provide more information about your party.
4. Enter the **Party Location** in the field labeled "Party Location".
5. Provide your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** to help us get in touch with you.
6. Enter any additional **Party Details** in the field labeled "Party Details".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Name**: The name of your party. This will help us identify your event.
* **Party Date**: The date of your party. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Party Time**: The time of your party. Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **Guest Count**: The estimated number of guests attending your party.
* **Party Location**: The location of your party.
* **Contact Email**: Your email address so we can contact you for further information.
* **Contact Phone**: Your phone number so we can contact you for further information.
* **Party Details**: Any additional information you'd like to share about your party.
* **Form Type**: The type of party you're hosting (Party 1, Party 2, or Party 3).
