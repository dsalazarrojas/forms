# cotc_nursing_information_meeting - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cotc_nursing_information_meeting form is a registration form for collecting nursing meeting information. It is used to capture details about meetings involving nurses, including meeting title, description, time, date, location, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting title in the **Meeting Title** field.
2. Provide a brief description of the meeting in the **Meeting Description** field.
3. Select the meeting time in the **Meeting Time** field using the date and time format (e.g., 2023-03-01 14:30).
4. Select the meeting date in the **Meeting Date** field using the date format (e.g., 2023-03-01).
5. Enter the meeting location in the **Meeting Location** field.
6. Enter your registration name in the **Registration Name** field.
7. Enter your registered email address in the **Registered Email** field.
8. Enter your registered phone number in the **Registered Phone** field.
9. Select your registered professions from the **Registered Professions** field (e.g., Nurse Practitioner, Nurse Administrator, or Nurse Specialist).
10. If you have any other registered professions, enter them in the **Others** field (optional).
11. Enter any additional comments about your registration in the **Registered Comments** field (optional).
12. Confirm that you agree to the registration terms by selecting **I Agree** in the **Registered Agree** field.
13. Confirm that you agree to the meeting terms by selecting **I Agree** in the **Meeting Agree** field.
14. Confirm that you agree to the meeting terms by selecting **I Agree** in the **Meeting Terms** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Title** (`meeting_title`, text, required): Enter a brief title of the meeting.
* **Meeting Description** (`meeting_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required): Select the time of the meeting using the date and time format (e.g., 2023-03-01 14:30).
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required): Select the date of the meeting using the date format (e.g., 2023-03-01).
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the meeting.
* **Registration Name** (`registration_name`, text, required): Enter your name for registration purposes.
* **Registered Email** (`registered_email`, email, required): Enter your registered email address.
* **Registered Phone** (`registered_phone`, text, required): Enter your registered phone number.
* **Registered Professions** (`registered_professions`, select_multiple, required): Select your registered professions (e.g., Nurse Practitioner, Nurse Administrator, or Nurse Specialist).
* **Others** (`registered_professions_others`, text, optional): If you have any other registered professions, enter them here.
* **Registered Comments** (`registered_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your registration.
* **Registered Agree** (`registered_agree`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the registration terms.
* **Meeting Agree** (`meeting_agree`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the meeting terms.
* **Meeting Terms** (`meeting_terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the meeting terms.
