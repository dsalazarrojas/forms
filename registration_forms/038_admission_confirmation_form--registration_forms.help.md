# Admission Confirmation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Admission Confirmation Form is a crucial step in the admission process for students enrolling in a program or course of study. This form helps ensure that you, as a student, have all the necessary information and requirements confirmed before starting your academic journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful answers.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields.
4. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Admission Confirmation --** (`confirmation_header`, Note, Not required): This section serves as a heading for the form and provides a clear indication of what the form is about.
* **Full Name** (`student_full_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`student_email`, Email, Required): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes. This will be used by the institution for important updates and announcements.
* **Phone Number** (`student_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number to ensure we can reach you for any updates or questions.
* **Address** (`student_address`, Text, Required): Provide your current residential address.
* **City** (`student_city`, Text, Required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`student_state`, Text, Required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`student_zip`, Text, Required): Enter the ZIP or postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`student_country`, Text, Required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Required): Enter your birth date.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, Text, Required): Enter your assigned student identification number.
* **Program Enrolled In** (`program_enrolled_in`, Text, Required): Choose the program you are enrolling in.
* **Degree Level** (`degree_level`, Select One, Required): Choose the level of degree you are pursuing.
* **Program Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date you start your program.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`expected_graduation_date`, Date, Optional): If you know the date you expect to graduate, please enter it.
* **Admission Status** (`admission_status`, Select One, Required): Select your current admission status.
* **Enrollment Confirmation** (`enrollment_confirmation`, Select One, Required): Confirm your enrollment in this program.
* **Housing Request** (`housing_request`, Select One, Optional): If you require on-campus housing, please select 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Financial Aid Status** (`financial_aid_status`, Select One, Optional): Select your financial aid status.
* **Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, Text, Optional): If you require any special accommodations, please enter the details.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Required): Enter the relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Confirmation Date** (`confirmation_date`, Date, Required): Enter the date and time you submit this form.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, Text, Required): Electronic signature to confirm your enrollment.

Note: The asterisk \* indicates a required field. Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
