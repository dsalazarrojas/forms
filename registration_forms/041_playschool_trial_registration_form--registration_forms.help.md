# Playschool Trial Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Playschool Trial Registration Form is a tool for parents or guardians to provide necessary information about their child and themselves for the registration process. This includes details about the child's name, date of birth, parent-child relationship, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's name in the "child Name" field.
2. Provide your child's date of birth in the "child DOB" field.
3. Select the correct gender of your child from the "child Gender" options.
4. Enter your name in the "Parent Name" field.
5. Indicate your relationship with the child in the "Parent Relationship" field.
6. Enter your contact information in the "Parent Contact" field.
7. Provide the name of the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Name" field.
8. Describe the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person in the "Emergency Relationship" field.
9. Enter the emergency contact phone number in the "Emergency Contact Number" field.
10. Confirm if you give consent for your child to attend the playschool.
11. Enter the date you give consent in the "Consent Date" field.
12. Enter the time you give consent in the "Consent Time" field.
13. Add any additional notes about your child in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **child Name** (`child_name`, text, required: false): Enter your child's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **child DOB** (`child_dob`, date, required: false): Enter your child's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **child Gender** (`child_gender`, text, required: false): Select the gender of your child from the options provided.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Relationship** (`parent_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe your relationship with the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian, etc.).
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`, text, required: false): Enter your contact information so we can reach you if needed.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required: false): Describe the relationship between the child and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter the contact phone number for the emergency contact person.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one, required: false): Confirm if you give consent for your child to attend the playschool.
* **Consent Date** (`consent_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you give consent for your child to attend the playschool.
* **Consent Time** (`consent_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you give consent for your child to attend the playschool.
* **Consent Notes** (`consent_notes`, text, required: false): Add any additional notes about giving consent.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`notes`, text, required: false): Provide any notes or comments for the emergency contact person.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
- This form is for administrative purposes only, and the information collected will be used for the child's registration process.
