# Pre Tryout Club Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for joining the Pre Tryout Club. It collects basic information from users to register them for the club.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your information.
2. Ensure that your email address is up-to-date and valid.
3. If your registration is successful, you will be notified by email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required: false): Please enter your first name as it is written on your official documents or ID.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Please enter your last name as it is written on your official documents or ID.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD) as it is written on your official documents or ID.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. This email will be used to send important club updates and information.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to contact you if needed.
* **Player ID** (number, required: false): Enter your player ID (if applicable).
