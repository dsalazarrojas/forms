# Vocational Training Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a student's admission to a vocational training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Program Title** in the designated field.
2. Provide your **Student Name** as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
3. Enter your **Student Email** in the format of [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
4. Enter your **Student Phone** number (optional but recommended for contact purposes).
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the YYYY-MM-DD format (Year-Month-Day).
6. Enter your **Date of Admission** to the program, which is the date you plan to start the training.
7. Select the **Program Level** that best describes your current skill level:
	* **Beginner**: You are new to the program and have no prior experience.
	* **Intermediate**: You have some experience and knowledge of the program subject.
	* **Advanced**: You are an experienced professional with significant knowledge in the program subject.
8. Enter the **Duration of the Program** you wish to enroll in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Title**: The title of the vocational training program you wish to enroll in.
- **Student Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Student Email**: Enter your email address in the format [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
- **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes (optional but recommended).
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (Year-Month-Day).
- **Date of Admission**: Enter the date you plan to start the training program.
- **Program Level**: Select the program level that best describes your current skill level:
	* **Beginner**: No prior experience.
	* **Intermediate**: Some experience and knowledge.
	* **Advanced**: Significant experience and knowledge.
- **Duration of Program**: Enter the duration of the program you wish to enroll in.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the program administrators for guidance.
- Ensure you have all necessary documents and information before completing the form.
