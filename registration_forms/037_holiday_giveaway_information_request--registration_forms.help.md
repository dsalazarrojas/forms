This guide is intended to be a clear and concise help document for the "Holiday Giveaway Information Request" form. This form is used to collect information from participants in a holiday giveaway program. 

The form is designed to gather essential details about each participant, including their name, email, phone number, and relationship with the company. This data will be used to contact the participant and facilitate the giveaway process.

**Holiday Giveaway Information Request - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants in a holiday giveaway program. This information will be used to contact participants and facilitate the giveaway process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the text field provided.
2. Enter your **Participant E-mail** in the email field. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
3. Enter your **Participant Phone** number in the text field provided.
4. Select the **Relationship** you have with the company from the dropdown menu.
5. Submit the form once you have filled out all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`holiday_giveaway_form_1`, text, **not required**): This field is used to enter your name. Please enter your name as it is spelled.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, **not required**): This field is used to enter a valid email address. This will be used to contact you about the giveaway.
* **Participant Phone** (`holiday_giveaway_phone`, text, **not required**): This field is used to enter your phone number. Please enter your phone number as it is formatted.
* **Relationship** (`holiday_giveaway_relationship`, select_one, **not required**): This field is used to select the nature of your relationship with the company. Please choose the option that best describes your relationship. Options include:
	+ Parent
	+ Spouse
	+ Child
	+ Friend
	+ Other
