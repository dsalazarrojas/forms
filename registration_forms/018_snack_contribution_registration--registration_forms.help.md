# Snack Contribution Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a snack contribution for a community or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields as described below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Snack Contribution Registration Title** (`title`): Enter a title for the snack contribution.
* **Contributor Info** (`contributor_info`): Enter information about the person or organization providing the snack contribution, such as their name and contact details.
* **Snack Info** (`snack_info`): Enter details about the snack, including its name and any relevant notes.
* **Serving Info** (`serving_info`): Provide information about where and when the snack will be served.
* **Event Info** (`event_info`): Enter details about the event or context in which the snack will be served.
* **Dietary Info** (`dietary_info`): Enter any dietary restrictions or notes related to the snack contribution.
* **Submit** (`submit`): Review your input and confirm your submission.
* **Error Message** (`error_message`): This field is not visible to the user; it is used to display any errors that occur during submission.
* **Snack Contribution ID** (`snack_contribution_id`): This field is not visible to the user; it is automatically generated and used to track the snack contribution.
* **Contribution Date** (`contribution_date`): Enter the date the snack contribution was made.
* **Serving Date** (`serving_date`): Enter the date the snack will be served.
* **Contributor Name** (`contributor_name`): Enter the name of the person or organization providing the snack contribution.
* **Contributor Email** (`contributor_email`): Enter the email address of the person or organization providing the snack contribution.
* **Contributor Phone** (`contributor_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person or organization providing the snack contribution.
* **Contributor Address** (`contributor_address`): Enter the address of the person or organization providing the snack contribution.
* **Snack Name** (`snack_name`): Enter the name of the snack being contributed.
* **Contribution Amount** (`contribution_amount`): Enter the amount of the snack contribution.
* **Snack Serving Quantity** (`snack_serving_quantity`): Enter the quantity of the snack to be served.
* **Dietary Notes** (`dietary_notes`): Enter any dietary notes or restrictions related to the snack contribution.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or context in which the snack will be served.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event or context in which the snack will be served.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event or context in which the snack will be served.
* **Event Address** (`event_address`): Enter the address of the event or context in which the snack will be served.

Note: Some fields are not visible to the user and are used for internal tracking or other backend purposes.
