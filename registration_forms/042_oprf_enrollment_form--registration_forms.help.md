# oprf_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open Resources for Programmatic Research and Development (OPRD) initiative requires the registration of students and programs for various purposes. This form is designed to collect detailed information about students and programs, which will be stored in the database for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, grade level, and other required fields as prompted.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled correctly.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Select the grade level of the student (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th).
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the program.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the student (if applicable).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the student (if applicable).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the school (if applicable).
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the school (if applicable).
* **Program Email** (`program_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the program (if applicable).
* **Program Phone** (`program_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the program (if applicable).
* **Program Website** (`program_website`, text, required: false): Enter the website of the program (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the teacher.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the school.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the teacher.
* **Program Address** (`program_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the program.
* **Teacher Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required: false): Select the grade level of the teacher (e.g., Elementary, Middle School, High School, College).
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of program (e.g., Day School, Residential, Therapeutic, Other).
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Student DOI** (`student_doi`, date, required: false): Enter the date of entry of the student.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required: false): Select the gender of the student (e.g., Male, Female, Non-binary).
* **Student Ethnicity** (`student_ethnicity`, select_one, required: false): Select the ethnicity of the student (e.g., Asian, Black or African American, Hispanic or Latino, Native American, White).
* **Student Race** (`student_race`, select_one, required: false): Select the race of the student (e.g., Asian, Black or African American, Hispanic or Latino, Native American, White).
* **Student Socioecon Status** (`student_socioecon_status`, select_one, required: false): Select the socioeconomic status of the student (e.g., Low-income, Middle income, Upper income, Not Disclosed).
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the student.

Please note that all fields are optional except for "Student Name". This form is designed to collect detailed information about students and programs for future reference and research.
