# Financial Forecasting Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Forecasting Seminar Registration form is designed to gather essential information from users who wish to participate in the seminar. The form collects data about the user's preferences, expectations, and contact details to facilitate a smooth and productive experience during the seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name and any other relevant details in the **User Input** field.
2. Select your preferred session(s) for the seminar from the **Session Choice** field.
3. Indicate your expectations for the seminar in the **Expectation** field.
4. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Info** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (User Input, text, optional): This field is for you to provide any additional information about yourself that you think is relevant to your participation in the seminar. It's a text field where you can enter up to 255 characters.
* **Session Choice** (Session Choice, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more sessions that you are interested in attending. This field allows you to choose from the available options.
* **Expectation** (Expectation, text, optional): Describe your expectations for the seminar in this text field. This will help us understand what you hope to achieve or gain from the experience.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you before, during, or after the seminar.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Provide your email address to receive updates, reminders, or any other information related to the seminar.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you select at least one session choice.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submission.
* If you have any special requests or accommodations, please mention them in the **Expectation** field.
