# Recreational Running Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants in recreational running events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Make sure to fill out the correct grade level and school for the participant.
3. Enter the correct event date for the participant.
4. Select the correct T-shirt size for the participant.
5. Provide accurate emergency contact information.
6. Indicate if the participant is a volunteer and select the relevant role(s).
7. Fill out any additional information that is required or optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, *): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`4`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Birth Date** (`5`, `date`, *): Enter the participant's birth date.
* **Grade Level** (`6`, `select_one`, *): Select the participant's grade level.
* **School** (`7`, `select_multiple`, *): Select the participant's school.
* **Event Date** (`8`, `date`, not required): Enter the event date, if applicable.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`9`, `select_one`, *): Select the participant's T-shirt size.
* **Notes** (`10`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional information about the participant.
* **Volunteer Status** (`11`, `select_multiple`, not required): Indicate if the participant is a volunteer and select the relevant role(s).
* **Volunteer Role** (`12`, `select_multiple`, not required): Select the volunteer role(s) for the participant.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`13`, `number`, not required): Enter the number of volunteer hours for the participant.
* **Volunteer Notes** (`14`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional information about the participant's volunteer role.
* **Emergency Name** (`15`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Phone** (`16`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`17`, `email`, *): Enter the participant's emergency contact's email address.
* **Guardian Name** (`18`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's guardian's name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`19`, `text`, *): Enter the participant's guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`20`, `email`, *): Enter the participant's guardian's email address.
* **Parent Permission** (`21`, `select_one`, *): Indicate if the parent has given permission.
* **Event Type** (`22`, `select_one`, *): Select the type of event.
* **Event Time** (`23`, `time`, *): Enter the event time.
* **Volunteer Tips** (`24`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional information about the volunteer role.
* **Additional Info** (`25`, `note`, not required): Provide any additional information about the participant.
