# High School Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The High School Camp Registration Form is designed to collect information for students who wish to attend a high school camp program. This form will help the camp organizers to gather essential details about the students, including their personal and academic information, to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Parent's Name and Guardian's Phone number.
4. Select your student's Grade Level (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
5. Enter your student's Age.
6. Choose your student's Gender (Male or Female).
7. Enter your student's Date of Birth and Date of Enrollment.
8. Enter your student's School Name.
9. Enter your student's Phone number and Counselor's Contact Information (if applicable).
10. Provide any additional notes or comments about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's First Name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Last Name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your student's Email address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Parent's Name.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Guardian's Phone number.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required: false): Enter your student's Grade Level (choose from Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, `number`, required: false): Enter your student's Age.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your student's Gender (Male or Female).
* **Student Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`, `date`, required: false): Enter your student's Date of Birth.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, `date`, required: false): Enter your student's Date of Birth (note: this field is redundant and should not be filled).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's School Name.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Phone number.
* **Student Enrollment Date** (`student_enrollment_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your student's Date of Enrollment.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your student's Grade Level (choose from Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, or Senior).
* **Student Counselor** (`student_counselor`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Counselor's Name.
* **Student Counselor Phone** (`student_counselor_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Counselor's Phone number.
* **Other Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Grade Level if it does not match any of the provided options.
* **Other Student Counselor** (`student_counselor_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Counselor's Name if it does not match any of the provided options.
* **Other Student Counselor Phone** (`student_counselor_phone_other`, `text`, required: false): Enter your student's Counselor's Phone number if it does not match any of the provided options.
* **Other Student Grade Level Details** (`student_grade_level_other_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about your student's Grade Level if needed.
* **Other Student Counselor Details** (`student_counselor_other_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about your student's Counselor if needed.
* **Other Student Counselor Phone Details** (`student_counselor_phone_other_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details about your student's Counselor's Phone number if needed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, required: false): This field is for the system's use only and is used to track the form's submission.
