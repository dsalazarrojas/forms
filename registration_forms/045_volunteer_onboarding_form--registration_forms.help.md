# Volunteer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal and contact information from volunteers, allowing the organization to get in touch with them and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Information section with your name, email, and any other relevant personal details.
2. Provide your contact details in the Contact Details section, including your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
3. Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship to you in the Emergency Contact section.
4. Share your volunteer information, including your availability and any other relevant details about your volunteer work.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information (Personal Information)** (`name`, `text`, required): Provide your name, email, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Details (Contact Details)** (`text`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`text`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship to you.
* **Volunteer Information (Volunteer Information)** (`text`, `text`, required): Share your volunteer information, including your availability and any other relevant details about your volunteer work.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Personal Information, Contact Details, and Emergency Contact).
* Use real and accurate information in each field.
