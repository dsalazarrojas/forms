# Virtual Trade Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for a virtual trade show. Please fill in your information accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name
2. Fill in your Last Name
3. Enter a valid Email Address
4. Enter your Phone number
5. Enter your Company name

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or other official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or other official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached by the trade show organizers.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address as this will be used for correspondence related to the trade show.
* If you are a member of the press, please indicate this on your company field.
