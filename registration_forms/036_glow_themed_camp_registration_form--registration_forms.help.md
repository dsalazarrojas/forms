# Glow Themed Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Glow Themed Camp Registration Form is designed to gather necessary information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the event. This form helps us understand participant preferences, medical conditions, and payment details to better serve you during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the registration form with your contact and participant details.
2. Provide information about any emergency contact you have designated for us.
3. Share any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
4. Confirm your consent for the event.
5. Choose your preferred payment method and provide any relevant payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (1)**: This is the main form section where you can provide your participant details, such as name, contact information, etc.
* **Participant Preferences (2)**: If you have any specific needs or requirements for the event, please let us know here.
* **Emergency Contact (3)**: If you have someone we can contact in case of an emergency, provide their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical Conditions (4)**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please describe them here.
* **Consent (5)**: Please confirm that you agree to participate in the event. This is a required field.
* **Special Instructions (6)**: If you need to provide any special instructions for your participation, please describe them here.
* **Additional Comments (7)**: If you have any additional comments or notes, you can provide them here.
* **Payment Method (8)**: Select your preferred payment method, either Cash or Bank transfer.
* **Payment Details (9)**: If you have selected Bank transfer as your payment method, please provide further details here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to complete the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.
