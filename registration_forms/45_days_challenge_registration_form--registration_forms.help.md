# 45 Days Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 45 Days Challenge Registration Form is a required step for participants to register for the 45-day challenge program. It collects personal and goal-oriented information that helps create a tailored plan for each participant.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form, which is divided into sections with labels and hints to guide you through the process.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked with `*` (required) and have no `hint`.
3. Select your preferred options from the dropdown menus.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes in the final field.
5. Finally, submit the form to register for the challenge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Participant full name (*)
* **Participant full name**: This is the name that will be displayed on certificates and leaderboards.

### 2. Participant email address (*)
* **Participant email address**: This is used to send you updates and results about the challenge.

### 3. Participant phone number
* **Participant phone number**: This is optional, but if you provide it, it will be used for urgent contact only.

### 4. Age
* **Age**: Enter your age in whole years.

### 5. Emergency contact name (*)
* **Emergency contact name**: Provide the name of someone who can be contacted in case of an emergency.

### 6. Emergency contact phone number (*)
* **Emergency contact phone**: Provide the phone number of your emergency contact, including the country code if necessary.

### 7. Preferred challenge start date (*)
* **Preferred challenge start date**: Enter your earliest preferred date to start the challenge.

### 8. Current fitness level (*)
* **Current fitness level**: Select your current level of fitness.

### 9. Primary challenge goal (*)
* **Primary challenge goal**: Select your main objective for the challenge.

### 10. Preferred workout types (select all that apply)
* **Preferred workout types**: Select all the workout types that interest you.

### 11. Relevant medical conditions or restrictions
* **Relevant medical conditions or restrictions**: Leave blank if none; trainers will review your form for safety.

### 12. Payment option (*)
* **Payment option**: Choose how you'd like to pay for the program.

### 13. I accept the challenge waiver and terms (*)
* **I accept the challenge waiver and terms**: A required step to participate in the challenge.

### 14. Additional comments, accessibility requests, or notes
* **Additional comments, accessibility requests, or notes**: Share any additional information or schedule constraints with us.
