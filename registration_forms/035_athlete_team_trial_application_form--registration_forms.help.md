# Athlete Team Trial Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for athletes to register for a trial with a sports team. It gathers information about the athlete's personal details, athletic background, and trial preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Start by filling out the personal details section**, which includes:
	* Your full name
	* Date of birth
	* Gender (select from the options provided)
2. **Next, enter your athletic background and experience**, including:
	* Your primary sport
	* Primary position
	* Secondary position (if applicable)
	* Years of competitive experience
	* Most recent team or club
	* Major career achievements and milestones
3. **Provide your physical metrics**, such as:
	* Height (in centimeters)
	* Weight (in kilograms)
4. **Choose your trial preferences**, including:
	* Preferred trial date and time slot
	* Preferred trial location
5. **Enter your emergency contact information**, including:
	* Emergency contact name
	* Emergency contact phone number
6. **Sign off on the form**, by entering your electronic signature and date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`): Enter your full name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_date`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Current School or University** (`school_affiliation`): Enter the name of your current school or university.
* **Primary Sport** (`primary_sport`): Enter the name of your primary sport.
* **Primary Position** (`primary_position`): Enter your primary position in your sport.
* **Secondary Position(s)** (`secondary_position`): If applicable, enter your secondary position(s).
* **Years of Competitive Experience** (`years_competitive`): Enter the number of years you have been competing in sports.
* **Most Recent Team or Club** (`previous_organization`): Enter the name of your most recent team or club.
* **Major Career Achievements and Milestones** (`major_achievements`): Enter a brief description of your major career achievements and milestones.
* **Height - cm** (`athlete_height`): Enter your height in centimeters.
* **Weight - kg** (`athlete_weight`): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Preferred Trial Date and Time Slot** (`trial_date_preference`): Select your preferred trial date and time slot from the provided options.
* **Preferred Trial Location** (`trial_venue`): Enter your preferred trial location.
* **Any existing medical conditions or current injuries?** (`medical_injuries_notes`): Describe any existing medical conditions or current injuries, including any limitations.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Electronic Signature** (`guardian_signature`): Enter the electronic signature of your parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Athlete Electronic Signature** (`athlete_signature`): Enter your electronic signature by typing your full name.
* **Date of Application Submission** (`application_date`): Enter the date of submission of this form.
