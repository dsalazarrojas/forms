# healthcare-training-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants in a healthcare training program. It collects various information about the participants, including their personal details, payment details, and program enrollment status.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section with your personal details.
2. Provide payment details in the payment information section.
3. Indicate your enrollment status in the training program section.
4. Read and agree to the participant agreement terms and conditions.
5. Enter the contact person responsible for handling your registration.
6. Provide your contact details, including phone number and email.
7. Choose the program date and time of your training session.
8. Select your participant type (e.g., new or returning).
9. Indicate your participant level (e.g., high or low).
10. Enter your participant address.
11. Specify the training facility where you'll be attending the training program.
12. Enter the program fee you'll be paying.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (Participant Information): Enter your personal details, such as name and other relevant information.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details): Provide your payment information, such as payment method and amount.
* **Training Program** (Training Program): Indicate whether you're enrolled in the training program or not.
* **Participant Agreement** (Participant Agreement): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the training program.
	+ This field is a note field, so simply read and agree.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person): Enter the name of the person responsible for handling your registration.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Program Date** (Program Date): Choose the date of your training session.
* **Program Time** (Program Time): Choose the time of your training session.
* **Participant Type** (Participant Type): Select whether you're a new or returning participant.
* **Participant Level** (Participant Level): Select your level of expertise or experience (e.g., High or Low).
* **Participant Address** (Participant Address): Enter your address.
* **Training Facility** (Training Facility): Specify the location of your training session.
* **Program Fee** (Program Fee): Enter the program fee you'll be paying.
