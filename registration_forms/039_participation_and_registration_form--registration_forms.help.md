# Participation And Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps below to complete this form:

1.  Enter the participant's name.
2.  Provide contact information, including email, phone, and address.
3.  Answer "Will you attend?" to confirm your attendance.
4.  Share information about your past experience with the events.
5.  Disclose any medical history that should be known.
6.  Agree to the terms and conditions.
7.  Enter your fundraising goal.
8.  Specify the frequency of your contribution.
9.  Enter any participant image or video.
10. Sign the participant signature.
11. Confirm participant attendance.
12. Confirm your contribution amount.
13. Confirm the frequency of your contribution.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Will you attend?** (`event_attendance`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you will attend, "False" otherwise.
* **Fundraising Goal** (`fundraising_goal`, number, required): Enter your fundraising goal.
* **Do you have past experience with the events?** (`past_experience`, select_multiple, required): Answer "True" if you have experience, "False" otherwise.
* **Do you have any medical history that we should know?** (`medical_history`, text, required): Disclose any medical history that should be known.
* **Do you agree with the terms and conditions?** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you agree, "False" otherwise.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information, including email, phone, and address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter event details, such as date and time.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter event name.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter event date.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter event time.
* **Participant Note** (`participant_note`, note, required): Enter any participant note.
* **Participant Additional Info** (`participant_additional_info`, text, required): Enter any additional participant information.
* **Participant Image** (`participant_image`, text, required): Enter any participant image.
* **Participant Video** (`participant_video`, text, required): Enter any participant video.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Enter participant signature.
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, select_one, required): Answer "True" if you will attend, "False" otherwise.
* **Participant Contribution** (`participant_contribution`, number, required): Enter participant contribution amount.
* **Participant Contribution Frequency** (`participant_contribution_frequency`, number, required): Enter frequency of contribution.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter participant name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter participant email.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter participant phone number.
