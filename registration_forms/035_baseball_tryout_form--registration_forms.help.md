# Baseball Tryout Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for baseball players to register for tryouts and gather information for the tryout process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you understand the purpose of each field and its requirements.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player First Name** (`player_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Player Last Name** (`player_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Player Date of Birth** (`player_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Player Age** (`player_age`, number, required): Enter your current age.
* **Player Gender** (`player_gender`, select_one, required): Select your gender (Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to say).
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your complete home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip code of your home address.
* **Tryout Date** (`tryout_date`, select_one, required): Select the date you plan to attend the tryout.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current school (if applicable).
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select your current grade level.
* **Years of Baseball Experience** (`years_experience`, select_one, required): Select how many years you have played baseball.
* **Positions Played** (`positions_played`, select_multiple, required): Select all the positions you have played.
* **Bats** (`bats`, select_one, required): Select the hand you bat with (Right, Left, Switch).
* **Throws** (`throws`, select_one, required): Select the hand you throw with (Right, Left).
* **Previous Team** (`previous_team`, text, optional): Enter the name of your previous team (if applicable).
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies the tryout committee should be aware of.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, text, required): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to the liability waiver.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Select whether you agree to the code of conduct.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): Enter your parent or guardian's signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date your parent or guardian signed the form.
