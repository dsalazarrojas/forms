# Military Appreciation Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register details about Military Appreciation events. It's essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your event is properly registered and communicated to the relevant parties.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as per your official identification documents.
2. **Email**: This field is for your email address. Enter your active and valid email address to ensure that you receive important updates and notifications.
3. **Event Date**: This field is for the date of the Military Appreciation event. Enter the date on which the event will take place.
4. **Guest Count**: This field is for the number of guests attending the event. Enter the actual number of guests expected to attend the event.
5. **Special Informations**: This field is for any additional information you'd like to provide about the event. Feel free to enter any relevant details that may be helpful for the organizers.
6. **Organization Name**: This field is for the name of the organization hosting the event. Enter the name of the organization sponsoring or hosting the event.
7. **Contact Phone**: This field is for your contact phone number. Enter your active and valid phone number to ensure that you receive important updates and notifications.
8. **Event Location**: This field is for the location of the event. Enter the exact location where the event will take place.
9. **Special Message**: This field is for any special messages or comments about the event. Feel free to enter any relevant details that may be helpful for the organizers.
10. **Event Type**: This field is for the type of event. Select one of the options: Military Appreciation, Appreciation Event, or Charity Event.
11. **Event Category**: This field is for the category of the event. Select one or more of the options: Military, Appreciation, Community, Charity, or Other.
12. **Event Theme**: This field is for the theme of the event. Select one of the options: Military, Charity, Community, or Other.
13. **Event Organiser**: This field is for the name of the event organizer. Enter the name of the person organizing the event.
14. **Event Details**: This field is for any additional details about the event. Feel free to enter any relevant information that may be helpful for the organizers.
15. **Special Request**: This field is for any special requests or comments about the event. Feel free to enter any relevant details that may be helpful for the organizers.
16. **Event Date Time**: This field is for the date and time of the event. Enter the date and time of the event.
17. **Event Time**: This field is for the time of the event. This is a separate field for the event time, please enter the time of the event.
18. **Event Duration**: This field is for the duration of the event. Enter the actual duration of the event in hours.
19. **Event Venue**: This field is for the venue of the event. Enter the exact location where the event will take place.
20. **Event Description**: This field is for a brief description of the event. Enter a brief description of the event to give a clear idea about what it's about.
21. **Event Status**: This field is for the status of the event. Select one of the options: Pending, Active, or Ended.
22. **Contact Email**: This field is for the contact email. Enter the active and valid email address to ensure that you receive important updates and notifications.
23. **Contact Phone Number**: This field is for your contact phone number. Enter your active and valid phone number to ensure that you receive important updates and notifications.
24. **Social Link**: This field is for any social media link related to the event. Feel free to enter any social media link that may be helpful for the organizers.
25. **Submit**: This field is for submitting the form. Please review all fields carefully before submitting the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as required) to avoid any issues with form submission.
* Double-check the accuracy of the information entered in each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact the relevant party or the organizers for clarification.
* This form will be reviewed and verified by the relevant parties before processing.
