# Agricultural Skills Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agricultural Skills Tournament Entry Form is a crucial document that collects essential information from participants to prepare for the competition. It includes sections for participant details, event category selection, emergency contact information, and waiver agreement.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Participant Name**: This is your full legal name. Please ensure it matches your government-issued ID.
2.  **Age Group**: Choose your age category from the options: Junior (9-13), Senior (14-18), Young Adult (19-21), or Adult (22+).
3.  **School or Club**: If you are competing as a group, enter your school or 4-H club name.
4.  **Event Categories**: Select all the events you wish to participate in from the list provided. This ensures you will be entered into the corresponding competition.
5.  **Emergency Contact Information**:
    *   **Emergency Contact Name**: Provide the name of your parent or guardian if you are under 18 years old.
    *   **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the best contact number for emergencies.
6.  **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, list them here so we can ensure your safety during the competition.
7.  **Waiver Agreement**: Please select one of the two options: "I Agree" or "Other" (if you have a specific reason not listed).
8.  **Parent/Guardian Signature**: If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian sign this section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name**: A text field to input your full legal name.
*   **Age Group**: A select-one field with age category options.
*   **School or Club**: A text field to input your school or 4-H club name (if applicable).
*   **Event Categories**: A select-multiple field to choose from the list of event categories.
*   **Emergency Contact Name**: A text field for the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone**: A text field for the best contact number for emergencies.
*   **Medical Conditions**: A text field to list any medical conditions or allergies.
*   **Waiver Agreement**: A select-one field with two options: "I Agree" or "Other".
*   **Parent/Guardian Signature**: A text field for a parent or guardian signature (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and safe competition experience.
*   If you are under 18, please ensure a parent or guardian signs the waiver and provides emergency contact information.
*   Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
