# Duck Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Duck Race Registration Form is a tool for participants to register for the duck race event. This form will collect your personal and contact details, as well as your payment preferences, to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address. If you don't have an email address, please use the 'phone' field instead.
2. Choose how you plan to participate in or observe the event by selecting one of the participant types: Duck Rider, Volunteer, or Spectator.
3. Select one or more payment methods you are comfortable with for registration fees. Please note that payment methods include Credit Card, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.
4. Enter your preferred date and time for the event. You can select any available date and time from the given options.
5. Enter your preferred time for the event. Please ensure it's a valid time for the selected date.
6. Leave any fields optional if you don't have any information to provide.
7. Review your submission for accuracy before proceeding to the next step.
8. Click the submit button to finalize your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us identify you during the event.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name to ensure accurate participant records.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you don't have an email address, please provide your phone number instead.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you plan to participate in or observe the event by selecting one of the options: Duck Rider, Volunteer, or Spectator.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the payment method(s) you prefer for your registration. Please note that payment methods include Credit Card, PayPal, and Bank Transfer.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date for the event you'd like to attend. Please ensure it's a valid date for the event.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time for the event you'd like to attend. Ensure it's a valid time for the selected date.
* **Fee** (`fee`, `number`, required/optional): Please note that this field is currently not required for the event. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.

## Tips
* Please review your submission carefully before proceeding to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
* If you're having trouble filling out the form, please refer back to the instructions above for guidance.
