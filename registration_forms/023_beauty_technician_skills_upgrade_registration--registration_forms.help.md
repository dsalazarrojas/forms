# Beauty Technician Skills Upgrade Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Beauty Technician Skills Upgrade Registration form collects essential information from beauty technicians to register them for a skills upgrade program. The form is used to gather detailed information about the technician, their current area of specialization, preferred course schedule, and payment and billing details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Provide your professional license ID in the "Professional License ID" field.
3.  Select your current area of specialization from the "Current Area of Specialization" dropdown menu.
4.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
6.  Select your preferred session month from the "Preferred Start Month" dropdown menu.
7.  Choose your preferred class time from the "Preferred Class Time" dropdown menu.
8.  Choose who will be responsible for the course fee from the "Who will be responsible for the course fee?" dropdown menu.
9.  Enter your billing address in the "Billing Address" field.
10. Confirm your registration by acknowledging the terms of payment in the "I acknowledge that registration is only confirmed upon receipt of deposit" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Professional License ID** (`professional_license_id`, `text`, required): Enter your professional license ID for verification purposes.
* **Current Area of Specialization** (`current_specialization`, `select_one`, required): Select your current area of specialization from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Module Selection** (`module_selection`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the skills upgrade modules you wish to attend.
* **Preferred Start Month** (`preferred_session_month`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred start month for your course.
* **Preferred Class Time** (`preferred_time_slot`, `select_one`, required): Choose the preferred class time for your course.
* **Who will be responsible for the course fee?** (`billing_party`, `select_one`, required): Choose who will be responsible for the course fee.
* **Billing Address** (`billing_address`, `text`, required): Enter your billing address for payment purposes.
* **I acknowledge that registration is only confirmed upon receipt of deposit** (`tuition_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your understanding of the payment terms.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are completed to avoid incomplete registration.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Review the payment and billing section carefully to avoid confusion.
* Confirm your understanding of the payment terms by selecting the correct option.
