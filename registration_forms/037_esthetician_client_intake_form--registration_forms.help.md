# Esthetician Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Esthetician Client Intake Form is a form used to collect information from clients before their appointment. This form helps the esthetician understand the client's medical history, allergies, skincare concerns, and other relevant information to provide the best possible services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and contact number in the provided fields.
2. Describe your medical history in the "Medical History" text field.
3. Select all applicable allergies from the "Allergies" dropdown menu.
4. Choose your primary skincare concern from the "Skincare Concerns" dropdown menu.
5. If you have any medical conditions, enter them in the "Medical Conditions" text field.
6. If you are taking any medications, enter them in the "Medications" text field.
7. If you have a scheduled appointment, select the date and time in the "Next Appointment" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your name, as it will be used to identify you in our records.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number, where we can reach you for further information or to confirm your appointment.
* **Medical History**: Describe any medical conditions or procedures you have had in the past, including dates and details.
* **Allergies**: Select all allergies you have from the provided list, including any food, skin, or environmental allergies.
* **Skincare Concerns**: Choose your primary skincare concern from the dropdown menu. This will help the esthetician tailor their services to your needs.
* **Medical Conditions**: If you have any medical conditions, such as acne, rosacea, or sensitive skin, enter them in this field.
* **Medications**: If you are taking any medications, enter them in this field.
* **Next Appointment**: If you have a scheduled appointment, select the date and time in this field. This will help us confirm your appointment and ensure that we are ready for your visit.

## Tips
- Please be as detailed as possible when filling out the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to ask your esthetician for clarification.
- The information collected on this form will be kept confidential and used only to provide the best possible services for your visit.
