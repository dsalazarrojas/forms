# property_investor_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to provide a detailed description of a property, its status, and contact information for the investor. This form ensures that all relevant property information is captured accurately and consistently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the property details, including the type, value, and status of the property.
2. Select the contact person and their email, phone number, and cell number.
3. Fill in the contact address and property address.
4. Select the agency handling the property.
5. Fill in any additional property documents and terms and conditions.
6. Sign and date the form to confirm the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **property_investor_form** (1): A text field to enter a brief description of the property.
* **contact_details** (2): A text field to enter the contact details of the person filling out the form.
* **property_details** (3): A text field to enter a brief description of the property.
* **contact_address** (4): A text field to enter the address of the contact person.
* **property_address** (5): A text field to enter the address of the property.
* **property_value** (6): A numerical field to enter the value of the property.
* **property_type** (7): A dropdown list to select the type of property (e.g. house, apartment, commercial building).
* **property_status** (8): A dropdown list to select the status of the property (e.g. available, sold, rented).
* **property_agent** (9): A dropdown list to select the agent handling the property (e.g. agent1, agent2).
* **property_investor_agency** (10): A multi-select list to select the agencies handling the property.
* **property_documents** (11): A text field to enter any additional property documents.
* **property_terms** (12): A text field to enter any terms and conditions.
* **contact_person** (13): A text field to enter the name of the contact person.
* **contact_email** (14): An email field to enter the email address of the contact person.
* **contact_phone** (15): A text field to enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **contact_cell** (16): A text field to enter the cell number of the contact person.
* **contact_fax** (17): A text field to enter the fax number of the contact person.
* **property_notes** (18): A text field to enter any additional property notes.
* **agent_notes** (19): A text field to enter any agent notes.
* **contact_notes** (20): A text field to enter any contact notes.
* **property_notes** (21): A text field to enter any property notes.
* **terms_and_conditions** (22): A multi-select list to select whether terms and conditions apply.
* **sign_date** (23): A date field to enter the date the form was signed.
* **sign_signature** (24): A text field to enter the signature of the person signing the form.
* **sign_date_signed** (25): A date field to enter the date the form was signed.
