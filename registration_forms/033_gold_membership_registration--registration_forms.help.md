# gold_membership_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "gold_membership_registration" form is a registration form for joining the membership program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your name: Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Add your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown list.
5. Specify the amount you want to pay for the membership fee.
6. Read and agree to the terms of service by checking the "terms_of_service" field.
7. Decide whether you want to create an account or not by selecting "create_account".
8. Confirm you agree to the terms of service by selecting "agree_terms".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more methods you are comfortable with: credit card, paypal, or bank transfer.
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, optional): Specify the amount you want to pay for the membership fee.
* **Terms of Service** (`terms_of_service`, note, optional): Read and agree to the terms of service by checking this field. (Note: This field is more of a confirmatory action rather than data input.)
* **Create Account** (`create_account`, select_one, optional): Decide whether you want to create an account or not. 
* **Agree to Terms** (`agree_terms`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you agree to the terms of service by selecting this field. 

## Tips

* Please ensure you understand the membership program terms and fees before filling out this form.
* If you choose to create an account, you might be asked to fill out additional fields for account creation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or contact support.
