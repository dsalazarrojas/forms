# Volunteer Shifts Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Shifts Registration Form is used to register for shifts during events. This form is for volunteers to provide their contact information and availability status for specific events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the volunteer name field.
2. Enter your email address in the volunteer email field.
3. Enter your phone number in the volunteer phone number field.
4. Select the date of the event you are available to work on from the event date field.
5. Choose the start and end times for your shift in the shift start and end time fields.
6. Enter the duration of your event in the event duration field.
7. Select your availability status for the shift from the options.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the notes field.
9. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Shifts Registration Form**: This is the title of the form, which explains that this form is for registering shifts.
* **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name to identify yourself as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about shifts and updates.
* **Volunteer Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about shifts and updates.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you are available to work on.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event you are available to work on.
* **Shift Start Time**: Enter the start time of your shift.
* **Shift End Time**: Enter the end time of your shift.
* **Event Duration**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Shift Status**: Select your availability status for the shift (Available/Not Available).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your shift.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration and submit it to us.
