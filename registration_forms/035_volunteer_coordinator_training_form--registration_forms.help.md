# Volunteer Coordinator Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteer coordinators to track their training progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration form with your details.
2. Provide details about the training program you are registering for.
3. Select a training date from the calendar.
4. Choose a training time that suits you.
5. (Optional) Specify the training location if it's not being held at a fixed location.
6. Enter the contact person's information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form (Page 1)
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): This is where you fill in your personal details to register for the training program.
### Training Program Details (Page 2)
* **Training Program Details** (`training_program_details`, text, optional): This is where you can provide any additional details about the training program you are registering for.
### Training Date (Page 3)
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Select a date for the training program from the calendar.
### Training Time (Page 4)
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Choose a time slot for the training program that suits you.
### Training Location (Page 5)
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): If the training program has a fixed location, specify it here.
### Contact Person (Page 6)
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's information, if any.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact us at form@gic.mx.
