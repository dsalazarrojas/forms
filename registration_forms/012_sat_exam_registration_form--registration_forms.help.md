# SAT Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SAT Exam Registration Form is a tool to register students for the SAT exam, collecting their individual and exam-related information for processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the SAT Exam Registration Form, follow these steps:
1. Begin with the "Student Information" page.
2. On this page, fill in the student's details in the required fields.
3. Proceed to the "Guardian Information" page.
4. Fill in the guardian's details if applicable.
5. Move on to the "Exam Details" page.
6. Fill in any specific exam details if needed.
7. Finally, review your entries on the "Payment Details" page and make any necessary adjustments.
8. Once you have reviewed your entries, click "Submit" to send the completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Information (Student Information)**:
    A field to enter the student's name and other identifying details.
- **Guardian Information (Guardian Information)**:
    A field to enter the student or guardian's contact information, only required if applicable.
- **Exam Details (Exam Details)**:
    A field to specify any particular details regarding the exam, if needed.
- **Payment Details (Payment Details)**:
    A field to enter payment specifics, such as method or date.
- **Submit (Submit)**:
    The final step to send the completed form.

## Tips
- Review each field carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out completely.
- Double-check for any mistakes before clicking "Submit".
