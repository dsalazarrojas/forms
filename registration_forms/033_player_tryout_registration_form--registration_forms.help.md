# Player Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about players who are trying out for a team. It gathers essential details about the player, including team affiliation, player contact information, emergency contact information, and player demographics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the team's name in the **Team Name** field.
2. Fill in the player's **Player Name** field.
3. Enter the date and time of the tryout in the **Date of Tryout** and **Time of Tryout** fields respectively.
4. Choose the type of tryout from the **Tryout Type** dropdown menu.
5. Select the player's position from the **Player Position** dropdown menu.
6. Enter the player's contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.
7. Enter the player's contact email address in the **Contact Email** field.
8. If applicable, describe the player's experience in the **Player Experience** field.
9. Provide the emergency contact's name in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
10. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
11. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the **Emergency Contact Email** field.
12. Describe the relationship between the player and the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Relationship** field.
13. Enter the emergency contact's address in the **Emergency Contact Address** field.
14. Enter the emergency contact's city in the **Emergency Contact City** field.
15. Enter the emergency contact's state in the **Emergency Contact State** field.
16. Enter the emergency contact's zip code in the **Emergency Contact Zip** field.
17. Enter the player's height and weight in the **Player Height** and **Player Weight** fields respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): This is the name of the team the player is trying out for.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required): This is the name of the player submitting the form.
* **Date of Tryout** (`date_of_tryout`, date, required): This is the date of the tryout the player is attending.
* **Time of Tryout** (`time_of_tryout`, time, required): This is the time of the tryout the player is attending.
* **Tryout Type** (`tryout_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' if the player is attending the tryout for the first time, 'No' if they are attending for the second time or more.
* **Player Position** (`player_position`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable positions the player can perform.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): This is the player's contact phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): This is the player's contact email address.
* **Player Experience** (`player_experience`, note, optional): This is an optional field for players to describe their experience, if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): This is the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): This is the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): This is the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): This describes the relationship between the player and the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): This is the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, optional): This is the city of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`emergency_contact_state`, text, optional): This is the state of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (`emergency_contact_zip`, text, optional): This is the zip code of the emergency contact person.
* **Player Height** (`player_height`, text, optional): This is the player's height.
* **Player Weight** (`player_weight`, text, optional): This is the player's weight.

Please note that some fields are only applicable in certain situations, and your input will help us tailor our services to your specific needs. Thank you for your cooperation.
