# School Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The School Registration Form is used for students to register for a course. This form will ask for personal and course information to ensure that each student's data is accurate and up-to-date. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that you do not duplicate or misrepresent your information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your first name, last name, and email address on pages 1-3.
2. Select your school ID from the dropdown list on page 4.
3. Choose your class subjects on page 6.
4. Enter the class date, class time, and student ID on pages 12, 13, and 18-20.
5. Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on any official school records.
* **Last Name** (2, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on any official school records.
* **Email** (3, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (4, required): Enter your phone number as you would like it to appear on any official school records.
* **School ID** (5, optional): Select your school ID number from the dropdown list.
* **Subjects** (6, optional): Choose the classes you are interested in taking by selecting multiple options from the list.
* **Date** (7, optional): Enter the date you would like to register for the class (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (8, required): Enter the time you would like to attend the class (format: HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Note** (9, optional): Add any additional information or comments about your registration.
* **Email** (10, optional): Enter an alternate email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (11, optional): Enter an alternate phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Class ID** (12, required): Enter your class ID number.
* **Class Time** (13, required): Enter the time of your class (format: HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Class Subjects** (14, optional): Choose the classes you are taking by selecting multiple options from the list.
* **Class Notes** (15, required): Add any additional information or comments about your class registration.
* **Class Date** (16, required): Enter the date of your class (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Class Phone** (17, required): Enter your phone number as you would like it to appear on any official class records.
* **Student ID** (18, required): Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Time** (19, required): Enter the time of your student ID (format: HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Student Subjects** (20, optional): Choose the classes you are taking by selecting multiple options from the list.
* **Student Notes** (21, required): Add any additional information or comments about your student registration.
* **Student Date** (22, required): Enter the date of your student registration (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Student Phone** (23, required): Enter your phone number as you would like it to appear on any official student records.
* **Student Class ID** (24, required): Enter your student class ID number.
* **Student Class Time** (25, required): Enter the time of your student class (format: HH:MM AM/PM).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to ensure accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact the school administration office.
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
