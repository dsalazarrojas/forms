# product training session confirmation form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to confirm the participation of individuals in a product training session. It collects information about the participant, including their name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name in the "participant name" field.
2. Enter the participant's email address in the "participant email" field.
3. Optionally, provide the participant's phone number in the "participant phone" field.
4. Choose the status of the participant (confirmed or not confirmed) from the "participant status" field.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant in the "notes" field.
6. Provide additional information about the participant in the "additional info" field.
7. If the participant's status has changed, update their status accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **participant name** (text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
- **participant email** (email, required): Enter the email address of the participant.
- **participant phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the participant.
- **training session date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the training session.
- **training session time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the training session.
- **participant status** (select_one, optional): Choose the status of the participant (confirmed or not confirmed).
- **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant.
- **additional info** (text, optional): Provide additional information about the participant.
- **participant status update** (text, optional): If the participant's status has changed, enter the updated status.
- **participant status update date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the status update.
- **participant status update time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the status update.
- **participant status update reason** (text, optional): Enter the reason for the status update.
- **participant status update notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the status update.
- **participant status update status** (select_one, optional): Choose the status update status (updated or not updated).
- **participant status update type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of status update (initial or follow up).
- **participant status update product** (text, optional): Enter the product related to the status update.
- **participant status update product status** (select_one, optional): Choose the status of the product (in progress or completed).
- **participant status update product type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of product update (product or non-product update).
- **participant status update by** (text, optional): Enter the person or system that updated the participant's status.
- **participant status update who** (text, optional): This field is a duplicate of "participant status update by".
