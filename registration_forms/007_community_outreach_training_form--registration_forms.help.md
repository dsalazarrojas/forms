# Community Outreach Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for community outreach training sessions. It will help us tailor the training to your needs and ensure that you receive relevant materials and updates. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, including your name and contact information.
2. Choose the training session that best suits your needs.
3. Select your preferred training format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
4. Indicate your level of experience with outreach training.
5. Specify your training objectives and any accessibility needs you may have.
6. If applicable, provide information about your employer's sponsorship and any dietary restrictions you may have.
7. Confirm whether you need certification upon completion of the training.
8. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: This section is for your personal details, including first and last name, email address, phone number, and date of birth. Please provide accurate information so we can get in touch with you for training updates and materials.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Provide a valid email address where you can receive training materials and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you for updates or in-person training arrangements.
* **Date of Birth**: If you are concerned about your date of birth, you may choose not to provide it, but please be aware that it may affect our ability to tailor the training to your needs.
* **Organization Affiliation**: If you have an organization affiliation, provide it here.
* **Job Title or Role**: Enter your job title or role within your organization.
* **Training Selection**: Choose the training session that best aligns with your needs and interests.
* **Training Program**: Select the training program that you would like to participate in.
* **Preferred Training Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the training session.
* **Training Format**: Choose your preferred training format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Experience Level**: Indicate your level of experience with outreach training.
* **What Do You Hope to Learn**: Specify your objectives for the training session.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: If you have any special needs, such as wheelchair accessibility, provide them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have dietary restrictions, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, please let us know.
* **How Did You Hear About This Training**: Choose how you learned about this training opportunity.
* **Is Your Employer Sponsoring This Training**: Confirm whether your employer is sponsoring your training.
* **Do You Need Certification Upon Completion**: Confirm whether you need certification upon completion of the training.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Use this space to add any additional comments or questions you may have about the training session.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you are attending an in-person training, provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Certification Needed**: This is not a required field but may be relevant for in-person training attendees.
