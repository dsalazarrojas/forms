# Porphyria Community Connection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the Porphyria Community Connection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Registration Form" field with your information (e.g. event name, description, etc.).
2. If you have a name, fill in the "name" field. If not, you can skip it.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Select a date for the event or appointment in the "date" field.
5. Choose a time for the event or appointment in the "time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): This is the main field where you can fill in any relevant information about the event or appointment.
* **name** (`name`, text, optional): If you want to be recognized by your name, fill this in. Otherwise, it's not necessary.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address here so we can get in touch with you.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): Choose a date for the event or appointment.
* **time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time for the event or appointment.
