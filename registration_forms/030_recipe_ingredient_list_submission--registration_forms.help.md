# recipe_ingredient_list_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "recipe_ingredient_list_submission" form is used to collect information about ingredients used in a recipe, including personal and recipe-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Email**, **Contact Phone**, **Ingredient Count**, and **Submission Date** and **Submission Time**.
2. Select the status of your submission from the **Submission Status** field. You can choose from a range of options, including **Active**, **Inactive**, **New**, **Published**, **Archived**, **Spam**, **Denied**, **Trash**, and **Review**.
3. If applicable, add a **Submission Comment** to provide additional context.
4. If you have an image related to the submission, you can add it to the **Ingredient Image** field.
5. If you have a URL related to the submission, you can add it to the **Submission URL** field.
6. In the **Ingredients** field, select the relevant options from the list.
7. Fill in the **Ingredient Name**, **Ingredient Description**, **Ingredient Quantity**, and **Ingredient Unit** fields with the respective information.
