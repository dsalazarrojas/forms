# Registration Form For Training Children - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians of children for training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for your child.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`): Enter your name and contact information as a parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact** (`parent_contact`): Enter your contact information, including phone and email.
* **Child Information** (`child_info`): Enter your child's name, age, and other relevant details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`): Provide any known medical history or conditions of your child.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Indicate if your child has any medical conditions (Yes/No).
* **Allergies** (`allergy`): Indicate if your child has any allergies (Yes/No).
* **Insurance** (`insurance`): Enter your child's insurance details (if applicable).
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your child's address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`emergency_contact_info`): Enter your contact information as an emergency contact for your child.
* **Medical Attention** (`medical_attention`): Enter your child's medical attention information (e.g., doctor's visits, hospitalization).
* **Emergency Contact Method** (`contact_method`): Indicate if you have given permission for emergency contact (Yes/No).
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`): Indicate if your child is enrolled in a training program (Yes/No).
* **Training Level** (`training_level`): Select the training level your child is in (High/Low).
* **Training Group** (`training_group`): Select the training group your child is in (Yes/No).
* **Date of Last Training** (`date_of_last_training`): Enter the date of your child's last training session.
* **Next Training Session** (`next_training_session`): Enter the next training session date for your child.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`): Enter the location of your child's training.
* **Training Cost** (`training_cost`): Enter the cost associated with your child's training.
* **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`): Indicate if your child attends training regularly (Yes/No).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your payment method for your child's training (Yes/No).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select your payment status for your child's training (Active/Inactive).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the amount paid for your child's training.
