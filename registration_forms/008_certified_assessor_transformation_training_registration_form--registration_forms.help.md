# Certified Assessor Transformation Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect registration information for the Certified Assessor Transformation Training program. It will ask for contact and personal details, preferred training options, and other relevant information to ensure that the training meets your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

- Carefully read each section and label carefully.
- Ensure you have all required fields filled out before submitting.
- Use the provided hints for guidance when filling out each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the training team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (`training_participant_full_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as you wish it to appear on your certificate.
* **Participant Email Address** (`training_participant_email`, Email, Required): Enter your email address for training updates and materials.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`training_participant_phone`, Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Current Job Title** (`participant_current_job_title`, Text, Required): Enter your current job title.
* **Organization or Company Name** (`participant_organization_name`, Text, Required): Enter your organization or company name.
* **Training Level** (`transformation_training_level`, Select One, Required): Select the training level you are registering for (Level 1: Foundational Assessor, Level 2: Advanced Assessor, Level 3: Lead Transformation Assessor, or Recertification Training).
* **Training Location Preference** (`transformation_training_location`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred training delivery method (In-Person, Regional Satellite Office, Live Virtual Workshop, or Self-Paced Online).
* **Preferred Training Cycle** (`preferred_training_date_cycle`, Select One, Required): Select the upcoming cycle you wish to join (Quarter 1, Quarter 2, Quarter 3, or Quarter 4).
* **Years of Assessment Experience** (`years_of_assessment_experience`, Number, Required): Enter the number of years you have worked in an assessor role.
* **Current Assessor Certifications** (`current_assessor_certifications`, Text, Optional): List any related certifications you currently hold.
* **Primary Assessment Domain Focus** (`primary_assessment_domain_focus`, Select One, Required): Select the area you primarily assess (Technical and IT, Operational and Quality, Leadership and Management, Soft Skills and Behavioral, Compliance and Regulatory, or Educational and Academic).
* **Dietary Requirements (In-Person Only)** (`dietary_requirements_preferences`, Text, Optional): If attending an in-person training, list any food allergies or dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_needs_training`, Text, Optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method_training`, Select One, Required): Select how your training will be funded (Corporate Sponsorship, Personal Credit Card, Scholarship Awarded, or Government Funding Grant).
* **Training Attendance Agreement** (`training_attendance_agreement`, Select One, Required): Select whether you agree to attend all scheduled modules for the selected training level.
* **Registration Date** (`training_registration_date`, Date, Required): This field will be automatically populated with today's date.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source_training`, Select One, Optional): If you were referred to this training by someone, select their source (Website, Social Media, Colleague Referral, Professional Organization, Internal HR Announcement, or Other).
* **Additional Needs and Requirements** (`section_additional_needs`, Note, Optional): If you have any additional needs or requirements, please list them in this section.
* **Enrollment Confirmation** (`section_enrollment_confirmation`, Note, Optional): This section confirms your enrollment.
