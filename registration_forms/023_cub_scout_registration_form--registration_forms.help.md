# Cub Scout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for Cub Scouting registration purposes, including demographic data and scouting-related details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your name and email address.
2. Enter your phone number and address (optional).
3. Enter your scout's name (optional).
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Select your scout group.
6. (Optional) Enter your parent's name and relation.
7. (Optional) Enter the name of the camp or location (if applicable).
8. (Optional) Enter your scout rank.
9. Add any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text, required): Enter your name in the required field to identify you.
* **email** (email, required): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **address** (text, optional): Enter your address if you want to provide it for logistics purposes.
* **scout_name** (text, optional): Enter your scout's name if they are registered.
* **date_of_birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification.
* **scout_group** (select_one, optional): Select your scout group from the options provided.
* **parent_name** (text, optional): Enter your parent's name if they will be responsible.
* **parent_relation** (select_one, optional): Select the relation between you and your parent.
* **camp_name** (text, optional): Enter the camp or location name (if applicable).
* **scout_rank** (text, optional): Enter your scout rank if registered.
* **notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
