# cyber_competition_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cyber_competition_registration_form is used to collect information from participants who wish to register for the Cyber Competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be used to contact you regarding competition details.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are experiencing issues with submitting the form, please try refreshing the page or contacting the competition administrator.
- Make sure to review and submit the form carefully before proceeding to avoid errors.
