# Hospital Supply Chain Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in participating in the Hospital Supply Chain Innovation Forum. The form gathers information about registrants' roles and hospital affiliations to facilitate networking and idea sharing during the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the registration process.
2. Fill in your **Name**, **Surname**, and **Email** fields with your contact details.
3. Select your **Role** from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your hospital's **Name** and **Department** (if applicable).
5. Choose a **Date** and **Time** for your participation in the forum.
6. Optionally, add any **Notes** about your participation or interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your given name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, optional): Enter your surname as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your professional email address for communication purposes.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select one of the following roles:
	+ Hospital Manager
	+ Healthcare Professional
* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital you are affiliated with.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or unit you work in (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose a date for your participation in the forum.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time for your participation in the forum.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation or interests.

## Tips
* Ensure you enter accurate and up-to-date information for a smooth registration process.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please indicate them in the **Notes** field.
* You will receive a confirmation email with further instructions once you submit this form.
