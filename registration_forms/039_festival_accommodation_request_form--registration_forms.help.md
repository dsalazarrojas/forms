# Festival Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help festival attendees request accommodation for the event. Please fill out the form with your details to enable our staff to process your request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your first name and surname in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Indicate whether you will be requiring accommodation ("Accommodation Type") and select "Yes" to proceed.
5. If you require accommodation, you will also need to specify your request in the "Accommodation Request" field, selecting from the available options.
6. Enter the date you plan to attend the festival in the "Festival Date" field.
7. Choose a check-in time that suits you best in the "Check In Time" field.
8. Optionally, select a check-out time that works for you in the "Check Out Time" field.
9. If you have any special requests (e.g., dietary restrictions), please provide them in the "Special Requests" field.
10. Enter the name of the tool assigned to you in the "Assigned Tool" field (this will be provided by the event staff).
11. Enter the name of the festival you will be attending in the "Festival Name" field.
12. Provide the location of the festival in the "Festival Location" field.
13. Enter the date of the event you will be attending in the "Event Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Accommodation Type** (select_one, optional): Indicate if you require accommodation for the festival. Select "Yes" if you need help with accommodation.
* **Accommodation Request** (select_multiple, optional): If you selected "Yes" for accommodation type, specify your accommodation request from the available options.
* **Festival Date** (date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the festival.
* **Check In Time** (time, optional): Choose your check-in time.
* **Check Out Time** (time, optional): Optionally, select a check-out time.
* **Special Requests** (note, optional): Provide any special requests or notes for the event staff.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): Enter the name of the tool assigned to you (provided by event staff).
* **Festival Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the festival you will be attending.
* **Festival Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the festival.
* **Event Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the event you will be attending.
