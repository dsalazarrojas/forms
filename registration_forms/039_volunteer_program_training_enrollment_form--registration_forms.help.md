# Volunteer Program Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help volunteers enroll in a training program. Please fill out the following fields to register your interest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:
1. Enter your name in the "Volunteer Name" field.
2. Choose the training session that aligns with your goals and availability in the "Training Session" field.
3. Enter the start date of your training session in the "Training Session Start Date" field.
4. Enter the end date of your training session in the "Training Session End Date" field.
5. Enter the location of your training session in the "Training Location" field.
6. Specify the duration of your training session in the "Training Duration" field.
7. Enter the name of the organization you are working with in the "Organization Name" field.
8. Enter the contact person's name for your organization in the "Organization Contact" field.
9. Confirm that you have read and agree to the volunteer agreement in the "Volunteer Agreement" field.
10. Specify your emergency contact's name and any additional emergency contacts in the "Emergency Contact" field.
11. Finally, enter your name as the submitter in the "Submitter Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Name (Volunteer Name)**: Enter your name as a volunteer.
* **Training Session (Training Session)**: Choose the training session that aligns with your goals and availability.
* **Training Session Start Date (Training Session Start Date)**: Enter the start date of your training session.
* **Training Session End Date (Training Session End Date)**: Enter the end date of your training session.
* **Training Location (Training Location)**: Enter the location of your training session.
* **Training Duration (Training Duration)**: Specify the duration of your training session.
* **Organization Name (Organization Name)**: Enter the name of the organization you are working with.
* **Organization Contact (Organization Contact)**: Enter the contact person's name for your organization.
* **Volunteer Agreement (Volunteer Agreement)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the volunteer agreement.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)**: Specify your emergency contact's name and any additional emergency contacts.
* **Submitter Name (Submitter Name)**: Enter your name as the submitter.
