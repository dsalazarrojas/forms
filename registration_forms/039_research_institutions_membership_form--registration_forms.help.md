# Research Institutions Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register an individual as a member of a research institution. It is essential for accurate and up-to-date member information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and relevant information about yourself and your institution affiliation.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you have the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or ID.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the research institution you are affiliated with.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department or unit within the institution you are affiliated with.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the institution.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, date, optional): Enter the date you became a member of the institution.
* **Member Since (Time)** (`member_since_note`, time, optional): Enter a specific time when you became a member of the institution or any additional details about your membership status.
* **Member Since Note** (`member_since_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership.
