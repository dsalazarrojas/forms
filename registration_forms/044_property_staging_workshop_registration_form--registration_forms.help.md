# Property Staging Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Staging Workshop Registration Form is designed to gather information from potential attendees for the Property Staging Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details in the "Attendee Details" section.
2. Select your workshop preferences in the "Workshop Preferences" section.
3. Provide your payment information in the "Payment Details" section.
4. Leave a message or feedback in the "Contact Us" section if you have any.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, text, required/optional): Please fill in your name, company (if applicable), and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`Workshop Preferences`, text, required/optional): Choose the workshop date and time that you are interested in attending. You can also specify any special requests or preferences.
* **Payment Details** (`Payment Details`, text, required/optional): Provide your payment method and any other relevant payment information.
* **Contact Us** (`Contact Us`, text, required/optional): If you have any questions or need to get in touch with us, leave a message here.
