# Affiliate Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the registration form for becoming an affiliate with our platform. The form is used to collect information about you and your company, which will be used to create your affiliate account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section as requested. Start by joining our program under the header section, then fill out your personal details such as first name, last name, and email address. Create a password for your affiliate dashboard, and then provide your company information if applicable. Finally, provide your physical address and select your promotional methods. End with agreeing to our terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Create Password** (`password`, text, required): Choose a secure password for your affiliate dashboard.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name, if applicable.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter your company's website URL (http://...).
* **Street Address** (`address_street`, text, required): Enter your company's physical address.
* **City** (`address_city`, text, required): Enter the city where your company is located.
* **State / Province** (`address_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where your company is located.
* **Postal / Zip Code** (`address_zip`, text, required): Enter your company's postal or zip code.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country where your company is located.
* **Promotional Methods** (`promo_methods`, select_multiple, required): Select all promotional methods that apply to your business.
* **Promotional Methods Options**:
	+ Website / Blog
	+ Email Marketing
	+ Social Media
	+ PPC Advertising
	+ Offline / Print
* **I agree to the Terms & Conditions** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Choose "True" to accept our terms of service, or "False" to decline.
