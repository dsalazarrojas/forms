# New Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new volunteers, collecting information about the individual, such as their name, contact details, and volunteer schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your volunteer type from the "Volunteer Type" dropdown list.
6. Choose your available shifts from the "Volunteer Shift" multiple select list.
7. If applicable, select a specific volunteer date and time from the "Volunteer Date" and "Volunteer Time" fields.
8. Add any additional notes about your availability or schedule in the "Note" field.
9. Finally, if you've been assigned a tool, enter it in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Volunteer Type** (select_one, optional): Select your volunteer role, such as event volunteer, office volunteer, or other.
* **Volunteer Shift** (select_multiple, optional): Choose your available shifts, such as morning, afternoon, or evening.
* **Volunteer Date** (date, optional): Select a specific date you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Time** (time, optional): Select a specific time you are available to volunteer.
* **Note** (note, optional): Add any additional information about your availability or schedule.
* **Assigned Tool** (text, optional): If you have been assigned a tool, enter it here.
