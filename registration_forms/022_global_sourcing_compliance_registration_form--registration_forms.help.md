# Global Sourcing Compliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for suppliers to register for global sourcing compliance. It's a simple registration form that collects essential information about your company and its practices.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your registration is successful.
2. Make sure to provide all the required information for each field.
3. Review your form carefully before submission to catch any errors or missing fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This is the main form that collects basic information about your company and its practices.
* **Supplier Info** (`supplier_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of your company and its business practices.
* **Sourcing Compliance** (`sourcing_compliance`, `text`, required: false): Explain your company's compliance status with global standards and regulations.
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter the details about your vendor, including its name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Vendor Contact** (`vendor_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter the contact information for your vendor.
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of your vendor.
* **Certification Info** (`certification_info`, `text`, required: false): If your company has obtained any certifications, enter the details about them.
* **Form Completion** (`form_completion`, `text`, required: false): Enter the date and time you completed this form.

**Tips:**
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Make sure to provide all required information for your registration to be successful.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
