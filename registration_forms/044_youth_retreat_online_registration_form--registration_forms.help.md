# youth_retreat_online_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals for a youth retreat online event.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Select your start date in the "Date" field.
6.  Select your start time in the "Time" field.
7.  Choose your role from the "Role" field.
8.  If you selected "Other" as your role, enter your other role in the "Other Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the individual registering.
*   **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the individual registering.
*   **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address of the individual.
*   **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the individual.
*   **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the start date the individual will be attending the retreat.
*   **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Select the start time the individual will be attending the retreat.
*   **Role** (`Role`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role at the event. Options include Participant, Chaperone, Staff, and Other (specify).
*   **Other Role** (`Other Role`, `text`, optional): Enter the other role you selected as your primary role.
