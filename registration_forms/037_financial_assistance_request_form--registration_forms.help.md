# Financial Assistance Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about individuals seeking financial assistance. It helps us understand their employment status, income, financial history, and other essential details to provide effective support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. For each field, choose the most applicable option from the drop-down menus.
3. If a field is not applicable, please indicate this clearly.
4. If you need help with any field, you can refer to the explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, Optional): This field is for any additional comments or explanations you may have about your financial situation. Please type in your thoughts and we'll review them carefully.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, Optional): Choose your employment status from the options provided (active, inactive, other). If you're not currently employed, choose 'other' and describe your situation in the **Job Title** field.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, Optional): If you're not employed, please provide your previous job title or a description of your current job responsibilities.
* **Monthly Income** (`monthly_income`, number, Optional): Enter your monthly income in numerical format (e.g., 5000 for $5,000 per month).
* **Financial History** (`financial_history`, select_multiple, Optional): Check any financial issues you've experienced, such as bankruptcy, foreclosure, or credit card debt.
* **Financial Support** (`financial_support`, select_one, Optional): Indicate if you currently receive financial support, such as alimony or child support.
* **Financial Support Source** (`financial_support_source`, text, Optional): If you receive financial support, specify the source (e.g., ex-spouse, government assistance).
* **Tax Return** (`tax_return`, select_one, Optional): Choose the year of your most recent tax return.
* **Income Frequency** (`income_frequency`, select_one, Optional): Describe how often you receive income (e.g., weekly, biweekly, monthly).
* **Income Frequency Other** (`income_frequency_other`, text, Optional): If your income frequency is not listed, please describe it in this field.
* **Income Frequency Source** (`income_frequency_source`, text, Optional): If applicable, specify the source of your income.
* **Household Members** (`household_members`, select_multiple, Optional): Indicate the number of people living in your household.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, number, Optional): Enter your household's total annual income in numerical format (e.g., 40000 for $40,000 per year).
* **Financial Status** (`financial_status`, select_one, Optional): Choose your current financial status (poor, struggling, stable).
* **Income Type** (`income_type`, text, Optional): If applicable, describe the type of income you receive (e.g., salary, self-employment, investment income).
* **Income Type Other** (`income_type_other`, text, Optional): If your income type is not listed, please describe it in this field.
* **Income Frequency Employed** (`income_frequency_employed`, select_one, Optional): If you're employed, choose the frequency of your pay (full-time, part-time, other).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_one, Optional): Indicate your job status (active, inactive, other).
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, text, Optional): If applicable, describe your job type (e.g., blue-collar, white-collar, freelance).
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, Optional): If applicable, describe your job location (e.g., city, state, country).
* **Annual Income** (`annual_income`, number, Optional): Enter your annual income in numerical format (e.g., 40000 for $40,000 per year).
* **Financial Aid Amount** (`financial_aid_amount`, number, Optional): If you're seeking financial aid, enter the amount you require.
* **Financial Aid Reason** (`financial_aid_reason`, text, Optional): Describe why you're seeking financial aid.
* **Financial Aid Source** (`financial_aid_source`, text, Optional): If applicable, specify the source of your financial aid.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, Optional): Use this field to provide any additional context or details about your financial situation.

Please fill out this form accurately and honestly, and we'll review your information to provide effective support.
