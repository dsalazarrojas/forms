# School Admission Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students seeking admission to a school or educational institution. It collects necessary information from both the student and their guardian to assess eligibility and suitability for admission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the student information section with your personal details.
2. Select your relationship with your guardian from the options provided.
3. Choose your student's educational background from the options provided.
4. Select your student's employment status and income source.
5. Enter the number of family members living with you and your family's income.
6. Select your guardian's employment status and income source.
7. Enter your guardian's family details.
8. Provide additional details about yourself and your guardian.
9. Enter your address, phone, and email.
10. Provide your digital signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Information (1)** (`Student Information`, `text`, required): Enter your student's name, address, phone, and email.
* **Guardian Contact (2)** (`Guardian Contact`, `text`, required): Enter your guardian's name, address, phone, and email.
* **Guardian Relationship (3)** (`Guardian Relationship`, `select_multiple`, required): Select how you are related to the student (Mother, Father, Guardian).
* **Student Documents (4)** (`Student Documents`, `select_multiple`, required): Select which ID documents are applicable to the student.
* **Student Educational Background (5)** (`Student Educational Background`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the student's educational background (Public, Private, Government).
* **Student Employment Status (6)** (`Student Employment Status`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Full-time).
* **Student Income Source (7)** (`Student Income Source`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the student's income source (Salary, Investment, Other).
* **Student Family Income (8)** (`Student Family Income`, `number`, optional): Enter the family income.
* **Student Family Members (9)** (`Student Family Members`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of family members living with you.
* **Guardian Employment Status (10)** (`Guardian Employment Status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the guardian's employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Full-time).
* **Guardian Income Source (11)** (`Guardian Income Source`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the guardian's income source (Salary, Investment, Other).
* **Guardian Family Income (12)** (`Guardian Family Income`, `number`, optional): Enter the family income.
* **Guardian Family Members (13)** (`Guardian Family Members`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of family members living with you.
* **Student Additional Details (14)** (`Student Additional Details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about yourself.
* **Guardian Additional Details (15)** (`Guardian Additional Details`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional details about your guardian.
* **Guardian Additional Information (16)** (`Guardian Additional Information`, `date`, optional): Enter any additional information about your guardian.
* **Student Additional Information (17)** (`Student Additional Information`, `date`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Student Address (18)** (`Student Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your student address.
* **Guardian Address (19)** (`Guardian Address`, `text`, optional): Enter your guardian address.
* **Student Phone (20)** (`Student Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your student phone number.
* **Guardian Phone (21)** (`Guardian Phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your guardian phone number.
* **Student Email (22)** (`Student Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your student email.
* **Guardian Email (23)** (`Guardian Email`, `email`, optional): Enter your guardian email.
* **Student Digital Signature (24)** (`Student Digital Signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your digital signature.
* **Guardian Digital Signature (25)** (`Guardian Digital Signature`, `text`, optional): Enter your guardian digital signature.

Note: Asterisks `*` are used for emphasis.
