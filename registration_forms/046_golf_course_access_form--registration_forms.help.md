# golf course access form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The golf course access form is used to collect information from players who want to access the golf course. This form helps us understand their preferences and ensures we manage the flow of players to the golf course efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your player details in the Player Information section.
2. Choose your tee time preferences in the Tee Time Preferences section.
3. Confirm your payment details in the Payment Details section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information** (`player_info`, text, required: false): Enter your player's name, contact number, and any other relevant information in this section. This will help us verify your identity and contact you about your access to the golf course.
* **Tee Time Preferences** (`tee_time_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the times you are available to play at the golf course. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for the following options:
	+ Yes: Select 'Yes' if you are available to play during peak hours.
	+ No: Select 'No' if you are not available to play during peak hours.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm whether you are willing to make payment for access to the golf course. Choose from 'Yes' or 'No' for the following options:
	+ Yes: Select 'Yes' if you are willing to make payment for access.
	+ No: Select 'No' if you are not willing to make payment for access.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your access request is processed efficiently.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [your contact information].
