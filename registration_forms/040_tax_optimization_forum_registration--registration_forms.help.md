# Tax Optimization Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is for users who need to register for the Tax Optimization Forum, providing a clear understanding of the form's purpose and how to complete it.
## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Tax Optimization Forum, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2.  Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3.  Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
4.  Confirm your email address in the `Confirm Email` field by typing it in again.
5.  Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
6.  Select your time zone from the list.
7.  If your time zone is not in the list, type it in the `Time Zone Other` field.
8.  Enter the name of the event you are registering for in the `Event Name` field.
9.  Choose a date for the event in the `Event Date` field.
10. Select a time for the event in the `Event Time` field.
11. Enter the location of the event in the `Event Location` field.
12. Select your interests by typing them in the `Interests` field.
13. Select your role for the event as Attendee, Organizer, or Speaker from the `Consent` field.
14. Optionally provide event details in the `Event Details` and `Other Event Details` fields.
15. Confirm your role selection in the `Terms and Conditions` field by selecting either `Yes` or `No`.
