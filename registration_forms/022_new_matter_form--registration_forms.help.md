# New Matter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Matter Form is used to collect information about new matters or cases. It is a simple and straightforward form designed to gather basic details about the matter at hand.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the New Matter Form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of the new matter or case in the **New Matter Form** field.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the client in the **Client Form** field.
3. Enter the details about the case or matter in the **Case Form** field.
4. Enter any additional details about the case or matter in the **Case Details Form** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Matter Form** (1, text, required):
	+ Enter the title of the new matter or case, such as "John Doe vs XYZ Corporation".
* **Client Form** (2, text, required):
	+ Enter the name and contact information of the client, such as "John Doe, johndoe@example.com, 555-1234".
* **Case Form** (3, text, required):
	+ Enter the details about the case or matter, such as "Dispute over unpaid invoice".
* **Case Details Form** (4, text, required):
	+ Enter any additional details about the case or matter, such as "Amount due: $1000, Date of incident: 2022-01-01".

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the correct processing of the matter.
