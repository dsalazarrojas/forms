# Briefing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for company briefings. It ensures that attendees provide the necessary information, including contact details and dietary needs, to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Phone Number
	* Organization
	* Job Title
	* Select Briefing Topic
	* Select Briefing Date
	* Select Briefing Time Slot
	* Select Attendance Mode
3. Optionally, select how you will attend, and select any dietary restrictions if necessary.
4. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
5. Indicate how you learned about this event and if you would like to receive future invites.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for identification purposes.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter the email address you would like to be used for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number for any last minute updates or changes.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization for our records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your current job title for our records.
* **Briefing Topic** (`briefing_topic`, select_one, required): Select the briefing topic you are interested in attending.
* **Briefing Date** (`briefing_date`, date, required): Select your preferred briefing date from the provided options.
* **Briefing Time Slot** (`briefing_time`, select_one, required): Select your preferred time slot for the briefing.
* **Attendance Mode** (`attendance_mode`, select_one, required): Select how you will attend the briefing (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as vegetarian or vegan.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Describe any accessibility requirements you may have.
* **Questions for the Briefing** (`additional_questions`, text, optional): Enter any questions you would like to be addressed during the briefing.
* **How did you hear about this briefing** (`how_heard`, select_one, optional): Select how you learned about this event.
* **Receive Future Briefing Invitations** (`receive_future_invites`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you would like to receive future briefing invitations.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
