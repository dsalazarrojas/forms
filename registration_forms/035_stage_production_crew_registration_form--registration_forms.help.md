# Stage Production Crew Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Production Crew Registration Form is used to register stage production crew members for a stage production. This form is completed by crew members to provide their contact information, availability, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your stage production crew name in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Name" field.
2. Enter a title for the form in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Title" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Email" field in the format `example@example.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Phone" field.
5. Select your job title in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Job Title" dropdown menu.
6. Select your employed status in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Employed by" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your available dates in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Available Dates" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 01/01/2022).
8. Select your availability in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Availability" dropdown menu.
9. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Notes" field.
10. Select your working hours in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Hours" dropdown menu.
11. Select your rate of pay in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Rate" dropdown menu.
12. Select your preferred stage in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Stage" dropdown menu.
13. Select your backstage status in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Backstage" dropdown menu.
14. Select whether you will be working front of house or back of house in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Front of House" and "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Back of House" dropdown menus.
15. Select whether you will be working with stage equipment or other equipment in the "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Stage Equipment" and "Stage Production Crew Registration Form Other Equipment" dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Name**: Enter your stage production crew name.
	+ Required field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Title**: Enter a title for the form.
	+ Required field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ Required field in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ Required field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Job Title**: Select your job title.
	+ Options include Stage Manager, Assistant Manager, and Production Team.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Employed by**: Select your employed status.
	+ Options include Stage Manager, Assistant Manager, and Production Team.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Available Dates**: Enter your available dates in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
	+ Required field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Availability**: Select your availability.
	+ Options include Available and Unavailable.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
	+ Optional field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Hours**: Select your working hours.
	+ Options include 1-5, 6-10, 11-15, 16-20, 21-25, 26-30, 31-35, 36 or more.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Rate**: Select your rate of pay.
	+ Options include 0 and 50.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Venue**: Select the venue where you will be working.
	+ Options include Main Stage, Backstage, Green Room, Load/Unload, Stage Door, and Other.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Venue Description**: Enter a description of the venue.
	+ Optional field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Venue Details**: Enter any additional details about the venue.
	+ Optional field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Stage**: Select the stage where you will be working.
	+ Options include Front of House, Back of House, Stage, and Other.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Stage Details**: Enter any additional details about the stage.
	+ Optional field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Backstage**: Select your backstage status.
	+ Options include Front of House, Back of House, Stage, and Other.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Backstage Details**: Enter any additional details about the backstage.
	+ Optional field.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Front of House**: Select whether you will be working front of house.
	+ Options include True and False.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Back of House**: Select whether you will be working back of house.
	+ Options include True and False.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Stage Equipment**: Select whether you will be working with stage equipment.
	+ Options include True and False.
* **Stage Production Crew Registration Form Other Equipment**: Enter any additional information about other equipment.
	+ Optional field.
