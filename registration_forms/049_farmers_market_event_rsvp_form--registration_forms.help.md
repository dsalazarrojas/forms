# Farmers' Market Event RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmers' Market Event RSVP Form is designed to help event organizers gather information from attendees about a specific event. This form will help you provide your details and confirm your interest in attending the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Details section with any relevant information about the event, such as its title, location, and dates.
2. Provide your contact details in the Contact Details section, including your name, email, and phone number (if available).
3. If you need to, you can also specify any additional event logistics in the Event Logistics section, such as parking options or accessibility requirements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Details**
(event_details, text, required: false)
Enter any details about the event you'd like to share with the organizers, such as its title, location, and dates.

### **Contact Details**
(contact_details, text, required: false)
Provide your name, email, and phone number (if available) so that the organizers can contact you about the event.

### **Event Logistics**
(event_logistics, text, required: false)
Enter any additional details about the event logistics, such as parking options or accessibility requirements.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form as accurately and completely as possible.
* You can add any additional comments or notes about the event in the Event Logistics section.
* If you have any special requests or needs, feel free to mention them in the Event Logistics section.
* Make sure to proofread your answers before submitting the form.
