# Saxophone Intensive Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to enroll in the Saxophone Intensive program. It captures essential information to schedule sessions and manage the program's participant list.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure correct enrollment processing.
2.  Review and verify the information you enter before submitting the form.
3.  Ensure that you have filled out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, text, \*) 
   This field gathers your basic details, including your name, contact information, and any other relevant participant information. Please provide your actual name and contact details for us to contact you for further communication and updates.
* **Music Background** (`Music Background`, text, \*) 
   Share your experience with playing saxophone, including any relevant training or experience. This helps us understand your skill level and pair you with the right instructor.
* **Preferred Session** (`Preferred Session`, select_multiple, \*) 
   Choose the time of day you'd like to attend the intensive sessions. You can select one or more of the options provided to accommodate your schedule.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, date, \*) 
   Select the date you want to attend the intensive sessions. Please make sure this date aligns with your schedule and available time.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, time, \*) 
   Choose the time of day for the session you've selected. Ensure that this time fits with your schedule.
* **Enrollment Details** (`Enrollment Details`, text, \*) 
   Provide any additional information about your enrollment or requests for special arrangements. Please keep it concise for better processing.
* **Payment Terms** (`Payment Terms`, text, ) 
   If you have any payment concerns or special arrangements, please specify them here. This helps us prepare the necessary documentation for payment processing.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, ) 
   Use this field to add any additional comments, thoughts, or ideas you'd like to share. Keep it short and sweet.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, \*) 
   Review and submit your form to complete your enrollment. This will send your information to our system for further processing.
* **Submit Date** (`Submit Date`, date, \*) 
   The date and time when you submit the form. This helps us track the submission timeline.
* **Submit Time** (`Submit Time`, time, \*) 
   The time when you submit the form, which is recorded alongside the submit date.
