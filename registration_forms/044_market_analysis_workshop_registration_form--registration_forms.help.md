# market_analysis_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for the market analysis workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information on page 1, including your name and contact details.
2. On page 2, provide your participant information, such as your title, name, and email.
3. On page 3, enter your payment information, including the payment type and method.
4. On page 4, specify your preferred payment type (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
5. On page 5, select your preferred payment method (e.g., Stripe, Authorize.net, Other).
6. On page 6, enter any additional payment details, if required.
7. On page 7, provide your billing information, if applicable.
8. On page 8, review and confirm your registration details.
9. On page 9, enter any additional workshop schedule information, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information (1)**: Enter your name and contact details to help the workshop organizers contact you.
* **Participant Information (2)**: Provide your title, name, and email to help us understand who you are and how to reach you.
* **Payment Information (3)**: Enter your payment details, including the amount you are paying and any additional notes.
* **Payment Type (4)**: Choose how you would like to pay for the workshop (e.g., Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Method (5)**: Select the method you would like to use for payment (e.g., Stripe, Authorize.net, Other).
* **Payment Details (6)**: Enter any additional information related to your payment, if required.
* **Billing Information (7)**: If you need to provide billing information (e.g., for company or organization), enter it here.
* **Registration Confirmation (8)**: Review and confirm your registration details to ensure accuracy.
* **Workshop Schedules (9)**: Enter any additional information related to the workshop schedule, if applicable.
