## Digital Transformation Benchmark Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Transformation Benchmark Registration form collects information from organizations to assess their digital maturity. Fill out this form to provide your organization's details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enroll Date: Enter the date you or your organization first started your digital transformation journey.
2.  Organization Name: Enter your organization's name as it appears on official documents or websites.
3.  Digital Maturity Stage: Select all applicable stages that describe your organization's current level of digital maturity.
4.  Goals for Benchmark Program: Describe your goals and objectives for participating in the benchmark program.
5.  Consultant Name: Enter the name of the consultant or advisor who helped with your digital transformation.
6.  Email: Enter your organization's email address for communication purposes.
7.  Phone: Enter your organization's phone number for communication purposes.
8.  Assigned Tool: Select the tool assigned to you for the benchmark program (e.g., chatjimmy, jupyter, powerpoint).
9.  Output File: Enter a file or document name that will be generated as a result of the benchmark program.
10. Description: Provide a brief description of your organization and its digital transformation journey.
