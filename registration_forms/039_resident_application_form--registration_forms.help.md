# Resident Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Application Form is designed to collect personal and contact information from residents, as well as details about their employment status, move-in date, and lease agreements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as first name, last name, email, contact number, address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Select your employment status and type from the options provided.
3. If applicable, enter your move-in date and provide a reason for moving in.
4. If applicable, enter your landlord's name and provide any lease details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
- **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
- **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city (if applicable).
- **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter your state.
- **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, required): Enter your zip code.
- **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current employment status.
- **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_one, optional): Select your employment type (if applicable).
- **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Enter your move-in date (if applicable).
- **Reason for Move In** (`move_in_reason`, text, optional): Provide a reason for moving in (if applicable).
- **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your landlord or property manager.
- **Tenant Reference** (`tenant_reference`, text, optional): Provide any relevant tenant reference information.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, optional): Select your relationship with your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Employment Status** (`emergency_contact_employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your emergency contact's employment status (if applicable).
- **Emergency Contact Employment Type** (`emergency_contact_employment_type`, select_one, optional): Select your emergency contact's employment type (if applicable).
- **Emergency Contact Move In Date** (`emergency_contact_move_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the move-in date of your emergency contact (if applicable).
- **Move Out Date** (`emergency_contact_move_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the move-out date of your emergency contact (if applicable).
- **Move In Date** (`emergency_contact_lease_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the lease of your emergency contact (if applicable).
- **Move Out Date** (`emergency_contact_lease_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the lease of your emergency contact (if applicable).
- **Lease Details** (`lease_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional lease details (if applicable).
