# Temporary Event Office Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register temporary events that require office space. Please provide the necessary details to help us accommodate your event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required and optional information for your event.
2. Make sure to provide the correct event name, date, and time.
3. Enter the location where the event will take place.
4. If applicable, provide the start and end times for the event.
5. List the expected number of attendees.
6. Enter the contact person's name and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your event.
* **Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your event.
* **Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the time of your event.
* **Location** (`event_location`, `text`, required): Enter the address or location of your event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, `time`, optional): If your event has a start time other than the main event time, enter it here.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, `time`, optional): If your event has an end time other than the main event time, enter it here.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, `text`, optional): List the expected number of attendees.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person for your event.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
