# Casual Dress Day Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Casual Dress Day Registration form is designed for employees to sign up for company-wide Casual Dress Days. By completing this form, you will be registered for the event and will receive updates on upcoming dates.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the registration process.
2. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Select your department from the list of options in the "Department" field.
4. Choose your registration type from the options provided in the "Registration Type" field.
5. Select one or multiple dates for upcoming Casual Fridays from the list of options in the "Select Upcoming Casual Friday Dates" field.
6. Read and agree to follow the dress code guidelines in the "Dress Code Guidelines" section.
7. Confirm that you have made a donation to our charity partner by selecting "Yes, via online portal" in the "Proof of Donation/Payment" field.
8. Optionally, provide an emergency contact person's name and their contact information in the "Emergency Contact (Optional Update)" field.
9. Add any comments or suggestions for future themes in the "Any questions or suggestions for themes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (employee_name)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Department (department)**: Select your department from the list of options provided.
* **Registration Type (registration_type)**: Choose how you would like to register: Single Event, Monthly Pass, or Annual Subscription.
* **Select Upcoming Casual Friday Dates (events_selection)**: Select one or multiple dates for upcoming Casual Fridays.
* **Dress Code Guidelines (section_guidelines)**: Read and review the guidelines for dress code.
* **Dress Code Requirements (guidelines_info)**: Understand that casual dress must be neat, clean, and professional.
* **Agree to Follow Dress Code Policy (agreement_terms)**: Confirm that you agree to follow the company dress code policy.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact_name)**: Optionally provide an emergency contact person's name and their contact information.
* **Proof of Donation/Payment (payment_confirmation)**: Confirm that you have made a donation to our charity partner.
* **Any questions or suggestions for themes (comments)**: Add any additional comments or suggestions for future themes.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) to complete the registration process.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact your HR representative for assistance.
