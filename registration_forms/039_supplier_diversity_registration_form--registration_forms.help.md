# Supplier Diversity Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their company details, diversity statement, and certification. The form is intended to help us understand and verify the diversity and inclusion practices of our potential suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the **Supplier Diversification** page with a brief statement that explains your company's commitment to diversity and inclusion.
2.  Complete the **Company Details** page with your company's basic information, including:
    *   **Company Name**: Enter your company's full name as it appears on your business license or registration.
    *   **Company Type**: Select the type of your company from the provided options (Large, Small).
    *   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this registration.
    *   **Address**: Enter your company's mailing address.
    *   **City**: Enter your city.
    *   **State**: Enter your state.
    *   **Postal**: Enter your zip code.
    *   **Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
    *   **Email**: Enter your business email address.
    *   **Website**: Enter your company's website URL.
3.  On the **Diversity Statement** page, provide a statement that explains your company's support for diversity and inclusion.
4.  On the **Support Statement** page, provide a statement that explains how your company supports our suppliers.
5.  If applicable, select any **Certifications** that you hold, such as Supplier Diversity or E-verify.
6.  If you have any other certifications, enter them in the **Other** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Supplier Diversification**: A brief statement that explains your company's commitment to diversity and inclusion. (Page 1)
*   **Company Name**: Enter your company's full name as it appears on your business license or registration. (Page 2)
*   **Company Type**: Select the type of your company from the provided options (Large, Small). (Page 2)
*   **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this registration. (Page 2)
*   **Address**: Enter your company's mailing address. (Page 2)
*   **City**: Enter your city. (Page 2)
*   **State**: Enter your state. (Page 2)
*   **Postal**: Enter your zip code. (Page 2)
*   **Phone**: Enter your business phone number. (Page 2)
*   **Email**: Enter your business email address. (Page 2)
*   **Website**: Enter your company's website URL. (Page 2)
*   **Diversity Statement**: A statement that explains your company's support for diversity and inclusion. (Page 3)
*   **Support Statement**: A statement that explains how your company supports our suppliers. (Page 3)
*   **Certification**: Select any applicable certifications you hold, such as Supplier Diversity or E-verify. (Page 4)
*   **Other**: If you have any other certifications, enter them in this field. (Page 4)
*   **Certification Number**: If you have a certification number, enter it here. (Page 4)

Note: Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.
