# Conference Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Records Release Form is used to request access to conference records, such as attendance certificates, presentation materials, and other documentation. This form is a formal request that requires your identification and authorization to ensure the confidentiality and integrity of the records.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your complete legal name as the Requestor Full Name.
2. Enter your email address as the Contact Email.
3. Provide your phone number as the Contact Number.
4. Enter your affiliated organization.
5. Select your relationship to the records from the options provided.
6. Enter the name of the conference for which you are requesting records.
7. Enter the date of the conference.
8. Select all the types of records you are requesting from the options provided.
9. Provide a description of the specific records you need.
10. If applicable, specify the date range of the records you need.
11. Enter the purpose of your request.
12. Provide more details about your purpose, if required.
13. Select your preferred delivery method for the records.
14. Enter the address to which the records should be delivered.
15. Select the urgency of your request, specifying how soon you need the records.
16. Indicate whether you have attached valid identification to verify your identity.
17. Select whether you authorize the release of the records.
18. Confirm that you understand the privacy implications of the records.
19. Enter the date of your authorization.
20. If required, enter any additional comments you have regarding your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Requestor Full Name** (`requestor_name`, text, required): Enter your complete legal name.
- **Email Address** (`requestor_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email.
- **Phone Number** (`requestor_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your affiliated organization.
- **Relationship to Records** (`relationship_to_records`, select_one, required): Select how you are connected to these records.
- **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference.
- **Conference Date** (`conference_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the conference.
- **Type of Records Requested** (`records_type`, select_multiple, required): Select all types of records you are requesting.
- **Specific Records Description** (`specific_records`, text, required): Describe exactly what records you need.
- **Date Range of Records** (`date_range`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the date range of the records you need.
- **Purpose of Request** (`purpose_of_request`, select_one, required): Select why you need these records.
- **Purpose Details** (`purpose_details`, text, optional): Provide more details about your purpose, if required.
- **Preferred Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive the records.
- **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, required): Enter the address to which records should be sent.
- **Urgency of Request** (`urgency`, select_one, required): Select how soon you need the records.
- **Have you attached valid identification** (`identification_attached`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have attached valid identification.
- **Authorization Statement** (`authorization_statement`, select_one, required): Select whether you authorize the release of the records.
- **Privacy Acknowledgment** (`privacy_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand the privacy implications of the records.
- **Date of Authorization** (`signature_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your authorization.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you have regarding your request.
