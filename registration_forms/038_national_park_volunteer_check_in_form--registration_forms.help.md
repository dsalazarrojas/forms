# National Park Volunteer Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for volunteers to check in for their shifts at a national park.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "volunteer_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "volunteer_email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "volunteer_phone" field.
4. Enter the start time of your shift in the "shift_start_time" field.
5. Enter the end time of your shift in the "shift_end_time" field.
6. Enter the name of the park where you will be volunteering in the "park_name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **park_name (text)**: Enter the name of the national park where you will be volunteering.
* **shift_start_time (time)**: Enter the start time of your shift. Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g. 08:00 instead of 8:00 AM).
* **shift_end_time (time)**: Enter the end time of your shift. Please use the 24-hour clock format (e.g. 16:00 instead of 4:00 PM).
* **volunteer_name (text)**: Enter your name.
* **volunteer_email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **volunteer_phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the instructions above for clarification.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the park staff or administration.
