# Night Run Helper Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Night Run Helper Registration form is a mandatory registration process for individuals interested in volunteering for a night run event. It collects essential contact information and role-specific details from volunteers to ensure a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name and last name.
2. Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
3. Select your volunteer type (optional) from the provided options.
4. Provide your phone number for contact and emergency purposes.
5. Enter your emergency contact's name.
6. Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
7. Enter your emergency contact's email address (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed during the event and in any communication with the organizers.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed during the event and in any communication with the organizers.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly, as we will use this for event updates, announcements, and communications.
* **volunteer_type** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select one or more of the following roles:
	+ Night Marshal
	+ Event Marshal
	+ Crew Member
	+ Support Staff
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter a phone number that can be reached by the organizers and other volunteers in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter a valid phone number for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter an email address for your emergency contact (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you need to cancel or change your registration, please contact us at [your email address or contact info].
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
* Please note that incomplete or inaccurate registrations might lead to delays or issues with your participation in the event.
