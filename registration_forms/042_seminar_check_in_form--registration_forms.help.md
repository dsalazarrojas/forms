# seminar_check_in_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains how to complete the seminar_check_in_form to successfully register for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your Full Name.
2. Enter your Phone number.
3. Enter a valid Email address.
4. Select a Date of Event from the available options.
5. Choose a Time of Event from the available options.
6. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
7. Choose one or more options from the "Select Multiple" field.
8. Optionally, provide any additional information in the "Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Phone** (2, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (3, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Date of Event** (4, required): Select the date of the event from the available options.
* **Time of Event** (5, required): Choose the time of the event from the available options.
* **Select One** (7, required): Choose one option from the available options.
* **Select Multiple** (8, required): Choose one or more options from the available options.
* **Note** (6, optional): Optionally, provide any additional information related to your attendance.
* **Note 2** (9, optional): This field appears to be redundant with "Note".
