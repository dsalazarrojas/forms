# ASL Level II Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from students registering for the ASL Level II course. Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and effective learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Answer the question about your previous ASL experience and completion of the previous course level.
3. Select your preferred course start date from the available options.
4. Choose your preferred class schedule and course format.
5. If applicable, provide your student ID number.
6. Select your preferred payment method.
7. Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
8. Agree to purchase required course materials.
9. Confirm your attendance commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for course-related communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact number for course-related communications.
* **Student ID (if applicable)**: If you have a student ID number, enter it here. This is not required for registration.
* **Previous ASL Experience**: Describe your previous experience with American Sign Language, if any.
* **ASL Level I Completion**: Indicate if you have already completed the ASL Level I course. If you are currently enrolled, select "Currently Enrolled".
* **Preferred Course Start Date**: Choose a start date that suits you best.
* **Class Schedule Preference**: Select the class schedule that works best for you. Options are:
	+ Monday/Wednesday 6-8 PM
	+ Tuesday/Thursday 6-8 PM
	+ Saturday 10 AM-12 PM
	+ Online Asynchronous
	+ No Preference
* **Course Format Preference**: Select how you would prefer to take the course. Options are:
	+ In-Person
	+ Online
	+ Hybrid
	+ No Preference
* **Accommodation Needs**: If you have any special needs, describe them here. This is not required for registration.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will pay for the course. Options are:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
	+ Student Account
	+ Financial Aid
	+ Other
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Course Materials Agreement**: Agree to purchase required course materials.
* **Attendance Commitment**: Confirm that you commit to attending at least 80% of classes.

Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth and effective learning experience.
