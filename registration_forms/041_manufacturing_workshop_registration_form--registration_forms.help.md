# Manufacturing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a manufacturing workshop. It gathers information about the participant, including their name, email, phone number, and status. The participant can also select their workshop preferences, such as if they will attend the workshop and if they have any specific notes. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Participant Information (Page 1)**: Enter your basic information in this section. This includes your name and email address.
2.  **Participant Name (Page 1)**: Enter your name, which should match your legal name.
3.  **Participant Email (Page 1)**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication.
4.  **Participant Phone (Page 1)**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication.
5.  **Workshop Preferences (Page 1)**: Select whether you will attend the workshop and if you have any specific preferences.
6.  **Workshop Date (Page 2)**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
7.  **Workshop Time (Page 2)**: Select the time you plan to attend the workshop.
8.  **Participant Notes (Page 2)**: Add any specific notes or information about your attendance.
9.  **Assigned Workshop (Page 2)**: Select if you are assigned to a specific workshop.
10. **Participant Status (Page 2)**: Select your current status, whether you are Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your basic information, including your name and email address.
*  **Participant Name**: Enter your legal name, as it will be used for communication.
*  **Participant Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and follow up after the workshop.
*  **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication.
*  **Workshop Preferences**: Select whether you will attend the workshop and if you have any specific preferences or requirements.
*  **Workshop Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop. 
*  **Workshop Time**: Select the time you plan to attend the workshop. 
*  **Participant Notes**: Add any specific notes or information about your attendance. 
*  **Assigned Workshop**: If you are assigned to a specific workshop, select the correct option. 
*  **Participant Status**: Select your current status, whether you are Active or Inactive. 

## Tips

*  Make sure to provide accurate information to avoid any confusion. 
*  Select the correct workshop date and time to avoid scheduling conflicts.
*  Be honest about your workshop preferences and assigned workshop.
