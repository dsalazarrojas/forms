# Babysitting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Babysitting Registration Form is designed to gather information about your family and their needs for our regular babysitting services. Please fill out this form to help us provide the best care for your children.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter all required information (marked as "true" in the form).
3. Review the information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Primary Parent or Guardian Name** (`primary_parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the primary parent or guardian who is registering.
- **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you for important updates and communications.
- **Primary Phone Number** (`primary_phone`, text, required): Enter your primary phone number where we can reach you.
- **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter your home address for our safety and emergency protocols.
- **Children's Names and Ages** (`children_details`, text, required): List all the children requiring care, including their ages.
- **Regular Days and Times Needed** (`regular_schedule_needed`, text, required): Specify the days and times when our services are needed.
- **Frequency of Service** (`frequency_of_service`, select_one, required): Choose how often you need our services:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ Occasional
- **Any Special Needs or Medical Requirements** (`special_needs_medical`, text, optional): If your child has any special needs or medical requirements, please specify.
- **Key Home Rules and Guidelines** (`home_rules_guidelines`, text, optional): List any specific rules or guidelines you'd like us to follow while providing care.
- **Household Emergency Meeting Spot** (`emergency_meeting_spot`, text, required): Specify a safe location for emergency meetings.
- **I Agree to the Service Rates and Payment Terms** (`payment_agreement_acceptance`, select_one, required): Check "I Agree" to confirm you've read and accepted our payment terms.
- **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method_on_file`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Venmo
	+ Cash/Check
	+ Bank Transfer
- **Requested Start Date** (`registration_effective_date`, date, required): Enter the date when you'd like our services to start.
