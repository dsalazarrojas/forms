# networking_event_feedback_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect feedback from users about a networking event.
 
## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the name of the event you're providing feedback for in the "Event Name" field.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field. Use the format MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field. Use the 24-hour format HH:MM.
8. Provide any positive feedback about the event in the "Positive Feedback" field.
9. Provide any neutral feedback about the event in the "Neutral Feedback" field.
10. Provide any negative feedback about the event in the "Negative Feedback" field.
 
## Field-by-Field Explanation
 
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you're providing feedback for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event using the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event using the 24-hour format HH:MM.
* **Positive Feedback** (`positive_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any positive feedback about the event.
* **Neutral Feedback** (`neutral_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any neutral feedback about the event.
* **Negative Feedback** (`negative_feedback`, text, optional): Enter any negative feedback about the event.
 
## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and helpful feedback. Feedback should be specific and provide suggestions for improvement. If you have any questions or need clarification, please contact the event organizers directly.
