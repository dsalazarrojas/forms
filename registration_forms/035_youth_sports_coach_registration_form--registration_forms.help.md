# Youth Sports Coach Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new youth sports coach with our organization. It requires information about the coach's personal details, sports experience, and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter your information in the format required (e.g. MM/DD/YYYY for dates).
3. If you need help with any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address as it is associated with your official identification documents.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your phone number as it is associated with your official identification documents.
### Address
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your current address. This field is not required.
### Sport
* **Sport** (`sport`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select the sports you have experience coaching.
### Experience
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, **required**): Enter your experience as a sports coach.
### Certifications
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select any certifications you have earned.
### Emergency Contact First Name
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
### Emergency Contact Last Name
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
### Relationship
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, **optional**): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
### Emergency Contact Email
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `email`, **optional**): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
### Background Check Conducted
* **Background Check Conducted** (`background_check_conducted`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date of the background check.
### Background Check Status
* **Background Check Status** (`background_check_status`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the status of the background check.
### Background Check Expires
* **Background Check Expires** (`background_check_expires`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the expiration date of the background check.
### Sex
* **Sex** (`sex`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your sex.
### Birth Date
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter your birth date in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
### Birth Place
* **Birth Place** (`birth_place`, `text`, **required**): Enter your birthplace.
### Sport Specialization
* **Sport Specialization** (`sport_specialization`, `text`, **required**): Enter your sport specialization.
### Sport Coaching Level
* **Sport Coaching Level** (`sport_coaching_level`, `select_one`, **required**): Select your coaching level.
### Highest Certification
* **Highest Certification** (`highest_certification`, `text`, **required**): Enter your highest certification.
### Highest Certification Earned
* **Highest Certification Earned** (`highest_certification_earned`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date you earned your highest certification.
### Highest Certification Status
* **Highest Certification Status** (`highest_certification_status`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select the status of your highest certification.
### Highest Certification Date Earned
* **Highest Certification Date Earned** (`highest_certification_date_earned`, `date`, **optional**): Enter the date you earned your highest certification.

Note: All dates should be entered in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
