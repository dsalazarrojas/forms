# Children's Enrollment And Liability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a child's enrollment in a program, including personal details, contact information, and medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that you provide up-to-date information about the child.
3. Review each field for any changes and update accordingly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child Full Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter the child's full name as it appears on their birth certificate or ID.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the child's date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the child's age.
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, optional): Select the child's gender (Male, Female, or Other).
* **Home Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter the child's home address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the child resides.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where the child resides.
* **Zip Code** (`zip`, text, required): Enter the child's zip code.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Relationship to Child** (`relationship`, select_one, required): Select the relationship between the parent or guardian and the child (Mother, Father, Guardian, or Other).
* **Parent Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Child** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the child.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any known allergies or medical conditions the child may have.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any current medications the child is taking.
* **Special Medical or Health Needs** (`special_medical_needs`, text, optional): Provide any special medical or health needs the child may have.
* **Physician or Doctor Name** (`physician_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the child's physician or doctor.
* **Physician Phone Number** (`physician_phone`, text, optional): Enter the physician or doctor's phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field for any changes and update accordingly.
* Ask for help if you need assistance filling out this form.
