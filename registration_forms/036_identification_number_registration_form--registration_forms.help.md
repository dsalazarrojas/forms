# Identification Number Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Identification Number Registration Form is for registering personal identification numbers, such as badges, cards, or certificates. This form helps collect and store relevant information about the identification number, including its expiration date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information in the "Identification Number" field.
2. Enter the expiration date in the "Expiration Date" field in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the expiration time in the "Expiration Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Identification Number** (`identification_number`, text, required): Enter the unique identification number you are registering.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, date, not required): Enter the date the identification number expires (MM/DD/YYYY). If the identification number does not expire, you can leave this field blank.
* **Expiration Time** (`expiration_time`, time, not required): Enter the time the identification number expires. If the identification number does not expire at a specific time, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Double-check the format of the expiration date to ensure it is correct (MM/DD/YYYY).
* If the identification number has no expiration time, leave the "Expiration Time" field blank.
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to submit the form correctly.
