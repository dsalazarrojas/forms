# voter_registration_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect registration details, contact information, and consent from individuals for voter registration purposes. Please use this form as is, or adapt it to your specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, one page at a time.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details (Page 1)** (`Registration Details`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing basic registration information.
	+ Please enter your name, ID number, or other relevant details as required.
* **Contact Information (Page 1)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing contact details such as email, phone number, or address.
	+ Enter your preferred contact method for follow-up or updates.
* **Give Consent (Page 1)** (`Give consent`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for acknowledging your consent to receive updates or follow-up information.
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm your consent.
* **Voter Information (Page 1)** (`Voter Information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is for providing additional voter information, such as voter ID number, or other relevant details.
	+ Enter any other information that is required or useful for your registration purpose.

## Tips

* Make sure to review and understand each field before providing information.
* Enter accurate and complete information in each field.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
