# hospitality_blockchain_network_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hospitality_blockchain_network_registration_form is a registration form for individuals or organizations to join the Hospitality Blockchain Network. This form collects contact information and other relevant details to facilitate a secure and seamless registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Organization's name in the Organization field.
4. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (contact_details, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Organization** (organization, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter a valid Email address associated with your registration.
* **Phone** (phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process. If you encounter any issues, please contact our support team for assistance.
