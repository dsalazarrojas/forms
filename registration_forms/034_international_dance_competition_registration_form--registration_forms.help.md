# International Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Dance Competition Registration Form is designed to collect participant's information and registration details for the upcoming dance competition. The form aims to gather essential details from each participant, ensuring a smooth and organized registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the Participant Name field with the full name of the participant.
2. Select the dance style that the participant is interested in competing in from the provided options.
3. If applicable, fill out the Teacher Name field with the name of the participant's dance teacher.
4. In the Contact Information field, provide the participant's contact details, including their email address.
5. Enter the participant's phone number in the Participant Phone field.
6. In the Studio Info field, provide any additional information about the participant's dance studio.
7. If necessary, fill out the Emergency Contact Info field with the contact details of the person responsible for the participant's care in case of an emergency.
8. Enter the Emergency Name field with the name of the person responsible for the participant's care in case of an emergency.
9. Enter the participant's Date of Birth.

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Dance Style** (`dance_style`, select_one, required: false): Select the dance style that the participant is interested in competing in from the provided options.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the participant's dance teacher.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide the participant's contact details, including their email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Studio Info** (`studio_info`, text, required: false): Provide any additional information about the participant's dance studio.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required: false): If necessary, fill out the contact details of the person responsible for the participant's care in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person responsible for the participant's care in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Date Of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter the participant's date of birth.
* **Teacher Info** (`teacher_info`, text, required: false): If applicable, fill out any additional information about the participant's dance teacher.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check that the participant's contact information and emergency contact information are accurate.
* Review and verify the participant's dance style selection carefully.
