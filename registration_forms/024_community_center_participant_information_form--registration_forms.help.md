# Community Center Participant Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participant registration and to gather necessary information for program communication and updates. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your personal details such as full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, and gender. Make sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
2. Select the relevant programs and activities you are interested in by choosing from the provided categories and specific program names.
3. If you have any physical activity limitations or medical conditions, please list them in the "Any Physical Activity Limitations or Medical Conditions" field.
4. Indicate any dietary allergies or restrictions you may have.
5. Provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
6. Review and agree to the photo release and participation acknowledgment terms.
7. Finally, provide your digital signature and the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Participant** (Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for program updates and results.
* **Primary Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Gender** (Select One, Required): Choose your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Residential Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (Text, Required): Provide the name of someone not living with you, who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Participant** (Text, Required): Describe your relationship to the participant.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Which program category are you registering for** (Select One, Required): Choose the program category that best fits your interests.
* **Specific Program or Activity Name** (Text, Required): Enter the specific program name you are interested in (e.g., "Senior Yoga", "Youth Basketball", "Pottery Class").
* **Any Physical Activity Limitations or Medical Conditions** (Text, Required): If applicable, list any physical activity limitations or medical conditions.
* **Any Dietary Allergies or Restrictions** (Text, Required): If applicable, list any dietary allergies or restrictions.
* **I grant permission for the community center to use photos of me/the participant for promotion** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you grant permission for photo use.
* **I acknowledge that I am participating at my own risk** (Select One, Required): Review and agree to the participation terms.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as a digital signature.
* **Date of Submission** (Date, Required): Enter the date and time of submission.
* **Submission Date and Signature Final Info** (Select One, Required): Review and confirm your submission.
