# Vacation Bible School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering students for the Vacation Bible School program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out your child's or student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2.  Enter your name and contact information in the "Parent Name", "Email", "Phone", and "Contact Person" fields.
3.  Select the program session you are registering for in the "Program Name" field.
4.  Choose the student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
5.  Fill out the emergency contact information for the student, including the "Emergency Contact Name", "Emergency Contact Relationship", and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.
6.  Review your information carefully to ensure accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name and any other parent/guardian's name who will be accompanying the student during the program.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the student's name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you have a specific date of birth for the student, enter it here.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person the student will be staying with during the program.
*   **Program Name** (`program_name`, `select_one`, optional): Select the session of the program you are registering for.
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the student's grade level.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Describe the relationship of the emergency contact person to the student.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the email of the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
