# Veterinary Supply Chain Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering users to participate in the Veterinary Supply Chain Workshop. It allows attendees to provide their information, select their preferred dates, and consent to share their data with various entities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form and familiarize yourself with the fields and options available.
2. Fill in the required fields (Attendee Name and Attendee Email).
3. Select your preferred date for the workshop.
4. Choose which entities you consent to share your data with (Clinic, Supplier, School, or any combination).
5. Select your name and email from the provided options.
6. Submit the form once you've filled in all the required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (User Name)**: Enter your name in this field.
* **User Information (Email)**: Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Preferred Dates**: Select your preferred date for the workshop from the provided calendar.
* **Consent**: Choose which entities you consent to share your data with:
	+ I consent to share my data with **Clinic**.
	+ I consent to share my data with **Supplier**.
	+ I consent to share my data with **School**.
* **Clinic**: Select your preferred clinic from the provided options.
* **Supplier**: Select your preferred supplier from the provided options.
* **School**: Select your preferred school from the provided options.
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name in this field (e.g., John Doe).
* **Attendee Email**: Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`.
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field.

Note: Please review the form carefully before submitting it, as some fields are optional.
