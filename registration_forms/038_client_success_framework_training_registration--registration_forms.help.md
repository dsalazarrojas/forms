# Client Success Framework Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Success Framework Training Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees who want to register for the Client Success Framework Training program. This form helps us ensure that we have the correct information to provide the best possible experience for each attendee.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your preferred training date and format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
3. Indicate your experience level with client success and the specific training program you are registering for.
4. Provide any additional requirements or accommodations you may need.
5. Review and agree to the training terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Full Name** (attendee_name, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### **Email Address** (attendee_email, email, required): Enter your active email address where you can be reached.
### **Phone Number** (attendee_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
### **Company Name** (company_name, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
### **Job Title** (job_title, text, required): Enter your job title within your company.
### **Department** (department, text, optional): Enter the name of your department within your company (if applicable).
### **Experience Level with Client Success** (experience_level, select_one, required): Select your experience level with client success:
	* Beginner: New to client success roles.
	* Intermediate: Has some experience with client success roles.
	* Advanced: Has significant experience with client success roles.
	* Expert: Has extensive experience with client success roles.
### **Which Training Program Are You Registering For?** (training_program, select_one, required): Select the training program you are registering for:
	* Fundamentals of Client Success
	* Advanced Client Success Strategies
	* Client Retention Best Practices
	* Team Leadership in Client Success
	* Other (please specify)
### **Preferred Training Date** (preferred_date, date, required): Select your preferred training date.
### **Preferred Training Format** (training_format, select_one, required): Select your preferred training format:
	* In-person
	* Virtual
	* Hybrid
### **Your Timezone** (timezone, text, optional): Enter your time zone (if applicable).
### **Any Dietary Restrictions (if in-person)** (dietary_restrictions, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions you may have.
### **What Do You Hope to Learn?** (learning_objectives, text, optional): Enter your specific learning goals for this training.
### **Current Client Success Challenges** (current_challenges, text, optional): Enter any current client success challenges you are facing.
### **Number of Team Members Attending** (team_attending, number, optional): Enter the number of team members attending this training (if applicable).
### **Prior Training Experience with Us** (prior_trainings, select_one, optional): Indicate if you have taken our training before:
	* True
	* False
### **Any Special Requirements or Accommodations** (additional_requirements, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or accommodations you may need.
### **I Agree to the Training Terms and Conditions** (agree_terms, select_one, required): Select to agree to the training terms and conditions:
	* I agree
	* I do not agree
