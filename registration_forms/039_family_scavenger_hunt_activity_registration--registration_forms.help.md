# Family Scavenger Hunt Activity Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Family Scavenger Hunt Activity Registration form is designed to collect information from family members who wish to participate in the activity. This form gathers essential details such as family members' names, emails, and phone numbers, as well as their agreement to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have the necessary permissions to register family members.
2.  Select the family members who will be participating from the dropdown menu.
3.  Enter the first name of each family member.
4.  Enter the last name of each family member.
5.  Enter the email address of each family member.
6.  Enter the phone number of each family member.
7.  Provide any additional notes or information about the family.
8.  Confirm that you agree to participate in the activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Information** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration`, text, not required): This field is a text input for any additional information about the registration process.
*   **Family Members** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_family`, select multiple, not required): Select all family members who will be participating in the activity. This will populate a list of options for you to choose from.
*   **First Name** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_first_name`, text, not required): Enter the first name of each family member.
*   **Last Name** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_last_name`, text, not required): Enter the last name of each family member.
*   **Email** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_email`, email, not required): Enter the email address of each family member.
*   **Phone** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_phone`, text, not required): Enter the phone number of each family member.
*   **Notes** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_notes`, note, not required): Provide any additional information about the family.
*   **Agreed to Participate** (`family_scavenger_hunt_activity_registration_agree_to_participate`, select one, not required): Confirm that you agree to participate in the activity.
