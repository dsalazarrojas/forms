This form is designed to collect information from donors who are RSVPing to a Donor Thank You Banquet event. The purpose of the form is to gather contact details, meal preferences, and RSVP status of the donors. This information will be used to coordinate the event and communicate with the donors.

The form should only be completed if the user is a donor attending the event.

**Form Purpose:** The Donor Thank You Banquet Registration form is intended for donors to register and provide their contact information, meal preferences, and RSVP status for the Donor Thank You Banquet event.

## Donor Thank You Banquet Registration - Help Guide
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your meal preferences (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, or Other - Specify).
3. If you have selected "Other (Specify)" for meal preferences, provide more information in the "Other (Specify)" field.
4. Indicate your RSVP status (True or False).
5. If you have selected "Other" for RSVP, provide more information in the "Other" field.
6. Review and submit your form.
