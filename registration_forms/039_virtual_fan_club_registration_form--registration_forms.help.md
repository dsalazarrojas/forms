# virtual_fan_club_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information for virtual fan club registration. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each field.
2. Make sure to agree to the terms and conditions at the end of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address associated with your identification.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your 10-digit phone number as it appears on your identification.
* **Country** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Select your country of residence from the list.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age in years.
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, optional): Enter your current occupation or job title. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you started your membership.
* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you will end your membership. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your current membership status from the list.
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your membership level from the list.
* **Membership Payment Status** (`membership_payment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your membership payment status from the list.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you became a member. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Member Since Time** (`member_since_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you became a member. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select to agree to the terms and conditions.

Tips:
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
* If you are having trouble with the form, please contact our support team.
* Our virtual fan club is a community-driven club, please be respectful and considerate of other members.
