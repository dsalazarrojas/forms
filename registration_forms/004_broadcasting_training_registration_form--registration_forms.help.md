# Broadcasting Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a broadcasting training program. It collects information about the individual's personal details, training preferences, and any special accommodations required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the training program you wish to register for.
3. Choose your preferred session time and date.
4. Indicate your level of experience in broadcasting.
5. If applicable, describe any relevant skills or experience you already have.
6. State what you hope to achieve from this training.
7. If necessary, specify any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
8. Select the agreement checkbox to confirm you agree to the terms and conditions.
9. Enter an email address where you can receive training confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, Text, Required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, Email, Required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, Text, Required): Enter a contact number where you can be reached.
* **Organization or Company** (Organization or Company, Text, Optional): Enter the name of your organization if applicable.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, Text, Optional): Describe your current position.
* **Training Program** (Training Program, Select One, Required): Select the training program you wish to register for.
* **Preferred Session** (Preferred Session, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred training session time.
* **Preferred Training Date** (Preferred Training Date, Date, Required): Select your preferred date for training.
* **Experience Level** (Experience Level, Select One, Required): Indicate your current level of experience in broadcasting.
* **Current Skills** (Current Skills, Text, Optional): Describe any relevant skills or experience you already have.
* **Learning Objectives** (Learning Objectives, Text, Optional): State what you hope to achieve from this training.
* **Dietary Requirements** (Dietary Requirements, Select Multiple, Optional): Specify any dietary restrictions we should know about.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Accessibility Needs, Text, Optional): Indicate any accessibility accommodations required.
* **I Agree to the Terms and Conditions** (Agreement, Select One, Required): Confirm you agree to the training policies.
* **Send Confirmation Email to this Address** (Send Confirmation Email to this Address, Email, Required): Enter an email address where you can receive training confirmation.
