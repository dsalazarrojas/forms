# prophets_directors_meeting_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register an Executive Director's attendance to a meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which includes:
	* Executive Director's name
	* Attendance type
	* Attendance date and time
	* Meeting type
2. If required, provide any special requirements or other meeting details.
3. Fill out any other optional fields as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Executive Director** (`executive_director`): Enter the Executive Director's name.
* **Attendance Type** (`attendance_type`): Select the type of attendance, such as Executive Director, Senior Management, or Other.
* **Attendance Date** (`attendance_date`): Enter the date of attendance in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Attendance Time** (`attendance_time`): Enter the time of attendance in the format 12:00 PM or 13:00.
* **Meeting Type** (`meeting_type`): Select the type of meeting, such as Leadership Meeting, Retreat, or Other.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`): If any special requirements need to be met, describe them here.
* **Other Meeting Details** (`other_meeting_details`): Enter any other important details about the meeting.
* **Executive Director ID** (`executive_director_id`): Enter the Executive Director's ID, if required.
* **Executive Director Name** (`executive_director_name`): Enter the Executive Director's name.
* **Executive Director Job Title** (`executive_director_job_title`): Enter the Executive Director's job title.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the company of the Executive Director.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the information entered for accuracy.
* If there are any issues or concerns, contact the relevant authority.
* The form is for internal use, so ensure that the information is only used within the organization.
