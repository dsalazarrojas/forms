# R2ise Peer Check In Report - Help Guide

## Purpose
The R2ise Peer Check In Report is a form designed to collect information on peer check-ins within a specific industry or category. This form helps users report their activities and interactions in a structured and standardized way, ensuring consistent and comparable data across different time periods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the `peer-check-in-form` field, which is a text input field where you can type your peer check-in information.
2. Provide your `peer-name` in the respective field. This is a text input field where you can enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### peer-check-in-form (text, optional)
*   The peer-check-in-form field is where you enter your peer check-in information. Please provide a clear and concise description of your activities and interactions.

### peer-name (text, optional)
*   The peer-name field is where you enter your name for the record. Please ensure that this is accurate and consistent with your other reports.

## Tips
*   Be as detailed as possible when filling out the peer-check-in-form field.
*   Ensure that your responses are accurate and truthful to maintain the reliability and validity of the data.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx for support.
