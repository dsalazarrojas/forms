# Simple Pageant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering users with general information such as name, contact details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the required fields, labeled with a "Required Field" label, which are:
	* Required Field (id: 10)
	* Another Required Field (id: 11)
2. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth in the Date of Birth field. Please use the date format MM/dd/yy to ensure correct submission.
5. Enter your Email Address in the Email Address field.
6. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
7. Select one option from the Select One dropdown field for your preference.
8. Select multiple options from the Select Multiple dropdown field for your preferences.
9. Enter a time in the Time field. Please use 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
10. If desired, add a note in the Note field for any additional information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`id: 1`, text, required: false): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`id: 2`, text, required: false): Enter your full last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 3`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/dd/yy.
* **Email Address** (`id: 4`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the standard email format (e.g., example@example.com).
* **Phone Number** (`id: 5`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the standard format (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Select One** (`id: 6`, select_one, required: false): Select one option from the dropdown list for your preference.
* **Select Multiple** (`id: 7`, select_multiple, required: false): Select multiple options from the dropdown list for your preferences.
* **Time** (`id: 8`, time, required: false): Enter a time in the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:30).
* **Note** (`id: 9`, note, required: false): Add any additional information in the note field.
* **Required Field** (`id: 10`, text, required: false): Enter the required field for submission.
* **Another Required Field** (`id: 11`, text, required: false): Enter another required field for submission.

## Tips

* Please ensure to fill out all the fields correctly to avoid any submission errors.
* Use standard formats for dates (MM/dd/yy), time (24-hour format), and email addresses (e.g., example@example.com).
* If a field is required, fill it out as it will be necessary for submission.
* The form is designed for general user information collection. Please use it accordingly.
