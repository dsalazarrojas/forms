# Abundance Flow Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for Abundance Flow sessions. By filling out this form, you will be providing essential information about yourself to help us better understand your needs and accommodate you for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number accurately.
3. Select the Abundance Flow session you are interested in registering for.
4. Choose a preferred session date and time (if applicable).
5. Answer questions about your intentions, background, and any previous experiences with wellness or coaching programs.
6. Confirm your consent to participate and agree to the terms of the program.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Field 2): Please enter your full name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Email address** (Field 3): Enter your email address to allow us to communicate with you about your session and any further instructions.
* **Phone number** (Field 4): Provide your phone number in case we need to contact you directly.
* **Date of birth** (Field 5): Enter your date of birth for our records, if you wish to share this information.
* **Session Type** (Field 7): Choose the Abundance Flow session type you are interested in registering for. This selection will determine the type of session you will attend.
* **Preferred session date** (Field 8): Choose a date for your session. Please note that sessions are subject to availability and scheduling.
* **Preferred session time** (Field 9): Select a time slot for your session, if you have a preference.
* **Have you participated in any wellness or coaching programs before?** (Field 11): Please select how many times you have participated in wellness or coaching programs.
* **Primary intention** (Field 12): Choose your primary intention for joining this session. This will help us tailor the experience to your needs.
* **What specific goals or outcomes do you hope to achieve?** (Field 13): Share your goals or outcomes for this session. This will help us support you effectively.
* **Do you have any physical or mental health conditions we should be aware of?** (Field 14): If you have any health concerns, please share them with us so we can ensure your comfort and safety.
* **I agree to participate willingly and understand that this program is not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment** (Field 16): Confirm that you understand the program's terms and are willing to participate.
* **How did you hear about Abundance Flow?** (Field 17): Share how you discovered Abundance Flow.
* **Any additional comments or questions** (Field 18): Use this space to share any additional thoughts, concerns, or questions you may have.

Please note that all fields marked as 'required' are essential for a successful registration process.
