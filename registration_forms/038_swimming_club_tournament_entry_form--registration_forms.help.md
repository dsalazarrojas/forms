# Swimming Club Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register swimmers to participate in a swimming club tournament. Fill out this form to provide necessary information about the swimmer and their parent(s) for the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). This form has a mix of text, select_one, select_multiple, date, and number fields that will guide you in filling out the form.
2.  Enter the swimmer's name in the `Swimmer Name` field.
3.  Enter the swimmer's club in the `Swimmers Club` field.
4.  Enter the swimmer's contact information in the `Swimmers Contact` field.
5.  Enter the parent's name in the `Parents Name` field.
6.  Enter the parent's contact information in the `Parents Contact` field.
7.  Select the appropriate event type from the `Event Type` field.
8.  Enter the event time in the `Event Time` field.
9.  Enter the event date in the `Event Date` field.
10. Enter the swimmer's date of birth in the `Swimmers Date Of Birth` field.
11. Enter the swimmer's age in the `Swimmers Age` field.
12. Select the swimmer's gender from the `Swimmers Gender` field.
13. Select the swimmer's level of swimming in the `Swimmers Level` field.
14. Enter the parents' date of birth in the `Parents Date Of Birth` field.
15. Enter the parents' age in the `Parents Age` field.
16. Enter the parent's phone number in the `Parents Phone` field.
17. Enter the parent's email in the `Parents Email` field.
18. Enter the swimmer's phone number in the `Swimmers Phone` field.
19. Enter the entry ID in the `Entry ID` field.
20. Select the entry status from the `Entry Status` field.
21. Enter the date submitted in the `Date Submitted` field.
22. Select the event from the `Event` field.
23. Select whether the swimmer is a member of a club from the `Club` field.
24. Select who submitted the entry from the `Submitted By` field.
