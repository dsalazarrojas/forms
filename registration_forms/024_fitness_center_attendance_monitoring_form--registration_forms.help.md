# fitness_center_attendance_monitoring_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect attendance information from users at a fitness center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email Address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the Date of your class in the "Class Date" field.
6. Enter the start time of your class in the "Start Time" field (optional).
7. Enter the end time of your class in the "End Time" field (optional).
8. Choose the fitness center where you attended the class in the "Fitness Center" field (optional).
9. Select the type of class you attended from the "Class Type" dropdown menu.
10. Choose the level of your class from the "Class Level" dropdown menu.
11. If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in the "Note" field (optional).
12. If you have any assigned tools or equipment, enter them in the "Assigned Tool" field (optional).
13. If relevant, select a category for your class in the "Category" field (optional).
14. If you want to add a description of your class, type it in the "Description" field (optional).
15. Enter the IDs of any other forms related to this attendance in the "Form IDs" field (optional).
16. If you want to save the output to a specific file, enter the file name in the "Output File" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Class Date**: Select the date of your class.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of your class (optional).
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of your class (optional).
* **Fitness Center**: Choose the fitness center where you attended the class (optional).
* **Class Type**: Select the type of class you attended from the dropdown menu.
* **Class Level**: Choose the level of your class from the dropdown menu.
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes you have about your class (optional).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any assigned tools or equipment (optional).
* **Category**: Select a category for your class (optional).
* **Description**: Add a description of your class (optional).
* **Form IDs**: Enter the IDs of any other forms related to this attendance (optional).
* **Output File**: Save the output to a specific file (optional).
