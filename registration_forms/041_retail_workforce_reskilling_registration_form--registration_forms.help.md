# Retail Workforce Reskilling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for retail employees who want to register for reskilling programs to enhance their skills and adapt to new roles or industries.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the reskilling programs you are interested in by choosing the relevant options in the "Choose reskilling programs" section.
2. Enter your name in the "Enter Employee Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your availability date in the "Availability" field (optional).
5. Enter your start date (if applicable) in the "Start Date" field (optional).
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes about your registration in the "Notes" field (optional).
8. Choose your preferred training time in the "Preferred Training Time" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose reskilling programs**: This section allows you to select the reskilling programs you are interested in. Please choose one or multiple options that suit your needs.
* **Enter Employee Name**: This field is for entering your employee name as it appears on your personnel records.
* **Email**: This field is for entering your work email address, which will be used for communication related to the reskilling programs.
* **Availability**: This field is for entering your availability date, which will help us schedule training sessions accordingly.
* **Start Date**: This field is for entering the date you want to start the reskilling program (if applicable).
* **Phone**: This field is for entering your contact phone number, which will be used for communication and reminders.
* **Notes**: This field is for providing any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Preferred Training Time**: This section is for choosing your preferred training time, which will be taken into consideration when scheduling training sessions.
