# Simple Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for registration purposes. Please complete the required fields for accurate and complete data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select your Date of Birth from the Date of Birth field.
6. Select the Date of Registration from the Date of Registration field.
7. Choose your Registration Type from the Registration Type field.
8. Enter your Service Name in the Service Name field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (1)** (text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (2)** (text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email (3)** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone (4)** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (5)** (date, required: false): Select the date of your birth.
* **Date of Registration (6)** (date, required: false): Select the date of your registration.
* **Registration Type (7)** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose your registration type. Options: Individual, Organization, Group.
* **Service Name (8)** (text, required: false): Enter the name of the service you are registering for.
