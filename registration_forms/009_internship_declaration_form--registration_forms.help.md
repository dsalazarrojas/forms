# internship_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help students accurately declare their internship experiences, providing detailed information about the internship, including start and end dates, location, and the student's role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have provided distinct information about each internship experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **student_info** (`student_info`, text, optional): Enter your student information, which will be used to track your account.
- **internship_details** (`internship_details`, text, optional): Briefly describe your internship experience, including the company you worked with, your role, and any notable accomplishments or challenges.
- **student_name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you'd like it to be displayed on your internship declaration.
- **student_id** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
- **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you'd like to be contacted about your internship declaration.
- **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you'd like to be contacted about your internship declaration.
- **start_date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your internship experience.
- **end_date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your internship experience.
- **internship_title** (`internship_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your internship.
- **company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company where you did your internship.
- **location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your internship.
- **supervisor_name** (`supervisor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor's name.
- **supervisor_email** (`supervisor_email`, email, optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
- **internship_hours** (`internship_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you worked during your internship.
- **academic_credits** (`academic_credits`, text, optional): Enter any academic credits you earned for your internship.
- **evaluation** (`evaluation`, note, optional): Write a short evaluation of your internship experience.
- **internship_type** (`internship_type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of internship experience you had (Intern, Co-op, Volunteer, or Paid).
- **internship_status** (`internship_status`, select multiple, optional): Choose the status of your internship experience (Active, Completed, In Progress, or Withdrawn).
- **student_comments** (`student_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your internship experience.
- **supervisor_comments** (`supervisor_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments from your supervisor about your internship experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields to avoid any issues with submitting the form.
* If you've had multiple internship experiences, you can submit this form multiple times, but ensure that each submission provides distinct information about a different internship experience.
* Review your submitted form for accuracy before submitting it.
* If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to your institution's administrative team for guidance.
