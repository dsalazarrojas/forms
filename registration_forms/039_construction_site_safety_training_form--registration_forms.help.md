# Construction Site Safety Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to record and track the details of safety training sessions on construction sites. The form captures information about the training date, time, location, trainer, and trainees, as well as their understanding and feedback about the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill in the required fields with the correct information. Make sure to select the correct options for each field, and provide any additional comments or feedback as necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the training session in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the training session in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the training was held.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor who conducted the training.
* **Trainer Credentials** (`trainer_credentials`, text, required): Enter the qualifications and experience of the instructor.
* **Training Topic** (`training_topic`, select_one, required): Select the subject of the training (e.g. General Site Safety, Fall Protection).
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, select_one, required): Select the length of the training session (e.g. 15 to 30 minutes, 1 to 2 hours).
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the trainee.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainee's employer.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required): Enter the trainee's job title or position.
* **Employee ID** (`employee_id`, text, optional): Enter the trainee's employee ID (if applicable).
* **Previous Similar Training** (`previous_training`, select_one, required): Select whether the trainee has had similar training in the past.
* **Training Format** (`training_format`, select_one, required): Select how the training was delivered (e.g. Classroom Lecture, Hands-On Demonstration).
* **Topics Covered** (`topics_covered`, select_multiple, required): Select all topics that were covered during the training session.
* **Comprehension Verified** (`comprehension_verified`, select_one, required): Select how the trainer confirmed understanding (e.g. Written Test, Group Discussion).
* **Test Score** (`test_score`, number, optional): Enter the percentage score if tested.
* **Training Materials Provided** (`training_materials`, select_one, optional): Select whether training materials were provided.
* **PPE Proper Use Demonstrated** (`ppe_demonstrated`, select_one, optional): Select whether PPE use was demonstrated.
* **Your Level of Understanding** (`participant_understanding`, select_one, required): Select how well you understand the training material.
* **Was Training Helpful** (`training_helpful`, select_one, required): Rate the usefulness of the training session.
* **Certification Provided** (`certification_provided`, select_one, optional): Select whether a certification was provided.
* **Additional Training Needed** (`additional_training_needed`, text, optional): Enter any additional training needed.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any feedback or comments about the training session.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, required): Sign your full name to confirm completion of the form.
* **Trainer Signature** (`trainer_signature`, text, required): Sign your full name to confirm delivery of the form.
