# HVAC Systems Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in or participating in HVAC systems training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
5. Select your date of birth from the calendar in the **Date of Birth** field.
6. Select the **Training Program** you are interested in or have participated in.
7. Select your **Participant Status**, which indicates whether you are new, current, or an alumnus of the training program.
8. Select the **Training Date** you plan to or have attended the training.
9. Select the **Training Time** of the training session.
10. Enter the number of **Training Hours** you have completed or plan to complete.
11. Select the **Training Location** where you attended or plan to attend the training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that will be used to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, select_one, optional): Select the HVAC systems training program you are interested in or have participated in. Options: HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you are New, Current, or Alumni of the training program.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to or have attended the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the training session.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have completed or plan to complete for the training program.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, optional): Select the location where you attended or plan to attend the training. Options: Location 1, Location 2, Location 3.
