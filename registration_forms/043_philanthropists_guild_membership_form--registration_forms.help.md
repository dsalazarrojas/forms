# Philanthropists Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Philanthropists Guild Membership Form is designed to gather information from users who want to become members of the Philanthropists Guild. This form is used to collect relevant details about the user and their intentions to join the guild.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page according to the instructions below.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Registration Form

* **Title**: `registration_form` (`title`, text, required): This field is the main title of the form, which you will not need to fill out. It is only for identification purposes.
* **Address Form**: `address_form` (`address_form`, select_one, required): Select one of the provided options to confirm your address for registration purposes.
* **City Form**: `city_form` (`city_form`, select_multiple, optional): Select all cities where you have a presence or affiliation with the Philanthropists Guild. You can choose multiple cities by holding down the Ctrl (or Command on a Mac) key while selecting.
* **State Form**: `state_form` (`state_form`, select_one, optional): Select your state of residence or main affiliation.
* **Zip Form**: `zip_form` (`zip_form`, number, required): Enter your zip code for registration purposes.
* **Email Form**: `email_form` (`email_form`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates from the Philanthropists Guild.
* **Phone Form**: `phone_form` (`phone_form`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes Form**: `notes_form` (`notes_form`, note, optional): Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your membership registration.

### Page 2: Zip Information

* **Zip Form**: `zip_form` (`zip_form`, text, required): Enter your zip code for registration purposes.
* **Zip Notes Form**: `zip_notes_form` (`zip_notes_form`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your zip code.
* **Organization Notes Form**: `organization_notes_form` (`organization_notes_form`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your organization or affiliation.
* **Submitted By Form**: `submitted_by_form` (`submitted_by_form`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization submitting the application on your behalf.

### Additional Information

* **Organization Form**: `organization_form` (`organization_form`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name and details of the organization you are affiliated with.
* **Role Form**: `role_form` (`role_form`, text, optional): Enter your role within the organization or the Philanthropists Guild, if applicable.
* **Form Notes**: `form_notes` (`form_notes`, note, optional): Any additional comments or questions you have for the registration process.
* **User Notes**: `user_notes` (`user_notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or personal notes for the Philanthropists Guild.
