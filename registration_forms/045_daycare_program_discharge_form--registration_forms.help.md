# Daycare Program Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Daycare Program Discharge Form is used to document a child's discharge from a daycare program. This form is essential for updating the child's status in the system and ensuring continuity of care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the discharge date in the format of a calendar date (MM/DD/YYYY).
2. Enter the discharge time in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
3. Provide a reason for the child's discharge in the text field.
4. Sign as the parent/guardian to confirm the discharge.
5. Sign as the child to confirm their understanding and agreement to the discharge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **discharge_date** (Date, Not Required): Enter the date the child is being discharged from the daycare program. This date should be in the format of a calendar date (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **discharge_time** (Time, Not Required): Enter the time of the child's discharge in the format of HH:MM AM/PM.
* **discharge_reason** (Text, Required): Provide a clear and concise explanation for the reason the child is being discharged from the daycare program.
* **parent_signature** (Note, Not Required): Sign as the parent/guardian to confirm the discharge.
* **child_signature** (Note, Not Required): Have the child sign to acknowledge and agree to the discharge.
* **discharge_comment** (Text, Not Required): Provide any additional comments or notes related to the child's discharge.
