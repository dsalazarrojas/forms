# Dream Life Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for the Dream Life Workshop. The form is used to register participants for the workshop and collect essential details such as name, email, phone number, and registration date. This form is used to ensure that participants can be effectively managed and informed about the workshop schedule and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the workshop title in the "Workshop Title" field.
2. Enter the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Enter the participant's email in the "Email" field.
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select the workshop date and time using the "Workshop Date" and "Workshop Time" fields.
6. Add any additional participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field.
7. Choose the workshop type from the options provided in the "Workshop Type" field.
8. Enter any additional workshop information in the "Additional Workshop Info" field.
9. Enter the registration date and time using the "Registration Date" and "Registration Time" fields.
10. Choose the payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
11. Enter the participant's workshop location in the "Workshop Location" field.
12. If the participant's location is not listed, enter it in the "Workshop Location (Other)" field.
13. Enter the event ID in the "Event ID" field.
14. Enter the workshop ID in the "Workshop ID" field.
15. Enter the workshop duration in the "Workshop Duration" field.
16. Enter the workshop capacity in the "Workshop Capacity" field.
17. Enter any additional information about the participant in the "Additional Info" field.
18. Enter the participant's email in the "Participant Email" field.
19. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
20. Enter the participant's address in the "Participant Address" field.
21. Enter the participant's city in the "Participant City" field.
22. Enter the participant's state in the "Participant State" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Title** (1, text, required): Enter the title of the workshop you wish to register for.
* **Participant Name** (2, text, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter the email of the participant.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Workshop Date** (5, date, required): Select the date of the workshop you wish to register for.
* **Workshop Time** (6, time, required): Select the time of the workshop you wish to register for.
* **Participant Notes** (7, note, required): Add any additional notes about the participant.
* **Workshop Type** (8, select_one, required): Choose the type of workshop you are registering for.
* **Additional Workshop Info** (9, text, required): Enter any additional information about the workshop.
* **Registration Date** (10, date, required): Enter the date of registration.
* **Registration Time** (11, time, required): Enter the time of registration.
* **Payment Method** (12, select_one, required): Choose the payment method for the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (13, text, required): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location (Other)** (14, text, required): If the location is not listed, enter it here.
* **Event ID** (15, text, required): Enter the ID of the event.
* **Workshop ID** (16, text, required): Enter the ID of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (17, number, required): Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity** (18, number, required): Enter the capacity of the workshop.
* **Additional Info** (19, text, required): Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **Participant Email** (20, email, required): Enter the email of the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (21, text, required): Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Address** (22, text, required): Enter the address of the participant.
* **Participant City** (23, text, required): Enter the city of the participant.
* **Participant State** (24, text, required): Enter the state of the participant.
