# Executive Staff Onboarding Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from executive staff members during their onboarding process, including their affiliation status and employee details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the correct page by clicking on the corresponding button.
2. Fill in the required fields for each page.
3. Submit the form once you have filled in all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registration Page 1

* **Registration** (`Employer/External Team/Internal Team`, Select Multiple, Required: False): Choose the team you belong to for registration purposes.
  - Please select the team that best describes your affiliation.
* **Employee Info** (`text`, Text, Required: False): Enter your employee information, including your name and job title.
  - Please enter your name and job title in this field.
* **Work Info** (`text`, Text, Required: False): Provide a brief description of your work responsibilities.
  - Enter a brief description of your work responsibilities.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are filling in the correct information for the correct team.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
