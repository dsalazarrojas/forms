# job_fair_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The job_fair_attendance_form is a registration form designed for attendees of a job fair event. It is used to collect demographic information and track attendee engagement with the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the `Name` field.
2. Enter your valid contact email in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the `Contact Phone` field.
4. Enter your company name in the `Company` field.
5. Enter your position in the `Position` field.
6. Enter your job fair ID in the `Job Fair ID` field.
7. Select your check-in time from the available options in the `Check In Time` field.
8. If you were checked in by someone, enter their name in the `Checked In By` field.
9. Add any follow-up notes in the `Follow-up Notes` field.
10. Select whether or not you attended the event from the options in the `Attended Event` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company** (`Company`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous company name.
* **Position** (`Position`, text, optional): Enter your current or previous position title.
* **Job Fair ID** (`Job Fair ID`, text, optional): Enter the ID or number assigned to you by the event organizers.
* **Check In Time** (`Check In Time`, time, optional): Select the time you arrived at the job fair event.
* **Checked In By** (`Checked In By`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who checked you in.
* **Follow-up Notes** (`Follow-up Notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
