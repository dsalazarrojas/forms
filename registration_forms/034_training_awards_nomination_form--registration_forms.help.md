# training_awards_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is used to gather information for nominating individuals for recognition and rewards related to their training and development activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Nomination" option from the first field.
2. Enter the name of the person who is being nominated (the "Nominator" field).
3. Enter the name of the organization or department where the nominator works (the "Nominator Organization" field).
4. Select the title of the nominator (the "Nominator Title" field).
5. Enter the name of the person being nominated (the "Nominee" field).
6. Enter the date of the nomination (the "Nomination Date" field).
7. Enter the time of the nomination (the "Nomination Time" field).
8. Enter the name of the award being nominated for (the "Award Name" field).
9. Enter the year of the award being nominated for (the "Award Year" field).
10. Enter a brief reason for the nomination (the "Reason for Nomination" field).
11. Enter the email address of the nominator (the "Nominator E-mail" field).
12. Enter the phone number of the nominator (the "Nominator Phone" field).
13. Enter any additional comments or message from the nominator (the "Nominator Message" field).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Nomination**: This is the selection field for choosing the type of nomination. Please select "Nomination" to proceed with the nomination.
* **Nominator**: Enter the name of the person who is nominating the individual.
* **Nominator Organization**: Enter the name of the organization or department where the nominator works.
* **Nominator Title**: Select the title of the nominator, such as President or Manager.
* **Nominee**: Enter the name of the person being nominated.
* **Nomination Date**: Enter the date of the nomination, in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Nomination Time**: Enter the time of the nomination.
* **Award Name**: Enter the name of the award being nominated for.
* **Award Year**: Enter the year of the award being nominated for.
* **Reason for Nomination**: Enter a brief explanation for the nomination.
* **Nominator E-mail**: Enter the email address of the nominator.
* **Nominator Phone**: Enter the phone number of the nominator.
* **Nominator Message**: Enter any additional comments or message from the nominator.
