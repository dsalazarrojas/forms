# small_group_leader_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals for small group leader training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Training Session** (select_one, required): Select your preferred training session.
- **Training Time** (time, required): Enter the time you will be available for training.
- **Additional Comments** (note, optional): Fill in any additional comments or information about your training.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's name.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (text, optional): Enter your relationship with your emergency contact.
- **Medical Conditions** (text, optional): List any medical conditions you have.
- **Allergies** (text, optional): List any allergies you have.
- **Medications** (text, optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
- **Medical History** (text, optional): Fill in any relevant medical history.
- **Other Emergency Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter any other emergency contact information.
- **Medical Notes** (note, optional): Fill in any additional medical notes.
- **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (text, optional): Enter another emergency contact's phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship 2** (text, optional): Enter your relationship with another emergency contact.
