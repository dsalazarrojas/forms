# Budget Friendly Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us provide a budget-friendly accommodation solution for your travel needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents (Guest Full Name).
2. Provide your Email Address for communication and updates.
3. Enter your phone number for easy contact.
4. Specify the total number of people traveling with you.
5. Enter your Check-In and Check-Out dates.
6. Choose the destination city or location you are traveling to.
7. Select the type of accommodation you prefer (e.g., Hostel, Budget Hotel, Guesthouse, etc.).
8. Choose the room type (e.g., Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room, etc.).
9. Select your budget range per night.
10. Choose the essential amenities you need (e.g., Free WiFi, Kitchen, Breakfast, etc.).
11. If needed, specify any accessibility requirements you have.
12. Enter any dietary restrictions you may have.
13. Select the location preference for the accommodation (e.g., Downtown, near transit, quiet area, etc.).
14. Choose your booking flexibility (e.g., Fixed dates, Flexible dates, etc.).
15. Indicate if you need a price guarantee in advance.
16. Select your preferred contact method for booking confirmation.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Full Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, `email`, required): Provide your email address for us to communicate with you and send updates.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Specify the total number of people traveling with you.
* **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Enter your Check-In date.
* **Check-Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required): Enter your Check-Out date.
* **Destination City or Location** (`destination_city`, `text`, required): Choose the city or location you are traveling to.
* **Accommodation Type Preferred** (`accommodation_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of accommodation you prefer (e.g., Hostel, Budget Hotel, Guesthouse, etc.).
* **Room Type** (`room_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the room type (e.g., Single Room, Double Room, Triple Room, etc.).
* **Budget Range per Night** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, required): Select your budget range per night.
* **Essential Amenities** (`amenities_needed`, `select_multiple`, not required): Choose the essential amenities you need (e.g., Free WiFi, Kitchen, Breakfast, etc.).
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, not required): If needed, specify any accessibility requirements you have (e.g., Wheelchair, elevator, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_requirements`, `text`, not required): If you have any dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Location Preference** (`location_preference`, `text`, not required): Select the location preference for the accommodation (e.g., Downtown, near transit, quiet area, etc.).
* **Booking Flexibility** (`booking_flexibility`, `select_one`, required): Choose your booking flexibility (e.g., Fixed dates, Flexible dates, etc.).
* **Price Guarantee Important** (`price_guarantee`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you need a price guarantee in advance.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred contact method for booking confirmation (e.g., Email, Phone, Text Message, etc.).
