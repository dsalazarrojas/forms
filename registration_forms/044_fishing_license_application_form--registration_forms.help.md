# Fishing License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to apply for a fishing license. Please complete it accurately to ensure a smooth and efficient application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your basic information.
2. Select the correct Fish License Type (New, Renewal, or Replacement).
3. Choose the type of fishing you wish to engage in (Freshwater, Saltwater, or Combination).
4. Indicate your preferred fishing location (River, Lake, or Ocean).
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Provide your Email and Phone numbers for communication purposes.
7. Add any Additional Info if needed.
8. Select the status of your current fish license (Active, Expired, or Pending).
9. Upload your User Photo (if required).
10. Sign the Signature field (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name, address, and other relevant contact information.
* **Fish License Type** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose the type of fish license you need (New, Renewal, or Replacement).
* **License Type** (Select One, Required/Optional): Select the type of fishing license you require (Freshwater, Saltwater, or Combination).
* **Fishing Location** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Choose your preferred fishing location (River, Lake, or Ocean).
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Additional Info** (Note, Required/Optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your application.
* **Fish License Status** (Select One, Required/Optional): Indicate the status of your current fish license (Active, Expired, or Pending).
* **User Photo** (Note, Required/Optional): Upload a photo of yourself for identity verification purposes (if required).
* **Signature** (Note, Required/Optional): Sign the form to confirm your application (if required).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to prevent delays in processing your application.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
* Double-check your application before submission to avoid errors.
