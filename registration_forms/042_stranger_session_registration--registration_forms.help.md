# Stranger Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stranger Session Registration form is designed to collect information for a stranger session, ensuring accurate scheduling and capacity management.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the date of the stranger session you wish to register for.
2. Choose the time slot for the session.
3. Specify the expected duration of the session.
4. Enter the number of participants you can accommodate.
5. Optionally, enter the maximum number of participants you can handle.
6. Provide your name and contact information (email and phone number) so we can get in touch with you.
7. Add any additional notes about your session.
8. Indicate if you are willing to attend future sessions.
9. If unsure, select "Maybe" for your availability.
10. Confirm your session scheduling preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_date** (Date): Select the date of the stranger session you wish to attend.
* **session_time** (Time): Choose a time slot for the session.
* **session_duration** (Text): Enter the expected duration of the session.
* **session_capacity** (Number): Enter the number of participants you can accommodate.
* **session_max_capacity** (Number): Optionally, enter the maximum number of participants you can handle.
* **participant_name** (Text): Provide your name.
* **participant_email** (Email): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **participant_phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **participant_notes** (Notes): Add any additional notes about the session.
* **session_scheduling** (Select One): Indicate if you are willing to attend future sessions.
	+ 'True': You are sure you'll attend future sessions.
	+ 'False': You are not willing to attend future sessions.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure about attending future sessions.
* **participant_scheduling** (Select One): Indicate if you are willing to participate in future sessions.
	+ 'True': You are sure you'll participate in future sessions.
	+ 'False': You are not willing to participate in future sessions.
	+ Maybe: You are unsure about participating in future sessions.

## Tips
* Be as accurate as possible when providing session information.
* If unsure about any field, try your best to estimate or provide an approximate value.
* Fill out the required fields carefully to ensure proper recordkeeping.
