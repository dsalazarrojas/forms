## Basketball Officials Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect personal and certification information from individuals who wish to register for a basketball officials' training session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your personal details in the Personal Information section.
2. Select your current official certification level and years of officiating experience.
3. Indicate if you have previously attended an official training session.
4. Choose the training session you wish to attend.
5. Select your preferred training date and location.
6. Indicate the level of basketball you wish to officiate.
7. Provide any relevant background information (playing or coaching experience).
8. Confirm your payment method and agree to the code of conduct for officials.
9. Review and confirm your registration information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Current official certification** (`current_certification`, select_one, required): Select your current official certification level (None, State Level, Regional Level, National Level, International).
* **Years of officiating experience** (`years_experience`, number, optional): Enter your years of officiating experience.
* **Previous official training** (`previous_training`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have previously attended an official training session.
* **Training session** (`training_session`, select_one, required): Choose the training session you wish to attend (Beginner Clinic, Intermediate Training, Advanced Certification, Rules Update, Mechanics Workshop).
* **Preferred training date** (`training_date`, date, required): Select your preferred training date.
* **Training location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Level to officiate** (`level_to_officiate`, select_multiple, optional): Select the level of basketball you wish to officiate (Youth (Under 12), High School, College, Professional, International).
* **Basketball playing experience** (`playing_experience`, text, optional): Enter any basketball playing experience.
* **Coaching experience** (`coaching_experience`, text, optional): Enter any coaching experience.
* **Training fee** (`training_fee`, number, optional): Enter the training fee (not applicable if free).
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select how you will pay for the training session (Credit Card, Check, Cash, Online Transfer).
* **Code of conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to the code of conduct for officials.
* **Registration complete** (`registration_complete`, select_one, required): Confirm that your registration information is accurate.
