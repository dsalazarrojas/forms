# Corporate Seminar Billing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect billing information from participants or clients for corporate seminars. Ensure that you fill out this form for a valid seminar billing purpose and not for any other reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Seminar Details** section with information about the seminar you attended or booked. This will help the billing team to identify the seminar correctly.
2. In the **Participant Details** section, fill in your personal information, such as name and email.
3. The **Billing Information** section is for providing billing details, such as name, email, and billing address.
4. Finally, fill in the **Payment Info** section with your payment details, such as payment method and amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Details (Seminar Details, text, required):** This is the first field on the form where you need to fill in details about the seminar. Please provide a clear and concise title of the seminar you attended or booked.
* **Participant Details (Participant Details, text, optional):** This is the second field on the form where you need to fill in your personal details. If you're filling out this form for someone else, make sure to fill in their details accurately.
* **Billing Information (Billing Information, text, optional):** This section is for providing billing details for the seminar. This is usually the name and contact information of the individual or company responsible for billing.
* **Payment Info (Payment Info, text, optional):** In this field, you'll need to fill in your payment method and amount. Please ensure that you're filling this out accurately to avoid any billing discrepancies.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field or have questions, you can reach out to the form creator at form@gic.mx for assistance.
* Make sure to save your progress as you go along, as the form does not autosave.
* If you're filling out this form for the first time, take a moment to review the explanation for each field to ensure you understand what's being asked.
