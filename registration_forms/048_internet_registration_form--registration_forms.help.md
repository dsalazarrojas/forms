# Internet Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internet Registration Form is used to collect user's contact information for registration and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used for communication.
3. Enter your **Street** address, which will be used to identify your location.
4. Enter the name of your **City**.
5. Select your **State** from the provided options.
6. Enter your **ZIP Code** from the provided options.
7. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
8. Choose a date and time for the appointment from the date and time fields.
9. You may add **Notes** for additional information.
10. You can add **Note 1** and **Note 2** for further information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address for communication.
* **Street** (`street`, `text`, optional): Enter your street address for identification purposes.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city name where you reside or have an office.
* **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Select your state from the provided options.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip`, `select_one`, required): Select your zip code from the provided options.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the appointment date from the provided options.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the appointment time from the provided options.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Note 1** (`note_1`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Note 2** (`note_2`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid any issues with the registration process.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team.
* The form is designed to be filled out in a few minutes. If you have any questions, please don't hesitate to ask.
