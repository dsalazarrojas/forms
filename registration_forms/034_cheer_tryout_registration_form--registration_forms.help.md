# Cheer Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from athletes who are interested in trying out for a Cheer team. This form will help us understand the athlete's personal data, cheer skills, experience, and medical status, which will be used in the evaluation and selection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Make sure to provide your preferred stunt position and highest tumbling skill mastered.
3. Select your current grade level and GPA.
4. Choose your preferred stunt position and other tumbling skills you are currently working on.
5. Indicate your medical release and authorization, and understanding of the non-refundable tryout fee.
6. Sign with your parent or guardian's name, and submit the form with your application date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Grade in Upcoming Fall**: Select your grade level for the upcoming fall.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your parent's or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your parent's or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone Number**: Enter your parent's or guardian's phone number.
* **Preferred Stunt Position**: Select one or more of the stunt positions you are interested in trying out.
* **Highest Tumbling Skill Mastered**: Enter the highest tumbling skill you have mastered (e.g., Standing Back Tuck, Running Layout).
* **Tumbling Skills Currently Working On**: List any tumbling skills you are currently working on.
* **Total Years of Cheer Experience**: Enter the total number of years you have been cheering.
* **Current GPA**: Enter your current GPA.
* **Medical Release and Authorization**: Select "I Authorize and Release" to indicate that you are physically fit to try out.
* **Understanding of Non-Refundable Tryout Fee**: Select "I Understand" to indicate that you understand the tryout fee is non-refundable.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Type your name to sign the form.
* **Date of Application**: Enter the date you submit this application.

Note: Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
