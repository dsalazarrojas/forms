# Digital Study Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering members of the Digital Study Club, collecting their user information, interests, and other relevant details. It's designed to be completed by potential members, allowing them to sign up and provide necessary information for the club's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information, including any required fields such as name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your interests from the provided options.
3. Select your preferred format and tool versions.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
5. Choose whether you are assigned a tool or not.
6. Select your output file format and name.
7. Choose your category and other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Member Details**: (`member_details`, text, required): This field is for collecting member details, but it seems to be optional. Please ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
* **Format Preferences**: (`format_preferences`, text, required): Select your preferred format and tool versions.
* **Interests**: (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose your interests from the provided options.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date**: (`date`, date, required): Select the date you are registering for.
* **Time**: (`time`, time, required): Select the time you are registering for.
* **Notes**: (`notes`, note, required): Provide any additional notes or comments.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Choose whether you are assigned a tool or not.
* **Output File**: (`output_file`, select_one, required): Select your output file format.
* **Category**: (`category`, select_one, required): Choose your category or type.
* **Assigned Tool Name**: (`assigned_tool_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the assigned tool.
* **Assigned Tool Version**: (`assigned_tool_version`, number, required): Enter the version of the assigned tool.
* **Form ID**: (`form_id`, number, required): This field seems to be for internal use, but its purpose is unclear.
* **Output File Name**: (`output_file_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the output file.
* **Category 2**: (`category_2`, select_one, required): Choose your category or type.
* **Description**: (`description`, text, required): Enter a description of your purpose or goals.
* **Form Version**: (`form_version`, number, required): Enter the version of the form.
* **Output File Format**: (`output_file_format`, select_one, required): Select your output file format.
* **Form Description**: (`form_description`, text, required): Enter a description of the form.
* **Category 3**: (`category_3`, select_one, required): Choose your category or type.

* **Form Version 2**: (`form_version_2`, text, required): Enter the version of the form.
