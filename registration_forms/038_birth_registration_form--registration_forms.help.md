# Birth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Birth Registration Form is a crucial document that provides official proof of a child's birth. It's a critical piece of information required for various administrative and legal purposes, such as applying for government services, accessing health care, and enrolling in school.

## How To Complete This Form
This form is divided into sections that guide you through the required information. Please complete the form by following these steps:

1.  Enter the newborn's details:
	* **Baby's First Name**: Enter the given name of the infant.
	* **Baby's Last Name**: Enter the family surname of the infant.
	* **Date of Birth**: Enter the full date of the infant's birth.
	* **Time of Birth**: Enter the hour and minute of the infant's birth.
	* **Sex of Infant**: Choose the correct sex of the infant.
	* **Weight at Birth**: Enter the weight of the infant in grams.
	* **Length at Birth**: Enter the length of the infant in centimeters or inches.
	* **Head Circumference**: Enter the circumference of the infant's head in centimeters or inches.
2.  Enter the birth location information:
	* **Place of Birth**: Choose the location where the infant was born.
	* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the hospital or birth center.
	* **City of Birth**: Enter the city where the infant was born.
	* **County of Birth**: Enter the county where the infant was born.
	* **State of Birth**: Enter the state where the infant was born.
	* **Country of Birth**: Enter the country where the infant was born.
3.  Enter the mother's details:
	* **Mother First Name**: Enter the mother's given name.
	* **Mother Last Name**: Enter the mother's current surname.
	* **Mother Date of Birth**: Enter the mother's birth date.
	* **Mother Age at Birth**: Enter the mother's age at the time of the child's birth.
	* **Mother's Current Address**: Enter the mother's current address.
	* **Mother Country of Origin**: Enter the mother's country of origin or citizenship.
4.  Enter the father's details (if applicable):
	* **Father's Name on Birth Certificate**: Choose whether the father's name will be included on the birth certificate.
	* **Father First Name**: Enter the father's given name.
	* **Father Middle Name**: Enter the father's middle name.
	* **Father Last Name**: Enter the father's surname.
	* **Father Date of Birth**: Enter the father's birth date.
	* **Father Age at Birth**: Enter the father's age at the time of the child's birth.
	* **Father's Current Address**: Enter the father's current address.
	* **Father Country of Origin**: Enter the father's country of origin or citizenship.
5.  Enter health information:
	* **Apgar Score**: Enter the 1-minute and 5-minute scores.
	* **Method of Delivery**: Choose the method of delivery.
	* **Any Congenital Conditions**: Enter any congenital conditions or defects.
