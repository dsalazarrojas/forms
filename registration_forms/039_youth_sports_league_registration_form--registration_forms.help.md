# youth_sports_league_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youth sports league registration form is designed to collect essential information from parents, athletes, and coaches to facilitate smooth administration and management of youth sports leagues. This form aims to gather general information, parent details, athlete specifics, payment data, emergency contact, athlete medical information, athlete skills, and coach information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to understand the required fields and sections.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
3. Submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (Page 1)** (`general_info`, `text`, required: false): This section collects basic details about the registration process. You can enter a brief description of the athlete or team.
* **Parent Information (Page 2)** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide essential information about the athlete's parent or guardian, including name, contact details, and any relevant contact preferences.
* **Athlete Information (Page 3)** (`athlete_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter essential details about the athlete, such as name, age, and any relevant contact information.
* **Payment Info (Page 4)** (`payment`, `text`, required: false): Submit payment or fee information as per the specified instructions.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 5)** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide contact information for an emergency contact person, such as a family member or friend.
* **Athlete Medical Info (Page 6)** (`athlete_medical`, `text`, required: false): Enter any relevant medical information about the athlete, including allergies, disabilities, or medical conditions.
* **Athlete Skills (Page 7)** (`athlete_skills`, `text`, required: false): List any relevant skills or abilities the athlete possesses, such as sports experience or training.
* **Coach Info (Page 8)** (`coach_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter essential details about the coach, including name, contact information, and any relevant coaching certifications.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all necessary information before completing the form.
- Double-check for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field or section, contact the relevant department or authority for guidance.
