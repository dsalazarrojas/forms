# Literature Discussion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Literature Discussion Registration Form is used to register participants for a literature discussion session. This form collects information from participants, including their name, contact details, and library/school affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information:
	* Fill in your name in the "Participant Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
2. Select your session participation:
	* Choose whether you will participate in each available session.
3. Add any additional sessions you'd like to participate in:
	* Enter any additional sessions you'd like to attend.
4. Provide your librarian/school information:
	* Enter your librarian's name in the "Librarian Name" field.
	* Enter your librarian's email address in the "Librarian Email" field.
	* Enter your librarian's phone number in the "Librarian Phone" field.
	* Enter your school or library name in the "School Name" field.
	* Enter your school or library contact person's name in the "School Contact" field.
	* Enter your school or library address in the "School Address" field.
5. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the session you are participating in.
  - **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to identify yourself as a participant.
  - **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the session.
  - **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the session.
  - **Session Participation** (`participant_session_participation`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether you will participate in each available session.
  - **Additional Sessions** (`participant_additional_sessions`, text, required: false): Enter any additional sessions you'd like to attend.
  - **Librarian Name** (`librarian_name`, text, required: false): Enter your librarian's name, if applicable.
  - **Librarian Email** (`librarian_email`, email, required: false): Enter your librarian's email address, if applicable.
  - **Librarian Phone** (`librarian_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your librarian's phone number, if applicable.
  - **Library Name** (`library_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the library or school you are affiliated with.
  - **Library Contact** (`library_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your library or school contact person.
  - **Library Address** (`library_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your library or school.
  - **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the school you are affiliated with.
  - **School Contact** (`school_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school contact person.
  - **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of your school.

## Tips
* Ensure you only register once per session or participant to avoid duplication.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your librarian or school administrator.
