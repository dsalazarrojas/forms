# Guest Behavior Analytics Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from attendees for the Guest Behavior Analytics Summit. Providing accurate and complete information is crucial to help us tailor the event to your interests and ensure a productive experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with your name, email address, job title, and company information.
2. Select your role in the organization from the provided options.
3. If you have social media profiles, please enter your relevant URLs (e.g., LinkedIn, Twitter, or Instagram).
4. Review and submit your form. Our team will review and process your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, `text`, required)
	+ This section asks for your name, email address, job title, and company information. Please provide accurate and up-to-date details.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your name as it appears on official documents or business cards.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required)
	+ Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your job title or position within your organization.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required)
	+ Enter your company name as it appears on official documents or business cards.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required)
	+ Select your role in the organization from the provided options.
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your department or team within your organization (if applicable).
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter a brief description of your job role or responsibilities.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter the location where you currently work or live.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your work phone number or mobile number.
* **Bio** (`bio`, `note`, optional)
	+ Enter a brief bio or description about yourself, your interests, or your expertise.
* **Company URL** (`company_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your company's website URL.
* **LinkedIn URL** (`linkedin_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your LinkedIn profile URL.
* **Twitter URL** (`twitter_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your Twitter profile URL.
* **LinkedIn ID** (`linkedin_id`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your LinkedIn ID or profile name.
* **Twitter ID** (`twitter_id`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your Twitter ID or profile name.
* **Instagram ID** (`instagram_id`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your Instagram profile name or ID.
* **LinkedIn URL (again)** (`linkedin_linkedin_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is a duplicate of the LinkedIn URL field and can be ignored.
* **Twitter URL (again)** (`twitter_linkedin_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is a duplicate of the Twitter URL field and can be ignored.
* **Instagram URL (again)** (`instagram_linkedin_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ This field is a duplicate of the Instagram URL field and can be ignored.
* **Facebook URL** (`linkedin_facebook_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your Facebook profile URL.
* **YouTube URL** (`linkedin_youtube_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your YouTube profile URL.
* **Snapchat URL** (`linkedin_snapchat_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your Snapchat profile URL.
* **Twitch URL** (`linkedin_twitch_url`, `text`, optional)
	+ Enter your Twitch profile URL.
