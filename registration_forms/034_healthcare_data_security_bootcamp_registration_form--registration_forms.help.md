# Healthcare Data Security Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in attending a Healthcare Data Security Bootcamp. The form gathers attendee details, payment information, preferences, and consent for the event. Completing this form will enable you to register for the bootcamp and receive the necessary information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Attendee Information page (page 1).
2. Proceed to the Payment Information page (page 2) and provide the required details.
3. On the Preferences page (page 3), let us know about your interests and requirements.
4. Read and agree to the terms of consent on the Consent page (page 4).
5. Finally, review and submit your registration information on the Submission page (page 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Information (Page 1)
* **Attendee Name** (`<name>`, Text, Optional): This field is for your full name.
* **Email** (`<email>`, Text, Required): Provide your email address to receive updates and notifications about the bootcamp.
* **Phone Number** (`<phone_number>`, Text, Optional): Share your phone number to ensure we can contact you regarding any last-minute updates or changes.

### Payment Information (Page 2)
* **Payment Method** (`<payment_method>`, Text, Required): Choose your preferred payment method (e.g., credit card, bank transfer).
* **Card Number** (`<card_number>`, Text, Required): Enter your credit card details for secure payment processing.
* **Expiration Date** (`<expiration_date>`, Text, Required): Provide the expiration date of your payment method.
* **CVV** (`<cvv>`, Text, Required): Enter your Card Verification Value (CVV) for security purposes.

### Preferences (Page 3)
* **Interests** (`<interests>`, Text, Optional): Share your interests in data security topics, such as compliance, encryption, or data analysis.
* **Requirements** (`<requirements>`, Text, Optional): If you have any special requirements or accommodations, please specify them here.

### Consent (Page 4)
* **Consent Agreement** (`<consent_agreement>`, Text, Required): Please review and agree to our terms of consent, which include data handling and usage policies.

### Submission (Page 5)
* **Review and Submit** (`<review_and_submit>`, Text, Required): Double-check your details and submit your registration to complete the form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have a stable internet connection before submitting the form.
