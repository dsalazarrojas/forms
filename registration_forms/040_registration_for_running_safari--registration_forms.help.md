# Registration For Running Safari - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to register for the running safari event, providing essential information and payment details to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Event Information section with the required details about the event you wish to register for.
2. Provide your personal information, including your name, age, contact email, and any dietary restrictions.
3. Choose the desired payment method and confirm your payment.
4. Select any special requirements or restrictions you need for the event.
5. Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
6. Confirm your registration and payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Information (id: 1, text, optional)**: Enter the name of the event you wish to register for.
* **Name (id: 2, text, optional)**: Enter your name for the event registration.
* **Age (id: 3, number, optional)**: Enter your age to ensure age-related requirements are met.
* **Contact Email (id: 4, email, optional)**: Enter your contact email address for correspondence about the event.
* **Payment Method (id: 5, select_one, optional)**: Choose how you would like to pay for the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions (id: 6, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any dietary restrictions you need to have accommodated during the event.
* **Special Requirements (id: 7, select_multiple, optional)**: Choose any special requirements you need for the event.
* **Notes (id: 8, text, optional)**: Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Confirmation (id: 9, time, optional)**: Confirm your registration and payment details.
* **Registration Date (id: 10, date, optional)**: Enter the date you plan to register for the event.
* **Confirm Payment (id: 11, select_one, optional)**: Confirm your payment details.
* **Special Requirements 2 (id: 12, select_multiple, optional)**: Select any additional special requirements.
* **Confirm Registration (id: 13, select_one, optional)**: Confirm your event registration.
* **Payment Status (id: 14, select_one, optional)**: Choose the payment status for your registration.
* **Payment Date (id: 15, date, optional)**: Enter the date of your payment.
* **Payment Time (id: 16, time, optional)**: Enter the time of your payment.
* **Payment Details (id: 17, text, optional)**: Enter any additional payment details.
* **Payment Amount (id: 18, number, optional)**: Enter the amount you paid for the event.
* **Payment Method 2 (id: 19, select_one, optional)**: Choose an alternative payment method.
* **Payment Status 2 (id: 20, select_one, optional)**: Choose the payment status.
* **Confirm Payment 2 (id: 21, select_one, optional)**: Confirm your payment details.
* **Payment Method 3 (id: 22, select_one, optional)**: Choose a payment method.
* **Payment Status 3 (id: 23, select_one, optional)**: Choose the payment status.
* **Payment Method 4 (id: 24, select_one, optional)**: Choose a payment method.
* **Payment Status 4 (id: 25, select_one, optional)**: Choose the payment status.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required.
