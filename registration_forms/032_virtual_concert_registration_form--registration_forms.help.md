The "Virtual Concert Registration Form" is designed to collect information from users who want to attend or participate in a virtual concert. The form is intended for organizers and administrators to gather relevant details about attendees, including their names, contact information, ticket purchase preferences, and payment methods. This form is not meant for general public use and is primarily used for internal registration purposes.

# virtual_concert_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Concert Registration Form is a tool for concert organizers to collect information from attendees. It is used to gather details such as names, contact information, ticket preferences, and payment methods for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete the form, follow these steps:
	* Fill in the First name field with your first name.
	* Fill in the Last name field with your last name.
	* Enter your Email address in the Email field.
	* Enter your Phone number in the Phone number field.
	* Select the Date of the event from the Date field.
	* Select the Time of the event from the Time field.
	* Enter the Event date from the Event date field.
	* Select the Event time from the Event time field.
	* Enter the Venue name of the event from the Venue name field.
	* Enter the name of the Concert from the Concert name field.
	* Enter a Description of the event from the Description field.
	* Enter the Ticket price from the Ticket price field.
	* Enter the Ticket quantity from the Ticket quantity field.
	* Select the Payment method for the event from the Payment method field.
	* Select the Registration status from the Registration status field.
	* Add any relevant Note for the event from the Note field.
	* Confirm your Email from the Email confirmation field.
	* Confirm your Phone from the Phone confirmation field.
	* Select Ticket details from the Ticket details field.
	* Select the Ticket price currency from the Ticket price currency field.
	* Enter the amount of Ticket price from the Ticket price amount field.
	* Enter the Event location from the Event location field.
	* Enter the Event address from the Event address field.
	* Enter the Artist image from the Artist image field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone number** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (5, date, required/optional): Select the date of the event.
* **Time** (6, time, required/optional): Select the time of the event.
* **Event date** (7, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event time** (8, time, required/optional): Select the time of the event.
* **Venue name** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the venue name of the event.
* **Concert name** (10, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the concert.
* **Description** (12, text, required/optional): Provide a description of the event.
* **Ticket price** (13, number, required/optional): Enter the price of the ticket.
* **Ticket quantity** (14, number, required/optional): Enter the quantity of the ticket.
* **Payment method** (15, select_one, required/optional): Select the payment method for the event.
* **Registration status** (16, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the registration status of the event.
* **Note** (17, text, required/optional): Add any relevant notes about the event.
* **Email confirmation** (18, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone confirmation** (19, select_one, required/optional): Confirm your phone number.
* **Ticket details** (20, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the details of the ticket.
* **Ticket price currency** (21, select_one, required/optional): Select the currency of the ticket price.
* **Ticket price amount** (22, number, required/optional): Enter the price amount of the ticket.
* **Event location** (23, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event address** (24, text, required/optional): Enter the address of the event.
* **Artist image** (25, text, required/optional): Enter the artist's image.

Note: Please ensure that you fill out all required fields to successfully submit the form.
