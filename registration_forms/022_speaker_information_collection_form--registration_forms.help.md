# Speaker Information Collection Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect essential information about speakers participating in an event or conference.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by filling in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field, using the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Write a brief bio about yourself in the "Bio" field.
7. Select your speaker type from the "Speaker Type" dropdown menu (Faculty, Student, or Event).
8. (Optional) If applicable, select any relevant speaker titles from the "Speaker Title" dropdown menu.
9. If you have a bio image, select the "Yes" option in the "Bio Image" dropdown menu.
10. If you have a bio video, select the "Yes" option in the "Bio Video" dropdown menu.
11. (Optional) Enter the name of your university in the "University" field.
12. (Optional) Enter the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
13. Lastly, enter your speaker photo in the "Speaker Photo" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Provide your date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Bio** (`bio`, text, required): Write a brief bio about yourself.
* **Speaker Type** (`speaker_type`, select_one, required): Select your affiliation type (Faculty, Student, or Event).
* **Speaker Title** (`speaker_title`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant speaker titles (Professor, Instructor, CEO, or Founder).
* **University** (`university`, text, optional): Enter the name of your university.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Bio Image** (`bio_image`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have a bio image.
* **Bio Video** (`bio_video`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you have a bio video.
* **Speaker Photo** (`speaker_photo`, text, optional): Enter your speaker photo.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Use standard date format for the "Date Of Birth" field.
* If you have a bio image or video, please make sure it is relevant and professional.
* If you have a speaker photo, please ensure it is a recent and clear picture.
