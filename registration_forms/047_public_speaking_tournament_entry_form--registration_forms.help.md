# Public Speaking Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register entries for public speaking events. Fill out this form to provide information about the event and yourself as a speaker. The form includes fields for speaker details, event details, and submitter information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form in a step-by-step manner, ensuring you provide accurate information.
2. Review each field carefully and make sure you have filled out all required fields.
3. If you need assistance, contact the event organizers for help.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if provided).
* **Speaker Category** (`speaker_category`, select_multiple, optional): Choose from the provided options to describe your speaker category (e.g., speaker type).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, number, optional): Enter the length of the event in minutes.
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself as a speaker.
* **Video URL** (`video_url`, text, optional): If you have a video of your past speaking engagement, provide the URL.
* **Speaker Experience** (`speaker_experience`, text, optional): Describe your past speaking experience.
* **Presentation Topic** (`presentation_topic`, text, optional): Enter the topic of your presentation.
* **Language** (`language`, select_one, required): Select the language you would like to speak at the event.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`, text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting this entry.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submitter Organization** (`submitter_organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the submitter's organization.
* **Submitter Title** (`submitter_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of the submitter.
* **Submitter Relationship** (`submitter_relationship`, text, optional): Enter a relationship or position of the submitter.
* **Submitter Notes** (`submitter_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or details about the submitter.
* **Submitter Comments** (`submitter_comments`, text, optional): Enter any comments or remarks from the submitter.
* **Submitter Signature** (`submitter_signature`, text, optional): Enter the submitter's digital signature or name.
* **Submitter Agreement** (`submitter_agreement`, select_one, required): Select an option agreeing to the terms.
* **Submitter Agreement Date** (`submitter_agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you agree to the submitter agreement.

Remember to provide accurate and up-to-date information in this form for your entry to be considered valid. Ensure you understand what each field means and fill it in accordingly. If you are unsure, refer back to this guide for clarification. If you need assistance or have concerns, reach out to the event organizers for support.
