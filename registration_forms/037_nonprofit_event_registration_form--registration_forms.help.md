# Nonprofit Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration and payment information from attendees for non-profit events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Registration Details** (Page 1) with your name, email address, and other required information.
2. Provide your **Payment Information** (Page 2) to secure your spot at the event.
3. Enter your **Attendee Information** (Page 3) to help us understand who will be attending.
4. If you have any **Contact Information** (Page 4) or would like to leave a **Registration Message** (Page 5), you can do so.
5. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (Page 6) for the event.
6. Fill in the **Event Details** (Page 7) about the event you are attending.
7. Provide **Nonprofit Information** (Page 8) about the event organizer.
8. Choose the type of **Event Type** (Page 9) that best describes the event you are attending.
9. If you have any **Additional Info** (Page 10), you can enter it here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Details** (Page 1): A required field to collect your name and email for event registration.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
- **Payment Info** (Page 2): A required field to collect payment information for the event.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
- **Attendee Information** (Page 3): A required field to collect information about the attendees.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
- **Contact Info** (Page 4): An optional field to collect contact information if desired.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Registration Message** (Page 5): An optional field to leave a message for the event organizers.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false
- **Payment Method** (Page 6): A select-one field to choose the payment method for the event.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
  * Options: Card, Bank Transfer, Cash
- **Event Details** (Page 7): A required field to collect information about the event.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
- **Nonprofit Information** (Page 8): A required field to collect information about the non-profit.
  * Type: text
  * Required: true
- **Event Type** (Page 9): A select-one field to choose the type of event.
  * Type: select_one
  * Required: false
  * Options: Conference, Fundraising Event, Training Session, Webinar, Workshop
- **Additional Info** (Page 10): An optional field to collect any additional information.
  * Type: text
  * Required: false

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate registration.
- If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to us.
- Ensure your payment information is accurate to avoid any payment discrepancies.
- Choose the correct event type to help us better understand the event.
