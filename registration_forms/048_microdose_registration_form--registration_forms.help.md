# Microdose Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from participants in a microdose program. It is intended to ensure that participants are aware of their involvement and have provided necessary information to proceed with the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the microdose program.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing all required information.
3. Double-check your answers to ensure they are correct and complete.
4. Submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any relevant information about yourself as a participant, such as your name, email address, or other contact details.
* **Health Info** (`health_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any relevant health information, such as medical conditions or allergies.
* **Intentions** (`intentions`, text, optional): This field is where you can describe your intentions for participating in the microdose program, such as your goals or expectations.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, optional): This field is where you can indicate your consent to participate in the microdose program.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional notes or comments about your participation in the microdose program.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrators at form@gic.mx.
