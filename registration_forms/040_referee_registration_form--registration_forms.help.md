# Referee Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Registration Form is designed to collect and verify the personal and professional information of referees for official purposes. It helps us ensure that our referees have the necessary qualifications and experience to perform their duties effectively and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Referee Registration Form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Referee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5. Enter your city in the "City" field.
6. Enter your state in the "State" field.
7. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
8. If applicable, select the number of years of experience you have in your profession in the "Experience" field.
9. Enter your referee's license number (if applicable) in the "License" field.
10. Enter the name of your organization (if applicable) in the "Organisation" field.
11. Enter any relevant qualifications you hold in the "Qualifications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number where we can reach you.
* **Address**: Enter your complete address for official purposes.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside or work.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside or work.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for official purposes.
* **Experience**: If you have professional experience, please select the number of years you have been working in this field.
* **License**: If you hold a referee's license, please enter the number or name of the license.
* **Organisation**: If you are registered with an organization, please enter their name.
* **Qualifications**: Enter any relevant qualifications you hold, such as certifications or training.
* **Phone**: This field is intended for you to enter any additional phone numbers where you can be reached. 
*   Note: Please be sure to enter the correct format for your phone number. 
*   **Referee_Experience**: This field is intended for you to enter the number of years of experience you have in your profession.
