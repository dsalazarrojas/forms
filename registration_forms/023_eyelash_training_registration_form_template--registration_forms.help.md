# Eyelash Training Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eyelash Training Registration Form Template is designed to collect information from students and trainers for eyelash training purposes. This form serves as a registration tool to capture essential details about students and trainers for eyelash training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with \* (asterisk) with accurate information.
2. Enter your student name in the student name field.
3. Provide a valid email address for yourself as a student.
4. Enter your student contact information, including phone number and address.
5. Enter your trainer's contact information, including email and address.
6. Select the training type (eyelash, extension, or both).
7. Enter any additional notes about the training session.
8. Enter your student's date of birth (DOB) and trainer's DOB.
9. Enter your trainer's license information.
10. Review and submit the form for accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, \*): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, \*): Enter a valid email address for yourself as a student.
* **Trainer Info** (`trainer_info`, text, \*): Enter your trainer's information.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, \*): Enter the course name.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, \*): Enter the start date of the training session.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, \*): Enter the end date of the training session.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text): Enter your student phone number.
* **Trainer Email** (`trainer_email`, email): Enter your trainer's email address.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_multiple): Select the type of training (eyelash, extension, or both).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes about the training session.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, \*): Enter your student address.
* **Trainer Address** (`trainer_address`, text, \*): Enter your trainer's address.
* **Student State** (`student_state`, text, \*): Enter your student state.
* **Trainer State** (`trainer_state`, text, \*): Enter your trainer's state.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, text): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date): Enter the training date.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, select_one): Select the status of the training (pending, active, or completed).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one): Select the payment status (paid, pending).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number): Enter the payment amount (optional).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date): Enter the payment date (optional).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note): Enter any additional notes about the payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text): Enter the payment method (optional).
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, \*): Enter your student date of birth.
* **Trainer DOB** (`trainer_dob`, date, \*): Enter your trainer's date of birth.
* **Trainer License** (`trainer_license`, text, \*): Enter your trainer's license information.

Please review each field carefully and enter accurate information to ensure a smooth and successful training registration process.
