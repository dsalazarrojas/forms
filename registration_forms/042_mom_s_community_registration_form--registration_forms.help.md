# Mom's Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic contact information and family status details from individuals registering with the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter your child's information, including their names, age, and number of children.
3. Select your family status from the available options.
4. Enter your occupation and level of education.
5. Review and submit the form when completed.
