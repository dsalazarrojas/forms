# Mom's Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect basic contact information and family status details from individuals registering with the community.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your personal information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Enter your child's information, including their names, age, and number of children.
3. Select your family status from the available options.
4. Enter your occupation and level of education.
5. Review and submit the form when completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
**First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false):
 Enter your first name.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false):
 Enter your last name.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false):
Enter your date of birth.

### Email
**Email** (`email`, email, required: false):
Enter your email address.

### Phone
**Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false):
Enter your phone number.

### Address
**Address** (`address`, text, required: false):
Enter your address.

### Child Names
**Child Names** (`child_names`, text, required: false):
Enter your child's names.

### Number of Children
**Number of Children** (`number_of_children`, number, required: false):
Enter the number of children you have.

### Child Age
**Child Age** (`child_age`, number, required: false):
Enter your child's age.

### Family Status
**Family Status** (`family_status`, select_one, required: false):
Select your family status from the available options: Married, Single, Divorced, or Widowed.

### Occupation
**Occupation** (`occupation`, text, required: false):
Enter your occupation.

### Education
**Education** (`education`, select_multiple, required: false):
Select your level of education from the available options: College, High School, University, Master, or PhD.

### Children's Ages
**Children's Ages** (`children_ages`, time, required: false):
Enter your children's ages. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields correctly.
* Double-check the form for any errors before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer to the field-by-field explanation above.
