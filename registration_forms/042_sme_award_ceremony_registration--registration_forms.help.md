# SME Award Ceremony Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SME Award Ceremony Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from Small Medium Enterprises (SMEs) for the award ceremony. This form should be completed by the SME or their representative to register their company for the ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for the registered user.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your company phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, optional): If your company has a website, enter the URL.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_multiple, required): Select the size of your company from the options: Small, Medium, Large, or Other.
* **SME Type** (`sme_type`, text, required): Enter the type of SME you represent (e.g., Technology, Finance, etc.).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of registration in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If your company has a specific time constraint, enter the time you would like to attend.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes about your company.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
- This form will only be used for the award ceremony registration and will not be shared with any other parties.
