# Padel And Pilates Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users who want to book and pay for a Padel and Pilates session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your session details, including any relevant information about your session preferences.
2. Enter your attendee information, including your email address and phone number (if desired).
3. Select your preferred payment method (credit card or PayPal).
4. Choose your session scheduling (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
5. Enter your session time (if applicable).
6. Review and complete the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your session, such as any specific exercises or equipment you would like to focus on.
* **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter any additional information about yourself as the attendee, such as your name, address, or preferences.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about your payment, such as the payment amount or payment method.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, true): Select your preferred payment method (credit card or PayPal).
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): This field is where you can enter the total cost of your session, if applicable.
* **Session Scheduling** (`session_scheduling`, select_multiple, true): Choose your preferred session scheduling (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): This field is where you can enter the specific time of your session (if applicable).
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, true): Enter a valid email address for yourself as the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter your phone number (if desired).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is where you can add any additional comments or notes about your session. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the required fields (Payment Method, Session Scheduling, and Attendee Email).
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling out the form, please contact us for assistance.
