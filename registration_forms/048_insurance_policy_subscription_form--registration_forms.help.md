# Insurance Policy Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Insurance Policy Subscription Form is a document used to record and manage insurance policies for subscribers. It contains various fields to gather information about the policyholder, such as contact details, policy specifics, and administrative information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the First Name field.
2. Enter your **email address** in the Email field.
3. Provide your **phone number** in the Phone field.
4. Enter your **home address** in the Address field.
5. Enter your **date of birth** in the Date of Birth field.
6. Select the type(s) of policy you are interested in, such as Life Insurance, Health Insurance, or Disability Insurance, from the Policy Type field.
7. Select the start date of your policy from the Policy Start Date field.
8. Select the end date of your policy from the Policy End Date field.
9. Specify the amount of your policy in the Policy Amount field.
10. Choose how often you want to pay your policy premiums, such as Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Annually, from the Policy Frequency field.
11. Provide any additional notes about your policy in the Policy Notes field.
12. Enter any notes from your agent or representative in the Agent Notes field.
13. Enter the date and time you submitted the form in the Submission Date and Submission Time fields.
14. Select the status of your policy, such as Active, Inactive, Pending, or Canceled, from the Policy Status field.
15. If your policy type is not listed, enter it in the Other Policy Type field.
16. Select the status of your policy from the Policy Status 2 field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Address** (text): Enter your home or residential address.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Policy Type** (select multiple): Select one or more types of insurance policy you are interested in.
* **Policy Start Date** (date): Select the start date of your policy.
* **Policy End Date** (date): Select the end date of your policy.
* **Policy Amount** (number): Enter the amount of your policy.
* **Policy Frequency** (select one): Choose how often you want to pay your policy premiums.
* **Policy Notes** (note): Enter any additional notes about your policy.
* **Agent Notes** (note): Enter any notes from your agent or representative.
* **Submission Date** (date): Enter the date you submitted the form.
* **Submission Time** (time): Enter the time you submitted the form.
* **Policy Status** (select one): Select the status of your policy.
* **Other Policy Type** (text): If your policy type is not listed, enter it here.
* **Policy Status 2** (text): Select the status of your policy.

Note: The required fields are marked as "false" in the form, but it is recommended that all fields be completed for accurate and efficient processing.
