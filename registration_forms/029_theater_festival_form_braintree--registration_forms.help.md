# theater_festival_form_braintree - Help Guide
## Purpose
The theater festival form is designed to collect registration information from attendees, volunteers, and organizers of the event. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure smooth event management.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Please note that the form fields are optional, but filling out all required fields is necessary for efficient event organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, page, required/optional: optional):
This field is the main form section. You will see other form fields listed below.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional):
Enter your name as it appears on your identification or documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional):
Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional):
Select your role in the event:
	+ Participant: Attendee or participant in the festival.
	+ Volunteer: Helper or volunteer for the event.
	+ Organizer: Person responsible for organizing the festival.
* **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, date, optional):
Enter your expected date of arrival for the event.
* **Duration of Stay** (`duration_of_stay`, number, optional):
Enter the number of days you plan to stay for the event.
* **Accommodation** (`accommodation`, text, optional):
Describe your accommodation arrangements (e.g., hotel name, address).

## Tips
- Please ensure the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the festival organizers for assistance.
- Review your filled form to ensure it's complete and correct before submission.
