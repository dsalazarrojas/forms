# pet_loss_support_group_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pet loss support group registration form is designed to collect registration information from participants who have experienced the loss of a pet and are seeking support. This form will help the support organization to understand the participant's needs and schedule sessions accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Name** and **Contact Number**, as these are required fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address, which will be used for communication and support.
3. Provide the **Pet Name** that was lost, which will help the support organization understand your situation.
4. Enter the **Deceased Date** of your pet, which will help the support organization schedule sessions at the right time.
5. Choose a **Group Session** that suits your needs, as this will determine the type of support you will receive.
6. Select a **Session Type** (In Person or Virtual) and **Session Duration** (30 minutes or 60 minutes) that works best for you.
7. Enter your **Counselor Name** and **Support Organization** name, which will help the support organization understand your preferences.
8. Enter the **Session Time** and **Schedule Date** for the session, which will help the support organization schedule sessions accordingly.
9. Add any **Notes** and **Session Notes** that you would like to share with the support organization, which will help them understand your needs better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): This is your name, which will be used to identify you as a participant.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, required): Enter your phone number so the support organization can contact you for sessions and support.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is your email address, which will be used for communication and support.
- **Pet Name** (`pet_name`, text, required): This is the name of the pet that was lost.
- **Deceased Date** (`pet_deceased_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your pet's passing, which will help the support organization schedule sessions at the right time.
- **Group Sessions** (`group_sessions`, select_one, required): Choose a group session that suits your needs.
- **Schedule Group Session** (`schedule_group_session`, date, required): Enter the date for the group session.
- **Counselor Name** (`counselor_name`, text, required): This is the name of the counselor who will be leading the session.
- **Support Organization** (`support_organization`, text, required): This is the name of the support organization that will be providing you with support.
- **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required): Enter the time for the session, which will help the support organization schedule sessions accordingly.
- **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, required): Enter the date for the session, which will help the support organization schedule sessions accordingly.
- **Session Type** (`session_type`, select_one, required): Choose between In Person or Virtual sessions.
- **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, select_multiple, required): Choose between 30 minutes or 60 minutes sessions.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes you would like to share with the support organization.
- **Session Notes** (`session_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes for the session, which will help the support organization understand your needs better.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all the required fields to ensure that the support organization can provide you with the necessary support.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the support organization.
- The support organization will review your registration and schedule sessions according to your needs.
