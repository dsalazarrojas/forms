# Parent Or Guardian Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about the parent or guardian of a student. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the parent or guardian's relationship with the student, their contact information, and their emergency contact details. This information is used to ensure the student's well-being and provide support in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Select the correct option for whether the person filling out the form is the parent or guardian of the student.
2. Fill in the name of the parent or guardian in the corresponding field.
3. Provide the address and date of birth of the parent or guardian.
4. Enter their contact number, email, and emergency contact number.
5. Specify their emergency contact's name, relationship, and contact information.
6. If applicable, list any medical conditions and allergies.
7. Provide a message for the emergency contact.
8. Sign and date the declaration above.
9. Finally, thank the parent or guardian for their cooperation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Is the parent or guardian of the student** (`parent_or_guardian_declaration_form_1`, select_one, required): 
  Select 'Yes' if you are the parent or guardian of the student, and 'No' otherwise. This is the first step to determine whether you should complete this form.
* **Name of parent or guardian** (`name_of_parent_or_guardian_1`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Name of parent or guardian** (`name_of_parent_or_guardian_2`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian (this field is a duplicate of the previous one, but with a slightly different label).
* **Address of parent or guardian** (`address_of_parent_or_guardian`, text, required): Provide the address of the parent or guardian.
* **Date of birth of parent or guardian** (`date_of_birth_of_parent_or_guardian`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact number of parent or guardian** (`contact_number_of_parent_or_guardian`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Email of parent or guardian** (`email_of_parent_or_guardian`, email, required): Enter the email of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency contact number of parent or guardian** (`emergency_contact_number_of_parent_or_guardian`, text, required): Enter the emergency contact number of the parent or guardian.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Specify the relationship of the emergency contact (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Emergency contact number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the emergency contact.
* **Medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, required): List any medical conditions of the student.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, required): List any allergies the student has.
* **Emergency contact message** (`emergency_contact_message`, text, required): Provide a message for the emergency contact.
* **Signature of parent or guardian** (`signature_of_parent_or_guardian`, note, required): Sign and date the declaration above with your signature.
* **Date of signature** (`date_of_signature`, date, required): Date the declaration above.
* **Time of signature** (`time_of_signature`, time, required): Time of signature.
* **IP address** (`ip_address`, text, required): This field is not used for the form's purpose and can be ignored.
* **Parent or guardian declaration** (`parent_or_guardian_declaration_form_5`, text, required): Enter a declaration confirming that the information above is accurate.
* **Parent or guardian declaration 2** (`parent_or_guardian_declaration_form_6`, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the previous one and can be ignored.
* **I have read and declare the information above to be accurate** (`parent_or_guardian_declaration_form_7`, text, required): This field is a confirmation of the declaration above.
* **Please sign and date the declaration above** (`parent_or_guardian_declaration_form_8`, text, required): Sign and date the declaration above.
* **Thank you for your cooperation** (`parent_or_guardian_declaration_form_9`, text, required): This is a thank you message and can be ignored.
