# Non Conformance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Non Conformance Form is used to report non-conforming or non-compliant behavior or issues that have occurred within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Customer Name** in the provided text field. This is the person or entity involved in the non-conformity.
2. Select the **Department** that is relevant to the non-conformity. Choose 'Yes' if the department is involved, 'No' otherwise.
3. Describe the **Issue** in the text field. Be as specific as possible about what happened.
4. Enter a detailed **Description** of the non-conformity.
5. Click the **Submit** button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (text): Enter the name of the person or entity involved in the non-conformity.
* **Department** (select_one): Check 'Yes' if the department that is relevant to the non-conformity is involved, 'No' otherwise.
* **Issue** (text): Describe the specific issue or incident that occurred.
* **Description** (text): Enter a detailed explanation of the non-conformity.
* **Submit** (text): Click this button to complete the form and submit your report.

## Tips

* Only fill out this form for genuine non-conforming or non-compliant incidents.
* Make sure to check with your supervisor or department head if you're unsure about reporting an issue.
* Ensure that this form is not filled out for issues already reported or being investigated.
* Be as specific as possible when describing the issue to provide accurate information for investigation and resolution.
