# Youth Alive Fellowship Manchester - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Alive Fellowship Manchester is a registration form designed to collect essential information from new members. This guide will help you complete the form correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the registration form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**:
	+ **Registration Form**: A text input field for providing your registration details.
	+ **Name** (required): A text input field for your name, which will be used for identity verification.
	+ **Email** (required): A text input field for your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
	+ **Confirm E-mail** (optional): A text input field to confirm your email address, ensuring accuracy in our records.
	+ **Date of Birth** (required): A date input field for your date of birth, which will be used for age verification and identification purposes.
	+ **Phone** (optional): A text input field for your phone number, which can be used for communication or other purposes.
	+ **Address** (optional): A text input field for your address, which can be used to provide you with information relevant to your location.
	+ **Membership Type** (optional): A select input field with options for choosing your membership type, which will determine your access to certain benefits or activities.
	+ **Submit** (required): A select input field to confirm that you have filled out the form correctly and are ready to submit.
	+ **Cancel**: A select input field to cancel the registration process and return to the previous page.

## Tips
- Double-check your email address for accuracy to avoid any errors.
- Ensure that your date of birth is correct to ensure accurate age verification.
- Choose your membership type carefully, as this will determine your access to certain benefits or activities.
