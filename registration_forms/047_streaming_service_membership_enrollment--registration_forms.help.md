# Streaming Service Membership Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to sign up for a streaming service membership.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. Please use the format "username@example.com".
4. Select your desired Subscription Plan from the "Subscription Plan" dropdown menu. Options include:
	* Monthly
	* Yearly
	* One-time
5. Enter your Address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your City in the "City" field.
7. Select your State from the "State" dropdown menu. Options include:
	* CA
	* NY
	* TX
8. Enter your Zip code in the "Zip" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your personal name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format "username@example.com".
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, required: false): Select the type of subscription you prefer:
	+ Monthly
	+ Yearly
	+ One-time
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required: false): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, required: false): Select your state of residence:
	+ CA
	+ NY
	+ TX
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, required: false): Enter your ZIP code.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your email address and subscription plan selection to ensure correct input.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our customer support team.
