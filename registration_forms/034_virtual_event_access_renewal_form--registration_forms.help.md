# Virtual Event Access Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees who wish to renew their access to virtual events. It is used to collect updated information from attendees to ensure they can continue to participate in upcoming virtual events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information (if any) for each field. You will need to provide the following information:
	* Attendee name
	* Attendee email
	* Renewal date (note: this is not strictly necessary but helps us keep track of your access)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **attendee name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **attendee email**: Enter your email address that we have on file or the one you used to originally register for the virtual event.
* **attendee phone**: Enter your phone number (not required but recommended in case we need to contact you).
* **renewal date**: Enter the date you would like to renew your access for. This will help us track your access expiration and ensure you receive timely notifications. (Optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out each field accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, you can contact our support team for assistance.
* Once you submit the form, your access will be renewed and you will receive confirmation via email.

Note: I've assumed that the "renewal date" field is optional but still useful for tracking purposes. If this is not the case, please let me know and I'll adjust the help guide accordingly.
