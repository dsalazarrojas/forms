# Financial Sustainability Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Sustainability Initiative Registration form is used to register and document financial sustainability initiatives. This form is required for initiatives that aim to promote responsible financial practices, reduce environmental impact, and foster social responsibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you have all the necessary information required to fill it out.
2. Select the appropriate initiative type from the list of options.
3. Enter a brief description of your initiative, including any relevant details.
4. Select a date for your initiative (if applicable).
5. Review your form to ensure accuracy and completeness before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Initiative Type** (`initiative_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of financial sustainability initiative you are registering. This field helps us understand the scope and nature of your initiative.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of your initiative, including any relevant details that will help us understand its goals, objectives, and outcomes.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select the date your initiative began or is scheduled to begin. This helps us track the timeline of your initiative.
* **Note** (`first_page`, note, required): This field is a placeholder for any additional notes or comments about your initiative. You can enter any relevant information that will help us understand your initiative better.
* **Financial Sustainability** (`financial_sustainability`, number, required): Not applicable as this field is not actually part of the form.

This form is for registering financial sustainability initiatives. Please make sure to review the form carefully and avoid duplicating existing entries. If you are unsure about any of the fields or have any questions, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
