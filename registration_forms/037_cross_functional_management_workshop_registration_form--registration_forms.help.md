# Cross Functional Management Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Cross-Functional Management Workshop. It captures essential details such as participant name, contact information, availability, and submission status to ensure efficient workshop organization and communication with participants.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure that you are registering with your real name and correct email address.
2. Select your availability for the workshop from the options provided.
3. Choose your participant role from the given options.
4. Confirm that you have started or completed the submission process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form Data** (`registration_form_data`, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional information you would like to provide about your registration.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your real name as it will be used for workshop communication.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that will be used for workshop communication and updates.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any urgent communication.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop (not editable in this form, will be pre-filled).
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the workshop (not editable in this form, will be pre-filled).
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, select_one, optional): Select your role in the workshop (Team Member, Team Lead, Participant, Speaker, Other).
* **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, select_multiple, required): Select all the available time slots for the workshop (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, All).
* **Submission Status** (`submission_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your submission status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Cancelled).
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, optional): This field is not editable in this form and will be pre-filled with the current date.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, time, optional): This field is not editable in this form and will be pre-filled with the current time.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are registering with your real name and correct email address.
* Double-check your participant availability and submission status for accurate information.
* If you encounter any issues or have concerns, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
