# Health Screening Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to obtain informed consent from patients before conducting health screenings or exams. It helps to ensure that patients understand the procedures, risks, and benefits of the screening or exam, and provides an opportunity for them to ask questions or express concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose and requirements.
2. Ensure that you fill out all required fields.
3. If any field is unclear, refer to this help guide or contact the healthcare provider for further assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, `select_one`, **Required**): Choose one of the provided options to indicate the type of consent being given.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, **Required**): Enter the date of the screening or exam.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, `time`, **Required**): Enter the time of day of the screening or exam.
* **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Select all that apply to indicate the type of consent given.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, **Required**): Provide any additional comments or concerns you have regarding the screening or exam.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **Required**): Enter the patient's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Health Screener** (`health_screener`, `text`, **Optional**): Provide any additional information about the health screener or contact person.

## Tips

* Be sure to review each field carefully before proceeding.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form should be completed in conjunction with the patient's informed consent process.
