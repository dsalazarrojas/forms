# Psa Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Psa Form is used for registering children in an educational setting. It captures essential details of each child for educational records and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information for each child you wish to register.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out for each child.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the first name of the child you are registering.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the last name of the child you are registering.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the date of birth of the child you are registering.
* **Age** (`age`, number, required): Enter the age of the child you are registering.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If the child has any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the child's parent or guardian.
* **Media Declaration** (`medical_declaration`, text, optional): If you are a parent, please declare any media or video recordings that the child may have been in contact with.
* **Terms And Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the form.
* **Parent Agreement** (`parent_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information entered is correct or indicate that it is not correct.
* **Add Another** (`add_another`, select_multiple, optional): Select to add more children or indicate that there are no more children to register.

**Children's Information**

* For each additional child, follow the same steps as above and fill out the **First Name**, **Last Name**, **Date Of Birth**, **Age**, **Contact Number**, and **Email** fields.
* Make sure to fill out a unique set of information for each child.
* Do not repeat information for each child.
* You can add up to 5 children in this form.

Please note that this form is designed to be filled out by parents or guardians of children. Please ensure that you are only registering children you have authority over.
