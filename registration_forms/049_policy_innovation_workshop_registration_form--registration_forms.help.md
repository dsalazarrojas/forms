# Policy Innovation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Policy Innovation Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from participants for the workshop. This form helps event organizers manage registrations efficiently and ensures that participants provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your event organizer (Jimmy, John, or Mary) from the dropdown menu.
2.  Enter your participant name in the text field.
3.  Enter a valid email address in the email field.
4.  If desired, enter your participant phone number in the text field.
5.  Select the date of the workshop from the date picker.
6.  Select the time of the workshop from the time picker.
7.  If desired, enter the workshop location in the text field.
8.  Optionally, add any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizers**: Select the event organizer who will be managing your registration. This is required for workshop planning and coordination. (Select: Jimmy, John, Mary)
*  **Participant Name**: Enter your participant name accurately to ensure efficient registration management. (Required)
*  **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive workshop updates and notifications. (Required)
*  **Participant Phone**: Enter your participant phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone. (Optional)
*  **Workshop Date**: Select the date of the workshop you will be attending. This field is required for planning purposes. (Required)
*  **Workshop Time**: Select the time of the workshop you will be attending. This field is required for scheduling purposes. (Required)
*  **Workshop Location**: Enter the workshop location if you wish to provide more details about the event venue. (Optional)
*  **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration. (Optional)

## Tips

*  Be accurate when entering your email and name to ensure smooth workshop communication.
*  If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer for assistance.
*  Please register only once for the workshop to avoid duplicates.
