# Workforce Efficiency Analysis Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from interested parties for the Workforce Efficiency Analysis Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Email in the **Email** field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select the **Workshop Date** you would like to attend.
6. Choose the **Workshop Time** that suits you best.
7. Choose your preferred **Registration Type** (In-Person, Online, or Hybrid).
8. Enter your Organization's name in the **Organization** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Date** (date, required: false): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (time, required: false): Choose the time slot that suits you best for the workshop.
* **Registration Type** (select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to attend the workshop (In-Person, Online, or Hybrid).
* **Organization** (text, required: false): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
