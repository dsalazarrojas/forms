# Massage Therapy Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Massage Therapy Course Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from users interested in enrolling in a massage therapy course. This form helps us manage course enrollment and ensure that all necessary details are captured accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are registering for the correct course.
2. Fill in your contact details, including your name and email address (if provided).
3. Provide a phone number to facilitate communication (if provided).
4. Choose the desired payment method (cash or card).
5. Indicate the amount you wish to pay (in dollars).
6. Select the massage course you would like to take (Basic Massage or Advanced Massage).
7. Provide the date and time of your preferred attendance.
8. Add any additional notes about your registration (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, optional): A field where you can enter any additional information about your registration, if needed.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name to help us identify you correctly.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your registration and any course updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here to ensure we can contact you easily.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose between paying with cash or card for your course registration.
* **Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, optional): Indicate the amount you wish to pay for the course.
* **Course** (`course`, select_one, optional): Select the massage course you wish to attend, either Basic Massage or Advanced Massage.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you prefer to attend the course.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time of your preferred course attendance.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or questions, please add them here so we can address them before the course starts.
