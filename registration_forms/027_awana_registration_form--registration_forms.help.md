# Awana Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Awana Registration Form, explaining each field and its purpose.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To start, click on the link to access the Awana Registration Form.
2.  Fill out each field as instructed and make sure to include accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Be sure to check off the required options for each field to ensure the form is complete and valid.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Parent/Guardian Full Name (ID 2)**: Enter the full name of the parent or guardian who is registering the child.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please enter the name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Parent Email Address (ID 3)**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
    *   This field is required.
    *   This will be used as the primary point of contact.
*   **Parent Phone Number (ID 4)**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
    *   This field is required.
    *   This will be used in case of emergency or if we need to contact the parent/guardian.
*   **Home Address (ID 5)**: Enter the home address of the parent or guardian.
    *   This field is required.
    *   This will be used to determine the child's location and contact information.
*   **Child's Full Name (ID 7)**: Enter the full name of the child who is registering.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please enter the name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Child's Date of Birth (ID 8)**: Enter the date of birth of the child.
    *   This field is required.
    *   This will help us determine the child's age and grade.
*   **Child's Grade (ID 9)**: Select the grade level of the child.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please choose the correct grade level from the dropdown list.
*   **Club Level (ID 10)**: Select the club level based on the child's age/grade.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please choose the correct level from the dropdown list.
*   **Photo Release (ID 15)**: Choose if permission is given to use the child's photo for materials.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please select the correct option to grant permission.
*   **Medical Release Authorization (ID 16)**: Choose if authorization is given for medical treatment in case of an emergency.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please select the correct option to grant authorization.
*   **Emergency Contact Name (ID 13)**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please enter the name of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone (ID 14)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please enter the contact's phone number.
*   **Medical Conditions/Allergies (ID 12)**: Enter any medical conditions or allergies the child may have.
    *   This field is optional.
    *   Please be specific with any medical conditions or allergies.
*   **Pickup Authorization List (ID 17)**: Enter any other people who can pick up the child.
    *   This field is optional.
    *   Please list any other people who can take the child from the club.
*   **Volunteer Interest (ID 18)**: Choose how you would like to volunteer in the club.
    *   This field is optional.
    *   Please select any options that interest you.
*   **Parent/Guardian Digital Signature (ID 19)**: Enter your digital signature to certify the information is accurate.
    *   This field is required.
    *   Please type your full name to confirm.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us.
*   This form is for one child per form, so please register each child separately.
