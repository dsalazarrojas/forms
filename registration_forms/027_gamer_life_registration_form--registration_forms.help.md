# Gamer Life Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for gamers to register their information and join a community or participate in events.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your First name.
2. Please provide your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Select your availability from the options provided.
5. Select your interests from the options provided.
6. Choose your time zone from the dropdown options.
7. Enter your Gamer ID.
8. If you have any additional comments, you can provide them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Availability**: Select all times when you are available to participate in events or activities. For example, you can select "Gaming" if you are typically available during gaming sessions.
* **Interests**: Select all the types of games or activities you are interested in, such as "Role Playing" or "Strategy". 
* **Time zone**: Choose your time zone from the dropdown options.
* **Gamer ID**: Enter a unique ID number assigned to you by the community or event organizers.
* **Additional comments**: Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share with the community.
