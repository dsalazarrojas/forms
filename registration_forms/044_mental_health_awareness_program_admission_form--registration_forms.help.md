# Mental Health Awareness Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Awareness Program Admission Form is used to collect information about participants in the program. This includes basic participant information, medical history, mental health history, contact preferences, emergency contact information, medical provider information, and participant signature details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Participant Information**: This section is used to collect basic information about the participant, such as their name, contact details, and medical history.
2. **Medical History**: This section is used to record any relevant medical history that may be relevant to the participant's treatment.
3. **Mental Health History**: This section is used to collect information about the participant's mental health history, if any.
4. **Contact Preferences**: This section is used to collect information about the participant's contact preferences, such as whether they have preferred contact methods.
5. **Emergency Contact**: This section is used to collect contact information for emergency contact, in case the participant requires assistance.
6. **Emergency Phone**: This section is used to collect the participant's emergency contact phone number.
7. **Alternate Emergency Phone**: This section is used to collect an alternate emergency contact phone number.
8. **Emergency Address**: This section is used to collect the emergency contact address.
9. **Consent**: This section is used to confirm the participant's consent for the program's services.
10. **Medical Provider**: This section is used to collect information about the participant's medical provider.
11. **Medical Insurance**: This section is used to collect information about the participant's medical insurance.
12. **Insurance Provider**: This section is used to collect information about the participant's insurance provider.
13. **Insurance Phone**: This section is used to collect the participant's insurance provider phone number.
14. **Participant Signature**: This section is used to collect the participant's signature, confirming their agreement to the program's terms and conditions.
15. **Participant Signature Date**: This section is used to collect the date of the participant's signature.
16. **Participant Signature Time**: This section is used to collect the time of the participant's signature.
17. **Participant Signature Location**: This section is used to collect the location of the participant's signature.
18. **Participant Signature Notes**: This section is used to collect any additional notes from the participant during their signature process.
19. **Participant Signature Image**: This section is used to upload an image of the participant's signature.
20. **Medical Provider Signature**: This section is used to collect the medical provider's signature, confirming their agreement to the program's terms and conditions.
21. **Medical Provider Phone**: This section is used to collect the medical provider's phone number.
22. **Medical Provider Address**: This section is used to collect the medical provider's address.
23. **Program Start Date**: This section is used to collect the start date of the program.
24. **Program Start Time**: This section is used to collect the start time of the program.
25. **Program End Date**: This section is used to collect the end date of the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required): Please provide your basic information, including your name, address, and contact details.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required): Please provide any relevant medical history that may be relevant to your treatment.
* **Mental Health History** (`mental_health_history`, `text`, required): Please provide any relevant mental health history.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Please select your preferred contact methods (Yes/No).
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Please provide the name and contact information of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): Please provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Alternate Emergency Phone** (`emergency_alternate_phone`, `text`, required): Please provide an alternate phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, `text`, required): Please provide the address of your emergency contact.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm your consent for the program's services (None/Other).
* **Medical Provider** (`medical_provider`, `text`, required): Please provide the name of your medical provider.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, `text`, required): Please provide your medical insurance information.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): Please provide your insurance provider information.
* **Insurance Phone** (`insurance_phone`, `text`, required): Please provide the phone number of your insurance provider.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm your signature (Yes/No).
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, `date`, required): Please provide the date of your signature.
* **Participant Signature Time** (`participant_signature_time`, `time`, required): Please provide the time of your signature.
* **Participant Signature Location** (`participant_signature_location`, `text`, required): Please provide the location of your signature.
* **Participant Signature Notes** (`participant_signature_notes`, `text`, required): Please provide any additional notes from your signature process.
* **Participant Signature Image** (`participant_signature_image`, `note`, required): Please upload an image of your signature.
* **Medical Provider Signature** (`medical_provider_signature`, `select_one`, required): Please confirm your medical provider's signature (Yes/No).
* **Medical Provider Phone** (`medical_provider_phone`, `text`, required): Please provide the phone number of your medical provider.
* **Medical Provider Address** (`medical_provider_address`, `text`, required): Please provide the address of your medical provider.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required): Please provide the start date of the program.
* **Program Start Time** (`program_start_time`, `time`, required): Please provide the start time of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, `date`, required): Please provide the end date of the program.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for assistance.
* Please review each section carefully before submitting the form.
