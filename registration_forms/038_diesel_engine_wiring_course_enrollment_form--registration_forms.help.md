# Diesel Engine Wiring Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to enroll in the diesel engine wiring course. It is a required form to collect necessary information for course registration and to ensure duplicate enrollments are avoided.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name (optional).
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Fill in your Date of Birth (optional).
6. Select the Course Date you wish to enroll in.
7. Select the type of course you want to take (Yes or No).
8. Select the location where you want to take the course (Yes or No).
9. Read and agree to the Enrollment Agreement.
10. Add any additional student or instructor notes as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): If different from your First Name, please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, optional): Enter your Date of Birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to start the course.
* **Course Type** (`course_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' to take the course or 'No' to decline.
* **Program Length** (`program_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of the program you are enrolling in.
* **Enrollment Agreement** (`enrollment_agreement`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms of the course.
* **Course Location** (`course_location`, select_multiple, required): Select all locations where you plan to take the course.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your student status.
* **Instructor Notes** (`instructor_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about your instructor interactions.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid duplicate enrollments.
- Be accurate when entering your Email and Phone number.
- Select the correct Course Date and Location for your intended course.
