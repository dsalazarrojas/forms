# Educational Leadership Dialogue Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Educational Leadership Dialogue Series Registration" form is designed for individuals to register for the program. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure a successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your valid Email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose the Session Date for which you wish to register.
6. Select the Session Time for the chosen Date.
7. Enter the Session Duration for the chosen Date and Time.
8. Enter your Institution name (if applicable).
9. Enter your Department (if applicable).
10. Enter your Role (if applicable).
11. Add any additional Notes regarding your registration.
12. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date for which you wish to register.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, required): Select the time for the chosen date.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the session you wish to attend.
* **Institution** (`institution`, `text`, optional): Enter your institution name (if applicable).
* **Department** (`department`, `text`, optional): Enter your department (if applicable).
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, optional): Enter your role (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes regarding your registration.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to choose the correct Session Date and Time.
- If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, you can add them in the Notes section.
- You can only register for one session per submission. If you wish to register for multiple sessions, please submit the form multiple times.
