# Adventure Sports Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Sports Access Form is a mandatory registration form for participants to access adventure sports facilities. This form is used to collect essential information from participants, including their name, activity selection, access level, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full legal name in the **Participant name** field.
2. Select the **Activity selected** field to choose the sport or activity you wish to access.
3. Choose your required **Access level** from the provided options (Participant, Coach, Spectator, or Vendor).
4. If applicable, provide your **ID verification** status (True or False).
5. Accept or decline the **Liability waiver** (True or False).
6. Enter your **Access start date** as the date when access is intended to begin.
7. If necessary, describe your **Special access needs** in the provided text area.
8. Enter your **Contact phone** number for access verification.
9. Finally, select the **Submission date**, which will automatically be filled in based on the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name** (required, text): Enter your full legal name.
* **Activity selected** (required, text): Choose the sport or activity you wish to access from the list provided.
* **Access level requested** (required, select_one): Select your required access level from the options (Participant, Coach, Spectator, or Vendor).
* **ID verification provided** (optional, select_one): Indicate whether you have provided ID verification (True or False).
* **Liability waiver accepted** (required, select_one): Accept or decline the liability waiver (True or False).
* **Access start date** (required, date): Enter the date when access is intended to begin.
* **Special access needs** (optional, text): Provide any necessary accommodations or special needs.
* **Contact phone** (required, text): Enter your contact phone number for access verification.
* **Submission date** (required, date): This field will be automatically filled in based on the current date.
