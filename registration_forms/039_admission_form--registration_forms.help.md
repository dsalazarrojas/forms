# Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about you, the applicant, to support your application for admission to our institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Enter your full name, email address, phone number, and other required contact information.
3. Provide your address, city, state/province, and country of residence.
4. Enter your date of birth.
5. Choose the program you are applying for and the degree level you are pursuing.
6. Select the term and year you wish to start.
7. Describe your educational background, including your previous institution(s) and GPA (if applicable).
8. Provide any relevant work experience and extracurricular activities.
9. Write a personal statement about your goals.
10. Provide contact information for your emergency contact.
11. Sign the form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
- **Address** (`applicant_address`, `text`, required): Enter your current residential address.
- **City** (`applicant_city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your residence.
- **State/Province** (`applicant_state`, `text`, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
- **ZIP/Postal Code** (`applicant_zip`, `text`, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
- **Country** (`applicant_country`, `text`, required): Enter the country of your residence.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your birth date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
- **Program Applying For** (`program_applying_for`, `text`, required): Choose the program you are applying for.
- **Degree Level** (`degree_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose the degree level you are pursuing (Associate, Bachelor's, Master's, Doctorate, Certificate, Diploma, Other).
- **Start Term** (`start_term`, `select_one`, required): Choose the term you wish to start (Fall, Spring, Summer, Winter, Other).
- **Start Year** (`start_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you wish to start.
- **Education Background** (`education_background`, `text`, required): Describe your educational background, including previous institutions.
- **Previous Institution** (`previous_institution`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your previous educational institution.
- **GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, optional): Enter your cumulative GPA, if applicable.
- **Standardized Test Scores** (`standardized_test_scores`, `text`, optional): Enter your scores for standardized tests (SAT, ACT, GRE, GMAT, etc.).
- **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe any relevant work experience.
- **Extracurricular Activities** (`extracurricular_activities`, `text`, optional): Describe any extracurricular activities.
- **Personal Statement** (`personal_statement`, `text`, optional): Write a personal statement about your goals.
- **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Provide contact information for your references.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Enter the relationship to your emergency contact.
- **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of submitting this application.
- **Applicant Signature** (`applicant_signature`, `text`, required): Sign the form electronically to confirm your application.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Use the provided hints for guidance on each field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- We are here to help you through the application process.
