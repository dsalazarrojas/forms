# new_client_and_pet_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The new_client_and_pet_information_form is designed to collect new client and pet information for business purposes. This form is likely used in a veterinary clinic or pet care facility to add new clients and their pets to the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "New Client" field to enter the client's name.
2. Enter the pet's name in the "Pet" field.
3. For the "Pet Type", select whether the pet is a "Yes" or "No" to indicate its type.
4. In the "Pet Breed" field, select multiple options to indicate the pet's breed.
5. In the "Owner" field, enter the owner's name.
6. In the "Address" field, enter the address of the client and pet (note: address is not clearly linked to pet).
7. Enter the city of the pet in the "City" field.
8. In the "State" field, enter the state where the pet lives.
9. Finally, in the "Zip Code" field, enter the zip code of the pet's location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Client** (`1`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the client's name.
* **Pet** (`2`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the pet's name.
* **Pet Type** (`3`, `select_one`, required: `false`): Select whether the pet is a "Yes" or "No".
* **Pet Breed** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select multiple options for the pet's breed.
* **Owner** (`5`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the owner's name.
* **Address** (`6`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the address of the client and pet.
* **City** (`7`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the city where the pet lives.
* **State** (`8`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the state where the pet lives.
* **Zip Code** (`9`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter the zip code of the pet's location.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about a field, you can always ask for help.
* This form is designed for new clients and pets, but if you are an existing client, you might not need to fill this form.
* Please note that address is not clearly linked to pet, so it might be assumed as client's address.
