# Resort Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resort Check In Form is a tool used by resort staff to gather essential information from arriving guests to facilitate a smooth and efficient check-in process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your guest name in the "Guest Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the "Date of Arrival" field and choose the date you plan to arrive at the resort.
5. Choose the "Time of Arrival" field and select the time you plan to arrive at the resort.
6. Enter your reservation ID, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, **required**): Enter the name of the guest checking in. This will be used to address them during the check-in process and to communicate with them about resort activities and updates.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This will help us to contact you with important resort updates and information, if needed.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will allow us to contact you in case of emergencies or if you need to be reached.
* **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to arrive at the resort. This will help us to schedule your room and prepare for your arrival.
* **Time of Arrival** (`time_of_arrival`, time, optional): Choose the time you plan to arrive at the resort. This will help us to coordinate your arrival with our staff.
* **Reservation ID** (`reservation_id`, number, optional): If you have a reservation ID, please enter it here. This will help us to locate your reservation and expedite the check-in process.
