# Educational Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational leadership training registration form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in educational leadership training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
Before starting the form, make sure you have all necessary information and have read the instructions carefully. This guide will walk you through each field and what you need to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Provide your valid email address for future communication and updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (4, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.

### Role
* **Role** (5, text, required: false): Describe your role or position in your organization or community.

### Skills
* **Skills** (6, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the skills that you are proficient in:
	+ Programming
	+ Leadership
	+ Management
	+ Communication

### Date of Birth
* **Date of birth** (7, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.

### Time Available
* **Time available** (8, time, required: false): Indicate how much time you can dedicate to the training program on a weekly basis.

### Location
* **Location** (9, text, required: false): Enter your location or where you would like to receive training.

### Interests
* **Interests** (10, select_multiple, required: false): Choose one or more of the interests that align you with the program:
	+ Learning
	+ Personal growth
	+ Networking

### Alternate Email
* **Alternate Email** (11, email, required: false): Provide an alternate email address where you can be reached in case the primary email is not available.

### Status
* **Status** (12, select_one, required: false): Indicate your current status:
	+ Active
	+ Inactive

### Additional Notes
* **Additional Notes** (13, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes that you think would be helpful for the trainers or coordinators.

### Select Multiple
* **Select Multiple** (14, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used for testing purposes, please do not fill out. 

### Time for Training
* **Time for Training** (15, time, required: false): Indicate how much time you are willing to dedicate for the training.

### Date of Training
* **Date of Training** (16, date, required: false): Enter the date you would prefer for the training.

### Select One
* **Select One** (17, select_one, required: false): This field is for testing purposes, please do not fill out.

### Notes
* **Notes** (18, note, required: false): Enter any other information that you feel would be beneficial for the trainers or coordinators to know.

Note: This guide is for informational purposes only. Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth process.
