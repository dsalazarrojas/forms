# Personal Training Program Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Program Access Form is designed for external clients to access your training programs. This form collects essential information to schedule and conduct the training sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Client Name** in the "Client Name" field. This is the primary identifier for your training session.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. This will be used to communicate with you about your training session.
3. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field. This will be used to contact you about your training session.
4. Select your preferred **Training Session Date** from the calendar.
5. Select your preferred **Training Session Time** from the clock.
6. Choose your preferred **Trainer** from the dropdown menu. You can select one or multiple trainers.
7. If applicable, select your purpose for the training session from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose the desired **Session Length** from the dropdown menu.
9. Add any **Client Notes** about your training session in the text area.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Training Session Date**: Select a date for your training session from the calendar. Please note that we may not be able to schedule sessions on dates that are too far in the future.
* **Training Session Time**: Select a time for your training session from the clock. Please note that we may not be able to schedule sessions at times that are too early or too late.
* **Preferred Trainer**: Choose one or multiple trainers that you would like to work with. Please note that availability of trainers may vary depending on your selected date and time.
* **Client Purpose**: If you have a specific purpose for your training session, please select it from the dropdown menu. If not applicable, please leave this field blank.
* **Session Length**: Choose the desired duration of your training session. Please note that we will do our best to accommodate your selected session length.
* **Client Notes**: Add any additional information or notes about your training session. This will help us to better understand your needs and provide you with the best possible service.
