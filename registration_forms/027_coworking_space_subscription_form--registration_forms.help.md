# coworking_space_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your coworking space subscription. Please provide the required information to complete your subscription.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out the "Workspace" field with the name of the coworking space you are subscribed to.
3. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select your subscription type from the "Subscription Type" dropdown menu.
7. Select your subscription term from the "Subscription Term" dropdown menu.
8. Choose your start date from the "Start Date" field.
9. Choose your end date from the "End Date" field.
10. Provide a brief description of your subscription in the "Description" field.
11. Select whether you have been assigned a tool from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workspace** (`workspace`, text, required): Enter the name of the coworking space you are subscribed to.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Subscription Type** (`subscription_type`, select_one, required): Select your subscription type ("Yes" or "No").
* **Subscription Term** (`subscription_term`, select_multiple, required): Select your subscription term ("Yes" or "No").
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose your start date.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Choose your end date.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of your subscription.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select whether you have been assigned a tool ("Yes" or "No").
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple tools that you have been assigned. 
*Note: This field seems to have the same name as the previous field, which could cause confusion. Please review and correct it.*

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields. 
* Be accurate when filling out your subscription details. 
* If you have any questions, contact your coworking space administrator.
