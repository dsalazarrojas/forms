# Network Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic and contact information from network participants. It is required for all participants, and the information will be used to store and manage participant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your organization (if applicable).
4. Enter your Email (if you have one).
5. Enter your Phone (if you have one).
6. Enter your Address (if you have one).
7. Enter your City (if you have one).
8. Select your State.
9. Enter your Zip Code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Organization** (`3`, `text`, optional): Enter your organization's name, if applicable.
* **Email** (`4`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address, if you have one.
* **Phone** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`6`, `text`, optional): Enter your physical address.
* **City** (`7`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`8`, `select_one`, required): Select your state (yes or no).
* **Zip** (`9`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have an organization, please provide it.
* If you have an email, please provide it.
* If you have a phone number, please provide it.
* If you have an address, please provide it.
* Make sure to select your state correctly.
* Enter your zip code in the correct format (e.g., 12345).
