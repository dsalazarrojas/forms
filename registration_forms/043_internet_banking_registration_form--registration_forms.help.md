# Internet Banking Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user registration information for online banking services. It's intended to be used by users who want to create new accounts, reset or update existing accounts, or perform other account-related actions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Username details. These fields are mandatory and must be completed for account creation.
2. Create a new password and confirm it in the next field. This is a security measure to protect your account.
3. In the "Review" field, you can add any additional comments or notes about your account.
4. Finally, click the "Submit" button to create or update your account.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address where you want to receive important account updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number to receive SMS or call alerts.
* **Username** (`username`, `text`, **required**): Choose a unique username for your account. This will be used to log in to your account.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, **required**): Create a new password for your account. This is a security measure to protect your account.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, **required**): Re-enter your password to confirm it's correct.
* **Review** (`review`, `note`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes about your account, if necessary.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, **required**): Click this button to create or update your account.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your details correctly, as they will be used to create or update your account.
* If you have any issues or concerns, you can contact our support team for assistance.
