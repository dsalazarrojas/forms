# Support Children Initiative Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Support Children Initiative Participation Form is a registration form designed to collect information from users who wish to participate in the Support Children Initiative. This form is used to gather contact information, role, and other relevant details to facilitate communication and coordination within the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form completely**: Please enter all required information (indicated by *) to ensure accurate processing of your application.
2. **Enter accurate and up-to-date information**: Double-check the accuracy of your name, email, and phone number.
3. **Choose your role correctly**: Select the role that best describes your position or involvement in the initiative.
4. **Add any additional comments**: If you have any comments or additional information regarding your participation, please use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **lastName**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
* **Organization**: If you are representing an organization, please enter the name of your organization.
* **Role**: Select one of the following roles:
	+ **Volunteer**: I am participating in the initiative as a volunteer.
	+ **Supporter**: I am supporting the initiative financially or otherwise.
	+ **Leader**: I am a leader or key person in the initiative.
* **Address**: Enter your address (home, work, or other).
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **DateOfBirthday**: If this field is not relevant, please leave it blank.
* **TimeAvailable**: If this field is not relevant, please leave it blank.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information you would like to provide.
* **ConfirmEmail**: Confirm your email address for verification purposes.
