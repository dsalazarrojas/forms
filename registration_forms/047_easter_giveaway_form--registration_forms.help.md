The purpose of this form is to collect information from users for an Easter giveaway event. The form is designed to gather essential details about the user, including their name, contact information, and preferences for the event. This form is crucial for the organizers to ensure that all necessary information is collected accurately and in a timely manner.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the Easter Date, which is the date of the Easter event. This field is optional and can be left blank if not applicable.
2.  Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields. Both of these fields are optional.
3.  Choose the correct Easter Time in the Time dropdown. This field is optional and has a specific list of time options.
4.  Select the Easter Time Zone from the dropdown list. This field is mandatory and has a list of time zone options.
5.  Enter your Email address in the Email field. This field is mandatory.
6.  Provide your Phone number in the Phone field. This field is mandatory.
7.  Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field. This field is optional.

# easter_giveaway_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users for an Easter giveaway event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Easter Date** (easter_date, date, optional): Enter the date of the Easter event.
*   **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
*   **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name.
*   **Easter Time** (easter_time, time, optional): Choose the time of the Easter event.
*   **Easter Time Zone** (easter_time_zone, select_one, mandatory): Select the correct time zone for the Easter event.
*   **Email** (easter_email, email, mandatory): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (easter_phone, text, mandatory): Enter your phone number.
*   **Note** (easter_note, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the Easter giveaway event.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all mandatory fields are filled before submitting the form.
*   Use the correct format for date (MM/DD/YY) and time (HH:MM AM/PM) when filling out the Easter Date and Easter Time fields.
*   Use the exact format for Email and Phone number as they will be used to contact you for further details.
