# Dance Class Booking Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for students to book their place in dance classes. To ensure accurate and smooth bookings, please fill out the form thoroughly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

- Fill in your booking details (page 1).
- Provide your contact information (page 2).
- Choose your payment method (page 3).
- Select the date and time of your desired class (page 4).
- Choose your preferred instructor (page 5).
- Provide additional class information (page 6).
- Select the level of your dance class (page 7).
- Choose the schedule and frequency of your class (page 8).
- Select the type of class you want to book (page 9).
- Enter your student details (pages 10-12).
- Provide any additional notes for your instructor (page 13).
- Get your form approved by the instructor (page 14).
- Get your form approved by the student (page 15).
- Submit your form (page 16).
- Select your payment method (page 17).
- Enter the payment amount (page 18).
- Add any notes about your booking (page 19).
- Check the status of your payment (page 20).
- Enter any additional information about your payment method (page 21).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Booking Details (page 1, `booking_details`)** (`title`, text, false): Enter a brief summary of your booking request.
* **User Contact (page 2, `contact_info`)** (`label`, text, false): Enter your contact information for us to reach out to you.
* **Payment Method (page 3, `payment`)** (`label`, text, false): Select the method by which you would like to pay for your class.
* **Class Date and Time (page 4, `date_and_time`)** (`label`, number, false): Enter the date and time of your desired class.
* **Instructors (page 5, `instructor`)** (`label`, select_multiple, false): Choose the instructor(s) you would like to teach your class.
* **Class Information (page 6, `class_info`)** (`label`, text, false): Enter any additional information about your class.
* **Class Type (page 7, `class_type`)** (`label`, select_one, false): Select the level of your class (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Schedule and Frequency (page 8, `class_schedule`)** (`label`, select_multiple, false): Choose how often you would like to attend your class (Weekly, Bi-Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Class Level (page 9, `class_level`)** (`label`, select_multiple, false): Select the level of your class (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Booking Type (page 10, `booking_type`)** (`label`, select_multiple, false): Choose the type of class you want to book (Regular, Drop-In).
* **Date (page 11, `date`)** (`label`, date, false): Enter the date of your desired class.
* **Time (page 12, `time`)** (`label`, time, false): Enter the time of your desired class.
* **Student Name (page 13, `student_name`)** (`label`, text, false): Enter your name as a student.
* **Student Email (page 14, `student_email`)** (`label`, email, false): Enter your email address as a student.
* **Student Phone (page 15, `student_phone`)** (`label`, text, false): Enter your phone number as a student.
* **Instructor Notes (page 16, `instructor_notes`)** (`label`, text, false): Add any notes for your instructor.
* **Instructor Approval (page 17, `instructor_approval`)** (`label`, select_one, false): Get your form approved by the instructor.
* **Student Approval (page 18, `student_approval`)** (`label`, select_one, false): Get your form approved by the student.
* **Submit (page 19, `submit`)** (`label`, text, false): Submit your form for review.
* **Submission Date (page 20, `submit_date`)** (`label`, date, false): The date and time you submitted your form.
* **Payment Method (page 21, `payment_method`)** (`label`, select_one, false): Select the method by which you paid for your class.
* **Payment Amount (page 22, `payment_amount`)** (`label`, number, false): Enter the amount you paid for your class.
* **Notes (page 23, `notes`)** (`label`, text, false): Add any additional notes about your booking.
* **Payment Status (page 24, `payment_status`)** (`label`, select_one, false): Check the status of your payment.
* **Payment Method Additional Info (page 25, `payment_method_additional_info`)** (`label`, text, false): Enter any additional information about your payment method.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to avoid any confusion.
* Double-check your contact and payment information before submitting.
* If you have any issues with your booking, contact us immediately.
