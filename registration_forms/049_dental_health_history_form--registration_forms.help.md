# Dental Health History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a patient's dental health history. It is intended to help healthcare professionals understand the patient's medical and dental conditions, current medications, and medical procedures. This information is crucial for providing personalized care and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the patient's information, including their name, date of birth, and contact details.
2. Answer the questions about the patient's medical history, including any previous medical conditions, current medications, and medical procedures.
3. Answer the questions about the patient's dental history, including any dental procedures, conditions, and allergies.
