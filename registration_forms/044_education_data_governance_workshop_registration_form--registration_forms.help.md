# Education Data Governance Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Education Data Governance Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for an educational workshop on data governance. This form is required for workshop registration and helps the organizers ensure accurate and unique registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Provide your personal information in the "Personal Info" field (Page 1).
* Select your workshop attendance preferences in the "Workshop Attendance" field (Page 2).
* Confirm your workshop attendance in the "Workshop Attendance Confirm" field (Page 3).
* Indicate your participation preferences in the "Workshop Participation" field (Page 4).
* If applicable, select the options for repeat workshop attendance and participation in the "Repeat Workshop Attendance" and "Repeat Workshop Participation" fields (Pages 5 and 6).
* Finally, confirm your repeat attendance and participation in the "Repeat Personal Info" field (Page 7).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Info** (Page 1): Enter your personal details such as name, email, and other relevant information. (Required: no, Type: text)
* **Workshop Attendance** (Page 2): Select one option for your workshop attendance. (Required: yes, Type: select_one)
* **Workshop Attendance Multiple** (Page 3): Select multiple options for your workshop attendance. (Required: yes, Type: select_multiple)
* **Workshop Participation** (Page 4): Indicate your participation preferences. (Required: yes, Type: checkbox)
* **Workshop Attendance Confirm** (Page 5): Confirm your workshop attendance. (Required: yes, Type: select_multiple)
* **Repeat Workshop Attendance** (Page 6): Select multiple options for repeat workshop attendance. (Required: no, Type: select_multiple)
* **Repeat Workshop Participation** (Page 7): Indicate your repeat participation preferences. (Required: no, Type: checkbox)
* **Repeat Personal Info** (Page 8): Re-enter your personal information for repeat registration. (Required: no, Type: text)

**Tips:**

* Please carefully review the "Workshop Attendance Confirm" field to ensure you are not registering for the same workshop multiple times.
* For repeat workshop attendance and participation, select the options only if you are actually attending or participating.
