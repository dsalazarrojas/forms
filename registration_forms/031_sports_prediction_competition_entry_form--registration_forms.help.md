# Sports Prediction Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to submit their predictions for a sports competition. Fill out the form to join the competition and submit your predictions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Make sure you have all the required information handy.
2. Fill out the form with your details and predictions.
3. Review your answers for accuracy before submitting.

* **Event ID**: Enter the ID number for the event you're interested in participating in. This helps us match your prediction with the correct competition.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you'd like to be known in the competition. This will be displayed on our leaderboard.
* **Prediction Score**: Enter the score you think the winning team will have. Use whole numbers only.
* **Prediction Date**: Enter the date and time you made your prediction.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you're predicting for.
* **Prediction Type**: Choose the outcome of the event: Win, Lose, or Draw.
* **Prediction**: Select one or more teams for each outcome.
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you'd like to be known in the competition. This is the name that will be displayed on our leaderboard.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address where we can contact you about your submission.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number where we can contact you about your submission.
