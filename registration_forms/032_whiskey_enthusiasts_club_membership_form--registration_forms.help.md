# Whiskey Enthusiasts Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the Whiskey Enthusiasts Club. It will help the club tailor its experience to your preferences and provide a better understanding of your interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields (marked as required).
2. Carefully consider and select from the options for the optional fields that interest you the most.
3. Double-check that all fields have been correctly filled out and reviewed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name to complete your profile.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name to complete your profile.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and membership updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contacting you regarding club activities.
* **What kind of whiskey do you prefer?** (`whiskey_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from the options to indicate your preferred types of whiskey (e.g., Scotch, Bourbon, Irish).
* **Favorite Whiskey Type** (`favorite_whiskey_type`, `select_one`, required): Select your favorite type of whiskey.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Enter your age to ensure you meet the club's age requirements.
* **How often do you drink whiskey?** (`drink_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you drink whiskey.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select the method you'd like to use for club payments.
* **Special Instructions** (`special_instructions`, `note`, optional): Add any special requests or information about your membership.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are ready to join the club.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you are available to join the club.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select from the options to indicate your desired membership type (e.g., standard, premium, VIP).
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information about your interests or experiences related to the club.
* **I agree to the terms of service** (`agreement_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select to confirm you've read and agree to the club's terms and conditions.
* **I agree to the privacy policy** (`agree_privacy_policy`, `select_one`, required): Select to confirm you've read and agree to the club's privacy policy.
* **I agree to receive newsletter updates** (`agree_newsletter`, `select_one`, optional): Select to receive updates about the club's events and activities.
* **I agree to be contacted for events** (`agree_events`, `select_one`, optional): Select to be contacted about attending club events.
* **I agree to be tagged on social media** (`agree_social_media`, `select_one`, optional): Select to be tagged on the club's social media channels.
* **I agree to donate to the club** (`agree_donate`, `select_one`, optional): Select to express your willingness to support the club financially.
* **I agree to volunteer** (`agree_volunteer`, `select_one`, optional): Select to express your interest in helping the club's activities.
* **I agree to share whiskey knowledge** (`agree_shares_whiskey_knowledge`, `select_one`, optional): Select to share your knowledge and experiences about whiskey.
* **I agree to share event information** (`agree_share_events`, `select_one`, optional): Select to share information about the club's events.
* **I agree to refer friends** (`agree_refer_friends`, `select_one`, optional): Select to refer friends or family interested in joining the club.
* **I have read and agree to the terms and conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select to confirm you've read and agree to the club's terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Review each field's purpose and options carefully before proceeding.
- Be honest and genuine in your answers.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask the club staff for clarification.
