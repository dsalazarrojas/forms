# BlueSnap Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The BlueSnap Conference Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees to facilitate a seamless and enjoyable conference experience. This form is used to gather personal details, professional preferences, and event management requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you answer all required fields.
3. Review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Conference Registration --** (Page 1, note): This section is a brief introduction to the conference.
* **First Name** (Required, text): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required, text): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Required, email): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Required, text): Please enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (Required, text): Please enter your company name.
* **Job Title** (Required, text): Please enter your job title.
* **Industry** (Required, select_one): Select your industry from the list provided:
	+ Technology
	+ Finance
	+ Healthcare
	+ Retail
	+ Manufacturing
	+ Education
	+ Other
* **Registration Type** (Required, select_one): Select your registration type from the list provided:
	+ General admission
	+ VIP pass
	+ Student
	+ Group (5+)
* **Registration Price** (Optional, number): If applicable, please enter the registration price.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional, text): If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify them.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed?** (Required, select_one): Select if you need any accessibility accommodations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Sessions of Interest** (Required, select_multiple): Select all the sessions you're interested in attending:
	+ Keynote speech
	+ Workshop A - Innovation
	+ Workshop B - Strategy
	+ Panel discussion
	+ Networking lunch
	+ Evening reception
* **Networking Interests** (Optional, text): If you have any specific networking interests, please specify them.
* **Is this your first time attending?** (Required, select_one): Select if this is your first time attending:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Company sponsorship interest?** (Optional, select_one): Select if your company is interested in sponsoring the event:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
* **Payment Method** (Required, select_one): Select your payment method:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Debit Card
	+ PayPal
	+ Invoice
* **Opt in to conference updates and future events?** (Optional, select_one): Select if you'd like to receive updates and event invitations:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Registration Date** (Required, date): Please enter your registration date.
