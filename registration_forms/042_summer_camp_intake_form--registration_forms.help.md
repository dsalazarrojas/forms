# Summer Camp Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Intake Form is a collection of fields that gather essential information from parents or guardians about their child(ren) for the summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and provide accurate information.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed as specified by the camp staff.
- Double-check that you have entered the correct information and review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information (1)**: Enter the summer camp information, such as the camp title or ID.
* **Camper Details (2)**: Enter the child's details, including their name, date of birth, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Details (3)**: Enter the parent/guardian's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Camper Information (4)**: Enter any additional camper information, such as medical conditions, allergies, or special needs.
* **Medical Information (5)**: Enter any relevant medical information about the camper, including medications, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Camper Preferences (6)**: Enter any camper preferences, such as dietary needs, special requests, or hobbies.
* **Medical History (7)**: Enter any relevant medical history, including medical conditions, allergies, or medical procedures.
* **Payment Information (8)**: Enter any payment details, such as payment method, payment status, or billing information.
* **Other Information (9)**: Enter any other relevant information about the camper, such as any other medical conditions or emergency contact details.
* **Registration Confirmation (10)**: This section is for notes and comments from the camper staff about the registration process.
* **Camper ID (11)**: Enter a unique ID for the camper.
* **Camper Name (12)**: Enter the camper's name.
* **Parent Name (13)**: Enter the parent/guardian's name.
* **Contact Email (14)**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Contact Phone (15)**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Medical Note (16)**: Enter any notes or comments about the camper's medical information.
* **Allergy Information (17)**: Enter any allergy information about the camper.
* **Emergency Information (18)**: Enter any emergency contact information, including name, relationship, and contact details.
* **Emergency Contact (19)**: Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Name (20)**: Enter the emergency contact person's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (21)**: Enter the relationship between the camper and the emergency contact person.
* **Camper Address (22)**: Enter the camper's address.
* **City (23)**: Enter the city where the camper resides.
* **State (24)**: Enter the state where the camper resides.
* **Zip (25)**: Enter the camper's zip code.
