# marathon_expo_vendor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The marathon expo vendor registration form is designed to collect information from vendors who will be participating in the marathon expo. This form will be used to gather general and contact details, as well as payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information section with your company's details.
2. Provide your contact information in the contact section.
3. Enter your payment details in the payment section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **general_info** (General Information, text, required: false): Enter your company's name, logo, and any other relevant information.
* **contact_info** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Fill in your contact person's name, email, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **payment** (Payment, text, required: false): Enter your payment method and any other payment-related information.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
