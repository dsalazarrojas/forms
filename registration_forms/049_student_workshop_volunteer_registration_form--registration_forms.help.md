# Student Workshop Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who want to volunteer for a workshop. It's a registration form for participants, facilitators, and presenters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first name, last name, date of birth, email address, and contact number.
2. Select your volunteer role: Participant, Facilitator, or Presenter.
3. Enter the title of the workshop you want to participate in.
4. Choose the date and time of the workshop you want to attend.
5. Specify the duration of the workshop you want to participate in.
6. Provide the location of the workshop.
7. Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact person.
8. Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
9. Enter your emergency contact person's email address.
10. If you have any medical conditions, list them in the "Medical Condition" field.
11. If you have any allergies, list them in the "Allergies" field.
12. Enter a description of your medical conditions in the "Medical Condition Details" field.
13. Enter any relevant details about your volunteer experience.
14. Choose the times you are available to volunteer: Day, Afternoon, or Weekend.
15. Enter any additional availability details.
16. If you have any information about your emergency contact's medical conditions, list them in the "Emergency Contact Medical Condition" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Role**: Select one or more of the following: Participant, Facilitator, or Presenter.
* **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop you want to participate in.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the workshop you want to attend.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop you want to participate in.
* **Workshop Duration**: Enter the duration of the workshop in hours.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter how you are related to the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Allergies**: If you have any allergies, enter them here.
* **Medical Condition Details**: Provide a description of your medical conditions.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Enter any relevant details about your volunteer experience.
* **Availability**: Select one or more of the following: Day, Afternoon, or Weekend.
* **Availability Details**: Enter any additional availability details.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Details**: Enter any details about your emergency contact relationship.
* **Emergency Contact Allergies**: If the emergency contact person has any allergies, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Condition**: If the emergency contact person has any medical conditions, enter them here.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Condition Details**: Provide a description of the emergency contact person's medical conditions.
