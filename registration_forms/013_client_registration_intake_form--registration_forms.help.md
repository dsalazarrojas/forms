# Client Registration Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and service-related information from clients, making it easier for our organization to provide effective and efficient services. 

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form accurately, follow these steps:
- Carefully read through each section and provide accurate information for each field.
- Ensure that you answer all required fields.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1.  Full Name (2)
* **Full Name (2)** (`Full Name`, `text`, required/true): 
  This field is for providing your full name. Please enter your full name without any abbreviations or nicknames. 

### 2. Date of Birth (3)
* **Date of Birth (3)** (`Date of Birth`, `date`, required/true): 
  This field is for providing your date of birth in the standard format (month/day/year). 

### 3. Email Address (4)
* **Email Address (4)** (`Email Address`, `email`, required/true): 
  This field is for your active email address that you check frequently. 

### 4. Phone Number (5)
* **Phone Number (5)** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required/true): 
  This field is for your active phone number where you can be reached. 

### 5. -- Address Information -- (6)
* **-- Address Information -- (6)** (`-- Address Information --`, `note`, required/false): 
  This section is for providing your address information. 

### 6. Street Address (7)
* **Street Address (7)** (`Street Address`, `text`, required/true): 
  This field is for your street address where you reside. 

### 7. City (8)
* **City (8)** (`City`, `text`, required/true): 
  This field is for your city where you reside. 

### 8. State or Province (9)
* **State or Province (9)** (`State or Province`, `text`, required/true): 
  This field is for your state or province where you reside. 

### 9. ZIP or Postal Code (10)
* **ZIP or Postal Code (10)** (`ZIP or Postal Code`, `text`, required/true): 
  This field is for your postal code. 

### 10. Country (11)
* **Country (11)** (`Country`, `text`, required/true):

### 11. -- Service Preferences -- (12)
* **-- Service Preferences -- (12)** (`-- Service Preferences --`, `note`, required/false):

### 12. Services Interested In (13)

### 13. How Did You Hear About Us? (14)

### 17. Any Specific Needs or Preferences? (18)

### 19. Additional Information (20)

## Tips
