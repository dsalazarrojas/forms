# Lash Technician Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Technician Course Registration Form is designed to collect registration data from users interested in enrolling in a Lash Technician course. This form helps course organizers and instructors track course progress and participant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Provide your address in the "address" field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" for "course_name" based on whether you are interested in the course.
7. Enter the course start date in the "course_start_date" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
8. Enter the name of the course instructor in the "instructor_name" field.
9. Enter the course duration in the number of weeks or months.
10. Enter the course price in the format of currency (e.g., "$X.XX").
11. Provide a brief description of the course in the "course_description" field.
12. Select "Cash," "Credit Card," or "Bank Transfer" as your payment method in the "payment_method" field.
13. Enter the amount paid in the "payment_amount" field (in the format of currency, e.g., "$X.XX").
14. Enter your enrollment date in the "enrollment_date" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
15. Enter your graduation date in the "graduation_date" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
16. Enter your name as a graduate in the "graduate_name" field.
17. Provide your contact information in the "graduate_contact" field.
18. Enter your email address in the "graduate_email" field.
19. Enter your phone number in the "graduate_phone" field.
20. Select "Basic," "Advanced," or "Expert" for "course_level" based on your course level.
21. Indicate whether you have or do not have certification for the course in the "certification" field.
22. Provide any additional comments or notes about your course experience in the "notes" field.
23. Select "Successful" or "Unsuccessful" for "form_submission" based on your course submission status.
24. Enter your submission date in the "submission_date" field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
25. Enter your name as the submitter in the "submitter" field.
