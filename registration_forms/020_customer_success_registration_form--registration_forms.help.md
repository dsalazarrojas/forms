# Customer Success Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Success Registration Form is a tool used to collect important information about our customers and their company details. This form helps us understand your business needs and provide the best possible support and service.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your company.
2. Make sure to complete each field correctly, as it will help us understand your requirements accurately.
3. If you're unsure about any field, please feel free to ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Success Registration Form** (`customer_success_registration_form`, required/optional): This is the title of the form, which is not editable.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, optional): Please enter your contact number to ensure we can reach you easily.
* **Email** (`email`, optional): Provide your email address so we can communicate with you efficiently.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Company** (`company`, optional): Enter your company name for accurate record-keeping and future reference.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, optional): Please enter your job title or role in the company.
* **Department** (`department`, optional): If applicable, enter your department within the company.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, optional): If you have a manager, please enter their name for us to know who to contact.
