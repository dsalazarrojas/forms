# Therapy Walk In Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapy Walk In Registration form is designed to collect user information for a therapy walk-in appointment. This information will help the medical facility prepare for the appointment and ensure that the patient receives proper treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your personal and medical information. You can type in the answers directly into the form fields. If you have any issues or require assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Please enter your name and any other relevant personal details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Therapy Information** (`therapy_info`, text, required: false): Describe the reason for your visit and the type of therapy you are seeking.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, required: false): Share any relevant medical history, including allergies, medications, or previous surgeries.
* **Insurance Information** (`insurance_info`, text, required: false): If applicable, provide your insurance information, including your policy number and provider.
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required: false): Sign and date the consent form, acknowledging that you understand and agree to the terms of your treatment.

## Tips
* Please fill in all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* This form is an electronic version of the traditional paper form, and your responses will be reviewed by the medical staff before your appointment.
* You can ask for a paper copy of this form if you need one for your records.
