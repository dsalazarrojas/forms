# Multilingual Instruction Research Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a Multilingual Instruction Research Group. It collects information about the research group, including its name, contact person, language, and goals. Fill out this form to ensure that your research group is registered and can participate in relevant activities and discussions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, denoted by an asterisk (\*).
2. Ensure that all information is accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **research_group_name** (`research_group_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your research group.
* **contact_person_name** (`contact_person_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the research group.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person or a valid email address associated with the research group.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number for the contact person or research group.
* **research_group_language** (`research_group_language`, select_one, optional): Select the language of the research group (e.g., French, German, Italian, Spanish, English).
* **research_group_description** (`research_group_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the research group's purpose and goals.
* **research_group_goals** (`research_group_goals`, select_multiple, optional): Select the goals of the research group (e.g., Goal 1, Goal 2, Goal 3, Goal 4).
* **date_of_birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the date of birth of the contact person or a relevant date associated with the research group.
* **research_group_start_date** (`research_group_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the research group.
* **research_group_end_date** (`research_group_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the research group (if applicable).
* **submitted_by** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted_date** (`submitted_date`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **research_group_type** (`research_group_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of research group (e.g., academic, industry, government).
* **submitted_by_name** (`submitted_by_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted_date_time** (`submitted_date_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **research_group_leaders** (`research_group_leaders`, text, optional): Enter the names of the research group leaders.
* **research_group_coordinators** (`research_group_coordinators`, text, optional): Enter the names of the research group coordinators.
* **research_group_members** (`research_group_members`, text, optional): Enter the names of the research group members.
* **research_group_members_count** (`research_group_members_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of research group members.
* **research_group_location** (`research_group_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the research group (e.g., university, city, country).
* **research_group_language_of_submission** (`research_group_language_of_submission`, text, optional): Enter the language of submission (e.g., English, French).
* **research_group_notes** (`research_group_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the research group.
* **submitted_by_email** (`submitted_by_email`, email, optional): Enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted_by_phone** (`submitted_by_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **submitted_by_country** (`submitted_by_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the person submitting the form.

Note: This form is only for registering a research group, and all fields are optional. However, ensure that you fill out all relevant fields to provide accurate and up-to-date information about your research group.
