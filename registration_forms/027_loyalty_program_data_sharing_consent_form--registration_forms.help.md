# Loyalty Program Data Sharing Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to obtain your consent for sharing your customer data with third parties. This form is used in conjunction with other registration forms and is not intended for general data collection or marketing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Review the sharing methods listed below and select the methods you are comfortable with.
2. Select one of the options for your consent.
3. Provide any additional comments you may have about data sharing.
4. Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Data** (`1`, `Customer Data`, text, optional): Enter any relevant information about yourself, such as your name, address, or other personal details. This will help us understand who you are and what data we are sharing with whom.
* **Sharing Methods** (`2`, `Sharing Methods`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the methods by which you are comfortable with your data being shared:
	+ Email: Sharing your data via email with third parties.
	+ Phone: Sharing your data via phone with third parties.
	+ Mail: Sharing your data via mail with third parties.
* **Consent** (`3`, `Consent`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options to indicate your consent status:
	+ I consent: You agree to share your data with third parties.
	+ I do not consent: You do not agree to share your data with third parties.
* **Additional Comments** (`4`, `Additional Comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or explanations you would like to share about your data sharing preferences.
* **E-mail** (`5`, `E-mail`, email, optional): Enter your email address to be contacted about your consent. This is optional but recommended in case we need to follow up with you about your consent.

## Tips
* Please make sure to answer all required fields, but feel free to provide as much information as you are comfortable with.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We value your privacy and will respect your data sharing preferences.
* If you change your mind, you can always contact us to update your consent status.
