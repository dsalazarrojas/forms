# seminar_coordinator_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The seminar coordinator volunteer registration form is designed to collect information from volunteers who will be assisting with a seminar. This form helps us understand the volunteers' details, preferences, and availability, which aids in organizing and scheduling the seminar events efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you're registering for the correct seminar event.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Make sure to submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Information** (1): This is the page to enter your general information, such as name and title.
* **Seminar Information** (2): Please provide details about the seminar you're registering for, including its purpose and any relevant notes.
* **Contact Details** (3): Enter your phone number and any other contact information.
* **Email** (4): Enter your email address to receive updates and important seminar information.
* **Phone** (5): Enter your phone number for further communication and coordination.
* **Date of Birth** (6): Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Seminar Date** (7): Enter the date of the seminar you're registering for.
* **Seminar Time** (8): Enter the time of the seminar you're registering for.
* **Volunteer Shifts** (9): Choose the volunteer shifts you're available to work during the seminar (Yes or No).
* **Seminar Duration** (10): Enter the expected duration of the seminar in hours.
* **Event Type** (11): Choose the type of event (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Volunteer Type** (12): Choose whether you are a Seminar Coordinator or a Volunteer.
* **Role** (13): Choose your role in the event (Chairperson, Assistant, or Support).
* **Availability** (14): Indicate your availability for the seminar (Available or Unavailable).
* **Comments** (15): Enter any additional comments or questions you have about the seminar or your role.

Note: Please submit the form once you've completed all the required fields.
