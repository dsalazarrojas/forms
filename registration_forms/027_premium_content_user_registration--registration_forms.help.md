# premium_content_user_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your premium content preferences to provide you with a personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred contact time from the options in the "Preferred Contact Time" field.
6. Select your preferred contact frequency from the options in the "Preferred Contact Frequency" field.
7. Review and agree to the terms in the "Agreement" field.
8. Specify the type of content you are interested in in the "Content Type" field.
9. Describe the content topic you are interested in in the "Content Topic" field.
10. Provide a brief description of the content you are interested in in the "Content Description" field.
11. Enter the link to the content you are interested in in the "Content Link" field.
12. Specify any video content you have in the "Content Video" field.
13. Specify any audio content you have in the "Content Audio" field.
14. Specify any image content you have in the "Content Image" field.
15. Specify any document content you have in the "Content Document" field.
16. Specify any file content you have in the "Content File" field.
17. Enter any other content you have in the "Content Other" field.
18. Select your subscription plan from the options in the "Subscription Plan" field.
19. Choose whether your subscription is free or paid in the "Subscription Term" field.
20. Select when you want to start your subscription from the options in the "Subscription Start" field.
21. Choose whether you want to end your subscription or not in the "Subscription End" field.
22. Confirm whether you agree to the content agreement in the "Content Agreement" field.
23. If applicable, enter the date of your content agreement in the "Content Agreement Date" field.
24. Specify your premium feature 1 in the "Premium Feature 1" field.
25. Specify your premium feature 2 in the "Premium Feature 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number.
* **Preferred Contact Time** (`preferred_contact_time`, select_multiple, required): Choose when you would like to be contacted (morning, afternoon, evening).
* **Preferred Contact Frequency** (`preferred_contact_frequency`, select_multiple, required): Choose how often you would like to be contacted (daily, weekly, biweekly).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, note, required): Review and agree to the terms.
* **Content Type** (`content_type`, text, required): Specify the type of content you are interested in.
* **Content Topic** (`content_topic`, text, required): Describe the content topic you are interested in.
* **Content Description** (`content_description`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the content you are interested in.
* **Content Link** (`content_link`, text, required): Enter the link to the content you are interested in.
* **Content Video** (`content_video`, text, required): Specify any video content you have.
* **Content Audio** (`content_audio`, text, required): Specify any audio content you have.
* **Content Image** (`content_image`, text, required): Specify any image content you have.
* **Content Document** (`content_document`, text, required): Specify any document content you have.
* **Content File** (`content_file`, text, required): Specify any file content you have.
* **Content Other** (`content_other`, text, required): Enter any other content you have.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, required): Choose your subscription plan (monthly, annual).
* **Subscription Term** (`subscription_term`, select_one, required): Choose whether your subscription is free or paid.
* **Subscription Start** (`subscription_start`, select_one, required): Choose when you want to start your subscription (start now, later).
* **Subscription End** (`subscription_end`, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to end your subscription or not (no end date, end date).
* **Content Agreement** (`content_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you agree to the content agreement.
* **Content Agreement Date** (`content_agreement_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of your content agreement.
* **Premium Feature 1** (`premium_feature_1`, text, required): Specify your premium feature 1.
* **Premium Feature 2** (`premium_feature_2`, text, required): Specify your premium feature 2.

## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields.
* Make sure to select all required options.
* If you have any issues, please contact our support team.
