# virtual_card_class_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering attendees for a virtual card class. The information collected will help us process your registration and provide you with updates about the class.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your attendee details.
2. Select your class choices.
3. Choose your consent.
4. Optionally, submit your form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, **Required**): Enter your details as you would like to be known for the class.
* **Class Choices** (`class_choices`, Optional): Choose one or more classes you'd like to join. Selecting multiple classes is allowed, so if you want to join multiple classes, please choose one option for each class.
* **Consent** (`consent`, Optional): Indicate your consent to participate in the class. You can select 'Agree', 'Decline', or 'Undetermined' depending on your decision.
* **Submission** (`submission`, **Note**): This is a submission button. Please use it to finalize your registration once you've completed all the necessary fields.
