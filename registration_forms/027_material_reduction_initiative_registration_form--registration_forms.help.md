# Material Reduction Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from individuals or organizations interested in participating in the Material Reduction Initiative. The data collected will help us verify your eligibility and onboard you or your organization onto the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name in the "Name" field. This is the primary contact person for the initiative.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that you frequently use.
3.  Specify your organization's name in the "Organization" field if you are a representative of an organization.
4.  Choose the type of participant you are. Select "Individual" if you are a single person, or "Organization" if you represent a group.
5.  Enter the size of your team, if applicable, in the "Team Size" field.
6.  Fill in the role you are interested in playing in the "Role" field.
7.  Provide the name of the program you are interested in participating in the "Program Name" field.
8.  Briefly describe the program you are interested in participating in the "Program Description" field.
9.  Input the start date of the program you are interested in. This should be the date it is expected to start.
10.  Input the end date of the program you are interested in. This should be the date it is expected to end.
11.  Ensure that you have filled out all the required fields marked as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (text): Enter your name as the primary contact person for the initiative.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address you frequently use. This will be our primary means of communication.
* **Organization** (text): If you are a representative of an organization, please enter the name of your organization.
* **Participant Type** (select_one): Choose "Individual" if you are a single person, or "Organization" if you represent a group.
* **Team Size** (number): Enter the number of people in your team, if applicable.
* **Role** (text): Describe the role you are interested in playing in the initiative.
* **Program Name** (text): Enter the name of the program you are interested in participating in.
* **Program Description** (text): Briefly describe the program you are interested in participating in.
* **Program Start Date** (date): Enter the expected start date of the program.
* **Program End Date** (date): Enter the expected end date of the program.

Note: All required fields are marked as "required" in the form. Please fill out all required fields to ensure that your submission is processed correctly.
