# Ethics Review Board Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethics Review Board Volunteer Registration form is used to collect information from individuals who are interested in volunteering for the Ethics Review Board.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1.  Enter your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name". This field is used to collect your name as it appears on your official identification.
2.  Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name". This field is used to collect your surname as it appears on your official identification.
3.  Enter your **Email** in the field labeled "Email". This field is used to contact you regarding your application and any further communication related to the Ethics Review Board.
4.  Enter your **Phone** number in the field labeled "Phone". This field is used to contact you via phone for further questions or clarification.
5.  Select your **Availability** in the field labeled "Availability". This field is used to determine your availability for volunteering and is used to match you with available volunteer opportunities.
6.  Read and confirm your **Consent** in the field labeled "Consent". This field is used to confirm that you have read and understand the terms of volunteering for the Ethics Review Board.
7.  Select your **University** in the field labeled "University". This field is used to determine your affiliation with a university.
8.  Select your **Department** in the field labeled "Department". This field is used to determine your affiliation with a department at the selected university.
9.  Select your **Hospital** in the field labeled "Hospital". This field is used to determine your affiliation with a hospital.
10.  Select your **Nonprofit** affiliation in the field labeled "Nonprofit". This field is used to determine your affiliation with a nonprofit organization.
11.  Select your **Role** in the field labeled "Role". This field is used to determine your role or position within the selected university or nonprofit organization.
12.  Select your **Board** affiliation in the field labeled "Board". This field is used to determine your affiliation with the board at the selected university or nonprofit organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, not required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, not required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address used for contact and communication.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for further questions or clarification.
*   **Availability** (`availability`, select\_multiple, not required): Select your availability to volunteer.
*   **Consent** (`consent`, note, not required): Confirm that you have read and understand the terms of volunteering for the Ethics Review Board.
*   **Confirm Consent** (`confirm\_consent`, select\_one, not required): Confirm your consent to volunteer.
*   **University** (`university`, select\_one, not required): Select your affiliated university.
*   **Department** (`department`, select\_one, not required): Select your department at the affiliated university.
*   **Hospital** (`hospital`, select\_one, not required): Select your affiliated hospital.
*   **Nonprofit** (`nonprofit`, select\_one, not required): Select your affiliation with a nonprofit organization.
*   **Role** (`role`, select\_multiple, not required): Select your role within the affiliated university or nonprofit organization.
*   **Board** (`board`, select\_multiple, not required): Select your board affiliation at the affiliated university or nonprofit organization.
