# Waterpark Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Waterpark Registration Form is used to collect demographic and personal information from users who want to register with Waterpark.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field in the format `username@example.com`.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Select your gender from the "gender" field.
7. If your gender is not listed, enter your gender in the "other" field.
8. Select your marital status from the "marital status" field.
9. If your marital status is not listed, enter your marital status in the "User Marital Status Other" field.
10. Select your nationality from the "nationality" field.
11. If your nationality is not listed, enter your nationality in the "User Nationality Other" field.
12. Enter your place of birth in the "place of birth" field.
13. Enter your national ID number in the "national id" field.
14. Enter your passport number in the "passport number" field.
15. Select your employment status from the "employment status" field.
16. If your employment status is not listed, enter your employment status in the "User Employment Status Other" field.
17. Enter your occupation in the "occupation" field.
18. Select your employment type from the "employment type" field.
19. If your employment type is not listed, enter your employment type in the "User Employment Type Other" field.
20. Select your languages known from the "languages known" field.
21. If any languages are not listed, enter them in the "other languages known" field.
22. Select your language level from the "language level" field.
23. If your language level is not listed, enter your language level in the "User Language Level Other" field.
24. Enter any additional notes about yourself in the "notes" field.
