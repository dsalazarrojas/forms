# Designer Showcases Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees, designers, or other stakeholders involved in the designer showcases event. The data collected will be used to track attendee preferences, track event progress, and facilitate event planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your **Attendee Type** by choosing from the options: "Designer", "Organizer", or "Guest".
6. Select your **Design Experience** by choosing from the options: "1-2 years", "3-5 years", "6-10 years", or "More than 10 years".
7. Enter the **Showcase Name** you are interested in attending.
8. Enter your **Organization** (if applicable).
9. Enter your **Role** at the organization (if applicable).
10. Add any additional **Notes** about your attendance or preferences.
11. Select the **Date** of the event you plan to attend.
12. Select the **Time** of the event you plan to attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A required field to collect your name.
* **Last Name**: A required field to collect your last name.
* **Email**: An optional field to collect your email address.
* **Phone**: An optional field to collect your phone number.
* **Attendee Type**: A field to select your role in the event (Designer, Organizer, or Guest).
* **Design Experience**: A field to select your level of design experience.
* **Showcase Name**: A field to collect the showcase name you are interested in attending.
* **Organization**: An optional field to collect your organization name (if applicable).
* **Role**: An optional field to collect your role at the organization (if applicable).
* **Notes**: A field to collect any additional information about your attendance or preferences.
* **Date**: A field to select the date of the event you plan to attend.
* **Time**: A field to select the time of the event you plan to attend.
* **Submitted**: A read-only field that indicates the date and time the form was submitted.
