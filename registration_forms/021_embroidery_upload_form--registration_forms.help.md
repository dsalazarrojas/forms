# Embroidery Upload Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Embroidery Upload Form is designed to collect customer contact information and files for embroidery services. This form allows customers to provide their email address, select the type of file to upload, and upload the file itself.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your customer email address in the "Customer Email" field.
2. Select the type of file you want to upload by choosing one or more options in the "File Type" field.
3. Enter a name for your file in the "File Name" field.
4. Describe the contents of your file in the "File Contents" field.
5. Write a message to the customer service team in the "Customer Message" field (optional).
6. Finally, click the "Submit" button.
