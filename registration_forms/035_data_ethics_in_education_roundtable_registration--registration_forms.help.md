# Data Ethics In Education Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Ethics in Education Roundtable Registration form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in attending or participating in the roundtable event. This form is essential for facilitating a productive discussion and gathering of ideas on data ethics in education.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the text field.
2. Provide your email address in the format of `username@example.com`.
3. Select your interests for the roundtable discussion.
4. Consent to the use of your data for research and organizational purposes.
5. (Optional) Provide any additional details you'd like to share with the organizers.
6. Consent to the sharing of your email address with the organizers for communication purposes.
7. Confirm your consent to researchers for the use of your data.
8. Provide any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **prophets_name**: Enter your name in this text field. This is a required field.
* **event_organizer_email**: Enter your email address in the format of `username@example.com`. This is a required field.
* **topic_interests**: Select all the topics you are interested in for the roundtable discussion. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **consent**: Select whether you consent to the use of your data for research and organizational purposes. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **additional_details**: Provide any additional details you'd like to share with the organizers. This is an optional field.
* **topics_of_interest**: Select all the topics you are interested in for the roundtable discussion. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **agenda_items**: Select all the agenda items you are interested in for the roundtable discussion. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **consent_to_organizers**: Select whether you consent to the use of your email address with the organizers. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **consent_to_researcher**: Select whether you consent to the use of your data for research purposes. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **consent_to_organizers_email**: Select whether you consent to the sharing of your email address with the organizers. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **consent_to_researcher_email**: Select whether you consent to the sharing of your email address with researchers. These options are:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **additional_information**: Provide any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers. This is an optional field.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* Make sure to provide a valid email address for communication purposes.
* You may select multiple options for topics and agenda items.
* You can provide any additional information you'd like to share with the organizers.
