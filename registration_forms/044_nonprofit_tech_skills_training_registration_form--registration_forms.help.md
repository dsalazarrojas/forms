# Nonprofit Tech Skills Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Nonprofit Tech Skills Training program. It collects essential information about the participant, their skills, and interests to ensure a tailored and effective training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and email address to allow us to contact you and keep you updated about the training.
2.  Enter your phone number to facilitate communication and coordination.
3.  Choose the tech skills you have experience with from the list.
4.  Select the topics you are interested in from the list.
5.  Click "Submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participants Name (<name>, <text>, required: false)**: Please enter your name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Participants Email (<name>, <email, required: false)**: Enter your valid email address so we can contact you about the training.
* **Participants Phone (<name>, <text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for communication and coordination purposes.
* **Tech Skills (<name>, <select_multiple, required: false)**: Choose the tech skills you have experience with from the list:
	+ Programming
	+ Data Science
	+ Web Development
	+ Artificial Intelligence
	+ Machine Learning
	+ Data Analysis
* **Topic Interests (<name>, <select_multiple, required: false)**: Select the topics you are interested in from the list:
	+ Digital Literacy
	+ E-Government
	+ E-Health
	+ E-Education
	+ E-Banking
	+ E-Commerce

## Tips
* Please ensure the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* You can choose multiple skills and topics, but please be specific about your experience and interests.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
