# Interest Registration For Product Launch - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Interest Registration For Product Launch" form is a tool for customers or users to register their interest in a product launch. This form helps us gauge interest and gather feedback for future product developments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Submit Interest Registration" button on the product landing page.
2. A new form will appear with several fields that you need to fill out.
3. Fill in your details accurately and thoroughly.
4. Choose any relevant options from the dropdown menus where applicable.
5. Click the "Submit" button to register your interest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Date
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required: false): This field is not applicable as this form has no date-related fields. You can ignore this field.

### Customer Name
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, required: false): This field asks for your name. Please enter your full name for us to get a better understanding of who is expressing interest in the product launch.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for your contact email. It will be used to reach out to you for further discussions or updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is for your contact phone number. If you prefer to be contacted via phone, please make sure to fill this in.

### Product Interest
* **Product Interest** (`product_interest`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to express your interest in various product options. Please select all that apply from the list.

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information or feedback you'd like to provide about your interest.

### Custom Fields
* **Custom Fields 1-19** (`custom_fields_1-19`, select_one, required: false): These fields are for any additional information specific to your interest. Please select an option from the provided list. You can fill out any or all of these fields as per your preference.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all relevant fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Choose all options that apply for your interest in the product launch.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, feel free to reach out to us.
* This form is a one-time submission form. Do not resubmit your interest unless you have new information or updates.
* We appreciate your interest and look forward to reaching out to you for further discussions or updates.
