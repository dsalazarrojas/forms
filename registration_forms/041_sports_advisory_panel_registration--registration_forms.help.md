# Sports Advisory Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Advisory Panel Registration form is a tool for individuals to sign up to be part of a sports advisory panel. This form collects relevant information to identify and categorize individuals for their respective roles within the panel.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal details.
2. Select the sport of interest from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your role in the sports advisory panel.
4. Provide a brief description of your experience and qualifications relevant to the panel.
5. Agree to the terms of service and privacy policy of the sportspersonnel (if applicable).
6. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This is the primary contact method for the sports advisory panel.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for further information.
* **Address**: Enter your address for communication and potential meetings.
* **What Sport**: Select the sport of interest for the sports advisory panel.
* **Role**: Choose your role in the sports advisory panel (sports leader, volunteer, committee member, or other).
* **About Me**: Provide a brief description of your experience and qualifications relevant to the panel.
* **Agree Term of Service**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service.
* **Terms of Service**: Please read and agree to the terms of service.
* **Agree Privacy Policy**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy of the sportspersonnel (if applicable).
* **Privacy Policy**: Please read and agree to the privacy policy of the sportspersonnel.
* **Confirm Submission**: Confirm your submission to complete the registration process.
* **Name of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Enter the name of the sportspersonnel, if you are registering on their behalf.
* **Sport of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Enter the sport of the sportspersonnel, if applicable.
* **Phone of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Enter the phone number of the sportspersonnel, if applicable.
* **Email of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Enter the email address of the sportspersonnel, if applicable.
* **Address of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Enter the address of the sportspersonnel, if applicable.
* **Agree to Privacy Policy of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the privacy policy of the sportspersonnel.
* **Agree to Terms of Service of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms of service of the sportspersonnel.
* **Confirm Submission of Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: Confirm your submission to complete the registration process for the sportspersonnel.
* **Other Sportspersonnel (if applicable)**: If not listed in the sportspersonnel, add additional information about the sportspersonnel.

# Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are registering on behalf of another person, ensure you have their permission to do so.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the sports advisory panel administrator.
* If you have any issues with the form, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please review our terms of service and privacy policy carefully before agreeing to them.
* This form is only intended for sports advisory panel registration, and not for general public registration or event registration.
