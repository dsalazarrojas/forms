# Employee Appreciation Luncheon Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Employee Appreciation Luncheon Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from employees attending the appreciation luncheon event. This form is used to ensure accurate headcount, dietary needs, and other relevant details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your name as it appears on your badge.
3. Provide your job title.
4. Enter your email address for contact purposes.
5. Add your phone number for easy communication.
6. Indicate your dietary needs (Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Meat, or None).
7. Choose the time slot you are available for the event.
8. Specify the number of attendees accompanying you.
9. Leave any additional message or comments for the organizers.
10. If necessary, enter a second event date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Guest Information
• **Name** (required): Enter your name as it appears on your badge.
### Name
• **Job Title** (required): Enter your job title.
### Email
• **Email** (required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
### Phone
• **Phone** (optional): Add your phone number for easy communication.
### Dietary Needs
• **Dietary Needs** (optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Vegetarian
	+ Non-Vegetarian
	+ Meat
	+ None
### RSVP
• **RSVP** (optional): Choose one of the following options:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
### Meal Preferences
• **Meal Preferences** (optional): Choose one or more of the following options:
	+ Beef
	+ Fish
	+ Chicken
	+ None
### Message
• **Message** (optional): Leave any additional comments or remarks for the organizers.
### Note
• **Note** (optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
### Preferred Time
• **Preferred Time** (optional): Choose the time slot you are available for the event.
### Event Date
• **Event Date** (optional): Enter the date of the second event.
### Event Date 2
• **Event Date 2** (optional): Enter the date of the second event (if necessary).
### Attendee Count
• **Attendee Count** (optional): Specify the number of attendees accompanying you.
### Message 2
• **Message 2** (optional): Leave any additional comments or remarks for the second event.
### Start Time 2
• **Start Time 2** (optional): Choose the time slot for the second event.
### End Time
• **End Time** (optional): Choose the end time for the event.
### Tips
- Ensure you fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Double-check your details for errors before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, please mention them in the 'Message' field.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the organizers.
