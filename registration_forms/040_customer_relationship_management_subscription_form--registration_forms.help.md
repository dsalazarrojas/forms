# customer_relationship_management_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The customer relationship management subscription form is used to collect information about a customer's subscription to the customer relationship management system. It is used to process and manage the subscription details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
2. Select the subscription type that best describes your subscription.
3. Enter the start date of your subscription.
4. Enter the end date of your subscription.
5. Choose one or more payment methods that apply to your subscription.
6. Enter the payment amount for your subscription.
7. Select the payment frequency of your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address associated with your subscription.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number associated with your subscription.
* **Subscription Type**: Select the type of subscription that applies to your service. This can be used to differentiate between types of subscriptions, such as trial, paid, or free.
* **Subscription Start Date**: Enter the date when your subscription begins.
* **Subscription End Date**: Enter the date when your subscription ends. If the end date is unknown, leave this field blank.
* **Payment Method**: Choose one or more payment methods that apply to your subscription. This can be used to process payments for your subscription.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you will be paying for your subscription.
* **Payment Frequency**: Select the frequency at which you will be paying for your subscription, such as monthly or annually.
