# Change Management Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Change Management Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from participants who want to register for a change management training session. This form helps the organizers to provide the best experience to the participants and ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and according to the instructions provided.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organizers for clarification.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date**: This field is used to record the date of registration. Please provide the date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Participant Name**: This field is used to record the full name of the participant.
* **Email Address**: This field is used to record the email address of the participant for confirmation and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: This field is used to record the contact phone number of the participant.
* **Job Title**: This field is used to record the current position of the participant.
* **Department**: This field is used to record the department of the participant.
* **Organization**: This field is used to record the name of the participant's organization.
* **Manager Name**: This field is optional and used to record the name of the participant's supervisor.
* **Training Session Selection**: This field is used to select the training session that the participant plans to attend. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Session Date**: This field is used to record the preferred date of the training session.
* **Change Management Experience**: This field is used to record the participant's level of experience in change management. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Learning Objectives**: This field is used to record the learning objectives of the participant. Please choose all relevant options from the provided list.
* **Certification Interest**: This field is used to record if the participant is interested in certification. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Payment Method**: This field is used to record the payment method of the participant. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Employer Sponsored**: This field is used to record if the participant's employer is sponsoring their registration. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Dietary Requirements**: This field is optional and used to record any dietary needs of the participant.
* **Accessibility Needs**: This field is optional and used to record any accessibility needs of the participant.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: This field is optional and used to record the source of awareness about the training session.
* **Special Requests**: This field is optional and used to record any special requirements of the participant.
* **Terms and Conditions**: This field is used to record if the participant agrees to the terms and conditions. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Cancellation Policy**: This field is used to record if the participant understands the cancellation policy. Please choose one option from the provided list.
* **Participant Signature**: This field is used to record the participant's signature. Please type your full name as your signature.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact the organizers for clarification.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is correct.
* Do not fill out the form if you are unsure about any field.
