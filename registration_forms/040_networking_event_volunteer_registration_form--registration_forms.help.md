# Networking Event Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Networking Event Volunteer Registration Form is used to register volunteers for a networking event. This form collects contact information, shift preferences, and role assignments for event organization and communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter Your Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): This is where you will enter your name as a volunteer for the event.
2. **Choose Your Shift** (`shift`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shift for the event from the provided options (8am-12pm, 12pm-4pm, 4pm-8pm).
3. **Enter Your Contact Information** (`contact_name`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number to ensure event organizers can contact you.
4. **Specify Your Event Details** (`event_start_date`, `event_end_date`, text, optional): Enter the date and time of the event, including the start and end dates.
5. **Choose Your Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select your role within the event from the provided options (Event Organizer, Volunteer, Host, Sponsor).
6. **Provide Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
7. **Add Any Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments for the event organizers.
8. **Specify Assigned Tools** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter any tools or equipment assigned to you for the event.
9. **Submit**: Once you have completed all required and optional fields, click submit to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name**: This field is optional, but please enter the name of the event to help event organizers keep track of multiple events.
* **Shift**: Select your preferred shift to ensure you are working at a time that suits you best.
* **Contact Name**: Please enter your name as a volunteer for the event to help event organizers contact you.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address to ensure event organizers can contact you before, during, or after the event.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number to allow event organizers to quickly contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date and time of the event to help event organizers prepare for the event.
* **Event End Date**: Enter the end date and time of the event to help event organizers plan accordingly.
* **Role**: Choose your role within the event to help event organizers know who to contact and what tasks you will be performing.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event to help event organizers plan logistics and transportation.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments for the event organizers to help them understand your needs and expectations.
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter any tools or equipment assigned to you for the event to ensure you have what you need to perform your role.
