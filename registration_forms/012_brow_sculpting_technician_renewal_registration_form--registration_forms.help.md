# Brow Sculpting Technician Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect and validate information from brow sculpting technicians who are renewing their registration. It is essential to complete all required fields to ensure a successful submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select all relevant certifications held from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose the reason for renewal from the provided options.
4. Select the area(s) of interest for training, if applicable.
5. Choose the preferred course type and date.
6. Select the payment method.
7. Enter the renewal registration fee.
8. Confirm the background check status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Technician Full Name** (`technician_name`, text, *): Enter your full name as it appears on your license or ID.
* **Technician License or ID Number** (`technician_id`, text, *): Enter your technician license or ID number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, *): Enter your active email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, *): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Current Salon or Studio Name** (`salon_name`, text, *): Enter the name of the salon or studio where you work.
* **Salon Address** (`salon_address`, text, *): Enter the address of the salon or studio where you work.
* **Years of Experience as Brow Technician** (`years_experience`, number, *): Enter the number of years you have been working as a brow technician.
* **Current Certifications Held** (`current_certification`, select_multiple, *): Select all the certifications you currently hold.
	+ Brow Sculpting Certification
	+ Microblading Certification
	+ Threading Certification
	+ Lamination Certification
	+ Advanced Brow Design
* **Reason for Renewal** (`renewal_reason`, select_one, *): Choose the reason for your renewal from the provided options.
	+ License Expiration
	+ Continuing Education
	+ Skills Update
	+ Career Advancement
* **Training Topics of Interest** (`training_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select the area(s) you want to improve.
	+ Advanced Techniques
	+ Color Theory
	+ Client Consultation
	+ Aftercare Protocols
	+ Safety Standards
* **Which renewal course would you like?** (`renewal_course`, select_one, *): Choose the type of course you prefer.
	+ Basic Renewal - 8 hours
	+ Advanced Renewal - 16 hours
	+ Specialty Track - 12 hours
	+ Online Renewal - Flexible
* **Preferred Course Date** (`course_date`, date, *): Enter your preferred course date.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, *): Choose your preferred payment method.
	+ Credit Card
	+ Check
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ PayPal
* **Renewal Registration Fee** (`renewal_fee`, number, *): Enter the amount due for the renewal registration.
* **Background Check Status** (`background_check`, select_one, *): Confirm the status of your background check.
	+ 'True'
	+ In Progress
	+ Need to Complete
