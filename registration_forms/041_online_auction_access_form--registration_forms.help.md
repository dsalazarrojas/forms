# Online Auction Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information and other necessary details for an online auction. Its primary purpose is to ensure that participants are properly registered and have the necessary information to participate in the auction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide registration information, such as your name and email address.
2. Enter the name of the auction you are participating in.
3. Choose the type of auction (Auction or Non-Auction).
4. Enter your contact phone number and address.
5. Add any additional notes about your participation.
6. Choose the payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
7. Enter the payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`registration_information`, `text`, not required): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional registration information you'd like to share.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Auction Name** (`auction_name`, `text`, not required): Enter the name of the auction you are participating in. Make sure to double-check the auction name to avoid errors.
* **Auction Type** (`auction_type`, `select_one`, not required): This field is used to identify the type of auction. You can select either Auction or Non-Auction as your preference.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, not required): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication related to the auction.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, not required): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, not required): Add any additional notes about your participation in the auction.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, not required): Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional details about your payment, such as the payment amount or payment date.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* Make sure to use a valid email address and phone number.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the auction administrators.
