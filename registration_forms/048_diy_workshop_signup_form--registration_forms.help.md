# DIY Workshop Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for workshop organizers to collect information and registrations for internal workshops and events. It gathers data on attendees' details, workshop specifics, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide the workshop name, date, time, location, and instructor's name.
4. Select the level of interest and choose the topics of interest for the workshop.
5. Enter the registration date and number of workshop participants.
6. If necessary, add any comments or additional information.
7. Choose the type of workshop and select the workshop duration and capacity.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for the workshop registration purpose.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for the workshop registration purpose.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop or event you're attending.
* **Date of Workshop**: Select the date of the workshop or event you're attending.
* **Time of Workshop**: Select the time of the workshop or event you're attending.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop or event.
* **Instructor**: Enter the name of the workshop instructor.
* **Level of Interest**: Select the level of interest for the workshop or event. 
* **Topics of Interest**: Choose the topics you're interested in for the workshop or event. 
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the workshop or event. 
* **Number of Workshop Participants**: Enter the number of people attending the workshop or event. 
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about the workshop or event.
* **Type of Workshop**: Select the type of workshop or event you're attending.
* **Time of Workshop**: Select the time of the workshop or event.
* **Duration of Workshop**: Enter the duration of the workshop or event in hours.
* **Capacity of Workshop**: Enter the maximum capacity of the workshop or event.
* **Submit**: Click this button to complete the form and submit your registration.
