# gym_membership_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gym_membership_extension_form is used to update membership information for individuals or entities within the gym's internal management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth (YYYY-MM-DD) in the Date of Birth field.
4. Enter your Membership ID in the Membership ID field.
5. Select your Membership Type from the available options (Membership/Non-Membership).
6. Select your Renewal Option from the available options (Renew Now, Renew in 1 Month, Renew in 3 Months, Renew in 6 Months).
7. Enter your Payment Details in the Payment Details field (payment method, amount, and payment method details).
8. Enter your Email in the Email field.
9. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
10. Enter the Start Date of your membership extension in the Extension Start Date field.
11. Enter the End Date of your membership extension in the Extension End Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your birthdate in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Membership ID**: Enter your existing membership ID.
* **Membership Type**: Select the type of membership you wish to extend (Membership/Non-Membership).
* **Renewal Option**: Choose when you wish to renew your membership (Renew Now, Renew in 1 Month, Renew in 3 Months, Renew in 6 Months).
* **Payment Details**: Enter payment method, amount, and payment method details.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number.
* **Extension Start Date**: Enter the start date of your membership extension in date format.
* **Extension End Date**: Enter the end date of your membership extension in date format.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- Enter accurate and valid information to avoid delays or errors in processing your membership extension.
