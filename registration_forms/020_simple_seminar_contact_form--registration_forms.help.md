# Simple Seminar Contact Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and inquiries from seminar attendees. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your registration and inquiry are processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Note that this should be a valid email address that you control.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. In the "Message" field, type any questions or comments you have.
6. If applicable, select the type of inquiry you are making by choosing one or both options in the "Inquiry Type" field.
7. Finally, if you have any additional files to attach, select them in the "Additional Files" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address that you control and use to receive notifications and updates.
* **Phone**: Your phone number (optional).
* **Message**: Any questions or comments you have regarding the seminar.
* **Inquiry Type**: Choose one or both options that apply to your inquiry:
	+ **Registration Enquiry**: For questions related to seminar registration.
	+ **General Inquiry**: For any other question or comment.
* **Additional Files**: Select one or more files that you would like to attach to your inquiry (e.g. images, documents).
