# Organizational Change Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and gather information about organizational changes, such as events, sessions, and attendees. It's essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that the event organizers, speakers, and attendees can be correctly notified and prepared for the event.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event details, including the event name, date, and time.
2. Provide information about the session topics, organizers, and attendees.
3. Define the roles and interests of the attendees.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name** (`event_organizer_name`, Text, Optional): Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, Text, Optional): Enter the topic or subject of the session, if applicable.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, Note, Optional): Add any relevant details about the attendees, such as their roles or special requests.
* **Role** (`role`, Select One, Optional): Select the role of the attendee, which can be Event Host, Organizer, or Speaker.
* **Session Interest** (`session_interest`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the session or topic the attendee is interested in.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Attendee Phone** (`attendee_phone`, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the attendee, if applicable.
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the attendee, if applicable.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the event.
## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure that the event is well-organized and attended.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
* Review and submit the form carefully to avoid errors.
