# Gym Membership Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Membership Enrollment Form is a registration form designed for individuals to sign up for a membership at the gym. This form helps the gym management to collect the necessary information to process the membership application and for you to confirm your details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "first-name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last-name" field.
3. Enter a valid email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy format) in the "date-of-birth" field.
6. If you have an emergency contact, provide their name in the "emergency-contact-person" field.
7. Enter their phone number in the "Emergency Phone" field.
8. Enter their email address in the "Emergency Email" field.
9. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the membership-plan field, indicating whether you want to be a member.
10. Select your preferred payment methods (if any) in the "payment-method" field.
11. Enter any additional payment details in the "payment-details" field.
12. Enter the payment date (mm/dd/yyyy format) in the "payment-date" field.
13. Enter the payment amount in the "payment-amount" field.
14. Choose the duration for your gym membership in the "gym-membership-duration" field.
15. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the emergency-notification field.
16. Enter the start date of your membership (mm/dd/yyyy format) in the "membership-start-date" field.
17. Enter the end date of your membership (mm/dd/yyyy format) in the "membership-end-date" field.
18. Enter your billing address in the "billing-address" field.
19. Enter your city in the "city" field.
20. Enter your state in the "state" field.
21. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
22. Enter your country in the "country" field.
23. Enter your Billing Email in the "Billing Email" field.
24. Enter your Billing Phone number in the "Billing Phone" field.
25. If you have an emergency contact, enter their address in the "emergency-address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first-name**: Please enter your first name.
* **last-name**: Enter your last name.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **date-of-birth**: Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **emergency-contact-person**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter their email address.
* **membership-plan**: Select 'Yes' to become a member, 'No' otherwise.
* **payment-method**: Select your preferred payment methods (if any).
* **payment-details**: Enter any additional payment details.
* **payment-date**: Enter the payment date in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **payment-amount**: Enter the payment amount.
* **gym-membership-duration**: Choose the duration for your gym membership.
* **emergency-notification**: Select 'Yes' to receive emergency notifications, 'No' otherwise.
* **membership-start-date**: Enter the start date of your membership in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **membership-end-date**: Enter the end date of your membership in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **billing-address**: Enter your billing address.
* **city**: Enter your city.
* **state**: Enter your state.
* **zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **country**: Enter your country.
* **Billing Email**: Enter your billing email address.
* **Billing Phone**: Enter your billing phone number.
* **emergency-address**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their address.

## Tips
* Please ensure you enter accurate and valid information for all fields.
* Review your form carefully before submission.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the gym management.
