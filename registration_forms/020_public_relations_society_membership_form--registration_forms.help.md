# Public Relations Society Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Relations Society Membership Form is designed to collect membership information from potential members of the Public Relations Society.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing your name and contact details in the "Member Details" section.
2. Select your membership type from the "Membership Type" dropdown menu.
3. Provide any additional information you'd like to share in the "Additional Info" section.
4. Review your form submission carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Member Details
* **Member Details**: This is the first section of the form where you can enter your name and other relevant details about yourself. Please fill out this section completely and accurately.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details**: Here, you can enter your contact information, such as your email address and phone number. This will help the Public Relations Society stay in touch with you.

### Membership Type
* **Membership Type**: Please select your membership type from the dropdown menu:
	+ Public Relations Society Member: Select this option if you are a member of the Public Relations Society.
	+ Public Relations Society Non-Member: Select this option if you are not a member of the Public Relations Society.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional information you'd like to share about yourself. This can include your profession, interests, or other relevant details.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Once you've filled out the form, click the "Submit" button to send your membership information to the Public Relations Society.
