# Retail Store Data Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Store Data Lab Registration Form is used to collect and record data from retail stores. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your data is properly recorded and analyzed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your participant details, including their contact information and date/time of visit.
2. Select the purpose of your visit from the options provided (e.g., Retail data lab, Analytics program, or Other).
3. Add any additional notes or comments about your visit.
4. Enter your store ID and team ID (if applicable).
5. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This field is for providing basic participant information, including any necessary details about the participant.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter the participant's contact information, including phone number and any other relevant details.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Date of Visit** (`date_of_visit`): Enter the date and time of your visit to the store.
* **Time of Visit** (`time_of_visit`): Enter the time of your visit to the store.
* **Name of Visit** (`name_of_visit`): Select the purpose of your visit from the options provided:
	+ **Retail data lab**: Select this option if you're visiting for data collection.
	+ **Analytics program**: Select this option if you're participating in an analytics program.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if your visit is for a different purpose.
* **Notes** (`notes`): Add any additional comments or notes about your visit.
* **Store ID** (`store_id`): Enter the store's ID number.
* **Team ID** (`team_id`): Enter the team's ID number.

## Tips

* Be accurate and thorough when filling out the form to ensure that your data is properly recorded and analyzed.
* Double-check that you're not duplicating any existing entries.
* If you're unsure about any information, ask your supervisor or contact the data lab administrator for guidance.

This guide should help you complete the form with ease and ensure that your data is accurately recorded. If you have any further questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact the data lab administrator.
