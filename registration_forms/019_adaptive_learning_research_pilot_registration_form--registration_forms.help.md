# Adaptive Learning Research Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture participant information for the Adaptive Learning Research Pilot. It will be used by the research team to understand who is participating, what they are comfortable with, and what they are able to commit to the study.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please fill out the form in its entirety, paying close attention to required fields.
- Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth participation experience.
- If you are unable to complete a required field, please contact the research team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: <label> (Participant name, required, text): This is the full name of the participant.
* **Email Address**: <label> (Email address, required, email): This is the participant's email address for study communications.
* **Role in Study**: <label> (Role in study, required, select_one): Please select the role that best describes your position or involvement in the pilot.
* **Years of Experience with Adaptive Learning**: <label> (Years of experience with adaptive learning, optional, number): Please provide an approximate number of years you have experience with adaptive learning.
* **Devices Used for Learning**: <label> (Devices used for learning, optional, select_multiple): Please select all devices you have used for learning.
* **Consent to Participate and Data Use**: <label> (Consent to participate and data use, required, select_one): Please select "I consent" to indicate your agreement to the research terms.
* **Time Commitment Availability**: <label> (Time commitment availability, required, number): Please provide the approximate number of hours per week you are available to participate in the pilot.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: <label> (Accessibility requirements, optional, text): If you have any accessibility requirements, please provide details here.
* **Additional Comments**: <label> (Additional comments, optional, text): If you have any additional comments or concerns, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to review the study's research terms and conditions before completing the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the research team.
* This form is only accessible to the research team and will not be shared publicly.
