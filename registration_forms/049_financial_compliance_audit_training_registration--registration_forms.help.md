# Financial Compliance Audit Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The financial compliance audit training registration form is designed to enable users to register for training sessions that suit their needs and preferences. This form collects essential information for session planning and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your First Name and Last Name.
2.  Enter your Email Address for contact purposes.
3.  Enter your Phone Number for easy contact.
4.  Select your preferred Training Session Date for the training session.
5.  Choose the time slot for the training session that suits you best.
6.  Select up to 2 Session Preferences based on your needs.
7.  Provide up to 2 Session Preferences 2 based on your needs.
8.  Choose up to 3 Additional Info based on your needs.
9.  Select up to 3 Training Locations that you can attend.
10.  Select up to 1 Assigned Tool that you would like to use.
11.  Select up to 1 Option for Submitter.
12.  Select up to 1 Option for Submitted By.
13.  Select up to 1 Option for Submitted To.
14.  Select up to 1 Option for Submitted At.
15.  Select up to 3 Submitter Info based on your needs.
16.  Select up to 3 Submitter Info 2 based on your needs.
17.  Enter your Submitter Phone Number for easy contact.
18.  Enter your Submitter E-mail for easy contact.
19.  Enter your Submitter Address for easy contact.
20.  Provide up to 3 Submitter Additional Comments based on your needs.
