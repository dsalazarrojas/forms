# Hair Texture Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants about their details, consent, and collection information for the Hair Texture Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, required): This section collects your basic details such as name and contact information.
* **Participant Consent** (Participant Consent, text, required): This section asks you to agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop. Please make sure to read and agree to all terms before submitting.
* **Participant Information** (Participant Information, text, optional): This section provides additional information about you as a participant. Feel free to fill out this section if you have any additional information to provide.
* **Collection Details** (Collection Details, text, required): This section asks you to provide information about the hair collection that you want to participate in. Please ensure that you have provided all necessary information before submitting.
* **Submission Information** (Submission Information, text, required): This section provides information about your submission to the workshop. Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields before submitting.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting.
