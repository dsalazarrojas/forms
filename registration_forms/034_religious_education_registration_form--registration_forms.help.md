# Religious Education Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register students for religious education programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the personal details:
	* Fill in your **First name** in the text field.
	* Fill in your **Last name** in the text field.
2. Next, enter your contact information:
	* Enter your **Email** address in the format `username@domain.com`.
	* Enter your **Contact Number** in the format `1234567890`.
3. Choose your preferred **Course**:
	* Select from the options: **English**, **French**, or **Spanish**.
4. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Select your **Time of Registration**:
	* Choose from the options: `morning`, `afternoon`, or `evening`.
6. Specify your **Religion**:
	* Select from the options: **Christian**, **Muslim**, or **Jew**.
7. Add any **Notes** about your registration:
	* Use this field to provide any additional information relevant to your registration.
8. Enter your **Student ID**:
	* This is a unique numerical identifier for you as a student.
9. Enter your **Address**:
	* This is your physical address where you can be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (text): Enter your given name.
* **Last name** (text): Enter your surname.
* **Student Number** (number): Enter your student ID number.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address in the format `username@domain.com`.
* **Contact Number** (text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Course** (select_one): Choose the course that interests you.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Time of Registration** (time): Select the time of day when you registered.
* **Religion** (select_one): Choose your religion.
* **Notes** (note): Provide any additional notes about your registration.
* **Student ID** (number): Enter a unique numerical identifier for you as a student.
* **Address** (text): Enter your physical address.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* Review your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the relevant authorities.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.
