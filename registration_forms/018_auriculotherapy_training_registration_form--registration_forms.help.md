# Auriculotherapy Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for an auriculotherapy training program. It collects personal and professional details from applicants to assess their eligibility and prepare for the training.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before You Begin
* Please make sure to complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (*).

### Completing the Form
1. Enter your details in the designated fields.
2. Choose your preferred options for each field.
3. Review your form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1

* **Registration Date** (1, date, *)
	+ Date you are registering for the training.
* **First Name** (2, text, *)
	+ Your first name.
* **Last Name** (3, text, *)
	+ Your last name.
* **Email Address** (4, email, *)
	+ Your email contact.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, *)
	+ Your contact phone.

### Page 2

* **Current Professional Credential** (6, select_one, *)
	+ Select your current healthcare background:
		+ Acupuncturist
		+ Massage Therapist
		+ Nurse
		+ Physician
		+ Chiropractor
		+ Naturopath
		+ Student
		+ Other Healthcare
		+ No Healthcare Background
* **Years of Healthcare Experience** (7, number, no)
	+ Number of years in healthcare field. (Note: This field is optional and not required for registration.)
* **Prior Auriculotherapy Experience** (8, select_one, *)
	+ Select your prior experience with auriculotherapy:
		+ No Experience
		+ Some Basic Knowledge
		+ Intermediate Experience
		+ Advanced Experience
* **Training Course Level Selected** (9, select_one, *)
	+ Select the training level you are registering for:
		+ Beginner Level 1
		+ Beginner Level 2
		+ Intermediate Level 3
		+ Advanced Level 4
		+ Certification Track

### Page 3

* **Training Start Date** (10, date, *)
	+ When would you like to begin the training.
* **Preferred Training Format** (11, select_one, *)
	+ Choose your preferred method of learning:
		+ In-Person
		+ Online
		+ Hybrid - Mix of Both
		+ Intensive Weekend
		+ Self-Paced
* **Location or Timezone Preference** (12, text, *)
	+ Enter your location or timezone preference.
* **Reason for Attending Training** (13, select_one, *)
	+ Select your motivation for attending the course:
		+ Career Enhancement
		+ Professional Certification
		+ Add to Existing Practice
		+ Personal Interest
		+ Career Change
		+ Referral Client Requirement
* **Career Goals After Training** (14, text, no)
	+ Optional: Describe how you plan to use this training. (Note: This field is not required for registration.)
* **Special Accommodation Needs** (15, text, no)
	+ Optional: Describe any accessibility requirements. (Note: This field is not required for registration.)
* **Learning Style Preference** (16, select_one, no)
	+ Optional: Choose how you prefer to learn:
		+ Hands-On Practice
		+ Lecture and Discussion
		+ Visual Demonstrations
		+ Small Group Work
		+ One-on-One Mentoring

### Page 4

* **Registration Fee Amount** (17, number, *)
	+ Confirm the course fee amount.
* **Payment Method** (18, select_one, *)
	+ Select how you will pay:
		+ Credit Card
		+ Bank Transfer
		+ Check
		+ Payment Plan
		+ Other
* **Interested in Payment Plan** (19, select_one, no)
	+ Optional: Would you like installment payments? (Note: This field is not required for registration.)
* **Emergency Contact Name** (20, text, no)
	+ Optional: Enter the name of your emergency contact. (Note: This field is not required for registration.)
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (21, text, no)
	+ Optional: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact. (Note: This field is not required for registration.)
* **Health Conditions to Note** (22, text, no)
	+ Optional: Enter any health issues relevant to training. (Note: This field is not required for registration.)
* **Agree to Course Terms and Conditions** (23, select_one, *)
	+ Accept the course policies.
