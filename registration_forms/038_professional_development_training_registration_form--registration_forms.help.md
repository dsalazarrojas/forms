# Professional Development Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their professional development training needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the below steps:

1. Fill in the fields according to your preferences and availability.
2. Provide accurate contact information so that we can reach out to you with more details.
3. Select the programs that you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
4. If you have any additional comments or questions, type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Details**: This field is for providing information about the program(s) you are interested in. Please enter the details in the text box.
- **Participant Info**: This field is for providing your basic contact information and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Training Info**: This field is for providing more specific information about the training you are interested in, if any.
- **Contact Info**: This field is for providing accurate contact information so that we can reach out to you with more details.
- **Additional Comments**: This field is for any additional comments or questions you may have.
- **Training Date**: This field is for selecting a date for the training you are interested in. Please use the date picker to choose a date.
- **Training Time**: This field is for selecting a time for the training you are interested in. Please use the time picker to choose a time.
- **Selected Programs**: This field is a dropdown menu where you can select the programs you are interested in.
- **Email**: This field is for providing your email address so that we can reach out to you with more details.
- **Phone**: This field is for providing your phone number so that we can contact you in case we need to.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields marked as required are filled in accordingly.
* You can select multiple programs for each session.
* If you have any issues with filling in the form, contact us at [contact info].
