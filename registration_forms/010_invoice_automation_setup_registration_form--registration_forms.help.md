# Invoice Automation Setup Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for setting up invoice automation registrations. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the User Info section with your personal details.
2.  Enter your business information in the Business Info section.
3.  Provide your contact details in the Contact Info section.
4.  Specify your payment preferences in the Payment Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Info (id: 1, type: text, required: false)**: Enter your personal details to identify yourself as a user.
*   **Business Info (id: 2, type: text, required: false)**: Provide details about your business to help us understand your company.
*   **Contact Info (id: 3, type: text, required: false)**: Enter your contact methods for any questions or updates regarding your invoice automation setup.
*   **Payment Info (id: 4, type: text, required: false)**: Specify your payment preferences for invoice automation services.

## Tips
- Ensure you fill out all fields if you want the registration to be complete.
- Review your form submissions carefully before finalizing the setup.
