# Video Streaming Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for video streaming subscription management, allowing users to input their information and subscription details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **User Information** section (page 1) with your personal details.
	* Enter your **Name** and **Email** (fields 1 and 6) to create or update your subscription.
2. Choose your **Subscription Plan** (field 3) from the available options.
3. Select your **Payment Method** (field 4) from the available options.
4. Fill out the **Billing Address** (field 5) if you're setting up a new subscription.
5. Enter your **Subscription Start Date** and **End Date** (fields 8 and 9) if you're setting up a new subscription.
6. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions** (field 10).
7. Select your **Subscription Status** (field 11) and **Payment Status** (field 12) as required.
8. Enter your **Subscription Plan ID** and **Payment Method ID** (fields 16 and 17) if you're updating an existing subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Please fill out your personal details, including your name and email.
* **Subscription Plan**: Select your desired subscription plan from the options provided (Monthly, Yearly, or One-time).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for your subscription (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or PayPal).
* **Billing Address**: Enter your billing address if you're setting up a new subscription.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive subscription-related notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you need to be contactable for subscription updates.
* **Subscription Start Date**: Select the start date of your subscription.
* **Subscription End Date**: Select the end date of your subscription.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of your subscription.
* **Subscription Status**: Select the status of your subscription (Active, Inactive, or Pending).
* **Payment Status**: Select the payment status of your subscription (Paid, Pending, or Failed).
* **Created By**: This field is auto-populated and displays the user who created the subscription.
* **Updated By**: This field is auto-populated and displays the user who last updated the subscription.
* **Deleted By**: This field is auto-populated and displays the user who deleted the subscription.
* **Subscription Start Date Time**: Select the start date and time of your subscription.
* **Subscription End Date Time**: Select the end date and time of your subscription.
* **Subscription Plan Title**: Enter a title for your subscription plan.
* **Subscription Plan ID**: Enter the ID of your subscription plan.
* **Payment Method ID**: Enter the ID of your payment method.
* **User ID**: This field is auto-populated and displays the ID of the user associated with the subscription.
* **Subscription ID**: This field is auto-populated and displays the ID of the subscription.
* **Created At**: This field is auto-populated and displays the creation date of the subscription.
* **Updated At**: This field is auto-populated and displays the last update date of the subscription.
* **Deleted At**: This field is auto-populated and displays the deletion date of the subscription.

## Tips
Please note that some fields are optional and only required when creating a new subscription. Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure a successful subscription process.
