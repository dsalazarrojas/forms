# Adventure Activity Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to be completed by a parent or guardian to provide informed consent for a child's participation in adventure activities. It includes sections to gather necessary participant information, medical information, and authorization details for the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your child's participant information:
	* Fill in the Participant Full Name field.
	* Enter the Participant Date of Birth in the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
2. Provide Parent or Guardian Information:
	* Fill in the Parent or Guardian Full Name field.
	* List any medical conditions or allergies in the Medical Conditions or Allergies field.
3. Give Permission for Participation:
	* Indicate your permission for your child's participation in the adventure activity in the Permission Granted field.
	* Select 'True' for permission or 'False' if you do not grant permission.
4. Sign and Date the Form:
	* Type your full name in the Signature of Parent or Guardian field.
	* Enter the date of signing in the Date Signed field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter the full name of the child participating in the adventure activity.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the correct date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Enter your full name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies that the child may have.
* **Permission Granted**: Select 'True' to grant permission for your child's participation or 'False' if you do not grant permission.
* **Signature of Parent or Guardian**: Type your full name to signify your agreement to the terms of the form.
* **Date Signed**: Enter the date you signed the form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have read and understood the form before signing.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the activity organizers or administrators.
* Keep a copy of the signed form for your records.
