# Virtual Riding Lesson Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for a virtual riding lesson. If you have any existing registrations for the same riding lesson, they will be marked as duplicates and cannot be submitted. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Decide if you are signing up for the lesson ("Riding Lesson Sign Up") 
2. Enter your name and contact information (Name, Email, and Phone)
3. Choose a preferred date and time for the lesson (Preferred Date and Preferred Time)
4. Add any additional notes about the session (Session Notes)
5. Select the instructor details (Instructor Details, Trainer, School)
6. Sign up for the lesson (Submit the form)

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Riding Lesson Sign Up**: Check the box to indicate if you are signing up for the lesson. 
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted by the instructor or school.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted by the instructor or school.
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date for the lesson you prefer to attend. 
* **Preferred Time**: Select the time for the lesson you prefer to attend.
* **Session Notes**: Add any additional information you would like to share with the instructor or school.
* **Instructor Details**: Enter any additional details about the instructor you are interested in.
* **Trainer**: Enter the name of the trainer you are interested in.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school where the lesson will take place.
* **Signup Date**: Select the date you are signing up for the lesson.
* **Signup Time**: Select the time you are signing up for the lesson.
* **Session Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Instructor Details**: Enter any additional details about the instructor you are interested in.
* **Trainer**: Enter the name of the trainer you are interested in.
* **School**: Enter the name of the school where the lesson will take place.
* **Signup Date**: Select the date you are signing up for the lesson.
* **Signup Time**: Select the time you are signing up for the lesson.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to complete the registration. 

# Tips
* Make sure to select the correct preferred date and time for the lesson. 
* Enter your email and phone number correctly to ensure proper contact information. 
* Enter any additional session notes to help the instructor or school tailor the lesson to your needs.
