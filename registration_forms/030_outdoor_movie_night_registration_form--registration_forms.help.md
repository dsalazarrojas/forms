# Outdoor Movie Night Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration for the Outdoor Movie Night event. Please fill out the required information to help us get to know you and cater to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. If you have a contact phone number, enter it in the Contact Phone field.
4. Choose whether you are an Adult or Youth from the Are you an adult or youth? dropdown list.
5. Let us know if you'll be attending the movie event in the Will you be attending the movie? dropdown list.
6. Select any dietary restrictions you have from the Dietary restrictions dropdown list.
7. Click submit once you've filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with event details.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number if you have one, in case we need to reach you during the event.
* **Are you an adult or youth?**: Choose whether you are an adult or a youth. This will help us cater to your needs during the event.
* **Will you be attending the movie?**: Let us know if you'll be joining us for the movie event. This helps us with event planning.
* **Dietary restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have, such as gluten-free or vegetarian, so we can cater to your needs during the event.
* **Dietary restrictions**: Please select all that apply, but do not select 'None' if you have no restrictions.
* **Submit**: Click submit once you've filled out all the required fields to complete your registration.
