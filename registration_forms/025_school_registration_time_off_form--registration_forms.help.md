# School Registration Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Registration Time Off Form is used to request and manage a student's absence from school. This form is completed by a parent or guardian and requires the school's approval before submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the reason for the student's time off from the available options.
2. Enter the date of absence in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Enter the start and end times of absence in the format of 12-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
4. Provide the duration of absence in hours.
5. Enter the student's name, ID, email, and phone number.
6. Choose whether teacher or admin approval is required.
7. Provide any additional comments regarding the absence.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Request Time Off** (Select One): Choose the reason for the student's time off from the provided options.
	+ Family Event
	+ Personal Event
	+ Funeral
	+ Other
* **Reason for Request** (Text): Briefly describe the reason for the student's absence.
* **Date of Absence** (Date): Enter the date of absence in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Start Time of Absence** (Time): Enter the start time of absence in the format 12-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **End Time of Absence** (Time): Enter the end time of absence in the format 12-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Duration of Absence** (Number): Provide the duration of absence in hours.
* **Start Time of Absence Time** (Time): Enter the start time of absence in the format 12-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **End Time of Absence Time** (Time): Enter the end time of absence in the format 12-hour clock (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments regarding the absence.
* **Parent Name** (Text): Enter the parent's name.
* **Student Name** (Text): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (Number): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the student's email address.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Teacher Approvals** (Text): Enter any teacher's approvals or comments.
* **Admin Approvals** (Text): Enter any admin's approvals or comments.
* **Teacher Approvals Required** (Select One): Choose whether teacher approvals are required.
* **Admin Approvals Required** (Select One): Choose whether admin approvals are required.
* **Approved by Teacher** (Select One): Choose whether the teacher has approved the absence.
* **Approved by Admin** (Select One): Choose whether the admin has approved the absence.
* **Status** (Select One): Choose the status of the absence.
* **Date of Last Update** (Date): The date of the last update.
* **Last Updated by** (Text): The person who made the last update.
* **Created by** (Text): The person who created the record.
* **Date Created** (Date): The date the record was created.
