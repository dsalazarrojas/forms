# Technical Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record attendance information for technical training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you have attended the training session you are registering for.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
4. Select the session date and start time you attended.
5. Check if you attended the session.
6. Enter any comments you have about the session.
7. Enter the session location (if applicable).
8. Enter the name of the trainer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session ID**: Enter the unique ID of the training session you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name**: This is your name as it should be on your identification.
* **Attendee Phone**: Your contact phone number.
* **Session Date**: The date the training session took place.
* **Session Start Time**: The start time of the training session.
* **Attendee E-mail**: Your work or personal email address.
* **Attended**: Check yes or no to indicate if you attended the session.
* **Comments**: Enter any feedback or comments you have about the session.
* **Session Location**: The location of the training session (if applicable).
* **Trainer Name**: The name of the trainer who led the session.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact your administrator.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask your administrator for guidance.
