# Community Podcast Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Podcast Registration Form is for participants to register their podcast for the community event. This form helps us understand your podcast's details and interests, ensuring a successful and enjoyable experience for you and other attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field carefully and accurately. Make sure to provide all required information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name** (Required)
First Name is the name that you would like to be known by.

### 2. **Last Name** (Required)
Last Name is the last name you would like to be known by.

### 3. **Email Address** (Required)
Email Address is where we will send updates and information about the event. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.

### 4. **Phone Number** (Optional)
Phone Number is your contact number for any follow-up questions or invitations.

### 5. **Organization or Affiliation** (Optional)
If you have an organization or affiliation, please provide it here. If not, please leave this field blank.

### 6. **Podcast Information** (Note)
Please provide a brief description of your podcast and its purpose.

### 7. **Podcast Name** (Required)
Podcast Name is the name of your show.

### 8. **Podcast Category** (Required)
Select the category that best describes your podcast from the given options.

### 9. **Podcast Description** (Required)
Please provide a brief description of your podcast.

### 10. **Number of Episodes Published** (Optional)
Please specify the number of episodes published.

### 11. **Podcast Launch Date** (Optional)
Specify the date when you started your podcast.

### 12. **Release Schedule** (Required)
Choose the frequency of your releases from the given options.

### 13. **Average Episode Length** (Required)
Select the approximate length of your episodes.

### 14. **Podcast Format** (Required)
Choose all formats that apply to your podcast.

### 15. **Target Audience** (Required)
Please specify the group of people interested in your podcast.

### 16. **Participation Goals** (Required)
Select all goals you would like to achieve from the options.

### 17. **Recording Equipment** (Optional)
Please specify the equipment you use for recording.

### 18. **Hosting Platform** (Optional)
Specify where your podcast is hosted.

### 19. **Social Media Handles** (Optional)
Provide your social media handles for easier connection.

### 20. **Podcast Website** (Optional)
Provide the URL of your podcast's website, if available.

### 21. **Availability for Recording** (Required)
Choose your availability for recording sessions.

### 22. **Will to Record Remotely** (Required)
Select whether you are willing or not willing to record remotely.

### 23. **Previous Appearances** (Optional)
Please indicate whether you have been on other podcasts.

### 24. **Topics of Interest** (Optional)
Please specify topics you would like to cover on your podcast.

### 25. **Additional Comments** (Optional)
Please share any additional information or thoughts about your podcast.
