# Team Building Activity Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Activity Waiver Form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge and agree to certain risks and conditions before engaging in team building activities. This form is mandatory for all team building activities to ensure that participants are aware of and assume the risks associated with the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully and sign the waiver and emergency contact sections.
3. Make sure to include all necessary information for emergency contacts and team participants.
4. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help or clarification from the activity organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Team Building Activity Waiver Form** (Required)
This is the title of the form. Please ensure that you are filling out the correct form for the activity you are participating in.

### 2. **Activity Name** (Required)
This field is for the name of the team building activity you are participating in. Please enter the official name of the activity as provided by the activity organizers.

### 3. **Risk Acknowledgement** (Required)
This field requires you to acknowledge that you understand and accept the risks associated with the activity. Please sign to confirm that you have read and understood the activity's risks.

### 4. **Emergency Contact** (Required)
Please fill in the name of your emergency contact person. This is the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency during the activity.

### 5. **Phone Number** (Required)
Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person. This should be a valid phone number that can be reached in case of an emergency.

### 6. **Email Address** (Required)
Enter the email address of your emergency contact person. This should be a valid email address that can be reached in case of an emergency.

### 7. **Emergency Phone** (Required)
Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person. This should be a valid phone number that can be reached in case of an emergency.

### 8. **Emergency Email** (Required)
Enter the email address of your emergency contact person. This should be a valid email address that can be reached in case of an emergency.

### 9. **Medical Condition** (Required)
List any medical conditions or health issues you may have. This information will help medical staff and emergency responders take necessary precautions if needed.

### 10. **Medication** (Required)
List any medications you are currently taking or have taken recently. This will help medical staff assess potential risks and take necessary steps if needed.

### 11. **Allergies** (Required)
List any allergies or sensitivities you may have. This will help medical staff and emergency responders take necessary precautions if needed.

### 12. **Doctor Name** (Required)
Enter the name of your doctor. This information will help medical staff assess your medical condition and provide necessary treatment if needed.

### 13. **Doctor Phone** (Required)
Enter the phone number of your doctor. This will help medical staff contact your doctor if necessary.

### 14. **Doctor Email** (Required)
Enter the email address of your doctor. This will help medical staff contact your doctor if necessary.

### 15. **Activity Participants** (Required)
Please select 'Yes' if you have participated in team-building activities before. This information is used to assess team dynamics and activity planning.

### 16. **Participants Count** (Required)
Enter the number of participants in your team. This will help activity organizers plan for the activity.

### 17. **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Required)
Please specify your relationship with the emergency contact person. This will help medical staff and emergency responders contact the right person if needed.

### 18. **Emergency Contact Message** (Required)
Please provide a message or instructions for the emergency contact person. This will help them take necessary actions if you are injured or ill during the activity.

### 19. **Waiver Signature** (Required)
Please sign to confirm that you have read and understood the risks associated with the activity. This is a mandatory waiver for all team building activities.

### 20. **Emergency Contact Signature** (Required)
Please sign to confirm that the emergency contact person has agreed to be contacted in case of an emergency.

### 21. **Participant Signature** (Required)
Please sign to confirm that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the activity.

### 22. **Team Builders** (Required)
Please list the names of your team members participating in the activity. This will help activity organizers and emergency responders keep track of team members.

### 23. **Team Organizers** (Required)
Please list the names of your team organizers or activity organizers. This will help emergency responders and medical staff contact the right people if needed.

### 24. **Activity Organizers** (Required)
Please list the names of the activity organizers. This will help emergency responders and medical staff contact the right people if needed.

### 25. **Additional Comments** (Optional)
Please provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the activity organizers or emergency responders.
