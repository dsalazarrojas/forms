# Peer To Peer Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a Peer To Peer event. It captures essential details about the event and attendees to facilitate smooth execution and communication among participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: role, event_date, event_time, and attendee_consent.
2. Select your role in the event (Leader, Host, or Participant).
3. Enter the event date and time in the respective fields.
4. Confirm that you or your team/organization has given consent to participate.
5. For optional fields, provide as much information as you can.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_name**: Enter a brief description of the event. This will help identify the event in the system. (text, required: false)
* **role**: Select your role in the event, which can be Leader, Host, or Participant. This helps the organizers know your responsibilities and expectations. (select_multiple, required: true)
* **prophets**: Unfortunately, it seems that the field "prophets" is actually a typo and was intended to be "prophet". Please select whether you or your team/organization will be the prophet. (select_one, required: false)
* **attendee_name**: Enter your name or the name of the team/organization you represent. This will help identify you in the system. (text, required: false)
* **attendee_email**: Enter the email address of the person attending the event or the email address of the team/organization. This will help the organizers contact you with event updates and information. (email, required: true)
* **attendee_phone**: Enter the phone number of the person attending the event or the contact number of the team/organization. This can be used for last-minute event updates or coordination. (text, required: false)
* **prophet**: It seems there was another typo, "prophets" is actually "prophet". Select whether you or your team/organization will be the prophet. (select_one, required: false)
* **attendee_consent**: Confirm that you or your team/organization has given consent to participate in the event. This is a required field for event registration. (select_one, required: true)
* **event_date**: Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This will help schedule the event accordingly. (date, required: true)
* **event_time**: Enter the time of the event in the format HH:MM. This will help ensure punctuality and coordination among participants. (time, required: true)
* **venue**: Enter the location of the event. While this is not a required field, providing this information can help with navigation and event organization. (text, required: false)
* **notes**: Provide any additional notes about the event or your role in it. This can be helpful for the organizers and other participants. (note, required: false)
