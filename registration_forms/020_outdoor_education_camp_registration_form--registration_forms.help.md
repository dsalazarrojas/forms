# outdoor_education_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals participating in outdoor education camps. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your personal details and any additional information that might be relevant to your camp experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Camper Information** (`camper_info`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, age, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Additional Camper Information** (`camper_info_repeat`, `text`, required: false): If there is more information about you that you would like to share with the camp staff, please enter it here.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Please provide the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required: false): If you have any medical conditions that the camp staff should be aware of, please describe them briefly.
* **Camp Details** (`camp_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide any other information that you think would be helpful for the camp staff to know about your camp experience.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can contact the camp staff for clarification.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know in the "Camp Details" field.
