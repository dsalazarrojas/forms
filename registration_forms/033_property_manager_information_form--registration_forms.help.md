# Property Manager Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Property Manager Information Form is a tool used to gather crucial information about a property, its current status, and the person or people associated with it. This form is essential for property managers to accurately document and track various aspects of properties under their care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form effectively, follow these steps:

- Start by filling out the required fields (indicated by a red asterisk * or bold text), which include:
  * First Name
  * Last Name
  * Property Status (Available, Renting, or Sold)
  * Property Type (Apartment, House, or Townhouse)
  * Rent Frequency (Bi-monthly, Monthly, or Weekly)
  * Rent Start Date and Rent End Date
  * Completed Date

- Fill out the optional fields, such as Tenant Name, Tenant Phone, Tenant Email, and others that apply to the property's status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the property manager or the person filling out the form.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the property manager or the person filling out the form.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the property manager or the person filling out the form.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email address for the property manager or the person filling out the form.
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the property.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of property (Apartment, House, or Townhouse).
* **Rent Frequency** (`rent_frequency`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the frequency of rent (Bi-monthly, Monthly, or Weekly).
* **Rent Start Date** (`rent_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of rent for the property.
* **Rent End Date** (`rent_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of rent for the property.
* **Move In Date** (`move_in_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the property was moved in.
* **Move Out Date** (`move_out_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the property was moved out.
* **Rent** (`rent`, number, optional): Enter the rent amount for the property.
* **Property Status** (`property_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the current status of the property (Available, Renting, or Sold).
* **Manager Notes** (`manager_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the property management.
* **Tenant Name** (`tenant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the tenant.
* **Tenant Phone** (`tenant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the tenant.
* **Tenant Email** (`tenant_email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email address for the tenant.
* **Property Notes** (`property_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about the property.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about the property.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about the property.
* **Signature** (`signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature or the signature of the person filling out the form.
* **Date Completed** (`date_completed`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (`time_completed`, time, optional): Enter the time when the form was completed.
* **Completed By** (`completed_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who completed the form.
* **Completed Date** (`completed_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the form was completed.

## Tips
- Be precise when filling out the form, and ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
- Use this form as a reference for your property management tasks.
- If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact your property management team.
