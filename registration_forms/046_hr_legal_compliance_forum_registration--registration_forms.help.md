# HR Legal Compliance Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for HR professionals to register for the HR Legal Compliance Forum, a platform for discussing and sharing knowledge on legal compliance topics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Registration Details section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your Event Details in the corresponding section.
3. Enter your Preferences in the third section.
4. Fill out your contact information in the last section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Details** (1, HR Legal Compliance Forum Registration, required): Enter your name and any other relevant registration details.
- **Event Details** (2, Event Details, required): Share any specific event-related details you'd like to attend.
- **Preferences** (3, Preferences, required): Specify your preferences for the forum, such as preferred dates or topics.
- **Contact Info** (4, Contact Info, required): Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you about the forum.
