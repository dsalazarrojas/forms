# First Responder Warrior Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from first responders to provide them with necessary support and resources. It is essential to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that responders receive the help and care they need.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "First Responder Name" field with your name as a first responder.
2. Enter your "Agency Name" and "Address" in the corresponding fields.
3. If you have a phone number, please fill out the "Phone" field. Note that this field is not required.
4. Fill out the "E-mail" field with your personal or work email address.
5. Enter the date and time of your injury using the "Date of Injury" and "Time of Injury" fields, respectively.
6. Provide as much detail as possible about your injury in the "Injury Details" field.
7. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields, respectively.
8. Fill out the "Emergency Contact E-mail" field with your contact's email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Responder Name (Text)**: Enter your name as a first responder.
* **Agency Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the agency or organization you work for.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter your work or home address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number, if applicable. (Note: This field is not required.)
* **E-mail (Email)**: Enter your personal or work email address.
* **Date of Injury (Date)**: Enter the date of your injury.
* **Time of Injury (Time)**: Enter the time of your injury.
* **Injury Details (Text)**: Provide as much detail as possible about your injury.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact E-mail (Email)**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are experiencing difficulty filling out the form, do not hesitate to reach out for assistance.
* Remember to review and correct any errors before submitting the form.
