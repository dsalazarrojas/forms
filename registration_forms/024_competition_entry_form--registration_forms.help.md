# Competition Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from the entrant for the competition management process. It includes fields for contact information, competition details, and entry submission. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth competition experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information section with your name, email, phone number, and mailing address.
2. Select the correct competition category and entry type (individual, team, or duo/pair).
3. Enter the competition date and location.
4. Select the age division and skill level of the primary entrant.
5. Fill out the entry title and description.
6. If applicable, provide information about previous competitions, team members, coach/coach name, and coach contact.
7. Specify how you will pay the entry fee and provide the transaction ID or reference.
8. Agree to the terms and conditions, liability waiver, and media consent.
9. Confirm that you understand the refund policy.
10. Sign the form with your full name and date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Competition Name**: Enter the name of the competition you are entering.
* **Competition Date**: Enter the date when the competition takes place.
* **Competition Location**: Enter the venue or location of the competition.
* **Entry Type**: Select the type of entry (individual, team, or duo/pair).
* **Primary Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the main contact person.
* **Primary Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the main contact person.
* **Primary Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the main contact person.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter the full address of the main contact person.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the birth date of the primary entrant.
* **Age Division**: Select the age category of the primary entrant.
* **Skill Level**: Select the skill level of the primary entrant.
* **Category Entering**: Select the category of the competition.
* **Entry Title or Name**: Enter the name of the entry.
* **Entry Description**: Provide a brief description of the entry.
* **Previous Competitions**: Indicate if this is the first time you compete or if you have competed before.
* **Qualifications or Achievements**: Enter any relevant qualifications or achievements.
* **Team Members**: List all team members if applicable.
* **Coach or Instructor Name**: Enter the name of the coach or instructor if applicable.
* **Coach Contact**: Enter the coach email or phone number if applicable.
* **Special Requirements**: Specify any special requirements or needs.
* **Equipment Requirements**: List any equipment you will need.
* **Music or Media Files**: Indicate if you will submit music or media files and if they will be attached.
* **Entry Fee Payment**: Select how you will pay the entry fee.
* **Payment Confirmation**: Enter the transaction ID or reference if applicable.
* **I Agree to Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the competition rules.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you waive liability claims.
* **Media Consent**: Confirm that you consent to media usage.
* **I Understand Refund Policy**: Confirm that you understand the refund policy.
* **Entrant Signature**: Type your full name as the signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of signing.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
* Review the competition rules and terms before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you understand the refund policy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the competition organizers for clarification.
