# Graphic Design Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect your graphic design subscription preferences, including your client information and project specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Client Information** on page 1.
2. Select your **Subscription Preferences** on page 2.
3. Provide additional details about your project and preferences in the **Project Information** section on page 3.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Client Information
- **Client Name** (`client_info`, text, required): Enter your name and title.
### Subscription Preferences
- **Subscription Preferences** (`subscription_info`, text, required): Describe your design subscription preferences.
### Project Information
- **Email** (`client_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`client_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Notes** (`client_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about your project.
### Project Details
- **Preferred Services** (`preferred_services`, select_multiple, optional): Select the graphic design services you're interested in (Design, Branding, Development, Strategy, Writing).
- **Preferred Format** (`preferred_format`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred format for your project (PDF, Web, Email, In-person, Phone).
- **Preferred Delivery** (`preferred_delivery`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred delivery speed for your project (Instantly, Within a day, Within a week, Within a month, Later).
- **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred contact method (Email, Phone, In-person, Video call, Chat).
- **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): Select the preferred language for your project (English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese).
### Project Status
- **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your project (Active, Pending, Complete, Hold, Canceled).
### Project Assignment
- **Assigned Agent** (`assigned_agent`, select_one, optional): Select the assigned agent for your project (ChatJImmy, Another Agent, No Agent).
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select if a tool is assigned to your project (Yes, No).
- **Assigned Date** (`assigned_date`, date, optional): Enter the assigned date for your project.
- **Assigned Time** (`assigned_time`, time, optional): Enter the assigned time for your project.
- **Due Date** (`due_date`, date, optional): Enter the due date for your project.
- **Due Time** (`due_time`, time, optional): Enter the due time for your project.
### Next Steps
- **Next Steps** (`next_steps`, select_multiple, optional): Select the next steps for your project (Review, Discuss, Design, Develop, Launch).
- **Next Steps Other** (`next_steps_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional next steps for your project.
### Project Address
- **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, optional): Enter your client address.
- **Client Website** (`client_website`, text, optional): Enter your client website.
- **Client Twitter** (`client_twitter`, text, optional): Enter your client Twitter handle.
### Additional Information
- **Project Information** (`project_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional project information.
- **Project Notes** (`project_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional project notes.
- **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional project notes.
