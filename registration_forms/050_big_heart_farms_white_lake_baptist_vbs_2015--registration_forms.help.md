# Big Heart Farms White Lake Baptist Vbs 2015 - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about a child's registration for the Big Heart Farms VBS 2015 program. Please fill out this form to help us understand your child's needs and preferences for a fun and safe experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section of the form, starting from "Child Information" and moving to "Emergency Contact" and "Consent and Agreements".
2. Be sure to fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*) next to the label.
3. Select options for "Grade Level (2015)", "Which Week Can Your Child Attend", "Will Your Child Attend", "Transportation Needed", "Lunch Provided by VBS", and "Permission for Photos or Videos" using the corresponding dropdown menus.
4. Enter text answers for "Child First Name", "Child Last Name", "Parent or Guardian First Name", "Parent or Guardian Last Name", "Street Address", "City", "State", and "ZIP Code".
5. Select multiple interests for "Child's Interests (Select all that apply)".
6. Enter any additional comments or questions in the "Additional Comments or Questions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Child First Name**: Enter the first name of the child registering for VBS.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the last name of the child registering for VBS.
* **Child Age**: Enter the child's age in years.
* **Grade Level (2015)**: Select the grade level your child is currently in for the 2015 school year.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the child's birthdate in the format "Month - Day - Year".
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the first name of the parent or guardian registering the child.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the last name of the parent or guardian registering the child.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the parent or guardian for VBS updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian for contact purposes.
* **Street Address**: Enter the street address of the child's residence.
* **City**: Enter the city where the child's residence is located.
* **State**: Enter the state where the child's residence is located.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter the ZIP code of the child's residence.
* **Which Week Can Your Child Attend**: Select the week(s) your child can attend VBS.
* **Will Your Child Attend**: Select the number of days your child will attend VBS.
* **Transportation Needed**: Select the transportation arrangements needed for your child.
* **Lunch Provided by VBS**: Select whether VBS will provide lunch for your child.
* **Any Food Allergies**: Enter any food allergies we should know about.
* **Medical Conditions or Medications**: Enter any medical conditions or medications your child has that we should know about.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations**: Enter any special needs or accommodations your child has that we should know about.
* **Behavioral or Learning Concerns**: Enter any behavioral or learning concerns we should know about.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person to contact in an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in an emergency.
* **Child's Interests (Select all that apply)**: Select any interests that apply to your child.
* **Permission for Photos or Videos**: Select the permission for taking photos or videos of your child.
* **I Acknowledge Receipt of VBS Information and Waiver**: Select your agreement to the VBS information and waiver.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
- Be sure to check the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) next to the label.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
- We appreciate your cooperation and help in making this year's VBS a fun and safe experience for all children.
