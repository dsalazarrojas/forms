# Home And Garden Show Exhibitor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by vendors to register their company and product information for the Home And Garden Show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the **Registration Details** page.
2. Move on to the **Vendor Info** page to provide your company's details.
3. On the **Booth Planning** page, specify your booth's size and layout.
4. On the **Contact Info** page, enter your contact information.
5. On the **Payment Details** page, enter your payment information.
6. If required, upload any additional documents on the **Upload Documents** page.
7. Review your information on the **Confirm Details** page.
8. Finally, submit your registration on the **Submission** page.
