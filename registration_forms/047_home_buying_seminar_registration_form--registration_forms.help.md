# Home-Buying Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for a home-buying seminar. It collects personal and contact information, language preference, and seminar details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully before filling it out.
2. Enter your personal details such as first name, last name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose your preferred language for the seminar.
4. Select the date and time of the seminar you'd like to attend.
5. If you have any other location preferences, you can specify them in the "Other Location" field.
6. If you're bringing any attendees, enter the number of people in the "Number of Attendees" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
8. If you're a real estate agent, enter your agency name in the "Real Estate Agency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Language** (select multiple, optional): Choose the language you'd like to use during the seminar. Options are English, Spanish, French, and Other.
* **Seminar Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the seminar you'd like to attend.
* **Seminar Time** (time, optional): Select the time of the seminar you'd like to attend.
* **Real Estate Agent** (text, optional): If you're a real estate agent, enter your agency name.
* **Number of Attendees** (number, optional): Enter the number of people attending with you.
* **Preferred Location** (text, optional): Enter the preferred location for the seminar.
* **Other Location** (text, optional): Enter any other location you'd like to attend.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Real Estate Agency** (text, optional): If you're a real estate agent, enter your agency name.
* **Seminar Topic** (text, optional): Not applicable in this form, likely a mistake in the schema.
