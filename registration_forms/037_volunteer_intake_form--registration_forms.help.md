# Volunteer Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential volunteers to help us better understand their availability, skills, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Zip.
2. Provide a valid Date of Birth (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Choose your Volunteer Type (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
4. Select your available Shift (if applicable) and Volunteer Hours (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you about volunteer opportunities.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address if you would like to be contacted or pick up materials for volunteer opportunities.
* **Zip** (Text, Required): Enter your zip code.
* **Volunteer Type** (Select One, Required): Choose how you would like to volunteer (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Volunteer Shift** (Time, Optional): If applicable, select the shift that works best for you.
* **Volunteer Hours** (Number, Optional): If applicable, select the number of hours you are able to volunteer.

Note: If you have any issues or questions while completing this form, please contact us at [contact email/phone number].
