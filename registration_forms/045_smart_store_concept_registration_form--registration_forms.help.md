# Smart Store Concept Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your Smart Store Concept, including retailer and store details, technology interests, and review status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Retailer Information**: Please provide information about your retailer, such as name and contact details.
2. **Store Details**: Provide information about your store, including store type, size, and location.
3. **Smart Store Concept**: Describe your Smart Store Concept, including your idea and any relevant technology interests.
4. **Business Details**: Share more information about your business, including its description and any additional details.
5. **Technology Interests**: Select any technology interests that apply to your Smart Store Concept.
6. **Review Status**: Indicate the current review status of your submission.
7. **Submitted By**: Select the person who submitted the form (Retailer Name or Business Name).
8. **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for follow-up purposes.
9. **Email**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
10. **Store Address**: Select whether your store is an online or physical store.
11. **Store Size**: Select the size of your store (Small, Medium, or Large).
12. **Store Type**: Select whether your store is new or existing.
13. **Store Location**: Select whether your store is located online or physically.
14. **Store Hours**: Enter the store hours of operation.
15. **Store Description**: Describe your store and its offerings.
16. **Smart Store Idea**: Describe your Smart Store Idea.
17. **Business Description**: Describe your business and its offerings.
18. **Technology Ideas**: Enter any technology ideas related to your Smart Store Concept.
19. **Technology Interests**: Select any additional technology interests that apply to your Smart Store Concept.
20. **Review Comments**: Enter any comments related to the review process.
21. **Review Date**: Enter the date of the last review.
22. **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
23. **Reviewed By**: Select the person who reviewed your submission.
24. **Store Image**: Upload an image of your store.
25. **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information that's relevant to your Smart Store Concept.
