# Community Learning Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for educational institutions or organizations to register with the Community Learning Partnership. It gathers necessary information to facilitate partnerships, collaborations, and resource sharing within the partnership.

## How To Complete This Form
To register with the Community Learning Partnership, follow the steps below:

- Please ensure you have not previously registered with the partnership before starting the registration process.
- Carefully fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to provide details about your partnership goals, resources, and the number of participants you can support.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, required): Enter your organization's or school's full name for registration purposes.
- **Organization** (organization, text, optional): Provide your organization's or school's name if applicable.
- **Contact Email** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email for partnership coordination and communication.
- **Programs of Interest** (program_interest, select_multiple, required): Select all the programs you are interested in participating with (e.g., Tutoring, Mentoring, Curriculum development, Workshops).
- **Available Times** (available_times, text, optional): Indicate the days or times you are available to partner with.
- **Number of Participants** (number_of_participants, number, optional): If offering classes or workshops, enter the number of participants you can support.
- **Resources Offered** (resources_offered, text, optional): Provide details about the resources you can offer (e.g., space, materials, instructors, or funding).
- **Partnership Goals** (partnership_goals, text, required): Briefly describe your expected outcomes and goals for the partnership.
- **Consent to Share** (consent_to_share, select_one, required): Agree to share your contact information with partners for collaboration purposes.

Note: If you're unsure about any field or have previously registered with the partnership, please contact the form administrators for assistance.
