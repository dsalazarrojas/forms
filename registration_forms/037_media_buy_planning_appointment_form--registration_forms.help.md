# Media Buy Planning Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Media Buy Planning Appointment Form is used to schedule a meeting with a client to discuss and finalize details for a media buying process. This form helps our team gather essential contact and meeting information from clients to ensure a smooth and efficient meeting arrangement.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name.
2. Fill in your last name.
3. Enter a valid email address.
4. Enter a valid phone number.
5. Enter your company name.
6. Select your role from the provided options (CEO, Marketing Team, Media Buyer).
7. Enter the name of the media buyer responsible for your account.
8. Select a meeting date.
9. Select a meeting time.
10. Enter any notes or comments about the meeting.
11. Enter the budget for the media buy.
12. Enter the meeting location.
13. Enter any additional information about the meeting.
14. Enter your confirmed email address.
15. Enter your confirmed phone number.
16. Enter meeting details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's surname.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: false): Enter the client's company name.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required: true): Select the client's role (CEO, Marketing Team, Media Buyer).
* **Media Buyer** (`media_buyer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the media buyer.
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a meeting date.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required: false): Select a meeting time.
* **Meeting Note** (`meeting_note`, `note`, required: false): Enter any notes or comments about the meeting.
* **Budget** (`budget`, `text`, required: false): Enter the budget for the media buy.
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the meeting location.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the meeting.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the confirmed email address.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the confirmed phone number.
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter meeting details.
