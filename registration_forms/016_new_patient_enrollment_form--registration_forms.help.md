# New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from new patients, as well as their medical history. The form is intended to be completed by individuals who are seeking medical services or treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. If you need to make any corrections or changes, please inform the support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`personal_information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ This is the first page of the form where you will provide your personal details such as your name, date of birth, and other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, `text`, required: false):
	+ On this page, you will provide your contact details such as your address, phone number, and email.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Please provide the contact information of an emergency contact person in case we need to reach out to them.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, `text`, required: false):
	+ In this page, you will provide your medical history, including any previous medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are currently taking.

## Tips
* Please take your time to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact our support team.
* Once you have completed the form, please review it carefully before submitting it to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.
