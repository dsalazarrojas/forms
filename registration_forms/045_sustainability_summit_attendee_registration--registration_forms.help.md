# Sustainability Summit Attendee Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Summit Attendee Registration form is used to gather information from attendees about their interests, dietary needs, and policy consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Select all the activities you are interested in attending during the summit. Please mark all that apply.
2. Indicate your dietary needs by selecting the relevant option.
3. Read and agree to the policy consent statement.
4. Enter your contact details (optional).
5. Select 'Yes' to submit and register for the summit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Activity Interests** (required): This field allows you to select all the activities you are interested in attending during the summit. Please mark all that apply.
* **Dietary Needs** (required): This field allows you to indicate your dietary needs, such as vegetarian or gluten-free. Select the relevant option that best describes your needs.
* **Policy Consent** (required): This is a legal consent statement that we require you to read and agree to before proceeding with your registration. Please take a moment to review it carefully before submitting your form.
* **Contact Details** (optional): If you wish to be contacted about any further instructions or updates about the summit, please enter your contact details here.
* **Submit and Register** (optional): If you are ready to submit your form and register for the summit, select 'Yes' here. If you are not ready, please select 'No'.
