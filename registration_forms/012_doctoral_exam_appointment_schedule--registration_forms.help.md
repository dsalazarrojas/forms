# Doctoral Exam Appointment Schedule - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctoral Exam Appointment Schedule form is used to schedule a doctoral examination with your faculty member. Please provide your information, select a suitable exam time slot, and confirm your preferred time slot.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your student information in the **Student Information** field.
2. Provide exam information in the **Exam Information** field.
3. Enter your faculty information in the **Faculty Information** field.
4. Select a suitable exam time slot from the **Select Exam Time** field.
5. Confirm your preferred exam time in the **Confirm Exam Time** field.
6. Select your preferred exam time slot from the **Preferred Time** field.
7. Confirm your preferred time in the **Confirm Preferred Time** field.
8. Add any additional notes or messages in the **Note** field (optional).
9. Click **Submit** to complete and confirm your schedule.
