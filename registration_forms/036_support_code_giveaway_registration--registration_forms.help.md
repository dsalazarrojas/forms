# Support Code Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Support Code Giveaway Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a support code giveaway program. This form helps us understand your participation requirements and provides a unique identifier for each registrant.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field to ensure that you have entered the correct data.
3. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`id: 1`): This is the initial registration page where you can provide your basic information.
* **Participant Information** (`id: 2`): Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Entry Details** (`id: 3`): Describe the entry you are interested in participating with.
* **Support Code Details** (`id: 4`): If you already have a support code, enter its details here.
* **Review Participant** (`id: 5`): You will be required to review and acknowledge your information for accuracy and completion.
* **Tracking and Logging** (`id: 6`): Enter any relevant tracking or logging information related to your entry.
* **Form Submission** (`id: 7`): Review and confirm the accuracy of your form submission before proceeding.
* **Support Code Giveaway Confirmation** (`id: 8`): This is the final confirmation page where you will receive a unique identifier for your entry.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
