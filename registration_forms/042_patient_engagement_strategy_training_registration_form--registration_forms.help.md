# Patient Engagement Strategy Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of registering for the Patient Engagement Strategy Training. The form captures relevant details such as the user's first and last name, email, and department. The form also includes fields for date-related information, such as date of birth, training date, and submit date, as well as a field for phone number. The form is used to track user registration and status for the training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Start by entering your **First Name** in the field labeled "First Name". This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field labeled "Last Name". This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the field labeled "Email". This is a required field.
4. Enter your **Date Of Birth** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This field is required.
5. Select your **Training Date** from the calendar picker. This field is not required.
6. Enter your **Phone** number in the format of XXX-XXX-XXXX. This field is not required.
7. Enter your **Department** in the field labeled "Department". This field is not required.
8. Enter your **Role** in the field labeled "Role". This field is not required.
9. Select your **Training Status** from the options: Active, Pending, Cancelled. This field is not required.
10. Enter your **Location** in the field labeled "Location". This field is not required.
11. Enter any additional **Notes** about your registration in the field labeled "Notes". This field is not required.
12. Select the **Submitted By** from the options: Jimmy, John, Jane. This field is not required.
13. Enter your **Submit Date** in the format of MM/DD/YYYY. This field is not required.
14. Enter your **Submit Time** in the 24-hour format. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Training Date**: Select a date you are available for the training from the calendar picker.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Department**: Enter your department name (e.g., Marketing, Sales, Finance).
* **Role**: Enter your job title (e.g., Marketing Manager, Sales Representative).
* **Training Status**: Select your training status: Active, Pending, Cancelled.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your office.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments about your registration.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form from the options: Jimmy, John, Jane.
* **Submit Date**: Enter the date you submitted the form in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Submit Time**: Enter the time you submitted the form in 24-hour format.
