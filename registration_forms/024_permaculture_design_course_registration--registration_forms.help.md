# Permaculture Design Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Permaculture Design Course Registration form serves as a central hub for capturing essential data for the course administration and participant management. The form captures vital information for registration, payment, and participant status.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your details in the **Registration** page.
2. Choose your payment method if you're paying for the course.
3. Fill out your name, email, and phone number accurately.
4. Provide information about yourself as a participant.
5. Enter course details, name, and training location.
6. Select relevant dates for registration and payment.
7. Confirm payment receipt.
8. Enter payment amount and method.
9. Choose your participant status.
10. Optionally, provide additional information about your participant status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration**: Enter your name for the course registration.
* **Payment Method**: Select whether you've paid for the course (Yes/No).
* **Name**: Enter your full name accurately.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Participant**: Briefly describe yourself as a participant.
* **Participant Number**: Enter your participant number (if any).
* **Participant Info**: Provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Course Details**: Enter the details of the course you're enrolling in.
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course.
* **Training Location**: Enter the location where the course is being held.
* **Registration Date**: Select the date of your registration.
* **Payment Date**: Select the date of payment receipt.
* **Payment Received**: Confirm that you've received payment for the course.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you've made payment (Yes/No).
* **Participant Status**: Choose whether you're Active or Inactive.
* **Participant Status Other Info 1-7**: Optionally, provide more information about your participant status.
