# Business Expo Mentor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about potential mentors for the Business Expo, a networking event for business professionals. We are looking for individuals with relevant experience and expertise to provide guidance and support to entrepreneurs and small business owners. 

Please complete this form to express your interest in being a mentor at the Business Expo. 

**Please note that you will be contacted after submitting this form for further evaluation.**

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form in a single session.
2. Make sure to answer all required questions.
3. Select all applicable options for select type questions.
4. Use clear and concise language when providing text answers.
5. If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Provide your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Current Job Title** (`current_title`, text, required): Describe your current job title.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Provide your current employer's name.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry where you have experience.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_of_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been working.
* **Areas of Expertise** (`expertise_areas`, text, required): Describe your areas of expertise.
* **Previous Mentoring Experience** (`mentoring_experience`, select_one, required): Select your level of mentoring experience.
* **Mentoring Approach** (`mentoring_approach`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your mentoring approach.
* **Available Dates for Expo Mentoring** (`expo_dates`, select_multiple, required): Select the dates you are available to mentor.
* **Time Commitment per Day** (`time_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose the number of hours you can commit to mentoring per day.
* **Ideal Mentee Profile** (`ideal_mentee`, text, optional): Describe the type of entrepreneurs or businesses you are interested in mentoring.
* **Specific Focus Areas** (`focus_areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas you are interested in focusing on during mentoring.
* **Preferred Startup Stage** (`stage_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose the stage of business you prefer to mentor.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional): Select if you require any accessibility accommodations.
* **Accessibility Details** (`accessibility_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about your accessibility needs.

Please note that some fields are not required, but it's still beneficial to fill them out for our evaluation purposes.
