# Fitness Gym Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register or update a member's information, including contact details, and select their membership plan for access to the fitness gym facilities and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is where we will contact you with important updates and reminders.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is the number we will use to contact you in case of emergencies.
5. Select your preferred membership plan from the "Membership Plan" dropdown menu.
6. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their first name in the "Emergency Contact First Name" field.
7. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their last name in the "Emergency Contact Last Name" field.
8. If you have an emergency contact, please provide their phone number in the "Emergency Phone" field.
