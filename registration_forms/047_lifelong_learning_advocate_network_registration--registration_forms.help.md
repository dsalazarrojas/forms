# Lifelong Learning Advocate Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register as a member of the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network. This network connects professionals and organizations passionate about lifelong learning, facilitating collaboration, and knowledge sharing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration details on page 1.
2. Select your organization on page 1.
3. Provide your contact details on page 2.
4. Choose your interests on page 3.
5. Specify your motivations for joining the network on page 4.
6. Review and submit your application on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (Page 1)
  • **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other contact information you'd like to share with the network team.
* **Organization** (Page 1)
  • **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you're representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Contact Details** (Page 2)
  • **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your email address, phone number, or other contact information to help the network team get in touch with you.
* **Interests** (Page 3)
  • **Interests** (`interests`, select multiple, optional): Choose all the areas related to lifelong learning that interest you, such as education, training, or research.
* **Motivations** (Page 4)
  • **Motivations** (`motivations`, select multiple, optional): Select all the reasons why you want to join the Lifelong Learning Advocate Network, such as to connect with others or contribute to the field.
* **Submit** (Page 5)
  • **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click to submit your registration application.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, especially if you're registering with an organization. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact the network team for guidance.
