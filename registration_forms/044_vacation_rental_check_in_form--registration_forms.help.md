# Vacation Rental Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vacation Rental Check In Form is used to collect information from guests checking into a vacation rental property managed by a hospitality company. This form is essential for ensuring that guests have a smooth and enjoyable stay by providing necessary details such as name, contact information, and arrival plans.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Vacation Rental Check In Form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field and your legal name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is where we will contact you with any important updates or information related to your stay.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is the number we will use to reach you if we need to communicate with you about your stay.
4.  Select the date of your arrival in the "Date of Arrival" field. Please ensure it is accurate so we can prepare for your stay.
5.  Choose your arrival time in the "Arrival Time" field. This will help us coordinate your check-in process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we will contact you with updates and information about your stay.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
*   **Date of Arrival** (`date_of_arrival`, date, required): Select the date of your arrival to ensure we prepare for your stay.
*   **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, required): Choose your expected arrival time to coordinate the check-in process.
