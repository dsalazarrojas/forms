# Lifeguard Certification Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lifeguard Certification Course Enrollment Form is used to collect information from participants of the course. It helps us to gather demographic, contact, and payment information, and any additional notes about the participant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in your **Participant Name**, **Participant Email**, **Participant Phone**, and **Participant Address** fields.
2. Next, select your preferred **Payment Method** (Check or Credit Card) and enter the corresponding **Payment Amount**.
3. If you have an Emergency Contact, provide their **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone**.
4. Add any relevant **Participant Notes** and **Emergency Contact Notes**.
5. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email**: Your active email address where you can be contacted.
* **Participant Phone**: Your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Participant Address**: Your current residential or physical address.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you would like to pay for the course (Check or Credit Card).
*   **Payment Amount**: Enter the total payment amount for the course. Please note that this field is not required, but it will be necessary to complete the payment process.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: If you have an emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any relevant information about yourself or any specific needs you have for the course.
* **Emergency Contact Notes**: Add any notes about your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that you can be contacted and reached for the course.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Please review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
