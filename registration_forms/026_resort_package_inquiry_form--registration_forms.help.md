# resort_package_inquiry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users about resort packages. The form has three sections: Package Information, Inquiry Details, and Additional Details. Fill out this form to provide your contact information, details about your inquiry, and any additional information you'd like to share.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Package Information section with your contact details.
2. In the Inquiry Details section, provide more information about your package inquiry.
3. In the Additional Details section, select all that apply from the list of options.
4. Review your answers and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Package Information** (Text)
* **Package Information** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact details such as name and email.
### **Inquiry Details** (Text)
* **Inquiry Details** (`<name>`, `text`, required/optional): Provide more information about your inquiry.
### **Additional Details** (Select Multiple)
* **Additional Details** (`<name>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select all that apply from the list of options to provide additional information about your inquiry.
  * **Option 1, Option 2, Option 3**: Choose all that apply.
