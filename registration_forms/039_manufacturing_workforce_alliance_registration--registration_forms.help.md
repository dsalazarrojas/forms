# manufacturing_workforce_alliance_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering partners and affiliates in a manufacturing workforce alliance. It collects information about each partner, including contact details, company information, interests, and sign-up status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your partner's name in the "Partner Name" field.
2. Enter your contact's name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
5. Enter your role in the "Role" field.
6. Select your interest areas from the "Interest Areas" dropdown menu. You can choose one or more areas of interest.
7. Select your sign-up status from the "Sign-ups" dropdown menu.
8. Enter any additional contact details in the "Contact Details" note field.
9. Add any additional notes about the registration in the "Notes" note field.
10. Finally, enter the date of the registration in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your partner organization.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of your contact person.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required/optional): Enter your role in the partner organization.
* **Interest Areas** (`interest_areas`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more areas of interest for your partner organization.
	+ Industry: Select this option if your partner organization is interested in industry-related activities.
	+ Workforce Program: Select this option if your partner organization is interested in workforce programs.
	+ Industry Group: Select this option if your partner organization is interested in industry group activities.
* **Sign-ups** (`sign_ups`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your partner organization's sign-up status.
	+ True: Select this option if your partner organization is signed up.
	+ False: Select this option if your partner organization is not signed up.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional contact details for your organization.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about the registration.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the registration.
