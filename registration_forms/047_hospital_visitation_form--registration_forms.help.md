# Hospital Visitation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect visitor information and reasons for visiting a hospital. It's used by hospital staff to manage visitor flow and ensure that visitors have the necessary permissions to access certain areas of the hospital.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the General Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your Visitor Information, including your contact details.
3. Select the names of people you are visiting (e.g. "John, Mary, David") from the Allowed To Visit list.
4. Briefly describe the purpose of your visit in the Visit Reason field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information** (General Information, text, required: false): This section is where you can enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Visitor Information** (Visitor Information, text, required: false): Enter your contact information so that we can reach you if needed.
* **Allowed To Visit** (Allowed To Visit, select_multiple, required: false): Select the names of people you are visiting from the list. Note that you can select multiple names.
* **Visit Reason** (Visit Reason, text, required: false): Briefly describe the purpose of your visit, such as "To visit a patient" or "To attend a meeting".

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that you have the necessary permissions to access the hospital's facilities.
* If you're unsure about any part of the form, please ask a hospital staff member for assistance.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
