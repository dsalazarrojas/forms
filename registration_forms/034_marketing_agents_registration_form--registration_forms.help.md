# Marketing Agents Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for marketing agents to register and provide their contact information and agency details. It is essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to facilitate smooth communication and collaboration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "First Page" section.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Click on the "Second Page" section.
7. Enter your agency's name in the "Agency Name" field.
8. Enter your agency's address in the "Agency Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, including the area code and extension if applicable.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, optional): Enter your agency's name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, optional): Enter your agency's address, including street, city, and state.
* **Second Page** (`marketing_agents_registration_form_page_2`, text, optional): This is a placeholder page that serves as a divider between the two pages of the form.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the form correctly.
