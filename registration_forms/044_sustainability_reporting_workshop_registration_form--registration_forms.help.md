# Sustainability Reporting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Reporting Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect participant information for the Sustainability Reporting Workshop. This form is used to gather essential details such as contact information, company, department, and workshop experience to facilitate a smooth and effective participation in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2.  Provide accurate and relevant information for each field.
3.  Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (participant_info)**: This field is not currently used for this form.
* **Name (name)**: Please fill in your full name as it should be displayed on the workshop materials and in the workshop list of participants.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your official company email address. This will be the primary contact channel for workshop updates and communications.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your company's phone number. This will be used as a secondary contact method in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **Company (company)**: Enter your company name where you work.
* **Position (position)**: Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Role (role)**: Enter your role in the sustainability reporting team or department.
* **Department (department)**: Enter the name of the department you belong to within your company.
* **Workshop Experience (workshop_experience)**: Describe your experience with the Sustainability Reporting Workshop, if any.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)**: Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person. This person will be contacted in case of an emergency or unexpected event during the workshop.
