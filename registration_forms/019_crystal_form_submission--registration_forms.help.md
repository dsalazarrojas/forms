# crystal_form_submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic contact information from individuals or companies submitting their projects or ideas. It is not affiliated with any form platform and its content is derived from public survey topic metadata.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your First Name**: Type your first name in this field.
2. **Enter your Last Name**: Type your last name in this field.
3. **Enter your Email**: Type your email address in this field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. **Enter your Company (optional)**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of your company here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
5. **Enter your Department (optional)**: If you are representing a department within a company, enter the name of your department here. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: <First Name> is the field to enter your first name. Enter your first name here and we will use it for further communication.
* **Last Name**: <Last Name> is the field to enter your last name. Enter your last name here and we will use it for further communication.
* **Email**: <Email> is the field to enter your email address. Please ensure it is a valid email address as we will use it for further communication.
* **Company (optional)**: <Company> is an optional field to enter the name of your company if you are representing one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Department (optional)**: <Department> is an optional field to enter the name of your department within a company if you are representing a department. If not, you can leave this field blank.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely to ensure smooth communication and processing of your submission.
* If you have any issues or concerns with the form, please contact <form@gic.mx>.
