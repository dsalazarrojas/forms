# Fishing Industry Licensing Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fishing Industry Licensing Application Form is designed to gather essential information from businesses operating in the fishing industry, such as commercial fishing vessels, seafood processors, and wholesalers, for the purpose of obtaining a fishing industry license.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please ensure that you are eligible to complete this form. If you're not sure, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
2. Fill in your Business Name as it appears on official records.
3. Select one or more industry types that your business operates in (Commercial Fishing Vessel, Recreational Fishing Boat, Seafood Processor, Wholesale Seafood Distributor, or Fishing Charters and Guides).
4. Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your business.
5. Provide your business's Address.
6. Enter the city where your business is located.
7. Enter the state where your business is located.
8. Enter your business's zip code.
9. Enter your business's Phone Number.
10. Enter your business's Email Address.
11. If you're submitting the application, select the Application Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business as it appears on official records. Please ensure it matches exactly.
* **Industry Type** (`industry_type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more industry types that your business operates in. This will help the relevant authorities categorize and process your application. You can select multiple types.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your business. If left blank, the application will be processed under the business's name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your business's physical address. If left blank, the application will be processed under the business's name.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state where your business is located.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your business's zip code.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your business's phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your business's email address. This will aid in communication and verification processes.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): If you're submitting the application, select the date you're applying for the fishing industry license.
