# grow_children_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from parents or guardians to register their children for school activities or programs. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Student" field with the name of your child.
2. Choose the correct class for your child from the dropdown options.
3. You may provide additional notes for your child's class in the "Class Details" field, if required.
4. Enter any relevant notes or comments regarding your child's involvement in the "Parents Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student
 * Student (text, text, optional): Enter your child's name.
### Class
 * Class (select_one, required): Select the correct class your child is enrolled in.
### Class Details
 * Class Details (note, optional): Provide any additional details about your child's class or activities.
### Parents Note
 * Parents Note (note, optional): Enter any notes or comments regarding your child's registration.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* You may add any additional comments or information in the "Parents Note" field if necessary.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
