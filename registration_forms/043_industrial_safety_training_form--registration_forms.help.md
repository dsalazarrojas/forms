# industrial_safety_training_form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The industrial safety training form is designed to collect essential information about industrial safety training, ensuring the well-being and safety of workers in industrial settings.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out the form accurately and completely, as it will help to gather all relevant information.
2.  Start by selecting the "Training course" from the list of options provided. If your answer is "Yes", proceed to the next step. If your answer is "No", you can skip to the participant information section.
3.  In the "Participant information" section, you can add any additional information about the participant, if needed.
4.  Select the "Safety training" date, if applicable.
5.  Enter the "Training hours" spent on the training course.
6.  Choose the "Training date" and "Training time" for the training course.
7.  Select the "Training status" of the course (Active or Inactive).
8.  Indicate whether the training course took place at a "Training facility" (Yes or No).
9.  Confirm if the "Training venue" was used during the course (Yes or No).
10. Choose the "Training topic" of the course (Yes or No).
11. Select the "Training facility details" (Yes or No).
12. In the "Training notes" section, add any additional information about the training course.
13. Select the "Participant status" (Active or Inactive).
14. Indicate the "Participant role" (Yes or No).
15. Fill out the "Participant name" in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Training course** (Yes / No)
*   Select Yes or No to indicate if the training course was attended.
*   If Yes, proceed to the next field.

### 2. **Participant information** (Note)
*   Add any additional information about the participant, if needed.

### 3. **Safety training** (Date)
*   Enter the date of the safety training.

### 4. **Training hours** (Number)
*   Enter the number of hours spent on the training course.

### 5. **Training date** (Date)
*   Choose the date of the training course.

### 6. **Training time** (Time)
*   Select the time of the training course.

### 7. **Training status** (Active / Inactive)
*   Select the status of the training course.

### 8. **Training facility** (Yes / No)
*   Choose Yes if the training course took place at a specific facility, No otherwise.

### 9. **Training venue** (Yes / No)
*   Indicate if the training course used a specific venue (Yes or No).

### 10. **Training topic** (Yes / No)
*   Select Yes if the training course was on a specific topic, No otherwise.

### 11. **Training facility details** (Yes / No)
*   Choose Yes if the training course had specific details, No otherwise.

### 12. **Training notes** (Note)
*   Add any additional information about the training course.

### 13. **Participant status** (Active / Inactive)
*   Select the status of the participant.

### 14. **Participant role** (Yes / No)
*   Indicate if the participant has a specific role.

### 15. **Participant name** (Text)
*   Enter the name of the participant.
