# Esports Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for teams and players to register for an esports tournament. It gathers essential information about the team and players, such as team details, contact information, and game-specific data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Team Details** section with your team's name, and provide a brief description of your team.
2. In the **Contact Info** section, enter your team's contact person's name, email, and phone number.
3. In the **Team Members** section, select whether your team has members who are attending the tournament.
4. In the **Team Experience** section, describe your team's experience in the game being played.
5. Fill out the **Game Specifics** section with the game's name, version, and any other relevant details.
6. In the **Tournament Details** section, select the date and time of the tournament.
7. You can optionally add any additional details about your team, such as prize pool and entry fee, but these are not required.
8. Finally, fill out any additional **Player Information** and **Team Notes** sections with any relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name as it will be displayed in the tournament bracket.
* **Contact Info (Name)**: Enter your team's contact person's name.
* **Contact Info (Email)**: Enter your team's contact person's email address.
* **Contact Info (Phone)**: Enter your team's contact person's phone number.
* **Team Members**: Select 'Yes' if your team has members attending the tournament, 'No' otherwise.
* **Team Experience**: Describe your team's experience in the game being played.
* **Game Name**: Enter the name of the game being played.
* **Game Version**: Enter the version of the game being played.
* **Tournament Date**: Select the date of the tournament.
* **Tournament Time**: Select the time of the tournament.
* **Prize Pool**: If applicable, enter the prize pool for the tournament.
* **Entry Fee**: If applicable, enter the entry fee for the tournament.
* **Payment Method**: If applicable, select the payment method used for entry fees.
* **Player Name**: Enter the name of each player on your team.
* **Player Email**: Enter the email address of each player on your team.
* **Player Phone**: Enter the phone number of each player on your team.
* **Player Address**: Enter the address of each player on your team.
* **Team Logo**: Optionally, upload a logo or image of your team.
* **Event Host**: Optionally, enter the name of the event host.
* **Location**: Optionally, enter the location of the tournament.
* **Rules**: Optionally, enter any specific rules or notes about your team.
