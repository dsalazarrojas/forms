# Youth Online Challenge Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Youth Online Challenge Registration form is used to collect information about participants in a youth-focused online challenge or event. This form helps us understand the participant's details, preferences, and emergency contact information. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your information. Remember to check the help text for each field to learn more about its purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the participant.
* **Participant Name** (participant_name, text, optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Age** (age, number, optional): Enter the participant's age.
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter the participant's email address. Please use a valid email address that you control.
* **Parent Consent** (parent_consent, text, optional): Check this box to confirm that a parent or guardian has given consent for the participant's participation.
* **Participant Address** (participant_address, text, optional): Enter the participant's home address.
* **Program Type** (program_type, select_one, optional): Select the type of program the participant is participating in (Virtual Program, Contest, Event).
* **Participant Gender** (participant_gender, select_multiple, optional): Select the participant's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Date of Birth** (participant_date_of_birth, date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Parent Name** (parent_name, text, optional): Enter the parent's name (if applicable).
* **Parent Address** (parent_address, text, optional): Enter the parent's home address (if applicable).
* **Parent Contact** (parent_contact, text, optional): Enter a contact method for the parent or guardian (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Guardian Consent** (guardian_consent, text, optional): Check this box to confirm that a guardian has given consent for the participant's participation.
* **Emergency Contact** (participant_emergency_contact, text, optional): Enter a contact person's name and phone number in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Phone** (participant_phone, text, optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Emergency Phone** (participant_emergency_phone, text, optional): Enter an emergency contact person's phone number.

Tips: Double-check your information before submitting the form, and please use a valid email address. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
