# Customer Loyalty Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Customer Loyalty Portal Access Form is designed to collect registration information from customers to provide them access to our loyalty portal. This form helps us verify customer identity and prevent duplicate registrations.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter accurate and valid information in each field.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`customer_first_name`, text, *): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`customer_last_name`, text, *): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your customer email address. This will be used for communication and to verify your identity.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Customer Portal Access** (`customer_portal_access`, select_one, *): Select "Yes" to grant access to the loyalty portal.
* **Registered By** (`registered_by`, number, *): Enter the User ID of the person registering you.
* **Customer Comments** (`customer_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your registration.
* **Company Name** (`customer_company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact our support team for assistance.
