# Fitness Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Challenge Registration Form is used to sign up for a fitness challenge. Please fill out this form with your details to register for the challenge that suits you best.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with your relevant information.
2. Select your desired challenge type from the challenge select fields.
3. Confirm your email and phone number to secure your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates about the challenge.
* **Date**: Not currently used, please leave blank.
* **Challenge Name**: Enter the name of the challenge you're interested in participating in.
* **Challenge Goals**: Enter your personal goals for participating in the challenge.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number to receive important updates about the challenge.
* **Challenge Select**: Select the specific challenge type you'd like to participate in (e.g. running, weightlifting, etc.). 
* **Email Confirmation**: Confirm your email address to secure your registration.
* **Phone Confirmation**: Confirm your phone number to secure your registration.
* **Submit**: This is the submit button to complete your registration.
* **Cancel**: This is the cancel button to cancel your registration.

I'll be happy to answer any questions or clarify any doubts you have about the help guide.
