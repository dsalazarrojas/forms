# retail_digital_loyalty_demo_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a digital loyalty demo, providing your business with access to exclusive content and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Choose a suitable demo date from the available options.
2. Provide your business details, including the store name and address.
3. Select a contact person and enter their email address and phone number (if applicable).
4. Specify whether to capture consents from customers during the demo.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes about your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Digital Loyalty Demo Request**: This is the title of the demo request form.
* **Choose Demo Dates**: Please select a suitable demo date from the available options. This will determine when the demo will take place.
* **Business Details**: Enter the name and address of your retail business.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person (if you choose to capture consents, this will be the email address used for notifications).
* **Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person (if you choose to capture consents, this will be the phone number used for notifications).
* **Capture Consents**: Select whether to capture consents from customers during the demo.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your business.
* **Store Name**: Enter the name of your retail store.
* **Business Type**: Select the type of business (Physical Store, Online Store, or Hybrid).
* **Store Hours**: Enter the store hours of operation.
* **Store Address**: Enter the address of your retail store.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of your retail store.
* **Store Phone**: Enter the phone number of your retail store.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the contact person.
* The demo date and time will be communicated to you separately once the form is submitted.
