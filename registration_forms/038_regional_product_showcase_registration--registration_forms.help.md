# Regional Product Showcase Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Product Showcase Registration form is designed to help you showcase regional products to a wider audience. This form collects relevant details about your product, including its description, image, and other supporting materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Organizer's name and details.
2. Provide the Name of the Product and a short description.
3. Add a Company Name if it's relevant.
4. Upload a Product Image for better visibility.
5. Check the Terms Agreement and Terms of Use Agreement checkboxes.
6. Select the required Product Video, Product Audio, and Product File checkboxes.
7. Add any additional information in the Other field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer**: This field is used to identify the person or organization hosting the product showcase event.
* **Name of Product**: Enter a brief and descriptive name for your product.
* **Product Description**: Briefly describe your product to help users understand its features and benefits.
* **Company Name**: If your product is associated with a company, please enter its name here.
* **Product Image**: Upload an image that represents your product for better visibility.
* **Product Video**: If you have a video that demonstrates your product, check this box to include it with your submission.
* **Product Audio**: If you have audio content that complements your product, check this box to include it with your submission.
* **Product File**: If you have a supporting file that users should be aware of, check this box to include it with your submission.
* **Other**: Use this field for any additional information that doesn't fit in the above categories.
