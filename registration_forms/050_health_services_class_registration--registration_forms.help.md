# health_services_class_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your health services class account. Please fill out this form with accurate information to ensure proper processing of your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide a valid Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it is known in your records.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it is known in your records.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for services and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for services and notifications.

## Tips
* Please ensure the accuracy of the information you provide to avoid any delay in processing your registration.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
