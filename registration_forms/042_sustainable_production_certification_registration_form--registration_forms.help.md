# Sustainable Production Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about your company's sustainable production practices, allowing us to assess your level of commitment to eco-friendly processes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly. Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
2. Each section of the form is designed to gather specific information about your company's sustainable practices. Please answer each question carefully to ensure you're providing the correct information.
3. If you need help or have any questions, feel free to contact us at form@gic.mx.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Applicant Information
**Applicant Information** (`applicant_info`, text, optional):
	* Please enter your name and title or position in your company.

### Production Processes
**Production Processes** (`production_processes`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select all the production processes that your company uses for sustainable practices:
		+ Greenhouse Gas Emissions
		+ Water Usage
		+ Land Use

### Company Details
**Company Details** (`company_details`, text, optional):
	* Enter a brief description of your company, including its name, location, and a short introduction.

### Contact Information
**Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional):
	* Provide a contact person's name, title, email, and phone number.

### Certification Requirements
**Certification Requirements** (`cert_requirements`, select_multiple, optional):
	* Select all the certification requirements your company is interested in:
		+ Energy Efficiency
		+ Waste Reduction
		+ Water Conservation

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you're unsure about any information, contact us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
- Registration is open to new users only; if you're already registered, do not fill out this form.
