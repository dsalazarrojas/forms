# Academic Seminar Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Seminar Volunteer Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from individuals interested in volunteering for an academic seminar. By providing your contact details, availability, skills, and preferences, you will be able to express your interest in helping with various tasks during the seminar. This form helps us to efficiently manage volunteer roles and ensure a smooth event experience for all attendees.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your full name, email address, and contact phone number.
2. Select your university or organization affiliation and current role.
3. Indicate the seminar title you are interested in volunteering for.
4. Explain your reasons for volunteering.
5. Choose the tasks you are willing to help with during the seminar (select all that apply).
6. Specify the days you are available to volunteer.
7. Choose your available time slots.
8. Declare your previous experience as a volunteer (if any).
9. List any special skills or languages you possess that could aid during the event.
10. Mention any dietary restrictions you have.
11. Describe any accessibility requirements.
12. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
13. Review and submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`, email, required): Enter your contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`volunteer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Institution** (`volunteer_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of your university or organization affiliation.
* **Current Role** (`volunteer_role`, text, required): Describe your current role or position.
* **Seminar Title** (`seminar_title`, text, required): Indicate the title of the academic seminar you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Reasons for Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest_reasons`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in volunteering for this seminar.
* **Preferred Volunteer Tasks** (`preferred_volunteer_tasks`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of tasks you are willing to help with during the seminar (e.g., Registration setup, Welcome desk, Technical support).
* **Available Days** (`availability_days`, select_multiple, required): Select the specific days you are available to volunteer for the seminar.
* **Available Time Slots** (`availability_time_slots`, text, required): Specify the time slots you are available to volunteer for.
* **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`previous_volunteer_experience`, select_one, required): Declare your previous experience as a volunteer (if any).
* **Special Skills or Languages** (`special_skills_languages`, text, optional): List any special skills or languages you possess that could aid during the event.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Mention any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): Describe any accessibility requirements you have.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
