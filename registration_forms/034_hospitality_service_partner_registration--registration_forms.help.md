# Hospitality Service Partner Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for service providers that offer hospitality-related services to register themselves with the organization. By completing this form, you will provide necessary information to be listed as a hospitality service partner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the field marked "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the field marked "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email address in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field marked "Phone".
5. Upload your required documents (Document 1 to 8) in the respective fields.
6. Enter your Date of Birth in the field marked "Date of Birth".
7. Enter your Time of Birth in the field marked "Time of Birth".
8. Enter your Business Name in the field marked "Business Name".
9. Select your Business Type from the options provided in the field marked "Business Type".
10. If your business is not listed among the options, please provide additional information in the field marked "Other Business Type".
11. Enter your website (if applicable) in the field marked "Website".
12. Indicate if you have a social media presence (if applicable) in the field marked "Social Media".
13. Enter your Address in the field marked "Address".
14. Enter your secondary address information in the field marked "Address 2".
15. Enter your City in the field marked "City".
16. Enter your State in the field marked "State".
17. Enter your Zip code in the field marked "Zip".
18. Enter your Country in the field marked "Country".
19. Upload your final required documents (Document 5 to 8) in the respective fields.
