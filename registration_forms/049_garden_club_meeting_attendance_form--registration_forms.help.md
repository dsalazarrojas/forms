# Garden Club Meeting Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Garden Club Meeting Attendance Form is a simple form designed to collect information about a user's attendance at a garden club meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first and last name in the `first_and_last_name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Select the date of the meeting from the calendar in the `meeting_date` field.
4. Choose the time of the meeting from the dropdown menu in the `meeting_time` field.
5. Enter the name of the event or meeting in the `event_name` field.
6. Indicate your attendance status by selecting "True" or "False" in the `attendance` field.
7. Add any comments about your attendance in the `comments` field. This is an optional field.
8. If you have any further comments or concerns, you can add them in the `comments` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First and Last Name (user_name)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Meeting Date (meeting_date)**: Select the date of the meeting from the calendar.
* **Meeting Time (meeting_time)**: Choose the time of the meeting from the dropdown menu.
* **Event Name (event_name)**: Enter the name of the event or meeting you attended.
* **Attendance (attendance)**: Indicate your attendance status by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Comments (comments)**: Add any additional comments about your attendance. This is an optional field.
* **Email Confirmation (email_confirmation)**: This field is not currently being used.
* **Phone (phone)**: This field is not currently being used for this form.
* **Comments (comments)**: You can add any additional comments or concerns here, if needed.
* **Email (email)**: You can confirm your email address here, if needed.
