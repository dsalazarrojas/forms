# Digital Transformation Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Transformation Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees to provide a seamless experience during the workshop. This form helps us understand your preferences, needs, and requirements to make the event a success.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the **First Time Attendee** section on page 1 with your details.
2. Proceed to the **Contact Information** section on page 2, where you will need to enter your **Email** and **Phone** (optional) details.
3. On page 3, you will see **Payment Options**. Select one or more of the options provided.
4. On page 4, you will see another **Select Options** section, which is optional.
5. On page 5, enter your **Time Of Day** you plan to attend the workshop.
6. On page 6, select the **Date Of Event** you plan to attend the workshop.
7. On page 7, enter your **Date Of Birth**.
8. Finally, fill out the remaining fields: **Select One**, **Select Multiple**, and **Note** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Time Attendee**: Please provide your details if this is your first time attending the workshop.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your **Email** and **Phone** (if applicable) to ensure we can reach you for workshop updates.
* **Payment Options**: Select one or more payment options provided to complete your registration.
* **Select Options**: This field is optional and allows you to choose one or more options.
* **Time Of Day**: Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Date Of Event**: Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for workshop updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Note**: If you have any comments or notes about your attendance, please add them here.
* **Select One**: Select one option from the provided choices.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose one or more options from the provided choices.
* **Note**: If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them here (optional).
