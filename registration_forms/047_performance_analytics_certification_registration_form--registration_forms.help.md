# Performance Analytics Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration purposes only and is not intended for general contact or inquiry.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the following fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Phone
	* Payment Method
	* Payment Amount
	* Certification Date (if applicable)
	* Certification Time (if applicable)
	* Notes (if applicable)
	* Category (e.g., Performance Analytics)
	* Assigned Tool (if applicable)
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Method (select_one)**: Choose how you plan to make the payment for the certification, e.g., Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Cash.
* **Payment Amount (number)**: Fill in the amount you are willing to pay for the certification.
* **Certification Date (date)**: If you have already received the certification, enter the date you received it.
* **Certification Time (time)**: If you have already received the certification, enter the time you received it.
* **Notes (note)**: Additional information or comments about your registration can be noted here.
* **Category (select_one)**: Specify the category under which you are seeking certification, e.g., Performance Analytics.
* **Assigned Tool (text)**: If applicable, enter the tool assigned to you during the certification process.
 
**Tips**
* Double-check your information before submission to avoid errors.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions to make payments as per chosen payment method.
* Contact the certification authority if you have any questions or concerns.
* Keep your information up to date to facilitate the certification process.
