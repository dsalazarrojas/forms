# voter_registration_records_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to release voter registration records. Complete it to provide personal information required for the process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name**.
2. Enter your **Email** address.
3. Provide your **Contact Number**.
4. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Enter your **Voter ID** (if applicable).
6. Enter your **Organization** (if applicable).
7. Provide the **Reason for Request** for releasing your voter registration records.
8. Enter the **Date of Request** (if applicable).
9. Sign and date the **Signature** field (optional).
10. Confirm that you have completed this form accurately by selecting **Yes** or **No** in the **Confirm** field.
11. Add any **Additional Comments** to support your request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter Name**: Provide your full name as it appears on your voter registration record.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Contact Number**: Provide your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Voter ID**: If you have a voter ID number, enter it here.
* **Organization**: If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Reason for Request**: Explain why you are requesting to release your voter registration records.
* **Date of Request**: If you are requesting records for a specific time period, enter the date.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the signature field to confirm your identity (optional).
* **Confirm**: Select **Yes** to confirm that you have completed this form accurately.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information supporting your request.
