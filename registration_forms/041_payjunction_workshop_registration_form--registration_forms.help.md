# PayJunction Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a workshop registration. It is used to gather details about the workshop and the attendee.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (Workshop Name, text, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Attendee Email** (Attendee Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone** (Attendee Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Start Time** (Workshop Start Time, time, required): Enter the start time of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Time** (Workshop End Time, time, required): Enter the end time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (Workshop Duration, number, required): Enter the duration of the workshop in hours.
* **Workshop Description** (Workshop Description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity** (Workshop Capacity, number, required): Enter the maximum number of attendees for the workshop.
* **Registration Type** (Registration Type, select_one, required): Choose between In-person or Online.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple payment methods: Bank Transfer, Credit Card, or PayPal.
* **Payment Details** (Payment Details, text, optional): Enter any additional payment details.
* **Workshop Location** (Workshop Location, text, optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Event Date & Time** (Event Date & Time, date, required): Enter the date and start time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Language** (Workshop Language, select_one, required): Choose the language of the workshop: English, Spanish, or French.
* **Other (please specify)** (Workshop Language Other, text, optional): Enter an other language if not listed.
* **Attendee Interest** (Attendee Interest, select_one, required): Choose one or multiple attendee interests: Marketing, Sales, or Product Management.
* **Other (please specify)** (Attendee Interest Other, text, optional): Enter another attendee interest if not listed.
* **Attendee Notes** (Attendee Notes, text, optional): Enter any notes about you as an attendee.
* **Event Notes** (Event Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the event.
* **Organizer Notes** (Organizer Notes, text, optional): Enter any notes for the event organizer.
* **Workshop Speaker** (Workshop Speaker, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop speaker.
* **Speaker Phone** (Speaker Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the workshop speaker.
* **Speaker Email** (Speaker Email, text, optional): Enter the email address of the workshop speaker.
