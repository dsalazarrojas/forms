# Smart POS Integration Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from businesses that wish to integrate their POS system with our platform for trial purposes. It will help us understand the business details and POS system information required for integration and onboarding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field with the required information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it for further integration and onboarding processes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Details** (Page 1, 1): This field is used to collect the business details required for integration. Please fill out this field with the name of your business.
* **POS System Information** (Page 1, 2): This field is used to collect information about your POS system. Please fill out this field with the details of your POS system.
* **Consent** (Page 1, 3): This field is used to collect your consent for integration and onboarding. Please select one of the available options.
* **Trial Details** (Page 1, 4): This field is used to collect trial details required for integration. Please fill out this field with the required information.
* **Business Representative** (Page 1, 5): This field is used to collect the name of the business representative. Please fill out this field with the name of the representative.
* **E-mail** (Page 1, 6): This field is used to collect the email address of the business representative. Please fill out this field with a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Page 1, 7): This field is used to collect the phone number of the business representative. Please fill out this field with a valid phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
- This form is for trial purposes only, if you have already integrated your POS system with our platform, please do not fill out this form.
