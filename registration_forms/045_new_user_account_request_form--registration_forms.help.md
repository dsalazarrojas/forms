# New User Account Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The New User Account Request Form is a registration form designed to collect information from individuals who want to create a new user account on our platform. This form helps us verify your identity and ensure that you can access our services securely and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Enter your email" field.
3. Enter a password in the "Enter your password" field. 
4. Confirm your email address by entering it again in the "Confirm your email" field.
5. Select your account type from the "Select your account type" dropdown menu.
6. Choose your preferences from the "Select your preferences" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Enter your phone number" field (optional).
8. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm your phone number" field (optional).
9. Click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enter your name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Enter your email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to and is not already in use on our platform.
* **Enter your password**: Enter a strong password that meets our password requirements. 
* **Confirm your email**: Enter your email address again to confirm its accuracy.
* **Select your account type**: Choose the type of account you want to create. This will determine the level of access and features you will have on our platform.
* **Select your preferences**: Choose the options that best suit your needs from the available preferences.
* **Enter your phone number** (optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Confirm your phone number** (optional): Enter your phone number again to confirm its accuracy.
* **Password 2** (optional): Enter a backup password for emergency access.
* **Confirm Password 2** (optional): Enter the backup password again for confirmation.
* **Select your account preferences** (optional): Choose the options that suit your needs from the available preferences.
* **User Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Confirm your phone number** (optional): Enter your phone number again to confirm its accuracy.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your application.
* **Select your account type** and **Select your preferences** fields are used to determine your account type and preferences.
* **User Phone** and **Confirm your phone number** fields are used to collect your phone number. 

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are having trouble completing the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
* If you are a returning user, please use our "Login" form instead.
* This form is not intended for users who want to edit or update their existing account information.
