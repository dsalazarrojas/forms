# Club Membership Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for club members to register their personal information, membership preferences, and agree to the club's terms. It collects essential details such as full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, street address, city, state or province, and postal code. The form also asks for membership type, start date, and interests and activities of the member. Please ensure you fill out this form accurately and honestly to facilitate a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the personal information section.
2. Provide your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Move on to the address information section.
7. Enter your street address in the "Street Address" field.
8. Enter your city in the "City" field.
9. Enter your state or province in the "State or Province" field.
10. Enter your postal code in the "Postal Code" field.
11. Select your membership type in the "Membership Type" field. Choose from "Basic", "Standard", "Premium", or "Lifetime".
12. Select your membership start date in the "Membership Start Date" field.
13. Choose your interests and activities in the "Interests and Activities" field. You can select multiple options.
14. Finally, agree to the terms in the "Terms and Approval" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: A required field to collect your full name.
- **Email Address**: A required field to collect your contact email address.
- **Phone Number**: A required field to collect your contact phone number.
- **Date of Birth**: A required field to collect your date of birth.
- **Street Address**: A required field to collect your physical address.
- **City**: A required field to collect your city name.
- **State or Province**: A required field to collect your state or province name.
- **Postal Code**: A required field to collect your postal code.
- **Membership Type**: A required field to select your membership level. Choose from "Basic", "Standard", "Premium", or "Lifetime".
- **Membership Start Date**: A required field to select your membership start date.
- **Interests and Activities**: A required field to select your activities of interest. You can choose multiple options.
- **Terms and Approval**: A non-required field to confirm agreement to the club's terms and conditions.
