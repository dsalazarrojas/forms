# industrial_data_exchange_platform_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial data exchange platform registration form is used to collect data for organizations that wish to join the Industrial Data Exchange Platform. This form is used for onboarding new users and collecting essential information about their organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the onboarding organization field with your company's name.
2. Enter your company's legal name.
3. Provide your address, including address line 1 and 2.
4. Enter your postal code.
5. Select your country.
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Create a password and confirm it in the "Confirm Password" field.
9. Select whether you accept the terms and conditions.
10. Select your organization type (Manufacturer, Supplier, or Other).
11. Select the industry your organization belongs to.
12. Finally, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Onboarding Organization**: Enter the name of your onboarding organization.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Onboarding Organization
- **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Company Name
- **Legal Name**: Enter your company's legal name.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Legal Name
- **Address Line 1**: Enter your address line 1.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Address Line 1
- **Address Line 2**: Enter your address line 2.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Address Line 2
- **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Postal Code
- **Country**: Select your country.
	* Type: `select_one`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Country
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
	* Type: `email`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Email
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Phone
- **Password**: Enter a password for your account.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Password
- **Confirm Password**: Confirm your password.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Confirm Password
- **Accept Terms**: Accept or decline the terms and conditions.
	* Type: `select_one`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Accept Terms
- **Organization Type**: Select your organization type (Manufacturer, Supplier, or Other).
	* Type: `select_multiple`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Organization Type
- **Industry**: Select the industry your organization belongs to.
	* Type: `select_multiple`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Industry
- **Confirm Password**: Confirm your password again.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Confirm Password
- **Submit**: Click this button to submit your registration.
	* Type: `text`
	* Required: No
	* Hint: Submit

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* Be cautious with your password and confirm it correctly.
* Accept or decline the terms and conditions.
* Ensure you select the correct organization type and industry for your organization.
