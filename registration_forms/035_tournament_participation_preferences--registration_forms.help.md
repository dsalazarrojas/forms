# Tournament Participation Preferences - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your preferences for participating in a tournament. It helps us understand your availability and participation options so that we can better organize and communicate with you throughout the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field as requested, and make sure to review your answers before submitting. If you have any issues or questions, don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Preferences** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Please provide a brief description of your tournament preferences. This will help us understand your specific needs and interests.
* **Availability** (`2`, `date`, required: false): Please select a date when you are available to participate in the tournament. You can choose a specific date or select a range of dates if applicable.
* **Team Details** (`3`, `text`, required: false): If you are participating as part of a team, please provide a brief description of your team and its composition.
* **Team Members** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select whether you have team members who will be participating in the tournament.
* **Team Member 1** (`5`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether Team Member 1 will be participating in the tournament.
* **Team Member 2** (`6`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select whether Team Member 2 will be participating in the tournament.
* **Team Name** (`7`, `text`, required: false): If you are participating as part of a team, please provide the name of your team.
* **Team Name (if applicable)** (`8`, `text`, required: false): If you are not participating as part of a team, please provide a team name for any team you are associated with.
* **Email** (`9`, `email`, required: false): Please provide your email address so that we can contact you regarding the tournament.
* **Phone** (`10`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your phone number so that we can contact you regarding the tournament.
* **Other Details** (`11`, `note`, required: false): If there is any additional information you would like to share about your participation, please provide it in the space below.

## Tips
