# Organizational Transparency Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Organizational Transparency Summit Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from organizations and their representatives for the summit. This includes details such as name, email, phone number, and address of the organization, as well as attendee's first and last name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Registration Information" page.
2. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required", with your information.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (id: 1, type: number, required: false): This page header serves as an introduction to the registration form. It is not a field to fill in.
* **Name** (id: 2, label: Name, type: text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (id: 3, label: Email, type: email, required: false): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Phone** (id: 4, label: Phone, type: text, required: false): Enter your organization's contact phone number.
* **Registration Organization Name** (id: 5, label: Registration Organization Name, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Registration Organization Representative** (id: 6, label: Registration Organization Representative, type: text, required: false): Enter the name of the person representing your organization.
* **Registration Organization Address** (id: 7, label: Registration Organization Address, type: text, required: false): Enter your organization's address.
* **Attendee First Name** (id: 8, label: Attendee First Name, type: text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Attendee Last Name** (id: 9, label: Attendee Last Name, type: text, required: false): Enter your last name.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure all required fields are filled in with correct information.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the summit organizers for assistance.
