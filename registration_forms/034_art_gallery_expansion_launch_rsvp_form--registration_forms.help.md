# Art Gallery Expansion Launch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Gallery Expansion Launch RSVP Form is designed to collect information about your plans to attend our upcoming launch event, ensuring that we can provide the best possible experience for all attendees, including accessibility accommodations and hospitality. Please review the following instructions to complete the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, start by selecting the correct answers for each field, making sure to fill out all required fields (denoted by a red asterisk *). If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Launch Event Invitation --**: This field is simply a heading to introduce the launch event invitation.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the guest list.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address to receive your digital entry pass and future updates about our events. Please make sure to use a valid email address.
* **Guest Category**: Select your category from the list provided:
	+ **VIP Collector**: You're a valued collector or supporter of our gallery.
	+ **Represented Artist**: You're an artist whose work is featured in our gallery.
	+ **Media - Press**: You represent a media or press outlet.
	+ **Local Government Official**: You're a local government official or representative.
	+ **General Patron**: You're a general supporter of our gallery.
	+ **Staff - Vendor**: You're a staff member or vendor working with us.
* **-- RSVP Details --**: This field is simply a heading to transition to the RSVP details.
* **Will you be attending the launch event?**: Please select "Yes - I am attending" if you plan to be there, and "No - I cannot attend" if you won't.
* **Will you be bringing a guest (Plus One)?**: Select "True" if you're bringing a guest, and "False" if not.
* **Name of your Guest**: If you're bringing a guest, enter their name here (if you're not bringing a guest, this field is not applicable).
* **Do you or your guest have any dietary restrictions?**: Select all that apply from the list provided:
	+ **None**
	+ **Vegetarian**
	+ **Vegan**
	+ **Gluten-Free**
	+ **Nut Allergy**
	+ **Dairy-Free**
* **Do you have any specific accessibility requirements?**: If you or your guest have any accessibility needs, please describe them here.
* **Preferred Phone Number for Event Updates**: Enter your preferred phone number to receive updates about our events.
* **Would you like to receive information about future VIP events?**: Select "True" if you'd like to stay updated.
* **RSVP Submission Date**: This field is automatically populated and cannot be edited.
* **Any additional notes for the organizing team?**: If you have any additional comments or requests for the organizing team, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields carefully.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
- We appreciate your prompt response to ensure we can provide the best experience for all attendees.
