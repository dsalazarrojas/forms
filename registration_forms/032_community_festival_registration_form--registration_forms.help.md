# Community Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Festival Registration Form is designed for participants to register for the festival of their choice, providing essential information for a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address to ensure we can contact you with festival updates and communications.
2. Choose the festival category you'd like to attend (Music, Food, Arts, Cultural, Film, or Harvest).
3. Select the festival days you plan to attend (Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, or All Days).
4. Choose your ticket type preference (General Admission, VIP, Early Bird, Group Package, Student, or Senior).
5. If you're part of a group, specify the total number of attendees in your group.
6. Indicate how you'll participate at the festival (Attendee, Vendor, Performer, Volunteer, Sponsor).
7. If you're a vendor, select the type of vendor you are (Food, Craft, Merchandise, or Information).
8. If applicable, provide information about any dietary restrictions you may have.
9. Let us know if you have any accessibility needs.
10. Select your T-Shirt size preference for festival merchandise (if applicable).
11. Provide the full name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
12. Consent to photo and video recording for promotional purposes.
13. Agree to the liability waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (participant_first_name): Enter your legal first name. 
* **Last Name** (participant_last_name): Enter your legal last name. 
* **Email Address** (email_address): Enter a valid email address for festival communications. 
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter your primary contact number. 
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. 
* **Street Address** (address): Enter your home address. 
* **City, State, ZIP** (city_state_zip): Enter the city, state, and ZIP code for your home address. 
* **Festival Category** (festival_category): Choose the festival you're registering for. 
* **Festival Dates** (festival_dates): Select the festival days you plan to attend. 
* **Ticket Type** (ticket_type): Choose your ticket preference. 
* **Group Size** (group_size): Enter the total attendees in your group. 
* **Type of Participation** (participation_type): Indicate how you'll participate at the festival. 
* **Vendor Type** (vendor_type): If applicable, choose the type of vendor you are. 
* **Accessibility Needs** (accessibility_needs): Provide any accessibility needs you may have. 
* **Dietary Restrictions** (dietary_restrictions): Enter any food allergies or restrictions. 
* **T-Shirt Size** (t_shirt_size): Choose your T-Shirt size preference. 
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name): Enter the full name of your emergency contact person. 
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person. 
* **Photo and Video Consent** (photo_consent): Consent to photo and video recording for promotional purposes. 
* **Liability Waiver** (waiver_agreement): Agree to the liability waiver.
