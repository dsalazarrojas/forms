# Watercolor Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from students who want to join a Watercolor Course. The form helps the course organizers gather essential details about the students and courses, ensuring a smooth and organized course management process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully and fill out only the necessary information.
2.  Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Complete all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the field-by-field explanation.
4.  If you encounter any issues or errors, contact the course administrators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide a text note about the student.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Course Date** (`course_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Time** (`course_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time you plan to attend the course.
* **Student Information 1** (`student_info_1`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide additional text notes about the student.
* **Course Type** (`course_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of Watercolor course you want to register for (e.g., Watercolor 101, Watercolor 201, etc.).
* **Course Days** (`course_days`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the days you plan to attend the course (e.g., Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.).
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you want to provide.
* **Student Information 2** (`student_info_2`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide additional text notes about the student.
* **Course Time Range** (`course_time_range`, `time`, optional): Select the time range you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the course in hours.
* **Course Price** (`course_price`, `number`, optional): Enter the price you are willing to pay for the course.
* **Course Description** (`course_description`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Instructor Information** (`instructor_info`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide additional text notes about the instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (`instructor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the instructor.
* **Instructor Email** (`instructor_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of the instructor.
* **Course Date Range** (`course_date_range`, `date`, optional): Select the date range you plan to attend the course.
* **Course Time Range 2** (`course_time_range_2`, `time`, optional): Select the time range you plan to attend the course.
* **Student Information 3** (`student_info_3`, `note`, optional): This field is used to provide additional text notes about the student.
* **Course Price Range** (`course_price_range`, `number`, optional): Enter the price range you are willing to pay for the course.
* **Course Duration 2** (`course_duration_2`, `number`, optional): Enter the duration of the course in hours.
* **Course Description 2** (`course_description_2`, `text`, optional): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Submit 2** (`submit_2`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or information you want to provide.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you encounter any issues, contact the course administrators for assistance.
