# halloween_map_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Halloween map registration form is designed to collect information from homeowners who want to participate in a Halloween event. This form helps event organizers know who is participating, their contact information, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your information and preferences.
2. Review your answers for accuracy.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Homeowner name** (`homeowner_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as the homeowner who will be participating in the event.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your address so that event organizers can reach you and know where to find you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for event updates and information.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in case event organizers need to contact you.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Select the date of the event for which you want to register.
* **Treats** (`treat`, select_multiple, required: false): Check all the types of treats you are willing to provide during the event (e.g., candy, toys, etc.).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): If you have any special requests or notes, please include them here.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required: false): This field is likely not used in this form, but if it was used in a previous version of the form, enter the name of the tool assigned to you.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields, but only those that are required.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you have any questions or need help, feel free to ask the event organizers.
