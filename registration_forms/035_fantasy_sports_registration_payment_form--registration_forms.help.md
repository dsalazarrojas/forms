# fantasy_sports_registration_payment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants to fantasy sports events. To complete this form, follow the steps outlined below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all required information readily available before proceeding.
2. Select the payment method for your registration (credit card, bank transfer, or cash).
3. Enter the payment amount.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Add any additional notes about your registration.
7. Enter your name as it appears on your registration documents.
8. Enter your address as it appears on your registration documents.
9. Select the date and time of your payment.
10. Select the name of the fantasy sports league you are registering for.
11. Verify the status of your payment (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant info**: Enter a brief description of your participation in the event. This field is not required.
* **payment_info**: Enter any additional payment information not included in other fields. This field is not required.
* **payment_method**: Select the method by which you will be paying for your registration. Options include credit card, bank transfer, and cash.
* **payment_amount**: Enter the amount you plan to pay for your registration. This field requires numerical input.
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address. This field is used for communication and is required.
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is not required but may be used for contact purposes.
* **participant_notes**: Enter any additional notes about your registration. This field is not required.
* **participant_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your registration documents. This field is not required.
* **participant_address**: Enter your address as it appears on your registration documents. This field is not required.
* **payment_date**: Select the date of your payment. This field is not required.
* **payment_time**: Select the time of your payment. This field is not required.
* **fantasy_league_name**: Select the name of the fantasy sports league you are registering for. This field is not required.
* **payment_status**: Verify the status of your payment (Active or Inactive). This field is not required.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your input for accuracy to avoid errors in your registration.
- If you have any issues with your registration, contact our customer support.
