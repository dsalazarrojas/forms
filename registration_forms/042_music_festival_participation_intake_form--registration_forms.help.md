# Music Festival Participation Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and performers at music festivals. It will help us understand their preferences, needs, and expectations, allowing us to tailor our festival experience to better serve them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Please select the correct option that best describes your role at the festival:**
	* Attendee: If you are a festival-goer or attendee.
	* Performer: If you are a musician or artist participating in the festival.
2. Fill out the respective form sections based on your selected role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Attendee Info (Page 1)
* **Attendee Info**: This is the main form section for attendees. Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information as prompted.

### Performer Info (Page 2)
* **Performer Info**: This is the main form section for performers. Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information as prompted.

### Vendor Info (Page 3)
* **Vendor Info**: This section is for vendors or organizations providing services or goods during the festival. Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information as prompted.

## Tips

* Please be as detailed and honest as possible when completing the form.
* If you're unsure about any information, feel free to ask our staff for assistance.
* We will use the collected data to tailor our festival experience to better serve you.
