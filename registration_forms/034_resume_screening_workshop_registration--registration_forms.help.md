# Resume Screening Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees to the Resume Screening Workshop. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the information as follows:
1. Enter your workshop details in the field below.
2. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for each preference and session preference.
3. Optionally, provide any additional expectations for the workshop.
4. Enter your contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Details**: This field is for you to enter your workshop details. Enter your name, company or organization and any additional comments you may have regarding your workshop details.
* **Attendee Preferences**: Select 'Yes' for any preferences you have such as dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, etc.
* **Session Preferences**: Select multiple sessions you are interested in attending by choosing 'Yes' for each applicable session.
* **Workshop Expectations**: Enter any additional expectations you may have for the workshop.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your name, email address and phone number to allow us to contact you with any workshop details.
