# Kindergarten Checklist Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Kindergarten Checklist Form is a document used to collect essential information from parents and teachers about a child's health, medical conditions, and school details. This form is crucial for the school administration to ensure the child's well-being and safety while at school.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and address in the "Parent Name" and "Street Address" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your contact information, including your phone number and email address.
3. In the "Emergency Contact Person" and "Emergency Contact Number" fields, list the contact details of an individual who can be reached in case of an emergency.
4. If your child has any medical conditions or allergies, please list them in the "Medical Conditions" and "Allergies" fields.
5. Indicate your child's school attendance status in the "School Attendance" field.
6. Fill in your child's school start date.
7. Enter your child's name, birthdate, and birth time.
8. Provide your child's age.
9. In the "Parent Signature" field, sign and date to confirm that you have reviewed and completed the form accurately.
10. Finally, the teacher will review the form and sign in the "Teacher Signature" field to confirm that they have reviewed and verified the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name, which will serve as a reference point for school records.
* **Street Address** (`address`, text, **Required**): Provide your home address for communication and record purposes.
* **Parent Phone** (`contact_number`, text, **Required**): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Parent Email** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_person`, text, **Required**): List a person that we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`, text, **Required**): Enter the phone number of the contact person listed above.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, **Required**): If your child has any medical conditions, please list them here.
* **Allergies** (`allergy_list`, text, **Required**): If your child has any allergies, please list them here.
* **School Attendance** (`school_attendance`, text, **Required**): Indicate your child's school attendance status.
* **School Start Date** (`school_start_date`, date, **Required**): Enter your child's school start date.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your child's name.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, date, **Required**): Enter your child's birthdate.
* **Birth Time** (`birthtime`, time, **Required**): Enter your child's birth time.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, **Required**): Enter your child's age.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, **Required**): Sign and date to confirm that you have reviewed and completed the form accurately.
* **Teacher Signature** (`teacher_signature`, select_one, **Required**): Check "Yes" if you have reviewed and verified the information.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your name.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your school name.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your department name.
* **Teacher Title** (`teacher_title`, select_multiple, **Required**): Check "Yes" if you have reviewed and verified the information.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email, **Required**): Enter your email address.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, **Required**): Enter your school address.
* **School City** (`school_city`, text, **Required**): Enter your school city.
* **School Province** (`school_province`, text, **Required**): Enter your school province.
* **School Postal Code** (`school_postal_code`, text, **Required**): Enter your school postal code.

## Tips
Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields accurately and completely. If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
