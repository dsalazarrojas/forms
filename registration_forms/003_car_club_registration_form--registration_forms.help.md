# Car Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal and vehicle information from users who wish to join the Car Club. It is essential that the submitted data is accurate and complete to ensure a smooth membership process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the personal information section. This includes:
	* First name
	* Last name
	* Email address
	* Phone number
	* Date of birth (optional)
2. Next, provide your vehicle details:
	* Vehicle year
	* Vehicle make
	* Vehicle model
	* Vehicle color (optional)
	* License plate number (optional)
3. If you have made any modifications to your vehicle, list them in the "List any modifications made to the vehicle" field.
4. Select your desired membership level:
	* Basic
	* Premium
	* VIP
	* Lifetime
5. Choose your interests within the club:
	* Monthly Meetups
	* Track Events
	* Car Shows
	* Autocross
	* Road Trips
	* Technical Workshops
	* Social Events
6. Finally, tell us how you heard about the club:
	* Social Media
	* Friend Referral
	* Web Search
	* Car Show
	* Local Event
	* Advertisement
	* Other

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number for club communication.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth if you wish to provide it. This information will help us understand your age group within the club.
* **Vehicle year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle make**: Enter the make of your vehicle (e.g., Honda, Ford, Toyota).
* **Vehicle model**: Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle color**: If your vehicle has a custom color, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **License plate number**: If you have a license plate number, enter it here. Otherwise, leave it blank.
* **List any modifications made to the vehicle**: If you have made any modifications to your vehicle, list them here (e.g., Engine, suspension, paint, interior, etc.).
* **Performance upgrades**: Select all the performance upgrades that apply to your vehicle (e.g., Turbocharger, Supercharger, Exhaust System, etc.).
* **Exterior modifications**: Select all the exterior modifications that apply to your vehicle (e.g., Custom Paint, Wheels/Rims, Body Kit, etc.).
* **Desired membership level**: Choose the membership level that best suits your needs:
	+ Basic: Access to club events and benefits.
	+ Premium: Access to exclusive events and benefits.
	+ VIP: Access to priority events and benefits.
	+ Lifetime: Lifetime membership with all benefits.
* **Club interests**: Select all the club interests that you are interested in:
	+ Monthly Meetups
	+ Track Events
	+ Car Shows
	+ Autocross
	+ Road Trips
	+ Technical Workshops
	+ Social Events
* **How did you hear about our club?**: Select the source where you heard about the club. This will help us understand our outreach and marketing efforts.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure a smooth membership process. If you have any questions or concerns, contact the club directly.
