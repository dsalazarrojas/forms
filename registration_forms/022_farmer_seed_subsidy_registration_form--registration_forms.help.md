# Farmer Seed Subsidy Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farmer Seed Subsidy Registration Form is a crucial tool for farmers to register their seed requirements and receive subsidies. This form helps agricultural extension officers and other officials to gather necessary information in a streamlined and efficient manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct category from the "Category" dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name, contact number, and email address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your date of birth and the assigned tool (if applicable).
4. Enter any relevant notes or comments in the "Note" field (if necessary).
5. Upload your output file or enter the assigned tool user (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Registration Forms`, `select_one`): Select the correct category from the dropdown menu to register your seed subsidies.
* **Farmer Name** (`farmer_name`, `text`): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `number`): Enter your contact number for further communication.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`): Enter your location or farm address for mapping and logistics.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`): Enter your date of birth as per your identification documents.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`): Enter the time zone or time of submission (optional).
* **Note** (`note`, `note`): Enter any relevant notes or comments about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`): Enter the assigned tool or equipment for your seed subsidy process (if applicable).
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `text`): Upload your output file or enter the assigned tool user (if applicable).
* **Assigned Tool User** (`assigned_tool_user`, `text`): Enter the assigned tool user or equipment user (if applicable).
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`): Enter the person submitting the form on your behalf (if applicable).
* **Submitted Time** (`submitted_time`, `time`): Enter the time of submission (if applicable).

## Tips

* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully to avoid any issues with your seed subsidy registration.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your local agricultural extension officer for guidance.
* This form is designed to be completed by farmers who are eligible for seed subsidies.
* If you are unsure about any field, consult the help guide or contact your local agricultural extension officer for clarification.
