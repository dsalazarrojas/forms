# Nature Creative Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Nature Creative Workshop. It helps us organize and tailor the workshop to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name.
2. Provide your email address and phone number (if you wish to be contacted).
3. Select your workshop preferences (e.g., specific activities or topics).
4. Enter the date you plan to register for the workshop (or if you're already registered, enter the date of your participation).
5. Choose a time slot (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes you'd like to share with the workshop organizers (this is optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be used for workshop purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to be used for workshop purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Provide your email address where you can be reached regarding workshop information (if you wish to be contacted by us).
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, enter your phone number (if you don't wish to be contacted, please leave this blank).
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more options for the workshop activities or topics that interest you.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): If you're already registered, enter the date you plan to participate in the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose a time slot for your participation (if applicable).
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with us regarding your participation in the workshop.

## Tips
Please ensure you are filling out the correct form and are not duplicating registrations. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
