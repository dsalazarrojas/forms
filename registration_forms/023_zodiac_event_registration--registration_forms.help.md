# Zodiac Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for a zodiac-themed event. The information collected will be used to create a database of participants and track their zodiac signs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name as the participant.
2. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
3. Provide your phone number for easy contact.
4. Enter your date of birth for zodiac sign calculations.
5. Select your zodiac sign from the provided options.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participants Name**: Enter your real name as the participant. (text, required)
* **Participants Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes. (email, required)
* **Participants Phone**: Enter your phone number for easy contact. (text, optional)
* **Participants Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This is required for zodiac sign calculations. (date, required)
* **Participants Time of Registration**: Enter the time you are registering. This is not currently used, but it is included for future development. (time, optional)
* **Participants Zodiac Sign**: Select your zodiac sign from the provided list. (select_one, optional)
* **Event Start Date**: Choose the start date of the event. (date, optional)
* **Event End Date**: Choose the end date of the event. (date, optional)

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for a proper zodiac sign calculation.
* If you are unsure about your zodiac sign, you can ask a trusted advisor for help.
* Double-check the dates for the event to ensure they are correct.
