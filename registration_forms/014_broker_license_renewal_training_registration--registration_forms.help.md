# Broker License Renewal Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a broker license renewal training course. To complete the form, please follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by reviewing the form and ensuring you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in your participant information, including your full name, email address, phone number, and brokerage firm name.
3. Select the type of broker license you hold and enter your current license expiration date.
4. Choose the number of credit hours you need to complete for your license renewal.
5. Select the category of training you require.
6. Choose the course you wish to take by selecting it from the options provided.
7. Select your preferred course date, location, and time slot.
8. Enter your preferred payment method and provide any billing address information if necessary.
9. If you have any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs, please indicate them.
10. Check the boxes to agree to the attendance and cancellation policies.
11. Enter your email address where you would like to receive confirmation of your registration.
12. Finally, review and submit your registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
* **Broker License Number**: Enter your current broker license number.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for course communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company or Firm Name**: Enter the name of your brokerage firm.
* **License Type**: Select the type of broker license you hold.
* **Current License Expiration Date**: Enter the date your current license expires.
* **Required Credit Hours**: Enter the number of credit hours you need to complete for your license renewal.
* **Training Category**: Select the category of training you require.
* **Course Name**: Select the course you wish to take.
* **Preferred Course Date**: Select the date you would like to attend the course.
* **Course Location**: Select your preferred course location.
* **Preferred Session Time**: Select your preferred time slot for the course.
* **Course Fee**: Enter the fee for the selected course.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for the course.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the address for billing if different from your registered address.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Indicate any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Enter any accessibility accommodations you need.
* **Special Requests**: Enter any additional requests or needs.
* **I understand the attendance policy**: Check to agree to the full attendance policy.
* **I agree to the cancellation policy**: Check to acknowledge the cancellation terms.
* **Send confirmation to this email**: Enter the email address where you would like to receive confirmation of your registration.
* **Registration Date**: This field will be automatically populated upon submission.
