# Business Entity Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a business entity with the relevant authorities. It collects information about the business, including its name, entity type, contact details, and business address.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the entity type from the dropdown list.
2. Enter the business name as it is registered.
3. Enter the state or jurisdiction of incorporation.
4. Enter the date of formation.
5. Enter the registration or filing number.
6. Select the contact person's name.
7. Enter the contact person's title.
8. Enter the contact person's email address.
9. Enter the contact person's phone number.
10. Enter the mailing address.
11. Enter the principal place of business.
12. Enter the city.
13. Select the state or province.
14. Enter the postal code.
15. Enter the country.
16. Enter the industry classification.
17. Enter the name of the principal owner or president.
18. If applicable, enter the additional office locations.
19. Enter a brief description of the business.
20. Enter the ownership percentage of the principal owner.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name**: Enter the legal registered name of the business. (Required)
* **Entity Type**: Select the type of business entity from the dropdown list. (Required)
* **State/Jurisdiction of Incorporation**: Enter the state or jurisdiction where the business is registered. (Required)
* **Date of Formation**: Enter the date the business was formed. (Required)
* **Registration Number**: Enter the registration or filing number of the business. (Required)
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person for the business. (Required)
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the primary contact person. (Required)
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person. (Required)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person. (Required)
* **Contact Address**: Enter the mailing address of the business. (Required)
* **Principal Place of Business**: Enter the main business location. (Required)
* **City**: Enter the city of the business location. (Required)
* **State/Province**: Enter the state or province where the business is located. (Required)
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the business location. (Required)
* **Country**: Enter the country where the business is located. (Required)
* **Industry Classification**: Select the industry classification of the business. (Required)
* **Business Description**: Enter a brief description of what the business does. (Required)
* **Ownership Percentage**: Enter the percentage of ownership of the principal owner or president. (Optional)
* **Principal Owner Name**: Enter the name of the principal owner or president. (Required)
* **Additional Office Locations**: If applicable, enter other office locations of the business. (Optional)
