# Social Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Agreement Form is a registration form used to collect essential information from participants, ensuring a smooth and safe experience for everyone involved. Please take a few minutes to complete this form carefully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through each section carefully and answer all questions truthfully.
2. Make sure to fill in all required fields (indicated by a *).
3. Use the provided text boxes to type your answers.
4. Review your form before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Agreement** (participant_agreement, text, required): This is where you confirm that you have read, understand, and agree to the terms and conditions of the event/program. Please type 'yes' or 'no' to indicate your agreement.
* **Participant Expectations** (participant_expectations, text, required): Describe your expectations during the event/program, such as what you hope to gain, achieve, or accomplish. Be as specific as possible.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (emergency_contact_info, text, optional): Provide the contact information of a person who will be contacted in case of an emergency. This can be a phone number, email, or address.
* **Participant Emergency Contact** (participant_emergency_contact, text, optional): List the name of the person you've just provided contact information for. This will help us identify who to contact in case of an emergency.

## Tips
* Please double-check your answers before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask the event/program staff.
* Remember to complete this form carefully, as it's essential for us to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.
