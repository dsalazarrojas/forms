# Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from individuals planning to attend the reunion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your **Graduation Year** from the list.
6. Indicate whether you will **Attend** the reunion or not.
7. Select the **Reunion Year** you will attend.
8. Choose the **Reunion Location** from the list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about the reunion.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you for any updates or emergencies.
* **Graduation Year**: Select the year you graduated from the institution.
* **Graduation Year** is a required field that will be used to verify your attendance.
* **Attending**: Select "High School", "College" or "Not Attending" indicating whether you plan to attend the reunion.
* **Reunion Year**: Select the year of the reunion you are planning to attend.
* **Reunion Location**: Select the location of the reunion you plan to attend.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you experience any issues while filling out the form, contact the reunion organizers for assistance.
