# Maritime Exhibition Invitation & RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendee information for a maritime exhibition event. Fill out the form to register your attendance, provide your contact details, and indicate your attendance preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Provide your First Name and Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number.
5. Select a Date to attend the event (if applicable).
6. Choose an Event Time (if applicable).
7. Indicate your Attendance status (True/False or Maybe).
8. Select a date by which you would like to RSVP (optional).
9. If necessary, provide Comments about your attendance.
10. If necessary, make any Special Requests you have for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Your first name is requested.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Not Required): Your last name is optionally provided for further contact.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, Not Required): Your email address is used for communication.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, Not Required): Your phone number for emergency contacts.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, Not Required): The date of the event. 
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, Not Required): The time of the event. 
- **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_one`, Not Required): Choose True if attending, False if not, or Maybe if uncertain.
- **RSVP by** (`rsvp_by`, `date`, Not Required): The date by which you will RSVP.
- **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, Not Required): Any comments about your attendance.
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, Not Required): Any specific requests for the event.
