# Cultural Association Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for cultural associations to renew their membership or affiliation with us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information, ensuring that you are a current member of the association.
2. Enter your member name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number (if available).
4. Select the renewal date (the date you would like to renew your membership).
5. Submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Renewal Application** (`renewal_application`, text, optional): This field is a free text area where you can provide a brief explanation of why you are renewing your membership. If you would like to include any additional information, you can use this field.
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, optional): This field is for your name as a member of the association.
* **Member Email** (`member_email`, email, optional): Please enter your email address that we have on file for you.
* **Member Phone** (`member_phone`, text, optional): If you would like to provide your phone number for contact purposes, please enter it here.
* **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to renew your membership. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth renewal process.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our office for assistance.
