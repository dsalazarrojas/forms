# gym_member_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users registering as gym members.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the first and last name fields as they are both required fields.
2. Enter a valid email address associated with your membership.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Select your birth date from the calendar picker.
5. Choose your member status from the select one options: Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
6. Select your membership length from the select one options: Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months.
7. Choose your start date and end date from the calendar picker.
8. Select your gym name from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required, text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required, text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (required, email): Enter a valid email address associated with your membership.
* **Phone** (required, text): Enter your phone number.
* **Birth Date** (required, date): Select your birth date from the calendar picker.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (optional, text): Provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (optional, text): Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Member Status** (required, select_one): Choose your member status from Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Member Category** (optional, select_multiple): Select one or more of the following options: Student, Member, Coach, or Staff.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (optional, select_multiple): Choose the relationship between the emergency contact and you from Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Wife, or Husband.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (optional, text): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (optional, text): Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Address** (required, select_multiple): Select one or more of the following options: Street, City, State, Country, or Zip.
* **Gym Name** (required, text): Enter the name of the gym you are registering to.
* **Start Date** (required, date): Choose the start date of your membership from the calendar picker.
* **End Date** (required, date): Choose the end date of your membership from the calendar picker.
* **Membership Length** (required, select_one): Select the length of your membership from Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months.
* **Membership Status** (required, select_one): Choose your membership status from Active, Inactive, or Suspended.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (optional, text): Enter the address of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact City** (optional, text): Enter the city of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact State** (optional, text): Enter the state of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Country** (optional, text): Enter the country of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Zip** (optional, text): Enter the zip code of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Type** (optional, select_one): Choose the relationship between the emergency contact and you from Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Wife, or Husband.
* **Notes** (optional, note): Enter any additional notes about your membership.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Double-check the accuracy of all information entered before submitting.
- If you are having trouble completing any of the fields, please contact your gym staff for assistance.
