# Double Dutch Competition Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register teams for the Double Dutch Competition. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields marked as required (e.g., Team Leader Name, Consent).
2. Provide accurate and complete information in each field.
3. Review your form submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team as you want it to be displayed in the competition.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Team Leader Name** (`team_leader_name`, text): Enter the name of the team leader or main point of contact.
* **Team Leader Phone** (`team_leader_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the team leader or main point of contact.
* **Team Leader Email** (`team_leader_email`, email): Enter the email address of the team leader or main point of contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required): Select "I have the consent of the parent/guardian" to confirm that you have obtained the necessary consent.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number): Enter the number of team members.
* **Division** (`division`, text): Enter the division or category your team will be competing in.
* **School** (`school`, text): Enter the name of the school or institution affiliated with your team.
* **Teacher** (`teacher`, text): Enter the name of the teacher or adult supervisor associated with your team.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email): Enter the email address of the school or institution.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the school or institution.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, text): Enter the name of the teacher or adult supervisor.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the teacher or adult supervisor.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, email): Enter the email address of the teacher or adult supervisor.
* **Emergency Details** (`emergency_details`, text): Provide any additional emergency contact information.
* **Additional Emergency Details** (`emergency_details_2`, text): Provide more details about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Details 3** (`emergency_details_3`, text): Add more emergency contact information if needed.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text): Enter any additional comments or information about your team.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your form submission before submitting it to avoid errors.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the event organizers or administrators.
* Review and update your form submission if any information changes.
