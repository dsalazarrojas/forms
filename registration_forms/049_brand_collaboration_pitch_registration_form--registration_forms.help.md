# Brand Collaboration Pitch Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you pitch your ideas for collaborations with our brand. We're looking for innovative ideas and partnerships that can benefit both parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your basic contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your organization, including its name and type.
3. Specify the title and description of your collaboration pitch.
4. Identify the target audience for your pitch.
5. Choose the type of collaboration you're interested in (e.g., content partnership, sponsored post, or product endorsement).
6. Select a proposed timeline for the collaboration.
7. Describe the expected impact and benefits of the collaboration.
8. If you have supporting materials, such as a media kit, attach them or specify that they're available upon request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Information**:
	+ **Full Name** (id: 2): Your personal name, as you'd like to be addressed. 
	+ **Email Address** (id: 3): Your primary contact email address. Please ensure it's up-to-date and active.
	+ **Phone Number** (id: 4): Your primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization Information**:
	+ **Organization or Brand Name** (id: 6): Your company or brand name, as it appears on your website or marketing materials.
	+ **Organization Type** (id: 7): Select the category that best describes your organization (e.g., Brand, Creator, Agency, etc.).
* **Collaboration Pitch**:
	+ **Collaboration Title or Concept** (id: 10): A concise title for your collaboration idea.
	+ **Pitch Description** (id: 11): A detailed explanation of your collaboration idea, including its objectives and benefits.
	+ **Target Audience** (id: 12): Identify the target audience for your collaboration.
* **Collaboration Details**:
	+ **Type of Collaboration** (id: 14): Choose the type of partnership you're looking for (e.g., content partnership, sponsored post, etc.).
	+ **Proposed Timeline** (id: 15): Select a suitable timeline for the collaboration.
* **Additional Information**:
	+ **Media Kit or Supporting Materials Available** (id: 20): If you have supporting materials, such as a media kit, select "Yes, attached" or "Available upon request".
	+ **Media Kit or Supporting Materials Submitted** (id: 20, note): You can also specify that you'll send materials separately.

## Tips
- Ensure you've reviewed the required fields carefully before submitting.
- If you've completed a draft but haven't attached supporting materials, specify that they'll be sent separately.
- If you've attached materials, ensure they're relevant and complete.
