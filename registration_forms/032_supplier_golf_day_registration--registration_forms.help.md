# Supplier Golf Day Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Golf Day Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from suppliers to organize and coordinate the Golf Day event. To ensure accurate and efficient data collection, please follow these steps:

## How To Complete This Form
### To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the label.
2. For fields marked "optional," you can provide additional information if needed but are not required to do so.
3. For fields with a "select_one" type, choose one of the provided options.
4. Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the event, including its title and date.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your company's contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Supplier Details** (`supplier_details`, text, required): Enter your supplier's name, company name, and any other relevant details.
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, required): Provide details about your registration, including your role and any specific requirements.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter your preferred payment method and any additional payment instructions.
* **Other Details** (`other_details`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information about your supplier or company.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Accept the terms and conditions of the Golf Day event by selecting "I accept the terms and conditions."
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click "Submit" to finalize your registration and send your information to the event organizers.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, text, not required): Click "Cancel" to exit the form without submitting your information.

Note: The "Terms and Conditions" field is not required but must be acknowledged by selecting one of the two options. 

**Tips:**
- Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate data collection.
- Review the "Terms and Conditions" carefully before accepting them.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers at form@gic.mx.
