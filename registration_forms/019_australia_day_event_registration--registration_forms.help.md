# Australia Day Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Australia Day Event. Please fill in the required information to let us know about your attendance and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your registration date.
2. Provide your first and last name.
3. Enter your email address and contact phone number.
4. Select the number of people attending with you (if any).
5. Specify the composition of your group (adults, children, etc.).
6. Choose your preferred event activities and sports (if applicable).
7. Indicate your interest in volunteering and preferred roles.
8. Select your available time slots.
9. Describe any accessibility requirements or mobility assistance you need.
10. Specify how you heard about the event.
11. Confirm if you have attended previous Australia Day events.
12. Add any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, required): Date you are registering for the event.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Total Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, required): The number of people attending with you (if any).
* **Group Composition** (`attendee_composition`, required): Specify the composition of your group (e.g., adults, children, etc.).
* **Ages of Children Attending** (`age_of_children`, optional): If children are in your group, specify their ages.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, optional): Specify any dietary requirements or restrictions you have (e.g., vegetarian, etc.).
* **Activity Preferences** (`event_activity_preferences`, required): Choose the activities you would like to attend (e.g., opening ceremony, sports, etc.).
* **Interest in Sports Events** (`sports_interest`, optional): Indicate if you are interested in sports events.
* **Music Preferences** (`music_preferences`, optional): Choose your preferred music genre.
* **Interest in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, required): Indicate if you are interested in volunteering.
* **Preferred Volunteer Role** (`volunteer_roles`, optional): If interested in volunteering, choose your preferred role.
* **Available Time Slots** (`availability_times`, required): Select the available time slots you are available to attend.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): Specify any accessibility requirements you need (e.g., wheelchair access, etc.).
* **Need Mobility Assistance** (`mobility_assistance`, optional): Indicate if you need mobility assistance.
* **Need Transportation Help** (`transportation_assistance`, optional): Indicate if you need transportation help.
* **How Did You Hear About Event** (`community_connection`, required): Specify how you heard about this event.
* **Attended Previous Australia Day Events** (`prior_australia_day_attendance`, optional): Confirm if this is your first time attending an Australia Day event.
* **Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_comments`, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you have.

Tips:
* Ensure all required fields are completed.
* Be specific about your group composition.
* If you have children, specify their ages.
* Choose multiple options for volunteer roles if interested.
* Select multiple time slots that you are available to attend.
