# Government Policy Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for government policy workshops. Please fill out the form with your accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Title, and User Role.
2. Provide your Workshop Topic and Language preferences.
3. Select the type of workshop you are interested in (Government Policy, Public Health, or Other).
4. If applicable, indicate if you have already attended the workshop (True or False).
5. Enter any additional Comments you would like to provide.
6. Optionally, you can provide your Organization and Phone Type if relevant.
7. Enter your Zip Code, Country, and City for your location information.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.
