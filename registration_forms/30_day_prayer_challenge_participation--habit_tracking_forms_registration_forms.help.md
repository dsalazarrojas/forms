# 30 Day Prayer Challenge Participation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register users for a 30-day prayer challenge. It collects information to understand their current prayer practice, goals, and preferences. Once submitted, the user will be directed to their next relevant step in the challenge process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information and select your preferences.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are submitting accurate and complete information.
3. Once you've completed the form, click submit to begin your 30-day journey.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name (Required)**: Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email address (Required)**: Enter a valid email address where you will receive daily prayer prompts.
* **Phone number (Optional)**: Enter a phone number where you can receive important updates (if you prefer not to receive email updates).
* **Age group**: Select your age group to help us tailor the challenge to your stage of life.
* **Church or ministry affiliation (Optional)**: If you are affiliated with a church or ministry, enter the name here.
* **Current prayer practice level (Required)**: Select your current level of prayer practice to help us understand your starting point.
* **Primary prayer focus during challenge (Optional)**: Select all that apply to indicate what you want to focus on during the challenge.
* **Daily time commitment (Required)**: Select how much time you can dedicate daily to the challenge.
* **Preferred daily reminder method (Required)**: Select how you prefer to receive daily reminders.
* **Accountability partner (Required)**: Select whether you'll be participating with an accountability partner.
* **Preferred prayer language (Required)**: Select your preferred prayer language.
* **Special prayer requests for the group (Optional)**: Share any special requests or intentions you have for the group.
* **How did you hear about this challenge (Optional)**: Select how you were referred to the challenge.
* **Have you participated in similar challenges before (Optional)**: Select if you have participated in similar challenges before.
* **Accessibility needs or accommodations (Optional)**: If you have any accessibility needs or accommodations, describe them here.
* **Prayer resources (Optional)**: Select all the prayer resources you regularly use.
* **Commitment to participate faithfully for 30 days (Required)**: Confirm your commitment to participate in the challenge for the full 30 days.
* **Additional comments or prayer intentions (Optional)**: Share any additional comments or prayer intentions here.
* **May we contact you about future events (Required)**: Confirm whether we can contact you about future events.

Note: This help guide is designed to be concrete and concise, explaining each field in the context of the 30-day prayer challenge registration form.
