# Scholarship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals to register for scholarships. It gathers essential information about the user, their contact details, educational background, scholarship specifics, and uploaded documents.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your user information, including any relevant details you'd like to share.
2. Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you about your application.
3. Describe your education background, including any relevant academic achievements or credentials.
4. Specify the scholarship you're applying for, including any additional details you'd like to submit.
5. Upload any required or supporting documents related to your application.
6. Review the form carefully to ensure you've provided all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information (<name>, <type>, optional)**: This field is for you to provide some basic details about yourself. Feel free to share whatever information you think is relevant to your application.
* **Contact Information (<name>, <type>, optional)**: This field is for you to provide contact details so we can get in touch with you about your application.
* **Education Information (<name>, <type>, optional)**: This section is for you to describe your educational background, including any academic achievements or credentials that you think will help with your application.
* **Scholarship Information (<name>, <type>, optional)**: In this section, please specify the scholarship you're applying for and provide any additional details you'd like to submit.
* **Upload Documents (<name>, <type>, optional)**: This is where you upload any documents that you believe will support your application. Please ensure they're in a suitable format.
* **Review Page (<name>, <type>, optional)**: This section is for you to review your application and make any necessary corrections before submission.
