# Field Trip Consent And Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Field Trip Consent And Intake Form" is designed to gather essential information from students, parents/guardians, and teachers for field trips, ensuring their safety and well-being. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read each field explanation to understand what information is required.
- Enter accurate responses for all required fields.
- Review all fields thoroughly before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Field 1: Field Trip Consent**
* Field 1 (`field_trip_consent`, text, required): Enter your consent to participate in the field trip.
* Explanation: This field requires your consent to participate in the field trip. Please select 'Yes' to indicate your agreement.
* Required: Yes

### **Field 2: Student Information**
* Field 2 (`student_information`, select one, optional): Please select 'Yes' to provide student information.
* Explanation: This field allows you to provide information about the student participating in the field trip.
* Required: No

### **Field 3: Emergency Contact Information**
* Field 3 (`emergency_contact_information`, select multiple, required): Provide the emergency contact information.
* Explanation: This field requires information about the emergency contact person, including their first name and last name.
* Required: Yes

### **Field 4: Student Health**
* Field 4 (`student_health`, note, optional): Enter any relevant health information about the student.
* Explanation: This field allows you to provide any health information about the student that may be relevant to the field trip.
* Required: No

### **Field 5: Student Allergies**
* Field 5 (`student_allergies`, select multiple, optional): List any allergies the student has.
* Explanation: This field requires listing any allergies the student has, such as peanuts or tree nuts.
* Required: No

### **Field 6: Emergency Contact Name**
* Field 6 (`emergency_contact_name`, select one, required): Select whether you are the emergency contact or not.

### **Field 7: Emergency Contact Relationship**
