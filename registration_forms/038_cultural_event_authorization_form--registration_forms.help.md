# Cultural Event Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event organizers to submit information about upcoming cultural events that require authorization. The form helps gather necessary details to evaluate and manage event requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of event you are submitting (e.g., Cultural Event, Art Exhibition, Music Festival).
2. Enter the name of your event.
3. Provide the date of your event.
4. Enter the start and end times of your event.
5. Provide a brief description of your event.
6. Enter the location where the event will take place.
7. List the event organizers.
8. Enter the contact person and their contact number.
9. Enter the contact email for your event.
10. Fill out the event status as approved, pending, or rejected.
11. If necessary, add any comments about your event.
12. If applicable, assign a specific tool or user to your event.
13. Finally, enter any comments or feedback about your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Type (`event_type`)**: Select the category of your event from the provided options.
* **Event Name (`event_name`)**: Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date (`event_date`)**: Enter the date of your event.
* **Start Time (`start_time`)**: Enter the start time of your event.
* **End Time (`end_time`)**: Enter the end time of your event.
* **Event Description (`event_description`)**: Provide a brief description of your event.
* **Event Location (`event_location`)**: Enter the location where your event will take place.
* **Event Organizers (`event_organizers`)**: List the people involved in organizing your event.
* **Contact Person (`contact_person`)**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Number (`contact_number`)**: Enter the contact number of the event organizer.
* **Contact Email (`contact_email`)**: Enter the contact email of the event organizer.
* **Event Status (`event_status`)**: Select the status of your event as approved, pending, or rejected.
* **Event Comments (`event_comments`)**: Add any comments or feedback about your event.
* **Assigned Tool (`assigned_tool`)**: If applicable, select the tool assigned to your event.
* **Assigned User (`assigned_user`)**: If applicable, select the user assigned to your event.
* **Comments (`comments`)**: Add any general comments or feedback about your event.
