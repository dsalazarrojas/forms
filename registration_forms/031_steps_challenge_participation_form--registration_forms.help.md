# steps_challenge_participation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants for an event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details, including your name, email, phone number, and company information (if applicable).
2. Confirm your participation and event details to ensure accuracy.
3. Review and confirm the details of other participants attending with you.
4. Add any notes or comments that you think are relevant.
5. Confirm your understanding of event details and company information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identity documents.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work or personal phone number.
* **Company Name**: If you are representing a company, enter the name of the company.
* **Employee Name**: If you are an employee of the company, enter your employee name (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter the company email address (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter the company phone number (if applicable).
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Consent**: Confirm that you have reviewed and agree to the participation terms and conditions.
* **Participants**: Select who will be participating in the event (you can choose from options such as Participant One, Participant Two, Participant Three).
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about the event or your participation.
* **Confirm Participation**: Confirm that you will participate in the event.
* **Confirm Event Details**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the event details.
* **Confirm Participant Details**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the participant details.
* **Confirm Company Details**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the company details.
* **Confirm Event Participants**: Confirm that you have reviewed and understood the event participant list.

Note: This form has several optional fields, but please fill them out if they are relevant to your participation. Also, be sure to review and confirm each field to ensure accuracy.
