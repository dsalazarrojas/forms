# Flower Arrangement Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for the Flower Arrangement Competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Name field with your full name.
2. Enter your Address in the Address field (optional).
3. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field (optional).
4. Enter your Email Address in the Email Address field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `username@example.com`).
5. Select your preferred Flower Arrangement Type from the list provided in the Flower Arrangement Type field.
6. Enter the name of the competition you are registering for in the Competition Name field.
7. Enter the date you would like to enter the competition in the Entry Date field (format: `YYYY-MM-DD`).
8. Enter the time you would like to enter the competition in the Entry Time field (format: `HH:MM:SS`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (User Name, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): If you have a preferred address, enter it here. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter a valid email address. This is how we will contact you for competition updates and information.
* **Flower Arrangement Type** (Flower Arrangement Type, select_one, required): Choose the type of flower arrangement you are entering. Select from the list provided:
	+ Flower Crown
	+ Flower Wreath
	+ Flower Bouquet
	+ Flower Basket
	+ Flower Vase
	+ Flower Collar
	+ Flower Corsage
	+ Flower Hairpiece
	+ Flower Garlands
	+ Flower Leis
* **Competition Name** (Competition Name, text, required): Enter the name of the competition you are registering for.
* **Entry Date** (Entry Date, date, required): Enter the date you would like to enter the competition in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Entry Time** (Entry Time, time, required): Enter the time you would like to enter the competition in the format `HH:MM:SS`.
