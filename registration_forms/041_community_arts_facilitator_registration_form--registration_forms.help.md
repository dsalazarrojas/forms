# Community Arts Facilitator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register community arts facilitators, collect their personal and professional information, and gather details about their facilitation experience and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as full name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select your primary art discipline and years of arts experience.
3. Choose the types of programs you are interested in facilitating (e.g., youth, adult, senior, etc.).
4. Indicate the age groups you prefer to work with.
5. Select your availability for facilitating programs.
6. Answer questions about your education and training, if applicable.
7. Attach a link to your portfolio or website, if desired.
8. Indicate if you have attached your resume.
9. Briefly explain why you are interested in being a community arts facilitator.
10. Confirm that you are willing to follow community guidelines and policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information (Registration Information, note, optional)**: This is a note field where you can provide any additional details about your registration.
* **Registration Date (Registration Date, date, required)**: Enter the date you are registering as a community arts facilitator.
* **Registration ID (Registration ID, text, required)**: Enter a unique reference number for your registration.
* **Personal Information (Personal Information, note, optional)**: This is a note field where you can provide your contact details such as street address, state or province, and zip code.
* **Full Name (Full Name, text, required)**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required)**: Enter your phone number.
* **City (City, text, required)**: Enter your city.
* **State (State, text, required)**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code (ZIP Code, text, optional)**: Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Arts Background (Arts Background, select_one, required)**: Choose your primary art discipline (e.g., visual arts, performing arts, etc.).
* **Years of Experience (Years of Experience, select_one, required)**: Select the number of years of arts experience you have.
* **Facilitation Experience (Facilitation Experience, select_one, required)**: Indicate your level of facilitation experience (e.g., extensive, some, limited, no experience).
* **Education and Training (Education and Training, text, optional)**: If applicable, describe your relevant education and training.
* **Program Preferences (Program Preferences, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the types of programs you are interested in facilitating (e.g., youth, adult, senior, etc.).
* **Age Groups (Age Groups, select_multiple, required)**: Select the age groups you prefer to work with.
* **Availability (Availability, select_multiple, required)**: Choose when you are available to facilitate programs.
* **Program Ideas (Program Ideas, text, optional)**: If you have ideas for programs, briefly describe them.
* **Materials Needed (Materials Needed, text, optional)**: If you need any materials, list them here.
* **Background Check (Background Check, select_one, required)**: Confirm that you are willing to undergo a background check.
* **References Available (References Available, select_one, required)**: Indicate if you can provide professional references.
* **Portfolio Link (Portfolio Link, text, optional)**: If you have a portfolio or website, enter the URL here.
* **Resume Attached (Resume Attached, select_one, required)**: Confirm if you have attached your resume.
* **Why Interested (Why Interested, text, required)**: Briefly explain why you are interested in being a community arts facilitator.
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: If you have any other information to share, enter it here.
* **Agreement (Agreement, select_one, required)**: Confirm that you agree to follow community guidelines and policies.

Note: The fields marked as "note" are not required but can be used for any additional comments or information. The fields marked as "select_multiple" or "select_one" are required and allow you to select multiple or one option from the provided list, respectively.
