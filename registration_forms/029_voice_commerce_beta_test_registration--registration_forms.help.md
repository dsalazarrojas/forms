# Voice Commerce Beta Test Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Voice Commerce Beta Test Registration form is used to register interested individuals for the beta test of the Voice Commerce platform. By completing this form, you will be able to provide feedback and help shape the development of the platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to contact you with updates and information about the beta test.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This is optional, but it will allow us to contact you with any urgent matters.
5. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. This will help us understand your age and demographic information.
6. Select your gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your location in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): This is your first name, which will be used to identify you.
* **Last Name** (text): This is your last name, which will also be used to identify you.
* **Email** (email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text): This is your phone number, which is optional but will help us contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Select your date of birth to help us understand your age and demographic information.
* **Gender** (select_one): Choose your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Location** (text): Enter your location, which will help us understand your regional preferences.

## Tips

* Please ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues with the form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will keep your information confidential and only use it for the purpose of the beta test.
* You will receive an email with instructions on how to access the beta test once your form is submitted.
* Please note that all fields except for "Date of Birth" are optional.
