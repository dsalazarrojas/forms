# Videography Club Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Videography Club Attendance Form is used for registering attendance at club meetings. It ensures that organizers have accurate and up-to-date records of who attended each meeting.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the **Meeting Date** of the meeting you attended by entering a valid date.
2. Choose the **Meeting Time** of the meeting, if applicable.
3. Enter the names of all **Attendees** who were present at the meeting, separated by commas.
4. Enter the **Videographer Name**, the person who led or hosted the meeting.
5. Add any additional **Comments** about the meeting.
6. If you wish to, enter the **Attendees Count**, the number of people present at the meeting.
7. Provide your **Email** address so that organizers can contact you regarding your attendance.
8. Enter your **Contact Number** for any additional communication.
9. Specify the **Location** where the meeting took place.
10. If you're a Videographer, enter your **Videographer ID** for identification purposes.
11. Finally, enter the name of the person who submitted the form, which will be used to confirm authenticity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the meeting you attended. If not required, this field will only be used for historical tracking of attendance records.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time of the meeting if available. Like the meeting date, this information will only be kept for historical purposes if not filled out.
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, text, required: false): List all attendees at the meeting, separated by commas. This field is not required but helps the club keep track of attendees.
* **Videographer Name** (`videographer_name`, text, required: false): The name of the person leading or hosting the meeting. This helps to identify who conducted the meeting and for any follow-up purposes.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Provide any additional comments or observations about the meeting.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`, number, required: false): If you know the exact number of attendees, please enter it here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address so that organizers can contact you for any follow-up or clarification.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Provide your contact number for any additional communication purposes.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Specify the location of the meeting, such as an office or event hall.
* **Videographer ID** (`videographer_id`, number, required: false): If you're a Videographer, enter your identification number for club record purposes.
* **Form Submitted By** (`form_submitted_by`, text, required: false): The name of the person who submitted the form, used for authenticity checks.

**Tips:**

- Please ensure that your email is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you're unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to ask your local club contact or the videographer who led the meeting.
- This form will be kept private and only for the purpose of club record keeping and follow-up communication.
