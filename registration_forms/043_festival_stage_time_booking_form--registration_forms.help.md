# Festival Stage Time Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help festival organizers and performers book time slots for performances on festival stages. It collects essential information to manage stage time allocations and performer scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form
To book a stage time slot, follow these steps:

1. **Review the form**: Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. **Fill in the required fields**: Enter the necessary information for each field, and ensure you understand the field labels and descriptions.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've completed the form, click the submit button to save your booking.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Stage Time Form 1**
* Label: Stage Time Form 1
* Type: Text
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: This is the first section of the form where you can provide any additional information about your performance. Enter your thoughts, comments, or any other relevant details.

### 2. **Time Allocations**
* Label: Time Allocations
* Type: Select Multiple
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: Here, you can indicate whether you want to book multiple time slots for your performance. Select "Yes" if you wish to book more than one time slot, and "No" if you're only booking one time slot.

### 3. **Performers**
* Label: Performers
* Type: Number
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: Enter the names of all performers who will be performing on your booked time slot, separated by commas.

### 4. **Start Time**
* Label: Start Time
* Type: Time
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: Enter the start time for your performance, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).

### 5. **End Time**
* Label: End Time
* Type: Time
* Required: Optional
* Explanation: Enter the end time for your performance, in 24-hour format (e.g., 15:00 for 3:00 PM).
