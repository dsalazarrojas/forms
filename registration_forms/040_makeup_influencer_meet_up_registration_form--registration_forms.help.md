# Makeup Influencer Meet Up Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Influencer Meet Up Registration Form is designed for individuals interested in attending a meetup for makeup influencers. The form collects information about attendees' event preferences and interests to ensure a successful and enjoyable event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the event date that you would like to attend.
3. Enter the event name.
4. Provide your name.
5. Enter your email address.
6. If you have a phone number, please enter it.
7. Select one or more social media platforms you have a presence on (e.g., Instagram, TikTok, Facebook).
8. Choose your preferences for the event (e.g., Foundation, Liquid, Cream Highlighter).
9. Enter any additional information you'd like to share.
10. Click "Submit" to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you would like to attend the meetup.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you would like to attend.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be known at the event.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address for event communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach you if needed.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, select_one, required/optional): Select one or more social media platforms you are active on (e.g., Instagram, TikTok, Facebook).
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose your preferences for the event (e.g., Foundation, Liquid, Cream Highlighter).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional information you would like to share, please enter it here.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable event experience.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please let us know in the "Additional Info" field.
* We'll be in touch with you shortly after submitting your registration to confirm your attendance.
