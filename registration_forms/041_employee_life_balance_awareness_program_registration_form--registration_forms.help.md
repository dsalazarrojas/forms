# Employee Life Balance Awareness Program Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Life Balance Awareness Program Registration Form is used to collect demographic and registration data from employees, helping the company to better understand their employee base and track registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Employee Id, if applicable.
2. Enter your Employee Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Select a category from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter your Date of Birth.
7. Select a time zone or schedule from the Time field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes in the Note field, if necessary.
9. (Optional) Enter who submitted the form in the Submitted By field.
10. (Optional) Enter who created the form in the Created By field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Id** (1, number, required: false): If you have an employee Id, please enter it here. This is a numeric identifier for the employee.
* **Employee Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): This is your unique email address associated with your work or personal account.
* **Phone** (4, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Category** (5, select_one, required: false): Select an option from the dropdown menu to categorize you within the organization.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (7, time, required: false): Select your time zone or work schedule.
* **Note** (8, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration, if necessary.
* **Submitted By** (9, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who submitted this form on your behalf.
* **Created By** (10, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person who created this form, if you know it.
