# Family Support Service Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Family Support Service Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals who are seeking support services for their families. Please review the form carefully and ensure you understand what information is required.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your user information, including your name and contact details, in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide information about your family members, including their names and ages, in the "Family Information" section.
3. Select the type of support you require from the options in the "Support Information" section.
4. Enter your address information, including your street address and postcode, in the "Address Information" section.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details so we can reach out to you regarding your support needs.
* **Family Information** (`Family Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide information about your family members, including their names and ages, so we can tailor our support services to your needs.
* **Support Information** (`Support Information`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the type of support you require, such as Family Support Worker or Home Visitor.
* **Address Information** (`Address Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address information, including your street address and postcode, so we can visit you or send support materials to your location.
* **Note** (`Note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes regarding your support needs or any other information you want to provide.

## Tips
* Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you select multiple options for the "Support Information" section, please make sure they are relevant and specific to your needs.
* If you have any issues or concerns while completing the form, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* The information collected will be kept confidential and used only for the purpose of providing support services to you and your family.
