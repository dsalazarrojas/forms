# Water Use Optimization Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in participating in the Water Use Optimization Program. The purpose of this form is to understand their interest in conservation programs and their experience with water use optimization technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Fill in your Address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your Zip Code in the "Zip Code" field.
7. Check your interest in conservation programs by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Conservation Program Interest" field.
8. If you have experience with water use optimization technologies, describe it in the "Water Use Optimization Technology Experience" field.
9. Select all conservation programs you have participated in from the "Conservation Programs Participated In" field.
10. Select all water use optimization programs you have participated in from the "Water Use Optimization Programs Participated In" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in this field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in this field.
* **Address**: Enter your address in this field.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code in this field.
* **Conservation Program Interest**: Indicate your interest in conservation programs by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Water Use Optimization Technology Experience**: If you have experience with water use optimization technologies, describe it in this field.
* **Conservation Programs Participated In**: Select all conservation programs you have participated in.
* **Water Use Optimization Programs Participated In**: Select all water use optimization programs you have participated in.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to contact us for clarification.
* Please note that this form is intended for information collection purposes only and will not be used for marketing or promotional purposes. 

Note: The required fields are marked with a "(required)" tag in the original YAML schema, but since all fields are currently marked as optional, we will not highlight this in the final guide.
