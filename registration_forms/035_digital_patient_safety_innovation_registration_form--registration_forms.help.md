# Digital Patient Safety Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Patient Safety Innovation Registration Form is designed to collect details about events related to digital patient safety innovations. This form helps healthcare professionals and organizations to stay informed about the latest developments and advancements in patient safety.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2. For fields with options, select the relevant information from the provided list.
3. If a field is not relevant, you can leave it blank or provide a brief explanation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, optional): Enter a brief title of the event, such as "Conference on Digital Patient Safety Innovations".
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event (e.g., Conference, Workshop, Symposium).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category(ies) of the event (e.g., Registration Forms, Event Registration Forms).
* **Event Status** (`event_status`, select_one, required): Select the status of the event (e.g., Confirmed, Not Confirmed, Canceled).
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the event.
