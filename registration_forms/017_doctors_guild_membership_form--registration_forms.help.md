# Doctors Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Doctors Guild Membership Form is a registration form designed to collect necessary information from doctors who wish to join the guild. This information will be used for membership registration and future communications.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Please enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
* Provide a valid Email address in the "Email" field, which will be used for communication.
* Select a Specialty from the dropdown menu in the "Specialty" field. Choose one that best describes your medical specialization.
* If applicable, enter your Years of Experience in the "Years of Experience" field.
* Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field, following the standard "dd/mm/yyyy" format.
* Enter a Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field, which will be used for contact purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (First Name) - **Text**
*   Enter your name, as it appears on your identification documents.

*   This field is not required.

### Last Name (Last Name) - **Text**
*   Enter your surname or last name as it appears on your identification documents.

*   This field is not required.

### Email (Email) - **Email**
*   Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.

*   This field is not required.

### Specialty (Specialty) - **Select One**
*   Choose one from the list of medical specialties to describe your area of expertise.

*   This field is not required.

### Years of Experience (Years of Experience) - **Number**
*   If applicable, enter your years of experience in the field of medicine.

*   This field is not required.

### Date of Birth (Date of Birth) - **Date**
*   Enter your date of birth following the "dd/mm/yyyy" format.

*   This field is not required.

### Phone Number (Phone Number) - **Text**
*   Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

*   This field is not required.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in correctly to avoid delays in processing your membership registration.
*   Make sure to use a valid email address and a working phone number to facilitate communication.
