# Event Rescheduling Preference Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from event attendees to understand their preferences for rescheduling events. Please fill out this form to help us better understand your needs and preferences for future event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all the required fields (marked as "Required" in the form).
2. Select your preferred day of the week for the event.
3. Choose a preferred time slot for the event.
4. If you have any additional comments or requests, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to send your preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Required): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **Day of Week** (Optional): Select a day of the week that works best for you to have the event.
	+ Option 1: Monday
	+ Option 2: Tuesday
	+ Option 3: Wednesday
	+ Option 4: Thursday
	+ Option 5: Friday
* **Preferred Time Slot** (Optional): Select a time slot that works best for you to have the event.
	+ Option 1: Morning (8:00 AM - 12:00 PM)
	+ Option 2: Afternoon (12:00 PM - 4:00 PM)
	+ Option 3: Evening (4:00 PM - 8:00 PM)
	+ Option 4: Other (please specify in the "Additional Comments" field)
* **Additional Comments** (Optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please type them here.
