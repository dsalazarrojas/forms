# Special Needs Education Training Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Needs Education Training Admission Form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to be admitted into a special needs education training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Please ensure that you have not already been admitted into the program before submitting this form.
* Fill in your personal information accurately.
* Enter your contact information for communication and follow up.
* If you are not the individual completing the form, please ensure you have their consent to do so.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication.
* **Contact number** (Contact number, text, required: false): Enter a contact number that can be used for follow up.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you are unable to complete the form online, please contact us to discuss alternative options.
* If you are a parent/guardian completing the form on behalf of the individual, please ensure you have their consent before proceeding.
