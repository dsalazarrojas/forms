# Flood Risk Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to be aware of flood risk in their area. It is not a certification or official document, but rather a tool for awareness and information collection.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Registration information (page 1)
2. Provide your Last Name (page 2)
3. Enter your Email (page 3)
4. Enter your Phone number (page 4)
5. Enter your Location (page 5)
6. Enter your Date of Birth (page 6)
7. Add any Notes (page 7)
8. Choose your Email Preferences (page 8)
9. Indicate your Flood Risk (page 9)
10. Provide your Emergency Contact information (pages 10-13)
11. Choose your Preferred Language and Medium (pages 14-15)
12. Indicate if you are Flood Fed (page 16)
13. Choose your Preferred Format (page 17)
14. Provide any additional Emergency Contact information (pages 18-22)
15. Add any additional Notes (page 23)
16. Upload any supporting documents (page 24)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration** (page 1): This is the first page of the form where you can fill in your Registration information. This field is optional.
* **Last Name** (page 2): Enter your last name in this field. This is a required field.
* **Email** (page 3): Enter your email address in this field. This is a required field.
* **Phone** (page 4): Enter your phone number in this field. This is an optional field.
* **Location** (page 5): Enter your location or address in this field. This is an optional field.
* **Date of Birth** (page 6): Enter your date of birth in this field. This is an optional field.
* **Note** (page 7): This field is for any additional notes or information you want to provide. This is an optional field.
* **Email Preferences** (page 8): Choose how you prefer to receive updates from us. You can select multiple options.
* **Flood Risk** (page 9): Indicate your flood risk level from the provided options. This is an optional field.
* **Emergency Contact** (page 10-13): Provide information about your emergency contact. You can include their name, address, phone number, email, and relationship.
* **Emergency Phone** (page 11): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email** (page 12): Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Emergency Location** (page 13): Enter your emergency contact's location.
* **Preferred Language** (page 14): Choose your preferred language for communication.
* **Preferred Medium** (page 15): Choose your preferred medium for communication (e.g., email, phone, or SMS).
* **Flood Fed** (page 16): Indicate if you are flood fed or have flood insurance. This is an optional field.
* **Preferred Format** (page 17): Choose the format you prefer to receive updates (e.g., PDF, Excel, or Word).
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (page 18): Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family, friend, or neighbor).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (page 19): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (page 20): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (page 21): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (page 22): Enter your emergency contact's email.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (page 23): Indicate your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., family, friend, or neighbor).
* **Notes** (page 24): This field is for any additional notes or information you want to provide. This is an optional field.
* **Upload Document** (page 25): You can upload any supporting documents if required. This is an optional field.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Choose your preferred language and medium for communication.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling in your contact information.
* You can upload any supporting documents if required.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
