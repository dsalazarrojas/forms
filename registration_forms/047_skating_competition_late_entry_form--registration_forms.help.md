# Skating Competition Late Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Skating Competition Late Entry Form is designed to allow participants to register for the competition outside of the initial registration period. This form will collect necessary information to process your registration and ensure you can participate in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details in the **Participant Details** section.
2. Enter your event details in the **Event Details** section.
3. Enter your **Contact Info** in case we need to reach you.
4. Provide the **Emergency Contact** person's details in case we need to contact them during the competition.
5. Fill out your **Skating Info** section with your skating experience and qualifications.
6. Enter your **Registration Status** details in the box, if you are registering for a specific event or session.
7. If applicable, provide your **Payment Details** for the registration fee.
8. Lastly, include any **Additional Info** that might be relevant for us to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This is where you will enter your basic information, such as name and age group.
  * Type: `text` (`participant_details`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Event Details**: Please enter the event name and session you are registering for.
  * Type: `text` (`event_details`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact name, email, and phone number so we can reach you.
  * Type: `text` (`contact_info`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name, relationship, and contact info of your emergency contact person.
  * Type: `text` (`emergency_contact`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Skating Info**: Please provide your relevant skating experience, qualifications, and any other relevant info.
  * Type: `text` (`skating_info`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Registration Status**: This is where you will confirm your registration status for the event.
  * Type: `text` (`registration_status`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Payment Details**: If you are paying the registration fee, please enter the payment details here.
  * Type: `text` (`payment_details`)
  * Required: `false`
* **Additional Info**: If you have any additional info that would help us process your registration, enter it here.
  * Type: `text` (`additional_info`)
  * Required: `false`
