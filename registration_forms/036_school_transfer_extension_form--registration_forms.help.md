# School Transfer Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Transfer Extension Form is a document designed to facilitate the transfer process for students between schools, enabling parents to provide essential information about their child's school transfer requests. This form ensures a smooth transfer process by collecting relevant details, making it easier to process and manage transfers efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure you have all required and optional fields filled out before submitting the form. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*) in their labels.
2.  Fill in your Parent Name in the designated field.
3.  Indicate whether your child is currently attending school or not in the "School Current Attendance" field.
4.  If your child is not currently attending, select "Yes" in the "Reason for Transfer" field and explain the reason in the text area below.
5.  Enter your child's student ID in the "Student ID" field.
6.  Specify the current school year for your child.
7.  Enter the date of your request for school transfer in "School Transfer Request Date" field.
8.  In the "Reason for Extension" field, explain the reason for the transfer extension request.
9.  Select the status of the transfer in the "School Transfer Status" field.
10.  Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Name
*   The field for the parent's name is a text field where you can input your name for your child's school transfer records.

### School Current Attendance
*   This field helps determine if your child is attending school currently or not. Select 'Yes' if your child is in school and 'No' if not.

### Student ID
*   The Student ID field is where you can provide your child's unique identifier number.

### Current School Year
*   This field is used to specify the current school year for your child. Select 'Yes' if they are currently in the current school year and 'No' if not.

### School Transfer Request Date
*   This date field is used to indicate when the school transfer request was made.

### Reason for Transfer
*   If your child is not attending school, this field is required to provide a reason for the transfer.

### Student Previous School Name
*   This field is where you can provide the name of the school your child previously attended.

### School Previous School Address
*   Enter the address of the school your child previously attended.

### School Current School Name
*   Enter the name of the school your child currently attends.

### School Current School Address
*   Provide the address of the school your child currently attends.

### School Previous School ID
*   Enter the ID of the school your child previously attended.

### Student Previous School ID
*   Enter your child's ID from their previous school.

### School Current School ID
*   Enter the ID of the school your child currently attends.

### Reason for Extension
*   This field is where you can give a reason for the transfer extension request.

### School Transfer Status
*   Select the current status of the transfer in this field, which can be Awaiting, Approved, or Rejected.
