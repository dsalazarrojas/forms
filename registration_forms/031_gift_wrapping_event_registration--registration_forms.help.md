# Gift Wrapping Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gift Wrapping Event Registration Form is designed to gather essential participant information for the event. This form will be used to manage participant registration efficiently and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and honestly. This will help us manage participant information accurately and prevent any potential issues with your registration.
2. Ensure that you have filled out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*). These fields are required for successful registration.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (participant_name)**: The participant's name is the primary identifier for the registration process. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Email (participant_email)**: Your email address will be used to communicate important event details, reminders, and updates with you. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone)**: Your phone number will be used for any urgent communication or follow-up. Please enter your primary phone number.
* **Gift Card Value (gift_card_value)**: If applicable, please enter the value of the gift card you are registering for. This will help us manage your gift card accurately.
* **Participant Information (participant_info)**: This field is an optional text area where you can provide any additional information about yourself or your gift card. Please use this space to share any relevant details that may help us serve you better.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are registering for the correct event and that all information is accurate and complete.
* If you are experiencing any issues or have questions, please contact us for assistance.
* We will verify all submitted information to ensure accuracy and completeness.
