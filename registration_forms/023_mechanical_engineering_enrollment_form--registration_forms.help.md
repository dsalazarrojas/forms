# Mechanical Engineering Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mechanical Engineering Enrollment Form is used to collect information from students who are interested in enrolling in the Mechanical Engineering program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form and read through each page carefully.
2.  Fill out each page according to your needs.
3.  Ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
4.  Review your completed form for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Mechanical Engineering Program Application
**Mechanical Engineering Program Application (text)** (Optional) - This field is intended for students to briefly introduce themselves and explain why they are interested in the Mechanical Engineering program.

### 2. Program
**Program (Select Multiple)** (Optional) - This field allows students to select one or more programs of interest. Please choose the program(s) that you are interested in from the provided options.

### 3. Student Name
**Student Name (text)** (Optional) - This field is for the student to enter their name.

### 4. Email
**Email (email)** (Optional) - This field is for the student to enter a valid email address.

### 5. Contact Number
**Contact Number (text)** (Optional) - This field is for the student to enter a contact number, if applicable.

### 6. Address
**Address (Note)** (Optional) - This field is for the student to enter their address.

### 7. Preferred Date
**Preferred Date (date)** (Optional) - This field is intended for students to select a preferred date for their program.

### 8. Expected Date
**Expected Date (date)** (Optional) - This field is intended for students to select an expected date for their program.

### 9. Preferred Time
**Preferred Time (time)** (Optional) - This field is intended for students to select a preferred time for their program.

### 10. Expected Time
**Expected Time (time)** (Optional) - This field is intended for students to select an expected time for their program.

### 11. Comments
**Comments (text)** (Optional) - This field is for students to enter any additional comments or information about their application.
