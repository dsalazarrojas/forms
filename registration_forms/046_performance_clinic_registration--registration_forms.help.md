# Performance Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants to register for a performance clinic. Please fill out this form to provide your details and express your preferences for the clinic.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Enter your details in the "Participant Details" page.
3. Select your preferred clinics in the "Preferred Clinics" page.
4. Add any additional information in the "Notes" page (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`Participant Details`, `text`, required/optional): This page is the first step to collect your basic information. Please provide your:
	+ Full name
	+ Contact email
	+ Contact phone number

### Preferred Clinics
* **Preferred Clinics** (`Preferred Clinics`, `text`, required/optional): In this page, you can select up to three preferred clinics from the available options. You can:
	+ Choose your top three preferred clinics
	+ Use the search function to find clinics by name or location
	+ Select clinics that best fit your needs

### Notes
* **Notes** (`Notes`, `text`, required/optional): This is an optional page where you can provide any additional information you'd like to share with the clinic staff. You can:
	+ Add any relevant details about your condition or medical history
	+ Ask questions or make requests to the clinic staff
	+ Provide any other information that might be helpful for your clinic experience

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your clinic experience is smooth and efficient.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact the clinic staff.
