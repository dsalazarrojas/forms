# Student Allergy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Allergy Registration Form is designed to provide detailed information about a student's allergies, ensuring proper handling and safety in case of allergy-related emergencies. This form helps teachers, medical staff, and school administrators understand the severity and type of allergies, treatment plans, and contact information for emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select one or multiple options for "Student Signature" and "Parent Signature" fields according to your role.
2. Fill in the student's basic information such as "Student Name", "Student School ID", and "Teacher School ID".
3. Choose the correct "Allergy Type" from the provided options.
4. Describe the "Description of Allergy" in detail.
5. Select the "Allergy Severity" level.
6. Provide a "Treatment Plan" if applicable.
7. Enter "Doctor Contact Information" and "Emergency Contact Information" for each allergy.
8. List "Allergies List" if applicable.
9. Finally, sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Allergy Type** (`Allergic to food`, `Allergic to environmental allergen`, `Allergic to insect stings`, `Allergic to medication`, `Allergic to plant`, `Allergic to insect`, `Allergic to metal`, `Allergic to latex`): Choose one or multiple options to specify the type of allergy the student has.
* **Description of Allergy** (`Please provide a detailed description of the allergy`): Provide a comprehensive description of the allergy, including symptoms and triggers.
* **Allergy Severity** (`Mild`, `Moderate`, `Severe`): Select the level of severity for each allergy.
* **Treatment Plan** (`What treatment do you have or are considering for this allergy?`): Describe the treatment plan for each allergy.
* **Doctor Contact Information** (`Name, Phone, and Email of your doctor`): Enter the contact information for the student's doctor.
* **Emergency Contact Information** (`Name, Phone, and Email of person responsible for emergency situations`): Enter the contact information for emergency situations.
* **Allergies List** (`List of allergies if any`): List all other allergies the student may have, if any.
* **Student Signature** (`Student`, `Parent`, `Guardian`): Select "Student", "Parent", or "Guardian" according to your role.
* **Parent Signature** (`Parent`, `Guardian`, `Both`): Select "Parent", "Guardian", or "Both" according to your role.
* **Date** (`null`): Fill in the date you submit the form.
* **Time** (`null`): Fill in the time you submit the form.
* **Submitted By** (`null`): Enter your name.
* **Submitted On** (`null`): Fill in the date you submit the form.
* **Submitted By User** (`null`): Enter your name.
* **Submitted On Time** (`null`): Fill in the time you submit the form.
* **User Information** (`null`): Enter your contact information.
* **User Signature** (`Teacher`, `Parent`, `Guardian`, `Student`): Select "Teacher", "Parent", "Guardian", or "Student" according to your role.
* **Student School ID** (`Provide your student school ID`): Enter your student school ID.
* **Teacher School ID** (`Provide teacher school ID`): Enter your teacher school ID.
* **Student Name** (`Name of the student`): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Name 2** (`Provide name of student`): Enter the name of the student.
* **Teacher** (`null`): Enter your name as a teacher.
* **Teacher's Name** (`Teacher's name`): Enter your teacher's name.

**Tips**
* Please ensure accuracy and completeness in filling out this form, as it is critical for emergency situations.
* If you are unsure about any information, please ask your doctor or teacher for guidance.
* This form is a one-time submission form. Please submit it only once for each student.
