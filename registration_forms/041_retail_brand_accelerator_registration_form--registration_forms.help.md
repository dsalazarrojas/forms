# Retail Brand Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about retail brands for registration purposes. It is used to create a record of the brand details and assign tasks or projects related to the brand.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Retail Brand Details** field with a brief description of the brand.
2. Select the **Business Stage** of the brand from the dropdown options (Early Stage, Growth Stage, Mature Stage).
3. Describe your **Goals** for the brand in the text field.
4. Enter your **Brand Name** as it appears on your packaging or marketing materials.
5. Select the **Brand Type** from the available options (although currently, this field is optional).
6. Enter your **Address**, including street address and other relevant details.
7. Enter your **Zip Code** for the brand's location.
8. Add your **Website** URL if applicable.
9. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
10. Enter your **Phone Number** for communication.
11. Enter the **Date** and **Time** of submission.
12. Add any **Notes** regarding the submission.
13. You can optionally select **Assigned Tool**, **Assigned User**, **Assigned Group**, **Assigned Project**, **Assigned Status**, and **Assigned Priority** to associate with this brand submission.
14. Add any additional **Comments** about the submission.
15. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** of the form submission.
16. Select the **Submission Status** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Retail Brand Details**: A brief description of the brand.
* **Business Stage**: The stage at which the brand is in (Early Stage, Growth Stage, Mature Stage).
* **Goals**: A description of the brand's goals.
* **Brand Name**: The name of the brand as it appears on packaging or marketing materials.
* **Brand Type**: The type of brand (optional).
* **Address**: The brand's address, including street address and other details.
* **Zip Code**: The zip code of the brand's location.
* **Website**: The brand's website URL if applicable.
* **Email**: The contact email for the brand.
* **Phone Number**: The brand's phone number for communication.
* **Date**: The date of submission.
* **Time**: The time of submission.
* **Notes**: Additional notes about the submission.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optionally assign a tool to the brand (optional).
* **Assigned User**: Optionally assign a user to the brand (optional).
* **Assigned Group**: Optionally assign a group to the brand (optional).
* **Assigned Project**: Optionally assign a project to the brand (optional).
* **Assigned Status**: Optionally assign a status to the brand (optional).
* **Assigned Priority**: Optionally assign a priority to the brand (optional).
* **Comments**: Additional comments about the submission.
* **Submission Date**: The date of submission.
* **Submission Time**: The time of submission.
* **Submission Status**: The status of submission (optional). 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your submissions for accuracy.
* Review your submission before submitting.
