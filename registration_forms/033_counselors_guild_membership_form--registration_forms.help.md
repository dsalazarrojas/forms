# Counselors Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Counselors Guild Membership Form is used to collect registration information from potential members. This form is intended to be filled out by individuals who wish to join the Counselors Guild as a member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Provide your Organization name.
4. Choose your Role by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Role dropdown menu.
5. Enter your contact number.
6. Select the type of membership you wish to join (if any) by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Membership Type dropdown menu.
7. Choose how you will pay for your membership by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the Payment Method dropdown menu.
8. If you are paying by other means, enter the method in the Other Payment Method field.
9. Select the Payment Date for your membership.
10. Once you have filled in all the required and optional fields, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or ID.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or ID.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter a valid email address that is checked regularly for important Guild updates and notifications.

### Organization
* **Organization** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your organization if you are affiliated with one.

### Role
* **Role** (`select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you hold a specific role, 'No' otherwise.

### Contact Number
* **Contact Number** (`text`, optional): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.

### Membership Type
* **Membership Type** (`select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply for the type of membership you wish to join.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you will be paying for your membership through our official channels, 'No' otherwise.

### Other Payment Method
* **Other Payment Method** (`text`, optional): If you are paying through other means, enter the method here.

### Payment Date
* **Payment Date** (`date`, optional): Select the date you will be paying for your membership.
