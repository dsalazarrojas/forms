# Global Health Summit Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals or entities that want to reserve a spot at the Global Health Summit. It is meant to collect the necessary information to accommodate their reservation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your First Name in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field. This field is not required.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field. This is not required.
5. Choose a Reservation Date for your summit spot in the "Reservation Date" field. This field is a date picker and is not required.
6. Select a Reservation Time for your summit spot in the "Reservation Time" field. This field is a time picker and is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name, for example, John.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Enter your last name, for example, Doe.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address, for example, johndoe@example.com.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, for example, 123-456-7890.
* **Reservation Date** (Reservation Date, date, optional): Select a date for your summit spot, using the date picker.
* **Reservation Time** (Reservation Time, time, optional): Select a time for your summit spot, using the time picker.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you have already submitted your reservation form, you will not be able to submit again.
* Double-check your information before submitting, as this will be used to finalize your summit reservation.
