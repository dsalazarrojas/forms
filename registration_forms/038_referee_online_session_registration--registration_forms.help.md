# Referee Online Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Referee Online Session Registration form is designed for referees to provide their details and register for online sessions. This form is essential for the organization to track and manage referee information and sessions effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your valid Email address.
3. Enter your Phone Number, if you wish to be contacted.
4. Fill in your Address, if applicable.
5. Select your Region of operation.
6. Choose your Session Date.
7. Select your Session Time.
8. Choose your Referee Level (High or Low).
9. Select your Referee Category (Yes or No).
10. Choose the Training Sessions you are participating in (Yes or No).
11. Select your Referee Association.
12. Enter your League Name.
13. Enter your Region (optional).
14. Fill in the Session IDs, if applicable.
