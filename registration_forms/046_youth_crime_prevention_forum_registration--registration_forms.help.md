# Youth Crime Prevention Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and collect information from individuals interested in attending or participating in events related to the Youth Crime Prevention Forum. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Select your Date of Birth from the calendar in "Date Of Birth" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Select the type of event you are interested in from the "Event Type" options.
7. Select the events you are interested in attending from the "Event Interest" options.
8. If required, select the date and time of the event you are interested in from the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields respectively.
9. Enter the date of your registration in the "Registration Date" field.
10. Select whether you are interested in attending or not from the "Attendance Interest" options.
11. Enter the location of the event in the "Event Location" field.
12. Add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
13. Select the category of the form (Registration, Event, or Contact) from the "Category" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to identify yourself.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to identify yourself.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Select your date of birth from the calendar.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to contact you.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number for future references.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of event you are interested in (Interest, Information, Other, Support).
* **Event Interest** (`event_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the events you are interested in attending (Interest, Information, Other, Support).
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): If required, select the date of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): If required, select the time of the event you are interested in.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date of your registration.
* **Attendance Interest** (`attendance_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select whether you are interested in attending or not (Interested, Confirmed, Unable to attend).
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select the category of the form (Registration Forms, Event Forms, Contact Forms).
