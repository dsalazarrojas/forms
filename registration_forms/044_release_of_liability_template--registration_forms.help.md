# Release Of Liability Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are signing a release of liability contract. This form ensures that the individual understands and agrees to the terms of the contract, which outlines their liability and responsibilities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you understand the terms of the contract.
2. Complete the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*), for your details as a submitter and contact information.
3. Select your citizenship status from the options provided.
4. Choose your ethnicity from the options provided.
5. If applicable, complete your educational status.
6. If applicable, report your SAT, ACT, ASVAB, or LSAT scores.
7. Read and select the consent option that applies to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter details (\*):** Please enter your details as a submitter.
* **Contact information (\*):** Please enter your contact information, including your name, address, and contact number.
* **Citizenship:** Please select your citizenship status:
	+ Choose from the dropdown options (e.g., US, Non-US)
* **Ethnicity:** Please select your ethnicity:
	+ Choose from the dropdown options (e.g., White, Black or African American, Hispanic or Latino, Asian or Pacific Islander, American Indian or Alaska Native, Other, Prefer not to answer)
* **Educational status:** If you are an active student or student loan borrower, please select from the options provided:

* **SAT score:** If you took the SAT, please enter the score.
* **ACT score:** If you took the ACT, please enter the score.
* **ASVAB score:** If you took the ASVAB, please enter the score.
* **LSAT score:** If you took the LSAT, please enter the score.
