# Event Attendance And Menu Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event planners to collect information from attendees about their event attendance and menu selection. It helps organizers gather details such as RSVPs, guest counts, and dietary restrictions to tailor the event accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and the questions asked on each page to understand what information is required from you as an attendee.
2. Fill in the form with your responses, making sure to provide accurate and complete information for each question.
3. If you have any doubts or questions, refer to the field explanations below or contact the form creators for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Attendance And Menu Selection Form** (`event_attendance_and_menu_selection_page_1`, text, required: false): 
Please select your response to the event attendance and menu selection question. This could be "I will attend" or "I won't attend", and also choose your preferred menu items.
* **Drag And Drop Interface Form** (`event_attendance_and_menu_selection_page_2`, text, required: false): 
This page asks you to confirm your responses from the previous page, including RSVPs, guest counts, menu choices, and dietary restrictions. Please verify the information you provided earlier to ensure accuracy.
* **RSVPs, Guest Counts, Menu Choices, and Dietary Restrictions** (`event_attendance_and_menu_selection_page_3`, text, required: false): 
This is a summary page to review your responses. Please review your RSVPs, guest counts, menu choices, and dietary restrictions before submitting the form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your responses are complete.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form creators.
- If you're still unsure about any field, try to use your best judgment and provide the most accurate information you can.
