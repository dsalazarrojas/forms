# Satellite Internet Service Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for customers to sign up for our satellite internet service. Please fill out the form below to provide the required information and complete your sign-up process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the field provided.
2. Enter your email address in the field provided.
3. Enter your phone number in the field provided.
4. Enter your address in the field provided.
5. Select your desired installation date from the calendar.
6. Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
7. If you are a provider, confirm that you have been authorized to sign up for the service on behalf of the customer.
8. Add any additional notes or comments in the field provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **customer_name** (customer_name, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **customer_email** (customer_email, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and information about your service.
* **customer_phone** (customer_phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you about your service.
* **customer_address** (customer_address, text, optional): Enter your address to help us verify your identity and set up your service.
* **installation_date** (installation_date, date, optional): Select your desired installation date for your satellite internet service.
* **customer_confirmation** (customer_confirmation, select_one, optional): Confirm that you agree to our terms and conditions.
* **provider_confirmation** (provider_confirmation, select_one, optional): If you are signing up for service on behalf of a customer, confirm that you have been authorized to do so.
* **notes** (notes, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your sign-up process or service needs.
