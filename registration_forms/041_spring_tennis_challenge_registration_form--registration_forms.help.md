# Spring Tennis Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spring Tennis Challenge Registration Form is used to register participants for the Spring Tennis Challenge. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information to ensure correct registration and to avoid duplication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Provide your **Date of Birth** in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" or your age if you're not comfortable sharing your date of birth.
3. Enter your **Email** address to receive important updates and notifications.
4. Enter your **Phone number** for communication and updates.
5. Choose the **Challenge Type** you wish to participate in by selecting one of the available options (Challenge Singles, Doubles, or Mixed Doubles).
6. Select your division(s) of play by choosing one or more from the options (A, B, C, D).
7. Select your playing level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
8. If applicable, order T-Shirts by selecting the size and color you prefer.
9. Provide any comments for the T-Shirt order, if needed.
10. In the **Challenge Comments** field, provide any relevant information about your participation in the challenge.
11. If you have an emergency contact, fill in their name, phone number, and relation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD" or your age if you're not comfortable sharing your date of birth.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication and updates.
* **Challenge Type** (`challenge_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of challenge you wish to participate in (Challenge Singles, Doubles, or Mixed Doubles).
* **Division** (`division`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more divisions of play (A, B, C, D).
* **Level** (`level`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your playing level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **T-Shirts Ordered** (`tshirts_ordered`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of T-Shirts ordered.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`tshirts_size`, `select_one`, optional): Select the size of T-Shirt you prefer.
* **T-Shirt Color** (`tshirts_color`, `select_one`, optional): Select the color of T-Shirt you prefer.
* **T-Shirt Color Option** (`tshirts_color_option`, `select_one`, optional): This field seems to be a duplicate of T-Shirt Color. You can select a combination of T-Shirt colors or an option specific to this field.
* **T-Shirt Comments** (`tshirts_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any comments about the T-Shirt order, if needed.
* **Division Comments** (`division_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant comments about your division selection.
* **Challenge Comments** (`challenge_comments`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant comments about your participation in the challenge.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, `text`, optional): Enter the relation of your emergency contact.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure correct registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
* You can order T-Shirts if you wish to participate in the challenge.
