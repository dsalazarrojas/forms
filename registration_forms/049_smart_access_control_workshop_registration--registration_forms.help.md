# Smart Access Control Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register users for the Smart Access Control Workshop. It collects basic user information and preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Information** on the first page.
2. On the second page, select your **Topic Interests** to help us tailor the workshop to your needs.
3. Choose your **Preferred Date** for the workshop (if available).
4. Enter your **Phone Number** for any follow-up communications.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to help us identify you.
* **Topic Interests (select multiple)**: Choose the topics that interest you to help us plan the workshop content.
	+ 'Yes' indicates you are interested in that topic.
	+ 'No' indicates you are not interested in that topic.
* **Preferred Date (date)**: Select the date you would prefer to attend the workshop. If no dates are available, you can leave this field blank.
* **Phone Number (text)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about workshop updates or follow-ups.

## Tips
Please ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date to ensure a smooth workshop experience. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask.
