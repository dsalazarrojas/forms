# Voter Registration Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and demographic information from individuals who want to register to access the voter registration portal.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the pages to select the relevant information for your registration.
2. Fill in the required and optional fields according to the instructions provided.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **User Registration** 
(User Registration, Text, Required/Optional: Required)
This field is the first step in the registration process. Please provide your name as it appears on official documents.

### **Contact Information** 
(Contact Information, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
Please provide your primary contact method (email, phone number, or email).

### **Address** 
(Address, Text, Required/Optional: Optional)
If you have a physical address, please provide it for verification purposes. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Review your answers before submission.
* Ensure that your contact information is accurate.
