# Digital Room Allocation Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Room Allocation Training Registration Form is a tool used to collect information from participants for digital room allocation training. This form helps trainers to gather essential details about each participant and ensure that they agree to the terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, such as name, email, phone number, job title, company, and department.
2. If applicable, enter the Training Session ID to connect your registration with the corresponding training session.
3. Select the date and location of the training session you will be attending.
4. Check the box to agree with the terms and conditions.
5. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Participant Agreed Terms Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### <u>Training Details (Page 1)</u>
* **Training Details** (`digital_room_allocation_training_registration_form_page_1`, `text`, not required): This is the title of the training session. You can enter the name of the training session, e.g., "Digital Room Allocation Training".
### <u>Participant Name</u>
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, not required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
### <u>Participant Email</u>
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, not required): Enter your official email address associated with your company.
### <u>Participant Phone</u>
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, not required): Enter your office phone number or mobile phone number.
### <u>Participant Job Title</u>
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, `text`, not required): Enter your current job title at your company.
### <u>Participant Company</u>
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, `text`, not required): Enter your company name.
### <u>Participant Department</u>
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, `text`, not required): Enter your department name.
### <u>Training Session ID</u>
* **Training Session ID** (`training_session_id`, `number`, not required): If you have a Training Session ID, enter it here to connect your registration with the corresponding training session.
### <u>Training Session Date</u>
* **Training Session Date** (`training_session_date`, `date`, not required): Enter the date of the training session you will be attending.
### <u>Training Location</u>
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, `text`, not required): Enter the location of the training session.
### <u>Agreed Terms</u>
* **Agreed Terms** (`participant_agreed_terms`, `select_multiple`, not required): Check the box to agree with the terms and conditions. You can also select "Other" if you have additional comments or concerns.
### <u>Agreed Terms Note</u>
* **Agreed Terms Note** (`participant_agreed_terms_note`, `note`, not required): Enter any additional comments or notes related to your agreed terms.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your participant details are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the training administrator.
* Make sure to review and agree with the terms and conditions before proceeding.
* You can enter any additional comments or notes in the "Agreed Terms Note" field if you need to provide further clarification.
