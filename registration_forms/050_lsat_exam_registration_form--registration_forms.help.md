# LSAT Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The LSAT Exam Registration Form is designed to collect demographic information from students registering for the LSAT exam. This information will be used to process the registration and provide personalized test experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it for verification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Test Date** (`test_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you want to take the LSAT exam. This will help schedule your exam date.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the testing location for your exam. Options are: test center, home school, or other ( specify in the other field).
* **Test Center** (`test_center`, select_one, required/optional): Select the test center for your exam from the options: New York, Chicago, or London.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's name as it appears on their ID or passport.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's ID number or student ID number (if applicable).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's address for mailing purposes.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your student's email address (if different from the email field above).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's phone number (if different from the phone field above).
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's address (if different from the address field above).
* **Student's Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required/optional): Enter your student's date of birth for age verification.
* **Student SSN** (`student_ssn`, select_one, required/optional): Select your student's SSN from the options: SSN, NIF, or Other (specify in the other field).
* **Student Ethnicity** (`student_ethnicity`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your student's ethnicity from the options: Asian, African, European, Latin American, or Other (specify in the other field).
* **Student Citizenship** (`student_citizenship`, select_one, required/optional): Select your student's citizenship from the options: US Citizen, Green Card Holder, or Not a Citizen.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your student's gender from the options: Male, Female, or Other.
* **Student Age** (`student_age`, number, required/optional): Enter your student's age.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your student's grade level from the options: 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, 12th, 13th, 14th, or Other (specify in the other field).
* **Student High School** (`student_high_school`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's high school name or name of the school they attended.
* **Student College** (`student_college`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's college name or name of the college they attended.
* **Student University** (`student_university`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's university name or name of the university they attended.
* **Test Date 2** (`test_date_2`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you want to take the LSAT exam 2 (if applicable).
* **Student Law School** (`student_law_school`, text, required/optional): Enter your student's law school name or name of the law school they attended.
* **Student Law Degree** (`student_law_degree`, select_one, required/optional): Select your student's law degree from the options: JD, LLM, or Other (specify in the other field).
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required/optional): Press this button to submit your form and complete the registration process.
