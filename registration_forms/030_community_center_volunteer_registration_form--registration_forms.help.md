# Community Center Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and professional information from individuals interested in becoming volunteers at the community center. The form will be used to assess suitability, schedule volunteers, and communicate with them about opportunities and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your full name, email address, and primary contact phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth, residential address, and preferred days of availability to volunteer.
3. Specify the number of hours you can commit to volunteering each week.
4. Select which program area you are most interested in volunteering for (e.g., Youth Programs, Senior Services, etc.).
5. Describe any relevant skills or expertise you bring to the community center.
6. Provide information about your previous volunteer experience.
7. Digital sign the form by typing your full name, confirming that the provided information is correct.
8. Select whether you want to receive the community center's newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Volunteer** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for follow-up and results delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number for communication.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Residential Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Availability and Interests** (Note, Optional): Select the programs and activities you are interested in or have experience with.
* **Which days of the week are you available to volunteer** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the days you are available to volunteer.
* **How many hours per week can you commit to volunteering** (Number, Required): Enter the number of hours you can volunteer each week.
* **Which program area are you most interested in volunteering for** (Select One, Required): Choose the program area you are most interested in.
* **What key skills or expertise would you bring to the community center** (Text, Required): Describe any relevant skills or expertise you have.
* **Describe any previous volunteer experience** (Text, Required): Provide information about your previous volunteer experience.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)** (Text, Required): Type your full name, confirming that the provided information is correct.
* **Date of Registration** (Date, Required): This field will be populated automatically.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you want to receive the newsletter.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the person not living with you who should be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter the emergency contact phone number.
* **Relationship to Volunteer** (Text, Required): Describe your relationship to the volunteer.
