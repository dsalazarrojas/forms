# workforce_culture_innovation_challenge_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather feedback, ideas, and details from participants of the Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read this help guide to understand the purpose and fields of the form.
2. Determine if you are a participant in the Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge.
3. If you have feedback, ideas, or details to share, select "Yes" on the "Submit Form" field.
4. If you are a participant with something to share, fill out the "name", "email", "innovation idea", and "participant details" fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Submit Form
**Submit Form (select_one)**: Select "Yes" if you are a participant with feedback, ideas, or details to share.
### Name (text)
**Name (text)**: Enter your name as a participant in the Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge.
### Email (email)
**Email (email)**: Enter your email address as a participant in the Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge.
### Innovation Idea (note)
**Innovation Idea (note)**: Describe your idea for innovation in the workplace.
### Participant Details (text)
**Participant Details (text)**: Share any additional details about your participation in the Workforce Culture Innovation Challenge.
## Tips
* Make sure to complete all required fields (none required).
* Be as detailed and descriptive as possible in your feedback, ideas, and details.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact your supervisor or challenge administrator.
