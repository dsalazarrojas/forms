# Corporate Luncheon Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Luncheon Guest Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from guests attending a corporate luncheon event. The form is used to identify guests, their affiliations, and contact information to facilitate effective communication and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
2.  Select your company and job title (if applicable) from the respective fields.
3.  Choose your guest type from the "Guest Type" field, which determines the category for your guest.
4.  Enter your contact email and phone number to ensure we can reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the guest you are registering.
*   **Company** (`company_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of your company.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the job title of the guest you are registering.
*   **Company Information** (`company_info`, select_multiple, optional):
    +   Guest
    +   Visitor
    +   Attendee
    +   Employee
*   **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the guest you are registering.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the guest you are registering.
*   **Guest Type** (`guest_type`, select_one, required):
    +   Guest
    +   Visitor
    +   Attendee
    +   Employee
*   **Job Title Details** (`job_title_details`, text, optional): If applicable, provide details about the guest's job title.
*   **Contact Email (Alternative)** (`contact_email_2`, email, optional): Enter an alternative contact email for the guest.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter an alternative phone number for the guest.
*   **Organization** (`company`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the guest's organization.
*   **Position** (`job_title_2`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the guest's position.
*   **Guest Category** (`guest_type_2`, select_multiple, required):
    +   Guest
    +   Visitor
    +   Attendee
    +   Employee

## Tips
*   Ensure that the guest information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   If you are unsure about the guest type, please refer to the event organizers or your organization's policies for guidance.
*   If you are entering multiple guests, please repeat the process for each guest separately.
*   For any issues or concerns about the form, please contact the event organizers or support team.
