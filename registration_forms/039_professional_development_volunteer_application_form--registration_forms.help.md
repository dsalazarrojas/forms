# Professional Development Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Development Volunteer Application Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in participating in the organization's professional development volunteer opportunities. This form helps the organization assess the qualifications and availability of potential volunteers to ensure a good fit for the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Info section.
3. Indicate your skills and areas of expertise in the Skills section.
4. Choose the volunteer initiatives you are interested in participating in by selecting from the list.
5. Specify your work schedule, including any available dates and times for volunteering.
6. Select your availability for the volunteer program.
7. Describe your professional development goals and how they align with the program's objectives.
8. Share any relevant professional development experience you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Contact Info**: Provide your email address and any other contact information for us to reach out to you.
* **Skills**: Check the skills you have experience with or are interested in developing. This will help us match you with volunteer opportunities that suit your expertise.
* **Volunteer Initiatives**: Select the volunteer initiatives that interest you the most. You can choose multiple options.
* **Work Schedule**: Specify your available work schedule, including any dates and times you are free to volunteer.
* **Availability**: Select your availability for the volunteer program. Be honest about your schedule to ensure we can match you with opportunities that work for you.
* **Professional Development Goals**: Describe how your professional development goals align with the objectives of our volunteer program.
* **Professional Development Experience**: Share any relevant professional development experience you have, such as training or certifications.
