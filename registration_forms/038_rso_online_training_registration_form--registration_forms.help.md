# RSO Online Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The RSO Online Training Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants to ensure they receive the correct online training session and can be contacted by the RSO team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select the preferred RSO session (RSO 1, RSO 2, or RSO 3).
2.  Enter your date of registration in the Date field.
3.  Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
4.  Enter your email address to receive updates and instructions.
5.  Enter your phone number for further contact.
6.  Select your department (Accounting, Engineering, or Operations).
7.  Choose the RSO officer who will be in contact with you.
8.  (Optional) Enter the date you wish to attend the RSO training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Select RSO Session** (Select One):
    Select the online RSO training session you wish to participate in. This will allow you to receive the correct training materials and instructions.
*   **Date** (Required):
    Enter the date on which you are completing this form to ensure accurate recording of your registration.
*   **Participant Name** (Required):
    Enter your full name as it appears on official documents to ensure correct contact and documentation.
*   **Participant Email** (Optional):
    Enter a valid email address where you can receive updates and instructions from the RSO team.
*   **Phone Number** (Optional):
    Enter your phone number to enable further communication and confirmation from the RSO team.
*   **Department** (Required):
    Select your department to enable correct tracking and reporting of your training participation.
*   **RSO Officer** (Required):
    Choose the RSO officer who will be in contact with you for further instructions and clarification.
*   **RSO Training Date** (Optional):
    Enter the date you wish to attend the RSO training to ensure correct scheduling and organization.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you select the correct RSO session and department to receive the correct training materials and instructions.
- Use a valid email address to receive important updates and confirmations from the RSO team.
- Be sure to enter a correct phone number to enable further communication and confirmation.
- Select your department correctly to ensure accurate reporting and tracking.
- Choose the correct RSO officer for further instruction and clarification.
- The date field for RSO training is optional but recommended to ensure correct scheduling and organization.
