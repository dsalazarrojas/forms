# Virtual Career Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for employers to register their company and career fair information for a virtual career fair. The purpose of this form is to gather essential details about the company, such as contact information, company description, and career fair event details. This information will be used to create a profile for the company and promote their career fair event on our platform.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your company's name and description.
2. Enter your contact email and other relevant contact information.
3. Add your company's website, Facebook, and Twitter handles (if applicable).
4. Fill in the career fair title, date, time, and duration.
5. Provide the location and registration URL (if applicable).
6. Enter any additional notes or guidelines for submissions.
7. Optional: Enter the registration fee and currency (if applicable).
8. Submit the form to register your company for the virtual career fair.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your company's representative.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your company (if different from the employer name).
* **Company Website** (`company_website`, text, required): Enter your company's website address.
* **Company Facebook** (`company_facebook`, text, required): Enter your company's Facebook handle.
* **Company Twitter** (`company_twitter`, text, required): Enter your company's Twitter handle.
* **Company Representative** (`company_representative`, text, required): Enter the name of the company representative (if different from the contact email).
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, required): Enter the company's phone number.
* **Company Street** (`company_street`, text, required): Enter the company's street address.
* **Company City** (`company_city`, text, required): Enter the company's city.
* **Company State** (`company_state`, text, required): Enter the company's state.
* **Company Zip** (`company_zip`, number, required): Enter the company's zip code.
* **Career Fair Title** (`career_fair_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the career fair.
* **Career Fair Date** (`career_fair_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the career fair.
* **Career Fair Time** (`career_fair_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the career fair.
* **Career Fair Duration** (`career_fair_duration`, text, required): Enter the duration of the career fair.
* **Career Fair Location** (`career_fair_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the career fair.
* **Career Fair URL** (`career_fair_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the career fair.
* **Career Fair Registration Link** (`career_fair_registration_link`, text, required): Enter the link to the career fair registration page.
* **Career Fair Notes** (`career_fair_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or guidelines for submissions.
* **Career Fair Registration Fee** (`career_fair_registration_fee`, number, required): Enter the registration fee for the career fair (if applicable).
* **Career Fair Registration Fee Currency** (`career_fair_registration_fee_currency`, text, required): Enter the currency of the registration fee (if applicable).
* **Career Fair Registration URL** (`career_fair_registration_url`, text, required): Enter the URL for career fair registration (if applicable).
* **Career Fair Submission Guidelines** (`career_fair_submission_guidelines`, text, required): Enter any submission guidelines for the career fair.
