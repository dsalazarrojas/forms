# Cookie Decorating Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to sign up for a cookie decorating class. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure you are registered for the correct class and provide us with the necessary details for a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your name and email address (fields 1-2) to create an account or log in.
2.  Enter your date of birth (field 4) to verify your age for class selection and instruction purposes.
3.  Choose your preferred class date and time (fields 6-7) by selecting a morning, afternoon, or evening session that suits you.
4.  Select your class type (field 8) based on your interests and skill level.
5.  Indicate if you have taken our classes before (field 11), and if so, specify which classes you've attended (field 12).
6.  Enter the number of children you're registering (field 17) and their ages (field 18) if applicable.
7.  Select your dietary requirements (field 22) and specify any special dietary needs (field 23).
8.  Choose how you will pay for the class (field 28), using a credit card, debit card, PayPal, or other methods.
9.  Enter any promo code you may have (field 29) if you have one.
10. Fill in any additional comments or information (fields 35 and 36) about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name (1)**: This is your full name as it appears on your identity card.
* **Email Address (2)**: This is the email address we will use to contact you for class reminders and updates.
* **Date of Birth (4)**: This is required for age verification purposes.
* **Preferred Class Date (6)**: Choose your preferred class date from the options provided.
* **Preferred Class Time (7)**: Select a time that suits you best.
* **Class Type (8)**: Choose a class type that interests you, based on your skill level and preferences.
* **Cookie Decorating Skill Level (10)**: Indicate your current skill level in cookie decorating.
* **Have You Taken Our Classes Before (11)**: If you have taken our classes before, select "True" and specify which classes you've attended.
* **Previous Class Details (12)**: Specify which classes you've taken previously.
* **Number in Your Group (14)**: Enter the total number of participants in your group.
* **Names of Other Participants (15)**: If registering for others, enter their names here.
* **Children Attending (17)**: Enter the number of children you're registering.
* **Ages of Children (18)**: Enter the ages of the children you're registering.
* **Dietary Requirements (22)**: Select your dietary requirements.
* **Dietary Details (23)**: Specify any special dietary needs.
* **Payment Method (28)**: Choose how you will pay for the class.
* **Promo Code (29)**: If you have a promo code, enter it here.
* **Additional Comments (35)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **I Accept the Class Terms and Cancellation Policy (36)**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the class.

Please note that some fields are not required and have no impact on your registration, such as fields 11 and 12. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
