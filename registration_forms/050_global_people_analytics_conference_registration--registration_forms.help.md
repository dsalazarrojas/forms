# Global People Analytics Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from conference attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Registration Form" field with your name or a brief description of who is registering.
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
4. Provide your Company name if applicable.
5. Enter your Job Title (if you have one).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name or description to identify who is registering.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your Email address. This should be a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Enter your company name if you have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title if you have one. If not, you can leave this field blank.
