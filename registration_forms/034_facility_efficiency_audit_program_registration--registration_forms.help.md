# facility_efficiency_audit_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The facility efficiency audit program is designed to help facilities improve their efficiency and effectiveness. This program provides a structured approach to identify areas of improvement and implement changes to increase productivity and reduce waste.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the first page and choose your answer to the question about participating in the program.
2. Fill out the contact information section with your facility's details.
3. Provide information about your facility, including its type and name.
4. Enter the facility owner's name and contact information (email and phone number).
5. Enter the facility's location.
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your facility.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participating in the program?**: This is the first question on the first page. Please answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your facility's interest in participating in the program.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your facility's contact information, including name, email, and phone number. This will help us reach out to you with more information and updates about the program.
* **Facility Info**: Provide a brief description of your facility, including its type and name. This will help us understand your facility's specific needs and goals.
* **Facility Type**: Select the type of facility you have (e.g. manufacturing, warehouse, office building).
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of your facility.
* **Facility Owner**: Enter the name of the person who owns or manages your facility.
* **Facility Email**: Enter the email address of the facility owner or manager.
* **Facility Phone**: Enter the phone number of the facility owner or manager.
* **Facility Location**: Enter the physical location of your facility (e.g. street address, city, state).
* **Facility Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your facility. This is an optional field, but can be useful for providing more information about your facility.
