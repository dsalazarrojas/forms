# Land Title Verification Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Land Title Verification Program Registration Form is used to collect information about land title verification requests. This form is used by agents or submitters to provide details about the land title, including applicant information, land use, and submitter information. The form helps to track and verify land titles for various uses such as land acquisition, development, or ownership transfers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in all required fields as accurately as possible.
2. If a field is not required, you can leave it blank.
3. Review all fields before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4. Sign and date the form if you are submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Details (1)**
	+ This field is used to collect details about the land applicant or owner.
	+ Please provide the name and other relevant information about the applicant.
* **Contact Info (2)**
	+ Enter the contact information of the applicant or agent.
	+ This includes name, email, and phone number.
* **Land Details (3)**
	+ Enter details about the land, such as its use (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, or other).
	+ You can also provide a description of the land.
* **Signature (4)**
	+ This field is used to capture the signature of the applicant or agent.
* **Land Use (5)**
	+ Select the use of the land from the options provided (residential, commercial, industrial, agricultural, or other).
	+ This field is optional but helps to categorize the land title verification request.
* **Land Area (6)**
	+ Enter the area of the land in square meters or acres.
	+ This field is optional but helps to provide more detail about the land.
* **Land Value (7)**
	+ Enter the monetary value of the land.
	+ This field is optional but helpful for financial purposes.
* **Applicant Name (8)**
	+ Enter the name of the applicant or owner of the land.
* **Applicant Email (9)**
	+ Enter the email of the applicant or agent.
* **Applicant Phone (10)**
	+ Enter the phone number of the applicant or agent.
* **Agent Name (11)**
	+ Enter the name of the agent handling the land title verification request.
* **Agent Phone (12)**
	+ Enter the phone number of the agent.
* **Agent Email (13)**
	+ Enter the email of the agent.
* **Submitter (14)**
	+ Enter the name of the person submitting the land title verification request.
* **Submitter Email (15)**
	+ Enter the email of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone (16)**
	+ Enter the phone number of the submitter.
* **Submitter Designation (17)**
	+ Enter the designation or title of the submitter.
* **Land Status (18)**
	+ Provide a brief description of the land status (e.g., for sale, under development, etc.).
* **Agent Agreement (19)**
	+ This field is used to capture the agent's agreement or confirmation.
* **Submitter Agreement (20)**
	+ This field is used to capture the submitter's agreement or confirmation.
* **Submitter Note (21)**
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes from the submitter.
* **Submitter Signature (22)**
	+ This field is used to capture the submitter's signature.
* **Submitter Date (23)**
	+ Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submitter Time (24)**
	+ Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **Submitter IP (25)**
	+ Enter the IP address of the submitter.
* **Agent Signature (26)**
	+ This field is used to capture the agent's signature.
* **Agent Date (27)**
	+ Enter the date the form was signed by the agent.

Note: The fields marked as "required" must be filled out, while others are optional. Ensure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
