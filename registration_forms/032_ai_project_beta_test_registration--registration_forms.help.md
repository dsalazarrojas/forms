# AI Project Beta Test Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in participating in the beta testing phase of an AI project. To ensure the form is filled out correctly and not duplicate, the system will check for existing registrations of users with the same email address.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the personal details section, including your full name and email address.
2. Provide your technical details, such as your primary operating system and web browser.
3. Specify your weekly availability for testing.
4. Answer the question regarding your previous beta testing experience.
5. State why you want to test the AI project.
6. Select the AI modules you are most interested in testing.
7. Choose your preferred feedback channel.
8. Agree to keep all beta features and screenshots confidential.
9. Provide the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to identify yourself in the beta testing team.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address where we will send beta access instructions and updates.
* **Occupation/Industry** (`occupation_industry`, `text`, optional): If you want to share your profession or industry, please do so here.
* **Technical Expertise** (`technical_savviness`, `number`, required): Rate your technical expertise from 1 to 5, where 1 is a basic user and 5 is a developer or engineer.
* **Primary Operating System** (`primary_os`, `select_one`, required): Choose the primary operating system you use for testing.
* **Primary Web Browser** (`primary_browser`, `select_one`, required): Choose your primary web browser for testing.
* **Device Hardware Specs** (`hardware_specs`, `text`, optional): If you are testing locally, please describe your device's hardware specifications.
* **Weekly Availability** (`weekly_availability`, `select_one`, required): Indicate how many hours you can dedicate to testing per week.
* **Previous Beta Experience** (`previous_beta_experience`, `select_one`, required): Have you participated in software beta tests before? If so, please answer yes or no.
* **Motivation for Joining** (`motivation_for_joining`, `text`, required): State why you are interested in testing our AI project.
* **Interested AI Modules** (`ai_modules_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the AI modules you are most interested in testing.
* **Preferred Feedback Channel** (`feedback_channel_preference`, `select_one`, required): Specify your preferred way of receiving feedback, such as via a dedicated Discord server, email, or in-app tool.
* **Discord Username** (`discord_username`, `text`, optional): If you want to join our Discord server, please provide your username.
* **Confidentiality & NDA Agreement** (`nda_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Agree to keep all beta features and screenshots confidential.
* **Date of Application** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Provide the date of your application.
