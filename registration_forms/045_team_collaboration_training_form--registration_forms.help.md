# Team Collaboration Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect participant information for team collaboration training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to enter all required and optional information as requested.
3. Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the training session you are participating in.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the training session you are participating in.
* **Feedback Comments** (`feedback_comments`, text, required: false): Provide any comments or feedback about the training session.
* **Training Session Name** (`training_session_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the training session you are participating in.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, required: false): Enter your company name if applicable.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number for any follow-up purposes.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the team you are representing.
* **Department Name** (`department_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your department if applicable.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to use a valid email address for communication purposes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the training administrator.

This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and efficiently. If you have any further questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to reach out.
