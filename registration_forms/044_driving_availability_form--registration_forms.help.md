# driving_availability_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "driving_availability_form" is used to collect information about the availability of individuals to drive, including trip details, travel dates, driving experience, and other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the "driving_availability_form" form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the page you'd like to start with. The form is divided into different pages, each with its own section of fields. For this form, you can select from:
	* "Trip Details"
	* "Travel Dates"
	* "Driving Experience"
	* "Driving License"
	* "Availability"
	* "Trip Duration"
	* "Distance"
	* "Trip Type"
	* "Trip Location"
	* "Driver License Number"
	* "Driver Experience"
	* "Driver Rating"

2. Fill out each page of the form, providing the requested information in each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Trip Details
**Trip Details (trip_details)**: Enter the trip details you'd like to share with us.

### Travel Dates
**Travel Dates (travel_dates)**: Enter your travel dates (in date format: dd/mm/yyyy).

### Driving Experience
**Driving Experience (driving_experience)**: Enter your driving experience in years.

### Driving License
**Driving License (driving_license)**: Select your driving license status:
	* Valid
	* Invalid

### Availability
**Availability (availability)**: Enter your availability in 24-hour format (HH:MM).

### Trip Duration
**Trip Duration (trip_duration)**: Enter the duration of your trip in hours.

### Distance
**Distance (distance)**: Enter the distance of your trip.

### Trip Type
**Trip Type (trip_type)**: Select the type of trip:
	* Out of Town
	* Out of Town - Long
	* Local

### Trip Location
**Trip Location (trip_location)**: Select your trip location:
	* City
	* Town
	* Country

### Driver License Number
**Driver License Number (driver_license_number)**: Enter your driver's license number.

### Driver Experience
**Driver Experience (driver_experience)**: Enter your driving experience in years.

### Driver Rating
**Driver Rating (driver_rating)**: Enter your driver rating (a number between 1 and 5).

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out. Fields with no specified type or options are text fields, and can accept any type of input.
