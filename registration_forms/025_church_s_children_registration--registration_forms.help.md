# Church's Children Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about children who will be participating in church activities. It includes details such as the child's personal information, medical and health considerations, emergency contact details, behavioral concerns, and permissions for participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Section 1: Child Information**
	* Enter the child's first name in the **Child's First Name** field.
	* Enter the child's last name in the **Child's Last Name** field.
	* Select the child's **Age Group** from the dropdown menu.
	* Enter the child's **Date of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
2. **Section 2: Parent or Guardian Information**
	* Enter the primary parent or guardian's name in the **Primary Parent or Guardian Name** field.
	* Select the relationship between the child and the primary parent or guardian from the **Relationship to Child** dropdown menu.
	* Enter the primary parent or guardian's email address in the **Email Address** field.
	* Enter the primary parent or guardian's phone number in the **Phone Number** field.
3. **Section 3: Health and Medical Information**
	* List any known allergies in the **Food or Environmental Allergies** field.
	* List any current medications the child is taking in the **Current Medications** field.
	* Describe any special health conditions or needs in the **Special Health Conditions or Needs** field.
4. **Section 4: Emergency Contact Information**
	* Enter the name of the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Name** field.
	* Enter the phone number of the emergency contact in the **Emergency Contact Phone** field.
	* Describe the relationship between the child and the emergency contact in the **Relationship to Child** field.
5. **Section 5: Permissions and Agreements**
	* Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether you give permission to take photos or video of your child in the **Permission to Take Photos or Video** field.
	* Select **Yes** or **No** to indicate whether you give permission to participate in church activities in the **Permission to Participate in Church Activities** field.
	* Enter the names of authorized persons who can pick up the child in the **Authorized Persons for Pickup** field.
	* Select **I agree** or **I do not agree** to indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions in the **Parent or Guardian Agrees to Terms and Conditions** field.

* **Child's First Name** (Child's First Name, text, required): Enter the child's first name.

* **Food or Environmental Allergies** (Food or Environmental Allergies, text, required): List any known allergies.
* **Current Medications** (Current Medications, text, required): List any current medications the child is taking.
* **Special Health Conditions or Needs** (Special Health Conditions or Needs, text, required): Describe any special health conditions or needs.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Child** (Relationship to Child, text, required): Describe the relationship between the child and the emergency contact.
