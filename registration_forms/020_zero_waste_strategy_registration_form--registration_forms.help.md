## Zero Waste Strategy Registration Form - Help Guide
### Purpose
This form helps us to collect information about individuals or organizations interested in adopting a zero waste strategy. This information will be used for further instructions and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
Fill out the form by following the instructions above.

### Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`title`, text, not required): Enter a title for your registration. This will be used to identify your registration.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter your name or the name of the participant.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): Enter the name of your organization (if applicable).
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, not required): Enter your participant email address for further communication.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, not required): Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, text, not required): Enter your participant's title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, not required): Enter the department you are representing (if applicable).
* **Location** (`location`, text, not required): Enter the location.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, not required): Select your registration date.
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, not required): Select your registration time.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, text, not required): Choose your time zone.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Only one registration per participant/organization is allowed.
* Incomplete or duplicated submissions will not be accepted.
