# Child Care Facility Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child's care at our facility. It outlines the activities and services we provide, and asks for permission for certain activities and services. This form is required for every child enrolled in our care.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and understand the activities and services we provide.
2. Answer all required questions truthfully and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Child Care Facility Consent Form --** (`consent_intro`): This is a note field that explains the purpose and scope of the form.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`): Please enter your name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Date of Birth** (`parent_date_of_birth`): Please enter your date of birth as the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`parent_phone`): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email Address** (`parent_email`): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Child's Name** (`child_name`): Please enter your child's name.
* **Child's Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`): Please enter your child's date of birth.
* **Child Care Facility Name** (`facility_name`): Please enter the name of the facility where your child will be cared for.
* **Enrollment Start Date** (`enrollment_start_date`): Please enter the start date of your child's enrollment.
* **Classroom Assignment** (`classroom_assignment`): Please select the classroom where your child will be assigned to (Infant room, Toddler room, Pre-K, Mixed age, or Other).
* **Consent for Regular Classroom Activities** (`regular_activities_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child participating in regular classroom activities.
* **Consent for Outdoor Play and Recreation** (`outdoor_play_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child participating in outdoor play and recreation.
* **Consent for Field Trips and Outings** (`field_trips_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child participating in field trips and outings.
* **Special Instructions or Restrictions for Field Trips** (`field_trip_details`): If you have any special instructions or restrictions for field trips, please enter them here.
* **Consent for Photos or Videos for Records** (`photography_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child's photos or videos being taken for records.
* **Consent for Social Media Sharing** (`social_media_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child's photos or videos being shared on social media.
* **Consent for Water Play and Activities** (`water_activities_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child participating in water play and activities.
* **Consent for Instruction from Outside Providers or Specialists** (`third_party_teachers_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child receiving instruction from outside providers or specialists.
* **Approved Behavioral Guidance Methods** (`behavioral_guidance_methods`): Please list any approved behavioral guidance methods you have discussed with the facility.
* **Unacceptable Disciplinary Methods** (`unacceptable_methods`): If there are any methods you do not consent to, please list them here.
* **Consent to Administer Medication as Prescribed** (`medication_administration`): Please select whether you consent to your child receiving medication as prescribed.
* **Consent for Emergency Medical Treatment** (`medical_treatment_consent`): Please select whether you consent to your child receiving emergency medical treatment.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons** (`parent_pickup_consent`): Please list any persons authorized to pick up your child.
* **Release of Information** (`release_of_information`): Please select whether you consent to the release of information to the school or other providers.
* **Acknowledgment** (`parent_acknowledgment`): Please select whether you have read and understand this consent form.
