# Medical Laboratory Technician Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who wish to register as medical laboratory technicians with an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill in the form with the following steps:
- **Page 1: Contact Details**
  Fill in your name and choose your preferred contact method (email or phone number).
- **Page 2: Technician Details**
  Provide any additional information about yourself, such as your qualifications or experience.
- **Page 3: Additional Details**
  Add any additional details that you think are relevant to your registration.
- **Page 4: Submission Details**
  Confirm your submission by filling in the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details (Page 1)**
  * **Name**: A required field to capture your name for contact purposes.
  * **Preferred Contact Method (Email or Phone Number)**: A field to allow you to choose how you would like to be contacted.
- **Technician Details (Page 2)**
  * **Technician Details**: A text box for you to provide any additional information about yourself.
- **Additional Details (Page 3)**
  * **Additional Details**: A text box for you to add any extra information that you feel is relevant.
- **Submission Details (Page 4)**
  * **Submission Details**: A field to confirm your submission and provide any additional comments or notes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, please refer to your qualification documents or contact your organization's representative.
- Please be honest and accurate in your responses.
- If you have any issues or concerns during the submission process, contact your organization's representative for assistance.
