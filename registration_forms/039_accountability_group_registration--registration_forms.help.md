# Accountability Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration of an Accountability Group, allowing users to provide their personal details and preferences to join or create a group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Primary Goal for Joining an Accountability Group, Specific Goals, Have You Participated in an Accountability Group Before, Commitment Level, Preferred Meeting Format, Preferred Meeting Day, and Preferred Meeting Time.
2. Provide your preferred Group Size, if applicable.
3. Optionally, describe what skills or strengths you bring to a group.
4. Add any Additional Comments or Questions.
5. Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the group.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Provide your valid email address where you can be reached by the group.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number where the group can contact you.
* **Primary Goal for Joining an Accountability Group** (Select One, Required): Select one of the available options: Career development, Health and fitness, Financial goals, Personal growth, Academic achievement, Business startup, or Other.
* **Specific Goals** (Text, Required): Describe in 3-6 months what you want to accomplish.
* **Have You Participated in an Accountability Group Before** (Select One, Required): Select 'True' if you have participated before, and 'False' otherwise.
* **Commitment Level** (Select One, Required): Indicate how committed you are to attending regular meetings: Very committed, Committed, Somewhat committed, or Exploring the idea.
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (Select One, Required): Select In-person, Virtual (video call), Hybrid (mix of both), or No preference.
* **Preferred Meeting Day** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose all the days that work for you: Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, Sunday.
* **Preferred Meeting Time** (Select One, Required): Select one of the available options: Morning (8am-12pm), Afternoon (12pm-5pm), or Evening (5pm-9pm).
* **Group Size Preference** (Select One, Optional): Choose Small (3-5 people), Medium (6-10 people), Large (11+ people), or No preference.
* **What Skills or Strengths Do You Bring to a Group** (Text, Optional): Describe your skills or strengths that will be beneficial to the group.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (Text, Optional): Add any additional information or questions you may have.

Note: Fields without explanations will be filled out with their respective labels, following best-effort intent.
