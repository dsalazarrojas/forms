# mission-conference-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mission-conference-registration-form is a registration form for attendees to Mission Conference. It gathers information about the attendees, including their contact details and payment method.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Attendee info" section with your name and other personal details.
2. In the "Contact info" section, enter your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact information.
3. Choose your preferred payment method in the "Payment method" section.
4. In the "Conference info" section, enter any additional information you'd like to share about your experience with the Mission Conference.
5. In the "Additional info" section, enter any other relevant information about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee info** (`attendee_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your name, role, or position in your company to help us know who is attending.
* **Contact info** (`contact_info`, Text, Optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other way to contact you.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, Select, Optional): Choose how you would like to pay for the conference, such as credit card, cash, or check.
* **Conference info** (`conference_info`, Text, Optional): Share any additional information about your experience with the Mission Conference.
* **Additional info** (`additional_info`, Text, Optional): Enter any other relevant information about your registration.
