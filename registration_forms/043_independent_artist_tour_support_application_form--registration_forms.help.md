# Independent Artist Tour Support Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for independent artists to apply for tour support. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to increase your chances of getting approved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each field carefully and completely. Make sure to provide all required information.
2. If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the event organizer or promoter for clarification.
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Tour Title (1)**: Enter the title of your tour, e.g., "Summer Music Festival Lineup".
* **Artist Name (2)**: Enter your stage name or the name you wish to be known by.
* **Tour Description (3)**: Briefly describe your tour, including the type of music you will be performing and any notable events or activities planned.
* **Tour Start Date (4)**: Enter the start date of your tour, in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Tour End Date (5)**: Enter the end date of your tour, in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Tour Budget (6)**: Enter your estimated tour budget, if applicable.
* **Contact E-mail (7)**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Contact Phone (8)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Tour Location (9)**: Enter the city, state, or location where your tour will take place.
* **Music Style (10)**: Select the genre of music you perform, e.g., Indie, Rock, Jazz, or Pop.
* **Genre (11)**: Select Yes if your tour is a live performance, No otherwise.
* **Project Description (12)**: Briefly describe your tour, including any notable events or activities planned.
* **Tour Dates (13)**: Select Yes if you are looking for specific dates, No otherwise.
* **Equipment Needed (14)**: List any equipment you require for your tour, e.g., sound system, lights, etc.
* **Budget Justification (15)**: Explain how you will use the funds provided for tour support.
* **Contact First Name (16)**: Enter your first name.
* **Contact Last Name (17)**: Enter your last name.
* **Contact Address (18)**: Enter your address.
* **Contact City (19)**: Enter the city where you are located.
* **Contact State (20)**: Enter your state or province.
* **Contact Zip (21)**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Contact Country (22)**: Enter your country of residence.

## Tips

* Make sure to submit your application well in advance of the tour dates.
* Ensure your submission is complete and accurate to avoid delays in review process.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to the event organizer or promoter.
