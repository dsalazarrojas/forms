# Childcare Photo Sign Up Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to sign up your child for a photo session at the childcare center. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that we have the necessary information to provide you and your child a wonderful experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  **Start by selecting the date for the photo session**:
    - Click on the dropdown date picker and choose the desired date for your photo session.
2.  **Enter child's details**:
    - Fill out the required fields under **Child Details**:
        -  **Child First Name**: Enter the child's legal first name.
        -  **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's legal last name.
        -  **Child Date of Birth**: Provide the child's birth date.
        -  **Child Age**: Enter the child's current age in years.
3.  **Choose photo session preferences**:
    -  **Photo Session Type**: Select the desired type of photo session (e.g., individual portraits, class/group photos, etc.).
    -  **Photo Session Date**: Select the scheduled date for the photo session.
4.  **Enter parent/guardian contact information**:
    -  **Parent/Guardian First Name**: Enter the parent/guardian's legal first name.
    -  **Parent/Guardian Last Name**: Enter the parent/guardian's legal last name.
    -  **Parent/Guardian Email**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
    -  **Parent/Guardian Phone**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
5.  **Provide consents for photo usage**:
    -  **Photo Usage Consent**: Specify how photos may be used (personal use only, personal and classroom display, etc.).
    -  **Social Media Consent**: Decide if you consent to photos being shared on social media.
    -  **Website Consent**: Decide if you consent to photos being used on the center's website.
    -  **Print Materials Consent**: Decide if you consent to photos being used in print materials.
6.  **Choose photo package**:
    -  **Photo Package Selection**: Select the desired photo package.
7.  **Add any additional information**:
    -  **Sibling Photos**: If siblings will be photographed, list their names.
    -  **Special Outfit or Prop Requests**: Enter any special requests for outfits or props.
    -  **Child Comfort Considerations**: Enter any considerations to help your child feel comfortable during the photo session.
8.  **Specify payment method**:
    -  **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method (e.g., credit card, debit card, check, etc.).
9.  **Finalize with your signature**:
    -  **Parent/Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
