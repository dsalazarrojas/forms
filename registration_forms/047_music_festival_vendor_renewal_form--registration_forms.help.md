# Music Festival Vendor Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Festival Vendor Renewal Form is designed to allow vendors to easily renew their vendor status and update their information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review each field carefully and ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vendors Name
* **Vendors Name** (`vendor_name`, text, not required): Enter your company name as it appears on official documents.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, not required): Enter the name of the person responsible for communicating with us.

### Vendor License
* **Vendor License** (`vendor_license`, select_one, not required): Select the type of license you hold for your business. Please choose one of the following:
	+ 1
	+ 2
	+ 3

### Business Name
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required): Enter your business name as it appears on official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your business email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your business phone number.

### Vendor Type
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, not required): Select the type of vendor you are. Please choose one of the following:
	+ Music
	+ Non-Music

### Vendor Address
* **Vendor Address** (`vendor_address`, text, not required): Enter your business address.

## Tips
- Make sure to use the exact business name as it appears on your official documents.
- If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for assistance.
- Please review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
