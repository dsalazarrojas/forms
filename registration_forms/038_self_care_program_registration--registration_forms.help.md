# Self Care Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Self-Care Program Registration form is designed to collect information from participants who want to register for our self-care program. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that we can provide you with the best possible support and resources.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with your personal details, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the program type and length that suits your needs.
3. Choose whether you have a manager and if they should be notified.
4. Provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to know.
5. Review and submit the form once you're done.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact email** (4, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the person we should contact in case of any issues.
* **Participant name** (5, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the participant you're registering (if applicable).
* **Self-care program** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the self-care program you want to register for.
* **Program length** (7, time, required/optional): Choose the length of the program you're registering for.
* **Start date** (8, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the program.
* **Start time** (9, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the program.
* **End date** (10, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the program.
* **End time** (11, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the program.
* **Contact phone** (12, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (13, text, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Organization** (14, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job title** (15, text, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (16, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Manager name** (17, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your manager.
* **Manager email** (18, email, required/optional): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager phone** (19, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager title** (20, text, required/optional): Enter your manager's title.
* **Manager department** (21, text, required/optional): Enter the department of your manager.
* **Location** (22, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your organization.
* **Manager** (23, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether your manager should be notified about your registration.
* **Program type** (24, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of self-care program you want to register for.
* **Comments** (25, note, required/optional): Add any additional comments or information that might be helpful for us to know.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We'll review and process your registration once you submit the form.
* We'll be in touch with you soon to confirm your registration and provide further instructions.
