# Alcohol Delivery Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for our alcohol delivery service. By completing this form, you will be able to create an account, choose a subscription plan, and provide the necessary information for your delivery preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
2. **Email Address**: Enter the email address you would like to use for login and notifications.
3. **Mobile Phone**: Enter your mobile phone number for delivery and communication purposes.
4. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to verify you are of legal drinking age.
5. **Membership Tier**: Choose your subscription plan from the options provided (Basic, Premium, Connoisseur, or Business/Office Plan).
6. **Preferred Beverage Categories**: Select the types of alcohol you would like to receive deliveries for (Craft Beer, Fine Wines, Single Malt Scotch, Gin/Vodka, Tequila/Mezcal, or Artisanal Cider).
7. **Expected Delivery Frequency**: Choose how often you would like to receive deliveries (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly, or On-demand only).
8. **Primary Delivery Address**: Enter your primary delivery address where you would like to receive your alcohol deliveries.
9. **ID Type for Verification**: Select the type of ID you have that will be used for verification purposes (State Driver License, Passport, Military ID, or Government ID Card).
10. **Recurring Payment Authorization**: Check if you authorize recurring membership charges (if applicable).
11. **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the Alcohol Delivery Service Terms and Responsible Service Policy.
12. **Newsletter Opt-in**: Choose if you would like to receive weekly specials and tasting notes.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your account or deliveries.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please note that you must be of legal drinking age to use our service.
* By completing this form, you are agreeing to our terms and conditions and our responsible service policy.
