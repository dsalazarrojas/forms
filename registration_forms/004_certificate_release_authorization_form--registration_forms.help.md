# Certificate Release Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to authorize the release of a certificate to a designated recipient. Fill out this form to grant permission for the certificate to be shared with someone else.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are authorizing the correct information.
2. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Select the preferred delivery method from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Certificate Holder Full Name** (`certificate_holder_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full legal name of the person who owns the certificate.
* **Certificate Holder Email** (`certificate_holder_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with the person who owns the certificate, for confirmation purposes.
* **Certificate Holder Phone** (`certificate_holder_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of the person who owns the certificate.
* **Certificate ID Number** (`certificate_id_number`, `text`, required): Enter the unique identifier for the certificate to be released.
* **Certificate Issuance Date** (`certificate_issuance_date`, `date`, required): Enter the approximate date the certificate was issued.
* **Authorized Recipient Name** (`authorize_release_to`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person or organization who will receive the certificate.
* **Relationship to Certificate Holder** (`recipient_relationship`, `select_one`, required): Select the relationship between the holder and recipient (e.g. Self, Employer, Academic Institution, etc.).
* **Recipient Email Address** (`recipient_email_address`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where the certificate should be sent.
* **Preferred Release Method** (`release_method_preferred`, `select_one`, required): Select the preferred method of delivery (e.g. Secure Digital Download Link, Direct Email (Encrypted), etc.).
* **Purpose of Release** (`purpose_of_release`, `text`, required): Provide a brief explanation for why the certificate is being shared.
* **Authorization Agreement Statement** (`authorization_statement_agree`, `select_one`, required): Select the correct authorization agreement statement (e.g. I Authorize and Agree, Other).
* **Date of Authorization** (`holder_authorization_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the authorization.
* **Identity Verification Method Used** (`identity_verification_method_used`, `select_one`, required): Select the method used to verify identity (e.g. Photo ID Upload, Security Question Response, etc.).
* **Additional Release Instructions** (`additional_release_instructions`, `text`, optional): Provide any specific details for the delivery team.
