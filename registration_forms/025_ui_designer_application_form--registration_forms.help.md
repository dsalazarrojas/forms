# UI Designer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The UI Designer Application Form is designed to gather information from applicants for a UI Designer position. This form is used to evaluate the qualifications and suitability of potential candidates for the role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the contact information section, including your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, and Portfolio Links.
2. Describe your experience in the Experience field, including relevant work history.
3. Add your portfolio links or attach a portfolio.
4. Provide information about your education background.
5. If applicable, fill out the previous work experience section with details about your previous job.
6. Finally, click the Submit button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Portfolio Links** (`portfolio_links`, text, optional): Enter links to your portfolio or attach a portfolio.
* **Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience in the field of UI Design.
* **Resume Links** (`resume_links`, text, optional): Enter links to your resume or attach a resume.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, optional): Select the category that best fits your skills: Web Design, UI/UX, Graphic Design, or Other.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Enter your skills in the field of UI Design.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Previous Workplace** (`previous_workplace`, text, optional): Enter the name of your previous workplace.
* **Previous Job Title** (`previous_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title in your previous workplace.
* **Job Duration** (`job_duration`, time, optional): Enter the duration of your previous job.
* **Job Start Date** (`job_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your previous job.
* **Job End Date** (`job_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your previous job.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the title of your previous job.
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, text, optional): Enter your job responsibilities in your previous job.
* **Job Notes** (`job_notes`, text, optional): Enter any notes about your previous job.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, note, optional): Attach a portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): Enter a link to your portfolio or attach a portfolio.
* **Portfolio Description** (`portfolio_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your portfolio.
* **Portfolio Image** (`portfolio_image`, text, optional): Enter an image of your portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link_portfolio`, text, optional): Enter a link to your portfolio.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your previous job.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): Click this button to submit your application.
