# process_automation_roundtable_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a roundtable discussion on process automation. The goal is to understand their interests, needs, and expectations from the discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name and Last Name fields, which will be used to identify you as an attendee.
2. Enter your Email address, which will be used for follow-up communication.
3. Check the "Attendance Interests" field to let us know if you plan to attend. If yes, please specify your interests.
4. Check the "Attendance Consent" field to indicate your consent to attend.
5. Enter your Role, Company, and Job Function to provide context about your work experience and responsibilities.
6. Optionally, you can also fill out the "Topics of Interest" field to provide more information about your interests.
7. For the "Location" field, let us know if you plan to attend in person or remotely.
8. Optionally, you can also fill out the "Date" and "Time" fields to specify your availability.
9. If you have any specific notes or comments, you can type them in the "Note" field.
10. You can also enter any additional information about your company and job function using the "Other Company" and "Other Job Function" fields.
11. If you want to share any financial information, you can enter numbers in the "Profit", "Expenses", "Revenue", and "Cost of Goods Sold" fields.
12. Finally, you can also specify your margin and margin percentage if applicable.
