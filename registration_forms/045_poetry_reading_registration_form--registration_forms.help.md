# Poetry Reading Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poetry Reading Registration Form is designed to gather information from attendees and performers for poetry readings. It helps event organizers collect and manage registrations efficiently, ensuring a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4.  Enter your address in the "Address" field.
5.  Type in your zip code in the "ZIP" field.
6.  Specify your city in the "City" field.
7.  Choose your state in the "State" field.
8.  If applicable, enter your name as a performer in the "Performer Name" field.
9.  Enter the name of the poem you will be performing in the "Poem Name" field.
10. Select the type of poem you will be performing from the "Poem Type" options.
11. Choose the date of the event in the "Date" field.
12. Select the start and end times for the event in the "Start Time" and "End Time" fields, respectively.
13. If you are a performer, select your form type as "Performer" in the "Form Type" field.
14. Add any additional notes about the event in the "Notes" field.
15. If you are a performer, select the type of performance from the "Performer Type" options.
16. Enter a brief bio in the "Bio" field.
17. If you are a performer, enter a link to your photo in the "Photo" field.
18. Enter a link to your website in the "Website" field.
19. Enter a link to your Instagram profile in the "Instagram" field.
20. Enter a link to your Facebook profile in the "Facebook" field.
21. Enter a link to your Twitter profile in the "Twitter" field.
22. Enter a link to your social media in the "Social Media" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name**: Enter your first name.
    - **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
    - **Email**: Enter your email address.
    - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
    - **Address**: Enter your address.
    - **ZIP**: Enter your zip code.
    - **City**: Enter your city.
    - **State**: Enter your state.
    - **Performer Name**: If you are a performer, enter your name.
    - **Poem Name**: If you are a performer, enter the name of the poem you will be performing.
    - **Poem Type**: Select the type of poem you will be performing.
        - **Free verse**
        - **Sonnet**
        - **Ode**
        - **Lyric**
        - **Ballad**
        - **Elegy**
        - **Haiku**
        - **Tanka**
        - **Senryu**
        - **Cinquano**
        - **Villanelle**
        - **Ghazal**
        - **Pantoum**
        - **Haibun**
    - **Date**: Choose the event date.
    - **Start Time**: Choose the event start time.
    - **End Time**: Choose the event end time.
    - **Form Type**: If you are a performer, choose "Performer".
    - **Notes**: Add any additional notes.
    - **Performer Type**: If you are a performer, select your performance type.
        - **Spoken Word**
        - **Performance**
        - **Reading**
        - **Other**
    - **Bio**: Enter a brief bio.
    - **Photo**: If you are a performer, enter a link to your photo.
    - **Website**: Enter a link to your website.
    - **Instagram**: Enter a link to your Instagram profile.
    - **Facebook**: Enter a link to your Facebook profile.
    - **Twitter**: Enter a link to your Twitter profile.
    - **Social Media**: Enter a link to your social media profile.
