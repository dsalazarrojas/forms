## Purpose
The "Sports Registration Form" is designed for new players to register for sports activities, teams, or programs. This form collects personal and contact information, player status, and other relevant details to ensure accurate and secure registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Select the correct player type from the options provided.
3. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
4. Provide your contact number and address.
5. Choose a date for your registration.
6. Select your team and division (if applicable).
7. Select your playing level.
8. Specify your position on the team.
9. Enter your player ID and password.
10. Confirm your player password.
11. Provide emergency contact information, including phone number, relationship, and email.
12. Choose whether you have the emergency contact's permission to share information.
13. Select whether you have parental permission.
14. Specify any medical conditions or notes.
15. Select your registration status.
