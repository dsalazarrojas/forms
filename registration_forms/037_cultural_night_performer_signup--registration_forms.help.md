# Cultural Night Performer Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for cultural night performers to sign up and provide information about their performances and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your User Info section with your name and any relevant details about yourself.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Information section, including your email address and phone number.
3. In the Performance Info section, describe your performance, including the type of art, music, or activity you will be showcasing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (User Info, text, required: false): This section is for you to provide some details about yourself, such as your name and any relevant background information.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Please provide your email address and phone number so we can reach out to you with further questions or to confirm your participation.
* **Performance Info** (Performance Info, text, required: false): Describe your performance, including the type of art, music, or activity you will be showcasing on the cultural night.

## Tips

* Please fill in as much detail as possible in each section to ensure that we have all the necessary information about your performance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
