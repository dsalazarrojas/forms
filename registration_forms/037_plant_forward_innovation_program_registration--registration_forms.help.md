# Plant Forward Innovation Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register an application to the "Plant Forward Innovation Program". This form is meant to capture contact information, project details, and attachments to support the application. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your contact information on "Application Information" page.
	* Enter your full name in the "Applicant Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. Select your program interest on the "Program Interest" page.
3. Describe your project in the "Project Description" field on the "Project Description" page.
4. Upload up to 5 files to support your application on the "Attachments" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your application (if applicable).
* **Program Interest** (`program_interest`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following programs:
	+ Innovation
	+ Development
	+ Commercialization
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of your project (if applicable).
* **Description** (`description`, note, optional): Describe your project in detail.
* **File 1** (`file_1`, text, required): Attach a file to support your application.
* **File 2** (`file_2`, text, optional): Attach another file to support your application.
* **File 3** (`file_3`, text, optional): Attach an additional file to support your application.
* **File 4** (`file_4`, text, optional): Attach more files to support your application.
* **File 5** (`file_5`, text, optional): Attach final file to support your application.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your application.
