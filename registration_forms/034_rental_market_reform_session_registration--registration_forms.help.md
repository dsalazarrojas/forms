# rental_market_reform_session_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rental Market Reform Session Registration" form is designed to collect registration information from attendees to sessions in a rental market setting. This form is crucial for maintaining accurate attendance records and ensuring that attendees' needs are properly documented.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the attendee's details:
	* Attendee Details: Provide the attendee's name and contact information.
	* Attendee Email: Enter the attendee's email address.
	* Attendee Phone: Enter the attendee's phone number.
	* Attendee Address: Enter the attendee's address.
2. Schedule the session:
	* Session Schedule: Enter the session details, if any.
3. Register the attendee:
	* Session Registrations 1/2: Choose whether the attendee has registered for Session 1/2.
4. Add any additional registration notes:
	* Registration Notes: Enter any additional registration notes or comments.
5. Set the registration date:
	* Registration Date: Select the date of the registration.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**: Enter the attendee's name, email, and phone number.
* **Session Schedule**: Enter any additional session details, if required.
* **Session Registrations 1/2**: Choose whether the attendee has registered for Session 1/2.
* **Registration Notes**: Enter any additional registration notes or comments.
* **Registration Date**: Select the date of the registration.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter the attendee's email address.
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter the attendee's phone number.
* **Attendee Address**: Enter the attendee's address.
* **Session Registrations 2**: Choose whether the attendee has registered for Session 2 (this field is identical to Session Registrations 1/2, but with a different label).
