# Airbnb Reservation Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and track essential information for each Airbnb reservation, ensuring accurate communication between hosts and guests. It is crucial to complete this form accurately and completely to avoid any potential issues or miscommunications during the booking process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in reservation ID**: Enter the booking reference number for quick reference and tracking.
2. **Enter guest details**: Provide the guest's first and last name, email, and phone number.
3. **Provide property information**: Enter the property's name and full address.
4. **Enter host details**: Fill in the host's name and phone number.
5. **Set dates and times**: Enter the check-in and check-out dates and times.
6. **Specify number of guests and nights**: Provide the total number of guests and the number of nights.
7. **Enter payment details**: Select the payment method and amount paid or remaining balance.
8. **Specify additional information**: Include any special requests, accessibility needs, and pets.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation ID**: Booking reference number for easy tracking and reference.
* **Guest First Name**: First name of the guest.
* **Guest Last Name**: Last name of the guest.
* **Guest Email**: Guest's contact email.
* **Guest Phone**: Guest's contact number.
* **Guest Country**: Country of residence.
* **Property Name**: Name of the rental property.
* **Property Address**: Full address of the rental property.
* **Host Name**: Host's full name.
* **Host Phone**: Host's contact number.
* **Check-in Date**: Arrival date for the reservation.
* **Check-in Time**: Arrival time for the reservation.
* **Check-out Date**: Departure date for the reservation.
* **Check-out Time**: Departure time for the reservation.
* **Number of Nights**: Total number of nights the guest will be staying.
* **Number of Guests**: Total number of guests in the group.
* **Adults**: Number of adults in the group.
* **Children**: Number of children under 2 years old.
* **Infants**: Number of infants under 2 years old.
* **Room Type**: Type of accommodation, such as entire home, private room, shared room, or hotel room.
* **Property Type**: Type of property, such as house, apartment, condo, or cottage.
* **Nightly Rate (USD)**: Rate per night for the reservation.
* **Cleaning Fee (USD)**: Cleaning fee amount if applicable.
* **Service Fee (USD)**: Platform service fee amount if applicable.
* **Total Amount (USD)**: Total reservation cost.
* **Payment Status**: Current payment status.
* **Payment Method**: Method of payment used for the reservation.
* **Special Requests**: Any special requests or instructions from the guest.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Any accessibility requirements or needs.
* **Pets Included**: Whether pets are part of the reservation.
* **Pet Fee (USD)**: Pet fee amount if applicable.
* **Security Deposit (USD)**: Security deposit amount if applicable.
* **Cancellation Policy**: Type of cancellation policy applied to the reservation.
* **Booking Source**: Source of the booking, such as Airbnb platform or direct booking.
* **Reservation Status**: Current status of the reservation.
* **Booking Date**: Date the reservation was made.
* **Additional Notes**: Any additional information or comments.

## Tips
- Please fill out this form accurately and completely to avoid any potential issues or miscommunications during the booking process.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to ensure all relevant information is included.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact your support team for assistance.
