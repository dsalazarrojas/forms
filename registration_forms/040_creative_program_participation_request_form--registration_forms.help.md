# Creative Program Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals or organizations interested in participating in a creative program. It helps us understand their requirements and preferences, ensuring a smooth and tailored experience for all involved. Please note that this form is not meant for duplicate or test requests. If you've submitted a request recently, please do not resubmit. If you're unsure about the purpose of this form, kindly review the program's terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide the title of the program you're interested in participating in.
2. Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for your organization or team.
3. Enter your email address for us to contact you with any updates or questions.
4. Enter your contact number for us to reach out to you directly.
5. Provide the date you would like to start participating in the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Title** (`program_title`, text, optional): This field is where you can enter the title of the program you're interested in participating in. Please be as specific as possible.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Here, you can enter the name of the person who will be responsible for your organization or team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): This is the email address we'll use to contact you with any updates or questions about your participation.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number so we can reach out to you directly if needed.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Provide the date you would like to start participating in the program.
