> # employer_branding_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant for registration of participants to the Employer Branding workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To register for the workshop, please fill out this form completely and accurately. The form is divided into different sections, which are explained in the field-by-field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name to be associated with your registration.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Type in your valid email address to receive important updates about the workshop.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number so that we can reach you in case of any updates or changes.
* **Dietary Concerns** (`dietary_concerns`, `note`, required): If you have any dietary restrictions or concerns, please list them here.
* **Participant Dietary Concerns** (`participant_dietary_concerns`, `note`, required): If you have any dietary restrictions or concerns about yourself, please list them here.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required): If you have any additional comments or information about your participation in the workshop, please enter them here.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate information to ensure that we can provide you with the best experience.
* If you have any questions or need help with this form, please don't hesitate to contact us.
