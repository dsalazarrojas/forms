# escape_room_group_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for group bookings at an escape room.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. Provide as much detail as possible for non-required fields.
3. If you have any special requests or needs, be sure to include them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Select the correct options for the "Group Type", "Payment Method", and "Catering Type" fields based on your group's needs.
5. If your group has any food allergies, please list them in the "Food Allergy" field.
6. Finally, provide any additional information about your group in the "Additional Information" field.
