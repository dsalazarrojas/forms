# Digital Media Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about a digital media event, such as a conference or workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill in the required fields as indicated by the asterisk (*).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (participant_name): *This field is required and should be filled in with the name of the participant, including their first and last name.*
* **Contact Number** (contact_number): *This field should be filled in with the participant's contact number for follow-up purposes.*
* **Email** (email): *This field is required and should be filled in with the participant's email address for communication and event updates.*
* **Event Type** (event_type): *Select one or multiple types of events the participant is attending, such as 'Event' or 'Workshop'.*
* **Event Date** (event_date): *Optional; if applicable, fill in the date of the event.*
* **Event Time** (event_time): *Optional; if applicable, fill in the time of the event. Be aware that this field is separate from the start and end times.*
* **Event Start Time** (event_start_time): *Optional; if applicable, fill in the start time of the event.*
* **Event End Time** (event_end_time): *Optional; if applicable, fill in the end time of the event.*
* **Event Location** (event_location): *Optional; if applicable, fill in the location of the event.*
* **Participant Info** (participant_info): *This field is optional; use it to provide additional information about the participant, such as any dietary needs or special requests.*
* **Note** (note): *This field is optional; you can use it to add any additional information or comments about the event or participant.*
* **Agreement** (participant_agreement): *This field is required; select 'I agree to the terms and conditions of this event' to confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions.*
* **Form Footer** (form_footer): *This field is optional; you can use it to add any final comments or instructions.*

## Tips

* Ensure that you have filled in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Review the participant information carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to the event organizers for clarification.
