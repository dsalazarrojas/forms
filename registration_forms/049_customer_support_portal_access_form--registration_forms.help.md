# Customer Support Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customers to request access to the Customer Support Portal. To submit a successful request, ensure you have filled out all required fields.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your Customer Name in the field below.
2. Provide your Company Name.
3. Select whether you have a Contact Method (e.g., phone or email).
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Enter your E-mail.
6. (Optional) Provide a Support Ticket number (if you have one).
7. Enter a description of your request.
8. Choose the Date and Time you would like to access the portal.
9. Select whether you are in a specific Time Zone.
10. Choose whether you would like to be assigned to a specific person or team.
11. Select whether you would like to have access granted.
12. Enter the Reason for your Request.
13. Select the Status of your request (Active or Inactive).
14. (Optional) Enter any additional comments about your request.
15. Click the "Submit" button to submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name** (Customer Name, text, *): Enter your full name, as it will be displayed in the portal and used for communication purposes.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, *): Provide the name of the company you represent.
* **Contact Method** (Contact Method, select_one, *): Select whether you have a contact method (Yes or No) to help us verify your identity and reach out to you for further information.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, *): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **E-mail** (E-mail, email, *): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you for further information.
* **Support Ticket Number** (Support Ticket Number, text, ): This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have an existing support ticket number.
* **Description** (Description, note, ): Enter a brief description of your reason for requesting access to the portal.
* **Date and Time** (Date and Time, date, *): Choose the Date and Time when you would like to access the portal.
* **Time Zone** (Time Zone, select_one, *): Select whether you are in a specific Time Zone (Yes or No).
* **Time Zone Options** (Time Zone Options, select_multiple, ): This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have specific Time Zone options.
* **Assign to** (Assign to, select_one, *): Choose whether you would like to be assigned to a specific person or team (Yes or No).
* **Assign to Options** (Assign to Options, select_multiple, ): This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have specific assignment options.
* **Access Granted** (Access Granted, select_one, *): Select whether you want access granted (Yes or No).
* **Access Granted Options** (Access Granted Options, select_multiple, ): This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have specific access granted options.
* **Reason for Request** (Reason for Request, text, *): Enter the reason for your request.
* **Request Status** (Request Status, select_one, *): Select whether your request status is Active or Inactive.
* **Request Status Options** (Request Status Options, select_multiple, ): This field is optional and should only be filled out if you have specific request status options.
* **Comments for Requester** (Comments for Requester, text, ): This field is optional and should be used to add any additional comments.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, ): Click this button to submit your request.
