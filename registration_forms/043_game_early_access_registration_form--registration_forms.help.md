# Game Early Access Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us gather information from game developers about their games that are in early access. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with the necessary details about your game, which will help us review and consider it for early access on our platform. Please note that your game must be a new release, and you must be the developer or have permission to represent the game.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the game title at the top of the form.
2. Enter your name, which is optional but recommended for us to contact you if we need more information.
3. Enter your email address, which is required to be contacted about your game.
4. Select the platform(s) where your game is available.
5. Choose whether your game is available on the selected platform(s).
6. Select which social networks you wish to share your game on.
7. Enter your game's URL, which should point to your game's website or store page.
8. Provide a brief description of your game.
9. Agree to the terms of service.
10. Click "Save and Share" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Game Title** (`game_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your game. This will be the title that appears on our platform.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name. This is optional, but it helps us contact you if we need more information.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used for us to contact you about your game.
* **Platform** (`platform`, select_one, required): Select the platform(s) where your game is available. For example, Steam, GOG, or Mobile App.
* **Platforms** (`platforms`, select_multiple, optional): Choose which platform(s) you wish to share your game on. Select all that apply.
* **Discord** (`discord`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you want to share your game on Discord.
* **Twitch** (`twitch`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you want to share your game on Twitch.
* **Twitter** (`twitter`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you want to share your game on Twitter.
* **Social Networks** (`social_networks`, select_multiple, optional): Select which other social networks you want to share your game on.
* **Game URL** (`game_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of your game's website or store page.
* **Game Description** (`game_description`, note, optional): Provide a brief description of your game.
* **Terms** (`terms`, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to our terms of service.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
* **Back to Game** (`back_to_game`, text, optional): This is a link to return to your game's page.
* **Share On** (`share_on`, text, optional): Enter a platform where you want to share your game.
* **Share on Discord** (`share_on_discord`, text, optional): Enter a Discord community where you want to share your game.
* **Share on Twitch** (`share_on_twitch`, text, optional): Enter a Twitch channel where you want to share your game.
* **Share on Twitter** (`share_on_twitter`, text, optional): Enter a Twitter username where you want to share your game.
* **Share on Roblox** (`share_on_roblox`, text, optional): Enter a Roblox username where you want to share your game.
* **Connect to Twitch** (`connect_twitch`, text, optional): Enter a Twitch account where you want to connect your game.
* **Connect to Discord** (`connect_discord`, text, optional): Enter a Discord account where you want to connect your game.
* **Connect to Twitter** (`connect_twitter`, text, optional): Enter a Twitter account where you want to connect your game.
* **Connect to Roblox** (`connect_roblox`, text, optional): Enter a Roblox account where you want to connect your game.
* **Save and Share** (`save_and_share`, text, optional): Click this button to save and share your game.
* **Save** (`save`, text, optional): Click this button to save your game information.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting your registration.
* Double-check your game URL to ensure it points to the correct location.
* Be sure to agree to our terms of service before submitting your registration.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at [your_email@example.com](mailto:your_email@example.com).
