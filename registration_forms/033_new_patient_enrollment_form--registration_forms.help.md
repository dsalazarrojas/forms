# New Patient Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new patients to provide their personal and contact information when they first visit a healthcare provider or medical facility.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
3. Make sure to include all required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, `text`, required: `false`): 
    This field is for providing personal details such as name, date of birth, and other identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: `false`): 
    This field is for providing contact details such as phone number, email, and address.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`Emergency Contacts`, `text`, required: `false`): 
    This field is for listing emergency contacts in case of urgent situations.
* **Medical History** (`Medical History`, `text`, required: `false`): 
    This field is for providing any relevant medical history or conditions that the patient may have.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare provider for clarification.
* This form is for general information purposes only and should not be used for any other purpose without consulting your healthcare provider.
