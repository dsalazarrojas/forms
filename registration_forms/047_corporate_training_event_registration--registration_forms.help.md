# Corporate Training Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register attendees for a corporate training event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Choose your session preferences carefully, as this will help us tailor the event to your needs.
3. If you have any special requirements, let us know so we can make sure to accommodate them.
4. Enter the session dates and times that work best for you.
5. Enter your attendee's contact information, including name, email, phone number, and company details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Registration Info

* **Registration Info** (`registration_info`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter a brief description of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Session Preferences

* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
   Choose all that apply to indicate which sessions you are interested in attending. Options are 'Yes' and 'No'.

### Special Requirements

* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `select_one`, required: false): 
   Choose 'Yes' to indicate if you have any special requirements that need to be accommodated.

### Session Dates

* **Session Dates** (`session_dates`, `date`, required: false): 
   Enter the session dates you are interested in attending.

### Session Times

* **Session Times** (`session_times`, `time`, required: false): 
   Enter the session times you are interested in attending.

### Attendee Info

* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter a brief description of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Name

* **Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the name of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Email

* **Email** (`attendee_email`, `email`, required: false): 
   Enter the email of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Phone

* **Phone** (`attendee_phone`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the phone number of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Company

* **Company** (`attendee_company`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the company name of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Job Title

* **Job Title** (`attendee_job_title`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the job title of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Department

* **Department** (`attendee_department`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the department of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Job Function

* **Job Function** (`attendee_job_function`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the job function of the attendee(s) registering for the event.

### Company Info

* **Company Info** (`company_info`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter any additional information about the company the attendee(s) is/are from.

### Address 1

* **Address 1** (`company_address_1`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the first line of the company's address.

### Address 2

* **Address 2** (`company_address_2`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the second line of the company's address.

### City

* **City** (`company_city`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the city of the company's address.

### State

* **State** (`company_state`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the state of the company's address.

### Zip

* **Zip** (`company_zip`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the zip code of the company's address.

### Country

* **Country** (`company_country`, `text`, required: false): 
   Enter the country of the company's address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
