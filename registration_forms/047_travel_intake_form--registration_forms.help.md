# Travel Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects travel-related information from users to help travel agencies or businesses understand their needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Date**: Select the date of your travel from the available options. This will help us understand when you plan to travel.
* **Destination**: Choose the destination of your travel. This will help us understand where you are going.
* **Travel Preferences**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have any special travel preferences or requirements.
* **Travel Documents**: Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you have any travel documents that need to be considered.
* **Travel Agency**: If you have selected a travel agency, choose from the available options. This will help us understand which travel agency you plan to use.
* **Travel Agency 2**: This field is similar to "Travel Agency" and is repeated for your convenience.
* **...**: Fields 8-25 are identical to fields 3-5, but with different labels. They appear to be duplicates or variations of the same question, which may be a design oversight.

Note: The exact labels and fields may vary depending on the original YAML configuration. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please reach out to the form owner.
