**Job Fair Checklist Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is designed to help organizations and event organizers manage and document job fairs in a systematic and comprehensive way. It collects essential details about the job fair, such as title, location, date, and contact information, ensuring that all required data is accurately recorded for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the job title in the "Job Title" field.
2. Enter the job location in the "Job Location" field.
3. Select the job date in the "Job Date" field.
4. Select the job time in the "Job Time" field.
5. Enter a brief description of the job in the "Job Description" field.
6. List any requirements for the job in the "Job Requirements" field.
7. Enter the venue details for the job fair in the "Job Venue" field.
8. Provide the contact email for the job fair in the "Job Contact Email" field.
9. Enter the contact phone number for the job fair in the "Job Contact Phone" field.
10. Select the event date and time in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Job Title (Job Title)**: Enter the title of the job being promoted.
* **Job Location (Job Location)**: Enter the location where the job is available.
* **Job Date (Job Date)**: Select the date of the job fair.
* **Job Time (Job Time)**: Select the time of the job fair.
* **Job Description (Job Description)**: Enter a brief description of the job being promoted.
* **Job Requirements (Job Requirements)**: List any requirements or qualifications necessary for the job.
* **Job Venue (Job Venue)**: Enter the location where the job fair will take place.
* **Job Contact Email (Job Contact Email)**: Enter the email address of the contact person for the job fair.
* **Job Contact Phone (Job Contact Phone)**: Enter the contact phone number for the job fair.
* **Event Date (Event Date)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time (Event Time)**: Select the time of the event.

Note: All fields are optional, except for "Job Title" which is required to be filled.
