# hope_community_church_vacation_bible_school_registration_form_2019 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Hope Community Church Vacation Bible School Registration Form 2019" is used for children to register for Vacation Bible School at Hope Community Church. This form requires accurate and complete information from the parent or guardian to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Ensure you have all the required information ready, such as first name, last name, email, phone number, grade level, and any special needs.
2.  Fill out the form completely and accurately, paying special attention to the grade level selection, as this affects the child's Bible school experience.
3.  Select the relevant grade level options that apply to the child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): This is where you enter the first name of the child.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): This is where you enter the last name of the child.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for the parent or guardian.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select the grade level of the child. Options include High, Low.
* **Grade Level Options** (`grade_level_options`, text, required/optional): This is where you can enter any additional grade level options that apply to the child.
* **5th - 7th Grade** (`grade_level_options`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the 5th, 6th, or 7th grade options that apply to the child.
* **K-4th Grade** (`grade_level_options`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the K-1st, 2nd, or 3rd grade options that apply to the child.

## Tips

* Be sure to double-check the information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, please contact the Hope Community Church VBS team for assistance.
