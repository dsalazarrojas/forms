# 011_ai_driven_diagnostics_workshop_registration_form--registration_forms - Help Guide

## Purpose
The AI Driven Diagnostics Workshop Registration Form is a crucial tool to collect accurate and necessary information from participants before the workshop. By providing your details, you'll help us tailor the workshop to your needs and ensure a smooth experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your basic contact details (Full Name, Professional Email, and Professional Title/Credentials).
2. Select your Medical Specialty/Field of Study from the given options.
3. Choose your current level of AI Knowledge (Beginner, Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
4. Select your programming proficiency level (None, Basic, Proficient, or Expert).
5. Choose your preferred workshop date and attendance format (In-Person or Virtual).
6. Decide whether you'll bring your own device or require a loaner workstation.
7. Optionally, provide motivation for joining the workshop and whether you require a Certificate of Completion.
8. If attending in-person, mention any dietary restrictions.
9. Specify any other special requirements or accommodations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your professional documents.
* **Professional Email** (Required): Enter your work email address for correspondence and communication.
* **Professional Title / Credentials** (Required): Mention your title or credentials (e.g., MD, PhD, Research Fellow).
* **Affiliation / Organization** (Required): Provide the name of your hospital, university, or laboratory.
* **Medical Specialty / Field of Study** (Required): Select from the given options (Radiology, Pathology, Cardiology, Oncology, Neurology, Bioinformatics, General Practice, or Other).
* **Current Level of AI Knowledge** (Required): Choose from Beginner, Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced to help us tailor the workshop.
* **Programming Proficiency** (Required): Select from None, Basic, Proficient, or Expert to ensure a suitable workshop environment.
* **Preferred Workshop Date** (Required): Choose from Session A (March 15-16, 2026) or Session B (April 05-06, 2026).
* **Attendance Format** (Required): Select In-Person (On-site Lab) or Virtual (Live Stream + Remote VM).
* **Hardware Needs** (Required): Choose whether you'll bring your own device or require a loaner workstation.
* **Motivation for Joining** (Optional): Describe the specific diagnostic challenge you hope to address with AI.
* **Do you require a Certificate of Completion?** (Required): Yes or No.
* **Dietary Restrictions (In-person only)** (Optional): Specify dietary restrictions if attending in-person.
* **Any other special requirements or accommodations?** (Optional): Mention any other special needs or requirements.
* **Motivation for Joining** (Optional): This field is intended for those who want to describe their motivation for joining the workshop.
* **Do you require a Certificate of Completion?** (Required): Select Yes or No.
* **Dietary Restrictions (In-person only)** (Optional): Select None, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Gluten-Free.
* **Any other special requirements or accommodations?** (Optional): Describe any other special needs or requirements you may have.
