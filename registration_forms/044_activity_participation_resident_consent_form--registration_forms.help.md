# Activity Participation Resident Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from participants in activities, ensuring their safety and well-being. This includes their personal details, medical conditions, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, date of birth, and current address.
2. Provide the name and relationship of your emergency contact person.
3. Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
4. Specify the name of the activity you'll be participating in.
5. Choose the date for the activity.
6. Confirm that you understand and acknowledge the risks associated with the activity.
7. Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation.
8. If you have any medical conditions, provide more details.
9. Decide if you consent to being photographed or recorded during the activity.
10. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the standard YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required): Enter your current address where we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., parent, spouse, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Name of Activity** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of the activity you'll be participating in.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, date, required): Choose the date when the activity will take place.
* **Acknowledgment of Risks** (`activity_risks_acknowledged`, select_one, required): Confirm that you understand and accept the risks associated with this activity. Choose "Yes, I acknowledge and accept the risks" to indicate your understanding.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, select_one, required): Indicate if you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation. Choose 'Yes' if you have any conditions.
* **Details of Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_details`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, provide more details about them.
* **Photo and Video Consent** (`photo_video_consent`, select_one, required): Decide if you consent to being photographed or recorded during the activity. Choose "Yes, I consent" to indicate your agreement.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Date and sign the form to confirm your consent.
