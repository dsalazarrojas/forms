# Childcare Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and payment details for childcare registration, including payment date, child and parent/guardian information, program selection, and payment method. This form is required for all new and returning students, as well as for students transferring from another program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the payment date and select the payment method.
2. Enter the child's information, including first name, last name, date of birth, age, and address.
3. Provide the parent/guardian's information, including first name, last name, email, phone number, and address.
4. Select the registration type (new enrollment, returning, sibling, or transfer).
5. Choose the program and schedule for your child.
6. Enter the desired start date and any additional fees.
7. Confirm the total amount due at registration.
8. If applicable, select the subsidy or assistance type and provide payment details.
9. Review and agree to the refund policy and terms and conditions.
10. Sign and date the form electronically.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Date**: Enter the date on which you are submitting the payment.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the child's legal first name.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's legal last name.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the child's birth date.
* **Child Age**: Enter the child's current age in years.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's legal first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's legal last name.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's primary email address.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the parent or guardian's primary phone number.
* **Home Address**: Enter the full street address.
* **City**: Enter the city of residence.
* **State**: Enter the state or province.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the postal or zip code.
* **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration (new enrollment, returning, sibling, or transfer).
* **Program Selection**: Choose the program for your child (full-day, half-day, etc.).
* **Schedule Selection**: Choose the schedule for your child (5 days per week, 3 days per week, etc.).
* **Start Date**: Enter the desired start date.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee amount.
* **Tuition Amount**: Enter the monthly tuition amount.
* **Additional Fees**: Enter any additional fees (materials, activities, etc.).
* **Total Amount Due**: Enter the total amount due at registration.
* **Subsidy or Assistance**: Select "Yes, full coverage", "Yes, partial coverage", "False", or "Applied, pending approval".
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method (credit card, debit card, check, cash, bank transfer, or payment plan).
* **Credit Card Type**: Select the type of credit card (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, or Not applicable).
* **Cardholder Name**: Enter the name as it appears on the card.
* **Card Number**: Enter the credit or debit card number.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the card expiration date.
* **CVV Code**: Enter the 3-digit security code.
* **Billing Address**: Enter the billing address for the card.
* **Payment Plan Requested**: Select "Yes, monthly payments", "Yes, bi-weekly payments", or "No, paying in full".
* **Auto-Pay Authorization**: Select "Yes, I authorize auto-pay" or "No, will pay manually".
* **Refund Policy Acknowledgement**: Select "Yes, I have read and understand" or "No, please provide information".
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select "Yes, I agree" or "No, I do not agree".
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of electronic signature.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting.
* Ensure that you have read and understood the refund policy.
* Review and agree to the terms and conditions before submission.
