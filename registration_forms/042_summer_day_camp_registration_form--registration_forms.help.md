# Summer Day Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Day Camp Registration Form is a tool used to collect contact and registration information from parents and guardians to facilitate the process of registering children for the summer day camp. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that your child's registration is processed correctly and that you receive important updates and information about the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "Required" in the form.
2. For non-required fields, such as "Parent Info" and "Notes," you can leave them blank if they do not apply to you.
3. Be accurate and thorough when providing information, especially in "Contact Info" and "Payment Info" sections.
4. Select the correct options for "Parent Info" and "Camper Info" sections carefully.
5. Enter your contact email and phone number accurately, as these are essential for communication.
6. Submit the form once you have filled out all required fields and verified the accuracy of your information.
