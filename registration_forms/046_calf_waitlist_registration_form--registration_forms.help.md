# Calf Waitlist Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for farmers to register their interest in purchasing calves from our farm. By filling out this form, you will be adding your name and contact information to our waitlist, and indicating your preferences for breed, age, gender, quantity, and pickup timeframe. This form ensures that we have accurate and up-to-date information to better serve you and your needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your full name in the "Your Name" field.
2.  Enter the name of your farm in the "Farm Name" field.
3.  Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  Enter your farm's full mailing address in the "Farm Address" field.
6.  Enter your city and state in the "City" and "State" fields.
7.  Enter your postal code in the "ZIP Code" field.
8.  Select your preferred breed from the "Calf Breed Preference" dropdown menu.
9.  Select your preferred gender from the "Calf Gender Preference" dropdown menu.
10.  Enter the quantity of calves you are interested in purchasing.
11.  Select your preferred age range for the calves from the "Preferred Age at Pickup" dropdown menu.
12.  Choose how you plan to use the calves from the "Intended Use" dropdown menu.
13.  Select your desired pickup timeframe from the "Pickup Timeframe" dropdown menu.
14.  If applicable, choose your preferred delivery option from the "Delivery Needed" dropdown menu.
15.  If applicable, choose the distance from your farm to our farm from the "Distance from Farm" dropdown menu.
16.  If applicable, select "Yes" if you are a previous customer; otherwise, select "No".
17.  If applicable, select your farm's size from the "Farm Size" dropdown menu.
18.  If applicable, indicate your experience level with calves from the "Livestock Experience" dropdown menu.
19.  If applicable, indicate how you heard about us from the "How Did You Hear About Us" dropdown menu.
20.  Enter any additional comments or questions in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Name**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on our records.
* **Farm Name**: Enter the name of your farm.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address you would like to use for waitlist updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number for us to reach you.
* **Farm Address**: Enter your farm's full mailing address.
* **City**: Enter your city name.
* **State**: Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **Calf Breed Preference**: Select the breed you are interested in.
* **Calf Gender Preference**: Select the gender you prefer.
* **Quantity Needed**: Enter the number of calves you are interested in.
* **Preferred Age at Pickup**: Select the age range you prefer for the calves.
* **Intended Use**: Select how you plan to use the calves.
* **Pickup Timeframe**: Select when you would like to pick up the calves.
* **Delivery Needed**: Select if you need delivery service.
* **Distance from Farm**: Select the distance from your farm to our farm.
* **Previous Customer**: Select if you have purchased from us before.
* **Farm Size**: Select your farm's approximate size.
* **Livestock Experience**: Select your experience level with calves.
* **How Did You Hear About Us**: Select where you learned about our farm.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
