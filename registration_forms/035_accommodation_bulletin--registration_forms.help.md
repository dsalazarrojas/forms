# Accommodation Bulletin - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests staying at an accommodation facility. It's crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and secure stay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your full name, nationality, and passport or ID number.
2. Enter your date of birth and select your gender.
3. Provide the name and address of your accommodation provider.
4. Enter the dates of your stay, including check-in and check-out dates.
5. Specify the purpose of your visit and the country you traveled from.
6. If applicable, provide information about your next destination and the contact person in that country.
7. Enter the contact details of an emergency contact.
8. Select the type of accommodation you are staying in.
9. Enter your room number and any other relevant details.
10. Sign the form electronically, confirming the accuracy of the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Guest Nationality** (2, text, required): Specify your nationality.
* **Passport or ID Number** (3, text, required): Enter your passport or national ID number.
* **Date of Birth** (4, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Gender** (5, select_one, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Accommodation Provider** (6, text, required): Enter the name of your accommodation provider (e.g., hotel, hostel, or apartment complex).
* **Accommodation Address** (7, text, required): Enter the complete address of your accommodation.
* **Check In Date** (8, date, required): Enter the date of your arrival at the accommodation.
* **Check Out Date** (9, date, required): Enter the date of your departure from the accommodation.
* **Purpose of Visit** (10, select_one, required): Specify the purpose of your visit from the provided options (e.g., Tourism, Business, Study).
* **Country of Origin** (11, text, required): Enter the country you traveled from.
* **Next Destination** (12, text, optional): If you have a next destination, enter the details.
* **Contact Person in Country** (13, text, optional): If you have a contact person in your next destination, provide their name and contact information.
* **Contact Person Address** (14, text, optional): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (15, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (16, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Accommodation Type** (17, select_one, required): Select the type of accommodation you are staying in from the provided options (e.g., Hotel, Hostel, Apartment).
* **Room Number** (18, text, optional): If applicable, enter your room or apartment number.
* **Signature** (19, text, required): Enter your electronic signature, confirming the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Signature Date** (20, date, required): Enter the date of your electronic signature.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Double-check the dates and addresses for correctness.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the accommodation provider or contact the emergency contact for assistance.
* Sign the form electronically, confirming the accuracy of the information provided.
