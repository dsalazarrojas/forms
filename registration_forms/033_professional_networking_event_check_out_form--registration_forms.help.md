# professional_networking_event_check_out_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information and feedback from attendees of a professional networking event. You can use it to provide your details, such as your name and email, and optionally offer feedback about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Type your email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone Number" field.
5. If you attended the event, type the event name in the "Event Name" field.
6. If you have any feedback about the event, type it in the "Feedback" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Type your valid email address to enable communication with you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it to allow for easier contact.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): If you attended the event, type the name of the event to help us understand which event you're referring to.
* **Feedback** (`feedback`, text, optional): Share any thoughts or suggestions about the event to help us improve future events.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If a field is marked as required, be sure to complete it to ensure proper processing of your submission.
- If you're unsure about a field, try to provide the most relevant information available to you.
