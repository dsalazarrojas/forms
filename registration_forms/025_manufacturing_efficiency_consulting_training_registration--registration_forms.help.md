# manufacturing_efficiency_consulting_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the manufacturing efficiency consulting training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your registration.
2. Enter a brief description of your training needs or goals.
3. Choose the session dates you wish to attend.
4. Enter the number of attendees that will be attending the session.
5. Select the session start and end times.
6. Enter the session duration.
7. Provide any additional comments or remarks.
8. Select the user assigned to this registration.
9. Choose the tool to be used for this registration.
10. Enter the form ID numbers as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category for your registration from the options provided. This will help us understand the nature of your training needs.
* **Description** (`description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your training goals or needs. This will help us to tailor the training to your requirements.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the participant details you wish to provide. These details will be used to contact you or your representatives. You can choose from First name, Last name, Address, Phone number, and Email.
* **Session Dates** (`session_dates`, `date`, required): Choose the dates you wish to attend the session. This will help us schedule the training accordingly.
* **Attendee Count** (`attendee_count`, `number`, required): Enter the number of attendees that will be attending the session. This will help us prepare for the session.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, `time`, required): Select the start time of the session. This will help us schedule the training accordingly.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, `time`, required): Select the end time of the session. This will help us schedule the training accordingly.
* **Session Duration** (`session_duration`, `number`, required): Enter the duration of the session. This will help us plan the training accordingly.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional comments or remarks you may have. This is an optional field, but it will help us understand your requirements better.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `email`, required): Select the user assigned to this registration. This will help us ensure that your registration is processed efficiently.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Choose the tool to be used for this registration. This will help us ensure that the right tool is used for your registration.
* **Form ID 1-13** (`form_id_1`, `form_id_2`, ..., `form_id_13`, required): Enter the required form ID numbers as provided in the instructions above. 

Note: Please fill out all the form ID numbers as required. These fields are essential for your registration to be processed correctly.
