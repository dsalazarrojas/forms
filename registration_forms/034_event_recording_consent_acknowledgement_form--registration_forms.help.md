# Event Recording Consent Acknowledgement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from users for event recording on their person or property.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out this form if you are participating in an event where your person or property may be recorded.
- Fill out this form thoroughly and accurately, providing clear responses to each question.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Recording Consent Acknowledgement** (`event_recording_consent`, text): A brief explanation of the event recording process.
	+ Please type a short statement explaining why you are participating in this event.
- **Event Video/Audio** (`event_video_audio`, text): A description of the recording method.
	+ Please specify if you will be recorded via video, audio, or both.
- **Recording Device Description** (`recording_device_description`, text): Description of the recording device.
	+ Describe the device that will be used for recording.
- **Consent Given** (`consent_given`, select_one): Indicate your consent status.
	+ Select "True" if you give your consent, "False" otherwise.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text): Any additional information or concerns.
	+ Use this space to provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
