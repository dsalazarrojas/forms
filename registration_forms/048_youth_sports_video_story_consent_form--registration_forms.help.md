# youth-sports-video-story-consent-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect consent from parents for recording and sharing videos of their child's participation in sports activities, as well as collect their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's **Parent Name**, which should be your full name.
2. Indicate your **Consent** by selecting one of the two options provided:
	* Record and share videos of my child
	* Do not record or share videos of my child
3. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used for communication purposes.
4. Enter your **Phone** number, which will be used for emergency contact purposes.
5. Enter your child's **Date of Birth** in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Enter the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Phone** number of an emergency contact person.
7. Select the **Emergency Contact Relatives** from the list, which will determine who the emergency contact person is.
8. Review your form before submission to ensure all required fields are filled in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Your full name as a parent.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: Yes.
* **Consent**: Indicate whether or not to record and share videos of your child.
	+ Type: Select one option.
	+ Required: No.
* **Email**: Contact email address used for communication purposes.
	+ Type: Email field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Phone**: Emergency contact phone number.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
	+ Type: Date field.
	+ Required: Yes.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of a contact person in case of emergencies.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of a contact person in case of emergencies.
	+ Type: Text field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Email address of a contact person in case of emergencies.
	+ Type: Email field.
	+ Required: No.
* **Emergency Contact Relatives**: Select who the emergency contact person is from the list.
	+ Type: Select multiple options.
	+ Required: No.
