# Accounting Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information required for onboarding and billing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your business information.
2. Enter your contact details.
3. Choose your subscription plan.
4. Select your billing cycle.
5. Choose your payment method.
6. Provide any additional information if necessary.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions of the service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business name**: This is the legal or trading name of your business. Please enter the exact name as it appears on your business cards, website, or other official documentation. (Required)
* **Contact person**: This is the primary contact person for your account management. Please enter their name and title. (Required)
* **Contact email**: This is the email address we will use for billing and notifications. Please enter a valid email address. (Required)
* **Contact phone**: This is the phone number we will use for urgent communication. Please enter a valid phone number (Optional)
* **Service plan**: Choose the level of service you wish to subscribe to. Please select from the options: Basic, Standard, or Premium. (Required)
* **Billing cycle**: Choose how frequently you want to be billed. Please select from the options: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually. (Required)
* **Service start date**: This is the date you would like the service to start. Please enter a valid date in the format mm/dd/yyyy. (Optional)
* **Payment method**: Choose your preferred payment method. Please select from the options: Bank transfer, Credit card, or Invoice. (Required)
* **Purchase order number**: If you have a purchase order number for your billing, please enter it here. (Optional)
* **Accounting software integration**: If you would like to integrate our service with any accounting software you use, please indicate here. (Optional)
* **Terms and conditions acceptance**: Please indicate your acceptance of the subscription terms. Please select from the options: I agree, I do not agree. (Required)
* **Additional notes**: Please provide any additional information that may help us during onboarding. (Optional)

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
