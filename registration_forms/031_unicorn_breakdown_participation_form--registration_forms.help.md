# Unicorn Breakdown Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details, breakdown timing, locations, and consent for the "Unicorn Breakdown Participation" process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the relevant fields and providing the required information.
2. Fill out the "Unicorn Participation Details" field with any relevant information about your participation in the event.
3. Choose a date for "Breakdown Timing" that corresponds to the timing of the event.
4. Select multiple locations for "Locations" where you will be participating in the breakdown.
5. Choose whether you "Consent" to participate in the breakdown by selecting the correct option.
6. Enter your "Email" for contact purposes, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Unicorn Participation Details** (text, optional): This field is designed for you to provide any relevant information about your participation in the event.
* **Breakdown Timing** (date, optional): Choose a date that corresponds to the timing of the event.
* **Locations** (select multiple, optional): Select all the locations where you will be participating in the breakdown.
* **Consent** (select one, optional): Choose whether you consent to participate in the breakdown by selecting "I consent" or "I do not consent".
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you would like to be contacted for any further information.
