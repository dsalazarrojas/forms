## fashion_design_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to attend a fashion design workshop. The form is intended to gather details such as participant contact information, workshop preferences, and demographic details. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a valid email address in the `participant_email` field.
2. Enter your name in the `participant_name` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `participant_phone` field.
4. Select your workshop preferences from the options in the `workshop_preferences` field.
5. Select your gender from the options in the `participant_gender` field.
6. Fill in your address in the `participant_address` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_details** (`participant_details`, text, not required): Enter your details to help us get to know you better. This will not be used to determine your eligibility to attend the workshop.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): Enter your contact details so we can keep in touch with you after the workshop.
* **workshop_preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, select_multiple, not required): Select the workshop preferences that interest you the most. This will help us tailor the workshop content to your needs.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, not required): Enter your name as it appears on your official documents. This is required for workshop attendance records.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you with workshop updates and reminders.
* **participant_gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, not required): Select your gender to help us cater to your needs during the workshop.
* **participant_address** (`participant_address`, text, not required): Enter your address so we can send workshop materials and updates to you.
