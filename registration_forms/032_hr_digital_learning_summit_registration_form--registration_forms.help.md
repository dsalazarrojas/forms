# HR Digital Learning Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants to the HR Digital Learning Summit, a conference where participants can learn from industry experts and network with peers. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields completely and accurately. 
2. Review the form for any errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`3`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid email address that you check frequently. This will be used for updates on your registration status and workshop schedules.
- **Phone** (`4`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
- **Date** (`5`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date you plan to attend the summit (e.g., 2024-02-27).
- **Time** (`6`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time you plan to start attending the summit (e.g., 09:00).
- **Note** (`7`, `note`, `optional`): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
- **Workshops** (`8`, `select_multiple`, `optional`): Select the workshops you would like to attend.
- **Workshop List** (`9`, `note`, `optional`): You will see a list of selected workshops.
- **Registration Date** (`10`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your registration.
- **Registration Time** (`11`, `time`, `optional`): Enter the time of your registration.
- **Registration Status** (`12`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose your current registration status.
- **Status List** (`13`, `note`, `optional`): You will see a list of your current status.
- **Payment Gateway** (`14`, `text`, `optional`): This field is not used in this form, it might be for internal use.
- **Payment Method** (`15`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose how you plan to pay for the workshops.
- **Payment Status** (`16`, `select_one`, `optional`): This field is not used in this form, it might be for internal use.
- **Payment Date** (`17`, `date`, `optional`): Enter the date of payment.
- **Workshop Name** (`18`, `text`, `optional`): Enter the name of the workshop you are interested in.
- **Workshop Price** (`19`, `number`, `optional`): Enter the price of the workshop you selected.
- **Participant Name** (`20`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your name as a participant.
- **Participant Email** (`21`, `email`, `optional`): Enter your email address as a participant.
- **Participant Phone** (`22`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your phone number as a participant.
- **Participant Status** (`23`, `select_one`, `optional`): Choose your current participant status.
- **Participant List** (`24`, `note`, `optional`): You will see a list of participants.
- **Payment Method List** (`25`, `note`, `optional`): This field is not used in this form, it might be for internal use.
