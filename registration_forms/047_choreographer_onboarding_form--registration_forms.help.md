# Choreographer Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for choreographers who wish to be considered for collaboration, freelance, or full-time positions with the organization. It will help us understand your personal and professional background, availability, and preferences to determine the best fit for your skills and experience.
## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please answer all the questions accurately and thoroughly.
2.  Use the provided options for select_multiple, select_one, and select_one fields.
3.  Enter dates in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
4.  Make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number.
5.  Attach any relevant documents or links to your portfolio, previous experience, or references.
6.  Review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Personal Information** (`personal_information`): This page is a note field for your general contact details.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`): Your first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`): Your last name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`): If you prefer to be addressed differently, please provide that name.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`): Your contact phone number.
*   **Email Address** (`email`): Your contact email address.
*   **Address** (`address`): Your physical address, including street, city, and zip code.
*   **Professional Background** (`professional_background`): A brief description of your experience and relevant background information.
*   **Years of Choreography Experience** (`years_experience`): Your total experience in choreography.
*   **Dance Styles Specialized In** (`dance_styles`): The dance styles you have experience with, including Ballet, Jazz, Contemporary, Hip hop, Tap, Modern, Ballroom, Latin, Cultural or folk, Commercial, and Other.
*   **Primary Style** (`primary_style`): The dance style you specialize in.
*   **Dance Education** (`education`): A description of your dance education and training.
*   **Certifications** (`certifications`): Any relevant certifications or qualifications you hold.
*   **Previous Experience** (`previous_experience`): A description of your previous work experience as a choreographer.
*   **Previous Companies or Studios** (`previous_companies`): A list of previous companies or studios you've worked with, including dates.
*   **Notable Productions or Works** (`notable_productions`): Notable works or productions you've been involved in.
*   **Awards or Recognition** (`awards_recognition`): Any awards or recognition you've received in your choreography career.
*   **Professional References** (`references`): Contact information for professional references.
*   **Portfolio or Website** (`portfolio`): A link to your portfolio or website, if applicable.
*   **Availability** (`availability`): A brief description of your schedule and availability.
*   **Available Days** (`available_days`): Select all the days of the week you are available to work.
*   **Available Times** (`available_times`): Select all the times of day you are available to work.
*   **Available Start Date** (`start_date`): The earliest date you can start working with us.
*   **Schedule Flexibility** (`schedule_flexibility`): The level of flexibility you have with your schedule.
*   **Teaching Experience** (`teaching_experience`): A brief description of your teaching background.
*   **Have Teaching Experience?** (`have_teaching_experience`): If you have teaching experience, select Yes, extensive, Yes, some, or False.
*   **Age Groups** (`age_groups`): The age groups you are comfortable working with, including Toddlers (2-4), Children (5-10), Teens (11-17), Adults, Seniors, or All ages.
*   **Skill Levels** (`skill_levels`): The skill levels you can teach, including Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional, or All levels.
*   **Project Preferences** (`project_preferences`): A description of the types of projects you prefer to work on.
*   **Types of Projects Interested In** (`interested_projects`): Select all the types of projects you are interested in, including Studio classes, Competition routines, Recital pieces, Music videos, Stage productions, Corporate events, Weddings, Private coaching, or Other.
*   **Rate Expectations** (`rate_expectations`): A brief description of your expected compensation or rate.
*   **Preferred Rate** (`preferred_rate`): Your preferred rate, if any.
*   **Rate Negotiable** (`rate_negotiable`): If your rate is negotiable, select True.
*   **Additional Skills** (`additional_skills`): A description of any additional skills you have beyond choreography.
*   **Do you have other relevant skills?** (`other_skills`): If you have any other skills, please describe them.
*   **Do you have your own equipment?** (`equipment`): If you have your own equipment, select Yes, all needed, Some equipment, or False.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): The name and phone number of an emergency contact.
*   **How did you hear about us?** (`how_did_you_hear`): The source that directed you to this opportunity.
*   **Why are you interested in working with us?** (`why_interested`): A brief description of your interest in working with the organization.
*   **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Any additional information you'd like to share.
*   **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`agree_terms`): Select Yes, I agree.
*   **Choreographer Signature** (`choreographer_signature`): A digital signature to confirm your submission.
*   **Date of Onboarding** (`onboarding_date`): The date you complete this form.
