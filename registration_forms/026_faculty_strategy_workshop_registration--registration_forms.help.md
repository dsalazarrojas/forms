# Faculty Strategy Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Faculty Strategy Workshop Registration form is used to gather information from faculty members for the Faculty Strategy Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by the asterisk `*` symbol) and any additional optional fields as necessary.
2. Review each field for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (1): *Provide a brief description of yourself, including your name, title, and department.*
* **Faculty Details** (2): *Please provide a description of your faculty role and any relevant background information.*
* **Workshop Preferences** (3): *Choose the session(s) you wish to attend during the workshop.*
* **Participation Consent** (4): *Please indicate your agreement to participate in the workshop.*
* **Session Preferences** (5): *Please select your preferred session topics or format.*
* **Session Details** (6): *Provide any additional details about your sessions preferences.*
* **Faculty Strategy Workshop Registration Form Submitter** (7): *Please provide your name and department (if any)*
* **Email Preferences** (8): *Select the email frequencies you would like to receive from us.*
* **Phone Preferences** (9): *Select the phone frequencies you would like to receive from us.*
* **Submit** (10): *Submit your completed form for review.*
* **Note** (11): *Provide any additional comments or notes about your workshop participation.*
* **Email** (12): *Your work email address (optional, but recommended for us to contact you).*
