# Event Attendance Sign In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from event attendees for event registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields with your personal and event-related information:

1. **Event Organizer Information** (Page 1, Field 1)
	* This field is for event organizers or staff to collect their information. Please do not fill out this field.
2. **Attendee Information** (Page 1, Field 2)
	* Provide your personal information as an event attendee.
3. **Event Details** (Page 1, Field 3)
	* Enter details about the event you are attending.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer Information (event_organizer_info)** (`text`, Required: False)
	* This field is not meant for event attendees to fill out. Please do not fill out this field.
* **Attendee Information (attendee_info)** (`text`, Required: False)
	* This field is for you to provide your personal information as an event attendee.
* **Event Details (event_details)** (`text`, Required: False)
	* Enter any additional details about the event you are attending, such as event name or location.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the event organizer or staff.
* You can use this form multiple times if you are attending multiple events.
