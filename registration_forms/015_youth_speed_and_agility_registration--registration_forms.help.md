# Youth Speed And Agility Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Speed And Agility Registration form is designed to collect essential information from participants and their parent or guardian to ensure their safety and well-being during the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section, including name, date of birth, and any other details that are required.
2. Provide contact information for your parent or guardian, including their name, contact method, and phone number.
3. List any medical conditions that affect your participant and provide any necessary medical notes.
4. Identify an emergency contact in case of an incident during the program.
5. Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information for all fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info (`participant_info`, text, optional)**: This section is for collecting basic participant information, such as their name and details. If you have any additional info that's not covered in this field, please mention it in the "Notes" section.
* **Parent/Guardian Info (`parent_info`, text, optional)**: This section is for collecting the parent or guardian's name and contact information. Please make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Date Of Birth (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, optional)**: This field is for recording the participant's date of birth. Make sure to use the correct format for the date of birth.
* **Address (`participant_address`, text, optional)**: This field is for collecting the participant's address. Please provide the complete address, including the street number and postal code.
* **Phone Number (`participant_phone_number`, text, optional)**: This field is for collecting the participant's phone number. Please make sure to use the correct format (XXX-XXXX-XXXX).
* **Parent/Guardian Permission (`parent_permission`, text, optional)**: This section is for collecting the parent or guardian's permission for the participant to participate in the program.
* **Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, text, optional)**: This field is for listing any medical conditions that affect the participant. Please provide any necessary medical notes.
* **Medical Notes (`participant_medical_notes`, text, optional)**: This field is for providing any additional medical information that's not covered in the "Medical Conditions" field.
* **Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, text, optional)**: This section is for identifying an emergency contact in case of an incident during the program.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional)**: This field is for collecting the emergency contact's phone number. Please make sure to use the correct format (XXX-XXXX-XXXX).

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness.
* Use the correct format for dates and phone numbers.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Please note that the form is optional, but we recommend filling it out to ensure we have accurate and up-to-date information about you.
