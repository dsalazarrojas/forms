# Athletics Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletics Program Enrollment Form is used to collect information from participants for enrollment in sports programs. This form requires accurate and complete information to ensure proper enrollment and safe participation in athletic activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
   - Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
   - Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
2. Select your gender.
3. Provide your school name and grade level.
4. (If applicable) Enter your student ID number.
5. Fill in your home address, city, state, and ZIP code.
6. Enter your parent or guardian's name and contact information.
7. Select the programs and sports you wish to enroll in.
8. Choose your season of participation.
9. Specify your academic year.
10. Indicate previous participation status.
11. List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
12. Mention any current medications.
13. Provide your health insurance provider's information.
14. Enter your doctor's name and phone number.
15. Specify the date of your most recent physical examination.
16. Indicate your current clearance status.
17. Choose how you plan to travel to practices and events.
18. (Optional) Enter the fee amount and fee waiver request.
19. Accept the code of conduct agreement.
20. Understand and accept the risks associated with athletic participation.
21. Grant permission for medical treatment.
22. Allow or deny media coverage.
23. Sign the form with your full name (twice for parent/guardian).
24. Date the signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full, first and last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your biological gender.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level**: Choose your current grade level.
* **Student ID Number**: If you have a student ID, please enter it here.
* **Street Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State**: Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter your parent or guardian's contact phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Specify your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Program Selection**: Select all the programs you wish to enroll in.
* **Sport Selection**: Select all the sports you wish to participate in.
* **Season**: Choose the season in which you wish to participate.
* **School Year**: Specify your current academic year.
* **Previous Participation**: Indicate whether you have participated in sports before.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy or member number.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Enter the name of your regular doctor.
* **Primary Physician Phone**: Enter the contact number of your regular doctor.
* **Physical Examination Date**: Enter the date of your most recent sports physical.
* **Medical Clearance Status**: Indicate your current clearance status.
* **Transportation Plan**: Choose how you plan to travel to practices and events.
* **Program Fee**: (Optional) Enter the fee amount.
* **Fee Waiver Needed**: Indicate whether you need a fee waiver.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement**: Accept the athletic code of conduct.
* **Assumption of Risk**: Understand and accept the risks associated with athletic participation.
* **Medical Treatment Permission**: Grant permission for medical treatment.
* **Media Release**: Allow or deny media coverage.
* **Participant Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign the form with your full name.
* **Signature Date**: Date your signature.

Note: All fields marked with "required: true" must be filled out, while fields not marked as required can be left blank if not applicable.
