# Workshop Participant Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to capture essential information about workshop participants to ensure a smooth and successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field, following the standard email format (username@domain.com).
4. Provide the name of the workshop you're registering for in the "Workshop Name" field.
5. Select your role in the workshop from the "Role" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Optionally, enter your address in the "Address" field if necessary.
8. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
9. Enter your city in the "City" field.
10. Enter your state in the "State" field.
11. Enter your country in the "Country" field.
12. (Optional) Add any additional notes about your registration in the "Note" field.
13. (Optional) If you have been assigned a tool, select it from the "Assigned Tool" dropdown menu.
14. (Optional) Enter any other details about the tool you've been assigned in the "Assigned Tool 0" field.
15. You will not need to fill in "Form ID", "Created By", "Updated By", "Deleted By", "Deleted At", "Created At", "Updated At" as these fields are used for internal purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is commonly known.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the standard format (username@domain.com).
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, required): Select your role in the workshop from the dropdown menu.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address if necessary.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Enter your country.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, optional): Select the tool you have been assigned.
* **Assigned Tool 0** (`assigned_tool_0`, `select_one`, optional): Select the tool you have been assigned.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, `number`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, `text`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`, `text`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`, `text`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`, `time`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Created At** (`created_at`, `time`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`, `time`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`, `text`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Created At 2** (`created_at_2`, `date`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Updated At 2** (`updated_at_2`, `date`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
* **Deleted At 2** (`deleted_at_2`, `date`, optional): You will not need to fill this field.
