# Ski School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski School Admission Form is designed to collect necessary information from students to provide a safe and enjoyable ski experience. Please fill out this form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your student's details.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled, marked as such.
3. Enter accurate contact information for yourself as the student's guardian.
4. Specify any medical conditions or special needs.
5. Review and submit the form for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (Student Name)** (`text`, **required:** no): Enter the student's full name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Parent Email (Parent Email)** (`email`, **optional**): Enter the email address you'd like us to use for communication.
* **Birth Date (Birth Date)** (`date`, **optional**): Enter the student's birthdate in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Contact Number (Contact Number)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter a phone number we can use to contact you.
* **Ski School ID (Ski School ID)** (`number`, **optional**): If you have a Ski School ID, enter it here.
* **Class Type (Class Type)** (`select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the ski classes your student is registered for (Ski 1, Ski 2, Ski 3, or Ski 4).
* **Student Height (Student Height)** (`number`, **optional**): Enter the student's height in inches.
* **Student Weight (Student Weight)** (`number`, **optional**): Enter the student's weight in pounds.
* **Guardian Name (Guardian Name)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter your name, the student's legal guardian.
* **Guardian Email (Guardian Email)** (`email`, **optional**): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Guardian Phone (Guardian Phone)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter an alternative contact person's phone number in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition (Medical Condition)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Select if your student has any medical conditions (Epilepsy, Asthma, or Other).
* **Special Needs (Special Needs)** (`select_multiple`, **optional**): Choose languages or other special needs (English, Spanish, Mandarin).
* **Medical Notes (Medical Notes)** (`note`, **optional**): Enter any additional medical notes or information.
* **Emergency Notes (Emergency Notes)** (`note`, **optional**): Enter any emergency notes or information.
* **Student Diet (Student Diet)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Select the student's dietary preference (None, Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Other).
* **Class Size (Class Size)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Choose the class size preference (Small, Medium, Large, Other).
* **Student Age (Student Age)** (`date`, **optional**): Enter the student's age in YYYY format.
* **Class Schedules (Class Schedules)** (`select_multiple`, **optional**): Select the days your student is available for ski classes (Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri, Sat, Sun).
* **Student Gender (Student Gender)** (`select_one`, **optional**): Select the student's gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Student Address (Student Address)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the student's home address.
* **Student City (Student City)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the student's city.
* **Country (Country)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the student's country.
* **Postal Code (Postal Code)** (`text`, **optional**): Enter the student's postal code.

Note: All fields marked as **required** must be filled before submission.
