# Afternoon Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the student's information, including their name and homeroom teacher.
2. Select the date of the student's absence.
3. Specify the periods or timeframes the student will be absent.
4. Provide a brief description of the reason for the student's absence.
5. Enter the parent or guardian's contact number for confirmation.
6. Indicate if the student requires makeup work.
7. Add any additional notes or instructions for the school staff.
8. Enter the parent's electronic signature name for confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student name** (Student name, Text, Required): Enter the student's full name as they are enrolled in school.
* **Homeroom teacher** (Homeroom teacher, Text, Optional): Enter the teacher assigned to the student's homeroom.
* **Date of absence** (Date of absence, Date, Required): Select the date the student will be absent.
* **Absent periods or timeframe** (Absent periods or timeframe, Text, Required): Specify the periods or timeframes the student will be absent. For example, "Afternoon only" or "Periods 4 and 5".
* **Reason for absence** (Reason for absence, Text, Required): Provide a brief description of the reason for the student's absence, such as "Illness" or "Appointment".
* **Parent or guardian contact number** (Parent or guardian contact number, Text, Required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number for confirmation of the absence.
* **Does the student require makeup work** (Does the student require makeup work, Select One, Required): Check if the teacher should provide makeup assignments for the student.
* **Additional notes** (Additional notes, Text, Optional): Add any special instructions or notes for the school staff.
* **Parent electronic signature name** (Parent electronic signature name, Text, Required): Enter your full name for electronic signature confirmation.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately to ensure the school staff can properly document the student's absence.
* Use a specific and concise reason for the student's absence to help the teacher and school staff understand the situation.
* Make sure to enter the correct parent or guardian contact number to facilitate confirmation of the absence.
