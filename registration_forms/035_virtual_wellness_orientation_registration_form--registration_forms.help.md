# Virtual Wellness Orientation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in attending a virtual wellness event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your role in the event (attendee, presenter, or organizer).
3. Choose the type of event you are interested in (wellness event, workshop, seminar, or conference).
4. Provide accurate contact information for team members and session leaders.
5. Fill out any additional fields as required or relevant to your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info**: Enter a brief description of yourself as a participant.
* **name**: Enter your full name.
* **email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **event_type**: Select the type of event you are interested in (wellness event, workshop, seminar, or conference).
* **participant_type**: Select your role in the event (attendee, presenter, or organizer).
* **session_info**: Enter a brief description of your session.
* **start_date**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **end_date**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **start_time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **end_time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **location**: Select where the event will be held (online or in-person).
* **description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **session_id**: Enter your session ID or name.
* **team_members**: Enter a list of team members who will be present at the event.
* **team_lead**: Enter your team leader's name.
* **session_leader**: Enter the session leader's name.
* **session_leader_contact**: Enter the session leader's contact information.
* **session_leader_email**: Enter the session leader's email address.
* **session_leader_phone**: Enter the session leader's phone number.
* **team_members_email**: Enter a list of team members' email addresses.
* **team_members_phone**: Enter a list of team members' phone numbers.
* **notes**: Enter any additional notes about your session or team.
* **confirmation**: Select the status of your submission (confirmed, not confirmed, or pending).
* **submission**: Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

Note: This form is intended to be filled out by users who are interested in attending, presenting, or leading a session at the virtual wellness event.
