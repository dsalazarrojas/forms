# School Fee Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from students for school fees and supplies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields and submit your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School fee payment form** (`school_fee_payment_form`, text, optional): This field is where you enter your school fee payment details, if any.
* **Payment details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): This field is for entering any additional payment information you need to submit.
* **Order supplies** (`order_supplies`, text, optional): This field is for ordering school supplies, if needed.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill in all the required fields correctly to avoid any errors.
* If you have any questions or issues while filling the form, contact the school administration.
