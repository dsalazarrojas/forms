# product_innovation_incubator_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The product_innovation_incubator_registration_form is used to register a product or idea into the innovation incubator program. This form gathers essential information about the product or idea, including founder details, idea description, program goals, contact information, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Founder Details section, which includes fields for the idea owner's name, email, and other contact information.
2. In the Idea Description section, provide a brief description of the product or idea.
3. Move on to the Program Goals section, where you can describe the program's objectives and how it will achieve them.
4. Fill out the Contact Information section with your email address and other relevant contact details.
5. Choose the correct industry category for your product or idea.
6. Select the idea stage (concept, development, launch, scaling, or proof of concept).
7. Enter the size of your team and the founding date of your company.
8. Enter your company website and social media links (LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram).
9. Confirm your email and phone number for contact purposes.
10. Finally, submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Founder Details**: This section is required and includes fields for the idea owner's name, address, and other contact information. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately.
* **Idea Description**: This section is also required and is used to describe the product or idea briefly.
* **Program Goals**: In this section, describe the program's objectives and how they will be achieved.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your email address and other relevant contact details for communication.
* **Industry**: Select the industry that best fits your product or idea from the provided options.
* **Idea Stage**: Choose the current stage of your idea (concept, development, launch, scaling, or proof of concept).
* **Team Size**: Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Founding Date**: Enter the date your company was founded.
* **Website**: Enter your company website URL.
* **LinkedIn**, **Facebook**, **Twitter**, **Instagram**: Enter your company's social media links.
* **Other (Social Media)**: If your company has other social media links not listed, enter them here.
* **Email Confirmation**: Confirm your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Confirmation**: Confirm your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Submit**: Complete the form by submitting it once all required fields are filled out accurately.
