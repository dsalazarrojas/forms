# Growth Management Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect user information and course details from individuals interested in enrolling in the Growth Management Course. This form is used to ensure that all necessary details are collected for the course enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Info" field.
2. Provide the course details in the "Course Details" field.
3. Enter your payment information in the "Payment Info" field.
4. Confirm your information by entering it in the "Confirm Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (`user_info`, text, optional)
User Info: This field is where you will enter your personal information. Please fill in your name and any other identifying details as required.

### Course Details (`course_details`, text, optional)
Course Details: This field is where you will enter the details of the course you are interested in enrolling in. Please provide any relevant information about the course you are interested in.

### Payment Info (`payment_info`, text, optional)
Payment Info: This field is where you will enter your payment information. Please fill in the necessary details for payment processing.

### Confirm Info (`confirm_info`, text, optional)
Confirm Info: This field is where you will confirm your information. Please re-enter the information you entered in the previous fields to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your application is processed correctly.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form administrators at `form@gic.mx`.
