# Urban Land Use Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users who are interested in attending the Urban Land Use Conference. It is designed to be simple and user-friendly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name, surname, company, email, phone number, and date of birth in the required fields.
2. Indicate your conference attendance by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
3. If you have any dietary requirements, select the corresponding option.
4. If you have any comments or feedback, type them in the comments field.
5. Finally, fill in the assigned tool field if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`registration_form`, text, optional): Please fill in your name.
* **Surname** (`surname`, text, optional): Fill in your surname.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Fill in your company name or affiliation.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Fill in your phone number (including country code).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format of year-month-day.
* **Conference Attendance** (`conference_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you will attend the conference, "No" otherwise.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, select_one, optional): Select your dietary requirements (Vegan, Vegetarian, or None).
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback you have about the conference.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Fill in the assigned tool if required.
* **Form IDs** (`form_ids`, text, optional): Not required. Please ignore this field.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, text, optional): Not required. Please ignore this field.
* **Jotform User** (`jotform_user`, text, optional): Not required. Please ignore this field.
