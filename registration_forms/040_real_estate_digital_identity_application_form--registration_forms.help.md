# Real Estate Digital Identity Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for digital identity verification and authentication in the real estate industry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please start by filling out the required fields, which are marked with an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
2. Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email address to identify yourself.
3. Enter your Phone Number, which will be used for contact purposes.
4. If applicable, provide your Date of Birth to verify your age.
5. Enter your Address, City, and Zip Code to provide your location.
6. Select your Employment Status, which can be Active, Inactive, or Retiree.
7. Choose your Employment Type, which can be W2, 1099, or Corporate.
8. Select your Industry, which can be Finance, Healthcare, or Technology.
9. Enter your Job Title, which is an optional field.
10. Provide your Company Name, which is an optional field.
11. Enter your Job Description, which is an optional field.
12. If applicable, enter your Years of Experience to showcase your experience.
13. Enter your Job Location, which is an optional field.
14. Select your Job Type, which can be Full-time, Part-time, or Freelance.
15. Choose your Job Shift, which can be Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
16. Enter your Work Hours, which is an optional field.
17. Enter Job Terminology, which is an optional field.
18. Select your Company Size, which can be an optional field.
19. Choose your Company Type, which can be Public, Private, or Non-Profit.
20. Select your Business Type, which can be Business or Individual.
21. Finally, provide your Signature, which is used for authentication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, *): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, *): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you want to provide your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for location verification.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city for location verification.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select your state of residence.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for location verification.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, optional): Select your current employment status.
* **Employment Type** (`employment_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your employment type.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_multiple, optional): Select your industry.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_of_experience`, text, optional): Enter your years of experience.
* **Job Location** (`job_location`, text, optional): Enter your job location.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, optional): Select your job type.
* **Job Shift** (`job_shift`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your job shift.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, optional): Enter your work hours.
* **Job Terminology** (`job_terminology`, text, optional): Enter job terminology.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, optional): Enter your company size.
* **Company Type** (`company_type`, select_one, optional): Select your company type.
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your business type.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, optional): Provide your signature for authentication purposes.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate information to avoid any issues with your digital identity application.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your administrator for assistance.
- The form will be reviewed and approved by our team before proceeding with your digital identity application.
