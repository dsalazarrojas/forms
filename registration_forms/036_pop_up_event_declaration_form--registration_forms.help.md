# Pop Up Event Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pop Up Event Declaration Form is used to declare your interest in participating in a pop-up event. This form collects basic information about you, such as your name and contact details, to ensure you receive updates and instructions about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the designated field. This is the name that you would like to be known by at the event.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field. This is also the name that you would like to be known by at the event.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field. This is the email address where you would like to receive updates and instructions about the event.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field. This is the phone number where you would like to receive calls and messages from the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name**: 
  * Enter your given name. This field is not required for participation.
* **last_name**:
  * Enter your surname.
  * This field is not required for participation.
* **email**: 
  * Enter the email address where you would like to receive updates and instructions about the event.
  * This field is required for participation, as you will receive important information about the event via email.
* **phone**: 
  * Enter your phone number where you would like to receive calls and messages from the event organizers.
  * This field is not required for participation, but recommended for better communication.
