# digital_merchandising_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for a digital merchandising workshop session. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your registration is complete and accurate.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your session type from the options provided in the "Session Type" field.
2. Choose your start and end dates for the workshop in the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your company information in the "Company" field.
7. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Select any additional attendees from the provided options in the "Attendees" field.
9. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Type (select_one)**: Select the type of session you would like to attend (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Start Date (date)**: Enter the date when the workshop will start.
* **End Date (date)**: Enter the date when the workshop will end.
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name (e.g., John, Jane).
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address (e.g., john.doe@example.com).
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number (e.g., 123-456-7890).
* **Company (text)**: Enter your company name (e.g., ABC Corporation).
* **Job Title (text)**: Enter your job title (e.g., Marketing Manager).
* **Attendees (select_multiple)**: Select any additional attendees that will be attending the workshop (e.g., John, Jane, Jane Doe).
* **Additional Comments (note)**: Add any additional comments about your registration (e.g., special requests, questions).

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you are registering multiple attendees, please select their names in the "Attendees" field.
* If you have any special requests or questions, please add them in the "Additional Comments" field.
