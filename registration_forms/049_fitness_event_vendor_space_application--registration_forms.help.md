# Fitness Event Vendor Space Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a vendor for a fitness event. It collects contact details, booth preferences, insurance status, and other relevant information from the vendor.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Contact Details**: Fill out this section with your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. **Booth Preferences**: Choose the type of booth you would like to have at the event.
3. **Insurance Status**: Select your insurance status from the options provided.
4. **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, such as power supply or internet access, please specify them here.
5. **Vendor Details**: Provide a brief description of your business or company.
6. **Vendor Name**: Enter your company name or business name.
7. **Vendor Email**: Enter your company email address.
8. **Vendor Phone**: Enter your company phone number.
9. **Vendor Address**: Enter your company address.
10. **Vendor Size**: If you are a small, medium, or large business, please select the corresponding size.
11. **Vendor Insurance**: Select the type of insurance you have.
12. **Vendor Notes**: If you have any notes or comments, please add them here.
13. **Special Preferences**: If you have any special preferences, such as power supply or internet access, please select them here.
14. **Vendor Agreement**: Select the type of vendor agreement you agree to.
15. **Additional Details**: If you have any additional details, such as a brief description of your business, please provide them here.
16. **Vendor Representative**: If you have a representative, please enter their name and contact information.
17. **Vendor License**: If you have a license, please upload it here.
18. **Vendor Tax ID**: If you have a tax ID, please upload it here.
19. **Vendor Business Type**: Select the type of business you have.
20. **Vendor Industry**: Enter your industry or sector.
21. **Vendor Services**: Briefly describe the services you offer.
22. **Vendor Equipment**: If you have any equipment or tools, please describe them here.
23. **Vendor Experience**: If you have experience in the industry, please enter it here.
24. **Vendor Space Requirements**: If you have any space requirements, such as power supply or internet access, please specify them here.
25. **Vendor Other Details**: If you have any other details, please provide them here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and documents before filling out the form.
- Some fields are optional, so feel free to fill them out at your discretion.
- If you have any questions or need help, please contact us.
