# Resilience And Well Being Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Resilience And Well Being Registration Form is a collection of demographic data and program interest information from individuals who wish to register for one of the offered programs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
5. Enter your Date of Birth.
6. Choose the program(s) you are interested in by checking the corresponding option(s) from the dropdown menu. 
7. Indicate how often you are interested in the program by selecting the corresponding option from the dropdown menu.
8. If you have any additional comments, feel free to provide them in the text box below.
9. Agree to the Terms and Conditions by typing "I agree" in the text box provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your first name as per your legal identity documents.
* **Last Name**: Your last name as per your legal identity documents.
* **Email**: A valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Your phone number where we can reach you for further information or updates.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth as per your legal identity documents.
* **Which program is the participant interested in?**: Select the program(s) you are interested in to be part of the corresponding program.
* **How often is the participant interested in the program?**: Choose how often you would like to participate in the program.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional information or comments about your interest in the program.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the program by typing 'I agree'.

## Tips

* Ensure that your email address is correct to facilitate communication and updates about the program.
* Make sure to choose the correct program and frequency of interest as per your preferences.
* If you have any specific requirements or needs, please provide them in the Additional Comments section.
* Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions before submitting the form.
