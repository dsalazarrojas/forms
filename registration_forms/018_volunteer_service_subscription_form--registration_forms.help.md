# Volunteer Service Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Service Subscription Form is a tool for volunteers to register their interest in serving with the organization. It collects essential contact information and volunteer preferences to facilitate communication and task assignment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date and time of availability in the "Date" and "Time" fields.
6. Choose your preferred volunteer type from the "Volunteer Type" dropdown menu. You can select multiple options if applicable.
7. Select your preferred role within the organization, such as Volunteer, Team Member, or Staff.
8. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your first name for reference purposes.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Enter your last name for reference purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, required: false): Select a date you are available to serve.
* **Time** (`time`, required: false): Select a time of availability.
* **Volunteer Type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your preferred volunteer type, such as Volunteer, Team Member, or Other.
*   *Volunteer*: A general volunteer who contributes time and skills to the organization.
  *Team Member*: A specific role within the organization that requires more commitment and responsibilities.
  *Other*: A volunteer who doesn't fit into the above categories.
* **Role** (`select_one`, required: false): Choose your preferred role within the organization, such as Volunteer, Team Member, or Staff.
* **Notes** (`note`, required: false): Provide any additional information or comments you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
* Be accurate and clear in your answers to help us better understand your needs and preferences.
* If you are registering multiple people, please fill out this form for each individual.
* If you have any questions or need more information, feel free to contact us.
