# Camping Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camping Waiver Form is a document that aims to ensure participants' safety and well-being during camping activities. It collects personal and medical information, acknowledges risks, and grants permission for emergency medical treatment. Completing this form is mandatory for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your personal information:
    *   **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
    *   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
    *   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
    *   **Age**: Enter your age.
    *   **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address.
    *   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
2.  If under 18, provide guardian information:
    *   **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your parent or guardian's name.
    *   **Parent or Guardian Phone Number**: Enter your parent or guardian's phone number.
    *   **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
3.  Disclose health information:
    *   **Medical Conditions or Disabilities**: List any medical conditions or disabilities you have.
    *   **Known Allergies**: List any known allergies you have.
    *   **Current Medications**: List any current medications you are taking.
4.  Indicate your physical fitness level:
    *   Select your level of physical fitness for outdoor camping activities.
5.  Acknowledge risks and liability:
    *   **Assume All Risks**: Indicate that you assume all risks associated with camping and outdoor activities.
    *   **Understand Hazards**: Confirm that you understand the inherent hazards of camping.
    *   **Release Liability**: Indicate that you release the organizers from all liability claims.
    *   **Authorize Emergency Treatment**: Confirm that you authorize emergency medical treatment if necessary.
6.  Provide emergency contact information:
    *   **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the emergency contact.
    *   **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
7.  Finally, sign the form:
    *   **Date of Signature**: Enter the date of your signature for record-keeping purposes.

Remember to review the form carefully and answer all questions truthfully. If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the relevant authorities for clarification.
