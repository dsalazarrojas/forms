# Archery Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Archery Registration Form is used to collect personal and archery-related information from individuals interested in joining the archery club. This form helps us understand your background, experience, and preferences to provide you with the best possible experience within the club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
2. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
3. Indicate your archery experience level and years of experience.
4. Choose a program of interest (e.g., target archery, 3D field archery, competition, recreation, or instruction).
5. Select your preferred meeting times (if applicable).
6. Read and agree to the membership agreement and liability waiver.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2): Your full name as you would like to be known by others.
* **Date of Birth** (3): The date of your birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Gender** (4): Your gender identity.
* **Email Address** (5): Your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (6): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (7): Your physical address where you can be reached.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (9): The name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (10): The phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (11): How you are related to the emergency contact person.
* **Archery Experience Level** (13): Your level of archery experience (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert).
* **Years of Archery Experience** (14): The number of years you have been practicing archery.
* **Why do you want to join the club?** (15): A brief statement about why you're interested in joining the club.
* **Program of Interest** (17): The type of archery program you are interested in (target archery, 3D field archery, competition, recreation, instruction).
* **Bow Type** (18): The type of bow you prefer to use for archery.
* **Preferred Meeting Times** (19): Choose one or more days and times for meetings that suit you best.
* **Membership Agreement and Liability Waiver** (21): Agree or disagree with the club's membership agreement and liability waiver.
* **Membership Fee Paid** (22): Indicate whether you have paid the membership fee.
