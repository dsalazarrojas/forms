# sustainable_logistics_council_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainable Logistics Council Registration Form is used to register new members to join the council. This guide will walk you through each section of the form, explaining what each field is and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Review all fields carefully and fill out each one completely.
2. Ensure all required fields (marked as required) have a valid input.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member First Name** (`member_first_name`, text, optional): This is the first name of the individual registering. If the individual has a preferred name, please enter it here.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If the individual is registering as a representative of a company, please enter the name of the company here.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If applicable, please enter the job title of the individual registering.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that the individual has access to and checks frequently.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter a valid phone number that the individual can be reached at.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the individual's date of birth in the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, time, optional): Select the time zone the individual is registered in.
* **Sustainability Focus** (`sustainability_focus`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the areas of focus for the individual's sustainability efforts.
	+ Yes: This individual's work focuses on sustainability.
	+ No: This individual's work does not focus on sustainability.
* **Council Level** (`council_level`, select_one, optional): Choose the council level the individual is registering at.
	+ High: This individual is registering as a high-level council member.
	+ Low: This individual is registering as a low-level council member.
* **Member Type** (`member_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of council member the individual is registering as.
	+ Yes: This individual is registering as a regular member.
	+ No: This individual is not registering as a member.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, please contact the Sustainable Logistics Council support team.
