# College Athlete Showcase Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Athlete Showcase Registration form is designed to collect information from college athletes participating in a showcase event. The purpose of this form is to provide a standardized way of collecting athlete's personal and sport-specific information for consideration for the showcase event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields: *First Name*, *Last Name*, *Date of Birth*, *Gender*, *Email Address*, *Phone Number*, *Street Address*, *City*, *State*, *ZIP Code*, *High School Name*, *High School City*, *High School State*, *Graduation Year*, *Current GPA*, *Primary Sport*, *Primary Position*, *Height*, *Weight*, *Uniform Number*, *Current Team Name*, *Head Coach Name*, *Coach Email*, *Coach Phone*, *Showcase Event Date*, *Showcase Location*, *Parent or Guardian Name*, *Parent Email*, *Parent Phone*, *Emergency Contact Name*, *Emergency Contact Phone*, *Insurance Provider*, *Policy Number*, *T-Shirt Size*, and *Registration Fee*.
2. Select your *Primary Sport* and *Primary Position* from the provided options.
3. If applicable, fill out the *Secondary Position* field.
4. Select your *T-Shirt Size* from the provided options.
5. Enter your *Registration Fee Payment Method* from the provided options.
6. Sign the *Liability Waiver Agreement* and *Media Release Consent*.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter your complete address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your postal code.
* **High School Name**: Enter the name of your current or former high school.
* **High School City**: Enter the city of your current or former high school.
* **High School State**: Enter the state of your current or former high school.
* **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated from high school.
* **Current GPA**: Enter your current GPA.
* **Primary Sport**: Select the sport you participate in.
* **Primary Position**: Enter the position you play in your primary sport.
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds.
* **Uniform Number**: Enter your uniform number, if applicable.
* **Current Team Name**: Enter the name of your current team.
* **Head Coach Name**: Enter the name of your head coach.
* **Coach Email**: Enter the email of your head coach.
* **Coach Phone**: Enter the phone number of your head coach.
* **Showcase Event Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the showcase event.
* **Showcase Location**: Enter the location of the showcase event.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the name of your primary contact.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the email of your primary contact.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of your primary contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Insurance Provider**: Enter your insurance provider.
* **Policy Number**: Enter your policy number.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your T-shirt size from the provided options.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay.
* **Registration Fee Payment Method**: Select how you plan to pay the registration fee.

## Tips

* Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
* Use the provided options for select-one fields, such as *Primary Sport* and *Primary Position*.
* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them under *Medical Conditions*.
* If you have any current medications, please list them under *Current Medications*.
* Please ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and accurate before submitting the form.
