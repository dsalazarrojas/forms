# Special Education Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for or provide information about special education training sessions. It captures essential details about the user and the session they wish to attend or provide.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the designated field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the designated field.
3. Enter your Email address in the designated field.
4. Enter your phone number in the designated field.
5. Select the training session you wish to attend from the options listed.
6. Choose the Start Date of the training session.
7. Choose the End Date of the training session.
8. Select the payment method you would like to use (bank transfer, credit card, check, or PayPal).
9. Enter the amount you would like to pay.
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the designated field.
11. Choose who submitted the form (you or someone on your behalf).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of special education training you wish to attend (Elementary, Secondary, Tertiary, or Pre-Service).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the training session will start.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date when the training session will end.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to make payment (Bank Transfer (Wire Transfer), Credit Card, Check, or PayPal).
* **Amount** (`amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you would like to pay for the training session.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, select_one, required): Choose who submitted this form (you or someone on your behalf).
