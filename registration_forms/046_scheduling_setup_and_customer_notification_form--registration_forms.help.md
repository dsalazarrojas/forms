# Scheduling Setup And Customer Notification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect customer information and scheduling details for appointment setup and notification purposes. It is an essential tool for businesses and organizations to gather necessary data from customers and send personalized notifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Provide your customer information, including any relevant details as required or optional fields.
2. Enter your scheduling details, such as the appointment date and start time.
3. Review and submit the form to send a notification to the relevant parties.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, optional): This field is used to collect general information about the customer, such as name, contact number, or email address.
* **Scheduling Details** (`scheduling_details`, text, optional): This field is used to collect specific details about the customer's scheduling preferences, such as preferred appointment dates and times.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, optional): This field is used to specify the appointment date.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): This field is used to specify the start time of the appointment.

## Tips
- Make sure to provide accurate and complete information in the customer information field.
- Double-check the appointment date and start time for any potential conflicts or scheduling errors.
- Review the form thoroughly before submission to avoid any missing or incorrect information.
