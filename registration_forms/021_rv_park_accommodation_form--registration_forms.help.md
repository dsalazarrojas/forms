> # rv_park_accommodation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for gathering information from users related to their reservation at a recreational vehicle (RV) park.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Provide your **User Information** (Page 1) by filling in the text field with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Submit **Accommodation Information** (Page 1) by filling in the text field with your accommodation preferences.
3. Select your preferred **Contact Method** (Page 1) by choosing one of the options: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, or Fax.
4. Fill in the **Reservation Information** (Page 1) text field with any additional details about your reservation.
5. Specify the number of **Guest Count** (Page 2) you will be bringing.
6. Select the **Stay Start Date** (Page 2) and **Stay End Date** (Page 2) for your stay using the date picker.
7. Choose the **Accommodation Type** (Page 2) from the options: Motor Home, Camper, RV, Tent, Cabin, or Lodge.
8. Enter the **Accommodation Name** (Page 3) of your accommodation if applicable.
9. If applicable, enter your **Accommodation Address** (Page 3).
10. Select the current **Reservation Status** (Page 3) of your reservation from the options: Pending, Confirmed, Cancelled, or Completed.
11. Choose the **Guest Type** (Page 3) from the options: Individual, Group, Family, or Corporate.
12. Select the **Confirmation** (Page 4) option to confirm your reservation.
13. Select a **Payment Method** (Page 4) from the options: Cash, Credit Card, or Bank Transfer.
14. If necessary, enter the **Payment Date** (Page 4) and **Payment Amount** (Page 4) for your payment details.
15. Select the **Payment Status** (Page 4) from the options: Not Paid, Paid, or Partially Paid.
16. Finally, provide any additional notes in the **Notes** field (Page 4-8) if necessary.
