# Dance Class Billing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Dance Class Billing Form is designed to capture billing information related to dance classes, including student details, payment methods, and class schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the date of the dance class session.
2. Enter the student's name.
3. Enter the student's ID or code.
4. Enter the number of hours attended in the class.
5. Select how the payment was made (online or offline).
6. Select the type of payment method used (bank transfer, check, etc.).
7. Enter the amount paid by the student.
8. Enter the date of the billing.
9. Select the status of the billing (paid, overdue, pending).
10. Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the student.
11. Enter a unique identifier for the form.
12. (Optional) Enter the student's email address.
13. (Optional) Enter the student's phone number.
14. (Optional) Add any additional comments or remarks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Class Billing**: Enter the date of the dance class session.
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID**: Enter the student's ID or code.
* **Class Hours**: Enter the number of hours attended in the class.
* **Billing Method**: Select how the payment was made (online or offline).
* **Payment Method**: Select the type of payment method used (bank transfer, check, etc.).
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount paid by the student.
* **Billing Date**: Enter the date of the billing.
* **Billing Status**: Select the status of the billing (paid, overdue, pending).
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool or equipment assigned to the student.
* **Form ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Student Email**: (Optional) Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: (Optional) Enter the student's phone number.
* **Notes**: (Optional) Add any additional comments or remarks.
