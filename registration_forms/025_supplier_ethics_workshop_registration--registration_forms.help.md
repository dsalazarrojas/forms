**Supplier Ethics Workshop Registration - Help Guide**
=============================================

## Purpose

The Supplier Ethics Workshop Registration form is a critical document that helps us track participants' registrations for the Supplier Ethics Workshop. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your participation in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Follow the steps below to fill out the form.
2.  Fill in all the required fields marked with \*.
3.  Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (2): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.*  
    This is your name as it appears on your ID card or passport.
*   **Email** (3): Enter your email address.*  
    This is the address you used to register for the workshop.
*   **Company Name** (4): Enter your company name (if you have one).*  
    If you don't have a company, enter "individual" or a relevant identifier.
*   **Phone Number** (7): Enter your phone number.*  
    This is the number you can be reached at after hours or in case of an emergency.
*   **Phone Number** (optional): This is a space to add any additional information you'd like to share with us.
*   **Agreed to Participate** (9): Select "Yes" if you are ready to take part in the workshop.*  
    This is the checkbox to confirm your participation and agree to our guidelines.
*   **Participant Agreed Note** (10): Enter any additional comments or concerns you have about the workshop.*  
    This is the space to provide any additional information about your participation.
*   **Agreed to Participate Date** (11): This is a date field that will be automatically populated when you select "Yes" to participate.
*   **Agreed to Participate Time** (12): This is a time field that will be automatically populated when you select "Yes" to participate.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all the fields before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact our team for assistance.
*   The form should not take more than 5 minutes to complete.
