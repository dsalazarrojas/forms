# Group Session Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Session Appointment Form is used to collect participant information for a group session appointment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the participant details section, which includes your name and any other relevant information about you.
2. Provide your address in the participant address section.
3. Enter your email address in the participant email section.
4. Enter your phone number in the participant phone section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter a brief description of yourself, including your name, to help us better understand who you are.
* **participant_address** (`participant_address`, text, required: false): Provide your address to help us contact you or send any necessary information.
* **participant_email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
