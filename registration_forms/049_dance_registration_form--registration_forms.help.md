# Dance Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Dance Registration Form is designed to collect vital information from participants in a specific dance event or competition. This form ensures that the necessary details are captured accurately and efficiently, making it easier for event organizers to manage registrations and communicate with participants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your name and email address accurately as they will be used for communication purposes.
2. Enter your phone number to ensure that you can be reached in case of any event updates or changes.
3. Indicate your dance category participation by selecting the relevant option(s) from the list provided.
4. Provide any additional comments about your participation, if necessary.
5. If you're representing an academy or course, please enter their names for recognition purposes.
6. Enter the name of the contest or festival, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed in event materials and communications.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address is essential for receiving updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number to allow event organizers to contact you.
* **Dance Category** (`dance_category`, select_multiple, required): Select all categories that apply to your participation.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Add any additional details about your participation, if necessary.
* **Academy Name** (`academy_name`, text, optional): If you're representing an academy, enter its name for recognition purposes.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): If you're representing a course, enter its name for recognition purposes.
* **Contest Festival Name** (`contest_festival_name`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the name of the contest or festival you're participating in.
* **Category Name** (`category_name`, text, optional): If you're associated with a specific category, enter its name.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to avoid any delays or issues with your registration.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, don't hesitate to contact event organizers for assistance.
* Double-check your entries before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
