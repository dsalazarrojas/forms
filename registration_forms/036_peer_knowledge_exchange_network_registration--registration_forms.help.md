# Peer Knowledge Exchange Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Peer Knowledge Exchange Network Registration form is used to register users for the Peer Knowledge Exchange Network. This form collects information about the user's community, role, expertise, learning goals, contact email, and contact phone. The purpose of this form is to create a profile for the user to connect with others in the network and access resources and opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Community Name**, which is the name of the community you wish to represent.
2. Enter your **Organization Name**, if applicable.
3. Select your **Role**, which describes your area of expertise or responsibility in the community.
4. Enter your **Expertise**, a brief description of your areas of knowledge or skill.
5. Describe your **Learning Goals**, which are the topics or skills you are looking to learn or develop.
6. Enter your **Contact Email**, which will be used to contact you.
7. Enter your **Contact Phone**, which is optional but can be used for further contact or communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Community Name** (`community_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the community you wish to represent. This is the name that will be displayed in the network.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with an organization, enter its name here.
* **Role** (`role`, text, required): Describe your area of expertise or responsibility in the community. This will help others understand your area of focus.
* **Expertise** (`expertise`, text, optional): Briefly describe your areas of knowledge or skill. This will help connect you with others who share similar interests.
* **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, text, optional): Describe the topics or skills you are looking to learn or develop. This will help you connect with others who are also interested in these areas.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used to contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, which can be used for further contact or communication.
