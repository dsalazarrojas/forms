# Computer Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help computer training administrators gather information from individuals who want to register for computer training sessions. It will be used to track who is interested in these sessions, which courses they want to attend, and how they would like to pay for the training.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the registration process.
2. Fill out the form with the required information.
3. Make sure to select the correct payment option.
4. Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to review for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for course materials and confirmation.
* **Course Selected** (`course_selected`, `select_one`, required): Choose the training course you are registering for from the list: Intro to Computers, Office Productivity, Coding Basics, Advanced IT.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your self-assessed experience level from the list: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
* **Preferred Schedule** (`preferred_schedule`, `select_one`, optional): Choose your preferred schedule from the list: Morning, Afternoon, Evening. If you don't select a schedule, you will be assigned a default schedule.
* **Payment Option** (`payment_option`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to pay for the training: Credit card, Invoice, Employer sponsored.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility or equipment needs, please list them here.
