# plastic_free_compliance_declaration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare a company's compliance with plastic-free regulations. It collects information about the company's details, vendor details, declaration date, declaration status, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your company's details, if applicable.
2. Indicate whether you have any vendors that are not compliant with plastic-free regulations.
3. Enter the date of the declaration.
4. Choose the declaration status, either Active or Inactive.
5. Add any additional comments or explanations, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Details** (Company Details, Text, Optional): Enter your company's details, including its name and address.
* **Vendors** (Vendors, Select Multiple, Optional): Check all applicable vendors that are not compliant with plastic-free regulations.
* **Declaration Date** (Declaration Date, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the declaration.
* **Declaration Status** (Declaration Status, Select One, Optional): Choose whether your declaration is Active or Inactive.
* **Declaration Comments** (Declaration Comments, Note, Optional): Add any additional comments or explanations about your company's compliance status.

## Tips
* Ensure you complete all required fields.
* If you select multiple vendors, make sure to enter the correct name of each vendor.
* Use the declaration date format YYYY-MM-DD.
