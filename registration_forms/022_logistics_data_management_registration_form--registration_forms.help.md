# Logistics Data Management Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering logistics data management information. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to enable effective logistics data management. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that your information is correctly recorded.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select the appropriate options for each field and provide accurate information in the text fields.
- Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`select_multiple`): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you wish to register your logistics data management information.
* **Logistics Name** (`text`): Enter the name of your logistics data management system or department.
* **Contact Person** (`text`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the logistics data management.
* **Contact Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`text`): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (`text`): Enter the address of the contact person.

## Tips
- Double-check that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
- Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please contact the logistics data management team.
