# Sonship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your sonship information with our organization. It helps us to keep track of your family relationships and ensures that we can contact you in case of emergencies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, email address, phone number, and other relevant information.
* Select your gender and marital status from the provided options.
* Choose your church affiliation, if applicable.
* Select your program participation status.
* Choose your language preference.
* Provide the contact information for your emergency contact person.
* If applicable, provide the dates of your last communion, baptism, and confession.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field for your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field for your last name.
* **Email**: A field for your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field for your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: A date field for your date of birth.
* **Date of Registration**: A date field for the date you are registering.
* **Address**: A text field for your address.
* **City**: A text field for your city.
* **State**: A text field for your state.
* **Zip**: A number field for your zip code.
* **Gender**: A dropdown menu to select your gender (Male, Female, Other).
* **Marital Status**: A dropdown menu to select your marital status (Single, Married, Divorced, Widow, Other).
* **Church Affiliation**: A dropdown menu to select your church affiliation (Non-denominational, Pentecostal, Anglican, Baptist, Catholic).
* **Program Participation**: A dropdown menu to select your program participation status (Active, Non-Active, New).
* **Language**: A dropdown menu to select your language preference (English, French, Spanish, Other).
* **Emergency Contact**: A text field for the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone**: A text field for the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Address**: A text field for the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency City**: A text field for the city of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency State**: A text field for the state of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Zip**: A number field for the zip code of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Relationship**: A text field for your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Date of Last Communion**: A date field for the date of your last communion.
* **Date of Last Baptism**: A date field for the date of your last baptism.
* **Date of Last Confession**: A date field for the date of your last confession.
