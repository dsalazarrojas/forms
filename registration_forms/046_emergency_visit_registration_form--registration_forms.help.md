# Emergency Visit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emergency Visit Registration Form is designed for patients visiting the emergency department to record and document essential information about their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure that the form fields are completed accurately and thoroughly.
2. Enter the patient's name in the **Patient Name** field.
3. Provide the patient's address in the **Patient Address** field.
4. Enter the date of the visit in the **Date of Visit** field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
5. Enter the time of the visit in the **Time of Visit** field in the format HH:MM:SS AM/PM.
6. Provide the **Patient Contact Information** field with the patient's relevant contact details.
7. If the patient's relationship to the visiting physician is not listed, choose **Other** and provide details in the **Other Relation** field.
8. If applicable, provide the patient's relationship to the visiting physician in the **Relation to Visiting Physician** field.
9. Finally, provide any additional details about the patient's relationship to the visiting physician in the **Other Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Emergency Visit Registration**: This is the main heading of the form that indicates its purpose.
* **Patient Name**: This field requires the patient's full name as it appears on their identification document.
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's complete address as per their official documents.
* **Date of Visit**: Enter the date of the visit using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Time of Visit**: Enter the time of the visit using the HH:MM:SS AM/PM format.
* **Patient Contact Information**: Provide the patient's contact details, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Relation to Visiting Physician**: List the patient's relationship to the visiting physician from the provided options (e.g., spouse, child, etc.).
* **Other Relation**: If the patient's relationship to the visiting physician is not listed, choose this option and provide details in the **Other Details** field.
* **Other Details**: Provide any additional information about the patient's relationship to the visiting physician.

## Tips

* Ensure that you accurately enter all necessary information about the patient's visit to provide proper care and documentation.
* If any field is unclear or missing, please contact your supervisor/manager for further guidance.
