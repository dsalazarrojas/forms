# Educational-Expo-Sponsorship-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The educational-expo-sponsorship-form is used to collect information from institutions and sponsors for an educational event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) or a label that says "required".
2. Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
3. Review each field carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Title (Page 1)
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event.
### Institution Name (Page 1)
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution hosting the event.
### Event Description (Page 1)
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event.
### Sponsor Amount (Page 1)
* **Sponsor Amount** (`sponsor_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount of sponsorship.
### Sponsor Relationship (Page 1)
* **Sponsor Relationship** (`sponsor_relationship`, text, required): Describe your relationship with the sponsor.
### Contact Name (Page 2)
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
### Email (Page 2)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
### Phone (Page 2)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
### Event Date (Page 2)
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
### Event Time (Page 2)
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
### Event Duration (Page 2)
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the event.
### Event Venue (Page 3)
* **Event Venue** (`event_venue`, text, optional): Enter the venue of the event.
### Event Address (Page 3)
* **Event Address** (`event_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the event.
### Sponsor Contact Name (Page 3)
* **Sponsor Contact Name** (`sponsor_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the sponsor contact person.
### Sponsor Email (Page 3)
* **Sponsor Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required): Enter the sponsor contact person's email address.
### Sponsor Phone (Page 3)
* **Sponsor Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, optional): Enter the sponsor contact person's phone number.
### Sponsor Address (Page 4)
* **Sponsor Address** (`sponsor_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the sponsor.
### Sponsorship Level (Page 4)
* **Sponsorship Level** (`sponsorship_level`, select_one, required): Select the sponsorship level.
### Additional Info (Page 4)
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
### Terms and Conditions (Page 4)
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
### Privacy Policy (Page 5)
* **Privacy Policy** (`privacy_policy`, text, optional): Review and agree to the privacy policy.
### Agreement Signature (Page 5)
* **Agreement Signature** (`agreement_signature`, select_multiple, required): Select whether you accept or decline the terms and conditions.
### Submit (Page 5)
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_multiple, optional): Select to submit the form or reset it.

Note: The page numbers refer to the page number on which the field is located.
