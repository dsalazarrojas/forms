# Fair Supply Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fair Supply Exchange Registration Form is designed for participants who want to offer or seek supplies for a fair supply exchange event. This guide will walk you through the form fields and help you complete it with ease.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form carefully**: Please ensure you answer all required fields (marked as *required*).
2. **Provide accurate information**: Make sure to fill out your name, event details, and contact information correctly.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you have filled out all required fields, click the submit button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User (User)
* **Label:** User
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** This field is for your personal information. Please enter your name.

### 2. Event Date (Event Date)
* **Label:** Event Date
* **Type:** Date
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter the date of the event you are participating in.

### 3. Event Time (Event Time)
* **Label:** Event Time
* **Type:** Time
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter the time of the event you are participating in.

### 4. Participant Name (Participant Name)
* **Label:** Participant Name
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** This field is for your participant information. Please enter your name.

### 5. Supply Offered (Supply Offered)
* **Label:** Supply Offered
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** If you are offering supplies, list them here.

### 6. Needs Met (Needs Met)
* **Label:** Needs Met
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** If you are seeking supplies, list what you need.

### 7. Email (Email)
* **Label:** Email
* **Type:** Email
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter your email address.

### 8. Phone (Phone)
* **Label:** Phone
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter your phone number.

### 9. Participant Notes (Participant Notes)
* **Label:** Participant Notes
* **Type:** Note
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** This field is for any additional notes you'd like to include about your participation.

### 10. Participant Role (Participant Role)
* **Label:** Participant Role
* **Type:** Select One
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Choose your role for the event (Organizer, Nonprofit, Community Group).

### 11. Participant Organization (Participant Organization)
* **Label:** Participant Organization
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter your organization's name.

### 12. Event Type (Event Type)
* **Label:** Event Type
* **Type:** Select One
* **Required:** True
* **Explanation:** Choose whether you are offering or seeking supplies (Supply Offer, Supply Need, Both).

### 13. Event Type Other (Event Type Other)
* **Label:** Event Type Other
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** If "Event Type" is "Other", provide more information.

### 14. Participant Email (Participant Email)
* **Label:** Participant Email
* **Type:** Email
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter your email address.

### 15. Participant Phone (Participant Phone)
* **Label:** Participant Phone
* **Type:** Text
* **Required:** False
* **Explanation:** Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the event organizer for guidance.
* The form should only be filled out once, so take your time and complete it carefully.
