# Environmental Awareness Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for an Environmental Awareness Workshop. It collects contact information and details about the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Select the correct workshop attendees from the options provided.
4. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**: Enter a brief description of the registration. This is not a required field.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person responsible for registering the attendee.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company affiliated with the attendee.
* **Workshop Date**: Enter the date of the Environmental Awareness Workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the Environmental Awareness Workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the Environmental Awareness Workshop.
* **Workshop Attendees**: Select from the options provided to specify which attendees will be present at the workshop. This field is required.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information about the attendee or workshop in this field.
