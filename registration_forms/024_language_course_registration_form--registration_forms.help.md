# language_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details from students and instructors for language course registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form:

1.  Provide your basic details as a student or instructor.
2.  Enter course-specific information.
3.  Fill in your teacher's contact information if you are an instructor.
4.  Provide additional course details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Name (1)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Student Email (2)**: Enter your personal email address for communication.
*   **Student Phone (3)**: Your contact number for any urgent messages or questions.
*   **Student Address (4)**, **Student City (5)**, **Student State (6)**, **Student Country (7)**: These are your geographical address details.
*   **Course Name (8)**: Enter the name of the course you are enrolling in.
*   **Start Date (9)** and **End Date (10)**: Select the dates for the course duration.
*   **Language (11)**: Indicate the language of the course.
*   **Teacher Name (12)**: Enter the name of your instructor or teacher for the course.
*   **Teacher Email (13)**: Enter your teacher's email address for contact.
*   **Teacher Address (14)**, **Teacher City (15)**, **Teacher State (16)**, **Teacher Country (17)**: These are your teacher's geographical address details.
*   **Course Description (18)**: Briefly describe the course you are enrolling in.
*   **Course Level (19)**: Indicate your current level of expertise or the level you aspire to achieve in the course.
*   **Course Start Date (20)**: Select the start date for the course duration.
*   **Course End Date (21)**: Select the end date for the course duration.
*   **Status (22)**: Choose the status of your course enrollment, which can be Active, Inactive, or Pending.
