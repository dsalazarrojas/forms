# Food Safety Certification Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from food service providers for food safety certification purposes. The data collected will be used to process and verify the provider's certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Provider Name", "Provider Address", "Provider Phone", and "Provider Email" fields with the relevant information about the food service provider.
2. Select one or more options for "Food Service Provider Type" from the dropdown list.
3. Choose the "Certifying Agency" from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the "Cert Number" and "Cert Date" fields with the corresponding information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name** (`Provider Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the food service provider.
* **Provider Address** (`Provider Address`, text, required): Enter the physical address of the food service provider.
* **Provider Phone** (`Provider Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the food service provider.
* **Provider Email** (`Provider Email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the food service provider.
* **Provider Fax** (`Provider Fax`, text, required): Enter the fax number of the food service provider.
* **Provider Website** (`Provider Website`, text, optional): Enter the website of the food service provider.
* **Food Service Provider Type** (`Food Service Provider Type`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more categories of food service provided (e.g., restaurant, bakery, food truck, etc.).
* **Certifying Agency** (`Certifying Agency`, select_one, required): Select the agency that issued the food safety certification.
* **Cert Number** (`Cert Number`, number, required): Enter the certification number issued by the certifying agency.
* **Cert Date** (`Cert Date`, date, required): Enter the date when the certification was issued.

## Tips
- Double-check the accuracy of the information provided.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the support team.
