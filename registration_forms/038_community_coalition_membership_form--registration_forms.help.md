# Community Coalition Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Coalition Membership Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the coalition. This form helps us understand your interests, skills, and experience relevant to the coalition, and facilitates your membership registration. By filling out this form, you will be able to express your interest in the coalition and specify your level of engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each section of the form carefully, providing accurate and honest information.
2. Select the correct options for each field according to your preferences.
3. Ensure all required fields are completed, as indicated by the label.
4. Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Full Name of Member**
 Full Name of Member (`member_full_name_coa`, `text`, required/optional): The member's full name as it appears on their official identification.

### 2. **Email Address**
Email Address (`member_email_coa`, `email`, required/optional): The member's email address where they can be reached for follow-up and results delivery.

### 3. **Primary Contact Phone Number**
Primary Contact Phone Number (`member_phone_coa`, `text`, required/optional): The member's primary contact phone number for communication and coordination.

### 4. **Date of Birth**
Date of Birth (`member_dob_coa`, `date`, required/optional): The member's date of birth as it appears on their official identification.

### 5. **Residential Address**
Residential Address (`member_address_coa`, `text`, required/optional): The member's residential street address, city, state, and zip for coordination and logistics purposes.

### 6. **Which program area are you most interested in for the coalition**
Which program area are you most interested in for the coalition (`primary_coalition_interest_coa`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the coalition program area that aligns with your interests and expertise.

### 7. **Which coalition membership type applies to you**
Which coalition membership type applies to you (`coalition_membership_type_coa`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of coalition membership that best describes your role and affiliation.

### 8. **What level of engagement would you like to have with the coalition**
What level of engagement would you like to have with the coalition (`coalition_engagement_level_coa`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the level of engagement that best suits your schedule and preferences.

### 9. **What key skills or expertise do you bring to the coalition**
What key skills or expertise do you bring to the coalition (`key_skills_expertise_coa`, `text`, required/optional): Describe your key skills or expertise relevant to the coalition's goals and objectives.

### 10. **Describe any previous coalition or advocacy experience**
Describe any previous coalition or advocacy experience (`previous_coalition_experience_coa`, `text`, required/optional): Share any relevant experience in coalition work, advocacy, or similar fields.

### 11. **Why are you interested in joining the coalition**
Why are you interested in joining the coalition (`reason_for_joining_coalition_coa`, `text`, required/optional): Explain why you want to join the coalition and what you hope to achieve through your membership.

### 12. **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**
Digital Signature (Type Full Name) (`member_signature_digital_coa`, `text`, required/optional): Type your full name, signifying your commitment to providing accurate and honest information.

### 13. **Date of Membership**
Date of Membership (`submission_date_final_coa`, `date`, required/optional): The date the form is submitted, which will be used as the membership start date.

### 14. **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter**
I would like to receive the community center's newsletter (`newsletter_opt_in_coa`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether you would like to stay updated on the coalition's programs and events through their newsletter.

### 15. **How did you hear about this coalition membership form**
How did you hear about this coalition membership form (`referral_source_coa`, `select_one`, required/optional): Share how you found out about this membership form, helping us understand our outreach efforts.

### 16. **Emergency Contact Person**
Emergency Contact Person (`emergency_contact_full_name_coa`, `text`, required/optional): Provide the name of a person not living with you, whom we can contact in case of an emergency.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the form accurately and successfully register for the coalition membership. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
