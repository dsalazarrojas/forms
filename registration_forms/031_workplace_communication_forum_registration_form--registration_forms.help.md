# Workplace Communication Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Communication Forum Registration Form is designed to collect information about employees or team members' workplace communication preferences and work-related details. This form helps managers and team leads understand who is available to communicate and work on specific projects or tasks, and HR to keep track of employee details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can reach you.
3. Provide your Phone Number so we can contact you.
4. Choose your workplace status by selecting the options provided.
5. Select your departments from the options provided.
6. Choose your team from the options provided.
7. Select your job title from the options provided.
8. Indicate whether you are a Manager or not.
9. Choose whether you are a Team Lead or not.
10. Select your job status from the options provided.
11. Choose your role from the options provided.
12. Select your reporting line from the options provided.
13. Choose your work location from the options provided.
14. Enter your Work Start Date and Work End Date.
15. Select your work shift from the options provided.
16. Enter your Role Description.
17. Enter your Job Description.
18. Enter your Job Requirements.
19. Choose your work hours from the options provided.
20. Choose your availability from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
  - **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
  - **Date**: Enter your Date of birth.
  - **Time**: Enter your availability time.
  - **Note**: Please enter any additional comments or information about yourself.
  - **Workplace**: Choose 'Yes' if you are currently working.
  - **Department**: Select your department from the options provided.
  - **Team**: Select your team from the options provided.
  - **Job Title**: Select your job title from the options provided.
  - **Manager**: Indicate whether you are a manager or not.
  - **Team Lead**: Indicate whether you are a team lead or not.
  - **Job Status**: Select your job status from the options provided.
  - **Role**: Select your role from the options provided.
  - **Reporting Line**: Select your reporting line from the options provided.
  - **Work Location**: Choose your work location from the options provided.
  - **Work Start Date**: Enter your start date of work.
  - **Work End Date**: Enter your end date of work.
  - **Work Shift**: Select your work shift from the options provided.
  - **Role Description**: Enter a brief description of your role.
  - **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your job.
  - **Job Requirements**: Enter any requirements or qualifications for your job.
  - **Work Hours**: Choose your available work hours from the options provided.
  - **Availability**: Choose your availability for work from the options provided.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Ensure your email address is correct to receive important communications.
- If you have any questions, please reach out to your manager or HR representative.
- This form will be used to keep track of your workplace details and preferences.
