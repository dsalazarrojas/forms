# Legal Compliance Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Compliance Training Registration Form is used to register participants for the legal compliance training program. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that you receive the necessary training and materials.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your **Form ID** in the designated field.
2. Enter your **Participant Name** in the **Participant Name** field.
3. Enter your organization's **Name** in the **Organization Name** field.
4. Enter your **Email** address in the **Email** field.
5. Specify your **Participant Role** by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
6. Select whether you will attend the **Training Session** by choosing 'Yes' or 'No'.
7. Enter the **Training Start Date** and **Training End Date** for the session you selected in the **Training Start Date** and **Training End Date** fields, respectively.
8. Enter the **Session Start Time** and **Session End Time** for the session you selected in the **Session Start Time** and **Session End Time** fields, respectively.
9. Enter the **Training Location** in the **Training Location** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, text, optional): This field allows you to provide a unique identifier for your registration. You can enter any character combination to distinguish your entry from others.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on the training materials.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you represent, if applicable.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about the training session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): You can enter your phone number to receive any necessary calls or updates from us.
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, select one, optional): Specify your role in the training program, either as a participant or not.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select one, optional): Indicate your intention to attend the training session by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu.
* **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the training session you selected.
* **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training session you selected.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the training session you selected.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the training session you selected.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Specify the location where the training session will take place.
