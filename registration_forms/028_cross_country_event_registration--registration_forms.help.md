# cross_country_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants for a cross-country event. Please complete this form to sign up for the event and provide necessary information for your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required information, marked as **required** in the "Field-by-Field Explanation" section.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): This field is for your personal name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): This field is for your personal last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): This field is for your contact address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): This field is for the city where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is for your contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is for your contact email address.
* **Waiver Consent** (`waiver_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more of the following options: Waiver of Liability, Assumption of Risk, and Medical Permission.
* **Race Preferences** (`race_preferences`, select_one, required): Choose the race distance you plan to participate in (5K, 10K, Half Marathon, or Full Marathon).
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may impact your participation, please provide a brief description.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, optional): Choose whether or not you have an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, provide their name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_number`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact, provide their phone number.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): If you have an emergency contact, choose how you are related to them.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizers.
* This form is the only way to register for the cross-country event.
