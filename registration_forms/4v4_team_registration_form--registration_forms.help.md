# 55th Street Guest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 55th Street Guest Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information from guests, including their personal details, contact information, and stay preferences. This form helps hosts or property managers to efficiently manage guest stays and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Guest full name**, **Email address**, and **Primary phone number** in the required fields.
2. Enter your **Arrival date** and **Arrival time** to inform hosts about your scheduled arrival.
3. If applicable, specify your **Departure date** and the **Number of nights staying**.
4. Select your preferred **Room type** from the options provided.
5. Provide your **Home address or mailing address**, which may be required for delivery or communication purposes.
6. Enter the **Emergency contact name** and **Emergency contact phone number** in case hosts need to reach out to someone on your behalf.
7. If you are bringing a vehicle, enter the **Vehicle license plate number**.
8. Finally, sign the form with your **Guest signature (full name)**, which serves as an electronic signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Guest full name** (`Guest full name`, Text, **Required**): This field is used to capture your full legal name, which will be used for communication and record-keeping purposes.
- **Email address** (`Email address`, Email, **Required**): Provide your email address for confirmation and communication purposes.
- **Primary phone number** (`Primary phone number`, Phone, **Required**): Enter your primary phone number for hosts to contact you in case of emergencies or important updates.
- **Arrival date** (`Arrival date`, Date, **Required**): Specify the date of your arrival to inform hosts about your scheduled arrival.
- **Arrival time** (`Arrival time`, Time, **Optional**): Enter the expected time of your arrival to ensure hosts are prepared.
- **Departure date** (`Departure date`, Date, **Optional**): If your stay is not limited, leave this field blank.
- **Number of nights staying** (`Number of nights staying`, Integer, **Optional**): Enter the number of nights you plan to stay.
- **Home address or mailing address** (`Home address or mailing address`, Text, **Optional**): Provide your home or mailing address for delivery or communication purposes.
- **Emergency contact name** (`Emergency contact name`, Text, **Required**): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency contact phone number** (`Emergency contact phone number`, Phone, **Required**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Vehicle license plate number** (`Vehicle license plate number`, Text, **Optional**): If you are bringing a vehicle, enter the license plate number.
- **Room preference** (`Room preference`, Select One, **Optional**): Select one of the room types offered, if applicable.
- **Guest signature (full name)** (`Guest signature (full name)`, Text, **Required**): Sign the form with your full name, which serves as an electronic signature.
- **Host instructions or notes** (`Host instructions or special instructions`, Text, **Optional**): Provide any special instructions or notes to the host, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
