# Organizational Strategy Alignment Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Organizational Strategy Alignment Event. Fill out this form to provide accurate and complete details about yourself and your event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Complete all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3. Enter your personal details in the Personal Details section.
4. Provide event information in the Event Info section.
5. Select your registration and submission status accordingly.
6. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (Personal Details, text, required: false): This section captures your personal details such as your name, job title, department, team, and role. Please enter your name and other relevant details as required.
* **Event Info** (Event Info, text, required: false): This section is for providing event information such as event date, start and end times, and event duration. Ensure you enter accurate event details.
* **Attendee Type** (Attendee Type, select_one, required: false): Select whether you are an internal or external attendee.
* **Company** (Company, text, required: false): Enter your company name, if applicable.
* **Name** (Name, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required: false): Enter your job title, if applicable.
* **Department** (Department, text, required: false): Enter your department, if applicable.
* **Team** (Team, text, required: false): Enter your team, if applicable.
* **Position** (Position, text, required: false): Enter your position, if applicable.
* **Registration Status** (Registration Status, select_one, required: false): Indicate if you are registered or not for the event.
* **Submission Status** (Submission Status, select_one, required: false): Indicate if your submission is completed or not.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (Event Start Time, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (Event End Time, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (Event Duration, text, required: false): Enter the duration of the event.
* **Note** (Note, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the event, if necessary.
* **Registration Status** (Registration Status, select_one, required: false): Select your registration status.
* **Submission Status** (Submission Status, select_one, required: false): Select your submission status.

## Tips
1. Ensure you have reviewed all sections carefully before submitting.
2. Double-check your entries for accuracy.
3. Use valid email addresses for the Email field.
4. For date and time fields, use the correct date and time format, respectively.
