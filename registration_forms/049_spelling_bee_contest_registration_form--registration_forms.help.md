# Spelling Bee Contest Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Spelling Bee Contest Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants and schools for the Spelling Bee Contest. This form will be used to create a participant profile and for event management and communication.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter the participant's name, school name, and other relevant information.
3. Select the participant's grade level and school t-shirt size and color.
4. Enter the event date and time.
5. Select the participant's speller level and experience level.
6. Indicate any allergies or medical conditions the participant may have.
7. Enter any additional information about the participant.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name as it appears on their school records.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the participant's school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the participant's current grade level (6th, 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
* **School Email** (`school_email`, email, required): Enter the school's email address.
* **Participant Grade** (`participant_grade`, number, optional): Enter the participant's grade level (if different from their school grade level).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for the school.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Speller Level** (`speller_level`, select_multiple, required): Select the participant's speller level (Novice, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Speller Experience** (`speller_experience`, select_one, required): Select the participant's speller experience level (1-2 years, 2-5 years, or 5+ years).
* **T-Shirt Size** (`participant_tshirt_size`, select_one, required): Select the participant's t-shirt size (Adult, S, M, L, or XL).
* **Allergies** (`participant_allergies`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the participant has any allergies (Yes or No).
* **Medical Conditions** (`participant_medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the participant has.
* **Medication** (`participant_medication`, text, optional): Enter any medication the participant is taking.
* **Additional Info** (`participant_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **School T-Shirt Size** (`school_tshirt_size`, select_one, required): Select the school's t-shirt size (Adult, S, M, L, or XL).
* **School T-Shirt Color** (`school_tshirt_color`, select_one, required): Select the school's t-shirt color (Red, Blue, Yellow, or Green).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the participant's and school's contact information.
* Enter accurate information about the participant's experience and abilities.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
