# Event Sponsorship Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from event sponsors for events, including their permission, agreement, and details of their sponsorship.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with your event sponsor's information, including their name, email, phone number, and sponsorship details.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement to the event terms and conditions.
3. Select all the applicable agreements (Terms and Conditions, Agreement, Waiver) that you, as the event sponsor, are agreeing to.
4. If necessary, provide any additional details about your sponsorship in the "Event Sponsorship Details" field.
5. Enter the date and time when you obtained permission from the event sponsor for their sponsorship.
6. Enter the duration of your sponsorship in hours or minutes.
7. Enter any additional notes about your sponsorship, if necessary.
8. Finally, enter the date and time when the agreement was signed by the event sponsor, and the date when the sponsorship will expire.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsor Name**: Please enter the name of your event sponsor. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsor Email**: Enter the email address of your event sponsor. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsor Phone**: Enter the phone number of your event sponsor. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission**: Choose "Yes" or "No" to indicate your agreement to the event terms and conditions. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Agreement**: Select all the agreements (Terms and Conditions, Agreement, Waiver) that you, as the event sponsor, are agreeing to. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Details**: If necessary, please provide any additional details about your sponsorship. This field is not required.
* **Event Sponsorship Additional Details**: If you have any additional information about your sponsorship, please enter it here. This field is not required.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Date**: Enter the date when you obtained permission from the event sponsor for their sponsorship. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Time**: Enter the time when you obtained permission from the event sponsor for their sponsorship. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Duration**: Enter the duration of your sponsorship in hours or minutes. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Notes**: If necessary, please enter any additional notes about your sponsorship. This field is not required.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Agreement Date**: Enter the date when the event sponsor signed the agreement. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Agreement Time**: Enter the time when the event sponsor signed the agreement. This is a required field.
* **Event Sponsorship Permission Expires**: Enter the date when the sponsorship will expire. This is a required field.
