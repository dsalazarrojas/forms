# Engineering Lunch And Learn Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Engineering Lunch And Learn event. It collects essential information from attendees, including session details, personal information, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are registered as a member of the team before filling out this form.
2. Enter your session details in the first field.
3. Provide your attendee details in the second field.
4. Specify your meal preferences in the third field.
5. Enter your email address in the fourth field.
6. Enter your phone number in the fifth field.
7. Enter any additional notes or comments in the sixth field.
8. Select the date of the session you'd like to attend.
9. Choose the time of the session you'd like to attend.
10. Enter the size of your team.
11. Select the type of team you belong to.
12. Enter the location of the session.
13. Specify the topic of the session you'd like to attend.
14. Enter the capacity of the session you'd like to attend.
15. Indicate your RSVP status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **session_details**: Enter a brief description of the session you'd like to attend.
* **attendee_details**: Enter the names and titles of the team members attending the session.
* **meal_preferences**: Choose your meal preferences for the session (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **email**: Enter a valid email address for the session organizer to contact you.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for the session organizer to contact you.
* **notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **session_date**: Select the date of the session you'd like to attend (e.g., 2024-02-15).
* **session_time**: Choose the time of the session you'd like to attend (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **team_size**: Enter the number of team members attending the session.
* **team_type**: Select the type of team you belong to (e.g., Software Engineer, Project Manager, Team Lead, etc.).
* **session_location**: Enter the location of the session (e.g., conference room, auditorium, etc.).
* **session_topic**: Enter the topic of the session you'd like to attend (e.g., Machine Learning, Data Science, etc.).
* **session_capacity**: Enter the capacity of the session you'd like to attend (e.g., 10).
* **rsvp**: Indicate whether you will attend (True), will not attend (False), or may be attending (Maybe).
