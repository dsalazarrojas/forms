# performance_venue_partnership_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for potential partners who wish to collaborate with the venue. It's essential to fill out the form accurately to increase your chances of being selected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the first name and last name fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
3. Provide your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
4. Enter your company's name and details.
5. Specify the contact person's name and their role in the partnership.
6. Enter the venue's address.
7. Choose the type of partnership (Artist/Performance Group, Theater/Dance Company, or Production Company).
8. Select the frequency of your collaborations (Quarterly, Monthly, or Bi-Monthly).
9. Choose the type of performance you will be offering (Live, Recording, or Both).
10. Specify how often you will be performing at the venue (Regularly, As needed, or One-time).
11. Enter the performance dates and start/end date.
12. Specify the performance time.
13. Add any additional venue details, if necessary.
14. Enter any technical requirements, if applicable.
15. Specify your communication preferences, if necessary.
16. Provide emergency contact information.
17. Add any additional notes or information about your partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name and their role in the partnership.
* **Venue Address**: Enter the venue's physical address.
* **Partner Role**: Choose your company's role in the partnership (Artist/Performance Group, Theater/Dance Company, or Production Company).
* **Collaboration Frequency**: Select how often you will collaborate with the venue (Quarterly, Monthly, or Bi-Monthly).
* **Performance Type**: Choose the type of performance you will be offering (Live, Recording, or Both).
* **Performance Frequency**: Specify how often you will be performing at the venue (Regularly, As needed, or One-time).
* **Performance Dates**: Enter the dates of your performances.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your partnership.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your partnership.
* **Performance Time**: Specify the performance time.
* **Venue Details**: Enter any additional venue-specific details.
* **Technical Requirements**: Enter any technical requirements for your performances.
* **Communication Preferences**: Specify your preferred communication method (e.g., email, phone, or in-person).
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide emergency contact information.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Notes**: Add any additional information or notes about your partnership.
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about your partnership, if necessary.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and submit your application.
