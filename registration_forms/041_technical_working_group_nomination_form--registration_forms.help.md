# Technical Working Group Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to nominate individuals for the Technical Working Group. It helps us gather essential information about the nominee, their expertise, and participation status to ensure a fair selection process.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields and provide as much detail as possible. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Nomination**: This is the name of the nominee. Enter your name or the name of the individual you wish to nominate. (`technical_working_group_nomination_form`, text, required)
* **Nominee Details**: Provide a brief description or details about the nominee. This field is optional but helps us understand the nominee's background and qualifications. (`nominee_details`, text, false)
* **Expertise**: Select one or more fields that best describe the nominee's area of expertise. You can choose from Automation, Data Science, Data Engineering, and Data Analytics. (`expertise`, select_multiple, true)
* **Participation Availability**: Indicate whether the nominee is available to participate in the Technical Working Group. You can choose from Available, Unavailable, or Prefer not to disclose. (`participation_availability`, select_one, true)
* **Additional Comments**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about the nominee. (`additional_comments`, text, false)
* **Nominee CV**: Upload the nominee's CV or Resume in PDF format. This is a required field. (`nominee_cv`, text, true)
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your nomination. This is a required field. (`submit`, text, true)

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are providing accurate and honest information about the nominee.
- Do not submit duplicate nominations.
- The Technical Working Group committee will review all nominations and respond accordingly.
