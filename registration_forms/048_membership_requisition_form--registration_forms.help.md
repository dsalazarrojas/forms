# membership_requisition_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new membership, providing essential information about the member, their contact details, and their role and membership status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the relevant information:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
	* **Role**: Select your role from the options provided (e.g., Member, Leader, Officer).
2. If applicable, fill in the optional fields:
	* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	* **Member Since**: Select the date you became a member.
	* **Committee**: Choose the committees you are a part of.
	* **Membership Type**: Select your membership type (e.g., Monthly, Monthly Recurring, Quarterly, Annually).
	* **Payment Terms**: Enter your payment terms (only if applicable).
	* **Frequency of Meetings**: Select the frequency of meetings you attend.
	* **Membership Status**: Select your current membership status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Member Since** (date, optional): Select the date you became a member.
* **Role** (select_one, required): Select your role from the options provided.
* **Committee** (select_multiple, optional): Choose the committees you are a part of.
* **Membership Type** (select_one, optional): Select your membership type.
* **Payment Terms** (number, optional): Enter your payment terms (only if applicable).
* **Frequency of Meetings** (select_one, optional): Select the frequency of meetings you attend.
* **Membership Status** (select_multiple, optional): Select your current membership status.
* **Comments** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership.

## Tips
* Please ensure your email address is valid and not already registered in the system.
* Fill in all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
* Review and verify the information before submitting the form.
* If you need help or have questions, contact our support team.
