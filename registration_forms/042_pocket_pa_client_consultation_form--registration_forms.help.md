# pocket_pa_client_consultation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The pocket_pa_client_consultation_form is a registration form for clients to provide information about their consultation preferences. This form is used to gather essential details that will help the consultant to schedule an appointment with the client.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Client Name** in the first field.
2. Choose your **Contact Method** from the options provided (Email, Phone, or Other).
3. If you selected **Other** as your contact method, please enter your **Other Contact Method** in the next field.
4. Specify the time of your **Appointment Time**.
5. Choose the duration of your **Appointment Duration** in the provided options.
6. Select the days you are available for the appointment from the list provided in **Available Days**.
7. Choose the appointment duration in minutes from the options in **Appointment Duration Unit**.
8. Finally, provide any additional **Client Message** to the consultant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Please provide your full name.
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact (Email, Phone, or Other).
* **Other Contact Method**: Enter your alternative contact method if you selected "Other" in the previous field.
* **Appointment Time**: Enter your desired time for the appointment in the format "HH:MM" (e.g., 14:00).
* **Appointment Duration**: Select the duration of your appointment from the options provided (15, 30, 45, 60 minutes).
* **Available Days**: Choose the days you are available for the appointment.
* **Appointment Duration Unit**: Choose the unit of time for your appointment duration.
* **Client Message**: Provide any additional information or comments for the consultant.
* **Client Message 2**: This is an optional field for you to provide any further information or comments.
