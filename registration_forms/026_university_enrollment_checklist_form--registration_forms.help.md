# University Enrollment Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Enrollment Checklist Form is a mandatory registration form for students. It is designed to collect essential information from students to facilitate their enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Student Information** section with your personal details.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** in the corresponding section.
3. Select the courses you are interested in enrolling in under **Select Courses**.
4. Upload any required documentation under **Upload Documentation**.
5. Confirm your enrollment status in the **Confirmation** section.
6. Provide your scheduled event details under **Scheduled Event**.
7. Add any special comments or notes under **Special Comments**.
8. Finally, select your current **Enrollment Status**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (Student Information, text, required: false): Fill out this section with your personal details, including your name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, text, required: false): Provide your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
* **Select Courses** (Select Courses, select_multiple, required: false): Choose the courses you are interested in enrolling in. You can select multiple courses if needed.
* **Upload Documentation** (Upload Documentation, text, required: false): Upload any required documentation, such as transcripts or ID cards.
* **Confirmation** (Confirmation, select_one, required: true): Confirm your enrollment status by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Scheduled Event** (Scheduled Event, text, required: false): Provide details about any scheduled events or appointments you have related to your enrollment.
* **Special Comments** (Special Comments, text, required: false): Add any special comments or notes about your enrollment, if necessary.
* **Enrollment Status** (Enrollment Status, select_one, required: true): Select your current enrollment status as "Active" or "Inactive".
