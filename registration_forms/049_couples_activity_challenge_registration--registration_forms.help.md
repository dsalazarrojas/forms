# Couples Activity Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Couples Activity Challenge Registration form is designed to collect information from couples participating in the activity challenge. It is intended to be filled out by each couple to provide their relevant details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your and your partner's information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Data**: This field is the main form container and should not be filled out by the user.
* **Name**: Enter your name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date**: Select the date you plan to participate in the challenge.
* **Time**: Select the time you plan to participate in the challenge.
* **Note**: Enter any additional information or comments about your participation in the challenge.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of your partner.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your partner's email address.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Couple Status**: Select the status of your relationship with your partner (Married, Engaged, In a Relationship, Single).
* **Participant Relationship Status**: Select how you and your partner know each other (Partner 1's Name, Partner 2's Name, Both).
* **Number of Participants**: Enter the total number of participants you plan to be part of.
 
## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you have any questions or need further assistance, contact the relevant authorities for support. 
* Please note that this form is for your information only and should not be filled out by anyone else on your behalf.
