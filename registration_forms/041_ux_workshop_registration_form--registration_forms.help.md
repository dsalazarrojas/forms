# UX Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a registration form for the UX workshop. It helps us collect necessary information from participants to facilitate the event's organization and planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the required fields.
2. Select a workshop from the options provided.
3. Choose a date and time for the event.
4. Provide any additional notes if necessary.
5. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (text)**: Enter your name as you want it to be displayed on the workshop materials and event communications.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address to receive notifications and updates about the workshop.
* **Phone (text)**: Optional, but it's helpful for us to have your contact number in case we need to reach you directly.
* **Date (date)**: Select the date you are available to attend the workshop. We will use this to schedule the event.
* **Time (time)**: Choose the time slot that best suits your schedule.
* **Select One Workshop (select_one)**: Pick one of the workshop options provided. This will help us determine which workshop to schedule for you.
* **Note (note)**: If you have any additional information or requests, type them in this field. This is optional but can be helpful for us to better understand your needs.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are registering for the correct workshop.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* We will use the information provided to plan and organize the workshop effectively.
