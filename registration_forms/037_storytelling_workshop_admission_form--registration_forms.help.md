# Storytelling Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Storytelling Workshop Admission Form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in joining a storytelling workshop. This form helps the organizers to collect relevant details such as the participant's interest in the workshop, their contact information, and their preferred date and time of attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (category, email, and preferred date).
2. Optionally, provide a title for the workshop in the "workshop_title" field.
3. If you have any additional comments or details about your attendance, you can provide them in the "description" field.
4. Please enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Finally, select a preferred date and time for your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Storytelling Workshop Admission Form** (`workshop_title`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide a title for the workshop you would like to attend. It's not required, so you can leave it blank if you don't have a title in mind.
* **Registration Forms** (`category`, select_one, required: true): Please select "Yes" if you are interested in registering for the storytelling workshop. This will help us confirm your interest.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required: false): If you have any additional comments or details about your attendance, you can provide them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: true): Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your attendance. Ensure to enter a valid email address that we can use to reach you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field is for you to provide your phone number. Please ensure to enter a valid phone number if you want to be contacted about your attendance.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required: true): Select a preferred date for your attendance. This will help us schedule the workshop at a time that suits you best.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required: false): This field is for you to select a preferred time for your attendance. Please ensure to select a time that works for you, taking into account your preferred date selection.
