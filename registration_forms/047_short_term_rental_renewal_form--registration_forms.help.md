# Short Term Rental Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Short Term Rental Renewal Form is used to update and modify existing short term rental agreements. This form ensures that the rental agreement accurately reflects any changes to the tenancy period, renewal terms, or other relevant factors.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2.  Enter your name and contact information in the "tenant_info" field.
3.  Select the correct "agreement_terms" that apply to your rental agreement.
4.  Choose the "renewal_terms" that will be applicable to your renewed rental agreement.
5.  Enter the start and end dates for your renewed rental agreement using the "rent_start_date" and "rent_end_date" fields.
6.  Provide your signature in the "tenant_signature" field.
7.  Enter the signature of the property's owner/landlord in the "landlord_signature" field.
8.  Enter any additional comments or notes in the "property_manager_note" field.
9.  Enter the name of the property owner/landlord in the "property_owner_name" field.
10. Enter the property owner/landlord's contact information in the "property_owner_contact" field.
11. Enter the property owner/landlord's email address in the "property_owner_email" field.
12. Enter the property owner/landlord's phone number in the "property_owner_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **tenant_info (Text)**: Enter your name and contact information. This field is used to identify you as the tenant.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **agreement_terms (Select Multiple)**: Select one or more of the following:
    *   Type: `select_multiple`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **agreement_end_date (Date)**: Enter the end date of the current rental agreement.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **renewal_terms (Select One)**: Select one of the following:
    *   Type: `select_one`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **rent_start_date (Date)**: Enter the start date of the renewed rental agreement.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **rent_end_date (Date)**: Enter the end date of the renewed rental agreement.
    *   Type: `date`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **tenant_signature (Text)**: Provide your signature.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **landlord_signature (Text)**: Enter the signature of the property's owner/landlord.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **property_manager_note (Note)**: Enter any additional comments or notes.
    *   Type: `note`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **property_owner_name (Text)**: Enter the name of the property owner/landlord.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **property_owner_contact (Text)**: Enter the property owner/landlord's contact information.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **property_owner_email (Email)**: Enter the email address of the property owner/landlord.
    *   Type: `email`
    *   Required: `false`
*   **property_owner_phone (Text)**: Enter the property owner/landlord's phone number.
    *   Type: `text`
    *   Required: `false`

## Tips

*   Ensure all required fields are completed accurately and completely.
*   Double-check for correct spelling and grammar in all fields.
*   Proofread the form before submission to avoid errors.
*   If you're unsure about any field or information, consult with your landlord or property manager.
