# Authority Line Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Authority Line Registration Form is a critical document that outlines an individual's authority level and responsibilities within an organization. It ensures that everyone has a clear understanding of their power and limitations, promoting accountability and streamlined decision-making. This form helps to identify and document authorities and responsibilities, facilitating informed decision-making and minimizing confusion or miscommunication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
2. Provide your Registration Date, which is the date when you were registered or granted authority.
3. Enter your full name, ensuring it matches your official government-issued identification.
4. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
5. Specify your organization or company name.
6. Indicate your job title or position within the organization.
7. Identify your department and its relation to your role.
8. Select your authority level from the provided options (e.g., Executive/C-Suite, Director Level, etc.).
9. Specify your spending authority limits (e.g., under $5k, $5k-$25k, etc.).
10. Confirm if you have signature authority (yes/no, sole signer, joint signer, etc.).
11. Indicate if you maintain credit line authority (yes/no, individual/shared).
12. (If applicable) Enter the total credit authority, if you have it.
13. Select your procurement authority (yes/no, full authority, limited items).
14. Choose your contract authority (up to $100k, up to $500k, etc.).
15. Confirm your policy implementation authority (full authority/department only/advisory role).
16. If applicable, specify your hiring and personnel authority (yes/no, limited/specific roles).
17. Choose your budget authority (yes/no, full department level/limited items).
18. Select your delegation authority (yes/no, fully/partially delegable).
19. Confirm your compliance training status (yes/no, recently/within 1 year/overdue).
20. (If applicable) Disclose any conflicts of interest.
21. Enter the name of your supervisor or approving authority.
22. Enter your supervisor's email address.
23. Read and accept the authority line terms of use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date**: The date when you were registered or granted authority.
* **Registrant Full Name**: Your complete official name as per government-issued identification.
* **Email Address**: Your contact email for official communication.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number.
* **Organization Name**: Your organization or company's name.
* **Job Title or Position**: Your role within the organization.
* **Department**: Your department and relation to your role.
* **Authority Level**: The level of authority you possess.
* **Spending Authority Limits**: The monetary limits within which you can approve expenditures.
* **Signature Authority**: Your ability to sign documents, sole or joint signer.
* **Authority Line of Credit**: Whether you maintain credit line authority (yes/no, individual/shared).
* **Credit Line Limit Amount**: The total credit authority you maintain, if applicable.
* **Procurement Authority**: Your capacity to approve purchases.
* **Contract Authority**: The monetary limit for signing contracts.
* **Policy Implementation Authority**: Your power to implement policies.
* **Hiring and Personnel Authority**: Your ability to hire and manage personnel.
* **Budget Authority**: Your capacity to approve budget items.
* **Delegation Authority**: Your ability to delegate authority to others.
* **Compliance Training**: Your completion status of required compliance training.
* **Conflicts of Interest Disclosure**: Any potential conflicts of interest you may have.
* **Supervisor Approval Name**: The name of your supervisor or approving authority.
* **Supervisor Email**: The email address of your supervisor or approving authority.
* **Accept Authority Line Terms**: You must read and agree to the terms of use.

Please note that some fields may not be applicable, as indicated by the YAML properties. If you need any further clarification, please refer to your organization's guidelines or consult with your supervisor or approving authority.
