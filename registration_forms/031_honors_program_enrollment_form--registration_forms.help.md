# Honors Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students to provide information to enroll in the Honors Program. It is required for all students who wish to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your email address so we can contact you.
3. Enter your phone number in case we need to reach out.
4. Enter your address, city, and state where you live.
5. Enter your zip code to ensure we can mail you important documents.
6. Enter your GPA to show your academic performance.
7. Choose your GPA scale from the options provided.
8. If you are not affiliated with the university, answer "Yes" to the "program_type" field.
9. If you select "Yes" in the "program_type" field, enter your institutional affiliation.
10. If you have a faculty sponsor, enter their name, contact information, and signature.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are enrolling in (optional).
* **Student Name**: Your full name as it appears on your student ID or driver's license.
* **Student Email**: Your university email address or the email address you would like us to use to contact you.
* **Student Phone**: Your phone number in case we need to reach out.
* **Student Address**: Your home address where you can be reached.
* **Student City**: The city where you live.
* **Student State**: The state where you reside.
* **Student Zip**: Your zip code for mailing purposes.
* **Student Phone**: Enter your phone number again for verification.
* **GPA**: Enter your current GPA.
* **GPA Scale**: Choose the scale your GPA is based on.
* **Program Type**: If you are not affiliated with the university, choose "Yes". If you are affiliated, choose "No".
* **Program Type Other**: If you selected "Yes" in the "program_type" field, enter your institutional affiliation.
* **Faculty Recommendation Letter**: If you have a faculty sponsor, select "Yes" and enter their name, contact information, and signature.
* **Faculty Name**: Enter your faculty sponsor's name.
* **Faculty Contact**: Enter your faculty sponsor's contact information.
* **Faculty Email**: Enter your faculty sponsor's email address.
* **Faculty Phone**: Enter your faculty sponsor's phone number.
* **Faculty Institution**: Enter your faculty sponsor's institution.
* **Faculty Position**: Enter your faculty sponsor's position.
* **Faculty Department**: Enter your faculty sponsor's department.
* **Faculty Affiliation**: If your faculty sponsor is not with the university, enter their institutional affiliation.
* **Faculty Signature**: Your faculty sponsor's signature.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have a faculty sponsor, please make sure they sign the form.
* If you are not affiliated with the university, please make sure to answer "Yes" in the "program_type" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at <contact_email>.
