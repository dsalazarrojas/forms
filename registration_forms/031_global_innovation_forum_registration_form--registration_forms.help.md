# global_innovation_forum_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in the Global Innovation Forum. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the participant, including their name, contact details, and role within their organization. The collected information will be used to facilitate communication and collaboration among participants in the forum.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your Organization name in the "Organization" field.
5. Enter your Role in the "Role" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Text, Required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your business cards or identification documents.
* **email** (Email, Required: false): Enter your official email address associated with your work or organization.
* **Phone** (Text, Required: false): Enter your work phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Organization** (Text, Required: false): Enter the name of your organization or institution.
* **Role** (Text, Required: false): Enter your job title or function within your organization.
