# state_orchestra_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a school orchestra for a state tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the school orchestra name and student information (school name, student name, address, etc.) in the appropriate fields.
2. Select the district where the school is located from the list of available options.
3. Choose the type of orchestra from the list of available options (e.g., Chamber, Vocal, Strings, etc.).
4. Select the division(s) of the students participating in the orchestra.
5. Enter the number of students and teachers participating in the orchestra.
6. If applicable, select "Yes" if there is a teacher accompanying the orchestra.
7. If applicable, enter the teacher's contact information (phone number, email, etc.).
8. If applicable, enter the school's contact information (phone number, email, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Orchestra**: Enter the name of your school orchestra.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student participating in the orchestra.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the school.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the school.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the school.
* **School District**: Select the district where your school is located from the list of available options.
* **Orchestra Type**: Choose the type of orchestra from the list of available options (e.g., Chamber, Vocal, Strings, etc.).
* **Division**: Select the division(s) of the students participating in the orchestra.
* **Student Count**: Enter the number of students participating in the orchestra.
* **Teacher Count**: Enter the number of teachers accompanying the orchestra.
* **Teacher**: Select "Yes" if there is a teacher accompanying the orchestra.
* **Teacher Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the teacher (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email**: Enter the email address of the teacher (if applicable).
* **Teacher Address**: Enter the address of the teacher (if applicable).
* **Teacher Position**: Enter the position of the teacher (if applicable).
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of the school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school.
* **School Website**: Enter the website of the school.
* **School Phone Alternate**: Enter an alternate contact phone number of the school.
* **Submission**: Enter the time of submission (if applicable).
