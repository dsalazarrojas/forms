# Restaurant Online Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for restaurant owners and staff to register their restaurant on our platform. It is a one-time registration process that provides us with the necessary information to verify and showcase your restaurant's details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your restaurant's information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter your restaurant's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your restaurant's phone number.
* **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your restaurant's business phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Please enter your restaurant's address.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Please enter your restaurant's city.
* **State** (`state`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more states that your restaurant operates in.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Please enter your restaurant's zip code.
* **Type Of Cuisine** (`type_of_cuisine`, select_one, optional): Select the type of cuisine your restaurant serves.
* **Hours of Operation** (`hours_of_operation`, time, optional): Please enter your restaurant's business hours.
