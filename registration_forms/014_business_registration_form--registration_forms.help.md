# Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your business for registration purposes. It helps us understand your business structure, operations, and identity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the proposed business name and type of business entity (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, etc.).
2. Provide a description of your business and its primary industry or sector.
3. Enter the owner's or proprietor's full name and type of identification (e.g., Driver License, Passport, Government ID, etc.).
4. Enter the owner's or proprietor's identification number.
5. If the owner or proprietor has a date of birth, please provide it.
6. Enter the owner's or proprietor's email address and phone number.
7. Provide the business address, city, state or province, postal code, and country.
8. If the mailing address is the same as the business address, select 'True'. Otherwise, provide the mailing address.
9. Enter the proposed start date of operations.
10. (Optional) Provide the estimated annual revenue and number of employees planned.
11. If you have partners, list their details in the 'Partnership Details' field.
12. Finally, submit the form with the current date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Proposed Business Name**: The legal name of your business.
	+ Enter the name you want to register for your business.
* **Type of Business Entity**: Select the structure of your business (e.g., Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Corporation, etc.).
	+ Choose the type that best represents your business.
* **Business Description**: Describe what your business will do.
	+ Provide a brief overview of your business operations.
* **Industry Classification**: The primary industry or sector of your business.
	+ Enter the main sector your business operates in.
* **Owner or Proprietor Name**: The full name of the owner or proprietor.
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Owner ID Type**: The type of identification (e.g., Driver License, Passport, Government ID, etc.).
	+ Select the type of identification you possess.
* **Owner ID Number**: The identification number associated with your ID type.
	+ Enter the number on your identification.
* **Owner Date of Birth**: The date of birth of the owner or proprietor (optional).
	+ If applicable, provide the date of birth.
* **Owner Email Address**: The primary contact email address of the owner or proprietor.
	+ Enter the email address you want to use for communication.
* **Owner Phone Number**: The primary contact phone number of the owner or proprietor.
	+ Enter the phone number you want to use for communication.
* **Business Address**: The physical location of your business.
	+ Enter the address where your business operates.
* **City**: The city where your business is located.
	+ Enter the city name.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where your business is located.
	+ Enter the state or province name.
* **Postal Code**: The ZIP or postal code of your business location.
	+ Enter the postal code.
* **Country**: The country where your business is located.
	+ Enter the country name.
* **Mailing Address Same as Business**: If your mailing address is the same as your business address, select 'True'. Otherwise, provide the mailing address.
	+ Indicate whether your mailing address is the same or different from your business address.
* **Mailing Address**: If your mailing address is different from your business address, provide the details.
	+ Enter the mailing address if different.
* **Proposed Startup Date**: The date when your business will commence operations.
	+ Enter the start date of your business.
* **Estimated Annual Revenue**: The projected first-year revenue of your business (optional).
	+ Enter the estimated revenue.
* **Number of Employees Planned**: The number of employees you plan to hire (optional).
	+ Enter the number of employees.
* **Partnership Details**: If you have partners, list their details (optional).
	+ Provide information about your partners.
* **Registration Form Submission Date**: The current date.
	+ Enter the current date to complete the form submission.
