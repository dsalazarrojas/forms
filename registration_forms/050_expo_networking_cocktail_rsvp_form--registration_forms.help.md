# expo_networking_cocktail_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for networking events organized by Expo Networking, used to RSVP (Response to Service Provider's Usage of Social) and provide information about your intention to attend.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Attendee's Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose your RSVP type in the "RSVP Type" select field:
	* "Attending" if you are planning to attend the event.
	* "Not Attending" if you are not planning to attend.
	* "Maybe" if your intention is uncertain.
4. If you want to add a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. If you work for a company, enter it in the "Company" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee's Name (Text)**: Enter your name so we can address you properly.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about event details.
* **RSVP Type (Select One)**: Choose how you plan to attend the event.
* **Phone (Text)**: If you want to provide your phone number for any reason.
* **Company (Text)**: If you work for a company, enter it in this field.
* **Position (Text)**: Enter your position or role in your company if you provide your company name.
