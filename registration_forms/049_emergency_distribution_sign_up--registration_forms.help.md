# Emergency Distribution Sign Up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Emergency Distribution Sign Up" form is a registration tool used to collect contact information and other relevant details from individuals or households in emergency situations. This form helps emergency services, disaster response teams, or organizations to gather essential data to provide appropriate assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your contact details.
2. Specify the size of your household.
3. Indicate any emergency assistance needs you or your household may have.
4. Enter your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Emergency Distribution Sign Up Form** (`emergency_distribution_sign_up_form`, text, optional): This is the primary text field where you can provide a brief description or message.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, number, optional): Indicate the number of people living in your household.
* **Emergency Assistance Needs** (`emergency_assistance_needs`, select multiple, optional): Specify any emergency assistance needs you or your household may have (e.g., food, shelter, medical attention).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
- If you have any specific emergency assistance needs, please select all that apply in the "Emergency Assistance Needs" field.
