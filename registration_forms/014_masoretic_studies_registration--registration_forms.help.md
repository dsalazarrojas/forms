# Masoretic Studies Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Masoretic Studies Registration form is designed to collect basic information from users interested in participating in a study or research project. It is not a comprehensive survey, but rather a brief registration form to gather contact and demographic details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your user details in the "User Input" field (page 1).
2. Provide information about your education background in the "Education" field (page 2).
3. Choose your preferred study format in the "Study Format" field (page 2).
4. Read and agree to the terms of consent (page 3).
5. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (`User Input`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information you wish to share with the researchers.
### Education
* **Education** (`Education`, text, optional): Describe your educational background, including any relevant degrees or certifications.
### Study Format
* **Study Format** (`Study Format`, text, optional): Indicate your preferred format for participating in the study, such as online, in-person, or phone-based.
### Consent
* **Consent** (`Consent`, note, optional): This is a read-only field that outlines the terms of consent. Please read it carefully and indicate your agreement by signing below.
### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number, so the researchers can get in touch with you.

## Tips
* Be as brief and concise as possible when providing your information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the researchers directly.
* Remember to review the terms of consent carefully before indicating your agreement.
* Make sure to enter accurate contact information so the researchers can reach you.
