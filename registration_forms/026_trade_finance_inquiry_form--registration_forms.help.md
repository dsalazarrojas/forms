# Trade Finance Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Finance Inquiry Form is a request for financial assistance or services related to trade finance. This form is used to provide detailed information about your inquiry, and it's essential to complete it accurately to ensure that your request is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields with your information.
2.  Select the relevant options for Trade Finance Inquiries and Inquiry Type.
3.  Enter your contact details (Company Name, Contact Name, Contact Email, and Contact Phone).
4.  Provide a message with your inquiry details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Trade Finance Inquiry Form** (`trade_finance_inquiry_form_title`, text, Optional): This is the title of the form. You can leave this field empty, but it's helpful to provide a brief description of your inquiry.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your company.
* **Trade Finance Inquiries** (`trade_finance_inquiries`, select_multiple, Optional): Select 'Yes' if you are seeking financial assistance related to trade finance inquiries.
* **Inquiry Type** (`inquiry_type`, select_one, Optional): Choose the type of inquiry you are making, such as Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name as the contact person for your inquiry.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you.
* **Message** (`message`, note, Optional): Enter a detailed message about your inquiry, including any relevant information or context.
