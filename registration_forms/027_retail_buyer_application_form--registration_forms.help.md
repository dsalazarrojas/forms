# Retail Buyer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Buyer Application Form is used to collect information from potential buyers about their store details, payment terms, and account type. This information will be used to verify your identity and assess your creditworthiness.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as per your store's information.
2. Ensure that you're providing accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Store Name** (`store_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your store.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person who will be responsible for this application.
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of your store.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your store's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your store's phone number.
* **Store Website** (`store_website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your store's website URL.
* **Social Media** (`social_media`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your store's social media handle (e.g. Facebook, Twitter).
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of store you have:
	+ Online Store
	+ Physical Store
	+ Both
* **Other Store Type** (`store_type_other`, `text`, required/optional): If your store type is not listed above, enter a brief description of your store type.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the payment terms you accept:
	+ Net 30
	+ Net 60
	+ Net 90
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the payment methods you accept:
	+ Credit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Check
	+ PayPal
* **Account Type** (`account_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the type of account you have:
	+ Individual
	+ Business
* **Account Holder** (`account_holder`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the account holder.
* **Store Name Alias** (`store_name_alias`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a nickname or alias for your store.
* **Date Opened** (`date_opened`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date your store was opened.
* **Time Opened** (`time_opened`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time your store opened.
* **Store Closed** (`store_closed`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the time your store closed.
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your store's business hours.
* **Store Notes** (`store_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your store.
* **Account Holder Phone** (`account_holder_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the account holder.
* **Account Holder Email** (`account_holder_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the account holder.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
