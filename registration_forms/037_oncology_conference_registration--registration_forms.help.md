# Oncology Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for attendees, presenters, and staff members to attend the oncology conference. This includes personal details such as name and email, as well as session preferences and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select your role in the conference (Attendee, Staff, or Presenter).
2.  Fill in your name, which will be used to identify you on the conference lists.
3.  Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
4.  If you have a phone number, enter it for contact purposes. This is optional.
5.  Choose the sessions you would like to attend by selecting them from the available options.
6.  If you have any additional comments or notes, type them in the comments field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (Select One): 
  *   This field determines your role in the conference (Attendee, Staff, or Presenter). 
  *   Please select the correct option that applies to you.
* **Name** (Text): 
  *   Please enter your full name as you would like to be listed on the conference materials.
  *   This field is required.
* **Email** (Email): 
  *   Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and updates.
  *   This field is required.
* **Phone** (Text): 
  *   If you have a phone number, enter it for contact purposes.
  *   This field is optional.
* **Department** (Text): 
  *   If you would like to indicate which department you are from, enter it here.
  *   This field is optional.
* **Job Title** (Text): 
  *   If you would like to specify your job title, enter it here.
  *   This field is optional.
* **Session 1** (Select Multiple): 
  *   Choose the sessions you would like to attend.
  *   Please select the sessions that interest you the most.

* **Session 3** (Select Multiple):

* **Additional Comments** (Note): 
  *   If you have any further comments or notes, type them in this field.
  *   This field is optional.
