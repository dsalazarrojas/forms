# Trading Card Deck Submission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a trading card deck submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your player name in the `Player Name` field.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
4. Enter the name of the trading card in the `Card Name` field.
5. Select the type of trading card in the `Card Type` field. You can choose from the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
6. If you selected 'Yes' for the card type, you can enter additional details in the `Card Type Options` field.
7. Enter the name of the deck in the `Deck Name` field.
8. Enter the date of the event in the `Event Date` field.
9. Enter the time of the event in the `Event Time` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, text, required: false): This field requires your player name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field requires a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): This field requires your phone number.
* **Card Name** (`card_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the trading card.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of trading card.
* **Card Type Options** (`card_type_options`, text, required: false): Only visible if you selected 'Yes' for card type.
* **Deck Name** (`deck_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the deck.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the event.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Make sure to check the information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you need any help or have questions, do not hesitate to contact our support team.
