# Inventory Management Trial Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register companies for inventory management trials. To be eligible for a trial, the company must be registered in this form. The form helps to gather essential details about the company, such as their contact information and trial start dates. This form is intended for companies interested in testing the inventory management system, and it serves as a prerequisite for the trial process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's purpose and required fields.
2. Select the segment leads that apply to your company (if applicable).
3. Enter the start date of the trial.
4. Enter the end date of the trial.
5. Fill in the company details, including name, address, contact number, and contact email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Company Details
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter the legal name of your company.
* **Address** (Address, text, required): Enter the physical location of your company.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required): Enter the contact number of your company representative.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter the email address of your company representative.

### Segment Leads
* **Segment Leads** (Segment Leads, select_multiple, optional): If your company is a part of multiple business segments, select all that apply. Currently, only three segments are available: Segment 1, Segment 2, and Segment 3.

### Start Date
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of your trial.

### End Date
* **End Date** (End Date, number, required): Enter the end date of your trial.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues during the trial process.
* If you are having trouble finding a field, refer to the form's instructions.
* If you need further assistance, contact our support team.
