# Warranty Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register their warranty information and provide their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information in the "User Information" section.
2. Fill out the "Warranty Information" section with your warranty details.
3. In the "Contact Details" section, provide your contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. User Information (User Information)
* **User Information (Text)** (`user_info`, text, optional): Fill in your name, title, or any other identification that you prefer to be known by.

### 2. Warranty Information (Warranty Information)
* **Warranty Information (Text)** (`warranty_info`, text, optional): Fill in your warranty details such as warranty type, description, or any other information you want to provide.

### 3. Contact Details (Contact Details)
* **Contact Details (Text)** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact name, email, phone number, or any other contact information.
