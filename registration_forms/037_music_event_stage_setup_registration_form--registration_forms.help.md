# Music Event Stage Setup Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Event Stage Setup Registration Form is designed to collect information about your music event, specifically about stage setup, performer details, and technical specifications. This form will help event coordinators and technical teams understand your event's requirements, ensuring a smooth execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully, making sure you provide accurate and clear information.
2. Fill out each field according to your event's specific needs.
3. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Stage Setup (stage_setup)** (`Text`, Optional): This field is for you to describe your stage setup, including the number of stages, dimensions, and any specific equipment you'll be using.
* **Performer Details (performer_details)** (`Text`, Optional): In this field, provide information about the performers participating in your event, including their names, genres, and any special requirements.
* **Stage Plot (stage_plot)** (`Text`, Optional): Here, you can describe the stage plot, including the stage's configuration, any specific stage settings, and any visual or audio requirements.
* **Requirements (requirements)** (`Text`, Optional): This field is for you to list any specific technical requirements for your event, including power, sound, and lighting specifications.
* **Tech Specs (tech_specs)** (`Text`, Optional): In this field, provide technical details about your stage setup, including sound, video, and electrical requirements.
* **Performance Details (performance_details)** (`Text`, Optional): Describe your performance, including the duration, schedule, and any special details.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)** (`Text`, Optional): Provide your contact information, including phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Submit (submit)** (`Text`, Optional): Once you've filled out all the fields, click this button to submit your registration.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
