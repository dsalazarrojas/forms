# public_school_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect general information from students, parents, or guardians, and agreement details from parents or guardians, for public school enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the public school enrollment form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields for General Info, Agreement Details, and Student Info.
2. Fill in the required fields for Student/Paent Info and Confirm Agreement.
3. Confirm the information entered in the Confirm Student Info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info** (General Info, text, required): Please enter your name, school name, and grade level (if applicable).
* **Agreement Details** (Agreement Details, text, required): Enter your agreement to the terms and conditions of the school.
* **Student Info** (Student Info, text, required): Enter your student's name, age, and grade level (if applicable).
* **Student/Paent Info** (Student/Paent Info, text, optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share about your student.
* **Confirm Agreement** (Confirm Agreement, text, required): Verify that you agree to the terms and conditions of the school.
* **Confirm Student Info** (Confirm Student Info, text, required): Confirm the information entered in the Student Info field.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, not required): Please submit your completed form. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting.
* Review your form before submitting to ensure all necessary information is accurate and complete.
* If you need to save your progress or come back later, you can save your form and pick up where you left off.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, feel free to contact your school's administration or support team.
