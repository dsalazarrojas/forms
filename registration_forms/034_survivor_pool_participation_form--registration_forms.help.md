# Survivor Pool Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Survivor Pool Participation Form is designed for users who want to participate in a pool for a specific season. It collects essential details about each participant, including their team information, contact details, payment method, and entry status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the registration form with your team information, including team name, team size, and member count.
2. Submit your picks for each team in the weekly picks form.
3. Confirm your picks submission.
4. Provide payment information, including payment method and payment details.
5. Select your entry status, including active, inactive, or withdrawn.
6. Select your payment status, including paid, pending, or unpaid.
7. Fill out the season details form with the season length, start date, and end date.
8. Confirm your entry information, including entry ID and registration fee.
9. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration** (id: 1, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter your team information, including team name (max 20 characters).
* **Weekly Picks** (id: 2, required: false, type: text)
	+ Submit your picks for each team.
* **Contact Info** (id: 3, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter contact information for each member, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Member Info** (id: 4, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter member information, including name, email, and phone number.
* **Team Info** (id: 5, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter team information, including team name and description.
* **Team Roster** (id: 6, required: false, type: select_multiple)
	+ Select whether your team is local, remote, or virtual.
* **Picks Submission** (id: 7, required: false, type: select_multiple)
	+ Confirm that you have submitted your picks for each team.
* **Picks Confirmation** (id: 8, required: false, type: select_multiple)
	+ Confirm that you have reviewed and confirmed your picks submission.
* **Picks Submission Confirmation** (id: 9, required: false, type: select_multiple)
	+ Confirm that you have submitted your picks.
* **Entry Info** (id: 10, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter entry information, including entry ID and registration fee.
* **Entry Confirmation** (id: 11, required: false, type: text)
	+ Confirm your entry information.
* **Payment Method** (id: 12, required: false, type: select_one)
	+ Select a payment method, including PayPal, Stripe, or bank transfer.
* **Payment Details** (id: 13, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter payment details.
* **Team Details** (id: 14, required: false, type: select_one)
	+ Select team details, including local, remote, or virtual.
* **Entry Status** (id: 15, required: false, type: select_one)
	+ Select entry status, including active, inactive, or withdrawn.
* **Payment Status** (id: 16, required: false, type: select_one)
	+ Select payment status, including paid, pending, or unpaid.
* **Season Details** (id: 17, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter season details, including season length and start date.
* **Team Size** (id: 18, required: false, type: number)
	+ Enter team size.
* **Member Count** (id: 19, required: false, type: number)
	+ Enter member count.
* **Entry ID** (id: 20, required: false, type: text)
	+ Enter entry ID.
* **Registration Fee** (id: 21, required: false, type: number)
	+ Enter registration fee.
* **Season Length** (id: 22, required: false, type: number)
	+ Enter season length.
* **Start Date** (id: 23, required: false, type: date)
	+ Enter start date and time.
* **End Date** (id: 24, required: false, type: date)
	+ Enter end date and time.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate and complete registration.
* Review and confirm your picks submission before proceeding.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct payment method and payment details.
* Select the correct team details and entry status.
* Enter accurate season details and start date.
* Double-check all fields before submitting the form.
* Contact the administrator if you have any questions or concerns.
