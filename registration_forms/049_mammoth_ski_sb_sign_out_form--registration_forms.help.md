# Mammoth_Ski_&_SB_Sign_Out_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from users who participate in snow sports activities at Mammoth Ski Resort and Snowbird (SB) in California, USA. It helps resort staff track user attendance, preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required and optional fields as prompted:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prophets** (`user_email`): Enter your email address. This will help the resort staff contact you with any updates or offers.
* **Sign Out Time** (`sign_out_time`): Select the time you plan to leave the resort. This helps the staff track your departure and ensure they have enough time to prepare for your next visitors.
* **Snow Sports** (`snow_sports`): Choose the snow sports activities you participated in during your visit. Select all activities that apply, such as Mammoth Skiing and Snowboarding. This helps the resort staff understand your interests and preferences.
* **User Name** (`user_name`): Enter your name. This will help the resort staff address you personally and create a more welcoming experience.

## Tips
* Please note that some fields are optional, but providing accurate and complete information will help the resort staff provide a better experience for you and other visitors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
