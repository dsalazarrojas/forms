# Training Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who wish to participate in a training giveaway. By filling out this form, users will be entered into a random draw for a chance to win the training giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form details in Step 1.
2. Enter your name in Step 2.
3. Enter your email address and a valid phone number in Step 3 and Step 4, respectively.
4. Enter your date of birth in Step 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Form Details** (`form_details`, text, optional)
Enter a brief description of why you want to participate in the training giveaway. This can be up to 255 characters.
### **Name** (`name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
### **Email** (`email`, email, optional)
Enter a valid email address to be contacted if you win the giveaway.
### **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional)
Enter your phone number to be contacted if you win the giveaway.
### **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional)
Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
