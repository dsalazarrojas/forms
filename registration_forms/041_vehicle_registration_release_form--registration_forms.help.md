# vehicle_registration_release_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vehicle registration release form is used to release a vehicle from the system, providing detailed information about the vehicle's status and condition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the vehicle number, make, model, and year of the vehicle being released.
2. Enter the owner's name, address, phone number, and email (if available).
3. Specify the vehicle's condition, status, and type.
4. Enter the release amount, reason, and date.
5. Select the vehicle's license plate, VIN, and year made (if applicable).
6. Check the vehicle's VIN type and status.
7. Select the release lien status and date (if applicable).
8. Enter the vehicle's MIL (Military) status and date (if applicable).
9. Select the vehicle's VIN status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vehicle Number** (`vehicle_number`, `number`, optional): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **Vehicle Make** (`make`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's make.
* **Vehicle Model** (`model`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's model.
* **Year** (`year`, `number`, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was made.
* **Owner Name** (`owner_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the owner's name.
* **Owner Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter the owner's address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter the owner's email.
* **Vehicle Condition** (`vehicle_condition`, `select_one`, optional): Select the vehicle's condition.
* **Vehicle Status** (`vehicle_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the vehicle's status.
* **Release Amount** (`release_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the release amount.
* **Release Reason** (`release_reason`, `text`, optional): Enter the reason for release.
* **Release Date** (`release_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the release date.
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the vehicle type.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, `select_one`, optional): Select the year the vehicle was made.
* **License Plate** (`license_plate`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's license plate.
* **VIN** (`vin`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's Vehicle Identification Number (VIN).
* **VIN Type** (`vin_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the VIN type.
* **Release Lien** (`release_lien`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the release lien status.
* **VIN MIL** (`vin_mil`, `text`, optional): Enter the vehicle's MIL (Military) status.
* **Release Date MIL** (`release_date_mil`, `date`, optional): Enter the release date (MIL).
* **VIN Year Made** (`vin_year_made`, `text`, optional): Enter the year the vehicle was made.
* **VIN Status** (`vin_status`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the VIN status.
