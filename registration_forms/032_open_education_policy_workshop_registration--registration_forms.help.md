# Open Education Policy Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help you register for the Open Education Policy Workshop. Please fill out the form accurately to provide your details and session choices.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the first page with your participant details.
2. Select your session choices for each page.
3. Make sure to check the box to confirm your session choices on each page.
4. Review your information and submit the form once you have completed all the pages.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This page is where you'll provide your basic details such as name, email, country, phone, role, institution, and title.
* **Session Choices 1** (`session_choices`): Select one of the two options for your session choice.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Check this box to confirm that you understand and agree to the workshop's terms and conditions.
* **Session Choices 2** (`session_choices_2`): Select multiple options for your session choices.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`): Provide any additional information about your session choices, if needed.
* **Session Choices 3-14** (`session_choices_3`-`session_choices_14`): Select multiple options for your session choices.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`): Check this box to confirm your session choices on each page.

Note: Some fields are required, so please make sure to fill them out accurately. If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.
