# Dance Academy Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Academy Enrollment Form is a document designed to capture essential details from students and their families to process their enrollment into our dance academy. It is crucial to provide accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and efficient enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `Student Name` field.
2. Provide the student's email address in the `Email` field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the `Parent Name` field.
4. Select your contact preference in the `Contact Preference` field.
5. Choose the class the student is enrolling in from the `Class Name` field.
6. Provide the start and end dates of the enrollment period in the `Start Date` and `End Date` fields.
7. Enter the teacher's name in the `Teacher` field.
8. Select the student's level (`High` or `Low`) in the `Student Level` field.
9. Indicate whether the student will have a payment term (`Yes` or `No`) in the `Payment Term` field.
10. Select the payment method (not provided in the options).
11. Enter the payment amount in the `Payment Amount` field.
12. Select the payment status (`Active` or `Inactive`) in the `Payment Status` field.
13. Enter the payment date in the `Payment Date` field.
14. Enter the student fee in the `Student Fee` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide the student's email address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
* **Contact Preference** (`contact_preference`, select_multiple, required): Select how you would like to be contacted (Yes or No).
* **Class Name** (`class_name`, select_one, required): Choose the class the student is enrolling in (Yes or No).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the enrollment period.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the enrollment period.
* **Teacher** (`teacher`, text, required): Enter the teacher's name.
* **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_one, required): Select the student's level (High or Low).
* **Payment Term** (`payment_term`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the student has a payment term (Yes or No).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, text, required): Select the payment method (not provided).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status (Active or Inactive).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the payment date.
* **Student Fee** (`student_fee`, text, required): Enter the student fee.
