# Space Camp Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Space Camp Accommodation Form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians regarding their stay at Space Camp. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences to provide a comfortable and safe environment for you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Parent Name** in the text field provided.
2. Provide your **Email** address to help us contact you in case of any updates or concerns.
3. Enter your **Phone** number to ensure we can reach you quickly.
4. Enter your **Address** to help us locate you within the camp premises.
5. Select your **Start Date** of stay, following the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
6. Select your **End Date** of stay, following the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. Enter the number of **Children** you will be bringing along.
8. List the **Children Names** of those who will be accompanying you.
9. If applicable, provide any **Special Needs** that we should be aware of to ensure a smooth stay.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and notifications from us.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number with the country code (e.g., +1-123-456-7890).
* **Address**: Enter your complete address, including house number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Start Date**: Select the date you plan to arrive at the camp, following the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date**: Select the date you plan to depart from the camp, following the date format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Number of Children**: Enter the total number of children accompanying you.
* **Children Names**: List the names of all children, separated by commas or on separate lines.
* **Special Needs**: If you or your children have any special requirements or needs, please provide details here.
