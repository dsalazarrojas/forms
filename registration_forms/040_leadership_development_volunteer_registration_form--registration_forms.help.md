# Leadership Development Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register as volunteers in a leadership development program. Completing this form will help the program organizers gather the necessary information to place you in a suitable position or team within the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Provide your phone number, if applicable.
4. Select your volunteer type from the options provided.
5. Choose any relevant skills you possess from the list.
6. Select any interests you have from the list.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.

* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use for communication.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): If you want to be contacted, provide your phone number.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in your contact information accurately.
- Select your volunteer type and skills/interests that are relevant to the program.
