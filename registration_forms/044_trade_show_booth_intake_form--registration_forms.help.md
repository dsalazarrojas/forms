# Trade Show Booth Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for companies to register for a trade show booth. Please complete the form with your company's details to ensure a smooth and successful trade show experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your Company Name and contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Website).
2. Select your company's size (Small, Medium, Large) and type (Retailer, Service Provider, Distributor).
3. Provide a brief Booth Description and Company Description (optional).
4. Choose whether you'll be exhibiting at the trade show (True or False).
5. Select the trade show event(s) you'll be participating in (Chicago, New York, Los Angeles).
6. List your Company Products and any relevant Product List (optional).
7. Indicate your Booth Setup and Take Down Times.
8. Specify your Booth Hours.
9. Enter your Booth Location.
10. Enter your Company Address (optional).
11. Add any Special Requests.
12. Provide any Trade Show Notes (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name**: Enter your company's name as it appears on your business cards or marketing materials.
* **Booth Size**: Select the size of your trade show booth (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Contact First Name**: Enter your first name as the primary contact person for your company.
* **Contact Last Name**: Enter your last name as the primary contact person for your company.
* **Company Representative**: Enter any additional representative(s) for your company (optional).
* **Email**: Enter your company's primary email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your company's primary phone number.
* **Website**: Enter your company's website URL.
* **Company Type**: Select your company's type (Retailer, Service Provider, Distributor).
* **Booth Description**: Briefly describe your trade show booth, including any unique features or attractions.
* **Company Description**: Provide a brief overview of your company and its products or services (optional).
* **Trade Show Exhibit**: Indicate whether you'll be exhibiting at the trade show (True or False).
* **Trade Show Event**: Select the trade show event(s) you'll be participating in (Chicago, New York, Los Angeles).
* **Company Products**: List your company's products or services.
* **Product List**: Add any additional products or services related to your company's offerings (optional).
* **Trade Show Services**: List any services you'll be offering at the trade show (optional).
* **Services List**: Add any additional services related to your company's offerings (optional).
* **Booth Setup Time**: Enter the time your booth will be set up (HH:MM).
* **Booth Take Down Time**: Enter the time your booth will be taken down (HH:MM).
* **Booth Hours**: Select the hours your booth will be open during the trade show (date format: MM/DD/YY).
* **Booth Location**: Enter your booth's location on the trade show floor.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company's physical address (optional).
* **Special Requests**: List any special requests or requirements for your trade show participation.
* **Trade Show Notes**: Add any additional trade show-related notes or comments (optional).
