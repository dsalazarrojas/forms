# Campaign Rally RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Campaign Rally RSVP Form is designed to collect essential information from attendees, ensuring a smooth and organized event experience. This guide will walk you through each field to help you complete the form accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Attendee Name**. Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
2. Enter your **Email Address** to receive event updates and reminders.
3. If you have a **Phone Number**, enter it as an optional contact method.
4. Indicate the **Number of Guests** you will be bringing to the event.
5. If you are interested in volunteering, mark the **Volunteer Interest** field with a 'True' or 'False' selection.
6. If you have any **Accessibility Needs**, describe them in the text field.
7. Estimate your **Arrival Time** at the event to help organizers plan logistics.
8. Specify your **Areas of Interest** at the rally, which will help organizers tailor the event to your needs.
9. Confirm that you **Agree to Event Terms** by selecting 'True' or 'False'.
10. Add any **Additional Comments** you may have for the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Attendee Name (Attendee Name)
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email Address (Email Address)
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address where you can be reached for event updates and reminders.

### Phone Number (Phone Number)
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number for optional communication with organizers.

### Number of Guests (Number of Guests)
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Indicate the number of people you will be bringing to the event.

### Volunteer Interest (Volunteer Interest)
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Mark 'True' if you are interested in volunteering at the rally.

### Accessibility Needs (Accessibility Needs)
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Describe any accommodations or requirements you need for the event.

### Arrival Time Estimate (Arrival Time Estimate)
* **Arrival Time Estimate** (`arrival_time_estimate`, `time`, optional): Estimate your expected arrival time at the event.

### Areas of Interest (Areas of Interest)
* **Areas of Interest** (`interest_areas`, `text`, optional): Specify the topics or areas you are most interested in at the rally.

### Agree to Event Terms (Agree to Event Terms)
* **Agree to Event Terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to abide by event policies by selecting 'True' or 'False'.

### Additional Comments (Additional Comments)
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other notes or information you would like to share with the event organizers.
