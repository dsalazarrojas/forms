# Administrative Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming administrative volunteers at our organization. It gathers personal details, skills, and experience relevant to administrative volunteer positions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, such as name, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select your preferred volunteer position and department.
3. Indicate your availability and earliest start date.
4. Specify your expected commitment duration and hours per week.
5. Provide details about your education and computer skills.
6. List any special skills or languages you speak fluently.
7. Write a brief motivation statement explaining why you want to volunteer with our organization.
8. Consent to a background check if required for your position.
9. Sign the form electronically to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`volunteer_full_name`, text, required): Enter your legal name.
* **Email Address** (`volunteer_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`volunteer_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Address** (`volunteer_address`, text, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City** (`volunteer_city`, text, required): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State/Province** (`volunteer_state`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (`volunteer_zip`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your residence.
* **Country** (`volunteer_country`, text, required): Enter the country of your residence.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your birth date.
* **Position Interested In** (`position_interested_in`, text, required): Select the administrative volunteer position you are interested in.
* **Department Interested In** (`department_interested_in`, select_one, required): Select the department you are interested in (Administration, Human Resources, Finance, Marketing, Operations, IT, Events, Fundraising, Other).
* **Availability Schedule** (`availability_schedule`, text, required): Specify your availability for volunteering.
* **Earliest Start Date** (`earliest_start_date`, date, required): Enter the earliest date you can start volunteering.
* **Expected Commitment Duration** (`expected_commitment_duration`, select_one, required): Specify your expected commitment duration (Less than 3 months, 3-6 months, 6-12 months, 1-2 years, 2+ years).
* **Hours Available Per Week** (`hours_per_week`, number, required): Specify the number of hours you can commit per week.
* **Highest Education Level** (`education_level`, select_one, required): Select your highest education level (High School, Associate Degree, Bachelor's Degree, Master's Degree, Doctorate, Other).
* **Education Details** (`education_details`, text, optional): Provide details about your education (Name of school, degree earned, graduation year).
* **Administrative Experience** (`administrative_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant administrative experience.
* **Computer Skills** (`computer_skills`, text, required): List your computer software skills.
* **Languages Spoken** (`languages_spoken`, text, optional): List all languages you speak fluently.
* **Special Skills** (`special_skills`, text, optional): List any special skills that would benefit the organization.
* **Motivation for Volunteering** (`motivation_for_volunteering`, text, required): Write a brief motivation statement explaining why you want to volunteer with our organization.
* **References Available** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Indicate if references are available upon request.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Consent to a background check if required.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of submitting this registration.
* **Volunteer Signature** (`volunteer_signature`, text, required): Sign the form electronically to confirm your registration.
