# Spot The Differences Contest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Spot The Differences Contest Registration form is used to collect user information for the purpose of the Spot The Differences Contest. This form helps the contest organizers to gather details about the users participating in the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the `First Name` field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `E-mail` field. Please make sure to use a valid email address as this will be used for future communications.
4. Fill in your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Select your date of birth in the `Date of Birth` field. This is a date field where you will need to select a date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter your age in the `Age` field. Please be accurate, as this field is used to verify your identity.
7. For `Country`, select the correct option that corresponds to your nationality. This field is not required but it will help the organizers understand your location.
8. For `Gender`, select the correct option that corresponds to your gender identity.
9. Finally, fill in any additional comments or notes in the `Agreement` field.
10. Check the checkbox in the `Submit` field to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Fill in your first name as you would like it to be displayed in the contest communication.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, optional): Fill in your last name as you would like it to be displayed in the contest communication.
* **E-mail** (E-mail, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to be used for future communications.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Fill in your phone number for the contest organizers to contact you if needed.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Select your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age** (Age, number, optional): Enter your age accurately for verification purposes.
* **Country** (Country, select_one, optional): Select your nationality for the contest organizers to understand your location.
* **Gender** (Gender, select_one, optional): Select the correct option that corresponds to your gender identity.
* **Submit** (Submit, text, required): Click this field to submit your application.
* **Agreement** (Agreement, text, optional): Fill in any additional comments or notes you would like to add.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): This field is not being used at the moment. Please do not fill it in.
  The `Address` field is not a required field and should not be filled in.
