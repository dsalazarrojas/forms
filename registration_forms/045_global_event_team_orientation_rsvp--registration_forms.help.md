# global_event_team_orientation_rsvp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "global_event_team_orientation_rsvp" form is designed to collect event details, attendee information, session preferences, and any special requirements for a global event team orientation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "event_details" page and fill in the text field with details about the event.
2. Move on to the "attendee_info" page and provide your details as an attendee.
3. The "session_preferences" page allows you to select which sessions you prefer to attend.
4. On the "special_requirements" page, enter any special requirements you may have.
5. On the "roles" page, select the roles you are interested in or have the ability to participate in.
6. Choose your session preferences on the "sessions" page.
7. Indicate whether you will be attending or not on the "attendance" page.
8. Finally, add any additional notes on the "notes" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_details** (Text): Enter the details about the event.
* **attendee_info** (Text): Provide your details as an attendee.
* **session_preferences** (Text): Select the sessions you are interested in or prefer.
* **special_requirements** (Text): Enter any special requirements you may have.
* **roles** (Select Multiple, options: 'Yes' and 'No'): Select the roles you are interested in or have the ability to participate in.
* **sessions** (Select Multiple, options: 'Yes' and 'No'): Choose your session preferences.
* **attendance** (Select One, options: 'Yes' and 'No'): Indicate whether you will be attending or not.
* **notes** (Note, no options): Enter any additional notes or comments you have.
