# Armed Force Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Armed Force Registration Form is designed to collect demographic and personal information from individuals who are interested in joining or have joined the armed forces.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Please ensure that you have all the necessary information and documentation ready before starting the form.
2. Complete each section of the form thoroughly and accurately.
3. Make sure to mark the required fields as "required" in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Personal Information
* **Full Legal Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full legal name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Citizenship Status** (`citizenship`, `select_one`, required): Select your citizenship status from the available options (Citizen, Permanent resident, Dual citizen, Other).
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, `text`, required): Please enter your nationality.
* **Contact Information**
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Please enter your valid email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
*   **Current Address** (`current_address`, `text`, required): Please enter your current address.
*   **City, State/Province, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`, `text`, required): Please enter your city, state or province, and zip code.
*   **Which branch are you interested in** (`branch_interest`, `select_one`, required): Select the branch you are interested in (Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard, Space Force).
*   **Type of service** (`service_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of service you are interested in (Active duty, Reserve, National Guard, Not sure).
*   **Current employment status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current employment status (Employed, Unemployed, Student, Self-employed, Other).
*   **Highest level of education** (`highest_education`, `select_one`, required): Select your highest level of education (High school diploma, GED, Some college, Associate degree, Bachelor degree, Graduate degree).
*   **Current or most recent GPA** (`gpa_score`, `number`, optional): Please enter your current or most recent GPA (if applicable).
*   **Do you have any medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you have any medical conditions.
*   **If yes, please describe** (`condition_details`, `text`, optional): Please provide more information about your medical conditions (if applicable).
*   **Current physical fitness level** (`physical_fitness_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your current physical fitness level (Excellent, Good, Average, Below average).
*   **Do you have a criminal record** (`criminal_record`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you have a criminal record.
*   **Do you need security clearance** (`security_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Select 'True' if you need security clearance.
*   **Additional information or comments** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Please provide any additional information or comments (if applicable).
