# Healthcare Digital Twin Research Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering a healthcare digital twin research project. It collects information about the project lead, research group, project description, location, start and end dates, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to ensure you understand the required fields and the purpose of each field.
2. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number if necessary.
4. Provide the name of the Organization you are associated with.
5. Describe your Role within the project.
6. Enter the name of the Research Group you are part of.
7. Provide a description of the research project, including its objectives and scope.
8. Enter the location where the research will take place.
9. Select the Research Start Date and Research End Date of the project.
10. Click Submit to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Your first name, as you would like to be known.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Your last name, as you would like to be known.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Your phone number, if you wish to be contacted.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): The name of the organization you are associated with.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Your role within the research project.
* **Research Group** (`research_group`, text, optional): The name of the research group you are part of.
* **Project Lead** (`project_lead`, text, optional): The name of the project lead, if applicable.
* **Research Description** (`research_description`, text, optional): A brief description of the research project, including its objectives and scope.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): The location where the research will take place.
* **Research Start Date** (`research_start_date`, date, optional): The start date of the research project.
* **Research End Date** (`research_end_date`, date, optional): The end date of the research project.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure the form is completed correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the responsible authority or contact the support team for guidance.
* Ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before providing any sensitive or confidential information.
