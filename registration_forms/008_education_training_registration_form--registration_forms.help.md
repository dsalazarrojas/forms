# Education Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for an education training course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields with your details.
2. Select your preferred course and workshop dates.
3. Confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**:
	* This field is where you can enter your registration message.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`):
	* Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`):
	* Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`):
	* Enter your phone number.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`):
	* Enter your institution's name.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`):
	* Enter the name of the course you wish to register for.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`):
	* Enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`):
	* Enter the time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Course Workshop Type** (`course_workshop_type`):
	* Select 'Yes' if you are attending the course workshop.
* **Participant Registration Date** (`participant_registration_date`):
	* Enter the date you registered for the workshop.
* **Participant Registration Time** (`participant_registration_time`):
	* Enter the time you registered for the workshop.
* **Course Workshop Status** (`course_workshop_status`):
	* Select 'Active' if the course workshop is active.
* **Notes** (`notes`):
	* Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`):
	* Enter your participant ID number.
* **Participant Registration Number** (`participant_registration_number`):
	* Enter your participant registration number.
* **Course Workshop Number** (`course_workshop_number`):
	* Enter the course workshop number.
* **Participant Name in English** (`participant_name_english`):
	* Enter your name in English.
* **Course Name in English** (`course_name_english`):
	* Enter the course name in English.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`):
	* Enter the workshop venue.
* **Workshop Venue in English** (`workshop_venue_english`):
	* Enter the workshop venue in English.
* **Workshop Language** (`workshop_language`):
	* Enter the language of the workshop.
* **Workshop Language in English** (`workshop_language_english`):
	* Enter the language of the workshop in English.
* **Workshop Start Date** (`workshop_start_date`):
	* Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date** (`workshop_end_date`):
	* Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Course Workshop Language** (`course_workshop_language`):
	* Enter the language of the course workshop.

# Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
