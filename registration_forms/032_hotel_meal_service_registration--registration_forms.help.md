## Hotel Meal Service Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Meal Service Registration form is used to collect guest information and preferences for meal service during their stay at the hotel. This form helps the hotel staff to better understand the guest's requirements and provide a more personalized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Guest Info section with your name and other relevant details.
2. Select your meal preferences from the Meals dropdown menu.
3. Choose a schedule for your meal service, if applicable.
4. If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or allergies, type them in the Special Requests text area.
5. Select the user who will be handling your meal service from the Assigned User dropdown menu.
6. Enter your email address in the Email field.
7. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Info**: This section requires you to provide your name and other relevant details to help the hotel staff identify you.
* **Meals**: Select one or more of the available meal options that you prefer. You can choose from Apple, Burger, Salad, or Sandwich.
* **Schedule**: Choose a date and time for your meal service, if you have one. This will help the hotel staff plan your meals accordingly.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, such as dietary restrictions or allergies, type them in this text area.
* **Assigned User**: Select the user who will be handling your meal service from the dropdown menu. This will ensure that your meals are prepared and delivered by the same person.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that the hotel staff can contact you with any updates or questions.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number so that the hotel staff can contact you with any urgent matters.
