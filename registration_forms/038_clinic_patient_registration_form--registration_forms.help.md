# Clinic Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Clinic Patient Registration Form is used to register patients with the clinic, collecting essential demographic information, contact details, and insurance information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) as they are mandatory for submission.
3. Enter your medical record number if you have one.
4. Provide your first and last name.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Select your gender.
7. Enter your email address and primary phone number.
8. Provide your address information (street, city, state, and zip code).
9. If applicable, enter your employer name and occupation.
10. Select your insurance carrier and enter your member ID.
11. Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Medical Record Number** (Text, Optional): If you have an existing medical record number, enter it here.
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Middle Name/Initial** (Text, Optional): Enter your middle name or initial (if applicable).
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (Select One, Required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Primary Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Alternate Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your alternate phone number (if applicable).
* **Street Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address.
* **Apartment/Suite Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **State** (Text, Required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Employer Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your employer name (if applicable).
* **Occupation** (Text, Optional): Enter your occupation (if applicable).
* **Insurance Carrier** (Text, Required): Select your insurance carrier.
* **Member ID** (Text, Required): Enter your member ID.
* **Group ID** (Text, Optional): Enter your group ID (if applicable).
