# Retail Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Application Form is a registration form used to collect information from applicants for retail positions. The form is designed to be completed by the applicant, and the collected data will be used to assess their eligibility and suitability for the position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First name in the First name field.
2. Fill in your Last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter your Date of birth in the Date of birth field (in the format mm/dd/yyyy).
6. Select your Job title from the list of options in the Job title field.
7. If applicable, select the available work shift(s) from the list of options in the Available to work field.
8. You may add any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
9. Enter the name of the Store location where you would like to work in the Store location field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your official documents.
* **Last name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address that you check frequently.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of birth** (Date of birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Job title** (Job title, select_one, required): Select the job title that best describes your current or desired role.
* **Available to work** (Available to work, select_multiple, optional): Select the shifts you are available to work (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Note** (Note, note, optional): Add any additional information or comments you would like to provide.
* **Store location** (Store location, text, required): Enter the name of the store location where you would like to work.
