# Data Ethics Awareness Workshop Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for requesting a data ethics awareness workshop. By filling out this form, you can help us tailor the workshop to your needs and interests.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Enter your information in the required fields (marked as **Required**).
2. Select your preferred workshop topics from the list provided.
3. Choose the workshop venue that suits your needs (Online or Offline).
4. If you have any specific date preferences, enter them in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
5. You can also enter a preferred time for the workshop.
6. If you have any specific duration preferences for the workshop, enter it in the format of hours.
7. Please enter the number of people attending the workshop.
8. Finally, enter the workshop location if it's an offline workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Topics** (`Workshop Topics`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select one or more workshop topics that interest you. You can choose from Data Ethics, Data Protection, or Data Privacy.
* **Workshop Venue** (`Workshop Venue`, `select_multiple`, **Required**): Please select the workshop venue that suits your needs. You can choose between Online and Offline.
* **Workshop Date** (`Workshop Date`, `date`, **Optional**): If you have a specific date in mind for the workshop, enter it in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Workshop Time** (`Workshop Time`, `time`, **Optional**): If you have a preferred time for the workshop, enter it in the format 12:00 PM.
* **Workshop Duration** (`Workshop Duration`, `number`, **Optional**): If you have a specific duration in mind for the workshop, enter it in hours.
* **Workshop Capacity** (`Workshop Capacity`, `number`, **Optional**): Please enter the number of people attending the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`Workshop Location`, `text`, **Optional**): If the workshop is offline, enter the workshop location.

Note: This form is a registration form and is intended to provide information for planning purposes only.
