# Business Customer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Customer Registration Form is a tool used to gather essential information about a business for registration and account setup purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the Company Information section, where you will input your company's name, type, industry category, size, and website (if applicable).
2. Move on to the Contact Information section, where you will provide details about the primary contact person, including their name, title, phone number, email address, and business address.
3. In the Billing Information section, you will choose whether to use the business address for billing or enter a different address.
4. Finally, provide the tax identification number and select the type of account you would like to have.
5. Select any services you are interested in from the list provided.
6. If you have an annual budget, select the range that best suits your needs.
7. Finally, read and accept the terms and conditions to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name (2)**: Enter the legal name of your business. This is a required field to ensure we have accurate records.
* **Business Type (3)**: Choose the type of business entity you are (e.g., Sole Proprietor, Partnership, Corporation, etc.). This helps us understand your business structure.
* **Industry Category (4)**: Select the primary industry category for your business.
* **Company Size (5)**: Indicate the number of employees in your company. This helps us tailor services to your needs.
* **Company Website (6)**: If your business has a website, provide the URL. This is not required but helps us with verification purposes.
* **Primary Contact Person (8)**: Enter the full name of the main contact person for your business.
* **Contact Title (9)**: Provide the title of the contact person, such as CEO or Manager.
* **Phone Number (10)**: Enter the business phone number.
* **Email Address (11)**: Enter the business email address.
* **Business Address (13)**: Provide the street address of your business.
* **City (14)**: Enter the city where your business is located.
* **State/Province (15)**: Enter the state or province where your business is located.
* **Postal Code (16)**: Enter the ZIP or postal code for your business.
* **Country (17)**: Enter the country where your business operates.
* **Use Business Address for Billing (18)**: If you would like to use the business address for billing purposes, select 'True'. Otherwise, choose 'False' to enter a different address.
* **Billing Address (19)**: If you chose to use a different address for billing, enter the address here.
* **Tax Identification Number (20)**: Enter the federal tax ID or EIN (Employer Identification Number) for your business.
* **Account Type (21)**: Select the type of account you would like to have (e.g., Basic, Premium, Enterprise).
* **Services of Interest (22)**: Choose any services you are interested in from the list provided.
* **Annual Budget (23)**: Select the estimated annual budget range that best suits your needs.
* **Accept Terms and Conditions (24)**: Read and accept the terms and conditions to complete the form.
