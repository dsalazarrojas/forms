# Anti Racism Employee Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Anti Racism Employee Training Registration form is a tool for registering employees for anti-racism training sessions. It helps the training coordinator to gather the necessary information to provide a well-structured and inclusive training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Employee full name" field and enter your name as it appears on your HR records.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Employee email" field. This will be used for session confirmations.
3.  Select your preferred session time in the "Preferred session date" field. Options are Session A (morning), Session B (afternoon), and Session C (evening).
4.  If there are any accessibility requirements you'd like to share, type them in the "Accessibility requirements" field. This will help us provide accommodations for a comfortable experience.
5.  If required by your manager or HR, please confirm their approval status in the "Manager approval status" field. Select "Approved" if your manager has given you clearance, "Pending" if it's in review, or "Not required" if this step is not applicable.
6.  Provide any additional comments or instructions for the training coordinator in the "Additional comments" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Employee full name** (`employee_name`, Text, Required)
    *   Enter your full name as it appears on your HR records.
### 2. **Employee email** (`employee_email`, Email, Required)
    *   Enter the email address you'd like to use for session confirmations.
### 3. **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional)
    *   This field is for your department or team name, if applicable.
### 4. **Preferred session date** (`session_date_preference`, Select, Required)
    *   Choose your preferred session time: Session A (morning), Session B (afternoon), or Session C (evening).
### 5. **Accessibility requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, Text, Optional)
    *   Share any special needs you may have for the training session.
### 6. **Manager approval status** (`manager_approval`, Select, Optional)
    *   Confirm your manager's approval status: Approved, Pending, or Not required.
### 7. **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional)
    *   Provide any additional details or instructions for the training coordinator.

## Tips
*   Make sure to enter accurate information for a smooth and tailored training experience.
*   If you have any concerns or requests, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
*   This form is designed to be accessible and easy to use. If you have any difficulties, contact us for assistance.
