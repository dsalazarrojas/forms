# Machine Learning Program Application - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to submit applications for Machine Learning programs. Select the programs you are interested in and provide your contact information to help us process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the programs you are interested in by choosing from the "Program Selection" field.
2. If you want to be contacted about your application, provide your contact information in the "Contact Info" field.
3. Please describe your educational background in the "Education Info" field.
4. Share your relevant experience in the "Experience Info" field.
5. Select the status of your application by choosing from the "Submit Status" field.
6. Select the review status of your application by choosing from the "Review" field.
7. Finally, enter your email address in the "Email" field, and your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other identifying information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your email address and/or phone number so we can get in touch with you about your application.
* **Program Selection** (`program_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more programs you are interested in applying for.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Education Info** (`education_info`, text, optional): Describe your educational background and any relevant qualifications.
* **Experience Info** (`experience_info`, note, optional): Share any relevant experience you have in the field of Machine Learning.
* **Submit Date** (`submit_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are submitting your application.
* **Submit Time** (`submit_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of day you are submitting your application.
* **Review** (`review`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your application.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Submit Status** (`submit_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your application.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
