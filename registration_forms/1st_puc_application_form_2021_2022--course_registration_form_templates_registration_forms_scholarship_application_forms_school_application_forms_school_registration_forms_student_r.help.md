# 1st PUC Application Form 2021 2022 - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 1st PUC application form is designed to collect personal, academic, and family information from applicants to the 1st PUC program. This form helps the admissions team to understand your background, goals, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

* Read through each section carefully and fill out the required information accurately.
* Make sure to include all required fields and attachments.
* Review and confirm your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`2`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your complete legal name as it appears on official documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`3`, `date`, **Required**): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD-MM-YYYY).
* **Gender** (`4`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Nationality** (`5`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your nationality.
* **Religion** (`6`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your religion.
* **Mother Tongue** (`7`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your mother tongue.
* **SSLC/10th Board** (`9`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the board from which you passed your 10th standard (SSLC).
* **SSLC School Name** (`10`, `text`, **Required**): Enter the name of the school you attended for 10th standard.
* **Year of Passing 10th** (`11`, `integer`, **Required**): Enter the year you passed 10th standard.
* **SSLC Percentage** (`12`, `decimal`, **Required**): Enter your SSLC percentage score.
* **Preferred Stream** (`14`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your preferred stream of study from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred Language Medium** (`15`, `select_one`, **Required**): Select your preferred language medium of instruction.
* **Email Address** (`17`, `email`, **Required**): Enter a valid email address for college communications.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`18`, `phone`, **Required**): Enter your 10-digit mobile phone number.
* **Alternate Phone Number** (`19`, `phone`, **Optional**): Enter an alternate phone number (landline or parent's number).
* **Permanent Address** (`20`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your full permanent address with pin code.
* **Correspondence Address** (`21`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter a different address for correspondence (if different from permanent address).
* **Father's Name** (`23`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your father's name.
* **Father's Occupation** (`24`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your father's occupation.
* **Father's Qualification** (`25`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your father's educational qualification.
* **Mother's Name** (`26`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your mother's name.
* **Mother's Occupation** (`27`, `text`, **Required**): Enter your mother's occupation.
* **Mother's Qualification** (`28`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter your mother's educational qualification.
* **Annual Family Income** (`29`, `integer`, **Optional**): Enter your approximate gross annual family income in Indian Rupees.
* **Scholarship Applied** (`31`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Check if you are applying for any scholarship.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`32`, `select_one`, **Optional**): Select how you heard about REVA Independent PU College.
* **Career Goals** (`33`, `text`, **Optional**): Briefly describe your career goals.
* **Special Needs** (`34`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any special requirements or accommodations.
* **Additional Comments** (`35`, `text`, **Optional**): Enter any other information you'd like to share.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`36`, `select_one`, **Required**): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of admission.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to include attachments if required.
* If you have any doubts, please contact the admissions team for assistance.
