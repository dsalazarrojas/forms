# holiday_discount_challenge_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
The holiday discount challenge is a limited-time offer that provides exclusive discounts on various products. The goal of this form is to collect information from interested users and register them for the challenge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the first two fields, making sure to include your correct names as they appear on your official identification documents.
2. Provide your date of birth in the date of birth field, following the MM/DD/YYYY format.
3. Enter your email address in the email field, ensuring it is correct and unique to you.
4. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
5. If you are representing a company, provide the company name in the company field. If not, leave this field blank.
6. Answer the job title question with either "Yes" or "No".
7. Select one or more departments that apply to you from the drop-down menu.
8. Select one or more teams that apply to you from the drop-down menu.
9. Finally, answer the prophets question with either "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a unique email address that is correct and belongs to you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, optional): Answer with either "Yes" or "No" depending on whether you hold a job title.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more departments that apply to you from the drop-down menu.
* **Team** (`team`, `select_one`, optional): Select one or more teams that apply to you from the drop-down menu.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, `select_multiple`, optional): Answer with either "Yes" or "No" depending on your response to this question.
