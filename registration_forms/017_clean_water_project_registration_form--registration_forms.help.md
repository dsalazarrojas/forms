# Clean Water Project Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or organizations interested in participating in the Clean Water Project. The goal is to register users and gather details necessary for the project's smooth operation.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Clean Water Project, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Register" button and fill out the form with your information.
2.  Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in the required fields.
3.  Specify your organization affiliation, if applicable.
4.  Choose your preferred role in the project (Volunteer, Donor, Technical Expert, Community Advocate, or Project Partner).
5.  Select your area of interest (Local Community, Regional, National, or International).
6.  If you have specific skills or expertise, please list them.
7.  Choose your typical availability (Weekdays, Weekends, Evening only, or Variable).
8.  Provide information on your previous experience with water projects.
9.  Write a brief statement explaining why you're interested in this project.
10. Consent to receive project updates and communications.
11. Agree to the terms and volunteer liability waiver.
12. (Optional) If you're an active volunteer, provide emergency contact information.
13. Finally, confirm your registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address** (`registrant_email`, email, required): A valid email address where you can be reached for project updates and communications.
* **Phone Number** (`registrant_phone`, text, required): Your phone number where you can be contacted for project-related matters.
* **Organization Affiliation** (`organization_affiliation`, text, optional): If you're representing an organization, provide its name.
* **Preferred Role** (`participation_role`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred role in the project (Volunteer, Donor, Technical Expert, Community Advocate, or Project Partner).
* **Geographic Area of Interest** (`geographic_interest`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more areas where you're interested in applying your skills or participating in the project.
* **Specific Skills or Expertise** (`specific_skills`, text, optional): List any specific skills or expertise you bring to the project.
* **Typical Availability** (`availability`, select_one, optional): Specify when you're usually available to contribute to the project.
* **Previous experience with water projects** (`experience_level`, select_one, required): Indicate your level of experience with water projects (None, Some experience, Experienced, or Professional Expert).
* **Why are you interested in this project?** (`motivation_statement`, text, required): Write a brief statement explaining your interest in the project.
* **Consent to receive project updates and communications** (`communication_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm that you want to receive project updates and communications.
* **Consent to the terms and volunteer liability waiver** (`liability_waiver`, select_one, required): Agree to the terms and volunteer liability waiver.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide the name and phone number of someone who can be contacted in case of an emergency if you're an active volunteer.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date of your registration.
