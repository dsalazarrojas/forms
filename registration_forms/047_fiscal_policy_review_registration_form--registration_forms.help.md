# Fiscal Policy Review Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fiscal Policy Review Registration Form is used to provide feedback and suggestions on policy changes that affect your work or business. It is not intended to replace or override existing policies, but to complement them with additional feedback.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the existing policies and procedures related to the fiscal policy review process.
2. Select the options that apply to your situation in the "Prophets" field.
3. Enter any relevant information in the "Affiliation" field.
4. List your topic interests in the "Topic Interests" field.
5. If you have any specific questions or concerns, please type them in the "Questions" field (this field is not displayed as it is a note field and will not be submitted).
6. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Prophets** (`prophets`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose from the available options that apply to your situation. Selecting an option will allow us to better understand your context and tailor the feedback process accordingly.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe your affiliation or connection to the policy area you are providing feedback on. Please provide a brief description of your affiliation.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests_1`, text, required/optional): In this field, list the topics or areas that are of interest to you. Please be as specific as possible to ensure that your feedback is relevant and useful.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number_1`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number, if applicable, to allow us to get in touch with you regarding your feedback.
* **Questions** (`questions`, note, required/optional): This field is not displayed as it is a note field and will not be submitted.

## Tips
Please note that the form should only be completed once per user, and duplicate submissions will not be accepted.
