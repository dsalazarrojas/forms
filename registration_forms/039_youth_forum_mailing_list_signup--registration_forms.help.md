# Youth Forum Mailing List Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new users to register and join the Youth Forum Mailing List. Please fill it out to receive emails and updates from the Youth Forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions on each page, filling out each field with your accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details**: Enter your general information in this field. This includes any name you'd like to be known by in the forum.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you'd like to be known by in the Youth Forum.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address that you'd like to use to receive updates and emails from the Youth Forum. Make sure to use a valid email address that you own.
* **Interests**: Select all the interests that apply to you, so we can tailor emails and updates to suit your needs.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for our records, but this is optional.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to help us verify your age and eligibility to join the Youth Forum.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth for further verification purposes.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about yourself that you'd like to share.
* **Submit**: Review and submit your registration for processing.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your registration before sending it to us for processing.
* **Note**: Please do not fill out this field. It is for internal use only.
* **Submit**: Please click this button to complete and submit your registration for processing.
* **Confirm**: Please confirm that your information is accurate before submitting your form.
