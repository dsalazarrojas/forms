# Immigration Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immigration Intake Form is a form used to collect information about a client's immigration-related data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information in the `Contact Information` field.
2. Provide your full name in the `Client Name` field.
3. Enter your birth date in the `Client Birth Date` field.
4. Answer `Yes` or `No` to the question of if this is an immigration event in the `Immigration Event` field.
5. If you selected `Yes` to the previous question, provide more details about the event in the `Immigration Event Other` field.
6. Select all applicable countries of immigration in the `Immigration Country` field.
7. Enter your attorney's name in the `Attorney Name` field.
8. Enter your attorney's email address in the `Attorney Email` field.
9. Enter your attorney's phone number in the `Attorney Phone` field.
10. Provide a brief description of any legal issues in the `Legal Issues` field.
11. Describe your current immigration status in the `Immigration Status` field.
12. Confirm the location of your immigration in the `Immigration Location` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Contact Information (Page 1)
* **Contact Information**: This field is used to store your contact information for any follow-up communication.

### Client Name (Page 2)
* **Client Name**: This field is for your full name. Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your documents.

### Client Birth Date (Page 3)
* **Client Birth Date**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.

### Immigration Event (Page 4)
* **Immigration Event**: Answering `Yes` here indicates that you are experiencing or have experienced an immigration-related event. This can be used for various immigration purposes such as visa applications or refugee status.

### Immigration Event Other (Page 5)
* **Immigration Event Other**: Provide more details about the immigration event if you selected `Yes` in the previous question.

### Immigration Country (Page 6)
* **Immigration Country**: This field is for selecting all countries where you have ever lived or been present. Please select all that apply.

### Attorney Name (Page 7)
* **Attorney Name**: Enter your attorney's name, as this information will be used for communication and case handling purposes.

### Attorney Email (Page 8)
* **Attorney Email**: Enter your attorney's email address for communication and follow-up purposes.

### Attorney Phone (Page 9)
* **Attorney Phone**: Enter your attorney's phone number for communication purposes.

### Legal Issues (Page 10)
* **Legal Issues**: A brief description of any current or past legal issues will help with processing your case.

### Immigration Status (Page 11)
* **Immigration Status**: Describe your current immigration status, e.g., resident alien, citizen, visa holder, etc.

### Immigration Location (Page 12)
* **Immigration Location**: Confirm the location where you currently reside or where your immigration event occurred.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask your attorney or contact our office.
* This form is not intended for official immigration applications or submissions.
* Please keep in mind that this form is used for general information gathering purposes only.
