# Preschool Checklist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Checklist Form is a registration form used to gather necessary information from parents for a preschool student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide the parent's information in the 'Parent Information' section.
2. Enter the student's information in the 'Student Information' section.
3. Choose the necessary classroom supplies and materials from the 'Classroom Supplies', 'Educational Materials', and 'Classroom Equipment' sections.
4. Select the appropriate school term and schedule from the 'Activity Schedule' and 'School Term' sections.
5. Enter the dates of the school term in the 'School Term Start' and 'School Term End' sections.
6. Add any observations or comments from the 'Student Observations' and 'Teacher Observations' sections.
7. Sign and date the 'Parent Signature' section.
8. Submit the form once all necessary fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information (1)**:
	+ This field is where you can enter the parent's name and any other contact information.
* **Student Information (2)**:
	+ This field is where you can enter the student's name, grade, and other relevant details.
* **Classroom Supplies (3)**:
	+ Select all necessary classroom supplies and materials that the student will need for the preschool year.
* **Educational Materials (4)**:
	+ Select all necessary educational materials that the student will need for the preschool year.
* **Classroom Equipment (5)**:
	+ Select all necessary classroom equipment that the student will need for the preschool year.
* **Teacher Information (6)**:
	+ This field is usually completed by the teacher and not the parent. 
* **School Year (7)**:
	+ Select the appropriate school year for the student.
* **Activity Schedule (8)**:
	+ Select the day of the week when the student will have activities.
* **School Term (9)**:
	+ Select the term when the student will attend school.
* **School Term Start (10)**:
	+ Enter the start date of the school term.
* **School Term End (11)**:
	+ Enter the end date of the school term.
* **Student Observations (12)**:
	+ Add any observations or comments about the student's progress.
* **Teacher Observations (13)**:
	+ Add any observations or comments from the teacher about the student's progress.
* **Parent Signature (14)**:
	+ Sign and date the form to confirm that the information is accurate.
* **Date Received (15)**:
	+ Enter the date the form was received by the school. 

## Tips
- Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
- Ensure to complete all required fields.
- Contact the administration or form creator if you're unsure about any field.
- Review the form before submission to ensure it's complete and accurate.
