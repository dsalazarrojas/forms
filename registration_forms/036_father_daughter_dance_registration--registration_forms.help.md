This form is designed for parents/guardians to register their daughters for a Father-Daughter dance event. The purpose of this form is to collect relevant information about the participant and her guardian's consent before the event. This form was created to provide a structured way of collecting necessary information, avoiding duplication and ensuring accuracy in the registration process.

# Father Daughter Dance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Father-Daughter dance event is a special occasion for fathers and daughters to share quality time together. This registration form is designed to facilitate a smooth and organized registration process for participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your registration information**: Fill in the "Registration Information" field with your name and contact details.
2. **Share event details**: If you have any specific requests or special instructions regarding the event, you can type them in the "Event Details" field. This will help us to better prepare for the event.
3. **Add contact information**: You can also add any additional contact details in the "Contact Information" field if you think it's necessary.
4. **Give guardian consent**: In the "Guardian Consent" field, select whether you give or deny consent for your daughter to participate in the event.
5. **Add any additional comments**: If you have any further information or requests, please type them in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, `text`, required): Please provide your name and contact details here. This will help us to keep in touch with you and inform you about any updates regarding the event.
* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `text`, required): You can add any specific information about your daughter's needs or requests for the event here.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, optional): If you need to provide any additional contact details, you can add them here.
* **Guardian Consent** (`Guardian Consent`, `select multiple`, required): Please select whether you give or deny consent for your daughter to participate in the event.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): You can add any further information or requests regarding the event here.
