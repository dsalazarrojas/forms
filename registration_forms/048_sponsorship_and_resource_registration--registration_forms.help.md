# Sponsorship And Resource Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and track sponsorship information from organizations or individuals providing funding or in-kind resources to our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your sponsorship details, including your name and contact information.
2. Select the level of funding you are providing (if applicable).
3. Specify the resources you are providing (if applicable).
4. Enter your website and address (if applicable).
5. Confirm your funding currency and funding start and end dates (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes or comments about your sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsor Details**: This section is used to collect your name, contact email, phone number, and website (if applicable).
* **Funding Levels**: Select the level of funding you are providing to our organization. The options are Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3.
* **In-Kind Resources**: Specify the resources you are providing to our organization. You can select multiple resources from the list.
* **Sponsor Name**: Enter your name as the representative of the organization providing the sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Representative**: Enter the name of the representative of your organization.
* **Sponsor Organization**: Enter the name of your organization.
* **Sponsor Funding Level**: Select the level of funding you are providing.
* **Sponsor Funding Amount**: Enter the amount of funding you are providing.
* **Sponsor In-Kind Resources**: Specify the resources you are providing.
* **Sponsor In-Kind Value**: Enter the value of the resources you are providing.
* **Sponsor Funding Currency**: Select the currency of your funding contribution.
* **Sponsor Funding Start Date**: Enter the start date of your funding contribution.
* **Sponsor Funding End Date**: Enter the end date of your funding contribution.
* **Sponsor Funding Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Funding Level Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your funding level.
* **Sponsor In-Kind Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your in-kind resources.
* **Sponsor Funding Currency Type**: Enter the type of funding currency you are using.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are providing accurate information to avoid any issues or discrepancies in the future.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Please fill out all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate submission.
* If you have any additional questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
