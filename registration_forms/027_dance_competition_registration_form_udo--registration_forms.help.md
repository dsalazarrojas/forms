# dance_comp_registration_form_udo - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for dancers to submit their registration information for a dance competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Contestant Details section with your name and any other relevant information.
2. Enter your dance school's information in the Dance School Info section.
3. Select your solo's division from the Division field.
4. Choose your age category from the Age Category field, selecting all that apply.
5. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contestant Details** (Contestant Details, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Dance School Info** (Dance School Info, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the dance school you represent.
- **Division** (Division, select_one, required/optional): Choose the division your solo belongs to (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
- **Age Category** (Age Category, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose all age categories that apply (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, required/optional): Enter your email and phone number.
- **E-mail** (E-mail, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
- **Name of School** (Name of School, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your school.
- **Dance School** (Dance School, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your dance school.
- **Submit** (Submit, note, required/optional): Click submit to finalize your registration. 

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate information to avoid any registration issues.
- Double-check the division and age categories before submitting.
- If you have any questions, contact the competition organizers. 

Please note that field explanations are based on the given YAML fields and the best-effort intent behind them.
