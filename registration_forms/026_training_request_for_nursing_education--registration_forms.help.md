# Training Request For Nursing Education - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help nursing education providers gather information about your training request. It ensures that they receive the necessary details to prepare for your training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form:
1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields respectively.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
3. Select all relevant training session types (e.g., Basic Life Support, Crisis Intervention, Medication Administration) in the "Training Session Type" field.
4. Choose a location for your training session (e.g., Hospital A, Clinic B, Nursing Home C) in the "Location" field.
5. Select a training date and time (if applicable) for your training session in the "Training Date" and "Training Time" fields.
6. Enter the number of nurses and students you plan to attend the training session in the "Number of Nurses" and "Number of Students" fields respectively.
7. If required, enter the number of teachers for your training session in the "Number of Teachers" field.
8. Add any comments or additional information about your training request in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification or as you would like to be referred to during the training session.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your valid email address where you can be reached regarding your training request.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number where the training provider can contact you for further questions.
* **Training Session Type** (`training_session_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all applicable training session types you require (e.g., Basic Life Support, Crisis Intervention, Medication Administration).
* **Location** (`location`, `select_one`, required): Choose a location where you would like to hold your training session (e.g., Hospital A, Clinic B, Nursing Home C).
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to hold your training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of your preferred training session.
* **Number of Nurses** (`number_of_nurses`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of nurses who will attend your training session.
* **Number of Students** (`number_of_students`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of students you will be training.
* **Number of Teachers** (`number_of_teachers`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the number of teachers who will be teaching your session.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any additional information regarding your training request.
