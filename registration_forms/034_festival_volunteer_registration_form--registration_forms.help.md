# Festival Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Festival Volunteer Registration Form is designed for volunteers to register for a festival event. This form gathers essential information to ensure a smooth and productive experience for both volunteers and organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have a valid reason to participate in the festival volunteer program.
2. Click on the "Festival Volunteer Registration Form" page.
3. Fill out the required fields (name, email, and phone number) with accurate and honest information.
4. Select your availability for the festival (Available Slots) and areas of interest (Interest Areas and Special Talents).
5. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **name** (`name`, `text`, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents. This will help us identify you and contact you regarding volunteer tasks.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, Required): Provide a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you about volunteer tasks and updates.
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, Required): Enter a phone number that we can reach you in case of emergencies or last-minute updates.
* **Available Slots** (`Available Slots`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select the days and time slots you are available to volunteer during the festival. This will help us schedule you for tasks accordingly.
* **Interest Areas** (`Interest Areas`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose the areas of the festival you are interested in volunteering for, such as event setup, crowd management, or artist services.
* **Special Talents** (`Special Talents`, `select_multiple`, Optional): If you have any special talents or skills that could be useful during the festival, select them here, such as event management or technical support.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `text`, Optional): If you are interested in using a specific tool or equipment, please enter it here. This will help us ensure that you have access to the necessary resources during your volunteer shift.

Tips: 

* Please make sure to fill out this form accurately and honestly. Inaccurate or misleading information can lead to disqualification from the festival volunteer program.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us for assistance.
* We appreciate your contribution to the festival and look forward to seeing you there!
