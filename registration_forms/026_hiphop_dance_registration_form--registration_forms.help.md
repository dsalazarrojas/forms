# hiphop_dance_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for general registration purposes, specifically for registering crew members for a hip hop dance event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as described below.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information.
3. Review the terms and conditions to ensure you understand the requirements and rules of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Crew Members** (`crew_members`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the names of all crew members attending the event.
* **Crew Manager** (`crew_manager`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the crew.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required/optional): Read and review the terms and conditions of the event. This is a read-only field and does not require input.
* **Registrant Name** (`registrant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as the registrant.
* **Accept Terms** (`accept_terms`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise stated.
