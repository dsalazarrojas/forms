# Telesales Training Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the Telesales Training Course. Please fill out the form with your details to sign up for a session.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow these steps:
  1. Select whether you would like to attend a training session or not.
  2. Fill in your first name and last name if you are attending.
  3. Enter your valid email address for correspondence.
  4. Provide your phone number for communication.
  5. Choose the desired session date and time from the options provided.
  6. Select the location of the session (if applicable).
  7. Enter the name of the trainer you would like to attend with.
  8. Provide the title of the trainer (if known).
  9. Select the type of training session you would like to attend (if applicable).
  10. Add any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session**: 
  • (`training_session`, `select_one`, required: false)
  This field asks whether you would like to attend a training session. Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending or "No" if not.
* **First Name**: 
  • (`first_name`, `text`, required: false)
  Please enter your first name if you are attending the session.
* **Last Name**: 
  • (`last_name`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter your last name if you are attending the session.
* **Email**: 
  • (`email`, `email`, required: false)
  This field requires a valid email address for correspondence.
* **Phone Number**: 
  • (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Session Date**: 
  • (`session_date`, `date`, required: false)
  Select the desired session date from the provided options.
* **Session Time**: 
  • (`session_time`, `time`, required: false)
  Choose the session time from the options provided.
* **Location**: 
  • (`location`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the location of the session (if applicable).
* **Trainer Name**: 
  • (`trainer_name`, `text`, required: false)
  Provide the name of the trainer you would like to attend with.
* **Trainer Title**: 
  • (`trainer_title`, `text`, required: false)
  Enter the title of the trainer (if known).
* **Training Session Type**: 
  • (`training_session_type`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
  Select the type of training session you would like to attend (if applicable).
* **Notes**: 
  • (`notes`, `note`, required: false)
  Add any additional comments or notes if necessary.

## Tips

- If you are attending a session, ensure to fill in all relevant details for proper communication and tracking.
- If you are not attending a session, you can still provide your details to help us with planning and resource allocation.
- Please review your answers before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
