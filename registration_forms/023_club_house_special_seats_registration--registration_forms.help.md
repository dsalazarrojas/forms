# Club House Special Seats Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Club House Special Seats Registration form is a user-facing form designed to collect information from attendees for a specific event within the Club House. This form helps the Club House team to better prepare and cater to the needs of its attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please ensure you have the correct event name and date before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your group.
3. Provide required information for a successful registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (1)**: Your full name, including any titles or suffixes (e.g., Mr., Mrs., Dr., etc.).
* **Email Address (2)**: Your email address where you would like to receive updates and confirmation of your registration.
* **Phone Number (3)**: Your phone number where you can be reached for any updates or issues.
* **Registration Date (4)**: The date you are registering for the event.
* **Event Name (5)**: The name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date (6)**: The date of the event you are attending.
* **Seat Location Preference (7)**: Choose your preferred seating area within the Club House.
* **Accessible Seating (8)**: If you need ADA accommodations or mobility assistance, select "Yes" to indicate this.
* **Accessibility Details (9)**: Provide more information about your accessibility needs.
* **Number of Seats Needed (10)**: Indicate how many seats you would like to reserve.
* **Names of Companions or Group (11)**: List the names of anyone accompanying you.
* **Is This a Special Occasion (12)**: If this is a special occasion (e.g., birthday, anniversary), indicate this.
* **Ticket Type or Membership Level (13)**: Choose the type of ticket or membership you require.
* **Dietary Restrictions (14)**: If you have dietary restrictions (e.g., vegetarian, vegan), indicate this.
* **Special Requests or Notes (15)**: Any additional requests or information you would like to share with the Club House team.
