# Futsal League Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Futsal League Team Registration Form is designed to gather information from teams that want to participate in the Futsal League. This form is used to collect team details and player information, which will help the league administrators verify the legitimacy and eligibility of the teams and players for the league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Go to the form and click on the "Team Details" page** to start filling out the form.
2. **Fill out the "Team Details" page with your team's name, location, and other relevant information**.
3. **Click on the "Next" button to proceed to the "Player Details" page**.
4. **Fill out the "Player Details" page with each player's name, contact information, and other relevant details**.
5. **Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit your registration**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Details** (`Team Name`, `Location`, `Email`, `Phone Number`, `Team ID`, `Team Logo URL`):
	+ This page is where you will fill out your team's name, location, and contact information.
	+ Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Player Details** (`Player Name`, `Contact Information`, `Role`, `Date of Birth`, `Contact Number`):
	+ This page is where you will fill out each player's name, contact information, and role within the team.
	+ Please make sure to fill in accurate and up-to-date information for each player.
* **Team League Info** (`League Name`, `Division`, `Season`, `Team Name (Alias)`):
	+ This page is where you will fill out your team's league affiliation, division, season, and team name (if different from the team name).
	+ Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Player League Info** (`Player League Name`, `Division`, `Season`):
	+ This page is where you will fill out each player's league affiliation, division, and season.
	+ Please fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* **Submit** (`_no label`, `_required: true`):
	+ This is the submit button. Click on it to complete and submit your registration.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Double-check your form before submitting it to ensure everything is correct.
* If you have any issues or need help, contact us at form@gic.mx.
