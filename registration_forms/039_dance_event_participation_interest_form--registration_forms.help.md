# Dance Event Participation Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential participants about their interest in dancing events, availability, dance style preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your dance style preference from the options provided on the first page.
2. Choose your available time slots for the event on the second page.
3. Add any additional comments or preferences on the third page (optional).
4. Enter your email address on the fourth page.
5. Enter your phone number on the fifth page, including your country code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Dance Style 1**: Choose the type of dance you are interested in participating in.
* **Dance Style 2**: Choose a second type of dance you are interested in participating in.
* **Dance Style 3**: Choose a third type of dance you are interested in participating in.
* **Availability**: Select the timeslot that you are available for the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so that we can contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number, including your country code, so that we can contact you about the event.
