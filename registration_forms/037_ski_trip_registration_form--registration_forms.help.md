# Ski Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ski Trip Registration Form is designed to collect information about each participant for the planning and organization of the ski trip.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the form to start filling it out.
*   Enter your information in the required fields (marked as required).
*   If a field is not required, you can leave it blank or fill it out as you see fit.
*   Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to save your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If you are a student, please enter your student ID number.
*   **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): If you have a guardian, please enter their name as it appears on their official documents.
*   **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): If you have a parent or guardian who would like to receive updates about the trip, enter their email address.

## Tips

*   Make sure to fill out the required fields accurately to ensure that your registration is complete.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact the ski trip organizers.
*   You can only submit this form once. If you try to submit it again, you will receive a message indicating that you have already registered.
