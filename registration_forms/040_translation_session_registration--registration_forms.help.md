# Translation Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Translation Session Registration form is a crucial step in the translation process. It helps us understand your preferences and requirements for a successful translation session. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure we have the necessary information to provide you with an accurate and satisfactory translation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your user information in the "User Information" field.
2.  Select the language pair you prefer for translation (Language Pair).
3.  Provide your availability for the translation session (Availability).
4.  Choose a schedule for your translation session (Schedule).
5.  Enter the contact details of your coordinator (Coordinator Contact, Coordinator Email).
6.  Add any additional comments or notes about your translation session (User Comments, Coordinator Comments).
7.  Confirm your language preferences for the second session (Language Pair 2).
8.  Finally, review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **User Information (user_input_1)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is used to store your user information. Please enter your name, title, or other identifying details as desired.

*   **Language Pair (language_pair_1)** (`select_one`, required/optional): In this field, select "Yes" if you require a translation from one language to another or "No" if you prefer a same-language translation.

*   **Availability (availability_1)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you are available for the translation session.

*   **Schedule (schedule_1)** (`time`, required/optional): Choose the time slot you prefer for your translation session.

*   **Coordinator Contact (coordinator_contact_1)** (`text`, required/optional): Enter the name or title of the coordinator managing your translation session.

*   **Coordinator Email (coordinator_email_1)** (`email`, required/optional): Provide the email address of your coordinator for any further communication.

*   **User Comments (user_comment_1)** (`text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or questions you have about your translation session.

*   **Notes (notes_1)** (`note`, required/optional): Leave any further notes that you'd like the coordinator to know about your translation session.

*   **Language Pair 2 (language_pair_2)** (`select_one`, required/optional): If you have any further language pairs to add to this session, select "Yes" here and fill in the subsequent fields.

*   **Coordinator Comments (coordinator_comment_1)** (`text`, required/optional): This field is for any comments the coordinator may have for you.

*   **Schedule 2 (schedule_2)** (`time`, required/optional): If you need a second schedule, choose the time slot for this session.

*   **Availability 2 (availability_2)** (`date`, required/optional): Enter the date and time you are available for this session.

## Tips
*   Please ensure you have the necessary permissions to submit this form for a translation session.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
*   Contact your coordinator for any clarification on this form or any translation-related queries.
