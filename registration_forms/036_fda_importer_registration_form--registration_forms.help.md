# fda_importer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for FDA registrants to provide information about their company and facility details for importation purposes. It is used to register companies and their facilities with the FDA for compliance with relevant regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter your company name and contact details to identify your organization.
2.  Provide facility details, including address and status.
3.  Indicate your company's type and product type.
4.  Answer questions about facility licenses, certificates, and types.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Importer Company**: Enter your company's name as it appears on any official documents or records.
*   **Facility Details**: Provide a brief description of your facility, including any relevant information about its purpose and location.
*   **Facility Address**: Enter your facility's address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
*   **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for the facility.
*   **Contact E-mail**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
*   **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
*   **Company Type**: Select 'Yes' if your company is an importer, 'No' if not.
*   **Company Type Other**: If your company type is not listed, enter a brief description.
*   **Product Type**: Select all categories that apply to your products, or enter 'Yes' and 'Other' if they are not listed.
*   **Product Type Other**: If you selected 'Other' for product type, enter a brief description.
*   **Product Category**: Select 'Yes' if your product category is correct, 'No' if not.
*   **Product Category Other**: If you selected 'No' for product category, enter a brief description.
*   **Facility License**: Select 'Yes' if your facility has a license, 'No' if not.
*   **Facility License Other**: If you selected 'No' for facility license, enter a brief description.
*   **Facility Certificate**: Select 'Yes' if your facility has a certificate, 'No' if not.
*   **Facility Certificate Other**: If you selected 'No' for facility certificate, enter a brief description.
*   **Facility Status**: Select 'Active' if your facility is active, 'Inactive' if not.
*   **Facility Status Other**: If you selected 'Inactive' for facility status, enter a brief description.
*   **Facility Type**: Select 'Yes' if your facility type is correct, 'No' if not.
*   **Facility Type Other**: If you selected 'No' for facility type, enter a brief description.
