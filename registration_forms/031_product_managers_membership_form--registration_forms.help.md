# Product Managers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **Product Managers Membership Form** is a tool used by product managers to provide their information for consideration of membership to a certain community or group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields, and select your answers from available options. Please note that some fields may have hints to guide you through the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill in your first name as per your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill in your last name as per your identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid work email.
- **Company Name** (`company`, text, optional): If you are affiliated with a company, provide the name of your organization.
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Indicate your job title within your organization.
- **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Provide the name of your department in your organization.
- **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Briefly describe your job role and responsibilities.
- **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): List any relevant skills you have that could be useful for group collaboration.
- **Experience** (`experience`, number, optional): If you are comfortable sharing, indicate your years of experience.
- **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required): Choose your work schedule type, e.g., Full time, Part time, Flexible.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): Indicate your location or workplace.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): If you prefer, provide your contact phone number.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, email, optional): If you feel it is necessary, provide an email of your emergency contact.
- **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are Manager, Team Member, or Intern.
- **Job Type (Other)** (`job_type_other`, text, optional): If none of the provided options match your job type, provide a description.
- **Experience (Years)** (`experience_years`, number, optional): If you are comfortable sharing, indicate your years of experience.
- **Experience Range** (`experience_range`, select_multiple, optional): Select your level of experience (Entry Level, Mid-level, Senior).
- **Submit** (`submit`, checkbox, required): Check to agree to the terms and submit your application. 

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid any issues with form submission.
