# Workforce Robotics Upskilling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant details and training preferences for workforce robotics upskilling programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Enter the participant's name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Training programs** (`training_programs`, select_multiple, required): Choose all the training programs that interest you.
* **Training location** (`training_location`, select_one, required): Select the location where you would like to take the training.
* **Training dates** (`training_dates`, date, optional): Enter the dates of your training.
* **Training hours** (`training_hours`, select_multiple, required): Choose the hours of your training.
* **Training facilitator** (`training_facilitator`, text, required): Enter the facilitator's name and details.
* **Emergency contact details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any emergency contact details.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments about the participant.
* **Participant agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your participant agreement.
* **Participant consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your participant consent.
* **Training program notes** (`training_program_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the training program.
* **Training location notes** (`training_location_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the training location.
* **Training facilitator notes** (`training_facilitator_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the training facilitator.
* **Participant program completion notes** (`participant_program_completion_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the participant's program completion.
* **Additional participant comments** (`additional_participant_comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments from the participant.
* **Facilitator agreement** (`facilitator_agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm the facilitator's agreement.
* **Training program facilitator notes** (`training_program_facilitator_notes`, note, optional): Add any notes about the facilitator's role in the training program.
* **Robot type** (`robot_type`, select_one, required): Confirm the robot type.
* **Robot model** (`robot_model`, select_one, required): Confirm the robot model.
* **Robot serial number** (`robot_serial_number`, text, required): Enter the robot's serial number.
* **Robot warranty** (`robot_warranty`, text, optional): Enter any warranty details about the robot.
* **Participant attendance status** (`participant_attendance_status`, text, required): Enter your attendance status.
* **Participant attendance status notes** (`participant_attendance_status_notes`, text, optional): Add any notes about your attendance status.
* **Participant attendance status date** (`participant_attendance_status_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your attendance status.
