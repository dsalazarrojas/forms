# mental_health_support_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us better understand your needs and preferences for mental health support. It is intended for users who are seeking support or guidance on mental health-related matters. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide us with information about your mental health concerns, preferences for support services, and emergency contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Answer the question about whether you are facing mental health support issues.
3. Choose your preferred support services from the list.
4. Specify if you have a follow-up appointment or meeting scheduled.
5. Select your contact preferences for communication.
6. List any emergency contacts you have, if applicable.
7. Check if you have any medical conditions that may affect your mental health.
8. Choose your mental health professional, if applicable.
9. Submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required): This is where you can provide your name and any other relevant personal details. Please make sure to fill in this field as accurately as possible.
* **Mental Health Support Issues** (`mental_health_support_issues`, select_one, required): Please answer 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you are currently facing any mental health support issues.
* **Support Services** (`support_services`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you are interested in receiving support for. You can choose one or multiple services from the list.
* **Follow Up** (`follow_up`, date, required): If you have a follow-up appointment or meeting scheduled, please specify the date and time. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred method of communication for support and guidance.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): If you have an emergency contact, please list their name and contact information.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, select_multiple, required): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your mental health, please indicate 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Mental Health Professional** (`mental_health_professional`, select_one, required): If you have a mental health professional, please choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Submit** (`submit`, note, required): Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
