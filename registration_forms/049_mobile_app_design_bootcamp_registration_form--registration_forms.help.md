# Mobile App Design Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to register for the Mobile App Design Bootcamp. Fill out the form to provide your registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information.
2. Select your preferred bootcamp dates and time.
3. Choose your preferred payment method.
4. Confirm your enrollment by selecting "True".
5. Select your assigned tool.
6. Upload any relevant output file (if required).
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Form Data
* **Form Data** (`form_data`, text, optional): This field is not applicable as it is not visible in the form.

### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.

### Participant Email
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Mobile Number
* **Mobile Number** (`mobile_number`, text, optional): Enter your mobile number.

### Preferred Bootcamp Dates
* **Preferred Bootcamp Dates** (`preferred_bootcamp_dates`, date, optional): Select your preferred bootcamp dates.

### Preferred Bootcamp Time
* **Preferred Bootcamp Time** (`preferred_bootcamp_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred bootcamp time.

### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred payment method. Options: Cash, Bank transfer, PayPal, Stripe.

### Participant Message
* **Participant Message** (`participant_message`, note, optional): This field is not visible in the form.

### Confirm Enrollment
* **Confirm Enrollment** (`confirm_enrolment`, select_one, optional): Confirm your enrollment by selecting "True".

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select your assigned tool. Options: chatjimmy, chatbot.

### Output File
* **Output File** (`output_file`, select_one, optional): Select the output file format. Options: 049 mobile app design bootcamp registration form--registration forms, 050 mobile app design bootcamp registration form--registration forms.
 
## Tips
- Please ensure your email is correct before submitting the form.
- Make sure your preferred bootcamp dates and time are correct.
- If you need to upload an output file, please make sure it is in the correct format.
