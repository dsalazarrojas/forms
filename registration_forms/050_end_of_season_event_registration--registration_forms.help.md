# End Of Season Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The End Of Season Event Registration form is used to collect information from participants at the end of a season in a specific industry or category.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Main Page**: This is the starting point of the form. It may contain additional information or instructions.
- **Participant Information**: Enter the participant's details, such as name, role, or position.
- **Event Information**: Provide details about the event, including date, time, location, or other relevant information.
- **Participant Details**: Enter any additional information required about the participant.
- **Participant Contact Info**: Enter the participant's contact information, such as phone number or email.
- **Event Info**: Enter further details about the event.
- **Participant Choices**: Optionally, select any choices or options related to the participant or event.
- **Payment Info**: Enter payment details, if required.
