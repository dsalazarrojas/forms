# Veterans Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Veterans Registration Form is designed to collect information from veterans and provide them with a platform to share their experiences and details about their military service. By filling out this form, you will be able to:

* Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your military service
* Connect with other veterans who share similar experiences
* Take advantage of resources and benefits available to veterans

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your accurate and truthful information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you are not duplicating any existing records.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first-name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last-name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Branch of Service** (`branch-of-service`, select_one, required): Select the branch of the military you served in (e.g. Army, Navy, Air Force, etc.).
* **Discharge Date** (`discharge-date`, date, required): Enter the date you were discharged from the military.
* **Service Number** (`service-number`, number, required): Enter your military service number, if applicable.
* **Veteran Status** (`veteran-status`, select_one, required): Select your current veteran status (Active, Inactive, etc.).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification before submitting the form.
* If you have any additional information that you would like to share, please use the additional comments section (not available in this form).
* If you are filling out this form on behalf of another veteran, please make sure to have their permission to do so.
