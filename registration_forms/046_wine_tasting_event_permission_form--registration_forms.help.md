# Wine Tasting Event Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wine Tasting Event Permission Form is a crucial document for wine tasting event organizers to gather essential information from participants. It helps ensure that the organizers have the necessary permissions and information to manage participant interactions during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Organizer's Name and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter the number of participants attending the event.
3. Provide the participant's names, emails, and phone numbers in the respective fields.
4. Indicate whether participants are 18 years or older (I am 18 years or older) or under 18 (I am under 18).
5. Enter the participant's dates of birth, ages, and address.
6. Select the food options and dietary restrictions for each participant.
7. Provide any additional participant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer Name**: Enter your name as the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter your email address as the event organizer.
* **Participant Count**: Enter the total number of participants attending the event.
* **Participant Names**: Enter the names of all participants attending the event.
* **Participant Emails**: Select the email addresses of all participants.
* **Participant Phone Numbers**: Select the phone numbers of all participants.
* **Participant Dates of Birth**: Enter the dates of birth for all participants.
* **Participant Ages**: Enter the ages of all participants.
* **Participant Address**: Enter the address of all participants.
* **Participant Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the participants.
* **Participant Consent**: Indicate whether participants are 18 years or older or under 18.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Participant Tasting Group**: Select whether participants will be in a tasting group or not.
* **Participant Tasting Capacity**: Select whether participants have tasting capacity or not.
* **Participant Food Options**: Select the food options for each participant.
* **Participant Special Diets**: Select the special diets for each participant.
* **Other Participant**: Enter any other participant information.
* **Participant Attending Status**: Indicate whether each participant is attending or not.
* **Participant Status Note**: Enter any additional status notes for each participant.
* **Participant Dietary Restrictions**: Enter any dietary restrictions for each participant.
* **Participant Allergies**: Enter any allergies for each participant.
* **Participant Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about each participant.
