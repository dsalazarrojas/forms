# speech_writing_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for the speech writing workshop, designed to collect participant information and workshop details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
3. Review your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): This is a free-text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `text`, required: false): Here, you can provide any specific information about the workshop you are interested in.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to be listed in the workshop.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.

## Tips
- Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can ask the workshop organizers for clarification.
- We will be in touch with you via the email address you provide. 

Note: The form fields are optional, except where marked as "required" (currently none in this form). The form's purpose is to collect general information from users, and it is not designed to be exhaustive.
