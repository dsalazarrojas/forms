# Security Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Security Program Registration Form is designed to gather information from participants for the security program. This form is used to ensure that the program is well-organized and tailored to the needs of the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. **Provide Participant Information**: Enter your name and any other relevant details in the Participant Information section.
2. **Choose Event Details**: Select the event options that apply to you.
3. **Enter Contact Information**: Provide your contact information, including phone number and any other relevant details.
4. **Share Security Experience**: Enter your security experience in the Security Experience field.
5. **Select Skills or Certifications**: Choose the skills or certifications you possess from the options provided.
6. **Update Preferences**: Select your preferred update options.
7. **Background Check**: Indicate whether you have undergone a background check.
8. **Mailing List**: Choose whether you want to be added to the mailing list.
9. **First Time Attending**: Indicate whether this is your first time attending the program.
10. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
11. **Add Additional Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments you have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the event options that apply to you. This field is optional, but it helps us understand the specific needs of the participants.
- **Contact Information** (`participant_contact`, text, required): Provide your contact information, including phone number.
- **Security Experience** (`participant_experience`, number, required): Enter your security experience. This field will be used to determine your level of expertise.
- **Skills or Certifications** (`participant_skills`, select_multiple, required): Choose the skills or certifications you possess from the options provided.
- **Update Preferences** (`update_preferences`, checkbox, required): Select your preferred update options. This will help us keep you informed about the program.
- **Background Check** (`background_check`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have undergone a background check. This is an optional field.
- **Mailing List** (`mailing_list`, checkbox, required): Choose whether you want to be added to the mailing list.
- **First Time Attending** (`first_time_attending`, select_one, required): Indicate whether this is your first time attending the program.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
- **Additional Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments you have.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth and successful program execution.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact the program administrators.
