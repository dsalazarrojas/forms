# Home Care Aide Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and contact information about Home Care Aides who work with seniors or individuals with disabilities. The information collected will be used for administrative purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter your address information in the following fields:
	* "Address Line 1"
	* "Address Line 2" (if applicable)
	* "City"
	* "State" (select from the dropdown menu)
	* "Zip"
5. Enter your contact information:
	* "Phone"
	* "Email"
6. You may leave fields "Address Line 3" to "Address Line 8" blank if you do not have additional address information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it is listed on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address Line 1** (`Address Line 1`, text, required: false): Enter your street address or post office box address.
* **Address Line 2** (`Address Line 2`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information, such as apartment or suite number.
* **City** (`City`, text, required: false): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`State`, select_one, required: false): Select the state where you live from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, required: false): Enter the zip code for your address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Address Line 3** (`Address Line 3`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **Address Line 4** (`Address Line 4`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **Address Line 5** (`Address Line 5`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **Address Line 6** (`Address Line 6`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **Address Line 7** (`Address Line 7`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.
* **Address Line 8** (`Address Line 8`, text, required: false): Enter any additional address information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact our support team.
