# Youth Candy Making Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians for their child's registration in a youth candy making activity. Please ensure that you are registering the correct child and providing accurate contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the `Parent Contact` field with the contact information for the parent or guardian.
2.  Select a schedule that fits your needs from the `Schedule` field.
3.  Provide any necessary safety information for your child in the `Safety Information` field (this field is optional).
4.  Enter your child's name in the `Youth Name` field.
5.  Optionally, provide your email and phone number in the `Parent E-mail` and `Parent Phone` fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: false): This is the main registration form. Please fill out any other required fields.
* **Parent Contact** (`2`, `text`, required/optional: false): Provide contact information for the parent or guardian. This is the contact information that we will use to reach you.
* **Schedule** (`3`, `select_one`, required/optional: false): Select a schedule that fits your needs. You can choose from Monday at 2 PM, Monday at 4 PM, or Wednesday at 2 PM.
* **Safety Information** (`4`, `note`, required/optional: false): Please provide any necessary safety information for your child. This could include allergies, medical conditions, or other important information.
* **Parent E-mail** (`5`, `email`, required/optional: false): Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates and notifications.
* **Parent Phone** (`6`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to receive calls and messages.
* **Youth Name** (`7`, `text`, required/optional: false): Enter your child's name. This is required information.
