# smart_manufacturing_software_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register companies in the smart manufacturing software system. It is a one-time registration process that captures essential company and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your company's name and address in the "Company Name" and "Company Address" fields, respectively.
2. Provide your contact's name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Enter your company's website in the "Company Website" field, if applicable.
6. Enter the name of the software you are registering for in the "Software Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name**: Enter the name of your company.
* **Company Address**: Enter your company's physical address.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person in charge of the company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Company Website**: Enter the website of your company, if applicable.
* **Software Name**: Enter the name of the software you are registering for.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are completed accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any difficulties or questions, contact our support team.
* You must complete this form only once to register your company in the system.
