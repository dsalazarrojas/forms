# photography_permit_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register photography events with the purpose of obtaining a permit.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each section according to the instructions below.
2. Make sure to fill out all required fields (\*).
3. Review your submission before submitting for accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Photographer Information
* **Photographer** (Yes/No): Indicate if you are the photographer or if you are registering on behalf of a photographer.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person for the photographer.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.

### Event Details
* **Event Type** (Yes/No): Indicate the type of event (e.g., wedding, commercial, etc.).
* **Event Details** (Text): Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of the event.

### Contact Information
* **Photographer Information** (Text): Enter a brief description of the photographer or their team.
* **Event Information** (Date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Start Date** (Date): Enter the start date of the event.
* **End Date** (Date): Enter the end date of the event.

### Additional Information
* **Photographer** (Yes/No): Indicate if the event requires a permit.
* **Contact Person** (Text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Email** (Email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
