# Genetics Certification Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect the necessary information for registration to the genetics certification exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review this help guide carefully.
2. Ensure you're familiar with the form's purpose and requirements.
3. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant registration information in this field.
* **Personal Info** (`personal_info`, text, optional): Provide any personal information you feel is necessary for your registration. This can include your contact details or other relevant contact information.
* **Academic Info** (`academic_info`, text, optional): Enter any academic qualifications or backgrounds that may be relevant to your registration in this field.

## Tips
* Double-check that you have filled out all required fields, including the registration form.
* Review your form submission carefully before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the form administrators at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
