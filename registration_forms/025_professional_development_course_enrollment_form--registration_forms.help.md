# Professional Development Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The professional development course enrollment form allows users to register for a professional development course. Fill out this form to indicate your interest in a course and provide necessary details for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred program from the options provided.
2. Choose the courses you would like to enroll in, if applicable.
3. Provide your email address.
4. Optionally, enter any additional notes or comments.
5. Select the date and time of the course.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Select Program**: (`select_program`, select_one, required): Select one of the available programs that you are interested in registering for.
  This field allows you to choose a program to enroll in the course.
- **Select Course**: (`select_course`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or multiple courses that you wish to enroll in, based on the options provided.
  This field allows you to select one or multiple courses to enroll in for your selected program.
- **Email**: (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we will send course notifications and updates.
  This field is necessary to communicate with you about course-related activities.
- **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
  This field allows you to be contacted by the course organizers or support staff if needed.
- **Notes**: (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.
  This field provides space for you to enter any important details or questions you may have about the course.
- **Date**: (`select_date`, select_one, optional): Choose the date of the course.
  Select the date of the course for which you are registering.
- **Time**: (`select_time`, select_one, optional): Choose the time of the course.
  Select the time of the course for which you are registering.
- **Participant Information**: (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional participant information.
  This field allows you to enter any additional details about yourself or your organization.
- **Participant Information Repeat**: (`participant_info_repeat`, text, optional): Enter any additional participant information (this is a repeat field for any additional details).
  This is another field for entering participant information, if needed.
- **Payment Details**: (`payment_details`, number, optional): Enter your payment details, if applicable.
  This field is for entering payment information, but it is not currently being used.

## Tips
- Please make sure to select a program and courses that you are actually interested in.
- Double-check that your email address is up-to-date and correct.
- If you need to make any changes to your registration, contact our support team.
