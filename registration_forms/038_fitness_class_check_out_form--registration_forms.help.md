# Fitness Class Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Class Check Out Form is used to collect information from customers when they wish to check out from a fitness class, allowing them to provide details about the class they attended, their payment method, and payment amount and date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the "Check Out Information" page and fill in the details about the class you attended.
2. On the "Payment Method" page, choose how you wish to make your payment (Credit Card or Bank Transfer).
3. On the "Class Details" page, select the type of class you attended (Morning, Evening, Weekend, or Other).
4. On the "Payment Amount" page, enter the amount you wish to pay for the class.
5. On the "Payment Date" page, select the date you wish to make the payment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check Out Information (Check Out Information, Text, Optional)**: This field allows you to enter any relevant information about the class you attended.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method, Select One, Optional)**: Choose how you wish to make your payment - Credit Card or Bank Transfer.
* **Class Details (Class Details, Select Multiple, Optional)**: Select the type of class you attended - Morning, Evening, Weekend, or Other.
* **Payment Amount (Payment Amount, Number, Optional)**: Enter the amount you wish to pay for the class.
* **Payment Date (Payment Date, Date, Optional)**: Select the date you wish to make the payment.
