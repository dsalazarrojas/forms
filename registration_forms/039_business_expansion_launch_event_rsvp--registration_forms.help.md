# Business Expansion Launch Event RSVP - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Expansion Launch Event RSVP form is used to collect information from guests about their attendance and preferences for the business expansion launch event. This includes details such as full name, email address, phone number, company name, job title, relationship to the company, RSVP status, number of guests, event date, preferred session, sessions of interest, dietary requirements, transportation, parking, accessibility accommodations, marketing and partnership interest, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Guest Information section with your full name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title (if applicable).
2. Choose your relationship to the company and select the correct RSVP status.
3. Enter the number of guests you will be bringing and provide their names (if applicable).
4. Select the date you can attend and choose your preferred session.
5. Select the sessions you are interested in attending.
6. Indicate any dietary requirements or restrictions.
7. Choose your transportation needs.
8. Specify if you need parking accommodations.
9. Check if you require any accessibility accommodations.
10. Express your interest in marketing and partnership opportunities.
11. Indicate if you would like to be contacted about your guests.
12. Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (2): Enter your complete name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (3): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Company Name** (5): Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title** (6): Enter your job title or position within the company (if applicable).
* **Relationship to Company** (7): Choose how you know the company (Employee, Client, Partner, Investor, Friend, Media, or Other).
* **RSVP Status** (8): Select your attendance status (Yes, I Will Attend, No, I Cannot Attend, Maybe, or Unsure).
* **Number of Guests** (10): Enter the number of people attending with you.
* **Event Date** (13): Select the date you can attend the event.
* **Preferred Session** (14): Choose your preferred session (Morning Session, Afternoon Session, Full Day Event, or No Preference).
* **Sessions of Interest** (15): Select the sessions you are interested in attending (Multiple selections allowed).
* **Dietary Requirements** (16): Select any dietary restrictions you have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Halal, Kosher, or None).
* **Transportation** (17): Choose your transportation needs (No, I Will Drive, Yes, Please Arrange, or Unsure).
* **Parking Needed** (18): Indicate if you need parking accommodations.
* **Accessibility** (19): Provide any special accessibility accommodations you require.
* **Marketing and Partnership Interest** (20): Express your interest in marketing and partnership opportunities (Yes, Very Interested, Somewhat Interested, or Not Interested).
* **May We Contact Your Guests** (21): Indicate if you would like to be contacted about your guests.
* **Additional Comments** (22): Add any additional comments or information about your attendance.
