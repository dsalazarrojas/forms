# Business Funding Review Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Business Funding Review Registration Form is a comprehensive document designed to gather essential information about your business to help lenders, investors, or other stakeholders make informed decisions about potential funding opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Fill out the form carefully and accurately**. Each field is crucial in providing a complete picture of your business.
2. **Ensure that you have the authority to complete the form**. If you are authorized to represent your business, please complete the form on behalf of the business owner or a designated representative.
3. **Be prepared to provide supporting documentation**. Some fields may require additional information or proof, so be prepared to provide relevant documents or explanations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Legal Business Name**: Enter the name as registered with your local government or business registry.
* **Tax ID or Business Registration Number**: Provide your Tax ID or Business Registration Number (e.g., EIN, ABN, or local equivalent).
* **Business Entity Type**: Select the type of business entity you operate under (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, LLC/C Corporation, Non-Profit Organization).
* **Years in Active Operation**: Enter the number of years your business has been in operation.
* **Annual Gross Revenue Range**: Select the range of your annual gross revenue (Under 100,000 USD, 100,001 - 500,000 USD, 500,001 - 2,000,000 USD, Above 2,000,000 USD).
* **Funding Amount Requested (USD)**: Enter the total capital you are seeking.
* **Primary Purpose of Funding**: Briefly describe how the funds will be used.
* **Current Outstanding Loan Obligations**: Select the amount of outstanding loans (No outstanding loans, Under 50,000 USD, 50,001 - 250,000 USD, Over 250,000 USD).
* **Primary Contact Full Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for the application.
* **Professional Email Address**: Enter the email address of the person responsible for the application.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the person responsible for the application.
* **Certify Accuracy of Provided Data**: Confirm that all provided data is accurate to your knowledge.

Note: Please review the instructions carefully and ensure that you have the authority to complete this form accurately. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the relevant department or representative.
