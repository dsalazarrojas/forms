# Class Reunion Attendance Time Off Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps us track the attendance and travel plans of class alumni for the upcoming class reunion. It collects information about the attendee's personal details, contact information, reunion plans, and time off requests from work or school. Your input will be used to plan the reunion event and ensure that everyone has a great experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your full name, maiden name (if applicable), graduation year, and class section or grade.
2. Provide your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Specify your current city and state or province.
4. Choose whether you will attend the class reunion and indicate if you have any guests.
5. If you are taking time off from work or school, provide the required details, including the number of days you need and any additional information.
6. If you need any special accommodations or have dietary restrictions, please let us know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed in the class reunion materials.
* **Maiden Name** (`maiden_name`, `text`, required): If you have a maiden name, please enter it here.
* **Graduation Year** (`graduation_year`, `number`, required): Enter the year you graduated from the class.
* **Class Section or Grade** (`class_section`, `text`, required): Choose your class section or grade level from the list.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your current phone number.
* **Current City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your current city or location.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): Enter your current state or province.
* **Reunion Date** (`reunion_date`, `date`, required): Choose the date of the class reunion.
* **Will you attend the class reunion** (`attendance_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you will attend the class reunion.
* **Number of Guests Attending With You** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, required): Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Names of Guests** (`guest_names`, `text`, required): Enter the names of your guests in a comma-separated list.
* **Current Employer Name** (`employer_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current employer.
* **Time Off Needed from Work** (`time_off_needed`, `select_one`, required): Choose the time off you need from work or school.
* **Time Off Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the start date of your time off.
* **Time Off End Date** (`end_date`, `date`, required): Enter the end date of your time off.
* **Planned Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to arrive at the reunion.
* **Planned Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to depart from the reunion.
* **Hotel Accommodation Needed** (`accommodation_needed`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you need hotel accommodations.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, required): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Any Special Requests or Accommodations** (`special_requests`, `text`, required): Enter any special requests or accommodations you need.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_info`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information or comments you would like to share.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields correctly to ensure your entry is valid.
* If you have any special requests or needs, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
* If you need any assistance or have questions, feel free to contact us.
