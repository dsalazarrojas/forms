# Choir Media Release Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to obtain media release and liability waiver consent from participants or their parents/guardians for media use, such as photos, videos, and audio recordings, related to the participant's involvement with the choir. It ensures that the participant or their parent/guardian understands the terms and conditions of media usage, including limitations and duration of permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the participant information section with your name, date of birth, age, phone number, and email address.
2. Indicate if you are a minor (under 18 years old) and provide parent/guardian information if necessary.
3. Grant permission for media use, including photographs, videos, and audio recordings, and indicate the scope of media usage (e.g., social media, print materials, etc.).
4. Indicate if you understand that the choir owns all rights to the media and that you will not receive compensation for media use.
5. Choose the duration of permission (indefinite, 5 years, 2 years, 1 year, or current season only).
6. Confirm that you understand you may revoke permission in writing for future media use.
7. Waive any claims related to media use within the agreed-upon scope.
8. Confirm that your agreement to use this form is voluntary and without coercion.
9. Confirm that you have read and understand this form and that all your questions have been answered.
10. Sign the form digitally to indicate your consent.
