# Nutrition Reform Consultation Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nutrition Reform Consultation Registration Form is a form used to register client and consultant information for nutrition reform consultations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the client's name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the consultant's name, email, and phone number.
3. Specify the consultation date and time.
4. Enter the consultant's fee, including the currency (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, JPY).
5. Choose whether the consultant's fee includes tax or not.
6. Add any additional notes about the consultation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name**: Enter the client's name.
* **Client Email**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Client Phone**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Consultant Name**: Enter the consultant's name.
* **Consultant Email**: Enter the consultant's email address.
* **Consultation Date**: Select the date of the consultation.
* **Consultation Time**: Select the time of the consultation.
* **Consultant Fee**: Enter the consultant's fee, including the currency (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, JPY).
* **Consultant Fee Currency**: Select the currency of the consultant's fee (USD, EUR, GBP, CNY, JPY).
* **Consultant Fee Tax**: Choose whether the consultant's fee includes tax or not.
* **Client Signature**: Provide a signature from the client.
* **Consultant Signature**: Provide a signature from the consultant.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the consultation.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate processing of the information.
- Use the correct format for dates and times (YYYY-MM-DD for dates, HH:MM:SS for times).
- Ensure that the consultant's fee is entered correctly, including the currency and tax status.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact [support contact] for assistance.
