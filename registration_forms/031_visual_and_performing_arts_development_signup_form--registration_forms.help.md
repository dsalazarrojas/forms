# visual_and_performing_arts_development_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in the Visual and Performing Arts Development program to register and provide necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **Email Address** (`email`, text, required: false): Enter your email address to allow us to contact you.
* **Email Type** (`email_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you want your email to be public, private, or hidden.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for us to reach out to you.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Select the date you are interested in attending an event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Choose the time you would like to attend an event.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, required: false): Read and agree to the terms of the program.
* **Consent Form** (`consent_form`, text, required: false): This form is included to ensure you understand and agree to the program's terms.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the field label for clarity.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact the program administrators.
