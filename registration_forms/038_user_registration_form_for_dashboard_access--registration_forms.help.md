# User Registration Form For Dashboard Access - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information to create a user account for accessing the dashboard. Fill out the form with your details to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all the required fields marked as "required".
2. Be sure to enter a valid email address and phone number.
3. Enter your desired username.
4. Create a password and confirm it to ensure accuracy.
5. Provide your first and last name, date of birth, and address (optional).
6. Submit the form once all fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Username** (`username`, `text`, required): Enter a unique username for your dashboard account.
* **Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Password** (`user_password`, `text`, required): Create a strong password for your account.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, required): Confirm your password to ensure accuracy.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, `note`, optional): Enter your address if you have one.
* **Useristency Form** (`user_registration_form_for_dashboard_access`, `text`, required): This field is not applicable for actual use, but it's included as part of the form structure and should be ignored.
