# Festival Partner Appreciation Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Partner Appreciation Night RSVP form is a tool for festival organizers to collect necessary information from partners. It helps to ensure accurate planning and management of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your partner's name.
2. Enter your partner's email address.
3. Provide your partner's phone number.
4. Enter your partner's address.
5. Select a date for the appreciation night event.
6. Choose a start time for the event.
7. Choose an end time for the event.
8. Specify the number of guests your partner will bring.
9. Add any special requests for the event.
10. Confirm your RSVP response.
11. Enter a message or note for the event.
12. Enter the name of the event.
13. Enter the date of the event.
14. Choose the start time of the event.
15. Choose the end time of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Festival Name** (`festival_name`, text, optional): Enter your partner's name or name of your festival.
* **Festival Email** (`festival_email`, email, optional): Enter your partner's email address.
* **Festival Phone Number** (`festival_phone`, text, optional): Enter your partner's phone number.
* **Festival Address** (`festival_address`, text, optional): Enter your partner's address.
* **Festival Date** (`festival_date`, date, optional): Choose a date for the appreciation night event.
* **Festival Start Time** (`festival_start_time`, time, optional): Choose a start time for the event.
* **Festival End Time** (`festival_end_time`, time, optional): Choose an end time for the event.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Specify the number of guests your partner will bring.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, optional): Add any special requests for the event.
* **RSVP Response** (`rsvp_response`, select_one, optional): Confirm your RSVP response.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Enter a message or note for the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_start_time`, time, optional): Choose the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time** (`event_end_time`, time, optional): Choose the end time of the event.
