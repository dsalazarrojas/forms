# land_restoration_project_meeting_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a land restoration project meeting. It helps to collect essential information about the meeting, including attendees' details, preferred meeting time, and meeting dates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and organization (if applicable).
2. Select your role in the meeting from the options provided.
3. Enter your email address, which will be used as your contact information.
4. Provide your phone number, which will be used as your preferred contact method.
5. Specify your preferred meeting time.
6. Select the start and end dates of the meeting.
7. Enter the location of the meeting, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization name (if applicable).
- **Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role in the meeting from the options provided, which are:
  - Administrator
  - Conservation Officer
  - Member
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used as your contact information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number, which will be used as your preferred contact method.
- **Preferred Meeting Time** (`preferred_meeting_time`, time, optional): Specify your preferred meeting time.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Select the start date of the meeting.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Select the end date of the meeting.
- **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the meeting, if applicable.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill in all required fields.
- If you select a role that requires additional information, be prepared to provide it at a later stage.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the meeting organizers for assistance.
