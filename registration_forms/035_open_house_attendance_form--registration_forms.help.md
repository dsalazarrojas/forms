# Open House Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to record attendance at Open House, Tour, or Meeting events. It captures essential details about the event and the attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Event Details**
	* This section collects information about the event. Fill in the event details accurately.
2. **Contact Info**
	* Enter your contact information in this section.
3. **Event Attendance**
	* Indicate the number of attendees who have arrived.
4. **Event Type**
	* Choose one of the following event types: Open House, Tour, or Meeting.
5. **Event Date and Time**
	* Enter the date and time of the event.
6. **Event Location**
	* Enter the location of the event (optional).
7. **Notes and Comments**
	* Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
8. **Form Submitter**
	* Indicate who submitted the form: Visitor, Staff, or Guest.
9. **Event Organizer's Information**
	* Enter any relevant information about the event organizer (optional).
