# Fantasy Sports Player Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fantasy Sports Player Selection form is designed to help users create their fantasy sports teams by selecting their preferred teams for each section of the fantasy sports league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred team for the Fantasy League in the first section.
2. Choose the players you have selected for the first section.
3. Confirm your selection for the second section.
4. Select your preferred teams for the second section.
5. Review your selections to ensure you're comfortable with your team composition.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Fantasy Team A** (`fantasy_league_choices`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This is the first section where you select your preferred team for the fantasy sports league. You have three options: Fantasy Team A, Fantasy Team B, or Fantasy Team C.
- **Selected Players** (`players_chosen_1`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section is used to confirm the players you have selected for the Fantasy Team A you chose earlier. Choose 'Yes' if you have selected the players, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Selected Players 2** (`player_selection_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): In this section, you're asked to confirm if you have selected any players for the second section. Choose 'Yes' if you have selected players, and 'No' otherwise.
- **Player Selection 2** (`player_selection_3`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): This section allows you to select your preferred teams for the second section of the fantasy sports league. Choose from the options 'Yes' or 'No'.
