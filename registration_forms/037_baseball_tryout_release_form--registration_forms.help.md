# Baseball Tryout Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Baseball Tryout Release Form is a crucial document that must be filled out by participants and their parents or guardians before they can attend a baseball tryout. This form serves as a waiver and consent document, allowing the organization to collect necessary information about the participant and their medical history.

## How To Complete This Form
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If the participant is under 18 years old, their parent or guardian must be present to sign the form.
- Read each field carefully and ensure you understand the information being asked.
- Sign the form where required, and date it as instructed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Player First Name**: Please enter the participant's first name.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: First name

- **Player Last Name**: Please enter the participant's last name.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Last name

- **Player Date of Birth**: Enter the participant's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: MM/DD/YYYY format

- **Player Age**: Enter the participant's current age.
  - Type: Number
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Current age

- **Parent or Guardian Full Name**: Please enter the name of the parent or guardian signing this form.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Legal guardian

- **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address for communication purposes.
  - Type: Email
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: For communications

- **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Best contact number

- **Home Address**: Enter the participant's home address.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Complete street address

- **City**: Enter the city where the participant resides.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Your city

- **State**: Enter the state where the participant resides.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Your state

- **ZIP Code**: Enter the participant's ZIP code.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Postal code

- **Tryout Date**: Enter the date of the tryout.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Date of tryout

- **Team or Organization**: Enter the team or organization the participant is trying out for.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Which team

- **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies the participant has.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Any conditions we should know

- **Current Medications**: List any current medications the participant is taking.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: List any medications

- **Medical Insurance Provider**: Enter the insurance company name.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Insurance company

- **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter the insurance policy number.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Insurance policy number

- **Primary Care Physician Name**: Enter the name of the participant's primary care physician.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Doctor name

- **Physician Phone**: Enter the doctor's phone number.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: Doctor phone number

- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Full name

- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Phone number

- **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Enter how the emergency contact is related to the participant.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: How are they related

- **Assumption of Risk**: Select "I understand and accept the risks" if the participant understands that tryouts involve physical activity and risk.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: I understand tryouts involve physical activity and risk

- **Liability Release**: Select "I agree to release liability" to release the organization from liability.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: I release the organization from liability

- **Medical Treatment Authorization**: Select "I authorize medical treatment" to authorize emergency medical treatment.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: I authorize emergency medical treatment

- **Photo and Video Consent**: Select "Yes, I consent" to allow the organization to use photos or videos of the participant.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: May organization use photos or videos

- **Code of Conduct Agreement**: Select "I agree to follow all rules" to agree to the code of conduct.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Will follow all rules and instructions

- **Is Participant Under 18**: Select "Yes, participant is a minor" if the participant is under 18 years old.
  - Type: Select One
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Parental consent required

- **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type the name as a signature for the parent or guardian.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Type full name as signature

- **Participant Signature**: Type the participant's signature.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Type full name

- **Signature Date**: Enter the date signed.
  - Type: Date
  - Required: Yes
  - Hint: Date signed

- **Witness Signature**: For office use only, this is a witness signature.
  - Type: Text
  - Required: No
  - Hint: For office use
