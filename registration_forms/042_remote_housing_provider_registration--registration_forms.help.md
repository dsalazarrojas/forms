# Remote Housing Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Housing Provider Registration form is designed for remote housing providers to register their details and information with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have all the required information ready.
2. Complete each field accurately and honestly, as per the field's description.
3. Ensure that you do not submit duplicate registrations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Provider Details
* **Provider Details** (`provider_details`, `text`, optional): This field is used to enter your provider's name, address, and email. Please enter your name as it appears on official documents. Enter your address where you can be contacted. Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.

### University Info
* **University Info** (`university_info`, `select_one`, optional): This field is used to select your university affiliation. Please select the name of your university from the provided options.

### Documentation
* **Documentation** (`documentation`, `note`, optional): This field is used to attach any supporting documentation for your registration. Please attach any relevant documents that prove your affiliation.

### Agreement
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, optional): This field is used to select the agreements you are willing to adhere to. Please select all the agreements that apply to you.

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you complete all the required fields accurately and honestly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the field's description or hint for clarification.
* Please review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure that it is accurate and complete.
