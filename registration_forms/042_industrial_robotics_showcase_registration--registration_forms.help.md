# industrial_robust_showcase_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The industrial robust showcase registration form collects information from event organizers, exhibitors, and attendees to provide a better experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill out the ones that apply to you.
2. Make sure to provide accurate information to ensure a smooth experience for everyone involved.
3. Save and submit your filled-out form once you're done.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer_info`, text): This field is for event organizers to provide information about themselves.
* **Exhibitor** (`exhibitor_info`, text): This field is for exhibitors to provide information about themselves.
* **Attendee** (`attendee_info`, text): This field is for attendees to provide information about themselves.
* **Participation Type** (`participation_type`, select_one): Select one of the options: Exhibitor, Sponsor, or Attendee.
* **Event Planning** (`event_planning_info`, text): This field is for event organizers to provide information about the event.
* **Interests** (`interests`, text): This field is for attendees to provide information about their interests.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_multiple): Select one or more of the options: phone, email, or website.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note): This field is for event organizers to provide any additional information about the event.
* **Confirm Participation Type** (`confirm_participation_type`, select_one): Select one of the options: Exhibitor, Sponsor, or Attendee.
* **Upload File** (`upload_file`, text): This field is for event organizers to upload a file related to the event.
* **Save and Submit** (`save_and_submit`, select_multiple): Select one of the options: Save and submit this form or Cancel.
* **Confirm Save and Submit** (`confirm_save_and_submit`, select_one): Select one of the options: Save and submit this form or Cancel.
