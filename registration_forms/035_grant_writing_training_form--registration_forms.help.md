# Grant Writing Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Grant Writing Training Form is used to register and manage grant writing workshops. This form helps administrators gather essential information about each workshop, including its title, date, time, grant writer, grant amount, workshop venue, contact email, and contact phone number.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* Workshop Title: Enter the title of the workshop.
	* Grant Writer: Enter the name of the grant writer.
	* Grant Amount: Enter the grant amount.
2. Enter any optional fields as needed:
	* Workshop Date: Enter the date of the workshop (optional).
	* Workshop Time: Enter the time of the workshop (optional).
	* Workshop Venue: Enter the location of the workshop (optional).
	* Email: Enter a contact email (optional).
	* Phone: Enter a contact phone number (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the workshop, e.g., "Grant Writing Basics" or "Advanced Grant Writing Techniques".
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the workshop in the standard date format, e.g., "2024-02-27" or "February 27, 2024".
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the workshop in the 24-hour format, e.g., "09:00" or "9:00 AM".
* **Grant Writer** (`grant_writer`, text, required): Enter the name of the grant writer.
* **Grant Amount** (`grant_amount`, number, required): Enter the grant amount.
* **Workshop Venue** (`workshop_venue`, text, optional): Enter the location of the workshop, e.g., "Conference Room A" or "Online Meeting Platform".
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a contact email for the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for the workshop.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate registration and management of the workshop.
* Use the standard date format for the workshop date (e.g., "2024-02-27" or "February 27, 2024").
* Use the 24-hour format for the workshop time (e.g., "09:00" or "9:00 AM").
