# property-maintenance-training-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information for property maintenance training registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your session choices by checking the relevant boxes on the "Session Choices" page.
2. Provide your participant details in the "Participant Details" text field.
3. Choose a date for the training session in the "Sessions" date field.
4. Indicate if you have any special requirements for the training session.
5. Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant in the "Participant Notes" field.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Choices** (Select Multiple, required: false): 
Select all the sessions you would like to attend. This will help us ensure that we have enough space and resources for you.
* **Participant Details** (Text, required: false): Please provide your name, email address, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Sessions** (Date, required: false): Choose a date for the training session you would like to attend.
* **Special Requirements** (Select One, required: false): If you have any special requirements or needs, such as accessibility accommodations, please let us know.
* **Participant Notes** (Note, required: false): Add any additional notes about yourself as a participant, such as any experience with property maintenance or previous training. 
* **Submit** (Text, required: false): Click this button to complete your registration.
