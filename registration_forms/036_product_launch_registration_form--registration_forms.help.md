# product_launch_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The **product_launch_registration_form** is designed to collect registration information for a product launch event. This form helps the event organizers collect details from attendees who want to participate in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **attendee name** in the `attendee_name` field.
2. Enter your **company** name in the `company` field.
3. Provide your **job title** in the `job_title` field.
4. Enter your **contact email** in the `contact_email` field.
5. Enter the **product name** in the `product_name` field.
6. Select the **launch date** for the event from the calendar.
7. Choose the **launch time** for the event from the time options.
8. Select your **registration type** by choosing 'Yes' or 'No' from the options.
9. If applicable, select multiple **registration types** (in-person, online, or remote) from the options.
10. Enter the **event name** in the `event_name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name** (`attendee_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your company if applicable.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Enter your job title or position.
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Product name** (`product_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the product you are registering for.
* **Launch date** (`launch_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Launch time** (`launch_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time of the event from the options.
* **Registration type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you are interested in registering for the event.
* **Registration types** (`registration_type_options`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the types of registration (in-person, online, or remote) that apply to you.
* **Event name** (`event_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
