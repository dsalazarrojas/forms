# subscription_service_licensing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for users to register their subscription service license. It has multiple pages, each with different fields to collect various pieces of information from the user.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in the following pages:
   - Page 1: subscription service licensing form
   - Page 2 to 10: Follow the same steps for each page, filling out the fields as required or optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **subscription service licensing form** (Page 1, label, text): This is the main title of the form. You will see the form details here.
- **User Name** (Page 2, label, text): Please fill out your name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (Page 2, label, email): Please fill out your email address.
- **Phone** (Page 3, label, text): Please fill out your phone number.
- **Date** (Page 4, label, date): Please fill out a valid date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (Page 4, label, time): Please fill out a valid time in the format of HH:MM:SS.
- **Select One** (Page 5, label, select_one): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'
- **Select Multiple** (Page 5, label, select_multiple): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'
- **Date** (Page 6, label, date): Please fill out a valid date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (Page 7, label, time): Please fill out a valid time in the format of HH:MM:SS.
- **Select One** (Page 8, label, select_one): Select one option from the dropdown menu.
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'
- **Select Multiple** (Page 9, label, select_multiple): Select multiple options from the dropdown menu.
  * 'Yes'
  * 'No'
- **Date** (Page 10, label, date): Please fill out a valid date in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
- **Time** (Page 10, label, time): Please fill out a valid time in the format of HH:MM:SS.
