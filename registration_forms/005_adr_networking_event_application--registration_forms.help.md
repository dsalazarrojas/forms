# ADR Networking Event Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the ADR Networking Event Application form. This form is used to gather information from potential attendees for an ADR networking event. It collects personal and professional details, interests, and session preferences to help tailor the experience for attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

### Before Starting
Ensure you have all the required and optional information ready before filling out the form. Some fields are mandatory and must be filled out to ensure we can properly register you for the event. Review the form carefully and take your time to answer each question accurately.

### Step-by-Step Guide

1. **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your professional identification or business cards.
2. **Email Address**: This is where you will receive confirmation of your registration, so make sure it's accurate and up-to-date.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for any follow-up questions or information.
4. **Organization / Law Firm**: Provide your current or previous affiliation with a firm or organization.
5. **Professional Role**: Select your primary role within the ADR industry, which helps us tailor the event to your interests.
6. **Years of Experience in ADR**: Enter your years of experience in Alternative Dispute Resolution to help us gauge your level of expertise.
7. **Areas of Practice / Interest**: Select all relevant areas of law or practice that interest you to help us tailor breakout sessions to your needs.
8. **What are your primary goals for attending this event?**: Briefly describe your main objectives for attending the networking event.
9. **Preferred Breakout Sessions**: Select the sessions that interest you most from the available options to help us schedule the most valuable experience for you.
10. **Dietary Restrictions / Allergies**: If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please select them here for our catering team to accommodate your needs.
11. **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any special accommodation, describe your needs so we can make necessary arrangements.
12. **Have you attended an ADR networking event with us before?**: Answer this question to help us track attendance and plan for repeat attendees.
13. **How did you hear about this event?**: Select the source from which you learned about the event to help us gauge marketing effectiveness.
14. **Any other comments or questions for the organizers?**: Use this space to share any additional thoughts or questions you may have.

* **How did you hear about this event?**: Choose the source from which you learned about the event.
* **What are your primary goals for attending this event?**: Briefly describe your main objectives.
* **Preferred Breakout Sessions**: Select the sessions that interest you most.
* **Additional Comments / Questions**: Use this space for any additional thoughts or questions.

## Tips

- Make sure you answer all mandatory fields to ensure proper registration.
- Select all relevant interests and practice areas to help us tailor the event experience.
- Be honest and accurate with your contact information to receive timely notifications.
- If you have any special needs or requests, don't hesitate to mention them to us.
- Review the form carefully to ensure you answer all relevant questions accurately.
