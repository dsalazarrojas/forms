# # yoga_studio_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from clients who want to access the yoga studio. It is used to gather essential details such as name, contact information, and payment information for membership purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your name in the "Clients Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
3. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your membership type from the "Membership Type" field.
5. If you want to join the studio, select the start date for your membership in the "Membership Start Date" field.
6. If you want to renew your membership, select the end date of your current membership in the "Membership End Date" field.
7. Choose the type of card you have from the "Card Type" field.
8. Enter the number of your credit card in the "Card Number" field.
9. Enter the CVV of your credit card in the "CVV" field.
10. If you have an expiration date for your card, enter it in the "Expiration Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clients Name** (`clients_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required): Enter your email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want us to call you for any reason.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose between monthly, annual, or trial membership options.
	+ If you choose monthly, your membership will automatically renew every month.
	+ If you choose annual, your membership will automatically renew every year.
	+ If you choose trial, you will have access to the studio for a limited time.
* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want your membership to start.
* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, date, optional): Select the date your current membership will end if you want to renew.
* **Card Type** (`card_type`, select_one, optional): Choose between Visa, Mastercard, or American Express.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, text, optional): Enter the number of your credit card.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, text, optional): Enter the 3-digit code on the back of your credit card.
* **Expiration Date** (`exp_date`, date, optional): Enter the expiration date of your credit card.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, especially the "Email" field.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* Please note that this form is for internal use only and not affiliated with any form platform.
