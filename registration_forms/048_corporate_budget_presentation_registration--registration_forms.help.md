# Corporate Budget Presentation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Budget Presentation Registration form is a crucial document that allows presenters to provide essential information about their corporate budget presentations. This form is used to gather details about the presenter, session preferences, budget requirements, and financial information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the user details section with your personal information.
2. Select the session choices that apply to your presentation.
3. Choose whether the presentation requires financial presentation requirements.
4. Choose the session topics that will be covered in your presentation.
5. Select the session preferences for your presentation.
6. Determine the budget requirements for your presentation.
7. Indicate the purpose of your presentation.
8. Provide financial details about your presentation.
9. Enter the presenter details for your presentation.
10. Select the company details for your presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User details** (`user_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter your personal information, including your name and contact details.
* **Session choices** (`session_choices`, select_multiple, required): Select the options that apply to your presentation. This field is not applicable for this form.
* **Financial presentation requirements** (`financial_presentation_requirements`, select_one, required): Choose whether your presentation requires financial presentation requirements.
* **Session topics** (`session_topics`, select_one, required): Select the session topics that will be covered in your presentation.
* **Session preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select the session preferences for your presentation.
* **Budget requirements** (`budget_requirements`, select_one, required): Determine the budget requirements for your presentation.
* **Presentation purpose** (`presentation_purpose`, select_one, required): Indicate the purpose of your presentation.
* **Financial details** (`financial_details`, select_multiple, required): Provide financial details about your presentation.
* **Presenter details** (`presenter_details`, select_multiple, required): Enter the presenter details for your presentation.
* **Company details** (`company_details`, select_multiple, required): Select the company details for your presentation.

Note: Although the form is labeled as "required" in the YAML file, some fields have a "false" value, indicating that they are actually optional. However, it's essential to fill them out accurately to provide complete information about your presentation.
