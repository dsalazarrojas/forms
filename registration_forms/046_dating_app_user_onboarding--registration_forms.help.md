# Dating App User Onboarding - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form gathers information about a new user's onboarding data for the dating app, including personal details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *) with your personal details.
2. Enter your First Name, Email, Date Of Birth, Password, and Phone Number.
3. Select your Gender and Relationship Status.
4. Choose your Date Of Interest and Time Of Interest.
5. Upload a User Photo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name as it appears on your documents.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address that you use to register with our app.
* **Date Of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Password** (text): Enter a secure password to access your account. Passwords must be at least 8 characters long and contain at least one letter and one number.
* **Password Repeat** (text): Re-enter your password to confirm your input.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number to receive notifications and updates.
* **Gender** (select_one): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Relationship Status** (select_multiple): Select all relationship status that apply to you.
* **Date Of Interest** (date): Enter a date when you are interested in meeting someone new.
* **Time Of Interest** (time): Enter a time of day when you are most interested in meeting someone.
* **User Photo** (text): Upload a photo of yourself to make your profile more engaging.

## Tips

* Please be accurate when filling out your details, as they will be used to match you with potential partners.
* Ensure your password is secure and not easily guessable.
* Upload a clear and recent photo for better visibility on the app.
* Relationship status should reflect your current status accurately.
* Be honest when selecting your date and time of interest to increase chances of successful matches.
