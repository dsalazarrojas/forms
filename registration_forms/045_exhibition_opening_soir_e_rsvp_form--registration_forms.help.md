> # Exhibition Opening Soirée RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Exhibition Opening Soirée RSVP Form is used to gather information from attendees for an exhibition opening event. This includes their RSVP status, contact details, and any additional information they may want to provide.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your RSVP status in the "RSVP Status" field.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
3. Provide any additional information you want to share in the "Additional Info" field.
4. Choose your payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field.
5. Enter your event status in the "Event Status" field.
6. Select your sponsorship status in the "Sponsorship" field.
7. Select your organizer from the options provided in the "Organiser" field.
8. Enter your organiser's email address in the "Organiser Email" field.
9. Enter your organiser's phone number in the "Organiser Phone" field.
10. Add any additional notes in the "Additional Note" field.
