# Appreciation Event Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for an appreciation event. It helps event organizers tailor the event to attendees' needs, ensuring a positive experience for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the participant's basic contact information.
2.  Confirm your attendance by marking if you will attend the event.
3.  If attending, specify the number of guests accompanying you.
4.  Declare any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
5.  Indicate your interest in specific event activities.
6.  Add any additional comments or requests you might have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it is known to others.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your surname as known to others.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Provide a valid email address that you can be reached at for any updates or information about the event.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for event details or updates.
*   **Department or Team** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you are a team member, enter your department for identification purposes.
*   **Event Details** (`event_details`, `note`, optional): Write down any specific information about the event you want to share with the organizers.
*   **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the appreciation event.
*   **Will you attend this appreciation event?** (`attendance_confirm`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your intention to attend the event by selecting 'Yes', 'No', or 'Maybe'.
*   **Number of Guests (including yourself)** (`num_guests`, `number`, optional): If you are bringing guests, specify the number here.
*   **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, select all that apply to ensure your needs are met.
*   **Activities You Are Interested In** (`activities_interest`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose the activities that interest you the most.
*   **Special Requests or Comments** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or comments, type them here.
