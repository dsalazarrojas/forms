# Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form is designed to collect information from users for virtual real estate tours. It helps the real estate company to manage and track registrations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To begin, fill in your Name.
2. Enter a valid Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for further contact or updates.
4. Select a preferred Appointment date for the virtual tour.
5. Choose the type of Real Estate from the available options.
6. Enter the Location where the virtual tour will take place.
7. Optionally, add any additional Message for the tour guide.
8. Confirm your Agreement to the terms and conditions.
9. Add any Notes for the tour guide to consider.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes. This will be used for sending updates and reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Provide your phone number for further contact or updates.
* **Appointment** (`appointment`, date, required): Select a date and time for the virtual tour. Make sure to choose a date and time that works for you.
* **Real Estate Type** (`real_estate_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of real estate you are interested in. This will help the tour guide to better prepare for the tour.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location where the virtual tour will take place. This will help the tour guide to be prepared for the location.
* **Message** (`message`, text, optional): Optionally, add any additional message for the tour guide.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions. Please review the terms and conditions before selecting this field.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes that the tour guide should consider during the virtual tour.
* **Real Estate Virtual Tour Registration Form** (`real_estate_virtual_tour_registration_form_submit_button`, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
