# yoga_session_attendance_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The yoga_session_attendance_form is designed to collect participant information and session details for yoga sessions. This form is used to register participants for a yoga session and to keep track of session status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the "participant_info" section.
2. Select the correct session date and status in the "class_date" and "class_status" sections.
3. Choose the correct class type in the "class_type" section.
4. Enter the session start and end times in the "session_start_time" and "session_end_time" sections.
5. Select whether the studio or instructor has confirmed your registration in the "studio" and "instructor" sections.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "note" section.
7. Finally, review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_info**: Enter your participant information, such as your name, email, and phone number.
* **class_date**: Select the date of the yoga session you are registering for.
* **session_id**: Enter the session ID provided by the session organizer.
* **instructor_id**: Enter the instructor ID provided by the session organizer.
* **registered**: This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
* **note**: Add any additional notes about your registration or the session.
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **submitted_by**: Select who submitted the registration (participant, instructor, or studio).
* **submit_date**: Enter the date you submitted the registration.
* **class_status**: Select the current status of the session (active, inactive, or cancelled).
* **class_status_note**: Add any additional notes about the session status.
* **class_type**: Choose the type of class (yoga, meditation, or other).
* **session_duration**: Enter the length of the session.
* **session_start_time**: Enter the start time of the session.
* **session_end_time**: Enter the end time of the session.
* **studio**: Select whether the studio has confirmed your registration.
* **instructor**: Select whether the instructor has confirmed your registration.
* **submitted**: This field is not currently used and can be ignored.
