# Material Innovation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and interest information from attendees of the Material Innovation Workshop. Please fill out this form to help us tailor the workshop experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the requested information for each field. All fields are optional, but your participation will help us better serve you during the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`registration_information`, text, not required): Please enter a brief message about your interests and goals for the workshop.
* **Name** (`name`, text, not required): Enter your name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for workshop communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number for workshop communication.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, select_multiple, not required): Choose the topics that interest you the most from the list provided.
	+ Innovation
	+ R&D
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, select_multiple, not required): If you have any special needs, please select the relevant options from the list.
	+ wheelchair accessible
	+ dietary restrictions
	+ hearing impact
	+ mobility issues
	+ other special needs
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit your registration.

## Tips
Please note that all fields are optional, but your participation will help us tailor the workshop experience to your needs. If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
