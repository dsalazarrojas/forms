# Mentor Meetup Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Mentor Meetup program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Participant Details** section with your information.
2. Provide **Event Details** if you are interested in participating in an event.
3. Enter your **Mentor Details** if you are interested in being a mentor.
4. Specify your **Participant Preferences** if you have any specific requirements or requests.
5. Read and agree to the **Participant Agreement** terms and conditions.
6. Add any additional **Participant Notes** if necessary.
7. Enter your **Participant Email** and **Participant Phone** for contact purposes.
8. Optionally, enter your **Participant Address** if you wish to provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: Provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Event Details**: Enter information about the event you are interested in participating in.
* **Mentor Details**: Enter information about your mentorship experience and skills.
* **Participant Preferences**: Specify any specific preferences or requirements you have for the mentorship program.
* **Participant Agreement**: Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your participation.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Participant Address**: Optionally, enter your address for contact purposes.
