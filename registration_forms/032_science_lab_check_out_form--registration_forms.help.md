# science_lab_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to digitally record the checkout of science lab equipment for tracking and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please enter your email address in the field labeled "User".
2. Select the name of the lab from the list provided.
3. Input the name of the equipment being checked out.
4. Enter the date you are starting to use the equipment.
5. Enter the date you expect to return the equipment.
6. Select the condition of the equipment from the options provided.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
8. Select the user you are assigning the equipment to from the list provided.
9. Select the status of the equipment from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User (Email)** (`user`, Optional): Please enter your email address.
* **Lab (Text)** (`lab_name`, Optional): Select the name of the lab you are checking equipment from.
* **Equipment (Text)** (`equipment_name`, Optional): Input the name of the equipment being checked out.
* **Start Date (Date)** (`start_date`, Optional): Enter the date you are starting to use the equipment.
* **End Date (Date)** (`end_date`, Optional): Enter the date you expect to return the equipment.
* **Equipment Condition (Select Multiple)** (`equipment_condition`, Optional): Select the condition of the equipment from the options "Good", "Bad", "Damaged".
* **Notes (Note)** (`notes`, Optional): Add any additional notes regarding the checkout of equipment.
* **Assigned to (Select One)** (`assigned_user`, Optional): Select the user you are assigning the equipment to from the list provided.
* **Status (Select One)** (`status`, Optional): Select the status of the equipment from the options "Checked out", "Checked in", "Available".
