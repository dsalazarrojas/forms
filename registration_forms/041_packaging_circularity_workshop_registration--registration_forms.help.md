# Packaging Circularity Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in attending the Packaging Circularity Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email, and any other relevant information in the "user input" field.
2. Specify your workshop preferences in the "workshop preferences" field.
3. Optionally, provide your contact details in the "contact details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant information.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, text, required): Specify your preferences for the workshop. Please be as detailed as possible so that we can better accommodate your needs.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact information for any additional context or follow-up.
