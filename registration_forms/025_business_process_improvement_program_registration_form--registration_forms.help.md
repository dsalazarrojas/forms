# Business Process Improvement Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Process Improvement Program Registration Form is intended to gather information from individuals interested in participating in the program to improve processes within the company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal information in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Choose the program level and focus area that interest you the most.
3. Describe your current process challenges and improvement goals.
4. Select your preferred learning format.
5. Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`3`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`4`, `email`, required): Enter your business email address.
* **Phone Number** (`5`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact number.
* **Company Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`8`, `text`, required): Enter your position within the company.
* **Department** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your department.
* **Direct Manager Name** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Program Track or Level** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Choose the program level that interests you: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Executive.
* **Improvement Focus Area** (`13`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the area of process improvement you are interested in: Operational Efficiency, Cost Reduction, Quality Management, Customer Experience, Supply Chain, Lean Management, or Six Sigma.
* **Current Process Challenges** (`15`, `text`, required): Describe the challenges you are facing with your current processes.
* **Improvement Goals** (`16`, `text`, required): Describe what you hope to achieve through the program.
* **Expected Benefits** (`17`, `text`, optional): Describe what benefits you anticipate from participating in the program.
* **Time Commitment Available per Week** (`19`, `select_one`, required): Choose how many hours you can dedicate to the program per week: Less than 5 hours, 5-10 hours, 10-15 hours, 15-20 hours, or 20+ hours.
* **Will Your Team Be Involved** (`20`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether your team will be involved in the program: Individual participation, Small team involved, or Whole department involved.
* **Organizational Support for Initiative** (`21`, `select_one`, optional): Describe the level of management support you have for the initiative: Full support, Partial support, Uncertain, or Limited support.
* **Preferred Learning Format** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you prefer to learn: In-Person Classroom, Online Self-Paced, Virtual Live Sessions, or Hybrid Approach.
* **Additional Information** (`24`, `text`, optional): Provide any other relevant information you feel is necessary for the program team to know.
* **Direct Manager Name** (`10`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your supervisor.
* **Department** (`9`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your department.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled in to ensure your registration is complete. If you have any questions, please reach out to the program team.
