# Art Exhibition Visitor Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about visitors to the art exhibition, helping us understand who our audience is, how they discovered the exhibition, and what they thought about their visit. This information will be used to improve future events and tailor our marketing strategies to better reach and engage with our visitors.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information. Make sure to answer all the required fields, as indicated by the red asterisks (\*) next to the label. You can select multiple options for some fields by holding down the Control key (\Ctrl) while clicking on the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Name** (`visitor_name`, text, *)
The **Name** field is where you can enter your name as a visitor. This field is required so we can address you personally.
### 2. **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, email, *)
The **Email Address** field is where you can enter your email address, which will help us stay in touch with you regarding future events and promotions.
### 3. **Zip - Postal Code** (`visitor_zip_code`, text, )
This field is optional but helps us understand the geographic distribution of our audience.
### 4. **Age Range** (`visitor_age_range`, select_one, )
Select your age range to help us understand the demographics of our visitors.
### 5. **What brought you to the exhibition today?** (`visit_reason`, select_multiple, *)
Choose the option(s) that best describes why you visited the exhibition.
### 6. **How would you rate your interest in the current exhibition theme?** (`exhibition_interest_rating`, select_one, *)
Select the option that best reflects your level of interest in the current exhibition theme.
### 7. **Overall satisfaction with your visit** (`visit_satisfaction_score`, number, *)
Rate your overall satisfaction with the exhibition on a scale of 1 to 5 stars.
### 8. **Any specific feedback or comments on the artworks?** (`feedback_comments`, text, )
Provide any specific feedback or comments you may have about the artworks on display.
### 9. **How did you hear about this exhibition?** (`hear_about_us`, select_one, *)
Select the option that best describes how you heard about the exhibition.
### 10. **Would you like to be added to our mailing list for future events?** (`mailing_list_opt_in`, select_one, *)
Indicate whether you would like to be added to our mailing list for future events.
### 11. **Are you interested in purchasing any of the works on display?** (`interest_in_buying`, select_one, )
Indicate whether you are interested in purchasing any of the artworks on display.
### 12. **If yes, which piece are you interested in?** (`specific_piece_interest`, text, )
Provide the title or description of the piece(s) you are interested in purchasing.
### 13. **Date of Visit** (`visit_date`, date, *)
Enter the date you visited the exhibition.

Note: The required fields are marked with a red asterisk (\*) next to the label. Please make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form successfully.
