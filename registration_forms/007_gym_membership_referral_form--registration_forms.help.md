# Gym Membership Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from current gym members who are referring friends or family to join the gym. The information collected will help the gym staff to process the referrals efficiently and provide a better service to both the referrer and the referee.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your basic contact information (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Provide the first and last name of the person you are referring.
3. Enter the email and phone number of the person you are referring.
4. Select how you know the person you are referring (e.g., Spouse, Partner, Friend, Family, etc.).
5. Choose how you know the person you are referring (e.g., Social Media, Online Search, Personal Recommendation, etc.).
6. Write a brief message explaining the referral.
7. If necessary, provide additional information about the referral source and how you know the person you are referring.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **First Name Referral** (`first_name_referral`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the person you are referring.
* **Last Name Referral** (`last_name_referral`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the person you are referring.
* **Email Referral** (`email_2`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person you are referring.
* **Referrer's First Name** (`first_name_referral`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the person who referred you.
* **Referrer's Last Name** (`last_name_referral`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the person who referred you.
* **Referrer's Email** (`email_2`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person who referred you.
* **Referrer's Phone** (`phone_2`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person who referred you.
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, date, optional): Enter the date you joined the gym.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, text, required): Enter your membership ID.
* **Name of Referee** (`name_of_referee`, text, required): Enter the name of the person you are referring.
* **Relationship to Referee** (`relationship_to_referee`, select_multiple, required): Select how you know the person you are referring (e.g., Spouse, Partner, Friend, Family, etc.).
* **How Do You Know Referee** (`how_do_you_know_referee`, select_multiple, required): Select how you know the person you are referring (e.g., Social Media, Online Search, Personal Recommendation, etc.).
* **Other** (`how_do_you_know_referee_other`, text, optional): Provide additional information about how you know the person you are referring.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Select where you met the person you are referring (e.g., Social Media, Online Search, Personal Recommendation, etc.).
* **Referral Source Other** (`referral_source_other`, text, optional): Provide additional information about where you met the person you are referring.
* **Referral Message** (`referral_message`, text, required): Write a brief message explaining the referral.
* **Referral Source Website** (`referral_source_website`, text, optional): Enter the website where you met the person you are referring.
* **Member Since** (`member_since`, date, optional): Enter the date you joined the gym.
* **Last Date Referral** (`last_date_referral`, date, optional): Enter the date the referral was made.
* **Member Since Other** (`member_since_other`, text, optional): Provide additional information about your membership period.
* **Member Since Other 2** (`member_since_other_2`, text, optional): Provide additional information about your membership period.
* **Date Joined Other** (`date_joined_other`, date, optional): Enter the date you joined the gym.
* **First Name Other** (`first_name_other`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the person who referred you.
* **Last Name Other** (`last_name_other`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the person who referred you.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask for help from the gym staff.
- The referral information will help the gym staff to process your referral efficiently and provide a better service to both you and the referee.
