# Cultural Festival Vendor Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Festival Vendor Intake Form is used to collect necessary information from potential vendors participating in a cultural festival. This form helps us understand your event's specifics, ensuring a smooth and successful experience for you and all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form

- Fill out this form once for each vendor and on behalf of the vendor itself or its authorized representative.
- Provide clear and concise answers to each question, using the space provided.
- Review your form before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name**: <Vendor Name> - Your festival or event name as it appears on the promotional materials and marketing.
*   **Vendor Description**: <Vendor Description> - Briefly describe your event, highlighting its unique aspects and features.
*   **Event Type**: <Select One> - Choose the type of event from the available options: Festival, Fair, Bazaar, Market, or Flea Market.
*   **Vendor Type**: <Select Multiple> - Select all the categories that apply to your event from the given options.
*   **Vendor Location**: <Vendor Location> - Enter the exact location of your event.
*   **Event Date**: <Event Date> - Provide the date of your event in the requested format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
*   **Event Time**: <Event Time> - Enter the start and end time of your event.
*   **Contact Name**: <Contact Name> - Enter the name of the person in charge of communication with the festival staff.
*   **Contact Phone**: <Contact Phone> - Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
*   **Contact Email**: <Contact Email> - Enter your contact email address for further inquiries and updates.

## Tips

- Please answer each question accurately and to the best of your abilities.
- Ensure all fields are filled out before submitting to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to reach out to us for help.
