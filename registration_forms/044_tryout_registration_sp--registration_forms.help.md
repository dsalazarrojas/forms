# Tryout Registration Sp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tryout Registration form is used to gather information about players for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tryout Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are an authorized representative and that the player is not already registered.
2. Click on the "Start" button to begin the registration process.
3. Fill out the form with the required information:
	* Player Name: Enter the name of the player.
	* Player Group: Select the player's group from the dropdown list. If no group is available, select "New Group".
	* Player Email: Enter the player's email address.
	* Player Phone: Enter the player's phone number.
	* Player Notes: Enter any relevant notes about the player.
	* Date: Enter the date of the tryout.
	* Time: Enter the time of the tryout.
	* Time 2: Enter the second time of the tryout (if applicable).
	* Date 2: Enter the second date of the tryout (if applicable).
	* Player Notes 2: Enter any additional notes about the player (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Name** (player_name, text, required: false): Enter the name of the player.
* **Player Group** (player_group, select_one, required: false): Select the player's group from the dropdown list. If no group is available, select "New Group".
* **Player Email** (player_email, email, required: false): Enter the player's email address.
* **Player Phone** (player_phone, text, required: false): Enter the player's phone number.
* **Player Notes** (player_notes, note, required: false): Enter any relevant notes about the player.
* **Date** (player_date, date, required: false): Enter the date of the tryout.
* **Time** (player_time, time, required: false): Enter the time of the tryout.
* **Time 2** (player_time_2, time, required: false): Enter the second time of the tryout (if applicable).
* **Date 2** (player_date_2, date, required: false): Enter the second date of the tryout (if applicable).
* **Player Notes 2** (player_notes_2, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the player (if applicable).
