# Gym Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Gym Membership Registration Form is a form used to collect information from users who want to register for a gym membership. This form is designed to gather necessary details such as user contact information, contact information, and billing details for processing membership payments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the user information fields.
2. Provide your contact information.
3. Enter your billing information.
4. Add any additional payment details if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (User Information)** (`User Information`, text, not required): 
  Enter your personal details such as your name, address, and any other relevant information.

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`Contact Information`, text, not required):
  Provide your contact details such as phone number, email, or any other preferred contact method.

* **Billing Information (Billing Information)** (`Billing Information`, text, not required): 
  Enter your billing address or any other relevant payment details.

* **Payment Details (Payment Details)** (`Payment Details`, text, not required):
  Add any additional payment information such as payment method, payment date, or payment amount.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are accurately filled with true and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out this form, contact form@gic.mx for assistance. 

Note: The form fields are not required, which means you can leave them blank, but please do so at your own discretion.
