# AI Predictive Modelling Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form collects information from attendees for the AI Predictive Modelling Workshop. Please provide your professional details, technical background, and preferences to help us tailor the content of the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all the required fields. Each field is marked as "required" and is essential for your registration.
2. Select the options that best describe your preferences and background.
3. Make sure to select only one option for each field that has multiple options.
4. Use the "Select All" or "Select None" options for fields with multiple select options.
5. If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, contact the workshop organizers for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Organization / Company** (`organization_company`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name if you are representing a company.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
* **Programming Language Proficiency** (`programming_skills`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the programming languages you are proficient in.
* **Previous Experience with Predictive Modelling** (`modeling_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of experience with predictive modelling.
* **Primary Industry for Application** (`industry_application`, `select_one`, required): Select the industry you intend to apply these models in.
* **Select Your Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `select_one`, required): Choose the workshop date you would like to attend.
* **Preferred Learning Format** (`learning_format_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred learning format.
* **Specific Topics of Interest** (`interest_topics`, `select_multiple`, required): Select up to two topics you are interested in.
* **Will you bring your own laptop?** (`hardware_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you will bring your own laptop or not.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Do you require a Certificate of Completion?** (`certificate_request`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you require a certificate or not.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of your registration.

Please make sure to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration experience.
