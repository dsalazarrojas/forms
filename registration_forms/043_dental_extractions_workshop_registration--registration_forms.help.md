# Dental Extractions Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees who wish to register for the Dental Extractions Workshop. It is used to determine which session attendees will attend and to gather their contact details for communication purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Select your preferred session by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each session.
2.  If you have any terms that you'd like to acknowledge, please enter them in the text area provided.
3.  Enter your attendee name in the text field.
4.  Enter your email address in the email field.
5.  Enter your phone number in the text field.
6.  Add any additional notes about your attendance, if necessary.
7.  Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session** (`1`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the session you would like to attend by choosing "Yes" or "No" for each session.
* **Signed Terms** (`2`, `note`, required: false): If you have any terms to acknowledge, please enter them in this text area. You can enter anything you like in this field.
* **Which Session** (`3`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose "Yes" or "No" for each session you're interested in attending.
* **Attendee Info** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of yourself as an attendee.
* **Name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or official documents.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required: false): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
* **Submit** (`9`, `text`, required: false): Click this button to complete your registration.
