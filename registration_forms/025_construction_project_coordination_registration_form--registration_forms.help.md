# Construction Project Coordination Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form gathers essential information for coordinating with participants on a construction project. It captures details such as contact information, role in the project, and preferences for coordination updates. This form ensures that all necessary contact details and permissions are collected for effective project coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Participant Identification details.
2. Provide your Full Name, Primary Email Address, and Primary Phone Number.
3. Select your role in the project from the options provided.
4. Enter your Employer or Company Name.
5. Choose the primary project assignment.
6. Select your preferred method of receiving coordination updates.
7. Indicate your regular availability for coordination meetings.
8. (Optional) If required software access is necessary, specify the software you need and confirm you have permission for it.
9. Specify your OSHA training level completed (if applicable).
10. Enter any Relevant Specialized Licenses or Certs.
11. (Optional) Add any Additional Coordination Requirements or Notes.
12. Enter the Date of Registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Participant**: Enter your full name for contact purposes.
* **Primary Email Address**: Enter your primary email address where coordination updates will be sent.
* **Primary Phone Number**: Enter your primary phone number for urgent updates.
* **Your Role in the Project**: Select your role in the project from the provided options.
* **Employer or Company Name**: Enter your employer or company name if different from your primary phone or email address.
* **Primary Project Assignment**: Enter the main project you are assigned to.
* **Preferred Method of Coordination Updates**: Choose how you want to receive updates (Email (Standard), SMS/Text Message (Urgent only), Project Portal/App, or Phone Call).
* **Regular Availability for Coordination Meetings**: Select all days you are available for coordination meetings.
* **Software Access Request**: Check software you require access to, if necessary (e.g., Procore, Bluebeam Revu, BIM 360/Autodesk Build, etc.).
* **I have authorized permission for this software access**: Confirm if you have permission to access the checked software.
* **OSHA Training Level Completed**: Specify your OSHA training level completed (None, OSHA 10-hour (Construction), OSHA 30-hour (Construction), OSHA 40-hour (HAZWOPER), or Other Specialized Safety Cert).
* **Relevant Specialized Licenses or Certs**: Enter any specialized licenses or certifications you hold (e.g., P.E., LEED AP, First Aid/CPR).
* **Additional Coordination Requirements or Notes**: Add any additional coordination requirements or notes if necessary.
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date you complete and submit this form.
