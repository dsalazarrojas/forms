# virtual-contest-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a virtual contest registration. It is intended for art, literature, or music contests where entrants can showcase their submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*) if applicable.
2. Enter your basic information such as first name, last name, email, and phone number.
3. Select your category of submission (art, literature, or music).
4. Fill in the details about your submission, including title, description, image, video, and audio file.
5. Enter your contact information, including name, email, phone number, website, and social media handle.
6. Select your category (art, literature, or music).
7. Review your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (5, select_one, required): Select your category (art, literature, or music).
* **Prize Amount** (6, number, required): Enter the prize amount for your submission.
* **Entry Title** (7, text, required): Enter a title for your submission.
* **Entry Description** (8, text, required): Enter a description for your submission.
* **Submit Date** (9, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your entry.
* **Submit Time** (10, time, required): Enter the time you submitted your entry.
* **Entry Image** (11, text, required): Enter the image file for your submission.
* **Entry Video** (12, text, required): Enter the video file for your submission.
* **Entry Audio** (13, text, required): Enter the audio file for your submission.
* **Submitter Name** (14, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Submitter Email** (15, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Submitter Website** (16, text, required): Enter your website.
* **Submitter Phone** (17, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Submitter Category** (18, select_one, required): Select your category (art, literature, or music).
* **Submitter Social Media Handle** (19, text, required): Enter your social media handle.
* **Submitter Bio** (20, text, required): Enter a brief bio about yourself.
* **Submitter Image** (21, text, required): Enter an image file for your profile.
* **Submitter Video** (22, text, required): Enter a video file for your profile.
* **Submitter Audio** (23, text, required): Enter an audio file for your profile.
* **Submitter Website** (24, text, required): Enter your website.
* **Submitter Social Media Handle Alt** (25, text, required): Enter an alternative social media handle.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with your submission.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the contest rules or guidelines for clarification.
- Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
