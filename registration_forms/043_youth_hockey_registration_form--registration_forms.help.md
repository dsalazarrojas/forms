# Youth Hockey Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Hockey Registration Form is used to register a youth hockey player. This form is intended to be completed by the parent or guardian of the player and is used to collect relevant player information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Date of Birth** fields with the player's details.
2. Enter the player's **Email** and confirm it by selecting **Confirm Email**.
3. Enter the player's **Phone** number.
4. Enter the emergency contact's **Name** and **Phone** number.
5. Enter the emergency contact's **Email**.
6. Select the player's **Player Position** from the available options.
7. Select the relationship between the player and the emergency contact.
8. Enter the **Team Name** (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter the player's first name.
- **Last Name**: Enter the player's last name.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter the player's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Email**: Enter the player's email address.
- **Confirm Email**: Confirm the player's email address to ensure it's correct.
- **Phone**: Enter the player's phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person who will act as the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
- **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the emergency contact's email address.
- **Player Position**: Select the player's position in the team from the available options (Forward, Defense, Goalie, Other).
- **Relationship**: Select the relationship between the player and the emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, etc.).
- **Team Name**: Enter the team name (if applicable).
