# Retail Sampling Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for vendors to register their information and samples for retail purposes. It's intended to gather necessary details about the vendor and their offering.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure all required fields are filled out. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your vendor name in the **Vendor Name** field.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and contact phone number, in the **Contact Info**, **Contact Name**, and **Contact Phone** fields.
4. Select your sample type from the **Sample Type** dropdown list.
5. Review your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (text, required): Enter your company or business name.
* **Contact Info** (email, required): Enter your primary contact email.
* **Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person or department responsible for contact.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the contact phone number.
* **Sample Type** (select multiple, optional): Select the type of samples you are offering (e.g., food, cosmetics, etc.).
