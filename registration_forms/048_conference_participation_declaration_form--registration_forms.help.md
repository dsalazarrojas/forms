# Conference Participation Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your conference participation, including your contact details, conference information, and participation type.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and the required information.
2. Fill out your contact details (Full Name, Email Address, and Organization).
3. Provide conference information (Conference Name, Conference Dates, and Conference Location).
4. Select your participation type (e.g., Attendee, Speaker, etc.).
5. Confirm your attendance (Will You Attend, Attendance Format, and Special Requirements).
6. Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
7. Agree to the conference's code of conduct and data processing policies.
8. Sign the declaration statement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participation Declaration** (`Participation Declaration`, 1): Declare your conference participation.
* **Declaration Date** (`Declaration Date`, 2): Provide the date of your declaration.
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, 3): Enter your details, such as job title and professional affiliation.
* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, 4): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, 5): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, 6): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, 7): Enter your company or institution.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, 8): Enter your job title.
* **Professional Affiliation** (`Professional Affiliation`, 9): Enter your association or body.
* **Conference Information** (`Conference Information`, 10): Enter event details.
* **Conference Name** (`Conference Name`, 11): Enter the conference name.
* **Conference Dates** (`Conference Dates`, 12): Enter the conference dates.
* **Conference Location** (`Conference Location`, 13): Enter the conference location.
* **Participation Type** (`Participation Type`, 14): Select your role at the conference (e.g., Attendee, Speaker, etc.).
* **Participation Details** (`Participation Details`, 15): Enter specific involvement details.
* **Session Title** (`Session Title`, 16): Enter the title of your session, if presenting.
* **Session Date** (`Session Date`, 17): Enter the date of your session, if presenting.
* **Session Time** (`Session Time`, 18): Enter the start time of your session, if presenting.
* **Co-Presenters** (`Co-Presenters`, 19): Enter the names of other presenters, if applicable.
* **Attendance Commitment** (`Attendance Commitment`, 20): Confirm your participation commitment.
* **Will You Attend** (`Will You Attend`, 21): Confirm if you will attend the conference.
* **Attendance Format** (`Attendance Format`, 22): Select how you will participate (e.g., In Person, Virtual, Hybrid).
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, 23): Enter any accessibility or other needs.
* **Audio Visual Requirements** (`Audio Visual Requirements`, 24): Enter any audio-visual requirements for your session.
* **Declaration Statement** (`Declaration Statement`, 25): Confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Accuracy Confirmation** (`Accuracy Confirmation`, 26): Confirm that the information provided is accurate.
* **Code of Conduct** (`Code of Conduct`, 27): Agree to follow the conference's code of conduct.
* **Data Consent** (`Data Consent`, 28): Agree to allow data processing.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, 29): Sign the declaration statement.

Tips:

* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure you provide all required information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers.
