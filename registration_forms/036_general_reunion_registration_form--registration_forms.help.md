# General Reunion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The General Reunion Registration Form is used to collect personal details for a general reunion event. This form is required for all attendees and should be filled out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to include all required fields.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which should be a valid email address.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Please use the standard date format, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY.
7. Enter your Time of Birth in the "Time of Birth" field. Please use the standard time format, e.g., 12:00 PM or 13:30.
8. Select your gender in the "Gender" field. You can select multiple options if applicable.
9. If you have any additional notes or comments, enter them in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your first name here. This field is required.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your last name here. This field is required.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address. This field is required.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This field is required.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format, e.g., MM/DD/YYYY. This field is required.
* **Time of Birth** (Time of Birth, time, required/optional): Enter your time of birth in the standard time format, e.g., 12:00 PM or 13:30. This field is required.
* **Gender** (Gender, select_multiple, required/optional): Select your gender. You can select multiple options if applicable. This field is required.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments you have. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use the standard date and time formats for the "Date of Birth" and "Time of Birth" fields.
* Be cautious when entering your email address and phone number, as they will be used to communicate with you regarding the reunion event.
