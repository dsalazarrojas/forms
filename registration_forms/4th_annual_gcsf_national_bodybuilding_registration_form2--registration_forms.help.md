# 50:50 Agreement Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 50:50 Agreement Survey is designed to collect feedback and opinions from respondents on the proposed 50:50 agreement. The survey aims to gather information on the respondent's understanding, perceptions, and concerns regarding the 50:50 arrangement, as well as their willingness to proceed under the current terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and context of the 50:50 agreement.
2. Fill out all required fields, which are marked as "required" in the form.
3. For optional fields, select or enter any information you feel is relevant or helpful.
4. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Respondent full name** (`respondent_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Organization / team (if applicable)** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are a member of an organization or team, enter the name of the organization or team. Leave blank if you are an individual respondent.
* **Role in the agreement decision** (`role_in_decision`, `text`, required): Describe your role in the 50:50 agreement decision, e.g., partner, manager, or stakeholder.
* **Date of response** (`survey_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are submitting this form.
* **I understand the proposed 50:50 split terms** (`understanding_of_terms`, `select_one`, required): Rate your understanding of the 50:50 agreement terms. Select one of the options: Strongly agree, Agree, Neutral, Disagree, or Strongly disagree.
* **Perceived fairness of the split (1-10)** (`perceived_fairness_rating`, `integer`, required): Rate the perceived fairness of the 50:50 arrangement on a scale of 1-10.
* **Primary concerns about the 50:50 arrangement (select all that apply)** (`primary_concerns`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any concerns you have about the 50:50 agreement.
* **Suggested adjustments or alternative proposals** (`suggested_adjustments`, `text`, optional): Enter any suggested adjustments or alternative proposals you have for the 50:50 agreement.
* **Would you be willing to proceed under the current 50:50 terms?** (`willingness_to_proceed`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the options: True, False, or Conditional on revisions.
* **Preferred compensation/equity handling (select all that apply)** (`compensation_or_equity_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any preferred compensation or equity handling options. Leave blank if not applicable.
* **Respondent email address** (`respondent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for follow-up.
* **Additional comments or context** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any extra information to help decision-makers.
