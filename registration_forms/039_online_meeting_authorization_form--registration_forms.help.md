# online_meeting_authorization_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_meeting_authorization_form is used for participants to provide their authorization and consent for online meetings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Participant Details** on page 1.
2. Provide **Meeting Info** on page 2.
3. Enter your **Email**, **Phone**, and **Meeting Date and Time**.
4. Select your **Participant Consent** and **Participant Consent Options**.
5. Review and confirm your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (page 1):
	+ **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Meeting Info** (page 1):
	+ **Meeting Info**: Enter a brief description of the meeting or event.
* **Email**:
	+ Email: Enter a valid email address you want to be contacted at for meeting-related correspondence.
* **Phone**:
	+ Phone: Enter a valid phone number for meeting-related contact.
* **Meeting Date**:
	+ Meeting Date: Enter the date of the meeting in **DDMM/YYYY** format.
* **Meeting Time**:
	+ Meeting Time: Enter the time of the meeting in **HH:MM** format.
* **Meeting Location**:
	+ Meeting Location: Enter a brief description of the meeting location or address.
* **Participant Consent**:
	+ Participant Consent: Select **Yes** to confirm you are authorized to participate.
* **Participant Consent Options**:
	+ Participant Consent Options: Select multiple options that apply to your participation.
* **Participant Consent Options**: 
	+ This field allows you to select multiple consent options.

## Tips
Please ensure you enter correct and accurate information to avoid any issues with meeting authorization. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the meeting organizers for clarification.
