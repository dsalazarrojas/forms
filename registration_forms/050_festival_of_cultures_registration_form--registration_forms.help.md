# Festival of Cultures Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration data from users for a festival of cultures event. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your registration is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** to register for the event.
2. If you have a **Phone**, please enter it for contact purposes.
3. Provide your **Date of Birth** for age verification.
4. Select your **Gender** from the options provided.
5. Choose the **Language Spoken** options that apply to you.
6. Enter your **Registration Date** to mark the date of your registration.
7. Select how frequently you visit the event, using the **Event Frequency** options.
8. Add any **Note** about yourself or your event participation.
9. **Confirm Email** to ensure we have your correct contact information.
10. **Password** is not a required field for this form and is intended for internal use only.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A required field to input your first name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name**: A required field to input your last name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: A required field to provide your contact email. Please enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: An optional field for your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: A required field to verify your age for event participation.
* **Gender**: A required field to select your gender from the provided options.
* **Language Spoken**: Select all languages you can speak, if applicable.
* **Registration Date**: A required field to mark the date of your registration.
* **Event Frequency**: Select how often you visit the festival of cultures event.
* **Note**: An optional field to add any relevant information about yourself or your participation in the event.
* **Confirm Email**: Ensure the email you input in the **Email** field is correct.
* **Password**: Not applicable for this form, please do not input any value in this field.
