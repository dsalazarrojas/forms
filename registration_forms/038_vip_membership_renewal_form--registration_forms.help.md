# VIP Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The VIP Membership Renewal Form is used for existing VIP members to renew their membership. This form allows members to update their information and select a new payment method for the upcoming membership period.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* customer_name
	* email
	* phone
	* renewal_date
2. Select a payment method:
	* Choose from the options provided (Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer)
	* Provide any additional information related to your selected payment method in the payment_method_note field
3. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### customer_name (Text, required)
* This field is for your name as a VIP member. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.

### email (Email, required)
* This field is for your email address associated with your VIP membership. Please enter the correct email address to ensure we can contact you about your membership.

### phone (Text, required)
* This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

### renewal_date (Date, required)
* This field is for the date you would like to renew your membership. Please select the date of the desired renewal.

### payment_method (Select One, required)
* Choose your preferred payment method for the upcoming membership period. Please select from the options provided: 
	+ Stripe: A digital payment method for secure and convenient payments.
	+ PayPal: A widely accepted online payment method.
	+ Bank Transfer: A secure payment method through your bank.

### payment_method_note (Note, optional)
* If you choose a payment method other than Stripe, you may provide additional information about your payment method in this field.
