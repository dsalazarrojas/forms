# IEP Services Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for IEP Services courses. It helps educational institutions or organizations collect information from participants about their details, preferences, and course submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read each section carefully and follow the instructions below.
- Ensure you have all required information before filling out the form.
- Fill in the form accurately and completely.
- Review your submission for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant details about yourself.
- **Participant Preferences** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Share your preferences or any additional information you think is necessary for your course experience.
- **Course Preferences** (`title`, `text`, required: false): Specify your course preferences, such as the course title, instructor, or other relevant details.
- **Submission** (`title`, `text`, required: false): This field is for any additional information or comments you have. 

## Tips
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
- Double-check your submission for accuracy and completeness.
- If you have any issues with form completion, try saving and then revisiting the form. 

Please note that the form's appearance and functionality may change based on the platform or integration used. This help guide will provide you with the essential information necessary to complete the form efficiently and accurately.
