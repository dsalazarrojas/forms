# Vacation Home Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from guests checking in to a vacation home, ensuring a smooth and efficient check-in experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details in the "User Input" field.
2. Provide your arrival information in the "Arrival Information" field.
3. Select your check-in status (Early Arrival, Late Arrival, or No Preference) from the dropdown options in the "check_in_status" field.
4. If you have any additional comments, you can type them in the "Additional Comments" field.
5. Finally, provide property information in the "Property Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): This field is for your personal details.
* **Arrival Information** (`arrival_info`, text, optional): Enter your arrival information here.
* **check_in_status** (`check_in_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your check-in status (Early Arrival, Late Arrival, or No Preference).
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments, you can type them in here.
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, text, optional): Provide any property information you think is relevant.
