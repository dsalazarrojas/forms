# Hypnobirthing Course Enrollment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are interested in enrolling in the Hypnobirthing course. The form aims to gather necessary details about the new student (if applicable) and their parent (if applicable), which will be used to process their enrollment and provide the best possible experience during the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on "Submit" to begin filling out the form. Each section represents a different part of the enrollment process.
2. Fill out each field with the requested information. Fields marked as "optional" can be left blank if not applicable.
3. Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Student** (`new_student`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the new student enrolling in the course.
	+ Please provide the student's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **New Parent** (`new_parent`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter the name of the new parent accompanying the student.
	+ Please provide the parent's name as it appears on their official documents.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, optional):
	+ This field is used to enter any additional information about the student, such as their address or contact details.
	+ Please provide any relevant details that may be required by the course administrators or instructors.

## Tips
- Ensure that all information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- Make sure to double-check the form for any errors before submitting.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the contact email provided at the top of this guide.
