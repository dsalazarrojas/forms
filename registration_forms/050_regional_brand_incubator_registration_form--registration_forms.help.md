# Regional Brand Incubator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Brand Incubator Registration Form is a tool designed to help potential incubators register their brand and provide essential information for the regional brand development process. This form is intended to gather necessary data from applicants, which will be used to support the development and growth of local brands in the region.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Start by reviewing the form's purpose and the fields that are required for a successful registration.
* Fill in the form's sections, one by one, providing accurate and complete information.
* Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure you have provided all necessary details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (Page 1)
* **General Information** (`general_info`, text, not required): This section is where you can provide any additional context or background information about your brand, such as a brief description of your business, your location, or any other relevant details.

### Applicant Details (Page 2)
* **Applicant Details** (`applicant_details`, text, not required): In this section, you can provide information about the person or team behind the brand, such as their name, email, and contact number.

### Contact Details (Page 3)
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, not required): This section is where you can provide contact information for your brand, such as a website, social media handles, or email address.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information in each section to ensure a successful registration.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for assistance.
