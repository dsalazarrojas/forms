# rsvp_for_homebuyer_discussion - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect attendance information and feedback from potential homebuyers. Please indicate if you plan to attend the discussion and provide any necessary contact information and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply select the relevant options from each field and enter any required information. Make sure to submit the form once you have filled out all required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Homebuyer Discussion** (select_one): Choose "Yes" if you plan to attend the homebuyer discussion, or "No" if you will not be attending.
* **Name** (text): Enter your name as you would like it to be recorded in our records.
* **Email** (email): Provide your email address so that we can contact you regarding the discussion or provide additional information.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you regarding the discussion or provide additional information.
* **Time** (time): Select the time at which you plan to arrive for the discussion.
* **Note** (note): Enter any additional notes or comments you would like to provide regarding your attendance or the discussion.
* **Attendees** (select_multiple): Choose "Yes" for each person who will be attending the discussion with you, or "No" if no one will be attending.
