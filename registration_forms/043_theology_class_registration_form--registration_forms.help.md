# Theology Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register students for a theology class at a seminary or religious institution. To register, please fill out this form with your required and preferred information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with your name, email, and student ID.
2. Choose your schedule preferences by selecting your preferred schedule, start date, and end date.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Confirm your enrollment status.
5. Confirm your start and end dates.
6. Choose your preferred time for the class.
7. Add any additional comments or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number assigned by the institution.
* **Course**: Select 'Yes' if you have registered for this course before; 'No' if this is your first time registering.
* **Preferred Schedule**: Choose a schedule that best suits your preferences (e.g., morning, afternoon, evening, or weekend).
* **Start Date**: Enter the date you plan to start attending the class in [YYYY-MM-DD format].
* **End Date**: Enter the date you plan to finish attending the class in [YYYY-MM-DD format].
* **Start Time**: Choose the time you prefer to start attending the class in [HH:MM format].
* **End Time**: Choose the time you prefer to end attending the class in [HH:MM format].
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number we can use to contact you for any updates or emergencies.
* **Confirm Phone**: Re-enter your phone number to ensure accuracy.
* **Course Preferences**: Provide any additional information about your course preferences (e.g., pace, topics).
* **Confirm Enrollment**: Confirm that you are enrolling in the class.
* **Preferred Time**: Choose a time that suits your schedule for attending the class.
* **Preferred Day**: Choose a day of the week that suits your schedule for attending the class.
* **Confirm Day**: Reconfirm your preferred day.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any other relevant comments or preferences you'd like to share with us.
* **Student Preferences**: Provide any specific preferences you have for the class (e.g., accommodations, pace).
* **Confirm Enrollment Status**: Confirm your enrollment status.
* **Confirm Start Date**: Reconfirm the start date of the class.
* **Confirm End Date**: Reconfirm the end date of the class.
* **Confirm Start Time**: Reconfirm the start time of the class.
* **Confirm End Time**: Reconfirm the end time of the class.
