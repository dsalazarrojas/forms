# Product Development Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide explains the Product Development Roundtable Registration Form. The form serves as a registration platform for attendees and speakers at product development roundtables, collecting crucial information to organize and plan the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure you have all necessary details ready.
2.  Review and complete each section of the form carefully.
3.  Enter required fields as specified.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Data** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter any general information about your registration.
*   **Address** (`text`, required: `false`): Provide your address for event organizers to communicate with you.
*   **Aroundtable Details** (`select_one`, required: `true`): Indicate if you have any special requests for the event.
*   **Special Requests** (`text`, required: `false`): Specify any unique requirements you may have.
*   **Dietary Preferences** (`select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select your dietary preferences for the event.
*   **Aroundtable Attendance** (`date`, required: `false`): Indicate the date you plan to attend the aroundtable.
*   **Aroundtable Start Time** (`time`, required: `false`): Set your expected start time for the aroundtable.
*   **Aroundtable End Time** (`time`, required: `false`): Enter the end time for your attendance at the aroundtable.
*   **Aroundtable Location** (`text`, required: `false`): Provide the location of the event for organizers.
*   **Payment Method** (`select_one`, required: `true`): Choose your payment method for the event fees.
*   **Comments** (`note`, required: `false`): Add any additional comments about your registration.
*   **Additional Comments** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter any extra information.
*   **Is Attending** (`select_one`, required: `true`): Confirm your attendance.
*   **Is Speaker** (`select_one`, required: `true`): Indicate if you are speaking at the event.
*   **Speaker Name** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as the speaker.
*   **Speaker Email** (`text`, required: `false`): Provide your email for communication.
*   **Speaker Title** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter your title or role at the event.
*   **Speaker Abstract** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter your speaker abstract for the event.
*   **Sponsor Request** (`text`, required: `false`): Request any sponsorships if needed.
*   **Dietary Restrictions** (`text`, required: `false`): Specify any dietary restrictions you have.
*   **Aroundtable Topic** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter the topic for your aroundtable session.
*   **Aroundtable Description** (`text`, required: `false`): Describe your aroundtable session.
*   **Aroundtable Tags** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter tags for your aroundtable session.
*   **Aroundtable Speaker** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as the speaker for the aroundtable.
*   **Submission Form Fields** (`note`, required: `false`): This field is a note for submission form fields.
