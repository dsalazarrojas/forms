# employee_casual_cook_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "employee_casual_cook_registration" form is designed to gather information from casual cooks who are interested in registering with the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Employee Title.
2. Enter your full name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Provide your years of experience in cooking.
5. Select the types of cuisines you are proficient in.
6. Choose the days you are available to work.
7. Select your preferred shifts.
8. Enter your start and end times for work.
9. Provide any additional notes about your availability.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Employee Title** (`employee_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title or position within the organization.
* **Enter Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name for reference purposes.
* **Enter Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address where you can be reached for work-related purposes.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working in the culinary industry.
* **Select Cuisines** (`cuisines`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the types of cuisines you are proficient in, such as American, Italian, Asian, Seafood, Mexican, Indian, or Other.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, select_one, optional): Select the days of the week you are available to work, such as Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday, or Sunday.
* **Shifts** (`availability_shifts`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the shifts you are available to work, such as Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekend.
* **Start Time** (`availability_start_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred start time for work.
* **End Time** (`availability_end_time`, time, optional): Enter your preferred end time for work.
* **Availability Notes** (`availability_notes`, text, optional): Provide any additional notes about your availability, such as limitations or special requests.
* **Specialties** (`cuisine_specialties`, text, optional): Enter any specialties you have, such as specific cooking techniques or cuisines.
