# Planetarium Friends Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Planetarium Friends Membership Form is a registration form designed for those interested in becoming a part of the Planetarium Friends community. By filling out this form, you can express your interest in joining our community and choose from various membership options to suit your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Please fill in your name and last name in the text fields "first_name" and "last_name".
2.  Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be our primary means of communication with you.
3.  If you want, you can also provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
4.  Choose your membership preference from the options "Planetarium Individual" or "Planetarium Family" in the "planetarium_membership_plan" field.
5.  Select the type of membership you are interested in by choosing from the options "Membership", "Donation", or "Gift" in the "planetarium_membership_type" field. Please note that this field is optional.
6.  If you are paying by credit or debit card, please enter the payment amount in the "payment_amount" field.
7.  Choose your preferred payment method from the options "Credit card" or "Debit card" in the "payment_method" field.
8.  Enter any additional information or notes about your payment in the "payment_notes" field.
9.  If you have any specific date and time preferences for your membership, you can enter them in the "date" and "time" fields. Please note that these fields are optional.
10.  Finally, please read and acknowledge our membership agreement by writing in the "agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **first_name** (text, required): Please fill in your first name.
*  **last_name** (text, required): Please fill in your last name.
*  **email** (email, required): Enter your email address for us to contact you.
*  **phone** (text, optional): If you want to provide your phone number for us to contact you, please enter it here.
*  **planetarium_membership_plan** (select_one, required): Please choose whether you are signing up as an individual or a family.
*  **planetarium_membership_type** (select_multiple, optional): Select the type of membership you are interested in. You can choose one or more options depending on your needs.
*  **payment_amount** (number, optional): If you are paying by credit or debit card, enter the amount you are paying.
*  **payment_method** (select_one, required): Please choose your preferred payment method.
*  **payment_notes** (text, optional): Enter any additional information about your payment.
*  **agreement** (text, optional): Please read and acknowledge our membership agreement by writing your name and date in this field.
*  **date** (date, optional): If you have any specific date preferences for your membership, you can enter them here.
*  **time** (time, optional): If you have any specific time preferences for your membership, you can enter them here.
*  **planetarium_membership_agreement** (note, optional): This is a note field for you to express your understanding of our membership agreement. Please read the agreement carefully and write in your name and date to acknowledge your understanding.
