# AI Driven Decision Strategy Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the AI-Driven Decision Strategy Workshop. The form is designed to collect information about you and your interests, so we can tailor the workshop content and make it more valuable for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form

Before starting, make sure you have the following:

*   A stable internet connection
*   Access to a computer or a mobile device

### How to Complete the Form:

1.  Click on the "Start Form" button to begin the registration process.
2.  Fill out the required fields (marked with an asterisk \*): Full name, Email address, Current job title, Company or organization name, Industry sector, Experience level with AI decision-making tools, Primary workshop goals, Preferred payment method, and Expected attendance days.
3.  For fields that are not required, feel free to fill them out if you have information available.
4.  For the "How many days do you expect to attend?" field, select the number of days you plan to attend the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name (\*):** Please enter your legal name as it appears on your identification.
*   **Email address (\*):** We'll use this for registration confirmation and workshop updates.
*   **Phone number:** Optional - for urgent workshop communications.
*   **Current job title (\*):** e.g. Manager, Director, Analyst, Executive.
*   **Company or organization name (\*):** Please enter your organization's name.
*   **Industry sector (\*):** Select the sector most relevant to your work.
*   **Experience level with AI decision-making tools (\*):** Select your level of experience with AI decision-making tools.
*   **What are your primary goals for attending this workshop (\*):** Select all that apply:
    \- Learn AI-driven decision frameworks
    \- Improve strategic planning skills
    \- Network with industry peers
    \- Implement AI tools in my organization
    \- Understand ethical implications of AI decisions
    \- Enhance data analysis capabilities
*   **Date of birth:** For demographic purposes only.
*   **Dietary restrictions or allergies:** Please specify any dietary needs for workshop meals.
*   **Emergency contact name:** Optional - for emergency contact information.
*   **Emergency contact phone:** Optional - for emergency contact phone number.
*   **How did you hear about this workshop (\*):** Select how you found out about the workshop.
*   **How many days do you expect to attend (\*):** Select the number of days you plan to attend the workshop.
*   **Any special requirements or accommodations?:** Optional - for workshop arrangements.
*   **Payment Information---:** 
*   **Preferred payment method (\*):** Select your preferred payment method.
*   **Billing address:** If different from mailing address.
*   **Additional comments or notes:** Any other information you'd like to share with us.

## Tips
