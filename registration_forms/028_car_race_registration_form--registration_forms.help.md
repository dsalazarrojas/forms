# Car Race Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Car Race Registration Form is a required document for all participants to register for a car race. Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and safe racing experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Read through each section carefully and fill in the required information.
2.  Ensure you provide accurate information about yourself, your vehicle, and any emergency contact.
3.  Review the racing class selection and emergency contact information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (Required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth to verify your age and eligibility to participate in the race.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your primary email address for registration confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your primary phone number for the event organizers to contact you.
* **Driver's License Number** (Required): Enter your valid state-issued driver's license number.
* **Racing Experience** (Optional): If you have any racing experience, please provide the number of years you've been racing.
* **Racing License Held** (Required): Select whether you hold a professional racing license or not.
* **Car Make and Model** (Required): Describe the car you will be racing, including its make and model.
* **Vehicle Year** (Required): Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (Optional): If available, please provide the 17-character VIN code of your vehicle.
* **Desired Racing Class** (Required): Select the class you wish to compete in (e.g., Street Stock, GT Class, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Full Name** (Required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **I Acknowledge the Car Race Registration Terms and Safety Regulations** (Required): Confirm that you've read and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Type Your Name for Race Registration Confirmation** (Required): Sign your name to confirm your registration.

Please ensure you fill out all required fields and double-check your information before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the event organizers for support.
