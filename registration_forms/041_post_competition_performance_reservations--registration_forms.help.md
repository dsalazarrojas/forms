# Post Competition Performance Reservations - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from artists for post-competition performance reservations. This helps us keep track of upcoming events and shows.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required and optional fields as needed.
2. Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Performance Date** (`reservation_date`): Enter the date you would like to perform.
* **Artist Name** (`artist_name`): Enter your name as it should be displayed for the booking.
* **Artist Email** (`artist_email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Artist Phone** (`artist_phone`): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Show Name** (`show_name`): Enter the name of the show or event you are interested in performing at.
* **Time** (`reservation_time`): Enter the time you are available to perform (format: `HH:MM`).
* **Show Details** (`show_details`): Enter any special requests or details about your performance.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event or show (if known).
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event or show (if known).
* **Event Details** (`event_details`): Enter any additional information about the event or show.
* **Performance Time** (`performance_time`): Enter your performance time (format: `HH:MM`).
