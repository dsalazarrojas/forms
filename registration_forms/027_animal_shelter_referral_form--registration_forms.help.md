# Animal Shelter Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Animal Shelter Referral Form is designed for individuals or organizations to refer animals to an animal shelter for adoption or rescue. This form provides crucial information about the animal, allowing the shelter to effectively care for and find a new home for the animal.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately, providing as much information as you can about the animal.
2. Make sure to select the correct options from the dropdown menus.
3. If you're unsure about any information, please provide as much detail as you can.
4. Attach any relevant photos or documents to support your referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Referrer Full Name**: The name of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Email**: The contact email of the person making the referral.
* **Referrer Phone**: The contact phone number of the person making the referral.
* **Your Relationship to Animal**: The relationship you have with the animal (e.g., current owner, finder, shelter staff, etc.).
* **Referral Date**: The date the animal was referred to the shelter.
* **Animal Name**: The name of the animal being referred.
* **Species**: The species of the animal (e.g., dog, cat, rabbit, etc.).
* **Breed**: The breed of the animal (if known).
* **Estimated Age**: The estimated age of the animal in years or months.
* **Sex**: The sex of the animal (male, female, or unknown).
* **Color and Markings**: A physical description of the animal's color and any distinguishing markings.
* **Estimated Weight**: The estimated weight of the animal.
* **Reason for Referral**: The reason the animal is being referred (e.g., owner surrender, stray, medical needs, etc.).
* **Additional Details**: Any additional information about the animal or situation.
* **Known Health Status**: The current health status of the animal (e.g., healthy, minor issues, needs medical attention, etc.).
* **Health Details**: A description of any known health conditions.
* **Known Behavior Status**: The current behavior status of the animal (e.g., friendly and social, shy or nervous, etc.).
* **Behavior Details**: A description of the animal's temperament and any behavioral concerns.
* **Microchip Status**: Whether the animal has a microchip and if so, the number (if known).
* **Microchip Number**: If the animal has a microchip, provide the number (if known).
* **Vaccination Status**: The vaccination status of the animal (e.g., up to date, partially vaccinated, not vaccinated, etc.).
* **Spay or Neuter Status**: Whether the animal is spayed or neutered (if known).
* **Urgency Level**: How soon the animal needs placement (e.g., immediate, urgent, soon, flexible, etc.).
* **Current Location**: The current location of the animal (e.g., address or description).
* **Do You Have Photos of the Animal**: Whether you have photos of the animal and if so, are you willing to share them.
* **Owner Contact Information**: The contact information for the animal's owner (if different from the referrer).
* **Additional Information**: Any other information you believe is relevant to the animal's situation.

Note: Please be as accurate and complete as possible when filling out this form. The shelter will use this information to provide the best care and find a suitable home for the animal. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at [insert contact information].
