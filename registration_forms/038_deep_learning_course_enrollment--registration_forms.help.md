# Deep Learning Course Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Deep Learning Course Enrollment form is a registration form designed to collect user information for enrolling in a deep learning course. This form is meant to gather essential details from users for course management and administration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Name** in the first field. This is a required field where you need to provide your name as you want it to be displayed in the course records.
2. Enter your **Email** address in the second field. This is also a required field and will be used for communication with the course administrators.
3. Enter your **Phone** number in the third field. This field is required and will be used for contacting you regarding the course.
4. In the fourth field, select the title of the course you want to enroll in. This is a required field and will help course administrators track the course registrations.
5. Choose your **Cohort** from the dropdown options in the fifth field. This is a required field and will help group users in the same course.
6. (Optional) If you have a specific **Start Date** in mind, you can enter it in the sixth field. This field is not required but will help course administrators plan the course schedule.
7. Select the **End Date** of the course in the seventh field. This is a required field and will help course administrators track the course duration.
8. Choose your **Status** from the dropdown options in the eighth field. This is a required field and will help course administrators track the course progress.
9. (Optional) If you have any **Comments** regarding your enrollment, you can enter them in the ninth field. This field is not required but will help course administrators understand your needs.
10. Enter the **Review Date** of your application in the tenth field. This is a required field and will help course administrators track the review timeline.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (1, text, required): Enter your name as you want it to be displayed in the course records.
* **Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address for course communication.
* **Phone** (3, text, required): Enter your phone number for course contact.
* **Course Title** (4, text, required): Select the title of the course you want to enroll in.
* **Cohort** (5, select_one, required): Choose your cohort from the available options.
* **Start Date** (6, date, optional): Enter the start date of the course if you have a specific date in mind.
* **End Date** (7, date, required): Select the end date of the course.
* **Status** (8, select_one, required): Choose your status from the available options.
* **Comments** (9, note, optional): Enter any comments regarding your enrollment if you have any.
* **Review Date** (10, date, required): Enter the review date of your application.
