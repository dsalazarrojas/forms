# Worker Skills Validation Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for workers to register themselves and provide information about their skills and work status for the Worker Skills Validation Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your worker information on the first page.
2. On the second page, answer the questions about your skills.
3. On the third page, review and submit your registration information.
4. On the fourth page, review your registration status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Worker Information (page 1)**: Enter your worker name, ID, and email address.
  - This is the first page of the form, where you will enter your basic worker information.
  - Your name, ID, and email address will be collected for registration purposes.
- **Worker Skills (page 2)**: Answer the questions about your skills.
  - This page will ask if you have certain skills.
  - Answer 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
- **Submit (page 3)**: Review and submit your registration information.
  - This is the last page before submitting your registration.
  - Review your information before submission.
- **Registration Information (page 4)**: Review your registration information.
  - This page will display the information you have submitted.
  - Check for accuracy before moving on.
- **Registration Status (page 5)**: Review your registration status.
  - This page will show the result of your submission.
  - You will be able to see if your registration was successful.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be accurate when answering questions.
- Review your submission carefully before submitting.

This form is intended to be completed by workers, so please be honest and accurate when answering questions.
