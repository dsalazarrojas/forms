# Surprise Birthday Invitation - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Surprise Birthday Invitation form is designed to collect essential details about a birthday celebration, including the celebration details, guest information, RSVP status, and birthday wishes. This form helps the birthday person (or their friends and family) to create a personalized invitation for the special event. It is meant for people to provide their information, RSVP, and share birthday wishes with the birthday person.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide Birthday Celebration Details**: Fill in the text box on page 1 with your thoughts, ideas, and plans for the birthday celebration.
2. **Add Guest Information**: On page 2, select all the guests you want to invite by choosing "Option 1" or "Option 2" from the dropdown menu.
3. **RSVP Status**: On page 3, select your RSVP status by choosing "Option 1" or "Option 2" from the dropdown menu.
4. (Optional) **Birthday Wishes**: On page 4, write down your birthday wishes for the birthday person in the text box.
5. **Celebration Date**: On page 5, select the date of the birthday celebration.
6. **Celebration Time**: On page 6, select the time of the birthday celebration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Birthday Celebration Details (Text, Optional)**: This field is a text box where you can write any ideas, thoughts, or plans you have for the birthday celebration. You can include details such as location, theme, activities, or dress code.
* **Guest Details (Select Multiple, Required)**: This field allows you to select all the guests you want to invite to the celebration. You can choose "Option 1" or "Option 2" from the dropdown menu.
* **RSVP Status (Select One, Required)**: This field is for you to inform us whether you can attend the birthday celebration or not. You can choose "Option 1" or "Option 2" from the dropdown menu.
* **Birthday Wishes (Note, Optional)**: This is a text box where you can write down your birthday wishes for the birthday person.
* **Celebration Date (Date, Optional)**: This field is for you to select the date of the birthday celebration.
* **Celebration Time (Time, Optional)**: This field is for you to select the time of the birthday celebration.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure the birthday person receives the correct information.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always reach out to us for clarification.
* Please keep your responses concise and clear to ensure the birthday person receives the correct information.
