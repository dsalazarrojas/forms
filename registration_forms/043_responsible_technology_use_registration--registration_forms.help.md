# Responsible Technology Use Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a participant's details for a specific technology program. It collects information such as form data, participant info, program details, consent, submission info, contact info, and submission creation details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for each field as prompted.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Data** (`form_data`): Enter the relevant data for the program or project.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`): Provide information about the participant, such as name, ID, or other relevant details.
* **Program Details** (`program_details`): Enter information about the program or project, such as name, description, or dates.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Check the box to indicate your consent for the program or submission.
* **Submission Info** (`submission_info`): Enter information about the submission, such as title, file, or notes.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`): Enter contact information, such as email or phone number.
* **Submission Created By** (`created_by`): Enter the name of the person who created the submission.
* **Submission Created At** (`created_at`): Enter the date and time when the submission was created.
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* Make sure to ask for help if you are unsure about any field or information.
* Review and update your submission as needed after initial submission.
