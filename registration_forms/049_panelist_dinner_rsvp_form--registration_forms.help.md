# Panelist Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panelist Dinner RSVP Form is used to gather information from panelists about their availability and preferences for a dinner event. This form is used by hosts to collect and manage RSVPs from panelists.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your RSVP status (Yes or No).
2. Choose your dietary needs.
3. Enter any special dietary needs you have.
4. Add any additional comments.
5. Select your availability for the dinner event.
6. Choose your preference for the dinner event (Option 1 or Option 2).
7. Select your guest's dietary needs (if applicable).
8. Enter your guest's name (if applicable).
9. Enter your guest's email (if applicable).
10. Enter your guest's phone number (if applicable).
11. Describe your guest's relationship to you (if applicable).
12. Enter your host information (if you are not already the host).
13. Add any RSVP comments.
14. Select your RSVP status (if already filled out in step 1).
15. Add any RSVP comments.
16. Add any host comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Panelist RSVP** (panelist_dinner_rsvp): Select whether you will be attending the dinner event (Yes or No).
* **Dietary Needs** (dietary_needs): Choose your dietary needs from the provided options.
* **Special Dietary Needs** (special_dietary_needs): Enter any special dietary needs you have.
* **Additional Comments** (dietary_comments): Add any additional comments about your dietary needs.
* **Panelist Availability** (panelist_availability): Select your availability for the dinner event.
* **Panelist Time** (panelist_time): Choose your preferred time for the dinner event.
* **Panelist Dinner** (panelist_dinner): Choose your preference for the dinner event (Option 1 or Option 2).
* **Guest Dietary Needs** (guest_dietary_needs): Select your guest's dietary needs (if applicable).
* **Guest Name** (guest_name): Enter your guest's name (if applicable).
* **Guest Email** (guest_email): Enter your guest's email (if applicable).
* **Guest Phone** (guest_phone): Enter your guest's phone number (if applicable).
* **Guest Relationship** (guest_relationship): Describe your guest's relationship to you (if applicable).
* **Host** (host): Enter your host information (if you are not already the host).
* **RSVP Comments** (rsvp_comments): Add any comments about your RSVP.
* **RSVP Status** (rsvp_status): Select your RSVP status (if already filled out in step 1).
* **RSVP Status Comments** (rsvp_status_comments): Add any comments about your RSVP status.
* **Host Comments** (host_comments): Add any comments from the host.
* **Panelist Comments** (panelist_comments): Add any comments from the panelist.
* **Panelist Name** (panelist_name): Enter your panelist name.
* **Panelist Email** (panelist_email): Enter your panelist email.
* **Panelist Phone** (panelist_phone): Enter your panelist phone number.

Note: Some fields are optional, and their corresponding explanations will be provided in parentheses.
