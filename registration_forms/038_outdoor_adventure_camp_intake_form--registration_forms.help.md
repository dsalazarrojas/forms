# Outdoor Adventure Camp Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who wish to join an outdoor adventure camp. The form will be used to gather details about each participant, including their emergency contact, medical information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have answered all required fields.
2. Provide accurate and truthful information about yourself and your emergency contact.
3. If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please select the relevant option in the "Medical Condition" field.
4. Add any additional notes or comments about yourself in the "Participant Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info (Page 1)**: Enter your name, address, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 1)**: Please provide emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Medical Info (Page 1)**: Enter any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Details (Page 2)**: Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact (e.g. Mother, Father, Spouse, etc.).
* **Participant Medical Info (Page 2)**: Enter any additional medical information about yourself.
* **Medical Condition (Page 2)**: Select any relevant medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Participant Notes (Page 2)**: Add any additional notes or comments about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Page 3)**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (Page 3)**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact E-mail (Page 3)**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have provided accurate and truthful information about yourself and your emergency contact.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the outdoor adventure camp staff.
* If you need to add or remove any fields, please let the staff know in advance.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure that you have answered all required fields.
