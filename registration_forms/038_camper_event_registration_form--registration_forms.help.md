# Camper Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect camper data for event registration, allowing us to gather essential information to ensure their safety and participation. Please review each field to ensure you complete it accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Review each field carefully and complete it according to the instructions provided.
2.  Enter your camper's first name, last name, and date of birth.
3.  Select the correct gender for your camper.
4.  Provide your full name as the parent or guardian.
5.  Enter your email address for contact purposes.
6.  Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
7.  Enter your camper's home address.
8.  Enter the city and state where your camper resides.
9.  Enter your camper's ZIP code.
10. Enter the event name that your camper will be attending.
11. Select the date of the event.
12. Enter the event location.
13. Select the type of event your camper will be participating in.
14. List any medical conditions your camper has.
15. List any allergies your camper has.
16. Select any dietary restrictions your camper has.
17. If applicable, list any current medications your camper is taking.
18. Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
19. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
20. Indicate the relationship between your camper and the emergency contact person.
21. Enter the name of your camper's primary physician.
22. Enter the phone number of your camper's primary physician.
23. If applicable, enter the name of your camper's insurance provider.
24. If applicable, enter the policy or member ID for your camper's insurance.
25. Select if transportation is needed for your camper.
26. Confirm that you have read and accept the permission slip.
27. Confirm that you accept the liability waiver.
28. Confirm that you have given permission for your camper's photo to be taken.
29. (If applicable) Enter a registration fee for your camper.
30. Select the payment method for your camper's registration fee.
31. Enter any additional comments about your camper.
32. Sign your full name to confirm you have reviewed and completed this form accurately and completely.
33. Enter the date you signed this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **camper_first_name** (Required/Text): Enter your camper's first name.
*   **camper_last_name** (Required/Text): Enter your camper's last name.
*   **date_of_birth** (Required/Date): Enter your camper's birth date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **age** (Required/Number): Enter your camper's age.
*   **gender** (Required/Select One): Select your camper's gender.
*   **parent_guardian_name** (Required/Text): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
*   **parent_guardian_email** (Required/Email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
*   **parent_guardian_phone** (Required/Text): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
*   **home_address** (Required/Text): Enter your camper's home address.
*   **city** (Required/Text): Enter the city or town your camper resides in.
*   **state** (Required/Text): Enter the state or province your camper resides in.
*   **zip_code** (Required/Text): Enter your camper's ZIP code.
*   **event_name** (Required/Text): Enter the event name your camper will be attending.
*   **event_date** (Required/Date): Enter the event date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
*   **event_location** (Required/Text): Enter the event location.
*   **event_type** (Required/Select One): Select the type of event your camper will be participating in.
*   **medical_conditions** (Required/Text): List any medical conditions your camper has.
*   **allergies** (Required/Text): List any allergies your camper has.
*   **dietary_restrictions** (Required/Select Multiple): Select any dietary restrictions your camper has.
*   **current_medications** (Optional/Text): If applicable, list any current medications your camper is taking.
*   **emergency_contact_name** (Required/Text): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
*   **emergency_contact_phone** (Required/Text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
*   **emergency_contact_relationship** (Required/Text): Indicate the relationship between your camper and the emergency contact person.
*   **physician_name** (Required/Text): Enter the name of your camper's primary physician.
*   **physician_phone** (Required/Text): Enter the phone number of your camper's primary physician.
*   **insurance_provider** (Optional/Text): If applicable, enter the name of your camper's insurance provider.
*   **insurance_policy_number** (Optional/Text): If applicable, enter the policy or member ID for your camper's insurance.
*   **transportation_needed** (Required/Select One): Select if transportation is needed for your camper.
*   **permission_slip** (Required/Select One): Confirm that you have read and accept the permission slip.
*   **liability_waiver** (Required/Select One): Confirm that you accept the liability waiver.
*   **photo_release** (Required/Select One): Confirm that you give permission for your camper's photo to be taken.
*   **registration_fee** (Optional/Number): If applicable, enter a registration fee for your camper.
*   **payment_method** (Optional/Select One): Select the payment method for your camper's registration fee.
*   **additional_comments** (Optional/Text): Enter any additional comments about your camper.
*   **parent_signature** (Required/Text): Sign your full name to confirm you have reviewed and completed this form accurately and completely.
*   **signature_date** (Required/Date): Enter the date you signed this form.

## Tips

*   Be sure to review each field carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
*   If a field is not applicable, do not enter anything.
*   Ensure you sign the form with your full name and date.
