# Annual General Meeting Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for reserving attendance at the Annual General Meeting. Please complete it carefully to ensure your reservation is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information for each field. Required fields are marked with an asterisk (\*). Make sure to review the help text for each field for more details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, \*): Enter your full name, including first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, \*): Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your best contact number.
* **Membership ID** (`membership_id`, text, optional): Enter your member identification number, if applicable.
* **Organization or Company** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization name.
* **Meeting Year** (`meeting_year`, select_one): Select the year of the AGM you are reserving for. Options are 2026, 2025, 2024, 2023.
* **Reservation Type** (`reservation_type`, select_one): Choose the type of reservation you require. Options are Individual Seat, Table for 8, Table for 10, Virtual Access, and Standing Room.
* **Number of Seats** (`number_of_seats`, number, \*): Enter the total number of seats needed.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose where you would like to sit. Options are No Preference, Front Section, Middle Section, Back Section, Near Exit, and Accessible Seating.
* **Table Companions** (`table_companions`, text, optional): Enter the names of people at your table.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple): Select all dietary restrictions that apply. Options are None, Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Nut Allergies, Shellfish Allergies, Kosher, Halal, and Other.
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional dietary details or allergies.
* **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, select_one): Choose your preferred meal. Options are Beef, Chicken, Fish, Vegetarian, Vegan, and No Meal Required.
* **Beverage Preference** (`beverage_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred drink. Options are Full Bar, Wine and Beer, Non-Alcoholic Only, and No Preference.
* **Materials Needed** (`materials_needed`, select_multiple): Select any materials you require. Options are Printed Agenda, Financial Report, Name Badge, Voting Card, Digital Only, and None.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): Enter any special accessibility needs or requests.
* **Parking Needed** (`parking_needed`, select_one): Select whether you will need parking. Options are True or False.
* **Expected Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter your expected arrival time.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one): Choose how you will pay for the reservation, if fees apply. Options are No Fee Required, Credit Card, Invoice, Check, and Included in Membership.
* **Reservation Fee** (`reservation_fee`, number, optional): Enter the amount due for the reservation, if applicable.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, select_one): Agree to the cancellation policy. Options are Yes, I Agree, and False.
* **Confirmation Method** (`confirmation_method`, select_one): Choose how you should be confirmed. Options are Email, Phone, Text Message, and Mail.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any other needs or requests.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any other information about your reservation.

Please review the form carefully and provide accurate information to ensure a smooth reservation process. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
