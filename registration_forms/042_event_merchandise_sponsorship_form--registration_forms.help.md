# Event Merchandise Sponsorship Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Merchandise Sponsorship Form is a tool used by event organizers to collect information from potential event sponsors. The form is designed to be completed by sponsors to provide event organizers with the necessary details to review and consider their sponsorship request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the event you are sponsoring in the **Event Name** field.
2. Enter your name in the **Sponsor Name** field.
3. Enter your organization's name in the **Organization** field.
4. Enter your address in the **Address** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
6. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
7. Select the date of the event in the **Event Date** field.
8. Select the start time of the event in the **Start Time** field.
9. Select the end time of the event in the **End Time** field.
10. Enter a message for the event organizers in the **Sponsor Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event you are sponsoring.
  This field should be filled with the official name of the event as stated on promotional materials and event listings.
- **Sponsor Name** (text, required): Enter your name as the sponsor of the event.
  This field should be filled with your name as you would like it to be displayed on event materials.
- **Organization** (text, optional): Enter your organization's name (if you are sponsoring on behalf of a company or institution).
  This field is optional and should only be filled if you are representing an organization.
- **Address** (text, optional): Enter your address.
  This field is optional and can be used to provide additional contact information.
- **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
  This field is required and should be filled with your active email address.
- **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  This field is optional and can be used to provide additional contact information.
- **Event Date** (date, required): Select the date of the event.
  This field is required and should be filled with the date of the event.
- **Start Time** (time, required): Select the start time of the event.
  This field is required and should be filled with the start time of the event.
- **End Time** (time, required): Select the end time of the event.
  This field is required and should be filled with the end time of the event.
- **Sponsor Message** (text, optional): Enter a message for the event organizers.
  This field is optional and can be used to provide additional information about your sponsorship.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
- Use the format specified for date and time fields.
- Review your form before submitting it to ensure that all information is up-to-date and complete.
