# Corporate Team Event Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Team Event Attendance Form is designed for corporate teams to track and manage event attendance, RSVPs, and other essential details. This form helps team organizers keep track of who's attending, their preferences, and other relevant information for event planning and follow-up purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand what information is being requested.
2. Fill out the form with the correct information for the event you're submitting for.
3. Provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
4. Double-check your responses before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Please provide the event name, date, time, and location. You can also add comments or notes about the event if you have any.
* **Team Details**: Enter information about your team, such as team members' attendance and roles within the team.
* **RSVP Information**: Indicate whether you can attend or not by selecting the correct option. If you can attend, please provide your name, email, and phone number for contact purposes.
* **Attendees**: List all team members who will be attending the event by selecting their names from the options provided.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event. This is the date of the event, and please ensure it matches the event details above.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event. This is the time of the event, and please ensure it matches the event details above.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event. This is the time when the event is expected to start.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event. This is the time when the event is expected to finish.
* **List of Attendees**: Provide a list of all team members who will be attending the event. You can enter multiple attendees by selecting their names from the options provided.
* **RSVP**: Indicate whether you have RSVPed to the event by selecting the correct option.
* **Attendees Names**: List the names of all team members who will be attending the event.
* **Attendees Emails**: Enter the email addresses of all team members who will be attending the event.
* **Attendees Phones**: Enter the phone numbers of all team members who will be attending the event.
* **Attendees Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the attendees.
* **Event Details Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Event Details Date**: Enter the date of the event. This is the date of the event, and please ensure it matches the event details above.
* **Event Details Time**: Enter the time of the event. This is the time of the event, and please ensure it matches the event details above.
* **Event Details Location**: Enter the location of the event. This is the location of the event, and please ensure it matches the event details above.
* **Event Details Date 2**: This field is a duplicate of the Event Details Date field, and you can ignore it.
* **Event Details Time 2**: This field is a duplicate of the Event Details Time field, and you can ignore it.
* **Attendees Details**: Add any additional details about the attendees.
* **Attendees Notes**: Add any additional notes about the attendees.

Note: The fields marked with "2" at the end can be ignored, as they are duplicates.
