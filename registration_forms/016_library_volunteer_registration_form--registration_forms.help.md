# Library Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register as a volunteer at the library. Please fill in your details and preferences to help us schedule you for volunteer shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields. You will need to provide the following:
* Your name and contact information (First Name and Last Name)
* Your email and phone number
* Your availability for volunteering (if applicable)
* Your preferred volunteer days and hours

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Please enter your first name as it is listed on your identification.
* **Last Name** (Required): Please enter your last name as it is listed on your identification.
* **Email** (Required): Please enter a valid email address where we can contact you about volunteer shifts.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Please enter your phone number where we can contact you about volunteer shifts.
* **Availability** (Optional): If you have a specific availability for volunteering, please select one or more of the provided options (Volunteer Days, Volunteer Hours, or Other).
* **Volunteer Days** (Required): Select one or more days when you are available to volunteer:
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Saturday
	+ Sunday
* **Volunteer Hours** (Required): Select one or more hour blocks when you are available to volunteer:
	+ 1 hour
	+ 2 hours
	+ 3 hours
	+ 4 hours
* **Other** (Optional): If none of the provided options for Availability or Volunteer Days/Hours suit your preferences, please describe your availability in this field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the Other field.
* Once you have submitted this form, you will be contacted to schedule your volunteer shifts.

This guide is intended to provide clear instructions on how to complete the form accurately and efficiently.
