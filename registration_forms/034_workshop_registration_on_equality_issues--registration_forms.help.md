# Workshop Registration On Equality Issues - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the workshop "Workshop Registration On Equality Issues." The form is meant to gather details about each participant, including their session preferences, accessibility needs, and additional contact information. This data will be used to facilitate smooth communication and organization of the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the participant details section:
    - Enter your name in the "Name" field.
    - Enter your role in the "Role" field.
    - Enter your contact information in the "Contact" field.
    - Enter your school name in the "School" field.
    - Enter your organization name in the "Organization" field.
    - Enter your location in the "Location" field.
    - Enter your date of birth in the "Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
    - Enter your time zone in the "Time Zone" field.
    - Enter the session time in the "Session Time" field.
    - Enter the session date in the "Session Date" field.
    - Enter the session location in the "Session Location" field.
2.  Select your session preferences:
    - For each session, choose "Yes" if you would like to attend or "No" if you will not attend.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  If you have any additional comments, please use the "Additional Information" field.
6.  Click on the "Submit" button to complete the form.
