# Women's Health Tech Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for the registration process of the Women's Health Tech Forum, a platform for discussion and networking among professionals and stakeholders in the healthcare and technology industries.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To register for the forum, please follow the steps below.
2.  First, fill in your First Name and Last Name details.
3.  Provide your Email address and validate it as per your institution's email policies.
4.  Indicate your Session Preferences and Attendee Details.
5.  Enter your Contact Info.
6.  Fill in the Date and Time of your attendance.
7.  Enter your Phone number and Comments (if any).
8.  Select your Gender and Age.
9.  Indicate whether you will attend the Session.
10. Enter any Comments about the Session.
11.  Fill in your Address details (City, State, Zip, Country).
12.  Fill in your Company, Job Title, Department, Job Function, Company URL, Image URL (if any).
13.  You may leave Comments about the event (if any).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`1`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your first name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your last name as it appears on official documents.
*   **Email** (`3`, `email`, `required`): Enter a valid Email address. Please ensure it matches your official Email of your institution.
*   **Session Preferences** (`4`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your Session preferences from the options provided.
*   **Attendee Details** (`5`, `note`, `optional`): Provide a brief introduction about yourself, including your role in the healthcare industry and your interests.
*   **Contact Info** (`6`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Select your contact preference for future communication and sessions.
*   **Phone** (`7`, `text`, `required`): Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
*   **Date** (`8`, `date`, `required`): Fill in the date of your expected attendance.
*   **Time** (`9`, `time`, `required`): Fill in the time of your expected attendance.
*   **Comments** (`10`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any additional comments or suggestions for the event organizers.
*   **Gender** (`11`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your Gender.
*   **Age** (`12`, `number`, `required`): Fill in your age.
*   **Session** (`13`, `select_multiple`, `required`): Indicate whether you will be attending the session.
*   **Comments on Session** (`14`, `text`, `optional`): Provide any comments or suggestions for the session.
*   **Address** (`15`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in your address details.
*   **City** (`16`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your city.
*   **State** (`17`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your state or province.
*   **Zip** (`18`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in your zip code.
*   **Country** (`19`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your country.
*   **Company** (`20`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in your company.
*   **Job Title** (`21`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in your job title.
*   **Department** (`22`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your department or team.
*   **Job Function** (`23`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in your job function.
*   **Company URL** (`24`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your company's website URL.
*   **Image URL** (`25`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in your image URL.
*   **Company URL** (`25`, `text`, `optional`): Fill in any additional comments about the event.
