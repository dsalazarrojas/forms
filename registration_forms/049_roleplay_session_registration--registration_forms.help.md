# Roleplay Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Roleplay Session Registration form is a tool for users to express their interest in participating in a roleplay session. It helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the session, making it easier for us to tailor the experience to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details:
	* `participant_name`: Enter your name as you'd like to be referred to during the session.
	* `session_title`: Provide a title for the session you're interested in participating in.
	* `session_date`: Enter the date of the session you'd like to attend.
	* `session_start_time`: Enter the start time of the session you'd like to participate in.
	* `session_end_time`: Enter the end time of the session you'd like to participate in.
	* `participant_character`: Describe the character you'd like to play during the session.
	* `participant_experience`: Share any relevant roleplay experience you have.
	* `event_host_name`: If you're attending a session hosted by someone else, enter their name.
	* `event_host_email`: If you're attending a session hosted by someone else, enter their email address.
	* `guidelines`: If you have any special requirements or needs, describe them here.
2. Confirm your agreement:
	* `submit`: Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name as you'd like to be referred to during the session.
* **session_title** (`session_title`, text, required: false): Provide a title for the session you're interested in participating in.
* **session_date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the session you'd like to attend.
* **session_start_time** (`session_start_time`, time, required: false): Enter the start time of the session you'd like to participate in.
* **session_end_time** (`session_end_time`, time, required: false): Enter the end time of the session you'd like to participate in.
* **participant_character** (`participant_character`, text, required: false): Describe the character you'd like to play during the session.
* **participant_experience** (`participant_experience`, text, required: false): Share any relevant roleplay experience you have.
* **event_host_name** (`event_host_name`, text, required: false): If you're attending a session hosted by someone else, enter their name.
* **event_host_email** (`event_host_email`, email, required: false): If you're attending a session hosted by someone else, enter their email address.
* **guidelines** (`guidelines`, note, required: false): If you have any special requirements or needs, describe them here.
* **submit** (`submit`, select_one, required: false): Check the box to agree to the terms and conditions of the session.

## Tips
* Please ensure you only fill out this form once for each roleplay session you're interested in.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the session or form, contact the event host.
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.
