# National Online Forensics Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for coaches to register their teams for online forensics competitions. Please complete the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete registration.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info**: Enter your team name or school name in this field.
* **Coach Info**: Enter your name in this field.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of forensics event your team is participating in from the options provided (National Event 1, National Event 2, or National Event 3).
* **Participants**: Indicate whether there are participants in your team (True or False).
* **Event Date**: Enter the date when the forensics event will take place.
* **Event Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Coach Contact Info**: Enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Event Details**: Provide additional information about the event if needed.
* **Additional Event Info**: Provide any extra details or information about the event (you can enter up to 4 extra fields for additional information).
* **Event Details**: This field is similar to the previous one, for more extra details or information about the event.
* **Additional Event Info 2**: Another field for extra details or information about the event.
* **Additional Event Info 3**: Yet another field for extra details or information about the event.
* **Additional Event Info 4**: The last extra field for more information about the event.

## Tips

- Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
- Ensure you've selected the correct event type and date for your team.
- If you have any questions or issues, contact us at the provided contact information.
