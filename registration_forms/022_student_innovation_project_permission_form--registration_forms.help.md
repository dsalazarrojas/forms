# Student Innovation Project Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register and obtain permission for students to participate in innovation projects. It's a crucial step for students to declare their interest in specific projects and for parents to give their consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1.  Enter your student's name in the "Student name" field.
2.  Provide your parent's name in the "Parent name" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Choose the date of signature in the "Signature date" field.
6.  Sign your name in the "Signature" field.
7.  Choose whether the student is allowed to participate by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Is student allowed to participate?" field.
8.  Select the specific project type in the "Student innovative project" field. You can choose "Robotics", "Coding", "Art", or "Music".
9.  Choose whether the parent is giving permission by selecting "True" or "False" in the "Parent permission" field.
10.  Enter the date of permission in the "Date of permission" field.
11.  Choose the school year in the "School year" field. You can choose "2022-2023", "2023-2024", or "2024-2025".
12.  Choose the school semester in the "School semester" field. You can choose "1" or "2".
13.  Select the school department in the "School department" field. You can choose "A", "B", or "C".
14.  Choose the student's grade level in the "Student grade level" field. You can choose "1", "2", "3", "4", "5", "6", "7", "8", "9", or "10".
15.  Enter the student's age in the "Student age" field.
16.  Enter the parent's signature date in the "Parent signature date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your student's name.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your parent's name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, date, required): Choose the date of signature.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): Sign your name.
* **Is student allowed to participate?** (`student_permission`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the student is allowed to participate by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Student innovative project** (`student_innovative_project`, select_one, required): Select the specific project type. You can choose "Robotics", "Coding", "Art", or "Music".
* **Parent permission** (`parent_permission`, select_one, required): Choose whether the parent is giving permission by selecting "True" or "False".
* **Date of permission** (`date_of_permission`, date, required): Enter the date of permission.
* **Time of permission** (`time_of_permission`, time, optional): Enter the time of permission.
* **School year** (`school_year`, select_one, required): Choose the school year. You can choose "2022-2023", "2023-2024", or "2024-2025".
* **School semester** (`school_semester`, select_one, required): Choose the school semester. You can choose "1" or "2".
* **School department** (`school_department`, select_one, required): Select the school department. You can choose "A", "B", or "C".
* **Student grade level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required): Choose the student's grade level. You can choose "1", "2", "3", "4", "5", "6", "7", "8", "9", or "10".
* **Student age** (`student_age`, number, required): Enter the student's age.
* **Parent signature date** (`parent_signature_date`, date, required): Enter the parent's signature date.
