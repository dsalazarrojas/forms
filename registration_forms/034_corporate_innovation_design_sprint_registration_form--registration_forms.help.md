# corporate_innovation_design_sprint_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The corporate innovation design sprint registration form is a tool for teams to register for the corporate innovation design sprint event. This form helps us understand team details, their interest in participating, and participant contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you are registering the correct team and that this is not a duplicate registration.
2. Complete the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are filled.
3. Select the correct options for each field as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Details** (`form_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter a brief description of the team and their background in this field. This will help us understand your team's experience and context.
* **Team Information** (`team_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter more details about your team, such as their experience and expertise.
* **Team Leader** (`team_leader`, select one, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are the team leader and will be the primary point of contact for the event.
* **Team Members** (`team_members`, select multiple, optional):
	+ Choose 'Yes' for each member of your team who will be participating in the event.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_details`, text, optional):
	+ Enter more details about the team members who will be participating in the event.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the primary point of contact for your team.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address, which will be used as the primary contact method for your team.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number, which will be used as an alternative contact method for your team.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date you are interested in attending the corporate innovation design sprint event.

Please note that all fields are optional unless specified as required. Ensure you provide accurate and complete information to facilitate a smooth registration process.
