# procurement_and_purchasing_training_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and record participant training enrollment details and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Review the form fields carefully and fill out the required fields accurately.
2.  Enter your participant training enrollment details in the "Training Session" field.
3.  Select the relevant training courses using the "Select Training Courses" field.
4.  Enter any additional comments in the "Comments" field.
5.  Submit the form when complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Training Enrollment** (`training_enrollment_details`): Enter a brief description of the participant's training enrollment.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_details`): Enter the name of the person responsible for the participant's training.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`): Enter the name or description of the training session.
* **Select Training Courses** (`course_selection`): Choose the relevant training courses for the participant.
	+ Select one or more options from the dropdown menu.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): Enter any relevant payment details.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`): Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant's training.
* **Form Submission Status** (`form_submission_status`): Indicate the form's current status. Options are Draft, Submitted, Approved, and Declined.
	+ Select one of the available options.
* **Form ID** (`form_id`): Enter a unique identifier for the form.
* **Form Category** (`category`): Enter the category of the form.
* **Output File Name** (`output_file`): Enter the name of the output file.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Enter the tool assigned to the participant.
* **User ID** (`user_id`): Enter the ID of the user responsible for the participant's training.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any comments about the participant's training.
* **Status** (`status`): Indicate the current status of the participant's training. Options are Draft, Submitted, Approved, and Declined.
	+ Select one of the available options.
* **Submitted By User** (`submitted_by_user`): Enter the name of the user who submitted the form.
* **Submitted Date/Time** (`submitted_date_time`): Enter the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Updated By User** (`updated_by_user`): Enter the name of the user who updated the form.
* **Updated Date/Time** (`updated_date_time`): Enter the date and time the form was updated.
* **Created By User** (`created_by_user`): Enter the name of the user who created the form.
* **Created Date** (`created_date`): Enter the date the form was created.
