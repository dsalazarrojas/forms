# mental_health_hotline_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for training sessions provided by the Hotline, a mental health service. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from participants, including their details, training preferences, contact information, and other relevant data. Please note that all fields are optional, and you can choose to provide only the required information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **participant_details** field with your name and any other relevant details you want to share with the Hotline team.
2. Enter the **training_session** field with your preferred training session number (if applicable).
3. Enter the **training_location** field with the location you'd like to attend (if applicable).
4. Provide your **contact_person** name and contact information in the **contact_person** field.
5. Enter your email address in the **contact_email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant_details** (Text, Optional): This field is where you can enter your name and any other relevant details you want to share with the Hotline team. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **training_session** (Number, Optional): This field is where you can select your preferred training session number. Please note that this field is not applicable for this form.
* **training_location** (Text, Optional): This field is where you can enter the location you'd like to attend. Please keep it concise and clear.
* **contact_person** (Text, Optional): This field is where you can enter your name and contact information. Please provide a clear and complete address, including your phone number and email address.
* **contact_email** (Email, Optional): This field is where you can enter your email address. Please ensure it's a valid email address to receive updates and communications from the Hotline team.

## Tips
* Please be as accurate and clear as possible when filling in your details.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the Hotline team at [your email or contact information].
* The Hotline team will use the information provided in this form to facilitate communication and training arrangements.
* Please do not share your password or login information with anyone.
* Please be patient and wait for further communication from the Hotline team regarding your training session.
* The data provided in this form will be kept confidential and securely stored.
