# Hair Product Brand Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register and provide information about themselves, their contact details, and product-related data. The collected data will be used for training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form by providing your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your user information, including any necessary contact details.
3. Enter your hair-related information, if applicable.
4. Add any product-related information, if relevant.
5. Provide any additional comments or remarks, if needed.
6. Click the "Submit" button to complete and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Enter your registration name and any other relevant details to complete this form.

### User Info (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Enter your user information, including any necessary contact details, such as email address or phone number.

### Contact Info (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Provide your contact information, including name and any other necessary details.

### Hair Info (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Enter any relevant hair-related details, such as hair type or product preferences.

### Product Info (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Enter any information about the hair product you are interested in, including brand, model, or other relevant details.

### Additional Info (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Enter any additional comments or remarks you have regarding your registration.

### Submit (<name>, <type: text, required: false>)
Click this button to complete and submit your registration.
