# Student Research Publishing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students to register for a workshop focused on research publishing. It collects essential information for a successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately with the required information.
2. Carefully review your submission before confirming your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter your first name as per your official identification.
* **Last Name (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter your last name as per your official identification.
* **Email (<name>, <type>: email, required: false)**: Enter a valid email address that can be used for communication related to the workshop.
* **Phone Number (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for any potential follow-up communication.
* **Workshop Date (<name>, <type>: date, required: false)**: Select the date of the workshop you plan to attend.
* **Workshop Time (<name>, <type>: time, required: false)**: Select the time slot for the workshop you prefer.
* **Registration Date (<name>, <type>: date, required: false)**: Record the date of your registration.
* **Institution (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter the name of your institution.
* **Role (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter your role in the research area.
* **Research Area (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Specify the research area you are interested in.
* **Research Title (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter the title of your research paper or project.
* **Paper Details (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Describe the details of your research paper or project.
* **Presentation Duration (<name>, <type>: number, required: false)**: Specify the duration of your presentation.
* **Audio File (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter the URL or local path of your audio file.
* **Poster (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter details about any poster you plan to display.
* **Slides (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Enter details about any slides you plan to present.
* **Abstract (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Write a brief abstract of your research paper or project.
* **Bio (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Briefly describe yourself and your research background.
* **Workshop Location (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Specify the location of the workshop venue.
* **Workshop Venue (<name>, <type>: text, required: false)**: Specify the name of the workshop venue.
* **Registration Confirmation (<name>, <type>: note, required: false)**: A confirmation message after successful registration.
* **Workshop Organizers (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, required: true)**: Indicate if you are willing to be organized or co-organized by the workshop organizers.
* **Workshop Topics (<name>, <type>: select_multiple, required: true)**: Choose the topics you are interested in or want to present.
* **Workshop Language (<name>, <type>: select_one, required: true)**: Choose the language of the workshop you prefer.
* **Registration Form (<name>, <type>: select_one, required: true)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the registration form terms.
