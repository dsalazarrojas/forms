# Tasting Experience Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tasting Experience Registration Form is designed to collect information about a user's tasting experience for a food or wine tasting event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the type of tasting experience you had (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
2. Enter your first name and last name as they appear on your official documents (first name, last name, and any middle initial if applicable).
3. Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
4. Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
5. Select the date of the event you attended.
6. Select the time of the event you attended.
7. Enter the end time of the event.
8. Enter the location of the event.
9. Provide a brief description of the event.
10. If you made a payment using a credit card, select "Yes" for the payment method.
11. Enter your credit card number, expiration date, and security code if you selected "Yes" for payment method.
12. Add any additional notes about your experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tasting Experience**: Select "Yes" if you had a tasting experience at the event, "No" if you did not.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you attended.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event you attended.
* **Event Time Ending**: Select the end time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Payment Method**: If you made a payment using a credit card, select "Yes" for payment method.
* **Credit Card Number**: Enter your credit card number if you selected "Yes" for payment method.
* **Credit Card Expiration**: Enter your credit card expiration date if you selected "Yes" for payment method.
* **Credit Card Security Code**: Enter your credit card security code if you selected "Yes" for payment method.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your experience.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues with processing your registration or payment.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
