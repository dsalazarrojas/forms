# Community Recovery Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who are interested in participating in a recovery program. It will help us understand their needs, preferences, and circumstances to provide personalized support and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the welcome note to understand the purpose and scope of the program.
2.  Enter your **Participant Full Name**, **Date of Birth**, and **Email Address**. These are mandatory fields.
3.  Select your **Gender Identity** from the provided options.
4.  Enter your **Phone Number** and **Emergency Contact Full Name**, as well as their **Phone Number**. These are also mandatory.
5.  Indicate whether you've participated in a recovery program before and provide details if yes.
6.  Select the recovery services you are interested in (e.g., Group Counseling, Individual Peer Support, etc.).
7.  Choose the times you are available to participate in the program.
8.  Write a brief statement about your primary goals for this program.
9.  Confirm that you consent to the terms of the program.
10. Read the **Confidentiality Statement** to understand how your information is handled.
11. Finally, confirm the **Registration Date** by clicking the 'Submit' button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name**: Please enter your full name as you'd like it to be used in the program. This is required.
* **Participant Date of Birth**: Enter your Date of Birth in the format 'MM/DD/YYYY' (e.g., 02/12/2000).
* **Gender Identity**: Select your gender identity from the provided options (Male, Female, Non-Binary, Prefer not to say).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can contact you for program updates and support.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where we can reach you for emergencies or program-related matters.
* **Emergency Contact Full Name**: Enter the full name of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Have you participated in a recovery program before?**: Indicate if you've participated in a recovery program before. If yes, please provide the name of the program.
* **Which recovery services are you interested in?**: Select all the services that interest you to help us tailor our support.
* **Your Weekly Availability**: Choose the times you are available to participate in the program.
* **Tell us a bit about your primary goals for this program**: Write a brief statement about your primary goals for this program.
* **I consent to the terms of the recovery program**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the terms of the program.
* **Confidentiality Statement**: Read this to understand how your information is handled.
* **Today's Date**: This field is automatically filled in and not editable. Confirm your registration by clicking 'Submit'.
