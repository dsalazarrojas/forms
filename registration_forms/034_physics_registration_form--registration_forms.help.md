# Physics Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is for students to register for a physics course. It will be used to gather information about the student's name, email, and telephone number, as well as their interest in taking a physics course.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues.
2. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Indicate if you are interested in taking a physics course by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' in the `physics_course` field.
4. Select your preferred class start and end dates using the `class_start_date` and `class_end_date` fields.
5. Provide any additional information about your physics interests or experience in the `student_physicstext` and `student_physicstext2` fields, if desired.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (student_name)** 
    * (student_name) is the field where you will enter your name. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Email (student_email)** 
    * (student_email) is the field where you will enter your email address. This is where we will contact you with course information and updates.
* **Student Telephone (student_telephone)** 
    * (student_telephone) is the field where you will enter your phone number. This is where we will contact you with last-minute course updates and reminders.
* **Physics Course (physics_course)** 
    * (physics_course) is where you will indicate if you are interested in taking a physics course. Please select 'Yes' if you are interested and 'No' if you are not.
* **Class Start Date (class_start_date)** 
    * (class_start_date) is where you will select the start date of your preferred class. Please note that this date is only a suggestion and is subject to change.
* **Class End Date (class_end_date)** 
    * (class_end_date) is where you will select the end date of your preferred class. Please note that this date is only a suggestion and is subject to change.
* **Additional Physics Interest (student_physicstext)** 
    * (student_physicstext) is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your physics interests or experience. If you don't have anything to add, you can leave this field blank.
* **Additional Physics Information (student_physicstext2)** 
    * (student_physicstext2) is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your physics interests or experience. If you don't have anything to add, you can leave this field blank.
