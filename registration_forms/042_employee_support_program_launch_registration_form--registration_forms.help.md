# Employee Support Program Launch Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Employee Support Program Launch Registration Form is designed to capture essential information from employees who wish to join the company's Employee Support Program. This form helps to identify and register employees who are eligible for program benefits. Please ensure that you have reviewed this guide and understand its purpose before proceeding to complete the form. If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact your HR department.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please ensure that you are a valid employee of the organization before attempting to complete this form.
2.  Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Review each field carefully to ensure you are entering the correct information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your official email address that you use for work-related activities.
*   **Role** (Select One, Optional):
    *   **Employee**: Select this option if you are a regular employee of the company.
    *   **Manager**: Select this option if you are a manager within the company.
*   **Team** (Select Multiple, Optional):
    *   **Sales Team**: Select this option if you are a member of the Sales Team.
    *   **Marketing Team**: Select this option if you are a member of the Marketing Team.
    *   **Operations Team**: Select this option if you are a member of the Operations Team.
*   **Department** (Select One, Optional):
    *   **Sales**: Select this option if you belong to the Sales department.
    *   **Finance**: Select this option if you belong to the Finance department.
    *   **HR**: Select this option if you belong to the HR department.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yy or dd-mm-yyyy).
*   **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your official job title within the company.
*   **Phone Number** (Text, Optional): Enter your official phone number that you use for work-related activities.
