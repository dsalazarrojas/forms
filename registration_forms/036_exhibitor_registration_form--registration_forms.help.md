# exhibitor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exhibitor registration form is for vendors and exhibitors who want to participate in the exhibition. It collects information about the vendor or exhibitor, including their contact details, payment method, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. If you are not sure about any of the fields, you can leave it blank or ask for help.
3. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Space** (Select One): 
    Choose 'Yes' if you will need a space for your products or services.
* **Additional Tables** (Text): 
    If you need extra tables, describe how many you need in this field.
* **Show Catalog** (Text): 
    Describe how you plan to show your catalog.
* **Advertising and Sponsorship** (Text): 
    Describe your advertising or sponsorship plans.
* **Tables Size** (Select One): 
    Choose 'Yes' if you will need tables to display your products or services.
* **Additional Services** (Select Multiple): 
    Choose 'Yes' for any additional services you require, such as electricity or internet access.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): 
    Choose 'Yes' if you have a payment method other than the options listed.
* **Contact Name** (Text): 
    Enter the name of the contact person for your company.
* **Email** (Email): 
    Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone** (Text): 
    Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Street Address** (Text): 
    Enter the street address of the contact person.
* **ZIP** (Text): 
    Enter the ZIP code of the contact person's location.
* **Payment Terms** (Text): 
    Describe your payment terms.
* **Payment Method Other** (Text): 
    If you chose 'Yes' in the Payment Method field, describe your payment method in this field.
* **Note** (Note): 
    Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
