# Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Registration Form is used to register team members for a school, sports, or event team. This form collects personal and contact information for each team member and their parents/guardians.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and valid information.
2. Double-check that you have filled in all fields correctly before submitting the form.
3. Review the form for any errors or typos before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the team member's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the team member's last name.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the team member's contact phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the team member's email address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_1_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team member's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Number** (`parent_1_contact_number`, text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's contact phone number.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_1_email`, email, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter the student's name (if applicable).
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the team's name (if applicable).

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check for errors or typos before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact us for assistance.
