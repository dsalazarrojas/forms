# Client Information Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Client Information Request Form is a tool used to collect essential information from clients and potential clients about their identification, contact preferences, and service requirements. This information is crucial for us to provide personalized services and communicate effectively with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the First Name and Last Name fields, respectively.
2. Enter your primary contact email address in the Email Address field.
3. Provide your best contact phone number in the Phone Number field.
4. Enter your complete mailing address in the Street Address field.
5. Fill in your city and state in the City and State fields, respectively.
6. Enter your ZIP code in the ZIP Code field.
7. If you have a company, enter its name in the Company Name field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
8. Enter the URL of your company website in the Company Website field. If not, you can leave this field blank.
9. Indicate your job title or position in the Job Title field. If not applicable, you can leave this field blank.
10. Select the type of registration you are interested in from the Registration Type field.
11. Choose the service you are interested in from the Service of Interest field.
12. Select your preferred start date for the service from the Preferred Start Date field.
13. Indicate how you heard about us from the How Did You Hear About Us? field.
14. If referred, enter the name of the person who referred you in the Referred By field.
15. If applicable, provide any special requirements or accommodations you need in the Special Requirements or Accommodations field.
16. If applicable, list any dietary restrictions you have in the Dietary Restrictions field.
17. Enter the name and contact information of your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Contact Phone, and Emergency Contact Relationship fields.
18. Select your preferred method of communication from the Communication Preferences field.
19. Indicate whether you consent to receive marketing communications from us in the May We Send You Marketing Communications? field.
20. Confirm that you have accepted the Terms and Conditions in the I Accept the Terms and Conditions field.
21. Confirm that you have accepted the Privacy Policy in the I Accept the Privacy Policy field.
22. Confirm that all information provided is accurate in the I Confirm All Information Is Accurate field.
23. Finally, select your registration date from the Registration Date field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Client First Name, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name** (Client Last Name, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (Client Email, email, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (Client Phone, text, required): Enter your best contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (Client Address, text, required): Enter your complete mailing address.
* **City** (Client City, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (Client State, text, required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (Client ZIP, text, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, optional): Enter your business name if applicable.
* **Company Website** (Company Website, text, optional): Enter your business website URL if applicable.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, optional): Enter your job title or position if applicable.
* **Registration Type** (Registration Type, select_one, required): Select the type of registration you are interested in.
* **Service of Interest** (Service of Interest, select_one, required): Select the service you are interested in.
* **Preferred Start Date** (Preferred Start Date, date, required): Select your preferred start date for the service.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (How Did You Hear About Us?, select_one, required): Indicate how you heard about us.
* **Referred By** (Referred By, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who referred you if applicable.
* **Special Requirements or Accommodations** (Special Requirements or Accommodations, text, optional): Enter any special requirements or accommodations if applicable.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions if applicable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text, optional): Indicate the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Communication Preferences** (Communication Preferences, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred method of communication.
* **May We Send You Marketing Communications?** (May We Send You Marketing Communications?, select_one, required): Indicate whether you consent to receive marketing communications from us.
* **I Accept the Terms and Conditions** (Terms Accepted, select_one, required): Confirm that you have accepted the Terms and Conditions.
* **I Accept the Privacy Policy** (Privacy Policy Accepted, select_one, required): Confirm that you have accepted the Privacy Policy.
* **I Confirm All Information Is Accurate** (Information Accuracy, select_one, required): Confirm that you have provided accurate information.
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required): Select your registration date.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure timely and effective service delivery.
* If you have any special requirements or accommodations, please indicate them in the Special Requirements or Accommodations field.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them in the Dietary Restrictions field.
* If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and contact information in the Emergency Contact Name, Emergency Contact Phone, and Emergency Contact Relationship fields.
