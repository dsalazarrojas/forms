# Social Justice Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Social Justice Seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the **participant_name** field, if you wish to be identified as a participant.
2. Provide your email address in the **email** field, so we can contact you regarding the seminar.
3. If you have any additional contact information, such as a phone number, enter it in the **contact_number** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name (text)**: Enter your name if you wish to be identified as a participant.
* **email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding the seminar. This is a required field.
* **contact_number (text)**: Enter any additional contact information you wish to provide, such as a phone number. This is an optional field.
