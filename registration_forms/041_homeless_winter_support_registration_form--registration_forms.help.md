# Homeless Winter Support Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic, registration, and contact information from individuals seeking homeless support during the winter season. The form aims to provide essential details to organizations or agencies offering assistance to support the well-being of homeless individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering your demographic information, including Client Information (fields 2 and 4) and Contact Information (fields 6 and 8).
2. Proceed to fill out the Registration Information fields (3, 5, 7).
3. If you have any Additional Information to share, enter it in field 9.
4. Submit your application by completing field 10, Registration Confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Information (1)**: Enter your personal details to help us understand your situation.
* **Client Information 2 (4)**: Please provide additional details about yourself to better assist us.
* **Registration Information (3)**: Provide your registration details for our records.
* **Registration Information 2 (5)**: Enter any further details about your registration.
* **Contact Information (6)**: Share your contact details, such as phone number or email.
* **Contact Information 2 (8)**: Provide any other contact details you feel necessary.
* **Additional Information (9)**: If you have any further details, please share them with us.
* **Submission (10)**: Complete this field to submit your application.
