# Advanced Mastocytosis Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Advanced Mastocytosis Workshop, ensuring that the participant provides accurate and essential details required for registration and participation in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the required participant details such as name, institution or practice, medical specialty, email, and phone number (if applicable).
2. Select the desired registration tier and payment method.
3. Indicate which sessions or areas of focus you are interested in (Select Multiple).
4. Choose your dietary preference for on-site lunch.
5. Confirm your agreement to the recording policy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Institution or Practice** (Institution or Practice, Text, Required): Enter the name of the institution or practice you are affiliated with.
* **Medical Specialty** (Medical Specialty, Select One, Required): Choose your primary area of expertise from the provided options.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address for registration confirmation and potential future correspondence.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, Text, Optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact, if applicable.
* **Session Interests** (Session Interests, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the sessions or areas you wish to focus on during the workshop.
* **Dietary Preference** (Dietary Preference, Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred dietary option for on-site lunch.
* **Registration Tier** (Registration Tier, Select One, Required): Select the registration tier that best suits your needs and budget.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, Select One, Required): Choose a payment method that works for you.
* **Additional Info** (Additional Info, Note, Optional): Provide any additional information that may be relevant for the organizers.
* **Specific Questions for the Experts** (Specific Questions for the Experts, Text, Optional): If you have specific topics you would like addressed during the workshop, provide them here.
* **Agree to Recording Policy** (Agree to Recording Policy, Select One, Required): Confirm your agreement to the recording policy for educational use.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely, as this information is essential for workshop organizers to process your registration efficiently.
