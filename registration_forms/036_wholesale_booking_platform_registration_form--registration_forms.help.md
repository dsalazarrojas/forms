# Wholesale Booking Platform Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from users to register their wholesale booking platforms.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the "User Input" page.
2. Provide the required information in the "Product Info" and "Billing Info" sections.
3. Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (text, required): This field is where you'll provide your personal details for registration.
* **Product Info** (text, required): Here, you'll enter information about your wholesale booking platform, such as its name, description, and other relevant details.
* **Billing Info** (text, not required): This section is for you to provide any additional billing information or notes, if necessary. You can leave this field blank if you don't have any billing information to share.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as the information provided will be used to process your registration.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
