# Operational Sustainability Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Operational Sustainability Program Registration Form is used to register new programs and track their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and fill out the required information for each section.
2. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
3. Choose the program details and sustainability committee members from the available options.
4. Add any additional comments or notes about your program.
5. Select a status for your program.
6. Enter the submission dates and times.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (id: 2, type: text, required: false):
	+ Enter your valid email address and phone number to contact you for any questions or follow-ups.
* **Program Details** (id: 3, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select one option from the available choices to describe your program.
* **Sustainability Committee** (id: 4, type: select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select multiple members from the available choices to describe your committee.
* **Additional Comments** (id: 5, type: text, required: false):
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your program.
* **Email** (id: 6, type: email, required: false):
	+ Enter a valid email address to be contacted about your program.
* **Phone** (id: 7, type: text, required: false):
	+ Enter a valid phone number to be contacted about your program.
* **Date Submitted** (id: 8, type: date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date you submitted your program.
* **Time Submitted** (id: 9, type: time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time you submitted your program.
* **Date Reviewed** (id: 10, type: date, required: false):
	+ Enter the date your program was reviewed.
* **Time Reviewed** (id: 11, type: time, required: false):
	+ Enter the time your program was reviewed.
* **Status** (id: 12, type: select_one, required: false):
	+ Select a status for your program from the available options.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have all the necessary information before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Select the correct program details and sustainability committee members.
* Enter valid phone and email addresses.
* Enter accurate submission and review dates and times.
* Review your program status carefully.
