## Purpose
The real-estate-crm-client-registration-form is designed to collect essential information about a client for our real estate CRM system. This information will be used to create a client profile and improve our communication with you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information on the first page.
2. Provide your contact information on the second page.
3. Select the type of market you are interested in on the third page.
4. Enter any additional contact information on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Info (1)
• **Registration Info** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): This field is for providing your basic registration information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details you would like to share.

### Contact Info (2)
• **Contact Info** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Here you can provide your contact details, such as phone number, email, or physical address.

### Market Info (3)
• **Market Info** (`title`, `select_one`, required/optional): Please select the type of market you are interested in, such as Single Family, Multi Family, Commercial, or Industrial.

### Contact Info 2 (4)
• **Contact Info 2** (`title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional contact information you would like to share, such as a second phone number or email address.
