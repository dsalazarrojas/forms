# Birthday Event Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a birthday event registration process. It is intended for parents or guardians of children celebrating their birthday at the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form with accurate information for the child's birthday event.
2. Ensure that you provide all required information for the child, parents or guardians, and event details.
3. Review the form carefully to ensure that all fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event, which is the birthday party for the child.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Event Start Time** (`event_time`): Enter the start time of the event in the format `HH:MM:SS AM/PM`.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event, including street address.
* **Birthday Child's Name** (`birthday_child_name`): Enter the name of the child having the birthday.
* **Age Turning** (`birthday_child_age`): Enter the child's age at the time of the birthday.
* **Birth Date** (`birthday_child_dob`): Enter the child's full birth date in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Special Needs or Allergies** (`special_needs_or_allergies`): If the child has any special needs or allergies, please describe them here.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`): Enter your full name as the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email** (`parent_email`): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`parent_phone`): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Home Address** (`parent_address`): Enter your home address, including street address.
* **City** (`parent_city`): Enter the city of your residence.
* **State or Province** (`parent_state`): Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **Postal Code** (`parent_zip`): Enter your postal code.
* **Total Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`): Enter the total number of guests attending the event.
* **Names of Other Guests** (`guest_names`): List the names of other guests attending the event.
* **Number of Children in Party** (`children_count`): Enter the total number of children attending the event.
* **Number of Adults in Party** (`adults_count`): Enter the total number of adults attending the event.
* **Interest in Activities** (`party_activities`): Select the activities that interest you or your child.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Food Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`): Select the dietary restrictions or allergies you or your child has.
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`): Describe any specific dietary requirements.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`): Make any other special requests or needs known.
* **Birthday Child Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`): Select the birthday child's shirt size for party favors or t-shirts.
* **Registration Fee per Person** (`registration_fee`): Enter the registration fee per person.
* **Total Registration Fee** (`total_guests_fee`): Enter the total registration fee for all guests.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the payment method for the event registration.
* **I understand payment is due by the deadline** (`payment_confirmation`): Confirm your understanding of payment requirements.
* **Permission for Event Photos** (`photo_consent`): Grant permission for event photos to be taken.
* **I Acknowledge Liability and Activity Risks** (`waiver_acknowledgment`): Confirm that you acknowledge liability and activity risks.
* **I Confirm Registration** (`registration_confirmation`): Confirm your registration for the event.
