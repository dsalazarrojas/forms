# ประก prophet Festival Coordination Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for festival coordination partners to register and provide information about themselves and their participation in the festival. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information about yourself and your festival.
2.  Select the correct options for your festival details and logo presence.
3.  Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly, as indicated by the required status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **partner_name** (partner name, text, required): Enter your partner name, as it will be displayed on the festival's platform.
*   **first_name** (first name, text, required): Enter your first name.
*   **last_name** (last name, text, required): Enter your last name.
*   **email** (email, email, required): Enter a valid email address for communication.
*   **phone** (phone, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **website** (website, text, required): Enter your official website link.
*   **description** (description, text, required): Provide a short description of your festival coordination partner.
*   **location** (location, text, required): Enter the address of your festival coordination partner location.
*   **category** (category, select_one, required): Select the category of your festival coordination partner.
*   **subcategory** (subcategory, text, optional): Provide additional details about your festival coordination partner's category (if not provided, it will be considered as "No" category).
*   **logo** (logo, select_multiple, required): Select whether your festival coordination partner has a logo.
*   **social_media** (social media, select_multiple, required): Select whether your festival coordination partner has a social media presence.
*   **festival_date** (festival date, date, required): Enter the date of your festival in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
*   **festival_start_time** (festival start time, time, required): Enter the start time of your festival in the format "HH:MM".
*   **festival_end_time** (festival end time, time, required): Enter the end time of your festival in the format "HH:MM".
*   **festival_address** (festival address, text, required): Enter the festival address.
*   **festival_organizer** (festival organizer, text, optional): Enter the name of the festival organizer (if not provided, it will be considered as "No" organizer).
*   **website** (website, text, required): Enter your official website link.
*   **social_media_links** (social media links, select_multiple, required): Select whether your festival coordination partner has social media links.
*   **notes** (notes, text, optional): Provide additional notes about your festival coordination partner.
*   **terms_and_conditions** (terms and conditions, text, optional): Agree to the terms and conditions of the festival coordination partner program.
*   **privacy_policy** (privacy policy, text, optional): Agree to the privacy policy of the festival coordination partner program.
*   **submission_confirmation** (submission confirmation, text, required): Confirm that you have submitted the form accurately.
*   **partner_confirmation** (partner confirmation, text, optional): Confirm that your partner's information is correct.
*   **submission_status** (submission status, select_one, required): Select the status of your submission.

## Tips
*   Please make sure to fill out all mandatory fields accurately and completely.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please contact the festival team for assistance.
*   Double-check your submission before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
