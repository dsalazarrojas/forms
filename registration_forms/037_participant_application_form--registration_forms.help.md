# Participant Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Application Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in attending seminars. This form helps the organizers manage participant registrations and ensure a smooth event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant information in the **Participant Information** section.
2. Select the seminar you're interested in from the **Seminar Type** dropdown.
3. Provide your contact details in the **Contact Information** section.
4. Specify your seminar participation status in the **Seminar Participants** field.
5. (Optional) Enter the maximum capacity and remaining capacity for the seminar in the **Maximum Capacity** and **Remaining Capacity** fields.
6. Enter the dates for **Registration Date** and **Submission Date** if required.
7. Finally, review your information and click **Submit**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: Enter your participant name and surname.
* **Seminar Information**: Select the type of seminar you're interested in. This will help us understand which seminar to assign you to.
* **Contact Information**: Provide your email address, phone number, and address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Type**: Specify your phone type (Home, Work, or Other) so we can contact you accordingly.
* **Seminar Participants**: Indicate if you are participating in the seminar. This will help us keep track of attendees.
* **Maximum Capacity**: If you're planning to bring a group, specify the maximum number of people you can accommodate.
* **Remaining Capacity**: If you're not planning to bring a group, specify the remaining capacity of the seminar for us to manage.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the seminar.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date you submitted your application.
* **Additional Information**: If you have any other relevant details, please enter them here.
* **Submit**: Review your information and click this button to submit your application.

Note: All fields are optional unless otherwise specified as required.
