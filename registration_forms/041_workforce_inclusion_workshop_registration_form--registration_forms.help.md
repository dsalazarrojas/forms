# Workforce Inclusion Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering attendees for the "Workforce Inclusion Workshop". Fill out this form to provide your contact information and preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out each field as prompted.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
3. Review your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, `text`, optional): This field is the primary field for entering your registration information.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your full name to help us know who you are and how to address you.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX` to help us contact you.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you are planning to attend the workshop.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether you are able to attend the workshop.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the options that apply to your workshop attendance.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments you may have about your registration.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, `number`, optional): This field is not used, but if you were to use it, it could be used to enter decimal values, such as monetary amounts.
* **Integer** (`integer`, `number`, optional): This field is not used, but if you were to use it, it could be used to enter whole numbers.

## Tips

- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
- Review your form before submission.
