# Author Reading RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to allow you to RSVP for an event with the author. You will be asked to provide your contact information, event attendance details, and any special requests or comments. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure your spot is reserved.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form pages and enter the required information for each field.
2. Select the correct options for each field as instructed.
3. Enter any special requests or comments in the designated field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP Date** (`rsvp_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you will be attending the event. This is an important part of your RSVP to ensure we have accurate headcounts.
* **Your Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it should be listed on any event materials or communications.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Provide your email address so we can send you updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number for any additional requests or last-minute updates.
* **Author Reading Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event as it appears on promotional materials.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Enter the venue or location of the event.
* **Author Name** (`author_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the author or reader being featured.
* **Will You Attend** (`rsvp_response`, select_one, required): Choose your response to attending the event.
* **Number of Guests in Your Party** (`number_of_guests`, number, optional): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing, if any.
* **Names of Additional Guests** (`guest_names`, text, optional): List the names and contact information for any additional guests.
* **Your Relationship to the Author** (`relationship_to_author`, select_one, optional): If you have a relationship with the author, describe how you know them.
* **Have You Read the Author's Work** (`read_authors_work`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are familiar with the author's books.
* **Favorite Work by This Author** (`favorite_work`, text, optional): Enter your favorite work by the author.
* **Transportation Needs** (`transportation_needs`, select_one, optional): Check if you need help getting to the event.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special needs, describe them here.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose where you would like to be seated.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, list them here.
* **Sign Up for Author Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Check if you wish to receive updates from the author.
* **Need Parking Information** (`parking_info_needed`, select_one, optional): Check if you need parking information.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`prefer_contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to receive updates and confirmations.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests for the event.

This guide is designed to help you complete the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
