# Rhine River Tour Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for tour operators to register their tours on the Rhine River. The form collects essential information about each tour, allowing us to efficiently manage and process tour registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your tour operator name in the "tour_operator" field.
2. Provide the name of your tour in the "tour_name" field.
3. Enter the dates of your tour in the "tour_dates" field.
4. Indicate the size of your party in the "party_size" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Confirm the dates of your tour in the "confirm_dates" field.
8. Confirm the size of your party in the "confirm_party_size" field.
9. Enter a confirmation email address in the "confirm_email" field.
10. Confirm your phone number in the "confirm_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **tour_operator**: Enter your tour operator name. This field is optional.
* **tour_name**: Enter the name of your tour. This field is optional.
* **tour_dates**: Enter the dates of your tour. This field is optional.
* **party_size**: Enter the size of your party. This field is optional.
* **email**: Enter your email address. This field is optional.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number. This field is optional.
* **confirm_dates**: Confirm the dates of your tour. This field is optional.
* **confirm_party_size**: Confirm the size of your party. This field is optional.
* **confirm_email**: Enter a confirmation email address. This field is optional.
* **confirm_phone**: Confirm your phone number. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your tour is correctly registered.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
