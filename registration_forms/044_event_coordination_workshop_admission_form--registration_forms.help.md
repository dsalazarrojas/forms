# event_coordination_workshop_admission_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for an event coordination workshop. It is intended for individuals interested in attending the workshop to provide their details for admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to start filling out the form.
2.  Fill out each field with the required information.
3.  Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant information about your participation in the workshop.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your contact details if you want to be contacted back.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to receive updates about the workshop.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about the workshop.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the workshop (if known).

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
