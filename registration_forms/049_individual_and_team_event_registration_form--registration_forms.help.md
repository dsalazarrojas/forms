# Individual And Team Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for events as an individual or a team. It collects relevant information to track and manage event registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your name and email address (if registering as an individual).
* Enter your team name and team email address (if registering as a team).
* Select the event date and time you want to participate in.
* Choose the event location.
* Select the team size you plan to bring.
* Provide a brief description of the event.
* Select the type of event (e.g., Football, Basketball, etc.).
* Choose the type of team (e.g., Individual, Team).
* List the team members (if registering as a team).
* Add any additional information you'd like to share.
* Confirm your registration status (e.g., Registered, Pending, Canceled).
* Provide additional notes or details about your registration (if needed).

* **Person Email** (`person_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address as an individual.

* **Team Email** (`team_email`, email, optional): Enter your team email address if registering as a team.

* **Team Phone** (`team_phone`, text, optional): Enter your team phone number if registering as a team.
