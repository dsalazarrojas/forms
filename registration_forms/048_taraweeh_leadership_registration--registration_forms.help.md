# Taraweeh Leadership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Taraweeh Leadership Registration form is used to collect information from individuals who wish to join the Taraweeh leadership program. This form helps the program verify each individual's identity and ensure accurate registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please ensure you have all the necessary information and documents required for registration.
* Fill in the required fields as accurately and completely as possible.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**: This field is the main form where you provide your registration details. Please enter your information as accurately as possible.
* **Taraweeh Name**: This field is used to capture your Taraweeh name. Please enter the name that corresponds to your Taraweeh identity.
* **Name**: This field is for your personal name. Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Title**: This field is used to capture your official title or designation. If you don't have one, you can leave it blank.
* **Contact Number**: Please enter a valid contact number that we can use to reach you.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached. Your email will be used for communication purposes.
* **Location**: This field is for your physical location. Please enter the location associated with your contact number or email address.
