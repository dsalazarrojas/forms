# Plan Your Conference Experience - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows you to plan your conference experience by selecting your preferred sessions and workshops. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred sessions from the "sessions" field.
2. Select your preferred breakout sessions from the "breakout sessions" field.
3. If you're interested in attending a workshop, select it from the "workshops" field.
4. Indicate whether or not you'll be planning on-site by selecting "True" or "False" from the "on-site planning" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field, if you'd like to be contacted.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field, if you'd like to be contacted.
