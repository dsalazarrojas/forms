# University Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Enrollment Form is used to capture essential information about new students who are enrolling in a university program. This form helps the university's administration to process the enrollment application and ensure a smooth transition for the students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details: Start by providing your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Provide your contact information: Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email address that you can access regularly.
3. Share your contact preferences: If you have a phone number, you can optionally provide it in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your birth details: Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Choose your program: Select your program from the list provided in the "Program" field.
6. Select your program details: If you have selected a program, please enter the name of the program in the "Program 1 name", "Program 2 name", or "Program 3 name" field, depending on the number of programs you selected.
7. Provide your admission details: Enter the date and time of your admission in the "Date of Admission" and "Time of Admission" fields.
8. Provide your address: Enter your current address in the "Address" field.
9. Provide your location details: Enter your city, zip, and state in the "City", "Zip", and "State" fields.
10. Choose your country of origin: Enter your country of origin in the "Country" field.
11. Select your department: Enter your department in the "Department" field.
12. Provide your student ID: Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field.
13. Choose your student status: Select your student status from the options provided in the "Student Status" field.
14. Choose your student type: Select your student type from the options provided in the "Student Type" field.
15. Assign to: Select the person or department you would like to assign your enrollment to from the "Assigned To" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you have one.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Program** (6, select_one, optional): Select your program from the list provided.
* **Program 1 name** (7, note, optional): Enter the name of your selected program.
* **Program 2 name** (8, note, optional): Enter the name of your selected program, if you have two programs.
* **Program 3 name** (9, note, optional): Enter the name of your selected program, if you have three programs.
* **Date of Admission** (10, date, optional): Enter the date of your admission.
* **Time of Admission** (11, time, optional): Enter the time of your admission.
* **Address** (12, text, optional): Enter your current address.
* **City** (13, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Zip** (14, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **State** (15, text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Country** (16, text, optional): Enter your country of origin.
* **Department** (17, text, optional): Enter your department.
* **Department ID** (18, text, optional): Enter your department ID.
* **Student ID** (19, text, optional): Enter your student ID.
* **Student Status** (20, text, optional): Enter your student status.
* **Student Status ID** (21, text, optional): Enter your student status ID.
* **Student Status Code** (22, text, optional): Enter your student status code.
* **Student Status Description** (23, text, optional): Enter your student status description.
* **Student Type** (24, select_multiple, optional): Select your student type from the options provided.
* **Assigned To** (25, select_one, optional): Select the person or department you would like to assign your enrollment to.
