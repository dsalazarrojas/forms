# Patient Centered Technology Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about participants in patient-centered technology collaboration projects, including their interests, roles, and areas of focus.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant name and email.
2. Select your participant's interests from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose the technology partnership type (if multiple options are selected, separate with commas).
4. Select your role as a participant.
5. If applicable, provide a brief description of your focus area for the technology partnership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as a participant in the project.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you about project updates and opportunities.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you wish to be contacted by phone).
* **Participant's Interests** (`participant_interests`, select_one, required): Select one of the following interests:
	+ Technology Development
	+ Technology Research
	+ Technology Implementation
* **Technology Partnership** (`technology_partnership`, select_multiple, required): Choose the type(s) of technology partnership(s) you're interested in (separate with commas):
	+ Patient-Centered Technology
	+ Technology Development
	+ Technology Implementation
* **Technology Focus Area** (`technology_focus_area`, text, optional): Describe a brief area of focus for your technology partnership.
* **Healthcare Team** (`healthcare_team`, text, optional): Describe a brief healthcare team or team member you're working with.
* **Researcher** (`researcher`, select_one, required): Select your role as a participant:
	+ Researcher
	+ Patient Advocate
	+ Healthcare Provider
* **Patient Advocate** (`patient_advocate`, select_one, required): Select your role as a participant:
	+ Patient Advocate
	+ Researcher
	+ Healthcare Provider
* **Technology Partner** (`technology_partner`, text, optional): Describe a brief technology partner you're working with.
* **Partnership Focus Area** (`partnership_focus_area`, text, optional): Describe a brief focus area for your partnership.
