# Airbnb Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for booking guests to provide essential information about their stay at an Airbnb accommodation. It helps hosts prepare for their arrival and ensures a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Click on the form link provided by your host.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* Primary Guest Name
	* Email Address
	* Mobile Phone
	* Check-In Date
	* Check-Out Date
	* Estimated Arrival Time
	* Number of Adults
3. If applicable, fill out additional fields:
	* Number of Children (if under 18)
	* Special Requests (e.g., Crib, Extra Towels)
	* Pet Information (if pets are allowed)
4. Read and select the House Rules Agreement.
5. Sign your name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Primary Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the guest who is booking the accommodation.
- **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the guest.
- **Mobile Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the guest's mobile phone number for contact during their stay.
- **Check-In Date** (`check_in_date`, `date`, required): Select the date the guest will be arriving at the accommodation.
- **Check-Out Date** (`check_out_date`, `date`, required): Select the date the guest will be departing from the accommodation.
- **Estimated Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time the guest is expected to arrive at the accommodation.
- **Number of Adults** (`num_adults`, `number`, required): Enter the number of adult guests that will be staying at the accommodation.
- **Number of Children** (`num_children`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of children (under 18) that will be staying at the accommodation. (If not applicable, please leave this field blank.)
- **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, optional): List any specific requests or requirements for the stay, such as Crib or Extra Towels.
- **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant information about pets that will be staying at the accommodation. (If pets are not allowed, please indicate so.)
- **ID Verification** (`id_upload_note`, `note`, optional): Please note that you will need to send a photo of your ID to your host via the app.
- **House Rules Agreement** (`house_rules`, `select_one`, required): Select the option that best describes your agreement with the house rules.
- **Signature** (`signature`, `text`, required): Sign your name to confirm your agreement with the house rules.
