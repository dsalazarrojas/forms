# player_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The player registration form is designed to collect player information for registration purposes. This form will help administrators collect player details such as name, contact information, and other relevant details for player profiles. To ensure accurate and complete data, please review each field carefully and provide accurate information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*). If a field is not marked, it is not required.
2. For fields with a type of "select_one" or "select_multiple", please select one or multiple options from the provided list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required: true): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents such as your passport or driver's license.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Address** (4, text, required: false): Enter your current address.
* **Email** (5, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (6, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Role** (7, select_one, required: false):
	+ Select "Coach" if you are a coach.
	+ Select "Player" if you are a player.
	+ Select "Staff" if you are a staff member.
* **Team** (8, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all teams you are associated with.
* **Emergency Contact** (9, text, required: false): Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (10, text, required: false): Enter your emergency contact person's phone number.
