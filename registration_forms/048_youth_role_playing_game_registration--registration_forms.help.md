# Youth Role Playing Game Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Youth Role Playing Game Registration form is designed to collect information from participants in a youth role playing game, including their session details, medical conditions, and special needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your session from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter the participant's name.
3. Enter the participant's age.
4. Enter the participant's parent's name.
5. Enter the parent's contact number.
6. Enter the participant's email address (if available).
7. Enter the participant's address (if applicable).
8. Enter the emergency contact's name and number (if different from the parent's).
9. Provide any additional player details in the text field.
10. Select any special needs the participant may have, such as wheelchair accessibility or hearing assistance.
11. Enter any medical conditions the participant may have.
12. Enter any medications the participant is taking.
13. Enter any allergies the participant may have.
14. Provide any additional medical condition notes.
15. Enter the participant's medical treatment details.
16. Enter any medical test details (if applicable).
17. Select any additional special needs the participant may have.
18. Enter any medical therapy details and dosage.
19. Enter the frequency of medical therapy.
20. Enter any medical condition treatment details.
21. Enter any medical test result details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session** (`Session 1`, `select_one`, required): Select the session you want to register for from the dropdown menu.
* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Age** (`Participant Age`, `number`, required): Enter the age of the participant.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the participant's parent or guardian.
* **Parent Contact Number** (`Parent Contact Number`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's or guardian's contact number.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter the participant's email address (if available).
* **Address** (`Address`, `text`, optional): Enter the participant's address (if applicable).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact (if different from the parent's).
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`Emergency Contact Number`, `text`, optional): Enter the emergency contact's number (if different from the parent's).
* **Player Details** (`Player Details`, `note`, optional): Provide any additional information about the participant.
* **Participant Special Needs** (`Participant Special Needs`, `select_multiple`, required): Select any special needs the participant may have, such as wheelchair accessibility or hearing assistance.
* **Participant Medical Condition** (`Participant Medical Condition`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical conditions the participant may have.
* **Participant Medication** (`Participant Medication`, `text`, optional): Enter any medications the participant is taking.
* **Participant Allergies** (`Participant Allergies`, `text`, optional): Enter any allergies the participant may have.
* **Participant Medical Conditions Notes** (`Participant Medical Conditions Notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional medical condition notes.
* **Participant Medical Treatment** (`Participant Medical Treatment`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical treatment details.
* **Participant Medical Test** (`Participant Medical Test`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical test details.
* **Participant Special Needs 2** (`Participant Special Needs 2`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any additional special needs the participant may have.
* **Participant Medical Therapy** (`Participant Medical Therapy`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical therapy details.
* **Participant Medical Therapy Type** (`Participant Medical Therapy Type`, `text`, optional): Enter the type of medical therapy.
* **Participant Medical Therapy Dosage** (`Participant Medical Therapy Dosage`, `text`, optional): Enter the dosage of medical therapy.
* **Participant Medical Therapy Frequency** (`Participant Medical Therapy Frequency`, `text`, optional): Enter the frequency of medical therapy.
* **Participant Medical Condition Treatment** (`Participant Medical Condition Treatment`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical condition treatment details.
* **Participant Medical Test Treatment** (`Participant Medical Test Treatment`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical test treatment details.
* **Participant Medical Test Result** (`Participant Medical Test Result`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical test results.
