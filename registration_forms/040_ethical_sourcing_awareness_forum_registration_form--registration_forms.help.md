# Ethical Sourcing Awareness Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Sourcing Awareness Forum is a platform for individuals interested in learning about and discussing ethical sourcing practices. By registering on this form, you will be granted access to the forum, where you can engage with others, share your knowledge, and participate in discussions on ethical sourcing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone (if provided) in the appropriate fields.
- If you are representing a company, provide your Company name, Company Title, Department, Job Title, Job Description, Job Industry, Job Function, Location, and Company Size.
- You can skip any field that is not relevant to your registration.
- Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please enter your work phone number (if you have one).
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the name of the company.
* **Company Title** (`company_title`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your title within the company.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the department you belong to.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your job title within the company.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter a brief description of your job responsibilities.
* **Job Industry** (`job_industry`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the industry your job belongs to.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter your job function within the company.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the location of your company or office.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter a brief description of the company size.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you are having trouble completing any field, you can contact the forum administrators for assistance.
- You can change your registration information at any time by logging in to your account and updating your profile.
