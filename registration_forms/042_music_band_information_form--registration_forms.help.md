# music_bands_information_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The music_bands_information_form is designed to collect general information about music bands. This includes their name, social media presence, venue preferences, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the name of the music band in the `music_bands_general_info` field.
2. Select the social media platforms the band is present on in the `music_bands_social_media` field.
3. Provide the venue preferences for the band in the `music_bands_venue_preferences` field. Note that there are multiple fields for this section, each with a label "venue_1", "venue_2", etc.
4. Add any additional contact information for the band in the `music_bands_additional_contacts` field. This field is a select_multiple field, and you can choose from Email, Phone, or Other.
5. If you selected "Other" in the `music_bands_additional_contacts` field, provide more information in the corresponding field (e.g., `music_bands_additional_contacts_1` to `music_bands_additional_contacts_10`).
6. Provide the primary contact information for the band in the `music_bands_primary_contact` field. Note that this field is a text field.
7. If you are providing additional contact information, enter it in the corresponding fields (e.g., `music_bands_additional_contacts_1` to `music_bands_additional_contacts_10`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **music_bands_general_info** (Text): Enter the name of the music band.
* **music_bands_social_media** (Select Multiple): Select the social media platforms the band is present on (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, Twitter).
* **music_bands_venue_preferences** (Text): Enter the music band's venue preferences.
* **venue_1** (Text): Enter the first venue preference.
* **venue_2** (Text): Enter the second venue preference.
* **venue_3** (Text): Enter the third venue preference.
* **venue_4** (Text): Enter the fourth venue preference.
* **venue_5** (Text): Enter the fifth venue preference.
* **music_bands_contact_info** (Text): Enter any additional contact information for the band.
* **primary_contact** (Text): Enter the primary contact name for the band.
* **primary_contact_email** (Email): Enter the primary contact email.
* **primary_contact_phone** (Text): Enter the primary contact phone number.
* **additional_contacts** (Select Multiple): Select the type of additional contact information you want to provide (e.g., Email, Phone, Other).
* **contact_1** (Text): Enter the additional contact information if you selected "Email" or "Phone" (or other option you selected).
* ... **contact_6** (Text): Enter any additional contact information.
* **contact_10** (Text): Enter any additional contact information.
* **contact_11** (Text): Enter any additional contact information.
* **contact_12** (Text): Enter any additional contact information.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure that the band's presence is correctly represented.
* If you need help or have questions, contact us.
* Please note that this form has been simplified, and some fields have been removed or reorganized to improve user experience. If you have any suggestions or need further assistance, please let us know.
