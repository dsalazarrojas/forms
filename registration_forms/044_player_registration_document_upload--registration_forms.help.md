# Player Registration & Document Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering players and uploading documents for them to participate in games or events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the Player Details section with your player information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
2. Upload any required documents for your participation in the event.
3. Once you have filled out all the required information, click on the Submit button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Player Details** (`player_details`): Fill out this section with your player information, including your email and phone number (if applicable).
  - **Email** (`email`): Enter your player's email.
  - **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your player's phone number.
  - **Player Number** (`player_number`): Enter your player's unique number, if applicable.
- **Document Upload** (`document_upload`): Upload any required documents for your participation in the event. Make sure to check the document upload section for further instructions on what documents are required.
  - **Document Name** (`document_name`): Enter a name for your uploaded document, which will be used for identification purposes.
- **Submit** (`submit`): Click on this button once you have filled out all the required information to submit your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely to ensure successful registration.
- Upload required documents according to the instructions provided in the document upload section.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the event administrators for assistance.
