# Developers Conference Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for developers and other individuals to register for the Developers Conference. It collects essential information to help us understand who's attending, their preferences, and other important details.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each section carefully and fill in the required information.
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (marked as required in the form).
- For fields that allow multiple selections, you can select one or more options that apply to you.
- Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Name**: The name of the conference you'll be attending. Enter the name of the conference you've registered for (e.g., "Developers Conference").
* **Email**: Your email address where you can be contacted. Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number. This will be used for any further communication or updates.
* **Attendee Type**: Please select your role at the conference. You can choose from "Developers", "Organizer", or "Sponsor".
* **Preferences**: Select one or more options that apply to you regarding meals and social events. You can choose from "Breakfast", "Lunch", "Dinner", and "Social Event".
* **Arrival Date**: The date you plan to arrive at the conference. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Arrival Time**: The time you plan to arrive at the conference. Use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Notes**: Any additional information you'd like to share with us. This is an optional field.
