# digital_booking_platform_partner_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a new partner on the digital booking platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Provide information about yourself as the representative of the business (User Information)
2. Enter your business details, including the information about your services offered (Business Details)
3. Select the services you offer (Service Offerings)
4. Enter your business address
5. Provide your contact person's information
6. Enter your email address
7. Enter your phone number
8. Add any additional notes about your business or partnership

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself as the representative of the business.
* **Business Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your business and its activities.
* **Service Offerings** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select all the services your business offers. Please note that you must select at least one service.
* **Business Address** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your business address, including the street address, city, state, and postal code.
* **Contact Person** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name and contact information of the person who will be the main point of contact for your business.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`8`, `note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your business or partnership.
