# Music Therapy Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Therapy Intake Form is used to collect essential information from patients or their legal representatives for music therapy sessions. This form helps us understand your needs and create a personalized treatment plan for your music therapy journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your contact information**: Enter your name, email address, and phone number in the designated fields.
2. **Share your contact details**: Enter your name and any relevant contact information.
3. **Describe your condition diagnosis**: Share a brief description of your medical condition or diagnosis.
4. **Share your medical history**: Enter any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your music therapy sessions.
5. **Set your treatment goals**: Clearly state your goals for the music therapy sessions.
6. **Schedule your contact date**: Select a date and time for the music therapy sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information** (music_therapy_form): This field is used to collect your contact information, including your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Contact Name** (contact_name): Enter your name, either as the patient or your representative.
* **Email** (contact_email): Enter your email address, where you can be contacted for music therapy updates.
* **Phone** (contact_phone): Enter your phone number, where you can be reached for music therapy sessions.
* **Condition Diagnosis** (condition_diagnosis): Describe your medical condition or diagnosis, such as a physical or mental health issue.
* **Medical History** (medical_history): Share any relevant medical history that may be helpful for your music therapy sessions.
* **Treatment Goals** (treatment_goals): Clearly state your goals for the music therapy sessions, such as reducing stress or improving mood.
* **Contact Date** (contact_date): Schedule a date for your music therapy sessions, choosing a date and time that works best for you.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and honest information to ensure the best possible outcome for your music therapy sessions.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will use the information collected from this form to create a personalized treatment plan for your music therapy sessions.
