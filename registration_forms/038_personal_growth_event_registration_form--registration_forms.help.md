# Personal Growth Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Growth Event Registration Form is designed for attendees to provide their information and register for a personal growth event. This form is used to collect basic information about the attendees, such as their name, email, phone, and registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (none are marked as required, but it is recommended to fill in as much information as possible).
2. Select the correct payment method (if available).
3. Add any additional information you think is relevant.
4. Review your input for accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name (attendee_name)**: Enter your full name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you about the event.
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you about the event.
* **Registration Date (registration_date)**: Enter the date you would like to register for the event.
* **Event Time (event_time)**: Enter the time you would like to attend the event.
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select the method you would like to use to pay for the event. Options include Card, Cash, and PayPal.
* **Additional Info (additional_info)**: Enter any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.
* **First Name (first_name)**: Enter your first name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Last Name (last_name)**: Enter your last name as you would like it to appear in our records.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in as much information as possible to ensure accurate records.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* Review your input carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
