# Athletic Scholarship Tryout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from athletes seeking to participate in a tryout session for a scholarship. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure your application is considered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your personal details in the required fields.
2.  Select your preferred tryout date.
3.  Choose the type of scholarship you are seeking.
4.  Answer questions about your athletic history, including your sport, position, and years of experience.
5.  Provide any relevant medical clearance information.
6.  Enter your parent/guardian's contact information (if under 18).
7.  Review and agree to the liability waiver and medical treatment permission.
8.  Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Athlete Full Name** (`athlete_name`, `text`, `required`): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`): Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your gender from the provided options.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, `required`): Enter your home address.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, `required`): Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State** (`state`, `text`, `required`): Enter your state or province.
*   **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, `required`): Enter your ZIP or postal code.
*   **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your current school.
*   **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your current grade level from the provided options.
*   **Current GPA** (`gpa`, `number`, `required`): Enter your current grade point average.
*   **Sport for Tryout** (`sport`, `select_one`, `required`): Select the primary sport you will be trying out for.
*   **Primary Position** (`position`, `text`, `required`): Enter the main position you play in your sport.
*   **Height** (`height`, `text`, `required`): Enter your height in feet and inches or centimeters.
*   **Weight** (`weight`, `number`, `required`): Enter your weight in pounds or kilograms.
*   **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, `required`): Enter how many years you have played your sport.
*   **Current Team** (`current_team`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your current school or club team.
*   **Coach Name** (`coach_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your current coach.
*   **Coach Contact** (`coach_contact`, `text`, `optional`): Enter your coach's contact information (if applicable).
*   **Athletic Achievements** (`athletic_achievements`, `text`, `required`): List any notable awards or honors you have received.
*   **Personal Records or Stats** (`personal_records`, `text`, `optional`): Enter any relevant personal records or statistics.
*   **Tryout Date Preference** (`tryout_date_preference`, `select_one`, `required`): Select your preferred tryout date.
*   **Scholarship Type Seeking** (`scholarship_type`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose the type of scholarship you are seeking.
*   **Financial Need Statement** (`financial_need`, `text`, `optional`): Briefly describe your financial need if applicable.
*   **Injury History** (`injury_history`, `text`, `required`): List any significant injuries you have experienced.
*   **Current Injuries or Limitations** (`current_injuries`, `text`, `required`): Enter any current injuries or limitations you have.
*   **Medical Clearance Status** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have been fully cleared or have restrictions.
*   **Health Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, `required`): Enter the name of your health insurance company.
*   **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, `text`, `required`): Enter your insurance policy or member number.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, `required`): Enter your parent or guardian's full name (if under 18).
*   **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, `required`): Enter your parent or guardian's contact phone number.
*   **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your parent or guardian's contact email address.
*   **Tryout Fee** (`tryout_fee`, `number`, `required`): Enter the amount you will pay as a tryout fee.
*   **Fee Waiver Needed** (`fee_waiver_needed`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you need a fee waiver.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, `optional`): Select the method you will use to pay the tryout fee.
*   **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you agree to the liability waiver.
*   **Medical Treatment Permission** (`medical_treatment_permission`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you grant permission for medical treatment.
*   **Athlete Signature** (`athlete_signature`, `text`, `required`): Enter your full name as a signature.
*   **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, `text`, `required` if under 18`): Enter your parent or guardian's full name as a signature if under 18.
*   **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, `required`): Enter the date of your signature.

## Tips

*   Be accurate and thorough in filling out the form to ensure your application is considered.
*   Review each field carefully to ensure you have answered correctly.
*   If you need help or have questions, contact your coach or school administrator.
*   Ensure you understand the terms of the liability waiver and medical treatment permission before signing.
