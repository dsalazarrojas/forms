# PickleballDoublesTournamentRegistrationForm - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pickleball Doubles Tournament Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from teams participating in a Pickleball Doubles tournament. The form requires team members to provide their details, including name, contact information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your team's name in the "Team Name" field.
2. Select the correct "Team Type" for your team from the provided options (Team or Non-Team).
3. Enter your team members' names in the "Team Members" field, separated by commas.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the correct event date and time from the provided options.
7. If necessary, add any additional notes in the "Note" field.
8. Review and submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
  - **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter your team's name.
  - **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if your team has members, "No" otherwise.
  - **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
  - **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
  - **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your team.
  - **Team Type** (`team_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Team" or "Non-Team" for your team type.
  - **Team Type** (`team_type_label`, text, optional): Enter a label for your team type.
  - **Team Members Label** (`team_members_label`, text, optional): Enter a label for your team members.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
* Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the tournament organizers.
