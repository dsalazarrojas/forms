# online_art_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for an online art class. To register, fill out the form with the required and optional fields as indicated below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields.
*   Enter your email address in the Email field.
*   Provide your phone number in the Phone Number field.
*   Select your chosen course from the course selection options.
*   Choose your preferred course date from the available dates.
*   Select your preferred payment method (cash, card, or transfer).
*   Enter the amount paid for the selected course.
*   Choose your student level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
*   Select your preferred art medium (drawing, painting, printmaking, or mixed media).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where the instructor will contact you for course updates.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number where you can be reached for course updates and inquiries.
*   **Select a Course** (`course_selection`, select_one, required): Choose one of the available courses listed in the options.
*   **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, required): Choose the date you wish to enroll in the selected course.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select a payment method that best suits your preference (cash, card, or transfer).
*   **Amount Paid** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the exact amount you paid for the selected course.
*   **Student Level** (`student_level`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more levels that best describe your art skill level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
*   **Art Medium** (`art_medium`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more art mediums that interest you (drawing, painting, printmaking, or mixed media).

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled out to successfully submit the form.
