> # product-line-reveal-rsvp-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVP information for events and product lines. Please use it to register your interest or confirm your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event you are interested in.
2. Select a date for the event (if applicable).
3. Choose a time for the event (if applicable).
4. Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event-name** (`event_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **date** (`date`, date, not required): If applicable, select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **time** (`time`, time, not required): If applicable, choose the time of the event.
* **email** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address for confirmation and follow-up purposes.
