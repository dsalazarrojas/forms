# Legal Compliace Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users about their legal compliance awareness, specifically regarding their attendance to a legal compliance session. It is not a mandatory form and is used for awareness and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Form field with your name, title, or any other information you feel is relevant.
2. Provide your Contact Details in the Contact Details field.
3. Indicate whether you attended the Compliance Session by selecting the correct option in the Compliance Session field.
4. Enter the date of the event in the Event Date field in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
5. If applicable, enter the end date of the event in the End Date field.
6. Enter the Session Time in the Session Time field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (1, text, required): Please enter your name, title, or any other relevant information to help us identify you.
* **Contact Details** (2, text, optional): Provide your contact information (e.g., email, phone number, or email address) so we can contact you for further questions or updates.
* **Compliance Session** (3, select_one, optional): Indicate if you attended or did not attend the compliance session by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Event Date** (4, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/2023).
* **End Date** (5, date, optional): If applicable, enter the end date of the event in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/31/2023).
* **Session Time** (6, time, optional): Enter the time of the session in 24-hour format (e.g., 08:00).
