# Adventure Guide Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for potential candidates to provide their details in order to be considered for an Adventure Guide position. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Ensure you have filled out all required fields (denoted by `*`).
- Enter your full name, years of guiding experience, and availability for shifts as requested.
- Provide details about your professional certifications, references, and emergency contact information.
- Consent to a background check, if required.
- Sign with the date of application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Candidate full name** (`candidate_name`, `text`, `*`): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Years of guiding experience** (`experience_years`, `number`, `*`): Enter the number of years you have been guiding. Please use whole numbers only.
* **Relevant certifications** (`certifications`, `text`, `optional`): If you have any relevant certifications, such as Wilderness First Aid or guiding licenses, please list them here.
* **Availability and preferred shifts** (`availability`, `text`, `*`): Enter your preferred days and times of availability for shifts.
* **Professional references** (`references`, `text`, `*`): Provide two professional references with their names, titles, and contact information. This will help us verify your experience and qualifications.
* **Consent to background check** (`background_check_consent`, `select_one`, `*`): You will be required to undergo a background check as part of the hiring process. Please confirm your consent.
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, `*`): Please provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact. This will be used in case we need to contact someone for you.
* **Signature date** (`signature_date`, `date`, `*`): Please enter the date you signed this application.
