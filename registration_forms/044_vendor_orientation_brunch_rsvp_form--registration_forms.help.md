# Vendor Orientation Brunch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for vendors to RSVP to our vendor orientation brunch. By filling out this form, you will inform us of your intention to attend or decline the event. This information will help us prepare for the number of attendees and make necessary arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the step-by-step instructions below:

1. Fill in your First Name in the first field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Provide your Email in the third field.
4. In the fourth field, select either 'RSVP Yes' or 'RSVP No' to indicate your intention to attend the brunch.
5. If you have any guests attending with you, enter the number in the 'Guest Count' field.
6. If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, type them in the 'Comments' field.
7. Finally, list any dietary restrictions you may have in the 'Dietary Restrictions' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents or identification.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address associated with your official contact information.
* **RSVP** (Select One, Required): Select 'RSVP Yes' to indicate your intention to attend the brunch or 'RSVP No' to decline.
* **Guest Count** (Number, Optional): If you are bringing guests with you, enter the number of people in your party.
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or requests you have, such as special requests for seating or accommodations.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Text, Optional): List any dietary restrictions or special requests you may have, such as vegan or gluten-free options.
