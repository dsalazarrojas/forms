# road_race_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is intended for participants registering for the road race event. It will walk you through each field of the road race registration form, explaining their purpose and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Registration Details** section (page 1) with your personal details.
2. Provide the required information for **Participants** (page 2).
3. Fill out the **Participant Details** sections with your personal and event-related information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (page 1): Enter your personal details to register for the event.
  * **Name** (participant_details_1_name): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  * **Email** (participant_details_1_email): Enter your email address for future communication.
  * **Phone** (participant_details_1_phone): Enter your contact phone number for communication purposes.
  * **Address** (participant_details_1_address): Enter your residential address.
  * **City** (participant_details_1_city): Enter the city you reside in.
  * **Country** (participant_details_1_country): Enter your country of residence.
  * **Postal Code** (participant_details_1_postal_code): Enter your postal code or zip code.
  * **Event Details** (participant_details_1_event_details): Briefly describe the event you are participating in.
  * **Additional Details** (participant_details_1_additional_details): Provide any additional information relevant to your participation.
  * **Agreed** (participant_details_1_agreed): Indicate that you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the event.
  * **Prophets** (participant_details_1_prophets): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
  * **Date of Birth** (participant_details_1_date_of_birth): Enter your date of birth.
  * **Time of Event** (participant_details_1_time_of_event): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
  * **Start Date** (participant_details_1_start_date): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
  * **Event Details 2** (participant_details_1_event_details_2): Provide additional event details if required.
  * **Additional Details 2** (participant_details_1_additional_details_2): Provide any additional information relevant to your participation.
  * **Participant** (participant): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
  * **Event Details 3** (participant_details_1_event_details_3): Provide details about the event you are participating in.
  * **Additional Details 3** (participant_details_1_additional_details_3): Provide any additional information relevant to your participation.
  * **Agreed 2** (participant_details_1_agreed_2): Indicate that you have agreed to the terms and conditions of the event.
  * **Date of Event** (participant_details_1_date_of_event): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
  * **Time of Event 2** (participant_details_1_time_of_event_2): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
  * **Start Date 2** (participant_details_1_start_date_2): Not applicable (no explanation provided).
