# Fitness Boot Camp Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Boot Camp Registration form is used to register new members for the program. It collects information such as member details, membership status, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Member ID.
5. Fill in your Phone number (optional).
6. Enter your Date of Birth.
7. Enter your Join Date.
8. Select your Membership Type (Month-to-Month, Month-to-Month with Auto Renewal, or Month-to-Month No Renewal).
9. Select your Membership Level (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
10. Select your Membership Term (1 month, 6 months, or 12 months).
11. Select your Payment Method (Pay Pal or Pay Pal/Stripe).
12. Check your Payment Status (Pending, Success, or Failed).
13. Enter your Payment Amount (optional).
14. Enter your Payment Date.
15. Enter any additional Notes (optional).
16. Select your Membership Status (Active, Suspended, or Inactive).
17. Select your Billing Status (Active or Inactive).
18. Select your Membership Level 2 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
19. Select your Billing Status 2 (Active or Inactive).
20. Select your Membership Level 3 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
21. Select your Billing Status 3 (Active or Inactive).
22. Select your Membership Level 4 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
23. Select your Billing Status 4 (Active or Inactive).
24. Select your Membership Level 5 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
25. Select your Billing Status 5 (Active or Inactive).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Member ID** (4, number, required): Enter your member ID.
* **Phone** (5, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Join Date** (7, date, required): Enter the date you joined the program.
* **Join Time** (8, time, required): Enter the time you joined the program in the format 00:00 or 12:00.
* **Membership Type** (9, select_multiple, required): Select your membership type (Month-to-Month, Month-to-Month with Auto Renewal, or Month-to-Month No Renewal).
* **Membership Level** (10, select_one, required): Select your membership level (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
* **Membership Term** (11, select_one, required): Select your membership term (1 month, 6 months, or 12 months).
* **Payment Method** (12, select_multiple, required): Select your payment method (Pay Pal or Pay Pal/Stripe).
* **Payment Status** (13, select_multiple, required): Select your payment status (Pending, Success, or Failed).
* **Payment Amount** (14, number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (15, date, required): Enter the payment date.
* **Notes** (16, note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
* **Membership Status** (17, select_multiple, required): Select your membership status (Active, Suspended, or Inactive).
* **Billing Status** (18, select_multiple, required): Select your billing status (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Level 2** (19, select_multiple, optional): Select your membership level 2 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
* **Billing Status 2** (20, select_multiple, optional): Select your billing status 2 (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Level 3** (21, select_multiple, optional): Select your membership level 3 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
* **Billing Status 3** (22, select_multiple, optional): Select your billing status 3 (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Level 4** (23, select_multiple, optional): Select your membership level 4 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
* **Billing Status 4** (24, select_multiple, optional): Select your billing status 4 (Active or Inactive).
* **Membership Level 5** (25, select_multiple, optional): Select your membership level 5 (Basic, Premium, Enterprise, or Pro).
* **Billing Status 5** (26, select_multiple, optional): Select your billing status 5 (Active or Inactive).

Note: Some fields are optional, but they might still be required for certain membership types or levels. Make sure to check the specific requirements for your membership.
