# Winter STEM Club Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Winter STEM Club Registration form is designed to collect information from students and their families for the Winter STEM Club event. It is crucial to provide accurate and up-to-date information for the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select the Session Date for which you are registering in the "Session Date" field.
7. Choose the Time of Day for your session in the "Time of Day" field.
8. Enter your Student ID in the "Student ID" field.
9. Enter your Parent's Name in the "Parent Name" field.
10. Enter your School Name in the "School Name" field.
11. Write any additional participant details in the "Participant Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your name, which will help the club organizers recognize you at the event.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): This is your surname.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter your active email address so we can contact you for updates and information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number in the format (123) 456-7890.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Select your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date of the session you wish to attend.
- **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required: false): Select the time you plan to attend in the format 12:00 PM.
- **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required: false): Enter your student ID if you have one.
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required: false): Enter your parent's name if you are a student.
- **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your school if applicable.
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, note, required: false): Add any additional details about yourself or your participant.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled for the form to be submitted successfully.
- Make sure to use the correct format for dates (MM/DD/YYYY) and times (12:00 PM).
- If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the Winter STEM Club organizers for assistance.
