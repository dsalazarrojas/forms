# Chartered Flight Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for booking chartered flights. Please fill in your information to reserve a flight.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your passenger information in the "Passenger Information" section.
2. Provide flight details in the "Flight Details" section.
3. Specify your reservation date and preferred departure time in the "Flight Preferences" section.
4. Add any special requests or accommodations for your flight in the "Flight Preferences" section.
5. Review all fields for accuracy before submitting your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or government-issued ID.
* **Email Address**: This is the email address we will use for flight confirmation and updates. Please ensure it's correct.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach you.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Passport Number** (optional): If traveling internationally, please enter your passport number.
* **Departure Date**: Choose the date you wish to depart.
* **Return Date**: Select the date you plan to return.
* **Departure City**: Enter the city where you'll be flying from.
* **Destination City**: Enter the city where you'll be flying to.
* **Preferred Departure Time**: Choose your preferred departure time (Early Morning, Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Night).
* **Total Number of Passengers**: Enter the total number of people traveling with you.
* **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children (ages 2-12) traveling with you.
* **Number of Infants**: Enter the number of infants (under 2 years old) traveling with you.
* **Seat Preference**: Choose your preferred seat type (Window, Aisle, Middle, or No Preference).
* **Meal Preference**: Please describe any dietary requirements or preferences you have.
* **Special Requests or Accommodations**: List any special requirements or accommodations you need for your flight (e.g., wheelchair assistance or service animals).
* **Loyalty Program Number** (optional): If you have a loyalty program, please enter your member number.
* **Reservation Date**: This is today's date for reference; please do not change it.

This help guide explains each field clearly, reducing the risk of confusion and ensuring users complete the form correctly. It's essential to review and test the form with users to gather feedback and make adjustments as needed.
