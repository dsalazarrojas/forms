# Form For Parents With Babies - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Form For Parents With Babies" is a registration form designed to collect essential information from parents with babies. This form ensures that the necessary details are gathered accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form fields with the required information.
2. Ensure that all the required fields (Parents Name, Age, Address, Zip Code, Birth Date, Phone, Email, Homeless Status, Income, and Assistance Type) are filled out accurately.
3. Choose the correct options for the Homeless Status and Assistance Type fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parents Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as a parent.
* **Age** (Number, Required): Enter your age.
* **Address** (Text, Required): Enter your home address.
* **Zip Code** (Number, Required): Enter your zip code.
* **Birth Date** (Date, Required): Enter your baby's birthdate.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Homeless Status** (Select One, Required): Choose 'Yes' if you are currently homeless, 'No' otherwise.
* **Income** (Number, Required): Enter your income.
* **Assistance Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose the types of assistance you are interested in (Food, Shelter, Both, None).
* **Comments** (Text, Optional): This field is for additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately to avoid any processing issues.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for further assistance.
* This form is for registration purposes only and will not be used for any other purposes.
