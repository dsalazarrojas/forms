# visitor_liability_waiver - Help Guide
## Purpose
The visitor liability waiver is a legal document that protects the business and its representatives from potential claims or liabilities arising from a visitor's presence on the premises.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, including all required fields.
2. Review the waiver of liability carefully, selecting all applicable options.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **visitor_name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **company_name** (Text, Optional): If you are visiting on behalf of a company, enter the name of your company.
* **contact_number** (Text, Optional): Provide a contact number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **visitor_email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **date_of_birth** (Date, Optional): If you are under 18 years old, please provide your date of birth.
* **emergency_contact_name** (Text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, enter their name.
* **emergency_contact_number** (Text, Required): Enter the contact number of your emergency contact person.
* **emergency_contact_relationship** (Text, Required): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **signature** (Note, Optional): This is a text field for any additional comments or notes.
* **acknowledgement_of_terms** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Acknowledge' if you have read and understand the terms and conditions.
* **waiver_of_liability** (Select Multiple, Required): Select all applicable options to waive liability:
	+ I have read, understand, and sign below.
	+ I, the undersigned, do hereby acknowledge, that I am aware of the risks associated with visiting the premises.
	+ I, the undersigned, do hereby agree to hold harmless the business, and its officers, agents, and employees from any and all claims.
* **visitor_signature** (Text, Required): Provide your signature to confirm your understanding and agreement.

Note that some fields are optional, but all required fields must be completed before submission. 

## Tips
* Please review the form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for clarification or assistance.
* Completion of this form is mandatory for all visitors to the premises.
