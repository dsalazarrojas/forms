# Content Creation Masterclass Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Content Creation Masterclass Registration Form is a form designed to collect registration information from participants for the masterclass. The form gathers essential details such as contact information, preferred masterclass session, payment details, dietary preferences, and any additional requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your participant details including your name, email, and phone number. These fields are mandatory and are necessary for us to contact you and provide updates about the masterclass.
2. Select the preferred masterclass session you'd like to attend. Please choose one session from the provided options.
3. Provide your payment details. This includes the registration fee payment amount, which is mandatory, and the preferred payment method. You can choose to pay by credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
4. If you have any specific dietary restrictions or requirements, please select them from the options provided.
5. If you have any additional requirements or accessibility needs, please specify them in the text field.
6. Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
7. Confirm that you agree with the registration terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please provide your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): This is the email address we will use to contact you about the masterclass.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Your phone number will be used for communication and updates.
* **Preferred Masterclass Session** (`masterclass_session_selection`, select_one, required): Choose one session from the options provided. This will determine which masterclass you will attend.
* **Registration Fee Payment Amount** (`registration_fee_payment`, number, required): This is the payment amount for the masterclass. Please enter the full amount.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method. You can select credit card, PayPal, or bank transfer.
* **Specific Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): If you have specific dietary restrictions or allergies, please select the relevant options.
* **Any additional requirements or accessibility needs?** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any additional requirements or accessibility needs, please specify them here.
* **Confirmation** (`signature_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm that the information you provided is accurate.
* **Registration Completed?** (`registration_completed`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have completed the form accurately.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`, date, required): Please enter the date you completed this form.
