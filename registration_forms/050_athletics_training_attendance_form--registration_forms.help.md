# Athletics Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to track athlete attendance and participation in training sessions, providing a record of their presence, status, and session details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your full name and student or team identification number (if applicable).
2. Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
3. Select your grade level and primary sport.
4. Choose your team level.
5. Enter the date and time of the training session.
6. Select the type of training session you participated in.
7. Indicate your attendance status and reason for absence (if applicable).
8. Enter your actual arrival and departure times (if late or departed early).
9. Select your injury status and describe any injuries.
10. Rate your pain level and participation level in the session.
11. Rate your effort in the session from 1 to 10.
12. Enter the name and location of your coach or trainer.
13. Note any weather conditions (if training was outdoors).
14. Add any session notes or goals for the next session.
15. Enter any additional comments.
16. Sign with your full name.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name (1)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Athlete ID Number (2)**: If you have a student or team identification number, enter it here. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
* **Date of Birth (3)**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **School Name (4)**: Enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Grade Level (5)**: Select your current grade level.
* **Sport (6)**: Choose your primary sport.
* **Team Level (7)**: Select your team level (varsity, junior varsity, freshman, club team).
* **Training Session Date (8)**: Enter the date of the training session.
* **Training Session Time (9)**: Enter the time of the training session.
* **Training Type (10)**: Select the type of training session you participated in.
* **Attendance Status (11)**: Indicate your attendance status. If absent, select the reason and provide details if applicable.
* **Actual Arrival Time (12)**: Enter the time you arrived, if late or departed early.
* **Departure Time (13)**: Enter the time you departed, if not present for the entire session.
* **Reason for Absence (14)**: If absent, select the reason and provide details if applicable.
* **Injury Status (15)**: Select your injury status.
* **Injury Description (16)**: Describe any injuries if applicable.
* **Pain Level (17)**: Rate your pain level from 1 to 10.
* **Participation Level (18)**: Select your level of participation in the session.
* **Effort Rating (19)**: Rate your effort in the session from 1 to 10.
* **Coach or Trainer Name (20)**: Enter the name of your coach or trainer.
* **Session Location (21)**: Enter the location of the training session.
* **Weather Conditions (22)**: Note any weather conditions if training was outdoors.
* **Session Notes (23)**: Add any notes about the training session.
* **Goals for Next Session (24)**: Enter any goals you want to work on for the next session.
* **Additional Comments (25)**: Add any other information.
* **Athlete Signature (26)**: Sign with your full name.
* **Signature Date (27)**: Enter the date of your signature.
