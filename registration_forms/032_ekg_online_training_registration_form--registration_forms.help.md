# Ekg Online Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register for online courses. It will help us keep track of your details and course preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter your Email address in the third field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. (Note: There is a second "Email" field, but it's not clear what it's for. Please ignore it.)
5. Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
6. Choose a date for the course in the fifth field.
7. Choose a time for the course in the sixth field.
8. Enter a note or any other relevant information in the seventh field.
9. Enter the course code in the eighth field.
10. Enter the name of the course you wish to take in the ninth field.
11. Enter any additional session scheduling information in the tenth field.
12. Enter any other details about the participant in the eleventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like us to contact you.
* **Date** (date, optional): Choose a date for the course you wish to take.
* **Time** (time, optional): Choose a time for the course you wish to take.
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional information about your course preferences.
* **Course Code** (text, optional): Enter the course code for the course you wish to take.
* **Course** (text, optional): Enter the name of the course you wish to take.
* **Session Scheduling** (text, optional): Enter any session scheduling information.
* **Participant Details** (text, optional): Enter any additional details about the participant.

Note: The second "Email" field is not necessary and should be ignored.
