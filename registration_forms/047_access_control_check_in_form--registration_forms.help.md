# Access Control Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for access control and visitor management purposes. It is a mandatory requirement for all visitors and attendees to complete this form to ensure a smooth and secure process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information ready, including your name, email address, phone number, organization/company (if applicable), and photo identification.
* Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Choose the correct options for each field, such as your attendance type and purpose of entry.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Full Name (`attendee_full_name`)
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or identification.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This is the first field to complete. Ensure you enter your correct name accurately.

### 2. Email Address (`attendee_email`)
* **Email address**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This field is used for communication and verification purposes.

### 3. Phone Number (`attendee_phone`)
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number that we can contact you with.
* Required: No
* Explanation: This is an optional field for communication purposes.

### 4. Organization or Company (`organization`)
* **Organization or company**: Enter your organization or company name if applicable.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This field helps us track your affiliation.

### 5. Type of Attendee (`attendee_type`)
* **Type of attendee**: Choose the category that best describes your attendance.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us understand your purpose of visit.

### 6. Date of Check-in (`check_in_date`)
* **Date of check-in**: Enter the date of your scheduled check-in.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: Ensure accuracy for our record-keeping.

### 7. Time of Check-in (`check_in_time`)
* **Time of check-in**: Enter the expected time of your check-in.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us plan for your arrival.

### 8. Entry Point or Gate (`entry_point`)
* **Entry point or gate**: Choose the entrance you will be using.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us direct you to the correct area.

### 9. Host or Contact Person on Site (`host_or_contact`)
* **Host or contact person on site**: Enter the name of the person you are visiting or contact person.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us verify your visit.

### 10. Purpose of Entry (`purpose_of_entry`)
* **Purpose of entry**: Choose the reason for your visit.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us understand your visit.

### 11. Areas Authorized to Access (`authorized_areas`)
* **Areas authorized to access**: Enter the areas you are permitted to visit.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us restrict access.

### 12. Expected Check-out Time (`expected_checkout_time`)
* **Expected check-out time**: Enter the time you plan to leave.
* Required: No
* Explanation: This field is optional for planning purposes.

### 13. Equipment or Materials (`equipment_brought`)
* **Are you bringing any equipment or materials onto the premises**: Choose if you have any equipment or materials to bring.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This helps us prepare for your visit.

### 14. Equipment Description (`equipment_description`)
* **If yes, please describe the equipment**: Enter a brief description of the equipment if you are bringing any.
* Required: No
* Explanation: This field is only required if you selected "yes" in the previous field.

### 15. Photo Identification Verification (`id_verified`)
* **Photo identification has been verified**: Choose if your photo identification has been verified.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This ensures you have been properly verified.

### 16. Safety Agreement (`safety_agreement`)
* **I agree to comply with all site access control and safety policies**: Choose to agree to our site rules.
* Required: Yes
* Explanation: This is a mandatory agreement to ensure your safety and compliance.
