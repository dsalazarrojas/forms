# Student Intern Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Intern Registration Form is designed for students to provide information about their internship details. This form will help us keep track of student intern registration data accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
5. Select your enrolled program from the "Program Enrolled" dropdown menu.
6. Select the start and end dates of your internship from the "Start Date" and "End Date" fields respectively, both in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
7. Choose your student status by selecting from "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending" in the "Student Status" field.
8. Add any additional information regarding your student status in the "Student Status Note" field.
9. Confirm whether you have signed the internship agreement by selecting "True", "False", or "Pending" in the "Student Internship Agreement" field.
10. If you have signed the agreement, enter the name of the person who signed it in the "Agreement Signed By" field.
11. Enter the date when you signed the agreement in the "Signed At" field.
12. Enter the name of the program that you signed the agreement with in the "Program Signed By" field.
13. Enter the date when you signed the program agreement in the "Program Signed At" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (student_intern_registration_form, text, required)**: Enter your name as you wish to be displayed in our records.
* **Email (email, email, required)**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Contact Number (contact_number, text, required)**: Enter your contact number so we can reach you for any updates.
* **Date of Birth (date_of_birth, date, required)**: Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Program Enrolled (program_enrolled, select_one, required)**: Select the program you are enrolled in from the dropdown menu.
* **Start Date (start_date, date, required)**: Select the start date of your internship in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **End Date (end_date, date, required)**: Select the end date of your internship in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Student Status (student_status, select_multiple, required)**: Choose your student status as "Active", "Inactive", or "Pending".
* **Student Status Note (student_status_note, note, required)**: Add any additional information regarding your student status.
* **Student Internship Agreement (student_internship_agreement, select_one, required)**: Confirm whether you have signed the internship agreement by selecting "True", "False", or "Pending".
* **Agreement Signed By (agreement_signed_by, text, required)**: Enter the name of the person who signed the agreement.
* **Signed At (signed_at, date, required)**: Enter the date when you signed the agreement.
* **Program Signed By (program_signed_by, text, required)**: Enter the name of the program that you signed the agreement with.
* **Program Signed At (program_signed_at, date, required)**: Enter the date when you signed the program agreement.
* **Signed Date (signed_date, date, required)**: Enter the date when you signed the agreement.

**Tips**: Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely to avoid any inconvenience during the registration process.
