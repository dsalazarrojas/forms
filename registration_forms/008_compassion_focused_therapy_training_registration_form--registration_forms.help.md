# Compassion Focused Therapy Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Compassion Focused Therapy Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining a Compassion Focused Therapy training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Fill in your participant information completely and accurately in the required fields.
- Select your preferred session date from the dropdown options.
- If you have any prior experience with CFT, please describe it briefly in the "prior experience with CFT" field.
- If you have any accessibility requirements, please let us know so we can provide necessary accommodations.
- If you need to provide an emergency contact, please include their name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address you use for confirmation and to receive training materials.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact phone number, including the country code if you are outside the local area.
* **Organization or Employer** (`organization`, `text`, optional): If you are registering on behalf of an organization or employer, enter their name.
* **Professional Role** (`professional_role`, `text`, required): Describe your professional role (e.g., therapist, nurse, counselor).
* **Preferred Session Date** (`preferred_session`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred session date from the available options (Weekday Evening, Weekend Intensive, Online Self-paced).
* **Prior Experience with CFT** (`prior_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any prior experience you have with CFT.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, `text`, optional): Let us know if you need any accommodations.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If you need to provide an emergency contact, enter their name and phone number.
