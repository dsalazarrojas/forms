# Adventure Tour Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information and consent from participants before they embark on an adventure tour. It helps to ensure their safety and the tour's success.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read and understand the form's purpose and instructions.
2. Fill in your full name in the "Participant name" field.
3. Enter your passport or ID number in the "Passport or ID number" field, if required for international travel.
4. If you have any medical conditions or take medication, provide the necessary information in the "Medical conditions and medications" field.
5. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency contact name and phone" field.
6. Select "I agree" in the "Consent to travel terms and conditions" field to confirm you've read and understood the terms.
7. If you're consenting to photo use for promotional materials, select "True" in the "Consent to photo use" field.
8. If you have any dietary requirements, mention them in the "Dietary requirements" field.
9. Finally, sign and date the "Signature date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Tour Consent --**: This section is a note explaining the purpose and importance of the form.
* **Participant name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Passport or ID number**: Provide your passport or ID number for international travel, if required.
* **Medical conditions and medications**: If you have any medical conditions or take any medications, please list them here.
* **Emergency contact name and phone**: Provide the name and phone number of someone we can contact in case of an emergency.
* **Consent to travel terms and conditions**: Select "I agree" to confirm you've read and understood the tour's terms and conditions.
* **Consent to photo use**: Select "True" if you're consenting to photos of you being used for promotional materials.
* **Dietary requirements**: If you have any dietary requirements, please mention them here (e.g. vegetarian, gluten-free).
* **Signature date**: Sign and date the form to confirm your consent.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* Read and understand the tour's terms and conditions before selecting "I agree".
