# Visitor Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect visitor information for security and identification purposes when visiting a business or organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the First Name field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the Email Address field.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the Phone Number field.
5. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the Date of Birth field. This should be in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
6. Enter your **Date of Visit** in the Date of Visit field. This should be in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
7. Enter your **Time of Visit** in the Time of Visit field. This should be in the format of HH:MM.
8. Enter the **Reason for Visit** in the Reason for Visit field.
9. Enter the **Company Name** of your organization or business.
10. If required, enter your **Visitor ID** in the Visitor ID field.
11. Select whether or not you grant **Photo Permission**.
12. Select whether or not a **Background Check** is required.
13. Sign your name in the **Visitor Signature** field, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter your phone number. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Date of Visit** (Required): Enter your date of visit in the format of DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Time of Visit** (Required): Enter your time of visit in the format of HH:MM.
* **Reason for Visit** (Required): Enter the reason for your visit.
* **Company Name** (Required): Enter the company or organization you are visiting.
* **Visitor ID** (Optional): If you have a visitor ID, enter it here.
* **Photo Permission** (Optional): Select whether or not you grant permission for a photo to be taken.
* **Background Check** (Optional): Select whether or not a background check is required.
* **Visitor Signature** (Optional): Enter your signature if required.
* **Visitor ID** can be left blank if not required.
* **Visitor Signature** is not required for visitors under 16 years old.
