# Gym Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register yourself as a member of the gym. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure your membership is processed smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the pages of the form, and fill out each section as instructed.
2. Start with the User Info page and enter your details.
3. Move on to the Contact Info page and fill out the required contact information.
4. Proceed to the Membership page and enter any additional information required for your membership.
5. If you need to add an emergency contact, go to the Emergency Contact page and enter their name, relationship, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`username`, `text`, required: false): Enter your username, which will be used to identify you in the gym's system.
* **Contact Info** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Fill out your name and other contact information.
* **Membership** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information required for your membership, such as your membership type or payment details.
* **Emergency Contact** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`relationship`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate your relationship with the emergency contact (e.g., Mom, Dad, Brother, Sister, Friend, Other).
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.

**Tips**

* Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing your membership.
* If you need assistance with completing this form, please reach out to the administration team.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure it's correct.
