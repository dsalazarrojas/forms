# Underfloor Heating Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users interested in attending underfloor heating training sessions. It helps us schedule sessions and allocate resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Select the sessions you're interested in attending by choosing 'Yes' for each relevant option under 'Select Sessions'.
3. Enter your contact information, including your email address and phone number.
4. Confirm your email address and phone number.
5. If you're assigned a session, select 'Yes' for 'Assigned Session' and confirm it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): Enter your name or any identifying information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your name and company (if applicable) for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email_address`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **Select Sessions** (`training_sessions`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose one or more sessions you're interested in attending from the options provided.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, `email`, optional): Re-enter your email address to confirm.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, `text`, optional): Re-enter your phone number to confirm.
* **Assigned Session** (`assigned_session`, `select_one`, optional): Select 'Yes' if you're assigned to a session.
* **Confirm Assigned Session** (`confirm_assigned_session`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm your assignment to a session.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about your attendance to the session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any relevant notes about your attendance.

## Tips
* Fill out the form carefully, as the information provided will be used to schedule sessions and allocate resources.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask your supervisor or departmental contact for guidance.
* Submit the form only once to avoid duplication.
