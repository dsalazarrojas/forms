# Processing Technology Expo Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Processing Technology Expo Registration Form is designed to collect attendee information for the Processing Technology Expo. This form helps us gather essential details about attendees, such as their names, contact information, and registration preferences. By completing this form, you will be registered for the Processing Technology Expo and will receive important updates and notifications related to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your name (first and last) and email address.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Indicate the date of your registration.
4. Select whether you will be attending the Processing Technology Expo.
5. If you are an attendee, you can optionally provide your company name and role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **date_of_registration** (Date of Registration, date, required): Select the date you will be attending the Processing Technology Expo.
* **attending** (Attending, select_one, required): Choose whether you will be attending the Processing Technology Expo (True or False).
* **company** (Company, text, optional): If you are an attendee, enter your company name.
* **role** (Role, select_multiple, optional): If you are an attendee, select your role at the event (Attendee, Speaker, or Exhibitor).
* **note** (Note, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes related to your registration.
* ... (Note fields are repeated to allow for more information, but please be concise)

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field or have any questions, please contact our support team for assistance. 

Note that some fields are optional, but providing additional information can help us better tailor the event to your needs.
