# Second Term Childcare Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from parents or guardians for the Second Term Childcare Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
- Click on each page to navigate through the form.
- Read the instructions and hints provided for each field.
- Fill in the required information for each field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, `text`, optional):
    This field is used to capture any additional comments or information you'd like to share about yourself or your child. You can write whatever feels relevant to your child's needs and circumstances.
- **Parent Information** (`Parent Information`, `text`, optional):
    In this field, please provide some basic details about yourself as a parent or guardian. This could include your name, contact information, or any other relevant details.
- **Program Selection** (`Program Selection`, `select_one`, optional):
    Here, you can choose one of the available options for the program you're interested in. The options are:
    - Option A
    - Option B
    - Option C
- **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, optional):
    This field is for any extra information that might be relevant to your child's participation in the program. You can use it to share any special needs or requirements your child might have.

## Tips
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you haven't missed any important fields.
- Use clear and concise language when filling out the form to help us better understand your needs.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
