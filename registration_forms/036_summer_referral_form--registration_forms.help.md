# Summer Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Referral Form is used to collect information from students and their parents/guardians during the summer months. This form is completed on a per-student basis and is not a duplicate or repeatable process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the **Student Name** field.
2. Enter your student's email address in the **Email** field.
3. Enter your phone number in the **Parent Phone** field.
4. (Optional) Enter your student's date of birth in the **Student DOB** field.
5. Enter the date you are making this referral in the **Referral Date** field.
6. Enter the name of the school or program your student is being referred to in the **School Name** field.
7. Select the **Referral Type** that applies to your student's situation.
8. Enter a brief reason for the referral in the **Referral Reason** field.
9. Provide any additional information you feel is relevant in the **Additional Info** field.
10. Indicate whether you are the **Submitted By** (parent/guardian) by selecting yes or no.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name**: Enter your student's full name as they would like to be known.
* **Email**: Enter your student's email address, which will be used for communication.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication.
* **Student DOB**: (Optional) Enter your student's date of birth, which is used for age verification.
* **Referral Date**: Enter the date you are making this referral.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school or program your student is being referred to.
* **Referral Type**: Select the type of referral that applies to your student's situation.
* **Referral Reason**: Enter a brief reason for the referral.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information you feel is relevant to the referral.
* **Submitted By**: Indicate whether you are the parent/guardian submitting this form by selecting yes or no.
