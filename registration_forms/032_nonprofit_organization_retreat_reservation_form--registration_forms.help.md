## Nonprofit Organization Retreat Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Nonprofit Organization Retreat Reservation Form is designed to collect and manage participant information, event details, and other necessary data for nonprofit organizations to efficiently plan and execute their events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information (Participant Details)
2. Provide event information (Event Info)
3. Select a date and time for the event (Dates)
4. Enter payment information (Payment Info)
5. Enter nonprofit details (Nonprofit Details)
6. Review and submit your form
