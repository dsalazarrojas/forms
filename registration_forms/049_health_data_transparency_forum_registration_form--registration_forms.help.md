# Health Data Transparency Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from users for the purpose of transparency and record-keeping. Your submission will help maintain the integrity of the Health Data Transparency Forum.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form correctly, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you've read this help guide to understand the importance and purpose of each field.
2. Fill out the mandatory fields, which include:
   * What is your name?
   * Email address
   * Desired password
   * Confirm password
   * Submission date
   * Submission time
   * Submission date and time
3. Provide any optional information you deem relevant, such as:
   * Alternative email address
   * Alternative phone number
   * Additional notes

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is your name?** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): This is your primary contact information.
* **Phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to provide an alternate contact number.
* **Desired password** (`password`, text, required): Choose a unique password for your account.
* **Confirm password** (`confirm_password`, text, required): Re-enter your password for verification.
* **Additional notes** (`submit_note`, note, optional): Leave any relevant comments or details.
* **Alternative email address** (`email_2`, email, optional): A secondary email address for contact purposes.
* **Alternative phone number** (`phone_2`, text, optional): A secondary phone number for contact purposes.
* **More notes** (`note_2`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or details.
* **Specific submission date** (`submit_date_2`, date, optional): If you have a specific date in mind for your submission.
* **Specific submission time** (`submit_time_2`, time, optional): If you have a specific time in mind for your submission.
* **Specific submission date and time** (`submit_datetime_2`, date, optional): If you have a specific date and time in mind for your submission.
