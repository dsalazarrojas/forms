# national-design-award-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a national design award project. It collects necessary contact and project information to help with the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact details in **Contact Details**.
2. Provide information about your project in **Project Information**.
3. Upload your project file in **Upload Project File**.
4. Upload your project image in **Project Image**.
5. Add a description and alternative text for your project image in **Project Image Description** and **Project Image Alt Text**.
6. If your project has a video, provide the link and description in **Project Video Link** and **Project Video Description**.
7. Upload your project video thumbnail in **Project Video Thumbnail**.
8. Add a transcript for your project video in **Project Video Transcript**.
9. If your project has an audio component, provide the link and description in **Project Audio Link** and **Project Audio Description**.
10. Upload your project audio file in **Project Audio File**.
11. Provide credits for your project image, video, and audio in **Project Image Credits**, **Project Video Credits**, and **Project Audio Credits**.
12. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Details**: Provide your name, email, and any other relevant contact information.
* **Project Information**: Describe your project, including its title, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Upload Project File**: Upload a file containing your project's design or specification, if applicable.
* **Upload Project Image**: Upload an image of your project.
* **Project Image Description**: Add a brief description of your project image.
* **Project Image Alt Text**: Provide alternative text for your project image, to be read by screen readers.
* **Project Video Link**: If your project has a video, provide the link to it.
* **Project Video Description**: Add a description of your video.
* **Project Video Thumbnail**: Upload a thumbnail image for your video.
* **Project Video Transcript**: Provide a transcript of your video, if applicable.
* **Project Video Credits**: Provide credits for your video creators or contributors.
* **Project Audio Link**: If your project has an audio component, provide the link to it.
* **Project Audio Description**: Add a description of your audio content.
* **Project Audio File**: Upload an audio file for your project.
* **Project Audio Credits**: Provide credits for your audio creators or contributors.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Upload only relevant and high-quality files and images.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
