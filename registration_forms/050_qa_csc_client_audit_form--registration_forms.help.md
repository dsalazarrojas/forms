# QA_CSC_Client_Audit_Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The QA_CSC_Client_Audit_Form is a tool designed to collect and record client information and details for auditing purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the Main Page with the client's name and any other information you have readily available.
2. Move on to the Client Details page, where you'll find more specific fields to fill out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (1): Enter the client's name as it is known.
* **Contact Details** (2): Provide any additional contact details you may have for the client.
* **Client Name** (3): This field is a duplicate of the first field and seems to be a placeholder for future development. Please disregard this field and focus on other details.
* **Contact Phone** (4): Enter the client's contact phone number.
* **Email** (5): Enter the client's email address.
* **Date Of Birth** (6): Enter the client's date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Gender** (7): Select the client's gender from the provided options (Male, Female, Other).
* **Client Status** (8): Indicate the client's status as Active or Inactive.
* **Date Of Registration** (9): Enter the client's date of registration (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time Of Registration** (10): Enter the client's time of registration (hh:mm AM/PM).
* **Registration Number** (11): Enter any registration number associated with the client.
* **Registered By** (12): Provide the name of the person who registered the client.
* **Registered At** (13): Enter the date the client was registered (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Last Updated By** (14): Enter the name of the person who last updated the client's information.
* **Last Updated At** (15): Enter the date and time the client's information was last updated (yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm AM/PM).
* **Notes** (16): Leave any additional notes about the client here.

## Tips

* Double-check all information provided to ensure accuracy.
* If unsure about any field, reach out to the client or the person who registered them.
* Ensure that you're using the correct format when entering dates and times.
