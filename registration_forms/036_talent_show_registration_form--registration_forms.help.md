# Talent Show Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Talent Show Registration Form is a document used to collect information from participants for a talent show event. This form aims to gather essential details such as name, contact information, performance details, and registration status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Describe your talent in the "Talent" field.
7. Select the category of your talent from the "Category" field.
8. If applicable, provide a description of your talent in the "Description" field.
9. Select the date and time of your performance in the "Date" and "Time" fields respectively.
10. If applicable, enter the registration fee in the "Registration Fee" field.
11. If applicable, select the registration type in the "Registration Type" field.
12. If applicable, provide additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.
13. Select the payment status in the "Payment Status" field.
14. Select the registration status in the "Registration Status" field.
15. If applicable, select the payment method in the "Payment Method" field.
16. If applicable, select the payment type in the "Payment Type" field.
17. Select the event status in the "Event Status" field.
18. If applicable, enter the participant number in the "Participant Number" field.
19. If applicable, enter the participant ID in the "Participant ID" field.
20. If applicable, enter the user ID in the "User ID" field.
21. Finally, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person responsible for your registration.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for communication.
* **Talent**: Describe your talent in a few sentences.
* **Category**: Select the category of your talent from the provided options.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your talent if desired.
* **Date**: Select the date of your performance from the calendar.
* **Time**: Select the time of your performance from the provided options.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event from the calendar.
* **Event Time**: Select the time of the event from the provided options.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee if applicable.
* **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration if applicable.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your registration if desired.
* **Payment Status**: Select the status of your payment from the provided options.
* **Registration Status**: Select the status of your registration from the provided options.
* **Payment Method**: Select the method of payment if applicable.
* **Payment Type**: Select the type of payment if applicable.
* **Event Status**: Select the status of the event from the provided options.
* **Participant Number**: Enter the participant number if applicable.
* **Participant ID**: Enter the participant ID if applicable.
* **User ID**: Enter the user ID if applicable.
* **Submit**: Submit the form to complete your registration.
