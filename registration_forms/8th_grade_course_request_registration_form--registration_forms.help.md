# 8th Grade Course Request Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The 8th Grade Course Request Registration Form is designed to gather information from students and parents/guardians regarding course requests for the upcoming year. This form helps the school administration to understand the students' preferences and requirements for the next academic year, allowing them to plan and allocate resources accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Choose up to 4 core courses from the list of options (English Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies).
3. Rank your top elective course preferences separated by commas.
4. Select any special programs or supports you may require (e.g., ELL, IEP, 504 plan).
5. Sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student full name**: Enter your first and last name as shown on school records.
* **Student ID number**: Enter your school-assigned ID number, or type "N/A" if unknown.
* **Current school name**: Enter the name of your current school.
* **Parent/Guardian name**: Enter the name of your parent or guardian who will be the primary contact for course questions.
* **Parent/Guardian email address**: Enter the email address of your parent or guardian for confirmations and schedule updates.
* **Parent/Guardian phone number**: Include the area code when entering your parent or guardian phone number.
* **Core course requests**: Choose up to 4 core courses from the list (English Language Arts, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies).
* **Elective course preferences**: Rank your top elective course preferences separated by commas.
* **Special programs or supports**: Select any special programs or supports you may require.
* **Parent/Guardian e-signature**: Type your name to serve as an electronic signature.
* **Date submitted**: Enter the date you completed and submitted this form.
