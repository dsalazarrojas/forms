# local_farmer_market_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a local farmer market and collect information from farmers and vendors. Fill out this form to provide your market name, vendor name, contact information, and market details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field according to your market name, vendor details, and market type.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Market Name** (`market_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your local farmer market.
- **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your vendor name, which is the name of the person or organization selling at the market.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for communication and notification purposes.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter a valid phone number for contact and communication purposes.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date of your market operations.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date of your market operations.
- **Market Type** (`market_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the type of market you are participating in, such as Farmers Market, Flea Market, or Roadside Market.
- **Vendor Description** (`vendor_description`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your products or services offered at the market.
- **Vendor Image** (`vendor_image`, note, required/optional): Upload a note with a brief description of your market's image and logo.

## Tips
- Ensure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact our support team.
