# Camper Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from contestants for a contest, ensuring accurate and complete registration.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2.  Ensure you have permission from parents or guardians if you are under 18.
3.  Double-check your entry title and description for clarity and accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Contestant First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
    *   Please type your first name as it is written on official documents.
*   **Contestant Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
    *   Please type your last name as it is written on official documents.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Provide your birth date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
    *   This will help us verify your age for entry eligibility.
*   **Age** (Number, Required): Enter your current age.
    *   This will help us determine your entry division.
*   **Gender** (Select One, Required): Select your gender.
    *   Choose from the options provided to ensure accurate representation.
*   **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your contact email address.
    *   This will be used for communication and updates.
*   **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
    *   This will be used for further contact if needed.
*   **Street Address** (Text, Required): Enter your home address.
    *   This will help us contact you in case of emergencies.
*   **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city or town.
    *   This will help us with shipping and contact.
*   **State** (Text, Required): Enter your state or province.
    *   This will help us with regional coordination.
*   **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter your postal code.
    *   This will aid in shipping and communication.
*   **Contest Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of the contest you are entering.
    *   This will help us track your entry correctly.
*   **Contest Category** (Select One, Required): Select the category of the contest.
    *   Choose from the options provided to ensure accurate representation.
*   **Entry Title or Description** (Text, Required): Enter a name or description of your entry.
    *   This will help judges evaluate your entry.
*   **Entry Description** (Text, Required): Provide a detailed description of your entry.
    *   This will help judges understand your work.
*   **Have You Participated Before** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you have participated in this contest before.
    *   This will help us track your experience.
*   **Age Division** (Select One, Required): Select your age division.
    *   This will help determine your entry eligibility.
*   **Team or Individual Entry** (Select One, Required): Select whether your entry is individual or part of a team.
    *   This will help us track your entry type.
*   **Team Name** (Text, Optional): If you are part of a team, enter your team's name.
    *   This will help us identify your team.
*   **Team Members** (Text, Optional): If you are part of a team, list all team members.
    *   This will help us track your team composition.
*   **Parent or Guardian Name** (Text, Optional): If you are under 18, enter your parent or guardian's name.
    *   This will help us verify your guardianship.
*   **Parent or Guardian Signature** (Text, Optional): If you are under 18, get a signature from your parent or guardian.
    *   This will serve as proof of consent.
*   **Submission Deadline** (Date, Required): Enter the date you will submit your entry.
    *   This will help us track your progress.
*   **I Have Read and Agree to Contest Rules** (Select One, Required): Confirm you have read and agree to contest rules.
    *   Please carefully review the rules before selecting this option.
*   **I Accept the Liability Waiver** (Select One, Required): Confirm you accept the liability waiver.
    *   Please read and understand the waiver before selecting this option.
*   **I Allow My Entry to Be Used for Publicity** (Select One, Required): Confirm you allow your entry to be used for publicity.
    *   Please consider your publicity preferences before selecting this option.
*   **Contestant Signature** (Text, Required): Type your full name as the contestant.
    *   This will serve as your official signature.
*   **Date Signed** (Date, Required): Date and sign your entry.
    *   This will confirm your submission.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting your entry.
*   Ensure you have permission from parents or guardians if you are under 18.
*   Be clear and detailed in your entry title and description.
*   Review the contest rules and guidelines carefully before agreeing to them.
