# Carrier Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Carrier Registration Form is a required document for carriers to register with the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) for authority to operate as a for-hire carrier. This form is used to gather information from the carrier for FMCSA's records and to ensure compliance with federal regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the registration type (New Carrier, Updated Profile, or Reactivation) that best describes your carrier's situation.
2. Enter your company's name accurately.
3. Fill in the DOT number and MC number.
4. Provide the required information for the primary office contact, including name, email, and phone number.
5. List all states where your company has operational coverage.
6. Indicate whether you have intrastate authority for operations within a single state.
7. Select all the equipment types your carrier offers for hire (e.g., Dry Van, Flatbed, etc.).
8. Confirm if you are capable of hauling hazardous materials.
9. Verify your safety rating and confirm you have a satisfactory safety rating with FMCSA.
10. Enter the name of your insurance carrier, as well as your primary insurance policy number.
11. Select how you would prefer to be paid (ACH, Check, QuickPay, or Factor).
12. Enter your bank routing number and account number (if applicable).
13. Confirm the date you are registering with FMCSA.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Type**: select the purpose of this form (New Carrier, Updated Profile, or Reactivation)
* **Carrier Company Name**: enter the name of your company.
* **DOT Number**: enter your carrier's Department of Transportation (DOT) number.
* **MC Number**: enter your Motor Carrier (MC) number.
* **Contact Name**: enter the name of your primary office contact person.
* **Contact Email**: enter your company's email address for all registration correspondence.
* **Contact Phone**: enter your office phone number.
* **States of Authority**: list all states where your company has operational coverage.
* **Intrastate Authority**: indicate if your company has intrastate authority for operations within a single state.
* **Equipment for Hire**: select all the equipment types your carrier offers for hire (e.g., Dry Van, Flatbed, etc.).
* **Hazmat Capable**: confirm if your carrier is capable of hauling hazardous materials.
* **Safety Rating**: confirm your carrier has a satisfactory safety rating with FMCSA.
* **Insurance Carrier Name**: enter the name of your insurance carrier.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: enter your primary insurance policy number.
* **Payment Preference**: select how you want to be paid (ACH, Check, QuickPay, or Factor).
* **Bank Routing Number**: enter your 9-digit bank routing number (if applicable).
* **Bank Account Number**: enter your bank account number (if applicable).
* **Authorized Digital Signature**: enter your name as the authorized digital signature.

## Tips

- Ensure all required fields are filled accurately and completely.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact FMCSA for clarification.
- Keep the form up to date with any changes to your carrier's information.
- If you are a new carrier, ensure all necessary documents are in order before proceeding.
