# School Talent Quest Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic details and contact information for participating students in the School Talent Quest Tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the School Name, which will be associated with the student's school affiliation.
2. Select the Grade Level of the student.
3. Enter the Student Name.
4. Enter the Student ID, which is a unique identifier for the student.
5. Enter the Student Email address.
6. Enter the Student Phone number.
7. Enter the Parent Name (if applicable).
8. Enter the Parent Email address (if applicable).
9. Enter the Parent Phone number (if applicable).
10. Enter the Teacher Name (if applicable).
11. Enter the School Email address.
12. Enter the School Phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name (Text, Required):** Please enter the name of the school the student is associated with.
* **Grade Level (Select One, Optional):** Please select the grade level of the student.
* **Student Name (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the student.
* **Student ID (Text, Optional):** Enter a unique identifier for the student (if applicable).
* **Student Email (Email, Optional):** Please enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone (Text, Optional):** Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Name (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the parent or guardian (if applicable).
* **Parent Email (Email, Optional):** Please enter the parent or guardian's email address (if applicable).
* **Parent Phone (Text, Optional):** Enter the parent or guardian's phone number (if applicable).
* **Teacher Name (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the student's teacher (if applicable).
* **School Email (Email, Optional):** Enter the email address of the school or institution (if applicable).
* **School Phone (Text, Optional):** Enter the school or institution's phone number (if applicable).

## Tips
Please ensure that the form is filled out accurately and completely. You may need to contact a school administrator if you need help with submitting this form.
