# product_development_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering a product development project with a company, and it gathers necessary information for project planning and execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your company's name, product name, and product type (if applicable).
2. Select your company's participant type (e.g., Participant or Stakeholder).
3. Enter your contact name and company's email address.
4. Provide your company's phone number and address (if applicable).
5. Fill in your job title (if you are a contact person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, optional): Enter your company's name.
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the product being developed.
* **Product Type** (`product_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of product being developed (Software, Hardware, or Service).
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, optional): Select your role in the project (Participant or Stakeholder).
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, email, optional): Enter your company's email address.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company's phone number.
* **Company Address** (`company_address`, text, optional): Enter your company's address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Fill in your job title (if you are a contact person).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all the required fields (if any) to ensure accurate project registration.
- If you have any doubts or issues with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
