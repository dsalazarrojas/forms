# Student Pre-Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Pre-Registration Form is designed to collect general information from students interested in registering for a program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to ensure you understand what information is being collected.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Fill in the required fields (indicated by a asterisk \*).
4. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Info (\*)**: Select the title that best describes your situation (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.).
* **Name**: Enter your name in the format "Last Name, First Name". For example, "John Doe, John".
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you. The email address should be in the format of "username@example.com".
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" (e.g., "123-456-7890").
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" (e.g., "01/01/1990").
* **Program**: Select the program you are interested in registering for (e.g., Bachelor, Master, Ph.D., Post Graduate).
* **Semester**: Select the semester you are interested in registering for (e.g., Spring, Fall, Summer).
* **Campus**: Select the campus you will be attending (e.g., North Campus, South Campus, Central Campus).
* **Faculty**: Enter your faculty of choice (e.g., "Faculty of Arts and Sciences").
* **Department**: Select whether you will be attending this department (e.g., "Yes" or "No").
* **Address**: Enter your current address (e.g., "123 Main St, Anytown, USA").
* **Country**: Select your country of origin (e.g., USA, Canada, Mexico).
* **State**: Select your state of residence (e.g., California, New York, Texas).

Note: The asterisk (\*) indicates that the field is required.
