# Kundalini Activation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kundalini Activation Registration form is designed to collect information from participants about their session scheduling preferences, participant details, and consent for the activation process. This form ensures that participants are properly registered and informed about the process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **General Information**: Start by providing your general information in the first section. This includes any details you'd like to share about yourself.
2. **Session Scheduling**: Schedule your session preferences in the second section. Be specific about your availability and the dates you'd like to attend.
3. **Participant Details**: Provide your participant details in the third section. This includes any relevant information about your physical and spiritual background.
4. **Kundalini Activation Consent**: Read and sign the consent section to confirm your understanding and agreement for the activation process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **General Information (text)** 
This section is for you to share any general information you'd like about yourself. This could be a short introduction or a brief description of your background.

### **Session Scheduling (text)**
In this section, please schedule your session preferences. Include dates and times you're available, and any specific needs you have for your sessions.

### **Participant Details (text)**
Here, you can share any relevant information about your physical and spiritual background that might help us better serve you during the activation process.

### **Kundalini Activation Consent (text)**
This section is for you to confirm your understanding and consent for the activation process. Please read and agree to the terms before proceeding.
