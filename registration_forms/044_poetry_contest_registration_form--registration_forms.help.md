# poetry_contest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Poetry Contest Registration Form is designed to collect information from schools and participants about their poetry submissions for the contest. This form helps us to verify the participant's and school's details and to ensure that the submissions meet the contest criteria.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. If applicable, enter your school's name and address in the "School Name" and "School Address" fields, respectively.
4. Enter your school's email in the "School Email" field.
5. Enter a participant name, email, and phone number in the "Participant Name", "Participant Email", and "Participant Phone" fields, respectively.
6. Enter a title of your poem in the "Poem Title" field.
7. Select the genre of your poem in the "Poem Genre" field.
8. Enter the length of your poem in the "Poem Length" field, choosing one of the options provided.
9. Select the language of your poem in the "Poem Language" field.
10. Enter the year your poem was written in the "Year Written" field.
11. Select a school ID (if applicable) in the "School ID" field.
12. Select a participant ID (if applicable) in the "Participant ID" field.
13. Select the type of poem (poem, short story, freeverse, or narrative) in the "Poem Type" field.
14. Select the maximum length of your poem in the "Max Length" field, choosing one of the options provided.
15. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter" field (optional).
16. Enter the email of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Email" field (optional).
17. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Phone" field (optional).
18. Enter the date of submission in the "Submission Date" field.
19. Enter the name of the school principal in the "School Principal" field (optional).
20. Enter the name of your teacher in the "Teacher Name" field.
21. Finally, select your school in the "School" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **School Name** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter your school's name.
* **School Address** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter your school's address.
* **School Email** (Email, Required): Enter your school's email.
* **School Phone** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter your school's phone number.
* **Participant Name** (Text, Required): Enter a participant name.
* **Participant Email** (Email, Not Required): If applicable, enter a participant's email.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter a participant's phone number.
* **Poem Title** (Text, Required): Enter a title of your poem.
* **Poem Genre** (Text, Not Required): Select the genre of your poem (poem, short story, freeverse, or narrative).
* **Poem Length** (Text, Not Required): Select the length of your poem (1-10, 11-20, or 21-30).
* **Poem Language** (Text, Not Required): Select the language of your poem (e.g., English, Spanish, French, etc.).
* **Year Written** (Date, Required): Enter the year your poem was written.
* **School ID** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter a school ID.
* **Participant ID** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter a participant ID.
* **Poem Type** (Select, Required): Select the type of poem (poem, short story, freeverse, or narrative).
* **Max Length** (Select, Required): Select the maximum length of your poem (1-10, 11-20, or 21-30).
* **Submitter** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Email** (Email, Not Required): If applicable, enter the email of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Phone** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Required): Enter the date of submission.
* **School Principal** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter the name of the school principal.
* **Teacher Name** (Text, Required): Enter the name of your teacher.
* **School** (Text, Not Required): If applicable, enter the name of your school.

Note: Fields marked as required must be completed.
