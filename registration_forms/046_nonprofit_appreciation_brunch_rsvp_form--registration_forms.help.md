# Nonprofit Appreciation Brunch RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering guests to the Nonprofit Appreciation Brunch event. Please provide your details to help us better plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information and select your preferences from the options provided. Make sure to submit your response to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the event.
* **name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name.
* **surname** (Text, Optional): Enter your surname.
* **email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **guest_details** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant details about your guests.
* **number_of_guests** (Number, Optional): Enter the number of guests attending with you.
* **date_of_event** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the event (if already known).
* **time_of_event** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the event (if already known).
* **rsvp** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose whether you will attend the event by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **additional_guests_details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional details about your guests (if any).
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments or messages.
* **organization** (Text, Optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **department** (Text, Optional): Enter your department's name.
* **position** (Text, Optional): Enter your job position.
* **job_title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title.
* **address** (Text, Optional): Enter your address.
* **city** (Text, Optional): Enter your city's name.
* **state** (Text, Optional): Enter your state's name.
* **zip** (Text, Optional): Enter your zip code.
* **phone_organizer** (Text, Optional): Enter the organizer's phone number.
* **email_organizer** (Email, Optional): Enter the organizer's email address.
* **additional_info** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information.
* **non_profit** (Text, Optional): Enter your non-profit organization's name.
* **comments** (Note, Optional): Enter any comments or messages.
