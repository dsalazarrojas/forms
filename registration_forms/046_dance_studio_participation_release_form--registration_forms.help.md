# Dance Studio Participation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for the dance studio's participant release form. The form is required to ensure the dance studio has the necessary information to communicate with participants and their emergency contacts in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the participant's information, including name, email (if applicable), and medical condition (if any).
2. Choose the relationship between the participant and their emergency contact.
3. Specify the emergency contact's relationship (if not one of the listed options).
4. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
5. Select the type of consent you wish to give the dance studio (e.g., sharing contact information with other participants or their families).
6. If you selected "Other" for the consent option, describe the specific consent you wish to give in the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`): Enter your name and any other identifying information you'd like to share with the dance studio.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and email address of the person you wish to list as your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact_relationship`): Select the relationship between you and your emergency contact from the provided options (e.g., Parent, Sibling, Spouse, Other).
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact (Specify)** (`emergency_contact_relationship_specify`): If you selected "Other" from the provided options, describe the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_number`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your name as a participant in the dance studio.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted by the studio.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`): If you have any medical conditions that the studio should be aware of, enter them here.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`): Select any medical conditions you may have from the provided options (e.g., Heart condition, Diabetes, Asthma, Allergies).
* **Medical Condition (Specify)** (`medical_condition_specify`): If you selected "Other" for a medical condition, describe the condition in more detail.
* **Consent** (`consent`): Select the level of consent you're willing to give the dance studio (e.g., sharing your contact information with other participants or their families).
* **Other (Please Specify)**: If you selected "Other" for the consent option, describe the specific consent you wish to give in this space.
