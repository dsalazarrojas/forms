# Local Host Partnership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering as a local host for an event or program. It's intended for users who have been identified as potential local hosts and are interested in hosting an event or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone Number, Partnership Description, Street Address, City, State, and ZIP/Postal Code.
2. Select the relevant Partnership Type from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose your State from the dropdown menu.
4. Optionally, accept the Terms and Conditions by clicking on the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): This is your name as it is commonly known.
* **Last Name** (text, required): This is your family name or surname.
* **Email** (text, required): Enter your email address where you'd like to be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Partnership Type** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more of the available options for your partnership type.
* **Partnership Description** (text, required): Provide a brief description of your partnership.
* **Street Address** (text, required): Enter your address for the partnership.
* **City** (text, required): Enter the city for the partnership.
* **State** (select one, required): Choose your state from the dropdown menu.
* **ZIP/Postal Code** (text, required): Enter the zip code for the partnership.
* **Terms and Conditions** (note, optional): Accept the terms and conditions to proceed with the submission.
* **Submit** (text, optional): This button submits the form once all required fields are filled out.

Note: All required fields are marked as such. Please fill out these fields before proceeding.
