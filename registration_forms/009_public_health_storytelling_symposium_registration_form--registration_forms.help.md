# public_health_storytelling_symposium_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for the Public Health Storytelling Symposium, a platform for sharing and learning about public health stories, experiences, and best practices.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter event details in the "Event Details" field, providing any relevant information about the event.
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, and organization in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide a title of your story in the "Title of Story" field.
4. Enter the details of your story in the "Story Details" field.
5. Select your registration type from the options provided: Public Health Professional, Advocate, or Student.
6. If you selected "Public Health Professional", "Advocate", or "Student", you can enter any additional information in the "Registration Type Reason" field.
7. Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the "Terms and Conditions" field.
8. Confirm your password in the "Confirm Password" field.
9. Enter the date, time, and duration of the event in the "Date of Event", "Time of Event", and "Duration of Event" fields, respectively.
10. Provide the location of the event in the "Location of Event" field.
11. Enter the name and bio of the speaker in the "Speaker" and "Speaker Bio" fields.
12. Enter the speaker's image and video URLs in the "Speaker Image URL" and "Speaker Video URL" fields.
13. Enter the speaker's audio URL in the "Speaker Audio URL" field.
14. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details (1)**: Enter any relevant information about the event.
* **Name (2)**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the symposium materials.
* **Email (3)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Organization (5)**: Enter your organization or affiliation (if applicable).
* **Title of Story (6)**: Enter a title for your story.
* **Story Details (7)**: Enter the details of your story in this field.
* **Registration Type (8)**: Select your registration type from the provided options (Public Health Professional, Advocate, or Student).
* **Registration Type Other (9)**: Enter any additional information if you selected "Public Health Professional", "Advocate", or "Student" as your registration type.
* **Registration Type Reason (10)**: Enter the reason for your selection of registration type.
* **Agree to Terms (11)**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Error Message (12)**: This field will display any error messages from the form submission.
* **Terms and Conditions (13)**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirm Password (14)**: Re-enter your password for confirmation.
* **Password (15)**: Enter your password.
* **Date of Event (16)**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Time of Event (17)**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Duration of Event (18)**: Enter the duration of the event.
* **Location of Event (19)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Speaker (20)**: Enter the name of the speaker.
* **Speaker Bio (21)**: Enter the speaker's biography.
* **Speaker Image URL (22)**: Enter the URL of the speaker's image.
* **Speaker Video URL (23)**: Enter the URL of the speaker's video.
* **Speaker Audio URL (24)**: Enter the URL of the speaker's audio.
* **Submit (25)**: Review and submit the form.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the symposium organizers.
