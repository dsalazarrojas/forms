# HR Data Intelligence Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration details from attendees for the HR Data Intelligence Conference. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure smooth event management and attendee tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Event Details" page (Page 1) with the required information, which includes the event title and any other relevant details.
2. Move on to the "Attendee Preferences" page (Page 2), where you will be asked to specify your preferences as an attendee, such as dietary requirements or accessibility needs.
3. The "Contact Info" page (Page 3) is optional, but if you wish to be contacted about the event, please provide your contact details.
4. The "Registration Details" page (Page 4) is also optional, but it's where you can provide any additional information that may be helpful for the event organizers.
5. Finally, the "Additional Questions" page (Page 5) allows you to provide any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details**: This is the first page of the form where you need to provide the required information about the event you are registering for.
	+ This field is required.
* **Attendee Preferences**: This page allows you to specify your preferences as an attendee, such as dietary requirements or accessibility needs.
	+ This field is required.
* **Contact Info**: This is an optional field where you can provide your contact details if you wish to be contacted about the event.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Registration Details**: This page is optional and allows you to provide any additional information that may be helpful for the event organizers.
	+ This field is not required.
* **Additional Questions**: This is the last page of the form where you can provide any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.
	+ This field is not required.
