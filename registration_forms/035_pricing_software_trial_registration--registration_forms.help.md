# Pricing Software Trial Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about you or your company for the purpose of registering for a software trial. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that your registration is successful.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the pricing plan that best suits your needs from the options provided.
2. Enter your name, email address, and any other required details.
3. Fill out the fields for your company's name, URL, and size (if applicable).
4. Provide your job title.
5. Select the department that corresponds to your role.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pricing Plan (pricing_plan)** (`pricing_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the pricing plan that meets your company's needs from the available options.
* **Name (name)** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name, which will be used to identify you or your company in our records.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Provide a valid email address that can be used to contact you or your company.
* **Phone (phone)** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number, which we may use to contact you if needed.
* **Company Name (company_name)** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's name as it appears on your website or other public platforms.
* **Company URL (company_url)** (`company_url`, `text`, required: false): Enter your company's public URL or domain name.
* **Job Title (job_title)** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter your job title or role within your company.
* **Company Size (company_size)** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the size of your company from the available options.
* **Department (department)** (`department`, `text`, required: false): Enter the department that corresponds to your job title.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields carefully to ensure successful registration.
* If you have any issues or questions about the registration process, please contact us for assistance.
* We'll review your registration and follow up with you shortly after submission.
