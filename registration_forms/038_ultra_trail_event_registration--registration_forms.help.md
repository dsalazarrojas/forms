# Ultra Trail Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Ultra Trail event, a challenging trail running event. Please ensure that you are registering for a valid event and not duplicating your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Click on the "Event Details" tab to provide information about the event you are registering for.
2. In the "Event Details" tab, enter the event name, date, and location where you are participating.
3. Click on the "Participant Info" tab to provide information about the participant.
4. In the "Participant Info" tab, enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
5. Click on the "Payment Details" tab to provide payment information for registration.
6. In the "Payment Details" tab, enter any payment method and amount details.
7. Review your submission before clicking the submit button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): Provide the name, date, and location of the event you are participating in.

### Participant Info
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other relevant participant details.

### Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter payment method and amount details.

## Tips
* Double-check the event details before submitting to ensure accuracy.
* Ensure you have entered your correct name and participant details.
* Review and understand your payment information before submitting.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
