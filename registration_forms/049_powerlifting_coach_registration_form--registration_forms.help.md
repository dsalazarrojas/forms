# Powerlifting Coach Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Powerlifting Coach Registration Form is designed to collect information from powerlifting coaches who wish to offer their services to athletes. By filling out this form, coaches can provide their contact details, training experience, and certification, which will be reviewed and approved by our team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required fields. While some fields are not marked as "Required," please ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Be honest and truthful when filling out the form. Inaccurate or incomplete information may delay the review process.
3. Ensure your contact details (email and phone number) are accurate and up-to-date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Coach Information** (1): Enter your name and any relevant information about yourself as a coach.
* **Contact Details** (2): Provide your email address and phone number. This is the primary contact method we will use to communicate with you.
* **Emergency Contact** (3): Enter a contact person to reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Training Experience** (4): Describe your training experience, including any relevant certifications or qualifications.
* **Certification** (5): If you hold any certifications related to powerlifting, list them here.
* **Coaching Services** (6): Describe the services you offer as a coach, such as personal training or group coaching.
* **Payment** (7): If you have any payment requirements or expectations, please list them here.
* **Coaching Location** (8): If you coach at a specific location, enter the address or location name.
* **Coaching Schedule** (9): If you have a specific schedule or availability for coaching, enter it here.
* **Coach Email** (10): Confirm your email address.
* **Phone Number** (11): Confirm your phone number.
* **Phone Number** (12): This field is not explicitly labeled. Based on the type "text", it is assumed to be for any additional phone number information or notes.

## Tips

* Be concise and clear in your answers.
* Use correct formatting and grammar.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them to your form submission.
* Our team will review your submission and follow up with you to confirm your registration.
