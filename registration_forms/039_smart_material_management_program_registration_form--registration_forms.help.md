# Smart Material Management Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Smart Material Management Program. It is a mandatory form that collects demographic and preference data from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Select your preferred material from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your preferred contact method by selecting the option from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose how frequently you would like to be contacted by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date you reported your material issue on the "reported_date" field.
7. Select a date for your registration on the "registration_date" field.
8. Enter a date for your contact on the "contact_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name**: Enter your first name.
- **last_name**: Enter your last name.
- **email**: Enter your email address.
- **preferred_material**: Select your preferred material from the dropdown menu.
- **preferred_contact**: Choose your preferred contact method by selecting an option from the dropdown menu.
- **contact_frequency**: Choose how frequently you would like to be contacted by selecting multiple options from the dropdown menu.
- **reported_date**: Enter the date you reported your material issue.
- **registration_date**: Select a date for your registration.
- **contact_date**: Enter a date for your contact.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
- Review your selection before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- This form should only be completed once per user.
