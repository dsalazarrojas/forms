# College Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Admission Form is designed to gather essential information from applicants for the college admission process. It includes fields for personal identification, academic records, and financial assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
### General Guidelines
- Please fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the specific field's explanation.
- Ensure that you submit a completed and signed application before the designated deadline.

### Completing the Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
3. **Middle Name**: (Optional) Enter your middle name if applicable.
4. **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. **Citizenship Status**: Select your citizenship status from the options provided.
6. **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and contact purposes.
7. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
8. **Mailing Address**: Enter your permanent mailing address.
9. **City**: Enter your city or town.
10. **State/Province**: Enter your state or province.
11. **Postal Code**: Enter your postal code or zip code.
12. **Country**: Enter your country of residence.
13. **Intended Major or Program**: Select your intended major or program of study.
14. **High School Name**: Enter the name of your high school.
15. **Graduation Year**: Enter the year you graduated from high school.
16. **Current High School GPA**: Enter your current high school GPA on a 4.0 scale.
17. **Standardized Test Score**: If applicable, enter your SAT or ACT score.
18. **Test Type**: Select the type of test you took (SAT, ACT, both, or not submitted).
19. **Extracurricular Activities**: (Optional) Enter any relevant extracurricular activities.
20. **Special Talents or Skills**: (Optional) Enter any special talents or skills you have.
21. **Volunteer Hours**: (Optional) If applicable, enter the number of volunteer hours you've completed.
22. **Would you like to write a supplemental essay?**: Select yes or no.
23. **Are you a first-generation college student?**: Select yes or no.
24. **Do you require financial aid?**: Select yes or no.
25. **Application Submission Date**: Enter the date you are submitting your application.
