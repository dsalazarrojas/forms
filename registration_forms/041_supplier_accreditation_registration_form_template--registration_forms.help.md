# Supplier Accreditation Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a comprehensive registration form for suppliers to provide their accreditation information. It is designed to collect various details such as name, contact information, business details, and social media links.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in your personal details on page 1.
2. Move on to page 2 to fill in your contact information.
3. On page 3, fill in your business details.
4. Finally, on page 4, provide your social media links.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name (Page 1)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name (Page 1)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email (Page 1)
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.

### Phone Number (Page 1)
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

### Street Address (Page 1)
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Please enter your street address where we can contact you.

### City (Page 1)
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Please enter the city where your business is located.

### State (Page 1)
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Please enter the state where your business is located.

### Country (Page 1)
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Please enter the country where your business is located.

### Business Name (Page 2)
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your business name as it appears on your official documents.

### Tax Payer ID (Page 2)
* **Tax Payer ID** (`tax_payer_id`, `number`, required): Please enter your tax payer ID.

### Business Address (Page 2)
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, `text`, required): Please enter your business address where we are located.

### Zip Code (Page 2)
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Please enter the zip code of your business address.

### Date of Birth (Page 2)
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Please enter your date of birth as it appears on your official documents.

### Business License (Page 2)
* **Business License** (`business_license`, `text`, required): Please enter your business license number.

### Years in Business (Page 3)
* **Years in Business** (`years_in_business`, `number`, required): Please enter the number of years your business has been in operation.

### Business Type (Page 3)
* **Business Type** (`business_type`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options: Option 1, Option 2.

### Business Category (Page 3)
* **Business Category** (`business_category`, `select_one`, not required): Please select one of the following options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3. (Note: This field is not required.)

### Business License Number (Page 3)
* **Business License Number** (`business_license_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your business license number.

### Business Type Description (Page 3)
* **Business Type Description** (`business_type_description`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief description of your business type.

### Business Sub Category (Page 4)
* **Business Sub Category** (`business_sub_category`, `text`, not required): Please select one of the following options: [Not Provided].

### Business Sub Category Description (Page 4)
* **Business Sub Category Description** (`business_sub_category_description`, `text`, not required): Please provide a brief description of your business sub category.

### Business Website (Page 4)
* **Business Website** (`business_website`, `text`, not required): Please enter your business website URL.

### Business Facebook (Page 4)
* **Business Facebook** (`business_facebook`, `text`, not required): Please enter your business Facebook link.

### Business Twitter (Page 4)
* **Business Twitter** (`business_twitter`, `text`, not required): Please enter your business Twitter link.

### Business Instagram (Page 4)
* **Business Instagram** (`business_instagram`, `text`, not required): Please enter your business Instagram link.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled accurately and completely.
* Please note that all fields are required unless otherwise specified.
