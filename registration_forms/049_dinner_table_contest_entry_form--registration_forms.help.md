# Dinner Table Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants to submit their entries to a dinner table contest. Fill out this form to share your dish and participate in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out all required fields to ensure your entry is complete.
- Use the provided hints for guidance on what information to include.
- Review each field carefully to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Organizer Info** (`event_organizer_info`, text, optional): This field is to enter any relevant information about the event organizer.
  - Please enter any details the event organizer has specified for the contest.
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Here, you can enter your name, contact information, and any other details about yourself.
  - Enter your name and any other details you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): If you have any further contact information, you can enter it here.
  - This could include your email or phone number.
* **Entry Type** (`entry_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the category your dish belongs to (Chef, Home Cook, etc.).
  - Select from the options provided.
* **Menu Description** (`menu_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your dish.
  - Tell us about your dish in a few words.
* **Ingredient List** (`ingredient_list`, text, optional): List the ingredients used in your dish, separated by commas.
  - Be as specific as possible.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, list them here.
  - This will help other participants with dietary needs know about your dish.
* **Menu Image Url** (`menu_image_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of an image of your dish.
  - A photo of your dish will make it more appealing.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
  - This is how you'll receive communications about the contest.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
  - This could be used for further contact.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
  - The date the event will take place.
