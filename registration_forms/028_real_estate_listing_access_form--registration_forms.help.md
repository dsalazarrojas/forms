# real_estate_listing_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about real estate listings, enabling users to provide property details, buyer information, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin.
2. Select the "Property Details" page.
3. Enter or select the property details as prompted.
4. Click on the "Buyer Information" page.
5. Enter or select the buyer information as prompted.
6. Select the "Property Type" and "Property Status" options from the provided lists.
7. Enter or select the listing agent information as prompted.
8. Click on the "Submit" button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Details (Property Details)** (Text): Enter the property's title, address, and any other relevant details.
* **Buyer Information (Buyer Information)** (Text): Provide the buyer's name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
* **Property Type (Property Type)** (Select One): Choose the type of property (Single Family, Multi Family, or Condo).
* **Property Status (Property Status)** (Select One): Select the property's status (Active or Sold).
* **Listing Agent (Listing Agent)** (Text): Enter the listing agent's name and contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure you have the necessary permissions and access rights to complete this form.
* Double-check the property details and buyer information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or support team for assistance.
