# Mountaineering Club Expedition Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mountaineering Club Expedition Admission Form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals who wish to join the club's expeditions. This form helps the club to assess your eligibility, plan for your safety, and ensure a smooth expedition experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Info (Page 1)** (`applicant_info`, text, not required):
	+ This field is where you provide basic information about yourself, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Details (Page 2)** (`contact_details`, text, not required):
	+ In this field, please provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Expedition Details (Page 3)** (`expedition_details`, text, not required):
	+ Here, you can specify the type of expedition you are interested in joining, such as rock climbing, hiking, or skiing.
* **Safety Planning (Page 4)** (`safety_planning`, text, not required):
	+ In this section, please explain how you plan to ensure your safety during the expedition, including any medical concerns or equipment you may need.
* **Expedition Registration (Page 5)** (`expedition_registration`, text, not required):
	+ This field is where you provide the specific details of the expedition you wish to join, including the dates and locations.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the club's administration team for assistance.
