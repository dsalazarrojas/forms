# BOR Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The BOR Conference Registration Form is for users to register for the conference, providing their personal details, conference preferences, and payment information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including First Name, Last Name, and Email Address. These are required fields.
2. Select your preferred Registration Type (e.g., Full Conference, Single Day, Student, etc.). This is also a required field.
3. Choose your dinner options (e.g., No dinner, Included with registration, etc.). You can select one option.
4. If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, you can specify them in the Dietary Restrictions or Allergies field.
5. Please select your preferred Payment Method (e.g., PayPal, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.). This is a required field.
6. If you have any special requests or accommodations, you can specify them in the Special Requests field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (<code>first_name</code>, <code>text</code>, required)
Please enter your first name.

### Last Name (<code>last_name</code>, <code>text</code>, required)
Please enter your last name.

### Email Address (<code>email_address</code>, <code>email</code>, required)
Enter your email address, which we will use for conference updates.

### Phone Number (<code>phone_number</code>, <code>text</code>, optional)
Optional: Please enter your phone number if you wish.

### Organization or Company (<code>organization</code>, <code>text</code>, optional)
Optional: Enter your organization or company name if you have one.

### Job Title (<code>job_title</code>, <code>text</code>, optional)
Optional: Enter your job title if you have one.

### Conference Date (<code>conference_date</code>, <code>text</code>, required)
Select the dates you plan to attend the conference.

### Registration Type (<code>registration_type</code>, <code>select_one</code>, required)
Select your registration type: Full Conference, Single Day, Student, Group, Professional, VIP, etc.

### Dinner Selection (<code>dinner_option</code>, <code>select_one</code>, required)
Select whether you want dinner included with your registration: No dinner, Included with registration, etc.

### Dietary Restrictions or Allergies (<code>dietary_restrictions</code>, <code>text</code>, optional)
Optional: Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.

### Registration Fee (<code>registration_fee</code>, <code>number</code>, optional)
Optional: Your registration fee will be calculated based on your selection.

### Preferred Payment Method (<code>payment_method</code>, <code>select_one</code>, required)
Select your preferred payment method: PayPal, Credit Card, Bank Transfer, etc.

### Additional Comments (<code>additional_notes</code>, <code>text</code>, optional)
Optional: Enter any additional comments you have.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct registration type and dates to avoid any confusion.
* If you have any special requests or accommodations, please specify them in the Special Requests field.
* Please ensure your email address is correct to receive conference updates.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please select the correct option.
* Review your payment method selection carefully to avoid any issues.
