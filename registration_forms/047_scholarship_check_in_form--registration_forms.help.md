# Scholarship Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Scholarship Check In Form is used to register students and scholars for a particular event, providing essential information for tracking and verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Full Name
2. Provide your Student ID number
3. Enter the time of your check in
4. Enter your Email address
5. Enter your Phone number
6. Enter the Name of the school you are attending
7. Enter the School ID number
8. Enter the name of the scholarship you are applying for
9. Enter the name of the program you are enrolled in
10. Enter the date of the event start

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your complete name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Student ID**: Enter your student ID number, if applicable.
- **Check In Time**: Select the time of your check-in, using the provided format (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Email**: Enter your email address associated with the scholarship program.
- **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
- **School Name**: Enter the name of the school you are attending.
- **School ID**: Enter your school ID number, if applicable.
- **Scholarship Name**: Enter the name of the scholarship you are applying for.
- **Program Name**: Enter the name of the program you are enrolled in.
- **Event Start**: Select the date of the event start, using the provided format (MM/DD/YYYY).

## Tips
Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid any discrepancies in the scholarship records. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact your scholarship administrator for clarification.
