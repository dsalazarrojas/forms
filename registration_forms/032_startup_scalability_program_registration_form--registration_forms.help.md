# Startup Scalability Program Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Startup Scalability Program Registration Form is designed to collect information from startups that wish to participate in the program. This form is used to assess a startup's eligibility and gather essential details about their business and scalability goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, marked with an asterisk (\*). For fields with select options, choose one or multiple answers depending on the type of field.
2. Answer each question accurately and thoroughly.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required): Please provide your name and contact information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your company's contact information.
* **Select Scalability Program** (`select_scalability_program`, select_one, required): Choose whether your startup has or will have scalability programs. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Select Scalability Programs** (`select_scalability_programs`, select_multiple, required): Select all scalability programs your startup has or will have. Options are 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Business Info** (`business_info`, text, required): Describe your startup's business model and goals.
* **Growth Opportunities** (`growth_opportunities`, text, required): Identify areas where you see growth opportunities for your startup.
* **Opportunities** (`opportunities`, text, optional): Provide any additional opportunities or areas of interest not covered in the previous fields.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Review and submit your completed form.
