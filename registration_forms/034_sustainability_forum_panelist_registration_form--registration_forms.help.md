# Sustainability Forum Panelist Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability Forum Panelist Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who want to participate in the sustainability-focused discussion forum. The form helps us understand their areas of interest, expertise, and contact details to better match them with relevant discussion topics and other users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields marked as "required" on the form.
2. For fields not marked as "required", you can choose to leave them blank or provide additional information if you feel it's relevant.
3. Make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sustainability Forum Panelist** (`form_panelist_registration_form_sustainability`, text, required): This is the title of the form, and you should fill in your name as it would appear in the discussion forum.
- **Expert Areas of Interest** (`expert_areas_of_interest_sustainability`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the areas of interest from the dropdown list: Sustainable Development, Environmental Management, and Social Responsibility.
- **Panelist Expertise** (`form_panelist_expertise_sustainability`, text, optional): If you have a relevant background or specialization in any of the areas of interest, you can provide details in this field.
- **Contact Information** (`form_panelist_contact_info_sustainability`, note, optional): Use this field to provide any contact information you're comfortable sharing with other users in the forum.
- **Email** (`form_panelist_email_sustainability`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you're willing to share it with other users in the forum.
- **Phone** (`form_panelist_phone_sustainability`, text, optional): Provide your phone number if you're comfortable sharing it with other users in the forum.
- **Profile Picture** (`form_panelist_profile_picture_sustainability`, text, optional): Upload a profile picture to help other users recognize you in the forum.
- **Agreed** (`form_panelist_agreed_sustainability`, select_multiple, required): Please indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of the forum by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown list.
- **Notes** (`form_panelist_notes_sustainability`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or thoughts you'd like to share with other users in this field.

## Tips
- Please fill in the form accurately and honestly to ensure a smooth and relevant experience for all users.
- Use clear and concise language when filling in the form to help other users understand your areas of interest and expertise.
- Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure they accurately reflect your intentions and capabilities.
