# Disaster Recovery Medical Coordination Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals or teams to register for a disaster recovery medical coordination system. It helps the system to efficiently manage medical responses during disasters.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your role in the "Role" field by choosing from the available options.
6. Select the teams you are affiliated with in the "Location" field by choosing from the available options.
7. Indicate your availability for medical response in the "Availability" field.
8. Specify the time you are available for medical response in the "Time" field.
9. If you have any skills or specialties, describe them in the "Skills" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): This is the first name of the person registering.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): This is the last name of the person registering.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address is required for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): This is the phone number of the person registering.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the available roles: Doctor, Volunteer, Staff, or Other.
* **Location** (`location`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the teams you are affiliated with from the available options: Emergency Response Team, Medical Team, Support Team, or Other.
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, required): Indicate your availability for medical response by selecting a date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Specify the time you are available for medical response.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Describe any skills or specialties you have that are relevant to medical response.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- You can register multiple roles or locations if applicable.
