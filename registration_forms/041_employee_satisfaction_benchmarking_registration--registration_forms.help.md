# Employee Satisfaction Benchmarking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect feedback from employees to measure their satisfaction with their organization's policies, procedures, and work environment.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the instructions carefully before starting.
2. Select the "Registration Form" page.
3. Fill out the form with your information.
4. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (employee_satisfaction_benchmarking_registration_form)**:
	+ This is the main registration form where you will provide your basic information.
	+ Please ensure that all fields are correctly filled out.
* **Organization Details (organization_details)**:
	+ Provide your organization's name, location, and any other relevant details.
	+ If you're unsure what to enter, you can leave this field blank.
* **Objectives (objectives)**:
	+ Describe the objectives or goals you hope to achieve through this feedback.
	+ Be specific and concise with your answers.

## Tips
- Be honest and thorough when providing your feedback.
- If you have any issues or concerns, do not hesitate to reach out to your supervisor or HR representative.
- Your input is valuable to us, and we appreciate your participation in this benchmarking exercise.
