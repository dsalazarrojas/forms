# Market Feasibility Research Session Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for participants to register their interest in participating in a Market Feasibility Research Session. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure that you are able to participate in the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: no): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: no): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: no): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone_number`, `text`, required: no): Please enter your phone number in the format 'XXX-XXX-XXXX'.
* **Phone Extension** (`participant_phone_extension`, `text`, required: no): Please enter your phone extension (e.g. 'x1234').
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email_number`, `text`, required: no): Please enter the email address we can use to contact you.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, `text`, required: no): Please enter your job title or position.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required: no): Please enter your company name or affiliation.

## Tips
* Ensure that you have a valid email address that is not associated with another participant in the same company.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact the session administrator.
