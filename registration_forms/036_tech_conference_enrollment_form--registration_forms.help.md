# Tech Conference Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for the Tech Conference. It will collect your personal details and session preferences to help us prepare for your event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Attendee Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Attendee Phone" field.
4. Select your session interests in the "Session Interest" field.
5. Choose your preferred sessions from the "Session 1", "Session 2", "Session 3", "Session 4", and "Session 5" fields.
6. (Optional) Add any additional comments about your experience in the "Attendee Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on our event materials.
* **Attendee Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated about the conference.
* **Attendee Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number for any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Session Interest** (select_multiple, required: false): Choose the topics that interest you the most for our sessions.
	+ Option 1: Session 1
	+ Option 2: Session 2
	+ Option 3: Session 3
* **Event Preferences** (note, required: false): If you have any comments or requests about the conference, please share them with us.
* **Session 1**, **Session 2**, **Session 3**, **Session 4**, **Session 5** (select_one, required: false): Choose one session per field that you'd like to attend.
* **Attendee Comments** (text, required: false): Share any additional comments or feedback about your experience with us.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* You can select multiple session interests.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please mention them in the "Event Preferences" field.
* You can also add any additional comments or feedback in the "Attendee Comments" field.
