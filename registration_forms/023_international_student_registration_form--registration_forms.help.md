# International Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from international students to enable a smooth and efficient registration process. It is crucial to fill in the form accurately and completely to avoid delays or errors in processing your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields as instructed.
2. Review and submit the form for review and approval.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **International Student Info** (international_student_info, text, Optional): This field is for students to provide brief personal details about themselves. Please fill in your name and any other relevant information that is not already included in other fields.
* **Student Nationality** (nationality, select_one, Optional): Please select whether you are an international student or not. If you are unsure, please contact the relevant authorities for guidance.
* **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Passport Number** (passport_number, text, Optional): Enter your passport number for identification purposes. Please ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Student ID Number** (student_id_number, text, Optional): If you have a student ID number, please enter it here for identification purposes.
* **Student Status** (student_status, select_one, Optional): Please select your current student status from the options provided. If you are unsure, please consult with the relevant authorities for guidance.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (emergency_contact_person, text, Optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please enter their name and contact information.
* **Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, select_one, Optional): Please select your relationship with the emergency contact person from the options provided.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Email Address** (email_address, email, Optional): Enter your email address for correspondence and communication purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and completely for a smooth registration process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with the relevant authorities or contact support for assistance.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact support for help.
