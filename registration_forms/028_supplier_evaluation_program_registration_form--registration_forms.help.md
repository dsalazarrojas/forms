# Supplier Evaluation Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Supplier Evaluation Program Registration Form is a registration mechanism for suppliers who want to participate in the evaluation program. This form helps us understand the suppliers' details, documentations, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier's details in the "Supplier details" field.
2. Provide any relevant documentation in the "Documentation" field.
3. Indicate your consent to participate in the program in the "Consent" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier details**: Enter the supplier's name, address, and any other relevant information.
* **Documentation**: Upload any relevant documentations such as certificates, licenses, or other supporting materials.
* **Consent**: Check the box to indicate that you agree to participate in the program and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Email**: Enter the supplier's email address to facilitate communication.
* **Phone**: Enter the supplier's phone number for communication purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure efficient processing of your submission.
* If you are unable to complete the form in one sitting, you can save your progress and come back to it later.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact us for assistance.
