# Home Health Equipment Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Home Health Equipment Training Registration form is used to collect information 
about home health equipment training sessions. This includes details such as 
participant status, agency status, and training session specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Follow the steps in sequence, filling out each page of fields as you progress.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information before proceeding.
3. Review each field carefully and fill out as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Training Session
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_multiple, required): Choose whether the participant has had a training session or not.
* **Agency** (`agency`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the participant's agency has access to the equipment.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Provider** (`provider`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the participant has a provider.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the training session.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the training session.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, select_multiple, required): Select the type of equipment used during the training session.
* **Equipment Provider** (`equipment_provider`, text, required): Enter the provider of the equipment.
* **Training Type** (`training_type`, select_one, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the training was of type 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the training session.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, required): Enter the length of the training session.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the participant's email.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Training Notes** (`training_notes`, text, required): Enter any additional notes about the training session.
* **Prophets** (`prophets`, text, required): Enter any prophet-related information.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the training session.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, required): Enter the provider of the training session.
* **Participant Providers** (`participant_providers`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the participant has providers.
* **Equipment Location** (`equipment_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the equipment.
* **Equipment Provider Location** (`equipment_provider_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the equipment provider.
* **Session Start Time** (`session_start_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the training session.
* **Session End Time** (`session_end_time`, time, required): Enter the end time of the training session.
* **Session Length Hours** (`session_length_hours`, number, required): Enter the length of the training session in hours.

## Tips
* Ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, do not hesitate to seek help.
* Review each field carefully before proceeding.
* Complete the form in sequence, filling out each page of fields as you progress.
