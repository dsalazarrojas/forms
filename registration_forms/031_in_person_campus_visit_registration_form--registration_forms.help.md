# In Person Campus Visit Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The In Person Campus Visit Registration Form is a self-service registration process for students to schedule an in-person campus visit. This form is designed to collect information about the student's visit preferences and schedule the visit accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields.
2. Select your campus visit preferences from the "Campus Visit Preferences" field.
3. Choose a date and time for your visit from the "Campus Visit Date" and "Campus Visit Time" fields.
4. Select the type of visit you are interested in from the "Campus Visit Type" field.
5. If you are a student, fill in your student name and ID number in the "Student Name" and "Student ID" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your university email address or any other email address you would like to be contacted at.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone.
* **Campus Visit Preferences**: Select "Yes" if you would like to be contacted by a member of the university staff or faculty about your visit. Select "No" if you do not want to be contacted.
* **Campus Visit Date**: Enter the date you would like to visit the campus.
* **Campus Visit Time**: Enter the time you would like to visit the campus.
* **Campus Visit Type**: Select the type of visit you are interested in:
	+ Academic: If you would like to meet with an academic department or faculty member.
	+ Campus Tour: If you would like to take a tour of the campus.
	+ Group Visit: If you would like to visit with a group of people.
	+ Informational Session: If you would like to attend an informational session about the university.
* **Student Name**: If you are a student, enter your name as it appears on your student ID.
* **Student ID**: If you are a student, enter your student ID number.
