# Genealogy Society Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for membership in the Genealogy Society. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth membership process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
5. Select your membership type by choosing one or more options from the "Membership Type" field.
6. Enter the date you have been a member in the "Member Since" field. This should be the date you first joined the society.
7. If applicable, enter the submitter's name in the "Submitter" field.
8. (Optional) Enter your organization in the "Organization" field.
9. (Optional) Provide any additional comments in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name. This is the name you are commonly known by.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name. This is the surname you are commonly known by.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number. This can be your work or personal phone number.
* **Membership Type** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose one or more membership types from the available options. This includes Genealogy Society, Library, and Heritage Group.
* **Member Since** (Date, Required): Enter the date you first joined the Genealogy Society. This is crucial for accurate membership records.
* **Submitter** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the individual submitting the membership application, if different from the member.
* **Organization** (Text, Optional): Enter your current or former affiliation with an organization (if any).
* **Comments** (Note, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your membership status.
