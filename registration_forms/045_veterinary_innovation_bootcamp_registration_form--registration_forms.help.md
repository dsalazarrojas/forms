# Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp. It will collect your contact information and motivations for participating in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Click on the "Participant Details" page.
- Fill out the form with your information. Make sure to include your name and contact email. You can also provide your phone number if you have one.
- In the "Motivations" page, briefly explain why you are interested in participating in the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp.
- In the "Special Needs" page, if you have any specific needs or requirements for the event, please list them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (text, required)**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email (email, required)**: Enter your participant's email address.
* **Phone (text, optional)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for further contact.
* **Motivations (text, optional)**: Briefly explain why you are interested in participating in the Veterinary Innovation Bootcamp.
* **Special Needs (note, optional)**: If you have any specific needs or requirements for the event, list them here.
