# Annual General Meeting Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for members to communicate with the Annual General Meeting (AGM) committee, providing information about their attendance, agenda items, and any specific concerns or ideas they would like to raise during the meeting. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your personal and contact information, such as your full name and email address.
2. Select the meeting year you are referring to from the dropdown menu.
3. Indicate whether you will be attending the meeting in person or virtually.
4. Choose the type of communication you would like to make, e.g., a question for the board, a topic suggestion, or feedback.
5. Enter a brief subject line and your message or question.
6. Select the agenda item this communication relates to, if applicable.
7. If you have supporting documents, indicate if you will be attaching any.
8. If you require a response, select the urgency level.
9. Indicate your preferred discussion format and any additional comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as a member of the organization.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number**: This field is not required, but it's best to provide a contact number for follow-up communication.
* **Membership ID**: If you have a membership ID, enter it here. This is not required.
* **Meeting Year**: Select the year of the meeting you are referring to from the dropdown menu.
* **Will You Attend?**: Indicate whether you will be attending the meeting in person or virtually.
* **What type of communication?**: Choose the type of communication you would like to make, such as a question for the board or a topic suggestion.
* **Subject**: Enter a brief subject line for your communication.
* **Message**: Enter your message or question.
* **Agenda Item**: If this communication relates to a specific agenda item, select it from the options.
* **Priority Level**: Indicate the level of urgency for your communication.
* **Preferred Discussion Format**: Select how you would like this communication to be addressed.
* **Supporting Documents**: Indicate if you are attaching any supporting documents with your communication.
* **Document Details**: If you are attaching documents, enter a description here.
* **Presentation Request**: If you would like to present, select this option.
* **Presentation Time Needed**: If you require time for your presentation, select the duration.
* **Confidentiality**: Indicate if this communication should be kept confidential.
* **Response Needed**: Select if you require a response.
* **Follow-Up Contact**: Indicate the best way to contact you for follow-up.
* **Previous Communication**: If you have raised this communication before, select this option.
* **Previous Details**: If you have previous communications, enter a description here.
* **Member Since**: If you are a member since, enter the year you became a member.
* **Chapter Location**: If you have a local chapter, enter the location.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments here.
