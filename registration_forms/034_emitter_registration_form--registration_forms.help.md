## Emitter Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Emitter Registration Form is used to collect information from emitter sites. This form is necessary for registering and configuring emitter sites.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button once all required fields are filled out.
2. Navigate through the form's pages, filling out each section with the relevant information.
3. Review the form's summary page for accuracy before submitting.
