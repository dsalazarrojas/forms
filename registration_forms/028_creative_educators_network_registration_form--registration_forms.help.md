# Creative Educators Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register new users to the Creative Educators Network, providing them with access to network resources and community engagement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the Email field that matches the one in the Confirm Email field.
3. Enter a password of your choice in the Password field.
4. Confirm your password in the Confirm Password field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field, and confirm it in the Confirm Phone field.
6. Check the box next to "I agree to the guidelines" to confirm that you have read and agree to the network's guidelines.
7. Confirm your agreement by selecting "Yes" in the Confirm I Agree field.
8. Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Yes" in the "I agree to the terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Confirm Email**: Enter the same email address that you entered in the Email field.
* **Password**: Enter a password of your choice.
* **Confirm Password**: Enter the same password that you entered in the Password field.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Phone**: Enter the same phone number that you entered in the Phone field.
* **I agree to the guidelines**: Check the box to confirm that you have read and agree to the network's guidelines.
* **Confirm I Agree**: Select "Yes" to confirm your agreement.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check that your email and phone numbers match in their respective confirmation fields.
* Ensure that you have read and agree to the network's guidelines and terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* If you are having trouble completing the form, feel free to contact the Creative Educators Network team for assistance.
