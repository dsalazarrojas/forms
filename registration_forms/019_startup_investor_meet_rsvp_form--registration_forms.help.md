# Startup Investor Meet RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Investor Meet RSVP Form is designed to collect information from investors about their RSVP status and preferences for the event. This form helps organizers prepare for the event and ensure that everyone has a productive and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Enter your Investor Details**: This section is for you to provide your basic contact information, such as your name and email.
2. **Indicate your RSVP Status**: Please select whether you plan to attend the event by choosing either "True" or "False" from the dropdown menu.
3. **Provide additional details**: If you plan to attend, you can provide your phone number, RSVP date, and start and end times. If you don't plan to attend, you can simply leave these fields blank.
4. **Customize your event details**: If you plan to participate in the event, you can provide more details such as the event name, date, time, location, description, and organizer information.
5. **Submit your RSVP**: Once you have completed all the necessary fields, click the submit button to send your RSVP information to the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Investor Details**: Enter your name and other contact information.
	+ **Name**: Your full name.
	+ **Email**: Your email address.
* **RSVP Status**: Select "True" if you plan to attend and "False" if you don't.
* **Name**: Leave this field blank if you don't plan to attend.
* **Email**: Leave this field blank if you don't plan to attend.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number if you plan to attend.
* **RSVP Date**: Enter the date you plan to RSVP.
* **Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the start and end time of the event.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Details Start Time**: Enter the start time of the event.
* **Event Details End Time**: Enter the end time of the event.
* **Event RSVP Status**: Select "True" if you plan to RSVP and "False" if you don't.
* **Investor Details 2**: This field is for additional notes or comments.
* **Investor Details Name**: Enter your name.
* **Title**: Enter your title.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization.
* **Investor Details 2**: This field is similar to "Investor Details" above.
* **Investor Details Name**: Enter your name.
* **Title**: Enter your title.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization.

Note: The fields marked as optional should be left blank if you don't have the information.
