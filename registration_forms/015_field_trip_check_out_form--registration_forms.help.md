# Field Trip Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Field Trip Check Out Form is a form used to collect information from students and teachers before embarking on a field trip. This form helps us keep track of student and teacher details, as well as trip details, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable trip for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the school in the text box.
2. Enter the student's name and student ID in their respective text boxes.
3. Enter the teacher's name, phone number, email, and address in their respective text boxes.
4. Enter the student's phone number, email, and address in their respective text boxes.
5. Enter the field trip location, date, start time, and return time in their respective fields.
6. Ensure all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **school_name**: Enter the name of the school where the field trip is taking place. (Text, required: false)
  - **school_id**: Enter the ID number of the school. (Number, required: false)
  - **student_name**: Enter the name of the student going on the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **student_id**: Enter the student ID number of the student going on the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **trip_id**: Enter the ID number of the trip. (Number, required: false)
  - **teacher_name**: Enter the name of the teacher chaperoning the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **teacher_phone**: Enter the phone number of the teacher chaperoning the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **teacher_email**: Enter the email of the teacher chaperoning the trip. (Email, required: false)
  - **teacher_address**: Enter the address of the teacher chaperoning the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **student_phone**: Enter the phone number of the student going on the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **student_email**: Enter the email of the student going on the trip. (Email, required: false)
  - **student_address**: Enter the address of the student going on the trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **field_trip_location**: Enter the location of the field trip. (Text, required: false)
  - **field_trip_date**: Enter the date of the field trip in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'. (Date, required: false)
  - **field_trip_start_time**: Enter the start time of the field trip in the format 'HH:MM'. (Time, required: false)
  - **field_trip_return_time**: Enter the return time of the field trip in the format 'HH:MM'. (Time, required: false)

## Tips
- Double-check all entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out, but be aware that some fields are not required in this version of the form.
- If you're unsure about any information, consult with your teacher or school administration.
