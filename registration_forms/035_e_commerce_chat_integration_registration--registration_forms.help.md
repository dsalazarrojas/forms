# E Commerce Chat Integration Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from businesses and e-commerce platforms for integrating a chat feature. Please follow the instructions below to complete it successfully.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read through each field carefully and fill it out according to the explanations provided below.
- Ensure all required fields are filled out.
- Submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **business_details**: Enter your business's name and a brief description (e.g., "My Fashion Store - Selling stylish clothing") to help us understand your business identity.
* **platform_info**: Describe the e-commerce platform you are using (e.g., Shopify, WooCommerce) and its version (e.g., 2.2.3) to help us know your platform setup.
* **chat_feature_preferences**: List the chat features you would like to integrate with your platform (e.g., live chat, chatbots, messaging). Be as specific as possible to help us configure the integration correctly.
* **business_info**: Enter your business's website URL and contact email to help us contact you for further assistance or questions regarding your integration.

## Tips
- Double-check all fields before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact us at contact@your-business-email.com.

Note: Replace "contact@your-business-email.com" with your actual business contact email.
