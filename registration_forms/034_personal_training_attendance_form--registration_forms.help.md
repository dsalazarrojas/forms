# Personal Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Training Attendance Form is designed to collect data about client attendance to personal training sessions. This form is intended for trainers to record client attendance and feedback after each session. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Click on the "Submit" button to save your input. If you need to edit the form or add more information, you can click on the "Save and Continue Editing" button. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents. 
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): This field is for your email address. Please enter your correct email address to ensure that you receive any updates or notifications related to this form.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): This field is for the date of the personal training session. Please select the date from the calendar or enter it manually in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): This field is for the time of the personal training session. Please select the time from the time picker or enter it manually in the correct format (hh:mm).
* **Client Attendance** (`client_attendance`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for tracking the client's attendance status. Please select one or more of the options provided:
	+ Client: The client attended the session.
	+ Not Client: The client did not attend the session.
	+ No Answer: The client's attendance status is not known or not reported.
* **Client Attendance Status** (`client_attendance_status`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is for providing further details on the client's attendance status. Please select one or more of the options provided:
	+ Attended: The client attended the session.
	+ Did Not Attend: The client did not attend the session.
	+ Late: The client was late to the session.
	+ No Answer: The client's attendance status is not known or not reported.
* **Workout Session Notes** (`workout_session_notes`, note, required: false): This field is for any notes or comments about the client's session. Please enter your observations or feedback about the session.
* **Trainer** (`trainer`, select_one, required: false): This field is for the trainer's name who conducted the session. Please select the trainer who led the session.
* **Client Comments** (`client_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any comments or feedback from the client. Please enter their comments or suggestions about the session.
* **Trainer Comments** (`trainer_comments`, text, required: false): This field is for any comments or feedback from the trainer. Please enter their observations or suggestions about the session.
* **Client Attendance Notes** (`client_attendance_notes`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional notes or feedback from the client. Please enter their comments or observations about the session.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, required: false): This field is for any additional information you want to provide about the session. Please enter your comments or observations.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Use this form to track client attendance and feedback after each session.
- Make sure to save and review your input carefully before submitting the form.
