# Grate Maintenance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for maintenance registration of Grates, ensuring the collection of necessary information for the maintenance process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Email address for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Phone number for easy communication.
4. Select the correct Grate ID from the dropdown list.
5. Describe the issue you are experiencing with the Grate in the Issue Description field.
6. Select the Service Request you need from the dropdown list (e.g., Service A, Service B, Service C).
7. Enter the date and time when the maintenance is scheduled.
8. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as per your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as per your identification documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for easy communication and record-keeping.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for quick contact and updates.
* **Grate ID** (select_one, required): Select the ID of the Grate you are registering for maintenance. Please refer to the provided options.
* **Issue Description** (text, optional): Describe the issue you are experiencing with the Grate in detail, including any symptoms or errors you are encountering.
* **Service Request** (select_one, required): Select the service you need for the Grate maintenance (e.g., Service A, Service B, Service C).
* **Scheduled Date** (date, required): Enter the date when the maintenance is scheduled.
* **Scheduled Time** (time, required): Enter the time when the maintenance is scheduled.
