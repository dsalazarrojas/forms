# Business Founder Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from business founders about their startup, team, and current stage of development. This information will be used to provide tailored support and resources during the onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your business and team.
2. Ensure you complete all required fields (marked as required).
3. Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy and completeness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Previous Role/Position** (`previous_role`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your most recent position or role.
* **Industry Background** (`industry_background`, `text`, optional): Enter the industry you come from.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_of_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter your total work experience.
* **Startup Name** (`startup_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your new business.
* **Startup Stage** (`startup_stage`, `select_one`, required): Select the current stage of your startup's development:
	+ Ideation
	+ Prototype/MVP
	+ Pre-launch
	+ Recently Launched
	+ Growth Stage
* **Problem You Are Solving** (`problem_statement`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the problem your startup addresses.
* **Your Solution** (`solution_description`, `text`, required): Describe how your startup solves this problem.
* **Target Customer** (`target_customer`, `text`, required): Identify your ideal customer.
* **Current Team Size** (`team_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the current number of people on your team.
* **Key Team Roles and Members** (`team_roles`, `text`, optional): List the main team members and their roles.
* **Funding Raised to Date** (`funding_raised`, `number`, optional): Enter the total capital raised to date.
* **Current Funding Stage** (`funding_stage`, `select_one`, optional): Select the current funding stage:
	+ Self-funded
	+ Pre-seed
	+ Seed round
	+ Series A
	+ Series B+
* **Key Milestones for Next 12 Months** (`key_milestones`, `text`, required): Describe the key milestones you aim to achieve.
* **Type of Support Needed** (`support_needed`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all relevant support types:
	+ Mentoring
	+ Investor Connections
	+ Operational Support
	+ Legal and Compliance
	+ Marketing and Sales
	+ Technology and Development
