# Pet Parent Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Parent Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about a pet and its owner. This form will help us understand the pet's health, behavior, and any other important details that might be useful for its care.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Pet Parent ID (ID number) of the pet, as provided by our system.
2. Fill out the Owner Information form with the pet's owner's details.
3. Provide details about the pet's information, including its type, breed, and color.
4. Answer the vaccination questions to indicate the pet's vaccination status.
5. Enter the date of the last vaccination, if applicable.
6. Select the contact method used by the pet's owner.
7. Enter the ID of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
8. Enter the owner's email and phone number for easy communication.
9. Provide any medical notes about the pet.
10. Enter any additional medical history details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Pet Parent ID** (`pet_parent_id`, number, required): Enter the unique ID number provided by our system for the pet.
* **Owner Information** (`owner_info`, text, optional): Fill out the form with the pet's owner's details, including name and contact information.
* **Pet Information** (`pet_info`, text, optional): Provide details about the pet, including its type, breed, and color.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter contact information for the pet's owner.
* **Vaccination Details** (`vaccination_details`, text, optional): Provide details about the pet's vaccination history, including the date and type of vaccinations received.
* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter any medical history or health issues the pet may have.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and ID of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Pet Type** (`pet_type`, select_one, optional): Select if the pet is yes or no.
* **Pet Breed** (`pet_breed`, select_one, optional): Select if the pet is yes or no.
* **Pet Color** (`pet_color`, select_one, optional): Select if the pet is yes or no.
* **Vaccination Type** (`vaccination_type`, select_one, optional): Select if the pet is yes or no.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the last vaccination, if applicable.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_one, optional): Select if the pet's vaccination is active or inactive.
* **Contact Method** (`contact_method`, select_one, optional): Select how the pet's owner will be contacted.
* **Contact Person ID** (`contact_person_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of the person who will be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter the owner's email address.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter the owner's phone number.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): Enter any medical notes or issues about the pet.
* **Medical History Details** (`medical_history_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical history details.
