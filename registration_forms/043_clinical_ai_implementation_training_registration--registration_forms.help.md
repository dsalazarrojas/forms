# Clinical AI Implementation Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in learning about clinical AI implementation. It captures essential information about the participant, their professional background, and their training preferences to ensure they receive the right training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email Address**.
2. Provide your **Phone Number** for communication purposes.
3. Enter the **Facility Name** where you work.
4. Select your **Job Title** and **Department** (if applicable).
5. Choose your **Role Type**: Clinical staff, IT staff, Administrative, Management, or Other.
6. If you have **Years of experience in healthcare**, please indicate them.
7. Select the **Training Type** you are interested in (Introduction to Clinical AI, System Administration, Clinical Integration, Advanced Features, or Custom Training).
8. Choose your **Preferred Session Date**.
9. Select the **Session Format**: In-person, Virtual, or Hybrid.
10. Choose your **Time Preference**: Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Flexible.
11. Indicate your **Technology proficiency level**: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
12. Select your **Familiarity with AI systems**: No experience, Minimal experience, Some experience, or Extensive experience.
13. In the **Learning Objectives** field, describe what you want to learn from the training.
14. If you have **Current challenges with AI implementation**, please describe them.
15. Provide any **Additional information or requests** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for communication and follow-up.
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility where you work.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Department**: If you have a department, please enter it. (Optional)
* **Role Type**: Choose the type of role you have at the facility.
* **Years of experience in healthcare**: If you have experience, please indicate the number of years.
* **Training Type**: Select the training type that interests you.
* **Preferred Session Date**: Choose a date for the training session. We will do our best to accommodate your preference.
* **Session Format**: Choose how you would like to participate in the training session.
* **Time Preference**: Select your preferred time slot for the training session.
* **Technology proficiency level**: Choose your level of proficiency with technology.
* **Familiarity with AI systems**: Select your level of familiarity with AI systems.
* **Learning Objectives**: Describe what you want to learn from the training.
* **Current challenges with AI implementation**: If you have challenges, please describe them.
* **Additional information or requests**: Provide any additional information or requests you may have.
