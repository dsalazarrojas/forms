# Facility Energy Benchmarking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Facility Energy Benchmarking Registration form is intended for facilities to collect and store contact information and facility details for energy benchmarking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, starting with your facility name and contact details.
2. Select your facility type from the options provided.
3. Provide your facility's square feet and year built.
4. Enter your contact's street address, city, and state.
5. Enter your contact's email address and phone number (if applicable).
6. Confirm your facility's participation in the energy benchmarking program.
7. Enter your participant's name, department, and title.
8. Enter your participant's email address.
9. Confirm your facility's participation in the energy benchmarking program.
10. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter your facility's name, which will be used for registration and energy benchmarking purposes.
* **First Name** (`contact_first_name`, text, required): Enter your contact's first name.
* **Last Name** (`contact_last_name`, text, required): Enter your contact's last name.
* **Facility Type** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): Select your facility type from the options provided: Facility, Utility Company, Other, or None.
* **Square Feet** (`building_sqft`, number, optional): Enter your facility's square feet, which will be used for energy benchmarking analysis.
* **Year Built** (`year_built`, select_one, required): Select the year your facility was built from the options provided.
* **Contact Street Address** (`contact_street_address`, text, required): Enter your contact's street address.
* **Contact City** (`contact_city`, text, required): Enter your contact's city.
* **Contact State** (`contact_state`, select_one, required): Select your contact's state from the options provided.
* **Contact Zip** (`contact_zip`, text, required): Enter your contact's zip code.
* **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact's email address (if applicable).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact's phone number (if applicable).
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your facility's participation in the energy benchmarking program.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your participant's name.
* **Participant Title** (`participant_title`, select_multiple, required): Select your participant's title from the options provided.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, text, required): Enter your participant's department.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your participant's phone number (if applicable).
* **Facility Participation Consent** (`facility_participation_consent`, select_one, required): Confirm your facility's participation in the energy benchmarking program.

This help guide is designed to assist facilities in completing the registration form accurately and efficiently. Please review the fields carefully to ensure you provide the required information.
