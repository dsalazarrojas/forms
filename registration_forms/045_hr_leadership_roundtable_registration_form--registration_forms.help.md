# HR Leadership Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Leadership Roundtable registration form is designed to collect information for the HR Leadership Roundtable event. Please fill out this form to provide your details and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out all required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Select your preferred topics for discussion during the event.
* Choose your preferred date and time for the event.
* Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (text, required: false)
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
### Last Name (text, required: false)
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
### Email (email, required: false)
* **Email**: Enter your professional email address.
### Company (text, required: false)
* **Company**: Enter your company name or affiliation.
### Topic 1 (select_one, required: false)
* **Topic 1**: Select the topic you are most interested in discussing during the event.
### Topic 2 (select_multiple, required: false)
* **Topic 2**: Select all the topics you are interested in discussing during the event.
### Preferred Date (date, required: false)
* **Preferred Date**: Select the date you would prefer to attend the event.
### Preferred Time (time, required: false)
* **Preferred Time**: Select the time you would prefer to attend the event.
### Note (note, required: false)
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
### Phone (text, required: false)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Preferred Topics (select_multiple, required: false)
* **Preferred Topics**: Select all the topics you are interested in discussing during the event.
### Assigned Tool (text, required: false)
* **Assigned Tool**: Enter the tool assigned to you for the event.
 
**Tips**

* Please ensure that you only select topics that you are interested in discussing.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please contact the event organizers.
* If you are unable to attend on your preferred date and time, please select an alternative date and time.
