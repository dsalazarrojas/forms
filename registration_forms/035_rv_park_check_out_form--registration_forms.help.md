# rv_park_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The rv_park_check_out_form is used to collect information from guests at the time of check-out. This information helps park staff to record and process guest stays, manage guest interactions, and ensure accurate billing and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Guest Name in the field provided.
2. Select your Arrival Date from the calendar or enter it manually.
3. Select your Departure Date from the calendar or enter it manually.
4. Choose your rating for the park from the provided options.
5. Enter any comments or additional information you'd like to share with the park staff.
6. Enter your Phone number and Email (if applicable).
7. Select the Park Staff Notes that best describe your experience.
8. Enter the Charge Amount for your stay (if applicable).
9. Choose your Payment Method from the available options.
10. Enter any additional Notes about your stay.
11. Select your Guest Rating for the park from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification or driver's license.
* **Arrival Date**: Enter the date you arrived at the park. If you're not sure of the date, you can use the calendar to select it.
* **Departure Date**: Enter the date you are departing from the park. If you're not sure of the date, you can use the calendar to select it.
* **Park Rating**: Choose one of the provided options to rate your experience at the park.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional information or comments about your stay.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for park staff to contact you (if applicable).
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
* **Park Staff Notes**: Select one or more options from the list that best describe your experience with the park staff.
* **Charge Amount**: If you have any outstanding charges or fees to settle, enter the amount here (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Choose the method you used to pay for your stay (e.g. credit card, cash, etc.).
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or information about your stay.
* **Guest Rating**: Choose one of the provided options to rate your overall experience at the park.
