# Zoom Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoom Webinar Registration Form is used to register attendees and registrants for a Zoom webinar. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure a smooth and successful webinar experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with your correct information.
2. Make sure to choose the correct options for your attendances and registrants.
3. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your attendee's name.
* **Email** (2, email, required: false): Enter your attendee's email.
* **Phone** (3, text, required: false): Enter your attendee's phone number.
* **Join Time** (4, time, required: false): Select the join time for your attendee.
* **Attendee Type** (5, select_multiple, required: false): Choose whether your attendee is attending or not.
* **Join Date** (6, date, required: false): Select the join date for your attendee.
* **Registrant Name** (7, text, required: false): Enter the registrant's name.
* **Registrant Email** (8, email, required: false): Enter the registrant's email.
* **Phone** (9, text, required: false): Enter the registrant's phone number.
* **Registrant Type** (10, select_one, required: false): Choose whether the registrant is attending or not.
* **Registrant Join Time** (11, time, required: false): Select the join time for the registrant.
* **Registrant Join Date** (12, date, required: false): Select the join date for the registrant.
