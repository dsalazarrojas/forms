# Future Search Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Future Search Event Registration form is designed to collect information for registering a future search event. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that the event is registered correctly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields:
	* **event_details**: Enter the title and description of the event.
	* **email**: Enter your email address.
	* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
2. Optionally, add the following:
	* **attendee_details**: Enter the name and other relevant details of the attendees.
	* **payment_details**: Enter the payment method and amount.
	* **preferences**: Select the relevant preferences for the event.
	* **consents**: Select the consents applicable to the event.
	* **note**: Add any additional notes for the event.
3. Set the event date and time:
	* **event_date**: Enter the date of the event.
	* **event_time**: Enter the time of the event.
4. If applicable, assign a tool:
	* **assigned_tool**: Enter the tool assigned to the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_details** (`event_details`, text, required): Enter the title and description of the event.
* **attendee_details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Enter the name and other relevant details of the attendees.
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, text, required): Enter the payment method and amount.
* **preferences** (`preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select the preferences applicable to the event.
* **consents** (`consents`, select multiple, optional): Select the consents required for the event.
* **note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes for the event.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you're filling out the form correctly and accurately.
* Review all the fields carefully before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field or need help with the form, please refer to this guide for clarification.
