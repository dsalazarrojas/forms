# Kindergarten Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kindergarten Enrollment Form is used for parents to register their child for kindergarten. The form collects necessary information from both the parent and child, including contact information, child's details, and school information. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose and how to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Kindergarten Enrollment Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form pages in order, starting from "Parent Information" and ending with "Review and Submit".
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 7): Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Email** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 8): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 9): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Parent Age** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 10): Enter your age.
* **Relationship** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 11): Select your relationship to the child (e.g., mother, father, guardian).
* **Child's First Name** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 12): Enter the child's first name.
* **Child's Last Name** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 13): Enter the child's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 14): Enter the child's date of birth.
* **Parent Signature** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 15): Sign the form to confirm your identity.
* **Child Signature** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 16): Enter the child's signature.
* **Child Signature Image** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 17): Upload a photo of the child's signature.
* **Additional Comments** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 18): Enter any additional comments or information about the child.
* **School Name** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 19): Enter the name of the school the child will attend.
* **School Address** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 20): Enter the address of the school.
* **Grade Level** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 21): Select the grade level the child will be attending (e.g., Pre-K, Kindergarten, First Grade).
* **Teacher Name** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 22): Enter the name of the teacher.
* **Teacher Signature** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 23): Sign the form to confirm the teacher's identity.
* **Teacher Signature Image** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 24): Upload a photo of the teacher's signature.
* **Submit** (Kindergarten Enrollment Form 25): Click to submit the form once all required fields are completed.

**Remember to review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.**
