# Social Security Benefits Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Security Benefits Training Registration form is designed to collect information from users for social security benefits training purposes. This data will be used to create a comprehensive profile of training needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct pages from the page selection menu at the top of the form.
2. Fill out each page, making sure to select the correct options for each field.
3. Once you have completed all pages, click the "Submit" button to submit your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This field is used to collect basic user information.
  * This is the first page of the form where you will enter your basic information.
* **Session Signups**: In this section, you will select all the sessions you want to sign up for. Please select all relevant sessions.
  * For example, if you want to sign up for multiple sessions, select all the sessions you are interested in.
* **Attendee Needs**: This field is for any special needs or requirements you have for your sessions.
  * If you have any special needs or requirements, please enter them here.
* **Session Preferences**: Here, you can select your preferred session format (e.g., yes, no, or other).
  * Please select the format you prefer for your sessions.
* **Social Security Benefits**: This field is for any relevant social security benefits information.
  * If applicable, please enter relevant social security benefits information.
* **Email**: Please enter your email address.
  * This will help us contact you with any updates or notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  * This is for us to contact you in case of any emergencies or updates.
* **Date**: Enter the date you plan to attend the training sessions.
  * Please ensure you enter the correct date.
* **Time**: Enter the time you plan to attend the training sessions.
  * Please make sure to enter the correct time.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit your form after filling out all pages.
  * Once you click submit, your data will be saved.
* **Accept Terms**: Please accept the terms and conditions of the training sessions.
  * This is important for your participation in the training sessions.
* **Terms & Conditions**: Read and accept the terms and conditions of the training sessions.
  * These are the rules and guidelines for your participation.
* **Training Sessions**: In this section, you can select all the training sessions you want to attend.
  * Please select all the sessions that interest you.
* **Session Topics**: Select the topic(s) you are interested in for the training session.
  * Please choose the topics that align with your interests.
* **Training Registrations**: Select all the training registrations you want to attend.
  * Please select all the registrations that interest you.
* **Attendee Info**: Select the attendee information that applies to you.
  * Please choose the information that best describes you.
* **Attendee Details**: Select the attendee details that apply to you.
  * Please choose the details that best describe you.
* **Attendee Preferences**: Select your attendee preferences.
  * Please choose your preferences from the list.
* **Attendee Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the attendee.
  * This is for any additional information about the attendee.
* **Attendee Availability**: Enter the date and time you are available to attend the training sessions.
  * Please make sure to enter the correct date and time.
* **Attendee Contact**: Enter your contact information.
  * This is for us to contact you with any updates or notifications.
* **Training Admin Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the training administration.
  * This is for any additional information about the training administration.
* **Session Admin Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the session administration.
  * This is for any additional information about the session administration.
