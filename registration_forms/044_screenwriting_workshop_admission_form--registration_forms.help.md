# Screenwriting Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals who wish to apply for the Screenwriting Workshop. The workshop is an intensive program designed to provide in-depth training on screenwriting techniques and principles. By completing this form, you will be submitting your application for consideration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your application details.
2. Upload your writing samples in .pdf format.
3. Write a brief bio about yourself.
4. Check that all information is accurate before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Application Details (application_details)**: This field is for providing your name and other relevant application details. Please fill this field with your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Name (name)**: This field is for your name. Please use your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email (email)**: This field is for your email address. Please ensure it's a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Writing Samples (writing_samples)**: Please submit your writing samples in .pdf format. This could be a script, a scene, or any other piece of writing that showcases your screenwriting skills.
* **Bio (bio)**: Write a brief bio about yourself, including any relevant experience and credentials. Please keep it concise and professional.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled in completely.
* Proofread your application carefully before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the workshop organizers at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
