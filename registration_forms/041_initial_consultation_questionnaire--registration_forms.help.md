# Initial Consultation Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is an initial consultation questionnaire designed to gather essential information from individuals seeking services. It's used to collect personal and scheduling details for the service provider.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. If applicable, enter your Date of Birth.
6. Enter the Date of Consultation and Time of Consultation, if you've already scheduled a consultation time.
7. Choose your Service Provider from the dropdown list.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your full first name (e.g., John).
* **Last Name**: Enter your full last name (e.g., Smith).
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive any communication from us.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth**: If you want to provide this information, enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-02-15).
* **Date of Consultation**: Enter the date of your scheduled consultation (YYYY-MM-DD format, e.g., 2024-03-16). **Note:** If you've scheduled a consultation time with us, this field should be filled out.
* **Time of Consultation**: Enter the time of your scheduled consultation in the format HH:MM (e.g., 14:30). **Note:** If you've scheduled a consultation time with us, this field should be filled out.
* **Service Provider**: Select the service provider you are interested in. Please note that this field is optional and is not required for the submission.
