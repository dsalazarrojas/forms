# Seminar Details Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Seminar Details Form is used to collect and record the details of a seminar, such as its title, description, date, time, location, and organiser's contact information. This form is typically used for internal purposes to ensure that all necessary information is collected and recorded accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the **Seminar Title**, a brief and descriptive name for the seminar.
2. Provide a detailed **Seminar Description**, summarizing the seminar's content and purpose.
3. Enter the **Seminar Date**, the date when the seminar will take place.
4. Select the **Seminar Time**, using the time input field.
5. Enter the **Seminar Location**, the venue or place where the seminar will be held.
6. Enter the **Seminar Organiser's Name**, the person or entity responsible for the seminar.
7. Enter the **Seminar Organiser's Phone Number**, the contact number of the seminar organiser.
8. Enter the **Seminar Attendees**, a list of people attending the seminar.
9. Enter the **Seminar Attendees' Email**, the email addresses of the seminar attendees.
10. Indicate whether the **Seminar is Organised Locally**, with an option to select multiple answers.
11. Enter the **Seminar Organiser's Email**, the email address of the seminar organiser.
12. Add any **Seminar Notes**, additional information or notes about the seminar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seminar Title** (`seminar_details_form`, text, optional): Enter a brief and descriptive name for the seminar.
* **Seminar Description** (`semnar_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of the seminar, summarizing its content and purpose.
* **Seminar Date** (`seminar_date`, date, optional): Enter the date when the seminar will take place.
* **Seminar Time** (`seminar_time`, time, optional): Select the time for the seminar using the time input field.
* **Seminar Location** (`seminar_location`, text, optional): Enter the venue or place where the seminar will be held.
* **Seminar Organiser** (`seminar_organiser`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or entity responsible for the seminar.
* **Seminar Organiser Phone** (`seminar_organiser_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact number of the seminar organiser.
* **Seminar Attendees** (`seminar_attendees`, text, optional): Enter a list of people attending the seminar.
* **Seminar Attendees Email** (`seminar_attendees_email`, email, optional): Enter the email addresses of the seminar attendees.
* **Seminar is Organised Locally** (`seminar_organisers`, select multiple, optional): Indicate whether the seminar is organised locally or not.
* **Seminar Organiser Email** (`seminar_organiser_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the seminar organiser.
* **Seminar Notes** (`seminar_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information or notes about the seminar.
