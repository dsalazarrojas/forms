# Chronic Disease Data Science Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Disease Data Science Symposium Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all participants. This form gathers information on attendee preferences, requirements, and interests, including attendance selection, session tracks, and presentation needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name, email address, phone number, job title, and organization.
2. Select the correct symposium date you plan to attend.
3. Choose your session tracks of interest.
4. If you are submitting an abstract, fill in the abstract title and select the presentation type (oral or poster).
5. If you are selecting a workshop, choose your preferred workshop.
6. Select your preferred payment method and accommodation needs.
7. Express your interest in networking and visiting exhibitors.
8. Specify how you heard about the symposium.
9. If you have any special requests, please include them in the special requests field.
10. Finally, agree to the symposium terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Attendee Information
* **Full Name** (`text`, required): Enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address for registration confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number for urgent contact.
* **Job Title** (`text`, required): Enter your professional title.
* **Organization** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your institution.

### Page 2: Professional Information
* **Job Title** (`text`, required): Enter your professional title.
* **Organization Type** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of organization you are affiliated with.
* **Field of Expertise** (`select_one`, required): Select your primary field of expertise.
* **Years of Experience** (`select_one`, required): Select your years of experience in your field.
* **Symposium Date** (`select_one`, required): Select the day you plan to attend.

### Page 3: Attendance Selection
* **Attendance Selection** (`select_one`, required): Select which day you will attend.
* **Session Tracks of Interest** (`select_multiple`, required): Select all session tracks that interest you.
* **Workshop Selection** (`select_one`, required): Choose your preferred workshop.

### Page 4: Presentation Needs
* **Abstract Submission** (`select_one`, required): If submitting an abstract, select yes for oral or poster presentation.
* **Abstract Title** (`text`, required if submitting): If submitting an abstract, enter the title.
* **Equipment Needs** (`select_multiple`, required if submitting): If submitting an abstract, select the equipment you need.
* **Presentation** (`text`, required if submitting): If submitting an abstract, specify any additional presentation information.

### Page 5: Payment Information
* **Registration Type** (`select_one`, required): Select your category of registration.
* **Payment Method** (`select_one`, required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Accommodation** (`select_one`, required): Select your accommodation needs.

### Page 6: Networking and Travel
* **Networking Interest** (`select_one`, required): Express your interest in networking events.
* **Exhibitor Interest** (`select_one`, required): Express your interest in visiting exhibitors.
* **How Heard** (`select_one`, required): Select how you heard about the symposium.
* **Travel Grant** (`select_one`, optional): If you are interested in a travel grant, select yes.
* **Travel Needs** (`text`, optional): If you require travel information, specify your needs.
* **Accommodation Needed** (`select_one`, required): Select whether you need hotel information.
* **Booking Needed** (`select_one`, required if booking hotel): If you need booking information, select yes.
* **Hotel Information** (`text`, required if booking hotel): If booking hotel, specify the information.

### Page 7: Special Requests
* **Special Requests** (`text`, required): If you have any special requests, include them here.

### Page 8: Agree to Terms
* **Agree to Terms** (`select_one`, required): Select yes to agree to the symposium terms.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you are submitting an abstract, make sure to select the correct presentation type.
* If you are selecting a workshop, choose your preferred workshop carefully.
* If you have any special requests, be as specific as possible to ensure your needs are met.
