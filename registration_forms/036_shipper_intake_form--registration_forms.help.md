# shipper_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipper_intake_form is a data collection tool used for tracking and managing package deliveries from shippers.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before starting.
2. Complete each field accurately and thoroughly.
3. Double-check your entries for errors and inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pickup Details**: Enter the pickup details of the package, including any relevant information about the pickup location or circumstances.
* **Shipper Details**: Enter shipper details, including name, phone number, and email. 
* **Package Details**: Enter package specifics, including weight, length, width, and height.
* **Pickup Date**: Enter the date the package was picked up.
* **Delivery Date**: Enter the date the package is expected to be delivered.
* **Shipper Phone**: Enter the shipper's phone number.
* **Shipper Email**: Enter the shipper's email address.
* **Shipper Address**: Enter the shipper's business address or location.
* **Package Weight**: Enter the package's weight in the required units.
* **Shipper Company**: Select the shipper company from the options provided (e.g. UPS, FedEx, DHL, USPS).
* **Shipper Contact Person**: Select the contact person responsible for the shipment.
* **Package Length**: Enter the package's length in the required units.
* **Package Width**: Enter the package's width in the required units.
* **Package Height**: Enter the package's height in the required units.
* **Package Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the package.
* **Pickup Time**: Enter the time the package was picked up.
* **Delivery Time**: Enter the expected delivery time.
* **Package Status**: Select the package status from the provided options (In Transit, Delivered, Undeliverable).
* **Shipper Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the shipper.
* **Package Tracking Number**: Select whether the package tracking number is available.
* **Shipper Signature**: Enter the shipper's signature (if applicable).
* **Shipper Signature Date**: Enter the date of the shipper's signature.
* **Shipper Signature Time**: Enter the time of the shipper's signature.
* **Shipper Signature Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the shipper regarding the signature.
