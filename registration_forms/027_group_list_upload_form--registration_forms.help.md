# group_list_upload_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The group_list_upload_form is a form used to collect contact details and upload files for groups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Group Rsters field with the relevant group information.
2. Enter the contact details in the Contact Details field.
3. Enter the email address in the Email field.
4. Enter the phone number in the Phone field.
5. Select the desired response to the Group List question.
6. Upload the required files in the File, File 2, File 3, and File 4 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Rsters** (text, optional): Enter the name of the group you are registering.
* **Contact Details** (number, optional): Enter any additional contact details, such as a reference number.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the group.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the group.
* **Group List** (select multiple, optional): Select Yes to indicate that the group is a list.
* **File** (text, optional): Upload any additional files required for the group.
* **File 2** (text, optional): Upload any additional files required for the group.
* **File 3** (text, optional): Upload any additional files required for the group.
* **File 4** (text, optional): Upload any additional files required for the group.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields, including Group Rsters and Contact Details.
* Ensure that the email address and phone number you enter are correct and valid.
* Use the File fields to upload any necessary documents or attachments.
* Select Yes in the Group List question if the group is a list.
* Review all fields carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
