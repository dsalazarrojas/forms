# Massage Service Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain a client's consent, acknowledgement of risks, and other necessary information to provide massage services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Answer the questions truthfully and accurately to the best of your ability.
2. For fields with multiple options (e.g., "Client Consent" and "Risk Acknowledgement"), select the correct answer for each question.
3. Enter the required information in the text fields (e.g., "Massage Service Terms", "Medical History", etc.).
4. Sign and date the form when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Consent** (Client Consent, Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to indicate you have read and understood the terms of service and acknowledge that you are aware of the risks involved in receiving a massage.
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (Risk Acknowledgement, Select Multiple, Required): Select all risks that you acknowledge, such as injury, allergic reactions, etc.
* **Massage Service Terms** (Massage Service Terms, Text, Required): Enter any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information that may impact your massage experience.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, Text, Required): Enter any medical conditions, allergies, or other relevant health information that may impact your massage experience.
* **Contact Information** (Contact Information, Text, Required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to ensure we can contact you in case of an emergency or if we need to follow up with you.
* **Special Instructions** (Special Instructions, Text, Optional): If you have any special requests or needs, enter them here.
* **Massage Service Provider** (Massage Service Provider, Select Multiple, Optional): Select whether you require a specific massage service provider or have any preferences.
* **Client Comments** (Client Comments, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback about your massage experience.
* **Staff Comments** (Staff Comments, Text, Optional): Enter any comments or feedback about the massage staff or services provided.
* **Date Completed** (Date Completed, Date, Required): Enter the date you completed this form.
* **Completed By** (Completed By, Select One, Required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have completed this form and are ready to receive a massage.
* **Completed By Title** (Completed By Title, Text, Optional): Enter your title or position (e.g., Mr./Ms./Mrs./Dr.).
* **Massage License** (Massage License, Text, Optional): If you have a massage license, enter the details here.
* **Business License** (Business License, Text, Optional): If the business providing the massage services has a license, enter the details here.
* **Business Name** (Business Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the business providing the massage services.
* **Address** (Address, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the business providing the massage services.
* **Business Phone** (Business Phone, Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the business providing the massage services.
* **Business Email** (Business Email, Text, Optional): Enter the email address of the business providing the massage services.
* **Fax** (Fax, Text, Optional): Enter the fax number of the business providing the massage services.
* **Business Website** (Business Website, Text, Optional): Enter the website of the business providing the massage services.
* **Insurance** (Insurance, Text, Required): Enter your insurance information (e.g., policy number, provider).
* **Liability** (Liability, Text, Optional): Enter any liability information (e.g., waiver, release).
* **Client Signature** (Client Signature, Text, Required): Sign and date the form to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Business Signature** (Business Signature, Text, Required): Sign and date the form to indicate the business's agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Review** (Review, Select Multiple, Optional): Select whether you would like to provide a review of your massage experience.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions truthfully and accurately to ensure a safe and enjoyable massage experience.
* If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to ask the business providing the massage services.
* Sign and date the form when required and ensure the business providing the massage services has signed and dated the form as well.
