# Sibling Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sibling Participation Request Form is a tool used to request participation in a sibling program or activity. It is designed to collect necessary information from siblings who want to join the program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are not already registered in the sibling program or activity.
3. Submit the form and wait for a response from the organization.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`name_1`, Text, Optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`name_2`, Text, Optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Relationship to the Program** (`relationship`, Text, Optional): Please describe your relationship to the program or activity, such as "I am a sibling of a student participating in the program."
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): Please enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Please enter your phone number. Note: This field is not required but is used as an additional contact method.
* **Form Footer** (This field is not displayed as it is a note and not a field for end user input)

Note: The fields marked as Optional are not required, but it's recommended to fill them out for better processing of your request.
