# tutoring_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tutoring Registration Form" is designed to collect information from students who are interested in seeking tutoring services. This form is used to gather details about the student, including their contact information and scheduling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your name in the "name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
* Describe your grade level in the "grade_level" field.
* Specify your learning needs in the "learning_needs" field.
* Provide a brief description of your scheduling preferences in the "scheduling" field.
* Choose a preferred schedule time in the "schedule_time" field.
* Choose a preferred schedule date in the "schedule_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, optional): Enter your current grade level (e.g., 9th, 10th, 11th, etc.).
* **Learning Needs** (`learning_needs`, text, optional): Describe the subject(s) you need help with or any other learning needs you have.
* **Scheduling** (`scheduling`, text, optional): Briefly describe when and how you would like to be tutored.
* **Schedule Time** (`schedule_time`, time, optional): Choose a preferred schedule time.
* **Schedule Date** (`schedule_date`, date, optional): Choose a preferred schedule date.
