# Industry Conference Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Industry Conference Registration Form is designed to collect information from conference attendees for registration and event management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select your time zone and preferred registration method (online, on-site, or mail).
4. Choose your payment method (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
5. If you have a preferred payment method that is not listed, enter it in the "Payment Method Other" field.
6. Enter the name of the event you are attending.
7. Enter the date and time of the event.
8. Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone from the list (PST, EST, CST, MST).
* **Registration Method**: Select how you want to register for the conference (online, on-site, or mail).
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you want to pay for the conference (credit card, bank transfer, or check).
* **Payment Method Other**: If you have a different payment method, enter it in this field.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the conference organizers for assistance.
