# Corporate Retreat Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from attendees of the corporate retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the first page, which includes fields for **User Name**, **Attendee Name**, **Email**, **Phone**, **Checkin Date**, and **Checkout Date**. Ensure that you fill out these fields accurately and completely.
2. Once you've completed the first page, proceed to the second page, which includes fields for **Company**, **Role**, **Department**, and **Job Title**. These fields are optional, so you can skip them if not applicable.
3. On the third page, select your **Attendance Confirmation** by choosing 'True', 'False', or 'Maybe' from the dropdown menu.
4. Finally, ensure that you've filled out all the required fields, and your form will be complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Your username, which should match the username associated with your company account.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): The name of the person registering for the retreat.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): The email address associated with your company account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Checkin Date** (`checkin_date`, date, required): The date you plan to check in for the retreat.
* **Checkout Date** (`checkout_date`, date, required): The date you plan to check out from the retreat.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required): The name of your company.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Your role within the company, if applicable.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): The department you belong to within the company, if applicable.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Your job title within the company, if applicable.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date you registered for the retreat.
* **Attendance Confirmation** (`attendance_confirmation`, select_one, required): Confirm your attendance status by selecting 'True', 'False', or 'Maybe' from the dropdown menu.
