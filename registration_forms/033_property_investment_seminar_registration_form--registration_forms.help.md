# Property Investment Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration to a Property Investment Seminar. It's designed to collect relevant information from participants to ensure an accurate headcount and to tailor the seminar content based on participant needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Participant Name** in the first field. This is the name of the person registering for the seminar.
2.  Enter your **Email** in the second field. This is the email address where you'll receive updates about the seminar.
3.  Enter your **Phone Number** in the third field. This is the contact number for any follow-up communication.
4.  Enter the name of your **Company** in the fourth field. This helps us understand your affiliation with the industry.
5.  Enter your **Job Title** in the fifth field. This gives us an understanding of your role within the company.
6.  Select the date of the **Seminar Date** you plan to attend from the calendar.
7.  Choose a time for the **Seminar Time** you're planning to attend.
8.  Select your **Participant Type** from the options provided. You can choose to be an Investor, Attendee, or Both.
9.  If you are a representative of your company, select the **Company Representative** option.
10. Add any additional **Notes** about your registration in the last field. This helps us tailor the content to your needs.
