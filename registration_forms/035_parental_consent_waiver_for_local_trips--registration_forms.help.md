# Parental Consent and Waiver for Local Trips - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent and Waiver for Local Trips form is a critical document that requires parents or guardians to provide necessary information and consent for their child's participation in local trips. This form is designed to ensure the child's safety and well-being during the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. **General Information**: Fill in the general information section with your child's basic details, such as name and date of birth.
2. **Parent Details**: Provide your contact information and relationship with the child.
3. **Trip Details**: Enter the trip details, including trip leader's name, phone number, and email.
4. **Emergency Contacts**: List your emergency contact information, including name, relationship, phone number, address, and email.
5. **Waiver**: Sign the waiver section to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the trip.
6. **Date of Consent**: Date and time of when you signed the form.
7. **Signed By**: Select the name of the person who signed the form.
8. **Signed Date**: Date and time when the form was signed.
9. **Signed Time**: Time when the form was signed.
10. **Signature**: Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
11. **Minor Details**: Select the minor's name, age, and grade level.
12. **Minor Consent**: Select whether the minor has given consent for the trip.
13. **Parent Consent**: Select whether you have given consent for the minor's participation in the trip.
14. **Organization**: Enter your organization's name, phone number, and email.
15. **Save and Submit**: Once you have completed all the required fields, click the "Save and Submit" button to submit the form.
