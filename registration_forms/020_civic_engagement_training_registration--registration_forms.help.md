# Civic Engagement Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register for civic engagement training sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field. 
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field, so that we can send you training materials.
3. Select your **Organization or Community Group (if any)** in the "Organization or Community Group (if any)" field, if applicable. 
4. Choose the **Select Training Modules** that you wish to attend. You can select multiple modules. 
5. Choose your **Preferred Training Date** from the options provided. 
6. Select your current **Experience Level** with civic engagement. 
7. Enter your motivation for attending the training in the "What do you hope to gain from this training?" field. 
8. If attending an in-person session, select your **Dietary Requirements** and any **Accessibility Needs**. 
9. Select how you **Heard About This Training**. 
10. If you are a student, select "I am a student (request waiver)" in the "I understand there is a 50.00 registration fee (waivable for students)" field. 
11. Agree to the **Cancellation Policy** by selecting "I have read and agree to the cancellation policy". 

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Registrant Name, text, required): Enter your full name for registration purposes. 
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address so that we can send you training materials. 
* **Organization or Community Group (if any)** (Text, optional): If you are a member of an organization or community group, please enter it here. 
* **Select Training Modules** (Select Training Module, select multiple, required): Choose the training modules you wish to attend. You can select multiple modules. 
* **Preferred Training Date** (Select Training Date, select one, required): Choose the date that suits you best for the training. 
* **Your current experience with civic engagement** (Experience Level, select one, required): Select your current experience level with civic engagement. 
* **What do you hope to gain from this training?** (Text, required): Enter your motivation for attending the training. 
* **Logistics and Dietary Needs** (Text, optional): Enter any logistical needs or dietary restrictions you may have for in-person training sessions. 
* **Any accessibility needs** (Text, optional): If you have any accessibility requirements, please enter them here. 
* **How did you hear about this training?** (Text, select one, optional): Select how you heard about this training. 
* **I understand there is a 50.00 registration fee (waivable for students)** (Select Training Module, select one, required): If you are a student, please select "I am a student (request waiver)". 
* **I have read and agree to the cancellation policy** (Select Training Date, select one, required): Agree to the cancellation policy by selecting "I have read and agree to the cancellation policy". 
* **Date of Registration** (Date of Registration, date, required): The date and time of registration. 

## Tips
Please ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.
