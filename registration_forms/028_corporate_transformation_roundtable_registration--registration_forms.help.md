# Corporate Transformation Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from interested executives to participate in the Corporate Transformation Roundtable event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is the name that will be used to address you in any communication related to the event.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is your surname, as you would like it to be displayed in any event materials.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the "Email" field. This will be used to send you event details and reminders.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field. This will be used to contact you in case of last-minute event changes or important details.
5. Choose your **Title** from the options provided in the "Title" field. This will help us understand your professional role and tailor the event content to your interests.
6. Enter your **Company** name in the "Company" field. This will be used to categorize you among fellow participants from your organization.
7. Select your **Topic Interests** by ticking the boxes next to "Yes" or "No" for each relevant topic. This will help us tailor the event content and discussions to your specific interests.
8. In the "Event Details" field, enter any additional information you feel would be relevant to sharing with fellow participants or the event organizers.
9. Choose "Yes" or "No" for **Attendance Consent** to indicate your permission to share your contact information with other attendees.
10. Finally, select "Yes" or "No" for **Agreement**, confirming your understanding of the event guidelines and your commitment to actively participate and contribute to the discussions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: 
	+ This is your given name, as would be used to address you in any event communication.
* **Last Name**:
	+ This is your surname, as would be used in event materials.
* **Email**:
	+ A valid email address where you will receive event details and reminders.
* **Phone**:
	+ A number where you can be reached in case of event changes or important details.
* **Title**:
	+ An indication of your professional role or title within your company.
* **Company**:
	+ The name of your organization where you work.
* **Topic Interests**:
	+ Select the topics that interest you the most, so we can tailor the event content to your needs.
* **Event Details**:
	+ Any additional information you'd like to share with fellow participants or the event organizers.
* **Attendance Consent**:
	+ Confirm your permission to share your contact information with other attendees.
* **Agreement**:
	+ Confirm your understanding and commitment to the event guidelines and participation.

## Tips

* Please fill in your information accurately and completely, as this will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will send you event details and reminders to the email address you provide.
* You are welcome to change your registration information before the event.
