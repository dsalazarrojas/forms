# Gymnastics Term Fee Payment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to record payment details for gymnastics term fees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the "Email Address" field.
4. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your goals in the "Goals" field.
5. Choose the "Payment Method" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter the Payment Amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
7. Select a Date for the payment in the "Payment Date" field.
8. Select a Time for the payment in the "Payment Time" field.
9. Choose the "Registration Type" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
10. Select the "Payment Status" by choosing "Active" or "Inactive".
11. Enter the Term in the "Term" field.
12. Enter the Fee in the "Fee" field.
13. Enter the Fee Amount in the "Fee Amount" field.
14. Select a Date for the fee in the "Fee Date" field.
15. Select a Time for the fee in the "Fee Time" field.
16. Choose whether to accept "Payment Terms" by selecting "Yes" or "No".
17. Select the "Fee Status" by choosing "Active" or "Inactive".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Goals** (Select Multiple, optional): Select your goals.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, optional): Choose the payment method.
* **Payment Amount** (Number, optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (Date, optional): Select a date for the payment.
* **Payment Time** (Time, optional): Select a time for the payment.
* **Registration Type** (Select One, optional): Choose the registration type.
* **Payment Status** (Select One, optional): Select the payment status.
* **Term** (Text, optional): Enter the term.
* **Fee** (Text, optional): Enter the fee.
* **Fee Amount** (Number, optional): Enter the fee amount.
* **Fee Date** (Date, optional): Select a date for the fee.
* **Fee Time** (Time, optional): Select a time for the fee.
* **Payment Terms** (Select One, optional): Choose whether to accept payment terms.
* **Fee Status** (Select One, optional): Select the fee status.
