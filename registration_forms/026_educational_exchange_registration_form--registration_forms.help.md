# educational_exchange_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for students participating in educational exchanges to register and provide necessary information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field with accurate and relevant information.
2. If you're unsure about any field, refer to the explanations below for guidance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): Enter your name and any other information about yourself.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): If you have a student ID, please enter it here.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the exchange program you're participating in.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your program.
* **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Exchange Duration** (`exchange_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your exchange program.
* **Institution Name** (`institution_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the institution hosting or participating in the exchange.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Address** (`contact_address`, text, optional): Enter your contact address.
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether your exchange is Inbound or Outbound.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, select_multiple, optional): Select your current student status: Active, Inactive, or Pending.

Please note that all fields are optional, but providing accurate information helps us to better understand your experience and preferences. Thank you for taking the time to fill out this form!
