# Dance Workshop Schedule Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow students to book a time slot for a dance workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the available schedule options for the dance workshop.
2.  Select the desired schedule date and time slot.
3.  Choose the instructor you wish to work with.
4.  Enter your student information, including your name, email, and phone number.
5.  Review and submit your booking details.
