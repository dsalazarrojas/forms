# Gym Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Form is a comprehensive form designed to collect essential information from applicants for gym membership registration. It is used to efficiently process membership applications and ensure that all the necessary details are collected accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Client Information**: Please provide your personal details, such as your first name, last name, address, city, state, and phone number.
2. **Emergency Contact Information**: Enter the name, phone number, and email of your emergency contact person.
3. **Relationship**: Select the relationship between the emergency contact person and you (Client, Family Member, Spouse, or Other).
4. **Membership Information**: Choose the membership type (e.g., Month-to-Month, Month-to-Month with Auto Draft, etc.) and duration (e.g., Month, 2 Months, etc.).
5. **Membership Details**: Select the start and end dates for your membership.
6. **Membership Status**: Indicate whether your membership is currently active or cancelled.
7. **Membership Notes**: Add any additional notes about your membership, if necessary.
8. **Client Notes**: Add any notes about the client, if necessary.
9. **Membership Price and Payment Information**: Enter the membership price and select the payment method (e.g., Credit/Debit, Online Payment, etc.) and frequency (e.g., Monthly, Bi-Monthly).
10. **Review**: Review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all the information is accurate and complete.
