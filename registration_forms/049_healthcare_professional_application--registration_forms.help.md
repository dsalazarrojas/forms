# healthcare_professional_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from healthcare professionals for registration or onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the following fields:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email
	* Job Title
	* Date of Birth
	* Years of Experience

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as you would like it to appear on official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as you would like it to appear on official documents.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your professional email address. This field is used for contact and communication purposes.
* **Job Title** (select_one, optional): Choose your job title from the list:
	+ Doctor
	+ Nurse
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Years of Experience** (number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working as a healthcare professional.

## Tips
* If you are a non-US healthcare professional, please note that this form may require additional information or verification procedures.
* Please ensure that all fields are properly filled out before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your supervisor or HR representative for clarification.
