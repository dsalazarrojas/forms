# halloween_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about Halloween events for contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Event Info field with the name of the event you want to register for.
2. Provide your information in the Attendee Info field.
3. Enter your contact information in the Contact Info field.
4. Select the Date and Time of the event in the Date Time field.
5. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you want to register for.
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, text, required): Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the event.
* **Date Time** (`date_time`, date, required): Select the date and time of the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number for the event contact.
