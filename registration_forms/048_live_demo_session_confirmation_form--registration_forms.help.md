# Live_Demo_Session_Confirmation_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to confirm participant details for a live demo session. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate records and a smooth event execution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant's name.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Select your attendance status (Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe).
5. Add any additional notes about your attendance or the session.
6. Review your input before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or identification documents.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address that you use for communication. We will use this to keep you updated on the demo session details.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used for last-minute updates or if we need to reach you directly.
- **Attendance Status** (`attendance_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose how you plan to attend the live demo session (Attending, Not Attending, or Maybe).
- **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or details about your attendance or the session that we should know about.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Name) accurately.
- Double-check your email address for correct formatting.
- If you're unsure about your attendance status, choose 'Maybe' to let us know you're interested but not confirmed yet.
- Use the 'Notes' field to provide any additional context or information about your participation.
