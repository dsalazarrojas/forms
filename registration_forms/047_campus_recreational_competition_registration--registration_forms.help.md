# Campus Recreational Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for campus recreational competitions, such as sports events. It collects essential information for liability and emergency contact purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your ID or passport.
2. **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your ID or passport.
3. **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number, which can be found on your student ID card or in your student portal.
4. **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted about the competition.
5. **Phone Number**: Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached in case of an emergency.
6. **Competition Name**: Enter the name of the competition you're registering for (e.g., basketball tournament).
7. **Sport or Activity**: Select the sport or activity you're participating in from the dropdown list (e.g., Basketball, Volleyball, etc.).
8. **Competition Date**: Enter the date of the competition in the correct format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
9. **Competition Time**: Enter the time of the competition in the correct format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
10. **Type of Participation**: Select your type of participation (e.g., Individual, Team Player, Team Captain, Coach/Organizer).
11. **Team Name**: If you're participating as part of a team, enter the team name.
12. **Number of Players on Team**: If you're participating as part of a team, enter the number of players on your team.
13. **Your Experience Level**: Select your level of experience from the dropdown list (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
14. **Previous Competition Experience**: If applicable, enter any previous competition experience you have.
15. **Medical Conditions or Physical Limitations**: If you have any medical conditions or physical limitations that may impact your participation, enter them here.
16. **Current or Recent Injuries**: If you have any current or recent injuries that may impact your participation, enter them here.
17. **Emergency Contact Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of someone who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
18. **Liability Acknowledgement**: Select "Yes, I accept" to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the liability waiver and rules.
