Life Insurance Subscription Form - Help Guide
=====================================

## Purpose
This form is used to register an individual for life insurance coverage. It is designed to collect essential information for the policyholder's identification and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field in the format of [name@example.com](mailto:name@example.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (no dashes or spaces, e.g., 1234567890).
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format of dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 12/01/1990).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Provide a valid email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false): Enter a valid phone number without any spaces or dashes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format of dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 12/01/1990).

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for any typos or errors.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us for assistance.
