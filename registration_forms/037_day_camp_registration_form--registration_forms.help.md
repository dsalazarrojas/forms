# Day Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participants to register for day camp activities. It collects demographic and health information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all campers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the required information. You can save your progress and come back later if you need to.
2. Start by entering the participant's name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Provide the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter the participant's date of birth in the "Participant Date Of Birth" field. This is the date the participant was born.
5. Select whether the participant attends school from the options "Yes" or "No" in the "Participant School" field.
6. Enter the participant's phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
7. Enter the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
8. Enter the participant's address in the "Participant Address" field.
9. List any allergies the participant has in the "Participant Allergies" field.
10. Enter any medical notes or conditions in the "Participant Medical Notes" field.
11. List any medications the participant takes in the "Participant Medications" field.
12. List any allergies related to medications in the "Participant Medication Allergies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Text)**: This is the name of the participant.
* **Parent Name (Text)**: This is the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Participant Date Of Birth (Date)**: This is the date the participant was born.
* **Participant School (Select One)**: Whether the participant attends school or not.
* **Participant Phone (Text)**: This is the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Email (Email)**: This is the participant's email address.
* **Participant Address (Text)**: This is the participant's address.
* **Participant Allergies (Text)**: List any allergies the participant has.
* **Participant Medical Notes (Note)**: Enter any medical conditions or notes.
* **Participant Medications (Text)**: List any medications the participant takes.
* **Participant Medication Allergies (Text)**: List any allergies related to medications.
