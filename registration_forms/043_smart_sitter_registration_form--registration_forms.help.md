# Smart Sitter Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Sitter Registration Form is designed to collect information from clients for services provided by sitters or caregivers. This form helps ensure that both the client and sitter have a smooth and efficient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Client Information Form (Page 1) with your personal details.
2. Provide your emergency contact information in the Emergency Contact Form (Page 2).
3. Specify the service details and preferences in the Service Details and Service Preferences Forms (Pages 3-4).
4. Choose the payment method for the services in the Payment Method Form (Page 5).
5. Review and sign the Service Agreement and Client Service Agreement (Pages 6-7).
6. Provide any additional information and services you require in the Additional Services Form (Page 8).
7. Specify any special requirements or services you need in the Services Form (Page 9).
8. Add any notes or comments for the sitter's reference in the Client Notes and Service Notes Forms (Pages 10-11).
9. Review and sign the Payment Notes and Client Notes Forms (Pages 12-13).
10. Finally, provide any other notes or preferences in the Client Preferences Forms (Pages 14-15).

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully before proceeding to the next page.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and consistent data throughout the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, ask the sitter for clarification.
* The form should be completed in one session to ensure that the client and sitter have a smooth experience.
* The sitter will review the form before starting work to ensure that they understand the client's requirements.
