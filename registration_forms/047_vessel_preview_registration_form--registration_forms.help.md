# vessel_preview_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "vessel_preview_registration_form" is used for previewing registration purposes, likely for registering vessels or similar items in an inventory or database management system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, and Date.
2. Optional fields: Phone, Note, Select One, Select Multiple, Decimal, and Integer can be filled in as needed.
3. Ensure you fill in the correct format for each field type (e.g., phone number in the phone field).
4. Review and submit the form once you have filled in all desired information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter the first name of the person or item being registered.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter the last name of the person or item being registered.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter the email address of the person or item being registered.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or item being registered.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event or item being registered.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the event or item being registered.
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional note or comment about the item being registered.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select`, optional): Choose one option from the available list. Please review the options list for specific choices.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select`, optional): Choose multiple options from the available list. Please review the options list for specific choices.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, `number`, optional): Enter a decimal value, such as a measurement or weight.
* **Integer** (`integer`, `number`, optional): Enter an integer value, such as a count or quantity.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in the correct format for each field type.
* Review the options list for the Select One and Select Multiple fields to ensure you are choosing the correct options.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
