# Health Maintenance Organization Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Maintenance Organization Registration form is a tool used to collect information from individuals or organizations registering for health maintenance services. This guide will walk you through the form's purpose, how to complete it, and provide a detailed explanation of each field.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

* Please make sure you have read and understood the purpose of this form before proceeding.
* Fill out the required fields marked with an *asterisk* (*).
* Enter your details accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your entries carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (form_first_name): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (form_last_name): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (form_email): Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
* **Phone** (form_phone): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Category** (form_category): Select one of the available options from the dropdown menu.
* **Description** (form_description): Enter a brief description of your organization or services offered.
* **Date** (form_date): Enter the date for your registration.
* **Time** (form_time): Enter the time of your registration.
* **Note** (form_note): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Select Multiple 1** (form_select_multiple_1): Choose one or more of the available options.
