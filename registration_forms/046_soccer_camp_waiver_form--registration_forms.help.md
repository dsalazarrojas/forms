# soccer_camp_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration and waiver purposes for children participating in a soccer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Camp Details" section with any relevant information about the camp, such as the camp name and date.
2. Enter your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
5. List any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in the camp in the "Medical Conditions" field.
6. Select all the activities you consent to participate in from the "Consent to Participate" field.
7. If applicable, provide your release of liability information in the "Release of Liability" field.
8. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
9. If you are a guardian, select your relationship to the child (e.g. Mother, Father, Both, Other) from the "Guardian Information" field.
10. Sign and date the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camp Details**: Enter any relevant information about the camp, such as the camp name and date.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and contact information of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have that may affect your participation in the camp.
* **Consent to Participate**: Select all activities you consent to participate in, such as Soccer, Camp Activities, Games.
* **Release of Liability**: If applicable, provide your release of liability information.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
* **Guardian Information**: If you are a guardian, select your relationship to the child.
* **Signature**: Sign and date the form to confirm your participation.
