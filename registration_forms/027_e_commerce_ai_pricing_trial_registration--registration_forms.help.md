# e_commerce_ai_pricing_trial_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for e-commerce businesses to register their trial pricing information. The form helps collect relevant details about the merchant, trial, and contact information to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields and provide as much detail as possible for the optional fields.
2.  Ensure you fill in the form accurately and completely.
3.  Review the form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Merchant Info** (Merchant Info, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about the merchant, such as their name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Trial Details** (Trial Details, text, optional): In this field, provide a brief description of the trial, including its duration, pricing, and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, text, optional): Here, you can enter the contact information of the person responsible for the trial, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Trial Signups** (Trial Signups, text, optional): Enter the number of trial signups you expect, including their type (e.g., free trial, paid trial).
* **Merchant Qualification** (Merchant Qualification, text, optional): Describe how you qualify merchants for your e-commerce business, such as through a review process or by meeting specific criteria.
* **Review** (Review, text, optional): Share any review or feedback from previous trial participants to help improve your service.
* **Trial Start Date** (Trial Start Date, date, optional): Enter the date when the trial is expected to start.
* **Trial End Date** (Trial End Date, date, optional): Enter the date when the trial is expected to end.

## Tips
* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form to ensure a successful registration process.
* Double-check the form before submitting it to ensure you have included all necessary information.
