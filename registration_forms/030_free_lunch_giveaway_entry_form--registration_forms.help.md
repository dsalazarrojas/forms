# Free Lunch Giveaway Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Free Lunch Giveaway Entry Form is a registration form designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a free lunch giveaway. The form is intended to gather contact and demographic details from potential participants to facilitate the organization and distribution of free lunch items.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information on the first page.
2. Enter the giveaway information on the second page.
3. Provide contact details on the third page.
4. Share your preferences on the fourth page.
5. Answer the contest question on the fifth page.
6. Finally, submit your application on the sixth and last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration** (text, required): This is the first page where you will enter your registration information. Please provide the required details to help us identify you correctly.
	+ This field is optional.
* **Giveaway** (text, optional): If you have any additional information about the giveaway you'd like to share, please enter it here.
	+ You can add any relevant details about your interest or eligibility for the giveaway.
* **Contact** (text, required): Provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
	+ This may include your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact method.
* **Preferences** (text, optional): Share your food preferences, dietary restrictions, or any other details that might affect your participation.
	+ This could include your preferred cuisine, any food allergies, or special requests.
* **Contest** (text, required): Answer the question related to the free lunch giveaway contest.
	+ The contest question will be displayed on the fifth page of the form.
* **Submission** (text, required): Finally, click the submit button to complete your application and participate in the free lunch giveaway.
	+ Make sure to review your entries before submitting, as this is the last chance to correct any mistakes.
