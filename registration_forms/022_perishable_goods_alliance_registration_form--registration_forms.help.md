# Perishable Goods Alliance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from users to register their entities involved in perishable goods alliances. The purpose of this form is to gather the necessary details to create a registration record for each entity.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill in the form carefully, ensuring that you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- If you are registering on behalf of another entity, please ensure that you have the necessary permissions to do so.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Base Information** (`base_info`, text, optional): This section is for general information about the entity you are registering. Please provide a brief description of the entity and its purpose.
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): This section is for the contact information of the person registering the entity. Please provide your name, email address, and phone number.
* **Partner Information** (`partner_info`, text, optional): This section is for information about the partner(s) involved in the perishable goods alliance. If there are multiple partners, list them separately.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): This section is for additional contact information about the partner(s) or entity. Please provide any relevant contact details, such as email addresses or phone numbers.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a final submit button to complete the registration process. Please review the form carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have all necessary permissions to register on behalf of the entity you are representing.
- Review the form carefully to avoid errors.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
