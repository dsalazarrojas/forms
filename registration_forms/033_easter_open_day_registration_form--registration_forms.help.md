# easter_open_day_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Easter Open Day registration form is used to capture information from individuals attending the event, including their contact details, demographics, and preferences. This information is essential for event management and organization, enabling us to provide a smooth and enjoyable experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the text field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the text field labeled "last_name".
3. Provide your **Email Address** in the text field labeled "email".
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the text field labeled "phone_number".
5. Select your **Date of Birth** in the date field labeled "date_of_birth".
6. Choose your preferred **Time of Service** in the time field labeled "time_of_service".
7. Specify the number of **Guests** you will be bringing with you in the number field labeled "number_of_guests".
8. Enter your **Age** in the number field labeled "age".
9. Select your preferred **Contact Preference** from the dropdown menu labeled "contact_preference".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
  - Type: email
  - Required: false

- **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the format of month/day/year (MM/DD/YYYY).
  - Type: date
  - Required: false

- **Time of Service**: Choose the time of service that suits your schedule.
  - Type: time
  - Required: false

- **Number of Guests**: Specify the number of guests you will be bringing with you.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Age**: Enter your age.
  - Type: number
  - Required: false

- **Contact Preference**: Select how you would like to be contacted in case of any updates or changes.
  - Type: select_one
  - Options: By Phone, By Email, By Text
  - Required: false
