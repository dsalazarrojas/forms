# young_member_group_expression_of_interest - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from young members who want to join the group. It helps the group administrators to understand the member's interests and motivations better.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Fill out your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Type the name of the group you are interested in joining in the "Group name" field.
6. Select the group interests that apply to you from the "Group interests" field.
7. Write a short paragraph explaining why you want to join the group in the "Why join" field.
8. Choose the reason that best motivates you to join the group from the "Group motivation" field.
9. Indicate how you found us from the "How did you find us" field.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree" from the "Agree to terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Fill out your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Group name** (`group_name`, text, required): Type the name of the group you are interested in joining.
* **Group interests** (`group_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select the group interests that apply to you.
*   • Yes: You are interested in this group interest.
*   • No: You are not interested in this group interest.
* **Why join** (`why_group`, text, required): Write a short paragraph explaining why you want to join the group.
* **Group motivation** (`group_motivation`, select_one, required): Choose the reason that best motivates you to join the group.
*   • Yes: This is the reason that motivates you to join the group.
*   • No: This is not the reason that motivates you to join the group.
* **How did you find us** (`how_did_you_find_us`, select_one, required): Indicate how you found us.
*   • Yes: You found us through this platform.
*   • No: You did not find us through this platform.
* **Agree to terms and conditions** (`agree`, select_one, required): Agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "I Agree".
*   • I Agree: You agree to the terms and conditions.
*   • Other: This is not the correct answer.
