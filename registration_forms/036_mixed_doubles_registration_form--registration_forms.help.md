# Mixed Doubles Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register mixed doubles players and teams for events. It collects information about players, such as their names, genders, ages, and ratings. The form also includes fields for event and team details, such as event names, dates, and times.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the player 1 and player 2 information sections, including their names, genders, and ages.
2. Enter the event name and date.
3. Provide contact information, including contact email and phone number.
4. Enter the team formation date and time.
5. Select the division (yes or no) and level (high or low) for each player.
6. Enter any notes or comments about each player.
7. Add any additional notes or comments about the team.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player 1 Name** (`player_1_name`, text, required): Enter the name of player 1.
* **Player 2 Name** (`player_2_name`, text, required): Enter the name of player 2.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Player 1 Gender** (`player_1_gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of player 1.
* **Player 2 Gender** (`player_2_gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of player 2.
* **Player 1 Age** (`player_1_age`, number, required): Enter the age of player 1.
* **Player 2 Age** (`player_2_age`, number, required): Enter the age of player 2.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Player 1 Notes** (`player_1_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes or comments about player 1.
* **Player 2 Notes** (`player_2_notes`, note, required): Enter any notes or comments about player 2.
* **Tournament Name** (`tournament_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the tournament.
* **Division** (`division`, select_one, required): Select whether the player is part of a division (yes or no).
* **Team Formation Date** (`team_formation_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the team formation.
* **Team Formation Time** (`team_formation_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the team formation.
* **Player 1 Level** (`player_1_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of player 1 (high or low).
* **Player 2 Level** (`player_2_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of player 2 (high or low).
* **Event Formation Time** (`event_formation_time`, time, required): Enter the event formation time.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the team.
* **Player 1 Rating** (`player_1_rating`, number, required): Enter the rating of player 1.
* **Player 2 Rating** (`player_2_rating`, number, required): Enter the rating of player 2.
* **Player 1 Gender** (`player_1_gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of player 1.
* **Player 2 Gender** (`player_2_gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of player 2.
* **Tournament ID** (`tournament_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the tournament.
* **Player 1 Notes** (`player_1_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about player 1.
* **Player 2 Notes** (`player_2_notes`, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments about player 2.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask for help.
