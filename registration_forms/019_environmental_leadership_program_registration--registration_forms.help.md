# Environmental Leadership Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Environmental Leadership Program Registration form is used to register participants for the Environmental Leadership Program. This program aims to promote environmental leadership and sustainability among individuals and organizations. The form is designed to gather essential information about the participants, their organizations, and their program details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill in the required fields (marked with *) and provide as much detail as possible in the optional fields.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on your situation.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details (Page 1)**
	+ **participant_details** (text): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Program Availability (Page 2)**
	+ **availability** (select_multiple): Indicate your availability to participate in the program by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Commitments (Page 3)**
	+ **commitments** (select_one): Indicate your level of commitment to the program by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Program Type (Page 4)**
	+ **program_type** (select_one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' based on the type of program you are registering for.
* **Start and End Dates (Page 5)**
	+ **start_date** (date): Enter the date you plan to start the program.
	+ **end_date** (date): Enter the date you plan to end the program.
* **Location (Page 6)**
	+ **location** (text): Enter the location where the program will take place.
* **Contact Information (Page 7)**
	+ **contact_name** (text): Enter your name as a contact person.
	+ **contact_email** (email): Enter your email address as a contact person.
	+ **contact_phone** (text): Enter your phone number as a contact person.
* **Organization Information (Page 8)**
	+ **org_name** (text): Enter your organization's name.
	+ **org_address** (text): Enter your organization's address.
	+ **org_city** (text): Enter your organization's city.
	+ **org_state** (text): Enter your organization's state or province.
	+ **org_postal** (text): Enter your organization's postal code.
	+ **org_country** (text): Enter your organization's country.
* **Program Details (Page 9)**
	+ **program_details** (text): Provide any additional details about the program.
* **Cohort Type (Page 10)**
	+ **cohort_type** (select_one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you are part of a cohort.
* **Program Length (Page 11)**
	+ **program_length** (select_one): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the program length.
* **Program Status (Page 12)**
	+ **program_status** (select_one): Select 'Active' or 'Inactive' to indicate the program status.
* **Program Commitments (Page 13)**
	+ **program_commitments** (select_one): Indicate your level of commitment to the program by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Program Goals (Page 14)**
	+ **program_goals** (select_multiple): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate the program goals.
* **Notes (Page 15)**
	+ **org_notes** (note): Provide any additional notes about the program.
	+ **org_notes_other** (text): Provide any other notes about the program.
	+ **org_notes_text** (text): Enter any additional text about the program notes.

Please note that some fields are required (marked with *), and you must fill those in to proceed. Double-check your answers before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
