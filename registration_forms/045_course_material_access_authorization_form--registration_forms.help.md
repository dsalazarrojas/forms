# Course Material Access Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Course Material Access Authorization Form is used to verify that the user has permission to access course materials. This form is used to determine if the user can access specific course materials based on their ID, institution, role, access level, start date, and end date.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User ID** (`<name>`, `number`, required/optional: `false`).
2. Enter your **Course Code** (`<type>`, `text`, required/optional: `false`).
3. Enter your **Institution** (`<type>`, `text`, required/optional: `false`).
4. Select your **Role** (`<type>`, `select_one`, required/optional: `false`).
   * Choose between 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether you have permission to access course materials.
5. Select your **Access Level** (`<type>`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: `false`).
   * Choose between High or Low to indicate the level of access you have to course materials.
6. Enter your **Access Start Date** (`<type>`, `date`, required/optional: `false`).
7. Enter your **Access End Date** (`<type>`, `date`, required/optional: `false`).
8. Enter your **Access Granted Until** (`<type>`, `date`, required/optional: `false`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User ID**: Enter your unique user ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Course Code**: Enter the code of the course you are trying to access.
* **Institution**: Enter the institution associated with your account.
* **Role**: Select Yes if you have permission to access course materials, No otherwise.
* **Access Level**: Select High for high level access or Low for low level access.
* **Access Start Date**: Enter the date when you first gained access to course materials.
* **Access End Date**: Enter the date when your access to course materials will expire.
* **Access Granted Until**: Enter the date when your access to course materials will end.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate verification.
* Be honest about your role and access level to avoid unauthorized access.
* Review your form carefully before submitting it.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, contact your administrator for assistance.
