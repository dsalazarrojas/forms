# Gym Membership Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Membership Discharge Form is a document used by gym members to notify the gym administration of their intention to cancel their membership. The form is used to gather information about the member and the reason for cancellation.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Fill out the form carefully** to ensure that your cancellation request is processed correctly.
- **Select Member 1, Member 2, or Member 3** from the list if you are cancelling membership for multiple members.
- **Choose a reason for cancellation** from the provided options or type in your own reason if it's not listed.
- **Add any additional comments** if you have any further explanations or concerns.
- **Sign and date the form** to confirm your identity and cancellation request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Cancel Membership** (`gym_membership_discharge_form_1`): This field allows you to formally notify the gym administration of your intention to cancel your membership.
* **Member Information** (`member_information`): Select the member whose membership you are cancelling (if it's not you).
* **Reason for Cancellation** (`reason_for_cancellation`): Choose one or more reasons why you are cancelling your membership from the list or provide your own reason.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): If you have any further explanations or concerns, add them here.
* **Member Signature** (`member_signature`): Date and sign this field to confirm your identity and cancellation request.
* **Gym Signature** (`gym_signature`): Date and sign this field to confirm the gym administration's acknowledgement of your cancellation request.
