# Compliance Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a compliance networking event. Please ensure that you are a professional in the compliance industry or affiliated with a company or institution in this field before registering.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
2. Enter your organization's name (if you are affiliated with one).
3. Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmations.
4. Select your preferred ticket type from the options provided.
5. If you are attending as a representative of an organization, please select up to three sessions of interest to help us tailor the event to your needs.
6. If you have any dietary or accessibility requirements, please specify them in the text field.
7. Finally, indicate your consent to share your contact details with event partners.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Organization**: Enter your company or institution's name, if applicable.
* **Email address**: Enter the email address you want to use for event updates and confirmations.
* **Phone number**: Enter your contact number, if you want to be reached during the event (optional).
* **Ticket type**: Select the type of ticket that suits your needs. Options include:
	+ General admission
	+ Speaker
	+ Sponsor
	+ Student
* **Sessions of interest**: Select up to three sessions that you are interested in attending. This will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Dietary requirements**: If you have any dietary needs or restrictions, please specify them here.
* **Accessibility requirements**: If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, please let us know so we can accommodate you.
* **Consent to share contact details**: Indicate whether you are willing to share your contact details with event partners.
