# Karaoke Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from registrants to participate in a Karaoke event. Please fill it out carefully and completely to ensure accurate registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your **registrar name**, which will be your identifier for the event.
2. Choose a **song to perform**, selecting one from the provided options.
3. If you want to be notified about the event details, please enter your **contact email**.
4. If desired, provide your **phone number** for further communication.
5. You can optionally leave an **additional message** to the organiser.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registrant name** (`registrant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
- **Song to perform** (`song_selection`, `select_one`, true): Choose a song from the list to perform on the night.
  - Select one song from the options provided.
- **Date of night** (`date_night`, `date`, false): Enter the date you want to participate in the event (not required).
- **Time of night** (`time_night`, `time`, false): Enter the time you would like to participate (not required).
- **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, true): Enter your email address to receive event updates and information.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, false): Provide your phone number for additional contact (not required).
- **Additional message** (`message`, `note`, false): Write any message you would like to communicate with the organiser.
