# High School Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information about students applying to a high school or high school programs. It is intended for school administrators or counselors to use in the admission process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Student Name" field with the student's full name.
2. Enter the student's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Provide the student's address in the "Address" field.
4. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
5. Enter the student's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Identify the emergency contact's relationship in the "Relationship" field (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
7. Enter the emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
8. Select the student's current grade level from the "Student Grade" field.
9. Indicate the student's attendance status at their current school in the "Student School Attendance" field.
10. Identify the student's citizenship status in the "Student Citizenship Status" field.
11. Select the languages spoken by the student in the "Student Languages" field.
12. If the student speaks any other languages, enter them in the "Other Languages" field.
13. If the student has any other languages they speak, enter the language in the "Other Language" field.
14. The "Teacher's Signature" and "School Administrator's Signature" fields are for administrative use and do not require input from the student.
15. Enter the date and time the form was completed in the "Date Completed" and "Time Completed" fields.
16. Enter the date and time the form was reviewed in the "Date Reviewed" and "Time Reviewed" fields.
17. Enter the student's name and school name (if different) in the "Student Name 2" and "School Name" fields (if applicable).
18. Provide the name and relationship of the student's reference person in the "Reference Person" field.
19. Provide any additional information about the student's school in the "School Address" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (1, text, required): The student's full name.
* **Contact Email** (2, email, required): The student's contact email address.
* **Address** (3, text, required): The student's home address.
* **Date Of Birth** (4, date, required): The student's date of birth.
* **Contact Phone** (5, text, required): The student's contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (7, select_one, required): The emergency contact's relationship (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (8, text, required): The emergency contact's phone number.
* **Student Grade** (9, select_multiple, required): The student's current grade level.
* **Student School Attendance** (10, select_multiple, required): The student's attendance status at their current school.
* **Student Citizenship Status** (11, select_one, required): The student's citizenship status.
* **Student Languages** (12, select_multiple, required): The student's spoken languages.
* **Other Languages** (13, text, required): Any other languages the student speaks.
* **Other Language** (14, text, required): If the student speaks any other languages, enter them here.
* **Teacher's Signature** (15, note, required): This field is for administrative use.
* **School Administrator's Signature** (16, note, required): This field is for administrative use.
* **Date Completed** (17, date, required): The date the form was completed.
* **Time Completed** (18, time, required): The time the form was completed.
* **Date Reviewed** (19, date, required): The date the form was reviewed.
* **Time Reviewed** (20, time, required): The time the form was reviewed.
* **Student Name 2** (21, text, required): The student's name.
* **School Name** (22, text, required): The student's school name (if different).
* **Reference Person** (23, text, required): The student's reference person's name and relationship.
* **Student Reference Relationship** (24, text, required): The student's reference person's relationship.
* **School Address** (25, text, required): Any additional information about the student's school.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If the student speaks any other languages, make sure to include them in the "Other Languages" field.
* If the student's school is different from their current school, make sure to enter both in the "School Name" and "Student Name 2" fields.
* Double-check that the dates and times entered are correct.
* If any other languages are selected in the "Student Languages" field, make sure to include them in the "Other Languages" field.
* Ensure that the student's name and school name (if different) are entered correctly in the "Student Name 2" and "School Name" fields.
