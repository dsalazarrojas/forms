# Youth Event Bus Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register youth event buses and collect information from guardians or responsible adults about the riders participating in an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the event details in the "Event Details" field.
2. Provide information about the rider in the "Rider Details" field.
3. Enter information about the guardian or responsible adult in the "Guardian Details" field.
4. Select your consent for the terms and conditions of the event.
5. Enter any additional notes or comments about the rider in the "Notes" field.
6. Enter a contact phone number in the "Contact Number" field.
7. Enter an email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter the rider's name in the "Rider Name" field.
9. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
10. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter the name of the event, the event date, and any other relevant details.
* **Rider Details**: Enter the rider's name, age, and any other relevant information about the rider.
* **Guardian Details**: Enter the name, phone number, email address, and any other relevant information about the rider's guardian or responsible adult.
* **Consent**: Select the option indicating that you are aware and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the rider.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number that the event staff can use to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Email**: Enter an email address that the event staff can use to contact you.
* **Rider Name**: Enter the name of the rider.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
