# Healthcare Cybersecurity Awareness Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Healthcare Cybersecurity Awareness Session Registration" form is designed to collect registration information for healthcare professionals attending a cybersecurity awareness session. This form is likely intended for both staff and participants to register for the session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Go through each section of the form, and select or input the required information.
2.  Ensure you complete all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
3.  Review and confirm the information provided before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Staff Registration (\*):** Select whether the person registering is a staff member or not.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Session Schedule (\*):** Choose whether the session schedule is relevant to your registration.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Session Information (\*):** Choose whether the session information is relevant to your registration.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Participant Information:** Provide participant information (this field is not required).
* **Date Of Birth:** Input your date of birth (format: YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Email (\*):** Input your email address.
* **Phone:** Input your phone number (optional).
* **Address:** Input your address (optional).
* **Notes:** Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Staff Registration 2:** Select whether the second staff registration is relevant to your registration.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Session Schedule 2:** Choose whether the second session schedule is relevant to your registration.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Participant Information 2:** Provide participant information 2 (this field is not required).
* **Email 2:** Input your email address 2 (this field is not required).
* **Phone 2:** Input your phone number 2 (optional).
* **Address 2:** Input your address 2 (optional).
* **Notes 2:** Add any additional comments or information about your registration 2.
* **Staff Registration 3:** Select whether the third staff registration is relevant to your registration.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Session Schedule 3:** Choose whether the third session schedule is relevant to your registration.
	+ Options: Yes/No
* **Participant Information 3:** Provide participant information 3 (this field is not required).
* **Email 3:** Input your email address 3 (this field is not required).
* **Phone 3:** Input your phone number 3 (optional).
* **Address 3:** Input your address 3 (optional).
* **Notes 3:** Add any additional comments or information about your registration 3.
* **Submit (\*):** Review and submit your registration.

Please note that some fields are not required and can be left blank.
