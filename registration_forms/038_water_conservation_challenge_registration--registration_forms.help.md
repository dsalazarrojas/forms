# Water Conservation Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Conservation Challenge Registration form is designed to gather demographic information from participants in a water conservation challenge. This information will help us analyze responses and identify trends.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Water Conservation Challenge Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your email address in the Email field.
4. Select your participant category (Yes/No) in the Participant Category field.
5. Choose your water challenge level (High/Low) in the Water Challenge Level field.
6. Enter your school name in the School Name field.
7. Select your school grade level (Yes/No) in the School Grade field.
8. Provide a brief description of your participation details in the Participant Details field.
9. Indicate whether you would like to receive contact information (Yes/No) in the Contact Info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Category** (Participant Category, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are a participant, 'No' otherwise.
* **Water Challenge Level** (Water Challenge Level, select_one, required: false): Choose your water challenge level from the options provided (High/Low).
* **School Name** (School Name, text, required: false): Enter your school name.
* **School Grade** (School Grade, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you are currently in school, 'No' otherwise.
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, note, required: false): Provide a brief description of your participant details.
* **Contact Info** (Contact Info, select_multiple, required: false): Select 'Yes' if you want to receive contact information, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Please fill out all required fields to ensure accurate submission.
- Make sure to select the correct participant category and school grade level.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us for assistance.
