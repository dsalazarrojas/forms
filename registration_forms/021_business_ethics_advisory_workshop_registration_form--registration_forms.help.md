# Business Ethics Advisory Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from registrants for the Business Ethics Advisory Workshop. Please follow the instructions carefully to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2.  For fields with multiple options, select all applicable choices.
3.  If you need assistance, contact us through the preferred contact method specified below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, required): This field is for your personal identification.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): This field is for your personal identification.
*   **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your professional email address. We will use this for communication and sending workshop materials.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Your contact number for further communication.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, required): Provide your official job title or position.
*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, required): Enter your company's name.
*   **Company Size** (`company_size`): Select your company's size.
*   **Industry** (`industry`, optional): Choose your industry from the options provided.
*   **Preferred Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, required): Choose between the available workshop sessions.
*   **Topics of Interest** (`workshop_topics`, required): Select all relevant topics to help us tailor the workshop content.
*   **Ethics Program Experience Level** (`experience_level`, optional): Indicate your experience level with ethics initiatives.
*   **Learning Goals** (`learning_goals`, optional): Describe what you hope to achieve from this workshop.
*   **Dietary Preferences** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you may have.
*   **Other Dietary Information** (`other_dietary`, optional): Provide additional dietary needs.
*   **Attendance Format** (`attendance_format`, required): Choose how you will attend the workshop.
*   **Accessibility Accommodations** (`accommodation_needed`, optional): If you need special accommodations, specify them here.
*   **Accommodation Details** (`accommodation_details`, optional): Describe the specific needs.
*   **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preferences`, optional): Indicate your preference for communication.
*   **I Agree to Workshop Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, required): Confirm your agreement before registering.

## Tips

- Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly.
- If you encounter any difficulties or need assistance, contact us using the preferred contact method.
- Please review and agree to the workshop terms and conditions.
