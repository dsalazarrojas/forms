# quality-technology-exchange-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering attendees for a quality technology exchange event. The goal is to capture essential information about each attendee to ensure accurate records and provide a better experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. For optional fields, fill out as much information as possible to help the organizers provide a better experience.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Name** (`conference_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the conference you are attending.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number that can be reached through.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are representing a company, enter its name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title within your company.
* **Role** (`role`, select_multiple, optional): Select all roles that apply to your participation in the event (e.g., Software Development, Project Management, Quality Assurance, etc.).
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you registered for the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time you registered for the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address again to confirm its accuracy.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the event organizers for assistance.
* This form is designed to provide a smooth and efficient experience for all attendees. Your cooperation is appreciated.
