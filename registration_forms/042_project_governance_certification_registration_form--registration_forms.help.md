# Project Governance Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Project Governance Certification Registration Form is used for participants to register for project governance certifications. The form collects information on various aspects related to the project, such as the organization and participant details, project specifics, and certification status. Please ensure that you are filling out this form for the correct purpose and not for any other intention.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate information.
2. Review each section carefully before submitting the form.
3. Ensure that you are filling out the form for the correct project and certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  This field is used to enter the name of the organization that the participant is representing.
* **Participant Name** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  This field is for the participant to enter their name.
* **Email** (<name>, <type>: email, required):
  Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Details** (<name>, <type>: text, optional):
  This field is for any additional participant details.
* **Participant Type** (<name>, <type>: select_one, required):
  Select the type of participant.
* **Certification Type** (<name>, <type>: select_one, required):
  Select the type of certification.
* **Program Type** (<name>, <type>: select_one, required):
  Select the type of program.
* **Start Date** (<name>, <type>: date, required):
  Enter the start date of the project.
* **End Date** (<name>, <type>: date, required):
  Enter the end date of the project.
* **Project Name** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter the name of the project.
* **Project Manager Name** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter the name of the project manager.
* **Participant Position** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your participant position.
* **Company** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your company name.
* **Department** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your department name.
* **Team Lead** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your team lead name.
* **Project Location** (<name>, <type>: select_one, required):
  Select the project location.
* **Certification Status** (<name>, <type>: select_one, required):
  Select the certification status.
* **Notes** (<name>, <type>: note, optional):
  This field is for any additional notes.
* **Certification Number** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your certification number.
* **Program Number** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your program number.
* **Project ID** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Enter your project ID.
* **Participant Signature** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Sign and date your signature.
* **Organization Signature** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Sign and date the organization's signature.
* **Submit** (<name>, <type>: text, required):
  Click submit to complete the form.

## Tips

* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have filled out all required fields.
* Review each section for accuracy.
* If you have any issues or need assistance, contact your administrator.
