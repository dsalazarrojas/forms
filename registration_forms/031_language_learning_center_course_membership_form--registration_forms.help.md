# Language Learning Center Course Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Language Learning Center's course membership program. Please fill out the form with your details and preferences to be assigned a suitable tool and tool type for your course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Course Details** page with your information.
2. Move on to the **Learner Details** page and provide your details.
3. Choose your **Preferred Time Slots** from the available options.
4. Select your **Language Picked** from the list.
5. Choose your **Course Picked** from the list.
6. You may add any **Notes** you'd like to include.
7. If applicable, select your **Assigned Tool Type** from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Details** (Required):
    * A space to enter your course details, if required.
* **Learner Details** (Required):
    * Please enter your details here.
* **Preferred Time Slots**:
    * Choose your preferred time slots for the course, selecting multiple slots if desired.
        - Options: 08:00, 08:45, 09:00, 12:00, 13:00, 15:00, 16:00, 18:00, 19:00, 20:00, 21:00, 08:45, 09:15, 12:30, 13:15, 14:30, 15:30, 16:30, 17:30, 18:30, 19:30, 21:30, 22:30, 06:00, 07:00, 08:00, 09:00, 10:00, 11:00, 12:00, 13:00, 14:00, 15:00, 16:00, 17:00, 18:00, 19:00, 20:00, 21:00, 22:00, 00:00
* **Language Picked** (Required):
    * Choose the language you'd like to learn or are interested in.
        - Options: English, French, Spanish, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic
* **Course Picked** (Required):
    * Select the course you are interested in joining.
        - Options: Course 1, Course 2, Course 3, Course 4
* **Notes** (Optional):
    * Add any additional comments or notes you'd like to include.
* **Assigned Tool** (Optional):
    * If selected, enter the tool assigned to you.
* **Assigned Tool Type** (Required):
    * Select the type of tool assigned to you.
        - Options: Jotform, Other
* **Comments** (Optional):
    * Add any additional comments you'd like to include.
