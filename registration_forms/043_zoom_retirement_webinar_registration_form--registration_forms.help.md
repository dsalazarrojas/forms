# zoom_retirement_webinar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Zoom retirement webinar registration form is designed to gather information from participants for the purpose of providing tailored and relevant content during the webinar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please use a valid email address that you check regularly.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. Please include your area code.
* Select your current "Tier 2 Status".
* Provide information about your "Financial Goals".
* Enter the number of years you have been working with the New York Public Designation (NYPD) in the "Years with NYPD" field.
* Choose an "Expected Retirement Date" for yourself.
* Indicate which financial institutions you are currently using or plan to use ("Financial Institutions" field).
* Describe your current investments in the "Current Investments" field.
* Enter the value of your current financial assets.
* Enter any financial objectives or goals you have in the "Financial Objectives" field.
* Provide any seminar suggestions or preferences you have in the "Seminar Suggestions" field.
* Select which topics you are interested in learning more about during the seminar ("Seminar Topics" field).
* Confirm your registration by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Registration Confirmation" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: This field collects your first name.
* **Last Name**: This field collects your last name.
* **Email**: This field collects your email address.
* **Phone Number**: This field collects your phone number, including your area code.
* **Tier 2 Status**: This field collects information about your current employment status.
* **Financial Goals**: This field allows you to describe your financial objectives or goals.
* **Years with NYPD**: This field is used to collect the number of years you have been working with the New York Public Designation (NYPD).
* **Expected Retirement Date**: This field is used to collect your expected retirement date.
* **Financial Institutions**: This field is used to select the financial institutions you are currently using or plan to use.
* **Current Investments**: This field is used to describe your current investments.
* **Financial Assets**: This field is used to collect the value of your current financial assets.
* **Financial Objectives**: This field is used to describe your financial objectives or goals.
* **Seminar Suggestions**: This field is used to provide suggestions or preferences for the seminar content.
* **Seminar Topics**: This field is used to select topics you are interested in learning more about during the seminar.
* **Registration Confirmation**: This field is used to confirm your registration.
