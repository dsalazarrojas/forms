# Marriage Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or couples seeking to register their marriage. The form is intended to be completed by the couple and the officiant who performed the marriage ceremony.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and your partner's name in the "Couple Info" field.
2. Provide details about you and your partner in the "Couple Details" field.
3. Enter information about the officiant who performed the marriage ceremony in the "Officiant Info" field.
4. Provide details about the wedding ceremony in the "Wedding Details" field.
5. Attach a copy of the officiant's certification or license in the "Officiant Cert" field.
6. If a witness was present during the wedding ceremony, please provide their information in the "Witness Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Couple Info** (Text, Optional): Enter the names of you and your partner as they will appear on the marriage certificate.
* **Couple Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any relevant details about you and your partner, such as previous marriages or children.
* **Officiant Info** (Text, Optional): Enter the name and contact information of the officiant who performed the marriage ceremony.
* **Wedding Details** (Text, Optional): Describe the details of the wedding ceremony, including the date, location, and time.
* **Officiant Cert** (Text, Optional): Attach a copy of the officiant's certification or license to verify their authority to perform the marriage ceremony.
* **Witness Info** (Text, Optional): If a witness was present during the wedding ceremony, enter their name and contact information.
