# professional_development_academy_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration in the Professional Development Academy. The purpose of this form is to collect necessary information from applicants for registration in the Professional Development Academy. To confirm, you are eligible to register in this program if you are an employee of a recognized organization and have been selected for this program. 
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:
1. Ensure you are eligible to register in the Professional Development Academy.
2. Enter your Organization ID, which is your unique identifier assigned by your organization.
3. Select the Program for which you are registering.
4. Enter your Name.
5. Enter your Email.
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Choose the Event Date of the program you are registering for.
8. Choose the Time of the program you are registering for.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization ID** (`organization_id`, number, required/optional: **false**): Enter your unique identifier assigned by your organization.
* **Program** (`program_id`, select_one, required/optional: **false**): Select the program you are registering for from the list of options.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional: **false**): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional: **false**): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional: **false**): Enter your valid phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional: **false**): Choose the date of the event you are attending.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional: **false**): Choose the time of the event you are attending.
## Tips
- Ensure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
- Double-check your input for errors.
- If you are unable to register, contact the Professional Development Academy for assistance.
- If you are unsure about a field, contact the Professional Development Academy for clarification.
