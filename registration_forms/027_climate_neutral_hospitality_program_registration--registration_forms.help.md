# Climate Neutral Hospitality Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Climate Neutral hospitality program is designed to help hotels and resorts reduce their carbon footprint and promote sustainability. By joining this program, your facility can gain access to resources, support, and expertise to help you achieve your sustainability goals.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely, making sure to provide accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct type of facility from the options provided.
3. Enter the correct number of guest rooms in your facility.
4. Provide the correct location and website (if applicable).
5. Fill out the contact information for the person responsible for the facility's sustainability efforts.
6. Select the current sustainability initiatives your facility is undertaking.
7. Choose the current carbon offset status.
8. Set a target year for carbon neutrality.
9. Describe the specific sustainability goals your facility aims to achieve.
10. Indicate what support or resources you need to help your facility reach its sustainability goals.
11. Provide a brief statement explaining why you are interested in joining the Climate Neutral program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Facility Name** (`facility_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your facility.
* **Type of Facility** (`facility_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of facility you have (e.g., hotel, resort, guesthouse, etc.).
* **Number of Guest Rooms** (`number_of_rooms`, number, required): Enter the number of guest rooms in your facility.
* **Location/Address** (`location`, text, required): Enter the physical address of your facility.
* **Facility Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the website of your facility (if applicable).
* **Contact Person Name** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for sustainability efforts at your facility.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter the job title of the person responsible for sustainability efforts (if applicable).
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the person responsible for sustainability efforts.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the person responsible for sustainability efforts.
* **Current Sustainability Initiatives** (`current_efforts`, select_multiple, required): Select all the sustainability initiatives your facility is currently undertaking.
* **When Did Sustainability Efforts Begin?** (`sustainability_timeline`, date, optional): Enter the year or date when your sustainability efforts started.
* **Current Carbon Offset Status** (`carbon_offset`, select_one, required): Choose the current carbon offset status of your facility.
* **Target Year for Carbon Neutrality** (`carbon_neutral_target`, number, required): Enter the target year for carbon neutrality.
* **Specific Sustainability Goals** (`sustainability_goals`, text, required): Describe the specific sustainability goals your facility aims to achieve.
* **Support or Resources Needed** (`support_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Select the support or resources you need to help your facility achieve its sustainability goals.
* **Primary Motivation for Joining the Program** (`primary_motivation`, text, required): Explain why your facility is interested in joining the Climate Neutral program.
* **Additional Information or Comments** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about your facility.
