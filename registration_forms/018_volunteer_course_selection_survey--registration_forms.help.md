# Volunteer Course Selection Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Volunteer Course Selection Survey is a form designed to help volunteers select courses they are interested in or available to participate in.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Survey Form" field with a brief description of your course selection.
2. Enter your "First name" and "Last name" as required.
3. Input your "Email" address in the correct format (example: [email address]@example.com).
4. Enter your phone number in the correct format (example: +1234567890 or 123-456-7890).
5. Select one or more "Course ID" options that match your course selection.
6. Select one or more "Course Preferences" options based on your course selection.
7. (Optional) Select a "Date" for your course selection.
8. (Optional) Select a "Time" for your course selection.
9. Fill out the "Submit" field with a brief summary or statement of your course selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Survey Form** (`survey_form`, text, required): A text input field where you can describe your course selection.
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, false): Input your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
* **Course ID** (`course_id`, select_multiple, false): Select one or more course IDs that you are interested in.
* **Course Preferences** (`course_preferences`, select_multiple, false): Select one or more course preferences that match your course selection.
* **Date** (`date`, date, false): Select a date for your course selection.
* **Time** (`time`, time, false): Select a time for your course selection.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, false): A field for a brief summary or statement of your course selection.
