# Workplace Agility Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Agility Training Registration form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in workplace agility training. This form helps us understand your preferences and requirements for the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your session preferences:
	* For each question, choose the response that best fits your needs.
2. Provide participant details:
	* Please select "Yes" or "No" for each question.
3. Enter any special needs:
	* If you have any specific needs or requirements for the training session, describe them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Preferences
**Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Please choose from the options to indicate your preferences for the training session. You can select more than one option.

### Participant Details
**Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `select_one`, optional): 
Please select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the participant has the specified characteristic. You can select only one option.

### Special Needs
**Special Needs** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): 
If you have any specific needs or requirements for the training session, describe them here.

### Session Preferences 2
**Session Preferences 2** (`session_preferences_2`, `select_multiple`, optional): 
Please choose from the options to indicate your preferences for the second session. You can select more than one option.
