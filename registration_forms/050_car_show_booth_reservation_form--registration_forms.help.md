# Car Show Booth Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from car show exhibitors to process their booth reservations for the upcoming event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, you will need to provide personal and business contact information, booth preferences, event details, and payment information. Please make sure to fill out all required fields. You can save your progress and return to the form later if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Information**: This section is not required, but it's where you can enter your contact details and company information if you wish.
	+ **Your contact details**: Enter your email address and phone number so we can get in touch with you for any questions or updates.
	+ **Company Name**: If you want to enter your company name.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for reservation communications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company Type**: Select the type of business you are representing (e.g., Individual exhibitor, Dealership, Restoration shop, etc.).
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Booth Preferences**: This section is not required, but you can enter any special requests for your booth setup.
* **Booth Size**: Select the size of the booth you need (e.g., 10x10 ft, 10x20 ft, etc.).
* **Booth Location Preference**: Select the preferred location of your booth (e.g., Main entrance, High traffic area, etc.).
* **Booth Number Request**: If you have a specific booth number preference, enter it here.
* **Previous Exhibitor**: Select whether you have exhibited at this event before.
* **Event Information**: This section is not required, but it's where you can enter the event name and date you are participating in.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Setup Date**: Enter the date you plan to set up your booth.
* **Teardown Date**: Enter the date you plan to tear down your booth.
* **Display Information**: This section is not required, but it's where you can enter what you will be displaying at your booth.
* **Display Type**: Select what type of display you will be showcasing (e.g., Classic cars, Custom cars, etc.).
* **Number of Vehicles**: Select the number of vehicles you will be displaying (e.g., 1, 2, 3, etc.).
* **Vehicle Descriptions**: Enter brief descriptions of the vehicles you will be displaying.
* **Utilities Needed**: Select all utilities you require for your booth (e.g., Electricity, Water, etc.).
* **Electrical Requirements**: Enter any electrical requirements you have for your booth.
* **Booth Setup**: This section is not required, but it's where you can enter any special requirements for your booth setup.
* **Table Needed**: Select whether you need a table for your booth.
* **Chairs Needed**: Select the number of chairs you need for your booth.
* **Display Materials**: Select all display materials you will be bringing (e.g., Banners, Signage, etc.).
* **Special Requirements**: Enter any special requirements you have for your booth.
* **Payment Information**: This section is not required, but it's where you can enter your payment details.
* **Booth Fee**: Enter the total fee you will be paying for your booth.
* **Payment Method**: Select how you will pay for your booth (e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.).
* **Payment Status**: Select your current payment status (e.g., Paid in full, Deposit paid, etc.).
* **Deposit Amount**: Enter the deposit amount if applicable.
* **Insurance**: Select whether you have liability insurance.
* **Marketing Information**: This section is not required, but it's where you can enter promotional details.
* **Company Description**: Enter a brief description of your company.
* **Website**: Enter your website URL.
* **Social Media**: Enter your social media handles.
* **Promotional Support**: Select whether you will help promote the event on social media.
* **How Did You Hear About Event**: Select how you heard about the event.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information.
* **Terms Agreement**: Select whether you agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Cancellation Policy Acknowledgment**: Select whether you understand the cancellation policy.
* **Reservation Date**: Enter the current date.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed to process your booth reservation.
