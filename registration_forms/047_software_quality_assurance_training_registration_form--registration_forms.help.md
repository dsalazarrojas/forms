# software quality assurance training registration form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for software quality assurance training. Fill out the form with your information to reserve a spot in the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the course you are interested in from the "Course" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the training, if applicable.
4. Provide your phone number and location, if you wish to be contacted for any reason.
5. Review and submit your form carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for any reason related to the training.
* **Course**: Select the course you are interested in from the dropdown menu. Please select one of the options: "Course 1", "Course 2", or "Course 3".
* **Start Date** (*Optional*): Enter the start date of the training. If you don't know the start date, you can leave this field blank.
* **End Date** (*Optional*): Enter the end date of the training. If you don't know the end date, you can leave this field blank.
* **Location** (*Optional*): Enter the location where the training will take place.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to complete your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Name, Course).
- If you have already submitted this form in the past, you will be prompted to review and update your previous submission.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
