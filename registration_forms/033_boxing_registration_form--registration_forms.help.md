# Boxing Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for boxing registration, including profile, contact, and health and safety details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out your profile details, including participant full name, date of birth, and gender.
2. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
3. Select your boxing experience level, preferred class type, and current weight.
4. Check the box indicating you understand the risks and agree to the liability waiver.
5. Finally, fill out the emergency contact information and select a registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Full Name** (`part_full_name`, text, required): This field is for entering your full name.
* **Date of Birth** (`birth_dt`, date, required): This is your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender** (`gender_opt`, text, optional): Select your gender, if applicable.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Boxing Experience Level** (`exp_lvl`, select_one, required): Select your boxing experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional.
* **Preferred Class Type** (`class_pref`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred class type: Fitness Boxing, Competitive Sparring, Youth Boxing, or Private Coaching.
* **Current Weight** (`weight_val`, text, required): Enter your current weight in kg or lb.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Any medical conditions or previous injuries?** (`medical_notes`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or previous injuries, please provide details.
* **I understand the risks and agree to the liability waiver** (`risks_agree`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree" to confirm you understand the risks and agree to the waiver.
* **Registration Date** (`reg_dt`, date, required): Select the date you are registering.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any medical conditions or previous injuries, please provide detailed information to ensure your safety.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your emergency contact listed above.
* Once you complete this form, you will be registered for boxing participation.
