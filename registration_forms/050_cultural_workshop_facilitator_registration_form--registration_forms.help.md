# Cultural Workshop Facilitator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register cultural workshop facilitators. It helps to collect information about the facilitators and their workshops, which can be useful for organizing and promoting cultural events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have a valid email address, as it is the only required field.
* Fill out the other fields with accurate and up-to-date information, if applicable.
* Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (<type>text, required: false)**: This field is meant for users to provide some personal information about themselves, such as their name and contact number. You can fill it out if you'd like to provide more context about yourself, but it's not necessary.
* **Email (<type>email, required: true)**: This is the only required field, and you must provide a valid email address. This will be used to contact you for further information or to confirm your registration.
* **Facilitator Info (<type>text, required: false)**: This field is for you to provide more information about yourself as a facilitator, such as your experience or qualifications. If you want to highlight your expertise, you can fill it out.
* **Workshop Info (<type>text, required: false)**: This field is for you to describe your workshop, including its topic, location, and any other relevant details. If you want to provide more context about your workshop, you can fill it out.
* **Submitter Info (<type>text, required: false)**: This field is for you to provide any additional comments or information about your submission. If you want to explain why you're registering as a facilitator or have any questions, you can fill it out.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your submission before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
