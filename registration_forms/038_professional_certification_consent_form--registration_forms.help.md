# professional_certification_consent_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering professional certifications. Users will be required to provide details about their organization, contact information, and a few other relevant details. Please ensure that you are submitting this form for the correct purpose and only once.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of your organization in the first field.
2. Provide your address in the second field.
3. Enter your email address for contact in the third field.
4. Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
5. Select the type of certification you are applying for (Certification, Affiliation, or Other).
6. Read through the terms and conditions and enter any additional comments or questions you may have in the fifth field.
7. Select a date for your date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Consent Type** (`consent_type`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options (Certification, Affiliation, or Other) to indicate the type of certification you are applying for.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, text, required: false): Read through the terms and conditions and enter any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format of `DD/MM/YY`.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you are submitting the form for the correct purpose.
* If you need to submit multiple certifications, please create a new form for each one.
