# student_individual_inventory - Help Guide
## Purpose
The student individual inventory form is designed to collect personal and demographic information from students for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
* Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
* Select your gender from the "gender" field.
* Enter your date of birth in the "date_of_birth" field in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* Select your grade level from the available options.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use a valid email format (e.g., example@example.com).
* Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, ID).
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Gender** (`gender`, text, optional): Select your gender from the available options.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy (e.g., 12/03/1999).
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, number, optional): Select your grade level from the available options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
