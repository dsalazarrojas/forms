# partners_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The partners_registration_form is used to register new partners in the system. This form helps collect necessary information about the partner, including contact details, business information, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required fields, denoted by the * symbol.
2. Fill in the optional fields according to your needs.
3. Select the correct options for the partner role, status, and any other fields that require selection.
4. Review the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **partner_details** (1): Enter a brief description of the partner.
* **contact_info** (2): Enter the contact information of the partner, including phone number and email.
* **business_info** (3): Enter the business information of the partner, including company name and address.
* **partner_role** (4): Select the partner role from the options provided, e.g., Partner/Agent, Partner/Agent-Partner/Agent.
* **partner_name** (5): Enter the name of the partner.
* **partner_title** (6): Enter the title of the partner.
* **partner_company** (7): Enter the company name of the partner.
* **partner_email** (8): Enter the email address of the partner.
* **partner_phone** (9): Enter the phone number of the partner.
* **partner_address** (10): Enter the address of the partner.
* **partner_notes** (11): Add any additional notes about the partner.
* **partner_status** (12): Select the current status of the partner from the options provided, e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending.
* **submit** (13): Review and submit the form after filling in all the required fields.
