# Disaster Relief Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to assist individuals affected by a disaster to register for relief assistance. It collects information necessary to provide effective relief efforts, including name, contact details, and information about the disaster.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Provide your address in the "Address" field.
* Enter your city in the "City" field.
* Enter your zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
* Select the type of disaster you are registering for from the "Disasters Type" dropdown menu.
* Provide a brief description of the damage you have suffered in the "Damage Description" field.
* If applicable, specify the contact person in the "Contact Person" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (Name, text, optional)**: Enter your full name as it is written on your identification documents.
- **Email (Email, email, optional)**: Enter your email address that you check regularly.
- **Phone Number (Phone Number, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
- **Address (Address, text, optional)**: Enter your full address to help us locate you for relief efforts.
- **City (City, text, optional)**: Enter the city you are in or affected by the disaster.
- **Zip Code (Zip Code, text, optional)**: Enter the zip code of your location.
- **Disaster Type (Disasters Type, select_one, optional)**: Select the type of disaster you are registering for from the dropdown menu:
  - Flood
  - Fire
  - Earthquake
  - Hurricane
- **Damage Description (Damage Description, text, optional)**: Describe the damage you have suffered.
- **Contact Person (Contact Person, text, optional)**: If someone else should be contacted for your information, enter their name and contact details here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any difficulties completing this form, contact our relief team for assistance.
* We appreciate your cooperation in helping us provide effective relief efforts.
