# sports_event_participant_roster - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering participants in a sports event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter event details.
2. Select participants from the list.
3. Enter participant's name, contact name, and participant type.
4. Enter participant's age, email, phone number, and address.
5. Enter any additional notes about the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`Event Details`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about the event.
* **Participant List** (`Participant List`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple participants from the list.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's name.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact name.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the participant type.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the participant's age.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the participant's address.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
