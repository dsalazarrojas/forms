# Population Health Data Science Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from attendees to the Population Health Data Science Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Make sure to enter your correct contact information to ensure we can get in touch with you regarding the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `Optional`): 
  This is the field for your first name. Please enter your first name here.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `Optional`): 
  This is the field for your last name. Please enter your last name here.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `Optional`): 
  This is the field for your email address. Please enter your email address here.

### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, `Optional`): 
  This is the field for your phone number. Please enter your phone number here.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are filling out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* This form is for registration purposes only and will be reviewed and verified before being accepted.
