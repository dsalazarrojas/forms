# Retail Inventory Forecasting Webinar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to register for a webinar on retail inventory forecasting, a topic relevant to professionals in the retail industry who want to improve their forecasting skills. Please note that this webinar is designed for people working in this specific industry; if you are not related to this domain, this webinar may not be beneficial for you.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address that you check regularly.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your company name in the "Company" field, if applicable.
6. Enter your position within your company in the "Position" field.
7. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
8. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
9. Enter your location in the "Location" field.
10. Enter your job function in the "Job Function" field.
11. Choose your preferred attendance option (in-person, online, phone, or other) in the "Attendance" field.
12. Select your primary language of communication in the "Language" field.
13. Enter your time zone in the "Time Zone" field, if you wish to be contacted during a specific time period (optional).
14. Enter the date you are planning to attend in the "Date" field, if you wish to be contacted before or after the event (optional).

* **Position** (`form_01_position`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your position within your company.
* **Job Title** (`form_01_job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`form_01_department`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your department.
* **Location** (`form_01_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your location.
* **Job Function** (`form_01_job_function`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job function.
* **Attendance** (`form_01_attendance`, `select_one`, required/true): Choose your preferred attendance option (in-person, online, phone, or other).

* **Time Zone** (`form_01_time_zone`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your time zone, if you wish to be contacted during a specific time period (optional).
