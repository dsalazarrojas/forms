# manly_man_2021_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The manly_man_2021_registration_form is a comprehensive form designed to collect and store information about units and their corresponding emergency contacts. This data will be used for internal organization and management purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by selecting the unit type and providing a brief description of the unit.
2. Complete the unit details section with accurate information about the unit size and type.
3. In the unit contact information section, fill out the unit leader's contact details.
4. In the unit member section, enter the first and last names of the unit members.
5. In the emergency contact information section, fill out the details for the emergency contact, including phone numbers, email, and address.
6. Finally, select whether the form has been submitted or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Troop Details**: Enter a brief description of the unit, e.g., "1st Platoon" or "Scouting Group."
* **Unit Type**: Type of unit, e.g., "Scouting," "Military," or "Recreational."
* **Unit Size**: Number of people in the unit.
* **Unit Name**: Name of the unit, e.g., "The Brave Hearts" or "Team Alpha."
* **Unit Contact**: Enter the name of the unit leader or contact person.
* **Unit Email**: Email address of the unit leader.
* **Unit Phone**: Phone number of the unit leader or contact person.
* **Unit Address**: Physical address of the unit or leader.
* **Unit Zip**: Zip code of the unit's address.
* **Troop Leaders**: Select which leader to contact, e.g., "First," "Second," or "Third."
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the unit member.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the unit member.
* **Email**: Email address of the unit member.
* **Phone**: Phone number of the unit member.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Relationship between the unit member and the emergency contact, e.g., "Parent" or "Spouse."
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Physical address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Notes**: Any additional information about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2**: Phone number of the secondary emergency contact.
* **Submitted By**: Select whether the form has been submitted or not.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Review the form carefully before submission to ensure all information is correct.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact the unit leader or contact person for clarification.
- The submitted data will be used for internal purposes only and will not be shared publicly.
