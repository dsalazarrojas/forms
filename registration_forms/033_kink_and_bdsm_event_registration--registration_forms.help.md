# Kink And BDSM Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Kink And BDSM Event Registration form is designed to collect information from interested participants. It is crucial to fill this form out accurately to ensure you receive the relevant information and updates about the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

*   Ensure you provide accurate and honest information in all the fields.
*   Fill out the form in a step-by-step fashion, one section at a time.
*   Make sure to check the 'required' fields and fill them out accordingly.
*   If you have any additional information, make use of the 'additional_info' field.
*   Review your information before submission to avoid any last-minute corrections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **name (Text, Required):** This is the field for your name. Please enter your name as you'd like to be addressed during the event.
*   **email (Email, Required):** Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at during and after the event.
*   **phone (Text, Required):** This is your contact phone number. Please ensure it's a valid number that can be reached at during and after the event.
*   **date_of_arrival (Date, Required):** Enter the date you plan to arrive at the event.
*   **time_of_arrival (Time, Required):** Enter the time you plan to arrive at the event.
*   **kink_interests (Select Multiple, Required):** Please select the kink interests you have. This will help us cater to your needs and provide the best experience for you.
*   **kink_experience (Text, Optional):** If you'd like, enter any relevant kink experience you have. This will help us understand your comfort level and provide guidance where needed.
*   **consent (Select One, Required):** Confirm that you have given your consent to participate in the event.
*   **additional_info (Note, Optional):** In this field, you can provide any additional information that might help us better understand your needs or preferences.
*   **payment_method (Select One, Required):** Confirm how you plan to pay for your registration.
*   **payment_method_other (Text, Optional):** If you're paying with something other than the listed options, provide that information.
*   **payment_amount (Number, Optional):** If you're paying a different amount from the listed options, provide that amount.
*   **payment_currency (Select One, Optional):** Confirm the currency you're paying with.
*   **participant_info (Select Multiple, Optional):** Please select any relevant participant information.
*   **participant_info_other (Text, Optional):** If you have any other participant information, provide it here.
*   **participant_info_other_info (Text, Optional):** If you need to elaborate on any participant information, use this field.
*   **participant_info_other_participant_info (Text, Optional):** If you'd like to provide more participant info, use this field.
*   **event_info (Select One, Optional):** If you'd like to provide any event information, do so in this field.
*   **event_info_other (Text, Optional):** If you'd like to elaborate on any event information, do so here.
*   **event_info_other_info (Text, Optional):** If you'd like to provide more event information, use this field.
*   **participant_info_other_participant_info_other (Text, Optional):** If you'd like to elaborate on any participant information, do so here.
*   **kink_info (Select Multiple, Optional):** Please select any relevant kink info you have.

## Tips

*   Make sure to proofread your information before submitting the form.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
*   Thank you for taking the time to register for the event. Your input is greatly appreciated.
