# IT Cost Optimization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your project in the IT Cost Optimization Program, which aims to help organizations optimize their IT costs by identifying and reducing waste, improving efficiency, and increasing productivity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide a clear and concise name for your project.
2. Select the category that best describes your project (Cost Optimization, Process Improvement, or Other).
3. Specify the goal of your project.
4. Describe the details of your goal.
5. Choose the focus area(s) for your project (Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, Data Backup, Data Recovery, IT Management, or Other).
6. Enter the name of the consultant involved with your project.
7. Enter the email address of the consultant.
8. Enter the contact phone number of the consultant (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise name for your project. This will be the title of your project in our records.
- **Category** (`category`, select, required): Select the category that best describes your project. This will help us understand the scope of your project.
- **Goal** (`goal`, text, required): Specify the primary objective of your project. This should be a short summary of what your project aims to achieve.
- **Goal Details** (`goal_details`, text, required): Describe the specific details of your project's goal.
- **Focus Area** (`focus_area`, select multiple, required): Choose the area(s) of IT that your project will focus on (e.g., Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, Cybersecurity, etc.).
- **Consultant Name** (`consultant_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the consultant involved with your project.
- **Consultant Email** (`consultant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the consultant.
- **Consultant Phone** (`consultant_phone`, text, optional): Enter the contact phone number of the consultant (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled in completely and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any information required, please contact your project manager or our team for clarification.
- This form is used to register your project in the IT Cost Optimization Program, so ensure that your project aligns with the program's goals and objectives.
