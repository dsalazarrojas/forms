# Democracy Project Experience Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user information for the Democracy Project, a platform promoting participative governance. Please ensure you have not already completed this form to avoid duplicate submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your details.
2. Provide your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Optionally, provide your contact phone number and address.
4. Indicate your experience level on the Democracy Project: High, Low, or no preference.
5. Select your project interests: yes or no.
6. Add any additional comments or messages in the "Message" field.
7. Choose a date for your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number if you have one.
* **Address** (5, text, optional): Enter your address for contact purposes.
* **Project Interests** (6, select_multiple, optional): Select yes if you are interested in the Democracy Project, no if not.
* **Experience Level** (7, select_one, optional): Choose your experience level with the Democracy Project: High, Low, or no preference.
* **Field 8** (8, text, optional): This field is not clearly labeled, but it seems to be an optional text field.
* **Message** (9, note, optional): Add any additional comments or messages about your experience with the Democracy Project.
* **Date** (10, date, optional): Choose a date for your submission.

## Tips
* Ensure you have not already completed this form to avoid duplicate submissions.
* Provide accurate information to help us better understand your experience and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
