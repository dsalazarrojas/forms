# Professional Development Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register for a professional development workshop. Please fill out this form to provide the required information to secure your spot in the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Review your entries for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the workshop you want to register for.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it will appear on the workshop's attendance list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and workshop updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for any necessary contact.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Choose the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Choose the time slot you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your registration.

## Tips
Remember to review your entries carefully before submitting to ensure accurate information. If you have any questions or need further assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us.
