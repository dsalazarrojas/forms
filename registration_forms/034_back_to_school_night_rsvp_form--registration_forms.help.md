# Back To School Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect RSVPs for the school's annual Back-to-School Night event. It is intended for parents and guardians to inform the school of their intention to attend and provide any necessary information for their attendance.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please select "Yes, I will attend" if you plan to attend the event. If you cannot attend, select "No, I am unable to attend".
2.  Enter your full name, as you would like it to be listed in the attendance list.
3.  Provide your email address to receive any necessary information regarding the event.
4.  List all the student(s) that will be attending the event.
5.  Choose the attendance format that best suits your needs (in-person or virtual).
6.  If you need childcare services, select "Yes, please" to be contacted for further information.
7.  Select the number of children that require childcare services.
8.  Choose the topics you are most interested in hearing about during the event.
9.  If you have any questions for the teaching staff, use this field to express them.
10.  If you are interested in joining the PTA, select "True" in the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **-- Back-to-School Night RSVP --** (1): This section provides a heading for the form. Please ignore and move on to the next section.
*   **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (2): Enter your full name as you would like it to be listed in the attendance list.
*   **Email Address** (3): This is the email address that the school will use to contact you with any important information regarding the event.
*   **Names of Student(s) at this school** (4): List all students who will be attending the event.
*   **Will you be attending Back-to-School Night?** (5): Please select "Yes, I will attend" if you plan to attend the event. If you cannot attend, select "No, I am unable to attend".
*   **Preferred Attendance Format (If Applicable)** (6): Select the format you prefer for the event (in-person or virtual).
*   **Do you require child care during the event?** (7): If you need childcare services, select "Yes, please" to be contacted for further information.
*   **Number of children for childcare** (8): If you selected "Yes, please" for childcare services, enter the number of children that require childcare.
*   **What topics are you most interested in hearing about?** (9): Choose the topics you are most interested in hearing about during the event.
*   **Do you have any general questions for the teaching staff?** (10): If you have any questions for the teaching staff, use this field to express them.
*   **Would you like to receive information about joining the PTA?** (11): Select "True" in the dropdown menu if you are interested in joining the PTA.
