# Dive Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to join a dive trip. Please fill out this form to provide us with necessary details about yourself and the trip you're interested in.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form by typing in the required information in each field.
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* You can use the space provided to add any additional comments or notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**:
  (`participant_info`, text, optional): Please provide a brief introduction about yourself. This can include your name, age, and any other relevant details about your diving experience.
* **Trip Details**:
  (`trip_details`, text, optional): Describe the dive trip you're interested in, including the location, dates, and any other relevant details.
* **Instructor Information**:
  (`instructor_info`, text, optional): If you're interested in working with a particular instructor, please provide their name and any other relevant details about their qualifications.
