# Innovation Lab Crawl Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Innovation Lab Crawl. It collects information about each participant, including their name, contact details, and role in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Provide your Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the participant list.
2. **Enter your Email**: Type in your email address where you can be reached for event updates and information.
3. **Choose your Organization (if applicable)**: If you represent an organization, enter the name of your company or institution.
4. **Enter your Phone Number (if applicable)**: If you'd like to be contacted by phone, enter your phone number.
5. **Choose your Role**: Select the role you'll be playing in the event:
	* **Innovation Leader**: You're an innovation leader or representative from a company or organization.
	* **Participant**: You're attending the event to learn and explore.
	* **Exhibitor**: You're representing a company or organization showcasing their products or services.
	* **Organizer**: You're helping organize the event.
6. **Enter Team Size (if applicable)**: If you're attending with a team, enter the number of team members.
7. **Enter Team Name (if applicable)**: If you're attending with a team, enter your team name.
8. **Describe your Team (if applicable)**: If you're attending with a team, provide a brief description of your team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: The name you'd like to be displayed in the participant list.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be reached for event updates and information.
* **Organization**: The name of your company or institution (if applicable).
* **Phone Number**: A phone number where you can be reached (if applicable).
* **Role**: The role you'll be playing in the event (Innovation Leader, Participant, Exhibitor, Organizer).
* **Team Size**: The number of team members attending (if applicable).
* **Team Name**: The name of your team (if applicable).
* **Team Description**: A brief description of your team (if applicable).
