# health_journalism_awareness_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Health Journalism Awareness Event, allowing you to participate and engage with the event's content and activities. This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the form accurately and effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Begin by filling in the required and optional information for each field. Each section of the form will guide you through the necessary information.
2.  Review your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Attendee Information**: <small>This field is not required. Please enter any information you would like to share about yourself as an attendee.</small>
    **Name**: Enter your name. 
*   **Select your roles**: <small>This field is not required. Please select any roles you would like to be associated with for the event.</small>
    **Name 2**: Enter any other name you would like to be associated with for the event. 
*   **What are your session preferences?**: <small>This field is not required. Please enter any session preferences or interests you would like to be aware of during the event.</small>
*   **Name**: Enter your name. (Required)
