# Hospital Innovation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees who wish to participate in the Hospital Innovation Roundtable event. Please fill out the form accurately and carefully to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, fill out the form with your first and last name.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you with event updates and information.
3. Select your role in the hospital or organization you represent.
4. Provide your organization name, department, and job title.
5. Enter your phone number and address.
6. Select your preferred event registration type (in-person or virtual).
7. Check the terms and conditions to ensure you agree to the event policies.
8. Finally, fill out any additional information or job details as required.
