# Chronic Illness Peer Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Chronic Illness Peer Support Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who are seeking support and guidance from others who share similar experiences with chronic illnesses. This form helps us understand your personal details, health background, and preferences for our support sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your date of birth.
3. Select your primary chronic condition that brings you to peer support.
4. Share your support goals and current treatments or management strategies.
5. Indicate your preferred meeting format, day of the week, time of day, and group size.
6. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number.
7. Confirm that you agree to maintain the confidentiality of all peer discussions.
8. Consent to being contacted by us to confirm your registration and session details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (2, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (3, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (4, email, required): Enter a valid email address to receive session updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (5, text, optional): Enter a phone number for urgent contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (6, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Primary Chronic Condition** (8, select_one, required): Select the condition that brings you to peer support.
	+ Arthritis
	+ Diabetes
	+ Fibromyalgia
	+ Chronic Fatigue Syndrome
	+ Multiple Sclerosis
	+ Other
* **Years Since Diagnosis** (9, number, optional): Enter the number of years since your diagnosis if applicable.
* **Support Goals** (10, select_multiple, required): Select all the support goals that apply to you.
	+ Share experiences with others
	+ Learn coping strategies
	+ Reduce isolation
	+ Get practical advice
	+ Emotional support
* **Describe Current Treatments or Management** (11, text, optional): Provide a brief overview of your current treatments or management strategies.
* **Preferred Meeting Format** (13, select_one, required): Choose your preferred meeting format.
	+ In-person
	+ Virtual
	+ Hybrid
	+ No preference
* **Preferred Day of Week** (14, select_one, optional): Choose a day of the week when you can attend sessions.
	+ Monday
	+ Tuesday
	+ Wednesday
	+ Thursday
	+ Friday
	+ Weekend
	+ Flexible
* **Preferred Time of Day** (15, select_one, optional): Choose a time of day when you can attend sessions.
	+ Morning
	+ Afternoon
	+ Evening
	+ Flexible
* **Preferred Group Size** (16, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred group size.
	+ Small group (5-8 people)
	+ Medium group (9-15 people)
	+ Large group (16+ people)
	+ One-on-one
* **Emergency Contact Name** (18, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (19, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to You** (20, text, optional): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Agree to Confidentiality** (21, select_one, required): Confirm that you agree to maintain the confidentiality of all peer discussions.
	+ Yes, I agree
	+ No, I do not agree
* **Consent to Contact** (22, select_one, required): Confirm that you consent to being contacted by us to confirm your registration and session details.
	+ Yes, contact me
	+ No, contact by email only

## Tips

* Please fill in your personal details accurately and truthfully.
* Select your preferred meeting format and time that suits you best.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at [support contact information].
* We will review your registration and confirm your session details with you before the session starts.
