# Smart Building Research Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for researchers to register their interest in participating in smart building research projects. It gathers information about the researcher's university, participant details, and research interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "University" field with the name of your university.
2. Enter your name as the "Participant" in the corresponding field.
3. Provide a brief description of the building details in the "Building Details" field.
4. Specify your research interests in the "Research Interests" field.
5. Select the participants involved in the research from the list in the "Participant List" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
7. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
8. If applicable, provide the website of your university in the "University Website" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **University** (`university_name`, Optional): Enter the name of the university where you are affiliated with.
* **Participant** (`participant_name`, Optional): Enter your name as the participant in this research project.
* **Building Details** (`building_details`, Optional): Provide a brief description of the building you are interested in.
* **Research Interests** (`research_interests`, Optional): Specify your research interests and areas of focus.
* **Participant List** (`participant_list`, Optional, Select Multiple): Select the participants involved in the research project.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, Optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, Optional): Enter your phone number for quick communication.
* **University Website** (`university_website`, Optional): If applicable, provide the website of your university for further information.
