# Training Program Calendar - Help Guide

## Purpose
The **Training Program Calendar** form is designed to gather information from participants about their experiences and feedback on a training program. This form aims to collect data on the purpose of the training, sessions attended, participant details, session notes, availability for follow-up, tools used during the training, feedback on tools, overall satisfaction, rating, recommendation, and final comments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by answering the first question, "What is the purpose of this training?" This field is required and should be filled with a brief description of the training's objective.
2. Next, select which of the following sessions the participant attended. Multiple session selections are allowed.
3. Enter the participant's name in the "What is the participant's name?" field.
4. If applicable, provide any questions or comments about the training in the "Do you have any questions about the training?" field.
5. Share any notes from the sessions attended in the "What are the notes from the session?" field. This field is required for each session.
6. Select the participant's availability for follow-up in the "What is the participant's availability for follow-up?" field.
7. Indicate the number of tools used during the training in the "How many tools do you have used during the training?" field.
8. Select which tools were used during the training in the "What are the tools used?" field. Multiple tool selections are allowed.
9. If applicable, provide feedback on the tools used in the "Do you have any feedback about the tools?" field.
10. Rate your overall satisfaction with the training in the "What is your overall satisfaction with the training?" field.
11. Select a rating for the training from the options provided in the "What is your rating for the training?" field.
12. Indicate if you would recommend this training in the "Would you recommend this training?" field.
13. Finally, provide any comments about the training in the "Do you have any comments about this training?" field.
14. Confirm that this is the end of the form by selecting "Is this the end of the form?" field.
