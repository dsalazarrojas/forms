# Kayaking Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for a kayaking activity or program. It is used to collect personal information, emergency contact details, and medical information from participants. Completing this form will help us ensure that we have the necessary contact and medical information in case of any incidents during the activity.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to provide the correct information for the emergency contact and guardian sections.
3. Read and understand the waiver carefully and select the correct response in the liability consent section.
4. Sign the waiver and emergency contact sections to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your name as it appears on your identification or official documents.
* **Last Name**: Your surname or family name.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be reached in case of any updates or notifications.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number where you can be reached during the activity.
* **Date**: The date of the kayaking activity or event you are participating in.
* **Time**: The time of the kayaking activity or event.
* **Address**: Your current address for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: The name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: The surname or family name of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: The phone number of the person you have designated as your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation**: The relation between you and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend).
* **Guardian Name**: The name of the person you have designated as your guardian.
* **Guardian Phone**: The phone number of the person you have designated as your guardian.
* **Guardian Relation**: The relation between you and the guardian (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Friend).
* **Liability Consent**: You must read and understand the waiver carefully and select the correct response.
* **Emergency Contact Signed**: Indicate if your emergency contact has signed and understood the waiver.
* **Guardian Signed**: Indicate if your guardian has signed and understood the waiver.
* **Waiver Signed**: Indicate if you have signed and understood the waiver.
* **Medical Condition**: If you have any medical condition that may affect your participation in the kayaking activity.
* **Medical Details**: Provide any additional medical details that may be relevant to your participation.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Condition**: If your emergency contact has any medical condition that may affect your participation in the kayaking activity.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Details**: Provide any additional medical details of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Authorization**: Indicate if your emergency contact or guardian has given authorization to share medical information.
* **Emergency Contact Medical Authorization**: Indicate if your emergency contact has given authorization to share medical information.
* **Medical Information**: Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant to your participation.
