# Internship Portal Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internship Portal Access Form is a simple form designed to collect essential information from students and interns to provide them access to the portal for internship opportunities. This form is meant to be completed by students or interns who are new to the portal and are looking to get started.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your personal and professional information.
2. Select your relevant skills and experiences from the dropdown menu.
3. Agree to the terms of use by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.
4. Once you have completed all the fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): This field is where you should enter your full name and any other identifying information. Please fill in your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required/optional): If you are a student, please fill in your student ID number or any other relevant student information.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for your contact information, including your email and phone number. Please make sure to include your current contact information.
* **Internship Info** (`internship_info`, text, required/optional): This field is for any relevant internship experience or information you would like to share.
* **Education Info** (`education_info`, text, required/optional): If you are a student, please fill in your current education level and any relevant academic information.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, required/optional): If you have relevant work experience, please describe it briefly.
* **Skills** (`skills`, select multiple, required/optional): Select any skills you have from the dropdown menu that are relevant to your internship interests.
* **Upload Documents** (`upload_documents`, note, required/optional): If required, upload any relevant documents to support your application.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit your application.
* **Terms of Use Agreement** (`agreement`, select one, required/optional): Please read and agree to the terms of use by selecting "Yes" from the dropdown menu.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* Ensure you select "Yes" to agree to the terms of use to continue with the application process.
