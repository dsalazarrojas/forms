# drama_club_audition_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The drama_club_audition_form is a registration form for individuals interested in joining the Drama Club. It captures personal details such as first and last name, email, and phone number. Users can select from various roles, submit their preferred time, and choose who submitted the form and when.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your First Name and Last Name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it is a valid email address.
3. Select your preferred role within the club by clicking on the "Role" field. Options include Drama Club Manager, Drama Club Assistant, Actor, Dancer, Singer, Musician, or Other.
4. If you wish, select the "Time" field and choose a valid time for your audition.
5. You can choose who submitted the form by selecting from "John", "Jane", or "Jim" in the "Submitted By" field.
6. Finally, click on the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields and choose a valid date and time for submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address here.
* **Role**: Select your preferred role within the club from the options provided.
* **Time**: Select a valid time for your audition.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Submitted By**: Select who submitted the form from the options provided.
* **Submission Date**: Choose a valid date for submission.
* **Submission Time**: Choose a valid time for submission.
* **Drama Club ID**: Select your Drama Club ID from the options provided.
* **Note**: Enter any notes or comments here.
* **Note 1**, **Note 2**, ..., **Note 7**, **Note 8**: Enter any additional notes or comments here.
* **Submission Form**: Select the submission form number from the options provided.

Note: All fields with "1", "2", "3", etc. in the label and options are likely IDs and can be ignored by users.
