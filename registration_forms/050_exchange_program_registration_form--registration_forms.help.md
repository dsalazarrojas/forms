# exchange_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exchange_program_registration_form is used for participants to register for an exchange program, providing necessary information for program administrators to process their registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Participant Information" section.
2. If you have a program administrator, fill in their information in the "Program Administrator" section.
3. Choose your program choice and agreement in the respective sections.
4. Sign and date your agreement.
5. Upload any additional images or files as needed.
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: 
  * **Participant Email**: A valid email address associated with the participant.
  * **Participant Phone**: A valid phone number associated with the participant.
  * **Participant Notes**: Any additional notes about the participant.
  * **Participant Additional Info**: Any additional information about the participant.
  * **Participant Program Choice**: Choose whether you are interested in participating in this program.
  * **Participant Agreement**: Choose whether you agree with the program terms.
  * **Participant Signature**: A signature from the participant, typically in the form of an image.
  * **Participant Signature Date**: The date of the participant signature.
  * **Participant Agreement Date**: The date of the participant agreement.
  * **Participant Additional Images**: Upload any additional images as needed.
  * **Participant Additional Files**: Upload any additional files as needed.
  * **Participant Additional Comments**: Any additional comments about the participant.
  * **Participant Additional Info 2**: Any additional information about the participant.
  * **Participant Additional Files 2**: Upload any additional files as needed.
  * **Participant Additional Comments 2**: Any additional comments about the participant.
* **Program Administrator Information**: 
  * **Program Administrator**: The name of the program administrator.
  * **Program Administrator Email**: A valid email address associated with the program administrator.
  * **Program Administrator Phone**: A valid phone number associated with the program administrator.
  * **Program Additional Info**: Any additional information about the program.
