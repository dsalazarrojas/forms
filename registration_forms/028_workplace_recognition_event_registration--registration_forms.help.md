# Workplace Recognition Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a workplace recognition event. It gathers essential information about the attendee, including their name, contact details, and event-specific data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter the Event Title to help us identify the event you're registering for.
3. Provide the correct Event Start Date and Event End Date for accurate event planning.
4. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
5. Provide your Phone number for easy communication.
6. Choose how you're registering (Online Registration or On-site Registration).
7. Enter your Registration Code to complete your registration.
8. If desired, add Additional Information to provide context about your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`attendee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is officially recorded in your personnel records or identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is officially recorded in your personnel records or identification documents.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, false): Enter the title of the event you're registering for, if known.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`, date, required): Choose the date the event started to ensure accurate event planning.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`, date, required): Choose the date the event ended to ensure accurate event planning.
* **Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`attendee_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for easy communication.
* **Registration Method** (`registration_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you're registering (Online Registration or On-site Registration).
* **Registration Code** (`registration_method`, text, required): Enter the registration code provided to you to complete your registration.
* **Additional Information** (`notes`, note, false): Provide any additional context or information about your attendance if desired.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information entered is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you're registering for this event before, do not register again.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact the event organizers for assistance.
