# Editable Resume Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect editable resume information from users for various purposes, such as job applications, career development, or personal projects. Please review our terms and conditions to understand how this data will be used.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal details section with your name, email, and any other relevant personal information.
2. Provide information about your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
3. List your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
4. Enumerate your skills, such as programming languages, software proficiency, or other relevant abilities.
5. Upload your resume in a supported file format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, or TXT).
6. Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields, click the "Submit" button to send your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Fill out this section with your name, email, and any other relevant personal information.
* **Work Experience** (`work_experience`, text, optional): Describe your work experience, including job titles, company names, and dates of employment.
* **Education** (`education`, text, optional): List your educational background, including institutions attended and degrees earned.
* **Skills** (`skills`, text, optional): Enumerate your skills, such as programming languages, software proficiency, or other relevant abilities.
* **Upload Resume** (`upload_resume`, text, optional): Upload your resume in a supported file format (e.g., PDF, DOCX, or TXT).
* **Submit** (text, required): Once you've filled out all the required and optional fields, click this button to send your information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Use a supported file format for the "Upload Resume" field.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
