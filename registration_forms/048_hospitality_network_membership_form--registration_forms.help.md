# Hospitality Network Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register new members for the Hospitality Network and collect payment and membership information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1.  Fill out the **full_name** field with your name, as it will be displayed publicly on the Hospitality Network site.
2.  Enter your **email** and verify it to ensure you can be reached for communication and updates.
3.  Enter your **contact_number** for easy contact and follow-up.
4.  Create a secure **password** and **confirm_password** for your account access.
5.  Provide your **preferences** about how you wish to use the Hospitality Network in the **preferences** field.
6.  Choose your **member_details** type, i.e., Individual, Family, or Student.
7.  Specify how you **found** out about the Hospitality Network, e.g., Social media, Word of mouth, Advertisement, or Other.
8.  If you found out through other means, describe in the **how_found_others** field.
9.  Select your **payment_method**, i.e., Online Payment, Offline Payment, or Bank Transfer.
10.  Choose your **payment_status**, i.e., Pending, Paid, or Not Paid.
11.  Select your **membership_type**, i.e., Monthly, Annually, or One-time.
12.  Choose your **membership_status**, i.e., Active, Inactive, or Trial.
13.  Enter the date you **created_at** your membership.
14.  Enter the date your **updated_at** the membership details.
15.  If applicable, mention who **created_by** and **updated_by**, e.g., your name or username.
16.  Enter the date you **member_since** joined the Hospitality Network.
17.  Specify the number of days you wish to have **membership_duration**.
18.  Enter the date of your **renewal_date**.
19.  If necessary, provide any additional **notes**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **full_name**: Enter your full name for public display on the Hospitality Network site.
* **email**: Enter your email address for communication and updates. Please double-check the address.
* **contact_number**: Provide a contact number for easy communication and follow-up.
* **password**: Create a secure password for your account access. Please choose a strong password.
* **confirm_password**: Confirm your password to ensure accurate account creation.
* **preferences**: Specify your preferences for using the Hospitality Network.
* **payment_details**: Note any payment-related information.
* **terms_of_service**: Accept our terms of service by selecting "Yes".
* **member_details**: Choose your member type, i.e., Individual, Family, or Student.
* **how_found**: Specify how you found out about the Hospitality Network.
* **how_found_others**: Provide additional details if you found out through other means.
* **member_since**: Enter the date you joined the Hospitality Network.
* **membership_duration**: Choose your membership duration in days.
* **payment_method**: Choose your payment method, i.e., Online Payment, Offline Payment, or Bank Transfer.
* **payment_status**: Select your payment status, i.e., Pending, Paid, or Not Paid.
* **membership_type**: Choose your membership type, i.e., Monthly, Annually, or One-time.
* **membership_status**: Choose your membership status, i.e., Active, Inactive, or Trial.
* **notes**: Provide any additional notes or details.
