# Business Partner Meet And Greet RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to gather essential information from business partners attending the meet and greet event.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Provide your attendee information:
   - Enter your full name in the "Attendee Name" field.
   - Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field to receive event confirmation.

2. Add your business details:
   - Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
   - Select your job title in the "Job Title" field.
   - Indicate your attendance status by choosing "Yes - I will attend," "No - unable to attend," or "Maybe - pending confirmation" in the "Attendance Status" field.

3. Specify your event and attendance details:
   - Select the date of the meet and greet event from the calendar in the "Event Date" field.
   - Enter the number of guests you plan to bring in the "Number of Guests" field.
   - Provide the names of any additional attendees in the "Guest Names" field.

4. Add any special requirements:
   - If you have dietary restrictions, specify them in the "Dietary Requirements" field.
   - Choose your drink preference (alcoholic, non-alcoholic, or no preference) in the "Beverage Preference" field.
   - Specify any accessibility needs in the "Accessibility Needs" field.

5. Share your interests and preferences:
   - Select your primary networking goals (potential partners, clients, industry peers, mentors, investors, or general networking) in the "Networking Goals" field.
   - Choose the types of partnerships you are interested in (strategic alliance, distribution, co-marketing, technology integration, referral partnership, or not sure yet) in the "Partnership Interests" field.
   - Provide a 30-second description of your business in the "Elevator Pitch" field.

6. Additional information:
   - Indicate if you will bring business cards in the "Business Card Availability" field.
   - If you are interested in presenting at future events, select "Yes - interested" in the "Presentation Interest" field.
   - Specify any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.
   - Finally, describe how you heard about the event in the "How Did You Hear About This Event" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name (Attendee Name, text, required)**: Enter your full name to confirm your attendance.
* **Email Address (Email Address, email, required)**: Enter your email address for event confirmation and follow-up.
* **Phone Number (Attendee Phone, text, optional)**: Enter your contact phone number (if available).
* **Company Name (Company Name, text, required)**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title (Job Title, text, required)**: Enter your job position in the company.
* **Attendance Status (Attendance Status, select_one, required)**: Choose "Yes - I will attend," "No - unable to attend," or "Maybe - pending confirmation" for event attendance.
* **Event Date (Event Date, date, required)**: Select the date of the meet and greet event from the calendar.
* **Number of Guests (Number of Guests, number, required)**: Enter the total number of attendees, including yourself.
* **Guest Names (Guest Names, text, optional)**: Enter the names of any additional attendees.
* **Dietary Requirements (Dietary Requirements, text, optional)**: Specify any food allergies or restrictions.
* **Beverage Preference (Beverage Preference, select_one, optional)**: Choose "Alcoholic beverages," "Non-alcoholic only," or "No preference" for drink preference.
* **Networking Goals (Networking Goals, select_multiple, required)**: Select your primary networking goals (potential partners, potential clients, industry peers, mentors, investors, or general networking).
* **Partnership Interests (Partnership Interests, select_multiple, required)**: Choose the types of partnerships you are interested in (strategic alliance, distribution, co-marketing, technology integration, referral partnership, or not sure yet).
* **Elevator Pitch (Elevator Pitch, text, optional)**: Provide a 30-second description of your business.
* **Business Card Availability (Business Card Availability, select_one, optional)**: Indicate if you will bring business cards (yes, limited, or no - digital only).
* **Presentation Interest (Presentation Interest, select_one, optional)**: If interested in presenting at future events, select "Yes - interested."
* **Table Preferences (Table Preferences, text, optional)**: Specify any seating preferences.
* **Accessibility Needs (Accessibility Needs, text, optional)**: Describe any accommodations needed for your attendance.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event (How Did You Hear About This Event, select_one, optional)**: Choose how you heard about the event (email invitation, social media, colleague referral, website, or other).
* **Additional Comments (Additional Comments, text, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or information.

This form helps in ensuring a smooth and successful event by providing essential information to facilitate meaningful connections and potential partnerships.
