# holiday-camp-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "holiday-camp-registration" form is used to register a camper for a holiday camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Select an activity for the camper from the list provided.
2.  Enter the camper's name.
3.  Enter the camper's email address.
4.  Choose the availability of the camper (yes or no).
5.  Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
6.  Enter the parent's email address.
7.  Choose the time of stay for the camper.
8.  If applicable, enter the fee for the camper's activity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Holiday Camp Activity**: Select the activity for the camper from the available options.
* **Camper Name**: Enter the camper's name in the format of [First Name Last Name].
* **Camper Email**: Enter the camper's email address. This will be used to communicate with the camper and their parent.
* **Availability of Camper**: Choose whether the camper is available (yes) or not available (no) for the activity.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's or guardian's name for the camper.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the parent's or guardian's email address for the camper.
* **Time of Activity**: Choose the duration of stay for the camper (e.g., one day, two weeks).
* **Camper Activity Fee**: If the activity has a fee, enter the amount. This is optional and will only apply if the activity has a fee associated with it.

## Tips
*   Make sure to select the correct activity for the camper.
*   Ensure that the camper's name and email are accurate.
*   If the camper is not available, please provide a valid reason.
*   Enter the correct parent's or guardian's contact information.
*   If the activity has a fee, ensure that you enter the correct amount.

Note: The form is designed to be completed by a parent or guardian and requires accurate information to ensure that the camper has a successful and enjoyable experience.
