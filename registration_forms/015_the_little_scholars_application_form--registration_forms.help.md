# The Little Scholars Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Little Scholars Application Form is designed for prospective students to provide necessary information for their application process. This form is a crucial step in getting admitted into The Little Scholars institution.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to while in The Little Scholars institution.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your family name as it appears on your identification or documents.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, optional): Provide your date of birth in the format `YYYY-MM-DD`.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter a contact phone number for communication purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your current address or the address where you can be contacted.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your student ID number or any other unique identifier you have.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school you are currently attending or have attended.
