# orthopedic_clinic_new_patient_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from new patients at an orthopedic clinic, including demographic, contact, and medical-related information. This information will be used to create a patient account and schedule follow-up visits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Enter your address information in the "Address Line 1", "Address Line 2", "City", "State", and "Country" fields.
6. Select your insurance provider in the "Insurance Provider" field.
7. Enter the date of your visit in the "Date of Visit" field.
8. Select your medical group in the "Medical Group" field.
9. Enter your medical condition in the "Medical Condition" field.
10. Select your insurance type in the "Insurance Type" field.
11. Choose your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
12. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
13. Enter their phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
14. Select your relation with the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relation" field.
15. Select your medical specialty in the "Medical Specialty" field.
16. If applicable, enter any doctor recommendation in the "Doctor Recommendation" field.
17. If applicable, enter follow-up visit information in the "Follow-up Visit" field.
18. Sign your name in the "Patient Signature" field.
19. Select a follow-up visit date (if necessary) in the "Date of Visit 2" field.
20. Select your insurance provider for the follow-up visit in the "Insurance Provider 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Select your date of birth.
* **Address Line 1** (Address Line 1, text, required): Enter your street address.
* **Address Line 2** (Address Line 2, text, required): Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter your city.
* **State** (State, text, required): Enter your state or province.
* **Country** (Country, text, required): Enter your country of residence.
* **Date of Visit** (Date of Visit, date, required): Select the date of your visit.
* **Insurance Provider** (Insurance Provider, text, required): Select your insurance provider.
* **Medical Group** (Medical Group, text, required): Select your medical group.
* **Medical Condition** (Medical Condition, text, required): Enter your medical condition.
* **Insurance Type** (Insurance Type, text, required): Select your insurance type.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (Preferred Contact Method, text, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (Emergency Contact Relation, text, required): Select your relation with the emergency contact.
* **Medical Specialty** (Medical Specialty, text, required): Select your medical specialty.
* **Doctor Recommendation** (Doctor Recommendation, text, required): If applicable, enter any doctor recommendation.
* **Follow-up Visit** (Follow-up Visit, text, required): If applicable, enter follow-up visit information.
* **Patient Signature** (Patient Signature, text, required): Sign your name.
* **Date of Visit 2** (Date of Visit 2, date, optional): Select the date of your follow-up visit (if applicable).
* **Insurance Provider 2** (Insurance Provider 2, text, optional): Select your insurance provider for the follow-up visit (if applicable).

Note: All fields marked as required must be completed to submit the form.
