# Community Choral Festival Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Choral Festival Tournament Entry Form is designed for choirs to register for the Community Choral Festival Tournament. It collects details about the choir, including performance information, technical needs, and logistical requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure all required fields are filled out.
2. Select all required fields with an asterisk (\*).
3. Double-check the form for any errors or duplications before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### -- Choir Information --
* **Name of the Choir** (`choir_full_name_choir`): Enter the name of your choir.
* **Choir Director's Full Name** (`choir_director_name_choir`): Enter the full name of your choir director.
* **Official Choir Email Address** (`choir_email_choir`): Enter the email address of your choir, for festival correspondence and results.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`choir_phone_choir`): Enter your choir's primary contact phone number.
* **Organization or School Affiliation** (`choir_organization_affiliation_choir`): Specify your choir's organization or school affiliation (e.g., Community Church, High School, Community Group).

### -- Performance Information --
* **Tournament Performance Category** (`performance_category_choir`): Select one of the provided options (Mixed Choir, Women's Choir, etc.).
* **Estimated Performance Duration (including transitions)** (`estimated_performance_time_choir`): Enter the estimated duration of your performance in minutes.
* **Number of Performers in your Group** (`number_of_performers_choir`): Enter the number of performers in your choir.
* **Planned Repertoire Titles and Composers** (`repertoire_titles_composers_choir`): List the titles and composers of the pieces you plan to perform.
* **Will you require a festival-provided piano** (`piano_accompaniment_required_choir`): Confirm whether you will require a festival-provided piano.
* **Do you require choral risers** (`choral_risers_needed_choir`): Confirm whether you will need choral risers.
* **Any specific audio/visual or technical requirements** (`audio_visual_requirements_choir`): Specify any specific technical requirements (e.g., microphones, playback devices).
* **Preferred Performance Time Slot** (`preferred_performance_times_choir`): Select all applicable time slots for your performance (e.g., Friday Evening, Saturday Morning).

### -- Final Submission and Acknowledgment --
* **I acknowledge that my choir will abide by the festival tournament rules** (`festival_rules_acknowledgment_choir`): Confirm that your choir agrees to abide by the festival rules.
* **I confirm that the entry fee has been or will be paid before the deadline** (`entry_fee_payment_confirmation_choir`): Confirm that the entry fee has been or will be paid.
* **Digital Signature of Choir Director** (`director_signature_digital_choir`): Enter the full name of your choir director as a digital signature.
* **Date of Entry** (`submission_date_final_choir`): Enter the date of your submission.

Note: This guide is designed to be a general explanation of the form. If you're unsure about a specific field or have any further questions, contact your festival organizers for assistance.
