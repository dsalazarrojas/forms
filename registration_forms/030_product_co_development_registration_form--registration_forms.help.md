# Product Co Development Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register your product for co-development with our partner. Please use this form to provide us with information about your product and your collaboration expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Answer the questions on the Registration Information page with your product details.
2. On the Collaboration Details page, provide information about your collaboration preferences.
3. On the Submission page, add any additional comments or information you'd like to submit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Title** (`title`, string, optional): Enter a brief title for your product.
- **Registration Information** (`product_co_development_registration_form_page_1`, text, optional): This section is for you to provide information about your product. Please include any relevant details such as product name, description, and category.
- **Collaboration Details** (`product_co_development_registration_form_page_2`, text, optional): Here, describe your collaboration expectations and preferences for your product development project.
- **Submission** (`product_co_development_registration_form_page_3`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to submit about your product and co-development project.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you have any further questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
