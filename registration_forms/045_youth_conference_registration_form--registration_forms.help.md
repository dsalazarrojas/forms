# Youth Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a youth conference, including details such as conference name, location, start and end dates, and registration method. It also allows conference organizers to add additional notes and information about the conference and registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the conference name in the "Conference Name" field.
2. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Select the conference start date in the "Conference Start Date" field.
5. Select the conference end date in the "Conference End Date" field.
6. Enter the conference location in the "Conference Location" field.
7. Provide a brief description of the conference in the "Conference Description" field (optional).
8. Enter the conference capacity in the "Conference Capacity" field (optional).
9. Enter the conference price in the "Conference Price" field.
10. Select the early bird registration option (Early Bird, Regular, or Late) in the "Early Bird Registration" field.
11. Select the registration method (Online or In Person) in the "Registration Method" field.
12. Add any additional notes about the conference and registration process in the "Conference Note", "Conference Price Note", "Registration Note", and "Early Bird Note" fields (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conference Name** (text): Enter the name of the conference.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Conference Start Date** (date): Select the start date of the conference.
* **Conference End Date** (date): Select the end date of the conference.
* **Conference Location** (text): Enter the location of the conference.
* **Conference Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the conference (optional).
* **Conference Capacity** (number): Enter the capacity of the conference (optional).
* **Conference Price** (number): Enter the price of the conference.
* **Early Bird Registration** (select_one): Select the early bird registration option (Early Bird, Regular, or Late).
* **Registration Method** (select_one): Select the registration method (Online or In Person).
* **Conference Note** (text): Add any additional notes about the conference (optional).
* **Conference Price Note** (text): Add any additional notes about the conference price (optional).
* **Registration Note** (text): Add any additional notes about the registration process (optional).
* **Early Bird Note** (text): Add any additional notes about the early bird registration option (optional).
* **Registration Method Note** (text): Add any additional notes about the registration method (optional).
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Conference Start Time** (time): Select the start time of the conference (optional).
* **Conference End Time** (time): Select the end time of the conference (optional).
* **Conference Duration** (number): Enter the duration of the conference (optional).
* **Conference Language** (text): Enter the conference language (optional).
* **Conference Level** (text): Enter the conference level (optional).
* **Conference Status** (select_one): Select the conference status (Active or Inactive).
* **Conference Status Note** (text): Add any additional notes about the conference status (optional).

Note: The asterisk (*) in the field labels indicates that the field is optional.
