# Therapists Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Therapists Network Membership Form is designed to help therapists create a professional profile on our platform, allowing them to connect with others, access resources, and share knowledge.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a unique Username in the "Username" field.
6. Enter a secure Password in the "Password" field.
7. Confirm your Password in the "Confirm Password" field.
8. (Optional) Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions by checking the "Terms and Conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the surname of the name you are known by.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid Email address that will be used to log in and communicate with the network.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact phone number for easy communication.
* **Username** (`username`, `text`, **required**): Choose a unique and memorable username that will represent your professional identity on the network.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, **required**): Enter a secure Password for your account. 
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, **required**): Re-enter your Password to ensure accuracy.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions`, `text`, **optional**): (Optional) Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions of the network before proceeding.

## Tips
- Use a unique and memorable Username to represent your professional identity.
- Enter a secure Password and Confirm it for accuracy.
- Be accurate when entering your contact information for easy communication.
- Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions before registering.
