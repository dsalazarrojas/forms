# Tattoo Parlor Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from customers, including their contact details and service preferences, as well as obtain their consent to receive services at the parlor.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, please fill out all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
- Ensure you select only the services you wish to receive from the parlor.
- Double-check the information you enter before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last name** (2, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to receive communication from the parlor.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Birthdate** (5, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YY format (e.g., 01/01/2000).
* **Address** (6, text, optional): Enter your current address for contact and billing purposes.
* **Service** (7, select_one, optional): Select the type of service you wish to receive from the parlor (Tattoo, Piercing, or Tattoo removal).
* **Designs** (8, select_multiple, optional): Select the design(s) you are interested in (Tiger, Snake, or Dragon).
* **Consent** (9, note, optional): Read and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the parlor's services.
* **Signature** (10, note, optional): Sign your name to confirm agreement with the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Ensure all fields are completed accurately to avoid delays in service processing.
- Review the terms and conditions carefully before signing the consent form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the parlor staff for assistance.
