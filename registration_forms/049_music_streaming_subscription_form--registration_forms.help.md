# music_streaming_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect user information and subscription details for a music streaming service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the User Information section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your subscription plan from the options in the Subscription Plan section.
3. Enter your payment information in the Payment Information section.
4. Select your preferred payment method in the Payment Method section.
5. Confirm your payment details in the Confirm Payment section.
6. Agree to the Terms and Conditions by selecting "Yes" in the Agree to Terms section.
7. Finally, click the Submit button to complete your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number in this section.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your subscription plan from the options provided.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your payment details in this section.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
* **Confirm Payment** (`confirm_payment`, text, required/optional): Re-enter your payment information to confirm.
* **Email** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Password** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_password`, text, required/optional): Enter a password for your account.
* **Password Confirmation** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_password_confirmation`, text, required/optional): Re-enter your password to confirm.
* **Agree to Terms** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_agree_to_terms`, select_one, required/optional): Select "Yes" to agree to the Terms and Conditions.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_terms_and_conditions`, note, required/optional): Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions by selecting "Yes" in the Agree to Terms section.
* **Submit** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_submit_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to complete your subscription.
* **Cancel** (`music_streaming_subscription_form_cancel_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to cancel your subscription.

Note: The Cancel button does not submit any data, it only cancels the current action.
