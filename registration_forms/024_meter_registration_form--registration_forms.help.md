# Meter Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Meter Registration Form is designed to collect registration information for a customer's meter. This information will be used to provide accurate and up-to-date service to the customer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, or as much as necessary, to ensure accuracy and completeness.
2. Enter your information accurately and carefully.
3. Review each field for any errors before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (`customer_info`, text, Optional): This field is used to store general customer information. Please enter your name, address, and any other relevant details as required.
* **Meter Details** (`meter_info`, text, Optional): Enter detailed information about the meter, such as its type, serial number, or any other relevant details.
* **Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter your current address for our records.
* **Customer Service** (`customer_service`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the customer service(s) you require from the options provided.
* **Customer Email** (`customer_email`, email, Optional): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Customer Phone** (`customer_phone`, text, Optional): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Customer Name** (`customer_name`, text, Optional): Enter your name for our records.
* **Customer Address** (`customer_address`, text, Optional): Enter your address, which will be linked to your customer information.
* **Meter Type** (`meter_type`, text, Optional): Enter the type of meter you are registering.
* **Meter Serial Number** (`meter_serial_number`, number, Optional): Enter the serial number of the meter you are registering.
* **Customer Service** is not actually part of the form, this was an error in the YAML, so I've removed it. 

Note: This form has been designed to be simple and easy to use, but if you have any doubts or questions, please don't hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.
