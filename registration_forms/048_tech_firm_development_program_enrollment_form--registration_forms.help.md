# Tech Firm Development Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather your information and skills to determine your eligibility for the Tech Firm Development Program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Resume Link).
2. Select the programming languages you are proficient in (e.g. C#, C++, C).
3. Describe your relevant experience, skills, and education.
4. Provide any additional skills or training you have received.
5. List the team you have been working with for training purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Please enter your email address.
* **Resume Link** (Text, Required): Please upload or paste a link to your resume.
* **Programming Languages** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select all programming languages you are proficient in (e.g. C#, C++, C).
* **Experience** (Text, Optional): Describe your relevant experience in the field.
* **Skills** (Text, Optional): List any relevant skills you have.
* **Education** (Text, Optional): Please provide your education background.
* **Training Team** (Text, Optional): List the team you have been working with for training purposes.

## Tips
* Ensure that your email address is up-to-date and can be verified.
* Use a professional format for your resume to increase the chances of being considered for the program.
* Be specific when describing your experience, skills, and education.
* Only select programming languages you are proficient in.
