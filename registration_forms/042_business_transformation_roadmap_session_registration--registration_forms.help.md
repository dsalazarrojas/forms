## Business Transformation Roadmap Session Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for a Business Transformation Roadmap Session. The session aims to discuss and plan business transformation initiatives, focusing on areas such as digital transformation, organizational change, process improvement, technology implementation, and business model change. By completing this form, participants will be able to express their preferences and provide information necessary for a successful session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name and title/position in the company.
2. Enter your company's name and industry sector.
3. Select your company's size (small, medium, large, or enterprise).
4. Enter your contact email address and phone number.
5. Provide your preferred dates for the session.
6. Choose your preferred session dates (up to two dates).
7. Select your preferred session time (morning, mid-morning, afternoon, or late afternoon).
8. Choose the session format you prefer (in-person, video conference, phone call, or hybrid).
9. Select the areas you would like to focus on during the session (digital transformation, organizational change, process improvement, technology implementation, business model change, or other).
10. Indicate your current transformation stage (planning, early implementation, active transformation, scaling, or post-implementation).
11. Describe the key challenges you are facing.
12. Outline your goals for the session.
13. If applicable, specify the number of team members attending the session.
14. If applicable, describe any previous transformation experience.
15. Finally, confirm your registration by providing today's date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Your Title or Position** (2, text, required): Enter your job title or position in the company.
* **Company Name** (3, text, required): Type in your company's name.
* **Industry** (4, text, optional): Select your industry sector.
* **Company Size** (5, select_one, required): Select your company's size (small, medium, large, or enterprise).
* **Email Address** (6, email, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (7, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Preferred Session Dates** (8, text, required): Type in your preferred session dates.
* **First Preferred Date** (9, date, optional): Select your first preferred date for the session.
* **Second Preferred Date** (10, date, optional): Select your second preferred date for the session.
* **Time Preference** (11, select_one, required): Choose your preferred session time (morning, mid-morning, afternoon, late afternoon, or flexible).
* **Session Format Preference** (12, select_one, required): Select the session format you prefer (in-person, video conference, phone call, or hybrid).
* **Transformation Focus Areas** (13, select_multiple, required): Select the areas you would like to focus on during the session (digital transformation, organizational change, process improvement, technology implementation, business model change, or other).
* **Current Transformation Stage** (14, select_one, required): Indicate your current transformation stage (planning, early implementation, active transformation, scaling, or post-implementation).
* **Key Challenges** (15, text, required): Describe the key challenges you are facing.
* **Goals for This Session** (16, text, required): Outline your goals for the session.
* **Number of Team Members Attending** (17, number, optional): If applicable, specify the number of team members attending the session.
* **Previous Transformation Experience** (18, text, optional): If applicable, describe any previous transformation experience.
* **Registration Date** (19, date, required): Confirm your registration by providing today's date.
