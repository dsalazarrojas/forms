# Youth Football Player Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from youth football players for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
- Review each section carefully to ensure all required information is provided.
- If a section is not required, you can skip it or leave it blank.
- If you're unsure about any information, ask for assistance from the relevant authority.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the player's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the player's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the player's date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the player's parent or guardian.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address of the player or their parent/guardian.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the contact phone number of the player or their parent/guardian.
* **Team** (`team`, select_one, required): Select the player's team from the options provided.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): If the player has any medical conditions, enter the details in this field.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, text, optional): If the player has any allergies, enter the details in this field.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Authorization** (`medical_authorization`, note, optional): This is a note field for any medical authorization.
* **Medical Report** (`medical_report`, text, required): Provide any relevant medical report or information.
* **Player Signature** (`player_signature`, text, required): The player must sign this field to confirm their registration.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, text, required): The parent or guardian must sign this field to confirm their consent.
* **Medical Release** (`medical_release`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select 'Yes' to allow medical treatment or procedures.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, select_multiple, optional): If applicable, select 'Yes' for payment terms and conditions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select the payment method (Online, Offline).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Select the payment frequency (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, optional): Select the payment status (Paid, Outstanding).
* **Additional Payment Method** (`payment_method_additional`, text, optional): Provide any additional payment information.
* **Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

Note: This guide is for the youth football player registration form, please refer to the relevant authorities for further information or clarification.
