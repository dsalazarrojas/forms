# Expense Allocation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Allocation Roundtable Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants about their preferences and expectations. This information will help the organizers to better understand the needs and priorities of the attendees.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will be attending the event.
2. Fill in your name in the designated field.
3. Provide a valid email address to facilitate communication.
4. Enter your phone number for any further contact or coordination.
5. Select your role in the roundtable discussion (Admin, Manager, or Other).
6. Choose the types of expenses you are interested in discussing (Travel, Entertainment, Accommodation, or Other).
7. Enter the amount you are willing to allocate for expenses (optional).
8. Select a date and time for the roundtable discussion (optional).
9. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (Yes, No): This field asks if you will be attending the event.
* **Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
* **E-Mail**: Enter a valid email address to allow the organizers to contact you for further information.
* **Phone**: Provide your phone number for any further coordination or contact.
* **Role** (Admin, Manager, Other): Select your role in the roundtable discussion to help the organizers better understand your expectations.
* **Expense Type** (Travel, Entertainment, Accommodation, Other): Choose the types of expenses you are interested in discussing.
* **Amount**: Enter the amount you are willing to allocate for expenses (if any).
* **Date**: Select a date for the roundtable discussion (if any).
* **Time**: Choose a time for the roundtable discussion (if any).
* **Note**: Add any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
## Tips
* Make sure to provide a valid email address and phone number to ensure that the organizers can contact you for further information.
* Choose your role and expense types accurately to help the organizers better understand your expectations.
* The amount field is optional, but it's recommended to fill it in if you have a specific amount in mind.
* If you have any specific comments or notes, use the "Note" field to provide more details.
