# Yoga Class Signup & Schedule Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who want to sign up for a yoga class. The form has multiple sections to gather relevant information about the participant and their schedule preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the participant info section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2.  In the Schedule Preference section, select the days and times you are available to attend the class.
3.  In the Preferred Classes section, select the classes you are interested in or prefer to attend.
4.  In the Class Preferences section, select any additional preferences, such as difficulty level or style of class, you have.
5.  For each subsequent page, repeat steps 1-4.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant info (participant\_info)**: This is the first section of the form where you will provide your name and contact information. Please fill in your name and contact details accurately to ensure we can get in touch with you about your class schedule.
*   **Schedule Preference (schedule\_preference)**: This section asks you to select the days and times you are available to attend the class. Please choose the days and times that work best for you.
*   **Preferred Classes (preferred\_classes)**: Here, you can select the classes you are interested in or prefer to attend. Please choose the classes that suit your interests and preferences.
*   **Class Preferences (class\_preferences)**: In this section, you can select any additional preferences, such as difficulty level or style of class, you have. Please choose the options that best fit your needs.
*   **Signup Date (signup\_date)**: This is the date you would like to sign up for the class. Please select a date that works best for you.
*   **Participant info 1-4 (participant\_info\_1-4)**: These are sections for you to provide additional information about yourself.
*   **Schedule Preference 1-4 (schedule\_preference\_1-4)**: These sections ask you to select your schedule preferences for each subsequent page.
*   **Preferred Classes 1-4 (preferred\_classes\_1-4)**: Here, you can select the classes you are interested in or prefer to attend for each subsequent page.
*   **Class Preferences 1-4 (class\_preferences\_1-4)**: These sections allow you to select any additional preferences, such as difficulty level or style of class, you have for each subsequent page.
*   **Signup Date 1-4 (signup\_date\_1-4)**: These are the dates you would like to sign up for each subsequent page. 

Note: The form is designed to be completed on multiple pages, each with its own sections to collect information. Please make sure to fill in all sections accurately to ensure your preferences are registered correctly.
