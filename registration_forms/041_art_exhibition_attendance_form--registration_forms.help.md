# Art Exhibition Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Art Exhibition Attendance Form is used to gather information from visitors about their attendance plans, contact preferences, and interests. This information will help the exhibition organizers tailor their services and events according to the visitors' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required information:
	* Full Name
	* Email Address
	* Exhibition Name
	* Exhibition Date
	* Party Size - Number of Visitors
	* Preferred Contact Method
2. Select your attendance details:
	* If you are attending the opening reception
	* If you are interested in a guided tour
	* If you are interested in an artist talk or panel discussion
	* If you are interested in a workshop or demonstration
3. Add any additional comments or special requests

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`visitor_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed for exhibition organizers and staff.
* **Email Address** (`visitor_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive communication from the exhibition organizers.
* **Exhibition Name** (`exhibition_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the exhibition you are attending.
* **Exhibition Date** (`exhibition_date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to visit the exhibition.
* **Party Size - Number of Visitors** (`party_size`, `number`, required): Indicate the number of people in your group attending the exhibition.
* **Phone Number** (`visitor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted by the exhibition organizers.
* **Phone Number Hint**: This information is optional, but it will help the organizers to contact you if needed.
* **Accessibility or Mobility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility or mobility needs, please specify them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please specify them here.
* **Types of Art Interested In** (`art_interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the types of art you are interested in (e.g., Painting, Sculpture, Photography, etc.).
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`contact_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select how you prefer to be contacted by the exhibition organizers (e.g., Email, Phone, Text message, No contact).
* **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`newsletter`, `select_one`, optional): If you want to receive updates from the exhibition organizers, select this option.
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional comments or special requests, please specify them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to help the exhibition organizers understand your needs and preferences.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact the exhibition organizers directly.
* The information collected through this form will be used to tailor the services and events according to your preferences.
