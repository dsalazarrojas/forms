# Correctional Facility Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from registrants visiting or interacting with a correctional facility. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and safe experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please read each field carefully to understand what information is required.
2. Enter the correct information for each field as prompted.
3. Make sure to answer all required fields to avoid any delays or issues with your visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **registrant_info** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your name and any other identifying information as requested.
* **contact_info** (Text, Required/Optional): Provide your contact information, including any phone number or email address you'd like to be reached at.
* **rule_acknowledgments** (Text, Required/Optional): Read and acknowledge the rules and protocols applicable to your visit.
* **visitor_details** (Text, Required/Optional): Share any relevant details about your visit, such as the purpose or any special requirements.
* **contact_person** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter a contact person's phone number.
* **contact_method** (Select One, Required/Optional): Choose your preferred contact method (Yes/No).
* **contact_number** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your contact number, if applicable.
* **rule_accepted** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Indicate which rules or protocols you've accepted.
* **rule_accepted_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations related to the rules accepted.
* **visit_frequency** (Select One, Required/Optional): Confirm whether you will visit the facility frequently or not.
* **visit_date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date of your visit.
* **visit_time** (Time, Required/Optional): Provide the time of your visit.
* **registrant_type** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Specify the type of registrant (e.g., visitor, patient, etc.).
* **registrant_type_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional details related to registrant type.
* **rule_10** (Select One, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to the 10th rule.
* **rule_10_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the 10th rule.
* **rule_16** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Indicate your response to the 16th rule.
* **rule_16_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the 16th rule.
* **rule_20** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to the 20th rule.
* **rule_20_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the 20th rule.
* **rule_21** (Select Multiple, Required/Optional): Select your response to the 21st rule.
* **rule_21_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the 21st rule.
* **rule_22** (Select One, Required/Optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to the 22nd rule.
* **rule_22_text** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the 22nd rule.
* **rule_23** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or explanations for the 23rd rule.
