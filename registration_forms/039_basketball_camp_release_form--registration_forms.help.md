# Basketball Camp Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Basketball Camp Release Form is a registration form designed to collect essential information from participants and their parents/guardians about their participation in the basketball camp. This form helps the camp staff to have accurate and up-to-date information about each participant, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant's information, including their name, date of birth, and age.
2. Enter your parent/guardian's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. Provide emergency contact information, including the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
4. If applicable, list any medical conditions or health issues your participant has.
5. List any allergies or medications your participant is currently taking.
6. If applicable, provide your participant's physician's name.
7. If applicable, provide your participant's insurance information.
8. Read and agree to the liability release and assume risk statements.
9. Confirm that all provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant name**: Enter your participant's name as it appears on their identification documents.
* **Date of birth**: Enter your participant's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Age**: Enter your participant's age.
* **Parent/Guardian name**: Enter your name and contact information as the parent or guardian of the participant.
* **Parent/Guardian email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with important updates.
* **Parent/Guardian phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of an emergency contact who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Relationship to participant**: Explain how you are related to the participant (e.g., parent, guardian, or emergency contact).
* **Medical conditions or health issues**: If your participant has any medical conditions or health issues, list them here.
* **Allergies**: If your participant has any allergies, list them here.
* **Current medications**: If your participant is taking any medications, list them here.
* **Physician name**: If your participant has a regular physician, provide their name.
* **Insurance provider**: If your participant has insurance, provide the name of the insurance provider.
* **Policy number**: If your participant has insurance, provide the policy number.
* **Insurance provider**: If your participant has insurance, provide the name of the insurance provider.
* **Liability release**: Acknowledge the risks associated with participation and release the camp and staff from liability.
* **Assume risk**: Acknowledge that you assume all risks associated with participation.
* **Release liability**: Agree to release the camp and staff from liability.
* **Parent consent**: As the parent/guardian, confirm your consent to your child's participation.
* **Confirm information**: Confirm that all provided information is accurate.

Note: Some fields are required, marked with an asterisk (*). Please make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues.
