# City Pass Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The City Pass Registration form is used to register for a City Pass, which grants access to city services. This includes public transportation, parking, libraries, recreational facilities, and museums. To register, you will need to provide personal and contact information, as well as proof of residency and intended use.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand the required fields and options.
2. Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
3. Provide your date of birth.
4. Enter your residential address.
5. Select your neighborhood (if applicable).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Provide your contact phone number.
8. Choose the type of proof of residency you are submitting (e.g., utility bill, lease agreement, voter ID, or tax statement).
9. Select the type of pass you would like to register for (e.g., standard resident pass, senior citizen pass, student pass, or visitor pass).
10. Indicate the primary intended use of your City Pass (if applicable).
11. If applicable, select whether you would like to enable automatic renewal of your pass.
12. Confirm that a non-refundable processing fee of $25.00 applies to new registrations.
13. Agree to the data privacy terms and conditions.
14. Sign your name digitally.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Residential Address**: Enter your current residential address.
* **Neighborhood**: If you live in a specific neighborhood, enter it here.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Contact Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Proof of Residency Type**: Select the type of proof of residency you are submitting (e.g., utility bill, lease agreement, voter ID, or tax statement).
* **Select Pass Type**: Choose the type of pass you would like to register for (e.g., standard resident pass, senior citizen pass, student pass, or visitor pass).
* **Primary Intended Use**: Select all the primary intended uses of your City Pass (e.g., public transportation, city parking, public libraries, recreational facilities, or museum access).
* **Enable Auto-Renewal**: If you would like to enable automatic renewal of your pass, select "Yes, please."
* **A non-refundable processing fee of 25.00 applies to new registrations**: This is a notification that a non-refundable processing fee of $25.00 applies to new registrations.
* **I agree to the data privacy terms and conditions**: Confirm that you understand and agree to the data privacy terms and conditions.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**: Sign your name digitally, typing your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date**: Enter the date you are submitting this application.
