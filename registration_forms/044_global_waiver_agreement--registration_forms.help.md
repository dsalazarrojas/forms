# Global Waiver Agreement - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Global Waiver Agreement form is used to collect information from participants and staff members prior to an event or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with all required fields.
2. Review and agree to the waiver terms.
3. Sign the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Details**: Enter your personal details including your name, which will be displayed as a field for emergency contact and participant name.
- **Emergency Contact**: Enter your emergency contact person's name and phone number.
- **Waiver Agreement**: Agree to participate in the event or activity by selecting the relevant option.
  - Select "I agree to participate" to continue with the event or activity.
  - Select "I disagree to participate" if you wish to opt out.
- **Date** and **Time**: Enter the date and time you plan to participate in the event or activity.
- **Participant Name**: Enter your name as a participant.
- **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
- **Email**: Enter your email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **Address**: Enter your address.
- **Zip**: Enter your ZIP code.
- **Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement.
- **Participant Concerns**: Enter any concerns you have as a participant.
- **Relationship**: Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
- **Participant Agreed**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the waiver terms.
- **Staff Agreed**: Confirm that staff members have read and agreed to the waiver terms.
- **Staff Signature**: Sign the form to confirm staff agreement.
- **Participant Signature**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement as a participant.
- **Participant Date**: Enter the date you signed the form.
- **Staff Date**: Enter the date staff members signed the form.
- **Event Details**: Enter any additional event details.
- **Participant Notes**: Enter any notes you have as a participant.
- **Staff Notes**: Enter any notes you have as a staff member.
- **Participant Signature 2**: Sign the form to confirm your agreement as a participant.
- **Staff Signature 2**: Sign the form to confirm staff agreement.
- **Submitted**: Confirm that the form has been submitted.

Note: Fields with required status "true" are mandatory and must be filled out.
