# talent-pool-registration-form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering candidates to a talent pool. The purpose of this form is to collect information about a candidate, including their name and email address.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter Your Name
Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.

### Step 2: Enter Your Email Address
Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Name** (`candidate_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Candidate Phone** (`candidate_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your name and email address accurately, as these will be used to identify you in the talent pool.
* If you have a phone number, please enter it to ensure we can reach you more efficiently.
* If you encounter any issues with the form, feel free to ask for help.
