# Rider Host Home Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Rider Host Home Application" form is designed to collect information from riders and their hosts to facilitate a smooth and secure experience for both parties. The form is used to gather details about the rider's visit, including household information, visiting dates and times, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "Household Details" section with your household's address and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your visiting rider's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
3. Enter the visit date and time in the "Visit Date" and "Visit Time" fields.
4. Specify the duration of the visit in the "Visit Duration" field.
5. Select your relationship with the rider from the "Rider Host Relationship" options.
6. List the household rules in the "Household Rules" field.
7. Provide emergency contact information, including phone number and email (if applicable).
8. Agree to the terms of the "Rider Host Agreement" by selecting the relevant options.
9. Finally, confirm the review date for the visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Household Details**: Enter your household's address and any other relevant information to help the rider understand your home's details.
* **Visiting Rider Details**: Provide the rider's name and any other relevant information to ensure a smooth interaction.
* **Visit Date**: Enter the date of the visit using the standard date format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Visit Time**: Enter the time of the visit in 12-hour format (HH:MM AM/PM).
* **Visit Duration**: Specify the duration of the visit to help the rider plan their stay.
* **Household Information**: Provide any additional information about your household that the rider might find helpful.
* **Rider Host Relationship**: Select your relationship with the rider from the available options to help the rider understand your connection.
* **Household Rules**: List any rules or guidelines that the rider should follow during their visit.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide emergency contact information, including phone number and email (if applicable).
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the email of the emergency contact (if applicable).
* **Rider Host Agreement**: Agree to the terms of the rider-host relationship by selecting the relevant options.
* **Review Date**: Confirm the review date for the visit to ensure both parties are on the same page.

## Tips
* Double-check your information for accuracy to avoid any issues during the visit.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions or approvals from your household members before completing the form.
