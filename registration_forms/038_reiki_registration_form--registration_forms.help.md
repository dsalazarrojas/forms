The Reiki Registration Form is designed to collect personal and payment information from individuals interested in attending a Reiki workshop or seeking Reiki healing sessions. This form ensures that all necessary details are gathered to facilitate a smooth and safe experience for the attendee. The form is divided into several sections, each covering a specific aspect of the registration process. To complete this form, please follow the instructions below. 

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register individuals for a Reiki workshop or healing session, collecting vital information such as name, contact details, and payment preferences. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the respective fields.
2. Select your Reiki level, which is crucial for determining the suitability of the workshop or session.
3. Choose the workshop date and time that suits you best.
4. Indicate your availability for the healing session, if applicable.
5. Select your preferred payment method and status.
6. Enter your payment amount, if applicable.
7. If necessary, provide additional comments or special requirements for your participation.
8. Provide emergency contact information.
9. List any medical information or allergies you may have.
10. Indicate any dietary restrictions.
11. List any relevant medical certifications.
12. Add any comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (1): Enter your first name to complete your registration.
* **Last name** (2): Enter your last name to complete your registration.
* **Email** (3): Enter your valid email address for correspondence and notifications.
* **Phone** (4): Optional. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Reiki Level** (5): Select your current Reiki level to ensure you're enrolled in the right workshop or session.
* **Workshop Date** (6): Choose the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (7): Select the time slot for the workshop that suits you.
* **Healing Session Date** (8): Optional. Enter the date of the healing session you're interested in.
* **Healing Session Time** (9): Optional. Select the time slot for the healing session that suits you.
* **Workshop Attendance** (10): Indicate whether you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Payment Method** (11): Choose how you plan to pay for the workshop or healing session.
* **Payment Status** (12): Indicate your current payment status.
* **Payment Date** (13): Optional. Enter the date of your payment.
* **Payment Amount** (14): Enter the amount of your payment.
* **Additional Comments** (15): Add any comments or notes about your registration or requirements.
* **Special Requirements** (16): Enter any special requirements or requests for your participation.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (17): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (18): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (19): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Information** (20): Add any relevant medical information or concerns.
* **Allergies** (21): Indicate if you have any allergies.
* **Medications** (22): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (23): Select any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Medical Certifications** (24): Indicate if you hold any relevant medical certifications.
* **Comments** (25): Add any comments or notes about your registration or participation. 

Note: All fields marked as "optional" are not mandatory, but they are crucial for ensuring a safe and comfortable experience for you during the workshop or session. Please fill in all required fields to complete your registration.
