# gaming_platform_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The gaming_platform_subscription_form is a registration form used to collect information from users who wish to subscribe to the gaming platform. This form is designed to capture relevant details such as contact information, payment methods, subscription preferences, and billing information to process the user's subscription request.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Email** fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Provide your **Phone** number if you wish to receive updates or notifications.
3. In the **User Info** field, provide any additional information you feel is relevant to your subscription.
4. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** from the dropdown menu. Currently, you can select from "Card", "Bank Transfer", or "PayPal".
5. Enter your **Card Details** if you have chosen "Card" as your payment method.
6. Provide your **Expiration Date** for your subscription.
7. Fill in your **Billing Address** if you wish to receive invoices or billing statements.
8. Select the type of **Subscription Type** you are interested in from the dropdown menu. Currently, you can select from "Gold", "Silver", or "Bronze".
9. Choose your **Subscription Term** in the time-based format (e.g., week, month, year).
10. Select your **Payment Schedule** frequency from the dropdown menu. Currently, you can select from "Weekly", "Biweekly", or "Monthly".
11. Enter your **Subscription Price** in the required format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Your up-to-date email address.
* **Phone**: Your phone number for updates or notifications (optional).
* **User Info**: Any additional information you feel is relevant to your subscription.
* **Payment Method**: Your preferred payment method (Card, Bank Transfer, PayPal).
* **Card Details**: Enter your card number, expiration date, and security code if you have chosen "Card" as your payment method.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter your subscription expiration date in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
* **Billing Address**: Your billing address for invoices or billing statements (if you wish to receive them).
* **Subscription Type**: Select the type of subscription you are interested in from "Gold", "Silver", or "Bronze".
* **Subscription Term**: Enter your subscription period in the time-based format (e.g., week, month, year).
* **Payment Schedule**: Select how often you want to be billed from the dropdown menu (Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly).
* **Subscription Price**: Enter your subscription price in the required format.

## Tips

* Be sure to review all required fields before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or questions with the form, contact our support team for assistance.
* Please note that our support team is available to help with any concerns or questions you may have regarding your subscription.
