# Cross Border Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross Border Registration Form is used to collect information from individuals who need to cross borders for various reasons.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Please review the form carefully and answer all questions to the best of your abilities.
2.  Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
3.  Ensure you have all necessary documents, such as your passport, ID, and any supporting documents required by the authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
**First Name** (first_name, text, required: false)
Your first name as it appears on your passport or ID document.

### Last Name
**Last Name** (last_name, text, required: false)
Your last name as it appears on your passport or ID document.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required: false)
The date you were born, in the format DD/MM/YYYY.

### Address
**Address** (address, text, required: false)
Your current address, as it appears on your passport or ID document.

### Country of Origin
**Country of Origin** (country_of_origin, text, required: false)
The country you are originally from.

### Visa Type
**Visa Type** (visa_type, select_one, required: false)
Do you have a visa? (Yes/No)

### Passport Number
**Passport Number** (passport_number, text, required: false)
Your passport number, as it appears on your passport.

## Tips
- Please double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
- If you are unsure about any question, please consult the authorities or seek guidance from the relevant authorities.
- Remember that providing false or incomplete information can result in serious consequences.
