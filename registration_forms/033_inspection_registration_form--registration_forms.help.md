# Inspection Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering clients' information for inspection purposes. It is essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that the client's details are correctly recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide the client's address in the "Client Address" field.
3. Enter the client's city in the "Client City" field.
4. Enter the client's state/province in the "Client State" field.
5. Enter the client's zip/postal code in the "Client Zip" field.
6. Enter the client's country in the "Client Country" field.
7. Enter the client's phone number in the "Client Phone Number" field.
8. Enter the client's email address in the "Client Email" field.
9. If relevant, select the price range from the "Price Range" field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Additional Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Name** (`client_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's full name as it appears on their identification.
* **Client Address** (`client_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's full address, including street number and name, apartment or suite number, and any other relevant details.
* **Client City** (`client_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's city or municipality.
* **Client State** (`client_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's state or province (e.g., California, Ontario).
* **Client Zip** (`client_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's zip or postal code.
* **Client Country** (`client_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's country of residence.
* **Client Phone Number** (`client_phone_number`, text, required/optional): Enter the client's phone number, including area code and exchange.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the client's email address in the format of "example@example.com".
* **Price Range** (`price_range`, number, required/optional): Select the price range for the inspection services, if applicable.
* **Additional Notes** (`note`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes regarding the client's inspection.
