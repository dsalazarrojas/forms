# Team Bonding Dinner RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from team members about their attendance and preferences for a team bonding dinner event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Choose the date you plan to attend in the "Attending Date" field.
4. Select the time you plan to attend in the "Attending Time" field.
5. Indicate whether you are attending the dinner in the "RSVP" field by choosing "True", "False" or "Maybe".
6. If you have any specific preferences or requests for the dinner, you can type them in the "Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`Name`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name to identify yourself as a team member.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Attending Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose the date you plan to attend the team bonding dinner event.
* **Attending Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Select the time you plan to attend the team bonding dinner event.
* **RSVP** (`select_one`, required/optional): Indicate whether you are attending by choosing "True", "False" or "Maybe".
* **Preferences** (`text`, required/optional): Share any specific preferences or requests you have for the dinner event.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Name, Email, Attending Date, and Attending Time) to ensure accurate tracking of your attendance and preferences. 
* If you have any special requests or preferences, please make sure to include them in the "Preferences" field. 
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
