# Online Marketplace Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help potential sellers enroll in an online marketplace, providing the necessary information to be listed on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure it meets your needs.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and truthful information about yourself and your product.
3. Choose the marketplace where you want to be listed.
4. Upload any necessary product photos or information.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seller Information**: Please enter a brief description of yourself as a seller, including your name and any relevant business or seller information.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Marketplace Selection**: Select the online marketplace where you want to be listed (e.g. Amazon, eBay, Etsy, or Walmart).
* **Payment Details**: Enter any payment information related to your product or service.
* **Shipping Address**: Enter your shipping address, including your street address, city, state, or province, and zip or postal code.
* **Product Information**: Enter a brief description of your product or service.
* **Product Pricing**: Enter the price of your product or service.
* **Product Photos**: Upload any necessary product photos or information.
* **Terms And Conditions**: Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
* **Review Form**: Fill out the review form, which is not currently enabled for your account.

## Tips

* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and truthfully.
* Use a clear and concise format for your product or service description.
* Upload any necessary product photos that accurately represent your product or service.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact form@gic.mx.
