# shipping_address_verification_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The shipping_address_verification_form is used to verify the shipping address of a customer, ensuring that it is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the shipping address form, including the street address, city, state, and zip code.
2. Indicate whether the shipping address is verified or not.
3. Enter any additional shipping information, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **shipping_address**: This field is for entering the customer's shipping street address. It's a plain text input field.
* **verify_shipping_address**: Please select 'Yes' if the address has been verified, or 'No' if you need to verify it.
* **shipping_info**: This field is for entering any additional shipping information, such as instructions or special requests.

## Tips
* Make sure to get the customer's permission before collecting and storing their shipping address information.
* This form should only be filled out once the customer has placed an order or is creating a new account.
* Ensure that the shipping address is accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with delivery or order fulfillment.
