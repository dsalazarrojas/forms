# Dossier Information Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register or update information about a dossier. It is used by users to provide basic contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your name and contact information.
2. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure correct dossier creation.
* If you are unsure if the information already exists, check the system's search function.
* If you are unable to complete the form, please contact the system administrator for assistance.
