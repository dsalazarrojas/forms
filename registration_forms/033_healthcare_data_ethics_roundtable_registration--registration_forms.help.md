# Healthcare Data Ethics Roundtable Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Healthcare Data Ethics Roundtable aims to provide a platform for discussing and addressing ethics in healthcare data management. Registration for this event is for participants interested in exploring this topic.

## How to Complete This Form
To register for the Healthcare Data Ethics Roundtable, please follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (1)** (`event_details`, text, Optional): This section is for you to briefly describe the event you would like to focus on for discussion. Please provide any relevant information that will help the roundtable participants understand your topic of interest.
* **Attendee Details (2)** (`attendee_details`, text, Optional): In this section, please provide any additional details you think are relevant to your participation in the roundtable, such as your name, affiliation, and role in the field.
* **Accessibility Needs (3)** (`accessibility_needs`, text, Optional): If you have any accessibility needs (e.g., disability, language support, etc.), please mention them here so we can provide necessary accommodations.
* **Topic Interests (4)** (`topic_interests`, select multiple, Optional): Please select all topics you are interested in discussing at the roundtable. You can choose from the following options: 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us tailor the discussions to your interests.
* **Event Interests (5)** (`event_interests`, select multiple, Optional): Similarly, please select all events you are interested in participating in, using the options: 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Dietary Needs (6)** (`dietary_needs`, select multiple, Optional): If you have specific dietary needs (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.), please select them here.
* **Contact Details (7)** (`contact_details`, text, Optional): If you wish to be contacted for any reason, please provide your contact information here.
* **Submit (8)** (`submit`, text, Optional): Please review your registration and submit it for processing.

## Tips
Please ensure you fill out as much information as you can, as this will help us tailor the roundtable discussions to your interests. Also, double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
