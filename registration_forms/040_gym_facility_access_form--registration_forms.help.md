# Gym Facility Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gym Facility Access Form is designed to collect information from gym facility users (members and guests) to grant them access to the gym facility. This form helps the gym management to track and manage visitor records, ensuring that users have the necessary permissions and access levels to enter the facility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the member information section (fields 1-4) with your details.
	* Fill in your name, email, and phone number to confirm your membership status.
2. For guests, fill out their information in the guest section (fields 5-7).
	* Provide guest's name, email, and phone number for reference.
3. Select the access level (field 10) that applies to you or your guest:
	* Choose from Administrator, Manager, Member, or Guest based on your role.
4. Select the category (field 13) that best describes the purpose of your access:
	* Choose from Registration Forms, Membership Forms, or Access Forms.
5. Optional fields:
	* Add any additional notes (fields 8 and 9) about your visit or guest.
	* Choose a specific tool or equipment to be assigned (field 14) for your access.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Information**: This section captures the essential details of the member (you).
	* Member Name (1): Enter your name as it is recorded in the gym's system.
	* Member Email (3): Enter your email address used for gym communication.
	* Member Phone (4): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
	* Member Date (11): Enter the date of your membership start or last visit.
	* Member Time (12): Enter the time of your last visit (if applicable).
* **Guest Information**: This section is for guests visiting with you.
	+ Guest Name (5): Enter the guest's name for reference.
	+ Guest Email (6): Enter the guest's email address.
	+ Guest Phone (7): Enter the guest's phone number.
* **Access Level**: Select the level of access you or your guest has.
	+ Administrator: High-level access, typically for gym staff or management.
	+ Manager: Intermediate access, granted for authorized personnel.
	+ Member: Standard access for members.
	+ Guest: Basic access for visitors.
* **Category**: Choose the category that best describes the purpose of your access.
	+ Registration Forms: For new member registrations.
	+ Membership Forms: For membership-related access.
	+ Access Forms: For accessing the facility or services.
* **Notes**: Provide additional comments or notes about your visit or guest.
	+ Member Notes (8): Any additional information about your membership status.
	+ Guest Notes (9): Notes about the guest's visit or any special requests.
* **Assigned Tool**: Optionally select a tool or equipment to be assigned for your access.
* **Output File**: Optionally specify a file to be generated for your access record.
* **Form IDs**: This field is not applicable in this version of the form.
