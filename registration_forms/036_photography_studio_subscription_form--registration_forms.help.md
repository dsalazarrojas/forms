# Photography Studio Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Studio Subscription Form is designed to collect information from customers interested in subscribing to the studio's services. Please fill out this form to register your subscription and receive exclusive benefits.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Client Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Client Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your desired subscription plan in the "Subscription Plan" field.
5. Select your start date for the subscription in the "Start Date" field.
6. Choose your end date for the subscription in the "End Date" field.
7. Enter the price for your subscription.
8. Select your subscription status from the available options.
9. Choose your payment method from the available options.
10. Select your payment status.
9. Optionally, add any additional notes about your subscription.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you want it to be displayed on your subscription details.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive notifications and communications from the studio.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, select one, optional): Choose your desired subscription plan (Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want your subscription to begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the date you want your subscription to end.
* **Price** (`price`, number, optional): Enter the price for your subscription.
* **Subscription Status** (`subscription_status`, select multiple, optional): Select your current subscription status (Active, Suspended, Expired).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select one, optional): Choose your payment method (Stripe, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select one, optional): Select your payment status (Paid, Pending, Unpaid).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your subscription.
