# Brand Collaboration Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for guests to RSVP to a brand collaboration mixer event. The event aims to foster connections between brands and professionals in the industry.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form, and fill in the required information for each field.
2. Please ensure you provide your accurate and up-to-date details to facilitate smooth communication and event planning.
3. Review each section carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for event updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Provide your contact information.
* **Company or Brand Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Describe your current position.
* **Professional Role** (`professional_role`, `select_one`, required): Choose your role type:
	+ Brand representative
	+ Influencer
	+ Content creator
	+ Marketing professional
	+ Business owner
	+ Investor
	+ Media
	+ Other
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you will attend the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time you will arrive at the event.
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, `select_one`, required): Choose your RSVP status:
	+ Yes, I will attend
	+ Yes, with guest
	+ Maybe
	+ No, cannot attend
	+ Undecided
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of guests attending (including yourself).
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): Describe any dietary restrictions or requirements.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Mention any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Collaboration Interests** (`collaboration_interests`, `text`, optional): Type of collaborations you're interested in.
* **I Confirm My RSVP** (`rsvp_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Check to confirm your RSVP.

Please note that this form is designed to be completed by guests who have been invited to the brand collaboration mixer event. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please reach out to the event organizers for assistance.
