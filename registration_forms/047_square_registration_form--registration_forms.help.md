# Square Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Square Registration Form is used to collect general information from users for registration purposes. This form is suitable for a wide range of applications, including events, conferences, or customer databases.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your User First Name.
2. Fill in your User Last Name.
3. (Optional) Provide your User Email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, `text`, required/optional: no): Your first name.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, `text`, required/optional: no): Your last name.
* **User Email** (`user_email`, `email`, required/optional: no): Your email address (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that the email address provided is valid and correct.
* This form can be used in any context where you need to register users with general information.
* You can modify this form to fit your specific needs by adding or removing fields.
