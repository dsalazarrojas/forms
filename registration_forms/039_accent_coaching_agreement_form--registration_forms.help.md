# Accent Coaching Agreement Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to establish a coaching agreement between you and your accent coach, outlining expectations and responsibilities for the coaching process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the respective fields.
2. Provide your phone number for communication purposes.
3. Enter your mailing address (optional).
4. Select your primary goals for accent coaching (you can choose more than one option).
5. Specify your native language or first language.
6. Choose the target accent or dialect you would like to achieve.
7. Select your preferred session frequency (e.g., once per week, twice per week, etc.).
8. Choose your preferred session duration (e.g., 30 minutes, 45 minutes, etc.).
9. Select your preferred session format (e.g., in-person, online, hybrid).
10. Enter the desired start date for your coaching sessions.
11. Select your payment arrangement (e.g., per session, monthly package, etc.).
12. Confirm that you understand the cancellation policy and agree to its terms.
13. Consent to having sessions recorded for review and progress tracking purposes.
14. Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this coaching agreement.
15. Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Accent Coaching Agreement Form --** (`form_header`, `note`, not required): This is the title of the form, which explains that this agreement establishes expectations and responsibilities between you and your accent coach.
* **Client full name** (`client_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email address** (`client_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (`client_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Mailing address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, not required): If you have a mailing address, enter it here (optional).
* **Accent coach name** (`coach_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your accent coach.
* **What are your primary goals for accent coaching** (`coaching_goals`, `select_multiple`, required): Select one or more of the options to specify your primary goals for accent coaching:
	+ Reduce accent for professional communication
	+ Improve pronunciation clarity
	+ Prepare for auditions or acting roles
	+ Enhance public speaking skills
	+ Learn a specific regional accent
	+ General communication improvement
* **Native language or first language** (`native_language`, `text`, required): Specify your native language or first language.
* **Target accent or dialect** (`target_accent`, `text`, required): Choose the target accent or dialect you would like to achieve (e.g., General American, Received Pronunciation, Neutral English).
* **Preferred session frequency** (`session_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred session frequency:
	+ Once per week
	+ Twice per week
	+ Three times per week
	+ Bi-weekly
	+ As needed
* **Preferred session duration** (`session_duration`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session duration:
	+ 30 minutes
	+ 45 minutes
	+ 60 minutes
	+ 90 minutes
* **Preferred session format** (`session_format`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred session format:
	+ In-person
	+ Online (video call)
	+ Hybrid
* **Desired start date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the desired start date for your coaching sessions.
* **Payment arrangement** (`payment_terms`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment arrangement:
	+ Per session
	+ Monthly package
	+ Full program upfront
* **I understand the cancellation policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you understand and agree to the cancellation policy.
* **I consent to sessions being recorded** (`recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): Consent to having sessions recorded for review and progress tracking purposes.
* **I agree to the terms and conditions** (`agreement_acknowledgment`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions outlined in this coaching agreement.
* **Date of agreement** (`agreement_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate information for communication purposes.
* Choose your preferred session options carefully, as they may affect the coaching process and your progress.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask your accent coach.
