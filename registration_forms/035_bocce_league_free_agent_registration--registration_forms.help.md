# Bocce League Free Agent Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bocce League Free Agent Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals interested in registering as free agents for the Bocce League. This information will be used to create a player profile and help facilitate the league's operations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read the form instructions and fields.
2. Complete all required fields marked with `**` in bold.
3. Select relevant information from the dropdown menus.
4. Ensure that the email address and phone number provided are accurate and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full last name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your contact email address. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, `text`, required): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state where you reside.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Bocce Experience Level** (`bocce_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your experience level with Bocce: 
  * **Beginner - Never Played**: You have no experience with Bocce.
  * **Beginner - Played Casually**: You have played Bocce occasionally.
  * **Intermediate - Some League Experience**: You have played in a Bocce league once or twice.
  * **Advanced - Experienced Player**: You have played in several Bocce leagues.
  * **Expert - Competitive Player**: You are an experienced competitive Bocce player.
* **Years Playing Bocce** (`years_playing`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you have played Bocce:
  * **Less than 1 year**: You have been playing for less than 1 year.
  * **1-2 years**: You have been playing for 1-2 years.
  * **3-5 years**: You have been playing for 3-5 years.
  * **6-10 years**: You have been playing for 6-10 years.
  * **Over 10 years**: You have been playing for more than 10 years.
* **Preferred Position** (`preferred_position`, `select_one`, optional): Select your preferred position:
  * **No Preference**: No preference.
  * **Lead Off**: You prefer to play as the lead-off player.
  * **Middle**: You prefer to play in the middle.
  * **Anchor**: You prefer to play as the anchor.
  * **Captain**: You prefer to play as the captain.
* **Game Day Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the days you are available to play:
  * **Monday Evening**: You are available to play on Monday evenings.
  * **Tuesday Evening**: You are available to play on Tuesday evenings.
  * **Wednesday Evening**: You are available to play on Wednesday evenings.
  * **Thursday Evening**: You are available to play on Thursday evenings.
  * **Friday Evening**: You are available to play on Friday evenings.
  * **Saturday Morning**: You are available to play on Saturday mornings.
  * **Saturday Afternoon**: You are available to play on Saturday afternoons.
  * **Sunday Morning**: You are available to play on Sunday mornings.
  * **Sunday Afternoon**: You are available to play on Sunday afternoons.
* **Team Preference** (`team_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your team preference:
  * **No Preference**: You have no team preference.
  * **Competitive Team**: You prefer a competitive team.
  * **Recreational Team**: You prefer a recreations team.
  * **Social Team**: You prefer a social team.
  * **Mixed Skill Level**: You prefer a team with mixed skill levels.
* **Want to Be Teammates With Someone?** (`want_to_be_teammates`, `text`, optional): If you want to be teammates with someone, list their names.
* **Previous League Participation** (`previous_league_participation`, `select_one`, required): Select your previous league experience:
  * **Yes, this league**: You have played in this league before.
  * **Yes, different league**: You have played in a different Bocce league.
  * **No, first time**: You have never played in a Bocce league.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your T-shirt size:
  * **XS**: Extra Small.
  * **S**: Small.
  * **M**: Medium.
  * **L**: Large.
  * **XL**: Extra Large.
  * **2XL**: 2X Large.
  * **3XL**: 3X Large.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, `select_one`, required): Select your registration fee:
  * **$50 - Standard**: Standard registration fee.
  * **$40 - Early Bird**: Early registration fee.
  * **$60 - Late Registration**: Late registration fee.
  * **Waived - Volunteer**: Registration fee is waived for volunteers.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method:
  * **Credit Card**: Credit card payment.
  * **Debit Card**: Debit card payment.
  * **PayPal**: PayPal payment.
  * **Venmo**: Venmo payment.
  * **Cash at Orientation**: Cash payment at orientation.
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Select your interest in volunteering:
  * **Yes, interested**: Interested in volunteering.
  * **Maybe**: Maybe interested in volunteering.
  * **No, just playing**: No interest in volunteering.
* **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_did_you_hear`, `select_one`, optional): Select how you heard about the Bocce League:
  * **Social Media**: Social media.
  * **Friend**: A friend.
  * **Website**: The Bocce League website.
  * **Flyer**: A flyer.
  * **Previous Player**: A previous player.
  * **Other**: Other (please specify).
* **Liability Waiver** (`waiver_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select your agreement to the liability waiver:
  * **Yes, I Accept**: Agree to the waiver terms.
  * **No, I Do Not Accept**: Do not agree to the waiver terms.
* **Code of Conduct** (`code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Select your agreement to the code of conduct:
  * **Yes, I Agree**: Agree to follow the league rules.
  * **False**: Do not agree to follow the league rules.
* **Player Signature** (`player_signature`, `text`, required): Type your full name as a signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): Select the date of registration.

## Tips
- Review the form carefully to ensure that all required fields are completed.
- Double-check the accuracy of your contact information.
- Select your preferred options for game day availability, team preference, and other fields based on your true availability and preferences.
- Review the liability waiver and code of conduct carefully before accepting them.
