# Industrial Resilience Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Industrial Resilience Council Registration Form is a form used to collect information from individuals interested in joining the council. This form is designed to gather relevant details to determine whether someone is a good fit for the council and to ensure that we have the most up-to-date information about our members.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each page.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, text, optional): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number that you would like to be contacted on.
* **Company/Organization** (`company_organization`, text, optional): If you are registering as a representative of a company or organization, enter the name of the company/organization.

### Page 2
* **Do you have experience in Industrial Resilience?** (`experience`, select_one, optional): Answer with either 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us understand your involvement with industrial resilience-related projects or initiatives.
* 

### Page 3
* **Additional Comments/Notes** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or notes, please add them here.

## Tips
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please reach out to us for clarification.
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* This form is for individual registrations only. If you are registering on behalf of a company or organization, please use the corresponding form.
