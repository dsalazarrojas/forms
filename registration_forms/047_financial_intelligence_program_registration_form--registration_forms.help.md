# Financial Intelligence Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Intelligence Program Registration Form is a form used to register participants for the Financial Intelligence Program. This form collects information from the participant to facilitate the program's administration and communication.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in the participant's email address in the "Participant Email" field.
2. Select the desired modules for the participant from the "Module Selection" dropdown menu.
3. If required, fill in the participant's name and other personal details.
4. Check the "Consent" field to confirm that the participant has given their consent to participate in the program.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address. This is the primary contact method for the program's communication and notifications.
* **Module Selection** (`module_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the modules that the participant wants to enroll in. Select multiple modules by holding down the `Ctrl` key while clicking on the options.
* **Consent** (`consent`, optional): Confirm that the participant has given their consent to participate in the program. This is a mandatory field to ensure that the participant is aware of the program's terms and conditions.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, optional): Enter the participant's full name. This field is optional but recommended for participants who want to be identified by their name in the program's communication.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, optional): Enter the participant's phone number. This field is optional and not required for the program's communication.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, optional): Enter the participant's physical address. This field is optional and not required for the program's communication.
* **Participant Note** (`participant_note`, optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant. This field is optional and not required for the program's administration.
Note: Fields marked as optional are not required to be filled in, but they are still recommended for a complete participant profile.
