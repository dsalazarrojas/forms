# Fundraiser Information Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about a fundraising event, enabling us to better understand and support the cause. Please provide accurate and detailed information to ensure we can effectively promote and manage the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter the name of the event in the **Event Name** field.
2. Select the date of the event in the **Event Date** field.
3. Enter the start and end time of the event in the **Event Time** field.
4. Provide the location of the event in the **Event Location** field.
5. Enter the name of the person responsible for the event in the **Contact Person** field.
6. Enter the email address of the contact person in the **Contact Email** field.
7. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the **Contact Phone** field.
8. Provide a brief description of the event in the **Event Description** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the event, e.g., "Annual Charity Run".
* **Event Date** (date, required): Select the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Time** (time, required): Enter the start and end time of the event in 24-hour format, e.g., 09:00-17:00.
* **Event Location** (text, optional): Provide the location of the event, e.g., "Local Park".
* **Contact Person** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person responsible for the event.
* **Contact Email** (email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Event Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of the event, e.g., "A charity run to support a local food bank".
