# Summer League Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by teams to register themselves in the Summer League. It collects essential team information to help league administrators manage teams effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Team Name field with your team's name.
2. Enter your team's email address in the Team Email field.
3. Provide your team's contact person's name, phone number, and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name**: Enter your team's name, e.g., "The Red Sox".
* **Team Email**: Enter your team's email address, e.g., "redsox@example.com".
*   **Team Contact Name**: Enter the name of your team's contact person, e.g., "John Doe".
*   **Team Phone**: Enter your team's contact phone number, e.g., "123-456-7890".
*   **Team Location**: Enter your team's location or address, e.g., "Boston, MA".
*   **Team Description**: Briefly describe your team, e.g., "Our team consists of 10 experienced players."
*   **Team League Name**: Select your team's sport, e.g., "Baseball".
*   **Team League Type**: Choose your team's skill level, e.g., "Recreational".
*   **Team League Division**: Choose your team's age/sex division, e.g., "Adult".
*   **Team Manager Name**: Enter your team manager's name, e.g., "Jane Doe".
*   **Team Manager Email**: Enter your team manager's email address, e.g., "janedoe@example.com".
*   **Team Manager Phone**: Enter your team manager's phone number, e.g., "987-654-3210".

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your team information is up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the league administrators.
* This form should be filled out only once per team to avoid duplication.
