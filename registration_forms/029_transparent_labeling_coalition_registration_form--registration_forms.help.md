# Transparent Labeling Coalition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for organizations to register with the Transparent Labeling Coalition. It is a mandatory process for all organizations seeking to participate in the coalition's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the required information ready.
* Fill in the Organization Name field with your organization's name.
* Enter your Organization's Address in the Address field.
* Provide the name of the Contact Person in the Contact Person field.
* Enter your organization's email in the Email field. Make sure it is a valid email address.
* Enter your organization's phone number in the Phone field.
* Select the Category of your organization from the dropdown list.
* Enter a brief Description of your organization, if desired.
* Enter the URL of your organization's website, if applicable.
* Enter any additional Notes about your organization, if desired.
* Enter the Event Details, if the form is for an event registration.
* Enter the Date of the event, if applicable.
* Enter the Time of the event, if applicable.
* Enter the Location of the event, if applicable.
* Select the tool assigned to your organization in the Assigned Tool field.
* Enter the Registration Date of your organization.
* Enter the Registration Time of your organization.
* Enter the name of the tool used for submission in the Submitted By Tool field.
* Enter the date and time of submission in the Submitted On and Submitted Time fields.
* Enter any additional Notes about the submission process in the Notes Tool field.
* Finally, confirm that the submission was made by the user in the Submitted By User field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter the address of your organization.
* **Contact Person** (Contact Person, text, required): Provide the name of the contact person for your organization.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter a valid email address for your organization.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Category** (Category, select_multiple, optional): Select the category of your organization from the list.
* **Description** (Description, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your organization, if desired.
* **Website** (Website, text, optional): Enter the URL of your organization's website, if applicable.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your organization.
* **Event Details** (Event Details, text, optional): Enter event details, if applicable.
* **Date** (Date, date, optional): Enter the date of the event, if applicable.
* **Time** (Time, time, optional): Enter the time of the event, if applicable.
* **Location** (Location, text, optional): Enter the location of the event, if applicable.
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, optional): Select the tool assigned to your organization.
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, required): Enter the date of registration.
* **Registration Time** (Registration Time, time, optional): Enter the time of registration.
* **Submitted By** (Submitted By, text, required): Enter the name of the tool used for submission.
* **Submitted On** (Submitted On, date, optional): Enter the date of submission.
* **Submitted Time** (Submitted Time, time, optional): Enter the time of submission.
* **Notes Tool** (Notes Tool, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the submission process.
* **Submitted By User** (Submitted By User, text, optional): Confirm that the submission was made by the user.
