# new_model_onboarding_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to onboard new models to the system, collecting basic contact information and other details to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Model Onboarding** (`new_model_onboarding`, `text`, optional): This is the header for the form section.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name to help us identify you in the system.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive important updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is your phone number, which we will use to contact you if needed. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure smooth onboarding process. 
* If you have any issues or questions during the onboarding process, feel free to reach out to us.
