# Cleanup Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from potential volunteers for a cleanup event. This information will help us ensure a safe and successful volunteer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the personal information section with your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide your emergency contact information, including their name, phone number, and relationship to you.
3. Select your preferred cleanup event and date.
4. Indicate your availability and preferred frequency of volunteering.
5. If you have previous volunteer experience, please describe your skills and experience.
6. If you are interested in leading a team, please indicate your level of interest.
7. Describe any physical limitations or restrictions you may have.
8. Indicate your ability to lift up to 20 pounds.
9. Choose your comfort level working outdoors.
10. If you have work gloves, please indicate whether you have them or need them to be provided.
11. If you need transportation assistance, please indicate your transportation needs.
12. If you are volunteering as part of a group, provide the group name and size.
13. If you have any medical conditions or dietary restrictions, please describe them.
14. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have.
15. Finally, review and accept the liability waiver, photo release, background check consent, and terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth. Please note that you must be at least 16 years old to volunteer.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, required): Enter your home address.
* **City** (`city`, required): Enter the city or town where you live.
* **State or Province** (`state`, required): Enter the state or province where you live.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, required): Enter your postal code.
* **Country** (`country`, required): Enter the country where you live.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact.
* **Which Cleanup Event Are You Interested In** (`cleanup_event`, required): Select your preferred cleanup event.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, optional): Select your preferred date for the cleanup event.
* **Your Availability** (`availability`, required): Select your availability for the cleanup event.
* **How Often Would You Like to Volunteer** (`volunteer_frequency`, required): Indicate your preferred frequency of volunteering.
* **Do You Have Previous Volunteer Experience** (`previous_volunteer_experience`, required): Indicate whether you have previous volunteer experience.
* **Relevant Skills or Experience** (`relevant_skills`, optional): Describe any skills or experience that may be helpful for the cleanup event.
* **Are You Interested in a Leadership Role** (`leadership_interest`, required): Indicate your interest in leading a team.
* **Do You Have Any Physical Limitations** (`physical_limitations`, optional): Describe any physical limitations or restrictions you may have.
* **Are You Able to Lift Up to 20 Pounds** (`able_to_lift`, required): Indicate your ability to lift up to 20 pounds.
* **Are You Comfortable Working Outdoors** (`comfortable_outdoors`, required): Indicate your comfort level working outdoors.
* **Do You Have Work Gloves** (`have_work_gloves`, required): Indicate whether you have work gloves or need them to be provided.
* **Glove Size (If Need Provided)** (`glove_size`, optional): Select the size of the gloves you need.
* **Do You Have Reliable Transportation** (`have_transportation`, required): Indicate whether you have reliable transportation.
* **Are You Willing to Carpool** (`willing_to_carpool`, optional): Indicate whether you are willing to carpool.
* **Are You Volunteering As** (`volunteering_as`, required): Select whether you are volunteering individually or as part of a group.
* **Group Name (If Applicable)** (`group_name`, optional): Enter the name of your group.
* **Approximate Group Size (If Applicable)** (`group_size`, optional): Enter the approximate size of your group.
* **Group Leader Name (If Applicable)** (`group_leader_name`, optional): Enter the name of your group leader.
* **Why Do You Want to Volunteer** (`why_volunteer`, required): Describe your motivation for volunteering.
* **Environmental Issues You Care About** (`environmental_interests`, required): Select all the environmental issues you care about.
* **How Did You Hear About This Opportunity** (`how_did_you_hear`, optional): Select how you heard about this opportunity.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, optional): Select the size of the T-shirt you prefer.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, optional): Describe any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Medical Conditions We Should Know About** (`medical_conditions`, optional): Describe any medical conditions you have.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **Liability Waiver Acceptance** (`liability_waiver`, required): Accept the liability waiver.
* **Photo Release** (`photo_release`, required): Indicate whether you consent to a photo release for promotional purposes.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, optional): Indicate whether you consent to a background check.
* **Terms and Conditions Acceptance** (`terms_acceptance`, required): Accept the terms and conditions.
* **Volunteer Signature** (`volunteer_signature`, required): Enter your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, required): Enter the date of your signature.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature (If Under 18)** (`guardian_signature`, optional): Enter the electronic signature of your parent or guardian.

## Tips

* Please review and complete each section carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please contact us for assistance.
* You can always contact us if you need help with any section of the form.
