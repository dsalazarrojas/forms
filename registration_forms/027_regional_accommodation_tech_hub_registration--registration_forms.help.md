# Regional Accommodation Tech Hub Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in attending the Regional Accommodation Tech Hub event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps below:
1. Provide your user information.
2. Describe your accommodation needs.
3. Indicate your participation consent (you can select multiple options).
4. Provide event information (if applicable).
5. Enter attendee details (if applicable).
6. Review and submit the form accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`1`, `text`, `no`): This section is for you to provide your basic contact information, such as your name, email, and any other relevant details. Please ensure you enter accurate and complete information.
* **Accommodation Needs** (`2`, `text`, `no`): In this section, describe your accommodation needs for the event. Be as detailed as possible so that we can better understand your requirements.
* **Participation Consent** (`3`, `select_multiple`, `no`): Please select all the participation options that apply to you. This will help us understand your interests and preferences.
* **Event Information** (`4`, `text`, `no`): If you're planning to attend the event, provide any relevant event information, such as the date, time, or location.
* **Attendee Details** (`5`, `text`, `no`): If you're attending the event as part of a group or team, please provide more details about your attendee group, such as their names and affiliations.
* **Tips**: Double-check all fields before submitting the form to ensure accuracy. If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us for assistance.
