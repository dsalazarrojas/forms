# sales_strategies_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sales strategies training registration form collects information from participants interested in attending a sales strategies training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Training Session" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
4. Select your preferred payment method from the "payment_details" field.
5. Choose your payment method(s) from the "payment_method" field.
6. Select the date you would like to attend the training session in the "Date" field.
7. Select the time you would like to attend the training session in the "Time" field.
8. Enter the amount you would like to pay in the "Amount" field.
9. Enter your address in the "participant_address" field.
10. Enter your postcode in the "postcode" field.
11. Enter your country in the "country" field.
12. Enter your phone number in the "participant_phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, text, false): Enter the name of the training session you are interested in.
* **participant_name** (`participant_name`, text, false): Enter your name.
* **email** (`email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (`phone_number`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
* **payment_details** (`payment_details`, select_one, false): Select your preferred payment method.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, false): Choose your preferred payment method(s).
* **Date** (`Date`, date, false): Select the date you would like to attend the training session.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, false): Select the time you would like to attend the training session.
* **Amount** (`Amount`, number, false): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **participant_address** (`participant_address`, text, false): Enter your address.
* **postcode** (`postcode`, text, false): Enter your postcode.
* **country** (`country`, text, false): Enter your country.
* **participant_phone** (`participant_phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number.
