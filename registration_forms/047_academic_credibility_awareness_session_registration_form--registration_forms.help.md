## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email address**: Enter your institutional email address.
* **Institution or university name**: Enter the name of your institution or university.
* **Your role**: Select your role within the institution, which can be undergraduate student, graduate student, faculty member, researcher, librarian, or administrator.
* **Session Selection**: This section is optional but allows you to choose your preferred session date. You can select a date that suits you best from the provided options.
* **Preferred session format**: Select your preferred session format. You can choose in-person, virtual (live webinar), hybrid, or no preference.
* **Which topics interest you most**: Select all the topics that interest you most from the provided list. You can choose one or more topics.
* **Have you previously attended academic integrity training**: Answer yes if you have attended academic integrity training before. If no, select "False".
* **What motivated you to register for this session**: Select the reason that motivated you to register for this session. You can choose one or more reasons from the provided list.
* **Do you have any accessibility needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, please enter a brief description of your requirements.
* **Do you have any questions you would like to be addressed during the session**: If you have any questions, please enter them here.
