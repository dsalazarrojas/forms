# Charity Drive Referral Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from individuals who are referring potential donors to a charity drive. The goal is to gather essential details about the referrer and the referred individual, enabling the charity drive team to follow up with the referrer and make informed decisions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal information (full name and email address) in the "Your Information" section.
2. Enter the potential donor's name and email address in the "Referral Information" section.
3. Select the best way to contact them (e.g., email, phone call, or text message) and provide a brief message for them to read.
4. Indicate your relationship to the referred individual (e.g., friend, colleague, or family member).
5. Choose the primary interest of the potential donor from the provided options (e.g., making donations, corporate sponsorship, volunteering, or promoting the event).
6. Select how you would like to be contacted by the charity drive team regarding this referral.
7. Confirm if the referred individual knows they are being referred.
8. Enter the date of the referral.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Your Full Name**: This is your name that will be associated with the referral.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Yes
* **Your Email Address**: Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about this referral.
	+ Type: Email field
	+ Required: Yes
* **Potential Donor Name**: Enter the name of the person you are referring.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: Yes
* **Referral Email Address**: Enter the email address of the potential donor.
	+ Type: Email field
	+ Required: Yes
* **Referral Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the potential donor (optional).
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Your Relationship to Referral**: Describe your connection to the potential donor (e.g., friend, colleague, family).
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **What is their primary interest?**: Select the main reason the potential donor is interested in supporting the charity drive.
	+ Type: Select multiple options
	+ Required: Yes
* **Best way to contact them**: Select how you would like to be contacted by the charity drive team regarding this referral.
	+ Type: Select one option
	+ Required: Yes
* **Brief message for the referral**: Enter a message that we can use when contacting them about the referral.
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
* **Referral Permission**: Confirm if the potential donor is aware they are being referred.
	+ Type: Select one option
	+ Required: Yes
* **Date of Referral**: Enter the date you referred the potential donor.
	+ Type: Date field
	+ Required: Yes
* **Referral ID or Campaign Code**: Enter a unique ID or code for the referral (optional).
	+ Type: Text field
	+ Required: No
