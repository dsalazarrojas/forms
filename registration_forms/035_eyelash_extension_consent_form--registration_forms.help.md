# Eyelash Extension Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Eyelash Extension Consent Form is a document that patients must complete before undergoing an eyelash extension procedure. It is a crucial part of the pre-procedure process to ensure that patients understand the benefits, risks, and requirements of the treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Indicate whether your eyelash artist is "Yes" or "No" in the "Lash Artist" field.
3. If your eyelash artist is performing the procedure, enter their name or ID in the "Equipment to be used" field.
4. In the "Procedure to be performed" field, describe the specific procedure your patient will undergo (e.g., lash lift, lash extension, etc.).
5. In the "Benefits of Eyelash Extensions" field, briefly describe the advantages of the treatment (e.g., longer, thicker, more defined lashes).
6. In the "Potential Risks of Eyelash Extensions" field, note any potential complications or side effects associated with the treatment (e.g., irritation, allergic reactions, etc.).
7. Enter your contact information, including your name and phone number, in the "Contact Information" field.
8. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
9. Finally, sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the treatment and its risks.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Lash Artist**: Indicate whether your eyelash artist is performing the procedure ("Yes" or "No").
* **Equipment to be used**: If your eyelash artist is performing the procedure, enter their name or ID.
* **Procedure to be performed**: Describe the specific procedure your patient will undergo (e.g., lash lift, lash extension, etc.).
* **Benefits of Eyelash Extensions**: Briefly describe the advantages of the treatment (e.g., longer, thicker, more defined lashes).
* **Potential Risks of Eyelash Extensions**: Note any potential complications or side effects associated with the treatment (e.g., irritation, allergic reactions, etc.).
* **Contact Information**: Enter your full name and phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Date**: Sign and date the form to confirm your understanding of the treatment and its risks.
