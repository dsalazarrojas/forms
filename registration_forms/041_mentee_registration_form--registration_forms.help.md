# mentee_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for mentees (those who are being mentored) to provide information about themselves for the mentorship program. It helps match mentees with suitable mentors based on their needs and interests.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (identified with a asterisk*) with your personal details and preferences.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure accurate and truthful information.
3. Submit the form to initiate the mentorship application process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, *): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, *): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (3, date, ): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Email** (4, email, *): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (5, text, ): Enter your phone number.
* **Mentorship Area of Interest** (6, select_multiple, ): Select all areas of interest for the mentorship program.
* **Expected Degree Level** (7, select_one, ): Select your expected degree level (Bachelor, Master, PhD).
* **Expected Degree Field** (8, text, ): Enter your expected degree field.
* **Student ID** (9, number, ): Enter your student ID (if applicable).
* **Mentorship Type** (10, select_one, ): Select the type of mentorship you are looking for (academic, career, social).
* **Mentorship Frequency** (11, select_one, ): Select the frequency of the mentorship (monthly, bi-monthly, weekly, daily).
* **Mentorship Length** (12, select_one, ): Select the length of the mentorship (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year).
* **Mentorship Duration** (13, select_multiple, ): Indicate if you are open to the mentorship duration (Yes, No).
* **Mentorship Start Date** (14, date, ): Enter the start date of the mentorship.
* **Mentorship End Date** (15, date, ): Enter the end date of the mentorship.
* **Mentorship Start Time** (16, time, ): Enter the start time of the mentorship.
* **Mentorship End Time** (17, time, ): Enter the end time of the mentorship.
* **Mentorship Status** (18, select_one, ): Select the status of the mentorship (inactive, active, completed).

Note: The asterisk (*) denotes a required field.
