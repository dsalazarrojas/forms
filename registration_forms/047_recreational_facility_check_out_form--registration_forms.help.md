# recreational_facility_check_out_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recreational facility check-out form is used to register a user's check-out from a recreational facility. Please use this form to accurately report your check-out from the facility. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Name field with your correct name.
2. Enter your correct Email address.
3. Fill in the Phone Number field with your contact phone number.
4. Select the correct Reservation Date when you checked out.
5. Choose the correct Equipment Type(s) that you checked out.
6. Select the correct Facilities Checked Out.
7. Fill in the Comments field with any comments or details about your check-out.
8. Enter the correct Assigned Tool, if applicable.
9. Select the correct Category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (`user_name`, `text`, required): Fill in your name as it appears on your user identification.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your correct email address. This will be used for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`, `date`, required): Select the date when you checked out from the facility.
* **Equipment Type** (`equipment_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all the equipment types that you have checked out.
* **Facilities Checked Out** (`facilities_checked_out`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the facilities you have checked out.
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required): Fill in any comments or details about your check-out.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `text`, required): Enter the tool that you have been assigned for the check-out.
* **Category** (`category`, `text`, required): Select the category for the check-out. 

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and truthfully.
