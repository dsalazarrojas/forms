# Tskis - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tskis form is used to collect information about an employee's employment status, including their job title, department, and contact details. This form is intended to be completed by employees, managers, and regional admins to provide an overview of an employee's employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Tskis form, follow these steps:
- Click on the "Submit" button to begin the form.
- Select the "Employment Status" option from the dropdown menu.
- Enter your name, job title, department, and other relevant details in the text fields.
- Select the correct employment type (e.g., New Hire, Rehire, Layoff) from the dropdown menu.
- Enter your date of joining, date of birth, and contact number in the date and text fields.
- Enter your email address and manager's name in the email and text fields.
- Enter your address, department, and job description in the text fields.
- Select the correct reason for change from the dropdown menu.
- Enter your manager's note in the text field.
- Enter the regional admin's name, email, phone number, job title, department, and note in the text fields.
- Select the correct regional admin's signature and designation.
- Enter the regional admin's designation date.
- Select the correct date of employment.
- Select whether the manager is employed or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employment Status**: Select the correct employment status from the dropdown menu.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department.
* **Employment Type**: Select the correct employment type (e.g., New Hire, Rehire, Layoff).
* **Date Of Joining**: Enter your date of joining in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Manager Name**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Regional Admin Name**: Enter the regional admin's name.
* **Regional Admin Email**: Enter the regional admin's email address.
* **Regional Admin Phone**: Enter the regional admin's phone number.
* **Regional Admin Job Title**: Enter the regional admin's job title.
* **Regional Admin Department**: Enter the regional admin's department.
* **Regional Admin Note**: Enter any additional notes from the regional admin.
* **Regional Admin Signature**: Select the correct regional admin signature.
* **Regional Admin Designation**: Select the correct regional admin designation.
* **Regional Admin Designation Date**: Enter the regional admin's designation date.
* **Date Of Employment**: Enter the date of employment.
* **Manager Employed**: Select whether the manager is employed or not.
