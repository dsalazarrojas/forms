# Autism Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for the Autism Training Program. Please fill out this form to register for the training that suits your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start from the top**: Begin by reading the instructions and guidelines for each section.
2. **Fill out the required fields**: Ensure you complete the required fields: Full Name, Email Address, Contact Number, Profession or Role, Session Topic, Preferred Training Date, and Training Format.
3. **Choose your options carefully**: Make informed decisions when selecting your session topic, training format, and accessibility requirements.
4. **Be thorough in optional fields**: If you have specific needs, please select them carefully to ensure we can accommodate them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information
* **Full Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on your certificate.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for session links and updates.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your best number for us to reach you.
* **Profession or Role**: Select your primary reason for attending the training (Teacher, Parent, Healthcare Provider, Student, Employer, or Other).
* **Organization Name**: If you are representing an organization, enter its name (optional).

### Training Selection
* **Session Topic**: Choose the training module that suits your needs (Introduction to Autism, Behavioral Strategies, Inclusive Education, Supporting Adults with Autism).
* **Preferred Training Date**: Select the date you prefer to attend from the available sessions.
* **Training Format**: Choose how you prefer to attend (In-person, Online Live, Self-paced).

### Specific Needs
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Select any support needed to participate (Sign Language Interpreter, Captioning, Sensory-friendly Environment, Wheelchair Access, Large Print Materials).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you require specific dietary accommodations for in-person sessions, please specify.

### Additional Information
* **Learning Objectives and Goals**: Share what you hope to gain from this training.
* **How did you hear about us?**: Help us improve our reach by sharing how you found out about this training (Social Media, Website, Email Newsletter, Professional Referral).

Note: If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for guidance. We are here to help!
