# Podcasting Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register volunteers for the podcasting team. It collects information about your organization, position title, volunteering opportunities of interest, contact details, and any additional comments or information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your organization's name in the "What is the name of your organization?" field.
2. Enter your position title in the "Position Title" field.
3. Select all the volunteering opportunities that interest you from the "What kind of volunteering opportunity are you interested in?" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your name, email, and phone number in the respective fields.
5. If you have a date of birth, please enter it in the "Date of Birth" field. This information will help us tailor the volunteering opportunities to your needs.
6. If you have any additional comments or information, please use the "Additional Comments or Information" field for this purpose.
7. Finally, click the "Submit" button to send your registration information to the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **What is the name of your organization?** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): This field is used to identify your organization. Please enter the exact name as it appears on official documents or websites.
* **Position Title** (`position_title`, text, required: false): Please enter your current position or title within your organization.
* **What kind of volunteering opportunity are you interested in?** (`volunteer_details`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the opportunities that interest you from the dropdown menu. You can choose one or more options.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your full name as it appears on official documents or IDs.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): If you have a date of birth, please enter it for our records.
* **Time of Registration** (`time_of_registration`, time, required: false): Please note the time you registered with this form.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (`note`, note, required: false): Use this field to provide any additional comments or information that might be helpful for our team to know.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to send your registration information to the team.
