# Plumbing Certification Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plumbing Certification Exam Form is a registration form for a professional plumbing certification exam. It allows users to register and provide information about their exam preferences, such as the type of exam, schedule, and fee structure. The form is used to gather information for the exam administration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Provide your name and contact information on page 1.
2.  Select your preferred exam type, date, time, and schedule on page 2.
3.  Choose your exam location, fee structure, and any additional exam notes on page 3.
4.  Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **candidate_info**: Provide your name and contact information.
*   **name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your certification.
*   **address**: Enter your address.
*   **email**: Enter your email address.
*   **phone**: Enter your phone number.
*   **exam_date**: Select your preferred exam date.
*   **exam_time**: Select your preferred exam time.
*   **exam_time_zone**: Enter your time zone.
*   **selected_exam_date**: Select your preferred exam date.
*   **selected_exam_time**: Select your preferred exam time.
*   **select_exam_type**: Select your preferred exam type (e.g., certification, licensure, continuing education).
*   **select_exam_type_other**: Select any additional exam types (e.g., other certifications, specializations).
*   **certification_level**: Enter your desired certification level (if applicable).
*   **exam_schedule**: Select your preferred exam schedule (if applicable).
*   **exam_schedule_time**: Select your preferred exam schedule time (if applicable).
*   **selected_exam_schedule**: Select your preferred exam schedule (if applicable).
*   **selected_exam_schedule_time**: Select your preferred exam schedule time (if applicable).
*   **exam_duration**: Select the duration of the exam (e.g., hours, days).
*   **exam_fee**: Enter your exam fee.
*   **exam_fee_currency**: Select your exam fee currency (e.g., USD, CAD).
*   **selected_exam_fee**: Select your preferred exam fee (e.g., paid in advance, at the door).
*   **selected_exam_fee_currency**: Select your preferred exam fee currency (e.g., USD, CAD).
*   **exam_location**: Enter your preferred exam location.
*   **exam_instructions**: Enter any additional exam instructions or special requests.
*   **submit**: Click "Submit" to complete your registration.
*   **note**: Enter any additional exam notes or comments.
*   **selected_exam_note**: Select your preferred exam note (if applicable).
*   **exam_status**: Select your exam status (e.g., passed, failed, pending).
*   **exam_comments**: Enter any additional exam comments or feedback.
*   **selected_exam_comments**: Select your preferred exam comments (if applicable).
*   **submitter_note**: Enter any additional submitter notes or comments.
*   **selected_submitter_note**: Select your preferred submitter note (if applicable).

## Tips

*   Be accurate when entering your name and contact information.
*   Select your preferred exam type and schedule carefully, as these will affect your exam experience.
*   Enter your exam location and fee structure carefully, as these will impact your exam fee and location.
*   Review your registration carefully before submitting.
