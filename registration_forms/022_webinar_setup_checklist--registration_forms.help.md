# Webinar Setup Checklist - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information for setting up a webinar, including general information, speaker details, schedule and timing, and other relevant settings.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the general information about the webinar, including its title.
2. Provide details about the speakers, such as their names.
3. Specify the schedule and timing of the webinar, including the date, start and end times, and time zone.
4. Select the format of the webinar (e.g. yes/no).
5. Choose the language(s) used in the webinar (e.g. yes/no).
6. Enter a registration link for the webinar.
7. Set up the duration and capacity of the webinar.
8. Determine if the webinar is timezone-aware and provide a brief description if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info** (`title`, required/optional): Enter the title of the webinar.
- **Speakers Info** (`speakers_info`, required/optional): Enter details about the speakers, such as their names.
- **Webinar Schedule** (`webinar_schedule`, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the webinar.
- **Webinar Format** (`webinar_format`, required/optional): Select the format of the webinar (yes/no).
- **Webinar Language** (`webinar_language`, required/optional): Choose the language(s) used in the webinar (yes/no).
- **Registration Link** (`registration_link`, required/optional): Enter a link for registering to the webinar.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the webinar.
- **Start Time** (`start_time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of the webinar.
- **End Time** (`end_time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of the webinar.
- **Timezone** (`timezone`, required/optional): Enter the time zone of the webinar.
- **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, required/optional): Enter a numerical offset for the time zone.
- **Duration** (`duration`, required/optional): Enter the duration of the webinar.
- **Capacity** (`capacity`, required/optional): Enter the capacity of the webinar.
- **Timezone Aware** (`timezone_aware`, required/optional): Select if the webinar is timezone-aware (yes/no).
- **Timezone Aware Hint** (`timezone_aware_hint`, required/optional): Enter a brief hint about being timezone-aware.
- **Timezone Aware Description** (`timezone_aware_description`, required/optional): Enter a brief description about being timezone-aware.

Note: Required/optional fields are indicated by the `required` property in the YAML.
