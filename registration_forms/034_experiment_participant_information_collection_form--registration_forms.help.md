# Experiment Participant Information Collection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Experiment Participant Information Collection Form is used to gather demographic data about participants in an experiment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone Number" field, if you have one.
3. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Participant Date of Birth" field (mm/dd/yyyy).
5. Select your education level from the list in the "Participant Education Level" field.
6. List any languages you speak in the "Participant Languages Spoken" field.
7. If you have any languages that are not in the list, enter them in the "Participant Languages Spoken Other" field.
8. Enter the number of languages you speak in the "Participant Languages Spoken Count" field.
9. List any additional education institutions you have attended in the "Participant Education Institutions" field.
10. List your hobbies in the "Participant Hobbies" field.
11. If you have any additional languages that are not in the list, enter them in the "Participant Languages Spoken Other Languages" field.
12. Enter the count of languages you speak for these additional languages in the "Participant Languages Spoken Count Other Languages" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Experiment Participant Information** (`experiment_participant_information`, text, required): This field is for a brief description of yourself as an experiment participant.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name in this field.
* **Participant Phone Number** (`participant_phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Participant Education Level** (`participant_education_level`, select_one, required): Select your education level from the list: High School, Bachelor, Masters, Doctoral.
* **Participant Education Fields** (`participant_education_fields`, select_multiple, optional): Select any education fields you have from the list: Arts, Engineering, Medicine, Science.
* **Participant Languages Spoken** (`participant_languages_spoken`, text, optional): List any languages you speak.
* **Participant Languages Spoken Other** (`participant_languages_spoken_other`, text, optional): If you have any additional languages that are not in the list, enter them here.
* **Participant Languages Spoken Count** (`participant_languages_spoken_count`, text, optional): Enter the number of languages you speak.
* **Participant Education Institutions** (`participant_education_institutions`, text, optional): List any additional education institutions you have attended.
* **Participant Hobbies** (`participant_hobbies`, text, optional): List your hobbies.
* **Participant Languages Spoken Other Languages** (`participant_languages_spoken_other_languages`, text, optional): If you have any additional languages that are not in the list, enter them here.
* **Participant Languages Spoken Count Other Languages** (`participant_languages_spoken_count_other_languages`, text, optional): Enter the count of languages you speak for these additional languages.
