# Birdwatching Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in the Birdwatching Tournament. It collects personal and birding-related information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone. Please fill out this form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Enter your personal details, including First Name, Last Name, Email Address, Phone Number, Street Address, City, State, and ZIP Code.
3. Select your participation type (Solo or Team) and provide team information if applicable.
4. Choose your preferred tournament details, including the tournament name, date, and division.
5. Select your birding experience level and indicate if you have participated in previous tournaments.
6. Indicate your equipment availability, including binoculars and spotting scope.
7. Choose how you will get around (e.g., personal vehicle, team vehicle, carpooling).
8. Select your transportation method (e.g., car, SUV, van, truck, bicycle).
9. Specify your preferred birding area and target species.
10. Select your tournament goals and additional registration options.
11. Choose your meal preference and dietary notes.
12. Select your meal preference and agree to follow birding ethics and tournament rules.
13. Choose your payment method and registration fee.
14. Indicate if you have additional donation or sponsorship information.
15. Review and agree to the liability waiver, code of ethics, and photo release.
16. Provide emergency contact information.
17. Add any additional comments or notes.
18. Select how you heard about the tournament.
19. Sign and confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter your contact email.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (Text, Required): Enter your street address.
* **City** (Text, Required): Enter your city.
* **State** (Text, Required): Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code** (Text, Required): Enter your postal code.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Required): Enter your birth date.
* **Team Information** (Note, Optional): Briefly describe your team if participating as a team.
* **Participation Type** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you're participating as a Solo or Team.
* **Team Name** (Text, Optional): If participating as a team, enter your team name.
* **Team Captain** (Text, Optional): If participating as a team, enter the captain's name.
* **Team Members** (Text, Optional): If participating as a team, list all team members.
* **Tournament Details** (Note, Optional): Select your preferred tournament options.
* **Tournament Name** (Select One, Required): Choose the tournament you're participating in.
* **Tournament Date** (Date, Required): Enter the tournament date.
* **Competition Division** (Select One, Required): Choose your division.
* **Experience Level** (Select One, Required): Select your birding experience level.
* **Previous Tournaments** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you've participated in previous tournaments.
* **Equipment** (Note, Optional): Indicate your equipment availability.
* **Binoculars** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you have high-quality, standard, or basic binoculars.
* **Spotting Scope** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you'll bring a spotting scope.
* **Camera Equipment** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you'll be photographing, and if so, with what type of equipment.
* **Transportation** (Select One, Required): Choose how you'll get around.
* **Vehicle Type** (Select One, Optional): If driving, select your vehicle type.
* **Area Assignment** (Select One, Required): Specify your preferred birding area.
* **Target Species** (Text, Optional): List the birds you hope to find.
* **Tournament Goals** (Select Multiple, Required): Choose what you hope to achieve in the tournament.
* **Registration Options** (Note, Optional): Select any additional options.
* **T-Shirt Size** (Select One, Required): Choose your preferred t-shirt size.
* **Meal Preference** (Select One, Required): Select your meal preference.
* **Dietary Notes** (Text, Optional): Specify any dietary restrictions.
* **Awards Ceremony** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you'll attend the awards ceremony.
* **Payment Information** (Note, Optional): Review your payment information.
* **Entry Fee** (Number, Required): Enter your registration fee.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Required): Choose your payment method.
* **Additional Donation** (Number, Optional): Indicate if you'd like to make an additional donation.
* **Sponsorship** (Select One, Optional): Indicate if you have sponsorship information.
* **Sponsor Name** (Text, Optional): If applicable, enter your sponsor's name.
* **Waivers and Agreements** (Note, Optional): Review and agree to the waivers and agreements.
* **Liability Waiver** (Select One, Required): Agree to the liability waiver.
* **Code of Ethics** (Select One, Required): Agree to follow birding ethics.
* **Tournament Rules** (Select One, Required): Agree to follow tournament rules.
* **Photo Release** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you agree to photo release.
* **Data Sharing** (Select One, Required): Indicate if you agree to data sharing.
* **Emergency Information** (Note, Optional): Provide emergency information for safety.
* **Medical Conditions** (Text, Optional): List any medical conditions.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Text, Required): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **How Heard About Tournament** (Select One, Optional): Indicate how you heard about the tournament.
* **Participant Signature** (Select One, Required): Sign and confirm your agreement.
* **Registration Date** (Date, Required): Enter your registration date.
