# Hockey Camp Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about participants and coaches for the hockey camp registration process. It helps us get to know you and your participant(s) better, ensuring that we can provide the best experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant's name in the **Participant Name** field.
2. Enter your participant's age in the **Participant Age** field.
3. Enter your participant's phone number in the **Participant Phone** field.
4. Enter your participant's email address in the **Participant Email** field.
5. Select a schedule for your participant from the **Participant Schedule** field.
6. Enter the name of the coach or staff member who will be working with your participant in the **Coach** field.
7. Provide any additional schedule details in the **Schedule Details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: This is the name of the participant. Please enter the name of the person registering for the camp.
* **Participant Age**: This is the age of the participant. Please enter a numerical value.
* **Participant Phone**: This is the contact phone number of the participant. This can be a home, work, or mobile number, but please make sure to include the country code.
* **Participant Email**: This is the email address of the participant. We will use this to contact you with important updates and information.
* **Participant Schedule**: This is the schedule for the participant. Please select a date and time for when the participant will be attending the camp.
* **Coach**: This is the name of the coach or staff member who will be working with your participant. This can be a coach who will be leading activities, or a staff member who will be overseeing your participant's experience.
* **Schedule Details**: This is an optional field where you can provide any additional information about your participant's schedule, such as special requirements or requests.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your participant's information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will use this information to ensure that your participant has a safe and enjoyable experience at the camp.
