## Purpose
The "Voter Registration Consent Form" is a document used to collect information from potential voters to facilitate their registration process. This form captures crucial details such as contact information, voting status, and demographic data to help the electoral authorities process their registration efficiently.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully and ensure you are eligible to vote.
2.  Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3.  Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`id: 1`, `name: user_info`, `type: text`, required: false): This section is where you can input any relevant information you feel is necessary for the election authorities to know.
* **Contact Info** (`id: 2`, `name: contact_info`, `type: text`, required: false): In this field, you can provide your contact details such as your name, address, phone number, and email to help the electoral authorities get in touch with you.
* **Voting Status** (`id: 3`, `name: voting_status`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Here you can select your current voting status from the options provided.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 4`, `name: date_of_birth`, `type: date`, required: false): You are required to input your date of birth to ensure you are eligible to vote.
* **Date of Registration** (`id: 5`, `name: date_of_registration`, `type: date`, required: false): This field captures the date you registered as a voter.
* **Voting History** (`id: 6`, `name: voting_history`, `type: text`, required: false): You can provide your voting history, if any.
* **Voter ID** (`id: 7`, `name: voter_id`, `type: number`, required: false): Enter your unique voter ID number for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`id: 8`, `name: email`, `type: email`, required: false): Your email address for the electoral authorities to get in touch with you.
* **Phone** (`id: 9`, `name: phone`, `type: text`, required: false): Provide your phone number for easy contact.
* **Note** (`id: 10`, `name: note`, `type: note`, required: false): If you have any additional comments, you can enter them here.
* **Category** (`id: 11`, `name: category`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select the category that best describes your voting status from the options provided.
* **Sub Category** (`id: 12`, `name: sub_category`, `type: select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant subcategories that apply to you.
* **Address** (`id: 13`, `name: address`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your current address for reference.
* **City** (`id: 14`, `name: city`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **State** (`id: 15`, `name: state`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of the state where you live.
* **Zip** (`id: 16`, `name: zip`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter your zip code for postal correspondence.
* **Country** (`id: 17`, `name: country`, `type: text`, required: false): Enter the name of your country.
* **Time** (`id: 18`, `name: time`, `type: time`, required: false): Enter the time you are available to contact you.
* **Time Zone** (`id: 19`, `name: time_zone`, `type: select_one`, required: false): Select your time zone from the options provided.

By following this guide, you will be able to complete the "Voter Registration Consent Form" accurately and efficiently. Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly and submit the form once you have reviewed it carefully.
