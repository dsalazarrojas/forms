# Government Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is used to register government training programs, allowing users to provide essential details about the training they are undertaking or have already completed. The form is intended to streamline the process of tracking and recording government training activities.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your details and select the relevant options from the dropdown menus.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
3.  Review your form before submitting to ensure accuracy and completeness.
4.  Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Details (\*):** Enter a brief description of the form.
* **Name:** Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email:** Enter your official email address used for communication.
* **Contact Number (\*):** Enter your primary contact number for official calls.
* **Start Date:** Enter the date you began the training program.
* **End Date:** Enter the date you completed or expect to complete the training program.
* **Location:** Enter the location where the training program was conducted or will be conducted.
* **Training Program (\*):** Select the type of training program you are registering (Government or Non-Government).
* **Training Type:** Select all applicable methods of training delivery (In-Person, Online, Hybrid, or Virtual).
* **Total Training Hours (\*):** Enter the total number of hours spent on the training program.
* **Total Training Cost (\*):** Enter the total cost associated with the training program.
* **Training Status (\*):** Select the current status of your training (Pending, In Progress, or Completed).
* **Additional Notes:** Enter any additional information or comments about the training program.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields marked with an asterisk (\*) are filled out.
* Use official email and contact numbers to facilitate communication.
* Select the correct training program and status to ensure accurate tracking.
* Use the "Additional Notes" field for any special comments or instructions.
