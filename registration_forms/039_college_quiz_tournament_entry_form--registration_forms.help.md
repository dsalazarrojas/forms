## College Quiz Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The College Quiz Tournament Entry Form is used to collect information from teams participating in the quiz tournament. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form carefully, ensuring you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and detailed information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure it meets the specific requirements.
3. If you are unsure about any field, contact the tournament organizers for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Team Name
* **Team Name (team_name)**: Enter the name of your quiz team. This is the name that will be displayed on the scoreboard and other tournament materials.
### School or College Name
* **School or College Name (school_name)**: Enter the name of your school or college. This is used to confirm your affiliation and eligibility.
### Tournament Name
* **Tournament Name (tournament_name)**: Enter the name of the tournament you are entering. This is used to determine which tournament your team is registered for.
### Tournament Date
* **Tournament Date (tournament_date)**: Enter the date of the tournament you are participating in. This is used to schedule your team's participation and for communication purposes.
### Team Captain Name
* **Team Captain Name (team_captain_name)**: Enter your name as the team captain. This is required for communication and identification purposes.
### Team Captain Email
* **Team Captain Email (captain_email)**: Enter your email address as the team captain. This is used for communication and updates.
### Team Captain Phone
* **Team Captain Phone (captain_phone)**: Enter your phone number as the team captain. This is used for communication and updates.
### Team Category
* **Team Category (team_category)**: Select the level of competition your team wishes to participate in: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Open, or Mixed Level.
### Team Size
* **Team Size (team_size)**: Enter the number of players on your team. This is used to determine the team's roster and for scheduling purposes.
### Player 1 Name
* **Player 1 Name (player_1_name)**: Enter the name of your first player. This is used to confirm player registration and for identification purposes.
### Player 1 Student ID
* **Player 1 Student ID (player_1_id)**: Enter the student ID of your first player. This is used to confirm player registration and for eligibility purposes.
### Player 2 Name
* **Player 2 Name (player_2_name)**: Enter the name of your second player. This is used to confirm player registration and for identification purposes.
### Player 2 Student ID
* **Player 2 Student ID (player_2_id)**: Enter the student ID of your second player. This is used to confirm player registration and for eligibility purposes.
### Player 3 Name
* **Player 3 Name (player_3_name)**: Enter the name of your third player. If you don't have a third player, leave this field blank.
### Player 3 Student ID
* **Player 3 Student ID (player_3_id)**: Enter the student ID of your third player. If you don't have a third player, leave this field blank.
### Player 4 Name
* **Player 4 Name (player_4_name)**: Enter the name of your fourth player. If you don't have a fourth player, leave this field blank.
### Player 4 Student ID
* **Player 4 Student ID (player_4_id)**: Enter the student ID of your fourth player. If you don't have a fourth player, leave this field blank.
### Alternate Player Name
* **Alternate Player Name (alternate_player_name)**: Enter the name of your alternate player. If you don't have an alternate player, leave this field blank.
### Alternate Player Student ID
* **Alternate Player Student ID (alternate_player_id)**: Enter the student ID of your alternate player. If you don't have an alternate player, leave this field blank.
### Team Experience
* **Team Experience (team_experience)**: Select the level of quiz experience your team has: First Time, Some Experience, Very Experienced.
### Entry Fee Paid
* **Entry Fee Paid (entry_fee_paid)**: Select whether your team has paid the entry fee: Yes, No, or Pending.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method (payment_method)**: Select how your team plans to pay the entry fee: Check, Credit Card, PayPal, Cash, School Account, or Other.
### Special Accommodations Needed
* **Special Accommodations Needed (special_accommodations)**: If your team needs any special accommodations, enter a brief description here.
### All Players Agree to Competition Rules and Waivers
* **All Players Agree to Competition Rules and Waivers (waiver_signed)**: Select Yes or No to confirm all players have read and agreed to the competition rules and waivers.
### Team Captain Confirms All Players Are Eligible
* **Team Captain Confirms All Players Are Eligible (eligibility_confirmation)**: Select Yes or No to confirm that all players meet the age and enrollment requirements.

Please complete this form accurately and thoroughly, and contact the tournament organizers if you have any questions or need further clarification.
