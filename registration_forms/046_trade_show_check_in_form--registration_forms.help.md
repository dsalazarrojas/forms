# Trade Show Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register attendees and track their check-in status for a trade show.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the registration form.
2. Enter your **Email** in the email field.
3. Enter your **Date of Birth** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
4. Select your **Checkin Time** from the time options.
5. Choose your **Checkin Status** from the options: Checked In, Not Checked In, or Pending.
6. Select your **Assigned Tool** from the options: chatjimmy or Other.
7. If you selected "Other" for Assigned Tool, provide your **Assigned Tool Other 2** details.
8. Enter your **Event ID** if applicable.
9. Choose your **Checkin User** from the options: user1, user2, or user3.
10. Select your **Checkin User 2** from the options: user1, user2, or user3.
11. If you select multiple checkin users, list them in the order of preference.
12. Enter your **Checkin Date** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
13. Select your **Checkin Time 2** from the options.
14. Choose your **Checkin Status 2** from the options: Checked In, Not Checked In, or Pending.
15. Enter your **Event Id 2** if applicable.
16. Enter your **Checkin Location 2** if applicable.
17. Enter your **Created By** if applicable.
18. Enter your **Updated By** if applicable.
19. Enter your **Created At** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
20. Enter your **Updated At** in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
21. Enter your **Assigned Tool Other 2** details if you selected "Other" for Assigned Tool 2.

* **Assigned Tool**: Select your assigned tool from the options.
* **Assigned Tool Other 2**: If you selected "Other", provide your assigned tool details.
* **Event ID**: Enter your event ID if applicable.
* **Checkin User**: Select your checkin user from the options.
* **Checkin User 2**: Select your checkin user from the options.
* **Checkin Date**: Enter your checkin date.
* **Checkin Time 2**: Select your checkin time.
* **Checkin Status 2**: Choose your checkin status from the options.
* **Event Id 2**: Enter your event ID if applicable.
* **Checkin Location 2**: Enter your checkin location if applicable.
* **Created By**: Enter your created by if applicable.
* **Updated By**: Enter your updated by if applicable.
* **Created At**: Enter your created at in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Updated At**: Enter your updated at in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the required fields.
* Use the correct date and time formats.
* Select the correct checkin status and assigned tool.
* Enter your checkin location if applicable.
* If you select multiple checkin users, list them in the order of preference.
