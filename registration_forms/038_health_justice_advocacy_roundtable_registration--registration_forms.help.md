# Health Justice Advocacy Roundtable Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants for a health justice advocacy roundtable discussion. We need to know about your contact information, role, and accessibility needs to ensure a successful discussion.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information: Enter your name, email, phone number, and organization (if applicable).
2. Describe your role: Select your role in the discussion, if any.
3. Specify your accessibility needs: Let us know about any accessibility needs you may have for the discussion.
4. Indicate your interests: Select the topics that interest you the most in the discussion.
5. Submit: Click on the "Submit" button to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (id: 7, text, required): Enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email** (id: 8, email, required): Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone** (id: 9, text, required): Enter a phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Organization** (id: 10, text, required): If you represent an organization, enter its name.
* **Role** (id: 11, select one, required): Select your role in the discussion (e.g., representative, participant, facilitator).
* **Department** (id: 12, text, optional): If you represent an organization, enter your department's name.
* **Job Title** (id: 13, text, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Bio** (id: 14, text, optional): Briefly describe your background or experience relevant to the discussion.
* **Interests 1-3** (id: 15-17, select multiple, required): Select the topics that interest you the most in the discussion.
* **Accessibility Needs 1-3** (id: 18-20, select one, required): Indicate if you have any accessibility needs (e.g., wheelchair, interpreter, etc.).
* **Accessibility Needs** (id: 21, text, optional): If you have additional accessibility needs not listed, describe them here.
* **Submit** (id: 22, text, required): Click on this button to complete the registration.

Note: All fields marked as "required" are necessary to complete the registration.
