# Factory Energy Recovery Project Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form collects information about your factory energy recovery project, including its title, type, status, and other relevant details. This form is essential for registering your project with the relevant authority, and it is crucial to fill it out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the project title. This is the name of your project, which will help identify it in the registry.
2. Select the type of your project from the dropdown menu. This will indicate whether your project is for energy recovery or not.
3. Choose the status of your project from the dropdown menu. This will tell us if your project is active or inactive.
4. Provide a description of your project. This will help us understand the details of your project.
5. Enter the name and email of the contact person responsible for your project. This will ensure that we can reach out to you with any questions or updates.
6. Enter the phone number of the contact person. This will help us reach out to you if we need any further information.
7. Enter the project address. This will help us locate your project on the map.
8. Select whether your project is located on the project site or not.
9. Enter the project date. This is the date you plan to start your project.
10. Enter the project time. This is the time you plan to start your project.
11. Enter any additional notes about your project. This is optional but can be helpful for our reference.
12. Enter the project energy source. This refers to the type of energy your project will use (e.g., solar, wind, etc.).
13. Enter the project capacity. This is the maximum amount of energy your project can generate.
14. Select whether your project capacity is expressed in kilowatts or megawatts.
15. Select whether your project capacity is a single value or a range of values.
16. Enter the project energy efficiency. This is the percentage of energy efficiency for your project.
17. Enter the project cost. This is the total cost of your project.
18. Enter the cost of your project capacity.
19. Select whether the capacity cost is in US dollars or another currency.
20. Select whether you want to submit this form or not.
21. Enter your name, and we will record that you have submitted this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Title** (`project_title`, text, required): Enter a brief and descriptive title for your project.
* **Project Type** (`project_type`, select_multiple, required): Select whether your project is for energy recovery or not.
* **Project Status** (`project_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your project as active or inactive.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, optional): Provide a detailed description of your project.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your project.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Project Address** (`project_address`, text, required): Enter the address of your project site.
* **Project Location** (`project_location`, select_one, required): Choose whether your project is located on the project site or not.
* **Project Date** (`project_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to start your project.
* **Project Time** (`project_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to start your project.
* **Project Note** (`project_note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your project.
* **Project Energy Source** (`project_energy_source`, text, required): Enter the type of energy your project will use.
* **Project Capacity** (`project_capacity`, number, required): Enter the maximum amount of energy your project can generate.
* **Project Capacity Unit** (`project_capacity_unit`, select_one, required): Choose whether your project capacity is in kilowatts or megawatts.
* **Project Capacity Type** (`project_capacity_type`, select_one, required): Choose whether your project capacity is a single value or a range of values.
* **Project Capacity Range** (`project_capacity_range`, text, optional): Enter the range of your project capacity.
* **Project Capacity Cost** (`project_capacity_cost`, number, required): Enter the cost of your project capacity.
* **Project Capacity Cost Unit** (`project_capacity_cost_unit`, select_one, required): Choose whether the capacity cost is in US dollars or another currency.
* **Project Capacity Cost Currency** (`project_capacity_cost_currency`, select_one, required): Choose whether the capacity cost is in US dollars or another currency.
* **Form Submission** (`form_submission`, note, optional): This is an optional field for your notes.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter your name, and we will record that you have submitted this form.
