# Pro Bono Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for the registration of pro bono services. It is intended to be filled out by users to provide details about themselves and their service requests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **User Info** section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select the **Service Request** that best describes your needs.
3. Fill out the **Contact Info** section with your contact information, such as phone number and email.
4. If necessary, add any additional **Review Note** comments.
5. If you have a preferred **Email** and **Phone**, please enter them.
6. Select the **Date** and **Time** for your service request.
7. Choose the **Assigned Tool** that will be used for your service.
8. Optionally, fill out the **Form ID** for internal use.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info**: This is the first section of the form, where you can enter your name and any other details.
* **Service Request**: This is a selection menu where you can choose the type of service you need. Please select the option that best describes your service request.
* **Contact Info**: This is the section where you can enter your contact information, such as phone and email.
* **Review Note**: If you want to add any additional comments or notes about your review, this is the place to do so.
* **Email**: If you have a preferred email, please enter it here.
* **Phone**: If you have a preferred phone number, please enter it here.
* **Date**: Enter the date for your service request.
* **Time**: Enter the time for your service request.
* **Assigned Tool**: This is a selection menu where you can choose the tool that will be used for your service.
* **Form ID**: This is an optional field for internal use. If you have a specific form ID, you can enter it here.
