# Friendship Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the "Friendship Application Form" with confidence. The form is designed to gather information from potential friends, helping you get to know them better and build meaningful connections. Please note that this form is for personal use and not intended for commercial purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Begin by providing your personal details on the first page, including any relevant information that you think will help your potential friend get to know you better.
2.  On the second page, list your interests and hobbies that you'd like to share with others.
3.  In the third and final page, describe what you're looking for in a friendship and what you're willing to offer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Title (Text, Optional)**: This field is an opportunity for you to share your name or a nickname you'd like to be known by in the friendship circle.
* **Interests (Text, Optional)**: List your favorite hobbies, sports, books, movies, or any other activities that bring you joy.
* **Friendship Details (Text, Optional)**: Describe what kind of friend you'd like to be, what you're looking for in a friendship, and what qualities you possess that would make you a great friend.

## Tips

*   Be honest and authentic in your responses to get the most out of this form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to ask for clarification.
*   Remember that this form is a starting point for connection, so don't be afraid to be creative and add any personal touches you'd like!
