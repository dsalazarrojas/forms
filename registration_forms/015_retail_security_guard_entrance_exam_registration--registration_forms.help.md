# Retail Security Guard Entrance Exam Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information for a retail security guard entrance exam registration. The form is intended for individuals who are interested in taking the exam and requires them to choose their preferred scheduling options, exam type, and shift length. Additionally, it collects personal information such as name, email, phone number, and address. The form also includes fields for confirmations of name, email, and phone number for verification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Schedule Preferences**: Choose your preferred time slot for the exam from the available options (8 am - 9 am, 9 am - 10 am, etc.).
2. **Scheduling Date**: Select the date you would like to take the exam.
3. **Exam Type**: Choose the type of exam you want to take (Type 1, Type 2, or Type 3).
4. **Shift Length**: Select the duration of your exam shift (2 hours, 4 hours, 6 hours, or 8 hours).
5. **Availability**: Enter your availability date (optional).
6. **Availability 2**: Enter your second availability date (optional).
7. **Eligibility**: Check if you are eligible to take the exam.
8. **Name**: Enter your name.
9. **Email**: Enter your email address.
10. **Phone**: Enter your phone number (optional).
11. **Address**: Enter your address.
12. **Confirm Name**: Confirm your name.
13. **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address.
14. **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number (if entered).
15. **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
16. **Notes 2**: Add any additional notes or comments 2 (optional).
