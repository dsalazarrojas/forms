# Evaluation Meeting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Evaluation Meeting Registration Form is a crucial tool for planning and organizing evaluation meetings. It helps to collect essential details about the meeting, allowing for effective organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the form link to access the form.
2. Choose a meeting date using the date selector.
3. Select the start and end times using the time selector.
4. Choose the meeting location from the provided options.
5. Add the meeting goals and objectives in the text field.
6. Select the expected participants from the list of options.
7. Enter the participant's name in the text field.
8. Enter the participant's email address in the text field (optional).
9. Enter the participant's phone number in the text field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Meeting Date** (Required)
 Meeting date for the evaluation meeting.

### 2. **Start Time** (Required)
Select the start time of the evaluation meeting.

### 3. **End Time** (Required)
Select the end time of the evaluation meeting.

### 4. **Meeting Location** (Required)
Choose a location for the evaluation meeting from the options provided.

### 5. **Meeting Goals** (Optional)
Provide a brief description of the meeting goals and objectives.

### 6. **Expected Participants** (Required)
Select the expected participants for the meeting from the options provided.

### 7. **Participant's Name** (Required)
Enter the name of the participant attending the meeting.

### 8. **Participant's E-mail** (Optional)
Enter the email address of the participant (if applicable).

### 9. **Participant's Phone** (Optional)
Enter the phone number of the participant (if applicable).
