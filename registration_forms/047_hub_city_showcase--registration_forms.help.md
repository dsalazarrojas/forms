# Hub City Showcase - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hub City Showcase form is used to collect player information for the event. This information will be used to create a database of participants and facilitate communication among organizers, sponsors, and players.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including player name, email, phone number, and birthdate.
2. Select your player gender from the options provided.
3. Provide your occupation and address.
4. Enter your city, state, and postal code.
5. Choose your t-shirt size.
6. Enter your shoe size.
7. Select your haircut and hair color.
8. Enter your height and weight.
9. Choose the unit of measurement for your height and weight.
10. Select the unit of measurement for your t-shirt and shoe size.
11. Fill out any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it should be displayed for event purposes.
* **Player Email** (`player_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Player Phone** (`player_phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for organizers to contact you.
* **Birthdate** (`birthdate`, `date`, required): Enter your birthdate in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Player Gender** (`player_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Occupation** (`player_occupation`, `text`, required): Enter your occupation or field of study.
* **Address** (`player_address`, `text`, required): Provide your address for event organization and communication.
* **City** (`player_city`, `text`, required): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`player_state`, `text`, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **Postal** (`player_postal`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code or zip code.
* **Country** (`player_country`, `text`, required): Enter your country of residence.
* **Notes** (`player_notes`, `note`, required): Write any additional comments or information about yourself.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`player_tshirt_size`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred t-shirt size.
* **Shoe Size** (`player_shoes_size`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred shoe size.
* **Haircut** (`player_haircut`, `select_multiple`, required): Select your preferred haircut style.
* **Hair Color** (`player_haircolor`, `select_one`, required): Select your hair color.
* **Eye Color** (`player_eyecolor`, `select_one`, required): Select your eye color.
* **Height** (`player_height`, `number`, required): Enter your height in the selected unit.
* **Weight** (`player_weight`, `number`, required): Enter your weight in the selected unit.
* **Height Unit** (`player_height_unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for your height.
* **Weight Unit** (`player_weight_unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for your weight.
* **T-Shirt Size Unit** (`player_tshirt_size_unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for your t-shirt size.
* **Shoe Size Unit** (`player_shoes_size_unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for your shoe size.
* **Haircut Unit** (`player_haircut_unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit of measurement for your haircut style.
