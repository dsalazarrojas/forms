# Team Building Workshop Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Building Workshop Admission Form is designed to collect information from participants for the team-building workshop. This form is used to gather details about the participants and their preferences for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Participant Information section with your details.
2. Select your preferred Workshop Date and Workshop Time.
3. Choose your Workshop Duration and Venue from the options provided.
4. Enter your Registration Fee.
5. Select your registration date.
6. If you have any special requirements, please specify them in the Special Requirements field.
7. Finally, review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: 
  - **Name** (`Name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
  - **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
  - **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number.
  - **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, `text`, required/optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please specify them here.
* 
* **Workshop Information**: 
  - **Workshop Date** (`Workshop Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date for which you want to attend the workshop.
  - **Workshop Time** (`Workshop Time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time slot for the workshop you would like to attend.
  - **Workshop Duration** (`Workshop Duration`, `number`, required/optional): Choose the duration of the workshop you would like to attend.
  - **Venue** (`Venue`, `text`, required/optional): Select the venue where you would like to attend the workshop.
  - **Registration Fee** (`Registration Fee`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the registration fee you are willing to pay.
  - **Registration Date** (`Registration Date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of your registration.
* 
* **Workshop Details**: 
  - **Workshop Date 1** (`Workshop Date 1`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date for the first workshop you would like to attend.
  - **Workshop Time 1** (`Workshop Time 1`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time slot for the first workshop you would like to attend.
  - **Workshop Duration 1** (`Workshop Duration 1`, `number`, required/optional): Choose the duration of the first workshop you would like to attend.
  - **Venue 1** (`Venue 1`, `text`, required/optional): Select the venue where you would like to attend the first workshop.
  - **Registration Fee 1** (`Registration Fee 1`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the registration fee for the first workshop.
  - **Registration Date 1** (`Registration Date 1`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of your registration for the first workshop.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any delays in processing your registration.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the workshop organizers for clarification.
* Please review your form carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
