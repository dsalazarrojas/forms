# Short Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a short application. It has six fields divided among three pages. Please fill out the form completely to ensure your application is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply navigate through the three pages and fill out the required fields. You can submit the form when you have finished filling out all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Page

* **First Page** (1, text, required: false): This page has a label but no description or hint.
* **First Page** is the first section of the form, but its description is not provided here.
*   To proceed, click the next page button.

### Second Page

* **Second Page** (2, text, required: false): This page serves as a transition or a placeholder in the form.
*   To continue, click the next page button.

### Contact Info

* **Contact Info (3, text, required: false): Please provide your contact information**
*   Enter your contact email, phone number, or any other relevant contact details.
*   This field is optional, but providing your contact information can help the reviewer process your application.

### Role and Interests

* **Role and Interests (4, text, required: false): Describe your role and interests**
*   Enter a brief description of your role and interests.
*   This field is optional, but it can help the reviewer understand your application better.

### Application Details

* **Application Details (5, text, required: false): Please provide application details**
*   Enter any other relevant information you feel is necessary for your application.
*   This field is optional, but it can help the reviewer have a better understanding of your application.

### Submit

* **Submit (6, text, required: false): Please submit your application**
*   Review your filled-out form, then click this button to submit your application.
*   This button will be enabled/disabled accordingly, based on the filled-out fields.
