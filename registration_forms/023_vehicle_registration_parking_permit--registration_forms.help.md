# Vehicle Registration Parking Permit - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a vehicle for parking purposes, providing essential information about the vehicle, its owner, and their contact details. This form will help create a parking permit for the registered vehicle.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the type of vehicle you are registering (Car, Motorcycle, Bus, or Truck).
2. Enter the make of your vehicle.
3. Enter the model of your vehicle.
4. Enter the color of your vehicle.
5. Enter the plate number of your vehicle.
6. Enter your student ID (if applicable).
7. Enter your parent's name.
8. Enter your parent's email address.
9. Enter your parent's phone number.
10. Enter the year of your vehicle (if known).
11. Enter the make year of your vehicle (if known).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vehicle Type** (`vehicle_type`, select_one): Select the type of vehicle you are registering (Car, Motorcycle, Bus, or Truck).
* **Vehicle Make** (`make`, text): Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Model** (`model`, text): Enter the model of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Color** (`color`, text): Enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Plate Number** (`plate_number`, text): Enter the plate number of your vehicle.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text): Enter your student ID (if applicable).
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text): Enter your parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email): Enter your parent's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text): Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, text): Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Make Year** (`vehicle_make_year`, number): Enter the make year of your vehicle (if known).

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately to avoid delays in processing.
* If applicable, enter your student ID to ensure correct registration.
* Enter your parent's contact information for easy communication.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your vehicle's documentation for correct information.
