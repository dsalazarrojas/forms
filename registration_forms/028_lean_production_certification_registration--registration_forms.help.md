## Lean Production Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lean Production Certification Registration form is used for participants to register themselves for a Lean Production Certification program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the Participant Details field, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Indicate your level of work experience in the Participant Experience field.
3. Select your preferred sessions for the certification program from the dropdown menu in the Preferred Sessions field.
4. Enter any certification details in the Certification Details field.
5. Enter information about your training provider in the Training Provider Info field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (Text, Required): Enter your full name and any other relevant details to help us get to know you better.
* **Participant Experience** (Number, Optional): Please indicate your level of work experience, e.g., "1-5 years", "5-10 years", etc.
* **Preferred Sessions** (Select Multiple, Required): Select up to 3 sessions that you would like to attend for the certification program.
* **Certification Details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional details about your certification, e.g., certification name, date, etc.
* **Training Provider Info** (Text, Optional): Enter information about your training provider, e.g., name, contact details, etc.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you only submit this form once to avoid duplication issues.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
