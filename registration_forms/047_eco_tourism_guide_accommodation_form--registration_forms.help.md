# eco_tourism_guide_accommodation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The eco_tourism_guide_accommodation_form is a user-facing form designed to register and gather information about eco-tourism guide accommodations. This form is part of the overall eco-tourism guide registration process, intended for users to provide their own information about accommodations they provide in their guide.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Select one of the options for fields that have multiple options, such as "accommodation_type" and "room_type".
3. Leave blank fields if you don't have any information to provide for that field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Information (page 1)
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and other relevant personal information.
### Accommodation Name (page 2)
* **accommodation_name** (`accommodation_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the accommodation, e.g., "Eco-Lodge XYZ".
### Accommodation Type (page 3)
* **accommodation_type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Hotel
	+ Lodge
	+ Camp
	+ Eco-lodge
	+ Other
### Accommodation Rating (page 4)
* **accommodation_rating** (`accommodation_rating`, number, optional): Enter a rating for your accommodation (e.g., 1-5 stars).
### Checkin Date (page 5)
* **Checkin date** (`checkin_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available for checkin.
### Checkout Date (page 6)
* **Checkout date** (`checkout_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are available for checkout.
### Room Type (page 7)
* **Room type** (`room_type`, select_one, optional): Select one of the following:
	+ Standard
	+ Deluxe
	+ Suite
### Address (page 8)
* **Address** (`accommodation_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your accommodation.
### Email (page 9)
* **email** (`accommodation_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for communication.
### Phone (page 10)
* **phone** (`accommodation_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
### Additional Notes (page 11)
* **Additional notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional information about your accommodation.
### Assigned Tool (page 12)
* **assigned_tool** (`assigned_tool_user`, text, optional): Not applicable for this form.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about a field, leave it blank and ask for help if needed.
* This form is part of the overall eco-tourism guide registration process.
