# Saving And Investment Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees interested in participating in a seminar about saving and investment strategies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and email address to provide contact information.
2. Enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
3. Select one or multiple investment goals that interest you (e.g., Savings, Investment, Wealth creation, Financial planning, Retirement planning).
4. Choose the seminar date and time that best suit your schedule.
5. Enter the seminar location (e.g., online platform, physical location).
6. Select the seminar duration (e.g., half-day, full-day).
7. Enter any additional comments or questions you might have about the seminar.
8. Confirm your registration status (Yes or No).
9. If attending, enter the seminar fees and confirm the payment status (Not paid or Paid).
10. Enter your preferred payment method and payment date.
11. If attending, enter the payment amount.
12. Enter the name of the seminar facilitator, coordinator, and admin.
13. Enter the seminar registration date.
14. Leave the "Created at" field empty as it's automatically generated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Details**: This section is for general seminar information.
	+ **Name**: Enter your name for our records.
	+ **Email**: Enter your email address to receive communications about the seminar.
* **Name**: This field is for personal information, please enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive communications.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number (optional).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (optional).
* **Address**: Enter your physical address (optional).
* **Investment Goals**: Select one or multiple goals that interest you (e.g., Savings, Investment, Wealth creation, Financial planning, Retirement planning).
* **Seminar Date**: Choose the seminar date that suits your schedule.
* **Seminar Time**: Choose the seminar time that suits your schedule.
* **Seminar Location**: Enter the seminar location (e.g., online platform, physical location).
* **Seminar Duration**: Select the seminar duration (e.g., half-day, full-day).
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or questions you might have about the seminar.
* **Confirm Registration**: Confirm if you are interested in attending the seminar (Yes or No).
* **Seminar Fees**: If attending, enter the seminar fees.
* **Registration Status**: Confirm your registration status (Not registered or Registered).
* **Registration Code**: Enter the registration code (if provided).
* **Payment Status**: Confirm your payment status (Not paid or Paid).
* **Payment Method**: Enter your preferred payment method.
* **Payment Date**: Enter the payment date (if applicable).
* **Payment Amount**: If attending, enter the payment amount.
* **Seminar Facilitator**: Enter the name of the seminar facilitator.
* **Seminar Coordinator**: Enter the name of the seminar coordinator.
* **Seminar Admin**: Enter the name of the seminar admin.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the seminar.
* **Created at**: This field is automatically generated and should be left empty.
