# Election Ballot Delivery Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to track the delivery of election ballots, including voter information, ballot status, and delivery details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to understand its purpose and the information required.
2. Select the relevant options for "Election Forms" and "Event Registration Forms" according to your situation.
3. Enter the voter's name in the "Voter Name" field.
4. Enter the voter's email address in the "Voter Email" field.
5. Enter the voter's phone number in the "Voter Phone" field.
6. Enter the ballot ID in the "Ballot ID" field.
7. Choose the delivery method (Mail, Email, or In Person) in the "Delivery Method" field.
8. Mark the delivery status as "Delivered" or "Not Delivered" in the "Delivery Status" field.
9. Enter the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
10. Enter the delivery time in the "Delivery Time" field.
11. Provide any additional comments or remarks in the "Remarks" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Election Forms** (`registration_forms`, select_multiple): Select all applicable forms related to election from the dropdown list.
* **Event Registration Forms** (`event_forms`, select_one): Select the specific event registration form related to the ballot delivery.
* **Category 1** (`category_1`, text): Enter any additional information or category details if necessary.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, text): Enter any additional information or category details if necessary.
* **Voter Name** (`voter_name`, text): Enter the name of the voter who received the ballot.
* **Voter Email** (`voter_email`, email): Enter the email address of the voter.
* **Voter Phone** (`voter_phone`, text): Enter the phone number of the voter.
* **Ballot ID** (`ballot_id`, text): Enter the unique ID of the ballot.
* **Delivery Method** (`delivery_method`, select_one): Choose how the ballot was delivered (Mail, Email, or In Person).
* **Delivery Status** (`delivery_status`, select_one): Mark the delivery status as "Delivered" or "Not Delivered".
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date): Enter the date when the ballot was delivered.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time): Enter the time when the ballot was delivered.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`, note): Provide any additional comments or remarks related to the ballot delivery.
