# Fireside Chat Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fireside Chat Registration form is designed to collect demographic information and preferences from users interested in attending a fireside chat event. The form is used to gauge user engagement levels, topic interest, and social media follow-up preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Fireside Chat Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your valid Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Choose one or more topics of interest from the "Topic of Interest" dropdown menu.
5. Select your engagement level with the event from the "Engagement Level" dropdown menu.
6. Confirm if you can attend early or late from the "Can Attend Early" and "Can Attend Late" dropdown menus.
7. Optionally, provide any additional comments about your preferences in the "Comment" field.
8. If you wish to receive updates via social media, select the "Follow on Social Media" options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name to help us better understand your preferences.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your valid last name to help us verify your identity.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Topic of Interest** (Select Multiple): Choose one or more topics that interest you, such as Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, or Data Science.
* **Engagement Level** (Select One): Indicate your level of engagement with the event, from Not Engaged to Extremely Engaged.
* **Can Attend Early** (Select One): Confirm if you can attend the event early or not.
* **Can Attend Late** (Select One): Confirm if you can attend the event late or not.
* **Comment** (Text Area): Optionally, provide any additional comments about your preferences.
* **Agree to Terms** (Select Multiple): If you wish to receive updates and notifications, select "Yes" to agree to terms.
* **Follow on Social Media** (Select Multiple): Choose to receive updates and notifications from our social media channels.
