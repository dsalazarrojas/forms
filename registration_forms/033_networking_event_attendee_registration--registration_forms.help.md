# networking_event_attendee_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to register and provide information for a networking event. It collects details such as attendee preferences, contact information, and payment methods to help event organizers manage and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration form data as prompted.
2. Provide your attendee details, including any dietary preferences and special needs.
3. Indicate your interests and availability.
4. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
5. Confirm your registration and provide any additional information or payment details as required.
6. Sign and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form Data**: This field is where you enter your registration information.
	+ Type in the required information as prompted.
* **Attendee Details**: Enter your name, title, and any other relevant details about yourself.
	+ Be sure to include any dietary preferences and special needs.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your contact email and phone number.
	+ This information will be used to contact you about the event.
* **Dietary Preferences**: Select your dietary preferences from the options provided.
	+ This will help us accommodate your needs during the event.
* **Special Needs**: Enter any special needs or requirements you may have.
	+ This will help us prepare for your needs during the event.
* **Interests**: Select the areas of interest to you from the provided options.
	+ This will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Availability**: Enter your availability for the event.
	+ This will help us schedule your activities during the event.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
	+ This is where we will contact you about the event.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
	+ This is optional, but will help us contact you if needed.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information or notes about yourself.
	+ This will help us understand your needs and preferences.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information about yourself.
	+ This is optional, but will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Payment Method**: Enter your payment method (e.g. credit card, cash).
	+ This is where you enter your payment details.
* **Payment Info**: Enter any additional payment information.
	+ This is optional, but will help us process your payment.
* **Payment Details**: Enter any additional payment details.
	+ This is optional, but will help us process your payment.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm your registration.
	+ Please select "True" to confirm your registration.
* **Confirmation Message**: Enter a confirmation message.
	+ This will help us verify your registration.
* **Signature**: Enter your signature.
	+ This is required for verification purposes.
* **Signature Type**: Select your signature type.
	+ This will determine the format of your signature.
* **Signature Image**: Upload or enter your signature image.
	+ This is optional, but will help us verify your signature.
* **Signature Text**: Enter your signature text.
	+ This is optional, but will help us verify your signature.
* **Attendee Type**: Select your attendee type.
	+ This will help us categorize you for the event.
* **Form Submit**: Enter your form submission confirmation.
	+ This is where you confirm you have finished filling out the form.
