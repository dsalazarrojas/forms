# Time Management In Professional Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect participant information for a professional training program, which will help trainers tailor the training experience to the participants' needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are in a quiet and distraction-free environment.
2. Review the form carefully and fill it out accurately.
3. If you need help with any field, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Data** (`participant_data`): This field collects basic information about you as a participant.
  * This field is not required.
  * Please enter your name, address, and other relevant details.
- **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): This field is for your name.
  * Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
  * This field is not required.
- **Email** (`email`): This field is for your email address.
  * Please enter a valid email address that can be used to contact you.
  * This field is not required.
- **Phone** (`phone`): This field is for your phone number.
  * Please enter your phone number in the standard format (XXX-XXX-XXXX).
  * This field is not required.
- **Training Date** (`training_date`): This field is for the date of the training.
  * Please enter the date of the training in the standard format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  * This field is not required.
- **Training Time** (`training_time`): This field is for the time of the training.
  * Please enter the time of the training in the standard format (HH:MM).
  * This field is not required.
- **Course Name** (`course_name`): This field is for the name of the course.
  * Please enter the name of the course you are interested in.
  * This field is not required.
- **Course Description** (`course_description`): This field is for a brief description of the course.
  * Please enter a brief description of the course.
  * This field is not required.
- **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`): This field is for any additional information about you.
  * Please enter any relevant notes or comments.
  * This field is not required.
- **Time Management Skills** (`time_management_skills`): This field is for selecting your level of time management skills.
  * Please select your level of time management skills from the options: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced.
  * This field is not required.
- **Training Hours** (`training_hours`): This field is for the number of training hours you require.
  * Please enter the number of training hours you need.
  * This field is not required.
- **Training Hours per Week** (`training_hours_per_week`): This field is for the number of training hours you need per week.
  * Please enter the number of training hours you need per week.
  * This field is not required.
- **Course Duration** (`course_duration`): This field is for the duration of the course.
  * Please enter the duration of the course in weeks.
  * This field is not required.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or issues, please contact the trainers.
- The form will be reviewed and confirmed after submission.
