# Railway Lodge Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to provide accommodation information for guests arriving at railway stations. Please fill out this form to provide essential details about your stay.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the check-in and check-out dates for your stay.
2. Choose your preferred check-in time.
3. Select the room type that suits your needs (Room 1, Room 2, or Room 3).
4. Indicate how many adults and children will be staying with you.
5. If applicable, specify the age of the children.
6. Select any special requirements you may have (Wheel Chair Access, Hearing Impaired, or Other).
7. Choose your preferred language (English, French, or Spanish).
8. Enter your contact email address.
9. Provide your contact phone number.
10. Write a brief message with any additional information about your stay.
11. (Optional) If you have any specific requests or requirements, use the "Note" field to provide more details.
12. If applicable, specify any tools you may need to be assigned to you (e.g., wheelchair).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check-in date** (`check_in_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to arrive at the railway station.
* **Check-in time** (`check_in_time`, time, required/optional): Choose the time you would like to arrive at the railway station.
* **Check-out date** (`check_out_date`, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to leave the railway station.
* **Room Preference** (`room_preference`, select_one, required/optional): Choose the room type that suits your needs (Room 1, Room 2, or Room 3).
* **Adult quantity** (`adult_quantity`, number, required/optional): Indicate how many adults will be staying with you.
* **Child quantity** (`child_quantity`, number, required/optional): Indicate how many children will be staying with you.
* **Child age** (`child_age`, number, required/optional): If children are accompanying you, please specify their age.
* **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you have any special requirements (e.g., Wheel Chair Access, Hearing Impaired), select them here.
* **Preferred language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred language for communication (English, French, or Spanish).
* **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **Contact phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required/optional): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Contact message** (`contact_message`, text, required/optional): Write a brief message with any additional information about your stay.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required/optional): (Optional) If you have any specific requests or requirements, use this field to provide more details.
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): If applicable, specify any tools you may need to be assigned to you (e.g., wheelchair).
* **Form Ids** (`form_ids`, text, required/optional): This field is for internal use only and does not require input.
