# sailing_club_membership_admission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for new members to register for membership in the sailing club. It collects information about the member's new and contact information, sailing experience and frequency, boat details, and medical information. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the "New Member Information" page with your general details.
2. Enter your "Contact Information", including your emergency contact details.
3. Provide information about your "Address Book" and "Boat Details".
4. Select your "Member Type" from the options provided.
5. Enter your "Sailing Experience" and "Sailing Frequency" details.
6. Choose your "Sailing Type" from the options provided.
7. Add any "Emergency Contact" and "Emergency Phone" information if necessary.
8. Fill in any "Medical Information" and "Medical Details" if required.
9. Specify any "Allergies" if needed.
10. Enter any "Medical Certifications".
11. Choose your "Membership Type" from the options provided.
12. Specify your "Member Status" and "Member Since" details if applicable.
13. Select your "Membership Status" from the options provided.
14. Enter the "Membership Expiration" date.
15. Enter "Boat License" and "Boat Insurance" details.
16. Enter the "Membership Dues".
17. Enter the "Membership Expiration Date".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **New Member Information**

*   New Member Information (<type>: text): Enter your name and any other relevant details. (required: false)

### 2. **Contact Information**

*   Contact Information (<type>: text): Provide your contact details including phone number. (required: false)

*   Address Book (<type>: text): Enter your address. (required: false)

### 7. **Sailing Frequency**

*   Sailing Frequency (<type>: number): Enter how often you sail. (required: false)

### 8. **Sailing Type**

### 9. **Emergency Contact**

### 10. **Emergency Phone**

### 11. **Emergency Address**

*   Emergency Address (<type>: text): Enter emergency address. (required: false)

### 12. **Medical Information**

### 13. **Medical Details**

### 14. **Allergies**
