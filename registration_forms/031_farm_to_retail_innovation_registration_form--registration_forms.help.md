# Farm To Retail Innovation Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Farm To Retail Innovation Registration Form is a tool used to gather information about innovative projects or products related to agriculture or food production. The form is used to collect contact and project details from users, including email, name, organization, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Start Form" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which is used as the primary contact method.
3. Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
4. Provide your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. Select the type of challenge or program your innovation is related to (e.g., Challenge, Accelerator, Program).
7. Choose the location of your innovation (e.g., National, Regional, International).
8. Enter the start and end dates of your project or initiative.
9. Select the type of collaboration (e.g., Partnership, Collaboration).
10. Enter the agrifood sector your innovation is related to.
11. Describe your product or innovation in the "Product Description" field.
12. Choose the type of innovation (e.g., Product, Service, Technology).
13. Enter the current status of your innovation.
14. Provide your physical address in the "Address" field.
15. Choose whether you have a country or region of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
16. Choose the region of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
17. Choose the city of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
18. Choose the state of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
19. Choose the zip code of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
20. Enter the name of the contact person.
21. Enter their phone number.
22. Enter their email address.
23. Enter their address.
24. Upload any files related to your innovation.
24. Review and submit your form once you have filled out all required and optional fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **1. Email** (email, optional): This is the primary contact method used to communicate with you regarding your innovation.
* **2. Name** (text, optional): Your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
* **3. Organization** (text, optional): The name of your organization.
* **4. Phone** (text, optional): Your phone number.
* **5. Challenge Type** (select_one, optional): The type of challenge or program your innovation is related to.
* **6. Location** (select_multiple, optional): The location where your innovation is based.
* **7. Start Date** (date, optional): The start date of your project or initiative.
* **8. End Date** (date, optional): The end date of your project or initiative.
* **9. Collaboration Programs** (select_one, optional): The type of collaboration (e.g., Partnership, Collaboration).
* **10. Agrifood Sector** (text, optional): The sector your innovation is related to (e.g., agriculture, food production).
* **11. Product Description** (text, optional): A brief description of your product or innovation.
* **12. Innovation Type** (select_multiple, optional): The type of innovation (e.g., Product, Service, Technology).
* **13. Innovation Status** (text, optional): The current status of your innovation.
* **14. Address** (text, optional): Your physical address.
* **15. Country** (select_one, optional): Whether you have a country of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
* **16. Region** (select_one, optional): Whether you have a region of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
* **17. City** (select_one, optional): Whether you have a city of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
* **18. State** (select_one, optional): Whether you have a state of operation (e.g., Yes, No).
* **19. Zip Code** (select_multiple, optional): The zip code of your operation (e.g., Yes, No).
* **20. Contact Person** (text, optional): The name of the contact person.
* **21. Contact Phone** (text, optional): The contact person's phone number.
* **22. Contact Email** (email, optional): The contact person's email address.
* **23. Contact Address** (text, optional): The contact person's address.
* **24. Innovation Story** (text, optional): A brief description of your innovation story.
* **25. File Uploads** (note, optional): Uploading relevant files related to your innovation.
* 
Please note that some fields may have different options and requirements based on the selected values in other fields. Ensure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth submission process.
