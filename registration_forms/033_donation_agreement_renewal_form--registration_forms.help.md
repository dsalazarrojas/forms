# Donation Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow donors to update their existing donation agreements with our organization. It's essential to fill out this form to ensure that your agreement is accurately reflected in our records and that you receive any benefits or services associated with your agreement.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Donor Name and Address.
2. Enter your Organization Name (if applicable).
3. Select the new Agreement Start and End Dates (if applicable).
4. Choose the Renewal Status (New Agreement, Renewal, or Cancellation) and provide a brief explanation in the Renewal Reason field.
5. Add any additional Notes you'd like to include.
6. Click "Submit" to finalize your changes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Donor Name**: Enter your full name as the donor.
* **Donor Address**: Enter your address as the donor.
* **Organization Name**: If you're representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **Agreement Start Date**: If you're renewing or updating your agreement, enter the new start date.
* **Agreement End Date**: If you're renewing or updating your agreement, enter the new end date.
* **Renewal Status**: Choose from the options:
	+ New Agreement: This is for new agreements or agreements that are starting on a new date.
	+ Renewal: This is for agreements that are being renewed or updated.
	+ Cancellation: This is for agreements that need to be cancelled.
* **Renewal Reason**: If you chose "Renewal" or "Cancellation", provide a brief explanation for your selection.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or information you'd like to include with your agreement update.
