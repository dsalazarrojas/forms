# treasury_innovation_forum_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Innovation Forum registration form is used to collect information from attendees, including their contact details, event preferences, and other relevant information. This form is a prerequisite for attending the forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with the required information.
3. Review and submit the form once you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Information** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, **required: false**): Please enter a brief description of yourself as an attendee.
* **attendee_name** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, **required: false**): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **email** (`id: 3`, `type: email`, **required: false**): Enter your valid email address.
* **phone** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, **required: false**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment** (`id: 5`, `type: select_multiple`, **required: false**): Select one or more payment options for the event.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **event** (`id: 6`, `type: select_one`, **required: false**): Choose the event you want to attend.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **time** (`id: 7`, `type: time`, **required: false`): Enter the time you plan to attend.
* **date** (`id: 8`, `type: date`, **required: false`): Enter the date you plan to attend.
* **note** (`id: 9`, `type: note`, **required: false`): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure accurate registration.
* Review and submit the form carefully before hitting the "Submit" button.
* If you encounter any issues, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
