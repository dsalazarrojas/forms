# Parental Permission Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from parents or guardians of students for a particular activity or event. It's essential for the school administration to have this information to ensure the student's safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the activity name in the "What is the name of the activity?" field.
2. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Enter the parent's name in the "Parent Name" field.
4. Enter the parent's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select a valid date for the activity in the "Date" field.
6. Enter the activity's time in the "Time" field.
7. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field.
9. Check the box for "Do you give consent?" to indicate whether you give consent or not.
10. Enter any additional comments or explanations in the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is the name of the activity?** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the activity or event for which you are giving permission for your child to participate.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the parent or guardian of the student.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if there are any issues with your child's participation.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a valid date for the activity.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a valid time for the activity.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your child's participation.
* **Do you give consent?** (`consent`, select_one, required): Check the box to indicate whether you give consent for your child to participate in the activity.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or explanations about your child's participation.
