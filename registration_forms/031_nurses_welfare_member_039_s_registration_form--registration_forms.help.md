# Nurses Welfare Member's Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from nurses who wish to become members of the Nurses Welfare organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form Data** (Page 1, id: 1)
	+ This section captures the basic information about you, including your name and contact details.
* **Member Details** (Page 1, id: 2)
	+ This section captures additional details about you, including your medical degree and specialty.
* **Contact Details** (Page 1, id: 3)
	+ This section captures your contact information, including your address and phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (Page 2, id: 4)
	+ This section captures the contact information of your emergency contact person.
* **Member Profile Picture** (Page 2, id: 5)
	+ This section allows you to upload your profile picture.
* **Membership Type** (Page 2, id: 6)
	+ This section captures your membership type, which determines your benefits and privileges within the organization.
* **Membership Status** (Page 2, id: 7)
	+ This section captures your membership status, which determines your eligibility for certain benefits.
* **Membership Expiration Date** (Page 2, id: 8)
	+ This section captures the date your membership expires.
* **Membership Renewal Date** (Page 3, id: 9)
	+ This section captures the date you last renewed your membership.
* **Membership Renewal Fee** (Page 3, id: 10)
	+ This section captures the fee associated with renewing your membership.
* **Contact Preference** (Page 3, id: 11)
	+ This section captures your contact preference, including email, phone, and SMS notifications.
* **Medical License Details** (Page 3, id: 12)
	+ This section captures your medical license details, including any relevant certifications or licenses.
* **Medical Degree** (Page 4, id: 13)
	+ This section captures your medical degree, including any specializations or concentrations.
* **Medical Specialty** (Page 4, id: 14)
	+ This section captures your medical specialty.
* **Work Experience** (Page 4, id: 15)
	+ This section captures your work experience, including any relevant certifications or training.
* **Work History** (Page 5, id: 16)
	+ This section captures your work history, including any relevant experience or qualifications.
* **Professional Membership** (Page 5, id: 17)
	+ This section captures your professional membership, including any relevant affiliations or memberships.
* **Membership Category** (Page 5, id: 18)
	+ This section captures your membership category, which determines your benefits and privileges within the organization.
* **Membership Group** (Page 6, id: 19)
	+ This section captures your membership group, which determines your eligibility for certain benefits.
* **Membership Status** (Page 6, id: 20)
	+ This section captures your membership status, which determines your eligibility for certain benefits.
* **Work Place** (Page 6, id: 21)
	+ This section captures your work place, including any relevant addresses or locations.
* **Work Position** (Page 7, id: 22)
	+ This section captures your work position, including any relevant titles or roles.
* **Work Start Date** (Page 7, id: 23)
	+ This section captures the date you started your work.
* **Work End Date** (Page 7, id: 24)
	+ This section captures the date you ended your work.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (Page 7, id: 25)
	+ This section captures the contact information of your emergency contact person, including their name and contact details.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the Nurses Welfare organization for assistance.
