# Baptist Church Youth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Baptist Church Youth Registration Form. This form is used to collect essential information about the youth participant to provide accurate and safe services. Please answer each question honestly and accurately to help us better serve you and your family.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out each field according to the instructions below.
2. Ensure all fields are completed as required.
3. Double-check the form for any mistakes before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): This field is where you enter the date of your registration for our youth programs.
* **Youth First Name** (`youth_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your youth's first name here.
* **Youth Last Name** (`youth_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your youth's last name here.
* **Date of Birth** (`youth_dob`, `date`, required): Enter your youth's date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YY).
* **Current Age** (`youth_age`, `number`, required): Enter your youth's current age.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your youth's current school grade level from the list.
* **Youth Email (if available)** (`youth_email`, `email`, optional): If your youth has an email address, enter it here.
* **Youth Phone (if available)** (`youth_phone`, `text`, optional): If your youth has a phone number, enter it here.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name here as the parent or guardian of the youth.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address here.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number here.
* **Secondary Contact Name** (`secondary_contact_name`, `text`, optional): If you're unavailable, provide a secondary contact name.
* **Secondary Contact Phone** (`secondary_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the secondary contact.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your youth's residential address here.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state of residence.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code here.
* **Youth Activities of Interest** (`activity_interest`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the activities your youth is interested in participating in.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If your youth has any dietary restrictions or allergies, enter them here.
* **Medical Conditions or Medications** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions or medication your youth is taking.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`, `text`, optional): If your youth has any special needs or accommodations, enter them here.
* **Baptism Status** (`baptism_status`, `select_one`, optional): Choose whether your youth has been baptized or not.
* **I give permission for youth to participate in church activities** (`parental_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you give permission for your youth to participate in church activities.
* **I authorize emergency medical treatment if necessary** (`emergency_authorization`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you authorize emergency medical treatment if necessary.
