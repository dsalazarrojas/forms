# Brigade Callout Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for brigade members to register for callout duties. It collects important information about your availability, qualifications, and contact details to ensure that you are properly assigned to tasks and emergencies. Please review the form carefully and ensure your input is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your member information: Enter your full name, member ID number, email address, and primary phone number to create your profile.
2. Select your role and unit: Choose your brigade unit and select your current role within the brigade.
3. Enter your availability: Select your on-call status and primary response area to determine your availability for callouts.
4. Provide emergency contact information: Enter the name, relationship, and phone number of your emergency contact to ensure we can reach them in case of an emergency.
5. Add any additional notes or medical conditions: Enter any additional information that may impact your duty or performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, text, required): Enter your full legal name as it appears on your ID.
* **Member ID Number** (`Member ID Number`, text, required): Enter your unique member ID number issued by the brigade.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for official communications.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`Primary Phone Number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
* **Secondary Phone Number** (`Secondary Phone Number`, text, optional): Enter an alternate contact number (if applicable).
* **Home Address** (`Home Address`, text, required): Enter your residential address.
* **Brigade Unit** (`Brigade Unit`, select_one, required): Select your assigned unit within the brigade.
* **Current Role** (`Current Role`, select_one, required): Select your current role within the brigade (e.g. Firefighter, Team Leader, Driver, etc.).
* **Years of Service** (`Years of Service`, number, required): Enter your total years of service with the brigade.
* **Certifications Held** (`Certifications Held`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant certifications you hold (e.g. First Aid, CPR, etc.).
* **On-Call Status** (`On-Call Status`, select_one, required): Select your current on-call availability status.
* **Primary Response Area** (`Primary Response Area`, select_one, required): Select your preferred response zone.
* **Estimated Response Time (minutes)** (`Estimated Response Time (minutes)`, number, required): Enter your estimated travel time to the station.
* **Blackout Dates or Unavailable Periods** (`Blackout Dates or Unavailable Periods`, text, optional): List any dates or periods you will be unavailable.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact** (`Relationship to Emergency Contact`, text, required): Enter your relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Limitations** (`Medical Conditions or Limitations`, text, optional): Describe any medical conditions or limitations that may affect your duty.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
