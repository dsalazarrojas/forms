# Pediatric Rehabilitation Course Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is a registration form for pediatric rehabilitation courses. It collects information from parents or guardians of children undergoing rehabilitation courses.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select whether you would like to attend a session or not.
2. Choose a date for the session if you would like to attend.
3. Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
4. Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
5. If you consent, enter your email address to receive updates and communications.
6. Enter the name of the course you are registering for, if applicable.
7. Choose when you would like to attend the session, if you have chosen to attend.
8. Consent to receiving phone calls from the system, if you have chosen to attend a session.
9. Consent to receiving SMS messages from the system, if you have chosen to attend a session.
10. Review your entries and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Session**: Choose whether you would like to attend a session. If you select "Yes", you can choose a date for the session.
* **Session Date**: Select a date for the session, if you have chosen to attend.
* **Parent Name**: Enter your name as a parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for communication purposes.
* **Consent to Email**: If you consent to receiving emails from the system, enter your email address here.
* **Parent Email**: (Optional) Enter your email address if you would like to receive updates and communications.
* **Course Name**: (Optional) Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Session Time**: If you have chosen to attend a session, choose when you would like to attend.
* **Consent to Phone**: If you have chosen to attend a session, consent to receiving phone calls from the system.
* **Consent to SMS**: If you have chosen to attend a session, consent to receiving SMS messages from the system.
* **Submit**: Review your entries and submit the form to complete your registration.
