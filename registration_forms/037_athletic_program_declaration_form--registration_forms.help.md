# Athletic Program Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to declare participation in athletic programs and gather relevant information about student athletes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "Required" in each section.
2. Ensure that the format for each field is correct, such as the MM/DD/YYYY format for dates.
3. Be accurate when filling out your personal details and medical conditions.
4. Select all that apply for multiple selection fields.
5. Double-check your form before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Athlete Full Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): This is the full name of the student athlete.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This is the student's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, `text`, required): This is the student's school identification number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the school the student attends.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the current grade level of the student.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, `text`, required): This is the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, `text`, required): This is the contact phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, `email`, required): This is the contact email of the parent or guardian.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): This is the home address of the student.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): This is the city of residence of the student.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): This is the state or province of residence of the student.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip_code`, `text`, required): This is the ZIP or postal code of the student's residence.
* **Athletic Program Declaration** (`program_declaration`, `select_one`, required): Select the athletic program the student is declaring for.
* **Season** (`season`, `select_one`, required): Select the season of participation.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `text`, required): This is the academic year of the student.
* **Have You Participated Before?** (`previous_participation`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of previous participation.
* **Other Sports Participating In** (`other_sports`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all other sports the student participates in.
* **Level of Commitment** (`commitment_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of commitment to the program.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required): List any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): List any current medications.
* **Health Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): This is the name of the health insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number** (`insurance_policy_number`, `text`, required): This is the policy or member number of the insurance provider.
* **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the regular doctor.
* **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, `text`, required): This is the contact number of the doctor.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): This is the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): This is the emergency contact number.
* **Transportation Plan** (`transportation_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select the transportation plan.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement** (`code_of_conduct`, `select_one`, required): Select the agreement to follow the athletic code of conduct.
* **Academic Eligibility Confirmation** (`academic_eligibility`, `select_one`, required): Select the confirmation of academic eligibility.
* **Assumption of Risk** (`assumption_of_risk`, `select_one`, required): Select the understanding and acceptance of the risks of athletic participation.
* **Media Release** (`media_release`, `select_one`, required): Select the permission for photos and media coverage.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, `text`, required): Type the full name as signature of the student.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature** (`parent_guardian_signature`, `text`, required): Type the full name as signature of the parent or guardian.
* **Signature Date** (`signature_date`, `date`, required): This is the date of the signature.

## Tips

* Ensure that you double-check your form before submission.
* Be accurate when filling out personal details and medical conditions.
* Select all that apply for multiple selection fields.
* Use the correct format for dates and phone numbers.
