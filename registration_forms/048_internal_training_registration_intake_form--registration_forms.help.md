# Internal Training Registration Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed for internal training registration and intake. It's intended for employees who want to register for training sessions within the company.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you've provided the correct information.
3. Click the "Submit" button to send your registration request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name, as this will be used to identify you in the training session.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name, as this will be used to identify you in the training session.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address. This is how we'll contact you regarding your training session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you want to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number here. This is optional.
* **Training Course** (`training_course`, select_one, required): Choose the training course you're interested in. Please select 'Yes' if you want to register for this course.
* **Date of Training** (`date_of_training`, date, required): Enter the date you'd like to attend the training session.
* **Time of Training** (`time_of_training`, time, required): Enter the time you'd like to attend the training session.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_multiple, required): Select the training location(s) you'd like to attend. Please select 'Yes' for each location you want to attend.
* **Trainer Name** (`trainer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the trainer you'd like to have for this training session.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your training session.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm your registration by selecting 'Yes'. If you don't confirm, your registration will not be processed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you've already registered for a training session, use the same contact information to avoid duplication.
