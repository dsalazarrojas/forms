# startup_exhibition_booth_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The startup exhibition booth registration form is designed to collect information from startups interested in exhibiting at an exhibition. This form gathers necessary details to manage exhibitor registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your **Registration Info**.
2. Enter your **Contact Details**.
3. Specify your **Booth Preferences**.
4. Add any **Extra Details** you'd like to share.
5. Review and agree with the **Terms and Conditions**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Info
* **Registration Info** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your company name, exhibition name, or any other relevant registration information.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`2`, `text`, required): Provide your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.

### Booth Preferences
* **Booth Preferences** (`3`, `text`, required): Tell us about your booth design, number of attendees, and any special requests you might have for your exhibition space.

### Extra Details
* **Extra Details** (`4`, `text`, required): Share any additional information about your startup that we might find useful for your exhibition appearance.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`5`, `text`, required): Review and agree to our terms and conditions to ensure a smooth exhibition experience.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any issues with your registration.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
