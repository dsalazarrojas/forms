> # Volleyball_Tournament_Registration_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from teams and players participating in the volleyball tournament. It will ask for details such as team name, team members, player names, email, phone numbers, and more. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the team name in the "Team Name" field.
2. For "Team Members", select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if the team members are participating in the tournament.
3. Enter the player name(s) for each member of your team in the "Player Name" field.
4. Enter the player email address in the "Player Email" field.
5. Enter the player phone number in the "Player Phone" field.
6. Specify the team's tournament experience level in the "Team Tournament Experience" field.
7. Choose the team's division from the "Division" field.
8. Select the team's age group from the "Team Age Group" field.
9. Enter the team's age range in the "Team Age Range" field (if applicable).
10. Enter the player's age in the "Player Age" field.
11. Enter the player's height in the "Player Height" field.
12. Enter the player's weight in the "Player Weight" field.
13. Select the player's shirt size from the "Player Shirt Size" field.
14. Choose the player's position(s) from the "Player Position" field.
15. Enter the team's address in the "Team Address" field.
16. Enter the team's phone number in the "Team Phone" field.
17. Enter the team's email address in the "Team Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your team to identify it in the tournament.
* **Team Members** (select_multiple, required): Indicate if team members are participating in the tournament.
* **Player Name** (text, required): Enter the name of each player on your team.
* **Player Email** (email, required): Enter the email address of each player.
* **Player Phone** (text, required): Enter the phone number of each player.
* **Team Tournament Experience** (text, required): Describe the team's tournament experience level.
* **Team Tournament Level** (select_one, required): Choose the team's tournament level (Team A, Team B, Team C).
* **Division** (select_one, required): Choose the team's division (A, B, C).
* **Team Age Group** (select_one, required): Choose the team's age group (Adult, Youth).
* **Team Age Range** (text, required): Enter the team's age range (if applicable).
* **Player Age** (number, required): Enter the player's age.
* **Player Height** (number, required): Enter the player's height.
* **Player Weight** (number, required): Enter the player's weight.
* **Player Shirt Size** (select_one, required): Choose the player's shirt size (S, M, L, XL).
* **Player Position** (select_multiple, required): Choose the player's position(s) (Setter, Libero, Opposite, Middle Blocker, Outside Hitter).
* **Team Address** (text, required): Enter the team's address.
* **Team Phone** (text, required): Enter the team's phone number.
* **Team Email** (email, required): Enter the team's email address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid any issues with registration.
- Review the team and player information carefully before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the tournament organizers for assistance.
