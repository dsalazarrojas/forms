# Smart Returns Optimization Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from businesses and organizations about their return volumes, business challenges, and other relevant details. This information will help us optimize returns processing and improve overall efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct **Return Volumes** from the dropdown list.
2. Choose the relevant **Business Challenges** that apply to your business.
3. If you have any **Business Details** that you'd like to share, please fill out the text field.
4. Enter your **Contact Email** and **Contact Phone** to ensure we can reach you for any further questions or clarifications.
5. Fill out the **Contact Name**, **Business Name**, and **Business Address** fields with your company's information.
6. Add your **Website** URL (if applicable).
7. Choose your **Time Zone** and **Business Hours**.
8. Finally, select the relevant **Business Days** when your business operates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Details** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Provide any additional details about your business that might be relevant to our team. This could include information about your business model, services, or other unique aspects.
* **Return Volumes** (<type>: select_multiple, <required>: false): Select the return volumes that apply to your business. This will help us understand your business's return patterns.
* **Business Challenges** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Choose the challenges your business faces related to returns. Select all that apply.
* **Business Details 2** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Provide any additional details about your business that might be relevant to our team.
* **Contact Email** (<type>: email, <required>: false): Enter your contact email address so we can reach you for any further questions or clarifications.
* **Contact Phone** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Name** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Enter the name of the person filling out this form.
* **Business Name** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Enter your business name.
* **Business Address** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Enter your business address.
* **Website** (<type>: text, <required>: false): Enter your business website URL.
* **Time Zone** (<type>: select_one, <required>: false): Choose your time zone.
* **Business Hours** (<type>: time, <required>: false): Enter your business hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Business Days** (<type>: select_multiple, <required>: false): Select the days your business operates.

## Tips

* Take your time to fill out this form accurately and carefully.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the support team for assistance.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure we can reach you effectively.
