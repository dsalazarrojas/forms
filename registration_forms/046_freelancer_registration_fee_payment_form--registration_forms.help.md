# Freelancer Registration Fee Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Freelancer Registration Fee Payment Form is designed for freelancers to register and make payment for their registration fees. This form helps you provide essential information to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Application Form" page.
2. Fill in the "Application Form" page with your information.
3. Proceed to the "Payment Details" page.
4. Choose your payment method and fill in the payment details.
5. Review the "Contact Information" and ensure your email and phone number are up-to-date.
6. Upload any required documents (if necessary).
7. Agree to the "Terms and Conditions".
8. Fill in your "Contact Information", "Address", "Payment Amount" and "Payment Method".
9. Choose your "Agreement Status" and "Payment Status".
10. Fill in "Bank Details" if you are using Bank Transfer as payment method.
11. Choose "Payment Date" and "Payment Time" for the payment.
12. Fill in your "Bank Account Number", "Bank Routing Number" and "Iban" if necessary.
13. Select "Payment Gateway".
14. Enter any "Payment Status Note" if necessary.
15. Fill in "Bank Transfer Date" and "Bank Transfer Time" if you are using Bank Transfer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Application Form**: Fill in your basic information such as name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Payment Details**: Select the payment method you will use, and fill in the corresponding details.
* **Agreement Form**: This is a mandatory form that you must agree to before proceeding.
* **Contact Information**: Ensure your email and phone number is up-to-date to receive updates.
* **Upload Documents**: If necessary, upload any required documents for your registration.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Agree to the terms and conditions of your registration.
* **Payment Amount**: Fill in the amount you are willing to pay for your registration.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method you will use (Card Number, Bank Transfer, or Online Payment).
* **Agreement Status**: Select your current agreement status (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Payment Status**: Select your current payment status (Pending, Success, or Failed).
* **Payment Date**: Fill in the date of your payment.
* **Payment Time**: Fill in the time of your payment.
* **Payment Currency**: Fill in the currency of your payment.
* **Bank Name**: Fill in the name of your bank if using Bank Transfer.
* **Bank Account Number**: Fill in your bank account number if using Bank Transfer.
* **Bank Routing Number**: Fill in your bank routing number if using Bank Transfer.
* **Iban**: Fill in your Iban if necessary.
* **Swift**: Fill in your Swift code if necessary.
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway you are using.
* **Payment Status Note**: Enter any additional information about your payment status.
* **Bank Transfer Date**: Fill in the date of your bank transfer if using Bank Transfer.
* **Bank Transfer Time**: Fill in the time of your bank transfer if using Bank Transfer.
