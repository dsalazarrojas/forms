# Housing Technology Incubator Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from applicants who want to join the Housing Technology Incubator. Please fill out the form to initiate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections and ensure you understand what each section requires.
2. Fill out the form with your details. You must fill out all required fields (marked as *required*).
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant info** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your name and other personal details.
* **Company info** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your company's name and other relevant details.
* **Project info** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your project's name and a brief description.
* **Innovation Program** (4, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of innovation program you are interested in (Incubator, Accelerator, Research center, Incubator-accelerator, or Other).
* **Start-up type** (5, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of start-up you have (Housing tech, Real estate, or Other).
* **Location** (6, text, required/optional): Enter the location of your start-up (city, country, etc.).
* **Contact name** (7, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your start-up.
* **Email** (8, email, required/optional): Enter the email address of the contact person for your start-up.
* **Phone** (9, text, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person for your start-up.
* **Description** (10, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your start-up project.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill out all required fields to avoid delays in the registration process.
* Double-check your email address for correctness before submitting.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
