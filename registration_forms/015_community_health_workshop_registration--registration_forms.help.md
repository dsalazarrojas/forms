# Community Health Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for a Community Health Workshop, a educational event focused on various health-related topics. It helps the workshop organizers collect necessary information from participants for efficient registration and event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Participant Full Name** under "Participant Full Name" field.
2. Select your **Age** from the provided options.
3. Enter your **Contact Email Address** under "Contact Email Address" field.
4. Choose your preferred **Preferred Workshop Session** from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, or Full Day Seminar).
5. Select your specific **Specific Health Interests** that you want to focus on from the available options (Chronic Disease Management, Mental Health and Wellness, Preventive Care, Nutritional Education, or Exercise and Fitness).
6. Indicate your current **Current Knowledge Level of Workshop Topic** from the available options (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
7. Confirm that you understand the workshop is for **Educational Purposes Only** by selecting "I Understand and Agree" from the options.
8. Agree to the **Release of Liability and Terms of Participation** by selecting "I Agree to Terms" from the options.
9. Type your **Electronic Signature** with your full name.
10. Select your preferred **Date of Registration** from the calendar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should be written on your registration.
* **Age**: Select your age group for workshop organization purposes.
* **Contact Email Address**: Enter your email address to contact you for further instructions or updates.
* **Preferred Workshop Session**: Choose when you'd like to attend the workshop. Please note that this will help us determine the correct time slot for your participation.
* **Specific Health Interests**: Select the topics you'd like to focus on during the workshop. This will help us tailor the content for your needs.
* **Current Knowledge Level of Workshop Topic**: Indicate your current understanding of the workshop's topic. This will help us ensure that you get the most out of your workshop experience.
* **I understand this workshop is for educational purposes only**: Please confirm that you understand the workshop is not a substitute for medical advice or treatment.
* **Release of Liability and Terms of Participation**: Confirm that you agree to our terms of participation, which can be found in our workshop's documentation.
* **Electronic Signature**: Type your full name as a digital representation of your signature.
* **Date of Registration**: Select the date you're registering for the workshop. This will help us track your registration and ensure a smooth check-in process.
