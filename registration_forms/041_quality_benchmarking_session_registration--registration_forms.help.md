# Quality_Benchmarking_Session_Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a Quality Benchmarking Session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Participant Details" section with your name, email, and contact information.
2. Select your preferred session times from the drop-down menu.
3. Choose your preferred session dates from the drop-down menu.
4. Indicate your interest in the session topic by selecting "Yes" or "No".
5. Provide additional information about yourself and your interests.
6. Enter any additional details that are relevant to the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): This field collects your name, email, and contact information.
* **Topic Interests** (`topic_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in the session topic.
* **Preferred Session Times** (`preferred_session_times`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred session time from the drop-down menu.
* **Preferred Session Dates** (`preferred_session_dates`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your preferred session dates from the drop-down menu.
* **Session Topic** (`session_topic`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the session topic that interests you.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact information you'd like to provide.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the session that you'd like to share.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that is relevant to the session.
* **Participant Topics** (`participant_topics`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in various session topics.
* **Event Organiser Details** (`event_organiser_details`, text, optional): Enter any information about the event organiser.
* **Consultant Details** (`consultant_details`, text, optional): Enter any information about the consultant.
* **Participant Details 2** (`participant_details_2`, text, optional): Provide additional contact information.
* **Quality Leader Details** (`quality_leader_details`, text, optional): Enter any information about the quality leader.
* **Participant Details 3** (`participant_details_3`, text, optional): Provide additional contact information.
* **Quality Benchmarking Session Details** (`quality_benchmarking_session_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the session.
* **Quality Benchmarking Session Details 2** (`quality_benchmarking_session_details_2`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about the session.
* **Session Attendance** (`session_attendance`, text, optional): Enter your expected attendance status.
* **Additional Details** (`additional_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that is relevant to the session.
* **Quality Benchmarking Session Attendance** (`quality_benchmarking_session_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Good" or "Poor" to indicate your expected session attendance.
* **Participant Interests** (`participant_interests`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your interests.
* **Participant Topics 4** (`participant_topics_4`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in session topics.

* **Participant Topics 6** (`participant_topics_6`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in session topics.
