# Geography Bee Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Geography Bee Tournament Entry Form is a document used for registering participants in a geography-themed competition. This form collects necessary information from students and parents/guardians to ensure a smooth and secure competition process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your school's name and your parent's name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your student's name and grade level.
3. Provide your student's and parent's email addresses.
4. Enter your parent's phone number.
5. Select the permission option for the student, parent, and school.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
7. Enter the competition date and time (if applicable).
8. Enter the school's address (if applicable).
9. Confirm your permissions for any additional schools or institutions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your parent/guardian.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the student participating in the competition.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, select_one, required): Select the grade level of the student (1-10).
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required): Enter the student's email address.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Permission** (`permission`, select_multiple, required): Select the permission option for the student, parent, and school.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the competition date (if applicable).
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the competition time (if applicable).
* **Address 1** (`address1`, text, optional): Enter the school's address 1.
* **Address 2** (`address2`, text, optional): Enter the school's address 2.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter the city of the school's location.
* **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter the state of the school's location.
* **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter the zip code of the school's location.
* **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter the country of the school's location.
* **School Name 2** (`school_name2`, text, optional): Enter the name of any additional school involved.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, select_multiple, optional): Select the permission option for any additional parent/guardian.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`, select_multiple, optional): Select the permission option for any additional student.
* **School Permission** (`school_permission`, select_multiple, optional): Select the permission option for any additional school.
* **Other School Name** (`other_school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of any other school involved.
* **Other School Permission** (`other_school_permission`, select_multiple, optional): Select the permission option for any other school.
* **Other School Permission Other** (`other_school_permission_other`, text, optional): Enter any additional information for any other school permission.

Note: Please fill in all required fields to ensure a complete and accurate registration.
