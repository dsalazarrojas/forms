# Dance EXchange After School Classes (Miss Stephanie) - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about students registering for Dance EXchange After School Classes. The data collected will be used to manage student registrations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field for correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the student registering for the classes.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `select_one`, optional): Select the grade of the student (first, second, or third).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship to Student** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, optional): Describe the relationship between the student and the emergency contact person.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, `select_one`, optional): Select whether the parent has given permission for the student to participate.
* **Student Allergies** (`student_allergies`, `text`, optional): List any known allergies or medical conditions affecting the student.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant medical conditions affecting the student.
* **Insurance Info** (`insurance_info`, `text`, optional): Enter any relevant insurance information.
* **Doctor Name** (`doctor_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the doctor who has treated the student's medical condition.
* **Doctor Phone** (`doctor_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the doctor who has treated the student's medical condition.
* **Doctor Address** (`doctor_address`, `text`, optional): Enter the address of the doctor who has treated the student's medical condition.
* **Medical Release** (`medical_release`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select any medical releases applicable to the student.
* **Medical Procedure** (`medical_procedure`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical procedures related to the student's condition.
* **Doctor Signature** (`doctor_signature`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant medical notes or comments from the doctor.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, `note`, optional): Enter any relevant comments or notes from the parent.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the student's registration.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of the student's registration.
* **School Year** (`school_year`, `text`, optional): Enter the school year the student is in.
* **School Term** (`school_term`, `text`, optional): Enter the school term the student is in.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the student's school.
* **Teacher Name** (`teacher_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the teacher in charge of the class.
* **Teacher Phone** (`teacher_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the teacher in charge of the class.
* **Teacher Email** (`teacher_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email of the teacher in charge of the class.
