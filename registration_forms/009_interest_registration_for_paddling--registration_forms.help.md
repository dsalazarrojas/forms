# Interest Registration For Paddling - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in participating in a paddling event.

## How To Complete This Form
Follow the steps outlined above to complete this form correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Interest** (`interest`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are interested in attending an event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event you are interested in.
* **Paddling Type** (`paddling_type`, select_multiple, optional): Choose all types of paddling activities you are interested in.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments about your interest in paddling.
* **Paddling Level** (`paddling_level`, text, optional): Enter your paddling level or skill level.
* **Paddling Frequency** (`paddling_frequency`, text, optional): Enter your preferred paddling frequency.
* **Paddling Location** (`paddling_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where you usually go paddling.
* **Paddling Equipment** (`paddling_equipment`, text, optional): Enter any specific paddling equipment you need or want to bring.
* **Paddling Interest** (`paddling_interest`, text, optional): Enter any additional interest or notes about paddling.
* **Paddling Notes** (`paddling_notes`, note, optional): Enter any other notes about your paddling experience or preferences.
* **Other Notes** (`other_notes`, note, optional): Enter any other notes about your paddling experience or preferences.
* **Event ID** (`event_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event you are interested in.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Submission** (`submission`, select_multiple, optional): Select "Yes" if you are interested in participating in this event.
