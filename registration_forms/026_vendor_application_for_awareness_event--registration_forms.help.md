# Vendor Application For Awareness Event - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help vendors provide their application for an awareness event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your application is processed efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the vendor's information, including business name, address, contact details, email, and phone number.
2. Provide details about the vendor's products and events, including product name, product description, event date, start time, end time, and event name.
3. Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your business.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, optional): Enter your business address.
* **Business Address 2** (`business_address_2`, text, optional): Enter your business address (if applicable).
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your store.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your event.
* **Event Date 2** (`event_date_2`, date, optional): Enter the date of your second event (if applicable).
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your event.
* **Start Time 2** (`start_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the start time of your second event (if applicable).
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your event.
* **End Time 2** (`end_time_2`, time, optional): Enter the end time of your second event (if applicable).
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, text, optional): Enter your store hours.
* **Store Hours 2** (`store_hours_2`, text, optional): Enter your store hours (if applicable).
* **Product Name** (`product_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your product.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of your product.
* **Vendor Website** (`vendor_website`, text, optional): Enter your website.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional contact details.
* **Business Licence** (`business_licence`, text, optional): Enter your business licence number (if applicable).
* **Vendor Application For Awareness Event Form** (`vendor_application`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your application.
