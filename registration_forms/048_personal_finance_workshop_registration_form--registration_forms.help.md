# Personal Finance Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Finance Workshop Registration Form is used to collect information from workshop participants, ensuring accurate and complete information for a successful workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form

### To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred workshop presentation from the "Workshop Presentation" dropdown menu.
2. Choose your language preference from the "Workshop Language" dropdown menu.
3. Provide your participant information by selecting your age group, participant type, and workshop date and time.
4. Enter your emergency contact and relationship details.
5. Fill in your personal contact details, including participant name, phone number, email address, and address.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
7. Complete any remaining fields, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Workshop Presentation (Required)
*   **Workshop Presentation**: Choose the presentation you would like to attend from the dropdown menu.

### 2. Workshop Location (Optional)
*   **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop (not required but recommended).

### 3. Participant Info (Optional)
*   **Participant Info**: Select your participant information, including age group, participant type, and workshop date and time.

### 4. Additional Comments (Optional)
*   **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation.

### 5. Participant Language (Required)
*   **Participant Language**: Choose your preferred language for the workshop.

### 6. Emergency Contact (Optional)
*   **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and relationship of your emergency contact.

### 7. Participant Name (Optional)
*   **Participant Name**: Enter your participant name.

### 8. Participant Phone (Optional)
*   **Participant Phone**: Enter your participant phone number.

### 9. Participant Email (Optional)
*   **Participant Email**: Enter your participant email address.

### 10. Participant Address (Optional)
*   **Participant Address**: Enter your participant address.

### 11. Participant DOB (Optional)
*   **Participant DOB**: Enter your participant date of birth.

### 12. Participant Workshop Type (Optional)
*   **Participant Workshop Type**: Select your workshop type from the dropdown menu.

### 13. Participant Preferred Date (Optional)
*   **Participant Preferred Date**: Enter your preferred date for the workshop.

### 14. Participant Preferred Time (Optional)
*   **Participant Preferred Time**: Enter your preferred time for the workshop.

### 15. Participant Language (Optional)
*   **Participant Language**: Choose your preferred language.

### 16. Participant Relationship (Optional)
*   **Participant Relationship**: Enter your participant relationship.

### 17. Participant Notes (Optional)
*   **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional participant notes.

### 18. Participant Other (Optional)
*   **Participant Other**: Enter any other participant information.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed to ensure accurate and complete information for the workshop organizers.
