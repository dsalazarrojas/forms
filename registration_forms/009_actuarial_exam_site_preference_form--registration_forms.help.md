# Actuarial Exam Site Preference Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Actuarial Exam Site Preference Form is a crucial part of the exam preparation and registration process. It allows candidates to express their preferences for exam sites, dates, and accommodations, enabling the exam administrators to efficiently allocate resources and provide the best possible experience for candidates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form accurately, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the required fields:
	* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Candidate ID Number**: Enter the ID number issued by the Actuarial Society.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for communication and updates.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for any urgent communications.
	* **Exam Code/Name**: Select the exam code you're registered for (e.g., P, FM, IFM, LTAM, STAM).
	* **Exam Session Month/Year**: Enter the exam session date you're planning to take.
	* **Primary Site Preference**: Select your preferred exam site from the given options (Region 1 - North America East, Region 2 - North America West, Region 3 - Europe / Middle East, Region 4 - Asia Pacific, Region 5 - South America).
	* **Specific City Preference**: If you know the city you'd prefer, enter it here; otherwise, leave it blank.
	* **Backup Site Preference**: Select your backup site preference from the given options (No Backup Needed, Alternate Regional Site, Remote / Online Proctoring (if available)).
	* **Special Testing Accommodations**: Select if you require any special accommodations due to medical or accessibility needs.
	* **Detailed Accommodation Description**: If you've selected any special accommodations, provide a detailed explanation of your requirements.
	* **Acknowledgement Deadlines**: Confirm that you understand that site preferences are subject to availability and deadlines.

2. Review and confirm that all the fields have been filled accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: A text field to enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Candidate ID Number**: A number field to enter the ID number issued by the Actuarial Society.
* **Email Address**: A field to enter your email address for communication and updates.
* **Phone Number**: A field to enter your phone number for urgent communications.
* **Exam Code/Name**: A drop-down list of exam codes (e.g., P, FM, IFM, LTAM, STAM).
* **Exam Session Month/Year**: A text field to enter the exam session date you're planning to take.
* **Primary Site Preference**: A drop-down list of exam site options (Region 1 - North America East, Region 2 - North America West, Region 3 - Europe / Middle East, Region 4 - Asia Pacific, Region 5 - South America).
* **Specific City Preference**: A text field to enter the city you'd prefer; leave it blank if not known.
* **Backup Site Preference**: A drop-down list of backup site options (No Backup Needed, Alternate Regional Site, Remote / Online Proctoring (if available)).
* **Special Testing Accommodations**: A drop-down list of special accommodation options (No - Standard Testing Environment, Yes - Extended Time Requested, Yes - Physical Accessibility Needed, Yes - Screen Reader / Assistive Tech Needed, Yes - Other (Please specify below)).
* **Detailed Accommodation Description**: A text field to provide a detailed explanation of your requirements if you've selected any special accommodations.
* **Acknowledgement Deadlines**: A drop-down list to confirm that you understand that site preferences are subject to availability and deadlines.

## Tips

* Double-check that all fields have been filled accurately and completely before submitting the form.
* Be aware that site preferences are subject to availability and deadlines.
* If you have any questions or concerns, reach out to the exam administrators for assistance.
