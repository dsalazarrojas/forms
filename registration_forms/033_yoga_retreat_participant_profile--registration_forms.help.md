# Yoga Retreat Participant Profile - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Yoga Retreat Participant Profile form is designed to collect essential information about the participants attending the yoga retreat. This information will be used to ensure that each participant has a safe and enjoyable experience during the retreat.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. This will be used for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "emergency contact name" field.
6. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "emergency contact phone" field.
7. Choose your health condition from the list in the "health condition" field. This is important for your safety and the safety of others.
8. If you have any medical devices that you need to bring or use during the retreat, select them in the "medical device" field.
9. If you take any medication, enter it in the "medication" field. This will help us understand any potential risks or needs.
10. If you have any disability or special needs, describe them in the "disability" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the person you want to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Health Condition**: Choose from the list your current health condition. This is important for your safety and the safety of others.
* **Medical Device**: If you need to bring or use any medical device during the retreat, select it from the list.
* **Medication**: If you take any medication, enter it in this field.
* **Disability**: If you have any disability or special needs, describe them in this field.
