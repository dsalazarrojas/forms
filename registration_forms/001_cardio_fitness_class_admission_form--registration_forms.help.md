# Cardio Fitness Class Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cardio Fitness Class Admission Form is designed to collect essential information from participants registering for a cardio fitness class. This information helps us plan and ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the "Full Name" field.
2.  Provide your valid email address in the "Email Address" field. This is used for class updates and confirmations.
3.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, including your area code.
4.  Select your current fitness level from the options provided in the "Current Fitness Level" field.
5.  Answer "Yes" or "No" to the question about physician clearance in the "Do you have physician clearance for exercise?" field. If you have a condition, select "I have a condition - please see attached documentation."
6.  Select all applicable health conditions from the list in the "Do you have any of the following health conditions?" field.
7.  If you're taking any medications that affect exercise, list them in the "Are you currently taking any medications that affect exercise?" field.
8.  Choose your primary fitness goals from the list in the "What are your primary fitness goals?" field.
9.  Select your preferred class time from the options provided in the "Preferred Class Time" field.
10. Choose the number of classes you can commit to per week from the options in the "How many classes per week can you commit to?" field.
11. Select your membership type from the options in the "Membership Type" field.
12. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone Number" fields, respectively.
13. Indicate your experience with gym equipment in the "Experience with gym equipment?" field.
14. If you have any special requests or accommodations, list them in the "Any special requests or accommodations?" field.
15. Finally, acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions in the "I understand the health risks of exercise and assume full responsibility" and "I accept the terms and conditions of the fitness center" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (1):
    *   Enter your full name to identify yourself.
*   **Email Address** (2):
    *   Provide a valid email address for class updates and confirmations.
*   **Phone Number** (3):
    *   Enter your phone number, including your area code, in case we need to contact you.
*   **Date of Birth** (4):
    *   Enter your date of birth to verify age eligibility.
*   **Current Fitness Level** (5):
    *   Select the level that best describes your current fitness experience.
*   **Do you have physician clearance for exercise?** (6):
    *   Answer "Yes" or "No" to this question. If you have a condition, provide attached documentation.
*   **Do you have any of the following health conditions?** (7):
    *   Select all conditions that apply to you.
*   **Are you currently taking any medications that affect exercise?** (8):
    *   List your medications if applicable.
*   **What are your primary fitness goals?** (9):
    *   Choose your primary reasons for attending the class.
*   **Preferred Class Time** (10):
    *   Select the time you prefer for classes.
*   **How many classes per week can you commit to?** (11):
    *   Choose the number of classes you can attend per week.
*   **Membership Type** (12):
    *   Select the membership option that suits you best.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (13):
    *   Enter the name of your emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (14):
    *   Enter your emergency contact's phone number, including their area code.
*   **Experience with gym equipment?** (15):
    *   Indicate your level of experience with gym equipment.
*   **Any special requests or accommodations?** (16):
    *   List any special requests or accommodations you might need.
*   **I understand the health risks of exercise and assume full responsibility** (17):
    *   Acknowledge and agree to the terms and conditions.
*   **I accept the terms and conditions of the fitness center** (18):
    *   Confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Our fitness center staff will review your application and get back to you with further instructions.
