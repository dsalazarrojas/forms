# Workplace Modernization Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from participants for the Workplace Modernization Lab. It helps us understand your interests, session preferences, and other details necessary for your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant details.
2. Select your interests from the provided options.
3. Choose your session time.
4. Review and acknowledge the terms of agreement.
5. Enter your contact details (if required).
6. Enter your email address.
7. Enter your phone number.
8. Select your session preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: Enter your name, as we need this information for registration purposes.
* **Interests**: Choose one or more of the options that best represent your areas of interest in the Workplace Modernization Lab.
* **Session Time**: Select the time slot that works best for your participation in the lab.
* **Terms Agreement**: Please review the terms of agreement to ensure you understand the lab's rules and expectations. This is a mandatory step to confirm your participation.
* **Contact Details**: If required, enter your contact details for any follow-up communication.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for any further communication or updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any further contact.
* **Session Preferences**: Choose one or more of the options that best represent your preferences for the lab sessions.
