# Destination Information Night RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Destination Information Night RSVP Form is designed to collect travel information from attendees, ensuring that organizers have accurate details to plan and coordinate the event smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the fields as prompted. If a field is not required, you can skip it.
2. Enter your Attendee Name to identify yourself as the traveler.
3. Select your Contact Information (Email, Phone, or Website) if you want to be reachable for any updates or inquiries.
4. Provide your Destination Address to help organizers understand where you'll be traveling from.
5. Enter your Return Date and Return Time to indicate when you plan to return.
6. Specify your Travel Arrival Time and Date to inform organizers of your expected arrival.
7. Choose your Travel Method (Car, Train, Plane, Bus, or Walking) to indicate how you'll be traveling.
8. Enter any additional Travel Info Session details if needed.
9. Fill out the Additional Info field with any further information that's relevant to your travel plans.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Travel Information Session**: This field is not required. It's meant to gather any specific travel information that may be necessary, such as a travel group or meeting spot.
* **Attendee Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be recognized on the night.
* **Contact Information**: Choose one or more of the options (Email, Phone, or Website) to provide a way for organizers to reach you. This is not required.
* **Destination Address**: Enter the address where you'll be traveling from.
* **Return Date**: Enter the date you plan to return. This is not required.
* **Return Time**: Enter the time you plan to return. This is not required.
* **Travel Arrival Time**: Enter the time you expect to arrive. This is not required.
* **Travel Arrival Date**: Enter the date you expect to arrive. This is not required.
* **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of your travel. This is not required.
* **Travel Distance**: Enter the distance of your travel. This is not required.
* **Travel Method**: Choose how you'll be traveling (Car, Train, Plane, Bus, or Walking). This is not required.
* **Travel Info Session**: This field is not required. It's meant to gather any specific travel information that may be necessary.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional details that may be relevant to your travel plans. This is not required.
