# Professional Development Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants about their interest in attending a professional development workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter the title of the workshop you are interested in attending in the "Workshop Title" field.
3. Provide a brief description of the workshop in the "Workshop Description" field.
4. Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Participant Phone" field.
7. Select the date and time of the workshop you are interested in attending in the "Workshop Date" and "Workshop Time" fields.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments about your attendance in the "Participant Note" field.
9. Indicate your attendance status by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Participant Attendance" field. If you have other plans for attendance on the selected date, you can enter details in the "Participant Attendance Other" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Title** (text, required: false): Enter the title of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Description** (text, required: false): Provide a brief description of the workshop.
* **Participant Name** (text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Participant Email** (email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Date** (date, required: false): Select the date of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Workshop Time** (time, required: false): Select the time of the workshop you are interested in attending.
* **Participant Note** (note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
* **Participant Attendance** (select multiple, required: false): Indicate your attendance status by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Participant Attendance Other** (text, required: false): If you have other plans for attendance on the selected date, enter details here.
