# Online Tuition And Homeschooling Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for students or parents to register for online tuition and homeschooling services.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by entering your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field in the format of YYYY-MM-DD.
4. Select the type of student you are registering, either "Regular Student" or "Homeschooling Student".
5. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
6. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
7. Enter the name of the parent or guardian in the "Parent Name" field.
8. Enter the name of the school in the "School Name" field.
9. Select the type of tuition you are registering for, either "Online Tuition" or "Offline Tuition".
10. Enter any additional session details in the "Session Details" field.
11. Choose the start and end times of the session in the "Session Start" and "Session End" fields respectively.
12. Enter the student's address in the "Student Address" field.
13. Enter the name of the guardian in the "Guardian Name" field.
14. Enter the contact number of the guardian in the "Guardian Number" field.
15. Enter the address of the guardian in the "Guardian Address" field.
16. List any student interests in the "Student Interests" field.
17. List any student skills in the "Student Skills" field.
18. List any student languages in the "Student Languages" field.
19. List any student subjects in the "Student Subjects" field.
20. Enter the name of the course in the "Course Name" field.
21. Enter a brief description of the course in the "Course Description" field.
22. Enter the duration of the course in the "Course Duration" field.
23. Enter the price of the course in the "Course Price" field.
24. Add any additional notes about the course in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.

* **Student Type** (`Student Type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are a "Regular Student" or "Homeschooling Student".
* **Contact Number** (`Contact Number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Parent Name** (`Parent Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the school.
* **Tuition Type** (`Tuition Type`, select_one, required): Select whether you are registering for "Online Tuition" or "Offline Tuition".
* **Session Details** (`Session Details`, text, required): Enter any additional session details.
* **Session Start** (`Session Start`, time, required): Choose the start time of the session.
* **Session End** (`Session End`, time, required): Choose the end time of the session.
* **Student Address** (`Student Address`, text, required): Enter the student's address.
* **Guardian Name** (`Guardian Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the guardian.
* **Guardian Number** (`Guardian Number`, text, required): Enter the contact number of the guardian.
* **Guardian Address** (`Guardian Address`, text, required): Enter the address of the guardian.
* **Student Interests** (`Student Interests`, text, required): List any student interests.
* **Student Skills** (`Student Skills`, text, required): List any student skills.
* **Student Languages** (`Student Languages`, text, required): List any student languages.
* **Student Subjects** (`Student Subjects`, text, required): List any student subjects.
* **Course Name** (`Course Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the course.
* **Course Description** (`Course Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the course.
* **Course Duration** (`Course Duration`, number, required): Enter the duration of the course.
* **Course Price** (`Course Price`, number, required): Enter the price of the course.
