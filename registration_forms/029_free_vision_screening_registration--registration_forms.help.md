# free_vision_screening_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a free vision screening. It is used to collect demographic information, scheduling details, and screening status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your participant information, including name and contact details on the Participant Information page.
2. Enter your personal details such as age and gender on the Participant Information page.
3. Provide your schedule details on the Participant Schedule page.
4. Select your screening type and status on the Screening Type and Status page.
5. Add any additional comments or notes on the Screening Notes page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information (Participant Information)** (`participant_name`, `title`, required/optional): Enter your name and contact details to help us reach out to you regarding your screening appointment.
* **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your name to help us identify you in our records.
* **Participant Email (Participant Email)** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your email address so we can reach out to you regarding your screening appointment.
* **Participant Phone (Participant Phone)** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Screening Date (Screening Date)** (`screening_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your screening appointment.
* **Participant Age (Participant Age)** (`participant_age`, `number`, optional): Enter your age to help us understand your demographics.
* **Participant Gender (Participant Gender)** (`participant_gender`, `select_one`, optional): Select your gender to help us understand your demographics.
*   - **Female**
*   - **Male**
*   - **Other**
* **Participant Gender Other (Participant Gender Other)** (`participant_gender_other`, `text`, optional): If you select 'Other', please enter your gender here.
* **Participant Date of Birth (Participant Date of Birth)** (`participant_dob`, `date`, optional): Enter your date of birth to help us understand your demographics.
* **Screening Type (Screening Type)** (`screening_type`, `select_one`, optional): Select the type of screening you are attending.
*   - **Eye Check**
*   - **Vision Check**
*   - **Other**
* **Screening Notes (Screening Notes)** (`screening_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your screening appointment.
* **Screening Status (Screening Status)** (`screening_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select the status of your screening appointment.
*   - **Scheduled**
*   - **Completed**
*   - **Cancelled**
* **Participant Schedule (Participant Schedule)** (`participant_schedule`, `time`, optional): Enter your schedule details to help us schedule your appointment.
* **On-site (On-site)** (`participant_onsite`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter any on-site information.
* **Dose (Dose)** (`participant_dose`, `number`, optional): Enter the dose information.
* **Comments (Comments)** (`participant_comments`, `note`, optional): Add any additional comments about your screening appointment.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Check your details before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions, feel free to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you regarding your screening appointment.
