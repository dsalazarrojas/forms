# Responsive Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Workshop Registration Form is a tool to collect registration information from users. This information will be used to create a unique and tailored workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration information section with your details.
2. Enter your first name.
3. Enter your last name.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your phone number.
6. Share any suggestions you may have about the workshop.
7. Add any further comments about the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This section is where you enter your registration information. Please fill in the required fields with your details.
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Suggestions** (`suggestion`, text, required: false): Share any suggestions you may have about the workshop.
* **Further comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Add any further comments about the workshop.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any trouble filling in the form, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We'll be in touch with you shortly after you submit the form to confirm your registration.
