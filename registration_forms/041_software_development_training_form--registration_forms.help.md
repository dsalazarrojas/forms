# Software Development Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Software Development Training Form is used to register for the software development training program. This form helps the training team to collect necessary information from employees to provide accurate resource allocation and ensure a smooth training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Employee Name in the text field.
2. Enter the name of your company in the Company Name field.
3. Provide the desired Job Title in the Job Title field.
4. Select 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown menu for the Training Program field, indicating whether you are interested in participating.
5. Choose a preferred date for the training in the date field.
6. Select a start time for the training in the time field.
7. Choose an end time for the training in the time field.
8. Enter the location of the training (e.g., online/virtual or physical location) in the Location field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Company Name** (Text, Optional): Type in the name of your company or organization.
- **Job Title** (Text, Optional): Enter your job title within your company.
- **Training Program** (Select One, Optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate interest in participating in the training program.
- **Preferred Date** (Date, Optional): Choose a date for the training that suits your schedule.
- **Start Time** (Time, Optional): Select the start time of the training.
- **End Time** (Time, Optional): Select the end time of the training.
- **Location** (Text, Optional): Specify the location where you want to attend the training (e.g., online, physical location, etc.).

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any special requests or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the training team.
- You will receive confirmation of your registration once the form is successfully submitted.
