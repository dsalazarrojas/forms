# Student Ministry Retreat Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Ministry Retreat Sign Up Form is designed to collect information from students participating in the ministry retreat. This form is used to register students for the retreat, which includes gathering their name, contact information, and relevant details such as date of birth and event dates. By filling out this form, students can ensure they have a spot in the retreat and receive important reminders and updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
3. Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. If applicable, provide your phone number and student group information (if not required).
5. Select the event dates you plan to attend.
6. If you have any additional notes or comments, please add them in the space provided.
