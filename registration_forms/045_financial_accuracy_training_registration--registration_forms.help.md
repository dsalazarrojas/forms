# Financial Accuracy Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in attending the Financial Accuracy Training. The purpose of this form is to gather details about the attendee, their session date preferences, and any specific goals they may have. This form will help us to better understand your needs and ensure that you receive the most suitable training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you provide all required information (marked as required).
3. If a field is marked as optional, feel free to provide any additional information you think will be helpful.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details (1)**:
	+ **<name>** (`attendee_details`, `text`, required): This field is your name.
	+ **<type>** is not applicable for this field.
	+ **required**: Yes, this field is required.
	* **Session Date Preferences (2)**:
	+ **<name>** (`session_date_preferences`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can specify your preferred dates for the training session. You can provide multiple dates if you have specific availability.
	+ **<type>** is not applicable for this field.
	+ **required**: No, this field is optional.
* **Goals (3)**:
	+ **<name>** (`goals`, `text`, optional): This field is where you can specify any specific goals you have for the training. For example, you can mention what you hope to achieve from the training or any specific topics you'd like to focus on.
	+ **<type>** is not applicable for this field.
	+ **required**: No, this field is optional.

## Tips
