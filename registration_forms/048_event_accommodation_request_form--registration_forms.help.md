# Event Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to request accommodations for an event. It is intended for attendees who require assistance with arranging accommodations for the event, such as booking a hotel room or other types of accommodations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Make sure you have a valid Event ID provided to you at the time of event registration.
2. Select the type of accommodation you are requesting, such as Hotel, Hostel, Airbnb, or Motel.
3. Enter the date you will be checking in for the event.
4. Enter the date you will be checking out of the accommodation.
5. Provide the name of the accommodation or location you are requesting.
6. Enter the number of guests that will be attending the event.
7. Enter your first and last name as it appears on your registration.
8. Enter your email address and phone number so we can contact you regarding your accommodation request.
9. If you have any special requests, such as a preferred room type or special needs, enter those in the "Special Requests" field.
10. Review your responses for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
