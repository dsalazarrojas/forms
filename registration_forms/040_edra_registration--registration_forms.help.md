# EDRA Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The EDRA Registration form is used to collect demographic information from users for informational purposes only. It is not required and will not be used for commercial purposes unless specifically noted.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the **first_name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last_name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **phone** field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the **date_of_birth** field in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name (text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **last_name (text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **email (email)**: Enter your email address that you use to communicate with us.
- **phone (text)**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
- **date_of_birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in the format `mm/dd/yyyy`, where `mm` is the month, `dd` is the day, and `yyyy` is the year of birth.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the information you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
- If you have any issues with the form, contact us for assistance.
- If you have already completed this form, please do not create duplicate entries.
