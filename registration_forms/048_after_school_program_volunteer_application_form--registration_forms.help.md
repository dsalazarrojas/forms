# After School Program Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The After School Program Volunteer Application Form is designed for individuals who want to volunteer for the after-school program. This form helps the program administrators understand your availability, qualifications, and preferences to place you as a volunteer.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out all required fields marked as "True".
2.  For select one fields, choose the best answer that applies to you.
3.  For select multiple fields, choose all options that apply to you.
4.  For text fields, enter your information as requested.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`id: 2`): Enter your complete name (first name, last name).
*   **Date of Birth** (`id: 3`): Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
*   **Address** (`id: 4`): Enter your current residential address.
*   **City, State, ZIP Code** (`id: 5`): Enter the city, state, and ZIP code of your address.
*   **Phone Number** (`id: 6`): Enter your primary phone number.
*   **Email Address** (`id: 7`): Enter your primary email address.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`id: 9`): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`id: 10`): Describe your relationship to your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`id: 11`): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
*   **Available Days** (`id: 13`): Select all the days you are available to volunteer.
*   **Available Time Slots** (`id: 14`): Select all the time slots you are available to volunteer.
*   **Expected Volunteer Duration** (`id: 15`): Choose the duration you plan to volunteer.
*   **Tutoring Preference** (`id: 17`): Select if you are interested in tutoring students.
*   **Preferred Subjects to Tutor** (`id: 18`): If interested in tutoring, select all subjects you prefer to tutor.
*   **Activity Leading Interest** (`id: 19`): Select if you are interested in leading activities.
*   **Preferred Activities to Lead** (`id: 20`): If interested in leading activities, select all activities you prefer.
*   **Administrative Support Interest** (`id: 21`): Select if you are interested in helping with administrative tasks.
*   **Background Check Consent** (`id: 23`): Select if you consent to a background check as part of the application process.
*   **Driving Requirement Consent** (`id: 24`): Select if you are willing to undergo an additional background check if driving students.
*   **Education Level** (`id: 26`): Select your highest level of education completed.
*   **Field of Education** (`id: 27`): Enter the field of study you graduated from.
*   **Previous Volunteer Experience** (`id: 28`): Select if you have previous experience volunteering with children or youth.
*   **Details of Previous Volunteer Experience** (`id: 29`): If yes, describe your previous volunteer experience with children or youth.
*   **Professional Experience** (`id: 30`): Select if you have professional experience working with children or youth.
*   **Details of Professional Experience** (`id: 31`): If yes, describe your professional experience working with children or youth.
*   **Special Skills or Talents** (`id: 32`): Enter any special skills or talents you would bring to the program.
*   **Reference 1 Name** (`id: 34`): Enter the name of your first reference.
*   **Reference 1 Relationship** (`id: 35`): Describe your relationship to your first reference.
*   **Reference 1 Phone** (`id: 36`): Enter the phone number of your first reference.
*   **Reference 2 Name** (`id: 37`): Enter the name of your second reference.
*   **Reference 2 Relationship** (`id: 38`): Describe your relationship to your second reference.
*   **Reference 2 Phone** (`id: 39`): Enter the phone number of your second reference.
*   **Terms Agreement** (`id: 41`): Select if you agree to the terms and conditions of the volunteer program.
*   **Confidentiality Agreement** (`id: 42`): Select if you agree to maintain confidentiality of student information.
*   **Electronic Signature** (`id: 43`): Sign this form electronically to submit your volunteer application.

## Tips

- Please make sure to fill out the form honestly, and if you are unsure about any field, describe your best effort.
- Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
- If you have any questions, please contact the program administrators.
