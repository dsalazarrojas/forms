# High Intensity Functional Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from individuals interested in participating in High Intensity Functional Training.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all necessary information.
2. Fill in your personal details and registration information accurately and thoroughly.
3. Provide your emergency contact information.
4. Double-check your submission for any errors or inconsistencies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Personal Details (Page 1)

* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, `text`, required: false): Please provide your personal details, including any relevant contact information you'd like to share with the training program organizers.

### Registration Info (Page 1)

* **Registration Info** (`registration_info`, `text`, required: false): This section is where you'll provide any additional information about your registration for the training program.

### Emergency Contact (Page 1)

* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): In case of an emergency, please provide the name and contact information of an emergency contact person. This will help the training staff ensure your safety and well-being during the program.

## Tips

* Please ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you're unsure about any field, please ask our support team at form@gic.mx for assistance.
* Review your submission carefully before submitting to avoid any errors or inconsistencies.
