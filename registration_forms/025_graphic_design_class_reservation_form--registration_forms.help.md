# Graphic Design Class Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve a seat in a graphic design class. Please fill out the form below to let us know your preferred date and time for the class, as well as any other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Information" field with your name and other details as required.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Choose your preferred date for the class from the "Date" field.
5. Choose your preferred time for the class from the "Time" field.
6. Indicate your interest in different tools by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Class" field.
7. Choose which tools you are interested in using during the class by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Session" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or information in the "Notes" field.
9. Fill out the "Assigned Tool" fields with any tools you are interested in using during the class.

- **User Information (1)**: Enter your name and other information as required.
  - This field is used to collect your basic contact information.
- **Name (2)**: Enter your name.
  - This field is used to collect your name.
- **Email (3)**: Enter your email address.
  - This field is used to collect your email address.
- **Phone (4)**: Enter your phone number.
  - This field is used to collect your phone number.
- **Date (5)**: Choose your preferred date for the class.
  - This field is used to collect your preferred date for the class.
- **Time (6)**: Choose your preferred time for the class.
  - This field is used to collect your preferred time for the class.
- **Class (7)**: Indicate your interest in different tools by selecting "Yes" or "No".
  - This field is used to determine which tools you are interested in using during the class.
- **Session (8)**: Choose which tools you are interested in using during the class by selecting "Yes" or "No".
  - This field is used to determine which tools you are interested in using during the class.
- **Notes (9)**: Enter any additional notes or information.
  - This field is used to collect any additional information or notes you have.
- **Assigned Tool (10-14)**: Enter any tools you are interested in using during the class.
  - These fields are used to collect any tools you are interested in using during the class.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
- If you are unsure of any information, please ask your instructor for guidance.
- Please be specific about your preferred date and time for the class.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please enter them in the "Notes" field.
