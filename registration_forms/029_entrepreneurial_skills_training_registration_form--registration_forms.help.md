# entrepreneurial_skills_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The entrepreneurial_skills_training_registration_form is a registration form for the entrepreneurial skills training program. It allows users to select a training session type and provide their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a training session type from the available options (Entrepreneurship, Leadership, or Communication).
2. Enter your participant's name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your participant's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Session** (select_one): Choose the type of training session you are interested in. You can select from Entrepreneurship, Leadership, or Communication.
* **Participant Name** (text): Enter your participant's name for registration purposes.
* **E-mail** (email): Enter your email address for contact information.
* **Participant Phone** (text): Enter your participant's phone number for contact information.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct training session type that aligns with your interests and needs.
* Double-check your email and phone number for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are experiencing difficulty or have questions, please contact [support email or contact information].
