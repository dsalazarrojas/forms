# Retail Blockchain Partnership Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who are interested in forming a partnership with a retail business to explore opportunities related to blockchain technology. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the **user_info** field.
2. Provide details about the partnership in the **partnership_details** field.
3. Add any relevant contact information in the **contact_info** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info** (`user_info`, text, required): Please fill in your name and any other relevant personal or business information.
* **partnership_details** (`partnership_details`, text, required): Provide a brief description of the partnership you are interested in, including the products or services you offer and any existing connections or agreements with retail businesses.
* **contact_info** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact details, such as email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you about your submission.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide clear and concise information in each field.
* If you have any attachments or supporting documents, please attach them separately and mention them in your submission.
