# Elderly Assistance Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for volunteers who wish to assist the elderly population.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your volunteer name and email address.
2.  Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3.  Indicate your availability for volunteer work by selecting the date and time you are available.
4.  Let us know if you are able to provide elderly assistance, and what role you are willing to take on.
5.  If you are affiliated with a local organization, please provide their name.
6.  If you have any additional comments or requests, please use the space provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. Volunteer Name
*   **Volunteer Name**: Enter your name as it appears on official documents.
### 2. Email
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address to ensure we can contact you for further information or volunteer opportunities.
### 3. Phone
*   **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you for further information or volunteer opportunities.
### 4. Date Available
*   **Date Available**: Select a date that you are available to volunteer.
### 5. Time Available
*   **Time Available**: Select a time that you are available to volunteer.
### 6. Elderly Assistance Volunteering
*   **Elderly Assistance Volunteering**: Select 'Yes' if you are able and willing to provide elderly assistance.
### 7. Volunteer Role
*   **Volunteer Role**: Select the specific roles you are willing to take on as a volunteer. For example, 'Yes, I can help with errands', 'Yes, I can provide transportation', etc.
### 8. Organization
*   **Organization**: If you are affiliated with a local organization, please provide their name here.
### 9. Address
*   **Address**: Enter your address to ensure we can send you information and updates about volunteer opportunities.
### 10. Additional Comments
*   **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please use this space to provide further information.
