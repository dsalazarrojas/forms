# Entrepreneurial Networking Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees about their participation in the entrepreneurial networking event. The collected data will help event organizers to categorize and tailor the experience for each attendee.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details such as First Name, Last Name, Business Name, Job Title, and Email.
2. Select your preferred Date and Time for the event.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments in the Note field.
4. Choose a Category of interest from the provided options.
5. Select multiple options from Select Multiple field.
6. Choose Select One from the provided options.
7. Enter a numerical value in the Integer field.
8. Enter a decimal value in the Decimal field.
9. Select Yes or No from Select One 3, Select Multiple 3, Select One 4, and Select Multiple 4 fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your business.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date of the event you will attend.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time of the event you will attend.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Choose a category that best describes your interests.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options that apply to you.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required): Choose one option from the list.
* **Integer** (`integer`, number, required): Enter a numerical value.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, required): Enter a decimal value.
* **Select One 3** (`select_one_3`, select_one, required): Choose Yes or No from the list.
* **Select Multiple 3** (`select_multiple_3`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple Yes or No options.
* **Select One 4** (`select_one_4`, select_one, required): Choose Yes or No from the list.
* **Select Multiple 4** (`select_multiple_4`, select_multiple, required): Select multiple Yes or No options.
* **Select One 5** (`select_one_5`, select_one, required): Choose Yes or No from the list.
