# Press Interview Slot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Press Interview Slot Registration form is designed for journalists and media professionals to register for a press interview. This form collects information about the journalist's details and the interview details to make it easier to manage and track the interview schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your name in the **name** field. This field is used to identify you as a journalist or media professional.
2.  Enter your email address in the **email** field. This will be used as a contact point for communication regarding the press interview.
3.  Enter your phone number in the **phone** field. This will be used for further communication.
4.  Select your journalist type from the **journalist_type** field. You can choose between Journalist and Media Professional.
5.  Enter your journalist details in the **journalist_details** field. This is a text area for any additional information about yourself as a journalist or media professional.
6.  Enter your name in the **Journalist Details Name** field. This is your name as a journalist or media professional.
7.  Enter your organization in the **organization** field. If you have an affiliation with a particular media organization or company, enter it here.
8.  Enter your phone number in the **Journalist Details Phone** field. This will be used for further communication as a journalist or media professional.
9.  Enter your email address in the **Journalist Details Email** field. This will be used for further communication as a journalist or media professional.
10. Enter the title of the media interview in the **title** field. This will be used as the title of the press interview.
11. Enter a brief summary of the media interview in the **summary** field. This will be a short description of the interview.
12. Enter the length of the media interview in the **slot_length** field. This is the duration of the press interview.
13. Enter the date of the media interview in the **date** field. This will be the date of the press interview.
14. Enter the start time of the media interview in the **start_time** field. This is the start time of the press interview.
15. Enter the end time of the media interview in the **end_time** field. This is the end time of the press interview.
16. Enter the location of the media interview in the **location** field. This is the location of the press interview.
17. Enter any notes about the media interview in the **notes** field. This is a text area for any additional information about the press interview.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as a journalist or media professional.
* **email** (Text, Required): Enter your email address as a contact point for further communication.
* **phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **journalist_type** (Select One, Optional): Select your type of journalist or media professional from Journalist or Media Professional.
* **journalist_details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as a journalist or media professional.
* **Journalist Details Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as a journalist or media professional.
* **organization** (Text, Optional): Enter your affiliation with a particular media organization or company.
* **Journalist Details Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number as a journalist or media professional.
* **Journalist Details Email** (Text, Optional): Enter your email address as a journalist or media professional.
* **media_interview_details** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the media interview.
* **title** (Text, Optional): Enter the title of the media interview.
* **summary** (Text, Optional): Enter a brief summary of the media interview.
* **slot_length** (Number, Optional): Enter the duration of the press interview.
* **date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the press interview.
* **start_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the start time of the press interview.
* **end_time** (Time, Optional): Enter the end time of the press interview.
* **location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of the press interview.
* **notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional information about the press interview.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely. If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields, do not hesitate to reach out.
