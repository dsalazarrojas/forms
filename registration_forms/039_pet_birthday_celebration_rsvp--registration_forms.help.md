# Pet Birthday Celebration RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Birthday Celebration RSVP form is designed to help pet owners decide whether to celebrate their pet's birthday and gather relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your response to whether you want to celebrate your pet's birthday.
* Enter your pet's name.
* Enter your pet's birthday.
* Choose your RSVP response for the celebration.
* Provide your phone number (optional).
* Provide your email address (optional).
* Confirm your RSVP status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Birthday Celebration** (1, select_one, required): Choose whether you want to celebrate your pet's birthday.
* **Pet Name** (2, text, required): Enter your pet's name to help us know who's birthday it is.
* **Pet Birthday** (3, date, required): Enter your pet's birthday.
* **RSVP** (4, select_multiple, required): Choose how you plan to RSVP for the celebration.
* **Owner Phone** (5, text, optional): Enter your phone number for us to get in touch.
* **Owner Email** (6, email, optional): Enter your email address for us to get in touch.
* **Pet Birthday Celebration RSVP** (7, select_multiple, required): Confirm your RSVP status for the pet's birthday celebration.
* **Pet Date of Birth** (8, date, required): Enter your pet's date of birth.
 
## Tips
* Make sure to choose correctly for each field.
* Double-check your responses for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to ask!
