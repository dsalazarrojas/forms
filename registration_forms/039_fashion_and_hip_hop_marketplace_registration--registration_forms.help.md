# Fashion And Hip Hop Marketplace Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
## How To Complete This Form
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **marketplace_form**: Enter a brief title for your marketplace profile.
* **user_info**: Please provide your personal information.
* **signup_info**: Enter information about your user account.
* **buyer_info**: If you are a buyer, enter your buyer information.
* **seller_info**: If you are a seller, enter your seller information.
* **vendor_onboarding**: Enter information about your vendor onboarding status.
* **vendor_profile**: Enter your vendor profile information.
* **marketplace_data**: Enter any additional marketplace data.
* **submit**: Choose whether to submit your registration (Yes) or not (No).
* **buyer**: Check if you are a buyer (Yes) or not (No).
* **seller**: Check if you are a seller (Yes) or not (No).
* **email**: Enter your email address.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **date**: Enter a date.
* **time**: Enter a time.
* **note**: Enter a note about your marketplace registration.
* **marketplace_email**: Enter the email address of your marketplace.
* **marketplace_phone**: Enter the phone number of your marketplace.
* **submitter**: Choose whether you are a submitter (Yes) or not (No).
* **marketplace**: Check if you are a marketplace (Yes) or not (No).
* **vendor**: Check if you are a vendor (Yes) or not (No).
* **onboarding_status**: Check the onboarding status of your marketplace (Active or Inactive).
* **buyer_email**: Enter the email address of your buyer.
* **seller_email**: Enter the email address of your seller.
* **vendor_email**: Enter the email address of your vendor.
