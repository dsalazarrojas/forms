# first_aid_training_licensing_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The first aid training licensing form is a crucial document for certification or registration purposes of healthcare professionals or organizations offering first aid training. This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have all necessary information available.
2. Review each field carefully to understand what is required.
3. Fill out the form accurately and completely.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): This field allows you to input information about the participant.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Please enter the emergency contact person's name and phone number.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's address.
* **Payment Info** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter any payment information related to the training, if applicable.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the payment method used for the training (e.g., credit card, bank transfer, etc.).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): Enter the payment amount received for the training.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of payment for the training.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of payment for the training.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes about the participant or payment.
* **First Aid Training Date** (`first_aid_training_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the first aid training.
* **First Aid Training Time** (`first_aid_training_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the first aid training.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course taken.

## Tips
* Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Ensure you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authority for clarification.
