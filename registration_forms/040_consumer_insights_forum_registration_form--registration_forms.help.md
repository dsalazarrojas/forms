# Consumer Insights Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Consumer Insights Forum Registration Form is a crucial tool used for the registration and organization of attendees for the Consumer Insights Forum. It gathers essential information about each attendee, enabling us to better understand their background, interests, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please select the date you plan to attend by choosing it from the available dates in the "Forum Date" dropdown menu.
2. Fill in your personal details:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address (used for communication)
   - Job Title
   - Company Name
   - Company Industry
   - Company Size
3. Select your role and area of expertise in the consumer insights field:
   - Consumer Insights
   - Market Research
   - Brand Management
   - Product Management
   - Marketing
   - Strategy
   - Executive Leadership
   - Other
4. Indicate your level of experience in consumer insights:
   - Less than 2 Years
   - 2-5 Years
   - 5-10 Years
   - 10-15 Years
   - 15+ Years
5. Choose the topics you are interested in attending by selecting multiple options from the list.
6. Provide a brief description of what you hope to learn from the forum.
7. If applicable, mention any current challenges you are facing.
8. Specify your networking goals, if any.
9. If you have any dietary restrictions or special accessibility needs, please let us know.
10. Indicate how you heard about the forum (not required but recommended for our records).
11. If you were referred by someone, please mention their name.
12. Choose whether you would like to receive marketing communications about future events.
13. Give consent for your photo to be taken (for promotional purposes).
14. Accept the terms and conditions of the forum.
15. Finally, select a date for your submission.

Note: Ensure you fill in the required fields to avoid incomplete submissions. Review your selections carefully, as they will be used to tailor your experience at the forum. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
