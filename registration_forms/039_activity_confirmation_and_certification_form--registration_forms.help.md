# Activity Confirmation And Certification Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Activity Confirmation And Certification Form is a crucial tool for tracking participant engagement and participation in activities, training sessions, and workshops. By completing this form, you will help confirm the participant's attendance and assess their performance, which is essential for certification and future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Enter the **Participant Name** in the text field provided. This is the full name of the person participating in the activity.
2. Enter the **Participant Email** in the text field. This email will be used for certificate delivery and communication.
3. Fill out the **Participant ID** text field if applicable and available.
4. Enter the **Name of Activity or Event** in the text field. This should be the official name of the training, workshop, or event.
5. Enter the **Date of Activity** in the date field. Select the date when the activity took place.
6. Enter the **Activity Location** in the text field. This should be the location where the activity took place.
7. Enter the name of the **Organizing Body** in the text field. This should be the name of the organization or institution hosting the activity.
8. Enter the **Activity Duration** in the numerical field. This should be the length of time the activity lasted (in hours).
9. Select your answer for **Attendance Verification** using the dropdown list. Choose whether the participant attended the full activity, partial activity, or did not attend.
10. Rate the participant's **Participation Quality** using the dropdown list. Choose Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory.
11. Select whether the participant achieved the **Learning Outcomes** using the dropdown list. Choose Yes, all outcomes achieved, Most outcomes achieved, Some outcomes achieved, Few outcomes achieved, or No outcomes achieved.
12. Choose whether the participant is **Eligible for Certification** using the dropdown list. Select Yes, fully eligible, Yes, with conditions, or No, not eligible.
13. Finally, provide any **Additional Comments** about the participant's performance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter the email address for certificate delivery and communication.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, text, optional): Enter a unique identifier for the participant if available.
* **Name of Activity or Event** (`activity_name`, text, required): Enter the official name of the training, workshop, or event.
* **Date of Activity** (`activity_date`, date, required): Select the date when the activity took place.
* **Activity Location** (`activity_location`, text, required): Enter the location where the activity took place.
* **Organizing Body** (`organizer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the organization or institution hosting the activity.
* **Activity Duration** (`activity_duration`, number, required): Enter the length of time the activity lasted (in hours).
* **Attendance Verification** (`attendance_verification`, select_one, required): Choose whether the participant attended the full activity, partial activity, or did not attend.
* **Participation Quality** (`participation_quality`, select_one, required): Rate the participant's engagement as Excellent, Good, Satisfactory, Needs Improvement, or Unsatisfactory.
* **Learning Outcomes Achieved** (`learning_outcomes_achieved`, select_one, required): Choose whether the participant achieved all, most, some, or no outcomes.
* **Eligibility for Certification** (`certification_eligibility`, select_one, required): Choose whether the participant is fully eligible, eligible with conditions, or not eligible.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional remarks about the participant's performance.
