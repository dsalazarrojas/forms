# Sustainability In Leasing Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sustainability In Leasing Seminar Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in attending the Sustainability In Leasing Seminar. This form helps us to get a headcount, understand your interests, and make necessary arrangements for the seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Name.
2. Enter your Email.
3. Provide your Phone number.
4. Select a Date and Time that suits you best.
5. Add any additional Note if necessary.
6. Choose your answer for "Select One" question.
7. Choose multiple options for "Select Multiple" question.
8. Provide Date and Time if required.
9. Select options for "Select One With Options" question.
10. Choose multiple options for "Select Multiple With Options" questions.
11. Enter an Integer value if required.
12. Enter a Decimal value if required.
13. Fill in Text With Label.
14. Fill in Text Without Label (Note: This field is optional).
15. Add a Note With Label if necessary.
16. Add a Note Without Label if necessary.
17. Fill in Email With Label.
18. Fill in Email Without Label (Note: This field is optional).
19. Fill in Phone With Label.
20. Fill in Phone Without Label (Note: This field is optional).
21. Select a Time With Label if necessary.
22. Select a Time Without Label if necessary.
23. Fill in Decimal With Label if required.
24. Fill in any additional information in the Text with Label field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: This is the main form where you can input your information. Please fill it out carefully.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to reach out to you.
* **Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Time**: Select the time you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Note**: Add any additional information or comments about your registration.
* **Select One**: Choose one of the options provided for this question.
* **Select Multiple**: Choose multiple options that apply to you.
* **Date Time**: If required, select the date and time you plan to attend the seminar.
* **Select One With Options**: Choose one of the options provided for this question.
* **Select Multiple With Options**: Choose multiple options that apply to you.
* **Integer**: Enter a whole number if required.
* **Decimal**: Enter a decimal number if required.
* **Text With Label**: Fill in this field with a text description.
* **Text Without Label**: This field is optional and does not require a label. 
* **Note With Label**: Add a note with a label.
* **Note Without Label**: This field is optional and does not require a label.
* **Email With Label**: Fill in this field with an email address.
* **Email Without Label**: This field is optional and does not require a label.
* **Phone With Label**: Fill in this field with a phone number.
* **Phone Without Label**: This field is optional and does not require a label.
* **Time With Label**: Select a time if required.
* **Time Without Label**: This field is optional and does not require a label.
* **Decimal With Label**: Enter a decimal number if required.
