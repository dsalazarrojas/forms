# Corporate Awards Ceremony RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to RSVP (Respond to an Invitation) to the Corporate Awards Ceremony. It is intended for individuals, their spouses, or colleagues to express their interest in attending the event and provide details about their guest(s) and the number of guests they will be bringing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your RSVP information on page 1.
2. Enter your name, the name of your guest(s), and the name of your guest's company (if applicable) on the corresponding fields.
3. Confirm if you are attending the event by selecting "True" for "Attending". If not, select "False".
4. If you are attending, provide the number of guests you will be bringing.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### RSVP Information
* **RSVP Information** (`RSVP Information`, text, required: false): This field is for you to enter your RSVP (Respond to an Invitation) information.

* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name(s) of your guest(s).

* **Guest Company** (`guest_company`, text, required: false): If your guest is affiliated with a company, enter the name of their company.

* **Title** (`guest_title`, text, required: false): Enter your guest's title (e.g. CEO, Manager, etc.).

* **Attending** (`attending`, select_one, required: false): Confirm if you are attending the event by selecting "True" or "False".

* **Others** (`attending_others`, text, required: false): If you selected "False" for "Attending", provide any additional information.

* **Number of Guests** (`number_guests`, number, required: false): Enter the number of guests you will be bringing if you are attending the event.

## Tips
* Please ensure to enter accurate information to help us prepare for the Corporate Awards Ceremony.
* If you are unable to attend, please select "False" for "Attending" and we will remove your entry.
