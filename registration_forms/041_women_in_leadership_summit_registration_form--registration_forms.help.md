# Women in Leadership Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the Women in Leadership Summit. Please fill out this form to ensure your spot at the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your Attendee Name.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Enter your Contact Number.
4. Enter your Job Title.
5. Enter your Organization.
6. Enter your Role at your current organization.
7. Enter your Bio (a brief description of yourself).
8. If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them here.
9. If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them here.
10. If you have any comments, please enter them here.
11. Choose how you would like to confirm your attendance: I am able to attend the event, Unable to attend the event, or Undecided.
12. Finally, select "Yes" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Contact Number**: Enter a phone number where you can be reached.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title at your current organization.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your current organization.
* **Role**: Enter your role at your current organization (e.g. CEO, Manager, etc.).
* **Bio**: Enter a brief description of yourself.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them here.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them here.
* **Comments**: If you have any comments or questions, please enter them here.
* **Confirm Attendance**: Choose how you would like to confirm your attendance: I am able to attend the event, Unable to attend the event, or Undecided.
* **Submit**: Select "Yes" to submit your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unable to attend, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [insert contact information].
