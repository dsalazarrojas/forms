The Priority Enrollment Registration Form is used to collect information from applicants for a priority enrollment program or initiative. This form aims to gather details from applicants and their priority status to further process their enrollment applications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the **Applicant Details** field on the first page.
2. Select the **Priority Status** on the second page from the options provided.
3. If you have any supporting documents, enter them in the **Supporting Documents** field on the third page.

* **Applicant Details** (text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details to help us process your enrollment application.

	+ Priority 2
	+ Priority 3
* **Supporting Documents** (text, required/optional): If you have any documents that support your application, enter them here.
