# Physical Exam Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Physical Exam Course Registration" form is designed to collect information about a physical exam course, including course title, exam type, dates, venue, and instructor details. This form is used to create, manage, and track physical exam courses.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the course title field with the title of the physical exam course.
2. Select the exam type (Yes or No) and fill in the other fields as required or optional.
3. Enter the start and end dates of the course.
4. Fill in the venue and location fields.
5. Add the instructor's name.
6. Enter the phone number and email address.
7. Specify the number of participants.
8. Select the payment status and method.
9. Set the course duration.
10. Enter the exam and course status.
11. Add any course notes and code (optional).
12. Set the created, updated and updated dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Title** (id: 1, text, required): Enter the title of the course.
* **Exam Type** (id: 2, select_one, required): Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if this is an exam course.
* **Start Date** (id: 3, date, required): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (id: 4, date, required): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Venue** (id: 5, text, required): Enter the venue where the course will be held.
* **Address** (id: 6, text, optional): Enter the address of the venue.
* **Location** (id: 7, text, optional): Enter the location of the venue (if different from address).
* **Instructor Name** (id: 8, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Phone Number** (id: 9, text, optional): Enter the instructor's phone number.
* **Email** (id: 10, email, optional): Enter the instructor's email.
* **Number of Participants** (id: 11, number, optional): Enter the number of participants in the course.
* **Payment Status** (id: 12, select_one, optional): Choose between "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the payment status.
* **Payment Method** (id: 13, select_multiple, optional): Choose between "Yes" or "No" to indicate if payment method is available.
* **Course Duration** (id: 14, number, optional): Enter the duration of the course.
* **Exam Date** (id: 15, date, optional): Enter the exam date.
* **time** (id: 16, time, optional): Enter the time of the exam.
* **Course Status** (id: 17, select_one, optional): Choose between "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate the course status.
* **Course Notes** (id: 18, text, optional): Enter any notes about the course.
* **Course Code** (id: 19, text, optional): Enter the course code.
* **Created By** (id: 20, text, optional): Enter the name of the person who created the course.
