# Animation Storyboarding Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from clients to facilitate the appointment of an animation project. It helps our team to better understand their needs, expectations, and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form

To ensure that you can effectively provide your animation needs, please complete the form accurately. The following steps will guide you through the process:

1.  Fill in your client information, including your name and company/project name (if applicable).
2.  Provide your email address for confirmation and any contact number for ease of communication.
3.  Choose a preferred date and time for the meeting.
4.  Decide on the appointment format (in-person, video conference, phone call, or hybrid).
5.  Specify the expected meeting duration.
6.  If applicable, provide the project name, brief description, and target audience.
7.  Describe the project's scope, including estimated scene and duration.
8.  Define the artistic style and any special requirements.
9.  Mention any special requirements or constraints.
10.  Lastly, provide the planned project start and deadline dates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Name**: Your full name, which we will use to address and communicate with you.
* **Company or Project Name**: Your organization or project name, if applicable.
* **Email Address**: The email address you'd like us to use for appointment confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number for easy communication.
* **Preferred Appointment Date**: The date you prefer for the meeting.
* **Preferred Appointment Time**: The time you prefer for the meeting.
* **Appointment Format**: The way you'd like to meet, either in-person, via video conference, phone call, or hybrid.
* **Expected Duration**: The length of the meeting you anticipate.
* **Project Name**: The name of your animation project.
* **Brief Project Description**: A short description of your project.
* **Project Genre or Type**: The type of animation project you're undertaking.
* **Target Audience**: Who the animation is for, e.g., children, adults, or a specific age range.
* **Estimated Number of Scenes**: The estimated number of scenes for your project.
* **Estimated Project Duration**: The expected final length of the animation.
* **Desired Visual Style**: The aesthetic you envision for your project.
* **Visual Style References**: Any reference points or videos that demonstrate your desired visual style.
* **Special Requirements or Constraints**: Any specific needs or limitations for your project.
* **Planned Project Start Date**: The date when you plan to start the project.
* **Project Deadline**: The deadline for completing the project.
