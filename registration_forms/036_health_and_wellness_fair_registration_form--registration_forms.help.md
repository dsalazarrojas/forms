# Health and Wellness Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Health and Wellness Fair event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure you have filled out all required fields.
3. Review your submission before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details**: This section captures your personal details as a participant. You can enter any relevant information about yourself.
* **Event Details**: Please provide any specific details about the event that you would like to participate in, such as dates and times you are available.
* **Contact Information**: Enter a way for us to contact you regarding event updates and information.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide a contact person's name and number to reach out in case of an emergency.
* **Event Preferences**: Share any specific preferences or requirements you may have for the event, such as accommodations or accessibility needs.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us at [contact information].
