# Pandemic Resilience Learning Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration and contact information from participants in the Pandemic Resilience Learning Network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form fields as instructed.
2. Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**:
	+ This section is used to collect basic registration information.
* **Participant Information**:
	+ This field is used to collect participant information such as name, address, phone number, etc.
	+ Please provide accurate and complete information for this field.
* **Emergency Contacts**:
	+ This field is used to collect emergency contact information.
	+ Please provide names and contact numbers for at least one emergency contact.
* **Contact Preference**:
	+ This field allows you to select your preferred contact method.
* **Participant Information 2 - 12**:
	+ These fields are used to collect additional participant information.
	+ Please provide accurate and complete information for these fields.
* **Contact Preference 2 - 7**:
	+ These fields allow you to select your preferred contact method for each field.
* **Emergency Contacts 2 - 4**:
	+ These fields are used to collect additional emergency contact information.
	+ Please provide names and contact numbers for each field.
* **Participant Information 13**:
	+ This field is used to collect additional participant information.
	+ Please provide accurate and complete information for this field.

Note: Please fill out each field accurately and completely, and review your answers before submitting the form. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
