# Digital Marketing Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for user registration for a digital marketing training program. The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who are interested in attending the training program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your company name in the "Company" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter your company website in the "Company Website" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Select a training session from the "Training Session" dropdown list.
8. Add any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field if needed.
9. Confirm your registration in the "Confirm" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: This is a text input field for general comments and information. Please fill in this field with any additional comments you may have.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to be used for communication.
* **Company**: Enter your company name where you work.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your company.
* **Company Website**: Enter your company website (if applicable).
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth (if required for training purposes).
* **Training Session**: Select which session you would like to attend from the dropdown list.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Confirm**: Confirm that you have read and understood the registration information.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address for communication purposes.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* Please note that the "confirm" field is not strictly required, but it is still recommended that you confirm your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
