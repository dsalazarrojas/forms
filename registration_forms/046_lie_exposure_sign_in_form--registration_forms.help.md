# Lie Exposure Sign In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Lie Exposure Sign In Form" is a critical tool for collecting participant information for a specific event. This form is designed to gather essential details about the participant, including their name, email, phone number, country, state, city, and a brief note if required. Accurate and thorough responses are necessary to ensure that the participant's data is properly recorded and processed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the event name to which you are registering.
2. Enter your event date or the date of the event you wish to attend.
3. Provide your name as the participant.
4. Enter your email address as the participant's contact information.
5. Enter your phone number as the participant's contact information.
6. Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the event (optional).
7. Select your country of origin.
8. Choose your state or province of origin.
9. Select your city of origin.
10. Choose your country code (e.g., +1 for the United States).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are registering for or the event you wish to attend. This is a required field to help us verify the event and ensure that you are registering for the correct event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you are registering for or the date of the event you wish to attend. This is a required field to ensure that we can schedule the event correctly.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as the participant in the event. This is a required field to identify you as the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address as the participant's contact information. This is a required field to ensure that we can contact you about the event.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number as the participant's contact information. This is a required field to ensure that we can contact you about the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation in the event. This is an optional field, but it helps us understand your preferences and requirements.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, select_multiple, required): Select your country of origin from the available options. This is a required field to ensure that we can process your registration correctly.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, select_one, required): Select your state or province of origin from the available options. This is a required field to ensure that we can process your registration correctly.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, select_one, required): Select your city of origin from the available options. This is a required field to ensure that we can process your registration correctly.
* **Participant Country Code** (`participant_country_code`, select_one, required): Choose your country code from the available options. This is a required field to ensure that we can process your registration correctly.
