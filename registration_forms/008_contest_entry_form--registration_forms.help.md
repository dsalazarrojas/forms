# Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who wish to participate in a contest. This form is used to gather contact and entry details from contestants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the entrant information:
	* Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
	* Enter your primary contact email address.
	* Enter your phone number.
	* Enter your date of birth (in MM/DD/YYYY format).
2. Confirm your age eligibility:
	* Choose "Yes, I am 18 or older" if you are 18 years or older.
3. Provide your address information:
	* Enter your street address.
	* Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
	* Enter your city.
	* Enter your state.
	* Enter your ZIP code.
4. Choose your entry category:
	* Select the category that best describes your entry.
5. Enter your entry details:
	* Enter the title of your submission.
	* Enter a description of your entry.
	* Upload your submission file.
6. Payment (optional):
	* If required, enter your entry fee payment confirmation number.
7. Other information (optional):
	* If you have entered before, indicate yes or no.
	* Choose how you heard about this contest.
	* Sign up for the contest newsletter (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Entrant Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Age Verification**: Choose "Yes, I am 18 or older" if you are 18 years or older.
* **Address Line 1**: Enter your street address.
* **Address Line 2**: Enter your apartment or suite number (if applicable).
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **State**: Enter your state.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP code.
* **Country**: Choose your country of residence.
* **Contest Category**: Select the category that best describes your entry.
* **Entry Title**: Enter the title of your submission.
* **Entry Description**: Enter a description of your entry.
* **Entry File Upload**: Upload your submission file.
* **Entry Fee Payment**: If required, enter your entry fee payment confirmation number.
* **Previous Entries**: If you have entered before, choose "Yes" or "No".
* **How Did You Hear About This Contest?**: Choose how you heard about this contest.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Choose "I accept" to agree to the contest rules.
* **Publicity Release**: Choose "Yes, I agree" to allow use of your name and entry for promotion.
* **Newsletter Signup**: Choose "Yes, sign me up" to receive contest updates.
