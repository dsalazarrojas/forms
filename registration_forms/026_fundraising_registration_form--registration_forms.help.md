# Fundraising Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fundraising Registration Form is used to collect information for fundraising efforts. This form is designed to be filled out once per user for each specific fundraising effort.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Enter your User Name, a unique identifier for your fundraising efforts.
3. Enter your email address to allow for future communication.
4. Enter your contact number for additional support and updates.
5. Specify the amount raised for your fundraising effort.
6. Describe your fundraising goal, a clear statement of what you are raising funds for.
7. Choose the type of fundraising activity you are engaged in.
8. Provide a detailed description of your fundraising effort.
9. Enter your user address, a physical location for your fundraising activity.
10. Finally, submit the form to register your fundraising effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (1, text, required): Enter your unique identifier for this fundraising effort.
* **Email** (2, text, required): Enter a valid email address to allow for communication and updates.
* **Contact Number** (3, text, required): Enter a number where you can be reached for additional support.
* **Amount Raised** (4, number, required): Enter the total amount raised for your fundraising effort.
* **Fundraising Goal** (5, text, required): Describe the purpose and objective of your fundraising effort.
* **Fundraising Type** (6, select_multiple, required): Choose from the list of available fundraising types offered.
* **Fundraising Description** (7, note, required): Provide a detailed description of your fundraising effort, goals, and progress.
* **User Address** (8, text, required): Enter your physical location for the fundraising activity.
* **Submit** (9, text, required): Complete this form and click submit to register your fundraising effort.

## Tips
* Double-check your input before submission to avoid errors.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you need help or clarification, contact the support team.
