# Food Truck License Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about your food truck business, including contact details, permit information, and business specifics. It will help the authorities process and issue licenses efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, such as contact information and business address.
2. Provide information about your food truck, including its license numbers and permit details.
3. Indicate which food items you sell.
4. Select your payment method and date/time of payment.
5. Enter any other relevant details, such as your business email and website.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Information (Page 1)**: Enter your personal details, including your name and contact information.
	+ **Applicant Name** (`applicant_info`): Enter your full name.
	+ **Applicant Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your phone number and email address.
* **Truck Details (Page 2)**: Provide information about your food truck, including its license numbers and permit details.
	+ **Truck License Number** (`truck_license_number`): Enter the unique license number of your truck.
	+ **Food License Number** (`food_license_number`): Enter the unique license number for your food business.
* **Permit Information (Page 3)**: Provide details about your permit, including its type and issuance date.
	+ **Food License Type** (`food_license_type`): Select whether you have a food license or not.
	+ **Food Cuisines** (`food_cuisine`): Select which types of food you sell (e.g., Yes/No for each type).
* **Business Information (Page 4)**: Enter your business details, including its name and address.
	+ **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter your business name.
	+ **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter your business address.
	+ **Business Phone** (`business_phone`): Enter your business phone number.
	+ **Business Email** (`business_email`): Enter your business email address.
	+ **Business Website** (`business_website`): Enter your business website.
* **Payment Information (Page 5)**: Indicate how you plan to pay for the license and when you paid.
	+ **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select your payment method (e.g., Yes/No).
	+ **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date you paid for the license.
	+ **Payment Time** (`payment_time`): Enter the time you paid for the license.

Note: Some fields are optional, but please ensure that you fill in all required fields to avoid any issues with your license application.
