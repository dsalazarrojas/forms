# Mini Album Class Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mini Album Class Registration" form is used to gather information from students to enroll in an album class. 

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Fill out any optional fields if necessary.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **student_info** (`student_info`, text, required): Fill in your name, last name, address, and other relevant information about yourself.
* **contact_details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your phone number, email address, and any other contact information that will help the instructor get in touch with you.
* **registration** (`registration`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or notes for registration, please provide them here.
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name, phone number, and address of the emergency contact person.
* **medical_info** (`medical_info`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies that the instructor should be aware of, please provide that information here.
* **agreement** (`agreement`, text, optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the album class.
* **student_signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): Please sign your name to confirm your registration.
* **submit** (`submit`, text, optional): This is a submit button, do not fill this out.
* **contact_us** (`contact_us`, text, optional): If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us at this number and address.
* **date** (`date`, date, optional): The date of the course start.
* **time** (`time`, time, optional): The time of the course start.
* **instructor_info** (`instructor_info`, text, optional): Additional information about the instructor.
* **instructor_phone** (`instructor_phone`, text, optional): The phone number of the instructor.
* **instructor_email** (`instructor_email`, text, optional): The email address of the instructor.
* **instructor_address** (`instructor_address`, text, optional): The address of the instructor.
* **instructor_notes** (`instructor_notes`, text, optional): Any additional notes from the instructor.
* **course_name** (`course_name`, text, optional): The name of the course.
* **student_id** (`student_id`, text, optional): A student ID number.
* **instructor_id** (`instructor_id`, text, optional): An instructor ID number.
* **student_dob** (`student_dob`, date, optional): The student's date of birth.
* **course_start_date** (`course_start_date`, date, optional): The date when the course will start.
* **course_end_date** (`course_end_date`, date, optional): The date when the course will end.
* **hours_per_week** (`hours_per_week`, number, optional): The number of hours per week.
* **days_per_week** (`days_per_week`, number, optional): The number of days per week.
* **student_attendance** (`student_attendance`, select multiple, optional): The days you will attend.

Note: The form includes fields for both students and instructors. Please fill in the relevant information as per your role. 

This guide is intended to be used for both students and instructors to understand the form. However, if you are an instructor, please fill in the corresponding fields for your own information.
