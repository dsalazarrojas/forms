# HR Policy Modernization Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Policy Modernization Roundtable Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to ensure a productive and inclusive discussion during the roundtable.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Registration Form** on page 1 with your basic information.
2. On page 2, provide your **Attendee Details**.
3. On page 3, indicate your **Session Preferences**.
4. On page 4, mention any **Accessibility Needs** you may have.
5. Finally, confirm your **Registration** on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`id: 1`, Text, Required/Optional: Not Applicable):
  This field is for basic registration information. You can enter your name or any other relevant details as required.
* **Attendee Details** (`id: 2`, Text, Required/Optional: Not Applicable):
  Please provide your details as required in this field.
* **Session Preferences** (`id: 3`, Text, Required/Optional: Not Applicable):
  Indicate your preferences for the roundtable discussion, such as topics or time slots.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`id: 4`, Text, Required/Optional: Not Applicable):
  If you have any accessibility needs or requirements, please mention them here.
* **Confirm Registration** (`id: 5`, Text, Required/Optional: Not Applicable):
  Confirm that you are registering for the roundtable.
