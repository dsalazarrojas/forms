# Fitness Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Class Registration Form is designed to collect user information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the pages of the form, which include User Details, Registration Details, and Emergency Contact.
2. Fill out each section according to your information, providing accurate and relevant data.
3. Ensure that all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`User Details`, `text`, optional): Enter your basic information, such as your name, email, and other contact details that you feel comfortable sharing with us.
* **Registration Details** (`Registration Details`, `text`, optional): Provide your fitness class registration information, such as your class preferences and any medical conditions or special requests you'd like to inform us about.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): In case of an emergency, please enter the name, relationship, and contact information of a person we can reach out to on your behalf.

## Tips

* Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and truthfully, as this information will be used to facilitate your participation in the fitness class.
* If you have any special requests or conditions, please make sure to mention them in the registration details field.
* If you need any assistance with the form, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
