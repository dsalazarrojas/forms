# Human Centered Learning Design Fellowship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering individuals interested in joining the Human Centered Learning Design Fellowship. It collects information that will be used to process your application.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
6. Enter your city in the "City" field.
7. Enter your state in the "State" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
9. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
10. Enter your Date of Submission in the "Date of Submission" field.
11. Enter your Time of Submission in the "Time of Submission" field.
12. Enter your Submitter ID in the "Submitter ID" field.
13. Enter your Organization in the "Organization" field.
14. Enter your Role in the "Role" field.
15. Enter your Department in the "Department" field.
16. Enter your Job Title in the "Job Title" field.
17. Select your Country from the drop-down list in the "Country" field.
18. If applicable, select one option from the "Select One" field.
19. Enter any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field (optional).
20. Read and agree to the terms of service in the "Terms" field.
21. (Optional) Enter your submission date in the "Submit Date" field.
22. (Optional) Enter the time of submission in the "Submit Time" field.
23. (Optional) Enter your name as the submitter in the "Submitter" field.
24. (Optional) Enter your phone number in the "Submitter Phone" field.
25. (Optional) Enter your email address in the "Submitter Email" field.

* **Submitter ID** (text, required): Enter your Submitter ID here.

* **Role** (text, required): Enter your role here.
