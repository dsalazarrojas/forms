# Cultural Event Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Event Communication Form is a tool for event organizers and participants to share information about cultural events. It is used to record the event's details, including the event name, date, time, participant's name, and any additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Name** field with the name of the event.
2. Enter the **Organiser Name** field with the name of the person or organization hosting the event.
3. Fill in the **Participant Name** field with your name, as the participant or attendee.
4. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
5. Enter your **Phone** number for easy contact.
6. Fill in the **Date** field with the date of the event.
7. Fill in the **Time** field with the scheduled time of the event.
8. If you have any feedback or comments about the event, use the **Feedback** field.
9. If you have any additional comments or requests, use the **Additional Comments** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the event.

### **Organiser Name** (`organiser_name`, text, required: false)
Enter the name of the person or organization hosting the event.

### **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false)
Enter your name as the participant or attendee.

### **Email** (`email`, email, required: false)
Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false)
Enter your phone number for easy contact.

### **Date** (`event_date`, date, required: false)
Enter the date of the event using the correct date format (YYYY-MM-DD).

### **Time** (`event_time`, time, required: false)
Enter the scheduled time of the event using the correct time format (HH:MM AM/PM).

### **Feedback** (`participant_feedback`, text, required: false)
Enter any feedback or comments you have about the event.

### **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, required: false)
Enter any additional comments or requests you have about the event.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill up the form correctly and completely to maintain accurate records.
- If you are an event participant, fill up the **Participant Name**, **Phone**, and **Email** fields with your correct details.
- If you are an event organizer, fill up the **Event Name**, **Organiser Name**, and **Date** and **Time** fields with the correct details.
