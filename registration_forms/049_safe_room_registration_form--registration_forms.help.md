# safe_room_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "safe_room_registration_form" is a form used to register a safe room and gather essential contact information for emergency purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the resident's name in the "Resident Name" field.
2. Provide the contact name in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter the contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
4. Enter the contact email in the "Contact Email" field, ensuring it is a valid email address.
5. Enter the address of the location in the "Address" field.
6. Enter the street name in the "Street" field.
7. Enter the apartment or unit number in the "Apt" field.
8. Enter the city name in the "City" field.
9. Enter the state name in the "State" field.
10. Enter the zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. Enter the latitude coordinates in the "Latitude" field.
12. Enter the longitude coordinates in the "Longitude" field.
13. Select the relationship between the resident and the emergency contact from the options.
14. Select one or more safe rooms from the options.
15. Click submit to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Resident Name** (text): Enter the name of the resident.
* **Contact Name** (text): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (email): Enter a valid email address for the contact person.
* **Address** (text): Enter the address of the location.
* **Street** (text): Enter the street name where the resident is located.
* **Apt** (text): Enter the apartment or unit number.
* **City** (text): Enter the city name where the resident is located.
* **State** (text): Enter the state name where the resident is located.
* **Zip** (text): Enter the zip code of the location.
* **Latitude** (number): Enter the latitude coordinates of the location.
* **Longitude** (number): Enter the longitude coordinates of the location.
* **Emergency Contact** (select_one): Select the relationship between the resident and the emergency contact.
* **Safe Room** (select_multiple): Select one or more safe rooms from the options.
  - **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Select "Emergency Contact", "Family Member", "Neighbor", or "Other" to define the relationship between the resident and the emergency contact.
  - **Safe Rooms**: Select one or more "Living Room", "Bedroom", "Attic", or "Basement" to define the safe room(s) for the resident.
