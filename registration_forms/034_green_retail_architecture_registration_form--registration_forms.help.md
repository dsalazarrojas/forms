# Green Retail Architecture Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from participants in a green retail architecture event or conference. It gathers details about the participants, their interests, and their affiliation with organizations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Scroll through the form pages and review each section carefully.
2. Answer all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk *).
3. Fill out the form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: This section collects basic information about you as a participant.
	+ **Title**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Select Your Interests**: Choose one or more interests that align with your work or studies in retail architecture.
	+ This section is optional but helps us understand your area of specialization.
* **Name**: Enter your name to associate with your registration.
	+ This field is optional but helps us identify you as a participant.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or designation in your current organization.
	+ This field is optional but helps us understand your current role.
* **Department**: Enter the department or team you belong to in your current organization.
	+ This field is optional but helps us understand your current work environment.
* **Email**: Enter your official email address associated with your job or organization.
	+ This field is optional but helps us stay in touch with you for event updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your office or mobile phone number for contact purposes.
	+ This field is optional but helps us reach you for event-related matters.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are attending (e.g., "Green Retail Architecture Conference").
	+ This field is optional but helps us understand the context of your attendance.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your current or affiliated organization.
	+ This field is optional but helps us understand your institutional background.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure that we have the most up-to-date information about you.
* If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, feel free to ask the event organizers for assistance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to prevent errors.
