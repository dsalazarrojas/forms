# Legal Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Legal Client Intake Form is used by law firms to collect essential information from clients, enabling the firm to manage cases effectively and provide high-quality services. This form is a critical tool for gathering client contact and identification details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled in according to the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Legal Client Intake Form** (Legal Client Intake Form, text, required): This is the title of the form.
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Legal Concerns** (legal_concerns, text, required): Describe the client's legal concerns or issues.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required): Enter the client's contact information, including any relevant details for communication.
* **Address** (address, text, required): Enter the client's address.
* **Email** (email, email, required): Enter the client's email address.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, number, required): Enter the client's phone number.
* **Agreement** (agreement, text, required): Confirm that the client understands and agrees to the terms and conditions of the firm's services.
* **Notes** (notes, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes relevant to the case.

## Tips
* Make sure to review and fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check the accuracy and completeness of the information entered.
* Use a standardized format for consistency and ease of reference.
* Keep the information up-to-date and current throughout the case management process.
