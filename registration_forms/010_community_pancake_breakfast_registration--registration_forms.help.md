# Community Pancake Breakfast Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect essential information from individuals who plan to attend the Community Pancake Breakfast event. The form helps us understand your preferences and needs, ensuring we can accommodate you properly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields.
2.  Enter your Email Address for event updates.
3.  Provide your Phone Number for contact purposes.
4.  Indicate the Total Number of Attendees, including yourself.
5.  If you have children under 12 years old, enter their count.
6.  Select your preferred Seating Preference from the options.
7.  Specify any Dietary Restrictions or Allergies you may have.
8.  Choose your Pancake Preference from the options.
9.  Select any Additional Items you would like to have available during the event.
10.  If you'd like to volunteer, select your preferred Volunteer Tasks from the options.
11.  Decide if you'd like to make a donation and enter the Donation Amount in dollars.
12.  Let us know how you heard about the event.
13.  Confirm if you're bringing friends or neighbors.
14.  Specify any Accessibility Needs you may have.
15.  Add any Additional Comments or Requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Attendee Information** (`attendee_info`, `note`, optional): Provide the name and details of the person registering, such as their name and relationship to the event.
*  **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
*  **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
*  **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive updates on the event.
*  **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
*  **Total Number of Attendees** (`household_size`, `number`, required): Enter the total number of people attending, including yourself.
*  **Number of Children** (`children_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the count of children under 12 years old.
*  **Event Details** (`event_details`, `note`, optional): Describe any specific needs or preferences you have for the event.
*  **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, `select_one`, required): Choose from Indoor Seating, Outdoor Seating, No Preference, or Takeout Only.
*  **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
*  **Pancake Preference** (`pancake_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select from Regular Pancakes, Blueberry Pancakes, Chocolate Chip Pancakes, Gluten-Free Option, Vegan Option, or No Pancakes.
*  **Additional Items Desired** (`additional_items`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose any Additional Items you would like to have available during the event.
*  **Would You Like to Volunteer** (`volunteer_interest`, `select_one`, required): Choose Yes - Before Event, Yes - During Event, Yes - After Event, or No - Just Attending.
*  **Preferred Volunteer Tasks** (`volunteer_tasks`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all that apply from Setup, Cooking, Serving, Cashier, Cleanup, or Greeting.
*  **Would You Like to Make a Donation** (`donation_interest`, `select_one`, optional): Choose True, False, or Maybe.
*  **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`, `number`, optional): Enter the donation amount in dollars.
*  **How Did You Hear About This Event** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, optional): Choose from Social Media, Flyer/Poster, Word of Mouth, Email Newsletter, Local Newspaper, Community Website, or Other.
*  **Are You Bringing Friends or Neighbors** (`bring_friends`, `select_one`, optional): Choose True or False.
*  **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): Specify any accessibility needs you may have.
*  **Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or requests you may have.
