# HR Analytics Panel Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Analytics Panel Registration Form is a tool for registering HR panel information and managing various aspects of user roles, interests, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name** in the text field labeled "Full Name". This field is used to capture your name for registration purposes.
2. Enter your **Email** in the email field labeled "Email". This is the email address we will use to contact you about your registration.
3. Select your **Department** from the text field labeled "Department". Please enter your department name as it appears in your organization's directory.
4. Enter your **Position** in the text field labeled "Position". This field is used to capture your role within your department.
5. Select your **HR Panel Interests** from the dropdown list labeled "HR Panel Interests". Please choose one or more of the options that best describe your interests.
6. If applicable, enter your **Date Of Interest** in the date field labeled "Date Of Interest". This field is used to capture the date you became interested in the HR panel.
7. Enter your **Time Commitment** in the time field labeled "Time Commitment". Please enter a 24-hour time in the format HH:MM or just the minutes for a half-hour commitment.
8. Enter your **Availability** in the text field labeled "Availability". This field is used to capture how much time you can commit to the HR panel.
9. Enter any additional **Notes** you may have about your registration in the text field labeled "Notes".
10. Confirm your **Confirmation** by selecting either "True" or "False" in the dropdown list labeled "Confirmation". Please confirm your interest in being part of the HR panel.
11. If necessary, provide any **Comments** about your registration in the free-form text field labeled "Comments".
12. You don't need to fill out the email fields like "Email Confirmed", "Position Confirmed", "Comments Confirmed", and "Department Confirmed" unless otherwise instructed by the registration committee.
13. Finally, enter the **Confirmation Date** and **Confirmation Time** if you're confirming your interest in the HR panel. These fields are optional and used to capture the date and time of your confirmation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (1, text, required: false): Capture your name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (2, email, required: false): Enter your company email for contact purposes.
* **Department** (3, text, required: false): Enter your department name from your organization's directory.
* **Position** (4, text, required: false): Enter your role or position within your department.
* **HR Panel Interests** (6, select_one, required: true): Select one or more of your interests from the provided options.
* **Date Of Interest** (7, date, required: false): Enter the date you became interested in the HR panel, if applicable.
* **Time Commitment** (8, time, required: false): Enter a 24-hour time in the format HH:MM or just the minutes for a half-hour commitment.
* **Availability** (9, text, required: false): Enter how much time you can commit to the HR panel.
* **Notes** (10, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Confirmation** (11, select_multiple, required: true): Confirm your interest in being part of the HR panel by selecting "True".
* **Comments** (12, text, required: false): Enter any comments about your registration.
* **Email Confirmed** (13, email, required: false): This field is for the registration committee's use.
* **Position Confirmed** (14, email, required: false): This field is for the registration committee's use.
* **Comments Confirmed** (15, email, required: false): This field is for the registration committee's use.
* **Department Confirmed** (16, text, required: false): This field is for the registration committee's use.
* **Confirmation Date** (17, date, required: false): Enter the date you confirmed your interest in the HR panel, if required.
* **Confirmation Time** (18, time, required: false): Enter the time you confirmed your interest, if required.
