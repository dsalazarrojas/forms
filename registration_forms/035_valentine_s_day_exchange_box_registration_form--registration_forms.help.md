# valentines_day_exchange_box_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The valentines_day_exchange_box_registration_form is used to register or sign up individuals for a Valentine's Day exchange box activity. This activity likely involves collecting and exchanging gifts among participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your "First Name" and "Last Name" to provide personal details.
2. Enter your valid "Email" address and confirm it in the "Confirm Email" field.
3. Optionally, provide your "Phone" number for follow-up communication.
4. Select your preferred "Exchange Box" size from the dropdown list.
5. Provide a monetary value for your gift in the "Gift Value" field.
6. Describe your gift in the "Gift Details" field.
7. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address to be used for communication.
* **Confirm Email** (`email_confirmation`, email, required): Confirm your email address to prevent errors.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
* **Birthday** (`birthday`, date, optional): Select your birthday, if applicable, for gift-giving purposes.
* **What type of exchange box are you interested in?** (`exchange_box_interests`, select one, optional): Choose the size of exchange box that interests you.
* **Gift Value** (`gift_value`, number, optional): Enter the monetary value of the gift you're willing to give or receive.
* **Gift Details** (`gift_details`, note, optional): Describe your gift in this field.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or messages to be included with the gift.
