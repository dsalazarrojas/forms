# Bachelorette Weekend Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Bachelorette Weekend Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to create a fun and enjoyable experience for everyone. This form will help us understand your preferences, contact details, and travel plans to ensure a well-planned trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Bachelorette Weekend Getaway Registration" heading to begin.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and mobile number accurately and completely.
3. Provide your estimated arrival and departure times.
4. Select your preferred activities and drinks from the given options.
5. Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
6. Check in with a friend or family member as an emergency contact.
7. Confirm your understanding of the estimated cost per person.
8. Choose your preferred payment method (Venmo, Cash App, Bank Transfer, or Other).
9. Finally, add any additional notes or requests you'd like to share with the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name** (`attendee_full_name`, **text**, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, **email**, required): Enter your email address for future communication and updates.
* **Mobile Number** (`attendee_phone`, **text**, required): Enter your mobile number for easy contact.
* **Estimated Arrival Time** (`arrival_datetime`, **text**, required): Specify when you plan to arrive at the location.
* **Estimated Departure Time** (`departure_datetime`, **text**, required): Enter your estimated departure time.
* **T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`, **select_one**, required): Choose your T-shirt size for the custom-made shirts.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Life-Threatening Allergies** (`dietary_allergies_weekend`, **text**, required): List any dietary restrictions or allergies you have, so we can accommodate your needs.
* **Alcohol Preference** (`alcohol_preference`, **select_multiple**, optional): Select from options to indicate your drink preferences.
* **Activity Interests** (`activities_interest`, **select_multiple**, required): Choose from options that sound like fun activities you'd enjoy.
* **Any Roommate Requests or Special Accommodation Needs?** (`accommodation_notes`, **text**, optional): Share any roommate preferences or special requirements you may have.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Number** (`emergency_contact_weekend`, **text**, required): Enter the name and number of a friend or family member to contact in case of an emergency.
* **I understand the Estimated Cost Per Person** (`total_estimated_cost_acknowledgment`, **select_one**, required): Confirm your understanding of the estimated cost per person.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_status_weekend`, **select_one**, required): Choose from options for your payment method.
* **Anything Else We Should Know?** (`notes_to_organizers`, **text**, optional): Leave any additional comments or requests for the organizers.

Note: Please make sure to review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to the group organizers.
