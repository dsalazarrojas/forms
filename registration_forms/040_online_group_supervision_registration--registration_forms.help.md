# Online Group Supervision Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for an online group supervision session. It captures essential information about the session and participant, ensuring a smooth and organized session for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **Session Details** field with any relevant information about the session.
2. In the **Participant Information** field, provide any personal details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
3. Choose your preferred **Supervision Preferences** in the corresponding field.
4. Select your **Payment Method** from the options provided.
5. Enter your **Phone** number if you have one.
6. Provide your **Email** address for contact purposes.
7. Repeat your **Email** address in the **Email Repeat** field for verification purposes.
8. If you have any additional **Notes** or comments, you can add them in the **Notes** field.
9. Finally, repeat any **Notes** you have in the **Notes Repeat** field for your records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details** (id: 1, label: Session Details, type: text, required: false): This field is used to capture any relevant information about the session.
* **Participant Information** (id: 2, label: Participant Information, type: text, required: false): Please provide any personal details about yourself, such as your name and contact information.
* **Supervision Preferences** (id: 3, label: Supervision Preferences, type: text, required: false): Select your preferred supervision style or approach.
* **Payment Method** (id: 4, label: Payment Method, type: select_one, required: false): Choose how you would like to pay for the supervision session.
* **Phone** (id: 5, label: Phone, type: text, required: false): If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
* **Email** (id: 6, label: Email, type: email, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Email Repeat** (id: 7, label: Email Repeat, type: email, required: false): Repeat your email address for verification purposes.
* **Notes** (id: 8, label: Notes, type: note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about the supervision session.
* **Notes Repeat** (id: 9, label: Notes Repeat, type: note, required: false): Repeat any additional notes or comments for your records.
