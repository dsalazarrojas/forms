# EMail Opt In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect a user's email address for opt-in purposes, enabling them to receive updates, promotions, or newsletters from us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Registration Form** field with any information you'd like to provide (optional).
2. In the **Confirm Email** field, re-enter your email address to ensure it's accurate.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`Registration Form`, text, optional): This field is a general text input where you can provide any information you'd like, but it's not required.
* **Confirm Email** (`Confirm Email`, text, optional): This field is used to re-enter your email address to confirm it's accurate. Please re-enter the same email address you entered in the **Email** field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): This is the field where you should enter your email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g., `user@example.com`).
