# Trivia Night Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants about their intention to attend the trivia night event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant information on the first page.
2. Indicate your team size and confirm if you are attending on the second page.
3. Enter the name of the team or individual you are attending with on the third page.
4. Add any additional details about your attendance on the fourth page.
5. Confirm your team name (if applicable) on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other details you would like to share about yourself.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, number, required: false): Indicate how many people are in your team.
* **Are you attending** (`is_attending`, select_one, required: false): Select one of the options to confirm your attendance.
* **Name of Attending** (`name_of_attending`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the person(s) you are attending with.
* **Other Attending** (`name_of_attending_other`, text, required: false): If you are attending with someone else, enter their name here.
* **Attending Details** (`attending_details`, text, required: false): Add any additional details about your attendance, such as a special request or message.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your team (if you're attending as a team).
* **Other Team Name** (`team_name_other`, text, required: false): If you are attending as part of a team, enter their name here.
