# Animal Care Equipment Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Animal Care Equipment Showcase event, including attendees, exhibitors, and speakers. It collects information on registrant details, event participation, and exhibitor information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Select your Registration Type (Attendee, Exhibitor, etc.).
3. Choose the event date(s) you will attend.
4. Select your Professional Category (e.g., Veterinarian, Veterinary technician).
5. Select the type of organization you are affiliated with.
6. Choose the equipment categories you are interested in (e.g., Diagnostic equipment, Surgical instruments).
7. If you are an exhibitor, please provide your booth size preference and any special booth requirements.
8. Select any session preferences (e.g., New technology demonstrations, Best practices workshops).
9. Indicate your interest in networking opportunities.
10. Specify your meal preferences (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan).
11. If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
12. Select how you heard about the event (e.g., Email newsletter, Social media).
13. Indicate if this is your first time attending the event.
14. State your expectations for the event.
15. Choose your registration fee category.
16. Select your payment method.
17. If registering additional attendees, please specify the number.
18. Provide any additional comments or questions.
19. Accept the event terms and conditions.
20. Finally, sign and date the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as it appears on your official records.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address or email address you check regularly.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your work phone number or personal phone number.
* **Registration Type**: Select how you wish to participate in the event (Attendee, Exhibitor, etc.).
* **Event Date**: Select the date(s) you will attend the event.
* **Professional Category**: Select your field of expertise or profession (e.g., Veterinarian, Veterinary technician).
* **Organization Type**: Select the type of organization you are affiliated with (e.g., Veterinary clinic, Animal shelter).
* **Equipment Categories of Interest**: Choose the equipment categories you are interested in learning about.
* **Exhibitor Information**: If you are an exhibitor, enter your booth size preference and any special requirements.
* **Exhibitor Preferences**: Select any sessions or topics you are interested in (e.g., New technology demonstrations, Best practices workshops).
* **Networking Interest**: Indicate your level of interest in networking opportunities.
* **Meal Preferences**: Specify your dietary requirements or preferences.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Describe any accessibility needs you may have.
* **How Heard**: Select how you heard about the event (e.g., Email newsletter, Social media).
* **First Time Attending**: Indicate if this is your first time attending the event.
* **Expectations**: State your goals or expectations for the event.
* **Registration Fee**: Select your registration fee category.
* **Payment Method**: Choose how you will pay for your registration.
* **Additional Attendees**: If registering additional attendees, specify the number.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Provide any additional comments or questions.
* **Accept Terms and Conditions**: Accept the event terms and conditions.
* **Registrant Signature**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Date**: Enter today's date.

Note: Please fill out all required fields.
