# Technology Demo Showcase RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to allow users to RSVP to a Technology Demo event, providing event information and attendee details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Scroll down to the first page labeled "Event Information".
* Select the event date and time that fits your schedule.
* On the second page, "Technology Demo", select the demo(s) you would like to attend.
* On the third page, "Attendee Information", provide your name, email address, and phone number.
* On the final page, "Notes", enter any additional comments or requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address. Please ensure it is correct.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date you plan to attend.
* **Time** (time, optional): Select the time slot you prefer.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional comments or requests.
* **Technology Demo** (select multiple, optional): Select the demo(s) you would like to attend.
* **Event Information** (text, optional): Enter any additional event information.
