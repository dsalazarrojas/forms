# Senior Care Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Senior Care Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to volunteer their time to support senior care services. This form will help us understand your interests, availability, and qualifications to ensure a suitable fit for our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form completely and accurately
- Make sure to review and agree to the terms and conditions
- Submit the form once complete

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted for updates and communications.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number where you can be reached for further information.
* **Zip** (`5`, `number`, required): Enter your postal zip code to determine your location and suitability for our programs.
* **Senior Care Shift** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the shifts you are available to volunteer for, including Senior Care 1, 2, or 3.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are available to start volunteering.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`8`, `date`, required): Enter the date you plan to end your volunteer services.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`9`, `number`, required): Enter the number of hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`10`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you plan to volunteer, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly.
* **Volunteer Description** (`11`, `text`, required): Describe your role or duties as a volunteer, including your responsibilities and expectations.
* **Volunteer Why** (`12`, `select_one`, required): Select the reason you are volunteering, such as To help seniors or To gain experience.
* **Volunteer Skills** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter any relevant skills or experience you have that would benefit the senior care program.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`14`, `text`, required): Describe any relevant volunteer experience you have in a senior care setting.
* **Volunteer References** (`15`, `text`, required): Provide the names and contact information of individuals who can vouch for your character and skills.
* **Volunteer Agree** (`16`, `note`, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of our program.
* **Volunteer Agree 2** (`17`, `note`, required): Read and agree to our volunteer policies and guidelines.
* **Volunteer Agree 3** (`18`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your willingness to abide by our rules and regulations, including "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Agree 4** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the areas where you are willing to agree, such as "True" or "False".
* **Volunteer Agree 5** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our volunteer terms and conditions, including "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Agree 6** (`21`, `select_multiple`, required): Agree to our policies and procedures, including "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Agree 7** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your willingness to follow our guidelines and regulations, including "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Agree 8** (`23`, `select_multiple`, required): Agree to our volunteer codes of conduct, including "Yes" or "No".
* **Volunteer Agree 9** (`24`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement to our volunteer policies and procedures, including "Yes" or "No".
