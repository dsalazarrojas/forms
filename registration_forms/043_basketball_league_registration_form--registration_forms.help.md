# Basketball League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register a team in a basketball league. It gathers information about the team's participation in the league, including team details, manager and coach information, and agreement to the league rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the "Team Information" section with the team's name, city, and year it was established.
2. Complete the "Team Manager Information" section with the manager's name, email, and phone number.
3. Fill out the "Coach Information" section with the head coach's name and years of coaching experience. You can also include assistant coaches' names if needed.
4. Select the league division and team skill level in the next two sections.
5. In the "Roster Information" section, estimate the size of your team's roster.
6. Answer the questions in the "League Participation" section to determine the team's previous participation in the league and what season they are registering for.
7. Review the "Agreement and Confirmation" sections, where you will be asked to agree to the league's rules and regulations, understand the fees, and confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team name** (Team name, text, required): Enter the name of your team.
* **City** (City, text, required): Enter the city your team is from.
* **Year team established** (Year team established, number, optional): Enter the year your team was established.
* **Team manager/owner name** (Team manager/owner name, text, required): Enter the name of your team's manager or owner.
* **Manager email** (Manager email, email, required): Enter your team's manager email address.
* **Manager phone** (Manager phone, text, required): Enter your team's manager phone number.
* **Head coach name** (Head coach name, text, required): Enter the name of your team's head coach.
* **Years of coaching experience** (Years of coaching experience, number, optional): Enter the number of years your head coach has been coaching.
* **Assistant coach names** (Assistant coach names, text, optional): Enter any assistant coaches' names (if any).
* **League division** (League division, select one, required): Select the league division your team belongs in (youth, teen, adult, senior, or mixed).
* **Team skill level** (Team skill level, select one, required): Select your team's skill level (recreational, competitive, or elite).
* **Estimated roster size** (Estimated roster size, number, required): Estimate the number of players on your team's roster.
* **Season registering for** (Season registering for, text, required): Enter the season you are registering for.
* **Previously participated in this league?** (Previously participated in this league?, select one, required): Select whether you have participated in this league before or not.
* **I agree to league rules and regulations** (I agree to league rules and regulations, select one, required): Agree to the league rules and regulations.
* **I understand the league fees and will pay on time** (I understand the league fees and will pay on time, select one, required): Agree to pay the fees on time.
* **I confirm all information is accurate** (I confirm all information is accurate, select one, required): Confirm that all information you provided is accurate.
