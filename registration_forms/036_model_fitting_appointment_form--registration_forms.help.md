# Model Fitting Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help clients schedule appointments for hair model fittings. It collects essential information to book an appointment, allowing our stylists to prepare and deliver the best experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select a schedule date from the calendar provided for the date you would like to have your appointment.
2. Choose a schedule time that suits your preferences.
3. Enter your name, so we can address you properly.
4. Provide your contact email and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.
5. Select your preferred date and time for the appointment (if different from the scheduled date and time).
6. Choose the type of appointment you would like to book (Haircut, Color, or Style).
7. Add any additional details about your appointment, so our stylist can prepare accordingly.
8. Click the "Submit" button to send your appointment request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Schedule Date** (schedule_date, date, required: false): Please select a date from the calendar for your scheduled appointment.
* **Schedule Time** (schedule_time, time, required: false): Choose a time slot for your scheduled appointment.
* **Client Name** (client_name, text, required: false): Enter your name, so we can address you properly.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required: false): Provide your contact email, so we can get in touch with you.
* **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required: false): Enter your contact phone number, in case we need to contact you regarding your appointment.
* **Preferred Date** (preferred_date, date, required: false): If you have a preferred date for your appointment, select it here.
* **Preferred Time** (preferred_time, time, required: false): If you have a preferred time for your appointment, select it here.
* **Appointment Type** (appointment_type, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of appointment you would like to book (Haircut, Color, or Style).
* **Appointment Details** (appointment_details, note, required: false): Add any additional details about your appointment, so our stylist can prepare accordingly.
* **Submit** (submit, text, required: false): Click this button to send your appointment request.
