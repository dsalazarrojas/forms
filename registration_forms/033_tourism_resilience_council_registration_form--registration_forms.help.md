# Tourism Resilience Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from interested individuals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your registration information in the "Registration Information" field.
2. Enter your full name in the "full_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please note that we will use this email address to verify your registration and prevent duplication of efforts.
4. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
5. You can provide any additional information you want to share in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information**: A field to collect basic registration information.
* **Full Name**: A field to enter your name as it appears on your identity documents.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address. This will be used for verification purposes and to prevent duplication of efforts.
* **Contact Number**: A field to enter your contact number.
* **Additional Information**: A field to provide any additional information you want to share about yourself.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate information in the form, especially your email address, to avoid any issues during the verification process. You can also provide any additional details in the "Additional Information" field to help us better understand your registration purpose.
