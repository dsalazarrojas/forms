# Interview Skills Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to register for an interview skills workshop. The form is intended for individuals interested in improving their interview skills and willing to attend the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go to the first page, labeled "User Input". Enter your name in the provided text field. This field is not required.
2. On the "Contact Information" page, enter your contact details such as name and email. This field is not required.
3. Move on to the "Workshop Selection" page. Select one or more options from the dropdown menu that suits your workshop preference. Note that this field is not required.
4. On the "Registration Information" page, enter any registration information you have. This field is not required.
5. Finally, on the "Additional Information" page, enter any extra details you would like to share. This field is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (id: 1)**: Enter your name in this text field. 
* **Contact Information (id: 2)**: Enter your contact details such as name and email.
* **Workshop Selection (id: 3)**: Select one or more options from the dropdown menu that suits your workshop preference. You can select one or multiple options.
* **Registration Information (id: 4)**: Enter any registration information you have.
* **Additional Information (id: 5)**: Enter any extra details you would like to share.
 
## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all the fields accurately and completely.
- Review your submission before submitting.
- Contact us at form@gic.mx if you have any questions or need further assistance.
