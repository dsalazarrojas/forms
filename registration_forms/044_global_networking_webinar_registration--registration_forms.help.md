# Global Networking Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Global Networking Webinar Registration" form is designed to register participants for a webinar. This form collects essential information from participants, including their name, email, company, session name, registration status, time and date of registration, country, job title, job function, session ID, and time zone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your participant email address in the "Participant Email" field.
3. Enter your participant company in the "Participant Company" field.
4. Select the name of the session you are interested in from the "Session Name" field.
5. Choose your registration status from the "Registration Status" field (Active or Inactive).
6. Enter the time and date you registered for the webinar in the "Time of Registration" and "Date of Registration" fields, respectively.
7. Choose your country from the "Participant Country" field.
8. Enter your participant job title in the "Participant Job Title" field.
9. Enter your participant job function in the "Participant Job Function" field.
10. Enter your session ID in the "Session ID" field.
11. Choose your time zone from the "Time Zone" field (EST, CST, MST, PST, or UTC).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the webinar materials.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address that we will use to communicate with you about the webinar.
* **Participant Company**: Enter your company name or organization.
* **Session Name**: Select the name of the session you are interested in.
* **Registration Status**: Choose whether you are actively registered or inactive (this field is not necessary if you have already registered).
* **Time of Registration**: Enter the time you registered for the webinar.
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date you registered for the webinar. (Note: This field is not necessary if you have already filled out "Time of Registration".)
* **Participant Country**: Select your country of residence.
* **Participant Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Participant Job Function**: Enter your job function or department.
* **Session ID**: Enter your session ID (usually provided by the session organizers).
* **Time Zone**: Select your time zone (EST, CST, MST, PST, or UTC).
