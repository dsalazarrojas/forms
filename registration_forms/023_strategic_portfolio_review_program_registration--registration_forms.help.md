# Strategic Portfolio Review Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for the Strategic Portfolio Review Program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the **Program title** field with a brief title of your program.
2. Enter your **Company** name in the Company field.
3. Describe the **Program objective** in a few words.
4. Select the **Program type** from the dropdown menu (Portfolio Review or Portfolio Presentation).
5. Enter the **Enrolment date** and **Enrolment time** of your program.
6. Check the box to **Consent** to participate in the program (I consent to participate in the program or I do not consent to participate in the program).
7. If required, fill in the **Participant details** field with any additional information.
8. Enter the **Program location**, if applicable.
9. Provide the **Program duration**, if required.
10. Enter the **Program start date** and **Program end date**, if applicable.
11. Fill in your **Phone**, **Email**, and **Program URL** fields with your contact information.
12. Enter the names of your **Organizers**, if applicable.
13. List the names of any **Consulting firms** involved in the program.
14. Enter the name of the **Program organizer**.
15. List the names of any **Strategy teams** involved.
16. Enter any **Program sponsors** names.
17. If necessary, add any **Program notes** in the **Program notes** field.
18. Select the **Program participants** (Participant 1, Participant 2, Participant 3, etc.).
19. Choose the **Program status** (In progress, Finished, or Canceled).
20. Add any additional **Program notes**, if necessary.
21. Enter the **Program URL**, if required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program title** (text): Enter a brief title of your program.
* **Company** (text): Enter your company name.
* **Program objective** (text): Describe the main goal of your program.
* **Program type** (select one): Choose between Portfolio Review or Portfolio Presentation.
* **Enrolment date** (date): Enter the date when you enrolled in the program.
* **Enrolment time** (time): Select the time of enrollment.
* **Consent** (select one): Check the box to participate (I consent to participate in the program or I do not consent to participate in the program).
* **Participant details** (note): Add any additional participant details, if required.
* **Program location** (text): Enter the location of your program.
* **Program duration** (number): Enter the duration of your program.
* **Program start date** (date): Enter the start date of your program.
* **Program end date** (date): Enter the end date of your program.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Program URL** (text): Enter the URL of your program.
* **Organizers** (text): Enter the names of the program organizers.
* **Consulting firms** (text): List the names of consulting firms involved in the program.
* **Program organizer** (text): Enter the name of the program organizer.
* **Strategy teams** (text): List the names of strategy teams involved.
* **Program sponsors** (text): Enter the names of program sponsors.
* **Program notes** (note): Add any additional program notes, if necessary.
* **Program participants** (select multiple): Select the participants of your program (Participant 1, Participant 2, Participant 3, etc.).
* **Program status** (select one): Choose the status of your program (In progress, Finished, or Canceled).
* **Program notes 2** (note): Add any additional program notes, if necessary.
* **Program URL 2** (text): Enter the URL of your program, if required.

Tips: Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
