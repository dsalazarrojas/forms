## Purpose
This form is used for corporate training intake, allowing users to input information about the training they are interested in, their company details, and their personal information. The purpose of this form is to gather data for training management and planning.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review the form questions carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information ready.
2.  Fill out the form by selecting the appropriate options for each question.
3.  Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for all required fields.
4.  Review your submission for any errors or inconsistencies before submitting.
