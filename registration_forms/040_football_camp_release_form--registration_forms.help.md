The `Football Camp Release Form` is a document designed for parents to provide informed consent and acknowledge risks associated with their child's participation in a football camp. This form is intended for parents to review, sign, and submit, ensuring they are aware of the camp's rules, risks, and responsibilities. To ensure accurate completion, please review each field carefully and answer accordingly.

# Football Camp Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Football Camp Release Form is a mandatory document that requires parents to provide informed consent and acknowledge risks before enrolling their child in the football camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand the terms and conditions of the camp.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and honest information.
3. Sign the form to indicate your consent and acknowledgement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Info
* **Parent Info** (`parent_info`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself as a parent.

### Parent Consent
* **Parent Consent** (`consent`, `text`, required/optional): This section is for you to express your informed consent to your child's participation in the camp. Please indicate that you have reviewed the camp's rules, risks, and responsibilities and are aware of the terms and conditions.

### Release and Waiver
* **Release and Waiver** (`release`, `text`, required/optional): Please sign this section to indicate your release of liability for any damages or injuries your child may sustain during the camp.

### Risk Acknowledgement
* **Risk Acknowledgement** (`risk_acknowledgement`, `text`, required/optional): Acknowledge that you are aware of the risks associated with your child's participation in the football camp, including but not limited to injuries, physical harm, and property damage.
