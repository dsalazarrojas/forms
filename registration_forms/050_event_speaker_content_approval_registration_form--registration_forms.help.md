# Event Speaker Content Approval Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect speaker information and content details for event approval and registration. Fill out this form to provide essential details about yourself and your session materials.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each page to navigate through the form.
2. Fill out each field according to the provided explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details** (`event_speaker_content_approval_registration_form_page_1`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your event or the occasion where you will be speaking. This will help us understand the context of your session materials.
* **Speaker Session Materials** (`speaker_session_materials`, text, optional): Share your session materials or presentation details, including any relevant images, documents, or other attachments.
* **Speaker Agreement Form** (`speaker_agreement_form`, text, optional): Check the boxes to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions of your session.
* **Speaker Contact** (`speaker_contact`, text, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, address, email, and phone number, so we can get in touch with you.

## Tips
- Please provide accurate and clear information to ensure a smooth approval process.
- If you have any attachments, upload them to support your session materials.
- If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask us for clarification.
- This form is designed to be user-friendly, but if you encounter any issues, please contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
