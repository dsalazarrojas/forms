# Martial Arts Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a legal document that requires all participants to agree on the terms and conditions of their martial arts training.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your participant name, parent or guardian's name, and email address.
- If applicable, provide your date of birth, emergency contact information, and any medical conditions you may have.
- Read and agree to the waiver terms, which include being aware of possible injuries and participating at your own risk.
- Finally, sign the participant and parent/guardian signatures fields to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name**: Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents (ID, passport, etc.).
* **Parent or guardian name**: Enter the name of a parent or guardian who will be responsible for you in case of an emergency.
* **Email**: Enter your personal email address where you can be contacted.
* **Date of birth**: Provide the date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Parent signature**: A parent or guardian must sign to confirm their agreement to let you participate in martial arts training.
* **Participant signature**: You must sign to confirm that you understand and agree to the waiver terms.
* **Emergency contact name**: Enter the name of the person you want to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Relationship**: Select the relationship of the emergency contact person to you (e.g., mother, father, brother, etc.).
* **Emergency contact address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Participant agreement**: Read and agree to the waiver terms, which include being aware of possible injuries and participating at your own risk.
* **Waiver**: Select all applicable waiver options to confirm your understanding of the terms.
* **Medical conditions**: If you have any medical conditions (e.g., high blood pressure, diabetes), enter them here.
* **Medical conditions 2**: Select any medical conditions that may affect your participation in martial arts training.
* **Medical information**: Provide any additional medical information that may be relevant.
* **Doctor signature**: If applicable, provide your doctor's signature and contact information.
* **Medical certificate**: Attach a medical certificate (e.g., doctor's note) confirming your medical conditions.
* **Insurance**: If applicable, provide your insurance details.
* **Medical certificate 2**: Another medical certificate may be required.
* **Insurance 2**: Another insurance provider may be required.
* **Participant information**: Enter any other relevant information about yourself (e.g., previous injuries).
* **Participant name 2**: Enter another name for the participant (if applicable).
* **Participant name 3**: Enter another participant name (if applicable).
