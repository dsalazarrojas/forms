# Passport Size Photo Submission - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to upload passport-sized photos as part of a registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form's instructions to understand what is required.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary photos to upload.
3. Upload each photo according to the instructions on each page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: This is the introduction page, which likely displays the form's title and instructions.
* **Photo**: This is the first photo upload page. Upload your passport-sized photo here.
* **Upload Photo 2**: This is the second photo upload page. Upload your second passport-sized photo here.
* **Upload Photo 3**: This is the third photo upload page. Upload your third passport-sized photo here.
## Tips
* Please ensure all photos are in the correct passport size format (typically 3.5 x 4.5 cm or 3.5 x 4.5 inches) before uploading.
* You may need to adjust your photo size or resolution before uploading.
