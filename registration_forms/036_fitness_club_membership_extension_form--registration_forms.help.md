# Fitness Club Membership Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Club Membership Extension Form is designed to gather information for extending or renewing existing membership. Please complete this form to provide the necessary details for your membership extension.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the membership you want to extend or renew from the dropdown menu in the "membership_id" field.
2. Provide your name in the "member_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "member_email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "member_phone" field.
5. If you want to extend or renew your membership, select "Yes" in the "membership_extension_type" field. If you're unsure, select "No" to discuss with the admin.
6. If you have any additional comments or reasons for your membership extension, you can provide them in the "membership_extension_comments" field.
7. Finally, select the duration of your membership extension from the "membership_extension_length" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **membership_extension_date**: Enter your desired membership extension date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **membership_extension_reason**: Provide a brief reason for your membership extension. If you're unsure, you can leave this field blank.
* **membership_extension_type**: Select "Yes" if you want to extend or renew your membership, "No" otherwise.
* **membership_extension_length**: Select the duration of your membership extension in months or years.
* **membership_extension_comments**: Add any additional comments or reasons for your membership extension.
* **membership_id**: Select your existing membership ID from the dropdown menu.
* **member_name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **member_email**: Enter your valid email address for club communication.
* **member_phone**: Enter your phone number for club contact.

## Tips
* Ensure that your membership ID and name match your existing club records to avoid any issues with your membership extension.
* If you're unsure about any field or the form process, please contact the club administrator for guidance.
* Please double-check your form entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
