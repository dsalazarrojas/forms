# voice_placement_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to submit information and data related to voice placement.

## How To Complete This Form

*   To complete this form, start by filling out the required fields. If any field is marked as "required", it must be filled out.
*   For the "voice type" field, select the relevant option from the dropdown menu.
*   For the "years of experience" field, enter the number of years you have experience in the voice placement industry.
*   For the "previous studios" field, list the studios or production companies you have worked with.
*   For the "demo reels" field, upload or paste a link to your demo reel.
*   For the "voice samples" field, upload or paste a link to your voice samples.
*   For the "resume" field, upload or paste a link to your resume.
*   Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your information.
*   If you need to cancel the form, click the "Cancel" button.
