# Cultural Performance Highlight Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Cultural Performance Highlight Consent Form is designed to collect consent from participants in cultural performances to use their performance in our materials.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name to identify yourself.
2. Enter your Email if you want to be contacted for further information.
3. If applicable, fill in your Phone number for easy contact.
4. Choose your Performance Type from the options provided.
5. Enter the date of your performance.
6. Select the time of your performance.
7. Provide the location of your performance.
8. Describe your performance details, such as the event or recital.
9. Check your consent to use your performance in our materials.
10. If you have any additional comments or information, please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (3, email, optional): If you want to be contacted for further information, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, optional): If you want to be contacted by phone, please provide your phone number.
* **Performance Type** (5, select_one, optional): Please select the type of performance you are participating in (e.g. event, recital, etc.).
* **Performance Date** (6, date, optional): Please enter the date of your performance.
* **Performance Time** (7, time, optional): Please select the time of your performance.
* **Performance Location** (8, text, optional): Please provide the location of your performance.
* **Performance Details** (9, text, optional): Please describe the performance you are participating in, including any relevant details.
* **Consent** (10, select_multiple, optional): Please check the boxes to indicate your consent to use your performance in our materials.
* **Additional Comments** (11, text, optional): If you have any additional information or comments, please provide them.
* **Agreement** (12, note, optional): Please read and agree to the terms and conditions of our consent.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
* You may be contacted by us for further information or to provide additional details.
