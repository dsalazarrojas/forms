# Household Hazardous Waste Collection Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Household Hazardous Waste Collection Registration" form is used to register a household's hazardous waste collection. This form is used by waste management authorities to track and manage the collection process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the correct category for your household hazardous waste collection.
2. Enter your first name, last name, and address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your phone number and email (if applicable).
4. Indicate whether you have collected household hazardous waste.
5. Select a collection date for your household hazardous waste.
6. (Optional) Enter a collection time if applicable.
7. Provide any special instructions or notes about your collection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Select the type of waste collection you are registering. Choose between "Household Hazardous Waste Collection" or "Non-Household Hazardous Waste Collection."
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your household's physical address where the collection took place.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached for further inquiries.
* **Email**: Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Household Hazardous Waste**: Indicate if you have collected household hazardous waste. Select 'Yes' if you have, 'No' otherwise.
* **Collection Date**: Select the date you collected the household hazardous waste.
* **Collection Time**: (Optional) Enter the time you collected the household hazardous waste.
* **Special Instructions**: Enter any special notes or instructions about your collection that you think the waste management authorities should know.
