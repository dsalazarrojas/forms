# Babysitting Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect important information about the child's presence, the babysitter's arrival, and the parents' plans for the evening. It's essential to complete this form accurately and thoroughly to ensure the child's safety and the sitter's responsibility.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the purpose and the required fields.
2. Complete the form accurately and honestly, providing all necessary information.
3. Make sure to confirm your answers when required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **-- Babysitting Daily Check-In --** (Arrival Header)
* **--** (Note): This is a header that informs you about the purpose of the form.

### 2. **Today's Date** (check_in_date)
* **Required**
* This field is used to record the date of the current session.
* Please select the correct date.

### 3. **Child(ren) being supervised** (child_name_entry)
* **Required**
* Please enter the name(s) of the child(ren) in your care.

### 4. **Sitter Arrival Time** (arrival_time_actual)
* **Required**
* Enter the time the babysitter arrived.

### 5. **Where will the parents be?** (parent_destination)
* **Optional**
* If the parents are going out for the evening, please specify their destination.

### 6. **Expected Parent Return Time** (expected_return_time)
* **Required**
* Enter the time the parents expect to return.

### 7. **Best Phone Number to reach parents tonight** (best_contact_tonight)
* **Required**
* Enter a phone number where the parents can be reached in case of an emergency.

### 8. **Emergency Backup Contact (If parents can't be reached)** (emergency_backup_person)
* **Required**
* Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person in case the parents cannot be reached.

### 9. **Child's Current Mood or Health Notes** (child_mood_health)
* **Optional**
* If there are any health or mood concerns, please provide notes.

### 10. **Meal and Snack Plan for tonight** (meal_plan_tonight)
* **Required**
* Enter a meal and snack plan for the child.

### 11. **Any medications to be given tonight?** (medications_due_tonight)
* **Optional**
* If medications are to be given, please specify dosage and time.

### 12. **Specific instructions for this session** (special_instructions_tonight)
* **Optional**
* If there are any specific instructions for the sitter, please provide them.

### 13. **Parent Check-in Acknowledgment** (check_in_confirmed_parent)
* **Required**
* Confirm that you have received and reviewed this form.

### 14. **Babysitter Check-in Acknowledgment** (check_in_confirmed_sitter)
* **Required**
* Confirm that you have received and reviewed this form.

## Tips
* Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting.
* Answer all required fields accurately and honestly.
* Confirm your answers when required.
* Keep in mind that this form is an essential tool for the sitter to provide the best care for the child.
