# seo-intake-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO Intake Form is designed to gather information about projects and their related details for SEO purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately and efficiently, follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields carefully to ensure you provide all required information.
2. Fill in each field with clear and concise details.
3. Save your progress regularly to avoid duplication of effort.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Project Details (1)** (`project_details`, text, not required): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Company Information (2)** (`company_info`, text, not required): Provide company details, if applicable.
* **SEO Questions (3)** (`seo_questions`, text, not required): Answer any relevant SEO-related questions.
* **Contact Information (4)** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your contact information.
* **SEO Answers (5)** (`seo_answers`, text, not required): Provide answers to any SEO-related questions.
* **Project Goals (6)** (`project_goals`, text, not required): Describe your project goals.
* **Project Start Date (7)** (`project_start_date`, date, not required): Enter your project start date.
* **Project End Date (8)** (`project_end_date`, date, not required): Enter your project end date.
* **Project Budget (9)** (`project_budget`, number, not required): Enter your project budget.
* **Project Status (10)** (`project_status`, select_one, not required): Select your project status (In Progress, Completed, or Not Started).
* **Company Name (11)** (`company_name`, text, not required): Enter your company name.
* **Company URL (12)** (`company_url`, text, not required): Enter your company URL.
* **Phone (13)** (`phone`, text, not required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email (14)** (`email`, email, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Project Owner (15)** (`project_owner`, text, not required): Specify the project owner.
* **SEO Specialist (16)** (`seo_specialist`, select_one, not required): Select the SEO specialist (John Doe, Jane Doe, or Jim Doe).
* **SEO Template (17)** (`seotemplate`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO templates.
* **SEO Technology (18)** (`seo_technology`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO technologies.
* **SEO Language (19)** (`seolanguage`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO languages.
* **SEO Keywords (20)** (`seokeywords`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO keywords.
* **SEO Targets (21)** (`seotargets`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO targets.
* **SEO Submits (22)** (`seosubmits`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO submits.
* **SEO Times (23)** (`seotimes`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple SEO times.
* **SEO Money (24)** (`seomoney`, number, not required): Enter your SEO budget.
* **SEO Budget (25)** (`seobudget`, number, not required): Enter your SEO budget.
