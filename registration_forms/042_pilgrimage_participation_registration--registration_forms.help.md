# Pilgrimage Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pilgrimage Participation Registration form collects user information for event participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
5. Select the Date and Time you plan to participate in the event.
6. Select one option from the "Select One" field.
7. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
8. Enter any additional comments or feedback in the "Note" fields (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number (if applicable).
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date you plan to participate in the event.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time you plan to participate in the event.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, `select_one`, required): Select one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or feedback.
