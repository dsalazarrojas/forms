# Launch Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new event. It gathers all the necessary information to make the event visible in the system and allow other users to view and register for it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the `user-info` page with your personal details.
2. Move on to the `event-info` page and fill in the event details such as name, date, location, and other necessary information.
3. In the `contact-info` page, fill in your contact information to ensure that we can reach out to you for any further instructions or clarifications.
4. Make sure to double-check your phone number and email address for accuracy.
5. If you have any additional notes or comments about the event, you can add them in the `notes` page.
6. Once you've filled in all the required fields, click submit to register your event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user-info** (required): This is the first page where you'll be asked to provide your personal details. Fill in your first and last name.
* **event-info** (required): This page is where you'll be asked to provide event details such as event name, date, location, and other necessary information. Make sure to fill in all the fields accurately.
* **contact-info** (required): This page is where you'll be asked to provide your contact information. Fill in your phone number and email address accurately.
* **event-location** (required): This field is used to specify the location of the event. Enter the name of the location where the event will take place.
* **event-date** (required): This field is used to specify the date of the event. Enter the date when the event will take place in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **event-name** (required): This field is used to enter the name of the event.
* **phone** (required): This field is used to enter your phone number.
* **email** (required): This field is used to enter your email address.
* **notes** (optional): This field is used to add any additional notes or comments about the event.
