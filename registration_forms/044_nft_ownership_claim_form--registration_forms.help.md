# Nft Ownership Claim Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to claim ownership of a non-fungible token (NFT) in case of a dispute or issue with the token's origin or legitimacy. Please use this form if you suspect that your NFT was created or transferred incorrectly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details as the token owner.
2. Enter the correct NFT ID, which can be found in your wallet or from the NFT's origin.
3. Enter the transaction hash, which is a unique code associated with the NFT's creation or transfer.
4. Select the date and time of the token's creation or transfer.
5. Enter your address, phone number, and email address for verification and communication purposes.
6. Submit the form to submit your claim.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner** (`owner`, text, optional): Enter your name and surname as the owner of the NFT.
* **Nft ID** (`nft_id`, text, optional): Enter the correct NFT ID, which can be found in your wallet or from the NFT's origin.
* **Transaction Hash** (`transaction_hash`, text, optional): Enter the transaction hash associated with the NFT's creation or transfer.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select the date of the token's creation or transfer.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select the time of the token's creation or transfer.
* **Owner Address** (`owner_address`, text, optional): Enter your physical address for verification and communication purposes.
* **Owner Phone** (`owner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for quick contact and communication.
* **Owner Email** (`owner_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for verification and communication purposes.

## Tips
* Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Make sure to provide all required information to ensure a smooth processing of your claim.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
