# School Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a school field trip, including student and teacher contact details, trip details, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's name, date of birth, email, phone number, and emergency contact information in the **Registration Details** section.
2. Enter the teacher's name, email, and phone number in the **Teacher's Information** section.
3. Provide trip details, such as date, time, location, duration, type, and status, in the **Trip Details** section.
4. Select the trip type (Educational, Recreational, or Other) and trip status (Confirmed, In Progress, Cancelled, or Pending) from the dropdown menus.
5. Enter the trip budget and notes in the **Trip Budget and Notes** section.
6. Check with both parents to confirm their consent.
7. Enter the teacher's comments, if any.
8. Select the student's and teacher's attendance status (Confirmed, Not Attending, or Absent).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Details
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their record.
* **Student Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the emergency contact person's name, phone number, and email.

### Teacher's Information
* **Teacher's Name**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher's Email**: Enter the teacher's email address.
* **Teacher's Phone**: Enter the teacher's phone number.

### Trip Details
* **Trip Date**: Enter the date of the trip in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Trip Time**: Enter the time of the trip in the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Trip Location**: Enter the location of the trip.
* **Trip Duration**: Enter the duration of the trip.
* **Trip Type**: Select the type of trip (Educational, Recreational, or Other).
* **Trip Status**: Select the status of the trip (Confirmed, In Progress, Cancelled, or Pending).

### Trip Budget and Notes
* **Trip Budget**: Enter the budget for the trip.
* **Trip Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the trip.

### Attendance
* **Student Attendance**: Select the attendance status of the student (Confirmed, Not Attending, or Absent).
* **Teacher's Attendance**: Select the attendance status of the teacher (Confirmed, Not Attending, or Absent).

### Consent and Comments
* **Parent 1 Consent**: Enter the consent of the first parent.
* **Parent 2 Consent**: Enter the consent of the second parent.
* **Teacher's Comments**: Enter any comments from the teacher.
