# Donor Network Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from new donors, administrators, and volunteers in the Donor Network.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** to ensure accurate identification and communication.
2. Choose your **Role** within the network (Administrator, Volunteer, or Donor) to specify your position and responsibilities.
3. Select your preferred **Contact Method** (Phone, Email, or Mail) and enter your **Contact Number** for easy communication.
4. Enter your **Organization** name to associate yourself with your affiliated group or institution.
5. Fill in your **Address**, **City**, and **State** for geographic information.
6. Provide your **Zip** code for postal address accuracy.
7. (Optional) Write a brief **Bio** about yourself to introduce your professional background or interests.
8. (Optional) Add a **Photo URL** or **Bio Video URL** and **Bio Audio URL** for more in-depth information about yourself (Note: These fields are not required for submission but may be reviewed during review process).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication and account creation.
* **Organization**: Enter your affiliated institution or group name.
* **Role**: Choose your position within the network (Administrator, Volunteer, or Donor).
* **Contact Method**: Select your preferred method of contact (Phone, Email, or Mail).
* **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Address**: Enter your address for geographic location information.
* **City**: Enter your city for geographic location information.
* **State**: Enter your state for geographic location information.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code for postal address accuracy.
* **Bio**: (Optional) Write a brief description about yourself for networking purposes.
* **Photo URL**: (Optional) Add a URL of a professional photo for visual introduction.
* **Bio Video URL**: (Optional) Add a URL of a video introducing yourself.
* **Bio Audio URL**: (Optional) Add a URL of an audio introduction about yourself.

## Tips

* Ensure all fields marked as required are filled in accurately to avoid delays in processing.
* Optional fields can be left blank, but be aware that they may be reviewed during the review process.
