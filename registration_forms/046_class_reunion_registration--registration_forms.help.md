# Class Reunion Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees who want to participate in the class reunion. It collects details about the individual, their contact information, personal preferences, event choices, and payment options.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Graduation Year**.
2. Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number**.
3. Enter your **Current City**, **Current State or Province**, and **Current Country**.
4. Select your **Marital Status** and **Number of Children**.
5. Choose the events you plan to attend from the **Events You Plan to Attend - Select All** dropdown list.
6. Specify any **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** and **Special Accommodations Needed**.
7. Select your preferred **Payment Method**.
8. Provide any **Additional Comments or Information**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (<required>): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (<required>): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Maiden Name** (<optional>): If you have a maiden name, enter it here.
* **Graduation Year** (<required>): Enter the year you graduated.
* **Email Address** (<required>): Enter your current email address.
* **Phone Number** (<required>): Enter your current phone number.
* **Current City** (<optional>): Enter the city where you currently reside.
* **Current State or Province** (<optional>): Enter the state or province where you currently reside.
* **Current Country** (<optional>): Enter the country where you currently reside.
* **Marital Status** (<optional>): Select your current marital status.
* **Number of Children** (<optional>): Enter the number of children you have.
* **Events You Plan to Attend - Select All** (<optional>): Choose the events you plan to attend from the list.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (<optional>): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (<optional>): Enter any special accommodations you require.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (<optional>): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Additional Comments or Information** (<optional>): Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
