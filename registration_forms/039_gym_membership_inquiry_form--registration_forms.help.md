# gym_membership_inquiry_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from potential members interested in joining a gym or fitness club. It is meant to be used to understand their background, contact preferences, and membership details in a concise and straightforward manner.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Potential Member Info**, including any relevant details about yourself.
2. Enter your **Contact Info**, including your preferred contact method (phone, email, or in person).
3. Select your desired **Membership Type** (Basic, Premium, or Elite).
4. Choose your **Contact Method** (phone, email, or in person).
5. Select your **Preferred Contact Time** (morning, afternoon, or evening).
6. If applicable, enter your **Membership Start Date** and **Membership End Date**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Potential Member Info**: 
	+ A text field for you to provide information about yourself.
* **Contact Info**:
	+ A text field for you to enter your contact information.
* **Membership Details**:
	+ A text field for you to provide any additional information about your membership.
* **Membership Type**:
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- Basic
		- Premium
		- Elite
* **Contact Method**:
	+ Select one or more of the following options:
		- Phone
		- Email
		- In Person
* **Preferred Contact Time**:
	+ Select one of the following options:
		- Morning
		- Afternoon
		- Evening
* **Membership Start Date**:
	+ Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Membership End Date**:
	+ Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

## Tips

- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your inquiry is processed accurately.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Please note that membership details and start/end dates are optional and only applicable if you are already a member.
