# Sauna Use Waiver - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sauna Use Waiver form is used to collect information from individuals who use the sauna facilities at our establishment. This form helps us to gather essential data about you, including your contact information, medical conditions, and emergency contact details. By filling out this form, you will be able to enjoy our sauna facilities while ensuring your safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Date of Birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
3. Provide your contact information, including Address, Phone, and Email.
4. Disclose any known Medical Conditions that may affect your sauna use.
5. Identify an Emergency Contact and their relationship to you.
6. Provide the Emergency Contact's contact information, including their Phone and any alternate contact information.
7. Confirm the relationship between you and your Emergency Contact.
8. Review and verify the accuracy of all provided information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address** (text, required): Enter your current address, including street, city, state, and postal code.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your active email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Medical Condition** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your sauna use, please provide details.
* **Emergency Contact** (text, required): Identify the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one, required): Specify your relationship to the Emergency Contact (e.g., Emergency Contact Person, Parent, Guardian, or Other).
* **Emergency Contact Number** (text, required): Enter the Emergency Contact's phone number.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact** (text, optional): If you have an alternate emergency contact, please provide their information.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Relationship** (select_one, optional): If applicable, specify your relationship to the Alternate Emergency Contact.
* **Alternate Emergency Contact Number** (text, optional): If you have an alternate emergency contact, enter their phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (text, required): Enter the Emergency Contact's name.
* **Parent** (text, required): Enter the name of your parent.
* **Guardian** (text, required): Enter the name of your guardian.
* **Other** (text, required): Enter the name of the "Other" emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Other** (select_one, required): Specify your relationship to the "Other" emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact Person** (select_one, required): Confirm your relationship to the Emergency Contact Person.
* **Relationship to Parent** (select_one, required): Confirm your relationship to the Parent.
* **Relationship to Guardian** (select_one, required): Confirm your relationship to the Guardian.
* **Relationship to Other** (select_one, required): Confirm your relationship to the "Other" emergency contact.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact Guardian** (select_one, required): Confirm your relationship to the Emergency Contact Guardian.
* **Relationship to Emergency Contact Other** (select_one, required): Confirm your relationship to the Emergency Contact "Other".
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Emergency Contact Person** (select_one, required): Confirm whether the Emergency Contact Person is an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Parent** (select_one, required): Confirm whether the Parent is an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Guardian** (select_one, required): Confirm whether the Guardian is an emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship Other** (select_one, required): Confirm whether the "Other" emergency contact is an emergency contact.

## Tips
Please make sure to complete all required fields accurately and thoroughly. If you are unsure about any information, please ask our staff for assistance.
