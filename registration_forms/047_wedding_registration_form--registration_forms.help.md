# wedding_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about a wedding, including details about the bride and groom, the date and time of the wedding, and the location and venue. It is intended to be a comprehensive guide for couples planning their special day.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the wedding information, including the bride and groom's details.
2. Enter the date and time of the wedding.
3. Provide information about the email and phone number for the couple.
4. Enter the location and venue details.
5. Fill in the ceremony and reception information, including any special details or requirements.
6. Add any relevant information about the officiant or ceremony.
7. Finally, provide any additional details about the ceremony and reception.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Wedding Information (Wedding Information)** (ID 1, text, required/optional): This field is a general heading for the wedding information section. Enter a brief description of the wedding, such as the type of wedding (e.g., "civil", "religious", etc.).
* **Bride** (ID 2, text, required/optional): Enter the name and any other details of the bride.
* **Groom** (ID 3, text, required/optional): Enter the name and any other details of the groom.
* **Date and Time** (ID 4, date, required/optional): Enter the date and time of the wedding. Please format the date according to your preference (e.g., dd/mm/yyyy or mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email and Phone** (ID 5, text, required/optional): Enter the email and phone number of the couple. This will help us stay in touch with you and provide any necessary information.
* **Location** (ID 6, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the wedding. This could be the address or a venue name.
* **Venue and Capacity** (ID 7, text, required/optional): Enter the venue name and capacity for the wedding.
* **Ceremony Info** (ID 8, text, required/optional): Provide any details about the ceremony, such as the location, type, or any specific requirements.
* **Reception Info** (ID 9, text, required/optional): Enter any details about the reception, such as the location, food, and beverages.
* **Officiant Info** (ID 10, text, required/optional): Enter any details about the officiant, such as their name, contact information, or any specific requirements.
* **Ceremony Details** (ID 11, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the ceremony.
* **Reception Details** (ID 12, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the reception.
