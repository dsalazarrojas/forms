# Network Volleyball Skills Registration & Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Network Volleyball Skills Registration & Payment Form. The form is used to register users for a network volleyball program and gather their payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your name, address, and any other required information.
2. Select the package you wish to register for from the Select Package drop-down list.
3. Enter your payment details in the Payment Details section.
4. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the Notes section.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field.
6. Provide your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Confirm your agreement with the program terms in the Agreement section.
8. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the Terms and Conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required): Fill out this section with your name, address, and any other required information. This section is necessary for program registration and communication.
* **Select Package** (`package_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose the package you wish to register for from the drop-down list. This section determines which level of the program you will participate in.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Enter your payment information for the selected package. This section is essential for payment processing.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration or payment. This section is for your convenience, but not required for registration.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication and program updates.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for communication and emergency contact.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm your agreement with the program terms in this section.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm that you have read and agreed to the program Terms and Conditions.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully to avoid any errors or duplication of attempts.
* Ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact the program administrators for assistance.
