# cross_border_volunteer_waiver_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cross-Border Volunteer Waiver Form is a document used to register volunteers who will be working or traveling across international borders. This form collects important information about the volunteer, including their contact details, medical history, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) next to the label.
2. Enter your name, contact information, and address in the respective fields.
3. Provide your country of birth and passport number.
4. Enter your passport date.
5. Describe your volunteer experience.
6. Sign the waiver agreements (1, 2, and 3).
7. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, relation, and contact information.
8. List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
9. List any medication you are currently taking.
10. Provide any additional medical history.
11. Specify the languages you speak.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (*): Enter your full name.
* **Contact Info** (*): Enter your contact information, including your phone number and email.
* **Address** (*): Enter your physical address.
* **Country of Birth**: Enter the country where you were born.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number.
* **Passport Date**: Enter the date your passport was issued.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Describe your volunteer experience, including any relevant training or certifications.
* **Waiver 1**: Sign the waiver agreement 1 to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Waiver 2**: Sign the waiver agreement 2 to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Waiver 3**: Sign the waiver agreement 3 to acknowledge that you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (*): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (*): Select your relation to your emergency contact (e.g. Mother, Father, Friend, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: Enter the email of your emergency contact.
* **Nationality** (*): Select your nationality.
* **Language** (*): Select the languages you speak.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2**: Enter the phone number of your backup emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2**: Enter the email of your backup emergency contact.

Note: The asterisk (\*) indicates a required field. Please make sure to fill in all required fields to complete the form correctly.
