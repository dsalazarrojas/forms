# Academic Diversity And Equity Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for individuals interested in joining the Academic Diversity And Equity Network. It gathers essential contact and demographic information to facilitate communication and collaboration within the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the registration process.
2. Fill in your full name as you prefer to be listed.
3. Enter your institutional or primary email address.
4. Select your role from the provided options (Student, Faculty, Staff, Administrator, or Other).
5. Choose any relevant areas of interest from the list of DEI topics.
6. If you require any accommodations, indicate them in the "Accessibility needs" field.
7. Choose whether to allow your name and email to be shared with the network.
8. If available, list any typical days or times you are available for events or meetings.
9. Add any additional comments about your interests or goals.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (Full name, text, required): Enter your full name as you prefer to be listed in the network.
* **Contact email** (Contact email, email, required): Enter your institutional or primary email address.
* **Institution** (Institution or organization, text, optional): This is your employer or academic institution.
* **Role** (Select your role, select_one, required): Choose from the options: Student, Faculty, Staff, Administrator, or Other.
* **Areas of interest** (Select relevant DEI topics, select_multiple, optional): Choose any relevant DEI topics that interest you.
* **Accessibility needs** (Indicate any accommodations required, text, optional): If you require any accommodations, list them here.
* **Consent to share contact** (Agree to have name and email shared, select_one, required): Choose 'True' to have your name and email shared with the network.
* **Availability** (Typical days or times for events or meetings, text, optional): List any typical days or times you are available for events or meetings.
* **Additional comments** (Anything else to add about interests or goals, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your interests or goals.
