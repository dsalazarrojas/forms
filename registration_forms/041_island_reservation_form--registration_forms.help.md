# Island Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in making a reservation on an island.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the travel dates for your island reservation.
2. Choose your preferred accommodations.
3. Enter your arrival and departure dates.
4. Select your island preferences.
5. Indicate your reservation status.
6. Enter your reservation ID (if applicable).
7. Provide your customer name.
8. Enter your customer email address.
9. Enter your customer phone number.
10. Enter any additional customer notes.
11. Select your payment method.
12. Indicate your payment status.
13. Select your cancellation policy.
14. Enter any cancellation fees.
15. Enter the cancellation policy link (if applicable).
16. Provide your customer signature.
17. Review and agree to the customer agreement.
18. Enter your customer address.
19. Enter your customer city.
20. Enter your customer state.
21. Enter your customer zip code.
22. Enter your customer country.
23. Enter your customer language.
24. Enter your customer time zone.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Travel Dates**: The travel dates for your island reservation.
* **Accommodations**: The type of accommodations you have chosen for your reservation.
* **Arrival Date**: The date you plan to arrive on the island.
* **Departure Date**: The date you plan to depart from the island.
* **Island Preferences**: Any special preferences you have for your island experience.
* **Reservation Status**: The status of your reservation (e.g., pending, confirmed, etc.).
* **Reservation ID**: A unique identifier for your reservation (if applicable).
* **Customer Name**: The name of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Email**: The email address of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Phone**: The phone number of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Notes**: Any additional information you would like to share about your reservation.
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment you will use for your reservation.
* **Payment Status**: The status of your payment (e.g., pending, completed, etc.).
* **Cancellation Policy**: The policy regarding cancellations for your reservation.
* **Cancellation Fee**: Any fee associated with cancelling your reservation.
* **Cancellation Policy Link**: A link to the cancellation policy document (if applicable).
* **Customer Signature**: A required field to confirm that the customer has reviewed and agreed to the terms.
* **Customer Agreement**: A required field to review and agree to the customer terms.
* **Customer Address**: The address of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer City**: The city of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer State**: The state of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Zip**: The zip code of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Country**: The country of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Language**: The language of the person making the reservation.
* **Customer Time Zone**: The time zone of the person making the reservation.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are essential for the correct processing of your reservation. Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
