# Talent Registration Form For Casting Agency - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect contact and other necessary information from talents (actors, performers, etc.) seeking to register their services with a casting agency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections below to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Attach any requested attachments (e.g., resume, reel, etc.).
4. Submit the form to the casting agency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your name, phone number, and email address for contact purposes.
* **Acting Experience** (`acting_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your acting experience, including any notable roles, productions, or credits.
* **Resume** (`resume`, text, required: true): Upload or attach your resume, which should include your contact information, acting experience, and any other relevant details.
* **Portfolio** (`portfolio`, text, required: false): Share any relevant images, videos, or links to your work.
* **Reel** (`reel`, text, required: false): Upload any relevant video or audio recordings of your work.
* **Headshot** (`headshot`, text, required: false): Upload a photo of yourself, preferably a high-quality image.

## Tips
* Ensure that your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
* Tailor your acting experience and resume to highlight your relevant skills and accomplishments.
* Use a professional-looking portfolio to showcase your best work.
* Keep your reel concise and engaging.
* Use a clear and recent headshot to make a good impression.
