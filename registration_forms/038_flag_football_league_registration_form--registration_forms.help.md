# Flag Football League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Flag Football League Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from players registering for the Flag Football League. This form ensures that all required data is gathered for efficient and accurate registration processing.
## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully, and ensure you answer all required questions.
2. Enter your details accurately and truthfully in the player information section.
3. Provide accurate emergency contact information.
4. Choose your preferred contact method from the list provided.
5. Enter payment information as required for registration fees.
6. Specify your team details if you're registering as part of a team.
7. Select your player skills from the options provided.
8. Confirm your registration status and payment status at the end of the form.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player Information** (`Player Information`, `text`, `required`): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant personal details to ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, `required`): Provide the name and contact information of someone we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Method** (`Contact Method`, `select_one`, `required`): Choose how you prefer to be contacted (phone, email, or address).
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, `text`, `required`): Enter your payment details, including payment method (e.g., check, online payment) and payment status (e.g., paid, pending).
* **Team Details** (`Team Details`, `text`, `required`: Enter your team name, jersey number, and any other relevant team information.
* **Player Skills** (`Player Skills`, `select multiple`, `required`): Select your skills as they apply to flag football. This could include skills such as throwing, catching, or running.
* **Registration Status** (`Registration Status`, `text`, `required`): Confirm your status as registered or pending.
* **Payment Status** (`Payment Status`, `text`, `required`): Confirm the status of your payment. This will ensure accurate and efficient processing of your registration. 

Note: The required fields are marked as required: true in the form structure. Please make sure you answer all these fields for a complete and successful submission.
