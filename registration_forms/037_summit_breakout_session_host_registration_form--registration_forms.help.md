# Summit Breakout Session Host Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a Summit Breakout Session Host and gather information about their session details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Enter the title of your session**: In this field, please enter the title of your Summit Breakout Session.
2. **Enter your name**: This is your name as the host of the session.
3. **Enter your email**: Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
4. **Enter your phone number**: Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
5. **Description of your session**: Describe your session in this field. (Note: This field is for brief description. For longer descriptions, please consider attaching a document.)
6. **Session Topic**: Select the topic of your session from the options provided.
7. **Session Time**: Choose the time for your session in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
8. **Session Date**: Choose the date of your session.
9. **Session Duration**: Enter the duration of your session in minutes.
10. **Session Start Time**: Choose the start time of your session.
11. **Session End Time**: Choose the end time of your session.
12. **Session Start Date**: Choose the start date of your session.
13. **Session Duration in Minutes**: Enter the duration of your session in minutes.
14. **Break Length**: Select the break length from the options provided.
15. **Break Length Minutes**: Enter the break length in minutes.
16. **Break Length Start**: Choose the start date of the break.
17. **Break Length End**: Choose the end date of the break.
18. **Break Length Duration**: Enter the duration of the break in minutes.
19. **Session Room**: Enter the session room number or name.
20. **Session Location**: Enter the session location.
21. **Session Capacity**: Enter the maximum capacity of your session.
22. **Session Capacity Max**: Enter the maximum capacity of your session.
23. **Host Name**: Enter your name as the host.
