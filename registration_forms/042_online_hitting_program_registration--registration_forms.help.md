# Online Hitting Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for an online hitting program. It will help us understand your hitting preferences and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that your email address and phone number are valid.
3. Select the correct level of experience from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Register for a Program** (program_registration): Please enter the name of the program you are registering for.
* **Date of Hitting** (program_date): Please enter the date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time of Hitting** (program_time): Please enter the time in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00 PM).
* **Hitting Location** (program_location): Please enter the location where the hitting will take place.
* **Coach's Name** (program_coach): Please enter the name of your coach.
* **Program Level** (program_level): Select the level of your experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Notes** (program_notes): Please provide any additional comments or notes about your hitting preferences.
* **Agree** (program_agree): Please choose one or multiple options from the list:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Agree 1** (program_agree_1): Please choose one or multiple options from the list:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Agree 2** (program_agree_2): Please choose one or multiple options from the list:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Undecided
* **Email** (form_email): Please enter a valid email address in the format email@domain.com.
* **Phone Number** (program_phone): Please enter a valid phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
