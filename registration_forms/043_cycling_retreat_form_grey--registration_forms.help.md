# cyclingRetreatFormGrey - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information and emergency contacts from participants in a cycling retreat or similar outdoor activity. It is designed to gather essential details such as participant names, contact information, and confirmation of terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please read the terms and conditions section carefully and select the appropriate option.
2.  Fill in your participant details:
	*   participantName: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
	*   email: Enter your email address.
	*   phoneNumber: Enter your phone number.
	*   dateOfBirth: Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
3.  Provide emergency contact information:
	*   emergencyContactName: Enter the name of the primary emergency contact.
	*   emergencyContactRelationship: Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact (e.g., family, friend, partner).
	*   emergencyContactPhoneNumber: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	*   Repeat steps 2 and 3 for multiple emergency contacts.
