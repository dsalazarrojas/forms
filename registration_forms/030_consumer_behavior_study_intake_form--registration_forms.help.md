# Consumer Behavior Study Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from consumers about their shopping habits, demographics, and preferences for a consumer behavior study.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in its entirety, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. For each question, select only one option that applies to you.
3. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender.
	+ Male
	+ Female
	+ Non-Binary
	+ Prefer Not to Say
	+ Other
* **Household Size** (`household_size`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of people in your household.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, `select_one`, optional): Select your highest level of education completed.
	+ High School or Less
	+ Some College
	+ Associate Degree
	+ Bachelor's Degree
	+ Master's Degree
	+ Doctorate
	+ Other
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, `select_one`, required): Select your current employment status.
	+ Employed Full-Time
	+ Employed Part-Time
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Unemployed
	+ Student
	+ Retired
	+ Other
* **Occupation** (`occupation`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job or profession.
* **Household Income** (`household_income`, `select_one`, optional): Select your household income bracket.
	+ Under $25,000
	+ $25,000 - $49,999
	+ $50,000 - $74,999
	+ $75,000 - $99,999
	+ $100,000 - $149,999
	+ $150,000+
	+ Prefer Not to Say
* **Marital Status** (`marital_status`, `select_one`, optional): Select your marital status.
	+ Single
	+ Married
	+ Divorced
	+ Widowed
	+ Separated
	+ Domestic Partnership
* **Number of Children** (`children_count`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of children under 18.
* **Shopping Habits** (`shopping_habits`, `text`, required): Describe your typical shopping habits.
* **Preferred Stores** (`preferred_stores`, `text`, optional): Enter the places where you usually shop.
* **Online Shopping Frequency** (`online_shopping_frequency`, `select_one`, required): Select how often you shop online.
	+ Daily
	+ Several Times a Week
	+ Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
	+ Never
* **Product Categories** (`product_categories`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the product categories you buy.
	+ Groceries
	+ Clothing
	+ Electronics
	+ Home Goods
	+ Beauty Products
	+ Health Products
	+ Sports and Outdoors
	+ Books and Media
	+ Toys and Games
	+ Other
* **Brand Preferences** (`brand_preferences`, `text`, optional): Enter specific brands you prefer.
* **Purchase Decision Factors** (`decision_factors`, `select_multiple`, required): Select what influences your purchase decisions.
	+ Price
	+ Quality
	+ Brand
	+ Reviews
	+ Recommendations
	+ Convenience
	+ Sustainability
	+ Promotions
	+ Packaging
	+ Availability
* **Media Consumption** (`media_consumption`, `text`, optional): Enter the media you regularly consume.
* **Social Media Usage** (`social_media_usage`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the social media platforms you use.
	+ Facebook
	+ Instagram
	+ Twitter
	+ TikTok
	+ LinkedIn
	+ Pinterest
	+ YouTube
	+ None
* **Availability for Study** (`study_availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select your availability for the study.
	+ Weekday Mornings
	+ Weekday Afternoons
	+ Weekday Evenings
	+ Weekend Mornings
	+ Weekend Afternoons
	+ Weekend Evenings
	+ Flexible
* **Why Do You Want to Participate?** (`participation_interest`, `text`, optional): Enter your motivation for joining the study.
* **Have You Participated in Studies Before?** (`previous_studies`, `select_one`, optional): Select if you have prior experience participating in studies.
	+ True
	+ False
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional information you want to share.
* **How Did You Hear About This Study?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Select the source of information about the study.
	+ Online Advertisement
	+ Social Media
	+ Email
	+ Flyer
	+ Referral
	+ Other

## Tips

1. Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Select only one option for each question that applies to you.
4. Enter specific examples when describing text fields.
5. Proofread your form for any errors or inconsistencies.
