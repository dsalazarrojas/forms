# Peace Corps Volunteer Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for Peace Corps Volunteers to request accommodation services during their stay. It will help us understand their needs and provide suitable living arrangements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Provide your information in the "user_info" field.
2. Fill in your volunteer information in the "volunteer_info" field.
3. Specify your accommodation preferences in the "accommodation_preferences" field.
4. Choose your preferred location in the "preferred_location" field.
5. Select your accommodation type from the options provided in the "accommodation_type" field. Please note that if you select "Other", you will need to specify your accommodation type in the "accommodation_type_other" field.
6. Enter your start date in the "start_date" field.
7. Enter your end date in the "end_date" field.
8. Provide your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
10. Provide your address in the "address" field.
11. Add any additional notes in the "notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Please enter a brief introduction to who you are and what you do.
* **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_info`, text, required/optional): Please provide information about your role and responsibilities as a Peace Corps Volunteer.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (`accommodation_preferences`, text, required/optional): Please describe your accommodation preferences (e.g. shared room, private room, etc.).
* **Preferred Location** (`preferred_location`, text, required/optional): Please specify the preferred city or area where you would like to stay.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, required/true): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Host Home
	+ Homestay
	+ Host Family
	+ Hostels
	+ Other
* **Accommodation Type Other** (`accommodation_type_other`, text, required/optional): If you selected "Other" above, please provide more information about your accommodation type.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/true): Please enter the date when you expect to arrive at your accommodation.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/true): Please enter the date when you expect to leave your accommodation.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/true): Please enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/true): Please enter your email address so we can contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/true): Please enter your address so we can locate you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Please add any additional notes that will help us understand your needs.
