# Customer Service Training Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to record attendance information for Customer Service Training sessions, ensuring accurate and up-to-date records of participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide details about the session, including session date, participant count, and session location.
2. Enter the customer's name and contact information (phone number and email).
3. Select the attendance status from the options provided.
4. Add any notes or comments from the customer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details (id: 3)** (`session_date`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the date of the Customer Service Training session.
* **Customer Name (id: 2)** (`customer_name`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the name of the customer attending the session.
* **Customer Contact Information (id: 4)** (`customer_phone`, `customer_email`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the customer's phone number and email address.
* **Attendance Status (id: 6)** (`attendance_status`, `required: false`)
	+ Select from the options: "Attended", "Not Attended", or "Unable to Attend".
* **Session Location (id: 7)** (`session_location`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the location of the Customer Service Training session.
* **Customer Feedback (id: 8)** (`feedback`, `required: false`)
	+ Provide any notes or comments from the customer.
* **Attendance Time (id: 17)** (`attendance_time`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the time of attendance for the session.
* **Attendance Date (id: 18)** (`attendance_date`, `required: false`)
	+ Enter the date of attendance for the session.

## Tips
- Be precise with dates and times to ensure accurate attendance records.
- If a customer attended the session and has feedback, please provide it in the feedback field.
- If a customer did not attend the session, please provide a reason for not attending in the feedback field.
