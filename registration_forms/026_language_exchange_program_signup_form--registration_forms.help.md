## Purpose
The Language Exchange Program Signup Form is designed to gather information from potential participants for the language exchange program.

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your School or Organization Name.
2. Provide your Contact Person's details.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Add your Phone number.
5. Select your Start Date and End Date of the program.
6. Specify your Language Level (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
7. Mention the Number of Participants.

* **School or Organization Name (text)**: Enter your school or organization name where you would like to be part of the language exchange program.

* **Start Date (date)**: Select the date you would like the language exchange program to start.
* **End Date (date)**: Select the date you would like the language exchange program to end.

* **Number of Participants (number)**: Mention the number of participants you would like to join the program.
