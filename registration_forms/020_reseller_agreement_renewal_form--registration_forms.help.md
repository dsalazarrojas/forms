# Reseller Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Reseller Agreement Renewal Form is a form used to collect information related to the renewal of reseller agreements between your company and your resellers. It is used to determine which agreements are eligible for renewal and to gather necessary information for the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form

- Please review your reseller agreements and select all agreements that are eligible for renewal.
- Answer the questions in the form accordingly.
- Enter the correct renewal date and time.
- If necessary, provide additional information in the reseller agreement renewal note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Reseller Agreement Renewal Form** (`reseller_agreement_renewal_form`, select_multiple, Required): Please select all agreements that are eligible for renewal.
* **Reseller Agreement** (`reseller_agreement`, text, Required): Enter the details of the agreement.
* **Reseller Agreement Renewal** (`reseller_agreement_renewal`, select_one, Required): Select whether the agreement is eligible for renewal.
* **Agreement Type** (`agreement_type`, select_one, Required): Select the type of agreement.
* **Reseller Agreement Renewal Date** (`reseller_agreement_renewal_date`, date, Required): Enter the renewal date of the agreement.
* **Reseller Agreement Renewal Time** (`reseller_agreement_renewal_time`, time, Required): Enter the renewal time of the agreement.
* **Reseller Agreement Renewal Note** (`reseller_agreement_renewal_note`, note, Required): Enter any additional information about the agreement renewal.
