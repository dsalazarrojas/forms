# expo_exhibitor_information_collection - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from exhibitors participating in the exhibition. The gathered data will be used to manage exhibitor lists, communicate with them, and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill out the form fields with the provided fields as a guide.
3. Review your submission before submitting to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your professional email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Company** (text, optional): If you represent a company, enter the name here.
* **Booth Number** (number, optional): If you have a specific booth number, enter it here.
* **Booth Location** (text, optional): If you have a preferred location within the exhibition hall, enter it here.
* **Start Date** (date, optional): If you have a preferred start date for the exhibition, enter it here.

## Tips
- Review your submission carefully before submitting to ensure accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, reach out to the exhibition organizers for guidance.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [exhibition email or contact info].
