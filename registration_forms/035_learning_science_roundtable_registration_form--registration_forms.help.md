# learning_science_roundtable_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from users for a learning science roundtable workshop. It will be used by organizers to gather contact and workshop details from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Form with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your contact information in the contact_info field.
3. Enter your workshop preferences in the workshop_preferences field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form (1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details for the workshop registration.
- **contact_info (2)**: Provide your contact information, which will be used to reach you regarding the workshop.
- **workshop_details (3)**: Unfortunately, this field is currently empty and does not have any guidance.
- **workshop_preferences (4)**: Unfortunately, this field is currently empty and does not have any guidance.
  (Please note: The current implementation of the form does not provide guidance for this field. However, you can provide any relevant information about your preferences or requirements for the workshop.)
