# Association Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Association Membership Registration Form is a crucial step in the membership process. It collects essential information required for membership registration, ensuring accurate and complete records for the association.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Start Registration" button to begin the registration process.
2.  Fill in your details as accurately and completely as possible.
3.  Review and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Registration Date** (`id: 1`, `title: Registration Date`, `label: Registration Date`, `hint: Date of membership registration`): This field captures the date of your membership registration.
    *   Please enter the date of your membership registration in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
*   **First Name** (`id: 2`, `name: member_first_name`, `label: First Name`, `hint: Your first name`): This field is for your first name.
    *   Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Last Name** (`id: 3`, `name: member_last_name`, `label: Last Name`, `hint: Your last name`): This field is for your last name.
    *   Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Email Address** (`id: 4`, `name: member_email`, `label: Email Address`, `hint: Primary email`): This is your contact email address.
    *   Enter a valid email address that can be used to communicate with you.
*   **Phone Number** (`id: 5`, `name: member_phone`, `label: Phone Number`, `hint: Contact phone`): This is your contact phone number.
    *   Enter your phone number as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **Street Address** (`id: 6`, `name: member_address`, `label: Street Address`, `hint: Full mailing address`): This field captures your full mailing address.
    *   Enter your full mailing address as it appears on your official identification documents.
*   **City** (`id: 7`, `name: member_city`, `label: City`, `hint: City of residence`): This field is for your city of residence.
    *   Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State/Province** (`id: 8`, `name: member_state`, `label: State/Province`, `hint: State or province`): This field is for your state or province of residence.
    *   Enter your state or province of residence.
*   **ZIP/Postal Code** (`id: 9`, `name: member_zip`, `label: ZIP/Postal Code`, `hint: Postal code`): This field captures your postal code.
    *   Enter the postal code for your mailing address.
*   **Country** (`id: 10`, `name: member_country`, `label: Country`, `hint: Country of residence`): This field is for your country of residence.
    *   Enter your country of residence.
*   **Membership Level** (`id: 11`, `name: membership_level`, `label: Membership Level`, `hint: Which membership tier?`): This field is for your membership level.
    *   Choose your membership level from the provided options.
*   **Membership Start Date** (`id: 12`, `name: membership_start_date`, `label: Membership Start Date`, `hint: When does membership begin?`): This field captures the start date of your membership.
    *   Enter the date when your membership begins.
*   **Membership Renewal Date** (`id: 13`, `name: membership_renewal_date`, `label: Membership Renewal Date`, `hint: Annual renewal date`): This field is for your membership renewal date.
    *   Enter the date when your membership is due for renewal.
*   **Annual Dues Amount** (`id: 14`, `name: annual_dues`, `label: Annual Dues Amount`, `hint: Membership fee`): This field is for your annual membership fee.
    *   Enter the amount of your annual membership fee.
*   **Dues Paid** (`id: 15`, `name: dues_paid`, `label: Dues Paid`, `hint: Has payment been received?`): This field is for payment confirmation.
    *   Confirm whether payment has been received or not.
*   **Payment Method** (`id: 16`, `name: payment_method`, `label: Payment Method`, `hint: How was payment made?`): This field is for your payment method.
    *   Choose how you made the payment.
*   **Payment Date** (`id: 17`, `name: payment_date`, `label: Payment Date`, `hint: When was payment received?`): This field captures the date when payment was received.
    *   Enter the date when payment was received (if applicable).
*   **Member Status** (`id: 18`, `name: member_status`, `label: Member Status`, `hint: Current membership status`): This field is for your current membership status.
    *   Choose your current membership status from the provided options.
*   **Subscribe to Newsletter** (`id: 19`, `name: newsletter_subscription`, `label: Subscribe to Newsletter`, `hint: Receive communications?`): This field is for newsletter subscription.
    *   Choose whether you want to receive communications from the association or not.
*   **Communication Preference** (`id: 20`, `name: communication_preference`, `label: Communication Preference`, `hint: How do you prefer to be contacted?`): This field is for your communication preference.
    *   Choose how you prefer to be contacted from the provided options.
*   **Committee Interest** (`id: 21`, `name: committee_interest`, `label: Committee Interest`, `hint: Volunteer on committees?`): This field is for committee interest.
    *   Choose whether you are interested in volunteering on committees or not.
*   **Areas of Committee Interest** (`id: 22`, `name: committee_areas`, `label: Areas of Committee Interest`, `hint: Which committees?`): This field is for areas of committee interest.
    *   Enter any committees you are interested in.
*   **Professional Background** (`id: 23`, `name: professional_background`, `label: Professional Background`, `hint: Brief description of experience`): This field is for a brief description of your professional background.
    *   Enter a brief description of your professional background.
*   **Referral Source** (`id: 24`, `name: referral_source`, `label: How Did You Hear About Us`, `hint: Source of referral`): This field is for the source of your referral.
    *   Choose the source of your referral from the provided options.
*   **Registration Complete Date** (`id: 25`, `name: registration_complete_date`, `label: Registration Complete Date`, `hint: Date form completed`): This field captures the date when the form is completed.
    *   This date is automatically populated when you submit the form.

## Tips

*   Ensure accurate and complete information is provided for each field.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
*   Contact the association's administration team if unsure about any field or have questions.
