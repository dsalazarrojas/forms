# Museum Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Museum Volunteer Registration Form is a necessary step for individuals who wish to contribute their time and skills to the museum's operations. This form collects personal information and preferences to help the museum tailor its communication and engagement with its volunteers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. This should be a valid email address where the museum can communicate with you regarding volunteer opportunities and events.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field. This helps the museum reach you for further information or to discuss volunteer opportunities.
5. Select your Date of Birth using the calendar or input it manually in the "Date of Birth" field.
6. Select your Date of Service (when you started volunteering) in the "Date of Service" field.
7. Choose your area of interest by selecting one or multiple options in the "Volunteer Area of Interest" field.
8. Indicate your availability for volunteering in the "Volunteer Availability" field by selecting all applicable days of the week.
9. If you prefer to be contacted by phone, email, or text message, select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### First Name
First Name (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here, as this will be used for internal communication and to personalize the museum's communication with you.

### Last Name
Last Name (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here to ensure we get to know you well.

### Email
Email (`email`, email, required: false): This is your email address where the museum will send you updates and information about volunteer opportunities and events.

### Phone
Phone (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so the museum can contact you for further information or to discuss volunteer opportunities.

### Date of Birth
Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): This field is for your birthdate, which is required for some purposes like insurance and other internal processes.

### Date of Service
Date of Service (`date_of_service`, date, required: false): If you have previously served as a volunteer, provide the date you started. This helps us understand your volunteering history.

### Volunteer Area of Interest
Volunteer Area of Interest (`volunteer_area_of_interest`, select_one, required: false): Choose the area you're interested in volunteering for, which can be one of the following:

*   Volunteer for Events
*   Volunteer for Education
*   Volunteer for Events and Education

### Volunteer Availability
Volunteer Availability (`volunteer_availability`, select_multiple, required: true): Choose the days of the week you are available to volunteer.

### Preferred Contact Method
Preferred Contact Method (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, required: false): Select how you prefer to be contacted by the museum, which can be through:

*   Phone
*   Email
*   Text Message
