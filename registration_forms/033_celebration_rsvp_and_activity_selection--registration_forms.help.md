# Celebration RSVP and Activity Selection - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information for a celebration event, including RSVP responses and activity selection.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in each section.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for each field based on your preferences.
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Name** (`Guest Name`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`Guest Email`, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`Guest Phone`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, required): Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Will You Attend?** (`RSVP Response`, required): Select your response to attending the event from the options provided.
* **Number of Guests (Including You)** (`Number of Guests`, required): Enter the total number of people attending, including yourself.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`Dietary Restrictions`, optional): Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Additional Dietary Needs** (`Additional Dietary Needs`, optional): If you have additional dietary needs, specify them here.
* **Which Activities Interest You?** (`Interested Activities`, required): Select all activities that interest you.
* **Primary Activity Choice** (`Primary Activity Choice`, optional): Select the activity that interests you most.
* **Special Accommodations Needed** (`Special Accommodations`, optional): If you need any special accommodations, specify them here.
* **Plus One Guest Name** (`Plus One Name`, optional): If you are bringing a guest, enter their name.
* **Plus One Dietary Restrictions** (`Plus One Dietary`, optional): If your plus one has dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Seating Preference** (`Seating Preference`, optional): Select your seating preference if available.
* **Do You Need Transportation?** (`Transport Needed`, optional): Select if you need transportation.
* **Additional Comments or Suggestions** (`Additional Comments`, optional): Enter any additional comments or suggestions.
* **I Confirm My RSVP Response** (`RSVP Confirmation`, required): Confirm your RSVP response.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out. Optional fields are not required but may be useful for providing additional information.
