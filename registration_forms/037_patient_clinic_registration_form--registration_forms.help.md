# Patient Clinic Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Patient Clinic Registration Form is used to gather essential details about a new patient visiting the clinic. This form helps us to create a complete patient record, which is necessary for proper care and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter the patient's last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Select the patient's **Date of Birth** using the date picker.
4. Provide the patient's **Address**.
5. Enter the patient's **Email** address.
6. Enter the patient's **Phone** number.
7. Enter the patient's **Medical Record Number** (if applicable).
8. Select the patient's **Insurance Provider** from the available options.
9. Choose the patient's **Patient Type** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Select the patient's date of birth using the calendar picker. This will help us to determine the patient's age and medical history.
* **Address (Text)**: Provide the patient's current address for contact and follow-up purposes.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the patient's active email address for secure communication.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter the patient's phone number for contact and emergency purposes.
* **Medical Record Number (Text)**: If the patient has an existing medical record number, enter it here.
* **Insurance Provider (Select)**: Select one or more insurance providers that cover the patient.

## Tips
