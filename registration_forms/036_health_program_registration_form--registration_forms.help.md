# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "prophets" form is used for registering and managing participant and provider information for programs. This includes collecting personal and contact details, program specifics, and status updates.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the requested information, paying attention to required fields.
2. Ensure that you enter the correct program type and status.
3. Add any relevant participant and provider notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1): Enter the participant's first name.
* **Last Name** (2): Enter the participant's last name.
* **Email** (3): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (4): Enter the participant's date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Phone Number** (5): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Program Name** (6): Enter the name of the program associated with the participant.
* **Program Type** (7): Select the type of program from the available options.
* **Program Start Date** (8): Enter the start date of the program in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Program End Date** (9): Enter the end date of the program in the YYYY-MM-DD format, if applicable.
* **Organization** (10): Enter the organization associated with the program.
* **Program Notes** (11): Enter any relevant notes about the program.
* **Address** (12): Enter the participant's address.
* **Contact Number** (13): Enter the contact number for the participant.
* **Contact Person** (14): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (15): Enter the contact email for the participant.
* **Program Status** (16): Select the status of the program from the available options.
* **Participant Notes** (17): Enter any relevant notes about the participant.
* **Participant Email** (18): Enter the participant's email address, if different from the main email.
* **Participant Phone** (19): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Address** (20): Enter the participant's address.
* **Provider Notes** (21): Enter any relevant notes about the provider.
* **Provider Email** (22): Enter the provider's email address.
* **Provider Phone** (23): Enter the provider's phone number.
* **Provider Address** (24): Enter the provider's address.
* **Submit** (25): Click to submit the form after filling out all required fields.

## Tips
1. Double-check the required fields before submitting the form.
2. Use the correct format for date fields (YYYY-MM-DD).
3. Select the correct program type and status.
4. Enter accurate and complete information for all fields.
