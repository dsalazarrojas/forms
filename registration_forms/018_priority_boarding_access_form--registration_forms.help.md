# Priority Boarding Access Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Priority Boarding Access Form is used to collect information from customers about their priority boarding access.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your customer information in the "Customer Information" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
5. Choose your boarding preference from the options provided in the "Boarding Preference" field.
6. Select your boarding status from the options provided in the "Boarding Status" field.
7. Choose your boarding type from the options provided in the "Boarding Type" field.
8. Select your travel document from the options provided in the "Travel Document" field.
9. Enter your boarding time in the "Boarding Time" field.
10. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
11. Enter your travel company in the "Travel Company" field.
12. Enter your travel agency in the "Travel Agency" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Customer Information**: Enter your full name and other relevant customer information. (Text field)
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used for communication. (Email field)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number. This will be used for contact. (Text field)
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter your date of birth. This is required for boarding purposes. (Date field)
* **Boarding Preference**: Choose your preferred boarding time. Priority 1, Priority 2, or Priority 3. (Select One field)
* **Boarding Status**: Choose your boarding status. Confirmed, Not Confirmed, or Unknown. (Select Multiple field)
* **Boarding Type**: Choose your boarding type. Standard, Fast Track, or Premium. (Select One field)
* **Travel Document**: Choose your travel document type. Passport, ID Card, or Driver's License. (Select One field)
* **Boarding Time**: Enter your boarding time. This is optional. (Time field)
* **Note**: Enter any additional notes or comments. This is optional. (Note field)
* **Travel Company**: Enter your travel company. This is optional. (Text field)
* **Travel Agency**: Enter your travel agency. This is optional. (Text field)

## Tips
* Ensure that you only enter your information once.
* Check your email address for correctness before submitting.
* Enter your phone number correctly to ensure you can be reached.

* Choose the correct boarding preference to ensure efficient boarding process.
* Choose the correct boarding status to reflect your actual boarding status.
* Choose the correct boarding type to reflect your boarding requirements.
* Choose the correct travel document to ensure boarding process.
* Enter your boarding time accurately to ensure you can be on time.
* Enter any additional notes or comments to provide more information.
* Enter your travel company and agency information accurately to ensure correct documentation.
