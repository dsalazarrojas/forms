# Portrait Photo Shoot Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a portrait photo shoot event. The form collects information from photographers about their upcoming photo shoots, including date, location, and contact details. This helps event organizers and clients manage photo shoot events efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Photographer Name** in the "Photographer Name" field.
2. Choose the **Shoot Date** of your event from the calendar.
3. Select the **Shoot Time** of your event.
4. Enter the **Location** of your shoot.
5. Provide any **Client Information** about the shoot.
6. Select the type of **Photo Type** for your shoot from the options provided (Landscape, Portrait, or Headshot).
7. Enter your **Contact Email** address.
8. Enter your **Contact Phone** number.
9. Add any additional **Photo Shoot Details** about your shoot.
10. Leave a **Comment** for the event organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Photographer Name**: Enter the name of the photographer (you or your assistant).
* **Shoot Date**: Select the date of your photo shoot event from the calendar.
* **Shoot Time**: Select the time of your photo shoot event.
* **Location**: Enter the location of your photo shoot event.
* **Client Information**: Enter any relevant information about the client(s) for your shoot.
* **Photo Type**: Select the type of photo shoot you will be doing (Landscape, Portrait, or Headshot).
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the shoot.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the shoot.
* **Photo Shoot Details**: Add any additional details about your photo shoot.
* **Comment**: Leave a comment for the event organizers about your photo shoot.
