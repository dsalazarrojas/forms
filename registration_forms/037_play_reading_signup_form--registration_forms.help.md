# play_reading_signup_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "play_reading_signup_form" is used to register participants for a play-reading event. It collects personal information from the participant to ensure correct role assignment and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your Participant Name in the text field.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Select your Role (Actor, Crew, or Audience).
4. Choose your Availability for the event.
5. If you have a Phone number, you can optionally enter it.
6. Specify your Time slot for the event.
7. You can optionally provide a Note about your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, **required**): Enter your name to identify you as a participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **required**): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about your participation.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, **required**): Choose your role in the production team (Actor, Crew, or Audience).
* **Availability** (`availability`, date, **required**): Select your availability for the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it so we can contact you.
* **Time** (`time`, time, **required**): Choose your time slot for the event.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation.

## Tips
* Make sure to register only once for each production team.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you have any issues, contact the event organizers for assistance.
