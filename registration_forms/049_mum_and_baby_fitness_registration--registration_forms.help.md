# Mum And Baby Fitness Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Mum And Baby Fitness Registration form is designed to gather essential information from parents and their babies for the purpose of registering them for our fitness classes. This form helps us to tailor our classes and programs to cater to the unique needs of each baby and parent pair, ensuring they have a safe, enjoyable, and productive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Make sure to provide detailed answers for all fields to help us understand you and your baby's needs better.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **parent_first_name (Text, Optional)**: Please provide your first name as a parent or guardian.
* **parent_last_name (Text, Optional)**: Please provide your last name as a parent or guardian.
* **parent_email (Email, Required)**: Enter your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you about your fitness journey.
* **parent_phone (Text, Optional)**: If you'd like us to contact you for any updates or questions, please provide your phone number.
* **baby_details (Text, Optional)**: Describe your baby's age, any medical conditions, or special needs that might impact their participation in our classes.
* **session_details (Text, Optional)**: Share any specific session preferences, such as dates and times, that suit you and your baby best.

## Tips
* Double-check that you've filled out all the required fields before submitting.
* Be as detailed as possible in your answers to help us provide the best possible experience.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided email address.
