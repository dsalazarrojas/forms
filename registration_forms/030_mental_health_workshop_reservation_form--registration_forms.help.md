# mental_health_workshop_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health workshop reservation form is designed for participants to register for a mental health workshop, providing essential information about themselves and their preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out all required fields.
2. Provide accurate information for each field to ensure a smooth and safe workshop experience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Participant Name (<participant_name>):**
  Please enter your full name as you would like to be referred to during the workshop.

- **Participant Email (<participant_email>):**
  Enter your valid email address, which will be used for communication about the workshop.

- **Participant Phone Number (<participant_phone>):**
  This field is optional, but it can be useful for workshop organizers and staff to contact you.

- **Workshop Name (<workshop_name>):**
  Indicate the name of the workshop you are interested in or registered for.

- **Workshop Date (<workshop_date>):**
  Enter the date of the workshop you are interested in or registered for.

- **Workshop Time (<workshop_time>):**
  Select the time of the workshop you are interested in or registered for.

- **Workshop Type (<workshop_type>):**
  Indicate the type of workshop you are interested in or registered for.

- **Workshop Location (<workshop_location>):**
  Enter the location of the workshop you are interested in or registered for.

- **Participant Notes (<participant_notes>):**
  Add any additional information about yourself or your needs that might be helpful for the workshop organizers.

- **Participant Interests (<participant_interests>):**
  Select all interests that apply from the provided options (Yes or No).

- **Participant Preferred Workshops (<participant_preferred_workshops>):**
  Select all workshops you are interested in from the provided options (Yes or No).

- **Participant Agreement (<participant_agreement>):**
  Confirm that you have read and agree to the workshop's terms and conditions.

- **Participant Allergies/Medical Conditions (<participant_allergies>):**
  List any allergies or medical conditions that might affect your participation in the workshop (optional).

- **Emergency Contact Information:**

  - **Name of Emergency Contact (<participant_emergency_contact>):**
    Provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.

  - **Relationship to You (<participant_emergency_relation>):**
    Describe your relationship to the emergency contact.

  - **Emergency Contact Phone Number (<participant_emergency_phone>):**
    Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

  - **Emergency Contact Address (<participant_emergency_address>):**
    Provide the address of your emergency contact.

  - **Additional Emergency Contact Notes (<participant_emergency_notes>):**
    Add any additional information about your emergency contact.

- **Other Contact:**

  - **Name of Other Contact (<participant_other_name>):**
    Provide the name of another contact person.

  - **Relationship to You (<participant_other_relationship>):**
    Describe your relationship to the other contact person.

  - **Phone Number of Other Contact (<participant_other_phone>):**
    Enter the phone number of your other contact.

  - **Email Address of Other Contact (<participant_other_email>):**
    Enter the email address of your other contact.
