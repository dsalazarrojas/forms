# Standby Participant Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Standby Participant Registration form is used to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in being part of a standby participant pool. This form helps us gather essential details for communication and coordination purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
2. Ensure you are not duplicating an existing registration.
3. Select your contact preference (Email, Phone, or Text) and provide the corresponding contact information.
4. If applicable, specify any special requirements or needs you may have.
5. Enter your waitlist position if you wish to be placed on the standby participant list.
6. Provide your contact information for emergency contacts (name, phone number, email).
7. If additional information is required, describe your special requirement 2.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session**: This field is a text input where you can specify the session you wish to participate in. Please note that this information is not strictly necessary for registration.
* **Contact Preference**: 
This field is a dropdown menu allowing you to specify your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, or Text). Select the method you would like to be used for communication regarding your participation in the standby participant program.
* **Special Requirement**: 
This field is a text input where you can specify any special requirements or needs you may have. Please provide any relevant details to ensure we can accommodate your needs.
* **Waitlist Position**: 
This field is a number input where you can specify your waitlist position if you wish to be placed on the list. Please note that this information is optional and only used to prioritize participants in the standby list.
* **Contact Number**: 
This field is a text input for your contact number. Please provide a valid contact number to ensure we can reach you.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: 
This field is a text input for your emergency contact name. Please provide the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: 
This field is a text input for your emergency contact phone number. Please provide a valid contact number to ensure we can reach the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email**: 
This field is a text input for your emergency contact email. Please provide a valid email address to ensure we can contact the emergency contact.
* **Special Requirement 2**: 
This field is a text input for any additional special requirements or needs you may have. Please provide any relevant details to ensure we can accommodate your needs.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information and special requirements for accuracy.
* Make sure you are not duplicating an existing registration.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us for assistance.
