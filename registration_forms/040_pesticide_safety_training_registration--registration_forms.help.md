# Pesticide Safety Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information and registrations for pesticide safety training sessions. Please use it for registering yourself or others for these sessions. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the session(s) you want to attend.
2. Enter attendee information.
3. Choose whether to sign up for the selected session(s).
4. Acknowledge the training session details.
5. Provide contact information (if necessary).
6. Enter your email address and phone number (if necessary).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Selections** (`Session Selections`, `select_multiple`, required: false): 
  Select all the sessions you want to attend from the provided options.
* **Attendee Info** (`Attendee Info`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose one of the three options to describe yourself.
* **Sign Ups** (`Sign Ups`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose "Yes" if you want to sign up for the selected session(s).
* **Acknowledge** (`Acknowledge`, `note`, required: false): 
  Read and agree to the training session terms and conditions.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `select_one`, required: false): 
  Choose one of the three options to describe your contact preference.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): 
  Enter a valid email address to receive updates and communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): 
  Enter a valid phone number for further communication.

## Tips
- Please fill in all required fields.
- Be precise in selecting your session preferences.
- Use a valid email address and phone number for smooth communication.
- Read and agree to the training session terms and conditions.
- If you have any issues or questions, feel free to contact the event organizers.
- This form is for registering for pesticide safety training sessions only.
- If you've registered before, please do not fill this form again.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event organizers for further clarification.
- Ensure that you fill in the correct fields for accurate event planning and organization.
