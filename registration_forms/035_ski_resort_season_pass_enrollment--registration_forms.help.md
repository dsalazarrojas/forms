# ski_resort_season_pass_enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "ski_resort_season_pass_enrollment" form helps users register themselves or someone else for a seasonal pass at a ski resort. It is a multi-page form designed to collect relevant information for processing the enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form sections carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and required information.
2. Select whether you are purchasing the pass for yourself or someone else.
3. Fill out the required fields for the selected option.
4. Review and complete any optional fields as needed.
5. Submit the form to initiate the enrollment process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select a Pass** (`pass_selection`, select_one, **Required**): 
  Choose whether you are purchasing a seasonal pass. Select 'Yes' to continue with the registration process.
* **Applicant Info** (`applicant_info`, number, **Required**): 
  Provide your age to help us process your seasonal pass registration. If you are registering someone else, please input their age.
* **Addons Info** (`add_on_info`, note, **Optional**): 
  Note any special requirements or equipment needs for the selected pass.
* **Payment** (`payment`, number, **Required**): 
  Enter the payment amount for the seasonal pass.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, **Optional**): 
  Provide an emergency contact name and phone number in case of an accident or other incident.

## Tips

* Be truthful in your responses as incorrect information may delay or deny your seasonal pass enrollment.
* Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* If you are registering someone else, please ensure you have their permission to do so.
* Review your information carefully before submitting to prevent errors.
* Contact the ski resort staff if you have any questions or concerns about the enrollment process.
