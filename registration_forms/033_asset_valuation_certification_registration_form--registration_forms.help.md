# Asset Valuation Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect personal and professional information from applicants seeking certification in asset valuation. The information collected will be used to assess the applicant's qualifications, experience, and background.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled.
3. Review each field carefully to ensure that your information is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Name** (Required, Text): Your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (Required, Email): The email address we will use for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Required, Text): Your contact number.
* **Mailing Address** (Required, Text): Your full address.
* **Date of Birth** (Required, Date): Your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Country of Residence** (Required, Select One): The country where you reside.
* **Highest Education Level** (Required, Select One): Your highest academic qualification.
* **Current Employer** (Required, Text): The name of your current company.
* **Job Title** (Required, Text): Your current position.
* **Years of Experience** (Required, Number): Your years of experience in the relevant field.
* **Asset Valuation Experience** (Required, Select One): Your level of experience in asset valuation.
* **Certification Level Sought** (Required, Select One): The certification level you are seeking.
* **Area of Specialization** (Required, Select Multiple): Your area of focus in asset valuation.
* **Do You Hold Other Certifications** (Required, Select One): If you hold other certifications.
* **List Existing Certifications** (Optional, Text): Describe your existing certifications.
* **Exam Delivery Preference** (Required, Select One): How you want to take the exam.
* **Preferred Exam Date** (Optional, Date): The date you prefer to take the exam.
* **Preferred Testing Location** (Optional, Text): The city or region where you want to take the exam.
* **Do You Need Study Materials** (Required, Select One): If you need study materials.
* **Professional Membership Status** (Required, Select One): Your status as a professional member.
* **Membership Number** (Optional, Text): If applicable, your membership number.
* **Registration Fee** (Required, Number): The program cost.
* **Payment Method** (Required, Select One): How you plan to pay.
* **Is Employer Sponsoring** (Required, Select One): If your employer is sponsoring you.
* **Need Continuing Education Credits** (Required, Select One): If you need CPE or similar credits.
* **How Did You Hear About the Program** (Required, Select One): How you were referred to the program.
* **I Accept Terms and Conditions** (Required, Select One): Agreement to the program policies.
* **I Agree to Code of Ethics** (Required, Select One): Agreement to the professional standards.
* **Applicant Signature** (Required, Text): Your signature to acknowledge the form submission.
* **Registration Date** (Required, Date): The date of submission.

Tips:
* Ensure that all fields are accurate and complete before submitting.
* Review the form carefully and ask for clarification if you are unsure about any field.
* Submitting duplicate forms may result in delays or rejection of your application.
