# Data Ethics Consulting Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Ethics Consulting Registration Form is designed to gather necessary information from clients interested in our data ethics consulting services. Please fill out this form to register your interests and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Client Details** with your name and relevant contact information.
2.  Select your **Client Interests** from the list (Data Privacy, Data Security, Data Confidentiality) that best describe your areas of concern.
3.  Provide any **Additional Notes** you'd like to add to help us better understand your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Details (Required)**:
    *   This field is where you'll enter your name and contact information.
* **Client Interests (Required)**:
    *   This field allows you to select one or more areas where you'd like our data ethics consulting services to focus on:
        *   Data Privacy: Issues related to data collection, storage, and use.
        *   Data Security: Concerns about data protection, encryption, and access control.
        *   Data Confidentiality: Matters of data sensitivity, classification, and access restrictions.
* **Additional Notes (Optional)**:
    *   This field is for any extra information you'd like to share with us, such as specific projects or deadlines you'd like to discuss.

## Tips
- Make sure to submit the form only once to avoid duplication.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- If you have any questions or need further clarification on any field, feel free to contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
