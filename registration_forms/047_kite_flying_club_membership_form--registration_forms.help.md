# Kite Flying Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering as a member of the Kite Flying Club. To become a member, please fill out the form with your details and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply follow these steps:

1. Scroll through the pages of the form and fill out each field with your information.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Information** (Member Information, text, optional)
	+ This is the first page of the form where you can enter your basic details as a prospective member.
	+ Please provide your name and any other information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, text, optional)
	+ On this page, you can enter your contact information, such as your address and phone number.
	+ This will help us stay in touch with you and keep you informed about club updates and events.
* **Membership Preferences** (Membership Preferences, text, optional)
	+ Here, you can express your preferences for your membership, such as your interests or any specific roles within the club you'd like to take on.
	+ Your input will help us tailor our club activities and communication to suit your needs.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your answers carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or need help with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
* We look forward to welcoming you as a member of the Kite Flying Club!
