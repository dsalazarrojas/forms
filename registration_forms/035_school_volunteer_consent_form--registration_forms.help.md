# School Volunteer Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for schools to collect volunteer information from parents and students for events or activities. It includes fields for volunteer details, student information, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct option for "Volunteer Permission" and "Student Permission".
2. Enter the necessary details for "Parent Information", "Event Information", "Emergency Contact", "Volunteer Experience", "Event Details", and "School Information" sections.
3. Select the correct "Volunteer Status" for the volunteer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Information** (`parent_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the parent's name, address, and phone number.
* **Event Information** (`event_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional information about the event or activity.
* **Volunteer Permission** (`volunteer_permission`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select "Yes" to indicate that the parent is granting permission for their child to volunteer.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, `select_one`, required: true): Select "Yes" to indicate that the parent has given permission for their child to volunteer.
* **Student Permission** (`student_permission`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate that the student has given permission to volunteer.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Enter contact information for an emergency contact in case of an urgent situation.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, `note`, required: false): This is a text field for the volunteer to provide additional information about their experience.
* **Volunteer Availability** (`volunteer_availability`, `date`, required: true): Enter the date and time of the volunteer's availability.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide additional information about the event or activity.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, `number`, required: true): Enter the school's ID number.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required: true): Enter the school's name.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required: true): Enter the school's address.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `text`, required: true): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: true): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **School Admin** (`school_admin`, `text`, required: true): Enter the name of the school administrator.
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, `select_one`, required: true): Select "Active" to indicate that the volunteer is active, or "Inactive" to indicate that they are not active.
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the student.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's name.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, `text`, required: false): Enter the school the student attends.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Student Permission 2** (`student_permission2`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate that the student has given permission to volunteer again.
* **Student Permission 3** (`student_permission3`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate that the student has given permission to volunteer again.

## Tips

- Make sure to select the correct volunteer status and permission options.
- Double-check the student's information for accuracy.
- Enter accurate contact information for the parent and school administrator.
- Review the form for completeness before submission.
