# Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register companies to the Manufacturing Innovation Hub. This form is used to collect relevant information about the company, including its contact details, to create a company profile and provide services related to the Manufacturing Innovation Hub.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by selecting the options from the "Company Details" field.
2. Fill in the "Company Name" field with your company's name.
3. Enter your company's address in the "Company Address" field.
4. Select the contact title from the "Contact Details" field.
5. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Manufacturing Innovation Hub Registration Form
* No explanation needed

### Company Details
* **Company Details** (`select_multiple`, required: false): This field allows you to select the options that describe your company's details. Please select all the options that apply to your company.

### Company Name
* **Company Name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your company's name as it appears on your business card or license.

### Company Address
* **Company Address** (`text`, required: false): Enter your company's address as it appears on your business card or license.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`select_one`, required: false): This field allows you to select the title of your contact person. Please select the title of your contact person.

### Contact Title
* **Contact Title** (`text`, required: false): Please enter the title of your contact person. This field is used to address your contact person.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (`text`, required: false): Enter your contact person's phone number.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your contact person's email address.
