# Health Literacy Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Literacy Innovation Forum registration form is designed to gather essential information from attendees to facilitate planning and organization of the event. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form carefully and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field clearly and concisely, as described in the Field-by-Field Explanation section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### General Information (1)
* **General Information** (`General Information`, `text`, required: false): This field is a text box where you can provide any general information about yourself or your organization.

### Attendee Details (2)
* **Attendee Details** (`Attendee Details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing any additional details about the attendee, such as their name, role, or affiliation.

### Preferences (3)
* **Preferences** (`Preferences`, `text`, required: false): In this field, you can specify any special requirements or requests you may have for the event.

### Consents (4)
* **Consents** (`Consents`, `text`, required: false): This field is for indicating your consent to receive updates, emails, or other forms of communication from the organizers.

### Contact Details (5)
* **Contact Details** (`Contact Details`, `text`, required: false): Provide your contact information, including your name, email, or phone number, so we can reach out to you if needed.

### Email (6)
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.

### Phone Number (7)
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Attendee Type (8)
* **Attendee Type** (`Attendee Type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you plan to attend the forum: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.

### If Other (9)
* **If Other** (`If Other`, `text`, required: false): Specify your other type of attendance, if not 'Yes' or 'No'.

### Please specify (10)
* **Please specify** (`Please specify`, `text`, required: false): This field is for providing additional information about your other type of attendance.

### Registration Method (11)
* **Registration Method** (`Registration Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose how you intend to register: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.

### Registration Method Other (12)
* **Registration Method Other** (`Registration Method Other`, `text`, required: false): If you chose 'Other', provide more information about your registration method.

### Registration Method Other Input (13)
* **Registration Method Other Input** (`Registration Method Other Input`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your registration method.

### Agreement (14)
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions. Choose 'Yes', 'No', or both options.

### Payment Method (15)
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required: false): Indicate how you intend to pay for the event: 'Yes', 'No', or 'Other'.

### Payment Method Other (16)
* **Payment Method Other** (`Payment Method Other`, `text`, required: false): If you chose 'Other', specify your method of payment.

### Payment Method Other Input (17)
* **Payment Method Other Input** (`Payment Method Other Input`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your payment method.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or issues, don't hesitate to reach out to the organizers.
- This form is for registration purposes only. If you need to make any changes or updates, contact the organizers directly.
