# event_partner_onboarding_session_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for event partners to provide information about an onboarding session. It collects details about the event and partner information, which will be used for internal purposes within the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the event partner information on Page 1.
2. Provide session details on Page 2.
3. Enter event details on Page 3.
4. Add RSVP details on Page 4.
5. Set contact preferences and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Partner Info**: This is the first page of the form where you will enter information about the event partner. 
  * **Name**: Enter the name of the event partner.
* **Session Details**: 
  * **Session Details**: Enter the session details, such as the session topic or agenda.
* **Event Details**: 
  * **Event Description**: Enter a brief description of the event.
  * **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event.
  * **Event Status**: Choose the status of the event: Active, Not Active, or Pending.
* **RSVP Details**: 
  * **RSVP Date**: Choose the date for the RSVP.
  * **RSVP Time**: Choose the time for the RSVP.
* **Contact Preferences**: Enter any additional contact preferences you have.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Time (duplicate)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of the previous "Event Time" field and can be ignored.
* **Partner Name**: Enter the name of the partner.
* **Partner Address**: Enter the address of the partner.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event: Public, Private, or Internal.
* **Event Type (duplicate)**: This field appears to be a duplicate of the previous "Event Type" field and can be ignored.
* **Event Category**: Choose the category of the event.
* **Event Subcategory**: Choose the subcategory of the event.
* **Event Tags**: Choose any tags relevant to the event.
* **Event Organizer**: Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email**: Enter the email of the event organizer.

Note: Some fields seem to be duplicates (e.g., "Event Time" and "Event Time (duplicate)") and can be ignored.
