# Field Trip Transportation Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Trip Transportation Authorization Form is used to authorize the transportation of students to and from school field trips. This form provides essential information about the trip, including student and vehicle details, to ensure a safe and successful journey.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the school code for the school the student attends.
2. Provide the student's name and ID number.
3. Enter the parent's name and email address.
4. Enter the student's date of birth.
5. Enter the driver's license number and vehicle make, model, year, and color.
6. Enter the vehicle license plate number.
7. Enter the vehicle insurance information.
8. Enter the vehicle registration number.
9. Enter the school phone number.
10. List the names and phone numbers of chaperones accompanying the trip.
11. Have the chaperone and student sign the form.
12. Enter the transportation agreement details.
13. Enter any additional notes.
14. Enter the vehicle capacity.
15. Enter a brief description of the vehicle.
16. Enter the school contact name.
17. Enter the school contact phone number.
18. Enter the school address.
19. Enter the date of the trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Code** (`school_code`, text, required): Enter the school code where the student attends.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter the student's name.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required): Enter the student's ID number.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter the student's date of birth (if applicable).
* **Driver's License Number** (`driver_license_number`, text, required): Enter the driver's driver license number.
* **Vehicle Make Model** (`vehicle_make_model`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's make and model.
* **Vehicle Year** (`vehicle_year`, number, required): Enter the vehicle's year.
* **Vehicle Color** (`vehicle_color`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's color.
* **Vehicle License Plate** (`vehicle_license_plate`, text, required): Enter the vehicle license plate number.
* **Vehicle Insurance** (`vehicle_insurance`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's insurance information.
* **Vehicle Registration** (`vehicle_registration`, text, required): Enter the vehicle's registration number.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, text, required): Enter the school phone number.
* **Chaperones' Names and Phone Numbers** (`chaperone_list`, text, required): List the names and phone numbers of chaperones accompanying the trip.
* **Chaperone Signature** (`chaperone_signature`, text, optional): Have the chaperone sign the form.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, text, optional): Have the student sign the form.
* **Transportation Agreement** (`transportation_agreement`, text, required): Enter the transportation agreement details.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Vehicle Capacity** (`vehicle_capacity`, number, required): Enter the vehicle capacity.
* **Vehicle Description** (`vehicle_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the vehicle.
* **School Contact Name** (`school_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the school contact name.
* **School Contact Phone** (`school_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the school contact phone number.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, text, required): Enter the school address.
* **Date of Trip** (`date_of_trip`, date, required): Enter the date of the trip.
