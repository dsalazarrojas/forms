# Chamber Of Commerce Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for businesses interested in becoming members of the Chamber of Commerce. It collects necessary information about the business and the business owner, which will be used to facilitate the membership process.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
*   Fill in all required fields.
*   Ensure that the business owner's contact information is accurate and up-to-date.
*   Choose the correct membership tier and term.
*   Indicate the correct referral source.
*   Select the appropriate areas of interest or involvement.
*   Enter the brief description of the business's mission.
*   Agree to the Chamber membership terms.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Business Name** (`business_name`): Enter the official legal name of your business.
*   **Type of Business** (`business_type`): Select the industry or business sector that best describes your business.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`): Enter the street address of your business.
*   **City, State, Zip Code** (`city_state_zip`): Enter the city, state, and zip code of your business location.
*   **Business Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter the main contact number of your business.
*   **Business Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter the official business email address.
*   **Website** (`website`): If your business has a website, enter the URL here. This field is optional.
*   **Business Owner or Principal Name** (`business_owner_name`): Enter the full name of the business owner or principal.
*   **Owner's Title** (`owner_title`): Enter the title or position held by the business owner or principal. This field is optional.
*   **Owner's Email** (`owner_email`): Enter the personal email address of the business owner or principal.
*   **Owner's Phone Number** (`owner_phone`): Enter the business owner or principal's phone number.
*   **Years in Business** (`business_years_operating`): If your business has been operating for more than 1 year, enter the number of years you have been in business. This field is optional.
*   **Number of Employees** (`employee_count`): Enter the total number of employees at this location. This field is optional.
*   **Annual Revenue Range** (`annual_revenue_range`): Select the approximate annual revenue range of your business.
*   **Membership Tier** (`membership_tier`): Choose the membership level that best suits your business needs.
*   **Membership Term** (`membership_term`): Select the length of your membership term.
*   **Referral Source** (`referral_source`): If you were referred to the Chamber by someone, please indicate the source of the referral.
*   **Areas of Interest or Involvement** (`chamber_involvement_interests`): Select all areas of interest or involvement that apply to your business.
*   **Brief Description of Business Mission** (`business_mission`): Enter a brief description of your business's mission.
*   **Agreement to Chamber Membership Terms** (`membership_agreement`): Agree to the Chamber's membership rules and benefits.
*   **Permission to Contact for Chamber Participation** (`contact_permission`): Indicate whether the Chamber may contact you about events and chamber activities.
