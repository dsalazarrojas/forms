# Lease Process Modernization Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the Lease Process Modernization process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact details section with your information.
2. Indicate your interests in the Lease Process Modernization process.
3. Provide your consent to participate in the process.
4. Answer questions about your participant or team's organization.
5. Fill out the participant or team member sections with details about your organization and team members.
