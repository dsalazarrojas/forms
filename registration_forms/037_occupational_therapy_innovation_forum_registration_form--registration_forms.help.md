# Occupational Therapy Innovation Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for the Occupational Therapy Innovation Forum.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide your Phone number (if you have one).
3. State your goals for attending the forum in the "Goals" field.
4. Specify your professional role and affiliation.
5. Choose a payment method and payment status.
6. Provide any additional notes or comments.
7. Give consent for terms and conditions, data collection, email, contact, and sharing of your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form Fields**: 
  - This is a text field for any additional comments or information you want to provide.

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your email address. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Goals** (`goals`, `note`, required/optional): State your goals for attending the forum, e.g., learn, network, or collaborate.

* **Professional** (`professional`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your professional role.
  •   **Student**: Select this if you are a student.
  •   **Practitioner**: Select this if you are a practitioner.
  •   **Educator**: Select this if you are an educator.

* **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your organization name.
* **Role in Organization** (`role_in_organisation`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your role in the organization.
  •   **Student**: Select this if you are a student.
  •   **Practitioner**: Select this if you are a practitioner.
  •   **Educator**: Select this if you are an educator.

* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select multiple affiliations you belong to.
  •   **Student**: Select this if you are a student.
  •   **Practitioner**: Select this if you are a practitioner.
  •   **Educator**: Select this if you are an educator.

* **Date of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you registered for the forum.
* **Expected Participation Date** (`expected_participation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you expect to participate in the forum.

* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the payment method you choose.
  •   **PayPal**: Select this if you pay by PayPal.
  •   **Bank Transfer**: Select this if you pay by bank transfer.
  •   **Cash**: Select this if you pay by cash.

* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required/optional): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your payment status.
  •   **Paid**: Select this if you have paid.
  •   **Not Paid**: Select this if you haven't paid.

* **Notes** (`notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes or comments.

* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any additional information you want to provide.

* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your consent status.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you give your consent.
  •   **No**: Select this if you don't give your consent.

* **Agreement to Terms** (`agreement_to_terms`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your agreement status to the terms and conditions.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you agree to the terms and conditions.
  •   **No**: Select this if you do not agree.

* **Consent to Data Collection** (`consent_to_data_collection`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your consent status for data collection.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you give your consent.
  •   **No**: Select this if you don't give your consent.

* **Consent to Email** (`consent_to_email`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your consent status for email.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you give your consent.
  •   **No**: Select this if you don't give your consent.

* **Consent to Contact** (`consent_to_contact`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your consent status for contact.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you give your consent.
  •   **No**: Select this if you don't give your consent.

* **Consent to Share** (`consent_to_share`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your consent status for sharing of your data.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you give your consent.
  •   **No**: Select this if you don't give your consent.

* **Consent to Use** (`consent_to_use`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your consent status for the use of your data.
  •   **Yes**: Select this if you give your consent.
  •   **No**: Select this if you don't give your consent.

## Tips
* Please ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Review and confirm your consent to terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organizers.
* Please note that not all fields are required, but it is best to provide all necessary information for a smooth event preparation.
* The form will guide you through the required information.
* Review the provided instructions carefully before submitting the form.
