# Financial Strategy Planning Cohort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register into a financial strategy planning cohort. Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues with registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Please enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Please enter your valid email address where you can be contacted. This will be used for communication and reminders.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Please enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YY.
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, optional): Please enter the date you are registering for this cohort. If left blank, the system will use the current date.
* **Cohort Name** (Cohort Name, select one, optional): Please select the cohort you are registering for. You can choose from the options provided, or select "Other Cohort" to provide your own name in the **Cohort Details** field.
* **Cohort Details** (Cohort Details, text, optional): If you selected "Other Cohort", please provide the name of the cohort you are registering for.
* **Submit** (Submit, select multiple, required): Please select "True" to agree to our terms and conditions, and "False" to cancel your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure successful registration.
- If you selected "Other Cohort", ensure you provide a valid name for your cohort in the **Cohort Details** field.
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
