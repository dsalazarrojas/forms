# Smart Material Tracking Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the Smart Material Tracking Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Attendee Name.
2. Enter your Email address (if you want us to contact you).
3. Add your Phone number (if you want us to contact you by phone).
4. Enter your Date of Birth (for workshop evaluation purposes).
5. Select your Time of Registration (to help us better understand your workshop schedule).
6. Choose one of the options for "Select One".
7. Choose multiple options for "Select Multiple".
8. Optionally, add any additional notes about your preferences or interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or driver's license.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address to receive workshop updates and important announcements.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number to receive phone updates or be contacted by us in case of last-minute changes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Provide your date of birth for workshop evaluation purposes (this information will not be shared with anyone).
* **Time of Registration** (`time_of_registration`, time, required: false): Select your preferred time of registration to help us schedule the workshop accordingly.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required: false): Choose one option from the available options to indicate your preference.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required: false): Choose multiple options from the available options to indicate your preferences.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional notes or comments about your preferences or interests.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
* Double-check your entries for accuracy.
* If you need help, feel free to ask us at the workshop.
