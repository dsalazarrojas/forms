# Virtual Home Viewing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Home Viewing Registration Form is a user-friendly form designed to gather essential information from users who wish to view a virtual home viewing. This form is intended to collect personal details and visit information for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first name in the "first name" field.
* Fill in your last name in the "last name" field.
* Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use a valid email address to ensure you receive any updates or notifications.
* Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
* Fill in the date of your visit in the "date of visit" field. This is the date you plan to visit the property.
* Fill in the time of your visit in the "time of visit" field. This is the time you plan to arrive at the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates or notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of visit** (`date_of_visit`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to visit the property.
* **Time of visit** (`time_of_visit`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the property.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
* Please check your email address for any updates or notifications regarding your virtual home viewing.
