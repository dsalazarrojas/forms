# Recreation Center Membership Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Recreation Center Membership Payment Form is a form intended to collect information and payment details from customers wishing to join or renew their membership at the recreation center.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone_number" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the "payment_method" dropdown menu.
6. Provide any additional payment details in the "payment_details" field.
7. Choose your desired membership type from the "membership_type" dropdown menu.
8. Enter the payment amount for your membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1 - First Name
* **First Name** (first_name, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.

### Page 1 - Last Name
* **Last Name** (last_name, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.

### Page 1 - Email
* **Email** (email, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.

### Page 1 - Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (phone_number, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

### Page 1 - Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### Page 1 - Payment Details
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, note, optional): Enter any additional payment details (e.g., payment terms, payment date).

### Page 1 - Membership Type
* **Membership Type** (membership_type, select_multiple, optional): Choose your desired membership type (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).

### Page 1 - Payment Amount
* **Payment Amount** (payment_amount, number, optional): Enter the payment amount for your membership.

## Tips
* Please double-check your entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Payment method options will be displayed as per your choice, and you will be redirected to the next step for payment verification after selection.
