# International Volleyball Exchange Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the International Volleyball Exchange Program. It is essential to provide accurate and complete information in the form to ensure a smooth and successful experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the User Information page with your basic details.
2. Proceed to the Contact Information page and enter your emergency contact's details.
3. On the Volleyball Information page, provide information about your volleyball preferences.
4. In the Emergency Contact section, you can add an additional contact person's information.
5. Review all the fields carefully and ensure that you have provided complete and accurate information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_information`, text, optional): This page is used to collect basic information about you, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, optional): This page is used to collect information about your emergency contact, including their name, email, and phone number.
* **Volleyball Information** (`volleyball_info`, text, optional): This page is used to provide information about your volleyball preferences and experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): This section is used to add an additional contact person's information.
* **Emergency Contact's Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact's Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
