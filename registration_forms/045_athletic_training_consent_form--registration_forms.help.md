# Athletic Training Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The athletic training consent form is a crucial document that ensures athletes and their parents/guardians understand the risks and benefits of participating in athletic training programs. It gathers essential information about the athlete's medical history, insurance, and contact details. By completing this form, you will be providing necessary consent for your child to participate in our programs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form with accurate and honest information.
2. Make sure to sign and date the form.
3. If you are under 18, please ensure your parent/guardian signs the form as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender (Male, Female, Non-Binary, or Prefer Not to Say).
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
* **Sport**: Select the sport you are interested in or currently playing (e.g., Football, Basketball, etc.).
* **Training Program**: Select the type of training program you are interested in (e.g., Strength and Conditioning, Speed and Agility, etc.).
* **Program Duration**: Select the length of time you would like to be enrolled in the training program (e.g., One-Time Session, One Week, etc.).
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your parent or guardian's full name if you are under 18.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter your parent or guardian's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter your parent or guardian's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person who can contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies you have.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications you are taking.
* **Previous Injuries**: List any significant injuries you have had in the past.
* **Current Injuries or Limitations**: List any current injuries or limitations you have.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy number or member ID.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Enter the name of your regular doctor.
* **Primary Physician Phone**: Enter the phone number of your regular doctor.
* **Physical Examination Date**: Enter the date of your last sports physical.
* **Medical Clearance Status**: Select the status of your medical clearance (Cleared Without Restrictions, Cleared With Restrictions, Not Cleared, or Pending Review).
* **Risk Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand the risks associated with athletic training.
* **Injury Risk Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand that injuries may occur during training.
* **Medical Treatment Permission**: Grant permission to seek emergency medical treatment if needed.
* **Scope of Treatment Permission**: Select the scope of treatment you are authorized to receive.
* **Liability Waiver**: Confirm that you understand you are waiving liability for ordinary negligence.
* **Media Release**: Confirm that you give permission for photos and videos to be taken during training.
* **Information Sharing Permission**: Select how much information you allow to be shared with coaches.
* **Commitment Statement**: Confirm that you commit to following trainer instructions.
* **Athlete Signature**: Sign with your full name as an athlete.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign with your full name if you are under 18.
* **Signature Date**: Date the form with the date you sign.

## Tips

- Please answer all questions truthfully and completely.
- Make sure to sign and date the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to ask.
