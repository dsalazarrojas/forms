# Beverage Recycling Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for participants who want to join the Beverage Recycling Initiative. It collects information about their experience, preferences, and goals to optimize the recycling process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields marked as "Required" on the form.
2. Select options for fields with multiple options (e.g., preferred collection method).
3. Enter text fields (e.g., name, address) as accurately as possible.
4. Read the "Terms" field carefully and select "Yes, I Accept" to agree to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address, where you will receive updates and notifications about the program.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for program communication.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of organization you represent:
	+ Individual
	+ School
	+ Business
	+ Non-Profit
	+ Government
	+ Community Group
	+ Other
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your full address for pickup or communication.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state.
* **ZIP Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code.
* **Previous Recycling Experience** (`recycling_experience`, select_one, required): Choose your experience level:
	+ No Experience
	+ Some Experience
	+ Moderate Experience
	+ Extensive Experience
* **Beverage Types You Recycle** (`beverage_types`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of beverage containers you recycle:
	+ Plastic Bottles
	+ Glass Bottles
	+ Aluminum Cans
	+ Steel Cans
	+ Cartons
	+ Juice Boxes
	+ Coffee Cups
	+ Other
* **Estimated Monthly Volume** (`monthly_volume`, select_one, required): Choose your estimated monthly volume:
	+ Less than 50
	+ 50 to 100
	+ 100 to 500
	+ 500 to 1000
	+ Over 1000
* **Preferred Collection Method** (`collection_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred collection method:
	+ Curbside Pickup
	+ Drop-off Center
	+ Community Collection Event
	+ Mail-In Program
	+ Other
* **Preferred Pickup Frequency** (`pickup_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose how often you would like pickups to occur:
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ On-Demand
* **Interest in Recycling Education** (`education_interest`, select_one, required): Select if you are interested in recycling education materials:
	+ Yes, Please Send Materials
	+ Maybe
	+ No, Thank You
* **Volunteer Interest** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Choose if you are interested in volunteering:
	+ Yes, I Want to Volunteer
	+ Maybe
	+ No, Thank You
* **What Motivates You to Participate** (`motivation`, text, required): Enter why you want to join the initiative.
* **Recycling Challenges You Face** (`challenges`, text, optional): Enter any challenges you face with recycling.
* **Suggestions for the Program** (`suggestions`, text, optional): Enter any suggestions for the program improvement.
* **How Did You Hear About This Initiative** (`how_heard_about`, select_one, optional): Choose where you heard about this program:
	+ Social Media
	+ Website
	+ Friend or Family
	+ Community Event
	+ Email Newsletter
	+ Local News
	+ Other
* **I Accept the Program Terms** (`terms_accepted`, select_one, required): Select "Yes, I Accept" to agree to the program terms.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your participation.
