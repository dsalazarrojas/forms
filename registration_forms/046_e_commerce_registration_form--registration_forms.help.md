# e-commerce_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for customer and partner registration. Fill out the form completely to provide your information and agree to the terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **First Name** field with your personal name.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the corresponding field.
3. Provide a valid **Email** address to receive notifications and updates.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for communication purposes.
5. If you have a **Company Name**, enter it in the corresponding field.
6. If you'd like, provide your **Address** for shipping or meeting purposes.
7. Enter your **Zip Code** to ensure accurate delivery or meeting arrangements.
8. Select your **Country** from the options provided.
9. For **Region**, select all that apply from the given options.
10. Enter your **Store Name**, if you have one.
11. Create a **Password** for your account.
12. If required, enter your **Date of Birth** for verification purposes.
13. If required, enter the **Time of Birth** for verification purposes.
14. If necessary, add any **Note** about your registration.
15. Agree to the **Agreement** terms by selecting the corresponding option.
16. If required, enter the **Agreement Date** for reference.
17. If required, enter the **Agreement Time** for reference.
18. Enter your **Agreement IP** if applicable.
19. Provide the **Agreement URL** if required.
20. Select all that apply for **Agreement Agreement**, if required.
21. Add any **Agreement Agreement Note**, if necessary.
22. If required, enter the **Agreement Agreement Date** for reference.
23. If required, enter the **Agreement Agreement Time** for reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your personal name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your family name.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required to receive notifications and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Company Name**: If you have a business or company, enter it here.
* **Address**: Enter your address for shipping or meeting purposes.
* **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for accurate delivery or meeting arrangements.
* **Country**: Select your country of residence from the provided options.
* **Region**: Select all that apply from the given options.
* **Store Name**: If you have a store or business, enter it here.
* **Password**: Create a strong password for your account.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
* **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth for verification purposes.
* **Note**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Agreement**: Agree to the terms of service by selecting the corresponding option.
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date you agree to the terms of service.
* **Agreement Time**: Enter the time you agree to the terms of service.
* **Agreement IP**: Enter your IP address if required.
* **Agreement URL**: Enter the URL of the agreement if required.
* **Agreement Agreement**: Select all that apply from the given options.
* **Agreement Agreement Note**: Add any additional notes about your agreement.
* **Agreement Agreement Date**: Enter the date you agree to the agreement.
* **Agreement Agreement Time**: Enter the time you agree to the agreement.
* **Agreement Agreement IP**: Enter your IP address if required.
* **Agreement Agreement URL**: Enter the URL of the agreement if required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the required fields completely.
* Review your information carefully before submission.
* Agree to the terms of service to proceed.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
