# Newsletter Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from interested volunteers who want to join our newsletter. It's a simple registration form designed to gather necessary details for communication and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name here. This is a required field if you want to be registered as a volunteer. 
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here. Similar to the First Name field, this is also a required field for registration purposes.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you regularly check. This is a required field for us to communicate with you about the newsletter and other updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number here. This field is optional but helps us contact you if needed.

## Tips
- Please ensure that the email address and phone number you provide are accurate and up to date. This ensures we can reach you efficiently.
- If you have any issues or questions while completing the form, feel free to contact us for help.
- Once you're done, click submit and we'll get back to you with further instructions and updates on the newsletter.
