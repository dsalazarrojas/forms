# Innovative Design Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Innovative Design Training Registration Form is a multi-page form designed to collect information from participants for the Innovative Design Training. This form is intended to capture demographic data, training preferences, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant name, email, and phone number on the first page.
2. Select your preferred training session type on the second page.
3. Enter your goals and participant notes on the third page.
4. Enter your participant registration number and select your payment amount on the fourth page.
5. Choose your payment terms and payment status on the fifth page.
6. Select your participant status on the sixth page.
7. Finally, review and select any additional participant statuses on the last page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name (Participant Name)** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false):
	+ Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on the training materials and certification.
* **Participant Email (participant_email**, email, required: false):
	+ Enter your valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Participant Phone (participant_phone, text, required: false)**:
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Session Type (training_session_preferences, select_multiple, required: false)**:
	+ Choose one or more sessions that you'd like to attend. You can select all options that apply.
* **Goals (goals, text, required: false)**:
	+ Enter your goals for participating in the training.
* **Participant Notes (participant_notes, text, required: false)**:
	+ Enter any additional notes about yourself or your participation.
* **Payment Amount (payment_amount, number, required: false)**:
	+ Enter the amount you'll pay for the training.
* **Payment Terms (payment_terms, select_one, required: false)**:
	+ Choose how you'd like to pay for the training.
* **Participant Registration Number (participant_registration_number, text, required: false)**:
	+ Enter your registration number for the training.
* **Payment Status (payment_status, select_one, required: false)**:
	+ Select your payment status.
* **Participant Status (participant_status, select_one, required: false)**:
	+ Choose your participant status.
* **Additional Participant Status (participant_status_2, select_multiple, required: false)**:
	+ Select any additional participant statuses that apply.
* **More Additional Participant Status (participant_status_3, select_multiple, required: false)**:
	+ Select any further additional participant statuses that apply.

Note: Each page has a specific set of fields that must be completed. Make sure to review each page before proceeding to the next one.
