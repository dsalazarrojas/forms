# Athletic Team Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Team Information Form is a one-time registration process for athletes to provide accurate and comprehensive information about themselves, their team affiliation, and medical history. It ensures their safe and successful participation in team activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately. Ensure you have all the necessary information and permissions.
2. Provide the required information for each field. Incomplete or missing information may cause delays in processing your registration.
3. Submit the form to the relevant authorities for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as per your government ID or passport.
* **Preferred Name**: This is the name you prefer to be called. It may be different from your full name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your gender from the provided options.
* **Athlete ID Number**: Enter your student or team identification number.
* **School Name**: Enter your school name.
* **Grade Level**: Select your current grade level from the provided options.
* **Street Address**: Enter your home address.
* **City**: Enter the city of your residence.
* **State**: Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP Code**: Enter your ZIP or postal code.
* **Athlete Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Athlete Email**: Enter your contact email.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your parent or guardian's full name.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter your parent or guardian's contact phone number.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter your parent or guardian's contact email.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship**: Describe the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Sport**: Select your primary sport from the provided options.
* **Team Level**: Select your team level from the provided options.
* **Primary Position**: Enter the main position you play.
* **Jersey Number**: Enter your assigned jersey number (if applicable).
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have played the sport.
* **Height**: Enter your height in feet and inches or cm.
* **Weight**: Enter your weight in pounds or kg.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies you may have.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Previous Sports Injuries**: List any previous sports injuries.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your insurance policy or member number.
* **Primary Physician Name**: Enter the name of your regular doctor.
* **Primary Physician Phone**: Enter your doctor's contact number.
* **Transportation Plan**: Select how you plan to get to practices and games from the provided options.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Select your T-shirt size for team apparel.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information the coaching staff should know.

Note: The information collected is for the athlete's safe and successful participation in team activities. Please be honest and accurate in filling out this form.
