# Personal Trainer Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Intake Form is designed to collect essential information from clients and trainees to ensure a safe and effective training experience. This form helps personal trainers to gather details about the client's fitness goals, medical history, and training preferences, as well as their agreement to the terms and conditions of the training program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Client Information** (`personal_trainer_intake_form_client_information`, text, optional): Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
2. **Fitness Goals** (`fitness_goals`, select_multiple, true): Select all the fitness goals you would like to achieve through training. This may include losing weight, building muscle, or improving overall health.
3. **Trainee Information** (`trainee_information`, select_one, true): Choose the type of trainee you are (e.g., athlete, beginner, or elite).
4. **Additional Trainee Information** (`additional_trainee_information`, text, true): Provide any additional details about your trainee status, such as your experience level or training background.
5. **Training Goals** (`training_goals`, text, false): Describe your specific training goals and objectives.
6. **Medical Information** (`medical_information`, text, false): Share any medical conditions or concerns that may impact your training.
7. **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, false): Describe your medical history, including any previous injuries, surgeries, or health issues.
8. **Contraindications** (`contraindications`, text, false): List any contraindications or health conditions that may impact your training.
9. **Diet and Nutrition** (`diet_and_nutrition`, select_one, true): Choose how you would like to approach your diet and nutrition during training (e.g., balanced, strict, or flexible).
10. **Trainor Information** (`trainor_information`, text, false): Provide any additional details about your trainer or training preferences.
11. **Training Frequency** (`training_frequency`, select_one, true): Select how often you would like to train (e.g., daily, weekly, or occasional).
12. **Training Frequency Note** (`training_frequency_note`, text, false): If you have any specific training frequency requests or concerns, please describe them here.
13. **Training Intensity** (`training_intensity`, text, false): Describe the intensity level you are comfortable with during training (e.g., light, moderate, or challenging).
14. **Additional Trainee Notes** (`additional_trainee_notes`, text, false): Share any additional details about your trainee experience or preferences.
15. **Additional Trainer Information** (`additional_trainer_information`, text, false): If you have any additional information about your trainer or training preferences, please provide it here.
16. **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, true): Choose your agreement type (e.g., Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
17. **Agreement Note** (`agreement_note`, text, false): If you have any additional comments about your agreement, please describe them here.
18. **Healthy Weight** (`healthy_weight`, text, false): Record your healthy weight.
19. **Unhealthy Weight** (`unhealthy_weight`, text, false): Record your unhealthy weight.
20. **Height** (`height`, text, false): Record your height.
21. **Body Fat Percentage** (`body_fat_percentage`, text, false): Record your body fat percentage.
22. **Training Goals Note** (`training_goals_note`, text, false): Share any additional details about your training goals.
23. **Diet and Nutrition Note** (`diet_and_nutrition_note`, text, false): Describe any specific diet and nutrition requirements or concerns.
24. **Medical History Note** (`medical_history_note`, text, false): Share any additional information about your medical history.
25. **Medical History Other** (`medical_history_other`, text, false): If you have any other medical history information, please provide it here.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out this form to ensure a safe and effective training experience.
* Review each section carefully and provide as much detail as possible.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to your trainer.

Please note that this form is designed to be filled out by the client/trainee.
