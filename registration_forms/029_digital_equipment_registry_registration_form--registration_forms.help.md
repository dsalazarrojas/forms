# digital_equipment_registry_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The digital equipment registry registration form is used to collect information about digital equipment to track and manage its details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the necessary fields with the correct information about the digital equipment.
2. Ensure you provide accurate details to avoid errors.
3. Review your submission before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form** (`Registration Form`, text, optional): This field is the primary form section where you can enter any additional information about the equipment.
- **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person or organization responsible for the equipment.
- **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the person or organization responsible for the equipment.
- **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the person or organization responsible for the equipment.
- **Contact Company** (`Contact Company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the company associated with the equipment.
- **Equipment Type** (`Equipment Type`, select one, optional): Choose the type of equipment (e.g., computer, tablet, smartphone).
- **Serial Number** (`Serial Number`, text, optional): Enter the serial number of the equipment (if available).
- **Purchase Date** (`Purchase Date`, date, optional): Enter the date the equipment was purchased.
- **Purchase Price** (`Purchase Price`, number, optional): Enter the price of the equipment (if available).
- **Status** (`Status`, select multiple, optional): Choose the status of the equipment (e.g., Active, Inactive).
- **Notes** (`Notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the equipment.
- **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, select one, optional): Choose whether the equipment is assigned to a tool (e.g., Yes, No).

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid errors.
* Double-check the information entered, especially for contact details, to ensure accuracy.
* Review your submission before submitting it to avoid any potential issues.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the relevant authorities for clarification.
