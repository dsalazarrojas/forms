# hybrid_classroom_technology_testing_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from participants who are interested in testing and demonstrating various technologies in a hybrid classroom setting.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your details.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you provide all the necessary information.
3. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.
