# Workplace Innovation Roundtable Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register individuals for the Workplace Innovation Roundtable. It collects necessary contact information and work-related details to facilitate communication and participation in the roundtable.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Provide your **Email** address in the third field, ensuring it's a valid email format.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the fourth field.
5. Fill in your **Organization** name in the fifth field.
6. Enter your **Department** in the sixth field.
7. Enter your **Job Title** in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as you would like to be addressed.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as you would like to be addressed.
* **Email**: A valid email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone**: A 10-digit phone number (area code and phone number) where you can be reached for calls or messages.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of your current or previous workplace.
* **Department**: Enter the name of your current or previous department.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or previous job title.

## Tips

* Double-check your registration information for accuracy before submission.
* Make sure to use a valid email and phone number for communication.
* If you're unable to complete the form, please contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
* You will receive a confirmation email after submission to verify your registration.
