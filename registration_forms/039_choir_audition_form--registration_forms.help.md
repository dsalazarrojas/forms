# Choir Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential applicants for a choir audition.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your personal details such as name, date of birth, and contact information.
2. Answer questions about your singing background, including the voice part you're interested in, your level of proficiency, and any previous experience.
3. Choose your preferred audition date and time.
4. Specify your availability for rehearsals and performances.
5. Confirm your understanding of the commitment required to be part of the choir.
6. Sign the application form to confirm your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 2): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Last Name** (id: 3): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents. 
* **Date of Birth** (id: 4): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
* **Age** (id: 5): Enter your age in years.
* **Gender** (id: 6): Select your gender identity.
* **Phone Number** (id: 7): Enter your phone number where we can contact you. 
* **Email Address** (id: 8): Enter your email address for communication. 
* **Address** (id: 9): Enter your street, city, and state for our records.
* **School or Organization** (id: 10): If applicable, enter the name of your school or organization.
* **Grade Level** (id: 11): If applicable, enter your grade level.
* **Vocal Information** (id: 12): Briefly describe your singing background and experience.
* **Preferred Voice Part** (id: 13): Select the vocal part you're interested in singing (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, Mezzo-soprano, Baritone, Not sure).
* **Vocal Range** (id: 14): Enter your vocal range (e.g., C3 to C6).
* **Years Singing** (id: 15): Enter the number of years you've been singing.
* **Previous Choir Experience** (id: 16): Select your level of previous choir experience (None, 1-2 years, 3-5 years, 6-10 years, More than 10 years).
* **Previous Choirs** (id: 17): Enter the names and dates of any previous choirs you've been part of.
* **Can Read Music** (id: 18): Confirm your ability to read sheet music (Yes, fluently, Yes, basically, A little, False).
* **Have Performed Solo** (id: 19): Confirm if you've performed solo before (Yes, frequently, Yes, a few times, Yes, once, False).
* **Musical Training** (id: 20): Briefly describe your musical training or education.
* **Play Instruments** (id: 21): Confirm if you play any musical instruments (True, False).
* **List Instruments** (id: 22): If yes, list the instruments you play.
* **Audition Preferences** (id: 23): Enter any specific requests for your audition, such as preferred date and time.
* **Preferred Audition Date** (id: 24): Select your preferred audition date. 
* **Preferred Audition Time** (id: 25): Select your preferred audition time. 
* **Alternate Dates** (id: 26): Enter any alternative audition dates you'd prefer.
* **What Piece to Perform** (id: 27): Enter the song title and composer you'd like to perform.
* **Language of Piece** (id: 28): Enter the language of the song you'd like to perform.
* **Need Accompianist** (id: 29): Confirm if you need an accompanist for your audition (True, No, I will bring one, No, I will sing a cappella, No, I have backing track).
* **Bring Backing Track** (id: 30): Confirm if you'll provide a backing track (True, False).
* **Availability** (id: 31): Briefly enter your availability for rehearsals and performances.
* **Available Days** (id: 32): Select all the days you're available for rehearsals. 
* **Available Times** (id: 33): Select all the times you're available for rehearsals. 
* **Schedule Conflicts** (id: 34): Enter any known schedule conflicts.
* **Commitment** (id: 35): Enter your level of commitment to the choir.
* **Understand Commitment** (id: 36): Confirm your understanding of the choir's commitment requirements (Yes, completely, Yes, mostly, Somewhat, No, need more information).
* **Willing to Practice** (id: 37): Confirm your willingness to practice independently (Yes, definitely, Yes, somewhat, Maybe, False).
* **Performance Attendance** (id: 38): Confirm your ability to attend all scheduled performances (True, No, have some conflicts, Not sure).
* **Additional Skills** (id: 39): Enter any other talents or skills you have.
* **Other Skills** (id: 40): Enter any other relevant skills you have.
* **Why Join Choir** (id: 41): Enter your reasons for joining the choir.
* **Musical Goals** (id: 42): Enter your musical goals.
* **How Heard About Us** (id: 43): Select how you heard about the choir (Friend or family, School, Social media, Advertisement, Previous performance, Website, Other).
* **Additional Comments** (id: 44): Enter any additional comments or questions.
* **Application Date** (id: 46): Enter the date you're submitting this application. 

Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly. Incomplete or false information may affect the outcome of your application.
