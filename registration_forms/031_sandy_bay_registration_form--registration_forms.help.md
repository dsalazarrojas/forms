# Sandy Bay Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sandy Bay Registration Form is a user registration form used to enroll participants in events held at Sandy Bay. It gathers essential information such as names, contact details, and event preferences to facilitate efficient event management.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure you receive the correct event information and permissions.
2. Be aware that some fields are required to be filled, and you cannot proceed without providing that information.
3. Select the correct event and shift from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (1, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Participant Name** (4, text, optional): If you are registering on behalf of someone else, enter their first name.
* **Participant Email** (5, email, required): Enter the email address of the participant, if applicable.
* **Event** (6, select_one, required): Select the specific event you wish to register for from the available options.
* **Shift** (7, select_multiple, optional): Choose the shift(s) you would like to attend. You can select one or multiple shifts, depending on your availability.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Add any relevant notes or comments about your registration, such as specific requirements or needs.

## Tips
- Double-check that you have filled out all required fields before submission.
- Be precise when selecting your event and shift to ensure you receive the correct event details.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide or contact our support team for assistance.
