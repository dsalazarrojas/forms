# Animal Welfare Tech Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for an Animal Welfare Tech Demo event. The demo aims to showcase technology solutions for animal welfare organizations, providing a platform for attendees to explore and learn from the showcased technologies.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide details about your organization, such as its name and type.
3. Specify your preferred demo date and format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
4. Select your preferred demo time slots.
5. Indicate the technologies you are interested in and currently using.
6. Describe your main technology challenges and what success would look like for your organization.
7. Indicate whether you are a decision maker for technology purchases and your role in this process.
8. Specify the size of your organization and your technology budget range.
9. Consent to being contacted with follow-up information and receive event details and reminders.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where we can send demo details and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for event notifications.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of animal organization you represent (e.g., Animal Shelter, Veterinary Clinic, etc.).
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Describe your position or role in your organization.
* **Preferred Demo Date** (`demo_date`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose a date for the demo (Monday - 10:00 AM, Tuesday - 2:00 PM, etc.).
* **Preferred Demo Format** (`demo_format`, `select_one`, required): Select how you would like to attend the demo (In-Person at Facility, Virtual via Zoom, Hybrid Option, or No preference).
* **City** (`location_city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (`location_state`, `text`, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Technologies of Interest** (`tech_interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the technologies you are interested in (e.g., Data Management System, Animal Record Database, etc.).
* **Currently Using** (`current_tech`, `text`, optional): Describe the technologies you are currently using.
* **Main Challenges** (`main_challenges`, `text`, optional): Describe your main technology challenges.
* **Success Goals** (`success_goals`, `text`, optional): Describe what success would look like for your organization.
* **Decision Maker** (`decision_maker`, `select_one`, optional): Indicate if you are a decision maker for technology purchases and your role in this process.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, optional): Approximate the number of staff in your organization.
* **Technology Budget Range** (`budget_range`, `select_one`, optional): Specify your annual technology budget range.
* **Agreements** (`agreement_header`, `note`, required): This is a note header, no specific instructions apply.
* **Consent to Contact** (`contact_consent`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you consent to be contacted with follow-up information.
* **Event Details and Reminders** (`event_details_email`, `select_one`, required): Select if you want to receive event details and reminders.
