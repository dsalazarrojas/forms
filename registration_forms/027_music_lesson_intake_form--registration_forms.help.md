# music_lesson_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students interested in taking music lessons. The data collected will be used by the music instructor to tailor the lessons according to the student's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "new_student" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
4. Select the type of music lesson you are interested in (e.g., Music Theory, Voice, Piano, etc.) in the "music_lesson_type" field.
5. Indicate the frequency of your lessons (e.g., once a week, twice a week, etc.) by selecting multiple options in the "lesson_frequency" field.
6. If you have a preferred lesson duration, specify it in the "lesson_duration" field. Note that this field is optional.
7. Choose the start and end dates for your lessons (if any) in the "start_date" and "end_date" fields, respectively. These fields are optional.
8. Enter the name of your instructor in the "instructor_name" field. This is optional but helps the instructor to know whom to contact.
9. Provide any additional information about yourself as a student in the "student_info" field. This is also optional.
10. Finally, include any notes or comments about your lessons in the "lesson_note" field. This is also optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **new_student** (`text`, required): Enter your name as a new student.
* **student_name** (`text`, optional): Provide your name if you wish to be identified by your name.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication with the instructor.
* **phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **music_lesson_type** (`select_one`, optional): Choose the type of music lesson you are interested in (e.g., Music Theory, Voice, Piano, etc.).
* **lesson_frequency** (`select_multiple`, optional): Indicate how often you would like to take lessons (e.g., once a week, twice a week, etc.).
* **lesson_duration** (`number`, optional): Specify the duration of your lessons in minutes.
* **start_date** (`date`, optional): Choose the start date for your lessons.
* **end_date** (`date`, optional): Choose the end date for your lessons.
* **instructor_name** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of your instructor.
* **student_info** (`text`, optional): Provide any additional information about yourself as a student.
* **lesson_note** (`text`, optional): Include any notes or comments about your lessons.
