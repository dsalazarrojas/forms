# Charity Membership Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed for existing charity members to renew their membership for the upcoming year. It ensures that we have up-to-date information about you and confirm your membership details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form carefully and thoroughly.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out before proceeding.
3. If you have any questions or need help, refer to this guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`member_first_name`, text, **required**): Your first name
* **Last Name** (`member_last_name`, text, **required**): Your last name
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, **required**): Contact email
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, **required**): Contact number
* **Member ID Number** (`member_id`, text, **optional**): If you have one
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, **required**): Choose from Individual member, Family membership, Student membership, Senior membership, Corporate member, or Lifetime member
* **Renewal Year** (`membership_year`, select_one, **required**): Select from the provided renewal years
* **Renewal Fee** (`renewal_fee`, select_one, **required**): Choose from $50 - Individual, $75 - Family, $25 - Student, $35 - Senior, $250 - Corporate, or $1000 - Lifetime
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, **required**): Choose from Credit card, Debit card, PayPal, Bank transfer, or Check
* **Additional Donation** (`additional_donation`, select_one, **optional**): Select from No donation, $25, $50, $100, $250, or Other amount
* **Has Your Contact Information Changed** (`contact_update`, select_one, **required**): Choose from No same as before or Yes needs update
* **New Mailing Address** (`new_address`, text, **optional**): If your contact information has changed, enter your new address
* **Communication Preference** (`communication_preference`, select_one, **required**): Choose from Email only, Mail only, Both email and mail, or Phone calls
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, **optional**): Choose from Yes definitely, Maybe, or False
* **Committee Interest** (`committee_interest`, select_multiple, **optional**): Select from Membership committee, Events committee, Fundraising committee, Communications committee, Advocacy committee, or Not interested
* **Continue Newsletter Subscription** (`newsletter_subscription`, select_one, **required**): Choose from Yes continue or No unsubscribe
* **How Involved Do You Want to Be** (`how_involved`, select_one, **required**): Choose from Very active, Moderately active, Receive updates only, or Minimal contact
* **Feedback on Your Membership** (`feedback`, text, **optional**): Share your thoughts on your membership experience
* **How Did You First Hear About Us** (`referral_source`, select_one, **optional**): Choose from Friend or family, Social media, Website, Event, Print media, or Other
* **Agree to Membership Terms** (`terms_agreement`, select_one, **required**): Choose from Yes I agree or No I do not agree
