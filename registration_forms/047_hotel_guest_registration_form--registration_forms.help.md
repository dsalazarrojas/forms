# Hotel Guest Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Registration Form is a standard form used by hotel staff to collect information from guests upon arrival. This form helps ensure accurate and efficient processing of guest data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information (if any) and provide any additional details that may be relevant to your stay.
2. Review and confirm the information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Registration** (id: 1)
	+ A text field for you to describe your guest information.
* **Arrival Date** (id: 2)
	+ Select your arrival date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Departure Date** (id: 3)
	+ Select your departure date in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Name** (id: 4)
	+ Enter your name.
* **Email** (id: 5)
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (id: 6)
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (id: 7)
	+ Enter your address.
* **Reservation Details** (id: 8)
	+ Provide any relevant details about your reservation.
* **Special Requests** (id: 9)
	+ Enter any special requests you may have.
* **Payment Method** (id: 10)
	+ Choose your preferred payment method (cash, credit card, or other).
* **Payment Amount** (id: 11)
	+ Enter the payment amount.
* **Guest Type** (id: 12)
	+ Choose whether you are traveling as an individual or a group.
* **Room Number** (id: 13)
	+ Enter your assigned room number.
* **Room Type** (id: 14)
	+ Choose the type of room you're staying in (single, double, or suite).
* **Special Requests 2** (id: 15)
	+ Enter any additional special requests.
* **Arrival Time** (id: 16)
	+ Select your expected arrival time.
* **Stay Duration** (id: 17)
	+ Enter the duration of your stay.
* **Guest Comment** (id: 18)
	+ Add any additional comments about your stay.

## Tips

* Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to ask hotel staff for assistance.
