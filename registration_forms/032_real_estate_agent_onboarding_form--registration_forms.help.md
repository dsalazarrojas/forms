# Real Estate Agent Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to onboard real estate agents onto our platform. It collects essential information about the agent, including contact details, agency information, and skills and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Real Estate Agent Details" section with your agency's name and address.
2. Enter your first and last name.
3. Provide your email address and phone number.
4. Select your agency's skills and certifications level (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
5. Choose whether you accept or decline our terms and conditions.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Real Estate Agent Details** (`real_estate_agent_details`, text, required): This field is used to enter your agency's name and address. Please enter the name of your agency as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address that we will use to contact you. Please ensure that this email address is up-to-date and correct.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number that we will use to contact you.
* **Agency Name** (`agency_name`, text, required): Enter your agency's name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Agency Address** (`agency_address`, text, required): Enter your agency's address as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* **Agency Website** (`agency_website`, text, optional): Enter your agency's website URL if you have one.
* **Skills and Certifications** (`skills_and_certifications`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of your skills and certifications (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Choose whether you accept or decline our terms and conditions.
