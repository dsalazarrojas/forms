# Mental Health Innovation Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mental Health Innovation Lab Registration Form is designed to collect information for project registration and proposal purposes. It will be used to evaluate and potentially support projects that align with the lab's objectives.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant Name in the "Participant Name" field.
2. Enter your Email and Phone number in the corresponding fields.
3. Provide your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Enter the Lab Name, Project Description, and Project Lead in the respective fields.
5. Select the Funding Source for your project from the dropdown options.
6. Describe your Research Question in the "Research Question" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Text): Enter your name as it should be displayed in the evaluation process.
* **Email** (Email): Provide your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **Lab Name** (Text): Enter the name of the lab or organization you represent.
* **Project Description** (Text): Briefly describe your project, including its objectives, methods, and expected outcomes.
* **Project Lead** (Text): Enter the name of the person leading the project.
* **Project Start Date** (Date): Enter the expected start date of your project.
* **Project End Date** (Date): Enter the expected end date of your project.
* **Funding Source** (Select One): Choose from the dropdown options to indicate the source of funding for your project.
* **Research Question** (Text): Describe the research question your project aims to address.

Note: All fields marked as "Required" are mandatory, while others are optional. Make sure to provide accurate and clear information to facilitate the evaluation process.
