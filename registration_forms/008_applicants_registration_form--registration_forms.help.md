# Applicants Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect the necessary information from applicants for the registration process. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Registration details --" button to proceed to the first section of the form.
2.  In the first section, you will be asked for your registration information. This section serves as an introduction to the form.
3.  Fill out the form fields as required and as much as possible. You can move on to the next section after you've completed this one.
4.  Repeat this process until you've filled out all sections of the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name (First and last name) in this field.
*   **Contact email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your primary email address for us to communicate with you.
*   **Phone number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your mobile or landline number. If you have both, please enter both separated by commas.
*   **Desired course or program** (`desired_course`, text, required): Choose your preferred course or program.
*   **Prior education** (`prior_education`, text, optional): Please enter your highest level of education completed.
*   **Registration date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted this registration form.
*   **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Name and phone number of the person to contact in emergency situations.
*   **Special requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any accessibility or dietary needs, please let us know.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your contact email and phone number for accuracy.
*   Enter the correct desired course or program to ensure you get the right information about the program.
*   If you need to provide special requirements, please be as detailed as possible to ensure we can provide the right support.
