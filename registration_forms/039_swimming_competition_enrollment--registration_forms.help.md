# Swimming Competition Enrollment - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering participants for a swimming competition. Please fill out the form with your information to sign up for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your First Name in the first field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the second field.
3. Enter your School Name, if applicable.
4. Provide your Contact Email in the third field.
5. Enter your Contact Phone number in the fourth field.
6. Enter the name of the swimming event you want to participate in.
7. Choose a date for the event.
8. Select the start time for the event.
9. Enter the end time for the event.
10. Enter the location of the event.
11. Select the type of competition (e.g., Swimming Competition).
12. Enter the number of participants in your group.
13. Enter a brief description of the event.
14. Enter any specific rules for the event.
15. Enter the contact person's name.
16. Enter the contact person's phone number.
17. Enter the contact person's email.
18. Enter the event website URL, if applicable.
19. Enter the event's Facebook page URL, if applicable.
20. Enter the event's Twitter handle, if applicable.
21. Enter the event's Instagram handle, if applicable.
22. Enter the event's LinkedIn page URL, if applicable.
23. Enter the event's YouTube channel URL, if applicable.
24. Enter any other relevant details for the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification document.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification document.
* **School Name** (`School Name`, `text`, optional): If you are a student or team, enter your school's name.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Event Name** (`Event Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the swimming competition you want to participate in.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required): Select the start time of the event.
* **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required): Select the end time of the event.
* **Location** (`Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of the event.
* **Type of Competition** (`Type of Competition`, `text`, required): Select the type of competition (e.g., Swimming Competition).
* **Number of Participants** (`Number of Participants`, `number`, required): Enter the number of participants in your group.
* **Event Description** (`Event Description`, `text`, required): Provide a brief description of the event.
* **Rules** (`Rules`, `text`, required): Enter any specific rules for the event.
* **Contact Person** (`Contact Person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Event Website** (`Event Website`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the event website, if applicable.
* **Event Facebook** (`Event Facebook`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the event's Facebook page, if applicable.
* **Event Twitter** (`Event Twitter`, `text`, optional): Enter the event's Twitter handle, if applicable.
* **Event Instagram** (`Event Instagram`, `text`, optional): Enter the event's Instagram handle, if applicable.
* **Event LinkedIn** (`Event LinkedIn`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the event's LinkedIn page, if applicable.
* **Event YouTube** (`Event YouTube`, `text`, optional): Enter the URL of the event's YouTube channel, if applicable.
* **Other** (`Other`, `text`, optional): Enter any other relevant details for the event.
* **Submit** (`Select One`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of participant you are (Swimming Competition or Other). 

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers.
* You will receive a confirmation email once your registration is successful.
