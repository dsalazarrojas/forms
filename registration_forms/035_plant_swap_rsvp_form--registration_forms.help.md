# plant_swap_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture information from participants about a plant swap event. It is designed to gather details about plants being swapped, participant information, and RSVP details in a clear and organized manner.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the plant swap details, including any relevant information about the plants being exchanged.
2. Fill out the participant details section with your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
3. Enter your RSVP details, including any comments or preferences you may have for the plant swap.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Plant Swap Details** (plant_swap_details, text, required): This field is for you to provide any relevant information about the plants being exchanged. Please enter a brief description of the plants, their quantity, and any other notable details.
* **Participant Details** (participant_details, text, optional): This field is for you to provide your contact information and any other details about yourself. Please include your name, email address, phone number, and any other relevant contact details.
* **RSVP Details** (rsvp_details, text, required): This field is for you to enter any comments or preferences you may have for the plant swap. Please provide your availability, any allergies or dislikes, and any other relevant information to help us prepare for the swap.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in the "Plant Swap Details" section to ensure accurate information is captured.
* If you have multiple participants or groups involved, please list each one separately in the "Participant Details" section.
* Use clear and concise language when entering your "RSVP Details" to help us efficiently plan the plant swap.
