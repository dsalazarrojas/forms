# PR Session Attendee Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for registering attendees for a Public Relations (PR) session. The form will help you provide your details and preferences for the event. Please ensure you fill in the correct information as it will be used for communication and event organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Ensure you have an active internet connection.
- Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
- Review the form for any errors before submission.
- Once submitted, a confirmation will be sent to the provided email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Type (1)** (`Event`, `Training`, `Media Briefing`, `Other`): Select the type of workshop you are interested in attending. This will help us organize the event according to your preferences.
* **Participant Name (2)** (`text`): Enter your name as it appears on your business card. Please use your full name and correct title.
* **Email (3)** (`email`): Enter your work email address where you can be reached for communication and event updates.
* **Company (4)** (`text`): If applicable, enter your company or organization name to help us track attendees from your organization.
* **Role (5)** (`text`): Describe your role within your organization, e.g., PR Manager, Marketing Manager, etc.
* **Interests (6)** (`PR, Marketing, Communications, Event Planning, Media Relations, Training and Development, Other`): Select the areas you are interested in attending, as this will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **How do you want to be communicated with? (7)** (`Email, SMS, Phone, Email and Phone`): Choose your preferred method of communication to ensure we reach you effectively.
* **Workshop Date (8)** (`date`): Enter the date of the workshop or event. If you are unsure, please check the event details for the correct date.
* **Workshop Time (9)** (`time`): Enter the time of the workshop or event, if known. If not, please leave it blank.
* **Additional Comments (10)** (`note`): If you have any further comments or requirements, please add them here. This will help us tailor the event to your needs.

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately to ensure efficient event organization.
- Review the form carefully before submitting it to avoid errors.
- Once submitted, be sure to check your email for confirmation and any further instructions.
