# Front Desk Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Front Desk Inquiry Form is used to gather information from guests for front desk inquiries. This form is designed to be completed by guests who need assistance or have questions regarding their stay or hotel services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the stay length field to indicate how long you plan to stay at the hotel.
2. Select the type of room you require from the options provided.
3. Enter the room number, if applicable.
4. Choose the arrival and departure dates of your stay.
5. Select the services you require, such as laundry, Wi-Fi, room service, or towels.
6. Indicate the type of inquiry you have, such as a general inquiry or a special request.
7. If necessary, provide special requests or additional information.
8. Enter your contact number and email address for communication purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Stay Length (Number)**: Enter the number of nights you plan to stay at the hotel.
* **Room Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of room that suits your needs:
	+ Single
	+ Double
	+ Suite
* **Room Number (Text)**: If you have a specific room number, enter it here.
* **Arrival Date (Date)**: Enter the date you plan to arrive at the hotel.
* **Departure Date (Date)**: Enter the date you plan to depart from the hotel.
* **Services Required (Select Multiple)**: Select the services you need during your stay:
	+ Laundry
	+ Wi-Fi
	+ Room Service
	+ Towels
* **Inquiry Type (Select One)**: Indicate the type of inquiry you have:
	+ General Inquiry
	+ Special Request
* **Special Requests (Text)**: If you have any special requests, provide them here.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact number for the hotel staff to contact you.
* **Contact Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address for the hotel staff to contact you.
