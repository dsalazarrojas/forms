# Packaging Lifecycle Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from organizations to validate their packaging lifecycle certifications. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure your organization's certifications are properly registered.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand its purpose and what information it requires.
2. Fill out the organization details (Page 1).
3. Select all the relevant lifecycle stages that apply to your organization (Page 2).
4. Choose any applicable certifications for your organization (Page 3).
5. Add any relevant documentation or comments (Page 4).
6. Fill out the organization contact information (Pages 5-8).
7. Submit your form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Details** (Page 1, text): Enter your organization's name and any other relevant details.
* **Lifecycle Stage** (Page 2, select multiple): Choose all the stages that apply to your organization's packaging lifecycle.
* **Certifications** (Page 3, select multiple): Select any certifications your organization has obtained.
* **Documentation** (Page 4, note): Add any supporting documentation or comments for your organization's certifications.
* **Organization Contact** (Pages 5-6, text): Enter your organization's contact information, including name and address.
* **Contact Name** (Page 7, text): Enter the name of the contact person responsible for this registration.
* **Contact Email** (Page 7, email): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (Page 8, text): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Address** (Page 8, text): Enter the address of the contact person.
* **Submit** (Page 10, note): Review and submit your form for registration.
