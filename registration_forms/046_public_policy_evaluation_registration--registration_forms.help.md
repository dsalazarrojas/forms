# prophets - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide is designed to help you complete the "prophets" form. The form is used for registration purposes and is meant for registering participants, non-participants, or other relevant categories. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant details.
3. Review your answers before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Registration

* **Registration** (`Registration`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your registration purpose.
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address. 
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date** (`Date`, `date`, required): Select the date of your registration.
* **Time** (`Time`, `time`, required): Select the time of your registration.
* **Participant Type** (`Participant Type`, `select_one`, required): Choose whether you are a participant or non-participant. 
* **Organization** (`Organization`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Affiliation** (`Affiliation`, `text`, required): Enter your affiliation with the organization.

Note: All required fields are mandatory and must be filled in for you to successfully submit the form. 

## Tips

* Be accurate and truthful when filling in your personal details. 
* If you need help or encounter any issues while filling the form, reach out for assistance. 

This guide has been designed to provide clear and concise instructions for completing the "prophets" form. If you have any further queries, feel free to reach out.
