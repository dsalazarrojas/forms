# Fundraising Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fundraising Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from potential volunteers for fundraising events. This form will be used to gather essential details about the volunteer, including their personal information, availability, and emergency contacts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your Contact Number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your Shift Start Time in the "Shift Start Time" field.
6. Enter your Shift End Time in the "Shift End Time" field.
7. Enter the Event Name in the "Event Name" field.
8. Choose your Volunteer Position (Volunteer or Non-Volunteer) in the "Volunteer Position" field.
9. Select your Volunteer Status (Active, Inactive, or Pending) in the "Volunteer Status" field.
10. Choose your availability for shifts in the "Volunteer Shifts" field.
11. Enter your Date of Birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
12. Describe your Volunteer Experience in the "Volunteer Experience" field.
13. List your Volunteer Skills in the "Volunteer Skills" field.
14. Enter details about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
15. Provide any necessary emergency contact notes.
16. Read and agree to the Volunteer Agreement and Volunteer Terms and Conditions.
17. Optionally, provide a volunteer reference and any information about your volunteer vehicles.
17. Enter any additional notes about your volunteer status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Email): Enter your email address here.
* **Contact Number** (Text): Provide your contact number here.
* **Shift Start Time** (Time): Enter your shift start time.
* **Shift End Time** (Time): Enter your shift end time.
* **Event Name** (Text): Enter the name of the event here.
* **Volunteer Position** (Select one): Choose whether you are a Volunteer or Non-Volunteer.
* **Volunteer Status** (Select multiple): Select your status as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Volunteer Shifts** (Select multiple): Choose your availability for shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Any).
* **Date of Birth** (Date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Volunteer Experience** (Text): Describe your volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer Skills** (Text): List your volunteer skills.
* **Emergency Contact** (Text): Enter details about your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Text): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text): Provide the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Volunteer Agreement** (Text): Read and agree to the volunteer agreement.
* **Volunteer Terms and Conditions** (Text): Read and agree to the volunteer terms and conditions.
* **Volunteer Reference** (Text): Optionally, provide a volunteer reference.
* **Volunteer Vehicles** (Text): Optionally, provide any information about your volunteer vehicles.
* **Volunteer Notes** (Text): Enter any additional notes about your volunteer status.
* **Volunteer Signature** (Text): Sign this form to confirm your agreement.

Note: Please ensure that you complete all required fields to ensure your volunteer application is successful.
