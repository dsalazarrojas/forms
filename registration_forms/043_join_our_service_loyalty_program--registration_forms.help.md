# join prophets loyalty program - Help Guide
## Purpose
The join prophets loyalty program form is designed to collect information from customers about their experiences and preferences related to the Prophets Loyalty Program. This form is for customers who want to participate in, or have questions about, the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form information, if required.
2. Provide your customer details, if required.
3. Choose whether you are using a service provider (Yes/No).
4. If you are using a service provider, provide additional information about your service provider.
5. Enter any customer information.
6. Enter any loyalty details.
7. Enter any customer preferences.
8. Review your submission and click "Submit" to send it in.
