# Hedging Match Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hedging Match Entry Form is a form used to record information about hedging matches. It is a crucial form that helps in keeping track of the details of matches, including the type of hedge, start and end dates, start and end times, match type, match timing, risk taker, team name, email, phone number, team size, hedged amount, team type, risk free, risk-free notes, hedge type, underlying asset type, entry status, status notes, created at, and updated at.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Hedging Match Entry Form, follow these steps:

1.  **Select the hedge type**: Choose the type of hedge from the options provided (Hedge Fund, Other, or Unknown).
2.  **Enter the underlying asset**: Provide the name of the underlying asset related to the hedging match.
3.  **Select the underlying asset type**: Enter the type of underlying asset (e.g., stock, bond, etc.).
4.  **Enter the start date**: Provide the start date of the hedging match.
5.  **Enter the end date**: Provide the end date of the hedging match.
6.  **Select the start time**: Choose the start time of the hedging match.
7.  **Select the end time**: Choose the end time of the hedging match.
8.  **Select the match type**: Choose the type of match (Match, Competition, or Training).
9.  **Select the match timing**: Choose the timing of the match (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
10. **Enter the risk taker**: Provide the name of the risk taker.
11.  **Enter the team name**: Provide the name of the team involved in the hedging match.
12.  **Enter email**: Provide the email of the team or risk taker.
13.  **Enter phone number**: Provide the phone number of the team or risk taker.
14.  **Select team size**: Enter the number of people in the team.
15.  **Enter hedged amount**: Enter the amount of hedged asset.
16.  **Select team type**: Choose the type of team (Individual, Team, or Unknown).
17.  **Select risk free**: Choose the level of risk-free (Low, Medium, or High).
18.  **Enter risk-free notes**: Provide notes about the risk-free level.
19.  **Select hedge type**: Choose the type of hedge (Short, Long, or Short and Long).
20.  **Select underlying asset type**: Enter the type of underlying asset.
21.  **Select entry status**: Choose the status of the entry (Active, Pending, or Inactive).
22.  **Enter status notes**: Provide notes about the status.
23.  **Enter created at**: Provide the date the entry was created.
24.  **Enter updated at**: Provide the date the entry was last updated.
