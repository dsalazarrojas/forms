# ATC Realtor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for realtors to register for the Debt to Wealth program. It helps us understand your background, experience, and goals as a realtor and ensures you understand the program's policies and requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by reading the form carefully and ensuring you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out the required fields:
	* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
	* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
	* **Real Estate License Number**: Enter your valid real estate license number.
	* **Real Estate Company**: Enter the name of your real estate brokerage.
	* **Business Phone Number**: Enter your business contact number.
	* **Business Email Address**: Enter your professional email address.
	* **Business Address**: Enter your office address.
	* **City**: Enter your office city.
	* **State**: Enter your office state.
	* **ZIP Code**: Enter your office postal code.
	* **Years of Experience in Real Estate**: Enter the number of years you've been in real estate.
	* **Specialization Area**: Select your primary area of expertise.
	* **Average Annual Sales Volume**: Select your typical annual sales volume range.
	* **Approximate Client Base Size**: Select the size of your current client base.
	* **How Familiar are you with Debt-to-Wealth Strategies**: Rate your current knowledge level.
	* **Program Goals**: Select all the goals you have for joining this program.
	* **Marketing Channels**: Select your current marketing methods.
	* **Continuing Education Hours**: Select the range of CE hours you typically complete annually.
	* **Availability for Events**: Select when you're most available to participate.
	* **Referral Source**: Select how you learned about the Debt to Wealth program.
	* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm your agreement with our policies.
3. For the optional fields:
	* **Marketing Channels**: You can select all the marketing methods you currently use.
	* **Continuing Education Hours**: You can select the range of CE hours you typically complete annually.
	* **Other Program Goals**: You can select additional goals you have for joining this program.
