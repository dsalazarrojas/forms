# Historical Tour Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Historical Tour Attendance Form is designed to collect information about past tours, focusing on attendance, headcounts, and accessibility-related data. This form will help tour managers track and analyze tour attendance, identify trends, and improve future tour planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the tour date and start and end times for the tour.
2. Add the tour start and end locations.
3. Enter the number of attendees.
4. Indicate if the tour was attended or not.
5. Specify the headcount status.
6. Provide any accessibility requests.
7. Enter the contact information (email, phone, name) and any messages.
8. Add any registration status and type.
9. Note any accessibility types.
10. Specify the registration status and type.
