# Art Discovery Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Discovery Event Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees to understand their background, interests, and needs for an upcoming Art Discovery event. This information will help event planners and organizers tailor the event to cater to the attendees' requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the Attendee Information section, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your age group from the provided options.
3. In the Event Details section, provide the event name, type, date, and location.
4. In the Art Interests section, select your level of interest in art and the types of art forms you are interested in.
5. Answer questions about your previous events attendance and accessibility needs.
6. Provide any additional information or questions you may have in the Additional Information or Questions section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (id: 2): Please enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Email Address** (id: 3): Enter your valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4): Enter your phone number for any follow-up communication.
* **Age Group** (id: 5): Select your age group from the provided options.
* **Event Name** (id: 7): Enter the name of the event you will be attending.
* **Type of Event** (id: 8): Select the type of event from the provided options.
* **Event Date** (id: 9): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Location** (id: 11): Enter the location of the event.
* **Art Interest Level** (id: 13): Select your level of interest in art from the provided options.
* **Art Forms You are Interested In** (id: 14): Select all the art forms you are interested in from the provided options.
* **Have you attended previous Art Discovery events** (id: 15): Select your previous events attendance status.
* **Do you have accessibility needs** (id: 17): Select whether you have any accessibility needs.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (id: 19): If you have any dietary restrictions, please enter them here.
* **Would you like to receive future event announcements** (id: 21): Select whether you would like to receive future event announcements.
* **How did you hear about this event** (id: 22): Enter how you heard about this event.
* **Additional Information or Questions** (id: 23): If you have any additional information or questions, please enter them here.
