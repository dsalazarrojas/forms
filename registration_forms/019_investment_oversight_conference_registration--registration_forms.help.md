# Investment Oversight Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Investment Oversight Conference Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees and stakeholders who want to participate in the Investment Oversight Conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your details as an attendee, delegate, or investor.
2. Select the type of participation you have in the conference (Attendee, Delegate, or Investor).
3. Provide any special requirements or requests you may have.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Enter your contact number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details as an attendee.
* **Participation Type** (`participation_type`, select_one, required): Choose how you plan to participate in the conference (Attendee, Delegate, or Investor).
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requirements or requests, such as dietary restrictions or equipment needs, please enter them here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your email address. This is where we will contact you with conference updates and information.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number, if you would like to receive calls from us about the conference.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your contact information, especially your email address.
* If you have any special requirements, please be as specific as possible so we can accommodate your needs.
* If you need assistance with anything, feel free to contact us.
