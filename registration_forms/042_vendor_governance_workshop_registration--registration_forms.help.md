# Vendor Governance Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Governance Workshop Registration form is a tool for vendors to provide information about themselves and their interests in attending the workshop. This guide will walk you through each section of the form to help you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide a valid email address to receive updates and information about the workshop.
3. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Select the type of business you represent (Company, Start up, or Individual).
5. Choose the session you would like to attend (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
6. Select a date and time for your session (Note: Date field is currently optional, but it's recommended to provide a specific date to ensure accurate registration).
7. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share about your attendance.
8. Finally, select any tools or resources you are interested in using or learning about during the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (Email): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and information about the workshop.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **What is the type of business?** (Select Multiple): Choose the type of business you represent (Company, Start up, or Individual).
* **Which session would you like to attend?** (Select Multiple): Choose the session you would like to attend (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Date** (Date): Select a date for your session (optional, but recommended to ensure accurate registration).
* **Time** (Time): Select a time for your session (optional, but recommended to ensure accurate registration).
* **Notes** (Note): Add any additional comments or notes about your attendance.
* **Tool** (Text): Select any tools or resources you are interested in using or learning about during the workshop.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that we can provide the best experience for you.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We recommend selecting a specific date and time to ensure accurate registration and to make it easier for us to coordinate your attendance.
