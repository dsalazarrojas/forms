# Field Visit Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Field Visit Permission Form is a document used to obtain permission for visitors to visit students at a school or educational institution. It gathers essential details for planning and conducting a successful field visit.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the school ID number.
2. Provide your name as a parent or guardian.
3. Enter your email address for contact.
4. Enter your child's name.
5. Enter your child's age.
6. Enter details about the visit (e.g., purpose, duration).
7. Choose a date for the visit.
8. Choose the visit time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### School ID
* **School ID** (Number, Required): Enter the ID number of the school where the visit will take place.

### Parent/Guardian Name
* **Parent-guardian-name** (Text, Optional): Provide your name as a parent or guardian to give permission for the visit.

### Contact Email
* **Contact-email** (Email, Optional): Enter your contact email address so that we can reach out to you with any updates or concerns.

### Student Name
* **Student-name** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's name.

### Student Age
* **Student-age** (Number, Optional): Enter your child's age.

### Visit Details
* **Visit-details** (Note, Optional): Enter any relevant details about the visit (e.g., purpose, duration).

### Visit Date
* **Visit-date** (Date, Optional): Choose the date for the visit.

### Visit Time
* **Visit-time** (Time, Optional): Choose the time for the visit.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check the visit details for any errors or inconsistencies.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
