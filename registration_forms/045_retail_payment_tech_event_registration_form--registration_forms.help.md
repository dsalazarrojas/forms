# Retail Payment Tech Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for retail payment tech events. It helps us gather accurate information about each attendee, ensuring a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by entering the **Event Name** (field **1**).
2. Select the **Event Date** (field **2**) using the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Fill out your **Name** (field **3**).
4. Enter your **Email** (field **4**) in the correct format (e.g., [username]@domain.com).
5. Provide your **Phone Number** (field **5**) in the international format (e.g., +1 123 456 7890).
6. Choose your preferred **Payment Method** (field **6**) from the available options:
	* Credit Card
	* PayPal
	* Bank Transfer
7. Enter the **Payment Amount** (field **7**) to be processed.
8. If applicable, add any **Payment Notes** (field **8**) for our reference.
9. Choose your **Shirt Size** (field **9**) from the provided options:
	* Small
	* Medium
	* Large
10. Select your **Vegetarian Meal Preference** (field **10**) from the available options:
	* True
	* False

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **1. Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you wish to register for.
* **2. Event Date**: Select the date you plan to attend the event.
* **3. Name**: Enter your full name as you would like to be listed on any event materials.
* **4. Email**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached for event updates and communications.
* **5. Phone**: Enter your phone number in the international format.
* **6. Payment Method**: Select the payment method you wish to use (credit, PayPal, or bank transfer).
* **7. Payment Amount**: Enter the exact amount you are paying for the event.
* **8. Payment Notes** (optional): Add any additional information or instructions about your payment.
* **9. Shirt Size**: Choose the size of shirt you will wear during the event.
* **10. Vegetarian Meal Preference**: Select whether you prefer a vegetarian meal or not.
