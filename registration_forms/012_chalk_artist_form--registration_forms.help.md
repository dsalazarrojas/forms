# Chalk Artist Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration of Chalk Artists for an event. It is intended to collect participant details and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your artist name in the "artist name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field, this will be used for event updates.
3. If you have a portfolio, provide the link in the "portfolio link" field.
4. Confirm that you have paid the registration fee in the "registration fee paid" field.
5. Indicate the art supplies you need to be provided in the "art supplies needed" field.
6. Select the booth size you need in the "booth size required" field.
7. If you have any accessibility requirements, provide a description in the "accessibility requirements" field.
8. Provide an emergency contact name and phone number in the "emergency contact" field.
9. Finally, confirm that you agree to the event's rules and policies in the "agreement to event policies" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Artist Name** (`artist name`, text, required): Enter your full legal or stage name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the email address you want to be contacted for event updates.
* **Portfolio Link** (`portfolio_link`, text, optional): If you have a portfolio, enter the link here.
* **Registration Fee Paid** (`registration_fee_paid`, select_one, required): Confirm that you have paid the registration fee. Options: Paid, Pending, Exempt.
* **Art Supplies Needed** (`art_supplies_needed`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate the art supplies you need to be provided. Options: Chalk provided, Surface provided, No supplies needed.
* **Booth Size Required** (`booth_size_required`, select_one, optional): Select the space needed for your artwork. Options: Small, Medium, Large.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`, text, optional): Provide any accommodations you need at the venue.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Agreement to Event Policies** (`agreement`, select_one, required): Confirm your acceptance of event rules and policies. Options: I agree, I do not agree.
