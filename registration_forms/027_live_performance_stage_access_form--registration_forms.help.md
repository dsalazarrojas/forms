# Live Performance Stage Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Performance Stage Access Form is used to grant access to the stage for events. This form helps event organizers to assign roles and access levels to staff and performers in a secure and standardized way.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct roles and access levels for each user.
3. Ensure that you are using your correct login credentials.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`): Please enter the name of the person responsible for organizing the event.
* **Performer** (`performer`): Please enter the name of the person performing on the stage.
* **Role** (`role`): Select the role of the user, such as Stage Manager, Sound Technician, or Light Technician.
* **Access Level** (`access_level`): Choose the access level of the user, such as High, Medium, or Low.
* **Restricted Areas** (`restricted_areas`): Select which areas the user has access to, or choose 'No' if they don't have restricted areas access.
* **Performance Start Time** (`performance_start_time`): Enter the date and time the performance starts.
* **Performance End Time** (`performance_end_time`): Enter the date and time the performance ends.
* **Performance Duration** (`performance_duration`): Enter the duration of the performance.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Venue** (`venue`): Enter the location where the event will take place.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter the email address of the user.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter the phone number of the user.
* **Assigned To** (`assigned_to`): Enter the name of the person to whom the access is assigned.
* **Assigned By** (`assigned_by`): Enter the name of the person who assigned the access.
* **Access Status** (`access_status`): Select the status of the access, such as Approved, Denied, or Pending.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any additional comments about the access.
* **Created At** (`created_at`): This field shows when the access was created.
* **Updated At** (`updated_at`): This field shows when the access was last updated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`): This field shows who created the access.
* **Updated By** (`updated_by`): This field shows who last updated the access.
* **Deleted At** (`deleted_at`): This field shows when the access was deleted.
* **Deleted By** (`deleted_by`): This field shows who deleted the access.
