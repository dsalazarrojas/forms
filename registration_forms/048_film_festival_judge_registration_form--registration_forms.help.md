# Film Festival Judge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Film Festival Judge Registration Form is designed for film festival judges to provide their personal details, preferences, and availability to the festival organizers. This data will be used to manage the judging process and communicate with the judges effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Fill in your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Write a brief bio in the "Bio" field.
6. Indicate if you are available to judge in the "Festival name" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
7. Select your role(s) in the "Role" field by choosing one or more options.
8. Choose your judging preferences in the "Judging preferences" field.
9. Select your available dates in the "Available dates" field.
10. Choose your preferred hours to work in the "Preferred hours" field.
11. Indicate if you speak more than one language in the "Language" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
12. Specify your bio length in the "Bio length" field.
13. Indicate your experience in judging in the "Experience" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
14. Choose your availability for judging in the "Availability" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".
15. Set your submission limit in the "Submission limit" field.
16. Optionally provide any additional comments in the "Additional comments" field.
17. Confirm your registration in the "Confirm" field by selecting "Yes" or "No".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Bio**: Write a brief introduction about yourself.
* **Festival name**: Indicate if you are available to judge the festival.
* **Role**: Choose your role(s) from the options provided.
* **Judging preferences**: Select your judging preferences.
* **Available dates**: Select your available dates.
* **Preferred hours**: Choose your preferred hours to work.
* **Language**: Indicate if you speak more than one language.
* **Bio length**: Specify the length of your bio.
* **Experience**: Indicate your experience in judging.
* **Availability**: Choose your availability for judging.
* **Submission limit**: Set your submission limit.
* **Submission limit hint**: Provide any additional information about your submission limit.
* **Submission limit note**: Note about your submission limit.
* **Submission limit email notification**: Choose if you want email notifications about your submission limit.
* **Submission limit SMS notification**: Choose if you want SMS notifications about your submission limit.
* **Submission limit phone call**: Choose if you want phone calls about your submission limit.
* **Additional comments**: Provide any additional comments.
* **Additional comments hint**: Provide any additional information about your comments.
* **Additional comments note**: Note about your comments.
* **Confirm**: Confirm your registration.

Note: Fields marked as required or with a note will be highlighted as such in the guide.
