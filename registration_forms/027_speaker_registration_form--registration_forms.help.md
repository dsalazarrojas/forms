# Speaker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Registration Form is designed to collect information from speakers about their personal details, talk topics, and other relevant information. This form helps event organizers understand the speakers' background, talk topics, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Speaker Information, including your name, title, and other relevant details.
2. Provide your Speaker Contact information, including your email and phone number.
3. Select your Talk Topics and describe your sessions.
4. Choose your Event Information, such as the event name and date.
5. List your Speaker Organizations and Experience.
6. Enter your Submitter Information and details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information (Speaker Information)**: Enter your personal details, including your name, title, and other relevant information.
* **Speaker Contact (Speaker Contact)**: Provide your email and phone number so we can reach you.
* **Talks and Sessions (Talks and Sessions)**: Describe your talks and sessions.
* **Talk Topics (Talk Topics)**: Select and describe your talk topics. 
*   **Talk Topics 2 (Talk Topics 2)**: Select and describe your second talk topic.
*   **Talk Topics 3 (Talk Topics 3)**: Select and describe your third talk topic.
*   **Event Information (Event Information)**: Enter the event name and date.
*   **Speaker Organizations (Speaker Organizations)**: List your speaker organizations.
*   **Speaker Experience (Speaker Experience)**: Describe your speaker experience.
*   **Submitter Information (Submitter Information)**: Enter your submitter information.
*   **Submitter Details (Submitter Details)**: Enter your submitter details.
*   **Submitter Interest (Submitter Interest)**: Enter your submitter interest.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in.
* If you're unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Please review your submission before submitting to ensure that all information is accurate.
