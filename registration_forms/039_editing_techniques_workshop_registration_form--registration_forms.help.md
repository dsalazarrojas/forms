# Editing Techniques Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather registration information for the Editing Techniques Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the corresponding fields.
2. Select your preferred workshop date from the available dates.
3. Choose your payment method (if you're paying by cash, please specify it in the "Other (please specify)" field).
4. If you have any additional information about your participation or need special accommodations, use the "Participant Additional Info" field.
5. If you're the workshop instructor or organizer, enter your contact details, including your name, email, and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it would be listed on the workshop materials and communication.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Participant EMail** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and information about the workshop.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact or other important updates.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required): Indicate your preferred time slot for the workshop.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the workshop (this might be a physical address or a specific venue).
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required): Add any special notes or requests you have about your participation.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose how you plan to attend the workshop.
	+ I am able to attend the entire workshop
	+ I am able to attend part of the workshop
	+ I am not able to attend the workshop
* **Participant Payment Method** (`participant_payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Choose how you plan to pay for the workshop.
	+ Cash
	+ Credit/Debit Card
	+ Bank Transfer
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Participant Other Payment Method** (`participant_other_payment_method`, text, optional): If you've selected "Other" as your payment method, enter the details here.
* **Participant Additional Info** (`participant_additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your participation or needs.
* **Workshop Instructor Name** (`workshop_instructor_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the instructor leading the workshop.
* **Workshop Instructor EMail** (`workshop_instructor_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of the instructor.
* **Workshop Instructor Phone** (`workshop_instructor_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the instructor.
* **Workshop Instructor Notes** (`workshop_instructor_notes`, note, required): Add any notes or information about the instructor.
