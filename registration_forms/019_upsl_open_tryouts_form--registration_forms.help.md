# upsl_open_tryouts_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The "upsl_open_tryouts_form" is designed for players to register for tryouts or leagues in sports or athletic activities. It gathers information about the player, including contact and emergency contact details, medical history, and registration preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling out the player details fields, which include general information about the player.
2. Next, provide your contact information so we can get in touch with you.
3. If you have any emergency contact, please provide their details.
4. In the medical history field, share any medical conditions or concerns we should know about.
5. In the "registration" field, select the reason for your registration.
6. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Details (1)** (`Player Details`, Text, optional): This field is for general information about you as a player.
* **Contact Information (2)** (`Contact Information`, Text, optional): Please provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Player Information 3 (3)** (`Player Information 3`, Text, optional): This field is intended for additional details about your playing experience or skills.
* **Emergency Contact Information (4)** (`Emergency Contact Information`, Text, optional): Provide the contact information for an emergency contact person.
* **Medical History (5)** (`Medical History`, Text, optional): Share any medical conditions or concerns we should know about.
* **Player Information 6 (6)** (`Player Information 6`, Text, optional): This field might be intended for additional details or notes.
* **Registration (7)** (`Registration`, Select Multiple, optional): Select the reason for your registration, such as "Registration for tryouts" or "Registration for league".
* **Registration (8)** is not a distinct page but seems to be a repeat of page 7, so we will ignore this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your registration.
* If you need help or have questions, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure you've included all required and optional fields.
