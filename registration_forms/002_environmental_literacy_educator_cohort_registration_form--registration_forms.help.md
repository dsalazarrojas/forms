# Environmental Literacy Educator Cohort Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Environmental Literacy Educator Cohort Registration Form is used to register for the Environmental Literacy Educator Cohort program. This form helps the program administrators to understand the educator's background and needs, and to provide them with the appropriate resources and support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Registration Forms" category from the dropdown menu.
2. Fill in the "Form ID" field with a unique identifier for your submission.
3. Choose your "Teaching Area" from the dropdown menu.
4. Select your "Teaching Level" from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your "Program Length" from the dropdown menu.
6. Select your "Target Audience" from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose your "Language" from the dropdown menu.
8. Select your "Teaching Language" from the dropdown menu.
9. Choose your "Submission Status" from the dropdown menu.
10. Fill in the "Program Name" field if you want to provide a name for your program.
11. Fill in the "Program Email" field if you want to provide an email address for your program.
12. Fill in the "Program Phone" field if you want to provide a phone number for your program.
13. Fill in the "Submission Date" field if you want to provide a date for your submission.
14. Fill in the "Submission Time" field if you want to provide a time for your submission.
15. Fill in the "Submission Note" fields if you want to provide additional notes for your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form ID** (`form_id`, number, optional): This field is used to identify your submission. Please fill in a unique identifier for your form.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required): Select "Registration Forms" to register for the program.
* **Teaching Area** (`teaching_area`, select_multiple, required): Choose the area you teach in from the dropdown menu.
* **Contact** (`contact`, email, optional): Please provide your email address if you want to receive updates and notifications from the program.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Please provide your phone number if you want to receive calls or messages from the program.
