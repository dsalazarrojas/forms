# VAT Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The VAT Registration Form is used to collect tax-related information from users for tax purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Please ensure that you have all necessary information before filling out the form.
2. Fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your name and other relevant personal details.
* **VAT Registration Info** (`vat_registration_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional information related to your VAT registration.
* **VAT Number** (`vat_num`, number, optional): Enter your VAT number if you have one.
* **Date** (`vat_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your VAT registration in the format 'YYYY-MM-DD'.
* **Address** (`vat_address`, text, optional): Enter your business or home address.
* **Email** (`vat_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`vat_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **IBAN** (`vat_iban`, text, optional): Enter your IBAN (International Bank Account Number).
* **Bank Name** (`vat_bank_name`, text, optional): Enter your bank's name.
* **Bank Account** (`vat_bank_account`, text, optional): Enter your bank account number.
* **Bank Address** (`vat_bank_address`, text, optional): Enter your bank's address.
* **Bank Country** (`vat_bank_country`, text, optional): Enter your bank's country.
* **Bank City** (`vat_bank_city`, text, optional): Enter your bank's city.
* **Bank Postal Code** (`vat_bank_postal_code`, text, optional): Enter your bank's postal code.
* **Bank BIC** (`vat_bank_bic`, text, optional): Enter your bank's BIC (Bank Identifier Code).
* **Bank SWF Number** (`vat_bank_swf_number`, text, optional): Enter your bank's BIC (Bank Identifier Code).
* **Reason of Tax Payer** (`vat_reason_of_tax_payer`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your VAT registration.
* **Status Tax** (`vat_status_tax`, select_one, required): Select your VAT status from the options provided.
* **Reason Of Cancellation** (`vat_reason_of_cancellation`, text, optional): Enter the reason for your VAT cancellation if applicable.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submission.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact us for assistance.
* This form is for tax purposes only, and any inaccuracies or discrepancies may lead to penalties or fines.
