# session_attendance_confirmation - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from individuals regarding their attendance at various sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Start by providing the relevant session information in the "Session" page.
2. On the "Attendance" page, select the session for which you are reporting your attendance.
3. On the "Organizer" page, enter the name of the person responsible for the session.
4. On the "Notes" page, provide any additional comments or remarks you have about the session.
5. On the "Date" page, select the date of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session (session_attendance_confirmation_page_1)
* **Session** (`title`, required/optional: not applicable): This field is the title of the session you are reporting attendance for. Please enter the name of the session as it appears on the schedule or materials.

### Attendance (session_attendance_confirmation_page_2)
* **Attendance** (`title`, required/optional: false): Select the session you attended from the options provided. Please choose the correct session to accurately report your attendance.

### Organizer (session_attendance_confirmation_page_3)
* **Organizer** (`title`, required/optional: false): Enter the name of the person in charge of the session you attended. This will help in crediting the correct person for their role.

### Notes (session_attendance_confirmation_page_4)
* **Notes** (`title`, required/optional: false): If you have any comments, remarks, or observations about the session, enter them here.

### Date (session_attendance_confirmation_page_5)
* **Date** (`title`, required/optional: false): Choose the date the session took place. Please select the correct date to ensure accurate reporting.
