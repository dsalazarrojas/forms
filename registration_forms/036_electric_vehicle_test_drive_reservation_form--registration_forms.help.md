# electric_vehicle_test_drive_reservation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The electric_vehicle_test_drive_reservation_form is used to register customers for an electric vehicle test drive. This form collects essential customer and vehicle information to facilitate a smooth test drive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Provide your contact information under "Customer Information", including your name and contact details.
2.  Specify the vehicle you are interested in test driving in the "Vehicle Information" section.
3.  Choose the language and contact method you prefer for communication about your test drive.
4.  If necessary, provide any additional details about your test drive in the "Test Drive Information" section.
5.  Select the status of your test drive from the "Test Drive Status" options.
6.  Specify how you would like to be contacted after your test drive from the "Follow Up Contact" options.
7.  Choose the outcome of your test drive from the "Test Drive Result" options.
8.  If applicable, provide your vehicle's details in the "Vehicle ID", "Vehicle Type", "Vehicle Color", and "Vehicle Year" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Customer Information (1)**: Provide your name and contact details so we can reach out to you about your test drive.
    *   **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
    *   **Phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you.
    *   **Email**: Enter your email address so we can send you updates about your test drive.
*   **Vehicle Information (2)**: Choose the electric vehicle you're interested in test driving. This will help us prepare the vehicle for your arrival.
    *   **Vehicle ID**: Enter the vehicle's ID to identify the specific vehicle you're interested in.
    *   **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of electric vehicle you're interested in (e.g., hatchback, sedan, etc.).
    *   **Vehicle Color**: Select the color of the vehicle you're interested in.
    *   **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of the vehicle you're interested in.
*   **Test Drive Information (3)**: If you have any specific requests or needs for your test drive, please mention them here.
*   **Preferred Language (4)**: Choose the language you'd like to use for communication about your test drive.
    *   **English**: Select English as your preferred language.
    *   **Spanish**: Select Spanish as your preferred language.
    *   **French**: Select French as your preferred language.
*   **Preferred Contact Method (5)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted about your test drive.
    *   **Email**: Select email as your preferred contact method.
    *   **Phone**: Select phone as your preferred contact method.
*   **Test Drive Details (6)**: If you have any additional details about your test drive, please mention them here.
*   **Notes (7)**: Enter any additional comments or information about your test drive.
*   **Customer Notes (8)**: Enter any comments or information you'd like to share with our team.
*   **Dealer Notes (9)**: Enter any comments or information you'd like to share with our dealer.
*   **Test Drive Status (10)**: Choose the status of your test drive from the options.
    *   **Pending**: Select if your test drive is pending.
    *   **Scheduled**: Select if your test drive is scheduled.
    *   **Rescheduled**: Select if your test drive has been rescheduled.
    *   **Completed**: Select if your test drive has already been completed.
*   **Follow Up Contact (11)**: Choose how you'd like to be contacted after your test drive.
    *   **Email**: Select email as your preferred contact method.
    *   **Phone**: Select phone as your preferred contact method.
*   **Test Drive Result (12)**: Choose the outcome of your test drive from the options.
    *   **Pass**: Select if your test drive was successful.
    *   **Fail**: Select if your test drive was not successful.
    *   **Pending**: Select if the outcome of your test drive is pending.
*   **Test Drive Score (13)**: Enter the score you received during your test drive.
*   **Test Drive Rating (14)**: Enter the rating you'd like to give to your test drive experience.
*   **Test Drive Comment (15)**: Enter any additional comments about your test drive experience.
*   **Test Drive Rating Comment (16)**: Enter any additional comments about your test drive experience.
*   **Customer Signature (17)**: If applicable, please sign here to confirm your agreement.
*   **Dealer Signature (18)**: If applicable, please sign here to confirm your agreement.
*   **Vehicle ID (19)**: Enter the vehicle's ID.
*   **Vehicle Type (20)**: Enter the type of vehicle you're test driving.
*   **Test Drive Location (21)**: Enter the location of your test drive.
*   **Vehicle Color (22)**: Enter the color of the vehicle you're test driving.
*   **Vehicle Year (23)**: Enter the year of the vehicle you're test driving.
*   **Test Drive Time (24)**: Enter the time of your test drive.
*   **Test Drive Date (25)**: Enter the date of your test drive.

Please review each field carefully and fill out the form correctly to ensure we can provide you with the best possible test drive experience.
