# Hospitality Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Hospitality Conference. Please provide the required information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name and contact information in the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone).
2. Select the type of event you are registering for (Conference, Workshop, or Meeting).
3. Enter the date and time of the event you are attending.
4. Choose how you confirm your email address (True or False).
5. Confirm your phone number (True or False).
6. If assigned a tool, choose the correct tool from the options provided.
7. Provide additional comments or information about your registration in the Notes field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and event updates.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number. This will be used for communication and event updates.
* **Event Type** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of event you are registering for (Conference, Workshop, or Meeting).
* **Event Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date of the event you are attending.
* **Event Time** (`time`, required): Enter the time of the event you are attending.
* **Registration Date** (`date`, required): Enter the date you are registering for the event.
* **Attendee Count** (`number`, required): Enter the number of attendees you are registering for.
* **Notes** (`note`, optional): Provide additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Confirm Email** (`select_one`, required): Confirm that the email address you provided is accurate.
* **Confirm Phone** (`select_one`, required): Confirm that the phone number you provided is accurate.
* **Assigned Tool** (`select_one`, required): If assigned a tool, select the correct tool from the options provided.
* **Output File** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information or comments about the output file.
