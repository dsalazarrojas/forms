# Tutoring Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutoring Program Enrollment Form is a document that helps us understand your needs and preferences as a student. It will be used to determine which programs you are eligible for and to provide you with the best support possible.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your first and last name.
2. Provide a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
3. Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
4. Select the dates and times you are available for tutoring.
5. Choose the days and hours you prefer for tutoring sessions.
6. Select the programs you are interested in enrolling in.
7. Provide contact information for your emergency contact.
8. Sign and date the form to confirm your commitment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Date Available** (`date_available`, date, optional): Please select the earliest date you are available for tutoring.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): If applicable, please select the start date of your preferred tutoring sessions.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): If applicable, please select the end date of your preferred tutoring sessions.
* **Time Available** (`time_available`, time, optional): Please select the time of day you are available for tutoring.
* **Tutoring Days** (`tutoring_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you are available for tutoring (Monday to Sunday).
* **Tutoring Hours** (`tutoring_hours`, select_multiple, optional): Select the hours you are available for tutoring (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Enrolled Programs** (`enrolled_programs`, select_one, optional): Select the programs you are interested in enrolling in.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): Please enter your student ID (if applicable).
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, optional): Please provide any additional information about yourself that you feel is relevant.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Please enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Please describe your relationship with your emergency contact person.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, optional): Please sign and date this section to confirm your commitment.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Please enter your parent's name (if applicable).
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`, note, optional): Please sign and date this section to confirm your commitment.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your student's name (if applicable).
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Please enter your student's address (if applicable).
* **Guardian Signature** (`guardian_signature`, note, optional): Please sign and date this section to confirm your commitment.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, optional): Please enter your guardian's name (if applicable).
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional): Please enter your guardian's phone number (if applicable).

**Tips**

* Please be accurate and truthful in your responses.
* Ensure that all fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
