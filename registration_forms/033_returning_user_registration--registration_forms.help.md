# Returning User Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for returning users who want to update their information or register again with the system. It is a simplified version of the full registration form, focusing on basic user details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the field provided.
2. Enter your Last Name in the field provided.
3. Enter your Email address in the field provided.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the field provided.
5. Select your Country from the options provided.
6. Select your State from the options provided.
7. Enter your City in the field provided.
8. Enter your Zip Code in the field provided.
9. Select your Country of Residence from the options provided.
10. Enter your Password in the field provided.
11. Re-enter your Password in the Repeat Password field for verification purposes.
12. Agree to the Terms and Conditions by selecting 'Yes' from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number here.
* **Country** (select multiple): Select your country of residence from the options provided.
* **State** (select multiple): Select your state from the options provided.
* **City** (text): Enter your city here.
* **Zip Code** (text): Enter your zip code here.
* **Country of Residence** (text): Enter your country of residence here.
* **Password** (text): Enter your password here for internal verification purposes.
* **Repeat Password** (text): Re-enter your password here for verification purposes.
* **Terms and Condition** (note): Read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting 'Yes' from the options provided.
* **Agreement** (select multiple): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms and conditions.
