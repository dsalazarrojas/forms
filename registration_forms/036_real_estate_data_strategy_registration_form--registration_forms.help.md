# real_estate_data_strategy_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in a real estate data strategy program. It will help us to understand who is participating, their areas of interest, and how to contact them.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Participant Details**.
2. Select your area of interest(s) from the list of options.
3. Select your **Location** from the list of options.
4. Enter your **Participant Details 2**, if applicable.
5. Enter your **Contact Details**.
6. Check the box for **Consent**.
7. If applicable, provide your **Date of Birth** and **Time of Birth**.
8. Enter your **Contact Info**.
9. Enter your **Email**.
10. Enter your **Phone** number.
11. Upload a file if required.
12. Finally, enter your **Participant Details 3**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (text, required): Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **What are your interests?** (select multiple, optional): This is where you select all the areas of interest from the options provided. You can select one or more of the options that apply to you.
* **Location** (select one, optional): Choose your location from the provided options.
* **Participant Details 2** (text, optional): Enter additional details about yourself, if applicable.
* **Contact Details** (text, optional): Enter your contact details.
* **Consent** (note, required): This is a checkbox to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth, if applicable.
* **Time of Birth** (time, optional): Enter your time of birth, if applicable.
* **Contact Info** (text, optional): Enter your contact information.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Upload file** (text, optional): Upload any required file.
* **Participant Details 3** (text, optional): Enter additional details about yourself, if required.
