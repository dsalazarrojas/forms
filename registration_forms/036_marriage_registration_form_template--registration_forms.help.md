# Marriage Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a marriage registration for internal record-keeping and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the married parties from the provided options: "Yes" or "No".
2. Provide details about the married parties, including names, ages, and other relevant information.
3. Enter the date of the marriage.
4. Enter the venue of the marriage.
5. Enter the officiating officer's name and contact information.
6. Enter the marriage certificate number.
7. (Optional) Enter the date of the marriage certificate.
8. (Optional) Upload a copy of the marriage certificate.
9. Provide any additional remarks about the marriage registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Married Parties** (`married_parties`): Select "Yes" if the marriage is registered, "No" otherwise.
* **Married Parties Details** (`married_parties_details`): Enter the names, ages, and other relevant information about the married parties.
* **Date of Marriage** (`date_of_marriage`): Enter the date of the marriage in the standard date format (e.g., 2022-02-15).
* **Venue of Marriage** (`venue_of_marriage`): Enter the location where the marriage took place (e.g., church, courthouse, etc.).
* **Officiating Officer** (`officiating_officer`): Enter the name and contact information of the officiating officer.
* **Marriage Certificate Number** (`marriage_certificate_number`): Enter the number of the marriage certificate.
* **Marriage Certificate Date** (`marriage_certificate_date`): (Optional) Enter the date of the marriage certificate. (This field is only required if the marriage certificate has a specific date.)
* **Marriage Certificate File** (`marriage_certificate_file`): (Optional) Upload a copy of the marriage certificate.
* **Remarks** (`remarks`): Provide any additional comments or information about the marriage registration.

## Tips
- Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- Use the standard date format for the date of marriage and marriage certificate date.
- Use a valid number for the marriage certificate number.
