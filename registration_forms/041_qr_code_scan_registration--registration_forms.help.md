# qr_code_scan_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `qr_code_scan_registration` form is designed to collect attendee information for events. This form is meant to be used to register attendees by scanning a QR code, which will then populate the form fields with relevant information, making it easier and faster for attendees to provide accurate data. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have a valid QR code to scan.
2. Scan the QR code using a device with a camera (e.g., a smartphone).
3. The QR code will populate the form fields automatically.
4. Review the filled-in form fields to ensure they are accurate.
5. Make any necessary corrections or additions as needed.
6. Submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, required): This field will be automatically populated by the QR code scan. Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (Event Date, date, required): This field will also be populated by the QR code scan. Enter the date of the event you are attending.
* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your government-issued ID or other official documents.
* **Attendee Email** (Attendee Email, email, optional): Enter your email address, if you wish to be contacted about future events or to receive updates.
* **Attendee Phone** (Attendee Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if you wish to be contacted in case of last-minute event changes or updates.

## Tips

* Ensure your device's camera is enabled and the QR code is in view.
* If the QR code scan fails, try scanning the code again or contact the event organizer for assistance.
* Double-check the accuracy of the populated form fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unable to scan the QR code, you can contact the event organizer to request a paper registration form.
