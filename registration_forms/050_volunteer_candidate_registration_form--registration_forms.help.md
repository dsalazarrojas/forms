# Volunteer Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register as volunteers. It collects information that will help us understand your interests, skills, and availability to match you with volunteer opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose your preferred volunteer role and skills from the relevant options.
3. Select your availability and time slots according to your schedule.
4. Provide details about your work experience and education.
5. Submit the form to register as a volunteer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Volunteer Role**: Select the role that best describes your volunteer interests and skills.
* **Skills**: Choose the skills you have or are willing to learn.
* **Availability**: Select the dates and time slots when you are available to volunteer.
* **Available Time**: Specify the time slots you are available to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Hours**: Enter the number of hours you are willing to volunteer per week.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are interested in volunteering with.
* **Department**: Enter the department you are interested in volunteering with.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position.
* **Work Experience**: Provide details about your work experience relevant to volunteering.
* **Education**: Enter your educational background relevant to volunteering.
* **Volunteer Experience**: Provide details about your previous volunteer experience.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select the type of volunteer work you are interested in.
* **Volunteer Shift**: Select the shift you prefer to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Frequency**: Select how often you are willing to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Committee**: Choose any committees you are interested in joining.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes about your volunteer interest.
* **Submit**: Submit your application to register as a volunteer.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth registration process.
