# Trade Fair Exhibitor Mixer RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help exhibitors and attendees register and provide information for the Trade Fair Exhibitor Mixer event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the required fields, which are marked as "required".
2. For fields with multiple options, select one or more options that apply to you.
3. Provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
4. Double-check your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your organization name.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Please enter your phone number.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Please enter your email address.
* **Event Website** (`event_website`, `text`, required): Please enter the website of the event.
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, `text`, required): Please enter your booth number (if applicable).
* **Preferred Diet** (`preferred_diet`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose from the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required): Please provide any additional comments or information.
* **RSVP Response** (`rsvp_response`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **RSVP Status** (`rsvp_status`, `select_one`, required): Please select one of the following options:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
* **Signature** (`signature`, `note`, required): Please sign this form to confirm your RSVP.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you have any special requests or additional comments, please provide them in the designated fields.
* If you have any follow-up questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
