# Birdwatching Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Birdwatching Workshop Registration Form is a form used to register participants for various birdwatching workshops offered by the organization. The form collects participant information, workshop preferences, and payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant information section: First name, last name, email address, phone number, and street address.
2. Select the workshop you wish to attend.
3. Provide the date and time of the workshop you wish to attend.
4. Select the duration of the workshop.
5. Choose the location of the workshop.
6. Answer questions about your birdwatching experience and equipment.
7. Select your preferred method of contact.
8. Provide emergency contact information.
9. Confirm your understanding of the liability waiver, photo release, and cancellation policy.
10. Sign the registration form to confirm your agreement with the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`id: 2`): Please enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`id: 3`): Please enter your last name.
- **Email Address** (`id: 4`): Please enter your contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (`id: 5`): Please enter your contact phone number.
- **Street Address** (`id: 6`): Please enter your complete street address.
- **City** (`id: 7`): Please enter the city where you reside.
- **State** (`id: 8`): Please enter the state where you reside.
- **ZIP Code** (`id: 9`): Please enter your postal code.
- **Date of Birth** (`id: 10`): Please enter your date of birth.
- **Workshop Selection** (`id: 11`): Choose the workshop you wish to attend from the dropdown menu.
- **Workshop Date** (`id: 13`): Please enter the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
- **Workshop Time** (`id: 14`): Choose the start time of the workshop.
- **Workshop Duration** (`id: 15`): Select the duration of the workshop.
- **Workshop Location** (`id: 16`): Choose the location of the workshop.
- **Birdwatching Experience** (`id: 17`): Please select your level of birdwatching experience.
- **Years of Birdwatching** (`id: 18`): Please select how many years you have been birdwatching.
- **Learning Goals** (`id: 19`): Select the learning goals you wish to achieve.
- **Equipment** (`id: 20`): If applicable, please list any birding equipment you possess.
- **Binoculars Owned** (`id: 21`): Please select whether you own binoculars.
- **Binocular Specifications** (`id: 22`): If you own binoculars, please enter their specifications.
- **Camera Equipment** (`id: 23`): If you own a camera, please select the type of camera you possess.
- **Field Guide** (`id: 24`): Please select whether you have a field guide.
- **Special Needs** (`id: 25`): If you have any special needs, please describe them.
- **Mobility Requirements** (`id: 26`): Please select your mobility level.
- **Accessibility Needs** (`id: 27`): If you have any accessibility needs, please describe them.
- **Medical Conditions** (`id: 28`): If you have any medical conditions, please describe them.
- **Dietary Requirements** (`id: 29`): Please select your dietary requirements.
- **Dietary Notes** (`id: 30`): Please provide any additional dietary notes.
- **Registration Options** (`id: 31`): This is an optional field for additional options.
- **Group Registration** (`id: 32`): If you are registering a group, please select the number of people.
- **Group Size** (`id: 33`): Please enter the number of people in your group.
- **Group Member Names** (`id: 34`): Please list the names of all members in your group.
- **Transportation** (`id: 35`): Please select how you will travel to the workshop.
- **Accommodation Needed** (`id: 36`): If you require accommodation, please select the option.
- **Registration Fee** (`id: 38`): Please enter the workshop fee.
- **Payment Method** (`id: 39`): Please select how you will pay for the workshop.
- **Discount Code** (`id: 40`): If you have a discount code, please enter it.
- **Membership Discount** (`id: 41`): Please select whether you are a member.
- **Financial Assistance** (`id: 42`): If you need financial assistance, please select the option.
- **Communication Preferences** (`id: 43`): This is an optional field for communication preferences.
- **Preferred Contact Method** (`id: 44`): Please select your preferred method of contact.
- **Email Updates** (`id: 45`): Please select whether you wish to receive workshop updates.
- **Newsletter Subscription** (`id: 46`): If you wish to subscribe to the newsletter, please select the option.
- **How Did You Hear About Workshop** (`id: 47`): If you have any other sources, please select them.
- **Additional Comments** (`id: 48`): Please provide any additional comments or questions.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (`id: 49`): Please enter the name of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (`id: 50`): Please enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Waivers and Agreements** (`id: 51`): This is a required field for waivers and agreements.
- **Liability Waiver** (`id: 52`): Please select whether you accept the liability waiver.
- **Photo Release** (`id: 53`): Please select whether you agree to the photo release.
- **Cancellation Policy** (`id: 54`): Please select whether you understand the cancellation policy.
- **Registration Signature** (`id: 55`): Please select whether you agree to all terms.
- **Registration Date** (`id: 56`): The date of registration is automatically populated.
