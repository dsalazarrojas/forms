# Trade Show Licensing Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Trade Show Licensing Form is designed to collect essential information from exhibitors and their representatives for the purpose of registering them for the trade show. This includes contact information, event details, and licensing information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by providing your exhibitor's information by filling out the fields on the "exhibitor_info" page.
2. Move on to the "contact_person" field to enter the name and title of the person who will be handling the communication regarding your exhibit.
3. Fill out the "contact_email" field with the email address associated with your organization or representative.
4. Enter your organization's contact phone number in the "contact_phone" field.
5. Provide your booth number in the "booth_number" field.
6. Fill out the "vendor_info" field with details about your company or organization.
7. Enter information about your product or goods in the "product_info" field.
8. Provide event information in the "event_info" field.
9. Review and select the appropriate terms of acceptance in the "terms_acceptance" field.
10. If you have any additional comments or remarks, you can enter them in the "notes" field.
11. Finally, enter your vendor license number in the "vendor_license_number" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Exhibitor Info**: A field for you to enter information about your exhibitor company.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name and title of the person who will be handling communication.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of your organization or representative.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your organization's contact phone number.
* **Booth Number**: Enter your booth number at the trade show.
* **Vendor Info**: Provide details about your company or organization.
* **Product Info**: Enter information about your product or goods.
* **Event Info**: Enter information about the event you will be participating in.
* **Terms Acceptance**: Review and select the terms you accept.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Vendor License Number**: Enter your vendor license number.
 
## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid any errors.
* Double-check your information for accuracy and completeness.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, contact our support team.
* Review and understand the terms and conditions before accepting them.
