# Weight Loss Challenge Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register users for a weight loss challenge, collecting information about their name, contact details, and medical conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name on the first page.
2. Enter your email address and phone number on the second page.
3. On the third page, indicate your expectation on this challenge.
4. On the third page, also declare if you have any medical conditions such as diabetes or allergies.
5. If you have any other medical conditions not listed, provide a brief description on the fourth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for updates and information about the challenge.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates and information about the challenge.
* **Expectation on this challenge** (`expectation`, `text`, required): Briefly describe your goals and what you hope to achieve through this challenge.
* **Diabetes** (`diabetes`, `select_one`, optional): If you have diabetes, select 'True' to indicate this medical condition. If not, select 'False'.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `select_multiple`, optional): If you have allergies, select all that apply from the list (e.g. Food allergy, Medication allergy). If you have other allergies not listed, provide a brief description on the next page.
* **Other Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions_text`, `text`, optional): If you have any other medical conditions not listed, provide a brief description here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a successful submission.
* If you have any doubts or questions about the form, contact us for assistance.
* Please be honest and accurate when providing your medical information, as it will be used to tailor the challenge to your needs.
