# hospitality_suite_manager_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is a registration form for hospitality suite managers to provide general information about themselves. Filling out this form helps our system create their profile, making it easier for them to be contacted and assigned to new roles or tasks.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately with your general details, experience, and availability.
2. Indicate whether you have the necessary documents required for your position.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Details (General Details)** (`general_details`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to collect your basic contact information.
* **Experience (Experience)** (`experience`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to describe your work experience related to hospitality suite management.
* **Availability (Availability)** (`availability`, `text`, required: false)
	+ This field is used to indicate your work schedule and availability.
* **Documents (Documents)** (`documents`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Please select "Yes" if you have the necessary documents required for your position, or "No" if you do not have them.
