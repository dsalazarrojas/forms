# Automation Vendor Licensing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help automation vendors register their products or solutions with the system. It gathers information about the vendor, their organization, the product, and licensing details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your vendor information, including name, contact name, email, and phone number.
2. Enter your organization's name and type.
3. Provide details about the product or solution being registered, including name, version, and description.
4. Specify the type of license required and the number of licenses needed.
5. Choose the support level required.
6. Select the license start and end dates.
7. Confirm that you have read and accepted the license terms and conditions.
8. Finally, confirm your registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`): Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`vendor_contact_name`): Enter the name of the primary contact person for your organization.
* **Vendor Email** (`vendor_email`): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Vendor Phone** (`vendor_phone`): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`): Enter the name of your organization (e.g., company or university).
* **Organization Type** (`organization_type`): Choose the type of organization you represent (e.g., Enterprise, Mid-Market, Small Business, Government, Education, Non-Profit).
* **Department** (`department`): Enter the department or team that will be using the product or solution.
* **Product/Solution Name** (`product_name`): Enter the name of the product or solution being registered.
* **Product Version** (`product_version`): Enter the version of the product or solution being registered.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`): Briefly describe the product or solution being registered.
* **License Type** (`license_type`): Choose the type of license you require (e.g., Perpetual, Subscription (Annual), Subscription (Monthly), Trial, Educational, Government).
* **Number of Licenses Needed** (`number_of_licenses`): Enter the number of licenses you require.
* **Types of Users** (`license_users`): Select the types of users who will be using the product or solution (e.g., Named Users, Concurrent Users, Department-Wide, Enterprise-Wide, Floating License).
* **Support Level Required** (`support_level`): Choose the level of support you require (e.g., Basic, Standard, Premium, Enterprise).
* **License Start Date** (`license_start_date`): Enter the date when the license will start.
* **License End Date** (`license_end_date`): Enter the date when the license will end.
* **I accept the license terms and conditions** (`terms_acceptance`): Confirm that you have read and accepted the license terms and conditions.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Enter the date you are registering this license.
