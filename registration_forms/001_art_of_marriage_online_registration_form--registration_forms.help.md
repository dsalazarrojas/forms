# Art Of Marriage Online Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Art Of Marriage Online Registration Form, which is intended to collect information from potential registrants for the Art Of Marriage online program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the **Email Address** field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the **Phone Number** field (optional).
5. Select your **Current Marriage Status** from the options provided.
6. If applicable, enter your **Wedding Anniversary Date**.
7. Enter your Spouse's Name in the **Spouses Name** field (optional).
8. Select the **Areas of Interest** you hope to focus on (optional).
9. Enter your expectations for the program in the **What are your expectations for this program** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted by phone.
* **Current Marriage Status**: Select the status that best describes your current relationship status.
	+ **Married**: You are currently married.
	+ **Engaged**: You are engaged to be married.
	+ **Cohabitating**: You are living with your partner but not married.
	+ **Other**: Select this option if you have a different relationship status.
* **Wedding Anniversary Date**: If you are married or have a significant other, enter your wedding anniversary date.
* **Spouses Name**: Enter your spouse's name (if applicable).
* **Areas of Interest**: Select the areas you hope to focus on in the program.
	+ **Communication**: You hope to improve communication in your relationship.
	+ **Conflict Resolution**: You hope to learn how to resolve conflicts effectively.
	+ **Financial Planning**: You hope to learn about effective financial planning for your relationship.
	+ **Parenting**: You hope to improve your parenting skills.
	+ **Spiritual Growth**: You hope to grow spiritually with your partner.
* **What are your expectations for this program**: Enter your expectations for the program (optional).
