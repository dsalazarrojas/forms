# Healthy Sexuality Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Healthy Sexuality Event Registration" form is designed to collect information from participants about their interests and preferences for a specific event. This form helps the event organizers to prepare and execute the event according to participant needs and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by providing your participant information, including your name and contact details in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" section to help us contact you if needed.
3. In the "Age" section, select your age group.
4. In the "Contact Information" section, provide any additional contact details that we might need to reach you.
5. Select your preferred session(s) in the "Session Preferences" section.
6. Choose your preferred session details and theme in the "Session Details" and "Session Theme" sections, respectively.
7. Provide the date of the event in the "Event Date" section.
8. In the "Participant Notes" section, you can provide any additional comments or notes about your participation.
9. In the "Participant Questions" section, type in any questions or concerns you may have about the event.
10. Finally, read and agree to the terms of participation by checking the "Agreement" checkbox.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required): Enter your participant information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can contact you if needed.
* **Age** (`Age`, `number`, required): Select your age group.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Provide any additional contact details we might need to reach you.
* **Session Preferences** (`Session Preferences`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose one or more sessions that interest you.
* **Session Details** (`Session Details`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session details.
* **Session Theme** (`Session Theme`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred session theme.
* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, `text`, required): Provide any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Participant Questions** (`Participant Questions`, `text`, required): Type in any questions or concerns you may have about the event.
* **Agreement** (`Agreement`, `checkbox`, required): Read and agree to the terms of participation by checking the box.
* **Session Agree** (`Session Agree`, `checkbox`, required): Read and agree to the terms of session participation by checking the box.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
* Review your form carefully to ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please type them in the "Participant Questions" section.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, please provide them in the "Participant Notes" section.
