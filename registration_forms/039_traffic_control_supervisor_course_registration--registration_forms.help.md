# Traffic Control Supervisor Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Traffic Control Supervisor Course. It collects information on participant demographics, organization affiliation, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out each section carefully, providing accurate and relevant information for your participation in the course.
2. If you have any prior experience as a traffic control supervisor, please fill out the `experience` and `experience_years` fields.
3. Select the relevant `session` and `session_status` options that apply to your circumstances.
4. Enter your email and phone number for communication purposes.
5. Provide any additional comments or notes if necessary.
6. Confirm your registration by selecting the correct option in the `confirm` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Organization** (`organization`, select_one, required): Choose your organization type (e.g., government agency, private company, etc.).
* **Sessions** (`sessions`, select_multiple, required): Select all the sessions you are interested in or currently attending.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to start the course or the date you last attended the course.
* **Participant Experience** (`experience`, text, optional): Describe your experience as a traffic control supervisor.
* **Experience Years** (`experience_years`, number, optional): Enter the number of years you have been working as a traffic control supervisor.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you plan to finish the course or the date your previous course ended.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your session attendance or preferences.
* **Provider Notes** (`provider_notes`, note, optional): Write any comments or notes about your provider or service.
* **Agency Notes** (`agency_notes`, note, optional): Write any comments or notes about your agency or service.
* **Session Status** (`session_status`, select_one, required): Choose the status of your session (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_one, required): Choose your participant status (e.g., Active, Inactive).
* **Course Notes** (`course_notes`, note, optional): Write any comments or notes about the course.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or feedback about your experience.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, text, optional): Enter the name of your training provider or service.
* **Training Provider ID** (`training_provider_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID of your training provider or service.
* **Agency** (`agency`, text, optional): Enter the name of your agency or service.
* **Agency ID** (`agency_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of your agency or service.
* **Firm** (`firm`, text, optional): Enter the name of your firm or service.
* **Firm ID** (`firm_id`, text, optional): Enter the ID of your firm or service.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your official email address for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for emergency contact or follow-up purposes.
* **Confirm Registration** (`confirm`, select_one, required): Confirm your registration by selecting the correct option.
