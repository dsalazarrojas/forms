# green_property_certification_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The green property certification registration form is a tool used to register properties that meet certain environmental and sustainability standards.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide the property address.
2. Enter the property owner's name.
3. Specify the property location.
4. Identify the property type.
5. Select the property certification status.
6. Choose the green certification level.
7. Enter the date of certification.
8. Enter the expiration date.
9. Indicate if the property has a sustainability certification standard.
10. Specify the certification body.
11. Enter the contact person's name and phone number.
12. Enter the contact email.
13. Enter the website.
14. Specify the sustainability committee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the property to be certified.
* **Property Owner Name** (`property_owner_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the property owner.
* **Property Location** (`property_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of the property, e.g., city, state, or country.
* **Property Type** (`property_type`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the type of property, e.g., residential, commercial, etc.
* **Property Certification Status** (`property_certification_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of the property's certification, e.g., Active or Inactive.
* **Green Certification Level** (`green_certification_level`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the level of green certification, e.g., High or Low.
* **Date of Certification** (`certification_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the property was certified.
* **Expiration Date** (`expiration_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date when the property's certification expires.
* **Sustainability Certification Standard** (`sustainability_certification_standard`, `select_one`, required/optional): Indicate if the property has a sustainability certification standard, e.g., Yes or No.
* **Certification Body** (`certification_body`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the organization that certified the property.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the property's certification.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Website** (`website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of the property or certification body.
* **Sustainability Committee** (`sustainability_committee`, `text`, required/optional): Specify the sustainability committee responsible for the property's certification.
