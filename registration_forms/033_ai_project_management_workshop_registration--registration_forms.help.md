# AI Project Management Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from attendees interested in attending the AI Project Management Workshop. It collects details about the attendee's background, experience, and preferences regarding the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name**, **Work Email**, **Company/Organization**, and **Job Title** details in the first section.
2. In the **Years of Experience in Project Management** field, enter your relevant experience.
3. In the **Primary Project Management Methodology** section, select the method you use most often.
4. In the **PM Tools Used** section, select all the tools you currently use for project management.
5. In the **Challenges in Managing AI Projects** section, select your biggest challenge.
6. In the **Workshop Selection & Logistics** section, choose your preferred workshop date and format (Virtual or In-Person).
7. In the **Payment Method** section, select your preferred payment method.
8. If you require a Certificate of Completion for Professional Development Units (PDUs) credits, select the corresponding option.
9. Finally, enter your preferred **Registration Date** and any **Discount Code** you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name (`full_name`, text, required)
Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.

### Work Email (`work_email`, email, required)
Enter your work email address.

### Company / Organization (`company_name`, text, required)
Enter the name of your company or organization.

### Job Title (`job_title`, text, required)
Enter your current job title.

### Years of Experience in Project Management (`pm_experience_years`, number, required)
Enter the number of years you have experience in project management.

### Primary Project Management Methodology (`primary_methodology`, select_one, required)
Select the methodology you use most often for project management.

### Which PM tools do you currently use? (`pm_tools_used`, select_multiple, required)
Select all PM tools you currently use for project management.

### What is your biggest challenge in managing AI projects? (`ai_pm_challenges`, select_one, required)
Select your biggest challenge in managing AI projects.

### Select Your Workshop Track (`session_selection`, select_one, required)
Choose the workshop track that interests you most.

### Preferred Workshop Date (`workshop_date`, select_one, required)
Choose your preferred workshop date.

### Attendance Format (`attendance_format`, select_one, required)
Choose your preferred attendance format (Virtual or In-Person).

### Discount Code (if any) (`discount_code`, text, optional)
Enter any discount code you may have.

### Payment Method (`payment_method`, select_one, required)
Select your preferred payment method.

### Do you require a Certificate of Completion for PDU credits? (`certificate_required`, select_one, required)
Select if you require a certificate of completion for PDUs.

### Date (`registration_date`, date, required)
Enter the date you are registering.
