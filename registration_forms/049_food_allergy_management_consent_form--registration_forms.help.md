# Food Allergy Management Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Food Allergy Management Consent Form is a document used by parents or guardians to provide information about their child's food allergies and medical treatments. This form helps healthcare providers and school staff understand the child's allergy management plan, ensuring their safety and well-being.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the necessary information required.
2. Fill out the form accurately and honestly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Sign and date the Parent Signature section, indicating that you have read and agree to the information provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): Provide a description of your child's food allergies, including any allergies they may have to specific foods, severity of the allergy, and any previous reactions or incidents.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, optional): Please sign and date this section to confirm that you have read and understand the information provided in this form.
* **Treatment Info** (`treatment_info`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the treatments that apply to your child's allergies, such as "Bake", "Eat", "Drink", or "Inject".
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, text, optional): List any medications your child is taking to manage their allergies, including dosage instructions and frequency.
* **Medication Dose** (`medication_dose`, number, optional): Enter the dose of medication your child is taking, if applicable.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the questions or sections, please contact the relevant healthcare provider or school administration for guidance.
- Make sure to keep a copy of this form for your records and update it as needed.
- Review and update this form annually or as needed, ensuring that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
