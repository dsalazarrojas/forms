# Ethical Research In Education Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ethical Research In Education Workshop Registration Form is designed to gather information from workshop attendees, including their general details, preferences, and consents. This form helps us understand who will attend the workshop and ensures we can tailor the experience to their needs. Please complete this form carefully to ensure a smooth and productive workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the general information section, which includes your name and other basic details.
2. In the "Workshop Priorities" field, select the priority level that best represents your interest in the workshop (Label 1, Label 2, or Label 3).
3. If applicable, fill out your attendance dates and time in the "Attendee Dates" and "Attendee Time" fields.
4. Add any additional notes about your workshop experience in the "Attendee Notes" field.
5. Provide your email address in the "Attendee Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Attendee Phone" field if you wish to be contacted.
7. Read the "Consent and Terms" field carefully and agree or decline to the terms.
8. Select your general consent status (I agree or I decline) in the "General Consent" field.
9. Finally, confirm your consent for the workshop (I agree or I decline) in the "Workshop Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Information (General Information)**: Provide your name and other basic details.
* **Workshop Priorities (Workshop Priorities)**: Select the priority level that best represents your interest in the workshop (Label 1, Label 2, or Label 3).
* **Attendee Dates (Attendee Dates)**: Enter your expected attendance dates.
* **Attendee Time (Attendee Time)**: Enter your expected attendance time.
* **Attendee Notes (Attendee Notes)**: Add any additional notes about your workshop experience.
* **Attendee Email (Attendee Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Attendee Phone (Attendee Phone)**: Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Consent and Terms (Consent and Terms)**: Carefully read and agree or decline to the terms.
* **General Consent (General Consent)**: Select your general consent status (I agree or I decline).
* **Workshop Consent (Workshop Consent)**: Confirm your consent for the workshop (I agree or I decline).
* **Special Requests (Special Requests)**: Enter any special requests you have for the workshop experience.
