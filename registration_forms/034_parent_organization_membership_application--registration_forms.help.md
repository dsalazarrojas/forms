# parent_organization_membership_application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent organization membership application form is used to register a new organization to become a member of our parent organization. It is essential to provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter the **Organization Name** of the organization you represent.
2. Provide the name and contact information of the **Contact Person** responsible for the organization.
3. Enter the **Email** address of the organization to ensure we can contact them for further information.
4. Enter the **Phone Number** of the organization's contact person.
5. Select the **Membership Type** that best describes the organization's membership.
6. Choose the **Membership Duration** for the organization's membership.
7. Enter the **Address** of the organization.
8. Select the **Start Date** when the membership should begin.
9. Select the **End Date** when the membership should end (if applicable).
10. Add any additional **Notes** about the organization or its membership.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are registering.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the name and contact information of the person responsible for the organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the organization's contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the organization's contact person.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of membership that best describes the organization's membership.
*   *Membership Type* options: 
    + Membership Type: The standard membership type for organizations.
    + Other: Select this option if the membership type is not listed (please provide more information in the **Notes** field).
* **Membership Duration** (`membership_duration`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the duration of the membership.
*   *Membership Duration* options: 
    + Month: Select this option for a monthly membership.
    + Other: Select this option if the membership duration is not listed (please provide more information in the **Notes** field).
* **Address** (`address`, date, optional): Enter the address of the organization.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the membership should begin.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the membership should end (if applicable).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional information about the organization or its membership.
