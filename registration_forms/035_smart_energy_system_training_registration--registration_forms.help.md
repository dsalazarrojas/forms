# Smart Energy System Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Energy System Training Registration form is designed to collect information from participants to schedule and manage training sessions. This guide will walk you through the form fields and help you complete it correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the Smart Energy System Training, follow these steps:
1. Select your session choice from the session choice field.
2. Provide your participant details in the participant details field.
3. Check the consent box to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.
4. Fill in your name and email address in the name and email fields.
5. Provide your university and course details in the university and course title fields.
6. Specify your course location and participant institution in the participant institution and course location fields.
7. Fill in your address, phone number, and participant note in the participant address, participant phone, and participant note fields.
8. Select your participant status as Active or Inactive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Session Choice (session_choice)
* **Session Choice**: Select your preferred session choice (Yes/No).

### Participant Details (participant_details)
* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details.

### Consent (consent)
* **Consent**: Check the box to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

### Name (name)
* **Name**: Fill in your name.

### Email (email)
* **Email**: Enter your email address.

### Participant Details (participant_details)
* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details.

### Session Date (session_date)
* **Session Date**: Specify the date of the session.

### Session Time (session_time)
* **Session Time**: Specify the time of the session.

### Participant Name (participant_name)
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name.

### University (university)
* **University**: Specify your university.

### Course Title (course_title)
* **Course Title**: Enter your course title.

### Course Description (course_description)
* **Course Description**: Enter a brief description of the course.

### Course Location (course_location)
* **Course Location**: Enter the location of the course.

### Participant Institution (participant_institution)
* **Participant Institution**: Specify your institution.

### Participant Address (participant_address)
* **Participant Address**: Enter your address.

### Participant Phone (participant_phone)
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.

### Participant Note (participant_note)
* **Participant Note**: Enter any additional notes.

### Participant Email (participant_email)
* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address.

### Session Start Date (session_start_date)
* **Session Start Date**: Specify the start date of the session.

### Session Start Time (session_start_time)
* **Session Start Time**: Specify the start time of the session.

### Session End Date (session_end_date)
* **Session End Date**: Specify the end date of the session.

### Session End Time (session_end_time)
* **Session End Time**: Specify the end time of the session.

### Participant Consent (participant_consent)
* **Participant Consent**: Check the box to acknowledge that you have read and understood the terms and conditions.

### Participant Instructor (participant_instructor)
* **Participant Instructor**: Select whether you are an instructor or not.

### Participant Status (participant_status)
* **Participant Status**: Select your participant status as Active or Inactive.

### Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Double-check your session choice before proceeding.
* Ensure that you have the necessary permissions from your university to participate in the training.
