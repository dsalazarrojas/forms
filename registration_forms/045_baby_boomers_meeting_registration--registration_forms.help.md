# Baby Boomers Meeting Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for meetings organized for the Baby Boomer demographic. It collects personal and meeting-related information for event planning and organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal information: Enter your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address to ensure you receive meeting updates and notifications.
2. Select your meeting details: Choose the Meeting Date and Meeting Location for the meeting you plan to attend.
3. Specify your attendance type: Indicate whether you will be attending in-person, virtually, or with a hybrid option.
4. Provide guest information (if applicable): If you're bringing guests, list their names and the number of attendees.
5. Specify your preferences: Mention any dietary restrictions or allergies, accessibility needs, and preferred topics of interest.
6. Confirm your attendance: Indicate whether you've attended previous meetings and express your interest in volunteering.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
- **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address for meeting updates and notifications.
- **Phone Number**: Optional, but recommended for easy communication. Leave blank if not applicable.
- **Birth Year**: Enter the year you were born to verify baby boomer status.
- **Meeting Date**: Select the meeting date you plan to attend.
- **Meeting Time**: Optional, but recommended for scheduling purposes. Leave blank if not applicable.
- **Meeting Location**: Enter the venue or address where the meeting will take place.
- **Type of Attendance**: Choose how you plan to attend (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
- **Will You Be Bringing Guests?**: Select 'True' if you're bringing guests, and 'False' otherwise.
- **Number of Guests**: Enter the total number of guests (including yourself).
- **Guest Names**: List the names of your guests (if applicable).
- **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Mention any food-related requirements or allergies.
- **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs**: Specify any special needs for accessibility or accommodations.
- **Topics You Are Interested In**: Select all topics that apply to you.
- **Have You Attended Previous Meetings?**: Indicate your meeting attendance history.
- **Interested in Volunteering?**: Express your interest in volunteering for future meetings.
- **Additional Information**: Enter any additional context or comments about your registration.
