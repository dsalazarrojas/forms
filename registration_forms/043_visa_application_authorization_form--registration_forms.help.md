# visa-application-authorization-form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information from individuals seeking visa authorization. The form is designed to gather necessary details from applicants to process their visa requests efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form

1. To complete this form, please provide accurate and truthful information in each field.
2. Fill in all the required fields, which are marked as required in the form.
3. For fields like 'Visa Type', you can select one option from the given list.
4. For 'Visa Authorization', select 'True' if your visa has been authorized, and 'False' if it hasn't.
5. For 'Visa Status', select the current status of your visa from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Applicant Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your passport.
* **Country of Origin** (text, optional): Enter the country where you were born.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Applicant Nationality** (text, optional): Enter your nationality as it appears on your passport.
* **Visa Issuing Country** (text, optional): Enter the country that issued your visa.
* **Visa Duration** (number, optional): Enter the duration of your visa in months.
* **Visa Type** (select_one, optional): Select the type of visa you are applying for (United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia).
* **Address Line 1** (text, optional): Enter your address line 1.
* **Address Line 2** (text, optional): Enter your address line 2.
* **City** (text, optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (text, optional): Enter your state.
* **Zip Code** (text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Passport Number** (text, optional): Enter your passport number.
* **Travel Document Number** (text, optional): Enter your travel document number.
* **Passport Country** (text, optional): Enter the country that issued your passport.
* **Document Expiry Date** (date, optional): Enter the expiry date of your travel document.
* **Document Issue Date** (date, optional): Enter the issue date of your travel document.
* **Document Country** (text, optional): Enter the country that issued your travel document.
* **Document Type** (text, optional): Enter the type of your travel document.
* **Travel Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of your planned travel.
* **Visa Authorization** (select_multiple, optional): Select 'True' if your visa has been authorized, and 'False' if it hasn't.
* **Visa Status** (select_one, optional): Select the current status of your visa (Pending, Approved, Denied).
* **Submit** (note, optional): This is a confirmation step to submit the form.
