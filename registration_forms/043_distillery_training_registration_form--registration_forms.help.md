# Distillery Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Distillery Training Registration Form is designed for users to register for a training session at a distillery. This form helps distilleries to collect relevant information about attendees, including their distillery name, name, email, phone number, class type, training location, signup date, training start time, class duration, and any additional notes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields: Distillery Name, Name, Email, and Submit.
2. Provide your distillery name in the Distillery Name field.
3. Enter your name in the Name field.
4. Enter your email address in the Email field.
5. Select either "Yes" or "No" in the Class Type field.
6. Choose a training location from the options: Downtown or Uptown.
7. Enter the date you signed up for the training in the Signup Date field.
8. Enter the start time of the training in the Training Start Time field.
9. Provide any additional information or comments in the Notes field.
10. Click the Submit button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Distillery Name** (`distillery_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the distillery where you will be attending the training session.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed on your training badge and for communication purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted about the training.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" if you are a regular distillery staff member, "No" if you are a guest or intern.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, select_one, optional): Choose where you will be attending the training: Downtown or Uptown.
* **Signup Date** (`signup_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you signed up for the training.
* **Training Start Time** (`training_start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the training.
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, text, optional): Enter the duration of the class in minutes or hours.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional information or comments about your registration.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your registration.
