# HR Analytics Masterclass Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for HR professionals to register for the HR Analytics Masterclass. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
- Fill in your registration form details
- Choose your preferred experience level
- Select your session preference
- Provide your contact details
- Add any additional notes if necessary

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration form (1)** (`text`, required/optional): Please fill in your name.
* **Attendee details (2)** (`text`, required/optional): Please fill in a brief description of the attendee details.
* **Experience level (3)** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Please select your level of experience in HR. Options: HR Professional, HR Manager, HR Specialist, Other.
* **Session preferences (4)** (`select_one`, required/optional): Please select your preferred session time. Options: Morning, Afternoon, Full day.
* **Contact details (5)** (`text`, required/optional): Please fill in your contact details.
* **Email (6)** (`email`, required/optional): Please fill in your email address.
* **Phone (7)** (`text`, required/optional): Please fill in your phone number.
* **Company (8)** (`text`, required/optional): Please fill in your company name.
* **Job title (9)** (`text`, required/optional): Please fill in your job title.
* **Additional notes (10)** (`note`, required/optional): Please add any additional notes about your registration.
