# Summer Class Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Summer Class Registration Form is a simple form designed to collect information from students who wish to register for a summer class. This form is meant to be used for registration purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Title**: The title of the form is for reference and is displayed at the top of the form. It's a required field.
2. **Summer Class Registration Form** : The summer_class_registration_form field is for the student's information. Enter their name.
3. **Student Email** : The student_email field is for the student's email address. Enter their valid email address.
4. **Student Phone** : The student_phone field is for the student's phone number. Enter their phone number.
5. **Select Program** : The select_program field is a multiple-choice field with options "Yes" and "No". This is for selecting the program the student is interested in.
6. **Summer Program** : The summer_program field is a single-choice field with options "Yes" and "No". This is for selecting a specific summer program.
7. **Start Date**: The start_date field is for selecting the start date of the class.
8. **End Date**: The end_date field is for selecting the end date of the class.
9. **Time**: The time field is for selecting the time of the class.
10. **Note**: The note field is for leaving any additional comments or notes about the student's registration.
11. **Submit Button**: The submit_button field is the final step to submit the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter all required fields to ensure successful submission of the form.
* If you're unsure about a particular field, refer back to the form's purpose and the field explanations above.
