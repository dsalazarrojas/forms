# exam_registration_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The exam_registration_confirmation_form is used to confirm the status of participants and exams.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  First, enter your first name in the first_name field.
2.  Next, enter your last name in the last_name field.
3.  In the email field, enter your email address.
4.  Enter your phone number in the phone_number field.
5.  Enter the name of the exam in the exam_name field.
6.  Enter the name of the participant in the participant_name field.
7.  Choose the participant status from the participant_status select field.
8.  Enter the exam start date in the exam_start_date field.
9.  Enter the exam end date in the exam_end_date field.
10.  Enter the exam duration in the exam_duration field.
11.  Select the communication method from the communication_method select field.
12.  Confirm the participant status in the confirm_participant_status select field.
13.  Confirm the exam status in the confirm_exam_status select field.
14.  Enter any additional notes in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **last_name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **phone_number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **exam_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the exam.
* **participant_name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **participant_status** (select_one, optional): Choose the participant status.
* **exam_start_date** (date, optional): Enter the exam start date.
* **exam_end_date** (date, optional): Enter the exam end date.
* **exam_duration** (text, optional): Enter the exam duration.
* **communication_method** (select_multiple, optional): Select the communication method.
* **confirm_participant_status** (select_one, optional): Confirm the participant status.
* **confirm_exam_status** (select_one, optional): Confirm the exam status.
* **notes** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes.
