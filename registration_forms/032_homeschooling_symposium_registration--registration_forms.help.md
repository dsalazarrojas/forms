# Homeschooling Symposium Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Homeschooling Symposium Registration form is designed to collect necessary information from attendees who wish to participate in the symposium.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the sessions you would like to attend by selecting the relevant options in the "SessionChoices" field.
2. Enter the name of the session you wish to attend in the "SessionName" field.
3. Select the start and end time of the session you wish to attend in the "SessionStartTime" and "SessionEndtime" fields respectively.
4. Provide your name in the "AttendeeName" field.
5. Enter your valid email address in the "AttendeeEmail" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "AttendeePhone" field.
7. Enter your full address in the "AttendeeAddress" field.
8. Enter your zip code in the "AttendeeZip" field.
9. Enter your city in the "AttendeeCity" field.
10. Enter your state in the "AttendeeState" field.
11. Enter your country in the "AttendeeCountry" field, and select your response to whether or not you have family members who will be attending with you.
12. If a family member will be attending with you, enter their name in the "FamilyMember1Name" field.
13. Enter their email address in the "FamilyMember1Email" field.
14. Enter their phone number in the "FamilyMember1Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **SessionChoices** (select multiple): Select the sessions you wish to attend.
* **SessionName** (text): Enter the name of the session you wish to attend.
* **SessionStartTime** (date): Select the start time of the session you wish to attend.
* **SessionEndtime** (time): Select the end time of the session you wish to attend.
* **AttendeeName** (text): Provide your name.
* **AttendeeEmail** (email): Enter your valid email address.
* **AttendeePhone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **AttendeeAddress** (text): Enter your full address.
* **AttendeeZip** (number): Enter your zip code.
* **AttendeeCity** (text): Enter your city.
* **AttendeeState** (text): Enter your state.
* **AttendeeCountry** (select one): Enter your country and select if you have family members who will be attending with you.
* **FamilyMember1Name** (text): If a family member will be attending with you, enter their name.
* **FamilyMember1Email** (email): If a family member will be attending with you, enter their email address.
* **FamilyMember1Phone** (text): If a family member will be attending with you, enter their phone number.

Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information to avoid any issues with your registration.
