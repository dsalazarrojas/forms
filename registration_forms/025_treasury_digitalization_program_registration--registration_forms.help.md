# Treasury Digitalization Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Treasury Digitalization Program Registration form is for users to provide information about themselves and be included in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Input in the designated field.
2. Provide your Name as you would like it to be displayed in the program.
3. Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your Phone number for easy contact.
5. Select your Department from the provided options: Accounting, Auditing, Financial Management, or IT.
6. Enter your Position Title.
7. Choose one or more Roles: Team Lead, Manager, or Analyst.
8. Add any additional Notes you may have about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (id: 1, text): Enter a brief description of yourself or your project.
* **Name** (id: 2, text): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the program.
* **Email** (id: 3, email): Enter your valid email address for program communication.
* **Phone** (id: 4, text): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Department** (id: 5, select_one): Select the department you belong to: Accounting, Auditing, Financial Management, or IT.
* **Position Title** (id: 6, text): Enter your job title or position.
* **Role** (id: 7, select_multiple): Choose one or more roles: Team Lead, Manager, or Analyst.
* **Notes** (id: 8, note): Provide any additional information about yourself that you think is necessary.
