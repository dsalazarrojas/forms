# Fashion Design Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Fashion Design Admission Form is a crucial step in the application process for prospective students interested in joining the Fashion Design program. The form helps us get to know you better and determine whether you are a good fit for our program. By filling out this form, you will be able to showcase your skills, interests, and availability. This information will be used to assess your eligibility and provide a smooth onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you regarding your application.
3. Provide your phone number as a secondary contact method.
4. Select one language as your favorite from the drop-down menu.
5. Choose all languages you are proficient in from the multi-select menu.
6. Enter your date of birth to help us understand your age and qualifications.
7. Select your preferred time for the admission process and any other relevant meetings.
8. Add any notes or comments about your application in the notes field.
9. Finally, select your preferred language for communication and enter the date and time you would like to be admitted to the program.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (text)**: This field is for your given name. Please fill it in with your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: This field is for your surname. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address (email)**: This field is for your email address. We will use it to contact you about your application status.
* **Phone Number (text)**: This field is for your phone number. We will use it as a secondary contact method.
* **Favorite Language (select_one)**: This field asks for your favorite language, the one you have a special connection with or feel most comfortable with.
* **Languages Spoken (select_multiple)**: Choose all languages you are proficient in from the drop-down menu.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: This field asks for your date of birth, which helps us determine your age and qualifications.
* **Preferred Time (time)**: Select your preferred time for admission and other relevant meetings.
* **Notes (note)**: This field is for any additional comments or questions you have about the program or application process.
* **Preferred Language for Communication (select_one)**: Select the language you would like to use for communication with us.
* **Date of Admission (date)**: Enter the date you would like to be admitted to the program.
* **Preferred Time for Admission (time)**: Select your preferred time for admission to the program.
