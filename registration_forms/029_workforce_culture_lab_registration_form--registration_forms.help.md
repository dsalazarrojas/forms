# Workforce Culture Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect demographic and contact information from participants for the Workforce Culture Lab study.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each page of the form carefully, as indicated below.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before proceeding.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Page Field** (1st_page_field, text, required): Enter a brief description of your role within the organization.
* **Second Page Field** (2nd_page_field, number, required): Enter the number of people in your team.
* **Third Page Field** (3rd_page_field, date, optional): Enter a date of your choice (e.g., birthday, start date, etc.).
* **Fourth Page Field** (4th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your team.
* **Fifth Page Field** (5th_page_field, number, required): Enter the number of years you have worked in your current role.
* **Sixth Page Field** (6th_page_field, select_one, required): Select one of the options listed to describe your current role.
* **Seventh Page Field** (7th_page_field, select_multiple, optional): Select multiple options that apply to your current experience.
* **Eighth Page Field** (8th_page_field, note, required): Enter a note about your work experience.
* **Ninth Page Field** (9th_page_field, select_multiple, required): Select multiple options that apply to your current experience.
* **Tenth Page Field** (10th_page_field, time, optional): Enter a specific time of your choice (e.g., work hours, favorite time of day, etc.).
* **Eleventh Page Field** (11th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your work habits.
* **Twelfth Page Field** (12th_page_field, number, optional): Enter a numerical value (e.g., number of projects, hours worked, etc.).
* **Thirteenth Page Field** (13th_page_field, email, optional): Enter your email address (e.g., work email, personal email, etc.).
* **Fourteenth Page Field** (14th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your work.
* **Fifteenth Page Field** (15th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your team.
* **Sixteenth Page Field** (16th_page_field, note, required): Enter a note about your work experience.
* **Seventeenth Page Field** (17th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your work habits.
* **Eighteenth Page Field** (18th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your team.
* **Nineteenth Page Field** (19th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your work.
* **Twentieth Page Field** (20th_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your experience.
* **Twentyfirst Page Field** (21st_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your work.
* **Twentysecond Page Field** (22nd_page_field, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your experience.
