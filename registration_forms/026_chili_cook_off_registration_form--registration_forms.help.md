# Chili Cook Off Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants and teams for the Chili Cook Off competition. It is essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that you are properly registered and accounted for in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Participant or Team Name in the **Participant or Team Name** field.
2. Enter your **Contact Email** in the **Contact Email** field. This will be used for registration confirmation and any urgent communication.
3. Enter your **Contact Phone** number in the **Contact Phone** field. This will be used for any urgent communication.
4. If you have paid the **Entry Fee**, select the corresponding option (e.g., "Yes" or a numeric value for the amount paid). If you have not paid the entry fee, you can select "No" or leave it blank.
5. Choose your **Cooking Category** from the options provided (e.g., Traditional, Vegetarian, or Chili with a Twist).
6. If you are a team, you can list your team members in the **Team Members** field.
7. If you have any special **Equipment Needs**, you can list them in this field.
8. Finally, select the **Agree to Event Terms** option to confirm that you have read and agree to the competition guidelines and waivers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Participant or Team Name**
* **Participant or Team Name** (text, required, Participant/Team Name): Enter the name of the participant or team that will be competing in the Chili Cook Off competition.

### **Contact Email**
* **Contact Email** (email, required, Email for registration confirmation): Enter your email address where the competition organizers will send you the registration confirmation and any urgent communication.

### **Contact Phone**
* **Contact Phone** (text, required, Phone for urgent communication): Enter your phone number where the competition organizers can contact you in case of any urgent matters.

### **Entry Fee Paid**
* **Entry Fee Paid** (number/Yes/No, required, Enter amount or select payment status): Select whether you have paid the entry fee by choosing "Yes" or "No", or enter the amount paid.

### **Cooking Category**
* **Cooking Category** (select_one, required, Choose the category you will enter): Choose the category that best describes your chili cook-off entry (e.g., Traditional, Vegetarian, or Chili with a Twist).

### **Team Members**
* **Team Members** (text, optional, List names of team members): If you are a team, list the names of all team members.

### **Equipment Needs**
* **Equipment Needs** (text, optional, Note any equipment you will bring or need): If you have any special equipment needs or requirements, list them in this field.

### **Agree to Event Terms**
* **Agree to Event Terms** (select_one, required, Confirm you agree to event guidelines and waivers): Select "I agree" to confirm that you have read and agree to the competition guidelines and waivers.
