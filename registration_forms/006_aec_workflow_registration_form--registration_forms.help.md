# AEC Workflow Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect professional information from Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC) industry experts for the purpose of workflow registration. It helps us to place you in the right sessions and workshops during the event, based on your interests and expertise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Start by providing your company and professional information, including your name, company name, and professional discipline (e.g., Architecture, Engineering, etc.).
2. Provide your work email address and work phone number so we can contact you.
3. Select your primary discipline and software proficiency from the given options.
4. Indicate your experience level with BIM implementation.
5. Choose your preferred sessions and workshop (if applicable).
6. Provide emergency contact information for someone who can reach out to in case of an emergency.
7. If you have any additional requirements or comments, feel free to share them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Company Name** (text, required): Enter the name of your company.
* **Primary Discipline** (select_one, required): Select your primary discipline from the given options (e.g., Architecture, Structural Engineering, etc.).
* **Professional License / Membership ID** (text, optional): Enter your professional license or membership ID (e.g., AIA, PE, RIBA number).
* **Work Email Address** (email, required): Enter your work email address.
* **Work Phone Number** (phone, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Primary Software Used** (select_multiple, required): Select all the software you are proficient with (e.g., Autodesk Revit, AutoCAD, etc.).
* **Workflow Focus Areas** (select_multiple, required): Select the areas you wish to explore during the event (e.g., BIM Coordination, Generative Design, etc.).
* **BIM Implementation Experience Level** (select_one, required): Indicate your experience level with BIM implementation.
* **Select a Technical Workshop** (select_one, required): Choose one workshop for the afternoon block.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (phone, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Any additional requirements or comments?** (text, optional): Share any additional requirements or comments you may have.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
