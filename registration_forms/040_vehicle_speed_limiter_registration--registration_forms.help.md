# Vehicle Speed Limiter Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This guide will walk you through completing the Vehicle Speed Limiter Registration form. Please refer to this guide to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill out the form with the required details, starting with the "Owner Details" page.
3. Proceed to the "Vehicle Data" page and enter the relevant information.
4. On the "Speed Limiter Installation Information" page, select the correct speed limiter type.
5. Complete the "Speed Limiter Details" page by selecting the installation status and providing any additional notes.
6. Finally, review your submission before submitting it for review and processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Owner Details (1)**: Enter your name as the owner of the vehicle or the name of the person authorized to represent you.
* **Vehicle Data (2)**: Enter the vehicle's make, model, and other relevant details.
* **Speed Limiter Installation Information (3)**: Enter the type of speed limiter installed on your vehicle.
* **Speed Limiter Details (4)**: 
  * Select whether the speed limiter is "Installed", "Not Installed", or "Not Applicable" from the options provided.
  * Enter any additional notes or comments about the speed limiter installation.
* **Vehicle Number (5)**: Enter the vehicle's identification number, if applicable.
* **Owner Name (6)**: Enter your name as the owner of the vehicle or the name of the person authorized to represent you.
* **Owner Contact (7)**: Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you regarding any updates or concerns.
* **Email Address (8)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Speed Limiter Installation Status (9)**: 
  * Select whether the speed limiter is "Installed", "Not Installed", or "Not Applicable" from the options provided.
  * This will help us track the status of your speed limiter installation.
* **Notes (10)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the speed limiter installation or your vehicle.
