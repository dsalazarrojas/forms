# Nonprofit Fundraising Campaign Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Fundraising Campaign Registration Form is designed to collect information about fundraising campaigns for nonprofit organizations. This form is used to register and manage campaigns, including event details, participant information, donor details, contact information, donation amount, and terms of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with the relevant information about the event, such as the title, description, and date.
2. In the **Participant Details** section, provide information about the participants, including their name, role, and contact information.
3. The **Donor Info** section is for general information about the donor, such as their name and organization.
4. In the **Contact** section, provide the contact information for the donor, such as their email and phone number.
5. In the **Donation Amount** section, specify the donation amount and any relevant details.
6. For **Fund**, select whether the donation is part of a specific fund or not.
7. The **Event Terms** section is for general terms of service related to the event.
8. The **Participant Terms** section is for general terms of service related to the participants.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (1, text, required/optional): This section is for providing information about the event, such as the title, description, and date.
* **Participant Details** (2, text, required/optional): This section is for providing information about the participants, including their name, role, and contact information.
* **Donor Info** (3, text, required/optional): This section is for providing general information about the donor, such as their name and organization.
* **Contact** (4, text, required/optional): This section is for providing contact information for the donor, such as their email and phone number.
* **Donation Amount** (5, number, required/optional): This section is for specifying the donation amount and any relevant details.
* **Fund** (6, select_one, required/optional): This section is for selecting whether the donation is part of a specific fund or not.
* **Event Terms** (7, note, required/optional): This section is for providing general terms of service related to the event.
* **Participant Terms** (8, note, required/optional): This section is for providing general terms of service related to the participants.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that the form is complete and accurate.
* Use clear and concise language when providing information in each section.
* Double-check the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
