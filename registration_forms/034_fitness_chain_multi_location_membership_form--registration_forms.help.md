# Fitness Chain Multi Location Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect membership information from users who wish to join or become members of the fitness chain at multiple locations. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form's sections and fill in the required information for each section.
2. Start by filling out the "Main" section, which is the first page of the form.
3. Proceed to the "Additional Info" section to provide any additional information that may be required.
4. Review the form's content and submit it when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Main
* **Name** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect your name.
* **Email** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Membership Type** (`text`, required): This field is used to select your membership type.

### Additional Info
* **Additional Information** (`text`, optional): Use this field to provide any additional information that may be required by the fitness chain.

### Thank You
* **Thank You** (`text`, required): This field is used to confirm that you have completed the form.

### Review
* **Review** (`text`, required): This field is used to review your form submission before submission.

### Contact
* **Contact** (`text`, optional): This field is used to provide any additional contact information that may be required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a successful form submission.
* Review each section carefully before proceeding to the next.
* Use the "Additional Info" field to provide any additional information that may be required.
* Review your submission before submitting the form.
* Use the "Contact" field to provide any additional contact information if necessary.
