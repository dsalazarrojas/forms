# Junior Golf Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for the junior golf program. Please fill out the form with your child's and your own information to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your child's and your own information.
2. Choose the program type that best suits your child's needs.
3. Select the session you prefer for your child to participate in.
4. Enter your child's start and end dates for the program.
5. Provide your child's and your own contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name.
* **Email**: Enter your child's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your child's phone number.
* **Program**: Choose the junior golf program that suits your child's needs. (Select from Summer Junior Golf Program, After School Junior Golf Program, or Winter Junior Golf Program)
* **Session**: Select the session your child would like to participate in. (Select from Summer, After School, or Winter)
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the program for your child.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the program for your child.
* **Parent's First Name**: Enter your first name as the child's parent or guardian.
* **Parent's Last Name**: Enter your last name as the child's parent or guardian.
* **Parent's Email**: Enter your email address as the child's parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter an emergency contact person and their contact information, if applicable.
