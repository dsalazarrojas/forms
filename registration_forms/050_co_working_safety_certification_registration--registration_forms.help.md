# Co-Working Safety Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Co-Working Safety Certification program, which ensures that you have the necessary knowledge and understanding to safely use the co-working space.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Complete all the fields in each section, making sure to select the correct options for each field.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
3. Sign the form digitally to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Full Name** (`registrant_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Primary Co-working Location** (`coworking_location`, select_one, required): Select the primary location where you will be using the co-working space (Downtown, Uptown, East Side, or West Side).
* **Session Details** (`section_session`, note, required): This field is a note for session details. Please enter any additional information about your preferred safety certification session.
* **Preferred Safety Certification Session Date** (`preferred_session_date`, date, required): Enter your preferred safety certification session date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Preferred Session Time Slot** (`session_time_slot`, select_one, required): Select your preferred session time slot (10:00 AM, 2:00 PM, or 6:00 PM).
* **Contact and Emergency** (`section_contact_emergency`, note, required): This field is a note for contact and emergency information. Please enter any additional details about your emergency contact.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for updates and information.
* **Mobile Phone Number** (`mobile_phone`, text, required): Enter your mobile phone number where you can be contacted in case of emergencies.
* **Emergency Contact Full Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Policy Acknowledgment** (`section_policy_ack`, note, required): This field is a note for policy acknowledgment. Please review the building's fire safety protocol and emergency evacuation plan.
* **I Have Read and Reviewed the Building Fire Safety Protocol** (`fire_safety_protocol_ack`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to indicate that you have reviewed the building's fire safety protocol.
* **I Understand the Designated Emergency Evacuation Plan** (`evacuation_plan_ack`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to indicate that you understand the emergency evacuation plan.
* **I Agree to Comply with All Building Safety and Security Regulations** (`safety_regulations_agreement`, select_one, required): Select 'True' to indicate that you agree to comply with all building safety and security regulations.
* **Digital Signature for Registration Verification** (`digital_signature`, text, required): Please enter your digital signature for registration verification.
* **Date of Registration Submission** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of your registration submission in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

Remember to review each field carefully and complete the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process. If you need help or clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to reach out to our support team.
