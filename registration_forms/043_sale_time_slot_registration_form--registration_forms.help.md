# Sale Time Slot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sale Time Slot Registration Form is used to register a sale event with the customer's information, including their name, email, phone number, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the customer's name in the "Customer Name" field.
2. Enter the customer's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the customer's phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select the desired "Time Slot" from the provided options.
5. Enter the sale date in the "Sale Date" field.
6. Enter the sale time in the "Sale Time" field.
7. If applicable, select the "Opt-out Email" options to specify which emails the customer should not receive.
8. Enter any additional "Customer Note" or "Customer Phone" information, if needed.
9. Confirm the "Sale Time Slot" selection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Name**: Enter the customer's full name to identify them in the system. Required.
* **Email**: Enter the customer's email address to send them notifications and updates. Required.
* **Phone**: Enter the customer's phone number to contact them, if needed. Required.
* **Time Slot**: Select the desired time slot for the sale event. Required.
* **Date**: Enter the sale date for the event. Required.
* **Sale Time**: Enter the sale time for the event. Required.
* **Opt-out Email**: Select which emails the customer does not want to receive. Optional.
* **Customer Note**: Enter any additional notes about the customer. Optional.
* **Customer Phone**: Enter any additional phone number information for the customer. Optional.
* **Sale Time Slot**: Select the time slot for the sale event. Required.
* **Sale Date**: Enter the sale date for the event. Required.
* **Sale Time**: Enter the sale time for the event. Required. 

## Tips
* Double-check the customer's information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that you select the correct time slot and sale date for the event.
* If you need to contact the customer, be sure to use their provided phone number.
* If you have any doubts or questions, please consult with the sales team.
