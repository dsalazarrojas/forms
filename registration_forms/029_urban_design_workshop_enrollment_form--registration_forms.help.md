# Urban Design Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Design Workshop Enrollment Form is used to register and enroll attendees in an Urban Design Workshop. This form collects information necessary to facilitate workshop logistics and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that your registration information is accurate and consistent throughout the form.
* Review your submission before submitting to ensure it is complete and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for additional communication and contact purposes.

Note: Fields marked as required: false are not mandatory but are recommended for proper registration and communication purposes.
