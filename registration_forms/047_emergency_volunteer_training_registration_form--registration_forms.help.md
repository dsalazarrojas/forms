# emergency_volunteer_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for emergency volunteers who need to register for training. It is used to gather information about the volunteer and their preferences.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred communication method in the "Preferred Communication Method" field.
6. Select your preferred training location in the "Preferred Training Location" field.
7. Indicate any medical conditions you have in the "Medical Conditions" field.
8. If you have any medical certifications, enter them in the "Medical Certifications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name in this field.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address. This will be used for communication.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Communication Method (Select One)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Preferred Training Location (Select Multiple)**: Select the locations you are available to be trained at. Options are "Yes" and "No".
* **Medical Conditions (Select Multiple)**: If you have any medical conditions, check the relevant box(es).
* **Medical Certifications (Text)**: If you have any medical certifications, enter them here.
