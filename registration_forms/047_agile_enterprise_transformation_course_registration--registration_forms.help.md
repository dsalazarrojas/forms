# Agile Enterprise Transformation Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Agile Enterprise Transformation Course. Please fill out the form with your relevant information to secure your spot in the course.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form to ensure you have all necessary information ready.
2. Enter your participant name and email address.
3. Select your attendee type (Individual, Team, Executive, or Sponsor).
4. Choose the sessions you will attend.
5. Select a payment method (Invoice, Credit card, or Bank transfer).
6. If applicable, provide any special requirements or access needs.
7. Agree to the course terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name for registration.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): If you represent an organization, enter your employer or sponsoring organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for course materials and updates.
* **Role in Organization** (`role_in_organization`, text, optional): Enter your job title or function.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, select_one, required): Choose the category that best describes your attendance: Individual, Team, Executive, or Sponsor.
* **Sessions Selected** (`sessions_selected`, select_multiple, required): Select the sessions or tracks you intend to attend.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you will pay: Invoice, Credit card, or Bank transfer.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special needs or access restrictions, describe them here.
* **Agree to Course Terms and Conditions** (`agree_terms`, select_one, required): Agree or indicate your stance on the course terms and conditions.
