# Business Networking Event Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from attendees for the business networking events. It is crucial to fill it out accurately for a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields (marked as `required` in the form). These fields are essential for processing your registration.
2. Make sure to select the correct event from the dropdown list.
3. Provide accurate information, especially for the event date.
4. For optional fields, feel free to provide as much detail as you'd like.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name**: Enter your full name as it should appear.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for event updates and confirmations.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number if you'd like to be contacted.
* **Company Name**: Enter your organization name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Select Event**: Choose the event you're interested in attending.
* **Event Date**: Select the date of the event you've chosen.
* **Number of Tickets**: Specify how many attendees will attend.
* **Additional Attendee Names**: Enter the names of any additional attendees.
* **Ticket Type**: Choose the type of admission you require (e.g. General, VIP, etc.).
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method (e.g. Credit card, Debit card, etc.).
* **Dietary Requirements**: If you have any food allergies or restrictions, please specify.
* **Networking Preferences**: Indicate the types of connections you're interested in (e.g. Potential clients, partners, etc.).
* **Elevator Pitch**: Briefly describe your business for others to know.
* **Business Card Availability**: Indicate if you'll bring business cards.
* **Session Preferences**: Choose the types of sessions you're interested in attending.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you require any accommodations, please specify.
* **Open to Receiving Referrals**: Indicate if you're open to business referrals.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Indicate if you're interested in volunteering at future events.
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Specify the source of your event information.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information about your attendance.
