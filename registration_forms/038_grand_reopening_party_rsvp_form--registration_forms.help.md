# grand_reopening_party_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to RSVP to a grand reopening party. It is used to gather information about who will be attending and their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the main page, simply type your name and confirm whether you will be attending the party.
2. On the second page, provide your guest's details if you are bringing someone with you.
3. On the third page, enter your contact information so we can get in touch with you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Main Page (required)**: This is the first page of the form where you will be asked to type your name and confirm whether you will be attending the party.
* **Guest Details (optional)**: If you are bringing someone with you, provide their details here. This is a separate field where you can enter their name and any other relevant information.
* **Contact Info (optional)**: Enter your contact information here so we can get in touch with you about the party. This includes your name, email address, and phone number.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (Main Page) to ensure we have your RSVP.
* If you are bringing a guest, please make sure to fill out their details as well.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
