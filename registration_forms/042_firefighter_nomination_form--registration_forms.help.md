# Firefighter Nomination Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Firefighter Nomination Form is a tool used to recognize and reward outstanding firefighter efforts. It is essential to complete this form accurately to ensure that the correct firefighter is being recognized and that the nomination is complete and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the Fire Department that the nominee is associated with from the list of options.
2. Enter the Fire Fighter's name in the required field.
3. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the nomination in the "Reason for Nomination" field.
4. Choose the date and time of the nomination from the calendar or enter it manually in the "Nomination Date/Time" field.
5. Select the Fire Station(s) that the nominee is associated with from the list of options.
6. Enter the Nominator's name and title in the respective fields.
7. Choose the Nominator's department from the list of options.
8. Enter the Nominator's email and phone number in the respective fields.
9. Optionally, select the Fire Station(s) that the Nominator is associated with.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Fire Department** (`fire_department`): Select the Fire Department that the nominee is associated with.
* **Fire Fighter Name** (`fire_fighter_name`): Enter the name of the firefighter being nominated.
* **Nominee Title** (`nominee_title`): Enter the title of the nominee, if applicable.
* **Reason for Nomination** (`reason_for_nomination`): Provide a brief explanation for the reason of the nomination.
* **Nomination Date** (`nomination_date`): Choose the date of the nomination from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Nomination Reason** (`nomination_reason`): Enter any additional information about the nomination, if necessary.
* **Fire Station** (`fire_station`): Select the Fire Station(s) that the nominee is associated with.
* **Nomination Date/Time** (`nomination_date_time`): Choose the date and time of the nomination from the calendar or enter it manually.
* **Nominator Name** (`nominator_name`): Enter the name of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Title** (`nominator_title`): Enter the title of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Department** (`nominator_department`): Choose the department of the person making the nomination from the list of options.
* **Nominator Email** (`nominator_email`): Enter the email of the person making the nomination.
* **Nominator Phone** (`nominator_phone`): Enter the phone number of the person making the nomination.
* **Fire Station 1** (`fire_station_1`): Select the first fire station associated with the nominee.
* **Fire Station 2** (`fire_station_1`): Select the second fire station associated with the nominee.
