# Library Membership Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Membership Registration Form is used by library patrons to register for membership at the library. This form helps the library to collect necessary information from patrons to provide them access to library resources and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Library Name field with the name of the library you are registering for.
2. Enter your Member ID, which is a unique identifier assigned to you by the library.
3. Provide your Email address, which will be used for communication and membership updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Library Name** (`library_name`, text, required)
	+ Enter the name of the library you are registering for.
* **Member ID** (`member_id`, number, required)
	+ Enter your unique Member ID assigned to you by the library.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required)
	+ Enter your Email address, which will be used for communication and membership updates.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are using this form for library membership registration only.
* If you are experiencing any issues or have questions, please contact your library staff for assistance.
