# Stunts Tumble Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stunts Tumble Registration Form is designed to collect basic contact information for users who wish to participate in the event. This form is used to register new users and store their data for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Provide a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
* Ensure that the event date is in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
* Double-check that the information provided is correct before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your organization.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for additional contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address for delivery purposes.
* **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, select_one, optional): Select your state of residence from the drop-down list.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, optional): Enter your zip code for delivery purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact us at [support email].
* This form is for individual registrations only, multiple submissions will not be accepted.
