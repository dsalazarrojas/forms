# event_registration_form_with_sofort_payment_method - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering events and offers the option to select payment methods for payment processing. The form collects information such as event registration, payment method (Sofort or PayPal), name, email, and phone number (optional). The collected data is used for event registration and payment processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred payment method from the options (Sofort or PayPal).
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Enter your email in the "email" field.
4. If you want to provide your phone number, enter it in the "phone" field (optional).
5. Enter any additional notes about your registration in the "note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **event_registration_form** (`event_registration_form`, text, required: false): This is the main event registration form.
* **sofort_payment_method** (`sofort_payment_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose between Sofort and PayPal as your preferred payment method.
* **name** (`name`, text, required: true): Enter your name in this field.
* **email** (`email`, email, required: true): Enter your email in this field.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you want to provide it (optional).
* **note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your registration in this field (optional).

## Tips

* Make sure to select a valid payment method and enter your name and email correctly.
* If you're having trouble with the payment method selection, contact support for assistance.
* Note: This form is for event registration purposes only. Do not use this form for other purposes.
