# Financial Governance Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for financial governance sessions. It collects essential details such as name, contact information, and financial expertise to ensure an effective session experience.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address accurately.
2. Enter your Phone number.
3. Describe your Financial Background.
4. Provide your Company Name.
5. Specify your Position.
6. Choose a session date and time.
7. Select your Financial Expertise, Experience, and Industry.
8. Select your preferred Registration Type (Financial Governance Session, Other, Both, or No Preference).
9. Choose relevant Session Topics (Financial Planning, Risk Management, Financial Analysis, Financial Modeling, or Financial Reporting).
10. Confirm whether you will be attending the session Online, In-person, or Both.
11. Choose your preferred Payment Method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
12. Enter your Payment Terms.
13. Enter the Attendee's Name.
14. Confirm your Email Opt-in preference (True or False).
15. Confirm your Email Confirmation preference (True or False).
16. Finally, submit the form or cancel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required)
	+ A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (required)
	+ A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email** (required)
	+ Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone** (required)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Financial Background** (required)
	+ Describe your financial background and expertise.
* **Company** (required)
	+ Enter the name of your company.
* **Position** (required)
	+ Specify your position in the company.
* **Session Date** (required)
	+ Choose the date you plan to attend the session.
* **Session Time** (required)
	+ Select the time slot for the session.
* **Financial Expertise** (required)
	+ Describe your financial expertise (e.g., accounting, finance, economics).
* **Financial Experience** (required)
	+ Describe your financial experience (e.g., years of work experience).
* **Financial Industry** (required)
	+ Specify your area of financial expertise (e.g., banking, insurance).
* **Comments** (optional)
	+ Enter any additional comments about your session preferences.
* **Registration Type** (required)
	+ Choose whether you prefer to register for the Financial Governance Session, Other, Both, or No Preference.
* **Session Topics** (required)
	+ Select relevant topics from the provided options (e.g., Financial Planning, Risk Management).
* **Submit** (optional)
	+ Submit the form to register for the session.
* **Cancel** (optional)
	+ Cancel the form if you have not completed it accurately.
* **Confirm** (required)
	+ Confirm your attendance (True or False).
* **Agreement** (required)
	+ Agree to the terms and conditions (True, False, or Undisclosed).
* **Session Attendance** (required)
	+ Choose whether you prefer to attend online, in-person, or both.
* **Payment Method** (required)
	+ Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Payment Terms** (required)
	+ Enter the payment terms and conditions.
* **Attendee Name** (required)
	+ Enter the name of the attendee.
* **Email Opt-in** (optional)
	+ Choose whether you want to be contacted by email (True or False).
* **Email Confirmation** (optional)
	+ Choose whether you want email confirmation (True or False).
