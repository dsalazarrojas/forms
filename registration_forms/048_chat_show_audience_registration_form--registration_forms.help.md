# Chat Show Audience Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential attendees to help the chat show host and organizers prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first name, last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide the show information you are interested in, including the show's name and date.
3. Choose the correct time and attendance type (in-person or virtual) for the show.
4. If you are attending with a group, specify the number of people and provide their names.
5. Select your seating preference and let us know about any mobility or accessibility needs.
6. Mention any dietary restrictions or special requests.
7. If you have a question or topic for the chat host, feel free to include it.
8. Finally, choose how you heard about the show and provide the registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Provide your email address for show updates and information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number in case we need to reach out.
* **Date of Birth**: (Optional) Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Chat Show Name**: Enter the name of the chat show you are interested in attending.
* **Show Date**: Enter the date of the show in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Show Time**: Enter the time the show starts in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Number of Guests in Your Party**: If you are attending with a group, enter the number of people.
* **Names of Other Attendees**: If you are attending with a group, list the names of all attendees.
* **Type of Attendance**: Choose whether you will be attending in-person or virtually.
* **Seating Preference**: Select your preferred seating location, or choose "No Preference" if you're open to any seat.
* **Mobility or Accessibility Accommodation**: If you have any mobility or accessibility needs, mention them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any food allergies or restrictions, mention them here.
* **Question or Topic for Chat Host**: If you have a question or topic you'd like to discuss, include it here.
* **How Did You Hear About This Show**: Choose how you heard about the show.
* **Registration Date**: This will automatically be filled out with the current date.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled out for your registration to be complete.
