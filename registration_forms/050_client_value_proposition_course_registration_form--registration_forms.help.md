# Client Value Proposition Course Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Client Value Proposition Course Registration Form is used to collect information from students registering for the Client Value Proposition course. This form helps us understand your background, experience, and course preferences to ensure you receive the best learning experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the fields provided.
2. Enter your valid Email Address to receive course updates and information.
3. Provide your Phone Number for contact purposes.
4. Select your current Job Title and Department (if applicable).
5. Choose your Experience Level with Value Proposition (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
6. Select the course(s) you wish to register for (Fundamentals of Value Proposition, Advanced Value Proposition Strategy, Value Proposition for Sales, Value Proposition for Marketing, or Other).
7. Choose your preferred course format (Online self-paced, Live webinar, In-person workshop, or Blended format).
8. Select your preferred course dates (if available).
9. Specify your Timezone (if in-person).
10. Briefly describe what you hope to learn from the course in the "What Do You Hope to Learn?" field.
11. If applicable, mention any value proposition challenges you face in your current role.
12. Specify your Industry or Sector.
13. If registering with colleagues, provide the number of team members attending.
14. If attending an in-person course, indicate any dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
15. Confirm if you have taken courses with us before.
16. Specify how you heard about this course (Email, Website, Referral, Social media, Advertisement, or Other).
17. Finally, confirm that you agree to the course terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address to receive course updates and information.
- **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, Text, Optional): Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
- **Job Title** (`job_title`, Text, Optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
- **Department** (`department`, Text, Optional): Enter your department name (if applicable).
- **Experience Level with Value Proposition** (`experience_level`, Select One, Required): Choose your experience level with Value Proposition (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
- **Which Course Are You Registering For?** (`course_selection`, Select One, Required): Select the course(s) you wish to register for.
- **Preferred Course Format** (`course_format`, Select One, Required): Choose your preferred course format (Online self-paced, Live webinar, In-person workshop, or Blended format).
- **Preferred Course Dates** (`preferred_dates`, Date, Optional): Select your preferred course dates (if available).
- **Your Timezone** (`timezone`, Text, Optional): If attending an in-person course, specify your timezone for accommodation purposes.
- **What Do You Hope to Learn?** (`learning_objectives`, Text, Optional): Briefly describe what you hope to learn from the course.
- **Current Challenges in Your Role** (`current_role_challenges`, Text, Optional): If applicable, mention any value proposition challenges you face in your current role.
- **Industry or Sector** (`industry`, Text, Optional): Enter your industry or sector (if applicable).
- **Number of Team Members Registering** (`team_members`, Number, Optional): If registering with colleagues, provide the number of team members attending.
- **Any Dietary Restrictions (if in-person)** (`dietary_restrictions`, Text, Optional): If attending an in-person course, indicate any dietary restrictions.
- **Any Accessibility Needs or Accommodations** (`accessibility_needs`, Text, Optional): If applicable, specify any accessibility needs or accommodations.
- **Have You Taken Courses with Us Before?** (`prior_courses`, Select One, Optional): Confirm if you have taken courses with us before.
- **How Did You Hear About This Course?** (`how_heard`, Select One, Optional): Specify how you heard about this course (Email, Website, Referral, Social media, Advertisement, or Other).
- **I Agree to the Course Terms and Conditions** (`terms_agreement`, Select One, Required): Confirm that you agree to the course terms and conditions.
