# Shipping Discount Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for business customers to register for shipping discounts. Businesses can fill out this form to provide their shipping details and qualify for discounts on their orders.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please ensure you are completing this form on behalf of your business.
2. Enter your Business Name.
3. Enter your Street Address.
4. Enter your City.
5. Enter your Zip Code.
6. Enter your Country.
7. Enter your Contact Email.
8. Enter your Contact Phone (optional).
9. Select whether you are eligible for shipping discounts (Yes/No).
10. Enter your Shipping Discount Rate (if eligible).
11. Enter the start date of your shipping discount (if eligible).
12. Enter the end date of your shipping discount (if eligible).
13. Enter your shipping cost (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Business Name (Business Name, text, required)**: Enter the name of your business as it appears on your business cards or official documents.
* **Street Address (Street Address, text, required)**: Enter the street address of your business.
* **City (City, text, required)**: Enter the city of your business.
* **Zip Code (Zip Code, text, required)**: Enter your business's zip code.
* **Country (Country, text, required)**: Enter the country of your business location.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email, email, required)**: Enter the email address of your business contact.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone, text, optional)**: Enter the phone number of your business contact (optional).
* **Shipping Discount Type (Shipping Discount Type, select_multiple, optional)**: Select 'Yes' if your business is eligible for shipping discounts.
* **Shipping Discount Rate (Shipping Discount Rate, number, optional)**: Enter the shipping discount rate you are eligible for (if you selected 'Yes' in the previous field).
* **Shipping Discount Start Date (Shipping Discount Start Date, date, optional)**: Enter the start date of your shipping discount (if you selected 'Yes' in the previous field).
* **Shipping Discount End Date (Shipping Discount End Date, date, optional)**: Enter the end date of your shipping discount (if you selected 'Yes' in the previous field).
* **Shipping Cost (Shipping Cost, number, optional)**: Enter your shipping cost (optional).

## Tips
Make sure to fill out the form accurately to avoid delays in processing your shipping discounts. If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our support team for assistance.
