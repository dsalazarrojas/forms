# Mining Operations Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Mining Operations Registration" form is used to collect information about mining operations. Fill out this form to provide details about your company and its mining sites.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the company details section (company name, type, contact person).
2.  Provide site information (site name, type, status, description).
3.  Enter contact information (email, phone).
4.  Add operational details (site address, phone, email).
5.  Select the sharing options to notify others about the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the company.
*   **Company Type** (`company_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of company (corporation, partnership, or sole proprietorship).
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the contact person's name.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the contact email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number.
*   **Site Name** (`site_name`, `text`, required): Enter the site's name.
*   **Site Type** (`site_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the site type (open pit, open pit with heap leach, open pit with plant, underground, or underground with mill).
*   **Site Status** (`site_status`, `text`, required): Enter the site status.
*   **Site Description** (`site_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the site.
*   **Supporting Documentation** (`site_documentation`, `text`, required): Attach any supporting documentation for the site.
*   **Operational Status** (`operational_status`, `text`, required): Enter the operational status.
*   **Operational Description** (`operational_description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of the operations.
*   **Operational Documentation** (`operational_documentation`, `text`, required): Attach any operational documentation.
*   **Operational Address** (`operational_address`, `text`, required): Enter the operational address.
*   **Operational Phone** (`operational_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the operational phone number.
*   **Operational Email** (`operational_email`, `email`, required): Enter the operational email address.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click submit to complete the form.
*   **Share** (`share`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the sharing options to notify others about the registration.

Please fill out the required fields and provide any additional information as needed.
