# Shopping Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information about a shopping event, including company details, event scheduling, and representative contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow the steps below:
1. Enter the company name and email in the respective fields.
2. Provide the company phone number and event date (including start and end times) as per the required fields.
3. Fill in the company representative's name, email, and phone number (if applicable).
4. Indicate whether or not the company representative will be available to attend the event, and if not, provide alternative contact information or details.
5. Select 'Yes' or 'No' for event status (whether or not the event is confirmed), and provide any follow-up information if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Shopping Event Registration** (1, Shopping Event Registration): This is the main heading of the form.
* **Company Name** (2, Company Name): Enter the name of the company associated with the shopping event.
* **Company Email** (3, Company Email): Enter the email of the company's representative or contact person.
* **Company Phone** (4, Company Phone): Enter the phone number of the company's representative or contact person.
* **Event Date** (5, Event Date): Enter the date of the shopping event in the format MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **Start Time** (6, Start Time): Enter the start time of the shopping event in the format HH:MM or HH:MM AM/PM.
* **End Time** (7, End Time): Enter the end time of the shopping event in the format HH:MM or HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Event Details** (8, Event Details): Provide any additional information about the shopping event, such as location or event description.
* **Company Representative Name** (9, Company Representative Name): Enter the name of the company representative or contact person (if applicable).
* **Company Representative Email** (10, Company Representative Email): Enter the email of the company representative or contact person (if applicable).
* **Company Representative Phone** (11, Company Representative Phone): Enter the phone number of the company representative or contact person (if applicable).
* **Company Representative Address** (12, Company Representative Address): Enter the address of the company representative or contact person (if applicable).
* **Company Representative Notes** (13, Company Representative Notes): Provide any additional information about the company representative.
* **Attendee Information** (14, Attendee Information): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether or not the company representative will attend the event. If 'No', provide alternative contact information or details.
* **Event Status** (15, Event Status): Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether or not the event is confirmed.
* **Follow-up Information** (16, Follow-up Information): Provide any additional information about the follow-up process or contacts.
* **Follow-up Details** (17, Follow-up Details): Provide any additional details about the follow-up process.
* **Follow-up Contact Information** (18, Follow-up Contact Information): Enter any contact information about the follow-up, such as phone number or address.
