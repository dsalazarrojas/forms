# sat_course_enrollment_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sat_course_enrollment_form_template is designed for students to register for SAT courses. This form collects essential information from students to complete their registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all required and optional fields completed.
2. Fill out the Student Info page with your details.
3. Provide payment information on the Payment Information page.
4. Choose a course from the Select a Course dropdown menu on the Course Selection page.
5. Enter your email address on the Email page.
6. Fill out the Phone page with your contact phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Student Info** (`1`, `Student Info`, required/optional: false): Fill out this page with your name, date of birth, and any other student information required for registration.
- **Payment Information** (`2`, `Payment Information`, required/optional: false): Enter your payment details, including card number, expiration date, and security code.
- **Course Selection** (`3`, `Course Selection`, required/optional: false): Choose a course from the list, such as SAT Prep, to select your desired course.
- **Select a Course** (`4`, `Select a Course`, required/optional: false): This is the actual select menu for the Course Selection page, where you will choose one of the listed courses, like SAP 101: SAT Prep.
- **Email** (`5`, `Email`, required/optional: false): Enter your email address to receive updates and confirmations.
- **Phone** (`6`, `Phone`, required/optional: false): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check your payment details before submitting the form.
- Ensure you select the correct course for your needs.
- Review your entered information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you encounter any errors, try resubmitting the form with correct information.
