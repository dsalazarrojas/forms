# Youth Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for parents or guardians to register their child for a youth group program. It collects necessary information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for both the child and the parent(s)/guardian(s).

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with your child's information.
2. Enter the required information for the parent/guardian(s).
3. Provide the emergency contact information.
4. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, Text, Required): Enter your child's first name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, Text, Required): Enter your child's last name as it is written on their identification documents.
* **Email** (Email, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Parent's First Name** (Parent's First Name, Text, Required): Enter the parent/guardian's first name.
* **Parent's Last Name** (Parent's Last Name, Text, Required): Enter the parent/guardian's last name.
* **Phone** (Phone, Text, Required): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Date** (Date, Date, Required): Enter the date of birth of your child.
* **Age** (Age, Number, Required): Enter your child's age.
* **Grade Level** (Grade Level, Select One, Required): Select your child's grade level at school.
* **School Name** (School Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the school your child attends.
* **Teacher's Name** (Teacher's Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of your child's teacher.
* **Guardian's Name** (Guardian's Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the parent/guardian that will be attending with your child.
* **Address** (Address, Text, Required): Enter the parent/guardian's home address.
* **Street** (Street, Text, Required): Enter the street address of the parent/guardian's home.
* **City** (City, Text, Required): Enter the city where the parent/guardian lives.
* **State** (State, Text, Required): Enter the state where the parent/guardian lives.
* **Zip** (Zip, Text, Required): Enter the zip code of the parent/guardian's home.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, Text, Required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship** (Relationship, Select One, Required): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person (Parent, Guardian, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, Text, Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Extension** (Extension, Text, Optional): Enter any extension for the emergency contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Cell** (Emergency Contact Cell, Text, Optional): Enter the cell phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Relationship Extension** (Relationship Extension, Select One, Optional): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person's phone extension (None, Home, Work, Other).
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Emergency Contact Address, Text, Optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Note** (Emergency Contact Note, Note, Optional): Enter any additional information about the emergency contact person.
