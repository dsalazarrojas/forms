# crisis_management_planning_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The crisis management planning workshop registration form is a tool designed to collect participant information for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with your personal details, including your name, email, and any other relevant information.
2. Provide your organization's information, including your organization's name, address, phone number, and email.
3. Select the participant type and confirm your participation.
4. Provide any additional notes or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration** (`registration`, text, required: false): This is the main registration field where you can enter your registration information.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your participant details, including your name, job title, department, location, and any additional notes.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required: false): Enter the details of the attendee, including their name, email, phone, and job title.
* **Attendee Info** (`attendee_info`, select_one, required: false): Select the attendee's information, including Option 1 or Other.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm your participation by selecting Confirm 1 or Confirm 2.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the participant type, including Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's phone number.
* **Participant Organization** (`participant_organization`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's organization name.
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's job title.
* **Participant Department** (`participant_department`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's department.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, text, required: false): Enter the participant's location.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments about the participant.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's name.
* **Organization Address** (`organization_address`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's address.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the organization's phone number.
* **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, email, required: false): Enter the organization's email address.
* **Confirm Organization** (`confirm_organization`, select_multiple, required: false): Confirm the organization's information by selecting Confirm 1 or Confirm 2.
