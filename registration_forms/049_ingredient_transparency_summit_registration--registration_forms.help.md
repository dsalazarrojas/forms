# Ingredient Transparency Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ingredient Transparency Summit Registration form is a two-page form designed to gather information from attendees for the summit. The form collects attendee details, session preferences, and consent to ensure a smooth and informed experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Begin by filling out the Registration Form (form_1) which is optional.
2. Proceed to the Attendee Details (attendee_details) page where you will provide information about yourself.
3. Select your preferred session in the Session Preference (session_preferences) page.
4. On the Consent (consent) page, select Yes or No to indicate your consent.
5. If you wish to provide additional information, fill out the Form 5 (form_5) page.
6. Finally, provide further details about yourself on the Attendee (attendee) page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (form_1)** (`form_1`, text, optional): This is an optional introductory page to the form.
* **Attendee Details (attendee_details)** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name, contact information, and any other relevant details about yourself.
* **Session Preference (session_preferences)** (`session_preferences`, select multiple, optional): Select your preferred session by choosing "Yes" for the session you prefer and "No" for the session you do not prefer.
* **Consent (consent)** (`consent`, select one, required): Select "Yes" to indicate your consent, or "No" if you do not wish to proceed with your registration.
* **Form 5 (form_5)** (`form_5`, text, optional): This is an optional page for any additional information you may wish to provide.
* **Attendee (attendee)** (`attendee`, text, optional): Please provide any further details about yourself that you did not include in the Attendee Details page.

## Tips
* Please be as detailed as possible in your responses.
* Make sure to select your preferred session and indicate your consent to complete the registration process.
* If you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact us.
