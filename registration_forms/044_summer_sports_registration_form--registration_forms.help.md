# Summer Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Sports Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants to ensure their successful and safe involvement in summer sports activities. This form will help us better understand your background, emergency contact details, and medical conditions, which are crucial for our staff to provide a comfortable and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Carefully read through each page to ensure you understand the questions and fields.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately, marking "N/A" or "Not Applicable" for fields that do not apply to you.
3. Double-check your answers for any errors or omissions.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that you regularly use, so we can contact you about the sports program.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, number, optional): Enter your primary phone number where we can reach you in case of emergencies or important updates.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): List any existing medical conditions that may impact your participation in sports activities. This will help us ensure your safety and well-being.
* **Athletic Background** (`athletic_background`, text, optional): Briefly describe your athletic experience or background, including any previous sports participation or training.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person, such as a parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, number, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Sports Experience** (`sports_experience`, text, optional): Describe any relevant sports experience or skills you have, including any previous coaching or training certifications.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Specify your relationship with the emergency contact person (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse, or friend).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you accurately represent yourself and your emergency contact.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, contact us before submitting the form.
* Once you have submitted the form, we will review your information and be in touch with you soon.
