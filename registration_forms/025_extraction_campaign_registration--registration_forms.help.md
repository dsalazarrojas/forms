# Extraction Campaign Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Extraction Campaign Registration form is designed to collect participant information, preferred dates, and campaign specifics for an extraction campaign.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form carefully, ensuring that all required fields are completed accurately. Some fields are optional and can be left blank.
2. Review your selections carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
3. If you encounter any issues or errors, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter your participant details, as this is the primary information for the campaign.
* **Campaign Type**: Select the type of campaign you are participating in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **Preferred Dates**: Enter your preferred dates for the campaign.
* **Consent**: Select any applicable options to indicate your consent for various aspects of the campaign.
* **Participant Email**: Enter your participant email address (this field is not strictly required but is used for communication).
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your participant phone number (this field is not strictly required but is used for communication).
* **Campaign Start Date**: Enter the start date of the campaign.
* **Campaign End Date**: Enter the end date of the campaign.
* **Workshop Duration**: Enter the duration of the workshop.
* **Workshop Type**: Select the type of workshop you are participating in (e.g., Option 1, Option 2).
* **No Tool**: Select the tools you are not using for the campaign (e.g., Option 1, Other).
* **No Tool 2 to 15**: Select the tools you are not using for the campaign (similar to "No Tool" above).

Note: This form is subject to changes, and new fields might be added or removed in the future. Please refer back to this guide for the most up-to-date information.
