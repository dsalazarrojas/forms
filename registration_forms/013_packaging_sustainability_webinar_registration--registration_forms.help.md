# Packaging Sustainability Webinar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Packaging Sustainability Webinar is a web-based event focused on sustainability in packaging.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee name in the `Attendee Name` field (ID 1). This is a free-form text field where you can enter your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your email address in the `Email Address` field (ID 2). This will be used for communication about the event and any further correspondence.
3. If you work for a company, enter your company name in the `Company Name` field (ID 3). This is optional.
4. If you have a role within your company related to sustainability or packaging, select it from the `Role` dropdown menu (ID 4). This helps us understand your interests and tailor the event experience.
5. Select all that apply for the `Interest` question (ID 5). This helps us understand your interests related to sustainability and packaging.
6. Check the box to confirm your consent (ID 6) for us to share your information with the event organizers.
7. Provide any additional comments or questions you may have in the `Additional Comments` field (ID 7). This is optional.
8. Enter your contact number in the `Contact Number` field (ID 8). This is required for us to reach you if we need to.
9. Select your time zone from the dropdown menu in the `Time Zone` field (ID 9). This helps ensure we schedule the webinar at a time that suits your schedule.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name (ID 1)**: A text field for you to enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address (ID 2)**: Enter your email address for event communication and correspondence.
* **Company Name (ID 3)**: If you work for a company, enter your company name here. This is optional.
* **Role (ID 4)**: Select your role within your company related to sustainability or packaging.
* **Interest (ID 5)**: Select all that apply that relate to your interests in sustainability and packaging.
* **Consent (ID 6)**: Check the box to confirm you consent for us to share your information with the event organizers.
* **Additional Comments (ID 7)**: Enter any additional comments or questions you may have about the event.
* **Contact Number (ID 8)**: Enter your contact number for us to reach you if needed.
* **Time Zone (ID 9)**: Select your time zone for scheduling the webinar at a suitable time for you.
