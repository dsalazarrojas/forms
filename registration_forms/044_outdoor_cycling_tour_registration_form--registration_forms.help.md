# Outdoor Cycling Tour Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Outdoor Cycling Tour Registration Form is used to collect essential information from participants for an outdoor cycling tour. This form will help us to gather and record details about you and your tour experience, ensuring that we provide you with the best possible services and support during your tour.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the General Information section with your basic details.
2. Provide your Participant Information, including your name, contact number, and any other relevant details.
3. Enter your Emergency Contact's Information in case we need to reach them during the tour.
4. Fill in your Participant Details, such as your height, weight, and other relevant medical information.
5. Enter your Medical Information, including any health conditions that may affect your cycling ability.
6. Add your Billing Information for payment and tour services.
7. Finally, provide Registration Information for tour details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **General Information (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): Please fill in your name and any other general details about yourself, such as your contact information.
* **Participant Information (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): Enter your participant name, contact number, and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Emergency Contact (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): If there's someone we should contact in case of an emergency, please fill in their name and contact number.
* **Participant Details (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): Provide your height, weight, and any other relevant medical or cycling-related information.
* **Medical Information (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): Enter any health conditions that may affect your cycling ability or require medical attention during the tour.
* **Billing Information (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): Enter your billing address, contact number, and any other relevant payment information.
* **Registration Information (Text)** (<name>, <type>, **required:** no): Fill in any additional details about your tour registration, such as tour preferences or requests.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with your tour registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* We are committed to providing a safe and enjoyable tour experience for all participants.
