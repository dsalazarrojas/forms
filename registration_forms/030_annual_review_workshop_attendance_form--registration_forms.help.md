# Annual Review Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for an annual review workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select your role in the review process.
3. Choose your preferred workshop date.
4. Indicate your attendance method (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
5. Confirm your commitment to attending the workshop.
6. Indicate your experience level with performance reviews.
7. Enter your goals for attending the workshop.
8. If you have any special accommodations needs, please specify.
9. Select topics of specific interest.
10. If necessary, provide any additional comments or feedback about previous workshops.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Company or Organization**: Enter the name of your employer.
* **Position or Job Title**: Enter your current role or title.
* **Department**: Enter your department or team (optional).
* **Role in the Review Process**: Select your role in the workshop (Manager, Employee, HR, Peer Reviewer, or Observer).
* **Preferred Workshop Date**: Choose the session you would like to attend.
* **Attendance Method**: Select how you plan to attend the workshop (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Role in the Review Process**: Select your role in the review process (Manager, Employee, HR, Peer Reviewer, or Observer).
* **Experience Level**: Select your level of experience with performance reviews (New, Some, Moderate, or Extensive).
* **Goals for Attending the Workshop**: Enter your goals for attending the workshop.
* **Special Accommodations**: If you have any special needs or requests, please specify.
* **Topics of Specific Interest**: Select topics you are interested in learning about (e.g., Giving effective feedback, Setting performance goals).
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please specify.
* **Special Accommodations Needed**: If you have any special needs or requests, please specify.
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Select any accessibility requirements you have (e.g., Wheelchair, Interpreter services).
* **Previous Feedback**: If you attended a previous workshop, please provide feedback about what went well or what needs improvement.
* **Commitment Confirmation**: Confirm your commitment to attending the workshop.
* **Travel and Parking**: If you will need parking or directions, please select yes for both or either option.
