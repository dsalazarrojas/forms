# Remote Work Transition Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for employees to register for remote work transition training programs. Please fill it out to let us know your interests and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the required fields: First Name, Job Category, Training Session, and Date Start (if applicable). These are marked as required.
*   Enter your Email, Phone, Last Name, Company, Job Title, and Date End (if applicable) in the corresponding fields.
*   Select your preferred Job Category and Training Session from the dropdown menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your work phone number.
*   **Company** (`company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.
*   **Job Category** (`job_category`, `select_one`, required): Select one of the available options: Software Development, Data Science, Project Management, Sales, Marketing, or Support.
*   **Training Session** (`training_session`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the training sessions that interest you, from the available options: Remote Work 101, Advanced Remote Work, and Intermediate Remote Work.
*   **Date Start** (`date_start`, `date`, required): Enter the date you would like to start the training. This field is optional if you are not ready to commit to a start date yet.
*   **Date End** (`date_end`, `date`, optional): Enter the date you would like to end the training. This field is optional and only needed if you are planning to end the training in the near future.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
*   Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
*   If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help.
*   You can always reach out to us for clarification on any field.
