# Hair Salon New Client Consultation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new clients who want to schedule a consultation at a hair salon.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the contact information field with your contact details.
2. Select your style preferences from the dropdown menu.
3. Choose a date and time for your visit.
4. Select your hair type.
5. Add any notes or messages you'd like to share with the stylist.
6. Upload an image of your style reference.
7. Schedule a date and time for your visit.
8. Select a stylist for your visit.
9. Provide the stylist's phone number.
10. Optionally, add your phone number and email for the stylist to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **contact_info** (text field): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **style_preferences** (select multiple): Select up to 3 style preferences from the dropdown menu.
* **schedule_visit** (date field): Choose a date for your visit.
* **hair_type** (select one): Select your hair type.
* **notes** (text field): Add any notes or messages for the stylist.
* **style_image** (text field): Upload an image of your style reference.
* **schedule_date** (date field): Choose a date for your visit.
* **schedule_time** (time field): Choose a time for your visit.
* **stytist_name** (text field): Select a stylist for your visit.
* **stylist_phone** (text field): Provide the stylist's phone number.
* **client_phone** (text field): Enter your phone number (optional).
* **client_email** (text field): Enter your email (optional).
* **note** (text field): Add any additional notes.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields.
- Double-check your schedule details to ensure the correct date and time.
- Choose a stylist that suits your preferences.
- Take a photo of your style reference to upload as an image.
- Provide accurate contact information for the stylist to reach you.
