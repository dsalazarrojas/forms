# Community Building Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the Community Building Workshop Registration Form, ensuring a smooth and successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to understand the required information.
2. Fill in the registration details, such as your name, email, phone number, and other contact information.
3. Choose your preferred workshop date and format (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
4. Select the workshop track that interests you the most.
5. Indicate your experience level and organization type.
6. Specify your payment method and whether you require a scholarship.
7. If applicable, mention any dietary restrictions and accommodations needed.
8. Finally, indicate how you heard about the workshop and your interest in networking and follow-up sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`1`, `note`, not required): This is a placeholder field to provide additional information about your registration, such as any special circumstances or requirements.
* **Registration Date** (`2`, `date`, required): Enter the date of this registration.
* **Registration ID** (`3`, `text`, required): Provide a unique registration reference number.
* **Participant Information** (`4`, `note`, not required): Share any additional details about yourself as a participant.
* **Full Name** (`5`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization** (`8`, `text`, not required): If applicable, provide the name of your organization.
* **Job Title** (`9`, `text`, not required): Mention your job title or position, if applicable.
* **Workshop Information** (`10`, `note`, not required): Provide details about the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Name** (`11`, `text`, required): Select the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop Date** (`12`, `date`, required): Choose your preferred workshop date.
* **Workshop Format** (`13`, `select_one`, required): Select how you plan to attend (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Workshop Track** (`14`, `select_one`, required): Choose the workshop track that interests you the most.
* **Experience Level** (`15`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your experience level (beginner, intermediate, advanced, expert).
* **Organization Type** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Specify the type of organization you represent (non-profit, community group, government, school, etc.).
* **Years in Community Work** (`17`, `select_one`, required): Select how long you have been doing community work.
* **Learning Goals** (`18`, `text`, required): Mention what you hope to learn from the workshop.
* **Challenges Faced** (`19`, `text`, not required): Share any challenges you face in your work.
* **Special Needs** (`20`, `select_one`, required): Indicate if you require any accommodations.
* **Accommodations Needed** (`21`, `text`, not required): Describe any accommodations you need.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`22`, `text`, not required): Mention any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Referral Source** (`23`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you heard about the workshop.
* **Payment Method** (`24`, `select_one`, not required): Select your preferred payment method.
* **Scholarship Needed** (`25`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate if you need financial assistance.
* **Networking Interest** (`26`, `select_one`, not required): Show your interest in networking opportunities.
* **Follow-Up Interest** (`27`, `select_one`, not required): Indicate if you are interested in follow-up sessions.
* **Additional Comments** (`28`, `text`, not required): Share any additional information.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`29`, `select_one`, required): Confirm you have read and agree to the cancellation policy.
