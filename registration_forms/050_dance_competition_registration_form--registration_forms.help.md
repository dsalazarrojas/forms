# Dance Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Competition Registration Form is designed to collect information from dancers registering for a dance competition. The form gathers details such as name, contact information, competition details, and other relevant data to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review the form for completeness and correctness before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Information (id: 1)**: Enter a brief description of the competition or event you are registering for.
- **Name (id: 2)**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Dancer Information (id: 3)**: Provide details about the dancer, including any relevant contact information.
- **Competition Information (id: 4)**: Enter details about the competition you are registering for.
- **Registration Information (id: 5)**: Enter any additional information about your registration.
- **Additional Info (id: 6)**: Provide any additional details that may be relevant to your registration.
- **Dancer Details (id: 7)**: Enter any relevant details about the dancer.
- **Terms and Conditions (id: 8)**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the competition.
- **Dancer Info 2 (id: 9)**: Select multiple dancers (if applicable) from the provided options.
- **Submit (id: 10)**: Review and submit your registration form.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
- Double-check the form before submitting for accuracy and completeness.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact the competition organizers for clarification.
