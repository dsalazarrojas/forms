# School Trip Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Trip Authorization Form is used by students or their guardians to provide necessary information for the school to approve or deny a trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the **name** field.
2. Enter the student's ID number in the **student-id** field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the **student-grade** dropdown menu.
4. Enter the trip date in the **trip-date** field in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
5. Enter the trip time in the **trip-time** field in the format HH:MM.
6. Enter the trip duration in the **trip-duration** field in the format HH:MM.
7. Optionally, enter the student's email address in the **student-email** field.
8. Click the submit button to send the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **name (name, text, required/optional)**: Please enter the student's name.
* **student-id (student-id, number, required/optional)**: Please enter the student's ID number.
* **student-grade (student-grade, select_one, required/optional)**: Please select the student's grade level.
* **trip-date (trip-date, date, required/optional)**: Enter the trip date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **trip-time (trip-time, time, required/optional)**: Enter the trip time in the format HH:MM.
* **trip-duration (trip-duration, time, required/optional)**: Enter the trip duration in the format HH:MM.
* **student-email (student-email, email, required/optional)**: Optionally, enter the student's email address.
* **form-data (form-data, text, required/optional)**: This field does not have any hints, so it's likely used for additional information that the user can provide.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you need to enter dates or times, use the correct format as specified.
* If you need help or have questions, you can contact your school administrator.
