# Health Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Health Club Registration Form" is used to collect user information for registration purposes. It is likely used to gather details from both new and existing users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Provide your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
5. Select your preferred membership plan from the "Membership Plan" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the first name of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact First Name" field.
7. Enter the last name of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Last Name" field.
8. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_one, required: false): Select your preferred membership plan.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter the first name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person. 

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure a smooth registration process.
* Enter a valid email address for email field.
* Select a suitable membership plan based on your preferences.
