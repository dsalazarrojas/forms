# Jeep Wave Oil Change Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the fields and process of filling out the Jeep Wave Oil Change Registration form. Please read each section carefully to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the appropriate Wave program for your vehicle.
2. Choose the service center where you will be taking your vehicle for the oil change.
3. Select the type of oil to be used for the change.
4. Enter the date and time of your appointment.
5. Provide any additional service details or instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Vehicle Information
* **Vehicle Information** (`Vehicle Information`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter a brief description of your vehicle, including its make, model, and any relevant features.

### Wave Program
* **Wave Program** (`Wave Program`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the Wave program that applies to your vehicle. Options include Wave, Wave Select, and Wave Plus.

### Wave Program Selected
* **Wave Program Selected** (`Wave Program Selected`, `note`, required: false)
	+ This field is for internal use only and will be pre-populated based on your selection in the Wave Program field.

### Oil Change Details
* **Oil Change Details** (`Oil Change Details`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter any specific details about the oil change, such as the type of oil to be used or any additional services required.

### Service Center
* **Service Center** (`Service Center`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the service center where you will be taking your vehicle for the oil change. Options include Center1, Center2, and Center3.

### Oil Type
* **Oil Type** (`Oil Type`, `select_one`, required: false)
	+ Select the type of oil to be used for the oil change. Options include Regular and Synthetic.

### Appointment Date
* **Appointment Date** (`Appointment Date`, `date`, required: false)
	+ Select a date and time for your appointment.

### Service Details
* **Service Details** (`Service Details`, `text`, required: false)
	+ Enter any additional service details or instructions, such as a preferred service advisor or special requests.

### Tips
* Please ensure that you have all necessary information ready before filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your service center for assistance.
