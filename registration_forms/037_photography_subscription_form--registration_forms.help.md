# Photography Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for a photography subscription service. It will help us understand your preferences and requirements for capturing your desired photo style.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Select the Date and Time you prefer for your photography session.
3. Choose your preferred Subscription Type (Monthly, Quarterly, or Biannually).
4. Select the Subscription Length you desire (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, or 12 Months).
5. Choose your preferred Payment Method (Credit Card, PayPal, Cash, or Check).
6. Select your preferred Photo Style (Portrait, Landscape, Wedding, Baby, or Family).

### Last Name (Text)

### Subscription Length (Single-select)

### Payment Method (Single-select)

### Preferred Photo Style (Single-select)
* **Preferred Photo Style**: Choose the photo style you prefer (Portrait, Landscape, Wedding, Baby, or Family).
* **Required**: Yes

## Tips
* Ensure you select the correct subscription type and length that suits your needs.
* Make sure to choose a payment method that is convenient for you.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* Review your preferences carefully to ensure they align with your requirements.
