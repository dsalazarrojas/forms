# Scriptwriting Residency Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for writers and artists applying for a scriptwriting residency. It collects your personal details, residency address, application type, and other relevant information needed for the residency program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Make sure you are eligible for the scriptwriting residency by reading the eligibility criteria in the residency program description.
- Select "Residency for Writers" or "Residency for Artists" from the "Application Type" field, depending on your profession.
- Enter your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields respectively.
- Enter your email address in the "Email" field, which will be used for communication during the residency process.
- Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field for any follow-up communication.
- Enter your residency address in the "Residency Address" field.
- Enter the program name you are interested in applying for in the "Program Name" field.
- Select the start date of your desired residency period in the "Start Date" field.
- Enter the duration of your desired residency in the "Duration" field.
- Enter a brief description of your purpose for applying for the residency in the "Purpose" field.
- Provide any additional information you would like to share with the program in the "Additional Information" field.
- Review your information for accuracy before clicking the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication during the residency process.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Provide your phone number for follow-up communication.
- **Residency Address** (`residency_address`, text, optional): Enter your current or intended residency address.
- **Application Type** (`application_type`, select_one, required): Select "Residency for Writers" or "Residency for Artists".
- **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the program you are interested in applying for.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of your desired residency period.
- **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of your desired residency in months.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your desired residency period.
- **Purpose** (`purpose`, note, optional): Enter a brief description of your purpose for applying for the residency.
- **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any additional information you would like to share with the program.
- **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Review and submit your application.
