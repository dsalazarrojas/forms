# Leadership Conference Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendance at a leadership conference. Please fill out the form with your details and event information to help us better organize and manage conference attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your title and department to help us understand your role and affiliation.
3. Choose the event type (e.g., Conference, Meet, Seminar, Webinar, or Training).
4. Enter the start and end dates and times of the event.
5. Specify the number of attendees you are bringing with you.
6. Add any comments or additional information about your participation in the event.
7. Finally, select the registration date for your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): The name of the event you are attending.
* **Name** (`name`): Your name as registered attendee.
* **Attendee Title** (`attendee_title`): Your title (e.g., CEO, Director, Manager, Staff, or Other).
* **Department** (`department`): Your department or team name.
* **Email** (`email`): Your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Your phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`): The start date of the event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`): The start time of the event.
* **End Date** (`end_date`): The end date of the event.
* **End Time** (`end_time`): The end time of the event.
* **Attendees Count** (`attendees_count`): The number of attendees you are bringing with you.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Any additional comments or information about your participation in the event.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): The date of your registration for the event.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): The type of event you are attending (e.g., Conference, Meet, Seminar, Webinar, or Training).
