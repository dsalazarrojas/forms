# medicaid_support_rally_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the Medicaid support rally. Please fill out the form to provide your event name, details, preferences, contact information, and event details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you wish to join.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): Provide any relevant details about yourself as an attendee.
* **Participation Preferences** (`participation_preferences`, text, optional): Specify your participation preferences for the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Select the time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Specify the location of the event.
