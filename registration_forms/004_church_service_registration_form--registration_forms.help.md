# Church Service Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for registering attendees for a church service. It helps the church to collect essential information about the attendees, including their name, event date, service type, and other details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required".
2. Enter your name in the "Attendee name" field.
3. Select the event date for which you are attending the service.
4. Choose the type of service you wish to attend (e.g., Morning service, Evening service, Bible study, or Prayer meeting).
5. Enter the number of guests accompanying you.
6. If you have any special requests or accommodations, enter them in the "Special requests or accommodations" field.
7. Provide your contact email address.
8. Optionally, enter your contact phone number.
9. If you are interested in volunteering, check the "Interested in volunteering" box.
10. Review the "Registration acknowledgement" field to confirm your registration details.
11. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee name (Attendee name, text, required)**: Enter your full name as it should appear on any registration confirmation email or other church records.
* **Event date (Event date, date, required)**: Select the date on which you plan to attend the service.
* **Service type (Service type, select_one, required)**: Choose the type of service you wish to attend (e.g., Morning service, Evening service, Bible study, or Prayer meeting).
* **Number of guests (Number of guests, number, required)**: Include yourself and the number of guests accompanying you.
* **Special requests or accommodations (Special requests or accommodations, text, optional)**: Enter any special requests or accommodations, such as dietary needs or mobility concerns.
* **Contact email (Contact email, email, required)**: Enter your email address for registration confirmation purposes.
* **Contact phone (Contact phone, text, optional)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Interested in volunteering (Interested in volunteering, select_one, optional)**: Check this box if you are interested in volunteering during or after the service.
* **Registration acknowledgement (Registration acknowledgement, note, required)**: Confirm that you understand that a confirmation email will be sent to you after submitting this form.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you experience any technical issues while filling out the form, contact the church administration for assistance.
* The church will use the information collected in this form to plan and manage the event effectively.
