# Art Class Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Art Class Renewal Form is designed to gather information from students about their experience with an art class. This form is used to assess the student's progress, satisfaction, and preferences for renewal.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your basic information, including your name, ID number, and email.
2. Indicate your satisfaction with the class and your instructor.
3. Provide feedback on the class's relevance to your goals.
4. Answer the questions about your progress in the class.
5. Decide whether you want to renew in the same class or not.
6. Choose your preferred renewal term.
7. Add any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Student Name): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Student ID Number**: Please enter your student ID number, as assigned to you by the art program.
* **Email Address**: Please enter your email address that the art program uses to contact you.
* **Renewal Date**: Please select the date you would like to renew your enrollment in the art class.
* **Current Class Name**: Please enter the name of the class you are currently enrolled in.
* **Current Instructor Name**: Please enter the name of your current instructor.
* **How Long Have You Been in This Class**: Please select how long you have been enrolled in the class:
	+ First semester
	+ 1 year
	+ 2 years
	+ 3+ years
* **Overall Satisfaction with Class**: Please select how satisfied you are with the class:
	+ Very satisfied
	+ Satisfied
	+ Neutral
	+ Dissatisfied
	+ Very dissatisfied
* **Instructor Effectiveness**: Please select how effective you think your instructor is:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Fair
	+ Poor
* **Curriculum Relevance to Goals**: Please select how relevant the class is to your goals:
	+ Excellent match
	+ Good match
	+ Adequate
	+ Poor match
* **Skill Improvement Since Starting**: Please select how much you have improved in the class:
	+ Significant improvement
	+ Good improvement
	+ Some improvement
	+ Minimal improvement
	+ No improvement
* **Progress Toward Original Goals**: Please select how well you have progressed toward your original goals:
	+ Exceeded goals
	+ Met goals
	+ Partially met goals
	+ Have not met goals
	+ Goals changed
* **Do you want to renew the same class**: Please select whether you want to renew the same class:
	+ 'True'
	+ No - different class
	+ Unsure
* **Preferred Next Class or Level**: Please select your preferred next class or level:
	+ Beginner level
	+ Intermediate level
	+ Advanced level
	+ Different medium
	+ Same class again
* **Renewal Term**: Please select your preferred renewal term:
	+ Spring semester
	+ Fall semester
	+ Summer session
	+ Year-round
	+ Single session
* **What went well in this class**: Please enter anything that went well in the class.
* **Suggestions for Improvement**: Please enter any suggestions you have for improving the class.
* **Schedule Preference for Renewal**: Please select your preferred schedule preference for renewal:
	+ Same time
	+ Different time
	+ Flexible
* **Additional Comments**: Please enter any additional comments or suggestions you may have.

## Tips

* Please answer the questions truthfully and completely.
* Please be honest about your progress and satisfaction with the class.
* Please provide any additional comments or suggestions you may have.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the art program.
