# Strength Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect personal contact and demographic information from individuals interested in participating in strength training programs. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure we can reach out to you with more information about our programs.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out each field with accurate and complete information.
*   Use correct formatting for date of birth (MM/DD/YYYY).
*   Ensure your email address is entered correctly (e.g., [your_email@example.com](mailto:your_email@example.com)).
*   Do not include extra characters or symbols in phone number fields.
*   Double-check your first and last name for correct spelling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Strength Training Registration** (`strength_training_registration_form`): This is the main topic field and should contain a brief description about why you're interested in strength training programs.
*   **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name as it is written on your identification or as you prefer it to be written.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name as it is written on your identification or as you prefer it to be written.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 12/25/1990).
*   **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address where you can be reached for program updates and information (e.g., [your_email@example.com](mailto:your_email@example.com)).
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your phone number for further contact or updates (e.g., 123-456-7890).
