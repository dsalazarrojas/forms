# Halloween Candy Buy Back Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is the registration form for the Halloween Candy Buy Back Program. Fill out this form to get started with the program and make a positive impact by recycling Halloween candy.

## How To Complete This Form
- To register for the Halloween Candy Buy Back Program, please complete the form in its entirety and submit it for processing.
- Please fill out the form with your real name and contact information.
- Select how you heard about the program.
- Choose the types of candies you are willing to give back.
- Provide a date and time when you plan to give back the candy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: This is where you enter your first name.
  - Type: text, length: variable
- **Last Name**: This is where you enter your last name.
  - Type: text, length: variable
- **Email**: This is where you enter a valid email address.
  - Type: email
  - Please note: This will be used for communication and reminders.
- **Phone**: This is where you enter your phone number.
  - Type: text, length: variable
- **How did you hear about us?**: This is a multiple choice question. Choose one of the following options:
  - Social Media
  - Online Search
  - Word of Mouth
  - Other
- **What types of candy do you have to give back?**: This is a multiple choice question. Choose one or multiple types of candy from the options below:
  - Chocolate
  - Gummies
  - Sweets
  - Sour
- **What date do you plan on giving back?**: This is where you enter a date.
  - Type: date
  - Please note: This will determine when your candy collection will be scheduled.
- **Time**: This is where you enter a time of day.
  - Type: time
  - Please note: This will help us schedule your candy collection at a convenient time.
