# public_health_digital_campaign_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register users for public health digital campaign training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Follow these steps to fill out the form:
	1. Complete the "Registration Details" page.
	2. Select your preferred training time on the "Training Preferences" page.
	3. Choose your available dates for the training on the "Training Date" page.
	4. Provide any additional information about your registration on the "Additional Info" page.
## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, optional): This is where you enter your basic registration information, such as your name, email, etc. Please enter any relevant details that will help us contact you about your registration.
* **Training Preferences** (`training_preferences`, select one, required): This field is used to select a time slot for the training session that suits you best. Please choose between Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, select multiple, optional): Here, you can choose one or multiple dates for the training session that you are available. Please select the dates that work for you from the available options.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): This is an open field where you can provide any additional information about your registration that is not captured in the other fields. Please use this to add any relevant details that will help us better understand your registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure you are registering for a training session that suits your schedule and availability.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
* If you have any additional questions or need further clarification, please reach out to us.
