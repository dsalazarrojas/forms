# Mystery Reader Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mystery Reader Registration form is designed to collect information from users about their registration for a specific purpose, such as a reading program or event. This form helps the organizers or administrators to manage registrations and preferences of users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **User Details** page with your personal information, including **Name** and **Email**.
2. Select your **Preferred Language** for the program or event.
3. Choose your **Time Zone** for the program or event.
4. Select your **Time Preferences** for the program or event.
5. Choose the **Available Dates** you are available to participate.
6. Indicate if you are a **Volunteer** for the program or event.
7. Select your **Reading Preferences** for the program or event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required: false): This page is for entering your personal details, including your name and email.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required: true): Select your preferred language for the program or event.
* **Time Zone** (`time_zone`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose your time zone for the program or event.
* **Time Preferences** (`time_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your time preferences for the program or event.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Choose the dates you are available to participate.
* **Volunteer Details** (`volunteer_details`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Indicate if you are willing to volunteer for the program or event.
* **Reading Preferences** (`reading_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Select your reading preferences for the program or event.

Note: All the **Drag and Drop** fields are for notes or additional comments, but are not required.
