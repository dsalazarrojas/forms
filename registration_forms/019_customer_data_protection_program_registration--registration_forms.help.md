# customer_data_protection_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a customer data protection program, which is a process to ensure that companies are properly managing their sensitive data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the company name and job title in the "Company Name" and "Job Title" fields, respectively.
2. Select the department that the data protection officer belongs to from the "Department" field.
3. Indicate the level of data sensitivity using the "Data Sensitivity" field.
4. Select all relevant roles and interests related to data protection from the "Role Interest" field.
5. Specify the job responsibilities of the data protection officer in the "Job Responsibilities" field.
6. Enter the company email and phone number in the "Company Email" and "Company Phone" fields, respectively.
7. Enter the role of the data protection officer in the "Role" field (this field is for informational purposes only).
8. Enter any additional comments about the data protection program in the "Additional Comments" field.
9. Enter the name of the person submitting the form in the "Submitter Name" field.
10. Select all relevant roles and interests related to the submitter in the "Submitter Role" field.
11. Select the company of the submitter from the "Submitter Company" field.
12. Enter the email of the submitter in the "Submitter Email" field.
13. Enter the phone number of the submitter in the "Submitter Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the company registering the data protection program.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required: false): Enter the job title of the data protection officer.
* **Department** (`department`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the department that the data protection officer belongs to (e.g., Marketing, Sales, IT, Finance, Operations, HR).
* **Data Sensitivity** (`data_sensitivity`, `number`, required: false): Indicate the level of sensitivity of the data being protected (e.g., low, medium, high).
* **Role Interest** (`role_interest`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant roles and interests related to data protection (e.g., Data Protection, Security, Compliance, Data Subject, Data Controller, Data Processor, Data Subject Requester, Data Processor Requester).
* **Job Responsibilities** (`job_responsibilities`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Specify the job responsibilities of the data protection officer (e.g., Account management, Audit and compliance, Data protection, Financial planning, Legal, Operations, Reporting, Security).
* **Company Email** (`company_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the company email.
* **Company Phone** (`company_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the company phone number.
* **Role** (`role`, `note`, required: false): This field is for informational purposes only and should not be filled out.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional comments about the data protection program.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Role** (`submitter_role`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all relevant roles and interests related to the submitter (e.g., Data Protection, Security, Compliance, Data Subject, Data Controller, Data Processor, Data Subject Requester, Data Processor Requester).
* **Submitter Company** (`submitter_company`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the company of the submitter (e.g., Company A, Company B, Company C, Company D, Company E).
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the email of the submitter.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the submitter.
