# e_commerce_license_application_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to guide e-commerce businesses in registering their license application. It collects essential information about the business structure, ownership, and other relevant details required for the license application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Choose the correct option for 'Business Structure' and describe your business in a concise manner.
2. Enter the details of your business ownership.
3. Select the correct 'License Type' for your business.
4. Enter your business name in the required format.
5. Enter the name and title of the contact person for your business.
6. Provide a valid email address for your business.
7. Enter your business phone number.
8. Enter your business's street address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Business Structure** (text, required): This field is for describing your e_commerce business structure. Please enter a concise and detailed description of your business structure.
- **Business Ownership** (text, required): This field is for entering the details of your business ownership. Please provide a clear and concise description of your business's ownership.
- **License Type** (select one, required): Choose the correct license type for your business from the available options (License A, License B, License C).
- **Business Name** (text, required): Enter your business name in the required format.
- **Contact Person** (text, required): Enter the name and title of the contact person for your business.
- **Email** (email, required): Provide a valid email address for your business.
- **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your business phone number.
- **Street Address** (text, required): Enter your business's street address.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all the fields are filled correctly before submitting the form.
- If you need any clarification or further assistance, please do not hesitate to reach out to us.
