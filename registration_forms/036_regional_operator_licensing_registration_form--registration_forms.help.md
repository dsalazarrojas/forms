# Regional Operator Licensing Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regional Operator Licensing Registration Form is a form used to register regional operators for the purpose of obtaining a license. This form collects essential information about the operator, their contact details, and license-specific information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "operator Name" field.
2. Provide your address in the "address" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
5. Enter the date of license issuance in the "date of license issuance" field.
6. Enter your license number in the "license number" field.
7. Select the type of license you hold from the "operator license type" field.
8. If you have multiple locations, indicate which ones are relevant by selecting the corresponding options in the "geo location" field.
9. Enter any additional location information in the "Geo Location 2" and "Geo Location 3" fields.
10. Select your region in the "Region 2" and "Region 3" fields.
11. Enter your license number in the "operator license number" field.
12. Enter the expiration date of your license in the "license expiration date" field.
13. If your license status is inactive, select "Inactive" from the "license status" field.
14. Enter the contact person's name in the "contact person" field.
15. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "contact person phone" field.
16. Enter the contact person's email address in the "contact person email" field.
17. Enter the contact person's address in the "contact person address" field.
18. If you have a secondary contact person, enter their name in the "Contact Person 2" field.
19. Enter any additional notes in the "note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **operator Name** (1/1, required): Enter your name as it is to be displayed on your license.
* **address** (1/2, optional): Enter your address, including street address and postal code.
* **email** (3/1, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **phone** (4/1, required): Enter your phone number, including country code and area code.
* **date of license issuance** (5/1, required): Enter the date your license was issued.
* **license number** (6/1, required): Enter your unique license number.
* **license issuance date** (7/1, required): Enter the date your license was issued.
* **operator license type** (8/1, required): Select the type of license you hold from the provided options.
* **geo location** (9/1, optional): If you have multiple locations, select the ones relevant to your business.
* **Geo Location 2** (10/1, optional): Enter any additional location information.
* **region** (11/1, optional): Enter your region, e.g., city or state.
* **operator license number** (12/1, required): Enter your license number again for verification purposes.
* **license expiration date** (13/1, required): Enter the date your license is set to expire.
* **license status** (14/1, optional): Select "Inactive" if your license is currently inactive.
* **contact person** (15/1, optional): Enter the name of the contact person for your business.
* **contact person phone** (16/1, optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **contact person email** (17/1, optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **contact person address** (18/1, optional): Enter the contact person's address.
* **Contact Person 2** (19/1, optional): If you have a secondary contact person, enter their name.
* **note** (20/1, optional): Enter any additional notes or information.
* **Geo Location 3** (21/1, optional): If you have additional locations, indicate which ones are relevant.
* **Region 2** (22/1, optional): Enter your region, e.g., city or state.
* **Operator License Type 2** (23/1, optional): Select the type of license you hold from the provided options.
* **Geo Location 4** (24/1, optional): If you have multiple locations, select the ones relevant to your business.
* **Region 3** (25/1, optional): Enter your region, e.g., city or state.
