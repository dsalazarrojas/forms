# Church Attendance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for church events, services, and gatherings. Please complete this form to provide your information and help us better understand your attendance and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by selecting the event or service you are attending from the options provided.
2. Fill in your first and last name, email address, and phone number (if applicable).
3. Choose your membership status, indicating whether you are a current member, former member, visitor, first-time visitor, or prospective member.
4. If you are a member, provide your member since date.
5. Fill in your street address, city, state or province, and zip code (if applicable).
6. Indicate the number of people in your group attending the event.
7. Select whether you are interested in volunteering and choose the areas of interest.
8. Choose whether you would like to receive our newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event or Service Name** (Required): Enter the name of the event or service you are attending.
* **Event Date** (Required): Enter the date of the event or service.
* **Event Time** (Required): Enter the time of the event or service.
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (Optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Date of Birth** (Optional): Enter your date of birth (if applicable).
* **Membership Status** (Required): Choose your membership status:
	+ Current member
	+ Former member
	+ Visitor
	+ First-time visitor
	+ Prospective member
* **Member Since (if applicable)**: Enter the date you have been a member (if you are a member).
* **Street Address** (Optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (Optional): Enter your city.
* **State or Province** (Optional): Enter your state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (Optional): Enter your zip or postal code.
* **Number of People in Your Group** (Optional): Enter the number of people in your group attending the event.
* **Children Attending** (Optional): Enter the number of children attending (if any).
* **Interested in Volunteering** (Optional): Choose whether you are interested in volunteering:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe
* **Areas of Interest for Volunteering** (Optional): Enter the areas of interest for volunteering (if you are interested).
* **Would you like to receive our newsletter** (Optional): Choose whether you would like to receive our newsletter:
	+ Yes
	+ No

Tips:
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the church office for assistance.
* If you are attending with a group, fill in the number of people and children attending accurately.
