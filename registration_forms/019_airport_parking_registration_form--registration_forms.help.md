# Airport Parking Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Airport Parking Registration Form is designed to gather information from customers for parking reservations at the airport. This form captures essential details such as customer contact information, vehicle details, parking preferences, and reservation requirements. By completing this form, customers can book their parking spot in advance and ensure a smooth and hassle-free experience at the airport.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name and email address in the "Customer Information" section.
2. Provide your phone number to ensure we can contact you in case of any changes or updates to your reservation.
3. Enter your vehicle's make and model, license plate number, and color in the "Vehicle Details" section.
4. Choose your departing terminal and select the drop-off and pick-up dates for your parking reservation.
5. Specify the type of parking service you require (e.g., short-term, long-term, or valet parking).
6. Indicate if you need electric vehicle charging and accessible (handicap) parking facilities.
7. If required, enter the estimated total cost of your parking reservation.
8. Finally, confirm that you agree to the airport's parking terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Enter a valid email address where we can contact you regarding your parking reservation.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of any updates or changes.
* **Vehicle Make and Model**: Enter the make and model of your vehicle, for example, "Toyota Camry".
* **License Plate Number**: Enter your vehicle's license plate number.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of your vehicle.
* **Departing Terminal**: Choose the terminal from which you will be departing.
* **Drop-off Date**: Enter the date and time you will be arriving at the airport.
* **Pick-up Date**: Enter the date and time you will be picking up your vehicle.
* **Parking Type**: Select the type of parking service you require (e.g., short-term, long-term, or valet parking).
* **Require Electric Vehicle (EV) Charging?**: Indicate if you need electric vehicle charging facilities.
* **Require Accessible (Handicap) Parking?**: Indicate if you need accessible parking facilities.
* **Estimated Total Cost ($)**: Enter the estimated total cost of your parking reservation (if applicable).
* **I agree to the airport parking terms and liability conditions**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the airport's parking terms and conditions.
