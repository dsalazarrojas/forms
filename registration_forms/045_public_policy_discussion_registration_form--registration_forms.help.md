# Public Policy Discussion Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants about themselves and their interests in public policy discussions. This information helps us better understand their background and preferences, allowing us to tailor the discussions to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Registration Information** on the first page. This includes your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** by entering your Phone number.
3. Share your **Birth Details** by selecting your Date of Birth and Time of Birth.
4. Describe your **Event Details** and **Event Interests** in the respective fields.
5. Finally, add any additional **Participant Comments** if you have any further information to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it is written on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Select your date of birth in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Select your time of birth in the required format (HH:MM).
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Describe the event or topic you are interested in.
* **Event Interests** (`event_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the interests that apply from the list provided.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
* We will review your registration information and confirm your participation in the public policy discussion.
