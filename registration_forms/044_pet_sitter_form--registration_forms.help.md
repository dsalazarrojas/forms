# Pet Sitter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Sitter Form is used to collect information about potential or existing pet sitters who work with the service. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the required fields, which include:
	* First Name
	* Last Name
	* Email (ensure it's a valid email address)
	* Address (city, state, and zip code)
2. Provide any additional information you'd like about your experience and education.
3. If you have a vehicle, fill out the Vehicle License and Vehicle Insurance fields.
4. Click submit to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Pet Sitter Information (Page 1)
* **Pet Sitter Profile Image**: This is a text field where you can add a brief description about yourself. 
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you use to communicate with us.

### Address Information (Page 2)
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you live.
* **State**: Select your state from the list of options (e.g., Alabama, Alaska, etc.).
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.

### Contact Information (Page 2)
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **License Number**: Enter any relevant license numbers (e.g., pet sitter license).
* **Vehicle License**: If you have a vehicle, enter your driver's license number.
* **Vehicle Insurance**: If you have a vehicle, enter your vehicle insurance information.

### Work Experience (Page 3)
* **Experience**: Describe your relevant work experience as a pet sitter.

### Education (Page 3)
* **Education**: Describe your relevant education or certifications.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all the required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your interest in working with our pet sitting service!
