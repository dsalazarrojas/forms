# Brow Lamination Course Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in registering for a brow lamination course. The form is used to gather personal details, preferences, and needs to provide an efficient and personalized experience for the training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email Address in the Email Address field.
3. Provide your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
4. Enter your Date of Birth, ensuring it's 18 or older, in the Date of Birth field.
5. If applicable, describe your Current Profession or Occupation in the Current Profession or Occupation field.
6. If you have experience in the beauty industry, indicate the number of years you've been working in it in the Beauty Industry Experience field.
7. Choose your experience level in brow services from the Brow Service Experience Level dropdown menu.
8. Select your preferred Course Date and Location from the Course Date and Location dropdown menus.
9. Choose the format of the course you prefer (In-Person Training, Online Training, or Hybrid) in the Course Format dropdown menu.
10. If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, please describe them in the Any Dietary Requirements or Restrictions field.
11. Choose how you'd like to pay for the course (Full Fee or Payment Plan) in the Course Fee dropdown menu.
12. If you're interested in obtaining certification, select Yes, I want certification; otherwise, choose No, just training.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (id: 1, text): Enter your First Name.
* **Last Name** (id: 2, text): Enter your Last Name.
* **Email Address** (id: 3, email): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (id: 4, text): Provide your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (id: 5, date): Enter your Date of Birth, ensuring it's 18 or older. This is a requirement for the course.
* **Current Profession or Occupation** (id: 6, text): If applicable, describe your current job or profession.
* **Beauty Industry Experience** (id: 7, number): If you have experience in the beauty industry, indicate the number of years you've been working in it.
* **Brow Service Experience Level** (id: 8, select_one): Choose your level of experience in brow services from the dropdown menu: Complete Beginner, Some Experience, or Experienced.
* **Preferred Course Date** (id: 9, select_one): Select your preferred course date.
* **Course Location** (id: 10, select_one): Choose your preferred course location from the dropdown menu: New York Studio, Los Angeles Studio, Chicago Studio, Miami Studio, or Online Course.
* **Course Format** (id: 11, select_one): Select the course format you prefer: In-Person Training, Online Training, or Hybrid.
* **What are your learning goals** (id: 12, text): Describe your career plans, certification goals, etc.
* **Any Dietary Requirements or Restrictions** (id: 13, text): If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, please describe them here.
* **Course Fee** (id: 14, select_one): Choose how you'd like to pay for the course: Full Fee or Payment Plan.
* **Are you interested in certification** (id: 15, select_one): If you're interested in obtaining certification, select Yes, I want certification; otherwise, choose No, just training.

## Tips

* Please ensure your Date of Birth is 18 or older.
* Be as specific as possible when choosing your Course Date and Location.
* If you have any dietary requirements or restrictions, please describe them clearly.
* If you're interested in certification, ensure you've selected the correct option.
