# Machine Learning Tournament Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Machine Learning Tournament Signup form is designed to collect user information for participation in a Machine Learning tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`text`, optional): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant user information.
* **Team Details** (`text`, optional): If you are part of a team, enter your team name, member names, and any other team-related information.
* **Participant Info** (`text`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as a participant, such as your role in the tournament or any other relevant details.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact form@gic.mx.
