# Energy Efficiency Consultation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Energy Efficiency Consultation Registration form is a tool used to collect information from potential clients interested in consulting on energy efficiency solutions for their properties. This form is designed to gather essential details to schedule a consultation and provide a successful consultation experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form fields accurately and thoroughly.
2. Ensure all required fields are filled out, as marked by an asterisk (\*).
3. Review the form carefully before submission to avoid any mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form Information
* **Form Information** (`form_information`, text, not required): This field is a text input for any additional comments or information you'd like to share about the consultation.

### Property Information
* **Property Information** (`property_info`, text, required): This field requires detailed information about the property you are interested in consulting for energy efficiency. Please provide a brief description of the property, including its address, size, and any relevant details that would be helpful for the consultant to know.

### Preferred Visit
* **Preferred Visit** (`preferred_visit`, text, not required): If you have a preferred date and time for the consultation, please provide it here. This will help us schedule the consultation at a time that suits you best.

### Consent
* **Consent** (`consent`, text, required): Please type 'yes' to confirm your consent for the consultation.

# Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
* If you have any questions or need clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
