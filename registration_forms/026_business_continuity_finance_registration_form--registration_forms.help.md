# Business Continuity Finance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential information from businesses affected by a disruption or emergency to help determine their financial needs. It is used to assist in providing financial support.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for "Business Information" section.
2. Select the type of disruption or event that caused the financial need.
3. Provide the estimated financial loss amount.
4. Determine the purpose of the funding and select the relevant option.
5. Indicate the repayment capacity and whether collateral is available.
6. Specify any other funding sources.
7. Choose the funding urgency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the legal name of your business.
* **Business Registration Number**: Enter the registration number or tax ID of your business.
* **Industry Type**: Select the primary business sector.
* **Years in Operation**: Enter the length of time your business has been operating.
* **Number of Employees**: Enter the current staff count.
* **Annual Revenue**: Enter the last fiscal year revenue.
* **Principal Place of Business**: Enter the street address of your business.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Title**: Enter the position or role of the primary contact person.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the business email address.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the business phone number.
* **Type of Disruption or Event**: Select the cause of the financial need.
* **Estimated Financial Loss**: Enter the amount of the financial loss.
* **Business Continuity Plan Status**: Select whether the business has a continuity plan in place.
* **Funding Amount Requested**: Enter the total amount needed.
* **Purpose of Funding**: Select the reason for the requested funds.
* **Repayment Capacity**: Select the ability to repay within a specified timeframe.
* **Collateral Available**: Indicate whether collateral is available for the loan.
* **Other Funding Sources**: List any other funding sources, such as insurance or grants.
* **Funding Urgency**: Select the urgency of the funding need.

Note: All fields marked as "required" must be completed to ensure accurate processing of your financial request.
