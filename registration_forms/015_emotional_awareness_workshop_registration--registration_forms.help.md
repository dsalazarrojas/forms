# emotional_awareness_workshop_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the emotional awareness workshop. It collects basic contact information and workshop preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure it is formatted correctly (e.g., [name]@example.com).
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. If you are able to attend the workshop on the scheduled date, select the date in the "Date" field. If not, please contact us to make alternative arrangements.
5. Choose your time slot by selecting the time you are able to attend from the "Time" field.
6. Select your level of awareness by choosing between "High" or "Low" in the "Awareness Level" field.
7. Choose your interests from the options in the "Interests" field.
8. Add any additional comments or notes about your registration in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (date, optional): If you can attend the workshop on the scheduled date, select it here.
* **Time** (time, optional): Choose your time slot for the workshop.
* **Awareness Level** (select_one, required): Select your level of awareness.
* **Interests** (select_multiple, required): Choose your interests.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any comments or notes about your registration.
* **Confirm Email** (email, optional): Confirm your email address for registration purposes. 

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out all required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Awareness Level, Interests).
- Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
- If you have any special requests or needs, please include them in the "Notes" field.
- We will contact you with more information about the workshop once we receive your registration.
