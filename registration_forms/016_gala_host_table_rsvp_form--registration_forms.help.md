# Gala Host Table RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to enable hosts or organizers of events, such as galas, to collect RSVPs from their guests. This will help them plan and prepare for the event by getting an overview of who will be attending, including their dietary restrictions, the number of guests attending, and the size of table needed for each guest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information:
   - **Title** (Optional): A brief title of the event.
   - **Name**: Your name, as the host or organizer of the event.
   - **Phone** (Optional): Your contact phone number.
   - **Email** (Optional): Your contact email address.
   - **Will Guests Be Attending?** (Optional): Choose whether guests will be attending or not.
   - **Guests' Diet** (Optional): Mention any dietary restrictions for the guests.
   - **Time of Event** (Optional): Enter the start time of the event.
   - **Date of Event** (Optional): Enter the date of the event.
   - **RSVP Host** (Optional): Mention the name of the RSVP host or organizer.
   - **Number of Guests Attending** (Optional): Enter the number of guests attending.
   - **Table Size** (Optional): Choose the size of the table needed for each guest.

2. Review and submit the form to send the information to the host or organizer of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Please enter your name as the host or organizer of the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number that guests can contact you with, please enter it.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): If you have an email address where guests can contact you with, please enter it.
* **Will Guests Be Attending?** (`guests_attending`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose whether guests will be attending or not.
* **Guests' Diet** (`guests_diet`, `text`, optional): Mention any dietary restrictions for the guests, such as vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.
* **Time of Event** (`time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the event.
* **Date of Event** (`event_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **RSVP Host** (`rsvp_host`, `text`, optional): Mention the name of the RSVP host or organizer.
* **Number of Guests Attending** (`number_of_guests`, `text`, optional): Enter the number of guests attending.
* **Table Size** (`table_size`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the size of the table needed for each guest.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in the required information accurately to help the host or organizer plan and prepare for the event.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, you can always contact the host or organizer for clarification.
* This form is for your convenience, so please fill it out as soon as possible to help the host or organizer get a headcount and prepare for the event.
