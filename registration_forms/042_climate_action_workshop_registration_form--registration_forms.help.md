# Climate Action Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests, goals, and needs for the Climate Action Workshop. Please fill out this form to provide us with your information and preferences so we can tailor the workshop experience to meet your expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and provide clear answers to the questions.
2. Make sure to select only one option for each question that best suits your needs.
3. For questions with "select_multiple" type, select as many options as apply.
4. Enter text responses in the text fields.
5. Choose your preferred workshop date and format.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **First Name (ID: 3)** (`text`, required)
Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.

### 2. **Last Name (ID: 4)** (`text`, required)
Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.

### 3. **Email Address (ID: 5)** (`email`, required)
Enter your email address that we can use to communicate with you.

### 4. **Phone Number (ID: 6)** (`text`, required)
Enter your phone number where we can reach you.

### 5. **Organization or Company (ID: 7)** (`text`, optional)
If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.

### 6. **Job Title or Position (ID: 8)** (`text`, optional)
Enter your job title or position in your organization.

### 7. **Primary Interest Area (ID: 11)** (`select_one`, required)
Select one primary interest area that aligns with your work or interests.

### 8. **Climate Action Experience Level (ID: 12)** (`select_one`, required)
Choose your current level of experience in taking climate action.

### 9. **What is Your Primary Goal for This Workshop? (ID: 14)** (`text`, required)
Describe what you hope to learn or accomplish through this workshop.

### 10. **What Motivates You to Take Climate Action? (ID: 15)** (`select_multiple`, required)
Select all applicable reasons that motivate you to take climate action.

### 11. **Do You Have Plans to Implement Climate Actions? (ID: 16)** (`select_one`, optional)
Choose if you have plans to implement climate actions, or select "Maybe" if you're unsure.

### 12. **Industry or Sector (ID: 18)** (`select_one`, optional)
Select the industry or sector where you work or focus your efforts.

### 13. **Key Climate Challenges You Face (ID: 19)** (`text`, optional)
Describe the key climate challenges you face in your work or organization.

### 14. **Preferred Workshop Date (ID: 10)** (`select_one`, required)
Select your preferred workshop date.

### 15. **Preferred Workshop Format (ID: 25)** (`select_one`, required)
Choose your preferred workshop format: In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid.

### 16. **Dietary Restrictions or Preferences (ID: 28)** (`text`, optional)
If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, enter them here.

### 17. **Interest in Networking (ID: 30)** (`select_one`, optional)
Choose how much you're interested in networking with other participants.

### 18. **Interest in Collaborating (ID: 31)** (`select_one`, optional)
Choose if you're interested in collaborating on climate initiatives.

### 19. **I Want to Receive Follow-Up Information (ID: 32)** (`select_one`, optional)
Choose if you want to receive follow-up information after the workshop.

Note: This help guide will help you navigate the form with ease, but please let us know if you have any questions or need clarification on any field.
