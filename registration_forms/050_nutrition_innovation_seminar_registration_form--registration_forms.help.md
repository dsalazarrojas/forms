# Nutrition Innovation Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to register for the Nutrition Innovation Seminar. The seminar is an excellent opportunity to learn about the latest developments and trends in the field of nutrition. Completing this form accurately will help the event organizers manage participant information effectively.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill in your Registration Name.
2.  Enter your Contact Email address if you wish to be contacted about the seminar.
3.  Enter your Contact Phone number if you wish to be contacted about the seminar.
4.  Please select your Participant Type from the list. Options include Student, Academic, Professional, and Other.
5.  If applicable, list any dietary restrictions or allergies.
6.  If you have any other dietary restrictions, please describe them in this field.
7.  If you have any special requirements, please describe them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Registration Name** (`Registration Name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on the seminar materials.
*  **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address if you wish to be contacted about the seminar.
*  **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted about the seminar.
*  **Participant Type** (`Participant Type`, select_multiple, optional): Select your role or affiliation from the list. Options include Student, Academic, Professional, and Other.
*  **Dietary Restrictions** (`Dietary Restrictions`, note, optional): List any food restrictions or allergies you have. This will help us cater to your needs.
*  **Other** (`Other`, text, optional): Please describe any other dietary restrictions you have that are not listed above.
*  **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements, such as accommodation or accessibility needs, please describe them in this field.
