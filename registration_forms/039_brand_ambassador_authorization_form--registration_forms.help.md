# Brand Ambassador Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information necessary for brand representation as an ambassador.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the required fields marked as "true".
2. Fill out the fields as follows:
    * "Full Name" field: enter your full legal name.
    * "Email Address" field: enter your primary email address.
    * "Phone Number" field: enter your contact phone number.
    * "Brand or Company Name" field: enter the brand or company you are representing.
    * "Scope of Authorization" field: select the specific scope of your authorization.
    * "Authorization Duration" field: select the duration of your authorization.
    * "Primary Responsibilities" field: select your primary responsibilities as an ambassador.
    * "Compensation Type" field: select how you will be compensated.
    * "Expense Coverage" field: select who covers event expenses.
    * "Authorization Age" field: confirm you are legally authorized.
    * "Authorization Consent" field: confirm authorization of this brand ambassador agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your full legal name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Brand or Company Name**: Enter the brand or company you are representing.
* **Scope of Authorization**: Select the specific scope of your authorization.
* **Authorization Duration**: Select the duration of your authorization.
* **Primary Responsibilities**: Select your primary responsibilities as an ambassador.
* **Content Creation Required**: Select whether you are required to create content.
* **Compensation Type**: Select how you will be compensated.
* **Expense Coverage**: Select who covers event expenses.
* **I agree to represent the brand professionally**: Check to confirm you agree to represent the brand professionally.
* **I understand IP and confidentiality terms**: Select whether you understand IP and confidentiality terms.
* **I agree to comply with brand guidelines**: Select whether you agree to comply with brand guidelines.
* **I fully agree to compensation terms**: Select whether you fully agree to compensation terms.
