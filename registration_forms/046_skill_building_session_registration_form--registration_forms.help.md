# Skill Building Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Skill Building Session Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in joining the session. This form will help the organizers and facilitators to create an account for each participant and plan the session accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the following fields:

1. **Session Name**: Enter the name of the skill building session you are interested in joining.
2. **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or any other government-issued ID.
3. **Email**: Enter your email address.
4. **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
5. **Start Date**: Select the start date of the session you are interested in.
6. **End Date**: Select the end date of the session you are interested in.
7. **Participant Address**: Enter your current address.
8. **Participant City**: Enter your city.
9. **Participant State**: Enter your state or province.
10. **Participant Zip**: Enter your zip or postal code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the skill building session you are interested in joining. This will help us to confirm your interest in the session and keep you updated on session announcements.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official ID or any other government-issued ID. This will help us to verify your identity.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address. This will be your primary contact method for any updates and announcements related to the session.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This will help the organizers and facilitators to contact you for any important updates or last-minute changes.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the start date of the session you are interested in. If you are unsure of the dates, please contact the organizers.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of the session you are interested in. If you are unsure of the dates, please contact the organizers.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your current address. This will be helpful for any local arrangements or logistical purposes.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip or postal code.

## Tips
Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information for each field. If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organizers for clarification.
