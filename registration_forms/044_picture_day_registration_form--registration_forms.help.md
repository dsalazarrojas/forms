# Picture Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Picture Day Registration Form is a tool for participants to provide necessary information to event organizers before attending a picture day event. 

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields (marked with *) and optional fields as needed.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
3. Review your submission for errors or missing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.*
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.*
* **Email**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you with event updates and notifications.*
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of last-minute event changes or cancellations.*
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.*
* **Time of Registration**: Enter the time you plan to arrive for the event.*
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event (if different from your date of birth).*
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the event (if different from your time of registration).*
* **Event Location**: Enter the location of the event (if different from your photo location).*
* **Photo Location**: Enter the location where your photo will be taken (e.g., studio, outdoor area, etc.).*
* **Photo Type**: Enter the type of photo you will be taking (e.g., passport, ID, etc.).*
* **Notes**: Enter any additional information you think the event organizers need to know about you or your photo shoot.

Please note: Some fields are marked as optional but are still required for accurate event organization. Be sure to fill out all required fields to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience.
