# Marketing Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marketing Client Registration Form is designed to capture crucial information from clients, enabling effective marketing interactions and targeted campaigns.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, select the relevant options in each field, and fill in the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **1. Marketing** (`select_one`, required: false)
  This field is a selection menu that allows you to choose the category of marketing (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) that you are interested in.
- **2. Name** (`text`, required: false)
  Please enter your name for reference and communication purposes.
- **3. Email** (`email`, required: false)
  Enter your email address so we can reach out to you.
- **4. Phone** (`text`, required: false)
  Enter your phone number for easy contact and follow-up.
- **5. Source** (`text`, required: false)
  Indicate how you heard about us (e.g., Google, Social, Mail, Other).
- **6. Interested in** (`select_multiple`, required: false)
  Select one or multiple categories you are interested in (Real Estate, Properties, Services).
- **7. Address** (`text`, required: false)
  Enter your address for future reference and follow-up.
- **8. Description** (`note`, required: false)
  Briefly describe your goals, needs, or expectations for a more personalized approach.
- **9. Lead Source** (`select_one`, required: false)
  Select how you got here (Google, Social, Mail, Other).
- **10. Contact Person** (`text`, required: false)
  Enter the name of the person to contact, if applicable.
- **11. Department** (`text`, required: false)
  Indicate the department or team you would like to interact with.
- **12. Role** (`text`, required: false)
  Enter your role or position within the organization.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required and optional fields accurately and consistently.
- Review your entries before submitting the form.
- This form is intended for internal use, and the information will be used to tailor our marketing efforts to your needs.
