# learning_motivation_study_group_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The learning motivation study group registration form is used to create a new study group for learning motivation purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the group type on page 1 to determine which type of group you are registering for.
2. Fill out the required fields on each page, which may include:
	* Name
	* Email
	* Start and end dates
	* Time
	* Address
	* Phone
	* Note
	* Session count
	* Max sessions
	* Session length
	* Start and end time
	* Duration
	* Days per week
	* Schedule
	* Location
	* Timezone
	* Timezone offset
	* Timezone name
	* Timezone description
	* Timezone offset minutes
	* Timezone offset minutes description
3. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Type** (`group_type`, select_one, required): Select "yes" to register for a study group or "no" to cancel your registration.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the study group.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the study group.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the study group.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the study group.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the study group.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the study group.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the study group.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes for the study group.
* **Session Count** (`session_count`, number, optional): Enter the number of sessions for the study group.
* **Max Sessions** (`max_sessions`, number, optional): Enter the maximum number of sessions for the study group.
* **Session Length** (`session_length`, number, optional): Enter the length of each session for the study group.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the study group.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the study group.
* **Duration** (`duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the study group.
* **Days per Week** (`days_per_week`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days of the week the study group will meet.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, time, optional): Enter the schedule of the study group.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the study group.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, optional): Select the timezone of the study group.
* **Timezone Offset** (`timezone_offset`, number, optional): Enter the timezone offset of the study group.
* **Timezone Name** (`timezone_name`, text, optional): Enter the timezone name of the study group.
* **Timezone Description** (`timezone_description`, text, optional): Enter the timezone description of the study group.
* **Timezone Offset Minutes** (`timezone_offset_minutes`, number, optional): Enter the timezone offset minutes of the study group.
* **Timezone Offset Minutes Description** (`timezone_offset_minutes_description`, text, optional): Enter the timezone offset minutes description of the study group.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for help.
