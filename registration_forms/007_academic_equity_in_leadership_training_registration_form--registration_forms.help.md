# Academic Equity In Leadership Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Academic Equity In Leadership Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants about themselves to ensure they have the right accommodations and experience tailored to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out each field carefully and accurately. If you have any questions or need assistance, do not hesitate to reach out to the contact provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your institutional email address for communication purposes.
* **Job title or role** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): If you have a job title or role, please enter it. This will help the training program organizers know your position in the institution.
* **Department or unit** (`department`, `text`, optional): If you have a department or unit, please enter it. This will help the training program organizers understand your context.
* **Motivation for attending** (`motivation`, `text`, required): Please provide a brief statement explaining why you want to attend the training. This will help the training program organizers understand your learning goals.
* **Accessibility needs** (`accessibility_needs`, `text`, optional): If you have any accessibility needs, please indicate them here. This will help the training program organizers make necessary arrangements.
* **Prior equity training experience** (`prior_training`, `select_one`, optional): If you have prior equity training experience, please select the relevant option. If not applicable, select None.
* **Session format preference** (`session_preferences`, `select_one`, required): Please select your preferred session format (In person, Online, or Hybrid).
* **Emergency contact name and phone** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, optional): If you have an emergency contact, please provide their name and phone number. This is an optional field.
