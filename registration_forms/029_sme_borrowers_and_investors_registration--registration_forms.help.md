# SME Borrowers And Investors Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering Small or Medium-Sized Enterprises (SMEs) with their basic information, contact details, and financial and legal details. It seems that the form is designed to gather various types of information about the SMEs, including their category, industry, location, type, status, size, and description.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all required fields (marked as 'required') with accurate and valid information.
2. For optional fields, feel free to provide any relevant information you have.
3. Make sure to select the correct 'sme_status' as it may impact the way your SME is classified.
4. Check the 'sme_agreement' and ensure you understand and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`id: 1`): Enter the name and other basic details of the SME.
* **Contact Information** (`id: 2`): Provide the contact details of the SME.
* **Business Details** (`id: 3`): Enter the details about the SME's business.
* **Financial Details** (`id: 4`): Enter the financial information about the SME.
* **Legal Details** (`id: 5`): Enter the legal information about the SME.
* **SME Details** (`id: 6`): Enter a brief description of the SME.
* **Agreement** (`id: 7`): Check the box to agree or disagree with the terms and conditions.
* **SME Category** (`id: 8`): Select one or more categories that apply to the SME.
* **SME Industry** (`id: 9`): Select one or more industries that apply to the SME.
* **SME Location** (`id: 10`): Select one or more locations that apply to the SME.
* **SME Type** (`id: 11`): Select the type of SME.
* **SME Status** (`id: 12`): Select the status of the SME.
* **SME Size** (`id: 13`): Select the size of the SME.
* **SME Description** (`id: 14`): Enter a brief description of the SME.
* **SME Website** (`id: 15`): Enter the website of the SME.
* **SME Telephone** (`id: 16`): Enter the telephone number of the SME.
* **SME Email** (`id: 17`): Enter the email address of the SME.
* **SME Notes** (`id: 18`): Enter any additional notes about the SME.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, try to contact the form administrators for guidance.
