# Product Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Registration Form is designed to collect information about new products being registered for use in our system. This form should be completed by customers or partners to provide a clear and concise overview of the product, its name, description, price, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling in the **Company Name** field with your company's name.
2. Next, enter the **Product Name** and provide a brief description of the product in the **Product Description** field.
3. If applicable, fill in the **Price** of the product.
4. Select the correct **Type** of product from the options provided.
5. Choose the option to **Submit** the form, or **Cancel** if you need to revisit or correct any information.
6. If your company has a registered email address, please enter it in the **Email** field.
7. Optionally, add your **Phone** number for contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Company Name**: Enter the name of the company registering the product.
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Text
  - **Description:** Enter the name of your company.
- **Product Name**: Enter the name of the product being registered.
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Text
  - **Description:** Enter a brief name for the product.
- **Product Description**: Enter a brief description of the product.
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Text
  - **Description:** Give a short summary of the product's features and purpose.
- **Price**: Enter the price of the product (if applicable).
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Number
  - **Description:** Enter the price of the product (in currency of your choice).
- **Type**: Select the type of product from the options provided.
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Select One
  - **Description:** Choose the type of product (e.g., hardware, software, etc.).
- **Submit**: Confirm that the form is complete and accurate, and choose to submit or cancel.
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Select One
  - **Description:** Confirm form completion and choose to submit or cancel.
- **Email**: Enter your company's email address (if applicable).
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Email
  - **Description:** Enter your company's registered email address.
- **Phone**: Enter your company's phone number (if applicable).
  - **Required:** No
  - **Type:** Text
  - **Description:** Enter your company's phone number for contact.
