# Management Effectiveness Review Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a Management Effectiveness Review (MER) in the company's internal systems. This form is used to capture essential information about the review, including the leader's name, focus areas, and expectations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and relevant information.
2. Select the correct review session from the list.
3. Enter the areas you'd like the consultant to focus on, separated by commas.
4. Describe your expectations for the consultant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**: This is the main form section where you can enter your registration details.
* **Leader Name**: Enter the name of the leader who will be attending the review session.
* **Review Sessions**: Select the session for which you are registering the MER.
* **Focus Areas**: List the areas you'd like the consultant to focus on during the review session, separated by commas (e.g., "Operational Excellence, Strategic Planning, Team Development").
* **Expectations**: Describe your expectations for the consultant to help ensure they understand their goals and objectives for the review session.

## Tips

* Ensure that you have not already registered a MER for this session before submitting this form.
* Double-check that you have filled in all the required fields correctly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact your local administrator for assistance.
