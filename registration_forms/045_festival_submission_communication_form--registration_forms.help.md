# festival_submission_communication_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from artists and vendors for festival submissions. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure that your submission is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information. You may skip any fields that do not apply to your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Artist Information**: This field is where you will provide information about yourself as the artist.
* **Art Information**: Use this field to describe your art, including any relevant details about the piece or performance.
* **Art Type**: Check 'Yes' if your submission is art, 'No' if it is a performance. This will help us understand the type of submission you are making.
* **Artist Statement**: Write a brief statement about your art or performance.
* **Contact Information**: This field is for you to provide your contact information so we can reach out to you.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can communicate with you about your submission.
* **Upload Files**: If you have any files to attach, such as images or videos, you can do so here.
* **Art Media**: Check the boxes next to the types of media your art or performance uses (e.g. painting, photography, music, etc.).
* **Art Price**: Enter the price of your art or performance.
* **Price Currency**: Select the currency of the price of your art or performance.
* **Vendor Information**: This field is for vendors to provide information about their business.
* **Vendor Statement**: Write a brief statement about your business.
* **Vendor Contact Information**: This field is for vendors to provide their contact information.
* **Vendor Email**: Enter the email address of your business so we can communicate with you.
* **Vendor Upload Files**: If you have any files to attach, such as business documents, you can do so here.
* **Performance Information**: This field is for performers to provide information about their performance.
* **Performance Type**: Check 'Yes' if your submission is a performance, 'No' if it is art. This will help us understand the type of submission you are making.
* **Performance Media**: Check the boxes next to the types of media your performance uses (e.g. music, dance, theater, etc.).
* **Performance Price**: Enter the price of your performance.
* **Performance Price Currency**: Select the currency of the price of your performance.
* **Artist Statement 2**: Write a brief statement about your art or performance.
* **Vendor Statement 2**: Write a brief statement about your business.
* **Performance Statement**: Write a brief statement about your performance.
* **Vendor Statement 3**: Write a brief statement about your business.
* **Performance Upload Files**: If you have any files to attach, such as videos or images, you can do so here.</thinking>
