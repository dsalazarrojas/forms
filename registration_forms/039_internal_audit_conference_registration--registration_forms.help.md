# Internal Audit Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Internal Audit Conference Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees on their session preferences and attendee details. This form is used to facilitate the organization and management of the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your session preferences for each session. You can choose 'Yes' or 'No' for each session.
2. Enter your attendee details, including any requirements you may have.
3. Confirm your submission and submission status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Preferences** (Yes/No, id: 1): Select whether or not you would like to attend each session. 
* **Attendee Details** (Text, id: 2): Enter your name, role, and any other details about yourself.
* **Session 1** (Yes/No, id: 4): Select whether or not you would like to attend Session 1.
* **Session 1 Description** (Text, id: 5): This field is not applicable as Session 1 is not actually required.
* **Session 2** (Yes/No, id: 6): Select whether or not you would like to attend Session 2.
* **Session 2 Description** (Text, id: 7): This field is not applicable as Session 2 is not actually required.
* **Session 2 Preferences** (Yes/No, id: 8): Select whether or not you would like to have these preferences for Session 2.
* **Attendee Requirements** (Text, id: 10): Enter any requirements you have, for example, accommodation or dietary needs.
* **Requirement 1** (Yes/No, id: 11): Select whether or not you require this option.
* **Requirement 1 Description** (Text, id: 12): This field is not applicable as Requirement 1 is not actually required.
* **Requirement 2** (Yes/No, id: 13): Select whether or not you require this option.
* **Requirement 2 Description** (Text, id: 14): This field is not applicable as Requirement 2 is not actually required.
* **Notes** (Text, id: 15): Enter any additional information or notes about your registration.
* **Session 3** (Yes/No, id: 16): Select whether or not you would like to attend Session 3.
* **Session 3 Description** (Text, id: 17): This field is not applicable as Session 3 is not actually required.
* **Session 3 Preferences** (Yes/No, id: 18): Select whether or not you would like to have these preferences for Session 3.
* **Session 4** (Yes/No, id: 19): Select whether or not you would like to attend Session 4.
* **Session 4 Description** (Text, id: 20): This field is not applicable as Session 4 is not actually required.
* **Session 4 Preferences** (Yes/No, id: 21): Select whether or not you would like to have these preferences for Session 4.
* **Session 5** (Yes/No, id: 22): Select whether or not you would like to attend Session 5.
* **Session 5 Description** (Text, id: 23): This field is not applicable as Session 5 is not actually required.
* **Session 5 Preferences** (Yes/No, id: 24): Select whether or not you would like to have these preferences for Session 5.
* **Submission** (Yes/No, id: 25): Confirm your submission.
* **Submission Confirmation** (Yes/No, id: 26): Confirm that you have submitted your registration.
* **Submission Status** (Active/Inactive, id: 27): This field is not actually required but displays the status of your submission.

Note: The fields related to Sessions 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5, and Requirements 1 and 2 are not actually required in the provided YAML, but have been included in the explanation for completeness.
