# Indoor Air Quality Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Indoor Air Quality Program Registration Form is used to register facilities that wish to participate in the Indoor Air Quality Program. This program aims to improve indoor air quality and provide a healthy environment for all.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Review the form and ensure that you have all the necessary information ready.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form to initiate the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Facility Name (2)
* **Facility Name**: Enter the name of the facility you are registering.
* This is a required field.

### Facility Address (3)
* **Facility Address**: Enter the address of the facility you are registering.
* This is a required field.

### Contact Name (4)
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the facility.
* This is a required field.

### Email (5)
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address of the contact person.
* This is a required field.

### Phone Number (6)
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* This is a required field.

### Service Need (7)
* **Service Need**: Select 'Yes' if the facility needs indoor air quality services.
* Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

### Page Two (8)
* **Page Two**: This is an informational page.

### Service Need (Service) (9)
* **Service Need (Service)**: Select 'Yes' if the service is needed for specific facilities or locations.
* Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

### Service Need (Need) (10)
* **Service Need (Need)**: Select 'Yes' if the service is needed for specific needs.
* Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

### Additional Information (11)
* **Additional Information**: Provide any additional information about the service need.
* This is a required field.

### Submit (12)
* **Submit**: Click to submit the registration form.
* This is a required field.

### Cancel (13)
* **Cancel**: Click to cancel the submission and start over.
* This is a required field.

Note: The "Cancel" field is not actually required, it's just a button to cancel the submission process.

Tips:
- Ensure that the email address and phone number entered are accurate and valid.
- Be specific when selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for Service Need and Service Need (Service) and Service Need (Need).
- Provide any additional information about the service need in the Additional Information field.
