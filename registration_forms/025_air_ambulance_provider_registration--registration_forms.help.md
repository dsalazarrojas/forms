# Air Ambulance Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of filling out the Air Ambulance Provider Registration form. It will explain each field and its significance to ensure a smooth and accurate submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2.  Fill in the required fields as prompted.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Provider Name** (`provider_name`, text, required): Enter your organization's name as it appears on your official documentation.
*   **Base Location** (`base_location`, text, required): Specify your primary airport or base location.
*   **Service Area** (`service_area`, select_one, required): Select the geographical coverage area of your services. Options include Local / Regional, National, and International.
*   **Aircraft Types Operated** (`aircraft_types`, select_multiple, required): Check all the types of aircraft your organization operates (e.g., Rotary Wing (Helicopter), Fixed Wing (Jet/Prop), Ground Ambulance (Support)).
*   **Medical Capabilities** (`medical_capabilities`, select_multiple, required): Select the level of care provided by your organization, such as Basic Life Support (BLS), Advanced Life Support (ALS), Critical Care / ICU, Neonatal Transport, or Organ Transplant Support).
*   **License / Certification Number** (`license_number`, text, required): Enter your license or certification number, which may be an FAA or Medical Board ID.
*   **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_info`, text, required): Specify your liability insurance provider.
*   **Primary Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Enter the name of your administrator or manager.
*   **24/7 Dispatch Phone** (`dispatch_phone`, text, required): Enter the emergency phone number.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your organization's email address.
*   **Website** (`website`, text, optional): Provide your organization's website URL (optional).
*   **Certification Upload** (`upload_cert_note`, note, optional): If applicable, upload copies of your CAMTS or other accreditations to the portal (optional).

Please ensure all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly. If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
