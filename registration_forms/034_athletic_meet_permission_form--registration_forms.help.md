# Athletic Meet Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Athletic Meet Permission Form is designed to collect information from athletes and their parents or guardians to ensure their safety and well-being during athletic meets. It is a requirement for all athletes to complete this form before participating in a meet.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's athlete information:
	* Enter your child's full name as it appears on their official documents (e.g., birth certificate).
	* Enter your child's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
	* Select your child's gender.
2. Enter your child's school information:
	* Enter the name of your child's school.
	* Select your child's grade level.
3. Enter meet information:
	* Enter the name of the meet your child will be participating in.
	* Enter the date of the meet.
	* Enter the location of the meet.
4. Provide information about your child's health and medication:
	* List any medical conditions your child has.
	* List any medications your child is currently taking.
	* Specify if you give permission for your child to receive medical treatment if needed.
5. Provide information about your child's insurance:
	* Enter the name of your child's health insurance provider.
	* Enter your child's insurance policy or member number.
6. Enter contact information:
	* Enter your full name.
	* Enter your contact phone number.
	* Enter your contact email address.
7. Enter emergency contact information:
	* If different from your contact information, enter your emergency contact's full name.
	* Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
8. Provide information about your child's meet participation:
	* List any events your child will be participating in.
	* Specify how your child's meals will be arranged.
9. Provide information about your child's photo and video:
	* Specify if you allow photos and videos to be taken of your child during the meet.
10. Sign and date the form:
	* Sign your name and date as the parent or guardian of your child.
	* Specify if you agree to the code of conduct and permission for medical treatment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Athlete Full Name**: Enter your child's full name as it appears on their official documents (e.g., birth certificate).
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in MM-DD-YYYY format.
* **Gender**: Select your child's gender.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Grade Level**: Select your child's current grade level.
* **Meet Name**: Enter the name of the meet your child will be participating in.
* **Meet Date**: Enter the date of the meet.
* **Meet Location**: Enter the location of the meet.
* **Departure Time**: Enter the time your child's team will depart for the meet.
* **Return Time**: Enter the expected return time for your child's team.
* **Transportation Method**: Select how your child will be transported to the meet (e.g., school bus, team van, parent-provided vehicle).
* **Parent Pickup Arrangements**: Specify if you will pick up your child at the meet location.
* **Events Participating In**: List any events your child will be participating in.
* **Meal Arrangements**: Specify how your child's meals will be arranged (e.g., team-provided, packed lunch, purchased at the venue).
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: List any food allergies or dietary restrictions your child has.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions your child has.
* **Current Medications**: List any medications your child is currently taking.
* **Medication Administration Permission**: Specify if you give permission for medical staff to administer medication if needed.
* **Health Insurance Provider**: Enter the name of your child's health insurance provider.
* **Insurance Policy Number**: Enter your child's insurance policy or member number.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your full name as the parent or guardian of your child.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: If different from your contact information, enter your emergency contact's full name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Medical Treatment Permission**: Specify if you give permission for medical staff to provide treatment if needed.
* **Photo and Video Permission**: Specify if you allow photos and videos to be taken of your child during the meet.
* **Code of Conduct Agreement**: Specify if you agree to the meet's code of conduct.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Sign your name and date as the parent or guardian of your child.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your signature.

**Tips**

* Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Review the form for any errors or missing information before submitting.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the meet organizers or coaches for clarification.
* Make sure to sign and date the form before submitting it.
