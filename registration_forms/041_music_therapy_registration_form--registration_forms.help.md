# Music Therapy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Therapy Registration Form is designed to collect information from registrants to facilitate the music therapy process. It's essential to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure proper communication between you, the music therapist, and the registrant.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and contact number.
2. Enter your session preferences, including the date and time you'd like to attend the session.
3. Specify any additional information or notes about your session.
4. Agree to the registrant consent terms.
5. Optionally, provide your payment method and additional payment information.
6. Enter any notes or comments you'd like to add.
7. Confirm your session preferences, including the date and time you'd like to attend the session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registrant Information (1)** (Required)
    This field is for your personal contact information.
* **Email (2)** (Required)
    Please enter your email address so we can contact you about your session.
* **Name (3)** (Required)
    Enter your name to identify yourself in our system.
* **Contact Number (4)** (Required)
    Provide your contact number so we can reach out to you.
* **Session Info (5)** (Required)
    Enter any additional information about your session preferences.
* **Preferred Session Date (6)** (Required)
    Select a date and time you'd prefer to attend the session.
* **Session Details (7)** (Required)
    Add any additional information about your session.
* **Registrant Consent (8)** (Required)
    Please agree to our registrant consent terms.
* **Music Therapist Notes (9)** (Optional)
    Add any notes or comments for the music therapist.
* **Contact Person (10)** (Optional)
    If you have a contact person, enter their name and contact information.
* **Address (11)** (Optional)
    Enter your home address for correspondence purposes.
* **City (12)** (Optional)
    Enter your city for location-based correspondence.
* **State (13)** (Optional)
    Enter your state for location-based correspondence.
* **Zip (14)** (Optional)
    Enter your zip code for location-based correspondence.
* **Country (15)** (Optional)
    Enter your country for international correspondence.
* **Session Notes (16)** (Optional)
    Add any additional comments or notes about your session.
* **Music Therapy Session (17)** (Optional)
    Enter the date and time you'd like to attend the session.
* **Session Length (18)** (Optional)
    Enter the length of your session in minutes.
* **Payment Method (19)** (Optional)
    Choose your payment method, either cash, credit card, or online payment.
* **Payment Info (20)** (Optional)
    Enter any additional payment information.
* **Notes (21)** (Optional)
    Add any general notes or comments.
* **Session Date (22)** (Optional)
    Confirm the date and time you'd like to attend the session.
* **Session Time (23)** (Optional)
    Confirm the time you'd like to attend the session.
* **Session Duration (24)** (Optional)
    Enter the length of your session in minutes.
* **Music Therapist (25)** (Optional)
    Enter the name of your music therapist, if applicable.

## Tips
- Be honest and accurate when filling out this form.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Please review your information carefully before submitting the form.
