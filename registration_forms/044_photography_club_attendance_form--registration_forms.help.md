# Photography Club Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Photography Club Attendance Form is used to record the attendance of members at club meetings. This form helps the club administrators to keep track of who attended each meeting, their contact information, and any comments they may have about the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **First Name**, **Last Name**, and **Email** fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your **Phone Number** if you would like to be contacted about the meeting.
3. Enter your **Photo ID Number** if you have been assigned one by the club.
4. Select the **Date** and **Time** of the meeting you attended.
5. Choose whether you were an **Attendee** or not at the meeting.
6. Add any **Comments** you may have about the meeting.
7. Finally, click the submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): Enter your email address that can be used to contact you about club activities.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you about club activities.
* **Photo ID** (`Photo ID`, number, optional): Enter your Photography Club Photo ID number assigned by the club.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Select the date of the meeting you attended.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): Select the time of the meeting you attended.
* **Attendee** (`Attendee`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you were an attendee at the meeting.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Add any comments or feedback you have about the meeting.
* **Submitted By** (`Submitted By`, text, optional): This field is reserved for internal use only.
 
## Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely to ensure your attendance is recorded correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the club administrators.
* If you are unable to attend a meeting, please let us know in advance so we can adjust the attendance records accordingly.
