# Cybersecurity Specialist Candidate Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is designed to collect information from candidates applying for a Cybersecurity Specialist position. It's essential that you provide accurate and relevant information to increase your chances of being considered for this role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Select the correct options for each field, taking into account the hints provided.
3. Enter your information accurately and in the correct format.
4. Save your progress as you go along, as the form does not support saving and loading partially completed forms.
5. Submit your completed form once you have reviewed and verified all the information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Candidate Information** (`candidate_info`, `select_one`, optional): Please select "Yes" if you are a suitable candidate for the position, or "No" otherwise.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `number`, optional): Please enter your contact information, including a valid phone number. This will help us reach you if we need to.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Resume** (`resume`, `text`, optional): Upload your resume or provide a link to a publicly accessible URL.
* **Professional Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your professional experience, including your job title, employer, and a short description.
* **Education** (`education`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all relevant education levels that apply.
* **Work History** (`work_history`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all relevant work history types that apply.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `select_one`, optional): Please select all relevant technical skills that you have acquired.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, optional): Please provide the names and contact information of your professional references.
* **Languages** (`languages`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all languages you are proficient in.
* **Certifications** (`certifications`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select all relevant certifications you have acquired.
* **Work Status** (`work_status`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you are actively working or not.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `select_one`, optional): Please select whether you have a job title or not.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the type of job you are applying for.
* **Work Location** (`work_location`, `select_one`, optional): Please select the location where you would work.

## Tips
* Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting.
* Use a clear and concise format for text inputs.
* Make sure to provide valid and correct formatting for email addresses and phone numbers.
* Save your progress as you go along to avoid losing your work.
