# Graphic Design Exam Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who are taking a graphic design exam. It is meant to be completed by students to register for the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Enter your student ID in the "Student ID" field.
3. Enter your student email address in the "Student Email" field.
4. Enter your student phone number in the "Student Phone" field.
5. Choose the date when the exam started in the "Exam Start Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Please enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional): Enter your university ID number, if applicable.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your university email address, if applicable.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, if applicable.
* **Exam Start Date** (`exam_start_date`, date, optional): Select the date when the exam started.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check the accuracy of the information you enter.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your instructor or administrator.
