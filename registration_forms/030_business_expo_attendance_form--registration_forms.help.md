# Business Expo Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Business Expo Attendance Form is used to collect information about attendees and their companies to provide them with relevant details about the expo. This form is intended for those who want to attend the expo and take advantage of its networking opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Expo Information section:
	* Provide the name of the expo you're attending.
	* Select the date of the expo.
	* Enter the location of the expo.
2. Fill out the Attendee Information section:
	* Enter your first name.
	* Enter your last name.
	* Enter your email address for expo updates and follow-up.
	* Enter your phone number.
3. Fill out the Company Information section:
	* Enter your company's name.
	* If you have a website, enter it.
4. Select your industry and company size:
	* Choose your industry from the list.
	* Select the number of employees in your company.
5. Fill out the Attendance Details section:
	* Select the type of attendance (e.g., Visitor/Attendee, Exhibitor/Vendor, Sponsor, Speaker, Media).
	* Choose which days you plan to attend.
6. Fill out the Interests and Goals section (optional):
	* If you have a primary interest at the expo, enter it.
	* If you have any business goals, select them from the list.
7. Fill out the Logistics section (optional):
	* If you have any dietary restrictions, select them.
8. Finally, add any additional information about yourself or your company.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Expo Name** (Expo Name, text, required): Enter the name of the expo you're attending.
* **Expo Date** (Expo Date, date, required): Enter the date of the expo in the correct format (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Expo Location** (Expo Location, text, required): Enter the location of the expo.
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for expo updates and follow-up.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Industry** (Industry, select_one, required): Choose your industry from the list.
* **Company Size** (Company Size, select_one, required): Select the number of employees in your company.
* **Attendance Type** (Attendance Type, select_one, required): Select the type of attendance (e.g., Visitor/Attendee, Exhibitor/Vendor, Sponsor, Speaker, Media).
* **Days Attending** (Days Attending, select_multiple, required): Choose which days you plan to attend.
* **Special Status** (Special Status, text, optional): If you have any special status or requirements, enter them here.
* **Primary Interest** (Primary Interest, text, required): Enter what you're most interested in at the expo.
* **Business Goals** (Business Goals, select_multiple, optional): If you have any business goals, select them from the list.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (Dietary Restrictions, select_one, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Additional Information** (Additional Information, text, optional): Add any additional information about yourself or your company.
