# Interest Registration For One On One - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to allow users to register their interest in one-on-one meetings. The form will automatically prevent duplicate registrations from the same IP address and will not allow users to register the same interest more than once.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details in the "Personal Details" section.
2. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" section.
3. Describe your interest in the "Interest Description" section.
4. Indicate your available time in the "Available Time" section.
5. Select your interest level in the "Select Interest" section.
6. Confirm your interest level in the "Confirm Interest" section.
7. Confirm your interest level again in the "Confirm Interest 2" section.
8. Select whether you would like to be matched with an expert in the "Select Expert" section.
9. Confirm your match with an expert in the "Confirm Expert" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Details** (personal-details, text, required): Enter your name and any other personal details you'd like to share.
* **Contact Details** (contact-details, text, required): Enter your contact information so we can get back to you.
* **Interest Description** (interest-description, text, required): Describe the topic you're interested in and why you'd like to be matched with an expert.
* **Available Time** (available-time, time, required): Indicate your available time for the one-on-one meeting.
* **Select Interest** (select-interest, select_one, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are interested in being matched with an expert, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Confirm Interest** (confirm-interest, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your interest level by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Confirm Interest 2** (confirm-interest-2, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your interest level again by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Select Expert** (select-expert, select_multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you would like to be matched with an expert, and 'No' otherwise.
* **Confirm Expert** (confirm-expert, select_multiple, optional): Confirm your match with an expert by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
