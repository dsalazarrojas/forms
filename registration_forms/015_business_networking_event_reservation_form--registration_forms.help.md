# Business Networking Event Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from event attendees for effective event organization and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and truthful information.
2. Provide details about your company and job title to help the event organizers understand your background.
3. Choose your dietary preference for event catering.
4. Specify any accessibility requirements for a comfortable event experience.
5. Select the topics of interest to help tailor the event schedule.
6. Indicate if you have attended previous events, and provide your referral source.
7. Consent to receive event updates and communication.
8. Agree to the event terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Reservation Date** (`reservation_date`): The date you are making this reservation for.
* **First Name** (`attendee_first_name`): Your given name.
* **Last Name** (`attendee_last_name`): Your surname.
* **Email Address** (`attendee_email`): Your primary contact email.
* **Phone Number** (`attendee_phone`): Your contact phone number.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`): The name of your company or organization.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Your position or role in your company.
* **Primary Business Focus** (`business_focus`): What does your company do?
* **Event Name** (`event_selection`): The name of the event you are reserving.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`): Date of the event.
* **Number of Additional Guests** (`additional_guests`): Number of people accompanying you.
* **Guest Names and Titles** (`guest_details`): Names and titles of your additional guests.
* **Dietary Preference** (`dietary_preference`): Specify your dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_other_details`): If other, provide further details about your dietary preference.
* **Accessibility Requirements** (`accessibility_requirements`): Indicate any special accommodations needed.
* **Networking Interests** (`networking_interests`): Select the types of connections you are seeking.
* **Topics of Interest** (`conference_topics`): Select topics you are interested in.
* **Previous Event Attendance** (`prior_events`): Have you attended previous events of this type?
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`): How did you find out about this event?
* **Consent to Communications** (`consent_communications`): Agree to receive event updates and communication.
* **Agree to Terms** (`terms_agreement`): Accept the event terms and conditions.
