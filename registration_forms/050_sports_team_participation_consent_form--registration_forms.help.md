# Sports Team Participation Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for parents or guardians to provide consent for their child's participation in sports team activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form completely and accurately to ensure we can contact you about your child's participation in the team.
2. Enter your child's date of birth to help us track their age and eligibility for team activities.
3. Enter your contact information so we can reach out to you with updates and important information about the team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Sports Team Participation Consent Form 1**: This is the main question for the sports team participation consent.
* **Parent First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Parent Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates and important information about the team.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth to help us track their age and eligibility for team activities.
