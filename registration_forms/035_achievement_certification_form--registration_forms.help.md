# Achievement Certification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is an Achievement Certification Form designed for organizations or institutions to recognize and document a person's accomplishments. It collects information from the recipient, including their name, email, and certification details, to generate both digital and printed certificates. The form is customizable and can be used for various purposes, such as educational or professional certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **Recipient Full Name** as it should appear on the certificate.
2.  Enter the **Certificate Title**, e.g., Excellence in Financial Management.
3.  Select the **Date of Achievement**, which represents the date of your accomplishment.
4.  Enter the **Course or Project Name** related to your achievement.
5.  Choose your **Certification Level**, such as Level 1 - Fundamental, Level 2 - Intermediate, Level 3 - Advanced, or Mastery.
6.  Enter the name of your **Instructor or Supervisor**, who verified your achievement.
7.  Provide your **Recipient Email for Delivery**, which will be used for digital certificate delivery.
8.  Select your **Delivery Preference**, either digital PDF or Printed Certificate.
9.  If selecting printed delivery, enter your **Mailing Address** for the certificate to be mailed to.
10. Enter the **Internal Verification Code** (optional, for organization tracking).
11.  Enter the **Authorizing Official Title**, e.g., Program Director, HR VP.
12.  Finally, select the **Issue Date** for the certificate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Recipient Full Name**: Enter the name as it should appear on the certificate.
* **Certificate Title**: Choose a title for your certification, e.g., Excellence in Financial Management.
* **Date of Achievement**: The date of your accomplishment or achievement.
* **Course or Project Name**: Provide context for your achievement, e.g., a course or project name.
* **Certification Level**: Select your level of achievement, such as Level 1 - Fundamental, Level 2 - Intermediate, Level 3 - Advanced, or Mastery.
* **Instructor or Supervisor Name**: The person verifying your achievement.
* **Recipient Email for Delivery**: Enter your email to receive the digital certificate.
* **Delivery Preference**: Choose between digital PDF or printed certificate delivery.
*   **Mailing Address**: Required for printed certificate delivery only.
*   **Internal Verification Code**: An optional field for organization tracking purposes.
*   **Authorizing Official Title**: The title of the person issuing the certification.
*   **Issue Date**: The date the certificate was issued.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you need to modify your submission, you can do so by contacting the form administrator.
- For printed certificate delivery, ensure the mailing address is correct and complete.
