# Workforce Retention Panel Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workforce Retention Panel Registration form is designed to collect information from HR leaders and participants for the workforce retention panel. This form aims to gather data on their areas of expertise and consent for the panel's activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section with your relevant information.
2. Select your areas of expertise from the provided options.
3. Provide your consent for the panel's activities in the note field.
4. Enter your HR leader's name and details in the corresponding field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Details
• **participant_details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details about yourself.

### Expertise Areas
• **expertise_areas** (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, required: false): Select all the areas where you have expertise. You can choose multiple areas from the provided options.

### Panel Consent
• **panel_consent** (`panel_consent`, note, required: false): Provide your consent for the panel's activities. Please type out your consent statement.

### HR Leader
• **hr_leader** (`hr_leader`, text, required: false): Enter your HR leader's name and details if you have one.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all the required fields.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to form@gic.mx.
