# Dynamic Dropdown Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect various types of user information, including personal details, time information, and selection fields. It's meant to be a template that can be easily customized for different use cases.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated by *).
2. For selection fields (e.g., "Select One", "Select Multiple"), choose one or more options from the dropdown list.
3. Enter any additional information or notes in the provided text fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your first name in this field.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your last name in this field.
* **Date of Birth** (`dob`, date, optional)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time of day in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter your email address in the format [name]@[domain].
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional)
	+ Choose one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose one or more options from the dropdown list.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information or notes.
* **Date** (`date_entry`, date, optional)
	+ Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.

	+ Enter a time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
