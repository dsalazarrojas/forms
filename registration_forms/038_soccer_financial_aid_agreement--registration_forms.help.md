# soccer_financial_aid_agreement - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from players who are seeking financial aid for soccer-related expenses. Please fill out the form below to provide the necessary details for processing your request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Player ID** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Club Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Player Name** in the third field.
4. Select the **Financial Aid Request** option that best describes the sport you are participating in (Soccer).
5. Enter the **Amount** of financial aid you are requesting in the fourth field.
6. Provide a brief **Purpose** for your financial aid request in the optional fifth field.
7. Enter the **Date** you began participating in the sport in the sixth field.
8. Enter the **End Date** you will be participating in the sport until in the seventh field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Player ID** (`player_id`, Number, Required): Enter your unique player ID number provided by your club.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`, Text, Required): Enter the name of the club or team you are participating in.
* **Player Name** (`player_name`, Text, Required): Enter your name as it appears on your official player documents.
* **Financial Aid Request** (`financial_aid_request`, Select, Required): Select the sport you are requesting financial aid for (e.g. Soccer).
* **Amount** (`amount`, Number, Required): Enter the amount of financial aid you are requesting.
* **Purpose** (`purpose`, Text, Optional): This is an optional field where you can provide additional context or details for your financial aid request.
* **Date** (`date_start`, Date, Required): Enter the date you began participating in the sport.
* **End Date** (`date_end`, Date, Required): Enter the date you will be participating in the sport until.
