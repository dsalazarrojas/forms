# real_estate_listing_boost_campaign_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information for a real estate listing campaign, specifically for properties for sale or lease.
## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the First name field with your first name.
2. Fill out the Last name field with your last name.
3. Enter a valid Email address in the Email field.
4. Select the Target listing type for the property you are registering.
5. Enter the address of the target listing in the Target listing address field.
6. Enter the price of the target listing in the Target listing price field.
7. Enter the city of the target listing in the Target listing city field.
8. Enter the state of the target listing in the Target listing state field.
9. Enter the zip of the target listing in the Target listing zip field.
10. Enter a description of the target listing in the Target listing description field (optional).
11. Select whether the listing has an agreement in the Listing agreement field.
12. Enter the date of the listing agreement in the Listing agreement date field.
13. Enter the time of the listing agreement in the Listing agreement time field.
14. Select whether the listing has an incentive in the Listing incentive field.
15. Enter the type of listing incentive in the Listing incentive type field (optional).
16. Enter the date of the listing incentive in the Listing incentive date field (optional).
17. Enter the time of the listing incentive in the Listing incentive time field (optional).
18. Enter the terms of the listing agreement in the Listing agreement terms field (optional).
19. Enter any campaign rules in the Campaign rules field (optional).
20. Enter any participant agreement in the Participant agreement field (optional).
21. Enter the date of the agreement in the Agreement date field (optional).
22. Enter the time of the agreement in the Agreement time field (optional).
23. Enter the end date of the campaign in the Campaign end date field (optional).
24. Enter the end time of the campaign in the Campaign end time field (optional).
25. Enter the tool assigned to this campaign in the Assigned tool field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Target listing type** (`target_listing_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of property (Commercial, Residential, Industrial).
* **Target listing address** (`target_listing_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the property.
* **Target listing price** (`target_listing_price`, number, required): Enter the price of the property.
* **Target listing city** (`target_listing_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the property.
* **Target listing state** (`target_listing_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the property.
* **Target listing zip** (`target_listing_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip of the property.
* **Target listing description** (`target_listing_description`, text, optional): Enter a description of the property (optional).
* **Listing agreement** (`listing_agreement`, select_one, required): Select whether the listing has an agreement (Yes, No, Maybe).
* **Listing agreement date** (`listing_agreement_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the listing agreement.
* **Listing agreement time** (`listing_agreement_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the listing agreement.
* **Listing incentive** (`listing_incentive`, select_one, required): Select whether the listing has an incentive (Yes, No).
* **Listing incentive type** (`listing_incentive_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of listing incentive (optional).
* **Listing incentive date** (`listing_incentive_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the listing incentive (optional).
* **Listing incentive time** (`listing_incentive_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the listing incentive (optional).
* **Listing agreement terms** (`listing_agreement_terms`, text, optional): Enter the terms of the listing agreement (optional).
* **Campaign rules** (`campaign_rules`, text, optional): Enter any campaign rules (optional).
* **Participant agreement** (`participants_agreement`, text, optional): Enter any participant agreement (optional).
* **Agreement date** (`agreement_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the agreement (optional).
* **Agreement time** (`agreement_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the agreement (optional).
* **Campaign end date** (`campaign_end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the campaign (optional).
* **Campaign end time** (`campaign_end_time`, time, optional): Enter the end time of the campaign (optional).
* **Assigned tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, optional): Enter the tool assigned to this campaign (optional).
