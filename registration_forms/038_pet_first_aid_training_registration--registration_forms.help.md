# Pet First Aid Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for pet first aid training courses. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in your First Name.
- Enter your Phone number.
- Choose the Training Date for the course.
- Select the Training Time for the course.
- Enter the Location of the training course.
- Fill in your Registration Date.
- If applicable, enter your email for contact purposes.
- If applicable, select your organization or affiliation.
- If applicable, enter the number of participants you wish to register.
- Choose your preferred Payment Method.
- If you have any additional comments or questions, fill in the Comments field.
- If you are registering on behalf of a pet, choose the Assigned Tool (chatjimmy or other).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Your name.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Your contact email address.
- **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, required): The date for the course.
- **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, required): The time for the course.
- **Location** (`location`, text, required): The location of the training course.
- **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants`, number, optional): The number of participants you wish to register.
- **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Your affiliation or organization.
- **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date you registered for the course.
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose between Online Payment or Offline Payment.
- **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Additional comments or questions.
- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Choose between chatjimmy or other.
