# After School Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and background information from volunteers who wish to join our after-school program. This information will be used to create a comprehensive profile of the volunteer and ensure a good match between the volunteer and the program needs.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your personal details, such as name, date of birth, address, phone number, and email address.
3. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, relationship, and phone number.
4. Specify your availability for volunteering, including days and time slots.
5. Indicate your preferred age group of students to work with and whether you are interested in tutoring or leading activities.
6. Share information about your education level, field of study, and occupation.
7. Answer questions about your previous experience with children and any special skills or talents you may have.
8. Consent to a background check as part of the volunteer registration process.
9. Provide references, including their name, relationship, phone number, and email address.
10. Agree to the terms and conditions of the volunteer program and maintain confidentiality of student information.
11. Finally, sign the electronic signature to submit your volunteer registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, required): Enter your complete legal name.
* **Preferred Name** (`preferred_name`, optional): What name would you like to be called by the students?
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, required): Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`gender`, required): Select your gender identity from the provided options.
* **Address** (`address`, required): Enter your current residential address.
* **City, State, ZIP Code** (`city_state_zip`, required): Enter the city, state, and ZIP code of your address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, required): Select the relationship of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`, required): Select the days of the week you are available to volunteer.
* **Available Time Slots** (`available_time_slots`, required): Select the time slots you are available to volunteer.
* **Expected Volunteer Duration** (`volunteer_duration`, required): Select how long you plan to volunteer with our program.
* **Preferred Age Group** (`preferred_age_group`, required): Select the age group of students you would like to work with.
* **Tutoring Interest** (`tutoring_interest`, required): Indicate whether you are interested in tutoring students.
* **Preferred Subjects to Tutor** (`preferred_subjects_to_tutor`, optional): If interested in tutoring, select the subjects you prefer to tutor.
* **Activity Leading Interest** (`activity_leading_interest`, required): Indicate whether you are interested in leading activities.
* **Preferred Activities to Lead** (`preferred_activities_to_lead`, optional): If interested in leading activities, select the activities you prefer to lead.
* **Education Level** (`education_level`, required): Select your highest level of education completed.
* **Field of Study** (`field_of_study`, optional): Enter the field of study you completed.
* **Employment Status** (`employment_status`, required): Select your current employment status.
* **Current Occupation** (`occupation`, optional): Enter your current occupation.
* **Previous Experience with Children** (`previous_experience_with_children`, required): Indicate whether you have previous experience working with children.
* **Details of Previous Experience** (`previous_experience_details`, optional): If yes, describe your previous experience working with children.
* **Special Skills or Talents** (`special_skills_talents`, optional): Enter any special skills or talents you may have.
* **Background Check Consent** (`background_check_consent`, required): Indicate whether you consent to a background check as part of the volunteer registration process.
* **References** (`references_section`, required): Please provide two references.
*   **Reference 1 Name** (`reference_1_name`, required): Enter the name of your first reference.
*   **Reference 1 Relationship** (`reference_1_relationship`, required): Select your relationship to the first reference.
*   **Reference 1 Phone** (`reference_1_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your first reference.
*   **Reference 1 Email** (`reference_1_email`, optional): Enter the email address of your first reference.
*   **Reference 2 Name** (`reference_2_name`, required): Enter the name of your second reference.
*   **Reference 2 Relationship** (`reference_2_relationship`, required): Select your relationship to the second reference.
*   **Reference 2 Phone** (`reference_2_phone`, required): Enter the phone number of your second reference.
*   **Reference 2 Email** (`reference_2_email`, optional): Enter the email address of your second reference.
* **Registration Agreement** (`terms_agreement`, required): Indicate whether you agree to the terms and conditions of the volunteer program.
* **Confidentiality Agreement** (`confidentiality_agreement`, required): Indicate whether you agree to maintain confidentiality of student information.
* **Electronic Signature** (`signature`, required): Sign to submit your volunteer registration.
