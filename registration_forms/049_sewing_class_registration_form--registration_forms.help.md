# sewing_class_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sewing Class Registration Form is designed to collect information from students registering for a sewing class. This form is meant for students who are new to the class and want to enroll in a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with your information.
* Make sure to select your preferred payment method and enter the corresponding payment amount.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the student.
* **Class Date** (`class_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to attend the class.
* **Class Time** (`class_time`, time, required): Enter the time you want to attend the class.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address (if you have one).
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number (if you have one).
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional): Enter your address (if you have one).
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your parent's email address (if you have one).
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter your parent's phone number (if you have one).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you plan to pay for the class (Cash, Check, or Credit Card).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the amount you plan to pay for the class.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the required fields (Class Date, Class Time, Payment Method, and Payment Amount).
* If you have any specific requests or notes, you can include them in the "Notes" field.
