# Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Coaching Registration Form is a critical step in the registration process for coaching services. This form collects essential information from users, including personal and contact details, medical and health status, coaching preferences, and payment information. The form is used to create a personalized coaching plan tailored to the registrant's needs and goals.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Enter your mailing address, city, and postal code.
3.  Provide your date of birth for age verification purposes.
4.  Select your preferred coaching program from the options provided.
5.  Choose your current skill level from the available options.
6.  Enter your preferred start date for coaching sessions.
7.  Select your preferred schedule from the available options.
8.  List the days you are available to attend coaching sessions.
9.  Enter any medical conditions or current injuries you may have.
10.  List any medications you are currently taking.
11.  Describe any physical limitations or restrictions you have.
12.  Specify your fitness or performance goals.
13.  Describe any previous coaching or training experience.
14.  Enter the name, phone number, and relationship of your emergency contact person.
15.  Select your preferred coach from the available options.
16.  Enter your preferred payment method for coaching sessions.
17.  Confirm the registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): This is your legal full name.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): This is your primary contact email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): This is your contact phone number.
*   **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, `text`, required): This is your full mailing address.
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, required): This is the city you reside in.
*   **State or Province** (`state_province`, `text`, required): This is your state or province of residence.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_postal_code`, `text`, required): This is your postal code.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): This is for age verification purposes.
*   **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required/optional): This field allows you to select your gender identity.
*   **Program Selection** (`program_selection`, `select_one`, required): Select the coaching program you wish to join.
*   **Current Skill Level** (`skill_level`, `select_one`, required): Choose your current skill level based on the provided options.
*   **Preferred Coach** (`preferred_coach`, `text`, required/optional): This field is optional, but selecting a preferred coach can help with scheduling and planning.
*   **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date`, `date`, required): Choose your preferred start date for coaching sessions.
*   **Schedule Preference** (`schedule_preference`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred schedule for coaching sessions.
*   **Days Available** (`days_available`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the days you are available to attend coaching sessions.
*   **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, required/optional): List any medical conditions or health issues that may impact your coaching experience.
*   **Current or Recent Injuries** (`injuries`, `text`, required/optional): List any current or recent injuries that may impact your coaching experience.
*   **Current Medications** (`medications`, `text`, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
*   **Physical Limitations** (`physical_limitations`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any physical limitations or restrictions you have.
*   **Fitness or Performance Goals** (`fitness_goals`, `text`, required): Describe your fitness or performance goals for the coaching program.
*   **Previous Training Experience** (`previous_training`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any previous coaching or training experience.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, `text`, required): Describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
*   **Primary Physician Name** (`physician_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your primary physician.
*   **Physician Phone** (`physician_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact phone number of your primary physician.
*   **Medical Clearance Obtained** (`medical_clearance`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have obtained medical clearance from your doctor.
*   **Liability Waiver Signed** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you have signed the liability waiver.
*   **Photo Release Authorization** (`photo_release`, `select_one`, optional): This field is optional, but selecting "Yes I Authorize" grants permission for promotional use of photos.
*   **Terms and Conditions Accepted** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions of the program.
*   **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select your payment method for coaching sessions.
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): This field is automatically populated with the current date.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy and completeness.
*   Select your preferred coach for scheduling and planning purposes.
*   List any medical conditions or injuries to ensure your safety and well-being.
*   Describe your current skill level and any previous coaching or training experience for optimal coaching results.
*   Confirm your emergency contact person and phone number for emergency situations.
*   Select your preferred schedule and days available for coaching sessions.
*   Choose your preferred payment method for coaching sessions.
*   Confirm your understanding of the terms and conditions of the program.

This help guide is designed to assist users in completing the Coaching Registration Form accurately and efficiently. By following these guidelines, you can ensure a smooth and successful coaching experience.
