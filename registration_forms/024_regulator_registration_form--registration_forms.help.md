# Regulator Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Regulator Registration Form is a tool used to collect registration information from regulators for various regulatory purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Name** in the designated field.
2. Provide your **Email** address.
3. Enter your **Phone** number.
4. Select your **Date Of Birth** or the date your regulatory body was established.
5. Enter your **Address** in the specified field.
6. Choose your **Regulatory Body** from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name**: Enter your personal or business name as it should appear in public records.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the date you were born or the date your regulatory body was established. Use the date format: MM/DD/YY or DD/MM/YY.
* **Address**: Enter your physical or business address.
* **Regulatory Body**: Select one of the options provided to specify your type of regulatory body.
