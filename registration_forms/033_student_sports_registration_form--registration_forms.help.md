# Student Sports Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students who wish to participate in sports activities. It gathers essential details about the student, including their contact information, medical history, and parental permission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the Parent Name in the "Parent Name" field.
2. Provide the contact information of the student's parent or guardian in the "Contact Number" field.
3. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
4. Specify the student's date of birth (DOB) in the "Student DOB" field.
5. Select the sport the student is interested in participating in the "Sport Name" field.
6. Choose the student's team in the "Team Name" field.
7. Enter the student's grade level in the "Student Grade" field.
8. Select the student's gender from the dropdown menu in the "Student Gender" field.
9. Enter the parent's phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
10. Provide the emergency contact's information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
11. Enter the student's school name in the "Student School" field.
12. Enter the student's grade level (e.g., 1st, 2nd, etc.) in the "Student Grade Level" field.
13. Enter the parent's email address in the "Parent Email" field.
14. Grant or deny permission for the student to participate in the "Parent Permission" field.
15. Optionally, provide any additional medical information or interests in the "Student Medical Info" fields.
16. Finally, click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, required): Provide the contact number of the student's parent or guardian.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student DOB** (`student_dob`, `date`, required): Specify the student's date of birth.
* **Sport Name** (`sport_name`, `text`, required): Select the sport the student is interested in participating in.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, `text`, required): Choose the student's team.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, `number`, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, `select_one`, required): Select the student's gender.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Provide the emergency contact's information.
* **Emergency Number** (`emergency_number`, `text`, required): Enter the emergency contact's number.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, `text`, required): Enter the student's school name.
* **Student Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, `number`, required): Enter the student's grade level.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter the parent's email address.
* **Parent Permission** (`parent_permission`, `select_multiple`, required): Grant or deny permission for the student to participate.
* **Student Interests** (`student_interests`, `text`, optional): Optionally, provide any additional interests or information about the student.
* **Student Medical Info** (`student_medical_info`, `note`, optional): Provide any medical information about the student.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): Enter any medical conditions the student has.
* **Medical Meds** (`medical_meds`, `text`, optional): List any medications the student is taking.
* **Medical Allergies** (`medical_allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies the student has.
* **Medical Equipment** (`medical_equipment`, `text`, optional): List any medical equipment the student uses.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional medical notes about the student.
* **Medical Special Notes** (`medical_special_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any special medical notes about the student.
* **Additional Medical Info** (`student_medical_additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional medical information about the student.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form completely and accurately.
* If you need to provide medical information, please be as detailed as possible.
* If you have any questions or need help, feel free to contact us.
* This form is confidential and will only be used for sports registration purposes.
* Please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* We appreciate your cooperation in filling out the form accurately and completely.
