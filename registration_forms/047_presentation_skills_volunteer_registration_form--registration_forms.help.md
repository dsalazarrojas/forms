# Presentation Skills Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from individuals who wish to volunteer as presentation skills facilitators.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the fields below with the required information. Each field is explained in more detail below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, optional): Please provide your name, username, or any other unique identifier you'd like to be known by in the volunteer role.
* **Presentation Info** (`presentation_info`, text, optional): Share with us any relevant details about your previous presentation experience, skills, or areas of expertise related to presentations.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you about opportunities or updates about the program.
* **Presentation Skills** (`presentation_skills`, text, optional): Describe your presentation skills, such as public speaking, content creation, or multimedia presentation design.

## Tips
* Double-check your email address for accuracy.
* If you've already registered, you won't be able to submit duplicate information.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact [support@yourwebsite.com](mailto:support@yourwebsite.com).
