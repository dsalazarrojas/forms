# Community Film Screening Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Film Screening Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees for the community film screenings. This form helps us understand your preferences and needs for the screening event, ensuring a tailored experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the film you are registering for from the options provided.
3. Choose your preferred screening date and time from the available options.
4. Indicate the number of tickets you require.
5. Select your preferred ticket type.
6. Confirm your membership status.
7. Indicate your seating preference.
8. If you have any accessibility needs, please provide detailed information in the relevant field.
9. Let us know if you are interested in concessions or have dietary restrictions.
10. Indicate how you heard about the screening.
11. Confirm if you have attended a screening before.
12. Select your film interests.
13. Express your interest in volunteering for the screening event.
14. Add any additional comments or information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for screening updates.
* **Phone Number**: Please enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Age**: Enter your age to help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **Film Selection**: Choose the film you wish to attend from the provided options.
* **Screening Date**: Select your preferred screening date from the options provided.
* **Screening Time**: Choose your preferred screening time from the options provided.
* **Number of Tickets**: Indicate the number of tickets you require.
* **Ticket Type**: Select your preferred ticket type from the options provided.
* **Membership Status**: Confirm your current membership status.
* **Seating Preference**: Indicate your preferred seating location (if no preference, select "No Preference").
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you require accommodations, please provide detailed information in this field.
* **Concessions Interest**: Let us know if you are interested in concessions.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please list them in this field.
* **How Did You Hear About Screening**: Select where you heard about the screening from the provided options.
* **Previous Attendance**: Confirm if you have attended a screening before.
* **Film Interests**: Select the film genres that interest you from the provided options.
* **Volunteer Interest**: Express your interest in volunteering for the screening event.
* **Additional Comments**: Provide any additional comments or information.

**Tips**

* If you are unsure about any of the options, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please ensure that you select the correct film and screening time to avoid conflicts.
* The form is designed to be completed in a few minutes, so please take your time and ensure accuracy.
* We appreciate your cooperation in providing accurate and complete information to ensure a successful event.
