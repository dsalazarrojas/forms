# Paintball And Airsoft Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a paintball and airsoft event. To ensure a successful and safe experience, please fill out this form thoroughly and accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant details, including name, age, and any other relevant information.
2. Choose your preferred scheduling options from the dropdown list.
3. Confirm that you understand and agree to the safety guidelines by selecting 'Yes' from the dropdown list.
4. If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the event organizers, please provide it in the "Additional Information" field.
5. Enter your contact details, including your name, phone number, and email address.
6. If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required): Please enter your name, age, and any other relevant information.
* **Scheduling Preferences** (`scheduling_preferences`, text, required): Choose your preferred scheduling options by typing in the box.
* **Safety Acknowledgement** (`safety_acknowledgement`, select_multiple, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm that you understand and agree to the safety guidelines.
* **Additional Information** (`participant_details_2`, text, optional): If you have any additional information you'd like to share with the event organizers, please provide it here.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If you have an emergency contact person, please provide their name and contact information.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your registration is processed correctly.
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact the event organizers.
