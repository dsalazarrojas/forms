# Educational Records Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Educational Records Release Form is a document used to obtain consent for the sharing of educational records, such as academic transcripts and grades.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by providing your personal details:
	* Fill in your name in the "What is your name?" field.
	* Enter your student ID number in the "What is your student ID?" field.
2. Provide information about your educational institution:
	* Enter the name of your institution in the "What is your educational institution?" field.
	* Enter information about your program of study in the "What is your program information?" field.
3. Specify your field of study and major:
	* Enter your field of study in the "What is your field of study?" field.
	* Enter your major in the "What is your major?" field.
4. Provide your graduation date and GPA (if applicable):
	* Enter your graduation date in the "When did you graduate?" field.
	* If you have a GPA, enter it in the "What is your GPA (if applicable)?" field.
5. Confirm your consent:
	* Select "True" if you consent to share your educational records.
	* Select "False" if you do not consent.
6. Finalize your contact information:
	* Enter your current email address in the "What is your current email address?" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "What is your phone number?" field.
	* Enter your mailing address in the "What is your mailing address?" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (Text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **What is your student ID?** (Text, required): Enter your student ID number issued by your educational institution.
* **What is your educational institution?** (Text, required): Enter the name of the institution where you studied.
* **What is your program information?** (Text, required): Enter information about your program of study, including the degree and level of study.
* **What is your field of study?** (Text, required): Enter the field of study related to your degree.
* **What is your major?** (Text, required): Enter your major or area of specialization.
* **When did you graduate?** (Date, required): Enter your graduation date.
* **What is your GPA (if applicable)?** (Number, optional): Enter your cumulative GPA if applicable.
* **Do you consent to share your educational records?** (Select One, required): Select "True" to consent to share your records. Select "False" if you do not.
* **What is your current email address?** (Email, required): Enter your current email address.
* **What is your phone number?** (Text, optional): Enter your phone number for communication.
* **What is your mailing address?** (Text, optional): Enter your mailing address for record sharing.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately to avoid delays or issues in processing your records.
