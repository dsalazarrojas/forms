# Festival Kickoff Reception RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Festival Kickoff Reception RSVP Form is a form designed to collect information from attendees who wish to participate in the Festival Kickoff Reception. Please note that this form is not mandatory, and it is up to each attendee to decide whether or not to complete it.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the FirstName field.
2. Enter your last name in the LastName field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
5. Enter the number of guests you plan to bring in the GuestCount field.
6. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate if you will be attending the event in the Attending field.
7. Enter your planned arrival date in the ArrivalDate field.
8. Enter your planned departure date in the DepartureDate field.
9. Enter your planned arrival time in the ArrivalTime field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FirstName**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official identification.
* **LastName**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **GuestCount**: Enter the total number of guests you plan to bring with you.
* **Attending**: Select "Yes" if you plan to attend the event, "No" otherwise.
* **ArrivalDate**: Enter your planned arrival date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **DepartureDate**: Enter your planned departure date in the format "YYYY-MM-DD".
* **ArrivalTime**: Enter your planned arrival time in the format "HH:MM".
