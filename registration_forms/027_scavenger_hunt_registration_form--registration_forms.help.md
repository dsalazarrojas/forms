# Scavenger Hunt Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This help guide is intended to assist participants in completing the Scavenger Hunt Registration Form. The form is designed to collect information about participants and their teams for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Participant Information**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed for the event.
	* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should be displayed for the event.
	* **Participant Email**: Enter your email address that will be used for communication.
	* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number that will be used for communication.
	* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
2. Provide team details:
	* **Team Size**: Enter the number of people in your team.
	* **Scavenger Hunt Details**: Enter any additional information about your team that might be useful for the event organizers.
	* **Participant Note**: Enter any notes or comments about your participation.
3. Enter team leader information:
	* **Team Leader First Name**: Enter the first name of the team leader.
	* **Team Leader Last Name**: Enter the last name of the team leader.
	* **Team Leader Email**: Enter the email address of the team leader.
	* **Team Leader Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team leader.
	* **Team Leader Note**: Enter any notes or comments about the team leader.
4. Schedule your registration:
	* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you would like to register for the event.
	* **Registration Time**: Enter the time you would like to register for the event.
