# Class 12 Exam Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Class 12 Exam Registration Form is for students who are registering for their final year of high school or equivalent education. This form is for administrative purposes, such as student information management, exam registration, and parental involvement.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your student registration number in the "Student Registration or Roll Number" field.
2. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
3. Select your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
4. Choose your gender in the "Gender" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
6. Select your phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
7. Enter your school name in the "School Name" field.
8. Enter your school code or UDISE number in the "School Code or UDISE Number" field (if applicable).
9. Choose your class section in the "Class Section" field.
10. Select your stream in the "Stream" field.
11. Choose your exam year in the "Exam Year" field.
12. Select your mandatory subjects in the "Core Subjects Selected" field (you can select multiple subjects).
13. Select your optional subjects in the "Elective or Optional Subjects" field (optional).
14. Check if you have any special needs or disabilities in the "Any special needs or disabilities" field.
15. Provide details of your special needs or required accommodations in the "Details of special needs or required accommodations" field (if applicable).
16. Enter your parent or guardian's name in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
17. Enter your parent or guardian's contact number in the "Parent or Guardian Contact Number" field.
18. Confirm that all the information provided is accurate and complete by selecting "Yes, I confirm" in the "I confirm all information provided is accurate and complete" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Registration or Roll Number**: This is a unique identifier assigned to you by your school. Enter the number here.
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name as written on your school records.
* **Date of Birth**: Select your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Gender**: Choose your gender from the options: Male, Female, Non-binary, or Prefer not to say.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone Number**: This is an optional field for you to provide your phone number.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your school as it appears on your school records.
* **School Code or UDISE Number**: If your school has a UDISE number, enter it here.
* **Class Section**: Choose your class section from the available options.
* **Stream**: Choose your stream from the options: Science, Commerce, Humanities, or Other.
* **Exam Year**: Select the year of your exam.
* **Core Subjects Selected**: Select all the mandatory subjects for your stream.
* **Elective or Optional Subjects**: Select any optional subjects you want to study. This field is optional.
* **Any special needs or disabilities**: Check if you have any special needs or disabilities.
* **Details of special needs or required accommodations**: Provide details of your special needs or required accommodations (if applicable).
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter your parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent or Guardian Contact Number**: Enter your parent or guardian's contact number.
* **I confirm all information provided is accurate and complete**: Confirm that all the information provided is accurate and complete.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your information for accuracy.
* If you have any special needs or disabilities, please provide details.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact your school administrator for assistance.
