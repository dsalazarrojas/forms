# english_practice_app_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the English practice application. It collects user and practice information to ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your **User Information** on the first page.
2. Provide your **Practice Information** (if required).
3. Enter your **Contact Information**.
4. Submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name and other relevant details about yourself.
* **Practice Information** (`practice_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional details about the practice you are registering for. This field is optional, so you can skip it if not required by the practice.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Enter your email address and any other contact details you would like to provide.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (User Information).
* If required to provide practice information, ensure it is accurate and relevant.
* Double-check your contact information for any typos or errors.
* Submit the form once you have reviewed all the fields for accuracy.
