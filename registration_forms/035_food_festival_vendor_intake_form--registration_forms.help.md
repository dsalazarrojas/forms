# food_festival_vendor_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is meant to collect information from vendors for the food festival, including vendor name, contact email, and other relevant details to help with organization and communication during the festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you provide all required information (if any).
3. Review the form before submission to catch any errors or missing fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Information** (`vendor_info`):
	+ A brief description of your food or service offerings.
	+ Provide any relevant information about your products or services.
* **Festival Date** (`festival_date`):
	+ The date of the food festival where you will be participating.
	+ Enter the date in the standard date format (e.g., `MM/DD/YYYY`).
* **Festival Time** (`festival_time`):
	+ The time of the food festival where you will be participating.
	+ Enter the time in the standard 24-hour format (e.g., `HH:MM`).
* **Festival Location** (`festival_location`):
	+ The location of the food festival where you will be participating.
	+ Enter the address or name of the venue.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`):
	+ Your name or business name as a food vendor.
	+ Enter the name you want to be displayed on festival materials and marketing efforts.
* **Vendor Description** (`vendor_description`):
	+ A brief description of your food or service offerings.
	+ Provide any relevant information about your products or services.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`):
	+ Your email address for festival communication and marketing efforts.
	+ Enter your email address in standard format (e.g., `jane.doe@example.com`).
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`):
	+ Your phone number for festival communication and coordination.
	+ Enter your phone number in the standard format (e.g., `123-456-7890`).
