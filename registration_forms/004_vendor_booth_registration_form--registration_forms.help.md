# Vendor Booth Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors who wish to register their booths at our event. Vendors will be asked to provide details about their company, contact information, products or services, and other relevant information to facilitate a smooth and successful event experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill out the form completely and accurately. All fields are required unless indicated as optional.
*   Make sure to double-check your contact information and booth details to ensure they are correct.
*   If you are unsure about any field, please consult the field-by-field explanation section below for more information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **vendor_booth_info (Text):** Enter a brief description of your booth and the products or services you will be offering.
*   **vendor_name (Text):** Enter your company name or individual name as a vendor.
*   **vendor_email (Text):** Enter your work email address. This is how we will contact you with any questions or updates.
*   **vendor_phone (Text):** Enter your work phone number. This is how we will contact you with any questions or updates.
*   **product_info (Note):** Enter a detailed description of your products or services, including prices and any other relevant details.
*   **vendor_address (Note):** Enter your company address or your personal address for shipping and logistics purposes.
*   **product_image (Text):** Upload an image of your product or service. This will help us to promote your booth and attract more customers.
*   **payment_details (Text):** Enter your payment details, such as payment method and any specific payment terms.
*   **vendor_license (Text):** Enter your business license number or other relevant identification numbers.
*   **vendor_tax_id (Number):** Enter your tax ID or other relevant tax identification numbers.
*   **vendor_terms (Note):** Read and agree to our terms and conditions by selecting "I accept the terms and conditions."
*   **vendor_agreement (Select One):** Select "I accept the terms and conditions" or "I decline the terms and conditions" based on your agreement with our event rules.
*   **assigned_tool (Select One):** Select "chatjimmy" or "Other" for our internal records.

## Tips

*   Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and consistency.
*   If you need more space for any of the text fields, you can upload images or other attachments in our internal system.
*   Our team will review and verify the information you provided before publishing your booth information on the website and promoting it to our customers.
