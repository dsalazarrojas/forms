# Pay What You Can Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pay What You Can Registration form is designed for users to register for events or services by paying a flexible amount based on their ability to pay.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Payment Amount field with the amount you are willing and able to pay for the selected event or service.
2. Choose the Event and Service options from the respective dropdown menus that you wish to register for.
3. If you want to be contacted for further information or follow-up, provide your Email and Phone number.
4. Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, Number, Optional): Fill in the amount you are willing and able to pay for the selected event or service.
* **Select Event** (`select_event`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the event(s) you wish to register for.
* **Select Service** (`select_service`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the service(s) you wish to register for.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): If you want to be contacted for further information or follow-up, enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Optional): If you want to be contacted for further information or follow-up, enter your phone number.
* **Note** (`note`, Note, Optional): Add any additional notes or comments about your registration.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in the Payment Amount field accurately to ensure you are paying what you can afford.
* Select the Event and Service options carefully to ensure you are registering for the correct events or services.
* If you have any questions or concerns about the form, contact the event or service provider directly.
* The form is designed to be flexible and accessible, but ensure you are honest about your payment amount to avoid any issues.
