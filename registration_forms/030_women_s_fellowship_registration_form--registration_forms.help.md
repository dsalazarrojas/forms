# Women's Fellowship Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Women's Fellowship Registration Form is used to register participants for a women's fellowship program. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter the participant's name in the first field.
2. Provide the name of the participant's organization (if applicable).
3. Select the participant's role in the role dropdown menu.
4. Enter the participant's email address.
5. Enter the participant's phone number (if applicable).
6. Enter the date and time of the participant's scheduled attendance.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant.
8. Select the tool that will be assigned to the participant (if applicable).
9. Enter a unique participant number.
10. Provide any additional comments or notes (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant. This is a required field.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the participant's organization (if applicable). This field is optional.
* **Role**: Select the participant's role from the dropdown menu:
	+ Chair: Participant who leads the meeting or event.
	+ Organizer: Participant who organized the meeting or event.
	+ Participant: Regular participant in the meeting or event.
	+ Volunteer: Participant who is volunteering their time.
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address. This is a required field.
* **Phone**: Enter the participant's phone number. This field is optional.
* **Date**: Enter the date of the participant's scheduled attendance. This is a required field.
* **Time**: Enter the time of the participant's scheduled attendance. This is a required field.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant. This field is optional.
* **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool that will be assigned to the participant from the dropdown menu:
	+ chatjimmy: Tool assigned for participant
	+ chatbot: Tool assigned for participant
* **Participant Number**: Enter a unique participant number. This is a required field.
* **Comments**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the participant. This field is optional.
