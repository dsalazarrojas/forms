# Charity Fundraiser Vendor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration of charity fundraiser vendors. The main goal is to collect necessary information from vendors to facilitate their participation in the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your vendor or business name.
2. Provide your contact person's name.
3. Enter your contact's email address.
4. Enter your contact's phone number.
5. Enter your business address.
6. Provide your website or social media link.
7. Enter the name of the event you will be participating in.
8. Select the date of the event.
9. Select your type of business or product.
10. Choose your booth size preference.
11. Indicate whether you will need power and if so, what kind.
12. Choose whether you will need table and chair rental.
13. Describe the products or services you will be offering.
14. Indicate if you have previously participated in this event.
15. Choose your preferred booth fee tier.
16. Select your preferred payment method.
17. Indicate whether you have liability insurance coverage.
18. Select your preferred setup time.
19. Provide any special requirements (e.g., accessibility needs).
20. Agree to the vendor rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor or Business Name** (1): This is the name of your business or vendor name as it appears on all marketing materials and event listings.
* **Contact Person Name** (2): This is the name of the person who will be handling your participation in the event.
* **Contact Email** (3): This is the email address of the contact person where event organizers can reach out to you with questions or updates.
* **Contact Phone** (4): This is the phone number of the contact person.
* **Business Address** (5): This is your business or vendor address where you can be reached or contacted.
* **Website or Social Media** (6): This is the URL of your website or social media page where event organizers can learn more about your business.
* **Event Name** (7): This is the name of the event you will be participating in.
* **Event Date** (8): This is the date of the event.
* **Vendor Type** (9): This indicates the type of business or product you will be offering (e.g., food, crafts, retail products, services).
* **Booth Size Preference** (10): This is the size of the booth space you will need for your setup.
* **Electrical Power Needed** (11): This indicates whether you will need power for your booth and if so, what type of power requirements you have.
* **Table and Chair Rental** (12): This indicates whether you will need tables and chairs for your booth.
* **Products or Services Description** (13): This is a description of the products or services you will be offering.
* **Have You Vended With Us Before?** (14): This indicates whether you have participated in this event before.
* **Booth Fee Selection** (15): This is the payment tier you will be paying for your participation in the event.
* **Payment Method** (16): This is the method through which you will be paying the booth fee.
* **Do You Have Liability Insurance?** (17): This indicates whether you have liability insurance coverage.
* **Preferred Setup Time** (18): This is the time you would prefer to set up your booth.
* **Special Requirements** (19): This is any special requirements you may have (e.g., accessibility needs).
* **Do You Agree to Vendor Rules?** (20): This is a declaration that you understand and agree to the event's rules and terms.
