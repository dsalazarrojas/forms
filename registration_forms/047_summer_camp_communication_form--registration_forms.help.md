# Summer Camp Communication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information about a camper's personal details, medical conditions, and contact information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for the camper during the summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the Camper Name field with the camper's full name.
2. Enter the Camper's contact email and phone number in the Contact Email and Contact Phone fields, respectively.
3. For the Emergency Contact field, select the relationship between the camper and the contact person from the drop-down menu.
4. Fill in the Start Date and End Date of the camper's stay at the camp.
5. Provide the camper's address in the Camp Address field.
6. If the camper has any medical conditions or allergies, please fill in the Medical Information fields accordingly.
7. If the camper has any special needs, please select the corresponding options from the Special Needs field.
8. Finally, click "Submit" to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Name** (Camper Name, text, required): Please fill in the camper's full name.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required): Enter the camper's contact email address.
* **Contact Phone** (Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the camper's contact phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship to Camper** (Relationship to Camper, select one, required): Select the relationship between the camper and the emergency contact from the drop-down menu.
* **Camper Age** (Camper Age, number, required): Enter the camper's age.
* **Camper Gender** (Camper Gender, select one, required): Select the camper's gender from the drop-down menu.
* **Special Needs** (Camper Special Needs, select multiple, optional): If the camper has any special needs, select the corresponding options from the drop-down menu.
* **Medical Information** (Camper Medical Information, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information about the camper.
* **Allergies** (Camper Allergies, text, optional): If the camper has any allergies, list them in this field.
* **Meds** (Camper Medications, text, optional): If the camper takes any medications, list them in this field.
* **Medical Info 2** (Camper Medical Information 2, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information about the camper (if not already filled in the previous field).
* **Notes** (Camper Notes, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the camper.
* **Start Date** (Start Date, date, required): Enter the start date of the camper's stay at the camp.
* **End Date** (End Date, date, optional): Enter the end date of the camper's stay at the camp (if not already filled in the previous field).
* **Total Days** (Total Days, number, optional): Enter the total number of days the camper will be staying at the camp.
* **Camp Address** (Camp Address, text, required): Enter the camp's address.
* **Camper Medical Info 3** (Camper Medical Information 3, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information about the camper (if not already filled in the previous field).
* **Camper Medical Info 4** (Camper Medical Information 4, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information about the camper (if not already filled in the previous field).
* **Assigned Tool** (Assigned Tool, text, optional): Enter any assigned tool or equipment for the camper.

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill in all the required fields to avoid any issues with the registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the camp administration.
* Double-check the information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
