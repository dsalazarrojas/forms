# Art Fair Closing Reception RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Fair Closing Reception RSVP Form is designed to gather essential information from guests about their attendance and interests at the closing reception of the art fair. This form will help us plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your guest information, including your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose your RSVP status: Yes - I will attend, No - I cannot attend, or Maybe - Still deciding.
3. Enter the number of additional guests you are bringing.
4. Provide names of any additional guests (if applicable).
5. Select your preferred contact method (email, phone, or text message).
6. Indicate your interest in receiving our newsletter.
7. Add any additional comments or special requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information**
  (Guest Name, text, required)
  Enter your name as it appears on your invitation.
* **Email Address**
  (Email, email, required)
  Enter the email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number**
  (Phone Number, text, required)
  Enter your phone number where we can reach you.
* **Closing Reception Date**
  (Closing Reception Date, date, required)
  Select the date of the closing reception you are planning to attend.
* **RSVP Status**
  (RSVP Status, select_one, required)
  Choose Yes - I will attend, No - I cannot attend, or Maybe - Still deciding.
* **Number of Additional Guests**
  (Number of Additional Guests, number, required)
  Enter the number of guests you are bringing.
* **Names of Additional Guests**
  (Names of Additional Guests, text, optional)
  Provide the names of any additional guests attending with you.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**
  (Dietary Restrictions or Allergies, text, optional)
  If you have any dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know so we can make necessary arrangements.
* **Interested in VIP Reception**
  (Interested in VIP Reception, select_one, optional)
  Indicate if you are interested in attending the VIP reception.
* **Accessibility or Special Needs**
  (Accessibility or Special Needs, text, optional)
  If you have any accessibility or special needs, please describe them so we can make necessary arrangements.
* **Interested in Meeting Artists**
  (Interested in Meeting Artists, select_one, optional)
  Indicate if you are interested in meeting the artists.
* **Specific Artists or Galleries of Interest**
  (Specific Artists or Galleries of Interest, text, optional)
  If you have a specific artist or gallery in mind, please provide their name.
* **Have You Participated in Previous Art Fair Events**
  (Have You Participated in Previous Art Fair Events, select_one, optional)
  If you have attended the art fair before, select Yes - I have, No - I haven't, or This is my first time.
* **Participant Type**
  (Participant Type, select_one, optional)
  Select the type of participant you are (Artist or exhibitor, Collector, Enthusiast, Student, Professional, Other).
* **Preferred Contact Method**
  (Preferred Contact Method, select_one, required)
  Select your preferred contact method (Email, Phone, Text message).
* **Subscribe to Art Fair Newsletter**
  (Subscribe to Art Fair Newsletter, select_one, optional)
  If you would like to receive our newsletter, select Yes - I will, No - I won't.
* **Additional Comments or Special Requests**
  (Additional Comments or Special Requests, text, optional)
  If you have any additional comments or requests, please provide them here.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields for us to process your RSVP correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
* We appreciate your participation and look forward to seeing you at the closing reception!
