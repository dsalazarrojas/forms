# Music And Theater Club Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Music And Theater Club Registration form is designed to gather information about users who want to join a music and theater club. By completing this form, you will be able to register and specify your interests and role within the club.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your User Info: Enter your name, email, and any other relevant contact details in the User Info section.
2. Select your Contact Details: Enter your phone number and any other contact details in the Contact Details section.
3. Choose your Group Preferences: Select one or more of the available group preferences (Drama, Dance, or Music) that interest you.
4. Select your Role: Choose your role within the club (Member, Assistant, or Leader).
5. Enter your Date Registered: Enter the date you are registering for the club.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your phone number and any other contact details.
* **Group Preferences**: Select one or more of the available group preferences (Drama, Dance, or Music) that interest you. This will help us understand your interests and preferences for the club.
* **Role**: Choose your role within the club (Member, Assistant, or Leader). This will help us understand your position within the club.
* **Date Registered**: Enter the date you are registering for the club. This is an internal tracking field to keep track of registrations.
