# Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help the PTA raise funds for their elementary school. By filling out this form, you can register as a donor and specify your donation preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information before starting.
2. Fill out the form in sections, one page at a time.
3. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Fundraiser For Elementary School PTA** (`title`): This is the title of the fundraiser.
* **Main Page** (`main_page`): This is the main page of the form. It contains general information and instructions.
* **Donation Amount** (`donation_amount`): This field allows you to specify the amount you would like to donate.
* **Frequency** (`donation_frequency`): Select the frequency of your donation. You can choose from one time, monthly, or yearly.
* **Duration** (`donation_duration`): Select the duration of your donation. You can choose from month, three months, six months, or one year.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your donation.
* **Share Via** (`share_via`): Select the channels you would like to share your donation with. You can choose from text, email, or social media.
* **Share To** (`share_to`): Select the people you would like to share your donation with. You can choose from friends, family, or social media.
* **Share Link** (`share_link`): Select the channels you would like to share your donation link with. You can choose from text, email, or social media.
* **Donation Method** (`donation_method`): Select the method of your donation. You can choose from online or offline.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select the method of payment for your donation. You can choose from credit card or bank transfer.
* **School Email** (`school_email`): Enter the email address of the school you are fundraising for.
* **Club Name** (`club_name`): Enter the name of the club or group you are fundraising for.
* **Description** (`description`): Enter a brief description of your donation.
* **Fundraiser Type** (`fundraiser_type`): Select the type of fundraiser. You can choose from general or specific.
* **School Name** (`school_name`): Enter the name of the school you are fundraising for.
* **Club Email** (`club_email`): Enter the email address of the club or group you are fundraising for.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`): Enter the phone number of the school you are fundraising for.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are accurate and complete.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
