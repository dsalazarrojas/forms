# Tax Client Interest Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for tax clients to express their interest in our tax planning and preparation services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Fill out the form sections as completely and accurately as possible.**
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you're interested in multiple services, select all that apply.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Interest** (`client_interest`): A brief description of why you're interested in our tax services.
* **Full Name** (`name`): Enter your full name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address in the format 'name@example.com'.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number without any special characters.
* **Interested In** (`tax_services`): Choose all tax services that interest you.
	+ Select 'None' if you're not interested in any of the listed services.
* **Other (Specify)** (`other_tax_services`): If you're interested in something not listed, describe it here.
* **Preferred Contact** (`preferred_contact`): Choose how you'd like to be contacted.
	+ Phone: We'll call or text you.
	+ Email: We'll email you.
	+ In-person: We'll meet in person to discuss your tax needs.
* **Other (Specify)** (`preferred_contact_other`): If you've selected 'In-person', describe your preferred location here.
* **Street Address** (`address`): Enter your street address for us to send you information.
* **City** (`city`): Enter the city where you live.
* **Zip** (`zip`): Enter your zip code.
* **State** (`state`): Enter 'Yes' if you're eligible for our services in your state.
* **Country** (`country`): Enter the country where you live.
* **Other Tax Services** (`tax_services_other`): If none of the listed services interest you, describe the tax services you're looking for.
* **Additional Notes** (`message`): Enter any additional information that might help us understand your needs.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your email and phone number for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask us for clarification.
* We'll review your submission carefully and follow up with you to confirm your interest and answer any questions.
