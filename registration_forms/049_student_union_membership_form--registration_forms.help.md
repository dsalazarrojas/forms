# Student Union Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Student Union Membership Form is designed to gather essential information from students to facilitate their union membership, activities, or services. This form collects personal details, academic background, and emergency contact information to ensure smooth processing and efficient service provision.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please complete this form with your accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly to avoid processing delays or issues.
3.  Double-check your information for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student Union Membership Form** (`student_union_membership_form`, text, optional):
    This field is a header for the form, which is not to be filled out.
*   **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your name as it appears on your identification document.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YY or MM/DD/YY).
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number, ensuring it's in the correct format (including the country code where necessary).
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your student ID to facilitate easy identification.
*   **Program** (`program`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your current program or course of study.
*   **Year of Admission** (`year_of_admission`, text, optional): If applicable, enter your year of admission for accurate record-keeping.
*   **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about your union membership or services.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact person's name.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
