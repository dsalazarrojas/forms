# Student Recital Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Recital Registration Form is a form used to collect information from students who are participating in a recital, including their name, age group, grade level, and other relevant details. This form is intended to provide a streamlined way to gather necessary information from students and their teachers, ensuring that the recital runs smoothly and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
* Select the correct options for the select_one fields (e.g., student age group, recital type).
* Enter the date and time of the event if applicable.
* Ensure that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Recital Name**: This field is for the student's name participating in the recital. Please enter the student's full name as it appears on their record.
* **Student Age Group**: Please select the age group that best describes the student's age. This field is not required.
* **Student Grade Level**: Select the student's grade level (High or Low) from the options provided. This field is not required.
* **Teacher Name**: This field is for the teacher's name who will be accompanying the student during the recital. Please enter the teacher's full name as it appears on their record.
* **Teacher Contact Info**: Please enter the teacher's contact information, including any relevant phone numbers and email addresses.
* **Event Date**: If the recital has a specific date, please enter it in the correct format (MM/DD/YY). This field is not required.
* **Event Time**: If the recital has a specific time, please enter it in 24-hour format (HH:MM). This field is not required.
* **Recital Type**: Please select the type of recital from the options provided. This field is not required.
* **Performance Piece**: This field is for the student's performance piece, e.g., a song or dance they will be performing. Please enter a brief description of the performance.
* **Teacher Email**: Please enter the teacher's email address. This field is required.
* **Teacher Phone**: This field is for the teacher's phone number. It is not required.
* **Teacher Phone**: Please ensure the email addresses are valid and in the correct format (username@domain.com).
