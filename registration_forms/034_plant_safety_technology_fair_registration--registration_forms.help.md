# Plant Safety Technology Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Plant Safety Technology Fair Registration" form is designed to collect attendee information and emergency contact details for the event. This form is used to ensure that event organizers have accurate and up-to-date information about attendees in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the answer to "Do you need to display your name publicly?" on the first page of the form.
2. Enter your "Attendee Information", which includes your name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose your emergency contact status and relationship with the emergency contact.
4. If you have any special requests, enter them in the "Attendee Special Request" field.
5. Indicate your dietary needs, including whether you have any specific dietary requirements.
6. Enter any additional dietary needs or preferences.
7. Choose whether to consent to the terms and conditions of the event.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Do you need to display your name publicly?** (`1`, `select_one`, required): This field determines whether your name will be displayed publicly during the event. Please select "Yes" if you are comfortable with your name being displayed, or "No" if you prefer it not to be displayed.
* **Attendee Information** (`2`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, email, and phone number in this field.
* **Emergency Contact** (`6`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose whether you have an emergency contact person and their relationship with you.
* **Emergency Phone** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Attendee Special Request** (`8`, `text`, required): If you have any special requests or requirements, please enter them here.
* **Attendee Diet** (`9`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you have any dietary requirements or preferences.
* **Attendee Diet** (`10`, `text`, required): Enter any additional dietary needs or preferences.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`12`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`13`, `text`, required): Enter any other relationship details with your emergency contact.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`15`, `note`, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Consent** (`16`, `select_one`, required): Choose to agree to the terms and conditions of the event.

## Tips

* Please review each field carefully to ensure accurate and complete information is provided.
* If you are unsure about any field or its purpose, please reach out to the event organizers for clarification.
* This form is not intended to be completed multiple times by the same person. If you have already registered for the event, please do not attempt to complete this form again.
