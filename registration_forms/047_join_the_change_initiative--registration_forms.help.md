# Join The Change Initiative - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Join The Change Initiative" form collects participant information and engagement preferences to facilitate the participant's integration into the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the form**: Complete the form by entering the required and optional information.
2. **Review each field**: Ensure you understand the label and explanation of each field before proceeding.
3. **Submit your application**: Once complete, submit your application to be considered for participation in the initiative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This field is used to capture your response to the initiative's registration question.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Please provide your participant information, including any relevant details about yourself.
* **Engagement Preferences** (`engagement_preferences`, text, required: false): Indicate your engagement preferences, such as the frequency of engagement and any specific areas of interest.

## Tips
* Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
* Ensure you provide enough information for a thorough review.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
