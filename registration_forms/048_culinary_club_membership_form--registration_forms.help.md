# Culinary Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Culinary Club Membership Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the club. The form is intended for registration purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read through the form carefully and fill out each required field.
* Start by entering your first name in the "First Name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
* Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to enter a valid email address.
* Select your membership type from the "Membership Type" dropdown menu.
* Choose which activities you are interested in from the "Activities Preferences" multiple-select list.
* If you have any dietary restrictions, you can enter them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
* If you have any special requests, you can enter them in the "Special Requests" field.
* Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, required):
	* Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required):
	* Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, required):
	* Enter a valid email address to receive club news and updates.
* **Membership Type** (`select_one`, required):
	+ Select "Personal" for individual membership.
	+ Select "Club" for group membership.
	+ Select "Group" for membership in a specific group within the club.
	+ Select "Organization" for business or institutional membership.
* **Activities Preferences** (`select_multiple`, required):
	+ Choose "Cooking classes" to participate in cooking classes.
	+ Choose "Events" to attend events and activities.
	+ Choose "Workshops" to attend workshops and training sessions.
	+ Choose "Potlucks" to participate in potlucks and food-sharing activities.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter any dietary restrictions or preferences here (e.g. vegan, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Special Requests** (`text`, optional):
	+ Enter any special requests or needs here (e.g. wheelchair accessibility, etc.).
* **Confirm Email** (`email`, required):
	+ Re-enter your email address to confirm it.
