# Health Index Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Health Index Certification Registration Form is used to register for the Health Index Certification. This form is intended for participants who wish to seek certification.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter the name of the contact person or organization responsible for your certification.
3. Provide your email address for communication purposes.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the type of certification you are seeking (Health Care Pro, Health Care Con, or Health Care Org).
6. Enter your address for certification purposes.
7. Enter your city.
8. Enter your state.
9. Enter your zip code.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* This field is used to input your name as it appears on your identification.

### Contact Name
* This field is used to input the name of the contact person or organization responsible for your certification.

### Email
* This field is used to input your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
* This field is used to input your phone number.

### Certification Type
* This field is used to select the type of certification you are seeking (Health Care Pro, Health Care Con, or Health Care Org).

### Address
* This field is used to input your address.

### City
* This field is used to input your city.

### State
* This field is used to input your state.

### Zip
* This field is used to input your zip code.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the certification authority.
* You may be required to provide additional documentation or information to support your certification application.
