# Casting Call Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration and submission to casting calls. It collects essential information from performers to facilitate the casting process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to ensure your submission is accepted.
2. Review each field's explanation for clarity on what information is expected.
3. Submit your completed form for consideration for the casting call.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Audition Information** (`-- Audition Information --`):
	+ This section contains essential information about the audition.
	+ Please ensure all fields within this section are accurately filled out to avoid issues with your submission.
* **Audition Date and Time** (`Audition Date and Time`, `MM/DD/YYYY`):
	+ Enter your available audition date and time.
	+ Format should be MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Audition Location** (`Audition Location`):
	+ Provide the name of the location where the audition will take place.
* **Production Details** (`-- Production Details --`):
	+ This section contains additional information about the production.
* **Production Title** (`Production Title`):
	+ Enter the title of the production (film, TV show, or play).
* **Production Type** (`Production Type`):
	+ Select the type of production (e.g. Feature Film, Television, Theater).
* **Director or Producer** (`Director or Producer`):
	+ Enter the name of the director or producer.
* **Performer Information** (`-- Performer Information --`):
	+ This section contains essential information about you as a performer.
* **Legal Full Name** (`Legal Full Name`):
	+ Enter your legal full name.
* **Stage Name (if different)** (`Stage Name (if different)`):
	+ If your stage name is different from your legal name, enter it here.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, `MM/DD/YYYY`):
	+ Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`):
	+ Provide your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Street Address** (`Street Address`):
	+ Provide your street address.
* **City, State, Zip** (`City, State, Zip`):
	+ Enter your city, state, and zip code.
* **Roles Applied For** (`-- Roles Applied For --`):
	+ This section is for you to list the roles you're interested in.
* **First Choice Role** (`First Choice Role`):
	+ Select your first choice role.
* **Second Choice Role** (`Second Choice Role`):
	+ Select your second choice role.
* **Experience** (`-- Experience --`):
	+ This section is for you to list your relevant experience.
* **Type of Experience** (`Type of Experience`):
	+ Select all types of experience you have (e.g. Film, Television, Theater).
* **IMDb Profile URL** (`IMDb Profile URL`):
	+ If you have an IMDb profile, enter the URL.
* **Professional Information** (`-- Professional Information --`):
	+ This section contains additional information about your professional background.
* **Do you have representation (agent/manager)?** (`Do you have representation (agent/manager)?`):
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if you have representation.
* **Representative Contact Information** (`Representative Contact Information`):
	+ If you have representation, enter the contact information for your agent or manager.
* **I agree to the terms of this audition and submission** (`I agree to the terms of this audition and submission`):
	+ Select 'Yes, I agree' to acknowledge you've read and agreed to the terms.

## Tips
* Ensure all required fields are filled out accurately.
* Review each field's explanation for clarity on what information is expected.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the casting team.
* Submit your completed form for consideration for the casting call.
