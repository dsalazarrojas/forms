# Brake Certification Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Brake Certification Training Registration form is used to register for a brake certification training program. This guide will walk you through the required fields to complete for a successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your participant information, including your First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Select your Training Level, Training Format, Preferred Training Date, Preferred Training Time, and Brake Systems to Study.
3. Provide information on your employment and company details.
4. Confirm your registration by checking the "I confirm my registration" checkbox.
