# Hitting Practice Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hitting Practice Registration form is designed to collect relevant information from users to manage practice sessions effectively. Please fill out the form accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the User Info section, where you will provide your basic information.
2. Move on to the Parent Info section, where you will provide any relevant details about your parent/guardian.
3. In the Contact Preferences section, select your emergency contact preferences.
4. Fill out the Participant Details section, where you will provide any additional details about yourself as a participant.
5. Finally, enter your session information in the Session Info section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (required/optional)**: Provide your basic user information, including your name and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Info (required/optional)**: If you are a parent/guardian, provide any relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Preferences (optional)**: Select which emergency contact you would like to be notified in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Details (required/optional)**: Enter any additional details about yourself as a participant, such as your age, experience, or any medical conditions.
* **Session Info (required/optional)**: Enter the session details, including date, time, and any other relevant information.
