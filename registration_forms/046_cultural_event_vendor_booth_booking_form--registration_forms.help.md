# cultural_event_vendor_booth_booking_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to book a vendor booth for a cultural event. It collects essential details from the vendor, such as their contact information, booth requirements, and submission status, to ensure a smooth and organized event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Vendor Details** in the first field. This includes any necessary information about you and your business.
2. In the **Booth Details** field, describe your booth setup and requirements for the event.
3. Indicate your **Requirements** for the event in the third field. Check all that apply.
4. Select your **Payment Terms** in the fourth field. This will determine how you'll be paid for your services.
5. Add any additional **Notes** you think are relevant to your booth setup in the fifth field (optional).
6. Fill in your **Contact Details** in the sixth field. This includes your email and phone number.
7. Write a brief description of your **Vendor Terms** in the seventh field (optional).
8. Select the current **Submission Status** of your booking in the eighth field. This will update the status of your submission.
9. Enter the **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** for your booking (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
10. If necessary, provide any additional **Submission Status Notes** to clarify your submission status (optional).
11. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers to ensure we can contact you about your booking.
11. Confirm your **Agreement** by entering it in the eleventh field.
12. Finally, select whether you have agreed to the **Vendor Agreement** in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Details** (Required) (`id: 1`, `type: text`, `required`: `true`): Enter your company or business name, contact person, and any other relevant details.
* **Booth Details** (Required) (`id: 2`, `type: text`, `required`: `true`): Describe your booth setup, including the size, location, and any other necessary information.
* **Requirements** (Required, Multiple Select) (`id: 3`, `type: select_multiple`, `required`: `true`): Check all requirements that apply, such as electricity, water, internet, and more.
* **Payment Terms** (Required) (`id: 4`, `type: select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select how you'll be paid for your services, such as by check, bank transfer, or credit card.
* **Notes** (Optional) (`id: 5`, `type: note`, `required`: `false`): Add any additional information you think is relevant to your booth setup.
* **Contact Details** (Required) (`id: 6`, `type: text`, `required`: `true`): Enter your email and phone number so we can contact you about your booking.
* **Vendor Terms** (Optional) (`id: 7`, `type: text`, `required`: `false`): Write a brief description of your business terms and conditions.
* **Submission Status** (Required) (`id: 8`, `type: select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select the current status of your submission, such as in progress, completed, cancelled, or declined.
* **Submission Date** and **Submission Time** (Optional) (`id: 9` and `id: 10`, `type: date` and `type: time`, `required`: `false`): Enter the date and time of your submission (optional, but recommended for tracking purposes).
* **Submission Status Notes** (Optional) (`id: 11`, `type: text`, `required`: `false`): Provide any additional notes about your submission status.
* **Email** (Required) (`id: 12`, `type: email`, `required`: `true`): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your booking.
* **Phone** (Required) (`id: 13`, `type: text`, `required`: `true`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about your booking.
* **Agreement** (Required) (`id: 14`, `type: text`, `required`: `true`): Enter your agreement to the terms and conditions of the event.
* **Vendor Agreement** (Required) (`id: 15`, `type: select_one`, `required`: `true`): Select whether you have agreed to the vendor agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at your email or phone number.
* Review the submission status carefully to ensure it accurately reflects your situation.
* Keep your contact details up to date to ensure we can reach you efficiently.
