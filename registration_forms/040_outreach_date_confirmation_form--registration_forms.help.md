# outreach_date_confirmation_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for event attendees to provide information about their event attendance and preferences. Use it to:
* Confirm event dates and attendance.
* Gather contact information (name, email, phone).
* Collect comments or feedback.
* Verify event type.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event you are planning to attend.
2. **Attendee Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on event materials.
3. **Preferred Date**: Select the date you would prefer to attend the event (if different from the event date).
4. **Email**: Enter your email address for future communication.
5. **Phone**: Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or messages.
6. **Confirm Attendance**: Choose 'Yes' to confirm your attendance or 'No' if you cannot attend.
7. **Comments**: Leave any additional comments or feedback about the event.
8. **Organization**: Enter your organization or affiliation (if applicable).
9. **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are planning to attend (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Date** (`Event Date`, date, required): The date of the event you are planning to attend.
* **Attendee Name** (`Attendee Name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on event materials.
* **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, select_multiple, optional): Select the date you would prefer to attend the event (if different from the event date).
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for future communication.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for follow-up calls or messages.
* **Confirm Attendance** (`Confirm Attendance`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' to confirm your attendance or 'No' if you cannot attend.
* **Comments** (`Comments`, note, optional): Leave any additional comments or feedback about the event.
* **Organization** (`Organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation (if applicable).
* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of event you are planning to attend (if applicable).

Note: If you have any issues while filling out this form, please contact us at your email or phone number.
