# Mandarin Speech And Drama Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for enrolling a student in the Mandarin Speech and Drama program. It collects information from the student and parent/guardian for enrollment purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enroll A Student: Enter the student's name.
2. Parent Contact Information: Enter the parent's contact email.
3. Schedule: Select the school and day(s) the student is available to attend.
4. Student Schedule: Enter the student's available dates for the program.
5. Parent Schedule: Enter the parent's available time(s) for the program.
6. Notes: Add any additional notes if necessary.
