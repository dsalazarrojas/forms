# Capital Markets CLE Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate registration for the Capital Markets Continuing Legal Education (CLE) program. By completing this form, you will be able to select sessions of interest and provide necessary information for registration, including your contact details and CLE credit requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Start by reading and following these instructions**: Take a few minutes to read through this help guide to ensure you understand what is required.
2. **Enter your contact information**: Provide your first and last name, email address, and phone number. This information will be used for communication with you about your registration.
3. **Enter your bar license and state/jurisdiction**: Provide your bar license number and the state or jurisdiction where you are licensed.
4. **Enter your law firm/organization**: Provide the name of your law firm or organization where you are employed.
5. **Select your practice area**: Choose one primary practice area that best describes your area of practice.
6. **Choose your registration type**: Select how you would like to register (in-person, virtual/online, or hybrid).
7. **Choose your session preferences**: Select which sessions you are interested in attending, and if you would like to attend the full program or select individual sessions.
8. **Choose your time slots**: Select the morning and/or afternoon sessions you are interested in attending.
9. **Choose your dietary restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have and provide further details if necessary.
10. **Choose your CLE credit requirements**: Select the types of CLE credits you need.
11. **Enter your payment information**: Select your preferred payment method and enter the required details.
12. **Enter any additional information**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
13. **Confirm your information**: Finally, confirm that all the information you provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number.
* **Bar License Number**: Enter your bar license number.
* **State Bar or Jurisdiction**: Enter the state or jurisdiction where you are licensed.
* **Law Firm/Organization**: Enter the name of your law firm or organization.
* **Position Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Primary Practice Area**: Choose one primary practice area that best describes your area of practice.
* **Registration Type**: Choose how you would like to register (in-person, virtual/online, or hybrid).
* **Attending Full Program**: Choose if you would like to attend the full program or select individual sessions.
* **Sessions of Interest**: Select all the sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Morning Session**: Choose if you are interested in attending the morning session.
* **Afternoon Session**: Choose if you are interested in attending the afternoon session.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have and provide further details if necessary.
* **Types of CLE Credit Needed**: Select the types of CLE credits you need.
* **Registration Fee Amount**: Enter the registration fee amount if required.
* **Preferred Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay (credit/debit card, check, bank transfer, or purchase order).
* **Firm Billing Information**: Enter any applicable firm billing information.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Choose if you have any accessibility needs.
* **Accommodation Details**: Provide further details if you have accessibility needs.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional comments or questions you have.
* **I Confirm**: Confirm that all the information you provided is accurate.

Note: This form is designed to be self-explanatory, so please take a few minutes to read through it carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose and requirements.
