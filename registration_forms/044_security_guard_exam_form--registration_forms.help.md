# security_guard_exam_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from security guards for training, certification, or compliance purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your first name in the **first name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **last name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **email** field. Please use a format like `example@example.com`.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the **phone number** field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the **date of birth** field in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
6. Select your role from the list of options in the **what is your role** field.
7. Choose one or more languages you speak from the list of options in the **languages spoken** field.
8. Provide any additional information or comments in the **notes** field.
9. If you have any additional information, enter it in the **notes2** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, Optional): Enter your email address. Use a format like `example@example.com`.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, Optional): If you have a phone number, enter it.
* **Date of Birth** (`date`, `date`, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **What is your role** (`select_one`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your role from the list of options.
* **Languages Spoken** (`select_multiple`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose one or more languages you speak from the list of options.
* **Notes** (`note`, `note`, Required): Enter any additional information or comments.
* **Notes 2** (`note2`, `note`, Optional): Enter any additional information or comments.
