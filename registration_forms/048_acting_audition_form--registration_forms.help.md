# Acting Audition Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Acting Audition Form is designed to collect information from actors who are interested in auditioning for a specific role. This form helps the casting office to assess their suitability and gather necessary details for the casting process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Auditionee Name** in the designated field.
2. Specify the **Role Auditioning For** you are interested in.
3. Select a **Date** and **Time** for your audition, ensuring you are available on the selected date and time.
4. If applicable, provide your **Vocal Range** and **Dance Background** to showcase your skills.
5. If you have any current **Conflicts** (e.g., other commitments), list them in the designated field.
6. Fill out the **Emergency Contact Name** and **Emergency Contact Phone**, providing accurate and up-to-date information.
7. Optionally, you can leave **Audition Notes** for the casting office's internal reference.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Auditionee Name** (Required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or acting card.
*   **Role Auditioning For** (Required): Specify the role for which you are auditioning.
*   **Audition Date** (Required): Select a date you are available to attend the audition.
*   **Audition Time** (Required): Select the time you are available to attend the audition.
*   **Physical Attributes** (Optional): If you have any physical characteristics that may be relevant to the role (e.g., height, hair color, etc.), list them here.
*   **Vocal Range** (Optional): Indicate your vocal range, if applicable (e.g., Soprano, Tenor, Bass).
*   **Dance Background** (Required): Select your dance background level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or None).
*   **Current Conflicts** (Optional): List any dates you are unavailable for rehearsals or other commitments.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (Required): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (Required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
*   **Casting Recommendation** (Optional): Indicate if you have been recommended for this role by a casting office.
*   **Audition Notes** (Optional): Leave any additional information or comments for the casting office's internal reference.
