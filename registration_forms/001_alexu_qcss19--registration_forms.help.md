# AlexU QCSS19 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for the AlexU QCSS19 program. This form helps us understand your personal details, affiliation, and emergency contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have completed all required fields.
2. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
3. Submit the form when you are finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your first and last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email address** (`email`, email, required): Provide a contact email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Include your phone number with country code if applicable.
* **Affiliation or institution** (`affiliation`, text, required): Enter the name of your university or organization.
* **Position level** (`position_level`, select_one, required): Select your position level from Student, Researcher, or Faculty.
* **Arrival date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional): Enter your expected arrival date for the program.
* **Dietary requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, text, optional): Specify any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Emergency contact name** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.

**Please note that all required fields are marked as such and must be completed before submitting the form.**
