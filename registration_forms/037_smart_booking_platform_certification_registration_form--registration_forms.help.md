# Smart Booking Platform Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Booking Platform Certification Registration Form is a user-friendly form designed to collect information from users who are interested in obtaining certification through the Smart Booking Platform. This form ensures that users provide the necessary details to process their certification request efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your Email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your Phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your preferred **Payment Method** from the available options.
6. Enter the name of the course you are registering for in the **Course Name** field.
7. Provide additional participant information in the **Participant Information** field.
8. Select the **Certification Type** that aligns with your certification requirements. This is a required field.
9. If applicable, enter the date you are seeking certification in the **Certification Date** field.
10. Select your Time In (if applicable) to specify the time of your certification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name for registration purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name for registration purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for certification details.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, optional): Select a payment method that suits your needs: Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the course you are registering for.
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation in the course.
* **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, select_one, required): Select the level of certification you are seeking: Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you are seeking certification, if applicable.
* **Time In** (`time_in`, time, optional): Select the time of your certification, if applicable.
