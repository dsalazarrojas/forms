# Pilates Session Inquiry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help users inquire about available Pilates sessions. It collects relevant information to facilitate the process of booking and scheduling a session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Class Inquiries" field and enter your desired class schedule preferences.
2. Select the date you would like to attend your Pilates session.
3. Choose your preferred class schedule from the available options.
4. Select the price of the class that suits your budget.
5. Choose your preferred payment method.
6. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for further contact.
8. Enter your address for logistics and scheduling.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class Inquiries** (`class_inquiries`, text, required): Enter your desired class schedule preferences, such as the days you are available to attend.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required): Select the date you would like to attend your Pilates session.
* **Class Schedule** (`class_schedule`, select_multiple, required): Choose your preferred class schedule from the available options (10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM).
* **Class Price** (`class_price`, select_one, required): Select the price of the class that suits your budget ($20, $30, $40, $50).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card).
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further contact.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address for logistics and scheduling purposes.
