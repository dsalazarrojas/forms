# performance_improvement_initiative_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Performance Improvement Initiative Registration Form is used to collect information from end users who want to participate in the performance improvement initiatives. It ensures that users are aware of and engaged with the initiatives that can benefit their growth and development.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the step-by-step guide below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Program Selection (Program Selection)** (`program_selection`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more programs you're interested in participating in. This is a multiple-select option, allowing you to choose more than one program if needed.
* **Name (Name)** (`name`, text, optional): Enter your full name. This is a text field for personal identification.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address. This is a field to gather your contact information.
* **Phone (Phone)** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number. This is a text field for communication purposes.

## Tips
- Double-check your selections and information before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, don't hesitate to reach out to the relevant department for clarification.
- Make sure to submit the form correctly to ensure your participation in the Performance Improvement Initiatives.
