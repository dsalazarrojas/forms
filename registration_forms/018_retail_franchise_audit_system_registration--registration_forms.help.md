# Retail Franchise Audit System Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Franchise Audit System Registration form is designed to collect store-specific data for registration, management, or operational efficiency purposes within the Retail Franchise Audit System.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the *required* label) with accurate and complete information.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out as accurately as possible to avoid errors.
3. Review your submission before submitting the form to confirm that all fields are correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Store Details** (`store_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your store, such as its name, description, or other relevant details.
* **Store Location** (`store_location`, number, optional): Enter the store's location number as assigned by the system or provided by the franchisor.
* **Store Owner** (`store_owner`, text, optional): Provide the store owner's name and contact information.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, optional): Enter the contact person's name and contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
* **Store Address** (`store_address`, text, optional): Enter the store's physical address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the store's email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the store's phone number.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, optional): Enter the store's name, which may be the same as or different from the store's location name.
* **Alternate Store Location** (`store_location_2`, number, optional): Enter an alternate store location number, if applicable.
* **Tips**: If you encounter any issues or have questions while filling out this form, contact our support team for assistance. Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately to avoid errors and ensure proper integration with the system.
