# self_sewing_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for a self-sewing course. Fill out the form with your details to register for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the Course Details page with the relevant information about the course.
2. On the Student Name page, enter your full name.
3. On the Date Of Birth page, enter your date of birth in the required format (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. On the Phone Number page, enter your phone number.
5. On the Email page, enter your email address.
6. On the Course Start Date page, enter the start date of the course.
7. On the Course End Date page, enter the end date of the course.
8. On the Course Location page, enter the location of the course.
9. On the Student Agreement page, read and agree to the terms of the course.
10. On the Course Terms page, read and understand the terms of the course.
11. On the Course Fee page, enter the fee for the course.
12. On the Payment Option page, select your preferred payment option.
13. On the Payment Choice page, select your preferred payment choice.
14. On the Submit Button page, select the Submit button to complete the form.
15. You can review and adjust your input on each page before submitting the form.
