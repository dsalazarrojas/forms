# Student Ambassador Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Ambassador Registration Form is a form designed to collect information from students who wish to represent their school as an ambassador. This form is used to gather details such as name, email, phone number, role, and other relevant information to ensure that the student's representation is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your information in the required fields. Note that only fields marked as required must be filled out.
2. Select your role as a student, faculty, or staff member from the role dropdown menu.
3. Choose your category from the select_multiple field. This will help categorize your school and representation.
4. Ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
5. Enter your start and end dates for the ambassadorship.
6. Provide a brief description of your role and responsibilities as an ambassador.
7. Finally, review and submit the form once you have filled out all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **School Name**: Please enter the name of your school or institution.
* **Student Name**: Please enter your name as a student.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that is associated with your school email or personal email.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number that can be contacted for any inquiries or communications.
* **Role**: Select your role as a student, faculty, or staff member.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of your ambassadorship period.
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of your ambassadorship period.
* **Description**: Provide a brief description of your role and responsibilities as an ambassador.
* **Category**: Select one or more categories from the select_multiple field to categorize your school and representation.
* **Assigned Tool**: This field is not currently used, but please leave it blank.
* **Form ID**: This field is not currently used, but please leave it blank.
* **Output File**: This field is not currently used, but please leave it blank.
* **Submitted By**: Select the user that filled out the form. This is not editable by users.
* **Submitted Date**: This field is automatically populated and displays the date and time the form was submitted.
* **Assigned Tool**: Please do not fill out this field. This field is not editable by users. 

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Ensure that your school name and category are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure of any field or have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact your school administrator for assistance.
