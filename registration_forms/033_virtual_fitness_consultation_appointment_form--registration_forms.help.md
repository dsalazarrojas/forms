# virtual_fitness_consultation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The virtual fitness consultation appointment form is designed to gather information from users to schedule a virtual appointment for a fitness consultation. This form will guide you through the process of filling out the form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the User Info section with your name, email, and phone number.
2. Choose an appointment time that suits you best in the Appointment Time section.
3. Select the type of service you require in the Service Type section.
4. Choose the services you want to book in the Services section.
5. Enter the appointment date and time in the Appointment Date and Appointment Time sections.
6. Provide additional information about the services you want to book in the Services Info section.
7. Enter your payment information in the Payment Info section.
8. Choose your payment method and confirm the payment status in the Payment Method and Payment Status sections.
9. Provide any additional comments or information in the Client Message and Additional Message sections.
10. Enter any additional details about the services you want to book in the Services Additional Details section.
11. Finally, enter the appointment location and any additional notes or instructions in the Appointment Location, Appointment Address, Appointment Notes, and Appointment Instructions sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This is where you need to enter your name, email, and phone number.
* **Appointment Time**: Choose an appointment time that suits you best.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service you require, such as Consultation, Coaching, Training, or Workout.
* **Services**: Choose the services you want to book, such as Consultation, Coaching, Training, or Workout.
* **Appointment Date**: Enter the date of the appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: Confirm the time of the appointment.
* **Services Info**: Provide additional information about the services you want to book.
* **Client Info**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional information about yourself.
* **Additional Info**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional information about your appointment.
* **Payment Info**: Enter your payment information.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your payment method, such as Card or Bank.
* **Payment Status**: Confirm your payment status.
* **Services Status**: Confirm the status of the services you want to book.
* **Client Message**: Provide any message to the client.
* **Additional Message**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional message.
* **Services Details**: Provide any additional details about the services you want to book.
* **Services Additional Details**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional details about the services you want to book.
* **Appointment Location**: Choose the location of the appointment, such as Online or In-person.
* **Appointment Address**: Enter the address of the appointment.
* **Appointment Notes**: Provide any additional notes or instructions about the appointment.
* **Appointment Instructions**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional instructions about the appointment.
* **Services Description**: Provide a brief description of the services you want to book.
* **Services Duration**: Provide the duration of the services you want to book.
* **Services Scheduling**: This is an optional section where you can provide any additional information about the scheduling of the services.
