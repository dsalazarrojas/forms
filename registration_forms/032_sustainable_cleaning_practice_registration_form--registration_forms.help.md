# Sustainable Cleaning Practice Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register and track sustainable cleaning practices, including their description, location, and status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in the required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. Enter your first and last name in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your email address and phone number in the corresponding fields.
4. Describe the sustainable cleaning practice in the Practice Description field.
5. Select the practice type (Sustainable, Organic, Biodegradable, or Non-Sustainable) in the Practice Type field.
6. Choose the level of the practice (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, or Level 4) in the Practice Level field.
7. Select the status of the practice (Registered, Active, Inactive, or Removed) in the Practice Status field.
8. Enter the practice URL (if applicable).
9. Add any additional notes about the practice in the Notes field.
10. Select the submitter and update date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Practice Description**: Describe the sustainable cleaning practice.
* **Location**: Enter the location of the practice.
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the practice (optional).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the practice (optional).
* **Contact Person**: Enter the contact person's name (optional).
* **Contact Email**: Enter the contact person's email address (optional).
* **Practice Type**: Select the practice type (Sustainable, Organic, Biodegradable, or Non-Sustainable).
* **Sustainability Goals**: Enter the sustainability goals of the practice (optional).
* **Practice Level**: Choose the level of the practice (Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, or Level 4).
* **Practice Status**: Select the status of the practice (Registered, Active, Inactive, or Removed).
* **Practice URL**: Enter the practice URL (if applicable).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes about the practice.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted Date**: Select the date and time of submission (automatically generated).
* **Updated By**: Enter the name of the person who last updated the form (automatically generated).
* **Updated Date**: Select the date and time of the last update (automatically generated).
* **Created By**: Enter the name of the person who created the form (automatically generated).
* **Created Date**: Select the date and time of creation (automatically generated).
* **Assigned To**: Enter the name of the person assigned to the practice (optional).
* **Assigned Date**: Select the date and time assigned (optional).
* **Assigned User**: Enter the name of the assigned user (optional).

Please note that some fields may be automatically filled in by the system, such as the submtitted date and time, created date and time, and updated date and time. 

Please be aware that some fields are optional, you can enter any additional notes about the practice, such as a more detailed description or any other relevant information.
