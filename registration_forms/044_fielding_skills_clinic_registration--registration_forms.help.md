# Fielding Skills Clinic Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Fielding Skills Clinic Registration form is used to collect player information for the Fielding Skills Clinic. This information is essential for the clinic's trainers to provide proper supervision and care during training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

*   **Player Details**: Enter your name, email, and phone number in this section.
*   **Emergency Contacts**: Provide emergency contact information, including name, email, and phone number.
*   **Medical Info**: Disclose any medical information that may be relevant to your training or participation in the clinic.
*   **Waivers**: Sign and date the waivers to acknowledge your understanding of the clinic's rules and regulations.
*   **Player Signature**: Sign and date this section to confirm your agreement.
*   **Guardian Signature**: If a guardian is accompanying you, sign and date this section to confirm their agreement.
