# long_term_care_planning_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The long-term care planning registration form is designed to gather information from clients or their representatives to support their long-term care needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter your name as the contact person.
2. Enter the email address of the contact person.
3. Enter the name of the care coordinator assigned to you.
4. Enter the email address of the care coordinator.
5. Enter the name of the senior living advisor.
6. Enter the email address of the senior living advisor.
7. Enter the name of the family support organization.
8. Enter the email address of the family support organization.
9. Select the follow-up date for further support.
10. Select the follow-up time for further support.
11. Enter any additional notes or comments about your care.
12. Enter your phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (1)**: This field is for entering the client's name and any other relevant information. Please make sure to enter your name as the contact person.
* **Contact Person (2)**: Enter your name here as the contact person for the client.
* **Contact Person Email (3)**: Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Care Coordinator (4)**: Enter the name of the care coordinator assigned to you.
* **Care Coordinator Email (5)**: Enter the email address of the care coordinator.
* **Senior Living Advisor (6)**: Enter the name of the senior living advisor.
* **Senior Living Advisor Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the senior living advisor.
* **Family Support Organization (8)**: Enter the name of the family support organization.
* **Family Support Organization Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the family support organization.
* **Follow-up Date (10)**: Select the date for follow-up support.
* **Follow-up Time (11)**: Select the time for follow-up support.
* **Notes (12)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your care.
* **Notes Text (13)**: Enter any additional text about your care.
* **Phone (14)**: Enter your phone number.

## Tips

* Please make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in each field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your care coordinator or healthcare professional for guidance.
* This form is for registration purposes only and will not be used for any other purpose.
* Once you have completed the form, please submit it to the care coordinator or healthcare professional for review and further assistance.
