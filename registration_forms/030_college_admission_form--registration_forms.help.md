# College Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Admission Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information from potential students who are interested in applying to the college. This form is used to collect demographic, academic, and personal information that will be used to evaluate a student's eligibility and suitability for admission. Please ensure that all provided information is accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before starting to fill out the form.
2. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Use the provided hints to guide you through each section.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`id: 1`, `name: first_name`, **required: true**): Enter your given name.
* **Middle Name** (`id: 2`, `name: middle_name`, **required: false**): Enter your middle name (if applicable).
* **Last Name** (`id: 3`, `name: last_name`, **required: true**): Enter your family name.
* **Date of Birth** (`id: 4`, `name: date_of_birth`, **required: true**): Enter your date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Gender** (`id: 5`, `name: gender`, **required: true**): Select your gender.
* **Email Address** (`id: 6`, `name: email`, **required: true**): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`id: 7`, `name: phone`, **required: true**): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Street Address** (`id: 8`, `name: address`, **required: true**): Enter your complete address.
* **City** (`id: 9`, `name: city`, **required: true**): Enter the name of the city.
* **State or Province** (`id: 10`, `name: state`, **required: true**): Enter the state or province.
* **ZIP or Postal Code** (`id: 11`, `name: zip`, **required: true**): Enter the postal code.
* **Country** (`id: 12`, `name: country`, **required: true**): Select your country of residence.
* **Citizenship Status** (`id: 13`, `name: citizenship`, **required: true**): Select your citizenship status.
* **High School Name** (`id: 14`, `name: high_school_name`, **required: true**): Enter the name of your high school.
* **High School Address** (`id: 15`, `name: high_school_address`, **required: true**): Enter the address of your high school.
* **Expected Graduation Date** (`id: 16`, `name: graduation_date`, **required: true**): Enter the date you expect to graduate.
* **Current GPA** (`id: 17`, `name: gpa`, **required: true**): Enter your current GPA.
* **Class Rank** (`id: 18`, `name: class_rank`, **required: false**): Enter your class rank (if available).
* **SAT Score** (`id: 19`, `name: sat_score`, **required: false**): Enter your SAT score (if submitted).
* **ACT Score** (`id: 20`, `name: act_score`, **required: false**): Enter your ACT score (if submitted).
* **Intended Major** (`id: 21`, `name: intended_major`, **required: true**): Enter the field of study you intend to pursue.
* **Intended Degree** (`id: 22`, `name: intended_degree`, **required: true**): Select the degree type you intend to pursue.
* **Enrollment Term** (`id: 23`, `name: enrollment_term`, **required: true**): Select the term you plan to enroll.
* **Enrollment Status** (`id: 24`, `name: enrollment_status`, **required: true**): Select your enrollment status.
* **Housing Preference** (`id: 25`, `name: housing_preference`, **required: true**): Select where you will live.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`id: 26`, `name: parent_name`, **required: true**): Enter the name of your primary contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`id: 27`, `name: parent_email`, **required: true**): Enter the email of your primary contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`id: 28`, `name: parent_phone`, **required: true**): Enter the phone number of your primary contact.
* **Interest in Financial Aid** (`id: 29`, `name: financial_aid_interest`, **required: true**): Select if you will apply for financial aid.
* **Have You Filed FAFSA** (`id: 30`, `name: fafsa_filed`, **required: false**): Select if you have filed FAFSA.
* **Scholarships You Are Applying For** (`id: 31`, `name: scholarships_applying`, **required: false**): List any scholarships you are applying for.
* **Extracurricular Activities** (`id: 32`, `name: extracurricular_activities`, **required: true**): List your extracurricular activities and leadership roles.
* **Honors and Awards** (`id: 33`, `name: honors_awards`, **required: false**): List your academic or other honors and awards.
