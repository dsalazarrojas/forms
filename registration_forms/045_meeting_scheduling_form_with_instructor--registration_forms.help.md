# Meeting Scheduling Form With Instructor - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to schedule meetings with instructors, allowing users to select a date, time, instructor, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the date of your meeting on the first field.
2. Choose the time of your meeting from the dropdown menu on the second field.
3. Select the instructor you would like to meet with from the dropdown menu on the third field.
4. Enter any additional details or location for your meeting in the fourth field.
5. If necessary, specify the start and end times of your meeting on the fifth and sixth fields.
6. If needed, provide the expected duration of your meeting on the seventh field.
7. Finally, provide any additional notes or comments about your meeting on the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Prophet's Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, **Required**: false): Enter the date of your scheduled meeting.
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `select_one`, **Required**: false): Choose a time for your meeting from the following options: 9:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 2:00 PM, 4:00 PM, or 5:00 PM.
* **Meeting Instructor** (`meeting_instructor`, `select_one`, **Required**: false): Select a staff member to meet with from the available options.
* **Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, **Required**: false): Enter any additional details about the meeting location (e.g. room number, building name).
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, **Required**: false): Provide any further information about your meeting (e.g. agenda items, discussion topics).
* **Meeting Start Time** (`meeting_start`, `time`, **Required**: false): Specify the start time of your meeting (if different from the selected meeting time).
* **Meeting End Time** (`meeting_end_time`, `time`, **Required**: false): Specify the end time of your meeting (if different from the selected meeting time).
* **Meeting Duration** (`meeting_durations`, `number`, **Required**: false): Enter the expected duration of your meeting.
* **Meeting Note** (`meeting_note`, `note`, **Required**: false): Provide any additional comments or notes about your meeting.
