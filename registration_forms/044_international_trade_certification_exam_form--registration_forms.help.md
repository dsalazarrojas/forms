# International Trade Certification Exam Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The International Trade Certification Exam Form is a form used to register for the International Trade Certification Exam. This form is used to gather information from users who wish to take the exam and ensure that users understand the terms and conditions of the exam.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether you are registering for the exam.
2. Enter the date of the exam you wish to take.
3. Enter the time of the exam you wish to take.
4. Enter the location of the exam.
5. Enter the name of the exam facility where you will be taking the exam.
6. Enter the name of the proctor who will be administering the exam.
7. Enter any additional comments or instructions for the exam.
8. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions of the exam.
9. Enter any additional hints or comments for agreeing to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Register For Exam** (`register_for_exam`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you are registering for the exam, "No" otherwise.
* **Exam Date** (`exam_date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the exam in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Exam Time** (`exam_time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the exam in the format HH:MM.
* **Exam Location** (`exam_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the exam.
* **Exam Facility** (`exam_facility`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" if you will be taking the exam at an external facility.
* **Exam Proctor** (`exam_proctor`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the proctor who will be administering the exam.
* **Exam Instructions** (`exam_instructions`, note, required: false): Enter any additional comments or instructions for the exam.
* **Exam Comments** (`exam_comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about the exam.
* **Agreed To Terms** (`exam_agreed`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" to indicate that you agree to the terms and conditions of the exam.
* **Agreed To Terms Hints** (`exam_agreed_hints`, text, required: false): Enter any additional hints or comments for agreeing to the terms and conditions.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have registered for the exam correctly before proceeding.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the exam administrators.
* Please review the terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* The exam date, time, location, and proctor information will be used to confirm your exam details.
