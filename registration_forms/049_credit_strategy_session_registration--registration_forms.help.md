# credit_strategy_session_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `credit_strategy_session_registration` form is designed to collect information for a credit strategy session. This form is intended for users to provide their details and preferences for the session, which will be used to tailor the session to their needs. Please make sure you have a valid reason for filling out this form and that your session details are accurate.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Session Details** in the text field on the first page.
2. Select up to three **Session Preferences** from the list on the second page.
3. Choose only one **Session Topics** from the list on the third page.
4. Enter your **Contact Details** in the text field on the fourth page.
5. Add any **Session Notes** you would like to include in the text field on the fifth page.
6. Select up to three **Client Consent** options from the list on the sixth page.
7. Enter your **User Notes** in the text field on the seventh page.
8. Select the **Session Status** on the eighth and final page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session Details (Text)** (`session_details`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your session, including any relevant information you want to provide.
* **Session Preferences (Select Multiple)** (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, optional): Choose up to three options that reflect your needs and preferences for the session.
* **Session Topics (Select One)** (`session_topics`, select_one, optional): Select a topic that best describes your session.
* **Contact Details (Text)** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, and phone number if you wish to be contacted.
* **Session Notes (Text)** (`session_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes you have regarding your session.
* **Client Consent (Select Multiple)** (`client_consent`, select_multiple, optional): Select up to three options to indicate your consent and understanding of the session's terms.
* **User Notes (Text)** (`user_notes`, text, optional): Add any comments or notes about your session.
* **Session Status (Select One)** (`session_status`, select_one, optional): Select the status of your session.

## Tips
* Make sure to review your session details carefully before submitting the form.
* Only select options that accurately reflect your preferences and consent.
* Use the session notes field to add any important information about your session.
* Review the session status carefully to ensure it accurately reflects your current status.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
