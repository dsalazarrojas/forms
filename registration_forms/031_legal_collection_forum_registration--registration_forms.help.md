# legal collection forum registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to create a legal collection forum, a professional conference, workshop, or forum. This form will help us gather relevant details such as user contact information, event details, and attendee information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your user information (name, email, phone number, and address) in the "User Information" section.
2. Provide your contact information (email and phone number) in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Enter event details (event name, location, description, start and end dates) in the "Event Details" section.
4. Select your payment method (PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer) in the "Payment Method" section.
5. Check the payment terms and conditions in the "Payment Terms" section.
6. Sign the legal agreement in the "Legal Agreement" section.
7. Enter the event start and end dates in the "Event Start Date" and "Event End Date" fields.
8. Enter the number of attendees in the "Attendee Count" field.
9. Add any additional notes about the event in the "Notes" field.
10. Select the event type (Conference, Workshop, or Forum) and status (Live, Postponed, or Canceled) in the "Event Type" and "Event Status" sections.
11. Choose the event venue (Online, Offline, or Hybrid) in the "Event Venue" section.
12. Enter the attendee's name, email, phone number, and address in the "Attendee Information" section.
13. Add any additional notes about the attendee in the "Attendee Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information (1)**: Enter your name and any other relevant contact information.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Event Details (3)**: Enter the event name, location, and description.
* **Payment Method (4)**: Select your preferred payment method (PayPal, Stripe, or Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Terms (5)**: Check the payment terms and conditions.
* **Legal Agreement (6)**: Sign the legal agreement to proceed.
* **Event Start Date (7)**: Enter the start date of the event.
* **Event End Date (8)**: Enter the end date of the event.
* **Attendee Count (9)**: Enter the number of attendees.
* **Notes (13)**: Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Event Type (17)**: Select the type of event (Conference, Workshop, or Forum).
* **Event Status (18)**: Select the status of the event (Live, Postponed, or Canceled).
* **Event Venue (19)**: Choose the venue type (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
* **Attendee Information (20-23)**: Enter the attendee's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Attendee Note (24)**: Add any additional notes about the attendee.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Use a legitimate email address and phone number for contact information.
* Ensure that the event details are accurate and up-to-date.
* Check the payment terms and conditions carefully before proceeding.
