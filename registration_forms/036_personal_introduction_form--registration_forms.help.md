# Personal Introduction Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Personal Introduction Form is designed to collect personal and professional information from users for registration and introduction purposes. This guide will walk you through each field in the form to help you complete it accurately.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form in the following order: first name, last name, email, background, interests, category, language, and workshop(s) attended.
2. Ensure that all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*) are filled out accurately and completely.
3. Use the correct format for the date of birth and time of birth fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* First Name (Required \*)
	+ Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
### Last Name
* Last Name (Required \*)
	+ Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
### Email
* Email (Required \*)
	+ Enter a valid email address that you can be reached at.
### Phone Number
* Phone Number (Optional)
	+ Enter your phone number if you prefer to be contacted via phone.
### Background
* Background (Required \*)
	+ Briefly describe your work, educational, or personal background.
### Interests
* Interests (Required \*)
	+ Enter a brief description of your hobbies or areas of expertise.
### Profile Picture
* Profile Picture (Optional)
	+ Upload a profile picture for your introduction.
### Date of Birth
* Date of Birth (Required \*)
	+ Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### Time of Birth
* Time of Birth (Optional)
	+ Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
### Note
* Note (Optional)
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments you'd like to include.
### Category
* Category (Required \*)
	+ Choose the category that best represents your area of expertise (e.g., Programming, Design, etc.).
### Language
* Language (Required \*)
	+ Select all languages you are proficient in.
### Which workshop you will attend
* Which workshop you will attend (Required \*)
	+ Select all workshops you plan to attend.

Note: This guide is for reference and may not cover all potential fields or options. Use the form's instructions and hints for specific guidance on each field.
