# Community Service Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Service Intake Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for community service opportunities. This form helps us understand your personal details, skills, and availability to match you with suitable roles and schedules.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your personal details, including your full name, date of birth, email address, contact phone number, and street address.
2. Select your available days of the week and preferred shift time.
3. List the skills you offer, such as administration, data entry, event planning, public speaking, graphic design, web development, social media management, manual labor, gardening, or teaching.
4. If you have any other skills not listed, please specify them.
5. Describe your relevant volunteer experience, if any.
6. Select your areas of interest, such as youth development, environment, animal welfare, senior support, arts and culture, health and wellness, or other options.
7. Confirm your consent for a background check for certain roles.
8. Indicate if you have reliable transportation.
9. Provide your emergency contact's name and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Please enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth to ensure age-appropriate roles.
* **Email Address**: Enter your active email address for communication purposes.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for communication and scheduling.
* **Street Address**: Enter your physical address for logistical purposes.
* **What skills do you offer?**: Select all skills that you are proficient in or have experience with.
* **Any other skills not listed?**: If you have skills not listed, please specify them here.
* **Briefly describe your relevant volunteer experience**: If you have prior experience, describe it briefly.
* **Days Available**: Select the days you are available to volunteer.
* **Shift Preference**: Select your preferred shift time, Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Areas of Interest**: Select areas where you would like to contribute.
* **Consent to background check?**: Confirm if you consent to a background check for certain roles.
* **Do you have reliable transportation?**: Confirm if you have access to reliable transportation.
* **Emergency Contact - Name and Phone**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
