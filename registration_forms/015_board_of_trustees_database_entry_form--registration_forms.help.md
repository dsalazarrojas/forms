**Board Of Trustees Database Entry Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Board Of Trustees Database Entry Form is designed to collect and store information about board members, allowing for easy access and management of board profiles. This form is essential for maintaining up-to-date and accurate records of board members, facilitating communication and collaboration among members, and ensuring that all necessary information is readily available.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Board Of Trustees Database Entry Form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by filling out the member profile header, which provides a brief introduction to the board member's profile.
2. Enter the full name of the board member in the "Full Name" field. This is a required field and should be accurate and up-to-date.
3. Provide the board member's primary email address in the "Primary Email Address" field. This is also a required field and will be used for communication and correspondence.
4. Enter the board member's primary phone number in the "Primary Phone Number" field. This is required for efficient communication.
5. Provide the board member's home address in the "Home Address" field, following the "Street, City, State, Zip" format. This is required for logistical purposes.
6. Move on to the "Professional Information" section, where you will provide details about the board member's current employer or organization.
7. Enter the board member's professional title in the "Professional Title" field. This is a required field that will help identify the member's role and responsibilities.
8. Select all relevant areas of professional expertise for the board member from the dropdown menu. This will help categorize and filter board members by their areas of expertise.
9. Provide the start and end dates of the board member's current term in the "Current Term Start Date" and "Current Term End Date" fields, respectively. These dates are required for accurate tracking and record-keeping.
10. Select the board member's primary committee assignment from the dropdown menu. This will help allocate responsibilities and coordinate committee work.
11. Craft a brief biography for the board member in the "Brief Biography for Website / Records" field, within the 100-200 word limit. This will be used to showcase the member's background and experiences.
12. Optionally, provide information about the emergency contact person, including the emergency contact's name and phone number.
13. Finally, submit the form to ensure that the board member's profile is accurately and thoroughly recorded.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`trustee_full_name`, text, required): Enter the board member's full name, including first and last name.
* **Primary Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter the board member's primary email address for communication and correspondence.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the board member's primary phone number for efficient communication.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): Enter the board member's home address in the "Street, City, State, Zip" format.
* **Current Employer/Organization** (`trustee_employer`, text, required): Enter the board member's current employer or organization.
* **Professional Title** (`trustee_title`, text, required): Enter the board member's professional title.
* **Areas of Professional Expertise** (`expertise_areas`, select_multiple, required): Select all relevant areas of expertise for the board member from the dropdown menu.
* **Current Term Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the board member's current term.
* **Current Term End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the board member's current term.
* **Primary Committee Assignment** (`committee_assignment`, select_one, required): Select the board member's primary committee assignment from the dropdown menu.
* **Brief Biography for Website / Records** (`biography_summary`, text, required): Craft a brief biography of the board member, within the 100-200 word limit.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
