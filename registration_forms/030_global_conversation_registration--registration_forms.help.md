# Global Conversation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Conversation Registration form is designed to collect participant details for a global conversation event. This form gathers essential information to ensure that you have a place in the conversation and receive relevant updates.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your participant details, including your name and other relevant information in the **Participant Details** section.
2. Choose your **Time Zone** from the list of options, as this will help us schedule the conversation according to your time zone.
3. Select the languages you are proficient in from the **Languages** dropdown list, as this will aid in communication during the conversation.
4. Enter the topic you would like to discuss in the **Topic** field.
5. (Optional) Provide your local time for the conversation in the **Time** field.
6. Leave a note if you have any additional comments or requests.
7. Enter your email address and phone number (if you have one) to help us contact you regarding updates and arrangements.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Details**: This is the first section where you provide your participant details, which may include your name, contact information, and other relevant details.
  - **Time Zone**: Select your time zone from the list provided to help us schedule the conversation according to your time zone. This field is required.
  - **Languages**: Choose the languages you are proficient in from the list. This will help us communicate effectively during the conversation. This field is required.
  - **Topic**: Enter the topic you would like to discuss. This can be as general or specific as you like. This field is required.
  - **Time**: Provide your local time for the conversation, if you are able to do so. This is an optional field.
  - **Notes**: Leave a note with any additional comments or requests you have. This field is optional.
  - **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you regarding updates and arrangements. This field is optional.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number (if you have one) for us to contact you. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Please ensure you have all the required fields filled before submitting the form.
- Double-check your Time Zone and Languages selections to ensure accuracy.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at your email address provided.
