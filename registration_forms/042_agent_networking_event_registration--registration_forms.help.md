# Agent Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Agent Networking Event Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees about their preferences, interests, and goals for the networking event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Attendee Full Name in the "Attendee Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Agency or Brokerage Name in the "Agency or Brokerage Name" field.
3. Select your primary Industry Focus area from the "Industry Focus" dropdown menu.
4. Enter your Contact Email address in the "Contact Email" field.
5. Enter your Phone Number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
6. Describe your primary networking goals for this event in the "Networking Goals" field.
7. Select how you heard about this event from the "Referral Source" dropdown menu.
8. Choose your Dietary Preferences for the Catered Lunch in the "Dietary Preferences" field.
9. Indicate your interest in future sponsorship opportunities by selecting all that apply in the "Sponsorship Interest" field (optional).
10. Agree to receive communications about future networking events by selecting "Yes, I agree" in the "Consent" field.
11. Choose to be included in the attendee directory shared with other participants by selecting "Yes, include me" in the "Directory Consent" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Full Name**: Please enter your full name as it will be displayed in the attendee list.
* **Agency or Brokerage Name**: Enter the name of your agency or brokerage.
* **Industry Focus**: Select your primary business area, such as Residential Real Estate, Commercial Real Estate, Insurance Services, Financial Planning, or Mortgage / Lending.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached for event updates and tickets.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number (optional, but recommended for easy communication).
* **Networking Goals**: Describe your primary goals for attending this event.
* **Referral Source**: Select how you heard about this event from the available options.
* **Dietary Preferences**: Choose your dietary preferences for the catered lunch.
* **Sponsorship Interest**: Select all that apply for future sponsorship opportunities (optional).
* **Consent**: Agree to receive communications about future networking events.
* **Directory Consent**: Choose to be included in the attendee directory shared with other participants.
