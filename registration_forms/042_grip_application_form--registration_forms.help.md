# Grip Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to apply for a grip position by providing personal details, equipment and skill lists, and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the first two fields.
2. Enter your email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your contact number in the Contact Number field.
4. Enter the role you are applying for in the Role field.
5. If you have relevant work experience, enter it in the Experience field.
6. List any equipment you have access to in the Equipment List field.
7. If you have professional references, list them in the References field.
8. If you have any skills, list them in the Skills field.
9. Please upload your resume in the Resume field.
10. Please upload your portfolio in the Portfolio field.
11. If you have supporting information about your references, upload it in the Upload References field.
12. Confirm your agreement with the terms of service and data protection by selecting "Yes" in the Agree Terms field.
13. Confirm your agreement with the privacy policy by selecting "Yes" in the Agree Privacy field.
14. Select "Yes" or "No" in the Submit field to confirm or reject submission of the application.
15. If you are applying for another position, fill in the duplicate fields (First Name 2, Last Name 2, Role 2, Contact Number 2, Experience 2) with your information.
16. If you have any additional equipment, upload it in the Upload Equipment field.
17. If you have any additional skills, upload them in the Upload Skills field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, not required): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, not required): Enter your contact number.
* **Role** (`role`, `text`, not required): Enter the grip position you are applying for.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, not required): Enter any relevant work experience.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `text`, not required): Not present in the form, but this field seems to be related to your availability for work.
* **Equipment List** (`equipment_list`, `text`, not required): List any equipment you have access to.
* **References** (`references`, `text`, not required): List professional references with name and contact information.
* **Skills** (`skills`, `text`, not required): List any skills you have.
* **Resumen** (`upload_resume`, `text`, not required): Upload your resume in PDF or Word format.
* **Portfolio** (`upload_portfolo`, `text`, not required): Upload your portfolio in PDF or Word format.
* **Upload References** (`upload_references`, `text`, not required): Upload supporting information about your references in PDF or Word format.
* **Agree Terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, not required): Confirm agreement with the terms of service by selecting "Yes".
* **Agree Privacy** (`agree_privacy`, `select_one`, not required): Confirm agreement with the privacy policy by selecting "Yes".
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, not required): Select "Yes" to submit the application, or "No" to reject submission.
* **First Name 2** (`first_name_2`, `text`, not required): Duplicate of First Name.
* **Last Name 2** (`last_name_2`, `text`, not required): Duplicate of Last Name.
* **Role 2** (`role_2`, `select_one`, not required): Duplicate of Role.
* **Contact Number 2** (`contact_number_2`, `text`, not required): Duplicate of Contact Number.
* **Experience 2** (`experience_2`, `text`, not required): Duplicate of Experience.
* **Upload Equipment** (`upload_equipment`, `text`, not required): Upload any additional equipment.
* **Upload Skills** (`upload_skills`, `text`, not required): Upload any additional skills.
* **Agree Terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, not required): Duplicate of Agree Terms.
* **Agree Privacy** (`agree_privacy`, `select_one`, not required): Duplicate of Agree Privacy.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `select_one`, not required): Duplicate of Submit.
* **Agree Terms** (`agree_terms`, `select_one`, not required): Not present in the form.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, contact the person or organization administering the form.
* Review the terms of service and data protection before selecting "Yes" in the Agree Terms field.
* Review the privacy policy before selecting "Yes" in the Agree Privacy field.
* Select "Yes" in the Submit field to confirm submission of the application.
* If you are applying for another position, make sure to fill out the duplicate fields with your information.
