# public_speaking_coaching_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from clients about their public speaking coaching sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with the required information.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all necessary fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Client Session**: Please enter a brief description of the client's session.
* **Session Details**: Enter any additional details about the client's session.
* **Booking Time**: Select the time you would like to schedule the coaching session.
* **Booking Date**: Select the date you would like to schedule the coaching session.
* **Coach Details**: Enter any additional information about the coaching session that you would like to share with the coach.
* **Coaching Type**: Choose whether the client has opted for coaching or not.
* **Client Requirements**: Enter any specific requirements that the client has.
* **Additional Comments**: Add any additional comments about the client or the coaching session.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Enter accurate and relevant information to ensure a successful coaching session.
