# Job Fair Registration Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for job seekers and event organizers to register and pay for job fairs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user name and email address.
2. Enter your job title and event date.
3. Select your job fair type (job seeker, event organizer, or employer).
4. Choose your payment method (online, on-site, or invoice).
5. Enter the total payment amount.
6. Select the payment status (paid, pending, or failed).
7. Optionally, enter notes for the payment.
8. Enter your employer's name, phone number, and event organizer's name (if applicable).
9. Enter your event address, city, state, zip, and country.
10. Enter the job fair date and time.
11. Enter the job fair location and description.
12. Enter the job fair URL (link to the job fair website).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (`user_name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`user_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the job you're applying for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Type** (`job_fair_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event (job seeker, event organizer, or employer).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose your payment method (online, on-site, or invoice).
* **Total Payment** (`payment_total`, number, required): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Select the payment status (paid, pending, or failed).
* **Notes** (`payment_note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the payment.
* **Employer Name** (`employer_name`, text, optional): If you're an employer, enter your company's name.
* **Employer Phone** (`employer_phone`, text, optional): If you're an employer, enter your company's phone number.
* **Event Organizer** (`event_organizer`, text, optional): If you're an event organizer, enter your company's name.
* **Event Address** (`event_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the event.
* **Event City** (`event_city`, text, required): Enter the city of the event.
* **Event State** (`event_state`, text, required): Enter the state of the event.
* **Event Zip** (`event_zip`, text, required): Enter the zip code of the event.
* **Event Country** (`event_country`, text, required): Enter the country of the event.
* **Event Notes** (`event_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about the event.
* **Job Fair Date** (`job_fair_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the job fair.
* **Job Fair Time** (`job_fair_time`, text, required): Enter the time of the job fair.
* **Job Fair Location** (`job_fair_location`, text, required): Enter the location of the job fair.
* **Job Fair Description** (`job_fair_description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the job fair.
* **Job Fair URL** (`job_fair_url`, text, required): Enter the URL of the job fair website.
* **Job Fair URL Text** (`job_fair_url_text`, text, required): Enter the text to display on the job fair URL.
* **Job Fair URL Link** (`job_fair_url_link`, text, optional): Enter the link text for the job fair URL.
