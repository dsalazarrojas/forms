# Voice Over Recording Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Voice Over Recording Reservation Form is a tool used to reserve and schedule voice over recording services. This form helps us to gather essential information from customers, such as project details, required dates and times, and contact information. It allows us to efficiently manage reservations and ensure smooth communication with clients.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **form_title** field with a brief description of the project.
2. Provide a detailed **form_description** of the project, including any specific requirements or needs.
3. Select a **date** for the recording session from the calendar provided.
4. Choose a **time** for the recording session from the available options.
5. Select one or more **project_details** options that best fit your project needs.
6. Enter any **required_details** specific to your project.
7. Enter any **project_requirements** necessary for the recording process.
8. Select the **recording_location** from the provided options.
9. Enter the type of **recording_type** required for your project.
10. Enter your **contact_info** including name, company name, and any other relevant contact details.
11. Enter your **email** address for easy communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **form_title**: A brief description of the project.
* **form_description**: A detailed description of the project, including any specific requirements or needs.
* **date**: Select a date for the recording session from the calendar provided.
* **time**: Choose a time for the recording session from the available options.
* **project_details**: Select one or more options that best fit your project needs (e.g., animation, video, etc.).
* **required_details**: Enter any specific details required for the recording process.
* **project_requirements**: Enter any special requirements for the recording process.
* **recording_location**: Select the location where the recording will take place.
* **recording_type**: Enter the type of recording required (e.g., voice, music, etc.).
* **contact_info**: Enter your name, company name, and any other relevant contact details.
* **email**: Enter your email address for easy communication.
* **submit**: Click the submit button to complete and finalize your reservation.
