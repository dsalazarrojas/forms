# Data Analytics Retail Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Analytics Retail Bootcamp Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants to facilitate their registration and preparation for the bootcamp. It is crucial to fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure that you receive the necessary materials and have a smooth experience during the bootcamp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Provide clear and concise answers for each field.
3. Double-check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
4. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (id: 1, name: first_name, label: First Name, type: text, required: false)
*   This field is for your personal name.

### Last Name (id: 2, name: last_name, label: Last Name, type: text, required: false)
*   This field is for your surname.

### Email (id: 3, name: email, label: Email, type: email, required: false)
*   This field is for your professional email address.

### Phone (id: 4, name: phone, label: Phone, type: text, required: false)
*   This field is for your contact phone number.

### What is your role in the organization? (id: 5, name: select_one, label: What is your role in the organization?, type: select_one, required: false)
*   Please select your role from the available options (Customer, Manager, Employee).

### Which departments do you want to be involved in? (id: 6, name: select_multiple, label: Which departments do you want to be involved in?, type: select_multiple, required: false)
*   Please select all relevant departments from the available options (Marketing, Sales, Operations).

### Date (id: 7, name: date, label: Date, type: date, required: false)
*   This field is for the date you want to participate in the bootcamp.

### Time (id: 8, name: time, label: Time, type: time, required: false)
*   This field is for the time you want to participate in the bootcamp.

### Additional notes (id: 9, name: note, label: Additional notes, type: note, required: false)
*   Use this field to provide any additional information you think is relevant to your registration.
