# Production Optimization Software Fair Registration
## Purpose
The Production Optimization Software Fair Registration form is designed to gather information from attendees for a more personalized experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your event title, type, date, time, and location details.
2. Provide your contact information (name, email, contact number, and company) for communication and follow-up.
3. Select the type of attendee you are (Regular or VIP).
4. Briefly describe yourself and your role in the production team.
5. Specify your contact preferences (e.g., email, phone, or chat).
6. Add any additional notes about your expectations or interests.
7. Choose the assigned tool you have experience with (chatjimmy or other).
8. Write a brief description of your event or interests.
9. Select the type of software you are interested in (Production Optimization Software or Other).
10. If applicable, provide the name of your production company and production team.
10. Finally, provide any additional job title or details about yourself.
