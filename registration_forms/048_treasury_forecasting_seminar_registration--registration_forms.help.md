# Treasury Forecasting Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the "Treasury Forecasting Seminar" registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the **Seminar Details** field with any relevant information about the seminar you want to attend.
2. In the **Attendee Details** field, enter your name, contact information, and any other details you want to share with the seminar organizers.
3. If you have any **Session Preferences**, use this field to specify your preferences for the seminar sessions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Seminar Details
* **Seminar Details** (required): Enter a brief description or any relevant details about the seminar you want to attend.

### Attendee Details
* **Attendee Details** (required): Enter your name, contact information, and any other details you want to share with the seminar organizers.

### Session Preferences
* **Session Preferences** (optional): Specify your preferences for the seminar sessions, such as the topics or time slots you are interested in.

### Consent
* **Consent** (required): Confirm that you have read and agree to the terms and conditions of the seminar.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure that your registration is complete.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, use the **Session Preferences** field to communicate them to the seminar organizers.
* Review your filled-out form before submitting it to ensure that all information is accurate and complete.
