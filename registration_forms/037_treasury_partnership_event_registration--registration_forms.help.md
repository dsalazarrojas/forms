# Treasury Partnership Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to help event organizers register their events with the Treasury Partnership organization. Please fill out this form to provide the necessary information for your event to be listed in our database.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Event Details** section with your event information.
2. Provide details about your **Attendees** in the **Attendee Details** section.
3. Select your **Session Choices** by checking the relevant boxes.
4. Choose your **Interests** by selecting the relevant options.
5. Add any additional **Notes** or comments about your event.
6. Enter your **Email** address for contact purposes.
7. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required): This field is for providing general information about your event, such as the name and description.
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, required): Please provide details about the attendees of your event, including their names and roles.
* **Session Choices** (`session_choices`, select_multiple, required): Select the sessions you would like to offer at your event by checking the relevant boxes.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, required): Choose the interests that are relevant to your event.
* **Notes** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your event that we should be aware of.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your event.
* **Session** (`session`, text, optional): This field is not currently in use, please do not fill it out.
