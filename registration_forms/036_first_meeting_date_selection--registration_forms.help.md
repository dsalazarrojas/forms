# First Meeting Date Selection - Help Guide

## Purpose
The First Meeting Date Selection form is designed to collect information about the first meeting date of a client. This form helps to determine the availability of a service provider and ensures that all relevant details are captured for future reference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Select Date**: Choose the date of the first meeting with the client.
2. **Meeting Time**: Choose the time of the meeting (if applicable).
3. **Select Service Provider**: Select whether or not the service provider will be available for the selected date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Date (Required/Optional)**: This field allows you to choose the date of the first meeting with the client. Select a date using the calendar picker or type it manually.
* **Meeting Time (Required/Optional)**: This field is used to choose the time of the meeting (if applicable). Enter the meeting time or select a time from the available options.
* **Select Service Provider (Required/Optional)**: This field indicates whether or not the service provider will be available for the selected date. Select "Yes" if the service provider is available, and "No" otherwise.

## Tips
* Ensure that the date selected is correct and accurately reflects the first meeting date.
* If the service provider is not available, select "No" in the Select Service Provider field.
* This form is designed to be used for future reference, so ensure that all details are accurate and complete.
