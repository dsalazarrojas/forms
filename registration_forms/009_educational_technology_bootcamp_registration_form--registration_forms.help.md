# Educational Technology Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from potential attendees of an educational technology bootcamp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the text fields provided in each page. For each field, please make sure to enter the required information according to the label and hint provided.
1. Go to [Page One: Personal Information](#Page-One: Personal Information)
2. Fill in the required information in the fields provided.
3. Move on to the next page for the next set of fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page One: Personal Information
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`text`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`text`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number.

### Page Two: Registration Details
* **Registration Number** (`text`, `text`, optional): Enter your registration number (if any).
* **Program of Interest** (`text`, `text`, required): Select the program that interests you the most.

### Page Three: Page Two Fields
* **Any Other Comments** (`text`, `text`, optional): Enter any other comments or information.

### Page Four: Contact Information
* **Contact Name** (`text`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.

* **Contact Phone** (`text`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.

### Page Five: Emergency Contact
