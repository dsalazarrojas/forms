# seo_training_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The SEO training enrollment form is designed to collect information from users for the purpose of enrolling them in SEO training.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to enable us to contact you for further information.
3. Provide your Phone number for easy contact.
4. Enter your Date of Birth for identity verification purposes.
5. Write down your Address for reference.
6. Specify your Training Provider if any.
7. Choose if you're interested in any Training Program.
8. Select your Course Level of interest (High or Low).
9. Mention your Course Interest for us to understand your needs.
10. If applicable, specify your School affiliation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Please fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Not required): Please fill in your last name (optional).
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Not required): Enter your Email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Not required): Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, Date, Not required): Enter your Date of Birth for identity verification purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, Note, Not required): Please provide your address for reference.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, Text, Not required): If you have a training provider, please specify it.
* **Training Program** (`training_program`, Select One, Not required): Choose if you're interested in any Training Program.
* **Course Level** (`course_level`, Select Multiple, Not required): Select your Course Level of interest (High or Low).
* **Course Interest** (`course_interest`, Text, Not required): Please mention your Course Interest for us to understand your needs.
* **School** (`school`, Text, Not required): If applicable, specify your School affiliation.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in for a successful submission.
* Use a valid email address and phone number for efficient communication.
* Choose your Course Level and Training Program according to your needs.
