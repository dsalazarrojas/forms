# Freshman Welcome Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students to help the orientation team better understand their background, interests, and experiences. This information will be used to tailor the orientation program to meet the needs of each student.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions for each field. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information for each field. If you need help with any of the fields, do not hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Name
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.

### Student Email
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address. This will be used to communicate with you about orientation events and information.

### Student Phone
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you in case of emergencies or to reach out to you about orientation events.

### Parent Email
* **Parent Email** (`student_email_2`, email, required/optional): Enter your parent's email address. This will be used to keep them informed about orientation events and information.

### Grade Level
* **Grade Level** (`student_grade_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select your current grade level.

### Orientation Group
* **Orientation Group** (`student_orientation_group`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the orientation group you would like to be part of.

### College Major
* **College Major** (`student_college_major`, select_one, required/optional): Select your college major.

### College Year
* **College Year** (`student_college_year`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the year you are entering college.

### High School Name
* **High School Name** (`student_high_school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your high school.

### High School GPA
* **High School GPA** (`student_high_school_gpa`, number, required/optional): Enter your high school GPA.

### High School City
* **High School City** (`student_high_school_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city of your high school.

### High School State
* **High School State** (`student_high_school_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state of your high school.

### High School Country
* **High School Country** (`student_high_school_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country of your high school.

### High School Grad Date
* **High School Grad Date** (`student_high_school_grad_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you graduated from high school.

### High School Grad Time
* **High School Grad Time** (`student_high_school_grad_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you graduated from high school.

### High School Grad City
* **High School Grad City** (`student_high_school_grad_city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you graduated from high school.

### High School Grad State
* **High School Grad State** (`student_high_school_grad_state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state where you graduated from high school.

### High School Grad Country
* **High School Grad Country** (`student_high_school_grad_country`, text, required/optional): Enter the country where you graduated from high school.

### High School Grad School
* **High School Grad School** (`student_high_school_grad`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your high school.

### Orientation Date
* **Orientation Date** (`student_orientation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you attended orientation.

### Major Decision
* **Major Decision** (`student_major_decision`, select_one, required/optional): Select if you have made a major decision.

### Interested In
* **Interested In** (`student_major_interested_in`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the majors that interest you.

### Concentration
* **Concentration** (`student_major_concentration`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the concentrations you are interested in.

### High School Activities
* **High School Activities** (`student_high_school_activities`, text, required/optional): Enter any notable high school activities.

### High School Awards
* **High School Awards** (`student_high_school_awards`, text, required/optional): Enter any high school awards you have received.

Note: Some fields are not required and can be skipped if not applicable.
