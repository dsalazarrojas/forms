# Ingredient Quality Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for the Ingredient Quality Symposium. It is designed to gather essential details about the attendee and symposium-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate information.
2. Choose the correct options for symposium-related fields.
3. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name (1)
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Fill out your full name.
### Last Name (2)
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Fill out your last name.
### Email (3)
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
### Company (4)
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you are registered to a company, fill out the name.
### Position (5)
* **Position** (`position`, text, required): Fill out your title or position within your company.
### Registration Date (6)
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Fill out the date you registered for the symposium.
### Registration Time (7)
* **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, optional): If you registered at a specific time, fill it out.
### Symposium Start Date (8)
* **Symposium Start Date** (`symposium_start_date`, date, optional): If the symposium has a start date, fill it out.
### Symposium End Date (9)
* **Symposium End Date** (`symposium_end_date`, date, optional): If the symposium has an end date, fill it out.
### Payment Status (10)
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, select_one, required): Choose your payment status (Paid or Pending).
### Registration Method (11)
* **Registration Method** (`registration_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you registered (Online or On-site).
### Symposium Location (12)
* **Symposium Location** (`symposium_location`, select_one, required): Choose the symposium location (Online or In-person).
### Symposium Type (13)
* **Symposium Type** (`symposium_type`, select_one, required): Choose the symposium type (Virtual or Offline).
### Symposium Venue (14)
* **Symposium Venue** (`symposium_venue`, select_one, optional): If the symposium has a venue, choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Symposium Language (15)
* **Symposium Language** (`symposium_language`, select_one, optional): If the symposium has a language, choose 'Yes' or 'No'.
### Symposium Program (16)
* **Symposium Program** (`symposium_program`, select_multiple, required): Choose the symposium programs (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
### Attendee Name (17)
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): Fill out the name of the attendee, if applicable.
### Attendee Email (18)
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, optional): Fill out the email of the attendee, if applicable.
### Attendee Position (19)
* **Attendee Position** (`attendee_position`, text, optional): Fill out the position of the attendee, if applicable.
### Attendee Company (20)
* **Attendee Company** (`attendee_company`, text, optional): Fill out the company of the attendee, if applicable.
### Attendee Reg Date (21)
* **Attendee Reg Date** (`attendee_reg_date`, date, optional): Fill out the date the attendee registered, if applicable.
### Attendee Reg Time (22)
* **Attendee Reg Time** (`attendee_reg_time`, time, optional): Fill out the time the attendee registered, if applicable.
### Attendee Symposium Start Date (23)
* **Attendee Symposium Start Date** (`attendee_symposium_start_date`, date, optional): Fill out the start date of the attendee's symposium, if applicable.
### Attendee Symposium End Date (24)
* **Attendee Symposium End Date** (`attendee_symposium_end_date`, date, optional): Fill out the end date of the attendee's symposium, if applicable.
### Attendee Notes (25)
* **Attendee Notes** (`attendee_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes about the attendee, if applicable.
