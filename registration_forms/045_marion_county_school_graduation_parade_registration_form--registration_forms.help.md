# marion_county_school_graduation_parade_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marion County School Graduation Parade registration form is designed to gather information from participants and their schools to organize the graduation parade events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all the fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you provide the correct participant and school names.
3. Enter the correct graduation date and time.
4. Specify the event location and start time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant's school.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the participant's graduation.
* **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the participant's graduation.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Enter the location where the parade will take place.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the parade event.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Enter the start time of the parade.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant (this field should be filled out by the participant themselves).
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the participant's school (this field should be filled out by the participant themselves).
