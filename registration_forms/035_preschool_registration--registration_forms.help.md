# Preschool Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Preschool Registration form is designed to collect information from parents or guardians to register their child for the preschool program. This form is intended for new registrations only. If you have already submitted a registration, please do not attempt to submit another.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your child's First Name.
2. Enter your child's Last Name.
3. Enter your email address (for contact purposes only).
4. Enter your child's Date of Birth (in the format MM/DD/YYYY).
5. Enter your child's address.
6. Enter your child's phone number.
7. Enter the name of your emergency contact.
8. Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
9. Select your relationship with your emergency contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your child's last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact purposes only. This will be used to communicate with you about your child's registration and program updates.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's Date of Birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g., 05/12/2022).
* **Address**: Enter your child's current address, including street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone**: Enter your child's phone number for contact purposes only.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to have contact you or your child in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Relationship**: Select how you know your emergency contact (e.g., parent, aunt, uncle, etc.).
