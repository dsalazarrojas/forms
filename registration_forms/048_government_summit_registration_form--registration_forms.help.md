# Government Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Summit Registration Form is designed to collect essential details from participants to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable experience at the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the **Registration Form Data** (Page 1) with your information as required.
2. Provide your **Contact Information** (Page 2), including your email address and other relevant details.
3. Submit your **Participant Details** (Page 3), such as your name and address.
4. Choose a date and time that suits you best for the **Registration Date** and **Time of Day** fields (Page 4).
5. Add any additional details you'd like to share about yourself in the **Participant Name** field (Page 5).
6. Enter your **Email** and **Phone** (Page 6) details for us to contact you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form Data** (`registration_form_data`): This section is not required and is intended to be left blank.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`): Enter your email address and other relevant contact details so we can get in touch with you.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This section is not required and is optional for you to add any additional details about yourself.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Choose a date for your attendance, but please note that it is not required.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`): Select a time of day for your attendance.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): This section is not required and is optional for you to add any additional details about yourself.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): This is not required and is optional for you to add your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`): This section is not required and is optional for you to add any additional details about your address.

## Tips
- Fill in the required fields carefully and accurately.
- Use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you effectively.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [your contact email or phone number].

Please note that this form is not intended for official government use. It is designed to facilitate participant interactions and engagement. If you are a government official and need to register for the summit, please use our separate registration process.
