# Global Online Academy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information from users interested in joining the Global Online Academy. Enter your details and course preferences, and you will receive more information about the program.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, fill out the required fields with your information and course preferences. Make sure to enter your details accurately and honestly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Course Details** (`course_details`, text, required): Enter the title of the course you are interested in joining. This will help us know which course you want to be part of.
* **Student Details** (`student_details`, text, required): Enter your personal details as a student, including your name, email, and any other relevant information. This will help us understand who is registering and contact them accordingly.
* **Sign Up Info** (`signup_info`, text, optional): This field is for any additional information you would like to provide about yourself. It's optional, but it can help us get a better understanding of you as a student.
* **Repeat Course Details** (`course_details_repeat`, text, required): This field is to be filled out again for each course you are interested in joining. For example, if you want to join multiple courses, enter the course titles in this field separated by commas or on new lines.
