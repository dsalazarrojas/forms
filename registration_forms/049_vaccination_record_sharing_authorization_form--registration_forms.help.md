# Vaccination Record Sharing Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from the patient or their representative about who to share their vaccination records with. It is intended to ensure that the patient's privacy and consent are respected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form and ensure you understand the questions and what information is being requested.
2. Read each field carefully and answer the questions truthfully.
3. Fill out the form completely, including selecting all relevant options.
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Sharing Authorization** (`sharing_authorization`, note, required: false):
This field is a note that explains the purpose of the form. It is not a field to be filled out, but rather a statement of intent.
 
- **Sharing Institution** (`sharing_institution`, select_one, required: false):
This field asks if you want to share your vaccination records with a healthcare institution. Select "Yes" if you agree to share, and "No" if you do not.

- **Sharing Institution ID** (`sharing_institution_id`, select_multiple, required: false):
This field asks if you want to share your vaccination records with a specific healthcare institution's ID. Select "Yes" if you agree to share, and "No" if you do not.

- **Sharing Who** (`sharing_who`, select_one, required: false):
This field asks who you want to share your vaccination records with. Select "Healthcare provider" or "Insurance company" to indicate who you want to share with.

- **Sharing With** (`sharing_with_who`, select_multiple, required: false):
This field asks with whom you want to share your vaccination records. Select "Yes" if you agree to share with the selected entity, and "No" if you do not.

- **Sharing With Who** (`sharing_with_who`, select_multiple, required: false):
This field is similar to "Sharing With" but is a repeat of the question to ensure you have selected all relevant parties.

- **Sharing With Who ID** (`sharing_with_who_id`, select_multiple, required: false):
This field is also similar to "Sharing With" but is asking about a specific ID for the entity you want to share with.

- **Sharing Date** (`sharing_date`, date, required: false):
This field is for selecting a date when you want to begin sharing your vaccination records. 

## Tips
- Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
- If you are unsure about any of the questions, do not hesitate to reach out for clarification.
- Double-check your answers before submitting the form.
