# travel_photography_workshop_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to collect information from users who want to enroll in a travel photography workshop. Please fill it out accurately and completely to ensure that we can tailor the workshop experience to your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used to confirm your email address.
3. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field by re-entering the same email address.
4. Select your preferred accommodation type from the "Accommodation Preferences" dropdown list.
5. Choose your preferred workshop level from the "Workshop Level" dropdown list.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown list.
7. If you have a preferred workshop date, please enter it in the "Preferred Workshop Date" field.
8. If you have any photo equipment, please list it in the "Photo Equipment" field.
9. If you have any dietary restrictions, please mention them in the "Dietary Restrictions" field.
10. Please provide your emergency contact information, including their name, relationship, phone number, email, and address.
11. Finally, read and agree to the "Terms and Conditions" before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email** (email): Re-enter your email address to confirm it.
* **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
* **Preferred Workshop Date** (date): If you have a preferred workshop date, enter it.
* **Accommodation Preferences** (select multiple): Select your accommodation preferences (Hotel, Airbnb, Other).
* **Workshop Level** (select one): Choose your preferred workshop level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Photo Equipment** (text): List any photo equipment you have.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (text): Mention any dietary restrictions.
* **Payment Method** (select one): Select your preferred payment method (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Details** (text): Provide your payment details.
* **Workshop Location** (text): If you have a preferred workshop location, enter it.
* **Workshop Duration** (text): If you have a preferred workshop duration, enter it.
* **Special Requests** (text): Mention any special requests.
* **Emergency Contact** (text): Enter your emergency contact's name.
* **Relationship** (text): Enter your relationship with the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text): Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email): Enter the emergency contact's email address.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (text): Enter the emergency contact's address.
* **Terms and Conditions** (note): Read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
* **Submit** (text): Submit the form after filling out all the fields.
