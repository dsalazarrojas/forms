# Leadership-Training-Intake-Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The leadership-training-intake-form is used to collect participant information for leadership training programs. It is essential that you provide accurate and complete information to ensure that your training program is tailored to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form completely and accurately.
2. Please ensure that all required fields are filled out.
3. If you are unsure about any of the fields, you can refer to the field explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Participant Information

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Participant Role** (`participant_role`, text, optional): Describe your role within the organization.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): Enter your organization's name.
* **Organization Size** (`organization_size`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if your organization has more than 10 employees.
	+ Select 'No' if your organization has 10 or fewer employees.
* **Training Needs** (`training_needs`, text, optional): Describe your training needs and expectations.

### Page 2: Training Program Details

* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have been assigned a tool or software as part of the training.
	+ Select 'No' if you do not have an assigned tool or software.
* **Date of Training** (`date_of_training`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the training program.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the training program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the training program.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you have completed the required training hours.
	+ Select 'No' if you have not completed the required training hours.
* **Training Language** (`training_language`, select_one, optional):
	+ Select 'Yes' if you are fluent in the training language.
	+ Select 'No' if you are not fluent in the training language.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Provide any additional information that may be helpful for your training program.

## Tips
* Please answer all questions honestly and accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact the form administrators.
* This form is confidential and your information will only be used for the purpose of the leadership training program.
