# PreTeen Activity Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The PreTeen Activity Registration form is designed to collect information about a student's pre-teen activities. This form is intended for pre-teen students who are participating in activities that require registration, such as sports, camps, or clubs. The form helps to gather essential details about the student's guardian, activity, and student information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the PreTeen Activity Registration form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form with the required information, including guardian information, activity selection, and student details.
2. Select the relevant options for each field, such as the guardian's relationship to the student and the activity date.
3. Enter any additional notes or comments about the student.
4. Review and confirm the information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional)
	+ This field is the first step in completing the form.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_information`, text, optional)
	+ Enter your name and contact information as the guardian.
* **Parent/Guardian 1** (`parent_guardian1`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select the first parent/guardian from the options provided.
* **Parent/Guardian 2** (`parent_guardian2`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Select the second parent/guardian from the options provided.
* **Activity Selection** (`activity_selection`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose the activity that the student is participating in.
* **Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, select_one, optional)
	+ Check the box to confirm that you are the legal guardian or emergency contact.
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, text, optional)
	+ Enter any additional information about the student's registration.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the student's ID number.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's name.
* **Student Birthdate** (`student_birthdate`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the student's birthdate.
* **Activity Date** (`activity_date`, date, optional)
	+ Enter the date of the activity.
* **Activity Time** (`activity_time`, time, optional)
	+ Enter the time of the activity.
* **Student School** (`student_school`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's school name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, number, optional)
	+ Enter the student's grade level.
* **Parent/Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Parent/Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, optional)
	+ Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's address.
* **Student Postal** (`student_postal`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's postal code.
* **Student City** (`student_city`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's city.
* **Student Province** (`student_province`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's province.
* **Student Country** (`student_country`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's country.
* **Student Zip** (`student_zip`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's zip code.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, optional)
	+ Enter the student's phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional)
	+ Enter any additional notes about the student.
* **Confirmation** (`confirmation`, text, optional)
	+ Review and confirm the information before submitting the form.

Note: This form is designed for pre-teen students and may vary depending on your specific use case. Make sure to review and adjust the form fields according to your needs.
