# July_Qoin_Registration_Process - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather business registration information for categorization and record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required and optional fields with accurate information about your business.
2. Select the relevant category for your business from the dropdown menu.
3. Add any additional tags that apply to your business.
4. Upload any relevant files (if applicable).
5. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, not required): This is the main registration form where you can provide a brief description of your business.
*   **Business Details** (`business_details`, text, not required): This field is for providing additional information about your business.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, text, not required): Enter your business's contact information here.
*   **Business Name** (`business_name`, text, not required): Enter your business's name.
*   **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, not required): Enter your business's address.
*   **Business Email** (`business_email`, email, not required): Enter your business's email address.
*   **Business Phone** (`business_phone`, text, not required): Enter your business's phone number.
*   **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, not required): Enter any additional registration details about your business.
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, not required): Enter the date of your business registration.
*   **Registration Time** (`registration_time`, time, not required): Enter the time of your business registration.
*   **Registration Note** (`registration_note`, note, not required): Enter any additional notes about your business registration.

### 2. Category Selection
*   **Category** (`category`, select_one, not required): Choose the category that best describes your business from the list.
*   **Other Category** (`category_other`, text, not required): If your business doesn't fit into any of the provided categories, enter a custom category name.
*   **Category ID** (`category_id`, number, not required): This is a system-generated category ID (do not input anything).

### 3. Tags Selection
*   **Tags** (`tags`, select_multiple, not required): Select relevant tags for your business from the list.
*   **Other Tag** (`other`, text, not required): If none of the provided tags fit, enter a custom tag.

### 4. File Uploads
*   **File Uploads** (`file_uploads`, note, not required): This field is reserved for uploading files, but it does not currently support file uploads.

### 5. Submission
*   **Submit** (`submit`, text, not required): Click this button to submit the form and complete the registration process.

## Tips
*   Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly to avoid errors or incomplete registrations.
*   If you have any issues or concerns while filling out the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
