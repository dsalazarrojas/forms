# Community Beautification Project Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Beautification Project Registration form is designed to gather information about volunteers interested in participating in community beautification activities. This form will help us understand your demographic information, volunteer preferences, and contact details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Information section, including your unique registration ID, registration date, and full name.
2. Enter your contact details, including your phone number, email address, and street address.
3. Choose your age group and preferred projects or beautification activities.
4. Select your preferred availability dates and times.
5. Choose your skills and interests (if you have any).
6. If you are registering as part of a group, enter the group name and size.
7. Choose your experience level with beautification projects.
8. Select the tools you can bring.
9. Choose your T-shirt size for volunteer purposes.
10. Enter your emergency contact name and phone number.
11. Read and agree to the volunteer waiver and release.
12. Finally, add any additional comments or information about yourself.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information**: This section requires your unique registration ID, registration date, and full name.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you are registering for the project.
* **Registration ID**: This is a unique reference number assigned to you for registration purposes.
* **Volunteer Information**: This section collects your contact details, including your phone number, email address, and street address.
* **Volunteer Information**: Enter your contact details, including phone number, email, and address.
* **Age Group**: Choose which age group you belong to (Under 18, 18-24, 25-34, 35-44, 45-54, 55-64, or 65+).
* **Project Preferences**: Select the types of beautification projects that interest you.
* **Project Types**: Choose which beautification activities you are interested in (e.g., Tree Planting, Flower Bed Maintenance, Graffiti Removal).
* **Preferred Areas**: If you have a preference for areas you want to help improve, enter them here.
* **Availability**: Select the dates and times you are available to volunteer.
* **Event Dates**: Choose which event dates you can attend.
* **Skills and Interests**: Select any skills or interests you have that are relevant to the project.
* **Group Participation**: If you are registering as part of a group, select Yes and enter the group name and size.
* **Group Name**: Enter the name of your group if you are registering as part of one.
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your group if you are registering as part of one.
* **Experience Level**: Choose your experience level with beautification projects (Experienced, Some Experience, Beginner, No Experience).
* **Tools Available**: Select the tools you can bring to the project.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Choose your T-shirt size for volunteer purposes.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Waiver Agreement**: Read and agree to the volunteer waiver and release.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about yourself.
