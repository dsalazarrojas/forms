# Academic Success Workshop Series Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Academic Success Workshop Series, a series of workshops designed to enhance student academic performance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the registration form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form header to understand what the workshop series is about.
2. Fill in your **First name**, **Last name**, and **Email address**.
3. Provide your **Student ID number**, as it is essential for processing your registration.
4. Choose your **Academic level** (Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate).
5. Select the **major or program of study**.
6. Choose the **workshops** you would like to attend (select all that apply).
7. Select your **preferred workshop schedule** (weekday mornings, weekdays afternoons, weekdays evenings, or Saturday mornings).
8. Choose your preferred **format** (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
9. If you have a **referral source**, you can select how you heard about the workshop series (e.g., academic advisor, professor, student email).
10. If you have **learning goals**, you can provide them in the text box.
11. If you require any **accessibility accommodations**, you can describe them in the text box.
12. Finally, confirm that you are committed to attending the workshops you selected and acknowledge that space is limited.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Your first name, as you want to be addressed.
* **Last name**: Your last name, as you want to be addressed.
* **Email address**: Enter your official university email address for communication and confirmation purposes.
* **Student ID number**: Provide your student ID number for accurate registration and attendance records.
* **Academic level**: Choose your current academic level (e.g., Freshman, Sophomore, Junior, Senior, or Graduate).
* **Major or program of study**: Select your major or program of study.
* **Workshops**: Select all the workshops you would like to attend. The options are:
	+ Time Management and Study Skills
	+ Effective Note-Taking Strategies
	+ Test Preparation and Anxiety Management
	+ Academic Writing Essentials
	+ Research Methods and Resources
	+ Career Planning and Goal Setting
* **Preferred workshop schedule**: Choose your preferred workshop schedule.
* **Preferred format**: Select your preferred format for attending the workshops (in-person, virtual, or hybrid).
* **Referral source**: If you have a referral source, select how you heard about the workshop series.
* **Learning goals**: If you have specific learning goals, describe them in the text box.
* **Accessibility accommodations**: If you require any accessibility accommodations, describe them in the text box.
* **Agreement**: Confirm that you are committed to attending the workshops you selected and understand that space is limited.
