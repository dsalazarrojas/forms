# Cookie and Tea Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from guests attending the Cookie and Tea Party event. It includes fields for personal details, event details, preferences, and additional information to help plan and organize the party efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Your Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
	* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
	* **Phone Number**: Enter your best contact number.
2. Provide event details:
	* **Party Date**: Enter the date of the event.
	* **Party Time**: Enter the time of the event.
	* **Party Location**: Enter the location of the event.
3. Select your preferences:
	* **Will you attend**: Choose whether you will attend the party.
	* **Tea Preference**: Select your preferred type of tea.
	* **Cookie Preference**: Select your preferred type of cookie.
	* **Dietary Requirements**: Select any special dietary needs.
4. Additional information:
	* **Number in Your Party**: Enter the total number of people attending with you.
	* **Names of Other Guests**: List the names of any other guests attending with you.
	* **Number of Children**: If applicable, enter the number of children attending.
	* **Ages of Children**: If applicable, list the ages of the children.
5. Optional fields:
	* **Will you bring your own apron**: If you plan to participate in activities, select whether you will bring your own apron or if you need one to be provided.
	* **Seating Preference**: Select your preferred seating arrangement (indoor or outdoor).
	* **Table Assignment Request**: If you have a preference for who to sit with, enter their name.
