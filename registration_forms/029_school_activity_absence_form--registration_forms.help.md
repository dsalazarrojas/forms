# School Activity Absence Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Activity Absence Form is used by parents to report the absence of their child from a school activity. This form provides a way for parents to communicate their child's absence to the school administration, which helps the school keep track of attendance and ensures that all students are accounted for during events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information about your child's absence.
2. Enter your child's name in the `Student Name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `Parent Email` field.
4. Provide a brief explanation for the reason of your child's absence in the `Reason` field.
5. Specify the date and time of the absence in the `Date Of Absence` and `Time Of Absence` fields, respectively.
6. Indicate the name of the activity your child was absent from in the `Activity Name` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, optional): Enter your child's name as it appears on their school records.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so the school can contact you about your child's absence.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): Briefly explain why your child was absent from the activity.
* **Date Of Absence** (`date_of_absence`, date, optional): Enter the date of the absence in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time Of Absence** (`time_of_absence`, time, optional): Enter the time of the absence in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Activity Name** (`activity_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the activity your child was absent from.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that the school administration has the correct information.
* If your child is absent from multiple activities, you will need to fill out this form multiple times.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the school administration for assistance.
