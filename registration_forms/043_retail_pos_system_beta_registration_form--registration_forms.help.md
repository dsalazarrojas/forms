# retail_pos_system_beta_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register a business with the retail point-of-sale system. Please fill out this form to provide essential information about your business.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to check your answers for accuracy and completeness.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter business name**: This is the name of your business. It should match the official name you use for your business. (<type:text, required: false>)
* **Contact person**: This is the name of the person who will be the primary contact for your business. This can be a real person or a generic contact name. (<type:text, required: false>)
* **Enter email**: This is the email address associated with your business. Please use a valid email address. (<type:email, required: false>)
* **Enter phone**: This is the phone number associated with your business. Please use a valid phone number. (<type:text, required: false>)
* **Business type**: Select the type of business you are registering: Retail Business, Restaurant, or Other. (<type:select_one, required: false>)
* **Enter location**: This is the physical location of your business. Please provide a valid address. (<type:text, required: false>)
* **Start date**: This is the date when you started your business. If you're unsure, use the start of your fiscal year or a date when you opened for business. (<type:date, required: false>)
* **End date**: This is the date when your business will stop operating. Please note that this field is not necessarily for closure; it might be useful for system maintenance or future planning. (<type:date, required: false>)
* **Additional information**: This field is for any additional comments or notes about your business that may be helpful for system administrators or other users. (<type:note, required: false>)
* **Consent**: Please indicate if you give consent for the collection and use of your business data. (<type:select_one, required: false>)
