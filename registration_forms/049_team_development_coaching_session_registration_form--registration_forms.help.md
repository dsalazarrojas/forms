# Team Development Coaching Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Development Coaching Session Registration Form is designed to collect information from attendees who wish to participate in a team development coaching session. This form helps us to understand your team's details, contact information, and coaching session preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your registration information in the "Registration Information" page.
2. Provide your team details in the "Team Details" page.
3. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" page.
4. Specify the session details and any additional information you want to share with us in the "Session Details" and "Additional Information" pages.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, `text`, not required): This is the first step to complete the form. Please enter your name and any other details you would like to share with us.
* **Team Details** (`Team Details`, `text`, not required): Here, you can provide information about your team, including its name and members.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, not required): Please enter your contact details, including your email address and phone number.
* **Session Details** (`Session Details`, `text`, not required): Describe the type of coaching session you are interested in, including any specific topics or areas of focus you would like to explore.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, not required): If there is any additional information you would like to share with us, please enter it here.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, `text`, not required): Once you have filled out all the pages, review and submit your form.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and complete while filling out the form to ensure we have all the necessary information to plan and manage the coaching session effectively.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at <form@gic.mx>.
