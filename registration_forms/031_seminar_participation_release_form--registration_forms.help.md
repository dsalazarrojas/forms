# Seminar Participation Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from seminar participants. It is used to obtain their consent and participation details for the seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant information, including name and email.
2. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Indicate your seminar participation, whether you have attended or plan to attend.
4. Choose whether you consent to participate or not.
5. Select the type of seminar you are attending.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the seminar.
7. Select the start and end times of the seminar.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information**: Enter your basic participant information.
* **Name**: Enter your name for reference purposes.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Seminar Participation**: Indicate whether you have attended or plan to attend the seminar.
* **Consent**: Choose whether you consent to participate or not.
* **Seminar Type**: Select the type of seminar you are attending.
* **Start Date**: Choose the start date of the seminar.
* **Start Time**: Choose the start time of the seminar.
* **End Date**: Choose the end date of the seminar.
* **End Time**: Choose the end time of the seminar.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled in accurately.
* Verify that your email and phone number are correct.
* Double-check the seminar dates and times.
