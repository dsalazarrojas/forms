# vocational_rehabilitation_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vocational rehabilitation intake form is used to collect information from individuals seeking vocational rehabilitation services. This form helps the vocational rehabilitation staff to assess the applicant's background, skills, and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Enter your full name in the "applicant_name" field.
3. Provide your contact email in the "contact_email" field.
4. Enter your contact phone number, city, state, and zip code in the "contact_phone", "contact_city", "contact_state", and "contact_zip" fields.
5. Indicate your work status, job title, and work type in the "work_status", "job_title", and "work_type" fields.
6. If you don't see a pre-defined option for your work status or type, enter your own in the "other_work_status" or "other_work_type" fields.
7. Enter your employer's name and contact information (work_phone, work_email, work_address) in the "employer" fields.
8. Enter any other information about yourself that is not listed above, including your primary language, native language, highest level of education completed, highest level of education enrolled, years of work experience, hours of work per week, and any other relevant details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **applicant_name**: Enter your full name.
* **contact_email**: Enter your contact email.
* **contact_phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **contact_city**: Enter your city.
* **contact_state**: Enter your state or province.
* **contact_zip**: Enter your zip code or postal code.
* **applicant_type**: Indicate your type of applicant (e.g., disability, veteran, etc.).
* **other_applicant_type**: Enter any other type of applicant you identify with (if not listed in the previous field).
* **primary_language**: Enter your primary language.
* **native_language**: Enter your native language.
* **highest_level_of_edu_completed**: Enter the highest level of education you have completed.
* **highest_level_of_edu_enrolled**: Enter the highest level of education you are currently enrolled in.
* **years_of_work_experience**: Enter the number of years you have worked.
* **hours_of_work_per_week**: Enter the number of hours you work per week.
* **work_status**: Indicate your current work status (e.g., employed, unemployed, etc.).
* **other_work_status**: Enter any other work status you identify with (if not listed in the previous field).
* **job_title**: Enter your job title.
* **work_type**: Indicate your type of work (e.g., full-time, part-time, etc.).
* **other_work_type**: Enter any other type of work you identify with (if not listed in the previous field).
* **employer**: Enter your employer's name.
* **work_phone**: Enter your employer's phone number.
* **work_email**: Enter your employer's email.
* **work_address**: Enter your employer's address.

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure that your application is considered for vocational rehabilitation services.
