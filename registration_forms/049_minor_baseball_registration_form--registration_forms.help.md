# Minor Baseball Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Minor Baseball Registration Form is a tool used to collect vital information from minor baseball players to ensure their safety and successful participation in the league.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read this guide carefully before starting the form.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Submit the form to the designated authorities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Information
* **Player Information** (`player_info`, `text`, required: false): This field is used to collect personal details of the minor baseball player. Please provide the player's name and any other relevant information.
### Contact Information
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Provide the contact information of the person responsible for the player's care, such as a parent or guardian.
### Team Information
* **Team Information** (`team_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter the team name and any other relevant details about the player's team affiliation.
### Emergency Contact
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): In case of an emergency, provide the contact information of a person who can be reached out to provide assistance.
### Parent Information
* **Parent Information** (`player_parent`, `text`, required: false): This field is intended for the parent or guardian to provide their contact information for the player.

## Tips
- Ensure the player's information is accurate and up-to-date.
- Double-check the form before submission.
- Ask for help if you are unsure about any field or requirement.
