# Cultural Program Subscription Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users interested in registering for a cultural program. It should only be used once by each user to prevent duplicate registrations.


## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions carefully before starting the form.
- Fill out the form in a logical order, following the sequence of fields.
- Review your answers before submitting to ensure they are accurate.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID**: (`form_id`, number, optional)
    The unique identifier for this cultural program registration. This will help us track your registration.
* **Category**: (`category`, select_one, optional)
    Please select the type of form you are registering for (Registration Forms, Event Form, or Subscription Form).
* **Description**: (`description`, text, optional)
    Briefly describe your reasons for wanting to register for this cultural program.
* **Email**: (`email`, email, optional)
    Enter your valid email address for us to contact you.
* **Phone**: (`phone`, text, optional)
    Provide your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Note**: (`note`, note, optional)
    Leave any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Form IDs**: (`form_ids`, number, optional)
    If applicable, provide the IDs of any other forms you have registered for.
* **Output File**: (`output_file`, text, optional)
    This is where you will be able to access your registration receipt.
* **Assigned Tool**: (`assigned_tool`, text, optional)
    This field is currently unused; you can ignore it.
* **User Input 10**: (`user_input_10`, text, optional)
    Another unused field; feel free to ignore it.


# Tips

* Take your time to ensure your answers are accurate and complete.
* Review your form before submitting to catch any errors.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact us for assistance.
