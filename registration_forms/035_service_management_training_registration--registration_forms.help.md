# Service Management Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Management Training Registration form is used to collect information about participants who are interested in attending service management training sessions. This form helps to gather their preferences and details to ensure they are properly registered and receive relevant training information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant details section.
2. Select your preferred dates for the training session.
3. Indicate your consent to participate in the training.
4. Enter any additional details about your training participation (optional).
5. Enter any additional details about your reported issues (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (page 1)**: Enter your name, job title, department, and other relevant details.
* **Preferred Dates (page 2)**: Select the days of the week you are available for training. You can choose multiple dates.
* **Consent (page 3)**: Confirm your willingness to participate in the training session by selecting "Yes".
* **Additional Details (page 4)**: Enter any additional details about your training participation, such as any relevant skills or experience.
* **Additional Details (page 5)**: Enter any additional details about your reported issues, such as any specific challenges or problems you are facing.
* **Reported Issues (page 6)**: Enter any issues or problems you have reported in the past (optional).
