# Musical Audition Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from students and their parents to facilitate the musical audition process at the school.

## How To Complete This Form
- Make sure you have all the necessary information before filling out the form.
- Fill out the form accurately and completely.
- Ensure that you include the student's and parent's details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their identification.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address for communication purposes.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the parent's name who is associated with the student.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Student Address**: Enter the student's physical address for delivery purposes.

## Tips
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the school administration for clarification.
- Make sure to review the form carefully before submitting it.
- Double-check that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
