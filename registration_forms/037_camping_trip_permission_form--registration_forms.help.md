# Camping Trip Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by parents or guardians to register their child for a camping trip, ensuring that the necessary information is collected for the student's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Enter the student's first name, last name, date of birth, age, grade or class, and parent/guardian information.
3. Specify the trip details, including trip name, dates, duration, destination, and estimated cost.
4. List any planned activities for the trip.
5. Provide medical and safety information, including known allergies or medical conditions and current medications.
6. Enter emergency contact information (if different from the parent/guardian above).
7. Give permission for the child to participate in the camping trip.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`student_first_name`, text, required): Enter the student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`, text, required): Enter the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`student_date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age** (`student_age`, number, required): Enter the student's age.
* **Grade or Class** (`student_grade`, text, required): Enter the student's grade or class.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Phone Number** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Trip Name or Location** (`trip_name`, text, required): Enter the trip name or location.
* **Trip Dates** (`trip_dates`, text, required): Enter the trip dates.
* **Duration of Trip** (`trip_duration`, number, required): Enter the trip duration in days.
* **Destination or Campground Name** (`trip_destination`, text, required): Enter the trip destination or campground name.
* **Estimated Trip Cost** (`trip_cost`, number, optional): Enter the estimated trip cost (if known).
* **Planned Activities** (`planned_activities`, text, optional): List any planned activities for the trip.
* **Known Allergies or Medical Conditions** (`allergies`, text, optional): List any known allergies or medical conditions.
* **Current Medications** (`medications`, text, optional): List any current medications.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the emergency contact name and phone number (if different from the parent/guardian above).
* **I Give Permission for My Child to Participate** (`permission_granted`, select one, required): Select "Yes, I give permission" to confirm your consent.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure a safe and enjoyable trip for the child.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the camp organizers for assistance.
* You can also use the "emergency contact" field to provide alternate contact information if different from the parent/guardian above.
