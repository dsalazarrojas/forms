# tropical_event_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "tropical_event_registration" form is designed to collect user registration information for a tropical event. It allows users to select their event preferences, provide contact information, and indicate their consent for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred options for the event using the "event_preferences" field on Page 1.
2. Fill in your name in the "attendee_name" field on Page 2.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field on Page 2.
4. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field on Page 2.
5. Select your preferred options for "event_preferences_2" on Page 2.
6. Indicate your consent by selecting "Option 1" or "Option 2" in the "consent" field on Page 3.
7. Add any additional notes about your registration in the "notes" field on Page 3.
8. Select your attendee preferences using the "attendee_preferences" field on Page 3.
9. Confirm your selections on the final page to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your preferred options for the event using the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
* **attendee_name** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your name in this field. This field is optional.
* **email** (`email`, required/optional): Enter your email address in this format: `example@example.com`. This field is optional.
* **phone** (`text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number without any special characters. This field is optional.
* **event_preferences** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select one option from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
* **consent** (`select_one`, required/optional): Select "Option 1" or "Option 2" to indicate your consent. This field is optional.
* **notes** (`note`, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your registration in this text area. This field is optional.
* **attendee_preferences** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.
* **event_preferences_2** (`select_multiple`, required/optional): Select one or more options from the dropdown menu. This field is optional.

## Tips
* Please fill in all relevant information for accurate and efficient processing.
* Ensure your email address is in the correct format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
* Confirm your selections on the final page before submitting your registration.
