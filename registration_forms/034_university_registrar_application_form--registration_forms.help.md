# University Registrar Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Registrar Application Form is a document used by students or individuals to provide necessary information to the university registrar's office. This form helps the registrar's office to keep track of student records and ensure smooth registration and admission processes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form accurately and honestly.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed.
3. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
4. If a field is unclear or confusing, refer to the field-by-field explanation below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): This field asks for your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): This field requests your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address for future communication.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Please provide your date of birth in the correct format.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, number, required/optional): Enter your unique student ID, if applicable.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, required/optional): Provide the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact phone number for future communication.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your current address for registration purposes.
* **Program enrolled** (`program_enrolled`, select_one, required/optional): Select the program you are applying for from the provided options.
* **Course interest** (`course_interest`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose the courses you are interested in, if applicable.
* **Upload documents** (`upload_documents`, note, required/optional): Attach any supporting documents or additional information required for registration.
