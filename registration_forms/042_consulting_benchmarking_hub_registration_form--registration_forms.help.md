# Consulting Benchmarking Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Consulting Benchmarking Hub Registration Form is a user-friendly form designed to collect essential information from organizations interested in participating in the benchmarking process. This form is intended to gather details about your organization, which will help facilitate a smooth and successful benchmarking experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing your organization's name in the **Organization Name** field.
2. Enter the name of the point of contact person for benchmarking in the **Point of Contact** field.
3. Enter your organization's contact email address in the **Contact Email** field, which will be used for program communications.
4. If your organization has a specific **Industry Sector** or specialization, select the corresponding option from the list.
5. If applicable, choose the **Firm Size Range** that best describes your organization's size.
6. Finally, describe your **Benchmarking Goals**, which will help us understand your expectations from the benchmarking process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name**: Please enter your organization's name as it is recognized in the industry.
* **Point of Contact**: Provide the name of the person responsible for coordinating with our team.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address that we can use to communicate with your organization.
* **Industry Sector**: Select the industry or specialization that best describes your organization's focus area.
* **Firm Size Range**: Choose the range that best describes the number of employees in your organization.
* **Benchmarking Goals**: Describe your expectations from the benchmarking process, so we can tailor the experience to your needs.

## Tips

* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any questions or need assistance with the benchmarking process, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
* If you need help with form modifications or have any suggestions, please don't hesitate to contact us as well.
