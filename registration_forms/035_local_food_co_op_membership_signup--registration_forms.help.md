# local_food_co_op_membership_signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new members for the local food co-op. It collects necessary information for membership activation and ensures that users understand their membership preferences and details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your User Information (Page 1).
2. Select your Membership Preferences (Page 2).
3. Provide your Payment Info (Page 3).
4. Confirm your information (Page 4).
5. Optionally, provide Membership Info and Additional Info (Pages 5-6).
6. Confirm your Email (Page 7).
7. Optionally, customize the Email Subject and Body (Pages 8-9).
8. Submit your registration (Page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### User Information (Page 1)
* **User Information**: This is the first step in the registration process. It asks for general user information such as name and contact details. Fill in your name and contact information accurately.

### Membership Preferences (Page 2)
* **Membership Preferences**: This field allows you to choose your preferred membership options. Please select your membership preferences from the options provided.

### Payment Info (Page 3)
* **Payment Info**: This field requires information about your payment method. Fill in your payment information to complete your registration.

### Confirm (Page 4)
* **Confirm**: This is a confirmation page that asks you to review your information before submission. Carefully review your information and ensure it is accurate.

### Membership Info (Page 5)
* **Membership Info** (Optional): This field is optional and allows you to provide additional membership details. If you have any specific membership requirements or preferences, please fill this in.

### Additional Info (Page 6)
* **Additional Info** (Optional): This field is optional and allows you to provide any other relevant information. If you have any additional information that is not captured elsewhere, please fill this in.

### Confirm Info (Page 7)
* **Confirm Info** (Optional): This field is to confirm your additional information. If you have filled out Additional Info, confirm that the information is accurate.

### Submit (Page 8)
* **Submit**: This is the final step in the registration process. Carefully review your information one last time before submitting.

### Email Confirmation (Page 9)
* **Email Confirmation**: This field requires confirmation of your email address to verify your identity.

### Confirm Email (Page 10)
* **Confirm Email**: This field asks you to confirm that the email address you provided is accurate. Select 'True' if the email is correct or 'False' if it is not.

### Email Subject (Page 11)
* **Email Subject**: This field is optional and allows you to customize the subject of the email sent to you after registration.

### Email Body (Page 12)
* **Email Body**: This field is optional and allows you to customize the body of the email sent to you after registration.

Note: Please ensure that all the information provided is accurate to avoid any issues with your membership activation.
