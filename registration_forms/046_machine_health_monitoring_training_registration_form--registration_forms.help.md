# Machine Health Monitoring Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register your interest in attending the Machine Health Monitoring Training. Please fill out the form with your details to ensure you get a spot in the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Registration Form section with your name, email, phone number, and preferred date and time for the training session.
2. In the Select One field, choose one option from the list provided.
3. In the Select Multiple field, choose multiple options from the list provided.
4. Add any additional notes you would like to share with the training team in the Note field.
5. Finally, click the Submit button to complete and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the main section where you can provide your name, email, phone number, and preferred date and time for the training session.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you about the training session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the format XXX-XXX-XXXX.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the training session. You can select a date from the calendar or enter it manually in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time for the training session. You can select a time from the clock or enter it manually in the format HH:MM.
* **Note** (`note`, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes you would like to share with the training team.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, required/optional): Choose one option from the list provided.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the list provided.
* **Select One Integer** (`select_one_integer`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Select Multiple Integer** (`select_multiple_integer`, select_one, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the list provided.
* **Select One Decimal** (`select_one_decimal`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Select Multiple Decimal** (`select_multiple_decimal`, select_one, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the list provided.
* **Select One Date** (`select_one_date`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Select Multiple Date** (`select_multiple_date`, select_one, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the list provided.
* **Select One Time** (`select_one_time`, text, required/optional): This field is not currently used in the form.
* **Select Multiple Time** (`select_multiple_time`, select_one, required/optional): Choose multiple options from the list provided.
* **Submit** (`submit_button`, text, required/optional): Click this button to submit the form.
