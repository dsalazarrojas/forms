# Finger Food Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you complete the Finger Food Event Registration form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the event you are registering for in the "Event Details" section.
2. Provide your basic contact information in the "Attendee Info" section.
3. Indicate your attendance status in the "RSVP" section.
4. Specify any dietary preferences in the "Dietary Preferences" section.
5. Add any additional comments or information in the "Note" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (Select One): Choose the event you are registering for.
	+ This field is used to select the event you wish to attend.
* **First Name** (Text): Enter your first name.
	+ This field is used to collect your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text): Enter your last name.
	+ This field is used to collect your last name.
* **Email** (Text): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is used to collect your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
	+ This field is used to collect your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Yes, I will attend** (Select One): Indicate that you will attend the event.
	+ This field is used to confirm your attendance status.
* **No, I won't attend** (Select One): Indicate that you won't attend the event.
	+ This field is used to confirm that you won't attend the event.
* **Maybe** (Select One): Indicate that your attendance status is uncertain.
	+ This field is used to indicate that you are unsure about your attendance.
* **Gluten Free** (Select Multiple): Select this option if you have gluten intolerance.
	+ This field is used to specify dietary preferences.
* **Lactose Free** (Select Multiple): Select this option if you have lactose intolerance.
	+ This field is used to specify dietary preferences.
* **Vegan** (Select Multiple): Select this option if you are vegan.
	+ This field is used to specify dietary preferences.
* **Other - specify** (Text): Enter any other dietary preference or restriction.
	+ This field is used to specify any other dietary restriction or preference.
* **Note** (Text): Add any additional comments or information.
	+ This field is used to add any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields correctly.
* If you have any special dietary needs, please select the corresponding options in the "Dietary Preferences" section.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please add them in the "Note" section.
* If you need any assistance or have questions, please contact us at [Contact Email].
