# Pet Grooming Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Grooming Registration Form is designed to collect information about your pet's details, preferences, and requirements for grooming services. Please provide accurate and complete information to help us better serve your pet's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling in the Pet Information section with your pet's details, including name, age, breed, and species.
2. In the Owner Information section, provide your contact information, such as name, phone number, and email.
3. The Appointment section is where you can schedule a date and time for your pet's grooming appointment.
4. The Contact Info field is where you can add any additional contact details or instructions for our staff.
5. The Pet Health section is where you can describe any health concerns or issues with your pet.
6. In the Services section, select all the services you'd like us to perform on your pet, such as nail trimming, grooming, or bathing.
7. If you'd like to add any additional services not listed, you can select them in the Additional Services section.
8. Finally, in the Payment Info field, provide any payment instructions or specifics, such as credit card details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Pet Information** (`Pet Information`, Text, Optional): Enter your pet's name, age, breed, and species to help us provide personalized services.
- **Owner Information** (`Owner Information`, Text, Optional): Provide your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email, so we can get in touch with you for any updates or concerns.
- **Appointment** (`Appointment`, Text, Optional): Select a date and time for your pet's grooming appointment.
- **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, Text, Optional): Add any additional contact information or instructions for our staff, such as parking or elevator access instructions.
- **Pet Health** (`Pet Health`, Text, Optional): Describe any health concerns or issues with your pet, so we can take extra care during the grooming process.
- **Services** (`Services`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select all the services you'd like us to perform on your pet, such as nail trimming, grooming, or bathing.
- **Additional Services** (`Additional Services`, Select Multiple, Optional): Select any additional services not listed, such as nail polish or conditioning.
- **Payment Info** (`Payment Info`, Text, Optional): Provide any payment instructions or specifics, such as credit card details.
