# Activity Waiver And Release Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Activity Waiver And Release Form is a document that requires participants to acknowledge and understand the risks associated with a particular activity and release liability for any injuries or medical conditions that may arise during or after the activity.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form in a clear and legible handwriting.
2. Ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
3. Please review each field carefully before signing the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (Participant Date of Birth, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Please enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (Emergency Contact Relationship, text, required): Please describe your relationship with the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Name of Activity** (Activity Name, text, required): Please enter the official name of the activity being participated in.
* **Activity Date** (Activity Date, date, required): Please enter the date when the activity will take place.
* **Acknowledge Risks** (Risks Acknowledgment, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I acknowledge and accept the risks" to confirm that you understand the risks associated with the activity.
* **Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions, select_one, required): If you have any medical conditions, please select "Yes" to indicate that you have a medical condition that may affect your participation.
* **Details of Medical Conditions** (Medical Conditions Details, text, optional): If you selected "Yes" in the previous field, please provide details of your medical conditions.
* **Release of Liability** (Release of Liability, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I release liability" to confirm that you release the organizers from liability for any injuries or medical conditions that may arise during or after the activity.
* **Photo and Video Consent** (Photo and Video Consent, select_one, required): Please select "Yes, I consent" to confirm that you consent to being photographed or recorded during the activity.
* **Signature Date** (Signature Date, date, required): Please date your signature to confirm your acknowledgment and consent to the terms and conditions of the activity.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the organizers of the activity.
* Please keep a copy of this form for your records.
