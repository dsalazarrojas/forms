# green_building_standards_seminar_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The green_building_standards_seminar_registration_form is designed to collect registration information from participants attending the Green Building Standards Seminar.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information before starting.
2. Fill in the required fields accurately and completely.
3. Double-check your answers for correctness and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name, title, and any other relevant details as a participant.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required: false): Provide your contact details, including email and phone number, so we can reach you with updates and instructions.
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, text, required: false): Describe your registration reason, such as the seminar topic or speaker of interest.
* **Dietary Information** (`dietary_info`, text, required: false): If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please specify them here.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, required: false): If you have any special requirements or requests, such as wheelchair accessibility, describe them here.
* **Emergency Contacts** (`emergency_contacts`, text, required: false): Provide the names and contact information of your emergency contacts in case we need to reach them.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required: false): Enter the seminar fee and any other payment-related information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required: true): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options: Credit card, Bank transfer, PayPal, or Apple Pay.
* **Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required: false): If you selected a payment method that requires an amount, enter the exact amount here.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, number, required: false): Enter the fee for the seminar.
