# video_consultation_appointment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for scheduling a video consultation appointment with a service provider. It collects essential information from both the client and the service provider, ensuring a smooth and effective appointment process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your user information on page 1, including any relevant details.
2. Choose a preferred appointment time on page 2.
3. Select a preferred appointment date on page 2.
4. Provide appointment details on page 3.
5. Enter your contact information (phone and email) on page 3.
6. Confirm your preferred language on page 4.
7. Confirm the service provider's language on page 4.
8. Choose the appointment duration on page 4.
9. Add any client notes on page 4.
10. Enter additional service provider information on page 5.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and any other relevant details.
* **Preferred Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Select a time for your appointment.
* **Preferred Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select a date for your appointment.
* **Appointment Details** (`appointment_details`, text, required): Describe the reason for your appointment and any necessary details.
* **Client Phone** (`client_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Client Email** (`client_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select one, optional): Confirm your preferred language (Yes or No).
* **Service Provider Language** (`service_provider_language`, select one, optional): Confirm the service provider's language (Yes or No).
* **Appointment Duration** (`appointment_duration`, select one, optional): Choose the duration of your appointment (15, 30, or 60 minutes).
* **Client Note** (`client_note`, note, required): Add any additional comments or notes about your appointment.
* **Service Provider Info 1** (`service_provider_info`, text, required): Enter the service provider's name and contact information.
* **Service Provider Info 2** (`service_provider_info2`, text, required): Enter additional information about the service provider.
* **Service Provider Info 3** (`service_provider_info3`, text, required): Enter more details about the service provider.
* **Service Provider Info 4** (`service_provider_info4`, text, required): Enter final information about the service provider.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your preferred appointment time and date to ensure they fit your schedule.
* Be clear and concise in your appointment details and client notes.
* Confirm your preferred language and the service provider's language to avoid misunderstandings.
* Choose the correct appointment duration for your needs.
* Review all entered information before submission to ensure accuracy.
