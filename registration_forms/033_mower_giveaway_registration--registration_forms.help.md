# Mower Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register their entry into a mower giveaway contest. The form is designed to collect contact information and confirm that users have read and understood the rules. The form is intended for users who wish to participate in the contest and have a valid entry.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose your entry option from the "Enter to Win" select list.
2. Select your contact information from the available options.
3. Confirm that you have read and understood the rules by selecting the corresponding option.
4. If you have a valid government-issued ID, select "True". Otherwise, select "False".
5. Enter your email address.
6. Enter your phone number.
7. Enter your zip code.
8. Add any additional notes if necessary.
9. Confirm your email address.
10. Confirm your phone number.
11. Confirm your zip code.
12. Confirm your additional notes.
13. Enter the date of the contest.
14. Enter the time of the contest.
15. Confirm that you are the owner of the equipment.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter to Win (Select Multiple)**
	+ This field allows you to select how you would like to enter the contest. Choose the option that best describes your entry method.
* **Contact Info (Select One)**
	+ This field allows you to select your contact information from the available options.
* **Rules (Select Multiple)**
	+ This field confirms that you have read and understood the rules of the contest. Select the corresponding option to confirm.
* **I am 18 or Older (Number)**
	+ This field confirms that you are 18 or older. Select "1" to confirm.
* **I have a Valid Government-Issued ID (Select One)**
	+ This field confirms that you have a valid government-issued ID. Select "True" if you have one, "False" otherwise.
* **Email (Email)**
	+ This field is for entering your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**
	+ This field is for entering your phone number.
* **Zip (Number)**
	+ This field is for entering your zip code.
* **Notes (Note)**
	+ This field is for adding any additional notes if necessary.
* **Confirm Email (Select One)**
	+ This field confirms that you have entered the correct email address. Select "True" if correct, "False" otherwise.
* **Confirm Phone (Select One)**
	+ This field confirms that you have entered the correct phone number. Select "True" if correct, "False" otherwise.
* **Confirm Zip (Select One)**
	+ This field confirms that you have entered the correct zip code. Select "True" if correct, "False" otherwise.
* **Confirm Notes (Select One)**
	+ This field confirms that you have entered the correct notes. Select "True" if correct, "False" otherwise.
* **Date (Date)**
	+ This field is for entering the date of the contest.
* **Time (Time)**
	+ This field is for entering the time of the contest.
* **I am the Owner of the Equipment (Select One)**
	+ This field confirms that you are the owner of the equipment. Select "True" if correct, "False" otherwise.
