# Machine Learning In Production Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect essential information from participants regarding a machine learning training event. This includes participant details, payment information, and course specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the participant's details, such as name and email.
2. Select the experience level of the participant.
3. Choose whether or not the participant has made payments.
4. Enter the name of the course the participant is taking.
5. Provide the details of the training provider, including experience.
6. Specify the course length and start and end dates.
7. Enter the payment method and date.
8. Specify the total number of participants.
9. Confirm whether or not payment has been received.
10. Enter any additional notes.
11. Select the payment status.
12. Enter additional notes for the training provider.
13. Enter any additional payment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`): This is the first section of the form where you will provide your basic information.
  - **Name** (`name`): Enter your name in this field.
  - **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address in this field.
  - **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): You may optionally enter your phone number.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`): Choose your level of experience with machine learning from the options provided.
* **Payments** (`payments`): Select whether you have made payments or not.
* **Course Title** (`course`): Enter the title of the course you are taking.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`): Enter the name of the training provider.
* **Training Provider Experience** (`training_provider_experience`): Enter the experience of the training provider.
* **Course Length** (`course_length`): Enter the length of the course.
* **Course Start Date** (`course_start_date`): Enter the start date of the course.
* **Course End Date** (`course_end_date`): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Course Status** (`course_status`): Enter the current status of the course.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Enter the payment method used.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`): Enter the date of payment.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`): Enter the total amount paid.
* **Total Participants** (`total_participants`): Enter the total number of participants.
* **Payment Received** (`payment_received`): Confirm whether or not payment has been received.
* **Notes** (`notes`): You may enter any additional notes.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`): Select the current payment status from the options provided.
* **Additional Notes for Training Provider** (`training_provider_notes`): You may enter any additional notes for the training provider.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`): You may enter any payment details.
* **Payment Details for Training Provider** (`training_provider_payment_details`): You may enter any additional payment details for the training provider.
* **Terms & Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`): Accept the terms and conditions of the training event.
