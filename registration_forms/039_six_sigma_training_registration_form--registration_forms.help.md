# six_sigma_training_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The six_sigma_training_registration_form is designed to collect relevant information from participants in a training program, including name, contact details, training preferences, and agreements.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill in the required fields, including:
- Participant Name
- Email
- Participant Agreement (I agree to the terms and conditions)
- Assigned Tool
You can also fill in the optional fields as needed, which include:
- Participant Company
- Participant Job Title
- Other (please specify)
- Training Location
- Training Date
- Training Time
- Training Duration
- Participant Notes
- Participant Signature
- Participant Agreement Date
- Participant Agreement Time
- Participant Agreement IP
- Participant Agreement IP Comments

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as you would like to be referred to during the training session.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.

### Training Location
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, `select_one`, required): Select the location of the training session from the options provided.

### Participant Company
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, `text`, required): Enter your company name.

### Participant Job Title
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title.

### Other (please specify)
* **Other (please specify)** (`participant_job_title_other`, `text`, required): If your job title is not listed, enter a brief description of your job title.

### Training Date
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of the training session.

### Training Time
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the start time of the training session.

### Training Duration
* **Training Duration** (`training_duration`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the duration of the training session from the options provided.

### Participant Notes
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required): Enter any additional notes about your participation in the training session.

### Participant Agreement
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the terms and conditions.

### Participant Agreement Other
* **Participant Agreement Other** (`participant_agreement_other`, `text`, required): If you selected "Other", enter a brief description of your agreement.

### Assigned Tool
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool you have been assigned to use during the training session.

### Participant Signature
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, `text`, required): Enter your digital signature.

### Participant Agreement Date
* **Participant Agreement Date** (`participant_agreement_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your agreement.

### Participant Agreement Time
* **Participant Agreement Time** (`participant_agreement_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your agreement.

### Participant Agreement IP
* **Participant Agreement IP** (`participant_agreement_ip`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you agree to the IP terms and conditions.

### Participant Agreement IP Comments
* **Participant Agreement IP Comments** (`participant_agreement_ip_comments`, `text`, required): If you selected "I do not agree", enter a brief explanation of your reason for not agreeing.

### Participant Agreement IP Date
* **Participant Agreement IP Date** (`participant_agreement_ip_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date of your agreement to the IP terms and conditions.

### Participant Agreement IP Time
* **Participant Agreement IP Time** (`participant_agreement_ip_time`, `time`, optional): Enter the time of your agreement to the IP terms and conditions.

### Participant Agreement IP IP
* **Participant Agreement IP IP** (`participant_agreement_ip_ip`, `text`, optional): Enter your IP address.

### Participant Agreement IP IP Other
* **Participant Agreement IP IP Other** (`participant_agreement_ip_ip_other`, `text`, optional): If you have a different IP address, enter a brief description of it.
