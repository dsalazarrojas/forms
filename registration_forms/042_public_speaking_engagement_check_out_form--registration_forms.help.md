# Public Speaking Engagement Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Public Speaking Engagement Check Out Form is designed to collect essential details about public speaking engagements. This form helps ensure that all necessary information is captured to facilitate a smooth and successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the speaker's name in the "Speaker Details" field.
2. Provide a brief description of the presentation topic in the "Presentation Topic" field.
3. Specify the duration of the presentation in the "Duration" field.
4. Enter the speaker's contact phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the speaker's email address in the "Email" field.
6. Add any additional notes about the speaker in the "Notes" field.
7. Select the types of files to be submitted by the speaker in the "Files" field.
8. Choose the AV requirements for the presentation in the "AV Requirements" field.
9. Indicate the sponsorship arrangement in the "Sponsor" field.
10. Provide any additional information about the speaker in the "Additional Information" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Details**: Enter the name of the speaker, which will be used to identify and address them.
* **Presentation Topic**: Briefly describe the topic of the presentation.
* **Duration**: Specify the length of the presentation in minutes or hours.
* **Phone**: Enter the speaker's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email**: Enter the speaker's email address for communication.
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about the speaker.
* **Files**: Select the types of files to be submitted by the speaker (Audio File, Video File, Presentation File).
* **AV Requirements**: Choose the required AV equipment for the presentation (Screen, Stage, Microphone, Projector).
* **Sponsor**: Indicate the sponsorship arrangement (No Sponsor, Sponsored by Self, Sponsored by Someone Else).
* **Additional Information**: Provide any extra details about the speaker.
