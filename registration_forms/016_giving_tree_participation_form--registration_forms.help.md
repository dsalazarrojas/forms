# Giving Tree Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Giving Tree Participation Form is a collection of information that will help us understand your preferences and interests. This form will help us tailor our giving tree programs to better meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your name and email address in the required fields.
* For the donation amount, please enter a whole number (e.g., 10, 20, 50). This will help us understand how often you would like to participate.
* For the date and time fields, select a date and time from the calendar or enter a specific date and time in the format MM/DD/YYYY HH:MM.
* If you have a preferred frequency of participation (e.g., daily, weekly, monthly), select from the options provided.
* Make sure to review and submit your form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as you would like to be listed on our giving tree reports and communications.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications from our giving tree program.
* **Donation** (`donation`, number, required): Specify the amount of your donation for our giving tree program.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Select a date for participation in our giving tree program, or enter a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Select a time for participation in our giving tree program, or enter a specific time in the format HH:MM.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): This field is a duplicate of the "Name" field at the beginning of the form and is intended to help us understand your name preferences. Please enter the same name as before.
* **Donation** (`donation`, number, required): This field is another opportunity to specify the amount of your donation. Please enter the same value as before.
* **Frequency** (`frequency`, select_one, required): Select how often you would like to participate in our giving tree program from the options provided: Daily, Weekly, Monthly, or Never.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us directly.
