# Mocktail Party Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a mocktail party event. It helps collect information about the event planner, venue, guests, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Event Planner's information in the "Event Planner" field.
2. Enter the venue name in the "Venue Name" field.
3. Enter the expected guest count in the "Guest Count" field.
4. Select the guest preferences in the "Guest Preferences" field.
5. Select the confirmation option in the "Confirmation" field.
6. Enter the headcount in the "Headcount" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
9. Select the date and time of the event in the "Date and Time" field.
10. Select the date and time of the event in the "Date and Time 2" field. (Note: This field is optional and seems to be redundant with the previous field. Consider removing it.)
11. Enter any additional notes about the event in the "Note" field.
12. Finally, enter a brief description of the event in the "Event Description" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Planner** (`Event Planner`, text, required): Enter the name or username of the person planning the event.
* **Venue Name** (`Venue Name`, text, required): Enter the name of the venue where the event will take place.
* **Guest Count** (`Guest Count`, number, required): Enter the expected number of guests attending the event.
* **Guest Preferences** (`Guest Preferences`, select multiple, required): Select the preferences of the guests, such as dietary restrictions or special requirements.
* **Confirmation** (`Confirmation`, select one, required): Select the confirmation option to confirm the event details.
* **Headcount** (`Headcount`, number, required): Enter the headcount (number of people attending) of the event.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date and Time** (`Date and Time`, date, required): Select the date and time of the event.
* **Date and Time 2** (`Date and Time 2`, time, optional): This field seems to be optional and redundant with the "Date and Time" field. Please ignore it.
* **Note** (`Note`, note, required): Enter any additional notes or comments about the event.
* **Event Description** (`Event Description`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the event.
