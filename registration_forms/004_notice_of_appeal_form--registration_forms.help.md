# Notice Of Appeal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Notice Of Appeal Form is a formal document used to submit a notice of appeal. This form will help you initiate the appeal process, which will be reviewed and processed accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant category for your appeal (e.g., Registration Forms, Information Request Forms, or Lawyer Forms).
2. Provide a clear and concise description of your appeal.
3. Specify the tool you are appealing (optional).
4. If required, enter the unique identifier for this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category** (`Category`, select_one, required): Select the relevant category for your appeal. This will help us to direct your appeal to the right department or team.
* **Description** (`Description`, text, required): Enter a clear and concise description of your appeal. This will help us to understand the context and nature of your appeal.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, text, optional): If applicable, specify the tool you are appealing. This is an optional field, but it can help us to better understand your appeal.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, number, optional): If required, enter the unique identifier for this form. This is used to track and reference your appeal.

## Tips
- Please ensure that your description is clear and concise.
- Select the most relevant category for your appeal.
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to contact us for assistance.
