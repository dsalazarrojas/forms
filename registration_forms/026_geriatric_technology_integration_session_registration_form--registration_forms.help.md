# Geriatric Technology Integration Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register for a geriatric technology integration session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details such as your first name, email, and phone number.
2. Select a date and time for the session.
3. Choose a location for the session.
4. Enter any additional notes or comments.
5. Select the topics you would like to cover during the session.
6. Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number.
* **Session Date** (required): Select the date of the session.
* **Session Time** (required): Select the time of the session.
* **Location** (required): Choose a location for the session. 
* **Notes** (optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about the session.
* **Submitted By** (optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Assigned Tool** (optional): Enter the tool assigned to you for the session.
* **Session Type** (optional): Select the type of session you would like to have.
* **Workshop Name** (required): Enter the name of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Geriatric Topics** (required): Select the topic you would like to cover during the session.
* **Submit** (required): Confirm your submission.
* **Form ID** (required): Enter a unique ID for your session.
* **Geriatric Topics 2, 3, 4, 5** (optional): Select additional topics you would like to cover during the session.
* **Submitted** (required): Confirm your submission.
* **Created** (optional): Enter the date the form was created.
* **Updated** (optional): Enter the date the form was last updated.
* **Created By** (optional): Enter the name of the person who created the form.
* **Created By Email** (optional): Enter the email of the person who created the form.
* **Form ID 2** (required): Enter a unique ID for your session.
* **Geriatric Topics 4, 5** (required): Select additional topics you would like to cover during the session.
* **Submitted** (required): Confirm your submission.

Please ensure all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
