# Healthy Snacking Expo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees to the Healthy Snacking Expo. Please fill out the form to provide us with your information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. Make sure to double-check that it matches the **Confirm Email** field.
4. Select your **Dietary Restrictions** from the dropdown menu.
5. Choose your **Session Interests** from the dropdown menu.
6. Select your **Dietary Preferences** from the dropdown menu.
7. Enter a brief description of your dietary restrictions in the "Dietary Restrictions Description" field.
8. Enter your **First Language** in the "First Language" field.
9. Enter your **Second Language** in the "Second Language" field.
10. Enter your **Third Language** in the "Third Language" field (optional).
11. Choose your **Session Preferences** from the dropdown menu.
12. Select your preferred **Time Slots** from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to receive important updates about the Healthy Snacking Expo.
* **Confirm Email**: Double-check that this matches your email address in the "Email" field for verification purposes.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., vegetarian, gluten-free, etc.).
* **Session Interests**: Choose sessions that interest you the most.
* **Dietary Preferences**: Select your dietary preferences (e.g., vegan, dairy-free, etc.).
* **Dietary Restrictions Description**: Briefly describe any dietary restrictions you have (e.g., specific reasons, medical conditions, etc.).
* **First Language**: Enter the language you are most comfortable speaking or writing in.
* **Second Language**: If you have a second language, enter it here (optional).
* **Third Language**: If you have a third language, enter it here (optional).
* **Session Preferences**: Choose your preferred session topics or formats (e.g., keynote, workshop, etc.).
* **Time Slots**: Select the time slots you are available to attend sessions during the Healthy Snacking Expo.

Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process.
