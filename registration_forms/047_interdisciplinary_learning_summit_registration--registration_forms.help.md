# Interdisciplinary Learning Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Interdisciplinary Learning Summit registration form is used to gather information from attendees who wish to participate in the summit. This includes collecting user details, session interests, and any accessibility needs. The form is designed to ensure a smooth and productive experience for all attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your **User Information** in the **User Information** section.
2. Indicate your session interests in the **Session Interests** section by selecting your preferred options.
3. Specify any **Accessibility Needs** you may have in the **Accessibility Needs** section.
4. Provide more details about the **Session Details** you're interested in.
5. Optionally, indicate your interests in the **Session Interests** section again.
6. Fill in your **User Details** in the **User Details** section.
7. Enter your **Email** address and **Phone** number in the respective sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Please enter your name and any other relevant information.
* **Session Interests**: Select all the sessions you are interested in by clicking on the checkboxes.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Choose one of the options that best describes your accessibility needs.
* **Session Details**: Provide any additional information about the sessions you are interested in.
* **Session Interests** (again): This is a duplicate field to ensure accurate counting of your interests.
* **User Details**: Enter any further details about yourself.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you in case of emergencies.

Note: Please fill in the required information accurately and completely to ensure we can provide you a smooth experience at the summit.
