# Cross Brand Affiliate Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering a new affiliate in the Cross Brand Affiliate Network. The purpose of this form is to gather information from the user to create a new affiliate account.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration form on the first page.
2. Enter your information in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).


## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration
* **name**: Enter your name as you would like it to appear on your affiliate account.

### Email
* **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be your login email for the Cross Brand Affiliate Network.

### Password
* **Password**: Enter a strong and unique password for your affiliate account. 

### Phone
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

### Confirm Password
* **Confirm Password**: Confirm your password to ensure accuracy.

### Terms of Service
* **Terms of Service**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the Cross Brand Affiliate Network's terms of service.

### Confirm Affiliate Terms
* **Confirm Affiliate Terms**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the Cross Brand Affiliate Network's terms of service.

### Affiliate First Name
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is spelled on your government-issued ID.

### Last Name
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is spelled on your government-issued ID.

### Address
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address.

### City
* **City**: Enter your city.

### State
* **State**: Enter your state or province.

### Zip
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.

### Country
* **Country**: Enter your country.

### Affiliate ID
* **Affiliate ID**: Enter your affiliate ID.

### Affiliate Name
* **Affiliate Name**: Enter your affiliate name.

### Affiliate URL
* **Affiliate URL**: Enter your affiliate URL.

### Affiliate Email
* **Affiliate Email**: Enter your affiliate email address.

### Affiliate Password
* **Affiliate Password**: Enter a strong and unique password for your affiliate account. 

### Affiliate Phone
* **Affiliate Phone**: Enter your affiliate phone number.

### Affiliate Terms
* **Affiliate Terms**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the Cross Brand Affiliate Network's terms of service.

### Confirm Affiliate Terms
* **Confirm Affiliate Terms**: Confirm that you have read and agree to the Cross Brand Affiliate Network's terms of service.

### Date
* **Date**: Select a date.

### Time
* **Time**: Select a time.

### Submit
* **Submit**: Click the submit button to complete your registration. 

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*).
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, refer back to this help guide.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact the Cross Brand Affiliate Network support team.
