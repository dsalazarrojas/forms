# Library Room Reservation Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user preferences and requirements for room access at the library. It's essential to provide accurate information to ensure that your needs are met during your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Review the form carefully to understand what information is required.
*   Fill out the form with the relevant details.
*   Make sure to complete all fields that are marked as "required".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Input** (`user_input`, text, optional): Briefly describe your reason for visiting the library and your expectations for room access.
*   **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to visit the library.
*   **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): Select the start time of your visit.
*   **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Select the end date of your visit.
*   **Duration** (`duration`, text, optional): Provide the expected duration of your visit.
*   **Room Preferences** (`room_preferences`, text, optional): Specify any special requests or preferences you have regarding the room.
*   **User Comments** (`user_comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information you think would be helpful for library staff to know.
*   **Staff Comments** (`staff_comments`, text, optional): This field is for library staff to note any important details or concerns after reviewing your submission.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, optional):
    +   **Label 1**: Select this option to submit your form.
    +   **Label 2**: Select this option to save your form as a draft.
    +   **Label 3**: Select this option to preview your form before submitting.
*   **Staff Confirmation** (`staff_confirmation`, select_multiple, optional):
    +   **Option 1**: Select this option to confirm your visit details.
    +   **Option 2**: Select this option to request changes to your visit details.
    +   **Option 3**: Select this option to decline or cancel your visit.
*   **Staff Message** (`staff_message`, text, optional): This field is for library staff to communicate any important information or requests related to your visit.

## Tips

*   If you need help with any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
*   Make sure to review your submission carefully before submitting.
*   Library staff will review your submission and contact you regarding your visit details.
*   Be prepared for potential follow-up questions or requests from library staff.
