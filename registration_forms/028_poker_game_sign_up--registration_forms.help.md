# poker_game_sign_up - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from players who want to sign up for a poker game. It's used to create a schedule and communicate with players about game details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "Player Information" section with your name and any relevant details.
2. Choose your buy-in amount from the options provided.
3. Select the type of game you want to participate in (Cash Game or Tournament).
4. Enter the date and time of the game.
5. Specify the number of players participating.
6. Choose the game type (Home Game, Club Game, or Online Game).
7. Enter the location of the game.
8. Add any additional notes or comments (optional).
9. Finally, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information** (text, required): This field is for entering your name and any relevant player details.
* **Buy-in Information** (select_multiple, optional): Choose how much you plan to buy-in for the game.
	+ Examples: $100, $200, $500, $1000, $2000, $5000
* **Game Information** (select_one, optional): Select the type of game you want to participate in.
	+ Options: Cash Game, Tournament
* **Game Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the game.
* **Game Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the game.
* **Number of Players** (number, optional): Specify the number of players participating in the game.
* **Game Type** (select_one, optional): Choose the type of game you want to participate in.
	+ Options: Home Game, Club Game, Online Game
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter the location of the game.
* **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about the game.
* **Submitted By** (text, optional): Enter your name to identify who submitted the form.
* **Submit Date** (date, optional): The date and time the form was submitted.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your game details are up-to-date and communicated effectively to other players. If you have any questions or need clarification, feel free to ask.
