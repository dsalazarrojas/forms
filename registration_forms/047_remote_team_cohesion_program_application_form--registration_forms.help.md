# Remote Team Cohesion Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Remote Team Cohesion Program is designed to help remote teams improve their collaboration and productivity. This form is used to collect information about your team's dynamics, communication channels, and collaboration style. Please fill out this form to apply to the program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill in all required fields (denoted by an asterisk *) with accurate and complete information.
- For fields with multiple options, select all that apply.
- Use the correct format for date fields (MM/DD/YYYY).
- For time fields, use the format HH:MM AM/PM.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your organization.
- **Team Lead Name** (`team_lead_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your team lead.
- **Team Lead Email** (`team_lead_email`, email, required): Enter the team lead's email address.
- **Team Lead Phone** (`team_lead_phone`, text, required): Enter the team lead's phone number.
- **Team Lead Job Title** (`team_lead_job_title`, text, required): Enter the team lead's job title.
- **Team Members** (`team_members`, select_multiple, required): List all team members that will be participating in the program.
  - Select 'Yes' for team members that will participate.
  - Select 'No' for team members that will not participate.
- **Team Member Roles** (`team_member_roles`, select_multiple, required): List the roles that each team member will have.
  - Select 'Yes' for roles that apply.
  - Select 'No' for roles that do not apply.
- **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, time, required): Enter the hours your team works (e.g., 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM).
- **Work Days** (`work_days`, time, required): Enter the days your team works (e.g., Monday to Friday).
- **Timezone** (`timezone`, time, required): Enter your team's time zone (e.g., EST, PST).
- **Timezone Offset** (`time_zone_offset`, number, required): If not in the US Eastern time zone, enter the correct time zone offset in UTC.
- **Team Lead Address** (`team_lead_address`, text, required): Enter the team lead's physical work address.
- **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to start the program.
- **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you want to end the program.
- **Team Lead Availability** (`team_lead_availability`, text, required): Enter the team lead's availability for the program.
- **Notes** (`notes`, note, required): Enter any additional notes about your team.
- **Team Dynamics** (`team_dynamics`, text, required): Provide information about team dynamics and collaboration.
- **Communication Channels** (`communication_channels`, text, required): List the communication channels your team uses for collaboration.
- **Collaboration Tools** (`collaboration_technologies`, text, required): List the collaboration tools your team uses.
- **Collaboration Frequency** (`collaboration_frequency`, text, required): Enter the frequency of collaboration among team members.
- **Collaboration Style** (`collaboration_style`, text, required): Enter how your team collaborates (e.g., asynchronously, synchronously).
- **Team Lead Collaboration Experience** (`team_lead_collaboration_experience`, text, required): Enter the team lead's collaboration experience.
- **Team Lead Management Experience** (`team_lead_management_experience`, text, required): Enter the team lead's management experience.
- **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this application form.

Note: The fields marked as 'required' are mandatory and must be filled in for a successful application.
