# Culinary Masterclass RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Culinary Masterclass event. Please fill it out accurately to ensure that you are registered for the correct event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure you have filled out the correct event ID.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Id** (`event_id`, number, required: false): Enter the ID of the event you are registering for. If you're unsure, please check the event schedule to ensure you have the correct ID.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **E-mail** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Course Date** (`course_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date for the course you are attending.
- **Course Time** (`course_time`, time, required: false): Choose the time for the course you are attending.
- **Preferences** (`preferences`, note, required: false): Enter any special requests or preferences for the course.
- **Comments** (`comments`, text, required: false): Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.

## Tips
* Please double-check that you have filled out the correct event ID.
* If you've already RSVP'd for the same event, please do not fill out this form again.
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (if any).
