# HIV Stigma Awareness Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from event organizers and attendees for HIV stigma awareness events. This information will be used to determine the needs and preferences of attendees, as well as to gather contact information for follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Select the type of event you are registering for (Event Signups).
2. Indicate if attendees have specific needs (Attendee Needs).
3. Determine if sessions will be planned (Session Planning).
4. Provide information on event planning (Event Planning).
5. Specify any attendee preferences (Attendee Preferences).
6. Choose how to contact attendees (Contact Details).
7. Specify event preferences (Event Preferences).
8. Indicate support details (Support Details).
9. Confirm medical history (Medical History).
10. Provide details on medication (Medication Details).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Signups** (Event Signups, select_multiple, required): Select the type of event you are registering for.
* **Attendee Needs** (Attendee Needs, text, optional): If attendees have specific needs, such as accommodations or accessibility requirements, please specify them here.
* **Session Planning** (Session Planning, select_one, optional): Determine if sessions will be planned for the event.
* **Event Planning** (Event Planning, note, optional): Provide information on event planning, such as dates, times, and locations.
* **Attendee Preferences** (Attendee Preferences, select_multiple, optional): Specify any preferences for attendees, such as language or diet requirements.
* **Contact Details** (Contact Details, select_multiple, optional): Choose how attendees can be contacted, such as phone, email, or social media.
* **Event Preferences** (Event Preferences, select_multiple, optional): Specify any preferences for the event, such as music or smoking policies.
* **Support Details** (Support Details, select_multiple, optional): Indicate support details, such as on-site medical support or language support.
* **Medical History** (Medical History, select_multiple, optional): Confirm medical history, such as any allergies or medical conditions.
* **Medication Details** (Medication Details, select_multiple, optional): Provide details on medication, such as any medication allergies or requirements.
