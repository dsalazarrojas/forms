# Summer Camp Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Summer Camp Reservation Form is a registration form for parents or guardians to register their child or children for a summer camp program. The form collects contact information from the parent or guardian and details about the child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Name" field with the child's name.
2. Enter the child's address in the "Address" field.
3. Provide the city in the "City" field.
4. If you wish to provide additional information about the child, please fill out the "Parent Info" field.
5. Enter the name of the child (if different from the first name) in the "Name 3" field.
6. Enter your contact email address in the "Email" field, following the standard email format (e.g., "username@domain.tld").
7. If you have any, please enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
8. Enter any additional details about the camp in the "Camp Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parents Information (Text)**: This field is optional and is used to provide additional information about the parent or guardian.
* **Name (Text)**: Enter the child's name.
* **Address (Text)**: Enter the child's address.
* **City (Text)**: Enter the city where the child lives.
* **Parent Info (Text)**: This field is optional and is used to provide additional information about the parent or guardian.
* **Name 3 (Text)**: Enter the name of the child (if different from the first name).
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address in the standard format (e.g., "username@domain.tld").
* **Phone (Text)**: If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Camp Info (Text)**: Enter any additional details about the camp you are registering for.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you register one child per form submission.
* If you have trouble registering, contact the summer camp team for assistance.
* Double-check your email address for accuracy before submitting the form.
* Enter any additional details about the camp you are registering for in the "Camp Info" field.
