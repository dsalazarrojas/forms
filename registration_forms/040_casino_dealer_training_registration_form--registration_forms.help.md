# Casino Dealer Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to gather essential information from potential trainees for the Casino Dealer Training Program. It's crucial to fill it out accurately and truthfully.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Registration Information section with your details.
2. Select the training program you're enrolling in from the options.
3. Provide your personal details in the Personal Information section.
4. Answer the Professional Information section with your current employment status and gaming experience.
5. State your training objectives and post-training employment plans.
6. Select your training schedule preference.
7. Choose whether you can commit to completing the full program and agree to background checks.
8. Add any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date**: This is the date when you're registering for the program.
* **Training Program Name**: Select the name of the training program you're enrolling in:
	+ Blackjack Dealer Training
	+ Roulette Dealer Training
	+ Poker Dealer Training
	+ Baccarat Dealer Training
	+ Multi-Game Training
	+ Advanced Dealer Certification
* **Training Session**: Describe the training session you're interested in.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Mailing Address**: Enter your mailing address where you can be reached.
* **City, State, Zip**: Enter your city, state, and zip code where you can be reached.
* **Current Employment Status**: Select your current employment status:
	+ Currently Employed
	+ Seeking Employment
	+ Unemployed
	+ Self-Employed
	+ Other
* **Prior Gaming or Casino Experience**: Select your level of gaming or casino experience:
	+ Extensive
	+ Moderate
	+ Some
	+ None
* **Previous Dealer Experience**: Describe any previous dealer experience if applicable.
* **What are your training objectives?**: Describe your goals for the training program.
* **Post-Training Employment Plans**: Select your post-training employment plans:
	+ Seeking immediate employment
	+ Career advancement
	+ Skill enhancement
	+ Personal interest
	+ Undecided
* **Preferred Training Start Date**: Select your preferred training start date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Training Schedule Preference**: Select your preferred training schedule:
	+ Full-time
	+ Part-time
	+ Evening Classes
	+ Weekend Classes
	+ Flexible
* **Can you commit to completing the full program?**: Select 'Yes' if you're committed to completing the full program.
* **I agree to background check and reference verification**: Select 'Yes' to agree on background check and reference verification.
* **Additional Comments or Questions**: Add any additional comments or questions you have.
