# AG Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for guests to provide their personal and event-related information for registration and to help us best serve them during their visit.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields, marked as **Required** in the field descriptions below.
2. For optional fields, select the options that apply to you.
3. Double-check that you've accurately filled out each field before submitting your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, **Required**): Enter your legal first and last name.
• **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, **Required**): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
• **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, phone, Optional): Provide your phone number for contact if needed.
• **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): If you choose to, enter your date of birth.
• **Age** (`age`, integer, Optional): Enter your age in years.
• **Mailing Address** (`address`, text, Optional): Enter your street, city, state, and zip code.
• **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact`, text, Optional): Enter the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
• **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_phone`, phone, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
• **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, **Required**): Enter the date you plan to arrive.
• **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, **Required**): Enter the time you expect to arrive.
• **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, select_one, Optional): Choose the level that best describes your experience (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional).
• **How Did You Hear About Us?** (`how_heard`, select_one, Optional): Select how you found out about us (Social media, Friend/Family, Website, Flyer/Poster, Other).
• **Event Interests** (`event_interest`, select_multiple, Optional): Select all events you're interested in (Workshops, Networking, Tours, Dining, Others).
• **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, Optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Nut-free, Other).
• **Any Special Requirements or Accommodations?** (`special_needs`, text, Optional): Enter any special requirements or accommodations (e.g., wheelchair access, hearing assistance).
• **Additional Comments or Notes** (`additional_comments`, text, Optional): Share any other information you think is relevant.
• **Preferred Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, Optional): Choose how you'd like us to contact you (Email, Phone, SMS).
• **I Agree to Terms and Conditions** (`consent_to_terms`, select_one, **Required**): Check this to submit your registration.
• **Terms and Conditions** (`note_terms`, note, **Required**): By submitting, you agree to our terms and conditions.
