# Camp Registration Form PayPal Checkout - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering camp attendees and processing payments through PayPal. To ensure that you are on the right page, please make sure you have all the required information and understand our registration and payment terms.

## How To Complete This Form
To successfully complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your registrant's full name, email address, and contact phone number in the designated fields.
2. Provide a valid street address and city, state, or province, and postal code for billing information.
3. Select the desired camp session and the number of participants registering.
4. Choose any additional options (if needed) and select the total estimated fee to be processed.
5. Enter any PayPal transaction ID if you've already made a payment outside of this integrated button (optional).
6. Read and agree to the registration and refund policy.
7. Finally, enter the date of submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registrant Full Name** (`registrant_full_name_pp`): Please enter the full name of the person registering or attending the camp.
* **Email Address** (`registrant_email_pp`): Enter a valid email address for receipt and registration confirmation purposes.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`registrant_phone_pp`): Enter a contact phone number for the registrant or parent.
* **Street Address** (`billing_street_address_pp`): Enter the registrant's street address for billing purposes.
* **City** (`billing_city_pp`): Enter the city where the registrant resides.
* **State/Province** (`billing_state_pp`): Enter the state or province where the registrant resides.
* **Postal/Zip Code** (`billing_zip_code_pp`): Enter the registrant's postal or zip code.
* **Select Your Camp Session** (`camp_session_selection_pp`): Choose the desired camp session from the options provided (fees are processed via PayPal).
* **Number of Participants** (`number_of_participants_pp`): Enter the total number of participants registering.
* **Additional Options - Add-ons** (`additional_options_pp`): Select any additional options or add-ons for the registrant.
* **Total Fee to be Processed** (`total_estimated_fee_pp`): This field is automatically calculated based on your selections.
* **PayPal Transaction ID** (`paypal_transaction_id_placeholder`): Leave blank if paying now through the integrated button.
* **Registration Waiver and Refund Policy** (`waiver_agreement_pp`): Please read and agree to the terms of registration and understand the refund policy.
* **Submission Date** (`registration_pp_timestamp`): Enter the date of submission.

Please make sure to review each field carefully to ensure a smooth and successful registration process.
