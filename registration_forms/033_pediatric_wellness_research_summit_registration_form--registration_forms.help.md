# Pediatric Wellness Research Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in the Pediatric Wellness Research Summit. The purpose of this form is to collect information about the attendees, their research interests, and their preferences for the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name on **Page 1**.
2. Enter your Last Name on **Page 2**.
3. Provide your Email Address on **Page 3**.
4. Enter your Phone Number on **Page 4**.
5. Enter your Date of Birth on **Page 5**.
6. Enter the time you registered on **Page 6**.
7. Describe the research topic that interests you on **Page 7**.
8. Provide payment information on **Page 8**, if applicable.
9. Select your preferred payment method on **Page 9**.
10. Choose which workshops you would like to attend on **Page 10**.
11. Choose the status of your registration on **Page 11**.
12. Enter any additional comments or notes on **Page 12**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Page 1)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Last Name (Page 2)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documentation.
* **Email Address (Page 3)**: Enter the email address you check regularly, as we will use it to communicate with you about the summit.
* **Phone Number (Page 4)**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you about any last-minute updates or changes to the summit.
* **Date of Birth (Page 5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format of MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Time of Registration (Page 6)**: Enter the time you registered for the summit.
* **Research Topic (Page 7)**: Describe the specific topic in pediatric wellness research that interests you.
* **Payment Information (Page 8)**: If you are paying for the summit, enter any relevant payment information.
* **Payment Method (Page 9)**: Select the payment method you will use to pay for the summit (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, or Option 4).
* **Workshop Attendance (Page 10)**: Choose which workshops you would like to attend by selecting the corresponding options (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **Registration Status (Page 11)**: Choose the status of your registration (Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3).
* **Notes (Page 12)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Additional Comments (Page 13)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Additional Comments (Page 14)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your registration.

Note: The fields marked as "required: false" are optional and can be filled in or left blank.
