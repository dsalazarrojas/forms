# Special Effects Techniques Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration and information collection for a Special Effects Techniques Training Form. It is designed to gather data on the training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Registration Form page.
2. Proceed to the Training Session page and select the appropriate options.
3. Move on to the Workshop Duration page and select the options.
4. Complete the Date and Time page with the required information.
5. Fill out the Start Time and End Time pages.
6. Proceed to the Training Facility page and select the appropriate options.
7. Move on to the Workshop Location page and select the options.
8. Complete the Training Instructor page with the relevant information.
9. Select the Workshop Topics page and select the options.
10. Fill out the Additional Notes page with any additional information.
11. Finally, fill out the Registration Email page with the required information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): This page is the first step in the form. It is the registration form where you can enter your name and other relevant details.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select whether you are interested in attending a training session or not.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select how long you are interested in the training session, e.g., 'Yes' or 'No'.
* **Date and Time** (`date_and_time`, date, required: false): This field is used to enter the date and time of the training session.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): This field is used to enter the start time of the training session.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required: false): This field is used to enter the end time of the training session.
* **Training Facility** (`training_facility`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select whether you are interested in a specific training facility.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the location of the training session.
* **Training Instructor** (`training_instructor`, select_one, required: false): This field is used to select the training instructor.
* **Workshop Topics** (`workshop_topics`, select_multiple, required: false): This field is used to select the topics of the training session.
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required: false): This field is used to enter any additional notes or information about the training session.
* **Registration Email** (`registration_email`, email, required: false): This field is used to enter your registration email for the training session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Use the select options to select the relevant information for each field.
* Enter accurate and relevant information for each field.
* Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure everything is correct.
