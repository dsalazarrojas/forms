# email_coaching_package_registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register an email coaching package.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the client's information in the "Client Information" section.
2. Enter the email address and phone number of the client in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Provide details about the coaching package, including the start and end dates, duration, and price.
4. Choose a payment method and confirm the payment status.
5. Enter any comments or feedback about the package.
6. Confirm the coach's information, including their name, email, phone number, and address.
7. Specify whether the package includes certain services.
8. Enter a description of the package and its terms.
9. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the package terms.
10. Submit the form to register the email coaching package.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Client Information (Client Information)**: Enter the client's name and other relevant details.
* **Contact Email (Contact Email)**: Enter the client's email address.
* **Contact Phone (Contact Phone)**: Enter the client's phone number.
* **Package Details (Package Details)**: Enter a brief description of the coaching package.
* **Start Date (Start Date)**: Enter the start date of the coaching package.
* **End Date (End Date)**: Enter the end date of the coaching package.
* **Duration (Duration)**: Enter the duration of the coaching package.
* **Package Price (Package Price)**: Enter the price of the coaching package.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method)**: Choose how the client will pay for the package (Cash, Bank transfer, or PayPal).
* **Payment Status (Payment Status)**: Confirm the payment status of the package (Paid, Not Paid, or Paid Partially).
* **Comments (Comments)**: Enter any comments or feedback about the package.
* **Coach Information (Coach Information)**: Enter the coach's name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Coach Name (Coach Name)**: Enter the coach's name.
* **Coach Email (Coach Email)**: Enter the coach's email address.
* **Coach Phone (Coach Phone)**: Enter the coach's phone number.
* **Coach Address (Coach Address)**: Enter the coach's address.
* **Coach License (Coach License)**: Enter the coach's license number or certification.
* **Package Services (Package Services)**: Specify whether the package includes certain services (Yes or No).
* **Package Topics (Package Topics)**: Enter a description of the package topics.
* **Package Description (Package Description)**: Enter a detailed description of the package.
* **Package Terms (Package Terms)**: Enter the terms of the package.
* **Package Terms Agreed (Package Terms Agreed)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the package terms (True or False).
* **Submit (Submit)**: Click to submit the form and register the coaching package.
* **Form Comments (Form Comments)**: Enter any additional comments or feedback about the form.
* **Package ID (Package ID)**: Specify whether the package is available for registration (Yes or No).

Note: This form is for administrative purposes only, and the fields are designed to collect information about the coaching packages and payments. Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and consistently to avoid errors.
