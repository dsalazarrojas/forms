# Startup Growth Consulting Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Growth Consulting Summit Registration form is used to gather information from attendees for the event. The form is designed to collect essential details that will enable us to manage and tailor the event according to our attendees' needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details, such as your name and email.
2. Select your interests from the list provided.
3. Choose your preferred options for consent and other relevant choices.
4. Upload any additional files or documents you wish to share with us.
5. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Attendee Details (Page 1)
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): This field is for your personal name, which will be used to address you during the event.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Please enter your valid email address so we can reach you if needed.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, optional): This is a text field for any additional notes or comments you would like to include.

### Interests (Page 2)
* **Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select the areas of interest that you would like to learn more about during the summit.

### Preferences (Page 3)
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose how you would like to be contacted.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): This is a field for your phone number, which will be used to contact you if needed.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter the name of your company.

### Upload Files (Page 4)
* **File 1**, **File 2**, **File 3** (`file1`, `file2`, `file3`, `text`, optional): Please upload any relevant files or documents you wish to share with us.

### Additional Questions (Page 5)
* **Checkbox 1**, **Checkbox 2**, **Checkbox 3**, **Checkbox 4** (`checkbox1`, `checkbox2`, `checkbox3`, `checkbox4`, `select_multiple`, optional): Please select your preferences or choices for the following questions.
* **Radio 1**, **Radio 2**, **Radio 3**, **Radio 4** (`radio1`, `radio2`, `radio3`, `radio4`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose your preferred option for the following question.

Note: Please review the fields carefully and ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
