# template_treasury_client_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The template_treasury_client_registration_form is a registration form designed for users to provide personal and demographic information to the Treasury. This form is intended for internal use only and should not be submitted multiple times by the same user.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (First name, Last name, Email, and Submit).
2. Optionally, you can provide additional information such as Phone, Date of birth, Time of birth, Nationality, Nationality options, Client type, Client type options, Address, City, State, Zip, Language, Language options, Gender, Gender options, Occupation, Education, Experience, Notes, Nationality notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`First name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`Last name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of birth** (`Date of birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of birth** (`Time of birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth.
* **Nationality** (`Nationality`, select_multiple, optional): Select your nationality. Options: Yes, No.
* **Nationality options** (`Nationality options`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your nationality.
* **Client type** (`Client type`, select_one, optional): Select your client type. Options: Yes, No.
* **Client type options** (`Client type options`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your client type.
* **Address** (`Address`, note, optional): Enter your address. 
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter the city you reside in.
* **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter the state you reside in.
* **Zip** (`Zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Country** (`Country`, text, optional): Enter your country of residence.
* **Language** (`Language`, select_one, optional): Select your language. Options: Yes, No.
* **Language options** (`Language options`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your language.
* **Gender** (`Gender`, select_one, optional): Select your gender. Options: Yes, No.
* **Gender options** (`Gender options`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your gender.
* **Occupation** (`Occupation`, text, optional): Enter your occupation.
* **Education** (`Education`, text, optional): Enter your educational background.
* **Experience** (`Experience`, number, optional): Enter your years of experience.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Nationality notes** (`Nationality notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about your nationality.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click to submit your registration.

## Tips
* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submitting the form.
* If you experience any issues with the form, contact the Treasury staff for assistance.
