# Panel Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Panel Application Form is designed to collect information from applicants for panels. This form is used to ensure that all necessary details are provided to process the application correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Business Name.
2. Enter your full Name.
3. Provide a valid Email address.
4. Choose your Business Type from the options (Business, Institution, Organization).
5. Enter your Address (if required).
6. Enter your Phone number.
7. Choose your Category (if applicable).
8. Add any additional Notes (if required).
9. Select the Application Status from the options (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
10. Choose the Assigned Tool from the options (if required).
11. Enter the Date and Time of application creation.
12. Enter the Date and Time of last update.
13. Enter a valid User ID (if required).
14. Enter the Panel Name (if required).
15. Enter the Application ID (if required).
16. Enter the Date and Time of submission.
17. Enter the User who submitted the application.
18. Enter the Date and Time of submission (if required).
19. Add any Application Status Notes (if required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Business Name (1)
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business.
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: No.

### Name (2)
*   **Name**: Enter your full name.
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### Email (3)
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your business.
*   **Type**: email.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### Business Type (4)
*   **Business Type**: Choose the type of your business (Business, Institution, Organization).
*   **Type**: select_one.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### Address (5)
*   **Address**: Enter your business address (if required).
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: No.

### Phone (6)
*   **Phone**: Enter your business phone number.
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: Yes.

### Category (7)
*   **Category**: Choose your category (if applicable).
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: No.

### Notes (8)
*   **Notes**: Add any additional comments or notes about your application.
*   **Type**: note.
*   **Required**: No.

### Application Status (9)
*   **Application Status**: Select the status of your application (Pending, Approved, Rejected).
*   **Type**: select_one.
*   **Required**: No.

### Assigned Tool (10)
*   **Assigned Tool**: Choose the tool assigned to your application (if required).
*   **Type**: select_one.
*   **Required**: No.

### Created At (11)
*   **Created At**: Date and Time of application creation.
*   **Type**: date.
*   **Required**: No.

### Updated At (12)
*   **Updated At**: Date and Time of last update.
*   **Type**: date.
*   **Required**: No.

### User Id (13)
*   **User Id**: Enter a valid User ID (if required).
*   **Type**: number.
*   **Required**: No.

### Panel Name (14)
*   **Panel Name**: Enter the Panel Name (if required).
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: No.

### Application Id (15)
*   **Application Id**: Enter the Application ID (if required).
*   **Type**: number.
*   **Required**: No.

### Submitted At (16)
*   **Submitted At**: Date and Time of submission.
*   **Type**: date.
*   **Required**: No.

### Submitted By (17)
*   **Submitted By**: Enter the User who submitted the application.
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: No.

### Submit Date (18)
*   **Submit Date**: Date and Time of submission (if required).
*   **Type**: date.
*   **Required**: No.

### Application Status Notes (19)
*   **Application Status Notes**: Add any Application Status Notes (if required).
*   **Type**: text.
*   **Required**: No.
