# Healthcare Professional Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect demographic information and relevant details from healthcare professionals for future reference and collaboration within the healthcare system.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the relevant fields for your healthcare provider information.
2.  Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
3.  Provide your contact details such as Email and Phone Number.
4.  Choose your Date of Birth.
5.  Select your Provider Type and Healthcare Facility from the respective dropdown menus.
6.  Choose your Medical Board Certifications and Onboarding Process.
7.  Fill in your Emergency Contact information.
8.  Provide your address details including City, State, Zip, and Country.
9.  Choose the Onboarding Date for your process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your given name.
*   **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your surname.
*   **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
*   **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
*   **Provider Type** (`provider_type`, select\_multiple, optional): Select your provider type from the options (Primary Care, Specialist, Other).
*   **Healthcare Facility** (`healthcare_facility`, select\_one, optional): Select the healthcare facility where you work from the options (Hospital, Clinic, Private Practice).
*   **Medical Board Certifications** (`medical_board_certifications`, select\_multiple, optional): Select your medical board certification status from the options (Active, Inactive, Expired).
*   **Onboarding Process** (`onboarding_process`, select\_one, optional): Select the onboarding process type from the options (Manual, Automated, Hybrid).
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact information.
*   **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
*   **City** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
*   **State** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your zip code.
*   **Country** (`country`, text, optional): Enter your country.
*   **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of your onboarding process.

## Tips
*   Double-check the entered information for accuracy.
*   Ensure that the entered information is up-to-date.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, contact the relevant authorities for assistance.
