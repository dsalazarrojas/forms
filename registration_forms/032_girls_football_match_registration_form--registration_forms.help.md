# girls-football-match-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for teams or individuals to register and provide information for a girls' football match event. It collects contact, team, and payment details to facilitate efficient processing and communication during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill in the form completely and accurately**, providing all required and optional information.
2. **Save your progress** if you need to take a break or revisit your entry later.
3. **Review and submit your form** once you have collected all necessary information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (1)** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ This field is for providing general information about the participant.
* **Contact Information (2)** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ Enter your contact information to help us get in touch with you.
* **Match Details (3)** (`Match Details`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ Provide any additional details about the match you'd like to participate in.
* **Team Information (4)** (`Team Information`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ Enter your team's name and details if applicable.
* **Payment Method (5)** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
	+ Select how you plan to make your payment (Option A or Option B).
* **Notes (6)** (`Notes`, `note`, required: `false`)
	+ Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.
* **Payment Status (7)** (`Payment Status`, `select_one`, required: `false`)
	+ Indicate whether you have made a payment (Paid or Not Paid).
* **Email (8)** (`Email`, `email`, required: `false`)
	+ Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone (9)** (`Phone`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ Provide your phone number for quick contact.
* **Event Date (10)** (`Event Date`, `date`, required: `false`)
	+ Specify the date of the event you'd like to participate in.
* **Event Time (11)** (`Event Time`, `time`, required: `false`)
	+ Enter the time of the event.
* **Team Name (12)** (`Team Name`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ Enter your team's name if applicable.
* **Team Manager Name (13)** (`Team Manager Name`, `text`, required: `false`)
	+ Provide the name of your team's manager if applicable.

Note: The required fields are not actually required, but it's essential to fill them in to ensure accurate processing.
