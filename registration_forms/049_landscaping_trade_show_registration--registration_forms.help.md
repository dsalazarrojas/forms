# Landscaping Trade Show Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration of exhibitors and attendees for the Landscaping Trade Show.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by providing exhibitor information in the "exhibitor_info" field.
2. Enter details about the exhibitor in the "exhibitor_details" field.
3. Provide attendee information in the "attendee_info" field.
4. Enter details about the exhibit in the "exhibit_details" field.
5. Enter submission details in the "submission_details" field.
6. Review the submission terms in the "submission_terms" field.
7. Enter contact information in the "contact_info" field.
8. Enter company information in the "company_info" field.
9. Upload a business card in the "business_card" field.
10. Review the submission terms again in the "submission_terms_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Exhibitor Information 
* **exhibitor_info** (text, required/optional): Enter information about the exhibitor you are registering.
### Exhibitor Details 
* **exhibitor_details** (text, required/optional): Enter details about the exhibitor, such as their company name and contact information.
### Attendee Information 
* **attendee_info** (text, required/optional): Enter information about the attendee, such as their name and contact information.
### Exhibit Details 
* **exhibit_details** (text, required/optional): Enter details about the exhibit, such as its name and description.
### Submission Details 
* **submission_details** (text, required/optional): Enter submission details, such as submission date and time.
### Submission Terms 
* **submission_terms** (text, required/optional): Review the submission terms and conditions for the Landscaping Trade Show.
### Contact Information 
* **contact_info** (text, required/optional): Enter contact information for the exhibitor or attendee.
### Company Information 
* **company_info** (text, required/optional): Enter information about the company, such as its name and address.
### Business Card 
* **business_card** (text, required/optional): Upload a business card for the exhibitor or attendee.
### Submission Terms (2) 
* **submission_terms_2** (text, required/optional): Review the submission terms and conditions for the Landscaping Trade Show again.
