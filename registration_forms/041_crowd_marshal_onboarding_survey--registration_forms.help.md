# Crowd Marshal Onboarding Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Crowd Marshal Onboarding Survey is a crucial step in the onboarding process for new users. It helps gather essential information to ensure a smooth and successful onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Select the correct event type from the dropdown menu.
2. Fill in your name and contact details (email and phone number) accurately.
3. Provide your emergency contact's name, phone number, and email address if required.
4. Choose the correct relationship with your emergency contact (if applicable).
5. If necessary, specify the relationship with your emergency contact in more detail.
6. Enter the event date and start time.
7. Provide the event location.
8. Choose your roles in the onboarding process.
9. Enter the onboarding date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Type
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of event you will be participating in. This will help us understand the context of your onboarding.

### Name
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your full name to help us identify you.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your email address for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.

### Event Date
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event you will be participating in.

### Event Time
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the start time of the event.

### Event Location
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, required): Provide the address of the event location.

### Roles
* **Roles** (`roles`, select_multiple, required): Choose your roles in the onboarding process. This will help us assign tasks and resources accordingly.

### Emergency Contact Name
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Provide the name of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Email
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, required): Provide the email address of your emergency contact.

### Relationship
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, required): Choose the type of relationship with your emergency contact (if applicable).

### Other (specify)
* **Other (specify)** (`emergency_contact_relationship_other`, text, required): If your relationship with your emergency contact is not listed, please specify it in this field.

### Emergency Contact Note
* **Emergency Contact Note** (`emergency_contact_note`, note, required): Provide any additional information about your emergency contact.

### Onboarding Date
* **Onboarding Date** (`onboarding_date`, date, required): Enter the date you completed this onboarding survey.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and thoroughly.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult the relevant documentation or reach out to your team lead for clarification.
* This form should only be completed once per user.
