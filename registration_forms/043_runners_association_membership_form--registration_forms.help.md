# Runners Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Runners Association Membership Form is used to collect information from users who wish to become a member of the Runners Association. This form is used to gather necessary details for the application and review process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields, and click submit. Ensure all fields are accurate, as they will be used for the association's internal records.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Runners Association Membership Form** (User Input): 
  This is the title of the form. No input is required here, just a heading.
* **Membership Application** (Membership Application): 
  Please enter a brief description of why you wish to become a member of the Runners Association.
* **Email** (email): 
  Enter your valid email address that you use for correspondence.
* **Phone** (phone): 
  Enter your phone number, if applicable, for easier contact.

## Tips
- Be honest and clear in your application.
- Ensure your contact information is accurate.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the association administrators.
