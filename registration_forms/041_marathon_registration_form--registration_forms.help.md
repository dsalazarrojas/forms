# Marathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a marathon event. It collects participant information, contact information, emergency contact information, participant availability, shirt preferences, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including name and contact details.
2. Enter your emergency contact information, if necessary.
3. Select your shirt preferences, such as size, color, and style.
4. Indicate if you want to receive a finisher shirt and bib.
5. Specify your participant division (Marathon, Half-marathon, or 5K).
6. Add any additional participant notes or comments.
7. Choose emergency services you may require, such as medical assistance or a language interpreter.
8. Select your finisher sheriff's details, including name, phone number, and email.
9. Enter any additional finisher sheriff bio information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter your name and contact information.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required): Enter your contact information.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter emergency contact information, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (`emergency_contact_info`, number, optional): Enter emergency contact info, if necessary.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter phone number of emergency contact.
* **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, date, optional): Enter participant availability.
* **Participant Availability Time** (`participant_availability_time`, time, optional): Enter participant availability time.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shirt size (Small, Medium, Large).
* **Shirt Color** (`shirt_color`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shirt color (Blue, Red, Green).
* **Shirt Style** (`shirt_style`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred shirt style (Short sleeve, Long sleeve).
* **Finisher Shirt** (`finisher_shirt`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you want to receive a finisher shirt.
* **Finisher Bib** (`finisher_bib`, select_one, optional): Indicate if you want to receive a finisher bib.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional participant comments.
* **Emergency Services** (`emergency_services`, select_multiple, optional): Choose emergency services you may require.
* **Finisher Sheriff** (`finisher_sheriff`, select_one, optional): Select your finisher sheriff's gender (Male, Female).
* **Finisher Sheriff Name** (`finisher_sheriff_name`, text, optional): Enter finisher sheriff's name.
* **Finisher Sheriff Name 2** (`finisher_sheriff_name_alt`, text, optional): Enter alternative finisher sheriff's name.
* **Finisher Sheriff Phone** (`finisher_sheriff_phone`, text, optional): Enter finisher sheriff's phone number.
* **Finisher Sheriff Email** (`finisher_sheriff_email`, email, optional): Enter finisher sheriff's email.

* **Participant Division 3** (`participant_division_alt3`, select_one, optional): Select alternative participant division (Marathon, Half-marathon).
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional participant comments.
