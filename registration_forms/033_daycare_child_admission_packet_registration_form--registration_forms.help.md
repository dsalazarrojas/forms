# Daycare Child Admission Packet Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about a child's personal details, emergency contact, and medical information for our daycare facility. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about your child. Please provide as much information as possible to help us provide better care and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "First Name" field with your child's first name.
2. Enter your child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your child's email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your child's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your child's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field. Use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. In the "Emergency Contact" field, provide the name and relationship of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.
7. Enter your child's address in the "Address" field.
8. Write any relevant medical information for your child in the "Medical Information" field.
9. Describe the relationship between your child and the emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): This is your child's first name. Enter your child's first name as it is written on their birth certificate or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): This is your child's last name. Enter your child's last name as it is written on their birth certificate or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): This is your child's email address. Enter the email address where you can be reached for any communication.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required/optional): This is your child's phone number. Enter a valid phone number where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): This is your child's date of birth. Select the date of birth from the calendar in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): This is the name of the person to be contacted in case of an emergency. Enter the full name of the person and a brief description of your relationship with them.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): This is your child's residential address. Enter the full address where your child lives.
* **Medical Information** (`medical_info`, text, required/optional): This is any relevant medical information about your child. Enter any medical conditions, allergies, or special needs that we should be aware of.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required/optional): This is a brief description of your relationship with the emergency contact person. For example, "Father", "Mother", "Guardian", etc.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about your child.
- If your child has any medical conditions or allergies, please provide as much information as possible.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
