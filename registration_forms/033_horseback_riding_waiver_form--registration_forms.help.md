# Horseback Riding Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect rider information, emergency contacts, and waiver details for horseback riding activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the `Rider Information` page with your details.
	* Provide your name and any other relevant information for identification.
2. Add your emergency contact's details on the `Emergency Contact` page.
	* Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
3. Answer the waiver questions on the `Waiver Information` page.
	* Read and sign the waiver form to confirm your understanding and consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Rider Information (Rider Information, Text, Optional)**: Provide your name, address, and any other relevant rider information.
* **Emergency Contact (Emergency Contact, Text, Optional)**: Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number so we can reach them in case of an emergency.
* **Waiver Information (Waiver Information, Text, Optional)**: Read and agree to the waiver terms. This section includes liability, medical, and other important details. Make sure to understand the terms before signing.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
- If you are under 18, have a parent or guardian sign the waiver for you.
- Keep a copy of the signed waiver for your records.
