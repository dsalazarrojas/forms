# equipment_retrofitting_initiative_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment retrofitting initiative registration form is used to register the equipment submitted for retrofitting. It collects information from the user about the equipment, the retrofitting initiative, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your user information on page 1.
2. Enter the equipment details, including type and model, on page 2.
3. Select the retrofitting initiative from the list on page 3.
4. Enter the contact information on page 4.
5. Fill in the submitter information, including name, phone, and email on page 5.
6. Enter the equipment serial number, year, and submitted date on page 6.
7. Enter any additional comments or notes on page 7.
