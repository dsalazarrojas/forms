# Webinar Organization Permission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for organizations to provide their permission to share data and record audio/video content with us for a specific Webinar. It helps us understand your organization's policies and ensure that we handle your data and content accordingly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. If you wish to share your data with us, select "Allow" in the "Permission to share data" field.
3. If you wish to record audio content, select "Allow" in the "Permission to record audio" field.
4. If you wish to record video content, select "Allow" in the "Permission to record video" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: A text field to enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Permission to share data**: Select "Allow" if you give us permission to share your data with our team.
* **Permission to record audio**: Select "Allow" if you give us permission to record audio content with your name and/or voice.
* **Permission to record video**: Select "Allow" if you give us permission to record video content with your face and/or name.
