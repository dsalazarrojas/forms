This is a help guide for the "entrepreneurship_program_enrollment_form". The form is designed to capture information from participants who are interested in enrolling in an entrepreneurship program. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary details from participants, including their category, name, email, phone number, address, and other relevant information. This form is not a requirement for all participants, but rather an optional way to provide additional context and support.

## entrepreneurship_program_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The entrepreneurship program enrollment form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in enrolling in the program. This information will help us to better understand their needs and provide relevant support.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Select the category of the participant (Registration Forms or Other).
2. Enter the participant's name and description in the text fields.
3. Enter the participant's email address, phone number, and address.
4. Select any assigned tool(s) that the participant is interested in using.
5. Choose a submission date and time for the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (Select One): The category is used to group participants into different categories (Registration Forms or Other). This will help us to better understand the purpose of the participant's submission.
* **Participant Name** (text): Enter the name of the participant who is interested in enrolling in the program.
* **Description** (text): Provide a brief description of the participant's interest in the program.
* **Participant Email** (email): Enter the participant's email address where they can be reached.
* **Participant Phone** (text): Enter the participant's phone number where they can be reached.
* **Participant Address** (text): Enter the participant's address for reference purposes.
* **Form Category ID** (text): This field is currently not used, please ignore it.
* **Assigned Tool** (Select Multiple): Select any tools that the participant is interested in using for the program. Currently, the available tools are chatjimmy and Other.
* **Submission Date** (date): Choose the date when the participant's submission was made.
* **Submission Time** (time): Choose the time when the participant's submission was made.
* **Form IDs** (number): This field is not used, please ignore it.

## Tips
* Please make sure to provide accurate information to help us better understand your needs.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
* This form is optional, but providing your submission will help us to better serve you and the program.
