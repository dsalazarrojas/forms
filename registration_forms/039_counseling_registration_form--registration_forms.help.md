# Counseling Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Counseling Registration Form is used to collect registration information for clients who are seeking counseling services. This form will help us create a record of your registration details, which will be used to facilitate the counseling process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Date of Birth) and provide accurate information.
2. If you want to provide additional information for the counselor, you can write it in the Counselor Note field.
3. Leave the Address field empty if you don't want to provide it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Please enter your first name, as it will be used to create your registration record.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name, as it will be used to create your registration record.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Please enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): You can provide your address if you want to share it with the counselor. This is not required.
* **Counselor Note** (`counselor_note`, text, optional): You can write any additional information for the counselor that will help them assist you better.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
