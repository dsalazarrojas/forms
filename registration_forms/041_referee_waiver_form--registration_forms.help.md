# Referee Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for referees to waive liability related to participation in events or activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully.
2. Enter your first and last name.
3. Provide your address and email.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Enter your date of birth.
6. Select your referee training certification.
7. Enter your referee certification number.
8. Select your referee certification expiration date.
9. Provide the emergency contact's name and information.
10. Select whether you allow the referee to list the emergency contact.
11. Describe your relationship with the referee.
12. Sign the referee signature section.
13. Sign and date the official signature section.
14. Date and sign the waiver of liability section.
15. Complete the waiver signed by section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Address**: Enter your full address.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Date of last referee training**: Enter the date of your last referee training in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Referee Training Certification**: Select your referee training certification.
* **Referee Certification Number**: Enter your referee certification number.
* **Referee Certification Expiration**: Enter your referee certification expiration date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Emergency Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Allow Referee to list emergency contact**: Select whether you allow the referee to list the emergency contact.
* **How do you know the Referee**: Describe your relationship with the referee.
* **Referee Signature**: Sign your name as the referee.
* **Official Signature**: Sign and date your name as the official.
* **Date of Signature**: Date and sign the official signature section.
* **Waiver of Liability**: Select the waiver terms and conditions you agree to.
* **Date of Waiver**: Enter the date of the waiver in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Signed by**: Enter your name as the one signing the waiver.
* **Referee Waiver Signed By Title**: Enter your title as the one signing the waiver.
* **Date of Waiver Signing**: Enter the date of the waiver signing in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Referee Waiver Signed By Abbreviation**: Enter an abbreviation for your title as the one signing the waiver.
