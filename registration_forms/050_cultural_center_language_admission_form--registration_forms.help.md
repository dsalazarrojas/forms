# Cultural Center Language Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Center Language Admission Form is used to collect essential information from individuals who wish to take a language course at the cultural center. This form is required for course enrollment and administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Start by filling out the first page of the form, which includes the following sections:
    * Language: Please select whether you have taken the language course you wish to enroll in or not.
    * Language Course: If you have taken the course, please indicate whether you would like to take it again.
2. On the second page of the form, provide your:
    * Name (First Name and Last Name)
    * Email Address
    * Phone Number
3. On the third page of the form, enter the following information:
    * Date of Birth
    * Birth Country
    * Passport Number (if applicable)

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **Language**: 
    * This field asks whether you have taken the language course you wish to enroll in. Select 'Yes' if you have taken the course, 'No' otherwise.
* **Language Course**: 
    * If you have taken the course, you can select 'Yes' if you wish to take it again. If you haven't taken the course, leave this field blank.
* **Name**:
    * Please enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on our records.

### Page 2
* **Email**:
    * Please enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone**:
    * Please enter your phone number for follow-up and contact.
* **First Name**:
    * Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name**:
    * Please enter your last name.

### Page 3
* **Date of Birth**:
    * Please enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Birth Country**:
    * Please enter the country of your birth.
* **Passport Number**:
    * Please enter your passport number (if you have one).
* **First Name**:
    * Please enter your first name again (this will be used as a confirmation).

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you have any issues or questions, do not hesitate to contact us at [contact information].
