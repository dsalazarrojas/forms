# Children Theater Audition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for parents or guardians to register their children for auditions with a theater company.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the child information section, providing the child's first name, last name, date of birth, age, and contact details.
2. Provide the parent or guardian's name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select the child's experience and abilities, including their singing and dancing ability levels, previous experience, and any special skills or talents.
4. Specify the child's availability for rehearsals and performances.
5. Optionally, select any role preferences or character interests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Child First Name**: Enter the child's first name.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the child's last name.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Age**: Enter the child's current age in years.
* **Gender**: Select the child's gender (Male, Female, Non-binary, or Other).
* **Child Email Address**: Enter the child's email address, if applicable.
* **Parent or Guardian Name**: Enter the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email Address**: Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone Number**: Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Street Address**: Enter the parent or guardian's street address.
* **City**: Enter the parent or guardian's city.
* **State**: Enter the parent or guardian's state.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the parent or guardian's zip code.
* **Previous Theater or Performance Experience**: Select the child's previous experience (School plays, Community theater, Professional experience, or Other).
* **Please Describe Any Relevant Experience**: Enter any additional details about the child's experience.
* **Singing Ability Level**: Select the child's singing ability level (Non-singer, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Dancing Ability Level**: Select the child's dancing ability level (No experience, Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Role Preferences or Character Interests**: Select any role preferences or character interests (Lead role, Supporting role, Ensemble, or Any role).
* **Prepared Song or Monologue**: Enter any prepared song or monologue the child will perform.
* **Special Skills or Talents**: Enter any special skills or talents the child has.
* **Availability for Rehearsals and Performances**: Select the child's availability for rehearsals and performances (Weekdays only, Weekends only, Both weekdays and weekends, or Limited availability).

Note: Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and thoroughly to ensure a smooth and efficient registration process.
