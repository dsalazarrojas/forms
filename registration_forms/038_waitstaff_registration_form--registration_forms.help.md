# Waitstaff Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from waitstaff, including their applicant details, availability, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the following fields on the `Waitstaff Registration` page:

    *   `Waitstaff Registration` (Text field)
    *   `Applicant Details` (Text field)
    *   `Availability` (Text field)
    *   `Availability 2` (Text field)
    *   `Shift End` (Text field)
    *   `Shifts` (Text field)
    *   `Hire Info` (Text field)
*   Make sure to provide accurate information in each field, as this data will be used to manage the waitstaff's schedule and employment details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Waitstaff Registration** (`Text field`)

    This field is the main title of the registration form. Enter your name or a descriptive title for your registration.
*   **Applicant Details** (`Text field`)

    Enter a brief description of yourself, your experience, and any relevant background information.
*   **Availability** (`Text field`)

    Indicate your availability for work, such as your days and hours of operation. This will help managers schedule you for shifts.
*   **Availability 2** (`Text field`)

    Provide additional details about your availability, such as holidays or time off.
*   **Shift End** (`Text field`)

    Enter the end date of your availability, indicating when you will be available for work until.
*   **Shifts** (`Text field`)

    Specify the shifts you are available to work, such as morning, afternoon, or night shifts.
*   **Hire Info** (`Text field`)

    Enter any relevant hiring information, such as references, previous experience, or certifications.
*   **Additional Tips**

    Make sure to review the provided fields carefully and enter accurate information. This data will be used for scheduling and managing your waitstaff schedule.
