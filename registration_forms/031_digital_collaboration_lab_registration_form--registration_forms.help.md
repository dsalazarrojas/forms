# Digital Collaboration Lab Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Digital Collaboration Lab Registration Form is used to collect information from participants for the Digital Collaboration Lab.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your **Email** address to allow us to contact you about your registration.
3. Provide your **Organization** and **Position Title** for your professional reference.
4. Enter your **Address** if you want to receive any mail or packages.
5. Fill in your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
6. Select your preferred **Registration Form** type.
7. Choose if you have been **Assigned a Tool**.
8. Select the **Event Type** of the Digital Collaboration Lab (e.g., Workshop, Event, Conference, or Webinar).
9. Check if you are interested in the **Workshop Topic**.
10. Select the **Event Length** of the Digital Collaboration Lab (e.g., 1 hour, 1 day, 2 days, 3 days, 4 days, or More than 4 days).
11. Enter the **Event Ending Date** and **Event Start Date** for the Digital Collaboration Lab.
12. Select the **Event Start Time** and **Event End Time** for the Digital Collaboration Lab.
13. Fill in **Event Name** for your Digital Collaboration Lab.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address to allow us to contact you about your registration.
* **Organization**: Enter your current or associated organization.
* **Position Title**: Enter your current or associated position title.
* **Address**: Enter your address if you want to receive any mail or packages.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Registration Form**: Select the type of registration form you are interested in.
* **Assigned Tool**: Check if you have been assigned a tool for the Digital Collaboration Lab.
* **Event Type**: Select the type of event you are participating in (e.g., Workshop, Event, Conference, or Webinar).
* **Workshop Topic**: Check if you are interested in the workshop topic.
* **Event Length**: Select the length of the Digital Collaboration Lab (e.g., 1 hour, 1 day, 2 days, 3 days, 4 days, or More than 4 days).
* **Event Ending Date**: Enter the ending date of the Digital Collaboration Lab.
* **Event Start Date**: Enter the start date of the Digital Collaboration Lab.
* **Event Start Time**: Enter the start time of the Digital Collaboration Lab.
* **Event End Time**: Enter the end time of the Digital Collaboration Lab.
* **Event Name**: Enter the name of the Digital Collaboration Lab you are participating in.

Note: The fields marked as **Required** are mandatory. Please ensure you fill them in accordingly.
