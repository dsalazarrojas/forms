## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants in a photo contest. Users will be asked to provide their contact details, photo submission information, and consent to the terms and conditions of the contest.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Phone number and Photo Title.
3. Select a Category for your photo (Landscape, Cityscape, Wildlife, Portrait, or Still Life).
4. Provide the Date and Time when the photo was taken.
5. Enter the Location where the photo was taken.
6. Add a Description of your photo.
7. If you are the Photographer, please enter your name. If not, leave this field blank.
8. Confirm whether you would like your photo to be part of an exhibition.
9. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Photo Title** (`photo_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a title for your photo.
* **Category** (`category`, select_one, required/optional): Select a category for your photo (Landscape, Cityscape, Wildlife, Portrait, or Still Life).
* **Date Taken** (`date_taken`, date, required/optional): Enter the date when the photo was taken.
* **Time Taken** (`time_taken`, time, required/optional): Enter the time when the photo was taken.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location where the photo was taken.
* **Description** (`description`, text, required/optional): Add a description of your photo.
* **Photographer** (`photographer`, text, required/optional): If you are the photographer, enter your name. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Exhibition** (`exhibition_id`, select_one, required/optional): Confirm whether you would like your photo to be part of an exhibition ('True' or 'False').

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to the help guide or contact the contest organizers.
* Review the terms and conditions carefully before submitting your registration.
