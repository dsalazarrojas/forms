# Tour Operator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to collect information about tour operators for internal purposes. It is intended for use by tour operators to provide their details to a specific entity, such as a travel agency or a travel platform.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the General Information section with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your company name in the Tour Operator field.
3. Choose your contact method in the Contact field.
4. Add any additional notes in the Notes field.
5. Enter your email address in the Email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
7. Enter your business address in the Address field.
8. Enter your city in the City field.
9. Enter your state or province in the State/Province field.
10. Enter your country in the Country field.
11. Enter your website in the Website field.
