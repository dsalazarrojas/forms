# Win A Survival Kit Contest Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in the Win A Survival Kit Contest. It is essential to provide accurate and complete details to increase your chances of winning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. Provide your contact information (email address and phone number).
3. Briefly describe the contest.
4. Acknowledge your understanding of the contest rules and terms of service.
5. Choose how you would like to receive the winner notification (email, phone, or both).
6. Add any additional comments you'd like to share.
7. Confirm your contact information and contest details.
8. Reiterate your understanding of the terms of service.
9. Confirm how you would like to receive the winner notification.
10. Add any additional information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your ID.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required): Enter your email address and phone number.
* **Contest Information** (`Contest Information`, `text`, required): Briefly describe the contest.
* **Contest Rules** (`Contest Rules`, `checkbox`, required): Acknowledge your understanding of the contest rules.
* **Terms of Service** (`Terms of Service`, `checkbox`, required): Acknowledge your understanding of the terms of service.
* **Winner Notification** (`Winner Notification`, `select_one`, required): Choose how you would like to receive the winner notification (email, phone, or both).
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, `text`, optional): Add any other information you'd like to share.
* **Confirm Contact Information** (`Confirm Contact Information`, `text`, optional): Enter your email address and phone number again.
* **Confirm Contest Information** (`Confirm Contest Information`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe the contest again.
* **Confirm Terms of Service** (`Confirm Terms of Service`, `checkbox`, optional): Reiterate your understanding of the terms of service.
* **Confirm Winner Notification** (`Confirm Winner Notification`, `select_one`, optional): Confirm how you want to receive the winner notification.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, optional): Add any additional information you'd like to share.
* **Additional Information is optional** and only if you have something else to add, please do, otherwise it's not required.
