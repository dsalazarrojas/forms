# Join The Bold Leaders Community - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will help you understand the purpose and process of filling out the Join The Bold Leaders Community form.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Fill out each field according to the instructions provided.
3. Double-check your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Application Form
* **Application Form (text)**: This is a brief text field where you can provide a short description of your application.
* **Contact Details (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Motivations and Interests (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide a brief description of your motivations and interests.
* **Membership Type (select_one)**: Choose the type of membership that best describes your affiliation with the Bold Leaders Community. Options include Personal, Professional, and Business.
* **Member Type (select_multiple)**: Choose the type of member that best describes your status within the Bold Leaders Community. Options include Individual and Group.
* **Team Members (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide the names and contact information of your team members (if applicable).
* **Join Date (date)**: This is a date field where you can select the date you wish to join the Bold Leaders Community.
* **Join Time (time)**: This is a time field where you can select the time you wish to join the Bold Leaders Community.
* **Location (select_one)**: Choose the location where you are based. Options include USA, Canada, and Other.
* **Email (email)**: This is a text field where you can provide your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide your phone number.
* **Website (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide your website URL (if applicable).
* **Bio (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide a brief bio or description of yourself.
* **Motivation (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide a motivation or reason for joining the Bold Leaders Community.
* **Skills (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide your relevant skills or expertise.
* **Experience (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide your relevant experience or background.
* **References (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide references or testimonials from past collaborations or projects.
* **References Link (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide a link to your references or portfolio.
* **How did you hear about us? (select_one)**: Choose the source that led you to the Bold Leaders Community. Options include Facebook, LinkedIn, and Other.
* **Additional Comments (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information.
* **Agreement (select_one)**: Confirm your agreement to the Bold Leaders Community's terms and conditions. Options include True and False.
* **Contact Agreement (select_one)**: Confirm your agreement to be contacted by the Bold Leaders Community. Options include True and False.
* **Payment Terms (select_one)**: Confirm your agreement to the Bold Leaders Community's payment terms. Options include True and False.
* **Contact Terms (select_one)**: Confirm your agreement to the Bold Leaders Community's contact terms. Options include True and False.
* **Contact Agreement Text (text)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional comments or information related to your contact agreement.
