# Tour Guides Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration and membership purposes to collect personal details and membership preferences from users interested in joining the Tour Guides Club, a community of travel enthusiasts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide a valid email address in the "email" field.
4. Create a password in the "password" field.
5. Confirm your password in the "confirm_password" field.
6. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
7. Select one or more membership preferences from the "membership_preferences" field.
8. Choose your preferred language from the "preferred_language" field.
9. Select one or more tour preferences from the "tour_preferences" field.
10. Choose your payment terms from the "payment_terms" field.
11. Select a payment method from the "payment_method" field.
12. Agree to the terms and conditions by reading and confirming the "agree_terms" field.
13. Confirm your membership by reading and confirming the "confirm_membership" field.
