# Eco Supply Vendor Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from vendors who want to register with us as eco supply providers. The form will help us verify the vendor's details and certifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the vendor's details, including contact person, email, phone number, and address.
2. Select the types of certifications you have obtained (e.g. energy, carbon, water, waste).
3. Click submit to save your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the person who will be the primary point of contact for your company.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Address**: Enter your company's physical address.
* **Products**: Briefly describe the eco-friendly products or services your company offers.
* **Certifications**:
	+ **Yes/No**: Select the types of certifications you have obtained. For example, "Yes" for energy, "No" for carbon, and so on.
* **Other Certifications**:
	+ **Yes/No**: Select "Yes" if you have any other certifications not listed above.
* **Submit**: Click this button to save your application.
* **Cancel**: Click this button to cancel your application.
* **Back to**: These buttons will allow you to navigate between sections of the form.
