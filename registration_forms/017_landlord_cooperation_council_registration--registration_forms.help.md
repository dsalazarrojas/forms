# Landlord Cooperation Council Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from landlords interested in joining the Landlord Cooperation Council. Please complete this form to create a record of your participation and interests in the council.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please make sure you are not already registered with the council. If you are unsure, please check with the council's administrators.
2. Fill in your name and contact details, including phone number and email address.
3. Select your interests, choosing one or more from the list provided.
4. Provide a note with any additional comments or information you would like to share with the council.
5. If you have any further comments, you may enter them in the additional note field.
6. Select a registration time for your interests.
7. If you have any other interests or comments, you may enter them in the additional note field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Landlord Name** (`landlord_name`, text, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed in the council's records.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, select_multiple, required): Select one or more contact methods (phone, email) to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_one, required): Choose one or more of the interests listed to indicate your areas of interest within the council (e.g., Financial Assistance, Property Management).
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required): Please indicate your consent to participate in the council by typing "I consent" (or similar).
* **Landlord Cooperation Council Registration** (`landlord_cooperation_council_registration`, date, required): This is a note field for entering your registration date (optional, but recommended for your records).
* **Landlord Cooperation Council Registration Time** (`landlord_cooperation_council_registration_time`, time, required): Select a time for your registration (optional, but recommended for scheduling purposes).
* **Landlord Cooperation Council Registration Note** (`landlord_cooperation_council_registration_note`, note, required): Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Landlord Cooperation Council Registration Phone** (`landlord_cooperation_council_registration_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for the council to contact you.
* **Landlord Cooperation Council Registration Interests** (`landlord_cooperation_council_registration_interests`, select_one, required): Choose one or more interests to indicate your areas of interest within the council (e.g., Financial Assistance, Property Management).
* **Landlord Cooperation Council Registration Consent** (`landlord_cooperation_council_registration_consent`, note, required): Please indicate your consent to participate in the council by typing "I consent" (or similar).
