# Day Of Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Day Of Service Registration form is designed for individuals to register for volunteer work. This form is used to collect information about the volunteer, including their personal details, emergency contact information, and medical conditions. The form also includes a section for volunteers to agree to the terms and conditions of service.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Provide your date of service in the "date" field.
4. Enter the time of service in the "time" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
7. Select your volunteer position in the "volunteer_position" field.
8. Enter the dates you are available to serve in the "service_dates" field.
9. Enter the times you are available to serve in the "service_times" field.
10. Provide any additional details about your volunteer experience in the "volunteer_details" field.
11. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact" field.
12. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_number" field.
13. Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_relationship" field.
14. If applicable, mention any medical conditions that may impact your ability to volunteer in the "medical_notes" field.
15. If applicable, list any dietary restrictions you have in the "dietary_restrictions" field.
16. If applicable, list any allergies you have in the "allergies" field.
17. If applicable, enter any additional emergency contact information in the "emergency_contact_info" field.
18. Read and agree to the "volunteer_agreement" field.
19. Review and agree to the "terms_and_conditions" field.
20. Enter the date and time of your service in the "date_of_service" and "time_of_service" fields, respectively.
21. Enter the location of your service in the "location_of_service" field.
22. Enter the duration of your service in the "service_duration" field.
23. Select the person you will be reporting to in the "assigned_to" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (first_name)** (`text`, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (last_name)** (`text`, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, required): Enter your date of service.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, required): Enter your time of service.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Volunteer Position (volunteer_position)** (`select_one`, required): Select your volunteer position (Volunteer, Team Member, Team Lead, Team Assistant).
* **Service Dates (service_dates)** (`date`, required): Enter the dates you are available to serve.
* **Service Times (service_times)** (`time`, required): Enter the times you are available to serve.
* **Volunteer Details (volunteer_details)** (`note`, required): Provide any additional details about your volunteer experience.
* **Emergency Contact (emergency_contact)** (`text`, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Number (emergency_contact_number)** (`text`, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship (emergency_contact_relationship)** (`text`, required): Indicate the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Medical Notes (medical_notes)** (`note`, required): If applicable, mention any medical conditions that may impact your ability to volunteer.
* **Dietary Restrictions (dietary_restrictions)** (`text`, required): If applicable, list any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Allergies (allergies)** (`text`, required): If applicable, list any allergies you have.
* **Emergency Contact Info (emergency_contact_info)** (`text`, required): If applicable, enter any additional emergency contact information.
* **Volunteer Agreement (volunteer_agreement)** (`note`, required): Read and agree to the volunteer agreement.
* **Terms and Conditions (terms_and_conditions)** (`note`, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of service.
* **Date of Service (date_of_service)** (`date`, required): Enter the date of your service.
* **Time of Service (time_of_service)** (`time`, required): Enter the time of your service.
* **Location of Service (location_of_service)** (`text`, required): Enter the location of your service.
* **Service Duration (service_duration)** (`text`, required): Enter the duration of your service.
* **Assigned To (assigned_to)** (`select_one`, required): Select the person you will be reporting to (chatjimmy, chatjimmy2, chatjimmy3, chatjimmy4, chatjimmy5).

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Ensure that you have selected the correct volunteer position and assigned to person.
* Review the volunteer agreement and terms and conditions carefully before agreeing to them.
* If applicable, provide any relevant medical notes, dietary restrictions, allergies, and emergency contact information.
* Be clear and concise in providing additional information in the "volunteer_details" and "emergency_contact_info" fields.
