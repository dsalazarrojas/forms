# festival participation records release form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The festival participation records release form is designed to collect information about festival participants for the purpose of keeping accurate participant records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and ensure that you have the necessary information before filling it out.
2. Complete each field with the required information. Note that email and phone numbers are only required if you would like to be contacted.
3. If you are filling out this form for someone else, please make sure they are aware that their information is being collected.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, `text`, required/optional): This field is used to collect general participant information. Please enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether or not you agree to participate in the festival.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about your participation in the festival.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): If you would like to be contacted, please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): If you would like to be contacted, please enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter your time of arrival in the format "hh:mm".
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address.
* **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your zip code.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Country** (`country`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **Additional Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, required/optional): Enter any additional notes about the participant.
* **Confirm Participant** (`confirm_participant`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose whether or not you confirm that you are a participant.
