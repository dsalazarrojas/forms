# Martial Arts Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Martial Arts Course Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in enrolling in a martial arts course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1.  Please provide the following information:
	*   Personal details in the Personal Info section.
	*   Your current belt ranking in the Belt Ranking section (if applicable).
	*   Contact information for yourself and emergency contacts in the Contact Info and Emergency Contact sections, respectively.
	*   Any relevant health or medical information in the Health/Medical Info section.
	*   Your preferred course package in the Course Packages section (if applicable).
	*   Your preferred payment method in the Payment Method section (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*  **Personal Info** (`Personal Info`, `text`, required): Please enter your name, email, and any other relevant personal details.
*  **Belt Ranking** (`Belt Ranking`, `select_one`, optional): If you already have a belt ranking within the martial arts discipline you are enrolling in, please select it from the provided options. Otherwise, select 'None'.
*  **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, `text`, optional): Please enter your contact information, including phone number and address, if applicable.
*  **Emergency Contact** (`Emergency Contact`, `text`, optional): Please provide the contact information for an emergency contact person.
*  **Health/Medical Info** (`Health/Medical Info`, `text`, optional): Please enter any relevant health or medical information that will help the course administrators to better understand your needs and limitations.
*  **Course Packages** (`Course Packages`, `select_one`, optional): Please choose your preferred course package from the provided options.
*  **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_one`, optional): Please select your preferred payment method from the provided options.
