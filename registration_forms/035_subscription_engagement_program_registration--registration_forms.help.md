# Subscription Engagement Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Engagement Program Registration form is designed for users to register their interest in participating in the program. This form collects demographic information about the user, including their contact preferences and language preference. The collected data will be used to tailor the program's communication and engagement activities to the user's preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your first and last name in the designated fields.
* Enter your email address in the required email field. Ensure the email address is valid.
* If you have a phone number, enter it in the designated field. This information is optional.
* Choose how you like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In-person) using the Select One field.
* Indicate your consent to being contacted through email, phone, and SMS.
* Select your preferred language and communication channel.
* Choose your preferred frequency of engagement (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* If you have a date of birth, enter it in the designated field. This information is optional.
* If you have a preferred time of day, enter it in the designated field. This information is optional.
* Add any additional notes or comments in the Note field. This information is optional.
* Confirm your email address to prevent errors.
* Confirm your phone number to prevent errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): A valid email address is required.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it here.
* **Contact Method** (`preferred_contact_method`, select_one, optional): Choose how you like to be contacted (Email, Phone, or In-person).
* **Consent to Email** (`consent_to_email`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your consent to being contacted through email.
* **Consent to SMS** (`consent_to_sms`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your consent to being contacted through SMS.
* **Consent to Phone** (`consent_to_phone`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate your consent to being contacted through phone.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language.
* **Preferred Channel** (`preferred_channel`, select_one, optional): Select your preferred channel of communication.
* **Preferred Frequency** (`preferred_frequency`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred frequency of engagement (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly).
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If you have a date of birth, enter it here.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time of day, enter it here.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Email Confirmation** (`email_confirmation`, email, optional): Confirm your email address to prevent errors.
* **Phone Confirmation** (`phone_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your phone number to prevent errors.
