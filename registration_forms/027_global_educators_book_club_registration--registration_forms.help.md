# Global Educators Book Club Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global Educators Book Club Registration form is designed to collect information about new members, including their details, availability, school information, and any additional notes. This information helps the book club understand each member's profile, allowing for better organization of events and activities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each section carefully to ensure you're filling out all relevant information.
2. Fill out all required fields (indicated as "Required" in the field explanation).
3. Be thorough and accurate when providing information.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Details** (`member_details`, text, optional): Please provide your name and any other relevant details about yourself as a member.
* **Member Availability** (`member_availability`, text, optional): Describe your availability for events, meetings, or other activities related to the book club. This will help the organizers schedule events that suit your needs.
* **School Information** (`school_info`, text, optional): Provide your school's name and any other relevant information about your school or your role within it.
* **Member Notes** (`member_notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or comments about yourself as a member. This can be anything from your reading interests to your contact information.

## Tips
* Review each section carefully to ensure accuracy and completeness.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask an organizer or another member for clarification.
* This form is intended for personal use, so please keep it confidential and do not share it with others.
