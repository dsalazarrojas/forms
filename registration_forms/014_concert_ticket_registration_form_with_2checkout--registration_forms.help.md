# Concert Ticket Registration Form With 2Checkout - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering for a concert event with secure 2Checkout payment. Please fill in the required information to complete the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the concert event you wish to attend from the "Concert Event" dropdown.
2. Enter the date of the concert event in the "Concert Date" field.
3. Choose the ticket tier level you prefer from the "Ticket Tier" dropdown.
4. Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase in the "Ticket Quantity" field.
5. Fill in your personal contact information, including your full name, email address, phone number, and street address.
6. Choose your preferred payment gateway from the "Payment Gateway" dropdown.
7. Agree to the payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Concert Event**: Select the concert event you wish to attend from the dropdown list.
* **Concert Date**: Enter the date of the concert event using the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Ticket Tier**: Choose the ticket level you prefer (Standard Entry, Premium Seating, VIP Access, or Platinum Package).
* **Ticket Quantity**: Enter the number of tickets you wish to purchase.
* **Full Name**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address for receipt and ticket information.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address**: Enter your street address.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province of your residence.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the ZIP code of your residence.
* **Country**: Choose your country of residence from the dropdown list.
* **Order Summary**: Review your order before proceeding to payment (optional).
* **Unit Price**: Enter the price per ticket (only visible for administrators).
* **Subtotal**: Calculate the subtotal before taxes (only visible for administrators).
* **Tax Rate**: Enter the tax rate percentage (only visible for administrators).
* **Total Due**: Display the total amount due (only visible for administrators).
* **Payment Gateway**: Select the payment gateway you prefer (2Checkout Credit Card, 2Checkout PayPal, or 2Checkout Bank Transfer).
* **Payment Provider**: Select the 2Checkout payment provider from the dropdown list.
* **Card Information**: Enter your card details securely.
* **Payment Terms**: Agree to the payment terms.
* **Complete Registration**: Choose to complete and pay or save and continue later.
