# Vendor Payment Portal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect payment information from vendors to enable registration on our payment portal. Please provide accurate details to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information before proceeding.
2. Complete each field carefully, as indicated in the field explanations below.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Page**: Please select this option to continue to the next page of the form.
  - **Contact Info**: Enter your contact email address where we can reach you for any updates or inquiries related to your registration.
  - **Payment Method**: Choose from the available payment options: credit card, bank transfer, or PayPal. You can select one or more options.
  - **Tax ID**: Enter your tax identification number for accounting purposes.
  - **Agreement**: Select an agreement type: agreement 1, agreement 2, or agreement 3. This will help us understand your payment terms.
  - **Notes**: Add any additional information you'd like us to know about your payment details.
  - **Phone**: Enter your phone number for any further contact.
  - **Next Page**: Select the next page of the form to proceed with your registration. Please choose from the available options: next page 1, next page 2, or next page 3.
  - **Submit**: Once you've completed all the fields, click this button to submit your registration.

## Tips
- Be sure to double-check your entries before submitting the form to avoid errors.
- If you're unsure about any field, please ask our support team for clarification.
- Please provide accurate and up-to-date information to ensure seamless integration with our payment system.
