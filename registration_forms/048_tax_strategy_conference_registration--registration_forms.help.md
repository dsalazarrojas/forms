# Tax Strategy Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tax Strategy Conference registration form is designed for attendees to provide their information and preferences for the event.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Event Details in the **Event Details** section.
2. Provide your Attendee Details in the **Attendee Details** section.
3. Select your Session Preferences in the **Session Preferences** section.
4. Specify your Tax Requirements in the **Tax Requirements** section.
5. Enter your Contact Details in the **Contact Details** section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Details** (event_details, text, required: false): Enter the name of the event you are registering for. This will help us verify the event and ensure you are registering for the correct event.
* **Attendee Details** (attendee_details, text, required: false): Enter your name, company, and job title to help us identify you as a registered attendee.
* **Session Preferences** (session_preferences, text, required: false): Select the sessions you are interested in attending. This will help us assign you to the right sessions and optimize our speaker selection.
* **Tax Requirements** (tax_requirements, text, required: false): Specify any tax requirements you have, such as a tax ID number, for our records.
* **Contact Details** (contact_details, text, required: false): Enter your contact information, including your email address, phone number, and any other relevant details.
