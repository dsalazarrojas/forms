# Financial Risk Modeling Session Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new financial risk modeling session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out the session information fields**:
	* `session_info`: Provide a brief description of the session.
	* `participant_info`: Enter the name and email of the participant(s) attending the session.
2. **Select the type of training**:
	* Choose the type of training you will be attending from the options provided.
3. **Enter session details**:
	* `start_date`: Enter the start date of the session (mm/dd/yyyy).
	* `end_date`: Enter the end date of the session (mm/dd/yyyy).
	* `duration`: Enter the duration of the session in hours.
	* `max_participants`: Enter the maximum number of participants allowed.
4. **Enter contact information**:
	* `contact`: Enter the email address of the contact person.
	* `phone`: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
5. **Enter session information**:
	* `location`: Enter the location of the session.
	* `notes`: Enter any additional notes about the session.
6. **Review and submit**:
	* Review all the information you have entered to ensure it is accurate.
	* Click the submit button to register the session.
