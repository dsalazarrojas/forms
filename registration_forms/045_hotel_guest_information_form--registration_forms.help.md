# Hotel Guest Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hotel Guest Information Form is a simple form designed to collect basic information from hotel guests. It is intended for guests to provide their personal details for hotel services or to be used for internal reference. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Hotel Guest Information Form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the form link to access the form.
2.  Click on the page corresponding to the "guest_details" field, and enter the guest's room number and other details as required.
3.  Click on the page corresponding to the "guest_name" field, and enter the guest's name.
4.  Click on the page corresponding to the "email" field, and enter the guest's email address.
5.  Click on the page corresponding to the "phone" field, and enter the guest's phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Details** (`guest_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the guest, such as their room preference or special requests.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the guest's name as it appears on their ID or as they like to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter the guest's email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the guest's phone number for contact purposes.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you are entering accurate and up-to-date information.
* You can enter any additional details that you think may be helpful for the guest's stay.
* If any field is marked as optional, you can skip it if it's not relevant to your guest's needs.
