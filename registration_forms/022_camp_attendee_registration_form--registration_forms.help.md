# Camp Attendee Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The "Camp Attendee Registration Form" is a crucial tool for registering campers to participate in the camp program. This form gathers essential information from attendees, including their contact details, dietary restrictions, and preferences. By submitting this form, campers will be able to sign up for the desired session and participate in the fun activities and events the camp has to offer.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the "Camp Attendee Registration Form" successfully:

1. Start by filling out the "Camper Registration Details" section, which includes the full name, age, and gender of the camper.
2. Next, provide the necessary contact information for the parent or guardian, including their name, email, and phone number.
3. Choose the preferred session and shirt size from the available options.
4. List any dietary restrictions or allergies that the camper may have.
5. Indicate the swim proficiency level of the camper.
6. If applicable, list a cabin mate request.
7. Read and agree to the terms and conditions of camp attendance.
8. Finally, select the registration date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Full Name** (`attendee_full_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of the camper.
* **Age at Start of Camp** (`attendee_age`, number, required): Enter the age of the camper at the start of the camp.
* **Gender** (`attendee_gender`, select_one, required): Select the gender of the camper (Male, Female, Non-binary / Other, Prefer not to say).
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email_registration`, email, required): Enter the email address of the parent or guardian, which will be used for all camp communications.
* **Primary Contact Phone** (`primary_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the primary phone number of the parent or guardian.
* **Select Your Preferred Session** (`preferred_camp_session`, select_one, required): Choose the preferred session from the available options.
* **T-shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size_camper`, select_one, required): Select the correct T-shirt size for the camper.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions_reg`, text, required): List any dietary restrictions or allergies the camper may have.
* **Swimming Proficiency Level** (`swimming_proficiency`, select_one, required): Select the swim proficiency level of the camper (Non-swimmer, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Cabin Mate Request** (`cabin_mate_request`, text, optional): If applicable, list a cabin mate request.
* **Participation Terms and Waiver** (`registration_terms_consent`, select_one, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of camp attendance.

By following these steps, campers will be able to successfully complete the registration form and have a great experience at the camp!
