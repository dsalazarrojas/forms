# Health Policy Advocacy Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for individuals to register for the Health Policy Advocacy Network. The purpose of this form is to gather information from members about their areas of interest, contact information, and other relevant details. This information will be used to communicate with you regarding updates and opportunities related to the network.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply select the relevant fields for each question and provide the necessary information. You can select multiple options in the `Member Enrollment`, `Areas of Policy Interest`, `Policy Areas of Interest`, and `Contact Information` fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Enrollment**: Select whether you are enrolling as a member of the Health Policy Advocacy Network. This will help us tailor our communications to you.
* **Areas of Policy Interest**: Select all areas where you are interested in learning more about or working on. This will help us connect you with relevant information and opportunities.
* **Contact Information**: Select how you would like to be contacted by the network, including whether you want to receive emails, phone calls, or text messages.
* **Member Name**: Enter your name as a member of the network.
* **Member Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Member Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Organization Name**: If you are registering on behalf of an organization, enter the name of the organization.
* **Member Position**: Enter your title or position within the organization.
* **Areas of Interest**: Select all areas where you are interested in learning more about or working on.
* **Policy Areas of Interest**: Select all policy areas where you are interested in learning more about or working on.
* **Other Areas of Interest**: Enter any other areas of interest not listed above.
* **Other Areas of Interest**: Select whether you are interested in other areas.
* **Member Contact Information**: Select how you would like to be contacted by the network.
* **Consent to be Contacted**: Select whether you consent to be contacted by the network.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Email)**: Select your preference for email contact.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Phone)**: Select your preference for phone contact.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Email Frequency)**: Select how often you would like to receive emails from the network.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Phone Number)**: Select your preference for phone contact.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Text)**: Select your preference for text contact.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Call)**: Select your preference for phone calls.
* **Consent to be Contacted (Other)**: Enter any other methods of contact you would like to be contacted by.
* **Frequency of Contact**: Select how often you would like to be contacted by the network.
* **Method of Contact**: Enter any other method of contact you would like to be contacted by.
* **Submission**: Provide any additional comments or information about your registration.
