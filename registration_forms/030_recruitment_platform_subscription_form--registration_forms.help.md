# recruitment_platform_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The recruitment platform subscription form is used to collect basic information about users and job seekers who want to register on the platform.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in the required fields with your details.
2. If you're not sure what to fill in, you can skip fields that are not required.
3. Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Employment Status**: Select your current employment status (Employed, Unemployed, or Student).
* **Job Title**: Enter your current or desired job title.
* **Job Description**: Enter a brief description of your current or desired job.

## Tips

* Be accurate when filling in your details to avoid any confusion or delay in processing your registration.
* If you have any questions or issues with the form, please contact us.
* The information collected through this form will be used for marketing or lead generation purposes.
* You are not required to complete this form, and you can opt out anytime.
