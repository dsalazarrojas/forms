# Parents Night Out Child Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parents Night Out Child Registration Form is designed to collect information about your child for a fun and safe experience during our Parent's Night Out event. Please fill out this form to ensure we have the necessary details to take care of your child during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field to select the corresponding input type.
2. Enter the required information for each field.
3. Review your entries before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your name as the parent/guardian.
- **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your child's name.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional):
	+ Select the date of the event you are registering for.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional):
	+ Choose the time of the event you are registering for.
- **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional):
	+ Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute updates or changes.
- **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional):
	+ Enter your email address so we can keep you informed about the event.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out for accurate processing.
- If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
- Review your entries before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
