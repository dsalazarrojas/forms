# Microlearning Design Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register attendees for the Microlearning Design Workshop. Fill out this form to secure your spot and provide the necessary details for your workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields to the best of your ability.
2. Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Workshop Details (Workshop Details, text, optional)**: This field is for providing any additional details about your workshop experience, such as specific interests or questions.
- **Attendee Details (Attendee Details, text, optional)**: This field is for providing any additional information about yourself that you would like to share with the workshop organizers.
- **Session Preferences (Session Preferences, text, optional)**: This field is for specifying your session preferences, if you have any specific needs or requirements for your learning experience.
- **Requirements (Requirements, text, optional)**: This field is for outlining any specific requirements or needs that you have for your workshop experience, such as accessibility needs.
- **Email (Email, email, optional)**: This field is for providing your contact email, which will be used for communication and confirmation purposes. We will use this email to send you important information and updates about the workshop.
- **Workshop Date (Workshop Date, date, optional)**: This field is for selecting a date for the workshop that suits you best. Please note that this field is optional, and if left blank, we will automatically assign you a date based on availability.
