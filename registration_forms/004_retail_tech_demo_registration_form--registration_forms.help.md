# retail_tech_demo_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects personal and company information from individuals interested in attending a demo session for a retail technology demonstration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields with your information and choose your demo interests and session schedule as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
• **First Name** (text, optional): Please enter your full first name.
• **Last Name** (text, optional): Please enter your full last name.
• **Email** (email, optional): Enter a valid email address for communication regarding the demo session.
• **Company** (text, optional): Enter your company name, if applicable.
• **Job Title** (text, optional): Enter your job title or role in your company.
• **Demo Interests** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more demo sessions that interest you.
• **Follow up consent** (select one, optional): Choose 'True' to receive follow-up information regarding the demo session.
• **Demo session** (time, optional): Enter the time slot you are interested in, if applicable.
• **Schedule** (time, optional): Enter the schedule you prefer for the demo session, if applicable.
• **Notes** (note, optional): Add any additional comments or information regarding your registration.

## Tips
- Ensure the email address is valid and easily contactable.
- Be accurate about your demo session interests and preferences.
- If you've registered previously, do not register again.
- Contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx) for any form-related questions or concerns.
