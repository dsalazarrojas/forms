# nexus_registration_form_english_spanish - Help Guide
## Purpose
The nexus registration form is used to collect information about students for administrative purposes. This form helps track student enrollment and registration details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your nationality in the "Nationality" field.
6. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Church" field.
7. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Program" field.
8. Enter your student number in the "Student Number" field.
9. Select "Yes" or "No" for multiple options in "Student ID" field.
10. Select "Yes" or "No" for multiple options in "Academic Program" field.
11. Enter your level in the "Level" field.
12. Select "High" or "Low" for "Academic Level" field.
13. Answer "Yes" or "No" for multiple options in "Program Academic" field.
14. Enter your student status in the "Student Status" field.
15. Enter the date you joined in the "Date Joined" field.
16. Enter the time you joined in the "Time Joined" field.
17. Enter the date you graduated in the "Graduation Date" field.
18. Enter the time you graduated in the "Graduation Time" field.
19. Enter your student status English in the "Student Status English" field.
20. Enter your church English in the "Church English" field.
21. Enter your program English in the "Program English" field.
22. Enter your level English in the "Level English" field.
23. Enter your academic program English in the "Academic Program English" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, text, required/optional): Select your nationality.
* **Church** (`church`, select_one, required/optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your church affiliation.
* **Program** (`program`, select_one, required/optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" to indicate your academic program participation.
* **Student Number** (`student_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your student number.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for multiple options.
* **Academic Program** (`academic_program`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select "Yes" or "No" for multiple options.
* **Level** (`level`, text, required/optional): Enter your level.
* **Academic Level** (`academic_level`, select_one, required/optional): Select "High" or "Low" for academic level.
* **Program Academic** (`program_academic`, select_multiple, required/optional): Answer "Yes" or "No" for multiple options.
* **Student Status** (`student_status`, text, required/optional): Enter your student status.
* **Date Joined** (`date_joined`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you joined.
* **Time Joined** (`time_joined`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you joined.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you graduated.
* **Graduation Time** (`graduation_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you graduated.
* **Student Status English** (`student_status_english`, text, required/optional): Enter your student status English.
* **Church English** (`church_english`, text, required/optional): Enter your church English.
* **Program English** (`program_english`, text, required/optional): Enter your program English.
* **Level English** (`level_english`, text, required/optional): Enter your level English.
* **Academic Program English** (`academic_program_english`, text, required/optional): Enter your academic program English.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
* If you need help or have questions, contact the administrative office.
* Please review all fields carefully before submission.
