# Camp Volunteer Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Camp Volunteer Registration form is a crucial tool to collect essential information from potential volunteers to provide a safe, efficient, and effective volunteering experience. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please complete this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your application is processed smoothly.
2. Fill out each field completely and correctly. If a field has a specific requirement (e.g., date or email), ensure the input matches the format specified.
3. For text fields, provide a clear and concise response.
4. If you're unsure about any field, please contact the camp administration for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`vol_full_name_reg`, text, required): Please enter your full name, including your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (`vol_email_address_reg`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive important updates and notifications from the camp.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`vol_phone_number_reg`, text, required): Provide your primary phone number for contact and communication purposes.
* **Home Address** (`vol_home_address_reg`, text, required): Enter your home address to ensure we can reach you in case of an emergency.
* **Date of Birth** (`vol_dob_reg`, date, required): Enter your date of birth for background screening purposes.
* **Areas of Interest or Special Skills** (`vol_skills_interests_reg`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas of interest or special skills you possess that align with the camp's programs and activities.
* **Previous Experience with Youth Programs** (`vol_previous_experience_youth`, text, optional): Share any relevant experience you have with youth programs or camps.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`vol_emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Provide the name and phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency while you're volunteering.
* **Medical Allergies or Conditions** (`vol_medical_allergies_concerns`, text, required): If you have any medical allergies or conditions, please list them here. If none, type "None".
* **Consent to Background Check** (`vol_background_check_consent`, select_one, required): Select "I Consent" to allow the camp to conduct a standard background check.
* **Volunteer Participation Agreement** (`vol_participation_agreement_waiver`, select_one, required): Select "I Agree and Accept" to agree to the volunteer terms and liability waiver.
* **Volunteer Electronic Signature** (`vol_digital_signature_reg`, text, required): Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Date of Registration** (`vol_reg_date_timestamp`, date, required): The date and time your registration is completed.
