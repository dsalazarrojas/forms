# Climate Resiliency Project Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register climate resiliency projects. It helps us understand and track project details, such as organization, project name, and funding information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields for organization information, including organization name, type, and address.
2. Enter the primary contact person's name and email address.
3. Provide a project name and description.
4. Select the primary resiliency focus and climate hazards addressed.
5. Enter the project start and end dates.
6. Provide the total project budget and currency.
7. If applicable, add funding sources and project partners.
8. Finally, describe the expected outcomes and metrics of the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`Organization Name`, required): Enter the name of your organization.
* **Organization Type** (`Organization Type`, required): Choose the type of organization (Government, Nonprofit, Business, Community Group, Educational, Healthcare, or Other).
* **Location/Address** (`Location/Address`, required): Enter the address of your organization.
* **Website** (`Website`, optional): Enter the website of your organization, if applicable.
* **Primary Contact Name** (`Primary Contact Name`, required): Enter the name of the primary contact person.
* **Job Title** (`Job Title`, optional): Enter the job title of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address** (`Email Address`, required): Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, required): Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Project Name** (`Project Name`, required): Enter the name of your project.
* **Project Description** (`Project Description`, required): Describe your project in brief.
* **Primary Resiliency Focus** (`Primary resiliency focus`, required): Select all applicable resiliency focus areas (Climate adaptation, Disaster preparedness, Infrastructure protection, Community preparedness, Biodiversity conservation, Resource management, Risk reduction, Emergency response, or Other).
* **Primary Climate Hazards** (`Primary climate hazards`, required): Select all applicable climate hazards (Flooding, Drought, Extreme heat, Storms/Hurricanes, Wildfires, Sea level rise, Erosion, or Other).
* **Project Start Date** (`Project Start Date`, required): Enter the start date of your project.
* **Expected Project End Date** (`Expected Project End Date`, required): Enter the expected end date of your project.
* **Total Project Budget** (`Total Project Budget`, required): Enter the total budget of your project.
* **Currency** (`Currency`, optional): Enter the currency of your project budget, if applicable.
* **Funding Sources** (`Funding sources`, optional): Enter the funding sources for your project, if applicable.
* **Project Partners or Collaborators** (`Project partners or collaborators`, optional): Enter any project partners or collaborators, if applicable.
* **Expected Outcomes and Metrics** (`Expected outcomes and metrics`, required): Describe the expected outcomes and metrics of your project.
