# Inclusive Tourism Innovation Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Inclusive Tourism Innovation Summit Registration form is used to gather information from attendees about their participation in the summit. By filling out this form, you will be able to provide essential details about yourself, your accessibility requirements, and your interests for the summit.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out each section as thoroughly and accurately as possible.
- You will be asked to provide details about yourself, such as accessibility needs, session interests, and dietary preferences.
- Make sure to submit your registration form once you have filled out all the required and optional sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Details (1)**: Enter your name and other details about yourself. This section is not required but provides additional context for us to know you better.
* **Accessibility Needs (2)**: If you have any special accessibility requirements, such as a physical disability or language barriers, please select 'Yes' for this field. If not, select 'No'. This will help us to provide a more inclusive environment for you.
* **Session Interests (3)**: Let us know which sessions interest you the most. You can select one or more of the options provided.
* **Dietary Preferences (4)**: If you have any dietary restrictions or preferences, please select 'Yes' or 'No'. This will help us to accommodate your needs during the summit.
* **Submit (5)**: Once you have filled out all the required and optional sections, click 'Submit' to send your registration details to us.
