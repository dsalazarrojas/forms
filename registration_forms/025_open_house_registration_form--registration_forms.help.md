# Open House Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Open House Registration Form is used to collect information from visitors or potential clients when they visit an open house event. This form helps us gather details about their identity, visit information, and specific visit details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the third field, ensuring it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
5. Enter the **Date Of Visit**, following the format mm/dd/yyyy.
6. Enter the **Time Of Visit**, in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
7. If applicable, enter your **Agent Name**, the person who brought you to the open house event.
8. Enter your **Property Address**, the address of the property you're visiting.
9. Select your **Visitor Type** from the options: **Buyer**, **Lease**, **Rent**, or **Other**.
10. Select your **Visitor Details** from the options: **Single family**, **Multi family**, or **Other**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
- **Date Of Visit** (`date_of_visit`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date of visit following the format mm/dd/yyyy.
- **Time Of Visit** (`time_of_visit`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time of visit in 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00).
- **Agent Name** (`agent_name`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the name of the agent who brought you to the open house event.
- **Property Address** (`property_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address of the property you're visiting.
- **Visitor Type** (`visitor_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your visitor type from the options: **Buyer**, **Lease**, **Rent**, or **Other**.
- **Visitor Details** (`visitor_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your visitor details from the options: **Single family**, **Multi family**, or **Other**.
