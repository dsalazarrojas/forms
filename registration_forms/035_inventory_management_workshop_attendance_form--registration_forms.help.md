# inventory_management_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for the inventory management workshop. Please complete it to confirm your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (Event Name, participant_name, email, phone, and workshop_name).
2. Enter your participant information correctly and accurately.
3. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are attending.
* **event_date** (`2`, `date`, not required): Select the date of the workshop you are attending.
* **event_time** (`3`, `time`, not required): Select the time of the workshop you are attending.
* **participant_name** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your name as a participant.
* **email** (`5`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **phone** (`6`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Workshop Name** (`7`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the workshop you are attending.
* **participant_details** (`8`, `text`, not required): Enter any additional details about your participation.
