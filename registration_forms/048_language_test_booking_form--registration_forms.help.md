# language_test_booking_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The language test booking form is designed to collect information from users for language tests. This form is used to book appointments for language tests, allowing users to choose a language, schedule, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred language from the options provided.
2. Enter your name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Choose a schedule for your appointment.
6. If desired, book an additional appointment by selecting a second schedule and date.
7. Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
8. Add any additional information you would like to provide.
9. Select your preferred time for your appointment.
10. Confirm your agreement to the terms and agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Select Language** (French, English, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, Japanese): Choose the language for which you would like to book the test.
* **Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address for communication and confirmation.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Select Schedule** (9am-5pm, 5pm-9pm, 10am-6pm, 11am-7pm, 12pm-8pm, 1pm-9pm): Choose the preferred schedule for your appointment.
* **Book an appointment** (optional): Select a date for an additional appointment.
* **Choose Language** (French, English, Spanish, Chinese, Arabic, Japanese): This option allows you to choose an additional language for the second appointment.
* **Additional Info**: Enter any additional information you would like to provide.
* **I agree to terms and conditions** (True, False): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions.
* **Notes**: Enter any notes or comments you would like to provide.
* **Enter Phone** (optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes (this is the second instance of the phone field).
* **Enter Email** (optional): Enter your email address (this is the second instance of the email field).
* **Schedule2** (9am-5pm, 5pm-9pm, 10am-6pm, 11am-7pm, 12pm-8pm, 1pm-9pm): Choose the preferred schedule for your second appointment.
* **Appointment2** (optional): Select a date for your second appointment.
* **Select Time** (optional): Select the time for your second appointment.
* **Additional Info2** (optional): Enter any additional information you would like to provide for the second appointment.
* **Terms Agreement2** (True, False): Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions for the second appointment.
* **Notes2** (optional): Enter any notes or comments you would like to provide for the second appointment.
