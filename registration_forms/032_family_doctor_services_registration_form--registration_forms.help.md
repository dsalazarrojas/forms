## Purpose
This form is used to register patients with family doctors, capturing vital information about the patient and the doctor they are registering with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the patient's name in the "Patient Name" field.
2. Enter the patient's date of birth in the "Patient Date of Birth" field, in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
3. Select whether the patient has primary insurance coverage by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Patient Primary Insurance" dropdown menu.
4. Select whether the patient has additional insurance coverage by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Patient Additional Insurance" dropdown menu.
5. Enter the doctor's name in the "Doctor Name" field.
6. Select the doctor's title by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the "Doctor Title" dropdown menu.
7. Enter the doctor's contact information in the "Doctor Contact" field.
8. Enter the doctor's phone number in the "Doctor Phone" field.
9. Enter the patient's email address in the "Patient Email" field.
10. Enter the patient's phone number in the "Patient Phone" field (this is optional).
11. Enter the patient's address in the "Patient Address" field.
12. Enter the doctor's address in the "Doctor Address" field.
13. Select the services the doctor provides by choosing multiple options from the "Doctor Services" dropdown menu.
14. (Optional) Enter any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Enter the patient's full name.
* **Patient Date of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Patient Primary Insurance**: Select "Yes" if the patient has primary insurance coverage, "No" otherwise.
* **Patient Additional Insurance**: Select "Yes" if the patient has additional insurance coverage, "No" otherwise.
* **Doctor Name**: Enter the doctor's name.
* **Doctor Title**: Select the doctor's title (e.g. "Dr.", "MD", etc.).
* **Doctor Contact**: Enter the doctor's contact information.
* **Doctor Phone**: Enter the doctor's phone number.
* **Patient Email**: Enter the patient's email address.
* **Patient Phone**: Enter the patient's phone number (optional).
* **Patient Address**: Enter the patient's address.
* **Doctor Address**: Enter the doctor's address.
* **Doctor Services**: Select the services the doctor provides.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the patient or doctor.

**Tips:**

* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check the patient's and doctor's addresses for accuracy.
* If you're unsure about any field, consult with the patient or doctor directly.
* This form is for internal use only; please do not share sensitive patient information with third parties.
