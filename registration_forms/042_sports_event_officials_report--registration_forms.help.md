# sports_event_officials_report - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_event_officials_report form is used to gather information on sports event officials for better event management and official coordination.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the Event Details section, which includes the Event ID field.
2. In the Officials Present section, select all officials who were present at the event.
3. Provide any relevant Observations or Decisions made during the event.
4. Add any Comments or notes about the event.
5. Sign the form to confirm completion.
6. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event ID** (required): Enter the unique identifier for the sports event.
- **Officials Present** (select_multiple): List all officials who were present at the event:
  - Officials A
  - Officials B
  - Officials C
- **Observations** (text): Describe any notable observations or actions observed during the event.
- **Decisions** (text): Note any decisions made during the event.
- **Comments** (text): Add any additional comments or notes about the event.
- **Signature** (text): Sign the form to confirm completion.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields, including Event ID.
- List all officials present in the Officials Present section.
- Keep Observations and Decisions concise and focused on the event details.
- Use the Comments field for any additional notes or explanations.
- Sign the form to confirm that it is completed accurately and thoroughly.
