# Literary Involvement Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Literary Involvement Interest Form" is a tool to gauge interest in literary events, such as writing workshops, book readings, or other author-related activities. This form helps users express their interest in attending or participating in such events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Start by filling in the "User Input" field with any additional information you'd like to share about your interest in literary events.
2. Enter your name in the "name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "email" field.
4. For any specific events you're interested in, describe them in the "event_name" field.
5. Specify the date of the events you're interested in by filling in the "event_date" field.
6. If applicable, indicate the time of the events you're interested in by filling in the "event_time" field.
7. If the event location is relevant, specify it in the "event_location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (`user_input`, `text`, optional): Please enter any additional information about your interest in literary events.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **event_name** (`event_name`, `text`, optional): Describe the specific event(s) you are interested in. This can be a workshop, book reading, or other author-related activity.
* **event_date** (`event_date`, `text`, optional): Indicate the date of the event you are interested in.
* **event_time** (`event_time`, `time`, optional): If applicable, provide the time of the event you are interested in. Format should be 24-hour time (e.g., 13:30).
* **event_location** (`event_location`, `text`, optional): If the event location is known, specify it here.
