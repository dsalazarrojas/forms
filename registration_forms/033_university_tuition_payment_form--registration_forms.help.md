# University Tuition Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registering University tuition payments. It captures essential information regarding the student's name, payment method, date, amount, and payment status.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your User Name in the User Name field.
2. Select the relevant Payment Method from the options provided.
3. Provide the Payment Date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Enter the Amount Due.
5. Select the Payment Status.
6. If applicable, provide additional payment details and payment method.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as a registered student or staff member.
* **Payment Method** (Select One): Choose from Cash, Check, or Bank Transfer for your payment method. 
* **Payment Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date of the payment in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Amount Due** (Number, Optional): Enter the exact amount due for the payment.
* **Payment Status** (Select One): Select from Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid.
* **Payment Method Details** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional details regarding your payment method, such as payment reference number.
* **Student ID** (Text, Optional): Enter your Student ID number if available.
* **University Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the University where the payment is being made.
* **Payment Amount** (Number, Optional): Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Term** (Select Multiple): Select one or more payment terms, such as Semesters 1-3 or Full Year.
* **Payment Method 2** (Select One): If you're making multiple payments, select a different payment method.
* **Payment Date 2** (Time, Optional): Enter the time of the second payment.
* **Amount Due 2** (Text, Optional): Enter the amount due for the second payment.
* **Payment Status 2** (Select One): Select the status for the second payment, such as Paid or Unpaid.
* **Additional payment methods can be used for tracking and verification purposes only and are not mandatory fields for completion of form.**

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check the amount and payment details for accuracy.
* Select all applicable payment terms for correct payment tracking.
