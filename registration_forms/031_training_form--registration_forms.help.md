# Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Training Form is designed to gather information from staff about their training experiences and status. This information will help the organization to improve and optimize its training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Review each field carefully and fill in the information as prompted.
2.  Ensure you are entering accurate and truthful information.
3.  If you're unsure about any field, please ask your supervisor or the form administrator for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Staff ID
*   **Staff ID**: Enter your staff ID number, a unique identifier assigned to you by the organization.
    *   **Type:** number
    *   **Required:** No

### Training Course
*   **Training Course**: Select 'Yes' if you have participated in a training course or 'No' if you haven't.
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Required:** No

### Training Date
*   **Training Date**: Enter the date you attended the training course.
    *   **Type:** Date
    *   **Required:** No

### Staff Name
*   **Staff Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No

### Staff Phone
*   **Staff Phone**: Enter your office phone number or a contact number where you can be reached.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No

### Email
*   **Email**: Enter your official work email address.
    *   **Type:** Email
    *   **Required:** No

### Department
*   **Department**: Enter your department name.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No

### Course Status
*   **Course Status**: Enter any relevant details about the training course you have attended or are attending.
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No

### Course Level
*   **Course Level**: Enter the level of the course you have attended or plan to attend (e.g., basic, intermediate, advanced).
    *   **Type:** Text
    *   **Required:** No

### Staff Notes
*   **Staff Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments about your training experience.
    *   **Type:** Note
    *   **Required:** No

### Assigned Tool
*   **Assigned Tool**: Select 'Yes' if a tool has been assigned to you as part of your training.
    *   **Type:** Select One
    *   **Required:** No

## Tips
*   Please double-check your entries before submitting the form.
*   If you have any issues or concerns, reach out to your supervisor or the form administrator.
*   This form is for internal use only, and the data collected will not be shared with external parties.
