# First Aid Trainers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from individuals who want to join the first aid trainers team. It will help us understand their qualifications, experience, and availability to be part of our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "What is your name?" field. Please make sure to spell it correctly.
2. Provide a valid email address in the "Email address" field. This will be used as your contact information.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone number" field. This will help us contact you if needed.
4. Select your role in the first aid team in the "Role in the first aid team" field. You can choose from Team Lead, Trainer, Support Staff, or Other (please specify).
5. Enter the number of years you have experience in first aid training in the "Years of first aid training experience" field.
6. Select any relevant first aid training certifications you have in the "Relevant first aid training certifications" field. You can choose from Basic Life Support, Advanced Life Support, Pediatric First Aid, or Other (please specify).
7. Check the "Availability to be on call" checkbox if you are available to be on call.
8. Select your preferred contact method in the "Preferred contact method" field. You can choose from Email, Phone, or SMS/Text Message.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (Text): Enter your full name.
* **Email address** (Email): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone number** (Number): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Role in the first aid team** (Select Multiple): Choose your role in the team:
	+ Team Lead: You are a team leader or have a leadership role.
	+ Trainer: You are a certified first aid trainer.
	+ Support Staff: You provide support to the team.
	+ Other (please specify): If your role is not listed, choose this option and describe your role.
* **Years of first aid training experience** (Number): Enter the number of years you have experience in first aid training.
* **Relevant first aid training certifications** (Select Multiple): Choose any relevant certifications you have:
	+ Basic Life Support: A certification in basic life support techniques.
	+ Advanced Life Support: A certification in advanced life support techniques.
	+ Pediatric First Aid: A certification in first aid for pediatric patients.
	+ Other (please specify): If you have a certification not listed, choose this option and describe it.
* **Availability to be on call** (Checkbox): Check this box if you are available to be on call.
* **Preferred contact method** (Select One): Choose how you want to be contacted:
	+ Email: We will contact you via email.
	+ Phone: We will contact you via phone.
	+ SMS/Text Message: We will contact you via SMS or text message.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* Be as accurate as possible when filling out the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
