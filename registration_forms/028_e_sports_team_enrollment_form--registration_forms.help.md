# e_sports_team_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e_sports_team_enrollment_form is used for users to enroll in e-sports teams, providing essential details about themselves, their gaming experience, and team preferences to facilitate a smooth team setup.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your details in the **User Details** section.
2. Share your gaming experience in the **Gaming Experience** section.
3. Specify your team preferences in the **Team Preferences** section.
4. Add any additional details about your team in the **Team Details** section.
5. Click **Submit** to send your application.
6. Verify your application by typing a confirmation message in the **Confirm** field.
7. Enter a confirmation message in the **Confirm Message** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (`user_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name and any other identifying information relevant to your e-sports team enrollment.
* **Gaming Experience** (`gamer_experience`, text, required: false): Describe your gaming experience, including your favorite games, skill levels, and areas of expertise.
* **Gaming Details** (`gaming_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your gaming experience, such as your playing style or strategies.
* **Team Preferences** (`team_preferences`, text, required: false): Specify your team preferences, including your ideal team size, roles, and communication styles.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional details about the team you are enrolling in, such as its name, location, or goals.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required: false): Click this button to send your application.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, required: false): Type a confirmation message to verify your application.
* **Confirm Message** (`confirm_message`, text, required: false): Enter a message to confirm your application.

## Tips
* Ensure you provide clear and concise answers to all fields for a smooth team enrollment process.
* Double-check your answers before submitting to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
