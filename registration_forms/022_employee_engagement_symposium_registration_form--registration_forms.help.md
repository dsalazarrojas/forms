# Employee Engagement Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect attendee information for the Employee Engagement Symposium. It's necessary for us to understand who you are, what type of event you'd like to attend, and your availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Attendee Information" page.
2. Fill out the required fields: Name, Email, Phone, Job Title, Department, and Company.
3. Choose the Event Type (Conference, Meeting, Seminar, or Other).
4. Select one or multiple Shifts (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All).
5. If you're attending, please indicate your Attendance (True or False).
6. Review and submit your form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Information**: This section is used to capture your basic details.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Title**: Enter your current job title.
* **Department**: Enter your department name.
* **Company**: Enter your company name.
* **Event Type**: Choose the type of event you'd like to attend (Conference, Meeting, Seminar, or Other).
* **Shift**: Select one or multiple shifts you're available for (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or All).
* **Shift Time**: If you select "All", this field is not required.
* **Attendance**: Indicate whether you'll be attending (True or False).
