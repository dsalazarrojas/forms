# Community Group Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for community group leaders to provide information necessary for their group's membership renewal. It ensures that all required data is collected accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Fill out all required fields** carefully, paying attention to hints for each field.
2. **Check your answers** before submitting to ensure accuracy.
3. **Submit the form** once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Group Name** (`group_name`): Enter your community group's official name.
* **Group ID Number** (`group_id`): Enter your existing group ID number.
* **Contact First Name** (`contact_first_name`): Enter your legal first name.
* **Contact Last Name** (`contact_last_name`): Enter your legal last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`): Enter your primary contact phone number.
* **Renewal Period** (`renewal_period`): Select the renewal period you are applying for (3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year, or 2 Years).
* **Any Changes in Membership** (`membership_changes`): Indicate if there have been any significant changes in your group's membership (No Changes, New Members Added, Members Left, Leadership Change, or Other Changes).
* **Current Member Count** (`current_member_count`): Enter the total active members in your group.
* **Activities This Period** (`group_activities`): Describe the activities your group held during the renewal period. (Optional)
* **Space Usage Satisfaction** (`space_usage`): Rate your satisfaction with the assigned space (Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Do You Need Different Space?** (`space_changes_needed`): Indicate if you need changes to your assigned space (No, Current Space is Fine, Yes, Need Larger Space, Yes, Need Smaller Space, or Yes, Different Type of Space).
* **New Requirements** (`new_requirements`): Enter any new requirements for the upcoming period. (Optional)
* **Compliance with Group Policies** (`compliance_status`): Select if your group has followed all policies (Yes, Fully Compliant, Mostly Compliant, Some Issues, or Major Issues).
* **Describe Any Issues** (`issues_description`): Explain any compliance issues your group faced.
* **Renewal Fee Amount** (`renewal_fee`): This field will be calculated automatically based on your group's membership.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`): Select how you plan to pay for the membership renewal (Credit Card, Check, Invoice, or Waiver).
* **Interested in Volunteering?** (`volunteer_interest`): Indicate if your group is interested in volunteering (Yes or No).
* **Feedback on Group Program** (`feedback`): Share any suggestions or feedback for the group program. (Optional)
* **Renewal Agreement** (`agreement`): Confirm that you agree to continue following all policies. (Yes, I Agree or Other)
