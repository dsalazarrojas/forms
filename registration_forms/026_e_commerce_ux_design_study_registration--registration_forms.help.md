# E-Commerce-UX-Design-Study-Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register for an e-commerce UX design study. It collects user information and other relevant details to help facilitate the study.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Provide accurate and valid information for each field.
3. Review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, true): Please enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Password** (`password`, `text`, true): Create a secure password for your account.
* **Confirm Password** (`confirm_password`, `text`, true): Re-enter your password to confirm it.
* **User-Title** (`user_title`, `select_one`, true): Choose your title (Mr., Mrs., or Dr.).
* **User-Phone** (`user_phone`, `text`, true): Enter your phone number.
* **Agreement** (`agreement`, `text`, true): Read and agree to the terms of the study.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, true): Enter the date.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, true): Enter the time.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, false): This field is optional and not required for registration.
* **User-Agreed-Date** (`user_agreed_date`, `date`, false): This is the date you agreed to participate.
* **User-Agreed-Time** (`user_agreed_time`, `time`, false): This is the time you agreed to participate.
* **User-Agreed-Note** (`user_agreed_note`, `note`, false): This is an optional field for notes.
* **Agreement-2** (`agreement_2`, `text`, true): Read and agree to the terms of the study again.
* **Select** (`select`, `select_multiple`, true): Choose one or more options.
* **Date-2** (`date_2`, `date`, true): Enter another date.
* **Time-2** (`time_2`, `time`, true): Enter another time.
* **Select-2** (`select_2`, `select_one`, true): Choose one option.
* **Date-3** (`date_3`, `date`, true): Enter a third date.
* **Time-3** (`time_3`, `time`, true): Enter a third time.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, false): This is a submit button, but it's not required for registration.
* **Submit-2** (`submit_2`, `text`, false): This is another submit button, also not required for registration.

Please note that some fields may not be required for registration, but they are still necessary for the study. Ensure you provide accurate and valid information for all fields.
