# Application Transfer Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Application Transfer Form is designed for students to provide information about their current and desired academic status when transferring to a new academic institution.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your Student Name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your Student ID Number.
3. Enter your Email Address.
4. Enter your Phone Number.
5. Select the "Current Enrollment Status" from the options provided.
6. Enter the "Number of Credits/Units Completed."
7. Enter the "Cumulative GPA."
8. Select the "Intended Transfer Semester" from the options provided.
9. Enter the "Intended Transfer Year."
10. Enter the "Estimated Transferable Credits."
11. Select the "What is your primary reason for transferring?" from the options provided.
12. Enter "Additional Transfer Details" if necessary.
13. Select the status of "Official Transcript attached?" and "Transfer essay attached?" from the options provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Student ID Number** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your student ID number issued by your current institution.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Current Enrollment Status** (`current_enrollment_status`, select_one, required): Select your current enrollment status from the options provided.
* **Number of Credits/Units Completed** (`credits_completed`, number, required): Enter the number of credits or units you have completed.
* **Cumulative GPA** (`cumulative_gpa`, number, required): Enter your cumulative GPA.
* **Intended Transfer Semester** (`transfer_semester`, select_one, required): Select the semester you plan to transfer.
* **Intended Transfer Year** (`transfer_year`, number, required): Enter the year you plan to transfer.
* **Estimated Transferable Credits** (`transferable_credits`, number, required): Enter the estimated transferable credits.
* **What is your primary reason for transferring?** (`reason_for_transfer`, select_one, required): Select the reason for your transfer from the options provided.
* **Provide additional transfer details** (`additional_transfer_details`, text, required): Enter any additional details about your transfer.
* **Official Transcript attached?** (`transcript_attached`, select_one, required): Select the status of your official transcript.
* **Transfer essay attached?** (`essay_attached`, select_one, required): Select the status of your transfer essay.
* **Intended Program at Transfer Institution** (`intended_transfer_program`, text, required): Enter the program you plan to study at the transfer institution.
* **Current School or University Name** (`current_school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your current institution.
* **Current Program or Major** (`current_program`, text, required): Enter the program or major you are currently studying.
* **Intended Transfer Institution** (`intended_transfer_institution`, text, required): Enter the name of the institution you plan to transfer to.
* **Transfer Reason** (`transfer_reason`, note, required): Enter any additional details about your transfer.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled in completely.
- If you need help or have questions, contact your academic advisor or the relevant authority.
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
