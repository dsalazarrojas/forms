# integrative-medicine-training-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to register for the integrative medicine training program. Please fill out this form to submit your information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each section with the required and optional information as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User info** (1, text, required/optional): This is the first step in the form, where you'll enter your personal information.
- **Course info** (2, text, required/optional): Enter details about the course you're registering for, if applicable.
- **Emergency contact** (3, text, required/optional): If there's someone we should contact in case of an emergency, please provide their name and contact information.
- **Workshop terms** (4, text, required/optional): This field is for any additional comments or information about your participation in the workshop.
- **Payment info** (5, text, required/optional): Enter information about your payment, if you've already made it or plan to make it.
- **Payment method** (6, select_one, required/optional): Choose your preferred payment method from the available options.
  - Option 1
  - Option 2
  - Option 3
- **Payment date** (7, date, required/optional): If you've already made a payment, enter the date of the payment. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
- **Payment time** (8, time, required/optional): If you've already made a payment, enter the time of the payment. Otherwise, leave this field blank.
- **Email** (9, email, required/optional): Enter your email address if you'd like to be contacted about the workshop.
- **Phone** (10, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted about the workshop.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your successful registration. If you have any issues or concerns, contact us for assistance.
