# Medical AI Regulation Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for Medical AI Regulation Awareness sessions. It collects information from attendees on their details, roles, expectations, consent, and contact details. This information will help the event organizers understand the attendees' preferences and needs, and provide a more tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name, email address, and other required fields for **Attendee Details** (id: 1).
2. Select one of the options for **AI Regulation Awareness** (id: 2) that best matches your understanding of AI regulation.
3. Choose one or more relevant roles from the options for **Roles** (id: 3).
4. Enter your expectations for the session, if any, in the **Expectations** (id: 4) field.
5. Date and time of your consent, if applicable, in the **Consent** (id: 5) field.
6. Provide your contact details in the **Contact Details** (id: 6) field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details** (id: 1, text, required): Enter your name, job title, and other contact information.
* **AI Regulation Awareness** (id: 2, select_one, required): Select your level of understanding of AI regulation, which is used to categorize and tailor the session content for you.
* **Roles** (id: 3, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more roles that apply to you, such as researcher, developer, or policymaker, to help us understand your interests and expertise.
* **Expectations** (id: 4, text, optional): Enter any specific expectations or topics you would like to be covered during the session.
* **Consent** (id: 5, date, optional): Date and time of your consent, if you have agreed to participate in the session.
* **Contact Details** (id: 6, text, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, and other contact information.

## Tips
* Please be accurate and honest when filling in your details and choices.
* If you have any specific requirements or requests, please mention them in the **Expectations** field.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification, do not hesitate to contact us using the **Contact Details** field.
