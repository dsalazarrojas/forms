# Virtual Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Training Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants interested in joining a virtual training session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your details in the Training Details section (page 1).
* Provide your information in the Participant Info section (page 2).
* Enter your contact details in the Contact Info section (page 3).
* Provide additional training information if necessary (page 4).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Training Details
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, `text`, optional): This section is where you can provide any details about the training you are interested in. Please write your thoughts, questions, or any other relevant information.

### Participant Info
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, optional): In this section, please provide information about yourself, including your name, title, and any other relevant participant details.

### Contact Info
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, `text`, optional): Enter your contact information, including your name, phone number, and email address, so we can reach you about the training.

### Training Info
* **Training Info** (`training_info`, `text`, optional): If you have any additional information about the training you are interested in, such as your preferred date or time, please provide it here.
