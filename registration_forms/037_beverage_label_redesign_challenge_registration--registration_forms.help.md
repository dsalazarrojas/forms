# Beverage Label Redesign Challenge Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from participants in the Beverage Label Redesign Challenge. This challenge aims to encourage designers to rethink and reimagine the labeling of various beverage categories, and this form helps us understand your background, experience, and design concept.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details, including your name, contact information, and education background.
2. Provide information about your design experience, including your proficiency in various design software and your design concept.
3. Choose the beverage category you are redesigning and describe your design concept and approach.
4. Submit your design files and specify the file format.
5. Confirm that your work is original and agree to the terms and conditions of the challenge.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contestant Name** (`contestant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`contestant_email`, `email`, required): We will use this to send you challenge updates and important information.
* **Phone Number** (`contestant_phone`, `text`, optional): This is for urgent communication or follow-up questions.
* **Age** (`age`, `number`, required): Your current age.
* **Country of Residence** (`country`, `select_one`, required): Where you currently live.
* **Current Profession** (`profession`, `text`, required): Your current job or role.
* **Design Experience Level** (`design_experience`, `select_one`, required): How much design experience you have.
* **Years of Design Experience** (`years_experience`, `select_one`, required): How many years have you been designing.
* **Highest Level of Education** (`education`, `select_one`, required): Your highest level of education completed.
* **Design Software Proficiency** (`design_software`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the design software you are proficient in.
* **Portfolio Website URL** (`portfolio_url`, `text`, optional): Link to your online portfolio.
* **Social Media Handle** (`social_media_handle`, `text`, optional): Your Instagram or Behance username.
* **Beverage Category for Redesign** (`beverage_category`, `select_one`, required): Choose the beverage category you are redesigning.
* **Design Concept Description** (`design_concept`, `text`, required): Describe your redesign concept and approach.
* **Design Inspiration** (`design_inspiration`, `text`, optional): What inspired your design concept.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, `text`, required): Who your design is intended for.
* **Design File Submission** (`file_submission`, `text`, required): Describe your submitted files or provide cloud link.
* **File Format Submitted** (`file_format`, `select_multiple`, required): Specify the file format you submitted.
* **Original Work Declaration** (`original_work`, `select_one`, required): Confirm that this is your original work.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_accepted`, `select_one`, required): Agree to the challenge terms and conditions.
* **How Did You Hear About This Challenge** (`how_heard_about`, `select_one`, optional): Where you found out about this challenge.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Any other information about your submission.
