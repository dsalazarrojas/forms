# Digital Content Provider Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for digital content providers to register their basic information, portfolio details, and onboarding status. It helps the administrators manage digital content providers efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Basic Info**:
	* Enter your name, email, contact number, and any other basic details you want to share.
2. Provide **Portfolio Info**:
	* Share your portfolio, description, and link to your work.
3. Attach a **Content Sample**:
	* Upload a sample of your content, if required.
4. Select the **Content Sample Type**:
	* Choose the type of content you uploaded (e.g., video, audio, image, or document).
5. Set the **Content Sample Size**:
	* Enter the size of the content sample you uploaded.
6. Fill in the **Onboarding Info**:
	* Enter the date and time of your onboarding.
	* Choose your onboarding status and manager's status.
7. Add **Onboarding Manager Details**:
	* Enter your manager's name, email, phone number, and notes.
8. Set the **Onboarding Review**:
	* Choose the review status of your onboarding.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Basic Info**: Enter your basic details to help us understand you better.
* **Email**: This is your email address, which we will use for communication.
* **Contact Number**: Your contact number, which we can use to reach you.
* **Portfolio Info**: Share your portfolio, so we can review your work.
* **Portfolio Description**: Describe your portfolio and its purpose.
* **Portfolio Link**: Share a link to your portfolio, if applicable.
* **Content Sample**: Upload a sample of your content, if required.
* **Content Sample URL**: If you uploaded a content sample, enter its URL.
* **Content Sample File**: If you uploaded a content sample, enter its file name.
* **Content Sample Type**: Choose the type of content you uploaded (e.g., video, audio, image, or document).
* **Content Sample Size**: Enter the size of the content sample you uploaded.
* **Onboarding Info**: Enter the date and time of your onboarding.
* **Onboarding Date**: Enter the date of your onboarding.
* **Onboarding Time**: Enter the time of your onboarding.
* **Onboarding Status**: Choose your onboarding status.
* **Onboarding Manager**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Onboarding Manager Email**: Enter your manager's email.
* **Onboarding Manager Phone**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Onboarding Manager Notes**: Enter any notes about your manager.
* **Onboarding Manager Status**: Choose your manager's status.
* **Onboarding Review Date**: Enter the date of your review.
* **Onboarding Review Time**: Enter the time of your review.
* **Onboarding Review Status**: Choose your review status.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields, marked as required.
* If you have any questions, contact us for further assistance.
* If you are unsure about any field, refer to this guide for clarification.
