# Lacrosse Team Tryout Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for players to register for a lacrosse team tryout. It collects essential information about the player, including contact details, medical conditions, and emergency contact information. The form also requires parent/guardian permission and player consent.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with accurate and complete information.
2. Provide the required fields marked as "required".
3. Review the form carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Information (1)**: Enter your name, address, phone number, and email.
	+ This is the initial contact information for the player.
* **Contact Information (2)**: Provide the contact information of the person responsible for the player.
	+ This is for emergency contact purposes.
* **When Would You Like To Attend (3)**: Choose a date for the tryout.
	+ Select the date you plan to attend the tryout.
* **At what time of day (4)**: Choose a time for the tryout.
	+ Select the time you plan to attend the tryout.
* **Skill Level (5)**: Indicate your skill level in lacrosse.
	+ Choose beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
* **Lacrosse Position (6)**: Select the position you play or are interested in.
	+ Choose Goalie, Defense, Midfielder, or Forward.
* **Parent/Guardian (7)**: Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
	+ This is for emergency contact purposes.
* **Phone (8)**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
	+ This is for emergency contact purposes.
* **Relationship (9)**: Indicate the relationship between you and the parent/guardian.
	+ Enter "Parent", "Guardian", or "Other".
* **Emergency Contact Info (10)**: Provide any additional information about the emergency contact.
	+ This can include a phone number, email, or address.
* **Medical Information (11)**: Enter any medical conditions or allergies.
	+ This is for medical purposes.
* **Do You Have Any Medical Conditions That We Should Know About? (12)**: List any medical conditions or allergies.
	+ This is for medical purposes.
* **Equipment Needs (13)**: Enter any equipment or special needs.
	+ This can include equipment or accommodations.
* **Parent/Guardian Permission (14)**: Grant permission for the player to participate in the tryout.
	+ Choose "True" to grant permission.
* **Date (15)**: Enter the date of the medical release.
	+ This is for medical purposes.
* **Signed By (16)**: Enter the name of the person signing the medical release.
	+ This is for medical purposes.
* **Parent/Guardian Signature (17)**: Sign this with the parent/guardian's signature.
	+ This is for authentication purposes.
* **Relationship Signed By (18)**: Enter your name as related to the signature.
	+ This is for authentication purposes.
* **Player Signature (19)**: Sign this with your own signature.
	+ This is for authentication purposes.
* **Contact Info Signed By (20)**: Enter your name as related to the contact information.
	+ This is for authentication purposes.
* **Medical Release Signed By Date (21)**: Enter the date of the medical release signature.
	+ This is for medical purposes.
* **Player Consent (22)**: Grant consent for the player to participate in the tryout.
	+ Choose to grant consent.
* **Parent/Guardian Consent (23)**: Grant consent for the parent/guardian to participate.
	+ Choose to grant consent.
* **Medical Info Signed By (24)**: Enter the name of the person signing the medical information.
	+ This is for authentication purposes.
* **Contact Info Signed By (2)**: Enter your name as related to the contact information.
	+ This is for authentication purposes.
* **Medical Release Signed By Date (25)**: Enter the date of the medical release signature.
	+ This is for medical purposes.

## Tips

* Be accurate and complete when filling out the form.
* Review the form carefully before submitting.
* Ensure all required fields are filled out.
* Sign all relevant fields with the required signatures.
