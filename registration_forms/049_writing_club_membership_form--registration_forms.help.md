# Writing Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Writing Club Membership Form is a required step for new members to join the club and access its benefits. The form helps the club's administrators collect essential information about new members to ensure they meet the basic requirements for membership. By completing this form, new members demonstrate their interest in joining the club and their commitment to contributing to the community.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** in the First name field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last name** in the Last name field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the Email field. This is a required field. Note that your email will be used as your login credential for the club's platform.
4. Enter your **Phone number** in the Phone number field. This is an optional field.
5. Enter your **Date of birth** in the Date of birth field. This is an optional field.
6. Enter your **Interests** in the Interests field. This is an optional field.
7. Select your **Writing genre** in the Writing genre field. You can choose one or multiple options: fiction, non-fiction, and poetry. This is an optional field.
8. Enter your **Experience** in the Experience field. This is an optional field.
9. Write a brief **Bio** in the Bio field, describing a bit about yourself and your writing style. This is an optional field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name**: Enter your first name. This field is required.
- **Last name**: Enter your last name. This field is required.
- **Email**: Enter your email address. This will be used as your login credential for the club's platform. This field is required.
- **Phone number**: Enter your phone number. This is an optional field.
- **Date of birth**: Enter your date of birth. This field is optional.
- **Interests**: Enter a brief description of your writing interests. This field is optional.
- **Writing genre**: Select one or multiple options: fiction, non-fiction, and poetry. This field is optional.
- **Experience**: Enter your writing experience in years. This field is optional.
- **Bio**: Write a brief description of yourself and your writing style. This field is optional.

## Tips
- Ensure that your email address is accurate and active.
- Make sure your writing genre options reflect your actual writing style.
- If you have any specific writing experience, include it in the Experience field.
