# Public Sector Innovation Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Public Sector Innovation Forum. It is recommended to fill it out carefully to avoid any inconvenience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Please make sure to use a valid email address.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Enter your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Type in your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
6. Enter the name of the department you belong to in the "Department" field.
7. Select the role you are playing in the event from the "Registration Type" dropdown menu.
8. If you are selecting "Other" as your role, provide a description in the "Role Description" field.
9. If you are selecting "Other" as your role, provide an additional description in the "Other Role Description" field.
10. Enter your affiliation with the event in the "Affiliation" field.
11. Check the "Registration Form Agreement" field to agree or disagree with the terms and conditions. If you select "I disagree with the terms and conditions", please provide your reason in the "Other Registration Form Agreement" field.
12. If you are providing a contact person's name, enter it in the "Contact Name" field.
13. Enter the contact person's phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
14. Enter the contact person's email address in the "Contact Email" field.
15. Add any additional information about yourself or your organization in the "Additional Info" field.
16. Select the date you submitted the form in the "Date Submitted" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please enter your first name as it is written on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please enter your last name as it is written on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to which the event team can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title as it is written on your identification.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are associated with.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Enter the department you are a part of.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select the role you will be playing in the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the event.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of registration you are applying for.
* **Role Description** (`role_description`, text, optional): If you have selected "Other" as your role, enter a description of your role.
* **Other Role Description** (`role_other_description`, text, optional): If you have selected "Other" as your role, provide an additional description of your role.
* **Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, optional): Enter your affiliation with the event.
* **Registration Form Agreement** (`registration_form_agreement`, select_multiple, optional): Check the box agreeing to the terms and conditions. If you disagree, enter a reason in the "Other Registration Form Agreement" field.
* **Other Registration Form Agreement** (`registration_form_agreement_other`, text, optional): If you select "I disagree with the terms and conditions", enter the reason here.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Add any additional information about yourself or your organization.
* **Date Submitted** (`submitted_at`, date, optional): Select the date the form was submitted.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you are registering for the correct event.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any doubts or questions, feel free to contact the event team.
