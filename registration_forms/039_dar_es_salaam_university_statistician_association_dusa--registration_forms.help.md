# dar_es_salaam_university_statistician_association_dusa_registration_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dar es Salaam University Statistician Association (DUSA) registration form is used to collect information from users who wish to register with the association.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information, which is marked as "true" in the required field.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information for each field.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (name)** (`text`, required): Enter your full name.
* **Email (email)** (`email`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone (phone)** (`text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Statistics (statistics)** (`number`, required): Enter statistical data, for example, 1, 2, 3, etc.
* **Date (date)** (`date`, required): Enter the date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Time (time)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time in the format 12:00 or 13:30.
* **Note (note)** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.
* **Select one option (select_one_field)** (`select_one`, required): Select only one option from the dropdown list.
* **Select multiple options (select_multiple_field)** (`select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Decimal (decimal)** (`number`, optional): Enter a decimal number.
* **Time 2 (time2)** (`time`, optional): Enter the time in the format 12:00 or 13:30.
* **Date 2 (date2)** (`date`, optional): Enter the date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Note 2 (note2)** (`note`, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields to avoid errors.
* Use the format dd/mm/yyyy for date and 12:00 or 13:30 for time.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure accuracy.
