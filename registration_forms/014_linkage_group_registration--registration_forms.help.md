# Linkage Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your group with us. Please fill out the form with accurate and up-to-date information about your group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the "group name" field with the name of your group.
2. Select the type of your group from the options provided.
3. Enter the location of your group.
4. Provide the email address of your group (required).
5. Enter your first name (optional).
6. Enter your last name (optional).
7. Enter the email address of each member (optional).
8. Enter the phone number of each member (optional).
9. Select the role of each member from the options provided (required).
10. Enter the name of each member (optional).
11. Enter the email address of the person submitting the form (required).
12. Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Group Name** (`group_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of your group.
- **Group Type** (`group_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the type of your group from the options: School, Nonprofit, Community Organization.
- **Group Location** (`group_location`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the location of your group.
- **Group Email** (`group_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address associated with your group.
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name.
- **Member Email** (`member_email`, `email`, optional): Enter the email address of each member.
- **Member Phone** (`member_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of each member.
- **Member Role** (`member_role`, `select_one`, required): Select the role of each member from the options: Student, Teacher, Parent, Other.
- **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of each member (not required but recommended for clarity).
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the person submitting the form.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the phone number of the person submitting the form.

## Tips
- Ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Use accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any confusion or delays in processing your registration.
