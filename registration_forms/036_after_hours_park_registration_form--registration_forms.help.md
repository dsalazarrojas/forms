# After Hours Park Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The After Hours Park Registration Form is used to register for after-hours access to parks within the park system. This form collects necessary information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for both you and other park users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections to ensure you understand what is required.
2. Fill out the form with your personal and group details.
3. Select the park you wish to visit and provide the necessary date and time information.
4. Choose your group's purpose and activities for the visit.
5. Confirm that you understand and will follow park rules and regulations.
6. Provide your contact information and emergency contact details.
7. Sign the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted for registration updates.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for contact regarding your registration.
* **Address**: Enter your residential address.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
* **Park Name**: Select the park where you wish to have after-hours access.
* **Access Date**: Enter the date when you need to visit the park.
* **Access Start Time**: Enter the time when you plan to enter the park.
* **Access End Time**: Enter the time when you plan to leave the park.
* **Purpose of Access**: Select the purpose of your visit (e.g., Photography, Stargazing, etc.).
* **Activity Details**: Describe the specific activities you plan to engage in during your visit.
* **Group Size**: Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Special Equipment**: If you are bringing any special equipment, list it here.
* **Safety and Compliance**: This section outlines the park's rules and regulations. Please review and acknowledge that you will follow them during your visit.
* **Park Rules Acknowledgment**: Confirm that you understand and will follow all park rules and regulations.
* **Noise Level Agreement**: Confirm that you will maintain appropriate noise levels that won't disturb nearby residents.
* **Waste Disposal Agreement**: Agree to pack out all waste and leave the park in its original condition.
* **Wildlife Protection Agreement**: Confirm that you will respect wildlife and not disturb natural habitats.
* **Safety Equipment**: Select any safety equipment you will bring (e.g., flashlights, first aid kit).
* **Risk Acknowledgment**: Acknowledge that after-hours park access involves inherent risks and you assume responsibility for your safety.
* **Restricted Areas**: If you need to access restricted areas, list them here.
* **Special Permits Needed**: If you require special permits, confirm and specify what you need.
* **Permit Details**: If you have special permits, provide details about them.
* **Weather Contingency Plan**: Plan for what you will do if weather conditions deteriorate during your visit.
* **Registration Confirmation**: This section confirms your registration details.
* **Terms Agreement**: Confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions for after-hours park access.
* **Information Accuracy**: Confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete.
* **Electronic Signature**: Sign to confirm your registration.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and do not hesitate to contact park staff if you have any questions or concerns.
