# Corporate Open House Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees and event hosts to facilitate the planning and execution of corporate events, such as open houses. The data collected will help the event organizers to manage event details, attendee information, and communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Attendee Name (ID: 1).
2. Enter your Email (ID: 2) for correspondence and updates.
3. Provide your Phone Number (ID: 3) for contact purposes.
4. Enter the Date (ID: 4) of the event.
5. Enter the Time (ID: 5) of the event.
6. Specify the Event Time (ID: 6) in the format 'HH:MM' or 'HH:MM AM/PM'.
7. Select the Event Date (ID: 7) from the calendar or manually enter it in the format 'DD/MM/YY'.
8. Enter your Company (ID: 8) name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
9. Enter your Job Title (ID: 9) as it appears on your business card or company directory.
10. Specify your Department (ID: 10) as it appears in your company directory.
11. Enter your Employee ID (ID: 11) as provided by your HR department or company database.
12. Enter your Company Name (ID: 12) as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
13. Enter the Job Function (ID: 13) that you perform in your company.
14. Select the Event Type (ID: 14) to indicate the type of event you are hosting or attending.
15. If necessary, specify an Other Event Type (ID: 15) in the text box.
16. Enter the Company Address (ID: 16) where the event is held.
17. Enter the Building Name (ID: 17) where the event takes place.
18. Enter the Floor Number (ID: 18) where the event is hosted.
19. Enter the Room Number (ID: 19) where the event is held.
20. Add a Registration Note (ID: 20) for any additional details or comments.
21. If necessary, add an Other Registration Note (ID: 21) in the text box.
22. Enter the Company Notes (ID: 22) for event hosts or organizers.
23. Enter the Job Notes (ID: 23) for event hosts or organizers.
24. Enter the Event Notes (ID: 24) for event hosts or organizers.
25. Select the Event Status (ID: 25) of the event (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
26. Enter the Attendee ID (ID: 26) if you are an event host or organizer.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Name (ID: 1)** (`Attendee Name`, text, required): Your name as it appears on your business card or company directory.
- **Email (ID: 2)** (`Email`, email, required): Your email address for correspondence and updates.
- **Phone (ID: 3)** (`Phone`, text, required): Your phone number for contact purposes.
- **Date (ID: 4)** (`Date`, date, required): The date of the event in the format 'DD/MM/YY'.
- **Time (ID: 5)** (`Time`, time, required): The time of the event in the format 'HH:MM' or 'HH:MM AM/PM'.
- **Event Time (ID: 6)** (`Event Time`, time, required): The time of the event in the format 'HH:MM' or 'HH:MM AM/PM'.
- **Event Date (ID: 7)** (`Event Date`, date, required): The date of the event in the format 'DD/MM/YY'.
- **Company (ID: 8)** (`Company`, text, required): Your company name as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
- **Job Title (ID: 9)** (`Job Title`, text, required): Your job title as it appears on your business card or company directory.
- **Department (ID: 10)** (`Department`, text, required): The department you belong to as it appears in your company directory.
- **Employee ID (ID: 11)** (`Employee ID`, text, required): The ID assigned to you by your HR department or company database.
- **Company Name (ID: 12)** (`Company Name`, text, required): The name of your company as it appears on your business card or marketing materials.
- **Job Function (ID: 13)** (`Job Function`, text, required): The job function you perform in your company.
- **Event Type (ID: 14)** (`Event Type`, select_one, required): The type of event you are hosting or attending.
- **Other Event Type (ID: 15)** (`Other Event Type`, text, required): If the event type is not listed, specify the type of event.
- **Company Address (ID: 16)** (`Company Address`, text, required): The address of your company where the event is held.
- **Building Name (ID: 17)** (`Building Name`, text, required): The name of the building where the event takes place.
- **Floor Number (ID: 18)** (`Floor Number`, number, required): The floor number where the event is hosted.
- **Room Number (ID: 19)** (`Room Number`, number, required): The room number where the event is held.
- **Registration Note (ID: 20)** (`Registration Note`, note, required): Any additional details or comments for the event registration.
- **Other Registration Note (ID: 21)** (`Other Registration Note`, text, required): If necessary, add an additional note for the event registration.
- **Company Notes (ID: 22)** (`Company Notes`, text, required): Notes for event hosts or organizers.
- **Job Notes (ID: 23)** (`Job Notes`, text, required): Notes for event hosts or organizers.
- **Event Notes (ID: 24)** (`Event Notes`, text, required): Notes for event hosts or organizers.
- **Event Status (ID: 25)** (`Event Status`, select_multiple, required): The status of the event (Active, Inactive, or Unknown).
- **Attendee ID (ID: 26)** (`Attendee ID`, text, required): If you are an event host or organizer, enter the ID of the attendee.
