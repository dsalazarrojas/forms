**Shared Workspace Reservation Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Shared Workspace Reservation Form is used to reserve a shared workspace. To use this form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please select the relevant details for your reservation. The form is divided into different sections, each with its own purpose.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Reservation Details** (reservation_details, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your reservation request.
- **Start Date** (date, text, required/optional): Enter the start date of your reservation (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **End Date** (end_date, text, required/optional): Enter the end date of your reservation (MM/DD/YYYY).
- **Start Time** (time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your reservation (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **End Time** (end_time, text, required/optional): Enter the end time of your reservation (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Reservation Start Time** (reservation_start_time, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your reservation (HH:MM AM/PM).
- **Space Details** (space_details, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about the workspace you are reserving.
- **Contact Name** (contact_name, text, required/optional): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the reservation.
- **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address or the email address of the person responsible for the reservation.
- **Contact Phone** (contact_phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person responsible for the reservation.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to avoid any errors in the reservation process.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the administration team for assistance.
- Please review your reservation details before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Once submitted, your reservation will be reviewed and confirmed by the administration team.
