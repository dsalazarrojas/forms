This help guide is intended for users who are registering their lodging property for the Circular Economy Lodging Program. The purpose of this form is to gather information about the property's sustainability and circular economy initiatives, interests, and goals. It is recommended that users review this guide carefully before starting the registration process to ensure they understand the expected outcomes and provide accurate information.

To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

## Purpose
The Circular Economy Lodging Program aims to promote sustainable practices and reduce waste in the lodging industry. By registering your property, you will be able to access a range of benefits, including technical consultations, training and education, and marketing assistance. The program will help you implement effective circular economy initiatives, such as waste reduction, energy efficiency, and local sourcing.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Enter the required information in each field, making sure to select the correct options for each dropdown menu.
2.  Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **-- Property Information --**: This section asks for basic property information, including property name, type, address, and city.
* **Lodging Property Name**: Enter the name of your property, e.g., "Green Hotel" or "Sustainable B&B".
* **Property Type**: Select the type of property from the list, e.g., Hotel, Bed and Breakfast, or Vacation rental.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of your property, including street number and postal code.
* **City**: Enter the city where your property is located.
* **Country**: Enter the country where your property is located.
* **-- Contact Information --**: This section asks for contact information, including contact person name, title, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Person Name**: Enter the name of the person responsible for this property.
* **Contact Person Title**: Enter the title of the contact person, e.g., Manager, owner, or sustainability officer.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **-- Property Details --**: This section asks for detailed property information, including number of guest rooms, annual occupancy rate, and approximate annual guest count.
* **Number of Guest Rooms**: Enter the number of guest rooms in your property.
* **Average Annual Occupancy Rate**: Enter the average annual occupancy rate of your property as a percentage.
* **Approximate Annual Guest Count**: Enter the approximate number of guests that stay at your property each year.
* **Year Property Was Established**: Enter the year your property was established.
* **-- Sustainability and Circular Economy Initiatives --**: This section asks about the current sustainability initiatives implemented in your property.
* **Current Sustainability Initiatives (Select all that apply)**: Select all the initiatives your property is currently implementing, e.g., waste reduction program, energy efficiency, or local sourcing.
* **Description of Current Sustainability Practices**: Enter a brief description of your current sustainability initiatives.
* **-- Program Interest and Goals --**: This section asks for your property's interest and goals in joining the circular economy program.
* **Areas of Interest in Circular Economy Program (Select all that apply)**: Select all the areas your property is interested in, e.g., waste management optimization, energy efficiency, or supply chain improvement.
* **Primary Goals for Joining This Program**: Enter the main reasons why you want to join the program.
* **-- Resources and Support --**: This section asks for the types of support you may need from the program.
* **Types of Support Needed (Select all that apply)**: Select all the types of support you may need, e.g., technical consultation, training, or marketing assistance.
* **Estimated Investment Capacity for Improvements**: Select the estimated investment capacity of your property for improvements.
* **-- Program Commitment --**: This section asks for your property's commitment to the program.
* **Desired Program Duration**: Select the desired program duration for your property.
* **Number of Staff That Will Participate**: Enter the number of staff members that will participate in the program.
* **How Will You Measure Program Success**: Enter how you will measure the success of the program.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete all required fields.
* Review your selections carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the program administrators.
* The program administrators will review your submission and contact you to discuss further details.
