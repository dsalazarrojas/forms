# Trade Show Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Trade Show Volunteer Registration Form is a tool for potential volunteers to provide information about their availability and preferences for the trade show event. This form helps event organizers to assign volunteers to available shifts and manage volunteer resources effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Volunteer Name field with your name as it appears on your identification.
2. Enter your Volunteer Email in the format `example@example.com` for contact purposes.
3. Provide your phone number in the Volunteer Phone field for communication.
4. Select your available dates from the Available Dates field using the calendar interface.
5. Choose your available time slots from the Available Time field using the dropdown options.
6. Indicate your availability for shifts by selecting Yes or No in the Shift field.
7. Specify which shifts you are available for by selecting multiple options from the Available Shifts field.
8. Select Yes or No for your availability in each of the following fields: Volunteer Type, Available Dates, Available Times, Roles, Teams, and Departments.
9. Provide any additional comments or notes in the Notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Volunteer Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com` for contact purposes.
* **Volunteer Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number for communication.
* **Available Dates** (`date`, `date`, required): Select your available dates using the calendar interface.
* **Available Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Choose your available time slots from the dropdown options.
* **Shifts** (`shift`, `select_multiple`, required): Indicate your availability for shifts by selecting multiple options.
* **Available Shifts** (`available_shifts`, `select_one`, required): Specify which shifts you are available for by selecting Yes or No.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required): Provide any additional comments or notes about your availability or preferences.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, `select_multiple`, required): Select Yes or No for your availability as a volunteer type.
* **Available Dates** (`available_dates`, `select_multiple`, required): Select Yes or No for your availability on specific dates.
* **Available Times** (`available_times`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose Yes or No for your available time slots.
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required): Select Yes or No for your availability for different roles.
* **Teams** (`teams`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose Yes or No for your availability for different teams.
* **Departments** (`departments`, `select_one`, required): Select Yes or No for your availability in different departments.
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your roles.
* **Teams** (`teams`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your teams.
* **Departments** (`departments`, `select_one`, required): Select one option for your department.
* **Roles** (`roles`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your roles.
* **Teams** (`teams`, `select_multiple`, required): Select multiple options for your teams.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your availability and preferences before submitting the form.
* If you have any conflicts or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
* The event organizers will use this information to assign you to available shifts and roles.
* You will receive further instructions and information about the event after submitting this form.
