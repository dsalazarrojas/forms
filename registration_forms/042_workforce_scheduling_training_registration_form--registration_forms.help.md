# Workforce Scheduling Training Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from employees to register for training sessions in the workforce scheduling system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all required fields with accurate information.
2. Select relevant options for "Department" and "Location".
3. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the "Email" field.
4. If you have any comments or additional information, use the "Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Employee Name (Text, Required)**: Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
- **Training Date (Date, Required)**: Select the date you would like to attend the training session.
- **Training Time (Time, Optional)**: If you have a specific time preference, enter it here (format: 08:00 or 08:00 AM).
- **Department (Select One, Required)**: Choose the department you are a part of (Sales, Marketing, Support).
- **Location (Select Multiple, Required)**: Select the locations you are available to attend the training session (New York, Chicago, London).
- **Email (Email, Required)**: Enter your work email address in the format of `example@example.com`.
- **Phone (Text, Optional)**: If you want to provide your phone number for any follow-up, enter it here.
- **Comments (Note, Optional)**: If you have any additional comments or information, use this field to provide more details.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to ensure smooth processing of your registration.
* If you have any doubts or need help, contact your HR representative.
* Review and submit the form carefully before clicking the "Submit" button.
