# interactive_exhibit_sponsorship_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact and financial information from organizations interested in sponsoring an exhibit. This information will be used to communicate with the organization and ensure a smooth partnership process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by completing the first page with your organization's name and contact person.
2. On the second page, provide your organization's address.
3. In the third page, select your phone number and choose a contribution amount.
4. On the fourth page, select a date for the payment and a time if necessary.
5. In the final page, add any notes about the payment and select any assigned tools for the exhibit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, required/optional: false): Enter your organization's name in the space provided.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, required/optional: false): Enter the name and title of the contact person in charge of this partnership.
* **Address** (`address`, required/optional: false): Enter your organization's address for communication and delivery purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required/optional: false): Select the best phone number to contact you. Please choose from 123-456-7890 or 098-765-4321.
* **Email** (`email`, required/optional: false): Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contribution Amount** (`contribution_amount`, required/optional: false): Choose the contribution amount from the options provided or enter a custom amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, required/optional: false): Select a specific date for the payment. If not provided, we will use the current date.
* **Payment Time** (`payment_time`, required/optional: false): If necessary, select the payment time to ensure a smooth payment process.
* **Payment Note** (`payment_note`, required/optional: false): Add any notes or comments about the payment.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, required/optional: false): Choose which tools will be assigned to the exhibit from the options provided or enter a custom option.
