# Youth Basketball League Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and medical information from youth basketball players and their emergency contacts for registration in the youth basketball league.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required personal information for the player:
	* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter the player's first name.
	* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter the player's last name.
	* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter the player's email address.
	* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the player's phone number.
	* **Team Name** (`team_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the team the player is registering with.
2. Provide the player's medical information:
	* **Player DOB** (`player_dob`, date, required): Enter the player's date of birth.
	* **Player Grade** (`player_grade`, text, required): Enter the player's grade level.
3. Add the player's parent or guardian information:
	* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player's parent or guardian.
	* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the player's parent or guardian.
4. Provide the emergency contact information:
	* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the player's emergency contact.
	* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
	* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, required): Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the player.
5. Provide any additional medical information:
	* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the player has.
	* **Allergy Info** (`allergy_info`, text, optional): Enter any allergy information the player has.
	* **Player Medical History** (`player_medical_history`, text, optional): Enter the player's medical history.
	* **Player Medical Info** (`player_medical_info`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information for the player.
6. Review and submit the form:
	* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, required): Enter the address of the emergency contact.
	* **Medical Info 2** (`medical_info2`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information for the player.
	* **Player Medical Info 2** (`player_medical_info2`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information for the player.
	* **Player Medical Info 3** (`player_medical_info3`, text, optional): Enter any additional medical information for the player.
	* **Medical History** (`medical_history`, text, optional): Enter the player's medical history.
	* **Medical Conditions 2** (`medical_conditions2`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions the player has.
7. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the registration process.
