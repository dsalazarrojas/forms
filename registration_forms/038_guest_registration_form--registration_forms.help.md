# Guest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Registration Form is used to create a record for a guest in the system. This form collects basic information about the guest, including their name, email, phone number, notes, and date of birth.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button to begin the registration process.
2. Enter the guest's name in the "Guest Name" field.
3. Enter the guest's email address in the "Guest Email" field.
4. Enter the guest's phone number in the "Guest Phone" field.
5. Add any additional notes about the guest in the "Guest Notes" field.
6. Enter the guest's date of birth in the "Guest Date of Birth" field.
7. Click on "Submit" to complete the registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the guest. This is a basic text field for entering the guest's name.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the guest's email address. This is a field for entering the guest's email.
* **Guest Phone** (`guest_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the guest's phone number. This is a basic text field for entering the guest's phone number.
* **Guest Notes** (`guest_notes`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional notes about the guest. This is a text field for entering notes about the guest.
* **Guest Date of Birth** (`guest_date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Enter the guest's date of birth. This is a date field for entering the guest's birthdate.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter the correct email address to avoid duplication of records.
* Double-check the entered information before submitting the form.
* If the guest has any additional information that is not covered by this form, please add it in the "Guest Notes" field.
