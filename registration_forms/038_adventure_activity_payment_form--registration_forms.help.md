# Adventure Activity Payment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adventure Activity Payment Form is designed to collect essential payment information for an adventure activity, allowing participants to securely provide their payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Start" button to begin filling out the form.
2. Provide your Payer full name in the "Payer full name" field (Field 2).
3. Enter the name of the activity you are paying for in the "Activity name" field (Field 3).
4. Enter the payment amount in the "Payment amount" field (Field 4).
5. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment method" options (Field 5).
6. If paying by credit card, enter the last four digits of your card number in the "Card last four" field (Field 6).
7. Enter your billing email address for receipt delivery in the "Billing email" field (Field 7).
8. Choose the date of payment in the "Payment date" field (Field 8).
9. Confirm that you have read and agreed to the refund policy by selecting "True" in the "Refund policy acknowledged" field (Field 9).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Payer full name (Required)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Activity name (Required)**: Enter the name of the activity you are paying for.
* **Payment amount (Required)**: Enter the exact amount you are paying in local currency.
* **Payment method (Required)**: Select from the available options (Credit card, Bank transfer, or Cash).
* **Card last four (Optional)**: For manual reconciliation purposes, enter the last four digits of your credit card number.
* **Billing email (Required)**: Enter your email address where you want to receive your receipt.
* **Payment date (Required)**: Choose the date of payment from the calendar or enter a date in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Refund policy acknowledged (Required)**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to the refund policy by selecting "True".
