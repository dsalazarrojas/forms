# Retail Buyer Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from registrants for the Retail Buyer Conference. Please fill out the form with accurate and complete information to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field carefully and accurately, following the instructions for each section.
- Make sure to complete all required fields, even if you're unsure of the information.
- Review your information before submitting the form to ensure it's accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`1`, `text`, required: false):
    * This section is for general information about you, the registrant.
* **Company Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false):
    * Please provide your company's name and any relevant details.
* **Contact Preferences** (`3`, `text`, required: false):
    * Share your contact preferences, such as email, phone number, or any other relevant contact method.
* **Company Info** (`4`, `text`, required: false):
    * Provide any additional information about your company that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Shipping** (`5`, `text`, required: false):
    * Specify any shipping instructions or special requests.
* **Payment** (`6`, `text`, required: false):
    * Please note any payment details or methods.
* **Attendee Details** (`7`, `text`, required: false):
    * Share any additional details about the attendees attending with you.
* **Attendee Info** (`8`, `text`, required: false):
    * Provide any other information about the attendees that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Submit** (`9`, `text`, required: false):
    * Once you've filled out all the required fields, click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.
