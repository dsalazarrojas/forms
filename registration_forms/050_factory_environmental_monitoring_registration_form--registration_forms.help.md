# Factory Environmental Monitoring Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a factory's environmental monitoring activities, including its name, contact information, and monitoring details. It ensures that all factories provide accurate and consistent environmental monitoring data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the factory's name in the "Factory Name" field.
2. Provide the factory's contact email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Enter the facility's address in the "Facility Address" field (optional).
4. Indicate the factory's monitor conditions (Yes or No) in the "Monitor Conditions" field.
5. Select the monitor start date in the "Monitor Start Date" field.
6. Select the monitor stop date in the "Monitor Stop Date" field.
7. Enter the monitor frequency in the "Monitor Frequency" field.
8. Indicate the factory's monitor location (Yes or No) in the "Monitor Location" field.
9. Provide the factory's contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field (optional).
10. Add any additional comments or notes in the "Note" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Factory Name** (`factory_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the full name of the factory, including its name and address. 
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, **required**): Enter a valid email address used to contact the factory.
* **Facility Address** (`facility_address`, `text`, **optional**): Enter the physical address of the factory, including street, city, and country.
* **Monitor Conditions** (`monitor_conditions`, `select_multiple`, **required**): Select all conditions that apply to the factory's environmental monitoring, such as "Yes" for water treatment or "No" for air pollution.
* **Monitor Start Date** (`monitor_start_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date when the factory started monitoring its environmental conditions.
* **Monitor Stop Date** (`monitor_stop_date`, `date`, **required**): Select the date when the factory stopped monitoring its environmental conditions.
* **Monitor Frequency** (`monitor_frequency`, `text`, **required**): Enter the frequency of the factory's environmental monitoring, such as weekly or bi-monthly.
* **Monitor Location** (`monitor_location`, `select_one`, **required**): Select the location where the factory's monitoring is taking place, such as "Yes" for on-site or "No" for off-site.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, **optional**): Enter a valid phone number used to contact the factory.
* **Note** (`note`, `text`, **optional**): Add any additional comments or notes related to the factory's environmental monitoring activities.
