# Veterinary Software Demo Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Veterinary Software Demo Registration form is designed to collect information from users and clinic details for demo purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required information in the provided fields. Each field is explained below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Info** (text, optional): Please provide your name, email address and other personal details.
- **Demo Request** (text, optional): Describe the specific features, purpose, or functionality of the demo you would like to explore.
- **Clinic Info** (text, optional): Enter your clinic or practice name, address, and other relevant details.
## Tips
- Please fill in all fields according to your needs.
- Ensure the provided information is accurate and clear.
- If you have any questions or need help, contact form@gic.mx.
