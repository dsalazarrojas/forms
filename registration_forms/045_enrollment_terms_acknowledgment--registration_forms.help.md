# Enrollment Terms Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Enrollment Terms Acknowledgment form is a document used to acknowledge and confirm the terms of enrollment for new students. This form is required to be completed by students to ensure they understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully and make sure you understand the terms and conditions of the program.
2. Select the correct options for each field as required.
3. Enter your acknowledgment of the terms in the provided text field.
4. Sign the Terms Agreement field to confirm your agreement to the terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enrollments Terms Acknowledgment** (1): This field is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant information or comments about the terms.
* **Program Terms 1** (2): This field provides a list of options for you to select from. Please choose the option that best applies to your situation.
* **Program Terms 2** (3): This field allows you to select multiple options. Please select all applicable options that apply to your situation.
* **Course Terms** (4): This field provides a list of options for you to select from. Please choose the option that best applies to your course enrollment.
* **Acknowledge Terms** (5): This field is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant information or comments about the terms.
* **Terms Agreement** (6): This field requires you to select one of the provided options to confirm your agreement to the terms.
* **Enrollment Date** (7): This field allows you to enter the date of your enrollment.
* **Enrollment Time** (8): This field allows you to enter the time of your enrollment.
* **Instructor Terms** (9): This field is a free-form text field where you can enter any relevant information or comments about the instructor or terms.

Note: Some fields are marked as "required" to ensure that you understand and agree to the terms. Please ensure that you complete all required fields.
