# Guest Experience Innovation Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Guest Experience Innovation Forum Registration form is designed to collect information from potential attendees of the guest experience innovation forum.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form on each of the 4 pages, ensuring that you provide accurate and complete information.
2. For each page, read the labels and hints carefully before providing your answers.
3. Make sure to provide the required information, as indicated by the label "(Required)".
4. If you need to cancel or change your registration, please contact us directly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guest Experience Innovation Forum Registration (Text)** (`form_1_page`, text, Required): Please enter a brief description of your expectations or goals for attending the forum.
* **Session Interests (Select One)** (`form_2_page`, select_one, Required): Select the session that interests you the most.
* **Special Requirements (Note)** (`form_3_page`, note, Required): Please enter any special requirements or requests you have for your attendance.
* **Email (Email)** (`form_4_page`, email, Required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone (Text)** (`form_5_page`, text, Required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
