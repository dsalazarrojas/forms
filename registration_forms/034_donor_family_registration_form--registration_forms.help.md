# Donor Family Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Family Registration Form is designed to collect contact and donation information from individuals who wish to donate to a specific cause or organization. This form helps the organization understand the contact details and donation preferences of the donors, enabling them to communicate and follow-up effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your contact details in the "Contact Details" field. This includes your name, email address, and phone number.
2. Provide information about your donation in the "Donation Info" field. Please be specific about the amount you wish to donate and the reasons for your donation.
3. Share information about your family members or contacts in the "Family Info" field. This will help the organization understand who is involved in the donation process and enable them to reach out to them if necessary.
4. Specify your contact preferences in the "Contact Preferences" field. This will help the organization tailor their communication to your needs.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Contact Details** (`id: 1`): Enter your name, email address, and phone number to ensure we can contact you for further communication and follow-up.
- **Donation Info** (`id: 2`): Describe the specific donation you wish to make, including the amount and reasons behind it.
- **Family Info** (`id: 4`): Provide details about your family members or contacts involved in the donation process.
- **Contact Preferences** (`id: 3`): Specify how you would like to be contacted (e.g., email, phone, mail) and any specific times or frequencies you would like to be contacted.

## Tips
- Please be accurate and truthful when filling out this form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
- You can only complete this form once for each donor family.
- If you have any issues or problems with the form, please let us know and we will be happy to assist.
