# Sports Analytics Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for sports analytics training programs. It is essential to provide accurate and thorough information to ensure a successful and productive training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Provide your organization's name and email address in the `Organization Name` and `Organization Email` fields, respectively.
2. Select your role in the `Role` field. This will help us understand your position and tailor the training to your needs.
3. Enter the date and time you plan to attend the training in the `Training Date` and `Training Time` fields.
4. Provide the full name of your participant in the `Participant Name` field.
5. Enter your email address in the `Participant Email` field.
6. Enter your phone number in the `Participant Phone` field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the `Additional Notes` field.
8. Confirm that you have received the email sent to you by checking the `Email Sent` field.
9. Confirm that you have received the training schedule by checking the `Confirmation Required` field and providing a confirmation message in the `Confirmation Message` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the full name of your organization.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select your role in the organization, such as Analytics Manager, Data Analyst, or Operations Manager.
* **Training Date** (`training_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the training.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, time, optional): Enter the time you plan to attend the training.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes you have about the training.
* **Email Sent** (`email_sent`, text, required): Confirm that you have received the email sent to you.
* **Confirmation Required** (`confirmation_required`, text, required): Confirm that you have received the training schedule by providing a confirmation message.
* **Confirmation Message** (`confirm_message`, text, optional): Provide a confirmation message to be sent to you.
* **Organization Name 2** (`organization_name_2`, text, optional): Enter the full name of your organization (if different from the first organization name).
* **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your organization.
