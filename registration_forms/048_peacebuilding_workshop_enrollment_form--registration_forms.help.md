# Peacebuilding Workshop Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from participants enrolling in the Peacebuilding Workshop. The data collected will be used for logistical reasons, such as tracking enrollments and facilitating communication among participants and workshop organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your workshop name to specify the workshop you are enrolling in.
2. Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the workshop.
3. If you are enrolling on behalf of an organization, enter its name.
4. Enter your position within your organization or a description of your role.
5. Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
6. Enter a phone number where we can reach you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are enrolling in, such as "Conflict Resolution 101" or "Community Building Workshop."
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed in the workshop.
* **Organization**: If you are enrolling on behalf of an organization, enter its name here. This could be your company, non-profit, or any other relevant affiliation.
* **Position**: Enter your title or role within your organization, such as "Program Manager" or "Director."
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address we can use to contact you regarding the workshop.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number where we can reach you for further questions or updates.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter your contact information accurately, as this will help us communicate with you effectively.
- If you are experiencing difficulties with your enrollment, please don't hesitate to contact us.
