# Labor Law Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Labor Law Seminar Registration Form is a simple form designed to gather information from attendees for the Labor Law Seminar. This form is not essential for business operations but is intended to collect necessary data for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow the steps outlined in this guide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (Event Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the labor law seminar. 
* **Attendee Name** (Attendee Name, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the attendee.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Contact Email** (Contact Email, email, required/optional): Enter your contact email for any further correspondence.
* **Confirm Email** (Confirm Email, email, required/optional): Re-enter your email address for verification purposes.
* **Attendance** (Attendance, select_one, required/optional): Choose either "Yes" or "No" to indicate your attendance status.
* **Registration Status** (Registration Status, select_one, required/optional): Select "Active" or "Inactive" to indicate your registration status.
* **Comments** (Comments, note, required/optional): Add any comments or notes related to your attendance or registration, if any.
