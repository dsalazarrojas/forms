# Freelancer Growth Bundle Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register or update freelancers in the system, capturing essential details to facilitate efficient management and tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Enroll Freelancers" option from the first field to indicate whether you are enrolling or not.
2. Choose your goals from the "Enter Goals" field, which is a required field.
3. Choose your experience level from the "Experience Levels" field, which is also required.
4. For the "Organize Applicants" field, select "Yes" if you need assistance with organizing your applicants, and provide additional comments or notes if needed in the "Notes" or "Additional Comments" fields.
5. In the "Notes", "Additional Comments", "Program Owner Notes", "Program Owner Email", "Program Owner Phone", "Coach", "Coach 2", "Coach 3", and "Coach 5" fields, you can provide any additional comments or information that may be helpful for the program or coach.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Enroll Freelancers** (Enroll Freelancers, select_multiple, Required): Select "Yes" to enroll or "No" to not enroll.
* **Enter Goals** (Enter Goals, select_one, Required): Enter your goals.
* **Experience Levels** (Experience Levels, select_one, Required): Choose your level of experience.
* **Organize Applicants** (Organize Applicants, select_multiple, Optional): Select "Yes" if you need help with organizing your applicants.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, note, Optional): Add any additional comments or notes.
* **Email** (Email, email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Program Owner Notes** (Program Owner Notes, note, Optional): Add notes for the program owner.
* **Goals** (Goals, text, Optional): Enter any specific goals.
* **Experience Levels 2** (Experience Levels 2, text, Optional): Enter your experience level.
* **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, Optional): Add additional comments or notes.
* **Program Owner Email** (Program Owner Email, email, Optional): Enter the program owner's email address.
* **Program Owner Phone** (Program Owner Phone, text, Optional): Enter the program owner's phone number.
* **Program Owner** (Program Owner, select_one, Required): Select "Yes" to indicate that there is a program owner.
* **Coach** (Coach, select_one, Required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a coach.
* **Coach 2** (Coach 2, select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a coach.
* **Coach 3** (Coach 3, select_one, Required): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a coach.
* **Coach 4** (Coach 4, select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a coach.
* **Coach 5** (Coach 5, select_one, Optional): Select "Yes" to indicate that you have a coach.
