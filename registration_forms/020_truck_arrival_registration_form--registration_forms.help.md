# truck_arrival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for the registration of trucks arriving at a facility. It is intended for tracking and documenting the arrival times, details, and notes of drivers and their vehicles.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please provide the following information:

1. Arrival Time: Enter the time when the truck arrived at the facility.
2. Driver Name: Enter the name of the driver of the truck.
3. Truck Number: Enter the number of the truck.
4. Arrival Station: Enter the name of the station or location where the truck arrived.
5. Departure Time: Enter the time when the truck is expected to leave the facility. (Optional)
6. Driver Company: Enter the name of the company the driver represents. (Optional)
7. Arrival Station Name: Enter the name of the station or location where the truck arrived (if different from the previous field). (Optional)
8. Notes: Enter any additional information or notes about the truck's visit.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, not required): Enter the time when the truck arrived at the facility.
* **Driver Name** (`driver_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the driver of the truck.
* **Truck Number** (`truck_number`, text, not required): Enter the number of the truck.
* **Arrival Station** (`arrival_station`, text, not required): Enter the name of the station or location where the truck arrived.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, time, not required): Enter the time when the truck is expected to leave the facility.
* **Driver Company** (`driver_company`, text, not required): Enter the name of the company the driver represents.
* **Arrival Station Name** (`arrival_station_name`, text, not required): Enter the name of the station or location where the truck arrived (if different from the previous field).
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, not required): Enter any additional information or notes about the truck's visit.

## Tips
* Please enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your supervisor or the truck driver.
* You can skip fields that are not required, but please fill out as much information as possible.
* The form is designed to be completed quickly and efficiently, so please take care to provide clear and concise answers.
