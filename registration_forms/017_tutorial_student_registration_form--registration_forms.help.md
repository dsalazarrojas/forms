# Tutorial Student Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tutorial Student Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who want to register for a tutorial. Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully to ensure successful registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format "dd/mm/yyyy".
6. Select your gender from the "Gender" dropdown menu.
7. Fill in your Student ID in the "Student ID" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format "username@domain.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Fill in your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format "dd/mm/yyyy".
* **Gender** (`gender`, select_one, required: false): Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required: false): Fill in your student ID.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have not registered before.
- Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you are having trouble finding your student ID, contact the tutorial administrators.
