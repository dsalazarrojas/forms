# Community Ladies Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the process of completing the Community Ladies Event Registration form.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the correct event from the list provided.
2. Fill in your full name, contact email address, and phone number.
3. Choose how you will be participating in the event, either in-person or virtually.
4. Indicate any dietary preferences or restrictions you may have.
5. If you are attending with additional guests, provide their names and number of guests.
6. Check the box if you require any special accommodations, such as accessibility access or translation services.
7. Confirm your payment method for the registration fee.
8. Finally, let us know how you heard about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Which Ladies Event are you registering for? (event_selection)**: Select the correct event you are registering for.
* **Your Full Name (attendee_name)**: Enter your full name as it should be listed on the registration list.
* **Contact Email Address (attendee_email)**: Enter your email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Contact Phone Number (attendee_phone)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **How will you be participating? (participation_mode)**: Choose how you will be attending the event, either in-person or virtually.
* **Dietary Preferences and Restrictions (dietary_preferences)**: Select all that apply for any dietary restrictions or preferences.
* **Which breakout session topics interest you most? (breakout_interests)**: Choose the breakout session topics that interest you most.
* **Number of Additional Guests (guest_count)**: Enter the number of additional guests you are bringing, not including yourself.
* **Names of Additional Guests (guest_names)**: Enter the names of your additional guests.
* **Special Accommodations Needed (special_needs)**: Check the box if you require any special accommodations, such as accessibility access or translation services.
* **Registration Fee Confirmation (payment_confirmation)**: Confirm how you will be paying for the registration fee.
* **How did you hear about this ladies event? (referral_source)**: Let us know how you heard about the event.
* **Date Registered (registration_date)**: This will be automatically populated with the current date.
