# Giveaway Partner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from giveaway partners who want to participate in our giveaway program. The information collected will be used to verify the partner's details and ensure they are eligible to participate in our giveaways.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the required information for each field.
2. Ensure you have a valid email address and a phone number.
3. Choose the correct partner type (e.g., Giveaway Host, Organizer, or Sponsor).
4. Select all relevant locations where your business is based or operates.
5. If required, enter the date of your giveaway or event.
6. Optionally, add any additional comments or notes about your partnership.
7. Review the information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Partner Name** (`partner_name`, text, optional): Enter your company name or the name of the partner you are representing.
* **Partner Email** (`partner_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Partner Phone** (`partner_phone`, text, optional): Enter your company phone number or the phone number where we can contact you.
* **Partner Note** (`partner_note`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your partnership.
* **Partner Type** (`partner_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of partner you are (e.g., Giveaway Host, Organizer, or Sponsor).
* **Partner Location(s)** (`partner_location`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the locations where your business is based or operates (e.g., Location 1, Location 2, Location 3).
* **Partner Date** (`partner_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of your giveaway or event.
* **Partner Time** (`partner_time`, time, optional): If applicable, enter the time of your giveaway or event.

Note: The required fields are not actually required, but it's essential to fill them in for a successful registration. 

Remember to double-check your information before submitting, and contact us if you have any questions or need further assistance.
