# Glasswork Presentation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register events or presentations for internal organization and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Event Details** section with the event's name, start and end dates, location, time, and description.
2. Enter your contact information, including email addresses and phone numbers.
3. Select "Yes" or "No" for **Event Type** and **Event Tags**.
4. Add any additional event staff members' email addresses and phone numbers.
5. Choose your registration option by selecting "Yes" or "No" for **Registration**.
6. Finally, review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event or presentation.
* **Event Start Date** (`event_start_date`): Enter the start date of the event or presentation.
* **Event End Date** (`event_end_date`): Enter the end date of the event or presentation.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): Enter the location of the event or presentation.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event or presentation.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): Briefly describe the event or presentation.
* **Event Type** (`event_type`): Select whether the event is a presentation or not.
* **Event Tags** (`event_tags`): Enter any relevant tags or keywords for the event or presentation.
* **Additional Event Information** (`event_details_1`): Enter any additional information about the event or presentation.
* **Event Staff** (`contact_preferences_1`): Enter email addresses and phone numbers of event staff members.
* **Email 1** (`email_1`): Enter the email address of a staff member.
* **Phone 1** (`phone_1`): Enter the phone number of a staff member.
* **Name 1** (`name_1`): Enter the name of a staff member.
* **Email 2** (`email_2`): Enter the email address of another staff member.
* **Phone 2** (`phone_2`): Enter the phone number of another staff member.
* **Name 2** (`name_2`): Enter the name of another staff member.
* **Registration** (`registration`): Select whether you want to register for the event or presentation.
* **Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences`): Enter your email addresses and phone numbers.
* **Additional Contact Preferences** (`contact_preferences_1`): Enter additional email addresses and phone numbers.
* **Additional Event Staff Information** (`event_details_2`): Enter any additional information about the event staff.
* **Ticket Price** (`ticket_price`): Enter the ticket price of the event or presentation.
* **Note** (`note`): Add any additional notes about the event or presentation.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*) as they are necessary for the form submission to be complete.
* Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any issues with the form, contact your local administrator for assistance.
