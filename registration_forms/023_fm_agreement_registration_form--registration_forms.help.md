# fm_agreement_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "fm_agreement_registration_form" is used to register agreements, allowing you to track and document your agreements. This form is designed to be filled out by individuals who have entered into agreements with your organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Select the agreements you are registering from the list of options provided.
2. Enter the name of the contact person involved in the agreement.
3. Enter the date of the agreement.
4. Select the contract term, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Agreements Details
* **Agreements Details** (`select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select all the agreements you are registering by choosing from the available options. Please select as many or as few agreements as needed to accurately reflect the agreements you have.

### Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`text`, required: `false`): Enter the name of the person or organization you have entered into the agreement with. This can be a name, initials, or any other identifier.

### Agreement Date
* **Agreement Date** (`date`, required: `false`): Enter the date on which the agreement was made or signed. This can be a specific date or a range of dates if applicable.

### Contract Term
* **Contract Term** (`time`, required: `false`): If the agreement has a specific term or duration, select it from the options provided. This can be a fixed term, such as "X years" or "Y months", or a more flexible term, such as "at the end of the month".

**Tips**

* Please ensure that you have selected the correct agreements from the options provided.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact the relevant department or person for clarification.
* Double-check your entries for accuracy to avoid errors.
