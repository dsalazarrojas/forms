# Coaching Position Expression Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Coaching Position Expression Of Interest Form is a form used to gather information from potential coaches who are interested in various coaching positions within our organization. This form helps us understand your qualifications, experience, and availability to help us match you with the right position.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out all required fields. Some fields are marked as "required" which means you must provide an answer.
2. Use the correct format for date fields (DD/MM/YYYY).
3. Select the correct options from dropdown menus.
4. Provide accurate and truthful answers to all questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Please fill out your full legal name.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): This is your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Your contact phone number.
* **Mailing Address** (`mailing_address`, text, required): Your full mailing address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): For identification purposes, please fill out your date of birth in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Position of Interest** (`position_interested`, select_one, required): Select the type of coaching position you are interested in (e.g. Head Coach, Assistant Coach, etc.).
* **Sport** (`sport`, text, required): The sport you are interested in coaching.
* **Preferred Age Group** (`age_group_preference`, select_one, required): Select the age group you prefer to coach (e.g. Ages 4-7, Adult, etc.).
* **Preferred Team Gender** (`gender_preference`, select_one, required): Select the team gender you prefer to coach (e.g. Boys or Men, Girls or Women, etc.).
* **Years of Coaching Experience** (`coaching_experience_years`, select_one, required): Select your level of coaching experience.
* **Playing Experience** (`playing_experience`, text, required): Describe your playing background, if any.
* **Coaching Certifications** (`certifications_held`, text, optional): List any relevant coaching certifications you have.
* **CPR and First Aid Status** (`cpr_first_aid_status`, select_one, required): Select your current certification status (e.g. Current Certification, Expired Certification, Never Certified).
* **Will to Complete Background Check** (`background_check_willingness`, select_one, required): Please select "True" to agree to a background check.
* **Availability Schedule** (`availability_schedule`, select_multiple, required): Select the days and times you are available to coach.
* **Season Availability** (`season_availability`, select_multiple, required): Select the seasons you are available to coach.
* **Available Start Date** (`start_date_availability`, date, required): Select the date you can start coaching.
* **Why Interested in this Position** (`why_interested`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in this coaching position.
* **Brief Coaching Philosophy** (`coaching_philosophy`, text, required): Describe your coaching approach and philosophy.
* **Relevant Skills and Strengths** (`relevant_skills`, text, required): List your relevant skills and strengths as a coach.
* **Previous Coaching Organizations** (`previous_organizations`, text, optional): List any previous coaching experiences.
* **Can Provide References** (`references_available`, select_one, required): Select "True" if you can provide references.
* **Number of References** (`reference_count`, select_one, required): Select the number of references you can provide.
* **Interested In** (`volunteer_or_paid`, select_one, required): Select the type of coaching position you are interested in (Volunteer, Paid, or Either).
* **Salary Expectations** (`salary_expectations`, text, optional): If you are expecting a salary, please describe it here.
* **Other Commitments** (`other_commitments`, text, optional): List any other commitments or responsibilities you have.
* **How Heard About This Position** (`how_heard_about_position`, select_one, required): Select how you found out about this position (e.g. Organization Website, Social Media, Job Board, etc.).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Provide any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, date, required): Today's date will be automatically filled out for you.

Note: Remember to double-check your answers before submitting the form. This will ensure we can review your application efficiently and get back to you soon.
