# Rv Space Rental Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information and details from renters applying for short-term or long-term rental of a property. It covers essential aspects such as lease signing, background checks, renter information, payment terms, and lease terms.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form in order, from page 1 to page 5.
- Provide accurate and truthful information for each field.
- Make sure to review and sign the lease agreement carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Rv space rental application form (Page 1)
- **Rv space rental application form**: Enter your name and other details as requested.
### Lease signing (Page 2)
- **Lease signing**: Enter the information required for lease signing.
### Short term guest registration (Page 3)
- **Short term guest registration**: Enter the details of your short-term guests.
### Long term tenants from weekly (Page 4)
- **Long term tenants from weekly**: Enter the information for long-term tenants (not applicable to short-term guests).
### Background checks (Page 5)
- **Background checks**: Provide any relevant background information required for the application.
### General Information (Page 6)
- **General Information**: Enter any other relevant information about yourself as a renter.
### Lease Agreement (Page 7)
- **Lease Agreement**: Review and sign this document carefully before submitting.
### Payment Terms (Page 8)
- **Payment Terms**: Enter the payment details required for rent.
### Tenant Details (Page 9)
- **Tenant Details**: Enter information about the number of tenants staying in the property.
### Renter Information (Page 10)
- **Renter Information**: Enter your personal details as a renter.
### Renter Contact (Page 11)
- **Renter Contact**: Enter your contact information for communication purposes.
### Payment Method (Page 12)
- **Payment Method**: Choose the payment method you intend to use for rent payments.
### Renter Address (Page 13)
- **Renter Address**: Enter your address for renter information.
### Lease Terms (Page 14)
- **Lease Terms**: Enter any lease terms or conditions you agree with.
### Renter Signature (Page 15)
- **Renter Signature**: Sign this document to confirm your acceptance of the lease.
### Property Details (Page 16)
- **Property Details**: Enter any details about the property you're interested in renting.
### Renter ID info (Page 17)
- **Renter ID info**: Enter your identification information.
### Property Status (Page 18)
- **Property Status**: Enter the status of your renter account.
### Renter Payment Terms (Page 19)
- **Renter Payment Terms**: Enter your payment terms and conditions.
### Renter Move In Date (Page 20)
- **Renter Move In Date**: Enter your expected move-in date.
### Renter Move Out Date (Page 21)
- **Renter Move Out Date**: Enter your expected move-out date.
### Renter Additional Information (Page 22)
- **Renter Additional Information**: Enter any additional information about your renter needs.
### Renter Lease Start Date (Page 23)
- **Renter Lease Start Date**: Enter your lease start date.
### Renter Lease End Date (Page 24)
- **Renter Lease End Date**: Enter your lease end date.
### Notes (Page 25)
- **Notes**: Enter any additional information or comments about your application.
