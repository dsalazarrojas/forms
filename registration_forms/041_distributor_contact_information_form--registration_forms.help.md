# Distributor Contact Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information for distributors, including name, email, phone number, and other details. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure proper communication with our team.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button to start the form.
2. Select the "First Name" field and enter the first name of the distributor.
3. Select the "Last Name" field and enter the last name of the distributor.
4. Enter the distributor's email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter any additional email address for the distributor in the "Alternate Email" field.
6. Enter the distributor's phone number in the "Phone" field.
7. Enter the street address of the distributor in the "Street" field.
8. Enter the postal code of the distributor in the "Postal Code" field.
9. Enter the city of the distributor in the "City" field.
10. Enter the state of the distributor in the "State" field.
11. Enter the country of the distributor in the "Country" field.
12. Enter the company name of the distributor in the "Company" field.
13. Enter the role of the distributor in the "Role" field.
14. Enter the fax number of the distributor in the "Fax" field.
15. Enter the website of the distributor in the "Website" field.
16. Enter the department of the distributor in the "Department" field.
17. Enter the job title of the distributor in the "Job Title" field.
18. Enter the address of the distributor in the "Address" field.
19. Enter the zip code of the distributor in the "Zip Code" field.
20. Enter the region of the distributor in the "Region" field.
21. Enter the time zone of the distributor in the "Time Zone" field.
22. Enter any additional notes about the distributor in the "Notes" field.
23. Enter the name of the contact person for the distributor in the "Contact Person" field.
24. Enter the email address of the contact person in the "Contact Email" field.
25. Enter the phone number of the contact person in the "Contact Phone" field.
26. Enter the fax number of the contact person in the "Contact Fax" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the distributor.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the distributor.
* **Email**: Enter the distributor's email address.
* **Alternate Email**: Enter any additional email address for the distributor.
* **Phone**: Enter the distributor's phone number.
* **Street**: Enter the street address of the distributor.
* **Postal Code**: Enter the postal code of the distributor.
* **City**: Enter the city of the distributor.
* **State**: Enter the state of the distributor.
* **Country**: Enter the country of the distributor.
* **Company**: Enter the name of the company that the distributor is associated with.
* **Role**: Enter the role of the distributor within the company.
* **Fax**: Enter the fax number of the distributor.
* **Website**: Enter the website of the distributor.
* **Department**: Enter the department within the company that the distributor is associated with.
* **Job Title**: Enter the job title of the distributor.
* **Address**: Enter the address of the distributor.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the zip code of the distributor.
* **Region**: Enter the region of the distributor.
* **Time Zone**: Enter the time zone of the distributor.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional notes about the distributor.
* **Contact Person**: Enter the name of the contact person for the distributor.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Fax**: Enter the fax number of the contact person.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact our team for assistance.
* This form should not be used for any other purpose other than its intended use.
