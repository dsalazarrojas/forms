# urban_design_contest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registration in the urban design contest. It gathers essential information from participants to facilitate the contest process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name, last name, and any other required fields.
2. Enter your email address and phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your organization's name.
4. Indicate your role in the contest by selecting the appropriate option.
5. Choose the type of submission you are making.
6. Select the date and time of your submission.
7. Enter any additional notes about your submission.
8. Provide your email and phone number associated with your submission.
9. Specify the role you are playing in the submission.
10. Attach any supporting documents (e.g., images, videos, etc.) if necessary.
