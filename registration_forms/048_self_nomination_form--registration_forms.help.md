# self_nomination_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The self-nomination form is a tool for users to provide information about themselves and their role in the organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your information in the **User Information** field. This field is where you will enter your details.
2. Select your role in the **Role** field from the provided options.
3. If you feel compelled, provide your reason for nomination in the **Reason for Nomination** field.
4. Optionally, provide any opportunity-related information in the **Opportunity** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (text): Enter your name and any other relevant contact information you'd like to share. This field is optional.
* **Role** (select_one): Choose one of the options provided, such as "Yes" or "No", to indicate your role in the organization.
* **Reason for Nomination** (text): If you wish to explain your reason for being nominated or considered, provide a brief description here. This field is optional.
* **Opportunity** (text): If there's a specific opportunity that you'd like to highlight, enter it in this field. This field is optional.
