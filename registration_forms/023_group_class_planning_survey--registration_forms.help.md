# Group Class Planning Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Group Class Planning Survey is a form designed to collect necessary information about a class you are planning or have already started. This information includes details such as class title, instructor name, class type, start date, duration, location, and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. **Start by providing a title for your class in the `survey_title` field. This is a short description of your class, which will help students and instructors easily identify it.
2. **Enter your name as the instructor in the `instructor_name` field. This is required to be able to contact you and for your students to know who will be teaching the class.
3. **Select the type of your class (Full-time, Part-time, or Online) in the `class_type` field. This helps determine how the class will be structured and who will be eligible to attend it.
4. **Enter the start date of your class in the `class_start_date` field. This is the date when the class will begin.
5. **Select the duration of your class in the `class_duration` field. This is the number of weeks the class will run.
6. **Enter the start time of your class in the `class_start_time` field. This is the time when the class will begin.
7. **Enter the location where your class will take place in the `class_location` field. This could be a physical location or an online platform.
8. **Select the days of the week when your class will meet in the `class_days` field. This helps determine which days the class will run.
9. **Select the AM/PM designation for your class start time in the `class_start_time_am_pm` field. This indicates whether your class starts in the morning or afternoon.
10. **Enter the time zone of your class in the `class_time_zone` field. This ensures that students in different time zones are aware of the local time of the class start time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Survey Title** (`survey_title`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief title for your class that will be displayed on the class schedule and other materials.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as the instructor of the class.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, required/optional): Select the type of your class (Full-time, Part-time, Online).
* **Class Start Date** (`class_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of your class.
* **Class Duration** (`class_duration`, number, required/optional): Select the duration of your class in weeks.
* **Class Start Time** (`class_start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of your class.
* **Class Location** (`class_location`, text, required/optional): Enter the physical or online location of your class.
* **Class Days** (`class_days`, select_one, required/optional): Select the days of the week when your class will meet (Mon - Fri, Sat - Sun).
* **Class Start Time AM/PM** (`class_start_time_am_pm`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether your class starts in the morning or afternoon.
* **Class Time Zone** (`class_time_zone`, text, required/optional): Enter the time zone of your class.
