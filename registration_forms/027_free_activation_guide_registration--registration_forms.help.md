# free_activation_guide_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information for the free activation process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your registration status by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
2. Enter your name in the text field provided.
3. Enter your email address in the email field.
4. Enter your phone number in the text field.
5. Enter your company name in the text field.
6. Enter your job title in the text field.
7. Enter your department in the text field.
8. Enter your manager's name in the text field.
9. Enter your manager's email address in the email field.
10. Enter your manager's phone number in the text field.
11. Enter your manager's title in the text field.
12. Enter the delivery date in the date field.
13. Enter the delivery time in the time field.
14. Enter any comments about the delivery in the text field.
15. Select the delivery method by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
16. Enter the delivery address in the text field.
17. Enter any onboarding notes in the text field.
18. Select the next steps by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.
19. Enter the onboarding status in the text field.
20. Finally, confirm that the submission is complete by choosing "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (Select Multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the registration status.
* **Name** (Text, required: true): Enter your name for the registration.
* **Email** (Email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Company Name** (Text, required: false): Enter your company name.
* **Company** (Text, required: false): Enter your company name again.
* **Job Title** (Text, required: false): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (Text, required: false): Enter your department.
* **Manager Name** (Text, required: false): Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email** (Email, required: false): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone** (Text, required: false): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Title** (Text, required: false): Enter your manager's title.
* **Delivery Date** (Date, required: false): Enter the delivery date.
* **Delivery Time** (Time, required: false): Enter the delivery time.
* **Comments** (Text, required: false): Enter any comments about the delivery.
* **Delivery Method** (Select One, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the delivery method.
* **Delivery Address** (Text, required: false): Enter the delivery address.
* **Onboarding** (Text, required: false): Enter any onboarding notes.
* **Training** (Text, required: false): Enter any training information.
* **Notes** (Text, required: false): Enter any notes.
* **Next Steps** (Select Multiple, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate the next steps.
* **Onboarding Status** (Text, required: false): Enter the onboarding status.
* **Submission** (Select One, required: true): Confirm the submission status.
