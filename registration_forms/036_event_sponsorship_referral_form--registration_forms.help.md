# event_sponsorship_referral_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect event sponsorship referrals, gathering information about the interests and reasons behind the referral.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide a brief description of the event or sponsorship opportunity in the "event_sponsorship_referral" field.
2. In the "event_sponsorship_interests" field, specify the interests or reasons behind the referral.
3. In the "event_sponsorship_why" field, provide a brief explanation of why you are referring this event or sponsorship opportunity.
4. If you have a suggested date for the event or sponsorship opportunity, enter it in the "event_sponsorship_suggested_date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **event_sponsorship_referral** (`event_sponsorship_referral`, text, optional): This field asks for a brief description of the event or sponsorship opportunity.
* **event_sponsorship_interests** (`event_sponsorship_interests`, text, required): This field inquires about the interests or reasons behind the referral. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of the event or sponsorship opportunity.
* **event_sponsorship_why** (`event_sponsorship_why`, text, optional): This field asks for a brief explanation of why you are referring this event or sponsorship opportunity.
* **event_sponsorship_suggested_date** (`event_sponsorship_suggested_date`, date, optional): If you have a suggested date for the event or sponsorship opportunity, enter it here.
