# Foundational Coaching Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Foundational Coaching Program Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in or already registered for the program. The form helps to ensure accurate and complete information is gathered for the program registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the First Name and Last Name fields with your personal details.
2. Enter your Email and Confirm Email addresses to verify your contact information.
3. Select your Program Interest to indicate your level of interest in the program.
4. Choose your Coaching Level to indicate your experience or skill level in coaching.
5. Enter the Date Registered and Registered At fields to indicate the date and time you registered for the program.
6. Add any relevant Notes to the Notes field.
7. Confirm your Phone number and Confirm Email addresses to ensure accuracy.
8. Choose the Program Type and Program Status to indicate the program's details.
9. Select the Program Start Date, Program End Date, and Program Start/End Time fields to schedule the program.
10. Enter the Program Location and Program Details fields to specify the program's location and details.
11. Finally, select your Coach and Assistant Coach (if applicable) and confirm your Agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Confirm Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure accuracy.
* **Program Interest**: Select one of the available options to indicate your level of interest in the program.
* **Coaching Level**: Select one of the available options to indicate your experience or skill level in coaching.
* **Date Registered**: Enter the date you registered for the program.
* **Registered At**: Enter the time you registered for the program.
* **Notes**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your registration.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Phone**: Confirm your phone number.
* **Confirm Date Registered**: Confirm the date you registered for the program.
* **Confirm Registered At**: Confirm the time you registered for the program.
* **Confirm Notes**: Confirm any notes or comments about your registration.
* **Agreement**: Select one of the available options to indicate your agreement to the program terms.
* **Program Start Date**: Enter the start date of the program.
* **Program End Date**: Enter the end date of the program.
* **Program Start Time**: Enter the start time of the program.
* **Program End Time**: Enter the end time of the program.
* **Program Location**: Enter the location of the program.
* **Program Details**: Enter any additional details about the program.
* **Coach**: Enter the name of your coach.
* **Assistant Coach**: Enter the name of your assistant coach (if applicable).
* **Program Type**: Select one of the available options to indicate the type of program.
* **Program Status**: Select one of the available options to indicate the status of the program.
