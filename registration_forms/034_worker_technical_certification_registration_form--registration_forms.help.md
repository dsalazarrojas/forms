# Worker Technical Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register workers for technical certification, which is required for certain roles within the organization. The form collects personal and demographic information from the worker, such as name, contact details, and address. The form is designed to be completed by the worker themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you are registering for the correct role and certification type.
2. Fill in your first name, last name, and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
4. Select your date of birth in the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
5. Enter your physical address in the address field.
6. Select your country of residence from the dropdown list.
7. Enter your city and state in the corresponding fields.
8. Enter your pin code (zip code).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (First Name)** (`first_name`, text, Required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`last_name`, text, Required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required: false): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required: false): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Required: false): Select your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Address** (`address`, text, Required: false): Enter your physical address where you can be contacted.
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, Required: false): Select your country of residence from the list.
* **City** (`city`, text, Required: false): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`state`, text, Required: false): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Pin Code (Zip Code)** (`pincode`, number, Required: false): Enter your pin code (zip code) where you reside.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to us for assistance.
