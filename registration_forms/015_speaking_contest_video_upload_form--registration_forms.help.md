# Speaking Contest Video Upload Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for users to submit their videos for speaking contests. It collects necessary information about the user, video, and contest details to help the contest organizers process and evaluate the submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. **User Information**:
	* Enter your personal information in the fields provided.
2. **Video File**:
	* Upload a valid video file in the required format.
3. **Video Title and Description**:
	* Enter a title and description for your video.
4. **Contest Selection**:
	* Choose the name of the contest you are submitting to.
5. **Category**:
	* Select the category for your video submission.
6. **Event Details**:
	* Enter the school name and event date and time.
7. **Organizer Information**:
	* Choose the organizer type (Event, School, or Club).
8. **Upload Video File**:
	* Upload the video file for the contest.
