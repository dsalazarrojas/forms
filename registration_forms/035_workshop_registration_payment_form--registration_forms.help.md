# Workshop Registration Payment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register and record payment information for workshops. It is intended for workshop organizers, registrants, and other authorized personnel to collect and track payment details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your registration information in the "Registration Information" section.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields, respectively.
4. Enter the workshop ID in the "Workshop ID" field.
5. Enter the price paid for the workshop in the "Price" field.
6. Select the payment method used in the "Payment Method" dropdown menu (Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal).
7. Indicate the payment status in the "Payment Status" dropdown menu (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
8. Enter the date of payment in the "Payment Date" field.
9. Select the workshop status in the "Workshop Status" dropdown menu (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
10. Enter the date of registration in the "Registration Date" field.
11. Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.
12. If other payment methods were used, enter them in the "Other Payment Method" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information (Registration Information, text, required)**: Enter your registration information in this field.
* **Email (Email, email, required)**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **First Name (First Name, text, required)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name, text, required)**: Enter your last name.
* **Workshop ID (Workshop ID, number, required)**: Enter the ID of the workshop you registered for.
* **Price (Price, number, required)**: Enter the price paid for the workshop.
* **Payment Method (Payment Method, select_one, required)**: Select the method used to make the payment (Visa, Mastercard, or PayPal).
* **Payment Status (Payment Status, select_multiple, required)**: Indicate the status of the payment (Paid, Unpaid, or Partially Paid).
* **Payment Date (Payment Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of payment.
* **Workshop Status (Workshop Status, select_one, required)**: Select the status of the workshop (Active, Inactive, or Archived).
* **Registration Date (Registration Date, date, required)**: Enter the date of registration.
* **Notes (Notes, note, required)**: Add any additional notes about the registration.
* **Other Payment Method (Other Payment Method, text, required)**: Enter any other payment methods used, if applicable.
