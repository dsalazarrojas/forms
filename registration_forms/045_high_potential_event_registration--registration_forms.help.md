# High Potential Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a high potential event. Please fill out this form to sign up for an event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have not already registered for this event in the past.
2. Fill out the required fields: Event Date, Participant Name, Participant Email, and Participant Phone.
3. Enter your participant name, email, and phone number accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Date (required)**: The date of the event you are registering for. Please enter the date in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Participant Name (required)**: The name of the participant registering for the event. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email (required)**: The email address associated with your participant account. Please ensure this is the correct email address you want to use for event communications.
* **Participant Phone (optional)**: The phone number associated with your participant account. This can be an office or mobile number, but please ensure it's accurate.
*   Note: If you are unable to fill out this form, please contact the event organizers to request assistance.
