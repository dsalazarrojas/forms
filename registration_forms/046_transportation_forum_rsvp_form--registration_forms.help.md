# Transportation Forum RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Transportation Forum RSVP Form is designed to collect participant information and their transportation preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information on Page 1.
2. Proceed to Page 2 and fill in your participant details.
3. On Page 3, select your preferred transportation options.
4. If you have any additional information to share, you can write it on Page 4.
5. On Page 5, select the transportation methods you are interested in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Page 1**: This page is a note page. You can write any comments or notes you have for the organizers.
* **Participant Information (Page 2)**: This is where you provide your name, email, and any other details about yourself as a participant.
* **Transportation Options (Page 3)**: Choose one of the options available to indicate how you want to be transported to the event.
* **Additional Info (Page 4)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about your participation or transportation needs.
* **Transportation Method (Page 5)**: Select all the transportation methods you are interested in. Please note that the options are not mutually exclusive, meaning you can select multiple methods.
