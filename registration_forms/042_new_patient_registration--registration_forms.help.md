# New Patient Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Patient Registration form is designed to collect essential information about new patients in a medical setting. The information collected will be used to update our patient database and facilitate communication with the patient.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "New Patient Registration" link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form with the required information, including First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth, Medical Practice, Phone Number, and Emergency Contact information.
3. Enter the patient's email address and emergency contact's name and phone number.
4. Select the relationship to the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, etc.).
5. Review the information entered before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter the patient's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date, optional): Enter the patient's date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Medical Practice** (select one, optional): Choose the patient's medical practice from the list provided (e.g., Practice A, Practice B, Practice C).
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter the patient's phone number.
* **Email Address** (email, optional): Enter the patient's email address. (Example: example@example.com)
* **Emergency Contact** (text, optional): Enter the patient's emergency contact's name and information.
* **Relationship** (select one, optional): Select the relationship between the patient and the emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for the patient.
* If the patient has a medical practice, select it from the options provided.
* If the patient does not have a phone number or email address, you can leave those fields blank.
* Double-check the relationship to the emergency contact and ensure it is accurate.
