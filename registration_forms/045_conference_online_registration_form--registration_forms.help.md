# Conference Online Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Conference Online Registration Form is a tool designed to help you register for a conference event. It allows you to provide your personal and professional information, select your preferred conference package, and provide any additional details required for your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review and select the conference you wish to attend from the list of available options.
2. Enter your details, including your first name, last name, email, phone number, and organization.
3. Select your preferred registration type (e.g., Full Conference, Single Day, Virtual Only).
4. If you are registering for a single day, select the day you wish to attend.
5. Choose any pre-conference workshops you wish to participate in.
6. Select your meal preferences.
7. If required, indicate any dietary restrictions or hotel accommodation needs.
8. Choose your preferred hotel accommodation type.
9. Select your payment method.
10. If applicable, enter a promo code for a discount.
11. Finally, confirm that you agree to the terms and policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Conference Selection** (ID 2)
	+ Select the conference event you wish to attend.
* **Conference Date** (ID 3)
	+ Enter the dates of the conference event.
* **Attendee Details** (ID 4)
	+ This is a free-text field for any additional details you may want to provide about yourself.
* **First Name** (ID 5)
	+ Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (ID 6)
	+ Enter your family name.
* **Email Address** (ID 7)
	+ Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (ID 8)
	+ Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Organization** (ID 9)
	+ Enter your company or organization name.
* **Job Title** (ID 10)
	+ Enter your position or title within your organization.
* **Mailing Address** (ID 11)
	+ Enter your full mailing address.
* **Registration Options** (ID 12)
	+ This is a free-text field for any additional options or requests related to your registration.
* **Registration Type** (ID 13)
	+ Select the type of registration you wish to have (e.g., Full Conference, Single Day, Virtual Only).
* **Day Selection** (ID 14)
	+ If registering for a single day, select the day you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Selection** (ID 15)
	+ Choose any pre-conference workshops you wish to participate in.
* **Meal Preferences** (ID 16)
	+ Select your meal preferences for the conference.
* **Lunch Day 1** (ID 17)
	+ Select your meal preference for the first day.
* **Lunch Day 2** (ID 18)
	+ Select your meal preference for the second day.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (ID 19)
	+ Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
* **Accommodation Information** (ID 20)
	+ This is a free-text field for any accommodation or hotel-related information.
* **Hotel Accommodation Needed** (ID 21)
	+ If needed, indicate that you require hotel accommodation.
* **Hotel Nights** (ID 22)
	+ If requiring hotel accommodation, enter the number of nights.
* **Room Type** (ID 23)
	+ Select your preferred hotel accommodation type.
* **Payment Information** (ID 24)
	+ This is a free-text field for any information related to your payment.
* **Payment Method** (ID 25)
	+ Select how you wish to make payment (e.g., credit card, debit card, PayPal).
* **Promo Code** (ID 26)
	+ If applicable, enter a promo code for a discount.
* **Terms Accepted** (ID 27)
	+ Confirm that you agree to the conference terms and policies.
