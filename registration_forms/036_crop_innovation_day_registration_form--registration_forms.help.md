# Crop Innovation Day Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Crop Innovation Day event. It is used to gather information about your interests, details, and requirements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select your session interests by ticking the relevant options in the "Session Interests" section.
2.  Enter your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" section, including your name, company, position, and any special requirements such as accommodation, meal, or transportation needs.
3.  Enter your email address and phone number in the "Contact Information" section.
4.  If you have any special requirements or comments, enter them in the "Comments" field.
5.  If you have specific output in mind for the event, such as a date or time, enter it in the relevant field.

### Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple): 
  Select all that apply to indicate your interests for the event.

- **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text): 
  Enter your name to complete this field.

- **First Name** (`attendee_details_first_name`, text): 
  Enter your first name.

- **Last Name** (`attendee_details_last_name`, text): 
  Enter your last name.

- **Company** (`attendee_details_company`, text): 
  Enter your company name.

- **Position** (`attendee_details_position`, text): 
  Enter your job position.

- **Other Session Interests** (`session_interests_other`, text): 
  If you have any other session interests not listed, specify them here.

- **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, select_multiple): 
  Check all that apply if you have any special requirements for the event.

- **Email** (`email`, email): 
  Enter your email address.

- **Phone** (`phone`, text): 
  Enter your phone number in the format 123-456-7890.

- **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text): 
  You may need to enter a specific tool assigned to you for the event.

- **Comments** (`comments`, note): 
  If you have any comments or notes, enter them here.

- **Output** (`output`, time): 
  Enter the output for the event in the format HH:MM or 12:34.

- **Output 2** (`output_2`, date): 
  Enter the date for the event.

- **Date** (`date`, date): 
  Enter the date for the event.

- **Time** (`time`, time): 
  Enter the time for the event.

- **Integer** (`integer`, number): 
  Enter an integer value.

- **Decimal** (`decimal`, number): 
  Enter a decimal value.

- **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one): 
  Select yes or no for this field.

- **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple): 
  Select yes, no, or other for this field.

- **Select One 2** (`select_one_2`, select_one): 
  Select yes, no, or other for this field.

- **Select Multiple 2** (`select_multiple_2`, select_multiple): 
  Select yes, no, or other for this field.

- **Date 2** (`date_2`, date): 
  Enter a date.

- **Time 2** (`time_2`, time): 
  Enter a time.

- **Decimal 2** (`decimal_2`, number): 
  Enter a decimal value.
