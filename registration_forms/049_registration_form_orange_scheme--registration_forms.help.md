# registration_form_orange_scheme - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect demographic information from users.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Verify your email address by entering it again in the "Confirm Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (3, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Email** (5, email, required: false): Verify your email address to prevent typos or errors.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information to ensure smooth verification processes.
* Double-check your email address in the "Confirm Email" field to prevent typos or errors.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact our support team.
