# Fitness Client Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Client Intake Form is a necessary step in the onboarding process for all clients. It helps the fitness professional understand the client's needs, limitations, and expectations, ensuring a safe and effective fitness experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please answer all questions carefully and thoroughly.
2. Ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
3. If you have any issues or concerns, reach out to the fitness professional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: <input type="text"> 
  * (`first_name`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your first name. Please provide your first name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
- **Last Name**: <input type="text"> 
  * (`last_name`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your last name. Please provide your last name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email**: <input type="text"> 
  * (`email`, `email`, required): This field is for entering your email address. Please enter your primary email address used for communication.
- **Phone**: <input type="text"> 
  * (`phone`, `text`, required): This field is for entering your phone number. Please use your primary phone number used for communication.
- **Health History**: <textarea> 
  * (`health_history`, `note`, required): This field is for providing any past injuries or medical conditions that could be relevant to your training. Please provide as much detail as possible.
- **Name**: <input type="text"> 
  * (`name`, `text`, required): This field is for verification purposes. Please enter your name exactly as it appears on your official documents.
- **Phone**: <input type="text"> 
  * (`phone`, `text`, required): This field is for re-entering your phone number. Please ensure it matches your primary phone number used for communication.
- **Work Hours**: <input type="time"> 
  * (`work_hours`, `time`, required): This field is for selecting your work hours availability. Please choose your available work hours from the dropdown menu.
- **Days Available**: <select> 
  * (`days_available`, `select_one`, required): This field is for selecting your available days for training. Please choose from the options provided.
- **Training Goals**: <textarea> 
  * (`training_goals`, `note`, required): This field is for describing your fitness goals. Please provide as much detail as possible.
- **Terms and Conditions**: 
  * (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for agreeing to our terms and conditions. Please select "I agree" to confirm.
- **Privacy Policy**: 
  * (`privacy_policy`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for agreeing to our privacy policy. Please select "I agree" to confirm.
- **Liability Release**: 
  * (`liability_release`, `select_multiple`, required): This field is for agreeing to our liability release. Please select "I agree" to confirm.

## Tips
- Please ensure all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or concerns, reach out to the fitness professional.
- Please review all terms, policies, and liability release carefully before agreeing to them.
