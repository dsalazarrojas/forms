# Community Dads Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register your interest in the Community Dads Initiative. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your contact information:
	* Full Name of Dad/Guardian
	* Email Address
	* Primary Contact Phone Number
	* Date of Birth
	* Residential Address
2. Select your participation preferences:
	* Choose the initiatives and activities you are interested in
	* Specify the days of the week you are available to participate
	* Select your level of participation (e.g., Active Member, Support Member, etc.)
3. Describe your skills and experience:
	* List your key skills or expertise
	* Describe any previous community or initiative experience
4. Confirm your submission:
	* Sign your name digitally
	* Specify the date of registration
	* Choose whether you want to receive the community center's newsletter

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name of Dad/Guardian**: Your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address**: Your email address for follow-up and results delivery.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number**: Your primary phone number for emergency contact.
* **Date of Birth**: Your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Residential Address**: Your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Which initiative area are you most interested in**: Select one or more areas from the options provided.
* **Which days of the week are you available to participate**: Select all that apply from the options provided.
* **What level of participation are you interested in**: Select one from the options provided.
* **What key skills or expertise would you bring to the initiative**: List your relevant skills or expertise.
* **Describe any previous community or initiative experience**: Describe your experience in similar initiatives or community work.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name)**: Sign your name digitally to confirm accuracy.
* **Date of Registration**: The date you are submitting this form.
* **I would like to receive the community center's newsletter**: Choose to stay updated on programs and events.
* **How did you hear about this initiative registration**: Select the source from which you heard about the initiative.
* **Emergency Contact Person**: Name of a person not living with you for emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number**: Phone number of the emergency contact person.

Note: This form requires a digital signature, and it's essential to ensure the accuracy of the information provided. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the initiative administrators.
