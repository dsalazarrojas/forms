# EFNE YLN Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants registering for the EFNE YLN meeting or event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form carefully and accurately.
- Ensure that all required fields are completed.
- If a field is not relevant to your participation, you can leave it blank.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (1, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Participant Email** (2, email, required): Enter your email address associated with your organization.
* **YLN Meeting Date** (3, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting or event you are participating in.
* **YLN Meeting Time** (4, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting or event you are participating in.
* **Kids Connect Meeting Date** (5, date, required): Enter the date of the meeting or event you are participating in for Kids Connect.
* **Kids Connect Meeting Time** (6, time, required): Enter the time of the meeting or event you are participating in for Kids Connect.
* **Participant Phone** (7, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Assigned Tool** (8, select_one, required): Select the tool assigned to you for the meeting or event.
* **Participant Notes** (9, note, optional): Enter any notes or comments you have about your participation in the meeting or event.
* **Participant YLN Role** (10, select_multiple, optional): Select your role in the meeting or event (admin, user, leader, or other).
* **Participant Kids Connect Role** (11, select_multiple, optional): Select your role in the meeting or event for Kids Connect (admin, user, leader, or other).

## Tips
- Double-check that all required fields are completed before submitting the form.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the meeting organizer for assistance.
