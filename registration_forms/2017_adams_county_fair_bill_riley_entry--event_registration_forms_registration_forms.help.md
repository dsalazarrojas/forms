# 2017 Adams County Fair Bill Riley Entry - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2017 Adams County Fair Bill Riley Entry form is designed to collect information from contestants for the Adams County Fair Bill Riley contest. It will be used to gather essential details about the entrant, their performance, and preferences for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill out the form carefully, ensuring all required fields are completed accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full name** (id: 2, Text, Required): Enter your full name as it should be displayed in the program.
* **Email address** (id: 3, Email, Required): Provide a valid email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone number** (id: 4, Phone, Optional): Enter your phone number, including the country/area code.
* **Date of birth** (id: 5, Date, Optional): Leave blank if you prefer to provide age only.
* **Age** (id: 6, Integer, Optional): Enter your age in whole years.
* **Mailing address** (id: 7, Text, Optional): Enter your street address, city, and state. If applicable, include your ZIP code.
* **Emergency contact name** (id: 8, Text, Optional): Name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency contact phone** (id: 9, Phone, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact, including the country/area code.
* **Entry type** (id: 10, Select One, Required): Select the option that best describes this registration:
	+ Solo
	+ Duo/Trio
	+ Group (4-10)
	+ Group (more than 10)
* **Group size** (id: 11, Integer, Optional): If applicable, enter the number of participants in your group.
* **Group member names** (id: 12, Text, Optional): List the names of members in your group separated by commas.
* **Performance category** (id: 13, Select One, Required): Choose the primary category for your performance:
	+ Vocal (solo)
	+ Instrumental
	+ Dance
	+ Comedy
	+ Variety
	+ Other
* **Experience level** (id: 14, Select One, Required): Select the best match for your entrant's experience:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Professional
* **Repertoire description/pieces to be performed** (id: 15, Text, Required): Include song titles, composers, or a short description of your performance.
* **Requested performance length** (id: 16, Integer, Required): Enter the length of your performance in minutes (typically 3-10 minutes).
* **Accompaniment arrangement** (id: 17, Select One, Required): Indicate how your performance will be accompanied:
	+ Own accompaniment (live)
	+ Backing track
	+ Piano provided by organizers
	+ No accompaniment required
* **Requires sound check** (id: 18, Select One, Optional): Check if you need an official sound check slot.
* **Preferred audition date** (id: 19, Date, Optional): If auditions are scheduled, enter the preferred date. Otherwise, leave blank.
* **How did you hear about this event?** (id: 20, Select One, Optional): Select how you heard about the event:
	+ Social media
	+ Friend/Family
	+ Fair website
	+ Flyer/Poster
	+ Previous contestant
	+ Other
* **Special requirements or accommodations** (id: 21, Text, Optional): Indicate any accessibility, staging, or equipment needs.
* **Media & Submission Instructions** (id: 22, Note, Optional): This section contains instructions for submitting audio or video files.
* **Audio/video submission link or upload notes** (id: 23, Text, Optional): Provide a URL or upload instructions for your submission.
* **Consent and release** (id: 24, Select One, Required): Select to confirm agreement with the Bill Riley contest rules and grant rights for use of recordings:
	+ I agree to the Bill Riley contest rules and grant rights to organizers to use recordings
	+ I do not agree
* **Additional comments or notes** (id: 25, Text, Optional): Provide any further information for the organizers.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately to ensure your entry is processed correctly. If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact the event organizers.
