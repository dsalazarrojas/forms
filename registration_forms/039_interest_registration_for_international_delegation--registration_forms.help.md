# Interest Registration For International Delegation - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from international delegations interested in participating in our events. The purpose of this form is to gather relevant details such as name, email, contact person's information, organization details, available dates, time zones, language, and other relevant information to facilitate coordination and communication. Please ensure that you have all necessary permissions to submit this form, and that you are not duplicating an existing registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your contact person's information, including their name in the "Contact Person" field, their email address in the "Contact Person Email" field, and their phone number in the "Contact Person Phone" field.
4. Enter your organization's name, email address, address, and other relevant details in the "Organization" fields.
5. Select the reason for your registration in the "Reason" field.
6. Select the available dates you are interested in participating from the "Available Dates" field.
7. Choose your preferred time zone and offset from the "Time Zone" fields.
8. Select your preferred language from the "Language" field.
9. Specify your preferred currency and country of origin in the "Currency" and "Country" fields, respectively.
10. Finally, enter your city and postal code in the "City" and "Postal Code" fields.
