# Wellness Community Membership Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Wellness Community Membership Registration Form is used to register new members into the wellness community. It is essential to ensure that all required fields are completed accurately and honestly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name in the "name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
3. Enter your password in the "password" field.
4. Confirm your email address in the "confirm email" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "date of birth" field.
6. Enter your address in the "address" field.
7. Enter your zip code in the "zip code" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
9. Agree to the terms and conditions, privacy policy, and sharing of member data in the respective fields.
10. Select the membership type (Individual, Couple, or Family).
11. Choose the membership plan (Monthly or Yearly).
12. Select the payment method (Bank Transfer or PayPal).
13. Enter the payment amount (leave blank if unsure).
14. Choose the payment frequency (One time or Monthly).
15. Add any additional notes in the "additional notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Password** (`password`, text, required): Enter a strong and unique password.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm email`, email, required): Confirm your email address.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip code`, number, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **I agree with the terms and conditions** (`agree_terms_and_conditions`, select_one, required): Agree to the terms and conditions.
* **I agree with the privacy policy** (`agree_privacy_policy`, select_one, required): Agree to the privacy policy.
* **I agree to share member data** (`agree_to_share_member_data`, select_one, required): Agree to share member data.
* **I agree to receive newsletter** (`agree_to_receive_newsletter`, select_one, required): Agree to receive newsletter.
* **I agree to share events** (`agree_to_share_events`, select_one, required): Agree to share events.
* **I agree to share on social media** (`agree_to_share_social_media`, select_one, required): Agree to share on social media.
* **I agree to share member data** (`agree_to_share_member_data`, select_one, required): Agree to share member data.
* **Member Notes** (`member_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the member.
* **Membership Type** (`member_type`, select_one, required): Choose the membership type (Individual, Couple, or Family).
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_one, required): Choose the membership plan (Monthly or Yearly).
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Choose the payment method (Bank Transfer or PayPal).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required): Enter the payment amount.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required): Choose the payment frequency (One time or Monthly).
* **Additional Notes** (`additional_notes`, note, required): Add any additional notes about the member.

## Tips

* Be honest and accurate when filling in the fields.
* Use the correct format for date of birth (MM/DD/YY).
* Ensure that the payment amount is correct based on the chosen membership plan.
* Agree to the terms and conditions, privacy policy, and sharing of member data before proceeding.
