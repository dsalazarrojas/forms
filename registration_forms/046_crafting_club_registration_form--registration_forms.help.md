# Crafting Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to register for the crafting club. This information will be used by club administrators to understand their member base and provide relevant services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure that you are filling out this form for the right purpose and not duplicating your registration.
2. Fill out all required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
3. Enter your information accurately and thoroughly.
4. Review your submission before clicking the submit button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact number, including country code and area code.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you wish to register for the club. The format is YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, text, required/optional): Choose the tool you have been assigned. If you haven't been assigned a tool, select "None" or describe in the "Notes" field.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more categories that describe your crafting interests. These will help us understand what services we can offer you.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): If you have any additional information or requests, such as tool requests or special considerations, please provide them in this field.

## Tips

* Ensure that your email address is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you select multiple categories, separate them with commas.
* Use the "Notes" field to provide any additional information that will help us serve you better.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
