# finance_communication_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a registration form for the finance communication workshop. To participate in the workshop, please fill out this form to provide your information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form to understand what information is required.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information.
3. Submit the form to register for the workshop.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **questions** (`questions`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the question that best describes your interest in the workshop.
* **workshop_preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that apply to your preferences for the workshop.
* **email_address** (`email_address`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive communications about the workshop.
* **name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name to be recognized in the workshop.
* **date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time you plan to attend the workshop.
* **email_preferences** (`email_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose all the options that apply to your email preferences.
* **registration_details** (`registration_details`, `note`, required: false): If you have any additional information about your registration, enter it here.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
- Make sure to choose options that accurately reflect your preferences. 
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact the workshop organizers.
