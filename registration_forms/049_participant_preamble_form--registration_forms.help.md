# Participant Preamble Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Participant Preamble Form is used to register participants for a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
2. Fill in the required fields (none are marked as required, but it's recommended to fill in all fields for a complete registration).
3. Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Information (event_info)**: Enter a brief description of the event you are registering for.
* **Participant Email (email)**: Enter your participant email address.
* **Participant Name (name)**: Enter your participant's name.
* **Contact Information (contact_info)**: Enter any additional contact information for the participant (e.g., phone numbers, addresses).
* **Address (address)**: Enter the participant's address (if applicable).
* **Phone (phone)**: Enter the participant's phone number (if applicable).
* **Participant Notes (participant_notes)**: Add any additional notes about the participant (if applicable).
