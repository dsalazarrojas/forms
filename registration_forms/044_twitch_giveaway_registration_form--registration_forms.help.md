# Twitch Giveaway Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users to participate in the Twitch Giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully to ensure you understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out all required fields accurately and correctly.
3. Use the correct format for specific fields, such as "email" and "time" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your full name as per your identification.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your surname as per your identification.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Phone Number** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any updates or issues related to your entry.
* **Twitch Username** (text, required): Enter your Twitch username that you use for your Twitch account.
* **Stream Key** (time, required): Enter the time your stream starts at. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Stream Start Time** (time, required): Enter the time you will be streaming. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Stream End Time** (time, required): Enter the time your stream will end at. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Entry Type** (select_one, required): Choose the type of entry you want to participate in. Please refer to the options below:
	+ Entry 1
	+ Entry 2
	+ Entry 3
* **Entry Date** (date, required): Enter the date you want to participate in the giveaway.
* **Entry Time** (time, required): Enter the time you want to participate in the giveaway. Use the 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Entry Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your entry.
* **Entry Email** (email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly use.
* **Entry Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any updates or issues related to your entry.
* **Entry Twitch Username** (text, required): Enter your Twitch username that you use for your Twitch account.

Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth registration process. If you have any questions or issues, please contact us for further assistance.
