# Worship Service Availability Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form helps us understand your availability to participate in our worship services. We want to make sure we can work with you to best serve your needs and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in your name to let us know who you are.
2. Choose how often you can attend services.
3. Select specific time slots on each day you are available.
4. Tell us how many hours you can volunteer per session.
5. Let us know how long you are willing to volunteer per session.
6. Indicate if you can volunteer on short notice.
7. Indicate if you are available to volunteer on holidays.
8. Choose if you would like to be contacted for other church opportunities.
