# Hospital Digital Transition Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register staff members for the Hospital Digital Transition Training program. It is a crucial step in the onboarding process for new staff members, ensuring that each participant has a unique experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Answer each question truthfully and to the best of your ability.
3. Ensure that you've answered all required fields (though none are currently marked as required).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Hospital Name** (`hospital_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the hospital where you work.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the training sessions that interest you. You can choose multiple sessions. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, select_one, optional): Select if you have any special preferences for your training sessions. 
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, optional): This field is not currently used in the form logic.
* **Staff Details** (`staff_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional details about yourself as a staff member.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role in the hospital. 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you've read and understood the purpose of this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to your supervisor or the hospital's IT department.
* This form is for registration purposes only and will not affect your current role or position within the hospital.
