# Fashion Show Participation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a way for you to express your interest in participating in the upcoming fashion show. It will help us gather your contact information and event details so that we can keep you informed about the show's schedule, venue, and any other important information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email Address in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your Phone Number, which is required to contact you about the show.
3. Enter the date of the fashion show you wish to participate in.
4. Select the time you will be arriving at the event venue.
5. Choose your preferred Time Zone for the event. Please note that this field is required to ensure accurate event scheduling.
6. If you have a specific role in mind for the fashion show (e.g. model, designer, vendor), select it from the provided options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Please enter your full first name.
* **Last Name**: Please enter your full last name.
* **Email**: A valid email address is required to communicate with you about the show's schedule and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Your contact number to reach out to you about the event.
* **Show Date**: Enter the date you wish to participate in the fashion show.
* **Show Time**: Please select the time you will arrive at the event venue.
* **Time Zone**: Select your preferred Time Zone for the event to ensure accurate scheduling.
* **Role**: Choose your desired role in the fashion show from the provided options.
