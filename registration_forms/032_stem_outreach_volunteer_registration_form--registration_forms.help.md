# stem_outreach_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from potential volunteers for outreach programs. Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that you are registered as a volunteer and can be contacted by our team for further instructions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields, marked as "required".
2. Make sure to enter your email address correctly in the "email" field. This will be used to contact you with further instructions.
3. If you have any special skills or qualifications that make you a strong candidate for the outreach program, please list them in the "skills" field.
4. Click the "submit" button to send your registration to our team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name** (Organization Name, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization you are registered with.
* **email** (email, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be contacted for further instructions.
* **phone** (phone, text, optional): If you have a phone number where you can be reached, enter it here.
* **volunteer_position** (volunteer_position, text, optional): If you have a specific volunteer position or role you are interested in, enter it here.
* **skills** (skills, text, optional): If you have any special skills or qualifications that make you a strong candidate for the outreach program, enter them here.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for further assistance.
