## Community Center Volunteer Accommodation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Community Center Volunteer Accommodation Form is designed to help volunteers request accommodations for specific events or programs. Please fill out this form to provide your details and requests.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Please fill out all fields marked as required, as indicated by the "required" tag in this guide.
2.  Ensure that you provide clear and accurate information to ensure your requests are properly assessed.
3.  If you have any additional comments or requests, feel free to include them in the "Any Additional Comments or Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name of Volunteer** (`volunteer_full_name_acc`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it should be displayed on any communication with our team.
*   **Email Address** (`volunteer_email_acc`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for follow-up and accommodation status.
*   **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`volunteer_phone_acc`, text, required): Enter a valid phone number where we can contact you for follow-up.
*   **Volunteer ID (If Assigned)** (`volunteer_id_number_acc`, text, optional): If you have a volunteer ID, please enter it. This is not required.
*   **Category of Accommodation** (`accommodation_category_acc`, select\_one, required): Select the category that best suits your accommodation request from the options provided:
    +   Mobility/Physical Access
    +   Visual/Hearing Assistance
    +   Learning/Cognitive Support
    +   Dietary/Allergy Related
    +   Other
*   **Detailed Description of the Accommodation Requested** (`detailed_accommodation_description_acc`, text, required): Describe what kind of accommodation you are requesting (e.g. wheelchair accessibility, visual aids, etc.).
*   **Urgency Level** (`urgency_level_accommodation_acc`, select\_one, required): Choose the urgency level for this request:
    +   High (Immediate attention required)
    +   Medium (Within 24-48 hours)
    +   Low (Non-urgent/Routine)
*   **Which Event or Program is this Accommodation for** (`specific_event_assignment_acc`, text, required): Specify the event or program this accommodation is for (e.g., Senior Social, Youth Sports, Arts and Culture).
*   **Do you have any Physical Mobility Requirements?** (`physical_mobility_requirements_acc`, select\_one, required): Check "True" if you have any physical mobility requirements, "False" otherwise.
*   **Do you need Visual or Hearing Assistance?** (`visual_hearing_assistance_needed_acc`, select\_one, required): Check "True" if you need visual or hearing assistance, "False" otherwise.
*   **Do you need Learning or Cognitive Support?** (`learning_cognitive_support_needed_acc`, select\_one, required): Check "True" if you need learning or cognitive support, "False" otherwise.
*   **Do you have any Dietary or Allergy Restrictions?** (`dietary_allergy_restrictions_acc`, select\_one, required): Check "True" if you have any dietary or allergy restrictions, "False" otherwise.
*   **I understand that my request will be reviewed and I will be contacted** (`accommodation_acknowledgment_acc`, select\_one, required): Select "I understand" to confirm that you understand the process.
*   **Date of Submission** (`submission_date_final_acc`, date, required): Enter the date you submit this form.
*   **Digital Signature** (`volunteer_signature_digital_acc`, text, required): Type your full name as it should be displayed on any communication with our team.
*   **Any Additional Comments or Requests** (`additional_comments_final_acc`, text, optional): If you have any further comments or requests, please include them here.
