# Handicap Parking Permit Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals with disabilities who require parking accommodations. It is used to gather details about the individual's handicap type, vehicle information, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. If you are completing this form on behalf of someone else, please ensure that you have their permission to do so.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information**: This section requires your contact information, including your name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Name**: Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Address**: Enter your address, including your street address, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone**: Select one of the provided phone numbers to indicate how you would like to be contacted.
* **Email**: Enter your email address to receive updates and notifications about your parking permit.
* **Handicap Type**: Please select the type of handicap you have, such as wheelchair, walker, cane, or other.
* **Handicap Date**: Enter the date when you first experienced your handicap.
* **Expiration Date**: Enter the date when your handicap permit is due to expire.
* **Assigned Tool**: If applicable, enter the assigned tool or device related to your handicap.
* **Submitted By**: If completing this form on behalf of someone else, please enter your name and title.
* **Submitted On**: Enter the date when you submitted this form.
* **Approved By**: If applicable, enter the name and title of the person who approved your permit.
* **Approved On**: Enter the date when your permit was approved.
* **Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to your handicap or parking permit.
* **Vehicle Type**: Select the type of vehicle you drive, such as car, van, truck, or bus.
* **Vehicle Make**: Enter the make of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Year**: Enter the year of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle License**: Enter the license plate number of your vehicle.
* **Assigned To**: If applicable, enter the name and title of the person or organization assigned to handle your parking permit.
* **Expiration Date Notes**: Enter any additional notes or reminders related to your permit expiration date.
* **Submitted By Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to who submitted this form.
* **Approved By Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes related to who approved your permit.
* **Approved On Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments related to the approval date.
* **Vehicle Registration Number**: Enter the vehicle registration number.
* **Vehicle Color**: Enter the color of your vehicle.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid delays or issues with your parking permit application.
