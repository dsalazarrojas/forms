# Webinar Registration For Leaders - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help you complete the Webinar Registration For Leaders form with ease. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Read through each section carefully and fill out the required fields.
2. Provide your consent to the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option.
3. Fill out your name, contact number, location, date, time, and email address as required.
4. If you have any additional notes or comments, you can provide them in the "Note" field, but this is optional.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Consent
• **Consent** (Select Multiple): Please select one or both of the options: "I agree to the terms and conditions" or "I do not agree to the terms and conditions". This field is required to ensure that you are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the webinar.

### Goals
• **Goals** (Text): This field is optional and allows you to specify your goals for attending the webinar. You can type out your goals as desired.

### Name
• **Name** (Text): Fill out your full name as required. This will be used to address you during the webinar.

### Contact Number
• **Contact Number** (Text): Provide your contact number as required. This will be used by the organizers to contact you before and after the webinar.

### Location
• **Location** (Select One): Select your location from the options: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, or Houston. This will help the organizers plan the webinar logistics accordingly.

### Date
• **Date** (Date): Fill out the date on which you plan to attend the webinar. This is a required field and should match the scheduled date of the webinar.

### Time
• **Time** (Time): Fill out the time at which you plan to attend the webinar. This is also a required field and should match the scheduled time of the webinar.

### Note
• **Note** (Note): This field is optional and allows you to provide any additional comments or notes you may have. You can type out your thoughts as desired.

### Email
• **Email** (Email): Provide your email address as required. This will be used by the organizers to send you updates and information about the webinar.

### Webinar Registration For Leaders
• **Webinar Registration For Leaders** (Text): This field seems to be a duplicate of the form title and is not necessary to fill out. Please ignore it.
