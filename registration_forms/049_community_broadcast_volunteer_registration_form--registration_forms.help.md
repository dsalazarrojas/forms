# Community Broadcast Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Broadcast Volunteer Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for the community broadcast. This form helps us get to know you, your skills, and your availability to better match you with volunteer opportunities within our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the registration information (fields 1-2) with your contact details.
2. Select your availability (fields 18-20) to ensure we can schedule volunteer shifts that work for you.
3. Choose your experience and skills (fields 14, 17-19) to help us understand your qualifications.
4. Provide any additional information or show ideas (fields 22, 29) to make your application complete.
5. Finally, review and agree (field 30) to our broadcast guidelines and policies.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`1`, `note`, required: false): Register to volunteer for community broadcast.
* **Registration Date** (`2`, `date`, required: true): Date of this registration.
* **Registration ID** (`3`, `text`, required: true): Unique registration reference number.
* **Volunteer Information** (`4`, `note`, required: false): Your contact details.
* **Full Name** (`5`, `text`, required: true): Your full name.
* **Email** (`6`, `email`, required: true): Your email address.
* **Phone** (`7`, `text`, required: true): Your phone number.
* **Address** (`8`, `text`, required: false): Your street address.
* **City** (`9`, `text`, required: true): Your city.
* **State** (`10`, `text`, required: true): Your state.
* **ZIP Code** (`11`, `text`, required: true): Your ZIP code.
* **Date of Birth** (`12`, `date`, required: false): Your date of birth.
* **Broadcast Interests** (`13`, `note`, required: false): What areas of broadcasting interest you.
* **Volunteer Roles** (`14`, `select_multiple`, required: true): Which roles interest you. (On-Air Host, Producer, Audio Engineer, News Reporter, Sports Announcer, Music DJ, Interview Host, Technical Support, Social Media, Marketing, Administration)
* **Music Genres** (`15`, `select_multiple`, required: false): What music genres are you interested in. (Rock, Pop, Hip-Hop, R&B, Country, Jazz, Classical, Electronic, Folk, World Music, Not Applicable)
* **Experience** (`16`, `note`, required: false): Your broadcasting experience.
* **Broadcasting Experience** (`17`, `select_one`, required: true): Do you have broadcasting experience? (Professional, College Radio, Community Radio, Podcast, No Experience)
* **Years of Experience** (`18`, `select_one`, required: true): How many years of experience do you have. (None, Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5+ years)
* **Equipment Experience** (`19`, `select_multiple`, required: false): What equipment are you experienced with. (Mixing Boards, Microphones, Recording Software, Streaming Platforms, Editing Software, None)
* **Availability** (`20`, `select_multiple`, required: true): When are you available to volunteer? (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Overnight, Weekends, Flexible)
* **Show Ideas** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Do you have ideas for shows or segments.
* **Training Needed** (`22`, `select_multiple`, required: false): What training would you need. (Equipment Operation, On-Air Techniques, Audio Editing, FCC Regulations, Content Planning, Social Media, No Training Needed)
* **Background Check** (`23`, `select_one`, required: true): Are you willing to undergo a background check? (Yes, No)
* **References Available** (`24`, `select_one`, required: false): Can you provide professional references? (Yes, No)
* **Demo Reel** (`25`, `select_one`, required: false): Do you have a demo reel or portfolio? (Yes - Will Submit, Yes - Link Available, No)
* **Demo Reel Link** (`26`, `text`, required: false): URL to your demo reel or portfolio.
* **Why Interested** (`27`, `text`, required: true): Why do you want to volunteer with us.
* **Additional Comments** (`28`, `text`, required: false): Any other information to share.
* **Agreement** (`29`, `select_one`, required: true): I agree to follow all broadcast guidelines and policies. (Yes - I Agree, No)

Note: Some fields have been left out due to their non-applicability to the current form structure.
