# Global Market Entry Advisory Forum Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your interests and preferences for the Global Market Entry Advisory Forum. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to get the most out of your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Click on the "submit" button to start filling out the form.
2. Fill out each field with your information.
3. Make sure to review your form before submitting it to ensure all required fields are completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional information about yourself that you think will help us get a better understanding of your interests and preferences.
* **Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Please fill out your name as you would like to be referred to within the forum.
* **Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address so we can contact you with updates and news about the forum.
* **Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): If you have a phone number that you would like to be contacted on, please fill it out. However, this is optional.
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, select multiple, optional): Choose the topics that interest you the most. You can select multiple options.
* **Topics of Interest** (`topics_interest`, select one, optional): Choose one topic that interests you the most.
* **Additional Info** (`participant_info_additional`, text, optional): If there's anything else you'd like to add about your interests and preferences, this is the place to do it.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill out all required fields (there are none).
* You can select multiple options for the "preferences" field.
* You can only select one topic for the "topics of interest" field.
* Double-check your form before submitting it to ensure all information is accurate.
