# Hair Coloring Certification Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register a user's hair coloring certification. Please fill out the form carefully to ensure accurate information is collected.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "Hair Coloring Certification" field, indicating whether you are certified.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter your address in the "Address" field.
7. Enter your city in the "City" field.
8. Select your state from the "State" field.
9. Select your country from the "Country" field.
10. Enter your zip code in the "Zip" field.
11. If you have a certification document, click on the "Upload Certification" field to upload it.
12. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input (1)**: Enter your response to the first question.
* **Hair Coloring Certification (2)**: Select "Yes" if you are certified, "No" otherwise.
* **Name (3)**: Enter your full name.
* **Email (4)**: Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone (5)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth (6)**: Enter your date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Address (7)**: Enter your complete address.
* **City (8)**: Enter your city or town.
* **State (9)**: Select your state from the list.
* **Country (10)**: Select your country from the list.
* **Zip (11)**: Enter your zip code.
* **Upload Certification (12)**: If you have a certification document, click here to upload it.
* **Submit (13)**: Click to submit your registration.
