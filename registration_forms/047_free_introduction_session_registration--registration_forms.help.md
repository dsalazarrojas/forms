# free-introduction-session-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "free-introduction-session-registration" form is used to collect registration information from attendees for free sessions. The form includes fields for attendee name, email, phone number, date, time, notes, confirmation status, registration status, session type, and confirmation message. The collected information helps event organizers track and communicate with attendees effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the attendee's name in the "Attendee Name" field.
2. Enter the attendee's email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter the attendee's phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select the date of the event from the calendar in the "Date" field.
5. Choose the time of the session in the "Time" field.
6. If desired, enter any additional notes about the attendee in the "Notes" field.
7. Indicate whether the attendee has confirmed their email address in the "Email Confirmed" field.
8. Select the registration status of the attendee from the drop-down menu in the "Registration Status" field.
9. Choose the type of session the attendee is registering for from the drop-down menu in the "Session Type" field. Multiple session types can be selected.
10. Enter a confirmation message for the attendee in the "Confirmation Message" field.
11. Enter the confirmation email for the attendee in the "Confirmation Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (Required): Enter the name of the attendee. This is a required field.
* **Email** (Required): Enter the email address of the attendee. This is used for communication and will be confirmed by the attendee. This field is also required.
* **Phone Number** (Required): Enter the phone number of the attendee. This can be useful for follow-up communications.
* **Date** (Required): Select the date of the event from the calendar. This is a required field.
* **Time** (Required): Choose the time of the session. This is also a required field.
* **Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional notes about the attendee. This field is optional, but can be useful for event organizers to reference later.
* **Email Confirmed** (Required): Indicate whether the attendee has confirmed their email address. This is a required field.
* **Registration Status** (Required): Select the registration status of the attendee from the drop-down menu. Options are pending, confirmed, or declined.
* **Session Type** (Required): Choose the type of session the attendee is registering for. Options are in-person, online, or other.
* **Confirmation Message** (Required): Enter a confirmation message for the attendee. This is a required field.
* **Confirmation Email** (Required): Enter the confirmation email for the attendee. This is also a required field.
