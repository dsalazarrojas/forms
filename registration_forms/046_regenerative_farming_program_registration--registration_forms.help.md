# Regenerative Farming Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for farmers to register their interest in the Regenerative Farming Program. Please provide us with accurate and truthful information to help us better understand your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field carefully, using your actual farm details and experiences.
2. Ensure you provide truthful information for each field.
3. If you have any questions or need help, please reach out to us at your convenience.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Farm Details** (farm_details, text, optional):
	* This field is for you to provide information about your farm, such as its name, location, and size.
- **Experience Level** (experience_level, select_one, optional):
	* Please select your level of experience in regenerative farming practices. This will help us understand your current level of knowledge and expertise.
	* Options: High, Low
- **Consent** (farm_consents, select_multiple, optional):
	* Please indicate your consent for us to share your information with other partners and stakeholders in the program.
	* Options: Yes, No
- **Landowner Info** (landowner_info, text, optional):
	* This field is for you to provide information about the landowner(s) of your farm, such as their name(s), contact details, and ownership percentage.
- **Farm Details 1** (farm_details_1, text, optional):
	* This field is for you to provide additional details about your farm, such as its soil type, crop/animal production methods, and any other relevant information.
- **Experience Level 1** (experience_level_1, number, optional):
	* Please provide a numerical value (e.g., years, months, days) representing your experience level in regenerative farming practices.
- **Consent 1** (farm_consents_1, select_one, optional):
	* Please indicate your consent for us to share your information with other partners and stakeholders in the program.
	* Options: Yes, No
- **Landowner Info 1** (landowner_info_1, text, optional):
	* This field is for you to provide additional information about the landowner(s) of your farm, such as their name(s), contact details, and ownership percentage.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you complete the form accurately and truthfully.
- If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
- We will review your form submission to ensure that it meets our requirements and guidelines. If we need further information from you, we will contact you directly.
