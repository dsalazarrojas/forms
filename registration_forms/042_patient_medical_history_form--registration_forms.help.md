# Patient Medical History Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Patient Medical History Form" is designed to collect comprehensive information about patients' medical history, including their personal identifiable information, health conditions, and habits. This form is intended for healthcare professionals to gather relevant information about patients' conditions, habits, and experiences, which can be used for assessment and treatment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your age in the "Age" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is a required field.
4. If you have any medical allergies, enter them in the "Allergy" field.
5. If you have any medical conditions or illnesses, select "Yes" or "No" accordingly in the "Illness" field.
6. If you have undergone any operations, select "Yes" or "No" accordingly in the "Operation" field.
7. If you have any healthy habits, select "Yes" or "No" for each habit in the "Habits" field. This field is designed to collect information about your regular habits that contribute to your health.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Age**: Enter your age in numerical value (e.g., 30).
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be reached.
* **Allergy**: If you have any known allergies, list them here.
* **Illness**: Select "Yes" if you have any current or past medical conditions or illnesses.
* **Operation**: Select "Yes" if you have undergone any medical operations or surgeries.
* **Habits**: Select "Yes" or "No" for each healthy habit:
	+ Regular Exercise
	+ Balanced Diet
	+ Stress Management
	+ Other (please specify)
* **Unhealthy Habits** (not included in the original form, but can be considered as a potential field for future expansion):
	+ Select "Yes" or "No" for each unhealthy habit:
		- Smoking
		- Drinking
		- Sedentary Lifestyle

## Tips
* Please answer all fields truthfully and accurately.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask your healthcare professional for guidance.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your healthcare provider.
