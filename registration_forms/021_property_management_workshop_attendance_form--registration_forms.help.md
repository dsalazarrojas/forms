# property-management-workshop-attendance-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from workshop attendees for property management workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first and last name fields with your actual name.
2. Enter your email address in the email field.
3. Enter your phone number (including any necessary country or area code) in the phone field.
4. Select the date of the workshop you will be attending.
5. Select the start and end times of the workshop.
6. Enter the duration of the workshop (in hours or minutes).
7. You may also provide any additional information about your attendance or any special requests in the "Additional Info" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **First Name** (`first_name`, Text): Your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text): Your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, Email): Your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, Text): Your phone number (including any necessary country or area code).
- **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, Date): The date of the workshop you will be attending.
- **Workshop Start Time** (`workshop_start_time`, Time): The start time of the workshop.
- **Workshop End Time** (`workshop_end_time`, Time): The end time of the workshop.
- **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, Number): The duration of the workshop.
- **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, Text): Any additional information you would like to provide about your attendance or special requests.
