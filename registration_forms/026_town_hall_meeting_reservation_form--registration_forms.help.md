# town-hall-meeting-reservation-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to reserve town hall meetings. It collects information about the meeting's details, such as its name, date, time, attendees, and contact name.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the meeting's name in the "Meeting Name" field.
2. Enter the meeting date in the "Date" field.
3. Enter the meeting time in the "Time" field.
4. List the attendees in the "Attendees" field.
5. Enter the contact name in the "Contact Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Meeting Name (`meeting_name`, text, required/optional):** Enter the title of the meeting you are reserving.
* **Date (`date`, date, optional):** Enter the date of the meeting.
* **Time (`time`, time, optional):** Enter the time of the meeting.
* **Attendees (`attendees`, text, optional):** List the individuals who will attend the meeting.
* **Contact Name (`contact_name`, text, optional):** Enter the name of the contact person or point of contact for the meeting.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure the meeting reservation is successfully processed.
* Review the fields carefully before submitting the form to avoid mistakes.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the form's administrators.
