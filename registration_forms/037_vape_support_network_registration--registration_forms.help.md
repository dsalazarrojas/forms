# Vape Support Network Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Vape Support Network, a service that provides support for people affected by vaping.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "Name" field. This is the name that will be used to refer to you in our communication.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This will be used as your primary contact method.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field. This is not required, but it will help us contact you more easily.
4. Select the type of support you need from the "What kind of support do you need?" dropdown menu. You can select multiple options.
5. Select the group you are participating in from the "Which group are you participating in?" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your location in the "Location" field. This will help us tailor support services to your needs.
7. Check the box to confirm that you consent to be contacted. You can select either "yes" or "no".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (2): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (3): Enter your phone number (not required, but recommended for easier communication).
* **What kind of support do you need?** (4): Select one or more of the following options:
	+ Vaping Support
	+ General Support
	+ Mental Health Support
	+ Physical Health Support
* **Which group are you participating in?** (5): Select one of the following options:
	+ Vape Support Network
	+ Peer Support Group 1
	+ Peer Support Group 2
* **Location** (6): Enter your location.
* **I consent to be contacted** (7): Check "yes" if you agree to be contacted, or "no" if you do not.
