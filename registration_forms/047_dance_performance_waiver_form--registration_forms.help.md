# Dance Performance Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Performance Waiver Form is designed to collect waiver information from participants and their guardians for dance performance events. It is an essential document that helps protect the rights and safety of both the participant and the event organizers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1.  Enter participant information: Fill out the Participant Info section with the necessary details about the participant.
2.  Review the waiver terms: Carefully read and agree to the Waiver Terms section.
3.  Sign and date the form: Sign and date the Participant Signature and Parent Signature fields to confirm that you have read and agreed to the waiver terms.
4.  Enter participant and parent names: Enter the participant and parent names in the respective fields.
5.  Sign and date the form as an instructor: If applicable, sign and date the Instructor Signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, text, required): Enter the participant's name, age, and any other relevant information about the participant.
* **Waiver Terms** (`waiver_terms`, text, required): Carefully read and agree to the waiver terms, which may include information about the event's risks, responsibilities, and rules.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event or the event's scheduled date.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Enter the start and end time of the event or the participant's scheduled time.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agreed to the waiver terms.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Enter the participant's name.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`, note, required): Sign your name to confirm that you have read and agreed to the waiver terms.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, text, required): Enter the parent's name.
* **Date Signed** (`date_signed`, date, required): Enter the date you signed the waiver form.
* **Instructor Signature** (`instructor_signature`, note, optional): If applicable, sign and date the Instructor Signature field.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and honestly.
* Read and understand the waiver terms before signing the form.
* If you are a parent or guardian, please sign the waiver form on behalf of the participant.
* If you are an instructor, please sign and date the Instructor Signature field only if you are authorized to do so.
* Keep a copy of the signed waiver form for your records.
