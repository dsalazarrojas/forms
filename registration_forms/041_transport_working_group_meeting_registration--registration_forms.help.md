# Transport Working Group Meeting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users for the Transport Working Group Meeting. Please fill out the form to provide your name, contact information, and session preferences to ensure a smooth and personalized experience at the meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form with your details.
2. Select your session preferences from the list provided.
3. Review your information before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: (`user_name`, text, optional)
	+ This field is for your name as it appears on official documents and communication.
* **Email**: (`user_email`, email, optional)
	+ Please provide your email address so we can contact you about meeting updates and sessions.
* **Phone Number**: (`user_phone`, text, optional)
	+ If you have a phone number, please enter it so we can reach you if needed.
* **Session Preferences**: (`session_preferences`, select_multiple, optional)
	+ Choose one or more sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Organization**: (`user_organization`, text, optional)
	+ If you are representing an organization, please enter it to help us track your affiliation.
