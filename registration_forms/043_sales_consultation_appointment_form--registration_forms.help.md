# Sales Consultation Appointment Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Sales Consultation Appointment Form is designed to gather information from clients for the purpose of scheduling sales consultations. This form helps our business representatives to understand client preferences and schedule consultations efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select a date for the consultation in the "Date" field.
6. Choose a time slot for the consultation in the "Time" field.
7. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "Note" field.
8. Select the category for the consultation from the dropdown menu. Options include "Consultation", "Meeting", and "Appointment".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`form_first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`form_last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`form_email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`form_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`form_date`, date, required: false): Select a date for the consultation.
* **Time** (`form_time`, time, required: false): Choose a time slot for the consultation.
* **Note** (`form_note`, note, required: false): Provide any additional comments or notes for the consultation.
* **Category** (`form_category`, select_one, required: false): Select the category for the consultation. Options include "Consultation", "Meeting", and "Appointment". 

## Tips
- Double-check that all fields are filled in correctly before submitting the form.
- Ensure that your email and phone number are accurate.
- If you have any special requests or requirements for the consultation, please include them in the "Note" field.
