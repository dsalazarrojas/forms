# cultural_exchange_fair_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cultural exchange fair registration form is used by participants to register for the cultural exchange fair. It is essential to fill this form accurately and completely to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the registration form details briefly.
2. Provide detailed information about your cultural background, availability, and event information.
3. Specify your payment details if applicable.
4. Indicate if you have submitted your information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Form Details
* **Registration Form Details** (`registration_form_details`, text, optional): A brief description of your registration form details.

### Participant Additional Info
* **Participant Additional Info** (`participant_additional_info`, note, optional): Any additional information about the participant that the organizers might find useful.

### Participant Cultural Info
* **Participant Cultural Info** (`participant_cultural_info`, text, optional): A description of the participant's cultural background.

### Participant Availability
* **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, date, required): The participant's availability for the cultural exchange fair.

### Participant Event Info
* **Participant Event Info** (`participant_event_info`, text, optional): Additional information about the event the participant is attending.

### Participant Event Details
* **Participant Event Details** (`participant_event_details`, text, optional): More details about the event the participant is attending.

### Participant Payment Details
* **Participant Payment Details** (`participant_payment_details`, text, optional): Information about the payment made by the participant.

### Participant Other Info
* **Participant Other Info** (`participant_other_info`, text, optional): Any other information about the participant that the organizers might find useful.

### Participant Agreement
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, note, required): The participant's agreement to the terms and conditions of the cultural exchange fair.

### Participant Submission
* **Participant Submission** (`participant_submission`, select_multiple, required): Indicate whether you have submitted your information. Options: 'Yes', 'No'.

### Tips
* Please fill in all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the organizers for assistance.
* Ensure that your availability matches the event's schedule.
* Double-check your submission status before submitting your information.
