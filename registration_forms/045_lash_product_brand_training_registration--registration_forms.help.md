# Lash Product Brand Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Product Brand Training Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in registering for a Lash product brand training session. This form helps us understand who is interested in attending the training and ensures that we have the most up-to-date information about our attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your session details (page 1).
2. Indicate your attendee preferences (page 2).
3. Provide your consent (page 3).
4. Enter your full name (page 4).
5. Enter your email address (page 5).
6. Specify the company you represent (page 6).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Details**: Please fill in the session date, time, and any other relevant session details you're interested in. This will help us schedule the session correctly and ensure we have the necessary materials ready for you.
* **Attendee Preferences**: If you have any dietary restrictions, mobility issues, or special requests, please let us know. We'll do our best to accommodate your needs.
* **Consent**: Please confirm that you agree to our training terms and conditions. This will help us ensure that you're aware of and agree to our policies.
* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address where we can reach you with updates, reminders, and session materials.
* **Company**: If you're representing a company, please enter the name of your company. This will help us understand your affiliation and tailor the training to your specific needs.

## Tips

* Make sure to complete the form accurately and thoroughly, as this will help us provide the best possible experience for you and our other attendees.
* If you have any questions or need further clarification on any field, please don't hesitate to ask your contact person.
* Remember to review and agree to our training terms and conditions before submitting the form.
