# digital_team_building_challenge_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your team for a digital team building challenge. Please fill it out carefully to ensure your team's registration is complete and accurate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Team Building Details field with a brief description of your team.
2. Choose your preferred challenge type (Yes/No).
3. Select your team size and number of teams participating.
4. Fill in the Goal Description field with a brief description of what you hope to achieve.
5. Enter your Team Lead's name.
6. Optionally, select your preferred start date, end date, start time, end time, and preferred time for the challenge.
7. Click submit to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Team Building Details** (required): A brief description of your team, including names of team members and their roles.
* **Preferred Challenge Type** (optional): Choose "Yes" if you are interested in participating in a team building challenge, "No" if not.
* **Challenge Duration** (optional): Select the duration of the challenge you are interested in (not applicable for this form, as no options are provided).
* **Start Date** (optional): Enter the start date of the challenge (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **End Date** (optional): Enter the end date of the challenge (YYYY-MM-DD format).
* **Start Time** (optional): Enter the start time of the challenge (HH:MM format).
* **End Time** (optional): Enter the end time of the challenge (HH:MM format).
* **Preferred Time** (optional): Enter your preferred start time for the challenge (HH:MM format).
* **Team Size** (optional): Enter the number of team members participating.
* **Number of Teams** (optional): Enter the number of teams participating.
* **Goal Description** (optional): A brief description of what you hope to achieve in the challenge.
* **Team Lead Name** (optional): Enter the name of your team leader.

## Tips
* Please double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions, please contact the event organizers.
* Please ensure you have the necessary permissions and approvals before submitting your team's registration.
