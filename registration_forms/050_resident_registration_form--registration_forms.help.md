# Resident Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Resident Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from residents, allowing the housing establishment to keep accurate records and provide efficient communication and services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in all the fields as accurately and completely as possible.
2. Ensure that all mandatory fields are filled out, though no field is technically marked as required.
3. Use a valid format for date and time entries (e.g., `YYYY-MM-DD` and `HH:MM` respectively).
4. Select a valid option for the lease terms field (e.g., 123, 456, or 789).
5. Ensure that all fields have a valid and correct entry to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Resident Information**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Contact Details**: Provide your address, phone number, and any other contact method you prefer to communicate with you.
* **Contact Info**: Enter your emergency contact's name and relationship to you.
* **Emergency Contact**: Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
* **Medical Info**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please indicate them here.
* **Lease Terms**: Select a number indicating the lease terms you are agreeing to.
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date you plan to move in (e.g., YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Move In Time**: Enter the time you plan to move in (e.g., HH:MM).

## Tips
- Double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.
- If you need assistance or clarification on any field, do not hesitate to contact the form administrator.
