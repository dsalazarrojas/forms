# Intercultural Dialogue Series Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Intercultural Dialogue Series Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from participants in the Intercultural Dialogue Series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter a valid email address in the email address field.
3. Enter a 10-digit phone number in the phone number field.
4. Select one of the available options for the "select one" field.
5. Select multiple options from the available choices for the "select multiple" field.
6. Enter the date and time for the event in the respective fields.
7. You can add notes about your registration, but these are optional.
