# Practical Guidance Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for the "Practical Guidance" workshop. It is intended to gather attendee contact details and workshop title. The information collected will be used to communicate with the attendees and for workshop organization purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Start by filling out the form in multiple pages. The form is divided into sections for better user experience.
2. Fill in the details accurately and completely.
3. Click "Submit" to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration**: This is the introductory page for the form.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Note
  * Purpose: This page is the initial step in the form and serves as an introduction.
  * Hint: null
- **Attendee name**: This field is for the attendee's name. Enter the name as it is written on their government-issued ID or passport.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Purpose: This field is to collect the attendee's name for identity verification and address personalization.
- **Email**: This field is for the attendee's email address.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Email
  * Purpose: This field is to collect the attendee's email address for future communication purposes.
- **Phone**: This field is for the attendee's phone number.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Purpose: This field is to collect the attendee's phone number for contact and emergency purposes.
- **Address**: This field is for the attendee's address.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Purpose: This field is to collect the attendee's address for workshop logistics and organization purposes.
- **Workshop title**: This field is for the workshop title. Enter the title as it is displayed on the workshop's marketing materials.
  * Required: No
  * Type: Text
  * Purpose: This field is to collect the workshop title for organization and documentation purposes.
