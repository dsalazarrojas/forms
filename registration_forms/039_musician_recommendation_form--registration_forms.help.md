# Musician Recommendation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Musician Recommendation Form is designed to collect information about musicians and their capabilities for event organizers, talent agencies, or music schools.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
3. Select your music style from the options provided.
4. Choose your instrument from the options provided.
5. Provide a brief bio of yourself.
6. Select who recommended you, such as an event organizer, talent agency, or music school.
7. Add any additional comments or event details.
8. Choose the date you were recommended.
9. Select the status of your recommendation (Confirmed, Confirmed Pending, or Not Confirmed).
10. Add any additional comments.
11. Enter the contact person's name.
12. Enter the contact person's email address.
13. Enter the contact person's phone number.
14. Select the music school you are associated with.
15. Enter the name of the music school.
16. Enter the address of the music school.
17. Enter the email address of the music school.
18. Enter the phone number of the music school.
19. Enter the website of the music school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it is known in your professional and personal life.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it is known in your professional and personal life.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Musician Experience** (`musician_experience`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select your experience in the music industry, such as Singer, Instrumental, Dancer, Actor, Comedian, or Other.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the instrument you play, such as Piano, Guitar, Violin, Drums, or Other.
* **Music Style** (`music_style`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select your music style, such as Classical, Jazz, Rock, Pop, or Other.
* **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, required/optional): Provide a brief description of your background and qualifications.
* **Recommended By** (`recommended_by`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select who recommended you, such as Music School, Event Organizer, Talent Agency, or Community.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or event details.
* **Date of Recommendation** (`date_of_recommendation`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date you were recommended.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments or event details.
* **Recommendation Date** (`recommendation_date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of your recommendation.
* **Recommendation Status** (`recommendation_status`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the status of your recommendation, such as Confirmed, Confirmed Pending, or Not Confirmed.
* **Additional Comments 2** (`additional_comments_2`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's email address.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Music School** (`music_school`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select the music school you are associated with.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the music school.
* **School Address** (`school_address`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the address of the music school.
* **School Email** (`school_email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter the email address of the music school.
* **School Phone** (`school_phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the phone number of the music school.
* **School Website** (`school_website`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the website of the music school.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name of the event.
