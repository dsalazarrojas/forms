# Music School Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music School Registration Form is designed to collect information from students who want to register for music lessons. This form helps us understand your needs and preferences to determine which classes are available for you.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your Student Name.
2. Choose your preferred class days from the list provided.
3. Select a start time for your lessons (if applicable).
4. If you play an instrument, let us know what it is.
5. Enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
6. Provide your phone number (if you'd like to be contacted).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Please fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Preferred Class Day** (`preferred_class_day`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the days you would like to attend music lessons. You can choose one or more days from the list.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, optional): If you know the time you would like to attend your lessons, please select it. If not, you can leave it blank.
* **Instrument** (`instrument`, text, optional): If you play an instrument, let us know what it is. This will help us tailor the lessons to your needs.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address so we can contact you about your lessons.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you would like to be contacted via phone, please provide your number.
