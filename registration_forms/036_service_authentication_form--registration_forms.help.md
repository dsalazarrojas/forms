# Service Authentication Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Service Authentication Form is designed to collect registration information from businesses and their services, ensuring that each business and user is uniquely identified in the system.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter your business name in the `business_name` field.
2.  Enter your owner's name in the `owner_name` field.
3.  Enter your business's email address in the `email` field.
4.  Enter your business's phone number in the `phone` field.
5.  Enter your business's address in the `address` field.
6.  Select the type of service your business provides in the `service_type` field.
7.  Select the level of service your business provides in the `service_level` field.
8.  Enter your business's email address in the `business_email` field.
9.  Enter a brief description of your service in the `service_description` field.
10. Select the ID of the user associated with your service in the `user_id` field.
11. Select the level of the user associated with your service in the `user_level` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business as it is registered with the system.
* **Owner Name**: Enter the name of the owner associated with your business.
* **Business Phone**: Enter your business's phone number.
* **Email**: Enter your business's email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your business's phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your business's address.
* **Service Type**: Select the type of service your business provides.
* **Service Level**: Select the level of service your business provides.
* **Business Email**: Enter your business's email address.
* **Service Description**: Enter a brief description of your service.
* **User ID**: Select the ID of the user associated with your service.
* **User Level**: Select the level of the user associated with your service.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and valid information for all fields.
* Double-check that the information entered matches the actual details of your business and service.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
