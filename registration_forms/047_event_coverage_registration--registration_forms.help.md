# Event Coverage Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Event Coverage Registration form is used to gather essential information about an event to ensure proper coverage and preparation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event title and details on the **Event Form** page.
2. Select your contact options and provide your contact email and phone number on the **Contact** page.
3. Choose whether to accept or decline the terms and conditions on the **Accept Terms** page.
4. Select the event date and time on the **Event Date** and **Event Time** pages.
5. Add any additional notes or comments on the **Note** page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Form (text)**: Enter your event title and details.
* **Event Details (text)**: Enter additional information about your event, such as location, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Contact (text)**: Enter your contact information, including your name, address, and any other relevant contact details.
* **Contact Options (select_one)**: Choose one of the options for contacting you, such as phone, email, or mail.
* **Email (email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Accept Terms (select_one)**: Choose whether to accept or decline the terms and conditions.
* **Event Date (date)**: Select the date of your event.
* **Event Time (time)**: Select the time of your event.
* **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your event.
