# Pet Health App Beta Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pet Health App Beta Program Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who want to join the beta program. This form helps us understand your pet's health and behavior, and allows you to access exclusive features and services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields (marked with an asterisk \*).
2. For select fields, choose the correct option from the list.
3. Make sure to agree to the terms and conditions and agree to share your data.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Owner Name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Pet Name**: Enter your pet's name.
* **Pet Type**: Select the type of pet you have (e.g. Dog, Cat).
* **Owner Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Phone**: This is a duplicate field, please enter your phone number again.
* **Email**: This is a duplicate field, please enter your email address again.
* **Owner Notes**: Enter any additional information about yourself and your pet.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: Choose how you would like to be contacted (Email, Phone, None).
* **Accept Terms**: Agree to the terms and conditions of the beta program.
* **Accept Privacy Policy**: Agree to the privacy policy of the beta program.
* **Agree to Share**: Agree to share your data with the app developers.

Note: The form will only be completed once per user, and the data will be used to provide you with the best possible experience on the app.
