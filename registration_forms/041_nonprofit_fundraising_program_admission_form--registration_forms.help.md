# Nonprofit Fundraising Program Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nonprofit Fundraising Program Admission Form is designed for nonprofit organizations to register for the program, providing them with a platform to showcase their fundraising efforts and connect with other participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email to receive important updates and information about the program.
3. Provide your Phone number for any urgent inquiries or follow-ups.
4. Enter the name of your Non Profit Organization.
5. Specify the type of event you are hosting (e.g., charity run, bake sale, or charity walk).
6. Enter the name of your Non Profit Fundraising Event.
7. Enter the name of the person organizing the event for your organization.
8. Set a realistic Fundraising Goal for your event.
9. Enter the Ending Date of your fundraising event.
10. Enter the Starting Date of your fundraising event.
11. Specify the duration of your fundraising event in hours.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive important updates and information.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any urgent inquiries or follow-ups.
* **Non Profit Organization Name** (`Non Profit Organization Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your nonprofit organization.
* **Event Type** (`Event Type`, `text`, required: false): Specify the type of event you are hosting (e.g., charity run, bake sale, or charity walk).
* **Non Profit Fundraising Event Name** (`Non Profit Fundraising Event Name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of your non profit fundraising event.
* **Non Profit Fundraising Program Organizer** (`Non Profit Fundraising Program Organizer`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the person organizing the event for your organization.
* **Fundraising Goal** (`Fundraising Goal`, `text`, required: false): Set a realistic fundraising goal for your event.
* **Non Profit Fundraising Ending Date** (`Non Profit Fundraising Ending Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the ending date of your fundraising event.
* **Non Profit Fundraising Starting Date** (`Non Profit Fundraising Starting Date`, `date`, required: false): Enter the starting date of your fundraising event.
* **Non Profit Fundraising Time** (`Non Profit Fundraising Time`, `time`, required: false): Enter the duration of your fundraising event in hours.
