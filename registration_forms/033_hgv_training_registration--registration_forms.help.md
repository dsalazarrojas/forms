# HGV Training Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather information from potential students about their HGV training requirements and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the form's first page.
2. On the first page, enter your license status (Active or Inactive).
3. On the subsequent pages, you will be asked to provide more information about your training preferences and contact details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **License Status** (hgv_license_status, select_one, Required/Optional): Select your current license status, which should be either Active or Inactive.
* **Schedule Preferences** (hgv_schedule_preferences, text, Optional): Enter any specific schedule requirements or restrictions you have for the training.
* **Training Provider** (hgv_training_provider, text, Optional): If you have a preferred training provider, enter their name or location.
* **Contact Details** (hgv_contact_details, text, Optional): Provide your contact information, including name, address, phone number, and email.
* **Training Schedule** (hgv_training_schedule, date, Optional): If you have a specific date in mind for the training, enter it in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all fields that are relevant to your situation.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact the training provider for guidance.
* This form is intended for informational purposes, and your input will not be used for actual registration.
