# Farmers Dairy Group - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Farmers Dairy Group registration form is used to collect and store product information for farmers. This information includes product name, description, category, price, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Select the desired Category for your product.
2. Enter a clear Description of your product.
3. Choose the correct Product Status for your product.
4. If necessary, add tags to describe your product.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Category** (`Category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category for your product from the dropdown list.
* **Description** (`Description`, `text`, required): Enter a clear and concise description of your product.
* **Form ID** (`Form ID`, `number`, required): Enter a unique ID for your form, typically a numeric value.
* **Output File** (`Output File`, `select_one`, required): Choose the output file type for your form data.
* **Assigned Tool** (`Assigned Tool`, `select_one`, required): Select the tool assigned to process your form data.
* **Product Name** (`Product Name`, `text`, required): Enter a unique name for your product.
* **Product Description** (`Product Description`, `text`, required): Enter a brief description of your product.
* **Product Price** (`Product Price`, `number`, required): Enter the price of your product.
* **Product Image** (`Product Image`, `text`, required): Upload an image of your product.
* **Product Category** (`Product Category`, `select_multiple`, required): Select the category for your product from the dropdown list.
* **Contact Name** (`Contact Name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
* **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`, `email`, required): Enter the email of the contact person.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
* **Contact Note** (`Contact Note`, `note`, required): Enter any additional contact information or notes.
* **Product Weight** (`Product Weight`, `number`, required): Enter the weight of your product.
* **Product Weight Unit** (`Product Weight Unit`, `select_one`, required): Select the unit for your product weight.
* **Product Location** (`Product Location`, `text`, required): Enter the location of your product.
* **Product Location Type** (`Product Location Type`, `text`, required): Enter the type of location for your product.
* **Product Status** (`Product Status`, `select_one`, required): Choose the status of your product from the dropdown list.
* **Product Status Note** (`Product Status Note`, `text`, required): Enter any additional notes about the product status.
* **Product Tags** (`Product Tags`, `text`, required): Enter tags to describe your product.
* **Product Tags Hint** (`Product Tags Hint`, `text`, required): Enter any hints about the product tags.
* **Product Tags List** (`Product Tags List`, `text`, required): Enter the list of product tags.
* **Form Submit** (`Form Submit`, `select_one`, required): Select the "Submit" option to complete the form.
