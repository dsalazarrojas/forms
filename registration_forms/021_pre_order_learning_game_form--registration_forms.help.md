# Pre Order Learning Game Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who want to pre-order a learning game before its official release. Please fill out this form to express your interest in purchasing the game and we will keep you updated when it's available for sale.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "User Input" field with your name and any other relevant details.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is the contact information that we will use to communicate with you once the game is available.
3. Provide the title of the game you are interested in pre-ordering in the "Game Title" field.
4. Briefly describe the game in the "Game Description" field. This will help us understand your request better.
5. If applicable, enter the price of the game you are pre-ordering in the "Game Price" field.
6. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
7. Check the status of the game you are pre-ordering in the "Game Status" field.
8. Optionally, add any additional notes or comments in the "User Note" field.
9. Finally, add any notes or comments about the game in the "Game Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input**: This field is for you to provide your name and any other details you'd like to share with us.
* **Email**: Enter your email address so we can contact you once the game is available for sale.
* **Game Title**: Enter the title of the game you are interested in pre-ordering.
* **Game Description**: Briefly describe the game to help us understand your request better.
* **Game Price**: If applicable, enter the price of the game you are pre-ordering.
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method from the dropdown menu.
* **Game Status**: Check the status of the game you are pre-ordering (In Stock, Out of Stock, or Pre-Order).
* **User Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about your pre-order request.
* **Game Note**: Add any notes or comments about the game you are pre-ordering.
