# Vision Board Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for participants to register for the Vision Board Workshop. It collects personal information and session preferences to prepare for the workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Enter your Date of Birth.
5. Choose your session preferences.
6. Confirm your consent to share data with a "Yes" or "No" response.
7. Add any relevant Notes if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Enter your first name as it is known to you.
* **Last Name (text)**: Enter your last name as it is known to you.
* **Email (email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone (text)**: Enter your phone number for communication with the workshop team.
* **Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Session Preferences (select multiple)**: Choose the sessions you are interested in attending by selecting "Yes".
* **Morning (select one)**: Choose if you are interested in the morning session.
* **Afternoon (select one)**: Choose if you are interested in the afternoon session.
* **Evening (select one)**: Choose if you are interested in the evening session.
* **Consent to Share Data (select one)**: Confirm your consent to share your data with a "Yes" or "No" response.
* **Consent to Share Data (select one)**: This field is likely a duplicate and can be ignored.
* **Notes (note)**: Add any relevant notes or comments about your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields.
* Double-check your session preferences before submitting the form.
* Be aware of the email address and phone number you provide, as they may be used for communication with the workshop team.
