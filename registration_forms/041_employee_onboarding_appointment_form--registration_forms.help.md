# Employee Onboarding Appointment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather essential details from new employees for an efficient onboarding process. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth transition into your new role.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your employee name, job title, email, and phone number as prompted.
2. Select a suitable date and start time for your onboarding appointment.
3. Once you've filled out all the required fields, review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name** (`employee_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your official records.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required: false): Enter your job title or position within the company.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your official company email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your work phone number (mobile or office).
* **Schedule Appointment** (`schedule_appointment`, date, required: false): Choose a date for your onboarding appointment from the calendar.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required: false): Select the start time for your onboarding meeting.

## Tips

* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Choose a date and start time that suits you best.
* If you experience any issues or have questions, contact your HR department for assistance.
* This form will help us create a tailored onboarding experience for you. Thank you for your cooperation.
