# Mindful Leadership In Healthcare Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mindful Leadership In Healthcare Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants registering for a Mindful Leadership In Healthcare workshop. This form is a crucial step in the registration process, ensuring that we have accurate and complete information about the participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Details section with your personal details.
2. Provide your contact information in the Contact Details section.
3. Add your emergency contacts in the Emergency Contacts section.
4. Enter the necessary details about your emergency contacts in the Emergency Contact Details section (if applicable).
5. Leave the Workshop Info section empty if not required.
6. In the Additional Info section, you can add any extra information you feel is important to share.
7. Read and agree to the terms of the form by clicking on the "Agree" button.
8. Finally, submit the form to complete the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details** (1, text, required): Please fill in your personal details, including your name, email, and phone number.
* **Contact Details** (2, text, required): Enter your contact information, such as your email, phone number, and address.
* **Registration Details** (3, text, optional): This section is for workshop registration details, but is not mandatory.
* **Emergency Contacts** (4, text, required): List your emergency contacts' names and relationships.
* **Emergency Contact Details** (5, text, optional): If you have emergency contacts, please add their details here.
* **Workshop Info** (6, text, optional): Leave this section empty if not required.
* **Submit And Share** (7, text, required): Click the "Submit" button to complete the registration process.
* **Agreement** (8, text, required): Please read and agree to the terms before submitting the form.
* **Submit** (9, text, required): Once you have completed the form, click the "Submit" button to finalize your registration.
