# Sports Club Membership Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register yourself or someone else for membership in the sports club.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read each field explanation carefully to ensure you understand what information is required.
2. Fill in the provided fields with accurate and correct information.
3. Double-check the form before submission to avoid errors.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.
* **Email** (Email, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that you use regularly.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required: false): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YY.
* **Contact Number** (Contact Number, text, required: false): Enter your contact number that you use for communication.
* **Address** (Address, text, required: false): Enter your current address for membership purposes.
* **Membership Number** (Membership Number, number, required: false): Enter your membership number if you already have one.
* **Membership Start Date** (Membership Start Date, date, required: false): Enter the date your membership started.
* **Membership End Date** (Membership End Date, date, required: false): Enter the date your membership is expected to end.
* **Membership Category** (Membership Category, select_one, required: false): Select your category of membership from the provided options: Member, Associate, Honorary, or Member (Student).
* **Club** (Club, select_multiple, required: false): Select the sports club you are interested in joining from the provided options: Sports, Fitness, Recreation, Arts, Music, Dance, Culture, Education, or Other.
* **Club Type** (Club Type, select_one, required: false): Select the type of club you are interested in from the provided options: Public, Private, Community, School, University, or Other.
* **Notes** (Notes, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes about your membership or club preferences.
* **Confirm Membership Number** (Confirm Membership Number, text, required: false): Re-enter your membership number for verification.

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* Ensure that you are not already registered as a member of the sports club.
* If you are experiencing issues or have questions, contact the sports club administration for assistance.
