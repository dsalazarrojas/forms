# subsidy_program_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The subsidy program enrollment form is a general-purpose form for individuals or organizations to apply for subsidies or benefits offered by the program. The form gathers user information, organization information, and other relevant details to process the application.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user information, including your name and contact details.
2. Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
3. Select the type of program you are applying for (Program A, Program B, or Program C).
4. Attach any required documentation (if applicable).
5. Enter your contact phone number.
6. Enter your contact email address.
7. Provide your address in two lines (street address and postal code).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (`user_information`, text, optional): This field is for your personal information, including your name and contact details.
* **organization_name** (`organization_name`, text, optional): If you are representing an organization, enter its name here.
* **program_type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of program you are applying for (Program A, Program B, or Program C).
* **required_docs** (`required_documentation`, text, optional): Attach any required documentation or supporting documents for your application.
* **contact_phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number.
* **contact_email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter your contact email address.
* **address_line1** (`address_line1`, text, optional): Enter the first line of your street address.
* **address_line2** (`address_line2`, text, optional): Enter the second line of your street address.
* **city** (`city`, text, optional): Enter your city.
* **state** (`state`, text, optional): Enter your state or province.
* **zip** (`zip`, text, optional): Enter your postal code or zip code.

## Tips

- Ensure you provide accurate and up-to-date information.
- Make sure to attach required documentation if requested.
- If you are representing an organization, ensure the organization name matches the name on any official documents.
- Double-check your contact details for accuracy.
