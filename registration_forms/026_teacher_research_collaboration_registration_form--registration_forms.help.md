# teacher_research_collaboration_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in teacher research collaborations. 

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete the form:

1. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your Email address in the "Email" field (this should be a valid email address).
4. Select your research interests from the "Research Interests" field (yes/no options are available).
5. Enter your Research Area in the "Research Area" field.
6. Enter your Research Project Name in the "Research Project Name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required/optional: `false`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required/optional: `false`): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required/optional: `false`): Enter a valid email address.
* **Research Interests** (`4`, `select_multiple`, required/optional: `false`): Select your research interests (yes/no options available).
* **Research Area** (`5`, `text`, required/optional: `false`): Enter your research area.
* **Research Project Name** (`6`, `text`, required/optional: `false`): Enter your research project name.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper tracking and communication.
* If you are unsure about any field, please don't hesitate to contact us for clarification.
* Once you have completed the form, you can review and edit your information as needed.
