# Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and manage your retail franchise information, enabling you to access exclusive resources and connect with other franchisees.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Retail Franchise Resource Portal Registration**
	* This is the form title and the main entry point for your registration.
2. **Affiliate Name**
	* This field should be filled with the name of your affiliation or organization.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
3. **Affiliate Phone**
	* Enter the phone number of your affiliation or organization.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
4. **Email**
	* Enter your affiliation's email address for official communication purposes.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
5. **Store Address**
	* Enter your store's physical address for location verification.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
6. **Store Name**
	* Enter your store's name as it appears in public records.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
7. **Location**
	* Enter your store's location (city, state, or country).
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
8. **Franchise Name**
	* Enter the name of the franchise you are affiliated with.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
9. **Franchise Number**
	* Enter the number or ID of your franchise.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
10. **Store Type**
	* Select the type of retail location from the dropdown menu:
		+ Retail
		+ Other
	* If you select "Other," you can provide a detailed description of your store type in the "What type of retail location is this?" field.
11. **What type of retail location is this?** (Optional)
	* If you selected "Other" for store type, describe the type of retail location in this field.
12. **Number of Locations**
	* Enter the number of retail locations you manage.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
13. **Number of Employees**
	* Enter the total number of employees in your organization.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
14. **Number of Franchisees**
	* Enter the number of franchisees you have in your network.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
15. **Franchisees Names**
	* Select the names of the franchisees in your network from the dropdown menu:
		+ John
		+ Mary
		+ Jane
	* If you have more or less than three franchisees, you can add or remove names as needed.
16. **Affiliate Type**
	* Select the type of affiliation from the dropdown menu:
		+ Franchisee
		+ Franchisee Owner
		+ Other
	* If you select "Other," you can provide a detailed description of your affiliation type in the "What type of affiliate are you?" field.
17. **What type of affiliate are you?** (Optional)
	* If you selected "Other," describe your affiliation type in this field.
18. **Franchise Type**
	* Select the type of franchise from the dropdown menu:
		+ Retail
		+ Hospitality
		+ Service - Automotive
		+ Other
	* If you select "Other," you can provide a detailed description of your franchise type in this field.
19. **Affiliation Relationship**
	* Select the relationship between your franchise and the parent or subsidiary company from the dropdown menu:
		+ Parent Company
		+ Subsidiary Company
		+ Other Company
	* If you select "Other," you can describe the relationship in the "What is the relationship with the parent or subsidiary company?" field.
20. **What is the relationship with the parent or subsidiary company?** (Optional)
	* If you selected "Other," describe the relationship in this field.
21. **Date of Birth**
	* Enter your date of birth for verification purposes.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
22. **Start Date**
	* Enter the date you started your franchise or affiliation.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
23. **End Date**
	* Enter the date you ended your franchise or affiliation.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
24. **Start Time**
	* Enter the start time of your work hours or business hours.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.
25. **End Time**
	* Enter the end time of your work hours or business hours.
	* Note: This field is not necessarily required.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure correct registration.
* If you have any issues or concerns during the registration process, contact our support team for assistance.
* Once you have completed the form, review it carefully before submitting to ensure all information is correct.
* If you need to make any changes after submission, contact our support team to update your registration.
