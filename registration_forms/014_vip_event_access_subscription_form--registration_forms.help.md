# vip_event_access_subscription_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register customers for access to a VIP event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your preferred payment method from the "Payment Method" field.
   * If you select "Other" from the "Payment Method" field, please enter the payment details in the "Payment Info" field.
6. If you select any of the "VIP Info" options, you will be able to access the corresponding VIP benefits.
7. Click "Submit" to complete the form and register for the VIP event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name here. This field is required for processing your registration.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name here. This field is required for processing your registration.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address here. This field is required for processing your registration and for us to contact you with event updates.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number here. This field is required for processing your registration.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, optional): Select your preferred payment method from the options provided.
	+ Select "Cash" to make a cash payment.
	+ Select "Credit Card" to use your credit card for payment.
	+ Select "Bank Transfer" to use a bank transfer for payment.
	+ Select "Other" to specify an alternate payment method.
* **Payment Info** (Payment Info, text, optional): If you selected "Other" in the "Payment Method" field, enter the details of your alternate payment method here.
* **VIP Info** (VIP Info, select_multiple, optional): Select the VIP benefits you would like to access. This field is optional, but selecting one or more of the options will grant you access to the corresponding benefits.
	+ Select "Red Carpet" for red carpet treatment.
	+ Select "VIP Lounge Access" for access to the VIP lounge.
	+ Select "Early Access" for early access to the event.
