# Professional Networking Group Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Professional Networking Group Renewal Form is a crucial step in the membership renewal process, allowing the group to update and maintain accurate member information. This form is designed to be filled out once by each member, ensuring that their details are up-to-date and preventing duplicate submissions.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the instructions carefully before starting.
- Make sure you are logged in to access the form.
- Fill out the form in a single sitting, ensuring all fields are accurate.
- Submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Membership Renewal Form** (`membership_renewal_form`, `note`, required: false)
This page contains a text area for any additional comments or notes about your renewal.
- Write any relevant comments or notes about your membership status in this box.
- Use this field to provide any additional context that might be helpful for the group.

### 2. **Member Details** (`member_details`, `select_multiple`, required: false)
Select your current status within the group:
- Choose 'Yes' if you are currently a member of the group.
- Choose 'No' if you are not a member or are unsure.

### 3. **Renewal Date** (`renewal_date`, `date`, required: false)
Enter your preferred renewal date:
- Select the date you would like to renew your membership.
- Make sure to pick a date that is not too far in the future.

### 4. **Member Name** (`member_name`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your name as it appears on your membership:
- Type in your full name exactly as it appears on your membership documents.
- Double-check for spelling and accuracy.

### 5. **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: false)
Enter your contact email:
- Type in your email address where you can be reached.
- Make sure to enter a valid email.

### 6. **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required: false)
Enter your phone number:
- Type in your phone number where you can be reached.
- Use international dialing code for non-local numbers.
