# Holistic Program Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Holistic Program Application Form is designed to gather information from interested participants to facilitate their application process for the holistic program. This form is a critical component of the application process, and it is essential to fill it out accurately and completely.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Holistic Program Application Form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill out the **Program Name** field with the name of the program you are interested in participating in.
2.  Enter your **Participant Name** in the designated field.
3.  Provide your **Email** address to allow us to contact you regarding your application.
4.  Enter your **Phone** number to enable quick communication.
5.  Enter your **Date of Birth** in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
6.  Provide your **Address** where you can be contacted.
7.  Enter your **Zip Code** for mailing purposes.
8.  Indicate the desired **Program Duration** (number of months or years) of your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Program Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the program you are interested in applying to. This will help us to match your application with the correct program.
*   **Participant Name** (Text, Optional): Please enter your name as it should be displayed on any correspondence related to your application.
*   **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address so that we can contact you regarding your application status and any additional questions we may have.
*   **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number to enable quick communication regarding your application.
*   **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g., 1990-06-15). This will help us to verify your age for the program requirements.
*   **Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address where you would like to be contacted or where you can be reached if needed.
*   **Zip Code** (Text, Optional): Enter your zip code to facilitate postal communication and to ensure correct mailing of any materials we may need to send you.
*   **Program Duration** (Number, Optional): Indicate the number of months or years you are interested in participating in the program.
