# Exclusive Preview Patron RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for patrons to RSVP to an exclusive preview event, providing attendees with the opportunity to secure their spot after viewing the event preview.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "Event Details" section with any relevant information about the event, such as event name, date, or location.
2. Provide your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" section, including any necessary information about yourself as an attendee.
3. Select one of the two options in the "Submit" field to indicate your preferred action after viewing the event preview: "Submit and Send" or "Save for Later".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Event Details
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): 
  This section is for you to provide any relevant information about the event you are RSVPing to.
### Attendee Details
* **Attendee Details** (`attendee_details`, text, optional): 
  This section is for you to provide any necessary information about yourself as an attendee.
### Submit
* **Submit** (`submit`, select_one, required): 
  Choose one of the two options: 
  - **Submit and Send**: This will submit your RSVP and send it directly for processing.
  - **Save for Later**: This will save your RSVP for later, allowing you to come back and submit it when ready.
