# Community Health Workshop RSVP - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to register for a Community Health Workshop. Please fill out the form accurately to help us better understand your needs and provide a comfortable experience for you.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form with your details accurately and honestly.
2.  Select the correct workshop title from the drop-down menu.
3.  Fill out your full name and email address to enable us to contact you.
4.  Indicate your attendance status (Yes, I will be there; No, I cannot attend; I am still undecided).
5.  If you're bringing guests, fill out the number of additional guests you'll be bringing.
6.  Provide any dietary restrictions or allergies you have, or fill out this field if you don't need to provide any information.
7.  Indicate if you need childcare services during the workshop.
8.  If you heard about this workshop through any means other than the provided options, select 'Other' from the drop-down menu.
9.  Lastly, if you require any special accommodations, type them in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, select_one, required): Select the specific workshop you're registering for. This will help us ensure that we have the correct materials and resources ready for you.
*   **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required): Select the date of the workshop you'll be attending to help us schedule and plan accordingly.
*   **Your Full Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Provide your full name so we know who's attending the workshop.
*   **Email Address** (`attendee_email`, email, required): This is where we'll send you confirmations and updates about the workshop.
*   **Will you be attending?** (`attendance_status`, select_one, required): Please let us know if you're able to attend, not attending, or if you're still undecided.
*   **Number of Additional Guests** (`guest_count`, number, optional): If you're bringing guests, let us know how many so we can make necessary arrangements.
*   **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_needs`, text, optional): If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, please let us know so we can cater to your needs.
*   **Do you require childcare during the workshop?** (`childcare_needed`, select_one, required): This is an important question as some workshops may require childcare services.
*   **How did you hear about this workshop?** (`marketing_source`, select_one, optional): Please let us know where you first learned about this workshop, and it will help us with our marketing efforts.
*   **Do you require any special accommodations?** (`special_accommodations`, text, optional): If you have specific needs or requirements, this is the field to fill out.
