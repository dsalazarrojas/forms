# Autism Support Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects demographic information from individuals to provide tailored support and accommodations for each participant in the Autism Support Event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your registration date and select the event name from the drop-down list.
2.  Enter your first and last name.
3.  Enter your email address and phone number.
4.  Select your relationship to the autism community.
5.  If you are attending with others, enter the number of people in your group and the ages of children attending (if applicable).
6.  If you have dietary restrictions or allergies, please specify them in the text field.
7.  If you have accessibility needs, select the required accommodations from the dropdown list.
8.  If you need communication support, select the required accommodations from the dropdown list.
9.  Select the breakout sessions you are interested in attending.
10.  If you are interested in networking with others, select your level of interest.
11.  If you are interested in volunteering at the event, select your level of interest.
12.  If you are willing to volunteer, select the tasks you would like to assist with.
13.  If you have attended previous events, select your attendance history.
14.  If you have any suggestions or comments, you can enter them in the text field.
15.  Finally, select your subscription preference for the event newsletter.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date you are registering for the event.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event you are registering for.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event scheduled.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Relationship to Autism Community** (`relationship_to_autism`, select_one, required): Select your relationship to the autism community (Parent of Child with Autism, Individual with Autism, etc.).
* **Number of Attendees** (`number_of_attendees`, number, required): Enter the number of people attending the event with you.
* **Number of Children Attending** (`children_attending`, number, optional): Enter the number of children attending the event with you, if applicable.
* **Ages of Children Attending** (`ages_of_children`, text, optional): Enter the age range of children attending the event with you.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies you may have.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_one, optional): Select any accessibility needs you may have (Wheelchair Access, Reserved Parking, etc.).
* **Communication Support** (`communication_support`, select_multiple, optional): Select any communication support you may need (ASL Interpreter, CART Captioning, etc.).
* **Interest in Breakout Sessions** (`breakout_session_interest`, select_multiple, optional): Select the breakout sessions you are interested in attending.
* **Interested in Networking** (`networking_interest`, select_one, optional): Select your level of interest in networking with others.
* **Interested in Volunteering** (`volunteer_interest`, select_one, optional): Select your level of interest in volunteering at the event.
* **Volunteer Role Preference** (`volunteer_roles`, select_multiple, optional): Select the volunteer role you would like to assist with.
* **Previous Events Attended** (`previous_events_attended`, select_one, optional): Select your attendance history with previous events.
* **Topics You Would Like Discussed** (`feedback_topics`, text, optional): Suggest any topics you would like to be discussed at future events.
* **Special Requests or Comments** (`special_requests`, text, optional): Enter any special requests or comments you may have.
* **Sign Up for Newsletter** (`newsletter_signup`, select_one, optional): Select whether you would like to sign up for the event newsletter.

Note: This help guide is a general outline, and the exact wording of each field can be adjusted based on the specific needs and requirements of the event organizers.
