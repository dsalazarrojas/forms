# NGO Seminar Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to check in attendees for an NGO seminar. It is designed to gather relevant information about the attendee, including their session participation and arrival time.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the "Seminar Entry" field with a brief description of your seminar participation.
* Provide your name and email address in the "Name" and "Email" fields, respectively.
* Select your session participation status from the "Session Participation" field.
* If you have any additional comments or notes, you can enter them in the "Additional Comments" field.
* If you have any other session participation that is not listed, please provide a detailed description in the "Session Participation Other" field.
* Enter your seminar ID, attendee ID, and session ID in the corresponding fields.
* Select your check in status from the "Check In Status" field.
* If you have any arrival time notes, please provide them in the "Arrival Time Note" field.
* Enter any additional attendee details in the "Attendee Details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Entry** (Text, Optional):
	+ A brief description of your seminar participation.
* **Name** (Text, Required):
	+ Your name, as it should be spelled correctly.
* **Email** (Email, Required):
	+ Your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Session Participation** (Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select all sessions you are participating in. If you are participating in multiple sessions, select all applicable options.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional):
	+ Any additional comments or questions you may have.
* **Session Participation Other (if not listed above)** (Text, Optional):
	+ A detailed description of any session participation that is not listed.
* **Seminar Id** (Number, Required):
	+ The seminar ID assigned to you by the seminar staff.
* **Attendee Id** (Number, Required):
	+ The attendee ID assigned to you by the seminar staff.
* **Session Id** (Number, Required):
	+ The session ID assigned to you by the seminar staff.
* **Check In Status** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Select your check in status: checked in, checked out, late check in, or no show.
* **Arrival Time Note** (Text, Optional):
	+ Any notes about your arrival time.
* **Attendee Details** (Text, Optional):
	+ Any additional details about yourself as an attendee.
* **Session Participation Hint** (Text, Optional):
	+ A text field where you can enter any additional session participation information.
* **Arrival Time** (Time, Optional):
	+ Your arrival time, in 24-hour format.
* **Check In Time** (Time, Optional):
	+ The time you checked in, in 24-hour format.
* **Attendee Follow-up** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Select if you need to be followed up by the seminar staff.
* **Notes** (Note, Optional):
	+ Any additional notes or comments.
* **Session Id** (Number, Required):
	+ The session ID assigned to you by the seminar staff.
* **Attendee Id** (Number, Required):
	+ The attendee ID assigned to you by the seminar staff.
