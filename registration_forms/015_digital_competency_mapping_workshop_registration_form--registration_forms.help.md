# Digital Competency Mapping Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register for the Digital Competency Mapping Workshop. It helps us plan and organize the event to meet your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your first name in the `first_name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `last_name` field.
3. Provide your email address in the `email` field. This will help us contact you if needed.
4. Enter your phone number in the `phone` field if you would like to be contacted via phone.
5. Select the preferred date for the workshop using the `preferred_date` field.
6. Choose the preferred time for the workshop using the `preferred_time` field.
7. Select the workshop type using the `workshop_type` field.
8. Enter the workshop location in the `workshop_location` field.
9. Add any additional information you want to provide in the `additional_info` field.
10. Click the "submit" button at the end to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): This is the first name of the person registering.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): This is the last name of the person registering.
* **Email Address** (`email`, email, required: false): Provide your email address so we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like us to contact you by phone.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, required: false): Select your preferred date for the workshop.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, required: false): Choose a preferred time for the workshop.
* **Workshop Type** (`workshop_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the workshop type you are interested in.
* **Workshop Location** (`workshop_location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you would like the workshop to be held.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, required: false): Add any extra information you think is helpful.

## Tips
* Double-check that all required fields are filled before submitting.
* Enter accurate and correct information to avoid delays or issues.
* Make sure to select a workshop type and date/time combination that suits your needs.
