# Virtual Trick Or Treat Party Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Trick Or Treat Party Registration Form is designed for parents to register their children for a virtual trick-or-treat party. This form helps us gather essential information to organize and execute the party smoothly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Party Details section with the requested information.
2. Choose your RSVP preference and provide a message if necessary.
3. Enter your contact information for us to reach you.
4. Decide if you'd like to share party details on Facebook and provide the party's Facebook link.
5. Enter any additional party details or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Party Details**: Enter the date and time of your party.
* **Party Date**: Enter the date of your party. Please use the date format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Party Time**: Enter the time of your party. Please use the 24-hour format HH:MM.
* **RSVP**: Choose whether you'll be attending the party or not. You can also provide a message if you'll be attending.
* **Contact Information**: Enter your parent email and phone number so we can reach you.
* **Parent Email**: Enter your email address for us to contact you.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Share Party Details**: Decide if you'd like to share your party details on Facebook.
* **Share on Facebook**: Choose whether you'd like to share the party on Facebook or not.
* **Facebook Party Group**: Enter the Facebook group where your party will be shared.
* **Party Facebook Link**: Enter the link to your Facebook party.
* **Party Message**: Enter a message for us to know you're attending the party.
* **RSVP Message**: Enter a message if you'll be attending the party.
* **Party Notes**: Enter any other party details or notes.
* **Parent Notes**: Enter any personal notes for us.
* **Other Party Details**: Enter any other details about your party.
* **Other RSVP**: Choose whether you'd like to attend the party or not.
* **Other Notes**: Enter any other notes about your party.
* **Submit**: Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.
