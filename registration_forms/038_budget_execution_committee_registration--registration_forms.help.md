# Budget Execution Committee Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new members for the Budget Execution Committee. It captures essential information about each member, including their name, contact details, roles, committee affiliation, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Member Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. **Email Address**: Provide your personal or professional email address where you can be contacted regarding committee matters.
3. **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number to ensure easy communication with committee members.
4. **Organization or Department**: Identify the organization or department you are representing, if any.
5. **Job Title**: Enter your job title or position within your organization.
6. **Role on Committee**: Select your role on the committee from the provided options (Chair, Vice Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Member, or Alternate).
7. **Member Type**: Choose your type of membership from the options provided (Full-time, Part-time, Representative).
8. **Committee Name**: Enter the name of the committee you are registering for.
9. **Term Start Date**: Enter the date your membership on the committee begins.
10. **Term End Date**: (Optional) Enter the date your membership on the committee ends, if applicable.
11. **Years of Budget Management Experience**: Provide the number of years you have experience in budget management.
12. **Areas of Expertise**: Select all relevant areas of expertise that apply to you (Financial Planning, Budget Analysis, Accounting, Forecasting, Program Management, Risk Assessment, or Other).
13. **Meeting Frequency Availability**: Choose how often you are available to attend meetings (Monthly, Quarterly, As Needed, or Flexible).
14. **Preferred Communication Method**: Select how you would like to be contacted regarding committee matters (Email, Phone, In-person, Virtual Meeting, or Mixed).
15. **Any Conflicts of Interest to Declare**: If you have any conflicts of interest, enter them here.
16. **Agree to Confidentiality Agreement**: Confirm your willingness to abide by confidentiality agreements.
17. **Registration Date**: The date of your registration will be auto-filled.
