# Fashion Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals who are participating in a fashion-related event. This data will be used to create a unique profile for you, and to provide insights to event organizers and sponsors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form with your personal details, such as name, email, and phone number.
- Choose your gender and select your event type (Fashion or Beauty).
- Provide your date of birth, marital status, and occupation.
- Enter your address and any additional notes you would like to share.
- Select the event name, start date, end date, duration, type, venue, city, and country.
- Provide any additional event notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name (full_name)**: Your full name as it appears on your identification document.
- **Email (email)**: Your primary email address that we will use to communicate with you.
- **Phone (phone)**: Your primary phone number that we will use to contact you.
- **Gender (gender)**: Select your gender from the provided options.
- **Date of Birth (date_of_birth)**: Your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
- **Marital Status (marital_status)**: Select your marital status from the provided options.
- **Age (age)**: Your age in years.
- **Occupation (occupation)**: Your current occupation or profession.
- **Nationality (nationality)**: Your country of birth or nationality.
- **Address (address)**: Your physical address.
- **Additional Notes (notes)**: Any additional information you would like to share with us.
- **Event Name (event_name)**: The name of the event you are participating in.
- **Event Start Date (event_start_date)**: The start date of the event.
- **Event End Date (event_end_date)**: The end date of the event.
- **Event Duration (event_duration)**: The duration of the event in hours and minutes.
- **Event Type (event_type)**: Select the type of event you are participating in (Fashion or Beauty).
- **Event Venue (event_venue)**: The location of the event.
- **Event City (event_city)**: The city of the event.
- **Event Country (event_country)**: The country of the event.
- **Event Notes (event_notes)**: Any additional notes about the event.
