# lost_pet_report_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The lost pet report form is a tool to help pet owners report lost pets to the authorities. Fill out this form with the required information about your pet and their last known location to aid in their safe recovery.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Enter a brief description of your pet in the "Pet Description" field.
2.  Provide the last known location of your pet in the "Last Known Location" field.
3.  Enter your name as it appears on your identification in the "Owner Name" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Owner Phone" field.
5.  Enter your email address in the "Owner Email" field.
6.  Enter the date you last saw your pet in the "Date Missing" field.
7.  Enter the time you last saw your pet in the "Time Missing" field.
8.  Provide details about the last seen location of your pet in the "Last Seen Location Details" field.
9.  Enter the link to a photo of your pet in the "Photo Url" field.
10. Select the type of your pet from the "Pet Type" dropdown menu.
11. Select the color of your pet from the "Pet Color" field.
12. Select the size of your pet from the "Pet Size" field.
13. Enter the age of your pet in the "Pet Age" field.
14. Enter the collar ID or microchip number of your pet in the "Collar ID" field.
15. Enter the microchip number of your pet in the "Microchip Number" field.
16. Select the type of microchip used on your pet from the "Microchip Type" field.
17. Enter any veterinary information about your pet in the "Vet Information" field.
18. Enter the location where you are reporting the lost pet in the "Report Location" field.
19. Enter the date of the report in the "Report Date" field.
20. Enter the time of the report in the "Report Time" field.
21. Enter the reason for the report in the "Report Reason" field (optional).
22. Enter any additional details about the report in the "Report Details" field (optional).
23. Sign the form with your signature in the "Owner Signature" field.
24. A signature from an officer will be required to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Description** (1, text, required): Enter a brief description of your pet.
* **Last Known Location** (2, text, required): Provide the location where you last saw your pet.
* **Owner Name** (3, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Owner Phone** (4, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Owner Email** (5, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Date Missing** (6, date, required): Enter the date you last saw your pet.
* **Time Missing** (7, time, required): Enter the time you last saw your pet.
* **Last Seen Location Details** (8, text, required): Provide details about the last seen location of your pet.
* **Photo Url** (9, text, required): Enter the link to a photo of your pet.
* **Pet Type** (10, select_one, required): Select the type of your pet.
* **Pet Color** (11, select_multiple, required): Select the color of your pet.
* **Pet Size** (12, select_multiple, required): Select the size of your pet.
* **Pet Age** (13, number, required): Enter the age of your pet.
* **Collar ID** (14, text, required): Enter the collar ID or microchip number of your pet.
* **Microchip Number** (15, text, required): Enter the microchip number of your pet.
* **Microchip Type** (16, select_one, required): Select the type of microchip used on your pet.
* **Vet Information** (17, text, required): Enter any veterinary information about your pet.
* **Report Location** (18, text, required): Enter the location where you are reporting the lost pet.
* **Report Date** (19, date, required): Enter the date of the report.
* **Report Time** (20, time, required): Enter the time of the report.
* **Report Reason** (21, text, optional): Enter the reason for the report.
* **Report Details** (22, text, optional): Enter any additional details about the report.
* **Owner Signature** (23, text, required): Sign the form with your signature.
* **Officer Signature** (24, text, required): A signature from an officer will be required to complete the form.
* **Submit** (25, text, optional): Click to submit the form.

Note: Please fill out the form with as much detail as possible to aid in the quick recovery of your lost pet.
