# Boat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Boat Registration Form is designed to gather essential information for the registration of a boat. This form collects data about the owner, boat details, and registration preferences to ensure accurate and efficient registration processing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Enter the owner's first name and last name in the respective fields.
2.  Enter the owner's contact email address in the "Owner Email" field.
3.  Provide the owner's phone number in the "Owner Phone" field.
4.  Enter the boat's street address in the "Street Address" field.
5.  Enter the city and state where the boat is docked in the "City" and "State" fields, respectively.
6.  Enter the boat's ZIP code in the "ZIP Code" field.
7.  Enter the boat's make and model in the "Boat Make" and "Boat Model" fields, respectively.
8.  Enter the year of manufacture in the "Boat Year" field.
9.  Enter the 12-character hull ID (HIN) in the "Boat Hull ID (HIN)" field.
10.  Enter the boat's length in feet in the "Boat Length" field.
11.  Select the boat type from the drop-down list in the "Boat Type" field.
12.  Select the primary material of the boat's hull from the drop-down list in the "Hull Material" field.
13.  Select the type of propulsion from the drop-down list in the "Propulsion Type" field.
14.  If the boat has an engine, enter its make and horsepower rating in the "Engine Make" and "Engine Horsepower" fields, respectively.
15.  Select the type of fuel from the drop-down list in the "Fuel Type" field.
16.  Select the primary use of the boat from the drop-down list in the "Primary Use" field.
17.  Enter the marina where the boat is docked in the "Home Port" field.
18.  Enter the marina name where the boat is stored in the "Marina Name" field (optional).
19.  If transferring ownership, enter the previous registration number in the "Previous Registration Number" field.
20.  If a lien is involved, enter the lien holder's name and address in the "Lien Holder Name" and "Lien Holder Address" fields, respectively.
21.  Select the registration duration from the drop-down list in the "Registration Type" field.
22.  Select the payment method from the drop-down list in the "Payment Method" field.
23.  Certify that you are the legal owner by selecting "Yes, I Certify" in the "Ownership Certification" field.
24.  Sign with your full name as the owner in the "Owner Signature" field.
25.  Enter the date of signing in the "Signature Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Owner First Name (required)**: Enter the first name of the boat's owner.
*   **Owner Last Name (required)**: Enter the last name of the boat's owner.
*   **Owner Email (required)**: Enter the email address of the boat's owner.
*   **Owner Phone (required)**: Enter the phone number of the boat's owner.
*   **Street Address (required)**: Enter the street address where the boat is docked.
*   **City (required)**: Enter the city where the boat is docked.
*   **State (required)**: Enter the state where the boat is docked.
*   **ZIP Code (required)**: Enter the ZIP code where the boat is docked.
*   **Boat Make (required)**: Enter the manufacturer name of the boat.
*   **Boat Model (required)**: Enter the model name of the boat.
*   **Boat Year (required)**: Enter the year of manufacture of the boat.
*   **Boat Hull ID (HIN) (required)**: Enter the 12-character hull ID (HIN) of the boat.
*   **Boat Length (required)**: Enter the length of the boat in feet.
*   **Boat Type (required)**: Select the category of the boat from the drop-down list.
*   **Hull Material (required)**: Select the primary material of the boat's hull from the drop-down list.
*   **Propulsion Type (required)**: Select the type of engine from the drop-down list.
*   **Engine Make (optional)**: If the boat has an engine, enter its manufacturer name. (not required)
*   **Engine Horsepower (optional)**: If the boat has an engine, enter its horsepower rating. (not required)
*   **Fuel Type (required)**: Select the type of fuel from the drop-down list.
*   **Primary Use (required)**: Select the primary use of the boat from the drop-down list.
*   **Home Port (required)**: Enter the marina where the boat is docked.
*   **Marina Name (optional)**: Enter the marina name where the boat is stored.
*   **Previous Registration Number (optional)**: If transferring ownership, enter the previous registration number. (not required)
*   **Lien Holder Name (optional)**: If a lien is involved, enter the lien holder's name. (not required)
*   **Lien Holder Address (optional)**: If a lien is involved, enter the lien holder's address. (not required)
*   **Registration Type (required)**: Select the registration duration from the drop-down list.
*   **Payment Method (required)**: Select the payment method from the drop-down list.
*   **Ownership Certification (required)**: Certify that you are the legal owner by selecting "Yes, I Certify".
*   **Owner Signature (required)**: Sign with your full name as the boat's owner.
*   **Signature Date (required)**: Enter the date of signing.
