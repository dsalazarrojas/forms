# Factory AI Integration Council Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Factory AI Integration Council Registration Form is designed to gather information from individuals interested in joining the Factory AI Integration Council. This form helps us understand your background, roles, and motivations for joining the council.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Click on the "Submit" button to begin filling out the form.
2.  Fill out the form by entering your information in each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Manufacturing Leaders** (`manufacturing_leaders`, text, not required): Please enter your background or experience in the manufacturing industry.
* **Roles** (`roles`, select_multiple, not required): Choose one or multiple roles that you think you'd be interested in representing on the council. Options: Yes, No. 
* **Interests** (`interests`, select_multiple, not required): Select one or multiple interests that you think align with the Factory AI Integration Council's goals. Options: Yes, No.
* **Motivations** (`motivations`, text, not required): Explain why you are interested in joining the Factory AI Integration Council. Please be as brief or detailed as you'd like.
* **AI Professionals** (`ai_professionals`, text, not required): If you have any experience with AI, please describe your background or certifications in this field.
* **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, select_one, not required): Indicate whether or not you'd like to be contacted for further discussion.
* **Email** (`email`, email, not required): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, not required): If you'd like to be contacted, please provide your phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, not required): If you're representing an organization, please provide the name of the organization.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form carefully and truthfully to help us better understand your interest in the Factory AI Integration Council.
* If you have any questions or need clarification on any of the fields, feel free to contact us.
* Thank you for your interest in joining the Factory AI Integration Council!
