# Youth Entrepreneurship Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants for a Youth Entrepreneurship Event. Event organizers, sponsors, or coordinators use this form to gather event details, participant information, contact information, and business details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the Event Details section, providing the name, date, and description of the event.
2. Provide the participant's information, including their name, age, and occupation.
3. Share your contact information, including your name and phone number.
4. If applicable, fill in the Business Information section with details about your business or venture.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details (Event Details)** (`id: 1`, `type: text`, required: false): This section is where you will find the form title, event name, date, and description.
* **Participant Info (Participant Info)** (`id: 2`, `type: text`, required: false): In this section, please provide your name, age, and occupation.
* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`id: 3`, `type: text`, required: false): Share your name and phone number to ensure we can get in touch with you.
* **Business Information (Business Information)** (`id: 4`, `type: text`, required: false): If you are registering with your business or venture, please provide details such as your business name and type of industry.

## Tips
- Fill in your details accurately and thoroughly.
- Make sure to save your submission before moving on to other forms or tasks.
- If any information is missing or incorrect, kindly let the event organizers know to avoid any inconvenience.
- Review your form before submitting to ensure you have filled in all essential details.
