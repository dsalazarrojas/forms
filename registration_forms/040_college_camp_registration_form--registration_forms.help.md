# College Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The College Camp Registration Form is a comprehensive form used to collect information from students registering for the college camp. This form is used to ensure that we have accurate and up-to-date information about each camper, including demographic details, medical information, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Enter your first name in the First Name field.
* Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
* Enter your date of birth in the Date of Birth field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
* Enter your email address in the Email Address field.
* Enter your phone number in the Phone Number field.
* Enter your complete street address in the Street Address field.
* Enter your city in the City field.
* Enter your state or province in the State field.
* Enter your ZIP Code in the ZIP Code field.
* Enter the name of your current school in the School Name field.
* Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* If applicable, enter your major or field of study in the Major or Field of Study field.
* Enter the name of the person who will be your primary contact for emergencies in the Emergency Contact Name field.
* Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Phone field.
* Select the relationship between your emergency contact and you from the dropdown menu.
* If applicable, list any medical conditions or allergies you have in the Medical Conditions field.
* If applicable, list any medications you are currently taking in the Current Medications field.
* If applicable, list any dietary restrictions you have in the Dietary Restrictions field.
* Enter the name of your health insurance provider in the Insurance Provider field.
* Enter your insurance policy number in the Policy Number field.
* Select which camp session you will be attending from the dropdown menu.
* Select which camp track or focus you are interested in from the dropdown menu.
* Select the events you plan to attend from the dropdown menu.
* Select your camp T-Shirt size from the dropdown menu.
* If you are staying on campus, select which housing option you need from the dropdown menu.
* Select your meal plan from the dropdown menu.
* If you need transportation to or from the camp, select which option you need from the dropdown menu.
* Enter what you hope to gain from the camp in the What Do You Hope to Gain From Camp field.
* Select how you heard about the camp from the dropdown menu.
* If you are interested in scholarship or financial aid, select which option you need from the dropdown menu.
* Enter the registration fee payment amount in the Registration Fee Payment field.
* Select how you will pay the registration fee from the dropdown menu.
* Select if you agree to the liability waiver terms from the dropdown menu.
* Select if you consent to media releases from the dropdown menu.
* Finally, enter any additional comments or questions you have in the Additional Comments or Questions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`camper_first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (`camper_last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your family name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Gender** (`gender`, `select_one`, required): Select your gender from the dropdown menu.
* **Email Address** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your primary email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your complete address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter your city name.
* **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter your state or province name.
* **ZIP Code** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter your ZIP code.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your current school.
* **Grade Level** (`grade_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your current grade level from the dropdown menu.
* **Major or Field of Study** (`major_field`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your major or field of study.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your primary contact for emergencies.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of your primary contact.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your primary contact.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your alternative emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of your alternative emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, `text`, required): Select the relationship between your emergency contact and you from the dropdown menu.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any medical conditions you have.
* **Current Medications** (`current_medications`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any medications you are currently taking.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any allergies you have.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Insurance Provider** (`insurance_provider`, `text`, required): Enter the name of your health insurance provider.
* **Policy Number** (`policy_number`, `text`, required): Enter your insurance policy number.
* **Camp Session Selection** (`camp_session`, `select_one`, required): Select which camp session you will be attending.
* **Camp Track or Focus** (`camp_track`, `select_one`, required): Select which camp track or focus you are interested in.
* **Events You Plan to Attend** (`events_attending`, `select_multiple`, required): Select all the events you plan to attend.
* **Camp T-Shirt Size** (`tshirt_size`, `select_one`, required): Select your camp T-Shirt size.
* **On-Campus Housing Needed** (`housing_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you are staying on campus, select which housing option you need.
* **Meal Plan Selection** (`meal_plan`, `select_one`, required): Select your meal plan.
* **Airport Transportation Needed** (`transportation_needed`, `select_one`, required): If you need transportation to or from the camp, select which option you need.
* **What Do You Hope to Gain From Camp** (`camp_expectations`, `text`, required): Enter what you hope to gain from the camp.
* **How Did You Hear About This Camp** (`how_heard`, `select_one`, required): Select how you heard about the camp.
* **Interested in Scholarship or Financial Aid** (`scholarship_interest`, `select_one`, required): If you are interested in scholarship or financial aid, select which option you need.
* **Registration Fee Payment** (`registration_fee`, `text`, required): Enter the registration fee payment amount.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required): Select how you will pay the registration fee.
* **Liability Waiver Agreement** (`liability_waiver`, `select_one`, required): Select if you agree to the liability waiver terms.
* **Media Release Consent** (`media_release`, `select_one`, required): Select if you consent to media releases.
* **Additional Comments or Questions** (`additional_comments`, `text`, optional): Enter any additional comments or questions you have.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, feel free to ask for assistance.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid errors.
