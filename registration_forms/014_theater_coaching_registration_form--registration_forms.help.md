# Theater Coaching Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Theater Coaching Registration Form is used for students and coaches to register for theater coaching sessions. This form helps to collect information about the student, their emergency contact, and the details of the coaching session.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form title with a clear and concise title for the coaching session.
2. Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the category and subcategories that best describe the coaching session.
5. Enter the date and time of the coaching session.
6. Enter the location of the coaching session.
7. Enter the name and contact information of the emergency contact person.
8. List any medical conditions or allergies that require special attention.
9. Provide any additional information about the student and the coaching session.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`form_title`, `text`, required): A clear and concise title for the coaching session.
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `text`, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Category** (`category`, `select_one`, required): Select the category of the coaching session (e.g., Registration Forms, Workshop Registration Form Templates).
* **Subcategories** (`subcategories`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the subcategories of the coaching session (e.g., Dance, Theater).
* **Coaching Session** (`coaching_session`, `date`, required): Enter the date and time of the coaching session.
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Enter the time of the coaching session.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Enter the location of the coaching session.
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Enter the date of the coaching session.
* **Coaching Hours** (`coaching_hours`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of coaching hours.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the student.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Name** (`student_parent_name`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of the parent/guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`student_parent_email`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone** (`student_parent_phone`, `text`, optional): Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, `text`, optional): List any medical conditions or allergies that require special attention.
* **Medical Details** (`medical_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional medical details.
* **Allergies** (`allergies`, `text`, optional): List any allergies that require special attention.
* **Medication** (`medication`, `text`, optional): List any medications that require special attention.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional information about the student and the coaching session.
