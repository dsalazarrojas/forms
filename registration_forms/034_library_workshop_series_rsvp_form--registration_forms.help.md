# Library Workshop Series RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Library Workshop Series RSVP Form is designed to allow users to RSVP to a workshop series hosted by the library. This form is used to gather information from users about their interest in attending the workshop series.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select your preferred workshops on the first page.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number on the second page.
3. Choose your RSVP date and time on the third page.
4. Provide any additional workshop details or preferences on the fourth page.
5. Write any additional information or messages you'd like to communicate to the host or organizer on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Selection**: Select one or multiple workshops you are interested in attending. You can choose from the following options: "Yes" or "No". You can select more than one workshop.
* **Name**: Enter your full name as we would like to address you in the workshop.
* **Email Address**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the workshop series.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about the workshop series.
* **RSVP Date**: Choose a date to RSVP to the workshop series. The format for the date is YYYY-MM-DD.
* **RSVP Time**: Choose the time you plan to attend the workshop series. The format for the time is "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Workshop Details**: Enter any additional information or preferences you have about the workshop series.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any other comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
* **RSVP Message**: Enter any additional message or notes you'd like to communicate to the host or organizer about your RSVP.
