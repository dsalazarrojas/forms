# premium_account_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to renew premium accounts, typically after an initial registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user input in the **user_input** field.
2. Provide payment details in the **payment_details** field.
3. Select your **account_status** from the options provided.
4. Indicate the **renewal_status** of your account by selecting one or more options.
5. Enter the **renewal_date** for your account renewal.
6. Select your preferred **payment_method** from the available options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input** (user_input, text, optional):
	+ This field allows you to enter any additional information about your account that you would like to include in the renewal.
* **Payment Details** (payment_details, text, optional):
	+ Enter any relevant information about your payment, such as payment amount or bank transfer details.
* **Account Status** (account_status, select_one, optional):
	+ Select the current status of your account, which can be Active, Inactive, or Deleted.
* **Renewal Status** (renewal_status, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Indicate the status of your account renewal by selecting one or more options, which can be Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
* **Renewal Date** (renewal_date, date, optional):
	+ Enter the date when your account is set to be renewed.
* **Payment Method** (payment_method, select_multiple, optional):
	+ Select your preferred method of payment, which can be Cash, Check, or Online Payment.
