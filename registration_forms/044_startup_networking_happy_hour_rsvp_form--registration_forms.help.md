# Startup Networking Happy Hour RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to the Startup Networking Happy Hour event. To complete the form, follow the instructions below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, simply fill out the required fields and select your preferences for the event. You will need to provide your first and last name, job title, and email address. Additionally, you will need to select the date and time of your attendance, and choose the type of event you are attending. You can also indicate if you will be attending, not attending, or if your attendance is uncertain.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
### Company
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, required/optional): If you are affiliated with a company, enter the name of the company. If not, you can leave this field blank.
### Job Title
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your job title or profession.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required/optional): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached.
### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
### Date
* **Date** (`date`, `date`, required/optional): Select the date of the event you plan to attend.
### Time
* **Time** (`time`, `time`, required/optional): Select the time of the event you plan to attend.
### Event Type
* **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the type of event you are attending:
	+ Networking
	+ Pitch Event
	+ Presentation Event
### Attendance
* **Attendance** (`attendance`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Indicate your attendance status:
	+ Yes
	+ No
	+ Maybe

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately to ensure we can contact you about your attendance.
* If your attendance status is uncertain, please select "Maybe" to help us plan for the event.
* If you have any special requests or accommodations, please contact us separately.
