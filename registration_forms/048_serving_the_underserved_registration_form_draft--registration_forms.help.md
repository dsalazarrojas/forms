# Serving The Underserved Registration Form Draft - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals or organizations interested in serving the underserved population. The form is a draft registration form that allows you to provide your health services, make a difference, and register with this form template.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and fill in the required information.
2. Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
3. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration form draft (id: 1)**: A field to provide your registration form draft. This field is not required.
* **Serving the underserved registration form draft (id: 2)**: A field to provide information about the serving the underserved registration form draft. This field is not required.
* **Registration form template (id: 3)**: A field to provide information about the registration form template. This field is not required.
* **Offer your health services (id: 4)**: A field to describe your health services. This field is not required.
* **Make the difference (id: 5)**: A field to provide a description of how you make the difference. This field is not required.
* **Register with this form template (id: 6)**: A field to confirm your registration with this form template. This field is not required.
* **Serving the underserved (id: 7)**: A field to provide information about serving the underserved. This field is not required.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all the required information accurately and completely.
* Review each field carefully before submitting your registration.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at form@gic.mx.
