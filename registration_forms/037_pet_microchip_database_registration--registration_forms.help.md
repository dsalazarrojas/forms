# Pet Microchip Database Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from pet owners for the purpose of registering their pet's microchip details in a database. The goal is to ensure accurate and up-to-date records of pet identification and ownership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, indicated by a red asterisk (\*), with the requested information.
2. Provide accurate and complete answers to all questions.
3. Upload any required documents or files as instructed.
4. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **pet_owner_info** (pet_owner_info): This field is a text input for the pet owner's information.
* **pet_details** (pet_details): This field is a text input for the pet's details.
* **contact_info** (contact_info): This field is a text input for the pet owner's contact information.
* **microchip_info** (microchip_info): This field is a text input for the microchip information.
* **upload_file** (upload_file): This field allows you to upload any required documents or files.
* **microchip_details** (microchip_details): This field is a text input for the microchip details.
* **owner_signature** (owner_signature): This field is for the pet owner's signature, typically a digital equivalent.
* **clinic_info** (clinic_info): This field is a text input for the veterinary clinic information.
* **review_agree** (review_agree): This field is a text input for the pet owner's agreement to review and update their pet's information.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any field, it's best to reach out to the relevant authority or clinic for guidance.
* Be patient and take the time to fill out the form accurately to avoid errors and delays in processing your pet's registration.
