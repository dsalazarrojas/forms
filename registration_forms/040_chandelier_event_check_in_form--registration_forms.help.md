# Chandelier Event Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for event attendees to register and check-in for events. It collects information about the attendee, the event, and other relevant details to facilitate event management, networking, and attendee experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Click on the "Check-in" button to begin the registration process.
2.  Select the "Check-in Date" field and choose the date of the event.
3.  Select the "Check-in Time" field and choose the time of arrival.
4.  Enter the attendee's "Attendee Name", "Email Address", and "Phone Number" information.
5.  Select the "Event Name" field and choose the name of the event.
6.  Select the "Event Date" field and choose the scheduled event date.
7.  Select the "Registration Status" field and choose the registration status (pre-registered, walk-in registration, etc.).
8.  Select the "Ticket Type" field and choose the type of admission (general admission, VIP access, etc.).
9.  If applicable, enter the "Table Assignment" and "Meal Preference" information.
10.  Answer the "Photo Consent" question and select "Yes" or "No" to indicate consent to be photographed.
11.  If necessary, provide "Special Accommodations" information.
12.  Enter the name of the staff member checking in the attendee in the "Checked In By" field.
13.  Add any "Additional Notes" about the attendee if necessary.
14.  Click on "Submit" to complete the registration process.
