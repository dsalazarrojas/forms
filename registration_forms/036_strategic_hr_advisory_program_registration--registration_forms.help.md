# strategic-hr-advisory-program-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register a participant for the Strategic HR Advisory Program. Please fill out this form if you are interested in participating in the program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in the `User Info` section, including your name and any other relevant details.
2. Select all the focus areas you are interested in from the `Focus Areas` section.
3. Enter your goals and any relevant information in the `Goals` section.
4. Choose your preferred program choice from the `Program Choices` section.
5. Add any additional participant information in the `Participant Info` section.
6. Select a date and time for your participation in the `Date of Participation` section.
7. Provide your availability for the program participation in the `Time of Participation` section.
8. Add any notes or comments about your participation in the `Notes` section.
9. Enter your email address in the `Email` section.
10. Provide your phone number in the `Phone` section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (Text)**: Enter your name and any other relevant details.
* **Focus Areas (Select Multiple)**: Choose all the areas where you want to focus on. The options available are:
	+ Focus Area 1
	+ Focus Area 2
	+ Focus Area 3
* **Goals (Text)**: Enter your specific goals for participating in the program. This could include what you hope to achieve or learn.
* **Program Choices (Select One)**: Choose the program option that best suits your needs. The options available are:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Participant Info (Text)**: Add any additional information about yourself that you think would be helpful for us to know.
* **Date of Participation (Date)**: Select a date on which you would like to participate in the program.
* **Time of Participation (Time)**: Choose a time when you would be available to participate in the program.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any notes or comments you have about your participation.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you with updates about the program.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you with any last-minute reminders or questions.
