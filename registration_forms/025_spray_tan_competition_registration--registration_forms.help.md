# Spray Tan Competition Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Spray Tan Competition. Please follow these steps to complete the form accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select your Spray Tan Salon from the provided list.
2. Choose the competition category that best describes your area of expertise.
3. Enter your name, phone number, and email address to confirm your registration.
4. Confirm your availability for the competition by indicating your competition date and start time.
5. Indicate your duration in the competition and any preferences you have.
6. Read and agree to the competition rules.
7. Provide any additional information about your salon, if applicable.
8. Enter your unique Competition ID and Assistant information, if applicable.
9. Confirm or reject additional categories as required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Spray Tan Salon (<id: 1>)
- **Spray Tan Salon** (Text, Required): This field should match your registered salon name.

### Category (<id: 2>)
- **Category** (Select One, Required): Choose your area of expertise from the options Black and Grey, Chest and Back, Legs, Face.

### Name (<id: 3>)
- **Name** (Text, Required): Enter your name as it is spelled.

### Phone (<id: 4>)
- **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number in international format (e.g., 1234567890).

### Email (<id: 5>)
- **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.

### Rule Agreement (<id: 6>)
- **Rule Agreement** (Note, Not Required): This is a non-mandatory agreement that you can read and acknowledge. 

### Competition Date (<id: 7>)
- **Competition Date** (Date, Required): Select the date you are available for the competition.

### Start Time (<id: 8>)
- **Start Time** (Time, Required): Select the time you are available for the competition.

### Duration (<id: 9>)
- **Duration** (Text, Required): Indicate how long you will participate in the competition.

### Preferences (<id: 10>)
- **Preferences** (Select Multiple, Required): Indicate any preferences you have for the competition.

### Rules (<id: 11>)
- **Rules** (Note, Required): Read and agree to the competition rules.

### Salon Information (<id: 12>)
- **Salon Information** (Text, Not Required): This field is for providing additional information about your salon.

### Competition ID (<id: 13>)
- **Competition ID** (Text, Required): Enter your unique Competition ID.

### Assistant ID (<id: 14>)
- **Assistant ID** (Text, Not Required): Enter your assistant's ID, if applicable.
