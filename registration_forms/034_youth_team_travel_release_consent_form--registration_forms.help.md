# Youth Team Travel Release Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to obtain consent from parents or guardians for their child to participate in youth team travel.

## How To Complete This Form

*   Fill in all required fields with accurate and complete information.
*   If a field is marked as optional, fill it in if you feel it's relevant to your situation.
*   Please take your time to fill out the form carefully and accurately.
*   Double-check your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Team Name**: This is the name of the team your child is traveling with. Please enter the name of the team exactly as it is written on their uniform, badge, or any other official documentation.
*   **Parent Name**: This is your name as the parent or guardian of the child participating in the trip.
*   **Parent Address**: Enter your address so we can contact you if necessary.
*   **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
*   **State**: Enter your state of residence.
*   **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for further identification.
*   **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number so we can contact you easily.
*   **Email**: Enter your email address for communication.
*   **Travel Date**: Enter the date when your child will be traveling.
*   **Travel Duration**: Enter the duration of the trip in days.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely.
*   If you have any doubts or concerns about the form, contact us for assistance.
*   Please submit the form once you have filled it out carefully and accurately.
*   You can contact us if you need help or clarification on any part of the form.
