# Martial Arts Class Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us get to know you better and ensure that we can provide the best class experience for you. Please take a few minutes to fill out the form below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the respective text fields.
2. Select your birth date from the calendar.
3. Choose your preferred martial arts style (you can select multiple styles if you have multiple interests).
4. Indicate your class level (beginner, intermediate, or advanced).
5. Let us know about your class preferences (e.g., weekdays only, weekend classes).
6. Enter your contact phone number.
7. Enter your email address.
8. Select your preferred time of day for classes.
9. Choose a date and time for your first class (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (text)**: Your name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (text)**: Your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Birth Date (date)**: Your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Martial Arts Style (select multiple)**: Select one or more styles that interest you:
	+ Karate
	+ Judo
	+ Boxing
	+ Wrestling
	+ Tai Chi
* **Class Level (select one)**: Choose your current level:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
* **Class Preferences (select multiple)**: Check the options that apply to you:
	+ Yes
* **Contact Number (number)**: Your phone number for us to reach you.
* **Email (email)**: Your email address for official communications.
* **Preferred Time (time)**: Select a time of day that suits you best.
* **Preferred Date (date)**: Choose a date and time for your first class (if not specified, we will contact you to schedule).
	+ Note: This field is optional and will be filled out if you provide a specific date and time for your first class.
