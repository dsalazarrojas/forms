# Angels Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register as an angel mentor or advisor, providing guidance, advice, and support to early-stage startups.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Full Name** in the "Full Name" field.
2. Enter your **Email Address** in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your **Contact Phone Number** in the "Contact Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Select your **Primary Areas of Expertise** by checking all that apply.
5. Enter your **Primary Industry Focus**.
6. Enter your **Years of Professional Experience**.
7. Select your **Preferred Mentee or Advisory Level**.
8. Choose your **Time Commitment (Hours per Month)**.
9. Select your **Interest in formal Advisory Board positions?**.
10. If you have a **LinkedIn Profile URL**, enter it in the "LinkedIn Profile URL" field (optional).
11. Write a **Brief Personal Statement** explaining why you want to be an angel mentor (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or LinkedIn profile.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your professional email address to which we can send follow-up emails.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number for further communication.
* **Primary Areas of Expertise** (`areas_of_expertise`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas where you have expertise, such as **Strategic Planning**, **Fundraising**, **Technical Development**, **Marketing**, **Operations**, or **Legal / Compliance**.
* **Primary Industry Focus** (`industry_focus`, text, required): Enter the industry where you have the most experience and focus, e.g. **Fintech, Healthcare, EdTech**.
* **Years of Professional Experience** (`years_of_professional_experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been in your profession.
* **Preferred Mentee or Advisory Level** (`preferred_mentee_level`, select_one, required): Select your preferred level of engagement, which can be **Early Stage Startup, Growth Stage, Individual Professional, or Student**.
* **Time Commitment (Hours per Month)** (`commitment_hours_per_month`, select_one, required): Select the amount of time you can commit per month to the community, which can be **1-2 hours, 3-5 hours, or 5+ hours**.
* **Interest in formal Advisory Board positions?** (`interest_in_advisory_board`, select_one, required): Select **True** if you are interested in formal Advisory Board positions.
* **LinkedIn Profile URL** (`link_to_linkedin_profile`, text, optional): Enter your LinkedIn profile URL if you have one.
* **Brief Personal Statement** (`brief_personal_statement`, text, optional): Write a brief statement explaining why you want to be an angel mentor.
