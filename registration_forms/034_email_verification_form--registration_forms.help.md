# Email Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Email Verification Form is used to verify a user's email address for internal processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your email address in the **Email Verification** field.
2. Enter the verification code sent to your email address in the **Verification Code** field.
3. Click the **Verify Email** button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email Verification** (`email_verification_form`, text, optional): Enter your email address.
	+ This field is used to enter your registered email address.
* **Verification Code** (`verification_code`, number, optional): Enter the verification code sent to your email address.
	+ This field is used to enter the verification code that was sent to your email address as a confirmation of your registered email address.
* **Verify Email** (`verify_email`, text, optional): Click this button to submit the form and verify your email address.
	+ This button is used to complete the email verification process.
