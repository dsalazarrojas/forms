# Workplace Efficiency Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workplace Efficiency Forum Registration form is a tool for users to register their interest in attending specific sessions or events related to workplace efficiency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name and any relevant details in the "attendee_name" field.
2. Choose the sessions or events you are interested in by selecting the options from the "session_interests" field.
3. Provide your email address and phone number for contact purposes in the "email" and "phone" fields, respectively.
4. Select a date and time for your availability in the "date" and "time" fields, if applicable.
5. Provide any additional notes or comments in the "note" field, if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **attendee_name**: Your name, which will be used to identify you as an attendee.
* **session_interests**: Choose one or more sessions or events you are interested in attending. This will help us track your interest and provide you with relevant information.
* **email**: Your email address, which we will use to send you updates and follow-up information about the sessions or events you are interested in.
* **phone**: Your phone number, which we will use to contact you if needed.
* **date**: If you have a specific date in mind when you are available to attend, please select it.
* **time**: If you have a preferred time slot, please select it.
* **note**: Any additional notes or comments you would like to share with us.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you fill out all the required fields, marked as "required" in the form.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special requirements, please mention them in the "note" field.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided contact information.
