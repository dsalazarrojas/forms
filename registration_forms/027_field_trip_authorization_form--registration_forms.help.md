# Field Trip Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for students to provide information about a field trip. It is meant to collect basic information from students about themselves and the trip.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Student Info section with your name and any additional information you need to share.
2. Fill out the Parent Info section with your parent's or guardian's name and any additional information you need to share.
3. In the Trip Info section, provide any details about the trip you are taking, including dates, locations, and any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Info (student_info)**: This section is where you should enter your name and any other information about yourself. Please be sure to include your name and any other relevant details you think we need to know.
* **Parent Info (parent_info)**: This section is where you should enter your parent's or guardian's name and any information they would like to share about themselves.
* **Trip Info (trip_info)**: This section is where you should provide any information about the trip you are taking, such as the dates, locations, and any other details that will help us understand the trip.

## Tips
- Be sure to fill out all required fields (although none are marked as required on this form, please be sure to provide as much information as possible to help us understand your trip).
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
