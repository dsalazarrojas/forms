# 5K Run Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants in the 5K Run event. It is intended to collect essential information from runners for event organization, logistics, and communication purposes.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your participant information, including your full name as it will appear on the race bib.
2. Provide your date of birth to verify your age category.
3. Enter your contact email address for receiving event updates and results.
4. Enter your contact phone number for urgent contact on race day.
5. Select your gender category.
6. Choose the race category you will participate in (competitive, recreational, or fun run).
7. Choose your T-shirt size.
8. Provide the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
9. List any medical conditions or allergies that the race staff should be aware of.
10. Confirm that you have paid the registration fee.
11. Acknowledge and accept the participant waiver and release.
12. If applicable, enter the name of the team or club you are representing.
13. If necessary, provide any additional comments or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant full name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name as it should appear on the race bib.
* **Date of birth** (`participant_dob`): Enter your date of birth to verify your age category.
* **Email address** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address for receiving event updates and results.
* **Phone number** (`contact_phone`): Enter your contact phone number for urgent race-day contact.
* **Gender category** (`gender_category`): Select your gender category (female, male, non-binary, or prefer not to say).
* **Race category** (`race_category`): Choose the distance or wave you will participate in (5K competitive, 5K recreational, or fun run).
* **T-shirt size** (`shirt_size`): Select your T-shirt size (XS, S, M, L, XL, or XXL).
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Medical conditions** (`medical_conditions`): List any medical conditions or allergies that the race staff should be aware of.
* **Registration fee** (`registration_fee`): Enter the amount paid, including cents, for the registration fee.
* **Waiver and release** (`waiver_signed`): Confirm that you have read and accepted the participant waiver and release.
* **Team or club name** (`team_or_club`): Enter the name of your team or club, if applicable.
* **Additional comments** (`additional_comments`): Provide any additional comments or special requests, if necessary.
