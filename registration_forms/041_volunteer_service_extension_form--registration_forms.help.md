# Volunteer Service Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to provide information about the volunteer service extension, including the volunteer's status, the type of service they will be providing, and the termination date and time.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the correct options for each field to provide accurate and complete information about your volunteer service extension.
2. Review the form carefully before submitting it to ensure all required fields are filled in.
3. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Service Extension** (`volunteer_service_extension_form`, text, optional): This field allows you to input the details of your volunteer service extension.
* **Volunteer Info** (`volunteer_service_extension_form_volunteer_info`, select multiple, optional): Select 'Yes' if you are a volunteer, 'No' if you are not a volunteer.
* **Volunteer Service** (`volunteer_service_extension_form_volunteer_service`, select one, optional): Select the type of service you will be providing (Volunteer or Service).
* **Termination Date** (`volunteer_service_extension_form_termination_date`, date, optional): Input the date on which your volunteer service will terminate.
* **Termination Time** (`volunteer_service_extension_form_termination_time`, time, optional): Input the time on which your volunteer service will terminate.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
* If you are unsure about any field or have questions, contact your administrator.
