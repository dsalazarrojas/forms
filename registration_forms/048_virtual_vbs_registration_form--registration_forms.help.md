# Virtual VBS Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering children for Virtual Bible Study (VBS) programs. It collects the required information for program planning and facilitation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the child's name.
2. Enter the parent's name.
3. Provide the contact number.
4. Enter the email address.
5. Select the child's date of birth.
6. Choose the age group the child belongs to.
7. Indicate the days the child is available to attend.
8. Choose the days the child is selected to attend.
9. Enter the start date of the program.
10. Enter the end date of the program.
11. Select the Bible version to be used for the program.
12. Choose the language of the program.
13. Select the session duration.
14. Choose the session frequency.
15. If you wish, provide additional information about the child.
16. Confirm your consent to share the registration information with other program participants.
17. Confirm if you consent to share your child's information with program organizers for contact purposes.
18. Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
19. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
20. If applicable, specify the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Enter child's name** (Required, Text): Fill in the name of the child.
- **Enter parent's name** (Required, Text): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
- **Contact number** (Required, Text): Enter a phone number where the parent or guardian can be contacted.
- **Email** (Required, Email): Enter a valid email address.
- **Date of birth** (Required, Date): Enter the child's date of birth.
- **Age group** (Required, Select One): Choose the age group the child belongs to (1-2 years, 2-3 years, 3-5 years, etc.).
- **Available days** (Required, Select Multiple): Select the days the child is available to attend.
- **Selected days** (Required, Select Multiple): Select the days the child has chosen to attend.
- **Start date** (Required, Date): Enter the start date of the program.
- **End date** (Required, Date): Enter the end date of the program.
- **Bible version** (Required, Select One): Choose the version of the Bible to be used for the program.
- **Language** (Required, Select One): Select the language to be used for the program.
- **Session duration** (Required, Select One): Choose the duration of each session.
- **Session frequency** (Required, Select One): Choose how often the sessions are held.
- **Additional information** (Optional, Note): Provide any additional information about the child (e.g., medical conditions, allergies).
- **Terms and Conditions** (Optional, Select Multiple): Confirm you have read and agreed to the program's terms and conditions.
- **Consent to share** (Optional, Select One): Confirm your consent to share the registration information with other program participants.
- **Consent to share with emergency contact** (Optional, Select One): Confirm if you consent to share the child's information with program organizers for contact purposes.
- **Emergency contact** (Optional, Text): Enter the name of an emergency contact person.
- **Emergency contact phone** (Optional, Text): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
- **Relationship** (Optional, Select One): If applicable, specify the relationship between the emergency contact person and the child.
