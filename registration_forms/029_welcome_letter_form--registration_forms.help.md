# Welcome Letter Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information about new employees, including their personal details and job-related information. The gathered data will be used to create a welcome letter for the new employee and to establish a professional relationship with them.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Make sure to select the correct department for the employee (if applicable).
3. Double-check the email address for accuracy.
4. Enter the employee's date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format (e.g., 01/01/2020).
5. Select the correct job title and manager's name for the employee.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text)**: Enter the employee's first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter the employee's last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter the employee's email address.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: Enter the employee's date of birth in the mm/dd/yyyy format.
* **Department (Select One)**: Select the department to which the employee belongs. If the employee doesn't belong to any department, select 'No'.
* **Job Title (Text)**: Enter the employee's job title.
* **Manager's Name (Text)**: Enter the name of the employee's manager.

## Tips

- Make sure to double-check all the fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Use a standard format for dates (mm/dd/yyyy).
- If you're unsure about any field, contact your HR department for clarification.
