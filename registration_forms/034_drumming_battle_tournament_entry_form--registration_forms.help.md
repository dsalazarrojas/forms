# drumming_battle_tournament_entry_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for solo and group registration for drumming battles. Users can register as individuals or as part of a group, providing details about their name, instrument, experience, and other relevant information. The form helps organizers collect necessary details from participants for the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select whether you are registering as a solo participant or as part of a group.
2. Fill in your name and instrument details.
3. If registering as part of a group, enter the group name and size, as well as details for each participant.
4. Provide any additional information such as email, contact, and other relevant details.
5. Confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Solo Registration** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name and instrument details for solo registration.
* **Group Registration** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Enter your group name for group registration.
* **Name** (`3`, `text`, required: false): Enter your name for solo registration or group leader's name.
* **Group Name** (`4`, `text`, required: false): Enter your group name for group registration.
* **Participant 1 Name** (`5`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the first participant in your group.
* **Participant 2 Name** (`6`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the second participant in your group.
* **Instrument** (`7`, `text`, required: false): Enter your instrument for solo registration or group leader's instrument.
* **Group Size** (`8`, `number`, required: false): Enter the number of participants in your group.
* **Group Size Help** (`9`, `note`, required: false): This field provides additional help or information for group size entry.
* **Group Type** (`10`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the type of group (Team, Duet, Other).
* **Participant 1 Instrument** (`11`, `text`, required: false): Enter the instrument of the first participant in your group.
* **Participant 2 Instrument** (`12`, `text`, required: false): Enter the instrument of the second participant in your group.
* **Participant 1 Experience** (`13`, `number`, required: false): Enter the experience level of the first participant in your group.
* **Participant 2 Experience** (`14`, `number`, required: false): Enter the experience level of the second participant in your group.
* **Participant 1 Achievement** (`15`, `text`, required: false): Enter any achievements of the first participant in your group.
* **Participant 2 Achievement** (`16`, `text`, required: false): Enter any achievements of the second participant in your group.
* **Email** (`17`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Contact** (`18`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional contact information.
* **Confirm** (`19`, `select_one`, required: false): Confirm your registration (True or False).
* **Participant 1 Details** (`20`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details for the first participant in your group.
* **Participant 2 Details** (`21`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional details for the second participant in your group.
* **Title** (`22`, `text`, required: false): Enter a title for your solo or group entry.
* **Category** (`23`, `text`, required: false): Enter a category for your solo or group entry.
* **Description** (`24`, `text`, required: false): Enter a description for your solo or group entry.
* **Output File** (`25`, `text`, required: false): Upload any additional output file.
* **Assigned Tool** (`26`, `text`, required: false): Enter the assigned tool for your solo or group entry.

Note: All fields are optional unless specified as required.
