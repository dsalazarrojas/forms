# Cultural Responsiveness Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register individuals for cultural responsiveness training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select your preferred Session Preferences from the list provided.
6. Check the Participation Consent checkbox to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms.
7. Enter the Session Date and Session Time.
8. Add any Additional Notes if needed.
9. Enter the assigned tool or resource.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, Text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written on your official ID or government-issued ID.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, Text, Required): Enter your last name as it is written on your official ID or government-issued ID.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check.
* **Phone** (`phone`, Text, Required): Enter your 10-digit phone number.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences`, Select Multiple, Required): Choose your preferred session time from the provided list.
  - Select one or more session time slots.
  - You can select multiple sessions if you prefer more than one session time.
* **Participation Consent** (`participation_consent`, Select One, Required): Check this box to confirm you have read and agreed to the terms of the training program.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, Date, Optional): Enter the date of the session you prefer.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, Time, Optional): Enter the time of the session you prefer.
* **Session Time Note** (`session_time_note`, Note, Optional): Add any notes about your preferred session time.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, Text, Optional): If assigned a tool or resource, enter it here.

## Tips
* Ensure that the information you enter is accurate and complete.
* Select only one or multiple session preferences that match your schedule and availability.
* Review and agree to the terms of the training program by checking the Participation Consent checkbox.
* Enter your email address correctly to receive updates and communication from the trainer.
