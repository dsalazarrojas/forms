# HR Modernization Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Modernization Seminar Registration Form is used to collect information from registrants for an HR Modernization Seminar. This form ensures that seminar organizers collect accurate and consistent data from attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form with your details.
2. Confirm your provided information for each field.
3. Submit the form when you're finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Seminar Details**: Provide information about the seminar you'd like to attend.
* **Registrant**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email**: Enter your company email address.
* **Phone**: Select one of the provided phone numbers to confirm your contact information.
* **Name**: Enter your full name.
* **Role**: Select your job role from the options provided.
* **Organization**: Enter your company's name.
* **Job Function**: Select your job function from the options provided.
* **Comments**: Add any additional comments about the seminar.
* **Confirm Email**: Select your email address to confirm.
* **Confirm Phone**: Select your phone number to confirm.
* **Confirm Name**: Select your name to confirm.
* **Confirm Role**: Select your job role to confirm.
* **Confirm Organization**: Select your company's name to confirm.
* **Confirm Job Function**: Select your job function to confirm.
* **Confirm Comments**: Add any additional comments about the seminar.
* **Confirm Confirm Email**: Select your email address to confirm.
* **Confirm Confirm Phone**: Select your phone number to confirm.
* **Confirm Confirm Name**: Select your name to confirm.
* **Confirm Confirm Role**: Select your job role to confirm.
* **Confirm Confirm Organization**: Select your company's name to confirm.
* **Confirm Confirm Job Function**: Select your job function to confirm.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted At**: Enter the date and time you submitted the form.
* **Updated At**: Enter the date and time the form was last updated.

Please note that some fields are required, indicated by the asterisk (\*) symbol.
