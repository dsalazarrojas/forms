# sports_league_participation_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The sports_league_participation_extension_form is designed for participants to update or modify their existing registration information in sports leagues.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. On page 1, provide your participant information. This includes your basic details such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant participant details.
2. On page 2, specify your team details, if applicable. This might include your team name, team members, and any other team-specific information.
3. On page 3, provide your payment details. This is where you can specify your payment method and any other payment-related information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info** (`participant_info`, `text`, required): Please enter your basic participant details, such as your name, address, phone number, and any other relevant information that is required to be updated or modified.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, `text`, required): If you are part of a team, please provide your team name, team members, and any other relevant team-specific information that needs to be updated or modified.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required): Specify your preferred payment method and any other payment-related information that needs to be updated or modified.

## Tips
Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form. If you are unsure about any field, you can always contact the form creator for assistance.
