# Cruise Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cruise registration form is designed to collect essential information about passengers for the smooth operation of the cruise. It is essential to fill out the form accurately to ensure a hassle-free experience during the cruise.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
3. Review the form carefully before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger Details** (`passenger_details`, text, optional): Enter the passenger's details, including their name and any other relevant information.
* **Cruise Line** (`cruise_line`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether the passenger has a preferred cruise line or not. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.
* **Itinerary** (`itinerary`, note, optional): If you have any specific requests or requirements for the itinerary, please describe them in this field.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can keep you updated on any important information related to the cruise.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of any urgent matters.
* **Booking Date** (`booking_date`, date, optional): Select the date you plan to book the cruise.
* **Booking Time** (`booking_time`, time, optional): If you have a preferred time for booking, select it here.
* **Ship Name** (`ship_name`, select_one, optional): Indicate whether you have a preferred ship or not. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your preference.
* **Cabin Number** (`cabin_number`, text, optional): If you have a specific cabin number in mind, enter it here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.
* **Passenger Name** (`passenger_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the passenger.
* **Nationality** (`nationality`, select_one, optional): Indicate the passenger's nationality. Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate their nationality.
* **Passport Number** (`passport_number`, text, optional): If the passenger has a passport, enter the passport number here.
