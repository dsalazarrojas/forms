# High School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this High School Enrollment Form is to allow prospective students to submit an enrollment application and their information for the school's records. 

## How To Complete This Form
Please fill in the form with accurate and complete information for both the student and guardian. Ensure that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions of the school by selecting the appropriate options in the "Student Enrollment Agreement" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Propective Student** (`prospective_student`): This is the student's name.
* **First Name** (`student_first_name`): The student's first name.
* **Last Name** (`student_last_name`): The student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): The student's date of birth.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`): The student's address.
* **Parent Name** (`parent_name`): The parent's name.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`): The parent's email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`): The student's phone number.
* **Student Enrollment Agreement** (`student_enrollment_agreement`): This is a multiple-choice field where you must select at least one option indicating you are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of the school. Options include:
	+ I am aware that I must pay all fees and other costs associated with my enrollment in this school
	+ I am aware that I must meet all conditions of enrollment in this school
	+ I am aware that I must comply with all school rules and regulations
	+ I am aware that I must be honest in my representations
* **Student Enrollment Agreement Checkbox** (`student_enrollment_agreement_checkbox`): This is another field for the student to select and agree to the terms and conditions of the school. Select at least one option from the multiple-choice options.
* **Parent Signature** (`parent_signature`): The parent's signature.
* **Student Signature** (`student_signature`): The student's signature.
* **Guardian** (`parent_guardsian`): The guardian's relationship with the student. Options include:
	+ Mother
	+ Father
	+ Guardian
	+ Other
* **Guardian Name** (`student_guardian_name`): The guardian's name.
* **Guardian Address** (`student_guardian_address`): The guardian's address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`student_guardian_phone`): The guardian's phone number.
* **Guardian Email** (`student_guardian_email`): The guardian's email address.
* **Student Parent Phone** (`student_parent_phone`): The student's parent phone number.
* **Student Parent Email** (`student_parent_email`): The student's parent email address.
* **Date** (`date`): The date.
* **Time** (`time`): The time.
* **Student School ID** (`student_school_id`): The student's school ID.
* **Student Class ID** (`student_class_id`): The student's class ID.
* **Student Enrollment Status** (`student_enrollment_status`): The student's enrollment status. Options include:
	+ Pending
	+ Enrolled
	+ Rejected
	+ Withdrawn
