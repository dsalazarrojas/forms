# Performing Arts Terms Acknowledgment - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to gather information from participants in performing arts activities, including their contact details, agreement status, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, following the on-screen prompts and guidelines.
2. Ensure that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the performing arts program before signing the agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant.
* **Contact Name**: Enter the name of the contact person for the participant.
* **Contact Email**: Enter the email address of the contact person (optional).
* **Agreement Version**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and understood the current agreement version.
* **Participant Address**: Enter the address of the participant (optional).
* **Contact Address**: Enter the address of the contact person (optional).
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant (optional).
* **Agreement Date**: Enter the date of the last agreement signed by the participant.
* **Participant DOB**: Enter the date of birth of the participant.
* **Contact DOB**: Enter the date of birth of the contact person (optional).
* **Class Dates**: Enter the dates when the classes took place.
* **Class Time**: Enter the time of the classes.
* **Production Dates**: Enter the dates of the production.
* **Production Time**: Enter the time of the production.
* **Participant Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the participant (optional).
* **Contact Notes**: Enter any additional notes or comments from the contact person (optional).
* **Classes Participated**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has attended classes.
* **Rehearsals Attended**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has attended rehearsals.
* **Productions Participated**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has participated in productions.
* **Agreement Acknowledged**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you have read and understood the agreement.
* **Agreement Signed**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that you have signed the agreement.
* **Participant Consent**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has given consent.
* **Contact Consent**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the contact person has given consent.
* **Participant Terms Acknowledged**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has read and understood the detailed terms.
* **Participant Detailed Terms Acknowledged**: Choose 'Yes' to indicate that the participant has read and understood the detailed terms.

Please note that all fields marked as required must be filled out before submitting the form.
