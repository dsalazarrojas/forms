# Homeschool Cooperative Expression Of Interest - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to express interest in joining a homeschool cooperative. The goal of this form is to provide essential information about you and your family to help us understand your interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and Zip Code.
2. Select the services you are interested in (Early Childhood Education, High School and Beyond, Support for Homeschooling Families, or Homeschooling Co-op).
3. Provide family information (optional).
4. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
5. If applicable, provide information about your primary and secondary parents (names and phone numbers).
6. Enter the names of your children.
7. Select the age and grade level of your children (optional).
8. Answer why you are choosing to homeschool your children.
9. Add any additional notes or information (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you'd like to be contacted by phone.
* **Street** (`Street`, text, optional): Enter your street address.
* **City** (`City`, text, optional): Enter the city where you reside.
* **State** (`State`, text, optional): Enter the state where you reside.
* **Zip Code** (`Zip Code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **What services are you interested in?** (`What services are you interested in?`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you're interested in:
	+ Early Childhood Education
	+ High School and Beyond
	+ Support for Homeschooling Families
	+ Homeschooling Co-op
* **Family Information** (`Family Information`, text, optional): Share any additional family information you'd like to provide.
* **Additional Comments** (`Additional Comments`, text, optional): Add any additional comments or information.
* **Primary Parent Name** (`Primary Parent Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the primary parent.
* **Primary Parent Phone** (`Primary Parent Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the primary parent.
* **Secondary Parent Name** (`Secondary Parent Name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the secondary parent.
* **Secondary Parent Phone** (`Secondary Parent Phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the secondary parent.
* **Names of Children** (`Names of Children`, text, optional): Enter the names of your children.
* **Age of Children** (`Age of Children`, select_one, optional): Select the age of your children:
	+ One
	+ Two
	+ Three
	+ Four or More
* **What type of school are the children currently attending?** (`What type of school are the children currently attending?`, select_one, required): Select the type of school your children are currently attending:
	+ Public
	+ Private
	+ Homeschool
	+ Other
* **Name of School** (`Name of School`, text, optional): Enter the name of the school.
* **City of School** (`City of School`, text, optional): Enter the city of the school.
* **State of School** (`State of School`, text, optional): Enter the state of the school.
* **Age of Children** (`Age of Children`, select_one, optional): Select the age of your children:
	+ Under 5
	+ 5-10
	+ 11-13
	+ 14-15
	+ 16 or Older
* **Grade Level of Children** (`Grade Level of Children`, select_one, optional): Select the grade level of your children:
	+ Preschool

	+ 3-5

	+ 9-11
	+ '12'
	+ 13-15
	+ 16-18
	+ 19 or Older
* **Why Homeschooling** (`Why Homeschooling`, text, optional): Explain why you're choosing to homeschool your children.
* **Additional Notes** (`Additional Notes`, text, optional): Add any additional notes or information.
