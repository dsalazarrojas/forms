# Ship Model Makers Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ship Model Makers Membership Form is used to register new members in the Ship Model Makers community. It helps the administrators to collect essential information about new members, their ship models, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields. These fields are required and should be filled in with your actual names.
2.  Enter your **Email** and **Phone** numbers in the next two fields. The email will be used for communication, and phone number will be used as a contact number.
3.  Provide your **Organization** details, which is the name of your ship model makers organization or company.
4.  Choose your **Role** within the Ship Model Makers community. This could be Member, Sponsor, or Partner.
5.  Agree to the **Terms and Conditions** and **Privacy Policy** to complete your registration.
6.  You can optionally fill in other fields if you have a ship model you want to share with the community. This includes **Ship Type**, **Ship Hull Type**, **Ship Length**, **Draft Date**, and other relevant details.
7.  Upload your **Ship Image** and **Ship Model** if you want to share them with the community.
8.  Finally, review and submit your registration form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: A text field to input your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to input your last name.
* **Email**: A text field to input your email address.
* **Phone**: A text field to input your phone number.
* **Organization**: A text field to input the name of your ship model makers organization or company.
* **Role**: Select your role within the Ship Model Makers community as Member, Sponsor, or Partner.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Select to agree to the community's terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Policy**: Select to agree to the community's privacy policy.
* **Ship Type**: A text field to input the type of ship model you are working on (optional).
* **Ship Hull Type**: A text field to input the type of hull your ship model has (optional).
* **Ship Length**: A number field to input the length of your ship model (optional).
* **Draft Date**: A date field to input the draft date of your ship model (optional).
* **Ship Facts**: Select multiple facts about your ship model like Bow shape, Length, Beam, and Draft.
* **Ship Image**: Upload your ship model image to share with the community.
* **Ship Model**: Upload your ship model 3D file to share with the community (optional).
* **Ship Notes**: A text field to input additional notes about your ship model.
* **Ship Image Alt Text**: A text field to input alternative text for your ship image to make it accessible to visually impaired users.
* **Ship Image Caption**: A text field to input caption for your ship image.
* **Member Type**: A text field to input type of member you are (optional).
* **Member Email**: A text field to input email address of the member (optional).
* **Member Phone**: A text field to input phone number of the member (optional).
* **Organization Type**: A text field to input type of organization you are (optional).
* **Sponsor Type**: A text field to input type of sponsor you are (optional).
* **Partner Type**: A text field to input type of partner you are (optional).
* **Ship Model Makers Membership Terms**: A text field to input any additional terms related to your membership.
* **Ship Model Makers Membership Privacy**: A text field to input any additional privacy terms related to your membership.
