# Music Studio Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register users in the music studio, providing necessary information for studio management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first page with your name and contact details.
2. Select your preferred music style.
3. Choose your available and preferred days for sessions.
4. Set your preferred studio time and instrument (if applicable).
5. Fill out any additional information about your teacher and level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Required): Enter 10 digits for your phone number.
* **What genre of music do you play?** (Select Multiple, Optional): Choose the music genre that best describes you (e.g., Jazz, Classical, Rock, etc.).
* **Preferred Time** (Time, Optional): Select a preferred time for your studio sessions.
* **Available Days** (Select One, Optional): Choose the days you are available for studio sessions.
* **Preferred Days** (Select One, Optional): Choose your preferred days for studio sessions.
* **Studio Time** (Time, Required): Select a time for your studio sessions.
* **Instrument** (Text, Optional): Enter your instrument of choice (if applicable).
* **Teacher** (Text, Optional): Enter your teacher's name (if applicable).
* **Level** (Select One, Optional): Choose your level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).

## Tips
- Please fill out the form accurately and comprehensively.
- Make sure to select your preferred and available days correctly to ensure efficient studio management.
- Enter your contact information correctly to enable smooth communication.
- If you have any issues or concerns, contact the music studio administration.
