# Health Fair Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants and attendees of the Health Fair event. It is designed to gather relevant details for registration, medical history, and emergency contact information. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
- Enter your name and email address in the corresponding fields.
- If you have any medical conditions, allergies, or medications, please provide this information in the "Medical History" field.
- For participants who are not health professionals, select your participant type (e.g., Patient, Family Member).
- If you need to contact you in case of an emergency, please provide the contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field.
- If you have any additional notes or comments, please include them in the "Notes" field.
- Please review the form for accuracy and completeness before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (2, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (3, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Information** (4, note, required/optional): Provide any additional information about your participation in the event.
* **Date** (5, date, required/optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the event.
* **Time** (6, time, required/optional): Enter the time you plan to attend the event.
* **Medical History** (7, text, required/optional): Provide any medical conditions, allergies, or medications you are currently taking.
* **Medical Condition** (8, text, required/optional): List any medical conditions you have.
* **Allergies** (9, select_multiple, required/optional): Select any allergies you have (e.g., Peanuts, Pollen).
* **Medication** (10, text, required/optional): List any medications you are currently taking.
* **Emergency Contact** (11, text, required/optional): Provide the contact information for someone who can be reached in case of an emergency.
* **Contact Info** (12, text, required/optional): Provide any additional contact information for yourself.
* **Medical Provider** (13, select_one, required/optional): Select the name of your medical provider.
* **Participant Type** (14, select_one, required/optional): Select your participant type (e.g., Health Professional, Patient, Family Member).
* **Participant Status** (15, select_one, required/optional): Select your participant status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Event Date** (16, date, required/optional): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (17, time, required/optional): Enter the time of the event.
* **Participant Id** (18, text, required/optional): Enter a participant ID (if applicable).
* **Notes** (19, note, required/optional): Provide any additional comments or notes about your participation.
