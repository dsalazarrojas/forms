# Workshop Facilitator Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workshop Facilitator Application Form is designed to collect information from potential workshop facilitators who wish to lead our workshops. This form helps us assess their experience, skills, and availability to host and facilitate our workshops.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your **Full name** in the first field.
2.  Enter your **Contact Email** in the second field.
3.  In the third field, describe your **Workshop experience**.
4.  Select the **Workshop topic** you are interested in leading.
5.  Choose the **Workshop duration** you are comfortable with.
6.  Select how often you are available to lead the workshop, choosing from **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, **Bi-Monthly**, **Bi-Quarterly**, **Bi-Weekly**, or **Annual**.
7.  Choose if you will be leading the workshop **Online**, **Off-site**, or **On-site**.
8.  In the **Workshop dates** field, select the dates you are available to lead the workshop.
9.  In the **Workshop time** field, select the time you are available to lead the workshop.
10. In the **Workshop facilitators note** field, add any additional information that might be helpful for us.
11. If you have any additional information about the workshop facilitators, you can add it in the **Workshop facilitators additional info** field.
12. If the workshop will be held in a physical location, you can add the **Workshop venue address**.
13. In the **Workshop facilitators agreed to terms** field, confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions.
14. Confirm that you have agreed to our **Workshop facilitators agreed to part**.
15. Confirm that you have agreed to our **Workshop facilitators reference**.
16. Confirm that you have agreed to our **Workshop facilitators agreed terms**.
17. Confirm that you have agreed to our **Workshop facilitators agreed part**.
18. Confirm that you have agreed to our **Workshop facilitators agreed reference**.
19. Confirm that you have agreed to our **Workshop facilitators agreed terms 2**.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents. (<type:text>, <required:true>)
*   **Contact Email**: Your email address where we can reach you. (<type:email>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop experience**: Briefly describe your experience in facilitating workshops. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop topic**: The topic you are interested in leading. (<type:text>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop duration**: The duration of the workshop you are willing to lead. (<type:number>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop frequency**: How often you are available to lead the workshop. (<type:select_multiple>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop location**: Where the workshop will be held, **Online**, **Off-site**, or **On-site**. (<type:select_one>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop dates**: Select the dates you are available to lead the workshop. (<type:date>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop time**: Select the time you are available to lead the workshop. (<type:time>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop facilitators note**: Any additional information you want to add about the workshop. (<type:note>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators additional info**: Additional information about the workshop facilitators. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop venue address**: The address of the workshop location, if applicable. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators experience**: Additional information about the workshop facilitators' experience. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators interest**: Additional information about the workshop facilitators' interest. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Contact Phone**: Your phone number where we can reach you. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Availability**: Any other times you are available to lead the workshop. (<type:text>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators agreed to terms**: Confirm that you have read and agreed to our terms and conditions. (<type:select_one>, <required:true>)
*   **Workshop facilitators agreed to part**: Confirm that you have agreed to our part. (<type:select_one>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators reference**: Confirm that you have agreed to our reference. (<type:select_one>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators agreed terms**: Confirm that you have agreed to our terms. (<type:select_one>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators agreed part**: Confirm that you have agreed to our part. (<type:select_one>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators agreed reference**: Confirm that you have agreed to our reference. (<type:select_one>, <required:false>)
*   **Workshop facilitators agreed terms 2**: Confirm that you have agreed to our terms 2. (<type:select_one>, <required:false>)

## Tips
*   Fill in the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
*   We will review your application carefully and respond to you accordingly.
