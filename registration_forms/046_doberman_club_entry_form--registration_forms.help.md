# Doberman Club Entry Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Doberman Club Entry Form is a critical tool for joining or sponsoring the Doberman club. It gathers essential information from users, including their name, contact details, and relevant skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Doberman Club Entry Form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **FormData** on the first page. This is the main entry point for your information.
2. Provide your **Name** in the "Name" field, which is a crucial piece of identifying information.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the "Email" field, which will be used for communication and updates.
4. In the "Phone" field, enter your contact phone number, which will help the club reach you for any important announcements or updates.
5. For the "Date" field, select your birthday or relevant date for the club's records.
6. For the "Time" field, choose your preferred time of day or any specific time-related information.
7. Select your **Membership Type** in the "Membership Type" field. This will indicate your role within the club, either as a Member or Sponsor.
8. List your **Skills** in the "Skills" field, which will help the club know about your expertise or areas of interest.
9. Add any additional **Notes** in the "Notes" field, which will be used to record any relevant details or comments.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **FormData**: This is the main entry page for your information. Please fill it in accurately.
* **Name**: Enter your real name, which will be used for club communications and records.
* **Email**: Enter your active email address for club updates and messages.
* **Phone**: Enter your contact phone number for club announcements and updates.
* **Date**: Select your birthday or any relevant date for the club's records.
* **Time**: Choose your preferred time of day or any specific time-related information.
* **Membership Type**: Select either Member or Sponsor, indicating your role within the club.
* **Skills**: List your relevant skills or areas of interest, which will help the club know about your expertise.
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or details that the club should know about you.

## Tips
* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely to avoid mistakes and confusion.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer back to this guide for clarification.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the club administration for assistance.
