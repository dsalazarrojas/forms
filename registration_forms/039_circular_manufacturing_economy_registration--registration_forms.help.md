# Circular Manufacturing Economy Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will walk you through the steps to complete the circular manufacturing economy registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **Full Name**. This is the name of the registrant.
2. Enter your **Email Address**. This is the email address of the registrant.
3. Enter your **Phone Number**. This is the phone number of the registrant.
4. Enter your **Company Name**. This is the name of the company.
5. Enter your **Company Address**. This should include the street address, city, and state, as well as zip code.
6. Enter your **Job Title**. This is the job title of the registrant.
7. Select your **Department**. Choose the department where the registrant works.
8. Select your **Company Size**. Choose the number of employees in the company.
9. Select your **Type of Manufacturing**. Choose the type of manufacturing the company is involved in.
10. Select your **Current Circular Practices**. Choose all the practices your company is currently implementing.
11. Select your **Current Waste Percentage**. Choose the percentage of waste generated by your company.
12. Enter your **Sustainability Goals**. Describe your company's goals for sustainability.
13. Select your **Registration Type**. Choose whether you are registering as an individual, team, or full company program.
14. If you are registering as a team, enter the **Number of Team Members**. This is not required.
15. If you are registering as a team, enter the **Team Member Names**. This is not required.
16. Select the **Session Tracks** you are interested in. Choose all that apply.
17. Select whether you are **Interested in Networking Sessions**. Yes or no.
18. Select whether you are **Interested in Exhibition Booth**. Yes, no, or maybe.
19. If you have **Dietary Requirements**, enter them here. This is not required.
20. If you have **Accessibility Needs**, enter them here. This is not required.
21. Select your **Payment Method**. Choose how you want to pay.
22. Enter any **Additional Comments** you have. This is not required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Participant Name, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for communication.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, required): Enter your company name.
* **Company Address** (Company Address, text, required): Enter your company's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Job Title** (Job Title, text, required): Enter your job title in the company.
* **Department** (Department, select_one, required): Choose your department in the company.
* **Company Size** (Company Size, select_one, required): Choose the number of employees in your company.
* **Type of Manufacturing** (Type of Manufacturing, select_one, required): Choose the type of manufacturing your company is involved in.
* **Current Circular Practices** (Current Circular Practices, select_multiple, required): Choose all the circular practices your company currently implements.
* **Current Waste Percentage** (Current Waste Percentage, select_one, required): Choose the percentage of waste generated by your company.
* **Sustainability Goals** (Sustainability Goals, text, required): Describe your company's goals for sustainability.
* **Registration Type** (Registration Type, select_one, required): Choose whether you are registering as an individual, team, or full company program.
* **Number of Team Members** (Number of Team Members, number, optional): If registering as a team, enter the number of team members.
* **Team Member Names** (Team Member Names, text, optional): If registering as a team, list the team member names.
* **Session Tracks** (Session Tracks, select_multiple, required): Choose all the session tracks you are interested in.
* **Interested in Networking Sessions** (Interested in Networking Sessions, select_one, required): Select whether you are interested in networking sessions.
* **Interested in Exhibition Booth** (Interested in Exhibition Booth, select_one, optional): Select whether you are interested in an exhibition booth.
* **Dietary Requirements** (Dietary Requirements, text, optional): If you have dietary requirements, enter them here.
* **Accessibility Needs** (Accessibility Needs, text, optional): If you have accessibility needs, enter them here.
* **Payment Method** (Payment Method, select_one, required): Choose your payment method.
* **Any Additional Comments** (Any Additional Comments, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information here.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields.
* If you are registering as a team, make sure to list all team members.
* If you have dietary or accessibility needs, please let us know so we can accommodate you.
* Choose your payment method carefully, as some payment methods may have additional fees.
* If you have any additional comments or information, please enter them in the "Any Additional Comments" field.
