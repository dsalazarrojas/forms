# Knitting Circle Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to sign up for a knitting circle, a social group focused on knitting activities. It helps us gather information about your schedule and preferences to better organize and tailor our events to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "User Input" page to begin filling out the form.
2. On the first page, provide your general contact information and any specific details about your event or meeting.
3. Move to the next page to select the time and date of your meeting.
4. Choose your preferred event start and end times from the dropdown menus.
5. Select your preferred meeting day from the dropdown menu.
6. Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
7. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Input**: This is the first page of the form where you can provide any general information about yourself and your event.
	+ **User Input**: A space to enter your user name or alias.
	+ **Event Details**: A space to enter any specific details about your event or meeting.
* **Event Start Time**: 
	+ Select your preferred start time for the meeting.
* **Event End Time**: 
	+ Select your preferred end time for the meeting.
* **Meeting Day**: 
	+ Choose your preferred day for the meeting.
* **Meeting Date**: 
	+ Enter the date of your meeting.
* **Meeting Time**: 
	+ Enter the time of your meeting.
* **User Preferences**: 
	+ Enter any additional comments or notes about your preferences.
* **Email**: 
	+ Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone**: 
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Note**: 
	+ Enter any additional notes or comments.
* **Member Preferences 2**: 
	+ Enter any additional preferences you have. 

Note that not all fields are required, and you are encouraged to fill out only the relevant fields for your specific situation. 

Please let us know if you have any questions or concerns with the form or its instructions.
