# music_festival_vendor_extension_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from vendors wishing to participate in a music festival.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your name and contact information in the `vendor_name` and `contact_number` fields.
2. Enter your email address in the `email` field.
3. Select your vendor type from the `vendor_type` dropdown menu.
4. Provide a brief description of your products or services in the `product_info` field.
5. Select the types of events you are willing to participate in from the `event_participation` multiple-choice field.
6. Choose the type of contract you are interested in from the `vendor_contract` dropdown menu.
7. Add any additional information about your products or services in the `additional_info` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, required): Enter your company name and/or your name as the representative of the vendor.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, number, optional): Enter your contact number for easy communication.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for easy communication and festival updates.
* **Vendor Type** (`vendor_type`, select_one, optional): Select the type of vendor you are (e.g., food, craft, merchandise).
* **Product Info** (`product_info`, text, optional): Briefly describe the products or services you will be offering.
* **Event Participation** (`event_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Select the types of events you are willing to participate in (e.g., main stage, side stage, workshops).
* **Vendor Contract** (`vendor_contract`, select_one, optional): Choose the type of contract you are interested in (e.g., standard, premium).
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your products or services.
