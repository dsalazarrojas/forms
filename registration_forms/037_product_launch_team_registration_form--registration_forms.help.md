# Product Launch Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Product Launch Team Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who are interested in joining the team. The form is used to gather essential details that will help the team understand the user's preferences, needs, and qualifications.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Choose your roles**: Select one or more roles that you are interested in joining. You can choose from Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3.
2. **Enter your skills**: Type in your relevant skills or areas of expertise.
3. **Provide your availability**: Enter your available dates and times for the team.
4. **Share your contact information**: Enter your email and phone number so we can reach out to you.
5. **Add any additional information**: Provide any additional details that you think would be helpful for the team to know.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Roles** (product_launch_team_registration_form_roles): Select one or more roles that you are interested in joining. This will help us understand your areas of interest and expertise. 
- **Skills** (product_launch_team_registration_form_skills): Type in your relevant skills or areas of expertise. This is your opportunity to showcase your qualifications and abilities.
- **Availability** (product_launch_team_registration_form_availability): Enter your available dates and times for the team. Please note that this field is not required but it will help us plan team activities more effectively.
- **Email** (product_launch_team_registration_form_email): Enter your email address so we can reach out to you. This is a required field and will not be shared with anyone.
- **Phone** (product_launch_team_registration_form_phone): Enter your phone number so we can contact you for further information or discussion. This is a required field but will not be shared with anyone.
- **Note** (product_launch_team_registration_form_note): This is an optional field where you can add any additional information that you think would be helpful for the team to know.
- **Additional Info** (product_launch_team_registration_form_additional_info): This is also an optional field where you can provide any additional details that you think would be helpful for the team to know.
