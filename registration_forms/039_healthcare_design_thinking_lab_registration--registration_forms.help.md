## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants in a healthcare design thinking lab.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant page (Main) and provide a brief description or title of your registration.
2. In the "Participant Details" page, provide your personal and professional information, including your name, organization, and role in the lab.
3. In the "Signup Details" page, enter your email address and any other details you want to share about your participation.
4. Click "Submit" to complete your registration.
