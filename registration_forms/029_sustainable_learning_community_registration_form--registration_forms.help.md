# Sustainable Learning Community Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to join the sustainable learning community. This form will be used by administrators or moderators to gather contact information, interests, and participation preferences of community members.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your member name in the text field below.
2. Provide your email address in the format of your@example.com.
3. Indicate your interests by selecting one or multiple options from the dropdown menu.
4. Describe your participation preferences in the text field.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Member Name**: `member_name` (text, required)
  Enter your name to identify yourself as a community member.

- **Email**: `member_email` (email, required)
  Enter your email address in the format of your@example.com.

- **Interests**: `member_interests` (select multiple, optional)
  Select one or multiple options that describe your interests.

- **Participation Preferences**: `participation_preferences` (text, optional)
  Describe how you would like to participate in the community.

- **Note**: (not applicable, not applicable)
  This field is not applicable to this form.

- **Form Footer**: (not applicable, not applicable)
  This field is not intended for user input, it seems to be a static text field.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter a valid email address.
- Select multiple interests by holding down the Ctrl key and selecting options.
- Describe your participation preferences clearly and concisely.
- Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.
