# Event Sponsorship Authorization Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used by event organizers to collect information from potential sponsors for events, including their contact details, sponsorship terms, and additional information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the event sponsor's information, including their name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide the sponsor's title and company name.
3. Enter the sponsor's contact name.
4. Specify the sponsorship terms or any specific requirements.
5. Add any additional information about the sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Sponsorship Information**: This field is used to capture the event sponsor's information, including their name and any other details they'd like to share.
* **Event Organizer**: This field is for the event organizer's name.
* **Sponsor**: This field is for the sponsor's name.
* **Sponsor Email**: This field is for the sponsor's email address. Please make sure to format the email according to your email standards.
* **Sponsor Phone**: This field is for the sponsor's phone number.
* **Sponsor Company**: This field is for the sponsor's company name.
* **Sponsor Title**: This field is for the sponsor's title or position.
* **Sponsor Contact Name**: This field is for the sponsor's contact person's name.
* **Sponsorship Terms**: This field is for any specific terms or conditions of the sponsorship.
* **Additional Information**: This field is for any extra details the sponsor would like to share about the sponsorship.

## Tips
* Please make sure to double-check the information before submitting.
* Use a standard format for the email address and phone number.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your event organizer.
