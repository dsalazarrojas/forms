# 2017 Artist Jamboree Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The 2017 Artist Jamboree Registration form is designed for artists who want to participate in the event. The form collects contact information, artistic discipline, experience level, and participation preferences to help the organizers tailor the event to the artist's needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your full name as it appears on your ID.
2. If you perform under a different name, enter your stage name.
3. Enter your email address to receive confirmation and updates.
4. Include your phone number with the area code.
5. For age verification purposes, enter your date of birth.
6. If applicable, enter your current age.
7. Enter your mailing address where you'd like to receive physical materials.
8. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
9. Select your primary artistic discipline from the given options.
10. If applicable, select your secondary artistic disciplines.
11. Choose your experience level: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional, or Master.
12. If applicable, enter the number of years you've been actively practicing your craft.
13. If you have a portfolio website or online gallery, enter the URL.
14. Select how you'd like to participate: Attendee, Exhibitor, Workshop Presenter, Vendor, Volunteer, or Performer.
15. If applicable, enter a brief description of your proposed workshop topic.
16. If applicable, indicate any special equipment needs or requirements.
17. If applicable, enter any dietary restrictions or allergies.
18. If applicable, indicate any special requirements or accommodations.
19. Select how you heard about the event.
20. Finally, review the terms and conditions and select the "True" option to confirm your agreement.
