# Convention Press Desk Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Convention Press Desk Registration Form is a crucial tool for journalists, media representatives, and other press personnel to register for press credentials, access, and services at our convention. This guide will walk you through each field and help you fill it out correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  To fill out this form, start by clicking on the "Submit" button.
2.  Enter your Journalist Full Name in the "Journalist Full Name" field. This is your legal name as it appears on your ID.
3.  Enter your professional email address in the "Email Address" field.
4.  Type in your best contact number in the "Phone Number" field.
5.  If applicable, enter your media organization name in the "-- Media Organization --" field.
6.  Enter the name of the publication or station you represent in the "Media Outlet Name" field.
7.  Choose the type of media organization you represent from the "Type of Media" select menu.
8.  Enter the URL of your media organization's website in the "Media Website URL" field.
9.  If you wish to provide it, enter the approximate audience size of your publication in the "Circulation or Audience Reach" field.
10. Fill in your job title in the "Job Title" field.
11. If you have a press card, enter the number in the "Press Card Number" field.
12. If you have a press card, enter the name of the organization that issued it in the "Press Card Issuing Organization" field.
13. Enter the number of years of journalism experience you have in the "Years of Journalism Experience" field.
14.  Enter any special coverage plans you have in the "Type of Coverage Planned" select menu.
15.  If you'd like to interview specific people, list them in the "Interview Requests" field.
16.  List any equipment you'll bring with you in the "Equipment You Will Bring" field.
17.  Choose the type of assignment from the "Assignment Type" select menu.
18.  If you're representing a publication or agency, enter the editor or supervisor's name and contact information in the "Editor or Supervisor Name" and "Editor Contact Information" fields.
19.  Enter the deadline for your story in the "Publication or Broadcast Deadline" field.
20.  If you're planning to attend press conferences, select "True" in the "Will you attend press conferences" field.
21.  If you need a press kit, choose the type from the "Do you need a press kit" select menu.
22.  If you need photo access, choose "True" in the "Do you need photo access" field.
23.  If you need broadcast access, choose "True" in the "Do you need broadcast access" field.
24.  If you have recent articles published, enter their links in the "Recent Relevant Articles" field.
25.  If you have an online portfolio, enter the URL in the "Online Portfolio URL" field.
26.  If you have professional social media handles, list them in the "Professional Social Media Handles" field.
27.  Choose "True" in the "I agree to follow media guidelines" field.
28.  Choose "True" in the "I confirm all information is accurate" field.
29.  Choose "True" in the "I agree to respect embargoes" field.
30.  If you have any additional notes or requests, enter them in the "Additional Notes or Requests" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Journalist Full Name** (`journalist_name`, text, required): Enter your legal full name as it appears on your ID.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Enter your professional email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your best contact number.
*   **Media Organization** (`section_media_organization`, note, optional): If you represent a media organization, enter its name here.
*   **Media Outlet Name** (`media_outlet`, text, required): Enter the name of the publication or station you represent.
*   **Type of Media** (`media_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of media organization you represent:
    *   Print Newspaper
    *   Magazine
    *   Television
    *   Radio
    *   Online News
    *   Blog
    *   Podcast
    *   News Agency
    *   Freelance
    *   Other
*   **Media Website URL** (`media_website`, text, optional): If you have a website, enter its URL.
*   **Circulation or Audience Reach** (`circulation_or_reach`, text, optional): If you wish to provide it, enter the approximate audience size of your publication.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
*   **Press Card Number** (`press_card_number`, text, optional): If you have a press card, enter its number.
*   **Press Card Issuing Organization** (`press_card_issuing_body`, text, optional): If you have a press card, enter the name of the organization that issued it.
*   **Years of Journalism Experience** (`years_experience`, number, optional): Enter the number of years of journalism experience you have.
*   **Type of Coverage Planned** (`coverage_type`, select_multiple, required): Choose the types of coverage you plan:
    *   News Article
    *   Feature Story
    *   Interview
    *   Live Report
    *   Photo Coverage
    *   Video Coverage
    *   Social Media
    *   Podcast Episode
    *   Review
    *   Other
*   **Interview Requests** (`interview_requests`, text, optional): If you'd like to interview specific people, list them here.
*   **Equipment You Will Bring** (`equipment_needed`, text, optional): List any equipment you'll bring with you.
*   **Assignment Type** (`assignment_type`, select_one, required): Choose the type of assignment:
    *   Assigned by Editor
    *   Self-Initiated
    *   Agency Assignment
    *   Freelance Pitch
*   **Editor or Supervisor Name** (`editor_name`, text, optional): If you're representing a publication or agency, enter the editor or supervisor's name.
*   **Editor Contact Information** (`editor_contact`, text, optional): If you're representing a publication or agency, enter the editor or supervisor's contact information.
*   **Publication or Broadcast Deadline** (`deadline_info`, text, optional): Enter the deadline for your story.
*   **Will you attend press conferences** (`press_conference_attendance`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you plan to attend press conferences.
*   **Do you need a press kit** (`press_kit_needed`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes - Digital", "Yes - Physical", or "False" if you need a press kit.
*   **Do you need photo access** (`photo_access_required`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you need photo access.
*   **Do you need broadcast access** (`broadcast_access_required`, select_one, optional): Choose "True" if you need broadcast access.
*   **Recent Relevant Articles** (`recent_articles`, text, optional): If you have recent articles published, enter their links.
*   **Online Portfolio URL** (`portfolio_url`, text, optional): If you have an online portfolio, enter its URL.
*   **Professional Social Media Handles** (`social_media_handles`, text, optional): List your professional social media handles.
*   **I agree to follow media guidelines** (`media_guidelines_accept`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you agree to follow media guidelines.
*   **I confirm all information is accurate** (`credential_accuracy`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you confirm all information is accurate.
*   **I agree to respect embargoes** (`embargo_agreement`, select_one, required): Choose "True" if you agree to respect embargoes.
*   **Additional Notes or Requests** (`additional_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes or requests.
