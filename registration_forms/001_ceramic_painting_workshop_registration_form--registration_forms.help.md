# Ceramic Painting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information and preferences for a Ceramic Painting Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email Address" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
4. Select your age group from the "Age Group" options.
5. Choose your preferred session from the "Preferred Session" options.
6. Select the specific date for the session in the "Workshop Date" field.
7. Choose the ceramic item you would like to work on from the "Ceramic Item to Paint" options.
8. Indicate your experience level with ceramic painting from the "Your Experience Level" options.
9. Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" and "Emergency Contact Phone" fields.
10. List any allergies or health concerns in the "Allergies or Health Concerns" field.
11. Confirm that you consent to being photographed during the workshop for promotional purposes in the "Photo Release Consent" field.
12. Agree to the workshop rules and safety guidelines by selecting "I Agree" in the "Terms and Conditions Agreement" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, required): Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Enter your email address for registration confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you.
* **Age Group** (Age Group, select_one, required): Select your age category (Child (Under 12), Teen (12-17), Adult (18-64), Senior (65+)).
* **Preferred Session** (Preferred Session, select_one, required): Choose the date and time you prefer to attend (Morning Session, Afternoon Session, Evening Session).
* **Workshop Date** (Workshop Date, date, required): Select the specific date for the session.
* **Ceramic Item to Paint** (Ceramic Item to Paint, select_one, required): Choose the item you would like to work on (Coffee Mug, Salad Plate, Cereal Bowl, Small Vase, Figurines).
* **Your Experience Level** (Your Experience Level, select_one, optional): Indicate your experience level with ceramic painting (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of a person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Allergies or Health Concerns** (Allergies or Health Concerns, text, optional): List any allergies or health concerns we should be aware of.
* **Photo Release Consent** (Photo Release Consent, select_one, required): Consent to being photographed during the workshop for promotional purposes.
* **Terms and Conditions Agreement** (Terms and Conditions Agreement, select_one, required): Agree to the workshop rules and safety guidelines.
