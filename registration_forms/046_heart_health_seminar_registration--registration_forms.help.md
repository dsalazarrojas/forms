# Heart Health Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Heart Health Seminar Registration form is designed to gather essential information from attendees to facilitate their participation in the heart health seminar. Please fill out this form carefully to ensure that the seminar organizers can contact you and make necessary arrangements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the requested information, making sure to select the correct options when applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your active email address where the seminar organizers can contact you with updates and notifications.
* **Phone number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted via phone, please enter your phone number here.
* **I understand the terms and conditions** (`consent_1`, select_one, required): Select "I accept the terms and conditions" to confirm your understanding of the agreement.
* **I accept the use of my contact information** (`consent_2`, select_one, required): Choose "I accept the use of my contact information" to allow the seminar organizers to contact you.
* **Medical condition** (`medical_condition`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please describe them here.
* **Dietary restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, text, optional): List any dietary restrictions you have, so we can accommodate your needs.
* **Event date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the seminar you'd like to attend.
* **Event time** (`event_time`, time, required): Choose the time of the seminar you'd like to attend.
* **Workshop** (`workshop`, select_one, required): Select the workshop you'd like to participate in, either "Workshop 1" or "Workshop 2".
* **Language** (`language`, select_multiple, required): Choose the languages you're comfortable speaking, either "English" or "Spanish".
* **Dietary restrictions comments** (`dietary_restrictions_comment`, text, optional): If you have any additional comments about your dietary restrictions, please enter them here.
* **Payment method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select your preferred payment method, either "Credit Card" or "Cash".
* **Credit card number** (`credit_card_number`, text, optional): Enter your credit card number if you've chosen "Credit Card" as your payment method.
* **Credit card expiration** (`credit_card_expiration`, text, optional): Enter your credit card expiration date if you've chosen "Credit Card" as your payment method.
* **Workshop preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, text, optional): If you have any preferences for the workshop, please enter them here.
* **Additional information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): If you have any other information you'd like to provide, enter it here.
