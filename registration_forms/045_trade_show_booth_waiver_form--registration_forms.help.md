# Trade Show Booth Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to ensure that trade show exhibitors are aware of and agree to the terms and conditions of participating in the trade show.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Review each field carefully and select the correct option for each.
2. Make sure to answer each field honestly and accurately.
3. Sign and date the form to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Acknowledge Terms
* **Acknowledge Terms (`acknowledge_terms`, select_one, optional):** 
    Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have read and understood the terms and conditions of the trade show.

### Waiver Date
* **Waiver Date (`waiver_date`, date, optional):** 
    Please enter the date you acknowledge the terms and conditions.

### Expiration Date
* **Expiration Date (`waiver_expiration_date`, date, optional):** 
    Please enter the date when this waiver will expire.

### Signature
* **Signature (`signature`, text, optional):** 
    Please print or type your signature in this field.

### Waiver Agreement
* **Waiver Agreement (`waiver_agreement`, select_one, optional):** 
    Please select "Yes" to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of the trade show.

### Agreement Acknowledgement
* **Agreement Acknowledgement (`agreement_acknowledgement`, text, optional):** 
    Please enter a brief acknowledgement of your agreement to the terms and conditions.

### Event Details
* **Event Details (`event_details`, select_multiple, optional):** 
    Please select the event details that apply to you.

### Waiver Agreement Signed
* **Waiver Agreement Signed (`waiver_agreement_signed`, select_one, optional):** 
    Please select "Yes" to confirm that you have signed this waiver agreement.

## Tips

* Make sure to review each field carefully before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask for clarification before proceeding.
* This form must be completed honestly and accurately to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience at the trade show.
