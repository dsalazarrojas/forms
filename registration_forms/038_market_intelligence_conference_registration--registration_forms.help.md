# market_intelligence_conference_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Market Intelligence Conference registration form is a tool for attendees to express their interests and requirements for the conference sessions. It helps the conference organizers to tailor the event to the attendees' needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your attendee details, including your name and any other relevant information.
2. Select your session interests from the list. Choose 'Yes' for the sessions you are interested in and 'No' for the ones you are not interested in.
3. Specify any additional session requirements you may have.
4. Review your session interests and requirements to ensure they are accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Attendee Details**: This is where you can enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
  * **Session Interests** (Select Multiple): Select 'Yes' for the sessions you are interested in and 'No' for the ones you are not interested in. You can select multiple sessions.
  * **Session Requirements** (Text): If you have any specific requirements for the sessions you are interested in, please specify them here.
  * **Requirements** (Text): This is an optional field to provide any additional requirements or comments you may have.
  * **Session Interests 1**, **Session Interests 2**: These are additional fields to select your session interests. Select 'Yes' for the sessions you are interested in and 'No' for the ones you are not interested in.
  * **Session Interests 1**, **Session Interests 2**: If you have any specific requirements for these sessions, please specify them in the **Requirements** field.

## Tips
- Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely, as this information will help the conference organizers tailor the event to your needs.
- If you have any other requirements or comments, please use the **Requirements** field.
- If you are unsure about any field, please contact the conference organizers for assistance.
