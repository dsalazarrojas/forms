# Business Coaching Program Admission Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Business Coaching Program Admission Form is a critical step towards gaining access to a valuable coaching program that will help your business grow and succeed. Please note that this form is a one-time submission, and you will not need to complete it again if you are accepted into the program.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review each field carefully to ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as such in the form.
3. Use the hints provided for guidance on what information is expected in each field.
4. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (Full Name, text, **Required**): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, **Required**): Enter your email address associated with your business or personal account.
* **Phone Number** (Phone Number, text, **Required**): Enter your contact number where you can be reached.
* **Company Name** (Company Name, text, **Required**): Enter the name of your business.
* **Your Position** (Your Position, text, **Required**): Enter your title or role within the company.
* **Business Information** (Business Information, note, **Not Required**): Enter a brief description of your business.
* **Industry** (Industry, select_one, **Required**): Select the sector related to your business from the provided options: Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Manufacturing, Retail, Professional Services, Education, Other.
* **Years in Business** (Years in Business, select_one, **Required**): Select the number of years you have been operating your business from the provided options: Less than 1 year, 1-3 years, 3-5 years, 5-10 years, More than 10 years.
* **Number of Employees** (Number of Employees, select_one, **Required**): Select the current team size of your business from the provided options: Solo, 2-5 employees, 6-10 employees, 11-20 employees, 21-50 employees, More than 50 employees.
* **Annual Revenue** (Annual Revenue, select_one, **Required**): Select an approximate revenue range of your business from the provided options: Under $100K, $100K-$500K, $500K-$1M, $1M-$5M, $5M-$10M, More than $10M.
* **Program Information** (Program Information, note, **Not Required**): Enter any additional information about the coaching program.
* **Program of Interest** (Program of Interest, select_one, **Required**): Select the coaching program you are interested in from the provided options: Executive Coaching, Leadership Development, Business Growth, Startup Accelerator, Team Coaching, Strategic Planning.
* **Preferred Start Date** (Preferred Start Date, select_one, **Required**): Select when you would like to begin the program from the provided options: Immediately, Within 1 Month, Within 3 Months, Next Cohort, Flexible.
* **Application Essay** (Application Essay, note, **Not Required**): Enter a brief statement about yourself and your motivations for joining the program.
* **Why Do You Want to Join** (Why Do You Want to Join, text, **Required**): Explain your motivation for joining the program.
* **What Is Your Biggest Challenge** (What Is Your Biggest Challenge, text, **Required**): Describe your main business challenge.
* **What Outcomes Do You Expect** (What Outcomes Do You Expect, text, **Required**): Enter the outcomes you hope to achieve from the program.
* **Can You Commit Time Required** (Can You Commit Time Required, select_one, **Required**): Confirm your availability and commitment to the program.
* **Have You Had Coaching Before** (Have You Had Coaching Before, select_one, **Required**): Confirm your previous coaching experience from the provided options: Yes - Positive Experience, Yes - Mixed Experience, Yes - Negative Experience, No - First Time.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (How Did You Hear About Us, select_one, **Required**): Select where you heard about the program from the provided options: Website, Referral, Social Media, Conference or Event, Advertisement, Other.
* **Investment Readiness** (Investment Readiness, select_one, **Required**): Confirm your investment readiness from the provided options: Yes - Budget Approved, Yes - Can Approve, Need More Information, Exploring Options.
* **Additional Comments** (Additional Comments, text, **Not Required**): Enter any additional comments or information you would like to share.

Please note that the required fields are marked as such in the form and must be completed in order to be eligible for the program.
