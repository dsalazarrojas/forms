# customer_consent_update_contact_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This help guide is for the customer consent update contact form. The form is used to update a customer's contact information and consent status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Client Consent" option to indicate whether the customer has given their consent or not.
2. Enter the customer's phone number.
3. Enter the customer's email address.
4. Enter the customer's address.
5. Select a date for the consent.
6. Add any additional notes about the customer's consent.
7. Select the tool assigned to the customer.
8. (Optional) Enter a unique form ID for system purposes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Consent Form** (customer_consent_update_form): This field is for the title of the form. It is a text field and is not required.
* **Client Consent** (client_consent): This field is a select multiple field where you can choose one or more of the options. You can choose from "Yes" or "No" to indicate whether the customer has given their consent.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): This field is for the customer's phone number. Enter the customer's phone number in the format (XXX) XXX-XXXX.
* **Email** (email): This field is for the customer's email address. Enter a valid email address.
* **Address** (address): This field is for the customer's address. Enter the customer's address in the format "street name, street number, city, state, country".
* **Consent Date** (consent_date): This field is for the date of the customer's consent. Select a date in the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Customer Consent Note** (customer_consent_note): This field is for any additional notes about the customer's consent. Enter any relevant information here.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): This field is a select one field where you can choose one of the options. You can choose from "Chatjimmy" or "Other".
* **Form ID** (form_id): This field is for a unique ID assigned to the form. Enter a number.
* **Output File** (output_file): This field is not applicable for the form, as it seems to be a reserved field for system purposes.

## Tips

* Make sure to select the correct options in the "Client Consent" field to accurately reflect the customer's consent status.
* Enter the customer's contact information in the correct format to ensure accuracy and avoid errors.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact our support team for assistance.
