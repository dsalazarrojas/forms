# Community Center Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Center Admission Form is designed to gather essential information from applicants interested in joining our community center. This form helps us to verify your identity, understand your interests, and provide a smooth onboarding experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields marked with a red asterisk (*).
2. For select fields, choose the most accurate option that best describes your information.
3. Ensure that your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
4. Review your submission carefully before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Applicant** (`applicant_name_adm`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents. This is the name we'll use to communicate with you.
* **Gender** (`applicant_gender_adm`, select_one, required): Please select the most accurate description of your gender identity. This information helps us tailor our programs and services to your needs.
* **Date of Birth** (`applicant_dob_adm`, date, required): Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY. This ensures we can verify your eligibility for certain programs and services.
* **Residential Address** (`applicant_address_adm`, text, required): Please enter your current residential address, including street, city, and zip code. This helps us communicate with you efficiently.
* **Email Address** (`applicant_email_adm`, email, required): Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly. We'll use this to send you important updates and notifications.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`applicant_phone_adm`, text, required): Please enter your primary phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Person** (`emergency_contact_name_adm`, text, required): Please enter the name of the person you'd like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Relationship to Applicant** (`emergency_contact_relation_adm`, text, required): Please describe your relationship to the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone_adm`, text, required): Please enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Type of Membership Requested** (`membership_type_requested`, select_one, required): Please select the type of membership that best suits your needs.
* **Primary Interest** (`primary_program_interest`, select_one, required): Please select the primary interest that brings you to our community center.
* **Referred by** (`referred_by_adm`, select_one, optional): If you were referred by someone, please let us know who referred you.
* **I agree to abide by the Community Center's rules and regulations** (`center_rules_acknowledgment`, select_one, required): Please acknowledge that you agree to our rules and regulations.
* **Digital Signature** (`applicant_signature_digital_adm`, text, required): Please type your full name as your digital signature.

Note: This form is for initial submission purposes only. If you've already submitted the form, please do not resubmit. If you're unsure about any field or have questions, please contact us at [contact information].
