# Hawaii Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Hawaii Event Registration Form is designed to collect information from users who want to register for a specific event in Hawaii. This form is used by event organizers to gather essential details for smooth event management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the Registration Info section with your basic details such as name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide event details like event date, time, location, and type.
3. Select the payment method and amount for registration.
4. If you have attendees, fill in their details like name, email, and phone number.
5. Confirm your registration status as Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Info** (`1`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in your basic details like name, email, and phone number.
* **Event Info** (`2`, `text`, **required**: false): Provide event details like event date, time, location, and type.
* **Contact Details** (`3`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in your contact details like name, email, and phone number.
* **Payment Info** (`4`, `text`, **required**: false): Select the payment method and amount for registration.
* **Registration Confirmation** (`5`, `text`, **required**: false): Confirm your registration status as Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled.
* **Event Date Time** (`6`, `text`, **required**: false): Enter event date and time.
* **Event Start Time** (`7`, `time`, **required**: false): Select the start time for the event.
* **Event Ending Time** (`8`, `time`, **required**: false): Select the ending time for the event.
* **Attendees Count** (`9`, `number`, **required**: false): Enter the number of attendees.
* **Attendees List** (`10`, `select_multiple`, **required**: false): Select Yes or No to indicate if you have attendees.
* **Attendee Name** (`11`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in the name of the attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`12`, `text`, **required**: false): Enter the email of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`13`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in the phone number of the attendee.
* **Attendee Comment** (`14`, `text`, **required**: false): Provide additional comments for the attendee.
* **Registration Status** (`15`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Confirm your registration status as Confirmed, Pending, or Cancelled.
* **Event Date** (`16`, `date`, **required**: false): Enter event date.
* **Event Time** (`17`, `time`, **required**: false): Select the event time.
* **Event Type** (`18`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in the type of event.
* **Event Location** (`19`, `text`, **required**: false): Enter event location.
* **Event Organizer** (`20`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in the name of the event organizer.
* **Attendee Info** (`21`, `text`, **required**: false): Provide additional information about the attendee.
* **Attendee Email** (`22`, `text`, **required**: false): Enter the email of the attendee.
* **Attendee Phone** (`23`, `text`, **required**: false): Fill in the phone number of the attendee.
* **Attendee Comment** (`24`, `text`, **required**: false): Provide additional comments for the attendee.
* **Registration Info 2** (`25`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select Online, Offline, or Both for registration info.
* **Payment Method** (`26`, `select_one`, **required**: false): Select the payment method as Bank Transfer, PayPal, or Credit Card.
* **Payment Amount** (`27`, `number`, **required**: false): Enter the payment amount.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields accurately to avoid any issues.
* Review your information before submission to ensure it is correct.
* If you are an event organizer, please fill this form carefully to avoid duplicating entries.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the event organizer.
