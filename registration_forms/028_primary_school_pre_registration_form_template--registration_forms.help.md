## Primary School Pre-Registration Form Template - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect pre-registration data from students and parents for primary school admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information for the student and parent.
2. Upload any necessary medical reports or signatures.
3. Review and submit the form once complete.
