# Project Manager Registration Form - Help Guide

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it should appear on official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it should appear on official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your work or personal email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your current job title or role.
* **Project ID** (`project_id`, number, optional): Enter the ID number for your project.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of your project or job.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of your project or job.
* **Work Phone** (`work_phone`, text, optional): Enter your work phone number.

* **Office Phone** (`office_phone`, text, optional): Enter your office phone number.
* **Work Email** (`work_email`, email, optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of your job or role (optional).
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your manager (if applicable).
