# Baby Photo Contest Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows parents and guardians to register their babies for a photo contest. The contest aims to showcase adorable baby photos and award prizes to the best ones.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Make sure to enter the correct contest category for your baby's age group.
3. Choose the correct gender of your baby.
4. Enter a catchy title for your photo.
5. Check the "I consent to the use of the submitted photo for contest promotion" box if you agree.
6. Confirm that you are the legal guardian of the child.
7. Select how you heard about the contest.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`parent_guardian_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as the parent or guardian of the baby.
* **Contact Email Address** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address where we will send notifications about the contest, including updates and results.
* **Phone Number** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you about the contest.
* **Baby's Full Name** (`baby_full_name`, text, required): Enter the baby's full name.
* **Baby's Date of Birth** (`baby_birth_date`, date, required): Enter the baby's date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Baby's Gender** (`baby_gender`, select_one, required): Select the correct gender of the baby.
* **Contest Category** (`entry_category`, select_one, required): Select the most appropriate category for your baby's photo based on their age group.
* **Photo Title or Caption** (`photo_title`, text, optional): Enter a catchy title or caption for your photo (not required but recommended).
* **I consent to the use of the submitted photo for contest promotion** (`publicity_consent`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes, I agree" if you allow your photo to be used for contest promotion on social media and our website.
* **I certify that I am the legal guardian of the child** (`legal_declaration`, select_one, required): Choose "I certify" to confirm your guardianship.
* **How did you hear about this contest?** (`how_did_you_hear`, select_multiple, optional): Select all applicable ways you heard about the contest.
