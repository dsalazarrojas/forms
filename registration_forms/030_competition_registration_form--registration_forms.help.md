# Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather essential information from competition participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience during the event. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to enable the organizers to provide the best possible experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out the form carefully, section by section, and ensure that all required fields are filled.
2.  Review each section's hints to better understand what information is required.
3.  Use the provided options for select questions to select the most accurate choice.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Full Name** (`Participant Full Name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the event materials.
*   **Email Address** (`Email Address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email for registration updates.
*   **Organization or School Affiliation** (`Organization or School Affiliation`, `text`, required): If you're representing an organization or school, enter the name here.
*   **Participant Age** (`Participant Age`, `number`, required): Provide your age for age-based category placement.
*   **Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, required): Include your area code for easy contact.
*   **T-Shirt Size** (`T-Shirt Size`, `select_one`, required): Select your preferred t-shirt size for the event.
*   **Dietary Requirements / Allergies** (`Dietary Requirements / Allergies`, `text`, required): List any dietary requirements or allergies for provided lunches and snacks.
*   **Do you require any special assistance or accessibility accommodations?** (`Do you require any special assistance or accessibility accommodations?`, `text`, optional): If you need any special assistance, describe your requirements here.
*   **Previous Competition Experience** (`Previous Competition Experience`, `select_one`, required): Indicate your level of experience with similar events.
*   **Participant Short Bio** (`Participant Short Bio`, `text`, optional): Write a brief 2-3 sentence introduction for the event program.
*   **I confirm that all information provided is accurate** (`I confirm that all information provided is accurate`, `select_one`, required): Select 'I confirm' to agree that the provided information is accurate.
*   **Date of Registration** (`Date of Registration`, `date`, required): Enter today's date for registration completion.
