# Public Policy Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration and attendance information from workshop attendees. It helps workshop organizers track who is attending, their contact details, payment status, and any additional comments or notes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Provide your **Registration Information**, including your name and any relevant details about your attendance.
2. Enter your **Contact Information**, such as your email address and phone number.
3. Share your **Attendance Details**, explaining how you plan to attend the workshop.
4. Provide **Workshop Details**, describing any relevant background or context related to the workshop.
5. Enter **Registration Information**, confirming your registration and workshop participation.
6. Share **Payment Information**, confirming your payment status (if applicable).
7. Add any **Additional Information**, providing any further context or comments about your workshop attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant details about your attendance.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Attendance Details** (`Attendance Details`, `text`, required/optional): Explain how you plan to attend the workshop, including any relevant background or context.
* **Workshop Details** (`Workshop Details`, `text`, required/optional): Describe any relevant background or context related to the workshop.
* **Registration Information** (`Registration Information`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your registration and workshop participation.
* **Payment Information** (`Payment Information`, `text`, required/optional): Confirm your payment status, if applicable.
* **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, `text`, required/optional): Provide any further context or comments about your workshop attendance.

## Tips
Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your registration and attendance information is up-to-date and correctly recorded. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the workshop organizers for assistance.
