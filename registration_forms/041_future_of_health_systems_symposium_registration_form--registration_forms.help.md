# Future Of Health Systems Symposium Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Future Of Health Systems Symposium Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to register for the symposium. This information will be used to manage the symposium registration process and ensure that all attendees have the necessary credentials to participate.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your attendee's name, email address, and any other required information.
2. Select your affiliation, role, and department from the provided options.
3. Select your symposium type, day, and time.
4. Optionally, you can add any additional information about yourself in the bio field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. Attendee Name
**Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, optional): This field is used to collect the name of the individual registering for the symposium. Please enter your name as it appears on your official documentation.

### 2. Email
**Email** (`email`, email, optional): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.

### 3. Affiliation
**Affiliation** (`affiliation`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your institution or organization. Please enter the name of your current or former affiliation.

### 4. Role
**Role** (`role`, text, optional): This field is used to collect information about your job title or position. Please enter a brief description of your role.

### 5. Department
**Department** (`department`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect information about the department you belong to. Please select one of the provided options.

### 6. Bio
**Bio** (`bio`, note, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information about yourself. Please use this field to add any relevant details.

### 7. Symposium Type
**Symposium Type** (`symposium_type`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to collect information about the type of symposium you are attending. Please select all that apply.

### 8. Symposium Day
**Symposium Day** (`symposium_day`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect information about which day of the symposium you plan to attend. Please select one of the provided options.

### 9. Symposium Time
**Symposium Time** (`symposium_time`, select_one, required): This field is used to collect information about whether you will be attending the symposium during the scheduled time. Please select one of the provided options.

### 10. Symposium Room
**Symposium Room** (`symposium_room`, text, optional): This field is used to collect any additional information about which room you will be in during the symposium. Please enter any relevant details.

**Tips**

* Make sure to fill in all required fields (Department, Symposium Type, Symposium Day, and Symposium Time).
* If you select "Yes" for Symposium Day and Symposium Time, please ensure you are available to attend during the scheduled time.
* You can add any additional information in the Bio field if needed.
