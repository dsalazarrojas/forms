# Apprenticeship Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for registration to the Apprenticeship Conference. Attendees can choose from various sessions and activities, provide dietary and accessibility information, and make special requests to ensure a comfortable and productive experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your **Attendee Information**:
	* Enter your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the corresponding fields.
	* Provide a valid **Email Address** to ensure we can contact you.
	* Enter your **Phone Number** for further communication.
	* Enter your **Organization or School** affiliation.
2. Choose your **Attendee Type**:
	* Select the option that best describes your role or involvement with apprenticeships (Apprentice, Instructor, Employer, Administrator, Other).
3. Optionally, you can add **Conference Information**, select **Sessions of Interest**, and provide **Dietary Restrictions**.
4. If necessary, indicate any **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** and **Special Requests** you might have.
5. Review your submission to ensure all required fields are filled out correctly and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
- **Email Address** (Required): A valid email address for communication and further updates.
- **Phone Number** (Required): A phone number for additional communication and follow-up.
- **Organization or School** (Required): Your affiliation with an organization or school.
- **Attendee Type** (Required): Select the option that best describes your role or involvement with apprenticeships.
- **Conference Information** (Optional): Provide any relevant conference information or notes.
- **Conference Dates** (Optional): Enter the dates you plan to attend the conference.
- **Sessions of Interest** (Optional): Choose the sessions you'd like to attend, selecting multiple if applicable.
- **Dietary Restrictions** (Optional): If you have any dietary restrictions, specify them here.
- **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** (Optional): If you need any accommodations, please list them.
- **Special Requests** (Optional): Indicate if you have any special requests, such as equipment rental or translation needs.

## Tips
- Ensure that all **required fields** are filled out correctly for a smooth registration process.
- Choose the **Sessions of Interest** that align with your career goals and interests.
- Be honest about any **Dietary Restrictions** or **Accessibility or Accommodation Needs** to help us provide an inclusive experience.
