# RSVP For Performing Arts Webinar - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVP (Response to Invitation) information from users who are interested in attending the Performing Arts Webinar. Please fill out the form with your details to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Ensure that you select the correct options for each field type.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This field is optional, but we recommend entering a valid email address so we can contact you with updates about the webinar.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number. This could be a local or international number, but please ensure it's correct so we can reach you if needed.
* **Select one option** (`select1`, select_one, required): Choose one of the options provided for your interest in attending the webinar.
* **Select multiple options** (`select2`, select_multiple, required): Choose one or more of the options provided that best describe your interest in attending the webinar.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required): Re-enter your email address to confirm accuracy.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required): Choose the time slot for the webinar you would like to attend.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Choose the date for the webinar you would like to attend.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required): Add any additional notes or comments about your interest in attending the webinar.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
* If you have any special requests or comments, please use the "Note" field.
* If you have any issues or concerns, contact us at [support email or phone number].
