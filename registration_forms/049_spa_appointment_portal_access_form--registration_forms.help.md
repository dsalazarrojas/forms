# spa_appointment_portal_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate registration for spa appointments, allowing users to enter their details and preferences for a smooth and efficient spa experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the **Registration Form** on the first page.
2. On the **customer_info** page, enter your email address in the **email** field.
3. On the **appointment_details** page, select whether you want an appointment **Yes** or **No**.
4. If you've selected **Yes**, choose an appointment time using the **appointment_time** field.
5. On the **select_service** page, choose the service you wish to book from the dropdown menu.
6. On the **payment_method** page, select your preferred payment method from the options: **Cash**, **Card**, or **Bank Transfer**.
7. Enter your **phone** number in the **phone** field.
8. Review your form before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form**: This is the first page of the form, where you'll enter your initial details.
* **customer_info**: Enter your email address to identify yourself as a registered user.
* **appointment_details**: Decide whether you want a spa appointment **Yes** or **No**. If you select **Yes**, you'll be asked to choose an appointment time.
* **appointment_time**: If you've selected **Yes** for an appointment, choose a time slot that suits you best.
* **Select Service**: Choose the type of service you wish to book from the available options.
* **payment_method**: Select your preferred payment method for the appointment.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number for communication and booking purposes.
* **email**: Enter your email address for identification purposes.
* **phone**: Enter your phone number to ensure we can contact you about your appointment.
