# Vocational School Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from students enrolling in our vocational programs. Please fill it out carefully to ensure accurate data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your program name in the "Program Name" field.
2. Enter your student name in the "Student Name" field.
3. Select the date you plan to start your program in the "Program Date" field.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
5. Enter your student email in the "Student Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the vocational program you are enrolling in.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Program Date** (`program_date`, date, required: false): Select the date you plan to start your program. Please use the standard date format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required: false): Enter your contact number where we can reach you for any updates or notifications.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter your valid student email address to receive important updates and notifications.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and comprehensively.
- Use standard date format for "Program Date" field (MM/DD/YYYY).
- Use a valid email address for "Student Email" field.
