# Promotional Email Authorization Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Promotional Email Authorization Form is a mandatory form that allows customers to opt-in or opt-out of promotional emails from our organization. This form is designed to ensure that customers are aware of and consent to receive marketing communications from us.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. **Click on the "Submit" button**: This will start the form and allow you to fill in your information.
2. **Enter your customer consent**: In the "Customer Consent" field, type "yes" to indicate that you consent to receive promotional emails from us.
3. **Enter your email address**: In the "Email Address" field, enter your email address where you would like to receive promotional emails.
4. **Select your preferred email format**: In the "Preferred Email Format" field, choose between HTML or Plain text formats.
5. **Select your marketing preference**: In the "Marketing Permission" field, select all the options that apply to indicate which types of marketing communications you would like to receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Customer Consent
* **Customer Consent (Required, Text)**: In this field, type "yes" to indicate that you consent to receive promotional emails from us.

### Email Address (Optional, Email)
* **Email Address (Optional, Email):** Enter your email address where you would like to receive promotional emails. Make sure to enter it as it appears in your email client (e.g. if your email address is `example@email.com`, enter `example@email.com`, not `example`) .

### Preferred Email Format (Required, Select)
* **Preferred Email Format (Required, Select)**: Choose between HTML and Plain text formats.

### Marketing Permission (Optional, Select Multiple)
* **Marketing Permission (Optional, Select Multiple):** Select all the options that apply. If you select none, you will not receive any promotional emails. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure that you receive promotional emails successfully.
* If you select HTML format, you may receive emails with rich formatting and images.
* If you select Plain text format, you will receive plain text emails without any formatting or images.
