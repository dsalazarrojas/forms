# Sports Club Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Club Enrollment Form is used to register users for a sports club. It is essential to fill out this form accurately to ensure that your enrollment request is processed correctly.

## How To Complete This Form

1. **Please read this guide thoroughly before starting the form** to ensure you understand the purpose and requirements of the form.
2. Ensure you have all the necessary information ready before filling out the form.
3. Enter your details accurately and completely in each field.
4. Review your form before submitting to catch any mistakes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user info`, text, **optional**):
	+ This field is for you to provide a brief description or any relevant details about yourself.
* **Name** (`Name`, text, **optional**):
	+ Enter your full name as per your identification documents.
* **Age** (`Age`, number, **optional**):
	+ Please provide your age as it may be required for certain activities or membership benefits.
* **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`, text, **optional**):
	+ Enter your phone number where you can be contacted for communication or updates.
* **E-mail** (`E-mail`, email, **optional**):
	+ Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Sports Club** (`Sports Club`, select_one, **optional**):
	+ Select the sports club you wish to join.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, select_multiple, **optional**):
	+ Check if you are available for regular events, meetings, or activities.
* **Start Date** (`Start Date`, date, **optional**):
	+ If you have a preferred start date for your enrollment, enter it here.
* **End Date** (`End Date`, date, **optional**):
	+ If you have a preferred end date for your enrollment, enter it here.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, **optional**):
	+ Select your preferred time slot for activities or events.
* **Address** (`Address`, note, **optional**):
	+ Enter your home or work address for communication or delivery purposes.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, **optional**):
	+ Add any additional comments or details about your enrollment request.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and thoroughly.
- If you are unsure about any field, contact us for clarification.
- Review your form carefully before submitting to avoid mistakes.
