# Carbon Reporting Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from attendees for a workshop on Carbon Reporting. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of the form to send your registration details.
2. Fill out each field carefully, following the instructions below for each section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Workshop name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Workshop date**: Enter the date of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Workshop start time**: Enter the start time of the workshop you would like to attend.
* **Workshop location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **First name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email address**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number (optional).
* **Company or organization name**: Enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Job title**: Enter your job title.
* **Industry sector**: Select the industry sector that best describes your company or organization (e.g., Manufacturing, Technology, etc.).
* **Company size**: Select the size of your company or organization (e.g., Small, Medium, Large, etc.).
* **Sustainability role**: Select your role in sustainability (e.g., Sustainability Manager, Environmental Officer, etc.).
* **Carbon reporting experience level**: Select your level of experience with carbon reporting (e.g., Beginner - No Experience, Experienced, etc.).
* **Learning objectives**: Select all the learning objectives that apply to you (e.g., Understand Carbon Reporting Standards, etc.).
* **Reporting framework**: Select the reporting framework your organization uses (e.g., ISO 14064, GHG Protocol, etc.).
* **Attendance type**: Select how you plan to attend the workshop (e.g., In-Person, Virtual, etc.).
* **Dietary preferences**: Enter any dietary preferences you have if you are attending in-person.
* **Workshop fee**: Enter the fee you are willing to pay for the workshop.
* **Additional information**: Enter any additional information or special requests you have.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your details before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us.
- We will review your registration and get back to you soon.
