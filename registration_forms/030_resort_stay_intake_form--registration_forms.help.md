# resort_stay_intake_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect guest information for a resort stay. It includes arrival and departure dates, guest contact details, room preferences, reservation ID, special requests, payment method, email, and additional comments.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully and answer each question truthfully.
- Provide accurate arrival and departure dates to ensure that your room is ready upon check-in and check-out.
- Select your preferred contact title (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.) and enter your contact name.
- Choose the type of room that suits your needs (Single, Double, Suite, or Other).
- Enter your reservation ID number.
- If you have any special requests (e.g., extra towels, early check-in), please enter them here.
- Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card).
- Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Information**: This section is for providing your name and contact details.
* **Arrival Date**: Please enter your expected arrival date in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Departure Date**: Enter your expected departure date in the correct format (DD/MM/YYYY).
* **Guest Contact**: Select your preferred contact title (Mr., Mrs., Ms., Dr.) and enter your name.
* **Room Type**: Choose the type of room that suits your needs (Single, Double, Suite, or Other).
* **Reservation ID**: Enter your reservation ID number.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, please enter them here.
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Credit Card).
* **E-mail**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Additional Comments**: If you have any additional comments or requests, please enter them here.

## Tips
- Please make sure to double-check your answers for accuracy.
- If you are unsure about any field, please ask resort staff for assistance.
- This form is designed to process your reservation smoothly, please be patient and complete the form thoroughly.
- If you have any further questions, please contact the resort directly.
