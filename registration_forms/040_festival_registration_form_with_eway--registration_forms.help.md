# festival_registration_form_with_eway - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a registration form for festivals, which allows users to provide information about their event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the "Event Name" field and enter the name of your festival.
2. Choose the start and end dates of your festival using the "start_date" and "end_date" fields.
3. Enter the venue of your festival in the "venue" field.
4. Select the type of festival (Film, Music, or Food and Wine) in the "event_type" field.
5. Provide your contact information in the "Contact Name", "Contact Email", and "Contact Phone" fields.
6. (Optional) You can add any additional notes about your festival in the "note" field.
7. Provide any additional details about your festival in the "event_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your festival.
- **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required): Choose the start date of your festival.
- **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required): Choose the end date of your festival.
- **Venue** (`venue`, text, required): Enter the venue where your festival will take place.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of festival (Film, Music, or Food and Wine).
- **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, required): Enter your name or the name of the person responsible for the festival.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, required): Enter your email address or the email address of the person responsible for the festival.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number or the phone number of the person responsible for the festival.
- **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Provide any additional notes about your festival.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, optional): Add any additional details about your festival.
