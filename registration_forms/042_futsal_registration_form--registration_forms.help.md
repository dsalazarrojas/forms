# Futsal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect team details for the futsal tournament registration. The information collected will be used to organize and manage the teams participating in the tournament.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out all the required fields marked as "required" on each page.
*   Ensure that each field is completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   Do not leave any fields blank unless explicitly stated as optional.
*   Review your entries for errors before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Team Details (<team_details>)**: This is the main section of the form where you'll enter your team's basic information such as team name, team manager, and other contact details.
* **Tournament Details (<tournament_details>)**: Here, you'll provide information about the tournament your team is registering for, including the tournament name and any specific requirements or rules you need to follow.
* **Team Player Details (<team_player_details>)**: In this section, you'll list each team member's details, including their name, position, and contact information.
* **Team Staff Details (<team_staff_details>)**: Finally, you'll enter the contact details of your team's staff members, including their names and contact information.

## Tips

*   Make sure to double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting the form.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please contact the tournament organizers for clarification.
*   Ensure all team members and staff have reviewed and agreed on the information provided.
*   Complete the form in one sitting to avoid errors and inaccuracies.
