# easter_basket_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a child for an Easter basket. It collects information about the child and their school and parent's details.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out all required fields.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and complete information.
3. Review and submit the form when finished.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Easter basket registration form** (`easter_basket_registration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form.
* **School ID** (`school_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of your child's school.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your child's school.
* **Non-profit Organization ID** (`non_profit_organization_id`, number, required/optional): Enter the ID of your non-profit organization.
* **Non-profit Organization Name** (`non_profit_organization_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your non-profit organization.
* **School** (`school`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your child is in school.
* **Non-profit Organization** (`non_profit_organization`, select_multiple, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if the school has a non-profit organization.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Birth Date** (`birth_date`, date, required): Enter your child's birth date.
* **Birth Year** (`birth_year`, number, required): Enter your child's birth year.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, required): Enter your email address (parent).
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, required): Enter your parent's phone number.
* **Child Name** (`child_name`, text, required): Enter your child's name.
* **Child DOB** (`child_dob`, date, required): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Student ID** (`student_id`, text, required): Enter your child's ID.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your child's name.
* **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your child is in school.
* **Student Gender** (`student_gender`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate your child's gender.
* **Student Birth Date** (`student_birth_date`, date, required/optional): Enter your child's birth date.
* **Student Birth Year** (`student_birth_year`, number, required/optional): Enter your child's birth year.
* **Student Parent Permission** (`student_parent_permission`, text, required/optional): Enter your child's parent's permission.
* **Submission Date** (`submission`, date, required/optional): This field is not used in this form.

Note: The fields marked as required/optional will be required to be filled out unless otherwise specified.
