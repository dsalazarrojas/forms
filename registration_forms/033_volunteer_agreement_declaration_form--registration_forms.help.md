# Volunteer Agreement Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is a declaration form for volunteers, designed to gather necessary information for volunteer management and coordination. It is used to collect contact details, volunteer-specific information, and a declaration of agreement for volunteering with an organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Provide your basic contact information** (`Volunteer Information` page)
	* Fill out the required fields with your name, email, and phone number.
2. **Share your volunteering details** (`Volunteer Details` page)
	* Enter accurate information about your volunteering role, dates, and any specific skills or qualifications you possess.
3. **Carefully review the agreement** (`Agreement` page)
	* Read the declaration carefully and ensure you understand the terms and conditions of volunteering with the organization.
4. **Sign the declaration** (`Signature` page)
	* Type your full name in the signature field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### **Volunteer Information** (Required)
* **Name**: Your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Your primary email address for communication with the organization.
* **Phone**: Your contact phone number for easy communication.

### **Volunteer Details** (Required)
* **Role**: Briefly describe your volunteering role (e.g., event volunteer, committee member, etc.).
* **Dates**: Specify the dates you are available to volunteer.
* **Skills/Qualifications**: If applicable, list any special skills or qualifications you bring to the role (e.g., language proficiency, first aid certification, etc.).

### **Agreement** (Required)
* **Please review the agreement carefully**: This is an essential step to understand your responsibilities and obligations as a volunteer.

### **Signature** (Required)
* **Type your full name**: Sign the declaration by typing your full name.
