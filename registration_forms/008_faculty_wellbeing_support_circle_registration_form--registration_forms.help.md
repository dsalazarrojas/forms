# Faculty Wellbeing Support Circle Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a wellbeing support circle, which is a group of faculty members who share similar interests and concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*), with your information.
2. Choose your faculty support circle interest from the options provided.
3. Select your faculty support circle lead from the options provided.
4. Add any comments or additional information about your wellbeing support circle interests.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (\*)** (text): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name (\*)** (text): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (email): This is your work email address.
* **Phone** (text): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Department** (text): Please enter your department or school where you work.
* **Role** (text): Please enter your role or title.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Interest** (text): Briefly describe your wellbeing support circle interest.
* **Faculty Member** (\*)** (select multiple): Choose your faculty member affiliation (University of Alberta, University of Calgary, University of Lethbridge, or other).
* **Comments** (note): Add any comments or notes about your wellbeing support circle interest.
* **Faculty Member Email** (email): This is your faculty member email address.
* **Faculty Member Phone** (text): Please enter your faculty member phone number.
* **Faculty Member Date of Birth** (date): Please enter your faculty member date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Faculty Member Department** (text): Please enter your faculty member department or school.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas** (select one): Choose whether you are a wellbeing support circle lead (True, False, or unsure).
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Name** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your name.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Email** (email): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your email address.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Phone** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your phone number.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Date of Birth** (date): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Department** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your department or school.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Role** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your role or title.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Interest** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please enter your wellbeing support circle interest.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Comments** (note): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please add any comments or notes.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Additional Info** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please add any additional information.
* **Wellbeing Support Circle Leas Additional Info 2** (text): If you are a wellbeing support circle lead, please add any additional information 2.
