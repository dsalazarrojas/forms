# e_sports_tournament_payment_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information for the payment of e-sports tournament participants and teams.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant information section with the required details about the participant.
2. In the team section, list all team members and select the team leaders.
3. Choose the payment method and fill in the participant and team fees.
4. Enter the tournament date and time.
5. Fill in the registration details such as registration date and time.
6. Add the email, phone number, and any additional information of the participant and team.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Team name**
**Team name** (text): This is the name of the team participating in the e-sports tournament. Enter the team's name accurately.

### 2. **Team members**
**Team members** (select_multiple): List all the team members participating in the e-sports tournament. You can select multiple team members from the provided options.

### 3. **Team leaders**
**Team leaders** (select_one): Select the team leaders from the provided options.

### 4. **Tournament date**
**Tournament date** (date): Enter the date of the tournament.

### 5. **Tournament time**
**Tournament time** (time): Enter the time of the tournament.

### 6. **Payment method**
**Payment method** (select_one): Choose the payment method for the participant and team fees.

### 7. **Participant fee**
**Participant fee** (number): Enter the participant fee accurately. It must be a decimal number.

### 8. **Total participant fee**
**Total participant fee** (number): Enter the total participant fee accurately. It must be a decimal number.

### 9. **Team fee**
**Team fee** (number): Enter the team fee accurately. It must be a decimal number.

### 10. **Total team fee**
**Total team fee** (number): Enter the total team fee accurately. It must be a decimal number.

### 11. **Additional info**
**Additional info** (text): Add any additional information about the participant or team if necessary.

### 12. **Registration date**
**Registration date** (date): Enter the date of registration.

### 13. **Registration time**
**Registration time** (time): Enter the time of registration.

### 14. **Email**
**Email** (email): Enter the email of the participant or team.

### 15. **Phone**
**Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the participant or team.

### 16. **Player name**
**Player name** (text): Enter the name of the player participating in the e-sports tournament.

### 17. **Player email**
**Player email** (email): Enter the email of the player participating in the e-sports tournament.

### 18. **Player phone**
**Player phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the player participating in the e-sports tournament.

### 19. **Player info**
**Player info** (text): Add any additional information about the player participating in the e-sports tournament.

### 20. **Team info**
**Team info** (text): Add any additional information about the team participating in the e-sports tournament.

### 21. **Player fee notes**
**Player fee notes** (text): Add any notes about the player fee.

### 22. **Team fee notes**
**Team fee notes** (text): Add any notes about the team fee.

### 23. **Notes**
**Notes** (text): Add any additional notes about the participant or team.
