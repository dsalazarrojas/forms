# webinar_lecture_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The webinar_lecture_registration_form is designed to register guests for a webinar or lecture event. This form collects speaker and guest information, event details, and registration status.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) or marked as "required" in the field description.
* Provide accurate and complete information for each field.
* Review the form before submitting to ensure all fields are filled in correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Webinar Title** (`webinar_title`, text, required: false): Enter the title of the webinar or lecture.
* **Speaker Name** (`speaker_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the speaker or presenter.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date of the webinar or lecture.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time of the webinar or lecture.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location of the webinar or lecture.
* **Speaker Email** (`speaker_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the speaker or presenter.
* **Speaker Phone** (`speaker_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the speaker or presenter.
* **Guest Name** (`guest_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the guest or attendee.
* **Guest Email** (`guest_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the guest or attendee.
* **Fee** (`fee`, number, required: false): Enter the fee associated with the webinar or lecture.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, select_one, required: false): Select the registration type for the guest or attendee.
* **Option A** (`registration_type_option_a`, select_one, required: false): Select the option A for the registration type.
* **Option B** (`registration_type_option_b`, select_one, required: false): Select the option B for the registration type.
* **Option C** (`registration_type_option_c`, select_one, required: false): Select the option C for the registration type.
* **Register** (`submit`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the registration options for the guest or attendee.
* **Option A** (`submit_option_a`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the option A for the registration options.
* **Option B** (`submit_option_b`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the option B for the registration options.

Note: Please ensure that you provide accurate and complete information for each field to ensure proper registration and tracking.
