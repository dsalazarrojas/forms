# Workshop Participation Verification Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to verify and document a participant's experience in a workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the title of the workshop you participated in from the "workshop participation verification form" field.
2. Fill out your name and email address in the "participant name" and "contact email" fields, respectively.
3. If you attended the workshop, select "Yes" in the "participant attendance" field.
4. Enter the date you attended the workshop in the "workshop attended date" field, if applicable.
5. Provide any additional comments in the "comments" and "additional comments" fields, if applicable.
6. Select the reason for your attendance in the "participant attendance reason" field.
7. Provide feedback on your experience in the "comments", "participant feedback", "participant satisfaction", and "participant survey" fields.
8. Rate your experience in the "participant rating" field.
9. Select the location where you took the survey in the "participant survey location" field.

* **contact email**: Enter your email address.

* **workshop attended date**: Enter the date you attended the workshop.
* **comments**: Enter any additional comments.
* **additional comments**: Select any additional comments.
* **participant attendance**: Select "Yes" if you attended the workshop.
* **participant attendance confirmed**: Select "Yes" if your attendance has been confirmed by the workshop organizers.
* **participant attendance reason**: Select the reason for your attendance.
* **comments**: Select any additional comments.
* **participant feedback**: Select any feedback.
* **participant satisfaction**: Select your satisfaction with the workshop.
* **feedback reason**: Enter any additional feedback.
* **participant satisfaction reason**: Enter any additional feedback.

* **participant survey**: Select "Yes" if you took the survey.
