# Employee Satisfaction Survey Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from attendees who are interested in registering for the Employee Satisfaction Survey Workshop. This information will be used to plan and execute the workshop efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure that your preferences are considered during the workshop planning process.
2. Make sure to register with your correct information, as duplicate registrations will not be allowed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details** (1, text, required): Please enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details to help us contact you.
* **Attendee Details** (2, text, required): Enter your attendee's name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **Preferred Dates** (3, select_multiple, required): Select all the dates you are available to attend the workshop. You can choose multiple dates if you are available on different dates.
* **Dietary Requirements** (4, select_one, required): Please indicate if you have any dietary requirements.
* **Discussion Topics** (5, select_multiple, required): Select the discussion topics that interest you the most.
* **Date** (6, date, required): Enter the date you are available to attend the workshop.
* **Time** (7, time, required): Enter the time you are available to attend the workshop.
* **Notes** (8, note, optional): Add any additional notes that you would like to share with us.
* **Comments** (9, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the workshop.
* **Preferred Location** (10, text, optional): Enter your preferred location for the workshop.
* **Preferred Time Zone** (11, select_one, required): Please indicate your preferred time zone.
* **Comments for Speaker** (12, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the speaker.
* **Preferred Format** (13, select_one, optional): Select your preferred format for the workshop.
* **Preferred Presentation Type** (14, select_one, optional): Select your preferred presentation type.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (15, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you have.
* **Preferred Language** (16, select_one, optional): Select your preferred language.
* **Preferred Cuisine** (17, select_one, optional): Select your preferred cuisine.
* **Comments for Food** (18, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the food.
* **Other Notes** (19, text, optional): Enter any other notes or feedback about the workshop.
* **Preferred Food** (20, text, optional): Enter your preferred food.
* **Comments for Travel** (21, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about travel arrangements.
* **Preferred Travel Arrangement** (22, select_one, optional): Select your preferred travel arrangement.
* **Comments for Speaker's Diet** (23, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about the speaker's diet.
* **Comments for Travel** (24, text, optional): Add any comments or feedback about travel arrangements.
* **Comments** (25, text, optional): Add any final comments or feedback about the workshop.
