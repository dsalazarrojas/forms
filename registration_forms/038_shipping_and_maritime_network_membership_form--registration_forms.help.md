# Shipping And Maritime Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to join the Shipping and Maritime Network. It helps us understand your background and qualifications for membership.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Choose the category of Shipping and Maritime Network, Membership, or Application, depending on your affiliation with the network.
2. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Enter your company name in the **Company** field.
6. Select your job title in the **Job Title** field.
7. Choose your role in the network from the options: **Member**, **Subscriber**, or **Partner**.
8. Write a brief bio in the **Bio** field.
9. Read and agree with the terms and conditions in the **Terms and Conditions** field.
10. Review and agree with the **Privacy Policy** field.
11. Finally, click the **Submit** button to submit your membership application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Category**: Choose the category that best describes your affiliation with the Shipping and Maritime Network. Options are Shipping and Maritime Network, Membership, and Application.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents or business cards.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address where you can be reached.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number with the country code (e.g., +1 for the United States).
* **Company**: Enter the name of your company, if applicable.
* **Job Title**: Enter your professional title or position in the company.
* **Role**: Choose your role in the network from Member, Subscriber, or Partner. If you're unsure, ask the network administrators for clarification.
* **Bio**: Write a brief introduction about yourself, your background, and why you want to join the Shipping and Maritime Network.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and agree to the terms and conditions of membership, which include responsibilities and expectations for members.
* **Privacy Policy**: Review and agree to the shipping and maritime network's privacy policy, which governs data collection and usage.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your membership application for review.
* **I confirm that I am a maritime professional and wish to join the Shipping and Maritime Network.** : This is a checkbox to confirm your eligibility and intention to join the network.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Read and review the terms and conditions of membership.
* **Privacy Policy**: Review and agree to the shipping and maritime network's privacy policy.
