# Reusable Container Pilot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about reusable containers for registration and tracking purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Select the first page to start entering your data.
2.  On the first page, fill in your name.
3.  On subsequent pages, fill in the requested information about the reusable container.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Name** (text, Required/Optional): Enter your name.
*   **Email** (email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address.
*   **Phone** (text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number.
*   **Organization Name** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of your organization.
*   **Organization Type** (select one, Required/Optional): Select the type of your organization.
*   **Address** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the address of your organization.
*   **City** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the city of your organization.
*   **State** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the state of your organization.
*   **Zip** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the zip code of your organization.
*   **Description** (text, Required/Optional): Enter a brief description of the reusable container.
*   **Start Date** (date, Required/Optional): Enter the start date of the pilot.
*   **End Date** (date, Required/Optional): Enter the end date of the pilot.
*   **Pilot Type** (select one, Required/Optional): Select the type of pilot.
*   **Pilot Status** (select one, Required/Optional): Select the status of the pilot.
*   **Submitted By** (select multiple, Required/Optional): Select the person who submitted the form.
*   **Comments** (note, Required/Optional): Enter any comments about the pilot.
*   **Confirm Start Date** (date, Required/Optional): Enter the confirm start date of the pilot.
*   **Confirm End Date** (date, Required/Optional): Enter the confirm end date of the pilot.
*   **Confirm Status** (select one, Required/Optional): Select the confirm status of the pilot.
*   **Comments Status** (select one, Required/Optional): Select the status of comments.
*   **Assigned Tool** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the assigned tool.
*   **Form Version** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the version of the form.
*   **Form ID** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the ID of the form.
*   **Form Category** (text, Required/Optional): Enter the category of the form.

Note: This form's purpose is for internal tracking and management of reusable containers. Please ensure that you have all necessary information before completing the form.
