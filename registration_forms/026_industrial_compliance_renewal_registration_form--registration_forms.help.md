# Industrial Compliance Renewal Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Industrial Compliance Renewal process. It collects and verifies information related to the company, site details, contact details, and renewal specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the company details section (fields 1-3) with your company's information.
2. Provide site details (fields 4-5) as required.
3. Enter your contact details (fields 6-7) to enable us to contact you for any clarifications or verification.
4. Choose the correct renewal details (fields 8) for your Industrial Compliance Renewal.
5. Select the correct site visit frequency (fields 9-22) for your site visits.
6. Enter the date and time of visit (fields 23-24) for each site visit frequency selected.
