# Plant Operations Leadership Forum Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for the Plant Operations Leadership Forum. This will help us connect you with the right people, resources, and facilitators to support your interests and needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To register for the forum, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Provide your email address in the **Email** field. Please ensure it's a valid email address.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your session interests from the list provided in the **Session Interests** field. You can choose one or multiple options.
6. Select any accessibility needs you may have from the list provided in the **Accessibility Needs** field. You can choose one or multiple options.
7. If you have any additional information or comments, please use the **Additional Notes** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your personal name, not a company or organization name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): This field is for your surname, not a company or organization name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Please enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for future contact.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the topics you're interested in from the provided options. This will help us connect you with the right people and resources.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, required: false): If you require any accommodations or support, please select from the options provided.
* **Additional Notes** (`notes`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information you want to share with us.
