# Change Champions Info Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather necessary information from interested individuals to tailor the Change Champions Info Session content and facilitate their experience. The data collected will be used for session planning and participant management.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the preferred session date and time from the provided options.
3. Indicate your interest level in becoming a Change Champion.
4. Confirm your previous experience with change initiatives.
5. Provide any necessary manager approval status and details.
6. Specify any dietary or accessibility requirements.
7. Confirm your understanding of the time commitment involved.
8. Choose your preferred method for receiving reminders.
9. Sign your name as a confirmation of your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`): Enter the date you are registering for the info session.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`): Enter your full name.
* **Email Address** (`participant_email`): Enter your official email address for confirmation and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`participant_phone`): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`): Enter your current job title.
* **Department** (`department`): Enter your department or division.
* **Office Location** (`location`): Enter your work location.
* **Manager Name** (`manager_name`): Enter the name of your direct manager (optional).
* **Session Selection** (`session_selection`): Select which info session you will attend.
* **Preferred Session Date** (`session_date`): Select your preferred session date.
* **Change Champion Interest** (`change_champion_interest`): Indicate your level of interest in becoming a Change Champion.
* **Previous Change Experience** (`previous_change_experience`): Confirm your experience with change initiatives.
* **Motivation Statement** (`motivation_statement`): Provide a brief explanation of why you are interested in this role (optional).
* **Relevant Skills** (`relevant_skills`): Select any skills you bring to the role (optional).
* **Time Commitment Understanding** (`time_commitment`): Confirm your understanding of the time commitment involved.
* **Manager Approval Status** (`manager_approval`): Confirm your manager's approval status (if applicable).
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`): Specify any dietary needs for catering (optional).
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`): Specify any accessibility needs you have (optional).
* **Questions for Session** (`questions_for_session`): Ask any questions you have about the session (optional).
* **Reminder Preference** (`reminder_preference`): Choose your preferred method for receiving reminders.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Sign your name as confirmation of your registration.

Note: Asterisks (`*`) indicate required fields.
