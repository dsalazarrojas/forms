# research_proposal_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and submit research proposals for review.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Enter your institution's name in the **Institution Name** field.
2. Provide a clear and concise title for your research proposal in the **Research Title** field.
3. Select the relevant category for your research from the **Research Category** field.
4. Write a detailed description of your project in the **Project Description** field.
5. Enter the name of the research lead or PI in the **Research Lead** field.
6. Enter the start date of your research project in the **Research Start Date** field.
7. Enter the end date of your research project in the **Research End Date** field.
8. Enter the location of your research project in the **Research Location** field.
9. Provide an estimate of your research budget in the **Research Budget** field.
10. Specify the funding sources for your research in the **Research Funding** field.
11. Select the current status of your proposal in the **Research Status** field.
12. Choose the type of institution affiliated with your research in the **Institution Type** field.
13. Enter your contact email address in the **Contact Email** field.
14. Enter your contact phone number in the **Contact Phone** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Institution Name**: Enter the name of your institution, as it will be associated with your proposal.
* **Research Title**: A clear and concise title for your research proposal, which should reflect its main focus.
* **Research Category**: Choose the category that best aligns with your research project's focus, from the options provided.
* **Project Description**: A detailed description of your research project, including its objectives, methods, and expected outcomes.
* **Research Lead**: The name of the researcher or PI who will be leading the project.
* **Research Start Date**: Enter the start date of your research project, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Research End Date**: Enter the expected end date of your research project, in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Research Location**: The location where your research project will take place.
* **Research Budget**: An estimate of your research budget, including any relevant costs and funding sources.
* **Research Funding**: Specify the sources of funding for your research project.
* **Research Status**: Select the current status of your proposal, from the options provided.
* **Institution Type**: Choose the type of institution affiliated with your research, from the options provided.
* **Contact Email**: Enter your email address, which will be used for communication and correspondence.
* **Contact Phone**: Enter your phone number, which will be used for communication and correspondence.

## Tips
* Ensure that your institution and proposal details align with the options provided.
* Double-check the accuracy of your submission before submitting.
* Be prepared to provide additional information or clarification if required during the review process.
