# Lash Industry Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Lash Industry Certification Registration Form is used to register an individual or organization for a certification program in the lash industry. This form helps to gather necessary information for the certification process, ensuring that applicants have a smooth and efficient experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Select the correct application type from the list.
3. Provide a brief training history in the note field.
4. Select any relevant training information from the dropdown list.
5. Choose the current enrollment status.
6. Enter the date of previous training, if applicable.
7. Enter date of birth.
8. Fill in the email address and phone number.
9. Provide the address for mailings.
10. Enter the name and graduation date of previous schools.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info**: This section is used to enter your user information. Please fill in your name and any other relevant details.
	+ **Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your documents.
* **Application Type**: Select the type of application you are submitting for. Choose from:
	+ Lash Technician: For those applying for a lash technician certification.
	+ Lash Certification: For those applying for a lash certification.
	+ Lash Trainer: For those applying for a lash trainer certification.
* **Training History**: Provide a brief description of your training history. This is a note field, so you can enter as much or as little information as you need.
* **Training Info**: Select any relevant training information from the list:
	+ Lash Technician: For those who have completed lash technician training.
	+ Lash Certification: For those who have completed lash certification training.
	+ Lash Trainer: For those who have completed lash trainer training.
* **Enrollment Status**: Choose the current status of your enrollment:
	+ Applied: You have submitted an application but have not yet been accepted or rejected.
	+ Accepted: Your application has been accepted.
	+ Rejected: Your application has been rejected.
	+ Pending: Your application is still being reviewed.
* **Previous Training**: If you have previously completed training, enter the date of that training.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email**: Enter your email address for contact and updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact and updates.
* **Address**: Enter your mailing address for any necessary mailings.
* **Previous School Name**: If you have previously attended school, enter the name of that school.
* **Previous Graduation Date**: If you have graduated from a previous school, enter the date of your graduation.

Tips:

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for clarification.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
