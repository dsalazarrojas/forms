# Lunch Trade Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a lunch trade event. It collects information about the participant, including their name, contact details, availability, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled in before submitting.
2. Enter your participant name in the "Participant Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred schedule from the list provided in the "Schedule" field.
6. Select your desired start time for the event in the "Start Time" field.
7. Choose your desired end time for the event in the "End Time" field.
8. Enter any additional notes or comments in the "Notes" field.
9. Select the tool you would like to use for the event in the "Assigned Tool" field.
10. Indicate the number of participants you will be bringing in the "Participant Count" field.
11. Confirm your availability by selecting the correct option in the "Confirmed" field.
11. If necessary, provide additional notes on your confirmed status in the "Notes Confirmed" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
• Participant Name: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
### Email
• Email: Enter your valid email address.
### Phone
• Phone: Enter your phone number.
### Schedule
• Schedule: Choose a day you are available to participate in the event.
### Start Time
• Start Time: Select the start time for your event session.
### End Time
• End Time: Select the end time for your event session.
### Notes
• Notes: Provide any additional comments or information about your participation.
### Assigned Tool
• Assigned Tool: Choose the tool you prefer to use for the event.
### Participant Count
• Participant Count: Select the number of participants you will be bringing.
### Confirmed
• Confirmed: Select 'True' if you are confirmed to participate, 'False' otherwise.
### Notes Confirmed
• Notes Confirmed: Provide any additional comments or information about your confirmation status.
