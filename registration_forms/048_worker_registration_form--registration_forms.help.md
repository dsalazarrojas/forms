# Worker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Worker Registration Form is used to register workers and collect their information for administrative and management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Ensure that the worker you are registering is not already in the system.
2.  Verify that the worker's information is up-to-date and accurate.
3.  Complete the sections of the form as follows:
    *   Enter the worker's employer address and name in the first section.
    *   Enter the worker's employee address and name in the second section.
    *   Select the worker's job title, supervisor, and job status in the third section.
    *   Choose the worker's employment type and enter any relevant additional information.
    *   Enter the worker's start and end dates of employment, as well as the duration of employment.
    *   Enter any other relevant information, such as the worker's supervisor name, phone number, and email.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Employer Address**: Enter the address of the worker's employer.
*   **Employer Name**: Enter the name of the worker's employer.
*   **Employee Address**: Enter the address of the worker who is registering.
*   **Employee Name**: Enter the name of the worker who is registering.
*   **Job Title**: Select the title of the worker's job.
*   **Supervisor**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the worker has a supervisor.
*   **Job Status**: Select one or more of the following options: 'Active', 'Inactive'.
*   **Job Level**: Select one or more of the following options: 'High', 'Low'.
*   **Employment Type**: Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate whether the worker is employed.
*   **Start Date**: Enter the date the worker started their employment.
*   **Job Title Description**: Enter any additional information about the worker's job title.
*   **Manager Name**: Enter the name of the worker's manager.
*   **Team**: Enter the team the worker is a part of.
*   **Job Category**: Enter the category of the worker's job.
*   **Employment Start Date**: Enter the date the worker started their employment.
*   **Employment End Date**: Enter the date the worker ended their employment.
*   **Employment Duration**: Enter the duration of the worker's employment.
*   **Supervisor Name**: Enter the name of the worker's supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Phone**: Enter the phone number of the worker's supervisor.
*   **Supervisor Email**: Enter the email of the worker's supervisor.
*   **Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the worker's manager.
*   **Job Status Note**: Enter any additional information about the worker's job status.
*   **Employment Type Note**: Enter any additional information about the worker's employment type.
*   **Job Level Note**: Enter any additional information about the worker's job level.

## Tips

*   Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
*   Ensure that the worker's information is not duplicated in the system.
*   Review the form carefully before submitting it.
*   If you need help, contact the system administrator.
