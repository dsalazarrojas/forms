# Community Health Fair RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Health Fair RSVP Form is designed to collect information from attendees to provide personalized care and services at the fair.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your legal first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your legal last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field, which will be used for event confirmation.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field, which will be used as your primary contact number.
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter your home address in the "Street Address" field.
7. Enter your city, state, and ZIP code in the "City, State, ZIP" field.
8. Select the date you plan to attend the health fair in the "Health Fair Date" field.
9. Enter the number of people attending with you, including yourself, in the "Number in Your Group" field.
10. Select the health services you are interested in from the "Health Services of Interest" list.
11. Indicate your insurance status in the "Insurance Status" field.
12. Confirm whether you have a primary care provider in the "Do You Have a Primary Care Provider" field.
13. Enter any primary health concerns you have in the "Primary Health Concerns" field.
14. Indicate if you require any accessibility accommodations in the "Accessibility Needs" field.
15. Confirm if you need an interpreter or language assistance in the "Interpreter Needed" field, and if so, select the required language.
16. Confirm if you need transportation assistance in the "Transportation Assistance Needed" field.
17. Indicate how you heard about the health fair in the "How Did You Hear About Health Fair" field.
18. Confirm if you are okay with being contacted for follow-up health resources in the "May We Contact You for Follow-Up" field.
19. Enter any additional comments or information in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. First Name
* First Name (`attendee_first_name`, text, required): Enter your legal first name.
### 2. Last Name
* Last Name (`attendee_last_name`, text, required): Enter your legal last name.
### 3. Email Address
* Email Address (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your email address, which will be used for event confirmation.
### 4. Phone Number
* Phone Number (`phone_number`, text, required): Enter your primary contact number.
### 5. Date of Birth
* Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
### 6. Street Address
* Street Address (`address`, text, required): Enter your home address.
### 7. City, State, ZIP
* City, State, ZIP (`city_state_zip`, text, required): Enter your city, state, and ZIP code.
### 8. Health Fair Date
* Health Fair Date (`health_fair_date`, date, required): Select the date you plan to attend the health fair.
### 9. Number in Your Group
* Number in Your Group (`group_size`, number, required): Enter the total number of attendees, including yourself.
### 10. Health Services of Interest
* Health Services of Interest (`services_interested`, select_multiple, required): Select the health services you are interested in.
### 11. Insurance Status
* Insurance Status (`insurance_status`, select_one, required): Indicate your insurance status.
### 12. Do You Have a Primary Care Provider
* Do You Have a Primary Care Provider (`primary_care_provider`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you have a primary care provider.
### 13. Primary Health Concerns
* Primary Health Concerns (`health_concerns`, text, optional): Enter any primary health concerns you have.
### 14. Accessibility Needs
* Accessibility Needs (`accessibility_needs`, text, optional): Indicate if you require any accessibility accommodations.
### 15. Interpreter Needed
* Interpreter Needed (`interpreter_needed`, select_one, required): Confirm if you need an interpreter or language assistance.
### 16. Language Needed
* Language Needed (`interpreter_language`, text, optional): If you need an interpreter, select the required language.
### 17. Transportation Assistance Needed
* Transportation Assistance Needed (`transportation_needed`, select_one, required): Confirm if you need transportation assistance.
### 18. How Did You Hear About Health Fair
* How Did You Hear About Health Fair (`referral_source`, select_one, optional): Indicate how you heard about the health fair.
### 19. May We Contact You for Follow-Up
* May We Contact You for Follow-Up (`follow_up_contact`, select_one, required): Confirm if you are okay with being contacted for follow-up health resources.
### 20. Additional Comments
* Additional Comments (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Ensure accuracy and consistency in filling out the form to prevent delays in processing your registration.
* If you need assistance with filling out the form, do not hesitate to ask for help.
* Review your answers carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
