# Parental Consent And Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Parental Consent And Waiver Form is used to collect information from parents or guardians about their child's activity participation and consent. This form helps us understand and record the necessary details for the child's engagement in activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
- Carefully read each section and provide accurate information for your child.
- Ensure you understand the purpose and requirements for each field.
- Enter your child's name in the "Parent Name" field (Page 2).
- Provide your contact information in the "Contact Information" field (Page 3).
- Enter any emergency contact information in the "Emergency Contact" field (Page 4).
- Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Parent Signature" field (Page 5), indicating your consent for your child's activity participation.
- If signed, enter the date in the "Date Signed" field (Page 6).
- Provide the date of the activity in the "Date of Activity" field (Page 7).
- Enter the location of the activity in the "Activity Location" field (Page 8).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Parent Name (Page 2)** (`parent_name`, Text, Not required): Please enter your child's name.
* **Contact Information (Page 3)** (`contact_information`, Text, Not required): Provide any contact information you'd like to share with us.
* **Emergency Contact (Page 4)** (`emergency_contact`, Text, Not required): Enter any emergency contact information for your child.
* **Parent Signature (Page 5)** (`parent_signature`, Select One, Not required): Confirm your consent by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Date Signed (Page 6)** (`date_signed`, Date, Not required): If you've confirmed your signature, enter the date you signed this form.
* **Date of Activity (Page 7)** (`activity_participation_date`, Date, Not required): Provide the date of the activity your child participated in.
* **Activity Location (Page 8)** (`activity_location`, Text, Not required): Enter the location where the activity took place.
