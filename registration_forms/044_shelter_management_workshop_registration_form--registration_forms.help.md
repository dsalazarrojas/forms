# shelter_management_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a shelter management workshop. It gathers essential information to facilitate planning and organization of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the **Participant Details** page, ensuring you provide your name and other required information.
* On the **Session Preferences** page, select one or multiple sessions you would like to attend (depending on the type of question asked). Please ensure you choose at least one session, as this is a required field.
* On the **Logistics** page, you can provide any additional information related to your participation, if necessary.
* On the **Submit** page, review your entries and ensure everything is accurate. This page will be used to finalize your registration.
* Provide your **Email Address** and **Phone Number**, as these are required fields for communication purposes.
* On the **Session Preferences** page, select one session you would like to join (this is a one-session option).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details (participant_details)**: 
  * **Name**: Please enter your name as you would like to be addressed in the workshop.
  * **Session Preferences (session_preferences)**: Choose one or more sessions you would like to attend to maximize your experience.
  * **Logistics (logistics)**: Provide any additional details you think might be helpful for organizers to know.
  * **Submit (submit)**: Review your entries to ensure accuracy.
  * **Email Address (email_address)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about the workshop.
  * **Phone Number (phone_number)**: Provide your phone number for further communication.
  * **Session Preferences (session_preferences_2)**: Please select one session you would like to join.
  * **Session Preferences (session_preferences_3)**: Select multiple sessions you would like to attend.
  * **Session Preferences (session_preferences_3)**: This field is similar to session_preferences but seems to be an error in the schema; you should choose one session from the options.
