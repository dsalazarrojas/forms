# Neighborhood Open House RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to RSVP to the neighborhood open house event. Please fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that your attendance is recorded correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First name and Last name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the date of the event from the calendar in the Event Date field.
5. Choose the event time from the time picker in the Event Time field.
6. Enter the number of guests you plan to bring with you in the Number of Guests field.
7. If you have any comments or special requests, you can add them in the Comments field (optional).
8. Finally, select your RSVP option from the dropdown menu in the Will Attend field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First name (Text, Required)
* This field is for your first name. Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Last name (Text, Required)
* This field is for your last name. Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Email (Email, Required)
* This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address where you can be reached.

### Phone (Text, Required)
* This field is for your phone number. Please enter your phone number where you can be reached.

### Event Date (Date, Required)
* This field is for the date of the event. Please select the date from the calendar.

### Event Time (Time, Required)
* This field is for the event time. Please select the time from the time picker.

### Number of Guests (Number, Required)
* This field is for the number of guests you plan to bring with you. Please enter the correct number.

### Comments (Note, Optional)
* This field is for any comments or special requests you may have. If you have any additional information to share, you can add it here.

### Will Attend (Select One, Required)
* This field is for confirming your attendance. Please select one of the options to confirm whether you will attend or not.
