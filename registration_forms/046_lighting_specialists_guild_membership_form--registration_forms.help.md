# Lighting Specialists Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information for registration with the Lighting Specialists Guild. It is intended for potential members who want to join the guild.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out your personal details: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, Date of Birth.
2. Select your Membership Type (e.g., Regular, Premium, Honorary).
3. Indicate your Certification Level (e.g., Certified, Non-Certified, None).
4. Describe your Work Experience.
5. Choose your relevant Skills (e.g., Electrical, Mechanical, Installation).
6. Select the Professional Services you offer (e.g., Design, Installation, Testing).
7. Choose your Membership Status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
8. Select your Payment Method (e.g., Online, Offline, PayPal).
9. Indicate your Payment Status (e.g., Paid, Unpaid).
10. Enter your Certification Number (if applicable).
11. Enter your Payment Date (if applicable).
12. Describe your Payment Method Details (if applicable).
13. Describe your Payment Status Details (if applicable).
14. Enter your Work Address.
15. Enter your Work City.
16. Enter your Work State.
17. Enter your Work Postal Code.
18. Enter your Work Phone.
19. Enter your Work Email.
20. Enter the name of your Emergency Contact.
21. Enter the phone number of your Emergency Contact.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, Required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`5`, `date`, Required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Membership Type** (`6`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Select your type of membership (e.g., Regular, Premium, Honorary).
* **Certification Level** (`7`, `select_one`, Optional): Indicate your certification level (e.g., Certified, Non-Certified, None).
* **Work Experience** (`8`, `note`, Optional): Describe your work experience.
* **Skills** (`9`, `select_multiple`, Optional): Choose your relevant skills (e.g., Electrical, Mechanical, Installation).
* **Professional Services** (`10`, `select_one`, Optional): Select the professional services you offer (e.g., Design, Installation, Testing).
* **Membership Status** (`11`, `select_one`, Optional): Choose your membership status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending).
* **Payment Method** (`12`, `select_one`, Optional): Select your payment method (e.g., Online, Offline, PayPal).
* **Payment Status** (`13`, `select_one`, Optional): Indicate your payment status (e.g., Paid, Unpaid).
* **Certification Number** (`14`, `text`, Optional): Enter your certification number (if applicable).
* **Payment Date** (`15`, `date`, Optional): Enter your payment date (if applicable).
* **Payment Method Details** (`16`, `text`, Optional): Describe your payment method details (if applicable).
* **Payment Status Details** (`17`, `note`, Optional): Describe your payment status details (if applicable).
* **Work Address** (`18`, `text`, Optional): Enter your work address.
* **Work City** (`19`, `text`, Optional): Enter your work city.
* **Work State** (`20`, `text`, Optional): Enter your work state.
* **Work Postal Code** (`21`, `text`, Optional): Enter your work postal code.
* **Work Phone** (`22`, `text`, Optional): Enter your work phone number.
* **Work Email** (`23`, `email`, Optional): Enter your work email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`24`, `text`, Optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`25`, `text`, Optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
