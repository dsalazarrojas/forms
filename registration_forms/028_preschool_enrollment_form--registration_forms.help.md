# Preschool Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from parents and guardians to enroll their children in a preschool program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please provide your basic information as a parent/guardian.
2. Fill out the contact information for yourself and any emergency contact person.
3. Enter the child's information, including any relevant health or medical details.
4. Provide payment information to secure your child's enrollment.
5. Double-check your answers for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Parent Information (`parent_info`, text, required: false)
Provide your basic information as a parent or guardian, including your name, address, and any other relevant details.

### Contact Information (`contact_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your contact information, including your phone number, email, and any other means of communication.

### Emergency Contact (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false)
Enter the name and contact information for the person to be contacted in case of an emergency.

### Child Information (`child_info`, text, required: false)
Enter the child's name, age, and any relevant health or medical information.

### Preschool Information (`preschool_info`, text, required: false)
Provide any other preschool-specific information, such as the child's previous educational background or any special needs.

### Payment Information (`payment_info`, text, required: false)
Enter your payment details, including payment method and amount, to secure your child's enrollment.
