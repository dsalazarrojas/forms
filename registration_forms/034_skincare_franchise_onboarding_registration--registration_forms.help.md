# Skincare Franchise Onboarding Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information about your skincare business and individual details to facilitate onboarding on our platform. Please complete all required fields to ensure a successful registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out the form by entering the required information.
- Review each field to ensure accuracy.
- Save your progress and come back to the form if you need to.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (1): Your personal name as it appears on your official identification documents.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Last Name** (2): Your surname as it appears on your official identification documents.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Email** (3): A valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
  - Type: email
  - Required: true

- **Franchise Name** (4): The name of your skincare franchise business.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Contact Phone** (5): Your contact number that we can use to reach you.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Address** (6): Your business address, including street, city, state, and country.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Date of Birth** (7): Your date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true

- **Time Zone** (8): Your time zone.
  - Type: time
  - Required: true

- **Business Name** (9): The name of your business, if different from the franchise name.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Business Address** (10): Your business address, including street, city, state, and country.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **City** (11): The city where your business is located.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **State** (12): The state where your business is located.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true

- **Postal Code** (13): The postal code of your business location.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Country** (14): The country where your business is located.
  - Type: text
  - Required: true

- **Franchise Type** (15): Whether your franchise offers onboarding.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true

- **Onboarding Date** (16): The date you want to be onboarded on.
  - Type: date
  - Required: true

- **Onboarding Time** (17): The time you want to be onboarded at.
  - Type: time
  - Required: true

- **Onboarding Method** (18): Whether you want to be onboarded manually or through our automated process.
  - Type: select_one
  - Required: true

- **Email Verification** (19): Whether you want to receive emails from us about your onboarding status.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: true

- **Consent** (20): Whether you agree to our terms and conditions.
  - Type: select_multiple
  - Required: true

- **Terms and Conditions** (21): A field for noting any additional terms and conditions.
  - Type: note
  - Required: true

- **Franchisee ID** (22): A unique identifier for your franchise.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Application ID** (23): A unique identifier for your application.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **User Agreement** (24): A field for noting any user agreements.
  - Type: text
  - Required: false

- **Notes** (25): A field for any additional notes.
  - Type: note
  - Required: false
