# Smart Property Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Smart Property Network Registration Form is designed to collect user information for registration or listing purposes. This form is intended for real estate agents or property owners to provide essential details about properties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Form section with your basic details.
2. Provide your real estate address in the Address section.
3. Specify your city, state (yes or no), and zip code.
4. Choose your property type (yes or no).
5. Enter your real estate license number and name (if applicable).
6. Fill in the Agent's Name and Agent Number fields (if you're an agent).
7. Select a date and time for the registration.
8. Add any additional notes if necessary.
9. Enter your email address and phone number (if desired).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form**:
	+ A text field where you can enter your basic details.
* **real_estate_address**:
	+ Enter your real estate address.
* **Address Line 1**:
	+ Enter the first line of your address.
* **Address Line 2**:
	+ Enter the second line of your address.
* **city**:
	+ Enter the city where your real estate is located.
* **state**:
	+ Select 'Yes' or 'No' to indicate if your state is applicable.
* **Zip**:
	+ Enter your zip code.
* **Property Type**:
	+ Choose your property type from the available options.
* **real_estate_license**:
	+ Enter your real estate license number.
* **Agent Name**:
	+ Enter your name if you're an agent.
* **Agent Number**:
	+ Enter your agent number.
* **Date Registration**:
	+ Select a date for the registration.
* **Time Registration**:
	+ Select a time for the registration.
* **Note**:
	+ Add any additional notes about your registration.
* **Email**:
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone**:
	+ Enter your phone number.

## Tips
* Ensure that you don't submit duplicate registrations.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact our support for guidance.
