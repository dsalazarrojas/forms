# Community Center Activity Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Community Center Activity Waiver Form is a mandatory form for participants in community center activities. It's designed to collect essential information about each participant, including their personal details, medical conditions, and liability release. The form is used to ensure the participant's safety and to protect the community center from potential liability claims.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. **Section 1: Participant Information**
	* Fill in your full name, date of birth, and email address in the required fields.
	* If you're under 18, provide the name of your legal guardian.
2. **Section 2: Activity Details**
	* Select the activities you'll be participating in from the list provided. Make sure to select all that apply.
3. **Section 3: Emergency Contact**
	* Provide the name and phone number of someone not living with you as an emergency contact.
4. **Section 4: Medical Conditions**
	* If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please inform us in the text field.
5. **Section 5: Liability and Assumption of Risk**
	* Carefully read the release of liability and assumption of risk section. If you have any concerns, ask your emergency contact for advice.
	* Select "I acknowledge and agree" to release the community center from liability claims.
6. **Section 6: Signature and Date**
	* Type your name as a digital signature in the provided field.
	* Select the date of this agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Full Name of Participant
**Full Name of Participant** (`participant_full_name_waiver`, text, required): Fill in your full name as it appears on your identification documents.

### Date of Birth
**Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required): Provide your date of birth for age verification purposes.

### Name of Legal Guardian (If participant is under 18)
**Name of Legal Guardian (If participant is under 18)** (`guardian_name_waiver`, text, optional): If you're under 18, provide the name and contact information of your legal guardian.

### Email Address
**Email Address** (`contact_email_waiver`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication and contact purposes.

### Primary Phone Number
**Primary Phone Number** (`contact_phone_waiver`, text, required): Provide your primary phone number for emergency contact purposes.

### Emergency Contact Name
**Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the name of someone not living with you as an emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Phone Number
**Emergency Contact Phone Number** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.

### Which activities will you be participating in
**Which activities will you be participating in** (`activities_participating_in`, select_multiple, required): Select all the activities you'll be participating in from the list provided.

### Any Medical Conditions or Allergies we should be aware of
**Any Medical Conditions or Allergies we should be aware of** (`medical_conditions_waiver`, text, optional): If you have any medical conditions or allergies, inform us in this field.

### Acknowledgment of Risk
**Acknowledgment of Risk** (`acknowledgment_of_risk`, select_one, required): Carefully read the release of liability and assumption of risk section. If you have any concerns, ask your emergency contact for advice. Select "I acknowledge and agree" to release the community center from liability claims.

### Release of Liability
**Release of Liability** (`release_of_liability`, select_one, required): Carefully read the release of liability and assumption of risk section. If you have any concerns, ask your emergency contact for advice. Select "I agree to the release" to release the community center from liability claims.

### Permission for Photos/Videos
**Permission for Photos/Videos** (`photo_release_option`, select_one, required): If you're willing, grant permission for us to take photos and videos of you for promotional use.

### Digital Signature
**Digital Signature** (`digital_signature_waiver`, text, required): Type your full name as a digital signature to serve as a formal agreement.

### Date of Agreement
**Date of Agreement** (`date_of_signing_waiver`, date, required): Enter the date of this agreement.
