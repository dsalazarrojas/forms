# Gardening Kit Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Gardening Kit Subscription Form is a form used to collect essential information from customers to process their subscription for a gardening kit service. This form is divided into several fields that need to be filled out to complete the subscription process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, providing all the necessary information.
2. Review each field for any errors or missing information before submitting the form.
3. If you encounter any issues or need assistance, contact our support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address to receive important information about your subscription and any updates from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): If you want to be contacted for any reason, please enter your phone number.
* **Subscription Plan** (`subscription_plan`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select the subscription plan that suits your needs. You can choose from Monthly, Quarterly, and Bi-Monthly plans.
* **Shipping Address** (`shipping_address`, `text`, required: false): Enter your shipping address for us to deliver your gardening kit.
* **Shipping City** (`shipping_city`, `text`, required: false): Enter the city where you want us to ship your gardening kit.
* **Shipping State** (`shipping_state`, `text`, required: false): Enter the state where you want us to ship your gardening kit.
* **Shipping Zip** (`shipping_zip`, `number`, required: false): Enter the zip code where you want us to ship your gardening kit.
* **Subscription Start Date** (`subscription_start_date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date when you want your subscription to start.
