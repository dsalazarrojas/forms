# Trial Attendance Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register attendees for a trial session, allowing individuals to sign up for a trial and provide their preferred date and time of attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. On the first page, enter your trial session sign-up information in the "Trial Session Sign-up" field.
2. On the second page, select your preferred date of attendance in the "Preferred Date" field.
3. On the second page, select your preferred time of attendance in the "Preferred Time" field.
4. Complete any other required fields as indicated.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Trial Session Sign-up
* **Trial Session Sign-up** (`Trial Session Sign-up`, `text`, required/optional): This field is where you enter your information about the trial session you are signing up for. You can enter any relevant details, such as the trial name, description, or location.

### Preferred Date
* **Preferred Date** (`Preferred Date`, `date`, required/optional): This field allows you to select your preferred date of attendance for the trial session.

### Preferred Time
* **Preferred Time** (`Preferred Time`, `time`, required/optional): This field allows you to select your preferred time of attendance for the trial session.
