# Auction Item Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new item for an auction. It collects essential details about the item, its submitter, and delivery information. Please complete this form to ensure your item is included in the auction.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your name and contact information (Steps 1-3).
2.  Enter a brief description of the item (Step 7).
3.  Select the item's category (Step 6).
4.  Choose the item's condition (Step 9).
5.  Enter the estimated value of the item (Step 8).
6.  Select the delivery method for the item (Step 10).
7.  Enter the item's name (Step 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Submitter Full Name** (Step 1): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Contact Email** (Step 2): Enter your email address that you'd like to be associated with your registration.
* **Contact Phone** (Step 3): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.
* **Item Information** (Step 4): You can enter any additional information about the item, such as its provenance or unique features.
* **Item Name** (Step 5): Enter a descriptive name for your item.
* **Item Category** (Step 6): Select the category that best describes the item (e.g., Art, Electronics, etc.).
* **Brief Description** (Step 7): Give a brief description of the item, explaining what makes it special.
* **Estimated Value** (Step 8): Enter your estimated value of the item (e.g., $100, $500, etc.).
* **Item Condition** (Step 9): Choose the condition of your item (e.g., New, Excellent, Good, Fair, or Poor).
* **Delivery Method** (Step 10): Select how you plan to get the item to the auction location (e.g., Drop off, Pick up, Mail in).
