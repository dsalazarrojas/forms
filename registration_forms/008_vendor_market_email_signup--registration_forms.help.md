# Vendor Market Email Signup - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from vendors interested in participating in the vendor market.

## How To Complete This Form

- First, ensure you are completing the form as an authorized representative of the vendor.
- Carefully review each field to provide accurate information.
- Fill out the first part of the form, which asks for your interest in participating in the market.
- Answer the questions truthfully, and make sure to provide a valid email address.
- If you have products to promote, select the options that apply to your products.
- If you have events, select whether you have a market event or not.
- For the second part of the form, select the options that apply to your products.
- Make sure to answer all required fields and take your time if you are unsure of any answer.
- Once you have completed the form, click the submit button to send the information to the vendor market team.

**Tips**
- If you are unsure of any information, please contact the vendor market team.
- If you have already filled out the form, your information will be automatically updated. 
- Please ensure you have permission from your company to participate in the vendor market. 
- The vendor market team will review your submission and contact you if further information is needed. 
- This form is for vendors interested in participating in the vendor market. If you are not a vendor, you should not fill out this form.
