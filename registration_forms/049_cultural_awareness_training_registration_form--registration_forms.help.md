# Cultural Awareness Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for cultural awareness training and provide personal information and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Please fill out all the required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
* Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
* Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
* Enter your date of birth in the "Date" field in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* Enter your organization where you work.
* Enter your job title.
* Enter your department.
* Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* Enter your phone number without any special characters.
* Select one or more category/categories you are interested in from the "Category" field.
* Select a role you would like to have during the training from the "Role" field.
* Select a location where you would like the training to take place from the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, required): Enter your organization where you work.
* **Job title** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title.
* **Department** (`department`, text, required): Enter your department.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number without any special characters.
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more categories you are interested in. Examples: Software Development, Data Science, Operations.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select a role you would like to have during the training. Examples: Team Lead, Project Manager, Intern.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, optional): Select a location where you would like the training to take place. Examples: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago.

## Tips
Please make sure to provide accurate and complete information to ensure you receive the right training materials and notifications.
