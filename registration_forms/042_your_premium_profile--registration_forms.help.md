# Your Premium Profile - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect user profile information for premium users. It's intended for users who want to showcase their professional profile, skills, and services offered.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name fields.
2. Enter your Email address.
3. Add your Phone number.
4. Enter your Website (if you have one).
5. Upload your Profile Picture (if applicable).
6. Write a brief Bio to describe yourself and your services.
7. Select your Location from the dropdown menu.
8. Choose your experience level from the options provided.
9. Select the services you offer from the options provided.
10. Choose your categories of expertise from the options provided.
11. Select your pricing terms from the options provided.
12. Choose your availability schedule from the options provided.
13. Select your work hours from the options provided.
14. Select your Timezone from the options provided.
15. List the languages you speak from the options provided.
16. Enter your Social Media Links (if applicable).
17. Add any relevant Tags to your profile.
18. Select the date your profile was completed.
19. Select the date your profile was last updated.
20. Choose who created your profile.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Website** (`website`, text, required): Enter your website (if you have one).
* **Profile Picture** (`profile_picture`, text, required): Upload a profile picture to make your profile more visually appealing.
* **Bio** (`bio`, note, required): Write a brief Bio to describe yourself and your services.
* **Location** (`location`, select_one, required): Select your location from the options provided.
* **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Choose your experience level from the options provided.
* **Services Offered** (`services_offered`, select_multiple, required): Select the services you offer from the options provided.
* **Categories** (`categories`, select_multiple, required): Choose your categories of expertise from the options provided.
* **Pricing** (`pricing`, number, required): Select your pricing terms from the options provided.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, required): Choose your availability schedule from the options provided.
* **Work Hours** (`work_hours`, select_multiple, required): Select your work hours from the options provided.
* **Timezone** (`timezone`, select_one, required): Select your Timezone from the options provided.
* **Languages** (`languages`, select_multiple, required): List the languages you speak from the options provided.
* **Social Media Links** (`social_media_links`, text, required): Enter your Social Media Links (if applicable).
* **Tags** (`tags`, text, required): Add any relevant Tags to your profile.
* **Profile Completed** (`profile_completed`, date, required): Select the date your profile was completed.
* **Last Updated** (`last_updated`, date, required): Select the date your profile was last updated.
* **Created By** (`created_by`, select_one, required): Choose who created your profile.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields.
- Be accurate and honest when filling out your profile information.
- Use the options provided for Location, Experience, Services Offered, Categories, Pricing, Availability, Work Hours, Timezone, and Languages.
- Use the correct formatting for date fields (Profile Completed and Last Updated).
- Use the correct formatting for select multiple fields (Services Offered, Categories, Availability, Work Hours, and Languages).
