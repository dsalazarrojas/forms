# Restaurant Manager Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals applying for a Restaurant Manager position. It's meant to collect information about the applicant's personal details, work experience, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To start, please fill out the form by providing your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter your Phone number to ensure we can contact you for further discussions.
3. If applicable, describe your Work Experience, Education, Previous Employment, and any References you'd like to share.
4. Choose your relevant Skills (Manager, Team Leader, or Team Member) and Availability (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, or Weekends).
5. Select your Job Type (Full-Time, Part-Time, or Freelance) and provide a Job Schedule if necessary.
6. Upload your Resume, which will be reviewed as part of your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, optional): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): A valid email address to contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): Your phone number for further discussions.
* **Work Experience** (`Work Experience`, `note`, optional): A brief description of your previous work experience.
* **Education** (`Education`, `note`, optional): Details about your educational background.
* **Previous Employment** (`Previous Employment`, `note`, optional): A description of your previous work history.
* **References** (`References`, `note`, optional): Names and contact details of professional references.
* **Skills** (`Skills`, `select_one`, optional): Choose one or more skills relevant to the Manager position.
	+ **Manager**: You have management experience and skills.
	+ **Team Leader**: You have leadership experience and skills.
	+ **Team Member**: You have experience working as part of a team.
* **Availability** (`Availability`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the times of day you are available to work.
	+ **Morning**
	+ **Afternoon**
	+ **Evening**
	+ **Weekends**
* **Job Type** (`Job Type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the type of job you're applying for.
	+ **Full-Time**
	+ **Part-Time**
	+ **Freelance**
* **Job Schedule** (`Job Schedule`, `time`, optional): Enter a specific job schedule if applicable.
* **Resume** (`Resume`, `note`, optional): Upload your resume to provide further information about your qualifications.

## Tips
- Please ensure the email address you provide is valid and accessible.
- Be as detailed as possible in your work experience, education, and references sections.
- Choose the correct skill and availability options relevant to the Manager position.
- Attach a clear and concise resume that highlights your relevant experience and qualifications.
