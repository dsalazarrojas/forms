# AI Conference Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI Conference Attendance Form is designed to collect information from attendees, including their details, registration preferences, and payment methods. This form ensures that the conference organizers have accurate and up-to-date information for attendee management, communication, and logistics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name as it should appear on your badge.
2. Enter your email address, and we'll send you confirmation and ticket details.
3. If you have a phone number, enter the international dialing code if you're outside your country.
4. If applicable, enter your organization or company name.
5. Choose your job title or role.
6. If you'd like, select your experience level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Professional).
7. Select the registration type or ticket category you've purchased.
8. Choose the days you plan to attend (e.g., Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Full Conference).
9. Select any sessions or tracks of interest to help us with room and materials planning.
10. If you have dietary restrictions or food allergies, choose from the available options.
11. Specify any accessibility or accommodation needs.
12. If you'd like, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person.
13. Choose how you paid or will pay (e.g., Credit Card, Invoice, PayPal, On-site Cash).
14. Enter the total paid or amount to be invoiced (numbers only).
15. If you were invoiced, provide the invoice or transaction number.
16. Enter your estimated arrival time for badge pickup.
17. Read and agree to the event's privacy policy and terms.
18. Decide if you'd like to be signed up for conference updates and newsletters.
19. Add any additional comments or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name**: Enter your name as it should appear on your badge. (Required)
* **Email address**: Provide the email address we'll use to send you confirmation and ticket details. (Required)
* **Phone number**: Enter your phone number, including the international dialing code if you're outside your country. (Optional)
* **Organization / Company**: If applicable, enter your company or organization name. (Optional)
* **Job title / role**: Select your job title or role. (Optional)
* **Date of birth**: If you'd like, enter your date of birth for eligibility purposes (e.g., student discount). (Optional)
* **Experience level**: Choose your experience level (e.g., Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Professional). (Required)
* **Registration type / ticket category**: Select the registration type or ticket category you've purchased. (Required)
* **Days attending**: Choose the days you plan to attend (e.g., Day 1, Day 2, Day 3, Full Conference). (Required)
* **Sessions or tracks of interest**: Select any sessions or tracks you're interested in to help us with room and materials planning. (Optional)
* **Dietary restrictions or food allergies**: Choose from the available options if you have any dietary restrictions or food allergies. (Optional)
* **Accessibility or accommodation needs**: Specify any accessibility or accommodation needs you may have. (Optional)
* **Emergency contact name**: If you'd like, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact person. (Optional)
* **Emergency contact phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person, including the international dialing code if necessary. (Optional)
* **Payment method**: Choose how you paid or will pay (e.g., Credit Card, Invoice, PayPal, On-site Cash). (Required)
* **Ticket price (USD)**: Enter the total paid or amount to be invoiced (numbers only). (Required)
* **Invoice or transaction number**: If you were invoiced, provide the invoice or transaction number. (Optional)
* **Estimated arrival time for badge pickup**: Enter your estimated arrival time for badge pickup. (Optional)
* **Consent and media release**: Read and agree to the event's privacy policy and terms. (Required)
* **Sign up for conference updates and newsletter**: Decide if you'd like to be signed up for conference updates and newsletters. (Optional)
* **Additional comments or special requests**: Add any additional comments or special requests you may have. (Optional)
