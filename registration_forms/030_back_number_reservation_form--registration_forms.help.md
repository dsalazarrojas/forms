# Back Number Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used to reserve your back number (jersey) for the upcoming season. Please fill out the form to ensure that your preferred number is secured.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out your player information, including your full name, team or age group, and coach's name.
2. Enter your preferred jersey number for each choice (1st, 2nd, and 3rd choice).
3. Choose your jersey size.
4. Enter your parent's email address for confirmation and notification.
5. Confirm that you understand the final number assignments are at the club's discretion.
6. Submit the form to secure your preferred number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Player Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Team or Age Group**: Select your team or age group, e.g., U14 Boys, Varsity Girls.
* **Head Coach Name**: Enter your coach's name as they are listed in the club's records.
* **1st Choice Number**: Enter your preferred jersey number, which will be used as your top choice.
* **2nd Choice Number**: Enter an alternative number in case your 1st choice is not available.
* **3rd Choice Number**: Enter another alternative number in case both 1st and 2nd choices are not available.
* **Significance of Choice (Optional)**: If you have a particular reason for your number preference, enter it here.
* **Number Worn Last Season**: If you're a returning player, enter the number you wore last season. If you're a new player, enter 0.
* **Uniform Jersey Size**: Select your jersey size, which will help us to order the correct size for you.
* **Parent Email for Confirmation**: Enter your parent's email address to ensure we can notify them of your number assignment.
* **I Understand that Final Number Assignments are at the Club's Discretion**: Confirm that you understand that the final number assignment is at the club's discretion.
* **Submission Date**: Date and time of submission for tracking and validation.
