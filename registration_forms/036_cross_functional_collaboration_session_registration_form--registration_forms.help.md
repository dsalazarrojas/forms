# cross_functional_collaboration_session_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cross-functional collaboration session registration form is used to gather information from participants for a collaborative event or meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out the form completely and accurately.
* Provide your name, email address, and phone number as requested.
* Add any additional notes you may want to share with the organizers.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it should appear on the session's list of participants.
* **Participant Email**: Enter a valid email address that you have access to. This will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted for any reason.
* **Participant Note**: Share any additional information you feel is relevant to your participation in the session.
* **Submit**: Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the submit button to register for the session.
