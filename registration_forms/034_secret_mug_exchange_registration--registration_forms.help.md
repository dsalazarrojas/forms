# Secret Mug Exchange Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Secret Mug Exchange Registration form is designed for attendees to provide their personal information and event details. Please fill out this form accurately and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the date of the event in the "Event Date" field.
6. Enter the time of the event in the "Event Time" field.
7. Enter any additional comments in the "Note" field (optional).
8. Enter your team or group name in the "Team or Group Affiliation" field (optional).
9. Enter your club or organization name in the "Club or Organization" field (optional).
10. Enter your community involvement in the "Community Involvement" field (optional).
11. Select your participant preferences in the "Participant Preferences" field.
11. Select your assigned tool in the "Assigned Tool" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Enter the time of the event.
* **Note** (`note`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments.
* **Team or Group Affiliation** (`team`, text, optional): Enter your team or group name.
* **Club or Organization** (`club`, text, optional): Enter your club or organization name.
* **Community Involvement** (`community`, text, optional): Enter your community involvement.
* **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, select_multiple, required): Select your participant preferences.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Select your assigned tool.

## Tips
* Ensure you fill in all required fields.
* Optional fields are for additional information but are not necessary for the registration process.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the event organizers.
