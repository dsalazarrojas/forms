# Retail Personalization API Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retail Personalization API Registration Form is a necessary step for anyone interested in utilizing our Retail Personalization API. By filling out this form, you will be providing us with the necessary information to tailor our API to your specific needs and ensure a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in your first name in the "First Name" field.
2.  Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3.  Provide your valid email address in the "Email" field.
4.  Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5.  Describe your use case in the "Use Case" field.
6.  Enter your company's name in the "Company Name" field.
7.  Provide your company's contact details in the "Contact Details" field.
8.  Enter your API key in the "API Key" field.
9.  Select your company type in the "Company Type" field.
10.  Choose your company size from the available options in the "Company Size" field.
11.  Select your industry from the available options in the "Industry" field.
12.  Choose your location from the available options in the "Location" field.
13.  Select your time zone from the available options in the "Time Zone" field.
14.  Enter the name of the person requesting the API in the "API Requester" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your business card or identification.
*   **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your business card or identification.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
*   **Phone**: Enter a valid phone number that we can use to contact you.
*   **Use Case**: Describe the specific use case you have for our Retail Personalization API.
*   **Company Name**: Enter your company's name exactly as it appears on your business card or identification.
*   **Contact Details**: Provide your company's contact details, including name and department.
*   **API Key**: Enter your retail personalization API key.
*   **Company Type**: Select the type of company you represent. Options: Retail Tech, SaaS, or Other.
*   **Company Size**: Select the size of your company. Options: Small, Medium, Large, or Enterprise.
*   **Industry**: Select the industry your company belongs to. Options: Fashion, Food and Beverage, Healthcare, or Other.
*   **Location**: Select the location of your company. Options: North America, Europe, Asia, Africa, or Other.
*   **Time Zone**: Select the time zone of your company.
*   **API Requester**: Enter the name of the person requesting the API.

Please submit the form once you've completed all fields accurately and thoroughly. This will ensure a smooth and efficient onboarding process for our Retail Personalization API.
