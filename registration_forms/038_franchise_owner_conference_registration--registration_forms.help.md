# Franchise Owner Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Franchise Owner Conference Registration form is a tool for franchise owners to provide information about their conference attendance, contact details, accommodation needs, special needs, dietary needs, accessibility needs, and event preferences.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Choose your attendance status by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No' from the "Attendance" field.
2. Fill out your contact details, including any required information (name, email, phone number, etc.).
3. If you have any accommodation needs, select the type of accommodation you require (Hotel, Other, or None).
4. If you have any special needs, select the options that apply to you (Yes, No, etc.).
5. If you have any dietary needs, select the options that apply to you (Yes, No, etc.).
6. If you have any accessibility needs, select the options that apply to you (Yes, No, etc.).
7. Finally, indicate your event preferences by selecting the options that appeal to you (Yes, No, etc.).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendance**: This field is used to indicate whether you will be attending the conference ('Yes' or 'No'). This will help us gauge the number of attendees and plan for catering and seating arrangements.
* **Contact Details**: This is the section for you to provide your contact information, including your name, email, and phone number. Please fill out this section accurately and completely.
* **Accommodation Needs**: If you have any accommodation requirements, such as a hotel reservation or special dietary needs, please select the corresponding option. If you don't have any requirements, you can leave this field blank.
* **Special Needs**: This field is used to collect information about any special needs or requirements you may have, such as wheelchair accessibility or language interpretation. Select the options that apply to you.
* **Dietary Needs**: This field is used to collect information about your dietary needs, such as vegetarian or gluten-free options. Select the options that apply to you.
* **Accessibility Needs**: Similar to special needs, this field is used to collect information about any accessibility needs you may have, such as sign language interpretation or audio descriptions.
* **Event Preferences**: Finally, this field is used to collect information about your interests and preferences for the conference program. Select the options that appeal to you.

## Tips

* Please be as accurate and thorough as possible when filling out this form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us directly.
* Thank you for taking the time to register for the conference!
