# Sports Program Intake Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect essential information from parents or guardians of child participants in sports programs. It helps us gather the necessary details to provide a safe and enjoyable experience for your child.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your child's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter your child's date of birth.
6. Select your child's sport name or sport of interest.
7. Enter your name in the "Parent Name" field.
8. If your child has a parent or guardian other than the person filling this form, provide their name in the "Parent Name" field.
9. Enter any relevant emergency contact information for your child.
10. Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person(s).
11. Mention any medical conditions your child may have.
12. Mention any allergies your child may have.
13. Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
14. Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
15. Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
16. Enter any notes about the emergency contact person.
17. Enter any additional medical notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (Email, Optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Date, Optional): Enter your child's date of birth.
* **Sport Name** (Text, Optional): Select your child's sport name or sport of interest.
* **Parent Name** (Text, Optional): Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
* **Parent Name** (Text, Optional): If you have a co-parent or guardian, enter their name.
* **Guardian Name** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of any other guardian or adult responsible for your child.
* **Emergency Contact Info** (Text, Optional): Provide any emergency contact information for your child.
* **Relationship** (Select Multiple, Optional): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person (e.g., Mother, Father, Sister, Brother, Friend, None).
* **Medical Conditions** (Text, Optional): Mention any medical conditions your child may have.
* **Allergies** (Text, Optional): Mention any allergies your child may have.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (Email, Optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the emergency contact person.
* **Medical Notes** (Text, Optional): Enter any medical notes.
* **Medical Notes 2** (Text, Optional): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Emergency Contact Info 2** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional emergency contact information for your child.
* **Relationship** (Select Multiple, Optional): Specify the relationship between you and the emergency contact person for the second contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone 2** (Text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email 2** (Text, Optional): Enter the email address of the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address 2** (Text, Optional): Enter the address of the second emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Notes 2** (Text, Optional): Enter any notes about the second emergency contact person.
