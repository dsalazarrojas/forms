# Sports Team Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sports Team Volunteer Registration Form is used to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering with the team.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out your contact information: First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Select your volunteer role: Team Leader, Event Coordinator, or General Volunteer.
3. Choose your availability: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
4. Provide your emergency contact information: Emergency Contact, Relationship, Phone Number, and Address.
5. Agree to the Consent.
6. Select your shirt size, shirt color, and shirt type.
7. Choose your shift: Morning, Afternoon, or Night.
8. Select your availability days: Monday to Sunday.
9. (Optional) Enter your volunteer experience, additional comments, volunteer start and end date, volunteer hours, and volunteer hours per week.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the event you'll be participating in.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the event you'll be participating in.
* **Volunteer Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Select your volunteer role: Team Leader, Event Coordinator, or General Volunteer.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_multiple, optional): Choose when you're available: Morning, Afternoon, or Evening.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, text, optional): Describe your volunteer experience.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Address** (`emergency_address`, text, optional): Enter your emergency contact's address.
* **Consent** (`consent`, note, required): Read and agree to our terms of service.
* **Shirt Size** (`shirt_size`, select_one, optional): Select your shirt size: XS, S, M, L, or XL.
* **Volunteer Shirt Color** (`volunteer_shirt_color`, select_one, optional): Select your volunteer shirt color: Yellow, Red, Blue, Green, or Black.
* **Shirt Type** (`shirt_type`, select_one, optional): Select your shirt type: Long Sleeve or Short Sleeve.
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_one, optional): Choose your shift: Morning, Afternoon, or Night.
* **Availability Days** (`availability_days`, select_multiple, optional): Select the days you're available: Monday to Sunday.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, date, optional): Enter your volunteer start date.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, date, optional): Enter your volunteer end date.
* **Volunteer Hours** (`volunteer_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of volunteer hours you're available per week.
* **Volunteer Hours Per Week** (`volunteer_hours_per_week`, number, optional): Enter the number of volunteer hours you're available per week.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your volunteer experience.
