# domino_tournament_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a domino tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information (participant_info)**: Enter your name and any other relevant participant information.
* **Team Information (team_info)**: Provide details about your team, including team members.
* **Tournament Information (tournament_info)**: Add any additional information about the tournament you'd like to share.

## Tips
* Double-check your participant and team information for accuracy.
* Make sure to provide clear and concise answers in all fields.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact form@gic.mx.
