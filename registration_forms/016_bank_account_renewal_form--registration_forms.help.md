# Bank Account Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form allows users to update their account information, select the type of account to be renewed, and verify their identity and employment status.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Provide your current account information, including account number and type.
2. Select the type of account to be renewed from the options provided.
3. Update your contact information, if necessary.
4. Verify your identity and employment status.
5. Agree to the updated terms and conditions.
6. Choose whether to enroll in paperless statements.
7. Provide your electronic signature.
8. Select the date of renewal submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Primary Account Holder Name** (`holder_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the name of the primary account holder.
* **Current Account Number** (`account_number`, text, required): This field is used to enter the current account number.
* **Account Type to Renew** (`renewal_account_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of account to be renewed.
* **Updated Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): This field is used to update the email address associated with the account.
* **Updated Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): This field is used to update the phone number associated with the account.
* **Current Residential Address** (`mailing_address`, text, optional): This field is used to enter the current residential address of the account holder.
* **Type of Identification on File** (`id_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the type of identification on file, such as Passport, Driver's License, or National ID Card.
* **ID Expiration Date** (`id_expiry_date`, date, required): This field is used to enter the expiration date of the identification on file.
* **Current Employment Status** (`employment_status`, select_one, required): This field is used to select the current employment status of the account holder, such as employed full-time or self-employed.
* **I have read and agree to the updated Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, select_one, required): This field is used to agree to the updated terms and conditions.
* **Enrollment in Paperless Statements** (`paperless_billing`, select_one, required): This field is used to choose whether to enroll in paperless statements.
* **Electronic Signature** (`digital_signature_name`, text, required): This field is used to enter the full legal name for electronic signature.
* **Date of Renewal Submission** (`signature_date`, date, required): This field is used to select the date of renewal submission.

Please note that all fields marked as "required" are mandatory and must be completed in order to submit the form.
