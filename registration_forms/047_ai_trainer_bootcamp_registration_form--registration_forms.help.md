# AI Trainer Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

This form is designed to gather participant information for the AI Trainer Bootcamp. It includes fields for your personal details, AI training experience, and preferences for the bootcamp sessions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form successfully, follow these steps:

1.  Ensure that you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
2.  Read each field's explanation to understand its purpose.
3.  Fill in your participant details, including your name, organization, and email address.
4.  Select your role or job title.
5.  Choose your experience level with AI training.
6.  Indicate your learning objectives.
7.  Select the sessions you prefer.
8.  Provide any relevant dietary needs.
9.  If applicable, add your emergency contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full name**: Please enter your first and last name as you would like them to appear on the bootcamp materials and certificates.
*   **Organization**: This is an optional field for you to provide information about your current workplace or affiliation.
*   **Email address**: Enter a valid email address where you can receive confirmation and important updates about the bootcamp.
*   **Role or job title**: Select one of the suggested roles or job titles that best describes your position within your organization.
*   **Experience level with AI training**: Choose the level of your experience with AI training from the options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert.
*   **Learning objectives**: Please type in what you hope to achieve from the AI Trainer Bootcamp. This will help the organizers tailor the experience to your needs.
*   **Session selection**: Choose the sessions that interest you the most. You can select one or more options depending on the type of selection field.
*   **Dietary needs**: If you have any dietary restrictions, please indicate them here. If not, you can leave this field blank.
*   **Emergency contact information**: If you need to provide an emergency contact person or number, you can do so here.

## Tips

*   Be honest and accurate when filling out the form.
*   Use the provided fields to help the bootcamp organizers plan and tailor the experience for you.
*   If you have any special requirements or concerns, please reach out to the contact person listed at the bottom of this document.
