# Ostomy Support Group Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ostomy Support Group Registration form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to join the Ostomy Support Group. This form is essential for our team to understand who is registering and ensure that they have the necessary contact details in case of an emergency.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Participant Name in the respective fields.
2. Enter your Email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Provide a Phone number where we can reach you.
4. In case of an emergency, please fill in the Emergency Contact Name and Emergency Phone number.
5. Enter your Participant Phone number for us to contact you.
6. Fill in your Address, City, and Zip Code to help us locate you.
7. Select your State from the list to indicate your location.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Enter your personal first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your personal last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name you would like to be known as within the Ostomy Support Group.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly for our group updates and communications.
* **Phone**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like us to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter a phone number where we can contact your emergency contact.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter a phone number where we can contact you directly.
* **Address**: Enter your home or preferred address where we can reach you.
* **City**: Enter the city where you reside.
* **State**: Select your state from the list to indicate your location.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code to ensure we can locate you accurately.
* **Tips**:
	+ Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and up-to-date.
	+ If you have any special requirements or needs, please let us know in the space provided.
	+ If you are unable to attend a meeting or event, please contact us to let us know.
	+ We are committed to protecting your privacy and confidentiality. We will not share any of your information without your consent.
	+ For questions or concerns, please contact us at your preferred contact method.
