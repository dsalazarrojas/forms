# Group Face Painting Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Group Face Painting Registration" form is designed to collect user and event information for face painting events. This form is meant for individuals or groups interested in booking face painting services.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
1. Fill in the provided fields with your relevant information and event details.
2. Review your submission for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: This field is for collecting your name and any other personal details you'd like to share with us. This will help us to better understand your preferences and requirements.
* **Preferred Date**: Please select a date for your event. We will do our best to accommodate your preferred date, but please be aware that our availability might be limited.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any specific requests or requirements for your event, please mention them here. We will do our best to accommodate your requests.
* **Booking Details**: Please provide us with any relevant booking details, such as number of attendees or number of face painters needed.
* **Contact Details**: Enter your email address or phone number where we can contact you with updates and confirmation of your booking.
* **Event Details**: Provide us with any additional event details, such as event location, event type, or any other relevant information.
* **Payment Details**: If you have any payment information or requirements, please provide them here. This will help us to process your booking efficiently.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled in accurately and completely to avoid any delays or issues with your booking.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us through the form's contact details field.
* We will do our best to accommodate your requests and preferences, but please be aware that our availability might be limited.
* Upon submitting this form, you will receive a confirmation of your booking. If you do not receive it, please get in touch with us through the contact details provided.
