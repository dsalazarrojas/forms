# Startup Demo Brunch RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect RSVPs for the demo brunch event. Please fill out the form to let us know if you can attend and any other relevant details about your attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:
1. Select your RSVP choice for the event.
2. Enter your guest's name.
3. Provide your phone number for contact purposes.
4. Enter your email address.
5. Select your guest type (e.g. yes, no).
6. Indicate if you have any dietary restrictions.
7. Choose your arrival date for the event.
8. Select your arrival time for the event.
9. Specify the number of guests attending with you.
10. (Optional) Add any additional message you would like to share with us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **RSVP** (`1`, select_one, required: false): 
    Please choose whether you will be able to attend the demo brunch by selecting either "Yes" or "No".
* **Guest Name** (`2`, text, required: false): 
    Please enter the name of the guest you are RSVPing for. If this is just for you, please enter your own name.
* **Phone** (`3`, text, required: false): 
    Please enter a contact phone number for yourself or your guest(s) in case we need to reach out.
* **Email** (`4`, email, required: false): 
    Please enter the email address of yourself or your guest(s) for communication purposes.
* **Guest Type** (`5`, select_multiple, required: false): 
    Select "Yes" if your guest is attending the demo brunch. Select "No" if they are not.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`6`, select_one, required: false): 
    Select "Yes" if you or your guest has any dietary restrictions that we should be aware of.
* **Arrival Date** (`7`, date, required: false): 
    Choose the date you plan to arrive for the demo brunch.
* **Arrival Time** (`8`, time, required: false): 
    Select the time you plan to arrive for the demo brunch.
* **Number of Guests** (`9`, number, required: false): 
    Enter the number of guests attending with you, including yourself.
* **Message** (`10`, note, required: false): 
    Add any additional information or message you would like to share with us about your attendance or your guest(s).
