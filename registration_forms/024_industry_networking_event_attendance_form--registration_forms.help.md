# industry_networking_event_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industry networking event attendance form is designed to collect information about your participation in industry events. This helps us understand your interests and preferences, which informs our future event planning.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant info (page 1).
2. Provide your contact information (page 2).
3. Specify your role in the industry (page 2).
4. Select the type of event you're attending (page 2).
5. Choose a date and time for the event (page 2).
6. Add any additional notes about your attendance (page 2).
7. Enter your email address (page 2).
8. Input your phone number (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Info (participant_info)**: This is the first page of the form where you can provide general information about yourself.
  - **Contact Info (contact_info)**: Enter your name and any other contact details you'd like to share.
  - **Company (company)**: Enter your company name or affiliation.
  - **Role (role)**: Select your role in the industry, such as CEO, Manager, Team Member, or Other.
  - **Event (event)**: Choose which type of event you're attending, like Industry Conference, Networking Event, or Trade Show.
  - **Date (date)**: Enter the date of the event you're attending.
  - **Time (time)**: Select the time of the event you're attending.
  - **Note (note)**: Add any additional notes or comments about your attendance.
  - **Email (email)**: Enter your email address so we can contact you about future events.
  - **Phone (phone)**: Input your phone number for further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "Required: true").
* Double-check your contact info, role, and event selection for accuracy.
* Use a valid email address and phone number to ensure you can receive updates and invitations from us.
