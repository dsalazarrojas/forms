# Pet Friendly Business Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from pet owners who wish to attend the conference with their pets. This will help us plan for pet-friendly accommodations and ensure their comfort during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Submit" button once you have filled out all the required and optional fields.
2. Fill out the registration form completely and accurately.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (Text)**: Enter your name as the owner of the pet attending the conference.
* **Pet Details (Text)**: Enter any relevant information about your pet, such as species, age, and size.
* **Session Interests (Select One)**: Choose one or more sessions that you would like to attend with your pet.
* **Owner Contact (Text)**: Enter your contact information as the owner of the pet, including your name and email address.
* **Pet Contact (Text)**: Enter your pet's contact information, including their name and phone number (if applicable).
* **Pet Type (Select One)**: Choose the type of pet you own (e.g. Dog, Cat, Other).
* **Pet Name (Text)**: Enter the name of your pet.
* **Pet DOB (Date)**: Enter your pet's date of birth.
* **Owner Name (Text)**: Enter your name as the owner of the pet.
* **Owner Email (Email)**: Enter your email address as the owner of the pet.
* **Owner Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number as the owner of the pet.
* **Session Preferences (Select Multiple)**: Choose one or more sessions that you would like your pet to attend (if applicable).
* **Special Requests (Text)**: Enter any special requests or accommodations you may need for your pet during the conference.
