# Finance Department Analysis Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for the registration of finance department analysis data. It is intended for internal use by the finance department to collect and analyze information about different departments.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your personal details in the "First Name", "Last Name", "Email", and "Phone" fields.
2. Enter the "Date" of the analysis.
3. Select the relevant "Finance Department" from the dropdown list.
4. Provide the name and contact information of your manager in the "Manager Name", "Manager Email", and "Manager Phone" fields.
5. If applicable, enter the location of your department, including the address, city, state, and zip.
6. Provide information about your team members in the "Team Members", "Team Members Role", "Team Members Email", and "Team Members Phone" fields.
7. Submit the form once you have completed all the required fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date (Date)**: Enter the date of the analysis.
* **Finance Department (Select One)**: Select the relevant finance department from the dropdown list.
* **Manager Name (Text)**: Enter your manager's name.
* **Manager Email (Email)**: Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Phone (Text)**: Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Department Location (Text)**: Enter the location of your department.
* **Department Location Address (Text)**: Enter the address of your department location.
* **Department Location City (Text)**: Enter the city of your department location.
* **Department Location State (Text)**: Enter the state of your department location.
* **Department Location Zip (Text)**: Enter the zip code of your department location.
* **Manager Manager (Text)**: Enter the manager's manager's name.
* **Manager Manager Email (Email)**: Enter the manager's manager's email address.
* **Manager Manager Phone (Text)**: Enter the manager's manager's phone number.
* **Team Members (Text)**: Enter the name of your team members.
* **Team Members Role (Text)**: Enter the role of your team members.
* **Team Members Email (Email)**: Enter the email address of your team members.
* **Team Members Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your team members.
* **Team Members Manager (Text)**: Enter the manager's name of your team members.
* **Team Members Manager Phone (Text)**: Enter the phone number of your team members' manager.
