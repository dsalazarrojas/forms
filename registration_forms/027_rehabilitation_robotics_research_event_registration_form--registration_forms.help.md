# Rehabilitation Robotics Research Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Rehabilitation Robotics Research Event Registration Form is designed to collect information about new research events in the rehabilitation robotics field. By filling out this form, you will be able to register your event and make it visible to the public.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the Registration Details (Page 1 of 2)
2. Fill in the Event Details (Page 2 of 2)
3. Review and submit your form

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Registration Details
* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`): A short description of the event, e.g. "Workshop on Robot-Assisted Therapy".
* **Name** (`name`): The name of the individual or institution submitting the event.
* **Name of Institution** (`name_of_institution`): The name of the institution hosting or affiliated with the event.
* **Role** (`role`): A selection of the role(s) of the individual or institution submitting the event.
* **Email** (`email`): The email of the individual or institution submitting the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`): The phone number of the individual or institution submitting the event.
* **Submitter** (`submitter`): A description of the individual or team submitting the event.
* **Submitter Email** (`submitter_email`): The email of the individual or team submitting the event.
* **Submitter Phone** (`submitter_phone`): The phone number of the individual or team submitting the event.
* **Submitter Address** (`submitter_address`): The address of the individual or team submitting the event.
* **Submitter Institution** (`submitter_institution`): The name of the institution of the individual or team submitting the event.
* **Submitter Position** (`submitter_position`): The position of the individual submitting the event.

### Page 2: Event Details
* **Date of Event** (`date_of_event`): The date of the event.
* **Time of Event** (`time_of_event`): The time of the event.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`): The location of the event.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`): A brief description of the event, e.g. "This event will cover the latest advances in robot-assisted therapy."
