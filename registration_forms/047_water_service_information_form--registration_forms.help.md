# Water Service Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Water Service Information Form is used to collect and store information about a customer's water service. This form is used to track and manage customer data, including contact information, service details, and billing history.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the customer's information, including their name and contact details.
2. Enter the service type (residential, commercial, or industrial) and any other relevant details.
3. Provide any necessary meter readings and service start/end dates.
4. Specify the billing cycle (monthly, quarterly, semi-monthly, or annually) and billing amount.
5. Update the billing status (active, inactive, or canceled) and assign the account to a customer or service technician.
6. Enter any notes or comments about the billing process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Customer Information** (customer_information): Enter the customer's name and contact details.
* **Address** (address): Enter the customer's address, including street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Phone Number** (phone_number): Enter the customer's phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email): Enter the customer's email address for contact purposes.
* **Service Type** (service_type): Select the type of service (residential, commercial, or industrial) that the customer is using.
* **Service Issue** (service_issue): Describe any issues or problems with the service.
* **Meter Readings** (meter_readings): Enter the current meter reading for the service.
* **Account Number** (account_number): Enter the account number associated with the service.
* **Meter Serial Number** (meter_serial_number): Enter the meter serial number for identification purposes.
* **Service Start Date** (service_start_date): Enter the date when the service started.
* **Service End Date** (service_end_date): Enter the date when the service ended.
* **Billing Cycle** (billing_cycle): Select the billing cycle (monthly, quarterly, semi-monthly, or annually) for the service.
* **Billing Amount** (billing_amount): Enter the total billing amount for the service.
* **Notes** (notes): Enter any notes or comments about the billing process.
* **Billing Status** (billing_status): Select the status of the billing (active, inactive, or canceled).
* **Last Updated** (last_updated): Enter the date when the billing information was last updated.
* **Updated By** (updated_by): Select who last updated the billing information (Administrator, Customer, or Service Technician).
* **Assigned To** (assigned_to): Select who the service is assigned to (Administrator, Customer, or Service Technician).
* **Billing Address** (billing_address): Enter the address where the billing will be sent.
* **Billing Contact** (billing_contact): Enter the contact person's name and title for billing purposes.
* **Billing Contact Info** (billing_contact_info): Enter any additional contact information for the billing contact.
* **Billing Notes** (billing_notes): Enter any notes or comments about the billing process.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure accurate and complete information.
* Double-check the billing cycle and billing amount to ensure accuracy.
* Update the billing status and assign the account to the correct person or group.
* Keep the billing notes up-to-date to track any changes or issues with the billing process.
