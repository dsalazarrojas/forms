# Animal Product Safety Audit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a facility's interest in conducting an Animal Product Safety Audit. Please provide accurate and thorough information to ensure we can tailor the audit to meet your facility's specific needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your organization's legal name, the primary contact person's name, and their phone number.
2. Provide your facility's physical address, where the audit will take place.
3. Select the type of audit you require (e.g., Routine Safety Audit, Certification Audit, etc.).
4. Choose the preferred start and end dates for the audit.
5. Enter the total number of employees at your facility, which helps us determine the audit scope.
6. Describe the specific scope and focus areas of the audit, which we'll use to tailor the audit to your facility's needs.
7. Enter a confirmation email to receive updates and notifications from us.
8. If you have any billing information or instructions (optional), please provide them.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Organization Legal Name** (`1`): Enter your organization's official name as registered in the relevant government agencies or jurisdictions.
* **Primary Contact Person** (`2`): Enter the name and title of the person responsible for the audit.
* **Contact Phone Number** (`3`): Enter the contact phone number for the person responsible for the audit.
* **Facility Physical Address** (`4`): Enter the physical address where the audit will take place.
* **Audit Type Selection** (`5`): Select the type of audit you require (e.g., Routine Safety Audit, Certification Audit, etc.).
* **Preferred Audit Start Date** (`6`): Enter the preferred start date for the audit.
* **Preferred Audit End Date** (`7`): Enter the preferred end date for the audit.
* **Total Number of Employees at Facility** (`8`): Enter the total number of employees at your facility.
* **Scope of Audit and Specific Focus Areas** (`9`): Describe the specific departments or processes that will be audited.
* **Registration Confirmation Email** (`10`): Enter an email address to receive updates and notifications from us.
* **Billing Information and Instructions** (`11`): If you have any billing information or instructions, enter them here (optional).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you fill in accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any issues or delays with the audit.
- Review and double-check your information before submitting the form.
- We will use the information provided in this form to tailor the audit to your facility's specific needs and ensure a smooth and successful audit process.
