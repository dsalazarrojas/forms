# National Park Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The National Park Program Enrollment Form is a form used to collect information from users interested in enrolling in a national park program. The collected data will be used for registration purposes only and will not be shared or used for any other reason.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Please ensure you are filling out the correct form for your intended program participation.
2. Fill in your contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and address.
3. Select your registration type (Individual or Group).
4. Provide a brief description of your program.
5. Enter any workshop details if applicable.
6. Fill in your emergency contact person and phone number.
7. Provide any medical information, if applicable.
8. Enter any additional comments if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Registration Information
* **Program Name (id: 2)**: Enter the name of the program you wish to enroll in.
* **Date (id: 3)**: Enter the date of the program you wish to participate in.
* **Time (id: 4)**: Enter the time of the program you wish to participate in.
* **Duration (id: 5)**: Enter the duration of the program you wish to participate in.
* **Description (id: 6)**: Provide a brief description of your program.
* **Workshop Details (id: 7)**: Enter any workshop details if applicable.
* **Registration ID (id: 8)**: Enter a registration ID if you have been provided one.
* **Registration Type (id: 9)**: Select "Individual" or "Group" to indicate your registration type.
* **Contact Person (id: 10)**: Enter the name of the person who will be your main contact.
* **Email (id: 11)**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone (id: 12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address (id: 13)**: Enter your address.
* **User Name (id: 14)**: Enter your name.
* **User E-mail (id: 15)**: Enter your email address.
* **User Phone (id: 16)**: Enter your phone number.
* **User Address (id: 17)**: Enter your address.
* **Emergency Contact Person (id: 18)**: Enter the name of the person who will be your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone (id: 19)**: Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Address (id: 20)**: Enter the address of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Info (id: 21)**: Enter any medical information you wish to provide.
* **Emergency Medical Info (id: 22)**: Enter any emergency medical information.
* **Medical Insurance (id: 23)**: Enter any medical insurance information.
* **Emergency Medical Insurance (id: 24)**: Enter any emergency medical insurance information.
* **Additional Comments (id: 25)**: Enter any additional comments or information.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are unsure about any of the fields, please contact us for clarification.
* This form is for registration purposes only and will not be used for any other reason.
