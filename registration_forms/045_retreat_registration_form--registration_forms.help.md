# Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Retreat Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants for a retreat event. It is intended for those who want to sign up for a retreat and want to provide their personal and event-specific details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant information in the **Participant Information** section.
2. Select your **Participant Details** from the options provided.
3. Enter the **Event Information**, which should be specific to the event you are attending.
4. Choose the **Retreat Location** where the event will take place.
5. Provide the **Event Dates** in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
6. Enter the **Event Times** in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
7. If necessary, add any **Notes** for the organizers.
8. Finally, confirm your participation by selecting **Yes** or **No** from the options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**: This is the first part of the form where you can input your personal details, such as your name and any other relevant information.
* **Participant Details**: Here, you can select which option best describes your participant type.
* **Event Information**: Enter any event-specific details you feel are necessary for the organizers.
* **Retreat Location**: Choose where the retreat will take place from the options provided.
* **Event Dates**: Enter the date of the event in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Event Times**: Enter the time of the event in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Notes**: This is an optional field for any additional comments or details you may want to share with the organizers.
* **Confirm Participation**: Confirm your participation by selecting 'Yes' or 'No'.
