# Country Club Seasonal Membership Application - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Country Club Seasonal Membership Application is a registration form designed for individuals interested in becoming seasonal members of the country club. It collects required information to understand your preferences and provide you with a tailored experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. To begin, click on the "Submitter Information" section and fill out the form with your details.
2. Enter your First Name in the "First Name" field.
3. Enter your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
4. Enter your valid Email address in the "Email" field.
5. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.
6. In the "Membership Type" field, select the type of membership you're interested in:
	* Annual: A full-year membership with all club facilities and activities.
	* Monthly: A flexible membership with limited access to facilities.
	* Quarterly: A part-year membership with reduced access to facilities.
7. If you'd like to upgrade your membership, select the membership plans that suit your needs:
	* Premium: Exclusive access to all facilities and activities.
	* Basic: Limited access to facilities and activities.
	* Free: Limited access to facilities and activities.
8. In the "E-signature" field, enter your electronic signature to confirm your application.
9. In the "Terms and Conditions" section, review the terms and conditions of your membership and agree to them.
10. Finally, fill out the "Submitter Information" section with any additional information you'd like to provide.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as you'd like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as you'd like it to be displayed on our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address for correspondence and communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any additional contact or communication.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the membership types available:
	+ Annual: A full-year membership with all club facilities and activities.
	+ Monthly: A flexible membership with limited access to facilities.
	+ Quarterly: A part-year membership with reduced access to facilities.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or more of the membership plans that suit your needs:
	+ Premium: Exclusive access to all facilities and activities.
	+ Basic: Limited access to facilities and activities.
	+ Free: Limited access to facilities and activities.
* **E-signature** (`e_signature`, text, required): Enter your electronic signature to confirm your application.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, optional): Review and agree to the terms and conditions of your membership.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, note, optional): Fill out any additional information you'd like to provide about yourself.
