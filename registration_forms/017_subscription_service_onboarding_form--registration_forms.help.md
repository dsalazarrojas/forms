# Subscription Service Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Subscription Service Onboarding Form is designed to collect information from new subscribers to our subscription service. The form ensures that users understand and agree to our terms and conditions, and provides us with the necessary details to set up their account.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Review the form carefully**: Before starting to fill out the form, review its contents and ensure you understand what information is required.
2. **Complete each field**: Fill out each field accurately and thoroughly. For fields with options, select the one that best represents your preference or agreement.
3. **Submit the form**: Once you've completed all the fields, review the form one last time for accuracy and submit it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Enter your personal details to help us identify you as a new subscriber. (Required: False)
* **Plan Pricing**: Select the plan that best suits your needs. Please note that this selection will determine your subscription's pricing. (Required: False)
* **Billing Details**: Enter your billing information, including your name, address, and any other required details. (Required: False)
* **Payment Method**: Select your preferred payment method. Please note that not all payment methods may be available for all plans. (Required: False)
* **Agreement Form**: This is a text field for our agreement terms and conditions. Please read and agree to the terms before proceeding. (Required: False)
* **Contact Details**: Enter any additional contact details you wish to provide, such as phone numbers or email addresses. (Required: False)
* **Agreement Text**: Review and agree to our agreement terms in this text field. (Required: False)
* **Agreement Checkbox**: Select all the options that apply to your agreement. (Required: False)

**Tips**: Please ensure that you have read and understood each field carefully before proceeding. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
