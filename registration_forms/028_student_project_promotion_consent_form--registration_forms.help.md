# Student Project Promotion Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Student Project Promotion Consent Form is designed to gather consent and relevant information from students for promotional purposes. This form will help us ensure that students are aware of how their project will be used for promotional activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the title of your project in the "Project Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Enter your guardian's contact information in the "Guardian Information" field.
4. Choose whether you are the student or your guardian providing the consent.
5. Select whether your project can be used for promotional purposes.
6. Enter your name in the "Student Name" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Student Email" field.
8. Enter your phone number in the "Student Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Project Promotion Consent Form** (`form_title`, text, false): This is the title of the form that will be displayed to the user.
* **Project Title** (`project_name`, text, false): Enter the title of your project.
* **Project Description** (`project_description`, text, false): Enter a brief description of your project.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_info`, text, false): If you are a minor, enter the contact information of your guardian.
* **Student or Guardian Consent** (`student_consent`, select_one, false): Choose whether you are the student or your guardian providing the consent.
	+ Options: Student, Guardian
* **Promotional Use** (`promotional_use`, select_multiple, false): Choose whether your project can be used for promotional purposes.
	+ Options: True, False
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, false): Enter your name.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, false): Enter your email address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, false): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields. If you are not sure about any field, please ask your guardian or project supervisor for clarification.
