# Quality Management Leadership Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Quality Management Leadership Workshop. Fill out this form to provide the necessary information to participate in the workshop. Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the "Register" button to begin the form.
2. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
3. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
5. Select the date you would like to attend the workshop in the "Schedule date" field.
6. Select the time you would like to attend the workshop in the "Schedule time" field.
7. Enter the location of the workshop in the "Workshop location" field.
8. If you have any special requirements, please describe them in the "Special requirements" field.
9. Finally, if you are attending with a consultant, please enter their name in the "Consultant name" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Text, Not required): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (Text, Not required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Schedule date** (Date, Required): Select the date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Schedule time** (Time, Required): Select the time you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop location** (Text, Not required): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Special requirements** (Text, Not required): If you have any special requirements, describe them here.
* **Consultant name** (Text, Not required): If you are attending with a consultant, enter their name here.
## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
