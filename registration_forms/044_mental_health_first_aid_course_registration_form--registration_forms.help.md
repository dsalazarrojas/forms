# mental_health_first_aid_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The mental health first aid course registration form is a simple form used to register participants for the course.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required information, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select the course option that suits you the best.
3. Choose the date and time of the course that you would like to attend.
4. Add any additional comments if needed.
5. Review your information and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
- **Email** (email): Enter your email address to confirm your identity.
- **Phone** (text): Enter your phone number.
- **Select a course** (select_one): Choose the course that you would like to attend.
- **Select a Date** (select_multiple): Choose the course dates that you would like to attend.
- **Date** (date): Enter the date of the course that you would like to attend.
- **Time** (time): Enter the time of the course that you would like to attend.
- **Note** (note): Add any additional comments about the course.
- **Confirm phone** (text): Re-enter your phone number to confirm.
- **Confirm email** (email): Re-enter your email address to confirm.
- **Submit** (text): Review and submit your registration.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you are registering under your own account and not duplicating a previous registration.
- Review your information carefully before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
