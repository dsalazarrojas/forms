# Tournament Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tournament Registration Form is designed to collect information from teams and players for the purpose of registering for a tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the correct registration type for your team or player.
2. Enter the tournament name and other relevant details.
3. Fill out the team and player information fields.
4. Submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Tournament Name**: Enter the name of the tournament you are registering for.
* **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
* **Team Name**: This is the team name field, which should match the team name field above.
* **Team Manager**: Enter the name of the team manager.
* **Team Manager Contact Info**: Enter any additional contact information for the team manager.
* **Team Email**: Enter the email address of the team manager.
* **Player Count**: Enter the number of players on your team.
* **Registration Type**: Select the type of registration you are making, such as "team" or "individual".
* **Start Date**: Enter the start date of the tournament (if applicable).
* **End Date**: Enter the end date of the tournament (if applicable).
* **Tournament Type**: Select the type of tournament, such as "singles" or "doubles".
* **Tournament Format**: Select the format of the tournament, such as "single-elimination" or "round-robin".
* **Tournament Location**: Enter the location of the tournament.
* **Team Address**: Enter the address of your team.
* **Team Phone**: Enter the phone number of the team manager.
* **Team Fax**: Enter the fax number of the team manager.
* **Team Website**: Enter the website of your team.
* **Player Address**: Enter the address of each player on your team.
* **Player Phone**: Enter the phone number of each player.
* **Player Fax**: Enter the fax number of each player.
* **Player Website**: Enter the website of each player.
* **Team Manager Note**: Enter any additional notes for the team manager.
* **Player Manager Note**: Enter any additional notes for each player manager.
* **Team Manager Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the team manager.
* **Player Manager Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of each player manager.

Note: Fields marked as "required" are compulsory and must be filled out. Fields marked as "optional" can be left blank, but may aid in the tournament organization and management process.
