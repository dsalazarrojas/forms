# Research Collaboration Matchmaking Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Research Collaboration Matchmaking Registration" form is a tool designed to help researchers find potential collaborators for their projects. By filling out this form, you can provide information about your research interests, preferences, and experiences, which will be used to match you with other researchers who share similar interests and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields, marked with an asterisk (*).
2. Select your preferences and interests from the relevant drop-down menus.
3. Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
4. Provide a brief description of your research profile.
5. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required): This field is used to capture general information about the user, such as name and contact details.
* **Collaboration Preferences** (`Collaboration Preferences`, select_one, required): Select one of the options that best describes your preferred way of collaboration. This will help us match you with researchers who share similar preferences.
* **Contact Info** (`Contact Info`, text, required): Enter your email address and phone number to facilitate communication with potential collaborators.
* **Researcher Profile** (`Researcher Profile`, text, required): Describe your research background, skills, and expertise.
* **Interests** (`Interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all the areas where you are interested in collaborating.
* **Collaboration Preferences** (`Collaboration Preferences`, select_one, optional): Select one of the options that best describes your preferred way of collaboration, if not already selected as a required field.
* **Research Focus** (`Research Focus`, select_multiple, optional): Select all areas where you are focusing your research.
* **Research Experience** (`Research Experience`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas where you have research experience.
* **Research Interests** (`Research Interests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas where you are interested in conducting research.
* **Research Areas** (`Research Areas`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the areas where you are interested in conducting research.
* **Research Tools** (`Research Tools`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the tools you use in your research.
