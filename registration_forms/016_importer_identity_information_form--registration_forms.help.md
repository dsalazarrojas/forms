# Importer Identity Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Importer Identity Information Form is used to collect essential information from trade teams who want to import goods. This form helps verify the identity of the trade team and establish a contact method for communication.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid email address in the Trade Team Email field.
3. Choose "I agree" from the Terms of Service dropdown menu, indicating that you have read and accepted the terms and conditions.
4. Review your information carefully before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
