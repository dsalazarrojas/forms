# Retailer Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from retailers for various business purposes. Please ensure that you are a legitimate retailer and do not submit this form if you are not. Duplicate submissions will be checked to prevent spam or abuse.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately with your personal and business information.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly.
3. If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your work or business email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work or business phone number.
* **Address** (`address`, text, required): Enter your store's address.
* **Store Name** (`store_name`, text, required): Enter your store's name as it appears on your storefront and marketing materials.
* **Store Type** (`store_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of store you represent:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Store Hours** (`store_hours`, time, required): Enter your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00-17:00).
* **Store Hours From** (`store_hours_from`, time, required): Enter the start time of your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 09:00).
* **Store Hours To** (`store_hours_to`, time, required): Enter the end time of your store's operating hours in 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00).
* **Store Hours Note** (`store_hours_note`, text, required): Enter any additional information about your store's operating hours, such as special holiday hours or seasonal closures.
