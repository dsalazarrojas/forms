# Volunteer Shift Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from volunteers about their availability and preferences for volunteering shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the shift date you are interested in volunteering for.
2. Select your start and end times for the shift.
3. Provide a brief description of your volunteer availability and any relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Volunteer Shift Request** (`Volunteer Shift Request`, `text`, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the shift you are interested in volunteering for.
* **Shift Date** (`Shift Date`, `date`, required/optional): Enter the date of the shift you are interested in volunteering for. Please ensure that this date is in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Start Time** (`Start Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the start time of your shift. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 14:00 for 2:00 PM).
* **End Time** (`End Time`, `time`, required/optional): Enter the end time of your shift. Please use the 24-hour format (e.g., 17:00 for 5:00 PM).
* **Volunteer Availability** (`Volunteer Availability`, `text`, required/optional): Enter any relevant information about your availability, such as any limitations or special requirements.
