# skill_development_session_permission_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to request permission to participate in a skill development session. It provides the session organizer with necessary information to assess and process the request.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the Session ID of the skill development session you are interested in participating in.
2. Enter your name as it would be displayed on any communication related to the session.
3. Enter your email address to facilitate communication with you.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it so that the session organizer can contact you for any further information or updates.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Session ID** (`Session ID`, `text`, required): Enter the ID of the session you are interested in participating in. This is a unique identifier for the session and will help the organizer track your request.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name as you would like it to be displayed on any communication related to the session.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address to ensure smooth communication with the session organizer.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, optional): If you have a phone number, enter it for the session organizer to contact you for any further questions or updates.
