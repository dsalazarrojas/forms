# promotional_contest_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect contact information from users interested in participating in our promotional contest. It helps us to verify their interest and confirm their agreement to our terms and conditions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the form with your contact information and details.
2. Review and agree to our terms and conditions.
3. Submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed in our records.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for contest updates and communications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number if you would like to be contacted by phone for contest updates or any other reason.

## Tips
- Make sure to use a valid email address and a phone number (if provided) to ensure you receive important updates and information about the contest.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us using the provided email address.
- Once you submit this form, you will receive a confirmation email with further instructions on how to proceed with the contest.
