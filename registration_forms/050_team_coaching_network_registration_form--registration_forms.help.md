# Team Coaching Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Team Coaching Network Registration Form is designed to gather information from users who are interested in joining the Team Coaching Network. The form asks for basic contact information, preferred team affiliation, and coaching or playing position.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the first name field with your given name.
2. Enter your last name in the second field.
3. Provide your email address in the third field.
4. Enter your phone number in the fourth field.
5. Indicate your coach or playing status in the fifth field.
6. Select the team you are interested in joining from the dropdown menu in the sixth field.
7. Choose one or multiple coaching or playing positions you are interested in from the dropdown menu in the seventh field.
8. If you have a specific date or time you would like to be contacted, you can enter it in the eighth and ninth fields.
9. Leave a note for the Team Coaching Network administrators in the tenth field, if you have any information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your first name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name as it is written on your identification documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Coach/Player**: Indicate if you are a coach or player by selecting "Coach" or "Player".
* **Team**: Select the team you would like to join from the dropdown menu.
* **Position**: Choose one or multiple positions you are interested in playing or coaching from the dropdown menu.
* **Date**: If you have a specific date you would like to be contacted, enter it here.
* **Time**: If you have a specific time you would like to be contacted, enter it here.
* **Note**: Leave a note for the Team Coaching Network administrators if you have any information you'd like to share.
