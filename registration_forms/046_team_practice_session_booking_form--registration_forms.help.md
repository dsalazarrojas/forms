# Team Practice Session Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to book a team practice session. It will help you create a record of the session details, including date, time, location, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Practice Session Date** (`practice_session_date`, Date, Required: false): This field is used to enter the date of the practice session. If you know the date of the session, fill it in here.
* **Practice Session Time** (`practice_session_time`, Time, Required: false): This field is used to enter the time of the practice session. If you know the time of the session, fill it in here.
* **Team Players** (`team_players`, Select Multiple, Required: false): This field allows you to select whether the team has players or not. Choose 'Yes' if the team has players and 'No' if they don't.
* **Coach Contact Name** (`coach_contact_name`, Text, Required: false): This field is used to enter the name of the coach who will be in contact for the practice session. If you know the coach's name, fill it in here.
* **Coach Contact Phone** (`coach_contact_phone`, Text, Required: false): This field is used to enter the phone number of the coach who will be in contact for the practice session. If you know the coach's phone number, fill it in here.
* **Coach Contact Email** (`coach_contact_email`, Email, Required: false): This field is used to enter the email address of the coach who will be in contact for the practice session. If you know the coach's email address, fill it in here.
* **Practice Location** (`practice_location`, Text, Required: false): This field is used to enter the location of the practice session. If you know the location, fill it in here.
* **Practice Duration** (`practice_duration`, Number, Required: false): This field is used to enter the duration of the practice session. If you know the duration, fill it in here.
* **Practice Start Time** (`practice_start_time`, Time, Required: false): This field is used to enter the start time of the practice session. If you know the start time, fill it in here.
* **Practice End Time** (`practice_end_time`, Time, Required: false): This field is used to enter the end time of the practice session. If you know the end time, fill it in here.
* **Practice Notes** (`practice_notes`, Note, Required: false): This field is used to enter any additional notes about the practice session. If you have any notes, fill them in here.
* **Players Notes** (`practice_players_notes`, Note, Required: false): This field is used to enter any notes about the players who will be attending the practice session. If you have any notes, fill them in here.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`, Text, Required: false): This field is used to enter the name of the team who will be practicing. If you know the team name, fill it in here.
* **Team Name 2** (`team_name_2`, Text, Required: false): This field is used to enter the name of the team who will be attending the practice session (if different from the first team name). If you know the team name, fill it in here.
* **Team Manager Notes** (`team_manager_notes`, Note, Required: false): This field is used to enter any notes about the team manager who will be attending the practice session. If you have any notes, fill them in here.
* **Team Manager Email** (`team_manager_email`, Email, Required: false): This field is used to enter the email address of the team manager who will be attending the practice session. If you know the team manager's email address, fill it in here.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the practice session administrator.
