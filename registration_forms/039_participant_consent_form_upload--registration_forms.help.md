# Participant Consent Form Upload - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant consent for research or other purposes. Please complete this form if you need to obtain consent from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Participant Consent Form Upload" tab to open the form.
2. You will be asked to upload one or more documents for participant consent. Please select the relevant documents from the options provided.
3. You can select multiple documents if necessary.
4. If no documents are available, you can leave this field blank.
5. Enter the Participant Consent Date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
6. Make sure to enter the correct date and format for participant consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Consent Form Upload
* **Participant Consent Form Upload** (`participant_consent_form_upload`, `text`, optional): This field allows you to upload one or more documents for participant consent. Please select the relevant documents from the options provided.

### Uploaded Documents
* **Uploaded Documents** (`uploaded_documents`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select one or more documents from the provided options to upload for participant consent.

### Participant Consent Date
* **Participant Consent Date** (`participant_consent_date`, `date`, optional): Enter the date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" to indicate the participant consent date. Ensure to enter the correct date and format for participant consent.
