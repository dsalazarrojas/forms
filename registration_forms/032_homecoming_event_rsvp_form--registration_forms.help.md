# Homecoming Event RSVP Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Homecoming Event RSVP Form is used to gather information from students and their families about their attendance and event preferences for the homecoming event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the text field labeled "First Name".
2. Enter your Last Name in the text field labeled "Last Name".
3. Enter your Email address in the email field labeled "Email".
4. Choose whether you are attending the event by selecting "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown menu labeled "Are you attending?".
5. Select your meal options by choosing from the dropdown menu labeled "Meal Options".
6. Enter any special requests in the text field labeled "Special Requests".
7. Provide event details in the time field labeled "Event Details".
8. Enter your School Name in the text field labeled "School Name".
9. Enter your Class Year in the text field labeled "Class Year".
10. Enter your Graduation Date in the date field labeled "Graduation Date".
11. Enter your Student Name in the text field labeled "Student Name".
12. Enter the Parent/Guardian Name in the text field labeled "Parent/Guardian Name".
13. Enter the Parent Email in the email field labeled "Parent Email".
14. Enter the Parent Phone in the text field labeled "Parent Phone".
15. Enter the Emergency Contact in the text field labeled "Emergency Contact".
16. Enter the Emergency Phone in the text field labeled "Emergency Phone".
17. Enter the Emergency Email in the email field labeled "Emergency Email".
18. Enter any Medical Conditions in the text field labeled "Medical Conditions".
19. Enter any Medical Notes in the text field labeled "Medical Notes".
20. Select your dietary restrictions by choosing from the dropdown menu labeled "Dietary Restrictions".
21. Select any accessibility needs by choosing from the dropdown menu labeled "Accessibility Needs".
22. Select any accessibility requests by choosing from the dropdown menu labeled "Accessibility Request".
23. Enter any other special requests in the text field labeled "Other Special Requests".
24. Enter any comments in the text field labeled "Comments".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Are you attending?** (`attending`, select_one, required): Choose "Yes" if you are attending the event, or "No" if you are not.
* **Meal Options** (`meal_options`, select_multiple, required): Select the meal options that you prefer, including Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, or No Meal.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, note, required): Enter any special requests you have, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, time, required): Enter the event details, including start and end times.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your school.
* **Class Year** (`class_year`, text, required): Enter your class year.
* **Graduation Date** (`graduation_date`, date, required): Enter your graduation date.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required): Enter your student name.
* **Parent/Guardian Name** (`parent_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Email** (`parent_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent Phone** (`parent_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, text, optional): Enter any medical conditions you have.
* **Medical Notes** (`medical_notes`, note, optional): Enter any additional medical notes.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions, including Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, or None.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, select_multiple, optional): Select any accessibility needs, including Wheelchair Accessible, Sign Language, or None.
* **Accessibility Request** (`accessibility_request`, select_multiple, optional): Select any accessibility requests, including Wheelchair Accessible, Sign Language, or None.
* **Other Special Requests** (`special_requests_other`, text, optional): Enter any other special requests.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments about your response.
