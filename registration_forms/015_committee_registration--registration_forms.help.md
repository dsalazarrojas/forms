# Committee Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Committee Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining a committee. This form is the first step in the committee registration process and aims to gather essential details that will help in the evaluation and selection process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Begin by filling out your contact information, including your full name, email address, and phone number.
2.  Select your primary and secondary committee choices and specify which roles you are interested in.
3.  Provide details about your qualifications and experience relevant to the selected committees.
4.  Specify the key skills and expertise you can bring to the committee.
5.  Explain why you are interested in joining the committee.
6.  Confirm your availability to attend regular meetings and specify your preferred meeting times.
7.  Confirm your interest in serving on the committee and sign your name to complete the application.
8.  Finally, submit your application and wait for further instructions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Full Name** (`applicant_full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as you would like to be addressed.
*   **Email Address** (`applicant_email`, email, required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
*   **Phone Number** (`applicant_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for any further communication or updates.
*   **Member ID** (`applicant_id_member`, text, optional): If you have a member ID, please enter it.
*   **Primary Committee Choice** (`primary_committee_choice`, select_one, required): Select your primary committee choice from the list provided. This is your top preference.
*   **Secondary Committee Choice** (`secondary_committee_choice`, select_one, optional): Select your secondary committee choice if you have multiple preferences.
*   **Interested Roles** (`interested_roles`, select_multiple, required): Specify the roles you are interested in serving on the committee.
*   **Qualifications and Experience** (`previous_exp_relevant`, text, required): Describe your qualifications and experience relevant to the selected committees.
*   **Key Skills and Expertise** (`key_skills_brought`, text, required): Enter your key skills and expertise that you can bring to the committee.
*   **Reason for Joining** (`reason_for_joining`, text, required): Explain why you are interested in joining the committee.
*   **Committee Meeting Availability** (`available_for_meetings`, select_one, required): Confirm your availability to attend regular committee meetings.
*   **Preferred Meeting Times** (`preferred_meeting_times`, select_multiple, required): Specify your preferred meeting times, selecting all that apply.
*   **Acknowledgment and Submission** (`applicant_signature_check`, text, required): Confirm that you are interested in serving on the committee and sign your name to complete the application.
*   **Date of Application** (`application_date_reg`, date, required): Enter the date you submitted your application.
*   **How Heard About** (`reference_source`, select_one, optional): Let us know how you heard about this committee registration.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that your application is completed accurately and thoroughly.
*   If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to contact the committee for guidance.
*   We appreciate your interest in joining our committee, and we look forward to reviewing your application.
