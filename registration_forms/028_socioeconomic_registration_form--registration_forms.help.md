# Socioeconomic Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Socioeconomic Registration Form is used to collect demographic information from individuals. This form helps us to understand your personal and socioeconomic characteristics, which may be used for research, administrative, or other purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out. If a field is not required, you may leave it blank.
3. Use a valid format for date fields (MM/DD/YYYY).
4. For numeric fields, enter a whole number without any decimal points.
5. For email fields, enter a valid email address in the format "username@domain.com".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name (id: 1)**: Enter your full legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address (id: 2)**: Enter your current address, including house number and apartment number (if applicable).
* **Phone (id: 3)**: Enter your phone number in the format "XXX-XXX-XXXX" or "XXX-XXX-XXXXX" (e.g., "123-456-7890" or "123-456-7890").
* **Email (id: 4)**: Enter your email address as it appears on your official documents or in your everyday use.
* **Birth Date (id: 5)**: Enter your date of birth in the format "MM/DD/YYYY" (e.g., "01/01/1990").
* **Income (id: 6)**: Enter your annual income in whole numbers (e.g., 50000).
* **Occupation (id: 7)**: Enter your current occupation or job title.
* **Family Size (id: 8)**: Enter the number of family members dependent on you, including yourself.

## Tips
* Be accurate and honest when filling out this form.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact us for assistance.
* If you have any further questions or need clarification, please refer to this help guide or contact us.
