# sports_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who are registering for a sports team or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with your information.
2. Select your preferred position from the dropdown menu.
3. Select any additional positions you are comfortable playing from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose whether you accept or decline the terms and conditions.
5. Read and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name** (`name`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address where you can be reached for communication.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached during emergencies or updates.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Enter the name of your guardian or parent.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required): Enter the email address of your guardian where they can be reached.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Enter the phone number of your guardian where they can be reached during emergencies or updates.
* **Play Position** (`play_position`, select_one, required): Select the position you can play on the team (e.g. First Base, Pitcher, etc.).
* **Prefer Position** (`prefer_position`, select_multiple, required): Select any additional positions you are comfortable playing (e.g. First Base, Pitcher, etc.).
* **Accept** (`accept_terms`, select_one, required): Select "Accept" to agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Terms** (`terms`, text, required): Read and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting "Accept" above.
* **Conditions** (`conditions`, text, required): Read the conditions of registration, including any rules and regulations of the sports team or program.

## Tips
* Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting.
* Make sure to select your preferred position and additional positions you are comfortable playing.
* Carefully read and agree to the terms and conditions before submitting the form.
