# Seminar Registration Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information for seminars. It is required for seminar registration and participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant name field with your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
2. Enter your email address in the email field. This will be used for communication purposes.
3. Enter your phone number in the phone number field. This will be used for contact and communication purposes.
4. Enter your date of birth in the date of birth field. This will help us verify your identity.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **participant name** (Text, Required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **email** (Email, Required): Enter your valid email address. This will be used for communication and updates.
* **phone number** (Number, Required): Enter your phone number. This will be used for contact and communication purposes.
* **date of birth** (Date, Required): Enter your date of birth. This will help us verify your identity and ensure you are eligible to participate in the seminar.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you are having trouble filling out a field, contact our support team.
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have already registered for the seminar, do not fill out this form again.
