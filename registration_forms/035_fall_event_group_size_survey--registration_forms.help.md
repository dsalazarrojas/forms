# Fall Event Group Size Survey - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fall Event Group Size Survey is a form designed to collect demographic information about groups attending fall events. This data will help event organizers to better understand the needs and expectations of their attendees and tailor their events accordingly.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information about the group you are representing.
2. Be honest and accurate in your responses to ensure that you are providing reliable data.
3. Submit the form when you have completed it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name**: `event_name` (<name>, `text`, required: no)
This field allows you to enter the name of the event your group is attending.
* **Group Type**: `group_type` (<name>, `select_one`, required: no)
Select the type of group you are representing (Family, Couple, Individual).
* **Group Size**: `group_size` (<name>, `number`, required: no)
Enter the number of people in your group.
* **Event Type**: `event_type` (<name>, `select_multiple`, required: no)
Select the type of event your group is attending (Fall Festival, Summer Camp, Winter Wonderland).
* **Age Range**: `age_range` (<name>, `text`, required: no)
Enter the age range of the group (if applicable).
* **Email**: `email` (<name>, `email`, required: no)
Enter a contact email for your group.
* **Phone**: `phone` (<name>, `text`, required: no)
Enter a contact phone number for your group.
* **Comments**: `comments` (<name>, `text`, required: no)
Provide any additional comments or preferences your group would like to share.
* **Event Date**: `event_date` (<name>, `date`, required: no)
Enter the date of the event your group is attending.
* **Event Time**: `event_time` (<name>, `time`, required: no)
Enter the time of the event your group is attending.
* **Event Note**: `event_note` (<name>, `note`, required: no)
Provide any additional event-specific notes or details.
* **Submission**: `submission` (<name>, `text`, required: no)
Please use this field to submit any other relevant information about your group.
