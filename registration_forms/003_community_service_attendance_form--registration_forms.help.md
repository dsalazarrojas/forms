# Community Service Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who have attended a community service event. It helps event organizers track attendance, gather feedback, and evaluate the success of the event.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through the form and ensure you have all the necessary information before starting to fill it out.
- Fill out the form completely and accurately.
- Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter your name as it appears on your official documentation (e.g., ID, license, passport).
- **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, **required**): Enter your email address associated with your official documentation.
- **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, `text`, **required**): Enter your contact number (phone number or other identifier).
- **Registration Status** (`registration_status`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the following options:
  - Pre-registered: If you pre-registered for the event.
  - Walk-in: If you attended the event without pre-registering.
  - Returning Volunteer: If this is not your first time volunteering with us.
- **Event Name** (`event_name`, `text`, **required**): Enter the name of the event you attended.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, **required**): Enter the date of the event you attended.
- **Sign-In Time** (`sign_in_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time you signed in for the event.
- **Sign-Out Time** (`sign_out_time`, `time`, **required**): Enter the time you signed out for the event.
- **Total Minutes Completed** (`total_minutes`, `number`, **required**): Enter the total number of minutes you volunteered for the event (whole numbers only).
- **School or Organization Name** (`organization_affiliation`, `text`, **optional**): If applicable, enter the name of the school or organization you are affiliated with.
- **How did you hear about this event?** (`heard_about_us`, `select_one`, **optional**): Select one of the following options:
  - Social Media
  - Word of Mouth
  - Website
  - Newsletter
  - Flyer
  - Other (please specify)
- **What other types of service interest you?** (`areas_of_interest`, `select_multiple`, **optional**): Select all that apply:
  - Environmental
  - Youth Mentoring
  - Food Security
  - Senior Care
  - Animal Welfare
  - Disaster Relief
- **Feedback or Comments** (`feedback_comments`, `text`, **optional**): Enter any comments or feedback you have about the event.
- **Would you like to volunteer with us again?** (`volunteer_again`, `select_one`, **required**): Select one of the following options:
  - Yes
  - No
  - Maybe

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your answers before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
