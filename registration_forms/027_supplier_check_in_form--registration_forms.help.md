# Supplier Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Supplier Check In Form is used to record and track supplier information for deliveries, pickups, or other interactions. This form helps logistics and administrative teams ensure accurate and efficient communication with suppliers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the supplier company name in the "Supplier Company" field.
2. Provide the contact name for the supplier in the "Contact Name" field.
3. Enter the contact email for the supplier in the "Contact Email" field.
4. Enter the delivery details (e.g., order number, invoice number) in the "Delivery Details" field.
5. Select the delivery date using the calendar icon next to the "Delivery Date" field.
6. Choose the delivery time using the clock icon next to the "Delivery Time" field.
7. Enter the delivery address in the "Delivery Address" field.
8. Enter the supplier's phone number in the "Delivery Phone" field.
9. Provide any additional delivery notes in the "Delivery Note" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Supplier Company** (`supplier_company`, text, optional): Enter the name of the supplier company.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the contact person at the supplier company.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the contact person at the supplier company. Please ensure this is a valid email address.
* **Delivery Details** (`delivery_details`, text, optional): Enter any relevant details about the delivery, such as order number or invoice number.
* **Delivery Date** (`delivery_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the delivery using the calendar icon. Please ensure it's a valid date in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Delivery Time** (`delivery_time`, time, optional): Choose the time of the delivery using the clock icon. Please ensure it's a valid time in the format HH:MM AM/PM.
* **Delivery Address** (`delivery_address`, text, optional): Enter the delivery address.
* **Delivery Phone** (`delivery_phone`, text, optional): Enter the supplier's phone number.
* **Delivery Note** (`delivery_note`, text, optional): Enter any additional delivery notes or comments.

## Tips
* Ensure you have entered all required fields for a successful submission.
* If you're unsure about a field, please consult your supervisor or logistics team.
