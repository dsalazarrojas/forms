# Ministry Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for ministry registration to capture essential information about a religious organization.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the required fields. If a field is not marked as required, you can leave it blank.
- Enter the title of the form in the "Ministry Registration Form" field.
- Indicate the church activity status in the "church_activity" field.
- Enter the details of the ministry in the "ministry_details" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Ministry Registration Form** (`title`, required): Enter the title of the registration form to be used for identification purposes.
* **Church Activity** (`church_activity`, required): Indicate whether the church is active or not.
* **Religious Group** (`religious_group`, required): Enter the name of the religious group.
* **Ministry Details** (`ministry_details`, required): Enter a brief description of the ministry. 

## Tips
- Make sure to complete all the required fields before submitting the form.
- Please provide accurate and truthful information in the form fields.
- If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us for support.
