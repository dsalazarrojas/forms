# emergency_medicine_data_analytics_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for individuals or organizations to register for emergency medicine data analytics events, such as workshops or conferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration form name in the field labeled "registration_form_name".
2. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
3. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "contact_phone".
4. Select the date of the event you are registering for in the field labeled "registration_date".
5. Select the time of the event you are registering for in the field labeled "registration_time".
6. Enter the name of the speaker in the field labeled "name_of_speaker".
7. Enter the job title of the speaker in the field labeled "job_title".
8. Enter the name of the organization the speaker is affiliated with in the field labeled "employer".
9. Enter the address of the organization the speaker is affiliated with in the field labeled "organization".
10. Enter the city of the speaker's location in the field labeled "city".
11. Enter the state of the speaker's location in the field labeled "state".
12. Enter the zip code of the speaker's location in the field labeled "zip".
13. Enter the country of the speaker's location in the field labeled "country".
14. Select the type of event you are registering for in the field labeled "registration_form_type".
15. Enter the number of people attending the event in the field labeled "number_of_people_attending".
16. Enter the names of attendees in the field labeled "names_of_attendees".
17. Enter the name of co-presenters in the field labeled "names_of_co_presenters".
18. Enter the address of co-presenters in the field labeled "addresses_of_co_presenters".
19. Enter the organization of co-presenters in the field labeled "organization_of_co_presenters".
20. Enter the job title of co-presenters in the field labeled "job_titles_of_co_presenters".
21. Enter the contact phone number of co-presenters in the field labeled "contact_phones_of_co_presenters".
22. Enter the contact email of co-presenters in the field labeled "contact_emails_of_co_presenters".
23. Enter the contact address of co-presenters in the field labeled "contact_addresses_of_co_presenters".
24. Enter a brief bio of co-presenters in the field labeled "bio_of_co_presenters".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **registration_form_name** (`text`, required): Please enter your registration form name for the event.
* **email** (`email`, optional): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **contact_phone** (`text`, optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **registration_date** (`date`, optional): Select the date of the event you are registering for.
* **registration_time** (`time`, optional): Select the time of the event you are registering for.
* **name_of_speaker** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the speaker.
* **job_title** (`text`, optional): Enter the job title of the speaker.
* **employer** (`text`, required): Enter the name of the organization the speaker is affiliated with.
* **organization** (`text`, required): Enter the address of the organization the speaker is affiliated with.
* **city** (`text`, optional): Enter the city of the speaker's location.
* **state** (`text`, optional): Enter the state of the speaker's location.
* **zip** (`number`, optional): Enter the zip code of the speaker's location.
* **country** (`text`, optional): Enter the country of the speaker's location.
* **registration_form_type** (`select_one`, required): Select the type of event you are registering for (Workshop or Conference).
* **number_of_people_attending** (`number`, optional): Enter the number of people attending the event.
* **names_of_attendees** (`text`, optional): Enter the names of attendees.
* **names_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the name of co-presenters.
* **addresses_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the address of co-presenters.
* **organization_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the organization of co-presenters.
* **job_titles_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the job title of co-presenters.
* **contact_phones_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact phone number of co-presenters.
* **contact_emails_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact email of co-presenters.
* **contact_addresses_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter the contact address of co-presenters.
* **bio_of_co_presenters** (`text`, optional): Enter a brief bio of co-presenters.
