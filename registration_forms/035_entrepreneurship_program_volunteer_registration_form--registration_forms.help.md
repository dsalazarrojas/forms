# Entrepreneurship Program Volunteer Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals interested in participating as volunteers in the Entrepreneurship Program. It collects necessary information to manage and coordinate volunteer efforts effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required fields (indicated by an asterisk*) with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Choose your skills and interests from the provided options.
3. Confirm your availability and willingness to participate in volunteer initiatives.
4. Click the "Submit" button to send your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Organization Name (Organization Name)**: Enter the name of the organization you're representing or affiliated with.
* **First Name (First Name)**: Type in your first name.
* **Last Name (Last Name)**: Type in your last name.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number for easier communication.
* **Availability (Availability)**: Select a date when you are available to volunteer.
* **Skills (Skills)**: Choose one or multiple skills from the list (Accounting, Business Development, Business Strategy, Marketing, Product Development, Product Management, Sales, User Experience).
* **Interests (Interests)**: Select one interest that best represents your motivations for participating in the program (Entrepreneurship, Community Development, Education, Environmental, Health, Technology, Arts, Business, Social Impact, Community Engagement, Financial, Product, Marketing).
* **Volunteer Initiatives (Volunteer Initiatives)**: Confirm whether you're willing to participate in volunteer initiatives (Yes, No).
* **Submit**: Review your information and submit your registration. This action will send your application to the program's administrators for review and processing.

## Tips
- Ensure your email address and phone number are accurate and up-to-date.
- Be realistic when selecting your availability dates.
- Choose skills and interests that accurately represent your abilities and motivations.
- Review your submission before clicking the "Submit" button to ensure accuracy and completeness.
