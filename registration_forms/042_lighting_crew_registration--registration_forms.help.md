# Lighting Crew Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register individuals or companies who are part of a lighting crew for event or project management purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your basic contact information, including your name, email, phone number, and a contact name and email (if applicable).
2. Select your level of experience in the lighting industry (e.g., 1-5 years, 5-15 years, or 20-25 years).
3. Choose the roles you are skilled in (e.g., lighting designer, lighting supervisor, electrician, or grip).
4. Select your availability for work (e.g., weeknights, weekends, anytime, or flexible).
5. Add any additional notes or information about yourself or your company.
6. Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
7. Enter the website of your company (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, required: false): Enter the name of the contact person for your company (if you're registering for a company).
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, required: false): Enter the email address of the contact person for your company (if you're registering for a company).
* **Company** (`company`, required: false): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).
* **Website** (`website`, required: false): Enter the website of your company (if applicable).
* **User Info** (`user_info`, required: false): Enter a brief description of yourself or your company.
* **Experience** (`experience`, required: false): Select your level of experience in the lighting industry.
* **Role** (`role`, required: false): Choose the roles you are skilled in.
* **Availability** (`availability`, required: false): Select your availability for work.
* **Note** (`note`, required: false): Add any additional notes or information about yourself or your company.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the contact person for your company (if you're registering for a company).
