# Children's Art Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Children's Art Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect necessary information from students and their parents or guardians to create a tailored workshop experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the student's first name in the "Student First Name" field.
2. Fill in the student's last name in the "Student Last Name" field.
3. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
4. Select the student's age group from the "Age Group" dropdown menu.
5. Fill in the student's current school grade in the "Current School Grade" field.
6. Fill in the parent or guardian's name in the "Parent or Guardian Name" field.
7. Enter the parent or guardian's email address in the "Parent Email Address" field.
8. Fill in the parent or guardian's phone number in the "Parent Phone Number" field.
9. Enter the street address in the "Street Address" field.
10. Fill in the city in the "City" field.
11. Select the state from the dropdown menu in the "State" field.
12. Fill in the zip code in the "Zip Code" field.
13. Select the workshop type that the student is interested in from the "Which workshop are you interested in" dropdown menu.
14. Choose the student's experience level from the "Student art experience level" dropdown menu.
15. Select the preferred workshop session from the "Preferred workshop session" dropdown menu.
16. Enter the preferred start date of the workshop in the "Preferred start date" field using the MM/DD/YYYY format.
17. For students with special interests, select all that apply in the "What types of art interest your child" field.
18. Answer "Yes" or "No" to "Does the student have any special needs or accommodations?".
19. If the student has special needs, describe them in the "Please describe any special needs" field.
20. If the student has any material allergies or sensitivities, fill in the "Any material allergies or sensitivities" field.
21. Finally, answer "Yes" or "No" to "I agree to the workshop terms and conditions".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student First Name** (Student First Name, text, required): Fill in the student's first name.
* **Student Last Name** (Student Last Name, text, required): Fill in the student's last name.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required): Enter the student's date of birth in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 01/01/1990).
* **Age Group** (Age Group, select_one, required): Select the student's age group from the dropdown menu.
* **Current School Grade** (Current School Grade, text, required): Fill in the student's current school grade.
* **Parent or Guardian Name** (Parent or Guardian Name, text, required): Fill in the parent or guardian's name.
* **Parent Email Address** (Parent Email Address, email, required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone Number** (Parent Phone Number, text, required): Fill in the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Street Address** (Street Address, text, required): Fill in the street address.
* **City** (City, text, required): Fill in the city.
* **State** (State, text, required): Select the state from the dropdown menu.
* **Zip Code** (Zip Code, text, required): Fill in the zip code.
* **Which workshop are you interested in** (Which workshop are you interested in, select_one, required): Select the workshop type that the student is interested in from the dropdown menu.
* **Student art experience level** (Student art experience level, select_one, required): Choose the student's experience level from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred workshop session** (Preferred workshop session, select_one, required): Select the preferred workshop session from the dropdown menu.
* **Preferred start date** (Preferred start date, date, required): Enter the preferred start date of the workshop in the MM/DD/YYYY format (e.g., 02/01/2022).
* **What types of art interest your child** (What types of art interest your child, select_multiple, required): Select all that apply for the student's special interests.
* **Does the student have any special needs or accommodations?** (Does the student have any special needs or accommodations?, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" or "No".
* **Please describe any special needs** (Please describe any special needs, text, required): If the student has special needs, describe them in this field.
* **Any material allergies or sensitivities** (Any material allergies or sensitivities, text, required): Fill in any material allergies or sensitivities the student may have.
* **I agree to the workshop terms and conditions** (I agree to the workshop terms and conditions, select_one, required): Answer "Yes" or "No".
