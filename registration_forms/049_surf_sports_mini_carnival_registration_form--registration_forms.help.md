# surf_sports_mini_carnival_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The surf sports mini-carnival registration form is designed to collect essential information from attendees to provide them with a personalized experience. To complete this form, please follow the steps outlined below.

## How To Complete This Form
- Begin by filling in your first name, last name, and date of birth in the corresponding fields.
- Choose the type of event you would like to attend by selecting one of the options in the "Event Type" field.
- Enter your phone number and email address for contact purposes.
- Provide the contact details of your emergency contact person, including their first and last name, phone number, and email address.
- For each event, select the corresponding option to indicate your preference.
- Confirm your age and gender to ensure we can cater to your needs.
- Finally, submit your registration and review the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter your date of birth.
* **Event Type** (select_one): Choose the type of event you would like to attend.
* **Phone Number** (text): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Email** (email): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (text): Enter the first name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (text): Enter the last name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone Number** (text): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (email): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Guardian First Name** (text): Enter the first name of your guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Last Name** (text): Enter the last name of your guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Phone Number** (text): Enter the phone number of your guardian (if applicable).
* **Guardian Email** (email): Enter the email address of your guardian (if applicable).
* **Event 1** (select_one): Select the event you would like to attend (if applicable).
* **Event 2** (select_one): Select the event you would like to attend (if applicable).
* **Event 3** (select_one): Select the event you would like to attend (if applicable).
* **Event 4** (select_one): Select the event you would like to attend (if applicable).
* **Terms and Conditions** (note): Review and confirm that you have read the terms and conditions.
* **Submit** (text): Submit your registration to complete the process.
* **Confirm** (select_multiple): Confirm that you have reviewed and understand the terms and conditions.
* **Age** (number): Enter your age.
* **Gender** (select_one): Select your gender.
* **Height** (number): Enter your height (if applicable).
* **Address** (text): Enter your address (if applicable).
