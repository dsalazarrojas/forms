# Join A Regional Communication Group - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect your information to create a profile for a regional communication group. By filling out this form, you will be able to connect with others in your area who share similar interests and goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required fields below. If you need help or have questions, contact your regional representative.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address where you can be contacted by group members.
* **Region** (`region`, select_one, optional): Select the region where you live or are located.
	+ Options: Yes/No
		- Yes: I live or work in the selected region.
		- No: I do not live or work in the selected region.
* **Platform** (`platform`, select_multiple, optional): Select the platforms you are comfortable communicating on.
	+ Options: Yes/No
		- Yes: I am comfortable with this platform.
		- No: I am not comfortable with this platform.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Tips** 
* Make sure to use the correct format for your date of birth (mm/dd/yyyy).
* If you are unsure of your platform preferences, try selecting all the available options.
Note: This form is for new users; if you are already a member of a regional communication group, you can contact your regional representative for assistance.
