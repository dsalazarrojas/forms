# Vendor Booth Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Booth Subscription Form is designed for vendors to reserve a booth at a specific event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the name of the event you are interested in participating in.
2. Select the date of the event.
3. Enter your name or company name.
4. Enter the booth number you are interested in reserving.
5. Provide a brief description of your company or services offered.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the event you are interested in participating in.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event from the provided calendar or enter a specific date in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Vendor Name** (`vendor_name`, text, optional): Enter your name or company name as it will be displayed on your booth.
* **Booth Number** (`booth_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of the booth you are interested in reserving, if applicable.
* **Description** (`description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of your company or services offered.
* **Vendor Booth Reservation** (`vendor_booth_reservation`, text, optional): Enter any other details you would like to note about your reservation.

## Tips
* Please make sure to enter accurate information as it will be used for event planning and communication purposes.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, please include them in the description field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please do not hesitate to contact the event organizers for clarification.
