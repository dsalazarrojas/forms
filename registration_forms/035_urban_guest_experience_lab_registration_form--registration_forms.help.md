# Urban Guest Experience Lab Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Urban Guest Experience Lab Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from guests who want to register for the Urban Guest Experience Lab. This form aims to gather basic details about the guests, ensuring a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:
- Start by filling in your information on the **first_page** field, where you can provide any relevant details you'd like to share.
- Move on to the **guest_info** field, where you can provide more information about your experience with the Urban Guest Experience Lab.
- Next, fill in the **guest_details** field with any additional details you'd like to share about your guest experience.
- Provide your contact information in the **contact_info** field, including any necessary contact details.
- Finally, submit your completed form in the **submission** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_page**: This is the first page of the form. You can provide any initial details you'd like to share about your registration.
* **guest_info**: This field is used to capture more information about your experience with the Urban Guest Experience Lab. Please be as detailed as possible.
* **guest_details**: This field is for any additional information about your guest experience that you'd like to share with us.
* **contact_info**: Provide your contact information, including your name, email address, and any other relevant details.
* **submission**: This is the final step to submit your registration form. Ensure that all fields are filled in before submitting.
