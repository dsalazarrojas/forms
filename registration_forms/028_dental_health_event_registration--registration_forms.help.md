# Dental Health Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dental Health Event Registration form is a tool for gathering information from attendees and participants of dental health events such as conferences, seminars, or workshops. This guide will walk you through the process of completing the form to ensure you provide accurate and relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Read each field carefully** and answer accordingly.
2. **Provide general information** about yourself and your attendance in the first field (`General Info`).
3. **Provide your contact details** in the second field (`Contact Info`).
4. **Enter event-specific information** about the event you are registering for.
5. **List your attendees** if you are registering for yourself and others.
6. **Submit your registration** to complete the process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **General Info (`General Info`, text, optional): Provide your general information such as your name, affiliation, or title.
- **Contact Info (`Contact Info`, text, optional): Enter your email address, phone number, or any other contact method you'd like to use for future communications.
- **Event Info (`Event Info`, text, optional): Describe the event you are registering for, including date, location, and any unique identifier if applicable.
- **Attendee Info (`Attendee Info`, text, optional): List the names and contact details of your colleagues or team members who will be attending the event alongside you.
