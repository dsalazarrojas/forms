# Pre Arrival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pre Arrival Registration Form is designed to collect information about individuals visiting your establishment. This form helps gather necessary details for efficient visitor management and ensures a smooth arrival experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all fields you feel are relevant to your visit. This form has four sections: User Information, Contact Information, Travel Information, and Arrival Information.
2. In User Information, provide a brief description about yourself.
3. In Contact Information, enter any contact details you'd like to share.
4. In Travel Information, specify any travel-related specifics you have.
5. In Arrival Information, enter your planned arrival details.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): A short description about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Share your contact details if you'd like.
* **Travel Information** (`travel_info`, text, optional): Specify any travel-related specifics, such as flight numbers or arrival methods.
* **Arrival Information** (`arrival_info`, text, optional): Enter your planned arrival details, including estimated time and any additional information necessary for a smooth arrival.

## Tips
* If you have any special requirements or requests, such as accessibility needs or dietary restrictions, please specify them in the Arrival Information field.
* If you're unsure about any field, leave it blank and provide the necessary information in the Arrival Information field.
* This form is a general guide to help you provide necessary information for your visit. Please be as detailed as possible in each field to ensure a pleasant and efficient experience.
