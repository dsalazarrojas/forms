# Volunteer Check In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Volunteer Check In Form is designed to collect information from volunteers checking in for events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate options for the "check-in status" fields.
2. Enter your contact information in the "contact number" field.
3. Add any additional notes about your check-in in the "notes" field.
4. Submit the form to finalize your check-in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form ID** (`form id`, number, optional): This field is used to track the form's unique identifier.
* **Volunteer Name** (`volunteer name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as it appears on your ID or badge.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Event Date** (`event date`, date, optional): Select the date of the event you are checking in for.
* **Event Time** (`event time`, time, optional): Select the time of the event you are checking in for.
* **Check In Time** (`check in time`, number, optional): This field is intended to track the time you check in. However, its meaning might be ambiguous without further clarification.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, optional): Please select the specific role you are playing during the event (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional shifts you will be working during the event (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or information about your check-in.
* **Contact Number** (`contact number`, text, optional): Enter your contact number for further communication.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, consult with event staff or management.
* Review the form before submitting it to ensure all required information is provided.
