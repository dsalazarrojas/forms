# Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative Registration form helps us understand your background, interests, and goals to tailor the program's activities and resources to your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form carefully and answer the questions honestly and accurately.
2. Select the relevant options for each field.
3. Fill in the required fields (indicated by the required label).
4. Double-check your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form (form_data_1)**: Please enter a brief description of your project or idea related to pedagogical storytelling. This can be a project you're currently working on or an idea you'd like to explore. Keep it concise, about 100 words or less.
- **Select Your Roles (roles)**: Choose one or more of the following roles:
	* Educator: If you're a teacher, professor, or instructor, choose this option.
	* Facilitator: If you're a workshop leader, coach, or facilitator, choose this option.
	* Both: If you identify as both an educator and facilitator, choose this option.
- **What Experience Do You Have (experience)**: Briefly describe your experience in pedagogical storytelling, including any relevant training, certifications, or experience working with students, teachers, or facilitators.
- **What Motivates You (motivations)**: Describe what motivates you to participate in the Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative. This can be to improve your teaching practice, develop new skills, or connect with others in the field.
- **Organize Your Participants (participants)**: If you're planning to work with a group, indicate how many participants you expect to engage with. Choose one of the following options:
	* 3-5
	* 6-10
	* 11-20
	* None
- **Which Workshop or Community Do You Want To Join? (workshop_or_community)**: Select the Pedagogical Storytelling Initiative or another community that aligns with your interests and goals.
- **Email (email)**: Enter your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you about program updates and activities.
- **Phone (phone)**: Enter your phone number, which we'll use to contact you in case of emergency or if needed to discuss your project. 

## Tips
- Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date contact information.
- If you're unsure about any field, contact us at [insert contact info]. We'll be happy to assist you.
- We'll review your application and get back to you soon after submission.
