# Worship Conference Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information for attendees or participants of a worship conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **User** (username or handle) in the "User" field.
2. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field.
3. Select your **Registrant Type** (Single or Multiple) from the drop-down menu.
4. Enter your **Name** in the "Name" field.
5. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
6. List the **Registrants** (individuals or groups attending with you) in the "Registrants" field.
7. If necessary, enter a **Message** in the "Message" field.
8. Select the **Date** of your attendance in the "Date" field.
9. Select the **Time** of your attendance in the "Time" field.
10. Select the **Submitted By** field from the drop-down menu.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User** (User, text, required/optional): Enter your username or handle that you will use to log in to the system.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that you want to use for communication.
* **Registrant Type** (Registrant-type, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you are a single or multiple registrant.
* **Name** (name, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Phone** (phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Registrants** (registrants, select_multiple, required/optional): List the individuals or groups attending with you. You can select multiple names separated by commas.
* **Message** (message, note, required/optional): If you have any special requests or comments, enter them here.
* **Date** (date, date, required/optional): Select the date you plan to attend the conference.
* **Time** (time, time, required/optional): Select the time you plan to attend the conference.
* **Submitted By** (submitted-by, select_one, required/optional): Select who submitted this registration (e.g., chatjimmy or other).

Note that some fields have optional or required properties depending on the specific requirements of the conference organizers. Ensure you provide accurate information to facilitate smooth communication and organization. 

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you are unsure about any field, ask a conference organizer for clarification.
- Ensure your registrants list is accurate and up-to-date to avoid errors during the conference.
