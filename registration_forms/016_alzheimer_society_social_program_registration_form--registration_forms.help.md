# Alzheimer Society Social Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for the Alzheimer Society Social Program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your accurate and complete participant information.
2. Select your preferred program session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
3. List any dietary restrictions or allergies.
4. Indicate if you need accessibility support.
5. Check if you grant consent for media release.
5. Add any additional notes or information for the organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant name** (Required): Enter your full name as it appears on your government-issued ID.
* **Contact phone** (Required): Enter your phone number where you can be reached for registration updates or emergency contact.
* **Emergency contact name** (Required): Enter the name of a family member or guardian who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Program session** (Required): Choose your preferred time of day for the program (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Dietary restrictions** (Optional): List any food allergies or restrictions you have.
* **Mobility needs** (Optional): Indicate if you require some level of accessibility support, such as mild or full assistance.
* **Consent for media release** (Optional): Check "Yes" if you grant permission for your photograph to be taken and shared on social media.
* **Additional notes** (Optional): Add any extra information for the organizers to know about you, such as any health conditions or medication you are taking.
