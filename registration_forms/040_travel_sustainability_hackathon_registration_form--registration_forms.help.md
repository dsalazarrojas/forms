# Travel Sustainability Hackathon Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Travel Sustainability Hackathon. Please fill out the form to register for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out the required and optional fields with your relevant information. You can add or modify text as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information**: Enter your personal details to help us get to know you better.
* **Event Information**: Provide event-related information, if any.
* **Travel Information**: Share your travel specifics, if applicable.
* **Sustainability Information**: Input your sustainability-related information.
* **Submit**: Click to submit your registration form.

## Tips
* Make sure to provide accurate and complete information.
* You can add or remove fields as per your needs.
* If you encounter any issues, feel free to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
