# Figurine Pre Order Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Figurine Pre Order Form is designed to collect essential details from customers who want to pre-order a figurine. This form allows users to input their shipping and payment information, as well as provide any additional order notes they may have.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the User Input field with your name and contact information.
2. Provide details about the figurine you're pre-ordering in the Figurine Details field.
3. Select your shipping address in the Shipping Address field.
4. Choose your shipping option in the Shipping Options field.
5. Add any payment information in the Payment Info field, if necessary.
6. Include any order notes you'd like to add in the Order Notes field.
7. Confirm your order by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the Confirm Order field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (text)** (`user_input`, text, optional): Enter your name and contact information to contact us with any questions or concerns.
* **Figurine Details (text)** (`figurine_details`, text, optional): Describe the figurine you're pre-ordering, including any specific details or requests you have.
* **Shipping Address (text)** (`shipping_address`, text, optional): Enter your shipping address to ensure your figurine is delivered correctly.
* **Shipping Options (select_one)** (`shipping_option`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you'd like to ship your figurine via standard or express shipping.
* **Payment Info (text)** (`payment_info`, text, optional): If you'd like to specify a payment method or provide payment information, enter it here.
* **Order Notes (note)** (`order_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or instructions for our team to follow when processing your order.
* **Confirm Order (select_one)** (`confirm_order`, select_one, optional): Confirm that you'd like to place your order.
* **User Email (email)** (`user_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about your order.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays or issues with your order.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to contact us.
* Our team will review and process your order carefully, and we'll be in touch with you soon.
