# Community Sports Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for a community sports event. It captures essential information required for event management, medical preparedness, and liability purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Review the form carefully to understand the required fields and their purpose.
2. Fill in your personal details in the first section.
3. Select your event information in the second section.
4. Provide medical information, if applicable, to ensure your safety and well-being.
5. Review and agree to the waiver and release of liability statement.
6. Sign the form to confirm your participation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information**
	+ **First Name** (Required): Your first name as per your identity card or passport.
	+ **Last Name** (Required): Your last name as per your identity card or passport.
	+ **Date of Birth** (Required): Your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY.
	+ **Email Address** (Required): A valid email address for event updates.
	+ **Phone Number** (Required): Your contact phone number.
	+ **Street Address** (Required): Your home address.
	+ **City** (Required): The city you reside in.
	+ **State** (Required): The state you reside in.
	+ **ZIP Code** (Required): Your zip code.
	+ **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (Required): The name and phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Event Information**
	+ **Sports Event** (Required): Select the type of sports event you are participating in (e.g., 5K Run, Basketball Tournament, etc.).
* **Medical Information**
	+ **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: If you have any medical conditions or allergies, please list them here for our knowledge.
* **Waiver and Release**
	+ **I Understand There Are Risks** (Required): Confirm that you understand the risks involved in participating in the event.
	+ **I Release the Organization from Liability** (Required): Confirm that you release the organization from liability in case of any accidents or injuries.
	+ **I Consent to Emergency Medical Treatment** (Required): Confirm that you consent to emergency medical treatment if needed.
	+ **Photo and Video Consent** (Required): Confirm that you consent to the use of your photo and video in event media.
* **Additional Information**
	+ **T-Shirt Size** (Optional): Select your preferred t-shirt size for event merchandise.
	+ **Volunteer Interest** (Optional): If you are interested in volunteering for the event, select "Yes".
* **Signature**
	+ **Participant Signature** (Required): Sign your full name as proof of your participation.
	+ **Date** (Required): The date you sign this form.

Remember to review the form carefully and answer all required questions honestly to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone involved. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.
