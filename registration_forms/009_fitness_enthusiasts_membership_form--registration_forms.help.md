# Fitness Enthusiasts Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members for the purpose of registering them for a fitness-related community or program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details, including first name and last name.
2. Enter your email address to ensure we can contact you.
3. Provide your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
4. Select your membership type (Personal, Corporate, Group, or Family).
5. Choose your current fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, or Expert).
6. Enter your emergency contact's name and select their relationship to you (Parent, Spouse, Friend, or Family).
7. Provide your emergency contact's phone number.
8. Enter your emergency contact's email address (optional).
9. Select the level of your relationship with your emergency contact (optional).
10. Provide any medical notes or conditions (optional).
11. List any allergies or medical conditions you have (optional).
12. Enter the name of your emergency contact 2 (optional).
13. Provide your emergency contact 2's phone number (optional).
14. Enter your emergency contact 2's email address (optional).
15. Select the level of your relationship with your emergency contact 2 (optional).
16. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
17. Select your payment plan (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
18. Choose how you would like to make payments (Bank, Credit, or PayPal).
19. Choose your membership plan (Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually).
