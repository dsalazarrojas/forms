# hiking_club_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hiking_club_enrollment_form is designed to collect contact information and schedule preferences from prospective members of the hiking club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose a date for the event from the calendar in the "Date" field.
6. Choose a time for the event from the dropdown menu in the "Time" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name to help us get to know you better.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name to complete your profile.

### Email
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you about club events and updates.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can reach you about any last-minute event changes or reminders.

### Date
* **Date** (`Date`, date, required/optional): Choose a date for the event you'd like to attend from the calendar.

### Time
* **Time** (`Time`, time, required/optional): Choose a time slot from the dropdown menu for the event you'd like to attend.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your information accurately to ensure we can contact you effectively.
* If you have any special requests or restrictions, please let us know in the comments section.
