# Camp Participant Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for registering campers and their guardians for a camp session. It is essential to complete the form accurately to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form pages to understand what information is required on each page.
2. Complete each page carefully, following the instructions and guidelines provided.
3. Ensure you provide all required information to avoid incomplete registrations.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Camper Full Name** (`participant_full_name_reg`, Text, Required): This field is for entering the camper's full name.
* **Age (At time of camp)** (`participant_age_reg`, Number, Required): Please enter the camper's age at the time of the camp session.
* **Gender** (`participant_gender_reg`, Select One, Required): Choose the camper's gender to help with cabin and group assignments.
* **Parent or Guardian Full Name** (`primary_guardian_name_reg`, Text, Required): Enter the full name of the parent or guardian.
* **Contact Email Address** (`guardian_email_reg`, Email, Required): Enter the parent or guardian's email address.
* **Primary Phone Number** (`guardian_phone_reg`, Text, Required): Enter the parent or guardian's phone number.
* **Session Preferences** (`session_preferences_header_reg`, Note, Not Required): This page is for selecting the camp session and any additional preferences.
* **Select Your Session** (`camp_session_choice_reg`, Select One, Required): Choose the preferred camp session from the options provided.
* **T-shirt Size** (`camper_t_shirt_size_reg`, Select One, Required): Select the preferred T-shirt size for the camper.
* **Medical Allergies or Conditions** (`medical_allergies_concerns_reg`, Text, Required): If the camper has any medical allergies or conditions, please list them here. If none, type "None".
* **Swimming Proficiency** (`swimming_ability_reg`, Select One, Required): Choose the camper's swimming ability level.
* **Photo and Media Release** (`photo_release_consent_reg`, Select One, Required): Choose whether to give permission for the camp to use photos of the camper for promotional purposes.
* **Liability Waiver Acknowledgment** (`participation_waiver_acknowledgement_reg`, Select One, Required): Acknowledge that you have signed the separate liability waiver.
* **Preferred Payment Plan** (`payment_plan_choice_reg`, Select One, Required): Choose the preferred payment plan for the registration.
* **Registration Date** (`participant_reg_timestamp`, Date, Required): This field is automatically populated with the registration date.
