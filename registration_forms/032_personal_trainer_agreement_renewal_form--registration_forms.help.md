# Personal Trainer Agreement Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Trainer Agreement Renewal Form is used to renew or modify an existing agreement between a personal trainer and a client. This form helps trainers and clients update their contract terms, payment details, and other relevant information in a structured and organized way.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in the form's title, which confirms the purpose of the form.
2. Fill in the **Trainer Info** section with your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about yourself as a trainer.
3. Provide your **Client Info** in the designated field, including their name, contact details, and any other relevant information about the client.
4. Review and update the **Contract Terms** section to reflect any changes to the agreement, such as new payment terms or conditions.
5. Specify the **Payment Terms** if applicable, including any changes to payment schedules or methods.
6. Date and sign the **Signature** field to confirm your and your client's agreement to the updated terms.
7. Choose a **Renewal Date** for the updated agreement, ensuring it aligns with your contract expiration date.
8. Enter the new **Contract Expiration Date** to reflect the updated term of the agreement.
9. Sign the form with your **Trainer Signature** to confirm acceptance of the updated terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title**: Enter the name of the form, confirming its purpose.
* **Trainer Info**: Your name, contact details, and any other relevant information about you as a trainer. (Required)
* **Client Info**: Your client's name, contact details, and any other relevant information about them. (Required)
* **Contract Terms**: Review and update any changes to the agreement, including new payment terms or conditions. (Optional)
* **Payment Terms**: Specify any changes to payment schedules or methods, if applicable. (Optional)
* **Signature**: Date and sign to confirm your and your client's agreement to the updated terms. (Required)
* **Renewal Date**: Choose a new date for the updated agreement, ensuring it aligns with your contract expiration date. (Optional)
* **Contract Expiration Date**: Enter the new term of the agreement. (Required)
* **Trainer Signature**: Sign with your signature to confirm acceptance of the updated terms. (Required)

## Tips

* Ensure all fields are filled out accurately and honestly to maintain a smooth and professional relationship with your clients.
* Review and update the contract terms regularly to reflect any changes or updates to your services or payment terms.
* Store the completed form in a secure location for future reference and to maintain a record of agreements.
