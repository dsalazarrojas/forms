# Restaurant Management Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for restaurant owners or management staff who want to register for the workshop. It helps the organizers collect contact and preference information from interested participants and ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Select the correct option for "Is this a public workshop?" and "Would you like to participate?" to ensure you are eligible to attend.
2. Fill in your name, email address, and phone number in the "Participant Name," "Email," and "Phone" fields.
3. Enter your date of birth in the "Date Of Birth" field.
4. Indicate the type of workshop you are interested in and select a date and time for the workshop you'd like to attend.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Is this a public workshop?** (`restaurant_management_workshop_registration_form`, select_one, required):
	+ This question helps us ensure that you are aware of the workshop's public status. Select "Yes" if you are registering for a public workshop and "No" otherwise.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required):
	+ This is your name as it appears on our records. Please enter your full name as you want to be addressed.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required):
	+ This is the email address we will use to contact you about workshop details and updates.
* **Date Of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required):
	+ We need to know your date of birth to verify your eligibility and categorize you in our registration records.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required):
	+ Please enter your phone number so we can contact you if necessary.
* **Is this a private workshop?** (`workshop_type`, select_multiple, required):
	+ This question helps us understand the type of workshop you are interested in. Select "Yes" if this is a private workshop and "No" otherwise.
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, date, required):
	+ Select a date for the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, time, required):
	+ Choose a time for the workshop you'd like to attend.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required):
	+ This question helps us understand your role in the restaurant. Select "Yes" if you are a participant in the workshop and "No" otherwise.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your contact and preference information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests, please contact us separately to make arrangements.
- The workshop organizers will be in touch with you via email and phone for further details and updates.
