# operational_compliance_conference_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The operational compliance conference registration form is designed to collect information from participants for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
- To complete this form, follow the steps outlined in this guide.
- Please fill out each section according to the instructions provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration Form (Page 1)** (`Registration Form`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    This is the first page of the form where you will enter your information.
    Please fill in your details as accurately as possible.
- **Participant Information (Page 2)** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    This section is for collecting additional information about you as a participant.
    Provide your details as instructed.
- **Payment Details (Page 3)** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    In this section, you will enter payment information related to your registration.
    Please provide the required details.
- **Session Preferences (Page 4)** (`Session Preferences`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    Choose the sessions you are interested in attending.
    Select the relevant options.
- **Participant Information (Page 5)** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    This section is for collecting more participant details.
    Enter the required information.
- **Payment Details (Page 6)** (`Payment Details`, `text`, required/optional: false):
    Enter payment information for your registration.
    Ensure accurate details.

## Tips
- Ensure that you complete each section accurately and thoroughly.
- If you have any doubts or need assistance, please contact form@gic.mx.
- Review your information before submitting the form.
- Double-check that you have filled in all required fields.
- Submit the form once you are confident with the information provided.
