# equipment_asset_sale_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The equipment_asset_sale_registration form is designed to collect information for the sale of equipment or assets, ensuring a smooth and transparent transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter the required seller information, including the seller's name and contact details.
2. Provide a detailed description of the item being sold, including its category and condition.
3. Enter the buyer's information, including their name and contact details.
4. Add any relevant images or descriptions of the item being sold.
5. Specify the payment details, including the sale price and payment method.
6. Confirm the payment date and time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Seller Information (1)**: Enter the seller's name and contact details to facilitate communication and payment processing.
* **Seller Name (2)**: Enter the seller's name, which will be associated with the sale transaction.
* **Item Information (3)**: Provide a detailed description of the item being sold, including its category and condition.
* **Item Description (4)**: Enter a detailed description of the item being sold, highlighting its features and characteristics.
* **Item Price (5)**: Enter the sale price of the item, in a format that allows for easy reading and calculation.
* **Item Category (6)**: Select the category of the item being sold (e.g., 'Yes' for sold or 'No' for not sold).
* **Item Condition (7)**: Specify the condition of the item being sold (e.g., 'Yes' for new or 'No' for used).
* **Buyer Information (8)**: Enter the buyer's name and contact details, to facilitate communication and payment processing.
* **Buyer Name (9)**: Enter the buyer's name, which will be associated with the sale transaction.
* **Buyer Contact (10)**: Enter the buyer's contact details, including their phone number and email address.
* **Item Image (11)**: Upload or link to an image of the item being sold, to provide visual representation.
* **Item Image Description (12)**: Enter a brief description of the item image, highlighting its features and characteristics.
* **Buyer Interest (13)**: Enter any relevant notes or comments from the buyer, regarding the sale transaction.
* **Payment Details (14)**: Enter the payment details, including the sale price and payment method.
* **Sale Price (15)**: Enter the sale price of the item, in a format that allows for easy reading and calculation.
* **Sale Price Currency (16)**: Enter the currency of the sale price, to ensure accurate calculation and representation.
* **Payment Method (17)**: Select the payment method used for the sale transaction (e.g., 'Yes' for cash or 'No' for other payment methods).
* **Payment Date (18)**: Enter the date of the payment, to ensure accurate record-keeping and tracking.
* **Payment Time (19)**: Enter the time of the payment, to ensure accurate record-keeping and tracking.
