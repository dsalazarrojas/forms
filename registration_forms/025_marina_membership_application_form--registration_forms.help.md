# Marina Membership Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marina Membership Application Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the marina membership program. The form is intended to guide users through the application process, ensuring that they provide necessary details for processing their application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Submit" button at the end of each page to proceed to the next one.
2. Fill in the necessary information for each section, providing accurate and valid data.
3. Ensure that you are providing all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
4. Complete all the pages, and finally, submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Membership Details** (Page 1)
	+ *member_since*: This field is not a straightforward question and might be asking for the duration of your membership or when your membership was last updated.
	+ *membership_level*: This is a selection field where you can choose your membership level from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
* **Contact Info** (Page 2)
	+ *contact_person*: Please choose the person responsible for your boat or marina information from the provided options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3).
	+ *email*: This is an optional field, providing your email address for easier communication.
* **Additional Contact Info** (Page 3)
	+ *phone*: Enter your phone number.
	+ *address*: Enter your address.
* **Notes** (Page 4)
	+ *notes*: Enter any additional comments or information about your application.
* **First and Last Name** (Page 5)
	+ *first_name*: Enter your first name.
	+ *last_name*: Enter your last name.
* **Date** (Page 6)
	+ *date*: Enter the date in the required format.
* **Submission** (Page 7)
	+ *submit*: Click this button to submit your application.
