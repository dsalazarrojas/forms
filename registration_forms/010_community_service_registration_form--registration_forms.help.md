# Community Service Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in volunteering for community service. It's a comprehensive form that gathers essential details to ensure a smooth and safe volunteer experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required information in each field, marked as "required" in the form.
2. Ensure you are honest and accurate in your responses.
3. Review your answers carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (2)**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address (3)**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone Number (4)**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth (5)**: Enter your date of birth to confirm age requirements.
* **City (7)**: Enter your current city of residence.
* **Zip or Postal Code (8)**: Enter your city's postal code.
* **When are you available? (9)**: Select all the times that you are available to volunteer. Please be specific if you have availability during weekday or weekend mornings, afternoons, or evenings.
* **Volunteer Type (11)**: Select the category that best describes your volunteer status.
* **Interests (12)**: Select the skills or interests that you have, such as food preparation, gardening, data entry, mentoring, event setup, cleanup, or photography.
* **Background Check Willingness (13)**: Select "Yes" if you are willing to undergo a background check, otherwise select "No".
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone (16)**: Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **I agree to the volunteer terms and conditions (17)**: Select "Yes" to confirm that you agree to the terms and conditions of volunteering with us.
