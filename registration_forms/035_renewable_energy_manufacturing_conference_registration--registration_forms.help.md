# Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register you for the Renewable Energy Manufacturing Conference. Please fill out this form to provide your personal details and preferences to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for you at the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email address in the Email field. Please use your actual email address to ensure you receive important updates from us.
4. Enter your Phone number in the Phone field, if you wish to be contacted by us.
5. Enter your Address in the Address field, if you wish to be contacted at home or office.
6. Choose whether you accept the terms and conditions by selecting the corresponding option in the Consent field.
7. Select your preferred language from the options in the Preferred Language field.
8. If you have any special requests, please specify them in the Special Request field.
9. Let us know about any dietary restrictions you have in the Dietary Restrictions field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your actual email address to receive important updates from us.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by us.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address if you wish to be contacted at home or office.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, optional): Choose whether you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Preferred Language** (`preferred_language`, select_multiple, optional): Select your preferred language from the options provided.
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, text, optional): Specify any special requests you may have.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_one, optional): Select your dietary restrictions, such as Gluten-free, Vegan, or Vegetarian.
