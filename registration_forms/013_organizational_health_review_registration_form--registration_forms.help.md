# organizational_health_review_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from teams about their organizational health review. This form helps teams to provide a comprehensive overview of their current state and can be used for future reference and improvement.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the relevant team information from the Team_Information dropdown.
2. Enter the assessment date in the format of mm/dd/yyyy.
3. Provide your contact information in the Contact_Information field.
4. Enter your contact phone number in the Contact_Phone field.
5. Enter your email address in the Contact_Email field.
6. Add any additional comments in the Additional_Comments field.
7. Fill in the Form_Completed_By field with your name.
8. Fill in the Form_Submitted_By field with your name.
9. Select the team members who are part of your team in the Team_Members field.
10. Enter the submission date in the Submission_Date field.
11. Fill in the Submitted_By field with your name.
12. Enter the time of submission in the Time_of_Submission field.
13. Enter the submission time in the Submitted_At field.
14. Provide your team name in the Team_Name field.
15. Enter the team size in the Team_Size field.
16. Provide the team location in the Team_Location field.
17. Enter the form completion date in the Form_Completion_Date field.
18. Enter your name in the Submitted_By_Name field.
19. Enter your email address in the Submitted_By_Email field.
20. Enter your phone number in the Submitted_By_Phone field.
21. Provide your job title in the Submitted_By_Job_Title field.
22. Provide your department in the Submitted_By_Department field.
23. Provide the location of submission in the Submitted_By_Location field.
24. Provide the name of the team who submitted in the Submitted_By_Team_Name field.
