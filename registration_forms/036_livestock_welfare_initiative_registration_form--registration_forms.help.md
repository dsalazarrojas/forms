# Livestock Welfare Initiative Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from farms and individuals who want to participate in the Livestock Welfare Initiative. The form helps to ensure that the farm or individual is eligible for the services and programs offered by the initiative.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your farm's name and address in the "Farm Name" field.
2. Select the correct animal species from the "Animal Species" dropdown menu.
3. Enter the age of the animals you are registering in the "Animal Age" field.
4. Enter the number of animals you are registering in the "Animal Count" field.
5. Enter your name and contact information in the "Contact Name", "Contact Phone", and "Email Address" fields.
6. Enter your farm's location in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Farm Name** (`farm_name`, text, required): Enter your farm's name as it is registered with the local authorities.
* **Animal Species** (`animal_species`, select_one, optional): Select the species of the animals you are registering (e.g. cattle, pigs, chickens).
* **Animal Age** (`animal_age`, number, optional): Enter the age of the animals you are registering.
* **Animal Count** (`animal_number`, number, optional): Enter the number of animals you are registering.
* **Contact Name** (`contact_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as the person in charge of the farm or animal care.
* **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, optional): Enter your email address for secure contact and communication.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter your farm's location.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely to ensure that you receive the correct information and support from the Livestock Welfare Initiative.
* If you have any issues or questions while filling out the form, please do not hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* This form is designed to be easy to use and complete, so please do not hesitate to ask if you need help or clarification on any of the fields.
