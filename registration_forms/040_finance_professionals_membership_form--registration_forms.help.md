# Finance Professionals Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for finance professionals who wish to register as members of a community or organization. The form collects information about the member's details, professional role, company, and contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Start by entering your member information, including your name and email address.
2. Select your professional role from the list of options provided.
3. Enter your company information, including the name and size.
4. Select your company sector from the list of options provided.
5. Enter your contact information, including your phone number, address, and zip code.
6. Select your membership level.
7. If you wish to continue with the registration process, click the "Continue" button to proceed to the next step.
8. Review your information carefully before clicking the "Save and Exit" button to submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Member Information (Page 1)**
	+ **Name** (`name`, `text`, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your primary phone number.
* **Member Information (Page 2)**
	+ **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter the full name of your company.
	+ **Company Size** (`company_size`, `select_one`, required): Select the size of your company.
	+ **Company Sector** (`company_sector`, `select_one`, required): Select the sector of your company.
* **Professional Information (Page 3)**
	+ **Role** (`role`, `select_one`, optional): Select your professional role from the list of options provided.
	+ **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, optional): Enter your current job title.
* **Contact Information (Page 4)**
	+ **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your physical address.
	+ **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the city of your physical address.
	+ **State** (`state`, `text`, required): Enter the state of your physical address.
	+ **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional information you would like to provide.
* **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your registration information.
* **Cancel** (`cancel`, `text`, optional): If you wish to cancel the registration process, click this button.
* **Back** (`back`, `text`, optional): Return to the previous page.
* **Next** (`next`, `text`, optional): Proceed to the next page.
* **Save and Exit** (`save_and_exit`, `text`, optional): Review and confirm your information before submitting.
* **Continue** (`continue`, `text`, optional): Click this button to proceed to the next step.
* **Previous** (`previous`, `text`, optional): Go back to the previous page.
