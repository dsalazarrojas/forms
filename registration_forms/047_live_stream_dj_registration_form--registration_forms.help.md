# Live Stream DJ Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Live Stream DJ Registration Form is for DJs to register for live music streaming events. It collects information about your DJing details, profile, and availability for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps below:
1. Fill in the form completely, ensuring all required fields are filled.
2. Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your DJing details.
3. Review your form before submission to ensure correctness.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **dj_details**: A brief description about your DJing style or specialty.
	+ Enter a 1-2 paragraph description about your performance or the music you play.
* **dj_profile_picture**: A link to your public profile picture (e.g. Instagram or Facebook).
	+ Provide a link to your social media profile picture or attach a image file.
* **dj_name**: Your full name as a DJ.
	+ Enter your name as you'd like to be recognized as on the event platform.
* **dj_bio**: A brief description about yourself as a DJ.
	+ Write a 1-2 paragraph description about your music style, genre, or experience.
* **dj_email**: Your valid email address.
	+ Enter your real email address for correspondence and event updates.
* **dj_phone**: Your phone number (optional).
	+ Enter your phone number for direct communication with event organizers.
* **dj_genre**: Are you available to play in this genre?
	+ Select 'Yes' if you can play in this genre, 'No' otherwise.
* **dj_stream_format**: What format would you like your stream to be in?
	+ Select 'Yes' for a live stream, 'No' for a recorded stream.
* **dj_stream_schedule**: Schedule of your available time slots for the stream.
	+ Enter the start and end time of your available time slot in 24-hour format.
* **dj_stream_time**: Your preferred start time for the stream.
	+ Enter the start time of your stream in 24-hour format.
* **dj_stream_durations**: How long would you like your stream to be?
	+ Enter the duration of your stream in minutes.
* **dj_confirmation**: A confirmation of your registration.
	+ Enter a message confirming your registration and availability.
* **dj_additional_info**: Any additional information about your stream or performance.
	+ Enter any specific details about your stream or performance.

Note: The form is designed for your convenience and ease of use. If you have any questions or need help, don't hesitate to contact us.
