# Tourist ID Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tourist ID Registration Form is designed for tourists to provide their personal and travel-related information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First Name, Last Name, and Email address in the corresponding fields.
2. Enter your Phone number for contact purposes.
3. Provide your Date of Birth to verify your age.
4. Enter the Country where you are from.
5. Enter the City where you are from.
6. Enter the name of the hotel where you are staying.
7. Enter the name of the tour operator handling your tour.
8. Enter the name of the host or guide accompanying your tour.
9. Select your Check-in Date.
10. Select your Check-out Date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (First Name, text, required/optional): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (Email, email, required/optional): Enter your valid email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, required/optional): Provide your date of birth to verify your age.
* **Country** (Country, text, required/optional): Enter the country where you are from.
* **City** (City, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you are from.
* **Hotel Name** (Hotel Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the hotel where you are staying.
* **Tour Operator Name** (Tour Operator Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the tour operator handling your tour.
* **Host Name** (Host Name, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the host or guide accompanying your tour.
* **Check-in Date** (Check-in Date, date, required/optional): Select the date you will check in to the hotel.
* **Check-out Date** (Check-out Date, date, required/optional): Select the date you will check out of the hotel.
