# esthetician_trade_show_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering attendees, exhibitors, or vendors for an esthetician trade show. It captures necessary information for registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the **First Name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last Name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email** field.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone** field.
5. Select your preferred **Booth Choice** from the dropdown menu.
6. Select all applicable **Payment Method** options using the checkboxes.
7. Select your attendee type from the **Attendee Type** dropdown menu.
8. Enter any **Special Requests** in the text field.
9. Enter any additional **Additional Info** in the text field.
10. Provide any **Comments** in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Booth Choice** (`booth_choice`, select_one, required): Select your preferred booth number.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, required): Select all applicable payment methods.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, select_one, required): Select your attendee type (attendee, exhibitor, or vendor).
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, required/optional): Enter any special requests.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): Enter any comments or additional information. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate information for the **Email** field as it will be used for communication.
* Select all applicable **Payment Method** options to ensure you are prepared for payment.
* Be specific with **Special Requests** to ensure they are met.
* Enter any **Additional Info** that may be relevant to your registration.
* Provide any **Comments** that may be helpful for the organizers.
