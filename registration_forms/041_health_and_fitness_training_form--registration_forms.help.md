# Health And Fitness Training Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information about you to create a personalized health and fitness training program tailored to your specific needs and goals. 

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling in your details in the "Registration Form" section.
- Select one or more training programs that interest you from the "Training Programs" options.
- Choose your training goals from the "Training Goals" options.
- If you have previous training experience, please provide it in the "Training Experience" field. 
- Select the days you are available for training from the "Available Days" options.
- Choose your preferred contact method from the "Preferred Contact Method" options.
- Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact.
- Read and agree to the terms of the client agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (`Registration Form`, text, optional)
  This section is where you can input your basic details such as name, email, etc.
* **Training Programs** (`Training Programs`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select one or more training programs that interest you. This will help us understand your training preferences.
* **Training Goals** (`Training Goals`, select_one, optional)
  Choose your primary training goal. This will help us tailor the program to your specific needs.
* **Training Experience** (`Training Experience`, date, optional)
  If you have previous training experience, please input the date and duration.
* **Available Days** (`Available Days`, select_multiple, optional)
  Select the days you are available for training sessions.
* **Preferred Contact Method** (`Preferred Contact Method`, select_one, optional)
  Choose how you would like to communicate with us. This will ensure we can contact you efficiently.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`Emergency Contact Name`, text, optional)
  Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`Emergency Contact Phone`, text, optional)
  Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Client Agreement** (`Client Agreement`, note, optional)
  Please read and agree to the terms of the agreement.
* **Terms of Agreement** (`Terms of Agreement`, text, optional)
  This is where you will find the terms of the agreement. Please read and understand before agreeing.
* **Relationship with Emergency Contact** (`Relationship with Emergency Contact`, select_one, optional)
  Choose your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **Training Coordinator** (`Training Coordinator`, text, optional)
  This is an optional field for our internal use. Please do not fill this in.

## Tips
- Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
- If you're unsure about any field, please refer to this help guide for clarification.
- Take your time to complete the form carefully, and if you need assistance, don't hesitate to contact us.
