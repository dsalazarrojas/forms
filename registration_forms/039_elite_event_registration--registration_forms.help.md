# Elite Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Elite Event Registration" form is designed to collect necessary details from attendees for the "Elite Event". Completing this form will enable you to provide your information to the event organizers and receive updates, notifications, or other communications related to the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on "First name" and enter your first name.
2. Click on "Last name" and enter your last name.
3. Enter your valid email address in the "Email address" field. Ensure it's a correct email address, as you will receive notifications and updates related to the event via this email.
4. Enter your contact number in the "Contact number" field. This is not required but is useful for contacting you.
5. Select your preferred category for the event ("VIP", "Standard", or "Basic").
6. Read and accept or decline the "Consent" terms and conditions by clicking on the "Accept" or "Decline" buttons.
7. Choose a date for the event from the calendar provided in the "Date" field.
8. Select a time for the event from the list in the "Time" field.
9. If applicable, select the tool assigned to you from the list in the "Assigned tool" field.
10. Choose one or more categories from the list that best describe your interest for this event in the "Category" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name (First name, text, required/optional)**: Your first name as it appears on your documents or how you prefer to be addressed.
* **Last name (Last name, text, required/optional)**: Your last name as it appears on your documents or how you prefer to be addressed.
* **Email address (Email address, email, required/optional)**: A valid email address that you can use for event updates, notifications, or other communications.
* **Contact number (Contact number, text, required/optional)**: Your contact number, if you want to be contacted via phone for any event-related purposes.
* **Preference (Preference, select_one, required/optional)**: Select your preferred category for this event, which can be "VIP", "Standard", or "Basic".
* **Consent (Consent, note, required/optional)**: You must read and accept or decline the terms and conditions for this event. Click on "Accept" or "Decline" to make your choice.
* **Date (Date, date, required/optional)**: Choose a date for the event from the provided calendar.
* **Time (Time, time, required/optional)**: Select a time for the event from the list.
* **Assigned tool (Assigned tool, select_one, required/optional)**: If assigned, select the tool you have been assigned for this event.
* **Category (Category, select_multiple, required/optional)**: Choose one or more categories that describe your interest for this event from the provided list.
