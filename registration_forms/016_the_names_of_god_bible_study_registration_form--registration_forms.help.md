# The Names Of God Bible Study Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from individuals interested in registering for the Bible Study program. The form will capture basic contact information and details about the individual's purpose for attending the study.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Registration Form** field with any additional comments or details about your interest in the Bible Study.
2. Enter your **Email** address so we can contact you with study details and updates.
3. Enter your **Name** as you would like it to appear on study materials and communication.
4. Provide your **Contact Info** in the space provided.
5. Enter your **Phone** number so we can contact you if needed.
6. Choose your preferred **Date** and **Time** for the study, or select "Anytime" if you're flexible.
7. Briefly describe your **Purpose** for attending the Bible Study.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form** (id: 1): A text field to provide any additional comments or details about your interest in the Bible Study.
* **Email** (id: 2): Enter your email address so we can contact you with study details and updates.
* **Name** (id: 3): Enter your name as you would like it to appear on study materials and communication.
* **Contact Info** (id: 4): Provide your contact information, such as your address or a specific contact method, if different from your phone or email.
* **Phone** (id: 5): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Date** (id: 6): Select your preferred date for the Bible Study. You can also choose "Anytime" if you're flexible.
* **Time** (id: 7): Select your preferred time for the study. You can also choose "Anytime" if you're flexible.
* **Purpose** (id: 8): Briefly describe why you want to attend the Bible Study.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive study updates and communication.
* If you have any dietary restrictions or special needs, please include them in the **Contact Info** field.
* If you select "Anytime" for **Date** and **Time**, please be flexible and open to different study dates and times that may be arranged.
