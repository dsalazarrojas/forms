# Trestle_Academy_Ghana_Hackahon - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is for registering for the Trestle Academy Ghana Hackathon event. It will help us understand your preferences, needs, and logistical arrangements for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please fill out each section carefully. You can save your progress and come back later if needed. The form is divided into the following sections:

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (`name`, `text`, required): Please enter your name as you would like it to be displayed during the event.
* **What is your contact email?** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so we can get in touch with you before and during the event.
* **What is your phone number?** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number in case we need to contact you about any last-minute arrangements.
* **Which of the following is your preferred language?** (`preferred_language`, `select_one`, required): Choose your preferred language for communication and instructions during the event.
* **What is your availability for the hackathon?** (`arrival_time`, `date`, required): Choose your availability for the hackathon event, and we will schedule accordingly.
* **When do you plan to arrive?** (`arrival_time_2`, `time`, required): Enter the time you plan to arrive at the event.
* **Do you have any notes or comments?** (`notes`, `note`, required): Share any important notes or comments about your participation in the event.
* **Are you a developer or non-developer?** (`developer_non_developer`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you are a developer or non-developer, which will help us tailor the event to your needs.
* **What is your preferred mode of transportation?** (`transportation`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred mode of transportation to the event venue.
* **Do you have any dietary restrictions or special requests?** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, required): Share any dietary restrictions or special requests you may have.
* **How many years of experience do you have with programming?** (`experience`, `number`, required): Enter your level of programming experience.
* **Preferred Transportation** (`preferred_transportation`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your preferred mode of transportation to the event venue.
* **Do you require any special accommodations during the hackathon?** (`special_accommodations`, `select_multiple`, required): Check if you need any special accommodations during the event.
* **Are you able to work in a team?** (`team_work`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you are able to work in a team or prefer to work individually.
* **What time do you plan to leave the hackathon?** (`departure_time`, `time`, required): Enter the time you plan to leave the event.
* **Do you have any allergies or health conditions we should know about?** (`allergies`, `text`, required): Share any allergies or health conditions we should be aware of.
* **Do you have any emergency contacts we should have?** (`emergency_contacts`, `text`, required): Provide any emergency contacts we should have in case of an emergency.
* **Are you able to attend the whole event?** (`attendance`, `select_one`, required): Check if you are able to attend the whole event.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure we have all the necessary information for a comfortable and enjoyable experience.
* If you have any questions or need help with the form, feel free to ask!
