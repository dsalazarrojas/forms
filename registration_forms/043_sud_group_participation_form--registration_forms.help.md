# SUD Group Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in a SUD group. It will help us understand who has expressed interest and when we can schedule their participation in the group.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your first name in the first name field.
2. Enter your last name in the last name field.
3. Provide your date of birth in the date of birth field.
4. Enter your zip code in the zip code field.
5. Enter your email address in the email field.
6. Enter your phone number in the phone field.
7. Select the type of program you are interested in participating in from the program type field.
8. If you selected 'Yes' for program type, please provide more details about it in the other program type field.
9. Select the date and time you are available to participate in the schedule date and schedule time fields.
10. Choose the time slot you prefer for your participation (morning, afternoon, or evening) in the schedule time slot field.
11. Enter the name of your provider in the provider name field.
12. If you have a title for your provider, please enter it in the provider name title field.
13. If you are not using a known provider, enter a brief description of how they will be supporting you in the other provider name field.
14. Enter the name of the support group you are interested in joining.
15. Enter the ID of the support group you are interested in.
16. Enter the type of the support group.
17. Briefly describe the support group you are interested in.
18. Provide information about who will be providing support to you from the support group.
19. Once you have filled in all the required fields, please submit the form to express your interest in the group.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name (Text, Required):** Enter your first name for identification purposes.
* **last_name (Text, Required):** Enter your last name for identification purposes.
* **date_of_birth (Date, Optional):** Enter your date of birth for identification purposes.
* **zip_code (Number, Optional):** Enter your zip code for location identification.
* **email (Email, Optional):** Enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **phone (Text, Optional):** Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **group_id (Text, Optional):** Enter the ID of the group you are interested in.
* **program_type (Select One, Optional):** Select the type of program you are interested in participating in.
* **other_program_type (Text, Optional):** If you selected 'Yes' for program type, provide more details about the program type you are interested in.
* **schedule_date (Date, Optional):** Enter the date you are available to participate in the program.
* **schedule_time (Time, Optional):** Enter the time you are available to participate in the program.
* **schedule_time_slot (Select One, Optional):** Choose the time slot (morning, afternoon, or evening) you are available to participate in the program.
* **provider_name (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the provider you will be working with.
* **provider_name_title (Text, Optional):** Enter your provider's title.
* **provider_name_other (Text, Optional):** If you are not using a known provider, enter a brief description of who will be supporting you.
* **support_group_name (Text, Optional):** Enter the name of the support group you are interested in joining.
* **support_group_id (Text, Optional):** Enter the ID of the support group you are interested in.
* **support_group_type (Text, Optional):** Enter the type of support group.
* **support_group_description (Text, Optional):** Briefly describe the support group.
* **support_group_provider (Text, Optional):** Provide information about who will be providing support to you from the support group.
