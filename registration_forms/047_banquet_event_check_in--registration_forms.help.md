# Banquet Event Check In - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to be filled out by guests attending a banquet event to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for all. It requests information about the guest, their preferences, and special needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the steps below:

1. Fill out each field accurately and completely.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for your preferences and special requests.
3. If you have any special requests or needs, include them in the "Special Requests" field.
4. Review and verify your information before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Guest Full Name** (`guest_full_name`, text, required): Enter the guest's full name.
* **Email Address** (`guest_email`, email, required): Enter the guest's email address.
* **Phone Number** (`guest_phone`, text, optional): Enter the guest's phone number (if applicable).
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Enter the date of the event.
* **Check-In Time** (`check_in_time`, time, required): Enter the time of check-in.
* **Reservation Number** (`reservation_number`, text, optional): If applicable, enter the reservation number.
* **Party Size** (`party_size`, number, required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Table Assignment** (`table_assignment`, text, optional): If assigned, enter the table number.
* **Meal Selection** (`meal_selection`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred meal selection from the options provided.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, required): Select any dietary restrictions or allergies you have.
* **Special Dietary Notes** (`special_dietary_notes`, text, optional): If necessary, provide more details about your dietary restrictions.
* **Beverage Package** (`beverage_package`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred beverage package.
* **VIP Status** (`vip_status`, select_one, required): Indicate if you are a VIP guest.
* **Gift Bag Received** (`gift_bag_received`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate if you received a gift bag.
* **Parking Validated** (`parking_validated`, select_one, optional): If applicable, indicate if parking was validated.
* **Coat Check Needed** (`coat_check_needed`, select_one, optional): If necessary, indicate if coat check is needed.
* **Seating Preference** (`seating_preference`, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred seating preference.
* **Accompanying Guests** (`accompanying_guests`, text, optional): If applicable, list the names of accompanying guests.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): If applicable, provide the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Photo Consent** (`photo_consent`, select_one, required): Indicate if it's okay to be photographed.
* **Feedback Card** (`feedback_card`, select_one, optional): If you want to provide feedback, indicate so.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, text, optional): If you have any special requests or needs, provide them here.
* **Check-In Notes** (`check_in_notes`, text, optional): Provide any notes for staff, if necessary.

## Tips

* Make sure to answer all required fields accurately and completely.
* If you're unsure about any field, ask a staff member for guidance.
* Be honest and truthful when providing information about your preferences and special needs.
* Enjoy your event!
