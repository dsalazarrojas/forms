# Fundraising Gala Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals registering for or participating in the fundraising gala. It includes details such as name, contact information, event details, and donation information.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the field provided.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the field provided.
3. Enter your **Email** address in the format `example@example.com`.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
5. If you are registering on behalf of an **Organization**, enter the name of the organization.
6. Provide a brief description of the **Event Details**.
7. Set your **Fundraising Goal** as required.
8. Select your **Donation Type** as either 'Yes' or 'No'.
9. Select your **Registration Type** as one or more of the following: 'Yes', 'No'.
10. Select your **Payment Method** as one or more of the following: 'Yes', 'No'.
11. Enter the **Event Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
12. Enter the **Event Time** in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
13. Enter the **Registration Date** in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
14. Set your **Registration Fee** as required.
15. Set your **Total Donation** as required.
16. Set your **Donation Amount** as required.
17. Enter the **Event Location** where the gala will take place.
18. If necessary, provide **Additional Info** about your registration or event.
19. Select if you are a **Guest** as either 'Yes' or 'No'.
20. Select if you are a **Member** as either 'Yes' or 'No'.
21. Select if you are **Attending** as either 'Yes' or 'No'.
22. Select your **Payment Status** as either Active or Inactive.
23. Set your **Payment Amount** as required.
24. If necessary, provide **Payment Method Details**.
25. Select if your **Donation is Recurring** as either 'Yes' or 'No'.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your given name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name**: Enter your surname as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address in the format `example@example.com`.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number in the format `XXX-XXX-XXXX`.
* **Organization**: Enter the name of the organization you are representing, if applicable.
* **Event Details**: Provide a brief description of the fundraising gala event.
* **Fundraising Goal**: Enter your target fundraising amount for the event.
* **Donation Type**: Select whether you are making a donation or not.
* **Registration Type**: Select one or more options indicating how you are registering or participating.
* **Payment Method**: Select one or more options indicating your payment method, if applicable.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the fundraising gala in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Event Time**: Enter the time of the fundraising gala in the format `HH:MM AM/PM`.
* **Registration Date**: Enter the date you registered for the event in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Registration Fee**: Enter the registration fee for the event as required.
* **Total Donation**: Enter the total donation made for the event as required.
* **Donation Amount**: Enter the amount of the donation made for the event as required.
* **Event Location**: Enter the location where the fundraising gala will take place.
* **Additional Info**: Provide any additional information about your registration or event.
* **Is Guest**: Select whether you are attending as a guest or not.
* **Is Member**: Select whether you are a member of the organization or not.
* **Is Attending**: Select whether you are attending the event or not.
* **Payment Status**: Select whether your payment is active or inactive.
* **Payment Amount**: Enter the total payment made for the event as required.
* **Payment Method Details**: Provide any additional information about your payment method.
* **Is Recurring**: Select whether your donation is recurring or not.
