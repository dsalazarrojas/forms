# summer_camp_participant_information_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to gather basic participant information for the summer camp, including emergency contact details and demographic data. This information will help us ensure the safety and well-being of all participants during the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form with the required and optional fields as instructed below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Fill out the participant's name as you would like it to appear in our records.
* **Participant Age** (`participant_age`, number, optional): Enter the participant's age to help us understand their level of maturity and ability to participate in activities.
* **Participant Gender** (`participant_gender`, select_one, optional): Select the participant's gender to help us provide a safe and inclusive environment.
* **Participant Date of Birth (Dob)** (`participant_dob`, date, optional): Enter the participant's date of birth to help us verify their age and provide accurate records.
* **Participant Contact** (`participant_contact`, text, optional): Provide a reliable contact method for us to reach out to the participant, such as phone numbers or email addresses.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter the participant's current address to ensure we can locate them in case of an emergency.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that we can use to send updates and information to the participant.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter a contact name and number for someone we can reach out to in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_multiple, optional): Select the relationship between the emergency contact and the participant (e.g., parent, guardian, friend).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly to ensure accurate records and emergency contact information.
* If you have any concerns or questions, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
