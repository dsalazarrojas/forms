# Additive Manufacturing Technology Fair Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Additive Manufacturing Technology Fair Registration form collects information from attendees to plan and manage the event. Please fill out the form accurately to ensure you receive the correct updates and information about the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your personal details:
	* **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it should be displayed on the event schedule and materials.
	* **Organization / Company**: Enter your organization or company name.
	* **Job Title / Role**: Enter your job title or role.
2. Provide your contact information:
	* **Work Email Address**: Enter your work email address.
	* **Contact Phone**: Enter your contact phone number.
3. Choose your attendance preferences:
	* **Which days will you be attending?**: Select the days you plan to attend the event. This will help the organizers plan for catering, logistics, and other aspects of the event.
4. Specify your primary area of interest:
	* **Primary Area of Interest**: Choose the area of additive manufacturing technology that interests you the most.
5. Let us know about your workshop preferences:
	* **Are you interested in participating in hands-on workshops?**: Check if you're interested in participating in the workshops. Limited spots are available, so be sure to select this option if you're interested.
6. Provide dietary requirements:
	* **Dietary Requirements for Lunch / Networking**: If you have any dietary requirements, such as vegetarian or gluten-free, please let us know.
7. Let us know about your networking plans:
	* **Will you attend the evening Networking Mixer?**: Check if you're interested in attending the networking mixer on Day 1 evening.
8. Choose your registration category:
	* **Registration Category**: Choose the category that best describes your role at the event. This will help us tailor the event experience for you.
9. Consent to share your contact information:
	* **I consent to sharing my contact info with event sponsors**: Check this box to give permission for event sponsors to contact you.
