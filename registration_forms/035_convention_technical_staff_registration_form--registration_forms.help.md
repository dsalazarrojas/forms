# Convention Technical Staff Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended for convention technical staff to register themselves for specific technical positions at a convention. The purpose of this form is to gather necessary information about the staff members' skills, experience, and availability to efficiently assign them to the right roles and shifts.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Read through the form carefully and answer each question to the best of your ability.
2. Enter your full name, email address, and phone number in their respective fields.
3. Select your technical role and experience level from the provided options.
4. Indicate your assigned area of work and shift preference.
5. Select all dates you are available to work.
6. List your technical skills and any additional certifications you may have.
7. Indicate your uniform size and whether you need tools to be provided.
8. Confirm if you have attended the technical briefing and provide the briefing date if applicable.
9. Specify any additional comments or information that may be relevant to your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Enter your legal name as it appears on your ID or passport.
* **Email Address**: Enter your primary email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your contact phone number for emergencies and communication.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the name of the person you would like to be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the contact phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Technical Role**: Select the technical role you are applying for, such as Audio Technician, Video Technician, or Lighting Technician.
* **Experience Level**: Select your experience level, which may be Entry Level, Intermediate, Experienced, or Expert.
* **Assigned Area**: Select the area where you will be working, which may be Main Stage, Breakout Rooms, Registration Area, or Exhibition Hall.
* **Shift Preference**: Select your preferred shift time, which may be Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Night, or Rotating.
* **Available Dates**: Select all dates you are available to work, which includes Setup Day 1, Setup Day 2, Event Day 1, Event Day 2, and Breakdown Day.
* **Audio Skills**: Select your level of expertise in audio skills, which may be Expert, Proficient, Basic, or None.
* **Video Skills**: Select your level of expertise in video skills, which may be Expert, Proficient, Basic, or None.
* **Lighting Skills**: Select your level of expertise in lighting skills, which may be Expert, Proficient, Basic, or None.
* **Networking Skills**: Select your level of expertise in networking skills, which may be Expert, Proficient, Basic, or None.
* **Software Proficiency**: If you have proficiency in any software, list them in this field.
* **Equipment Experience**: If you have experience with specific equipment, list them in this field.
* **Safety Certification**: Confirm if you have safety certification.
* **Uniform Size**: Select your uniform size, which may be XS, S, M, L, XL, or XXL.
* **Tools Provided**: Indicate if you need tools to be provided, or select No - I have my own or Partially.
* **Tools or Equipment Needed**: If you need specific tools or equipment, list them in this field.
* **Briefing and Training**: Confirm if you have attended the technical briefing and provide the briefing date if applicable.
* **Venue Familiarity**: Confirm if you are familiar with the venue, which may be Yes - Worked here before, Yes - Visited before, or No - First time.
* **Assigned Supervisor**: If applicable, enter the name of your assigned supervisor.
* **Team Assignment**: If applicable, enter which team you are assigned to.
* **Radio Channel Assigned**: If applicable, enter the radio channel assigned to you.
* **Dietary Requirements**: Select your dietary requirements, which may be None, Vegetarian, Vegan, or Gluten-Free.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any accessibility needs, describe them in this field.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any other relevant information that may be necessary for your registration.
* **Availability Notes**: If you have any scheduling constraints, describe them in this field.
