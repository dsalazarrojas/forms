# Online Yoga Class Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Online Yoga Class Registration Form is designed to gather essential information from students to ensure a smooth and tailored online yoga class experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the "Registration Form" section with your student information.
2. Select your preferred payment method and class type.
3. Choose your class level and frequency.
4. Select the class duration that suits your needs.
5. Enter your instructor's information (if applicable).
6. Enter your registration date and time.
7. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (Text, Required):
	+ Enter your name, email, and any other relevant student information.
* **Student Info** (Text, Optional):
	+ Add any additional student information that you feel is necessary.
* **Contact Info** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any relevant contact information, such as phone numbers or physical addresses.
* **Date and Time** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter the date and time you would like to attend the class.
* **Payment Info** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter your payment information, such as credit card details or PayPal account information.
* **Payment Method** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose from Credit Card, PayPal, or Bank Transfer.
* **Class Type** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Class Level** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Class Frequency** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose from Weekly, Biweekly, or Monthly.
* **Class Duration** (Select One, Optional):
	+ Choose from 30 minutes, 1 hour, or 2 hours.
* **Instructor Info** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the instructor, such as their email or phone number.
* **Registration Date** (Date, Optional):
	+ Enter the date you would like to register for the class.
* **Registration Time** (Time, Optional):
	+ Enter the time you would like to register for the class.
* **Instructor Email** (Email, Optional):
	+ Enter the email of the instructor.
* **Instructor Number** (Text, Optional):
	+ Enter any relevant information about the instructor.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the instructor.
* Be sure to provide your payment information if you choose to pay online.
