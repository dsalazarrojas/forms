# Medical Imaging Equipment Repair Technician Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register users for the Medical Imaging Equipment Repair Technician Training. It allows users to select the courses they wish to take and provide their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the course(s) you wish to enroll in from the "Course Enrollment" section.
2. Enter your name in the "Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Confirm your phone number in the "Confirm Phone" field.
6. Confirm your email address in the "Confirm Email" field.
7. Select the "Submit" option from the "Submit" section to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Course Enrollment** (`Course Enrollment`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more courses you wish to enroll in.
  * Select as many courses as you need from the dropdown list.
- **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name.
  * This is where you enter your name, for example, John Doe.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
  * This is where you enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
  * This is where you enter your phone number.
- **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required/optional): Confirm your phone number.
  * This is where you can confirm your phone number.
- **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required/optional): Confirm your email address.
  * This is where you can confirm your email address.
- **Submit** (`Submit`, select_one, required/optional): Select this option to submit your registration.
  * Click this button to complete your registration.
  * You can also click the "Cancel" option if you do not want to submit your registration.

## Tips
- Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information in the form fields.
- Double-check your phone number and email address before submitting your registration.
- If you have any issues with the form, please contact the support team.
