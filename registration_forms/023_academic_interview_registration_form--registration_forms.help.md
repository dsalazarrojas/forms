# Academic Interview Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for an academic interview and provide necessary information to the interview team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your full name in the "Applicant full name" field.
2. Provide your preferred contact email in the "Contact email" field.
3. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact phone number" field (optional).
4. Select the position or role you are applying for in the "Position or role for interview" field.
5. Choose your preferred availability for the interview in the "Availability preference" field.
6. Select your preferred interview format in the "Preferred interview format" field.
7. Provide any additional requirements or accommodations you may need in the "Additional requirements" field (optional).
8. Indicate your consent to record the interview in the "Consent to record interview" field.
9. If applicable, provide references or links to your portfolio in the "References" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant full name** (`applicant_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Contact email** (`email`, email, required): Provide your preferred email for scheduling and communication.
* **Contact phone number** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your contact phone number including country code if international.
* **Position or role for interview** (`position_applied`, text, required): Select the job title or academic role you are applying for.
* **Availability preference** (`availability_preference`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred date range or times for the interview.
	+ Options: Morning, Afternoon, Evening
* **Preferred interview format** (`interview_format`, select_one, required): Select your preferred interview format.
	+ Options: In person, Virtual, Either
* **Additional requirements** (`additional_requirements`, text, optional): Provide any accessibility needs or accommodations you require.
* **Consent to record interview** (`consent_recording`, select_one, required): Indicate permission to record the interview.
	+ Options: True, False
* **References** (`references`, text, optional): Provide references or links to your portfolio if applicable.
