# fitness_program_participation_consent_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Program Participation Consent Form is a critical document that ensures participants understand and agree to the terms and conditions of the fitness program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your participant's name in the "participant_name" field.
2. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
3. Provide a brief description of yourself in the "participant_info" field.
4. Enter your emergency contact information in the "emergency_contact" field.
5. Enter your emergency phone number in the "emergency_phone" field.
6. Enter your emergency address in the "emergency_address" field.
7. Select your fitness level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) in the "fitness_level" field.
8. Check the boxes indicating you have medical conditions (I have a medical condition, I have an injury, or I have a disability) in the "medical_condition" field.
9. Select the relationship of your emergency contact in the "emergency_contact_relationship" field (Parent, Spouse, or Friend).
10. Check the boxes indicating you have read and understand the fitness program terms and conditions, signed up for the program, and read and understand the emergency procedures in the "participant_consent" field.
11. Check the box indicating you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the program in the "terms_and_conditions" field.
12. Enter the current date in the "date" field (optional).
13. Enter the current time in the "time" field (optional).
14. Enter any additional notes in the "note" field (optional).
15. Select the participant who will be using the assigned tool (User or Admin) in the "assigned_tool" field (optional).
16. Check the boxes indicating you have read and understand the fitness program terms and conditions, signed up for the program, and read and understand the emergency procedures in the "participant_consent_2" field.
17. Check the box indicating you have read and understand the terms and conditions of the program in the "terms_and_conditions_2" field.
18. Enter the current date in the "date_2" field (optional).
19. Enter the current time in the "time_2" field (optional).
20. Enter any additional notes in the "note_2" field (optional).
21. Check the box indicating you confirm your age (18 years or older) and sign below to confirm your age in the "participant_signature" field.
22. Select the participant who will be signing this document (I am the person signing this document, I am signing on behalf of a minor child, or I am signing on behalf of a non-participating parent) in the "participant_name_confirmation" field.
23. Check the box indicating the consent status (Active or Inactive) in the "consent_status" field.
24. Enter your signature date in the "participant_signature_date" field.
25. Select the box indicating the participant who will be signing this document (I am the person signing this document, I am signing on behalf of a minor child, or I am signing on behalf of a non-participating parent) in the "participant_name_confirmation" field.
