# Nonprofit Leadership Seminar Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register attendees for the Nonprofit Leadership Seminar. It gathers basic contact information and organizational details to ensure attendees can participate in the seminar of their choice. Please complete this form accurately and truthfully to provide the necessary information for a successful event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Type your organization's name in the "Organization" field.
5. Choose your role in the nonprofit sector in the "Role" field.
6. Choose the seminar format you'd like to attend (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid) in the "Seminar Type" field.
7. Select the date of the seminar you wish to attend in the "Seminar Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: Your first name is required to be able to properly address you as a seminar attendee.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name to help us identify you accurately.
* **Email**: Provide a valid email address to receive seminar updates and notifications.
* **Organization**: Please enter your organization's name to help us understand your connection to the nonprofit sector.
* **Role**: This field helps us understand your role within the nonprofit sector. Please choose the option that best describes you.
* **Seminar Type**: Choose the seminar format you prefer (In-Person, Virtual, or Hybrid).
* **Seminar Date**: Select the date of the seminar you wish to attend. This field will be updated as the dates become available.
