# Wildlife Rehabilitation Workshop Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to register and collect information from attendees interested in attending the wildlife rehabilitation workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please fill in your name and email address in the **Attendee Details** section.
2. Select one of the preferred dates for the workshop (23-25 June 2023, 30 June - 1 July 2023, or 7-9 July 2023) in the **Preferred Dates** section.
3. If you are interested in specific areas of wildlife rehabilitation, select the corresponding options in the **Interests** section.
4. Confirm your consent for the workshop by ticking the **Consent** checkbox.
5. Enter your contact details in the **Contact Details** section, including your name, email address, phone number, address, city, state, and zip code.
6. If you have any additional comments or requests, you can add them in the **Comments** section.
7. Finally, provide your preferred date and time for the workshop in the **Date and Time** section.

* **Consent (4)**: Confirm that you are aware of and agree to the workshop's terms and conditions.
