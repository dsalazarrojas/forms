# Net Zero Construction Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Net Zero Construction Training Registration form is designed to collect information from attendees who want to register for training sessions related to Net Zero construction. The form helps us understand the attendee's preferences, requirements, and session choices to provide a seamless training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the training provider from the options provided.
2. Choose the sustainability organization (if applicable).
3. Provide attendee details, such as their email and phone number (if applicable).
4. Choose the sessions and courses you are interested in.
5. Select the session dates and time (if applicable).
6. Add any session notes (if applicable).
7. Confirm your attendee email and phone number (if applicable).
8. Choose your preferred training location.
9. Select your attendee requirements (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Training Provider**: Select the provider of the training session you are interested in. Options are AEC and Other.
* **Sustainability Organization**: If you are affiliated with a sustainability organization, select it from the options provided. Options are ASHRAE, ASHRAE Member, ASHRAE Member Organization, and Non-ASHRAE Member Organization.
* **Attendee Details**: Provide your email and phone number (if applicable) for communication and contact purposes.
* **Session Choice 1**: Select the sessions you are interested in attending. Options are Session 1 and Session 2.
* **Session Choice 2**: Confirm your session choices (if any).
* **Session Requirement 1**: Select the requirements you need for the sessions. Options are Yes and No.
* **Session Requirement 2**: Confirm your session requirements (if any).
* **Course Choice 1**: Select the course you want to attend. Options are Course 1 and Course 2.
* **Session Dates**: Select the date(s) for the session(s) you are interested in.
* **Session Time**: Select the time for the session(s) you are interested in.
* **Session Note**: Add any additional notes or comments about the session(s).
* **Attendee Email**: Confirm your email address for communication purposes.
* **Attendee Phone**: Confirm your phone number (if applicable).
* **Training Location**: Select your preferred training location. Options are Site and In-person.
* **Attendee Requirements 1**: Select the requirements you need for the session(s). Options are Yes and No.
* **Attendee Requirements 2**: Confirm your attendee requirements (if any).
