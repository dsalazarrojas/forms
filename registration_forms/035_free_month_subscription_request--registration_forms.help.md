# free_month_subscription_request - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to request a free month subscription. It helps us understand the user's details and subscription preferences to provide a personalized offer.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your user details in the "User Details" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Select your preferred subscription plan from the "Subscription Plan" dropdown list.
5. Add any additional notes about your subscription request in the "Notes" field.
6. Confirm that you have read and accepted the terms of service.
7. Confirm that you agree to the user agreement.
8. Optionally, specify any additional terms or details about your user agreement.
9. Optionally, provide more details about your subscription plan and its duration and frequency.
10. Provide your first and last name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
11. Optionally, choose additional terms of the user agreement you wish to subscribe to.
12. Provide any other details about the user agreement in the "User Agreement Details" field.
13. Finally, submit the form to complete your free month subscription request.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details**: Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself.
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address to receive communication about your subscription.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Subscription Plan**: Choose your preferred subscription plan from the dropdown list (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription request.
* **Terms of Service Accepted**: Confirm that you have read and accepted our terms of service.
* **I Agree**: Confirm that you agree to our user agreement.
* **User Agreement**: Optionally, choose additional terms of the user agreement you wish to subscribe to.
* **Other (Please specify)**: If you have selected "User Agreement" above, specify the details about the agreement.
* **User Agreement Details**: Provide any additional details about the user agreement.
* **Subscription Plan Details**: Provide more details about your subscription plan, such as its duration and frequency.
* **Subscription Plan Duration**: Choose the duration of your subscription plan (Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly).
* **Subscription Plan Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your subscription plan (Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Notes**: Add any additional notes or comments about your subscription plan.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **User Agreement**: Optionally, choose additional terms of the user agreement you wish to subscribe to.
* **Other (Please specify)**: If you have selected "User Agreement" above, specify the details about the agreement.
* **User Agreement Details**: Provide any additional details about the user agreement.
* **Subscription Plan Frequency**: Choose the frequency of your subscription plan (Weekly, Biweekly, Monthly, Quarterly).
* **Submit**: Submit the form to complete your free month subscription request.
