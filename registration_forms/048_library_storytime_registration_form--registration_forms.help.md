# Library Storytime Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a library storytime session. Please fill out the form to provide information about yourself and your child's (if applicable) attendance.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Make sure to include your email address and phone number for contact.
3. Select the time slot you wish to attend.
4. Check off the boxes for child and adult attendance if applicable.
5. Add any additional comments or information you wish to share.
6. Confirm that you have read and understand this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, required: false): Please enter your email address for contact purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Time Slot** (`select_one`, required: false): Select the time slot you wish to attend.
* **Storytime Type** (`select_multiple`, required: false): Check off the box next to "Yes" if you wish to attend storytime for yourself.
* **Child Attendance** (`select_one`, required: false): Check off the box next to "Yes" if you are bringing a child.
* **Adult Attendance** (`select_one`, required: false): Check off the box next to "Yes" if an adult will be attending with you.
* **Additional Comments** (`note`, required: false): This is a text area for you to add any additional comments or information you wish to share.
* **Confirm** (`select_one`, required: false): Confirm that you have read and understand this form by selecting "Yes".
