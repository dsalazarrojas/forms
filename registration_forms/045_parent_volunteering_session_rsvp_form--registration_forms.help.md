# Parent Volunteering Session RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help parents sign up for their child's volunteer session. It collects essential information such as name, contact details, and volunteer preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out this form to sign up for your child's volunteer session.
2. Ensure you've completed all the required and optional fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name. This will help us get in touch with you about any further details or updates.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name. This will help us verify your identity and ensure accurate information is stored.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. This is where we'll be sending you any important updates or reminders about the session.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number. This will help us get in touch with you about any urgent matters.
* **Volunteer Type** (`volunteer_type`, select_one, optional): Select how you'd like to volunteer:
	+ **General Volunteer**: General volunteers assist with day-to-day operations.
	+ **Volunteer Leader**: Volunteer leaders take charge of specific tasks and lead others.
	+ **Volunteer Coordinator**: Volunteer coordinators oversee and manage volunteers.
* **Volunteer Session** (`volunteer_session`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sessions you'd like to volunteer:
	+ **Session 1**
	+ **Session 2**
	+ **Session 3**
* **Volunteer Session Details** (`volunteer_session_details`, note, optional): Provide any additional details about your volunteer preferences or session-specific information.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, text, optional): Confirm that you've read and understand the form information.
* **Confirm 2** (`confirm_2`, text, optional): Confirm that you've submitted the form correctly.

## Tips
* Please ensure you've filled out all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for accuracy.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to reach out to us.
