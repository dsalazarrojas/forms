# digital_prescription_safety_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for the registration of participants in digital prescription safety training. Fill out this form to provide necessary details for your session.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the Participant Details section with the required information.
2. Next, provide Session Details, if necessary.
3. If required, select the sessions you would like to participate in and sign up for.
4. Optionally, add any additional Notes about the participant.
5. Enter the participant's Email address for contact purposes.
6. Enter the participant's Phone number for further communication.
7. Select the participant's Date of Birth for age verification.
8. Enter the participant's Address for location purposes.
9. If necessary, select the Session Date and Time for your training session.
10. Provide a signature for the participant.
11. Finally, indicate whether the training provider is Yes or No.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's name and any other relevant details.
* **Session Details** (`session_details`, text, required/optional): Provide any additional details about the session, such as location or any specific requirements.
* **Schedule a Session** (`schedule_session`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the sessions you would like to participate in. Options: Yes, No.
* **Sign-ups** (`sign_ups`, select_one, required/optional): Select whether you have signed up for the sessions. Options: Yes, No.
* **Preferred Sessions** (`preferred_sessions`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the sessions you would prefer to participate in. Options: Yes, No.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional comments or notes about the participant.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter the participant's email address for contact purposes.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's phone number for further communication.
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_dob`, date, required/optional): Select the participant's Date of Birth for age verification.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, required/optional): Enter the participant's address for location purposes.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required/optional): If necessary, select the Session Date for your training session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required/optional): If necessary, select the Session Time for your training session.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, note, required/optional): Provide a signature for the participant.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate whether the training provider is yes or no.

## Tips
* Be sure to double-check all required fields before submitting the form.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the training provider for clarification.
* Remember to review the form carefully to ensure you provide all necessary details.
