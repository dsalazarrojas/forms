# immunization declaration form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The immunization declaration form is used to declare your vaccination status, providing information about your healthcare provider, school name (if applicable), and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user information on the first page.
2. Select your vaccination status from the available options on the second page.
3. Enter your vaccination date in mm/dd format (e.g., 01/01 for January 1st).
4. Select your healthcare provider.
5. If you are declaring for school purposes, select the name of your school from the drop-down menu.
6. If you are a child, indicate your consent status.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required/optional): Fill in your name and contact information on this page.
* **Vaccination Status** (`vaccination_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select all the vaccinations you have received. Please choose from the available options: polio, measles, hep A, hep B, influenza, and COVID-19.
* **Vaccination Date** (`vaccination_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you received your vaccinations in mm/dd format (e.g., 01/01 for January 1st).
* **Healthcare Provider** (`healthcare_provider`, text, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of your healthcare provider.
* **School Name** (`school_name`, select_one, required for school purposes): If you are declaring for school purposes, select the name of your school from the drop-down menu.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_multiple, required/optional): If you are a child, indicate your consent status. Please choose from the available options: 'yes' and 'no'.
