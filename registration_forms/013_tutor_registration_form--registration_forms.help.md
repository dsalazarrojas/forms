# Tutor Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Tutor Registration Form is designed to collect information about a new tutor, including their contact details, availability, payment preferences, and emergency contact information. This form helps us create or update a tutor's profile for efficient communication and scheduling.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide your name in the **New Tutor** field (id: 1) as you would like to be listed.
2. Enter the **Center Location** where you are based (id: 2).
3. In the **Contact Details** section (id: 3), you can provide additional contact information for us to reach out to you (optional).
4. Choose your available days of the week (id: 4) by selecting one or more days from the list.
5. In the **Available Time Slots** field (id: 8), you can provide more specific time slots you are available, if applicable.
6. Enter your **Experience** in teaching or related fields (id: 9).
7. Provide your **Education** background (id: 10).
8. List your relevant **Skills** (id: 11).
9. If not already listed under **Experience**, describe your **Availability** (id: 12).
10. In the **Payment Details** section (id: 13), enter any payment information you're willing to share (optional).
11. Choose your preferred **Payment Frequency** (id: 14) from the options provided.
12. Enter the **Payment Rate** (id: 15) you expect, and provide **Payment Rate Details** (id: 16) for clarity.
13. If applicable, provide **Contract Details** (id: 17).
14. In the **Other Info** section (id: 18), include any other relevant information about your teaching or availability.
15. Provide the **Emergency Contact** name (id: 19), **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 20), and **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 21), if applicable.
16. Enter the **Emergency Contact Email** (id: 22) and provide any additional **Emergency Contact Other** details (id: 23).
17. Finally, provide any other information in **Emergency Contact Other Description** (id: 24).
18. Click the **Submit** button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **New Tutor** (id: 1): Enter your name as you would like to be listed.
* **Center Location** (id: 2): Where you are based or work.
* **Contact Details** (id: 3): Optional, for our team to contact you.
* **Available Days** (id: 4): Select one or more days you are available to work.
* **Available Time Slots** (id: 8): If you have specific time slots available.
* **Experience** (id: 9): Briefly describe your teaching or relevant experience.
* **Education** (id: 10): Background information about your academic qualifications.
* **Skills** (id: 11): List relevant skills you offer as a tutor.
* **Availability** (id: 12): Describe your work schedule and flexibility.
* **Payment Details** (id: 13): Any payment information you are willing to share.
* **Payment Frequency** (id: 14): Choose your preferred payment frequency.
* **Payment Rate** (id: 15): Enter your expected payment rate.
* **Payment Rate Details** (id: 16): For clarity, provide details about your payment rate.
* **Contract Details** (id: 17): If applicable, provide any contract information.
* **Other Info** (id: 18): Any additional details relevant to your teaching or availability.
* **Emergency Contact** (id: 19): Name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (id: 20): Phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (id: 21): Relationship to the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (id: 22): Email address of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other** (id: 23): Other details about the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Other Description** (id: 24): Description of the emergency contact.
* **Submit** (id: 25): Click to complete and submit your registration.

## Tips

- Please ensure all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
- If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us for assistance.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to avoid any errors.
