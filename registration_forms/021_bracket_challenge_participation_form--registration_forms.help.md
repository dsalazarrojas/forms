# Bracket Challenge Participation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect participant information and bracket challenge settings for a specific event, such as the NCAA March Madness or NFL Playoffs. It's essential to fill out the form carefully to ensure that your participant data and bracket settings are accurate and up-to-date.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information:
	* **Full Name**: Your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
	* **Email Address**: Your email address where you'll receive bracket updates and notifications.
	* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone number for any questions or issues.
2. Select your event participation details:
	* **Bracket Challenge Event**: Choose the specific bracket challenge you're entering (e.g., NCAA March Madness, NFL Playoffs, etc.).
	* **Event Year**: The year of the bracket challenge.
	* **Your Bracket Entry Name**: A unique name for your bracket entry.
3. Make your predictions:
	* **Champion Prediction**: Who you think will win the challenge.
	* **Runner-up Prediction**: Second-place pick.
	* **Cinderella Story Pick**: Surprise upset team.
4. Choose your strategy and experience:
	* **Prediction Strategy**: How you made your picks (e.g., statistics and analytics, gut feeling, etc.).
	* **Bracket Challenge Experience**: How many brackets you've done in the past.
5. Join a league or group:
	* **League or Group Name**: If you're participating in a group league, provide the name of your league or group.
	* **League Code or ID**: Code or ID to join the group.
6. Confirm your terms and agreement:
	* **I Agree to Bracket Challenge Rules**: Check the box to confirm you've read and agree to the rules.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`participant_name`, required, true): Your full legal name as it appears on official documents.
