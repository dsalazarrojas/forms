# education-market-research-registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a market research study. It collects information about the participant's contact details, availability, and questions or comments they have about the research.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with your information. You will need to provide your contact details, confirm your availability, and answer any questions or provide comments about the research.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of yourself as a participant.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, and address.
* **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, optional): Enter any questions you have about the research.
* **Participant Availability** (`participant_availability`, date, optional): Select a date and time when you are available to participate in the research.
* **Follow-up Instructions** (`follow_up_instructions`, text, optional): Enter any instructions or guidance you need from the researcher.
* **Participant Signup** (`participant_signup`, select_multiple, optional): Confirm whether you are a participant or not.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name.
* **Research Institution** (`research_institution`, select_one, optional): Confirm whether the participant is a research institution or not.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Follow-up Questions** (`follow_up_questions`, text, optional): Enter any follow-up questions you have.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, text, optional): Enter any additional comments you have.
* **Participant Questions** (`participant_questions`, text, optional): Enter any questions you have about the participant experience.
* **Research Institution Questions** (`research_institution_questions`, text, optional): Enter any questions you have about the research institution.
* **Research Question** (`research_question`, text, optional): Enter any questions you have about the research.
* **Research Objective** (`research_objective`, text, optional): Enter the objective of the research.
* **Research Methodology** (`research_methodology`, text, optional): Enter the methodology of the research.
* **Research Schedule** (`research_schedule`, date, optional): Select a date and time for the research.

Note: This form is for internal use and is not designed for public submission.
