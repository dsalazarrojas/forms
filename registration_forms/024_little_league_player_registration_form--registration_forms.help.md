# Little League Player Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Little League Player Registration Form is a tool designed to collect essential information from players for the administration and organization of a little league. By filling out this form, players and their families can ensure that all necessary details are captured for the player's participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill in the Player Information page with the following details:
    *   Name
    *   Team Name
    *   Player Grade
    *   Player Position
*   Proceed to the Team Information page and fill in the following details:
    *   Team Name
    *   Team Number
*   Move on to the Contact Information page and enter:
    *   Player Email
    *   Player Phone
*   In the Player Details page, select the player's grade and position using the provided options:
    *   Player Grade: 7th, 8th, 9th, 10th
    *   Player Position: Red, Yellow, Pitcher, Catcher, Fielder, Batter
*   On the same page, enter the player's Date of Birth.
*   Finally, select the team's grade from the given options.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Player Information
*   **Player Name**: Enter your name as it is to be known on the team.
*   **Team Name**: Enter the name of the team you are affiliated with.
*   **Player Grade**: Select your grade level.
*   **Player Position**: Indicate your primary role in the team.

### Team Information
*   **Team Name**: Enter the name of your team.
*   **Team Number**: Enter your team number.

### Contact Information
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication purposes.
*   **Phone**: Enter your phone number.

### Player Details
*   **Player Grade**: Select your grade level.
*   **Player Position**: Indicate your primary role in the team.
*   **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth.
*   **Team Grade**: Select your team's grade level.
*   **Player DOOL (Drop-off time)**: Enter your drop-off time.
*   **Player DOOL (Drop-off time 2)**: Enter your second drop-off time (if applicable).

## Tips
*   Be accurate with the information provided.
*   Ensure the team and player grade are correct to avoid discrepancies.
*   Use a valid email address and phone number for communication.
