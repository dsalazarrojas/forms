# Hotel Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect membership information from hotel guests who want to join the hotel's membership program. By filling out this form, you will be able to join the program and receive exclusive benefits and perks.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your member information (Form 1).
2. Choose a membership plan that suits your needs (Form 2).
3. Enter your contact person's name and details (Form 3).
4. Enter your address (Form 4).
5. Enter your phone number (Form 5).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Form 1: Member Information
* **Member Information** (`form_1`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your name and any other relevant information about yourself. This will help us create your membership account.
### Form 2: Membership Plan
* **Membership Plan** (`form_2`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose a membership plan that suits your needs. Options include:
	+ Plan 1
	+ Plan 2
	+ Plan 3
### Form 3: Contact Person
* **Contact Person** (`form_3`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact details of the person responsible for your membership.
### Form 4: Address
* **Address** (`form_4`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your address so we can contact you regarding your membership benefits.
### Form 5: Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`form_5`, `text`, required/optional): Enter your phone number so we can contact you regarding your membership benefits or any updates.

## Tips
* Please ensure that you provide accurate and up-to-date information to avoid any delays or issues with your membership account.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
