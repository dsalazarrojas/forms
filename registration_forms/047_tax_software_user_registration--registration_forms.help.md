# Tax Software User Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Tax Software User Registration" form is used to register and set up your account information in our tax software platform. Please complete this form to ensure that you have access to our services and features.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First name** and **Last name** in the respective fields.
2. Provide your **Email** address to receive notifications and updates.
3. Select your **User type** from the options provided. (Note: if you're unsure about this field, please refer to our support team for clarification.)
4. If you have any additional comments or contact information, please include them in the **Contact details** field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email**: Enter your email address where you'd like to receive notifications and updates from us.
* **User type**: Select 'Yes' if you are an authorized representative of your organization, or 'No' if you are an individual. (Note: if you're unsure about this field, please refer to our support team for clarification.)
* **Contact details**: Include any additional comments or contact information that you'd like to provide about yourself or your organization.
