# Legislation Competition Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a legislation competition event. Fill out the form with your details to participate in the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please complete this form to provide basic contact information and details about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`Name`, text, optional): This field is used to enter your name for registration. You may choose to provide your name or leave this field empty if you are not comfortable sharing it.
* **Name of organisation** (`Name of organisation`, text, optional): If you are registering on behalf of an organization, please enter the name of the organization here. Otherwise, you can leave this field empty.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, optional): This field is used to enter your email address. Please ensure to enter a valid email address that can be used for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, optional): Please enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted by us.
* **Name of event** (`Name of event`, text, optional): If you are registering for a specific event, please enter the name of that event here.
* **Date** (`Date`, date, optional): Please enter the date of the event or the date of your expected participation.
* **Time** (`Time`, time, optional): If applicable, please enter the time of the event or your expected participation time.
* **Location** (`Location`, text, optional): If you are registering for an event with a specific location, please enter the location here.

## Tips
- Ensure you provide accurate and complete information in each field.
- If you have any questions or need assistance, please contact us directly.
