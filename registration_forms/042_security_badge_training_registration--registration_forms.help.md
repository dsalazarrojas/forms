# Security Badge Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The `Security Badge Training Registration` form is a tool to collect information from security personnel about their security badge training sessions. This form is meant to be used in conjunction with the organization's security badge training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the `First Name` field.
2. Enter your last name in the `Last Name` field.
3. Enter your email address in the `Email` field.
4. Enter your phone number in the `Phone` field.
5. Select your department from the `Department` dropdown menu.
6. Choose the shift for which you are registering for from the `Shift` dropdown menu.
7. Select the sessions you wish to attend from the `Session` dropdown menu.
8. Enter the date of the session in the `Date` field.
9. Enter the time of the session in the `Time` field.
10. Add any additional notes regarding your registration in the `Note` field.
11. If applicable, select the assigned tool for this session from the `Assigned Tool` dropdown menu.
12. Select any relevant categories for this session from the `Category` dropdown menu.
13. Enter the session date in the `Session Date` field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address here.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number here.
* **Department** (`department`, text, optional): Select your department from the dropdown menu:
	+ Security
	+ Facilities
	+ Operations
	+ Other
* **Shift** (`shift`, select_one, optional): Choose the shift for which you are registering:
	+ Security
	+ Facilities
	+ Operations
	+ Other
* **Session** (`session`, select_multiple, optional): Select the sessions you wish to attend:
	+ Security Badge Training 1
	+ Security Badge Training 2
	+ Security Badge Training 3
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date of the session.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the session.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Add any additional notes regarding your registration.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): If applicable, select the assigned tool for this session:
	+ ChatJIMMY
	+ Not Assigned
* **Category** (`category`, select_multiple, optional): Select any relevant categories for this session:
	+ Yes
	+ No
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Enter the session date.

**Tips**
- Make sure to enter your email address correctly to ensure you receive notifications regarding your session registration.
- If you are unsure about any of the fields, please ask your supervisor for clarification.
- Please select all relevant sessions and categories that apply to your registration.
