## Purpose
This form is designed to gather information from users who want to register for the future cities forum. The purpose of this form is to collect contact information and preferences from attendees, which will be used to tailor the forum experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form accurately, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Registration Form** section with the required information.
2. Provide your **Attendee Name**.
3. Enter your **Email** address.
4. Confirm your **Email** address in the **Confirm Email** field.
5. Provide your **Phone** number.
6. Choose your preferred **Method of Communication**.
7. If you have any special requests, type them in the **Special Requests** field.
8. Select the **Date** and **Time** of the event you plan to attend.
9. Select multiple options in the **Select Multiple** field as needed.
10. Enter your **Email Address** for verification or additional contact information (if necessary).
11. Enter your **Zip/Postal Code**.
12. Enter your **Country**.
13. Enter your **State** or province.
14. Enter your **City**.
