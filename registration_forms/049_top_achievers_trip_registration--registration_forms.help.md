# Top Acheivers Trip Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from travelers registering for a trip, capturing basic contact details and trip specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first and last name in the respective fields.
2. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select the correct travel dates in the "Dates" field.
4. Choose your trip type (incentive, recognition, or other) in the "Trip Type" field.
5. Enter your reason for traveling in the "Travel Reason" field (optional).
6. Choose your class of service (economy, business, or first) in the "Class" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: A text field to enter your last name.
* **Email**: A field to enter your email address, which will be used for communication and trip updates.
* **Dates**: A date field to select your travel dates.
* **Trip Type**: A multiple-select field to choose the type of trip you're registering for (incentive, recognition, or other).
* **Travel Reason**: A note field to provide a brief description of why you're traveling.
* **Class**: A select-one field to choose your class of service (economy, business, or first).
* **Trip Type**: A multiple-select field to re-ask for the type of trip you're registering for, for consistency and validation purposes. 

## Tips
* Double-check your information before submitting the form.
* Make sure to select the correct travel dates.
* Provide a clear and concise reason for your travel, if applicable.
