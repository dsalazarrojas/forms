# industrial_reliability_analytics_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The industrial_reliability_analytics_training_registration form is a registration form for the Industrial Reliability Analytics Training session. It is designed to collect essential participant information to ensure a smooth and successful training experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out the form title and participant name fields on Page 1.
2. Enter the participant's email address and phone number on Page 2.
3. Select the participant's experience level and preferred date on Page 3.
4. Choose the training provider on Page 4.
5. Optionally, provide workshop details on Page 5.
6. Enter the session dates and time on Page 6.
7. Add any additional information on Page 7.
8. Confirm your participant and workshop agreements on Page 8.
9. Review and agree to the cancellation policy and terms and conditions on Page 9.
10. Optionally, provide participant comments on Page 10.
11. Finally, select the training status on Page 11.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Industrial Reliability Analytics Training Registration** (`form_title`, `text`, Required): 
  Enter the title of the training session you are registering for.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, Required): 
  Enter your name, as it will be used for registration and communication purposes.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, Required): 
  Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, Required): 
  Enter your phone number where you can be reached for updates and information.
* **Experience Level** (`experience_level`, `select_multiple`, Required): 
  Choose your experience level from Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, `date`, Required): 
  Select the date you prefer for the training session.
* **Training Provider** (`training_provider`, `select_one`, Required): 
  Choose the provider for the training session from Provider A, Provider B, or Provider C.
* **Workshop Details** (`workshop_details`, `note`, Optional): 
  Enter any additional information you think will be helpful for the workshop.
* **Session Dates** (`session_dates`, `text`, Optional): 
  Enter the dates of the workshop sessions.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, `time`, Optional): 
  Enter the time of the workshop sessions.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `note`, Optional): 
  Enter any additional information you think is relevant for the workshop.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, `note`, Optional): 
  Enter any notes you want to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Training Details** (`training_details`, `note`, Optional): 
  Enter any notes you want to share with the workshop organizers.
* **Participant Address** (`participant_address`, `text`, Optional): 
  Enter your address for the workshop location.
* **Training Location** (`training_location`, `text`, Optional): 
  Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Training Dates** (`training_dates`, `text`, Optional): 
  Enter the dates of the training sessions.
* **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, `text`, Optional): 
  Enter the number of hours of the workshop.
* **Training Time** (`training_time`, `time`, Optional): 
  Enter the time of the workshop sessions.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_multiple`, Required): 
  Choose how you will be paying for the workshop from Online, Offline, or Bank Transfer.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, `note`, Required): 
  Read and agree to the participant agreement terms.
* **Workshop Agreement** (`workshop_agreement`, `note`, Required): 
  Read and agree to the workshop agreement terms.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, `note`, Required): 
  Read and agree to the cancellation policy terms.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `note`, Required): 
  Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, `note`, Optional): 
  Enter any comments or feedback you have for the workshop.
* **Training Status** (`training_status`, `select_one`, Optional): 
  Choose the status of your training from Pending, Confirmed, or Cancelled.
