# Kickboxing Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a kickboxing course. It is a simple form designed to collect basic information about the user and course details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Start by filling out the "User Info" page with your details.
2. On the "Course" page, enter the name of the course you want to register for.
3. On the "Registration Details" page, select the duration of the course and the date and time you would like to start and end the course.
4. Enter the location where you would like to take the course.
5. If you have an instructor, enter their name and any additional information you would like to share about them.
6. If you have any medical conditions, enter a brief description.
7. Finally, enter your emergency contact and phone number.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Info** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Enter your name and other personal details.
* **Course** (`course`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the course you want to register for.
* **Date of Registration** (`date_of_registration`, date, required: false): Select the date you would like to start the course.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required: false): Enter the start date of the course.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required: false): Enter the end date of the course.
* **Duration** (`duration`, select multiple, required: false): Select the duration of the course from 1 week, 1 month, 2 months, or 1 year.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required: false): Enter the time you would like to take the course.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required: false): Enter the location where you would like to take the course.
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Instructor Info** (`instructor_info`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about the instructor.
* **Student Info** (`student_info`, text, required: false): Enter any information about the student taking the course.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, text, required: false): Enter any medical conditions you have.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: false): Enter the name and information of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Number** (`emergency_number`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Medical Insurance** (`medical_insurance`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your medical insurance provider.
* **Medical Insurance Number** (`medical_insurance_number`, text, required: false): Enter the number of your medical insurance.
* **Medical Insurance Provider** (`medical_insurance_provider`, text, required: false): Enter the provider of your medical insurance.
* **Medical Insurance Type** (`medical_insurance_type`, text, required: false): Enter the type of medical insurance you have.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of the student taking the course.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, email, required: false): Enter the email address of the student.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, text, required: false): Enter the phone number of the student.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, text, required: false): Enter the address of the student.
* **Student Date of Birth** (`student_dob`, date, required: false): Enter the date of birth of the student.
* **Student Date of Birth Day** (`student_dob_day`, text, required: false): Enter the day of the student's date of birth.
* **Student Date of Birth Month** (`student_dob_month`, text, required: false): Enter the month of the student's date of birth.
