# Property Owner Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect and store information about a property, including its owner's details and specific characteristics.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the first name and last name of the property owner.
2. Provide the email address of the property owner.
3. Enter the phone number of the property owner.
4. Enter the address of the property.
5. Specify the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.).
6. Enter the size of the property (in square feet or units).
7. Choose whether the property is for rent or sale.
8. Enter the move-in and move-out dates, if applicable.
9. Enter the name of the property agent or manager.
10. Enter the phone number and email of the property manager.
11. Enter the name of the property inspector.
12. Enter the phone number and email of the property inspector.
13. Enter any notes or comments about the property.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter the first name of the property owner. This is a required field.
* **Last Name**: Enter the last name of the property owner. This is a required field.
* **Email**: Enter the email address of the property owner. This field is optional but recommended.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the property owner. This field is optional.
* **Property Address**: Enter the address of the property. This field is optional.
* **Property Type**: Enter the type of property (e.g., house, apartment, etc.). This field is optional.
* **Property Size**: Enter the size of the property (in square feet or units). This field is optional.
* **Rent or Sale**: Choose whether the property is for rent or sale. This field is optional.
* **Move In Date**: Enter the date the property is available for move-in. This field is optional.
* **Move Out Date**: Enter the date the property will be vacated. This field is optional.
* **Agent Name**: Enter the name of the property agent or manager. This field is optional.
* **Agency Name**: Enter the name of the agency managing the property. This field is optional.
* **Agent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the property agent or manager. This field is optional.
* **Property Status**: Choose the status of the property (Available, Not Available, Under Renovation, or Sold). This field is optional.
* **Property Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the property. This field is optional.
* **Rent Price**: Enter the rent price of the property. This field is optional.
* **Sale Price**: Enter the sale price of the property. This field is optional.
* **Property Manager**: Enter the name of the property manager. This field is optional.
* **Property Manager Phone**: Enter the phone number of the property manager. This field is optional.
* **Property Manager Email**: Enter the email address of the property manager. This field is optional.
* **Property Inspector**: Enter the name of the property inspector. This field is optional.
* **Property Inspector Phone**: Enter the phone number of the property inspector. This field is optional.
* **Property Inspector Email**: Enter the email address of the property inspector. This field is optional.
* **Property Manager Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the property manager. This field is optional.
* **Property Agent**: Enter the name of the property agent. This field is optional.
