# Jewelry Making Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from attendees of a jewelry making workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (\*).
2. For each field, provide the relevant information for the attendee.
3. Ensure that the "Form Completed by" field is filled out accurately with your role in relation to this form submission (e.g., Administrator, Organizer, or Attendee).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Title** (`jewelry_making_workshop_attendance_form`): This is the title of the form.
* **Full Name** (`full_name`): Enter your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter your company name.
* **Role in Company** (`role`): Enter your role within the company (e.g., Employee, Manager, etc.).
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Name of the Event** (`event_name`): Enter the name of the event you're attending.
* **Date of the Event** (`event_date`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time of the Event** (`event_time`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Date of the Workshop** (`workshop_date`): Enter the date of the workshop.
* **Location of the Workshop** (`workshop_location`): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Number of Attendees Registered** (`attendees_registered`): Enter the number of attendees registered for the event.
* **Comments** (`comments`): Enter any additional comments or notes for the event.
* **Form Completed by** (`form_completed`): Select your role in relation to this form submission:
	+ Administrator: If you're an administrator responsible for managing the event.
	+ Organizer: If you're the person organizing the event.
	+ Attendee: If you're attending the event.

Note: This form should only be filled out once per attendee. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
