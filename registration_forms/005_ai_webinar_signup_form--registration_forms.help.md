# AI Webinar Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for attendees to register for an AI webinar. It's a collection of fields that gather information about your personal details, industry, and interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your full name and professional email address in the "Full Name" and "Email Address" fields, respectively.
2. If applicable, enter your job title and current organization in the "Job Title" and "Company or Organization" fields.
3. Select your industry from the provided options in the "Industry Sector" field.
4. Choose the topic of interest from the options in the "Topic of Interest" field.
5. Rate your current AI knowledge level in the "Current AI Knowledge Level" field.
6. Specify your expected takeaways from the webinar in the "Expected Takeaways" field.
7. Select your timezone for scheduling purposes in the "Timezone" field.
8. Choose a preferred date for the webinar in the "Preferred Webinar Date" field.
9. Select a preferred time for the webinar in the "Preferred Webinar Time" field.
10. If you have any accessibility needs or special requirements, enter them in the "Special Accommodations" field.
10. If you consent to marketing communications, select "Yes" in the "Consent to Marketing Communications" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Full Name**: Enter your complete legal name.
  (Type: text, Required: true)
- **Email Address**: Enter your professional email address for webinar access and materials.
  (Type: email, Required: true)
- **Job Title**: Enter your current position (optional).
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Company or Organization**: Enter your current organization (optional).
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Industry Sector**: Select your field of work from the provided options.
  (Type: select_one, Required: false, Options: Technology, Healthcare, Finance, Education, Government, Manufacturing, Other)
- **Topic of Interest**: Choose the topic that interests you most.
  (Type: select_one, Required: true, Options: AI Ethics and Governance, Machine Learning Fundamentals, AI in Healthcare, AI for Business Transformation, Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, AI Implementation Strategies)
- **Current AI Knowledge Level**: Rate your familiarity with artificial intelligence concepts.
  (Type: select_one, Required: true, Options: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert)
- **Expected Takeaways**: Specify what you hope to learn from this webinar.
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Timezone**: Select your timezone for scheduling purposes.
  (Type: select_one, Required: true, Options: EST, CST, MST, PST, GMT, CET, Other)
- **Preferred Webinar Date**: Choose a date that works best for you.
  (Type: date, Required: true)
- **Preferred Webinar Time**: Select a time that works best for you.
  (Type: time, Required: true)
- **Special Accommodations**: Enter any accessibility needs or special requirements (optional).
  (Type: text, Required: false)
- **Consent to Marketing Communications**: Choose whether to consent to marketing communications.
  (Type: select_one, Required: false, Options: Yes, No)

## Tips
- Please fill the form accurately to ensure correct information is captured for webinar scheduling and communication purposes.
- Ensure you select a valid date and time for the webinar that works best for you.
- If you have any special requirements or accessibility needs, please let us know.
