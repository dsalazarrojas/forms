# Insurance Claims Adjuster Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Insurance Claims Adjuster Training Registration form is designed to collect information from users registering for training programs as Insurance Claims Adjusters or Trainers. This form helps us process your registration and facilitates communication with our team.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Last Name in the "Last Name" field.
2. Enter the name of the workshop you are attending in the "Workshop Name" field.
3. Provide your Email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your Company name in the "Company" field.
5. Select your Role as either "Adjuster" or "Trainer" from the "Role" dropdown menu.
6. Enter your Phone number in the "Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Last Name** (required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Workshop Name** (required): Enter the name of the workshop you are registering for.
* **Email** (required): Enter your official email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Company** (required): Enter your company name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Role** (required): Select your role as either "Adjuster" or "Trainer".
* **Phone** (required): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.

## Tips
* Please ensure you have filled in all required fields accurately and completely.
* Double-check your details before submitting the form.
* Only submit this form once with correct and up-to-date information.
* If you have any issues or questions, please contact our support team for assistance.
