# Tenant Mental Wellness Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Tenant Mental Wellness Program Registration Form is designed to register tenants who are interested in the mental wellness program offered by [organization/property manager]. This form will help us understand your needs and preferences, ensuring that you receive the right support and resources to address your mental health concerns.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Enter your name in the "What is your name?" field.
2. Provide your contact information in the "How can we contact you?" field.
3. Indicate your interest in the mental wellness program in the "Are you interested in the mental wellness program?" field.
4. Select the type of report you need in the "What type of report do you need?" field.
5. Choose how you would like to be contacted in the "How would you like to be contacted?" field.
6. Confirm that you are working with a mental wellness provider in the "Mental Wellness Provider" field.
7. Enter the start and end dates of the program in the "Program Start Date" and "Program End Date" fields.
8. Provide any additional comments or information about your situation in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **What is your name?** (`tenant_details`, text, required/optional): This is the field where you enter your name.
- **How can we contact you?** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): This field is where you provide your contact information, so we can reach out to you.
- **Are you interested in the mental wellness program?** (`program_interest`, select_one, required): Choose yes or no to indicate your interest in the program.
- **What type of report do you need?** (`report_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of report you need to help you with your mental health concerns.
- **How would you like to be contacted?** (`contact_method`, select_one, required): Choose how you would like to be contacted (phone, email, or mail).
- **Mental Wellness Provider** (`mental_wellness_provider`, select_one, required): Confirm that you are working with a mental wellness provider.
- **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, required): Enter the start date of the program.
- **Program End Date** (`program_end_date`, date, required): Enter the end date of the program.
- **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, note, required): Provide any additional comments or information about your situation.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you fill out all required fields.
* If you have any concerns or questions, do not hesitate to reach out to us.
* We will review your form submission carefully to ensure that we can provide you with the right support and resources.
