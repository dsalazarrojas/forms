# Global Education Exchange Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect basic information from participants for registration purposes in an academic or educational setting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant details in the first field.
2. Provide program details in the second field.
3. Enter your contact details in the third field.
4. Enter any additional exchange details in the fourth field.
5. If you need to provide emergency contact details, enter them in the last field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, title, or any other information relevant to your participation.

### Program Details
* **Program Details** (`program_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your program of interest or any relevant information about the educational program you are involved in.

### Contact Details
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, such as name, email, and phone number, for future communication purposes.

### Exchange Details
* **Exchange Details** (`exchange_details`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional information related to the educational exchange you are participating in.

### Emergency Contact Details
* **Emergency Contact Details** (`emergency_contact_details`, text, required/optional): Enter the contact information of an emergency contact person or group in case of an emergency.

## Tips

* Please provide accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
* If you are unsure about any field, you can always ask for clarification.
* This form is intended for general registration purposes and may not cover all specific requirements of your program or institution; please review the form carefully before submitting it.
* If you need to edit or update your information, contact the relevant authorities directly.
