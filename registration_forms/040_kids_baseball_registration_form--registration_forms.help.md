# kids_baseball_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The kids_baseball_registration_form is a registration form for children to participate in a baseball team. This form is used to collect essential information from parents or guardians about their child, including contact details, birth date, and consent.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the child's first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter the child's last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your first name in the "Parent First Name" field.
4. Enter your last name in the "Parent Last Name" field.
5. Enter your email address in the "Parent Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Parent Phone" field.
7. Enter the child's date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy" in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter the child's age in the "Age" field.
9. Select one of the options in the "Parent Consent" field to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text): Enter the child's first name.
* **Last Name** (text): Enter the child's last name.
* **Parent First Name** (text): Enter your first name.
* **Parent Last Name** (text): Enter your last name.
* **Parent Email** (email): Enter your email address to enable us to contact you.
* **Parent Phone** (text): Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of an emergency.
* **Date of Birth** (date): Enter the child's date of birth in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
* **Age** (number): Enter the child's age.
* **Parent Consent** (select_one): Select one of the options indicating your consent.

## Tips

* Ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* If you are unsure about any field, please feel free to contact us for assistance.
* Please be aware that this form is for new players only; if you have previously registered your child, do not use this form again.
* Please note that by submitting this form, you are granting us consent to collect and use the provided information for the purpose of registering your child for the baseball team.
