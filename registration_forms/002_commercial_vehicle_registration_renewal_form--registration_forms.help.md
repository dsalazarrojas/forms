To avoid any discrepancies, it's essential to review the form carefully and ensure it's completed for the correct vehicle.

# Commercial Vehicle Registration Renewal Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The commercial vehicle registration renewal form is used to update the information for a commercial vehicle's registration. This form ensures the vehicle's details are accurate and compliant with regulations, allowing you to continue operating it without interruption.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Select the type of registration renewal: Choose between Individual Commercial Vehicle and Fleet Renewal (multiple vehicles).
2. Provide registrant details: Enter the full name and address as they appear on the current registration.
3. Update vehicle information: Enter the vehicle's identification number (VIN), license plate number, GVWR, and fuel type.
4. Confirm insurance and compliance: Enter the commercial insurance policy number, provider name, and confirm that the vehicle is properly insured.
5. Confirm safety inspections: Select whether the vehicle has passed a safety inspection in the last 12 months and enter the date of the inspection.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renewal Type** (`Renewal Type`, `select_one`, **Required**: true): Select the type of registration renewal that suits your needs.
* **Registered Owner Full Name or Business Name** (`Registrant Name`, `text`, **Required**: true): Enter the name as it appears on the current registration.
* **Registered Address** (`Registrant Address`, `text`, **Required**: true): Include the full address with street, city, state, and zip code.
* **Primary Contact Phone Number** (`Phone Number`, `text`, **Required**: true): Enter the primary contact phone number.
* **Email Address for Confirmation** (`Email Address`, `email`, **Required**: true): Enter the email address for confirmation purposes.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)** (`Vehicle VIN`, `text`, **Required**: true): Enter the vehicle's identification number for verification.
* **Current License Plate Number** (`License Plate Number`, `text`, **Required**: true): Enter the current license plate number.
* **Gross Vehicle Weight Rating (GVWR)** (`GVWR`, `number`, **Required**: true): Enter the vehicle's GVWR in pounds (lbs).
* **Fuel Type** (`Fuel Type`, `select_one`, **Required**: true): Select the type of fuel used by the vehicle.
* **Renewal Term Duration** (`Renewal Term`, `select_one`, **Required**: true): Select the renewal period.
* **Commercial Insurance Policy Number** (`Policy Number`, `text`, **Required**: true): Enter the active policy number.
* **Insurance Provider Name** (`Provider Name`, `text`, **Required**: true): Enter the name of the insurance provider.
* **US DOT Number** (`US DOT Number`, `text`, **Required**: false): This field is not required for most commercial vehicles, but it's essential for those engaging in interstate commerce.
* **Has the vehicle passed a safety inspection in the last 12 months?** (`Safety Inspection`, `select_one`, **Required**: true): Select whether the vehicle has passed a safety inspection.
* **Date of Last Safety Inspection** (`Inspection Date`, `date`, **Required**: true): Enter the date of the last safety inspection.
* **I confirm the information is correct and the vehicle is properly insured** (`Signature Confirmation`, `text`, **Required**: true): Type your full name to sign and confirm the information.
