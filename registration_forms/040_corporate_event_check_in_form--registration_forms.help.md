# Corporate Event Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Event Check In Form is used to register and provide information about your attendance to a corporate event. This helps the event organizers to track attendance and manage the event more efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your company name.
2. Enter your name and email address.
3. Provide your phone number.
4. Enter the title of the event you are attending.
5. Choose the date of the event.
6. Select the time of the event.
7. (Optional) Enter the duration of the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the company you are representing.
* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, text, required): Enter your name as an attendee.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event Title** (`event_title`, text, required): Enter the title of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required): Select the time of the event.
* **Event Duration** (`event_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the event if known.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the company name you enter is accurate and up-to-date.
* Ensure that your email and phone number are accurate and accessible.
* Double-check the event title, date, and time to ensure they match the actual event details.
* If you have any special requests or requirements, feel free to contact the event organizers.
