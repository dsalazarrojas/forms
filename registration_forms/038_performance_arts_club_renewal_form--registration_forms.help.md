# performance_arts_club_renewal_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The performance arts club membership renewal form is used to update the membership status and payment records for the Performance Arts Club.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in all the required fields, including membership type, payment method, and contact information.
2. Ensure that you have provided accurate and up-to-date information in all fields.
3. Review the form carefully before submitting it for processing.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Membership Details** (`membership_details`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your membership details, including your name and other relevant information.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select the payment method you would like to use for your membership renewal, such as Payment by Credit Card, Payment by Check, etc.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Provide any additional payment details, such as payment date and amount.
* **Payment Terms and Conditions** (`payment_terms_and_conditions`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ Please review the payment terms and conditions carefully before proceeding.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your contact information, including email and phone number.
* **Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (`contact_address`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your address.
* **Payment Total** (`payment_total`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the total payment amount.
* **Payment Date** (`payment_date`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the payment date.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`):
	+ Select the type of membership you would like to renew, such as Monthly, Quarterly, or Yearly.
* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the start date of your membership.
* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the end date of your membership.
* **Payment Method Details** (`payment_method_details`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Provide any additional payment method details.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, `note`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter any additional comments or information about your membership renewal.
* **Membership Level** (`membership_level`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select the level of membership you would like to renew, such as Basic, Premium, or Standard.
* **Membership Status** (`membership_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select the status of your membership, such as Active, Inactive, or Pending.
* **Reason for Renewal** (`reason_for_renewal`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the reason for your membership renewal.
* **Payment Status** (`payment_status`, `select_one`, required: `false`):
	+ Select the payment status, such as Paid, Pending, or Unpaid.
* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter any additional information about your membership.
* **Membership Number** (`membership_number`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter your membership number.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, `number`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the payment amount.
* **Membership Start Date** (`membership_start_date`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the start date of your membership.
* **Membership End Date** (`membership_end_date`, `date`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the end date of your membership.
* **Membership Years Renewed** (`membership_years_renewed`, `text`, required: `false`):
	+ Enter the number of years you have renewed your membership.
