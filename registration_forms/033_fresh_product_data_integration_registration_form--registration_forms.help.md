# Fresh Product Data Integration Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fresh Product Data Integration Registration Form is designed to help businesses or individuals register their product data integration, providing a platform to collect and manage product information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Registration form title** field, which is a brief description of the registration.
2. Select the **Category** field by choosing between **Supplier** or **Retailer**, depending on your business type.
3. Optionally provide a **Description** of your product or services.
4. Add **Product feed** details, which may include relevant URLs or files.
5. If required, select **Data fields** that pertain to your product or services.
6. Add your **Technical contacts** email address for further communication.
7. Add your **Business contacts** email address, which may be used for billing or other purposes.
8. Choose the **Route submission** option that best describes your submission process.
9. Add any additional **Notes** about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Registration form title**: Enter a brief description of the registration to be used as a reference.
- **Category**: Choose between **Supplier** or **Retailer** to classify your business or individual as a supplier or retailer.
  - This field helps us understand what kind of integration you're providing.
- **Description**: Provide a detailed description of your product or service.
  - This field should be as specific and accurate as possible to facilitate proper integration.
- **Product feed**: Upload or paste relevant URLs or files to provide additional product data.
  - This field may be used for further analysis or reporting.
- **Data fields**: Select the fields relevant to your product or service from the options provided.
  - These fields should pertain to your specific product or service.
- **Technical contacts**: Enter your technical contact's email address for further communication.
  - This field is typically used for technical support or troubleshooting.
- **Business contacts**: Enter your business contact's email address for billing or other purposes.
  - This field is used for general communication or other business purposes.
- **Route submission**: Choose how you want to submit data, such as through a specific API or file transfer.
  - This field helps us understand how to receive your data for proper integration.
- **Notes**: Add any additional notes about your registration.
  - This field is used for any extra information we might need to know.
