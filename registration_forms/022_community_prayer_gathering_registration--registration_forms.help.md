# Community Prayer Gathering Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration purposes only and will not be used for any other reason. Please ensure that you fill it out accurately and truthfully to help us plan and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Attendee Information" section.
2. Provide your first and last name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Provide your street address (optional).
6. Enter your city (optional).
7. Enter your state (optional).
8. Enter your zip code (optional).
9. Provide details about your attendance in the "Gathering Information" field.
10. Select the prayer gathering name, date, time, and location.
11. Choose your attendance type (In-Person, Virtual/Online, or Both).
12. Enter the total number of people in your party.
13. If you are bringing children under 12, enter their number.
14. Check if you need childcare services.
15. Check if you have a prayer request.
16. If yes, provide details about your prayer request.
17. Check if you would like to volunteer.
18. Select the volunteer roles you are interested in.
19. If you have dietary restrictions, provide details.
20. If you need transportation assistance, check the box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Information**: Your personal details to help us contact you.
  * First Name: Your name.
  * Last Name: Your surname.
  * Email Address: For event updates.
  * Phone Number: Your contact number (optional).
  * Street Address: Your residence address (optional).
  * City: Your city (optional).
  * State: Your state (optional).
  * ZIP Code: Your postal code (optional).
* **First Name**: Your first name.
* **Last Name**: Your last name.
* **Email Address**: Your email address.
* **Phone Number**: Your phone number.
* **Street Address**: Your street address.
* **City**: Your city.
* **State**: Your state.
* **ZIP Code**: Your zip code.
* **Gathering Information**: Details about your attendance.
* **Prayer Gathering Name**: The name of the prayer gathering you will attend.
* **Gathering Date**: The date of the prayer gathering.
* **Gathering Time**: The time of the prayer gathering.
* **Gathering Location**: The location of the prayer gathering.
* **Attendance Type**: How you will attend (In-Person, Virtual/Online, or Both).
* **Total Number in Your Party**: The number of people in your party.
* **Number of Children Attending**: If you are bringing children under 12.
* **Do You Need Childcare Services**: Check if you need childcare services.
* **Do You Have a Prayer Request**: Check if you have a prayer request.
* **Prayer Request Details**: If yes, provide details about your request.
* **Do You Want to Volunteer**: Check if you would like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Roles of Interest**: Select all roles you are interested in.
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: If meals are provided, provide details about your dietary restrictions.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you need any accommodations.
* **Do You Need Transportation Assistance**: Check if you need transportation assistance.
* **How Did You Hear About This Gathering**: How you heard about the gathering.
* **Is This Your First Time Attending**: Check if this is your first time attending.

## Tips
- Ensure that all fields are filled out accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, refer to the hints provided.
