# Charity Program Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

This guide will walk you through the Charity Program Extension Form, which is used to request an extension for the participant's current program. The form is used to collect information that will help determine the participant's eligibility for an extension and ensure that they can complete the program successfully.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in the required fields with the requested information.
2. Be as detailed as possible in the form sections that require it.
3. If you need help or clarification on any of the fields, feel free to reach out to your case manager or support team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Participant Information

* **First Name** (`participant_first_name`): Your first name is required to verify your identity. Please enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`participant_last_name`): Your last name is required to verify your identity. Please enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email Address** (`email`): This is your contact email address where you will receive important notifications and updates.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): This is your contact number where you can be reached for further communication.

### Program Information

* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`): If you have an ID assigned by the program, please enter it here. This will help us track your progress.
* **Current Program** (`program_name`): Please select the name of the program you are currently enrolled in.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`): Enter the date you started the program.
* **Scheduled Completion Date** (`program_end_date`): Enter the date you are scheduled to complete the program.
* **Reason for Extension Request** (`extension_reason`): Select the reason why you are requesting an extension from the options provided.
* **Describe Your Situation** (`extension_details`): Please explain why you need more time to complete the program.

### Extension Request

* **Requested Extension Length** (`extension_length`): Select the duration of the extension you are requesting.
* **Proposed New End Date** (`new_end_date`): Enter the date you plan to complete the program.
* **Progress Made So Far** (`progress_made`): Please describe the progress you have made in the program so far.
* **Remaining Requirements** (`remaining_requirements`): List the remaining requirements you need to complete the program.
* **Plan for Completion** (`completion_plan`): Outline how you plan to complete the program.

### Support and Commitment

* **Additional Support Needed** (`support_needed`): Select from the options if you require additional support, such as extra counseling sessions or financial assistance.
* **Current Case Manager** (`case_manager`): If you have a case manager, enter their name or ID.
* **Have You Had Extensions Before?** (`prior_extensions`): Indicate if you have had prior extensions.
* **Commitment Statement** (`commitment_statement`): Select your level of commitment to completing the program.

### Additional Information

* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`): Please provide any other information that might help us understand your situation.

By following this guide, you will be able to provide the necessary information for a successful extension request.
