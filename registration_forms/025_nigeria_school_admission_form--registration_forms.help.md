# Nigeria School Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register new students in a school, and gather necessary information for their admission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's details in the "Enter New Student" field.
2. Provide the student's name, date of birth, email, phone number, and address in the subsequent fields.
3. Select the student's level (High, Low, Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary) and course (Science, Arts, Business, Engineering, or Social Science).
4. Enter the admission date and time.
5. Provide a reason for admission.
6. Enter your signature as the student and parent.
7. Enter the school's details (name, address, phone number, and email).
8. Confirm that the information provided is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enter New Student**: Enter a brief description of the new student.
* **Student Name**: Enter the name of the student.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter the student's date of birth in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Student Email**: Enter the student's email address.
* **Student Phone**: Enter the student's phone number.
* **Parent Name**: Enter the name of the parent/guardian.
* **Parent Email**: Enter the parent/guardian's email address.
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the parent/guardian's phone number.
* **Student Level**: Select the student's level (High, Low, Primary, Secondary, or Tertiary).
* **Select Student Level**: Select the student's level from the provided options.
* **Student Course**: Select the student's course (Science, Arts, Business, Engineering, or Social Science).
* **Select Student Course**: Select the student's course from the provided options.
* **Admission Date**: Enter the date of admission in YYYY-MM-DD format.
* **Admission Time**: Enter the time of admission in 24-hour format (HH:MM).
* **Reason For Admission**: Enter a brief reason for the student's admission.
* **Student Signature**: Sign as the student to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Parent Signature**: Sign as the parent/guardian to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of the school.
* **School Address**: Enter the address of the school.
* **School Phone**: Enter the phone number of the school.
* **School Email**: Enter the email address of the school.
* **School Signature**: Sign as the school to confirm the accuracy of the information provided.
* **Submission**: Enter any additional comments or remarks.
* **Confirmation**: Confirm that the information provided is accurate and complete.
