# Motorsport Event Driver Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Motorsport Event Driver Declaration Form is a document that drivers must complete before participating in a motorsport event. This form is designed to gather essential information about the driver, their vehicle, and their declaration of safety measures.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully and answer all questions accurately.
2. Complete each field in the form, ensuring that all required fields are filled out.
3. Verify that all fields are accurate and up-to-date before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Event Info** (`event_info`, text, required: false): Please enter a brief description of the event, including the name of the event, date, and location.
 
- **Vehicle Info** (`vehicle_info`, text, required: false): Provide information about the vehicle you will be using for the event. This can include the make, model, and license plate number.
 
- **Safety Equipment Checklist** (`safety_equipment`, select_multiple, required: true): Please select all the safety equipment you will be using during the event. This includes a helmet, fireproof suit, and first aid kit.
 
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about the event, please enter it here.
 
- **Event Questions** (`event_questions`, note, required: false): If you have any questions or concerns about the event, please enter them here.
 
- **Contact Info** (`contact_info`, email, required: true): Enter your contact information, including your email address.
 
- **Declaration** (`declaration`, text, required: true): Please provide a clear statement declaring your agreement to participate in the event, including your understanding of the rules and regulations.
 
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required: true): Enter the name and contact information of an emergency contact person.
 
- **Signature** (`signature`, number, required: true): Sign this form to confirm your declaration.
 
- **Event Info Additional** (`event_info_additional`, text, required: false): If you have any additional information about the event, please enter it here.
