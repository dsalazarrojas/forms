# Team Social Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registrations and feedback from team members for team social events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure your registration is successful.
2. Provide your name and email address to help us contact you about the event.
3. Indicate your plans to attend the event (Yes/No).
4. Share any dietary needs (e.g., Yes/No, food allergies, etc.).
5. Specify your activity preferences (e.g., Yes/No, specific activities, etc.).
6. Confirm your attendance plans (Yes/No).
7. Provide any additional comments or feedback about the event.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Name**: Your name, which will help us identify you as a team member.
* **Email**: Your email address, which we'll use to communicate with you about the event.
* **RSVP**: Indicate your plans to attend the event by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **RSVP Response**: Provide any additional comments about your RSVP decision (e.g., "I'll be there but will bring my own food").
* **Dietary needs**: If you have any dietary restrictions or special requests (e.g., "Yes, I need a vegetarian option").
* **Activity preferences**: Specify any activities or events you'd like to participate in or avoid (e.g., "Yes, hiking").
* **Attending**: Confirm your plans to attend the event (Yes/No).
* **Attending response**: Provide any additional comments or feedback about your attending plans.
* **Comments**: Share any general comments or feedback about the event or your experience.

## Tips
* Be honest about your dietary needs and activity preferences to ensure we can accommodate you.
* Use the "Yes/No" options for simple selections, and use the text fields for more detailed information.
* Double-check your email address and name for accuracy.
