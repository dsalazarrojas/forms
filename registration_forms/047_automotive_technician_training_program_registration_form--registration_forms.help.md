# Automotive Technician Training Program Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Automotive Technician Training Program Registration Form is designed to collect essential information about you as an applicant to our program. This form will help us get to know you better, understand your background, and ensure a smooth and effective application process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form correctly, follow these steps:

1. Fill out each field accurately and truthfully, making sure to answer every required field.
2. Review each section carefully and ensure that you have provided all necessary information.
3. Check that your contact information is up to date and correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`full_name`, text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents (e.g., passport, driver's license).
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Please enter your active email address where you can be contacted for official updates about the program.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required): Please enter your phone number where you can be reached for official communication.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth, ensuring it matches your official documents.
* **Street Address** (`street_address`, text, required): Enter your current street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where you live.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state where you live.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter your zip code.
* **Highest Degree Attained** (`highest_degree`, select_one, required): Choose the highest degree you have attained (e.g., High School Diploma, Associate Degree, Bachelor Degree, etc.).
* **Prior Automotive Experience** (`prior_automotive`, select_one, required): Choose how much prior automotive experience you have (e.g., None, Some (Less than 1 year), Moderate (1-3 years), Substantial (3+ years)).
* **Current Certifications or Licenses** (`certifications_held`, text, optional): If applicable, enter any current certifications or licenses you hold (e.g., ASE, EPA, etc.).
* **Preferred Program Track** (`program_track`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred program track (e.g., General Automotive Technician, Diesel Technician, etc.).
* **Program Duration Preference** (`program_duration`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred program duration (e.g., 6 Months, 1 Year, 2 Years, Full-Time Intensive).
* **Preferred Start Date** (`start_date_preference`, date, required): Choose your preferred start date for the program.
* **Specialty Areas of Interest** (`specialty_interests`, select_multiple, required): Select all areas of interest that apply to you (e.g., Engine Repair, Transmission, Electrical Systems, etc.).
* **Career Goals Upon Completion** (`career_goals`, text, required): Describe your career goals after completing the program.
* **Can You Commit to Full-Time Study** (`full_time_commitment`, select_one, required): Choose if you can commit to full-time study.
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): The date you submit your application.

Please note that some fields are required and must be filled out correctly for your application to be processed. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
