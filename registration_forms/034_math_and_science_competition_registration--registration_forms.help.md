# Math And Science Competition Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information and agreements from participants in math and science competitions.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Begin by providing your participant information, including your name, title, employer, position, phone number, and email address.
2. Select the options that apply to you for participant selections and event selections.
3. Write any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with us.
4. Confirm your email address by re-entering it in the confirm email field.
5. Sign and agree to the terms and conditions by selecting 'Yes' for each agreement question.
6. Complete the participant comments and event comments fields with any additional information you'd like to provide.
7. Finally, review and submit your registration form.
