# Sculpture Workshop Reservation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from users to reserve a spot in the Sculpture Workshop. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field. This is a required field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field. This is a required field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. This is used to contact you about the workshop. Make sure to enter it correctly, as it will be used to send important information. 
4. Enter your email address again in the "Email (again)" field. This is a duplicate of the "Email" field, likely to ensure the entered email is correct.
5. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field, if you wish to be contacted by phone.
6. Select the desired workshop time from the "Workshop" dropdown. Choose from "Morning", "Afternoon", or "Evening" to determine when you want to attend the workshop.
7. Enter the date you want to attend the workshop in the "Workshop Date" field.
8. Enter the time of the workshop you want to attend in the "Workshop Time" field.
9. Enter the number of participants attending with you in the "Number of Participants" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email (Text)**: Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Email (again) (Text)**: Enter your email address again to confirm its accuracy.
* **Phone (Text)**: Enter your phone number to be contacted by the workshop organizers.
* **Workshop (Select One)**: Choose the time of the workshop you want to attend.
*   * Morning *
*   * Afternoon *
*   * Evening *
* **Workshop Date (Date)**: Enter the date you want to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time (Time)**: Enter the time of the workshop you want to attend.
* **Number of Participants (Number)**: Enter the number of participants that will be attending with you. 

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your email address correctly to receive workshop updates.
* Choose a workshop time that suits you best.
* Enter the correct date for the workshop you want to attend.
* If you need to bring more than one person, update the "Number of Participants" field accordingly.
