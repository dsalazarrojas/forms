# stakeholder_meeting_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "stakeholder_meeting_attendance_form" is used to register a stakeholder meeting attendance, enabling meeting organizers to track attendance status and gather additional information from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide meeting details on **Meeting Details** page.
2. Enter participant details on **Participant Details** page.
3. Select attendance status and attendance type on **Attendance Status** and **Attendance** pages respectively.
4. Add any **Additional Info** relevant to the meeting.
5. Enter **Submitted By** and **Submit Date/Time** for the submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Details
* **Meeting Details** (`1`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of the meeting.

### Participant Details
* **Participant Details** (`2`, `text`, required: false): Provide any relevant details about the participant, such as name, role, or department.

### Attendance Status
* **Attendance Status** (`3`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select all the status that apply to the participant's attendance.
  Example: Active, Inactive

### Attendance
* **Attendance** (`4`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the attendance type.
  Example: Present, Absent, Late, Undetermined

### Additional Info
* **Additional Info** (`5`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional information about the participant's attendance.

### Submitted By
* **Submitted By** (`6`, `note`, required: false): Enter the name or initials of the person submitting the form.

### Submit Date/Time
* **Submit Date** (`7`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date the form was submitted.
* **Submit Time** (`8`, `time`, required: false): Enter the time the form was submitted.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you have filled out all required fields accurately.
- Double-check the form before submitting to prevent any errors.
- If you are unsure about any field, you can reach out to the meeting organizer for assistance.
