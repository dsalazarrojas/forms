# Fitness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Registration Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals who want to participate in a fitness-related program or activity.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Personal Info** page with your name, email address, and any other relevant personal details.
2. Provide your **Contact Info**, including your phone number and any other relevant contact information.
3. Enter your **Application Info**, detailing why you are interested in participating in the fitness program or activity.
4. Share your **Motivation**, explaining why you want to participate in the fitness program or activity.
5. Answer the **Health Info** questions to provide more context about your health and fitness background.
6. Provide information about your **Sports Experience** if you have any.
7. Share any relevant **Nutrition Info** about your dietary habits and preferences.
8. Finally, click the **Submit** button to complete the form and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Personal Info** (Required): This is the first page of the form where you will provide your personal details such as your name and email address. Please fill out this page completely to ensure that we have the correct information about you.
- **Contact Info** (Optional): This page is where you will provide your contact information, including your phone number. This is not required, but it is recommended so that we can get in touch with you if needed.
- **Application Info** (Required): This page is where you will explain why you are interested in participating in the fitness program or activity. Please provide a clear and concise explanation of your motivation.
- **Motivation** (Optional): This page is where you can elaborate on your motivation for participating in the fitness program or activity. Please feel free to add any additional details that you think are relevant.
- **Health Info** (Optional): This page is where you will provide more context about your health and fitness background. Please answer the questions truthfully to help us better understand your needs.
- **Sports Info** (Optional): This page is where you will provide information about your sports experience, if you have any. Please be as detailed as possible.
- **Nutrition Info** (Optional): This page is where you will share any relevant information about your dietary habits and preferences. Please answer honestly to help us tailor the program to your needs.
- **Submit** (Required): This is the final step to complete the form. Please review all the pages carefully before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out completely.
