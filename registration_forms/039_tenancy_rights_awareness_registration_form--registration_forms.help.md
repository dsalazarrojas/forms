# Tenancy Rights Awareness Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for participants to register their awareness of tenancy rights, providing essential information for further communication and record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your Participant Name, Email, and Phone.
3. Select the correct address format (e.g., country, state, and postcode).
4. Check the boxes for "Receive Updates" and "Receive Newsletter" according to your preferences.
5. Select the correct language and time zone.
6. Review the terms and conditions.

* **State**: <u>Select your state or region.</u>
