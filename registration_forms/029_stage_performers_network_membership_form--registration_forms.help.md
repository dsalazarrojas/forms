# Stage Performers Network Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Stage Performers Network Membership Form is a crucial step in the membership enrollment process for individuals or groups interested in joining the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Performer Details**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents or business registration.
*   **Email**: A valid email address is required for communication purposes.
*   **Phone Number**: Provide your contact number for easy communication and scheduling.
*   **Performance History**: Describe your performance experience, including notable performances, awards, or achievements.
*   **Membership Type**: Choose between Individual, Ensemble, or Company as your membership type.
*   **Additional Members**: If part of a group, list the names of the additional members.
*   **Additional Information**: Enter any additional details relevant to your membership application.
*   **Verify Email**: Confirm your email address to ensure accurate communication.
*   **Date**: Enter the date you'd like to schedule your first performance or event.
*   **Time**: Select the time for your scheduled performance or event.
*   **Membership Status**: Select your current membership status from New Member, Existing Member, or Suspended Member.
*   **Notes**: Additional notes can be entered here for the network to consider.

## Tips
*   Ensure all fields are accurate and up-to-date.
*   Use a valid email address for communication purposes.
*   Be specific when describing your performance history.
*   Choose the correct membership type.
*   Enter all required information for the membership application.
*   Verify your email address.
*   Schedule performances or events accurately.
*   Select your correct membership status.
*   Enter any additional notes for the network to consider.
