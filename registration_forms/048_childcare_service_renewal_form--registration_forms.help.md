# Childcare Service Renewal Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to facilitate the renewal process for childcare services. It requires parents or guardians to provide necessary information to update their child's enrollment and schedule. Please ensure that you have all the required information and documents before proceeding.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below:

1. Ensure you have all the required information and documents ready.
2. Select the renewal date from the calendar.
3. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
4. Review and confirm your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Renewal Date**: Choose the date you would like to renew your child's services. Please note that this date should be in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email**: Enter the primary email address for contact purposes.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone**: Enter the primary phone number for contact purposes.
* **Home Address**: Enter the full street address.
* **City**: Enter the city of residence.
* **State**: Enter the state or province.
* **Zip Code**: Enter the postal or zip code.
* **Child First Name**: Enter the legal first name of the child.
* **Child Last Name**: Enter the legal last name of the child.
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the child's birth date.
* **Child Age**: Enter the current age of the child in years.
* **Current Program**: Select the program the child is currently enrolled in.
* **Renewal Period**: Choose the period you would like to renew for (e.g., next month, next 3 months, next 6 months, full year, summer session, school year).
* **Continuing Same Schedule**: Indicate if you will continue with the same schedule.
* **Schedule Changes**: Describe any schedule changes if you are not continuing with the same schedule.
* **Updated Emergency Contact**: Indicate if your emergency contact information has changed.
* **Emergency Contact Name**: Enter the full name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone**: Enter the phone number for the emergency contact (if the answer is yes).
* **Updated Medical Information**: Indicate if your child's medical information has changed.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies**: List any medical conditions or allergies if you answered yes.
* **Current Medications**: List any current medications if you answered yes.
* **Updated Authorized Pickup**: Indicate if your authorized pickup list has changed.
* **Authorized Pickup Persons**: List all people authorized to pick up your child if you answered yes.
* **Tuition Rate Acknowledgement**: Confirm that you have reviewed the updated tuition rates.
* **Payment Method Update**: Indicate if you need to update your payment method.
* **New Payment Method**: Select the new payment method if you answered yes.
* **Renewal Fee**: Enter the renewal fee amount if applicable.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Confirm that you agree to the renewed terms and conditions.
* **Parent or Guardian Signature**: Type your full name as your electronic signature.
* **Signature Date**: Enter the date of your electronic signature.

Please review and submit the form accurately to complete the renewal process.
