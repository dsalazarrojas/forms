# Annual Foot And Ankle Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide provides an overview of the Annual Foot And Ankle Conference Registration form, including its purpose, explanation of how to complete it, and a field-by-field guide to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill in your name, contact details, and other personal information accurately.
3. Select your medical specialty from the provided options.
4. Choose the conference year you are attending.
5. Select your registration type.
6. Indicate the days you will be attending.
7. If attending workshops, choose from the available options.
8. If you need continuing education credits, select the required number.
9. Provide your license number and state for CME tracking.
10. Select your dietary restrictions.
11. Indicate your interest in networking events and exhibit hall.
12. If you need hotel accommodations or transportation assistance, let us know.
13. Confirm your first-time attendee status.
14. Choose how you heard about the conference.
15. Select your payment method.
16. Enter the total fee due.
17. Agree to the cancellation policy.
18. If necessary, provide any additional comments or special requests.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Name** (`attendee_name`, Text, Required): Please enter your full name.
* **Professional Credentials** (`credentials`, Text, Optional): Enter your professional credentials.
* **Email Address** (`email`, Email, Required): For conference updates and confirmation.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, Text, Optional): Enter your best contact number.
* **Organization or Institution** (`organization`, Text, Optional): Indicate where you work.
* **Medical Specialty** (`specialty`, Select One, Required): Select your area of specialization.
* **Conference Year** (`conference_year`, Select One, Required): Choose the year you are attending.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, Select One, Required): Select your registration category.
* **Day Selection** (`day_selection`, Select One, Optional): Choose which days you will attend.
* **Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose workshops if applicable.
* **CME Credits Needed** (`cme_credits_needed`, Select One, Required): Indicate if you need CME credits.
* **License Number** (`license_number`, Text, Optional): Enter your license number for CME tracking.
* **License State** (`license_state`, Text, Optional): Enter your state of licensure.
* **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, Select Multiple, Required): Select your dietary restrictions.
* **Dietary Details** (`dietary_details`, Text, Optional): Enter any dietary restrictions or allergies.
* **Networking Events** (`networking_events`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose events you will attend.
* **Exhibit Hall Interest** (`exhibit_interest`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you will visit the exhibit hall.
* **Hotel Accommodation Needed** (`hotel_needed`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you need hotel recommendations.
* **Transportation Assistance** (`transportation_needed`, Select One, Optional): Indicate if you need transportation assistance.
* **First Time Attendee** (`first_time_attendee`, Select One, Required): Confirm if this is your first time attending the conference.
* **Referral Source** (`referral_source`, Select Multiple, Optional): Choose how you heard about the conference.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, Select One, Required): Select how you will pay.
* **Registration Fee** (`registration_fee`, Number, Required): Enter the total fee due.
* **Cancellation Policy** (`cancellation_policy`, Select One, Required): Agree to the cancellation policy.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, Text, Optional): Enter any special requests or needs.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, Text, Optional): Enter any additional comments.
