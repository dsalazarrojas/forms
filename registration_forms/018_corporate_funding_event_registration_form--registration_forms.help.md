# Corporate Funding Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Funding Event Registration Form is a tool used for registering and organizing corporate funding events such as conferences, meetings, or any other type of corporate event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Ensure you have already not registered for the same event. If you have, you will not be able to register again.
2. Fill in the form with the required information about the event.
3. Choose the correct event type from the dropdown list.
4. Enter the event name, date, time, location, and other relevant details.
5. Provide contact information of the event organizer.
6. If you are an attendee, fill in your details as well.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Type** (Event, Conference, Meeting): Choose the type of event you are registering.
* **Event Name** (text): Enter the name of the event.
* **Event Date** (date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Event Location** (text): Enter the location of the event.
* **Event Organizer** (text): Enter the name of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Phone** (text): Enter the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Event Organizer Email** (email): Enter the email of the event organizer.
* **Attendee Name** (text): If you are an attendee, enter your name.
* **Attendee Company** (text): If you are an attendee, enter your company name.
* **Attendee Title** (text): If you are an attendee, enter your title.
