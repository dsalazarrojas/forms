# Class Attendance Sign In Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Class Attendance Sign In Form is a tool used for students to sign in their attendance for a class. It captures essential details such as class name, instructor name, date, start time, room number, and student information for attendance tracking.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the class or course you are attending.
2. Type in the name of your instructor or teacher.
3. Provide the current date.
4. Select the start time of your class.
5. Optionally, enter the room number or location where the class is held.
6. Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed on the attendance record.
7. Enter your student ID number.
8. Provide your school email address.
9. Choose the time of your arrival.
10. Select your status as "On time", "Late arrival", or "Early departure".
11. If applicable, provide a reason for your late arrival.
12. If you are leaving early, enter the time of your departure.
13. If applicable, provide a reason for your early departure.
14. Confirm your participation in class activities by selecting "Yes, fully participated", "Yes, partially participated", or "No, did not participate".
15. If you have an assignment to submit, select "Yes, submitting now" or "Already submitted".
16. If you have any questions for the teacher, type them in the designated field.
17. Self-assess your understanding of the material by selecting "Very well", "Well", "Somewhat", "Not very well", or "Need help".
18. Finally, sign and submit the form to confirm your attendance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Class or Course Name**: Enter the name of the class or course you are attending.
* **Instructor Name**: Type in the name of your teacher or instructor.
* **Class Date**: Provide the current date.
* **Class Start Time**: Select the scheduled start time of your class.
* **Room Number or Location**: Optionally, enter the room number or location where the class is held.
* **Student Full Name**: Enter your full name as you want it to be displayed on the attendance record.
* **Student ID Number**: Enter your student ID number.
* **Student Email**: Provide your school email address.
* **Sign-In Time**: Choose the time of your arrival.
* **Attendance Type**: Select your status as "On time", "Late arrival", or "Early departure".
* **Reason for Late Arrival** (if applicable): Provide a reason for your late arrival.
* **Departure Time** (if applicable): If you are leaving early, enter the time of your departure.
* **Reason for Early Departure** (if applicable): If you are leaving early, provide a reason for your departure.
* **Did You Participate in Class Activities**: Confirm your participation in class activities.
* **Are You Submitting an Assignment**: If you have an assignment to submit, select "Yes, submitting now" or "Already submitted".
* **Do You Have Questions for the Instructor**: If you have any questions, type them in the designated field.
* **How Well Do You Understand Today's Material**: Self-assess your understanding of the material.
* **Student Signature**: Sign and submit the form to confirm your attendance.
