# Strategic Planning Meeting RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to help the meeting organizers track the attendance and plans of team members who have been invited to the strategic planning meeting.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your name (if applicable) as part of the form is a "Attendees" field which will ask your name.
2. Select the date of the meeting you plan to attend.
3. Choose the time of the meeting you plan to attend.
4. Enter the location of the meeting.
5. If you are planning to attend, select "Yes" for RSVP, otherwise select "No".
6. If you are attending, select the type of registration you need (if applicable).
7. Enter any comments about your attendance or any other relevant information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Meeting Details
* **Meeting Details** (`meeting_details`, `text`, required: false): A brief description of the meeting you plan to attend.

### Meeting Date
* **Meeting Date** (`meeting_date`, `date`, required: false): The date of the meeting.

### Meeting Time
* **Meeting Time** (`meeting_time`, `time`, required: false): The time of the meeting.

### Meeting Location
* **Meeting Location** (`meeting_location`, `text`, required: false): The location of the meeting.

### Attendees
* **Attendees** (`attendees`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select team members you plan to bring with you (e.g., John Doe, Jane Smith).

### RSVP
* **RSVP** (`rsvp`, `select_one`, required: false): Select "Yes" if you plan to attend, otherwise select "No".

### Registration Type
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required: false): If you are planning to attend, select the type of registration you need (e.g., "Yes").

### Comments
* **Comments** (`comments`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional comments or information about your attendance.
