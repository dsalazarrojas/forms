# Employee Growth Forum Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to gather important information from employees about their job details, including job title, department, and manager's contact information. The form is essential for the company's HR team to understand employee structures and growth opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please make sure you have all necessary information ready before starting the form.
2. Fill in your Employee Name, email, Job Title, and Department (if applicable).
3. Provide your phone number and manager's contact information, including email, name, phone number, and job title.
4. Choose how you report to your manager (Direct Report or Indirect Report).
5. Enter your start and end dates.
6. Choose your time slot.
7. Optionally, provide any comments if needed.
8. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Name (1)** (`employee_name`, text, required): This is where you enter your full name as an employee.
* **Email (2)** (`email`, email, required): Please enter your company email address.
* **Job Title (3)** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title as assigned by the company.
* **Department (4)** (`department`, text, optional): If you're in a department, please enter its name.
* **Phone Number (5)** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your work phone number.
* **Job Level (6)** (`job_level`, text, optional): Enter your job level as assigned by the company.
* **Job Function (7)** (`job_function`, text, optional): Enter your job function as assigned by the company.
* **Team (8)** (`team`, text, optional): Enter the name of your team.
* **Role (9)** (`role`, text, optional): Enter your role in the company.
* **Work Location (10)** (`work_location`, text, optional): Enter your work location.
* **Start Date (11)** (`start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started working with the company.
* **End Date (12)** (`end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you ended your employment with the company.
* **Time (13)** (`time`, time, required): Choose your time slot.
* **Comments (14)** (`comments`, note, optional): If you have any comments or concerns, please enter them here.
* **Manager Email (15)** (`manager_email`, email, required): Enter your manager's email address.
* **Manager Name (16)** (`manager_name`, text, required): Enter your manager's full name.
* **Manager Phone Number (17)** (`manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your manager's phone number.
* **Manager Job Title (18)** (`manager_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your manager's job title.
* **Manager Department (19)** (`manager_department`, text, optional): Enter your manager's department.
* **Manager Job Level (20)** (`manager_job_level`, text, optional): Enter your manager's job level.
* **Manager Job Function (21)** (`manager_job_function`, text, optional): Enter your manager's job function.
* **Manager Work Location (22)** (`manager_work_location`, text, optional): Enter your manager's work location.
* **Reporting Manager (23)** (`reporting_manager`, select_one, required): Choose how you report to your manager (Direct Report or Indirect Report).
* **Reporting Manager Email (24)** (`reporting_manager_email`, email, required): Enter your reporting manager's email address.
* **Reporting Manager Name (25)** (`reporting_manager_name`, text, required): Enter your reporting manager's full name.
* **Reporting Manager Phone Number (26)** (`reporting_manager_phone`, text, required): Enter your reporting manager's phone number.
* **Reporting Manager Job Title (27)** (`reporting_manager_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's job title.
* **Reporting Manager Department (28)** (`reporting_manager_department`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's department.
* **Reporting Manager Job Level (29)** (`reporting_manager_job_level`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's job level.
* **Reporting Manager Job Function (30)** (`reporting_manager_job_function`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's job function.
* **Reporting Manager Work Location (31)** (`reporting_manager_work_location`, text, optional): Enter your reporting manager's work location.
