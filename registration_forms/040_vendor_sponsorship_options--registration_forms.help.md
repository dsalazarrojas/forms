# Vendor Sponsorship Options - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor Sponsorship Options form is used to collect information from vendors about their interest in sponsoring an event. This form is used to facilitate communication and coordination with vendors.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the sponsorship options available and select the ones that best fit your sponsorship goals.
2. Fill out the fields that apply to your selected sponsorship options.
3. Provide any additional details that are required or relevant to your sponsorship.
4. Review the Terms and Conditions to understand the rules and guidelines for the sponsorship.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Sponsorship Options** (`vendor_sponsorship_options_form_1`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the sponsorship options that best fit your goals. This field is a list of options that you can choose from.
	+ Options: Yes, No
* **Sponsorship Options** (`vendor_sponsorship_options_form_2`, note, required: false): This field is not used in the backend, it's just a placeholder.
* **Event Details** (`event_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about the event that you think are relevant to your sponsorship.
* **Vendor Details** (`vendor_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your company that you think are relevant to your sponsorship.
* **Sponsorship Package** (`sponsorship_package`, text, required: false): Describe your sponsorship package in detail.
* **Sponsorship Package Price** (`sponsorship_package_price`, number, required: false): Provide the price of your sponsorship package.
* **Sponsor** (`sponsor`, text, required: false): Provide your company name.
* **Email** (`sponsor_email`, email, required: false): Provide your company email address.
* **Phone** (`sponsor_phone`, text, required: false): Provide your company phone number.
* **Sponsorship Details** (`sponsorship_details`, text, required: false): Provide any additional details about your sponsorship.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required: false): Review and agree to the Terms and Conditions before submitting the form.
