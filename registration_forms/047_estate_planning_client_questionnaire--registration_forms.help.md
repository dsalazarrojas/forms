# Estate Planning Client Questionnaire - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information about your estate planning needs and provide a comprehensive picture of your assets, beneficiaries, and wishes. This information will help us understand your unique circumstances and create a tailored plan to meet your goals.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

* Fill out the form in the provided fields.
* Ensure that you have not already submitted a similar questionnaire.
* Review and validate your information before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Estate Planning Client Questionnaire (page 1)
* **Estate Planning Client Questionnaire** (`estate_planning_client_questionnaire`, text, optional): This field is a text-based description of your estate planning needs and goals.
### First Name (`first_name`, text, optional): 
* Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification documents. This will help us understand your personal details.
* Example: John
### Last Name (`last_name`, text, optional): 
* Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification documents. This will help us understand your family background.
* Example: Doe
### Address (`address`, text, optional): 
* Enter your full address as it is currently stated on your identification documents. This will help us locate you in case of any urgent matters.
* Example: 123 Main St.
### Phone (`phone`, text, optional): 
* Enter your primary contact phone number. This will help us reach you in case of any urgent matters.
* Example: 555-123-4567
### Email (`email`, email, optional): 
* Enter your primary email address. This will help us communicate with you efficiently.
* Example: [john.doe@example.com](mailto:john.doe@example.com)
### Date of Birth (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): 
* Enter your date of birth in the format of YYYY-MM-DD. This will help us understand your age and any applicable rules.
* Example: 1990-01-01
### Social Security Number (`social_security_number`, number, optional): 
* Enter your social security number as it appears on your identification documents. This will help us understand your identity.
* Example: 123-45-6789

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have entered accurate and up-to-date information.
* If you are unsure about any field, please consult with your financial advisor or planner before proceeding.
* You can submit this form multiple times, but please note that duplicate submissions may delay the planning process.
* Please review and validate your information before submission to avoid errors.
