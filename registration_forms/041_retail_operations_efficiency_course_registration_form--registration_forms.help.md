# retail_operations_efficiency_course_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect participant information for the Retail Operations Efficiency Course. It aims to gather relevant details about the participant's company, job title, and agreement to attend the course. The form is intended for participants who are interested in improving their retail operations efficiency skills.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your participant information, including your company name, job title, and manager's name (if applicable).
2. Select the courses you are interested in attending from the list provided.
3. Read and agree to the participant agreement.
4. Add any additional comments you may have about your participation.
5. Sign and date the form to confirm your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Participant Information** (`participant_info`): Enter your name and contact information.
- **Course Information** (`course_info`): This field is not currently active, but you can add any additional information about your course preferences if needed.
- **Payment Information** (`payment_info`): This field is not currently active, but you can add any additional information about your payment preferences if needed.
- **Participant Courses** (`participant_courses`): Select the course(s) you are interested in attending from the list provided. The courses available are:
  • Operations Management
  • Visual Merchandising
  • Store Planning
  • Inventory Management
  • Loss Prevention
  • Store Operations
  • Supply Chain
  • Merchandising
  • Retail Management
  • Store Experience
- **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`): Read and agree to the terms and conditions of the course.
- **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`): Add any additional comments you may have about your participation.
- **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Sign and date the form to confirm your registration.
- **Participant Company** (`participant_company`): Enter your company name.
- **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`): Enter your job title.
- **Participant Manager** (`participant_manager`): Enter your manager's name (if applicable).

## Tips
- Please ensure that you select at least one course to participate in.
- Make sure to sign and date the form to confirm your registration.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us at [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
