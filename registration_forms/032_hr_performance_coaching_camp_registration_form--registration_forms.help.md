# HR Performance Coaching Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Performance Coaching Camp Registration Form is designed for employees to register for a performance coaching camp, a program aimed at enhancing their professional development and growth. This form is used to collect relevant information from employees, their managers, and to document the details of the coaching camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Ensure you have all the necessary information and approvals before proceeding.
2. Fill in your employee details, including your email and phone number.
3. Provide your manager's approval, if required.
4. Specify your coaching goals and the dates for the coaching camp.
5. Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share.
6. Select the status of your registration (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).
7. Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Employee Details (1)
* **Employee Details** (`employee_details`, text, optional): This field is for you to enter your employee details. Please provide your full name and any other relevant information.

### Manager Approvals (2)
* **Manager Approvals** (`manager_approvals`, text, optional): If your manager's approval is required, please enter their name and approval status.

### Coaching Goals (3)
* **Coaching Goals** (`coaching_goals`, text, optional): Describe your coaching goals and expectations for the camp.

### Performance Coaching Camp Details (4)
* **Performance Coaching Camp Details** (`performance_coaching_camp_details`, text, optional): Provide any additional information about the performance coaching camp.

### Start Date (5)
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the coaching camp.

### End Date (6)
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the coaching camp.

### Status (7)
* **Status** (`status`, select, optional): Select the status of your registration (Not Started, In Progress, or Completed).

### Notes (8)
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional comments or notes about your registration.

### Email (9)
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.

### Phone (10)
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.

## Tips
- Ensure you have all the necessary approvals and information before submitting your registration.
- Review your submission carefully before clicking the submit button.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact your HR department.
