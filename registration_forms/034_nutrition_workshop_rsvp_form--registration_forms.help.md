# nutrition_workshop_rsvp_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering your interest and providing necessary information for the upcoming nutrition workshop. Please complete this form carefully to ensure that we can accommodate your needs and preferences during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out your Participant Name, Email, Phone, Time, Date, Workshop, and RSVP Choice fields as required by the form.
2. If you have any dietary restrictions, provide them in the Dietary Restrictions field.
3. You can select multiple options for the RSVP Choice field.
4. Make sure to review your submission before clicking the submit button to ensure that your information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Name
• **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required): Enter your name as it should be displayed during the event.

### Email
• **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address so that we can contact you with updates and reminders.

### Phone
• **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so that we can contact you with last-minute updates or changes.

### Time
• **Time** (`time`, `time`, required): Select the time you plan to attend the workshop. Please note that this field does not affect registration or attendance.

### Date
• **Date** (`date`, `date`, required): Select the date you plan to attend the workshop.

### Workshop
• **Workshop** (`workshop`, `select_one`, required): Indicate whether you are interested in attending the workshop.

### RSVP Choice
• **RSVP Choice** (`rsvp_choice`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all the ways you would like to participate in the workshop. This field is optional but helps us plan the event according to your preferences.

### Dietary Restrictions
• **Dietary Restrictions** (`dietary_restrictions`, `text`, required): If you have any dietary restrictions, specify them here. This will help us accommodate your needs during the event.

### Dietary Restrictions (hint)
• **Dietary Restrictions (hint)** (`dietary_restrictions_hint`, `text`, optional): This field is for any additional information about your dietary restrictions. It's not required but can be helpful for us to plan accordingly.

## Tips
- Please make sure to review your submission carefully before clicking the submit button.
- If you are interested in attending multiple workshops, please submit a separate form for each one.
- You will receive an email with further instructions on how to complete your registration after submitting this form.
