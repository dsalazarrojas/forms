# Public Interest Networking Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is designed to help attendees register for a Public Interest Networking Event. It collects information about the attendee, their role in the industry, and their contact details.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first field.
2. Provide your **Email** in the second field.
3. Select your **Role** in the third field from the options provided (Account Manager, Community Manager, or Organizer).
4. If you wish, enter your **Organization** in the fourth field.
5. If you wish, enter your **Job Title** in the fifth field.
6. If you wish, enter your **Department** in the sixth field.
7. If you wish, enter your **Job Function** in the seventh and final field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name**: A text field to collect your first name.
* **Email**: A text field to collect your email address.
* **Role**: A dropdown select field with three options: Account Manager, Community Manager, or Organizer. Please select one of these roles that best describes your position.
* **Organization**: A text field to collect the name of your organization (if you have one).
* **Job Title**: A text field to collect your job title (if you have one).
* **Department**: A text field to collect the name of your department (if you have one).
* **Job Function**: A text field to collect your job function (if you have one).

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields (Role).
* If you have any issues with submitting the form, contact form@gic.mx.
