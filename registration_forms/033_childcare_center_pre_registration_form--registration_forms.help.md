# Childcare Center Pre-Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Childcare Center Pre-Registration Form is a way for us to get to know you and your family before we begin the registration process. This form helps us tailor our services to meet your needs and ensures a smooth and efficient experience for both our families and staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Answer all required fields.
3. Review your information carefully before submitting.
4. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pre-Registration Date** (`pre_registration_date`, date, required): This field is where you'll enter the date of your pre-registration.
* **Child First Name** (`child_first_name`, text, required): This is the child's legal first name.
* **Child Last Name** (`child_last_name`, text, required): This is the child's legal last name.
* **Child Nickname** (`child_nickname`, text, optional): This is the child's preferred name or nickname.
* **Child Date of Birth** (`child_date_of_birth`, date, required): This is the date of the child's birth.
* **Child Age at Enrollment** (`child_age_at_enrollment`, number, required): This is how old the child will be when starting care.
* **Child Gender** (`child_gender`, select_one, required): Select the child's gender.
* **Parent or Guardian First Name** (`parent_guardian_first_name`, text, required): This is the legal first name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Last Name** (`parent_guardian_last_name`, text, required): This is the legal last name of the parent or guardian.
* **Parent or Guardian Email** (`parent_guardian_email`, email, required): This is the primary email address for contact.
* **Parent or Guardian Phone** (`parent_guardian_phone`, text, required): This is the primary phone number for contact.
* **Home Address** (`home_address`, text, required): This is the full street address.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): This is the city of residence.
* **State** (`state`, text, required): This is the state or province.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): This is the postal or zip code.
* **Desired Start Date** (`desired_start_date`, date, required): This is when you would like to start care.
* **Care Schedule Needed** (`care_schedule_needed`, select_one, required): Select the schedule you need.
* **Preferred Room or Age Group** (`preferred_room_or_age_group`, select_one, required): Select the preferred room or age group.
* **Siblings Enrolled or Enrolling** (`siblings_enrolled`, select_one, required): Select if you have other children enrolled or enrolling.
* **Sibling Names** (`sibling_names`, text, optional): List the names of siblings.
* **How Did You Hear About Us** (`how_did_you_hear_about_us`, select_one, required): Select how you learned about our childcare center.
* **Childcare Subsidy Needed** (`childcare_subsidy_needed`, select_one, required): Select if you will be applying for childcare subsidy or assistance.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs_or_accommodations`, text, optional): This is a text field for listing any special needs or accommodations.
* **Medical Conditions or Allergies** (`medical_conditions_or_allergies`, text, required): This is a text field for listing any medical conditions or allergies.
* **Primary Language Spoken at Home** (`primary_language_spoken`, select_one, required): Select the primary language spoken at home.
* **Tour Requested** (`tour_requested`, select_one, required): Select if you would like to schedule a tour.
* **Preferred Tour Date** (`tour_date_preference`, date, optional): This is when you would like to tour.
* **Additional Questions or Comments** (`additional_questions_or_comments`, text, optional): This is a text field for any additional questions or comments.
