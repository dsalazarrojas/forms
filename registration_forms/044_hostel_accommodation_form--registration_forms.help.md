# Hostel Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to provide information about their accommodation preferences and needs when booking a room in a hostel. 
## How To Complete This Form
Please follow these steps to complete this form:
1. Provide your user information in the **user_info** field.
2. Select your preferred contact method(s) in the **contact_method** field.
3. Choose your accommodation type (single, double, or triple) in the **accommodation_choice** field.
4. Enter your check-in date in the **checkin_date** field.
5. Enter your check-out date in the **checkout_date** field.
6. Provide your accommodation preferences in the **accommodation_preferences** field.
7. Choose your payment method (credit card or bank transfer) in the **payment_method** field.
8. Add any additional information or comments in the **note** field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **user_info**: A text field to enter your name, email, or other contact information.
* **contact_method**: Select one or more contact methods (email, phone, or other) to reach you.
* **accommodation_choice**: Choose the type of accommodation you prefer (single, double, or triple).
* **checkin_date**: Enter the date you would like to check-in.
* **checkout_date**: Enter the date you would like to check-out.
* **accommodation_preferences**: Enter any additional information about your accommodation preferences.
* **payment_method**: Choose your preferred payment method (credit card or bank transfer).
* **note**: A text field for any additional information or comments you would like to add.
