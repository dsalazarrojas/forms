# Snow Tube Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects registration information for the Snow Tube event, including participant details, contact information, and emergency contact data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form by providing the required information in the corresponding fields.
2. Ensure that all required fields are filled out, as indicated by the asterisk (\*).
3. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): This field is for general information about the participant, but it's not required to fill out.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the participant.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, `email`, required: false): Enter the participant's email address.
* **Contact Details** (`contact_details`, `text`, required: false): This is an open field for any additional contact information you may need to provide.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, enter the emergency contact's name, phone number, or other relevant details.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required: false): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Waiver Consent** (`waiver_consent`, `text`, required: false): This field is for consent or agreement to the event's waiver terms. Please review and agree to the waiver before submitting.
* **Notes** (`notes`, `note`, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments related to the participant's registration.
