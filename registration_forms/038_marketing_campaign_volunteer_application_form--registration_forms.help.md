# Marketing Campaign Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to gather information from potential volunteers for marketing campaigns. The form helps to assess a volunteer's suitability for a campaign based on their availability, skills, and other relevant information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. **Marketing Campaign Information**: This page allows you to provide a brief description of the campaign you are applying for. Please be specific about the campaign name and its details.
2. **Contact Information**: This page requires your contact details, including your email and phone number. Please ensure that your contact information is up-to-date and accurate.
3. **Skills**: Select the skills you possess that are relevant to the campaign. This will help us assess your suitability for the role.
4. **Availability**: Choose your availability for the campaign, considering the dates and time slots available.
5. **Role Preference**: Indicate your preference for the role you are interested in applying for.
6. **Experience**: Select your level of experience in the field related to the campaign.
7. **Volunteer Type**: Choose the type of volunteer you are applying as, e.g., individual or team member.
8. **Submit**: Review your application before submitting it. Ensure that all required fields are completed and that your information is accurate.
9. **Review**: You will be notified of the review date once your application is submitted.
10. **Review Date**: This field is automatically populated once your application is submitted.
11. **Submitter**: You will be automatically populated with your name and email after submitting the application.
12. **Organization**: If you are applying on behalf of an organization, please provide its name.
13. **Position**: If you are applying on behalf of an organization, please provide your position.
14. **Department**: If you are applying on behalf of an organization, please provide the department you belong to.
15. **Contact**: If you are applying on behalf of an organization, please provide your contact information.
16. **Role Description**: A brief description of the role you are applying for.
17. **Role Title**: A title for the role you are applying for.
18. **Job Title**: If you have a job title, please provide it.
19. **Work Phone**: Your work phone number (if applicable).
20. **Work Email**: Your work email (if applicable).
21. **Personal Email**: Your personal email.
22. **Volunteer Hours**: The number of volunteer hours you are willing to commit to.
23. **Volunteer Start Date**: The date you are available to start volunteering.
24. **Notes**: Any additional notes or information you want to provide.
