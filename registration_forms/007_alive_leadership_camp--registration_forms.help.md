# ALIVE Leadership Camp - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ALIVE Leadership Camp registration form is designed to collect essential information from applicants to facilitate a safe and enjoyable experience at the camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your full name, email address, phone number, and age.
2. Enter your emergency contact's name and phone number.
3. (Optional) If applicable, provide your church affiliation, previous leadership experience, spiritual background, and special dietary requirements.
4. Disclose any medical conditions or medications you are taking.
5. Choose whether you consent to participate in all scheduled camp activities.
6. Provide your consent for photo and video recording during camp activities (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Camper Full Name)** (`camper_full_name`, `text`, required): Enter your complete and correct full name.
* **Email Address (camper_email)** (`camper_email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address for camp communications.
* **Phone Number (camper_phone)** (`camper_phone`, `text`, required): Enter your emergency contact number.
* **Age (camper_age)** (`camper_age`, `number`, required): Enter your current age as of the camp start date.
* **Emergency Contact Name (emergency_contact_name)** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Phone (emergency_contact_phone)** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
* **Church Affiliation (church_affiliation)** (`church_affiliation`, `text`, optional): If applicable, enter your church affiliation.
* **Previous Leadership Experience (leadership_experience)** (`leadership_experience`, `text`, optional): Describe any prior leadership roles or experiences you have had.
* **Spiritual Background (spiritual_background)** (`spiritual_background`, `text`, optional): Describe your faith journey and relationship with God.
* **Expectations from Camp (camp_expectations)** (`camp_expectations`, `text`, required): Enter what you hope to gain from the ALIVE Leadership Camp.
* **Special Dietary Requirements (special_dietary_requirements)** (`special_dietary_requirements`, `text`, optional): If you have any food allergies or dietary restrictions, enter them here.
* **Medical Conditions (medical_conditions)** (`medical_conditions`, `text`, optional): If you have any health conditions, enter them here.
* **Current Medications (medication_list)** (`medication_list`, `text`, optional): List any medications you take regularly.
* **Consent to Participate in Camp Activities (consent_camp_activities)** (`consent_camp_activities`, `select_one`, required): Choose 'Yes' to participate in all scheduled camp activities.
* **Consent for Photo and Video Recording (consent_photo_video)** (`consent_photo_video`, `select_one`, optional): Choose 'Yes' to consent to being photographed or recorded during camp activities.
