# Dance Team Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Dance Team Registration Form is used to register a dance team for a specific event or purpose. Please ensure that you are registering your team for the correct purpose and event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Dance Team Information section with your team's details.
2. Enter the Team Lead Contact information.
3. List the participant names separated by new lines in the Participants field.
4. Fill out the Team Details section with any additional information about your team.
5. Select your team's practice experience, performance level, team size, practice frequency, and practice duration from the respective drop-down menus.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Dance Team Information** (`dance_team_info`, `text`, required/optional): This field is the team's main information section. Please fill out your team's name, description, and any other relevant details.
* **Team Lead Contact** (`team_lead_contact`, `text`, required/optional): Enter the name and contact information of the team's lead contact person.
* **Participants** (`participants`, `text`, required/optional): List the names of all team members separated by new lines. Please make sure to include each member's name accurately.
* **Team Details** (`team_details`, `text`, required/optional): Add any additional information about your team, such as their practice venue or schedule.
* **Practice Experience** (`practice_experience`, `number`, required/optional): Indicate the number of years your team has been practicing dance.
* **Performance Level** (`performance_level`, `select_multiple`, required/optional): Select the performance level of your team, which can be Advanced, Intermediate, Beginner, or None.
* **Team Size** (`team_size`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the size of your team from the available options.
* **Practice Frequency** (`practice_frequency`, `select_one`, required/optional): Select how often your team practices dance, which can be Daily, Several times a week, Once a week, Several times a month, Rarely, or Never.
* **Practice Duration** (`practice_duration`, `select_one`, required/optional): Choose the length of time your team spends practicing dance, which can be Less than 1 hour, 1-2 hours, 2-4 hours, 4-6 hours, 6-8 hours, or More than 8 hours.

## Tips
- Please fill out each field accurately and thoroughly to ensure your team's registration is completed correctly.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the event organizers for assistance.
- This form is not intended to be duplicated or submitted multiple times for the same team.
