# Care Coordination Improvement Session Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for attendees to register for a care coordination improvement session. To complete the form, please follow the steps below.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please fill out the required fields in the order they appear. You will need to provide the following information:
* Your full name
* The name of your organization or practice
* Your email address
* Your professional role or title
* The date and time of the session you are interested in
* The areas of interest for the session
* Your preferred workshop format
* Any dietary restrictions or accessibility requirements
* Any questions for the session leaders

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Organization/Practice Name**: Enter the name of your organization or practice where you are affiliated with.
* **Attendee Email Address**: Enter your work or personal email address where you can be reached for session updates.
* **Professional Role/Title**: Select the option that best describes your role or title.
* **Session Date & Time**: Select the date and time of the session that fits your schedule.
* **Areas of Interest**: Choose the topics you would like to focus on during the session.
* **Preferred Workshop Format**: Select how you like to learn best, such as lecture-style or interactive group workshop.
* **Dietary Restrictions (for in-person sessions)**: If you have any food allergies or specific requirements, list them here.
* **Accessibility Accommodations Needed**: If you have any specific participation requirements, list them here.
* **Questions for the Speakers**: If you have any questions for the session leaders, enter them here.
* **Registration Confirmation**: Confirm your registration for the selected session by selecting "Registered and Confirmed".
