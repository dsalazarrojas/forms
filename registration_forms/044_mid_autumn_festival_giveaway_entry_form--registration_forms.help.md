# mid-autumn-festival-giveaway-entry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for people interested in participating in the mid-autumn festival giveaway. Fill out the form with your personal details and options to be considered for the giveaway.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Make sure to use a valid email format (e.g., `example@example.com`).
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose how you heard about the festival from the "How did you hear about us?" dropdown menu.
6. Select any options that apply to you from the "Which of the following apply to you?" dropdown menu.
7. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of Birth" field.
8. Enter your time of birth in the "Time of Birth" field.
9. (Optional) Add any additional notes in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name**: Enter your given name.
- **Last Name**: Enter your surname.
- **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted.
- **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
- **How did you hear about us?**: Choose how you learned about the mid-autumn festival.
  - Social Media: Heard about the festival on social media platforms.
  - Online Search: Found out about the festival through an online search.
  - Word of Mouth: Heard about the festival from someone else.
  - Other: Heard about the festival through another means.
- **Which of the following apply to you?**: Select any options that relate to you.
  - Option 1: Not specified.
  - Option 2: Not specified.
  - Option 3: Not specified.
  - Option 4: Not specified.
  - Option 5: Not specified.
- **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g., 1990-01-01).
- **Time of Birth**: Enter your time of birth in the 24 hour format (e.g., 12:00).
- **Notes**: Add any additional information you think is relevant to your entry.
