## Sponsor Briefing Breakfast RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to capture information from attendees and sponsors who are interested in attending the sponsor briefing breakfast event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps outlined above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (`Registration`, text, required): Enter your name as it appears on your business card or identification.
- **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, text, required): Enter your name, email, and phone number.
- **Dietary Preferences** (`Dietary Preferences`, text, required): Select your dietary requirements from the available options (e.g. Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
- **Additional Information** (`Additional Information`, text, optional): You can provide any additional comments or details about your attendance.
- **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required): Click this button to submit your completed form.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure that your information is received by the event hosts and sponsors.
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any special requests or requirements, be sure to include them in the "Additional Information" field.
- If you are unable to attend the event, please let us know in the "Additional Information" field so we can update our guest list accordingly.
