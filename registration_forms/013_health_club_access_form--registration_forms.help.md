# health_club_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The health_club_access_form is designed for user registration and access to health-related services.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, please follow the instructions for each field below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Name** (`member_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person registering for health club access. 
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address. 
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format DD/MM/YYYY. 
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number in the format of country code + phone number. 
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Choose one option from the list: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. 
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Choose multiple options from the list: Option 1, Option 2, or Option 3. 
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): This field is currently not supported. 
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): This field is not used in this form.
