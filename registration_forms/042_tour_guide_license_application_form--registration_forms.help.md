# Tour Guide License Application Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for tour guides to apply for a license to operate as a tour guide. It requires information such as first name, last name, address, date of birth, phone number, email, passport ID, years of experience, license type, languages spoken, resume, license number, certification, references, emergency contact, emergency phone, emergency email, nationality, nationality other, passport country, passport number, passport expiration date, license status, and license status reason. The form is used to assess the applicant's eligibility and provide a platform for them to upload required documents and information.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information, which is indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field labels.
2. Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information for each field.
3. For fields that require a specific format (such as date and phone number), ensure that you enter it in the correct format.
4. If you need help with any of the fields, click on the "I need help with this field" button and a detailed explanation will be provided.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (*): Enter your first name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Last Name** (*): Enter your last name as it appears on your passport or ID.
* **Address**: Enter your current address where you can be reached.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number in the format +X X X X X X X X, where X is a digit.
* **Email** (*): Enter your email address that you check regularly.
* **Passport ID**: Enter your passport ID number as it appears on your passport.
* **Years of Experience**: Enter the number of years you have been working as a tour guide.
* **License Type** (*): Select the type of license you are applying for (Tourist, Local, or International).
* **Languages**: Choose the languages you speak fluently from the list (English, Spanish, French, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, Italian).
* **Resume**: Attach a copy of your resume or CV that highlights your experience and qualifications.
* **License Number**: Enter your existing license number, if applicable.
* **Certification**: Attach any relevant certifications or qualifications you hold.
* **References**: Provide the names and contact information of two professional references.
* **Emergency Contact**: Provide the name and contact information of a person who can be contacted in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Phone**: Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Email**: Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Nationality**: Select your nationality as it appears on your passport.
* **Nationality Other**: If your nationality is not listed, select "Other" and enter your nationality.
* **Passport Country**: Enter the country where your passport was issued.
* **Passport Number**: Enter your passport number as it appears on your passport.
* **Passport Expiration Date**: Enter the date your passport will expire in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
* **License Status**: Select the status of your existing license (Active, Inactive, or Expired).
* **License Status Reason**: Provide the reason for your license status if it is not Active.
* **Submitted By**: Enter the name of the person submitting the application.

Note: Asterisks (\*) indicate required fields. Please make sure to fill out all required fields before submitting the application.
