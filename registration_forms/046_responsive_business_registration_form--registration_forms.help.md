# Responsive Business Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Responsive Business Registration Form is a tool used to register businesses in a centralized database, providing a quick and easy way to gather and store basic contact and category information. This guide will walk you through the form's sections and fields, helping you complete it efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To register a business, simply follow these steps:
1. **User Input**: This is the first section of the form. Enter any relevant information about yourself as the representative of the business.
2. Complete the required fields for the business, including:
	* **Business Name**: Enter the name of your business as it should be represented in the database.
	* **Business Address**: Enter the physical address of your business location.
	* **Business Email**: Enter a valid email address associated with your business.
	* **Business Phone**: Enter the phone number for your business.
3. **Category**: Choose the relevant category for your business from the dropdown menu.
4. **Business Description**: Provide a brief description of your business.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **User Input** (text): Enter any relevant information about yourself as the representative of the business.
- **Business Name** (text): Enter the name of your business as it should be represented in the database.
- **Business Address** (text): Enter the physical address of your business location.
- **Business Email** (email): Enter a valid email address associated with your business.
- **Business Phone** (text): Enter the phone number for your business.
- **Business Category** (select_one): Choose the relevant category for your business from the dropdown menu.
- **Business Website** (text): Enter the website of your business.
- **Business Description** (text): Provide a brief description of your business.

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
- If you need to register multiple businesses, create a new instance of the form.
- If you're unsure about any field, please contact our support team for assistance.
