# Indoor Playground Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Indoor Playground Reservation Form is used to reserve indoor playtime slots for children at a facility or center. Users must provide essential information, including their details and preferences, to book a playtime slot that suits their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. **Enter User Details**: Fill in the "User Input" field with your name and other relevant details.
2. **Select Time Slot**: Choose a time slot that suits your availability from the dropdown menu.
3. **Provide Date and Time**: Enter the date and time you would like to reserve.
4. **Enter Contact Information**: Fill in your contact name, email, and phone number to enable staff to get in touch with you.
5. **Add Notes (Optional)**: If you have any special requests or comments, type them in the "Notes" field.
6. **Enter Message to Staff**: If you need to communicate with staff about your reservation, type it in the "Message to Staff" field.
7. **Submit**: Review your form and click "Submit" to finalize your reservation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Input
* **User Input** (text, required): Enter your name and other relevant details.

### Time Slot
* **Time Slot** (number, required): Choose a time slot that suits your availability.

### Date
* **Date** (date, required): Enter the date you would like to reserve.

### Time
* **Time** (time, required): Enter the time you would like to reserve.

### Contact Name
* **Contact Name** (text, required): Enter your contact name.

### Contact Email
* **Contact Email** (email, required): Enter your email address.

### Contact Phone
* **Contact Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number.

### Notes
* **Notes** (note, optional): If you have any special requests or comments, type them here.

### Message to Staff
* **Message to Staff** (text, optional): If you need to communicate with staff about your reservation, type it here.

### Submit
* **Submit** (text, required): Review your form and click to finalize your reservation.
