# Herbal Plant Exploration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Herbal Plant Exploration Registration" form is used to register users for exploratory research projects that involve interacting with herbal plants.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with your relevant information.
2. Make sure to submit the form only once to avoid duplicate submissions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (text, required): Please enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address if you want to be contacted about your registration status or any further research projects.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number if you want to be contacted about your registration status or any further research projects.
* **Date** (date, optional): Select the date you plan to start exploring herbal plants.
* **Time** (time, optional): Select the time you plan to start exploring herbal plants.
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes or comments about your exploratory research project.
* **Select One** (select one, optional): Choose whether you are interested in participating in our research project or not.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are interested.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are not interested.
* **Select Multiple** (select multiple, optional): Choose the areas of herbal plant exploration you are interested in participating in.
	+ Choose 'Yes' for each area that interests you.
* **Integer** (number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing your level of experience with herbal plants (e.g., number of projects completed).
* **Decimal** (number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing your level of confidence in your herbal plant knowledge (e.g., 0.5 for "moderate" confidence).
* **Registration Fee** (number, optional): Enter a numerical value representing the fee you are willing to pay for participating in the research project.

## Tips

* Please fill out the form carefully and completely.
* Make sure to submit the form only once.
* If you have any questions or need clarification about any field, contact the administrator.
