# Membership Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering new members of an organization or community. It collects demographic information and date-related details about the new member.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out the **New Member Details** page with your personal or organizational information.
2. On the **Member Details** page:
   * Enter your **First Name**.
   * Enter your **Last Name**.
   * Enter your **Email** (a valid email address).
   * Choose your **Membership Type** (Individual or Organization).
   * Select your **Member Since** date.
   * Confirm your **Member Since** date on the **Confirm Member Since** field.
