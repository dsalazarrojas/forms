# equine_facilitator_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering for equine facilitator training, which involves gaining knowledge and skills in working with horses.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
2. Select your experience with horses, including the duration and your training history.
3. Indicate your certification status with horses.
4. Choose a desired workshop date and time.
5. Select your availability for the workshop.
6. Provide any additional information about your experience with horses that is not listed above.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Please enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): Please enter your date of birth.
* **Equine Experience** (`equine_experience`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your experience with horses, including the duration and your training history.
* **Training History** (`training_history`, `select_one`, required: false): Select the option that best describes your training history with horses.
* **Equine Facilitation Certification** (`certification`, `select_one`, required: false): Select your certification status with horses.
* **Desired Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, required: false): Select a date you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Desired Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `select_one`, required: false): Select a time you would like to attend the workshop.
* **Availability** (`availability`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select your availability for the workshop.
* **Other Equine Experience** (`other_equine_experience`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information about your experience with horses that is not listed above.
* **Message** (`message`, `text`, required: false): Provide any additional information you would like to share with us.
