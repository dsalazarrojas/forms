# green_energy_volunteer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from potential volunteers for the Green Energy initiative.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Specify the name of the organization you are affiliated with in the "organization" field.
5. Select your role in the Green Energy initiative by choosing one of the options in the "role" field.
6. Enter the time you are available to volunteer in the "Time" field.
7. Enter the date you are available to volunteer in the "Date" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **first_name** (Text, Required): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Text, Optional): Enter your phone number.
* **organization** (Text, Optional): Enter the name of the organization you are affiliated with.
* **role** (Select One, Optional): Choose your role in the Green Energy initiative:
	+ Option 1
	+ Option 2
	+ Option 3
* **Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the time you are available to volunteer.
* **Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the date you are available to volunteer.
