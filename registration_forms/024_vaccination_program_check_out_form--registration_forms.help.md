# Vaccination Program Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Vaccination Program Check Out Form is designed to gather essential information from patients who have received or are due for a vaccination. This form helps the program track patient progress, preferences, and contact information, ensuring that each patient receives the proper follow-up care and attention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the patient's information, such as their name and contact details.
2. Select the preferred method of contact (phone, email, or SMS) and language (English, Spanish, or French) for the patient.
3. Choose the type of vaccination the patient received or is due for (Flu, COVID-19, or MMR).
4. Select the follow-up location and status (Scheduled, Rescheduled, or Completed).
5. Indicate whether the vaccination is completed or not.
6. If necessary, indicate if a follow-up is required.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Patient Name**: Please enter the patient's full name as they would like to be addressed.
* **Contact Information**: Enter the patient's contact details, including their address, phone number, and email. (Optional)
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any allergies or medical conditions that may be relevant to their vaccination. (Optional)
* **Vaccination Details**: Select the type of vaccination the patient received or is due for. Choose from: Flu, COVID-19, or MMR.
* **Preferred Contact Method**: How would you like to be contacted? Choose from: phone, email, or SMS.
* **Preferred Language**: Which language would you prefer to receive communication in? Choose from: English, Spanish, or French.
* **Appointment Date**: If this is not applicable, you can leave this field blank. Otherwise, enter the date of the vaccination appointment.
* **Appointment Time**: If this is not applicable, you can leave this field blank. Otherwise, enter the time of the vaccination appointment.
* **Follow Up Location**: Select the location where the patient is due for their follow-up appointment. Choose from: Clinic A, Clinic B, or Clinic C.
* **Follow Up Status**: Choose the status of the patient's follow-up appointment. Select from: Scheduled, Rescheduled, or Completed.
* **Vaccination Completed**: Indicate if the vaccination is completed or not.
* **Follow Up Required**: If the patient requires follow-up after the vaccination, indicate that here.

Note: Some fields are optional and only required if the patient's situation calls for it. Please fill out this form carefully and accurately to ensure the patient receives the proper care and attention.
