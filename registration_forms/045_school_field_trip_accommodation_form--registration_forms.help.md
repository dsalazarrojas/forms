# School Field Trip Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The School Field Trip Accommodation Form is a data collection tool used to organize and arrange accommodations for school field trips.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill out each section with the required information.
2.  Review each field carefully to ensure accuracy.
3.  If a field is not relevant, leave it blank.
4.  Ensure that you have authorized permission to submit this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trip Details (Page 1)** (`trip_details`, `text`, not required): This section is for general trip information, such as the trip name, dates, and destination.
    *   Enter the trip name, start date, end date, and destination.
*   **Student Info (Page 2)** (`student_info`, `text`, not required): This section is for student details.
    *   Enter the student name, age, and any other relevant student information.
*   **School Info (Page 3)** (`school_info`, `text`, not required): This section is for school information.
    *   Enter the school name, address, and contact information.
*   **Accommodation Details (Page 4)** (`accommodation_details`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation details.
    *   Enter the accommodation name, address, and any other relevant details.
*   **Student List (Page 5)** (`student_list`, `text`, not required): This section is for listing students who will be attending the trip.
    *   Enter the student names and relevant information.
*   **Accommodation Preferences (Page 6)** (`accommodation_preferences`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation preferences.
    *   Enter the accommodation type, such as single or double room, or dormitory.
*   **Travel Info (Page 7)** (`travel_info`, `text`, not required): This section is for travel information.
    *   Enter the travel date, time, and mode of transportation.
*   **Trip Schedule (Page 8)** (`trip_schedule`, `text`, not required): This section is for trip schedule.
    *   Enter the trip schedule, including dates, times, and locations.
*   **Student Attendance (Page 9)** (`student_attendance`, `text`, not required): This section is for student attendance.
    *   Enter the expected student attendance and any relevant attendance details.
*   **Accommodation Availability (Page 10)** (`accommodation_availability`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation availability.
    *   Enter the accommodation availability, such as availability dates and times.
*   **Accommodation Options (Page 11)** (`accommodation_options`, `select_multiple`, not required): This section is for accommodation options.
    *   Select the accommodation options, such as single room, double room, or dormitory.
*   **Room Type (Page 12)** (`room_type`, `select_one`, not required): This section is for room type.
    *   Select the room type, such as single room, double room, or dormitory.
*   **Accommodation Name (Page 13)** (`accommodation_name`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation name.
    *   Enter the accommodation name.
*   **Address (Page 14)** (`accommodation_address`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation address.
    *   Enter the accommodation address.
*   **Phone (Page 15)** (`accommodation_phone`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation phone number.
    *   Enter the accommodation phone number.
*   **Email (Page 16)** (`accommodation_email`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation email.
    *   Enter the accommodation email.
*   **Price (Page 17)** (`accommodation_price`, `text`, not required): This section is for accommodation price.
    *   Enter the accommodation price.
*   **Status (Page 18)** (`accommodation_status`, `select_one`, not required): This section is for accommodation status.
    *   Select the accommodation status, such as available or unavailable.
*   **Student Status (Page 19)** (`student_status`, `select_one`, not required): This section is for student status.
    *   Select the student status, such as confirmed or not confirmed.
*   **Notes (Page 20)** (`notes`, `text`, not required): This section is for notes.
    *   Enter any additional notes or comments.
*   **Trip Leader (Page 21)** (`trip_leader`, `text`, not required): This section is for trip leader information.
    *   Enter the trip leader name and contact information.
*   **Trip Supervisor (Page 22)** (`trip_supervisor`, `text`, not required): This section is for trip supervisor information.
    *   Enter the trip supervisor name and contact information.
*   **School Name (Page 23)** (`school_name`, `text`, not required): This section is for school name.
    *   Enter the school name.
*   **School Address (Page 24)** (`school_address`, `text`, not required): This section is for school address.
    *   Enter the school address.
*   **Contact Person (Page 25)** (`school_contact`, `text`, not required): This section is for contact person information.
    *   Enter the contact person name and contact information.

## Tips
*   Always fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
*   Review each field before submitting the form.
*   Ensure that you have authorized permission to submit this form.
*   Double-check for any mistakes or inaccuracies.
*   Consult the trip leader or school supervisor if you're unsure about any part of this form.
