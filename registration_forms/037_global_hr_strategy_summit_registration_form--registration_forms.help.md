# Global HR Strategy Summit Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Global HR Strategy Summit Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants about their interests and special requirements for the summit. Please fill out this form accurately and completely to ensure your needs are taken into account.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Click on the "Register" button to start the registration process.
2. Fill out the registration details section with your name and contact information.
3. Select your interests for the summit by checking the boxes that apply to you.
4. If you have any special requirements, please list them in the text fields provided.
5. Review your registration details carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Details** (`registration_details`, text, required): Please fill out your name and contact information in this section.
* **Session Interests** (`session_interests`, select_multiple, optional): Check the boxes that apply to you to indicate your interests for the summit. You can select multiple interests.
* **Session Interests 2** (`session_interests_2`, select_multiple, optional): This is another opportunity to select your interests for the summit. You can select multiple interests.
* **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): If you have any special requirements, please list them in this text field.
* **Special Requirements 2** (`special_requirements_2`, text, optional): Another text field for special requirements.
* ...
* **Special Requirements 22** (`special_requirements_22`, text, optional): The last text field for special requirements.

Note: The fields with numbers 2-21 are similar to Special Requirements 1, and should be filled out in a similar manner.

## Tips
* Please ensure you are filling out this form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the summit organizers.
* You can review and edit your registration details before submitting the form.
