# Toothbrushing Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Toothbrushing Program Registration form is designed to collect information from parents and guardians about their child's participation in the program.
## How To Complete This Form
1. **Toothbrushing Program Registration**: Fill in the title of the program your child is participating in.
2. **Parent Name**: Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
3. **Parent Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
4. **Program Start Date**: Select the start date of the program your child is enrolled in.
5. **Program End Date**: Select the end date of the program your child is enrolled in.
6. **Student Name**: Enter your child's name.
7. **Student DOB**: Enter your child's date of birth.
8. **Grade Level**: Select your child's grade level from the options provided.
9. **School**: Enter your child's school name (if applicable).
10. **Student Email**: Enter your child's email address (if applicable).
11. **Student Phone**: Enter your child's phone number (if applicable).
12. **Guardian Name**: Enter your name as the guardian or parent.
13. **Guardian Email**: Enter your email address (if applicable).
14. **Guardian Phone**: Enter your phone number (if applicable).
15. **Parent Signature**: This field is for the parent or guardian to sign and acknowledge their commitment to the program.
16. **Student Signature**: This field is for the student to sign and acknowledge their participation in the program.
