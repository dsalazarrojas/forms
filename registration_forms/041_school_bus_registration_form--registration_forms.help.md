# School Bus Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The School Bus Registration Form is a data collection tool used to gather information about the student, bus, and school details for the purpose of registering a student for a bus trip. This form ensures that all necessary information is captured accurately and efficiently, making it easier for the school administration to manage the bus trip logistics.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the student and bus details.
2. Ensure that all fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
3. Review the form for any errors or discrepancies before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Bus Registration** (Text): Enter a brief description of the school bus registration.
* **Student Name** (Text): Enter the student's name as per school records.
* **Student Grade Level** (Select One): Select the student's grade level as per school records.
* **Bus Trip Date** (Date): Enter the date of the bus trip.
* **Bus Trip Time** (Time): Enter the time of the bus trip.
* **Student Phone** (Text): Enter the student's phone number as per school records.
* **Student Email** (Email): Enter the student's email as per school records.
* **Student Address** (Text): Enter the student's address as per school records.
* **Bus Route** (Text): Enter the bus route number or name as per school records.
* **Driver Name** (Text): Enter the driver's name as per school records.
* **Driver Phone** (Text): Enter the driver's phone number as per school records.
* **Driver Email** (Email): Enter the driver's email as per school records.
* **Bus License Plate** (Text): Enter the bus license plate number as per school records.
* **School Name** (Text): Enter the school name as per school records.
* **School Address** (Text): Enter the school address as per school records.
* **School Phone** (Text): Enter the school phone number as per school records.
* **School Email** (Email): Enter the school email as per school records.
* **Bus Model** (Text): Enter the bus model as per school records.
* **Bus Year** (Text): Enter the bus year as per school records.
* **Student DOB** (Date): Enter the student's date of birth as per school records.
* **Bus Capacity** (Text): Enter the bus capacity as per school records.
* **Bus License Status** (Text): Enter the bus license status as per school records.
* **Bus Insurance Provider** (Text): Enter the bus insurance provider as per school records.
* **Bus Insurance Status** (Text): Enter the bus insurance status as per school records.
* **Student Registration Number** (Text): Enter the student registration number as per school records.

Note: Some fields may not be applicable to certain school or student scenarios. Please ensure that you fill out the form correctly and accurately according to your school's policies and procedures.
