# practitioner_database_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering new practitioners in the database, ensuring accurate and complete information is entered to avoid duplication of records.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the **Clinic Details** field with the relevant information.
2. Fill out the **Practitioner Details** field with accurate and up-to-date information.
3. Sign and date the **Consent** field to confirm understanding of the form.
4. Select **Verification** as "verified" if you are certain the entered information is accurate.
5. Upload required documents such as certificates, proofs of identification, and licenses as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Clinic Details**: Enter the details of the clinic where you work, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Practitioner Details**: Fill out your personal details, including name, address, and contact information.
* **Consent**: Sign and date to confirm you have read and understood the form and its terms.
* **Verification**: Choose "verified" if you are certain the entered information is accurate.
* **Upload Certificate**: Attach your certificate or proof of certification as required.
* **Upload Identification Card**: Attach your identification card or proof of identification as required.
* **Upload Credential Document**: Attach your credential document as required.
* **Upload License**: Attach your license as required.
* **Upload Proof of Credential**: Attach your proof of credential as required.
* **Upload Other Document**: Attach other documents as required.
* **Upload Proof of Residency**: Attach your proof of residency document as required.
* **Upload Proof of Education**: Attach your proof of education document as required.
* **Upload License Copy**: Attach your license copy as required.
* **Upload Proof of Identity**: Attach your proof of identity document as required.
* **Upload Proof of Address**: Attach your proof of address document as required.
* **Upload Other Document Copy**: Attach other document copies as required.
* **Upload Other Document 2**: Attach other document 2 as required.
* **Upload Other Document 3**: Attach other document 3 as required.
* **Upload Other Document 4**: Attach other document 4 as required.
* **Upload Other Document 5**: Attach other document 5 as required.
* **Upload Other Document 6**: Attach other document 6 as required.
* **Upload Other Document**: Attach other document as required.
* **Upload Other Document 7**: Attach other document 7 as required.
* **Upload Other Document 8**: Attach other document 8 as required.

Note: The last 8 fields are for additional documents that can be uploaded, but are not required.

**Tips**: Please ensure that all uploaded documents are in a format that is easily readable and accessible for the database administrators to review.
