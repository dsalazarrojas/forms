# Public Service Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Service Registration Form is a critical tool for organizations to collect essential information from public participants. This guide will help you understand the form's purpose, structure, and fields to ensure you complete it accurately and efficiently.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Public Service Registration Form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Public Service Information** section (Page 1) with any relevant details about the public service you are interested in.
2. In the **Participant Information** section (Page 2), provide your personal contact information, including your name, contact number, and address.
3. Ensure that you fill out all required fields, marked with an asterisk (*), such as **Phone** and **Address**.
4. Choose your preferred contact method in the **Participant Contact Method** section.
5. Add any additional comments or notes in the **Additional Comments** section if necessary.
6. Select the **Registration Date** when you registered for the public service.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Public Service Information (Page 1)
* **Public Service Information** (Public Service Information, text, *): This field is used to provide any relevant information about the public service you are interested in.

### Participant Information (Page 2)
* **Participant Information 1** (Participant Information, text): This field is used to provide your personal contact information.
* **Participant Information 2** (Contact Information, text): This field is used to provide your contact information.
* **Participant Information 3** (Phone, text): This field is used to provide your phone number.
* **Participant Information 4** (Address, text): This field is used to provide your address.
* **Participant Information 5** (Participant Contact Method, text): This field is used to choose your preferred contact method.
* **Participant Information 6** (Additional Comments, note): This field is used to add any additional comments or notes.

### Registration Information (Page 3)
* **Registration Date** (Registration Date, date, *): This field is used to select the date you registered for the public service.

### Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* Use a valid phone number and address format.
* You can add any additional comments or notes if necessary.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
