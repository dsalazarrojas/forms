# Girl Scout Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Girl Scout Registration Form is used to collect registration information from individuals who wish to join the Girl Scouts. The form is designed to gather basic contact information and emergency contact details to ensure that we can effectively communicate with you and keep you safe.

## How To Complete This Form

### Step 1: Enter your personal details
Enter your first and last name, email address, and phone number to create your account.

### Step 2: Add your emergency contact information
Provide the contact information for a person who can be reached in case of an emergency, including their name, relationship to you, phone number, email address, and address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name** (`first_name`, text, Required): Enter your first name as it is written in your identification documents.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, Required): Enter your last name as it is written in your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, Required): Enter a valid email address that you check regularly.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, Required): Enter your phone number that can be used to contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, Optional): Enter your date of birth in the format `MM/DD/YYYY`.
* **Parent name** (`parent_name`, text, Optional): Enter the name of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent email** (`parent_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of your parent or guardian.
* **Parent phone** (`parent_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of your parent or guardian.
* **Emergency contact** (`emergency_contact`, select_multiple, Optional): Select the relationship of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, Optional): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, Optional): Enter the email address of the emergency contact person.
* **Emergency contact address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, Optional): Enter the address of the emergency contact person.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information.
* Use a valid email address that you check regularly.
* If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian fill out this form on your behalf.
* If you are a parent or guardian, please fill out this form for your child.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact us at [support email/phone number].
