# Adult Care Service Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Adult Care Service Registration form is used to gather information about the client who will be receiving care services. This form is essential for ensuring that the service provider has all the necessary details to deliver effective care.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in all required fields marked as "required".
2.  Ensure that all dates are in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
3.  For insurance type, select one of the available options.
4.  For service frequency, select the option that best fits the client's needs.
5.  Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### 1. **Client First Name (`client_first_name`, `text`, `required`):
Client First Name: Enter the first name of the adult seeking care.

*   First name of the adult seeking care

### 2. **Client Last Name (`client_last_name`, `text`, `required`):
Client Last Name: Enter the last name of the adult seeking care.

*   Last name of the adult seeking care

### 3. **Date of Birth (`client_date_of_birth`, `date`, `required`):
Date of Birth: Enter the birth date of the adult seeking care.

*   Birth date of the adult seeking care

### 4. **Email Address (`client_email`, `email`, `required`):
Email Address: Enter the primary contact email of the adult seeking care.

*   Primary contact email of the adult seeking care

### 5. **Phone Number (`client_phone`, `text`, `required`):
Phone Number: Enter the best contact number for the adult seeking care.

*   Best contact number for the adult seeking care

### 6. **Residential Address (`client_address`, `text`, `required`):
Residential Address: Enter the full street address of the adult seeking care.

*   Full street address of the adult seeking care

*   Name of designated emergency contact

*   Phone number of emergency contact

Relationship to Client: Select how the emergency contact relates to the client.

*   How the emergency contact relates to the client

*   Name of insurance company

### 13. **Policy Number (`policy_number`, `text`, `required`):

*   Insurance policy number

### 14. **Primary Medical Conditions (`medical_conditions`, `text`, `optional`):

*   List any chronic conditions or diagnoses the client has
