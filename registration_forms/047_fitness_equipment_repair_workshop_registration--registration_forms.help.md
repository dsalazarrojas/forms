# Fitness Equipment Repair Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fitness Equipment Repair Workshop Registration form is designed to allow attendees to register for a workshop on fitness equipment repair. The form helps collect information about the attendee's details, the equipment issue they want to bring to the workshop, their session preferences, and their contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Scroll through each page of the form.
2. For each page, review the fields and labels.
3. Fill in the fields as required or as needed.
4. Review and submit the form once completed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Details**: Enter your name and other relevant details as required or as needed.
* **Equipment Issue**: Describe the fitness equipment that you want to bring to the workshop for repair.
* **Session Preferences**: Select one of the available session options (Session 1, Session 2, Session 3) that best suits your needs.
* **Attendee Email**: Enter your email address as we may contact you for further information or updates.
* **Attendee Phone**: Enter your phone number as we may contact you for further information or updates.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields (although this form has none, it's still good practice to fill in all fields for accuracy and to avoid missing any important information).
- Review your submission carefully before clicking the submit button to ensure everything is accurate.
- If you need help or have any questions, don't hesitate to contact us.
