# Travel Agents Association Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register a new member for the Travel Agents Association.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal information, including your first name, last name, phone number, and email address.
2. Enter your agent username and agent since.
3. Select your membership status from the options provided.
4. If applicable, enter any certifications you hold.
5. Choose the start and end dates of your membership, and select the date you actually joined.
6. If you have any additional comments or notes, enter them in the text box.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates from the association.
* **Phone** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Agent Username** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter a unique username for your agent profile.
* **Agent Since** (Number, Required/Optional): Enter the year you started working as an agent (e.g., 2000).
* **Agency Name** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter the name of your agency (if applicable).
* **Membership Status** (Select One, Required): Choose whether you are an active or inactive member.
* **Certifications** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any relevant certifications you hold (if applicable).
* **Membership Start** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date you began your membership with the association.
* **Membership End** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date you expect your membership to end (if applicable).
* **Membership Date** (Date, Required/Optional): Enter the date you actually joined the association (for administrative purposes).
* **Note** (Text, Required/Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership application.

## Tips
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- Ensure your email address and phone number are up-to-date to receive notifications and updates.
- If you have any issues or questions, contact the association for assistance.
