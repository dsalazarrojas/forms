# supplier_payment_optimization_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The supplier payment optimization registration form is designed to collect information from suppliers about their payment preferences and terms. This form helps us better understand their needs and optimize payment processes for improved efficiency and accuracy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the supplier payment information on the first page.
2. Indicate whether the supplier has a contact person or not on the second page.
3. Specify the payment terms and conditions on the third page.
4. Choose the payment frequency (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.) on the fourth page.
5. Enter the payment amount on the fifth page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Supplier Payment Information** (`supplier_payment_info_form`, Text, Required): Enter the supplier's payment details, including any relevant payment information.
* **Contact Person (supplier_contact_form, Select Multiple, Optional):** Select 'Yes' if the supplier has a designated contact person, or 'No' if not.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms_form`, Text, Optional): Describe the supplier's payment terms and conditions, if applicable.
* **Payment Frequency** (`payment_frequency_form`, Number, Optional): Choose the payment frequency from the options (e.g., monthly, quarterly, etc.).
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount_form`, Number, Optional): Enter the payment amount.

## Tips
* Ensure that the supplier payment information is accurate and up-to-date to avoid delays or errors in payment processing.
* If you are unsure about any field, please reach out to the supplier for clarification.
* Complete the form with the required fields marked as 'Required'.
