# Vendor KYC Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Vendor KYC Registration Form is designed to collect information from vendors for Know Your Customer (KYC) purposes. This form is required for all vendors to register and provide their contact and identification information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate information.
2. Select the correct options for the select multiple and select one fields.
3. If you have documents to upload, please attach them accordingly.
4. Indicate whether you are willing to upload documents or not.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Inputs (User Inputs, text, required: false)**: Enter your name in this field.
* **Contact Info (Contact Info, select multiple, required: false)**: Select one or more of the options (First Name, Last Name) that best describe your contact information.
* **Email (Email, email, required: false)**: Enter your email address to be contacted for further communication.
* **Phone (Phone, text, required: false)**: Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Address (Address, text, required: false)**: Enter your address for further reference.
* **Documents (Documents, note, required: false)**: This field is for uploading documents if required or specified.
* **Upload Documents (Upload Documents, select one, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you are willing to upload documents, 'No' otherwise.
* **Category (Category, select one, required: false)**: Select 'Yes' if you fall under this category.
* **Vendor Name (Vendor Name, text, required: false)**: Enter your name or company name.
* **Description (Description, text, required: false)**: Briefly describe your company or services offered.

## Tips
* Please ensure all fields are filled in accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure or need help, do not hesitate to contact us.
