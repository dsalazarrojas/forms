# Diversity And Inclusion Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is intended to collect information from participants and guests attending the Diversity And Inclusion Workshop. The data collected will be used to track attendance, manage workshop logistics, and ensure that the event is a success. Please note that this form is not intended to collect sensitive or confidential information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your participant or guest information:
   - Enter your name in the "Participant Name" field.
   - Enter your email address in the "Participant Email" field.
   - Select your role as a "Participant" or "Guest" in the "Participant Role" field.
   - Enter any additional information you'd like to share in the "Notes" field.

2. Provide workshop details:
   - Enter the name of the workshop you will be attending in the "Workshop Name" field.
   - Enter the start date of the workshop in the "Start Date" field.
   - Enter the end date of the workshop in the "End Date" field.
   - Enter the start time of the workshop in the "Start Time" field.
   - Enter the end time of the workshop in the "End Time" field.
   - Enter the session of the workshop in the "Session" field.
   - Enter the location of the workshop in the "Location" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, text, required/optional): Enter your name as a participant or guest attending the workshop.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address, which will be used for communication purposes.
* **Participant Role** (`role`, select_one, required): Select your role as a participant or guest.
  * Participant: If you are a registered participant.
  * Guest: If you are attending without being a registered participant.
* **Workshop Name** (`workshop_name`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of the workshop you will be attending.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Start Time** (`start_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the start time of the workshop.
* **End Time** (`end_time`, time, required/optional): Enter the end time of the workshop.
* **Session** (`session`, text, required/optional): Enter the session of the workshop you will be attending.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with the workshop organizers.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you have the correct email address and role selected to ensure accurate communication and workshop planning.
* If you are attending as a guest, please ensure that you are accompanied by your registered participant.
* If you have any special requests or accessibility needs, please note them in the "Notes" field.
