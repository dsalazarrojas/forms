# Student Admission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for student registration and submission of information. It captures essential details about the students, including their personal information, program selection, and submission data. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal information (Student Name, Email, Student Address, Student Phone, Student Contact, and Student Notes).
2. Select the program you are interested in (Program A, Program B, or Program C).
3. Set the Application Date and Submission Date as per your requirement.
4. Fill in the Submitter Information (Submitted By, Submission Time, and Submitter Name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Information** (`student_info`, `text`, optional): This field is for filling in the student's name.
* **Select Program** (`program_select`, `select_one`, optional): Choose the program you are interested in.
* **Application Date** (`application_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date of your application.
* **Student Name** (`student_name`, `text`, optional): Fill in your name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Student Email** (`student_email`, `email`, optional): Enter your student email address. *Please note that this field has a different label for internal purposes*.
* **Student Contact** (`student_contact`, `text`, optional): Fill in your contact information.
* **Student Address** (`student_address`, `text`, optional): Enter your student address.
* **Student Phone** (`student_phone`, `text`, optional): Fill in your phone number.
* **Student Notes** (`student_notes`, `note`, optional): Add any additional notes about yourself.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, `text`, optional): Fill in the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submission Date** (`submission_date`, `date`, optional): Set the date of submission.
* **Submission Time** (`submission_time`, `time`, optional): Set the time of submission.
* **Submitter Information** (`submitter_info`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select the person submitting the form.
* **Submitter Name** (`submitter_name`, `text`, optional): Fill in the name of the person submitting the form.
