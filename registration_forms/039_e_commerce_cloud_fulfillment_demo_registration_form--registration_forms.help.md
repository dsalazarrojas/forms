# e-commerce cloud fulfillment demo registration form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The e-commerce cloud fulfillment demo registration form is designed to collect information from customers who are interested in exploring the demo version of the e-commerce cloud fulfillment service.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please navigate through the six pages of the form, reading each page carefully before proceeding.
2. Fill out each page according to the instructions provided.
3. Review your answers before submitting the form to ensure that all required fields are filled out.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Introduction and Contact Information (page 1)**: Please provide your name and contact information so we can get in touch with you.
* **Demo Requests and Preferences (page 2)**: Tell us about your specific demo requests, including any preferences you may have.
* **Personal Details (page 3)**: Share your personal details with us so we can better understand your needs.
* **Demo Type and Location (page 4)**: Select the demo type and location that suits your needs.
* **Demo Assignment and Category (page 5)**: Choose the demo assignment and category that you're interested in.
* **Form Output and Submission (page 6)**: Review and agree to the terms of the demo form submission.
