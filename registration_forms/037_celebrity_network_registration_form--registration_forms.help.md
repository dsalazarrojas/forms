# Celebrity Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Celebrity Network Registration Form is used to create a user account on the Celebrity Network platform. It gathers information about the user's personal details, interests, and preferences to create a customized experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Select your account type.
3. Choose your interests and notification preferences.
4. Add a profile picture (optional).
5. Agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email Address** (email, required): Enter your login email.
* **Confirm Email Address** (email, required): Re-enter your email for verification.
* **Phone Number** (text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Username** (text, required): Choose a unique username for your account.
* **Date of Birth** (date, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Account Type** (select one, required): Select your account type: Fan Member, Premium Member, VIP Member, Professional, or Celebrity Account.
* **Interest Categories** (select multiple, required): Choose the categories that interest you most (Movies, Television, Music, Sports, Fashion, Entertainment News, Celebrity Gossip, Fan Art, Events, Social Causes).
* **Notification Preferences** (select multiple, optional): Select how you would like to receive notifications (Email Updates, SMS Notifications, App Push Notifications, Weekly Digest, Celebrity News, Exclusive Offers, Event Announcements).
* **Favorite Celebrities** (text, optional): Enter the names of your favorite celebrities.
* **Short Bio or About You** (text, optional): Write a short description about yourself.
* **Location** (text, optional): Enter your city and state.
* **Profile Picture** (text, optional): Enter the URL of your profile picture.
* **Twitter Handle** (text, optional): Enter your Twitter handle (if you have one).
* **Instagram Handle** (text, optional): Enter your Instagram handle (if you have one).
* **Terms Acceptance** (select one, required): Confirm that you accept the terms and conditions.
* **Privacy Acceptance** (select one, required): Confirm that you accept the privacy policy.
* **Marketing Consent** (select one, optional): If you want to receive promotional communications, select Yes.
