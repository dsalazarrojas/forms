# vineyard-wine-tasting-tournament-entry-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The vineyard wine-tasting-tournament-entry-form is a registration form used for users to register new tournaments.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review the form fields carefully and ensure you are registering a unique tournament.
2. Fill out all required fields to complete the form.

* **Tournament Name** (`id: 1`, `label: Tournament Name`, `type: text`, `required: true`): Enter the name of the tournament.
* **Event Date** (`id: 2`, `label: Event Date`, `type: date`, `required: false`): Enter the date of the event.
* **Event Time** (`id: 3`, `label: Event Time`, `type: time`, `required: true`): Enter the time of the event.
* **Tasting Type** (`id: 4`, `label: Tasting Type`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the type of tasting (Yes/No).

* **Winery Name** (`id: 7`, `label: Winery Name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the winery.
* **Winery Email** (`id: 8`, `label: Winery Email`, `type: email`, `required: true`): Enter the email of the winery.
* **Wine Tasting Score** (`id: 9`, `label: Wine Tasting Score`, `type: number`, `required: false`): Enter the wine tasting score.
* **Winery Website** (`id: 10`, `label: Winery Website`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the winery website.
* **Judge Name** (`id: 11`, `label: Judge Name`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the name of the judge.

* **Wine Clarity** (`id: 21`, `label: Wine Clarity`, `type: select_one`, `required: true`): Select the wine clarity (Yes/No).

* **Wine Basis** (`id: 23`, `label: Wine Basis`, `type: text`, `required: false`): Enter the wine basis.
* **Judge Verification** (`id: 24`, `label: Judge Verification`, `type: select_one`, `required: false`): Select the judge verification (Yes/No).
* **Submit** (`id: 25`, `label: Submit`, `type: note`, `required: false`): This is a submit button to finish your registration.
