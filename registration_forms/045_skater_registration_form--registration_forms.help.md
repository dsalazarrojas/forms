# Skater Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from skaters (or their guardians) to ensure accurate and proper care during events and programs. It gathers personal details, medical information, and emergency contact details to guarantee a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Provide your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
5. Select your preferred "Skate Type" from the options provided.
6. Choose the types of events you'll be participating in from the "Event Type" options.
7. Select your skating level (Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced) from the "Skater Level" dropdown.
8. Enter your age in the "Skater Age" field.
9. Enter your height in the "Skater Height" field.
10. Enter your weight in the "Skater Weight" field.
11. Choose your gender from the "Skater Gender" field.
12. Indicate whether you have skating experience ("True", "False", or "Not Sure") from the "Skating Experience" dropdown.
13. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
14. Enter your emergency contact's phone number in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
15. Specify your relationship with your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Relationship" field.
16. Choose how you grant permission for your emergency contact to be notified in case of an emergency from the "Emergency Contact Permission" dropdown.
17. Add any additional notes about your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Permission Note" field.
18. Describe any medical conditions that may affect your skating in the "Skater Medical Condition" field.
19. Enter any relevant medical notes for yourself in the "Skater Medical Note" field.
20. Enter any medical emergency instructions for yourself in the "Skater Medical Emergency" field.
21. Describe any medical conditions that may affect your child/ward in the "Parent Medical Condition" field.
22. Enter any relevant medical notes for your child/ward in the "Parent Medical Note" field.
23. Enter any medical emergency instructions for your child/ward in the "Parent Medical Emergency" field.
24. Sign and date the form by entering the current date in the "Signature Date" field.
25. Sign your name in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that we can use to contact you.
* **Phone Number** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you.
* **Skate Type** (5, select_one, required/optional): Select your preferred type of skate from the options provided (e.g., roller skating, ice skating, etc.).
* **Event Type** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Select the types of events you'll be participating in from the options provided.
* **Skater Level** (7, select_one, required/optional): Choose your skating level from the options provided (e.g., beginner, intermediate, advanced).
* **Skater Age** (8, date, required/optional): Enter your age.
* **Skater Height** (9, number, required/optional): Enter your height in meters.
* **Skater Weight** (10, number, required/optional): Enter your weight in kilograms.
* **Skater Gender** (11, text, required/optional): Select your gender from the options provided.
* **Skating Experience** (12, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you have skating experience ("True", "False", or "Not Sure").
* **Emergency Contact Name** (13, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (14, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (15, text, required/optional): Specify your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., parent, guardian, spouse, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Permission** (16, select_one, required/optional): Grant permission for your emergency contact to be notified in case of an emergency from the options provided.
* **Emergency Contact Permission Note** (17, text, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your emergency contact.
* **Skater Medical Condition** (18, text, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your skating.
* **Skater Medical Note** (19, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical notes for yourself.
* **Skater Medical Emergency** (20, text, required/optional): Enter any medical emergency instructions for yourself.
* **Parent Medical Condition** (21, text, required/optional): Describe any medical conditions that may affect your child/ward.
* **Parent Medical Note** (22, text, required/optional): Enter any relevant medical notes for your child/ward.
* **Parent Medical Emergency** (23, text, required/optional): Enter any medical emergency instructions for your child/ward.
* **Signature Date** (24, date, required/optional): Sign and date the form by entering the current date.
* **Signature** (25, text, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your registration.
