# Process Digitization Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering attendees for the Process Digitization Summit. Please fill out this form accurately to ensure that you receive the necessary information for the event and that we can provide you with the best experience possible.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Choose your attendee type from the options provided.
2. Fill out your demographic information, including your first name, last name, organization, job title, and contact details.
3. If applicable, select any accessibility needs you may have.
4. Choose your t-shirt preferences, including size, style, and color.
5. Provide your arrival and departure times.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Attendee Type**: Choose the category under which you are registering (Digital Transformation Conference, Sponsor, Press, or Guest).
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Organization**: Enter your organization's name.
* **Job Title**: Enter your job title.
* **Role**: Choose your role at the summit (Participant, Speaker, Sponsor, or Exhibitor).
* **Has-Wheelchair**: Select if you need wheelchair assistance.
* **Has-Other-Needs**: Select any other accessibility needs you may have.
* **Email**: Enter your email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Address**: Enter your address.
* **Zip**: Enter your zip code.
* **City**: Enter your city.
* **Accessibility Needs**: If you have any other accessibility needs, describe them here.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: If you have any dietary restrictions, describe them here.
* **Special Requests**: If you have any special requests, describe them here.
* **T-Shirt Size**: Choose the size of t-shirt you prefer (S, M, L, XL, or XXL).
* **T-Shirt Style**: Choose the style of t-shirt you prefer (Classic, Sport, or Hoodie).
* **T-Shirt Color**: Choose the color of t-shirt you prefer (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, White, or Black).
* **Arrival Time**: Enter your expected arrival time.
* **Departure Time**: Enter your expected departure time.
* **T-Shirt Size (XL)**: If you prefer an extra large t-shirt, choose the size here.
* **T-Shirt Color (XL)**: If you prefer an extra large t-shirt, choose the color here.
* **Badge Preferences**: Choose where you want your badge to be printed (Front, Back, or Both).
* **Shirt Style**: Choose the style of shirt you prefer (Classic, Sport, or Hoodie).
* **Shirt Color**: Choose the color of shirt you prefer (Red, Blue, Green, Yellow, White, or Black).
