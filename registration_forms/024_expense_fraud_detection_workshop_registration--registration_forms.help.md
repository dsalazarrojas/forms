# Expense Fraud Detection Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Expense Fraud Detection Workshop Registration form is a supplementary form used by workshop organizers to collect attendee information for workshop management and follow-up.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Select the appropriate registration type (Attendee, Delegate, or Guest)
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name as they appear on your identification documents.
3. Provide a valid Email address for communication.
4. Enter your Phone number for easy contact.
5. Enter your Department for reference purposes.
6. Choose your Registration Date when you can attend the workshop.
7. Indicate your Attendance Status by selecting from the available options (Registered, Confirmed, Cancelled).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Type** (Required): Choose one of the three options: Attendee, Delegate, or Guest. This will determine your role in the workshop.
* **First Name** (Required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (Required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (Required): Provide a valid email address that can be used for communication and updates.
* **Phone** (Optional): Enter your phone number for easy contact in case we need to reach you.
* **Department** (Optional): Enter your department name for reference purposes.
* **Registration Date** (Required): Choose the date when you can attend the workshop.
* **Attendance Status** (Required): Select your current status (Registered, Confirmed, or Cancelled).
