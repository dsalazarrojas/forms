# Travel Rewards Program Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Travel Rewards Program Enrollment form is a user-facing form designed to collect information from individuals interested in enrolling in the travel rewards program.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the "Member Sign Up" page to begin the enrollment process.
2. Fill in your details for "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email".
3. Select your city of residence from "Address".
4. Input your "Phone Number".
5. Confirm with "Segment Pledge".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Member Sign Up** (`member_sign_up`, text, optional): This is the initial page for enrolling in the travel rewards program. It's a required field to start the enrollment process.
* **Segment Pledge** (`segment_pledge`, number, optional): This field is used to confirm your enrollment in the program. Please enter the correct segment pledge number as per the available options.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Address** (`address`, select_one, optional): Select your city of residence from the available options:
	+ New York
	+ Los Angeles
	+ Chicago

## Tips
* Ensure the details entered are accurate and up-to-date to avoid any issues with your enrollment.
* If you have any issues with the form, please contact our support team for assistance.
