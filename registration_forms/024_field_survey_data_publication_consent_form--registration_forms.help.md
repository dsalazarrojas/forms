# Field Survey Data Publication Consent Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in a field survey and to obtain their consent to publish their data.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Select the participant from the list provided.
2. Choose the relationship between you and the participant.
3. Select the level of consent you wish to obtain from the participant.
4. Choose the intended use of the data.
5. Select the contact information for the participant.
6. Enter the publication date for the participant's data.
7. Choose the data retention policy for the participant's data.
8. Select the data distribution options for the participant's data.
9. Choose whether to include a contact person for the participant's data.
10. Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the terms of data publication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`): Select the participant from the list provided.
	+ Select from: Participant 1, Participant 2, Participant 3
* **Participant Relationship** (`participant_relationship`): Choose the relationship between you and the participant.
	+ Select from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`): Select the level of consent you wish to obtain from the participant.
	+ Select from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Data Use** (`data_use`): Choose the intended use of the data.
	+ Select from: Option 1, Option 2
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`): Select the contact information for the participant.
	+ Select from: Yes, No
* **Publication Date** (`publication_date`): Enter the publication date for the participant's data.
	+ Format: MM/DD/YYYY
* **Data Retention** (`data_retention`): Choose the data retention policy for the participant's data.
	+ Select from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Data Distribution** (`data_distribution`): Select the data distribution options for the participant's data.
	+ Select from: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3
* **Contact Person** (`contact_person`): Choose whether to include a contact person for the participant's data.
	+ Select from: Yes, No
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`): Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the terms of data publication.
	+ Select from: Yes, No

## Tips
* Please fill out the form only once for each participant.
* Make sure to select the correct relationship between you and the participant.
* Choose the correct data retention and distribution options for the participant's data.
* Enter the correct publication date for the participant's data.
* Sign the form to confirm your agreement to the terms of data publication.
