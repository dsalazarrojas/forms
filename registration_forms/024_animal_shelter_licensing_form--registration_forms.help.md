# Animal Shelter Licensing Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to license your pet and provide essential information to the animal shelter.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
2. For select_one fields, choose the most suitable option that applies to your situation.
3. If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
4. Make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Pet Owner Full Name**: The legal name of the pet owner.
* **Email Address**: The contact email of the pet owner.
* **Phone Number**: The contact phone number of the pet owner.
* **Street Address**: The current address of the pet owner.
* **City**: The city where the pet owner lives.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where the pet owner lives.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: The postal code of the pet owner's address.
* **Pet Name**: The name of the pet.
* **Species**: The species of the pet, e.g., Dog, Cat, Rabbit, etc.
* **Breed**: The breed of the pet, if known.
* **Color and Markings**: A physical description of the pet.
* **Sex**: The sex of the pet, either Male or Female.
* **Age**: The age of the pet in years.
* **Weight**: The weight of the pet in pounds.
* **Is Your Pet Microchipped?**: Whether the pet is microchipped or not.
* **Microchip Number**: The microchip number of the pet, if applicable.
* **Is Your Pet Spayed or Neutered?**: Whether the pet is spayed or neutered or not.
* **Date of Procedure**: The date of the spaying or neutering procedure, if applicable.
* **Veterinarian Name**: The name of the primary veterinarian for the pet.
* **Veterinary Clinic**: The name of the veterinary clinic where the pet receives care.
* **Veterinarian Phone**: The contact phone number of the veterinarian.
* **Rabies Vaccination Status**: The rabies vaccination status of the pet, e.g., Up to Date, Due Soon, etc.
* **Rabies Vaccination Expiration Date**: The date of the rabies vaccination expiration, if applicable.
* **License Type**: The type of license being applied for, e.g., 1-Year License, 2-Year License, etc.
* **How Many Pets Do You Own?**: The total number of pets owned by the pet owner.
* **Has This Pet Been Licensed Here Before?**: Whether the pet has been licensed before or not.
* **Previous License Number**: The previous license number, if applicable.
* **Payment Method**: The method of payment, e.g., Credit Card, Debit Card, etc.
* **License Fee**: The calculated license fee based on the selections.
* **Additional Comments**: Any additional comments or notes from the pet owner.
* **I Certify That All Information Provided Is Accurate**: A certification from the pet owner that the information provided is accurate.
* **Owner Electronic Signature**: The electronic signature of the pet owner.
* **Application Date**: The date of the application.

Please note that all fields with "required: true" are mandatory and must be filled out accurately. If you have any questions or need help, please don't hesitate to ask.
