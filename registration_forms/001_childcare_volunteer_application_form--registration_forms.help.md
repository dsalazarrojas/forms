# Childcare Volunteer Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help us understand your qualifications, experience, and availability as a potential volunteer for our childcare program. We require certain information to ensure a safe and nurturing environment for the children we care for.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your personal details in the **Personal Information** section.
2. Provide your contact information in the **Contact Information** section.
3. Answer questions regarding your **Background Check** and **Availability**.
4. Share your **Motivation** for volunteering with us.
5. Finally, submit the form to complete your application.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Enter your legal first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your legal last name.
* **Date of Birth**: We need to verify your age for this role. Please enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Email Address**: We will use this to contact you for further information and updates.
* **Phone Number**: Include area code and country code if applicable.
* **Street Address**: Your residential address.
* **City**: The city where you reside.
* **State or Province**: The state or province where you reside.
* **Postal Code**: Your postal code.
* **Do you have any criminal convictions?**: Select 'Yes' if you have been convicted of any crime.
* **Do you consent to a background check?**: We require this for all volunteers. Please select 'Yes' to confirm.
* **Years of childcare experience**: Include paid and volunteer experience. Please be specific about the age groups you've worked with.
* **Age groups you've worked with**: Select all that apply. This can include infants, toddlers, preschoolers, school-aged children, and teenagers.
* **Do you have current CPR certification?**: Select 'Yes' if you hold an active CPR certification.
* **Do you have first aid certification?**: Select 'Yes' if you hold an active first aid certification (optional).
* **Preferred Volunteer Schedule**: Select your availability. Options include weekdays, weekends, flexible, or specific times.
* **Hours available per week**: Please approximate the number of hours you can volunteer per week.
* **Why are you interested in volunteering with our childcare program?**: Share your motivation in 50-200 words.
* **Can you provide three professional references?**: Select 'Yes' if you can provide references for your work experience.
* **Background Information**: This section is for your reference. Please use it as a note.
* **Availability**: This section is for your reference. Please use it as a note.
* **Motivation and Background**: This section is for your reference. Please use it as a note.
