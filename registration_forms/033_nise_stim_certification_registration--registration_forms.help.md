# NISE Stim Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from applicants for the NISE Stim Certification program registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully review the form and its fields to ensure you have all necessary information ready.
- Fill in your details accurately and completely, especially for the required fields (marked with an asterisk *).
- Select your preferred dates and training provider (if applicable).
- Double-check your details before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Applicant Details** (*): Enter your name, address, and any other relevant personal details as prompted.
- **Preferred Dates**: Select the desired dates for your certification training. Please note that the training dates may be subject to change.
- **Credentials**: Provide any relevant credentials, certifications, or experience that may support your application.
- **Applicant Institution**: Enter the name of the institution where you are currently employed or associated with.
- **Certification Dates**: Select the date you completed your certification or when you expect to receive it.
- **Training Provider**: Enter the name of the training provider you used for your certification.
- **Program Enrollment**: Mention the program you are enrolling in or associated with (if any).
- **Preferred Time**: Select your preferred time for the certification training (if applicable).
- **Certification Requirements**: Describe the requirements you have met for certification.
- **Email** (*): Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone**: Optional, but enter your contact number if you want to be reached.
- **Submit**: Once you have filled in all necessary information, click this button to submit your registration.

Note: Remember to double-check your details before submitting the form. If you encounter any errors or have questions, contact your registration team for assistance.
