# Speaker Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Speaker Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from speakers to manage the event efficiently. Please fill out the form to provide your details for a smooth event experience.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on the form link to access the form.
2. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
3. Review your information before submission to ensure it is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Speaker Information** (speaker_info): This field is for your general information and is not required.
* **Speaker Name** (speaker_name): Enter your name as it appears on your identification or business card.
* **Speaker Email** (speaker_email): Enter your email address that you regularly check for event-related communications. This should be a valid email address.
* **Speaker Phone** (speaker_phone): Enter your phone number that you are contactable on. This can be a mobile or landline number.
* **Speaker Bio** (speaker_bio): Briefly describe your expertise or experience in a few sentences.
* **Speaker Services** (speaker_services): Select all services you offer (e.g., consulting, coaching, speaking).
* **Speaker Availability** (speaker_availability): Choose a date for your availability.
* **Speaker Experience** (speaker_experience): Enter your years of experience in your field.
* **Availability Time** (availability_time): Select a time slot for your availability.
* **Assigned Tool** (assigned_tool): This field is not required but can be used to assign tools to speakers for their sessions.
* **Submission** (submission): This is a note field for any additional comments or information you want to provide.

Note: The fields marked as "required" are mandatory and need to be filled out.
