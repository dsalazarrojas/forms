# Digital Humanities Collaboration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Digital Humanities Collaboration Registration form is a tool used to collect information from researchers and project leads about their digital humanities projects. This form helps us understand the projects' details, such as title, description, location, and budget. It also allows us to gather information about the lead's contact information, research interests, and collaboration preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter the **Project Title** of your digital humanities project in the "Project Title" field.
2. Provide a brief description of your project in the "Project Description" field.
3. Enter your name and contact information in the "Project Lead" and "Project Lead Email" fields.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Project Lead Phone" field.
5. Indicate whether your project will be a lab project by selecting "Yes" or "No" in the "Lab Participants" field.
6. List your research interests in the "Research Interests" field.
7. Describe your collaboration preferences in the "Collaboration Preferences" field.
8. If your project has a specific submission date, enter it in the "Submission Date" field (optional).
9. If your project has a specific submission time, enter it in the "Submission Time" field (optional).
10. Enter your project budget in the "Project Budget" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Title** (Text, Required): Enter the title of your digital humanities project.
* **Project Description** (Text, Required): Provide a brief description of your project.
* **Project Lead** (Text, Required): Enter your name.
* **Project Lead Email** (Email, Required): Enter your email address.
* **Project Lead Phone** (Text, Required): Enter your phone number.
* **Lab Participants** (Select One, Required): Indicate whether your project will be a lab project by selecting "Yes" or "No".
* **Research Interests** (Text, Required): List your research interests.
* **Collaboration Preferences** (Text, Required): Describe your collaboration preferences.
* **Submission Date** (Date, Optional): Enter the specific submission date of your project (if applicable).
* **Submission Time** (Time, Optional): Enter the specific submission time of your project (if applicable).
* **Project Budget** (Number, Required): Enter your project budget.
* **Project Location** (Text, Optional): Enter the location of your project (optional).
