# Government Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Government Vendor Registration Form is designed to collect information about vendors that can provide goods and services to the government. It helps to ensure that vendors are properly registered, which can help to prevent fraud and ensure that the government has accurate information about the vendors it deals with.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, vendors must carefully read and follow the instructions for each field below. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the government office that issued this form.
