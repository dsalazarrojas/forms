# wildlife_volunteer_program_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The wildlife volunteer program is designed to engage individuals in conservation efforts. This form is used to register and track volunteer information.


## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out one page at a time.
2. Ensure you provide accurate information for each field.
3. Review each field explanation for specific guidance.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Information** (`user_info`, text, required: false): Provide a brief description of yourself as a volunteer. 
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, required: false): Enter your email address and phone number for communication purposes.
* **Volunteer Experience** (`volunteer_experience`, select_multiple, required: false): Check if you have volunteer experience related to wildlife conservation.
* **Wildlife Interests** (`wildlife_interests`, select_multiple, required: false): Select the areas of wildlife interest that you have experience or knowledge about.
* **Availability** (`availability`, select_one, required: false): Choose when you are available to volunteer. This could be for Wildlife Conservation Efforts, Special Events, Community Outreach, or other options.
* **Conservation Goals** (`conservation_goals`, number, required: false): If you have a number, please enter how many conservation goals you have or aim for. This could be a number of species, projects or events.
* **Conservation Commitment** (`conservation_commitment`, number, required: false): If you have a number, please enter how many hours you can commit to conservation each month. 
* **Volunteer Frequency** (`volunteer_frequency`, select_one, required: false): Select how often you would like to volunteer.
* **Volunteer Start Date** (`volunteer_start_date`, date, required: false): If you have started volunteering, enter the date you started.
* **Volunteer End Date** (`volunteer_end_date`, date, required: false): If you have ended your volunteer service, enter the date you stopped.
* **Volunteer Status** (`volunteer_status`, select_multiple, required: false): Mark your current volunteer status. This could be Active, Inactive, or other options.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number for communication purposes.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your volunteer service.

Note: Please be accurate when filling out this form. If you have any doubts or questions, do not hesitate to ask.
