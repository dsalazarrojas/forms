# Nature Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for participant registration in the nature program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by providing your basic details such as name, email, and contact information.
- Select your interest in the nature program by checking 'Yes' or 'No' accordingly.
- Provide your age and date of birth accurately.
- Enter any additional comments or notes about your enrollment in the 'Nature Program Details' field.
- If you have a reference, please select the corresponding option.
- Agree to share your information and terms of service.
- Finally, provide any additional notes if necessary.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (Required): Enter your full name as you would like to be referred to.
* **Participant Email** (Required): Enter a valid email address that we can contact you with.
* **Nature Program** (Required): Select 'Yes' to indicate your interest in the nature program.
* **Participant Age** (Required): Enter your age accurately.
* **Participant Phone** (Required): Enter your contact phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (Required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (mm/dd/yyyy).
* **Nature Program Details** (Required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your enrollment.
* **Participant Gender** (Multiple Selections): Select all the gender categories that apply to you.
* **Participant Interest** (Note): Enter any additional comments or notes about your interests.
* **Nature Program Location** (Required): Enter the location of the nature program you are enrolling in.
* **Participant Agreement** (Required): Select 'Yes' to agree to the participant agreement.
* **Participant Terms of Service** (Multiple Selections): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms of service.
* **Participant Notes** (Required): Enter any additional comments or notes about your enrollment.
* **Additional Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes.
* **Nature Program Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the nature program.
* **Participant Reference** (Optional): Select 'Yes' to indicate if you have a reference.
* **Participant Agrees to Share** (Optional): Select 'Yes' to agree to share your information.
* **Participant Agrees to Terms of Service** (Multiple Selections, Optional): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms of service.
* **Additional Nature Program Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the nature program.
* **Participant Agrees to Terms of Service 2** (Optional): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms of service.
* **Participant Agrees to Terms of Service 3** (Optional): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms of service.
* **Participant Agrees to Terms of Service 4** (Optional): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms of service.
* **More Nature Program Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the nature program.
* **Participant Agrees to Terms of Service 5** (Optional): Select 'Yes' to agree to the terms of service.
* **Extra Nature Program Notes** (Optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about the nature program.
