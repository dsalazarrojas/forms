# Ultimate Beneficial Owner Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Ultimate Beneficial Owner Registration Form is designed to collect information about the ultimate beneficial owner of a legal entity. This form is used for compliance and internal record-keeping purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
- Carefully read through each page and understand the required information before starting to fill out the form.
- Ensure that you have accurate and up-to-date information about the ultimate beneficial owner.
- Fill out each field correctly and thoroughly, as indicated by the field labels and explanations below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **legal_entity (Text, Required):** Enter the name of the legal entity.
* **owner_name (Text, Required):** Enter the name of the ultimate beneficial owner.
* **owner_address (Text, Required):** Enter the address of the ultimate beneficial owner.
* **owner_date_of_birth (Date, Optional):** Enter the date of birth of the ultimate beneficial owner, if applicable.
* **owner_nationality (Select One, Required):** Select 'Yes' if the ultimate beneficial owner is a resident of the country in which the legal entity is located.
* **owner_id_number (Number, Required):** Enter the ID number of the ultimate beneficial owner.
* **owner_residence (Text, Required):** Enter the residence of the ultimate beneficial owner.
* **owner_position (Text, Required):** Enter the position or title of the ultimate beneficial owner.
* **owner_relationship (Text, Optional):** Describe the relationship between the ultimate beneficial owner and the legal entity, if applicable.
* **beneficial_owner_type (Select Multiple, Required):** Select all that apply for the beneficial owner type of the ultimate beneficial owner.
* **beneficial_owner_shareholding (Number, Optional):** Enter the shareholding percentage of the ultimate beneficial owner, if applicable.
* **beneficial_owner_ownership_type (Select One, Required):** Select 'Yes' if the ultimate beneficial owner has ownership or control over the legal entity.
* **ultimate_beneficial_owner_id (Select One, Optional):** Select 'Yes' if the ultimate beneficial owner is the ultimate beneficial owner of the legal entity.
* **ultimate_beneficial_owner_date (Date, Optional):** Enter the date the ultimate beneficial owner became the ultimate beneficial owner, if applicable.
* **ultimate_beneficial_owner_shareholding (Number, Optional):** Enter the shareholding percentage of the ultimate beneficial owner, if applicable.

Note: This form requires accurate and up-to-date information about the ultimate beneficial owner. Ensure that you fill out each field correctly and thoroughly to avoid submission issues.
