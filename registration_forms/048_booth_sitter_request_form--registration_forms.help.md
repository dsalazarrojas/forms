# Booth Sitter Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from exhibitors about their booth needs for event coverage. This form helps ensure that booth sitters are adequately prepared and informed about the exhibitor's booth and requirements for a smooth and successful event experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill in the **Exhibitor Name** field with your company or name.
2. Enter the **Contact Person** field with the full name of the contact person.
3. Type in the **Email Address** field with the contact email address.
4. Enter the **Phone Number** field with the contact phone number.
5. Enter the **Event Name** field with the name of the event.
6. Fill in the **Event Dates** field with the dates of the event.
7. Enter the **Booth Number** field with your assigned booth number.
8. Select the **Date Sitter Needed** field with the day you need coverage.
9. Enter the **Start Time** field with the time coverage should start.
10. Enter the **End Time** field with the time coverage should end.
11. Select all the tasks that apply in the **Sitter Responsibilities** field.
12. Write a brief description of your booth in the **Booth Background Information** field.
13. If needed, fill in the **Key Messages for Sitter** field with any important information.
14. If needed, enter the **Materials Location** field with the location of materials.
15. If needed, enter any **Special Instructions** in this field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Exhibitor Name** (Text, Required): Your company or name.
- **Contact Person** (Text, Required): Full name of the contact person.
- **Email Address** (Email, Required): Contact email address.
- **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Contact phone number.
- **Event Name** (Text, Required): Name of the event.
- **Event Dates** (Text, Required): When is the event.
- **Booth Number** (Text, Required): Your assigned booth number.
- **Date Sitter Needed** (Date, Required): Which day do you need coverage.
- **Start Time** (Time, Required): When should coverage start.
- **End Time** (Time, Required): When should coverage end.
- **Sitter Responsibilities** (Select Multiple, Required): Monitor booth, Greet visitors, Distribute materials, Answer basic questions, Process inquiries, Record leads, Collect feedback, Other.
- **Booth Background Information** (Text, Required): Brief description of your booth.
- **Key Messages for Sitter** (Text, Optional): What key messages you want them to know.
- **Materials Location** (Text, Optional): Location where materials are kept.
- **Special Instructions** (Text, Optional): Any other important details.

**Tips**

- Double-check the event dates and times to ensure the correct information.
- Be specific when filling in the **Booth Responsibilities** field to ensure the sitter understands their role.
- If you have any special requirements or requests, fill in the **Special Instructions** field.
- Review the form carefully before submitting to avoid missing any important information.
