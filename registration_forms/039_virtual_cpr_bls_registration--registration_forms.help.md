# Virtual CPR/BLS Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register participants for virtual CPR/BLSE training sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Review each page carefully to ensure you've provided all required information.
2. Fill out the form accurately and completely.
3. Ensure you've signed the participant agreement and other relevant sections.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Session Scheduling** (`session_scheduling`, date, required: false): Select the date for the session you wish to attend.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about yourself that may be helpful for the organizers.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant E-mail** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, required: false): Select the date for the session you wish to attend.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, required: false): Select the time for the session you wish to attend.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, select_one, required: false): Choose the location you wish to attend from the options provided.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of class you wish to attend from the options provided.
* **Organized Form Submission** (`organized_form_submission`, note, required: false): This field is for any additional information or comments about your submission.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about yourself.
* **CPR/CRP** (`cpr_cpr`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you have a current CPR/CRP certification.
* **BLS/BLSE** (`bls_blse`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you have a current BLS/BLSE certification.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Accept the participant agreement terms.
* **Session Scheduling Agreement** (`session_scheduling_agreement`, select_one, required: false): Accept the session scheduling terms.
* **Participant Agreement Terms** (`participant_agreement_terms`, select_one, required: false): Accept the participant agreement terms.
* **Participant Certificate** (`participant_certificate`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you need a certificate upon completion.
* **Participant Certificate Terms** (`participant_certificate_terms`, select_one, required: false): Accept the participant certificate terms.
* **Session Certificate** (`session_certificate`, select_one, required: false): Choose whether you need a session certificate.
* **Session Certificate Terms** (`session_certificate_terms`, select_one, required: false): Accept the session certificate terms.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, select_one, required: false): Sign to indicate acceptance of terms and conditions.
* **Session Signature** (`session_signature`, select_one, required: false): Sign to indicate acceptance of session terms and conditions.
* **Participant Signature Terms** (`participant_signature_terms`, select_one, required: false): Accept the participant signature terms.
* **Participant Signature Date** (`participant_signature_date`, date, required: false): Sign and date your acceptance of terms and conditions.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required: false): Enter any additional information or comments about yourself.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required: false): Enter your name as it appears on your official identification.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required: false): Enter your official email address.
* **Participant Location** (`participant_location`, select_one, required: false): Choose the location you wish to attend from the options provided.
* **Class Type** (`class_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of class you wish to attend from the options provided.

## Tips
* Ensure you've reviewed each field carefully to avoid missing required information.
* If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.
* Please submit only one registration form per participant.
