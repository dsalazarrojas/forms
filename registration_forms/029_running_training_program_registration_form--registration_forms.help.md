# running_training_program_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users who want to participate in a running training program.

## How To Complete This Form
Please follow the steps outlined above to complete this form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Information** (`user_info`, text, optional): Enter your user information, such as your name and any other relevant details.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, text, optional): Provide your contact information, including your address, phone number, and email address.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Select a date using the calendar picker.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Select a time using the clock picker.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select_one, optional): Select "Yes" or "No" from the dropdown list.
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select_multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options from the dropdown list.
* **Select Integer** (`select_integer`, text, optional): Enter a number.
* **Decimal** (`decimal`, number, optional): Enter a decimal number.
* **Training Level** (`training_level`, select_multiple, optional): Select a training level from the dropdown list.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Select a payment method from the dropdown list.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, text, optional): Enter the relationship between you and your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, date, optional): Select a date for the program start.
* **Training Plan** (`training_plan`, text, optional): Enter the details of your training plan.
* **Training Plan Type** (`training_plan_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of training plan.
* **Training Plan Start Date** (`training_plan_start_date`, date, optional): Select a start date for the training plan.
* **Training Plan End Date** (`training_plan_end_date`, date, optional): Select an end date for the training plan.
* **Training Plan End Time** (`training_plan_end_time`, time, optional): Select an end time for the training plan.
