**Music Class Participant Application Form - Help Guide**

## Purpose
This form is used by potential participants to register for a music class. Fill out this form to provide your personal details and musical interests.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1.  **Start by providing your Participant Information**:
    * **Participant Information** (`Participant Information`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
2.  **Select your Preferred Instrument**:
    * **Preferred Instrument** (`Preferred Instrument`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the instruments you would like to play. You can select one or multiple instruments.
3.  **Share your Music Interests**:
    * **Music Interests** (`Music Interests`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose the genre of music you enjoy. You can select one or multiple genres.
4.  **Enter your Availability**:
    * **Start Date** (`Start Date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you want to start the music class.
    * **End Date** (`End Date`, `date`, required): Please enter the date you want to end the music class.
5.  **Add any extra information**:
    * **Participant Notes** (`Participant Notes`, `note`, optional): If you have any comments or requests, you can add them here.
    * **Participant Notes 2** (`Participant Notes 2`, `note`, optional): You can add another note if needed.
    * **Participant Notes 3** (`Participant Notes 3`, `note`, optional): Add yet another note if you need to.

## Tips

*   Be honest when selecting your music interests to ensure you receive the most suitable music class.
*   If you have any special requests or comments, please add them in the Participant Notes fields.

The purpose of this form is to collect information about participants' personal details and musical preferences to assign them to the most suitable music class.
