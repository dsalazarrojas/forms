# mother_s_day_cruise_registration_form_template - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for users to provide their details for the Mother's Day cruise event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please fill out the form completely, ensuring all required fields are completed.
2. Ensure your email address is accurate to receive communication and updates from the cruise organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`1`, `text`, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`2`, `text`, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`3`, `email`, required): Enter your personal email address to receive updates and communication from the cruise organizers.
* **Phone** (`4`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Preferred Cabin Type** (`5`, `select_one`, required): Select the type of cabin you prefer, either 'Yes' or 'No' for cabin preferences.
* **Preferred Cabin Number** (`6`, `number`, required): Enter the specific cabin number you are interested in.
* **Departure Date** (`7`, `date`, required): Enter your departure date for the cruise.
* **Return Date** (`8`, `date`, required): Enter your return date for the cruise.
* **Arrival Time** (`9`, `time`, required): Enter your expected arrival time for the cruise.
* **Special Requests** (`10`, `text`, optional): If you have any special requests or preferences, please list them here.
