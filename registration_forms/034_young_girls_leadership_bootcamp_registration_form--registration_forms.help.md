# Young Girls Leadership Bootcamp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in joining the Young Girls Leadership Bootcamp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out each page, and make sure to complete all required fields.
2. Review your input carefully before submitting the form.
3. If you need to go back to a previous page, use the navigation buttons at the top of the page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1:
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Reported Name** (`reported_name`, text, optional): Enter the name you would like to be referred to.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Address** (`address`, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Zip Code** (`zip_code`, number, optional): Enter your zip code.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, optional): Enter your time of birth. (Note: This field is not currently used for any specific functionality)

### Page 2:
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, optional): Select your participant type (Junior, Senior, or Adult).
* **Group Participation** (`group_participation`, select_multiple, optional): Select whether you will participate in groups (Yes or No).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Email** (`emergency_contact_email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.

### Page 3:
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Participant Questions** (`participant_questions`, text, optional): Enter any questions or concerns you may have as a participant.
* **Emergency Contact Relation** (`emergency_contact_relation`, text, optional): Enter your relation with your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, text, optional): Enter the address of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, optional): Enter the city of your emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact State** (`emergency_contact_state`, text, optional): Enter the state of your emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact Zip Code** (`emergency_contact_zip_code`, number, optional): Enter the zip code of your emergency contact person's address.
* **Emergency Contact Country** (`emergency_contact_country`, text, optional): Enter the country of your emergency contact person's address.
* **Participant Reference** (`participant_reference`, text, optional): Enter a participant reference number or name.
* **Participant Reference URL** (`participant_reference_url`, text, optional): Enter the URL of the participant reference.
* **Participant Reference Identifier** (`participant_reference_identifier`, text, optional): Enter a unique participant reference identifier. (Note: This field is not currently used for any specific functionality)

## Tips
* Make sure to review your input carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to contact us.
* We will be in touch with you shortly after receiving your form submission.
