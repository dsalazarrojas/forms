# Choir Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who wish to join the choir. The information collected will be used to manage the choir's membership and schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill in your personal details on the Member Information page, including your first name, last name, date of birth, age, phone number, and email address.
2. On the Vocal Information page, select your vocal part (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, etc.) and indicate your vocal range.
3. Select your years of singing experience and previous choir experience on their respective pages.
4. On the Availability page, select all the days and times you are available to attend rehearsals and performances.
5. On the Membership Details page, select your membership type (Full member, Associate member, etc.), enter your membership year, and choose how you wish to pay your membership fee.
6. On the Additional Information page, enter any additional information you'd like to share with the choir.
7. On the Registration page, agree to follow choir rules and guidelines, commit to regular attendance, and provide your digital signature.
8. Finally, date and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`member_first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`member_last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`): Enter your date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Age** (`age`): Enter your age.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`): Enter your phone number.
* **Email Address** (`email`): Enter your email address.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Phone** (`emergency_contact`): Enter the name and phone number of an emergency contact.
* **Vocal Part** (`voice_part`): Select your vocal part (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass, etc.).
* **Vocal Range** (`vocal_range`): Enter your vocal range.
* **Years of Singing Experience** (`years_singing`): Select your years of singing experience.
* **Previous Choir Experience** (`choir_experience`): Select your previous choir experience.
* **Can you read music?** (`can_read_music`): Select if you can read music fluently, basically, or not at all.
* **Previous Choirs** (`previous_choirs`): Enter the names and dates of any previous choirs you've attended.
* **Rehearsal Schedule** (`rehearsal_schedule`): Enter your available rehearsal schedule.
* **Available Days** (`available_days`): Select all the days you are available to attend rehearsals and performances.
* **Available Times** (`available_times`): Select all the times you are available to attend rehearsals and performances.
* **Known Schedule Conflicts** (`schedule_conflicts`): Enter any known schedule conflicts.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`): Select your membership type (Full member, Associate member, etc.).
* **Membership Year** (`membership_year`): Enter your membership year.
* **Membership Fee** (`membership_fee`): Select how you wish to pay your membership fee.
* **Concert Uniform Size** (`uniform_size`): Select your concert uniform size.
* **Choir T-Shirt Size** (`t_shirt_size`): Select your choir t-shirt size.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`): Enter any additional information you'd like to share with the choir.
* **Relevant Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`): Enter any relevant medical conditions for safety purposes.
* **Special Needs or Accommodations** (`special_needs`): Enter any special needs or accommodations.
* **How did you hear about this choir?** (`how_did_you_hear`): Select how you heard about the choir.
* **Why do you want to join?** (`why_join`): Enter why you wish to join the choir.
* **I agree to follow choir rules and guidelines** (`agree_rules`): Select if you agree to follow choir rules and guidelines.
* **I commit to regular attendance** (`commit_attendance`): Select if you commit to regular attendance.
* **Digital Signature** (`member_signature`): Enter your digital signature.
* **Date of Registration** (`registration_date`): Enter the date of your registration.

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled in before submission.
* Please answer all fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please reach out to the relevant contact person.
* Once submitted, you will receive a confirmation email with instructions on what to do next.
* Please review your submission carefully before submission to ensure accuracy.
