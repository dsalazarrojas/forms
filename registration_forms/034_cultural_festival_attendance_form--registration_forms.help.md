# Cultural Festival Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Cultural Festival Attendance Form is designed to collect information about your festival attendance. This form is used to record your event details, preferences, and payment methods.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the personal details section with your first name, last name, and email address.
2. Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
3. Select the type of event you attended (Festival, Concert, Show, or Exhibition).
4. Enter the date of your attendance.
5. If applicable, enter the ticket price you paid.
6. Choose your preferred payment method (Cash, Card, or Online).
7. Add any additional notes or comments.
8. Select the event details that align with your experience (Food and Beverage, Entertainment, Games, or Activities).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: Please enter your first name. If you have a nickname, feel free to use it.
* **Last name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address where you can be reached for event updates.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Event type**: Choose the type of event you attended (Festival, Concert, Show, or Exhibition).
* **Attendance date**: Select the date of your attendance (mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy format).
* **Ticket price**: If you paid for a ticket, enter the price you paid.
* **Payment method**: Choose how you paid for your ticket (Cash, Card, or Online).
* **Notes**: Add any additional comments or thoughts about your experience.
* **Event details**: Select all event details that apply to your experience (Food and Beverage, Entertainment, Games, or Activities).

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled in, if applicable.
* If you're unsure about a field, refer to the provided hints for guidance.
* Double-check your entries before submission to ensure accuracy.
* If you need assistance, contact us for support.
