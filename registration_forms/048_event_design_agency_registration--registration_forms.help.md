This form is designed to collect information from event design agencies who wish to register their details with our platform. The purpose of this form is to gather essential information about the agency, including their services offered, contact details, and portfolio links. This form is meant for agencies to showcase their credentials and connect with our team to potentially collaborate on upcoming events.

To register an event design agency, follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
2.  Provide accurate and up-to-date information about your agency, services, contact details, and portfolio links.
3.  Review and submit your completed form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Design Agency Registration** (event_design_agency_registration_form, text, required: false)
    * This is the name of your event design agency.
* **Agency Details** (agency_details, text, required: false)
    * Enter a brief description of your agency, including your experience and specialties.
* **Services** (services, select_multiple, required: false)
    * Select the services you offer, such as event design, branding, or video production.
* **Contact Email** (contact_email, email, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's primary contact email address.
* **Services List** (services_list, text, required: false)
    * Provide a list of specific services your agency offers, such as event planning or graphics design.
* **Contact Telephone** (contact_telephone, text, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's contact telephone number.
* **Event Type** (event_type, select_one, required: false)
    * Choose the type of event you specialize in, such as weddings or corporate events.
* **Event Description** (event_description, text, required: false)
    * Enter a brief description of the events you design.
* **Services List 2** (services_list_2, text, required: false)
    * Provide an additional list of services your agency offers.
* **Portfolio** (portfolio, note, required: false)
    * This is a text field for you to describe your agency's portfolio.
* **Portfolio Link** (portfolio_link, text, required: false)
    * Enter a link to your agency's portfolio.
* **Portfolio URL** (portfolio_link_url, text, required: false)
    * Enter a URL link to your agency's portfolio.
* **Contact Facebook** (contact_facebook, email, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's Facebook contact email address.
* **Agency Address** (agency_address, text, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's physical address.
* **Agency Address 2** (agency_address_2, text, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's secondary address.
* **Agency Address 3** (agency_address_3, text, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's tertiary address.
* **Agency Website** (website, text, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's website URL.
* **Agency Instagram** (instagram, select_one, required: false)
    * Choose your agency's Instagram option (Option A, Option B, or Option C).
* **Agency Video** (agency_video, text, required: false)
    * Enter a YouTube URL link to your agency's video.
* **Portfolio Video** (portfolio_video, text, required: false)
    * Enter a YouTube URL link to your portfolio video.
* **Agency Video Description** (agency_video_description, text, required: false)
    * Enter a description of your agency's video.
* **Agency Video Description 2** (agency_video_description_2, text, required: false)
    * Enter a description of your agency's video 2.
* **Contact Twitter** (contact_twitter, text, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's contact Twitter handle.
* **Agency Facebook** (contact_facebook_2, email, required: false)
    * Enter your agency's Facebook contact email address.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill out all required fields to ensure your agency's registration is complete.
* Use clear and concise language when filling out the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to contact our team for assistance.
