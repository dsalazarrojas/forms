# Social Skills Summer Camp Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Social Skills Summer Camp Form is used to collect personal and emergency contact information from participants in the social skills summer camp program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in its entirety, or at least the required fields marked as such.
2. Ensure that you have permission from the participant to collect their personal and emergency contact information.
3. Enter the participant's name and email address in the correct format (e.g. `john.doe@example.com`).
4. Enter the participant's phone number in the correct format (e.g. `123-456-7890`).
5. Enter the emergency contact's name.
6. Enter the emergency contact's relationship to the participant (e.g. parent, guardian, etc.).
7. Enter the emergency contact's phone number.
8. Enter the emergency contact's email address in the correct format (e.g. `john.doe@example.com`).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Summer Camp Form** (summer_camp_form, text, required: false)
	+ This field is a text field for general comments or notes about the participant.
* **Participants Name** (participants_name, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the participant's name.
* **Participants E-mail** (participants_email, email, required: false)
	+ This field is for the participant's email address. Please enter it in the correct format (e.g. `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Participants Phone** (participants_phone, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the participant's phone number. Please enter it in the correct format (e.g. `123-456-7890`).
* **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the emergency contact's name.
* **Emergency Contact Relationship** (emergency_contact_relationship, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the emergency contact's relationship to the participant (e.g. parent, guardian, etc.).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required: false)
	+ This field is for the emergency contact's phone number.
* **Emergency Contact E-mail** (emergency_contact_email, email, required: false)
	+ This field is for the emergency contact's email address. Please enter it in the correct format (e.g. `john.doe@example.com`).
