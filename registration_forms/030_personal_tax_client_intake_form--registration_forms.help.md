# Personal Tax Client Intake Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Personal Tax Client Intake Form is designed to collect essential personal and financial information from clients seeking tax services. This form helps tax professionals understand the client's situation, allowing them to provide tailored advice and accurate tax services.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please review the form carefully to understand the required and optional fields.
2. Ensure that you complete all the fields accurately and honestly.
3. If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to ask the tax professional for clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Personal Information (1)**:
	+ **Personal Information** (`personal_taxes`, text, required/optional): Please enter your personal details as requested.
* **Address (2)**:
	+ **Address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Phone (3)**:
	+ **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number in the international format (e.g., +123 456 7890).
* **Email (4)**:
	+ **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address in a standard format (e.g., joe@example.com).
* **Tax Advisor (5)**:
	+ **Tax Advisor** (`tax_advisor`, select_one, required/optional): Please select 'Yes' if you have a tax advisor, 'No' otherwise.
* **Tax Return Info (6)**:
	+ **Tax Return Info** (`tax_return_info`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have tax returns, 'No' otherwise.
* **Income Earned (7)**:
	+ **Income Earned** (`income_earned`, number, required/optional): Enter your total income earned in the relevant financial year.
* **Dependents (8)**:
	+ **Dependents** (`dependents`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of dependents you have.
* **Date of Birth (9)**:
	+ **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Birth (10)**:
	+ **Time of Birth** (`time_of_birth`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of birth in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00).
* **Note (11)**:
	+ **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Enter any relevant notes or comments.
* **Date of Marriage (12)**:
	+ **Date of Marriage** (`date_of_marriage`, date, required/optional): Enter your date of marriage in the standard date format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Time of Marriage (13)**:
	+ **Time of Marriage** (`time_of_marriage`, time, required/optional): Enter your time of marriage in 24-hour format (e.g., 12:00).
* **Dependent Information (14)**:
	+ **Dependent Information** (`dependents_info`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select 'Yes' if you have dependent information, 'No' otherwise.

## Tips
- Please ensure that you answer all the questions honestly and accurately.
- If you are unsure about any field, do not hesitate to ask the tax professional for clarification.
- Review the form carefully before submission to avoid errors.
