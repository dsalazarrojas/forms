# Cultural Society Costume Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to submit entries for the Cultural Society's Costume Tournament. The form collects information about the user's chosen costume, event, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Click on the "Enter Entry" page.
2. Enter your entry information in the fields provided:
	* Event: Select the event associated with this costume entry.
	* Photo: Enter a photo URL or a brief description of your costume.
	* Description: Provide a brief description of your costume.
	* Organizer: Enter the name of the organizer associated with this entry.
	* Contact: Enter a contact email address.
	* Phone: Enter a phone number.
	* Submitter: Enter your name as the submitter.
	* Date: Enter the date of the event.
	* Time: Enter the time of the event.
	* Note: Enter any additional notes about this entry.
3. Click on the "Submit" button to submit your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Event** (Option 1, 2, or 3, optional): Select the event associated with this costume entry.
* **Photo** (text, optional): Enter a photo URL or a brief description of your costume. This can be a URL to a public image or a short text description of your costume.
* **Description** (text, optional): Provide a brief description of your costume. Keep it concise and focused on your costume design and creativity.
* **Organizer** (text, optional): Enter the name of the organizer associated with this entry. This is usually the name of the person or group hosting the event.
* **Contact** (email, optional): Enter a contact email address. This can be your own email or the contact email of the event organizer.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter a phone number. This can be your own phone number or the phone number of the event organizer.
* **Submitter** (text, optional): Enter your name as the submitter. This is usually your own name, but can be any name associated with the entry.
* **Date** (date, optional): Enter the date of the event. Make sure to format it correctly (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Time** (time, optional): Enter the time of the event. Use the 24-hour clock format (HH:MM).
* **Note** (note, optional): Enter any additional notes about this entry. This is a free-text field, so enter whatever is relevant to your entry.
* **Submit** (text, optional): Click on this button to submit your entry and complete the form.
