# youth_leadership_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The youth leadership registration form is a tool for collecting information from youth leaders to create a record of their registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with the required information for the youth leader.
2. Double-check the form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter the first name of the youth leader.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter the last name of the youth leader.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter the email address of the youth leader.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter the phone number of the youth leader.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter the name of the organization the youth leader is associated with.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Select the role of the youth leader (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Program Type** (`program_type`, select_one, optional): Select whether the youth leader is part of the program (e.g., 'Yes' or 'No').
* **Leadership Program** (`leadership_program`, select_multiple, optional): Select the programs the youth leader is part of (multiple selections are allowed).
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Enter the start date of the youth leader's program.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, optional): Enter the end date of the youth leader's program.
* **Time** (`time`, time, optional): Enter the time of the youth leader's program.
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional notes about the youth leader.
* **Submitted By** (`submitted_by`, text, optional): Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On** (`submitted_on`, date, optional): Enter the date the form was submitted.

Note: All fields with "optional" status are not mandatory but are included for completeness. Ensure you fill out all required fields for accurate registration.
