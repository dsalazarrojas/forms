# Webinar Attendee Email Validation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and verify attendee email addresses for a webinar. This form is designed to help webinar organizers track attendance and send reminders to attendees.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill out your email address in the "Attendee Email" field.
* Enter your email address again in the "Confirm Email" field to verify that it's correct.
* Enter the title of the webinar you want to attend in the "Webinar Title" field.
* Select the start date of the webinar from the calendar picker (note: this field is not explicitly labeled, so I'm inferring the functionality based on the name).
* Select the end date of the webinar from the calendar picker (again, inferring the functionality based on the name).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Attendee Email** (`attendee_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address that you want to use to receive updates and reminders about the webinar.
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, email, required/optional): Re-enter your email address to verify that it's correct. This helps prevent typos and ensures we have the right email on file.
* **Webinar Title** (`webinar_title`, text, required/optional): Enter the title of the webinar you want to attend. This helps us associate your email with the correct event.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, required/optional): Select the start date of the webinar. Please make sure to select a date that works for you.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, date, required/optional): Select the end date of the webinar. Again, please choose a date that suits your schedule.
