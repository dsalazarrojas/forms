# Mixology Showdown Tournament Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register entries for the Mixology Showdown tournament. It gathers information from participants to help us better manage the competition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the form title and your first name, last name.
2. Provide your team name and whether you have team members participating.
3. Enter your contact email address and phone number for us to get in touch.
4. Answer questions about your team experience and mixology skills.
5. Enter the name of your cocktail, and any other relevant details such as category, recipe, and photo.
6. Provide a brief description of your entry, including ingredients, instructions, and glassware used.
7. Optionally, select the assigned tools, judge, location, shift, and organizer for your entry.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Mixology Showdown Tournament Entry Form** (`form_title`): Enter a descriptive title for your entry, e.g., "Tropical Oasis".
* **First Name** (`first_name`): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`): Enter your last name.
* **Team Name** (`team_name`): Enter your team name (if applicable).
* **Team Members** (`team_members`): Indicate if you have any team members participating.
* **Contact Email** (`contact_email`): Enter a valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter a phone number where we can contact you.
* **Team Experience** (`team_experience`): Answer whether your team has experience in mixology.
* **Mixology Skills** (`mixology_skills`): Select the mixology skills you have, e.g., "Yes" for "Yes".
* **Cocktail Name** (`cocktail_name`): Enter the name of your cocktail.
* **Category** (`category`): Enter the category of your cocktail, e.g., "Cocktail", "Mocktail", etc.
* **Recipe** (`recipe`): Enter the recipe for your cocktail.
* **Photo** (`photo`): Upload a photo of your cocktail.
* **Entry Date** (`entry_date`): Enter the date you're submitting your entry.
* **Entry Time** (`entry_time`): Enter the time you're submitting your entry.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`): Select the assigned tool for your entry (if applicable).
* **Assigned Judge** (`assigned_judge`): Select the judge assigned to your entry (if applicable).
* **Assigned Location** (`assigned_location`): Select the location assigned to your entry (if applicable).
* **Assigned Shift** (`assigned_shift`): Select the shift assigned to your entry (if applicable).
* **Assigned Organizer** (`assigned_organizer`): Select the organizer assigned to your entry (if applicable).
* **Comments** (`comments`): Add any comments or notes about your entry.
* **Garnish** (`garnish`): Enter the garnish used for your cocktail.
* **Glassware** (`glassware`): Enter the glassware used for your cocktail.
* **Ingredients** (`ingredients`): Enter the ingredients used for your cocktail.
* **Instructions** (`instructions`): Enter the steps to make your cocktail.

Note: The fields with "Yes" and "No" options are for selection purposes only, do not be concerned with the actual "yes" or "no" value.
