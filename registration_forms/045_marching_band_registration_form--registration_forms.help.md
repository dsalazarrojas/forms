# Marching Band Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Marching Band Registration Form is used to collect information about students who want to join the marching band. It is essential to provide accurate details in the form to ensure a smooth registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Complete each field with accurate and up-to-date information.
2.  Ensure that all required fields (marked as required) are filled out.
3.  Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Student ID** (`student_id`, Number, Not Required): Enter the student's ID number.
*   **Student Name** (`student_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the student's name.
*   **Parent Name** (`parent_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the parent or guardian.
*   **Contact E-mail** (`contact_email`, Email, Not Required): Enter the contact email for the student.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Not Required): Enter the student's phone number.
*   **Student Grade** (`student_grade`, Select One, Not Required): Select the student's grade from 1-8.
*   **Instrument** (`instrument`, Select Multiple, Not Required): Choose the instrument the student will play in the band. Options include Clarinet, Euphonium, Flute, Saxophone, Trombone, Trumpet, Tuba, Violin, French Horn, Percussion, Mellophone, Sousaphone, and Other.
*   **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, Text, Not Required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, Text, Not Required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
*   **Emergency Contact Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, Text, Not Required): Explain the relationship between the student and the emergency contact.
*   **Student Address** (`student_address`, Text, Not Required): Enter the student's address. 

Please ensure to fill out the form carefully and accurately to avoid any issues during the registration process.
