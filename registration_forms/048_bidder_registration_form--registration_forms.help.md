# Bidder Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Bidder Registration Form is used to collect information from companies or individuals interested in bidding on projects. This form captures personal and business details, as well as financial and other relevant information required for the bidding process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Ensure that you are authorized to register your company.
3. Review and understand each field before providing information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your personal first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your personal last name.
* **Personal Email Address** (`email_personal`, email, required): Enter your personal email address for contact purposes.
* **Personal Phone Number** (`phone_personal`, text, required): Enter your personal phone number.
* **Company Legal Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter the official registered name of your company.
* **Type of Business Entity** (`company_type`, select_one, required): Select the type of business entity you are registering (Sole Proprietorship, Partnership, Limited Liability Company, Corporation, Non-Profit Organization, Government Entity, or Other).
* **Business Registration or License Number** (`registration_number`, text, required): Enter your company's official registration ID or license number.
* **Federal Tax Identification Number (EIN)** (`tax_id`, text, required): Enter your company's Federal Tax Identification Number (EIN) or equivalent.
* **Years in Business** (`years_operating`, number, required): Enter the number of years your company has been in operation.
* **Business Address** (`business_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address of your company.
* **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city of your company's location.
* **State or Province** (`state_province`, text, required): Enter the state or province of your company's location.
* **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, text, required): Enter the postal code of your company's location.
* **Country** (`country`, text, required): Enter the country of your company's location.
* **Business Email Address** (`email_business`, email, required): Enter the official company email address.
* **Business Phone Number** (`phone_business`, text, required): Enter the main business phone number.
* **Company Website** (`website`, text, optional): Enter the URL of your company website if applicable.
* **Authorized Officer or Representative Name** (`officer_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the person authorized to bid on behalf of your company.
* **Authorized Officer or Representative Title** (`officer_title`, text, required): Enter the title or position of the authorized officer or representative.
* **Authorized Officer or Representative Email Address** (`officer_email`, email, required): Enter the direct contact email of the authorized officer or representative.
* **Authorized Officer or Representative Phone Number** (`officer_phone`, text, required): Enter the direct contact phone number of the authorized officer or representative.
* **Primary Bidding Category** (`bidding_category`, select_one, required): Select the main area of your business (Construction, Manufacturing, Consulting, Services, Supplies, Technology, Professional Services, or Other).
* **Specific Areas of Expertise** (`specific_expertise`, text, required): List your company's key competencies or areas of expertise.
* **Professional Certifications or Credentials** (`certifications`, text, optional): Enter any relevant professional certifications or credentials held by your company.
* **Annual Revenue** (`annual_revenue`, number, required): Enter the last fiscal year revenue of your company.
* **Number of Employees** (`number_employees`, number, optional): Enter the total headcount of your company.
* **I Accept the Terms and Conditions** (`terms_conditions_acceptance`, select_one, required): Select whether you accept or do not accept the terms and conditions.
* **I Certify That I Am Authorized to Sign for This Company** (`authorization_statement`, select_one, required): Select whether you certify or do not certify that you are authorized to sign on behalf of your company.
* **Additional Information** (`additional_information`, text, optional): Enter any other relevant details about your company.
