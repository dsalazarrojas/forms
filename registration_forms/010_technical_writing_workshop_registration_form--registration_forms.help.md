# Technical Writing Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect participant information and workshop details for the Technical Writing Workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Participant Information section with your personal details.
2. Provide the necessary information about the workshop you are interested in attending.
3. Optionally, enter the pricing details if you want to book a workshop slot.
4. Select your workshop preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant personal details.
* **Workshop Information** (`workshop_info`, text, required): Describe the workshop you are interested in attending, including its title, date, and duration.
* **Workshop Pricing** (`workshop_pricing`, text, optional): If you want to book a workshop slot, enter your preferred pricing details.
* **Workshop Preferences** (`workshop_preferences`, text, optional): Select your workshop preferences, such as your favorite topics or interests.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields marked as `required` to ensure a successful submission.
- If you're unsure about any field, please reach out to us at form@gic.mx for assistance.
- This form is for registration purposes only.
