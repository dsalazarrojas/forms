# Natural Ingredient Research Network Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from researchers interested in joining the Natural Ingredient Research Network. The information collected will be used to understand your research interests, focus areas, and other relevant details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your **Registration Information** on page 1, which includes your name, research group, and email address.
2. On page 2, select your **Research Interests** from the options provided.
3. On page 3, select your **Research Focus** areas from the options provided.
4. On page 4, select your **Research Area** from the options provided.
5. Enter your **Research Location** on page 5.
6. Provide a brief **Research Description** on page 6.
7. List your natural **Ingredients** on page 7.
8. On page 8, select your submission status for the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
9. On page 9, select whether you have previously registered with the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
10. On page 10, select whether you have access to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
11. On page 11, select whether you have access to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
12. On page 12, select whether you have access to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
13. On page 13, enter your **Time** of registration.
14. On page 14, enter any additional **Note** about your registration.
15. On page 15, upload any relevant **File**.
16. On page 16, select whether you will **Submit** to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information (1)**: Enter your name, research group, and email address.
* **Research Interests (2)**: Select your areas of interest from the provided options.
* **Research Focus (3)**: Select your focus areas from the provided options.
* **Research Area (4)**: Select your research area from the provided options.
* **Research Location (5)**: Enter your research location.
* **Research Description (6)**: Provide a brief description of your research.
* **Natural Ingredients (7)**: List the natural ingredients you are working with.
* **Submission (8)**: Select whether you are submitting to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
* **Natural Ingredient Research Network Registration (9)**: Select whether you have previously registered with the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
* **Access to Natural Ingredient Research Network (10)**: Select whether you have access to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
* **Access to Natural Ingredient Research Network (11)**: Select whether you have access to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
* **Access to Natural Ingredient Research Network (12)**: Select whether you have access to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
* **Time (13)**: Enter your registration time.
* **Note (14)**: Enter any additional notes about your registration.
* **File (15)**: Upload any relevant files.
* **Submit (16)**: Select whether you will submit to the Natural Ingredient Research Network.
