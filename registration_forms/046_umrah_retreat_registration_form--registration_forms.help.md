# Umrah Retreat Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Umrah Retreat Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants for a smooth and safe experience during the retreat. This guide will walk you through the form's sections and help you understand the required and optional fields.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Start by filling in your **Participant Information** section, providing your personal details.
2. Move on to the **Contact Information** section, where you'll provide contact details for yourself and your emergency contact.
3. In the **Participant Details** section, ensure you're honest about your medical conditions, allergies, and dietary restrictions.
4. Choose your **Participant Type** to help us better understand your requirements.
5. Enter your **Dates** of travel for both arrival and departure.
6. Specify your **Check-in Time** and **Departure Time** for easy planning.
7. Provide your **Accommodation Details**, including a valid address and type (resort, hotel, etc.).
8. Confirm your **Accommodation Email** and **Phone** for communication.
9. Write any **Accommodation Message** if you need to provide additional information.
10. If you have an **Emergency Contact**, list all group members and provide their names, phones, and relationship with you.
11. Declare any **Medical Conditions**, **Allergies**, and **Medications** that might affect your participation.
12. Choose your **Dietary Restrictions** to help us prepare meals.
13. Write down any **Special Requests** you have for a more comfortable experience.
14. Finally, sign the **Participant Signature** and **Participant Agreement** sections, confirming your understanding of the retreat's rules.
