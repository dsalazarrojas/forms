# Financial Strategy Analysis Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

The purpose of this form is to collect attendee registration information for the Financial Strategy Analysis Training program. This form is used to gather essential details such as name, contact information, and session preferences to ensure attendees can attend the training session that best suits their schedule.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field.
3. Provide your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. Choose your preferred session time from the "Session preferences" field.
6. If you have any special requirements or requests, please provide them in the "Special requirements" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First name**: Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Last name**: Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents (e.g., driver's license, passport).
* **Email**: Enter your valid email address where you can be reached for confirmation and updates. This will be used for communication with the training organizers.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number where you can be reached during the session (e.g., for any last-minute instructions).
* **Session preferences**: Choose your preferred session time:
	+ **Morning**: Session takes place in the morning (typically 9:00 AM - 12:00 PM).
	+ **Afternoon**: Session takes place in the afternoon (typically 1:00 PM - 4:00 PM).
	+ **Full day**: Session takes place from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
* **Special requirements**: If you have any specific requirements or requests (e.g., wheelchair accessibility, language support), please let us know in this field.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper communication and session planning.
* If you are unable to attend your preferred session, please let us know as soon as possible to avoid conflicts.
