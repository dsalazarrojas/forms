# Veterinary Pharmaceutical Demo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects information from participants interested in veterinary pharmaceutical demos. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the form completely and honestly.
2. Enter your name and contact information.
3. Select your area of interest within the veterinary pharmaceuticals industry.
4. If you have multiple phone numbers, enter each one under the "first_phone" and "last_phone" fields.
5. Choose a date and time for the demo, if applicable.
6. Provide any additional notes about your participation or interest in the demo.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your official email address associated with your work or professional account.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your main contact phone number.
* **Area of Interest** (`veterinary_pharmaceutical_demo_interest_area`, select_one, required): Select the area within veterinary pharmaceuticals that interests you the most.
* **First Phone** (`first_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your secondary phone number, if you have one.
* **Last Phone** (`last_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your third phone number, if you have more than one.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would be available to attend the demo, if applicable.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would be available to attend the demo, if applicable.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required/optional): Provide any additional notes about your participation or interest in the demo.
* **Confirm Phone** (`confirm_phone`, text, required/optional): Confirm your secondary phone number, if applicable.
