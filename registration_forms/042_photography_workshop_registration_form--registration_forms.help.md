# photography_workshop_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for a photography workshop.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Workshop Details** page with the basic workshop information.
2. Move on to the **Participant Details** page, where you will provide personal details and additional notes.
3. In the **Payment and Status** section, select the payment method and provide payment details.
4. Review and confirm your participant status and payment terms.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **Workshop Details (id: 1)**
  • **Workshop Details**: Enter the basic information about the workshop, such as title or description.

- **Participant Details (id: 2)**
  • **Participant Name**: Enter your name as it would be on your identification.
  • **Participant Email**: Provide your active email address.
  • **Participant Phone**: Enter your phone number.
  • **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes about your participation.

- **Payment and Status (id: 6)**
  • **Payment Method**: Select how you would like to pay for the workshop (cash, credit card, paypal, stripe).
  • **Payment Amount**: Enter the amount you plan to pay.
  • **Payment Date**: Enter the date you plan to make the payment.
  • **Payment Status**: Review your payment status.

- **Participant Status (id: 13)**
  • **Participant Status**: Confirm your status as a workshop participant (confirmed, pending, cancelled).

- **Additional Information (id: 14)**
  • **Additional Info**: Add any additional information about your participation or requirements.
  • **Additional Notes**: Add any further comments or notes about your registration.

- **Assigned Tool (id: 16)**
  • **Assigned Tool**: Select the tool or equipment you are bringing or plan to use (chatjimmy, Other).

- **Participant Signature (id: 17)**
  • **Participant Signature**: Add your digital signature to confirm your registration.

- **Participant Photo Experience (id: 18)**
  • **Participant Photo Experience**: Enter any relevant photo experience or background information.

- **Payment Terms (id: 19)**
  • **Payment Terms**: Review and agree to the payment terms.

- **Participant Terms (id: 20)**
  • **Participant Terms**: Review and agree to the participant terms.

- **Payment Terms Status (id: 21)**
  • **Payment Terms Status**: Confirm your payment terms status (accepted, denied, pending).

- **Participant Terms Status (id: 22)**
  • **Participant Terms Status**: Confirm your participant terms status (accepted, denied, pending).

- **Participant Agreement (id: 23)**
  • **Participant Agreement**: Review and agree to the participant agreement.

- **Photo Workshop Terms (id: 24)**
  • **Photo Workshop Terms**: Review and agree to the photo workshop terms.

- **Photo Workshop Conditions (id: 25)**
  • **Photo Workshop Conditions**: Review and agree to the photo workshop conditions.
