## Final Step Confirmation - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Final Step Confirmation form is designed to verify the accuracy of transaction details and confirm whether a user has completed the final step in a transaction process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the transaction details presented in the form to ensure accuracy.
2. Choose "Yes" or "No" for the Confirm Transaction Details and Confirm Event Details fields, indicating your confirmation status for each.
3. Click the "Submit" button to confirm your final step and complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Confirm Transaction Details** (Confirm Transaction Details, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm that the transaction details are accurate, or "No" to indicate any discrepancies.
* **Confirm Event Details** (Confirm Event Details, Select Multiple, Optional):
	+ Select "Yes" to confirm that the event details are accurate, or "No" to indicate any discrepancies.

## Tips
Please carefully review the transaction details to ensure accuracy before proceeding.
Only confirm transaction and event details if you have thoroughly reviewed them.
If you have any doubts or concerns, contact your administrator or support team for assistance.
