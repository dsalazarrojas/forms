# Kids Monopoly Game Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register children to play the Kids Monopoly Game, a fun and educational activity designed to teach kids about financial literacy.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Ensure you have all the necessary information with you.
* Fill in your child's personal details, including their first name, last name, and email address.
* Provide your child's phone number for contact purposes.
* Fill in your guardian's personal details, including their first name, last name, and email address.
* Provide your guardian's phone number for contact purposes.
* Select the preferred game session from the list of available sessions.
* Agree to the consent term by selecting "True".

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your child's first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your child's last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your child's email address. Please use a valid email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your child's phone number. Please use the international format with a "+" symbol, for example, "+1-123-456-7890".
* **Guardian First Name** (`guardian_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your guardian's first name.
* **Guardian Last Name** (`guardian_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your guardian's last name.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your guardian's email address. Please use a valid email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your guardian's phone number. Please use the international format with a "+" symbol, for example, "+1-123-456-7890".
* **Preferred Game Session** (`session`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or multiple game sessions from the list. Please choose the sessions you are interested in.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Agree to the terms of the consent form by selecting "True". 

## Tips
* Please ensure that you have filled in all the required fields before submitting the form.
* Make sure to use valid email addresses and phone numbers.
* You can select one or multiple game sessions, depending on your preference.
* If you are having trouble selecting a game session, please contact us for assistance.
* By submitting this form, you are agreeing to the terms and conditions of the game.
