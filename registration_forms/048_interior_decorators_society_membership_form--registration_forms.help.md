# Interior Decorators Society Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who want to register as members of the Interior Decorators Society. It collects personal details, payment information, and confirmatory sections to process your membership application.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Provide your personal details, such as your full name, email, and contact number.
2.  Enter your address and zip code.
3.  Choose your membership type (individual or organization).
4.  Select your payment method (online or offline payment).
5.  Enter your card details (card number, expiration date, and CVV for online payments).
6.  Confirm your membership type and payment details.
7.  Confirm your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **User Details**: Enter your username.
*   **Full Name**: Enter your full name as it appears on your official documents.
*   **Email**: Enter a valid email address for communication with the society.
*   **Contact Number**: Enter your contact number for communication.
*   **Address**: Enter your physical address.
*   **Street**: Enter the street address.
*   **City**: Enter the city where you live.
*   **Zip Code**: Enter your zip code for postal communication.
*   **Membership Type**: Select "Individual" for personal membership or "Organization" for business membership.
*   **Payment Details**: Enter any additional details about your payment method.
*   **Payment Method**: Select "Online Payment" for online payment or "Offline Payment" for payment through check or bank transfer.
*   **Card Details**: Enter your card details (card number, expiration date, and CVV) for online payments.
*   **Card Number**: Enter your card number.
*   **Expiration Date**: Enter your card's expiration date (MM/YY).
*   **CVV**: Enter your card's CVV (Card Verification Value).
*   **CVV Confirmation**: Confirm your CVV to prevent errors.
*   **Signature**: Enter your physical signature as a proof of your identity.
*   **Note**: Enter any additional comments or notes about your membership.
*   **Confirm Membership**: Confirm that you have read and agree with the membership terms.
*   **Confirm Submission**: Confirm that you have completed the form correctly and agree to the terms.

## Tips

*   Please ensure that all fields are filled correctly to avoid delays in processing your membership application.
*   For online payments, ensure that your card details are accurate to avoid failed transactions.
*   If you have any issues with filling the form, contact the society's support team for assistance.
