# Multi Email Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering multiple email addresses for an event or training. It's used to collect contact information, event details, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "last_name" field.
3. Enter your email addresses in the "email", "email_2", "email_3" fields. You can enter up to four email addresses.
4. Provide event details in the "event_details" field.
5. Enter the date of the event in the "event_date" field.
6. Enter the time of the event in the "event_time" field.
7. Enter the location of the event in the "event_location" field.
8. Select the team you are registered to in the "team" field.
9. Select the training sessions you are registered for in the "training" field.
10. Add any additional notes in the "notes" field.
11. Select the person assigned to you in the "assigned_to" field.
12. Enter your contact number in the "contact_number" field.
13. Enter your contact email in the "contact_email" field.
14. Select the follow-up activities you require in the "follow_up" field.
15. Select the follow-up activities you require in the "follow_up_2" field.
16. Select the workflow you belong to in the "workflow" field.
17. Select the event you are registered to in the "event_id" field.
18. Select the member you are registered as in the "member_id" field.
19. Select the team you are registered to in the "team_id" field.
20. Select the tool assigned to you in the "assigned_tool" field.
21. Select the follow-up activities you require in the "follow_up_3" field.
22. Add any additional notes in the "notes_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **first_name** (Text): Enter your first name.
- **last_name** (Text): Enter your last name.
- **email** (Email): Enter up to four email addresses associated with your registration.
- **email_2**, **email_3**, **email_4** (Email): Enter additional email addresses associated with your registration.
- **event_details** (Text): Enter any additional details about the event.
- **event_date** (Date): Enter the date of the event.
- **event_time** (Time): Enter the time of the event.
- **event_location** (Text): Enter the location of the event.
- **team** (Select One): Select the team you are registered to.
- **training** (Select Multiple): Select the training sessions you are registered for.
- **notes** (Note): Add any additional notes about your registration.
- **assigned_to** (Select One): Select the person assigned to you.
- **contact_number** (Text): Enter your contact number.
- **contact_email** (Email): Enter your contact email.
- **follow_up** (Select Multiple): Select the follow-up activities you require.
- **follow_up_2** (Select Multiple): Select additional follow-up activities you require.
- **workflow** (Select Multiple): Select the workflow you belong to.
- **event_id** (Select One): Select the event you are registered to.
- **member_id** (Select One): Select the member you are registered as.
- **team_id** (Select One): Select the team you are registered to.
- **assigned_tool** (Select One): Select the tool assigned to you.
- **follow_up_3** (Select Multiple): Select additional follow-up activities you require.
- **notes_2** (Note): Add any additional notes about your registration.

## Tips
- Ensure that you fill out all required fields accurately and completely.
- Enter up to four email addresses associated with your registration.
- Double-check your selections in the "team", "training", "follow_up", "follow_up_2", "workflow", "event_id", "member_id", "team_id", "assigned_tool", and "follow_up_3" fields to ensure that you are registered correctly.
