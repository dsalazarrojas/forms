# Mother Daughter Retreat Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for mothers and daughters to register for a mother-daughter retreat. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure we have all necessary details to accommodate your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
- Fill out each field carefully and accurately.
- Enter the required fields (indicated as required).
- Review your responses for spelling and formatting errors before submitting.
- If a field does not apply to you, leave it blank or mark it as "not applicable" (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Mother Daughter Event** (`mother_daughter_event`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of the mother-daughter event or activity you would like to participate in or suggest for others.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Mother First Name** (`mother_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your mother's first name.
* **Mother Last Name** (`mother_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your mother's last name.
* **Daughter First Name** (`daughter_first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your daughter's first name.
* **Daughter Last Name** (`daughter_last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your daughter's last name.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you would like to participate in the retreat.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required/optional): Enter the time you would like to participate in the retreat.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, required/optional): If you have any special needs or requirements, please describe them here.
* **Consent** (`consent`, select_one, required/optional): Check "True" if you give your consent for us to contact you and "False" otherwise.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required/optional): Enter the number of guests you would like to bring with you.

## Tips
- Be sure to proofread your responses before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us.
