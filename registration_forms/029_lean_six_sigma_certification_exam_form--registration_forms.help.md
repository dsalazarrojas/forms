# lean_six_sigma_certification_exam_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed for individuals to register for and provide information about their Lean Six Sigma Certification Exam. It is used to gather data for internal tracking and certification purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details such as Name, Surname, Email, and Phone.
2. Provide your Date of Birth.
3. Select the Exam Date, Exam Time, and Exam Location.
4. Choose your Certification Level.
5. Indicate your Submission Status (Yes, No, or Other).
6. Choose the Submission Date and Submission Time.
7. Leave comments if necessary.
8. Confirm your Submission (Yes, No, or Other).
9. Select your Submission Status 2 (Yes, No, or Other).
10. Agree or disagree with certain terms.
11. Choose the Agreement Date and Agreement Time.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (1, text, required): Fill in your full name.
* **Surname** (2, text, required): Fill in your surname.
* **Email** (3, email, required): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required): Fill in your phone number.
* **Date of Birth** (5, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Exam Date** (6, date, required): Select the date of your certification exam.
* **Exam Time** (7, time, required): Select the time of your certification exam.
* **Exam Location** (8, text, required): Fill in the location of your certification exam.
* **Certification Level** (9, text, required): Choose your certification level.
* **Submission Status** (10, select_multiple, required): Select Yes, No, or Other to indicate your submission status.
* **Submission Date** (11, date, required): Enter the date of your submission.
* **Submission Time** (12, time, required): Select the time of your submission.
* **Comments** (13, note, required): Leave any additional comments about your submission.
* **Submission Confirmation** (14, select_one, required): Confirm your submission by selecting Yes, No, or Other.
* **Submission Status 2** (15, select_multiple, required): Select Yes, No, or Other to indicate your submission status 2.
* **Agreement** (16, select_one, required): Agree or disagree with certain terms.
* **Agreement Date** (17, date, required): Enter the date of your agreement.
* **Agreement Time** (18, time, required): Select the time of your agreement.
* **Agreement 2** (19, select_one, required): Agree or disagree with certain terms 2.
* **Agreement Date 2** (20, date, required): Enter the date of your agreement 2.
* **Agreement Time 2** (21, time, required): Select the time of your agreement 2.
