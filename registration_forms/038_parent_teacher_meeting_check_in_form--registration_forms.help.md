# Parent-Teacher Meeting Check-in Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The parent-teacher meeting check-in form is used to collect information from parents and teachers about the meeting details and attendance of students.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please provide the following information:
   * Check in information (Page 1)
   * Parent's Name
   * Parent's Email
   * Parent’s Phone
   * Student’s Name
   * Grade Level
   * Meeting Date
   * Meeting Time
   * Meeting Location
   * Student Attendance

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Check In Information** (Page 1): A brief description of the purpose of the meeting check-in.
* **Parent's Name** (Required): Your name as it appears on your child's report card.
	+ Enter your name as it appears on your child's report card.
* **Parent's Email** (Required): Enter your email address.
	+ Enter your email address for communication.
* **Parent's Phone** (Required): Enter your phone number.
	+ Enter your phone number for easy contact.
* **Student's Name** (Required): Enter your child's name.
	+ Enter your child's name accurately.
* **Grade Level** (Required): Select the grade level of your child from the options provided (K-5, 6-8, 9-12).
	+ Choose your child's current grade level.
* **Meeting Date** (Required): Select the date of the meeting.
	+ Choose the date of the meeting accurately.
* **Meeting Time** (Required): Select the time of the meeting.
	+ Choose the time of the meeting precisely.
* **Meeting Location** (Required): Enter the location of the meeting.
	+ Enter the location of the meeting accurately.
* **Student Attendance** (Required): Select whether your child will be attending the meeting (Yes, No).
	+ Choose whether your child will attend the meeting.
