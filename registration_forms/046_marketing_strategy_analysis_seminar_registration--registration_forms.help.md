# Marketing Strategy Analysis Seminar Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration to the Marketing Strategy Analysis Seminar. It collects personal and professional information from potential attendees to facilitate a smooth and relevant experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out each field with your personal and professional details.
2. Select the relevant options for event type, segment, and role.
3. Confirm your email and phone number to ensure accurate contact information.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First name (Required)** (`first_name`, text): Enter your first name. 
- **Last name (Required)** (`last_name`, text): Enter your last name.
- **Email (Required)** (`email`, email): Enter a valid email address.
- **Phone (Optional)** (`phone`, text): Enter your phone number if you wish to be contacted.
- **Job title (Required)** (`job_title`, text): Enter your job title or position.
- **Company (Required)** (`company`, text): Enter the name of your company.
- **Department (Optional)** (`department`, text): Enter the name of your department if applicable.
- **Role (Required)** (`role`, select_multiple): Select one or more areas where you are involved (Sales, Marketing, Product, Customer, Finance, Other).
- **Job description (Optional)** (`job_description`, text): Briefly describe your job or role.
- **Job function (Optional)** (`job_function`, text): Enter your job function or position.
- **Industry (Optional)** (`industry`, text): Enter the industry you work in.
- **Location (Optional)** (`location`, text): Enter your location or office address.
- **Time (Optional)** (`time`, time): Enter the time zone you are in.
- **Date (Optional)** (`date`, date): Enter the date you are available.
- **Event type (Required)** (`event_type`, select_one): Select the type of event you are interested in (Webinar, Seminar, Conference, Workshop, Other).
- **Event date (Required)** (`event_date`, date): Enter the date of the event you are interested in.
- **Event time (Required)** (`event_time`, time): Enter the time of the event you are interested in.
- **Segment (Required)** (`segment`, select_multiple): Select one or more areas where you are interested (Sales, Marketing, Product, Customer, Finance, Other).
- **Follow-up (Required)** (`follow_up`, text): Briefly describe any follow-up information you would like to provide.
- **Agree terms (Required)** (`agree_terms`, text): Agree to the terms and conditions of the event.
- **Notes (Optional)** (`notes`, note): Add any additional notes or comments.
- **Confirm email (Required)** (`confirm_email`, email): Confirm your email address.
- **Confirm phone (Required)** (`confirm_phone`, text): Confirm your phone number.

## Tips
- Double-check your contact information for accuracy.
- Be specific when selecting the event type and segment.
- Provide a brief description for follow-up information.
