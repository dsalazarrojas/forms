# Trail Visitor Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Trail Visitor Check In Form is designed to collect visitor information for trail check-in purposes. Fill out this form to provide essential details about your visit.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill out all the required fields (marked as *required*) to ensure accurate and complete information.
2.  For fields with a dropdown menu, select the correct option by clicking on the menu and choosing the desired value.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Trailhead** (`trail_visitor_form2`): Select the trailhead you are visiting.
    *   This field is required to ensure accurate trailhead information.
*   **Park** (`trail_visitor_form3`): Select all applicable parks you will be visiting.
    *   This field is required to ensure accurate park information.
*   **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`): Enter your estimated arrival time.
    *   This field is required to schedule your check-in and ensure trail staff are prepared.
*   **Trail Visitor Form 4** (`trail_visitor_form4`): This field is not applicable and may be skipped.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields before submitting the form to avoid errors.
*   If you are unsure about any field, ask a trail staff member for guidance.
*   This form is intended to be a quick and convenient way to provide visitor information; however, if you have any special requests or comments, please include them in the notes field (which is not displayed in this help guide).
