# Batch Date Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for a training program and provide essential information for the training administration to prepare for your needs.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your participant information:
	* Enter your full name in the "Full Name" field.
	* Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field.
	* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
2. Select the batch date for the training:
	* Choose the start date of your training batch from the "Available Batch Dates" field.
3. Select the registration type:
	* Choose the type of registration that suits your needs from the "Registration Type" field.
4. Provide any necessary background information:
	* If necessary, provide a brief description of your previous experience in the field.
5. Provide emergency contact information:
	* Enter the name of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
	* Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.
6. Confirm your payment method:
	* Select your preferred payment method from the "Preferred Payment Method" field.
7. Agree to the registration terms:
	* Confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the training by selecting "I Agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (`participant_full_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your full name.
* **Email Address** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): This field is used to collect your email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect your phone number.
* **Available Batch Dates** (`selected_batch_date`, `select_one`, required): This field allows you to select the start date of your training batch.
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select the type of registration that suits your needs.
* **Briefly describe your previous experience in this field** (`background_info`, `text`, optional): This field is used to provide any necessary background information about your previous experience.
* **Dietary Requirements** (`dietary_requirements`, `text`, optional): This field is used to collect any dietary requirements you may have for the training.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (`emergency_contact_name`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, `text`, required): This field is used to collect the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Preferred Payment Method** (`payment_mode`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to select your preferred payment method.
* **I agree to the registration terms and cancellation policy** (`agreement_terms`, `select_one`, required): This field is used to confirm your agreement to the terms and conditions of the training.
* **Date of Registration** (`submission_date`, `date`, required): This field is used to collect the date of registration.

## Tips
- Double-check your information before submitting the form to ensure accuracy.
- Review the available batch dates carefully to select the correct date for your training.
- Make sure to select your preferred payment method carefully to avoid any issues with payment confirmation.
