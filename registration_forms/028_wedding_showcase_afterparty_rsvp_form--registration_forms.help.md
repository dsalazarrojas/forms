# Wedding Showcase Afterparty RSVP Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Wedding Showcase Afterparty RSVP Form is used to let guests know whether they will be attending the event and why. This information is collected to facilitate communication and help with event organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the field marked "First Name".
2. Enter your last name in the field marked "Last Name".
3. Enter your email address in the field marked "Email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field marked "Phone".
5. Select whether you will be attending the event by choosing from the options "Will Attend", "Will Not Attend", or "Maybe" in the field marked "Will Attend".
6. If you're not attending, or if you're unsure, you can provide a reason for your response in the field marked "Reason".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address to receive updates and communications about the event.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Will Attend** (`attending`, select_one, required): Choose whether you will be attending the event, or select "Maybe" if your response is uncertain.
* **Reason** (`reason`, text, optional): If you won't be attending, or if you're unsure, provide a brief explanation for your response.
