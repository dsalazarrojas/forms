# Lightsaber Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to help attendees register for the Lightsaber Workshop. The form collects essential information from attendees, including contact details, session preferences, and availability.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your registration information, including your email address and other required details.
2. Select your preferred phone numbers from the list provided.
3. Choose one session that suits your schedule.
4. If you have specific availability, enter it in the format of a time.
5. Add any additional comments or questions in the notes field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information (Required)**: This is the title of the form.
* **Email (Required)**: Enter your email address in the standard format (e.g., `john.doe@example.com`).
* **Phone (Required)**: Select one or more phone numbers that you can be contacted on.
* **Session (Required)**: Choose one session from the provided options.
* **Availability (Optional)**: Enter a specific time if you have special requests or constraints.
* **Notes (Optional)**: Add any additional comments or questions you have about the workshop.
