# Dance Club Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information for new or existing members of the dance club.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields (indicated by an asterisk \*).
2. For fields with a drop-down menu (e.g. dance style, membership length), select the corresponding option.
3. For fields with a date type, enter the date in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 2022-01-01).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name \* (Text)
* Enter your first name.

### Last Name \* (Text)
* Enter your last name.

### Email (Text)
* Enter your email address.

### Phone (Text)
* Enter your phone number.

### Birthday (Text)
* Enter your birthday in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 2022-01-01).

### Dance Style \* (Select One)
* Select the type of dance style you are interested in (e.g. Ballet, Contemporary, etc.).

### Membership Length \* (Select Multiple)
* Select the length of your membership (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).

### Note (Optional Note)
* Enter any additional information you would like to share with the dance club.

### Parent's First Name \* (Text)
* Enter the first name of the parent or guardian.

### Parent's Last Name \* (Text)
* Enter the last name of the parent or guardian.

### Parent's Phone (Text)
* Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.

### Parent's Birthday (Date)
* Enter the birthday of the parent or guardian in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 2022-01-01).

### Parent's Dance Style \* (Select One)
* Select the type of dance style the parent or guardian is interested in (e.g. Ballet, Contemporary, etc.).

### Parent's Membership Length \* (Select Multiple)
* Select the length of the parent or guardian's membership (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).

### Emergency Contact Name \* (Text)
* Enter the name of the emergency contact.

### Relationship (Text)
* Enter the relationship between the emergency contact and the member (e.g. Parent, Guardian, etc.).

### Emergency Contact Phone (Text)
* Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.

### Emergency Contact Birthday (Date)
* Enter the birthday of the emergency contact in the YYYY-MM-DD format (e.g. 2022-01-01).

### Emergency Contact Dance Style \* (Select One)
* Select the type of dance style the emergency contact is interested in (e.g. Ballet, Contemporary, etc.).

### Emergency Contact Membership Length \* (Select Multiple)
* Select the length of the emergency contact's membership (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).

### Emergency Contact Note (Optional Note)
* Enter any additional information about the emergency contact.

### First Sibling's First Name (Text)
* Enter the first name of the first sibling.

### Last Sibling's Last Name (Text)
* Enter the last name of the first sibling.

### Sibling's Phone (Text)
* Enter the phone number of the first sibling.

### Sibling's Birthday (Optional Date)
* Enter the birthday of the first sibling, if applicable.

### Sibling's Membership Length \* (Select Multiple)
* Select the length of the first sibling's membership (e.g. Monthly, Quarterly, Annually).

### Emergency Contact Permission \* (Select One)
* Select whether to grant emergency contact permission (True or False).

Please note that the emergency contact section is meant to be filled out by the emergency contact themselves, not the member.
