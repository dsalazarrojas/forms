# Kids Summer Camp Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and contact information from parents or guardians of children attending a summer camp.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the Personal Information page with your child's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
2. Provide your Parent/Parent Info, including your name, contact details, and any relevant medical information.
3. Enter your Contact Information, including your address, phone number, and email address.
4. Share your Child's History, such as their educational background, allergies, or any specific needs.
5. Enter any Medical Information, including any health conditions or medications your child is taking.
6. Sign the form to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the camp's terms and conditions.
7. Select the type of consent you are providing, either as a Parent, Guardian, or Other.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Personal Information** (`Personal Information`, `text`, required: `false`): Please fill out this page with your child's name, date of birth, and any other relevant details.
* **Parent Info** (`Parent Info`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter your name as the parent or guardian responsible for your child's care. This includes your contact details, such as phone number and email address.
* **Contact Information** (`Contact Information`, `text`, required: `false`): Provide your address, phone number, and email address so we can get in touch with you.
* **Child's History** (`Child's History`, `text`, required: `false`): Share your child's educational background, allergies, or any specific needs they may have.
* **Medical Information** (`Medical Information`, `text`, required: `false`): Enter any health conditions or medications your child is taking.
* **Signature** (`Signature`, `text`, required: `false`): Please sign this form to acknowledge that you have read and agreed to the camp's terms and conditions.
* **Consent** (`Consent`, `select_multiple`, required: `false`): Select the type of consent you are providing, either as a Parent, Guardian, or Other.
