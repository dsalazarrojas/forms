## Endangered Species Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Endangered Species Registration Form is used to collect data for endangered species registration. It gathers information on the conservation status, species attributes, and habitat details of the species in question.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that the data is correct and up-to-date.
2. Select the conservation status of the species from the dropdown menu.
3. Enter the name of the species in the "species name" field.
4. Provide a description of the species in the "species description" field.
5. Upload an image of the species in the "species image" field.
6. Select the category of the species from the dropdown menu.
7. If applicable, provide information on the species' conservation plan.
8. Fill out the species' habitat details, including location, longitude, latitude, elevation, and alternative coordinates.
9. Answer the species' dietary and predator needs in the "species diet" and "species predators" fields.
10. Indicate if the species interacts with humans and provide any relevant information.
11. Provide the address and notes on the conservation society or organization.
12. Select the conservation status of the species from the dropdown menu again for verification.
13. Repeat the species name to verify.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Conservation Status** (`conservation_status`, select_one, required): Select the current conservation status of the species. This will help the conservation organization to track and monitor the species' status.
* **Species Name** (`species_name`, text, required): Enter the name of the species to be registered. This will help identify the species and track its records.
* **Species Description** (`species_description`, text, optional): Provide a brief description of the species to help understand its characteristics and traits.
* **Species Image** (`species_image`, text, required): Upload an image of the species to aid in identification and verification.
* **Species Category** (`species_category`, select_multiple, required): Select the category of the species to classify it correctly.
* **Conservation Action Plan** (`conservation_action_plan`, text, optional): If applicable, provide details on the conservation plan for the species.
* **Species Habitat** (`species_habitat`, text, optional): Fill out the species' habitat details, including location, longitude, latitude, elevation, and alternative coordinates.
* **Species Population** (`species_population`, number, required): Enter the current population of the species.
* **Species Diet** (`species_diet`, select_multiple, optional): Select the diet of the species to understand its feeding habits.
* **Species Predators** (`species_predators`, select_multiple, optional): Identify the species' predators to understand its threats.
* **Species Humans** (`species_humans`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if the species interacts with humans and provide any relevant information.
* **Conservation Society** (`conservation_society`, text, optional): Provide the address and notes on the conservation society or organization.
* **Conservation Organization** (`conservation_organization`, text, optional): Provide the address and notes on the conservation organization.
* **Conservation Status 2** (`conservation_status_2`, select_one, required): Select the conservation status of the species from the dropdown menu again for verification.
* **Species Name 2** (`species_name_2`, text, optional): Repeat the species name to verify.
* **Species Image 2** (`species_image_2`, text, optional): Repeat the species image to verify.
* **Species Category 2** (`species_category_2`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category of the species from the dropdown menu again for verification.
* **Conservation Status 3** (`conservation_status_3`, select_one, optional): Select the conservation status of the species from the dropdown menu for final verification.
* **Species Name 3** (`species_name_3`, text, optional): Repeat the species name to verify.
* **Species Image 3** (`species_image_3`, text, optional): Repeat the species image to verify.
* **Species Category 3** (`species_category_3`, select_multiple, optional): Select the category of the species from the dropdown menu again for verification.

Please note that some fields are marked as optional or have alternative options to cater to different species and conservation needs. Ensure that you fill out the form accurately and completely to help the conservation organization better manage and protect the species.
