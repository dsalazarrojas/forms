# Online Woodworking Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for online woodworking training sessions. Please fill out the form to provide the necessary information about your workshop participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name.
2. Enter your Last Name.
3. Provide your Email address.
4. Enter your Phone number.
5. Choose the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
6. Choose the time of the workshop you wish to attend.
7. Enter the workshop location.
8. Indicate if you are enrolling in the session.
9. Enter the name of the instructor.
10. Enter the phone number of the instructor.
11. Enter the title of the course.
12. Enter a description of the course.
13. Enter the duration of the course.
14. Enter the duration of each session.
15. Enter the enrollment limit for the workshop.
16. Check the current status of the training.
17. Select the type of training offered (yes/no).
18. Select the workshop topic.
19. Choose if the training is in the selected language (yes/no).
20. Enter the location of the training.
21. Enter the start date of the workshop.
22. Enter the end date of the workshop.
23. Enter the start time of each session.
24. Enter the end time of each session.
25. Add any additional notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (<name>: text, required): Enter your first name. This is a required field.
* **Last Name** (<name>: text, required): Enter your last name. This is a required field.
* **Email** (<name>: email, required): Enter your email address. This will be used to contact you.
* **Phone** (<name>: text, required): Enter your phone number. This will be used to contact you.
* **Workshop Date** (<name>: date, required): Choose the date of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Time** (<name>: time, required): Choose the time of the workshop you wish to attend.
* **Workshop Location** (<name>: text, required): Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Session Enrollment** (<name>: select_one, required): Indicate if you are enrolling in the session.
* **Instructor Name** (<name>: text, required): Enter the name of the instructor.
* **Instructor Phone** (<name>: text, required): Enter the phone number of the instructor.
* **Course Title** (<name>: text, required): Enter the title of the course.
* **Course Description** (<name>: text, required): Enter a description of the course.
* **Course Duration** (<name>: number, required): Enter the duration of the course.
* **Session Duration** (<name>: number, required): Enter the duration of each session.
* **Enrollment Limit** (<name>: number, required): Enter the enrollment limit for the workshop.
* **Training Status** (<name>: select_one, required): Check the current status of the training.
* **Training Type** (<name>: select_multiple, required): Select the type of training offered.
* **Workshop Topic** (<name>: text, required): Enter the workshop topic.
* **Training Language** (<name>: select_one, required): Choose if the training is in the selected language.
* **Training Location** (<name>: text, required): Enter the location of the training.
* **Workshop Start Date** (<name>: date, required): Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date** (<name>: date, required): Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Session Start Time** (<name>: time, required): Enter the start time of each session.
* **Session End Time** (<name>: time, required): Enter the end time of each session.
* **Notes** (<name>: note, required): Add any additional notes.
