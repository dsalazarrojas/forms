# Zoo Field Trip Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The form is a registration process for students to participate in the zoo field trip. It's a crucial step to gather essential information about each student to ensure a successful and safe experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide the student's date of birth (if applicable) in the "Student DOB" field.
3. Select the student's grade level from the "Student Grade" field.
4. Enter your first and last name in the "Contact First Name" and "Contact Last Name" fields.
5. Enter your email address in the "Contact Email" field.
6. Enter your phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
7. Indicate whether the student will attend the trip ("Yes" or "No") in the "Student Attendence" field.
8. Confirm whether you have received permission from the student for them to attend the trip ("Yes" or "No") in the "Student Permission" field.
9. Provide any additional information about the student in the "Additional Info" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **School Information**: Please enter your school name and any other relevant information related to your school.
  - This field is not required but helps us understand where the student is from.
- **Student Details**:
  - This field is not required but allows you to provide any additional information about the student.
- **Emergency Contacts**:
  - This field is not required but is useful for us to reach out to you in case of an emergency.
- **Student Permissions**:
  - This field is not required but is crucial for us to understand whether the student has given their permission to attend the trip.
- **Additional Info**:
  - This field is not required but is a good place to add any additional information you want to share with us.
- **Student Name** (`Student Name`): The student's name as it appears on their official documents.
  - Please enter the student's name accurately to ensure we can identify them correctly.
- **Student DOB** (`Student DOB`): The student's date of birth.
  - This field is required for age verification purposes.
- **Student Grade** (`Student Grade`): The student's grade level.
  - Please select the correct grade level for the student.
- **Contact First Name** (`Contact First Name`): Your first name.
  - Please enter your first name accurately to ensure we can contact you correctly.
- **Contact Last Name** (`Contact Last Name`): Your last name.
  - Please enter your last name accurately to ensure we can contact you correctly.
- **Contact Email** (`Contact Email`): Your email address.
  - Please enter a valid email address so we can contact you.
- **Contact Phone** (`Contact Phone`): Your phone number.
  - Please enter your phone number so we can contact you in case of an emergency.
- **Student Attendence** (`Student Attendence`): A yes/no field indicating whether the student will attend the trip.
  - Please select "Yes" if the student will attend the trip and "No" otherwise.
- **Student Permission** (`Student Permission`): A yes/no field indicating whether you have received permission from the student for them to attend the trip.
  - Please select "Yes" if you have received permission from the student and "No" otherwise.
- **Student Medical Info** (`Student Medical Info`): Any medical information related to the student.
  - This field is not required but is useful for us to understand any medical conditions the student may have.

## Tips
* Please fill out the form accurately and truthfully to ensure a smooth experience for the students.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us.
* If you have any additional information you'd like to share, please use the "Additional Info" field.
* If the student will not be attending the trip, please provide the reason for non-attendance in the "Reason for Non-Attendance" field.
