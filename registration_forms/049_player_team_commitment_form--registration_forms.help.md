# Player Team Commitment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Player Team Commitment Form is a crucial document that requires the player and parent's commitment to the team, as well as emergency contact information. Please read carefully and fill out the form accurately to avoid any errors.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form in order from page 1 to page <number of pages>.
- For each page, fill out the required fields and provide additional information if applicable.
- Make sure to double-check your entries for accuracy before submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the player's first name.
	+ Please enter your first name exactly as it is written on your official identification.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, Required): Enter the player's last name.
	+ Please enter your last name exactly as it is written on your official identification.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, Required): Enter the player's date of birth.

* **Medical Info** (`medical_info`, `note`, Optional): Enter any medical information that may be relevant to your participation in the team.
