# Sound Engineers Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is used for registration as a Sound Engineers member. To be eligible for membership, you must complete the form with accurate and up-to-date information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your General Information (Page 1)
	* Fill in your title to identify your profile.
2. Enter your Contact Information (Page 2)
	* Provide your contact details so we can get in touch with you.
3. Choose your Membership Type (Page 3)
	* Select either "Individual" or "Organisation" to indicate your type of membership.
4. Select your Membership Plan (Page 4)
	* Choose between "Monthly" or "Annually" for your membership plan.
5. Enter your Communication Information (Page 5)
	* Enter your email address to receive important updates and announcements.
6. Enter your Phone Number (Page 6)
	* Provide your phone number so we can contact you.
7. Enter your Address (Page 7)
	* Enter your address so we can mail you any relevant documents.
8. Enter your Company Name (Page 8)
	* If you are representing an organisation, enter your company name.
9. Enter your Job Title (Page 9)
	* If you are an individual, enter your job title; if you are an organisation, enter the job title of the main contact person.
10. Write a Brief Bio (Page 10)
	* Write a short description about yourself or your organisation to introduce yourself to the community.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Title** (`title`, `text`, required): Enter your name to help us identify you.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_info`, `text`, required): Enter your name, phone number, and email address to enable communication with us and other community members.
* **Membership Type** (`membership_type`, `select_one`, required): Select whether you are an "Individual" or "Organisation" to determine the type of membership privileges you should be granted.
* **Membership Plan** (`membership_plan`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose between "Monthly" and "Annually" for your membership plan, which will determine your membership fees.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address to receive important updates and announcements.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Address** (`address`, `text`, required): Enter your address to receive any mailed documents.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, `text`, required): Enter your company name if you are representing an organisation.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Enter your job title if you are an individual, otherwise enter the job title of the main contact person for your organisation.
* **Bio** (`bio`, `text`, required): Write a brief description about yourself or your organisation to introduce yourself to the community.
* **Monthly** (`Monthly`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Monthly" for a monthly membership plan.
* **Annually** (`Annually`, `select_multiple`, required): Select "Annually" for an annual membership plan.

## Tips
* Ensure that you complete all required fields to be eligible for membership.
* Double-check your contact information to ensure it is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review your membership plan carefully before submitting your application.
* If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask our support team for assistance.
