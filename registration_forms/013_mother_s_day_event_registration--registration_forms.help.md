# Mothers Day Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registration for the Mother's Day event. It helps us plan and accommodate for attendees and their guests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, marked as "required".
2. Choose one or more activities for the event.
3. Provide your email address and phone number (if applicable).
4. Briefly describe any additional details about your attendance or guests.
5. Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **School Organizers** (Text): Enter the name(s) of the school(s) or organization(s) you are representing.
* **Guest Counts** (Number): Enter 0 or 1 to indicate if guests will attend. 0 means you are not bringing any guests, 1 means you are bringing guests.
* **Activity Preference** (Select Multiple): Choose one or more activities for the event. This will help us plan for the number of attendees and resources needed.
* **Email Address** (Email): Provide your email address so we can contact you about event updates.
* **Phone Number** (Text): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.
* **Attendee Details** (Note): Briefly describe any additional details about your attendance or your guests.
* **Comments** (Text): Add any additional comments or information you'd like to share.
* **Additional Comments** (Text): Add any further comments or questions.
