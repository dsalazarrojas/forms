# Public Event Participation Waiver Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Public Event Participation Waiver Form is designed to collect necessary information from individuals or their legal guardians to participate in a public event. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the participant's personal details such as name, date of birth, phone number, and email address.
2. Provide the guardian's contact information if applicable.
3. Select the correct options for the participant's consent and the guardian's consent.
4. Choose the assigned tool for the participant's event participation.
5. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, required): Please enter the participant's full name as it appears on their government-issued identification.
* **Parent or Legal Guardian Consent** (`guardian_consent`, select_one, required): Please select one of the two options to indicate whether the participant has a parent or legal guardian's consent.
	+ Option 1: I have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ Option 2: I have not read and do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`, select_multiple, required): Please select all relevant options that apply to your participation in the event.
	+ Option 1: (Insert description of Option 1)
	+ Option 2: (Insert description of Option 2)
	+ Option 3: (Insert description of Option 3)
* **Participant Date of Birth** (`participant_date_of_birth`, date, required): Please enter the participant's date of birth in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required): Please enter the participant's email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required): Please enter the participant's phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Participant Street** (`participant_street`, text, required): Please enter the participant's street address.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, required): Please enter the participant's city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, required): Please enter the participant's state.
* **Participant Zip** (`participant_zip`, text, required): Please enter the participant's zip code.
* **Participant Agreement** (`participant_agreement`, select_one, required): Please select one of the two options to indicate your agreement to the terms and conditions.
	+ Option 1: I have read and agree to the terms and conditions.
	+ Option 2: I have not read and do not agree to the terms and conditions.
* **Guardian Information** (`guardian_info`, text, optional): Please enter any additional information about the guardian, if applicable.
* **Guardian Name** (`guardian_name`, text, required): Please enter the guardian's name.
* **Guardian Email** (`guardian_email`, email, required): Please enter the guardian's email address.
* **Guardian Phone** (`guardian_phone`, text, required): Please enter the guardian's phone number in the format 123-456-7890.
* **Guardian Street** (`guardian_street`, text, required): Please enter the guardian's street address.
* **Guardian City** (`guardian_city`, text, required): Please enter the guardian's city.
* **Guardian State** (`guardian_state`, text, required): Please enter the guardian's state.
* **Guardian Zip** (`guardian_zip`, text, required): Please enter the guardian's zip code.
* **Event Name** (`event_name`, text, optional): Please enter the name of the event, if desired.
* **Event Description** (`event_description`, text, optional): Please enter a brief description of the event, if desired.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required): Please enter the event date in MM/DD/YYYY format.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, text, required): Please enter the event time in 12:00 AM - 12:00 AM format.
* **Event Location** (`event_location`, text, optional): Please enter the location of the event, if desired.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, required): Please select one of the two options to assign the participant a tool for the event.
