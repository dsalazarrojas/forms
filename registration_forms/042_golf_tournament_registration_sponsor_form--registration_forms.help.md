# Golf Tournament Registration & Sponsor Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information and sponsorship details for a golf tournament.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

*   Fill out the participant registration form with your information.
*   Fill out the sponsor information form with your sponsorship details.
*   Provide your participant contact information for payment processing.
*   Review your payment details carefully.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Participant Name** (participant_name): Please enter your name as it would appear on your participant form.
*   **Participant Email** (participant_email): Enter your email address.
*   **Participant Phone** (participant_phone): Enter your phone number.
*   **Participant Address** (participant_address): Enter your address for payment processing.
*   **Participant City** (participant_city): Enter the city of your address.
*   **Participant State** (participant_state): Enter the state of your address.
*   **Participant Zip** (participant_zip): Enter the zip code of your address.
*   **Participant Form Payment Info** (participant_form_payment_info): Enter any additional payment information.
*   **Participant Form Payment Method** (participant_form_payment_method): Choose 'Yes' if you're paying by credit card, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Sponsor Name** (sponsor_name): Enter your name as it would appear on the sponsor form.
*   **Sponsor Address** (sponsor_address): Enter your sponsor address.
*   **Sponsor City** (sponsor_city): Enter the city of your sponsor address.
*   **Sponsor State** (sponsor_state): Enter the state of your sponsor address.
*   **Sponsor Zip** (sponsor_zip): Enter the zip code of your sponsor address.
*   **Sponsor Email** (sponsor_email): Enter your sponsor email address.
*   **Sponsor Phone** (sponsor_phone): Enter your sponsor phone number.
*   **Sponsor Info** (sponsor_info): Enter any additional sponsor information.
*   **Payment Details** (payment_details): Enter your payment details.
*   **Payment Method** (payment_method): Choose 'Yes' if you're paying by credit card, 'No' otherwise.
*   **Registration Form** (participant_form): Enter your participant registration information.
