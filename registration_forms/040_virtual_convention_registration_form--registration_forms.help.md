# Virtual Convention Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect contact and demographic information from attendees for the virtual convention.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Enter your first name in the field labeled "first_name".
2. Enter your last name in the field labeled "last_name".
3. Enter your email address in the field labeled "email".
4. Enter your phone number in the field labeled "phone".
5. Enter the date you wish to attend in the field labeled "date".
6. Enter the time you wish to attend in the field labeled "time".
7. Select the option that best suits your preference from the "select_one" field.
8. Select the options that best suit your preferences from the "select_multiple" field.
9. Enter any additional notes in the field labeled "note".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address in the format "example@example.com".
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number here.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you wish to attend the virtual convention in the "MM/DD/YYYY" format.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you wish to attend in the format "HH:MM AM/PM".
* **Select One** (`select_one`, select, required: false): Select the option that best suits your preference.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, Option 6
* **Select Multiple** (`select_multiple`, select, required: false): Select all the options that best suit your preferences.
	+ Options: Option 1, Option 2, Option 3, Option 4, Option 5, Option 6, Option 7, Option 8
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Enter any additional notes or comments here.
