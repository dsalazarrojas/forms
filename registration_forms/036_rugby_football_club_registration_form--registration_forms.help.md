# Rugby Football Club Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Rugby Football Club Registration Form is used for individuals or teams to register with the club. It collects essential information about the registrant and their emergency contacts. Please ensure you only register once with the club to avoid duplicate entries.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read this guide carefully before starting the form.
- Answer all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
- Double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (contact_name, text, required): Enter your personal name as it appears on official documents.
- **Email** (contact_email, email, required): Enter your valid email address for club communication.
- **Phone** (contact_phone, text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact.
- **Address** (contact_address, text, required): Enter your current home or work address for emergency purposes.
- **Team Name** (team_name, text, required): If registering as a team, enter your team's name.
- **Team Number** (team_number, number, required): If registering as a team, enter your team's registration number.
- **Membership Type** (membership_type, select_one, required): Select your membership type: Team, Individual, or Guest.
- **Play Level** (play_level, select_one, required): Select your playing level: Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced.
- **Date of Birth** (date_of_birth, date, required): Enter your date of birth for age verification purposes.
- **Emergency Contact Name** (emergency_contact_name, text, required): Enter the name of the person responsible for your emergency contact information.
- **Emergency Contact Relation** (emergency_contact_relation, text, required): Specify your relation to the emergency contact (e.g., family member, friend, partner).
- **Emergency Contact Phone** (emergency_contact_phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Email** (emergency_contact_email, email, required): Enter the email of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Address** (emergency_contact_address, text, required): Enter the address of your emergency contact.
- **Emergency Contact Message** (emergency_contact_message, text, required): Provide any additional emergency contact information.
- **Team Manager Name** (team_manager_name, text, optional): If you're registering as a team, enter your team manager's name.
- **Team Manager Phone** (team_manager_phone, text, optional): If you're registering as a team, enter your team manager's phone number.
- **Team Manager Email** (team_manager_email, email, optional): If you're registering as a team, enter your team manager's email address.
- **Team Manager Address** (team_manager_address, text, optional): If you're registering as a team, enter your team manager's address.
- **Referee** (referee_name, text, optional): Enter the name of your referee (if applicable).
- **Referee Phone** (referee_phone, text, optional): If you have a referee, enter their phone number (if applicable).

Note: Please be accurate when filling out this form. If you need help or have questions, feel free to contact the rugby club staff.
