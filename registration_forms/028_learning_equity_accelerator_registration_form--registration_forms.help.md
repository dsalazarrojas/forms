# Learning Equity Accelerator Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The Learning Equity Accelerator Registration Form is designed for users seeking to register for the Learning Equity Accelerator program. This form captures essential information from users to facilitate their registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the required fields with your first name and last name.
2.  Enter your organization's name in the "organization" field.
3.  Enter your email address in the "email" field.
4.  Provide your phone number in the "phone" field.
5.  Specify your job title in the "job_title" field.
6.  Enter your address in the "address" field.
7.  Select your city in the "city" field.
8.  Select your country in the "country" field.
9.  Choose your state from the list provided in the "state" field.
10. Enter your zip code in the "zip" field.
11.  If you have any additional information, you can provide it in the "learn_more" field.
12.  In the "contact_person" field, mention the person you would like to contact you in the case of further communication.
13.  Choose the type of job you are seeking to register for in the "job_type" field.
14.  Select the contact method you would prefer for further communication in the "contact" field.
15.  Choose your roles for the program in the "role" field.
16.  Finally, click "submit" to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name here.
*   **Organization** (`organization`, `text`, required): Enter your organization's name here.
*   **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address here.
*   **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Provide your phone number here.
*   **Job Title** (`job_title`, `text`, required): Specify your job title here.
*   **Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your address here. (Optional)
*   **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Select your city here. (Optional)
*   **Country** (`country`, `text`, optional): Select your country here. (Optional)
*   **State** (`state`, `select_one`, required): Choose your state from the list provided here.
*   **Zip** (`zip`, `text`, required): Enter your zip code here.
*   **Learn More** (`learn_more`, `note`, optional): If you have any additional information, you can provide it here. (Optional)
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, optional): Mention the person you would like to contact you in the case of further communication. (Optional)
*   **Job Type** (`job_type`, `select_one`, required): Choose the type of job you are seeking to register for here.
*   **Contact** (`contact`, `select_one`, required): Select the contact method you would prefer for further communication here.
*   **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your roles for the program here.
*   **Submit** (`submit`, `text`, optional): Click "submit" to submit your registration. (Optional)

Note: All required fields must be filled out before submitting the form.
