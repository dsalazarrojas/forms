# Car Crew Sign Up Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from users interested in joining the Car Crew. This information will be used to manage crew activities, ensure effective communication, and maintain a safe and enjoyable environment for all members.

## How To Complete This Form
To sign up for the Car Crew, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you have all the required information ready.
2. Fill in your personal details, such as your full name, email address, phone number, and home address.
3. Provide information about your vehicle, including the make and model, year of manufacture, vehicle color, and any modifications made to it.
4. Select your preferred crew activities and frequency of participation.
5. Choose your crew membership type and emergency contact information.
6. Acknowledge the crew rules and regulations and sign the form with your name.
7. Finally, provide any additional comments or requests you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name (Full Name)**: Enter your first and last name.
* **Email Address (Email Address)**: Enter the email address we will use to contact you about crew updates.
* **Phone Number (Phone Number)**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you during crew activities.
* **Home Address (Home Address)**: Enter your home address for mail and meeting points.
* **Car Make and Model (Car Make and Model)**: Describe your car in detail.
* **Year of Manufacture (Car Year)**: Enter the year of manufacture of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)**: Enter your vehicle's 17-character VIN code (optional).
* **Vehicle Color (Vehicle Color)**: Enter the primary color of your vehicle.
* **Vehicle Modifications (Vehicle Modifications)**: List any modifications made to your vehicle.
* **Crew Activities You are Interested In (Crew Activities You are Interested In)**: Select all the crew activities you are interested in (Group Cruises, Track Days, Charity Events, Car Shows, Technical Workshops).
* **Crew Participation Frequency (Crew Participation Frequency)**: Select how often you plan to participate in crew activities (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Occasionally).
* **Crew Membership Type (Crew Membership Type)**: Select your desired crew membership level (Regular Member, Elite Member, Honorary Member).
* **Crew Emergency Contact Name (Crew Emergency Contact Name)**: Enter the name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Crew Emergency Contact Phone (Crew Emergency Contact Phone)**: Enter the phone number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **I acknowledge the crew rules and regulations (I acknowledge the crew rules and regulations)**: Select "Yes, I agree" to acknowledge the crew rules and regulations.
* **Signature Confirmation (Crew Signature Confirmation)**: Enter your name for signature confirmation.
* **Anything else you want to share (Anything else you want to share)**: Enter any additional information or requests you may have.

Note: Fields marked as "required" must be filled in to submit the form.
