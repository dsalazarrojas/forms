# festival_preparation_checklist_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form helps in the preparation and organization of festival events by collecting information on festival details, performer details, stage and sound checks, vendor information, and other relevant details.
## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the festival details, including the festival name and any other relevant information.
2. Provide performer details, including the number of performers, their contact information, and any additional notes.
3. Specify the stage and sound check details, including the stage number and sound check time.
4. List the power details, including the generator options you have chosen.
5. Provide contact information for your team, including the contact person and their contact info.
6. If you need help, contact us at [insert contact email or contact person].

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Festival Details** (Festival 1, text, required): Enter the festival name and any other relevant information.
- **Performer Details** (Performer 1, number, required): Specify the number of performers you expect to have at your festival.
- **Stage Details** (Stage 1, select_one, required): Choose the stage where your performers will be performing.
- **Sound Check** (Sound Check, date, required): Specify the date for your sound check.
- **Sound Check Time** (Sound Check Time, time, required): Specify the time for your sound check.
- **Power Details** (Power 1, select_multiple, required): Choose the generators you will use for your festival.
- **Stage Setup** (Stage Setup, number, optional): If you need additional stages, specify the number here.
- **Power Usage** (Power Usage, number, optional): If you need additional power, specify the amount here.
- **Communication Details** (Communication 1, email, optional): Provide your contact email address.
- **Emergency Contact** (Emergency Contact, text, optional): Provide a contact person or team in case of an emergency.
- **Contact Person** (Contact Person, select_one, required): Choose the contact person for your team.
- **Contact Info** (Contact Info, note, optional): Leave any additional contact information here.
- **Additional Notes** (Additional Notes, note, optional): Leave any additional notes or information for your team.
- **Notes** (Notes, text, required): Provide any important festival notes or information.
- **Vendor Details** (Vendor 1, select_multiple, required): Choose the vendors you will be working with.
- **Vendor Contact** (Vendor Contact, email, optional): Provide the contact email address for your vendor.
- **Vendor Info** (Vendor Info, note, optional): Leave any additional vendor information here.
- **Vendor Notes** (Vendor Notes, text, optional): Leave any additional vendor notes or information.
- **Vendor Terms** (Vendor Terms, select_one, required): Agree to the vendor terms.
- **Vendor Status** (Vendor Status, select_multiple, required): Check the vendor status.
- **Vendor Schedule** (Vendor Schedule, date, required): Specify the date for your vendor schedule.
- **Vendor Payment** (Vendor Payment, number, required): Specify the payment for your vendor.
- **Vendor Address** (Vendor Address, note, optional): Leave any additional vendor address information here.
- **Vendor Phone** (Vendor Phone, text, optional): Leave any additional vendor phone number here.
- **Vendor Email** (Vendor Email, email, optional): Leave any additional vendor email address here.

## Tips
- Make sure to carefully review the required fields before submitting the form.
- Double-check the festival details to ensure accuracy.
- Ensure that you have all necessary contact information and vendor details ready.
- If you need help, contact us at [insert contact email or contact person].
