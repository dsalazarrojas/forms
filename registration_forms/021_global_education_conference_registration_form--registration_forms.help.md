# global_education_conference_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register for a global education conference. It collects information about the conference, contact details, participant information, and payment information for conference organizers.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form fields as instructed below.
2. Make sure to complete all required fields (indicated by a star \*).
3. Review your information carefully before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Conference Details (Page 1)
* **Conference Details**: \* (`conference_details`, `text`, required: false): Enter a brief description of your reason for attending or participating in the conference.

### Contact Info (Page 2)
* **Contact Info**: \* (`contact_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter your contact information, including name, email, and phone number.

### Participant Info (Page 3)
* **Participant Info**: \* (`participant_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any additional information about your participation in the conference, such as your title, affiliation, or presentation topic.

### Payment Info (Page 4)
* **Payment Info**: \* (`payment_info`, `text`, required: false): Enter any payment details, such as registration fee or sponsor information.

## Tips
* Please make sure to review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the form administrator at form@gic.mx.
* This form is for your convenience and is intended to be completed by users who wish to participate in the global education conference.
