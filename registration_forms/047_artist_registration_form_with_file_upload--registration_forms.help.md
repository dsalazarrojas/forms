# Artist Registration Form With File Upload - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Artist Registration Form is designed to collect information about you as an artist and allow you to upload relevant documents or samples of your work. This form is for artists who wish to be registered with our organization and be considered for collaboration or representation opportunities.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with your personal and contact information.
2. Select your primary artistic discipline from the options provided.
3. Upload any relevant documents or samples of your work.
4. Confirm that all information is accurate and complete.
5. Provide permission for our organization to contact you with opportunities.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Artist or Stage Name** (`artist_name`, `text`, required): This is the name you wish to be credited as.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, optional): If you are under 18, please contact us directly.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your primary contact email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Street Address** (`address`, `text`, optional): Enter your mailing address.
* **City** (`city`, `text`, optional): Enter your city name.
* **Primary Artistic Discipline** (`primary_discipline`, `select_one`, required): Select your main area of artistic work (e.g., Music, Visual Art, Theater, etc.).
* **Primary Medium or Style** (`primary_medium`, `text`, required): Enter your specific focus within your discipline.
* **Years of Experience** (`years_experience`, `number`, optional): Enter the number of years you have been creating.
* **Artist Biography** (`artist_biography`, `text`, required): Enter a professional bio, 50-100 words.
* **Notable Performances or Exhibitions** (`notable_performances`, `text`, optional): List any major venues or shows you have participated in.
* **Awards or Recognition Received** (`awards_recognition`, `text`, optional): List any awards or honors you have received.
* **Resume or CV Upload** (`resume_upload`, `text`, optional): Upload your professional resume.
* **Portfolio Samples or Work Samples** (`portfolio_samples`, `text`, optional): Upload examples of your work.
* **Professional Headshot Photo** (`headshot_photo`, `text`, optional): Upload a high-quality headshot photo.
* **Additional Documents or Materials** (`additional_documents`, `text`, optional): Upload any other relevant files.
* **Confirm Registration Information** (`registration_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Verify that all information is accurate and complete.
* **Permission to Contact** (`contact_permission`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you wish to be contacted (Yes, please contact me, No, do not contact, Email only).
