# online_platform_user_enrollment_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The online_platform_user_enrollment_form is designed to collect user information for platform enrollment.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the user details, including any relevant information about yourself.
2. Provide your contact details, including any email or phone number associated with you.
3. Describe your user preferences, such as any specific requirements you have for your account.
4. Confirm your enrollment by providing a clear and accurate statement of your intentions.
5. Set up your account according to your preferences.
6. If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Details (user_details)** (`user_details`, text, optional): Please fill in any relevant information about yourself.
* **Contact Details (contact_details)** (`contact_details`, text, optional): Provide your email, phone number, or any other contact information associated with you.
* **User Preferences (user_preferences)** (`user_preferences`, text, optional): Describe any specific requirements you have for your account, such as username, password, or any other setting.
* **Enrollment Confirmation (enrollment_confirmation)** (`enrollment_confirmation`, text, optional): Confirm your enrollment by providing a clear and accurate statement of your intentions.
* **Account Setup (account_setup)** (`account_setup`, text, optional): Set up your account according to your preferences.
* **Contact Us (contact_us)** (`contact_us`, text, optional): If you have any questions or concerns, contact us.
* **Form Submitted By (form_submitted_by)** (`form_submitted_by`, select_one, optional): Choose one of the options: Jimmy, Jimmy and 1 other, or None.
Note: The "Form Submitted By" field is not required for you to complete; the platform is aware of who you are.
