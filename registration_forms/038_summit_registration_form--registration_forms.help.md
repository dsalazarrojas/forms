# summit_registration_form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This is a registration form designed to collect information from individuals attending, organizing, or speaking at a summit event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow the instructions below.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (First Name)** (`text`, optional): Please enter your first name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Last Name (Last Name)** (`text`, optional): Please enter your last name as you would like it to be displayed.
* **Email (Email)** (`email`, required): Please enter a valid email address that we will use to communicate with you.
* **Role (Role)** (`select_one`, required): Please select your role at the summit:
	+ Admin: You are a member of the summit's organizing team.
	+ Attendee: You are registering as a participant or attendee.
	+ Organizer: You are responsible for organizing the summit event.
	+ Speaker: You are presenting at the summit.
* **Company (Company)** (`text`, optional): If you are representing a company, please enter its name.
* **Job Title (Job Title)** (`text`, optional): If you have a job title, please enter it.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter a valid email address, as we will use it to communicate with you about the summit.
* If you are unsure about your role, please ask the summit organizers for clarification.
* If you are representing a company, please enter the name as it appears on your business card or letterhead.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please don't hesitate to contact us at form@gic.mx.
