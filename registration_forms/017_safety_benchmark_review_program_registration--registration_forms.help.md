# Safety Benchmark Review Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Safety Benchmark Review Program Registration form is used to collect information about your program, which will be used to create a review schedule and facilitate the review process. This includes collecting details about your program name, program ID, preferred dates and times, goals, and email.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your program name in the "Program Name" field.
2. Enter your program ID number in the "Program ID" field.
3. Select your preferred date for the review in the "Preferred Date" field.
4. Select your preferred time for the review in the "Preferred Time" field.
5. If you have multiple preferred dates or times, select them in the "Preferred Date 2" field.
6. Enter your goals or objectives for the review in the "Goals" field.
7. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Program Name** (`program_name`, text, optional): Enter your program name in this field.
* **Program ID** (`program_id`, number, optional): Enter your program ID number in this field.
* **Preferred Date** (`preferred_date`, date, optional): Select your preferred date for the review.
* **Preferred Time** (`preferred_time`, time, optional): Select your preferred time for the review.
* **Preferred Date 2** (`preferred_date_two`, date, optional): If you have multiple preferred dates or times, select them in this field.
* **Goals** (`goals`, text, optional): Enter your goals or objectives for the review.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address in this field.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information to ensure proper scheduling and follow-up on the review.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact your representative or review administrator.
* Double-check your preferred dates and times to ensure they align with your availability.
