# Workflow_Optimization_Training_Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Workflow_Optimization_Training_Form is designed to collect data for employee training purposes. It gathers information on employee preferences, needs, details, schedule, duration, location, fees, registration status, and registration date to generate reports or support other organizational processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form sections to understand the purpose of each field.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) as accurately as possible.
3. Ensure you've provided all necessary information before submitting or saving.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### 1. **Training Preferences**
* **Training Preferences (training_preferences)**: This field allows you to express your training preferences. Enter a brief description of your needs, and it will be reviewed for report generation.

### 2. **Employee Training Needs**
* **Employee Training Needs (training_needs)**: Describe your training needs to assist in the training planning process.

### 3. **Employee Details**
* **Employee Details (employee_details)**: Select an option that best describes your current employee details.
	+ Option 1: New
	+ Option 2: Experienced
	+ Option 3: Managerial

### 4. **Training Schedule**
* **Training Schedule (training_schedule)**: Enter the date you're planning to start the training.

### 5. **Training Duration**
* **Training Duration (training_duration)**: Enter the expected duration of the training in hours.

### 6. **Training Location**
* **Training Location (training_location)**: Describe the desired training location.

### 7. **Training Fees**
* **Training Fees (training_fees)**: Enter the total training fees (if any).

### 8. **Registration Status**
* **Registration Status (registration_status)**: Select the status that best describes your current registration.
	+ Option 1: Pending
	+ Option 2: Approved

### 9. **Registration Date**
* **Registration Date (registration_date)**: Enter the date of your registration.
