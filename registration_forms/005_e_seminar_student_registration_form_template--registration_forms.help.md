# e Seminar Student Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect registration information from students for a seminar or educational event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your **Email** in the "Email" field. Please ensure that the email address is valid and formatted correctly.
4. Enter your **Phone** number in the "Phone" field.
5. Enter the **Date** of your seminar attendance in the "Date" field.
6. Enter your **School** name in the "School" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### First Name
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.

### Last Name
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.

### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that will be used for communication purposes.

### Phone
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number to enable easy communication.

### Date
* **Date** (`date`, date, optional): Enter the date you plan to attend the seminar.

### School
* **School** (`school`, text, optional): Enter the name of your school as it appears on your official documents.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all fields are filled out accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please contact the event organizers for assistance.
* If you are experiencing technical issues or have trouble accessing the form, try refreshing your browser or clearing your browser cache.
* Please note that your information will not be shared or sold to any third party.
