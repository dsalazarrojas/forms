# PPP Loan Forgiveness Application Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for businesses that want to apply for loan forgiveness under the PPP program. It helps us understand your business and your loan information so we can process your application.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately, making sure to enter your business and loan information.
2. Make sure to enter a valid zip code.
3. Choose the correct business type for your company.
4. Enter your loan amount, date of last loan, and loan status.
5. Enter your employee ID and date of last payroll.
6. Review the form carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (Text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Business Name** (Text, required: false): Enter your business name.
* **Address** (Text, required: false): Enter your business address.
* **City** (Text, required: false): Enter your city.
* **State** (Select One, required: false): Choose your state.
* **Zip** (Number, required: false): Enter a valid 5-digit zip code.
* **Loan Amount** (Number, required: false): Enter the amount of your loan.
* **Business Type** (Select Multiple, required: false): Choose the type of business you are (Retail, Restaurant, Other).
* **Email** (Email, required: false): Enter your business email.
* **Phone** (Text, required: false): Enter your business phone number.
* **Date** (Date, required: false): Enter the date of your last loan.
* **Time** (Time, required: false): Enter the time of your last loan.
* **Note** (Note, required: false): Enter any additional information or notes about your loan.
* **Date of Last Loan** (Date, required: false): Enter the date of your last loan.
* **Time of Last Loan** (Time, required: false): Enter the time of your last loan.
* **Loan Status** (Select One, required: false): Choose the status of your loan (Applied, Approved, Denied).
* **Employee ID** (Number, required: false): Enter your employee ID.
* **Date of Last Payroll** (Date, required: false): Enter the date of your last payroll.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter accurate and complete information to ensure your application is processed correctly.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact us.
* Only submit the form once, as duplicate submissions will be rejected.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
