# precision-oncology-data-forum-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from attendees for the precision oncology data forum. It collects details such as contact information, address, event and workshop preferences, and any additional notes from the attendee. The form is intended for registration purposes only.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow the instructions below:

1. Fill in your contact information on pages 1-2.
2. Enter your address on page 3.
3. Choose your city, state, and country on pages 4-6.
4. Select your preferred event on page 7.
5. Select your preferred workshop on page 8.
6. If necessary, add any additional notes on page 9.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Page 1: Registration Form
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, required/optional): This is the title of the form, and can be left blank.
### Page 2: Contact Info
* **contact-info** (`contact_info`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information to allow us to reach out to you.
### Page 3: Address
* **address** (`address`, text, required/optional): Enter your address details so we can contact you.
### Page 4: City
* **city** (`city`, text, required/optional): Enter the city where you reside or are attending from.
### Page 5: State
* **state** (`state`, text, required/optional): Enter the state where you are from or will be attending from.
### Page 6: Country
* **Country** (`country`, select_one, required): Choose your country of residence or origin. Options: China, USA, UK.
### Page 7: Email
* **email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
### Page 8: Phone
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
### Page 9: Event
