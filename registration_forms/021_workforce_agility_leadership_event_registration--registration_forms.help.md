## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Workforce Agility Leadership event, allowing attendees to provide their details and session interests.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your event details in the "Event Details" field (optional).
2. Select your session interests from the lists provided for "Session Interests", "Session Interests 2", "Session Interests 3", "Session Interests 4", and "Session Interests 5".
3. Review and agree to the "Event Terms" and "Terms and Conditions".
