# Lemonade Stand Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Lemonade Stand Registration Form is used to collect participant information, including their details and those of their guardians, for educational or research purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form carefully to understand each field's purpose.
2. Fill out the form with the requested information for each participant and guardian.
3. Ensure that the participant and guardian details are entered accurately and consistently for all fields.
4. Complete any additional pages or sections as needed, following the same principles of accuracy and consistency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for entering the participant's personal details, such as their name and any other relevant information that may be required for the purpose of the study or activity.
* **Guardian Details** (`guardian_details`, `text`, required: false): If applicable, this section is for entering the guardian's or parent's details, including their name and contact information. The participant may be a minor or someone who is not of legal age to enter their own contact information.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required: false): This field is used to record the participant's birthdate. Please ensure this is entered in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, `text`, required: false): This field is for entering the participant's phone number, which may be useful for contacting the participant or their guardian about study updates or reminders.
