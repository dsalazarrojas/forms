# Ball Person Academy Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Ball Person Academy Registration Form is a comprehensive tool designed to collect information from individuals interested in joining the Ball Person Academy. The form helps the academy's administration to better understand the applicants' backgrounds, experience, and preferences, ensuring a smooth and efficient registration process.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully to ensure you understand each section and field.
2. Fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, providing as much detail as possible.
3. Make sure to complete all required fields to avoid any issues with your application.
4. If you have any doubts or concerns, contact the academy's administration for assistance.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your valid email address.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, `phone`, required): Enter your phone number where you can be contacted.
* **Academy Name** (`academy_name`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the Ball Person Academy branch you are applying to.
* **Program Level** (`program_level`, `select_one`, required): Select the level of the program you are interested in:
	+ Beginner
	+ Intermediate
	+ Advanced
	+ Professional
	+ Coaching Staff
* **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you are registering for the program.
* **Program Start Date** (`program_start_date`, `date`, required): Enter the date you want to start the program.
* **Tennis Experience Level** (`tennis_experience`, `select_one`, required): Select your level of tennis experience:
	+ No Experience
	+ Recreational Player
	+ Competitive Player
	+ Former Professional
	+ Coach or Instructor
* **Previous Ball Person Experience** (`ball_person_experience`, `select_one`, optional): Select if you have any previous experience with Ball Person:
	+ No
	+ Yes
	+ Some Experience
* **Major Events Worked** (`prior_events_worked`, `text`, optional): If applicable, list any major events you have worked on or participated in.
* **Primary Sport Interest** (`primary_sport`, `select_one`, optional): Select your primary sport interest:
	+ Singles
	+ Doubles
	+ Mixed Doubles
	+ All
* **Availability for Events** (`availability_level`, `select_one`, required): Select your availability for events:
	+ Full-Time
	+ Part-Time
	+ Weekends Only
	+ Flexible
* **Preferred Seasons to Work** (`preferred_seasons`, `select_multiple`, optional): Select all seasons you prefer to work:
	+ Spring
	+ Summer
	+ Fall
	+ Winter
	+ Year-Round
* **Relevant Certifications or Training** (`certifications`, `text`, optional): List any relevant certifications or training you have, such as First Aid, CPR, Sports Management, etc.
* **Physical Fitness Level** (`physical_fitness`, `select_one`, required): Select your physical fitness level:
	+ Excellent
	+ Good
	+ Average
	+ Fair
* **Special Skills or Qualifications** (`special_skills`, `text`, optional): List any special skills or qualifications you have, such as languages, previous sports experience, etc.
* **Registration Confirmation** (`registration_confirmation`, `select_one`, required): Confirm your registration for the Ball Person Academy:
	+ Confirm Registration
	+ Decline
