# Invoice Processing Live Demo Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The "Invoice Processing Live Demo Registration" form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in learning about invoice processing. This form helps us to better understand the needs and interests of our audience, and to tailor our content and follow-up communication to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "first_name" field.
2. Enter your title in the "title" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "email" field. Please use a valid email address that you access regularly.
4. Enter your company name in the "company" field.
5. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
6. Enter your job title in the "job_title" field.
7. Enter your company website in the "website" field.
8. Select the size of your company from the "company_size" dropdown menu.
9. Select your job function from the available options in the "job_function" field. You can choose one or multiple options.
10. Enter any additional notes about yourself in the "participant_notes" field, if needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **first_name** (Text): Enter your first name.
* **title** (Text): Enter your title or professional position.
* **email** (Email): Enter a valid email address that you access regularly. This will be used for follow-up communication.
* **company** (Text): Enter your company name.
* **phone** (Text): Enter your phone number.
* **job_title** (Text): Enter your job title or professional position.
* **website** (Text): Enter your company website.
* **company_size** (Select One): Select the size of your company:
	+ Small
	+ Medium
	+ Large
* **job_function** (Select Multiple): Select your job function:
	+ Sales
	+ Marketing
	+ Finance
	+ HR
* **participant_notes** (Note): Enter any additional notes about yourself, if needed.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to help us tailor our follow-up communication to your needs.
* If you are not sure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We will keep your information confidential and use it only for the purpose of the live demo registration.
