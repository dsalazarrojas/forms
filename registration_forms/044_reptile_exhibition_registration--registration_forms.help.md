# reptile_exhibition_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering reptiles for an exhibition. You can use it to provide your reptile's details and sign up for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your reptile's name.
2. Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
3. Enter your reptile's phone number.
4. Read and agree to the rule agreements.
5. Select the species of your reptile.
6. Select the sex of your reptile.
7. Select the size(s) of your reptile.
8. Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
9. Choose a date for the event.
10. Choose a time for the event.
11. Enter the location of the event.
12. Select the type of event your reptile is participating in.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of your reptile.
- **Email** (email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address for contact purposes.
- **Phone** (text, required/optional): Enter your reptile's phone number.
- **Rule Agreements** (text, required/optional): Read and agree to the rule agreements by entering your name in the text field.
- **Species** (select_one, required/optional): Choose the species of your reptile.
- **Sex** (select_one, required/optional): Select the sex of your reptile.
- **Size** (select_multiple, required/optional): Select the size of your reptile.
- **Event Name** (text, required/optional): Enter the name of the event you are participating in.
- **Event Date** (date, required/optional): Choose a date for the event.
- **Event Time** (time, required/optional): Choose a time for the event.
- **Event Location** (text, required/optional): Enter the location of the event.
- **Event Type** (select_one, required/optional): Select the type of event your reptile is participating in.
