# Corporate Finance Dialogue Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Corporate Finance Dialogue Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in participating in corporate finance discussions. This includes roundtables, webinars, and expert discussions. By filling out this form, you will be helping us understand your interests and preferences for these events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields (indicated as required: true).
2. Select your interests for the topic of interest field.
3. Choose your consent for each type of discussion (roundtable, webinar, expert discussion).
4. Select your roundtable topic (if applicable).
5. Provide a date and time for your participation (if applicable).
6. Add any additional notes or comments (if applicable).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Topic of interest** (`topic_of_interest`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all topics that interest you.
* **Consent for Roundtable Discussion** (`consent_roundtable`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose your consent for Roundtable Discussion 1 or Roundtable 2.
* **Consent for Webinar** (`consent_webinar`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose your consent for Webinar 1 or Webinar 2.
* **Consent for Expert Discussion** (`consent_expert_discussion`, select_one, required: false):
	+ Choose your consent for Expert Discussion 1 or Expert Discussion 2.
* **Roundtable topic** (`roundtable_topic`, select_multiple, required: false):
	+ Select all topics that interest you for the roundtable.
* **Expert discussion topic** (`expert_discussion_topic`, text, required: false): Enter any additional information about your interest in the expert discussion.
* **Date** (`date`, date, required: false): Enter the date you are available to participate.
* **Time** (`time`, time, required: false): Enter the time you are available to participate.
* **Note** (`note`, note, required: false): Add any additional comments or notes about your participation.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your company (if applicable).

Note: This guide assumes that the user will be able to understand the meaning of each field and its relevance to the corporate finance discussions. Please review and adjust accordingly to ensure clarity and accuracy.
