# LPN Assignment Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose

The LPN Assignment Registration Form is used to register LPNs (Licensed Practical Nurses) for assignment to various healthcare settings. This form helps in managing the placement of LPNs with healthcare facilities and teams. Fill out this form to initiate the LPN placement process.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Fill out all required fields with the correct information to ensure accurate data entry.
2. Review and validate your responses before submitting the form.
