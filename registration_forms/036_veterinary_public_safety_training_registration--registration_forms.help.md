# veterinary_public_safety_training_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended for veterinary professionals to register their interest in participating in public safety training programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and answer all required questions.
2. For fields with multiple options, select the most accurate response for your situation.
3. If you're unsure about any question, ask your supervisor or training coordinator for guidance.
4. Once you've completed all fields, review your responses for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_info`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Please select all that apply to describe your professional status as a veterinarian.
	+ Choose 'Yes' if you are currently practicing as a veterinarian or a related profession.
	+ Choose 'No' if you are not currently practicing as a veterinarian or related profession.
* **Experience Details** (`experience_details`, `note`, required: false): This is an optional field for you to provide any additional information about your experience related to public safety training.
* **Registration Information** (`registration_info`, `select_one`, required: false): Please select 'Yes' to confirm your registration for the training program.
* **Schedule Session** (`schedule_session`, `date`, required: false): Enter the date you would like to schedule your training session.
