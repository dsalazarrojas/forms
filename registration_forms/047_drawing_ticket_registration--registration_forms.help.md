# Drawing Ticket Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Drawing Ticket Registration form is used to register a drawing ticket for a specific winner. This form ensures that our records are up-to-date and accurate, and helps prevent duplicate entries.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Enter your phone number.
5. Select the drawing ticket you want to register from the list of available options.
6. If you are the winner, select the "Winner" option from the list.
7. Fill in the name of the person whose name will be on the ticket.
8. Fill in the ticket number.
9. Select the date of the drawing.
10. Click the "Submit" button to complete the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (email, optional): Enter your email address, where you will receive notifications and updates about the drawing.
* **Phone** (text, optional): Enter your phone number, where we can contact you if needed.
* **Drawing Tickets** (select_one, optional): Select the drawing ticket you want to register from the list of options.
* **Winner** (select_one, optional): If you are the winner, select the "True" option.
* **Name on Ticket** (text, optional): Enter the name of the person whose name will be on the ticket.
* **Ticket Number** (text, optional): Enter the ticket number for which you are registering.
* **Drawing Date** (date, optional): Select the date of the drawing.
* **Submit** (text, optional): Click this button to complete and submit the form.
* **Name on Ticket** (text, optional): This field is intended to hold the name of the person whose name will be printed on the ticket.
* **Ticket Number** (text, optional): This field is intended to hold the ticket number for which you are registering.
* **Drawing Date** (date, optional): This field is intended to hold the date of the drawing.
*   **Winner** (select_one, optional): If you are the winner, select the "True" option to indicate that you are the winner.
* **Submit** (text, optional): This button is intended to be clicked to complete and submit the form.
