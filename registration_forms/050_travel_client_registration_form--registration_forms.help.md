# Travel Client Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect travel-related information from clients for efficient travel management and record keeping.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and relevant information about the client's travel details.
2. Select the correct method of travel (air travel, cruise, train, or other).
3. Provide necessary contact information for both the travel agency and client.
4. Indicate the client's agreement on the terms and conditions.
5. Add any relevant comments or notes about the client's travel.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **General Info** (`general_info`, text, required): Enter a brief description of the client's travel.
* **Arrival Info** (`arrival_info`, text, required): Enter the client's expected arrival information.
* **Method of Travel** (`method_of_travel`, select_one, required): Select the mode of transportation used for the travel (air travel, cruise, train, or other).
* **Travel Details** (`travel_details`, text, required): Enter detailed information about the client's travel.
* **Travel Agency** (`travel_agency`, text, required): Enter the travel agency's name and contact information.
* **Client Agreed** (`client_agreed`, select_one, required): Indicate whether the client has agreed to the terms and conditions (yes or no).
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required): Enter the travel agent's name and contact information.
* **Travel Client Name** (`travel_client_name`, text, required): Enter the client's name.
* **Travel Client Contact Name** (`travel_client_contact_name`, text, required): Enter the contact person's name for the client.
* **Client Signature** (`client_signature`, note, required): Sign and date this form to confirm you have filled it out correctly.
* **Staff Signature** (`staff_signature`, note, required): The staff member handling the client's travel must sign and date this form to confirm they have reviewed and accepted the information.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required): Enter any additional comments or notes about the client's travel.
* **Travel Agency Phone** (`travel_agency_phone`, text, required): Enter the travel agency's phone number.
* **Travel Agency Email** (`travel_agency_email`, email, required): Enter the travel agency's email address.
* **Client Agreed Time** (`client_agreed_time`, time, required): Indicate the time at which the client agreed to the terms and conditions.
* **Travel Agent Phone** (`travel_agent_phone`, text, required): Enter the travel agent's phone number.
* **Travel Agent Email** (`travel_agent_email`, email, required): Enter the travel agent's email address.
* **Travel From** (`travel_from`, text, required): Enter the client's departure location.
* **Travel To** (`travel_to`, text, required): Enter the client's destination.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, date, optional): Enter the client's expected arrival date.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, time, optional): Enter the client's expected arrival time.
* **Travel Date** (`travel_date`, date, required): Enter the client's travel date.
* **Travel Time** (`travel_time`, time, required): Enter the client's travel time.
* **Travel Agent** (`travel_agent`, text, required): Enter the travel agent's name.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, required): This is a note field for the staff to add a digital signature after reviewing the form.
