# Instrument Flight Training Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from students to enroll in instrument flight training. It helps the training program to understand the students' background, flight experience, and scheduling preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your contact information and any other personal details in the "user_info" field.
2. Enter your flight experience and any relevant information in the "flight_info" field.
3. Share any relevant information about your instructor in the "instructor_info" field.
4. Select your preferred scheduling options in the "scheduling" field.
5. Answer any additional questions in the "student_intake" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **user_info** (text, optional): Enter your name, email, phone number, or any other contact information.
* **flight_info** (text, optional): Share your flight experience, ratings, or any other relevant information about your flying history.
* **instructor_info** (text, optional): Enter any relevant information about your instructor, such as their name, rating, or contact information.
* **scheduling** (text, optional): Select your preferred scheduling options for the training, such as dates, times, or locations.
* **student_intake** (text, optional): Answer any additional questions or provide any other relevant information about yourself.
