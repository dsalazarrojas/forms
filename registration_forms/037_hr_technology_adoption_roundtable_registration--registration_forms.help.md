# HR Technology Adoption Roundtable Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form collects registration information for the HR Technology Adoption Roundtable to facilitate collaboration and discussion among HR professionals. 

## How To Complete This Form

1. Review the form carefully and ensure you provide accurate and complete information.
2. Fill in all required fields.
3. Select one option for 'Organization' and 'Role' fields.
4. Enter your email address in the 'E-mail' field.
5. Enter your phone number in the 'Phone' field.
6. Provide any comments you have in the 'Comments' field.
7. Enter your company name in the 'Company' field.
8. Enter your department name in the 'Department' field.
9. Enter your job title in the 'Job Title' field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User Name** (1, text, optional): This field is where you enter your name.
* **Organization** (2, select_one, optional): This field asks you to confirm whether your organization is planning to adopt HR technology. Select 'Yes' if your organization plans to adopt HR technology, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Role** (3, select_one, optional): This field asks for your role in HR technology adoption. Select 'Yes' if you are involved in the adoption process, or 'No' otherwise.
* **Interests** (4, text, optional): This field allows you to specify your interests related to HR technology adoption.
* **E-mail** (5, email, optional): This field is where you enter your professional email address.
* **Phone** (6, text, optional): This field is where you enter your phone number.
* **Consent** (7, text, optional): This field is intended for you to provide any additional comments or consent for participation in the roundtable.
* **Comments** (8, text, optional): This field is where you can provide any additional comments or information about your registration.
* **Company** (9, text, optional): This field is where you enter your company name.
* **Department** (10, text, optional): This field is where you enter your department name.
* **Job Title** (11, text, optional): This field is where you enter your job title.

## Tips

* Be as specific as possible when filling out the form to ensure accurate representation.
* If you are unsure about any field, please contact the organizers for clarification.
* Duplicate registrations may cause inconvenience to other attendees and organizers. Please ensure you are not already registered and that your organization is not already represented.
