# Pie Giveaway Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pie Giveaway Registration form is designed to collect contact and delivery information from participants in a pie giveaway event. By filling out this form, you'll be entered to receive a free pie gift.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. If applicable, fill in your phone number in the "Phone" field.
4. Provide your address in the "Address" field.
5. Indicate if you are eligible for the giveaway in the "Are you eligible for this giveaway?" field.
6. Choose your preferred delivery method in the "How will you receive your gift?" field.
7. If you selected "Other" as your delivery method, provide additional details in the "Other delivery details" field.
8. Select the type of pie you'd like to receive in the "What would you like your pie gift to be?" field.
9. Enter the name of the person receiving the gift in the "Name of gift receiver" field.
10. Describe the relationship between you and the gift receiver in the "Relationship to gifter" field.
11. If you'd like to include a gift message, enter it in the "Gift message" field.
12. Choose the delivery date in the "Delivery Date" field.
13. If you'd like to specify a delivery time, enter it in the "Delivery Time" field.
14. If you'd like to include details about the gift, enter them in the "Gift Details" field.
15. Enter the name to be printed on the gift card in the "Name to be printed on card" field.
16. Write a message to be printed on the gift card in the "Message to be printed on card" field.
17. Enter the type of gift card design in the "Card Design" field.
18. Choose the gift card type in the "Card Type" field.
19. Enter the name and job title of the designer in the "Name of designer" and "Job Title of designer" fields.
20. Finally, sign your name in the "Signature" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Email** (Email, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Address** (Address, text, optional): Enter your address.
* **Delivery Info** (Delivery Info, note, optional): This field is for any additional delivery information.
* **Date of Birth** (Date of Birth, date, optional): Enter your date of birth.
* **Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY)** (Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY), date, optional): Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
* **Are you eligible for this giveaway?** (Are you eligible for this giveaway?, select_one, optional): Indicate if you are eligible for the giveaway.
* **How will you receive your gift?** (How will you receive your gift?, select_one, optional): Choose your preferred delivery method.
* **Other delivery details** (Other delivery details, text, optional): If you selected "Other" as your delivery method, provide additional details.
* **What would you like your pie gift to be?** (What would you like your pie gift to be?, select_one, required): Select the type of pie you'd like to receive.
* **Name of gift receiver** (Name of gift receiver, text, optional): Enter the name of the person receiving the gift.
* **Relationship to gifter** (Relationship to gifter, text, optional): Describe your relationship to the gift receiver.
* **Gift message** (Gift message, text, optional): Enter a message to be printed on the gift card.
* **Delivery Date** (Delivery Date, select_one, required): Choose the delivery date for the gift.
* **Delivery Time** (Delivery Time, time, optional): Enter the delivery time for the gift.
* **Gift Details** (Gift Details, note, optional): Enter any additional gift details.
* **Name to be printed on card** (Name to be printed on card, text, optional): Enter the name to be printed on the gift card.
* **Message to be printed on card** (Message to be printed on card, text, optional): Enter a message to be printed on the gift card.
* **Card Design** (Card Design, text, optional): Enter the gift card design.
* **Card Type** (Card Type, select_one, required): Select the gift card type.
* **Name of designer** (Name of designer, text, optional): Enter the name of the designer.
* **Job Title of designer** (Job Title of designer, text, optional): Enter the job title of the designer.
