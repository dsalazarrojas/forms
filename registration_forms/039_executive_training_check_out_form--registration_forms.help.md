# Executive Training Check Out Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from participants in an executive training program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your participant information in the "Participant Information" section.
2. Enter your contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
3. Select your payment method from the "Payment Method" dropdown menu.
4. Enter the payment terms in the "Payment Terms" text field.
5. If you have any additional comments or notes, please enter them in the "Participant Notes" text area.
6. Enter your payment amount in the "Payment Amount" field.
7. Sign your name in the "Participant Signature" field.
8. Enter your organization's name in the "Organization Name" field (if applicable).
9. Enter your organization's contact information in the "Contact Information" section.
10. Enter your participant's and organization's address information in the respective "Participant Street", "Participant City", "Participant State", "Participant Postal Code", "Organization Street", "Organization City", "Organization State", "Organization Postal Code" fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name, title, and other relevant details about yourself.
* **Contact Information** (`contact_information`, text, required/optional): Enter your contact information, including your email, phone number, and any other relevant details.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_info`, text, required/optional): Enter any additional payment information or any other details about your payment.
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required/optional): Select one of the options from the dropdown menu to choose how you would like to pay.
* **Payment Terms** (`payment_terms`, text, required/optional): Enter any payment terms or conditions.
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, required/optional): Enter any additional comments or notes about your participation in the program.
* **Payment Amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the amount you would like to pay.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, time, required/optional): Sign your name to confirm your participation.
* **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's name (if applicable).
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization Email** (`organization_email`, email, required/optional): Enter your organization's email address.
* **Organization Phone** (`organization_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's phone number.
* **Participant Street** (`participant_street`, text, required/optional): Enter your street address.
* **Participant City** (`participant_city`, text, required/optional): Enter your city.
* **Participant State** (`participant_state`, text, required/optional): Enter your state.
* **Participant Postal Code** (`participant_postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your postal code.
* **Organization Street** (`organization_street`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's street address.
* **Organization City** (`organization_city`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's city.
* **Organization State** (`organization_state`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's state.
* **Organization Postal Code** (`organization_postal_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your organization's postal code.
* **Participant Country** (`participant_country`, text, required/optional): Enter your country.
* **Participant Region** (`participant_region`, text, required/optional): Enter your region.
* **Participant ZIP** (`participant_zip`, text, required/optional): Enter your ZIP code.
* **Participant Country Code** (`participant_country_code`, text, required/optional): Enter your country code.
