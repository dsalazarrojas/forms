# Makeup Artist Conference Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Makeup Artist Conference Registration form is a tool used to gather information from potential attendees who want to register for the conference. This form is essential in helping us understand your preferences and needs, which will allow us to provide you with the best possible experience during the event.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Please select the sessions you are interested in attending from the list provided.
2. If you have any special needs, such as accessibility requirements or dietary restrictions, please let us know in the text field provided.
3. Fill in your contact details, including your name, email address, and phone number.
4. Once you have completed all the required fields, submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Select Sessions** (`select_sessions`, select_multiple, required: false): This field allows you to choose from a list of available sessions. You can select multiple sessions by holding down the Ctrl key (or Command key on a Mac) while clicking on the sessions you're interested in.
* **Special Needs** (`special_needs`, text, required: false): If you have any special needs that we should be aware of, such as accessibility requirements or dietary restrictions, please enter them in this field.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required: false): Please enter your name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you about the conference.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number so we can contact you if needed.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in all required fields to ensure your registration is complete.
* If you're unsure about any of the fields, please don't hesitate to contact us for assistance.
* We will use the information provided to tailor the conference experience to your needs.
