# Overnight Hike Check In Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Overnight Hike Check In Form is used to gather information from participants for an overnight hike event. It ensures that all participants provide accurate and complete information about themselves.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields, which are marked as "required" below.
2. Enter your name in the "participant_name" field.
3. Provide your phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
4. Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
5. If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, please enter them in the "participant_medical_conditions" field.
6. Select the event staff members who will be present during the hike from the "event_staff" field.
7. Optional: If you have previous hiking experience, you can enter it in the "participant_hiking_experience" field.
8. Optional: If you have previous overnight hiking experience, you can enter it in the "participant_overnight_hiking_experience" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **participant_name**: Enter your full name.
- **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number.
- **participant_email**: Enter your email address.
- **participant_medical_conditions**: If you have any medical conditions that may affect your participation, enter them here.
- **event_staff**: Select the event staff members who will be present during the hike.
- **participant_hiking_experience**: If you have previous hiking experience, enter the details here (optional).
- **participant_overnight_hiking_experience**: If you have previous overnight hiking experience, enter the details here (optional).
- **event_start_time**: If you have a preferred start time for the event, enter it here (optional).
- **event_location**: If you want to provide a specific location for the event, enter it here (optional).

## Tips
- Make sure to fill in all required fields before submitting the form.
- If you're unsure about any of the fields, please contact the event staff for assistance.
- The event staff will review and manage the participant information to ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all participants.
