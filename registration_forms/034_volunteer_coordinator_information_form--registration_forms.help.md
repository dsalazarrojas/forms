# Volunteer Coordinator Information Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect and store information about volunteers, including their program, start and end dates, status, contact details, and any additional information relevant to their role.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each field carefully and ensure you have the necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the required fields, indicated by an asterisk (\*) in the field label.
3. Use the provided options for select fields, such as the Volunteer Status field.
4. Enter your information in the text fields, including phone numbers and email addresses.
5. Select the correct option for the Termination Reason field, if applicable.
6. Review and submit the form once complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Volunteer Coordinator**: Enter the name of the person completing this form.
* **Volunteer Program**: Enter the name of the volunteer program the volunteer is involved with.
* **Volunteer Start Date**: Enter the date the volunteer started their volunteer work.
* **Volunteer End Date**: Enter the date the volunteer ended their volunteer work.
* **Volunteer Status**: Select the current status of the volunteer from the options provided (Active, Inactive, On Leave).
* **Volunteer Phone Number**: Enter the volunteer's phone number.
* **Volunteer Email**: Enter the volunteer's email address.
* **Volunteer Notes**: Enter any relevant notes about the volunteer's role or performance.
* **Additional Information**: Enter any additional information relevant to the volunteer's role or work.
* **Additional Notes**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the volunteer.
* **References**: Select whether or not the volunteer has references.
* **Termination Date**: Enter the date the volunteer's work was terminated, if applicable.
* **Volunteer Type**: Select the type of volunteer from the options provided (Regular, Seasonal, Volunteer Leader).
* **Volunteer Status History**: Enter any history of changes in the volunteer's status.
* **Additional Status**: Select any additional status information.
* **Supervisor**: Enter the name of the volunteer's supervisor.
* **Supervisor Email**: Enter the supervisor's email address.
* **Supervisor Phone Number**: Enter the supervisor's phone number.
* **Supervisor Notes**: Enter any notes about the supervisor.
* **Director**: Enter the name of the director.
* **Director Email**: Enter the director's email address.
* **Director Phone Number**: Enter the director's phone number.
* **Director Notes**: Enter any notes about the director.
* **Termination Reason**: Select the reason for termination from the options provided (Volunteer Quit, Termination Requested, Termination Reason Not Specified).
* **Supervisor Termination Reason**: Enter the reason for the supervisor's termination.
* **Director Termination Reason**: Enter the reason for the director's termination.
* **Volunteer Type Description**: Enter a description of the volunteer type.
* **Status Description**: Enter a description of the volunteer's status.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments about the volunteer.
* **Submit**: Click this button to submit the form once complete.

Note: This help guide is a general explanation of the form and its fields. Please refer to the form's actual fields for specific requirements and guidelines.
