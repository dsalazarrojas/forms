# Retail Digital Payment Summit Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect information from attendees, organizers, or sponsors about their participation in the Retail Digital Payment Summit. This includes details about the event, the type of participation, attendee information, and any special requirements.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form sections and ensure you have all the necessary information.
2. Enter your event details in the "Event Details" section.
3. Select your participation type in the "Participation Type" section.
4. Enter your attendee details in the "Attendee Details" section.
5. If you have any special requirements, list them in the "Special Requirements" section.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Details**: Enter the name of the event, its date, and the location. This section is used to store event information for future reference.
* **Participation Type**: Select "Payment" if you're participating as a payment expert or "Participation" if you're participating as an attendee. This field helps us determine your role in the event.
* **Attendee Details**: Enter your full name, title, and organization name. This information will be used to identify you as an attendee and contact you for further details.
* **Special Requirements**: If you have any specific requirements or requests for the event, list them in this section. Examples include dietary restrictions, accessibility needs, or any special equipment you may need.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out the form accurately and thoroughly, as this information will be used to manage your registration and participation.
* If you're unsure about any field or section, contact the event organizers for clarification.
* This form is for the Retail Digital Payment Summit. If you're not participating in this event, please do not fill out this form.
