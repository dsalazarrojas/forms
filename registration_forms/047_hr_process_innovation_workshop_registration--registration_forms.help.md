# HR Process Innovation Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The HR Process Innovation Workshop Registration form is used to collect attendee information for the HR Process Innovation Workshop. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the form with your information as prompted.
2. Review your submission carefully before submitting it.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Form Id** (1, Number, Required: false)
    This field is not currently used in this form.
* **Name** (2, Text, Required: false)
    Enter your full name as you would like it to appear on the workshop materials and communication.
* **Email** (3, Email, Required: false)
    Enter your email address where you can be contacted by the workshop organizers.
* **Phone** (4, Text, Required: false)
    Enter your phone number for any follow-up communications or emergency contact.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter your information accurately to ensure smooth communication and materials distribution.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact the workshop organizers directly.
