# Developer Meetup Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Developer Meetup Signup Form is designed to collect information from developers about their meetups. This includes details about the meetup, such as its start and end dates and times, location, and organizer's contact information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your meetup details, such as the start and end dates and times, location, and attendee count.
2. Choose the registration type for your meetup.
3. Enter your name and email address.
4. Enter your phone number (optional).
5. Add any additional notes about your meetup.
6. Select the location for your meetup.
7. Enter your name and email address as the meetup organizer.
8. Enter the organizer's phone number (optional).
9. Add any additional notes about your meetup as the organizer.
