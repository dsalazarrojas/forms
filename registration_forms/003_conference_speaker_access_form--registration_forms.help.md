# Conference Speaker Access Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is created to collect relevant information from speakers to provide them with the necessary access and accommodations during the conference. It is essential to fill out this form to ensure a smooth experience for all speakers.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form completely and accurately.
2. Enter your Full Name, Primary Email Address, and Cell Phone Number.
3. Provide your Organization or Institution Affiliation.
4. Enter the Title of Your Presentation.
5. If known, please enter the Session ID or Name.
6. Write a short professional bio (max 150 words).
7. Select your preferred Badge Pickup Date.
8. Specify any Specific Audio-Visual Requirements.
9. Indicate any Specific Dietary Restrictions (e.g., Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
10. Choose whether you require hotel accommodation.
11. Provide emergency contact information.
12. Confirm authorization to record and distribute your presentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`speaker_full_name`, text, required): Enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or conference badges.
* **Primary Email Address** (`email_address`, email, required): Enter your primary email address where you can be reached for conference-related communications.
* **Cell Phone Number** (`speaker_phone_number`, text, required): Enter your cell phone number to be used during the event. Please note that this number will be used for communication purposes only.
* **Organization or Institution Affiliation** (`affiliation_organization`, text, required): Enter your organization or institution name.
* **Title of Your Presentation** (`presentation_title`, text, required): Enter the title of your presentation.
* **Session ID or Name (if known)** (`session_id`, text, optional): If you are aware of the session ID or name, please enter it.
* **Short Professional Bio** (`speaker_brief_bio`, text, required): Write a short professional bio (max 150 words) for the conference program.
* **-- Event Access and Logistics --** (`section_logistics`, note, optional): This is a heading for the next section.
* **Preferred Badge Pickup Date** (`badge_pickup_date`, date, required): Select your preferred badge pickup date.
* **Specific Audio-Visual Requirements** (`audio_visual_needs`, text, optional): Specify any specific audio-visual requirements you may need during your presentation (e.g., laptop connections, wireless mics).
* **Any Specific Dietary Restrictions?** (`speaker_dietary_needs`, select_multiple, required): Choose any dietary restrictions you may have (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, etc.).
* **Do you require hotel accommodation?** (`accommodation_required`, select_one, required): Choose whether you require hotel accommodation.
* **Emergency Contact Name and Number** (`emergency_contact_info`, text, required): Provide emergency contact information.
* **Authorization to Record and Distribute Presentation?** (`release_authorization`, select_one, required): Confirm whether you authorize the recording and distribution of your presentation for the online conference archive.
