# Fundamentals Learning Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Fundamentals Learning Program Registration form is used to register students for a learning program. It collects information about the student, such as their name, contact details, and program enrollment status. This data helps us understand who is enrolling in our programs and track their progress.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your student's name in the "Student Name" field.
2. Provide your student's email address in the "Student E-mail" field. Please ensure that your email address is valid and up-to-date.
3. Enter your student's phone number in the "Student Phone" field. This will help us contact you for any updates or notifications.
4. Select the student's program from the "Student Program" dropdown menu. Choose the program that best fits your student's needs.
5. Enter the student's date of birth in the "Student Date of Birth" field.
6. Choose the start and end dates of the program for the student in the respective fields.
7. Finally, select the student's enrollment status from the "Enrollment Status" field. This will help us track their progress.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Student Name (text)**: Enter your student's full name in this field.
* **Student E-mail (email)**: Provide a valid and up-to-date email address for your student.
* **Student Phone (text)**: Enter your student's phone number.
* **Student Program (select_one)**: Select the program that best fits your student's needs.
* **Student Date of Birth (date)**: Enter your student's date of birth.
* **Start Date of Program (date)**: Enter the start date of the program for your student.
* **End Date of Program (date)**: Enter the end date of the program for your student.
* **Enrollment Status (select_multiple)**: Select the enrollment status for your student from the options provided.
