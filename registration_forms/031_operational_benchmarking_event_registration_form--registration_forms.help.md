# Operational Benchmarking Event Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect registration information for an operational benchmarking event. The purpose of the form is to gather personal details and event preferences from participants.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your name in the "Name" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Select your role in the "Role" field.
4. Type in your job title in the "Job Title" field.
5. Enter your department in the "Department" field.
6. Check if you are part of a team in the "Team" field.
7. Enter your contact number in the "Contact Number" field.
8. Type in your company name in the "Company" field.
9. Fill in your address in the "Address" field.
10. Select your country in the "Country" field.
11. Choose your timezone in the "Timezone" field.
12. Enter the date of the event in the "Date" field.
13. Enter the time of the event in the "Time" field.
14. Select the location of the event in the "Location" field.
15. Add a brief description of the event in the "Description" field (optional).
16. Add any notes or comments in the "Note" field (optional).
17. Click the "Submit" button to submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (ID: 1, Text): Enter your full name.
* **Email** (ID: 2, Email): Enter your email address.
* **Role** (ID: 3, Text): Select your role in the organization (e.g., Employee, Manager, etc.).
* **Job Title** (ID: 4, Text): Type in your job title (e.g., CEO, Developer, etc.).
* **Department** (ID: 5, Text): Enter your department (e.g., Sales, Marketing, etc.).
* **Team** (ID: 6, Select One): Check if you are part of a team.
* **Contact Number** (ID: 7, Text): Enter your contact number.
* **Company** (ID: 8, Text): Type in your company name.
* **Address** (ID: 9, Text): Fill in your address.
* **Country** (ID: 10, Text): Select your country.
* **Timezone** (ID: 11, Time): Choose your timezone.
* **Date** (ID: 12, Date): Enter the date of the event.
* **Time** (ID: 13, Time): Enter the time of the event.
* **Location** (ID: 14, Select Multiple): Select multiple locations (Yes/No).
* **Description** (ID: 15, Text): Add a brief description of the event (optional).
* **Note** (ID: 16, Note): Add any notes or comments (optional).
* **Submit** (ID: 17, Text): Click to submit your registration.
* **Cancel** (ID: 18, Text): Click to cancel your registration.
* **Back** (ID: 19, Text): Click to return to the previous page.
* **Help** (ID: 20, Text): Click for help or more information.
* **Reset** (ID: 21, Text): Click to reset the form.
* **Delete** (ID: 22, Text): Click to delete your registration (not currently implemented).
* **Update** (ID: 23, Text): Click to update your registration (not currently implemented).
* **Cancel Submit** (ID: 24, Text): Click to cancel your submission.
