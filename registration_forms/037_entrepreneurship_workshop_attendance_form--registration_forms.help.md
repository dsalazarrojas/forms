# Entrepreneurship Workshop Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants who are interested in attending an entrepreneurship workshop. It helps us understand the needs, expectations, and demographics of the attendees. This will enable us to tailor the workshop content and experience to their needs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out your First name.
2. Fill out your Last name.
3. Enter your Email address.
4. Provide your Date of birth.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name**: <span style="color: grey">Enter your first name as it appears on official documents.</span>
* **Last name**: <span style="color: grey">Enter your last name as it appears on official documents.</span>
* **Email**: <span style="color: grey">Enter a valid email address you use to receive updates and communication about the workshop.</span>
* **Date of birth**: <span style="color: grey">Enter the date you were born in the format DD/MM/YYYY (Day, Month, Year). If you're not comfortable sharing your date of birth, it's not a required field.</span>

## Tips
Please note that the form is optional and anonymous. However, we encourage you to fill it out to get the most out of the workshop experience. If you have any questions or need help with the form, contact us at form@gic.mx.
