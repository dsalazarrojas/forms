# Site Induction Register Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information from new site users before they start work on the site.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill out your First Name and Surname in the respective text fields.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field.
4. Select the Date of Induction from the calendar.
5. Answer the question "Induction By" with either "Yes" or "No".
6. Enter your Supervisor's Name and their Email address.
7. Optionally, add any additional Notes about your induction.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (1, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Surname** (2, text, required/optional): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (3, email, required/optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone** (4, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Induction** (5, date, required/optional): Select the date of your induction from the calendar.
* **Induction By** (6, select_multiple, required/optional): Answer either "Yes" or "No" to this question.
* **Supervisor Name** (7, text, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's name.
* **Supervisor Email** (8, email, required/optional): Enter your supervisor's email address.
* **Location** (9, text, required/optional): Enter any additional details about your location.
* **Notes** (10, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes about your induction.
