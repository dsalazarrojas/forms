# Driving Lesson Package Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Driving Lesson Package Registration" form is designed to collect necessary information from users who want to sign up for driving lessons.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Fill in the "driving_school_name" field with the name of the driving school you are registering with.
2. Select the payment method you prefer from the "payment_method" field.
3. Enter the total number of lessons you want to take in the "total_lessons" field.
4. Enter the price per lesson in the "lesson_price" field.
5. Select the type of package you want to purchase from the "package_type" field.
6. Enter the number of lessons you want to take in the "number_of_lessons" field.
7. Select the payment status of your package from the "payment_status" field.
8. Add any notes or comments in the "notes" field.
9. Enter your email address in the "email" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field.
11. Review your information and click "Submit" to complete the registration.
