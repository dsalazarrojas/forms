# Smart Climate Monitoring Program Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new Smart Climate Monitoring Programs. It allows users to provide essential information about their program, including objectives, locations, and equipment preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the Registration Form (Page 1) with the name of your Smart Climate Monitoring Program.
2. Describe your program's Objectives, providing details on what the program aims to achieve.
3. List the Locations where your program will be conducted.
4. Choose your preferred equipment for the program, selecting 'Yes' or 'No' options.
5. Select your preferred methods of communication for the program, choosing multiple options as needed.
6. State your Program Goals, providing a brief description of what the program aims to accomplish.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (Page 1)** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your Smart Climate Monitoring Program.
* **Objectives** (`objectives`, text, required: false): Describe the main objectives of your program, such as its goals, targets, or key results you aim to achieve.
* **Locations** (`locations`, text, required: false): List the specific locations where your program will be conducted, including cities, regions, or countries.
* **Equipment Preferences** (`equipment_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Choose how you prefer to manage equipment for your program:
	+ 'Yes' for automatic equipment management
	+ 'No' for manual equipment management
* **Communication Preferences** (`communication_preferences`, select_multiple, required: false): Select your preferred methods of communication for your program:
	+ 'Yes' for email
	+ 'No' for phone
	+ 'Yes' for SMS
	+ 'No' for other methods (e.g., social media)
* **Program Goals** (`program_goals`, text, required: false): Briefly describe what you aim to accomplish with your Smart Climate Monitoring Program, summarizing its key objectives and targets.

## Tips
* Be specific and clear when filling in the form to ensure your program's details are well-understood.
* Use the provided fields to ensure you cover all essential information about your Smart Climate Monitoring Program.
* Review and edit your responses before submitting the form to ensure accuracy and completeness.
