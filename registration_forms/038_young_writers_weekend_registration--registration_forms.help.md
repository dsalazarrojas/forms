# Young Writers Weekend Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Young Writers Weekend Registration form is designed to collect information from participants for the Young Writers Weekend event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your registration details on **page 1**.
2. Select your preferred workshop(s) on **page 2**.
3. Choose your payment status on **page 3**.
4. Click the **submit** button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **registration (Text)** (`<name>`, `<type>: text`, required: false): Enter your registration details in this field.
* **workshop (Select One)** (`<name>: workshop`, `<type>: select_one`, required: false): Select your preferred workshop. Please choose 'Yes' if you wish to attend the selected workshop.
* **payment (Select Multiple)** (`<name>: payment`, `<type>: select_multiple`, required: false): Choose your payment status. Select 'Yes' if you are paying or 'No' if you are not paying.
* **submit (Note)** (`<name>: submit`, `<type>: note`, required: false): Click this button to submit your registration.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in your registration details accurately and completely.
* Choose your workshop and payment status carefully, as they will determine your eligibility to attend the Young Writers Weekend event.
