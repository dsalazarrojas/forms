# Seminar Convention Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to register for the Seminar Convention. Please use this guide to fill out the form correctly.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Enter your first name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your last name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your email address in the Email field. Make sure it's a valid email address.
4. If you want to be contacted regarding the seminar, enter your phone number in the Phone field (optional).
5. If you are registering for a company, enter the name of your company in the Company field (optional).
6. Specify your role in the Role field.
7. If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their First Name in the Accompanying Person First Name field (optional).
8. If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their Last Name in the Accompanying Person Last Name field (optional).
9. If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their Email in the Accompanying Person Email field (optional).
10. If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their Phone in the Accompanying Person Phone field (optional).
11. Confirm if a payment method has been arranged for the seminar. If yes, select one or more from Payment Options.
12. Select one of the payment methods from Payment Options if you have confirmed payment arrangement.
## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`First Name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to communicate with you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, `text`, optional): If you would like to be contacted regarding the seminar, enter your phone number.
* **Company** (`Company`, `text`, optional): If you are registering for a company, enter the name of your company.
* **Role** (`Role`, `text`, required): Specify your role in the seminar.
* **Accompanying Person First Name** (`Accompanying Person First Name`, `text`, optional): If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their first name.
* **Accompanying Person Last Name** (`Accompanying Person Last Name`, `text`, optional): If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their last name.
* **Accompanying Person Email** (`Accompanying Person Email`, `email`, optional): If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their email address.
* **Accompanying Person Phone** (`Accompanying Person Phone`, `text`, optional): If an accompanying person is coming with you, enter their phone number.
* **Payment Method** (`Payment Method`, `select_multiple`, required): Confirm if a payment method has been arranged for the seminar.
* **Payment Options** (`Payment Options`, `select_one`, optional): If you have confirmed payment arrangement, select one or more of the payment methods from the options.
