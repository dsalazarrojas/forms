# hr-professional-collaboration-circle-registration-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The hr-professional-collaboration-circle-registration-form is a tool designed to collect information from HR professionals, managers, and team leaders for a professional collaboration circle. This form helps manage member registration, job details, and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the Form Name field.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field.
3. Provide your Job Title in the Job Title field.
4. Enter your Name in the Name field.
5. Select your Role from the Role field.
6. Enter your Department in the Department field.
7. Enter your Company in the Company field.
8. Specify your Location in the Location field.
9. Briefly describe your Job Description in the Job Description field.
10. Select the Job Type (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.) from the Job Type field.
11. Indicate the Job Status (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending, etc.) from the Job Status field.
12. Provide any additional Job Type Details if necessary.
13. List your Job Skills in the Job Skills field.
14. Mention your Job Experience in the Job Experience field.
15. Specify your Job Preferences in the Job Preferences field.
16. Indicate whether you are accepting candidates for your job in the Job Accepting Candidates field.
17. Briefly describe your Job Interviews in the Job Interviews field.
18. Mention your Job Offers in the Job Offers field.
19. Specify your Job Reports in the Job Reports field.
20. Provide your Job Performance metrics.
21. Mention your Job Training programs.
22. List your Job Mentors and Mentees.
23. Specify your Job Sponsorship details.
24. Answer any Job Questions you receive.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Name** (`form_name`, text, required/optional): Enter a unique name for the form to help identify it.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required/optional): Enter your professional email address.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, required/optional): Provide your job title or position.
* **Name** (`name`, text, required/optional): Enter your full name.
* **Role** (`role`, select_one, required/optional): Select your role within the organization (e.g., HR, Manager, Team Leader).
* **Department** (`department`, text, required/optional): Enter the department you belong to.
* **Company** (`company`, text, required/optional): Enter your company name.
* **Location** (`location`, text, required/optional): Specify your location or workplace.
* **Job Description** (`job_description`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your job responsibilities and role.
* **Job Type** (`job_type`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select multiple job types (e.g., Full-time, Part-time, Contract, etc.).
* **Job Status** (`job_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Indicate the status of your job (e.g., Active, Inactive, Pending, etc.).
* **Job Type Details** (`job_type_details`, text, required/optional): Provide additional details about your job type.
* **Job Skills** (`job_skills`, text, required/optional): List your professional skills.
* **Job Experience** (`job_experience`, text, required/optional): Mention your job experience and qualifications.
* **Job Preferences** (`job_preferences`, text, required/optional): Specify your job preferences and expectations.
* **Job Accepting Candidates** (`job_accepting_candidates`, text, required/optional): Indicate whether you are accepting candidates for your job.
* **Job Interviews** (`job_interviews`, text, required/optional): Briefly describe your job interview process.
* **Job Offers** (`job_offers`, text, required/optional): Mention any job offers you have received.
* **Job Reports** (`job_reports`, text, required/optional): Specify any job reports or reviews.
* **Job Performance** (`job_performance`, text, required/optional): Provide metrics on your job performance.
* **Job Training** (`job_training`, text, required/optional): Mention any job training programs you have completed.
* **Job Mentors** (`job_mentors`, text, required/optional): List your job mentors or supervisors.
* **Job Mentees** (`job_mentees`, text, required/optional): List any mentees or trainees under you.
* **Job Sponsorship** (`job_sponsorship`, text, required/optional): Specify any job sponsorship details.
* **Job Questions** (`job_questions`, text, required/optional): Answer any job questions you receive.

## Tips

* You can modify this form to suit your specific needs and requirements.
