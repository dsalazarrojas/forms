# Co Branded Campaign Lead Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Co Branded Campaign Lead Form is a comprehensive form designed to gather information from potential leads on their interest in partnering with the company. This form helps the company evaluate the lead's suitability and determine the best partnership opportunity.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Enter your Email Address in the Email Address field.
4. Enter your Phone Number in the Phone Number field.
5. Select the correct Submission Date of when you submitted this form.
6. Enter your Company Name in the Company Name field.
7. Enter your Job Title or Position in the Job Title or Position field.
8. Choose your Industry from the options provided.
9. Select the Company Size that best describes your company.
10. Choose your Campaign Interest Level from the options provided.
11. Enter your Partnership Goals in the Partnership Goals field.
12. Select your approximate Budget Range.
13. Choose your Campaign Timeline from the options provided.
14. (Optional) Enter your Target Audience in the Target Audience field.
15. (Optional) Enter any Additional Information in the Additional Information field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`lead_first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name** (`lead_last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email Address** (`lead_email`, email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (`lead_phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number for further communication.
* **Submission Date** (`lead_submission_date`, date, required): Select the date and time you submitted this form.
* **Company Name** (`company_name`, text, required): Enter your company's name.
* **Job Title or Position** (`job_title`, text, required): Enter your job title or position.
* **Industry** (`industry`, select_one, required): Select the industry your company belongs to.
* **Company Size** (`company_size`, select_one, required): Choose the size of your company.
* **Campaign Interest Level** (`campaign_interest`, select_one, required): Choose the level of your interest in the campaign.
* **Partnership Goals** (`partnership_goals`, text, required): Describe what you are looking for in a partnership.
* **Budget Range** (`budget_range`, select_one, required): Choose your approximate budget for the campaign.
* **Campaign Timeline** (`timeline`, select_one, required): Choose the expected campaign timeline.
* **Target Audience** (`target_audience`, text, optional): Enter your target audience (if any).
* **Additional Information** (`additional_info`, text, optional): Enter any further information or details.

Please fill out the form carefully and accurately, and don't hesitate to reach out if you need any assistance.
