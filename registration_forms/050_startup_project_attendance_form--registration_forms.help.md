# Startup Project Attendance Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Startup Project Attendance Form is used for tracking the attendance and details of participants in a project.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Project Name** field with the name of the project you are registering.
2. Enter the **Participant Name** of the person attending the project.
3. Enter the **Participant Email** of the participant.
4. Enter the **Participant Phone** number of the participant.
5. Select the **Project Status** of the project from the available options (Active, Inactive, Cancelled).
6. Enter the **Project Start Date** when the project began.
7. Enter your **Participant Goals** for the project.
8. Confirm that you have given your **Participant Consent** to participate in the project.
9. Confirm that you understand the **Project Consent**, which is likely an agreement to participate in the project.
10. Provide any **Additional Comments** about your participation in the project.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Project Name** (Text, Required): The name of the project you are registering.
* **Participant Name** (Text, Required): The name of the person attending the project.
* **Participant Email** (Email, Optional): The email address of the participant.
* **Participant Phone** (Text, Optional): The phone number of the participant.
* **Project Status** (Select Multiple, Optional): Select the status of the project from the available options (Active, Inactive, Cancelled).
* **Project Start Date** (Date, Optional): The date when the project began.
* **Participant Goals** (Text, Optional): Your personal goals for the project.
* **Participant Consent** (Select One, Optional): Confirm that you have given your consent to participate in the project.
* **Project Consent** (Select One, Optional): Confirm that you understand the project's terms and conditions.
* **Additional Comments** (Text, Optional): Provide any additional comments or information about your participation in the project.
