# Lawn Bowls Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect registration information from participants for lawn bowls events. It will be used to store and manage participant data for the purpose of organizing and administering events.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the instructions below:
1. Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the respective fields. These fields are required for identification purposes.
2. Enter your Email address in the Email field. This will be used for communication with the participant.
3. Provide your Phone number in the Phone field. This is for emergency contact purposes.
4. If you have a Birth Date, enter it in the Birth Date field. This will help organizers manage participant age restrictions.
5. Select your session preferences from the Session Preferences field. This will help organizers schedule sessions that fit your needs.
6. Enter the name and phone number of your emergency contact in the Emergency Contact Name and Emergency Contact Phone fields. This is for critical contact information in case of an emergency.
7. If you have any Medical Conditions, please provide the necessary information in the Medical Conditions field.
8. Finally, select the Consent field to confirm your participation. Please answer 'Yes' if you agree to the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (text, required): Enter your given name.
* **Last Name** (text, required): Enter your surname.
* **Email** (email, required): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (text, required): Enter your phone number for emergency contact purposes.
* **Birth Date** (date, optional): Enter your birth date if applicable.
* **Session Preferences** (select multiple, optional): Select one or more sessions that you would like to participate in. Please note that session availability is subject to change.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (text, optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact person.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (text, optional): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact person.
* **Medical Conditions** (text, optional): If you have any medical conditions, please provide the necessary information.
* **Consent** (select one, required): Select 'Yes' to confirm your participation and acknowledge the terms and conditions of the event.
