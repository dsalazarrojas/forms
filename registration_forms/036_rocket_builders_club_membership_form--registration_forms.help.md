# Rocket Builders Club Membership Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Rocket Builders Club Membership Form is designed to collect information from potential members to facilitate the registration process. This form is used to gather personal details, payment information, and membership preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the membership application page with any relevant information.
2. Proceed to the personal details page and provide accurate and up-to-date information.
3. On the payment information page, enter your payment details to secure your membership.
4. Finally, review your membership preferences and submit the form to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Membership Application
* **Membership Application** (`membership_application`, text, optional): This is the first page where you can provide any additional information you think will be helpful for the membership process.
* **Personal Details** (`personal_details`, text, optional): Please provide your personal details, including your name, address, and contact information.
* **Payment Information** (`payment_information`, text, optional): Enter your payment details, such as your credit card information or other accepted payment methods.
* **Membership Preferences** (`membership_preferences`, text, optional): Choose your preferred membership level, any special preferences, or additional options for your membership.

## Tips
* Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and up-to-date.
* Review each field carefully before submitting the form to avoid errors.
* If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to contact us at `form@gic.mx`.
