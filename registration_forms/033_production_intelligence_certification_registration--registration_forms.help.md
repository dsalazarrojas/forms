# Production Intelligence Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registration purposes, allowing individuals to register for a certification or membership program. It collects personal contact information, certification level, and other relevant details to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field for correctness before submitting the form.
3. Double-check that you have selected the correct certification level.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that you regularly check and use.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required): Enter a phone number that you can be reached at.
* **Certification Level** (`certification_level`, select_one, required): Select the level of certification you are interested in.
	+ Choose from the options: Level 1, Level 2.
* **Reason For Certification** (`reason_for_certification`, text, optional): Provide a brief explanation for why you are seeking certification (if not required).
* **Motivation** (`motivation`, text, optional): Share your motivation for seeking certification (if not required).

## Tips

* Ensure that all required fields are filled out correctly and accurately.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correctness.
* If you're unsure about any field, contact the registration team for clarification.
