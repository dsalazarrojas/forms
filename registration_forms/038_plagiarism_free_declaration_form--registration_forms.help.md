# Plagiarism Free Declaration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Plagiarism Free Declaration Form is used to declare that all work submitted is original and has not been plagiarized.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review the form carefully and fill in the required fields accurately.
2. Enter your name and ID in the `Author` and `Author ID` fields, respectively.
3. Enter your institution in the `Institution` field.
4. In the `Work Declaration` field, declare that all work submitted is original.
5. In the `Plagiarism Acknowledgement` field, declare that all work submitted has not been plagiarized.
6. Enter your submission details, including date and time of submission, in the `Submission Details` field.
7. Enter your submission location in the `Submission Location` field.
8. Reviewer comments and signature can be entered in the `Reviewer Comments` and `Reviewer Signature` fields, respectively.
9. Finally, sign the form with your `Author Signature`.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Form Header**: This is a note field that provides a brief introduction to the form.
* **Author**: Enter your name in this field. This is required to be filled in by the author.
* **Author ID**: Enter your ID in this field. This is required to be filled in by the author.
* **Institution**: Enter your institution in this field. This is required to be filled in by the author.
* **Work Declaration**: Declare that all work submitted is original in this field. This is a critical field to ensure that the work is plagiarism-free.
* **Plagiarism Acknowledgement**: Declare that all work submitted has not been plagiarized in this field. This is also a critical field to ensure that the work is plagiarism-free.
* **Submission Details**: Enter your submission details, including date and time of submission. This field is optional and can be used for additional information.
* **Submission Location**: Enter your submission location in this field. This field is optional and can be used for additional information.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any comments from the reviewer in this field. This field is optional.
* **Reviewer Signature**: Enter the reviewer's signature in this field. This field is optional.
* **Author Signature**: Enter your signature in this field. This field is required to be filled in by the author.
* **Submission Date**: Enter the date of submission in this field. This field is optional.
* **Submission Time**: Enter the time of submission in this field. This field is optional.
* **Reviewer Comments**: Enter any additional comments from the reviewer in this field. This field is optional.
* **Submission Location**: Enter the location where the submission was made in this field. This field is optional.

## Tips

* Please fill in all required fields accurately and honestly.
* Review the form carefully before submitting it.
* Ensure that you have declared that all work submitted is original and has not been plagiarized.
* Keep a record of your submission details for future reference.
* If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact [form@gic.mx](mailto:form@gic.mx).
