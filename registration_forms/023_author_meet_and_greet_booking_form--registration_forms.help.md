# Author Meet And Greet Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for users to book a meeting with an author and provide necessary information to facilitate a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Fill in your booking date and time in the "Booking Date" field.
* Provide your name and email address in the "Your Name" and "Email Address" fields.
* Enter your phone number in the "Phone Number" field.
* Select the author you are interested in from the list in the "Which Author Are You Interested In" field.
* Choose the date and time for the meet-and-greet event in the "Event Date" and "Event Time" fields.
* Provide the number of people in your group and their names in the "Number of Attendees in Your Group" and "Names of Group Members" fields.
* Check the box if you plan to purchase a book at the event in the "Will You Purchase Book at Event" field.
* If you have read any books by the author, list them in the "Which Author Books Do You Own" field.
* If you have a favorite book or piece by this author, enter it in the "Favorite Work by This Author" field.
* Choose how long you have been a fan of this author in the "How Long Have You Been a Fan" field.
* Enter the reason you want to attend the meet-and-greet event in the "Why Do You Want to Attend" field.
* If you have any questions for the author, enter them in the "Question for the Author" field.
* If you have any special accessibility needs, provide them in the "Accessibility Requirements" field.
* Check the boxes if you need any mobility, hearing, or other accommodations in the "Need Mobility Assistance", "Hearing Accommodation Needed", and "Other" fields.
* Choose how you learned about the event in the "How Did You Hear About This Event" field.
* Check the boxes if you give permission to take your photo or post on social media in the "Permission for Photo/Video" field.
* Check the box if you would like to sign up for the author's mailing list in the "Sign Up for Author Mailing List" field.
* Provide any additional comments in the "Additional Comments" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Booking Date**: When are you making this reservation?
* **Your Name**: Full name of attendee
* **Email Address**: Your email contact
* **Phone Number**: Your contact phone
* **Which Author Are You Interested In**: Name of the author
* **Event Date**: When is the meet-and-greet scheduled
* **Event Time**: What time slot would you prefer
* **Number of Attendees in Your Group**: How many people in your party
* **Names of Group Members**: List other attendees if multiple
* **Will You Purchase Book at Event**: Do you plan to buy a book
* **Which Author Books Do You Own**: List books you have read
* **Favorite Work by This Author**: What is your favorite book or piece
* **How Long Have You Been a Fan**: When did you discover this author
* **Why Do You Want to Attend**: What are you hoping to experience
* **Question for the Author**: Is there something you want to ask
* **Accessibility Requirements**: Any special accommodations needed
* **Need Mobility Assistance**: Wheelchair, accessible seating, etc
* **Hearing Accommodation Needed**: Do you need hearing assistance
* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: For any refreshments provided
* **How Did You Hear About This Event**: Where did you find out
* **Permission for Photo/Video**: Can we take your photo at event
* **Sign Up for Author Mailing List**: Would you like to subscribe
* **Additional Comments**: Anything else we should know
