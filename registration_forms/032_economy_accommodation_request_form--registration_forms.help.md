# Economy Accommodation Request Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Economy Accommodation Request Form is designed to help users request accommodation for a specific period. This form gathers information about check-in dates, special requests, accommodation type, and number of guests to provide users with the most suitable accommodation options.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Click on "Submit" to start the form.
2.  Complete all required fields marked with an asterisk (\*).
3.  Select the correct options for each field from the dropdown menus or checkboxes.
4.  Review your submission before clicking "Submit" to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Check In Dates** (`check_in_dates`, date, required): Enter the dates you would like to check in to the accommodation. This field is required to ensure we have the correct dates for your accommodation request.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, select_multiple, optional): Select all the special requests you have, such as 'Yes' if you have any, to help us provide you with the best possible accommodation options.
* **Accommodation Type** (`accommodation_type`, select_one, optional): Choose the accommodation type that suits your needs, such as Hotel, Hostel, or Airbnb.
* **Number of Guests** (`number_of_guests`, number, required): Enter the total number of guests that will be staying with you. This field is required to ensure we provide the correct accommodation size for your group.
