# Robotics Camp Booking Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register your child for a robotics camp. Please fill out the form with your information to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for your child and the camp staff.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Review your answers before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: This is the name of the parent or guardian who is registering their child.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Parent Email**: Enter a valid email address of the parent or guardian.
	+ Type: Email
	+ Required: False
* **Parent Phone**: Enter the phone number of the parent or guardian.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Child Name**: This is the name of the child who is attending the robotics camp.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False
* **Child Date of Birth**: Enter the date of birth of the child.
	+ Type: Date
	+ Required: False
* **Camp Name**: This is the name of the robotics camp that the child is attending.
	+ Type: Text
	+ Required: False

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all the required fields.
- Double-check your information before submitting the form.
- If you are already registered for this camp, you will be redirected to a confirmation page.
