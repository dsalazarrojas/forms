# Country Week Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Country Week Participation Form is designed to collect information from participants for the Country Week event. This form is not mandatory, and participants are free to fill it out as per their convenience. The gathered data will be used to keep a record of participant information for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the Country Week Participation Form, follow these steps:

*   Click on the form link provided to you (usually in an email invitation or on the event website).
*   Fill out the form with the required and optional fields as prompted.
*   Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
*   Once you're satisfied with your filled-out form, click the "Submit" button.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
###  Participant 1 Form
*   **Participant 1 Form** (`Participant 1 Form`, `Number`, Not Required):
    This field is the first part of the form where you can provide your unique participant number for the event. It's not compulsory, so you can fill it out or leave it blank.
*   **Participant 1 Email** (`Participant 1 Email`, `Email`, Not Required):
    Enter the email address of the participant. This field is not mandatory, but having it filled out will help us contact the participant in case of any communication.
*   **Participant Name** (`Participant Name`, `Text`, Not Required):
    Please enter the name of the participant.

## Tips
*   Make sure to fill out the form carefully, as any mistakes can lead to incorrect participant data.
*   If you're unsure about any field, please contact the event organizers for clarification.
*   If you have any issues while filling out the form, you can contact us at [support email or contact info].
