# Healthcare Professional Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from new hires in the healthcare industry to facilitate a smooth onboarding process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the New Hire Information page with your basic details.
2. Provide information about your job details.
3. Enter your contact information.
4. Answer questions about your medical license and certification.
5. Fill in the dates related to your medical certification and license.
6. Choose your preferred contact method.
7. Finally, enter your contact number and email address.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **New Hire Information (Text)**: Please enter your basic details such as name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Job Details (Text)**: Please enter a brief description of your job role and responsibilities.
* **Contact Information (Text)**: Please enter your contact details such as name, address, and other relevant information.
* **Medical License (Select One)**: Answer "Yes" if you hold a medical license, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Board Certification (Select One)**: Answer "Yes" if you hold a medical board certification, "No" otherwise.
* **Medical Board Certification (Select Multiple)**: Select all certifications held by you. For example, if you hold "Yes" for two certifications, please select both.
* **Medical Certification (Select Multiple)**: Select all certifications held by you.
* **Onboarding Status (Select One)**: Answer "Active" if you are currently onboarding, "Inactive" otherwise.
* **Medical Certification Date (Date)**: Enter the date you obtained your medical certification.
* **Medical License Date (Date)**: Enter the date you obtained your medical license.
* **Medical Certification Date (Date)**: Enter the date you obtained your last medical certification.
* **Contact Method (Select One)**: Choose how you would like to be contacted. Please select "Phone", "Email", or "Text".
* **Contact Number (Text)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Contact Email (Email)**: Enter your contact email address.
