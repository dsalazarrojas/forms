# Mascot Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Mascot Registration Form is designed to collect detailed information about a mascot and its characteristics. This form will help us better understand the mascot's features, personality, and traits.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in the required information:
	* User Name: Enter your name to associate it with the mascot registration.
	* Email: Enter your email address to contact you regarding the mascot registration.
	* Phone Number: Enter your phone number for any follow-up contact.
2. Choose the mascot's type:
	* Select "Yes" or "No" to confirm whether the mascot is of a certain type.
3. Describe the mascot:
	* Enter a brief description of the mascot.
4. Upload the mascot's image:
	* Upload a high-quality image of the mascot.
5. Upload the mascot's video:
	* Upload a video showcasing the mascot's personality or behavior.
6. Add more details about the mascot:
	* Name: Enter a name for the mascot.
	* Age: Enter the mascot's age.
	* Breed: Select the mascot's breed.
	* Gender: Select the mascot's gender.
	* Country: Enter the country where the mascot is from.
	* Hobby: Enter the mascot's hobby or interest.
	* Occupation: Enter the mascot's occupation.
	* Interest: Enter the mascot's interest.
	* Friends: Enter the number of friends the mascot has.
	* Family: Enter the mascot's family information.
	* Habitat: Enter the mascot's habitat or environment.
	* Language: Select the language the mascot speaks or understands.
	* Height: Enter the mascot's height.
	* Weight: Enter the mascot's weight.
	* Size: Enter the mascot's size.
	* Length: Enter the mascot's length.
	* Width: Enter the mascot's width.

## Tips
* Make sure to enter accurate and up-to-date information about the mascot.
* Use high-quality images and videos to showcase the mascot's personality and behavior.
* Be as detailed as possible when filling in the fields to provide the best possible information about the mascot.
