# distributor_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The distributor registration form is used to collect information from distributors who wish to partner with our company. This form helps us gather necessary details about the distributor's representative, including their name, contact information, order details, and payment information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Please enter the representative's name in the **Representative Name** field.
2. Enter the recruiter's name in the **Recruiter Name** field.
3. Select whether the order is **Yes** or **No** for each of the order details and payment details fields.
4. Enter the representative's email address in the **Representative Email** field.
5. Enter the representative's phone number in the **Representative Phone** field.
6. Select the order date and time in the **Order Date** and **Order Time** fields, respectively.
7. Select the payment terms for the order in the **Payment Terms** field.
8. Enter the distributor's name in the **Distributor Name** field.
9. Enter the distributor's address in the **Distributor Address** field.
10. Select the payment method for the order in the **Payment Method** field.
11. Select the payment date and time in the **Payment Date** and **Payment Time** fields, respectively.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Representative Name**: Enter the name of the representative who will be handling the order.
* **Recruiter Name**: Enter the name of the person who recruited the representative.
* **Order Details**: Select whether the order is Yes or No.
* **Payment Details**: Select whether the payment is Yes or No.
* **Terms and Conditions**: Enter any additional terms and conditions for the order.
* **Representative Email**: Enter the representative's email address.
* **Representative Phone**: Enter the representative's phone number.
* **Order Date**: Select the date the order was placed.
* **Order Time**: Select the time the order was placed.
* **Payment Terms**: Select whether the payment terms are Yes or No.
* **Distributor Name**: Enter the name of the distributor.
* **Distributor Address**: Enter the distributor's address.
* **Payment Method**: Select the payment method used for the order.
* **Payment Date**: Select the date of the payment.
* **Payment Time**: Select the time of the payment.
