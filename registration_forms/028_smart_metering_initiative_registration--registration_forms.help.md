**Smart Metering Initiative Registration - Help Guide**

## Purpose
The Smart Metering Initiative Registration form is a critical tool for participants to register for the Smart Metering Initiative. This form collects essential information to ensure accurate participant data and provide a seamless experience. Please take a moment to review the form carefully to ensure accurate and complete submission.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Review each section carefully and ensure you have all necessary information before proceeding.
2. Fill out the fields as instructed and provide accurate information.
3. Make sure to choose the correct contact method for communication from the "Preferred Contact Method" dropdown.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name (form_first_name)**: Enter your first name as it appears on official documents. This is an optional field, but please provide it for accurate identification.
* **Last Name (form_last_name)**: Enter your last name as it appears on official documents. This is an optional field, but please provide it for accurate identification.
* **Email (form_email)**: Enter a valid email address for communication from the Smart Metering Initiative. This will be the primary contact method unless you select otherwise in the "Preferred Contact Method" field.
* **Phone Number (form_phone)**: Enter your phone number for secondary contact. Please ensure it is a valid number for communication.
* **Date of Birth (form_date_of_birth)**: Enter your date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy. This will help us tailor the initiative to your specific needs.
* **Participant ID (form_participant_id)**: Enter your unique participant ID, if you have one. If not, do not worry, you can register without it.
* **Consent (form_consent)**: Please select "I understand and agree to the terms and conditions" to proceed with your registration. This confirms your understanding and acceptance of the initiative's terms.
* **Preferred Contact Method (form_preferred_contact_method)**: Select one or multiple methods for communication: Email, Phone, or Text Message. This will ensure you receive updates and notifications from the Smart Metering Initiative.
* **Comments (form_comments)**: If you have any additional comments or information to share, please type them here.
* **Additional Notes (form_additional_notes)**: Enter any additional notes or details about your registration or participation in the initiative.
