# Summer School Completion Discharge Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for discharging students from summer school programs. It helps school administrators gather necessary information to update student records and ensure a smooth transition for students returning to regular school or moving on to other educational programs.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the student's name in the `student_name` field.
2. Enter the student's number in the `student_number` field (if applicable).
3. Select the correct school term the student was in.
4. Select the school year the student was in.
5. Choose the student's level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
6. Choose the student's status (Active or Inactive).
7. Enter the teacher's name, number, and contact information (if applicable).
8. Enter the date of discharge.
9. Add any relevant notes.
10. Enter the information of the school administrator who is submitting the form.
11. Enter the contact information of the school administrator who is submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Student Name (`student_name`, **text**, required)**: Enter the student's name as it appears on their records.
* **Student Number (`student_number`, **number**, optional)**: Enter the student's unique number (if applicable).
* **School Name (`school_name`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the name of the school the student attended.
* **School Term (`school_term`, **select_one**, optional)**: Select the school term the student was in.
* **School Year (`school_year`, **select_multiple**, optional)**: Select the school year the student was in.
* **Student Level (`student_level`, **select_one**, optional)**: Choose the student's level (Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3).
* **Student Status (`student_status`, **select_multiple**, optional)**: Choose the student's status (Active or Inactive).
* **Teacher Name (`teacher_name`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the teacher's name.
* **Teacher Number (`teacher_number`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the teacher's number (if applicable).
* **Teacher Email (`teacher_email`, **email**, optional)**: Enter the teacher's email.
* **Teacher Phone (`teacher_phone`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the teacher's phone number.
* **School Address (`school_address`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the address of the school.
* **Date of Discharge (`date_of_discharge`, **date**, optional)**: Enter the date of discharge.
* **Note (`note`, **note**, optional)**: Add any relevant notes about the student's discharge.
* **Submitted By (`submitted_by`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the name of the person submitting the form.
* **Submitted On (`submitted_on`, **time**, optional)**: Enter the time the form was submitted.
* **School Admin Name (`school_admin_name`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the name of the school administrator submitting the form.
* **School Admin Number (`school_admin_number`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the number of the school administrator submitting the form.
* **School Admin Email (`school_admin_email`, **email**, optional)**: Enter the email of the school administrator submitting the form.
* **School Admin Phone (`school_admin_phone`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the phone number of the school administrator submitting the form.
* **School Admin Title (`school_admin_title`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the title of the school administrator submitting the form.
* **Parent Name (`student_parent_name`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the parent's name.
* **Parent Number (`student_parent_number`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the parent's number (if applicable).
* **Parent Phone (`student_parent_phone`, **text**, optional)**: Enter the parent's phone number.
* **Parent Email (`student_parent_email`, **email**, optional)**: Enter the parent's email.

Note to self-administrators: This form is meant to be completed with the most up-to-date information available. Please ensure to fill it out thoroughly and accurately to facilitate a smooth transition for the student.
