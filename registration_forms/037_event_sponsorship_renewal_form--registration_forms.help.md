# event-sponsorship-renewal-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The event sponsorship renewal form is designed to help event sponsors renew their sponsorship of an event. This form will guide you through the necessary steps to complete the renewal process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Review the form to ensure you are the intended user.
2. Fill in the required fields (if any) according to your needs.
3. Choose the correct options for each field.
4. Double-check your answers to ensure accuracy.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Sponsorship Renewal Form**: This form is used to confirm that you are renewing your sponsorship.
* **Event Name**: Select the name of the event you are renewing your sponsorship for.
* **Event Date**: Enter the date of the event for which you are renewing your sponsorship.
* **Sponsor Name**: Select your name as the sponsor.
* **Sponsor Information**: Choose the correct options for your sponsorship status:
	+ `Yes` if you are renewing your sponsorship.
	+ `No` if you are not renewing your sponsorship.
* **Renewal Reason**: Choose the reason for renewal:
	+ `Yes` if you are renewing for a specific reason.
	+ `No` if you are not renewing for a reason.
* **Sponsorship Renewal Form**: Confirm that you are submitting this form to renew your sponsorship.
