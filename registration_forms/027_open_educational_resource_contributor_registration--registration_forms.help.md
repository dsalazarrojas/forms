# open_educational_resource_contributor_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to register open educational resource contributors for onboarding purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Fill in the required information such as your Full Name, Institution, Contact Information, and Expertise.
2.  Select your Contribution Types, Expertise Categories, and Contribution Frequency to help us understand your contribution style.
3.  Choose your Contribution Format, Language, and License for your contributions.
4.  Provide a brief Contribution Description and URL if applicable.
5.  Select your Contributor Role and choose your preferred Onboarding Date and Time.
6.  Fill in any additional Notes if you have any specific requirements or preferences.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Onboarding Information** (Text): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information about your onboarding process or preferences.
* **Full Name** (Text): Please fill in your name as it appears on your official identification documents.
* **Institution** (Text): Please fill in the name of your institution, school or organization you are affiliated with.
* **Contact Information** (Text): Please provide your email and any other relevant contact details.
* **Phone** (Text): Please fill in your phone number for us to contact you.
* **Expertise** (Text): Please describe your area of expertise in the field of open educational resources.
* **Contribution Details** (Text): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional details about your contribution.
* **Contribution Types** (Select Multiple): Please select all the types of contributions you are willing to make (e.g., creating resources, editing, translating, adapting).
* **Expertise Categories** (Select Multiple): Please select all the categories that best describe your expertise (e.g., Category 1, Category 2, Category 3).
* **Contribution Frequency** (Select One): Please select how frequently you are willing to contribute (e.g., Frequently, Occasionally, Rarely, Never).
* **Contribution Format** (Select One): Please select the format in which you are willing to contribute (e.g., Yes, No).
* **Contribution Language** (Select One): Please select the language in which you are willing to contribute (e.g., English, Spanish, French).
* **Contribution License** (Select One): Please select the license under which you are willing to contribute (e.g., CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, CC-BY-NC).
* **Contribution License Notes** (Text): This field is optional and can be used to provide any additional information about your contribution license.
* **Contributor Role** (Select One): Please select your role as a contributor (e.g., Author, Editor, Translator, Adapter).
* **Contribution Description** (Text): Please provide a brief description of your contribution (e.g., what you are willing to contribute, how you can contribute).
* **Contribution URL** (Text): If you have a URL for your contribution, please provide it here.
* **Onboarding Date** (Date): Please select a date for your onboarding process.
* **Onboarding Time** (Time): Please select a time for your onboarding process.
* **Last Onboarding Date** (Date): If you have previously onboarded, please select the date of your last onboarding process.
* **Last Onboarding Time** (Time): If you have previously onboarded, please select the time of your last onboarding process.
* **Next Onboarding Date** (Date): If you have a next onboarding schedule, please select the date.
* **Next Onboarding Time** (Time): If you have a next onboarding schedule, please select the time.
* **Notes** (Note): Please add any additional notes or preferences you have for your onboarding process.
