# Pole Bending Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Pole Bending Event Registration form is used to collect participant information and payment details for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your participant information in the "Participant Information" field.
2. Enter a valid email address in the "Email" field. This field is required.
3. Enter a phone number in the "Phone Number" field (optional).
4. Select a payment method from the options provided in the "Payment Method" field. This field is required.
5. Enter the total cost of your participation in the "Total Cost" field (optional).
6. Add any additional participant notes in the "Participant Notes" field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Participant Information
* **Participant Information** (`participant_information`, text, optional): Enter any additional information about yourself as a participant.
### Email
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
### Phone Number
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, optional): Enter your phone number for our records.
### Payment Method
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_one, required): Select a payment method from the options provided:
	+ Cash
	+ Card
	+ Transfer
### Total Cost
* **Total Cost** (`total_cost`, number, optional): Enter the total cost of your participation (e.g., $100).
### Participant Notes
* **Participant Notes** (`participant_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes about your participation.
