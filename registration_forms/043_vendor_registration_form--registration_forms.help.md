# Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect and verify vendor information for potential partnerships or collaborations with our organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Review and double-check your input before submitting the form.
3. Submit the form to initiate the registration process.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Vendor Name** (1): Please enter the name of your company or organization.
* **Vendor Email** (2): Enter your company's or organization's email address. This will be used for communication and account creation.
* **Phone Number** (3): Provide your company's or organization's phone number for communication purposes.
* **Business Name** (4): Enter the name of your business or organization.
* **Address** (5): Provide the physical address of your company or organization.
* **Service Description** (6): Briefly describe the services your company or organization offers.
* **Website** (7): If applicable, provide the URL of your company or organization's website.
* **Contact Person** (8): Enter the name and title of the person handling contact with our organization.
