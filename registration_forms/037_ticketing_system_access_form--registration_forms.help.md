# ticketing_system_access_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The ticketing_system_access_form is used to access the ticketing system. Users can use this form to enter information related to tickets, including user info, contact info, and ticket details.

## How To Complete This Form
To access the ticketing system, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields with the necessary information.
2. Review the entire form for accuracy before submitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Info (1)**: Enter your username or other identifying information.
* **Contact Info (2)**: Enter your contact details, such as name and address.
* **email (3)**: Enter your email address.
* **Password (4)**: Enter your password to access the system.
* **Role (5)**: Select your role in the ticketing system.
* **Ticket Number (6)**: Enter the ticket number if you know it.
* **Date (7)**: Select the date related to the ticket.
* **Time (8)**: Select the time related to the ticket.
* **Ticket ID (9)**: Enter the ticket ID if you have it.
* **Ticket Status (10)**: Select the status of the ticket (Active or Inactive).
* **Notes (11)**: Enter any additional notes or comments about the ticket.
* **Phone (12)**: Enter your phone number.
* **Confirm Password (13)**: Confirm your password to ensure accuracy.

## Tips
* Double-check all fields for accuracy before submitting.
* If you're unsure about any field, refer to your ticketing system documentation or contact the system administrator.
