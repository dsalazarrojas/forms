# Donor Impact Showcase Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Donor Impact Showcase Registration Form is designed for donors to share their impact stories and experiences with our organization. By filling out this form, you will be able to create a unique showcase of your involvement and share it with others.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. **Provide your name**:
   - Fill out the "What is your name?" field with your real name.
   - This field is required to ensure that we can identify you as the donor.

2. **Share your story**:
   - Fill out the "What is your story?" field with a brief description of your experience or impact story.
   - This field is required and should be a concise and clear account of your story.

3. **Choose your event type**:
   - Select from the options provided in "What type of event are you showcasing?" to classify the type of event you're sharing.
   - This field is required, and your selection will help us understand the context of your story.

4. **Provide event details**:
   - Fill out "What is the event date?" with the actual date of the event.
   - Fill out "What is the start time of the event?" and "What is the end time of the event?" with the corresponding times of the event.
   - These fields are all required and will help us understand the duration and timing of your event.

5. **Specify event duration**:
   - Fill out "What is the event duration?" with the length of time the event took place.
   - This field is required and will help us understand the scope of your event.

6. **Specify event location**:
   - Fill out "What is the event location?" with the location where the event took place.
   - This field is required and will help us understand the context of your event.

7. **Add additional information**:
   - Fill out the "Additional Information" field with any extra details you'd like to share about your event.
   - This field is optional and is meant to provide more context or information about your story.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **What is your name?** (required, text): 
  - Enter your full name to identify yourself as the donor.

* **What is your story?** (required, text): 
  - Share a brief and clear account of your experience or impact story.

* **What type of event are you showcasing?** (required, select_one): 
  - Choose one of the provided options to classify the type of event you're sharing.

* **What is the event date?** (required, date): 
  - Enter the actual date of the event.

* **What is the start time of the event?** (required, time): 
  - Enter the start time of the event.

* **What is the end time of the event?** (required, time): 
  - Enter the end time of the event.

* **What is the event duration?** (required, number): 
  - Enter the length of time the event took place.

* **What is the event location?** (required, text): 
  - Enter the location where the event took place.

* **Additional Information** (optional, text): 
  - Provide any extra details you'd like to share about your event.
