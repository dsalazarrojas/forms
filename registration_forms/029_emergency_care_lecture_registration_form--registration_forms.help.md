# Emergency Care Lecture Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Emergency Care Lecture Registration Form is designed to collect the necessary information from individuals who wish to attend the Emergency Care Lecture. This form is used to gather registration details such as name, contact information, and medical license details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
4. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
5. If applicable, enter your medical license number in the "Medical License" field.
6. If applicable, enter your medical insurance information in the "Medical Insurance" field.
7. Enter the name of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Name" field.
8. Enter the phone number of your emergency contact in the "Emergency Contact Phone" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First name, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (Last name, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (Email, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (Phone, text, required): Enter your phone number.
* **Medical License** (Medical License, text, optional): If you have a medical license, enter the number here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Medical Insurance** (Medical Insurance, text, optional): If you have medical insurance, enter the details here. If not, leave this field blank.
* **Emergency Contact Name** (Emergency Contact Name, text, required): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (Emergency Contact Phone, text, required): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
