# Homeless Shelter Accommodation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Homeless Shelter Accommodation Form is a tool used to gather information from individuals seeking accommodation at a shelter. This form helps the shelter staff understand the applicant's needs and provide necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your Registration Date (page 1).
2. Enter your First Name and Last Name (page 2).
3. Provide your Date of Birth (page 3).
4. Select your Gender (page 4).
5. Enter your Contact Number and Emergency Contact Name and Number (pages 5-7).
6. Provide your Address (page 8).
7. Explain the Reason for Homelessness (page 9).
8. Specify the Length of Stay Request (page 10).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Date (Date)**: The date you are registering for shelter.
* **First Name (Text)**: Your first name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Your last name, as it appears on your identification.
* **Date of Birth (Date)**: The date of your birth.
* **Gender (Select One)**: Your gender, which can be Male, Female, Other, or Prefer not to specify.
* **Contact Number (Text)**: A number where the shelter staff can contact you.
* **Emergency Contact Name (Text)**: The name of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Emergency Contact Number (Text)**: The number of the person to contact in case of an emergency.
* **Address (Text)**: The address where you are currently living or wish to live.
* **Reason for Homelessness (Text)**: A brief explanation of why you are experiencing homelessness.
* **Length of Stay Request (Number)**: The number of days or weeks you are requesting to stay at the shelter.

## Tips

* Make sure to fill in all the fields accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unable to provide the registration date, please explain the reason for this in the Reason for Homelessness field.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask shelter staff for assistance.
