# Vaping Cessation Support Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to help you register for support to quit vaping. It will help us understand your needs and preferences so we can provide you with the best possible assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your personal details.
2. Choose your preferred method of support.
3. Indicate your desired support frequency.
4. Select your reason for quitting.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`First Name`, text, required): Enter your first name as it is spelled on your official identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`Last Name`, text, required): Enter your last name as it is spelled on your official identification documents.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address where we can contact you.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required): Enter your phone number where we can contact you.
* **Date of Birth** (`Date of Birth`, date, required): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Address** (`Address`, text, required): Enter your residential address where we can send you support materials.
* **Preferred Support Method** (`Preferred Support Method`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Counseling
	+ Text Messaging
	+ Online Program
	+ Phone
	+ Other
	+ None
* **Support Frequency** (`Support Frequency`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ Daily
	+ Weekly
	+ Bi-Weekly
	+ Monthly
	+ Rarely
* **Quit Reason** (`Quit Reason`, select_one, required): Select one of the following options:
	+ To improve my health
	+ To set a good example
	+ To quit smoking and vaping
	+ To be a role model
	+ Other
	+ None

## Tips

* Please make sure to fill out the form accurately and completely.
* If you have any questions or concerns, contact us at [support email/phone number].
* We will use the information you provide to offer you tailored support to help you quit vaping.
