# social_prescribing_program_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in the Social Prescribing Program. It is used to collect information about participants, including their contact details, interests, and wellbeing status. 

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

- Fill in your name in the "participant_name" field.
- Enter your email address in the "participant_email" field.
- Enter your phone number in the "participant_phone" field.
- Select your top three interests from the "interest_1", "interest_2", and "interest_3" dropdown lists.
- Answer "yes" or "no" to the wellbeing question "wellbeing_question" and provide a score out of 10 for "wellbeing_score" if required.
- Add any additional comments in the "submission_comment" field.
- Select any additional submission questions or files that apply to you.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **participant_name**: Enter your name as it appears on your identification documents. (text)
* **participant_email**: Enter your email address that we can use to contact you. (email)
* **participant_phone**: Enter your phone number that we can use to contact you. (text)
* **interest_1**: Select your top interest (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **interest_2**: Select your second interest (e.g. Option 1, Option 2, Option 3)
* **interest_3**: Select your third interest (e.g. Option 4, Option 5, Option 6) (optional)
* **consent**: Not applicable in this form.
* **wellbeing_question**: Answer "yes" or "no" to this question. 
* **wellbeing_score**: If you answered "yes" to "wellbeing_question", enter a score out of 10 for your wellbeing. (number)
* **submission_comment**: Add any additional comments or information about your submission. (text)
* **submission_question**: Select any additional questions or options that apply to you (e.g. submission Option 1, submission Option 2)
* **submission_file**: If you have any supporting files, upload them here (e.g. document, image, video). 

Please note that some fields are marked as "required", which means you must fill them in to complete the form. If you are unsure about any field, please contact us for assistance.
