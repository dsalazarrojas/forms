# Immunization Permission Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Immunization Permission Form is a document that collects permission from parents or guardians for their child's immunization. It is used by schools to gather necessary information to administer vaccinations to their students.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name as the parent or guardian.
2. Enter your child's name.
3. Enter your child's date of birth.
4. Enter the name of your child's school.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Parent Name**: Enter your full name as the parent or guardian of the child.
* **Child Name**: Enter your child's full name, as it appears on their school records.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your child's date of birth in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **School Name**: Enter the name of your child's school where they are enrolled.

## Tips
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (Parent Name and Child Name).
- If you are unsure about any of the information, contact the school's administration for clarification.
- Ensure that you have signed and returned the form to the school to give them the necessary permission.
