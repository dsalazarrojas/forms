# Virtual Course Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Virtual Course Registration Form is a user-friendly form designed to collect necessary details for course registration. It is a straightforward form with a minimal number of fields, making it easy to fill out and submit.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the **Course Name** field with the exact name of the course you are interested in registering for.
2. Enter your **Student Name** in the designated field.
3. Provide your **Email** address, which will be used for communication about your course enrollment.
4. Enter your **Phone** number for contact purposes.
5. Select your **Date Of Birth** from the calendar or manually enter it in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd).
6. Choose the type of **Course Type** that best describes your course preference (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
7. Select one or more **Course Level** that suits your skills and experience (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
8. Choose the primary **Course Language** you are comfortable with (English, Spanish, or French).
9. Enter the **Course Duration** you are expecting for the course, if applicable.
10. Enter the **Course Fees** you are willing to pay, if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Course Name**: Enter the name of the course you are interested in registering for. This helps us to accurately match your preferences with the available course options.
* **Student Name**: Please fill out your full name, as this is the name that will be used for course communication.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that you check regularly. This will be used for important updates and notifications.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number for us to contact you in case of any last-minute updates or changes.
* **Date Of Birth**: Select or enter your date of birth in the correct format (yyyy-mm-dd). This helps us to determine your age and eligibility for the course.
* **Course Type**: Choose the type of course that best suits your learning style (Online, Offline, or Hybrid).
* **Course Level**: Select one or more levels that match your skills and experience (Basic, Intermediate, or Advanced).
* **Course Language**: Choose the primary language you are comfortable with for the course (English, Spanish, or French).
* **Course Duration**: Enter the expected duration of the course, if applicable. This is not required but helps us to plan your course schedule.
* **Course Fees**: Enter the amount you are willing to pay for the course, if applicable. This helps us to determine your payment options.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill out all required fields accurately.
* Double-check your email address and phone number for correct formatting.
* Select the correct options for each field to ensure you get the most relevant course recommendations.
