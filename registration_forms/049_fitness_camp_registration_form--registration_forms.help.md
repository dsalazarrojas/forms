# fitness_camp_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The fitness camp registration form is a user-facing form designed to collect demographic information from individuals interested in joining a fitness camp program. This form is intended for potential campers and should not be used by camp directors or staff.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Enter your **First Name** in the first text field.
2. Enter your **Last Name** in the second text field.
3. Enter your **Email Address** in the third text field. Please ensure it is a valid email address.
4. Enter your **Phone Number** in the last text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required: false): Enter your last name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter a valid email address where you can be contacted. This will be used for important updates and notifications.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number where you can be reached.

## Tips
* Ensure that you enter accurate and valid information, as it will be used to create or update your registration.
* If you are unable to complete the form at this time, you can save your progress and return to it later.
* If you are a camp director or staff, please refer to the "Camp Director Registration Form" for your registration purposes.
