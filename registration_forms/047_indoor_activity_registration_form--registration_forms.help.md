# indoor_activity_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The indoor activity registration form is designed to collect information from participants for indoor activities. Please provide accurate and complete information to ensure efficient communication and organization.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the indoor location where the activity will take place.
2. Provide the participant's details, including name and any other relevant information.
3. Choose your event preferences from the available options.
4. Mention any participant preferences you may have.
5. Select the date and time of the activity.
6. Enter your email address for communication purposes.
7. Enter your phone number for any further communication.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **Indoor Location** (`indoor_location`, `text`, required: false): Enter the address or description of the indoor location where the activity will take place.
- **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, required: false): Provide a brief description of the participant, including name, if desired.
- **Event Preferences** (`event_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Choose the event preferences that apply to you from the options provided.
- **Participant Preferences** (`participant_preferences`, `text`, required: false): Mention any participant preferences you may have, such as dietary restrictions or accessibility needs.
- **Date** (`date`, `date`, required: false): Select the date of the activity.
- **Time** (`time`, `time`, required: false): Select the time of the activity.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number for any further communication.
