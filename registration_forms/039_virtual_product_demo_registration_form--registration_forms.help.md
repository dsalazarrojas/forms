# Virtual Product Demo Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Virtual Product Demo Registration Form is used to collect registration information from customers or prospects interested in exploring a product or service. This information is used to schedule a demo session and capture relevant details about the registrants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your first name and last name in the corresponding fields.
2. Provide your email address in the `email` field.
3. Choose your company from the available options, if applicable.
4. Select your job title and job function.
5. Choose your preferred schedule for the demo session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
6. Choose the available slots for the demo session from the list of options.
7. Select a date for the demo session.
8. Choose the start and end times for the demo session.
9. Provide your contact number.
10. Add any additional notes or comments.
11. Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
12. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your email address.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): If you're representing a company, enter the company name.
* **Job Title** (`job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Enter your job function.
* **Schedule** (`schedule`, select_one, required): Choose your preferred schedule for the demo session (Morning, Afternoon, or Evening).
* **Available Slots** (`available_slots`, select_multiple, optional): Choose the available slots for the demo session.
* **Appointment Date** (`appointment_date`, date, required): Select a date for the demo session.
* **Appointment Time** (`appointment_time`, time, required): Choose the start and end times for the demo session.
* **Contact Number** (`contact_number`, text, required): Enter your contact number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Add any additional notes or comments.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, optional): Add any additional comments.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, note, required): Review and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **User Agreement** (`user_agreement`, note, required): Review and agree to the user agreement.
* **Product Description** (`product_description`, text, optional): Add a brief description of the product.
* **Demo Link** (`demo_link`, text, optional): Add a link to the demo session.
* **Demo Link URL** (`demo_link_url`, text, optional): Add the URL of the demo link.
* **Demo Start Time** (`demo_start_time`, time, optional): Choose the start time for the demo session.
* **Demo End Time** (`demo_end_time`, time, optional): Choose the end time for the demo session.
* **Demo Duration** (`demo_duration`, number, optional): Enter the duration of the demo session.
* **Demo Schedule** (`demo_schedule`, select_one, optional): Choose the demo schedule (Online, Phone, or In-person).
* **Demo Notes** (`demo_notes`, note, optional): Add any additional demo notes.
* **Submit** (`submit`, text, required): Review and submit the form.

Note: All fields marked as required must be filled in before submitting the form.
