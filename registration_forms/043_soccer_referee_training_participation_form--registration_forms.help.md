# Soccer Referee Training Participation Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Soccer Referee Training Participation Form is designed to help users register for a soccer referee training program. This form collects information from potential or existing referees for administrative purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Enter your First name in the First name field.
2. Enter your Last name in the Last name field.
3. Enter your Email in the Email field.
4. Enter your Contact number in the Contact number field.
5. Select the Training date for which you are registering for training.
6. Describe your previous Referee experience in the Referee experience field.
7. Review and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (Text, Required/Optional: false): Enter your first name here.
* **Last name** (Text, Required/Optional: false): Enter your last name here.
* **Email** (Email, Required/Optional: false): Enter your email address here. This will be used for verification or communication purposes.
* **Contact number** (Text, Required/Optional: false): Enter your contact number here. This will be used to contact you for further information.
* **Training date** (Date, Required/Optional: false): Select the date for which you are registering for training.
* **Referee experience** (Text, Required/Optional: false): Describe your previous experience as a referee, including any relevant certifications or training you have received. This will help the training program provide you with the best possible experience.
