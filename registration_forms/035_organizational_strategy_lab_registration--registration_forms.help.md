# Organizational Strategy Lab Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide is to help participants understand how to complete the Organizational Strategy Lab registration form.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the Registration Form (Page 1) with your details.
2. Enter your participant details (Page 2).
3. Specify your goals for attending the lab session (Page 3).
4. Select your session preferences (Page 4).
5. Click the "Submit" button (Page 5) to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Registration Form (Page 1)**: 
  * **Name** (`registration_form`, text, required: false): Enter your name.
* **Participant Details (Page 2)**: 
  * **Participant Details (`participant_details`, text, required: false)**: Provide any additional information about yourself that is relevant to your participation in the lab session.
* **Goals Form (Page 3)**: 
  * **Goals Form (`goals_form`, text, required: false)**: Specify your goals and objectives for attending the lab session. This will help us tailor the session to your needs.
* **Session Preferences (Page 4)**: 
  * **Session Preferences (`session_preferences`, text, required: false)**: Choose your preferred session options, such as date and time.
* **Submit (Page 5)**: 
  * **Submit Button (`submit_button`, text, required: false)**: Click this button to submit your registration.
