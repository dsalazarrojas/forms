# Beverage Export Program Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Beverage Export Program Registration form is designed for individuals to register for a beverage export program. This form collects personal information and preferences to facilitate the registration process.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal information, including full name, email address, and phone number.
2. Select the relevant date for your registration.
3. Choose your primary selection from the options provided (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
4. If required, provide a whole number for quantity or rating.
5. Enter any additional details that may be relevant to your registration.
6. Confirm your agreement to the terms by selecting the applicable options.
7. If applicable, provide an estimated value for your registration.
8. Enter a preferred time for your scheduling needs.
9. Leave any final comments or notes.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full name** (full_name): Enter your legal full name for accurate records.
* **Email address** (email): Enter a valid email for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone number** (phone): Include your country code if you are outside the local region.
* **Date** (reference_date): Select the relevant date for your registration (e.g., start or end of event).
* **Primary selection** (primary_selection): Choose the option that best applies (Attendee, Exhibitor, or Speaker).
* **Quantity or rating** (quantity_or_rating): Provide a whole number for quantity or rating if required.
* **Additional details** (additional_details): Enter any additional relevant information for your registration.
* **Agreement** (agreement): Select the options that apply to confirm your agreement.
* **Estimated value** (estimated_value): Use decimal format when providing an estimated value.
* **Preferred time** (preferred_time): Enter a time if scheduling is required.
* **Final comments or notes** (final_comments): Enter any optional closing remarks or comments.
