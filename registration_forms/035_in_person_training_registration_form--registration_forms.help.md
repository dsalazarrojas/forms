# In Person Training Registration Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from participants attending in-person training sessions. It helps us understand their requirements, dietary restrictions, and other relevant details for a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields as accurately and thoroughly as possible.
2. Select the relevant training session from the provided options.
3. Provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the training team.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Page 1: Session Name
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training session you are attending.
* **Training Session** (`training_session`, select_one, optional): Select the training session you will be attending.
* **Session Date** (`session_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the training session.
* **Session Time** (`session_time`, time, optional): Select the session time.
* **Location** (`location`, text, optional): Enter the location of the training session.

### Page 2: Participant Information
* **Instructor Name** (`instructor_name`, text, optional): Enter the name of the training instructor.
* **Participant Name** (`participant_name`, text, optional): Enter your name as a participant.
* **Participant Company** (`participant_company`, text, optional): Enter your company name (if applicable).
* **Participant Job Title** (`participant_job_title`, text, optional): Enter your job title (if applicable).
* **Participant Email** (`participant_email`, email, optional): Enter your email address.
* **Participant Phone** (`participant_phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Notes** (`notes`, note, optional): Provide any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the training team.

### Page 3: Attendance and Special Requests
* **Participant Attendance** (`participant_attendance`, select_multiple, optional): Indicate if you will be attending the training session.
* **Participant Dietary Restrictions** (`participant_dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any dietary restrictions you may have (e.g., gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan).
* **Participant Visa** (`participant_visa`, text, optional): Enter your visa information (if applicable).
* **Participant Visa Number** (`participant_visa_number`, text, optional): Enter your visa number (if applicable).
* **Participant Visa Expiration** (`participant_visa_expiration`, date, optional): Enter the visa expiration date (if applicable).
* **Participant Visa Issue** (`participant_visa_issue`, text, optional): Enter the visa issue date (if applicable).
* **Participant Visa Type** (`participant_visa_type`, text, optional): Enter your visa type (if applicable).

### Page 4: Special Requests
* **Participant Onsite Parking** (`participant_onsite_parking`, text, optional): Provide information on your onsite parking requirements.
* **Participant Onsite Visa** (`participant_onsite_visa`, text, optional): Enter your onsite visa information (if applicable).
* **Participant Onsite Housing** (`participant_onsite_housing`, text, optional): Enter your onsite housing requirements (if applicable).
* **Participant Emergency Contact** (`participant_emergency_contact`, text, optional): Provide information on your emergency contact.
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, select_one, optional): Select the tool assigned to you for the training session.
* **Participant Dietary Restrictions** (`participant_dietary_restrictions`, select_multiple, optional): Select any additional dietary restrictions you may have.
* **Participant Signature** (`participant_signature`, text, optional): Enter your signature.
* **Participant Comments** (`participant_comments`, note, optional): Enter any additional comments or notes you'd like to share with the training team.

### Tips

* Ensure you fill in all required fields accurately and thoroughly.
* Review your information carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, please reach out to the training team.
