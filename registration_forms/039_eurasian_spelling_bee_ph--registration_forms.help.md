# Eurasian Spelling Bee Ph - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed for registering participants in the Eurasian Spelling Bee Ph. Please fill out the form with your personal and event information to complete your registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill in your **First Name** and **Last Name** in the first two fields.
2. Enter your **Email** in the third field.
3. Provide your **Phone Number** in the fourth field.
4. Select your **Date of Birth** in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
5. Choose the **Event Type** as "Eurasion Bee" or "Other".
6. Enter the **Event Date** in the date format (dd/mm/yyyy).
7. Select the **Event Time** in the time format (HH:MM).
8. Fill in the **Address 1**, **Address 2**, **Postal Code**, and **City**.
9. Select your **Country** from the list.
10. Choose the **Organization** type as "Self", "Company", or "Other".
11. Enter your **Role** as described on the job description.
12. Enter the **Event Details** in the free-text field.
13. Enter the **Contact Person** and **Contact Email**.
14. Enter the **Contact Phone Number**.
15. Enter the **Emergency Contact**, **Emergency Relation**, and **Emergency Phone Number**.
16. Enter the **Emergency Email**.
17. Read and agree to the **Terms and Conditions**.
18. Confirm your **Agreement** by typing "I agree".

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, required): Enter your last name.
- **Email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter your email address.
- **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required): Enter your phone number.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, `date`, required): Select your date of birth in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
- **Event Type** (`event_type`, `select_one`, required): Select "Eurasion Bee" or "Other" as the event type.
- **Event Date** (`event_date`, `date`, required): Enter the event date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
- **Event Time** (`event_time`, `time`, required): Select the event time in the format HH:MM.
- **Address 1** (`address1`, `text`, required): Enter the first line of your address.
- **Address 2** (`address2`, `text`, required): Enter the second line of your address.
- **Postal Code** (`postal_code`, `text`, required): Enter your postal code or zip code.
- **City** (`city`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the city.
- **Country** (`country`, `text`, required): Select your country from the list.
- **Organization** (`organization`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose "Self", "Company", or "Other" as the type of organization.
- **Role** (`role`, `text`, required): Enter your role as described on the job description.
- **Event Details** (`event_details`, `text`, required): Enter any additional information about the event.
- **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the contact person.
- **Contact Email** (`contact_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the contact person.
- **Contact Phone** (`contact_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the contact person.
- **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required): Enter the name of the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Relation** (`emergency_relation`, `text`, required): Enter the relation of the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Phone** (`emergency_phone`, `text`, required): Enter the phone number of the emergency contact.
- **Emergency Email** (`emergency_email`, `email`, required): Enter the email address of the emergency contact.
- **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `text`, required): Read the terms and conditions carefully.
- **Agreement** (`agreement`, `note`, required): Confirm your agreement by typing "I agree".
