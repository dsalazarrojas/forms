# customer_service_workshop_attendance_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register the attendance of customers to a workshop. It collects information about the workshop and the participant, including their details, workshop attendance status, and other relevant data.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the participant details such as name, email, phone number, company, department, job title, position, and any other relevant information.
2. Enter the workshop title, start and end dates, and time.
3. Choose the attendance status of each participant.
4. Enter any additional comments or notes for the participant and workshop.
5. Enter the attendance status and count for the participant.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Name**: Enter the name of the participant attending the workshop.
* **Participant Email**: Enter the email address of the participant. This is a required field.
* **Participant Phone**: Enter the phone number of the participant.
* **Participant Company**: Enter the name of the participant's company.
* **Participant Department**: Enter the name of the participant's department.
* **Participant Job Title**: Enter the job title of the participant.
* **Participant Position**: Enter the position of the participant.
* **Workshop Title**: Enter the title of the workshop being attended.
* **Workshop Start Date**: Enter the start date of the workshop.
* **Workshop End Date**: Enter the end date of the workshop.
* **Workshop Time**: Enter the time of the workshop.
* **Workshop Location**: Enter the location of the workshop.
* **Participant Attended**: Choose whether the participant attended the workshop.
* **Participant Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes for the participant.
* **Workshop Host**: Enter the name of the workshop host.
* **Workshop Facilitator**: Enter the name of the workshop facilitator.
* **Workshop Notes**: Add any additional notes about the workshop.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional notes for the participant.
* **Attendance Status**: Enter the status of the participant's attendance, such as "attended", "absent", or "late".
* **Participant Attended Count**: Enter the number of participants who attended the workshop.
* **Participant Absent Count**: Enter the number of participants who were absent.
* **Participant Late Count**: Enter the number of participants who were late.
* **Participant Partially Attended Count**: Enter the number of participants who partially attended the workshop.
* **Workshop Capacity**: Enter the capacity of the workshop.
* **Participant Registration Date**: Enter the date the participant registered for the workshop.
