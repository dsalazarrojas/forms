# Sponsor Product Display Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Sponsor Product Display Registration Form is used to collect information from sponsors for displaying their products on the platform. This form is necessary for the platform to gather details about the products and sponsors, which will be used to make informed decisions about product display.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill in your user information in the "User Info" field.
2. Enter the product name in the "Product Name" field.
3. Provide a detailed description of the product in the "Product Description" field.
4. Enter the URL of the product in the "Product URL" field.
5. Enter your sponsor information in the "Sponsor Info" field.
6. Indicate if the product meets the display requirements in the "Display Requirements" field.
7. Upload a product image in the "Product Image" field.
8. Provide alternative text for the product image in the "Product Image Alt Text" field.
9. Choose the submission type (Yes/No) in the "Submission Type" field.
10. Select the submission status (Active/Inactive) in the "Submission Status" field.
11. Choose the submission date and time in the "Submission Date" and "Submission Time" fields.
12. Enter your contact email in the "Contact Email" field.
13. Enter your contact phone number in the "Contact Phone" field.
14. Enter any additional notes about the submission in the "Notes" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### User Info (text, optional)
* This field is for the user to enter their information, such as name and contact details.

### Product Name (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter the name of the product.

### Product Description (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter a detailed description of the product.

### Product URL (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter the URL of the product.

### Sponsor Info (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter their information, such as name and contact details.

### Display Requirements (select_multiple, optional)
* This field allows the sponsor to indicate if the product meets the display requirements.

### Product Image (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to upload the product image.

### Product Image Alt Text (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter alternative text for the product image.

### Submission Type (select_one, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to choose the submission type (Yes/No).

### Submission Status (select_one, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to select the submission status (Active/Inactive).

### Submission Date (date, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter the submission date.

### Submission Time (time, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter the submission time.

### Contact Email (email, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter their contact email.

### Contact Phone (text, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter their contact phone number.

### Notes (note, optional)
* This field is for the sponsor to enter any additional notes about the submission.
