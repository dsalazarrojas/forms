# Data Literacy For Teachers Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Data Literacy For Teachers Registration Form is designed to collect registration information from teachers who want to participate in various programs and receive follow-up information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name in the First Name field.
2. Enter your Last Name in the Last Name field.
3. Provide your Email Address in the Email Address field.
4. Select all applicable programs you want to sign up for in the Program Sign-ups field.
5. Choose how you want to receive follow-up information in the Follow-up Method field.
6. (Optional) Add any additional details about yourself in the Participant Details field.
7. (Optional) If you have a specific Education Team, feel free to add it in the Education Team field.
8. (Optional) If you work with a Training Coordinator, provide their name in the Training Coordinator field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (First Name, text, required): Enter your first name, for instance, John.
* **Last Name** (Last Name, text, required): Enter your last name, for example, Doe.
* **Email Address** (Email Address, email, required): Provide your valid email address where you can be reached for follow-up information.
* **Program Sign-ups** (Program Sign-ups, select_multiple, required): Choose all programs you want to sign up for from the provided options (Program A, Program B, Program C).
* **Participant Details** (Participant Details, text, not required): Add any additional details about yourself that might be helpful for our team to know.
* **Follow-up Method** (Follow-up Method, select_one, required): Select how you would like to receive follow-up information (Email, Phone, None).
* **Education Team** (Education Team, text, not required): If you have an education team, please provide their name and information.
* **Training Coordinator** (Training Coordinator, text, not required): If you work with a training coordinator, provide their name and information.
