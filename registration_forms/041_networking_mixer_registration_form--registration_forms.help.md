# networking_mixer_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used for registering attendees for a networking mixer event. It collects essential information to help the event organizers manage and prepare for the event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the required fields (marked as "required") with accurate and up-to-date information.
2. Enter your name and event details in the corresponding fields.
3. Select your role in the company (CEO, Manager, Director, Employee, or Other).
4. Provide your company's social media links if applicable.
5. Review and submit your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Event Name (1)**: Enter the name of the event you are attending.
* **Event Date (2)**: Select the date of the event.
* **Event Time (3)**: Choose the time of the event.
* **Event Venue (4)**: Enter the location of the event.
* **Attendee Name (5)**: Enter your name as it should be displayed at the event.
* **Company (6)**: Enter your company name.
* **Job Title (7)**: Enter your job title.
* **Email (8)**: Enter your contact email address.
* **Phone (9)**: Enter your contact phone number.
* **Role (10)**: Select your role in the company (CEO, Manager, Director, Employee, or Other).
* **Company URL (11)**: Enter your company's website URL if applicable.
* **Job Title URL (12)**: Enter the URL of your job title description if applicable.
* **Facebook (13)**: Enter your company's Facebook page link if applicable.
* **Twitter (14)**: Enter your company's Twitter handle if applicable.
* **Instagram (15)**: Enter your company's Instagram page link if applicable.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues or concerns, please contact the event organizers.
* This form is for internal use only, so please do not share it with anyone outside of the event.
