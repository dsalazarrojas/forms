# Massage Retreat Participation Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose

This form is used for participants to register for the massage retreat, providing necessary information for a smooth and enjoyable experience.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form carefully, providing accurate information for each field.
2. Make sure to review your information before submitting the form.
3. If you need help or have questions, do not hesitate to contact the retreat organizers.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Name** (`name`, `text`, required: false): Please enter your name for the registration purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, required: false): Enter your phone number to enable easy communication during the retreat.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, required: false): Enter your email address for future correspondence and updates.
* **Attendee Type** (`attendee_type`, `select_one`, required: false): Choose your role as a participant, partner, or instructor in the retreat.
* **Arrival Date** (`arrival_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your expected arrival date for the retreat.
* **Departure Date** (`departure_date`, `date`, required: false): Enter your expected departure date from the retreat.
* **Arrival Time** (`arrival_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter your expected arrival time for the retreat.
* **Departure Time** (`departure_time`, `time`, required: false): Enter your expected departure time from the retreat.
* **Massage Preferences** (`massage_preferences`, `select_multiple`, required: false): Select one or more massage preferences from the list provided.
* **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`, `text`, required: false): Provide a contact person's name and phone number in case of an emergency.
* **Special Requests** (`special_requests`, `text`, required: false): Enter any special requests you may have for the retreat.

## Tips

* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you need assistance or have any questions, contact the retreat organizers.
