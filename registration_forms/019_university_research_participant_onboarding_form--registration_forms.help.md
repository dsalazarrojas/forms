# University Research Participant Onboarding Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The University Research Participant Onboarding Form is a crucial step in the participant registration process for university research studies. This form helps ensure that new participants are properly onboarded and can be contacted for study-related activities.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in the **Participant Details** field with the participant's name and other relevant identification information.
2. Indicate the participant's **Availability** by selecting either 'Yes' or 'No' from the dropdown list.
3. If the participant is available, provide detailed information in the **Availability Details** field.
4. Enter the participant's **Contact Number** in the format specified (e.g., XXX-XXX-XXXX).
5. Enter the participant's **Email** address in a format that is correctly formatted (e.g., user@example.com).
6. Provide the participant's **Address** in a clear and concise format.
7. Enter any other **University Details** relevant to the participant.
8. Confirm your **Consent** by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for each option.
9. Add any **Participant Notes** relevant to the participant in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Details**: Enter the name and any other necessary identification information for the participant.
* **Availability**: Indicate if the participant is available to participate in the study. Select 'Yes' for available or 'No' for not available.
* **Availability Details**: If available, provide detailed information about the participant's availability, such as schedule, work hours, or any other relevant details.
* **Contact Number**: Enter the participant's contact number in the format specified (e.g., XXX-XXX-XXXX).
* **Email**: Enter the participant's email address in a format that is correctly formatted (e.g., user@example.com).
* **Address**: Enter the participant's address in a clear and concise format.
* **University Details**: Enter any other relevant details about the participant's university affiliation.
* **Consent**: Confirm your consent by selecting 'Yes' or 'No' for each option. This confirms that you have read and understood the terms of participation.
* **Participant Notes**: Add any additional information relevant to the participant in the text field.
