# 008_ai_and_law_conference_registration--registration_forms - Help Guide
## Purpose
The AI and Law Conference registration form is designed to gather attendee information and preferences for the conference.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your personal details in the "Attendee Details" section.
2. Select your professional title and organization/university.
3. Provide your professional email address.
4. Choose how you will be attending the conference.
5. Select the topics you are interested in.
6. Choose a pre-conference workshop (if applicable).
7. Confirm if you require CLE credits.
8. Specify the jurisdiction for CLE credits (if applicable).
9. Choose your attendance preferences for the networking dinner and other logistics.
10. Confirm your dietary requirements.
11. Provide any special accommodations or accessibility needs.
12. Consent to being recorded and photographed during the event.
13. Specify how you heard about the conference.
14. Provide any additional comments or questions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name** (`full_name`, `text`, required): Please enter your full name.
* **Professional Title / Position** (`professional_title`, `text`, required): Enter your professional title or position.
* **Organization / University** (`organization_university`, `text`, required): Enter your organization or university name.
* **Professional Email Address** (`email_address`, `email`, required): Enter your work email address where you can be reached for conference communications.
* **How will you be attending?** (`participation_type`, `select_one`, required): Select how you will be attending the conference (in-person, virtual, speaker, or student).
* **Areas of Interest in AI and Law** (`legal_focus_areas`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose all topics that you are interested in (Intellectual Property & AI, Ethics and Algorithmic Bias, Data Privacy and Cybersecurity, Regulatory Frameworks, AI in Legal Practice, Criminal Justice and AI).
* **Pre-Conference Workshop Selection** (`workshop_selection`, `select_one`, required): Choose a pre-conference workshop to attend (if applicable).
* **CLE Credits** (`cle_credits_requested`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you require CLE credits.
* **Jurisdiction for CLE Credits** (`jurisdiction_for_cle`, `text`, optional): If yes, specify the state or country.
* **Dietary Requirements / Allergies** (`dietary_requirements`, `select_multiple`, required): Choose your dietary requirements or allergies (Vegetarian, Vegan, Gluten-Free, Kosher, Halal).
* **Networking Dinner Attendance** (`networking_dinner_attendance`, `select_one`, required): Choose if you will attend the networking dinner.
* **Accessibility or Special Accommodations** (`special_accommodations`, `text`, optional): Provide any special accommodations or accessibility needs.
* **Recording Consent** (`recording_consent`, `select_one`, required): Confirm if you consent to being recorded and photographed during the event.
* **How did you hear about the conference?** (`referral_source`, `select_one`, optional): Choose where you heard about the conference (Professional Association, Social Media, Academic Institution, Colleague).
* **Additional Questions or Comments** (`additional_notes`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional comments or questions.
