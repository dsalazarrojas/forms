# wellness_questionairre - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to gather general information from users, including demographic and personal data such as email, phone number, and user preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Provide your user input in the "User Input" field.
2. Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
3. Enter a password in the "Password" field.
4. Confirm your password in the "Confirm" field.
5. Select one option from the "Select" field.
6. Select multiple options from the "Select Multiple" field.
7. Choose a date in the "Date" field.
8. Choose a time in the "Time" field.
9. Provide any notes or comments in the "Note" field.
10. Enter your phone number in the "Phone" field.
11. Provide additional user information in the "User 2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Input (User Input)**: Enter any relevant information you'd like to share about yourself.
* **Email (Email)**: Enter your email address to allow us to contact you or send you relevant information.
* **Password (Password)**: Enter a password, which will be used for internal purposes.
* **Confirm (Confirm)**: Re-enter the password you entered in the "Password" field to verify its accuracy.
* **Select (Select)**: Choose one option from the list to express your preference.
* **Select Multiple (Select Multiple)**: Choose multiple options from the list to express multiple preferences.
* **Date (Date)**: Choose a date in the format mm/dd/yyyy.
* **Time (Time)**: Choose a time in the format hh:mm AM/PM.
* **Note (Note)**: Provide any additional notes or comments about your preferences.
* **Phone (Phone)**: Enter your phone number to allow us to contact you.
* **User 2 (User 2)**: Enter any additional user information you'd like to provide.

## Tips
* Please ensure that all required fields are filled out before submitting the form.
* If you're unsure about any field, feel free to ask for clarification.
