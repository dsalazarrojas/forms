# Brand Activation Host Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Brand Activation Host Registration Form is designed to collect information from individuals interested in becoming Brand Activation Hosts. This form is used to assess a person's experience, availability, and suitability to host brand-related events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Full Name" field with your legal name.
2.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for communication purposes.
3.  Provide your phone number in the "Phone Number" field for contact.
4.  Enter the city or region where you are located in the "City or Region" field.
5.  Select your level of experience with event hosting in the "Event Hosting Experience" field.
6.  Choose the types of events you have hosted or are interested in hosting in the "Types of Events Hosted" field.
7.  Select your level of experience with brand activations in the "Experience with Brand Activations" field.
8.  Indicate your availability for weekday and weekend events in the "Weekday Availability" and "Weekend Availability" fields respectively.
9.  Choose your preferred event frequency in the "Preferred Event Frequency" field.
10. Enter the average audience size for your events in the "Average Audience Size" field.
11. Provide a brief description of your target audience demographics in the "Target Audience Demographics" field.
12. Enter your social media presence in the "Social Media Presence" field.
13. Select the reason you are interested in hosting events in the "Why Are You Interested" field.
14. Describe any unique value or strengths you bring to event hosting in the "Unique Value or Strengths" field.
15. Read and agree to the terms and conditions in the "I agree to host terms and conditions" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Text, Required): Enter your legal name as it appears on your identification documents.
* **Email Address** (Email, Required): Enter the email address you want us to use for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number** (Text, Required): Enter your contact number for communication and scheduling purposes.
* **City or Region** (Text, Required): Enter the city or region where you are located.
* **Event Hosting Experience** (Select One, Required): Choose your level of experience with event hosting.
	+ No experience
	+ 1-2 events
	+ 3-5 events
	+ 6-10 events
	+ 10+ events
	+ Professional host
* **Types of Events Hosted** (Select Multiple, Not required): Choose the types of events you have hosted or are interested in hosting.
	+ Brand activations
	+ Product launches
	+ Networking events
	+ Social gatherings
	+ Corporate events
	+ Community events
	+ Experiential marketing
	+ Other
* **Experience with Brand Activations** (Select One, Required): Choose your level of experience with brand activations.
	+ Extensive experience
	+ Moderate experience
	+ Some experience
	+ Limited experience
	+ No experience
* **Weekday Availability** (Select One, Required): Indicate your availability for weekday events.
	+ Available
	+ Limited availability
	+ Not available
	+ Flexible
* **Weekend Availability** (Select One, Required): Indicate your availability for weekend events.
	+ Available
	+ Limited availability
	+ Not available
	+ Flexible
* **Preferred Event Frequency** (Select One, Not required): Choose your preferred event frequency.
	+ Multiple per month
	+ Monthly
	+ Quarterly
	+ Bi-annually
	+ Annually
	+ As needed
* **Average Audience Size** (Select One, Required): Enter the average audience size for your events.
	+ Under 10 people
	+ 10-25 people
	+ 25-50 people
	+ 50-100 people
	+ 100-200 people
	+ 200+ people
* **Target Audience Demographics** (Text, Not required): Provide a brief description of your target audience demographics.
* **Social Media Presence** (Number, Not required): Enter your social media presence across various platforms.
* **Why Are You Interested** (Text, Required): Select the reason you are interested in hosting events.
* **Unique Value or Strengths** (Text, Not required): Describe any unique value or strengths you bring to event hosting.
* **I agree to host terms and conditions** (Select One, Required): Check to confirm you agree to the terms and conditions of the application.
