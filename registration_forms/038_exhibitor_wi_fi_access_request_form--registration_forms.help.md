# exhibitor-wi-fi-access-request-form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for exhibitors to request access to the Wi-Fi network at the event or exhibition.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1.  Read the form carefully and ensure you understand the purpose and requirements.
2.  Select 'Yes' or 'No' to confirm if you need Wi-Fi access.
3.  Fill in your details, including Name, Email, Phone, Company, and Address.
4.  Provide a brief explanation for your Wi-Fi request in the 'Wi-Fi Request' field.
5.  Specify the reason for your Wi-Fi request in the 'Wi-Fi Request Reason' field.
6.  Agree to the terms of the Wi-Fi request in the 'Wi-Fi Request Terms' field.
7.  Add any additional notes about your request in the 'Wi-Fi Request Notes' field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Exhibitor Wi-Fi Access Request** (`exhibitor_wifi`): Select 'Yes' if you need Wi-Fi access. This will help us know if you require access to the Wi-Fi network.
* **Name** (`name`): Please provide your full name as it appears on your business card or ID.
* **Email** (`email`): Enter your email address for contact and communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`): Enter your phone number so we can reach you if needed.
* **Company** (`company`): Enter the name of your company or organization you represent.
* **Address** (`address`): Enter your office or business address for reference and record keeping purposes.
* **Wi-Fi Request** (`wifi_req`): Briefly describe what you plan to use the Wi-Fi for (e.g., internet, demo, etc.).
* **Wi-Fi Request Reason** (`wifi_req_req`): Explain the reason for your Wi-Fi request. This will help us understand your needs and ensure we can accommodate you.
* **Wi-Fi Request Terms** (`wifi_req_terms`): Accept the terms of the Wi-Fi request, which includes the rules and guidelines for using the Wi-Fi network.
* **Wi-Fi Request Notes** (`wifi_req_notes`): You can add any additional comments or details about your Wi-Fi request, if needed. This is optional.
