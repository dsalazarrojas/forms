* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your first name.

* **Additional Info** (`additional_info`, note, optional): Provide any additional information about your participation if required.
* **Confirm** (`confirm`, select_one, optional): Confirm your registration by selecting "Yes".
* **Confirm Email** (`confirm_email`, select_one, optional): Confirm your email address by selecting "Yes".
