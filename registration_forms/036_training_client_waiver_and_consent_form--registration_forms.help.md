# Training Client Waiver And Consent Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect personal and emergency contact information from clients or trainees for training purposes.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Enter your personal details such as First Name and Last Name.
2. Provide your Email address.
3. Enter your Phone number.
4. Select your Date of Birth.
5. If you have an emergency contact, provide their First Name and Last Name, and select the nature of your relationship.
6. If applicable, add a note about your emergency contact in the provided text field.
7. Enter your emergency contact's Phone number, Address, and Province.
8. Enter any other details about your emergency contact, such as other phone numbers or addresses.
9. Add any other details about your emergency contact that don't fit in the other fields.
10. Sign the form to indicate your consent.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your first name as you want it to be displayed.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter your last name as you want it to be displayed.
* **Email** (`email`, email, **optional**): Enter your email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, **optional**): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, **optional**): Enter your date of birth in the standard date format (DD/MM/AAAA).
* **Emergency Contact First Name** (`emergency_contact_first_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the first name of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Last Name** (`emergency_contact_last_name`, text, **optional**): Enter the last name of your emergency contact.
* **Relationship** (`emergency_contact_relationship`, select_one, **optional**): Select the nature of your relationship with your emergency contact (e.g., Mother, Father, Brother, Sister, Spouse, Domestic Partner).
* **Emergency Contact Phone** (`emergency_contact_phone`, text, **optional**): Enter the phone number of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Address** (`emergency_contact_address`, note, **optional**): Enter a note about your emergency contact's address, if any.
* **Emergency Contact City** (`emergency_contact_city`, text, **optional**): Enter the city of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Province** (`emergency_contact_province`, text, **optional**): Enter the province of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Postal Code** (`emergency_contact_postal_code`, text, **optional**): Enter the postal code of your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Postal Code 2** (`emergency_contact_postal_code_2`, text, **optional**): Enter any additional postal code for your emergency contact.
* **Emergency Contact Signature** (`emergency_contact_signature`, note, **optional**): Sign the form to indicate your consent for emergency contact purposes.
* **Other** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, **optional**): Add any other information that you think would be important for your emergency contact to know.
* **Other Phone** (`emergency_contact_other`, text, **optional**): Add any other phone numbers for your emergency contact.
* **Other Address** (`emergency_contact_other`, note, **optional**): Add any other address details for your emergency contact.
* **Signature** (`signature`, note, **optional**): Sign the form to indicate your consent for training purposes.

This form requires a signature to be signed by both you and your emergency contact if applicable.
