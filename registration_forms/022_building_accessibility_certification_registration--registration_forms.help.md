# Building Accessibility Certification Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Building Accessibility Certification Registration" form is designed to collect information about a building's accessibility features, contact details, and certification status. This form is essential for building owners, architects, or other stakeholders who wish to obtain an accessibility certification for their building.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Fill in the required contact information, including your organization's name, contact person's name, title, email address, and phone number.
2.  Enter the building's details, such as official name, full address, city, state or province, and zip or postal code.
3.  Specify the building type and year it was constructed or last renovated, if applicable.
4.  Provide the total square footage and number of floors in the building.
5.  Select the type of certification you are seeking, such as ADA Compliance, Universal Design, or Accessibility Standards.
6.  List the accessibility features present in the building, including wheelchair ramps, accessible parking, elevators, and others.
7.  Indicate whether you have been previously certified and, if so, the date of the previous certification.
8.  Self-assess the building's compliance status, such as fully compliant or not compliant at all.
9.  Describe any known accessibility barriers in the building.
10.  Indicate whether supporting documents, such as floor plans or previous reports, are attached.
11.  Suggest a preferred inspection date, if applicable.
12.  Mention any special requirements or considerations for the inspection.
13.  Finally, make a declaration that the provided information is accurate.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

*   **Organization Name** (`organization_name`, text, required): Enter the full legal name of your organization.
*   **Contact Person** (`contact_person`, text, required): Type in the primary contact name.
*   **Contact Title** (`contact_title`, text, required): Enter the contact person's job title.
*   **Email Address** (`email`, email, required): Provide the contact person's email address.
*   **Phone Number** (`phone`, text, required): Enter the contact person's phone number.
*   **Registration Date** (`registration_date`, date, required): Enter the date of registration for the building.
*   **Building Name** (`building_name`, text, required): Type in the official name of the building.
*   **Building Address** (`building_address`, text, required): Enter the full street address of the building.
*   **City** (`city`, text, required): Enter the city where the building is located.
*   **State or Province** (`state`, text, required): Enter the state or province where the building is located.
*   **ZIP or Postal Code** (`zip_code`, text, required): Enter the zip or postal code of the building.
*   **Building Type** (`building_type`, select_one, required): Select one or more of the available building types, such as Commercial Office, Residential, and Mixed Use.
*   **Year Constructed** (`year_constructed`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the year the building was constructed.
*   **Year Last Renovated** (`year_renovated`, number, optional): If applicable, enter the year the building was last renovated.
*   **Total Square Footage** (`total_square_footage`, number, required): Enter the total square footage of the building.
*   **Number of Floors** (`number_of_floors`, number, required): Enter the total number of floors in the building.
*   **Certification Type** (`certification_type`, select_one, required): Select one of the available certification types, such as ADA Compliance, Universal Design, or Accessibility Standards.
*   **Accessibility Features Present** (`accessibility_features`, select_multiple, required): Select all the accessibility features present in the building, such as wheelchair ramps, accessible parking, and elevators.
*   **Previous Certification** (`previous_certification`, select_one, required): Indicate whether you have been previously certified and, if so, the date of the previous certification.
*   **Previous Certification Date** (`previous_cert_date`, date, optional): If applicable, enter the date of the previous certification.
*   **Current Compliance Status** (`compliance_status`, select_one, required): Self-assess the building's compliance status, such as fully compliant or not compliant at all.
*   **Known Accessibility Barriers** (`known_barriers`, text, optional): Describe any known accessibility barriers in the building.
*   **Documentation Attached** (`documentation_attached`, select_one, required): Indicate whether supporting documents, such as floor plans or previous reports, are attached.
*   **Preferred Inspection Date** (`preferred_inspection_date`, date, optional): Suggest a preferred inspection date, if applicable.
*   **Special Requirements** (`special_requirements`, text, optional): Mention any special requirements or considerations for the inspection.
*   **Information Accuracy Declaration** (`declaration`, select_one, required): Make a declaration that the provided information is accurate.

## Tips

*   Double-check all fields for accuracy and completeness before submitting the form.
*   Ensure the correct contact information and building details are provided.
*   Be honest when self-assessing the building's compliance status.
*   Describe any known barriers in the building accurately.
*   Attach supporting documents, if applicable.
*   Suggest a reasonable preferred inspection date.
*   Mention any special requirements or considerations.
*   Make a declaration that the provided information is accurate.
