# special_olympics_fl_statewide_database_app_2 - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Special Olympics FL Statewide Database App 2 form is designed to collect information from participants for the Special Olympics Florida Statewide Database App 2. This form is used to record and store data about participants' events and their related information.

## How To Complete This Form
1. First, select the event(s) you want to register the participant for.
2. Fill out the Registration Form with the participant's information.
3. Fill out the Contact Form with the contact person's information.
4. Make sure to review your entries carefully and accurately before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form (1) (text, optional)**: This is the form to register the participant.
* **Event Selection (3) (select_multiple, required)**: Select the event(s) you want to register the participant for.
* **Participant Name (4) (text, optional)**: Enter the participant's name.
* **Participant Notes (9) (note, optional)**: Enter any additional information about the participant.
* **Form Notes (10) (note, optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the registration.

* **Contact Form (2) (text, optional)**: This is the form to contact the person responsible for the participant.
* **Contact Name (5) (text, optional)**: Enter the contact person's name.
* **Contact Phone (7) (text, optional)**: Enter the contact person's phone number.
* **Contact Email (8) (email, optional)**: Enter the contact person's email.
* **Contact Notes (optional)**: Enter any additional comments or notes about the contact person.
