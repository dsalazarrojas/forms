# Nursery Registration Of Interest Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Nursery Registration Of Interest Form is a simple registration form used to gather basic contact information and interest preferences from parents or legal guardians of children. It allows the nursery to collect demographic data and get in touch with interested parties.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your contact information and details.
2. Select your grade level of interest (if applicable).
3. Choose which programs or services you are interested in (if applicable).
4. Optionally, provide additional comments or how you found us.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name**: Please enter your first name here.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name here.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address here.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number here.
* **Address**: Enter your address here, including street and house number.
* **City**: Enter the name of the city where you reside.
* **Zip**: Enter your postal zip code here.
* **School Year**: This field is not applicable to most users, but if your child is currently in school, please select the relevant grade level.
* **Grade Level**: Please choose the grade level your child is currently attending or will be attending. This will help us tailor our programs to your needs.
* **Interested In**: This field is optional, but if you have any interests or preferences for the nursery programs, please select all that apply.
* **How Did You Find Us**: This field is optional, but it helps us understand how you heard about us.
* **Comments**: This is an open field for any additional comments or questions you may have.

Note: All fields are optional unless marked as required. Please fill in only the fields that are relevant to you.
