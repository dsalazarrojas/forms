# Eco Logistics Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Eco Logistics Certification Registration Form is designed to collect information from applicants for Eco Logistics Certification. The form is used to register applicants and verify their practices and supporting documents.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:
1. Provide your "Applicant Details" which includes your name, address, and contact information.
2. Describe your "ECO Practice" in the text field provided.
3. Add a brief description of your ECO Practice in the text field provided.
4. Upload any "Supporting Files" related to your ECO Practice.
5. Upload up to 4 "File 1" to "File 4" for further documentation.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Applicant Details (1)** (`applicant_details`, text, required/optional): This field is used to input your name, address, and contact information. Please fill out this field completely for verification purposes.
* **ECO Practice (2)** (`eco_practice`, text, required/optional): This field is used to describe your ECO Practice. Be as detailed as possible to help the reviewing team understand your practice.
* **ECO Practice Description (3)** (`eco_practice_description`, text, required/optional): This field is used to provide a brief description of your ECO Practice. Please keep it concise.
* **Supporting Files (4)** (`supporting_files`, text, required/optional): This field is used to upload any supporting documents related to your ECO Practice.
* **File 1 (5)** (`file_1`, text, required/optional): This field is used to upload the first file related to your ECO Practice.
* **File 2 (6)** (`file_2`, text, required/optional): This field is used to upload the second file related to your ECO Practice.
* **File 3 (7)** (`file_3`, text, required/optional): This field is used to upload the third file related to your ECO Practice.
* **File 4 (8)** (`file_4`, text, required/optional): This field is used to upload the fourth file related to your ECO Practice.

## Tips
* Please fill out all required fields completely.
* Make sure to keep your "ECO Practice Description" concise and focused on your ECO Practice.
* Upload supporting documents that are relevant to your ECO Practice.
* Each file upload should be related to your ECO Practice.
* Review your form before submission to ensure accuracy and completeness.
