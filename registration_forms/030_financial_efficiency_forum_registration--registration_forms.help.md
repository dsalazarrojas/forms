# Financial Efficiency Forum Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Financial Efficiency Forum Registration form is a preliminary step to gather contact information from users interested in participating in the Financial Efficiency Forum. This form is not a comprehensive registration process, and users will be contacted separately to confirm their submission and finalize their registration.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill out each field with the requested information. Start by filling in the User Information section, where you can enter your name. Then, select one or more topics that interest you from the Topic Interests section. If you have any special requirements or preferences, enter them in the Special Requirements section. Next, enter your email address in the Email field, and your phone number in the Phone field. Finally, add any additional notes or comments in the Notes field. Once you have filled out all the required fields, click the Submit button to complete your submission.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **User Information** (`User Information`, text, required/optional): Enter your name in this field.
* **Topic Interests** (`Topic Interests`, select_multiple, required/optional): Select one or more topics that interest you from the dropdown list.
* **Special Requirements** (`Special Requirements`, text, required/optional): If you have any special requirements or preferences, enter them in this field.
* **Email** (`Email`, email, required/optional): Enter your email address to receive notifications and updates.
* **Phone** (`Phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number for follow-up contact.
* **Notes** (`Notes`, note, required/optional): Add any additional notes or comments you'd like to share with us.
* **Submit** (`Submit`, text, required/optional): Click this button to complete your submission.
