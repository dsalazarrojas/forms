# Retail Marketing Certification Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users seeking retail marketing certification. It is a registration form for users to enroll in certification programs related to retail marketing.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your first name in the "User First Name" field.
2. Enter your last name in the "User Last Name" field.
3. Enter your email address in the "Email" field. Ensure it is a valid email address.
4. If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
5. Select your chosen certification program from the "Certification Program" dropdown menu.
6. Enter the date you obtained or will obtain your certification in the "Certification Date" field. Use the date format (YYYY-MM-DD).
7. If you have any additional information to provide, enter it in the "Note" field.
8. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **User First Name** (`user_first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name as it appears on your official documents.
* **User Last Name** (`user_last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name as it appears on your official documents.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and notifications.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number if you have one.
* **Certification Program** (`certification_program`, select_one, optional): Select one of the available certification programs to register for.
* **Certification Date** (`certification_date`, date, optional): Enter the date you obtained or will obtain your certification in the format (YYYY-MM-DD).
* **Note** (`note`, note, optional): Enter any additional information you wish to provide about your registration.

## Tips

* Be sure to check your email address for accuracy before submitting.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team.
* Ensure you meet the requirements for your chosen certification program.
* Keep in mind that submitting this form will not automatically grant you certification. A separate evaluation process will follow after submission.
