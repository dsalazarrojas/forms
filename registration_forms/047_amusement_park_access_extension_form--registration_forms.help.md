# Amusement Park Access Extension Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Amusement Park Access Extension Form is used to request an extension of your park visit beyond its original date. Please fill out this form to provide detailed information about your request.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the "Request Date" field with the date you would like to extend your visit to.
2. Enter your "Request Number" from the original ticket purchase or confirmation.
3. Select the type of extension you are requesting, such as "Same day extended hours" or "Additional day".
4. Specify the "Requested Extension Date" and "Extension Duration" accordingly.
5. Enter your "First Name", "Last Name", and "Email Address" as they appear on your ticket.
6. Provide your "Phone Number" and "Address" for contact purposes.
7. Select the "Current Ticket Type" that you currently hold.
8. Enter the "Original Visit Date" and "Original Expiry Date" as they appear on your ticket.
9. Specify the number of guests in your party and select the "Additional Areas" you would like access to.
10. Choose your preferred "Payment Method" and provide the necessary "Card Number", "Card Expiry", and "CVV" details.
11. Select your preferred "Preferred Entry Time" and specify "Attractions of Interest" if you have any.
12. Indicate if you require "Dining Reservations" or not.
13. Specify any "Accessibility Needs" or "Medical Conditions" you may have.
14. Acknowledge the "Terms and Conditions" and "Refund Policy".
15. Confirm your understanding that the extension is "Subject to Availability".
16. Review your submission and ensure all required fields are filled out correctly.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Request Date** (`request_date`, required): The date you would like to extend your visit to.
* **Request Number** (`request_number`, optional): Your ticket number from the original purchase or confirmation.
* **Type of Extension** (`extension_type`, required): Select the type of extension you are requesting, such as "Same day extended hours" or "Additional day".
* **Requested Extension Date** (`requested_extension_date`, required): The date you would like to extend your visit to.
* **Extension Duration** (`extension_duration`, optional): Specify the duration of your extension request.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, required): Enter your first name as it appears on your ticket.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, required): Enter your last name as it appears on your ticket.
* **Email Address** (`email`, required): Enter your email address as it appears on your ticket.
* **Phone Number** (`phone`, required): Enter your phone number for contact purposes.
* **Address** (`address`, optional): Provide your full address for contact purposes.
* **Current Ticket Type** (`ticket_type`, required): Select the type of ticket you currently hold.
* **Original Visit Date** (`original_date`, required): Enter the original visit date as it appears on your ticket.
* **Original Expiry Date** (`original_expiry`, required): Enter the original expiry date as it appears on your ticket.
* **Number in Party** (`number_in_party`, required): Enter the number of guests in your party.
* **Additional Areas** (`current_access_areas`/`requested_access_areas`, optional): Select all areas you would like to have access to.
* **Type of Payment** (`payment_method`, required): Choose your preferred payment method.
* **Card Number** (`card_number`, optional): Enter your credit/debit card number if paying by card.
* **Card Expiry** (`card_expiry`, optional): Enter your card expiry date if paying by card.
* **CVV** (`cvv`, optional): Enter your CVV if paying by card.
* **Preferred Entry Time** (`preferred_entry_time`, optional): Choose your preferred entry time.
* **Attractions of Interest** (`attractions_interest`, optional): Specify any attractions you would like to experience.
* **Dining Reservations** (`dining_reservations`, optional): Select if you require dining reservations.
* **Accessibility Needs** (`accessibility_needs`, optional): Specify any accessibility needs you may have.
* **Medical Conditions** (`medical_conditions`, optional): Specify any medical conditions you may have.
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_acceptance`, required): Acknowledge the terms and conditions of the extension.
* **Refund Policy** (`refund_policy`, required): Acknowledge the refund policy of the extension.
* **Availability Understanding** (`availability_understanding`, required): Confirm that you understand that the extension is subject to availability.
* **Approval Status** (`approval_status`, optional): Review the approval status of your request.
* **Approved By** (`approved_by`, optional): Review the staff member who approved your request.
* **Approval Date** (`approval_date`, optional): Review the date of approval.
* **Confirmation Number** (`confirmation_number`, optional): Review the confirmation number of your request.
* **Additional Comments** (`additional_comments`, optional): Provide any additional comments or requests.
* **Guest Signature** (`guest_signature`, required): Sign your name as the guest.

## Tips
- Make sure to review all fields carefully before submitting your request.
- Ensure that you have not previously submitted a request or that your original visit has ended.
- Provide accurate and detailed information to ensure your request is processed smoothly.
- Review the terms and conditions of the extension carefully before submitting your request.
- If you have any doubts or concerns, please contact park staff for assistance.
