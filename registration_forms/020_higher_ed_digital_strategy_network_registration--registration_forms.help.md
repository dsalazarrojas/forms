# higher_ed_digital_strategy_network_registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering users for a higher education digital strategy network. It collects information about the user's basic contact details and professional background to facilitate networking within the network.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
2. Enter a valid Phone number (if required).
3. Choose your University from the dropdown list.
4. Select your Role from the list.
5. Choose your Interest Areas from the list.
6. Add any additional information in the Message field (optional).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (text): Your first name. Please fill in your first name, which will be used to identify you within the network.
- **Last Name** (text): Your last name. Please fill in your last name, which will be used to identify you within the network.
- **Email** (email): Your email address. This will be your primary contact address within the network.
- **Phone** (text): Your phone number. This can be a landline or mobile number (optional).
- **University** (select one): Choose the University you are affiliated with from the dropdown list.
  - Arizona State University
  - University of Michigan
  - University of Texas
- **Role** (select multiple): Choose one or more roles that best describe your professional background within the network.
  - IT Manager
  - Digital Strategist
  - Digital Marketing Specialist
  - Other
- **Interest Areas** (select multiple): Choose one or more areas of interest that you would like to connect with within the network.
  - Digital Content Creation
  - Digital Marketing
  - Other
- **Message** (text): Any additional information you would like to share with the community or network administrators (optional).

## Tips
* Please ensure that you enter accurate and up-to-date information to facilitate effective networking.
* You can change your information later if necessary.
* If you encounter any issues or have questions, contact the network administrators for assistance.
