# Milestone Celebration Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from hosts/organizers of milestone celebrations such as birthdays, weddings, graduations, etc.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the required fields marked with an asterisk (*) symbol. These fields are essential to ensure that the information is accurate and up-to-date.
2. Enter the number of guests attending the celebration in the "Guests Count" field.
3. Provide your contact information, including your name and email address.
4. Enter the planner's name and email address.
5. Select the correct "RSVPs" option to indicate if you are attending the celebration.
6. Choose the correct "Event Date", "Event Time", and "Event Type" according to the event details.
7. Enter the "Celebration Location" and any additional "Celebration Note" if necessary.
8. Finally, select the correct "Terms and Conditions" option to confirm your agreement.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Guests Count (5)**: Enter the number of guests attending the celebration.
* **Host Name (6)**: Enter the name of the host/organizer of the celebration.
* **Host Email (7)**: Enter the email address of the host/organizer of the celebration.
* **Planner Name (8)**: Enter the name of the person planning the celebration.
* **Planner Email (9)**: Enter the email address of the planner.
* **Event Date (10)**: Select the date of the celebration.
* **Event Time (11)**: Select the time of the celebration.
* **Event Type (13)**: Choose the type of celebration (e.g. birthday, wedding, graduation, etc.).
* **Guest Information (3)**: This field is meant for additional guest information, but it is optional and not required.
* **RSVPs (4)**: Select whether you are attending the celebration.
* **Event Note (14)**: Enter any additional note related to the event (optional).
* **Celebration Date (15)**: Select the date of the celebration.
* **Celebration Time (16)**: Select the time of the celebration.
* **Celebration Note (17)**: Enter any additional note related to the celebration (optional).
* **RSVP Note (18)**: Enter any additional note related to the RSVP (optional).
* **Host Note (19)**: Enter any additional note related to the host (optional).
* **Planner Note (20)**: Enter any additional note related to the planner (optional).
* **Celebration Location (21)**: Enter the location of the celebration.
* **Contact Number (22)**: Enter the contact number of the host/organizer.
* **Contact Email (23)**: Enter the contact email of the host/organizer.
* **Other Details (24)**: Enter any additional details related to the celebration (optional).
* **Terms and Conditions (25)**: Select the option to confirm that you have read and agreed to the terms and conditions.
