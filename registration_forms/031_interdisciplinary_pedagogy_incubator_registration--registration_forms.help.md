# Interdisciplinary Pedagogy Incubator Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for the Interdisciplinary Pedagogy Incubator program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your First Name.
2. Fill in your Last Name.
3. Enter your email address.
4. Specify your program of interest.
5. Provide a brief description of your participant details.
6. Select whether you have any interests.
7. Select whether you consent to participate.
8. Enter your Participant ID.
9. Select your date of participation.
10. Select your time of participation.
11. Enter any additional notes (optional).
12. Select whether you have been assigned a tool.
13. Select whether you have an output file.
14. Select whether you have an assigned user.
15. Select whether you have a category 1 category.
16. Select whether you have a category 2 category.
17. Select whether you have a category 3 category.

19. Select whether you have a category 4 category.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name** (`first_name`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, `text`, `required`): Fill in your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, `required`): Enter your email address. Please use a valid email format (e.g. `username@example.com`).
* **Program of Interest** (`program_of_interest`, `text`, `required`): Specify the program you are interested in.
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, `text`, `required`): Provide a brief description of your participant details.
* **Interests** (`interests`, `select_multiple`, `not required`): Select whether you have any interests.
* **Consent** (`consent`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you consent to participate.
* **Participant ID** (`participant_id`, `number`, `required`): Enter your Participant ID.
* **Date of Participation** (`date_of_participation`, `date`, `required`): Select your date of participation.
* **Time of Participation** (`time_of_participation`, `time`, `required`): Select your time of participation.
* **Note** (`note`, `note`, `not required`): Enter any additional notes (optional).
* **Assigned Tool** (`assigned_tool`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have been assigned a tool.
* **Output File** (`output_file`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have an output file.
* **Assigned User** (`assigned_user`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have an assigned user.
* **Category 1** (`category`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have Category 1.
* **Category 2** (`category_2`, `select_one`, `required`): Select whether you have Category 2.
