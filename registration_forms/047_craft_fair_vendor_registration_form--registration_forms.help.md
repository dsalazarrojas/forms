# Craft Fair Vendor Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Fair Vendor Registration Form is designed to collect information from vendors to facilitate their registration and participation in the craft fair. This form ensures that all necessary details are provided to the vendors and helps the organizers of the fair to communicate with them effectively.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in the registration form on page 1.
2. Provide your contact information on page 2.
3. Enter your business details on page 3.
4. Specify your craft fair preferences on page 4.
5. Read and agree to the vendor requirements on page 5.
6. Review and submit the form on page 6.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration (page 1)** (`registration_form`, text, optional): This is the registration page where you can provide your name and other necessary details for the craft fair.
* **Contact Information (page 2)** (`contact_information`, text, optional): Please enter your name and contact details (email, phone number, etc.) so that the craft fair organizers can reach out to you.
* **Business Details (page 3)** (`business_details`, text, optional): Provide information about your business, including your business name, address, and other relevant details.
* **Craft Fair Preferences (page 4)** (`craft_fair_preferences`, text, optional): Specify what type of craft or product you will be selling, and any special requests or requirements you may have for your booth or space.
* **Vendor Requirements (page 5)** (`vendor_requirements`, text, optional): Read through the vendor requirements and terms of agreement to ensure you understand your responsibilities and obligations as a vendor.
* **Agreement (page 6)** (`agreement`, text, optional): Review and agree to the terms of the vendor agreement, which includes the rules and regulations for participating in the craft fair.

## Tips
* Make sure to double-check your information before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or concerns, don't hesitate to reach out to the craft fair organizers.
* Review and read through the vendor requirements carefully to ensure you understand your responsibilities.
