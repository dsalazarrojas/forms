# yoga_instructor_training_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect necessary information from you, the potential yoga instructor, to assess your qualifications for our training program.

## How To Complete This Form
- Start by filling out the form completely and honestly.
- Make sure to fill out all required fields (marked as "required" in the form).
- If you are unsure about any field, feel free to ask for help or clarification.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required): Enter your full first name as it appears on your official documents. 
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required): Enter your full last name as it appears on your official documents.
- **Email** (`email`, email, required): Enter your valid email address that we can use to contact you.
- **Instructor Type** (`instructor_type`, select_one, required): Choose 'Yes' if you are an experienced yoga instructor, or 'No' if you are a student or a beginner.
- **Experience** (`experience`, number, required): Enter the number of years you have been practicing yoga.
- **Training Level** (`training_level`, select_one, required): Choose whether your training level is High, Low, or something else.
- **Training Start Date** (`training_start_date`, date, required): Enter the date you started your last training program.
- **Training End Date** (`training_end_date`, date, required): Enter the date you finished your last training program.
- **Studio** (`studio`, text, required): Enter the name of your current studio or where you usually practice.
- **Bio** (`bio`, text, optional): Write a short bio about yourself.
- **Certifications** (`certifications`, text, optional): List any relevant yoga certifications you have.
- **Education** (`education`, text, optional): List your educational background.
- **Training Hours** (`training_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have trained.
- **Practice Areas** (`practice_areas`, text, optional): List the areas of yoga you have practice expertise in.
- **Taching Certifications** (`teaching_certifications`, text, optional): List any teaching certifications you have.
- **Teaching Hours** (`teaching_hours`, number, optional): Enter the number of hours you have been teaching.

Note: The optional fields (Bio, Certifications, Education, Training Hours, Practice Areas, Teaching Certifications, Teaching Hours) are not required but are encouraged to be filled out as they provide additional context to your application. 

## Tips
- Make sure to double-check your information for accuracy before submitting the form.
- If you have any questions or concerns, do not hesitate to contact us.

I hope this guide has helped you understand the form and its purpose. If you have any further questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to us for assistance.
