# Women's Winter Study Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users to register for a winter study session, providing essential information about their session preferences and personal details.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select your session preferences by choosing one of the options in the "Choose session preferences" section.
* Enter your name in the "First Name" and "Last Name" fields.
* Enter your email address in the "Email" field.
* If you have a phone number, enter it in the "Phone" field.
* Choose a date for the study session using the date picker.
* Select a time of day for the study session using the time picker.
* Choose a study session and location that suits your needs (e.g., "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening", "Online", "On-site", or "Other").
* Select your participant type, which determines the type of study session you'll be joining (Women, Men, or Both).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Choose session preferences** (`study_preferences`, select_one, required: false): Select "Yes" or "No" to indicate your interest in participating in the study session.
* **Session Name** (`session_name`, text, required: false): Enter the name of your study session.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required: false): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`surname`, text, required: false): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, required: false): Enter your email address.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required: false): Enter your phone number.
* **Study Date** (`study_date`, date, required: false): Choose the date of the study session.
* **Time of Day** (`time_of_day`, time, required: false): Select the time of day for the study session.
* **Study Session** (`study_session`, select_one, required: false): Choose the study session that suits your needs (Morning, Afternoon, Evening).
* **Study Location** (`study_location`, select_one, required: false): Select the location of the study session (Online, On-site, Other).
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, required: false): Choose the type of participant you are (Women, Men, Both).

## Tips
* Double-check your session preferences and personal details before submitting the form.
* Ensure you've selected the correct study session and location.
* If you have any issues or questions, contact our support team for assistance.
