# Community Cleanup Volunteer Check Out Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is designed to collect important information from volunteers after a community cleanup event. Please complete the form accurately and thoroughly to help us improve future events.

## How To Complete This Form

1.  Start by filling out the "Full Name of Volunteer" field with your actual name as a volunteer.
2.  If you were assigned a Volunteer ID, please enter it in the "Volunteer ID (If assigned)" field. If not, you can leave it blank.
3.  Enter your email address in the "Email Address" field for follow-up and results delivery purposes.
4.  Record your check-in time in the "Your Check-In Time" field, providing an approximate time.
5.  Record your check-out time in the "Your Check-Out Time" field, providing an approximate time.
6.  In the "Estimated Number of Bags of Litter Collected" field, estimate the number of bags of litter collected during the event.
7.  Identify the area where you completed the cleanup in the "Which area did you complete cleaning" field. For example, "North Park" or "Riverbank".
8.  If you found any unusual or hazardous items, describe the location in the "Any unusual or hazardous items found (e.g., tires, chemicals)" field.
9.  Answer the "Rate your overall experience today" question in the "Rate your overall experience today" field with a rating from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
10.  Share any feedback or suggestions for improvement in the "What could we do better for the next cleanup event" field.
11.  Check the "I have returned all tools and equipment" box in the "I have returned all tools and equipment" field to ensure all necessary materials are returned.
12.  Leave your digital signature in the "Digital Signature (Type Full Name)" field, as this confirms the accuracy of the information provided.
13.  Enter the date of check-out in the "Date of Check-Out" field.
14.  Finally, select whether you are interested in participating in the next cleanup event in the "I am interested in volunteering for the next cleanup event" field.


## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name of Volunteer (text)**: Enter your actual name as a volunteer.
* **Volunteer ID (If assigned) (text)**: If assigned, enter your Volunteer ID.
* **Email Address (email)**: Enter your email address for follow-up and results delivery.
* **Your Check-In Time (time)**: Enter an approximate time of check-in.
* **Your Check-Out Time (time)**: Enter an approximate time of check-out.
* **Estimated Number of Bags of Litter Collected (number)**: Estimate the number of bags of litter collected.
* **Which area did you complete cleaning (text)**: Identify the area where you completed the cleanup.
* **Any unusual or hazardous items found (e.g., tires, chemicals) (text)**: Describe any unusual or hazardous items found during the cleanup.
* **Rate your overall experience today (number)**: Rate your overall experience from 1 (Poor) to 5 (Excellent).
* **What could we do better for the next cleanup event (text)**: Share any feedback or suggestions for improvement.
* **I have returned all tools and equipment (select_one)**: Check this box to ensure all necessary tools are returned.
* **Digital Signature (Type Full Name) (text)**: Leave your digital signature as a confirmation of accuracy.
* **Date of Check-Out (date)**: Enter the date of check-out.
* **I am interested in volunteering for the next cleanup event (select_one)**: Select whether you are interested in participating in the next cleanup event.
* **How did you hear about this volunteer check-out form (select_one)**: Choose how you found out about this volunteer check-out form.

## Tips
- Please ensure that all required fields are completed accurately.
- Use the provided hints for guidance on each field.
- Review the form before submitting to ensure you have completed all necessary fields.
- Take a moment to provide feedback and suggestions to help us improve future events.
