# Seaport Winter Event Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to register for the Seaport Winter Event.

## How To Complete This Form

To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Read the form carefully and fill in your information according to the prompts.
* Enter your Registration Details if required.
* Fill in your First Name, Last Name, and Email.
* Provide your Phone number, Date of Birth, and select your Event Preferences and Emergency Contact status.
* List your Dietary Restrictions, Medical Conditions, and Additional Comments if applicable.
* Click "Submit" to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Details**: Enter your registration information if required.
* **First Name**: Enter your first name.
* **Last Name**: Enter your last name.
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address.
* **Phone**: Enter your phone number.
* **Date of Birth**: Enter your date of birth in the required format (e.g., MM/DD/YYYY).
* **Event Preferences**: Select 'Yes' if you have any preferences for the event.
* **Emergency Contact**: Select 'Yes' if you have an emergency contact.
* **Dietary Restrictions**: Select 'Yes' if you have any dietary restrictions.
* **Medical Conditions**: List any medical conditions you have.
* **Additional Comments**: Enter any additional comments or information about your registration.

## Tips

* Please ensure that you provide accurate and truthful information.
* Review your registration details carefully before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or need help, contact the event organizers.
