# Jazz Event Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Jazz Event Registration form is used to gather information from individuals interested in attending a jazz event. This form is designed to collect personal details and event-related information for registration purposes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, please follow these steps:

1. Review the form fields to understand what information is required.
2. Enter your registration information, including your name and email address.
3. If you have a phone number, feel free to enter it for contact purposes.
4. Choose a start date for the event.
5. Click the "Submit" button to complete your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Information** (`main_information`, text, optional): This field is the main section of the form where you can enter any additional information you'd like to share about your attendance or any special requests you have.
* **Name** (`name`, text, optional): Please enter your name as you would like to be listed on the event registration list.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your email address to receive important event updates and reminders.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): If you'd like to be contacted by phone, please enter your phone number.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, date, optional): Select the date you would like to start attending the event. Note: This field is only for event start date selection, if you're unsure of the format, please use MM/DD/YYYY.
