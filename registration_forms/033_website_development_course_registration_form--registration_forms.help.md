# Website Development Course Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants in the course. It is essential to provide accurate information for a smooth enrollment process.

## How To Complete This Form
- Read the form carefully before filling it out.
- Ensure that you have not already registered for the same course.
- Enter your information correctly, as it will be used for communication purposes.
- Select 'Yes' or 'No' for the Course Selection field to indicate your interest in the course.
- Enter your name and other details accurately in the respective fields.
- Provide payment details if you have already paid for the course.
- Enter any additional comments or questions you may have.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Participant Details** (`participant_details`, text, required: false): Enter your name, email address, and any other relevant details to help us contact you.
* **Course Selection** (`course_choice`, select_one, required: false): Select 'Yes' to indicate that you are interested in the course.
* **Course Name** (`course_name`, text, required: false): If you know the name of the course, enter it here. Otherwise, it will be displayed once you select the course.
* **Course Duration** (`course_duration`, number, required: false): If you have knowledge about the course duration, enter it here.
* **Course Price** (`course_price`, number, required: false): If you have paid for the course, enter the price you paid.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, required: false): If you have already paid for the course, provide your payment details.
* **Schedule** (`course_schedule`, text, required: false): If you want to schedule your course session, provide your preferred schedule.
* **Additional Questions** (`additional_questions`, note, required: false): This is a free-text field for any additional questions or comments you may have.
* **Comments** (`comments`, note, required: false): This is another free-text field for any comments you may have.
