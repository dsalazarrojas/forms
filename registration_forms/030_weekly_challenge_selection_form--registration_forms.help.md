# Weekly Challenge Selection Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to select the details of a weekly challenge for participants. Please provide the necessary information to ensure the challenge runs smoothly. Make sure to fill out all required fields and provide accurate information to avoid errors.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Fill out the form with the required information.
2. Select your answer for "Challenge Selection" (Yes or No).
3. Enter the start and end dates for the challenge.
4. Enter the start and end times for the challenge.
5. Provide your participant preferences in the "Participant Preferences" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Participant Info (participant_info)**: This is a text field where you can provide any additional information about yourself as a participant.
* **Challenge Selection (challenge_selection)**: Select "Yes" if you want to participate in the challenge, and "No" if you do not.
* **Start Date (start_date)**: Enter the date from which the challenge will start.
* **End Date (end_date)**: Enter the date until which the challenge will run.
* **Start Time (start_time)**: Enter the time at which the challenge will start.
* **End Time (end_time)**: Enter the time until which the challenge will run.
* **Participant Preferences (participant_preferences)**: Provide any additional information about your preferences as a participant, such as any specific needs or requirements.
