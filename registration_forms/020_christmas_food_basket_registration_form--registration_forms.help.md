# Christmas Food Basket Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose

The Christmas Food Basket Registration Form is designed to gather information from households about their eligibility and needs for the Christmas Food Basket program. This form will help our team understand your household's situation, allowing us to provide the necessary assistance.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly.
2. Make sure to answer all required fields.
3. If you need assistance, don't hesitate to ask.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### Family Information

* **Primary Contact First Name**: Enter the first name of the primary contact person.
* **Primary Contact Last Name**: Enter the last name of the primary contact person.
* **Email Address**: Enter the email address of the primary contact person.
* **Phone Number**: Enter the phone number of the primary contact person.
* **Household Address**: Enter the address where the household lives.
* **City**: Enter the city where the household lives.
* **State or Province**: Enter the state or province where the household lives.
* **ZIP or Postal Code**: Enter the ZIP or postal code of the household's address.
* **Household Composition**: This is a note field for household composition information.
* **Total Household Size**: Enter the total number of people living in the household.
* **Number of Adults**: Enter the number of adults living in the household.
* **Number of Children**: Enter the number of children living in the household.
* **Ages of Children**: Enter the ages of the children in a comma-separated list.
* **Number of Seniors (65+)**: Enter the number of seniors (65+) living in the household.

### Dietary & Special Needs

* **Dietary Restrictions or Allergies**: Select all that apply from the provided options.
* **Dietary Details**: Provide any additional dietary details or preferences.
* **Preferred Food Items**: Select all that apply from the provided options.

### Financial Information

* **Household Income Range**: Select the income range that best describes the household's income.
* **Do you need additional assistance beyond food?**: Select 'True' if the household requires additional assistance.
* **Additional Assistance Needed**: If the household requires additional assistance, provide a brief description of the specific needs.

Note: Please ensure you answer all required fields accurately. If you need any clarification, please contact us.
This guide is subject to revision and update as necessary to reflect any changes in the form or the program.
