# Irish Dance Camp Registration Form Template - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Irish Dance Camp Registration Form is designed to collect essential information from participants to ensure a smooth and enjoyable experience for everyone.

## How To Complete This Form
1.  Start by filling in your participant details, which includes your name, contact information, and any other relevant details.
2.  Move on to the participant consent section, where you will be asked to agree to the camp's terms and conditions.
3.  Select the classes you want to attend by checking the relevant boxes.
4.  Provide your payment information, which includes your fees and payment method.
5.  Fill in your contact information, including your address, phone number, and any other contact details.
6.  Enter your emergency contact information, including their name, relationship, and contact number.
7.  Disclose any medical conditions, allergies, or health issues you may have.
8.  Finally, provide any emergency medical information, such as any medical conditions you may have that could affect your participation in the camp.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
*   **Participant Details** (`registration_form`):
    This field is where you can fill in your basic details such as name and contact information.
*   **Participant Details** (`participant_details`):
    This field is where you can provide additional participant details.
*   **Participant Consent** (`participant_consent`):
    This field is where you will be asked to agree to the camp's terms and conditions.
*   **Class Sign-ups** (`class_signups`):
    This field allows you to select the classes you want to attend by checking the relevant boxes.
*   **Payment Info** (`payment_info`):
    This field is where you can provide your payment information, including fees and payment method.
*   **Contact Info** (`contact_info`):
    This field is where you can fill in your contact information, including address, phone number, and any other contact details.
*   **Emergency Contact** (`emergency_contact`):
    This field is where you can provide your emergency contact information, including their name, relationship, and contact number.
*   **Medical Info** (`medical_info`):
    This field is where you can disclose any medical conditions, allergies, or health issues you may have.
*   **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`):
    This field is where you can provide any medical conditions, allergies, or health issues you may have.
*   **Emergency Medical** (`emergency_medical`):
    This field is where you can provide any emergency medical conditions or issues you may have.

## Tips

Please make sure to fill in all the required fields, as this will help the camp organizers to process your registration efficiently.

If you have any questions or need further clarification on any of the fields, please contact us at form@gic.mx.
