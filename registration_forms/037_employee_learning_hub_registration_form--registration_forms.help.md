# Employee Learning Hub Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register new employees in the Employee Learning Hub. It collects essential employee information, including personal details, job specifics, and manager information. Please fill out the form accurately and thoroughly to ensure correct registration.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Begin by filling out the **Employee Registration** form (Page 1).
2. Select the course you wish to enroll in from the **Select Course** dropdown menu (Page 2).
3. Input your employee data, including your **Name**, **Email**, **Phone Number**, and **Date of Birth** (Page 3).
4. Provide your **Time of Hire**, **Employee ID**, and **Manager Name**, **Manager Email**, and **Manager Phone Number** (Page 4-5).
5. Choose your **Reporting Manager** and select your **Job Title**, **Job Description**, **Job Category**, **Job Function**, and **Job Level** (Page 6-8).
6. Finally, select your **Employee Status** and submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Employee Registration**: A text field for inputting your employee name.
* **Select Course**: A dropdown menu to choose the course you wish to enroll in.
* **Employee Data**: A text field for inputting your personal details, including your name, email, phone number, and date of birth.
* **Status**: A dropdown menu to select your current status.
* **Time of Hire**: A time field for inputting your hire date.
* **Employee ID**: A number field for inputting your employee ID.
* **Manager Name**: A text field for inputting your manager's name.
* **Manager Email**: A text field for inputting your manager's email.
* **Manager Phone Number**: A text field for inputting your manager's phone number.
* **Reporting Manager**: A dropdown menu to select your reporting manager.
* **Job Title**: A text field for inputting your job title.
* **Job Description**: A text field for inputting your job description.
* **Job Category**: A dropdown menu to select your job category.
* **Job Function**: A dropdown menu to select your job function.
* **Job Level**: A dropdown menu to select your job level.
* **Job Grade**: A dropdown menu to select your job grade.
* **Employee Status**: A dropdown menu to select your status.
