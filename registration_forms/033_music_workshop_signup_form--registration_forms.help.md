# Music Workshop Signup Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Music Workshop Signup Form is designed to collect participant information for a music workshop event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Select your preferred session time by ticking the relevant options in the "Session Selection" page.
2. Provide your participant details, including any relevant information, in the "Participant Details" page.
3. Share your participant preferences, if any, in the "Participant Preferences" page.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Session Selection
• **Session Selection** (Select Multiple, required: false): Select your preferred session time from the available options (Morning, Afternoon, Full Day). You can choose one or more sessions that suit your schedule and preferences.

### Participant Details
• **Participant Details** (Text, required: false): Provide your name, email address, and any other relevant details to help the workshop organizers understand your identity and needs.

### Participant Preferences
• **Participant Preferences** (Text, required: false): If you have any specific requirements or preferences for the workshop, such as dietary needs or accessibility requirements, please share them here.
