# Worker Equipment Familiarization Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Worker Equipment Familiarization Registration Form" is used to register and record the familiarity of workers with equipment in a specific facility. This form helps to track the worker's experience and certification status for equipment usage.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select "Yes" or "No" for the "equipment_used" field to indicate whether you have used the equipment before.
2. Fill in your "First name" and "Last name" in the corresponding fields.
3. Enter your "Email address" and "Phone number" in the respective fields.
4. Provide a brief "Equipment description" if you are registering for an equipment.
5. Enter the "Equipment ID" if you are registering for an equipment.
6. Select the "Equipment type" from the options provided.
7. Enter the number of "Training hours" completed for the equipment.
8. Fill in the "Training date" and "Training time" for when you received training on the equipment.
9. Enter the "Certification date" for when you received certification on the equipment.
10. Select "Certified" or "Not Certified" for the "is_certified" field.
11. Review the form before submitting it. 

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **equipment_used**: 
    Select "Yes" to indicate that you have used the equipment before, or "No" to show that you have not used it. 
- **First name**: 
    Enter your first name to complete this field. It is a required field as you need to identify yourself for registration. 
- **Last name**: 
    Enter your last name to complete this field. It is a required field as you need to identify yourself for registration. 
- **Email address**: 
    Enter your email address for contact purposes. It will be used to notify you of any updates or notifications. 
- **Phone number**: 
    Enter your phone number for contact purposes. It will be used to reach out to you if needed. 
- **Brief description of equipment**: 
    Provide a brief description of the equipment you are registering for. This will help us understand your familiarity with the equipment. 
- **Equipment ID**: 
    Enter the ID of the equipment you are registering for. This will help us track the equipment's usage. 
- **Equipment type**: 
    Select the type of equipment from the provided options. This will help us categorize the equipment for future reference. 
- **Training hours**: 
    Enter the number of hours you have spent training on the equipment. This will help us track your progress. 
- **Training date**: 
    Enter the date when you completed the training on the equipment. This will help us track your training record. 
- **Training time**: 
    Enter the time when you completed the training on the equipment. This will help us track your training record. 
- **Certification date**: 
    Enter the date when you received certification on the equipment. This will help us track your certification status. 
- **Certification expiration**: 
    Enter the date of certification expiration, if any. This will help us track your certification status. 
- **is_certified**: 
    Select "Certified" to show that you are certified, or "Not Certified" to show that you are not certified. This will help us track your certification status. 
- **submit**: 
    Review and submit the form after filling out all the necessary fields.
