# Junior Cricket League Entry Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from users for the junior cricket league entry. This includes contact details, team preferences, and match preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Select the contact information page.
2. Fill in the contact name, phone number, and email address.
3. On the teams page, choose the team(s) you want to participate.
4. Select the type of league you want to participate in (Cricket League, T20, or ODI).
5. Choose the match day you prefer (Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday).
6. Add any comments or notes about your team's preferences.
7. Submit the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Contact Information (Contact Information)** (`contact_name`, `contact_phone`, `contact_email`): This page collects your contact information so we can contact you.
* **Team Names (team_names)** (`team_names`): Choose the team(s) you want to participate in the junior cricket league.
* **League Type (league_type)** (`league_type`): Select the type of league you want to participate in, such as Cricket League, T20, or ODI.
* **Team Managers (team_managers)** (`team_managers`): Choose the team manager(s) for your selected team(s).
* **Match Preferences (match_preferences)** (`match_preferences`): Select the day you prefer to have your match, such as Monday, Tuesday, or Wednesday.
* **Comments (comments)** (`comments`): Add any comments or notes about your team's preferences.
* **Name (name)** (`contact_name`): Fill in your name for contact purposes.
* **Phone (phone)** (`contact_phone`): Enter your phone number so we can contact you.
* **Email (email)** (`contact_email`): Enter your email address so we can contact you.
