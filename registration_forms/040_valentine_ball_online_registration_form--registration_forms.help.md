# Valentine Ball Online Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This is a registration form for the Valentine's Ball online event.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form with your information accurately and truthfully.
2. Make sure to select the correct options for "Couple Size" and "Attending With".
3. If you have any special requests, type them in the "Special Request" field.
4. If you have any additional comments, type them in the "Comments" field.
5. Review your submission before submitting to ensure everything is correct.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, required/optional): Enter your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, text, required/optional): Enter your email address in the correct format.
* **Phone** (`phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Event Date** (`event_date`, date, required/optional): This is not applicable for this form as the event date is already known.
* **Event Time** (`event_time`, time, required/optional): This is not applicable for this form as the event time is already known.
* **Couple Size** (`couple_size`, select_one, required): Select the number of people attending with you:
	+ Solo: 1 person
	+ Couple: 2 people
	+ Family: More than 2 people
* **Attending With** (`attending_with`, select_multiple, required): Select all people you're attending with:
	+ My Partner: Your romantic partner
	+ Friend: A friend or a friend's family member
	+ Family: A family member or family friend
	+ Other: Someone else not listed here
* **Special Request** (`special_request`, note, required/optional): If you have any special requests, such as accommodations or dietary restrictions, please type them in here.
* **Comments** (`comments`, text, required/optional): If you have any additional comments or information about your registration, please type it in here.
