# Remote Diagnostics Usability Testing Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register participants for remote diagnostics usability testing sessions.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:

1. Fill in your user ID.
2. Select your participant type from the options provided.
3. Choose your current status in the testing process.
4. If applicable, enter your date of birth, participant status ending date, diagnostic category, diagnostic type, diagnostic sub-type, diagnostic description, diagnostic status, and diagnostic notes.
5. Review your information for accuracy before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Remote Diagnostics Usability Testing Registration** (`remote_diagnostics_usability_testing_registration`, text, optional): This field is the title of the form, which is automatically generated and not required to be filled in.
* **User ID** (`user_id`, number, optional): Enter your user ID to identify yourself in the system.
* **Participant Type** (`participant_type`, select_one, optional): Select your participant type from the options provided: Participant, Moderator, or Both.
* **Participant Status** (`participant_status`, select_multiple, optional): Choose your current status in the testing process: Not Started, Completed, or In Progress.
* **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): If applicable, enter your date of birth for demographic purposes.
* **Participant Status Ending Date** (`participant_status_ending_date`, date, optional): If you have a participant status ending date, enter it for tracking purposes.
* **Diagnostic Category** (`diagnostic_category`, text, optional): Enter the category of the diagnostic session.
* **Diagnostic Type** (`diagnostic_type`, text, optional): Enter the type of diagnostic session.
* **Diagnostic Sub-Type** (`diagnostic_sub_type`, text, optional): Enter the sub-type of diagnostic session.
* **Diagnostic Description** (`diagnostic_description`, text, optional): Enter a brief description of the diagnostic session.
* **Diagnostic Status** (`diagnostic_status`, select_one, optional): Choose the status of the diagnostic session: Not Started, Completed, or In Progress.
* **Diagnostic Notes** (`diagnostic_notes`, text, optional): Enter any additional notes about the diagnostic session.

## Tips
* Make sure to fill in accurate and complete information for a smooth and efficient registration process.
* Double-check your entries for errors or inconsistencies before submitting the form.
* If you have any questions or issues, contact the testing team for assistance.
