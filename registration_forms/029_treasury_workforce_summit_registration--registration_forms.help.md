# Treasury Workforce Summit Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Treasury Workforce Summit Registration Form is designed to gather information about attendees for the summit. Please take a few minutes to fill out this form accurately and completely. Your registration will help us tailor the summit experience to your needs and preferences.

## How To Complete This Form
1. Click on each field and fill in the required information. You can select multiple options for the "Participation Type" field.
2. Use the provided options for the "Participation Type" field to choose how you would like to participate in the summit.
3. Type your preferences in the "Preferences" field. You can include any additional information you'd like to share.
4. Submit the form once you've completed all the fields.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Registration Form** (`registration_form`, text, optional): This field is a general description of the form and is not a required field.
* **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Please provide your first name.
* **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Please provide your last name.
* **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter your company or work email address.
* **Job Function** (`job_function`, text, optional): Please describe your job function or role.
* **Participation Type** (`participation_type`, select multiple, optional): Select one or multiple options to indicate how you would like to participate in the summit.
	+ Option 1: Attendee
	+ Option 2: Speaker
	+ Option 3: Sponsor
* **Preferences** (`preferences`, note, optional): Share any additional information or preferences you have. This field is not a multiple-choice option and you can type freely.
* **Company** (`company`, text, optional): Please enter your company name.

Note: You can fill the form at your own pace and take breaks whenever you need to. If you have any questions or concerns, please don't hesitate to reach out to us at form@gic.mx.
