# Academic Conference Reservation Form - Help Guide

## Purpose
The Academic Conference Reservation Form is used to collect information from participants who want to attend or present at an academic conference.

## How To Complete This Form

1. Enter your first name in the **First name** field.
2. Enter your last name in the **Last name** field.
3. Enter your email address in the **Email address** field. This should be a valid email address that we can use to contact you.
4. Enter your phone number in the **Phone number** field, including the country code for international numbers.
5. Enter your institution or organization name in the **Institution or organization** field.
6. Enter your position or title in the **Position or title** field.
7. Choose your **Registration type** from the options provided (Full Conference, Single Day, Virtual Attendance, Student Rate, or Group Registration).
8. Select all the dates you plan to attend from the **Which dates will you attend** field.
9. Choose whether you will be presenting at the conference from the **Will you be presenting at the conference** field.
10. If you are presenting, enter the title of your presentation in the **Title of your presentation (if applicable)** field.
11. Select your dietary requirements for catered events from the **Dietary requirements for catered events** field.
12. If you have any accessibility requirements, enter them in the **Do you have any accessibility requirements** field.
13. Choose whether you need help with accommodation arrangements from the **Do you need help with accommodation arrangements** field.
14. Select whether you want to participate in the networking dinner from the **Would you like to participate in the networking dinner** field.
15. Choose your preferred payment method from the **Preferred payment method** field.
16. Enter any special requests or comments in the **Any special requests or comments** field (optional).
