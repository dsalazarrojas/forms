# range_time_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The purpose of this form is to collect shooting time range information from participants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your **lane time**.
2. Enter your **shoot start time**.
3. Enter your **shoot end time**.
4. (Optional) Enter your **lane capacity** if it applies to your shooting session.
5. Enter your **first name** and **last name**.
6. Enter your **email address** to receive updates and confirmations.
7. (Optional) Enter your **phone number** for further contact.
8. Provide any additional **shoot details** you'd like to share.
9. Enter **range details** if applicable.
10. Click the **submit** button to register your shooting time range.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### lane time
* **lane time** (`lane time`, `time`, required): Enter the time range you wish to shoot in.

### shoot start time
* **shoot start time** (`shoot start time`, `time`, required): Enter the start time of your shooting session.

### shoot end time
* **shoot end time** (`shoot end time`, `time`, required): Enter the end time of your shooting session.

### lane capacity
* **lane capacity** (`lane capacity`, `text`, optional): If you wish to specify a capacity for your shooting session, enter it here.

### first name
* **first name** (`first name`, `text`, required): Enter your first name.

### last name
* **last name** (`last name`, `text`, optional): Enter your last name (optional).

### email
* **email** (`email`, `email`, required): Enter a valid email address to receive updates and confirmations.

### phone
* **phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number for further contact (optional).

### shoot details
* **shoot details** (`shoot details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional shoot details you'd like to share.

### range details
* **range details** (`range details`, `text`, optional): Enter any range-specific details if applicable.

### submit
* **submit** (`submit`, `text`, required): Click this button to submit your registration.

## Tips
* Be accurate when entering your shooting times to ensure correct scheduling.
* Double-check your email address for correct format before submitting.
* If you experience any issues while submitting, contact the form owners or relevant authorities.
* Ensure you provide accurate capacity information if applicable.
