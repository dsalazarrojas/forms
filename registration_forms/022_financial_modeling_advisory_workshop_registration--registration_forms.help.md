# Financial Modeling Advisory Workshop Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to register for the Financial Modeling Advisory Workshop. To ensure accurate registration and a successful experience, please complete the form carefully and thoroughly.

## How To Complete This Form
Please navigate through the form pages below, filling out the necessary information for each section. Each section has been designed to ensure your registration is accurate and complete.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Workshop Title
* **Workshop Title** (`workshop_title`, `note`, required): This section is for you to enter a title for the workshop you wish to register for.

### Contact Information
* **Email** (`email`, `email`, optional): Enter your email address. This will be used for communication purposes only, and we assure you that we respect our users' data privacy.

### Contact Information
* **Phone** (`phone`, `text`, optional): Enter your phone number. This information will only be used for workshop updates and communication regarding the workshop you registered for.

### Personal Details
* **Name** (`name`, `text`, optional): Enter your name. This will help us to personalize your experience during the workshop.

### Workshop Schedule
* **Workshop Date** (`workshop_date`, `date`, optional): Select the date you wish to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Time** (`workshop_time`, `time`, optional): Select the time slot you prefer for the workshop.
* **Workshop Duration** (`workshop_duration`, `text`, optional): Specify the duration you plan to attend the workshop for.

### Registration Preferences
* **Registration Type** (`registration_type`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you would like to register: Online, Offline, or Hybrid.
* **Location** (`location`, `text`, optional): Specify the location you would like to attend the workshop at.
* **Company** (`company`, `text`, optional): Enter the name of your company or organization.

### Professional Information
* **Role** (`role`, `select_multiple`, optional): Choose your role in the financial field: Financial Modeler, Modeler/Analyst, or Data Scientist.
* **Experience** (`experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe your relevant experience in the financial modeling field.
* **Workshop Experience** (`workshop_experience`, `text`, optional): Briefly describe any prior workshop experience you may have.

### Payment Information
* **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, `select_one`, optional): Choose how you plan to pay: Cash, Online Payment, or Bank Transfer.
* **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, `text`, optional): Provide any additional payment information.

### Registration Notes
* **Registration Notes** (`registration_notes`, `note`, required): Please enter any additional notes or comments for your registration.

### Terms and Conditions
* **Terms and Conditions** (`terms_and_conditions`, `select_one`, optional): Please agree to the terms and conditions of the workshop.
