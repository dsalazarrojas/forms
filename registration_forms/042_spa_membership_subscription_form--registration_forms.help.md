# Spa Membership Subscription Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for spa membership subscription, where users can provide their personal details and payment information to sign up for a spa membership.

## How To Complete This Form
- Please fill out the form completely and accurately to ensure that your membership application is processed correctly.
- Review the fields below to understand what information is required and what each field is used for.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **First Name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your full first name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Last Name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your full last name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
- **Email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address that you use and check regularly.
- **Phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number that you would like to be contacted on.
- **Date of Birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
- **Payment Method** (`payment_method`, select_multiple, optional): Choose one or more payment methods from the list (Credit Card, PayPal, Bank Transfer).
- **Payment Details** (`payment_details`, text, optional): Enter any additional payment details, such as card number or transfer account information.
