# Youth Finance Support Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for users who are seeking financial support for themselves or their dependents. It helps to gather essential details about the user, their financial goals, and relevant documents, if any.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:

1. Fill out the User Details section with your basic information.
2. In the Finance Goals section, select all the reasons you are seeking financial support (Financial Support, Education, or Employment).
3. If you have any relevant documents, please upload them in the Documents section.
4. In the Review & Follow-up section, indicate your preferred mode of contact (e.g., phone, email, or in-person).

## Field-by-Field Explanation

- **User Details** (`user_details`, `text`, required: false): This section is for you to provide your basic details, such as your name, contact information, and any other relevant information that you feel is essential to share with us.
  - Please enter your name and contact information as you would like to be contacted.

- **Finance Goals** (`finance_goals`, `select_multiple`, required: false): This section is to help us understand the reasons behind your financial support request. You can select all the reasons that apply to you.
  - Please select all the options that align with your financial goals, such as Financial Support, Education, or Employment.

- **Documents** (`documents`, `text`, required: false): This section is for uploading any relevant documents that you think will support your financial support request.
  - If you have any documents that you feel are essential to share with us, please upload them here.

- **Review & Follow-up** (`review_follow_up`, `text`, required: false): This section is to indicate your preferred mode of contact for follow-up actions.
  - Please let us know how you would like to be contacted for follow-up actions, such as phone, email, or in-person.

## Tips
Remember to fill out the form honestly and accurately, and please ensure that all fields are filled out as per your needs. If you have any questions or need further clarification, please feel free to contact us using the contact information provided at the end of this help guide.
