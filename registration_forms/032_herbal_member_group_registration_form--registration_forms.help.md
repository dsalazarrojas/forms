# Herbal Member Group Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The "Herbal Member Group Registration Form" is a form designed to collect information from users for membership registration purposes within the Herbal group. It is essential to ensure that each field is completed accurately, as it will be used for registration processes.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete the form successfully, follow these steps:

1. Fill in all required fields marked as "Required" in the form.
2. Ensure you fill in the correct format for "date" and "time" fields. For dates, use the `mm/dd/yyyy` format and for times, use the `HH:MM AM/PM` format.
3. For "email", ensure it's a valid email address.
4. For "member_type", select one of the provided options.
5. For "email_sent", select multiple options as needed.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **member_name** (`member_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the name of the member. Please type in your name accurately.
* **group_name** (`group_name`, text, required): This field is used to collect the name of the group you are registering for. Please type the name of your group accurately.
* **email** (`email`, email, required): This field is used to collect your email address. Please enter a valid email address.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, required): This field is used to collect your phone number. Please type your phone number correctly.
* **date** (`date`, date, required): This field is used to collect the date of your membership start. Please use the `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **time** (`time`, time, required): This field is used to collect the time of your membership start. Please use the `HH:MM AM/PM` format.
* **member_type** (`member_type`, select_one, required): This field is used to select your type of membership. Please choose one of the provided options.
* **notes** (`notes`, note, required): This field is used to collect any additional notes.
* **group_notes** (`group_notes`, note, required): This field is used to collect any additional notes for your group.
* **email_sent** (`email_sent`, select_multiple, required): This field is used to select when and where you want to receive emails. Please choose multiple options as needed.
* **member_since** (`member_since`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of your membership start. Please use the `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **group_since** (`group_since`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of your group membership start. Please use the `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **notes2** (`notes2`, note, optional): This field is used to collect any additional notes.
* **member_since_date** (`member_since_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of your membership start. Please use the `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **group_since_date** (`group_since_date`, date, optional): This field is used to collect the date of your group membership start. Please use the `mm/dd/yyyy` format.
* **member_since_time** (`member_since_time`, time, optional): This field is used to collect the time of your membership start. Please use the `HH:MM AM/PM` format.
* **group_since_time** (`group_since_time`, time, optional): This field is used to collect the time of your group membership start. Please use the `HH:MM AM/PM` format.

## Tips
* Please fill in the form accurately and truthfully.
* If you are unsure about any field, please ask for help.
* If you need to make changes, please submit a new registration form with the updated information.
