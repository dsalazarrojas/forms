# Gym Membership Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This guide will help you understand the purpose and requirements of the Gym Membership Waiver Form. The form is used to collect information about your membership details, emergency contacts, and payment information. It's essential to complete this form accurately and truthfully to ensure your membership is properly registered and managed.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill in your membership details, including your membership start and end dates, membership type, and term length.
2. Provide information about your emergency contact, including their name, phone number, and email.
3. Indicate whether you have an emergency phone number and email.
4. Agree to the membership terms and conditions.
5. Indicate your payment method and due date.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **gym_membership_waiver_form** (`gym_membership_waiver_form`, text, required/optional): Enter a brief description of your membership.
* **membership_details** (`membership_details`, text, required/optional): Describe your membership details, including type and term length.
* **emergency_contact** (`emergency_contact`, text, required/optional): Enter the name of your emergency contact.
* **emergency_relationship** (`emergency_relationship`, text, required/optional): Describe your relationship with your emergency contact.
* **emergency_phone** (`emergency_phone`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's phone number.
* **emergency_email** (`emergency_email`, text, required/optional): Enter your emergency contact's email address.
* **membership_terms** (`membership_terms`, text, required/optional): Agree to the membership terms and conditions.
* **terms_conditions** (`terms_conditions`, text, required/optional): Read and agree to the terms and conditions.
* **membership_type** (`membership_type`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether you have an active or inactive membership.
* **membership_term_length** (`membership_term_length`, number, required/optional): Enter the length of your membership term.
* **membership_term_unit** (`membership_term_unit`, text, required/optional): Specify the unit of measurement for your membership term (e.g., months, years).
* **membership_start_date** (`membership_start_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date you started your membership.
* **membership_end_date** (`membership_end_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the date your membership will end.
* **member_agreement** (`member_agreement`, text, required/optional): Agree to the terms of your membership.
* **payment_amount** (`payment_amount`, number, required/optional): Enter the payment amount for your membership.
* **payment_method** (`payment_method`, text, required/optional): Specify the payment method used for your membership.
* **payment_due_date** (`payment_due_date`, date, required/optional): Enter the payment due date for your membership.
* **payment_frequency** (`payment_frequency`, select_one, required/optional): Indicate whether you pay monthly or not.
* **payment_status** (`payment_status`, select_multiple, required/optional): Specify whether your membership is active or inactive.
* **membership_status** (`membership_status`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether your membership is active or inactive.
* **member_since** (`member_since`, text, required/optional): Describe your membership duration.
* **member_until** (`member_until`, text, required/optional): Specify when your membership will end.
* **membership_notes** (`membership_notes`, text, required/optional): Add any additional membership notes.
* **membership_type_2** (`membership_type_2`, select_one, required/optional): Choose whether your membership is active or inactive.
* **membership_terms_2** (`membership_terms_2`, text, required/optional): Agree to the additional terms and conditions.
