# cruise_line_contract_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The cruise line contract form is a critical document used to solidify agreements between travelers and travel agencies for cruise line contracts. It captures all necessary details, including passenger information, payment options, contract terms, and travel specifics.

## How To Complete This Form
To fill out the form, follow the instructions below:

1. Fill out all required fields with accurate and complete information.
2. Review each field carefully to ensure that you understand what information is being requested.
3. Select the correct options for each field based on the available choices.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Passenger details**: Enter the name, date of birth, and ID of each passenger. This information is essential for verifying passenger identities and confirming contract details.
* **Payment options**: Choose one or more payment methods from the available options. This will determine how the contract will be paid for.
* **Special requests**: Enter any special requests or preferences you have for the contract. This may include dietary restrictions, medical conditions, or other specific needs.
* **Contract terms**: Review and agree to the contract terms, which outline the conditions of the contract.
* **Contract signed**: Choose the option for which party signed the contract.
* **Travel date**: Enter the date of travel, including the month and year.
* **Travel time**: Enter the time of travel, including the hours and minutes.
* **Passengers count**: Enter the total number of passengers traveling.
* **Contract value**: Enter the total value of the contract, including the currency.
* **Contract signed date**: Enter the date the contract was signed, including the month and year.
* **Payment method**: Choose the method of payment for the contract.
* **Payment status**: Choose the status of the payment, indicating whether it has been paid or not.
* **Special instructions**: Enter any special instructions or preferences for the contract.
* **Travel details**: Enter any specific details about the travel, including flight numbers, hotel information, or other relevant details.
* **Contract terms agreed**: Choose whether you agree to the contract terms.
* **Contract signed by**: Choose the party that signed the contract.
* **Travel agency**: Choose the travel agency involved in the contract.
* **Contract signed date and time**: Enter the date and time the contract was signed.
* **Contract signed time**: Enter the time the contract was signed.
* **Travel agency address**: Enter the address of the travel agency.
* **Travel agency contact**: Enter the contact information for the travel agency.
* **Contract value currency**: Choose the currency of the contract value.
* **Payment reference**: Enter any payment reference or tracking number.
* **Contract signed by title**: Choose the title of the party that signed the contract.
* **Contract signed date**: Enter the date the contract was signed.
*   **Travel agency contact information** : Enter the contact information for the travel agency, including name, email, and phone number.

Note: This help guide is designed to be clear and concise, but if you have any further questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to reach out to the travel agency or travel representative for assistance.
