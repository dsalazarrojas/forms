# Hybrid Learning Program Enrollment Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is used to collect information about students participating in the hybrid learning program.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

* Select "Yes" or "No" for Student Programs.
* Enter your student's name in the text field provided.
* Enter your student's email address in the email field.
* Select your program start and end dates in the date fields.
* Enter your student's phone number in the text field.
* Enter the program duration (number of months or years).
* Select "Active" or "Inactive" as your current program status.
* Select your student's level as "High" or "Low".
* Enter your institution's name in the text field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
### Student Programs
**Student Programs** (1): 
* A drop-down menu to select if the student is enrolled or not.

*   **Student Name** (2): 
  Enter your student's name here.

*   **Student Email** (3): 
  Enter your student's email address here.

*   **Program Start Date** (4): 
  Select your program start date.

*   **Program End Date** (5): 
  Select your program end date.

*   **Student Phone** (6): 
  Enter your student's phone number here.

*   **Program Duration** (7): 
  Enter the duration of your program in months or years.

*   **Program Status** (8): 
  Select your current program status as "Active" or "Inactive".

*   **Student Level** (9): 
  Select your student's level as "High" or "Low".

*   **Institution Name** (10): 
  Enter your institution's name here.
