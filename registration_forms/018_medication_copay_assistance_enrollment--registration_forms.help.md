# Medication Copay Assistance Enrollment - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is intended to collect necessary information for patients who are seeking medication copay assistance. It will help us understand your medical condition, pharmacy, and support contacts to provide accurate assistance.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow these steps:
1. Fill in your personal details, such as your name, email, and phone number.
2. Provide your medical information, including your medical condition and pharmacy details.
3. Choose the medication(s) you are currently taking from the list provided.
4. Select your enrollment status and pharmacy details.
5. Fill in your patient ID and patient support contacts if applicable.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Enrollment Form** (`medication_copay_form`, Text, Optional): This field is the main section of the form. It is optional, but please fill it in if you have any questions or need assistance.
* **Patient Name** (`patient_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for your patient name. Please provide your full name as it appears on your identification.
* **Email** (`email`, Email, Optional): This field is for your email address. Please use a valid email address to ensure we can contact you.
* **Phone Number** (`phone_number`, Text, Optional): This field is for your phone number. Please use a valid phone number for easy contact.
* **Medical Group** (`medical_group`, Text, Optional): This field is for the name of your medical group.
* **Medical Condition** (`medical_condition`, Text, Optional): This field is for your current medical condition.
* **Pharmacy Information** (`pharmacy_info`, Text, Optional): This field is for your pharmacy information, if applicable.
* **Medication List** (`medication_list`, Select Multiple, Optional): This field is a list of the medications you are currently taking. Please select all medications that apply.
* **Medication Name** (`medication_name`, Text, Optional): This field is for the name of the medication you are taking.
* **Start Date** (`start_date`, Date, Optional): This field is for the start date of your medication regimen.
* **End Date** (`end_date`, Date, Optional): This field is for the end date of your medication regimen.
* **Enrollment Status** (`enrollment_status`, Select One, Optional): This field is for your current enrollment status. Please select "Active" or "Inactive" accordingly.
* **Pharmacy** (`pharmacy`, Text, Optional): This field is for your pharmacy name and address.
* **Pharmacy Address** (`pharmacy_address`, Text, Optional): This field is for your pharmacy address.
* **Pharmacy City** (`pharmacy_city`, Text, Optional): This field is for your city's name.
* **Pharmacy State** (`pharmacy_state`, Text, Optional): This field is for your state's name.
* **Pharmacy Zip** (`pharmacy_zip`, Text, Optional): This field is for your zip code.
* **Patient Support** (`patient_support_program`, Text, Optional): This field is for your patient support information, if applicable.
* **Patient Support Phone** (`patient_support_phone`, Text, Optional): This field is for your patient support phone number.
* **Patient Support Email** (`patient_support_email`, Email, Optional): This field is for your patient support email address.
* **Pharmacy ID** (`pharmacy_id`, Text, Optional): This field is for your pharmacy ID number.
* **Patient ID** (`patient_id`, Text, Optional): This field is for your patient ID number.
* **Patient DOB** (`patient_dob`, Date, Optional): This field is for your patient date of birth.
* **Patient Address** (`patient_address`, Text, Optional): This field is for your patient address.
* **Patient City** (`patient_city`, Text, Optional): This field is for your city's name.
* **Patient State** (`patient_state`, Text, Optional): This field is for your state's name.
* **Patient Zip** (`patient_zip`, Text, Optional): This field is for your zip code.

## Tips
* Please make sure to fill in all required fields.
* If you have any questions or need assistance, please use the "Enrollment Form" field.
* This form is for patients seeking medication copay assistance. If you are not a patient, please do not fill this form.
* Please provide accurate and truthful information in all fields.
