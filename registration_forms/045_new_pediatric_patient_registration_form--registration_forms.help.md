# New Pediatric Patient Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The New Pediatric Patient Registration Form is a crucial document used to collect vital information about new pediatric patients. This guide will walk you through each step of the form and explain what each field means in detail.

## How To Complete This Form
- **Start by filling out the form on the first page** (New Patient Form). This page is the starting point for collecting all the necessary information.
- **Proceed to the second page** and fill out the remaining fields in a step-by-step manner.
- **Make sure to review and correct any errors** before submitting the form.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

### New Patient Form (Page 1)
* **Patient Name**: Enter the name of the patient as it appears on their birth certificate or identification documents.
* **Date Of Birth**: Enter the patient's date of birth in the format MM/DD/YYYY.
* **Address**: Enter the patient's address, including the street, apartment number, city, state, and zip code.
* **Medical History**: Describe the patient's medical history, including any previous illnesses, allergies, or medical conditions.
* **Medical Specialty**: Indicate whether the patient has a medical specialty (e.g., "Yes" for pediatric specialist).
* **Medical Condition**: Check all the boxes that apply to the patient's medical condition (e.g., "Yes" for diabetes, "No" for other conditions).
* **Contact Info**: Enter the contact information of the person submitting the form, including their name, phone number, and email address.
* **Email**: Enter the patient's email address (if applicable).
* **Doctor Comments**: Add any additional comments or notes from the doctor.
* **New Patient Status**: Choose the status of the new patient (Active, Inactive, or New).
* **New Patient Status Options**: Select the new patient status option (New, Active, or Inactive).
