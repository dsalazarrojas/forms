# popstar_singing_contest_registration_form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is for registering participants in a singing contest. It collects personal details and song submissions from applicants.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter your name in the "John" field.
2. Provide your email address in the "email_address" field.
3. Enter your birth date in the "birth_date" field in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
4. Choose a name of a pop star you'd like to emulate in the "popstar_name" field.
5. If you have a song you'd like to submit, attach it in the "song_upload" field (this field is optional).
6. Enter the link to your song in the "song_link" field.
7. Enter a unique "required_field_1" field.
8. Enter another unique "required_field_2" field.

## Field-by-Field Explanation
- **John (text, required)**: Enter your name, as it will appear on the contest registration list.
- **email_address (email, required)**: Enter a valid email address associated with your account.
- **birth_date (date, required)**: Enter your birth date in the format "mm/dd/yyyy".
- **popstar_name (text, required)**: Choose a name of a famous singer you'd like to emulate.
- **song_upload (text, optional)**: If you have a song to submit, upload it here. Otherwise, leave this field empty.
- **song_link (text, required)**: Enter the link to your song submission.
- **required_field_1 (text, required)**: Enter a unique value for this field, as it's required for registration.
- **required_field_2 (text, required)**: Enter another unique value for this field, as it's required for registration.

## Tips
Please make sure to fill out all required fields to successfully submit your registration. If you have any issues or questions, contact us at form@gic.mx.
