# Sports Safeguarding Training Registration - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from individuals who want to participate in sports safeguarding training. It is intended for anyone who needs to complete it, regardless of their role or affiliation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Fill out the form in a clear and accurate manner.
2. Enter your first name in the "First name" field (page 1).
3. Enter your last name in the "Last name" field (page 1).
4. Enter your email address in the "email" field (page 1).
5. Enter your date of birth in the "Date of birth" field (page 1).
6. Enter your phone number in the "phone" field (page 1).
7. Enter your organization or affiliation in the "Organization" field (page 1).
8. Select your role in the "Role" field (page 1).
9. Choose the date of the training session you wish to attend in the "Training session date" field (page 2).

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **First name** (`first_name`, text, optional): Enter your first name.
* **Last name** (`last_name`, text, optional): Enter your last name.
* **email** (`email`, email, optional): Enter a valid email address.
* **Date of birth** (`date_of_birth`, date, optional): Enter your date of birth in the format YYYY-MM-DD.
* **phone** (`phone`, text, optional): Enter your phone number.
* **Organization** (`organization`, text, optional): Enter your organization or affiliation.
* **Role** (`role`, text, optional): Select your role (e.g., coach, official, parent).
* **Training session date** (`training_session_date`, date, optional): Choose the date of the training session you wish to attend.
