# Art Festival Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Art Festival Registration Form is designed to collect information from participants and artists for the Art Festival. This form helps us plan and prepare for the festival by gathering essential details about you and your participation.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply follow these steps:

1. Fill in your contact information (Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Date of Registration)
2. Select your participation type (Artist, Vendor or Crafter, Workshop Facilitator, Volunteer, or Attendee)
3. Provide your booth size and setup preferences (if applicable)
4. Describe your artwork or services
5. Choose a payment method for registration fees
6. Confirm you have read and understood the festival rules and terms
7. Confirm you have read and understood the cancellation policy

## Field-by-Field Explanation
* **Full Name** (Participant Name): Enter your full name as you would like it to be displayed on the festival promotional materials.
* **Email Address**: Enter your valid email address for communication purposes.
* **Phone Number**: Enter your phone number for festival contacts.
* **Date of Registration**: Enter the date you are registering for the festival.
* **Type of Participation**: Select your role in the festival (Artist, Vendor or Crafter, Workshop Facilitator, Volunteer, or Attendee).
* **Booth or Space Size Needed**: Choose the size of your booth or space required (if applicable).
* **Description of Work or Services**: Describe what you will be displaying or offering at the festival.
* **Primary Medium or Category**: Enter the primary medium or category of your artwork or service.
* **Registration Fee Amount**: Enter the amount you will pay for your registration fees (if applicable).
* **Payment Method**: Choose your preferred payment method (Credit Card, Check, Cash, PayPal, Bank Transfer, or Other).
* **Electricity Needed**: If you need electricity for your booth, select 'True'.
* **Special Setup Requirements or Needs**: Enter any special requirements or needs for your booth (Tables, shelving, lighting, etc.).
* **I agree to Festival Rules and Terms**: Confirm you have read and agree to the festival rules and terms.
* **I understand the Cancellation Policy**: Confirm you have read and understood the cancellation policy.
