# Science Communication Workshop Registration Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Science Communication Workshop Registration Form is designed to collect information from registrants to facilitate the registration process and ensure a smooth experience.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, please follow the steps below:
1. Enter your First Name and Last Name in the respective text fields.
2. Provide a valid Email address to receive important updates about the workshop.
3. Enter your Phone number (if applicable).
4. Select one of the provided Payment Details options.
5. Specify the Workshop Date you plan to attend.
6. Choose the Workshop Start Time you plan to arrive at.
7. Select the Workshop End Time you plan to leave at.
8. Add any additional Notes about your registration.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **First Name (Text)**: Enter your first name as it is written on your official ID or documents.
* **Last Name (Text)**: Enter your last name as it is written on your official ID or documents.
* **Email (Email)**: A valid Email address is required to receive important updates about the workshop. Please ensure you have access to the entered Email address.
* **Phone (Text)**: If you have a phone number, please enter it for contact purposes.
* **Payment Details (Select One)**: Please select the payment option that best suits your situation. The options and their implications should be explained in a separate section of the workshop's information page.
* **Workshop Date (Date)**: Choose the date you plan to attend the workshop.
* **Workshop Start Time (Time)**: Choose the time you plan to arrive at the workshop on the selected date.
* **Workshop End Time (Time)**: Choose the time you plan to leave the workshop on the selected date.
* **Notes (Note)**: Add any additional information about your registration, such as any special requests or requirements.

## Tips
Please ensure that the information provided is accurate and complete to avoid any registration issues. If you have any doubts or questions, please contact the workshop organizers directly.
