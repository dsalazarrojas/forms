# Urban Planners Guild Membership Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
This form is designed to collect information from potential members interested in joining the Urban Planners Guild. The form helps the guild maintain a record of its members and ensures that members receive relevant resources, updates, and information.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Go through each page, and provide the required information in the corresponding field.
2. Fill in your Full Name, which is your real name.
3. Enter your Email address, which will be used for communication from the guild.
4. Provide your Phone number, which is not mandatory but can be used for contact purposes.
5. Enter the name of your Organization, which can be your current or past work place.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Full Name**: Your real name as it appears on your government-issued ID or passport.
* **Email**: The email address you check regularly for communication from the guild.
* **Phone**: Your contact number, which can be used for direct communication with the guild. This is optional.
* **Organization**: Your current or past work place name, which helps the guild understand your professional background.
* **Membership Application**: This field is not applicable as a field but it seems to be a label for the first page which is a general description of the form. The first page is meant to be filled with the basic information and should be left blank for now.

## Tips
* Ensure that you are using a valid email address and phone number that you regularly check.
* If you are unsure about what information to provide, feel free to reach out to the guild administrators for guidance.
* You will receive updates and resources from the guild via email once you submit this form.
