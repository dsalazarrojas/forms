# Craft Workshop Waiver Form - Help Guide
## Purpose
The Craft Workshop Waiver Form is a necessary document that participants must complete before attending a workshop. It helps ensure their safety and understanding of the workshop's rules and regulations.

## How To Complete This Form
To complete this form, follow these steps:

1. Enter the name of the workshop you are participating in.
2. Provide your name as a participant.
3. Enter your email address so we can contact you with important information.
4. Add your phone number so we can reach you in case of emergencies.
5. Choose the date you plan to participate in the workshop.
6. Sign and date your participant signature line to confirm your acceptance of the terms and conditions.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Workshop Name**: Enter the name of the workshop you are participating in, such as "Painting Class" or "Woodworking Workshop."
* **Participant Name**: Type your name as a participant, including any middle initials if applicable. For example, "John D. Doe."
* **Email**: Enter a valid email address that we can use to contact you with workshop updates and reminders.
* **Phone**: Add your phone number so we can reach you in case of emergencies or last-minute changes.
* **Date**: Select the date you plan to participate in the workshop, using the format "MM/DD/YYYY".
* **Participant Signature**: Sign and date this line to confirm your acceptance of the workshop's terms and conditions.

## Tips

* Make sure to enter your email address and phone number correctly to ensure we can reach you in time.
* Choose the correct date to avoid conflicts with other workshops or events.
* Read the workshop's terms and conditions carefully before signing this form.
