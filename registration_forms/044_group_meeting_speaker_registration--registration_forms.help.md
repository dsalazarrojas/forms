# Group Meeting Speaker Registration - Help Guide

## Purpose
This form is for speakers to provide information and register for a group meeting or event. It is used to collect speaker contact details, session specifics, and other relevant information.

## How to Complete This Form
To complete this form, simply fill in the required fields with the relevant information:

* Ensure that you have read and understood the purpose and usage of this form.
* Select the correct options for each field based on your relevant experience.
* Fill in the text fields with accurate and descriptive information.
* Enter the correct date and time for your session.
* Select the relevant options for your speaker level and language proficiency.

## Field-by-Field Explanation

* **Speaker Information**
  * **Speaker Name**: This field is for your name as a speaker. Please enter your full name as it appears on your business cards or identification.
  * **Speaker Proposal**: This field is for a brief description of your proposed session or presentation. Please provide a concise and descriptive overview of your session.
  * **Session Details**: This field is for more detailed information about your session, such as the topic, duration, and format.
  * **Speaker Bio**: This field is for a brief bio or description of yourself, including your background and experience.
  * **Session Date**: This field is for the date of your session. Please enter the date in the correct format (YYYY-MM-DD).
  * **Session Time**: This field is for the start and end time of your session. Please enter the time in the correct format (HH:MM).
  * **Speaker Email**: This field is for your email address. Please enter a valid email address that you use for business or professional purposes.
  * **Speaker Phone**: This field is for your phone number. Please enter a valid phone number that you use for business or professional purposes.
  * **Session Duration**: This field is for the duration of your session. Please enter the length of time your session will run for.
  * **Session Location**: This field is for the location of your session. Please enter the physical address or venue of your session.
  * **Session Tags**: This field is for additional tags or keywords that describe your session. Please enter relevant keywords that describe your session.
  * **Speaker Topics**: This field is for the topics or areas of expertise you specialize in. Please enter relevant topics that describe your area of expertise.
  * **Speaker Interests**: This field is for your interests or areas of interest. Please enter relevant interests that describe your areas of interest.
  * **Speaker Availability**: This field is for your availability. Please enter the date and time you are available to present.
  * **Speaker Notes**: This field is for any additional notes or comments. Please enter any relevant information that you would like to share with the event organizers.
  * **Speaker Files**: This field is for any relevant files or resources you would like to share with the event organizers. Please enter the URL or location of the files.
  * **Session Files**: This field is for any files or resources related to your session. Please enter the URL or location of the files.
  * **Speaker Affiliations**: This field is for your professional or academic affiliations. Please enter any relevant affiliations that you would like to highlight.
  * **Speaker Expertise**: This field is for your area of expertise. Please enter your relevant areas of expertise or specialty.
  * **Speaker Experience**: This field is for your level of experience. Please enter your level of experience, such as beginner, intermediate, or advanced.
  * **Speaker Language**: This field is for your language proficiency. Please select whether you are proficient in languages such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
  * **Session Language**: This field is for the language of your session. Please select whether your session will be in languages such as 'Yes' or 'No'.
  * **Speaker Level**: This field is for your level of expertise. Please select from the options provided, such as 'High', 'Low'.
  * **Speaker Agreement**: This field is for your agreement to the terms and conditions. Please select whether you agree to the terms and conditions.

Note: Please ensure that all fields are completed accurately and completely.
